# Cottage industries — skilled hands, low status — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five of Ethiopia's cottage industries.
**Answer:** Any five of: metalworking, pottery, tannery, carpentry, masonry, weaving, jewellery, basket-making.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain the chain from stigma to simple tools.
**Answer:** The public disdained and marginalised artisans; held in contempt, their crafts attracted neither honour nor investment of effort in refinement — so locally produced implements and household furniture showed little improvement or sophistication.
