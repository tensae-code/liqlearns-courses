# Hands-on — Cottage industries — skilled hands, low status

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Craft census
Walk your home or market and list ten handmade things. For each, name the craft from the lesson's list that made it. How many could your household live without?

## The smith's day
Mime the smith's work — lift, strike, turn the iron — while a partner times thirty strikes. Then discuss: should this worker rank low in society? Use the lesson's consequence in your answer.
