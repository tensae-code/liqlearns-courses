# Italy In The New Millennium — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the volume closes at home — Obama's invitation to collaboration and Bhutto on fundamentalism's roots (unit Documents), Carrère d'Encausse on Russia and Zamagni on the 2008 crisis, and a Laboratorio on Russia's war in Ukraine — the present as history's open file.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 6, Cap. 22 §6 Italy of the present time
