# Lesson plan — Italy In The New Millennium

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Italy In The New Millennium
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Italy, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the volume closes at home — Obama's invitation to collaboration and Bhutto on fundamentalism's roots (unit Documents), Carrère d'Encausse on Russia and Zamagni on the 2008 crisis, and a Laboratorio on Russia's war in Ukraine — the present as history's open file
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unità 6, Cap. 22 §6 Italy of the present time** — the volume closes at home — Obama's invitation to collaboration and Bhutto on fundamentalism's roots (unit Documents), Carrère d'Encausse on Russia and Zamagni on the 2008 crisis, and a Laboratorio on Russia's war in Ukraine — the present as history's open file
2. **Modulo 18 §§3–5 Il centro-sinistra e la scelta europea; Le trasformazioni sociali; La stagione del centro-destra** — the euro choice, a society transformed and the long centre-right season; Amato and Graziosi's Fare storia verdict — "the last illusion: the choice not to choose and its costs"
3. **Modulo 19 §§7–8 L'Italia: crisi economica e nuovi equilibri politici; L'Italia: verso la Terza Repubblica?** — Diamanti on the League's roots and Caracciolo on the political system's crisis (Fare storia pieces) close the volume where it began — Italy's republic still being remade, its name (Second? Third?) an open question

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
