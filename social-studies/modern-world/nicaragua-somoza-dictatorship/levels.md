This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — under the dynasty's second half the country is surveyed whole — wealth for the family, hardship and protest for the people

Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — growth booms on cotton and the 1960 Central American Common Market, yet cotton expansion strips thousands of peasants of land and culture is kept under the dynasty's thumb

Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — by 1979 the family owns a fifth of Nicaragua's cultivable land and a fortune of 500-600 million dollars built on forced sales, commissions and monopolies
