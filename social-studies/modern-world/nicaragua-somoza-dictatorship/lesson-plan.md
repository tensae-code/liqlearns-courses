## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **La dictadura militar somocista: surgimiento y características** — the US-created Guardia Nacional becomes the Somoza family's personal army; Somoza García coups Sacasa in 1936, takes the presidency 1 Jan 1937 and enriches himself by repression
2. **Nicaragua en la segunda mitad del siglo XX** — under the dynasty's second half the country is surveyed whole — wealth for the family, hardship and protest for the people
3. **La acción heroica de Rigoberto López Pérez** — at a party in León's Casa del Obrero on 21 Sept 1956, Rigoberto López Pérez shoots Somoza García; the book calls it the beginning of the end, though the sons inherit power and repress harder
4. **La sociedad nicaragüense entre 1936-1979** — growth booms on cotton and the 1960 Central American Common Market, yet cotton expansion strips thousands of peasants of land and culture is kept under the dynasty's thumb
5. **Ascenso y consolidación de la dinastía somocista** — Sacasa resigns into exile on 6 June 1936; Somoza García takes the presidency in January 1937 ruling by his formula of silver for friends and lead for enemies, buying the Guardia's loyalty
6. **La fortuna somocista y la modernización económica** — by 1979 the family owns a fifth of Nicaragua's cultivable land and a fortune of 500-600 million dollars built on forced sales, commissions and monopolies
7. **La sociedad nicaragüense entre 1936 y 1979** — population doubles and half the country moves to the cities by 1979, migration from countryside to town remaking Nicaraguan society under the dynasty
8. **Características de la dictadura somocista: bases del poder** — the departing marines leave West Point-trained Somoza García commanding the Guardia Nacional — the first step to owning the country
9. **Ascenso y consolidación de la dinastía somocista** — Somoza has Sandino murdered in 1934, takes the presidency in 1937 and starts a forty-year dynasty; the 1950 Pact of the Generals buys conservative collaboration
