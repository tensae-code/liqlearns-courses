# The United Nations and Unicef

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in 1945 the victors found the UN so war cannot return; in 1946 it creates UNICEF because Europe's war orphans are starving — the peace is. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
