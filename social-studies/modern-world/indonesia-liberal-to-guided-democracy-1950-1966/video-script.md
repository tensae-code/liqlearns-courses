# Video script — Indonesia — from Liberal to Guided Democracy, 1950–1966

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between 1950 and 1966 Indonesia polarises by party and identity inside the Cold War constellation, centre–region imbalance spawns DI/TII, PRRI/Permesta and the RMS, and the dominoes run to 30 September 1965.
