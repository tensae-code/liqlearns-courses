# Hands-on — Indonesia — from Liberal to Guided Democracy, 1950–1966

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Indonesia — from Liberal to Guided Democracy, 1950–1966 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bab 2, Demokrasi Liberal hingga Demokrasi Terpimpin (Gambaran Umum), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
