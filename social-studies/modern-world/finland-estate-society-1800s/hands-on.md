# Hands-on — Finland — estate society in the 1800s

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Finland — estate society in the 1800s — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Sääty-yhteiskunta 1800-luvun Suomessa (the estate society: into what groups were people divided, and how did each group live?), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
