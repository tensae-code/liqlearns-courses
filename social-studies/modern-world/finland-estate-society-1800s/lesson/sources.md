Finland's own way (Suomalainen sääty-yhteiskunta (History deck, FI/SV), Grade 7 (student/teacher: STUDENT + TEACHER)): A whole multidisciplinary module (about 4–5 lessons) lives inside one historical world — in History lessons pupils learn the estate order and 1800s names, invent a person of an assigned estate, then in mother-tongue lessons they interview that person, hunt archive photos of their home place in Finna Street, and write and exchange letters in the character's voice. Named but NOT placed as separate parts: the module-structure decks for pupils (they repeat the History and language decks' steps as an overview) and the language lessons' purely linguistic parts (letter form and peer assessment of the character plan, language deck slides 5 and 12–14).
