# Finland — estate society in the 1800s — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sääty-yhteiskunta 1800-luvun Suomessa (the estate society: into what groups were people divided, and how did each group live?)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — 1800s Finland sorts everyone into ranked estates, and your birth decides your work, dress and prospects before you can walk

## Sub lesson 2 — Etu- ja sukunimet 1800-luvun Suomessa (first names and surnames; did everyone even have a surname?)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — pupils check name registers and old-name lists to learn what a child of their estate would actually have been called

## Sub lesson 3 — Ryhmäjako (the seven groups: aateliset nobles, papit clergy, porvarit burghers, talonpojat peasants, torpparit crofters, piika ja renki maid and farmhand, loiset the landless lodgers)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — each pair or small group draws one rung of the ladder, from noble to landless lodger, and must learn to live on it

## Sub lesson 4 — Oman roolihahmon luominen (creating your own 1800s role character: name, age, family, home place, work — or a child's life)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — armed with the estate and name research, each pupil invents one believable 1800s Finn and writes their plan

## Sub lesson 5 — Haastattelu (interviewing each other's role characters)
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — partners question each other in role — name, family, home, work — and the 1800s world has to hold up under questioning

## Sub lesson 6 — Kirje roolihahmon äänellä (writing, sending and reading a letter in your character's voice; model letter headed Mäntyharju, 5 Oct 1889)
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — each pupil writes home-news from inside the 1800s — mood, recent life, worries and one piece of good news — signs with the character's name, and reads the partner's letter to see another estate's life
