# India Independence And Building — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 12, India: partition to republic.
**Answer: Congress-led struggle forces the Mountbatten Plan of 1947 and partition into Hindu India and Muslim Pakistan; the struggle continues to full independence and the Republic is proclaimed on 26 January 1950; non-aligned abroad, India builds heavy industry including nuclear power at home, wins food self-sufficiency through the Green Revolution, and by the 1980s stands among the world's top ten industrial producers, with an atomic bomb in 1974 and a satellite in 1975 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
