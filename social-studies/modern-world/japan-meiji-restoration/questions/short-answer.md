# Japan Meiji Restoration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Japan's Meiji Restoration.
**Answer: lower samurai, ruined by gold outflows and Tokugawa failure, overthrow the shogunate in 1868, restore the boy emperor, abolish the domains, conscript an army and chase industry under 'enrich the country, strengthen the army' (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *世界历史 九年级 下册 (World History, Grade 9, second volume)* (China), Lesson 4: Japan's Meiji Restoration, printed p. 14.**
