## Sub lesson 1 — The founders — Jinnah, Iqbal and Fatima Jinnah
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Three lives carry the founding — Muhammad Ali Jinnah, born in Karachi in 1876, who joins the Muslim League in 1913, presides over the 1940 Lahore session and becomes the new state's first Governor-General before his death in 1948; Allama Iqbal, born in Sialkot in 1877, whose 1930 Allahabad address first dreams the separate homeland aloud; and Fatima Jinnah, the Quaid's sister, a dentist who stands beside the movement and is remembered as Madar-e-Millat, Mother of the Nation
