# Video script — The founders — Jinnah, Iqbal and Fatima Jinnah

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three lives carry the founding — Muhammad Ali Jinnah leads it to the Governor-General's house, Allama Iqbal dreams the separate homeland aloud at Allahabad in 1930, and Fatima Jinnah, the Quaid's sister, stands beside the movement as Madar-e-Millat, Mother of the Nation.
