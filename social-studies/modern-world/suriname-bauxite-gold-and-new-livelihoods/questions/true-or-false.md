# Suriname — bauxite, gold and new livelihoods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As plantations decline, freed and immigrant small farmers, balata bleeders, timber workers and gold seekers make new livings; bauxite mining (from the late 1910s) and the Brokopondo dam project later transform the economy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De opkomst van nieuwe middelen van bestaan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De opkomst van nieuwe middelen van bestaan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After supervision ends the freed choose work — town trades, small farming, or the hard gamble of gold-seeking in the interior.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Werken om te leven

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Werken om te leven
