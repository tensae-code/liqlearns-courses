# Video script — Suriname — bauxite, gold and new livelihoods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

As plantations decline, freed and immigrant small farmers, balata bleeders, timber workers and gold seekers make new livings; bauxite mining (from the late 1910s) and the Brokopondo dam project later transform the economy.

After supervision ends the freed choose work — town trades, small farming, or the hard gamble of gold-seeking in the interior.
