# The Third World Nations Between The Blocs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Freed colonies refuse both camps, meet at Bandung and try to bargain as a third force.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §15 The countries of the Third World

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §15 The countries of the Third World

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Newly independent states of Asia, Africa and Latin America refuse both camps at Bandung and try to build a third path between them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 3, Lessons 46–48: the countries of the "Third World"

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 3, Lessons 46–48: the countries of the "Third World"
