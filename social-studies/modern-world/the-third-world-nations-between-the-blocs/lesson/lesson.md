# The Third World Nations Between The Blocs

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: freed colonies refuse both camps, meet at Bandung and try to bargain as a third force As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
