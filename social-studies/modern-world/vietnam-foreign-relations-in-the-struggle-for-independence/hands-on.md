# Hands-on — Vietnam Foreign Relations In The Struggle For Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Foreign Relations In The Struggle For Independence — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 12 The Indochinese Communist Party's diplomacy, 1930–1945: as a Comintern section the Party organises support for the USSR's resistance, fundraises with French and Chinese organisations in Indochina for China's war victims, builds the Overseas Chinese movement, and contacts anti-Japanese movements from Burma to Indonesia; through Việt Minh it stands openly with the Allies — talks with the Republic of China's representatives, a seat in the Vietnam Revolutionary Alliance, and by late April 1945 a working cooperation with the American OSS, sealed in the Tân Trào photograph with the "Deer Team", from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
