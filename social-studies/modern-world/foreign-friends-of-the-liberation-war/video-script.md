# Video script — Foreign friends of the Liberation War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 7 honours the foreign friends — starting with a reporter. On 25 March 1971, foreign journalists are expelled from Dhaka.

Simon Dring, 26, hides in the Hotel Intercontinental and cables the massacre to The Daily Telegraph.

Then the BBC and Akashvani carry the war to the world. A war the world can see cannot be denied.

The circle widens. About one crore refugees — ten million people — flood into India, which shelters, feeds and speaks for them under Indira Gandhi.

The Soviet Union backs Bangladesh diplomatically. Senator Edward Kennedy visits the camps. George Harrison sings for Bangladesh.

Shelter, diplomacy, witness and song — four kinds of friendship in wartime.
