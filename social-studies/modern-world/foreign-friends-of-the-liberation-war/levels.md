# Foreign friends of the Liberation War — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The journalists — Simon Dring and the world's wires
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Name the reporter who hid and cabled the massacre: Simon Dring, to The Daily Telegraph. Say when foreign reporters were expelled: 25 March 1971. Name the broadcasters who carried the war to the world: the BBC and Akashvani.

## Sub lesson 2 — Refugees, diplomacy and song
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Say how many refugees fled to India: about one crore (ten million). Name India's leader who sheltered and spoke for them: Indira Gandhi. Name the other friends and their kinds of help: the Soviet Union (diplomacy), Edward Kennedy (witness), George Harrison (song).
