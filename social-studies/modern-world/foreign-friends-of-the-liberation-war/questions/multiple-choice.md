# Foreign friends of the Liberation War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Simon Dring hid in —
a) The Hotel Intercontinental, Dhaka  b) A refugee camp  c) The BBC office
**Answer: a) The Hotel Intercontinental, Dhaka** — where Dring hid.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dring cabled the massacre to —
a) Akashvani  b) The Daily Telegraph  c) The Soviet Union
**Answer: b) The Daily Telegraph** — the paper he cabled.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The refugees who fled to India numbered about —
a) One lakh  b) One crore (ten million)  c) One thousand
**Answer: b) One crore (ten million)** — the book's count.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** George Harrison helped by —
a) Visiting the camps  b) Singing for Bangladesh  c) Cabling a newspaper
**Answer: b) Singing for Bangladesh** — the friendship of song.
