# Hands-on — Foreign friends of the Liberation War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The hidden cable

Write the short cable you imagine Dring sending: three facts the world must know, in as few words as a cable allows.

## Why seeing matters

Write two sentences: why does the book count journalists among a liberation war's friends?

## Four friendships

Make a four-row chart — shelter, diplomacy, witness, song — and write who gave each and what it did.

## A crore in view

Write two sentences imagining your whole town multiplied until it reaches ten million people on the move: what would they need first?
