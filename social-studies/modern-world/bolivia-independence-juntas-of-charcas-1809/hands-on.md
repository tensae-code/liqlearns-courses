# Hands-on — Bolivia's independence — the juntas of Charcas, 1809

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bolivia's independence — the juntas of Charcas, 1809 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Año 3: Primera etapa — el movimiento juntista en la Real Audiencia de Charcas (1809), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
