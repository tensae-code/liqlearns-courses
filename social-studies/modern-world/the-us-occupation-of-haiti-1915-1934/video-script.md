# Video script — The US occupation of Haiti, 1915–1934

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Haiti guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Presidents fall one after another, debts press in, and president vilbrun guillaume sam is assassinated on 28 july 1915 — us marines land the same day

An imposed convention hands washington haiti's customs, finances and a us-officered gendarmerie — sovereignty survives on paper only

The occupation builds roads with forced corvée labour and a centralised armed force — the machinery pupils are asked to link to haiti's later authoritarian model

Caco guerrillas under leaders like charlemagne péralte fight from the hills while writers and nationalists resist in print

Through the era's newspapers, pupils watch elites who served, hedged or turned against the occupier as the years changed the bargain

The marines leave in 1934; pupils weigh roads and sanitation against humiliation, deaths and a militarised state, and deliver a verdict

Hold that story: US Marines land in 1915 and stay until 1934 — customs, finances and a gendarmerie pass under Washington's hand, Caco guerrillas and writers resist, and pupils must weigh roads and sanitation against humiliation, deaths and a militarised state.
