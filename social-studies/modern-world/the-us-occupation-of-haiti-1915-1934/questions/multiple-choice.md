# The US occupation of Haiti, 1915–1934 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Preludes to the occupation  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Preludes to the occupation** — presidents fall one after another, debts press in, and President Vilbrun Guillaume Sam is assassinated on 28 July 1915 — US Marines land the same day.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide this part is —
a) Séance 1, Le contexte externe et interne ou les préludes à l'occupation  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Séance 1, Le contexte externe et interne ou les préludes à l'occupation** — the guide's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Haitian state placed under tutelage  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
