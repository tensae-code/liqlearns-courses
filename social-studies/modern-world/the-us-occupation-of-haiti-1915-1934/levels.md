# The US occupation of Haiti, 1915–1934 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Preludes to the occupation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Presidents fall one after another, debts press in, and president vilbrun guillaume sam is assassinated on 28 july 1915 — us marines land the same day (Séance 1, Le contexte externe et interne ou les préludes à l'occupation)

## Sub lesson 2 — The Haitian state placed under tutelage
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: An imposed convention hands washington haiti's customs, finances and a us-officered gendarmerie — sovereignty survives on paper only (Séance 2, La mise sous tutelle de l'État haïtien ?)

## Sub lesson 3 — The occupation and institutionalisation
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The occupation builds roads with forced corvée labour and a centralised armed force — the machinery pupils are asked to link to haiti's later authoritarian model (Séance 3, L'occupation et le processus d'institutionnalisation)

## Sub lesson 4 — Resistances to the occupation
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Caco guerrillas under leaders like charlemagne péralte fight from the hills while writers and nationalists resist in print (Séance 4, Les résistances à l'occupation)

## Sub lesson 5 — Collaboration with the occupier
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Through the era's newspapers, pupils watch elites who served, hedged or turned against the occupier as the years changed the bargain (Séance 5, La collaboration avec l'occupant, des relations complexes et changeantes)

## Sub lesson 6 — Towards a balance sheet of the occupation
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The marines leave in 1934; pupils weigh roads and sanitation against humiliation, deaths and a militarised state, and deliver a verdict (Séance 6, Vers un bilan de l'occupation américaine ?)
