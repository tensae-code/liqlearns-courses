## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 15, India under the British, later 19th century
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — on 1 January 1877 Queen Victoria is proclaimed Empress of India at the great durbar pictured here, sealing a conquest administered by a Viceroy and a five-man council ruling like a government; the British conciliate a slice of the feudal upper class into henchmen and deliberately deepen ethnic and religious divisions; plantations eat the peasants' land, mines and processing works and new ports grow for export, and an official "keep them ignorant" policy fosters backward customs — against it stand the Sepoy rising (1857–1859) and the Congress-led movement of 1905–1908
