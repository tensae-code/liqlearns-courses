# Video script — India Under British Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Vietnam's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

on 1 January 1877 Queen Victoria is proclaimed Empress of India at the great durbar pictured here, sealing a conquest administered by a Viceroy and a five-man council ruling like a government; the British conciliate a slice of the feudal upper class into henchmen and deliberately deepen ethnic and religious divisions; plantations eat the peasants' land, mines and processing works and new ports grow for export, and an official "keep them ignorant" policy fosters backward customs — against it stand the Sepoy rising (1857–1859) and the Congress-led movement of 1905–1908
