**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 15, India under the British, later 19th century.
**Answer: on 1 January 1877 Queen Victoria is proclaimed Empress of India at the great durbar pictured here, sealing a conquest administered by a Viceroy and a five-man council ruling like a government; the British conciliate a slice of the feudal upper class into henchmen and deliberately deepen ethnic and religious divisions; plantations eat the peasants' land, mines and processing works and new ports grow for export, and an official "keep them ignorant" policy fosters backward customs — against it stand the Sepoy rising (1857–1859) and the Congress-led movement of 1905–1908 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
