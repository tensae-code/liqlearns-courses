# Malaya's road to Merdeka — elections, Reid and 1957 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The machinery of independence
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Communities Liaison Committee draws the races' leaders together, the Member System (Sistem Ahli) apprentices local leaders in government, a national education system is designed, and parties multiply — KMM, PKMM, API, UMNO, MCA, MIC, Hizbul Muslimin, PAS and the IMP among them

## Sub lesson 2 — The first general election
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: elections climb from municipal to state to federal level; the 1955 general election (proclaimed by Sir Donald MacGillivray) fills the Federal Legislative Council, and Tunku Abdul Rahman becomes Chief Minister at the head of the first cabinet

## Sub lesson 3 — Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: at Lancaster House, London (18 January–6 February 1956) the Rulers' representatives and the Alliance win agreement on self-government; the Reid Commission (announced 8 March 1956, at work by late June, receiving 131 written memoranda) drafts the constitution; and after the midnight lowering of the Union Jack at Padang Kelab Selangor, some 20,000 people stand in the rain from 6 a.m. at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957 to hear independence proclaimed — the First Malaya Plan 1956–1960 already charting the economy
