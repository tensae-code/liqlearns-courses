# Malaya's road to Merdeka — elections, Reid and 1957 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The machinery of independence.
**Answer: the Communities Liaison Committee draws the races' leaders together, the Member System (Sistem Ahli) apprentices local leaders in government, a national education system is designed, and parties multiply — KMM, PKMM, API, UMNO, MCA, MIC, Hizbul Muslimin, PAS and the IMP among them (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The first general election.
**Answer: elections climb from municipal to state to federal level; the 1955 general election (proclaimed by Sir Donald MacGillivray) fills the Federal Legislative Council, and Tunku Abdul Rahman becomes Chief Minister at the head of the first cabinet (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka.
**Answer: at Lancaster House, London (18 January–6 February 1956) the Rulers' representatives and the Alliance win agreement on self-government; the Reid Commission (announced 8 March 1956, at work by late June, receiving 131 written memoranda) drafts the constitution; and after the midnight lowering of the Union Jack at Padang Kelab Selangor, some 20,000 people stand in the rain from 6 a.m. at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957 to hear independence proclaimed — the First Malaya Plan 1956–1960 already charting the economy (any close wording).**
