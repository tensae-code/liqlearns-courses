# Malaya's road to Merdeka — elections, Reid and 1957 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Communities Liaison Committee draws the races' leaders together, the Member System (Sistem Ahli) apprentices local leaders in government, a national education system is designed, and parties multiply — KMM, PKMM, API, UMNO, MCA, MIC, Hizbul Muslimin, PAS and the IMP among them
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 7: the machinery of independence).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Elections climb from municipal to state to federal level; the 1955 general election (proclaimed by Sir Donald MacGillivray) fills the Federal Legislative Council, and Tunku Abdul Rahman becomes Chief Minister at the head of the first cabinet
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 8: the first general election).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At Lancaster House, London (18 January–6 February 1956) the Rulers' representatives and the Alliance win agreement on self-government; the Reid Commission (announced 8 March 1956, at work by late June, receiving 131 written memoranda) drafts the constitution; and after the midnight lowering of the Union Jack at Padang Kelab Selangor, some 20,000 people stand in the rain from 6 a.m. at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957 to hear independence proclaimed — the First Malaya Plan 1956–1960 already charting the economy
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 9–10: Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka).
