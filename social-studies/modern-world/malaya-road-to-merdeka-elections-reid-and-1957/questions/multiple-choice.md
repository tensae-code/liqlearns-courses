# Malaya's road to Merdeka — elections, Reid and 1957 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The machinery of independence  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The machinery of independence** — the Communities Liaison Committee draws the races' leaders together, the Member System (Sistem Ahli) apprentices local leaders in government, a national education system is designed, and parties multiply — KMM, PKMM, API, UMNO, MCA, MIC, Hizbul Muslimin, PAS and the IMP among them

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 7: the machinery of independence  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 7: the machinery of independence** — the book's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The first general election  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The first general election** — elections climb from municipal to state to federal level; the 1955 general election (proclaimed by Sir Donald MacGillivray) fills the Federal Legislative Council, and Tunku Abdul Rahman becomes Chief Minister at the head of the first cabinet

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book this part is —

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka** — at Lancaster House, London (18 January–6 February 1956) the Rulers' representatives and the Alliance win agreement on self-government; the Reid Commission (announced 8 March 1956, at work by late June, receiving 131 written memoranda) drafts the constitution; and after the midnight lowering of the Union Jack at Padang Kelab Selangor, some 20,000 people stand in the rain from 6 a.m. at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957 to hear independence proclaimed — the First Malaya Plan 1956–1960 already charting the economy
