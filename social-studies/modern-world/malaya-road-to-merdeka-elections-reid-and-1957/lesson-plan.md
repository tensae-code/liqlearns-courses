# Lesson plan — Malaya's road to Merdeka — elections, Reid and 1957

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Malaya's road to Merdeka — elections, Reid and 1957

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Malaya climbs to Merdeka step by step — parties and the Member System, the 1955 general election and Tunku Abdul Rahman as Chief Minister, the Reid Commission's constitution — until independence is proclaimed in the rain at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The machinery of independence** — the Communities Liaison Committee draws the races' leaders together, the Member System (Sistem Ahli) apprentices local leaders in government, a national education system is designed, and parties multiply — KMM, PKMM, API, UMNO, MCA, MIC, Hizbul Muslimin, PAS and the IMP among them (Bab 7: the machinery of independence)
2. **The first general election** — elections climb from municipal to state to federal level; the 1955 general election (proclaimed by Sir Donald MacGillivray) fills the Federal Legislative Council, and Tunku Abdul Rahman becomes Chief Minister at the head of the first cabinet (Bab 8: the first general election)
3. **Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka** — at Lancaster House, London (18 January–6 February 1956) the Rulers' representatives and the Alliance win agreement on self-government; the Reid Commission (announced 8 March 1956, at work by late June, receiving 131 written memoranda) drafts the constitution; and after the midnight lowering of the Union Jack at Padang Kelab Selangor, some 20,000 people stand in the rain from 6 a.m. at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957 to hear independence proclaimed — the First Malaya Plan 1956–1960 already charting the economy (Bab 9–10: Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka)
