# China Changing Everyday Life 1950s To Today — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the 1950s–70s families buy grain, cloth and bicycles only with ration coupons; the 'three big items' change from sewing machine, bicycle and watch to TV, fridge and washer, then to house, car and insurance.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Stories of life

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 七年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Stories of life

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wardrobes turn from blue-grey uniformity to fashion, ration coupons become museum pieces, and trains give way to high-speed rail and mobile payment.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Social life changes

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
