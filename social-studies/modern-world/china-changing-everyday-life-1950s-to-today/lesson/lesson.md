# China Changing Everyday Life 1950s To Today

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in the 1950s–70s families buy grain, cloth and bicycles only with ration coupons; the 'three big items' change from sewing machine, bicycle and watch to TV, fridge and washer, then to house, car and insurance As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Stories of life — in the 1950s–70s families buy grain, cloth and bicycles only with ration coupons; the 'three big items' change from sewing machine, bicycle and watch to TV, fridge and washer, then to house, car and insurance. Social life changes — wardrobes turn from blue-grey uniformity to fashion, ration coupons become museum pieces, and trains give way to high-speed rail and mobile payment. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
