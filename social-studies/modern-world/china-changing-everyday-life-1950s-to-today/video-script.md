# Video script — China Changing Everyday Life 1950s To Today

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as China's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the 1950s–70s families buy grain, cloth and bicycles only with ration coupons; the 'three big items' change from sewing machine, bicycle and watch to TV, fridge and washer, then to house, car and insurance
