# Hands-on — Micronesia — a leap into the modern world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One lifetime, two worlds
Interview or imagine an elder who was a child before the leap and an adult after it. Write their day twice — morning before the leap, morning after — using only things this lesson names.

## The leap in your place
List the last big leap where you live: the first phone network, paved road or supermarket. Mark what it changed in one year, and what took a generation to change. Compare with Micronesia's leap.
