# Micronesia — a leap into the modern world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The leap into the modern world happened in the —
a) 1920s–30s  b) 1960s–70s  c) 1990s–2000s
**Answer: b) 1960s–70s** — the decades the book gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these arrived in the leap?
a) Radios, new schools and hospitals  b) Railways and factories  c) German district offices
**Answer: a) Radios, new schools and hospitals** — the arrivals the book lists.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Peace Corps volunteers and Civic Action Teams came from —
a) Japan  b) America  c) Germany
**Answer: b) America** — the country the book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Micronesianization meant —
a) Islanders leaving the islands  b) Islanders taking charge of more of the Trust Territory government  c) Americans taking more posts
**Answer: b) Islanders taking charge of more of the Trust Territory government** — the change the book describes.
