# Micronesia — a leap into the modern world — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The changes of the 1960s–70s came slowly, over a whole century.
**Answer: False** — the book calls it a leap — money, radios, schools, hospitals, roads and airfields transformed the islands within the 1960s–70s.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Micronesianization prepared islanders to argue for self-government.
**Answer: True** — islanders running more of the government themselves led toward the self-government story.
