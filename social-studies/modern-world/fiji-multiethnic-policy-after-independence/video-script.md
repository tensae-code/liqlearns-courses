# Video script — Fiji — multi-ethnic policy after independence, 1970 to 2010

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colonial policy kept "races" apart; after 1970 governments proclaim a special role in managing ethnic affairs, the language shifts from multiracial to multi-ethnic, and by 2010 councils for multi-ethnic affairs and national reconciliation sit under the Prime Minister.
