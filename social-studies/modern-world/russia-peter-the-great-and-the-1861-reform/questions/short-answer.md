# Russia Peter The Great And The 1861 Reform — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Russia's reforms.
**Answer: Peter the Great works incognito in foreign shipyards, builds a new army and a new capital on the Baltic he wins from Sweden, then a later tsar frees the serfs in 1861 to push Russia toward industry (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *世界历史 九年级 下册 (World History, Grade 9, second volume)* (China), Lesson 2: Russia's reforms, printed p. 6.**
