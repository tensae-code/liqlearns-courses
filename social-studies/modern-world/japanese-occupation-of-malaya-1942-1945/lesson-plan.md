# Lesson plan — The Japanese occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Japanese occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Malaysia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Japan occupies Malaya from 1942 to 1945 and rules by Nippon Seishin; the people fight back through Force 136 and individuals like Gurchan Singh and Sybil Kathigasu, until the atomic bombs end it in August 1945
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Occupation and resistance** — Japan occupies the country and rules by Nippon Seishin — English and Chinese schools closed, Japanese language imposed, the Emperor revered; the people fight back through Force 136 (raised in 1943 under South East Asia Command at Colombo, landed in Ulu Perak, raising the Askar Melayu Setia with Kapten Mohd Salleh Hj. Sulaiman and the Gerik District Officer) and through individuals like Gurchan Singh, the "Lion of Malaya" leafleting against the occupation, and Sybil Kathigasu; the atomic bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end it, Japan surrendering on 15 August (Bab 3.5–3.9: occupation and resistance)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
