# The Japanese occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Occupation and resistance  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Occupation and resistance** — Japan occupies the country and rules by Nippon Seishin — English and Chinese schools closed, Japanese language imposed, the Emperor revered; the people fight back through Force 136 (raised in 1943 under South East Asia Command at Colombo, landed in Ulu Perak, raising the Askar Melayu Setia with Kapten Mohd Salleh Hj. Sulaiman and the Gerik District Officer) and through individuals like Gurchan Singh, the "Lion of Malaya" leafleting against the occupation, and Sybil Kathigasu; the atomic bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end it, Japan surrendering on 15 August

a) Bab 3.5–3.9: occupation and resistance  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 3.5–3.9: occupation and resistance** — the book's own heading for this part.
