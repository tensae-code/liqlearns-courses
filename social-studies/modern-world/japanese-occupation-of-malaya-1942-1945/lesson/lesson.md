# The Japanese occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Japan occupies Malaya from 1942 to 1945 and rules by Nippon Seishin; the people fight back through Force 136 and individuals like Gurchan Singh and Sybil Kathigasu, until the atomic bombs end it in August 1945. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Occupation and resistance

Japan occupies the country and rules by Nippon Seishin — English and Chinese schools closed, Japanese language imposed, the Emperor revered; the people fight back through Force 136 (raised in 1943 under South East Asia Command at Colombo, landed in Ulu Perak, raising the Askar Melayu Setia with Kapten Mohd Salleh Hj. Sulaiman and the Gerik District Officer) and through individuals like Gurchan Singh, the "Lion of Malaya" leafleting against the occupation, and Sybil Kathigasu; the atomic bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end it, Japan surrendering on 15 August (Bab 3.5–3.9: occupation and resistance)
