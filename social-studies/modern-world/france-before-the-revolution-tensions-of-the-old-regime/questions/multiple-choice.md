# France before the Revolution — tensions of the old regime — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Va-nu-pieds episode was —
a) A tax revolt  b) A sea voyage  c) A coronation
**Answer: a) A tax revolt** — la révolte des Va-nu-pieds, a tax revolt in the beat.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Riches et pauvres à Paris shows —
a) Parisian poverty beside wealth  b) Only royal gardens  c) No poverty at all
**Answer: a) Parisian poverty beside wealth** — rich and poor in Paris.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In an eighteenth-century salon, a new public —
a) Argued about everything the old order forbids  b) Was forbidden to speak  c) Only played music
**Answer: a) Argued about everything the old order forbids** — the salon beat.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Together these scenes show the old regime —
a) Cracking under its own weight  b) Stronger than ever  c) Already abolished
**Answer: a) Cracking under its own weight** — cracking under its own weight.
