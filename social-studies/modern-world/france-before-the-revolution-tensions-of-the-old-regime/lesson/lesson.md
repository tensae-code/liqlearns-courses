# France before the Revolution — tensions of the old regime

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Tax revolts such as the Va-nu-pieds and Parisian poverty show an old regime cracking under its own weight — while in the salons a new public argues about everything the old order forbids. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Revolts and Parisian poverty

Tax revolts and Parisian poverty show an old regime cracking under its own weight — the revolt of the Va-nu-pieds and the rich and poor of Paris are the manual's witnesses.
