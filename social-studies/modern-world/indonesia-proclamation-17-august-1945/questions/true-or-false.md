# Indonesia — the Proclamation, 17 August 1945 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On 17 August 1945 Sukarno reads the short text, the red-and-white sewn by Fatmawati rises, IPPHOS photographers fix the moment, and the Domei news agency carries the words to the world.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab IV.C Detik-Detik Proklamasi

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — the British interregnum, 1811–1816.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab IV.C Detik-Detik Proklamasi
