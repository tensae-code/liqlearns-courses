# Video script — Indonesia — the Proclamation, 17 August 1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

On 17 August 1945 Sukarno reads the short proclamation text, the red-and-white sewn by Fatmawati rises, IPPHOS photographers fix the moment, and the Domei news agency carries the words to the world.
