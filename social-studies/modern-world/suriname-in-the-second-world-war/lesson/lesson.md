# Suriname in the Second World War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: As a Dutch colony Suriname is at war; Germans are interned, the German ship Goslar is scuttled in the Suriname river, defences and a militia are organised, imports fail, and bauxite earnings leave the colony able to pay its own way As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — De Tweede Wereldoorlog in Suriname

As a Dutch colony Suriname is at war; Germans are interned, the German ship Goslar is scuttled in the Suriname river, defences and a militia are organised, imports fail, and bauxite earnings leave the colony able to pay its own way (De Tweede Wereldoorlog in Suriname)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Oorlog met Duitsland

The book defines world war, sets out 1939–45, and brings the war home: as a Dutch colony Suriname is at war with Germany (Oorlog met Duitsland)

## Sub lesson 3 — De veiligheid in ons land

Governor Kielstra orders blackout from 7pm to 7am, dark curtains and air-raid shelters against a German attack that never comes (De veiligheid in ons land)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — De bauxietindustrie in ons land

War cuts imports and queues lengthen in Paramaribo, yet scarcity sparks local production — small farmers, shoemakers, tailors, lemonade factories — while bauxite booms for the Allied war effort (De bauxietindustrie in ons land)
