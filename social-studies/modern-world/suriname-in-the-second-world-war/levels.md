# Suriname in the Second World War — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — De Tweede Wereldoorlog in Suriname
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — As a Dutch colony Suriname is at war; Germans are interned, the German ship Goslar is scuttled in the Suriname river, defences and a militia are organised, imports fail, and bauxite earnings leave the colony able to pay its own way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Oorlog met Duitsland
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The book defines world war, sets out 1939–45, and brings the war home: as a Dutch colony Suriname is at war with Germany.

## Sub lesson 3 — De veiligheid in ons land
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Governor Kielstra orders blackout from 7pm to 7am, dark curtains and air-raid shelters against a German attack that never comes.

## Sub lesson 4 — De bauxietindustrie in ons land
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — War cuts imports and queues lengthen in Paramaribo, yet scarcity sparks local production — small farmers, shoemakers, tailors, lemonade factories — while bauxite booms for the Allied war effort.
