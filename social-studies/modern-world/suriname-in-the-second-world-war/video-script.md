# Video script — Suriname in the Second World War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

As a Dutch colony Suriname is at war; Germans are interned, the German ship Goslar is scuttled in the Suriname river, defences and a militia are organised, imports fail, and bauxite earnings leave the colony able to pay its own way.

The book defines world war, sets out 1939–45, and brings the war home: as a Dutch colony Suriname is at war with Germany.

Governor Kielstra orders blackout from 7pm to 7am, dark curtains and air-raid shelters against a German attack that never comes.

War cuts imports and queues lengthen in Paramaribo, yet scarcity sparks local production — small farmers, shoemakers, tailors, lemonade factories — while bauxite booms for the Allied war effort.
