# Suriname in the Second World War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As a Dutch colony Suriname is at war; Germans are interned, the German ship Goslar is scuttled in the Suriname river, defences and a militia are organised, imports fail, and bauxite earnings leave the colony able to pay its own way.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De Tweede Wereldoorlog in Suriname

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De Tweede Wereldoorlog in Suriname

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book defines world war, sets out 1939–45, and brings the war home: as a Dutch colony Suriname is at war with Germany.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Oorlog met Duitsland

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Oorlog met Duitsland

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Governor Kielstra orders blackout from 7pm to 7am, dark curtains and air-raid shelters against a German attack that never comes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De veiligheid in ons land

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De veiligheid in ons land

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** War cuts imports and queues lengthen in Paramaribo, yet scarcity sparks local production — small farmers, shoemakers, tailors, lemonade factories — while bauxite booms for the Allied war effort.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De bauxietindustrie in ons land

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De bauxietindustrie in ons land
