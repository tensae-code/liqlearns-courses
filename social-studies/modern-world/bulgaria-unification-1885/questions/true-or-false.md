# Bulgaria — the Unification, 1885 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1885 Bulgarians unite the two halves Berlin had split — and defend the union in war.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 15: The Unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История и цивилизации за седми клас (History and Civilizations for the 7th Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 15: The Unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Unification proclaimed at Plovdiv and defended against Serbia at Slivnitsa.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 42: The Unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia and its defence (1885–1886)
