# Fiji's great leaders — Sukuna, Mara and Patel

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ratu Sukuna, first Fijian university graduate and Oxford lawyer, fights with the French Foreign Legion, founds the Native Land Trust Board and mentors independence leaders; Ratu Mara, founding father, is chief minister then first PM; A.D. Patel, farmers' barrister, founds the NFP preaching a fully integrated independent Fiji As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
