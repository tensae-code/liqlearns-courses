# Central America Independence Federation And Liberal Reform — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 26 Independencia de Centroamérica.
**Answer: Napoleon in Spain, Cádiz 1812, the French Revolution and US independence meet criollo–peninsular rivalry and pre-independence movements; independence (15 September 1821) is signed at Guatemala by Gainza, Delgado, Cañas, Aycinena and others as an elite settlement, then comes annexation to Iturbide's Mexico and absolute independence on 1 July 1823 (Chiapas stays with Mexico) (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* (Honduras), Lección 26 Independencia de Centroamérica, printed p. 142.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 26: la Federación y la Reforma Liberal centroamericana.
**Answer: the Federation's 1824 constitution abolishes slavery; Arce presides, Valle dies travelling to take the presidency he won, and Morazán (1835–1839) separates church and State and frees education; a generation later Barrios in Guatemala and Soto and Rosa in Honduras carry the Liberal Reform — codes, the 1880 constitution, secularised cemeteries and church goods, the southern road (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* (Honduras), Lección 26: la Federación y la Reforma Liberal centroamericana, printed p. 145.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: annexation, elections and the federation's advances.
**Answer: the criollo elite annexed Central America to Iturbide's Mexico (5 January 1822) over the fierce resistance of Molina, Barrundia and Córdova; freed 1 July 1823, the federation held 1824 elections (liberal Arce vs the conservatives' Valle), planned an interoceanic canal, abolished slavery, consecrated asylum and limited the death penalty — before dissolving in 1839; Morazán's 1842 testament is printed at length (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Octavo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), annexation, elections and the federation's advances, printed p. 156.**
