# Hands-on — The Industrial Revolution — what changed and why England first

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Home or factory?
Weave or braid something small by hand and time it. Then imagine 20 people with machines in one room doing it all day. Write what changes: speed, cost, where people work.

## Power hunt
List every power at work in your home today — hand, animal, water, wind, fuel, electricity. Mark which ones the year 1750 had, and which one steam added.
