# The Industrial Revolution — what changed and why England first — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give England's three starting advantages.
**Answer:** Wealthy people able to make, buy and use machines and build factories; favourable market conditions; natural resources — coal and iron ore.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did steam beat water power?
**Answer:** Water power ties a mill to a river; steam is usable anywhere and stronger, so factories could stand where coal, workers and markets were.
