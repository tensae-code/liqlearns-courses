# The Industrial Revolution — what changed and why England first — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Industrial Revolution began about —
a) 1450  b) 1650  c) 1750  d) 1900
**Answer: c) 1750** — About 1750, beginning in England.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its second great move was production moving —
a) From factories to homes  b) From home to organised factory level  c) From cities to farms only  d) From England to Spain
**Answer: b) From home to organised factory level** — Home production gave way to the factory.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The revolution spread from England gradually to —
a) Only Africa  b) Other European countries and America  c) Only Asia  d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) Other European countries and America** — Europe and America took it up gradually.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which was NOT one of England's advantages in the book?
a) Wealthy people to build factories  b) Favourable markets  c) Coal and iron ore  d) The world's largest population
**Answer: d) The world's largest population** — The book lists wealth, markets and coal-and-iron resources.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Steam power took over because it was —
a) Usable anywhere and stronger  b) Free  c) Silent  d) Older than water power
**Answer: a) Usable anywhere and stronger** — Water binds a mill to a river; steam works anywhere.
