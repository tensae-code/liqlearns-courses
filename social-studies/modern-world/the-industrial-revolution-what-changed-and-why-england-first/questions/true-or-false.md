# The Industrial Revolution — what changed and why England first — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hand production was slow and costly; machine production was fast and cheap.
**Answer: True** — That contrast is the revolution's first move.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Steam engines boiled water with coal to raise power from compressed steam.
**Answer: True** — The book's own description of the steam engine.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** England had no coal, so steam began elsewhere.
**Answer: False** — England's plentiful cheap coal made her the fitting place.
