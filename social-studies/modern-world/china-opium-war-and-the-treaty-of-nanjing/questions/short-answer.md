# China Opium War And The Treaty Of Nanjing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: National crisis and the people's brave resistance.
**Answer: Lin Zexu vows 'if opium is not ended for one day, I do not return for one day'; Britain attacks in 1840, Guan Tianpei dies at his guns, Sanyuanli villagers surround the British in the rain with hoes and knives, and the Treaty of Nanjing cedes Hong Kong, 21 million silver dollars and five treaty ports — China's first unequal treaty (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Unit 8 Lesson 1: National crisis and the people's brave resistance, printed p. 88.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The two Opium Wars.
**Answer: a British writer admits the opium seller 'corrupts, debases and destroys' his victims; 40 warships come in 1840 anyway, the Arrow incident is manufactured in 1856, and the treaties of Nanjing and Beijing open, cede and indemnify China — the start of modern Chinese history (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 16: The two Opium Wars, printed p. 96.**
