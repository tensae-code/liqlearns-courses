# China Opium War And The Treaty Of Nanjing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lin Zexu vows 'if opium is not ended for one day, I do not return for one day'; Britain attacks in 1840, Guan Tianpei dies at his guns, Sanyuanli villagers surround the British in the rain with hoes and knives, and the Treaty of Nanjing cedes Hong Kong, 21 million silver dollars and five treaty ports — China's first unequal treaty.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in National crisis and the people's brave resistance

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about National crisis and the people's brave resistance

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A British writer admits the opium seller 'corrupts, debases and destroys' his victims; 40 warships come in 1840 anyway, the Arrow incident is manufactured in 1856, and the treaties of Nanjing and Beijing open, cede and indemnify China — the start of modern Chinese history.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The two Opium Wars

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The two Opium Wars
