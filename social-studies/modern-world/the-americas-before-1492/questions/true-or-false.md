# The Americas before 1492 — three worlds on the eve of contact — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Americas were empty land waiting to be found.
**Answer: False** — They were lived-in worlds with diverse Native societies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** West African slavery before the Atlantic trade was already identical to the later Atlantic system.
**Answer: False** — It existed in forms of its own; the Atlantic trade transformed it beyond recognition.
