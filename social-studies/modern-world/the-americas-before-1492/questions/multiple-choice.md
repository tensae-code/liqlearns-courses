# The Americas before 1492 — three worlds on the eve of contact — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before 1492, the Americas were —
a) Empty land with no peoples  b) Full of diverse Native societies  c) Ruled by Spain
**Answer: b) Full of diverse Native societies** — the Americas held many different Native societies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Europe on the eve of contact is described as —
a) Unchanging for a thousand years  b) On the brink of change  c) Already ruling the Americas
**Answer: b) On the brink of change** — Europe stood on the brink of change.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In West Africa before the Atlantic trade, slavery —
a) Did not exist at all  b) Already existed in forms of its own  c) Was already the Atlantic system
**Answer: b) Already existed in forms of its own** — slavery already existed in forms the Atlantic trade would transform.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Atlantic trade would change slavery by —
a) Ending it at once  b) Transforming it beyond recognition  c) Leaving it exactly as it was
**Answer: b) Transforming it beyond recognition** — the trade transformed slavery beyond recognition.
