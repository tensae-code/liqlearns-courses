# Video script — Colombia — the Frente Nacional and the origins of the FARC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Liberals and conservatives agree to alternate and share power in the Frente Nacional, political culture fails to democratise, and in 1964 the FARC are born.

Two central agreements set the rules — liberals and conservatives alternate and share power, political culture fails to democratise, and in 1964, with the 14 June operation, the FARC are born.

Fear of a US invasion drives the missile crisis; the Cuban revolution's influence breeds new armed groups in Colombia, and the Frente Nacional's own problems — corruption among the gravest — are named.
