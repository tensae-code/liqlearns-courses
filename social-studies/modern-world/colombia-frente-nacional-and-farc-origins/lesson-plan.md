# Lesson plan — Colombia — the Frente Nacional and the origins of the FARC

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Colombia — the Frente Nacional and the origins of the FARC
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Colombia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** liberals and conservatives agree to alternate and share power in the Frente Nacional, political culture fails to democratise, and in 1964 the FARC are born
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 12: The Frente Nacional** — two central agreements set the rules — liberals and conservatives alternate and share power, political culture fails to democratise, and in 1964, with the 14 June operation, the FARC are born
2. **Temas 21–23: Latin America and the Cold War / The Cold War in Colombia / The Frente Nacional** — fear of a US invasion drives the missile crisis; the Cuban revolution's influence breeds new armed groups in Colombia, and the Frente Nacional's own problems — corruption among the gravest — are named

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
