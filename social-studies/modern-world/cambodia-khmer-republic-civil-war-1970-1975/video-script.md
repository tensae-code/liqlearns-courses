# Video script — Cambodia — the Khmer Republic and civil war, 1970–1975

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the 1970 coup removes Sihanouk, the republic is proclaimed, and five years of civil war tear the country apart.

the March 1970 coup removes Sihanouk; the republic fights a losing civil war — the lesson opens on the funeral of its soldiers (photo, 23 March 1973).

crisis builds through Samlaut (1967) and a 1,500-million-riel deficit (1969); Lon Nol's "national salvation government" (August 1969) precedes the 18 March 1970 vote that removes Sihanouk while he is in Moscow and Beijing.
