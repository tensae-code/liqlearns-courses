# Lesson plan — Habibullah and Amanullah — independence and reform

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Habibullah and Amanullah — independence and reform
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Afghanistan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Habibullah keeps Afghanistan neutral and modernising on the eve of the world war
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 22: What do you know of Amir Habibullah Khan's reign?** — Habibullah keeps Afghanistan neutral and modernising on the eve of the world war
2. **Lesson 23: How did the Afghans win their independence from the British?** — in 1919 the people fight on three fronts — Kandahar under Sardar Abdul Qudus Khan, and Generals Saleh Muhammad and Nadir Khan — against a financially and technically superior colonial power
3. **Lesson 24: What were the fruits of Amanullah Khan's journeys?** — the king who won independence tours the world and brings home the plans of a modernising reign
4. **Lesson 15: The reign of Amir Habibullah Khan (1901–1919)** — Habibullah keeps neutrality in the world war and is assassinated on its eve of independence
5. **Chapter 2 (From the Restoration of Independence to the End of the Republic, 1919–1978), Lesson 17: The kingship of Amir Amanullah Khan and his role in restoring the country's independence (February 1919 – January 1929)** — Amanullah declares full independence and wins it in the 1919 war
6. **Lesson 18: The restoration of independence and the three battlefronts** — the people fight on three fronts — Kandahar, Paktia and the north — against the colonial power
7. **Lesson 19: Reforms and reforming (1921–1928)** — constitutions, schools, codes and unveiled progress remake the state in seven years
8. **Lesson 20: King Amanullah Khan Ghazi's European journey, 1927 (1306 SH)** — the king tours Europe's capitals and brings home machines, treaties and bigger plans
