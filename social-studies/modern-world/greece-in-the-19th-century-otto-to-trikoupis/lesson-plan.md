## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit D, ch. 1 The reign of Otto — Ioannis Kolettis** — Bavarian teenager Otto arrives as king, Athens becomes capital, and Kolettis's Great Idea points the little kingdom at all the unredeemed Greeks
2. **Unit D, ch. 2 The reign of George I — Charilaos Trikoupis** — a Danish prince takes the throne, the Ionian Islands are gained, and Trikoupis modernises with railways, the Corinth Canal and chronic debt
3. **Unit D, ch. 3 Revolutionary movements in Macedonia and Crete** — Cretans rise again and again for union, and Macedonia's Greeks arm as the Balkan nations circle the dying empire
4. **Unit D, ch. 4 Greece at the end of the 19th century** — Thessaly gained, then bankruptcy in 1893 — a small, poor, proud state of schools, emigrants and irredentist dreams
5. **Unit D exercise dossier (Greece in the 19th century)** — events sorted between the 1844 and 1864 constitutions and the Ionian union, cities matched to their cultural institutions
6. **§18 From Otto's arrival (1833) to 3 September 1843** — a Bavarian court rules without a constitution until soldiers and people in the square demand one
7. **§19 From 3 September 1843 to Otto's expulsion (1862)** — constitutional monarchy, the Crimean humiliation at Piraeus, and a dynasty that never learns Greek politics
8. **§20 From Otto's expulsion (1862) to the Goudi movement (1909)** — George I, the Ionian and Thessalian gains, Trikoupis's modernisation, bankruptcy and the 1897 disaster
9. **§23 Greek economy and society in the 19th century** — currant booms and busts, emigration to America, and a peasant country slowly building towns and schools
