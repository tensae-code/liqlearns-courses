## Sub lesson 1 — 5.1 Context: policing the nation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — By the 1870s Britain is policed by many separate county and borough forces run by watch committees, with the Home Secretary controlling only London

## Sub lesson 2 — 5.2 The local context of Whitechapel
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Whitechapel's 30,000 people live with overcrowding, homelessness, workhouses and gang-ruled streets; poverty itself breeds the district's crime

## Sub lesson 3 — 5.3 Tensions in Whitechapel
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Irish navvies and Jewish Eastern European immigrants crowd into the East End; friction, Jewish cultural change and fear of anarchism strain the district

## Sub lesson 4 — 5.4 Police organisation in Whitechapel
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — H Division's roughly 500 beat constables and 15 detectives patrol set beats under sergeants and inspectors, stretched thin over a hostile district

## Sub lesson 5 — 5.5 Investigative policing in Whitechapel
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — In 1888 five women are murdered by 'Jack the Ripper'; press pressure, rivalry with the City police and primitive detective methods fail to catch him, and the case changes investigation itself
