# Video script — The United States and Latin America — Monroe to the Maine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Monroe warns Europe off, the United States takes Mexico's north in 1846, the Platt Amendment binds Cuba, and the Maine's explosion gives the excuse for war on Spain.

Monroe's discourse warns Europe off; on 13 May 1846 Congress declares war on Mexico and takes Arizona, California, Nevada and New Mexico; and the Maine's explosion gives the excuse for war on Spain.

The Platt Amendment's articles let Washington bind Cuba's foreign agreements, the template of early-century intervention.
