# The United States and Latin America — Monroe to the Maine

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Monroe warns Europe off, the United States takes Mexico's north in 1846, the Platt Amendment binds Cuba, and the Maine's explosion gives the excuse for war on Spain. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
