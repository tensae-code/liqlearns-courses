# Indonesia — forming the Republic, 1945 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the days after the Proclamation the PPKI writes a constitution, elects Sukarno and Hatta, and builds ministries and regions — a state assembled while the Japanese still hold the arms depots.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 1.A Pembentukan Negara dan Pemerintahan Republik Indonesia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII (Kurikulum Merdeka)* is about Indonesia — diplomacy and guerrilla war, 1946–1949.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 1.A Pembentukan Negara dan Pemerintahan Republik Indonesia
