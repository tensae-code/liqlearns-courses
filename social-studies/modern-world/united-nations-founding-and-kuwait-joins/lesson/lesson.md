# The United Nations is founded — and Kuwait joins

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: After the Second World War the world judged the League of Nations a failure and built the United Nations instead; Kuwait joined in 1963, and its young foreign minister raised its flag there himself. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The United Nations — founding, main organs, aims

The second world war ends in 1945, the world judges the league of nations (1920) a failure, and builds the un instead; kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister sheikh sabah al ahmad al jaber al sabah raises kuwait's flag at the organisation.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The United Nations organisation

Out of the second war's ruin the victors build the united nations to keep the peace their league had failed to keep.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: the united nations — founding, main organs, aims; the united nations organisation. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
