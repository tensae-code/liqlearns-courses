# Hands-on — The United Nations is founded — and Kuwait joins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw Kuwait's flag-raising at the UN in 1963 and caption who raised it.

## Task 2
Timeline: 1920 League, 1945 UN founded, 1963 Kuwait joins — one line each.

## Task 3
Write the UN's founding promise in one sentence of your own.

## Task 4
Compare League and UN in a two-row table: born when, judged how.
