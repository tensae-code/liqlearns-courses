# Lesson plan — The United Nations is founded — and Kuwait joins

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The United Nations is founded — and Kuwait joins
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Kuwait, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the Second World War the world judged the League of Nations a failure and built the United Nations instead; Kuwait joined in 1963, and its young foreign minister raised its flag there himself.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The United Nations — founding, main organs, aims** — the Second World War ends in 1945, the world judges the League of Nations (1920) a failure, and builds the UN instead; Kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raises Kuwait's flag at the organisation (Unit 3 Lesson 2: The United Nations — founding, main organs, aims)
2. **The United Nations organisation** — out of the second war's ruin the victors build the United Nations to keep the peace their League had failed to keep (Chapter 6: The United Nations organisation)
