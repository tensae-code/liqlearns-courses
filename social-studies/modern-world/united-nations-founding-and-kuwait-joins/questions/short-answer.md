# The United Nations is founded — and Kuwait joins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who raised Kuwait's flag at the UN?
**Answer: Its foreign minister, Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah — later Amir (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why was the UN built instead of keeping the League?
**Answer: The League of Nations (1920) was judged a failure after the Second World War.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What failure stands behind the UN's founding?
**Answer: The League's failure to keep the peace (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When does the Grade 12 book place the UN in its story?
**Answer: At the end of the Second World War chapter — the war's ruin produces the UN.**
