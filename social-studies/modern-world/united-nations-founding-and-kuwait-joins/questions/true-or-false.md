# The United Nations is founded — and Kuwait joins — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait was a founding member of the UN in 1945.
**Answer: False** — Kuwait joins in 1963

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The UN replaced a League judged a failure.
**Answer: True** — the world judges the League a failure and builds the UN

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The League of Nations kept the peace, so no UN was needed.
**Answer: False** — the League failed to keep it; the UN was built instead

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The UN is the Second World War's child.
**Answer: True** — out of the second war's ruin the victors build it
