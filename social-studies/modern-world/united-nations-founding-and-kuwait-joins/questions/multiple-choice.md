# The United Nations is founded — and Kuwait joins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The UN was built after —
a) the Second World War ended in 1945  b) the First World War  c) the Gulf War
**Answer: a) the Second World War ended in 1945** — the Second World War ends in 1945 and the UN is built

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The League of Nations (1920) was judged —
a) a failure  b) a success  c) too young
**Answer: a) a failure** — the world judges the League of Nations a failure

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait joined the UN in —
a) 1963  b) 1945  c) 1961
**Answer: a) 1963** — Kuwait joins in 1963

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The UN was built to —
a) keep the peace the League had failed to keep  b) divide colonies  c) tax world trade
**Answer: a) keep the peace the League had failed to keep** — to keep the peace their League had failed to keep

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** It was built out of —
a) the second war's ruin  b) the first war's ruin  c) a trade fair
**Answer: a) the second war's ruin** — out of the second war's ruin

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The builders were —
a) the victors of the Second World War  b) the Gulf states  c) the League's staff
**Answer: a) the victors of the Second World War** — the victors build the United Nations
