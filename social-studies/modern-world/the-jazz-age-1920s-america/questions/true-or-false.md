# The Jazz Age — 1920s America — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Klan revived during the 1920s.
**Answer: True** — The Klan's revival answers the new generation with backlash.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1920s had prosperity without any cultural conflict.
**Answer: False** — Backlash — Prohibition, the Klan, Scopes — ran through the same decade.
