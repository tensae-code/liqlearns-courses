# The Jazz Age — 1920s America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Jazz Age is the —
a) 1860s  b) 1920s  c) 1950s
**Answer: b) 1920s** — The Jazz Age: Redefining the Nation, 1919–1929.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mass-produced novelties of the boom included —
a) Cars, radio and movies  b) Steam engines only  c) Telegraphs only
**Answer: a) Cars, radio and movies** — prosperity mass-produces cars, radio and movies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Prohibition —
a) Encouraged drinking  b) Banned drink  c) Lasted one week
**Answer: b) Banned drink** — Prohibition banned drink.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Scopes trial centred on —
a) Evolution and the old faith  b) The tariff  c) Prohibition repeal
**Answer: a) Evolution and the old faith** — the Scopes trial answers with backlash over evolution.
