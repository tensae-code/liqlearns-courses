# Lesson plan — Costa Rica — the welfare state, 1950–1980

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica — the welfare state, 1950–1980
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Costa Rica, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A welfare (benefactor) state shapes Costa Rican society for three decades — until its own crisis gathers (the section even carries a "Crisis del Estado Benefactor" crossword) A second Costa Rican book tells the same story its own way, and both tellings stand here as the lesson's two parts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **El Estado benefactor (CONED Grade 11 anthology)** — A welfare (benefactor) state shapes Costa Rican society for three decades — until its own crisis gathers (the section even carries a "Crisis del Estado Benefactor" crossword) (Unidad II, Tema 2-B: El Estado benefactor)
2. **El Estado Benefactor y la sociedad costarricense (1950–1980) (Porras y Gamboa)** — The welfare state — state banking, the Estado empresario (CODESA), health and education — builds a broad middle class, with urban problems and the Guanacaste land seizures at its edges (Unidad 2, B: El Estado Benefactor y la sociedad costarricense (1950–1980))
