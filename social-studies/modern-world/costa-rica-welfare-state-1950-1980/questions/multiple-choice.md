# Costa Rica — the welfare state, 1950–1980 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) El Estado benefactor (CONED Grade 11 anthology)  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all

**Answer: a) Unidad II, Tema 2-B: El Estado benefactor** — the book's own heading for this part.
