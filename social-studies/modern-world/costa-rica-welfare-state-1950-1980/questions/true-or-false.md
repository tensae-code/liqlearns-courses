# Costa Rica — the welfare state, 1950–1980 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A welfare (benefactor) state shapes Costa Rican society for three decades — until its own crisis gathers (the section even carries a "Crisis del Estado Benefactor" crossword)
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Unidad II, Tema 2-B: El Estado benefactor).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The welfare state — state banking, the Estado empresario (CODESA), health and education — builds a broad middle class, with urban problems and the Guanacaste land seizures at its edges
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Unidad 2, B: El Estado Benefactor y la sociedad costarricense (1950–1980)).
