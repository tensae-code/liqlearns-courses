# Video script — Iran — the imposed war, 1980–88

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iran's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saddam invades in 1980 expecting a short war; eight years of trenches, missiles and chemical attacks follow before the ceasefire.

the war retold as defence of the revolution itself — fronts, cities under missiles, and the accepted ceasefire.

the port city falls to Iraq's invasion and endures occupation — the war made personal through one city's suffering.

in 1982 Iranian forces liberate Khorramshahr, and the city's return becomes the war's most beloved story.
