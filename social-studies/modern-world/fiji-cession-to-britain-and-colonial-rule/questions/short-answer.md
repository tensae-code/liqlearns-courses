**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Chapter 2: Significance of Cession; the Commission; the Deed of Cession.
**Answer: chaotic Fiji, a collapsing Cakobau government and thriving blackbirding force Gladstone's Britain to send Goodenough and Layard (1873); the commissioners go beyond their brief, win the chiefs round, and Sir Hercules Robinson takes an unconditional cession signed at Levuka on 10 October 1874, Cakobau pensioned as Vunivalu and Ma'afu recognised as Tui Lau (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
