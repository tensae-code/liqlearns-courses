# Hands-on — Zahir Shah — forty years

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Zahir Shah — forty years — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Lesson 27: Did you know Muhammad Zahir Shah reigned forty years?, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
