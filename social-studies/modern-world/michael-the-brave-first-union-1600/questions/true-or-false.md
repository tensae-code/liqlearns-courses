# Michael The Brave First Union 1600 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Michael the Brave brings Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule in 1600; his seal joins the eagle, the aurochs head and the Transylvanian lions.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 13: Întâiul unificator de țară: Mihai Viteazul

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 13: Întâiul unificator de țară: Mihai Viteazul

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Orphan boy Patrașcu marches with Michael's army over the Carpathians and rests in a village on the Oituz, seeing the union as a soldier's road.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bucuria lecturii: Oastea Unirii

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bucuria lecturii: Oastea Unirii

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Michael buys his throne from the Turks, turns on them, and enters Alba Iulia on 1 November 1599 to hold Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unitatea III, Lecția 4: Războiul antiotoman sub conducerea lui Mihai Viteazul. Unirea Țărilor Române

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Istoria românilor şi universală: Manual pentru clasa a VI-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unitatea III, Lecția 4: Războiul antiotoman sub conducerea lui Mihai Viteazul. Unirea Țărilor Române
