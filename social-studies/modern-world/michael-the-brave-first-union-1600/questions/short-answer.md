# Michael The Brave First Union 1600 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 13: Întâiul unificator de țară: Mihai Viteazul.
**Answer: Michael the Brave brings Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule in 1600; his seal joins the eagle, the aurochs head and the Transylvanian lions (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* (Moldova), Tema 13: Întâiul unificator de țară: Mihai Viteazul, printed p. 34.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bucuria lecturii: Oastea Unirii.
**Answer: orphan boy Patrașcu marches with Michael's army over the Carpathians and rests in a village on the Oituz, seeing the union as a soldier's road (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* (Moldova), Bucuria lecturii: Oastea Unirii, printed p. 46.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea III, Lecția 4: Războiul antiotoman sub conducerea lui Mihai Viteazul. Unirea Țărilor Române.
**Answer: Michael buys his throne from the Turks, turns on them, and enters Alba Iulia on 1 November 1599 to hold Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
