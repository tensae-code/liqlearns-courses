# Hands-on — Bulgaria From Autonomy To Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bulgaria From Autonomy To Independence — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Theme 4: Bulgaria from autonomous principality to independent state — Alexander of Battenberg, the Stambolov era, Sofia's tram and electric light, and the declaration of independence, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
