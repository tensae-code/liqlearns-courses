# Southeast Asia Under Western Colonialism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the book's table runs country by country — Portugal takes Malacca in 1511 (its ship, the Flor do Mar, is now a museum there) and the eastern Indonesian islands; Spain seizes most of the Philippines by the mid-16th century and rules 350 years; the Dutch complete the conquest of Indonesia by the mid-19th century; Britain, France and the Netherlands contest Malaya, Burma and Indochina; Siam is entered by traders in the 16th century and pressed only after Britain has taken India and parts of Malaya and Burma.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 4, how the Western powers entered Southeast Asia
