# The Eastern Question — nationalism in the Balkans, empires in collision — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the three forces that together created the Eastern Question.
**Answer:** Rising nationalist feeling among the Balkan peoples; the declining Ottoman (Turkish) Empire; and the divergent interests of the Great Powers in the Near East.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the crises keep coming instead of being settled?
**Answer:** Because economic and social change, international rivalry and unsolved problems combined — any settlement threatened some great power's interests, so the problems were left to fester into crisis after crisis.
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: why do the Balkan Wars matter beyond the Balkans?
**Answer: They destroyed Ottoman power in Europe, proved Balkan nationalism would fight, and set Austria-Hungary and Russia on opposite sides of the Serbian question — the fuse toward the First World War.**
