# The Eastern Question — nationalism in the Balkans, empires in collision — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Eastern Question arose in part from rising nationalism among —
a) The Balkan peoples  b) The Irish  c) The Italians only  d) The Americans
**Answer: a) The Balkan peoples** — Balkan peoples' nationalism is the first force named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The declining empire at the question's centre was —
a) The Roman Empire  b) The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire  c) The Austrian Empire only  d) The Persian Empire
**Answer: b) The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire** — The declining Ottoman Empire.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The third force in the Eastern Question was —
a) The Catholic Church  b) The divergent interests of the Great Powers  c) The Zollverein  d) The slave trade
**Answer: b) The divergent interests of the Great Powers** — The Great Powers' divergent interests in the Near East.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Eastern Question produced —
a) One quick settlement  b) A succession of Balkan crises  c) Balkan unity under the Ottomans  d) The unification of Germany
**Answer: b) A succession of Balkan crises** — A succession of crises with serious consequences for all Europe.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Balkan problems stayed unsolved partly because —
a) Nobody knew the region  b) Every solution alarmed one power or another amid international rivalry  c) The peoples refused states  d) The Ottomans grew stronger
**Answer: b) Every solution alarmed one power or another amid international rivalry** — Rivalry and unsolved problems combined; solutions threatened rivals.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The consequences of the Balkan crises were serious for —
a) The Balkans only  b) All Europe  c) Asia only  d) No one
**Answer: b) All Europe** — The book stresses consequences for all Europe.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In the First Balkan War (1912), the Balkan League attacked —
a) Austria-Hungary  b) The Ottoman Empire  c) Russia  d) Italy
**Answer: b) The Ottoman Empire — The League stripped the Ottomans of nearly all their European territory.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The Second Balkan War (1913) was fought —
a) Between the Balkan states themselves, Bulgaria against its former allies  b) Between Britain and France  c) Between Russia and Japan  d) Between Spain and Portugal
**Answer: a) Between the Balkan states themselves, Bulgaria against its former allies — The victors fell out over the spoils.**
