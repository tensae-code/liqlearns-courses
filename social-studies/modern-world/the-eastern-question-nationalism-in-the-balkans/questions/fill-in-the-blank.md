# The Eastern Question — nationalism in the Balkans, empires in collision — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The declining empire in the Eastern Question was the ____ Empire.
**Answer: Ottoman**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The peoples rising in the south-east of Europe were the ____ peoples.
**Answer: Balkan**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Eastern Question produced a succession of Balkan ____.
**Answer: crises**
