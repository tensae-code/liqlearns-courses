# The Eastern Question — nationalism in the Balkans, empires in collision — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ottoman Empire was growing stronger throughout the Eastern Question period.
**Answer: False** — It was declining — that decline is one of the three forces.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Great Powers all wanted the same outcome in the Balkans.
**Answer: False** — Their interests diverged — each feared a rival's gain.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Balkan nationalism worked against the Eastern Question's settlement.
**Answer: True** — Rising nationalism was one of the forces that made the question unanswerable.
**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The Grade 12 book treats the Balkan Wars of 1912–13 as the fuse toward 1914.
**Answer: True**
