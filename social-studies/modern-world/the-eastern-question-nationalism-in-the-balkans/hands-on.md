# Hands-on — The Eastern Question — nationalism in the Balkans, empires in collision

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-force diagram
Draw three arrows meeting at one point labelled 'Eastern Question': Balkan nationalism, Ottoman decline, Great Power interests. Under each arrow, one line of explanation.

## The watchers
List the Great Powers watching the Balkans. For each, write one sentence: what would it gain, and what would it fear, if the Ottoman Empire fell tomorrow?
