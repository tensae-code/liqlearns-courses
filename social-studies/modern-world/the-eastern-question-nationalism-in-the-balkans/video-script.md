# Video script — The Eastern Question — nationalism in the Balkans, empires in collision


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on the Balkans — mountains, small peoples, one old empire over them all.

The empire's colour fades: the Ottoman power is declining.

Inside it, new colours glow — Serbs, Greeks, Bulgars — peoples dreaming of their own states.

Around the edges, giants lean in: Russia, Austria, Britain, France — each wanting the falling pieces.

Crisis follows crisis. Nobody solves it. Everybody arms.

This is the Eastern Question — and all Europe will feel its answer.

[Grade 12 depth: The Balkan Wars, 1912–13 — the fuse toward 1914 — added scene. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
