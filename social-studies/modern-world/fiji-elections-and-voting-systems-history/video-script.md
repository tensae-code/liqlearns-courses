# Video script — Fiji's elections and voting systems — 1972, 1977 and 2006

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence; the Alliance wins the 1972 election (52 seats) and the split, multi-party 1977 elections, and pupils research how the 2006 Alternative Voting system worked and what changed after it.
