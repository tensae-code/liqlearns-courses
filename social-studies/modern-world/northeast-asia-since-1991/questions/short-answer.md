# Northeast Asia Since 1991 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 19, Northeast Asia since 1991: Japan, Korea, China.
**Answer: the World Bank figures in the opener give China, Japan and Korea alone a fifth of world GDP and a quarter of its people; Japan stagnates through alternating recession and weak growth yet stays a great economic-financial centre (GDP $3,580 billion in 1991, $5,010 billion in 2021; life expectancy near 85) until China passes it in 2010 after four decades as the world's second economy; South Korea, one of the four Asian "dragons", shifts decisively to high-tech industry — Samsung, LG, Hyundai — multiplying GDP some 5.5 times (1991–2021) to a top-ten ranking with per-capita income near $35,000; China sets the goal of a "socialist market economy", grows for thirty years at the world's highest rate to $17,820 billion (about 18% of world GDP in 2021), builds growth-pole regions from Pudong to Chengdu, and from 2013 pursues the Belt and Road initiative — though the book notes low growth quality, pollution and a wide rich–poor gap (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
