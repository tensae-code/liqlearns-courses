## Sub lesson 1 — The system of Ottoman rule
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the machine itself — at the centre the Caliph-Sultan, his Grand Vizier running the diwan, and the Shaykh al-Islam whose fatwas test the legality of the Sultan's own decisions (a caliphate that lasts until Ataturk abolishes it in 1924); below, thirty-two wilayas, fifteen of them Arab, each under a wali appointed for a single year, watched by a diwan of notables, with a defterdar answering for the taxes straight to Istanbul, a garrison, and local strongmen left to run the interior; and an army of three kinds — feudal cavalry paid in land, the Janissaries raised from Christian boys taken as a blood tax, and local levies
