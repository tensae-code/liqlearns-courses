# The system of Ottoman rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The system of Ottoman rule.
**Answer: the machine itself — at the centre the Caliph-Sultan, his Grand Vizier running the diwan, and the Shaykh al-Islam whose fatwas test the legality of the Sultan's own decisions (a caliphate that lasts until Ataturk abolishes it in 1924); below, thirty-two wilayas, fifteen of them Arab, each under a wali appointed for a single year, watched by a diwan of notables, with a defterdar answering for the taxes straight to Istanbul, a garrison, and local strongmen left to run the interior; and an army of three kinds — feudal cavalry paid in land, the Janissaries raised from Christian boys taken as a blood tax, and local levies (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الحديث والمعاصر للصف التاسع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (Modern and Contemporary History of the Arabs for Grade 9, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), The system of Ottoman rule, printed p. 52.**
