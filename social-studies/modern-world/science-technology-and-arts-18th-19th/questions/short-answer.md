# Science Technology And Arts 18th 19th — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 13, technique, literature and art remake the world.
**Answer: Fulton's steamship (1807) and more than 500 steamers in English ports by 1836, the blast furnace and new steel and aluminium, electricity's motors, telephone and radio, the internal combustion engine behind cars and aircraft; literature gives Balzac's Human Comedy, Hugo's Les Misérables (1862, "a hymn to love" for the utterly poor), Tolstoy's War and Peace, Pushkin, Goethe, Dickens; music runs from Bach and Mozart to the romantic storm of Beethoven, Chopin and Tchaikovsky, painting from David and Delacroix to Goya, Levitan and Van Gogh — an art that, the book says, condemns the age's injustices and wakes the poor to fight for a free and happy life (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 13, technique, literature and art remake the world, printed p. 59.**
