## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 13, technique, literature and art remake the world
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fulton's steamship (1807) and more than 500 steamers in English ports by 1836, the blast furnace and new steel and aluminium, electricity's motors, telephone and radio, the internal combustion engine behind cars and aircraft; literature gives Balzac's Human Comedy, Hugo's Les Misérables (1862, "a hymn to love" for the utterly poor), Tolstoy's War and Peace, Pushkin, Goethe, Dickens; music runs from Bach and Mozart to the romantic storm of Beethoven, Chopin and Tchaikovsky, painting from David and Delacroix to Goya, Levitan and Van Gogh — an art that, the book says, condemns the age's injustices and wakes the poor to fight for a free and happy life
