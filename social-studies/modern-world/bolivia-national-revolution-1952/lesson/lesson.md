# Bolivia — the National Revolution of 1952

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: On 9–10 April 1952 an armed mobilisation of soldiers and worker leaders defeats the army; worker control (control obrero) follows in the mines, and the Agrarian Reform remakes the countryside, though spontaneously and insufficiently. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — La Revolución Nacional de 1952

On 9–10 April 1952 an armed mobilisation of soldiers and worker leaders defeats the army; worker control (control obrero) follows in the mines, and the Agrarian Reform remakes the countryside, though spontaneously and insufficiently (La Revolución Nacional de 1952)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
