# Lesson plan — Bolivia — the National Revolution of 1952

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Bolivia — the National Revolution of 1952
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Bolivia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On 9–10 April 1952 an armed mobilisation of soldiers and worker leaders defeats the army; worker control (control obrero) follows in the mines, and the Agrarian Reform remakes the countryside, though spontaneously and insufficiently.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **La Revolución Nacional de 1952** — On 9–10 April 1952 an armed mobilisation of soldiers and worker leaders defeats the army; worker control (control obrero) follows in the mines, and the Agrarian Reform remakes the countryside, though spontaneously and insufficiently.
2. **El Voto Universal y el MNR** — The revolution's deepest change is universal suffrage, first exercised in the 1956 elections, while the MNR's two wings pull the National Revolution into internal conflict.
3. **Año 6: La Revolución Nacional** — Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text.
4. **Año 6: Reorientación de la Revolución Nacional** — The National Revolution is reoriented — stabilisation, the eastern development turn and the road to 1964.
