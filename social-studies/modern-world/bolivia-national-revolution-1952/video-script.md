# Video script — Bolivia — the National Revolution of 1952

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

On 9–10 April 1952 an armed mobilisation of soldiers and worker leaders defeats the army; worker control (control obrero) follows in the mines, and the Agrarian Reform remakes the countryside, though spontaneously and insufficiently.

The revolution's deepest change is universal suffrage, first exercised in the 1956 elections, while the MNR's two wings pull the National Revolution into internal conflict.

Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text.

The National Revolution is reoriented — stabilisation, the eastern development turn and the road to 1964.
