# Hands-on — Bolivia — the National Revolution of 1952

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bolivia — the National Revolution of 1952 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, La Revolución Nacional de 1952, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
