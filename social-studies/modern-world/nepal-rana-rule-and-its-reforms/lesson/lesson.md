# Nepal — Rana rule and its reforms

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Jung Bahadur seizes power in 1903 BS and makes the premiership hereditary for 104 years; the book weighs the rule's dark side (no rights, caste evils, sati and slavery, luxury imports) against its reforms (Muluki Ain, Bir Hospital and library, Chandra Shumsher abolishing sati 1977 BS and slavery 1981 BS, Trichandra College 1975 BS) As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
