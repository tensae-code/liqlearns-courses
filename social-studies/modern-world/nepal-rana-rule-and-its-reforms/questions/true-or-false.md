# Nepal — Rana rule and its reforms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jung Bahadur seizes power in 1903 BS and makes the premiership hereditary for 104 years; the book weighs the rule's dark side (no rights, caste evils, sati and slavery, luxury imports) against its reforms (Muluki Ain,…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Rana Rule — Political, Social and Economic Condition

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 6* is about National Luminary Gautam Buddha.
**Answer: False** — it is about Rana Rule — Political, Social and Economic Condition

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Jang Bahadur seizes power in the Kot massacre and founds a hereditary prime-ministership that lasts 104 years; the lessons trace its rigid class order, its few reforms and heavy extraction, and the causes — education,…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Rise of Rana Rule and the Causes of Its End; Rana Rule's Economic and Social Effects

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan, Class 9* is about Major Political Events from 2046 BS to 2063 BS.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Rise of Rana Rule and the Causes of Its End; Rana Rule's Economic and Social Effects

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** after the unifier, power passes through Shah kings, Bhimsen Thapa's long prime-ministership and the court struggles that end in Jang Bahadur's Kot massacre and hereditary Rana rule
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in State Power after Unification

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* is about My Neighbours Then and Now.
**Answer: False** — it is about State Power after Unification

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** the modern centuries are surveyed whole — an agrarian economy and its slow change, the Muluki Ain legal code, and the culture and art of the Shah and Rana centuries
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Modern Period — Economy, Law, Culture and Art Skill

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* is about My Neighbour's Occupation Before and Now.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Modern Period — Economy, Law, Culture and Art Skill
