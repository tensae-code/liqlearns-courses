# Nepal — Rana rule and its reforms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Rana Rule — Political, Social and Economic Condition.
**Answer: Jung Bahadur seizes power in 1903 BS and makes the premiership hereditary for 104 years; the book weighs the rule's dark side (no rights, caste evils, sati and slavery, luxury imports) against its reforms (Muluki Ain, Bir Hospital and library, Chandra Shumsher abolishing sati 1977 BS and slavery 1981 BS, Trichandra College 1975 BS) (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 6* (Grade 6), Unit 5 Lesson 4: Rana Rule — Political, Social and Economic Condition, printed p. 99.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The Rise of Rana Rule and the Causes of Its End; Rana Rule's Economic and Social Effects.
**Answer: Jang Bahadur seizes power in the Kot massacre and founds a hereditary prime-ministership that lasts 104 years; the lessons trace its rigid class order, its few reforms and heavy extraction, and the causes — education, martyrs, parties and the 2007 BS revolution — that ended it (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan, Class 9* (Grade 9), Unit 7 Lessons 6–7: The Rise of Rana Rule and the Causes of Its End; Rana Rule's Economic and Social Effects, printed pp. 188, 193.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: State Power after Unification.
**Answer: after the unifier, power passes through Shah kings, Bhimsen Thapa's long prime-ministership and the court struggles that end in Jang Bahadur's Kot massacre and hereditary Rana rule (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* (Grade 12), Unit 6 Lesson 7: State Power after Unification, printed p. 180.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: The Modern Period — Economy, Law, Culture and Art Skill.
**Answer: the modern centuries are surveyed whole — an agrarian economy and its slow change, the Muluki Ain legal code, and the culture and art of the Shah and Rana centuries (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* (Grade 12), Unit 6 Lesson 8: The Modern Period — Economy, Law, Culture and Art Skill, printed p. 187.**
