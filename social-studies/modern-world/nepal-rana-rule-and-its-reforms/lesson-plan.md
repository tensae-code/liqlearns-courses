# Lesson plan — Nepal — Rana rule and its reforms

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Nepal — Rana rule and its reforms
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Nepal, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Jung Bahadur seizes power in 1903 BS and makes the premiership hereditary for 104 years; the book weighs the rule's dark side (no rights, caste evils, sati and slavery, luxury imports) against its reforms (Muluki Ain, Bir Hospital and library, Chandra Shumsher abolishing sati 1977 BS and slavery 1981 BS, Trichandra College 1975 BS)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Rana Rule — Political, Social and Economic Condition (Grade 6)** — Jung Bahadur seizes power in 1903 BS and makes the premiership hereditary for 104 years; the book weighs the rule's dark side (no rights, caste evils, sati and slavery, luxury imports) against its…
2. **The Rise of Rana Rule and the Causes of Its End; Rana Rule's Economic and Social Effects (Grade 9)** — Jang Bahadur seizes power in the Kot massacre and founds a hereditary prime-ministership that lasts 104 years; the lessons trace its rigid class order, its few reforms and heavy extraction, and the…
3. **State Power after Unification (Grade 12)** — after the unifier, power passes through Shah kings, Bhimsen Thapa's long prime-ministership and the court struggles that end in Jang Bahadur's Kot massacre and hereditary Rana rule
4. **The Modern Period — Economy, Law, Culture and Art Skill (Grade 12)** — the modern centuries are surveyed whole — an agrarian economy and its slow change, the Muluki Ain legal code, and the culture and art of the Shah and Rana centuries

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
