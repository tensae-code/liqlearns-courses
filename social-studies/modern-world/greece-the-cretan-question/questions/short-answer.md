# Greece The Cretan Question — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §21 The Cretan question (1821–1905).
**Answer: Crete rises in 1821, 1866, 1878 and 1897, wins autonomy under Prince George, and waits for union (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία Γ΄ Γυμνασίου — Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Modern and Contemporary History, Grade 9 Gymnasium)* (Greece), §21 The Cretan question (1821–1905), printed p. 64.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The Cretan question (1821–1905).
**Answer: Crete rises again and again against Ottoman rule, wins autonomy under Prince George, and waits for union with Greece (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* (Greece), The Cretan question (1821–1905), printed p. 64.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Worksheet 4: Toward the end of Ottoman rule (19th century).
**Answer: Tanzimat reforms and the Halepa charter of 1878 loosen the old order; Christian townspeople rise as the Muslim landowning class declines (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Θεματικός Φάκελος — Από τη Βενετοκρατία στην Οθωμανική κυριαρχία: Το Ηράκλειο Κρήτης (Thematic Folder — From Venetian Rule to Ottoman Domination: Heraklion, Crete), Γ' Γυμνασίου* (Greece), Worksheet 4: Toward the end of Ottoman rule (19th century), printed p. 33.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: The Cretan Question.
**Answer: Crete's repeated risings force Europe to grant autonomy while union with Greece waits (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Νεότερου και Σύγχρονου Κόσμου από το 1453 μ.Χ. έως σήμερα (History of the Modern and Contemporary World from 1453 to today), Α' ΕΠΑ.Λ. (vocational lyceum), General Education* (Greece), The Cretan Question, printed p. 53.**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 4 A: The struggle of the national uprising in Crete (1821–1830).
**Answer: Crete rises with the mainland in 1821, is crushed by Egyptian troops, and is left outside the first Greek state (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Θέματα Νεοελληνικής Ιστορίας (Themes of Modern Greek History), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, Humanities orientation group; one author per theme: Georgios Margaritis (economy), Agathoklis Azelis (political parties), Nikolaos Andriotis (the refugee question), Theocharis Detorakis (the Cretan question), Konstantinos Fotiadis (Black Sea Hellenism)* (Greece), Theme 4 A: The struggle of the national uprising in Crete (1821–1830), printed p. 175.**
