# Lesson plan — The Renaissance spreads north — France, Germany and the Netherlands

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Renaissance spreads north — France, Germany and the Netherlands
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Italian spark crosses the Alps — French kings build Loire châteaux, German masters like Dürer marry Italian perspective to northern detail, Netherlandish painters fill panels with merchants' worlds, El Greco stretches souls in Spain, and Rudolf's Prague becomes a cabinet of wonders.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
