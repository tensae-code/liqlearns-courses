# The Renaissance spreads north — France, Germany and the Netherlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) France and Germany take it up  b) The spark crosses the Alps  c) The Netherlands, Spain and Prague
**Answer: b) The spark crosses the Alps** — The Italian spark crosses the Alps into the other countries of Europe.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) France and Germany take it up  b) The spark crosses the Alps  c) The Netherlands, Spain and Prague
**Answer: a) France and Germany take it up** — French kings build Loire châteaux, and German masters like Dürer marry Italian perspective to northern detail.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The spark crosses the Alps  b) France and Germany take it up  c) The Netherlands, Spain and Prague
**Answer: c) The Netherlands, Spain and Prague** — Netherlandish painters fill panels with merchants' worlds, El Greco stretches souls in Spain, and Rudolf's Prague becomes a cabinet of wonders.
