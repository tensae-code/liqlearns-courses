# Video script — The Renaissance spreads north — France, Germany and the Netherlands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The Italian spark crosses the Alps into the other countries of Europe.

French kings build Loire châteaux, and German masters like Dürer marry Italian perspective to northern detail.

Netherlandish painters fill panels with merchants' worlds, El Greco stretches souls in Spain, and Rudolf's Prague becomes a cabinet of wonders.
