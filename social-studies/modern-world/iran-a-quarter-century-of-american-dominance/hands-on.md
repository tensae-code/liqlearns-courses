# Hands-on — Iran — a quarter-century of American dominance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran — a quarter-century of American dominance — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, A quarter century of America's dominance over Iran, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
