# Hands-on — 1789 — the French Revolution breaks out

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ladder of estates
Draw the three estates as a ladder. Write on each rung who stands there, what they hold, and what they pay. Say aloud which rung carried the kingdom.

## Walk the year
Lay four cards on the floor — Estates-General, Tennis Court Oath, Bastille, Declaration. Walk from card to card, saying at each stop what happened and why it led to the next.
