# 1789 — the French Revolution breaks out — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The First and Second Estates held most privileges and paid most taxes.
**Answer: False** — they held the privileges and were free of most taxes; the Third Estate paid.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Third Estate's deputies declared themselves a National Assembly.
**Answer: True** — after the Tennis Court Oath.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Bastille was a royal fortress-prison in Paris.
**Answer: True** — its guns commanded the city's east.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Estates-General had not met for generations before 1789.
**Answer: True** — calling it was itself an extraordinary act.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Estates-General had not met for generations before 1789.
**Answer: True** — calling it was itself an extraordinary act.
