# Video script — 1789 — the French Revolution breaks out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on France. At Versailles the court glitters; in the villages the bread price climbs, week by week, on a rising line.
The Estates-General assembles — three rooms, three estates. One door locks. In a tennis court, hands rise together in an oath.
Paris: the crowd flows toward a fortress. The Bastille's towers fall. The date burns itself into the calendar — 14 July 1789.
A document unrolls over the map: the Rights of Man. The ladder of estates fades; in its place, one word stands — the Nation.
