# Venezuela Indigenous Resistance Missions And German Governors — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Missions subdue less peacefully than was said, Margarita has a girl governor, German governors out of Coro hunt gold, and guarura shells sound in the Caracas valley.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in A conflict of great proportions: times of indigenous resistance

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 4to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about A conflict of great proportions: times of indigenous resistance

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sword and cross together take the territory against indigenous resistance, town by town.
