# Hands-on — Indonesia — Ki Hadjar Dewantara and Taman Siswa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Indonesia — Ki Hadjar Dewantara and Taman Siswa — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, A.1 Mengapa Perlu Mempelajari Ilmu Sejarah?, Kisah Ki Hadjar Dewantara, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
