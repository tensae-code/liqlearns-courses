# Indonesia — Ki Hadjar Dewantara and Taman Siswa

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ki Hadjar Dewantara, a Javanese aristocrat, drops his noble title, founds the Taman Siswa schools, and from 1912 builds national education under colonial rule As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — A.1 Mengapa Perlu Mempelajari Ilmu Sejarah?, Kisah Ki Hadjar Dewantara

The Javanese aristocrat who drops his noble title, founds Taman Siswa, and with Cipto Mangunkusumo and Douwes Dekker from 1912 builds national education under colonial rule, choosing 'nothing but a happy heart' over wealth.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: A.1 Mengapa Perlu Mempelajari Ilmu Sejarah?, Kisah Ki Hadjar Dewantara. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
