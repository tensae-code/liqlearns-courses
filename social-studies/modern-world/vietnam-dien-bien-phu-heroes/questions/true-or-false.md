# Vietnam Dien Bien Phu Heroes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tày soldier Bế Văn Đàn kneels and takes a machine gun on his own shoulders — "Fire now!" — and dies still holding it; Phan Đình Giót, wounded again and again, throws his body across a bunker's firing slit at Him Lam.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 15, stories of the heroes of Điện Biên Phủ (Bế Văn Đàn; Phan Đình Giót)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 15, stories of the heroes of Điện Biên Phủ (Bế Văn Đàn; Phan Đình Giót)
