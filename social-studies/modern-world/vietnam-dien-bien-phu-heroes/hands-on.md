# Hands-on — Vietnam Dien Bien Phu Heroes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Dien Bien Phu Heroes — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 15, stories of the heroes of Điện Biên Phủ (Bế Văn Đàn; Phan Đình Giót), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
