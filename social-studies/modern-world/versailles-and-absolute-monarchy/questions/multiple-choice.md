# Versailles and absolute monarchy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Louis XIV moved the court to —
a) Versailles  b) Nantes  c) Valladolid
**Answer: a) Versailles** — he moves the court to Versailles.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** From Versailles he ruled France from —
a) One palace  b) Many parliaments  c) The ports alone
**Answer: a) One palace** — he rules France from one palace.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Versailles, the king governed —
a) By himself  b) Only through the salons  c) Not at all
**Answer: a) By himself** — le roi gouverne par lui-même.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The king in this lesson is —
a) Louis XIV  b) Henri IV  c) François Ier
**Answer: a) Louis XIV** — Louis XIV, in the beat.
