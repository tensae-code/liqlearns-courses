# Versailles and absolute monarchy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where did Louis XIV move the court, and from where did he then rule France?
**Answer: To Versailles; he ruled France from one palace (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the king govern at Versailles, in the manual's phrase?
**Answer: By himself — le roi gouverne par lui-même (any close wording).**
