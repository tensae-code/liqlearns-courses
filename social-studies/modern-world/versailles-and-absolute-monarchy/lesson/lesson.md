# Versailles and absolute monarchy

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Louis XIV moves the court to Versailles and rules France from one palace — the king governs by himself. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The court moves to Versailles

Louis XIV moves the court to Versailles and rules France from one palace — the whole court, and with it the kingdom's attention, gathers where the king is.

In your mind's eye: the palace of Versailles with the court gathered around Louis XIV. Say where the court moved and how the king governed.

## Sub lesson 2 — The king governs by himself

The king governs by himself — at Versailles the absolute monarchy shows itself: one king, one palace, one centre of rule.
