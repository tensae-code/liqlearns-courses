# Hands-on — Vietnamese Society Through The Ages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnamese Society Through The Ages — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Chuyên đề 1, II.3, Vietnamese society from Văn Lang to Đổi mới, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
