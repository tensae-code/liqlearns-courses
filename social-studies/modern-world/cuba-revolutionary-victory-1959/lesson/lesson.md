## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: 5.7 The great victory — Batista flees on 1 January 1959; a general strike defeats the last junta and the Caravan of Freedom carries the rebels to Havana; 8.6–8.10 Struggle in the cities; consolidation; advance of the Rebel Army; triumph of the Revolution; culture in the neocolonial Republic — llano and sierra converge; the 1958 invasions, Santa Clara and the January strike complete the victory the book has been building since Hatuey; 5.4 The revolutionary situation in 1958 — failed April strike, summer offensive defeated, invasions west: by December 1958 the dictatorship is a shell. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
