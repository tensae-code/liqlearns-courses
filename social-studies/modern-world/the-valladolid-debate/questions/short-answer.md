# The Valladolid debate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What question did Spain debate at Valladolid in 1550?
**Answer: Whether the peoples of America have souls and rights (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who argued against whom at Valladolid?
**Answer: Las Casas against Sepúlveda (any close wording).**
