# The Valladolid debate — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Valladolid debate took place in 1550.
**Answer: True** — the beat's date.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At Valladolid everyone agreed at once and there was no controversy.
**Answer: False** — it is filed as a controverse — Las Casas against Sepúlveda.
