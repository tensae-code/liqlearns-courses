# The Valladolid debate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Valladolid debate happened in —
a) 1550  b) 1492  c) 1789
**Answer: a) 1550** — in 1550, in the beat.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The debate asked whether America's peoples —
a) Had souls and rights  b) Had gold  c) Could sail to Spain
**Answer: a) Had souls and rights** — whether they have souls and rights.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The two debaters were —
a) Las Casas and Sepúlveda  b) Erasmus and Luther  c) Henri IV and Louis XIV
**Answer: a) Las Casas and Sepúlveda** — Las Casas against Sepúlveda.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The country holding the debate was —
a) Spain  b) France  c) England
