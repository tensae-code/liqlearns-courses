# America in the twenty-first century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 9/11 launched —
a) The Great Society  b) The war on terror  c) The New Deal
**Answer: b) The war on terror** — 9/11 launches the war on terror.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The war on terror's wars were in —
a) Vietnam and Korea  b) Afghanistan and Iraq  c) Mexico and Canada
**Answer: b) Afghanistan and Iraq** — wars in Afghanistan and Iraq.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The financial crisis shook —
a) Only farmers  b) Banks, houses and jobs together  c) Nothing
**Answer: b) Banks, houses and jobs together** — the financial crisis at home.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The survey closes with the election of —
a) Reagan  b) Barack Obama  c) Johnson
**Answer: b) Barack Obama** — the hope-and-change election of Barack Obama.
