# America in the twenty-first century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three closing beats of the whole US survey.
**Answer: 9/11 and the war on terror with wars in Afghanistan and Iraq; the financial crisis; old disputes over race and rights and Obama's hope-and-change election (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Obama's election a fitting close to this book's story?
**Answer: Because the survey is told as the widening, contested meaning of American freedom — the first African American president, elected on hope and change, is that story arriving in the present (any close wording).**
