# America in the twenty-first century — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barack Obama was the first African American president.
**Answer: True** — The survey closes with his hope-and-change election.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The twenty-first century opened for America in complete peace.
**Answer: False** — 9/11 launched the war on terror and wars in Afghanistan and Iraq.
