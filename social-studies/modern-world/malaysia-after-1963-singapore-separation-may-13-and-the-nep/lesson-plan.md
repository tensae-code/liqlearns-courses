## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Singapore separates** — Tun Abdul Razak's negotiations with Lee Kuan Yew fail across 29 June–1 July 1965; the Tunku directs Razak to settle separation with Dr. Ismail Abdul Rahman, Tan Siew Sin and V.T. Sambanthan in early August 1965, and Singapore leaves Malaysia (Bab 6.2: Singapore separates)
2. **The communist threat after 1963** — in Sarawak the army's operations — Hammer (1965), RASCOM's and Sri Aman among them — break the communist movement division by division, while the peninsula faces its own renewed insurgency (Bab 6.3: the communist threat after 1963)
3. **The inter-ethnic tragedy** — the race riots of 13 May 1969 force an emergency; the Yang di-Pertuan Agong consents to the National Operations Council (MAGERAN) under Tun Abdul Razak Hussein, which governs from 16 May 1969 to 23 February 1971 to restore order (Bab 6.5: the inter-ethnic tragedy)
4. **Rebuilding unity and the economy** — the Rukun Negara is proclaimed as the nation's unity charter; education is re-knit (the Cabinet Committee of September 1974 reviews the national system with Bahasa Melayu at its centre); and the New Economic Policy (Dasar Ekonomi Baru, 1970–1990), launched through the First Outline Perspective Plan, sets out to eradicate poverty and restructure society's economic imbalances (Bab 7–8: rebuilding unity and the economy)
