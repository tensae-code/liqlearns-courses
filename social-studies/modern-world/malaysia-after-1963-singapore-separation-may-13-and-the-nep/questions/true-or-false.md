# Malaysia after 1963 — Singapore's separation, 13 May and the New Economic Policy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tun Abdul Razak's negotiations with Lee Kuan Yew fail across 29 June–1 July 1965; the Tunku directs Razak to settle separation with Dr. Ismail Abdul Rahman, Tan Siew Sin and V.T. Sambanthan in early August 1965, and Singapore leaves Malaysia
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 6.2: Singapore separates).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Sarawak the army's operations — Hammer (1965), RASCOM's and Sri Aman among them — break the communist movement division by division, while the peninsula faces its own renewed insurgency
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 6.3: the communist threat after 1963).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The race riots of 13 May 1969 force an emergency; the Yang di-Pertuan Agong consents to the National Operations Council (MAGERAN) under Tun Abdul Razak Hussein, which governs from 16 May 1969 to 23 February 1971 to restore order
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 6.5: the inter-ethnic tragedy).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The Rukun Negara is proclaimed as the nation's unity charter; education is re-knit (the Cabinet Committee of September 1974 reviews the national system with Bahasa Melayu at its centre); and the New Economic Policy (Dasar Ekonomi Baru, 1970–1990), launched through the First Outline Perspective Plan, sets out to eradicate poverty and restructure society's economic imbalances
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 7–8: rebuilding unity and the economy).
