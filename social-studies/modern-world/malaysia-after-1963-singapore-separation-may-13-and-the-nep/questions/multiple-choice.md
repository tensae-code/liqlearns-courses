# Malaysia after 1963 — Singapore's separation, 13 May and the New Economic Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Singapore separates  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Singapore separates** — Tun Abdul Razak's negotiations with Lee Kuan Yew fail across 29 June–1 July 1965; the Tunku directs Razak to settle separation with Dr. Ismail Abdul Rahman, Tan Siew Sin and V.T. Sambanthan in early August 1965, and Singapore leaves Malaysia
