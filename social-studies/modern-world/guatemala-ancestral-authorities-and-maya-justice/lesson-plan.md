# Lesson plan — Guatemala — ancestral authorities and Maya justice

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala — ancestral authorities and Maya justice
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Guatemala, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** where the state court punishes, Guatemala's ancestral authorities gather the community to repair the harm and restore balance — restorative Maya justice, which the guide insists is not violence or lynching (teacher guide — evidence only).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two kinds of justice compared: restorative and punitive.
2. Say what ancestral authorities aim to do: repair the harm and restore balance.
3. Say what the guide insists is NOT Maya justice: violence or lynching.
