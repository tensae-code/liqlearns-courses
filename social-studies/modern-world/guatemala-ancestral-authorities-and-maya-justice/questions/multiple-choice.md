# Guatemala — ancestral authorities and Maya justice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maya justice in the guide is —
a) restorative — repairing harm and restoring balance  b) only imprisonment  c) lynching
**Answer: a) restorative — repairing harm and restoring balance** — its character.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ancestral authorities work by —
a) gathering the community  b) sending cases to Spain  c) fining in cacao
**Answer: a) gathering the community** — their method.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide says lynching is —
a) not Maya justice  b) the centre of Maya justice  c) required by custom
**Answer: a) not Maya justice** — its insistence.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on —
a) teacher-guide evidence only  b) a student book  c) a Spanish chronicle
**Answer: a) teacher-guide evidence only** — its evidence, honestly labelled.
