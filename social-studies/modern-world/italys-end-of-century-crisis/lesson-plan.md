# Lesson plan — Italys End Of Century Crisis

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Italys End Of Century Crisis
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Italy, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Banca Romana scandal fells Giolitti, Adwa (1896) fells Crispi, and Bava Beccaris' cannon fire on Milan's crowds (1898) marks the liberal state's darkest hour
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unità 5, Cap. 18 §§4–5 From Giolitti's first government to Crispi's second; The end-of-century crisis** — the Banca Romana scandal fells Giolitti, Adwa (1896) fells Crispi, and Bava Beccaris' cannon fire on Milan's crowds (1898) marks the liberal state's darkest hour
2. **Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§1–2 The end-of-century crisis and the start of a new political course; Socialists and Catholics, new protagonists of Italian political life** — after Adwa and the Milan cannonades the liberal state changes course; Turati's socialists and the Catholic movement enter politics as mass forces the old trasformismo cannot digest
