# Italys End Of Century Crisis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Banca Romana scandal fells Giolitti, Adwa (1896) fells Crispi, and Bava Beccaris' cannon fire on Milan's crowds (1898) marks the liberal state's darkest hour.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 18 §§4–5 From Giolitti's first government to Crispi's second; The end-of-century crisis

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 2: Il Settecento e l'Ottocento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 18 §§4–5 From Giolitti's first government to Crispi's second; The end-of-century crisis

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** after Adwa and the Milan cannonades the liberal state changes course; Turati's socialists and the Catholic movement enter politics as mass forces the old trasformismo cannot digest.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§1–2 The end-of-century crisis and the start of a new political course; Socialists and Catholics, new protagonists of Italian political life

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
