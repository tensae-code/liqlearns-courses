# Lesson plan — Dominican Republic — our history, heroes and symbols

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Dominican Republic — our history, heroes and symbols
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Dominican Republic, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The great events of the Dominican past are handed to children as a treasure they inherit.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 5 Mi país, su identidad y cultura: Acontecimientos históricos que forman parte del patrimonio cultural** — the great events of the Dominican past are handed to children as a treasure they inherit
2. **Unidad 5 Heroínas y héroes nacionales: legado de honor** — the nation's heroines and heroes leave an example the children are asked to honour
3. **Unidad 5 Los símbolos patrios en la identidad** — flag, shield and anthem tell every Dominican child who they are
4. **Unidad 5 Personajes populares: personajes destacados de mi país** — beloved popular figures carry the country's culture in music, sport and story

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
