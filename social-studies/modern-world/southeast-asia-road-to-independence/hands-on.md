# Hands-on — Southeast Asia Road To Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Southeast Asia Road To Independence — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 6 Three stages to independence: feudal-led resistance gives way to bourgeois movements (the Philippines rising of 1896–1898); from 1920 the proletariat enters — communist parties in Indonesia (1920) and across the region in the 1930s; 1945–1975 completes it — Indonesia, Vietnam and Laos declare in 1945, the Philippines is granted 1946, Burma 1948, Brunei last in 1984, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
