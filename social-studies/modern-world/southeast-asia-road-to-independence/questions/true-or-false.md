# Southeast Asia Road To Independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the flag changes hands from kings to parties to peoples, and in one autumn of 1945 three nations declare themselves.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 6 Three stages to independence: feudal-led resistance gives way to bourgeois movements (the Philippines rising of 1896–1898); from 1920 the proletariat enters — communist parties in Indonesia (1920) and across the region in the 1930s; 1945–1975 completes it — Indonesia, Vietnam and Laos declare in 1945, the Philippines is granted 1946, Burma 1948, Brunei last in 1984

**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 6 Three stages to independence: feudal-led resistance gives way to bourgeois movements (the Philippines rising of 1896–1898); from 1920 the proletariat enters — communist parties in Indonesia (1920) and across the region in the 1930s; 1945–1975 completes it — Indonesia, Vietnam and Laos declare in 1945, the Philippines is granted 1946, Burma 1948, Brunei last in 1984
