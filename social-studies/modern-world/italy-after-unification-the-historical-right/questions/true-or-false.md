# Italy After Unification The Historical Right — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Historical Right balances the budget and fights brigantaggio in the South (Storici a confronto: was it a civil war?); Venice (1866) and Rome (1870) complete the map, and the Southern question opens.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§5–6 The first years of united Italy: the political, economic and social situation; The annexation of Veneto and Rome as capital

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 2: Il Settecento e l'Ottocento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§5–6 The first years of united Italy: the political, economic and social situation; The annexation of Veneto and Rome as capital

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a poor, divided kingdom taxes flour and fights brigands (Fare storia: Lupo on southern brigandage) until Porta Pia (Eventi, 1870) makes Rome the capital.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 4, Cap. 14 The first years of united Italy (demography, economy and society; Governing united Italy; The revolts against unity and brigantaggio; The economy and fiscal policy; The conquest of Veneto and the taking of Rome)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 2 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XVII al XIX secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 4, Cap. 14 The first years of united Italy (demography, economy and society; Governing united Italy; The revolts against unity and brigantaggio; The economy and fiscal policy; The conquest of Veneto and the taking of Rome)
