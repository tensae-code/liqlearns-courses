# Honduras Conquest And Colonisation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 23 Conquista y colonización: la conquista
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Gil González Dávila comes for gold, Cortés sends Cristóbal de Olid, who founds Triunfo de la Cruz, captures Dávila, betrays Cortés and is condemned to die for treason; Cortés restores order, stays a year and leaves Hernando de Saavedra as governor

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 23: la colonización y sus consecuencias
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — mining cities rise (Trujillo, Comayagua, Tegucigalpa, Yuscarán) inside the Captaincy of Guatemala under New Spain; Indigenous people lose land, are forced to labour, resist and rebel, while Catholicism, Spanish, new classes, mestizaje and African slavery remake the land

## Sub lesson 3 — the prolonged conquest of Honduras
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — unlike the Aztec and Inca empires that fell with their chiefs, Central America's many dispersed peoples resisted from 1502 to 1573; in Honduras the zones of Trujillo-Aguán, the east, the west-centre and the Ulúa-Sula valley held out, and whole populations were decimated by mita and encomienda

## Sub lesson 4 — colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — grid-plan cities rose around the plaza mayor; encomienda, repartimiento, mita and slavery powered mining and farming, and the meeting of Europeans, Indigenous peoples and Africans made Honduras a crucible — today 90% mestizo
