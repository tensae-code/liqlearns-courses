# Honduras Conquest And Colonisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 23 Conquista y colonización: la conquista.
**Answer: Gil González Dávila comes for gold, Cortés sends Cristóbal de Olid, who founds Triunfo de la Cruz, captures Dávila, betrays Cortés and is condemned to die for treason; Cortés restores order, stays a year and leaves Hernando de Saavedra as governor (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* (Honduras), Lección 23 Conquista y colonización: la conquista, printed p. 106.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 23: la colonización y sus consecuencias.
**Answer: mining cities rise (Trujillo, Comayagua, Tegucigalpa, Yuscarán) inside the Captaincy of Guatemala under New Spain; Indigenous people lose land, are forced to labour, resist and rebel, while Catholicism, Spanish, new classes, mestizaje and African slavery remake the land (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* (Honduras), Lección 23: la colonización y sus consecuencias, printed p. 108.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: the prolonged conquest of Honduras.
**Answer: unlike the Aztec and Inca empires that fell with their chiefs, Central America's many dispersed peoples resisted from 1502 to 1573; in Honduras the zones of Trujillo-Aguán, the east, the west-centre and the Ulúa-Sula valley held out, and whole populations were decimated by mita and encomienda (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), the prolonged conquest of Honduras, printed p. 150.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje.
**Answer: grid-plan cities rose around the plaza mayor; encomienda, repartimiento, mita and slavery powered mining and farming, and the meeting of Europeans, Indigenous peoples and Africans made Honduras a crucible — today 90% mestizo (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje, printed p. 158.**
