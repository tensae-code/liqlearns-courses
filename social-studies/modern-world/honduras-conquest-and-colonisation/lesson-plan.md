## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 23 Conquista y colonización: la conquista** — Gil González Dávila comes for gold, Cortés sends Cristóbal de Olid, who founds Triunfo de la Cruz, captures Dávila, betrays Cortés and is condemned to die for treason; Cortés restores order, stays a year and leaves Hernando de Saavedra as governor
2. **Lección 23: la colonización y sus consecuencias** — mining cities rise (Trujillo, Comayagua, Tegucigalpa, Yuscarán) inside the Captaincy of Guatemala under New Spain; Indigenous people lose land, are forced to labour, resist and rebel, while Catholicism, Spanish, new classes, mestizaje and African slavery remake the land
3. **the prolonged conquest of Honduras** — unlike the Aztec and Inca empires that fell with their chiefs, Central America's many dispersed peoples resisted from 1502 to 1573; in Honduras the zones of Trujillo-Aguán, the east, the west-centre and the Ulúa-Sula valley held out, and whole populations were decimated by mita and encomienda
4. **colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje** — grid-plan cities rose around the plaza mayor; encomienda, repartimiento, mita and slavery powered mining and farming, and the meeting of Europeans, Indigenous peoples and Africans made Honduras a crucible — today 90% mestizo
