# International Relations After The Cold War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One superpower remains, new centres rise, and the rules of the post-bipolar world are still being fought over.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §19 Changes in international relations

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §19 Changes in international relations

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One superpower remains, alliances search for new purposes, and regional wars replace the old East–West standoff.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 4, Lessons 58–59: changes in international relations

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 4, Lessons 58–59: changes in international relations

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** After the Cold War the world argues between one centre of power and many, while integration projects multiply.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 5 §22: prospects of unification and international structures
