# Lesson plan — Muhammad Ali Pasha and the building of modern Egypt

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Muhammad Ali Pasha and the building of modern Egypt
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Jordan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an Albanian soldier from Macedonia rises in the Ottoman army, ends the Mamluks at the Cairo Citadel feast of 1811, and builds modern Egypt — self-sufficient economy, factories, schools, a European-style army and fleet, campaigns in Arabia, Greece and the Levant — until the London Treaty of 1840 and the firman of 1841 fix his house in Egypt and Sudan
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.
