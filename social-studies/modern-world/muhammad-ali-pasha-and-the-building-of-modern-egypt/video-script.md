# Video script — Muhammad Ali Pasha and the building of modern Egypt

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Jordan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

an Albanian soldier from Macedonia rises in the Ottoman army, ends the Mamluks at the Cairo Citadel feast of 1811, and builds modern Egypt — self-sufficient economy, factories, schools, a European-style army and fleet, campaigns in Arabia, Greece and the Levant — until the London Treaty of 1840 and the firman of 1841 fix his house in Egypt and Sudan.
