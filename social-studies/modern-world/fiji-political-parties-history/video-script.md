# Video script — Fiji's political parties — their history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Parties form in the 1960s to contest the road to independence; Brij Lal's Broken Waves cartoon and text anchor a study of the 1966 election, and leader portraits (Jai Ram Reddy; Vanuatu's Father Walter Lini as a Pacific comparison) show who built them. A seats table runs across Fiji's elections to the 6-13 May 2006 result (SDL/United Fiji 36 seats), taking in the election that made Mahendra Chaudhry, of the Fiji Labour Party, Fiji's first Indian prime minister.
