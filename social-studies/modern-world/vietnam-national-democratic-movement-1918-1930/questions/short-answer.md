# Vietnam National Democratic Movement 1918 1930 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 5, Vietnam's national-democratic movement abroad and at home, 1918–1930.
**Answer: abroad, the Tâm Tâm Xã society in Canton (1923) attempts to assassinate Governor-General Merlin at Sa Điền (1924) while Phan Châu Trinh works through the Association of Patriotic Vietnamese in France (1919); at home the Constitutional Party (1923) campaigns in Saigon, crowds boycott monopolies and demand "Vietnamese use Vietnamese goods" (1919), and mass movements demand freedom for Phan Bội Châu (1925) and Nguyễn An Ninh (1926) and fill the streets for Phan Châu Trinh's funeral (1926) (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 5, Vietnam's national-democratic movement abroad and at home, 1918–1930, printed p. 22.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 5, the workers enter the struggle.
**Answer: Hải Phòng's Sác-nô sailors strike in 1919 and 600 Saigon–Chợ Lớn dyers in 1920; then, in August 1925, over a thousand Ba Son shipyard workers under Tôn Đức Thắng strike and stop a French warship carrying troops against the Chinese revolution — the milestone, quoted from historian Đinh Xuân Lâm, where Vietnamese workers enter organised struggle with a clear political purpose; from 1926 organised strikes spread to Mạo Khê, Trường Thi and Phú Riềng (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 5, the workers enter the struggle, printed p. 24.**
