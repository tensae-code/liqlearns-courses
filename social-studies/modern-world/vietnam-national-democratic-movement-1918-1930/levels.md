# Vietnam National Democratic Movement 1918 1930 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 5, Vietnam's national-democratic movement abroad and at home, 1918–1930
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — abroad, the Tâm Tâm Xã society in Canton (1923) attempts to assassinate Governor-General Merlin at Sa Điền (1924) while Phan Châu Trinh works through the Association of Patriotic Vietnamese in France (1919); at home the Constitutional Party (1923) campaigns in Saigon, crowds boycott monopolies and demand "Vietnamese use Vietnamese goods" (1919), and mass movements demand freedom for Phan Bội Châu (1925) and Nguyễn An Ninh (1926) and fill the streets for Phan Châu Trinh's funeral (1926)

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 5, the workers enter the struggle
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Hải Phòng's Sác-nô sailors strike in 1919 and 600 Saigon–Chợ Lớn dyers in 1920; then, in August 1925, over a thousand Ba Son shipyard workers under Tôn Đức Thắng strike and stop a French warship carrying troops against the Chinese revolution — the milestone, quoted from historian Đinh Xuân Lâm, where Vietnamese workers enter organised struggle with a clear political purpose; from 1926 organised strikes spread to Mạo Khê, Trường Thi and Phú Riềng
