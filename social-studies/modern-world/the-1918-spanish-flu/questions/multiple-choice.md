# The 1918 Spanish flu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Spanish flu reached Iceland in —
a) 1918  b) 1809  c) 1944
**Answer: a) 1918** — in 1918 the flu reaches Iceland.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The illness is called in Icelandic —
a) Spænska veikin  b) Fjallkona  c) Vistarband
**Answer: a) Spænska veikin** — Spænska veikin — the Spanish flu.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The flu killed —
a) Slowly over a century  b) In weeks  c) No one in Iceland
**Answer: b) In weeks** — kills in weeks.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Compared with war, the flu in Iceland —
a) Killed what war never touched  b) Killed no one  c) Came by ship from Denmark only as news
**Answer: a) Killed what war never touched** — kills in weeks what war never touched.
