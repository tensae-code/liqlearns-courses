# The 1918 Spanish flu — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Spanish flu reached Iceland in 1918.
**Answer: True** — in 1918 the Spanish flu reaches Iceland.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Iceland was untouched by the 1918 flu.
**Answer: False** — it reached Iceland and killed in weeks.
