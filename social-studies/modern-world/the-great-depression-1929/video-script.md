# Video script — The Great Depression — 1929 and the world it broke

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on New York. A price line climbs, climbs — and falls off a cliff. October 1929.
The red line travels: across the Atlantic to Europe's factories, out to farms on every continent. Trade arrows thin and vanish.
A queue forms under a Wall Street sign — the breadline. In the countryside, a harvest is sold for a third of its old price.
In the squares of Europe, crowds gather around men promising work and blame. The misery is loading the next decade's guns.
