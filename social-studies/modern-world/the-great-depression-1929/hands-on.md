# Hands-on — The Great Depression — 1929 and the world it broke

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The spiral on paper
Cut a paper strip and write the spiral's steps along it; join the ends into a loop and turn it slowly, reading each step as it passes — the loop is the point: the Depression fed on itself.

## A family's budget, 1929 and 1932
Write a farm family's income when crop prices are 100, then when they fall 60%. List what the family must give up at the second figure.
