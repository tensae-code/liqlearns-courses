# Post-impressionism — four rebels after the Impressionists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Van Gogh burns feeling into colour  b) Cézanne and Gauguin break away  c) Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the poster
**Answer: b) Cézanne and Gauguin break away** — Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, and Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Van Gogh burns feeling into colour  b) Cézanne and Gauguin break away  c) Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the poster
**Answer: a) Van Gogh burns feeling into colour** — Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour — and sells almost nothing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Cézanne and Gauguin break away  b) Van Gogh burns feeling into colour  c) Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the poster
**Answer: c) Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the poster** — Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the cabaret poster.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Four heirs break four different ways — Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive, Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour and sells almost nothing, and Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the cabaret poster  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
