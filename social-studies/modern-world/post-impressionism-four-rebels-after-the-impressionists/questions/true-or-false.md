# Post-impressionism — four rebels after the Impressionists — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour — and sells almost nothing.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Four heirs break four different ways — Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive, Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour and sells almost nothing, and Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the cabaret poster.
