# Post-impressionism — four rebels after the Impressionists

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: Post-impressionism — four rebels after the Impressionists. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cézanne and Gauguin break away

Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, and Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.
