# Hands-on — Post-impressionism — four rebels after the Impressionists

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw four different post-Impressionist works side by side from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: Cézanne and Gauguin break away; Van Gogh burns feeling into colour; Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the poster. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
