# Video script — Post-impressionism — four rebels after the Impressionists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, and Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive.

Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour — and sells almost nothing.

Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the cabaret poster.

That is the story: four heirs break four different ways — Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive, Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour and sells almost nothing, and Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the cabaret poster.
