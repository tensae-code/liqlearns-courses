# Fiji — sandalwood, beche-de-mer and the beachcombers

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools; shipwrecked beachcomber Charles Savage reaches Bau in 1808 with muskets from the wreck and shifts the balance of war; when sandalwood fades, the beche-de-mer trade takes villages over As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
