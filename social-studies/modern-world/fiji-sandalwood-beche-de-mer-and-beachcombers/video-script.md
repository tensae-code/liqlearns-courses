# Video script — Fiji — sandalwood, beche-de-mer and the beachcombers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools; shipwrecked beachcomber Charles Savage reaches Bau in 1808 with muskets from the wreck and shifts the balance of war; when sandalwood fades, the beche-de-mer trade takes villages over.
