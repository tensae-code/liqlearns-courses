# Hands-on — Fiji — sandalwood, beche-de-mer and the beachcombers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Fiji — sandalwood, beche-de-mer and the beachcombers — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Early European contact: sandalwood trade, Charles Savage, beche-de-mer, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
