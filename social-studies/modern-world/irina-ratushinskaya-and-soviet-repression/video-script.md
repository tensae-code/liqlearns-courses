# Video script — Irina Ratushinskaya and Soviet repression

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ukrainian teacher Irina Ratushinskaya loses her job for refusing to discriminate against Jewish students, is sentenced in 1982 to seven years' hard labour for her religious poetry, and is freed and expelled in 1986 after an international outcry.
