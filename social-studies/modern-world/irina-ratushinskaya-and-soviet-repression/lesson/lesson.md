# Irina Ratushinskaya and Soviet repression

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ukrainian teacher Irina Ratushinskaya loses her job for refusing to discriminate against Jewish students, is sentenced in 1982 to seven years' hard labour for her religious poetry, and is freed and expelled in 1986 after an international outcry.
