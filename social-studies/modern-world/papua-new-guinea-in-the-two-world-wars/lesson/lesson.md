# Papua New Guinea in the two world wars

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Two world wars started far away arrive in PNG — the colonisers fight each other in WWI, and in WWII whole provinces feel the fighting; children research and document the impacts on communities, individuals and groups. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
