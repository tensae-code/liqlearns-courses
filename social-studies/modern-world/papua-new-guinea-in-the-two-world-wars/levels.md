# Papua New Guinea in the two world wars — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — World War I / World War II / Impacts of World War II
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — two world wars started far away arrive in PNG — the colonisers fight each other in WWI, and in WWII whole provinces feel the fighting; children research and document the impacts on communities, individuals and groups

## Sub lesson 2 — Impact of World War II
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Japan takes Rabaul in 1942 and makes it its South-West Pacific headquarters; ANGAU runs the unoccupied territory with PNG carriers, stretcher-bearers and interpreters; American abundance — and black soldiers with white soldiers' privileges — astonishes villagers, while bombs destroy gardens, homes and lives

## Sub lesson 3 — Significance of War Tracks during World War II
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three jungle tracks become lifelines — Kokoda, Black Cat and Bulldog — up near-vertical slopes through rivers, forest and mosquitoes, with PNG carriers hauling supplies and ammunition to exhausted soldiers; today trekkers walk Kokoda while the other two fall silent

## Sub lesson 4 — Fuzzy Wuzzy Angels
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — on the Owen Stanley Track, PNG carriers — "impromptu angels with the fuzzy-wuzzy hair" — carry badly wounded Australians down the mountain "as gentle as a nurse", and mothers in Australia pray for them by name; students map every battle site with dates and participants
