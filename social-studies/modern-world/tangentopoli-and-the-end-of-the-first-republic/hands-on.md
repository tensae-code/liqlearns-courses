# Hands-on — Tangentopoli And The End Of The First Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Tangentopoli And The End Of The First Republic — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unità 5, Cap. 20 §5 The end of the "First Republic", from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
