# Bhutan — British missions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** hunting a trade road to Tibet, Britain sends Bogle (1774, who plants potatoes at Tashichhodzong), Hamilton, Turner (1783, whose sketches show Bhutan to the world), Kishan Kant Bose, Pemberton (1838, rejected) and finally Ashley Eden (1864), whose humiliation lights the fuse of war
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Six, The Bilateral Missions).
