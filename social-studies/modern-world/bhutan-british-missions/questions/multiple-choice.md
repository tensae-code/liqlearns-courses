# Bhutan — British missions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter Six, The Bilateral Missions  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter Six, The Bilateral Missions** — hunting a trade road to Tibet, Britain sends Bogle (1774, who plants potatoes at Tashichhodzong), Hamilton, Turner (1783, whose sketches show Bhutan to the world), Kishan Kant Bose, Pemberton (1838, rejected) and finally Ashley Eden (1864), whose humiliation lights the fuse of war
