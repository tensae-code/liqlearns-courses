# The American West, 1840–1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Homesteaders chased —
a) Free land  b) Office jobs  c) Sea trade
**Answer: a) Free land** — homesteaders chase free land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gold and cattle built —
a) Boom economies  b) Monasteries  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Boom economies** — gold and cattle build boom economies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Chinese immigrants and Hispanic citizens were —
a) Given command of the West  b) Pushed to the margins  c) Made governors
**Answer: b) Pushed to the margins** — they are pushed to the margins.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The assault on American Indian life runs in this lesson from —
a) Yorktown to Gettysburg  b) Sand Creek to Wounded Knee  c) Boston to Atlanta
**Answer: b) Sand Creek to Wounded Knee** — from Sand Creek to Wounded Knee.
