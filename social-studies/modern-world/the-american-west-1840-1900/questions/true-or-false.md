# The American West, 1840–1900 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The West's growth came without cost to Native peoples.
**Answer: False** — The US waged a generation-long assault on American Indian life and culture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Boom towns could rise overnight on gold strikes.
**Answer: True** — Gold and cattle build boom economies — towns that exploded overnight.
