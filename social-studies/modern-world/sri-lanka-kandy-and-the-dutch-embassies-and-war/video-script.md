# Video script — Sri Lanka — Kandy and the Dutch embassies and war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Envoys swap gifts in 1602, 1656, 1688 and 1721, Vimaladharmasuriya II keeps the peace and wins back monks from Burma, but Kirti Sri's Kandy is invaded in 1765 and the 1766 treaty strips the kingdom of its coast.
