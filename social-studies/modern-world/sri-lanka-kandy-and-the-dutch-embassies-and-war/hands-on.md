# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — Kandy and the Dutch embassies and war

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — Kandy and the Dutch embassies and war — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Embassies, peace and the 1766 treaty, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
