# Lesson plan — Indonesia — the VOC and the founding of Batavia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Indonesia — the VOC and the founding of Batavia
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Indonesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the VOC grows from spice trader into a territorial empire: Houtman reaches Banten in 1596, Admiral Keumalahayati fights the Dutch at Aceh, and Batavia is built into the company's Asian capital
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab I.B Perlawanan Bangsa Indonesia terhadap Kolonialisme (opening)** — Cornelis de Houtman reaches Banten in 1596, the Treaty of Tordesillas logic divides the world, Admiral Keumalahayati fights the Dutch at Aceh, and the VOC builds Batavia into its Asian capital while crushing Banda, whose Fort Nassau ruins still stand
2. **Bab I.B Menganalisis Kemaharajaan VOC / Keserakahan Kongsi Dagang** — the chartered company grows from spice trader into a territorial empire whose greed, monopolies and violence the chapter puts on trial

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
