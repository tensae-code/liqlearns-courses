# Indonesia — the VOC and the founding of Batavia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cornelis de Houtman reaches Banten in 1596, the Treaty of Tordesillas logic divides the world, Admiral Keumalahayati fights the Dutch at Aceh, and the VOC builds Batavia into its Asian capital while crushing Banda, whose Fort Nassau ruins still stand.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.B Perlawanan Bangsa Indonesia terhadap Kolonialisme (opening)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — the Makassar War and the Treaty of Bongaya.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.B Perlawanan Bangsa Indonesia terhadap Kolonialisme (opening)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The chartered company grows from spice trader into a territorial empire whose greed, monopolies and violence the chapter puts on trial.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.B Menganalisis Kemaharajaan VOC / Keserakahan Kongsi Dagang

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — the Makassar War and the Treaty of Bongaya.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.B Menganalisis Kemaharajaan VOC / Keserakahan Kongsi Dagang
