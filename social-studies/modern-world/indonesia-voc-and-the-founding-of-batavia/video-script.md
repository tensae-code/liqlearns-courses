# Video script — Indonesia — the VOC and the founding of Batavia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The VOC grows from spice trader into a territorial empire: Houtman reaches Banten in 1596, Admiral Keumalahayati fights the Dutch at Aceh, and Batavia is built into the company's Asian capital.
