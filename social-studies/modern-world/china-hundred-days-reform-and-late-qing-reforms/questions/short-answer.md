# China Hundred Days Reform And Late Qing Reforms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Late Qing attempts to reform and become strong.
**Answer: reformers around the young Guangxu emperor try in 1898 to remake schools, offices and the army in a hundred days, and the court later abolishes the thousand-year keju examinations themselves (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Unit 8 Lesson 3: Late Qing attempts to reform and become strong, printed p. 102.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The struggle to save the nation (1) — 1898.
**Answer: Kang Youwei and the Guangxu emperor issue a hundred days of reform edicts until Cixi's coup locks the emperor on an island and executes six reformers (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 18: The struggle to save the nation (1) — 1898, printed p. 109.**
