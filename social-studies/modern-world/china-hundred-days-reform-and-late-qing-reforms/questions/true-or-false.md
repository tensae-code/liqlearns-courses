# China Hundred Days Reform And Late Qing Reforms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reformers around the young Guangxu emperor try in 1898 to remake schools, offices and the army in a hundred days, and the court later abolishes the thousand-year keju examinations themselves.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Late Qing attempts to reform and become strong

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Late Qing attempts to reform and become strong

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kang Youwei and the Guangxu emperor issue a hundred days of reform edicts until Cixi's coup locks the emperor on an island and executes six reformers.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The struggle to save the nation (1) — 1898

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The struggle to save the nation (1) — 1898
