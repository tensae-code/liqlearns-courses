# Honduras Discovery Encounter And The Name — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two unlike worlds meet — armour, horses, gunpowder and monarchy against maize, nature-linked spirituality and hunting weapons; storm-tossed Spaniards thank God they escaped "estas Honduras", Guaymuras and Higueras give way to the name Honduras.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 22: encuentro de dos culturas y el nombre Honduras

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 22: encuentro de dos culturas y el nombre Honduras

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Europe gains gold, silver, tobacco and empires and the Atlantic becomes the highway; America gains colonists, mestizaje, slave sales, mine extraction, an imposed culture, language and religion, unknown diseases and a falling population.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 23: consecuencias del descubrimiento

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 23: consecuencias del descubrimiento

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 1492 was chance, not plan: Columbus miscalculated, and the book insists a populated continent cannot be "discovered" — two worlds met, with Galeano's "Las venas abiertas" assigned as the radiography of what followed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in the "encounter of cultures" debate

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about the "encounter of cultures" debate
