# Honduras Discovery Encounter And The Name — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 22: encuentro de dos culturas y el nombre Honduras.
**Answer: two unlike worlds meet — armour, horses, gunpowder and monarchy against maize, nature-linked spirituality and hunting weapons; storm-tossed Spaniards thank God they escaped "estas Honduras", Guaymuras and Higueras give way to the name Honduras (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* (Honduras), Lección 22: encuentro de dos culturas y el nombre Honduras, printed p. 103.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 23: consecuencias del descubrimiento.
**Answer: Europe gains gold, silver, tobacco and empires and the Atlantic becomes the highway; America gains colonists, mestizaje, slave sales, mine extraction, an imposed culture, language and religion, unknown diseases and a falling population (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* (Honduras), Lección 23: consecuencias del descubrimiento, printed p. 130.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: the "encounter of cultures" debate.
**Answer: 1492 was chance, not plan: Columbus miscalculated, and the book insists a populated continent cannot be "discovered" — two worlds met, with Galeano's "Las venas abiertas" assigned as the radiography of what followed (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), the "encounter of cultures" debate, printed p. 144.**
