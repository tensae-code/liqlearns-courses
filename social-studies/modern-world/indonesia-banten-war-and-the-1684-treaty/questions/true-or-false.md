# Indonesia — the Banten war and the 1684 treaty — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banten tears itself apart in the 1682-84 war between Sultan Ageng and his son Sultan Haji, and Haji's treaty with the VOC on 17 April 1684 hands the company the kingdom.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.B

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — the Padri War.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.B

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Banten resists company encroachment until succession war and treaty deliver it to the VOC.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.A.4 Perlawanan Banten

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — the Padri War.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.A.4 Perlawanan Banten
