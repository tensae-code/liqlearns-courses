# Indonesia — the Banten war and the 1684 treaty

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Banten tears itself apart in the 1682–84 war between Sultan Ageng and his son Sultan Haji, whose treaty of 17 April 1684 hands the kingdom to the VOC As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
