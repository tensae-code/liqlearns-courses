# Video script — Indonesia — Daendels and the Great Post Road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Governor-General Daendels (1808–1811), sent to hold Java against Britain, quotes the French Revolution, rules as a dictator, and drives the Anyer–Panarukan Great Post Road out of the people by kerja rodi forced labour.
