# Indonesia — Daendels and the Great Post Road — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sent by the Batavian Republic to hold Java against Britain, Governor-General Daendels (1808-1811) quotes the French Revolution, then rules as a dictator and drives the Anyer-Panarukan Great Post Road out of the people by kerja rodi forced labour.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.C.1 Masa Pemerintahan Republik Bataaf

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — Riau rises and the Chinese revolt of 1740.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.C.1 Masa Pemerintahan Republik Bataaf
