# Indonesia — Daendels and the Great Post Road

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Governor-General Daendels (1808–1811), sent to hold Java against Britain, quotes the French Revolution, rules as a dictator, and drives the Anyer–Panarukan Great Post Road out of the people by kerja rodi forced labour As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab I.C.1 Masa Pemerintahan Republik Bataaf

Sent by the Batavian Republic to hold Java against Britain, Governor-General Daendels (1808-1811) quotes the French Revolution, then rules as a dictator and drives the Anyer-Panarukan Great Post Road out of the people by kerja rodi forced labour.
