# European Integration From Coal And Steel To The Eu — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** coal and steel first, then a common market, a single market and a single currency — rivals choose to grow together.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Milestones in Europe's unification efforts (1951–2001)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Milestones in Europe's unification efforts (1951–2001)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Greece joins in 1981, struggles to keep up, adopts the euro and argues over what membership costs and gives.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Greece and Europe's unification; Greece as an EU member state

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Greece and Europe's unification; Greece as an EU member state

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The road to European unification: realities and prospects
