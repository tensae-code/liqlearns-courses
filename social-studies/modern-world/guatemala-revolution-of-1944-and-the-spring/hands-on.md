# Hands-on — Guatemala — Revolution of 1944 and the Spring

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Part 1 — Revolución de 1944 — Arévalo and Arbenz, the spring

Draw or map this part from memory, then write its story beat in your own words: students, teachers and young officers overthrow Ubico's successor on 20 October 1944; Arévalo builds schools, a labour code and social security; Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touches United Fruit land — and in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrows him.

## Part 2 — After the spring — Castillo Armas and Ydígoras

Draw or map this part from memory, then write its story beat in your own words: Castillo Armas annuls the 1945 constitution, returns the expropriated land and bans unions before his 1957 assassination; under Ydígoras the closed political space breeds the MR-13 rebels of 1962, seed of the guerrilla.
