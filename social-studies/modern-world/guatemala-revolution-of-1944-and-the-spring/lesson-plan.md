# Lesson plan — Guatemala — Revolution of 1944 and the Spring

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala — Revolution of 1944 and the Spring
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Guatemala, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** students, teachers and young officers overthrow Ubico's successor on 20 October 1944; Arévalo builds schools, a labour code and social security, Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touches United Fruit land — and in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrows him, after which Castillo Armas undoes the spring.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Revolución de 1944 — Arévalo and Arbenz, the spring** — students, teachers and young officers overthrow Ubico's successor on 20 October 1944; Arévalo builds schools, a labour code and social security; Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touches United Fruit land — and in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrows him
2. **After the spring — Castillo Armas and Ydígoras** — Castillo Armas annuls the 1945 constitution, returns the expropriated land and bans unions before his 1957 assassination; under Ydígoras the closed political space breeds the MR-13 rebels of 1962, seed of the guerrilla

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the other History lessons of this title. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the revolution date: 20 October 1944.
2. Name Arévalo's builds: schools, a labour code and social security.
3. Name Arbenz's land reform: Decree 900.
4. Name what Castillo Armas annulled: the 1945 constitution.
5. Say what he did with the expropriated land and unions: returned the land, banned unions.
6. Name the 1962 rebels: MR-13, seed of the guerrilla.
