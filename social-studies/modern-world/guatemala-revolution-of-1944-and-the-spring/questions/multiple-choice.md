Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1944 Revolution happened on —

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Decree 900 was Arbenz's —

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Arbenz was overthrown in 1954 by the army backed by —
