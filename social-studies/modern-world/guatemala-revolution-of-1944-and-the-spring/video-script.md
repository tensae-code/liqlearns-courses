# Video script — Guatemala — Revolution of 1944 and the Spring

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Guatemala book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

On 20 October 1944, students, teachers and young officers overthrow Ubico's successor. Then comes the spring: Arévalo builds schools, a labour code and social security; Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touches United Fruit land — and in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrows him. Guatemala's Grade 4 book teaches 1945–1954 as a democratic spring cut short.

The book follows the spring's undoing. Castillo Armas annuls the 1945 constitution, returns the expropriated land and bans unions before his 1957 assassination. Under Ydígoras, the closed political space breeds the MR-13 rebels of 1962 — the seed of the guerrilla, and of the war to come.
