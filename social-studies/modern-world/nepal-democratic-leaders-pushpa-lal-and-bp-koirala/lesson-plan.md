# Lesson plan — Nepal — democratic leaders: Pushpa Lal and B. P. Koirala

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Nepal — democratic leaders: Pushpa Lal and B. P. Koirala
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Nepal, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pushpa Lal Shrestha, expelled and exiled, founds the Nepal Communist Party in 2006 BS and spends his life organising against Rana and Panchayat rule until his death in New Delhi in 2035 BS; B. P. Koirala, jailed by the Ranas, becomes Nepal's first elected prime minister in 2016 BS, is imprisoned again by King Mahendra, and returns from exile in 2033 BS to preach national reconciliation
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.
