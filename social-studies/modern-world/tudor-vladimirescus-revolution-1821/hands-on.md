# Hands-on — Tudor Vladimirescus Revolution 1821

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Tudor Vladimirescus Revolution 1821 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
