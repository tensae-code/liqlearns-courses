# Tudor Vladimirescus Revolution 1821 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tudor, a small boyar from Oltenia, raises the pandurs and addresses memoranda to sultan and emperors to block foreign intervention; in spring 1821 Chișinău fills with refugees from the Eterist rising.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tudor expects Russia to back the Eteria and the Greeks to help the Romanians; Ypsilanti instead means to use the Principalities, Tudor reaches Colentina outside Bucharest, and the rising is crushed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor şi universală: Epoca modernă. Manual pentru clasa a XI-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu
