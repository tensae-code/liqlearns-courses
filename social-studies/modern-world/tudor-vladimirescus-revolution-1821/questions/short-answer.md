# Tudor Vladimirescus Revolution 1821 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821.
**Answer: Tudor, a small boyar from Oltenia, raises the pandurs and addresses memoranda to sultan and emperors to block foreign intervention; in spring 1821 Chișinău fills with refugees from the Eterist rising (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* (Moldova), Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821, printed p. 94.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: §16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu.
**Answer: Tudor expects Russia to back the Eteria and the Greeks to help the Romanians; Ypsilanti instead means to use the Principalities, Tudor reaches Colentina outside Bucharest, and the rising is crushed (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor şi universală: Epoca modernă. Manual pentru clasa a XI-a* (Moldova), §16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu, printed p. 68.**
