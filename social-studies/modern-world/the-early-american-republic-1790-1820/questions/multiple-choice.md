# The early American republic, 1790–1820 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two camps that fought over the republic's soul were —
a) Federalists and Democratic-Republicans  b) Sons and Daughters of Liberty  c) North and South only
**Answer: a) Federalists and Democratic-Republicans** — Federalists and Democratic-Republicans fight over the republic's soul.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Parties in this period were —
a) Banned  b) Born in the fighting between the camps  c) Imported from Britain
**Answer: b) Born in the fighting between the camps** — parties are born in the fighting.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In 1812 the young nation went to war with —
a) France  b) Britain  c) Spain
**Answer: b) Britain** — in 1812 the young nation goes back to war with Britain.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The War of 1812 ended with the republic —
a) Destroyed  b) Surviving it  c) Joining Britain
**Answer: b) Surviving it** — it survives it.
