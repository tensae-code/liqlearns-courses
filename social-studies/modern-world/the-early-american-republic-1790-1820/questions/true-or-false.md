# The early American republic, 1790–1820 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The early republic had no serious internal arguments.
**Answer: False** — Federalists and Democratic-Republicans fought over the republic's soul.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Surviving the War of 1812 proved the young nation could stand.
**Answer: True** — The republic survives a second war with Britain.
