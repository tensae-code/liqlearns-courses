# Where the Gulf's peoples came from — origins and migrations

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Gulf's peoples were made by movement — Arab tribes migrating to the coast until new communities and new political bodies filled it. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Origins of the Gulf states' peoples

Learners untangle stock, migration, internal and external migration, then trace arab tribes moving into the gulf until the coast fills with new communities.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why tribes moved — the causes of internal migration

Drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade push tribe after tribe toward arabia's east coast, where the moves knit into new political bodies.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: origins of the gulf states' peoples; why tribes moved — the causes of internal migration. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
