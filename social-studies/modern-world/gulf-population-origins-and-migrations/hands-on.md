# Hands-on — Where the Gulf's peoples came from — origins and migrations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw two arrows on a blank Gulf map: one internal move, one external move — label each.

## Task 2
Interview an elder or imagine one: write a family's migration story in four sentences.

## Task 3
Make a cause-and-effect chain: drought → move → new settlement → new political body.

## Task 4
Write which push cause you think was strongest, and why, in three sentences.
