# Video script — Where the Gulf's peoples came from — origins and migrations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Kuwait's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Learners untangle stock, migration, internal and external migration, then trace arab tribes moving into the gulf until the coast fills with new communities.

Drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade push tribe after tribe toward arabia's east coast, where the moves knit into new political bodies.
