# Lesson plan — Where the Gulf's peoples came from — origins and migrations

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Where the Gulf's peoples came from — origins and migrations
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Kuwait, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Gulf's peoples were made by movement — Arab tribes migrating to the coast until new communities and new political bodies filled it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Origins of the Gulf states' peoples** — learners untangle stock, migration, internal and external migration, then trace Arab tribes moving into the Gulf until the coast fills with new communities (Unit 3 Lesson 1: Origins of the Gulf states' peoples)
2. **Why tribes moved — the causes of internal migration** — drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade push tribe after tribe toward Arabia's east coast, where the moves knit into new political bodies (Unit 3 Lesson 2: Causes of internal migration in the Gulf)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
