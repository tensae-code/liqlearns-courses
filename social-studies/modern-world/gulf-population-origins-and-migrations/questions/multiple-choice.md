# Where the Gulf's peoples came from — origins and migrations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gulf coast filled with —
a) new communities as Arab tribes moved in  b) empty forts  c) foreign armies only
**Answer: a) new communities as Arab tribes moved in** — Arab tribes move in until the coast fills with new communities

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration inside one land is called —
a) internal migration  b) external migration  c) stock
**Answer: a) internal migration** — the lesson untangles internal and external migration

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The peoples traced moving into the Gulf are —
a) Arab tribes  b) Viking crews  c) Mongol armies
**Answer: a) Arab tribes** — Arab tribes moving into the Gulf

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tribes were pushed toward the east coast by —
a) drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade  b) free land grants  c) railway building
**Answer: a) drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade** — drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade push them

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** On the east coast the moves knit into —
a) new political bodies  b) empty camps  c) sea ports only
**Answer: a) new political bodies** — the moves knit into new political bodies

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The coast the tribes moved toward was —
a) Arabia's east coast  b) Arabia's west coast  c) the Mediterranean
**Answer: a) Arabia's east coast** — they move toward Arabia's east coast
