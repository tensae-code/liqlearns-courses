# Where the Gulf's peoples came from — origins and migrations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gulf's peoples never moved — they were always where they are now.
**Answer: False** — Arab tribes migrated into the Gulf

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration can create new communities.
**Answer: True** — the coast fills with new communities

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tribes moved only for adventure, never from need.
**Answer: False** — drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade pushed them

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Migration helped create the Gulf's political bodies.
**Answer: True** — the moves knit into new political bodies
