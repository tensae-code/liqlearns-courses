# Video script — Young Poland — art under the partitions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit.

Highland cottages become a national style, and stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions.

A generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map.

That is the story: Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit — highland cottages become a national style, stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions, and a generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map.
