# Young Poland — art under the partitions

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: Young Poland — art under the partitions. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — A capital of the spirit

Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.

Tell your companion the story as three beats: Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit. Then: Highland cottages become a national style, and stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions. And finally: A generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
