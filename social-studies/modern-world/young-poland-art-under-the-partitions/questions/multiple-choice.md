# Young Poland — art under the partitions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) A national style from highland cottages  b) A capital of the spirit  c) A country in culture before the map
**Answer: b) A capital of the spirit** — Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) A national style from highland cottages  b) A capital of the spirit  c) A country in culture before the map
**Answer: a) A national style from highland cottages** — Highland cottages become a national style, and stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) A capital of the spirit  b) A national style from highland cottages  c) A country in culture before the map
**Answer: c) A country in culture before the map** — A generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit — highland cottages become a national style, stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions, and a generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
