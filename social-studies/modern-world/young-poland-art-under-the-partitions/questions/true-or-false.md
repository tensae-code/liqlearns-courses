# Young Poland — art under the partitions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Highland cottages become a national style, and stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit — highland cottages become a national style, stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions, and a generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map.
