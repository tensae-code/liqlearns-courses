# Hands-on — Young Poland — art under the partitions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw a Zakopane highland cottage in the national style and a Wyspiański stained-glass vision from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: A capital of the spirit; A national style from highland cottages; A country in culture before the map. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
