# Lesson plan — Young Poland — art under the partitions

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Young Poland — art under the partitions
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit — highland cottages become a national style, stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions, and a generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.
