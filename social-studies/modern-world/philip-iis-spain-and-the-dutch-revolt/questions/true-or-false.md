# Philip Iis Spain And The Dutch Revolt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** from the Escorial Philip rules a world empire, fights the Dutch revolt and the Ottomans at Lepanto, and launches the Armada against England.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Philip's Spain wins Lepanto (1571) against the Ottomans and loses the northern Netherlands to revolt — one empire, two seas, opposite fates.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi
