# Philip Iis Spain And The Dutch Revolt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II.
**Answer: from the Escorial Philip rules a world empire, fights the Dutch revolt and the Ottomans at Lepanto, and launches the Armada against England (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II, printed p. 502.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi.
**Answer: Philip's Spain wins Lepanto (1571) against the Ottomans and loses the northern Netherlands to revolt — one empire, two seas, opposite fates (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi, printed pp. 540, 543.**
