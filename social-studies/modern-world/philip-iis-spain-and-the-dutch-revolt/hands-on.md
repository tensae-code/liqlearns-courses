# Hands-on — Philip Iis Spain And The Dutch Revolt

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Philip Iis Spain And The Dutch Revolt — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
