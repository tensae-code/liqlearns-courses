# Honduras Independence 1821 Causes And Acta — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** US independence, the French Revolution, Napoleon in Spain and South American wars press from outside; criollo–peninsular rivalry and high taxes press from inside; on 15 September 1821 the five provinces proclaim independence, Valle drafts the Acta, and the Pliegos reach Comayagua on 28 September.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before 1821 come risings — Delgado in El Salvador, León and Granada in Nicaragua, Tegucigalpa's Plaza Mayor in 1812; the criollo-led process frees Indigenous, criollo, mestizo and Black people from three centuries of subjection.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Honduras has TWO independence acts: 15 September 1821, a transfer of power among Spanish-descended groups "to prevent the people proclaiming it themselves", and the stronger 1 July 1823 act breaking with Spain AND Mexico after Iturbide's fall.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in the two actas
