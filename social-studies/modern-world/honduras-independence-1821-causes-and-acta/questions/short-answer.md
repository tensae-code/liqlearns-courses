# Honduras Independence 1821 Causes And Acta — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación.
**Answer: US independence, the French Revolution, Napoleon in Spain and South American wars press from outside; criollo–peninsular rivalry and high taxes press from inside; on 15 September 1821 the five provinces proclaim independence, Valle drafts the Acta, and the Pliegos reach Comayagua on 28 September (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* (Honduras), Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación, printed p. 114.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado.
**Answer: before 1821 come risings — Delgado in El Salvador, León and Granada in Nicaragua, Tegucigalpa's Plaza Mayor in 1812; the criollo-led process frees Indigenous, criollo, mestizo and Black people from three centuries of subjection (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* (Honduras), Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado, printed p. 116.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: the two actas.
**Answer: Honduras has TWO independence acts: 15 September 1821, a transfer of power among Spanish-descended groups "to prevent the people proclaiming it themselves", and the stronger 1 July 1823 act breaking with Spain AND Mexico after Iturbide's fall (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), the two actas, printed p. 166.**
