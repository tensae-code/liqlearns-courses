This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — US independence, the French Revolution, Napoleon in Spain and South American wars press from outside; criollo–peninsular rivalry and high taxes press from inside; on 15 September 1821 the five provinces proclaim independence, Valle drafts the Acta, and the Pliegos reach Comayagua on 28 September

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — before 1821 come risings — Delgado in El Salvador, León and Granada in Nicaragua, Tegucigalpa's Plaza Mayor in 1812; the criollo-led process frees Indigenous, criollo, mestizo and Black people from three centuries of subjection

## Sub lesson 3 — the two actas
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Honduras has TWO independence acts: 15 September 1821, a transfer of power among Spanish-descended groups "to prevent the people proclaiming it themselves", and the stronger 1 July 1823 act breaking with Spain AND Mexico after Iturbide's fall
