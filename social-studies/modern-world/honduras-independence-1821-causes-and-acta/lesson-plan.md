## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación** — US independence, the French Revolution, Napoleon in Spain and South American wars press from outside; criollo–peninsular rivalry and high taxes press from inside; on 15 September 1821 the five provinces proclaim independence, Valle drafts the Acta, and the Pliegos reach Comayagua on 28 September
2. **Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado** — before 1821 come risings — Delgado in El Salvador, León and Granada in Nicaragua, Tegucigalpa's Plaza Mayor in 1812; the criollo-led process frees Indigenous, criollo, mestizo and Black people from three centuries of subjection
3. **the two actas** — Honduras has TWO independence acts: 15 September 1821, a transfer of power among Spanish-descended groups "to prevent the people proclaiming it themselves", and the stronger 1 July 1823 act breaking with Spain AND Mexico after Iturbide's fall
