# Fiji before Christianity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist; the bete priest in the burekalou foretells the future and directs worship of ancestral gods, and war and cannibalism end only when converted chiefs lead their people to Christianity.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Before Christianity

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Year 6 Social Science Textbook (revised) (title from contents/front matter; PDF opens at the copyright page)* is about Games that unite nations — Olympics, Commonwealth and Pacific Games.
**Answer: False** — it is about Before Christianity

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before Christianity, Fiji's ancient religions centre on gods such as Ravuyalo and Rakola; the bete priest serves them in the bure kalou, and pupils interrogate Derrick's 1946 history as a source.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 4.2 Ancient religion in Fiji (with R.A. Derrick, A History of Fiji, 1946, extract)
