# Video script — Fiji before Christianity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist; the bete priest in the burekalou foretells the future and directs worship of ancestral gods, and war and cannibalism end only when converted chiefs lead their people to Christianity. Before Christianity, Fiji's ancient religions centre on gods such as Ravuyalo and Rakola; the bete priest serves them in the bure kalou, and pupils interrogate Derrick's 1946 history as a source.
