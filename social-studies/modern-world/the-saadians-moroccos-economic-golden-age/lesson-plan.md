# Lesson plan — The Saadians — Morocco's economic golden age

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Saadians — Morocco's economic golden age
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Morocco, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History Lesson 11: the Saadian state — economic prosperity — sharifs from the south reunite Morocco, grow rich on sugar and gold, and spend it on a palace of marble and onyx.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Morocco's Grade 5 national story, as the Al-Moufid teacher guide teaches it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Founding, stages and the sugar industry** — Sharifs from the south reunite Morocco; the guide teaches the Saadian state's founding and stages and studies its ambitious economic project in depth — above all the sugar industry, at the Chichaoua works.
2. **Monument study — El Badi Palace** — The wealth is studied through one standing monument: El Badi Palace, a palace of marble and onyx built from sugar-and-gold riches.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
