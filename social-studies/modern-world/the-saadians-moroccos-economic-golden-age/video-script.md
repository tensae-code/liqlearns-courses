# Video script — The Saadians — Morocco's economic golden age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sharifs from the south reunite Morocco; the guide teaches the Saadian state's founding and stages and studies its ambitious economic project in depth — above all the sugar industry, at the Chichaoua works.

The wealth is studied through one standing monument: El Badi Palace, a palace of marble and onyx built from sugar-and-gold riches.
