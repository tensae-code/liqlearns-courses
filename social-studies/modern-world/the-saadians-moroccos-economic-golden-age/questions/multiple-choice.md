# The Saadians — Morocco's economic golden age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Saadians grew rich on — —
a) Sugar and gold  b) Grain for Rome  c) Sea trade only
**Answer: a) Sugar and gold** — Sugar and gold — the golden age's wealth.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sugar works studied in depth are at — —
a) Chichaoua  b) Fez  c) Lixus
**Answer: a) Chichaoua** — Chichaoua — the Chichaoua works.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Their monument studied here is — —
a) El Badi Palace  b) The Qarawiyyin  c) The Almoravid Dome
**Answer: a) El Badi Palace** — El Badi Palace — of marble and onyx.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Saadians came from — —
a) The south, as sharifs  b) The Sahara as reformers  c) Rome
**Answer: a) The south, as sharifs** — The south — sharifs who reunited Morocco.
