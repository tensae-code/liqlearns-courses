# The Saadians — Morocco's economic golden age — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Saadian age is taught as economic prosperity.
**Answer: True — economic prosperity is the lesson's own framing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** El Badi Palace was built before the Saadians grew rich.
**Answer: False — it was built from their sugar-and-gold wealth.**
