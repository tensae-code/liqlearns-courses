# The Saadians — Morocco's economic golden age

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Founding, stages and the sugar industry

Sharifs from the south reunite Morocco; the guide teaches the Saadian state's founding and stages and studies its ambitious economic project in depth — above all the sugar industry, at the Chichaoua works.

## Sub lesson 2 — Monument study — El Badi Palace

The wealth is studied through one standing monument: El Badi Palace, a palace of marble and onyx built from sugar-and-gold riches.
