**Answer: Named risings — quartier-morin 1697, maribaroux 1720, makandal's poison network in the 1750s — grow into maroon communities that negotiate, raid and survive (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Vodou and syncretism as resistance.
