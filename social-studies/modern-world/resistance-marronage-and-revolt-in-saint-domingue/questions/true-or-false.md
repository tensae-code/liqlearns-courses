# Resistance, marronage and revolt in Saint-Domingue — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Starting from albert mangonès' statue of the unknown maroon, pupils trace everyday resistance and flight — the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 1, Résister et marronner).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The same revolt is read twice — in the masters' frightened gazettes and in historians' reconstructions — and the two stories do not match
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 2, De la vision des maîtres à l'analyse des historiens).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Named risings — quartier-morin 1697, maribaroux 1720, makandal's poison network in the 1750s — grow into maroon communities that negotiate, raid and survive
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 3, Révoltes et sociétés marronnes).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Banned again and again by law from the code noir onward, vodou gatherings persist — the repeated bans themselves prove the faith was a meeting-place of resistance
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 4, Le vaudou et le syncrétisme, moyens et formes de la résistance).
