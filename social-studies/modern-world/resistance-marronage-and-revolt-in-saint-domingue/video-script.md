# Video script — Resistance, marronage and revolt in Saint-Domingue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Haiti guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Starting from albert mangonès' statue of the unknown maroon, pupils trace everyday resistance and flight — the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society

The same revolt is read twice — in the masters' frightened gazettes and in historians' reconstructions — and the two stories do not match

Named risings — quartier-morin 1697, maribaroux 1720, makandal's poison network in the 1750s — grow into maroon communities that negotiate, raid and survive

Banned again and again by law from the code noir onward, vodou gatherings persist — the repeated bans themselves prove the faith was a meeting-place of resistance

Hold that story: From everyday resistance and flight, through named risings and maroon communities, to Vodou gatherings banned again and again — the enslaved of Saint-Domingue resist, and the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society.
