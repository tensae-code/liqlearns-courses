# Resistance, marronage and revolt in Saint-Domingue — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — To resist and to maroon
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Starting from albert mangonès' statue of the unknown maroon, pupils trace everyday resistance and flight — the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society (Séance 1, Résister et marronner)

## Sub lesson 2 — From the masters' vision to the historians' analysis
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The same revolt is read twice — in the masters' frightened gazettes and in historians' reconstructions — and the two stories do not match (Séance 2, De la vision des maîtres à l'analyse des historiens)

## Sub lesson 3 — Revolts and maroon societies
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Named risings — quartier-morin 1697, maribaroux 1720, makandal's poison network in the 1750s — grow into maroon communities that negotiate, raid and survive (Séance 3, Révoltes et sociétés marronnes)

## Sub lesson 4 — Vodou and syncretism as resistance
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Banned again and again by law from the code noir onward, vodou gatherings persist — the repeated bans themselves prove the faith was a meeting-place of resistance (Séance 4, Le vaudou et le syncrétisme, moyens et formes de la résistance)
