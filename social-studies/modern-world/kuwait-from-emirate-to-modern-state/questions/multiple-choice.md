# Kuwait — from emirate to modern state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The "Amir of Hearts" is —
a) Jaber Al Ahmad  b) Saad Al Abdullah  c) Meshal Al Ahmad
**Answer: a) Jaber Al Ahmad** — Jaber Al Ahmad, "Amir of Hearts" (1977–2006)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Jaber Al Ahmad proposed —
a) the Gulf Council  b) the United Nations  c) OPEC+
**Answer: a) the Gulf Council** — he proposed the Gulf Council

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The "Hero of Liberation" is —
a) Saad Al Abdullah  b) Jaber Al Ahmad  c) Sabah bin Jaber
**Answer: a) Saad Al Abdullah** — Saad Al Abdullah, "Hero of Liberation"

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Today's Kuwait is led by —
a) Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad  b) Sheikh Saad Al Abdullah  c) Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber
**Answer: a) Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad** — today's Kuwait under Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Kuwait's diplomacy is described as —
a) balanced diplomacy and mediation  b) armed neutrality  c) isolation
**Answer: a) balanced diplomacy and mediation** — it practises balanced diplomacy and mediation

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Facing aggression, Kuwait holds —
a) its sovereignty firm  b) its ports open to attackers  c) its council seat empty
**Answer: a) its sovereignty firm** — it answers aggression with sovereignty held firm
