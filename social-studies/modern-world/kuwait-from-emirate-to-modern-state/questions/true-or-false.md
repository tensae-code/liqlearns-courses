# Kuwait — from emirate to modern state — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait Towers opened in 1961.
**Answer: False** — Jaber Al Ahmad opened them in 1979

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Women's political rights were won under Jaber Al Ahmad.
**Answer: True** — he won women their political rights

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Contemporary Kuwait avoids all diplomacy.
**Answer: False** — it practises balanced diplomacy and mediation

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Humanitarian work is part of Kuwait's modern face.
**Answer: True** — diplomacy and humanitarian work close the book's Kuwait story
