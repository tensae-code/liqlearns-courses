# Kuwait — from emirate to modern state

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ruler by ruler the emirate became a modern state — Jaber Al Ahmad, Amir of Hearts, who proposed the Gulf Council and won women their political rights, then Saad Al Abdullah, Hero of Liberation — and today's Kuwait practises balanced diplomacy under Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kuwait's leaders and making the transformation from emirate to modern state

Ruler by ruler — jaber al ahmad, "amir of hearts" (1977–2006), who proposed the gulf council, opened kuwait towers in 1979 and won women their political rights, then saad al abdullah, "hero of liberation" — the emirate becomes a modern state.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Contemporary Kuwait — sovereignty, diplomacy and humanitarian work

Today's kuwait under sheikh meshal al ahmad practises balanced diplomacy and mediation, gathers world honours, and answers aggression — the book names iran's 2026 missile and drone attacks — with sovereignty held firm.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
