# Hands-on — Kuwait — from emirate to modern state

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw Kuwait Towers and write Jaber Al Ahmad's three achievements around it.

## Task 2
Make a two-ruler card: Amir of Hearts / Hero of Liberation — one line on each name.

## Task 3
Write a mediator's notebook entry: a dispute, Kuwait's balanced offer, the outcome hoped for.

## Task 4
List Kuwait's modern roles from the lesson: mediator, humanitarian actor, sovereign state — one example each.
