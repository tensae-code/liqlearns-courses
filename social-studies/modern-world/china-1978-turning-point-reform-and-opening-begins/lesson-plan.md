## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The great historical turning point** — a May 1978 newspaper article, 'Practice is the sole criterion of truth', breaks the Gang of Four's mental shackles; Deng Xiaoping backs it, and in December the Third Plenary Session ends 'class struggle as the key link' and turns the whole country toward modernisation
2. **Economic system reform** — Xiaogang's farmers secretly divide the collective land by household fingerprint contract, harvests leap, and the household responsibility system sweeps rural China before reform enters the cities
3. **Opening to the outside** — Shenzhen grows from a border fishing town into a skyscraper special economic zone, and opening widens from four zones to the whole coast and interior
4. **The road of socialism with Chinese characteristics opens** — 1978's turn, the special zones, the market's rehabilitation and Deng's Southern Tour relaunch the whole project
