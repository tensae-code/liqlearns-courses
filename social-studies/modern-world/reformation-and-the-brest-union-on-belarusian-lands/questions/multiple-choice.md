Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Religion and the church in the second half of the 16th century  b) The GDL in the second half of the 17th century  c) Religion and the church in the 17th century
**Answer: a) Religion and the church in the second half of the 16th century** — this part tells religion and the church in the second half of the 16th century

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Calvinism sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome  b) war with Russia and Sweden ruins the land, the Vasa line ends, and after Jan Sobieski's death the magnates make open war on each other for the state  c) the Counter-Reformation, backed by kings and Sejm, drives to uproot Orthodoxy and Protestantism while the Uniate church becomes the land's largest confession
