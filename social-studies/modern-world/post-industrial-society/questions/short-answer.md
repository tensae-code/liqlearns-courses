# Post Industrial Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 5 §§19–20: post-industrial society.
**Answer: the scientific-technical revolution moves work from factory to screen, and creative, educated labour becomes society's main wealth (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 12 (World History 12), by Vahram Petrosyan (Themes 1–3), Aram Nazaryan (Introduction, Themes 4, 6, Theme 5 §§19–20), Aram Kosyan (Theme 5 §§21–24); methodical part by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), Theme 5 §§19–20: post-industrial society, printed p. 99.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Programme Theme 5: The world at the modern stage.
**Answer: the formation of the post-industrial (information) society, economic and unification prospects, and media literacy as a named outcome (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Համաշխարհային պատմություն XII դասարան — Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History, Grade 12 — Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), Programme Theme 5: The world at the modern stage, printed p. 15.**
