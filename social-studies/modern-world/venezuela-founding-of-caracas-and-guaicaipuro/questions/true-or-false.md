# Venezuela Founding Of Caracas And Guaicaipuro — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tocanai falls and Paramaconi retreats; weakened resistance and land watered with indigenous warriors' blood precede the city, and cacique Guaicaipuro is killed resisting.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Stories of the founding of Caracas and the death of Guaicaipuro

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Stories of the founding of Caracas and the death of Guaicaipuro

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Even a Caracas district, Catia, carries cacique Catia's name — the capital's map is an indigenous-and-colonial palimpsest.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Capital of the Republic

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 3er grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Capital of the Republic
