# Kazakhstan Abai Kunanbaev — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abai's poems and Words of Edification call his people to learning, work and honesty.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§51–52 Abai Kunanbaev — great poet and thinker

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§51–52 Abai Kunanbaev — great poet and thinker

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Abai's poetry and prose judge and teach his own people.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§58–59 The greatness of Abai Kunanbaev

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§58–59 The greatness of Abai Kunanbaev

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the title "sage" is earned through the Words of Edification.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §6.6 Why is Abai Kunanbaiuly called "Khakim Abai" (Abai the Sage)?

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §6.6 Why is Abai Kunanbaiuly called "Khakim Abai" (Abai the Sage)?
