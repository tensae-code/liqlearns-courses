# Video script — Collapse of the old European colonialism (Unit 6 Lesson 1)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

the empires built since the discoveries fall continent by continent — in the Arab homeland because the people's struggle never stopped and the Islamic awakening guarded identity; in Asia because liberation movements and secret societies rise and new Islamic states appear; in Africa because a plundered continent answers with uprisings, the UN's self-determination principle and Afro-Asian solidarity, until the exhausted colonial powers withdraw.
