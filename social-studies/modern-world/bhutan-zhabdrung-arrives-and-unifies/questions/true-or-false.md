# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung arrives and unifies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594 as the Drukpa lineage holder, Ngawang Namgyal enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and the land begins to come under one rule
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter 5.1 Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** enthroned at Ralung as Pema Karpo's incarnation, the young Zhabdrung is challenged by a rival claimant backed by the Tsangpa ruler, refuses to surrender the Rangjung Kharsapani relic, dreams of a raven leading him south, and flees toward Bhutan
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Two, Dispute over Incarnation of Kunkhen Pema Karpo / Turning the Horse Southward).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** the exile reaches Lhomon and, dzong by dzong, brings the western valleys under one rule as Thuchen Ngawang Namgyal, founding the state the rest of the book inherits
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter One, Arrival to the Land of Lhomon / Western Unification).
