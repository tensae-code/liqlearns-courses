# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung arrives and unifies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter 5.1 Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal.
**Answer: born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594 as the Drukpa lineage holder, Ngawang Namgyal enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and the land begins to come under one rule (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter Two, Dispute over Incarnation of Kunkhen Pema Karpo / Turning the Horse Southward.
**Answer: enthroned at Ralung as Pema Karpo's incarnation, the young Zhabdrung is challenged by a rival claimant backed by the Tsangpa ruler, refuses to surrender the Rangjung Kharsapani relic, dreams of a raven leading him south, and flees toward Bhutan (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter One, Arrival to the Land of Lhomon / Western Unification.
**Answer: the exile reaches Lhomon and, dzong by dzong, brings the western valleys under one rule as Thuchen Ngawang Namgyal, founding the state the rest of the book inherits (any close wording).**
