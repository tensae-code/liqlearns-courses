# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung arrives and unifies — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 5.1 Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594 as the Drukpa lineage holder, Ngawang Namgyal enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and the land begins to come under one rule

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter Two, Dispute over Incarnation of Kunkhen Pema Karpo / Turning the Horse Southward
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: enthroned at Ralung as Pema Karpo's incarnation, the young Zhabdrung is challenged by a rival claimant backed by the Tsangpa ruler, refuses to surrender the Rangjung Kharsapani relic, dreams of a raven leading him south, and flees toward Bhutan

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter One, Arrival to the Land of Lhomon / Western Unification
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the exile reaches Lhomon and, dzong by dzong, brings the western valleys under one rule as Thuchen Ngawang Namgyal, founding the state the rest of the book inherits
