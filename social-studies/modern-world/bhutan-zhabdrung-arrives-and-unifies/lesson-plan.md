# Lesson plan — Bhutan — the Zhabdrung arrives and unifies

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bhutan — the Zhabdrung arrives and unifies
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Bhutan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594 as the Drukpa lineage holder, Ngawang Namgyal enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and the land begins to come under one rule; enthroned at Ralung as Pema Karpo's incarnation, the young Zhabdrung is challenged by a rival claimant backed by the Tsangpa ruler, refuses to surrender the Rangjung Kharsapani relic, dreams of a raven leading him south, and flees toward Bhutan; the exile reaches Lhomon and, dzong by dzong, brings the western valleys under one rule as Thuchen Ngawang Namgyal, founding the state the rest of the book inherits
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
