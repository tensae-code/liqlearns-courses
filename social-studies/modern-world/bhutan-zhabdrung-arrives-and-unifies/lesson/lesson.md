# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung arrives and unifies

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594 as the Drukpa lineage holder, Ngawang Namgyal enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and the land begins to come under one rule; enthroned at Ralung as Pema Karpo's incarnation, the young Zhabdrung is challenged by a rival claimant backed by the Tsangpa ruler, refuses to surrender the Rangjung Kharsapani relic, dreams of a raven leading him south, and flees toward Bhutan; the exile reaches Lhomon and, dzong by dzong, brings the western valleys under one rule as Thuchen Ngawang Namgyal, founding the state the rest of the book inherits. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
