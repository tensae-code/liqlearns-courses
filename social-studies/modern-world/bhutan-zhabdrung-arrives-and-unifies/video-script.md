# Video script — Bhutan — the Zhabdrung arrives and unifies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bhutan book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594 as the Drukpa lineage holder, Ngawang Namgyal enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and the land begins to come under one rule; enthroned at Ralung as Pema Karpo's incarnation, the young Zhabdrung is challenged by a rival claimant backed by the Tsangpa ruler, refuses to surrender the Rangjung Kharsapani relic, dreams of a raven leading him south, and flees toward Bhutan; the exile reaches Lhomon and, dzong by dzong, brings the western valleys under one rule as Thuchen Ngawang Namgyal, founding the state the rest of the book inherits
