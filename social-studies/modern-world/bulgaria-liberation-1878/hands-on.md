# Hands-on — Bulgaria — Liberation, 1878

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Lesson 12: The Russo-Turkish War (1877–1878) and the Liberation of Bulgaria, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
