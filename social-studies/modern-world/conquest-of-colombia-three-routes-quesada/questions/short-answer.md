# The conquest of Colombia — three routes and Quesada — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Guía 5: What was the Conquest of our territories, and how?.
**Answer: In fifty years the Spanish conquer by contract and dispute; three routes — Caribbean coast, south-west and centre — push inland, and Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada leaves Santa Marta with 600 foot soldiers and over 100 horsemen (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
