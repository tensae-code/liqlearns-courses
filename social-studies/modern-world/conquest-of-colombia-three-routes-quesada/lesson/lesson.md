# The conquest of Colombia — three routes and Quesada

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in fifty years the Spanish conquer Colombia by contract and dispute along three routes — Caribbean coast, south-west and centre — and Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada pushes inland from Santa Marta. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
