# Video script — The conquest of Colombia — three routes and Quesada

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

In fifty years the Spanish conquer Colombia by contract and dispute along three routes — Caribbean coast, south-west and centre — and Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada pushes inland from Santa Marta.

In fifty years the Spanish conquer by contract and dispute; three routes — Caribbean coast, south-west and centre — push inland, and Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada leaves Santa Marta with 600 foot soldiers and over 100 horsemen.
