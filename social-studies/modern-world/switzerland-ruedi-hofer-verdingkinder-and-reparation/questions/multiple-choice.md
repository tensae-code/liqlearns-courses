# Switzerland — Ruedi Hofer, the Verdingkinder and reparation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ruedi Hofer was passed to —
a) Over 30 farm places  b) Three farm places  c) One farm, where he stayed
**Answer: a) Over 30 farm places**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At eight years old, Hofer —
a) Lost half a hand in an unexplained explosion  b) Ran away to sea  c) Was adopted by a farmer
**Answer: a) Lost half a hand in an unexplained explosion**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The state calls its payment of at most 25,000 francs —
a) A solidarity contribution, not compensation  b) A full compensation  c) A pension
**Answer: a) A solidarity contribution, not compensation**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Parliament's 2017 law set up a fund of —
a) 300 million francs  b) 500 million francs  c) 25 million francs
**Answer: a) 300 million francs**
