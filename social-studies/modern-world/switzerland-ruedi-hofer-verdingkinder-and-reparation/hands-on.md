# Hands-on — Switzerland — Ruedi Hofer, the Verdingkinder and reparation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The scythe and the hand
Hofer drew the scythe strapped to his ruined hand. Write what that one drawing proves that a payment figure cannot say — and what it cannot prove.

## Name the payment
Write two labels for the state's 25,000 francs: Compensation and Solidarity contribution. Under each, write what the word promises. Then say which label you judge honest, and why.
