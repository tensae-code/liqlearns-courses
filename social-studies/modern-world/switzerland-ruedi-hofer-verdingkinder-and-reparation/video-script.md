# Video script — Switzerland — Ruedi Hofer, the Verdingkinder and reparation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Switzerland's *Sorge oder Zwang?* set tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

He was hired out as a child — to more than thirty farms.



At eight, an explosion took half his hand. No steady job ever followed.



His own verdict: money cannot undo a childhood.



The state answered with a law, an apology — and a carefully chosen word.
