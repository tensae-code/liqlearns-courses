## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Passed from farm to farm** — Ruedi Hofer is passed to over 30 farm places as a child labourer — a Verdingkind, a hired-out child. At eight he loses half a hand in an unexplained explosion. He is exploited and abused, and never gets a steady job. His gift finds another road: he rescues people with his dogs.
2. **One child among a hundred thousand** — The companion widens the frame: over 100,000 Swiss children are hired out. Grisons children herd seasonally, or walk a week to labour in Upper Swabia as Schwabengänger. The 1955 foster ordinance leaves the seasonal children out. Only the 2017 federal law (AFZFG) names the wrong and pays the solidarity contribution. (Teacher guide — evidence.)
3. **Can money undo a childhood?** — The source pages let Hofer answer himself: his drawings show the scythe strapped to his ruined hand, and he writes that money cannot undo a childhood. The victims' counsellor explains the state's careful word: its 25,000 francs are a "solidarity contribution", deliberately not called compensation.
4. **Initiative against law** — At the upper level pupils judge the politics of repair: Guido Fluri's initiative demands a 500-million fund; parliament answers with the 2017 law and a 300-million fund paying at most 25,000 francs. Minister Sommaruga apologises in 2013 — looking away, she says, is also an act — and a 2016 postage stamp carries the remembrance into every post office. (Teacher guide — evidence.)

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Hofer's story: over 30 farm places, half a hand lost at eight, no steady job, rescuing people with his dogs.
2. Say how many Swiss children were hired out, and what the 2017 federal law did.
3. Say what Hofer writes about money and childhood, and why the state calls its payment a solidarity contribution, not compensation.
4. Say what the initiative demanded, what parliament's law gave instead, and what Sommaruga's apology said.
