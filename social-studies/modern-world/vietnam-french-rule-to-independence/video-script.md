# Video script — Vietnam — French rule to independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

French missionaries and gunboats arrive from the 17th century; Gia Long (1802–1820) unifies Vietnam with French help, yet by 1885 France rules it all — and works its rubber plantations with hundreds of thousands of labourers.

the lesson is Ho Chi Minh's life (photos, p. 156): born 19 May 1890, sailor and exile, founder of the Indochinese Communist Party (3 February 1930), proclaimer of the republic (1945), victor over France (1954), dead in Hanoi on 3 September 1969.
