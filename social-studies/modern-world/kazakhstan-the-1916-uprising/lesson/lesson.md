## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: §3 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan — the Tsar's decree conscripting Kazakhs for rear labour sparks a steppe-wide revolt under Amangeldy Imanov, drowned in blood and flight to China, §§8–9 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan — the labour-conscription decree ignites the steppe; Amangeldy's army and the Torgai front hold out longest, §3 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 — the Tsar's labour-conscription decree sparks a steppe-wide revolt, drowned in blood and flight, §§1.4–1.5 The 1916 uprising inquiries — the labour-conscription decree ignites the steppe; the revolt is crushed. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
