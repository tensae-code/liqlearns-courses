# Lesson plan — Cuba Conquest Hatuey And Caonao

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Cuba Conquest Hatuey And Caonao
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Cuba, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ocampo sails round the island (bojeo); Velázquez lands in 1510; cacique Hatuey, fled from La Española, warns Cuba and is burned alive refusing heaven with Spaniards in it; at Caonao a feast turns into a massacre
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **2.2 Conquest of Cuba** — Ocampo sails round the island (bojeo); Velázquez lands in 1510; cacique Hatuey, fled from La Española, warns Cuba and is burned alive refusing heaven with Spaniards in it; at Caonao a feast turns into a massacre
2. **Ch.2 activities: arrival of the Spaniards and occupation exercises** — pupils retrace the conquest chapter's route — Ocampo, Velázquez, Hatuey — through map, ordering and writing tasks
3. **2.1 Clash of two cultures with unequal development** — two worlds at different stages collide in 1492; the book names the inequality itself as the conquest's first cause
