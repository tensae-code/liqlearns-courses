# Cuba Conquest Hatuey And Caonao — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ocampo sails round the island (bojeo); Velázquez lands in 1510; cacique Hatuey, fled from La Española, warns Cuba and is burned alive refusing heaven with Spaniards in it; at Caonao a feast turns into a massacre.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 2.2 Conquest of Cuba

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 2.2 Conquest of Cuba

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** pupils retrace the conquest chapter's route — Ocampo, Velázquez, Hatuey — through map, ordering and writing tasks.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2 activities: arrival of the Spaniards and occupation exercises

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Cuaderno de actividades — Historia de Cuba, Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.2 activities: arrival of the Spaniards and occupation exercises

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** two worlds at different stages collide in 1492; the book names the inequality itself as the conquest's first cause.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 2.1 Clash of two cultures with unequal development

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Noveno grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 2.1 Clash of two cultures with unequal development
