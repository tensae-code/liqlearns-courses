# Video script — Cuba Conquest Hatuey And Caonao

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cuba's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ocampo sails round the island (bojeo); Velázquez lands in 1510; cacique Hatuey, fled from La Española, warns Cuba and is burned alive refusing heaven with Spaniards in it; at Caonao a feast turns into a massacre.
