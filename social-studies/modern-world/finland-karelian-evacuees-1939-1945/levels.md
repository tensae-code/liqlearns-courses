# Finland's Karelian evacuees, 1939–1945 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Teema 2 background: 420,000 people leave home
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after the Winter War Finland cedes about a tenth of its land and 420,000 Karelians and others must rebuild their lives elsewhere; the 1940 quick-settlement law shares out land, but housing is wretched and friction flares over dialect, customs and Orthodox faith — alongside warm welcomes that Finland is judged to have managed well

## Sub lesson 2 — Tehtävä 1 (yläkoulu): letter from Säkylä, 26 Feb 1941
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a Karelian front-line soldier writes that locals in his new home village abuse the evacuees with an ethnic slur, and asks whether law and decency no longer rule in the land he fought for

## Sub lesson 3 — Tehtävä 2 (yläkoulu): letter from Rovaniemi, 27 Feb 1941
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a poor evacuee who has hunted three months for a home asks who the much-praised "gift houses" donated from Sweden are really for, since even two poor families together cannot afford one

## Sub lesson 4 — Tehtävä 3 (yläkoulu): letter from Suonenjoki, 20 Sep 1941
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a poor evacuee family in one tiny stove-room watches its rent climb from under 100 to 150 marks as the rich landlord passes his cession tax onto them, with no rent board to appeal to

## Sub lesson 5 — Tehtävä 4 (lukio): letter from Oulu, 21 Apr 1941
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a reservist corporal from Pitkäranta, who lost everything he owned, is refused an advance on his 34,000-mark compensation claim and cannot clothe his family of three on his small wage

## Sub lesson 6 — Tehtävä 5 (lukio): letters from Säkylä (1941) and Reposaari, 17 Oct 1944, with the Laukko crofter eviction of 1906–07 as comparison
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a family in Kangasala is bundled into a kitchen corner while spare rooms stand empty, officials give no help, and pupils compare the evacuees' lot with the crofter families evicted into the frost at Laukko a generation earlier

## Sub lesson 7 — Tehtävä 6 (lukio): a 1944 letter and Jahvetti's office's reply (Kauhava letter)
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — an evacuee wife pleads her husband's case for leave from the front; the office's reply refuses, and pupils must find the 1944 battle situation behind the refusal and weigh the women's experience of evacuation

## Sub lesson 8 — introduction: cession and resettlement
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Finland cedes a tenth of its land; 420,000 people leave home, the 1940 rapid-resettlement law buys land for them, yet housing stays desperate and friction follows

## Sub lesson 9 — Task 1, Säkylä letter 1941
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a Karelian front-line veteran writes that locals call Karelians 'ryssä' — he fought for the home he no longer has

## Sub lesson 10 — Task 2, Rovaniemi letter 1941
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a poor evacuee who has hunted a home for three months asks who the fairy-tale-expensive Swedish 'gift houses' are really for

## Sub lesson 11 — Task 3, Suonenjoki letter 1941
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a rich landlord raises a poor evacuee family's rent for one tiny stove-room, blaming his cession tax; no rent board exists to appeal to

## Sub lesson 12 — Task 4, Oulu letter 1941
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a reservist sergeant from Pitkäranta, everything left behind, is refused a 3,000 mk advance on his 34,000 mk compensation claim

## Sub lesson 13 — Task 5, Säkylä repeated and Reposaari 1944 letters
Sub lesson 13 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a family is dumped into a kitchen corner while rooms stand empty; the writer recalls the 1906–07 Laukko crofter evictions as the same wound

## Sub lesson 14 — Task 6, 1944 letter and reply
Sub lesson 14 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — an evacuee wife argues her husband should get leave; the reply refuses as the 1944 offensive burns — women's war inside the resettlement story

## Sub lesson 15 — Task 3, the evacuee photographs
