# Finland's Karelian evacuees, 1939–1945 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the Winter War Finland cedes about a tenth of its land and 420,000 Karelians and others must rebuild their lives elsewhere; the 1940 quick-settlement law shares out land, but housing is wretched and friction flares over dialect, customs and Orthodox faith — alongside warm welcomes that Finland is judged to have managed well.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Teema 2 background: 420,000 people leave home

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Jahvetti Teema 2* is about Tehtävä 6 (lukio): a 1944 letter and Jahvetti's office's reply (Kauhava letter).
**Answer: False** — it is about Teema 2 background: 420,000 people leave home

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A Karelian front-line soldier writes that locals in his new home village abuse the evacuees with an ethnic slur, and asks whether law and decency no longer rule in the land he fought for.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tehtävä 1 (yläkoulu): letter from Säkylä, 26 Feb 1941
