# Video script — Finland's Karelian evacuees, 1939–1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Finland's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After the Winter War Finland cedes about a tenth of its land and 420,000 Karelians and others must rebuild their lives elsewhere; the 1940 quick-settlement law shares out land, but housing is wretched and friction flares over dialect, customs and Orthodox faith — alongside warm welcomes that Finland is judged to have managed well.
