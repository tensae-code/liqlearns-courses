# Vietnam Democratic Front 1936 1939 — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 7, the Democratic Front years.
**Answer: after the Comintern's Seventh Congress (July 1935) and France's Popular Front victory, the Indochinese Communist Party founds the Indochinese Democratic Front (July 1936); the Indochina Congress movement petitions openly, crowds greet the Goda mission and Governor-General Brévié (1937), railwaymen strike at Trường Thi, 25,000 rally in Hanoi on 1 May 1938, and a legal press flourishes — a rehearsal, the book judges, for the August Revolution (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
