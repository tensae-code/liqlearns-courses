# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — the resistance reorganises: Falintil and the 1983 ceasefire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three fronts, one resistance
Draw three boxes joined to one centre labelled 'the resistance'. Label each box 'front' and write in one sentence why working on three fronts makes a resistance harder to finish.

## A ceasefire timeline
Draw a line: war, then 1983 ceasefire, then collapse and uprisings. Under the ceasefire stop, write one way a pause in fighting can itself be used as a weapon.
