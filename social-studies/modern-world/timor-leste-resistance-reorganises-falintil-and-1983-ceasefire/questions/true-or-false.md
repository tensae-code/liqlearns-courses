# Timor-Leste — the resistance reorganises: Falintil and the 1983 ceasefire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When the resistance was beaten as a conventional army, the struggle ended.
**Answer: False — it reorganised: Falintil rebuilt as guerrillas on three fronts.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches that even a ceasefire could become a weapon in this war.
**Answer: True — the 1983 Kontaktu Dame is taught that way, with uprisings after its collapse.**
