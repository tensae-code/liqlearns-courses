# Timor-Leste — the resistance reorganises: Falintil and the 1983 ceasefire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After defeat as a conventional army, Falintil rebuilt as —
a) Guerrillas  b) A navy  c) A police force
**Answer: a) Guerrillas** — Guerrillas — the resistance reorganised itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The reorganised resistance worked on how many fronts?
a) Three fronts  b) One front  c) Ten fronts
**Answer: a) Three fronts** — Three fronts — so no single defeat could finish it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kontaktu Dame was —
a) The 1983 ceasefire  b) A battle in 1975  c) A song
**Answer: a) The 1983 ceasefire** — The 1983 ceasefire — the contact for peace.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After the ceasefire collapsed, there followed —
a) Uprisings  b) A lasting peace  c) Independence at once
