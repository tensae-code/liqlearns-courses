## Sub lesson 1 — The Horn of Africa — divided by empires
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The empires carved up the Somali lands — Djibouti to France, the north to Britain, the south and Eritrea to Italy — and banned Arabic and plundered the resources; Sayyid Muhammad ibn Abd Allah Hassan's revolt held out against Britain from 1899 to 1920, the Somali Youth League carried the struggle from 1943, and north and south united as the Somali Republic in 1960; Eritrea's armed struggle from 1961 won independence in 1993, Djibouti in 1977, and the Comoros — French since 1892 — declared independence in 1975.
