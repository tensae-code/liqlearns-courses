# Hands-on — Haider Ali and Tipu Sultan resist the British

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Haider Ali and Tipu Sultan resist the British — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Haider Ali and Tipu Sultan resist the British, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
