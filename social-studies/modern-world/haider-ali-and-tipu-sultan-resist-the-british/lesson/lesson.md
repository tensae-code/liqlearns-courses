# Haider Ali and Tipu Sultan resist the British

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Mysore's father-and-son rulers are the last great hurdle to British supremacy — Haider Ali beats the British at Madras, and his son Tipu Sultan modernises Mysore and dies defending it. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
