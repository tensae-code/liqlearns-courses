# Venezuela Bolivars House And Birth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rain falls in Caracas the day Simón is born; the patio house is happy until death breaks the childhood, and the enslaved Hipólita and Matea raise the child.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bolívar's house, his birth-day and an interrupted childhood

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bolívar's house, his birth-day and an interrupted childhood

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The birth of the child who will liberate is told as a door opening — birds leave the cage as the liberator enters the world.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in A Liberator is born

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 3er grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about A Liberator is born
