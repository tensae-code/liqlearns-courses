# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — British rule and village agriculture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — British rule and village agriculture — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, What British rule did to the village economy, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
