# German Sāmoa — Solf and the Mau a Pule

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Governor Solf (1900) abolishes the Tupu, installs Mataafa as Ali'i Sili, rewrites the fa'alupega to honour the Kaiser, and blocks the Sāmoan-owned Oloa copra company; orator Lauaki Mamoe petitions for a Sāmoan voice, and Solf exiles him and his supporters' families to Saipan — Lauaki dies at sea in 1915 on the ship finally bringing him home. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
