# Kazakhstan Collectivisation And The Famine — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** forced sedentarisation and grain and livestock seizures bring the Asharshylyk famine of 1931–1933, which kills a vast share of the Kazakh people and scatters the rest.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§18–19 Collectivisation in Kazakhstan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана [History of Kazakhstan]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§18–19 Collectivisation in Kazakhstan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** livestock is seized, nomads are settled by force, and the herds that feed the nation vanish.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§32–33 The policy of forced collectivisation in agriculture and its economic consequences

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (1900–1945 гг.) [History of Kazakhstan (1900–1945)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§32–33 The policy of forced collectivisation in agriculture and its economic consequences

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the Asharshylyk kills and exiles millions; the Kazakhs become a minority in their own republic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§34–35 The famine of 1931–1933 — the tragedy of the Kazakh people in the 20th century; the demographic consequences of collectivisation

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *История Казахстана (1900–1945 гг.) [History of Kazakhstan (1900–1945)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§34–35 The famine of 1931–1933 — the tragedy of the Kazakh people in the 20th century; the demographic consequences of collectivisation

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** forced sedentarisation brings the Asharshylyk famine of 1931–1933.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§18–19 Collectivisation in Kazakhstan

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *История Казахстана (Начало XX века — 1945 г.) [History of Kazakhstan (start of the 20th century – 1945)] — textbook for grade 9 (8)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§18–19 Collectivisation in Kazakhstan

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** forced collectivisation and sedentarisation bring the famine.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Section 3 "Kazakhstan under the pressure of the totalitarian system" (its collectivisation inquiries)
