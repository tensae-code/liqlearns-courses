# Video script — Pacific Region — events that shaped its history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

King George Tupou I's Tonga expansion wars and his stand as "Maker of Modern Tonga", Rabuka's two Fiji coups, the Bougainville Crisis draining PNG's budget, and Port Moresby's APEC summit — political, economic, social, religious and cultural events sorted and interpreted.

Independence comes unevenly — different colonial masters, most of Melanesia free in the 1970s under indigenous elites, then the crises: Bougainville's resource rebellion, Fiji's denial of Indo-Fijian rights, and island economies dependent on foreign companies extracting timber, minerals, oil and fish.
