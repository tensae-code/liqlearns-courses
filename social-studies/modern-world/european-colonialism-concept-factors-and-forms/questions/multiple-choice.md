# European colonialism — what it is, what feeds it, its forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonialism, in the chapter's arc, is discovery hardened into —
a) domination  b) trade  c) friendship
**Answer: a) domination** — discovery hardens into domination

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonialism was fed by —
a) industry and rivalry  b) pearl diving  c) monasteries

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Europe ruled its colonies —
a) in several forms  b) in one form only  c) through one king
