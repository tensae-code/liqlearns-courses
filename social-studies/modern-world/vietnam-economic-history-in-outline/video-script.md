# Video script — Vietnam Economic History In Outline

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Vietnam's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the first Vietnamese organise a developed wet-rice agriculture with crafts, fishing and sea trade — the Sa Huỳnh, Chăm-pa and above all Phù Nam communities trade actively across the East Sea; through the Chinese-rule and independent-monarchy ages wet-rice farming with great irrigation works stays the base while thousands of craft villages (bronze casting, pottery, weaving, woodwork) and trading villages feed markets at home and abroad, and from the 17th to the late 18th century the international trade centres — Thăng Long (Kẻ Chợ), Vân Đồn, Phố Hiến, Thanh Hà, Hội An — draw Japanese, Chinese, Indian, English, French and Dutch merchants; colonialism imports mines, industry and 2,600 km of railways but the huge profits flow to French finance capital headed by the Bank of Indochina, and the book's photographs show a farmer pulling the plough in place of a buffalo beside rubber-plantation workers; the modern economy passes through war devastation, the divided decades (socialist North, US-dependent market South), embargo and the subsidy model's errors into crisis — then Đổi mới's multi-component market economy brings high growth and deep global integration
