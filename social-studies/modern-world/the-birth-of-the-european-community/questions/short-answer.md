Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 4, Cap. 15 §4 The Europe of the Economic Community and the Franco-German axis.
**Answer: Rome's treaties (1957) turn the coal-and-steel pool into a common market, and Paris-Bonn reconciliation becomes its engine — the integration Schuman imagined takes institutional flesh (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 9 §8 Le democrazie europee e l'avvio dell'integrazione economica.
