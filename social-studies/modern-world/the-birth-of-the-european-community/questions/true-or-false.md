# The Birth Of The European Community — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rome's treaties (1957) turn the coal-and-steel pool into a common market, and Paris-Bonn reconciliation becomes its engine — the integration Schuman imagined takes institutional flesh.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 15 §4 The Europe of the Economic Community and the Franco-German axis

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 15 §4 The Europe of the Economic Community and the Franco-German axis

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** europeismo (Focus) becomes institutions — the Atlantic Pact (Fare storia Document) guards the West while coal, steel and then the common market bind it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 9 §8 Le democrazie europee e l'avvio dell'integrazione economica

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
