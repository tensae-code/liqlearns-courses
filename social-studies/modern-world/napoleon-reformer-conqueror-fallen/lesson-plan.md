# Lesson plan — Napoleon — reformer, conqueror, fallen

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Napoleon — reformer, conqueror, fallen
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — the age of revolutions unit; the Grade 11 book's closing lesson, with Grade 9 guide and Grade 11 guide evidence stocked (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Napoleon Bonaparte rose from revolutionary general to emperor: he rebuilt France's taxes, bank, schools and laws in the Napoleonic Code, redrew Europe's map by conquest — and fell, leaving the Congress of Vienna to restore the old order's borders.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the two French Revolution lessons — the republic exhausted by the Terror is the France Napoleon took over. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The road up — Italy, Egypt, power (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 9 guide's career steps: Italy 1796 made his name, Egypt followed, and rule brought merit-based office and state schools.
2. **The builder — tax, bank, schools, the Code** — Napoleon's domestic settlement: honest taxes, a national bank, state education and one law code for all.
3. **The conqueror falls; Vienna restores; the verdict (teacher guide — evidence)** — conquests remake Europe, defeat follows, the Congress of Vienna restores the pre-1790 borders — and the guides judge him an enlightened despot.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Republic and Reign of Terror. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Trace Napoleon's road: Italy 1796, Egypt, power, emperor.
2. Name his great reforms — tax, bank, schools, the Code.
3. Tell how his conquests remade Europe's map, and how he fell.
4. Say what the Congress of Vienna tried to restore — and give the guides' verdict on Napoleon.

## If the learner is stuck
Hold the two hands apart: with one hand he built (tax, bank, schools, Code); with the other he conquered (Italy, Egypt, Europe's map). The fall comes when the conquering hand overreaches; Vienna then tries to unbuild the map, but not the ideas.
