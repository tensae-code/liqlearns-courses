# Video script — Napoleon — reformer, conqueror, fallen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Italy, 1796. A young general's arrows cross the Alps; Austria's flags fold.
Egypt: pyramids, soldiers, scholars with notebooks. Then Paris — a tired republic, and the general takes the reins.
Inside France the map calms: a bank, a tax office, a school, and one book of law laid on every desk in the land.
Then the map swells — colour flooding across Europe — and shrinks, winter by winter, until it is France again. At Vienna, old men redraw the borders. But outside the congress hall, the ideas are already walking home in a million soldiers' packs.
