# Hands-on — Napoleon — reformer, conqueror, fallen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The map swells and shrinks
Draw Europe's outline three times on one page: 1796, the empire's height, 1815. Colour Napoleon's reach in each and write under each map the event that changed it.

## Judge him
Hold a two-minute court: one learner prosecutes Napoleon the conqueror, one defends Napoleon the builder, one judges — using the guide's phrase 'enlightened despot' as the sentence to argue for or against.
