# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — Kotte splits and Dharmapala

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — Kotte splits and Dharmapala — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Kotte breaks and bends to Lisbon, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
