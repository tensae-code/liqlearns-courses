# The Modernising East During The Cold War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §14 Eastern countries in the Cold War years: the modernising East.
**Answer: Japan, China and India choose different roads out of poverty while the superpowers court them (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), §14 Eastern countries in the Cold War years: the modernising East, printed p. 106.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 3, Lessons 42–45: the countries of the East in the Cold War years — the modernising East.
**Answer: Japan rebuilds into an economic giant, China turns communist then reforms, and Asian states modernise while the superpowers court and fight over them (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), Theme 3, Lessons 42–45: the countries of the East in the Cold War years — the modernising East, printed p. 95.**
