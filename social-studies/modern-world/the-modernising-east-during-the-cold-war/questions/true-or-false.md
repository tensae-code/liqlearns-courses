# The Modernising East During The Cold War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan, China and India choose different roads out of poverty while the superpowers court them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §14 Eastern countries in the Cold War years: the modernising East

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §14 Eastern countries in the Cold War years: the modernising East

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Japan rebuilds into an economic giant, China turns communist then reforms, and Asian states modernise while the superpowers court and fight over them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 3, Lessons 42–45: the countries of the East in the Cold War years — the modernising East

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 3, Lessons 42–45: the countries of the East in the Cold War years — the modernising East
