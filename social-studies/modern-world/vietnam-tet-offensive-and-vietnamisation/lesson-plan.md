# Lesson plan — Vietnam Tet Offensive And Vietnamisation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Tet Offensive And Vietnamisation
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** America escalates to "Local War"; the victory at Vạn Tường (18 August 1965) opens the "find Americans to fight" tide and the dry-season counteroffensives of 1965–67 are defeated; then, on the night of 30–31 January 1968, the General Offensive and Uprising of Tết Mậu Thân strikes almost every city at once — the US Embassy, Independence Palace and the airport in Saigon among them — and America declares "de-Americanisation", admits the Local War strategy's failure and accepts Paris talks; under "Vietnamisation" the Provisional Revolutionary Government of the Republic of South Vietnam is founded (6 June 1969), Hồ Chí Minh dies leaving his Testament — "our people will definitely win complete victory… our Fatherland definitely will unify" — and the victories table runs on: Cambodia (April–June 1970), Lam Sơn 719 with Lao forces (February–March 1971), and the Quảng Trị strategic offensive (March–June 1972) piercing three defence lines and putting over 200,000 enemy troops out of action — Vietnamisation fails
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 17, Local War, Tết 1968 and Vietnamisation** — America escalates to "Local War"; the victory at Vạn Tường (18 August 1965) opens the "find Americans to fight" tide and the dry-season counteroffensives of 1965–67 are defeated; then, on the night of 30–31 January 1968, the General Offensive and Uprising of Tết Mậu Thân strikes almost every city at once — the US Embassy, Independence Palace and the airport in Saigon among them — and America declares "de-Americanisation", admits the Local War strategy's failure and accepts Paris talks; under "Vietnamisation" the Provisional Revolutionary Government of the Republic of South Vietnam is founded (6 June 1969), Hồ Chí Minh dies leaving his Testament — "our people will definitely win complete victory… our Fatherland definitely will unify" — and the victories table runs on: Cambodia (April–June 1970), Lam Sơn 719 with Lao forces (February–March 1971), and the Quảng Trị strategic offensive (March–June 1972) piercing three defence lines and putting over 200,000 enemy troops out of action — Vietnamisation fails
2. **Bài 8 "Local War" and the 1968 turn: after the fabricated Gulf of Tonkin incident the US lands its own army (from mid-1965) and bombs the North; Vạn Tường (18-8-1965) shows American units can be beaten, two dry-season counter-offensives are smashed, and the North shoots down 3,243 aircraft while multiplying its aid to the South tenfold; the Tet General Offensive and Uprising (early 1968), centred on the cities, breaks Washington's will — the US admits "Local War" has failed, stops bombing the North unconditionally and sits down in Paris** — the offensive that loses the ground war headlines wins the political war in American living rooms
3. **Bài 8 "Vietnamisation" and the road to Paris, 1969–1973: Saigon's army takes the ground role under American firepower while the war widens to Cambodia and Laos; the Provisional Revolutionary Government is founded (6-6-1969), the three Indochinese peoples win together (Cambodia 1970, Route 9–Southern Laos 1971), the 1972 Strategic Offensive pierces three defence lines, and the North's answer to the second bombing war — the twelve-day B-52 defeat over Hanoi and Hải Phòng, "Điện Biên Phủ in the air" (18–29-12-1972) — brings the US back to sign the Paris Agreement (1973)** — each American exit strategy is answered on a new battlefield until the bombers themselves are beaten over the capital
