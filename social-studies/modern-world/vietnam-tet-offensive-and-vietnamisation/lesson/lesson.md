# Vietnam Tet Offensive And Vietnamisation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: America escalates to "Local War"; the victory at Vạn Tường (18 August 1965) opens the "find Americans to fight" tide and the dry-season counteroffensives of 1965–67 are defeated; then, on the night of 30–31 January 1968, the General Offensive and Uprising of Tết Mậu Thân strikes almost every city at once — the US Embassy, Independence Palace and the airport in Saigon among them — and America declares "de-Americanisation", admits the Local War strategy's failure and accepts Paris talks; under "Vietnamisation" the Provisional Revolutionary Government of the Republic of South Vietnam is founded (6 June 1969), Hồ Chí Minh dies leaving his Testament — "our people will definitely win complete victory… our Fatherland definitely will unify" — and the victories table runs on: Cambodia (April–June 1970), Lam Sơn 719 with Lao forces (February–March 1971), and the Quảng Trị strategic offensive (March–June 1972) piercing three defence lines and putting over 200,000 enemy troops out of action — Vietnamisation fails As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
