**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Central America Patria Grande And Integration

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 29: el sueño de Morazán y la integración** — every integration attempt tries to revive Morazán's Federation dream and has failed as union, yet Esquipulas (Cerezo, Azcona) births SICA and the Central American Parliament as regional institutions
2. **Lección 14 América Central, mi patria grande** — Centroamérica is a historical term — the five Federation countries — while América Central is the geographic isthmus of seven, Panama once part of Colombia; the distinction itself is a history lesson
3. **Lección 32 La integración de América Central** — SICA summits of heads of state pursue a region of peace, liberty, democracy and development — integration as a present-day political project with a Federation past
4. **Lección 31 Bloques de integración en América** — NAFTA, SICA, CARICOM, MERCOSUR and CAN map a continent organising into blocs for trade, technology and bargaining power
5. **the integration blocs** — the 20th century answered fragmentation with blocs — the Central American Common Market (planned since 1951), the Andean Community (1969) and Mercosur (1991) — while US modernisation arrived as the Alliance for Progress
