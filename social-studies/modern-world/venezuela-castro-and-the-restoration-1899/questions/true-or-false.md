# Venezuela Castro And The Restoration 1899 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cipriano Castro marches from Colombia in 1899 at the head of the Liberal Restoration, seizes Caracas and opens the twentieth century while the General Asphalt conspires.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in From the Restoration to the fall of Medina: Castro takes Caracas

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about From the Restoration to the fall of Medina: Castro takes Caracas

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The century begins with Castro's nationalist government, then Gómez takes power and oil moves to the centre of national life.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The twentieth century opens with a nationalist government under the sign of oil

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 6to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The twentieth century opens with a nationalist government under the sign of oil

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Castro's Liberal Restoration marches from the Andes to Caracas and governs defying the foreign houses and fleets.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Government of Cipriano Castro (1899–1908)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Nuestra Historia Republicana — Ciencias Sociales, 2do año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Government of Cipriano Castro (1899–1908)
