# Rococo — the aristocracy's playful art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Salons, shells and scrolls  b) Glory traded for pleasure  c) A party on the eve of revolution
**Answer: b) Glory traded for pleasure** — After the Sun King's death the aristocracy trades glory for pleasure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Salons, shells and scrolls  b) Glory traded for pleasure  c) A party on the eve of revolution
**Answer: a) Salons, shells and scrolls** — Rooms shrink to salons, and curves turn to shells and scrolls.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Glory traded for pleasure  b) Salons, shells and scrolls  c) A party on the eve of revolution
**Answer: c) A party on the eve of revolution** — Painting fills with silk, flirtation and garden parties — on the eve of a revolution nobody at the party expects.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) After the Sun King's death the aristocracy trades glory for pleasure — rooms shrink to salons, curves turn to shells and scrolls, and painting fills with silk, flirtation and garden parties on the eve of a revolution nobody at the party expects  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
