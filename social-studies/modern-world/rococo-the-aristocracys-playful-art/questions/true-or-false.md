# Rococo — the aristocracy's playful art — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Rooms shrink to salons, and curves turn to shells and scrolls.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — After the Sun King's death the aristocracy trades glory for pleasure — rooms shrink to salons, curves turn to shells and scrolls, and painting fills with silk, flirtation and garden parties on the eve of a revolution nobody at the party expects.
