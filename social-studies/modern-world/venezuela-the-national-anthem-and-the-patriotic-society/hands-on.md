# Hands-on — Venezuela The National Anthem And The Patriotic Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Homeland: the National Anthem was born in the Patriotic Society, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
