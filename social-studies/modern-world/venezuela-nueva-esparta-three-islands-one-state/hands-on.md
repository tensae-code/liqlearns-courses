# Hands-on — Venezuela Nueva Esparta Three Islands One State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela Nueva Esparta Three Islands One State — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Three islands, one state, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
