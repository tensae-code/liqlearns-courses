# China Self Strengthening Movement — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beaten officials learn Western arms and machines, founding arsenals, shipyards and China's first modern national industries.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Self-Strengthening Movement and modern national industry

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Self-Strengthening Movement and modern national industry

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Arsenals and the Beiyang fleet rise even as France and then Japan strip away Vietnam and Taiwan and smash the fleet at Weihaiwei.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Seeking a way out (2) — Self-Strengthening and the wars

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Seeking a way out (2) — Self-Strengthening and the wars
