# Indonesia — the Proclamation spreads and is defended

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Printed proclamations travel to the regions, crowds fill Jakarta's Ikada field, graffiti repeats the pledge on walls, and Sultan Hamengku Buwono IX binds Yogyakarta to the Republic As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab IV.D Sambutan terhadap Proklamasi Kemerdekaan

Printed proclamations travel to the regions, crowds fill Jakarta's Ikada field for Sukarno's rally, graffiti repeats the pledge on walls, and Sultan Hamengku Buwono IX's amanat binds Yogyakarta to the Republic.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
