# Hands-on — 1917 twice — February and October in Russia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two revolutions, two cards
Make two cards, FEBRUARY and OCTOBER. On each, write who fell, who rose, and one promise made. Lay them in order and read them as a two-line news bulletin.

## Peace, land, bread
The Marxists' three promises fitted three hungers. For each promise, write who in Russia was hungry for it — soldiers, peasants, city workers — and why.
