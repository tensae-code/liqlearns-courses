# 1917 twice — February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Open opposition was forbidden in the Czar's Russia, so opponents organised in secret.
**Answer: True** — the autocracy allowed no open opposition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The February Revolution put the Marxists in power.
**Answer: False** — February felled the Czar; October raised the Marxists.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The provisional government after February kept Russia in the war.
**Answer: True** — war, hunger and land grievances remained — and October followed.
