# Video script — 1917 twice — February and October in Russia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a vast empire at war. At the front, lines break. In the capital, bread queues lengthen in the snow.
February: the crowd fills the streets; the soldiers lower their rifles and step aside. A throne stands empty.
The months pass. The war still eats. In October, one disciplined party moves through the night city — bridges, telegraph, palace.
The map recolours. A new kind of state stands where the empire stood, and the century will organise itself around its rivalry.
