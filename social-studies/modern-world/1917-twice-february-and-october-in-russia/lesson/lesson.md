# 1917 twice — February and October in Russia

## Do it first

One empire. One year. Two revolutions. In February the soldiers would not fire on the crowds, and a throne three centuries old simply ended. In October a small, disciplined party seized the capital in a night. Between those two moments lies the birth of a new kind of state.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Czar's Russia and its secret opponents

Russia entered the First World War ruled by the Czars' autocracy — one man's power over a vast empire of peasants, with no parliament that could truly check him. War turned hardship into crisis: defeats at the front, hunger in the cities, and a government that answered suffering with police. Discontented sectors of Russian society organised in secret, because open opposition was forbidden.

The lesson names three currents. The liberals wanted parliamentary government — a czar limited by law and an elected assembly. The Social Revolutionaries spoke to the peasants, Russia's great majority, urging them to seize the landlords' land. And the most radical group, the Marxists, followed Karl Marx: they held that the workers themselves must take power and rebuild society root and branch.

## Sub lesson 2 — February, then October

The February Revolution came first. As hunger protests filled Petrograd, the capital, the troops sent to disperse the crowds refused — and the Czar's autocracy, unsupported, collapsed. A provisional government of liberals took its place, promising reform but keeping Russia in the war that was devouring it. The grievances that had felled the Czar — war, hunger, land — remained exactly where they were.

So October came. The Marxists — organised, disciplined, and promising peace, land and bread — took power in the October Revolution. Out of the war's ruins arose the world's first communist state: a state built on Marx's ideas, ruled by one party, which would stand for most of the twentieth century as the great alternative — and the great rival — to the capitalist West.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the year twice over: February — the Czar falls; October — the Marxists rise. Name the three opposition currents and say which one October crowned.
