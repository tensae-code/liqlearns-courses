# The Renaissance — its great figures — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Leonardo's two most famous paintings.
**Answer:** The Mona Lisa and the Last Supper.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Keep Galileo and Newton distinct in one sentence each.
**Answer:** Galileo improved the telescope's use and studied the heavens and physics; Newton developed calculus and gave the law of gravity.
