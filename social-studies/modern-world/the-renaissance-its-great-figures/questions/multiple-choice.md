# The Renaissance — its great figures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shakespeare wrote —
a) The Mona Lisa  b) Hamlet, Romeo and Juliet, Julius Caesar and Macbeth  c) The gravity law  d) The 95 theses
**Answer: b) Hamlet, Romeo and Juliet, Julius Caesar and Macbeth** — Four plays the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Leonardo da Vinci was known above all for —
a) The Creation of Adam  b) The Mona Lisa and the Last Supper  c) Inventing the telescope  d) The Spinning Jenny
**Answer: b) The Mona Lisa and the Last Supper** — Painter of the Mona Lisa and the Last Supper — and master of many trades.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Creation of Adam was contributed by —
a) Leonardo  b) Shakespeare  c) Michelangelo  d) Copernicus
**Answer: c) Michelangelo** — Michelangelo (1475–1564).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Copernicus established that —
a) The Earth is the centre  b) The Sun is the centre and Earth revolves around it  c) The Earth is flat  d) The Moon circles the Sun
**Answer: b) The Sun is the centre and Earth revolves around it** — The Polish astronomer's finding dethroned the Earth.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Newton developed —
a) The telescope  b) Calculus, and the law of gravity  c) The printing of plays  d) The compass
**Answer: b) Calculus, and the law of gravity** — Newton: calculus and gravity's law. Galileo: the telescope's improved use.
