# The Renaissance — its great figures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Leonardo was a master of many trades, not painting alone.
**Answer: True** — Painter, sculptor, musician, engineer, scientist.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Galileo's named contribution in this lesson is the improved use of the telescope.
**Answer: True** — The telescope is Galileo's; the gravity law is Newton's.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Copernicus taught that Earth stands still at the centre.
**Answer: False** — He established the Sun as the centre, with Earth revolving.
