# Hands-on — The Renaissance — its great figures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five cards
Make five cards, one per figure. Front: the name and dates. Back: the work. Shuffle and test a partner — name from work, work from name.

## Centre test
Hold a lamp as the Sun. Walk a ball — the Earth — around it while a partner walks a smaller ball around you. You have just acted Copernicus: Earth moves, Sun stays central.
