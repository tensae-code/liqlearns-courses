# The world after 1945 — losses, division and rebuilding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** American rebuilding money for Western Europe was the ____ Plan.
**Answer: Marshall**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The institution built to lend for reconstruction and development is the ____ Bank.
**Answer: World**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The IMF is the International ____ Fund.
**Answer: Monetary**
