# The world after 1945 — losses, division and rebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The war left millions homeless or displaced and whole economies in ruins.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Europe emerged from the war united under one government.
**Answer: False — Germany and Europe were divided between the two superpowers' spheres.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The welfare-state idea spread in the recovering West after 1945.
**Answer: True**
