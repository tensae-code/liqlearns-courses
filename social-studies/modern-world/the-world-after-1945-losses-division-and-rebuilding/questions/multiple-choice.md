# The world after 1945 — losses, division and rebuilding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 12 book opens its post-1945 unit with —
a) A celebration parade  b) An inventory of the war's aftermath  c) A biography of Stalin  d) A map of Africa
**Answer: b) An inventory of the war's aftermath — Losses first, then the new order.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After 1945, Germany was —
a) Unified under France  b) Divided — west under the Western Allies, east under the Soviet Union  c) Given to Poland  d) Made neutral and disarmed only
**Answer: b) Divided — west under the Western Allies, east under the Soviet Union — Europe divided with it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Marshall Plan was —
a) A Soviet rebuilding plan  b) American money poured into Western Europe's rebuilding  c) A German reparations scheme  d) A UN military plan
**Answer: b) American money poured into Western Europe's rebuilding — Factories restarted and trade revived in the West.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A welfare state is one where the state —
a) Owns all factories  b) Takes responsibility for health, housing, education and support in unemployment and old age  c) Bans parties  d) Rules by army
**Answer: b) Takes responsibility for health, housing, education and support in unemployment and old age — The post-war promise in the recovering West.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The World Bank was built to —
a) Command armies  b) Lend for reconstruction and development  c) Print a world currency  d) Run the UN
**Answer: b) Lend for reconstruction and development — The IMF steadied currencies and trade.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The IMF's work was to —
a) Steady currencies and trade between nations  b) Collect taxes worldwide  c) Set farm prices  d) Build railways
**Answer: a) Steady currencies and trade between nations — The International Monetary Fund's founding purpose.**
