# The world after 1945 — losses, division and rebuilding — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: give the aftermath inventory's three heads.
**Answer: Human and material losses on a continental scale; Germany and Europe divided; two superpowers standing and a demand for a new world organisation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: how did the West rebuild, in the book's account?
**Answer: By the Marshall Plan's American money, and by welfare states taking responsibility for citizens' health, housing, education and support.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: what do the World Bank and the IMF each do?
**Answer: The World Bank lends for reconstruction and development; the IMF steadies currencies and trade between nations.**
