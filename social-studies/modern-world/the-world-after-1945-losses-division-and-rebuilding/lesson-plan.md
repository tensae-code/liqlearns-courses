# Lesson plan — The world after 1945 — losses, division and rebuilding

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The world after 1945 — losses, division and rebuilding
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 12 History, Unit 5 (§5) — Global and Regional Developments Since 1945 (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked into this title in the Ethiopia Wave 4 History fix build.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Grade 12 book inventories the Second World War's aftermath before it builds the new order: human and material losses, a Germany and a Europe divided, two superpowers — then the rebuilding: Marshall Plan recovery, welfare states, and the World Bank and IMF.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on this title's Second World War lessons. It needs nothing else.
