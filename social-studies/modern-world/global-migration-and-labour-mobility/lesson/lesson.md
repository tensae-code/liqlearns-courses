# Global migration and labour mobility

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Economic influence on global migration / Labour mobility

Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit
