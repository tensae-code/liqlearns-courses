# Kuwait's constitution and political life — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait's constitution came before its independence.
**Answer: False** — independence was 1961, the constitution 1962

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Women's political rights are part of Kuwait's constitutional story.
**Answer: True** — learners meet Kuwaiti women's political rights
