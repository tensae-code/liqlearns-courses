# Kuwait's constitution and political life

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In 1962 Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — Father of the Constitution — proclaimed Kuwait's constitution, founding a political life of sovereignty, democracy, human rights and women's political rights. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Development of political life in Kuwait

The father of the constitution, sheikh abdullah al salem, proclaims the 1962 constitution, and learners meet sovereignty, democracy, human rights and kuwaiti women's political rights.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
