# The Weimar Republic — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rosa Luxemburg's Spartacus programme (Document) asks what the League wants; the republic is born between revolution and Freikorps, and Wirsching's Storiografia traces its democratic constitution's origins.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Anteprima defends Weimar against hindsight — an advanced party democracy that survived its birth traumas and fell, above all, to the world crisis's political fallout; Schulze's Fare storia piece weighs its difficulties.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar
