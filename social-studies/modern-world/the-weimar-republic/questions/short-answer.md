# The Weimar Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic.
**Answer: Rosa Luxemburg's Spartacus programme (Document) asks what the League wants; the republic is born between revolution and Freikorps, and Wirsching's Storiografia traces its democratic constitution's origins (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic, printed p. 211.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar.
**Answer: the Anteprima defends Weimar against hindsight — an advanced party democracy that survived its birth traumas and fell, above all, to the world crisis's political fallout; Schulze's Fare storia piece weighs its difficulties (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar, printed p. 53.**
