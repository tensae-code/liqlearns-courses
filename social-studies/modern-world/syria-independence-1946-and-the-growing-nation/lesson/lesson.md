# Syria — independence 1946 and the growing nation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Under French colonialism Syria's people numbered three million; after independence in 1946 schools and hospitals spread, deaths fell, and the people grew past twenty-three million. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Syria — independence 1946 and the growing nation

Hassan asks why the population keeps growing and the teacher tells the story: under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals, and many of its youths were martyred defending the homeland; after independence from French colonialism in 1946 the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew past 23 million by 2010.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
