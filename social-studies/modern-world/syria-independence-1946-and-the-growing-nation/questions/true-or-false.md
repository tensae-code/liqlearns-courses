# Syria — independence 1946 and the growing nation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hassan asks why the population keeps growing and the teacher tells the story: under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals, and many of its youths were martyred defending the homeland; after independence from French colonialism in 1946 the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew past 23 million by 2010.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 3 الحياة في بلدي سورية, Lesson السكان في بلدي سورية (Population in my country Syria)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الرابع (Social Studies, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Syria — independence 1946 and the growing nation
