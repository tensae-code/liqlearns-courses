## Sub lesson 1 — Syria — independence 1946 and the growing nation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Hassan asks why the population keeps growing and the teacher tells the story: under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals, and many of its youths were martyred defending the homeland; after independence from French colonialism in 1946 the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew past 23 million by 2010.
