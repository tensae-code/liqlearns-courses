# Video script — Colombia in the 19th century — daily life, science and women

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Washerwomen, naturalists, market days and modernismo measure how everyday Colombia changes across the 19th century.

Washerwomen work outside Bogotá in 1830, naturalists catalogue over 20,000 plant and 7,000 animal species, modernismo arrives in literature, market days end in the chicherías, and women — 10% of the country then, near 40% of the workforce now — measure the century's change.
