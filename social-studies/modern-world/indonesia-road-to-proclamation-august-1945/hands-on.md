# Hands-on — Indonesia — the road to the Proclamation, August 1945

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Indonesia — the road to the Proclamation, August 1945 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bab IV.A-B Kekalahan-Kekalahan Jepang / Menuju Proklamasi, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
