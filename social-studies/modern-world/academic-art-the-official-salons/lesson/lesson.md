# Academic art — the official salons

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: Academic art — the official salons. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The academy stands against revolt

Against every revolt stands the academy.
