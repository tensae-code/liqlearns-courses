# Hands-on — Academic art — the official salons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw an official salon hung with polished mythological and historical canvases from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: The academy stands against revolt; Salons, prizes and polished canvases; The establishment to rebel against. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
