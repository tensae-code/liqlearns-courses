# Academic art — the official salons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Salons, prizes and polished canvases  b) The academy stands against revolt  c) The establishment to rebel against
**Answer: b) The academy stands against revolt** — Against every revolt stands the academy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Salons, prizes and polished canvases  b) The academy stands against revolt  c) The establishment to rebel against
**Answer: a) Salons, prizes and polished canvases** — Official salons, state prizes and polished mythological and historical canvases define what governments call good art.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The academy stands against revolt  b) Salons, prizes and polished canvases  c) The establishment to rebel against
**Answer: c) The establishment to rebel against** — The academy is the establishment the young rebels of the century define themselves against.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Against every revolt stands the academy — official salons, state prizes and polished mythological and historical canvases define what governments call good art, the establishment the young rebels of the century define themselves against  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
