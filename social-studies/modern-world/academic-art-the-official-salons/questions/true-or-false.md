# Academic art — the official salons — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Official salons, state prizes and polished mythological and historical canvases define what governments call good art.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Against every revolt stands the academy — official salons, state prizes and polished mythological and historical canvases define what governments call good art, the establishment the young rebels of the century define themselves against.
