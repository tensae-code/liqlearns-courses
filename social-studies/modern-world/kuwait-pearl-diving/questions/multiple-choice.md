# Pearl diving — Kuwait's hard sea harvest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pearl crews sailed —
a) for the season  b) for a single day  c) only in winter storms
**Answer: a) for the season** — crews sail for the season

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Life aboard the pearl ships bound the men in —
a) solidarity  b) rivalry  c) silence
**Answer: a) solidarity** — life aboard binds men in solidarity

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The trade later faded because —
a) men turned away from its hardships  b) the pearls ran out in one year  c) ships were banned
**Answer: a) men turned away from its hardships** — the trade fades as men turn away from its hardships
