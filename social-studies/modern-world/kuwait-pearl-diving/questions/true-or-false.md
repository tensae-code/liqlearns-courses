# Pearl diving — Kuwait's hard sea harvest — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pearl diving was easy, safe work.
**Answer: False** — men turned away from its hardships

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The whole town shared the pearl season — crews at sea, families waiting ashore.
**Answer: True** — women and children wait to welcome the fleet home
