# Pearl diving — Kuwait's hard sea harvest

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: For generations Kuwait's men sailed each season to dive for pearls — a trade of solidarity aboard, waiting families ashore, and hardships that finally turned men away. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pearl diving — the season, the ship, the farewell

Crews sail for the season, life aboard binds men in solidarity, women and children wait to welcome the fleet home, and the trade later fades as men turn away from its hardships.
