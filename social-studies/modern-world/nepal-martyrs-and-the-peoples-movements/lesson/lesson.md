# Nepal — martyrs and the people's movements

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: under 104 years of Rana rule Shukraraj, Gangalal, Dharmabhakta and Dasharath Chand are killed for opposing it; martyrs fall again under the 30-year Panchayat and in the Second People's Movement of 2062–63 BS, which restores people's rights as Loktantra As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
