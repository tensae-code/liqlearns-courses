# Hands-on — The Second World War — the Pacific and the results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Island stepping stones
Lay paper 'islands' across the floor. Step only on islands, from 'Japan's 1942 lands' toward 'Japan'. That island-by-island advance is how the American forces closed in.

## Two results lists
Put the First World War's results beside this war's results (both lessons give them). Circle what repeats — a world body founded, America rising — and star what is new: atomic weapons used, a Cold War set off.
