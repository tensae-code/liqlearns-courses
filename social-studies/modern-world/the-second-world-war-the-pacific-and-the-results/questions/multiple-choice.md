# The Second World War — the Pacific and the results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan's 1942 invasion took —
a) The Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia and New Guinea  b) Only Hawaii  c) India and Burma only  d) Australia
**Answer: a) The Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia and New Guinea** — Four lands named for the 1942 sweep.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The American navy checked Japan's advance and —
a) Withdrew  b) Defeated Japan repeatedly and took many islands  c) Signed a peace in 1942  d) Bombed Pearl Harbor
**Answer: b) Defeated Japan repeatedly and took many islands** — Checked, defeated repeatedly, islands taken — then air strikes on Japan.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Atomic bombs fell on Hiroshima and Nagasaki on —
a) June 6 and 9, 1944  b) August 6 and 9, 1945  c) December 7 and 9, 1941  d) April 25 and 30, 1945
**Answer: b) August 6 and 9, 1945** — August 6 (Hiroshima) and August 9 (Nagasaki), 1945.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two powers emerged as great powers?
a) Britain and France  b) America and Russia  c) Germany and Japan  d) Italy and Spain
**Answer: b) America and Russia** — America and Russia — the pair the Cold War lesson follows.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The world body founded after this war was —
a) The League of Nations  b) The United Nations  c) The Triple Entente  d) The Axis
**Answer: b) The United Nations** — The United Nations replaced the League.
