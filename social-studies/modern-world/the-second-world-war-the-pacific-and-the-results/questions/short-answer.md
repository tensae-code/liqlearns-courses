# The Second World War — the Pacific and the results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How was Japan's Pacific advance reversed?
**Answer:** The American navy checked the advance, defeated Japan repeatedly and took many islands, from which the American air force struck Japan; after the atomic bombs of August 1945 Japan accepted defeat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the Second World War's results as the book gives them.
**Answer:** Heavy loss of life and property; communism spread; America and Russia emerging as great powers; the Cold War set off; states' borders changed; the anti-colonial struggle quickened; the United Nations founded.
