# The Second World War — the Pacific and the results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan's government accepted defeat after the atomic bombs.
**Answer: True** — Unable to withstand the attack, Japan accepted defeat and the war ended in Asia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The anti-colonial struggle slowed down after the war.
**Answer: False** — It quickened — one of the book's results.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Cold War was set off as one of the war's results.
**Answer: True** — Named in the results list; the next lesson takes it up.
