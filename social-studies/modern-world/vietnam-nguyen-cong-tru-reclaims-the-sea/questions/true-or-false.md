# Vietnam Nguyen Cong Tru Reclaims The Sea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** sent as Doanh điền sứ, he gathers the landless poor to dyke the sea and founds the Tiền Hải and Kim Sơn districts; grateful settlers build his temple while he is still alive and call him "the ancestor who opened the land".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 13, land reclamation under the Nguyễn and the story of Nguyễn Công Trứ

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 13, land reclamation under the Nguyễn and the story of Nguyễn Công Trứ
