# Hands-on — Bolivia — the War of the Pacific

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bolivia — the War of the Pacific — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, La Guerra del Pacífico, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
