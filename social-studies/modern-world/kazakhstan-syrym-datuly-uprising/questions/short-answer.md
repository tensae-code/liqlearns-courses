**Answer: Syrym Datuly leads the Junior Zhuz against both the tsar's officials and a puppet khan, and Igelstrom's reform meant to abolish khan power collapses (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: Syrym Datuly's 14-year war in the Junior Zhuz fights tsar and puppet khan alike (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Kazakhstan's book, where is this part taught?
