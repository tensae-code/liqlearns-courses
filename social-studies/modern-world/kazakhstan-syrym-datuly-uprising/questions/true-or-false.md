# Kazakhstan Syrym Datuly Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Syrym Datuly leads the Junior Zhuz against both the tsar's officials and a puppet khan, and Igelstrom's reform meant to abolish khan power collapses.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§19–20 The national-liberation movement of 1783–1797 led by Syrym Datuly

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§19–20 The national-liberation movement of 1783–1797 led by Syrym Datuly

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Syrym Datuly's 14-year war in the Junior Zhuz fights tsar and puppet khan alike.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§21–22 The national-liberation movement led by Syrym Datov

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§21–22 The national-liberation movement led by Syrym Datov

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Syrym's demands are read as a rights programme — pasture, khan election, no Cossack raids.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4.2 Which rights of the Junior Zhuz Kazakhs did Syrym Datuly defend?

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §4.2 Which rights of the Junior Zhuz Kazakhs did Syrym Datuly defend?
