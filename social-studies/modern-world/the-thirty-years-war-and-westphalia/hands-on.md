# Hands-on — The Thirty Years War And Westphalia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Thirty Years War And Westphalia — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unitatea IV, Lecția 6: Europa în timpul Războiului de 30 de Ani, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
