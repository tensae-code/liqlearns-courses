# Hands-on — Popular Fronts Against Fascism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Popular Fronts Against Fascism — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Chuyên đề 2.I.3 The Popular Fronts: communist, socialist and patriotic forces unite against fascism; France's Popular Front reforms and holds the republic; Spain's republic is defended by International Brigades from 53 countries; Indochina's Democratic Front gathers forces in 1936–1939, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
