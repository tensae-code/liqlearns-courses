# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — The fall of Kandy, 1815

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — The fall of Kandy, 1815 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The invasions of Kandy and the 1815 cession, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
