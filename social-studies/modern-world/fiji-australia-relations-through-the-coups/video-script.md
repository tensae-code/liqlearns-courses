# Video script — Fiji and Australia — relations through the coups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Australia recognises Fiji at independence in 1970; the two 1987 coups take Canberra by surprise; Australian newspaper cartoons of the 2000 coup and hostage crisis, the Great Council of Chiefs and the tourism collapse are interrogated as sources for Australia's opinion of Fiji.
