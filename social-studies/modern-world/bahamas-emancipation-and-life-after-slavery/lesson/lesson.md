# Bahamas — emancipation and life after slavery

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Emancipation reaches Bahamian slaves in the 1830s, and freed people build their own villages — Adelaide, Gambier and Fox Hill — winning some land but facing a property-only vote and schools that stop early, so Government High School is founded to give Black Bahamians more. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Africans Freeds: emancipation and the freed villages

news of emancipation reaches Bahamian slaves in 1835; freed people build settlements at Adelaide, Gambier and Fox Hill, some win land from former owners, but whites control business, the vote needs property, schools stop at elementary level — so Government High School is founded to give Black Bahamians more (Africans Freeds: emancipation and the freed villages)
