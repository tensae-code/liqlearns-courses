# Bahamas — emancipation and life after slavery — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** news of emancipation reaches Bahamian slaves in 1835; freed people build settlements at Adelaide, Gambier and Fox Hill, some win land from former owners, but whites control business, the vote needs property, schools stop at elementary level — so Government High School is founded to give Black Bahamians more
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Africans Freeds: emancipation and the freed villages).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** the enslaved who worked and lived in The Bahamas establish some of its oldest communities — Adelaide, Fox Hill and Bain Town — and freed slaves from America choose the islands as their new home
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bahamian History Prior to Independence 1973 Part 2: the slave-built communities).
