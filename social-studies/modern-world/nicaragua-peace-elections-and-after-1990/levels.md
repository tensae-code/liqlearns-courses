# Nicaragua Peace Elections And After 1990 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pacificación en Nicaragua: Esquipulas I y II
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Central America's presidents meet at Esquipulas and on 6-7 Aug 1987 sign procedures for a firm and lasting peace, choosing coexistence over war

## Sub lesson 2 — Elecciones de febrero de 1990 y Nicaragua desde 1990 hasta la actualidad
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the UNO wins the February 1990 elections and power changes hands by ballot, opening the Nicaragua of today

## Sub lesson 3 — Gobiernos liberales 1996-2000 y 2001-2006
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Alemán and Bolaños govern on free-market policies; the book charges corruption, privatised electricity and telephones, and rolled-back free health and education

## Sub lesson 4 — Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional y sus programas sociales
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the GRUN returns with social programmes presented as restoring what the liberal governments removed

## Sub lesson 5 — La lucha armada y los acuerdos de paz
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — US-financed Contra war and Reagan's 1985 embargo besiege the Revolution; pressure aims to force negotiation, and peace talks follow

## Sub lesson 6 — Elecciones de 1990
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — under the peace accords, the US-backed UNO coalition of Violeta Barrios de Chamorro wins the 25 February 1990 elections with 54.74 percent to the FSLN's 40.82

## Sub lesson 7 — Transformaciones del gobierno de la UNO
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Chamorro abolishes patriotic military service, demobilises both armies and burns the weapons, while agrarian-reform projects are dismantled and rearmed ex-combatants reappear

## Sub lesson 8 — Los gobiernos neoliberales 1996-2006
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Alemán (1997) and Bolaños (2002) continue structural adjustment under IMF conditions while health and education are neglected, until debt relief under the HIPC initiative is negotiated

## Sub lesson 9 — El Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional desde 2007
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the returning GRUN runs its social programmes through women — Bono Productivo, Usura Cero, Hambre Cero, Plan Techo — under a "Christian, socialist, solidary" banner

## Sub lesson 10 — La lucha armada y los acuerdos de paz
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — from 1981 the US funds the Contra army and imposes a trade embargo; the presidents' peace process answers, and Ortega's 1984 election with 67 percent is recognised abroad except in Washington

## Sub lesson 11 — El Gobierno de la UNO y los gobiernos neoliberales
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the UNO's Violeta Chamorro wins 55 percent in 1990 on war-weariness; demobilisation, privatisation and structural adjustment follow, Alemán wins in 1996, and unemployment and emigration to Costa Rica and the US deepen

## Sub lesson 12 — El Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional desde 2007
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the returning GRUN builds a "Christian, socialist, solidary" model of direct citizen power, running its programmes through women — Bono Productivo, Usura Cero, Plan Techo

## Sub lesson 13 — Los nuevos problemas y el modelo de poder ciudadano
Sub lesson 13 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the book closes the century by turning to the GRUN's Christian, Socialist and Solidary citizen-power model, women's advance into political power after the 1920 US suffrage amendment, and the region's new problems of irregular migration and child poverty
