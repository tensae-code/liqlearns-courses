# Nicaragua Peace Elections And After 1990

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Central America's presidents meet at Esquipulas and on 6-7 Aug 1987 sign procedures for a firm and lasting peace, choosing coexistence over war As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
