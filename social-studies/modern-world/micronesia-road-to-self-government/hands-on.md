# Hands-on — Micronesia — the road to self-government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell it in island words
Choose a big idea — freedom, unity, patience — and make a one-minute story from your own place's animals, sea or land that explains it, as Amaraich did with the octopus. Tell it; your companion must say the idea back from the story alone.

## Four districts, one constitution
In groups of four 'districts', draft three rules all four can accept for a shared federation — then try adding a fifth group with different demands, and feel why the Marshalls and Palau story happened.
