# Micronesia — the road to self-government — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The octopus in the meetinghouse
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that in 1965 Congress of Micronesia member Andon Amaraich told the octopus story — independence killed and scattered like the octopus's arms, its blood waiting in the rafters to come back together — to explain self-rule in island words.

## Sub lesson 2 — Negotiations — and the Marshalls and Palau go their own way
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that the Congress of Micronesia led status negotiations with the United States, and the Marshalls and Palau chose to go their own way, while four districts approved a constitution creating the FSM federation.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pressing America, appealing to the UN — and the founding fathers
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that the Congress's young leaders spent years pressing the United States and appealing to the United Nations when America stalled, until the Trust Territory won independence, and that the book honours the leaders as the FSM's founding fathers.
