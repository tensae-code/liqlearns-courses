# Micronesia — the road to self-government — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Four districts approved a constitution creating the Federated States of Micronesia.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 9 book teaches four districts approved the constitution and created the federation.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The independence fight was won in a single negotiation.
**Answer: False** — the government book teaches years of pressing the United States, with appeals to the United Nations when America stalled.
