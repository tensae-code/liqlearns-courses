# Micronesia — the road to self-government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Andon Amaraich told the octopus story in —
a) 1947  b) 1965  c) 1979
**Answer: b) 1965** — the year the government book gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the story, independence is like the octopus because it was —
a) Killed and scattered, but waiting to come back together  b) Too strong to catch  c) Hidden under the sea forever
**Answer: a) Killed and scattered, but waiting to come back together** — the story as the book tells it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The districts that left the federation path to go their own way were —
a) Yap and Chuuk  b) The Marshalls and Palau  c) Kosrae and Pohnpei
**Answer: b) The Marshalls and Palau** — the districts the Grade 9 book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** When America stalled, the Congress's leaders appealed to —
a) The United Nations  b) Japan  c) The League of Nations
**Answer: a) The United Nations** — the appeal the government book records.
