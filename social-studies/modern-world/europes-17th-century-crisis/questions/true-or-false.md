# Europes 17th Century Crisis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** population dips, harvests shrink in a colder century and Mediterranean Europe stagnates while the North Sea economies quietly grow.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 6, Cap. 18 §§1–2 An age of great changes; Production and markets: the Europe that declines and the one that grows

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 6, Cap. 18 §§1–2 An age of great changes; Production and markets: the Europe that declines and the one that grows

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Spanish Italy stagnates, revolts shake Naples and the provinces, and the peninsula's primacy passes north.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in L'Italia dopo Cateau-Cambrésis; Industria e commercio nel '600; Il settore agricolo; Le rivolte

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about L'Italia dopo Cateau-Cambrésis; Industria e commercio nel '600; Il settore agricolo; Le rivolte
