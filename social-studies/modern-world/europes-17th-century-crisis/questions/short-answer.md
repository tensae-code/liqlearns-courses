# Europes 17th Century Crisis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 6, Cap. 18 §§1–2 An age of great changes; Production and markets: the Europe that declines and the one that grows.
**Answer: population dips, harvests shrink in a colder century and Mediterranean Europe stagnates while the North Sea economies quietly grow (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 6, Cap. 18 §§1–2 An age of great changes; Production and markets: the Europe that declines and the one that grows, printed pp. 550, 557.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: L'Italia dopo Cateau-Cambrésis; Industria e commercio nel '600; Il settore agricolo; Le rivolte.
**Answer: Spanish Italy stagnates, revolts shake Naples and the provinces, and the peninsula's primacy passes north (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), L'Italia dopo Cateau-Cambrésis; Industria e commercio nel '600; Il settore agricolo; Le rivolte, printed pp. 623, 632, 635, 636.**
