# Video script — Colombia — modernisation and rival ideals of the State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the 1920s Colombia enters modernisation — farm frontier, awakening economy, rural malaise — while rival ideals of the State collide over who shares in it.

By the late 1920s Colombia enters modernisation with most people barely participating in its cultural, social and economic processes, and rival ideals of the State collide.

The farm frontier expands and industry starts late, the 1920s economy awakens, and rural discontent builds over the agrarian structure.
