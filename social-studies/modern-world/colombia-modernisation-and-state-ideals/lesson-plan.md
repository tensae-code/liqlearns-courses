# Lesson plan — Colombia — modernisation and rival ideals of the State

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Colombia — modernisation and rival ideals of the State
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Colombia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** from the 1920s Colombia enters modernisation — farm frontier, awakening economy, rural malaise — while rival ideals of the State collide over who shares in it
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 6: The impact of modernisation in Colombia** — by the late 1920s Colombia enters modernisation with most people barely participating in its cultural, social and economic processes, and rival ideals of the State collide
2. **Temas 6–8: Expansion of the agricultural frontier, the awakening of the economy in the twenties, rural malaise** — the farm frontier expands and industry starts late, the 1920s economy awakens, and rural discontent builds over the agrarian structure

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
