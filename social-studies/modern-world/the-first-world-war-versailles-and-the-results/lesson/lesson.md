# The First World War — Versailles and the results

## Sub lesson 1 — Versailles — the victors' terms

After the war, the **Paris Peace Conference** was called in **1919** to sign peace between victors and defeated. It was led by the **four victor powers** — America's President **Woodrow Wilson**, Britain's **David Lloyd George**, France's **Georges Clemenceau** and Italy's **Vittorio Emanuele Orlando**, as printed in the book. After months, the treaty was signed with Germany at **Versailles**, with measures that **would not spare her**: Germany was made to **pay the victors much money** and was **stripped of her African and Asian colonies**. Hold the colonies point: the imperial scramble that helped cause the war ended by re-dividing the loser's colonies among the winners.

## Sub lesson 2 — The war's results

The book's results list is the war's true size. **Ten million soldiers dead** and **about twenty million wounded**. **Massive property destruction and economic crisis in Europe.** **America rising as a great power** while Europe bled. **New states founded** from the fallen empires. **Kingly governments abolished in Russia, Germany and Austria.** And the **League of Nations established** — the victors' attempt at a body that would stop the next war before it started. The next lessons test that hope: within a generation came dictators, a second world war, and the League's replacement.
