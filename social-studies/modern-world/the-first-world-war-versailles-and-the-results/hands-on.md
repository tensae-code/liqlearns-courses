# Hands-on — The First World War — Versailles and the results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bill on the table
Write a mock treaty page: two clauses only — 'Germany pays…' and 'Germany's colonies…'. Sign it as one of the Big Four. Then write one line on how the losing side would read it.

## Count the cost
Write the two numbers large: 10,000,000 dead; 20,000,000 wounded. Under each, write one human sentence — a village, a family — so the numbers stay human.
