# The First World War — Versailles and the results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The peace conference was called at Paris in —
a) 1914  b) 1918  c) 1919  d) 1925
**Answer: c) 1919** — The Paris Peace Conference, 1919.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The treaty with Germany was signed at —
a) Berlin  b) Versailles  c) Sarajevo  d) Stalingrad
**Answer: b) Versailles** — Versailles — the treaty carries the place's name.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Versailles, Germany was stripped of —
a) Her navy only  b) Her African and Asian colonies  c) Her capital city  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Her African and Asian colonies** — And made to pay the victors much money.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The war left about how many soldiers dead?
a) One million  b) Ten million  c) Twenty million  d) Fifty million
**Answer: b) Ten million** — Ten million dead; about twenty million wounded.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Kingly governments were abolished in —
a) Britain, France, America  b) Russia, Germany and Austria  c) Italy, Spain, Turkey  d) Only Russia
**Answer: b) Russia, Germany and Austria** — Russia, Germany and Austria lost their kingly governments.
