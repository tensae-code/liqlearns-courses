# The First World War — Versailles and the results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** America's President at the conference was Woodrow Wilson.
**Answer: True** — Wilson leads the book's list of the four victor powers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The League of Nations was established after the war.
**Answer: True** — One of the war's results — the attempt to prevent the next war.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Versailles spared Germany all payments.
**Answer: False** — The measures would not spare her: she paid much money and lost her colonies.
