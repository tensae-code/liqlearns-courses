# The First World War — Versailles and the results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the Big Four and their countries, as printed.
**Answer:** Woodrow Wilson (America), David Lloyd George (Britain), Georges Clemenceau (France), Vittorio Emanuele Orlando (Italy).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the war's results in the book's order.
**Answer:** Ten million soldiers dead and about twenty million wounded; massive property destruction and economic crisis in Europe; America rising as a great power; new states founded; kingly governments abolished in Russia, Germany and Austria; the League of Nations established.
