# Armenia Hamidian Massacres And Genocide Policy Beginnings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 1.1 The beginning of the genocide policy in the Ottoman Empire.
**Answer: Sultan Abdul Hamid II answers the Armenian Question with violence instead of the promised reforms — his Hamidiye regiments and the 1894–96 massacres at Sasun, Zeitun and Constantinople open the road to extermination (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* (Armenia), 1.1 The beginning of the genocide policy in the Ottoman Empire, printed p. 10.**
