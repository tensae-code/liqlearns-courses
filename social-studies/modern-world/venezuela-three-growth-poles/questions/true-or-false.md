# Venezuela Three Growth Poles — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Caracas, the oil west and Guayana grow as three poles; ports are rebuilt for oil, Judibana rises as a garden-city camp, and a journey crosses the oil basins east to west.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Three poles of growth since the late nineteenth century

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Three poles of growth since the late nineteenth century

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The capital grows explosively on oil rent into a metropolis, the visible pole of the oil country's new geography.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Caracas in oil times: "Caracas, there it is..."
