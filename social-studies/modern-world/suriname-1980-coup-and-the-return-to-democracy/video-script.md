# Video script — Suriname — the 1980 coup and the return to democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Independence brings a Surinamese army in place of the Dutch TRIS; a military coup then puts soldiers in power over the young republic.

From 1985 talks seek free elections, but in July 1986 the interior war breaks out between the Jungle Commando and the National Army, devastating Albina, Pokigron and Moiwana's region in the east.
