# Hands-on — Suriname — the 1980 coup and the return to democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Suriname — the 1980 coup and the return to democracy — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Een staatsgreep in ons land, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
