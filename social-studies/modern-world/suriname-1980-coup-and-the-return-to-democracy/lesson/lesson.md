# Suriname — the 1980 coup and the return to democracy

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Independence brings a Surinamese army in place of the Dutch TRIS; a military coup then puts soldiers in power over the young republic As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
