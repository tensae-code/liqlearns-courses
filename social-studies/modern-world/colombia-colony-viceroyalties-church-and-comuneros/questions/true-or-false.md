# Colombia's colony — viceroyalties, Church and Comuneros — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Guía 1: What was the Colony?

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Primera Cartilla, Grado Quinto, Escuela Nueva* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Guía 1: What was the Colony?

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The colony organises labour and land through the mita turns, the encomienda grants and the resguardo towns, while the Church collects tithes and names parish priests
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Guía 12: Economy and colonial society in New Granada

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Grado 7, Postprimaria Rural (Sociales)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Guía 12: Economy and colonial society in New Granada

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Carlos V defines an administration for the Indies in 1524, officials face the Juicio de Residencia, the viceroyalty traces to the Capitulaciones de Santa Fe, and Reales Audiencias and the Cabildo — Spain's medieval town council adapted — govern America
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Guía 14: Colony — government and administration

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Grado 7, Postprimaria Rural (Sociales)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Guía 14: Colony — government and administration

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** France holds northern North America and Antillean islands and Holland is present too; the mita obliges a quota of Indigenous workers, colonial society ranks its castes with the enslaved at the bottom — a ranking North America's states already argue over — and colonised communities rapidly learn the imposed language while keeping their own
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unidad 2 / Temas 8–11: Political, economic, social and cultural aspects of colonial America
