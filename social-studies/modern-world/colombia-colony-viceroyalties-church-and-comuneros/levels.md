# Colombia's colony — viceroyalties, Church and Comuneros — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 1: What was the Colony?
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves

## Sub lesson 2 — Guía 12: Economy and colonial society in New Granada
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the colony organises labour and land through the mita turns, the encomienda grants and the resguardo towns, while the Church collects tithes and names parish priests

## Sub lesson 3 — Guía 14: Colony — government and administration
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Carlos V defines an administration for the Indies in 1524, officials face the Juicio de Residencia, the viceroyalty traces to the Capitulaciones de Santa Fe, and Reales Audiencias and the Cabildo — Spain's medieval town council adapted — govern America
