# Video script — Brazil — urbanisation and industrialisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Brazil's Grade 10 História SEED-PR tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every city was once a new idea.



The book begins with the first cities in history — the first places where people gathered in large numbers to live and work together.



Then it lands in Brazil. In the 20th century, industry pulled country people into the cities, and the cities grew explosively.



But they grew faster than homes, water and work could follow — and families lived inside that gap.
