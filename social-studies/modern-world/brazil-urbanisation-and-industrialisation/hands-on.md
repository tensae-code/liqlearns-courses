# Hands-on — Brazil — urbanisation and industrialisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pull and gap
Draw a factory on one side of a page and country homes on the other. Draw arrows for the people moving between them, and label the arrow with what pulled them. Underneath, write the three things the growing city could not provide fast enough.

## A city growing too fast
Draw a small city, then draw it again twice as big with the same few houses and water taps. Say out loud what life inside that second drawing would feel like.
