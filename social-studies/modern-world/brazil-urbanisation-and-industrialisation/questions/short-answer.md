# Brazil — urbanisation and industrialisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What pulled country people into Brazil's cities, and what did the cities grow faster than?
**Answer: Industry pulled them in; the cities grew faster than homes, water and work could follow (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book start with the first cities in history before reaching Brazil?
**Answer: Because a city is a new way of living with a history of its own — Brazil's explosive growth is the latest chapter of that long story (any close wording).**
