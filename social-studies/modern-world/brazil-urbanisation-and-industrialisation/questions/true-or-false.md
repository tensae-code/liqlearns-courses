# Brazil — urbanisation and industrialisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Brazil's urban growth in the 20th century was explosive.
**Answer: True — industry pulled country people in and the cities grew explosively.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Homes, water and work always kept up easily with Brazil's city growth.
**Answer: False — the cities grew faster than homes, water and work could follow.**
