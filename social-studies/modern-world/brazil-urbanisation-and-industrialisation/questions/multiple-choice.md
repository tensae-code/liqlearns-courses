# Brazil — urbanisation and industrialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book begins the story of cities with —
a) The first cities in history  b) Brazil in the 20th century only  c) Modern skyscrapers
**Answer: a) The first cities in history**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In 20th-century Brazil, country people were pulled into cities by —
a) Industry and its work  b) A law forbidding country life  c) The closing of all farms
**Answer: a) Industry and its work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Brazil's 20th-century urban growth was —
a) Explosive — very fast and very large  b) Slow and tiny  c) Stopped by the government
**Answer: a) Explosive — very fast and very large**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The growing cities grew faster than —
a) Homes, water and work could follow  b) The rivers could flow  c) People could walk
**Answer: a) Homes, water and work could follow**
