# Europe In The 1970s Democracies And Enlargement — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greece, Spain and Portugal exit dictatorship — Panagulis' fight for Greek democracy is the chapter's document — and knock on the Community's door; enlargement becomes democratisation's reward.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 17 §4 Europe between détente, political crises and the enlargement of the EEC

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 17 §4 Europe between détente, political crises and the enlargement of the EEC

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** disarmament talks run beside democratisation — Greece, Spain and Portugal join the West's club and the Community enlarges to receive them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 12 §§4–6 I negoziati sul disarmo; Mutamenti politici in Europa occidentale; Integrazione europea e nuove democrazie

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
