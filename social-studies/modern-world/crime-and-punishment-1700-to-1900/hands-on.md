# Hands-on — Crime and punishment, 1700 to 1900

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Crime and punishment, 1700 to 1900 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 3.1 Changing definitions of crime, c1700–c1900, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
