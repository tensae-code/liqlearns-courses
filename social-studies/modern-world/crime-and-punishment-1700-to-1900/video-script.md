# Video script — Crime and punishment, 1700 to 1900

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as the United Kingdom's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between 1700 and 1900, crime changes with trade and towns, the Bloody Code falls, and police and prisons take over.
