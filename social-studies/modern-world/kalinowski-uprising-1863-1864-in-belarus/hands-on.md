# Hands-on — The Kalinowski uprising, 1863–1864, in Belarus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The uprising of 1863–1864 in Belarus — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The uprising of 1863–1864 in Belarus, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
