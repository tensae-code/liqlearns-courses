# Argentina — building the state, 1860–1916 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four tools the book gives for building the Argentine state, and say what they turned the provinces into.
**Answer: An army, laws, schools and railways — they turned a loose set of provinces into a single country (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three changes do stories and images show remaking daily life in Buenos Aires and the pampas?
**Answer: Ports, trains and new towns, transforming the country in one generation (any close wording).**
