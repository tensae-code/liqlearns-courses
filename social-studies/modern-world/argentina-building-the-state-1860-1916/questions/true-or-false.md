# Argentina — building the state, 1860–1916 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before this period, Argentina was a loose set of provinces after decades of division.
**Answer: True** — the building of one national state starts from that division.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign investment tied Argentina's new wealth to markets across the ocean.
**Answer: True** — British and other foreign capital built the railways and meat-packing plants of the export boom.
