# Argentina — building the state, 1860–1916 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Between 1860 and 1916, Argentina's leaders built —
a) One national state out of a loose set of provinces  b) A set of separate kingdoms  c) An empire across the ocean
**Answer: a) One national state out of a loose set of provinces** — turning divided provinces into a single country.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these was one of the tools of that building?
a) Railways  b) Castles  c) Pyramids
**Answer: a) Railways** — along with the army, laws and schools.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Photographs and accounts of the period show Buenos Aires and the pampas —
a) Unchanged for a hundred years  b) Transformed in one generation  c) Empty of people
**Answer: b) Transformed in one generation** — ports, trains and new towns remade daily life.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Much of the capital that built Argentina's railways and meat-packing plants came from —
a) Britain and other foreign countries  b) The provinces' own gold mines only  c) No one — they cost nothing
**Answer: a) Britain and other foreign countries** — foreign capital built the machines of the export boom.
