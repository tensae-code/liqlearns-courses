# Hands-on — Argentina — building the state, 1860–1916

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four tools of a state
Draw four boxes. In each box, draw or write one tool the Argentine leaders used to build one state: an army, laws, schools, railways. Under each box, write in one sentence what that tool does to turn provinces into one country.

## Then and now in one picture
Draw two scenes side by side: Buenos Aires or the pampas before the change, and after one generation of ports, trains and new towns. Say out loud three differences between your two scenes.

## Where did the money come from?
Draw a simple line across a page for the ocean. On one side draw Argentina's railways and meat-packing plants; on the other side write where much of the capital came from. Under your drawing, write one sentence on what that tied Argentina's wealth to.
