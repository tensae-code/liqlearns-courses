# Video script — Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on the German lands — many states, two giants among them.

Prussia in the north. Austria in the south. Only one can lead.

First the quiet weapon: the Zollverein. Customs fall. Trade binds the states together.

Then the chancellor speaks: Blood and Iron!

War one. War two — Sadowa! Austria is shut out. War three — France falls.

Now the camera glides into a palace of mirrors — Versailles, in conquered France.

Crowns gleam. An empire is proclaimed. William of Prussia is Kaiser.

1871. Germany stands — proclaimed on French soil.
