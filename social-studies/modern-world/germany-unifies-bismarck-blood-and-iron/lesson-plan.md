# Lesson plan — Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Development of Capitalism and Nationalism 1815–1914 (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Germany unified under **Prussia** against its rival Austria: prepared by the **Zollverein**, driven by Bismarck's **'Blood and Iron'** through three wars — and in **1871 the German Empire was proclaimed at Versailles**, on French soil, with France ceding Alsace and Lorraine by the Treaty of Frankfurt.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Italy lesson just before it — the second great unification, won a harder way. It needs nothing else.

Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Blood and Iron — Prussia clears the road** — Explain the Zollverein's economic preparation, Bismarck's Blood and Iron policy, and the three wars that excluded Austria and then defeated France.
2. **Versailles 1871 — an empire on French soil** — State the Treaty of Frankfurt terms (10 May 1871: Alsace and Lorraine ceded, huge indemnities) and the proclamation of the German Empire at Versailles with William of Prussia as Kaiser.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain how the Zollverein prepared unification without war.
2. Describe Bismarck's Blood and Iron policy and Prussia's three wars.
3. State the Treaty of Frankfurt's terms and where and when the German Empire was proclaimed.
