# Hands-on — Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three doors
Draw three doors labelled with Prussia's three obstacles: Denmark's duchies question, Austria, France. Under each, write what the war through that door achieved. Sadowa belongs under Austria's door.

## The insult mapped
On a sketch map, mark Alsace and Lorraine and shade them as France's loss in 1871. Then mark Versailles with a star and write one sentence: why proclaim an empire in the enemy's palace?
