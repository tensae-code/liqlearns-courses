# Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Germany unified under the leadership of —
a) Austria  b) Prussia  c) Bavaria  d) France
**Answer: b) Prussia** — Unification ran under Prussia, against its rival Austria.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Zollverein prepared unification by —
a) Winning three wars  b) Tying the German states together in a customs union  c) Crowning the Kaiser  d) Defeating France
**Answer: b) Tying the German states together in a customs union** — The customs union prepared unification economically.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bismarck's policy is summed up in the slogan —
a) 'Peace and Trade'  b) 'Blood and Iron'  c) 'Liberty, Equality, Fraternity'  d) 'One People, One Church'
**Answer: b) 'Blood and Iron'** — 'Blood and Iron' — unification by armies.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The battle the book's exercise fixes among Prussia's wars is —
a) Waterloo  b) Sadowa  c) Adwa  d) Gettysburg
**Answer: b) Sadowa** — Sadowa — the victory that excluded Austria.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** By the Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May 1871), France ceded —
a) Alsace and Lorraine  b) Paris  c) Its colonies in Africa  d) Corsica
**Answer: a) Alsace and Lorraine** — France ceded coal- and iron-rich Alsace and Lorraine and paid huge indemnities.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The German Empire was proclaimed in 1871 at —
a) Berlin  b) Frankfurt  c) The Palace of Versailles  d) Vienna
**Answer: c) The Palace of Versailles** — Proclaimed at Versailles, on French soil, with William of Prussia as Kaiser.
