# Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did the three wars clear Germany's road, step by step?
**Answer:** Each removed an obstacle: the wars settled the duchies question, shut Austria out of German affairs (Sadowa), and finally defeated France — leaving Prussia free to proclaim the Empire.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why was proclaiming the Empire at Versailles a deliberate act?
**Answer:** Versailles was the palace of France's kings: proclaiming the German Empire there, on French soil after France's defeat and cession of Alsace-Lorraine, announced Germany's arrival as a great power in the enemy's own hall.
