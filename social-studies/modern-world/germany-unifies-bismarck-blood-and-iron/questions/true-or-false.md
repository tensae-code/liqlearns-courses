# Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Austria led the German unification.
**Answer: False** — Prussia led it, against Austria as its rival.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** France gained territory by the Treaty of Frankfurt.
**Answer: False** — France ceded Alsace and Lorraine and paid huge indemnities.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** King William of Prussia became Kaiser of the new German Empire.
**Answer: True** — William was proclaimed Kaiser at Versailles in 1871.
