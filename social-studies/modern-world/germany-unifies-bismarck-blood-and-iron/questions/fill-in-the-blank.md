# Germany unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The customs union that prepared German unity was the ____.
**Answer: Zollverein**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The German Empire was proclaimed at the Palace of ____ in 1871.
**Answer: Versailles**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** France ceded Alsace and ____ to Germany in 1871.
**Answer: Lorraine**
