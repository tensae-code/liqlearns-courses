# Vietnam Southern Patriotic Tradition — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 24, the South's patriotic tradition and Trương Định
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — when the French seize Gia Định, commander Trương Định refuses a court posting in 1862, stays to fight, is proclaimed "Marshal who Pacifies the West" by the people, and kills himself rather than be captured; Nguyễn Trung Trực burns the French ship Espérance

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 24, Nguyễn Thị Định and the Đồng Khởi movement
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — she launches and leads the Đồng Khởi uprising in Bến Tre and becomes deputy commander of the southern liberation armed forces against the US

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 13, the South resists again and the North buys time
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — French troops fire on the Independence Day rally of 2 September 1945 and, with British help, attack the Southern Administrative Committee on the night of 22–23 September; Saigon–Chợ Lớn workers cut power and water and barricade the streets with tables, beds and cabinets, destroying factories rather than leave them to the enemy, while the whole country answers with "Support the heroic resistance of the Southern compatriots" and Nam tiến platoons march south; in February 1946 Hồ Chí Minh names the South the "Bronze Wall of the Fatherland"; after the Sino-French treaty of 28 February 1946 puts French troops in the North in Chiang's place, the DRV chooses "peace to advance" — the Preliminary Agreement of 6 March 1946 recognising the DRV as a free state within the Indochinese Federation and French Union, and the Tạm ước of 14 September 1946
