**Answer: when the French seize Gia Định, commander Trương Định refuses a court posting in 1862, stays to fight, is proclaimed "Marshal who Pacifies the West" by the people, and kills himself rather than be captured; Nguyễn Trung Trực burns the French ship Espérance (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 24, Nguyễn Thị Định and the Đồng Khởi movement.
**Answer: she launches and leads the Đồng Khởi uprising in Bến Tre and becomes deputy commander of the southern liberation armed forces against the US (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 13, the South resists again and the North buys time.
**Answer: French troops fire on the Independence Day rally of 2 September 1945 and, with British help, attack the Southern Administrative Committee on the night of 22–23 September; Saigon–Chợ Lớn workers cut power and water and barricade the streets with tables, beds and cabinets, destroying factories rather than leave them to the enemy, while the whole country answers with "Support the heroic resistance of the Southern compatriots" and Nam tiến platoons march south; in February 1946 Hồ Chí Minh names the South the "Bronze Wall of the Fatherland"; after the Sino-French treaty of 28 February 1946 puts French troops in the North in Chiang's place, the DRV chooses "peace to advance" — the Preliminary Agreement of 6 March 1946 recognising the DRV as a free state within the Indochinese Federation and French Union, and the Tạm ước of 14 September 1946 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
