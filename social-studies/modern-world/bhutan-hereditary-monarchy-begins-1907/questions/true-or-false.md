# Bhutan — hereditary monarchy begins, 1907 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after the theocratic centuries, 17 December 1907 crowns Gongsar Ugyen Wangchuck as the first hereditary Druk Gyalpo, and the date becomes Bhutan's National Day
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter 6.1 National Symbols, National Day).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** with the last Druk Desi stepped down in 1906 and the theocratic system plainly failing, the state council accepts Ugyen Dorji's proposal and on 17 December 1907 seals the genja oath enthroning Ugyen Wangchuck as first Druk Gyalpo; the 1910 Punakha treaty doubles his subsidy and he opens Bhutan's first modern schools
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Five, Institution of Hereditary Monarchy).
