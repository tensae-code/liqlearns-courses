# Baroque and Rococo across central Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Churches and palaces rise  b) The styles travel  c) Each place translates
**Answer: b) The styles travel** — Baroque and Rococo travel east and north from Rome and France.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Churches and palaces rise  b) The styles travel  c) Each place translates
**Answer: a) Churches and palaces rise** — Pilgrimage churches and palaces rise across the German lands, Austria and Bohemia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The styles travel  b) Churches and palaces rise  c) Each place translates
**Answer: c) Each place translates** — Each court and monastery translates Rome and Versailles into its own accent.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The styles travel east and north — pilgrimage churches and palaces rise across the German lands, Austria and Bohemia, each court and monastery translating Rome and Versailles into its own accent  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
