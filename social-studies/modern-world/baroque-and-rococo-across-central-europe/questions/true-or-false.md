# Baroque and Rococo across central Europe — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Pilgrimage churches and palaces rise across the German lands, Austria and Bohemia.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — The styles travel east and north — pilgrimage churches and palaces rise across the German lands, Austria and Bohemia, each court and monastery translating Rome and Versailles into its own accent.
