# Lesson plan — Baroque and Rococo across central Europe

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Baroque and Rococo across central Europe
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The styles travel east and north — pilgrimage churches and palaces rise across the German lands, Austria and Bohemia, each court and monastery translating Rome and Versailles into its own accent.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The styles travel** — Baroque and Rococo travel east and north from Rome and France.
2. **Churches and palaces rise** — Pilgrimage churches and palaces rise across the German lands, Austria and Bohemia.
3. **Each place translates** — Each court and monastery translates Rome and Versailles into its own accent.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
