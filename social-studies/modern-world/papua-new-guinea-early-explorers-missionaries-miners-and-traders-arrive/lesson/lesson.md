# Papua New Guinea — early explorers, missionaries, miners and traders arrive

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Four kinds of outsiders reach the island of New Guinea one after another — navigators charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners and traders after wealth — children name them, date them and trace their routes on the map. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
