# Video script — Papua New Guinea — early explorers, missionaries, miners and traders arrive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four kinds of outsiders reach the island of New Guinea one after another — navigators charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners and traders after wealth — children name them, date them and trace their routes on the map.

Five European groups build colonial power — administrators (the Company, then Governor Hahl), gold miners who open Sudest, Misima and Wau-Bulolo, settlers like Queen Emma of Rabaul (welcomed by some, resisted by the Binandere), and mission churches whose local catechists and pastors carry schools and health centres.

The Spice Islands and a rumoured golden southern continent pull Europeans in; mapping creeps up the rivers from the coast until the 1930 patrols walk into a Highlands world Europe did not know existed.

Empire gets a face — one foreigner's life, impact and legacy reconstructed from the evidence he left behind.
