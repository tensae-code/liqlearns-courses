# Papua New Guinea — early explorers, missionaries, miners and traders arrive — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Early Explorers / Missionaries / Miners and Traders
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — four kinds of outsiders reach the island of New Guinea one after another — navigators charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners and traders after wealth — children name them, date them and trace their routes on the map

## Sub lesson 2 — Roles of Europeans in Papua and New Guinea
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — five European groups build colonial power — administrators (the Company, then Governor Hahl), gold miners who open Sudest, Misima and Wau-Bulolo, settlers like Queen Emma of Rabaul (welcomed by some, resisted by the Binandere), and mission churches whose local catechists and pastors carry schools and health centres
