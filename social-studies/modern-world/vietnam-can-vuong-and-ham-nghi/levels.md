## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 18, the Cần Vương movement breaks out, 1885
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after the Patenôtre treaty a war party still stands in the Huế court around Tôn Thất Thuyết, Minister of War, who builds forces, stockpiles food and weapons and puts the boy Hàm Nghi on the throne; when his counterattack on the French in the capital fails on 5 July 1885 he carries the king to Tân Sở in Quảng Trị and, in Hàm Nghi's name, issues the Cần Vương edict — the Appeal to Aid the King — calling literati and people across the country to rise; the movement burns to the century's end
