# Lesson plan — Micronesia — traditional leadership under change

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — traditional leadership under change
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Warfare ended, population collapsed, and churches and German land reforms moved authority — so that Henry Nanpei's power came from church office and reform-granted land, not only from his Nahnken father: traditional leadership survived foreign rule by changing where its power came from.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Warfare ends, population collapses** — say that warfare ended and population collapsed, shaking the old bases of chiefly authority.
2. **Churches and German land reforms move authority** — say that churches and German land reforms moved authority to new holders and new grounds.
3. **Henry Nanpei: power from church and land** — say that Henry Nanpei's power came from church office and reform-granted land, not only from his Nahnken father.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
