# Micronesia — traditional leadership under change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two shocks that shook the old leadership were —
a) Ended warfare and collapsed population  b) New roads and radios  c) The copra trade and stores
**Answer: a) Ended warfare and collapsed population** — the shocks the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** German reforms re-granted —
a) Church offices  b) Land  c) Fishing rights only
**Answer: b) Land** — the reform the book names.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Henry Nanpei's father held the rank of —
a) Nahnmwarki  b) Nahnken  c) Ariki
**Answer: b) Nahnken** — the rank as filed.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Henry Nanpei's own power came mainly from —
a) His father's rank alone  b) Church office and reform-granted land  c) German military rank
**Answer: b) Church office and reform-granted land** — the grounds the book gives.
