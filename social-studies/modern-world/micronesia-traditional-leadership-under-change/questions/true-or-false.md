# Micronesia — traditional leadership under change — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Church office became a new road to authority in the islands.
**Answer: True** — the book teaches churches moved authority, giving office to new holders.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Traditional leadership simply vanished under foreign rule.
**Answer: False** — it survived by changing its grounds — Henry Nanpei held power through church office and reform-granted land as well as chiefly birth.
