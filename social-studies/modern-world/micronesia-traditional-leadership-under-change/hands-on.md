# Hands-on — Micronesia — traditional leadership under change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Old ground, new ground
Draw a chiefly stool standing on three legs labelled 'birth', 'church office' and 'reform land'. Rub out one leg at a time and say whether the stool — the leader's power — still stands, and what the book's answer is for Nanpei.

## Authority in your place
List three people with authority where you live. Beside each, write where their authority comes from — birth, election, office, land, knowledge. Compare with Micronesia's three grounds.
