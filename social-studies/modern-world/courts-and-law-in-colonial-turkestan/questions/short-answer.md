# Courts and Law in Colonial Turkestan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. 3 §17 The organisation of court proceedings.
**Answer: Russian courts for settlers, qazi and biy courts tolerated for natives — two justices in one land, and everyone knows which is above (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Узбекистана (History of Uzbekistan — second half of the 19th to beginning of the 20th century), Grade 9* (Uzbekistan), Ch. 3 §17 The organisation of court proceedings, printed p. 62.**
