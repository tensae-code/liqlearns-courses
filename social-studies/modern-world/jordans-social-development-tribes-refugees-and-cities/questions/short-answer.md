**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lessons 1–2: social life 1921–1999.
**Answer: the Emirate's society is tribal to its roots — landowners, merchants, officials, clergy and craftsmen in the qasabas, Circassians and Chechens among them since the 1800s; a mobile medical unit (one doctor, two nurses) tours the Badia from 1937 while mukhtars report malaria, typhoid, smallpox and measles, until the first Health Ministry in 1950; women's public life begins in charity — the first women's association, the Women's Social Solidarity Association, in 1926; after 1950 the society counts four peoples — city, countryside, badia and the Palestinian refugee camps — Jordan grants the refugees citizenship, UNRWA serves the camps, the nuclear family spreads with the move to the cities, and the Royal Medical Services crown the age with King Hussein Medical City (1973) and the Youth Welfare Organisation (1966) (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
