# Liberalism and socialism

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In a factory role-play at the century's start, pupils playing factory hands must decide for themselves: found a union, and strike? — the ideas of liberalism and socialism tested in action. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
