# Liberalism and socialism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the role-play, pupils play —
a) Factory hands  b) Factory owners only  c) Kings
**Answer: a) Factory hands** — pupils play factory hands.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The role-play is set —
a) At the century's start  b) In Viking times  c) In 1944
**Answer: a) At the century's start** — factory hands at the century's start.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hands must decide whether to —
a) Found a union and strike  b) Buy the factory  c) Leave the country
**Answer: a) Found a union and strike** — found a union and strike.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The worksheet's question "Viltu fara í verkfall?" asks —
a) Do you want to go on strike?  b) Do you want a holiday?  c) Do you want a new job?
**Answer: a) Do you want to go on strike?** — "Viltu fara í verkfall?" — will you strike?
