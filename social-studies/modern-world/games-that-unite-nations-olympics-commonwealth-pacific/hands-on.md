# Hands-on — Games that unite nations — Olympics, Commonwealth and Pacific Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Games that unite nations — Olympics, Commonwealth and Pacific Games — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Olympic, Commonwealth and Pacific Games, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
