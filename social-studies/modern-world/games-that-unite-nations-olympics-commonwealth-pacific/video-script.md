# Video script — Games that unite nations — Olympics, Commonwealth and Pacific Games

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The modern Olympics revive Olympia's ancient games through Coubertin's IOC (1894); the Empire Games begin in 1930 and become the Commonwealth Games; the South Pacific Games begin in Suva, Fiji, in 1963.
