# Hands-on — France conquers Algeria, 1830

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — France conquers Algeria, 1830 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Algeria conquered, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
