# France conquers Algeria, 1830

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: France declares war in 1827, blockades the ports for three years and takes Algiers on 5 July 1830. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Algeria conquered

France declares war in 1827, blockades the ports for three years and takes Algiers on 5 July 1830

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
