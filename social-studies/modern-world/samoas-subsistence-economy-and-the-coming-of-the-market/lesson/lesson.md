# Sāmoa's subsistence economy and the coming of the market

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: pre-European Sāmoa produces through aiga and village, pays specialists in fine mats, and trades ceremonial treasures with Fiji and Tonga (red feathers, 'ie toga, whale teeth); beachcombers, missionaries and traders bring sugar, flour, iron and cloth, Sāmoans grow copra for cash, and the DHPG's plantations import indentured labour and turn Apia into a commercial centre. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
