# Video script — Sāmoa's subsistence economy and the coming of the market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

pre-European Sāmoa produces through aiga and village, pays specialists in fine mats, and trades ceremonial treasures with Fiji and Tonga (red feathers, 'ie toga, whale teeth); beachcombers, missionaries and traders bring sugar, flour, iron and cloth, Sāmoans grow copra for cash, and the DHPG's plantations import indentured labour and turn Apia into a commercial centre.
