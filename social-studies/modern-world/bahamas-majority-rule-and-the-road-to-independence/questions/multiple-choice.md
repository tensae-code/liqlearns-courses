**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Majority Rule – Independence Part 1: the Burma Road Riot, 1942  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Majority Rule – Independence Part 1: the Burma Road Riot, 1942** — pay varies by sex and race — four shillings for men, two for women — and Bay Street merchants force an American company to keep Bahamian wages unequal; workers march from Oakes Field to Bay Street, the Riot Act is read, and five days later they return with a wage increase won; in 1953 the PLP is founded, the first party to represent the Black majority
