# Suriname — cultures under colonial rule

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other cultures survived and were lived despite it As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Het beleven van cultuur

Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other cultures survived and were lived despite it (Het beleven van cultuur)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
