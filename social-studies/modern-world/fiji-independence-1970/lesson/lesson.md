# Fiji's independence, 1970

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead, and on 10 October 1970 Fiji ends 96 years of British rule As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fiji's Independence and the 1874-1970 timeline

Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead, and on 10 October 1970 Fiji ends 96 years of British rule.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moving Towards Independence: constitutional changes 1904-1970

Step by step the Legislative Council gains Indian members (1916), the membership system and the common-versus-communal-roll fight shape the 1963 Assembly and 1965 constitution, Alliance and Federation parties spar after 1966, and the April 1970 London conference agrees a fully sovereign independent Fiji.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Background to 1970 and leadership in the first ten years, 1970-1980

Fiji enters independence in 1970 as a multi-racial society carrying the colonial legacy; Mara's Alliance leads, the Governor-General's role is questioned in the 1977 crisis, and pupils match leaders to the independence settlement.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — The 1970 Constitution: road to independence

As in 1965, the London conference turns on representation in the House; the resulting 1970 constitution's main features and cross-voting seats carry Fiji into independence, and its first governments are surveyed.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
