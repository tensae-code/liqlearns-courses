# Video script — Fiji's independence, 1970

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead, and on 10 October 1970 Fiji ends 96 years of British rule. Step by step the Legislative Council gains Indian members (1916), the membership system and the common-versus-communal-roll fight shape the 1963 Assembly and 1965 constitution, Alliance and Federation parties spar after 1966, and the April 1970 London conference agrees a fully sovereign independent Fiji. Fiji enters independence in 1970 as a multi-racial society carrying the colonial legacy; Mara's Alliance leads, the Governor-General's role is questioned in the 1977 crisis, and pupils match leaders to the independence settlement. As in 1965, the London conference turns on representation in the House; the resulting 1970 constitution's main features and cross-voting seats carry Fiji into independence, and its first governments are surveyed.
