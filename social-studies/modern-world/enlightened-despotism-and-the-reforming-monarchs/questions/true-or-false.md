# Enlightened Despotism And The Reforming Monarchs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Frederick II, Maria Theresa, Joseph II and Catherine rule by reason from above; in Italy Beccaria's On Crimes and Punishments (1764) rewrites Europe's idea of justice.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 2: Il Settecento e l'Ottocento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** monarchs borrow the philosophes to strengthen the state; Beccaria's utility of punishments (Fare storia) shows Italy exporting reform ideas to all Europe.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§8–9 Enlightened absolutism; Reformism in Italy

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 2 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XVII al XIX secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§8–9 Enlightened absolutism; Reformism in Italy
