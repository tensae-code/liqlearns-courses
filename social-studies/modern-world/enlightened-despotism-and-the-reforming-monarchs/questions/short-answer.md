# Enlightened Despotism And The Reforming Monarchs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy.
**Answer: Frederick II, Maria Theresa, Joseph II and Catherine rule by reason from above; in Italy Beccaria's On Crimes and Punishments (1764) rewrites Europe's idea of justice (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 2: Il Settecento e l'Ottocento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy, printed pp. 49, 56.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§8–9 Enlightened absolutism; Reformism in Italy.
**Answer: monarchs borrow the philosophes to strengthen the state; Beccaria's utility of punishments (Fare storia) shows Italy exporting reform ideas to all Europe (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
