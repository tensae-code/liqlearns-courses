# Bahamas — pirates, wreckers and Woodes Rogers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Pirates and Buccaneers: from boucan fires to Blackbeard  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Pirates and Buccaneers: from boucan fires to Blackbeard** — shipwrecked English, French and Dutch hunters cure meat over a boucan fire on Hispaniola until Spaniards chase them to sea as pirates; Blackbeard, Anne Bonny, Mary Read, Calico Jack, Stede Bonnet, Henry Morgan and Charles Vane infest The Bahamas until Woodes Rogers pardons some and hangs the rest

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Pirates and Buccaneers: from boucan fires to Blackbeard  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Pirates and Buccaneers: from boucan fires to Blackbeard** — the book's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Wrecking: the false-light trade  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Wrecking: the false-light trade** — in the mid-1600s wrecking is big business — wreckers lure ships onto cays and reefs, sometimes with false lights, strip the goods and sell them, and many Bahamians make a living from the wrecks

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book this part is —
a) Wrecking: the false-light trade  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Wrecking: the false-light trade** — the book's own heading for this part.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Early Connections: pirates, Woodes Rogers and the first parliament  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Early Connections: pirates, Woodes Rogers and the first parliament** — pirates seize the islands in the 1600s and ruin their reputation until Woodes Rogers, first Royal Governor, clears them out; under him the islands organise their first parliament in 1729, with John Colebrook as first Speaker and 24 members
