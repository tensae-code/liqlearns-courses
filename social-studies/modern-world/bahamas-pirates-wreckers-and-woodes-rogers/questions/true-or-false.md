# Bahamas — pirates, wreckers and Woodes Rogers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** shipwrecked English, French and Dutch hunters cure meat over a boucan fire on Hispaniola until Spaniards chase them to sea as pirates; Blackbeard, Anne Bonny, Mary Read, Calico Jack, Stede Bonnet, Henry Morgan and Charles Vane infest The Bahamas until Woodes Rogers pardons some and hangs the rest
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Pirates and Buccaneers: from boucan fires to Blackbeard).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** in the mid-1600s wrecking is big business — wreckers lure ships onto cays and reefs, sometimes with false lights, strip the goods and sell them, and many Bahamians make a living from the wrecks
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Wrecking: the false-light trade).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** pirates seize the islands in the 1600s and ruin their reputation until Woodes Rogers, first Royal Governor, clears them out; under him the islands organise their first parliament in 1729, with John Colebrook as first Speaker and 24 members
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Early Connections: pirates, Woodes Rogers and the first parliament).
