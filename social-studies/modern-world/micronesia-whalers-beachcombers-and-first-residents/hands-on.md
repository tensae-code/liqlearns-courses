# Hands-on — Micronesia — whalers, beachcombers and first residents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guest or ruler?
Sort six cards — trader, whaler, beachcomber, missionary, soldier, governor — into 'guest under island law' and 'bringer of foreign law' for the years of this lesson. Defend each placing in one sentence.

## The island ledger
Write the island's side of one trading day: what the island gave, what the ship gave, and one risk the island took that no money could repay — sickness. Read it aloud as the chief's record-keeper.
