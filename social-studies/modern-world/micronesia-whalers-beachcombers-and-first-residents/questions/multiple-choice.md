# Micronesia — whalers, beachcombers and first residents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beche-de-mer traders were hunting —
a) Pearls  b) Sea cucumber for the China market  c) Turtles
**Answer: b) Sea cucumber for the China market** — the trade the book describes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A beachcomber was —
a) A chief's guard  b) A deserting sailor who stayed and lived under the chiefs  c) A mission teacher
**Answer: b) A deserting sailor who stayed and lived under the chiefs** — the book's beachcomber.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The crews of which two ships were massacred?
a) The Waverly and the Harriet  b) The Endeavour and the Resolution  c) The Manila galleons
**Answer: a) The Waverly and the Harriet** — the ships the book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The disease the book names as cutting island populations is —
a) The pox  b) Malaria  c) Influenza
**Answer: a) The pox** — the disease as filed.
