# Micronesia — whalers, beachcombers and first residents — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign governments ruled the eastern Carolines before the whalers and traders arrived.
**Answer: False** — the traders and whalers came first; foreign rule came later.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Beachcombers lived under the authority of the island chiefs.
**Answer: True** — the book says beachcombers lived under chiefs.
