# Micronesia — whalers, beachcombers and first residents — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did whalers and beche-de-mer traders crowd the eastern Carolines?
**Answer: Whaling and the China-market trade in beche-de-mer (sea cucumber) drew ships to the islands for goods, supplies and trade (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two ways the first resident foreigners changed island life before colonial rule.
**Answer: Any two of: crews of the Waverly and Harriet massacred in violent meetings; beachcombers living under chiefs, translating and trading; disease — the pox — cutting island populations (any close wording).**
