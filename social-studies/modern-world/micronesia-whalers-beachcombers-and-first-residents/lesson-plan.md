# Lesson plan — Micronesia — whalers, beachcombers and first residents

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — whalers, beachcombers and first residents
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Whalers and beche-de-mer traders crowded the eastern Carolines, deserting sailors became beachcombers living under chiefs, and the crews of the Waverly and Harriet were massacred while disease — the pox — cut island populations: the first foreigners who stayed changed island life even before any government ruled.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Whalers and beche-de-mer traders crowd the eastern Carolines** — say that whalers and beche-de-mer traders crowded the eastern Carolines.
2. **Beachcombers: deserters living under chiefs** — say that deserting sailors became beachcombers living under chiefs.
3. **The Waverly and Harriet massacres — and the pox** — say that the crews of the Waverly and Harriet were massacred, and that disease — the pox — cut island populations.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
