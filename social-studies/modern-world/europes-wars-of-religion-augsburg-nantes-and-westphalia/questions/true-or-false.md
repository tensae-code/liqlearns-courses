# Europes Wars Of Religion Augsburg Nantes And Westphalia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Charles V cannot crush the Protestant princes, so Augsburg lets each ruler choose his land's faith — "cuius regio, eius religio"; France's wars end with Henry IV's Edict of Nantes; and the Thirty Years War (1618–1648) burns Germany until Westphalia lets Protestants worship freely.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 3: religious conflicts in Europe
