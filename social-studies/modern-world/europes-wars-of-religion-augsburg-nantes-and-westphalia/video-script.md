# Video script — Europes Wars Of Religion Augsburg Nantes And Westphalia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as North Macedonia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Charles V cannot crush the Protestant princes, so Augsburg lets each ruler choose his land's faith — "cuius regio, eius religio"; France's wars end with Henry IV's Edict of Nantes; and the Thirty Years War (1618–1648) burns Germany until Westphalia lets Protestants worship freely.
