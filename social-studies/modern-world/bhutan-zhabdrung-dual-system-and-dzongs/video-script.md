# Video script — Bhutan — the Zhabdrung's dual system and dzongs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bhutan book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the unifier codifies a distinct Bhutanese Drukpa Kagyu order, national dress and driglam namzha, laws, taxes and the great dzongs, then in 1651 sets up the Chhoe-sid dual system — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for temporal rule — that outlives his death that same year; in his new home the exile builds the nation state Palden Druk, raising fortified dzongs region by region as joint seats of monk body and administration under the dual system; proclaimed supreme ruler around 1627, the Zhabdrung pairs the spiritual law 'like a silken knot' that tightens with bad deeds with the secular law 'like a golden yoke' that grows heavier with wrongs, and in 1651 publishes the Kathrim code — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for politics, three Ponlops and a Lhengye Tshog council below
