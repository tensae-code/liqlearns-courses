# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung's dual system and dzongs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the unifier codifies a distinct Bhutanese Drukpa Kagyu order, national dress and driglam namzha, laws, taxes and the great dzongs, then in 1651 sets up the Chhoe-sid dual system — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for temporal rule — that outlives his death that same year
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** in his new home the exile builds the nation state Palden Druk, raising fortified dzongs region by region as joint seats of monk body and administration under the dual system
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Two, Home away from Home / Chapter Three, dzongs under the Zhabdrung).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** proclaimed supreme ruler around 1627, the Zhabdrung pairs the spiritual law 'like a silken knot' that tightens with bad deeds with the secular law 'like a golden yoke' that grows heavier with wrongs, and in 1651 publishes the Kathrim code — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for politics, three Ponlops and a Lhengye Tshog council below
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter One, The Dual System of Governance: Chhoe-sid Nyiden).
