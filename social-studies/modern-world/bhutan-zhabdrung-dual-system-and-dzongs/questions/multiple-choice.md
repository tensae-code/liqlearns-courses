# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung's dual system and dzongs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions** — the unifier codifies a distinct Bhutanese Drukpa Kagyu order, national dress and driglam namzha, laws, taxes and the great dzongs, then in 1651 sets up the Chhoe-sid dual system — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for temporal rule — that outlives his death that same year

a) Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions** — the book's own heading for this part.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter One, The Dual System of Governance: Chhoe-sid Nyiden  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter One, The Dual System of Governance: Chhoe-sid Nyiden** — proclaimed supreme ruler around 1627, the Zhabdrung pairs the spiritual law 'like a silken knot' that tightens with bad deeds with the secular law 'like a golden yoke' that grows heavier with wrongs, and in 1651 publishes the Kathrim code — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for politics, three Ponlops and a Lhengye Tshog council below
