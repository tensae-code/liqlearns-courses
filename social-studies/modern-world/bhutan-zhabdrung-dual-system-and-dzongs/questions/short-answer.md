# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung's dual system and dzongs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions.
**Answer: the unifier codifies a distinct Bhutanese Drukpa Kagyu order, national dress and driglam namzha, laws, taxes and the great dzongs, then in 1651 sets up the Chhoe-sid dual system — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for temporal rule — that outlives his death that same year (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter Two, Home away from Home / Chapter Three, dzongs under the Zhabdrung.
**Answer: in his new home the exile builds the nation state Palden Druk, raising fortified dzongs region by region as joint seats of monk body and administration under the dual system (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter One, The Dual System of Governance: Chhoe-sid Nyiden.
**Answer: proclaimed supreme ruler around 1627, the Zhabdrung pairs the spiritual law 'like a silken knot' that tightens with bad deeds with the secular law 'like a golden yoke' that grows heavier with wrongs, and in 1651 publishes the Kathrim code — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for politics, three Ponlops and a Lhengye Tshog council below (any close wording).**
