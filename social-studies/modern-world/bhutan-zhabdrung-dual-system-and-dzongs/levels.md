# Bhutan — the Zhabdrung's dual system and dzongs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the unifier codifies a distinct Bhutanese Drukpa Kagyu order, national dress and driglam namzha, laws, taxes and the great dzongs, then in 1651 sets up the Chhoe-sid dual system — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for temporal rule — that outlives his death that same year

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter Two, Home away from Home / Chapter Three, dzongs under the Zhabdrung
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: in his new home the exile builds the nation state Palden Druk, raising fortified dzongs region by region as joint seats of monk body and administration under the dual system

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter One, The Dual System of Governance: Chhoe-sid Nyiden
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: proclaimed supreme ruler around 1627, the Zhabdrung pairs the spiritual law 'like a silken knot' that tightens with bad deeds with the secular law 'like a golden yoke' that grows heavier with wrongs, and in 1651 publishes the Kathrim code — Je Khenpo for religion, Desi for politics, three Ponlops and a Lhengye Tshog council below
