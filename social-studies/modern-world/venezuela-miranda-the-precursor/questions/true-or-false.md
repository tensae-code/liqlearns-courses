# Venezuela Miranda The Precursor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Miranda crosses the world seeking support, organises his expedition in Haiti and sails for America to free it before anyone else dares.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Grandfather Sebastián talks of Miranda

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Grandfather Sebastián talks of Miranda

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Miranda's 1806 expeditions fail on the coast but announce the tempest of independence.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Miranda's expeditions that announced the storm

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Miranda's expeditions that announced the storm
