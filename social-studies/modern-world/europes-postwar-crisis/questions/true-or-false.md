# Europes Postwar Crisis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keynes' Economic Consequences of the Peace (the unit's book study) warns that a vindictive settlement will poison Europe; inflation, borders and broken societies prove him right in advance.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 2, Cap. 6 §1 The legacies and aftershocks of the war

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 2, Cap. 6 §1 The legacies and aftershocks of the war

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** inflation (the module's keyword), broken finances and new nation-states whose minorities Mazower's Fare storia piece counts — the peace creates as many problems as it settles.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§1–3 Le conseguenze economiche della guerra; I mutamenti nella vita sociale; Stati nazionali e minoranze

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
