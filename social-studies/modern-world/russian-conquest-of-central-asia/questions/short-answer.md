# Russian Conquest of Central Asia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 37. The dream of conquering Central Asia and 38. Defence of the Homeland.
**Answer: Russia advances fortress by fortress from the steppe; Tashkent, Samarkand and Khiva fall despite desperate local defence, and the khanates become a colony's borderland (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* (Uzbekistan), 37. The dream of conquering Central Asia and 38. Defence of the Homeland, printed pp. 116, 118.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. 2 §§6–7 The beginning of the Russian Empire's conquests in Central Asia.
**Answer: fortress lines creep down the Syr Darya; Ak-Mechet, Chimkent and Aulie-Ata fall as the empire closes on Tashkent (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Узбекистана (History of Uzbekistan — second half of the 19th to beginning of the 20th century), Grade 9* (Uzbekistan), Ch. 2 §§6–7 The beginning of the Russian Empire's conquests in Central Asia, printed p. 26.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. 2 §8 The continuation of the campaigns of conquest in Central Asia.
**Answer: Chernyaev takes Tashkent (1865), Kaufman takes Samarkand (1868) — in five years the map of the homeland is redrawn by two generals (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Узбекистана (History of Uzbekistan — second half of the 19th to beginning of the 20th century), Grade 9* (Uzbekistan), Ch. 2 §8 The continuation of the campaigns of conquest in Central Asia, printed p. 34.**
