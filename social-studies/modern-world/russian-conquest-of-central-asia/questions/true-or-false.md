# Russian Conquest of Central Asia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Russia advances fortress by fortress from the steppe; Tashkent, Samarkand and Khiva fall despite desperate local defence, and the khanates become a colony's borderland.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 37. The dream of conquering Central Asia and 38. Defence of the Homeland

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 37. The dream of conquering Central Asia and 38. Defence of the Homeland

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** fortress lines creep down the Syr Darya; Ak-Mechet, Chimkent and Aulie-Ata fall as the empire closes on Tashkent.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2 §§6–7 The beginning of the Russian Empire's conquests in Central Asia

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Узбекистана (History of Uzbekistan — second half of the 19th to beginning of the 20th century), Grade 9* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2 §§6–7 The beginning of the Russian Empire's conquests in Central Asia

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Chernyaev takes Tashkent (1865), Kaufman takes Samarkand (1868) — in five years the map of the homeland is redrawn by two generals.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2 §8 The continuation of the campaigns of conquest in Central Asia

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *История Узбекистана (History of Uzbekistan — second half of the 19th to beginning of the 20th century), Grade 9* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2 §8 The continuation of the campaigns of conquest in Central Asia
