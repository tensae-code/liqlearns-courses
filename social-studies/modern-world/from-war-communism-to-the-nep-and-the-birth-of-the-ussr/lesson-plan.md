**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§5–6 Economic policy from war communism to the NEP; The birth of the Soviet Union and the death of Lenin** — famine and revolt force the NEP retreat (Graziosi's Storiografia calls it the only alternative to a new civil war); the USSR is founded as a federation — Stalin's Document argues its limits — and Lenin's death opens the succession
2. **Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§7–8 La Russia comunista; L'Urss da Lenin a Stalin** — the Comintern's 21 Points (Fare storia) export the model while the USSR consolidates at home; Lenin (Personaggi) dies and Stalin inherits — the succession that decides the century's other totalitarianism
