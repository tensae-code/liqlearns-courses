# From War Communism To The Nep And The Birth Of The Ussr — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** famine and revolt force the NEP retreat (Graziosi's Storiografia calls it the only alternative to a new civil war); the USSR is founded as a federation — Stalin's Document argues its limits — and Lenin's death opens the succession.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§5–6 Economic policy from war communism to the NEP; The birth of the Soviet Union and the death of Lenin

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§5–6 Economic policy from war communism to the NEP; The birth of the Soviet Union and the death of Lenin

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Comintern's 21 Points (Fare storia) export the model while the USSR consolidates at home; Lenin (Personaggi) dies and Stalin inherits — the succession that decides the century's other totalitarianism.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§7–8 La Russia comunista; L'Urss da Lenin a Stalin

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§7–8 La Russia comunista; L'Urss da Lenin a Stalin
