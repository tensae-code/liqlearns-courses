# From War Communism To The Nep And The Birth Of The Ussr — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§5–6 Economic policy from war communism to the NEP; The birth of the Soviet Union and the death of Lenin.
**Answer: famine and revolt force the NEP retreat (Graziosi's Storiografia calls it the only alternative to a new civil war); the USSR is founded as a federation — Stalin's Document argues its limits — and Lenin's death opens the succession (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§5–6 Economic policy from war communism to the NEP; The birth of the Soviet Union and the death of Lenin, printed pp. 187, 190.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§7–8 La Russia comunista; L'Urss da Lenin a Stalin.
**Answer: the Comintern's 21 Points (Fare storia) export the model while the USSR consolidates at home; Lenin (Personaggi) dies and Stalin inherits — the succession that decides the century's other totalitarianism (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§7–8 La Russia comunista; L'Urss da Lenin a Stalin, printed pp. 57, 59.**
