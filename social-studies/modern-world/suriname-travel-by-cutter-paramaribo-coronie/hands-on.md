# Hands-on — Suriname — travel by cutter, Paramaribo–Coronie

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Suriname — travel by cutter, Paramaribo–Coronie — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Met de kotter naar Paramaribo, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
