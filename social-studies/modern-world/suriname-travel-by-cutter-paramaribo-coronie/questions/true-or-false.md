# Suriname — travel by cutter, Paramaribo–Coronie — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With no road to Coronie, the three-sailed cutter Alfons carries coconuts, pigs and passengers; ten-year-old Simon's prize trip to Paramaribo takes two days under sail.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Met de kotter naar Paramaribo

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 5 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Met de kotter naar Paramaribo
