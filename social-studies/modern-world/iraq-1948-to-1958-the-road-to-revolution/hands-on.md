# Hands-on — Iraq, 1948–1958 — the road to revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iraq, 1948–1958 — the road to revolution — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Iraq 1948–1958, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
