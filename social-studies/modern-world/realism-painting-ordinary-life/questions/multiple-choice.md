# Realism — painting ordinary life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Ordinary life painted  b) Backs turned on heroes and gods  c) A scandal and a claim
**Answer: b) Backs turned on heroes and gods** — The realists turn their backs on heroes and gods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Ordinary life painted  b) Backs turned on heroes and gods  c) A scandal and a claim
**Answer: a) Ordinary life painted** — They paint stone-breakers, funerals in provincial towns and peasants at work.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Backs turned on heroes and gods  b) Ordinary life painted  c) A scandal and a claim
**Answer: c) A scandal and a claim** — It is a scandal at the salons — and a claim that ordinary life is worthy of the biggest canvases.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The realists turn their backs on heroes and gods and paint stone-breakers, funerals in provincial towns and peasants at work — a scandal at the salons, and a claim that ordinary life is worthy of the biggest canvases  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
