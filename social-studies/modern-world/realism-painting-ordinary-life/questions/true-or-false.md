# Realism — painting ordinary life — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** They paint stone-breakers, funerals in provincial towns and peasants at work.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — The realists turn their backs on heroes and gods and paint stone-breakers, funerals in provincial towns and peasants at work — a scandal at the salons, and a claim that ordinary life is worthy of the biggest canvases.
