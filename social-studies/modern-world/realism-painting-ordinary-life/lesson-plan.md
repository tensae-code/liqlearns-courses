# Lesson plan — Realism — painting ordinary life

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Realism — painting ordinary life
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The realists turn their backs on heroes and gods and paint stone-breakers, funerals in provincial towns and peasants at work — a scandal at the salons, and a claim that ordinary life is worthy of the biggest canvases.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.
