# Video script — Realism — painting ordinary life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The realists turn their backs on heroes and gods.

They paint stone-breakers, funerals in provincial towns and peasants at work.

It is a scandal at the salons — and a claim that ordinary life is worthy of the biggest canvases.
