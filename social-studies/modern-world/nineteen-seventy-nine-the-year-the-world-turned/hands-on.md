# Hands-on — Nineteen Seventy Nine The Year The World Turned

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Nineteen Seventy Nine The Year The World Turned — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unità 4, Cap. 18 §1 1979–1989, a decade of turning, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
