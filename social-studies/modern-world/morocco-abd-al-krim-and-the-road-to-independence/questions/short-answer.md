# Morocco — Abd al-Krim and the road to independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Morocco — Abd al-Krim and the road to independence.
**Answer: The European powers settled their rivalry at Algeciras in 1906; France occupied Fez in 1911 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912; Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's Rif revolt destroyed the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaimed the Republic of the Rif before the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiled him in 1926; the Berber Dahir of 1930 woke the national movement, the Istiqlal Party demanded independence in 1943, France exiled Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953, and the armed struggle forced his return in 1955 and independence in 1956.
