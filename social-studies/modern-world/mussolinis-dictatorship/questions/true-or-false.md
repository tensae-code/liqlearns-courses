# Mussolinis Dictatorship — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after Matteotti, the exceptional laws dissolve opposition and free press; Gramsci's Document reads fascism's associative crimes as the regime's true constitution, and the Storia culturale pauses on the very word "totalitarianism".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 3, Cap. 8 §1 The affirmation of the dictatorship and the repression of dissent

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 3, Cap. 8 §1 The affirmation of the dictatorship and the repression of dissent

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** De Felice and Gentile (Fare storia) argue over fascism's nature — myth and organisation — while the narrative weighs how totalitarian Italy really was against Germany's and Russia's models.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 6 §§1–2 Lo Stato fascista; Il totalitarismo italiano e i suoi limiti

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 6 §§1–2 Lo Stato fascista; Il totalitarismo italiano e i suoi limiti
