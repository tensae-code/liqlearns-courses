# The emergence of Bangladesh, 1971 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1947 partition made East Pakistan, in the Grade 6 book's words —
a) A free province  b) A colony of the West  c) Part of India
**Answer: b) A colony of the West** — the book's verdict on 1947.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Europeans came to Bengal in the order —
a) English, French, Dutch, Portuguese  b) Portuguese, Dutch, French, English  c) Dutch, Portuguese, English, French
**Answer: b) Portuguese, Dutch, French, English** — the order the Grade 7 book gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bangabandhu landed in independent Bangladesh on —
a) 16 December 1971  b) 10 January 1972  c) 26 March 1971
**Answer: b) 10 January 1972** — the homecoming date.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** On 23 May 1973 Bangabandhu received —
a) The Joliot-Curie Medal for Peace  b) A military title  c) A trade prize
**Answer: a) The Joliot-Curie Medal for Peace** — the peace medal.
