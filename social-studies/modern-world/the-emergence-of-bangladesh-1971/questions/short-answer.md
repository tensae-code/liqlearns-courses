# The emergence of Bangladesh, 1971 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the stages of Bengal's long arc from the janapadas to 1971.
**Answer: Janapadas; Pala, Sena, Muslim and Mughal rulers; British rule; East Pakistan after 1947; Bangabandhu's movement and independence in 1971 (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How did students carry the Liberation War's spirit into the rebuilding?
**Answer: By farming, building and teaching literacy across the country (any close wording).**
