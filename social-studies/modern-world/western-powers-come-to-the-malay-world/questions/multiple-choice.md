# Western powers come to the Malay world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Why the West came  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Why the West came** — the book first shows a prosperous country — strategic ports trading its minerals, fertile soil and sea produce to the world, with a flourishing intellectual life — then the pressure builds: Britain's Industrial Revolution (and by the 1870s Germany's and France's) demands markets and raw materials, the Suez Canal and the steamship shorten the road east, and the Western powers race one another with different strategies of trade, treaty and force

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 1 Kedatangan Kuasa Barat: why the West came  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 1 Kedatangan Kuasa Barat: why the West came** — the book's own heading for this part.
