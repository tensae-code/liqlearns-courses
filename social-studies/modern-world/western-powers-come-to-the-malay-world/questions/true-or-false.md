# Western powers come to the Malay world — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book first shows a prosperous country — strategic ports trading its minerals, fertile soil and sea produce to the world, with a flourishing intellectual life — then the pressure builds: Britain's Industrial Revolution (and by the 1870s Germany's and France's) demands markets and raw materials, the Suez Canal and the steamship shorten the road east, and the Western powers race one another with different strategies of trade, treaty and force
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 1 Kedatangan Kuasa Barat: why the West came).
