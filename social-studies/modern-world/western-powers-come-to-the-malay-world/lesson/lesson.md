# Western powers come to the Malay world

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The West comes to a prosperous Malay world because industrial Europe demands markets and raw materials, the Suez Canal and the steamship shorten the road east, and the Western powers race one another with trade, treaty and force. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why the West came

The book first shows a prosperous country — strategic ports trading its minerals, fertile soil and sea produce to the world, with a flourishing intellectual life — then the pressure builds: Britain's Industrial Revolution (and by the 1870s Germany's and France's) demands markets and raw materials, the Suez Canal and the steamship shorten the road east, and the Western powers race one another with different strategies of trade, treaty and force (Bab 1 Kedatangan Kuasa Barat: why the West came)
