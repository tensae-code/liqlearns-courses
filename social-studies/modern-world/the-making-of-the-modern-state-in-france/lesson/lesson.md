# The making of the modern state in France

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: François Ier's Ordinance of Villers-Cotterêts imposes French in law and the modern state takes shape in France — and Colbert later builds the king's finances, factories and fleet into one state machine. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Villers-Cotterêts — French in law

François Ier's ordinance imposes French in law and the modern state takes shape — one language for the law is one state speaking with one voice.

In your mind's eye: a royal ordinance being proclaimed in French on one side and Colbert inspecting a factory and a fleet on the other. Say what each man did to build one state.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colbert's state machine

Colbert builds the king's finances, factories and fleet into one state machine — money, making and sea power pulled together under the crown.
