# The making of the modern state in France — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ordinance of Villers-Cotterêts imposed —
a) French in law  b) Latin in law  c) No language in law
**Answer: a) French in law** — it imposes French in law.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ordinance was —
a) François Ier's  b) Colbert's alone  c) An English law
**Answer: a) François Ier's** — François Ier's ordinance.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Colbert built into one state machine the king's —
a) Finances, factories and fleet  b) Only gardens  c) Only churches
**Answer: a) Finances, factories and fleet** — finances, factories and fleet.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson is about the making of —
