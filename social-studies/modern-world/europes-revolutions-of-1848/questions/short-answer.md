# Europe's revolutions of 1848 — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The revolutions of 1848.
**Answer: France makes Napoleon III; Austria-Hungary's peoples rise until the Russian tsar's army crushes them in 1849; Prussia's king dodges the constitution his people demand (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
