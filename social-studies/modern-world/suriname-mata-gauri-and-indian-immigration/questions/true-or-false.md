# Suriname — Mata Gauri and Indian immigration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gauri, born in India in 1863, is recruited, sails three months to Suriname, works six years on a Commewijne sugar plantation, then saves, buys land at Kwattaweg, funds a school in her Indian village and at 100 gives land for the Mata Gauri cultural building.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Mata Gauri

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 5 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Mata Gauri
