# Video script — Suriname — Mata Gauri and Indian immigration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gauri, born in India in 1863, is recruited, sails three months to Suriname, works six years on a Commewijne sugar plantation, then saves, buys land at Kwattaweg, funds a school in her Indian village and at 100 gives land for the Mata Gauri cultural building.
