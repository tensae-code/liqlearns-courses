# Calvin Anabaptists And The Church Of England — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Calvin builds a disciplined Geneva, Anabaptists pay for Münster with their lives, and Henry VIII's Act of Supremacy makes a national church for a king's divorce.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 15 §4 The birth of new Protestant churches

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 15 §4 The birth of new Protestant churches

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Calvin's Geneva disciplines a city into a model church, Anabaptists radicalise the message, and Henry VIII nationalises it — three different Reformation futures.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ginevra e Calvino; L'Europa riformata; Personaggi: Enrico VIII

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ginevra e Calvino; L'Europa riformata; Personaggi: Enrico VIII
