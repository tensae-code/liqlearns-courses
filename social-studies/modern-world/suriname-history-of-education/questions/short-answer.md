**Answer: Before schools, Indigenous children learned hut-building, tools, hunting and healing from adults and the piai; enslaved children were barred from school and learned of slavery's injustice from their parents. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Het onderwijs verandert.
