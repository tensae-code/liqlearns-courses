# Suriname — history of education

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Before schools, Indigenous children learned hut-building, tools, hunting and healing from adults and the piai; enslaved children were barred from school and learned of slavery's injustice from their parents As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hoe was het vroeger?

Before schools, Indigenous children learned hut-building, tools, hunting and healing from adults and the piai; enslaved children were barred from school and learned of slavery's injustice from their parents (Hoe was het vroeger?)

## Sub lesson 2 — Het onderwijs verandert

Fearing revenge at abolition, the government lets Christian churches from 1844 prepare the enslaved for freedom through religion and reading lessons; schooling slowly opens and changes (Het onderwijs verandert)

## Sub lesson 3 — Hoe belangrijk is goed onderwijs?

A country needs nurses, teachers, police and builders who cannot be trained at home — the lesson argues why good schooling matters for an independent society (Hoe belangrijk is goed onderwijs?)
