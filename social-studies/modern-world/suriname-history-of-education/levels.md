Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 2 — Het onderwijs verandert
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fearing revenge at abolition, the government lets Christian churches from 1844 prepare the enslaved for freedom through religion and reading lessons; schooling slowly opens and changes.
