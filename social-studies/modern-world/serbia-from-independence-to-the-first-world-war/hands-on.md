# Hands-on — Serbia From Independence To The First World War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Serbia From Independence To The First World War — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Theme 4: Serbia from independence to the First World War — Milan's reforms provoke a crushed revolt (1883), a boy king seizes power (1893), and Serbia fights both Balkan Wars, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
