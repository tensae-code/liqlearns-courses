## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: conquistadors overthrow the Maya, Aztec and Inca worlds for gold and silver; European diseases finish what swords began; labour hunger drags enslaved Africans across the Atlantic, As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
