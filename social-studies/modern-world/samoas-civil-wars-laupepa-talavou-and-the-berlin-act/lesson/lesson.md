# Sāmoa's civil wars — Laupepa, Talavou and the Berlin Act

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Laupepa drives Talavou from Mulinu'u in 1860, yet Talavou wins and yields him the kingship in 1873; the deportation of the American premier Steinberger splits king and Taimua-Faipule in 1877; the Lackawanna treaty (1881) makes rivals alternate as king and vice-king; German consul Weber backs Tamasese, Laupepa is exiled to the Marshalls, and the 1889 Apia hurricane wrecks the rival warships before the Berlin Act restores Laupepa under a three-power joint government. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
