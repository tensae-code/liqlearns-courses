# Video script — Sāmoa's civil wars — Laupepa, Talavou and the Berlin Act

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Laupepa drives Talavou from Mulinu'u in 1860, yet Talavou wins and yields him the kingship in 1873; the deportation of the American premier Steinberger splits king and Taimua-Faipule in 1877; the Lackawanna treaty (1881) makes rivals alternate as king and vice-king; German consul Weber backs Tamasese, Laupepa is exiled to the Marshalls, and the 1889 Apia hurricane wrecks the rival warships before the Berlin Act restores Laupepa under a three-power joint government.
