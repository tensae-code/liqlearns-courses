## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Goudi movement (1909)** — officers camp at Goudi demanding reform, refuse power for themselves, and summon a Cretan politician, Venizelos
2. **Venizelos as prime minister: the politics of 1910–1912** — a revisionary assembly, a new constitution and an army and navy rebuilt just in time for war
3. **Theme 2 C: From the bankruptcy to the military movement at Goudi (1893–1909)** — defeat in 1897 and financial control discredit the old parties until the officers' 1909 movement forces the door open
4. **Theme 2 D: The Liberal Party of Venizelos** — Venizelos builds Greece's first modern programme party and carries the reforms of 1910–1912

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
