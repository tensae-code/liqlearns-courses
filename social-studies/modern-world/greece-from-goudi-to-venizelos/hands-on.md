# Hands-on — Greece From Goudi To Venizelos

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Greece From Goudi To Venizelos — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Goudi movement (1909), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
