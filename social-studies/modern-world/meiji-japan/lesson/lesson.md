# Meiji Japan

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Meiji state abolishes the feudal domains, frees the land market and builds 1,300 factories, then sells them cheap to the houses that. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. V §§26–27 Japan at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century

the Meiji state abolishes the feudal domains, frees the land market and builds 1,300 factories, then sells them cheap to the houses that become Mitsui and Mitsubishi — the first Eastern country to take Europe's industrial technique ready-made, its monopoly capitalism still woven with feudal leftovers and a landlord-bourgeois monarchy

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
