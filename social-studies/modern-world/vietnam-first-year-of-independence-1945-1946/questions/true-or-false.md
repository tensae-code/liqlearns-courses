# Vietnam First Year Of Independence 1945 1946 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chiang's troops hold the North with Việt Quốc and Việt Cách saboteurs while the British open the road for France in the South; on 6 January 1946 the first general election seats over 300 deputies — Hồ Chí Minh tops the poll after his appeal of 5 January — and the March 1946 National Assembly forms a coalition resistance government, conceding 70 unelected seats to Việt Quốc and Việt Cách and food and currency circulation to Chiang's army to buy peace; a Constitution follows on 9 November 1946, People's Councils govern locally, and the Liberation Army becomes the National Guard (September 1945) then the National Army (May 1946) — Hồ Chí Minh's quoted rule for it all: every government organ "is a servant of the people".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 13, building the new state amid two occupying armies

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 13, building the new state amid two occupying armies

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** against the famine the government launches rice jars, "share rice, share clothes" and a "golden heart day", punishes hoarders, orders "not one inch of land left fallow", gives confiscated land to poor peasants and cuts rents and taxes; the Independence Fund and Gold Week (17–24 September 1945) gather some 60 million đồng and 370 kg of gold — the book names donors such as Trịnh Văn Bô — and Vietnamese currency is issued in September 1946; against illiteracy above 90%, the decree of 8 September 1945 founds the Bình dân học vụ popular-education service, which teaches 2.5 million people in the North and Centre to read and write within a year.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 13, fighting famine and illiteracy
