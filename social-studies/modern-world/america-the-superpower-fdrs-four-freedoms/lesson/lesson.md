# America the superpower — Roosevelt's Four Freedoms

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the book asks why the USA is the surviving superpower — natural resources, a skilled workforce, stable capitalist democracy, military might and the "American Dream" — and prints Roosevelt's Four Freedoms address (6 January 1941) and Arsenal of Democracy argument: send the democracies "ships, planes, tanks, and guns" now, or fight the dictators later. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
