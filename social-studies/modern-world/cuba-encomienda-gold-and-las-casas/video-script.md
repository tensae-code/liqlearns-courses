# Video script — Cuba Encomienda Gold And Las Casas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cuba's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indians wash river gold from dawn to dusk under the whip in encomiendas; Las Casas gives up his own encomienda and becomes Protector of the Indians, yet by 1553 only some 3,000 of 300,000 remain; African slavery is authorised from 1512.
