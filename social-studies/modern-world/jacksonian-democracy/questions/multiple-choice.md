# Jacksonian democracy, 1820–1840 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jacksonian democracy widened voting and office mainly for —
a) All adults  b) White men  c) Native nations
**Answer: b) White men** — it widens democracy for white men.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nullification was —
a) A state's claim to void federal law  b) A new tax  c) A bank charter
**Answer: a) A state's claim to void federal law** — Jackson crushes nullification.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Jackson's fight against the national bank ended with the bank —
a) Strengthened  b) Crushed  c) Moved to London
**Answer: b) Crushed** — he crushes the national bank.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Trail of Tears was part of —
a) Indian Removal  b) The bank war  c) Nullification
**Answer: a) Indian Removal** — the Indian Removal — the Trail of Tears.
