# Jacksonian democracy, 1820–1840 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jackson's democracy widened for everyone equally, including Native peoples.
**Answer: False** — It widened for white men; Native nations were driven west in the Removal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Jackson crushed both nullification and the national bank.
**Answer: True** — He crushes nullification and the national bank.
