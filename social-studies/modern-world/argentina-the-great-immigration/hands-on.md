# Hands-on — Argentina — the great immigration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Push and pull
Draw two arrows pointing at a ship in the middle of a page. Label the arrow from behind "push" and write under it the reasons people left Europe. Label the arrow from in front "pull" and write under it what Argentina promised. Say out loud which reasons you think were strongest.

## A law that fetches people
Write, in your own words, a short notice the Argentine state might have posted in Europe after the 1876 Immigration and Colonisation Law — saying what the state offered and why it wanted workers to come. Keep it to what the lesson teaches: offices in Europe, paid passages, land and work.

## One third of a country
Draw a crowd of thirty figures. Colour about ten of them a different colour. Under your drawing, write what those coloured figures stand for in Argentina in 1914, and name the two countries most of them were born in.
