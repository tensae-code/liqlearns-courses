# Argentina — the great immigration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What PUSHED millions of people to leave Western Europe?
a) Crisis and crowding at home  b) A law ordering them out  c) A fashion for sea travel
**Answer: a) Crisis and crowding at home** — with too little work and land, families looked across the Atlantic.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What PULLED them to Argentina?
a) Its cold climate  b) The promise of land and work  c) Free houses for everyone
**Answer: b) The promise of land and work** — a young country building itself needed hands.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1876 Immigration and Colonisation Law made the Argentine state —
a) Close its ports  b) Open offices in Europe and pay passages to attract workers  c) Send its own people to Europe
**Answer: b) Open offices in Europe and pay passages to attract workers** — the state went out to fetch the newcomers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** By 1914, the foreign-born share of Argentina's people was —
a) Nearly a third, mostly from Italy and Spain  b) One person in a hundred  c) More than nine tenths
**Answer: a) Nearly a third, mostly from Italy and Spain** — a country peopled in one generation.
