# Argentina — the great immigration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain the great immigration to Argentina in push-and-pull terms.
**Answer: Crisis and crowding in Western Europe pushed millions to leave home, and Argentina's promise of land and work pulled them across the Atlantic (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the Argentine state itself do to attract European workers under the 1876 law?
**Answer: It opened offices in Europe and paid passages, actively recruiting the workers the new country wanted (any close wording).**
