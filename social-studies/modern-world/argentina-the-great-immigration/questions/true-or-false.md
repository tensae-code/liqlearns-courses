# Argentina — the great immigration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Alberdi's saying behind Argentina's immigration policy was "to govern is to populate."
**Answer: True** — the idea became the 1876 Immigration and Colonisation Law.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Most of Argentina's immigrants crossed the Atlantic mainly to escape Argentina's promise of land and work.
**Answer: False** — that promise was the pull that drew them to Argentina; crisis and crowding in Europe were the push.
