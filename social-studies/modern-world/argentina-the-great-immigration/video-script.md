# Video script — Argentina — the great immigration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: millions cross an ocean to a new country, as Argentina's Grade 6 teacher guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the years when Argentina was building its state, life in Western Europe was hard for millions. Crisis and crowding pushed people out of their homes — too little work, too little land, too many mouths.

Across the Atlantic, a young country was calling. Argentina promised land and work. So families packed what they had, boarded ships, and crossed the ocean to a country most of them had never seen.

And Argentina did not simply wait for them. "To govern is to populate," Alberdi had said — a country with empty land needs people. In 1876 that idea became a law, the Immigration and Colonisation Law. The state opened offices in Europe and paid passages, going out to fetch the workers it wanted.

It worked beyond anyone's imagining. By 1914, nearly a third of all the people in Argentina had been born in another country — most of them in Italy and Spain. A new country, peopled in one generation.
