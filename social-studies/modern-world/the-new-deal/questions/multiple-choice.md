# The New Deal, 1932–1941 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The New Deal president was —
a) Hoover  b) Franklin Roosevelt  c) Wilson
**Answer: b) Franklin Roosevelt** — Franklin Roosevelt and the New Deal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The First New Deal's first job was —
a) To win a war  b) To stop the fall — banks, jobs, farms  c) To cut all spending
**Answer: b) To stop the fall — banks, jobs, farms** — banks, jobs and farm programmes stop the fall.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Social Security belongs to —
a) The First New Deal  b) The Second New Deal  c) The Progressive era
**Answer: b) The Second New Deal** — the Second New Deal — Social Security.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The New Deal remade —
a) Only farm prices  b) The federal government's place in American life  c) Nothing lasting
**Answer: b) The federal government's place in American life** — it remakes the federal government's place in American life for good.
