# The New Deal, 1932–1941 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do the First and Second New Deals differ?
**Answer: The First stopped the fall with banks, jobs and farm programmes; the Second went further, building lasting structures — Social Security, labour rights, works programmes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three Second New Deal measures.
**Answer: Social Security, labour rights and works programmes (any close wording).**
