# The New Deal, 1932–1941 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Labour rights were part of the Second New Deal.
**Answer: True** — The Second New Deal — Social Security, labour rights, works programmes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After the New Deal, Americans expected less action from Washington in a crisis.
**Answer: False** — They expected Washington to act — the expectation was remade for good.
