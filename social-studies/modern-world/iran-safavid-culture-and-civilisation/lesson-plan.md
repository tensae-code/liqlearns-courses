# Lesson plan — Iran — Safavid culture and civilisation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Iran — Safavid culture and civilisation
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Iran, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Isfahan becomes half the world — mosques, squares, carpets and a court culture that brands Iran's identity to this day
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Culture and civilisation in the Safavid era** — Isfahan becomes half the world — mosques, squares, carpets and a court culture that brands Iran's identity to this day.
2. **Social, economic, scientific and cultural conditions of Iran in the Safavid era** — the united state at work — bazaars, silk, scholars and Isfahan's building boom.
3. **Isfahan, half the world** — a class trip in words — the Safavid capital's square, mosques and bridges earn their proverb: Isfahan is half the world.
4. **Why did culture and art flourish in the Safavid period?** — peace, wealth and royal patronage are the child's first causal chain — a united rich state buys beauty, and beauty survives it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
