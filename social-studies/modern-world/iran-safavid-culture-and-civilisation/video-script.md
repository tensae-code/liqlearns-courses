# Video script — Iran — Safavid culture and civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iran's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Isfahan becomes half the world — mosques, squares, carpets and a court culture that brands Iran's identity to this day.

the united state at work — bazaars, silk, scholars and Isfahan's building boom.

a class trip in words — the Safavid capital's square, mosques and bridges earn their proverb: Isfahan is half the world.

peace, wealth and royal patronage are the child's first causal chain — a united rich state buys beauty, and beauty survives it.
