Timor-Leste's own way: Grade 6 Social Science is one national story told in three periods — colonial times, independence and Indonesian occupation, then solidarity and human rights. Period II (this book) teaches 1975–2002 as lived memory: children sing the resistance songs, act the events as drama, and interview ordinary people who survived the occupation, writing their mini-biographies. History in this lesson is summary in the course's own words — the book's stories, songs and poems are filed as plot summaries only, never transcribed.
