# Timor-Leste — the Carnation Revolution and the proclamation of 1975 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Carnation Revolution was political change in —
a) Portugal  b) Timor-Leste  c) Indonesia
**Answer: a) Portugal** — Portugal — its fall opened decolonisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The song the book sings the Carnation Revolution through is —
a) Grândola Vila Morena by Zeca Afonso  b) Pátria  c) Esperanças Rasgadas
**Answer: a) Grândola Vila Morena by Zeca Afonso** — Grândola Vila Morena by Zeca Afonso — the song-signal that toppled a dictatorship in Lisbon.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fretilin proclaimed the Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste on —
a) 28 November 1975  b) 20 May 2002  c) 30 August 1999
**Answer: a) 28 November 1975** — 28 November 1975 — with Francisco Xavier do Amaral as president.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The anthem sung for the first time at the proclamation was —
a) Pátria  b) Grândola Vila Morena  c) The Indonesian anthem
**Answer: a) Pátria** — Pátria — sung for the first time as the republic was proclaimed.
