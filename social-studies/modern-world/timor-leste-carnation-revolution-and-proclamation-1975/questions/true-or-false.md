# Timor-Leste — the Carnation Revolution and the proclamation of 1975 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A song helped signal the revolution that toppled Portugal's dictatorship.
**Answer: True — the book teaches the Carnation Revolution through Zeca Afonso's Grândola Vila Morena.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The proclaimed republic enjoyed many peaceful years before any invasion.
**Answer: False — the book gives it nine days of freedom in Dili before the invasion.**
