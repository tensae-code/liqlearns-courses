# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — the Carnation Revolution and the proclamation of 1975

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A song as a signal
Write the name of the song and the singer from this lesson. Under it, write in one sentence how a song can carry a revolution's signal.

## Nine days on a line
Draw a short line for the nine days of freedom. Mark its start: 28 November 1975, the proclamation. Write at its end what the next lesson will bring — and say out loud why nine short days still matter.
