# Hands-on — Venezuela Bolivar Wants America United

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela Bolivar Wants America United — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Almost all Latin America liberated, and Bolívar wants it united, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
