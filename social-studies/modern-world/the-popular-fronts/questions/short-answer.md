Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 5 §8 Le democrazie europee e i «fronti popolari».
**Answer: France and Spain answer fascism with alliances of the left; the democracies that hold do so by coalition — the exception that tests the decade's rule (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
