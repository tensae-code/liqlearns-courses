# Video script — Fiji — education: mission schools to integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Missionaries open Fiji's first schools; from 1879 European settlers' children go to Levuka Public School, Fiji's first public school, while chiefs ask the colonial government to school their sons, and Hannah Dudley founds the Methodist Mission School for Indian children. "multi-racial schools" grow through case-study schools — Brother Lambert's 1937 report, Suva Grammar turning co-educational — until in 2010 the Minister orders school names changed so schools themselves preach integration.
