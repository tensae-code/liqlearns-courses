# Hands-on — Turkey's National Struggle begins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The chain, linked

Draw the chain as six links — Mondros, occupations, Kuvâ-yi Milliye, Amasya, the congresses, the Assembly. Under each link, write in a few words what it answers in the link before.

## Write the circular

In your own words, write the Amasya Circular's core as three lines a town crier could read in a square. Then write the one line you think mattered most, and why.

## Two papers

Draw two documents side by side: the Grand National Assembly's opening declaration and the Treaty of Sèvres. Label who signs each, where, and what future each one promises Anatolia.
