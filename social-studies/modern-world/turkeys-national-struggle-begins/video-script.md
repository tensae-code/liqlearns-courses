# Video script — Turkey's National Struggle begins

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The war is lost. The Mondros armistice disarms the empire, occupations follow — and Turkey's Grade 12 book tells how Anatolia answers.

First the Kuvâ-yi Milliye, local resistance. Then words: the Amasya Circular declares the nation's independence will be saved by the nation's own determination. The Erzurum and Sivas congresses organise it; the Grand National Assembly opens in Ankara.

Against it stands one other paper: Sèvres, the death warrant. A second state rises in Anatolia while the capital signs.
