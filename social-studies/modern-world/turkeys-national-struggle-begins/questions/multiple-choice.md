# Turkey's National Struggle begins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mondros armistice brought —
a) Victory and new lands  b) Disarmament and occupations  c) The republic's founding
**Answer: b) Disarmament and occupations** — defeat's terms.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Kuvâ-yi Milliye were —
a) The national forces — local resistance  b) A foreign army  c) A tax office
**Answer: a) The national forces — local resistance** — the first answer to occupation.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Amasya Circular declared that independence would be saved by —
a) Foreign protectors  b) The nation's own determination  c) The capital's diplomats
**Answer: b) The nation's own determination** — the movement's founding words.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Treaty of Sèvres was —
a) The partition of the homeland — the empire's death warrant  b) Turkey's recognition  c) A trade treaty
**Answer: a) The partition of the homeland — the empire's death warrant** — the paper Ankara refused.
