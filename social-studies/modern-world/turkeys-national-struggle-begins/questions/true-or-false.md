# Turkey's National Struggle begins — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Erzurum and Sivas congresses organised the resistance across Anatolia.
**Answer: True** — the movement gives itself shape.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grand National Assembly opened in the occupied capital, Istanbul.
**Answer: False** — it opened in Ankara, in Anatolia — the second state's seat.
