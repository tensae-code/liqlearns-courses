# Hands-on — Iran under Pahlavi rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran under Pahlavi rule — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Iran in the period of Pahlavi government, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
