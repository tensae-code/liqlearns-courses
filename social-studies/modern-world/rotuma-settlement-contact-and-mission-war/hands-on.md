# Hands-on — Rotuma — settlement, contact and the mission war

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Rotuma — settlement, contact and the mission war — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Legend and early settlers; discovery/contact; missionaries and religious war, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
