# Video script — Rotuma — settlement, contact and the mission war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rotuma's strongest cultural invasions come from Samoa and Tonga in the Polynesian "backwash"; Captain Edward Edwards of HMS Pandora, hunting the Bounty mutineers, sights it in 1791; missionaries follow, and religious war over the Sau system tears the island apart.
