# Guatemala — the internal armed conflict — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book names the war's roots as —
a) injustice, racism and exclusion  b) a foreign invasion only  c) a drought
**Answer: a) injustice, racism and exclusion** — its causes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The URNG united four groups in —
a) 1982  b) 1944  c) 1996
**Answer: a) 1982** — the unification year.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The FAR began the war in —
a) the east  b) Belize  c) the capital only
**Answer: a) the east** — its region.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Some 13,000 died in —
a) the first phase alone  b) one battle  c) the peace talks
**Answer: a) the first phase alone** — the toll the book gives.
