# Lesson plan — Guatemala — the internal armed conflict

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala — the internal armed conflict
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Guatemala, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** born of injustice, racism and exclusion, Guatemala's war runs from the FAR in the east to the four groups united as URNG in 1982; Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wipes out whole villages, and some 13,000 die in the first phase alone.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the war's roots as the book gives them: injustice, racism and exclusion.
2. Name the united guerrilla front of 1982: the URNG, four groups united.
3. Name the offensive that wiped out villages: Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive.
