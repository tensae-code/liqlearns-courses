# Guatemala — the internal armed conflict

Before you read on, think about this lesson's one thing: born of injustice, racism and exclusion, Guatemala's war runs from the FAR in the east to the four groups united as URNG in 1982; Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wipes out whole villages, and some 13,000 die in the first phase alone. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conflicto armado interno — a war born of injustice

Guatemala's Grade 4 book names the war's roots plainly: injustice, racism and exclusion. The war runs from the FAR in the east to the four guerrilla groups united as URNG in 1982. Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wipes out whole villages. Some 13,000 die in the first phase alone — the book counts the cost in villages and lives, not only in battles.
