# European whaling in the Pacific — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Pacific people obtain whales before the fleets came, and what did they make from the teeth?
**Answer: They used only stranded whales; the teeth were taken for chiefs' necklaces (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What happened to the whales in one century of commercial hunting, and what let them begin to recover?
**Answer: They were driven near extinction; under legal protection they slowly recover (any close wording).**
