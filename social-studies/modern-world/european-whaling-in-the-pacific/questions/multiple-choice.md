# European whaling in the Pacific — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before the whaling fleets came, Pacific people used —
a) Whales they hunted at sea  b) Only stranded whales  c) No whales at all
**Answer: b) Only stranded whales** — the way the reader teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Whale teeth were taken for —
a) Fish hooks  b) Chiefs' necklaces  c) Boat building
**Answer: b) Chiefs' necklaces** — as the book teaches it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The whaling fleets that reached Tonga in the 1890s came from —
a) North America  b) Tonga itself  c) Kiribati
**Answer: a) North America** — the fleets named in the book.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The fleets hunted —
a) Only old whales  b) Stranded whales only  c) Mothers and calves
**Answer: c) Mothers and calves** — the hunt the book describes.
