# European whaling in the Pacific — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** North American whaling fleets reached Tonga in the 1890s.
**Answer: True** — the date and the fleets the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The whales recovered because the fleets simply lost interest, with no change in the law.
**Answer: False** — the book says they slowly recover under legal protection.
