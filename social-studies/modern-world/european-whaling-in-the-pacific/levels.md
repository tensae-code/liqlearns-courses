# European whaling in the Pacific — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Whales before the fleets
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that Pacific people used only stranded whales, and that whale teeth were taken for chiefs' necklaces.

## Sub lesson 2 — The whaling fleets come
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that North American whaling fleets reached Tonga in the 1890s and hunted mothers and calves.

## Sub lesson 3 — Near extinction, then protection
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain that one century of commercial hunting drove the whales near extinction, and that they slowly recover under legal protection.
