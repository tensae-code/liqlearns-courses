# Aurangzeb, the Marathas and the long Mughal decline

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Aurangzeb stretches the Mughal Empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas, revolts from Punjab to the south, and weak heirs until the last emperor is exiled to Rangoon after 1857. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Aurangzeb, the Marathas and the long Mughal decline

Aurangzeb stretches the empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas (1630–1680), revolts from Punjab to the south, and after his death a parade of weak emperors until the last, Bahadur Shah Zafar, is exiled to Rangoon after 1857 and dies there in 1862.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
