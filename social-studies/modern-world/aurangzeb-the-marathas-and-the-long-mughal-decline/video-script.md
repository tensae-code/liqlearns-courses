# Video script — Aurangzeb, the Marathas and the long Mughal decline

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aurangzeb stretches the Mughal Empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas, revolts from Punjab to the south, and weak heirs until the last emperor is exiled to Rangoon after 1857.
