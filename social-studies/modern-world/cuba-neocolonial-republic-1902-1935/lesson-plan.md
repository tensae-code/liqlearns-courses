# Lesson plan — Cuba Neocolonial Republic 1902 1935

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Cuba Neocolonial Republic 1902 1935
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Cuba, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Republic is born in 1902 with the Platt Amendment in its constitution; presidents come and go under Washington's eye while sugar wealth flows north
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **2.1 Birth and characteristics of the neocolonial Republic to 1935** — the Republic is born in 1902 with the Platt Amendment in its constitution; presidents come and go under Washington's eye while sugar wealth flows north
2. **6.1–6.4 Formation of the neocolonial Republic; confronting its evils; native oligarchy's struggle for power; corrupt, surrendering governments to 1925** — parties of the oligarchy rotate in office while US capital buys the island's sugar, railways and mines
3. **3.2 Cuba 1902–1925: social and political movements against the neocolonial order** — veterans, workers, feminists and students contest the republic's terms in its first quarter-century
4. **Unit 3 suggestions: social and national-liberation struggles against neocolonial dependence 1899–1935** — teachers frame the republic's protests as a national-liberation struggle continuing by other means
