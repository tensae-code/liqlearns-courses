# Classicism — art looks back to Rome in the age of revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Roman virtue for new powers  b) Disgusted with Rococo  c) An empire style for a new Caesar
**Answer: b) Disgusted with Rococo** — Disgusted with Rococo frivolity, Europe digs up Pompeii and turns back to Rome.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Roman virtue for new powers  b) Disgusted with Rococo  c) An empire style for a new Caesar
**Answer: a) Roman virtue for new powers** — Europe dresses revolution and empire alike in Roman virtue — temples for banks and parliaments, and David's oath scenes for republics.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Disgusted with Rococo  b) Roman virtue for new powers  c) An empire style for a new Caesar
**Answer: c) An empire style for a new Caesar** — Napoleon's empire style dresses a new Caesar in Rome's forms.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Disgusted with Rococo frivolity, Europe digs up Pompeii and dresses revolution and empire alike in Roman virtue — temples for banks and parliaments, David's oath scenes for republics, and Napoleon's empire style for a new Caesar  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
