# Classicism — art looks back to Rome in the age of revolution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Europe dresses revolution and empire alike in Roman virtue — temples for banks and parliaments, and David's oath scenes for republics.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Disgusted with Rococo frivolity, Europe digs up Pompeii and dresses revolution and empire alike in Roman virtue — temples for banks and parliaments, David's oath scenes for republics, and Napoleon's empire style for a new Caesar.
