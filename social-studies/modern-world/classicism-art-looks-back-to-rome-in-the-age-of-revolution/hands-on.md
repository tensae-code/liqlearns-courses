# Hands-on — Classicism — art looks back to Rome in the age of revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw a classicist building with Roman columns raised for a bank or parliament from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: Disgusted with Rococo; Roman virtue for new powers; An empire style for a new Caesar. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
