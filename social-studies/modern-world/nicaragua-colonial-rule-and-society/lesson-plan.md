## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Fundación de las primeras ciudades** — León and Granada are founded as the first Spanish cities, planting colonial power in indigenous land
2. **Dominación colonial: características político-administrativas de las colonias españolas** — Spain rules through a colonial administration built to command the provinces from above
3. **Organización político-administrativa de los pueblos indígenas bajo la colonia** — indigenous towns are reorganised under colonial rule, their own authorities fitted into Spain's system
4. **Características económicas coloniales: el indígena y las tierras que cultivaba** — the colonial economy rests on indigenous labour and on taking and working the land the indigenous farmed
5. **Características sociales de la colonia** — colony sorts people into ranked groups — Spaniards, criollos, mestizos, indigenous and enslaved — and rank decides a life
6. **La iglesia en la vida colonial; misioneros, Leyes de Indias y Contreras** — missionaries evangelise the indigenous; when the New Laws try to protect them, governor Contreras rebels against the limits on his power
7. **Los ingleses y los misquitos en la región del Caribe** — English pirates, raiding slow Spanish galleons, ally with the Miskitu — expert turtle-fishers and sailors — so Spain never truly holds the Caribbean coast
8. **Fundación de ciudades y reducciones a pueblos** — Granada and León are founded as conquest proceeds; survivors are congregated into 198 reducciones — supervised new towns that, paradoxically, preserve communal land and indigenous cabildos
9. **Características de la colonia española en Nicaragua** — cacao tribute collapses as the indigenous population falls, gold mining and pitch enrich colonisers, society hardens into peninsulares, criollos and dominated indigenous, mestizo and mulatto classes under León's governor
10. **Fundación de las primeras ciudades en Nicaragua** — Hernández de Córdoba founds León and Granada in 1524 to fix royal authority on conquered land, each city with its assigned purpose
11. **Características económicas de la Colonia: encomienda, añil y ganado** — the New Laws dress forced labour as encomienda and repartimiento; indigo haciendas worked by indigenous labour and cattle estates in Matagalpa, Estelí and Chontales carry the colonial economy
12. **Características sociales de la población colonial** — colonial society is ranked strictly by origin and skin colour — peninsulares on top, then criollos, with the dominated below
13. **Aspecto político-administrativo de la Colonia** — Nicaragua is governed as a province of the Kingdom of Guatemala, divided into five partidos that later become the departments, each town under its cabildo
