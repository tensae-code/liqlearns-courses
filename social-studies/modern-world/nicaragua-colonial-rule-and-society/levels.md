Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — indigenous towns are reorganised under colonial rule, their own authorities fitted into Spain's system

Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the colonial economy rests on indigenous labour and on taking and working the land the indigenous farmed

## Sub lesson 5 — Características sociales de la colonia
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — colony sorts people into ranked groups — Spaniards, criollos, mestizos, indigenous and enslaved — and rank decides a life

## Sub lesson 6 — La iglesia en la vida colonial; misioneros, Leyes de Indias y Contreras
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — missionaries evangelise the indigenous; when the New Laws try to protect them, governor Contreras rebels against the limits on his power

## Sub lesson 7 — Los ingleses y los misquitos en la región del Caribe
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — English pirates, raiding slow Spanish galleons, ally with the Miskitu — expert turtle-fishers and sailors — so Spain never truly holds the Caribbean coast

## Sub lesson 8 — Fundación de ciudades y reducciones a pueblos
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Granada and León are founded as conquest proceeds; survivors are congregated into 198 reducciones — supervised new towns that, paradoxically, preserve communal land and indigenous cabildos

## Sub lesson 9 — Características de la colonia española en Nicaragua
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — cacao tribute collapses as the indigenous population falls, gold mining and pitch enrich colonisers, society hardens into peninsulares, criollos and dominated indigenous, mestizo and mulatto classes under León's governor

## Sub lesson 10 — Fundación de las primeras ciudades en Nicaragua
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Hernández de Córdoba founds León and Granada in 1524 to fix royal authority on conquered land, each city with its assigned purpose

## Sub lesson 11 — Características económicas de la Colonia: encomienda, añil y ganado
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the New Laws dress forced labour as encomienda and repartimiento; indigo haciendas worked by indigenous labour and cattle estates in Matagalpa, Estelí and Chontales carry the colonial economy

## Sub lesson 12 — Características sociales de la población colonial
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — colonial society is ranked strictly by origin and skin colour — peninsulares on top, then criollos, with the dominated below

## Sub lesson 13 — Aspecto político-administrativo de la Colonia
Sub lesson 13 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Nicaragua is governed as a province of the Kingdom of Guatemala, divided into five partidos that later become the departments, each town under its cabildo
