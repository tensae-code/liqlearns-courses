# Video script — The Matavanu eruption and the Le'auva'a–Salamumu resettlements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Matavanu erupts on Savai'i in 1905 and buries Salaeula and Lealatele; German administrators, Catholic priests and Methodist pastors move the homeless to Upolu, founding Le'auva'a (1908) and Salamumu (1909) — villages that still vote in Savai'i constituencies today.
