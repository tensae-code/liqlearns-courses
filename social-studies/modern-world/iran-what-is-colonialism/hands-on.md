# Hands-on — Iran — what is colonialism?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran — what is colonialism? — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, What is colonialism (استعمار)?, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
