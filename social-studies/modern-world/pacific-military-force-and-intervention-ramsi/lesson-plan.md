# Lesson plan — Military force and intervention in the Pacific — RAMSI

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Military force and intervention in the Pacific — RAMSI
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Papua New Guinea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Guadalcanal and Malaita tensions explode in 1998; the Malaita Eagle Force seizes the Prime Minister in 2000, parliament elects a new one aboard an Australian warship, the Townsville peace follows — and in 2003 RAMSI lands 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Military force and change in the Pacific Region / Intervention of external forces in the Pacific Region** — Guadalcanal and Malaita tensions explode in 1998; the Malaita Eagle Force seizes the Prime Minister in 2000, parliament elects a new one aboard an Australian warship, the Townsville peace follows — and in 2003 RAMSI lands 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias
2. **Foreign intervention** — intervention against isolationism — sanctions, embargoes, blockades and armies; the Iraq wars' cost to the US is the worked example of what intervening does to the intervener
