# Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bernini makes marble fly

Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as three beats: Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds. Then: Borromini bends walls like waves, and Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow. And finally: Ceiling painters break the roof open onto heaven. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
