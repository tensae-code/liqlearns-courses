# Hands-on — Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw St Peter's Square embracing a crowd and a ceiling painting opening onto heaven from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: Bernini makes marble fly; Walls bend, saints enter tavern light; Ceilings open onto heaven. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
