# Lesson plan — Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds; Borromini bends walls like waves; Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow; and ceiling painters break the roof open onto heaven.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.
