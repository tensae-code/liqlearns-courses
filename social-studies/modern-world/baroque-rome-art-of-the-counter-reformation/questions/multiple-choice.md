# Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Walls bend, saints enter tavern light  b) Bernini makes marble fly  c) Ceilings open onto heaven
**Answer: b) Bernini makes marble fly** — Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Walls bend, saints enter tavern light  b) Bernini makes marble fly  c) Ceilings open onto heaven
**Answer: a) Walls bend, saints enter tavern light** — Borromini bends walls like waves, and Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Bernini makes marble fly  b) Walls bend, saints enter tavern light  c) Ceilings open onto heaven
**Answer: c) Ceilings open onto heaven** — Ceiling painters break the roof open onto heaven.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds; Borromini bends walls like waves; Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow; and ceiling painters break the roof open onto heaven  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
