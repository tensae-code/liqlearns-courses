# Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Borromini bends walls like waves, and Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**Answer: False** — Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds; Borromini bends walls like waves; Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow; and ceiling painters break the roof open onto heaven.
