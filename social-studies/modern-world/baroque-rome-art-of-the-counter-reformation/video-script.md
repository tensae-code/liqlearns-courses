# Video script — Baroque Rome — art of the Counter-Reformation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds.

Borromini bends walls like waves, and Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow.

Ceiling painters break the roof open onto heaven.
