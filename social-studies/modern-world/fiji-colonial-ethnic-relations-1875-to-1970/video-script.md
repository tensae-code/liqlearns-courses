# Video script — Fiji — communities under colonial rule, 1875 to 1970

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

British resettlement policy and a European-dominated economy shape how Fijians, Indians and Europeans meet between 1875 and 1970; between them sit the 7,800 kailoma (part-Europeans) counted in 1961, belonging fully to neither side.
