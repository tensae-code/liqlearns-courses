# Indonesia — the native press and Islamic modernism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The native press carries progress into Malay-reading homes while Islamic modernism and reform movements reorganise faith, schools and charity for a new age.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 3.A.2-3 Pers Membawa Kemajuan / Modernisme dan Reformasi Islam

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — from Liberal to Guided Democracy, 1950–1966.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 3.A.2-3 Pers Membawa Kemajuan / Modernisme dan Reformasi Islam
