# Saudi Arabia — Founding Day and National Day — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** More than two hundred years lie between the founding of 1727 and the unification of 1932.
**Answer: True** — 1727 to 1932 is 205 years.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Founding Day and National Day remember the same event in the same year.
**Answer: False** — Founding Day remembers 1727; National Day remembers 1932.
