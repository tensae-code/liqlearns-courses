# Saudi Arabia — Founding Day and National Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who founded the Saudi state, and in which year?
**Answer: Imam Muhammad bin Saud, in 1727 (1139هـ) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who unified the country, and in which year?
**Answer: King Abdulaziz, in 1932 (1351هـ) (any close wording).**
