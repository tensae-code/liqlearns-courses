# Saudi Arabia — Founding Day and National Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Founding Day remembers —
a) The unification of 1932  b) Imam Muhammad bin Saud founding the Saudi state in 1727  c) The approving of the flag
**Answer: b) Imam Muhammad bin Saud founding the Saudi state in 1727** — the founding the book teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** National Day remembers —
a) King Abdulaziz unifying the country in 1932  b) The founding of 1727  c) The building of Riyadh
**Answer: a) King Abdulaziz unifying the country in 1932** — the uniting the book teaches.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Flag Day remembers —
a) A football victory  b) The harvest  c) Abdulaziz approving the flag's form
**Answer: c) Abdulaziz approving the flag's form** — the third symbol in the lesson.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grade 4 book, the national days are taught inside —
a) The Geography unit  b) The Citizenship unit, in the lesson on national symbols  c) The Economics unit
**Answer: b) The Citizenship unit, in the lesson on national symbols** — where the book places them (Unit 1, Lesson 4).
