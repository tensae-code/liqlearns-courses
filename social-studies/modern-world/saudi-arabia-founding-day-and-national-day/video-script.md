# Video script — Saudi Arabia — Founding Day and National Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Saudi Arabia Grade 4 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia keeps two birthdays, and its Grade 4 book teaches both to nine-year-olds as national symbols.

The first is Founding Day. In 1727, Imam Muhammad bin Saud founded the Saudi state. The book tells children its roots run more than 300 years deep.

The second is National Day. In 1932 — more than two hundred years after the founding — King Abdulaziz unified the country into one kingdom.

And beside them stands Flag Day, remembering when Abdulaziz approved the flag's form.

One state begins in 1727. It is united into a kingdom in 1932. And children mark both days every year — because both the beginning and the uniting are theirs.
