# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia — Founding Day and National Day

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two birthdays on a line
Draw a long timeline. Mark 1727 at one end and 1932 near the other. Under 1727 write "Imam Muhammad bin Saud founds the Saudi state"; under 1932 write "King Abdulaziz unifies the country". Walk the line with your finger and say how many years lie between the two days — more than two hundred.

## Draw the two days
Draw two panels. In the first, an old town in 1727 with a flag above it, labelled "Founding Day". In the second, a modern kingdom in 1932, labelled "National Day". Under each, write one sentence: who acted, and what began or was completed.

## A symbol of your own
The book teaches the days as national symbols. Choose one symbol for your own family or class — a flag, a day, an object — draw it, and say in two sentences what it remembers and why it deserves a day of its own.
