# The FSM — an independent nation of islands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The FSM is a member of the United Nations.
**Answer: True** — the book names UN membership among nationhood's marks.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Emigration ended Micronesians' ties to home.
**Answer: False** — remittances bind the spread-out nation to home — emigration spread Micronesians abroad while ties continue.
