# The FSM — an independent nation of islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The FSM's first president, inaugurated in 1979, was —
a) Andon Amaraich  b) President Nakayama  c) Henry Nanpei
**Answer: b) President Nakayama** — the president the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nakayama's inauguration ended nearly —
a) A decade of foreign rule  b) A century of foreign rule  c) Five centuries of foreign rule
**Answer: b) A century of foreign rule** — the span the book gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The FSM's tie to America is the —
a) Trust Territory  b) Compact of Free Association  c) League Mandate
**Answer: b) Compact of Free Association** — the agreement the book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Independence Day in the FSM is —
a) November 3  b) July 4  c) May 10
**Answer: a) November 3** — the day as filed.
