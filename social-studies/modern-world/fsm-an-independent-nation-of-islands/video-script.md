# Video script — The FSM — an independent nation of islands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as Micronesia's books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

*Over Seas and Time* ends its national story here: An Independent Nation of Islands. In 1979, President Nakayama was inaugurated as the first president of the Federated States of Micronesia — ending nearly a century of foreign rule that had run Spain, Germany, Japan and America in turn.

Independence did not mean separation from America: the Compact of Free Association ties the FSM to the United States in a chosen partnership — free association, not rule. The FSM took its seat in the United Nations, and November 3, Independence Day, marks the nationhood every year.

The new nation's people move: emigration spreads Micronesians abroad, many under the Compact's terms, and remittances — money sent home — bind the spread-out nation together. The FSM's story now runs in two places at once: the islands, and the Micronesian communities overseas who still belong to them.
