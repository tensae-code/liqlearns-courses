# Lesson plan — The FSM — an independent nation of islands

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The FSM — an independent nation of islands
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** President Nakayama's 1979 inauguration ended nearly a century of foreign rule, the Compact of Free Association tied the Federated States of Micronesia to America, UN membership and November 3 Independence Day mark its nationhood, and emigration and remittances now spread Micronesians abroad while binding them home.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1979: Nakayama's inauguration ends foreign rule** — say that President Nakayama's 1979 inauguration ended nearly a century of foreign rule.
2. **The Compact, the UN and Independence Day** — say that the Compact of Free Association ties the FSM to America, and UN membership and November 3 Independence Day mark nationhood.
3. **A nation spread abroad: emigration and remittances** — say that emigration and remittances spread Micronesians abroad while binding them to home.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
