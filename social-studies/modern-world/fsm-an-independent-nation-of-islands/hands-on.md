# Hands-on — The FSM — an independent nation of islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four flags, one morning
Draw the four foreign flags' era as four panels and the 1979 inauguration as a fifth, larger panel. Under the fifth, write what a citizen could do that morning that no one could do in the other four.

## Remittance map
Draw your islands (or your town) in the centre and arrows to three far places where its people now live. On each arrow write one thing that travels home along it — money, news, a visitor, a song.
