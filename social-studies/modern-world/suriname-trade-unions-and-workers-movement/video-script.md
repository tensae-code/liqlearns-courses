# Video script — Suriname — trade unions and the workers' movement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

With no legal right to organise and leaders jailed or exiled (Doedel in an asylum for life, De Kom banished), workers still build unions and union federations to win better hours and conditions, striking when needed.
