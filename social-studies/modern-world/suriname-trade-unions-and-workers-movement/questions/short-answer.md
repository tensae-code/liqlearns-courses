# Suriname — trade unions and the workers' movement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Vakbonden en vakcentrales.
**Answer: With no legal right to organise and leaders jailed or exiled (Doedel in an asylum for life, De Kom banished), workers still build unions and union federations to win better hours and conditions, striking when needed. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden*, Thema 1 Les 3: Vakbonden en vakcentrales, printed p. 19.**
