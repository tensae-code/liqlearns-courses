# Kazakhstan Akhmet Baitursynuly — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the linguist who reformed the Kazakh alphabet and edited its first newspaper is honoured in life and destroyed by the regime he outlived.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation"

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана [History of Kazakhstan]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation"

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the alphabet reformer gives Kazakh a scientific grammar — and is shot as an "enemy of the people".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§39–40 A. Baitursynov's contribution to the development of Kazakh linguistics

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (1900–1945 гг.) [History of Kazakhstan (1900–1945)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§39–40 A. Baitursynov's contribution to the development of Kazakh linguistics

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the alphabet reformer honoured in life, destroyed by the regime.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation"

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *История Казахстана (Начало XX века — 1945 г.) [History of Kazakhstan (start of the 20th century – 1945)] — textbook for grade 9 (8)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation"

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** the alphabet reformer and newspaper editor earns his title.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §1.1 Why is A. Baitursynov called "teacher of the nation"?

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы 9 [History of Kazakhstan 9]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §1.1 Why is A. Baitursynov called "teacher of the nation"?

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** the teacher of the nation is revisited as scholar and alphabet-maker.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4.1 The Baitursynov inquiries of Section 4 "Kazakhstan's culture, education and science"
