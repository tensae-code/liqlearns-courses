# Westward expansion and the Mexican-American War, 1800–1860 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lewis and Clark are remembered for —
a) Crossing the continent  b) Founding the national bank  c) Leading the Confederacy
**Answer: a) Crossing the continent** — Lewis and Clark cross the continent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Missouri's admission cracked —
a) The national bank  b) The Union's balance between free and slave states  c) The Constitution's printing
**Answer: b) The Union's balance between free and slave states** — Missouri's admission cracks the Union's balance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Texas broke away from —
a) Spain  b) Mexico  c) Britain
**Answer: b) Mexico** — Texas breaks from Mexico.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mexican-American War (1846–1848) took —
a) A small border strip  b) Half of Mexico  c) All of Canada
**Answer: b) Half of Mexico** — the war takes half of Mexico.
