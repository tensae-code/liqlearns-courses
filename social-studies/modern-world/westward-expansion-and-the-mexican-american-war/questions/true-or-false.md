# Westward expansion and the Mexican-American War, 1800–1860 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every expansion of US land raised the question: free soil or slave soil?
**Answer: True** — The new land left the unanswerable question: free soil or slave soil?

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Westward expansion had nothing to do with slavery.
**Answer: False** — Missouri's balance and the Mexican cession both turned on slavery's spread.
