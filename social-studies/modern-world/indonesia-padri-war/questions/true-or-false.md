# Indonesia — the Padri War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In West Sumatra the Padri movement's religious war becomes a war against the Dutch, drawn by the Dutch officer-illustrator Justus Pieter de Veer who fought in it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.B

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — Asian networks awaken nationalism.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.B

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** West Sumatra's religious civil war draws the Dutch in, and Imam Bonjol's Padris end fighting the colonial army instead.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.B.3 Perang Padri

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — Asian networks awaken nationalism.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.B.3 Perang Padri
