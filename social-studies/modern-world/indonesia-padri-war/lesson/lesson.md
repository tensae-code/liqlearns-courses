# Indonesia — the Padri War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In West Sumatra the Padri movement's religious war becomes a war against the Dutch, ending with Imam Bonjol's Padris fighting the colonial army As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab I.B

In West Sumatra the Padri movement's religious war becomes a war against the Dutch, drawn by the Dutch officer-illustrator Justus Pieter de Veer who fought in it.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 2.B.3 Perang Padri

West Sumatra's religious civil war draws the Dutch in, and Imam Bonjol's Padris end fighting the colonial army instead.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
