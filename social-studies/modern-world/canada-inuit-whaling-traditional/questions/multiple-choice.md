# Inuit whaling — the traditional hunt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inuit hunters have taken whales since long ago because —
a) Whales were easy to catch alone  b) One whale fed and supplied a whole community  c) Whales were kept as pets
**Answer: b) One whale fed and supplied a whole community** — the reason the reader gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The whale's bones were used for —
a) Tools and sleds  b) Boats' sails  c) Writing paper
**Answer: a) Tools and sleds** — as the book teaches it.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Maktaq is —
a) The oil in the lamps  b) The skin and blubber, eaten as food  c) A kind of harpoon
**Answer: b) The skin and blubber, eaten as food.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the old-way hunt, the crew went out —
a) In a big steel ship  b) Each hunter alone on the ice  c) Together in an open boat with hand harpoons
**Answer: c) Together in an open boat with hand harpoons** — teamwork, guided by the Elders' knowledge.
