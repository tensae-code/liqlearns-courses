# Inuit whaling — the traditional hunt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things one whale gave the community.
**Answer: Bones for tools and sleds, oil for lamps, and skin and blubber (maktaq) for food (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose knowledge guided the old-way hunt, and how was the whale hunted?
**Answer: The Elders' knowledge guided it; a crew hunted together in an open boat with hand harpoons, and the whole camp shared the catch (any close wording).**
