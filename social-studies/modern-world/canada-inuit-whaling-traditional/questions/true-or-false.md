# Inuit whaling — the traditional hunt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whale oil was burned in lamps, giving the community light.
**Answer: True** — oil for lamps is one of the whale's gifts in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When the bowhead was brought in, only the crew that caught it shared the catch.
**Answer: False** — the whole camp shared the catch.
