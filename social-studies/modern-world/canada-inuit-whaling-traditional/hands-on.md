# Hands-on — Inuit whaling — the traditional hunt

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One whale, many gifts
Draw a big whale. Around it, draw or write the things it gave the community: bones for tools and sleds, oil for lamps, skin and blubber (maktaq) for food. Under the drawing, write in one sentence why one whale mattered so much.

## The crew in the boat
Draw an open boat with a crew rowing together, and an Elder pointing the way. Say out loud whose knowledge guided the hunt, and what each person in the boat had to do together.

## Sharing the catch
Act it out with others: one person is the crew bringing the catch in; everyone else is the camp. Share out pretend pieces of the catch until everyone has a share — then say why the whole camp shared, in your own words.
