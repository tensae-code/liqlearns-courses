# Inuit whaling — the traditional hunt — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why hunt whales? What the whale gave
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say why the whale was hunted and name what it gave — bones for tools and sleds, oil for lamps, skin and blubber (maktaq) for food.

## Sub lesson 2 — Whaling the old way
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell how Elders' knowledge and a crew in an open boat with hand harpoons brought the bowhead in, and that the whole camp shared the catch.
