# Video script — Aussiedler — ethnic Germans return, and Germany's new immigration law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

ethnic Germans (Aussiedler) return in waves — 1.4 million in 1950–87, 2.7 million more in 1988–2000 — until quotas (225,000 a year, then 103,000) slow the flow; asylum is tightened in 1993, a 2000 citizenship law gives German-born children of foreigners citizenship for the first time in some ninety years, and a green card opens the door to skilled IT workers instead.
