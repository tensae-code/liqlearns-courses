# Aussiedler — ethnic Germans return, and Germany's new immigration law

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: ethnic Germans (Aussiedler) return in waves — 1.4 million in 1950–87, 2.7 million more in 1988–2000 — until quotas (225,000 a year, then 103,000) slow the flow; asylum is tightened in 1993, a 2000 citizenship law gives German-born children of foreigners citizenship for the first time in some ninety years, and a green card opens the door to skilled IT workers instead. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
