# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule.
**Answer: by the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and the Brunei sultan cedes the territory; the Brooke dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions it must obey (any close wording).**
