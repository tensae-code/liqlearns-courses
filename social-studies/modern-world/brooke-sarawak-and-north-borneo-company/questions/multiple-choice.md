# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule** — by the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and the Brunei sultan cedes the territory; the Brooke dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions it must obey

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 5: Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 5: Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule** — the book's own heading for this part.
