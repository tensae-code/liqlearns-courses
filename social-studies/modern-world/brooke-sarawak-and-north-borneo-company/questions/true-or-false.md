# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** By the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and the Brunei sultan cedes the territory; the Brooke dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions it must obey
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 5: Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule).
