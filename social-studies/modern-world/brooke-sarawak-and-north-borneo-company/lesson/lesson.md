# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: James Brooke rules Sarawak from 1841–42, pledged to respect Malay law and custom, and his dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule

By the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and the Brunei sultan cedes the territory; the Brooke dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions it must obey (Bab 5: Sarawak and Sabah under Western rule)
