# Hands-on — The plantation economy of colonial Saint-Domingue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Inventory an estate
Write an inventory card for one habitation, the book's way: its crop (sugar, coffee or indigo), its planter, the size of the exploitation, and the enslaved workforce employed on it. Say out loud what each line tells you about the estate as a production unit.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a habitation plan from memory — fields, works and quarters — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
