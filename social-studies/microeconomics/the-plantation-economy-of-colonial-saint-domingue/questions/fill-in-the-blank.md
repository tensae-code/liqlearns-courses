# The plantation economy of colonial Saint-Domingue — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The estate at the centre of the plantation economy is called a _____.
**Answer: habitation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils inventory the _____, maps and plans of colonial habitations near their school.
**Answer: vestiges**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For each estate, pupils record the planter, the size of the _____ and the enslaved workforce.
**Answer: exploitation**
