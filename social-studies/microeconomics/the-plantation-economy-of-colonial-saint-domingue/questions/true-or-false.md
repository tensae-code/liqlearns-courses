# The plantation economy of colonial Saint-Domingue — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's habitations produced sugar, coffee and indigo.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches the plantation economy only from a definition, with no work on the ground.
**Answer: False** — pupils inventory the vestiges, maps and plans of actual colonial habitations near their school.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The workforce recorded for each estate was its enslaved workforce.
**Answer: True**
