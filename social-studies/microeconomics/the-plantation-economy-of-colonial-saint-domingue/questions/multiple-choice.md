# The plantation economy of colonial Saint-Domingue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The production unit of the plantation economy was —
a) the habitation — the estate  b) the port warehouse  c) the market stall
**Answer: a) the habitation — the estate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Temps 2, pupils inventory —
a) ships in the harbour  b) the vestiges, maps and plans of colonial habitations near their school  c) prices in the market
**Answer: b) the vestiges, maps and plans of colonial habitations near their school**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For each estate, pupils record —
a) its exports only  b) its planter, the size of the exploitation and the enslaved workforce  c) its church and school
**Answer: b) its planter, the size of the exploitation and the enslaved workforce**
