Haiti's own way: the plantation economy is reconstructed from Haiti's own ground — pupils inventory the vestiges, maps and plans of actual colonial habitations (sugar, coffee and indigo estates) near their school, recording each estate's planter, size and workforce, before the system is generalised. This lesson keeps that way: the habitation is read from its traces on the ground. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
