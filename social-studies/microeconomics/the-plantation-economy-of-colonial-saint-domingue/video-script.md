# Video script — The plantation economy of colonial Saint-Domingue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The plantation economy of colonial Saint-Domingue stood on one unit: the habitation — an estate organised to produce sugar, coffee or indigo with enslaved labour.

The Haitian book builds it from the ground. Pupils inventory the vestiges, maps and plans of real colonial habitations near their school — the traces the plantation economy left behind.

For every estate they record three things: the planter, the size of the exploitation, and the enslaved workforce. Read that way, estate by estate, the whole system stands up from its own traces.
