# The traditional economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Decisions by custom"?
a) decisions guided by custom, history and the elders' experience  b) family or tribe at the centre; production for need, rarely a surplus  c) trade by barter; a farming surplus starts the move toward money
**Answer: a) decisions guided by custom, history and the elders' experience**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The family at the centre"?
a) decisions guided by custom, history and the elders' experience  b) trade by barter; a farming surplus starts the move toward money  c) family or tribe at the centre; production for need, rarely a surplus
**Answer: c) family or tribe at the centre; production for need, rarely a surplus**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Barter, then change"?
a) family or tribe at the centre; production for need, rarely a surplus  b) trade by barter; a farming surplus starts the move toward money  c) decisions guided by custom, history and the elders' experience
**Answer: b) trade by barter; a farming surplus starts the move toward money**
