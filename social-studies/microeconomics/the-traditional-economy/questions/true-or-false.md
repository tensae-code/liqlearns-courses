# The traditional economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a traditional economy, decisions are guided by custom, history and the experience of elders.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a traditional economy, decisions are guided by custom, history and the experience of elders.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Decisions by custom); sub lesson 2 says: In a traditional economy the family or tribe is the centre, and production is mostly only what is needed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade in a traditional economy is by barter, and a farming surplus starts the evolution toward money.
**Answer: True**
