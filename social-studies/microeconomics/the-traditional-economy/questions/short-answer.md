# The traditional economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Decisions by custom" in your own words.
**Answer:** In a traditional economy, decisions are guided by custom, history and the experience of elders.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The family at the centre" in your own words.
**Answer:** In a traditional economy the family or tribe is the centre, and production is mostly only what is needed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Barter, then change" in your own words.
**Answer:** Trade in a traditional economy is by barter, and a farming surplus starts the evolution toward money.
