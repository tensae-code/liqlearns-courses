# The traditional economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a traditional economy, decisions are guided by _____, history and the elders' experience.
**Answer: custom**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The centre of a traditional economy is the _____ or tribe.
**Answer: family**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade between groups in a traditional economy is by _____.
**Answer: barter**
