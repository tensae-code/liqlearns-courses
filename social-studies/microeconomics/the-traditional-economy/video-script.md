# Video script — The traditional economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In a traditional economy, decisions are guided by custom, history and the experience of elders. What was done last year, and by the fathers before, is the plan for this year.

The family or tribe is the centre of the economy. Production is farming, fishing, hunting or gathering, and it is mostly only what is needed — rarely a surplus beyond the group's own use.

Trade, when it happens between non-competing groups, is by barter — goods directly for goods. Such economies begin to evolve toward money once farming produces a surplus worth exchanging regularly.
