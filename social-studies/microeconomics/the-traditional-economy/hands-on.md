# Hands-on — The traditional economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Role-play a barter exchange between two non-competing groups — fish for grain — with no money allowed. Note what made the trade possible and what made it hard.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a farming and herding community exchanging goods directly, no money in sight — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
