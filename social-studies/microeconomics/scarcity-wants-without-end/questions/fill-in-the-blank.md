# Scarcity — wants without end, resources with limits — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good is scarce when less is available than people would take at _____ price.
**Answer: zero**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a shortage, people cannot get what they want at the _____ price.
**Answer: prevailing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If resources were _____, there would be no economic problem at all.
**Answer: abundant**
