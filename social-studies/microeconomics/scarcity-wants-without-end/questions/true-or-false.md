# Scarcity — wants without end, resources with limits — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good is scarce when less is available than people would take at zero price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good is scarce when less is available than people would take at zero price.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (What scarcity is); sub lesson 2 says: A shortage is people being unable to get what they want at the prevailing price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If resources were abundant, there would be no economic problem at all.
**Answer: True**
