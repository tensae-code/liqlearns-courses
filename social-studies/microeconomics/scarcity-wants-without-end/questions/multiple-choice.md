# Scarcity — wants without end, resources with limits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What scarcity is"?
a) less available than people would take at zero price  b) a shortage is short-term at the prevailing price; scarcity is universal and endless  c) if resources were abundant there would be no economic problem
**Answer: a) less available than people would take at zero price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Scarcity is not shortage"?
a) less available than people would take at zero price  b) if resources were abundant there would be no economic problem  c) a shortage is short-term at the prevailing price; scarcity is universal and endless
**Answer: c) a shortage is short-term at the prevailing price; scarcity is universal and endless**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "No scarcity, no problem"?
a) a shortage is short-term at the prevailing price; scarcity is universal and endless  b) if resources were abundant there would be no economic problem  c) less available than people would take at zero price
**Answer: b) if resources were abundant there would be no economic problem**
