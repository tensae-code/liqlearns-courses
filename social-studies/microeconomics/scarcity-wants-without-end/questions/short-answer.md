# Scarcity — wants without end, resources with limits — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What scarcity is" in your own words.
**Answer:** A good is scarce when less is available than people would take at zero price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Scarcity is not shortage" in your own words.
**Answer:** A shortage is people being unable to get what they want at the prevailing price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "No scarcity, no problem" in your own words.
**Answer:** If resources were abundant, there would be no economic problem at all.
