# Hands-on — Scarcity — wants without end, resources with limits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Hold a small cup of water and a long list of thirsty 'wants' on cards. Show that the cup empties while the cards keep coming — then explain shortage versus scarcity to a partner.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a balance tipped between a short stack of goods and an endless queue of wants, with a small shortage-today sign beside it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
