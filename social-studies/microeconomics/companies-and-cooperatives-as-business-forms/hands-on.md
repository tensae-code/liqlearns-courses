# Hands-on — Companies and cooperatives as business forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the forms
Write these on slips: one owner carrying all responsibility; shareholders with a board and a CEO; members pooling investment with one vote each. Sort them into sole business, joint-stock company and cooperative, then check against the lesson.

## Count the votes
A cooperative has 5 members; one put in half the money. Hold a vote on a pretend question and count it the cooperative way — one member, one vote — then say how a joint-stock company would weigh the same votes.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — limited liability and raising capital — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
