# Companies and cooperatives as business forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A joint-stock company is owned by —
a) its chief executive alone  b) its shareholders  c) the state
**Answer: b) its shareholders**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A shareholder who loses money when the company fails loses —
a) everything they own  b) only the share they put in — liability is limited  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) only the share they put in — liability is limited**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a cooperative company, votes are counted —
a) by the size of each member's capital share  b) one member, one vote  c) by the chair alone
**Answer: b) one member, one vote**
