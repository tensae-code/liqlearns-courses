# Companies and cooperatives as business forms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a joint-stock company the shareholders elect a board, led by a chair, and a CEO runs the company day by day.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A company can raise capital only from one owner's purse.
**Answer: False — A company raises capital by selling shares to many people, and can take bank finance beside it — far beyond one owner's purse.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a cooperative company, a member with a bigger capital share has more votes than a member with a smaller share.
**Answer: False — Every member is equal: whatever the size of a member's capital share, each member has one vote.**
