# Companies and cooperatives as business forms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A shareholder's _____ is limited — the shareholder risks the share put in, not everything they own.
**Answer: liability**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Shares can be sold to many people, so a company can raise _____ beyond one owner's purse.
**Answer: capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a cooperative, members pool their investment, share profit and _____, and each member has one vote.
**Answer: loss**
