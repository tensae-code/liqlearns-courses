# Companies and cooperatives as business forms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who owns a joint-stock company, and who runs it day by day?
**Answer:** The shareholders own it. A board and its chair direct it, and the chief executive (CEO) runs it day by day.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two strengths does the company form have, and what two costs stand against them?
**Answer:** Limited liability for shareholders and the power to raise capital (and bank finance). Against them: higher set-up costs and state rules protecting investors, which the company must follow.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is a cooperative company founded, and how do its members stand to each other?
**Answer:** A group pools its investment and works together under the cooperatives law to meet its livelihood and job needs, sharing profit and loss. Every member is equal — one member, one vote, whatever the size of the member's capital share.
