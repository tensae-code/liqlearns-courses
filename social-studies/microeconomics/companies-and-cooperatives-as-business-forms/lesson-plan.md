# Lesson plan — Companies and cooperatives as business forms

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Companies and cooperatives as business forms
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Iran, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's Grade 10 Economics book, in Lesson 2 "Choosing the type of business", teaches how a **joint-stock company** is formed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Iran's Grade 10 Economics Lesson 2 and Grade 8 Social Studies Lesson 2, in their sections on forming a company and on cooperative companies.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The joint-stock company — shareholders, board, chair and CEO**
2. **Limited liability and raising capital — why the company form can grow**
3. **Cooperatives — members together, one member one vote**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the lessons named in this lesson's own level line above. Recommended, never forced.
