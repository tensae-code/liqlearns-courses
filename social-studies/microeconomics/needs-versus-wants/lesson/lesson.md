# Needs versus wants

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — needs versus wants — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — A need — what you have to have

A **need** is something you **have to have** — to **live, be safe, grow, learn, be healthy and be clean**. Needs come first.

## Sub lesson 2 — A want — what you wish you had

A **want** is something you **wish you had**. A want is not something you have to have to live.

## Sub lesson 3 — Decide what is most important — prioritise needs

You must **decide what is most important** and **prioritise needs** — put needs before wants when you choose.

## Say it to your companion

Explain needs versus wants to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Using the lesson, say in your own words what needs versus wants teaches, part by part.

2. Write the key words from memory — a need — what you have to have, a want — what you wish you had, decide what is most important — prioritise needs — and write one sentence of your own for each.

3. Check your Do it first sentence against the lesson: fix anything the lesson teaches differently.

4. Answer this lesson's questions from questions/ without the lesson open; re-read only the parts you missed.
