# Needs versus wants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about a need — what you have to have?
**Answer:** A need is something you have to have — to live, be safe, grow, learn, be healthy and be clean. Needs come first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about a want — what you wish you had?
**Answer:** A want is something you wish you had. A want is not something you have to have to live.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about decide what is most important — prioritise needs?
**Answer:** You must decide what is most important and prioritise needs — put needs before wants when you choose.
