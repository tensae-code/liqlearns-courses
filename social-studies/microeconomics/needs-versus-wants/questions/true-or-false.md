# Needs versus wants — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A need is something you have to have — to live, be safe, grow, learn, be healthy and be clean.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A want is something you wish you had.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** You must decide what is most important and prioritise needs — put needs before wants when you choose.
**Answer: True**
