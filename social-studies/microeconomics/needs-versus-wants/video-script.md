# Video script — Needs versus wants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Needs versus wants — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: A need is something you have to have — to live, be safe, grow, learn, be healthy and be clean. Needs come first. A want is something you wish you had. A want is not something you have to have to live. You must decide what is most important and prioritise needs — put needs before wants when you choose.
