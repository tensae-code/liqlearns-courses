# Hands-on — Isoquants — same output, different mixes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay worker counters and machine counters on a table in five combinations that a partner agrees could each make 100 chairs. Join the five points with string — your first isoquant — and slide a finger along it naming what is traded.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a contour-style map of three isoquants, farther out meaning more output — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
