# Isoquants — same output, different mixes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An isoquant joins the labour–capital mixes that yield exactly the _____.
**Answer: same output**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On an isoquant map, a farther-out isoquant means _____.
**Answer: more output**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Along an isoquant the MRTS is _____ — each extra worker replaces less capital.
**Answer: diminishing**
