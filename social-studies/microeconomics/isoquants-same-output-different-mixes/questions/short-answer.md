# Isoquants — same output, different mixes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Same output, different mixes" in your own words.
**Answer:** An isoquant joins input combinations that all yield exactly the same output.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The map and its four properties" in your own words.
**Answer:** A farther-out isoquant means more output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The MRTS — the swap rate worsens" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MRTS diminishes along an isoquant — each extra worker replaces less capital than the one before.
