| Bulgaria part — Economics expansion — Exercises, "Marketing research" (Маркетингово проучване): it is easier to sell a product that is sought on the market than to produce a new product and then look for a market for it; marketing research answers what product consumers seek and with what quality, and at what prices and in what quantities they are ready to buy; the market chance of a business idea is scored on six indicators — is there a need for such a product, are there competing products on the market, is the product better quality than the competitors', is it in fashion at the moment, is there a tendency for demand to rise, and at what price can it be offered — each scored weak (2), medium (4) or high (6) points: 12–18 points a small chance, 19–27 a medium chance, 28–36 a big chance of market realisation | Bulgaria, *Bulgaria Grade 7 Technology and Entrepreneurship*, Exercises, "Marketing research" (Маркетингово проучване) — printed p. 70 |
