# Market research — testing a business idea

## Do it first

Think of something you could make and sell. Before you read on, guess honestly: is it sought — and how would you know?

## Sub lesson 1 — Sell what is sought — research first

Bulgaria's Grade 7 exercises teach the order of work: **it is easier to sell a product that is sought on the market than to produce a new product and then look for a market for it**. So the research comes first, before producing.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the research answers — product, quality, price, quantity

**Marketing research answers what product consumers seek and with what quality, and at what prices and in what quantities they are ready to buy.** Four things, all about the consumer's side of the market: the product, its quality, its price and its quantity.

## Sub lesson 3 — Scoring the market chance — six indicators

The exercise scores the idea: **the market chance of a business idea is scored on six indicators — is there a need for such a product, are there competing products on the market, is the product better quality than the competitors', is it in fashion at the moment, is there a tendency for demand to rise, and at what price can it be offered — each scored weak (2), medium (4) or high (6) points: 12–18 points a small chance, 19–27 a medium chance, 28–36 a big chance of market realisation**.

## Say it to your companion

Explain the lesson to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Using the lesson, state what marketing research answers, all four parts.

2. Write the six indicators from memory and the point values for weak, medium and high.

3. Score an idea of your own on all six indicators, total it, and name its chance band.
