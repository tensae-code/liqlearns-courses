# Market research — testing a business idea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What four things does marketing research answer?
**Answer:** What product consumers seek, with what quality, and at what prices and in what quantities they are ready to buy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the six scoring indicators.
**Answer:** Is there a need for such a product; are there competing products on the market; is the product better quality than the competitors'; is it in fashion at the moment; is there a tendency for demand to rise; at what price can it be offered.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An idea scores 4, 6, 4, 2, 6 and 4. What is its market chance?
**Answer:** The total is 26 points — a medium chance of market realisation (19–27).
