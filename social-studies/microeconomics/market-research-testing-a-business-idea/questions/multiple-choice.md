# Market research — testing a business idea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** It is easier to sell —
a) a product that is sought on the market  b) a new product and then look for a market  c) anything at all
**Answer: a) a product that is sought on the market**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marketing research answers what product consumers seek, with what quality, and —
a) at what prices and in what quantities they are ready to buy  b) who produced it  c) where the factory stands
**Answer: a) at what prices and in what quantities they are ready to buy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A score of 30 points means the idea's market chance is —
a) small  b) medium  c) big
**Answer: c) big**
