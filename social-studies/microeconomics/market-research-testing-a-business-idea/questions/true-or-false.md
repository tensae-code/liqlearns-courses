# Market research — testing a business idea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each of the six indicators is scored weak (2), medium (4) or high (6) points.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A score of 15 points is a medium chance of market realisation.
**Answer: False — 12–18 points is a small chance; 19–27 is medium.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One of the six indicators is whether there is a tendency for demand to rise.
**Answer: True**
