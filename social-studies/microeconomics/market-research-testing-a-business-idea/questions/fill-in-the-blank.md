# Market research — testing a business idea — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** It is easier to sell a product that is _____ on the market than to produce first and look for a market after.
**Answer: sought**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The market chance of a business idea is scored on _____ indicators.
**Answer: six**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A score of 28–36 points is a _____ chance of market realisation.
