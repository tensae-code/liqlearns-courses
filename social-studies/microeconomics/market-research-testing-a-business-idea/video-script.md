# Video script — Market research — testing a business idea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Research before you produce: it is easier to sell a product that is sought than to make one and hunt for its market. Market research answers four things — what product consumers seek, with what quality, at what prices, in what quantities. Then score the idea on six indicators, 2, 4 or 6 points each: 12 to 18 a small chance, 19 to 27 medium, 28 to 36 a big chance.
