# Lesson plan — Market research — testing a business idea

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Market research — testing a business idea
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Bulgaria, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Market research answers what product consumers seek, with what quality, and at what prices and in what quantities they are ready to buy — and it scores a business idea's market chance on six indicators, 2, 4 or 6 points each: 12–18 a small chance, 19–27 a medium chance, 28–36 a big chance.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Bulgaria's Grade 7 marketing-research exercise, worked by the class's own student firms.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
