# Hands-on — Market research — testing a business idea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Score a real idea
Take a product idea of your own or your class firm's. Write the six indicators in a column and score each 2, 4 or 6 with a reason. Add the total and name the chance — small, medium or big.

## Ask the market
Ask five people the research questions for one product: would you seek it, at what quality, at what price, how many? Write their answers beside the four research answers.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 3 aloud — the six indicators and the three score bands — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
