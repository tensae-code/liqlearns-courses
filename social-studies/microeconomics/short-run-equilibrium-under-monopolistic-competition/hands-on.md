# Hands-on — Short-run equilibrium under monopolistic competition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tilt a demand string two ways on one board: steep for a monopolist, flatter for a firm with close substitutes. The class reads off which firm loses more sales from the same price rise.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the equilibrium diagram with its POSR profit rectangle, beside flatter monopoly and flat competition demand curves for contrast — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
