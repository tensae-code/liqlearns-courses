# Short-run equilibrium under monopolistic competition — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With close substitutes all around, this firm's demand is more _____ than a monopolist's.
**Answer: elastic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Short-run equilibrium under monopolistic competition is set where _____ — the same rule as under monopoly.
**Answer: MR = MC**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With price above average cost at the equilibrium output, the firm earns _____ in the short run.
**Answer: supernormal profit**
