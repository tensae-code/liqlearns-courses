# Short-run equilibrium under monopolistic competition — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A monopolistically competitive firm's demand is more elastic than a monopolist's because close substitutes exist.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Short-run equilibrium under monopolistic competition requires MR = MC, with MR cutting MC from below.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's worked diagram shows the firm earning supernormal profit in the short run, with price above average cost.
**Answer: True**
