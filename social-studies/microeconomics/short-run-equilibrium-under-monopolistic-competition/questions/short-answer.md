# Short-run equilibrium under monopolistic competition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A downward but elastic demand" in your own words.
**Answer:** A monopolistically competitive firm's demand is more elastic than a monopolist's because close substitutes exist.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Equilibrium — MR equals MC again" in your own words.
**Answer:** Short-run equilibrium under monopolistic competition requires MR = MC, with MR cutting MC from below.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The book's outcome — supernormal profit" in your own words.
**Answer:** The book's worked diagram shows the firm earning supernormal profit in the short run, with price above average cost.
