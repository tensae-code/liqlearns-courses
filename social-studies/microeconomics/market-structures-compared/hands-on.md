# Hands-on — Market structures compared

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Four learners hold market cards — many sellers identical goods, many sellers branded goods, few giants, one supplier. The class orders them along a line on the floor and defends the order with the three classification tests.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a spectrum bar from perfect competition to monopoly — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
