# Market structures compared — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The three classification tests" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market structures are classified by competition, product type, and the numbers of sellers and buyers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The two poles" in your own words.
**Answer:** Perfect competition is the pole with very many sellers of an identical product.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the classification matters" in your own words.
**Answer:** A firm's market structure decides whether it takes the market price or can set its own.
