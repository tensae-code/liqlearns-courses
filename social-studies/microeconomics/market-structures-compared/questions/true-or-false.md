# Market structures compared — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market structures are classified by competition, product type, and the numbers of sellers and buyers.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Perfect competition is the pole with very many sellers of an identical product.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A firm's market structure decides whether it takes the market price or can set its own.
**Answer: True**
