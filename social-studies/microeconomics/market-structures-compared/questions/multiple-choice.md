# Market structures compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The three classification tests"?
a) degree of competition, type of product, numbers of sellers and buyers  b) perfect competition at one pole, imperfect structures beyond it  c) structure decides whether a firm takes price, sets it, or competes by brand
**Answer: a) degree of competition, type of product, numbers of sellers and buyers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The two poles"?
a) degree of competition, type of product, numbers of sellers and buyers  b) structure decides whether a firm takes price, sets it, or competes by brand  c) perfect competition at one pole, imperfect structures beyond it
**Answer: c) perfect competition at one pole, imperfect structures beyond it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the classification matters"?
a) perfect competition at one pole, imperfect structures beyond it  b) structure decides whether a firm takes price, sets it, or competes by brand  c) degree of competition, type of product, numbers of sellers and buyers
**Answer: b) structure decides whether a firm takes price, sets it, or competes by brand**
