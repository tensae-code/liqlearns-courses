# Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Demand is not mere desire" in your own words.
**Answer:** Demand is a desire backed by ability to pay and willingness to pay, at a given price and time.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Quantity demanded" in your own words.
**Answer:** Quantity demanded is the amount a consumer is willing and able to buy at one given price in a given period.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The book's birr examples" in your own words.
**Answer:** Jeans at Birr 500 may be demanded where the same jeans at Birr 900 are not, and a heater is demanded in the cold season but not the hot season.
