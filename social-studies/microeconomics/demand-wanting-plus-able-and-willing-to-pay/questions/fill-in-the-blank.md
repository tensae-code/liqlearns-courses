# Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand is desire backed by _____ to pay and willingness to pay.
**Answer: ability**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Quantity demanded is the amount bought at _____ in a given period.
**Answer: one given price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A heater at Birr 1,000 may be demanded in the cold _____ but not the hot one.
**Answer: season**
