# Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Demand is not mere desire"?
a) desire backed by ability and willingness to pay, at a given price and time  b) the amount willing and able to be bought at one given price in a given period  c) jeans demanded at Birr 500 but not 900; a heater in the cold season, not the hot
**Answer: a) desire backed by ability and willingness to pay, at a given price and time**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Quantity demanded"?
a) desire backed by ability and willingness to pay, at a given price and time  b) jeans demanded at Birr 500 but not 900; a heater in the cold season, not the hot  c) the amount willing and able to be bought at one given price in a given period
**Answer: c) the amount willing and able to be bought at one given price in a given period**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The book's birr examples"?
a) the amount willing and able to be bought at one given price in a given period  b) jeans demanded at Birr 500 but not 900; a heater in the cold season, not the hot  c) desire backed by ability and willingness to pay, at a given price and time
**Answer: b) jeans demanded at Birr 500 but not 900; a heater in the cold season, not the hot**
