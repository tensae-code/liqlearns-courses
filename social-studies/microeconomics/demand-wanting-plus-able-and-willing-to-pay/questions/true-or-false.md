# Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand is a desire backed by ability to pay and willingness to pay, at a given price and time.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Demand is a desire backed by ability to pay and willingness to pay, at a given price and time.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Demand is not mere desire); sub lesson 2 says: Quantity demanded is the amount a consumer is willing and able to buy at one given price in a given period.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Jeans at Birr 500 may be demanded where the same jeans at Birr 900 are not, and a heater is demanded in the cold season but not the hot season.
**Answer: True**
