# Lesson plan — Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Introduction to Demand and Supply of the Grade 9 Economics course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Demand is an effective desire — wanting a good, backed by ability and willingness to pay, at a given price and time; quantity demanded is the amount at one given price.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Microeconomics, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
