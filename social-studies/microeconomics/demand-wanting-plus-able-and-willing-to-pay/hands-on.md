# Hands-on — Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Give a learner 500 play birr. Offer goods at several prices; each time, the learner must say 'demand' or 'only a want' and name the missing piece — ability or willingness.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a shopper with Birr 500 looking at jeans tagged 500 and 900 — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
