# Demand — wanting, plus able and willing to pay

## Do it first

Jeans are tagged Birr 500 and Birr 900. With Birr 600 in your pocket, which pair do you demand — and is 'I want the 900 pair' demand at all? Say why before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — Demand is not mere desire

Demand is not mere desire or want. It is a desire backed by ability to pay and willingness to pay, with the good available — stated for a given price and a given time. Missing any piece, it is only a wish.

[Picture 24025 — a shopper with Birr 500 looking at jeans tagged 500 and 900]

## Sub lesson 2 — Quantity demanded

Quantity demanded is the amount a consumer is willing and able to buy at one given price in a given period. Change the price and you are talking about a different quantity demanded — the pair (price, amount) is the unit of the idea.

## Sub lesson 3 — The book's birr examples

Jeans at Birr 500 may be demanded where the same jeans at Birr 900 are not — ability draws the line. And a room heater at Birr 1,000 may be demanded in the cold season but not the hot season — time is part of every demand statement.
