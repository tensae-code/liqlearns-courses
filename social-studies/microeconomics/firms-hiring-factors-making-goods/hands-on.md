# Hands-on — Firms — hiring factors, making goods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Set up a pretend firm with chairs and a table. One learner hires 'workers' and 'a room', makes paper goods, and sells them — narrating each hire and sale with the lesson's words.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a firm in the middle, hiring on one side and selling finished goods on the other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
