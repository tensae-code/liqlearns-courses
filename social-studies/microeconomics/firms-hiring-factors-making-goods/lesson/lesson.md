# Firms — hiring factors, making goods

## Do it first

Name one business near you. What does it hire — whose work, whose building, whose money — and what does it sell, and to whom? Say the two sides aloud.

## Sub lesson 1 — Firms hire the factors

Firms — the word is used interchangeably with producers — hire factor services from households. They are the main buyers of factors: the land, labour, capital and enterprise that production needs pass through their hands.

[Picture 24006 — a firm in the middle, hiring on one side and selling finished goods on the other]

## Sub lesson 2 — Firms produce and sell

Firms produce commodities and sell them to households, other firms, the government or other countries. Hiring on one side and selling on the other is the firm's whole rhythm.

## Sub lesson 3 — Private and government firms

Firms include both private enterprises and government enterprises — ownership differs, the hiring-and-selling work is the same. The teacher guide adds the firm's aim in one line: firms aim to maximise profit (teacher guide — evidence).
