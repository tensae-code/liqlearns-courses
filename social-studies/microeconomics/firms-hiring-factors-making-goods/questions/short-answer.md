# Firms — hiring factors, making goods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Firms hire the factors" in your own words.
**Answer:** Firms hire factor services from households and are the main buyers of factors.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Firms produce and sell" in your own words.
**Answer:** Firms produce commodities and sell them to households, other firms, government or other countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Private and government firms" in your own words.
**Answer:** Firms include both private and government enterprises, and firms aim to maximise profit.
