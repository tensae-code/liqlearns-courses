# Firms — hiring factors, making goods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Firms hire factor services from households and are the main buyers of factors.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Firms hire factor services from households and are the main buyers of factors.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Firms hire the factors); sub lesson 2 says: Firms produce commodities and sell them to households, other firms, government or other countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Firms include both private and government enterprises, and firms aim to maximise profit.
**Answer: True**
