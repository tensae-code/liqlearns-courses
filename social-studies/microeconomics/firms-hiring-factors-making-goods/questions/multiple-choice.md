# Firms — hiring factors, making goods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Firms hire the factors"?
a) the main buyers of factor services  b) producing commodities and selling them to households, firms, government and other countries  c) private and government enterprises, aiming to maximise profit
**Answer: a) the main buyers of factor services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Firms produce and sell"?
a) the main buyers of factor services  b) private and government enterprises, aiming to maximise profit  c) producing commodities and selling them to households, firms, government and other countries
**Answer: c) producing commodities and selling them to households, firms, government and other countries**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Private and government firms"?
a) producing commodities and selling them to households, firms, government and other countries  b) private and government enterprises, aiming to maximise profit  c) the main buyers of factor services
**Answer: b) private and government enterprises, aiming to maximise profit**
