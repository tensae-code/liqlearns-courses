# Hands-on — Monopolistic competition and product differentiation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Hold two nearly identical goods. One partner 'differentiates' one of them with a story, a name and packaging from scrap paper; the class votes whether the difference is real or fancied — either way, name the market power it buys.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a row of near-identical products — shoes, soaps, soft drinks — each branded differently — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
