# Monopolistic competition and product differentiation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monopolistic competition has many firms selling products that are distinctive in some way.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Differentiation may be real or fancied — advertising and packaging on an essentially identical product.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under monopolistic competition collusion is virtually impossible, making differentiation the only route to market power.
**Answer: True**
