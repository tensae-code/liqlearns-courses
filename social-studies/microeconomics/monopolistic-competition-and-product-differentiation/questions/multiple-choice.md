# Monopolistic competition and product differentiation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Many firms, distinct products"?
a) many firms, each with a distinct variety over which it holds a small monopoly  b) differentiation real (materials, specification, location) or fancied (advertising, packaging, brand)  c) collusion virtually impossible, so differentiation is the only route to market power
**Answer: a) many firms, each with a distinct variety over which it holds a small monopoly**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Real and fancied differentiation"?
a) many firms, each with a distinct variety over which it holds a small monopoly  b) collusion virtually impossible, so differentiation is the only route to market power  c) differentiation real (materials, specification, location) or fancied (advertising, packaging, brand)
**Answer: c) differentiation real (materials, specification, location) or fancied (advertising, packaging, brand)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Differentiation — the only route to power"?
a) differentiation real (materials, specification, location) or fancied (advertising, packaging, brand)  b) collusion virtually impossible, so differentiation is the only route to market power  c) many firms, each with a distinct variety over which it holds a small monopoly
**Answer: b) collusion virtually impossible, so differentiation is the only route to market power**
