# Monopolistic competition and product differentiation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In monopolistic competition many firms sell products that are _____ in some way — styles, kinds, brands.
**Answer: distinctive**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Advertising and packaging on an identical product create _____ differentiation — spurious, but effective.
**Answer: fancied**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With collusion impossible, _____ is the only route to market power under monopolistic competition.
**Answer: differentiation**
