# Monopolistic competition and product differentiation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Many firms, distinct products" in your own words.
**Answer:** Monopolistic competition has many firms selling products that are distinctive in some way.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Real and fancied differentiation" in your own words.
**Answer:** Differentiation may be real or fancied — advertising and packaging on an essentially identical product.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Differentiation — the only route to power" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under monopolistic competition collusion is virtually impossible, making differentiation the only route to market power.
