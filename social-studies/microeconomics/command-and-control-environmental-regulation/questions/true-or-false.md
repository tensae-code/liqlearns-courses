# Command-and-control environmental regulation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Command-and-control sets a price on pollution and lets firms choose.
**Answer: False — it commands limits and equipment directly; pricing pollution is the market-oriented tool.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first US environmental laws used no emission limits.
**Answer: False — emission limits and required equipment were their core tools.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Command-and-control cuts no pollution at all.
**Answer: False — it does cut pollution; the question is its cost-effectiveness.**
