# Command-and-control environmental regulation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two commands does this regulation give?
**Answer:** Emission limits and required equipment or methods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What form did the first US environmental laws take?
**Answer:** Command-and-control: uniform limits and required technology.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What effectiveness question does the book ask?
**Answer:** Whether the pollution cut is achieved at reasonable cost, given firms' differing cleanup costs.
