# Command-and-control environmental regulation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Command-and-control regulation works by —
a) emission limits and required equipment  b) taxes only  c) voluntary promises
**Answer: a) emission limits and required equipment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first US environmental laws were mainly —
a) command-and-control  b) pure tax schemes  c) unregulated
**Answer: a) command-and-control**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A weakness of uniform limits is that they —
a) ignore that firms' cleanup costs differ  b) never cut pollution  c) cost nothing
**Answer: a) ignore that firms' cleanup costs differ**
