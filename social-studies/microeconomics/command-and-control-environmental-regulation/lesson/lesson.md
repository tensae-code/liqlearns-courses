## Sub lesson 1 — What command-and-control means

**Command-and-control regulation** orders polluters directly: the law **sets emission limits** and **requires specific equipment or methods**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The first US environmental laws

The **first US environmental laws** took this form — uniform limits and required technology applied to polluters across the country.

## Sub lesson 3 — How effective is it?

The book asks **how effective** command-and-control is — it cuts pollution, but the **same limit for all** ignores that firms' cleanup costs differ.
