# Income consumption curve — normal and inferior goods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The income consumption curve joins the consumer's optima as income rises at fixed prices.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Normal goods, such as meat and milk, are consumed more as income rises.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inferior goods, such as public-bus travel in the book's example, are consumed less as income rises.
**Answer: True**
