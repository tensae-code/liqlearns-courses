# Income consumption curve — normal and inferior goods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Joining the optima" in your own words.
**Answer:** The income consumption curve joins the consumer's optima as income rises at fixed prices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Normal goods — meat and milk" in your own words.
**Answer:** Normal goods, such as meat and milk, are consumed more as income rises.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Inferior goods — the bus, with a caution" in your own words.
**Answer:** Inferior goods, such as public-bus travel in the book's example, are consumed less as income rises.
