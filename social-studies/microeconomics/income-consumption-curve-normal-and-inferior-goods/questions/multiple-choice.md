# Income consumption curve — normal and inferior goods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Joining the optima"?
a) the curve joining the tangency optima traced as income rises  b) consumption rises with income — the book's examples are meat and milk  c) consumption falls with income — bus travel; the label can change with the person's income
**Answer: a) the curve joining the tangency optima traced as income rises**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Normal goods — meat and milk"?
a) the curve joining the tangency optima traced as income rises  b) consumption falls with income — bus travel; the label can change with the person's income  c) consumption rises with income — the book's examples are meat and milk
**Answer: c) consumption rises with income — the book's examples are meat and milk**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Inferior goods — the bus, with a caution"?
a) consumption rises with income — the book's examples are meat and milk  b) consumption falls with income — bus travel; the label can change with the person's income  c) the curve joining the tangency optima traced as income rises
**Answer: b) consumption falls with income — bus travel; the label can change with the person's income**
