# Income consumption curve — normal and inferior goods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Joining the optima traced as income rises gives the _____.
**Answer: income consumption curve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Goods whose consumption rises with income, like meat and milk, are called _____ goods.
**Answer: normal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Goods whose consumption falls as income rises, like public-bus travel, are called _____ goods.
**Answer: inferior**
