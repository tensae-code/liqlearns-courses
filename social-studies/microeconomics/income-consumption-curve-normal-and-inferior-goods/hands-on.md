# Hands-on — Income consumption curve — normal and inferior goods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Move a coin to a new tangency point each time a partner calls a higher income. For meat, step the coin toward more; for bus rides, step it toward fewer — and say the two words 'normal' and 'inferior' at the right steps.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — rising budget lines with their tangency points joined, one good's quantity rising, the other's falling — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
