# Demand — schedules, curves and the function — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand means willingness to buy plus ability to pay; either one alone is not demand.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Amina's coffee schedule, quantity demanded falls from 9 kg at Birr 0 to zero at Birr 45.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The law of demand says quantity demanded rises as price falls, other things equal.
**Answer: True**
