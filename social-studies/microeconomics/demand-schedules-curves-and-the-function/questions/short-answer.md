# Demand — schedules, curves and the function — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Demand — willingness plus ability" in your own words.
**Answer:** Demand means willingness to buy plus ability to pay; either one alone is not demand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The demand function and schedule" in your own words.
**Answer:** In Amina's coffee schedule, quantity demanded falls from 9 kg at Birr 0 to zero at Birr 45.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The demand curve and the law" in your own words.
**Answer:** The law of demand says quantity demanded rises as price falls, other things equal.
