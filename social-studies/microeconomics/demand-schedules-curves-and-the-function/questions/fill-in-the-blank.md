# Demand — schedules, curves and the function — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand is willingness to buy plus _____ — wanting without paying power is not demand.
**Answer: ability to pay**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At Birr 0, Amina's schedule shows a quantity demanded of _____ of coffee.
**Answer: 9 kg**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** By the law of demand, as price _____, quantity demanded rises — other things equal.
**Answer: falls**
