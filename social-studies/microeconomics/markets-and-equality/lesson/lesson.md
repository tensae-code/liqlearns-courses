# Markets and equality

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — markets and equality — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The small trader earns little beside the complex owner

The **small weekly-market trader earns little** beside a **shopping-complex owner** — the same market system, very different earnings.

## Sub lesson 2 — Many buyers cannot afford even the cheapest goods

**Many buyers cannot afford even the cheapest goods** — being in the market is not the same as being able to buy.

## Sub lesson 3 — Earnings and access depend on the money you start with

The lesson's point: **market earnings and access depend on the money you start with** — capital decides who earns much and who can buy.
