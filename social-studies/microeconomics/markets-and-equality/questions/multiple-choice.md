# Markets and equality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beside a shopping-complex owner, the small trader earns —
a) little  b) the same  c) the most
**Answer: a) little**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Many buyers cannot afford —
a) even the cheapest goods  b) only luxuries  c) nothing at all is sold
**Answer: a) even the cheapest goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Market earnings and access depend on —
a) the money you start with  b) luck alone  c) the weather
**Answer: a) the money you start with**
