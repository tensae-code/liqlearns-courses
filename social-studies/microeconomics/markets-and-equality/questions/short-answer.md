# Markets and equality — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Compare the small trader's and the complex owner's earnings.
**Answer:** The small weekly-market trader earns little beside the shopping-complex owner.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What shuts many buyers out of the market?
**Answer:** They cannot afford even the cheapest goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What decides market earnings and access?
**Answer:** The money you start with.
