# Markets and equality — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All sellers earn equally in markets.
**Answer: False — the small trader earns little beside the complex owner.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Everyone in a market can afford what is sold.
**Answer: False — many cannot afford even the cheapest goods.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Markets give equal earnings whatever you start with.
**Answer: False — earnings and access depend on starting money.**
