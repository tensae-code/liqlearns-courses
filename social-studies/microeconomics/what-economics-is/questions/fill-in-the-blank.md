# What economics is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word economics comes from the Greek _____, household management.
**Answer: oikonomia**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The definition of economics grew from wealth, to welfare, and settled on _____.
**Answer: scarcity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economics studies the _____ allocation of scarce resources to meet unlimited needs.
**Answer: efficient**
