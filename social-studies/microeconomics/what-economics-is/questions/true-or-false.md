# What economics is — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word economics comes from the Greek oikonomia, which means household management.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The word economics comes from the Greek oikonomia, which means household management.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Where the word comes from); sub lesson 2 says: The definition of economics grew from wealth, to welfare, and settled on scarcity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economics studies the efficient allocation of scarce resources to meet unlimited human needs as fully as possible.
**Answer: True**
