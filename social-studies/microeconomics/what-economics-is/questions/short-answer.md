# What economics is — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Where the word comes from" in your own words.
**Answer:** The word economics comes from the Greek oikonomia, which means household management.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "How the definition grew" in your own words.
**Answer:** The definition of economics grew from wealth, to welfare, and settled on scarcity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The definition this course uses" in your own words.
**Answer:** Economics studies the efficient allocation of scarce resources to meet unlimited human needs as fully as possible.
