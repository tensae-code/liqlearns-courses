# Hands-on — What economics is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay out ten stones and a list of twenty wants on paper. Move the stones to the wants you would meet first, and explain each move with the words scarce, allocate and efficient.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a household weighing limited birr and goods against a long list of wants — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
