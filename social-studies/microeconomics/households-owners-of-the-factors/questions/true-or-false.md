# Households — owners of the factors — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Households own the factors of production: land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Households own the factors of production: land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Households own the factors); sub lesson 2 says: Households receive rent, wages, interest and profit for their factor services.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Households spend most of their income, save part of it, and pay taxes.
**Answer: True**
