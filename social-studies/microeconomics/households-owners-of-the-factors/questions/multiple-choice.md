# Households — owners of the factors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Households own the factors"?
a) owners of land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship  b) rent, wages, interest and profit — one payment per factor  c) spending most income, saving part, paying taxes; aiming to maximise income
**Answer: a) owners of land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Selling services, receiving incomes"?
a) owners of land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship  b) spending most income, saving part, paying taxes; aiming to maximise income  c) rent, wages, interest and profit — one payment per factor
**Answer: c) rent, wages, interest and profit — one payment per factor**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Spending, saving, taxing"?
a) rent, wages, interest and profit — one payment per factor  b) spending most income, saving part, paying taxes; aiming to maximise income  c) owners of land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship
**Answer: b) spending most income, saving part, paying taxes; aiming to maximise income**
