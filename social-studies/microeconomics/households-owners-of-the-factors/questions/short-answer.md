# Households — owners of the factors — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Households own the factors" in your own words.
**Answer:** Households own the factors of production: land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Selling services, receiving incomes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Households receive rent, wages, interest and profit for their factor services.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Spending, saving, taxing" in your own words.
**Answer:** Households spend most of their income, save part of it, and pay taxes.
