# Households — owners of the factors — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The factors of production are owned by _____.
**Answer: households**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Land earns rent, labour earns _____, capital earns interest and entrepreneurship earns profit.
**Answer: wages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Households spend most of their income, _____ part of it, and pay taxes.
**Answer: save**
