# Hands-on — Households — owners of the factors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Use four cups labelled rent, wages, interest, profit. Act out one week: move a stone into a cup each time your household 'receives' that income, and say which factor earned it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a household handing over work and land-use and receiving four labelled income streams — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
