## Sub lesson 1 — Productive efficiency

**Productive efficiency** means goods are produced **at the lowest possible cost** — perfectly competitive firms are driven to it in the long run.

## Sub lesson 2 — Allocative efficiency

**Allocative efficiency** means the **mix of goods matches what society values** — price equals marginal cost, so resources go where they are worth most.

## Sub lesson 3 — The model against the real world

Perfect competition achieves **both efficiencies together** — and the book then **compares the model with real-world markets**, which fall short in named ways.
