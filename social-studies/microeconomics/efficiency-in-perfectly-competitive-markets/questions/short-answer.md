# Efficiency in perfectly competitive markets — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is productive efficiency?
**Answer:** Producing goods at the lowest possible cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is allocative efficiency?
**Answer:** The mix of goods produced matches what society values, with price equal to marginal cost.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the comparison with real markets show?
**Answer:** Real-world markets fall short of the model's twin efficiencies in named ways.
