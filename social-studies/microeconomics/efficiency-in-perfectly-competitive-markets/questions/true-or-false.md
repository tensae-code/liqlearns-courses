# Efficiency in perfectly competitive markets — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Productive efficiency is about charging the highest price.
**Answer: False — it is about producing at the lowest possible cost.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Allocative efficiency ignores what buyers value.
**Answer: False — it means the mix matches what society values, with price equal to marginal cost.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Real-world markets match the model exactly in every way.
**Answer: False — the book compares the model with real markets, which fall short in named ways.**
