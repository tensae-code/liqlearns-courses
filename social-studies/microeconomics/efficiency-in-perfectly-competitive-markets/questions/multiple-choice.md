# Efficiency in perfectly competitive markets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Productive efficiency means producing —
a) at the lowest possible cost  b) the most expensive way  c) only one good
**Answer: a) at the lowest possible cost**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Allocative efficiency holds when —
a) price equals marginal cost  b) price is far above marginal cost  c) firms choose the mix by habit
**Answer: a) price equals marginal cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In perfect competition, productive and allocative efficiency are —
a) achieved together  b) impossible together  c) never achieved
**Answer: a) achieved together**
