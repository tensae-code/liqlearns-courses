# From price changes to the demand curve — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Joining the optima traced as one price falls gives the _____.
**Answer: price consumption curve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each optimum drops a point pairing its price with its chosen _____ of X.
**Answer: quantity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The demand curve derived from the optima slopes _____ — lower prices, larger quantities.
**Answer: downward**
