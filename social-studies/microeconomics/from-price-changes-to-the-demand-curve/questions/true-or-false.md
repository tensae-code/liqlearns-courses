# From price changes to the demand curve — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The price consumption curve joins the consumer's optima as the price of one good changes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each optimum's price and chosen quantity of X form one point of the demand diagram.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The derived demand curve slopes downward because lower prices lead the consumer to choose larger quantities.
**Answer: True**
