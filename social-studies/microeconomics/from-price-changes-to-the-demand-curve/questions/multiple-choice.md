# From price changes to the demand curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The price consumption curve"?
a) joining the optima from successive falls in one price  b) each optimum contributes one price-quantity point to the lower panel  c) the joined points form the downward-sloping demand curve, derived from rankings alone
**Answer: a) joining the optima from successive falls in one price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Dropping the points down"?
a) joining the optima from successive falls in one price  b) the joined points form the downward-sloping demand curve, derived from rankings alone  c) each optimum contributes one price-quantity point to the lower panel
**Answer: c) each optimum contributes one price-quantity point to the lower panel**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The demand curve derived"?
a) each optimum contributes one price-quantity point to the lower panel  b) the joined points form the downward-sloping demand curve, derived from rankings alone  c) joining the optima from successive falls in one price
**Answer: b) the joined points form the downward-sloping demand curve, derived from rankings alone**
