# From price changes to the demand curve — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The price consumption curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The price consumption curve joins the consumer's optima as the price of one good changes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Dropping the points down" in your own words.
**Answer:** Each optimum's price and chosen quantity of X form one point of the demand diagram.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The demand curve derived" in your own words.
**Answer:** The derived demand curve slopes downward because lower prices lead the consumer to choose larger quantities.
