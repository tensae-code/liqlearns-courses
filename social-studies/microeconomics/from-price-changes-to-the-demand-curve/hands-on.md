# Hands-on — From price changes to the demand curve

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pivot a string budget line three times for three falling prices. At each pivot, drop a counter straight down onto a price-quantity grid below, then join the counters — the demand curve appears under your hands.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the two-panel figure — pivoting budget lines above, the demand curve forming point by point below — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
