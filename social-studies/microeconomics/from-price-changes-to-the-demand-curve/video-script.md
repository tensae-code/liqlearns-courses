# Video script — From price changes to the demand curve

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hold income and the other good's price constant, and let the price of X fall step by step. Each fall pivots the budget line outward and produces a new optimum. Joining those successive optima gives the price consumption curve — the path of chosen bundles as one price changes.

Each optimum carries two numbers: the price of X that produced it, and the quantity of X chosen at that price. Plot those pairs in a lower panel — price on the vertical axis, quantity on the horizontal — and each optimum drops one point onto the new diagram, in the order the prices fell.

Join the dropped points and the individual's demand curve stands complete: downward sloping, because each lower price, through the pivoting line and the new tangency, leads the consumer to choose a larger quantity. The ordinal theory has now produced, from rankings alone, the same demand curve the cardinal approach reached through utils.
