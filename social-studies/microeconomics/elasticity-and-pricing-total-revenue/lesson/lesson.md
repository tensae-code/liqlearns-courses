## Sub lesson 1 — Elastic demand: a rise cuts revenue

When demand is **elastic**, a **price rise lowers total revenue** — quantity falls by a bigger percentage than price rises.

## Sub lesson 2 — Inelastic demand: a rise raises revenue

When demand is **inelastic**, a **price rise raises total revenue** — quantity falls only a little, so the higher price wins.

## Sub lesson 3 — Short run against long run

**Elasticity differs between the short run and the long run** — given time, buyers and sellers adjust more, so the **equilibrium after a change differs** by horizon.
