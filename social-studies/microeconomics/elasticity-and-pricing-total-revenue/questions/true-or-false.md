# Elasticity and pricing — total revenue — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elastic demand means quantity barely responds to price.
**Answer: False — elastic demand means quantity responds strongly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inelastic demand means a rise always cuts revenue.
**Answer: False — with inelastic demand a rise raises total revenue.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elasticity is the same in the short and long run.
**Answer: False — elasticity grows as people get time to adjust.**
