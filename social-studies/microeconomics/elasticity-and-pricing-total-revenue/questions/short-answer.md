# Elasticity and pricing — total revenue — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a rise cut revenue under elastic demand?
**Answer:** Quantity demanded falls by a bigger percentage than the price rise.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a rise raise revenue under inelastic demand?
**Answer:** Quantity falls only a little, so the higher price more than makes up for it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the horizon matter for equilibrium?
**Answer:** Short-run and long-run elasticity differ, so the new equilibrium after a change differs by horizon.
