# Elasticity and pricing — total revenue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With elastic demand, a price rise —
a) lowers total revenue  b) raises total revenue  c) leaves revenue unchanged
**Answer: a) lowers total revenue**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With inelastic demand, a price rise —
a) raises total revenue  b) lowers total revenue  c) doubles quantity
**Answer: a) raises total revenue**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elasticity is usually greater in —
a) the long run  b) the short run  c) neither — it never changes
**Answer: a) the long run**
