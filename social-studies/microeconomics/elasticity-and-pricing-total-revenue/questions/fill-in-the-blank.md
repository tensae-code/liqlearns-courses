# Elasticity and pricing — total revenue — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With _____ demand, a price rise lowers total revenue.
**Answer: elastic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With _____ demand, a price rise raises total revenue.
**Answer: inelastic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Given more time, elasticity usually _____.
**Answer: increases (is greater in the long run)**
