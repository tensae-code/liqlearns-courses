# Ranking choices — the ordinal approach — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ordinal approach asks consumers only to _____ bundles — first, second, third.
**Answer: rank**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If A beats B and B beats C, then A beats C — that consistency rule is called _____.
**Answer: transitivity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The approach that ranks bundles without measuring utils is the _____ approach.
**Answer: ordinal**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The MRS is the rate at which a consumer will _____ one commodity for another while total satisfaction stays the same.
**Answer: substitute**
