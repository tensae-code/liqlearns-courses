# Ranking choices — the ordinal approach — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Ranking instead of measuring" in your own words.
**Answer:** The ordinal approach studies choice from the ranking of bundles, with no measurable utils.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Its assumptions" in your own words.
**Answer:** The ordinal assumptions include a diminishing marginal rate of substitution and transitive choices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Cardinal and ordinal side by side" in your own words.
**Answer:** The cardinal approach counts utils while the ordinal approach ranks bundles.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "The marginal rate of substitution, defined (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MRS is the rate at which a consumer substitutes one commodity for another while total satisfaction stays the same.
