# Ranking choices — the ordinal approach — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ordinal approach studies choice from the ranking of bundles, with no measurable utils.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ordinal assumptions include a diminishing marginal rate of substitution and transitive choices.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cardinal approach counts utils while the ordinal approach ranks bundles.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The MRS is the rate at which a consumer substitutes one commodity for another while total satisfaction stays the same.
**Answer: True**
