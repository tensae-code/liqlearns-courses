## Sub lesson 1 — Tying sales and bundling

**Tying sales** force a buyer who wants one product to take another; **bundling** sells products only as a package — both can **extend a firm's power** into a second market.

## Sub lesson 2 — Predatory pricing

**Predatory pricing** — pricing **below cost to drive rivals out**, then raising price once the market is captured — is policed under antitrust law.

## Sub lesson 3 — Judged in a real-world case

The book **judges these practices in a real-world case** — whether a practice is anticompetitive depends on its **actual effects**, not its name.
