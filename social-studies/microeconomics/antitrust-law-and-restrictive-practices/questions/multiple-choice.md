# Antitrust law and restrictive practices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tying sales means —
a) forcing a buyer of one product to take another  b) discounting for students  c) selling abroad
**Answer: a) forcing a buyer of one product to take another**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Predatory pricing is —
a) pricing below cost to drive rivals out  b) any low price  c) a sale at festival time
**Answer: a) pricing below cost to drive rivals out**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Restrictive practices are judged by —
a) their actual effects in a real case  b) their names alone  c) the firm's size alone
**Answer: a) their actual effects in a real case**
