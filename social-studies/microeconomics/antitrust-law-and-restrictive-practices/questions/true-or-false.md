# Antitrust law and restrictive practices — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bundling always harms competition in every case.
**Answer: False — the practices are judged case by case; they can extend market power, which is why they are policed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every low price is predatory pricing.
**Answer: False — predatory pricing means below-cost pricing meant to drive rivals out, then raising price.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A practice is anticompetitive whenever a rival complains.
**Answer: False — it is judged by its actual effects in a real-world case.**
