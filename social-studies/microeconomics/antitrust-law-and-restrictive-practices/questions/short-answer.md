# Antitrust law and restrictive practices — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a tying sale?
**Answer:** Forcing a buyer who wants one product to also take another.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the predator's plan?
**Answer:** Lose money below cost until rivals exit, then raise price in the captured market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book judge restrictive practices?
**Answer:** In a real-world case, by their actual effects on competition.
