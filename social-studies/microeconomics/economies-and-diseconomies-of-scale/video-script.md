# Video script — Economies and diseconomies of scale

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook pairs the two forces on the long-run curve. Economies of scale: as the firm grows, unit cost falls — bigger machines are cheaper per unit of output, bulk buying wins discounts, specialists replace jacks-of-all-trades, and fixed costs spread over more units.

Growth does not pay forever. Diseconomies of scale: past some size, unit cost rises — management layers multiply, decisions slow, communication frays, and coordinating thousands of workers costs more than the scale saves.

Together the two forces bend long-run average cost into its wide U: falling while economies rule, flat near the best size, rising when diseconomies take over.
