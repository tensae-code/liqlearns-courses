# Economies and diseconomies of scale — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As the firm grows and unit cost falls, it enjoys _____ of scale.
**Answer: economies**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Unit cost rising with growth marks _____ of scale.
**Answer: diseconomies**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economies first, diseconomies later: long-run average cost forms a wide _____.
**Answer: U**
