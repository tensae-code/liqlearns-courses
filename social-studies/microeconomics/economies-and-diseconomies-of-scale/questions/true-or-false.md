# Economies and diseconomies of scale — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bulk buying and specialisation are sources of economies of scale.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Past some size, running the firm gets harder faster than scale saves.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Industries differ in typical firm size because their economies and diseconomies bite at different scales.
**Answer: True**
