# Economies and diseconomies of scale — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two sources of economies of scale.
**Answer:** Bigger machines cheaper per unit of output and bulk-buying discounts (also: specialisation, spreading fixed costs).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do diseconomies arise?
**Answer:** Management layers, slow decisions and frayed communication make coordination cost more than scale saves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the bottom of the U mark?
**Answer:** The scale the firm's technology rewards — where unit cost is lowest in the long run.
