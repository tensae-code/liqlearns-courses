# Economies and diseconomies of scale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economies of scale mean that as the firm grows, unit cost —
a) rises
b) falls
c) stays fixed
**Answer: b) falls**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Diseconomies of scale arise mainly from —
a) bulk discounts
b) coordination and management difficulties of very large firms
c) falling demand
**Answer: b) coordination and management difficulties of very large firms**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Long-run average cost is U-shaped because —
a) prices rise then fall
b) economies rule first, then diseconomies
c) taxes change
**Answer: b) economies rule first, then diseconomies**
