# Lesson plan — Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Yemen, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Goods are classified in several ways at once: material goods and services; goods for individual and for collective consumption; consumer goods and production goods; durable and perishable goods; and necessities and luxuries.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on it stands on the Yemen Grade 11 book's own five sortings of goods — one good can carry a label from every sorting at once.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
