Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 2, Lesson 3 — Economic goods — teaches: Goods are classified in several ways at once: material goods and services; goods for individual and for collective consumption; consumer goods and production goods; durable and perishable goods; and necessities and luxuries. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
