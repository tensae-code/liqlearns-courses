# Hands-on — Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pick four things at home. Sort each one all five ways from the lesson — material or service, individual or collective, consumer or production, durable or perishable, necessity or luxury — and say each sorting aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — goods sorted into labelled trays — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
