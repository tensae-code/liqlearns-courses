# Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One good can carry a label from every sorting at once.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Durable goods are used up at once.
**Answer: False — durable goods serve over a long period and are used again and again; perishable goods are consumed quickly.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Luxuries come before necessities when means are short.
**Answer: False — necessities come first; luxuries are done without when means are short.**
