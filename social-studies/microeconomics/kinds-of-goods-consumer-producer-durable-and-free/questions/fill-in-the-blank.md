# Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods used to make other goods are called _____ goods.
**Answer: production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Consumption by the whole community together is _____ consumption.
**Answer: collective**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The goods life requires, which come first in spending, are _____.
**Answer: necessities**
