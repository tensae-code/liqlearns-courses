# Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five sortings of goods in the lesson.
**Answer:** Material goods and services; individual and collective consumption; consumer and production goods; durable and perishable goods; necessities and luxuries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sort a loaf of bread three ways.
**Answer:** Material good; individual consumption (one family eats it); consumer good — and perishable, a necessity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference between a consumer good and a production good?
**Answer:** A consumer good satisfies needs directly; a production good is used to make other goods.
