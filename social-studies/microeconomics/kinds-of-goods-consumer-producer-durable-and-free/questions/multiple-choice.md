# Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A service differs from a material good because —
a) it is always free  b) it is work done for others, not a thing you can hold  c) it lasts longer
**Answer: b) it is work done for others, not a thing you can hold**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A machine used to make other goods is a —
a) consumer good  b) luxury  c) production good
**Answer: c) production good**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Goods that are consumed quickly and must be replaced are —
a) perishable  b) durable  c) collective
**Answer: a) perishable**
