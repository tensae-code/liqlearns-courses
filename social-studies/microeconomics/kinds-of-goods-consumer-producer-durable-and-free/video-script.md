# Video script — Kinds of goods — consumer, producer, durable and perishable

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Goods can be sorted in several ways at once. First: material goods you can touch, and services — work done for you. Goods are also sorted by who consumes them: one person's use, individual consumption, or everyone's together, collective consumption.

Then by purpose: consumer goods meet needs directly, while production goods make other goods. By lasting: durable goods serve again and again, perishable goods are gone quickly. And by urgency: necessities first, luxuries after. One good can carry a label from every sorting at once.
