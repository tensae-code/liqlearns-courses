# Social institutions in economic life — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Property rules say who owns what and who may use, lend or sell it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The family is where income is shared and the next workers are raised.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If institutions change, the same people's economic behaviour can change too.
**Answer: True**
