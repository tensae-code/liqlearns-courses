# Social institutions in economic life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a social institution framing economic life?
a) a market price
b) property
c) a shopping list
**Answer: b) property**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inside a family, decisions about who works and what is bought first are —
a) purely private feelings
b) economic decisions
c) never about money
**Answer: b) economic decisions**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The state frames economic life by —
a) owning every shop
b) writing and enforcing the rules, taxing and spending
c) setting every family's menu
**Answer: b) writing and enforcing the rules, taxing and spending**
