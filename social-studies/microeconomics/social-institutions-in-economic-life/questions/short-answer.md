# Social institutions in economic life — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three institutions the lesson names.
**Answer:** Property, the family and the state.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the family called an economic institution?
**Answer:** Because needs are first met there, income is shared there, and economic decisions — who works, who studies, what is bought first — are taken there.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the state do as an institution?
**Answer:** It writes and enforces the rules (property, contracts, money) and it taxes, spends and provides services.
