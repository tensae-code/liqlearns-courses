# Social institutions in economic life — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The settled rules a society lives by, such as property, family and the state, are its social _____.
**Answer: institutions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The family meets needs, shares _____ and raises the next generation of workers.
**Answer: income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Institutions come first; economic _____ happens inside them.
**Answer: behaviour**
