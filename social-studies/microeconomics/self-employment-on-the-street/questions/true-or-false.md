# Self-employment on the street — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Street vendors work for a fixed employer.
**Answer: False — they organise their own work; they are self-employed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Street vendors never plan what to buy.
**Answer: False — planning purchases is part of their work.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Self-employed street workers have full job security.
**Answer: False — they work with no security.**
