# Self-employment on the street — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bachchu Manjhi is —
a) a self-employed rickshaw puller  b) a salaried manager  c) a factory owner
**Answer: a) a self-employed rickshaw puller**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vendors run —
a) temporary shops  b) permanent malls  c) government offices
**Answer: a) temporary shops**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Street workers save for —
a) family back home  b) a company pension  c) nothing
**Answer: a) family back home**
