# Self-employment on the street — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who organises the rickshaw puller's work?
**Answer:** He does — Bachchu Manjhi is self-employed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do street vendors plan and run?
**Answer:** They plan purchases and run temporary shops.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do street workers save for, and what risk do they carry?
**Answer:** They save for family back home; their daily earnings have no security.
