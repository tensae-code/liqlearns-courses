# Self-employment on the street

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — self-employment on the street — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — They organise their own work

**Bachchu Manjhi the rickshaw puller** and the **street vendors organise their own work** — no employer sets their hours or tasks; they are **self-employed**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning purchases, running temporary shops

Street vendors **plan their purchases** and run **temporary shops** — buying stock each day and setting up where customers pass.

## Sub lesson 3 — Daily earnings, no security, saving for family back home

Their **earnings are daily and carry no security** — a bad day means no income — yet they **save for the family back home**.
