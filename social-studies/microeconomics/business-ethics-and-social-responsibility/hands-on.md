# Hands-on — Business ethics and social responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work a dilemma
Take the shopkeeper's scales from the opening task. Work the dilemma the guide's way: name the choice, name everyone affected, test it against results, duty and character, then decide and defend your decision aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the three circles of this lesson from memory — the choice (theories and dilemmas), the workplace (conduct inside the firm), and society (responsibility outside it). Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
