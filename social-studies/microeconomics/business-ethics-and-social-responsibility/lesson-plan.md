# Lesson plan — Business ethics and social responsibility

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Business ethics and social responsibility
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Albania, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A business is judged not only by its profit but by whether its choices are right: ethical theories and dilemmas test those choices, ethics governs conduct at work and across the organisation, and social responsibility is the business's duty to the society it works in.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own choices — the moments when doing the profitable thing and doing the right thing pull in different directions.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
