# Video script — Business ethics and social responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A business is judged by more than profit. Ethical theories test its choices: by their results, by duty and rules, and by character.

Inside the firm, ethics means honesty, fairness and safety at work, and truthful dealing with customers and suppliers across the whole organisation.

And social responsibility says a business owes the society it works in more than its tax bill — limiting the harm it causes and contributing where it can.
