# Business ethics and social responsibility — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three tests the lesson uses for an ethical choice.
**Answer:** By results (the most good overall), by duty and rules (some things must not be done), and by character (what a good person or business habitually does).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between workplace ethics and organisational ethics?
**Answer:** Workplace ethics is conduct between people at work — honesty, fairness, respect, safety. Organisational ethics is the same demand for the whole business — truthful dealing with customers and suppliers, keeping contracts, leaders setting the standard.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the lesson say a business owes society more than its tax bill?
**Answer:** Because it uses society's people, roads, law and environment; so it must limit the harm it causes and contribute where it can, not only pay tax.
