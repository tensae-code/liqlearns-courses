# Business ethics and social responsibility — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dilemma is a choice where the ethical theories pull in different directions.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Truthful advertising and honest weights and measures are part of organisational ethics.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A written code of ethics is enough on its own, even if daily rewards point the other way.
**Answer: False** — an organisation gets the ethics it practises at the top and rewards day by day; codes matter only when daily incentives point the same way.
