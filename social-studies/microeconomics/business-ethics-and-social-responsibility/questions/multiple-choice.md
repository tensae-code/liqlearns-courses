# Business ethics and social responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Judging a choice by its results means asking —
a) whether it follows the oldest rule  b) which choice does the most good overall  c) what the neighbours wear
**Answer: b) which choice does the most good overall**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Workplace ethics includes —
a) honesty in records, fairness in hiring and pay, and safety for workers  b) the fastest machine  c) the cheapest advert
**Answer: a) honesty in records, fairness in hiring and pay, and safety for workers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Social responsibility says a business —
a) owes society nothing beyond profit  b) owes society more than its tax bill — limiting harm and contributing where it can  c) must give away all its profit
**Answer: b) owes society more than its tax bill — limiting harm and contributing where it can**
