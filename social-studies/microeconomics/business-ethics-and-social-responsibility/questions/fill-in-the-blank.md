# Business ethics and social responsibility — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A choice where profit and duty pull apart is called a _____.
**Answer: dilemma**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Judging by _____ means asking what a good person, and a good business, habitually does.
**Answer: character**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A business's duty to the society it works in is its social _____.
**Answer: responsibility**
