# Average and marginal product — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Average product is output per worker when labour is the variable input.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hiring decisions are made on the marginal product, not the average.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The marginal curve pulls the average curve behind it.
**Answer: True**
