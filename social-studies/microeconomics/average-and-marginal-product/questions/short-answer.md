# Average and marginal product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is average product computed?
**Answer:** Total product divided by the number of units of the variable input (AP = TP ÷ L).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does marginal product measure?
**Answer:** The extra total product gained by adding one more unit of the variable input, other inputs fixed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the rule joining the two curves.
**Answer:** When marginal product is above average product, average product rises; when marginal is below average, average falls.
