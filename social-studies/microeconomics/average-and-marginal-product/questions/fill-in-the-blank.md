# Average and marginal product — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Output per worker is the _____ product.
**Answer: average**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The extra output of one more worker is the _____ product.
**Answer: marginal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When the marginal worker adds less than the average, the average _____.
**Answer: falls**
