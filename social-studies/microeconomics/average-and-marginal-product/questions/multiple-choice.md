# Average and marginal product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Average product equals —
a) total product times workers
b) total product divided by the variable input
c) marginal product plus total product
**Answer: b) total product divided by the variable input**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marginal product is —
a) output per worker
b) the extra output from one more unit of input
c) total output
**Answer: b) the extra output from one more unit of input**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** While marginal product is above average product, average product —
a) falls
b) rises
c) is zero
**Answer: b) rises**
