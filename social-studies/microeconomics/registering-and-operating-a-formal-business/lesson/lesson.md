# Registering and operating a formal business

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 5, The Procedure for Opening a Firm. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — The charter and the foundation agreement — the firm's own papers

To open a firm, its founders first write the firm's **charter** and a **foundation agreement** — the papers that say what the firm is, who founded it and how it will work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Registration at the khokimiyat, the bank account and the tax inspectorate

The firm is **state-registered at the city or district khokimiyat**. Then it opens a **bank account** and pays in the charter fund **within 30 days**, and registers with the **tax inspectorate**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The seal and the trademark — and drafting your own

The firm gets its **seal** and its **trademark**. The book sets pupils to draft a charter and a logo for their own firm, following the same procedure.
