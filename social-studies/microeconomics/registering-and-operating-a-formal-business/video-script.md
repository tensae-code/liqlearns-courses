# Video script — Registering and operating a formal business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

To open a firm, its founders first write the firm's charter and a foundation agreement — the papers that say what the firm is, who founded it and how it will work.

The firm is state-registered at the city or district khokimiyat.

The firm gets its seal and its trademark.
