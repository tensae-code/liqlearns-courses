# Registering and operating a formal business — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A firm is founded on its _____ and its foundation agreement.
**Answer: charter**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After registration the firm opens a bank account, pays in the _____ fund within 30 days, and registers with the tax inspectorate.
**Answer: charter**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The firm gets its seal and its _____.
**Answer: trademark**
