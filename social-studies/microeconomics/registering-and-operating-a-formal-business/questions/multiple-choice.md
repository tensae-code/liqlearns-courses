# Registering and operating a formal business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A firm's founding papers are —
a) its seal and trademark only  b) its charter and foundation agreement  c) its bank account
**Answer: b) its charter and foundation agreement**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State registration happens —
a) at the tax inspectorate  b) at the city or district khokimiyat  c) at the bank
**Answer: b) at the city or district khokimiyat**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The firm's own marks are —
a) its seal and trademark  b) its tax number only  c) its charter fund
**Answer: a) its seal and trademark**
