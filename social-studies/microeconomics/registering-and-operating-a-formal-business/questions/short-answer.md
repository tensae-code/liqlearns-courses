# Registering and operating a formal business — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two papers found a firm?
**Answer:** Its charter and its foundation agreement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where is a firm state-registered in the book?
**Answer:** At the city or district khokimiyat.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do pupils draft for their own firm?
**Answer:** A charter and a logo.
