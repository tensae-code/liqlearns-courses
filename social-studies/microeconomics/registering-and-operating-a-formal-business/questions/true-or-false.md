# Registering and operating a formal business — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The charter and foundation agreement come before registration.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The charter fund must be paid into the bank account within 30 days.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pupils draft a charter and a logo for their own firm.
**Answer: True**
