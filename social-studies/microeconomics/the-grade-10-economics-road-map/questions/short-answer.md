# The Grade 10 economics road map — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Units 1–3 — choice, markets, production" in your own words.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Units 4–6 — structures, finance, growth" in your own words.
