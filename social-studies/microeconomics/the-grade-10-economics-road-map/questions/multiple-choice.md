# The Grade 10 economics road map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Units 1–3 — choice, markets, production"?
a) consumer behaviour, then demand and supply, then production and cost  b) market structures, then banking and finance, then economic growth  c) the Ethiopian economy, then business startups and innovation
**Answer: a) consumer behaviour, then demand and supply, then production and cost**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Units 4–6 — structures, finance, growth"?
a) consumer behaviour, then demand and supply, then production and cost  b) the Ethiopian economy, then business startups and innovation  c) market structures, then banking and finance, then economic growth
**Answer: c) market structures, then banking and finance, then economic growth**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Units 7–8 — Ethiopia, then enterprise"?
a) market structures, then banking and finance, then economic growth  b) the Ethiopian economy, then business startups and innovation  c) consumer behaviour, then demand and supply, then production and cost
**Answer: b) the Ethiopian economy, then business startups and innovation**
