# The Grade 10 economics road map

## Do it first

Look at the eight unit titles in order. Which three do you think are about one buyer or one firm, and which are about the whole country? Say your answers first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Units 1–3 — choice, markets, production

The course opens with theory. Unit 1 studies consumer behaviour — how a buyer gets the most satisfaction from a limited income. Unit 2 builds the theories of demand and supply. Unit 3 turns to the producer: production and cost. Each unit deepens a Grade 9 foundation into measured, calculated theory.

## Sub lesson 2 — Units 4–6 — structures, finance, growth

Unit 4 compares market structures, from perfect competition to monopoly. Unit 5 follows money: banking and finance, from the Bank of Abyssinia to mobile banking. Unit 6 steps back to the whole economy and studies economic growth — what makes output and living standards rise.

## Sub lesson 3 — Units 7–8 — Ethiopia, then enterprise

Two whole units are Ethiopia itself and enterprise. Unit 7 measures the Ethiopian economy — its GDP, agriculture, industry and services. Unit 8 closes the course with business startups and innovation: how new firms begin, and how new ideas change an economy.
