# Hands-on — The Grade 11 economics road map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
On a long strip of paper, lay out one card per unit, in order. Walk along the strip and say at each card what that unit is about, tying each one back to Ethiopia as the book asks.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a seven-unit road map from how a consumer chooses to Ethiopia's own sectors and strategies — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
