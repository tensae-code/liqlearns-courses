# Video script — The Grade 11 economics road map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome. This course has seven stops. First, how a consumer chooses. Second, how firms decide in each kind of market. Third, counting the nation's income. Fourth, consumption, saving and investment. Fifth, trade and finance. Sixth, economic development. Seventh, Ethiopia's own sectors and strategies.

Every unit works the same way: it starts from what you know, defines its words, works an example in birr, summarises, and closes with review questions. Ethiopia runs throughout — the National Bank, Iqub, ADLI and the Growth and Transformation Plans.
