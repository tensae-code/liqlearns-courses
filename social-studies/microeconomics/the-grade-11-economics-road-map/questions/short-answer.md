# The Grade 11 economics road map — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Units 1–2 — the consumer, then the firm" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unit 1 Theory of Consumer Behavior and Demand reviews cardinal utility, then builds the full ordinal account: indifference curves, the budget line, the optimum, income and price consumption curves, and demand derived.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Units 3–4 — the national accounts, then spending and saving" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unit 3 National Income Accounting measures GDP and GNP three ways, builds the deflator and the CPI, and climbs the income ladder down to disposable income.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Units 5–7 — trade, development, and Ethiopia itself" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unit 5 Trade and Finance moves from domestic trade through mercantilism, Smith and Ricardo to the balance of payments, trade policy, exchange rates, regional integration and globalization in the Ethiopian context — with an Ethiopia–China coffee-and-cloth worked example.
