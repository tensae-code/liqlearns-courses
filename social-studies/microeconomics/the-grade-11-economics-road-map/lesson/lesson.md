# The Grade 11 economics road map

## Do it first

Look at the unit titles in order. Which do you think are about one buyer or one firm, and which are about the whole country? Say your answers first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Units 1–2 — the consumer, then the firm

Unit 1 Theory of Consumer Behavior and Demand reviews cardinal utility, then builds the full ordinal account: indifference curves, the budget line, the optimum, income and price consumption curves, and demand derived. Unit 2 Market Structure and the Decision of Firms takes perfect competition, monopoly with price discrimination, monopolistic competition and oligopoly through demand, revenue and cost curves to a short-run equilibrium. The worked budgets are in birr — a 100-birr bread-and-sugar budget, Bereket's 60 birr of pocket money, and Fenet's cups of tea.

## Sub lesson 2 — Units 3–4 — the national accounts, then spending and saving

Unit 3 National Income Accounting measures GDP and GNP three ways, builds the deflator and the CPI, and climbs the income ladder down to disposable income. Unit 4 Consumption, Saving and Investment works the Keynesian propensities, studies Iqub — Ethiopia's rotating savings — as its case study, and takes investment types to the accelerator.

## Sub lesson 3 — Units 5–7 — trade, development, and Ethiopia itself

Unit 5 Trade and Finance moves from domestic trade through mercantilism, Smith and Ricardo to the balance of payments, trade policy, exchange rates, regional integration and globalization in the Ethiopian context — with an Ethiopia–China coffee-and-cloth worked example. Unit 6 Economic Development separates growth from development, builds the HDI step by step (Ethiopia's 2019 score 0.485, 173rd of 189), and follows Sen, the MDGs, the SDGs and Agenda 2063. Unit 7 Main Sectors, Sectoral Policies and Strategies of Ethiopia marches agriculture, industry and services through the Imperial, Derg and post-1991 regimes. Every unit follows the same rhythm: introduction, Key Concepts, Learning Outcomes, sections opening with their own outcomes and a Start-up Activity that recalls Grade 10 or asks about the learner's own locality and family, interleaved Activities, Note boxes pinning definitions, worked examples in birr, a Unit Summary, and Review Questions in fixed parts.

## Say it to your companion
