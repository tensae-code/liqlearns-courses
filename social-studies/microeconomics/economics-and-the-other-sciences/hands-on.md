# Hands-on — Economics and the other sciences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with one question
Take one real question — 'should the town build a market?' Say out loud what each of the five sciences in the lesson would ask about it: sociology, politics, history, statistics and mathematics.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — economics in the middle of a circle, joined by lines to sociology, politics, history, statistics and mathematics — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
