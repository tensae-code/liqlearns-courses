**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Yemen).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 1, Lesson 3 — Economics' relation to the other sciences — teaches: Economics does not stand alone: it borrows from and feeds sociology, politics, history, statistics and mathematics — each science giving economics something it needs, and receiving something back. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
