# Video script — Economics and the other sciences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economics studies people producing, exchanging and consuming — so it cannot stand alone. From sociology it takes the study of society and its customs. From politics, the study of power and the state, which taxes, spends and makes the laws markets live under.

From history it takes the record of how economies actually lived and changed. And from statistics and mathematics it takes its tools for counting and measuring — prices, quantities and incomes turned into numbers that can be compared. Each neighbour also learns from economics in return.
