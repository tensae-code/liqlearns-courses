# Economics and the other sciences — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The science of society that economics borrows from is _____.
**Answer: sociology**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ gives economics its memory — the record of how economies lived and changed.
**Answer: History**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Collecting, ordering and comparing numbers is the method of _____.
**Answer: statistics**
