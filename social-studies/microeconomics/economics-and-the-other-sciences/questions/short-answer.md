# Economics and the other sciences — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five sciences the lesson joins to economics.
**Answer:** Sociology, politics, history, statistics and mathematics.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does economics need politics?
**Answer:** Because the state taxes, spends, coins money and sets the laws inside which every market operates.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does economics take from statistics and mathematics?
**Answer:** From statistics, the method of collecting and comparing economic numbers; from mathematics, the exact language of schedules, curves and formulas.
