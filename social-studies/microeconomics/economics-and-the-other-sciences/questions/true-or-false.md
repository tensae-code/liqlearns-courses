# Economics and the other sciences — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economics stands completely alone, borrowing nothing from other sciences.
**Answer: False — it borrows from and feeds sociology, politics, history, statistics and mathematics.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Governments tax, spend and make the laws markets live under — which is why economics works with politics.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Statistics helps economics collect and compare numbers such as prices and incomes.
**Answer: True**
