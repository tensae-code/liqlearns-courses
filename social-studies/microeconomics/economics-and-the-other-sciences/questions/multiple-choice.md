# Economics and the other sciences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economics borrows the study of society and customs from —
a) sociology  b) mathematics  c) history
**Answer: a) sociology**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The record of how economies lived and changed comes from —
a) politics  b) statistics  c) history
**Answer: c) history**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Schedules, curves and formulas are economics' borrowing from —
a) sociology  b) mathematics  c) politics
**Answer: b) mathematics**
