Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.

Putting it together: the types of human resource management theory and how they are applied to the current production and distribution of goods and services — taught in Unit 1, Topic 4: Human resource management theories and application (Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG, printed p. 46).
