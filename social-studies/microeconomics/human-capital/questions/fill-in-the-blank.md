# Human capital — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Skills and education embodied in workers form _____ capital.
**Answer: human**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Families, firms and states all invest in the same stock: _____ inside people.
**Answer: capability**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The same machines produce more in _____ hands.
**Answer: skilled**
