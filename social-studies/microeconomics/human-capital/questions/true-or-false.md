# Human capital — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital is built by investment, like other capital.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** States invest in human capital through public education.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Skilled workers adopt new methods faster than unskilled ones.
**Answer: True**
