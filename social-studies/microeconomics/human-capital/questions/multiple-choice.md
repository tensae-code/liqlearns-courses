# Human capital — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human capital is —
a) machines owned by workers
b) skills and education embodied in workers
c) wages paid to workers
**Answer: b) skills and education embodied in workers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Studying's costs include —
a) only fees
b) fees, training time and earnings given up
c) nothing
**Answer: b) fees, training time and earnings given up**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Labour as a factor is best measured as —
a) a count of hands only
b) hands multiplied by what their owners know
c) hours alone
**Answer: b) hands multiplied by what their owners know**
