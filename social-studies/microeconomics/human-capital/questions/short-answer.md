# Human capital — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is human capital?
**Answer:** The skills, knowledge and education embodied in workers, built up by schooling, training and practice.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three investors in human capital.
**Answer:** The person/family, firms (training) and the state (public education).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does human capital raise an economy's output?
**Answer:** Skilled workers produce more with the same equipment and learn new methods faster.
