# Utility and consumer choice — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Utility is the _____ a consumer gets from consuming a good.
**Answer: satisfaction**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cardinal approach measures utility in numbers called _____.
**Answer: utils**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cardinal approach assumes the consumer is _____ — aiming at the most satisfaction their income allows.
**Answer: rational**
