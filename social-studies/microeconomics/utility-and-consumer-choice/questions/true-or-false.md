# Utility and consumer choice — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Utility is the satisfaction a consumer gets from a good, and it varies by person, place and time.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cardinal approach measures utility in numbers called utils.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cardinal assumptions let the consumer's satisfaction be added, compared and maximised with arithmetic.
**Answer: True**
