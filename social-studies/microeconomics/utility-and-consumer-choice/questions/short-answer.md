# Utility and consumer choice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Utility — satisfaction, and it is relative" in your own words.
**Answer:** Utility is the satisfaction a consumer gets from a good, and it varies by person, place and time.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The cardinal approach and its assumptions" in your own words.
**Answer:** The cardinal approach measures utility in numbers called utils.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the assumptions matter" in your own words.
**Answer:** The cardinal assumptions let the consumer's satisfaction be added, compared and maximised with arithmetic.
