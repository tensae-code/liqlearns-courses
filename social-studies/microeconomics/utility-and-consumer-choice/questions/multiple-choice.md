# Utility and consumer choice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Utility — satisfaction, and it is relative"?
a) the satisfaction from a good — relative to person, place and time  b) utility measured in utils, under five stated assumptions  c) assumptions that let satisfaction be added, compared and maximised
**Answer: a) the satisfaction from a good — relative to person, place and time**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The cardinal approach and its assumptions"?
a) the satisfaction from a good — relative to person, place and time  b) assumptions that let satisfaction be added, compared and maximised  c) utility measured in utils, under five stated assumptions
**Answer: c) utility measured in utils, under five stated assumptions**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the assumptions matter"?
