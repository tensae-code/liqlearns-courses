# Hands-on — Utility and consumer choice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Show a partner the same bottle of water twice: first as if after a long walk in the sun, then as if after drinking two bottles already. Act out how the satisfaction changes, and name what changed — person, place or time.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the same cup of coffee giving different satisfaction to different people — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
