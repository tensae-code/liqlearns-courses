# Advantages and disadvantages of economic systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Arguing the advantages and disadvantages of the different"?
**Answer:** Arguing the advantages and disadvantages of the different economic systems against each other.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Putting it together: arguing the advantages and disadvantages"?
**Answer:** Putting it together: arguing the advantages and disadvantages of the different economic systems against each other — taught in Unit 3, Topic 3: Advantages and disadvantages of different economic systems (Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG, printed p. 69).
