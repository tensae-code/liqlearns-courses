# The putting-out system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In putting-out, the merchant supplies —
a) yarn, and takes back finished cloth  b) looms only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) yarn, and takes back finished cloth**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Weavers depend on the merchant for —
a) materials and sales  b) only water  c) nothing
**Answer: a) materials and sales**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Weavers are paid —
a) very little, despite owning costly looms  b) the most in the chain  c) in gold
**Answer: a) very little, despite owning costly looms**
