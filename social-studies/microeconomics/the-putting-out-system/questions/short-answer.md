# The putting-out system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the merchant supply and take back?
**Answer:** He supplies yarn and takes back finished cloth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do weavers depend on the merchant for?
**Answer:** Materials and sales.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is unfair in the weavers' position?
**Answer:** They own costly looms yet are paid very little.
