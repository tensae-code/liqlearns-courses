# The putting-out system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The merchant supplies _____ to the weavers.
**Answer: yarn**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Weavers depend on him for materials and _____.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Weavers own costly _____ yet are paid little.
