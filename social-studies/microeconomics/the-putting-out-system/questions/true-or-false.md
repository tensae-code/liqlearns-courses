# The putting-out system — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weavers sell their cloth freely to any buyer.
**Answer: False — the merchant takes back the finished cloth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Weavers are independent of the merchant.
**Answer: False — they depend on him for materials and sales.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Owning looms makes weavers the best paid.
**Answer: False — they are paid very little despite the looms.**
