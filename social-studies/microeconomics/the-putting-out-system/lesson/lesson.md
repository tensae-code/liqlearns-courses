# The putting-out system

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — the putting-out system — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The merchant supplies yarn, takes back finished cloth

In the **putting-out system** at **Erode's cloth market**, the **merchant supplies yarn** to home-based weavers and **takes back the finished cloth**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Weavers depend on him for materials and sales

The weavers **depend on the merchant for materials and sales** — without him they cannot get yarn or reach a market.

## Sub lesson 3 — Paid very little despite owning costly looms

Weavers are **paid very little**, **despite owning costly looms** — owning the tool does not give them power in the system.
