# Video script — The law of diminishing returns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The law of diminishing (non-proportional) returns, in Greece's Grade 12 textbook: as more and more units of a variable input are added to fixed inputs, the extra output from each added unit eventually falls.

The cause is proportion. Workers multiply while the machines, rooms and land do not: each added worker has less of the fixed input to work with, waits more, crowds more, and so adds less.

The law is a short-run law: it rules wherever at least one input is fixed. It explains why output cannot be raised without limit by piling one input onto a fixed base — in the farm, the workshop and the kitchen alike.
