# The law of diminishing returns — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The law says marginal product falls from the very first added worker.
**Answer: False — It falls eventually — early additions may raise marginal product first.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Diminishing returns are a fault of the input mix, not of the workers.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Piling one input onto a fixed base cannot raise output without limit.
**Answer: True**
