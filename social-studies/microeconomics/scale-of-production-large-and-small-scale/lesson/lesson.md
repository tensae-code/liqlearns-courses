## Sub lesson 1 — What scale of production means

The scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.

## Sub lesson 2 — Small-scale production

Small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.

## Sub lesson 3 — Large-scale production and what decides the scale

Large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach.
