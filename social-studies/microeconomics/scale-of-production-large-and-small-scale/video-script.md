Scale of production — large and small scale — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: the scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.; small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.; large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach..
