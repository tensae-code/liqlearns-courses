# Scale of production — large and small scale — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the scale of production?
**Answer:** The scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does small-scale production use?
**Answer:** Small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What decides the scale of production a firm can reach?
**Answer:** Large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach.
