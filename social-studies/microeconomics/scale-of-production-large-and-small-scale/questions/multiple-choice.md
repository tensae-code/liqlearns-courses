# Scale of production — large and small scale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About what scale of production means — the lesson teaches that —
a) the scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.  b) small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.  c) large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach.
**Answer: a) the scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About small-scale production — the lesson teaches that —
a) the scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.  b) small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.  c) large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach.
**Answer: b) small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About large-scale production and what decides the scale — the lesson teaches that —
a) the scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.  b) small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.  c) large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach.
**Answer: c) large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach.**
