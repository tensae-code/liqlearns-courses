# Scale of production — large and small scale — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Scale of production means the price a firm charges.
**Answer: False** — The scale of production is the size at which a firm produces — small scale or large scale.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Small-scale production needs the largest amounts of capital.
**Answer: False** — Small-scale production uses little capital and simple methods, serving a small or local market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nothing decides the scale a firm can reach.
**Answer: False** — Large-scale production produces in great quantity with much capital, and the size of the market, the capital available and the nature of the product decide the scale a firm can reach.
