# Scale of production — large and small scale — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The _____ of production is the size at which a firm produces.
**Answer: scale**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Small-scale production uses little _____ and simple methods.
**Answer: capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The size of the market and the capital available help decide the _____ a firm can reach.
**Answer: scale**
