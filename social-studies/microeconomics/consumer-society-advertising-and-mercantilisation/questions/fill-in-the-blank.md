# Consumer society advertising and mercantilisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is arranged (size, colours, display, music, people) to make you crave a product and run to buy it, supposedly to be happy;
**Answer: Advertising**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** And _____ — today almost everything can be bought: services, products, lifestyles, fashion
**Answer: mercantilisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Even values — with rural communities asked how rising consumption of _____ products affects agricultural production
