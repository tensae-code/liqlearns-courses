# Video script — How to produce? Labour-intensive or capital-intensive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How to produce is also called the problem of choice of technique: once what is decided, the method must be chosen. The same output can usually be made by more than one combination of factors.

A labour-intensive technique uses more labour relative to capital per unit of output — wheat harvested with hand tools. A capital-intensive technique uses more capital relative to labour — the same wheat harvested with modern machinery.

The right choice depends on which factors are plentiful and on their relative prices, so that limited resources yield the most output. Where labour is plentiful and cheap, hand methods can be the efficient answer, not a backward one.
