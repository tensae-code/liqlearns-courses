# How to produce? Labour-intensive or capital-intensive — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The problem of technique" in your own words.
**Answer:** How to produce is the problem of choice of technique — choosing the method after deciding what to produce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The two techniques" in your own words.
**Answer:** Labour-intensive technique uses more labour relative to capital per unit of output; capital-intensive uses more capital relative to labour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Choosing rightly" in your own words.
**Answer:** The right technique depends on which factors are plentiful and their relative prices, so limited resources yield the most output.
