# How to produce? Labour-intensive or capital-intensive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The problem of technique"?
a) the problem of choice of technique, decided after what to produce  b) labour-intensive: more labour per unit; capital-intensive: more capital per unit  c) the choice depends on plentiful factors and their relative prices
**Answer: a) the problem of choice of technique, decided after what to produce**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The two techniques"?
a) the problem of choice of technique, decided after what to produce  b) the choice depends on plentiful factors and their relative prices  c) labour-intensive: more labour per unit; capital-intensive: more capital per unit
**Answer: c) labour-intensive: more labour per unit; capital-intensive: more capital per unit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Choosing rightly"?
a) labour-intensive: more labour per unit; capital-intensive: more capital per unit  b) the choice depends on plentiful factors and their relative prices  c) the problem of choice of technique, decided after what to produce
**Answer: b) the choice depends on plentiful factors and their relative prices**
