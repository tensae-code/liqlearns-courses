# How to produce? Labour-intensive or capital-intensive — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How to produce is the problem of choice of _____.
**Answer: technique**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A labour-intensive technique uses more _____ relative to capital per unit of output.
**Answer: labour**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The right technique depends on which factors are plentiful and their relative _____.
**Answer: prices**
