# How to produce? Labour-intensive or capital-intensive — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How to produce is the problem of choice of technique — choosing the method after deciding what to produce.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How to produce is the problem of choice of technique — choosing the method after deciding what to produce.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The problem of technique); sub lesson 2 says: Labour-intensive technique uses more labour relative to capital per unit of output; capital-intensive uses more capital relative to labour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The right technique depends on which factors are plentiful and their relative prices, so limited resources yield the most output.
**Answer: True**
