# Hands-on — How to produce? Labour-intensive or capital-intensive

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Time a task both ways: carry ten cups of water by hand (labour-intensive) versus with a borrowed wheelbarrow (capital-intensive). Record people, tools and time for each.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the same wheat field worked two ways — many hands with simple tools, and one tractor — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
