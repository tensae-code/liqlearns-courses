# Public choice — rational ignorance and voter turnout — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** US presidential turnout is about —
a) 55–65%  b) 100%  c) 5–10%
**Answer: a) 55–65%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rational ignorance means —
a) staying uninformed because one vote rarely decides, so learning costs more than it returns  b) voters are foolish  c) no one may study issues
**Answer: a) staying uninformed because one vote rarely decides, so learning costs more than it returns**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Turnout varies because —
a) people weigh the cost of voting against its expected benefit  b) elections are secret  c) weather alone decides
**Answer: a) people weigh the cost of voting against its expected benefit**
