# Public choice — rational ignorance and voter turnout — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Voting costs the voter nothing at all.
**Answer: False — voting costs time to register, learn and turn out.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every voter studies every issue in full.
**Answer: False — rational ignorance is the calculated result of one vote rarely deciding.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Turnout is always 100% in presidential elections.
**Answer: False — US presidential turnout is about 55–65%.**
