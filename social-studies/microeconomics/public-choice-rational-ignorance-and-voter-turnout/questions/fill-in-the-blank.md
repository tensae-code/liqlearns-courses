# Public choice — rational ignorance and voter turnout — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** US presidential turnout runs at about _____%.
**Answer: 55–65**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Staying uninformed because one vote rarely decides is rational _____.
**Answer: ignorance**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** People vote when the expected _____ beats the cost.
**Answer: benefit**
