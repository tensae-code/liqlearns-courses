# Public choice — rational ignorance and voter turnout — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does voting cost the voter?
**Answer:** Time — to register, to learn about the choices, and to turn out.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is ignorance 'rational' here?
**Answer:** One vote almost never decides, so the cost of becoming informed exceeds its expected benefit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does this lesson explain turnout?
**Answer:** As an economic choice: people turn out when the expected benefit beats the cost.
