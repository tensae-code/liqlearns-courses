## Sub lesson 1 — Voter participation and its costs

**Voting has costs** — time to register, learn and turn out — and **US presidential turnout runs at about 55–65%** of eligible voters.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rational ignorance

It is **rational to stay ignorant** about most issues: one vote almost never decides, so the **cost of becoming informed exceeds the expected benefit** — **rational ignorance**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Turnout as an economic choice

Participation itself is an **economic choice** — people turn out when the **expected benefit beats the cost**, which is why turnout **varies** rather than reaching everyone.
