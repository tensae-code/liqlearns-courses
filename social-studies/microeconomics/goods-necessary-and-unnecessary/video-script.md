# Video script — Goods — necessary and unnecessary

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everything made that people need and consume is called goods. But not all goods are the same. Some goods are necessary: basic foods and clothing — the things a person cannot do without. Other goods are unnecessary: luxury cars and jewellery — nice to have, but a person can live without them.

When you sort goods this way, you know what must come first. Necessary goods first — the food and the clothing. Unnecessary goods after, only if they are still wanted and can be had.
