# Hands-on — Goods — necessary and unnecessary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Find five things at home that were made for people to use. Hold each one and say out loud: necessary good, or unnecessary good — and why.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two shelves from memory — one labelled "necessary goods" and one labelled "unnecessary goods" — and put the book's examples on the right shelves. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every item you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
