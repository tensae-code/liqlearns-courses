# Goods — necessary and unnecessary — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are goods?
**Answer:** Goods are everything made that people need and consume.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes a good necessary?
**Answer:** A good is necessary when a person cannot do without it — like basic foods and clothing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do necessary goods come before unnecessary goods?
**Answer:** Because life itself is at risk if necessary goods are missing, while unnecessary goods — like luxury cars and jewellery — can be done without and come after.
