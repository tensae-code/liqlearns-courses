# Goods — necessary and unnecessary — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A shirt and a car are both goods, because both are made and consumed by people.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Clothing is an unnecessary good.
**Answer: False** — clothing is one of the book's examples of a necessary good; a person cannot do without it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Jewellery is a necessary good because people want it very much.
**Answer: False** — jewellery is an unnecessary good; wanting a thing does not make it necessary, because a person can do without it.
