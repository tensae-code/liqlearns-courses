# Goods — necessary and unnecessary — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everything made that people need and consume is called _____.
**Answer: goods (سلع)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Basic foods and _____ are the book's examples of necessary goods.
**Answer: clothing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Luxury cars and _____ are the book's examples of unnecessary goods.
**Answer: jewellery**
