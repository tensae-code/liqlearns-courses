# Goods — necessary and unnecessary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods are —
a) everything made that people need and consume  b) only things grown on farms  c) only money
**Answer: a) everything made that people need and consume**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a necessary good?
a) jewellery  b) basic food  c) a luxury car
**Answer: b) basic food**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unnecessary goods are goods that —
a) nobody makes  b) a person can do without  c) are free
**Answer: b) a person can do without**
