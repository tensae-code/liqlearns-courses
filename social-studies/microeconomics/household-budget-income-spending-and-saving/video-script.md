# Video script — Household budget — income, spending and saving

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 7 book teaches the household budget, says the narrator: a plan of income against spending. The income comes from wages, savings interest, dividends, business income and pensions. The book works a family table in thousand tögrög — and inside the plan sit saving goals, and loans and debt the family must carry. Plan the money before it is spent.
