# Hands-on — Household budget — income, spending and saving

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the table
On paper, draw a household budget table with two sides — income and spending. On the income side write the five sources from the lesson: wages, savings interest, dividends, business income, pensions. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything missing.

## Say it with things at home
Put five stones on the table, one for each income source, and name what each stone is. Then take one stone away and say which part of the family's plan changes.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
