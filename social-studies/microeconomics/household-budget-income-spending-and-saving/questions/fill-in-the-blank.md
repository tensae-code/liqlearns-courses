# Household budget — income, spending and saving — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A household budget is a plan of _____ against spending.
**Answer: income**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two sources of household income named in the book are wages and _____.
**Answer: pensions**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The budget includes _____ goals — amounts the family means to set aside on purpose.
**Answer: saving**
