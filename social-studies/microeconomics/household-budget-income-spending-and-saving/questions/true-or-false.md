# Household budget — income, spending and saving — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book works the family budget as a table in thousand tögrög.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Household income in the book comes from wages alone.
**Answer: False — the book names wages, savings interest, dividends, business income and pensions.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Loans and debt sit outside the household budget in the book's teaching.
**Answer: False — loans and debt are faced inside the same plan, and repaying them is spending the budget must carry.**
