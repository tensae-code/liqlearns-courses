# Household budget — income, spending and saving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A household budget is —
a) a list of shops  b) a plan of income against spending  c) a record of debts only
**Answer: b) a plan of income against spending**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is named in the book as a source of household income?
a) rainfall  b) homework  c) dividends
**Answer: c) dividends**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Saving goals in Mongolia's budget are —
a) part of the plan, set inside the budget  b) whatever is left by accident  c) never planned
**Answer: a) part of the plan, set inside the budget**
