# Household budget — income, spending and saving — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a household budget, as Mongolia's Grade 7 book teaches it?
**Answer:** A plan of income against spending, worked as a family table in thousand tögrög.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five sources of household income the book lists.
**Answer:** Wages, savings interest, dividends, business income and pensions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things, beyond everyday spending, does the book's budget plan carry?
**Answer:** Saving goals, and loans and debt — repaying borrowed money is spending the plan must carry.
