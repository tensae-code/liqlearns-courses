Division of labour — many hands, each doing one part — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: when Tiwanaku became a city its economy stopped being self-sufficient and became interdependent; the division of labour raised farm productivity, and the surplus freed community labour to build Kalasasaya and Puma Punku without slavery; agriculture and herding organised society around the division and specialisation of labour, and the surplus began trade.
