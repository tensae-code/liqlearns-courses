# Division of labour — many hands, each doing one part — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tiwanaku's city economy stayed completely self-sufficient.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches the opposite — the city economy became interdependent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tiwanaku's great buildings were raised by slave labour.
**Answer: False** — the book says the surplus freed community labour to build them without slavery.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade began before there was any surplus to exchange.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches that the surplus produced was commercialised, beginning trade.
