# Division of labour — many hands, each doing one part — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about tiwanaku stops being self-sufficient?
**Answer:** When Tiwanaku became a city its economy stopped being self-sufficient and became interdependent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about specialised occupations and the surplus?
**Answer:** The division of labour raised farm productivity, and the surplus freed community labour to build Kalasasaya and Puma Punku without slavery.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the agricultural revolution — trades, surplus and first trade?
**Answer:** Agriculture and herding organised society around the division and specialisation of labour, and the surplus began trade.
