# Division of labour — many hands, each doing one part — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When Tiwanaku became a city, its economy stopped being self-sufficient and became _____.
**Answer: interdependent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The division of labour raised farm productivity so much that a _____ of production fed the population.
**Answer: third**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The surplus produced was commercialised, beginning _____ — first on exchange, later on buying and selling.
**Answer: trade**
