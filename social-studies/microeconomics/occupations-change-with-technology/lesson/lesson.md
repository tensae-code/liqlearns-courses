## Sub lesson 1 — The first occupations were fishing hunting and gathering

The first occupations were fishing, hunting and gathering; agriculture brought pottery, basketry and weaving;

## Sub lesson 2 — Science technology and new inventions change how trades

Science, technology and new inventions change how trades are done (hand sewing to machine, horse to truck) and create new sectors, markets and professions (mobile-minute sellers, racing drivers, pilots) that did not exist in grandparents' time
