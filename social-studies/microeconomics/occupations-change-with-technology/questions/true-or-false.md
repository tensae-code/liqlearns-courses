# Occupations change with technology — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first occupations were fishing, hunting and gathering;
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about the first occupations were fishing hunting and gathering.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about science technology and new inventions change how trades; the first occupations were fishing hunting and gathering is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Science, technology and new inventions change how trades are done (hand sewing to machine, horse to truck) and create new sectors, markets and professions (mobile-minute sellers, racing drivers, pilots) that did not exist in grandparents' time
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
