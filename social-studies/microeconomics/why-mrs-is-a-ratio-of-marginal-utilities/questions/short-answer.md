# Why MRS is a ratio of marginal utilities — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Moving along the curve — gains cancel losses" in your own words.
**Answer:** Moving along one indifference curve, the total change in utility is zero.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Solving the balance" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MRS equals the marginal utility of X divided by the marginal utility of Y.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "What the ratio explains" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MRS falls along the curve because MU of X falls while MU of Y rises.
