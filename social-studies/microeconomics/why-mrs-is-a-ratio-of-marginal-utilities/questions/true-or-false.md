# Why MRS is a ratio of marginal utilities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Moving along one indifference curve, the total change in utility is zero.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The MRS equals the marginal utility of X divided by the marginal utility of Y.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The MRS falls along the curve because MU of X falls while MU of Y rises.
**Answer: True**
