# Why MRS is a ratio of marginal utilities — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Moving along one indifference curve, the total change in utility is _____ — gains cancel losses.
**Answer: zero**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Solving the zero-change balance shows the MRS is a _____ of the two marginal utilities.
**Answer: ratio**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Along the curve MU of X falls and MU of Y rises, so the MRS _____.
**Answer: falls**
