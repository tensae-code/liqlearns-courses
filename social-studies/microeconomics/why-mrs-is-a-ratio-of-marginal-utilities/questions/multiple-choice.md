# Why MRS is a ratio of marginal utilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Moving along the curve — gains cancel losses"?
a) utility gained from extra X exactly cancels utility lost from less Y  b) MRS = MU of X divided by MU of Y, from setting gain equal to loss  c) MUx falls and MUy rises along the curve, so their ratio — the MRS — falls
**Answer: a) utility gained from extra X exactly cancels utility lost from less Y**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Solving the balance"?
a) utility gained from extra X exactly cancels utility lost from less Y  b) MUx falls and MUy rises along the curve, so their ratio — the MRS — falls  c) MRS = MU of X divided by MU of Y, from setting gain equal to loss
**Answer: c) MRS = MU of X divided by MU of Y, from setting gain equal to loss**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "What the ratio explains"?
a) MRS = MU of X divided by MU of Y, from setting gain equal to loss  b) MUx falls and MUy rises along the curve, so their ratio — the MRS — falls  c) utility gained from extra X exactly cancels utility lost from less Y
**Answer: b) MUx falls and MUy rises along the curve, so their ratio — the MRS — falls**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** If the marginal utility of X is 12 utils and the marginal utility of Y is 4 utils, what is the MRS of X for Y?
a) 3  b) 0.33  c) 48
**Answer: a) 3** — MRS = MUx / MUy = 12 / 4 = 3 units of Y per unit of X.
