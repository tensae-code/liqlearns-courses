# Hands-on — Why MRS is a ratio of marginal utilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Use a balance scale or two equal piles of stones: add stones for 'utility gained' on one side and remove for 'utility lost' on the other, keeping the scale level as you move along an imaginary curve.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a see-saw of utility gained and utility lost balancing at zero — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
