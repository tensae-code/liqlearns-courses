# Family business profit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Profit is what remains after costs are taken from the money coming in.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To work out profit, you add the two totals together.
**Answer: False — You take what went out away from what came in; you do not add them together.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If more goes out than comes in, the business has made a loss.
**Answer: True**
