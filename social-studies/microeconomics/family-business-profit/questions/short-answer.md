# Family business profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In one sentence, what is profit?
**Answer: Profit is the money left over when a family business's costs are taken away from the money it takes in.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A business took in 500,000 riel and spent 380,000 riel. What is its profit and how did you get it?
**Answer: Its profit is 120,000 riel: 500,000 minus 380,000.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things a family can do with a small profit.
**Answer: It can save it, or use it to buy more materials for the business (or share it in the family).**
