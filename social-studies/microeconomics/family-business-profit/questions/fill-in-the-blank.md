# Family business profit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Profit is what remains after _____ are taken from the money coming in.
**Answer: costs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ came in and 380,000 riel went out, the profit is 120,000 riel.
**Answer: 500,000 riel**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If what goes out is bigger than what comes in, the business has made a _____.
**Answer: loss**
