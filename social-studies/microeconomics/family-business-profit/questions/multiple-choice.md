# Family business profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is profit for a family business?
a) all the money from sales, before any costs  b) the money left after costs are taken from money coming in  c) the money the family borrowed to start
**Answer: b) the money left after costs are taken from money coming in**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To work out profit, you —
a) take the total that went out from the total that came in  b) count only the biggest single sale  c) add the two totals together
**Answer: a) take the total that went out from the total that came in**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What has happened if money going out is bigger than money coming in?
a) nothing — the two totals never matter  b) the business has made a double profit  c) the business has made a loss
**Answer: c) the business has made a loss**
