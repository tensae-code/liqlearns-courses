# Video script — Family business profit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

For a family business, profit is the money left over after the business has paid for everything it used. If a family sells woven silk, the money from the sales is what comes in; the thread, the dye and the stall fee are what goes out. Profit is not the whole pile of money from sales — it is only the part that remains once the costs are taken away.

To work profit out, the family first adds up everything that came in over a period — every sale added together. Then it adds up everything that went out in the same period. The second total is taken from the first. If 500,000 riel came in and 380,000 riel went out, the profit is 120,000 riel. The two totals must cover the same period, or the answer means nothing.

If what goes out is bigger than what comes in, there is no profit — the business has made a loss, and the family must find which costs grew or which sales fell. A small profit can be saved, used to buy more materials, or shared in the family. Watching profit period by period tells the family whether the business is healthy or slowly eating its own money.
