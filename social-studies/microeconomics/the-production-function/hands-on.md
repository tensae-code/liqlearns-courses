# Hands-on — The production function

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Set up a paper mill: input cards (grain, workers, machines) go in one side of a drawn mill, flour cards come out. Change only the worker cards and call it the short run; then change all cards and call it the long run.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a mill turning inputs in at one side and flour out at the other, beside one fixed hectare with a growing line of workers — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
