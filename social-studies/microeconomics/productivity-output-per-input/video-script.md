The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

To study an economy, Bulgaria's Grade 10 book groups its indicators three ways: natural — raw materials, land, animals, railway lines, quantities of goods; value — production measured in money; labour — the employed, by sex, age, education and qualification. Two stand out: labour productivity — the labour invested in a definite product per unit of time — and the cost price, the money expenses of producing and selling.
