# Productivity — output per input — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The volume of arable land and the number of animals are —
a) value indicators  b) natural indicators  c) labour indicators
**Answer: b) natural indicators**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Labour productivity is —
a) the number of the employed  b) the quantity of invested labour for the production of a definite product per unit of time  c) the money expenses of production
**Answer: b) the quantity of invested labour for the production of a definite product per unit of time**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cost price expresses —
a) the money expenses for the production and the sale of the production  b) the length of the railway lines  c) the number of animals
**Answer: a) the money expenses for the production and the sale of the production**
