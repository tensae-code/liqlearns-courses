# Productivity — output per input — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one example of each kind of indicator.
**Answer:** Natural: the quantity of raw materials used, the volume of arable land, the number of animals, the length of the railway lines or the quantities of goods. Value: the quantity of produced production and performed work in money units. Labour: the number of the employed, classified by sex, age, education and qualification.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Define labour productivity in the book's words.
**Answer:** The quantity of invested labour for the production of a definite product per unit of time.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the cost price express, and why is it read beside productivity?
**Answer:** The cost price expresses the money expenses for the production and the sale of the production. Read beside productivity — output against the labour invested per unit of time — the two judge production from both sides: how much comes out, and what it costs.
