# Hands-on — Productivity — output per input

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the indicators
Write these on slips: arable land in hectares, production in leva, the employed by education, railway lines in kilometres, animals counted, work performed in money. Sort them into natural, value and labour piles, then check against the lesson.

## Measure a small productivity
Time a simple repeated task at home — folding ten paper bags, copying a page. Work out how much labour went into one product in your time unit, the book's way, and write the figure down.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — labour productivity — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
