# Hands-on — The slave economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tell the story of the slave society out loud: first the split — owners and slaves — then the two roads into slavery, debt and war captivity, then who else stood in that society — free farmers and craftsmen, and the caste form.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the society as three boxes — slave-owners, slaves, free farmers and craftsmen — and write under each box what the book says about it. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and add anything you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
