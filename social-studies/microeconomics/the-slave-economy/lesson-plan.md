# Lesson plan — The slave economy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** The slave economy
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Eritrea's Grade 7 telling, the slave society is the first class society, taught as an economic system: a society split into slave-owners and slaves, where slaves were made by debt and by war captivity, free farmers and craftsmen worked alongside, and the system also took a caste form.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the traditional (primitive communal) economy — the sharing society with no rich or poor — and shows the next rung of Eritrea's systems ladder: what changed when surplus and private property split society into classes.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
