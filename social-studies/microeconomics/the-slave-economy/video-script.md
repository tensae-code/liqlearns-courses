# Video script — The slave economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea's Grade 7 book climbs a ladder of economic systems. The first rung was the communal society, where everything was shared. The next rung is the slave society — the first class society.

For the first time, society splits: slave-owners on one side, slaves on the other. And how did a person become a slave? Two roads, the book says: debt — owing what you could not pay — and war, taken captive in battle. Beside owners and slaves stood free farmers and craftsmen, working for themselves; and in its caste form, the system fixed people's places by birth.

One split, two roads into slavery, and free workers alongside — that is the slave economy.
