# The slave economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slave society is the first _____ society.
**Answer: class**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A person could be made a slave for a _____ he could not pay, or taken captive in _____.
**Answer: debt; war**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ farmers and _____ were free — not owned — and worked for themselves.
**Answer: farmers; craftsmen**
