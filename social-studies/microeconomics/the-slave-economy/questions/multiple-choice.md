# The slave economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eritrea's Grade 7 book calls the slave society —
a) the last class society  b) the first class society  c) a society with no classes
**Answer: b) the first class society**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book, the two roads by which a person became a slave were —
a) debt and war captivity  b) old age and illness  c) trade and travel
**Answer: a) debt and war captivity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Alongside slave-owners and slaves, the society also had —
a) only soldiers  b) free farmers and craftsmen, and the system also took a caste form  c) factory owners
**Answer: b) free farmers and craftsmen, and the system also took a caste form**
