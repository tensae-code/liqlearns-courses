# The slave economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What splits society in the slave economy that the communal society did not have?
**Answer:** The split into classes — slave-owners who own the land, tools and workers, and slaves who are owned and keep nothing of what they produce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain the two roads into slavery, and say which worked from inside the society.
**Answer:** Debt — a person who owed what he could not pay was made a slave for it; and war — captives taken in war were made slaves. Debt worked from inside the society; war captivity brought slaves in from outside.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who stood in the slave society besides owners and slaves, and what is the caste form?
**Answer:** Free farmers and craftsmen, who were not owned and kept what they made. In the caste form, a person's place in the order of work and rank is fixed by birth.
