# The slave economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the slave society, the slaves owned the land and the slave-owners worked for them.
**Answer: False** — the slave-owners owned the land, the tools and the slaves themselves; the slaves worked and kept nothing of what they produced as their own.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** War captivity made slaves from inside the society, and debt brought them in from outside it.
**Answer: False** — it is the other way round: debt made slaves from inside the society; war captivity brought them in from outside.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the caste form of the system, a person's place was fixed by birth.
**Answer: True**
