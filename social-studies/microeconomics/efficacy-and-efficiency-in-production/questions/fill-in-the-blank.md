# Efficacy and efficiency in production — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Efficacy is _____ — the productive process does what it set out to do.
**Answer: reaching the goal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Efficiency is reaching the goal with _____ — nothing wasted on the way.
**Answer: the least use of resources**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inside the productive process efficacy and efficiency are _____: a producer must first reach the goal, and then reach it without wasting resources.
**Answer: distinguished and related**
