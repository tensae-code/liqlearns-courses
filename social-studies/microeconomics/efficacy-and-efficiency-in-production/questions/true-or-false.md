# Efficacy and efficiency in production — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Efficacy is reaching the goal — the productive process does what it set out to do.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Efficiency is reaching the goal with the least use of resources — nothing wasted on the way.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inside the productive process efficacy and efficiency are something else entirely: a producer must first reach the goal, and then reach it without wasting resources.
**Answer: False** — Inside the productive process efficacy and efficiency are distinguished and related: a producer must first reach the goal, and then reach it without wasting resources.
