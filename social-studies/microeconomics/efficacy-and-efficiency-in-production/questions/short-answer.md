# Efficacy and efficiency in production — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about efficacy — reaching the goal?
**Answer:** Efficacy is reaching the goal — the productive process does what it set out to do.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about efficiency — with the least resources?
**Answer:** Efficiency is reaching the goal with the least use of resources — nothing wasted on the way.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the two related inside the productive process?
**Answer:** Inside the productive process efficacy and efficiency are distinguished and related: a producer must first reach the goal, and then reach it without wasting resources.
