# Efficacy and efficiency in production

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Nicaragua book opens it — Efficacy and efficiency in production. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Efficacy — reaching the goal

Efficacy is reaching the goal — the productive process does what it set out to do.

## Sub lesson 2 — Efficiency — with the least resources

Efficiency is reaching the goal with the least use of resources — nothing wasted on the way.

## Sub lesson 3 — The two related inside the productive process

Inside the productive process efficacy and efficiency are distinguished and related: a producer must first reach the goal, and then reach it without wasting resources.
