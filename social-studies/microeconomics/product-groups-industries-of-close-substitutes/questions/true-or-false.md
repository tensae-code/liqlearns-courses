# Product groups — industries of close substitutes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An industry, properly defined, is all the firms making one homogeneous product.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under product differentiation there is no industry, only a product group of firms making close substitutes.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The demands within a product group have high cross-elasticity: one firm's price change visibly shifts the others' demand.
**Answer: True**
