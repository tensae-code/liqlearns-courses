# Product groups — industries of close substitutes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Industry — one homogeneous product" in your own words.
**Answer:** An industry, properly defined, is all the firms making one homogeneous product.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Under differentiation — the product group" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under product differentiation there is no industry, only a product group of firms making close substitutes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Linked by cross-elasticity" in your own words.
**Answer:** The demands within a product group have high cross-elasticity: one firm's price change visibly shifts the others' demand.
