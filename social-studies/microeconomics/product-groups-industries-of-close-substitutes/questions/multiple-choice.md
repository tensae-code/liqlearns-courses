# Product groups — industries of close substitutes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Industry — one homogeneous product"?
a) an industry is all firms making a single homogeneous product  b) no industry under differentiation — a product group of close substitutes instead  c) members are linked by high cross-elasticity — one firm's price visibly shifts rivals' demand
**Answer: a) an industry is all firms making a single homogeneous product**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Under differentiation — the product group"?
a) an industry is all firms making a single homogeneous product  b) members are linked by high cross-elasticity — one firm's price visibly shifts rivals' demand  c) no industry under differentiation — a product group of close substitutes instead
**Answer: c) no industry under differentiation — a product group of close substitutes instead**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Linked by cross-elasticity"?
a) no industry under differentiation — a product group of close substitutes instead  b) members are linked by high cross-elasticity — one firm's price visibly shifts rivals' demand  c) an industry is all firms making a single homogeneous product
**Answer: b) members are linked by high cross-elasticity — one firm's price visibly shifts rivals' demand**
