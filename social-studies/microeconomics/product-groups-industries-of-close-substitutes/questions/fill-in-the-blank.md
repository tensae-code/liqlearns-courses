# Product groups — industries of close substitutes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An industry is all the firms making one _____ product.
**Answer: homogeneous**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Firms making close substitutes form a _____ — there is no single industry under differentiation.
**Answer: product group**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A product group is held together by high _____ of demand between its members' products.
**Answer: cross-elasticity**
