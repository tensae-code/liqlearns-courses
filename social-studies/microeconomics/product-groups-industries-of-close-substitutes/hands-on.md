# Hands-on — Product groups — industries of close substitutes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Stand as firms of one product group holding demand strings. When one 'firm' calls a price cut, the others let their strings sag — high cross-elasticity made visible.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one firm's demand curve shifting as a rival in its group cuts price — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
