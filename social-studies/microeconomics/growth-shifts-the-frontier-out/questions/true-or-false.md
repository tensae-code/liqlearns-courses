# Growth shifts the frontier out — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic growth is a rise in total output from more or better resources, or advances in technology.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Economic growth is a rise in total output from more or better resources, or advances in technology.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (What growth is); sub lesson 2 says: Growth is drawn as an outward shift of the whole production possibilities frontier.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Asymmetric growth pivots the PPF out on one axis only, when one sector grows.
**Answer: True**
