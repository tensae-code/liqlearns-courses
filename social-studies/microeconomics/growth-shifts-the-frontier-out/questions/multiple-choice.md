# Growth shifts the frontier out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What growth is"?
a) a rise in total output from more or better resources or better technology  b) an outward shift of the whole PPF  c) one-sector growth pivoting the curve on one axis only
**Answer: a) a rise in total output from more or better resources or better technology**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The outward shift"?
a) a rise in total output from more or better resources or better technology  b) one-sector growth pivoting the curve on one axis only  c) an outward shift of the whole PPF
**Answer: c) an outward shift of the whole PPF**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Asymmetric growth"?
a) an outward shift of the whole PPF  b) one-sector growth pivoting the curve on one axis only  c) a rise in total output from more or better resources or better technology
**Answer: b) one-sector growth pivoting the curve on one axis only**
