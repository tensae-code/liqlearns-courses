# Growth shifts the frontier out — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What growth is" in your own words.
**Answer:** Economic growth is a rise in total output from more or better resources, or advances in technology.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The outward shift" in your own words.
**Answer:** Growth is drawn as an outward shift of the whole production possibilities frontier.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Asymmetric growth" in your own words.
**Answer:** Asymmetric growth pivots the PPF out on one axis only, when one sector grows.
