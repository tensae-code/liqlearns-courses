# Hands-on — Growth shifts the frontier out

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
With a string curve on the ground, grow the economy: add 'new technology' cards and push the whole string outward. Then grow only one end and show the pivot.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two nested curves, the outer one labelled more resources / better technology, plus a one-sided pivot — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
