# Video script — Growth shifts the frontier out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economic growth is a rise in total output. It comes from more resources, better resources, or advances in technology — the three ways an economy's productive capacity itself gets bigger.

On the diagram, growth is drawn as an outward shift of the whole PPF: combinations that were unattainable yesterday become attainable today, on every part of the curve at once.

Growth in only one sector pivots the curve out on one axis only — the book calls this asymmetric growth, and illustrates it for food technology and for computer technology separately. The other axis stays exactly where it was.
