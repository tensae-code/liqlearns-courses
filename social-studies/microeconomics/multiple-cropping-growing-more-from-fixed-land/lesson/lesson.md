## Sub lesson 1 — Land under cultivation is fixed

In Palampur, **land under cultivation is fixed** — no new land can be added, so output cannot grow by expanding the fields.

## Sub lesson 2 — Multiple cropping — more than one crop from the same land

**Multiple cropping** grows **more than one crop on the same land in a year** — the way to raise output when land cannot grow.

## Sub lesson 3 — Electric tubewell irrigation makes it possible

Multiple cropping is **made possible by electric tubewell irrigation** — water on demand through the year lets field follow field.
