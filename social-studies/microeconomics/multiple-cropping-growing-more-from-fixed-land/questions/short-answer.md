# Multiple cropping — growing more from fixed land — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can't Palampur simply farm more land?
**Answer:** Land under cultivation is fixed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does multiple cropping raise output?
**Answer:** It grows more than one crop on the same land in a year.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What makes multiple cropping possible in Palampur?
**Answer:** Electric tubewell irrigation.
