# Multiple cropping — growing more from fixed land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Palampur's cultivated land is —
a) fixed  b) endless  c) doubling yearly
**Answer: a) fixed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Multiple cropping means —
a) more than one crop on the same land in a year  b) one crop forever  c) farming two villages
**Answer: a) more than one crop on the same land in a year**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Multiple cropping is made possible by —
a) electric tubewell irrigation  b) rain alone  c) importing crops
**Answer: a) electric tubewell irrigation**
