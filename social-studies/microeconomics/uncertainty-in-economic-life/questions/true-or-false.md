# Uncertainty in economic life — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A farmer plants without knowing the rain — that is uncertainty, not necessarily a bad decision.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Keeping reserves is one way a firm lives with uncertainty.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Two careful people can decide differently on the same facts because outcomes are unknowable in advance.
**Answer: True**
