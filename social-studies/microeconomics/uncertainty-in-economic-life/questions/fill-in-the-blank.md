# Uncertainty in economic life — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Decisions taken now, before outcomes are known, are taken under _____.
**Answer: uncertainty**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** People answer uncertainty by spreading _____ instead of staking everything on one outcome.
**Answer: risk**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Knowing the past shrinks the unknown but does not remove _____.
**Answer: uncertainty**
