# Uncertainty in economic life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Uncertainty in economic life means decisions are taken —
a) after seeing the outcome
b) without knowing future outcomes
c) only by the state
**Answer: b) without knowing future outcomes**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A common answer to uncertainty is to —
a) stake everything on one outcome
b) spread the risk over more than one outcome
c) stop deciding
**Answer: b) spread the risk over more than one outcome**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Even with full information about the past, the future stays —
a) fully known
b) uncertain
c) irrelevant
**Answer: b) uncertain**
