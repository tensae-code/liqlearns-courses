# Uncertainty in economic life — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two examples of decisions under uncertainty from the lesson.
**Answer:** The farmer planting without knowing the rain, and the shopkeeper stocking goods without knowing the buyers (also: the student studying without knowing the job).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do firms live with uncertainty?
**Answer:** They hold stocks and reserves and avoid contracts whose possible loss would ruin them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does information not remove uncertainty?
**Answer:** Because the future has not happened yet — information covers the past and present, not outcomes still to come.
