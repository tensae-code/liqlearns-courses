# Video script — Uncertainty in economic life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every economic decision is a bet on a future nobody has seen. The farmer plants without knowing the rain; the shopkeeper stocks goods without knowing the buyers; the student studies without knowing the job. Greece's Grade 12 textbook gives this its own section: uncertainty in economic life.

Because the future is uncertain, people spread risk instead of staking everything on one outcome: the farmer plants more than one crop, the saver keeps more than one store of value, the worker learns more than one skill.

Uncertainty remains even for the well informed. Knowing prices, costs and past seasons shrinks the unknown; it does not remove it, because the future itself has not happened yet.
