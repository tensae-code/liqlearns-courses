# Hands-on — Production of goods and services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pick up one made thing at home — a phone or a shirt. Say out loud what raw materials it began from. Then name one service your family used this week and say what it produced for you.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two columns from memory — "material production" and "immaterial production (services)" — and write three examples in each. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every example you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
