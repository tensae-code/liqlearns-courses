# Production of goods and services — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Raw materials on their own already meet people's needs, with no production needed.
**Answer: False** — production is the work that changes raw materials into products and services people can use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A phone is a material product.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A service is not production, because nothing material is handed over.
**Answer: False** — services are immaterial production; a lesson taught or a patient treated is something people need that has truly been produced.
