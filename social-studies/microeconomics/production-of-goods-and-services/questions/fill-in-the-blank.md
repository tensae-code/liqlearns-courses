# Production of goods and services — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production turns _____ into products and services people need.
**Answer: raw materials**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cars, phones and clothing are examples of _____ production.
**Answer: material**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** School education, hospital treatment and tourism are examples of _____.
**Answer: services**
