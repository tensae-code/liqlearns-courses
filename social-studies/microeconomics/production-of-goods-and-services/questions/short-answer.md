# Production of goods and services — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define production.
**Answer:** Production is turning raw materials into products and services people need.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the book's three examples of material production.
**Answer:** Cars, phones and clothing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the book's three examples of services, and say what kind of production they are.
**Answer:** School education, hospital treatment and tourism — they are immaterial production, services people need that leave no material thing behind.
