# Production of goods and services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production is —
a) selling things in a shop  b) turning raw materials into products and services people need  c) saving money
**Answer: b) turning raw materials into products and services people need**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is material production?
a) a hospital treatment  b) a school lesson  c) making clothing
**Answer: c) making clothing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Immaterial production provides —
a) services  b) raw materials  c) only cars
**Answer: a) services**
