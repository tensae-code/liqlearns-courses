# Video script — Production of goods and services

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Production is turning raw materials into the products and services people need. Some production is material: it makes things you can touch — a car, a phone, a piece of clothing. Other production is immaterial: it provides services — a teacher's lesson at school, a doctor's treatment at hospital, a guide's work in tourism.

Both are production. Both start from what is available and end with something people need. The difference is only in what comes out: a thing you can hold, or a service you receive.
