# Hands-on — Consumer optimum — where the curve touches the line

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay a string budget line across paper curves. Slide a coin along the line from one end to the other, calling out the curve it sits on at each stop, until it settles where the touched curve is highest — the tangency point.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a budget line with three indifference curves, only the middle one touching it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
