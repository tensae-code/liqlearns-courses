# Consumer optimum — where the curve touches the line — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At the optimum, the indifference curve is _____ to the budget line — it just touches it.
**Answer: tangent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the tangency point, the MRS equals the _____ of the two goods.
**Answer: price ratio**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Only at the _____ point do the consumer's rate of swap and the market's rate agree.
**Answer: tangency**
