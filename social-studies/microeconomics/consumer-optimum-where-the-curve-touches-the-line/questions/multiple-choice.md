# Consumer optimum — where the curve touches the line — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The highest reachable curve"?
a) the optimum is the tangency of the budget line with the highest reachable curve  b) at tangency, MRS = price ratio and all income is spent  c) away from E, the consumer's valuation and the market's disagree, pushing the consumer toward E

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The tangency condition — MRS equals the price ratio"?

**Answer: c) at tangency, MRS = price ratio and all income is spent**
