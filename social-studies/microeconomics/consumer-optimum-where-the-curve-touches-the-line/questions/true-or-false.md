# Consumer optimum — where the curve touches the line — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The consumer's optimum is where an indifference curve is tangent to the budget line.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the consumer's optimum, the MRS equals the price ratio of the two goods.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Away from the tangency point, the MRS differs from the price ratio, so moving along the line can still raise satisfaction.
**Answer: True**
