# Consumer optimum — where the curve touches the line — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The highest reachable curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The consumer's optimum is where an indifference curve is tangent to the budget line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The tangency condition — MRS equals the price ratio" in your own words.
**Answer:** At the consumer's optimum, the MRS equals the price ratio of the two goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why other points fail" in your own words.
**Answer:** Away from the tangency point, the MRS differs from the price ratio, so moving along the line can still raise satisfaction.
