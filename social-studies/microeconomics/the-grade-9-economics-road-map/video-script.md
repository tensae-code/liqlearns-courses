# Video script — The Grade 9 economics road map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome. This course has eight stops. First, what economics is. Second, the basic economic problems and economic systems. Third, economic resources and markets. Fourth, demand and supply. Fifth, production and cost. Sixth, money. Seventh, the whole economy at once. Eighth, starting your own enterprise.

Every unit works the same way. It starts from what you already think, gives you an activity after every section, sums itself up, and closes with review questions. The examples are Ethiopia's own — prices in birr, teff, Axumite coins and Ethiopian founders.
