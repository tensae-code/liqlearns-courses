# Small producers — compete or perish

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — small producers — compete or perish — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ravi's unit shrinks from 20 workers to 7

**Ravi's Hosur capacitor unit shrank from 20 workers to 7** as competition from imports squeezed it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Imports halve his price

**Imports halved the price** Ravi could charge — the same capacitor must now sell for half, while his costs did not halve.

## Sub lesson 3 — Units close across a sector employing 20 million

Units in **batteries, plastics, toys, tyres and dairy** have closed too — in a **small-industry sector employing 20 million** people, so the closures are a national question, not one man's bad luck.

## Say it to your companion
