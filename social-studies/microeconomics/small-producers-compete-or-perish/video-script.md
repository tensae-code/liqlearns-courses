Small producers — compete or perish — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: Ravi's Hosur capacitor unit shrinks from 20 workers to 7 as imports halve his price, and units in batteries, plastics, toys, tyres and dairy close, in a small-industry sector employing 20 million.
