# Small producers — compete or perish — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ravi's unit grew under import competition.
**Answer: False — it shrank from 20 workers to 7.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Imports raised the price Ravi could charge.
**Answer: False — they halved it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Small industries employ only a handful of people.
**Answer: False — the sector employs 20 million.**
