# Small producers — compete or perish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ravi's unit shrank from 20 workers to —
a) 7  b) 20  c) 70
**Answer: a) 7**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Imports did what to Ravi's price?
a) halved it  b) doubled it  c) left it untouched
**Answer: a) halved it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The small-industry sector employs —
a) 20 million  b) 20 thousand  c) 2 million
**Answer: a) 20 million**
