# What economics asks — what, how and for whom to produce — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic activity is —
a) only buying and selling  b) producing, distributing, exchanging and consuming goods and services  c) only farming
**Answer: b) producing, distributing, exchanging and consuming goods and services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first of the economist's three questions is —
a) what do we produce  b) who is the king  c) what is the price of oil
**Answer: a) what do we produce**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third question — for whom do we produce — asks —
a) which shop is cheapest  b) who will receive and use what is produced  c) how many workers there are
**Answer: b) who will receive and use what is produced**
