# What economics asks — what, how and for whom to produce — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consuming is one of the four movements of economic activity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An economy can produce everything at once, so the question "what do we produce" never needs asking.
**Answer: False** — resources used for one product cannot also make another, so what to produce must be chosen.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The three questions — what, how, and for whom to produce — belong together and are asked about everything an economy makes.
**Answer: True**
