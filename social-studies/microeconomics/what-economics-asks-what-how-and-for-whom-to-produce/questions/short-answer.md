# What economics asks — what, how and for whom to produce — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four movements of economic activity.
**Answer:** Producing, distributing, exchanging and consuming goods and services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must "what do we produce" be chosen before production begins?
**Answer:** Because resources used for one product cannot also make another, so a farm, a factory and a country must each choose what to produce.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the economist's three questions.
**Answer:** What do we produce, how do we produce it, and for whom do we produce it.
