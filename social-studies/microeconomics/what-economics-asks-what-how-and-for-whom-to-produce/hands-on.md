# Hands-on — What economics asks — what, how and for whom to produce

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Take one thing in your kitchen — a carton of milk or a box of dates. Walk it through the four movements out loud: who produced it, how it was distributed to the shop, how it was exchanged, and who consumes it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a chain of four boxes — producing, distributing, exchanging, consuming — and write your kitchen item's journey in the boxes. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
