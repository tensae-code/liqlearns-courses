# Video script — What economics asks — what, how and for whom to produce

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Follow a box of dates. Someone grows and picks them — that is producing. Trucks carry them to the shops — distributing. You hand over money and take the box — exchanging. Your family eats them — consuming. Those four movements together are economic activity, and economics studies all of them.

And for every product, the economist asks three questions. What do we produce — dates, or milk, or something else? How do we produce it — with which land, which workers, which machines? And for whom do we produce it — who will receive and use what is made? Three questions, asked about everything an economy makes.
