# Costs, price and profit in a small project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The marketing and selling phase of the school project is defined as —
a) determining the cost of the product, the price and the profit margin, identifying buyers and selling  b) baking only  c) choosing the oven's colour
**Answer: a) determining the cost of the product, the price and the profit margin, identifying buyers and selling**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Profit or loss in the liquidation is found by —
a) adding income and expenses  b) subtracting total expenses from total income — positive is a profit, negative a loss  c) counting the loaves left over
**Answer: b) subtracting total expenses from total income — positive is a profit, negative a loss**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rent, fixed salaries and depreciation are —
a) variable costs  b) fixed costs — paid whether the firm produces or not  c) not costs at all
**Answer: b) fixed costs — paid whether the firm produces or not**
