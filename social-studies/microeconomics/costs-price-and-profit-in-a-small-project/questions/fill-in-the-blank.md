# Costs, price and profit in a small project — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The marketing phase determines the cost, the price and the profit _____.
**Answer: margin**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A _____ result in the liquidation is a profit; a negative result is a loss.
**Answer: positive**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cost of one juice is found by dividing each kind of cost by the _____ juices projected and adding the two unit costs.
**Answer: 1,000**
