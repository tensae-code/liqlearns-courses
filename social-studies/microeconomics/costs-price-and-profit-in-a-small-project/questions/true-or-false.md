# Costs, price and profit in a small project — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cost, price and profit margin are fixed only after all the selling is done.
**Answer: False** — they are determined in the marketing and selling phase itself, before and while the product reaches its buyers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the liquidation format, income or investment goes on one side and expenses (egresos) on the other.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The selling price is fixed by looking at competitors' prices, quality and the profit margin wanted.
**Answer: True**
