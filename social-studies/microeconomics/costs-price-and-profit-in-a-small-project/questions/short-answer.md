# Costs, price and profit in a small project — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the project's marketing and selling phase determine, and what else does it do?
**Answer:** It determines the cost of the product, the price and the profit margin (margen de ganancia) — and it identifies buyers and sells.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe the liquidation's set format and how profit or loss is found.
**Answer:** Income or investment goes on one side and expenses (egresos) on the other; the balance comes from the two totals, and profit or loss is total income minus total expenses — positive is a profit, negative a loss.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Distinguish variable from fixed costs with the juice stall's examples, and say how the net profit margin is computed.
**Answer:** Variable costs change with production — raw materials, packaging, labour, sales commissions; fixed costs are paid whether the firm produces or not — rent, fixed salaries, depreciation, interest, electricity, promotion. The net profit margin (margen de ganancia neta) is projected sales minus total cost.
