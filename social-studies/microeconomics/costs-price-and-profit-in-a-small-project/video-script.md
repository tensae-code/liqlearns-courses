# Video script — Costs, price and profit in a small project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A small project lives or dies on three numbers: its cost, its price and its profit margin.

Guatemala's pupils learn them by running a real school bakery — and the closing liquidation tells them, on a set format, whether the class made a profit or a loss.

Then a juice stall teaches costing whole: variable costs against fixed costs, the cost of one juice, and a selling price fixed against competitors, quality and the margin wanted.
