Guatemala's own way: entrepreneurship is taught by running a real school bakery project end to end — build the oven, quote suppliers, bake, sell — and the economics lands in the project's own paperwork: a phase that fixes cost, price and profit margin, and a closing liquidation that works out whether the class made a profit or a loss; and costs are learned on a juice stall's real arithmetic, in quetzales, down to the straws and napkins.
