# Hands-on — Costs, price and profit in a small project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work the liquidation
Take a small project — real or invented — and set out its liquidation on the book's format on paper: income or investment on one side, expenses on the other. Add each side, find the balance, and write in words whether it is a profit or a loss, and why.

## Cost one unit
Choose one product and list its costs in two columns, variable and fixed, the book's way. Divide each column by the number of units you plan to make, add the two unit costs, and write the cost of one unit. Then fix its selling price, naming the competitors' price, the quality and the margin you used.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
