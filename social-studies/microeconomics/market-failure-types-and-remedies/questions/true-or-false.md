# Market failure — types and remedies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market failure is when the free market cannot allocate resources efficiently on its own.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The common types are public goods, externalities, asymmetric information, monopoly power and missing markets.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Remedies run from taxes and subsidies through regulation and public provision to property rights.
**Answer: True**
