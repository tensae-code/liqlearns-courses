# Market failure — types and remedies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "When the market's answer is wrong" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market failure is when the free market cannot allocate resources efficiently on its own.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The common types" in your own words.
**Answer:** The common types are public goods, externalities, asymmetric information, monopoly power and missing markets.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The remedy menu" in your own words.
**Answer:** Remedies run from taxes and subsidies through regulation and public provision to property rights.
