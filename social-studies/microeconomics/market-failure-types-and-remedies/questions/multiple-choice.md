# Market failure — types and remedies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "When the market's answer is wrong"?
a) Market failure is when the free market cannot allocate resources efficiently on its own  b) The common types are public goods, externalities, asymmetric information, monopoly power and missing markets  c) Remedies run from taxes and subsidies through regulation and public provision to property rights
**Answer: a) Market failure is when the free market cannot allocate resources efficiently on its own**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The common types"?
a) The common types are public goods, externalities, asymmetric information, monopoly power and missing markets  b) Remedies run from taxes and subsidies through regulation and public provision to property rights  c) Market failure is when the free market cannot allocate resources efficiently on its own
**Answer: a) The common types are public goods, externalities, asymmetric information, monopoly power and missing markets**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The remedy menu"?
a) Remedies run from taxes and subsidies through regulation and public provision to property rights  b) Market failure is when the free market cannot allocate resources efficiently on its own  c) The common types are public goods, externalities, asymmetric information, monopoly power and missing markets
**Answer: a) Remedies run from taxes and subsidies through regulation and public provision to property rights**
