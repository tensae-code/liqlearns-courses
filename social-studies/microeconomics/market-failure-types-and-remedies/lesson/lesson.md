# Market failure — types and remedies

## Do it first

A market sets a price, buyers buy, sellers sell — and the whole town's river ends up poisoned. The market worked. Did it fail?

## Sub lesson 1 — When the market's answer is wrong

A free market fails when it cannot allocate resources efficiently on its own — the price system, left alone, produces too much of some things and too little of others. Market failure is not a crash; it is a wrong answer given calmly.

## Sub lesson 2 — The common types

The book's common types: public goods, externalities, asymmetric information, monopoly power and missing markets. Each is a different fault in the price machinery, and each gets its own lesson in this unit.

## Sub lesson 3 — The remedy menu

The solution menu runs from taxes and subsidies through regulation and public provision to defining property rights. The craft is matching: each failure in this unit gets the remedy that fits its fault, not a remedy at random.
