# Hands-on — Market failure — types and remedies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match fault to remedy
Write the five failure types on cards and the four remedy families on others. Match them, then find one failure where two remedies could both work and say which you would try first.

## Find a failure nearby
Name one market failure within a kilometre of your home. Say which type it is and which remedy from the menu fits it best.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
