# Video script — Consumption goods and production goods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Consumption goods and production goods — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Goods are of consumption (made to be consumed directly, e.g. Food) or of production (used to produce other goods — tools like scissors and hoes, tractors; Or transformed into other goods — barley into beer, cotton into cloth)
