# Hands-on — Externalities — private cost, social cost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the two costs
Draw MPC and MSC apart for a smoky factory, mark the market output and the social optimum, and shade the deadweight loss. Label MEC as the vertical gap.

## Leak hunt
List three externalities you met this week — one negative, one positive, one arguable — naming the third party in each case.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
