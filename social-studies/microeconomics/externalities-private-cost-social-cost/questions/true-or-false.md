# Externalities — private cost, social cost — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An externality is a cost or benefit falling on third parties outside the price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** MSC = MPC + MEC, and the market over-produces a negative externality.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Positive externalities like education and vaccination are under-produced by the market.
**Answer: True**
