# Externalities — private cost, social cost — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cost falling on third parties outside the price is an ____.
**Answer: externality**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marginal social cost equals marginal private cost plus marginal ____ cost.
**Answer: external**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Vaccination is under-produced because it carries a ____ externality.
**Answer: positive**
