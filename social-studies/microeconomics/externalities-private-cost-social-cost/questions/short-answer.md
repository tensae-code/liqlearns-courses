# Externalities — private cost, social cost — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Costs and benefits that leak" in your own words.
**Answer:** An externality is a cost or benefit falling on third parties outside the price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "MSC = MPC + MEC" in your own words.
**Answer:** MSC = MPC + MEC, and the market over-produces a negative externality.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The mirror — positive externalities" in your own words.
**Answer:** Positive externalities like education and vaccination are under-produced by the market.
