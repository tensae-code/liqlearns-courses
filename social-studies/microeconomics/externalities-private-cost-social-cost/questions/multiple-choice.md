# Externalities — private cost, social cost — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Costs and benefits that leak"?
a) An externality is a cost or benefit falling on third parties outside the price  b) MSC = MPC + MEC, and the market over-produces a negative externality  c) Positive externalities like education and vaccination are under-produced by the market
**Answer: a) An externality is a cost or benefit falling on third parties outside the price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "MSC = MPC + MEC"?
a) MSC = MPC + MEC, and the market over-produces a negative externality  b) Positive externalities like education and vaccination are under-produced by the market  c) An externality is a cost or benefit falling on third parties outside the price
**Answer: a) MSC = MPC + MEC, and the market over-produces a negative externality**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The mirror — positive externalities"?
a) Positive externalities like education and vaccination are under-produced by the market  b) An externality is a cost or benefit falling on third parties outside the price  c) MSC = MPC + MEC, and the market over-produces a negative externality
**Answer: a) Positive externalities like education and vaccination are under-produced by the market**
