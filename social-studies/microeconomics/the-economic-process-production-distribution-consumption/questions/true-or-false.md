# The economic process — production, distribution, consumption — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumption is the first phase of the cycle.
**Answer: False** — the cycle runs production, distribution, consumption.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distribution means destroying unsold goods.
**Answer: False** — it means reaching the commercial circuits and consumers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Imbalance between production and consumption never affects prices.
**Answer: False** — the book ties it directly: scarcity and rises one way, cheapening the other.
