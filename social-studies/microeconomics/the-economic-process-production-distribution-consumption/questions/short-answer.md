# The economic process — production, distribution, consumption — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about production — transforming raw material?
**Answer:** Production transforms raw material — the first phase of the economic process.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about distribution — reaching the circuits and the consumers?
**Answer:** Distribution carries the product through the commercial circuits to the consumers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about consumption — closing the circuit, and what imbalance does?
**Answer:** Consumption closes the circuit; low production with high consumption brings price rises and scarcity, the opposite brings cheapening.
