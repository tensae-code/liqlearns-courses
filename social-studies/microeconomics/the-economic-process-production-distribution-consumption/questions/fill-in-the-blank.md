# The economic process — production, distribution, consumption — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first phase of the economic process is _____.
**Answer: production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The middle phase, _____, carries goods to consumers.
**Answer: distribution**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Low production with high consumption leads to price rises and _____.
**Answer: scarcity**
