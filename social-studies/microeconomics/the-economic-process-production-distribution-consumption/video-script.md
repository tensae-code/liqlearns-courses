# Video script — The economic process — production, distribution, consumption

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The economic process — production, distribution, consumption — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: production transforms raw material — the first phase of the economic process; distribution carries the product through the commercial circuits to the consumers; consumption closes the circuit; low production with high consumption brings price rises and scarcity, the opposite brings cheapening.
