# The economic process — production, distribution, consumption

## Sub lesson 1 — Production — transforming raw material

An economic activity produces or exchanges goods and services to satisfy a population's needs, and every such activity runs a three-phase cycle. First, production: transforming raw material into goods and services.

## Sub lesson 2 — Distribution — reaching the circuits and the consumers

Second, distribution: the product reaches the commercial circuits and the consumers. A good produced but never distributed satisfies no need — the middle phase carries it to its market.

## Sub lesson 3 — Consumption — closing the circuit, and what imbalance does

Third, consumption closes the circuit and returns the capital to sustain the scheme. The balance matters: low production with high consumption leads to price rises and scarcity; the opposite, to cheapening.
