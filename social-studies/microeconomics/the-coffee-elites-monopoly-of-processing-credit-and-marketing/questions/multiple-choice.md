# The coffee elite's monopoly of processing, credit and marketing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About el monopolio cafetalero — the lesson teaches that —
a) The coffee elite held a monopoly over the beneficiado (processing).
b) The credit and the commercialisation of the bean.
c) And with it political power.
**Answer: a) The coffee elite held a monopoly over the beneficiado (processing).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About el monopolio cafetalero — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) And with it political power.
b) The credit and the commercialisation of the bean.
c) The coffee elite held a monopoly over the beneficiado (processing).
**Answer: b) The credit and the commercialisation of the bean.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About el monopolio cafetalero — more — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) The coffee elite held a monopoly over the beneficiado (processing).
b) The credit and the commercialisation of the bean.
c) And with it political power.
**Answer: c) And with it political power.**
