# Total, average and marginal revenue — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Total revenue is the firm's total cost.
**Answer: False** — revenue is money received from sales, not money spent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Average revenue and price are always different things.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches they are two names for the same thing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Marginal revenue is the revenue of the first unit only.
**Answer: False** — it is the revenue added by one more unit.
