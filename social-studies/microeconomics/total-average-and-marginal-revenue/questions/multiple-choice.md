# Total, average and marginal revenue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About total revenue — the lesson teaches that —
a) total revenue is all the money a firm receives from selling its output  b) average revenue is total revenue per unit sold — the same thing as price  c) marginal revenue is the revenue added by one more unit sold
**Answer: a) total revenue is all the money a firm receives from selling its output**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About average revenue — and price — the lesson teaches that —
a) total revenue is all the money a firm receives from selling its output  b) average revenue is total revenue per unit sold — the same thing as price  c) marginal revenue is the revenue added by one more unit sold
**Answer: b) average revenue is total revenue per unit sold — the same thing as price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About marginal revenue — under competition and under monopoly — the lesson teaches that —
a) total revenue is all the money a firm receives from selling its output  b) average revenue is total revenue per unit sold — the same thing as price  c) marginal revenue is the revenue added by one more unit sold
**Answer: c) marginal revenue is the revenue added by one more unit sold**
