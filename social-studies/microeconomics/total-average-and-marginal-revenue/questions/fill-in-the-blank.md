# Total, average and marginal revenue — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All the money a firm receives from selling its output is _____ revenue.
**Answer: total**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Average revenue and _____ are two names for the same thing.
**Answer: price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The revenue added by selling one more unit is _____ revenue.
**Answer: marginal**
