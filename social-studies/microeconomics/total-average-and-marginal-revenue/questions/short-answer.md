# Total, average and marginal revenue — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is total revenue?
**Answer:** Total revenue is all the money a firm receives from selling its output.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is average revenue, and what is it the same as?
**Answer:** Average revenue is total revenue per unit sold, and it is the same thing as price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is marginal revenue?
**Answer:** Marginal revenue is the revenue added by selling one more unit.
