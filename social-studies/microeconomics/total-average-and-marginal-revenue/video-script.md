# Video script — Total, average and marginal revenue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Total, average and marginal revenue — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: total revenue is all the money a firm receives from selling its output; average revenue is total revenue per unit sold — the same thing as price; marginal revenue is the revenue added by one more unit sold.
