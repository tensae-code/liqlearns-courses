# Total, average and marginal revenue

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — total, average and marginal revenue — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Total revenue

The Class 11 revenue chapter starts with **total revenue**: all the money a firm receives from selling its output. Every other revenue measure is built from this total.

## Sub lesson 2 — Average revenue — and price

**Average revenue** is total revenue divided by the units sold. The book's key point: average revenue and **price** are two names for the same thing — the revenue per unit is the price per unit.

## Sub lesson 3 — Marginal revenue — under competition and under monopoly

**Marginal revenue** is the revenue added by selling one more unit. The book shows all three revenues in tables and diagrams **under perfect competition and under monopoly** — flat under competition, falling under monopoly.
