# Where the price of a shirt goes

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — where the price of a shirt goes — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The exporter's price splits into costs and profit

On the exporter's side, the **per-shirt price splits into cloth, wages and office costs, against his profit** — every rupee of the price is accounted for.

## Sub lesson 2 — The US shop's $26 price

In the US shop the shirt sells for **$26**, and that price splits into **purchase, advertising, storage and a large profit**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Cost, price and profit at each link

The lesson's skill: at **each link of the chain**, separate **cost, price and profit** — who pays what, who keeps what.

## Say it to your companion

Explain where the price of a shirt goes to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Using the lesson, say in your own words what where the price of a shirt goes teaches, part by part.

3. Check your Do it first sentence against the lesson: fix anything the lesson teaches differently.

4. Answer this lesson's questions from questions/ without the lesson open; re-read only the parts you missed.
