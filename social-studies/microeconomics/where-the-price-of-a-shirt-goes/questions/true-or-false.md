# Where the price of a shirt goes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The exporter's whole price is profit.
**Answer: False — cloth, wages and office costs come out of it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The US shop makes only a tiny profit on the $26 shirt.
**Answer: False — its profit share is large.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Only the final shop has costs and profit.
**Answer: False — every link has cost, price and profit.**
