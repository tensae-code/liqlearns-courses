# Where the price of a shirt goes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The exporter's price covers —
a) cloth, wages, office costs and his profit  b) only profit  c) only cloth
**Answer: a) cloth, wages, office costs and his profit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The US shop's price is —
a) $26  b) $2.60  c) $260
**Answer: a) $26**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At each link you separate —
a) cost, price and profit  b) only the colours  c) only the sizes
**Answer: a) cost, price and profit**
