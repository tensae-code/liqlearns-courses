# Video script — Where the price of a shirt goes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Where the price of a shirt goes — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: the per-shirt money split is worked out — the exporter's price, cloth, wages and office costs against his profit, and the US shop's $26 price split into purchase, advertising, storage and a large profit.
