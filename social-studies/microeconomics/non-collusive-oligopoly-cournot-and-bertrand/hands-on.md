# Hands-on — Non-collusive oligopoly — Cournot and Bertrand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Play both games in pairs: first each writes a quantity in secret and reveals together (Cournot); then each writes a price (Bertrand) and undercuts round by round until price hits cost — the class calls the outcome.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two panels — rivals choose quantities versus rivals choose prices — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
