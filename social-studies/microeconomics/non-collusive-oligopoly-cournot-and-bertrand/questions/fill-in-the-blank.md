# Non-collusive oligopoly — Cournot and Bertrand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under non-collusive oligopoly, uncertainty about reactions makes prices _____ and advertising aggressive.
**Answer: rigid**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Cournot model, rival firms compete on _____ of a homogeneous product.
**Answer: quantities**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Bertrand model, rival firms compete on _____ and end at the competitive output.
**Answer: price**
