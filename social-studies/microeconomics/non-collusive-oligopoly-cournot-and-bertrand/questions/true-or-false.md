# Non-collusive oligopoly — Cournot and Bertrand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Non-collusive oligopolists must guess rivals' reactions, which makes prices rigid and advertising aggressive.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Cournot model, firms compete on quantities, set independently and at the same time.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Bertrand model, price competition drives the firms to produce the competitive level of output.
**Answer: True**
