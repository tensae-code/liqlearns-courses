# Non-collusive oligopoly — Cournot and Bertrand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Competing without agreement"?
a) independent competition: guessed reactions, rigid prices, aggressive advertising  b) Cournot firms choose quantities of a homogeneous product, independently and simultaneously  c) Bertrand firms compete on price and end up producing the competitive output level
**Answer: a) independent competition: guessed reactions, rigid prices, aggressive advertising**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Cournot — competing on quantities"?
a) independent competition: guessed reactions, rigid prices, aggressive advertising  b) Bertrand firms compete on price and end up producing the competitive output level  c) Cournot firms choose quantities of a homogeneous product, independently and simultaneously
**Answer: c) Cournot firms choose quantities of a homogeneous product, independently and simultaneously**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Bertrand — competing on price"?
a) Cournot firms choose quantities of a homogeneous product, independently and simultaneously  b) Bertrand firms compete on price and end up producing the competitive output level  c) independent competition: guessed reactions, rigid prices, aggressive advertising
**Answer: b) Bertrand firms compete on price and end up producing the competitive output level**
