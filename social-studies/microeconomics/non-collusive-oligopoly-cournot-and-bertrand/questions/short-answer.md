# Non-collusive oligopoly — Cournot and Bertrand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Competing without agreement" in your own words.
**Answer:** Non-collusive oligopolists must guess rivals' reactions, which makes prices rigid and advertising aggressive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Cournot — competing on quantities" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the Cournot model, firms compete on quantities, set independently and at the same time.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Bertrand — competing on price" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the Bertrand model, price competition drives the firms to produce the competitive level of output.
