# The production possibilities frontier — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What the frontier shows" in your own words.
**Answer:** The PPF shows every combination of two goods an economy can produce with its given resources and technology.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Its assumptions" in your own words.
**Answer:** The PPF assumes resources fixed in quantity and quality, two classes of output, full employment and unchanged technology.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "On, inside, outside" in your own words.
**Answer:** Points on the PPF are efficient, points inside are inefficient, and points outside are unattainable.
