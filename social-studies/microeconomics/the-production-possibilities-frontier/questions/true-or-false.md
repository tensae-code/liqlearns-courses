# The production possibilities frontier — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The PPF shows every combination of two goods an economy can produce with its given resources and technology.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The PPF shows every combination of two goods an economy can produce with its given resources and technology.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (What the frontier shows); sub lesson 2 says: The PPF assumes resources fixed in quantity and quality, two classes of output, full employment and unchanged technology.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Points on the PPF are efficient, points inside are inefficient, and points outside are unattainable.
**Answer: True**
