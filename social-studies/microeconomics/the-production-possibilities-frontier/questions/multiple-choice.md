# The production possibilities frontier — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What the frontier shows"?
a) every combination of two goods producible with given resources and technology  b) fixed resources, two outputs, full employment, unchanged technology  c) on = efficient, inside = inefficient, outside = unattainable
**Answer: a) every combination of two goods producible with given resources and technology**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Its assumptions"?
a) every combination of two goods producible with given resources and technology  b) on = efficient, inside = inefficient, outside = unattainable  c) fixed resources, two outputs, full employment, unchanged technology
**Answer: c) fixed resources, two outputs, full employment, unchanged technology**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "On, inside, outside"?
a) fixed resources, two outputs, full employment, unchanged technology  b) on = efficient, inside = inefficient, outside = unattainable  c) every combination of two goods producible with given resources and technology
**Answer: b) on = efficient, inside = inefficient, outside = unattainable**
