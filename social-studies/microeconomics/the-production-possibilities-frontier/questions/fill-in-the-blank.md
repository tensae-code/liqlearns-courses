# The production possibilities frontier — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The PPF shows every _____ of two goods the economy can produce.
**Answer: combination**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The PPF assumes resources _____ in quantity and quality, and technology unchanged.
**Answer: fixed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Points outside the PPF are _____.
**Answer: unattainable**
