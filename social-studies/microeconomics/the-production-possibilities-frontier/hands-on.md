# Hands-on — The production possibilities frontier

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Draw the curve on the ground with string. Place a stone on it, inside it and outside it; for each stone, say 'efficient', 'inefficient' or 'unattainable' and why.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the food–computers curve with one point on it, one inside and one outside, each labelled — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
