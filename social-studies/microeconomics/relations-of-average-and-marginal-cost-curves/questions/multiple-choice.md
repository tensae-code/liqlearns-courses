# Relations of the average and marginal cost curves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marginal cost crosses average cost —
a) at average cost's highest point
b) at average cost's minimum point
c) nowhere
**Answer: b) at average cost's minimum point**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** While marginal cost is below average cost, average cost —
a) rises
b) falls
c) doubles
**Answer: b) falls**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Minimum average cost is found where —
a) MC is at its own minimum
b) MC cuts AC
c) AC meets the price line
**Answer: b) MC cuts AC**
