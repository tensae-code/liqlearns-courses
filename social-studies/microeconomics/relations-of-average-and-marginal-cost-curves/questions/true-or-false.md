# Relations of the average and marginal cost curves — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The MC–AC crossing rule also holds for average variable cost.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Average cost stops falling exactly where marginal cost meets it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At the crossing, unit cost is at its lowest.
**Answer: True**
