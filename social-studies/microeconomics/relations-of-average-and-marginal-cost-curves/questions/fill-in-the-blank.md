# Relations of the average and marginal cost curves — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** MC crosses AC at AC's _____ point.
**Answer: minimum**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A new unit costing less than the average drags the average _____.
**Answer: down**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The output where MC cuts AC has the lowest _____ cost.
**Answer: average / unit**
