# Relations of the average and marginal cost curves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does marginal cost cross average cost?
**Answer:** At the minimum point of the average cost curve (and likewise of average variable cost).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain the pull in one sentence.
**Answer:** The marginal unit is the newest entry in the average: below the average it pulls the average down, above it, up.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is minimum average cost read off a diagram?
**Answer:** At the point where the marginal cost curve crosses the average cost curve.
