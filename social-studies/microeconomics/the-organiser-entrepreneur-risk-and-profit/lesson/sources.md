Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 3, Lesson 4 — Organisation — teaches: The organiser (entrepreneur) combines the factors of production, bears the risk of loss, and earns profit — revenue minus costs: profit is the organiser's reward for organising and for risking. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
