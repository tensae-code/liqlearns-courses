# Hands-on — The organiser — entrepreneur, risk and profit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with a small venture
Invent a tiny venture — a juice stall. List the land, labour and capital it needs, say who the organiser is, what loss he risks, and work out a profit: choose a revenue, subtract your costs. Say the whole chain aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — an organiser bringing land, workers and tools together into one workshop, carrying the risk himself, with revenue and costs weighed on a balance — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
