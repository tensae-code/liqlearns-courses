# The organiser — entrepreneur, risk and profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The organiser's first job is to —
a) combine the factors of production  b) fix all prices  c) lend money to the state
**Answer: a) combine the factors of production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If the venture fails, the loss falls on —
a) the customers  b) the organiser  c) nobody
**Answer: b) the organiser**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit equals —
a) revenue plus costs  b) revenue minus costs  c) costs minus revenue
**Answer: b) revenue minus costs**
