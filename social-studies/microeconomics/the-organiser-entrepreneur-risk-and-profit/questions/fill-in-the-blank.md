# The organiser — entrepreneur, risk and profit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The _____ combines land, labour and capital into one working unit.
**Answer: organiser**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The organiser bears the _____ of loss.
**Answer: risk**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit = _____ minus costs.
**Answer: revenue**
