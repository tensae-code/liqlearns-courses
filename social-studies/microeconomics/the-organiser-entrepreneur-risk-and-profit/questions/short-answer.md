# The organiser — entrepreneur, risk and profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the organiser do?
**Answer:** He combines the factors of production into one working unit aimed at a product, and carries the venture as one enterprise.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is risk-bearing part of the organiser's role?
**Answer:** Because he commits resources before anything is sold, under uncertainty — if the venture fails, the loss falls on him.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A venture takes in 50,000 rials and spends 42,000. What is the profit, and whose is it?
**Answer:** 8,000 rials — the organiser's profit, revenue minus costs.
