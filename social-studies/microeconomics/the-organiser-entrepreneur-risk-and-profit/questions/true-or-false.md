# The organiser — entrepreneur, risk and profit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land, labour and capital combine themselves without an organiser.
**Answer: False — the organiser combines them into one working unit; left separate, they produce nothing together.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The organiser bears the risk of loss.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit is what revenue leaves after costs are paid.
**Answer: True**
