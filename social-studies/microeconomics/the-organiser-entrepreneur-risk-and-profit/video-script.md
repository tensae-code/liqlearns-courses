# Video script — The organiser — entrepreneur, risk and profit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Factors of production do not combine themselves. The organiser — the entrepreneur — brings land, labour and capital together into one producing unit, and decides what will be made and how.

The organiser also bears the risk: if the venture fails, the loss falls on him. His reward, when it succeeds, is profit — what revenue leaves after costs are paid. No risk-bearer, no organising; that is why organisation stands as its own element of production.
