Worked-answer errors corrected here, named only in this file: (1) The teacher guide's cloth-and-sugar worked answer mislabels its column ("opportunity cost per kg of sugar in metres of cloth") while computing successive changes in sugar, and its last step breaks the increasing-cost trend it is meant to show — this lesson therefore teaches the method and the verified X–Y solution (5, 15, 20 units of Y per unit of X, recomputed step by step: Y falls 100→90→60→20 as X rises by 2 each step, so each step costs 10, 30, 40 units of Y for 2 units of X) and does NOT copy the guide's cloth-and-sugar figures. (2) Opportunity cost is read as a magnitude (absolute value), as the student book notes.

Pairing note carried from the placements: the student textbook is the finished 2023 edition, while the teacher guide is ©2022 with an unfinished placeholder printing block — edition-mismatched companions; the guide's answer keys are partly incomplete/misaligned with this book's review questions, and no answer-key figure is taught here unchecked.
