# Opportunity cost — the next best thing you gave up — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The opportunity cost of a decision is the value of the next best alternative given up.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The opportunity cost of a decision is the value of the next best alternative given up.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The next best alternative); sub lesson 2 says: Opportunity cost is measured in goods and services, not money.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the worked X–Y table, the opportunity cost per unit of X rises: 5, then 15, then 20 units of Y.
**Answer: True**
