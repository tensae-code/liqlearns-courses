# Opportunity cost — the next best thing you gave up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The next best alternative"?
a) the value of the next best alternative given up  b) measured in goods and services, resting on substitution between uses  c) cost per unit of X rising 5, 15, 20 units of Y in the worked table
**Answer: a) the value of the next best alternative given up**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Measured in goods, not money"?
a) the value of the next best alternative given up  b) cost per unit of X rising 5, 15, 20 units of Y in the worked table  c) measured in goods and services, resting on substitution between uses
**Answer: c) measured in goods and services, resting on substitution between uses**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Computing it from a table"?
a) measured in goods and services, resting on substitution between uses  b) cost per unit of X rising 5, 15, 20 units of Y in the worked table  c) the value of the next best alternative given up
**Answer: b) cost per unit of X rising 5, 15, 20 units of Y in the worked table**
