# Opportunity cost — the next best thing you gave up — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Opportunity cost is the value of the _____ alternative given up.
**Answer: next best**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Opportunity cost is measured in _____, not money.
**Answer: goods and services**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the X–Y table the cost per unit of X rises 5, 15, then _____ units of Y.
**Answer: 20**
