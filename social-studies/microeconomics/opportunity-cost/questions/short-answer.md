# Opportunity cost — the next best thing you gave up — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The next best alternative" in your own words.
**Answer:** The opportunity cost of a decision is the value of the next best alternative given up.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Measured in goods, not money" in your own words.
**Answer:** Opportunity cost is measured in goods and services, not money.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Computing it from a table" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the worked X–Y table, the opportunity cost per unit of X rises: 5, then 15, then 20 units of Y.
