# Hands-on — Opportunity cost — the next best thing you gave up

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
With two piles of counters (cloth and sugar), act out moving along a production table: each time you gain cloth, move the sugar you gave up into a 'cost' bowl and say its amount.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a fork in a road, one path taken and the other fading, labelled with what was given up — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
