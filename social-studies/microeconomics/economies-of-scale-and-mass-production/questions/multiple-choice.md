# Economies of scale and mass production — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About la producción en línea y las economías de escala — the lesson teaches that —
a) Assembly-line mass production brought economies of scale.
b) A very small number of plants could supply consumption for the whole world.
c) With global marketing and maintenance systems.
**Answer: a) Assembly-line mass production brought economies of scale.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About la producción en línea y las economías de escala — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) With global marketing and maintenance systems.
b) A very small number of plants could supply consumption for the whole world.
c) Assembly-line mass production brought economies of scale.
**Answer: b) A very small number of plants could supply consumption for the whole world.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About la producción en línea y las economías de escala — more — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) Assembly-line mass production brought economies of scale.
b) A very small number of plants could supply consumption for the whole world.
c) With global marketing and maintenance systems.
**Answer: c) With global marketing and maintenance systems.**
