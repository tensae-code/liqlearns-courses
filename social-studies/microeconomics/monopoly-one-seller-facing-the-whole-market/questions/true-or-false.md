# Monopoly — one seller facing the whole market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A monopolist is the whole industry, with no rivals and no close substitutes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A monopolist's marginal revenue lies below its demand curve because selling more requires cutting the price on all units.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A monopolist's total revenue first rises and then falls as the price is progressively cut.
**Answer: True**
