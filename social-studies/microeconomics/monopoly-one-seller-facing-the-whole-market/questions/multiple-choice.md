# Monopoly — one seller facing the whole market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "One seller is the industry"?
a) one firm is the industry: no rivals, no close substitutes, entry blocked  b) MR lies below the downward demand curve and falls twice as fast  c) cost curves unchanged; TR rises then falls as price is cut
**Answer: a) one firm is the industry: no rivals, no close substitutes, entry blocked**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Demand slopes down, so MR lies below it"?
a) one firm is the industry: no rivals, no close substitutes, entry blocked  b) cost curves unchanged; TR rises then falls as price is cut  c) MR lies below the downward demand curve and falls twice as fast
**Answer: c) MR lies below the downward demand curve and falls twice as fast**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Costs as usual, revenue rises then falls"?
a) MR lies below the downward demand curve and falls twice as fast  b) cost curves unchanged; TR rises then falls as price is cut  c) one firm is the industry: no rivals, no close substitutes, entry blocked
**Answer: b) cost curves unchanged; TR rises then falls as price is cut**
