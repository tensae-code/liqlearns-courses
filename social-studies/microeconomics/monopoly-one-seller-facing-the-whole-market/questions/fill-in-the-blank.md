# Monopoly — one seller facing the whole market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The monopolist is not one firm in an industry — the monopolist is the whole _____.
**Answer: industry**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A monopolist's marginal revenue curve lies _____ its demand curve.
**Answer: below**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** As a monopolist cuts price further and further, total revenue first rises and then _____.
**Answer: falls**
