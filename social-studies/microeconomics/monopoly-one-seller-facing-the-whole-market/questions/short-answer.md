# Monopoly — one seller facing the whole market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "One seller is the industry" in your own words.
**Answer:** A monopolist is the whole industry, with no rivals and no close substitutes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Demand slopes down, so MR lies below it" in your own words.
**Answer:** A monopolist's marginal revenue lies below its demand curve because selling more requires cutting the price on all units.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Costs as usual, revenue rises then falls" in your own words.
**Answer:** A monopolist's total revenue first rises and then falls as the price is progressively cut.
