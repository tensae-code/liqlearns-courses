# Hands-on — Monopoly — one seller facing the whole market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
One learner is the monopolist; the class is the town. The monopolist offers quantities at falling prices on cards, and the class tracks total revenue on the board — watching it rise, then fall.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a downward demand curve with the MR curve beneath it, high-output/low-price and low-output/high-price points marked — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
