# Government — taxing, spending, providing services — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government provides collective services _____ — police, education, health, sanitation and courts.
**Answer: free**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government's income is mainly _____ levied on households and firms.
**Answer: taxes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The primary purpose of a tax system is to raise _____ for public expenditure.
**Answer: revenue**
