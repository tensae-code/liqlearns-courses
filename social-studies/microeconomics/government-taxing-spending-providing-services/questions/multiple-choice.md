# Government — taxing, spending, providing services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What government provides"?
a) collective services provided free — police, education, health, sanitation, courts  b) income mainly from direct and indirect taxes on households and firms  c) raising revenue for public expenditure; every tax sustains public investment
**Answer: a) collective services provided free — police, education, health, sanitation, courts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Taxes pay for it"?
a) collective services provided free — police, education, health, sanitation, courts  b) raising revenue for public expenditure; every tax sustains public investment  c) income mainly from direct and indirect taxes on households and firms
**Answer: c) income mainly from direct and indirect taxes on households and firms**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the tax system exists"?
a) income mainly from direct and indirect taxes on households and firms  b) raising revenue for public expenditure; every tax sustains public investment  c) collective services provided free — police, education, health, sanitation, courts
**Answer: b) raising revenue for public expenditure; every tax sustains public investment**
