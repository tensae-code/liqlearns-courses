# Government — taxing, spending, providing services — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government provides collective services free, such as police, education, health, sanitation and courts.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government provides collective services free, such as police, education, health, sanitation and courts.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (What government provides); sub lesson 2 says: Government's income is mainly taxes — direct and indirect — levied on households and firms.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The primary purpose of a tax system is to raise revenue for public expenditure.
**Answer: True**
