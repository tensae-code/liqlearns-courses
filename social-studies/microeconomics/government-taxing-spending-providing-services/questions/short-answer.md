# Government — taxing, spending, providing services — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What government provides" in your own words.
**Answer:** Government provides collective services free, such as police, education, health, sanitation and courts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Taxes pay for it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Government's income is mainly taxes — direct and indirect — levied on households and firms.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the tax system exists" in your own words.
**Answer:** The primary purpose of a tax system is to raise revenue for public expenditure.
