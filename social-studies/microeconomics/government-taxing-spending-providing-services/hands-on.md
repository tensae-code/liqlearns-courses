# Hands-on — Government — taxing, spending, providing services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
With play money, act the flow: two learners pay 'taxes' to a third, who then 'provides' a drawn school and clinic for all. Narrate each step.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — tax arrows flowing in from households and firms, service arrows — school, clinic, police — flowing back out — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
