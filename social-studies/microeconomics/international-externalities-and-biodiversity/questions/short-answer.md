# International externalities and biodiversity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes an externality international?
**Answer:** Its effects fall on people in other countries, across the border.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is biodiversity?
**Answer:** The variety of living species, a shared inheritance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who partners on these problems in the book?
**Answer:** High-income and low-income countries, together.
