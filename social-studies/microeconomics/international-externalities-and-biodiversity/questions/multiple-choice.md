# International externalities and biodiversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An international externality is —
a) harm (or benefit) crossing a border onto others  b) a purely local spillover  c) a domestic tax
**Answer: a) harm (or benefit) crossing a border onto others**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Biodiversity loss is an international problem because —
a) species loss in one country impoverishes all  b) species stay inside borders  c) only zoos are affected
**Answer: a) species loss in one country impoverishes all**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** International externalities require —
a) partnership between countries  b) one nation acting alone  c) no action
**Answer: a) partnership between countries**
