## Sub lesson 1 — Externalities that cross borders

Some **externalities cross borders** — pollution and species loss in one country **harm others**, so the problem is **international**, not national.

## Sub lesson 2 — Biodiversity

**Biodiversity** — the variety of living species — is a shared inheritance whose loss is an **international externality**: one country's clearing can impoverish all.

## Sub lesson 3 — No nation can solve it alone

**No nation can solve these alone** — the book shows **high-income and low-income countries partnering** on international environmental problems.
