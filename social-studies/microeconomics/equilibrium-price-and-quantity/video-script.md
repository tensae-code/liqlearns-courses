# Video script — Equilibrium price and quantity — where the curves cross

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Market equilibrium is the situation where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. At every other price the two sides disagree; at this one price they match exactly, so neither side pushes the price away.

The price at which the quantities are equal is the equilibrium price; the amount bought and sold at that price is the equilibrium quantity. Price names the level; quantity names the trade that happens there.

Graphically, both are read off the intersection point of the demand and supply curves: across to the price axis for the price, down to the quantity axis for the quantity. And the equilibrium moves over time as demand and supply patterns change.
