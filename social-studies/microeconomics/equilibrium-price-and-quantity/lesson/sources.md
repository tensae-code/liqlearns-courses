Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Economic & Legal Education): the macro topics are taught from Vietnam's own statistics and history — the CPI chart 2016–2021, the 775% inflation of 1986, COVID-era unemployment of 3.2%, the Q3 2022 labour-market bulletin — and the State's controlling role is quoted from the Law on the State Bank and the Labour Code.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Grade 11 Economics Year 11 Part 1): A full direct-approach course built on the circular flow, with Fiji examples throughout — ginger supply curves, Fiji import tables, quarry and land-rent income, the Attu family budget, Fiji credit facilities, sugar-industry production stages and Reserve Bank of Fiji policy tools.

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium.

Albania's own way (Albania Grade 12 Ekonomia 12 Teacher Guide): a full Grade 12 course that runs micro to macro and then turns practical — business ethics, success skills and personal finance close the course; Albanian anchors throughout (Bank of Albania videos for the personal-budget lesson, the Albanian government budget in the government chapter, the types of money in circulation in Albania). France's own way (SES 2de, Manuels Libres): SES teaches economics, sociology and political science as one subject from Seconde; the economics is built from data and cases (INSEE, the France Relance plan read as a Keynesian model, housing and oil prices, the gender pay gap in spreadsheets) with a synthesis and exercise for nearly every notion.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 10 Aganina): Economics is taught inside an entrepreneurship course — market theory arrives as the theory an entrepreneur needs, with tenge-denominated supply examples (a potato supply curve at 50-70 tenge), world oil prices deciding whether an Almaty-region furniture workshop profits, and Kazakhstan's own post-Soviet transition as the opening history.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 10 Duisenkhanov Part 1): the same national curriculum taught in a three-lesson rhythm — one theory lesson, one game, one Kazakhstani or world business case (Rocket Study, Yuframe, Shymbulak, Elon Musk) — so every Economics concept is immediately played and then applied.

Malaysia's own way (Malaysia Grade 10 Ekonomi Tingkatan 4): a full KSSM economics course in Malay with bilingual BM–BI glossaries, ringgit-denominated tables throughout, and Malaysian institutions (Bank Negara, KWSP/EPF, PERKESO) as the living examples.
