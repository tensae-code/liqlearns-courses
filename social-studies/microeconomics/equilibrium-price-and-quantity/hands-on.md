# Hands-on — Equilibrium price and quantity — where the curves cross

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two lines of learners — buyers wanting more as price falls, sellers offering more as price rises — shuffle along a price line until the counts match; the class calls 'equilibrium' at the match.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — crossing demand and supply curves with dashed lines from the crossing point to a price and a quantity axis — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
