# Equilibrium price and quantity — where the curves cross — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market equilibrium is where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Market equilibrium is where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Where the quantities are equal); sub lesson 2 says: The price where quantities are equal is the equilibrium price, and the amount traded there is the equilibrium quantity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Equilibrium price and quantity are read off the intersection point of the demand and supply curves.
**Answer: True**
