# Equilibrium price and quantity — where the curves cross — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Where the quantities are equal" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market equilibrium is where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The two readings" in your own words.
**Answer:** The price where quantities are equal is the equilibrium price, and the amount traded there is the equilibrium quantity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reading it off the graph" in your own words.
**Answer:** Equilibrium price and quantity are read off the intersection point of the demand and supply curves.
