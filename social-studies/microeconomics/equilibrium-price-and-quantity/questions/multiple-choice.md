# Equilibrium price and quantity — where the curves cross — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Where the quantities are equal"?
a) quantity demanded equals quantity supplied  b) the equilibrium price, and the equilibrium quantity traded at it  c) read off the intersection point of the two curves; it moves as patterns change
**Answer: a) quantity demanded equals quantity supplied**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The two readings"?
a) quantity demanded equals quantity supplied  b) read off the intersection point of the two curves; it moves as patterns change  c) the equilibrium price, and the equilibrium quantity traded at it
**Answer: c) the equilibrium price, and the equilibrium quantity traded at it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Reading it off the graph"?
a) the equilibrium price, and the equilibrium quantity traded at it  b) read off the intersection point of the two curves; it moves as patterns change  c) quantity demanded equals quantity supplied
**Answer: b) read off the intersection point of the two curves; it moves as patterns change**
