# Equilibrium price and quantity — where the curves cross — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At market equilibrium, quantity demanded _____ quantity supplied.
**Answer: equals**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The amount bought and sold at the equilibrium price is the equilibrium _____.
**Answer: quantity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Both equilibrium values are read off the _____ point of the curves.
**Answer: intersection**
