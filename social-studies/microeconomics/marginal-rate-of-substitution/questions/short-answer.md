# Marginal rate of substitution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What the MRS means" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MRS is the units of one good a consumer will give up for one more unit of another, staying equally satisfied.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The falling MRS — 4, then 3, then 1.5" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the book's table the MRS falls step by step: 4, then 3, then 1.5.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Diminishing MRS makes the curve convex" in your own words.
**Answer:** A diminishing MRS makes the indifference curve convex to the origin.
