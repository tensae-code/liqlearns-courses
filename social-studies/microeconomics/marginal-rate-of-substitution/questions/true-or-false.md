# Marginal rate of substitution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The MRS is the units of one good a consumer will give up for one more unit of another, staying equally satisfied.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's table the MRS falls step by step: 4, then 3, then 1.5.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A diminishing MRS makes the indifference curve convex to the origin.
**Answer: True**
