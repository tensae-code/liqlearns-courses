# Marginal rate of substitution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rate at which a consumer swaps one good for another at constant satisfaction is the marginal rate of _____.
**Answer: substitution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As a consumer moves down an indifference curve, the marginal rate of substitution _____ — 4, then 3, then 1.5 in the book's table.
**Answer: falls**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Because the MRS diminishes, the indifference curve is _____ to the origin.
**Answer: convex**
