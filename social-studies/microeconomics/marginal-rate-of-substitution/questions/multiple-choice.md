# Marginal rate of substitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What the MRS means"?
a) units of Y given up for one more unit of X, satisfaction unchanged  b) the MRS falls 4, then 3, then 1.5 as bananas grow plentiful and oranges scarce  c) the falling MRS is the flattening slope that makes the curve convex
**Answer: a) units of Y given up for one more unit of X, satisfaction unchanged**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The falling MRS — 4, then 3, then 1.5"?
a) units of Y given up for one more unit of X, satisfaction unchanged  b) the falling MRS is the flattening slope that makes the curve convex  c) the MRS falls 4, then 3, then 1.5 as bananas grow plentiful and oranges scarce
**Answer: c) the MRS falls 4, then 3, then 1.5 as bananas grow plentiful and oranges scarce**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Diminishing MRS makes the curve convex"?
a) the MRS falls 4, then 3, then 1.5 as bananas grow plentiful and oranges scarce  b) the falling MRS is the flattening slope that makes the curve convex  c) units of Y given up for one more unit of X, satisfaction unchanged
**Answer: b) the falling MRS is the flattening slope that makes the curve convex**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Moving down the book's orange-banana curve, the MRS takes which sequence of values?
a) 4, then 3, then 1.5  b) 1.5, then 3, then 4  c) 4, then 4, then 4
**Answer: a) 4, then 3, then 1.5** — The MRS diminishes along the curve: 4, then 3, then 1.5.
