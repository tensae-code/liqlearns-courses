# Hands-on — Marginal rate of substitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Trade real objects with a partner along a curve: start by swapping 4 counters of one colour for 1 of another, then 3 for 1, then fewer — saying the MRS aloud at each swap.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one curve with three chords of visibly different steepness labelled with their MRS values — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
