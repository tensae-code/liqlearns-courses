# Production — turning inputs into outputs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production combines inputs to create an output for consumption.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Production combines inputs to create an output for consumption.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Inputs in, output out); sub lesson 2 says: Inputs are the economic resources used: labour, capital, land and entrepreneurial ability.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tangible outputs can be seen and touched; intangible outputs like training and health care are valuable but not physical.
**Answer: True**
