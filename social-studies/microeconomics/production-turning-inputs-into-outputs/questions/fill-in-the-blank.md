# Production — turning inputs into outputs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production combines _____ to create an output for consumption.
**Answer: inputs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inputs are the economic _____ used in production.
**Answer: resources**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurance, consultancy and training are _____ outputs — valuable but not physical.
**Answer: intangible**
