# Production — turning inputs into outputs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Inputs in, output out" in your own words.
**Answer:** Production combines inputs to create an output for consumption.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What the inputs are" in your own words.
**Answer:** Inputs are the economic resources used: labour, capital, land and entrepreneurial ability.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Tangible and intangible outputs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Tangible outputs can be seen and touched; intangible outputs like training and health care are valuable but not physical.
