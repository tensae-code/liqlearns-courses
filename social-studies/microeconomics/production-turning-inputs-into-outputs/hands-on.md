# Hands-on — Production — turning inputs into outputs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Mime a production line with real objects: learners pass a raw item along, each adding one input ('labour', 'a tool', 'land's gift'), until an 'output' is held up and classified.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — an input–process–output strip for wheat, then a tangible car beside an intangible training service — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
