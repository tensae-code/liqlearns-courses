# Video script — Production — turning inputs into outputs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Production combines inputs to create an output for consumption — inputs converted into outputs that give more satisfaction. The book's example: wheat grown on a plot with labour, capital and seed.

Inputs are the economic resources used — labour, capital, land and entrepreneurial ability. The same four factors from Unit 3 return here in their working clothes, inside a real production process.

Outputs divide into tangible outputs — physical things that can be seen and touched, like buildings, machinery and cars — and intangible outputs, valuable but not physical: insurance, consultancy, software, training and health care.
