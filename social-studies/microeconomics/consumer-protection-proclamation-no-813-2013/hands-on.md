# Hands-on — Consumer protection — Proclamation No. 813/2013

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rights in the market
Write three market scenes, each breaking a different one of the five rights. Exchange scenes and name the broken right and the malpractice in each.

## Check a label
Take one packaged product. List everything its label tells you, everything it does not, and which consumer right covers each gap.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
