# Consumer protection — Proclamation No. 813/2013 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sellers exploit buyers through false advertising, adulterated goods, wrong weights and hidden terms.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's consumer protection law is Proclamation No. 813/2013.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The guaranteed rights are to information, to choose, to safety, to be heard and to redress.
**Answer: True**
