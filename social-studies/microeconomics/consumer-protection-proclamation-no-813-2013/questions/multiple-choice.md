# Consumer protection — Proclamation No. 813/2013 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "How sellers can exploit buyers"?
a) Sellers exploit buyers through false advertising, adulterated goods, wrong weights and hidden terms  b) Ethiopia's consumer protection law is Proclamation No. 813/2013  c) The guaranteed rights are to information, to choose, to safety, to be heard and to redress
**Answer: a) Sellers exploit buyers through false advertising, adulterated goods, wrong weights and hidden terms**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's answer — Proclamation No. 813/2013"?
a) Ethiopia's consumer protection law is Proclamation No. 813/2013  b) The guaranteed rights are to information, to choose, to safety, to be heard and to redress  c) Sellers exploit buyers through false advertising, adulterated goods, wrong weights and hidden terms
**Answer: a) Ethiopia's consumer protection law is Proclamation No. 813/2013**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The rights it guarantees"?
