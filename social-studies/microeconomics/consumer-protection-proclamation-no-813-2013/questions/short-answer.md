# Consumer protection — Proclamation No. 813/2013 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "How sellers can exploit buyers" in your own words.
**Answer:** Sellers exploit buyers through false advertising, adulterated goods, wrong weights and hidden terms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Ethiopia's answer — Proclamation No. 813/2013" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia's consumer protection law is Proclamation No. 813/2013.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The rights it guarantees" in your own words.
**Answer:** The guaranteed rights are to information, to choose, to safety, to be heard and to redress.
