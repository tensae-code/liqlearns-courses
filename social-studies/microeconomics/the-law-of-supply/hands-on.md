# Hands-on — The law of supply

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
One learner is the seller: at each price called, they load that many sacks onto a mat. The class watches the pile grow with price — supply's upward slope in sacks.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the seller's oranges schedule beside its upward-sloping curve — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
