# The law of supply

## Do it first

A seller brings 60 kilos of oranges at Birr 10 a kilo, and 100 kilos at Birr 30. Price rose — what did his offered quantity do? Compare with demand's answer to the same question.

## Sub lesson 1 — The law stated

Ceteris paribus, price and quantity supplied move directly together: higher price, higher quantity supplied; lower price, lower quantity supplied. Supply answers price in the same direction — the exact opposite of demand.

## Sub lesson 2 — Schedule and curve

A supply schedule tabulates the law: the worked seller's schedule for oranges rises from 60 kg per week at Birr 10 per kg to 100 kg at Birr 30. The supply curve graphs the schedule upward to the right.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why sellers answer this way

A higher price makes selling more worthwhile: it covers the cost of bringing extra quantity and rewards the effort. That is why the offered quantity grows with price while demanded quantity shrinks — the two laws face each other across the market.
