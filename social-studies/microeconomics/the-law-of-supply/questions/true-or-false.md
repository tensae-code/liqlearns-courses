# The law of supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ceteris paribus, price and quantity supplied move directly together — higher price, higher quantity supplied.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ceteris paribus, price and quantity supplied move directly together — higher price, higher quantity supplied.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The law stated); sub lesson 2 says: The seller's supply schedule rises from 60 kg per week at Birr 10 per kg to 100 kg at Birr 30, and the supply curve slopes upward.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A higher price makes it more worthwhile to bring extra quantity to market.
**Answer: True**
