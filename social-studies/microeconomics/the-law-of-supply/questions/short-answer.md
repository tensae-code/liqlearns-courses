# The law of supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The law stated" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ceteris paribus, price and quantity supplied move directly together — higher price, higher quantity supplied.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Schedule and curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The seller's supply schedule rises from 60 kg per week at Birr 10 per kg to 100 kg at Birr 30, and the supply curve slopes upward.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why sellers answer this way" in your own words.
**Answer:** A higher price makes it more worthwhile to bring extra quantity to market.
