# Hands-on — Short-run equilibrium — MR equals MC

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay a price string across paper cost curves twice: once where it cuts MC falling, once rising. Act out expanding output past each crossing and say what happens to profit in each case.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three panels side by side — price line above, at, and below the AC minimum, profit and loss rectangles shaded — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
