# Short-run equilibrium — MR equals MC — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The two conditions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Short-run equilibrium requires MR = MC with MC rising, cutting MR from below.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "For the competitive firm — price equals MC" in your own words.
**Answer:** For a competitive firm the equilibrium rule is price equals marginal cost.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Profit, break-even or loss" in your own words.
**Answer:** Price above average cost at the equilibrium output gives profit; at minimum AC, break-even; below, loss.
