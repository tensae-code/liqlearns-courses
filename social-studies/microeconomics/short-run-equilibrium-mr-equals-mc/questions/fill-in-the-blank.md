# Short-run equilibrium — MR equals MC — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The second equilibrium condition is that MC is _____, cutting MR from below.
**Answer: rising**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For a price-taking firm, equilibrium output is where price equals _____.
**Answer: marginal cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit, break-even or loss is read by comparing the price with _____ at the equilibrium output.
**Answer: average cost**
