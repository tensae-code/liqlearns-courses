# Short-run equilibrium — MR equals MC — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The two conditions"?
a) MR = MC with MC rising and cutting from below; the falling crossing is rejected  b) with MR equal to price, the rule collapses to P = MC  c) price above AC: profit; at minimum AC: break-even; below AC: loss
**Answer: a) MR = MC with MC rising and cutting from below; the falling crossing is rejected**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "For the competitive firm — price equals MC"?
a) MR = MC with MC rising and cutting from below; the falling crossing is rejected  b) price above AC: profit; at minimum AC: break-even; below AC: loss  c) with MR equal to price, the rule collapses to P = MC
**Answer: c) with MR equal to price, the rule collapses to P = MC**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Profit, break-even or loss"?
a) with MR equal to price, the rule collapses to P = MC  b) price above AC: profit; at minimum AC: break-even; below AC: loss  c) MR = MC with MC rising and cutting from below; the falling crossing is rejected
**Answer: b) price above AC: profit; at minimum AC: break-even; below AC: loss**
