# Video script — Formal and informal business activities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Informal and formal business activities distinguished, with examples of each and a survey of all the businesses in the local community.

Formal versus informal economic activities defined with local examples, ranked by their economic and social impact on people's livelihoods, and how to sustain both kinds.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG: Grade 5 Trade is built on producers and consumers in the local community — consumer rights, a project to advertise a product, formal versus informal business activities, a fund-raising production project, and communication in trade traced from conch shell and kundu drum to mobile phone, internet and media advertising.
