# Formal and informal business activities — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Informal and formal business activities distinguished, with examples"?
**Answer:** Informal and formal business activities distinguished, with examples of each and a survey of all the businesses in the local community.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Formal versus informal economic activities defined with local"?
**Answer:** Formal versus informal economic activities defined with local examples, ranked by their economic and social impact on people's livelihoods, and how to sustain both kinds.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG: Grade 5 Trade is built on producers and consumers in the local community — consumer rights, a project to advertise a product, formal versus informal business activities, a fund-raising production project, and communication in trade traced from conch shell and kundu drum to mobile phone, internet and media advertising.
