# Formal and informal business activities — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Informal and formal business activities _____, with examples of each and a survey of all the businesses in the local community.
**Answer: distinguished**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Formal versus informal economic activities defined with local examples, ranked by their economic and social impact on people's _____, and how to sustain both kinds.
**Answer: livelihoods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG: Grade 5 Trade is built on producers and consumers in the local community — consumer rights, a project to advertise a product, formal versus informal business activities, a fund-raising production project, and _____ in trade traced from conch shell and kundu drum to mobile phone, internet and media advertising.
**Answer: communication**
