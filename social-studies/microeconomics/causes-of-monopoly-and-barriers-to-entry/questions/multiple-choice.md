# Causes of monopoly and barriers to entry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A monopoly survives because of —
a) high demand
b) barriers that keep rivals out
c) low costs alone
**Answer: b) barriers that keep rivals out**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A patent is an example of a —
a) resource barrier
b) legal barrier
c) scale barrier
**Answer: b) legal barrier**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A water network is the classic case of monopoly by —
a) patent
b) scale — one network serves the market cheapest
c) resource ownership
**Answer: b) scale — one network serves the market cheapest**
