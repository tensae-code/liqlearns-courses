# Causes of monopoly and barriers to entry — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monopoly is protected by _____ to entry.
**Answer: barriers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An exclusive licence granted by the state is a _____ barrier.
**Answer: legal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where one firm serves the whole market cheapest, monopoly rests on _____ barriers.
**Answer: scale**
