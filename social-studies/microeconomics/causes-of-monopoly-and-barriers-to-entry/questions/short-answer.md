# Causes of monopoly and barriers to entry — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What keeps a monopoly a monopoly?
**Answer:** Barriers to entry that prevent rival firms from entering its market.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish legal from resource barriers.
**Answer:** Legal — entry forbidden by law or exclusive grant; resource — one firm controls the essential input, so rivals cannot produce.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain the scale barrier.
**Answer:** Efficient size is so large relative to the market that one firm is cheapest alone; entrants must match that scale or lose on cost.
