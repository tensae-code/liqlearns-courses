# Causes of monopoly and barriers to entry — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Remove the barrier to entry and monopoly begins to leak away.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Owning the only spring a drink is made from is a resource barrier.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An entrant facing a scale barrier must enter huge or enter uncompetitive.
**Answer: True**
