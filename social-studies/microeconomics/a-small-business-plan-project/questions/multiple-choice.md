# A small business plan project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A business idea, in the book, starts from —
a) an unmet local need  b) a copied foreign firm  c) a loan application
**Answer: a) an unmet local need**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which three things does the book's business plan set out?
a) name, logo and slogan  b) goal, organisation and production plan  c) price, advert and shop sign
**Answer: b) goal, organisation and production plan**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The plan's financial calculation covers —
a) income only  b) costs and wages only  c) income, costs, profit and tax
**Answer: c) income, costs, profit and tax**
