# A small business plan project — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The small business project is a group project pitched for local investment.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The business plan needs no production plan if the idea is good.
**Answer: False — the plan sets out goal, organisation and production plan.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's case study is a riddle-recording kindergarten.
**Answer: True**
