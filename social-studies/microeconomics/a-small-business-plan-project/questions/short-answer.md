# A small business plan project — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the book's small business project begin?
**Answer:** With a business idea — an unmet local need the business will meet.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the parts of the business plan the book requires.
**Answer:** The goal, the organisation, the production plan, and the financial calculation of income, costs, profit and tax.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is the finished project presented, and what case study does the book use?
**Answer:** As a group project pitched for local investment; the case study is a riddle-recording kindergarten.
