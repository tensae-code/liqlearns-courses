# A small business plan project

## Do it first

Think of something your own local area needs that nobody provides. Before you read on, say what it is — that gap is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — The business idea — an unmet local need

Mongolia's Grade 8 book closes its economy chapter with a project, 3.3 Small business project, and it starts where real businesses start: a **business idea** — an **unmet local need**. Before any plan is written, the group finds something their own local area needs that nobody is providing, and builds the business on that gap.

## Sub lesson 2 — The business plan — goal, organisation and production plan

The idea becomes a **business plan**. The book's plan sets out the business's **goal**, its **organisation** — how the business is set up and run — and its **production plan**: what will be made or done, and how. A plan that cannot say these three things is not yet a plan.

## Sub lesson 3 — The financial calculation — income, costs, profit and tax — pitched for investment

The plan ends in numbers: a **financial calculation of income, costs, profit and tax**. The project is done **as a group** and **pitched for local investment** — presented to win backing in the pupils' own area. The book's case study is a **riddle-recording kindergarten**: a small local idea, planned and costed like any business.

## Say it to your companion

Explain a small business plan project to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Using the lesson, write a one-line business idea for your own area, naming the unmet need it meets.

2. A group's plan has a goal and good figures but no production plan. Say what is missing, from the lesson.

3. Write the key words from memory — business idea, business plan, production plan, profit, tax — and write one sentence of your own for each.

4. Answer this lesson's questions from questions/ without the lesson open; re-read only the parts you missed.
