# Video script — A small business plan project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 8 book ends its economy chapter with a project, says the narrator: a small business plan. Start with an idea — an unmet local need. Write the plan: goal, organisation, production plan. Then the numbers: income, costs, profit and tax. Do it as a group, and pitch it for local investment — like the riddle-recording kindergarten in the book.
