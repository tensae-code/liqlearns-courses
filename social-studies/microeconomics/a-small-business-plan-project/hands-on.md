# Hands-on — A small business plan project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the gap
Walk or picture your own local area. List three needs nobody is meeting. Pick one and say, in one sentence, the business idea it gives you.

## Cost it roughly
For your idea, list what income it could bring in and what costs it would carry. The difference the lesson names is profit — say whether tax would still come out of it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
