# Lesson plan — Subsistence and cash-crop farming

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Subsistence and cash-crop farming
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Fiji, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Farming runs on a spectrum: subsistence farming grows only what the family and village need; extra crops can be sold or traded (semi-subsistence, as in Tonga); and a crop grown for sale can become a cash crop exported overseas, as Niue's taro is exported to New Zealand.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Pacific reader's own taro case study — subsistence farming, selling the extra, and the cash crop for export, told through Tonga, Niue and Tuvalu.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
