# Subsistence and cash-crop farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farming that grows only what the family and village need is _____ farming.
**Answer: subsistence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tonga practises _____-subsistence farming.
**Answer: semi**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Niue taro is exported to New _____.
**Answer: Zealand**
