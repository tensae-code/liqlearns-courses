# Subsistence and cash-crop farming — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is subsistence farming?
**Answer:** Farming that grows only what the family and village need — nothing is grown for a market.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is semi-subsistence farming different?
**Answer:** The family is still fed first, but extra crops are sold or traded — in Tonga, food is sold at market or exported.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What makes a crop a cash crop?
**Answer:** It is grown to be sold — as Niue taro is grown as a cash crop exported to New Zealand.
