# Subsistence and cash-crop farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsistence farming grows —
a) only what the family and village need  b) crops for export only  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) only what the family and village need**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Semi-subsistence farming, as Tonga practises it, means —
a) no farming  b) feeding the family first, with food also sold at market or exported  c) importing all food
**Answer: b) feeding the family first, with food also sold at market or exported**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Niue taro in the lesson is —
a) never eaten  b) a cash crop exported to New Zealand  c) grown only in New Zealand
**Answer: b) a cash crop exported to New Zealand**
