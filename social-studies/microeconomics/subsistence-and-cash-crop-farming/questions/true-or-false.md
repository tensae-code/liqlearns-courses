# Subsistence and cash-crop farming — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In subsistence farming, extra crops are the main purpose of the garden.
**Answer: False — subsistence farming grows only what the family and village need; selling the extra is the next step along the spectrum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Extra crops from a garden can be sold or traded.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The same crop, taro, can be a subsistence food or a cash crop depending on the purpose it is grown for.
**Answer: True**
