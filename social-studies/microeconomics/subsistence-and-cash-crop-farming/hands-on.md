# Hands-on — Subsistence and cash-crop farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort one harvest
Take one crop you know. Split a drawn harvest into three piles — eaten at home, sold at the local market, exported — and say which kind of farming each pile belongs to: subsistence, semi-subsistence or cash-crop farming.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a taro garden in the Pacific — one part of the harvest carried home for the family, one part taken to market, and one part packed for export overseas — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
