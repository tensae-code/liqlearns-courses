# Hands-on — Four market structures — the spectrum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Sort four shop cards along a rope from 'many sellers, no power over price' to 'one seller, sets the price'. Defend each card's place to a partner.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a four-stop spectrum bar from many sellers of identical goods to one seller alone — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
