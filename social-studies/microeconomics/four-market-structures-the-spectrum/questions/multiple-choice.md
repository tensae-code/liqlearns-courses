# Four market structures — the spectrum — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What a market structure is"?
a) structures classified by numbers and size, product type, and information flow  b) perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, pure monopoly — most to least competitive  c) a firm's place on the spectrum decides its price power, demand curve and long-run profits
**Answer: a) structures classified by numbers and size, product type, and information flow**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The four structures in order"?
a) structures classified by numbers and size, product type, and information flow  b) a firm's place on the spectrum decides its price power, demand curve and long-run profits  c) perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, pure monopoly — most to least competitive
**Answer: c) perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, pure monopoly — most to least competitive**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the spectrum matters"?
a) perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, pure monopoly — most to least competitive  b) a firm's place on the spectrum decides its price power, demand curve and long-run profits  c) structures classified by numbers and size, product type, and information flow
**Answer: b) a firm's place on the spectrum decides its price power, demand curve and long-run profits**
