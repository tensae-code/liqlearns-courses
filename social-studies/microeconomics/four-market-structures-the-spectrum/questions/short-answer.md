# Four market structures — the spectrum — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What a market structure is" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market structures are classified by the number and size of firms and consumers, the type of product, and the flow of information.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The four structures in order" in your own words.
**Answer:** The four structures run from perfect competition through monopolistic competition and oligopoly to pure monopoly.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the spectrum matters" in your own words.
**Answer:** A firm's position on the spectrum determines whether it takes the price as given or sets it.
