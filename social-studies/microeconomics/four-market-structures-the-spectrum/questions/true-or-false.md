# Four market structures — the spectrum — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market structures are classified by the number and size of firms and consumers, the type of product, and the flow of information.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four structures run from perfect competition through monopolistic competition and oligopoly to pure monopoly.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A firm's position on the spectrum determines whether it takes the price as given or sets it.
**Answer: True**
