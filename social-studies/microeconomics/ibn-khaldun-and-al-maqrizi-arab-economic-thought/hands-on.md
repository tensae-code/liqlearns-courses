# Hands-on — Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — Arab economic thought

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with prices
Pick one good sold near your home. Say out loud, in Ibn Khaldun's way, what would happen to its price if it suddenly became scarce — and then if it became plentiful. Then say, in Al-Maqrizi's way, what happens to prices when money itself becomes plentiful.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — two scholars at work — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
