# Video script — Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — Arab economic thought

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ibn Khaldun explained how a society's work is divided — the division of labour — and taught that the value of a thing is determined by the labour in it. He also saw that prices are set by supply and demand: when a good is scarce its price rises, and when it is plentiful its price falls.

Al-Maqrizi looked at money itself. He connected the quantity of money to prices: when money becomes plentiful, prices rise. Two thinkers, writing in Arabic centuries ago, were already reasoning like economists.
