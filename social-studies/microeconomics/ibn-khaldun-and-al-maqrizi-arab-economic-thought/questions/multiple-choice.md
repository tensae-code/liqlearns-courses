# Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — Arab economic thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For Ibn Khaldun, the value of a thing is determined by —
a) its weight in gold  b) labour  c) the ruler's decree
**Answer: b) labour**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ibn Khaldun saw prices as set by —
a) supply and demand  b) custom alone  c) the quantity of money
**Answer: a) supply and demand**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Al-Maqrizi connected price rises to —
a) bad harvests only  b) the division of labour  c) the quantity of money
**Answer: c) the quantity of money**
