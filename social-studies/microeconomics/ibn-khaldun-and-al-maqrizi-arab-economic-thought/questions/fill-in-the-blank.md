# Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — Arab economic thought — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ibn Khaldun explained the _____ of labour — a society producing more when work is split among people.
**Answer: division**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When a good is scarce, Ibn Khaldun saw its price _____; when plentiful, its price falls.
**Answer: rise (rises)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ connected the quantity of money to price rises.
**Answer: Al-Maqrizi**
