# Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — Arab economic thought — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things did Ibn Khaldun teach about value and prices?
**Answer:** Value is determined by labour, and prices are set by supply and demand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the division of labour raise what a society produces?
**Answer:** Because work split among people, each doing a part, produces far more than each person doing everything alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happens to prices when money becomes plentiful, in Al-Maqrizi's teaching?
**Answer:** Prices rise — the quantity of money is connected to price rises.
