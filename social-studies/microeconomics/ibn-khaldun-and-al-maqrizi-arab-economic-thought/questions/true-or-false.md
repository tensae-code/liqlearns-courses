# Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — Arab economic thought — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ibn Khaldun taught that value is determined by labour.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For Ibn Khaldun, prices never change, because custom fixes them.
**Answer: False — he saw prices set by supply and demand: scarce goods rise in price, plentiful goods fall.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Al-Maqrizi held that when money becomes plentiful, prices rise.
**Answer: True**
