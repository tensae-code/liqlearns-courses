**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Yemen).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 1, Lesson 1 — Historical development of economic thought — teaches: The Arab-Islamic thinkers Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi made lasting contributions to economics: Ibn Khaldun explained the division of labour, taught that value is determined by labour, and saw prices set by supply and demand; Al-Maqrizi connected the quantity of money to rises in prices. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
