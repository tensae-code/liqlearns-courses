# Video script — Is economics a science, an art, or both?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

As a science, economics builds a systematic body of knowledge that links cause and effect: when this changes, that follows. It describes what is, and because its links are tested, it can predict what is likely to happen next.

As an art, economics supplies principles and procedures for actually solving problems — unemployment, poverty and inflation are its named examples. Knowing why a problem happens is the science; the steps for fixing it are the art.

The book's verdict is that economics is both a science and an art at once. The same economist measures a problem scientifically, then turns artist to design the cure — neither half works well without the other.
