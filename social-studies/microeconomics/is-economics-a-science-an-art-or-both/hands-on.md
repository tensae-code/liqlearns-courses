# Hands-on — Is economics a science, an art, or both?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Find a newspaper or market price report. Read one measured fact aloud (the science), then say one action it suggests (the art), and explain the difference to a partner.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a split panel — observing and measuring on one side, fixing a problem on the other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
