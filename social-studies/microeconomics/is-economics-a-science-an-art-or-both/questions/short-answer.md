# Is economics a science, an art, or both? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Economics as a science" in your own words.
**Answer:** As a science, economics describes what is and can predict, because it links cause and effect.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Economics as an art" in your own words.
**Answer:** As an art, economics gives principles and procedures for solving problems such as unemployment, poverty and inflation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The verdict — both" in your own words.
**Answer:** The book's verdict is that economics is both a science and an art.
