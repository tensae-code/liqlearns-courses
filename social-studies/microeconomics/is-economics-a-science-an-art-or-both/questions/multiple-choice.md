# Is economics a science, an art, or both? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Economics as a science"?
a) a systematic body of knowledge linking cause and effect  b) principles and procedures for solving problems like unemployment, poverty and inflation  c) economics is both a science and an art
**Answer: a) a systematic body of knowledge linking cause and effect**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Economics as an art"?
a) a systematic body of knowledge linking cause and effect  b) economics is both a science and an art  c) principles and procedures for solving problems like unemployment, poverty and inflation
**Answer: c) principles and procedures for solving problems like unemployment, poverty and inflation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The verdict — both"?
a) principles and procedures for solving problems like unemployment, poverty and inflation  b) economics is both a science and an art  c) a systematic body of knowledge linking cause and effect
**Answer: b) economics is both a science and an art**
