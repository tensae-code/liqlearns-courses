# Is economics a science, an art, or both? — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As a science, economics describes what is and can predict, because it links cause and effect.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As a science, economics describes what is and can predict, because it links cause and effect.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Economics as a science); sub lesson 2 says: As an art, economics gives principles and procedures for solving problems such as unemployment, poverty and inflation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's verdict is that economics is both a science and an art.
**Answer: True**
