# Is economics a science, an art, or both? — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As a science, economics links _____ — when this changes, that follows.
**Answer: cause and effect**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Supplying principles for actually solving problems is economics as an _____.
**Answer: art**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's verdict is that economics is _____ — a science and an art.
**Answer: both**
