# The entrepreneurial idea and the market niche

## Do it first

Think of a small business you know. Before you read on, write the need it answers — and whether it was first with something new, or one more copy.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three questions the idea starts from

Bulgaria's Grade 6 book teaches: **the entrepreneurial idea starts from three questions — what do consumers need, what products should be offered, at what price should they be sold** — and **the market realisation of the products depends on the answers**. The idea is not a product looking for buyers; it is an answer to a need, at a price.

## Sub lesson 2 — Be first with something new — your own market niche

The book's advice to beginning businessmen: **you will be remembered much more easily with a new product than by proving you can make the known products better — that way you can create your own market niche in which you will be first**. And a warning with it: **if your product suits one group of people, do not count on everyone buying it**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Build on tendencies, not fashion — and price low at the same quality

The book closes the advice: **create products that will be sought for a long time — a stable business is built more on tendencies and less on fashion**; and **the lower the prices at the same quality, the more buyers there will be for your goods and services**. A lasting need, a first-place niche and a fair price — that is the whole recipe.
