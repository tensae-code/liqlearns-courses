# Video script — The entrepreneurial idea and the market niche

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every entrepreneurial idea starts from three questions: what do consumers need, what products should be offered, at what price should they be sold. Be remembered with a new product, not by copying known ones — that is how you open your own market niche and stand first in it. Build on tendencies, not fashion, and at the same quality, lower prices win more buyers.
