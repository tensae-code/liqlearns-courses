# The entrepreneurial idea and the market niche — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The entrepreneurial idea starts from three _____: what consumers need, what products to offer, at what price.
**Answer: questions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With a new product you can create your own market _____ in which you will be first.
**Answer: niche**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A stable business is built more on _____ and less on fashion.
**Answer: tendencies**
