# The entrepreneurial idea and the market niche — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The entrepreneurial idea starts from —
a) one question about price  b) three questions — what consumers need, what products to offer, at what price  c) copying a rival
**Answer: b) three questions — what consumers need, what products to offer, at what price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A beginner is remembered much more easily —
a) by making known products slightly better  b) with a new product, creating his own market niche  c) by advertising most
**Answer: b) with a new product, creating his own market niche**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A stable business is built more on —
a) fashion  b) tendencies  c) luck
**Answer: b) tendencies**
