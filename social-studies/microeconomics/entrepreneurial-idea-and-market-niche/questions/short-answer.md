# The entrepreneurial idea and the market niche — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the three questions of the entrepreneurial idea.
**Answer:** What do consumers need? What products should be offered? At what price should they be sold?

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the book advise a new product over making known products better?
**Answer:** Because you will be remembered much more easily with a new product — that way you create your own market niche in which you will be first.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two closing rules does the book give the beginner?
**Answer:** Create products that will be sought for a long time — a stable business is built more on tendencies and less on fashion; and the lower the prices at the same quality, the more buyers there will be.
