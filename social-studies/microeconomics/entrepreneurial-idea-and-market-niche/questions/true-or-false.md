# The entrepreneurial idea and the market niche — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The market realisation of the products depends on the answers to the three questions.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If your product suits one group of people, you can count on everyone buying it.
**Answer: False — the book warns the opposite: do not count on everyone buying it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At the same quality, lower prices bring more buyers.
**Answer: True**
