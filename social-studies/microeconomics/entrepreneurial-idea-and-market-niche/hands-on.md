# Hands-on — The entrepreneurial idea and the market niche

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Answer the three questions
Pick a need you have noticed around you. Write the three questions at the top of a page and answer each for one product idea, in full sentences.

## Find the niche
Name a known product sold near you. Now describe a new product beside it that would open a niche of its own — and name the one group of people it suits, without counting on everyone.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud — the three questions — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
