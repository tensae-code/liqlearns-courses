# Three kinds of resources — natural, human and transformed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three kinds of resources the book sorts everything into.
**Answer:** Natural resources, human resources and transformed (man-made) resources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one example of each kind from the book.
**Answer:** Natural: water (or sunlight, coal); human: a farmer (or teacher, nurse); transformed: a hammer (or tractor, bus).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the class learn the sorting in the book?
**Answer:** By picture-sorting in groups — sorting photographs of everything around them into the three kinds.
