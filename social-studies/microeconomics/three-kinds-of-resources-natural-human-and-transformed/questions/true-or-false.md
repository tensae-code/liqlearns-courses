# Three kinds of resources — natural, human and transformed — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coal is sorted as a natural resource in the book.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A nurse is a human resource.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A hammer is a natural resource given by nature.
**Answer: False** — a hammer is a transformed (man-made) resource; people made it.
