# Three kinds of resources — natural, human and transformed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a natural resource in the book's sorting?
a) a hammer  b) sunlight  c) a bus
**Answer: b) sunlight**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a human resource in the book's sorting?
a) coal  b) water  c) a teacher
**Answer: c) a teacher**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A tractor is a transformed resource because —
a) nature gives it ready-made  b) people made it  c) it is a person
**Answer: b) people made it**
