# Three kinds of resources — natural, human and transformed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water, sunlight and coal are _____ resources — given by nature.
**Answer: natural**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The farmer, the teacher and the nurse are _____ resources.
**Answer: human**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The hammer, the tractor and the bus are _____ resources — made by people.
**Answer: transformed**
