# Three kinds of resources — natural, human and transformed

## Do it first

Look around you and name everything you can see that people use: water, sunlight, a farmer, a hammer, money, a bus. Into how many kinds could you sort them? Say your sorting before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Natural resources — given by nature

Natural resources are the resources nature gives: water, sunlight and coal are the book's examples, found in the world around the class and sorted first in the picture-sorting.

## Sub lesson 2 — Human resources — the people who work

Human resources are people whose work society uses: the book names the farmer, the teacher and the nurse — a person is a resource because of the work they do for others.

## Sub lesson 3 — Transformed resources — made by people

Transformed, or man-made, resources are made by people out of other resources: the book names the hammer, the tractor and the bus — things that were produced, and that are then used to produce more.

## Say it to your companion

Explain three kinds of resources — natural, human and transformed to your companion in your own words, using the book's own examples. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
