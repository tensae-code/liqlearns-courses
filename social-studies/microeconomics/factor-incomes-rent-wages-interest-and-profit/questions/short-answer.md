# Factor incomes — rent, wages, interest and profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four factor incomes and the means of production each one pays.
**Answer:** Land — rent; labour — wages; capital — interest; organisation — profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which income does organisation earn?
**Answer:** Profit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book distinguish wages from salary?
**Answer:** Wages are paid for manual labour, by the employer; salary is the other form of payment for work — the book keeps the two apart.
