# Factor incomes — rent, wages, interest and profit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Labour earns wages.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Organisation earns interest.
**Answer: False** — organisation earns profit; capital earns interest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book distinguishes wages from salary.
**Answer: True**
