# Factor incomes — rent, wages, interest and profit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land earns _____, and labour earns _____.
**Answer: rent; wages**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital earns _____, and organisation earns _____.
**Answer: interest; profit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ are paid for manual labour by the employer, and are distinguished from salary.
**Answer: Wages**
