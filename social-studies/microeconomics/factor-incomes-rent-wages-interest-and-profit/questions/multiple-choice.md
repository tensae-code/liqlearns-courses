# Factor incomes — rent, wages, interest and profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land earns —
a) wages  b) rent  c) interest
**Answer: b) rent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital earns —
a) interest  b) profit  c) rent
**Answer: a) interest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book, wages are —
a) paid for manual labour, by the employer  b) the same word as salary  c) paid to the landowner
**Answer: a) paid for manual labour, by the employer**
