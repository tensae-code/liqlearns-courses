# Video script — Factor incomes — rent, wages, interest and profit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Land earns **rent**: the income paid for the use of land.

Capital earns **interest**: the income paid for the use of capital.

The book distinguishes wages from salary.
