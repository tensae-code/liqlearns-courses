# Factor incomes — rent, wages, interest and profit

## Do it first

A garment workshop uses land, workers, money and an organiser. Each of the four is paid — but each payment has its own name. Can you name any of them before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Rent and wages — land and labour are paid

Land earns **rent**: the income paid for the use of land. Labour earns **wages**: the income paid for work. In the book, wages are for manual labour and are paid by the employer.

## Sub lesson 2 — Interest and profit — capital and organisation are paid

Capital earns **interest**: the income paid for the use of capital. Organisation earns **profit**: the income that goes to the one who organises the production and brings the other means together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Wages and salary — how they differ

The book distinguishes wages from salary. **Wages** are paid for manual labour, by the employer. A **salary** is the other, regular payment for work — the two words are not used for the same payment.
