# Forms of business organisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the weak side of a one-person business?
**Answer: The owner carries every loss alone and can raise only as much money as one person has or can borrow.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why might owners choose a shared-capital form?
**Answer: Together they can raise a bigger start than one person could manage, and they can share the work and decisions.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the form more than a label?
**Answer: Because it changes the owners' rights and risks every day the business runs.**
