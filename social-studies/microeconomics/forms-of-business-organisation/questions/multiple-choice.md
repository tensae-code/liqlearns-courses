# Forms of business organisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a one-person business, who makes the decisions and keeps the profit?
a) a committee of all the workers  b) the single owner  c) the government office in the district
**Answer: b) the single owner**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a shared-capital form, the owners —
a) put money in together and own the business together  b) each run a separate business alone  c) never share the profit
**Answer: a) put money in together and own the business together**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does choosing a business form decide?
a) nothing that matters after the first day  b) only the colour of the shop sign  c) who owns, who decides, who shares profit and who carries loss
**Answer: c) who owns, who decides, who shares profit and who carries loss**
