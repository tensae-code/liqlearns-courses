# Forms of business organisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a one-person business, the single _____ makes the decisions and keeps the profit.
**Answer: owner**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a shared-capital form, two or more people put _____ in together.
**Answer: money (capital)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Choosing a form is choosing who owns, who decides, who shares the profit and who carries the _____.
**Answer: loss**
