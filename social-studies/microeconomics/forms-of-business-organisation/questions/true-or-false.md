# Forms of business organisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a one-person business the single owner decides and keeps the profit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a shared-capital form, the owners never share the profit.
**Answer: False — The owners in a shared-capital form do share the profit; sharing is the point of the form.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The form decides ownership, decisions, profit-sharing and who carries losses.
**Answer: True**
