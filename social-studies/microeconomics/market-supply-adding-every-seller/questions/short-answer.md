# Market supply — adding every seller — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The supply function" in your own words.
**Answer:** The supply function is QS = f(P): quantity supplied as a function of price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Adding every seller" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market supply is found by horizontally adding every seller's quantity supplied at each price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The market supply curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The market supply curve is the horizontal summation of individual supply curves.
