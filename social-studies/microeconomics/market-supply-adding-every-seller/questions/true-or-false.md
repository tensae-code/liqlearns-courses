# Market supply — adding every seller — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The supply function is QS = f(P): quantity supplied as a function of price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The supply function is QS = f(P): quantity supplied as a function of price.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The supply function); sub lesson 2 says: Market supply is found by horizontally adding every seller's quantity supplied at each price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market supply curve is the horizontal summation of individual supply curves.
**Answer: True**
