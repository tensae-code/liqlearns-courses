# Market supply — adding every seller — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The supply function"?
a) QS = f(P) — quantity supplied as a function of price  b) every seller's quantity added at the same price  c) the horizontal summation of individual supply curves, sloping upward
**Answer: a) QS = f(P) — quantity supplied as a function of price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Adding every seller"?
a) QS = f(P) — quantity supplied as a function of price  b) the horizontal summation of individual supply curves, sloping upward  c) every seller's quantity added at the same price
**Answer: c) every seller's quantity added at the same price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The market supply curve"?
a) every seller's quantity added at the same price  b) the horizontal summation of individual supply curves, sloping upward  c) QS = f(P) — quantity supplied as a function of price
**Answer: b) the horizontal summation of individual supply curves, sloping upward**
