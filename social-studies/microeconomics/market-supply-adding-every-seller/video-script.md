# Video script — Market supply — adding every seller

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The supply function states the supply relationship mathematically: QS = f(P) — quantity supplied as a function of price, other things equal. It is the supply twin of Qd = f(P), with the slope running the other way.

Market supply is the total all sellers offer at each price, found by horizontally adding every seller's quantity supplied at that price — the same sideways addition you used for market demand.

The market supply curve is the horizontal summation of individual supply curves: wider than any one seller's curve, and still sloping upward, because every curve inside it slopes upward.
