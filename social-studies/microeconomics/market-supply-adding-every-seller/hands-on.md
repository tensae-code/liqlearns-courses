# Hands-on — Market supply — adding every seller

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Sellers stand in a row holding quantity cards for one price; a recorder sums them aloud, price by price, until the market schedule stands complete on the board.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two seller curves adding sideways into one market supply curve, QS = f(P) beneath — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
