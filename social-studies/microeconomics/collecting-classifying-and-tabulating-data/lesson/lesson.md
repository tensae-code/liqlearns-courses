# Collecting, classifying and tabulating data

## Sub lesson 1 — Where economic data comes from

The Class 11 book names the sources of economic data: **government** sources, **semi-government** and **private** sources, **technical and research institutes**, and the reports of **international organisations**. Knowing the source is the first check on the numbers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Classifying data

Raw data is sorted into groups that share a feature — that is **classification**. Classified data can be compared group against group, which unclassified piles of numbers cannot.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tabulating data — simple and compound

**Tabulation** sets classified data out in a table. The book distinguishes **simple tabulation** (one feature) from **compound tabulation** (more than one), and gives the principles a good table follows.
