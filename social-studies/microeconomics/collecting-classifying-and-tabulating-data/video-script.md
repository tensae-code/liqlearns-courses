# Video script — Collecting, classifying and tabulating data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Collecting, classifying and tabulating data — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: economic data comes from government, semi-government, private, research and international sources; classification sorts raw data into groups that share a feature; simple tabulation uses one feature; compound tabulation uses more than one.
