# Collecting, classifying and tabulating data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the book's sources of economic data.
**Answer:** Government, semi-government, private, technical and research-institute sources, and international organisations' reports.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is classification of data?
**Answer:** Classification sorts raw data into groups that share a feature, so groups can be compared.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference between simple and compound tabulation?
**Answer:** Simple tabulation uses one feature; compound tabulation uses more than one.
