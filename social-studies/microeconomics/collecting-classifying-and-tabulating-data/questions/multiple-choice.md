# Collecting, classifying and tabulating data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About where economic data comes from — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic data comes from government, semi-government, private, research and international sources  b) classification sorts raw data into groups that share a feature  c) simple tabulation uses one feature; compound tabulation uses more than one
**Answer: a) economic data comes from government, semi-government, private, research and international sources**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About classifying data — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic data comes from government, semi-government, private, research and international sources  b) classification sorts raw data into groups that share a feature  c) simple tabulation uses one feature; compound tabulation uses more than one
**Answer: b) classification sorts raw data into groups that share a feature**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About tabulating data — simple and compound — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic data comes from government, semi-government, private, research and international sources  b) classification sorts raw data into groups that share a feature  c) simple tabulation uses one feature; compound tabulation uses more than one
**Answer: c) simple tabulation uses one feature; compound tabulation uses more than one**
