# Collecting, classifying and tabulating data — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reports of international organisations are one _____ of economic data.
**Answer: source**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sorting raw data into groups that share a feature is _____.
**Answer: classification**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tabulation with more than one feature is _____ tabulation.
**Answer: compound**
