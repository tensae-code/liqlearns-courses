# The industrial revolution machines wages and displaced artisans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two sides of mechanisation in source documents — _____ textile employers argue carding/spinning machines and the flying shuttle cut manual work by a third yet improved wages and commerce;
**Answer: Leeds**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The artisans' _____ petition answers that machines took artisans' livelihoods, left many without work or bread, and would let a few rich employers monopolise all business;
**Answer: 1794**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ commission adds industry and commerce growth alongside rising tax weight and merchandise prices pressing on wages;
**Answer: 1806**
