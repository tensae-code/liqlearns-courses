# The industrial revolution machines wages and displaced artisans — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the two sides of mechanisation in source documents?
**Answer:** The two sides of mechanisation in source documents — Leeds textile employers argue carding/spinning machines and the flying shuttle cut manual work by a third yet improved wages and commerce;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about the artisans' 1794 petition answers that machines took?
**Answer:** The artisans' 1794 petition answers that machines took artisans' livelihoods, left many without work or bread, and would let a few rich employers monopolise all business;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the 1806 commission adds industry and commerce growth?
**Answer:** The 1806 commission adds industry and commerce growth alongside rising tax weight and merchandise prices pressing on wages;
