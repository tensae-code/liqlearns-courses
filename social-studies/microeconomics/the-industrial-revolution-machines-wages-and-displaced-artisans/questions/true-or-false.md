# The industrial revolution machines wages and displaced artisans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two sides of mechanisation in source documents — Leeds textile employers argue carding/spinning machines and the flying shuttle cut manual work by a third yet improved wages and commerce;
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about the two sides of mechanisation in source documents.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about the artisans' 1794 petition answers that machines took; the two sides of mechanisation in source documents is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1806 commission adds industry and commerce growth alongside rising tax weight and merchandise prices pressing on wages;
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
