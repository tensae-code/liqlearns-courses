## Sub lesson 1 — The two sides of mechanisation in source documents

The two sides of mechanisation in source documents — Leeds textile employers argue carding/spinning machines and the flying shuttle cut manual work by a third yet improved wages and commerce;

## Sub lesson 2 — The artisans' 1794 petition answers that machines took

The artisans' 1794 petition answers that machines took artisans' livelihoods, left many without work or bread, and would let a few rich employers monopolise all business;

## Sub lesson 3 — The 1806 commission adds industry and commerce growth

The 1806 commission adds industry and commerce growth alongside rising tax weight and merchandise prices pressing on wages; a child-labour testimony shows the human cost
