# Social entrepreneurship — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Business run to lift a community, not only for profit, is _____ entrepreneurship.
**Answer: social**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's case of social entrepreneurship is Dr Akhtar Hameed _____.
**Answer: Khan**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Very small loans that let poor people start enterprises are called _____.
**Answer: microfinance**
