# Social entrepreneurship — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is social entrepreneurship?
**Answer:** Social entrepreneurship is business run to lift a community, not only for profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose work is the book's case of social entrepreneurship?
**Answer:** Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan's work is the book's case.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is microfinance?
**Answer:** Microfinance gives very small loans to people too poor for ordinary bank loans, so they can start small enterprises.
