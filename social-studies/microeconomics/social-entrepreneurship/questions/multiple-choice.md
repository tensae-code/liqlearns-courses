# Social entrepreneurship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About business run to lift a community — the lesson teaches that —
a) social entrepreneurship is business run to lift a community, not only for profit  b) Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan is the book's case of social entrepreneurship in action  c) microfinance gives very small loans so poor people can start small enterprises
**Answer: a) social entrepreneurship is business run to lift a community, not only for profit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About dr akhtar hameed khan — the lesson teaches that —
a) social entrepreneurship is business run to lift a community, not only for profit  b) Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan is the book's case of social entrepreneurship in action  c) microfinance gives very small loans so poor people can start small enterprises
**Answer: b) Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan is the book's case of social entrepreneurship in action**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About microfinance — small loans that start enterprises — the lesson teaches that —
a) social entrepreneurship is business run to lift a community, not only for profit  b) Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan is the book's case of social entrepreneurship in action  c) microfinance gives very small loans so poor people can start small enterprises
**Answer: c) microfinance gives very small loans so poor people can start small enterprises**
