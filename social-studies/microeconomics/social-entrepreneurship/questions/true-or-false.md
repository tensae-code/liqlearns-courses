# Social entrepreneurship — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Social entrepreneurship aims only at the owner's profit.
**Answer: False** — its aim is lifting the community, not only profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan is presented as a factory owner seeking profit only.
**Answer: False** — he is the book's case of community-lifting enterprise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Microfinance gives the largest loans to the richest borrowers.
**Answer: False** — it gives very small loans to people too poor for ordinary bank loans.
