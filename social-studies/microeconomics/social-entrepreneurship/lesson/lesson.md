# Social entrepreneurship

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Business run to lift a community

**Social entrepreneurship** is business run to lift a community, not only for profit. The Class 5 book teaches it as its own kind of entrepreneurship: the enterprise succeeds when the people around it rise.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan

The book's case is **Dr Akhtar Hameed Khan**, whose work showed how organised community effort and small loans could change poor people's lives. He is the person through whom Pakistani learners meet social entrepreneurship.

## Sub lesson 3 — Microfinance — small loans that start enterprises

Dr Khan's work is tied to the **Islamic Micro Finance Organization**: **microfinance** gives very small loans to people too poor for ordinary bank loans, so they can start small enterprises of their own.
