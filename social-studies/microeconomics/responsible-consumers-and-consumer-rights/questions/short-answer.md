# Responsible consumers and consumer rights — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two tests must a purchase pass for the book's responsible consumer?
**Answer:** It must be needed, and it must be affordable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three abuses against consumers from the book's list.
**Answer:** Any three of: price speculation; hoarding of products; false offers; contract/guarantee breaches; wrong weights or prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the Sundde, and what does the responsible consumer do with it?
**Answer:** It is the national superintendency for the defence of socioeconomic rights; the responsible consumer denounces speculation, hoarding, false offers and other abuses to it.
