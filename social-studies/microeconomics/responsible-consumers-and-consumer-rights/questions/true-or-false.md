# Responsible consumers and consumer rights — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buying what you cannot afford is responsible consumption in the book.
**Answer: False** — a responsible consumer buys what is needed and can be afforded.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wrong weights and prices are among the abuses named in the book.
**Answer: True** — alongside speculation, hoarding, false offers and guarantee breaches.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Denouncing speculation and hoarding is part of defending socioeconomic rights in the book.
**Answer: True** — the responsible consumer defends those rights by denouncing to the Sundde.
