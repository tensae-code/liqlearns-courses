# Responsible consumers and consumer rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A responsible consumer buys —
a) what is needed and can be afforded  b) everything that is advertised  c) whatever others are buying
**Answer: a) what is needed and can be afforded**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The abuses the book lists include —
a) price speculation, hoarding and false offers  b) saving and budgeting  c) comparing prices
**Answer: a) price speculation, hoarding and false offers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Abuses are denounced to —
a) the Sundde  b) the nearest shopkeeper  c) nobody — nothing can be done
**Answer: a) the Sundde**
