# Responsible consumers and consumer rights — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's consumer is responsible, never a _____ (consumption addict).
**Answer: consumista**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hiding products away to force prices up is called _____ of products.
**Answer: hoarding**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sundde stands for the Superintendencia Nacional para la Defensa de los Derechos _____.
**Answer: Socioeconómicos**
