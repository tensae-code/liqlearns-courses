# Video script — Responsible consumers and consumer rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

It must be needed, and it must be affordable.

Any three of: price speculation; hoarding of products; false offers; contract/guarantee breaches; wrong weights or prices.

It is the national superintendency for the defence of socioeconomic rights; the responsible consumer denounces speculation, hoarding, false offers and other abuses to it.
