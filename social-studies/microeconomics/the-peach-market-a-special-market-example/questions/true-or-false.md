# The peach market — a special market example — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ripe peaches cannot be stored for a better week without rotting.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With near-vertical supply, demand sets the price almost alone.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where quantity is fixed for the moment, price does all the adjusting.
**Answer: True**
