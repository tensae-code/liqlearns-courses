# The peach market — a special market example — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The peach harvest arrives —
a) evenly all year
b) in a short season, all at once
c) only when prices are high
**Answer: b) in a short season, all at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In season, peach supply is —
a) horizontal
b) near-vertical
c) downward sloping
**Answer: b) near-vertical**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where else does near-vertical short-run supply appear?
a) supermarkets restocking daily
b) fish at the morning quay and seats for tonight's match
c) factories with spare machines
**Answer: b) fish at the morning quay and seats for tonight's match**
