# The peach market — a special market example — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The peach crop arrives in a short _____, all at once.
**Answer: season**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fixed harvest gives supply a near-_____ shape.
**Answer: vertical**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The peach market isolates equilibrium when _____ cannot adjust in time.
**Answer: supply / quantity**
