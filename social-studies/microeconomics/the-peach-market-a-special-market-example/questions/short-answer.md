# The peach market — a special market example — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must the peach market clear each day's pile?
**Answer:** Because ripe peaches rot — the picked quantity cannot be held back for later sale.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does price swing so hard in the peach market?
**Answer:** Supply is near-vertical once picked, so demand shifts move price almost one-for-one with no quantity answer.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What general lesson does the peach example teach?
**Answer:** When quantity is fixed for the moment, price alone adjusts to clear the market — demand sets the price.
