# Video script — The peach market — a special market example

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook closes its equilibrium chapter with a market of its own countryside: peaches. The crop ripens in a short season and arrives at market in a rush — and ripe peaches cannot wait.

With the harvest fixed once picked, the peach supply curve in season is near-vertical: quantity barely answers price. A high price cannot call more peaches into existence this week; a low price cannot send picked peaches back to the tree.

The peach market is the textbook's special example because it isolates one force: what an equilibrium looks like when supply cannot adjust in the time available.
