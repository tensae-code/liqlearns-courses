# Hands-on — Why opportunity cost rises — the law of increasing opportunity cost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act it out: three learners — a born weaver, a fair weaver, a poor weaver — move one by one from farming to weaving. After each move, the group says what farming lost.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — steps up a staircase getting taller along the curve, each step the cost of one more unit — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
