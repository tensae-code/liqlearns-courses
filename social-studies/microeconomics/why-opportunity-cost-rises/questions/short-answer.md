# Why opportunity cost rises — the law of increasing opportunity cost — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The law stated" in your own words.
**Answer:** As more of one good is produced, the opportunity cost of each extra unit rises.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why it rises — specialised resources" in your own words.
**Answer:** Opportunity cost rises because resources are specialised and not equally adaptable to both uses.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the curve bows out" in your own words.
**Answer:** Increasing opportunity cost makes the PPF concave — bowed out — to the origin.
