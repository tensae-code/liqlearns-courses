# Why opportunity cost rises — the law of increasing opportunity cost — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The law stated"?
a) each extra unit costs more of the other good than the one before  b) resources are specialised and not equally adaptable to both uses  c) the PPF is concave (bowed out) to the origin
**Answer: a) each extra unit costs more of the other good than the one before**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Why it rises — specialised resources"?
a) each extra unit costs more of the other good than the one before  b) the PPF is concave (bowed out) to the origin  c) resources are specialised and not equally adaptable to both uses
**Answer: c) resources are specialised and not equally adaptable to both uses**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the curve bows out"?
a) resources are specialised and not equally adaptable to both uses  b) the PPF is concave (bowed out) to the origin  c) each extra unit costs more of the other good than the one before
**Answer: b) the PPF is concave (bowed out) to the origin**
