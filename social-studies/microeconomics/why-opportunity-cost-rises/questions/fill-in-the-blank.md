# Why opportunity cost rises — the law of increasing opportunity cost — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the law of increasing opportunity cost, each extra unit's cost _____.
**Answer: rises**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Opportunity cost rises because resources are _____ and not equally adaptable.
**Answer: specialised**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The PPF is _____ to the origin because opportunity cost increases.
**Answer: concave**
