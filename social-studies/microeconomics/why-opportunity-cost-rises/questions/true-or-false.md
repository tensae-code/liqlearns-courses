# Why opportunity cost rises — the law of increasing opportunity cost — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As more of one good is produced, the opportunity cost of each extra unit rises.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As more of one good is produced, the opportunity cost of each extra unit rises.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The law stated); sub lesson 2 says: Opportunity cost rises because resources are specialised and not equally adaptable to both uses.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Increasing opportunity cost makes the PPF concave — bowed out — to the origin.
**Answer: True**
