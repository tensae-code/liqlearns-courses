# Price elasticity of demand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elasticity measured between two points of the curve is _____ elasticity.
**Answer: arc**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A price elasticity of 0.2 — below 1 — is called _____.
**Answer: inelastic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Medicines cannot wait, so their demand is stiffened by _____ — one determinant of elasticity.
**Answer: urgency**
