# Price elasticity of demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Point and arc measures" in your own words.
**Answer:** Arc elasticity measures responsiveness between two points of the demand curve.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The five cases" in your own words.
**Answer:** A price elasticity below 1 is called inelastic.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "What decides the size" in your own words.
**Answer:** Medicines are price-inelastic partly because they are urgent — they cannot wait.
