# Price elasticity of demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arc elasticity measures responsiveness between two points of the demand curve.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A price elasticity below 1 is called inelastic.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Medicines are price-inelastic partly because they are urgent — they cannot wait.
**Answer: True**
