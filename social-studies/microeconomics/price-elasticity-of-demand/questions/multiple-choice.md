# Price elasticity of demand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Point and arc measures"?
a) point elasticity at a point; arc elasticity between two points — worked arc 0.6  b) elastic above 1, inelastic below 1, unitary 1, perfect cases 0 and infinity  c) necessities, substitutes, income share, urgency, durability, frequency and time

**Answer: c) elastic above 1, inelastic below 1, unitary 1, perfect cases 0 and infinity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "What decides the size"?
