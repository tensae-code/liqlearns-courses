# Hands-on — Price elasticity of demand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Sort ten good-cards — medicine, salt, restaurant meals, holiday trips, bread, brand-name soap — from stiffest to stretchiest demand. Defend each place with one determinant from the lesson.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — five demand curves from vertical to horizontal — the five cases of price elasticity — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
