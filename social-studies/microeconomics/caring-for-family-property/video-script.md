# Video script — Caring for family property

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Caring for family property — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: The family's property is its wealth — the things the family owns and lives from. The book opens its property teaching here, at home, before school and public property. The family's property must be looked after and not wasted or damaged. Wasting or damaging it makes the family poorer. Caring for family property is a daily duty for everyone in the home — the first caring lesson, before the same duty is taught for school property and public property in the sector.
