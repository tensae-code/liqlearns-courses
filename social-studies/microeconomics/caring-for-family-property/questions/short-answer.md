# Caring for family property — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about the family's property is its wealth?
**Answer:** The family's property is its wealth — the things the family owns and lives from. The book opens its property teaching here, at home, before school and public property.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about look after it — do not waste or damage it?
**Answer:** The family's property must be looked after and not wasted or damaged. Wasting or damaging it makes the family poorer.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about caring as a daily duty?
**Answer:** Caring for family property is a daily duty for everyone in the home — the first caring lesson, before the same duty is taught for school property and public property in the sector.
