# Caring for family property — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The family's property is its wealth — the things the family owns and lives from.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The family's property must be looked after and not wasted or damaged.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Caring for family property is a daily duty for everyone in the home — the first caring lesson, before the same duty is taught for school property and public property in the sector.
**Answer: True**
