# Caring for family property

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — caring for family property — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The family's property is its wealth

The family's **property** is its **wealth** — the things the family owns and lives from. The book opens its property teaching here, at home, before school and public property.

## Sub lesson 2 — Look after it — do not waste or damage it

The family's property must be **looked after** and **not wasted or damaged**. Wasting or damaging it makes the family poorer.

## Sub lesson 3 — Caring as a daily duty

Caring for family property is a daily duty for everyone in the home — the first caring lesson, before the same duty is taught for school property and public property in the sector.
