# Video script — How prices are set supply costs and competition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How prices are set supply costs and competition — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Prices are defined between those who offer and those who demand, by three criteria — how many units are available (more units, lower price; Few units, price rises), the cost of production (ingredients, machinery, labour — the sale must recover the investment and leave a profit), and competition among producers; Applied to the rural market, where a farm producer's price must sum seeds, fertiliser and day-labourers' pay plus a little more
