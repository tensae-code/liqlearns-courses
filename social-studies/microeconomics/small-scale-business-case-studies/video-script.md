# Video script — Small scale business case studies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Case studies of small-scale economic activities — a transport business, an accommodation business and a trade store — examining how each operates and what it contributes.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG: Senior Primary Trade examines small-scale economic activities in the local community and evaluates their impact on development — formal activities must be registered and regulated, and producers' and consumers' contributions are traced from the local community up to the provincial and national economies.

Putting it together: case studies of small-scale economic activities — a transport business, an accommodation business and a trade store — examining how each operates and what it contributes — taught in Economic Activities unit, Lessons 133–135 (KSAV) (Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG, printed p. 112).
