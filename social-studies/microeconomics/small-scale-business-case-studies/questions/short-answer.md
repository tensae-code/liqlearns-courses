# Small scale business case studies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Case studies of small-scale economic activities — a"?
**Answer:** Case studies of small-scale economic activities — a transport business, an accommodation business and a trade store — examining how each operates and what it contributes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG: Senior Primary Trade examines small-scale economic activities in the local community and evaluates their impact on development — formal activities must be registered and regulated, and producers' and consumers' contributions are traced from the local community up to the provincial and national economies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Putting it together: case studies of small-scale economic"?
**Answer:** Putting it together: case studies of small-scale economic activities — a transport business, an accommodation business and a trade store — examining how each operates and what it contributes — taught in Economic Activities unit, Lessons 133–135 (KSAV) (Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG, printed p. 112).
