# Video script — Point and arc elasticity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Point elasticity measures the responsiveness of quantity demanded at one point of the demand curve — at one exact price. Greece's Grade 12 textbook sets it beside its twin: elasticity measured at a point uses the curve's slope at that price together with the price and quantity there.

Arc elasticity measures responsiveness between two points of the curve — over the arc between an old price and a new one. It is the measure used when the price change is not tiny but a real step from one price to another.

The two measures answer two different questions. Tiny change at a known price → point elasticity. A stated move from price A to price B → arc elasticity.
