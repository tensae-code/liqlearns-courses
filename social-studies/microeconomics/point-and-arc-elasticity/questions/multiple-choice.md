# Point and arc elasticity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point elasticity measures responsiveness —
a) between two distant prices
b) at one exact point of the curve
c) averaged over a year
**Answer: b) at one exact point of the curve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Arc elasticity is used when —
a) the price change is a real step between two prices
b) nothing changes
c) only one price exists
**Answer: a) the price change is a real step between two prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On a straight-line demand curve, point elasticity —
a) is the same everywhere
b) differs from place to place
c) is always one
**Answer: b) differs from place to place**
