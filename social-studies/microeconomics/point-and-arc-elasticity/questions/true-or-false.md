# Point and arc elasticity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point elasticity answers how quantity responds to a tiny price change at that price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Arc elasticity describes the stretch between two points, not just either end.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Point elasticity is higher near the price axis of a straight-line demand curve.
**Answer: True**
