# Point and arc elasticity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does point elasticity measure?
**Answer:** How strongly quantity demanded responds to a small price change at one exact point of the demand curve.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When is arc elasticity the right measure?
**Answer:** When price changes by a real step from one price to another, so responsiveness over that stretch is wanted.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why can one number not describe a whole straight-line demand curve?
**Answer:** Because responsiveness differs along it — high near the price axis, low near the quantity axis.
