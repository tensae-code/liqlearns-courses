# Point and arc elasticity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elasticity measured at one exact price is _____ elasticity.
**Answer: point**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Elasticity measured between two points, over a stretch of the curve, is _____ elasticity.
**Answer: arc**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A tiny change at a known price calls for _____ elasticity; a step between two prices calls for arc elasticity.
**Answer: point**
