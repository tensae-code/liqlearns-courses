# Permanent jobs — pay and benefits — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sudha is paid only on days she is called in.
**Answer: False — she gets a regular monthly salary.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Provident fund is paid only when orders end.
**Answer: False — it is regular saving built into the permanent job.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Casual work offers the same benefits as Sudha's job.
**Answer: False — the closing table contrasts casual work's lack of them.**
