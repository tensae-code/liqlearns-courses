# Permanent jobs — pay and benefits — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Sudha's job and pay pattern?
**Answer:** She is a marketing manager with a regular monthly salary.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are provident-fund savings?
**Answer:** Money put aside from pay for the worker's future.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two benefits beyond Sudha's salary.
**Answer:** Paid holidays and medical cover (also provident-fund savings).
