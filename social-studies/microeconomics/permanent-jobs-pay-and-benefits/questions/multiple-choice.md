# Permanent jobs — pay and benefits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sudha is —
a) a marketing manager with a permanent job  b) a casual daily-wage worker  c) a street vendor
**Answer: a) a marketing manager with a permanent job**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Provident-fund savings are —
a) savings put aside for the worker's future  b) a tax on customers  c) the firm's profit
**Answer: a) savings put aside for the worker's future**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Permanent jobs add —
a) paid holidays and medical cover  b) longer unpaid hours  c) no benefits
**Answer: a) paid holidays and medical cover**
