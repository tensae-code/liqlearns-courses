# Permanent jobs — pay and benefits

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Sudha's regular monthly salary

**Sudha the marketing manager** holds a **permanent regular job** with a **regular monthly salary** — pay that arrives whether or not orders rush in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Provident-fund savings

Her job adds **provident-fund savings** — money put aside from her pay for her future, part of what a permanent job pays beyond the wage.

## Sub lesson 3 — Paid holidays and medical cover — the contrast with casual work

She also gets **paid holidays and medical cover**; the book's closing table **contrasts** this security with **casual work**, which has none of it.
