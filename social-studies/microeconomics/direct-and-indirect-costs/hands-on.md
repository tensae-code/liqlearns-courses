# Hands-on — Direct and indirect costs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the costs
Write these costs on slips of paper: cloth for sewing, the sewer's pay, rent, electricity, telephone, insurance, loan interest, the manager's salary. Sort them into two piles — direct and indirect. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every slip in the wrong pile.

## Say it with things at home
Lay out the materials of something made at home and say their cost aloud as material cost; point to the person who made it for direct labour; then name one bill the house pays anyway, as an indirect cost.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
