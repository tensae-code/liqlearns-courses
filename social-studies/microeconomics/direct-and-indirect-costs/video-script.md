# Video script — Direct and indirect costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every business must know its costs — and Laos' Grade 11 books sort them in two. Direct costs: material costs and direct labour costs, the costs of the product itself. Indirect costs: the rest — rent, salaries, loan interest, electricity, telephone, insurance. Learn the sorting, with the books' own tables and the SMOC exercise, and you can cost anything you make.
