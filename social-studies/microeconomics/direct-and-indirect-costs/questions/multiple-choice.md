# Direct and indirect costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every business must —
a) ignore its costs  b) know its costs  c) hide its costs
**Answer: b) know its costs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Direct costs split into —
a) rent and insurance  b) material costs and direct labour costs  c) profit and loss
**Answer: b) material costs and direct labour costs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is an indirect cost?
a) the material in the product  b) the maker's labour  c) rent
**Answer: c) rent**
