# Direct and indirect costs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two kinds of costs are direct costs and indirect costs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rent is a direct cost because the workshop cannot work without it.
**Answer: False — rent is an indirect cost; direct costs are material costs and direct labour costs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Loan interest, electricity, telephone and insurance are all indirect costs.
**Answer: True**
