# Direct and indirect costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Into what two kinds do the books sort a business's costs, and how is the sorting practised?
**Answer:** Direct costs and indirect costs. It is practised with classification exercises and worked tables — and, in the Business book, the SMOC sorting exercise (Salary, Material, Other, Capital).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the two direct costs?
**Answer:** Material costs — the materials that go into what the business makes — and direct labour costs — the labour that makes it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the indirect costs the books name.
**Answer:** Rent, salaries (the owner's and managers' salaries in the Business book), loan interest, electricity, telephone and insurance — all the other expenses of running the business.
