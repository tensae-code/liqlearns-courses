# Price and non-price competition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price competition is competing by —
a) better quality  b) lower price  c) a new name
**Answer: b) lower price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Non-price competition wins customers by —
a) quality and difference  b) charging less  c) closing earlier
**Answer: a) quality and difference**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A "blue ocean" market is —
a) a market by the sea  b) an uncontested market a firm creates for itself  c) a market with many rivals
**Answer: b) an uncontested market a firm creates for itself**
