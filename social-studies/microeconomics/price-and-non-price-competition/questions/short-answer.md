# Price and non-price competition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is price competition?
**Answer:** Competing on lower price — winning customers by charging less.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is non-price competition?
**Answer:** Competing on quality and difference — winning customers by offering something better or different, not by charging less.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three competitive strategies or strategy ideas the books teach.
**Answer:** Flank attack, encirclement, and uncontested "blue ocean" markets; Duisenkhanov's lesson adds the types of competitors a strategy must reckon with.
