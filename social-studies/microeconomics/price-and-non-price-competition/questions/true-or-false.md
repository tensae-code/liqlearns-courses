# Price and non-price competition — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price competition means offering a better product at the same price.
**Answer: False — price competition is competing on lower price; quality and difference are non-price competition.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The two kinds of competition are taught together, and learners must describe and judge each.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Flank attack and encirclement are named among the competitive strategies.
**Answer: True**
