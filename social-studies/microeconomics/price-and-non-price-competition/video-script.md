# Video script — Price and non-price competition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan's Grade 10 books split competition by the weapon used, says the narrator. Price competition wins customers by charging less. Non-price competition wins them by quality and difference. And the strategies have names — flank attack, encirclement, and the blue ocean, a market of your own where no rival fights you.
