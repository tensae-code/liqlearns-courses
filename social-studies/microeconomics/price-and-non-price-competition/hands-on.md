# Hands-on — Price and non-price competition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the weapons
On paper, draw two columns — price competition and non-price competition. Write three moves in each column from businesses you know. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything in the wrong column.

## Say it with things at home
Put two stones on the table, one for each kind of competition, and name what each stone is. Then add a third stone out on its own and say why it is the blue ocean.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
