# Strengths and weaknesses of the market system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the order — evidence first, judgement after — matter?
**Answer: A judgement made after the evidence is worth more than one made before it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one strength of the market system.
**Answer: Free prices and competition guide production without an office deciding everything (or: private property and choice push people to work and start businesses; or: competition presses prices down and quality up).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the book's judgement of the market system mixed?
**Answer: Because the system has real strengths and real failures, so neither plain praise nor plain rejection fits the evidence.**
