# Strengths and weaknesses of the market system — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book asks pupils to accept the market system without looking at it first.
**Answer: False — The book asks pupils to evaluate first — not to accept or reject before looking.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Free prices and competition answer the three questions in a market system.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A market left to itself can leave people without work or income.
**Answer: True**
