# Strengths and weaknesses of the market system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils _____ the market system before accepting or rejecting it.
**Answer: evaluate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a market system, free prices and _____ answer what, how and for whom to produce.
**Answer: competition**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A market left entirely to itself can leave people without work or _____.
**Answer: income**
