# Strengths and weaknesses of the market system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book ask pupils to do first with the market system?
a) accept it without looking at it  b) evaluate its strengths and failures  c) reject it without looking at it
**Answer: b) evaluate its strengths and failures**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On the strength side, what answers what, how and for whom to produce?
a) free prices and competition  b) chance, with no signals at all  c) one office deciding every good
**Answer: a) free prices and competition**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one failure the lesson counts against the market system.
a) it removes the need for any choices  b) it makes all prices identical everywhere  c) it can leave people without work or income
**Answer: c) it can leave people without work or income**
