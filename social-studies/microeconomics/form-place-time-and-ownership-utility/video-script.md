# Video script — Form, place, time and ownership utility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Business creates utility in four ways: form, place, time and ownership.

Business is the creation of utilities: a good gains value in four ways — form, place, time and ownership.

Form utility changes raw material into a finished good, and place utility carries the good from where it is produced to where it is consumed.

Time utility stores goods in a warehouse when they are not needed and supplies them at the right time, and ownership utility transfers ownership of a good from one person to another by exchange.
