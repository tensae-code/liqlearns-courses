# Form, place, time and ownership utility — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about business creates utilities?
**Answer:** Business is the creation of utilities: a good gains value in four ways — form, place, time and ownership.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about form and place utility?
**Answer:** Form utility changes raw material into a finished good, and place utility carries the good from where it is produced to where it is consumed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about time and ownership utility?
**Answer:** Time utility stores goods in a warehouse when they are not needed and supplies them at the right time, and ownership utility transfers ownership of a good from one person to another by exchange.
