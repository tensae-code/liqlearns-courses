# Form, place, time and ownership utility — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Business is the creation of utilities: a good gains value in four ways — form, place, time and ownership.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Form utility changes raw material into a finished good, and place utility carries the good from where it is produced to where it is consumed.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Time utility stores goods in a warehouse when they are not needed and supplies them at the right time, and ownership utility transfers ownership of a good from one person to another something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — Time utility stores goods in a warehouse when they are not needed and supplies them at the right time, and ownership utility transfers ownership of a good from one person to another by exchange.
