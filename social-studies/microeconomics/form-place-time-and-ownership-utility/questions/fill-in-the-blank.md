# Form, place, time and ownership utility — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Business is the creation of utilities: a good gains value in four ways — _____.
**Answer: form, place, time and ownership**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Form utility changes _____, and place utility carries the good from where it is produced to where it is consumed.
**Answer: raw material into a finished good**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Time utility stores goods in a warehouse when they are not needed and supplies them at the right time, and ownership utility transfers ownership of a good from one person to another _____.
**Answer: by exchange**
