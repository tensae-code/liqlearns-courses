# Entrepreneurship and its kinds — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Entrepreneurship is only about inheriting a family shop.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches it as a mindset first — seeing a need and starting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Small and large businesses are the same size.
**Answer: False** — they are told apart by their scale.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's industrial case is about a foreign car factory only.
**Answer: False** — its case is Pakistani — citrus growing as enterprise.
