# Entrepreneurship and its kinds — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Seeing a need and daring to start is the entrepreneurial _____.
**Answer: mindset**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Businesses told apart by scale in the book's first kinds are small business and _____ business.
**Answer: large**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Making things at scale in industry is _____ entrepreneurship; the book's case is citrus growing.
**Answer: industrial**
