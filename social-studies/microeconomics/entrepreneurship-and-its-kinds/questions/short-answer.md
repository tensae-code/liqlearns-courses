# Entrepreneurship and its kinds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the Class 5 book teach first about entrepreneurship?
**Answer:** It teaches the entrepreneurial mindset — seeing a need and daring to start — before the kinds of business.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the first two kinds of entrepreneurial business the book names?
**Answer:** Small business and large business, told apart by scale.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is industrial entrepreneurship, and what case does the book use?
**Answer:** Industrial entrepreneurship is making things at scale in industry; the book's case is citrus growing.
