# Entrepreneurship and its kinds

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Entrepreneurship and the entrepreneurial mindset

Pakistan's Class 5 book teaches **entrepreneurship** as a way of thinking first: the **entrepreneurial mindset** — seeing a need, daring to start, and building something that serves it — before any kind of business is named.

## Sub lesson 2 — Small business and large business

The kinds begin with size: a **small business** run by few people for a local market, and a **large business** working at a much bigger scale. The book sets the two side by side as the first kinds a learner meets.

## Sub lesson 3 — Industrial entrepreneurship

The third kind is **industrial entrepreneurship** — making things at scale in industry. The book tells it through Pakistani enterprise stories, with citrus growing as a case of enterprise built on the country's own produce.
