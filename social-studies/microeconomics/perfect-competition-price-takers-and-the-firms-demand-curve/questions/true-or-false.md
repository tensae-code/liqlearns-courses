# Perfect competition — price takers and the firm's demand curve — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under perfect competition the industry is the price maker and each firm is a price taker.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For a competitive firm, price, average revenue, demand and marginal revenue are all the same horizontal line.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under perfect competition total revenue is a straight line through the origin because price is constant.
**Answer: True**
