# Perfect competition — price takers and the firm's demand curve — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A competitive firm, too small to move the market price, is called a _____.
**Answer: price taker**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The competitive firm's demand curve is a _____ line at the market price.
**Answer: horizontal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With a constant price, total revenue is drawn as a _____ through the origin.
**Answer: straight line**
