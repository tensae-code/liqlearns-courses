# Perfect competition — price takers and the firm's demand curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The industry makes the price"?
a) the market sets the price; each tiny firm takes it as given  b) a horizontal demand line where P = AR = demand = MR  c) TR rises by the price per unit — a straight line through the origin
**Answer: a) the market sets the price; each tiny firm takes it as given**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The firm's horizontal demand curve"?
a) the market sets the price; each tiny firm takes it as given  b) TR rises by the price per unit — a straight line through the origin  c) a horizontal demand line where P = AR = demand = MR
**Answer: c) a horizontal demand line where P = AR = demand = MR**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Total revenue — a straight line through the origin"?
a) a horizontal demand line where P = AR = demand = MR  b) TR rises by the price per unit — a straight line through the origin  c) the market sets the price; each tiny firm takes it as given
**Answer: b) TR rises by the price per unit — a straight line through the origin**
