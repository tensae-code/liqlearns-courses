# Perfect competition — price takers and the firm's demand curve — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The industry makes the price" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under perfect competition the industry is the price maker and each firm is a price taker.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The firm's horizontal demand curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** For a competitive firm, price, average revenue, demand and marginal revenue are all the same horizontal line.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Total revenue — a straight line through the origin" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under perfect competition total revenue is a straight line through the origin because price is constant.
