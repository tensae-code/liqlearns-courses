# Hands-on — Perfect competition — price takers and the firm's demand curve

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act the market: twenty learners call out supply and demand prices until they meet; then one 'firm' tries to sell above the agreed price and the class shows what happens — nobody buys.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the two-panel figure — industry demand and supply crossing, the single firm's flat demand line at that price — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
