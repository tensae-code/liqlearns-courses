# Trade unions and collective bargaining — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "A trade union as the workers' bargaining agent"?
**Answer:** A trade union as the workers' bargaining agent (PNG's Trade Union Congress); collective bargaining over wages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Conditions; hours and benefits"?
