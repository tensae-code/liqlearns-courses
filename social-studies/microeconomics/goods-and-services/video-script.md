# Video script — Goods and services

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A good is a thing you can touch and hold — bread, clothes, toys, maize, a clay pot. A service is something people do for you — the doctor caring for the sick, the teacher teaching, the driver driving.

The cook shows that one worker can give both: the food is a good, and the cooking is a service.

At home, at school and in the community, ask of every job: does it hand over a thing, or does it do something that helps? That sorts the potter, the weaver, the welder and the tailor by their goods, and the doctor, the nurse and the teacher by their services.
