# Hands-on — Goods and services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort your home
Walk through your home and list ten things work has produced there. Mark each G for good or S for service — remember the cook's cooking is a service even though the food is a good. Check your list against lesson/lesson.md.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a market scene with a potter, a tailor and a teacher in it. Label each worker with the good or service they give. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
