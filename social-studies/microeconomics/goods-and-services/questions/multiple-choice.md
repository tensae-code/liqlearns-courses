# Goods and services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a good?
a) a lesson from a teacher  b) bread you can touch and hold  c) a ride from a driver
**Answer: b) bread you can touch and hold**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A service is —
a) a thing you can hold  b) something people do for you, like teaching or caring for the sick  c) a clay pot on a shelf
**Answer: b) something people do for you, like teaching or caring for the sick**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cook in the infant guides gives —
a) only a service  b) only a good  c) both a good (the food) and a service (the cooking)
**Answer: c) both a good (the food) and a service (the cooking)**
