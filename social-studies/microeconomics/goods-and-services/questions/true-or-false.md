# Goods and services — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maize, bread and clay pots (pfuko) are goods, because they are things you can touch and hold.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Teaching is a good, because the teacher hands you a thing.
**Answer: False — teaching is a service: it is something a person does for you, not a thing you can hold.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A tailor gives a good — clothes — and a doctor gives a service — caring for the sick.
**Answer: True**
