# Goods and services — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Things you can touch and hold, like bread, clothes and toys, are called _____.
**Answer: goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Things people do for you, like teaching, driving and caring for the sick, are called _____.
**Answer: services**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Grade 2 the community's producers include the potter, the weaver, the welder and the _____.
**Answer: tailor**
