# Goods and services — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between a good and a service?
**Answer:** A good is a thing you can touch and hold, like bread or a clay pot; a service is something people do for you, like teaching, driving or caring for the sick.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the cook count as giving both a good and a service?
**Answer:** Because the cook gives the food, which is a good you can touch and eat, and the cooking itself, which is a service — work done for you.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one good and one service from your own community, and say who gives each.
**Answer:** Any correct pair from the lesson, e.g. the good: vegetables from a local farmer (or tools from a welder); the service: teaching from a teacher (or care from a nurse) — the worker must match the good or service named.
