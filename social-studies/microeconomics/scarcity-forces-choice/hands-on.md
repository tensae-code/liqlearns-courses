# Hands-on — Scarcity forces choice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay five cards in the book's chain order — scarce, limited resources, limited output, choice, cost — shuffle them, and have a partner rebuild the chain and explain each link.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the chain as five linked boxes from scarce to cost — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
