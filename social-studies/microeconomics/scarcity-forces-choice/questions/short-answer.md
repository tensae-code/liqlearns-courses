# Scarcity forces choice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The chain begins" in your own words.
**Answer:** Because resources are scarce, output is limited and not every want can be met.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Everyone must choose" in your own words.
**Answer:** Households, firms and governments must choose what to produce, in what quantity, and what to leave unproduced.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Choice ends in cost" in your own words.
**Answer:** Every choice ends in a cost: the value of what was left unchosen.
