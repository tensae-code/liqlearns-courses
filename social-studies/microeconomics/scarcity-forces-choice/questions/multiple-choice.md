# Scarcity forces choice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The chain begins"?
a) scarcity leads to limited resources and limited output  b) choosing what to produce, how much, and what to leave unproduced  c) every choice carries a cost — the thing left unchosen
**Answer: a) scarcity leads to limited resources and limited output**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Everyone must choose"?
a) scarcity leads to limited resources and limited output  b) every choice carries a cost — the thing left unchosen  c) choosing what to produce, how much, and what to leave unproduced
**Answer: c) choosing what to produce, how much, and what to leave unproduced**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Choice ends in cost"?
a) choosing what to produce, how much, and what to leave unproduced  b) every choice carries a cost — the thing left unchosen  c) scarcity leads to limited resources and limited output
**Answer: b) every choice carries a cost — the thing left unchosen**
