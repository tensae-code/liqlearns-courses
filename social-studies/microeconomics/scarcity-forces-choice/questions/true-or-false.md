# Scarcity forces choice — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Because resources are scarce, output is limited and not every want can be met.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Because resources are scarce, output is limited and not every want can be met.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The chain begins); sub lesson 2 says: Households, firms and governments must choose what to produce, in what quantity, and what to leave unproduced.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Every choice ends in a cost: the value of what was left unchosen.
**Answer: True**
