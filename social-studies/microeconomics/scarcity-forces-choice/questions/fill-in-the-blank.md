# Scarcity forces choice — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Because resources are scarce, output is _____.
**Answer: limited**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Households, firms and governments must _____ what to produce and what to leave unproduced.
**Answer: choose**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The last link of the chain is _____ — what the choice left unchosen.
**Answer: cost**
