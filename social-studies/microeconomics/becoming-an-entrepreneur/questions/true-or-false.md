# Becoming an entrepreneur — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An idea that fails one of the three tests is not yet a business.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The management part of the plan says who does what and how decisions are made and checked.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A business plan is only paperwork; the idea, the money and the people need never agree.
**Answer: False** — the plan is the idea, the money and the people made to agree on paper before they must agree in real life.
