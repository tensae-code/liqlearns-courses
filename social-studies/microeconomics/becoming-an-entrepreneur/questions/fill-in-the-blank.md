# Becoming an entrepreneur — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why buyers choose this business over its rivals is its competitive _____.
**Answer: advantage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The plan for winning customers — product, price, place and promotion — is its _____.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under pressure, a business does what was _____ when heads were cool.
