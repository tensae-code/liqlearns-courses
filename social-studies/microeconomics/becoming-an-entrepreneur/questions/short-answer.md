# Becoming an entrepreneur — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three tests an idea must pass, and what each asks.
**Answer:** Product — what exactly is offered and does it meet a real need; customer — who exactly buys, how many, and can they pay; competitive advantage — why they buy from this business rather than rivals.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four parts of the business plan the guide teaches.
**Answer:** Marketing, finance, management and ethical decisions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are ethical decisions written into the plan at the start?
**Answer:** Because later, under pressure, a business does what was decided when heads were cool; deciding in advance how workers, customers, suppliers and neighbours will be treated holds the business to it.
