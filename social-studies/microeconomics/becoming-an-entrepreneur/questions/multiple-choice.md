# Becoming an entrepreneur — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An idea is judged on three tests: product, customer and —
a) the founder's age  b) competitive advantage  c) the colour of the shop
**Answer: b) competitive advantage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The finance part of a business plan sets out —
a) the money needed to start, where it comes from, and expected costs and income  b) the shop's opening hours only  c) the founder's hobbies
**Answer: a) the money needed to start, where it comes from, and expected costs and income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethical decisions belong —
a) only after the business is rich  b) inside the business plan itself, decided from the start  c) nowhere in business
**Answer: b) inside the business plan itself, decided from the start**
