# Hands-on — Becoming an entrepreneur

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge your own idea
Take the business from the opening task and mark it against the three tests — product, customer, competitive advantage. Write one sentence for each test, honestly. Where a test fails, write what would have to change for it to pass.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the plan's four parts from memory — marketing, finance, management, ethical decisions — with one line under each saying what it settles. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
