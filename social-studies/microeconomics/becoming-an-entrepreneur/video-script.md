# Video script — Becoming an entrepreneur

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Becoming an entrepreneur starts with judging the idea: the product, the customer, and the competitive advantage — why buyers choose you over rivals.

Then the idea becomes a business plan: marketing to win the customers, finance to start and survive, management so everyone knows their task.

And the plan carries the ethical decisions from the start — how workers, customers and neighbours will be treated — decided while heads are cool.
