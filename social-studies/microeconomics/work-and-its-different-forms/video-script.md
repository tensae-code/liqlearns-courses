# Video script — Work and its different forms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Work matters: it is how people meet their needs and how a country is served. And work takes different forms — different jobs, done by different people.

One form has its own name: a profession. A profession is a job built on knowledge and experience.

Every country has its most important jobs. In the book's country, the policeman stands among them — the worker who keeps people safe.
