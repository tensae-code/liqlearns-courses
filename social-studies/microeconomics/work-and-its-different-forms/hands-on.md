# Hands-on — Work and its different forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Walk through your home and street in your mind and collect the jobs you pass — write each one on a card. Sort the cards: professions (jobs built on knowledge and experience) in one pile, other work in the other, and say out loud why each card goes where it goes.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's street of workers from memory — different jobs side by side, the policeman among them — and label every job. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
