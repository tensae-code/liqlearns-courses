# Work and its different forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's lesson "I love to work" first teaches —
a) the price of goods  b) the importance of work and its different forms  c) how banks work
**Answer: b) the importance of work and its different forms**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A profession (مهنة) is —
a) any job at all  b) a job built on knowledge and experience  c) a job with no learning in it
**Answer: b) a job built on knowledge and experience**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Among the most important jobs in my country, the book names —
a) the policeman  b) the tourist  c) the shopper
**Answer: a) the policeman**
