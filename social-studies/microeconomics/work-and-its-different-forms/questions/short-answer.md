# Work and its different forms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does work matter?
**Answer:** Work is how people meet their needs and how a country is served and kept going.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a profession?
**Answer:** A profession (مهنة) is a job built on knowledge and experience.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one job the book counts among the most important jobs in my country, and say what it gives.
**Answer:** The policeman — the worker who keeps people safe.
