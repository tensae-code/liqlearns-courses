# Work and its different forms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Work takes different forms — different jobs, done by different people.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A profession is a job built on knowledge and experience.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The policeman is named in the book among the most important jobs in my country.
**Answer: True**
