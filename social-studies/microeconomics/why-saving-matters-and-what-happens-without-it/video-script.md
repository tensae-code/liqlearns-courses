# Video script — Why saving matters and what happens without it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why saving matters and what happens without it — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: Saving rescues a person in accidents and sudden problems — the book names illness and losing a job among them, and becoming poor. Saving is also for celebrations and for old age — times ahead that the saver prepares for before they arrive. A person who does not save falls into poverty and lives badly — the book's closing warning of its saving curriculum.
