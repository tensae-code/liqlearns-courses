# Why saving matters and what happens without it — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about saving rescues in sudden problems?
**Answer:** Saving rescues a person in accidents and sudden problems — the book names illness and losing a job among them, and becoming poor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about saving for celebrations and old age?
**Answer:** Saving is also for celebrations and for old age — times ahead that the saver prepares for before they arrive.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about a person who does not save?
**Answer:** A person who does not save falls into poverty and lives badly — the book's closing warning of its saving curriculum.
