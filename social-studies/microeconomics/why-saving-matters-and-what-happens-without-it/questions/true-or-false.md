# Why saving matters and what happens without it — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving rescues a person in accidents and sudden problems — the book names illness and losing a job among them, and becoming poor.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saving is also for celebrations and for old age — times ahead that the saver prepares for before they arrive.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A person who does not save falls into poverty and lives badly — the book's closing warning of its saving curriculum.
**Answer: True**
