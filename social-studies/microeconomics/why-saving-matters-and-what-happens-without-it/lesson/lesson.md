# Why saving matters and what happens without it

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — why saving matters and what happens without it — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Saving rescues in sudden problems

**Saving rescues** a person in **accidents** and **sudden problems** — the book names **illness** and **losing a job** among them, and becoming poor.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saving for celebrations and old age

Saving is also for **celebrations** and for **old age** — times ahead that the saver prepares for before they arrive.

## Sub lesson 3 — A person who does not save

A person who **does not save** falls into **poverty** and **lives badly** — the book's closing warning of its saving curriculum.

## Say it to your companion

Explain why saving matters and what happens without it to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
