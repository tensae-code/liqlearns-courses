# Simón Rodríguez — trades, work and social economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two trainings did Rodríguez say people need?
**Answer:** Training in a trade (un oficio) for their economy, and in politics to organise and build the Republic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What must school teaching combine, for Rodríguez?
**Answer:** Study and productive work.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name Rodríguez's three educational maxims.
**Answer:** Popular education; assignment to useful exercises; and a founded aspiration to property.
