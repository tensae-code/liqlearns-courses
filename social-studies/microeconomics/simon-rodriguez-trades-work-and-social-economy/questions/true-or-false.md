# Simón Rodríguez — trades, work and social economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For Rodríguez, a trade is a foundation of a person's economy.
**Answer: True** — people must be trained in a trade for their economy, and in politics to build the Republic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rodríguez separated study completely from productive work.
**Answer: False** — his teaching combines the two.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The aspiration to property is given by work, in Rodríguez's teaching.
**Answer: True** — work is a human right and a social duty, part of the art of living.
