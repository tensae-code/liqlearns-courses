# Simón Rodríguez — trades, work and social economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Simón Rodríguez thought people must be trained in —
a) a trade (un oficio) for their economy  b) only reading and writing  c) nothing — school is unnecessary
**Answer: a) a trade (un oficio) for their economy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rodríguez's school teaching must combine —
a) study and productive work  b) study only, with no work  c) work only, with no study
**Answer: a) study and productive work**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rodríguez's three maxims are —
a) popular education, assignment to useful exercises, and a founded aspiration to property  b) reading, writing and arithmetic  c) silence, obedience and memorising
**Answer: a) popular education, assignment to useful exercises, and a founded aspiration to property**
