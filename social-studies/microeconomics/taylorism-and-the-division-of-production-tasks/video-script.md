# Video script — Taylorism and the division of production tasks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Taylorism and the division of production tasks — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Taylor's 1911 scientific management separated intellectual from manual work and standardised every procedure; Taylorism times each task rigorously and sets a standard time for it; Ford's assembly line is the maximum specialisation of labour — each worker does one small operation as the work moves past.
