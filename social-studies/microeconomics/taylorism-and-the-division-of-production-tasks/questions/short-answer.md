# Taylorism and the division of production tasks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about taylor's scientific management, 1911?
**Answer:** Taylor's 1911 scientific management separated intellectual from manual work and standardised every procedure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about rigorous timekeeping of each task?
**Answer:** Taylorism times each task rigorously and sets a standard time for it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about ford's assembly line — specialisation at its maximum?
**Answer:** Ford's assembly line is the maximum specialisation of labour — each worker does one small operation as the work moves past.
