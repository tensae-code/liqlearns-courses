# Taylorism and the division of production tasks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About taylor's scientific management, 1911 — the lesson teaches that —
a) Taylor's 1911 scientific management separated intellectual from manual work and standardised every procedure  b) Taylorism times each task rigorously and sets a standard time for it  c) Ford's assembly line is the maximum specialisation of labour — each worker does one small operation as the work moves past
**Answer: a) Taylor's 1911 scientific management separated intellectual from manual work and standardised every procedure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About rigorous timekeeping of each task — the lesson teaches that —

**Answer: b) Taylorism times each task rigorously and sets a standard time for it**
