# Taylorism and the division of production tasks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Taylor proposed that each worker plan his own tasks freely.
**Answer: False** — his system separated the intellectual planning from the manual doing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Taylorism never measured how long tasks take.
**Answer: False** — rigorous timekeeping of each task is one of its named parts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On the assembly line each worker builds a whole car alone.
**Answer: False** — the line is the maximum specialisation — one small operation per worker.
