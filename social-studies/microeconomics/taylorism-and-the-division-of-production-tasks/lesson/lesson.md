## Sub lesson 1 — Taylor's scientific management, 1911

Taylor's 1911 Principles of Scientific Management proposed organising industrial work scientifically: specialisation of functions, standardised procedures, and the intellectual work of planning separated from the manual work of doing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rigorous timekeeping of each task

At the system's heart sat the stopwatch: rigorous timekeeping of each task, so that every movement had its measured standard time and every worker's output could be compared against it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ford's assembly line — specialisation at its maximum

Ford's assembly line carried the division of production tasks to its maximum: the work moves, the worker stays, and each worker performs one small specialised operation as each car passes — specialisation of labour at its fullest.
