# How industries are classified — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A steel plant is agro-based.
**Answer: False — steel is mineral-based.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cottage industry is the largest size class.
**Answer: False — cottage is the smallest; large scale is the largest.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ownership does not classify industries.
**Answer: False — private, public, joint and cooperative are ownership classes.**
