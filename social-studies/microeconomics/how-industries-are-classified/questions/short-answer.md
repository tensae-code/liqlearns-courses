# How industries are classified — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two raw-material classes of industry.
**Answer:** Any two of: agro, mineral, marine, forest based.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the size classes, and one more Class X axis.
**Answer:** Cottage, small scale, large scale; also by role (basic/key vs consumer) or by bulk and weight (heavy/light).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four ownership classes.
**Answer:** Private, public, joint and cooperative.
