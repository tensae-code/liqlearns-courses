# How industries are classified — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cotton mill is —
a) agro-based  b) mineral-based  c) marine-based
**Answer: a) agro-based**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By size, industries run —
a) cottage, small scale, large scale  b) tiny only  c) public only
**Answer: a) cottage, small scale, large scale**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A cooperative dairy belongs to the —
a) cooperative sector  b) private sector only  c) foreign sector
**Answer: a) cooperative sector**
