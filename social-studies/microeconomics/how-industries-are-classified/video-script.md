# Video script — How industries are classified

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How industries are classified — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: industries classified by raw material (agro, mineral, marine, forest based), by size (cottage, small scale, large scale) and by ownership (private, public, joint, cooperative) — and at Class X depth by role and by bulk and weight as well.
