# How industries are classified

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — By raw material — agro, mineral, marine, forest based

Industries are classified **by raw material: agro-based, mineral-based, marine-based and forest-based** — named for what they process.

## Sub lesson 2 — By size and by role — cottage to large scale; basic against consumer

They are classified **by size: cottage, small scale and large scale** — and at Class X depth **by role: basic or key industries against consumer industries**, and by capital investment.

## Sub lesson 3 — By ownership — private, public, joint, cooperative

They are classified **by ownership: private, public, joint and cooperative sectors** — who owns the industry decides its class.
