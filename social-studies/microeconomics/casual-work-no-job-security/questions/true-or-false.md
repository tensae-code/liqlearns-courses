# Casual work — no job security — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nirmala is a permanent manager.
**Answer: False — she is a casual worker in a garment unit.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order season means shorter hours for casual workers.
**Answer: False — she works very long hours then.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Casual workers keep their jobs between orders.
**Answer: False — Nirmala is laid off when orders end.**
