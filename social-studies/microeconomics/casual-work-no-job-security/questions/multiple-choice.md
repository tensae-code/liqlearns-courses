# Casual work — no job security — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nirmala works in —
a) an export garment unit  b) a bank  c) her own shop
**Answer: a) an export garment unit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the order season, hours are —
a) very long  b) very short  c) fixed by law at six
**Answer: a) very long**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When orders end, Nirmala is —
a) laid off  b) promoted  c) given paid leave
**Answer: a) laid off**
