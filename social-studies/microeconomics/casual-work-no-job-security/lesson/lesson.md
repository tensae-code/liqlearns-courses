# Casual work — no job security

## Sub lesson 1 — Nirmala in an export garment unit

**Nirmala** works in an **export garment unit** — sewing clothes for foreign orders as a casual worker.

## Sub lesson 2 — Very long hours in the order season

In the **order season** she works **very long hours** — when orders rush in, the casual worker's day stretches with them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Laid off when orders end — no security or protection

When **orders end, she is laid off** — casual factory work carries **no job security or protection** between orders.
