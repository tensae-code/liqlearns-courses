# Revenue, costs and profit of a business

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 8, Gross Income, Profit and Loss. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Total revenue — TR = p · q

**Total revenue** is all the money a firm takes in from selling its goods: **TR = p · q** — the price of one unit times the number of units sold.

## Sub lesson 2 — Profit and loss — P = TR − TC

**Profit** is what is left: **P = TR − TC**, total revenue minus total costs. When the difference is negative, the firm makes a **loss**. The book works this on Umid's photo studio's weekly accounts.

## Sub lesson 3 — Profit — the firm's thermometer and the reward for risk

The book calls profit the firm's **thermometer** — it shows the firm's health — and the **reward for risk**. Profit is used to pay taxes, to expand production and to pay dividends.
