# Revenue, costs and profit of a business — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** TR = p · q: price times the number of units sold.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When TR − TC is negative, the firm makes a profit.
**Answer: False** — It makes a loss.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit is used for taxes, expanding production and dividends.
**Answer: True**
