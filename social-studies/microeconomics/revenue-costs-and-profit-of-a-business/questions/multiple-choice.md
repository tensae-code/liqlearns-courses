# Revenue, costs and profit of a business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Total revenue TR equals —
a) p · q  b) p − q  c) q/p
**Answer: a) p · q**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Profit P equals —
a) TR + TC  b) TR − TC  c) TC − TR
**Answer: b) TR − TC**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book calls profit the firm's —
a) thermometer  b) charter  c) seal
**Answer: a) thermometer**
