## Sub lesson 1 — Equations as a tool of analysis

A simple linear equation is a tool of economic analysis, stating a straight-line relationship between two things.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading a linear equation

In a linear equation the two sides balance, and the unknown is found by undoing the operations around it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Using equations on economic problems

Economists set relationships such as demand or cost as equations and solve them to find the unknown value.
