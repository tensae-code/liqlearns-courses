# Simple linear equations in economics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a simple linear equation used for in economics?
**Answer:** A simple linear equation is a tool of economic analysis, stating a straight-line relationship between two things.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the unknown in a linear equation found?
**Answer:** In a linear equation the two sides balance, and the unknown is found by undoing the operations around it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do economists use equations on economic problems?
**Answer:** Economists set relationships such as demand or cost as equations and solve them to find the unknown value.
