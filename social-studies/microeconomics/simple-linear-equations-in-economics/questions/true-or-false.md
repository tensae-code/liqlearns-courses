# Simple linear equations in economics — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A linear equation can only be used in pure mathematics, never in economics.
**Answer: False** — A simple linear equation is a tool of economic analysis, stating a straight-line relationship between two things.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The two sides of an equation never need to balance.
**Answer: False** — In a linear equation the two sides balance, and the unknown is found by undoing the operations around it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Equations cannot be used for demand or cost.
**Answer: False** — Economists set relationships such as demand or cost as equations and solve them to find the unknown value.
