# Video script — Economic resources — turning nature into goods and services

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What turns nature into wealth people can use? Palestine's Grade 5 book answers: an economic resource is a natural resource people convert into a good or service. Soil when farmed. Minerals when extracted, traded or manufactured. Sunlight when used for power. Industries rest on those conversions — they raise income and create jobs. And the book shows it at home: Hebron stone and marble, quarried, cut and sold as building material and an export.
