# Hands-on — Economic resources — turning nature into goods and services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Set out soil in a pot, a stone, and a sunny windowsill. For each, say first what it is in nature, then the converting work that makes it an economic resource — farming, quarrying and cutting, using sunlight for power.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two columns — natural resource / economic resource — and put soil, minerals and sunlight in both, with the converting work written between them. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every conversion you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
