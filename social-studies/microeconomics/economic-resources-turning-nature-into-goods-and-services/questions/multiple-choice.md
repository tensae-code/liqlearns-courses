# Economic resources — turning nature into goods and services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is an economic resource, in the book's definition?
a) a natural resource people convert into a good or service they benefit from  b) any object found in nature, used or not  c) money saved in a bank
**Answer: a) a natural resource people convert into a good or service they benefit from**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do minerals become economic resources?
a) when they are extracted, traded or manufactured  b) when they are left unnamed  c) when they are photographed
**Answer: a) when they are extracted, traded or manufactured**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do economic resources matter, and what is the book's local case?
a) industries rest on them, raising income and creating jobs — Hebron stone and marble  b) they matter only as decoration  c) they lower income
**Answer: a) industries rest on them, raising income and creating jobs — Hebron stone and marble**
