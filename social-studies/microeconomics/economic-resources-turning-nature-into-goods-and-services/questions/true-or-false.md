# Economic resources — turning nature into goods and services — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil becomes an economic resource only when nobody touches it.
**Answer: False — soil becomes an economic resource when it is farmed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Minerals become economic resources when they are extracted, traded or manufactured.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Hebron stone and marble are the book's local case of an economic resource.
**Answer: True**
