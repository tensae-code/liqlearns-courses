# Economic resources — turning nature into goods and services — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When does sunlight become an economic resource?
**Answer:** When it is used for power — a natural resource (sunlight) converted into a service people benefit from.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three conversions the book gives.
**Answer:** Soil when farmed, minerals when extracted, traded or manufactured, and sunlight used for power.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does Hebron stone and marble show?
**Answer:** Quarried stone becomes jobs, building material and an export — a natural resource turned into an economic resource.
