# Economic resources — turning nature into goods and services — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An _____ resource is a natural resource people convert into a good or service.
**Answer: economic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ become economic resources when they are extracted, traded or manufactured.
**Answer: Minerals**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Industries rest on economic resources, raising income and creating _____.
**Answer: jobs**
