# Economic cost and accounting cost — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Accountants count only purchased inputs, the explicit costs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Accountants count only purchased inputs, the explicit costs.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The accountant's cost); sub lesson 2 says: Economic cost equals implicit costs plus explicit costs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The two profits differ because the economist subtracts implicit costs that the accountant leaves out.
**Answer: True**
