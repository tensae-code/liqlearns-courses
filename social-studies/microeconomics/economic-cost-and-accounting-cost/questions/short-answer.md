# Economic cost and accounting cost — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The accountant's cost" in your own words.
**Answer:** Accountants count only purchased inputs, the explicit costs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The economist's cost" in your own words.
**Answer:** Economic cost equals implicit costs plus explicit costs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the profits differ" in your own words.
**Answer:** The two profits differ because the economist subtracts implicit costs that the accountant leaves out.
