# Economic cost and accounting cost — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The accountant's cost"?
a) only the explicit costs — wages, materials, depreciation, interest, utilities  b) economic cost = implicit costs + explicit costs  c) economic profit is smaller by the implicit costs
**Answer: a) only the explicit costs — wages, materials, depreciation, interest, utilities**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The economist's cost"?
a) only the explicit costs — wages, materials, depreciation, interest, utilities  b) economic profit is smaller by the implicit costs  c) economic cost = implicit costs + explicit costs
**Answer: c) economic cost = implicit costs + explicit costs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the profits differ"?
a) economic cost = implicit costs + explicit costs  b) economic profit is smaller by the implicit costs  c) only the explicit costs — wages, materials, depreciation, interest, utilities
**Answer: b) economic profit is smaller by the implicit costs**
