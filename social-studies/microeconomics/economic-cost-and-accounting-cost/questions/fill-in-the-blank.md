# Economic cost and accounting cost — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Accountants count only the _____ costs.
**Answer: explicit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Economic cost = _____ costs + explicit costs.
**Answer: implicit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The economist's profit figure is _____ than the accountant's, by the implicit costs.
**Answer: smaller**
