# Hands-on — Economic cost and accounting cost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two ledgers on paper for the same pretend firm: build the accountant's first, then add the economist's extra lines in another colour and compare the two profit figures aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two ledgers for the same firm, the economist's ledger one line longer than the accountant's — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
