# Hands-on — What shifts demand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Draw one demand curve on paper. With arrows, show a partner: a movement along it when price changes, then the whole curve shifting right for higher income and left for a cheaper substitute.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — side-by-side diagrams — movement along a demand curve versus a shift of the whole curve — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
