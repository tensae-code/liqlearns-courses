# What shifts demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Prices of substitutes and complements, income, tastes, population, season and expectations all determine demand.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A change in own price moves you along the curve; a change in any other determinant shifts the whole curve.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the shift grid, a rightward demand shift raises both equilibrium price and equilibrium quantity.
**Answer: True**
