# What shifts demand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods used together, like tea and sugar or a phone and its SIM card, are called _____.
**Answer: complements**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When income changes, the whole demand curve _____ — it does not just move along itself.
**Answer: shifts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A rightward demand shift _____ both equilibrium price and equilibrium quantity.
**Answer: raises**
