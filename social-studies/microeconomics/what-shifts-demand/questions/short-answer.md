# What shifts demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The determinants beyond own price" in your own words.
**Answer:** Prices of substitutes and complements, income, tastes, population, season and expectations all determine demand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Movement along versus shift of the curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** A change in own price moves you along the curve; a change in any other determinant shifts the whole curve.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The shift-effects grid" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the shift grid, a rightward demand shift raises both equilibrium price and equilibrium quantity.
