# Movement along vs shift of the supply curve — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A movement along the supply curve leaves the curve itself in place.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Better technology shifts the supply curve right.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The movement/shift test is the same on the supply side as on the demand side.
**Answer: True**
