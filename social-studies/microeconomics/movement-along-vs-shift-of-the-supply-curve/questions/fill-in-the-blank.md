# Movement along vs shift of the supply curve — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An own-price change causes a _____ along the supply curve.
**Answer: movement**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Costlier inputs _____ the supply curve to the left.
**Answer: shift**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Own price means movement; another supply determinant means _____.
**Answer: shift**
