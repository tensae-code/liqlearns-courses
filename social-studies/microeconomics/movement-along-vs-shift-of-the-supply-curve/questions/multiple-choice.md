# Movement along vs shift of the supply curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If only the good's own price falls, quantity supplied —
a) shifts the curve left
b) moves along the same curve
c) is unchanged
**Answer: b) moves along the same curve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A rise in input prices causes supply to —
a) move along the curve
b) shift left
c) shift right
**Answer: b) shift left**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A failed harvest raises price. The first event is —
a) a movement along the supply curve
b) a shift of the supply curve
c) a demand shift
**Answer: b) a shift of the supply curve**
