# Movement along vs shift of the supply curve — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to the supply curve when only price changes?
**Answer:** Nothing — firms move along the same curve to a new quantity supplied.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two determinants that shift supply.
**Answer:** Input prices and technology (also: taxes on the producer).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the supply diagram test.
**Answer:** Own-price change → movement along the curve; any other supply determinant → shift of the whole curve.
