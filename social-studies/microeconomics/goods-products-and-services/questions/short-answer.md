# Goods, products and services — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a farmer's rice a good in one case and a product in another?
**Answer:** What it is produced for: grown for the farmer's own use it is a good; grown or taken to market for sale it is a product.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two perishable and two non-perishable products from the book.
**Answer:** Perishable: fruit and vegetables. Non-perishable: clothes, cosmetics and furniture (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a service?
**Answer:** Work done for other people — it can be paid.
