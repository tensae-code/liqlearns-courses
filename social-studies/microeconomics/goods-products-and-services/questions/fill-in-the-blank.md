# Goods, products and services — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Things stored in a market for sale are _____.
**Answer: products**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ products such as fruit and vegetables must be consumed or specially preserved at once.
**Answer: Perishable**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Work done for other people is a _____, which can be paid.
**Answer: service**
