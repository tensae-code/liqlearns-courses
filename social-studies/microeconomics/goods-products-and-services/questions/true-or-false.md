# Goods, products and services — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The same rice is a good when it is grown to eat and a product when it is taken to market to sell.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Clothes, cosmetics and furniture are perishable products.
**Answer: False** — they are non-perishable products; they keep until an expiry date.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A service can be paid.
**Answer: True**
