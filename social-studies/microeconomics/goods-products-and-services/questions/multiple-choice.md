# Goods, products and services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rice a farmer grows to eat at home is —
a) a product  b) a good  c) a service
**Answer: b) a good**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fruit and vegetables are perishable products, so they must be —
a) kept for years  b) consumed or specially preserved at once  c) never sold
**Answer: b) consumed or specially preserved at once**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Work done for other people is called —
a) a good  b) a product  c) a service
**Answer: c) a service**
