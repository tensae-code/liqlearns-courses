Bangladesh's own way (Bangladesh Grade 6 History and Social Science): in the new competency-based curriculum, Economics first appears inside History and Social Science as "Society and Resources" — children sort photographs of everything around them (water, sunlight, a farmer, a hammer, money, a bus) into kinds of resources before any formal definition, and learn goods vs products through a farmer's rice: grown to eat it is a good, grown to sell it is a product.

| Sub lesson 2 — Perishable and non-perishable products | Bangladesh, *Bangladesh Grade 7 History and Social Science*, About Wealth — teaches: perishable products (fruit, vegetables) must be consumed or specially preserved at once, while non-perishable products (clothes, cosmetics, furniture) keep until an expiry date and need their own preservation — printed p. 171 |

Bangladesh's own way (Bangladesh Grade 7 History and Social Science): Class 7 builds a full little production economics course inside "About Wealth" (সম্পদের কথা) — starting from a torn school uniform, it follows yarn to cloth to clothes to teach final, intermediate and primary goods, the four means of production, and what each supplier is paid, ending with a sharecropper and a factory owner side by side to show why incomes differ, and a woman construction worker paid less than the prescribed wage.
