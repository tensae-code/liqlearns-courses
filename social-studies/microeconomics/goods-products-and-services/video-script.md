# Video script — Goods, products and services

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Things stored in a market for sale are **products**; things produced for one's own use are **goods**.

Products split by how long they keep.

Work done for other people is a **service**.
