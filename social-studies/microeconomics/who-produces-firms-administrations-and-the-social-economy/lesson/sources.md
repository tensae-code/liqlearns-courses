| Bulgaria part — Economics expansion — "The enterprise — organisational form of business" (Предприятието – организационна форма на бизнеса): the organisational form of business is the enterprise, which produces goods, performs services or another entrepreneurial activity with the aim of profit; the firm (фирма) is the name under which the enterprise performs its activity — there is no enterprise without a definite name, just as there can be no firm without an enterprise; to do business "in the open" (на светло) the enterprise must have registration and act according to the country's laws — registration is the entering of the firm, its seat and management address, subject of activity, owner and manager in a special list, the Trade Register (Търговски регистър); the management of the firm covers the areas of production, sales, finances, personnel and innovations under the manager, set out in the firm's organisational-management structure (exercises, printed p. 69) (filed as microeconomics/enterprises-and-factories-as-producers; stocked here as the closest existing lesson covering that thing, the original slug recorded in this row) | Bulgaria, *Bulgaria Grade 7 Technology and Entrepreneurship*, "The enterprise — organisational form of business" (Предприятието – организационна форма на бизнеса) — printed pp. 66–69 |

France's own way (SES 2de, Manuels Libres): SES teaches economics, sociology and political science as one subject from Seconde; the economics is built from data and cases (INSEE, the France Relance plan read as a Keynesian model, housing and oil prices, the gender pay gap in spreadsheets) with a synthesis and exercise for nearly every notion. This lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.


Bulgaria's own way (Bulgaria Grade 4 Technology and Entrepreneurship): Grade 4 is where money itself becomes the topic — its history from barter to banknotes, the bank as the go-between of people with surplus money and people who need it, and taxes explained as the price of needs no one can meet alone.

Bulgaria's own way (Bulgaria Grade 7 Technology and Entrepreneurship): Grade 7 registers a firm — the class founds a student company, chooses between sole trader, partnership and limited company by who answers with their own property, writes its founding budget, and scores its market chance before it sells anything.
