# Who produces — firms, administrations and the social economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the manual mean by an enterprise's common structure?
**Answer:** Enterprises of every size are organised the same way: people arranged in a hierarchy, joined by the contract, producing goods and services to sell.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is an administration's production different from an enterprise's?
**Answer:** An administration produces for everyone, not for a buyer: its production is non-market, paid for collectively and given because people need it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the manual's three kinds of producers.
**Answer:** Enterprises, public administrations, and the social and solidarity economy (ESS).
