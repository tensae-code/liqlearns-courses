# Who produces — firms, administrations and the social economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What structure do enterprises share, in France's manual?
a) They are all owned by the state  b) A common structure — people organised in a hierarchy, joined by the contract  c) They have no organisation
**Answer: b) A common structure — people organised in a hierarchy, joined by the contract**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A public administration's production is non-market because —
a) it produces nothing real  b) it is offered to everyone and paid for collectively, not sold to each user at its price  c) it is produced abroad
**Answer: b) it is offered to everyone and paid for collectively, not sold to each user at its price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The social and solidarity economy (ESS) is built on the rule that —
a) profit comes before people  b) only the state may produce  c) people come before profit
**Answer: c) people come before profit**
