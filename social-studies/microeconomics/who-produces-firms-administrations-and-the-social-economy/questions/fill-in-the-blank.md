# Who produces — firms, administrations and the social economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Enterprises share a common structure: people organised in a _____, joined by the contract.
**Answer: hierarchy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Schooling, hospital care and roads produced for everyone by the state are the production of public _____.
**Answer: administrations**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ESS is the social and _____ economy.
**Answer: solidarity**
