# Who produces — firms, administrations and the social economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the manual, the contract is part of what joins the people of an enterprise into one structure.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A public school lesson and a private company's lesson are always the same kind of production, because the product is the same.
**Answer: False** — this lesson sorts by the producer's kind: a public school is an administration's non-market production; a company selling lessons is an enterprise's production.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cooperatives and associations of the ESS really produce and can really sell what they produce.
**Answer: True** — they produce like enterprises, but under the rule that people come before profit.
