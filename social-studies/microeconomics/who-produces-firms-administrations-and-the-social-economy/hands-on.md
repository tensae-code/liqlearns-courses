# Hands-on — Who produces — firms, administrations and the social economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Name five goods or services you used today. For each one, say out loud which kind of producer made it: an enterprise, a public administration, or an organisation of the social and solidarity economy. Say what tells you.

## Draw it and check it
Draw three boxes — ENTERPRISE, PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION, ESS — and put each of your five examples in its box, with the book's test beside it: does it sell what it produces, does it serve everyone, or does it put people before profit? Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every box you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
