# Video script — Who produces — firms, administrations and the social economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ask "who produces?" and France's Seconde manual gives three answers. First, the enterprise: bakeries, workshops and firms, all sharing one structure — people organised in a hierarchy, joined by a contract, producing to sell. Second, the public administrations: the state and its services, producing schools, hospitals and roads for everyone, not for a buyer. Third, the social and solidarity economy — the ESS: cooperatives and associations that produce with people, not profit, first.

Three kinds of producer. Every good and service you use comes from one of them.
