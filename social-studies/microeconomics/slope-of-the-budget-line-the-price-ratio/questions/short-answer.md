# Slope of the budget line — the price ratio — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The slope is a price ratio" in your own words.
**Answer:** The slope of the budget line is the negative of the price ratio of the two goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Verifying −0.2 two ways" in your own words.
**Answer:** The bread-and-sugar budget line's slope is −0.2, verified from both the price ratio and the intercepts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Fixed prices, constant slope" in your own words.
**Answer:** With fixed prices, the budget line is straight and its slope is constant along its whole length.
