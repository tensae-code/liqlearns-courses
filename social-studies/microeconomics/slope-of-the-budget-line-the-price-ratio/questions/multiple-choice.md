# Slope of the budget line — the price ratio — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The slope is a price ratio"?
a) slope = −(price of X / price of Y), the market's exchange rate between the goods  b) −0.2 from the price ratio and −0.2 again from the intercepts  c) fixed prices give a straight line whose slope only a price change can tilt
**Answer: a) slope = −(price of X / price of Y), the market's exchange rate between the goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Verifying −0.2 two ways"?
a) slope = −(price of X / price of Y), the market's exchange rate between the goods  b) fixed prices give a straight line whose slope only a price change can tilt  c) −0.2 from the price ratio and −0.2 again from the intercepts
**Answer: c) −0.2 from the price ratio and −0.2 again from the intercepts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Fixed prices, constant slope"?
a) −0.2 from the price ratio and −0.2 again from the intercepts  b) fixed prices give a straight line whose slope only a price change can tilt  c) slope = −(price of X / price of Y), the market's exchange rate between the goods
**Answer: b) fixed prices give a straight line whose slope only a price change can tilt**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Bread costs 4 birr and sugar costs 20 birr. What is the slope of the budget line with bread on the X-axis?
a) -0.2  b) -5  c) 0.2
**Answer: a) -0.2** — Slope = −Px/Py = −4/20 = −0.2.
