# Slope of the budget line — the price ratio — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The budget line's slope is the negative of the _____ of the X-axis good to the Y-axis good.
**Answer: price ratio**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For bread at 4 birr against sugar at 20 birr, the budget line's slope is _____.
**Answer: −0.2**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With fixed prices the budget line is straight and its slope is _____ from end to end.
**Answer: constant**
