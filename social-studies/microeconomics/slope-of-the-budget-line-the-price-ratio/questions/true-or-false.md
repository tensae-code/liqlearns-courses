# Slope of the budget line — the price ratio — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slope of the budget line is the negative of the price ratio of the two goods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The bread-and-sugar budget line's slope is −0.2, verified from both the price ratio and the intercepts.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With fixed prices, the budget line is straight and its slope is constant along its whole length.
**Answer: True**
