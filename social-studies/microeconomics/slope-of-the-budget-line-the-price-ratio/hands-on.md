# Hands-on — Slope of the budget line — the price ratio

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Walk a chalk budget line on the ground: step 5 paces along the bread axis for every 1 pace up the sugar axis, and feel the steady steepness that never changes from end to end.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the same line with rise-over-run triangles marked between two pairs of points — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
