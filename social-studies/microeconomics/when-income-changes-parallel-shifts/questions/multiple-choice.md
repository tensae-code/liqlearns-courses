# When income changes — parallel shifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Higher income — both intercepts rise"?
a) both intercepts rise in the same proportion when income rises  b) both intercepts fall together and the line shifts inward, parallel  c) the slope is a price ratio, so unchanged prices mean a parallel shift
**Answer: a) both intercepts rise in the same proportion when income rises**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Lower income — the line shifts inward"?
a) both intercepts rise in the same proportion when income rises  b) the slope is a price ratio, so unchanged prices mean a parallel shift  c) both intercepts fall together and the line shifts inward, parallel
**Answer: c) both intercepts fall together and the line shifts inward, parallel**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the shift is parallel"?
a) both intercepts fall together and the line shifts inward, parallel  b) the slope is a price ratio, so unchanged prices mean a parallel shift  c) both intercepts rise in the same proportion when income rises
**Answer: b) the slope is a price ratio, so unchanged prices mean a parallel shift**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Income rises from 100 to 200 birr with bread at 4 birr and sugar at 20 birr. What are the new intercepts?
a) 50 breads and 10 sugars  b) 25 breads and 10 sugars  c) 50 breads and 5 sugars
**Answer: a) 50 breads and 10 sugars** — 200 / 4 = 50 breads and 200 / 20 = 10 sugars — both intercepts double.
