# When income changes — parallel shifts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Higher income — both intercepts rise" in your own words.
**Answer:** When income rises at fixed prices, both intercepts of the budget line rise in the same proportion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Lower income — the line shifts inward" in your own words.
**Answer:** When income falls at fixed prices, the budget line shifts inward parallel to itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the shift is parallel" in your own words.
**Answer:** Income changes shift the budget line in parallel because its slope — a price ratio — does not change.
