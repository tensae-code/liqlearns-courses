# When income changes — parallel shifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An income rise at fixed prices raises _____ in the same proportion, shifting the line outward.
**Answer: both intercepts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fall in income shifts the budget line _____, parallel to itself.
**Answer: inward**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The shift is parallel because the line's _____ — a price ratio — has not changed.
**Answer: slope**
