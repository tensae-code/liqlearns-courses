# Hands-on — When income changes — parallel shifts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay a string budget line on a table. Slide the whole string away from the corner without rotating it — that is an income rise. Slide it back toward the corner — an income fall. Say what stayed the same.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three parallel budget lines fanning outward as income rises — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
