# Video script — Behaviours of producers and consumers in communist economies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The influence of communist ideology on the exchanging of goods and services, and producer and consumer behaviour patterns in communist economies, evaluated.

How socialism influences the exchange of goods and services between government and people, producer and consumer behaviour patterns in a socialist economy, and how private enterprises operate inside a communist economy.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.
