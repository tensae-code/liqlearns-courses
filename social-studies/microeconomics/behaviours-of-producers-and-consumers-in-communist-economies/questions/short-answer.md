# Behaviours of producers and consumers in communist economies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "The influence of communist ideology on the exchanging"?
**Answer:** The influence of communist ideology on the exchanging of goods and services, and producer and consumer behaviour patterns in communist economies, evaluated.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "How socialism influences the exchange of goods and"?
**Answer:** How socialism influences the exchange of goods and services between government and people, producer and consumer behaviour patterns in a socialist economy, and how private enterprises operate inside a communist economy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.
