# Video script — Small-scale and large-scale production

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Small-scale and large-scale production — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: small-scale production works with little capital and few workers, judged on its own terms; the named strength of small-scale production is the producer's independence; a named cost of small scale is wastage of by-products.
