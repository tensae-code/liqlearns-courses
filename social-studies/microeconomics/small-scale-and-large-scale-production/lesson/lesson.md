# Small-scale and large-scale production

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — small-scale and large-scale production — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Small-scale production on its own terms

The Class 11 book gives **small-scale production** its own section: production carried on with little capital and few workers, judged on its own terms and not only as a failed large firm.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its benefit — independence

The strength the book names for small-scale production is **independence**: the small producer is his own master, deciding what to make and how to work without answering to a large organisation.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its costs — set against large-scale

Small scale has costs too: the book names **wastage of by-products** — what a large firm would use or sell, the small producer throws away. Set side by side, small and large scale each win on different ground.
