# Small-scale and large-scale production — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book treat small-scale production?
**Answer:** As production with little capital and few workers, judged on its own terms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What benefit of small-scale production does the book name?
**Answer:** Independence — the small producer is his own master.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What cost of small-scale production does the book name?
**Answer:** Wastage of by-products — what a large firm would use or sell, the small producer throws away.
