# Small-scale and large-scale production — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About small-scale production on its own terms — the lesson teaches that —
a) small-scale production works with little capital and few workers, judged on its own terms  b) the named strength of small-scale production is the producer's independence  c) a named cost of small scale is wastage of by-products
**Answer: a) small-scale production works with little capital and few workers, judged on its own terms**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About its benefit — independence — the lesson teaches that —
a) small-scale production works with little capital and few workers, judged on its own terms  b) the named strength of small-scale production is the producer's independence  c) a named cost of small scale is wastage of by-products
**Answer: b) the named strength of small-scale production is the producer's independence**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About its costs — set against large-scale — the lesson teaches that —
a) small-scale production works with little capital and few workers, judged on its own terms  b) the named strength of small-scale production is the producer's independence  c) a named cost of small scale is wastage of by-products
**Answer: c) a named cost of small scale is wastage of by-products**
