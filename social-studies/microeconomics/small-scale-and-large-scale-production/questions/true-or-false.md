# Small-scale and large-scale production — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Small-scale production is just a large firm that failed.
**Answer: False** — the book judges it on its own terms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Small producers must answer to a large organisation for every decision.
**Answer: False** — independence — being one's own master — is its named strength.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Small-scale production wastes nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — wastage of by-products is its named cost.
