# Small-scale and large-scale production — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production with little capital and few workers is _____-scale production.
**Answer: small**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The small producer's named strength, being his own master, is _____.
**Answer: independence**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A named cost of small-scale production is wastage of _____.
**Answer: by-products**
