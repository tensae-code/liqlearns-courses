# Feudalism — land, serfdom and the self-sufficient manor — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was the base of wealth in the feudal mode of production?
**Answer:** Land ownership.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did serfs give the lord, and what did they hold in return?
**Answer:** They gave part of the harvest and three days of labour a week on his reserve, walls and defence; they held plots for life for their family's subsistence.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happened to town merchant economies as fiefs became self-sufficient?
**Answer:** They fell into ruin, because the fiefs produced everything they needed themselves.
