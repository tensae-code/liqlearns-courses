# Feudalism — land, serfdom and the self-sufficient manor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under feudalism, wealth was based mainly on owning factories.
**Answer: False** — land ownership was the base of wealth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Serfs worked the lord's reserve, walls and defence three days a week.
**Answer: True** — alongside handing over part of the harvest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fief bought most of what its inhabitants needed from towns.
**Answer: False** — the fief produced everything its inhabitants needed.
