# Feudalism — land, serfdom and the self-sufficient manor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the feudal mode of production, the base of wealth was —
a) land ownership  b) money in banks  c) factory machinery
**Answer: a) land ownership**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Serfs gave their lord —
a) part of the harvest and three days of labour a week  b) money rent only  c) one day of labour a year
**Answer: a) part of the harvest and three days of labour a week**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Great fiefs became —
a) self-sufficient production centres  b) great trading ports  c) industrial towns
**Answer: a) self-sufficient production centres**
