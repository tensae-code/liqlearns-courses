# Hands-on — Oligopoly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two learners run rival cement stalls with price cards. One cuts a price; the other must respond on the spot. The class narrates the interdependence after three rounds, then replays with ten stalls to feel the difference.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two large cement plants facing each other, and two telecom shops side by side on one street — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
