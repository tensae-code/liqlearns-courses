# Oligopoly — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oligopoly's few firms are _____ — each must guess the others' reactions.
**Answer: interdependent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Scale, capital, patents and raw-material control are oligopoly's entry _____.
**Answer: barriers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A duopoly has _____ firms — Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia are the living example.
**Answer: two**
