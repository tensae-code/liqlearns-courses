# Oligopoly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A few firms, each watching the others"?
a) a few interdependent firms — each firm's demand shifts as rivals react  b) entry barriers of scale, capital, patents and raw-material access  c) duopoly: two firms — Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia
**Answer: a) a few interdependent firms — each firm's demand shifts as rivals react**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Barriers and the Ethiopian cases"?
a) a few interdependent firms — each firm's demand shifts as rivals react  b) duopoly: two firms — Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia  c) entry barriers of scale, capital, patents and raw-material access
**Answer: c) entry barriers of scale, capital, patents and raw-material access**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Duopoly — the two-firm case"?
a) entry barriers of scale, capital, patents and raw-material access  b) duopoly: two firms — Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia  c) a few interdependent firms — each firm's demand shifts as rivals react
**Answer: b) duopoly: two firms — Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia**
