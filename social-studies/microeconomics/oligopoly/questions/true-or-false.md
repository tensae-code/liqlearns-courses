# Oligopoly — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oligopoly's defining trait is interdependence among a few firms.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Entry into oligopoly is blocked by barriers such as scale, capital, patents and raw-material access.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Duopoly is the two-firm case of oligopoly, seen in Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**
