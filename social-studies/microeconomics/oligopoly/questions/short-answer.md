# Oligopoly — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A few firms, each watching the others" in your own words.
**Answer:** Oligopoly's defining trait is interdependence among a few firms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Barriers and the Ethiopian cases" in your own words.
**Answer:** Entry into oligopoly is blocked by barriers such as scale, capital, patents and raw-material access.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Duopoly — the two-firm case" in your own words.
**Answer:** Duopoly is the two-firm case of oligopoly, seen in Ethio Telecom and Safaricom Ethiopia.
