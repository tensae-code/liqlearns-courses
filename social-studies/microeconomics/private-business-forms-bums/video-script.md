# Video script — Private business forms — BUMS

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Private business forms (BUMS) start with the sole proprietorship, owned and run by one person, and the firma, a partnership run under a shared name.

A CV is a limited partnership with active and silent partners, while a limited company (PT) raises capital in shares owned by shareholders.

BUMS drive much of Indonesia's private activity; each form trades off control, capital and the owner's risk in a different way.
