# Private business forms — BUMS — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private business forms (BUMS) start with the sole proprietorship, owned and run by one person, and the firma, a partnership run under a shared name.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A CV is a limited partnership with active and silent partners, while a limited company (PT) raises capital in shares owned by shareholders.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** BUMS drive much of Indonesia's private activity; each form trades off control, capital and the something else entirely in a different way.
**Answer: False** — BUMS drive much of Indonesia's private activity; each form trades off control, capital and the owner's risk in a different way.
