# Private business forms — BUMS — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private business forms (BUMS) start with the sole proprietorship, owned and run by one person, and the _____, a partnership run under a shared name.
**Answer: firma**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A CV is a limited partnership with active and silent partners, while a limited company (PT) raises capital in _____ owned by shareholders.
**Answer: shares**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** BUMS drive much of Indonesia's private activity; each form trades off control, capital and the _____ in a different way.
**Answer: owner's risk**
