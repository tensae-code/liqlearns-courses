# Private business forms — BUMS — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the sole proprietorship and the firma?
**Answer:** Private business forms (BUMS) start with the sole proprietorship, owned and run by one person, and the firma, a partnership run under a shared name.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the cv partnership and the limited company?
**Answer:** A CV is a limited partnership with active and silent partners, while a limited company (PT) raises capital in shares owned by shareholders.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about their role, strengths and weaknesses?
**Answer:** BUMS drive much of Indonesia's private activity; each form trades off control, capital and the owner's risk in a different way.
