# Video script — Two ways of reasoning: deduction and induction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Deduction reasons from the general to the particular. You start with a general principle, apply it to a specific case, and then check the result against the facts. The movement is downward: rule first, case second.

Induction reasons the other way: from the particular to the general. You collect observations and build a generalisation from them — the book's example generalises from grain prices falling at harvest time, when supply rises.

The two methods complement each other. Induction builds rules out of real markets; deduction tests those rules on new cases. Sound economics moves up and down between fact and rule, never trusting one direction alone.
