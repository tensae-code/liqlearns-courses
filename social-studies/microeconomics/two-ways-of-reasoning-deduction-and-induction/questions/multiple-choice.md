# Two ways of reasoning: deduction and induction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Deduction — from the rule down"?
a) reasoning from the general to the particular  b) building a general rule from collected observations  c) the two methods complement each other
**Answer: a) reasoning from the general to the particular**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Induction — from cases up"?
a) reasoning from the general to the particular  b) the two methods complement each other  c) building a general rule from collected observations
**Answer: c) building a general rule from collected observations**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The two together"?
a) building a general rule from collected observations  b) the two methods complement each other  c) reasoning from the general to the particular
**Answer: b) the two methods complement each other**
