# Two ways of reasoning: deduction and induction — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Deduction reasons from the general to the particular, then checks the case against facts.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deduction reasons from the general to the particular, then checks the case against facts.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Deduction — from the rule down); sub lesson 2 says: Induction builds a generalisation from collected observations, like grain prices falling at harvest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Deduction and induction complement each other — one builds rules, the other tests them on new cases.
**Answer: True**
