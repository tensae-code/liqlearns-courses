# Two ways of reasoning: deduction and induction — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Deduction — from the rule down" in your own words.
**Answer:** Deduction reasons from the general to the particular, then checks the case against facts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Induction — from cases up" in your own words.
**Answer:** Induction builds a generalisation from collected observations, like grain prices falling at harvest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The two together" in your own words.
**Answer:** Deduction and induction complement each other — one builds rules, the other tests them on new cases.
