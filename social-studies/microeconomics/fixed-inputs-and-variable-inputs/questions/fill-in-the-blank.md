# Fixed inputs and variable inputs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fixed input's quantity _____ be changed in the period considered.
**Answer: cannot**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A variable input's quantity _____ be changed in the period considered.
**Answer: can**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The periods of production are defined by the input distinction, not the _____.
**Answer: calendar**
