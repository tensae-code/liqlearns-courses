# Fixed inputs and variable inputs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fixed input's quantity cannot be changed in the period considered, like buildings and machinery.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fixed input's quantity cannot be changed in the period considered, like buildings and machinery.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Fixed inputs); sub lesson 2 says: A variable input's quantity can be changed in the period considered, like raw materials and labour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fixed–variable distinction, not the calendar, defines the periods of production.
**Answer: True**
