# Fixed inputs and variable inputs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Fixed inputs" in your own words.
**Answer:** A fixed input's quantity cannot be changed in the period considered, like buildings and machinery.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Variable inputs" in your own words.
**Answer:** A variable input's quantity can be changed in the period considered, like raw materials and labour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The distinction defines the periods" in your own words.
**Answer:** The fixed–variable distinction, not the calendar, defines the periods of production.
