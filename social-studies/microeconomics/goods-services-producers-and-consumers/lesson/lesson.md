# Goods, services, producers and consumers

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — goods, services, producers and consumers — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Goods — things you can hold

In Pakistan's Class 4 Social Studies book, the Economics chapter starts by sorting the things around the learner. **Goods** are things you can hold and use — the goods pictured in a shop. A good is a thing, made or grown, that meets a want.

## Sub lesson 2 — Services — work done for you

A **service** is work done for you by someone else — the book's example is the doctor, whose help you use but cannot hold in your hand. Goods and services are the two kinds of thing an economy provides.

## Sub lesson 3 — Producer and consumer

The **producer** is who makes or provides the goods and services; the **consumer** is who uses them. The same chapter that names goods and services names these two people facing each other in every exchange.
