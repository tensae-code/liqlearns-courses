# Goods, services, producers and consumers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About goods — things you can hold — the lesson teaches that —
a) goods are things you can hold and use  b) a service is work done for you, like a doctor's help  c) the producer makes or provides, and the consumer uses
**Answer: a) goods are things you can hold and use**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About services — work done for you — the lesson teaches that —
a) goods are things you can hold and use  b) a service is work done for you, like a doctor's help  c) the producer makes or provides, and the consumer uses
**Answer: b) a service is work done for you, like a doctor's help**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About producer and consumer — the lesson teaches that —
a) goods are things you can hold and use  b) a service is work done for you, like a doctor's help  c) the producer makes or provides, and the consumer uses
**Answer: c) the producer makes or provides, and the consumer uses**
