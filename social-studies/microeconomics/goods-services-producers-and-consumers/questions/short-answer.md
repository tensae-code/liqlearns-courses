# Goods, services, producers and consumers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are goods?
**Answer:** Goods are things you can hold and use, made or grown to meet a want.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a service, and what example does the book give?
**Answer:** A service is work done for you; the book's example is the doctor.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who is the producer and who is the consumer?
**Answer:** The producer makes or provides goods and services; the consumer uses them.
