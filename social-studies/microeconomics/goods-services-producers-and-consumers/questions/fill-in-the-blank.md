# Goods, services, producers and consumers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Things you can hold and use, like the goods in a shop, are called _____.
**Answer: goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Work done for you by someone else, like a doctor's help, is a _____.
**Answer: service**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who makes or provides goods and services is the _____; who uses them is the consumer.
**Answer: producer**
