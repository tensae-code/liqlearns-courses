# Goods, services, producers and consumers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods are actions that someone does for you.
**Answer: False** — actions done for you are services; goods are things you can hold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A doctor's help is a good because you can hold it.
**Answer: False** — you cannot hold the doctor's work; it is a service.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The consumer is the one who makes the goods.
**Answer: False** — the maker is the producer; the consumer uses what is made.
