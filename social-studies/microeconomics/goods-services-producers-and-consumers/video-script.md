# Video script — Goods, services, producers and consumers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Goods, services, producers and consumers — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: goods are things you can hold and use; a service is work done for you, like a doctor's help; the producer makes or provides, and the consumer uses.
