# Hands-on — Average and marginal cost curves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Trace the curves in the air as a partner reads outputs 0 to 7: hand falling for MC, flattening, then rising through the bottoms of the AVC and ATC curves the class draws on the board.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the four cost curves with marginal cost crossing AVC and ATC at their bottoms, and product and cost curves as mirror images — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
