# Average and marginal cost curves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The per-unit family" in your own words.
**Answer:** Average total cost equals average fixed cost plus average variable cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "MC's crossings and optimum capacity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal cost cuts average total cost at average total cost's minimum point.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Long-run curves and the mirror rules" in your own words.
**Answer:** When average and marginal product peak, average and marginal cost bottom out.
