# Average and marginal cost curves — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ATC equals AFC plus _____ — read as a vertical distance between curves.
**Answer: AVC**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** MC cuts ATC at ATC's _____ point — the optimum capacity.
**Answer: minimum**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When AP and MP peak, AC and MC _____ — the mirror-image rule.
**Answer: bottom out**
