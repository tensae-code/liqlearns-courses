# Average and marginal cost curves — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Average total cost equals average fixed cost plus average variable cost.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marginal cost cuts average total cost at average total cost's minimum point.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When average and marginal product peak, average and marginal cost bottom out.
**Answer: True**
