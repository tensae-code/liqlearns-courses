# Average and marginal cost curves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The per-unit family"?
a) AFC, AVC and ATC per unit, plus MC — the cost of one more unit  b) MC bottoming at 5 at 5 units and cutting AVC and ATC at their minimums  c) flatter long-run U-shapes; product and cost curves as mirror images
**Answer: a) AFC, AVC and ATC per unit, plus MC — the cost of one more unit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "MC's crossings and optimum capacity"?
a) AFC, AVC and ATC per unit, plus MC — the cost of one more unit  b) flatter long-run U-shapes; product and cost curves as mirror images  c) MC bottoming at 5 at 5 units and cutting AVC and ATC at their minimums
**Answer: c) MC bottoming at 5 at 5 units and cutting AVC and ATC at their minimums**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Long-run curves and the mirror rules"?
a) MC bottoming at 5 at 5 units and cutting AVC and ATC at their minimums  b) flatter long-run U-shapes; product and cost curves as mirror images  c) AFC, AVC and ATC per unit, plus MC — the cost of one more unit
**Answer: b) flatter long-run U-shapes; product and cost curves as mirror images**
