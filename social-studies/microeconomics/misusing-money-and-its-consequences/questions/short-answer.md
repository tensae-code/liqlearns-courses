# Misusing money and its consequences — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about misusing money in the home?
**Answer:** Misusing money is using the family's money badly — not on what the home needs. The book teaches this as a home lesson, in the family, where money is first handled.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about poverty, loss and hunger?
**Answer:** The first consequences the book names are poverty, loss and hunger — the home that misuses money ends with less, and with not enough to eat.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about theft, quarrels and suffering?
**Answer:** Misusing money also leads to theft, quarrels and suffering in the home — the harm spreads from money to how people live together.
