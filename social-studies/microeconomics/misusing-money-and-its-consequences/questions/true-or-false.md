# Misusing money and its consequences — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Misusing money is using the family's money badly — not on what the home needs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first consequences the book names are poverty, loss and hunger — the home that misuses money ends with less, and with not enough to eat.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Misusing money also leads to theft, quarrels and suffering in the home — the harm spreads from money to how people live together.
**Answer: True**
