# Video script — Misusing money and its consequences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Misusing money and its consequences — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: Misusing money is using the family's money badly — not on what the home needs. The book teaches this as a home lesson, in the family, where money is first handled. The first consequences the book names are poverty, loss and hunger — the home that misuses money ends with less, and with not enough to eat. Misusing money also leads to theft, quarrels and suffering in the home — the harm spreads from money to how people live together.
