# Mergers, concentration ratios and the HHI — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Firms combine by mergers and _____.
**Answer: acquisitions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ law's purpose is to guard competition.
**Answer: Antitrust**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ Index squares each firm's market share and adds them.
**Answer: Herfindahl-Hirschman (HHI)**
