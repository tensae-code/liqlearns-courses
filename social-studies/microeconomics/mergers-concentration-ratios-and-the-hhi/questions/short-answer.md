# Mergers, concentration ratios and the HHI — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What can a merger do to a market?
**Answer:** Shrink the number of rivals, raising market power.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are mergers reviewed?
**Answer:** Because the combined firm may gain power over price and harm competition.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two measures of market power in this lesson.
**Answer:** Concentration ratios and the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI).
