# Mergers, concentration ratios and the HHI — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mergers always increase the number of rivals.
**Answer: False — mergers can shrink the number of rivals.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Antitrust law aims to create monopolies.
**Answer: False — its purpose is to guard competition.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A concentration ratio squares market shares.
**Answer: False — squaring shares is the HHI; a concentration ratio adds the top firms' shares.**
