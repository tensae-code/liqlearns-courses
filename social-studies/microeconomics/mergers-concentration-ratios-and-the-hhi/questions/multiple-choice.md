# Mergers, concentration ratios and the HHI — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A merger is —
a) firms joining together  b) a firm splitting apart  c) a new tax
**Answer: a) firms joining together**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Antitrust law exists to —
a) guard competition  b) protect monopolies  c) fix all prices
**Answer: a) guard competition**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The HHI is calculated by —
a) squaring each firm's share and adding them  b) counting firms only  c) averaging prices
**Answer: a) squaring each firm's share and adding them**
