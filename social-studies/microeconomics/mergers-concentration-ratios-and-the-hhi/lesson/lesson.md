## Sub lesson 1 — Mergers and acquisitions

Firms combine by **mergers and acquisitions** — joining together or one buying another — which can **shrink the number of rivals** in a market.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why antitrust law watches

**Antitrust law's purpose** is to **guard competition** — mergers are reviewed because a combined firm may gain power over price.

## Sub lesson 3 — Concentration ratios and the HHI

Market power is **measured** by **concentration ratios** (the top firms' combined share) and the **Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI)**, which squares each firm's market share and adds them.
