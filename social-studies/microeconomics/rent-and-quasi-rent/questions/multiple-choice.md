# Rent and quasi-rent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About rent as a factor reward — the lesson teaches that —
a) rent is the reward paid for the use of land  b) on the Ricardian line, rent comes from fertility differences and is not a part of price  c) quasi-rent is the short-run extra earning of a man-made factor in fixed supply
**Answer: a) rent is the reward paid for the use of land**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About the ricardian line — fertility and price — the lesson teaches that —
a) rent is the reward paid for the use of land  b) on the Ricardian line, rent comes from fertility differences and is not a part of price  c) quasi-rent is the short-run extra earning of a man-made factor in fixed supply
**Answer: b) on the Ricardian line, rent comes from fertility differences and is not a part of price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About quasi-rent — the lesson teaches that —
a) rent is the reward paid for the use of land  b) on the Ricardian line, rent comes from fertility differences and is not a part of price  c) quasi-rent is the short-run extra earning of a man-made factor in fixed supply
**Answer: c) quasi-rent is the short-run extra earning of a man-made factor in fixed supply**
