# Rent and quasi-rent — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The reward paid for the use of land is _____.
**Answer: rent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On the Ricardian line, rent arises from differences in land's _____.
**Answer: fertility**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The rent-like short-run earning of a machine is called _____-rent.
**Answer: quasi**
