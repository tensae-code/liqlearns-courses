# Rent and quasi-rent — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is rent?
**Answer:** Rent is the reward paid for the use of land, one of the factor rewards.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the Ricardian account of rent?
**Answer:** Land differs in fertility; the better land earns rent over the worst land in use, and rent is not a part of the price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is quasi-rent?
**Answer:** Quasi-rent is the extra earning, in the short run, of a man-made factor whose supply cannot quickly be increased.
