# Rent and quasi-rent — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rent is the reward of labour.
**Answer: False** — labour's reward is wages; rent is land's reward.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ricardo taught that rent decides the price of produce.
**Answer: False** — on his line price is set on the worst land and rent follows — rent is not a part of price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Quasi-rent is paid for land only, forever.
**Answer: False** — it is the rent-like earning of man-made factors, for a time only.
