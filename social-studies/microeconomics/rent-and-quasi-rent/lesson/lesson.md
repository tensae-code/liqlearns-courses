# Rent and quasi-rent

## Sub lesson 1 — Rent as a factor reward

The Class 11 distribution chapter opens with **rent**: the reward paid for the use of land, one of the factor rewards alongside wages, interest and profit. The book first sets out rent's kinds.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Ricardian line — fertility and price

The book teaches the **Ricardian** account: land differs in **fertility**, and the better land earns rent over the worst land in use. On this line, **rent is not a part of the price** of the produce — price is set on the worst land, and rent follows from it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Quasi-rent

Beside true rent stands **quasi-rent**: the extra earning of a man-made factor — like a machine — whose supply cannot be increased quickly in the short run. It is rent-like, but only for a time.
