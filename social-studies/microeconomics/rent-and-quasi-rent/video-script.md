# Video script — Rent and quasi-rent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Rent and quasi-rent — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: rent is the reward paid for the use of land; on the Ricardian line, rent comes from fertility differences and is not a part of price; quasi-rent is the short-run extra earning of a man-made factor in fixed supply.
