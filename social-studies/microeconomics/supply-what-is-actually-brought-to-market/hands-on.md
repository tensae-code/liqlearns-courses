# Hands-on — Supply — what is actually brought to market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pack a box with 20 'produce' cards. Deal some to a 'market table' and keep the rest in a 'cold store' box; the class reads off stock and supply from the two piles.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a full cold store with only part of its sacks loaded onto a market truck — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
