# Supply — what is actually brought to market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Stock — all that exists" in your own words.
**Answer:** Stock is everything produced minus what is already sold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Supply — what reaches the market" in your own words.
**Answer:** Supply is the quantity actually brought to market, and it can be less than or equal to stock.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The definition, with price in it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Supply is the quantities producers are willing and able to offer at various prices in a given period.
