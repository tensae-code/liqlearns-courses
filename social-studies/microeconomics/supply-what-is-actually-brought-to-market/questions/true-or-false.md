# Supply — what is actually brought to market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stock is everything produced minus what is already sold.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Stock is everything produced minus what is already sold.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Stock — all that exists); sub lesson 2 says: Supply is the quantity actually brought to market, and it can be less than or equal to stock.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Supply is the quantities producers are willing and able to offer at various prices in a given period.
**Answer: True**
