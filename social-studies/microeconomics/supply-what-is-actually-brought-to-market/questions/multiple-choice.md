# Supply — what is actually brought to market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Stock — all that exists"?
a) everything produced minus what is already sold  b) supply is at most stock — producers hold stock back for better prices  c) quantities willing and able to be offered at various prices; quantity supplied at one price
**Answer: a) everything produced minus what is already sold**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Supply — what reaches the market"?
a) everything produced minus what is already sold  b) quantities willing and able to be offered at various prices; quantity supplied at one price  c) supply is at most stock — producers hold stock back for better prices
**Answer: c) supply is at most stock — producers hold stock back for better prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The definition, with price in it"?
a) supply is at most stock — producers hold stock back for better prices  b) quantities willing and able to be offered at various prices; quantity supplied at one price  c) everything produced minus what is already sold
**Answer: b) quantities willing and able to be offered at various prices; quantity supplied at one price**
