# Supply — what is actually brought to market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stock is everything produced minus what is already _____.
**Answer: sold**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Supply can be _____ than or equal to stock, never more.
**Answer: less**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Supply is the quantities producers are willing and able to _____ for sale at various prices.
**Answer: offer**
