# Video script — Supply — what is actually brought to market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stock is everything produced minus what is already sold: the whole amount on hand, wherever it is sitting. Stock counts existence; it says nothing about what is offered for sale today.

Supply is only the quantity actually brought to market at a given time and price. Producers hold stock back — the book's example: harvest produce kept in cold storage for better off-season prices — so supply can be less than or equal to stock, never more.

The supply of a commodity is the various quantities producers are willing and able to offer for sale at various prices in a given period; quantity supplied is the amount at one specific price — the mirror image of the demand definitions.
