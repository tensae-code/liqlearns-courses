# Indifference curves and the indifference map — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The indifference schedule" in your own words.
**Answer:** An indifference schedule lists combinations of two goods that give the same satisfaction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The indifference curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** An indifference curve is also called an iso-utility curve, because satisfaction is equal all along it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The indifference map" in your own words.
**Answer:** On an indifference map, curves farther from the origin carry higher satisfaction.
