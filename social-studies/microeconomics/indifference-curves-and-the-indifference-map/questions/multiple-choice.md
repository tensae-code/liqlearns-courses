# Indifference curves and the indifference map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The indifference schedule"?
a) a table of two-good combinations giving equal satisfaction  b) the plotted curve joining all equal-satisfaction combinations  c) a whole family of curves; farther from the origin means more satisfaction
**Answer: a) a table of two-good combinations giving equal satisfaction**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The indifference curve"?
a) a table of two-good combinations giving equal satisfaction  b) a whole family of curves; farther from the origin means more satisfaction  c) the plotted curve joining all equal-satisfaction combinations
**Answer: c) the plotted curve joining all equal-satisfaction combinations**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The indifference map"?
a) the plotted curve joining all equal-satisfaction combinations  b) a whole family of curves; farther from the origin means more satisfaction  c) a table of two-good combinations giving equal satisfaction
**Answer: b) a whole family of curves; farther from the origin means more satisfaction**
