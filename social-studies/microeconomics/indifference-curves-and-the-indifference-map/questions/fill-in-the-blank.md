# Indifference curves and the indifference map — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A table of equal-satisfaction combinations of two goods is an indifference _____.
**Answer: schedule**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An indifference curve is also called an _____ curve — satisfaction is equal at every point on it.
**Answer: iso-utility**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A whole family of indifference curves is called an indifference _____.
**Answer: map**
