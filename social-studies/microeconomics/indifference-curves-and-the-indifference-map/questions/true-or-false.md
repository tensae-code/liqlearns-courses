# Indifference curves and the indifference map — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An indifference schedule lists combinations of two goods that give the same satisfaction.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An indifference curve is also called an iso-utility curve, because satisfaction is equal all along it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On an indifference map, curves farther from the origin carry higher satisfaction.
**Answer: True**
