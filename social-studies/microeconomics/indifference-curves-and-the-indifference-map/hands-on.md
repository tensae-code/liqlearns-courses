# Hands-on — Indifference curves and the indifference map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
On squared paper or a floor grid drawn with string, place stones at the four combinations from the lesson's table, then lay a string through them to form the curve.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the orange-banana schedule beside its plotted curve, then three nested curves as a map — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
