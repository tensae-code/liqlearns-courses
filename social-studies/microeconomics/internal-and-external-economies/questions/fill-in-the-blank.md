# Internal and external economies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The advantages that come with larger production are the _____ of scale.
**Answer: economies**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Economies arising inside the firm as it grows are _____ economies.
**Answer: internal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economies from the growth of the industry around a firm are _____ economies.
**Answer: external**
