# Internal and external economies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are economies of scale?
**Answer:** Economies are the advantages that come with a larger scale of production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three internal economies from the book's list.
**Answer:** Any three of: financial resources, the entrepreneur's ability, transport facilities, repair facilities, advertisement, credit facility, government subsidies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are external economies, and where can scale lead?
**Answer:** External economies come from the industry's growth around the firm; a firm that keeps gaining economies can grow toward monopoly.
