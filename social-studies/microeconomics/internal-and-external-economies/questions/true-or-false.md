# Internal and external economies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bigger scale brings no advantages at all.
**Answer: False** — the chapter is built on the advantages size brings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Internal economies come from outside the firm.
**Answer: False** — internal means inside the firm itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** External economies arise inside one firm only.
**Answer: False** — external means from outside — the industry around the firm.
