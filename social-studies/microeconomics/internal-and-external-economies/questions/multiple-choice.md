# Internal and external economies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About scale of production — the lesson teaches that —
a) economies are the advantages that come with a larger scale of production  b) internal economies arise inside the growing firm — finance, ability, transport, credit and more  c) external economies come from the industry's growth around the firm; unchecked growth can end in monopoly
**Answer: a) economies are the advantages that come with a larger scale of production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About internal economies — the lesson teaches that —
a) economies are the advantages that come with a larger scale of production  b) internal economies arise inside the growing firm — finance, ability, transport, credit and more  c) external economies come from the industry's growth around the firm; unchecked growth can end in monopoly
**Answer: b) internal economies arise inside the growing firm — finance, ability, transport, credit and more**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About external economies and the road to monopoly — the lesson teaches that —
a) economies are the advantages that come with a larger scale of production  b) internal economies arise inside the growing firm — finance, ability, transport, credit and more  c) external economies come from the industry's growth around the firm; unchecked growth can end in monopoly
**Answer: c) external economies come from the industry's growth around the firm; unchecked growth can end in monopoly**
