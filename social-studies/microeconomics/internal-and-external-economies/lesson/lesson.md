# Internal and external economies

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Scale of production

The Class 11 book's scale chapter starts with the **scale of production** — how large a firm's production is — because size itself brings advantages. Those advantages are the **economies** of scale.

## Sub lesson 2 — Internal economies

**Internal economies** arise inside the firm as it grows. The book lists them: stronger **financial resources**, the **entrepreneur's ability**, **transport** and **repair facilities**, **advertisement**, **credit facility**, **government subsidies** and repair centres.

## Sub lesson 3 — External economies and the road to monopoly

**External economies** arise outside the single firm — from the growth of the industry and its area around it. The book follows scale to its end point: a firm that keeps gaining economies can grow toward **monopoly**, where one seller stands alone.
