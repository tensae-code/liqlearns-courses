# Video script — Internal and external economies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Internal and external economies — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: economies are the advantages that come with a larger scale of production; internal economies arise inside the growing firm — finance, ability, transport, credit and more; external economies come from the industry's growth around the firm; unchecked growth can end in monopoly.
