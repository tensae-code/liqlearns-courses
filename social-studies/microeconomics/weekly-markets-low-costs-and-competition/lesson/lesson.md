# Weekly markets — low costs and competition

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — No rent, electricity or hired wages

Weekly-market traders pay **no rent, no electricity bills and no hired wages** — their costs are low, so their prices can be low.

## Sub lesson 2 — Stock stored at home

Traders **store their stock at home** — no warehouse rent either; the home is the godown.

## Sub lesson 3 — Many rival sellers — buyers bargain prices down

With **many rival sellers** side by side, **buyers can bargain prices down** — competition, with low costs, is why weekly-market goods are cheaper.
