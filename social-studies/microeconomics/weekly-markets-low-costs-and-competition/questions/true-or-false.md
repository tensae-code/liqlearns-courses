# Weekly markets — low costs and competition — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weekly-market traders pay full shop rent.
**Answer: False — they pay no rent, electricity or hired wages.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Traders rent warehouses for their stock.
**Answer: False — they store stock at home.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A single seller in the weekly market sets any price he likes.
**Answer: False — many rivals let buyers bargain prices down.**
