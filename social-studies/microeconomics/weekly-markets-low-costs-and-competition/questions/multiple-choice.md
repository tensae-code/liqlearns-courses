# Weekly markets — low costs and competition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weekly-market traders pay no —
a) rent, electricity or hired wages  b) money for stock  c) transport at all
**Answer: a) rent, electricity or hired wages**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Stock is stored —
a) at home  b) in rented warehouses  c) at the buyer's house
**Answer: a) at home**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Many rival sellers let buyers —
a) bargain prices down  b) pay more  c) buy nothing
**Answer: a) bargain prices down**
