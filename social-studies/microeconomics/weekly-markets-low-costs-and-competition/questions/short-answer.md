# Weekly markets — low costs and competition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two costs weekly-market traders do not pay.
**Answer:** Any two of: rent, electricity, hired wages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do weekly-market traders store stock?
**Answer:** At home.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why can buyers bargain prices down?
**Answer:** Many rival sellers compete side by side.
