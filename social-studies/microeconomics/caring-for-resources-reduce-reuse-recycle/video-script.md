# Video script — Caring for resources — reduce, reuse, recycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Even renewable resources can be depleted if overused — a forest cut faster than it regrows is the standing warning. Pollution is putting harmful substances into nature: oil in water, toxic air, dumped garbage.

Conservation means not misusing, spoiling or wasting resources. It is practised in three named moves — reduce, reuse, recycle — which the next parts take one by one.

Reduce: use less — lights off, walk or cycle. Reuse: use again — jugs, jars, paper and bags a second time. Recycle: remake — glass, paper, cardboard, aluminium and steel into new things, with mixed-material plastics noted as hard to recycle.
