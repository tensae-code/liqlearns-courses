# Hands-on — Caring for resources — reduce, reuse, recycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Take one real discarded item. Demonstrate its reuse, describe its recycling path, and name the reduced purchase that would have prevented it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three labelled bins and actions — a light switched off, a jar reused, paper going to recycling — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
