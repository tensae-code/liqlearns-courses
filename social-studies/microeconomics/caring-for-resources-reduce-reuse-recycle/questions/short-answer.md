# Caring for resources — reduce, reuse, recycle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Pollution and depletion" in your own words.
**Answer:** Pollution is putting harmful substances into nature, and even renewable resources can be depleted if overused.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Conservation means care" in your own words.
**Answer:** Conservation means not misusing, spoiling or wasting, practised as reduce, reuse and recycle.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reduce, reuse, recycle" in your own words.
**Answer:** Reduce means use less, reuse means use again, and recycle means remaking materials into new things.
