# Caring for resources — reduce, reuse, recycle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Putting harmful substances into nature is _____.
**Answer: pollution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Conservation means not misusing, spoiling or _____.
**Answer: wasting**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Reduce means use _____ — lights off, walk or cycle.
**Answer: less**
