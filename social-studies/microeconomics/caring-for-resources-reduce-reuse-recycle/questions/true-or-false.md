# Caring for resources — reduce, reuse, recycle — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pollution is putting harmful substances into nature, and even renewable resources can be depleted if overused.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pollution is putting harmful substances into nature, and even renewable resources can be depleted if overused.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Pollution and depletion); sub lesson 2 says: Conservation means not misusing, spoiling or wasting, practised as reduce, reuse and recycle.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Reduce means use less, reuse means use again, and recycle means remaking materials into new things.
**Answer: True**
