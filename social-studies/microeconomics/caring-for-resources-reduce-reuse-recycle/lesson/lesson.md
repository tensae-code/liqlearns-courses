# Caring for resources — reduce, reuse, recycle

## Do it first

Your home throws things away every day. Pick one thrown thing and find three fates for it better than the dump: use less of it, use it again, or remake it. Say all three.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pollution and depletion

Even renewable resources can be depleted if overused — a forest cut faster than it regrows is the standing warning. Pollution is putting harmful substances into nature: oil in water, toxic air, dumped garbage.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conservation means care

Conservation means not misusing, spoiling or wasting resources. It is practised in three named moves — reduce, reuse, recycle — which the next parts take one by one.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reduce, reuse, recycle

Reduce: use less — lights off, walk or cycle. Reuse: use again — jugs, jars, paper and bags a second time. Recycle: remake — glass, paper, cardboard, aluminium and steel into new things, with mixed-material plastics noted as hard to recycle.

## Say it to your companion

Explain caring for resources — reduce, reuse, recycle to your companion in your own words: what it is, the one fact you would never want to forget from it, and one example from your own surroundings or from Ethiopia. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
