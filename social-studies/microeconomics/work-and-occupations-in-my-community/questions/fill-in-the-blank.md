# Work and occupations in my community — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ professions and unpaid domestic work all have value for the community;
**Answer: Occupations**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A farmer's produce goes to community _____ or to trade
**Answer: consumption**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** And _____ would have to be paid for if nobody did it at home
**Answer: housework**
