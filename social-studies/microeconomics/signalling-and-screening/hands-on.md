# Hands-on — Signalling and screening

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Signal audit
List five claims people make in markets near you. For each, name a signal that would separate truth from bluff and say why a liar could not copy it cheaply.

## Be the bank
Write the screening checklist you would run on a loan applicant: five checks, what each check reveals, and which hidden type each check catches.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
