# Signalling and screening — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Screening is the uninformed side investigating before the deal, as a bank examines a borrower's books.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Signalling is the informed side sending a proof that is costly to fake, like a certificate.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A signal works only when it separates the genuine from the cheap claim.
**Answer: True**
