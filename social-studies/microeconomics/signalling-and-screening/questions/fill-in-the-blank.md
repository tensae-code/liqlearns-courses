# Signalling and screening — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bank examining a borrower's books before lending is ____.
**Answer: screening**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A certificate shown by a qualified worker is a ____.
**Answer: signal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A signal that the unqualified can copy just as cheaply does not ____ the two.
**Answer: separate**
