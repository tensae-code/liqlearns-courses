# Signalling and screening — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Screening — the uninformed side investigates"?
a) Screening is the uninformed side investigating before the deal, as a bank examines a borrower's books  b) Signalling is the informed side sending a proof that is costly to fake, like a certificate  c) A signal works only when it separates the genuine from the cheap claim
**Answer: a) Screening is the uninformed side investigating before the deal, as a bank examines a borrower's books**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Signalling — the informed side shows proof"?
a) Signalling is the informed side sending a proof that is costly to fake, like a certificate  b) A signal works only when it separates the genuine from the cheap claim  c) Screening is the uninformed side investigating before the deal, as a bank examines a borrower's books
**Answer: a) Signalling is the informed side sending a proof that is costly to fake, like a certificate**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Hana and Hikma — a signal that separates"?
a) A signal works only when it separates the genuine from the cheap claim  b) Screening is the uninformed side investigating before the deal, as a bank examines a borrower's books  c) Signalling is the informed side sending a proof that is costly to fake, like a certificate
**Answer: a) A signal works only when it separates the genuine from the cheap claim**
