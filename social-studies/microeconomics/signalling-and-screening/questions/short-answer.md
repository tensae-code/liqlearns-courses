# Signalling and screening — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Screening — the uninformed side investigates" in your own words.
**Answer:** Screening is the uninformed side investigating before the deal, as a bank examines a borrower's books.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Signalling — the informed side shows proof" in your own words.
**Answer:** Signalling is the informed side sending a proof that is costly to fake, like a certificate.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Hana and Hikma — a signal that separates" in your own words.
**Answer:** A signal works only when it separates the genuine from the cheap claim.
