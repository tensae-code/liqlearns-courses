# Video script — Impulse buying and needs vs wants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A need is a thing we must have, like bread for the family. A want is a thing we would like to have, like sweets. Money is limited, so needs come first.

Impulse buying is buying a want at once, without thinking or planning, just because you saw it. The money meant for bread can end up spent on sweets — and the bread is still needed.

To manage impulse buying: decide what the money is for first, buy the needs, and let wants wait for what is left.
