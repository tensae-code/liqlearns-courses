# Impulse buying and needs vs wants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a need?
a) sweets bought on the spot  b) bread the family will eat  c) a toy seen in the window
**Answer: b) bread the family will eat**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Impulse buying is —
a) planning a shopping list first  b) saving money before spending  c) buying something at once without thinking or planning
**Answer: c) buying something at once without thinking or planning**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the "Mwansa and the K20" scenario, the way to manage impulse buying is —
a) needs first, then wants only with what is left  b) wants first, needs if anything remains  c) spend all the money at the first stall
**Answer: a) needs first, then wants only with what is left**
