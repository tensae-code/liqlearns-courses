# Impulse buying and needs vs wants — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A want is a thing we must have to live.
**Answer: False** — a want is a thing we would like to have but can do without; a thing we must have is a need.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the K10 story, money meant for bread is spent on sweets the moment the sweets are seen.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A want can wait, but a need must be provided for first.
**Answer: True**
