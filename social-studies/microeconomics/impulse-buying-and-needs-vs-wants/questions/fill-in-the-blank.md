# Impulse buying and needs vs wants — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bread the family will eat is a _____, and sweets are a want.
**Answer: need**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Buying something at once without thinking or planning is called _____ buying.
**Answer: impulse**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The class practises with play money in a classroom _____.
**Answer: market**
