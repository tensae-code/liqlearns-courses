# Impulse buying and needs vs wants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between a need and a want?
**Answer:** A need is a thing we must have, like bread for the family; a want is a thing we would like to have but can do without, like sweets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is impulse buying a problem?
**Answer:** Because the money is spent on the spot on a want, and the need the money was meant for is still there, unmet.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the module teach learners to manage impulse buying?
**Answer:** By putting needs first — deciding what the money is really for before spending, as in the "Mwansa and the K20" scenario — and practising in a classroom market with play money.
