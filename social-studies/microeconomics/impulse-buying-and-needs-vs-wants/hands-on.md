# Hands-on — Impulse buying and needs vs wants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Play the classroom market at home: use play money — paper kwacha you make yourself, a K10 and a K20. Set up stalls with real or drawn things, some needs and some wants. Buy only your needs first, count what is left, and only then choose one want if the play money covers it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the K10 bread-and-sweets story from memory in three pictures — the money, the sweets seen, the choice made — and label each one. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
