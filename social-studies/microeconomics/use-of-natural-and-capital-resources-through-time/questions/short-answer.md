# Use of natural and capital resources through time — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "How natural and capital resources have been used"?
**Answer:** How natural and capital resources have been used; how that use changed through time.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "The theories of natural and capital resources; including"?
**Answer:** The theories of natural and capital resources; including how human labour was used with them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "How natural and capital resources have been used — more"?
**Answer:** How natural and capital resources have been used, how that use changed through time, and the theories of natural and capital resources, including how human labour was used with them.
