# Hands-on — Characteristics of economic needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with your own needs
List five of your own needs. Beside each, write which characteristic it shows best — satiable, substitutable, complementary, or growing — and say the whole list aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — one family's needs drawn as branches — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
