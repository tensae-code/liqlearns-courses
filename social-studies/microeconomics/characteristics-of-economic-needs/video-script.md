# Video script — Characteristics of economic needs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Needs have a character of their own. They are multiple — no person has just one. They are relative — what counts as a need differs from person to person and place to place. And they are satiable: a single need, once met, quiets down — enough food ends hunger, for now.

Needs also substitute for each other — one good can take another's place — and complement each other, arriving in pairs that are used together. And needs grow: with civilisation, with income, with time, place, culture and age, the list of what a person needs gets longer.
