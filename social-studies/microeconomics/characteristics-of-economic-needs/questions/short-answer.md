# Characteristics of economic needs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does it mean that needs are relative?
**Answer:** What counts as a need differs by person, society and circumstance — a need is judged against a life, not in the abstract.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the difference between substitutable and complementary needs.
**Answer:** Substitutable needs can be met by goods that replace each other; complementary needs come linked and are met by goods used together.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four things needs grow with.
**Answer:** Any four of: civilisation, income, time, place, culture, age.
