# Characteristics of economic needs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A need that quiets once it is met is called —
a) multiple  b) satiable  c) complementary
**Answer: b) satiable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When one good can take another's place in meeting a need, the need is —
a) substitutable  b) relative  c) multiple
**Answer: a) substitutable**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Needs grow with all of these EXCEPT —
a) income  b) age  c) the price of oranges alone
**Answer: c) the price of oranges alone**
