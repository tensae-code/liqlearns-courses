# Characteristics of economic needs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A person has only one need at a time.
**Answer: False — needs are multiple; they arrive together, and meeting one does not end the others.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Needs are relative — what counts as a need differs by person and place.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Needs stay the same across a whole life.
**Answer: False — needs change and grow with time, place, culture and age, and with income and civilisation.**
