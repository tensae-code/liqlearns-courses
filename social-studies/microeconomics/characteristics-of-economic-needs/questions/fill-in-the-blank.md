# Characteristics of economic needs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Needs are multiple, relative, satiable, substitutable and _____.
**Answer: complementary**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A single need, once met, quiets down — needs are _____.
**Answer: satiable**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Needs grow with civilisation, income, time, place, _____ and age.
**Answer: culture**
