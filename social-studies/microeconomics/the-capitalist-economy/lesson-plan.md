# Lesson plan — The capitalist economy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** The capitalist economy
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — The Basic Economic Problems and Economic Systems of the Grade 9 Economics course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A capitalist economy: private ownership, production for profit, consumer sovereignty and competition, with government limited largely to defence and law and order.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Microeconomics, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this course.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Private ownership, profit motive**
2. **Its working features**
3. **Limited government — and the cost**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
