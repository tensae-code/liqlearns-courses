# The capitalist economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a capitalist economy the means of production are privately owned and production is for profit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a capitalist economy the means of production are privately owned and production is for profit.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Private ownership, profit motive); sub lesson 2 says: Capitalism's features include private property, consumer sovereignty, competition and self-interest as the motive force.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a capitalist economy government is limited largely to defence and law and order, and income inequality is large.
**Answer: True**
