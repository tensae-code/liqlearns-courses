# The capitalist economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Private ownership, profit motive" in your own words.
**Answer:** In a capitalist economy the means of production are privately owned and production is for profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Its working features" in your own words.
**Answer:** Capitalism's features include private property, consumer sovereignty, competition and self-interest as the motive force.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Limited government — and the cost" in your own words.
**Answer:** In a capitalist economy government is limited largely to defence and law and order, and income inequality is large.
