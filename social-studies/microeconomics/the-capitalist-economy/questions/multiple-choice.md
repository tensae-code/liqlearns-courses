# The capitalist economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Private ownership, profit motive"?
a) private ownership and production for profit; also called free-market or laissez-faire  b) private property, consumer sovereignty, competition, self-interest  c) government confined largely to defence and law and order; large income inequality
**Answer: a) private ownership and production for profit; also called free-market or laissez-faire**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Its working features"?
a) private ownership and production for profit; also called free-market or laissez-faire  b) government confined largely to defence and law and order; large income inequality  c) private property, consumer sovereignty, competition, self-interest
**Answer: c) private property, consumer sovereignty, competition, self-interest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Limited government — and the cost"?
a) private property, consumer sovereignty, competition, self-interest  b) government confined largely to defence and law and order; large income inequality  c) private ownership and production for profit; also called free-market or laissez-faire
**Answer: b) government confined largely to defence and law and order; large income inequality**
