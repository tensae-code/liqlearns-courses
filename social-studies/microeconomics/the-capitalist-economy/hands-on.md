# Hands-on — The capitalist economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Run a mini-market: three sellers set prices for the same fruit while buyers choose freely. After five minutes, the group names which features of capitalism appeared.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — privately owned shops and factories competing on one street, a small government building at the far end — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
