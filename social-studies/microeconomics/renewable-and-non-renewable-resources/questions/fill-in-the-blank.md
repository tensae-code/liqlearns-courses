# Renewable and non-renewable resources — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Renewable resources can be _____ over time by natural processes.
**Answer: replaced**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Non-renewable resources are a _____ found in the ground.
**Answer: fixed stock**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Recycling can _____ non-renewable resources but cannot refill them.
**Answer: stretch**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** How much disturbance an ecosystem absorbs before its parameters change is its ____.
**Answer: resilience**
