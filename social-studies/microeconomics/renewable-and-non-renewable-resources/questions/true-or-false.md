# Renewable and non-renewable resources — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Renewable resources can be replaced over time by natural processes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Renewable resources can be replaced over time by natural processes.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Renewable — replaced by nature); sub lesson 2 says: Non-renewable resources are a fixed stock found in the ground, like natural gas, coal, oil and metals.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Recycling can stretch non-renewable resources but the overall supply stays roughly constant.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Renewables are potentially exhaustible if harvested past regeneration, and an ecosystem's resilience is the disturbance it absorbs before changing.
**Answer: True**
