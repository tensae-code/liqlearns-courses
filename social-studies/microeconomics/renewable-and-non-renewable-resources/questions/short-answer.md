# Renewable and non-renewable resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Renewable — replaced by nature" in your own words.
**Answer:** Renewable resources can be replaced over time by natural processes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Non-renewable — a fixed stock" in your own words.
**Answer:** Non-renewable resources are a fixed stock found in the ground, like natural gas, coal, oil and metals.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Recycling stretches, never refills" in your own words.
**Answer:** Recycling can stretch non-renewable resources but the overall supply stays roughly constant.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Renewable, yet exhaustible — ecosystems and resilience" in your own words.
**Answer:** Renewables are potentially exhaustible if harvested past regeneration, and an ecosystem's resilience is the disturbance it absorbs before changing.
