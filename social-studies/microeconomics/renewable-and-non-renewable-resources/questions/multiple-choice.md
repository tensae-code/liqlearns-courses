# Renewable and non-renewable resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Renewable — replaced by nature"?
a) replaced by natural processes — sunshine fast, soil over hundreds of years  b) a fixed stock in the ground — gas, coal, oil, metals  c) recycling stretches a fixed stock but cannot refill it
**Answer: a) replaced by natural processes — sunshine fast, soil over hundreds of years**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Non-renewable — a fixed stock"?
a) replaced by natural processes — sunshine fast, soil over hundreds of years  b) recycling stretches a fixed stock but cannot refill it  c) a fixed stock in the ground — gas, coal, oil, metals
**Answer: c) a fixed stock in the ground — gas, coal, oil, metals**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Recycling stretches, never refills"?
a) a fixed stock in the ground — gas, coal, oil, metals  b) recycling stretches a fixed stock but cannot refill it  c) replaced by natural processes — sunshine fast, soil over hundreds of years
**Answer: b) recycling stretches a fixed stock but cannot refill it**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Renewable, yet exhaustible — ecosystems and resilience"?
a) A renewable resource can never be exhausted, however fast it is harvested  b) Renewables are potentially exhaustible if harvested past regeneration, and an ecosystem's resilience is the disturbance it absorbs before changing  c) An ecosystem is only the animals in a place, never the physical system
**Answer: b) Renewables are potentially exhaustible if harvested past regeneration, and an ecosystem's resilience is the disturbance it absorbs before changing**
