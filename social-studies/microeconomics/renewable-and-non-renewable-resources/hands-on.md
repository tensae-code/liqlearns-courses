# Hands-on — Renewable and non-renewable resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two boxes: 'comes back' and 'fixed stock'. Deal resource cards into them; then move one card — soil — and debate why it sits on the slow edge of renewable.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a tree regrowing beside a coal seam being emptied, the seam not refilling — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## The Grade 12 layer — harvest within regeneration
Take one renewable resource (a woodlot, a fish stock, a pasture). Write its regeneration rate in plain words, a harvest rate that respects it, and the first sign that harvesting has passed it — the moment a renewable becomes exhaustible.
