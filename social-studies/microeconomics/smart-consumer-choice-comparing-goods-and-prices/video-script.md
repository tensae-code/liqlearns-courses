# Video script — Smart consumer choice — comparing goods and prices

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A smart consumer reads first and buys second. The label tells the good's name, producer, trademark, quantity, contents, dates, use and quality — and the barcode is its computer code. Shop reasonably: only the most necessary and the best you can afford, from an exact list. And before you choose, compare price, quality, delivery, trial, return, guarantee and service — advantages plus, drawbacks minus.
