# Hands-on — Smart consumer choice — comparing goods and prices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a real label
Take a packaged good at home. Write down everything its label tells you — name, producer, quantity, contents, dates, use and storage. Tick each item the Grade 3 lesson names, and find the barcode.

## Make the shopping list first
Before a real or pretend shop visit, write the exact list of goods you seek, as the checklist asks. Afterwards, mark anything bought that was not on the list and say why it was bought.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 3 aloud — the six things researched before choosing — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
