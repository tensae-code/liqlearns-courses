# Smart consumer choice — comparing goods and prices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the label of a good carry?
a) only its price  b) its name, producer, trademark, quantity, contents, production and use-by dates, use, storage, quality and purpose  c) the seller's name only
**Answer: b) its name, producer, trademark, quantity, contents, production and use-by dates, use, storage, quality and purpose**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To shop reasonably means —
a) to buy everything that attracts attention  b) to buy only the most necessary and the best of what you can afford  c) to always buy the cheapest
**Answer: b) to buy only the most necessary and the best of what you can afford**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Before choosing a good, the pupil researches —
a) its colour only  b) its price, quality, delivery term, trial and return possibilities, guarantee and service  c) what friends bought
**Answer: b) its price, quality, delivery term, trial and return possibilities, guarantee and service**
