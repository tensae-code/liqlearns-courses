# Smart consumer choice — comparing goods and prices — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The _____ of a good carries its name, producer, trademark, quantity and contents.
**Answer: label**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To shop _____ means to buy only the most necessary and the best of what you can afford.
**Answer: reasonably**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A chosen good is researched for price, quality, delivery term, trial and return possibilities, _____ and service.
**Answer: guarantee**
