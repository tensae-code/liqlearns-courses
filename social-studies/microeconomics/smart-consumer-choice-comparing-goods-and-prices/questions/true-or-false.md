# Smart consumer choice — comparing goods and prices — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The barcode is the computer code for the price and other information about the good.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Advertising must always be believed when you shop.
**Answer: False — the reasonable-shopping checklist asks whether advertising must always be believed; a smart consumer checks instead.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the comparison research, advantages are marked "+" and drawbacks "−".
**Answer: True**
