# Technology, displacement and new forms of work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about tecnología, empleo y nuevas formas de trabajo?
**Answer:** Technology produces more in less time but displaces unskilled workers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about tecnología, empleo y nuevas formas de trabajo — more?
**Answer:** Raising poverty and unemployment in less developed countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about tecnología, empleo y nuevas formas de trabajo — more — more?
**Answer:** While globalisation also creates online and freelance work across time zones.
