# Technology, displacement and new forms of work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About tecnología, empleo y nuevas formas de trabajo — the lesson teaches that —
a) Technology produces more in less time but displaces unskilled workers.
b) Raising poverty and unemployment in less developed countries.
c) While globalisation also creates online and freelance work across time zones.
**Answer: a) Technology produces more in less time but displaces unskilled workers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About tecnología, empleo y nuevas formas de trabajo — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) While globalisation also creates online and freelance work across time zones.
b) Raising poverty and unemployment in less developed countries.
c) Technology produces more in less time but displaces unskilled workers.
**Answer: b) Raising poverty and unemployment in less developed countries.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About tecnología, empleo y nuevas formas de trabajo — more — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) Technology produces more in less time but displaces unskilled workers.
b) Raising poverty and unemployment in less developed countries.
c) While globalisation also creates online and freelance work across time zones.
**Answer: c) While globalisation also creates online and freelance work across time zones.**
