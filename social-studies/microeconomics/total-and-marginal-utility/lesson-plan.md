# Lesson plan — Total and marginal utility

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Total and marginal utility
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Theory of Consumer Behaviour of the Grade 10 Economics course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Total utility rises as you consume more, but each extra unit adds less — marginal utility falls, reaches zero at saturation, then turns negative.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Microeconomics, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this course. Grade 10 stacks on the Grade 9 Economics foundations in the same titles.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
