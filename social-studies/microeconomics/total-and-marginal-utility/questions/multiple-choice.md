# Total and marginal utility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Total utility and marginal utility"?
a) TU the whole satisfaction; MU the extra from one more unit  b) MU zero at saturation (37 utils at 7 units), negative beyond it  c) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before
**Answer: a) TU the whole satisfaction; MU the extra from one more unit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Saturation — where more stops helping"?
a) TU the whole satisfaction; MU the extra from one more unit  b) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before  c) MU zero at saturation (37 utils at 7 units), negative beyond it
**Answer: c) MU zero at saturation (37 utils at 7 units), negative beyond it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The law of diminishing marginal utility"?
a) MU zero at saturation (37 utils at 7 units), negative beyond it  b) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before  c) TU the whole satisfaction; MU the extra from one more unit
**Answer: b) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before**
