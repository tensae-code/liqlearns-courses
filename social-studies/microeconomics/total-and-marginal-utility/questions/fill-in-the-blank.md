# Total and marginal utility — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The extra satisfaction from one more unit is called _____ — MU for short.
**Answer: marginal utility**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the saturation point, marginal utility falls to _____ — and beyond it, it turns negative.
**Answer: zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The law of _____ marginal utility says each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before.
**Answer: diminishing**
