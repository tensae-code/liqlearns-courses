# Total and marginal utility — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Total utility and marginal utility" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal utility equals the change in total utility divided by the change in quantity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Saturation — where more stops helping" in your own words.
**Answer:** At the saturation point marginal utility is zero, and beyond it marginal utility turns negative.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The law of diminishing marginal utility" in your own words.
**Answer:** The law of diminishing marginal utility says each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before, other things equal.
