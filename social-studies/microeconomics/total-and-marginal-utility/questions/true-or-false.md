# Total and marginal utility — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marginal utility equals the change in total utility divided by the change in quantity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the saturation point marginal utility is zero, and beyond it marginal utility turns negative.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The law of diminishing marginal utility says each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before, other things equal.
**Answer: True**
