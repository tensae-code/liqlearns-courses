# Hands-on — Total and marginal utility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Use stones for utils. Act out the banana table with a partner: add a smaller pile of stones for each extra banana, no stones at saturation, and take stones away beyond it. Say the rule aloud at each step.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a total utility curve rising, flattening and falling, with the marginal utility curve crossing zero at the peak — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
