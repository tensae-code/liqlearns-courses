# Consumer rights and COPRA — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods in the market are always exactly as promised.
**Answer: False — consumers face adulteration and defective goods.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Consumers have no legal rights in the marketplace.
**Answer: False — COPRA 1986 gives rights to safety, information, choice and redressal.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ISI marks are decorations with no meaning.
**Answer: False — ISI and Agmark certify that goods meet standards.**
