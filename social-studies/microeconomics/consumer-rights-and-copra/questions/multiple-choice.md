# Consumer rights and COPRA — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumers face —
a) adulteration, defective goods and unfair practices  b) only high prices  c) no risks
**Answer: a) adulteration, defective goods and unfair practices**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** COPRA is from —
a) 1986  b) 2013  c) 1947
**Answer: a) 1986**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ISI and Agmark are —
a) certification marks  b) shop names  c) taxes
**Answer: a) certification marks**
