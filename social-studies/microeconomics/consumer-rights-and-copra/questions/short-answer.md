# Consumer rights and COPRA — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two dangers consumers face.
**Answer:** Any two of: adulteration, defective goods, unfair practices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two consumer rights under COPRA.
**Answer:** Any two of: safety, information, choice, redressal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How are consumer rights enforced and signalled?
**Answer:** Through consumer courts, and by certification marks like ISI and Agmark.
