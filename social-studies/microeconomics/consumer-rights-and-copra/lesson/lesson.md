## Sub lesson 1 — The consumer's dangers — adulteration, defects, unfair practices

In the marketplace the consumer faces **adulteration, defective goods and unfair practices** — the dangers the rights exist against.

## Sub lesson 2 — COPRA 1986 and the rights

The **consumer movement** won the **Consumer Protection Act 1986 (COPRA)**, giving rights to **safety, information (MRP, expiry dates), choice and redressal**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Consumer courts and certification marks — ISI and Agmark

Rights are enforced through **consumer courts**, and **certification marks such as ISI and Agmark** tell the buyer a product meets its standard before she pays.
