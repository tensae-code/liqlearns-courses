Consumer rights and COPRA — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: the consumer in the marketplace faces adulteration, defective goods and unfair practices; the consumer movement, the Consumer Protection Act 1986 (COPRA), the rights to safety, information (MRP, expiry dates), choice and redressal through consumer courts, and certification marks such as ISI and Agmark.
