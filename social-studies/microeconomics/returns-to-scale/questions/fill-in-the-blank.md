# Returns to scale — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Returns to scale scale _____ by one factor and compare output's growth with it.
**Answer: all inputs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Double inputs but less than double output is _____ returns to scale.
**Answer: decreasing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Technological change shifts the whole production function _____ — more from the same inputs.
**Answer: upward**
