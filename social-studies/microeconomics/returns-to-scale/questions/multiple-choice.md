# Returns to scale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Scaling everything at once"?
a) output's multiple compared with the inputs' multiple m  b) increasing, constant and decreasing returns in three bakery panels  c) an upward shift of the whole function, capital-saving, labour-saving or neutral
**Answer: a) output's multiple compared with the inputs' multiple m**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The three cases in the bakery"?
a) output's multiple compared with the inputs' multiple m  b) an upward shift of the whole function, capital-saving, labour-saving or neutral  c) increasing, constant and decreasing returns in three bakery panels
**Answer: c) increasing, constant and decreasing returns in three bakery panels**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Technological change — the function itself moves"?
a) increasing, constant and decreasing returns in three bakery panels  b) an upward shift of the whole function, capital-saving, labour-saving or neutral  c) output's multiple compared with the inputs' multiple m
**Answer: b) an upward shift of the whole function, capital-saving, labour-saving or neutral**
