# Hands-on — Returns to scale

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three bakery panels in play: learners mime doubling all inputs and hold up bread cards — more than double, exactly double, less than double — while the class names the returns to scale in each panel.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a bakery doubling everything and getting more than, exactly, and less than double the bread, in three panels — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
