# Video script — The price mechanism prices as signals and incentives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Price as signal and incentive in the market system — how price drives production, how it shapes consumer behaviour, and the interaction of producers, consumers and price.

The factors a price must answer to — competitors, costs, the state of the market, the state of the economy, the bargaining power of customers, supply and demand, government regulations and the target market.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.
