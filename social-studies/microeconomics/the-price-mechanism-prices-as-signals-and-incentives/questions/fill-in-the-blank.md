# The price mechanism prices as signals and incentives — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price as signal and incentive in the market system — how price drives production, how it shapes consumer behaviour, and the _____ of producers, consumers and price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The factors a price must answer to — _____, costs, the state of the market, the state of the economy, the bargaining power of customers, supply and demand, government regulations and the target market.
