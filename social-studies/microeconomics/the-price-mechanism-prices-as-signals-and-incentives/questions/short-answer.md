# The price mechanism prices as signals and incentives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Price as signal and incentive in the market"?
**Answer:** Price as signal and incentive in the market system — how price drives production, how it shapes consumer behaviour, and the interaction of producers, consumers and price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "The factors a price must answer to —"?
**Answer:** The factors a price must answer to — competitors, costs, the state of the market, the state of the economy, the bargaining power of customers, supply and demand, government regulations and the target market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.
