Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 12 Business Studies TG): the Grade 12 Business Studies guide carries a hidden Economics course inside Strand 1 — its Content Standard 1.1 is expressly about "economic principles" and its content background is drawn from Economics textbooks (Bandara's Economics for Grade 12 and Mickleburg's Economics Principles and Policies in the South Pacific) — and it returns to Economics in pricing (elasticity, supply and demand), in consumer protection enforced by the Independent Consumer and Competition Commission (ICCC), and in how goods are priced and paid for on the internet.
