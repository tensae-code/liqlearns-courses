# Reading and drawing economic graphs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economists gather data from the media, among other sources.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a direct relationship, two things rise together.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A graph shows everything that matters, with nothing left out.
**Answer: False — graphs have limitations; a graph shows only the variables it was drawn with.**
