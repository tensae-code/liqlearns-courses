# Reading and drawing economic graphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set names graph kinds from the lesson?
a) line, time-series, pie and bar  b) map, globe and atlas  c) essay, poem and story
**Answer: a) line, time-series, pie and bar**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When one variable rises as the other falls, the relationship is —
a) direct  b) inverse  c) flat
**Answer: b) inverse**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson's warning about graphs is that they —
a) are always wrong  b) have limitations  c) need no data
**Answer: b) have limitations**
