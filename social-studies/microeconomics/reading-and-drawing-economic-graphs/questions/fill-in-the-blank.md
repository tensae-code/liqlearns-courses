# Reading and drawing economic graphs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A graph drawn over time is a _____-series graph.
**Answer: time**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The variable that changes first is the _____ variable.
**Answer: independent**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The steepness and direction of a line on a graph is its _____.
**Answer: slope**
