# Reading and drawing economic graphs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four graph kinds in the lesson.
**Answer:** Line graphs, time-series graphs, pie graphs and bar graphs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between a direct and an inverse relationship?
**Answer:** In a direct relationship two things rise together; in an inverse relationship one rises as the other falls.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must an economist remember the limitations of graphs?
**Answer:** Because a graph shows only the variables it was drawn with, in the shape its maker chose — it can leave out what matters, so conclusions drawn from it must be checked.
