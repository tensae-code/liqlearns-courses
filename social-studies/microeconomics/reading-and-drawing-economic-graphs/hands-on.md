# Hands-on — Reading and drawing economic graphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw it yourself
Take four numbers from a newspaper or from your own week (prices, temperatures, scores). Draw the same numbers two ways — as a bar graph and as a line graph — label the dependent and independent variables, and say whether the relationship slopes direct or inverse.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — four small graphs side by side — a line graph, a time-series graph, a pie chart and a bar graph — drawn from numbers in a newspaper — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
