# Lesson plan — Reading and drawing economic graphs

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Reading and drawing economic graphs
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Fiji, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Economists gather data (from the media, among other places) and draw it — line, time-series, pie and bar graphs — reading slopes and direct and inverse relationships between dependent and independent variables, while remembering the limitations of graphs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Fiji Year 11 book's own graphs lesson — the graph kinds, how to read slopes and relationships, and what graphs cannot show.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
