# Hands-on — From one seller to the market supply

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Five learners act as sellers with quantity cards at Birr 3, 4 and 5. The class adds the cards sideways at each price to build the market schedule, then scales the result up to 120 sellers by multiplication.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — sellers' supply curves stacking sideways into one market supply curve — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
