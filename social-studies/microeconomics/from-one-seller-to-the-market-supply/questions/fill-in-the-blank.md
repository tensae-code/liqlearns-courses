# From one seller to the market supply — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market supply is built by _____ summation of sellers' quantities at each price.
**Answer: horizontal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At Birr 4, 120 sellers like the one in the lesson offer _____ tons of potatoes in total.
**Answer: 9,000**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Market supply at a price equals one seller's supply at that price _____ the number of sellers.
**Answer: times**
