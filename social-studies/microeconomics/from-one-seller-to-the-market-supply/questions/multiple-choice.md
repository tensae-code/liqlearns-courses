# From one seller to the market supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Adding sellers at each price"?
a) horizontal summation of sellers' quantities at each price  b) one seller offers 75 at Birr 4; 120 sellers offer 9,000 tons  c) the market figure must rebuild exactly from the one-seller figure
**Answer: a) horizontal summation of sellers' quantities at each price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Scaling one seller to the market"?
a) horizontal summation of sellers' quantities at each price  b) the market figure must rebuild exactly from the one-seller figure  c) one seller offers 75 at Birr 4; 120 sellers offer 9,000 tons
**Answer: c) one seller offers 75 at Birr 4; 120 sellers offer 9,000 tons**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why the scaling must check"?
a) one seller offers 75 at Birr 4; 120 sellers offer 9,000 tons  b) the market figure must rebuild exactly from the one-seller figure  c) horizontal summation of sellers' quantities at each price
**Answer: b) the market figure must rebuild exactly from the one-seller figure**
