# From one seller to the market supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Adding sellers at each price" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market supply is found by adding sellers' quantities at each price — horizontal summation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Scaling one seller to the market" in your own words.
**Answer:** At Birr 4, one seller with Qs = 20p − 5 offers 75, and 120 sellers offer 9,000 in total.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why the scaling must check" in your own words.
**Answer:** The market supply at a price must equal one seller's supply at that price times the number of sellers.
