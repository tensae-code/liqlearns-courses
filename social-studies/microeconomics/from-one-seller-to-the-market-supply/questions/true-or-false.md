# From one seller to the market supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market supply is found by adding sellers' quantities at each price — horizontal summation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At Birr 4, one seller with Qs = 20p − 5 offers 75, and 120 sellers offer 9,000 in total.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market supply at a price must equal one seller's supply at that price times the number of sellers.
**Answer: True**
