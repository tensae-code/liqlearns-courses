# The firm's supply curve is its marginal cost curve — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A competitive firm supplies nothing below the shutdown price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Above the shutdown price, the firm supplies where price equals marginal cost.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The firm's short-run supply curve is the part of its MC curve lying above AVC.
**Answer: True**
