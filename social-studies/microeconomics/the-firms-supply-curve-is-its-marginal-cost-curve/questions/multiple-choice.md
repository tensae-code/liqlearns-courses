# The firm's supply curve is its marginal cost curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Nothing below the shutdown price"?
a) supply is zero at every price below the shutdown point  b) above AVC, supplied quantity is read off P = MC along the rising MC curve  c) the firm's supply is MC above AVC; summed sideways it gives industry supply
**Answer: a) supply is zero at every price below the shutdown point**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Above it — price equals MC decides quantity"?
a) supply is zero at every price below the shutdown point  b) the firm's supply is MC above AVC; summed sideways it gives industry supply  c) above AVC, supplied quantity is read off P = MC along the rising MC curve
**Answer: c) above AVC, supplied quantity is read off P = MC along the rising MC curve**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The supply curve, and the industry's"?
a) above AVC, supplied quantity is read off P = MC along the rising MC curve  b) the firm's supply is MC above AVC; summed sideways it gives industry supply  c) supply is zero at every price below the shutdown point
**Answer: b) the firm's supply is MC above AVC; summed sideways it gives industry supply**
