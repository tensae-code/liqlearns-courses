# The firm's supply curve is its marginal cost curve — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Nothing below the shutdown price" in your own words.
**Answer:** A competitive firm supplies nothing below the shutdown price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Above it — price equals MC decides quantity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Above the shutdown price, the firm supplies where price equals marginal cost.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The supply curve, and the industry's" in your own words.
**Answer:** The firm's short-run supply curve is the part of its MC curve lying above AVC.
