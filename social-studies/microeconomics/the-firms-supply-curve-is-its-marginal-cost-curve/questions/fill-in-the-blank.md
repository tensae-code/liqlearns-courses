# The firm's supply curve is its marginal cost curve — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Below the shutdown price, the quantity a competitive firm supplies is _____.
**Answer: nothing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Above the shutdown price, the firm supplies the quantity where price equals _____.
**Answer: marginal cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The firm's supply curve is the part of its MC curve lying _____.
**Answer: above AVC**
