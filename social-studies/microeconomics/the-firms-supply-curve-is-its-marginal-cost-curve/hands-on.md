# Hands-on — The firm's supply curve is its marginal cost curve

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
A partner calls prices from low to high. Below the shutdown price, hold up an empty tray. Above it, add stones along a rising MC string — your offers are the supply curve being built live.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — an MC curve with only the segment above AVC drawn bold and relabelled supply — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
