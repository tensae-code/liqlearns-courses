# Free resources and scarce resources — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Resources are inputs used to produce goods and services.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Resources are inputs used to produce goods and services.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Resources are inputs); sub lesson 2 says: A free resource is one where more is available than people want at zero price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An economic resource is scarce — less is available than people want at zero price — and price is the test of which kind a resource is.
**Answer: True**
