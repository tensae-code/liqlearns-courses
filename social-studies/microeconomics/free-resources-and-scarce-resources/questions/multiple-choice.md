# Free resources and scarce resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Resources are inputs"?
a) inputs used to produce goods and services  b) more available than people want at zero price — air, sunshine, a stream  c) less available than wanted at zero price, carrying a non-zero price; price is the test
**Answer: a) inputs used to produce goods and services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Free resources"?
a) inputs used to produce goods and services  b) less available than wanted at zero price, carrying a non-zero price; price is the test  c) more available than people want at zero price — air, sunshine, a stream
**Answer: c) more available than people want at zero price — air, sunshine, a stream**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Economic resources — and the test"?
a) more available than people want at zero price — air, sunshine, a stream  b) less available than wanted at zero price, carrying a non-zero price; price is the test  c) inputs used to produce goods and services
**Answer: b) less available than wanted at zero price, carrying a non-zero price; price is the test**
