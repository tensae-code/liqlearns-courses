# Free resources and scarce resources — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Resources are _____ used to produce goods and services.
**Answer: inputs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A free resource has more available than people want at _____ price.
**Answer: zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ is the test of whether a resource is free or economic.
**Answer: price**
