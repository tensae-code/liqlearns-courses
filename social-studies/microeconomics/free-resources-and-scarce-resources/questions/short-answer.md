# Free resources and scarce resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Resources are inputs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Resources are inputs used to produce goods and services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Free resources" in your own words.
**Answer:** A free resource is one where more is available than people want at zero price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Economic resources — and the test" in your own words.
**Answer:** An economic resource is scarce — less is available than people want at zero price — and price is the test of which kind a resource is.
