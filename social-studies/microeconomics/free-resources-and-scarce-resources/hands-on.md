# Hands-on — Free resources and scarce resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Fill a cup from a stream (free-flowing) and then 'sell' bottled water from a jug with play coins. Explain why one carries a price and the other may not.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — sunshine and stream water on the free side; fertile land, clean water and skilled labour on the priced side — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
