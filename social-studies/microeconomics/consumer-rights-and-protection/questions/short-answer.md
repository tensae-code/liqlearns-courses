# Consumer rights and protection — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two rights the consumer holds under Belarus's law on consumer protection.
**Answer:** The right to quality goods, and the right to full information about what they are buying.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What can a consumer claim when goods turn out to be faulty?
**Answer:** Free repair or replacement of the faulty goods, and compensation where the fault has caused loss.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who defends the consumer's rights?
**Answer:** State bodies, the courts, and consumer societies.
