# Consumer rights and protection — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The consumer has the right to quality goods and full _____ about what they are buying.
**Answer: information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where a fault has caused loss, the consumer has the right to _____ — the loss made good.
**Answer: compensation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Consumer rights are defended by state bodies, _____ and consumer societies.
**Answer: courts**
