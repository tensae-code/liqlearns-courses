# Consumer rights and protection — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The consumer's rights under the law are legal, economic and social rights.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A consumer who buys faulty goods must pay for the repair themselves.
**Answer: False** — the consumer has the right to free repair or replacement of faulty goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Consumer societies are among the defenders of consumer rights.
**Answer: True**
