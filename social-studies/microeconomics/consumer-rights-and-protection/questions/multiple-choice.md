# Consumer rights and protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Belarus's law on consumer protection, the consumer has the right to —
a) pay any price the seller names  b) quality goods and full information  c) no receipt
**Answer: b) quality goods and full information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When goods are faulty, the consumer can claim —
a) free repair or replacement  b) nothing  c) a higher price
**Answer: a) free repair or replacement**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Consumer rights are defended by —
a) sellers alone  b) nobody  c) state bodies, courts and consumer societies
**Answer: c) state bodies, courts and consumer societies**
