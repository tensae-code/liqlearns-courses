# Hands-on — Consumer rights and protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Take one real thing at home that is faulty, or a card naming one. Stand at an imaginary shop counter and say your claim out loud: the fault, and the right you stand on — free repair, replacement, or compensation.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's three defenders from memory — state bodies, courts and consumer societies — standing behind a consumer at a counter, and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the defenders there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
