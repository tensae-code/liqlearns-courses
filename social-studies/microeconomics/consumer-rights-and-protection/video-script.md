# Video script — Consumer rights and protection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A consumer has rights under the law on consumer protection: the right to quality goods, and the right to full information about what they buy.

When goods are faulty, the consumer can claim free repair or replacement — and compensation where the fault caused loss.

And the consumer is not alone: state bodies, courts and consumer societies defend these rights.
