**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Belarus).**

Belarus's own way: economics arrives inside a philosophy-and-civics course as the ethics of work — profit defined on one diagram, then wages, the subsistence minimum and the consumer basket as measures of a decent life. This lesson keeps that way: the consumer's rights are met as law the person as consumer can stand on, inside the same §29 chapter. This lesson is the general consumer-rights lesson; the standing Ethiopia lesson (Consumer protection — Proclamation No. 813/2013) teaches Ethiopia's own proclamation and is a different thing. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
