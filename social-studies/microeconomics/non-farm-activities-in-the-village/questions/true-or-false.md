# Non-farm activities in the village — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Most of Palampur works outside farming.
**Answer: False — only a quarter does.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Shopkeepers grow all their stock in the village.
**Answer: False — they buy from city wholesale markets.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The village non-farm sector cannot grow beyond farming services.
**Answer: False — Kareem's computer centre shows a new kind of activity.**
