# Non-farm activities in the village

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — non-farm activities in the village — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Only a quarter works outside farming

Only **a quarter of Palampur works outside farming** — the **non-farm sector** is small, and the lesson asks what it needs to grow.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dairy, jaggery, shops and transport

The non-farm work includes **dairy sold through collection and chilling centres, Mishrilal's small jaggery manufacturing, shopkeepers buying from city wholesale markets, and transport**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Kareem's computer centre — a new village activity

**Kareem's computer centre** is the newest non-farm activity — a service the village did not have before, showing where the sector can grow.
