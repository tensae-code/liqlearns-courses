# Types of business activity

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 2, Types of Entrepreneurial Activity. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Production and commerce — making, and buying to resell

The book names five kinds of business activity. **Production** business makes goods. **Commerce** buys goods and resells them at a margin — the difference between the buying price and the selling price is the trader's gain.

## Sub lesson 2 — Financial business and intermediation — dealing in money, and bringing sides together

**Financial business** deals in money and securities. **Intermediation** brings buyers and sellers together — the book draws it as a scheme: producer – intermediary – consumer, where the intermediary links the one who made the good to the one who needs it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Insurance — and where each kind of firm should sit

**Insurance** is the fifth kind: it covers risks for a payment. The book closes with a town-map exercise: pupils decide where each kind of firm should be sited in a town — production, commerce, finance, intermediation and insurance each in its fitting place.
