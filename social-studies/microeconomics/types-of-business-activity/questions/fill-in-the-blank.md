# Types of business activity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ buys goods to resell at a margin, while _____ makes goods.
**Answer: commerce; production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book draws intermediation as producer – _____ – consumer.
**Answer: intermediary**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fifth kind of business activity is _____.
**Answer: insurance**
