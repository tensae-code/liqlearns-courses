# Types of business activity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production business —
a) makes goods  b) deals in money and securities  c) covers risks for a payment
**Answer: a) makes goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intermediation —
a) deals in money  b) brings buyers and sellers together  c) makes goods
**Answer: b) brings buyers and sellers together**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurance as a business activity —
a) makes goods  b) buys to resell  c) covers risks for a payment
**Answer: c) covers risks for a payment**
