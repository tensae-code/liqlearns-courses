# Types of business activity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Commerce buys goods and resells them at a margin.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Financial business deals in money and securities.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book lists four kinds of business activity.
**Answer: False** — It lists five: production, commerce, financial business, intermediation and insurance.
