# Types of business activity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first two kinds of business activity the book lists.
**Answer:** Production and commerce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the book draw intermediation?
**Answer:** As a scheme: producer – intermediary – consumer.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the town-map exercise ask pupils to decide?
**Answer:** Where each kind of firm should be sited in a town.
