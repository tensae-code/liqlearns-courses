# Video script — Division of labour — at home and at work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 7 book teaches the division of labour in two steps, says the narrator. First at home: the household shares its tasks, each person taking a part. Then at work: specialisation — each worker doing one kind of work well. And the book follows a Mongolian actor's career to show how profession and skill shape income. Work divided is work organised.
