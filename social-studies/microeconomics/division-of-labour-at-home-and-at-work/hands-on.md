# Hands-on — Division of labour — at home and at work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Who does what at home
List the tasks done in your home in a day. Beside each, write who does it. That list is your household's division of labour — then open lesson/lesson.md and compare with sub lesson 1.

## One job, many hands
Take one product you know — a meal, a shoe, a song. List the different kinds of work inside it, one per line, and name the skill each kind needs.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
