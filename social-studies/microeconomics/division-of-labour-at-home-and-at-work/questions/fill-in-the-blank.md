# Division of labour — at home and at work — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At home, the division of labour means sharing _____ among the household.
**Answer: tasks**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Doing one kind of work well is called _____.
**Answer: specialisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A person's profession and _____ shape the income they can earn.
**Answer: skill**
