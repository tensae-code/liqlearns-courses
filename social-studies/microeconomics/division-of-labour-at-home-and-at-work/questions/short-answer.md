# Division of labour — at home and at work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does Mongolia's book first teach the division of labour?
**Answer:** At home — the household shares its tasks, each member taking a part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is specialisation, as the book teaches it in production?
**Answer:** Each worker doing one kind of work well, instead of everyone doing a little of everything.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book show that profession and skill shape income?
**Answer:** Through a Mongolian actor's career — a profession built on a practised skill, whose earnings follow from the specialised work.
