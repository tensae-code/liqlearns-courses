# Division of labour — at home and at work — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The household division of labour is about sharing tasks among the family's members.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Specialisation means every worker does a little of every job.
**Answer: False — specialisation means each worker does one kind of work well.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's income case study follows a Mongolian actor's career.
**Answer: True**
