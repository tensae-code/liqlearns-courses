# Division of labour — at home and at work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book first teaches the division of labour through —
a) sharing tasks at home  b) foreign trade  c) bank accounts
**Answer: a) sharing tasks at home**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Specialisation means —
a) everyone doing everything  b) each worker doing one kind of work well  c) working without tools
**Answer: b) each worker doing one kind of work well**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's case study, income is shaped by —
a) luck alone  b) the weather  c) profession and skill
**Answer: c) profession and skill**
