# Elasticity and total expenditure — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a price cut raise total expenditure when demand is elastic?
**Answer:** Because quantity grows proportionally more than price falls, so the product P × Q grows.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a price rise raise total expenditure when demand is inelastic?
**Answer:** Because quantity falls proportionally less than price rises, so the product P × Q grows.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the full three-line rule.
**Answer:** Elastic demand — price and total expenditure move oppositely; inelastic — they move together; unit elasticity — total expenditure does not change.
