# Elasticity and total expenditure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under elastic demand, a price cut —
a) lowers total expenditure
b) raises total expenditure
c) leaves it unchanged
**Answer: b) raises total expenditure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under inelastic demand, a price rise —
a) raises total expenditure
b) lowers total expenditure
c) doubles quantity
**Answer: a) raises total expenditure**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At unit elasticity, a price change leaves total expenditure —
a) doubled
b) unchanged
c) at zero
**Answer: b) unchanged**
