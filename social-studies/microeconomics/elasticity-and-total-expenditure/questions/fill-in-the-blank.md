# Elasticity and total expenditure — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under elastic demand, price and total expenditure move in _____ directions.
**Answer: opposite**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under inelastic demand, price and total expenditure move in the _____ direction.
**Answer: same**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elastic: opposite. Inelastic: same direction. Unit: _____ change in total expenditure.
**Answer: no**
