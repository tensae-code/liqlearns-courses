# Elasticity and total expenditure — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under elastic demand the quantity response is proportionally bigger than the price change.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Necessities are the classic case where a price rise raises total expenditure.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A seller who knows the elasticity can predict a price change's effect on money taken.
**Answer: True**
