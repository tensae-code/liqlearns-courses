# Video script — The emergence of economics as a science

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economics became a science when industry transformed the world. The industrial revolution created factories, cities and markets on a new scale — and in 1776 Adam Smith published the Wealth of Nations, the book the science is born with.

A century and a half later, the crisis of 1929 broke the old confidence. In 1936 Keynes published his General Theory, and economics was reshaped: the whole economy — its spending, its jobs, its slumps — became the science's central problem.
