# Hands-on — The emergence of economics as a science

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with two dates
Write 1776 and 1936 on two cards. Under each, write the book and the event it answers to — the industrial revolution for Smith, the 1929 crisis for Keynes. Say both aloud from memory.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — two books on a timeline — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
