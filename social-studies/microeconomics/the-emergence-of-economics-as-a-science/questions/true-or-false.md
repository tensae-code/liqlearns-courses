# The emergence of economics as a science — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economics became a science in ancient times.
**Answer: False — economic ideas are ancient, but economics as a science was born with the industrial revolution and Smith's Wealth of Nations (1776).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Wealth of Nations is Adam Smith's book.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Keynes's General Theory reshaped economics after the 1929 crisis.
**Answer: True**
