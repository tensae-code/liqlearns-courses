# The emergence of economics as a science — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is 1776 taken as the birth of economics as a science?
**Answer:** Because Adam Smith's Wealth of Nations (1776) gathered the economy into one systematic work, giving economics its own questions and method.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What event made a reshaping of economics necessary?
**Answer:** The crisis of 1929 — mass unemployment and collapsed demand that the old explanations could not handle.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did economics turn to after Keynes's General Theory?
**Answer:** The whole economy at once — total spending, employment and slumps (macroeconomics).
