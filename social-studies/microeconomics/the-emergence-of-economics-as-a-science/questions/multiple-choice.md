# The emergence of economics as a science — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economics was born as a science with —
a) the first markets  b) the industrial revolution  c) the invention of coins
**Answer: b) the industrial revolution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Wealth of Nations was published in —
a) 1776  b) 1929  c) 1936
**Answer: a) 1776**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Keynes's General Theory (1936) was written after —
a) the industrial revolution  b) Smith's death  c) the crisis of 1929
**Answer: c) the crisis of 1929**
