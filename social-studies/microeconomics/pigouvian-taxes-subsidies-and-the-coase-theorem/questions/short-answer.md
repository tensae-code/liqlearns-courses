# Pigouvian taxes, subsidies and the Coase theorem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Pigou's tax" in your own words.
**Answer:** A Pigouvian tax charges the polluter the external cost so private cost rises to social cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The subsidy mirror" in your own words.
**Answer:** Positive externalities are fixed with a subsidy, the mirror of the Pigouvian tax.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Coase — let them bargain" in your own words.
**Answer:** With clear property rights and low bargaining costs, the parties bargain to the efficient outcome — the Coase theorem.
