# Pigouvian taxes, subsidies and the Coase theorem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Pigou's tax"?
a) A Pigouvian tax charges the polluter the external cost so private cost rises to social cost  b) Positive externalities are fixed with a subsidy, the mirror of the Pigouvian tax  c) With clear property rights and low bargaining costs, the parties bargain to the efficient outcome — the Coase theorem
**Answer: a) A Pigouvian tax charges the polluter the external cost so private cost rises to social cost**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The subsidy mirror"?
a) Positive externalities are fixed with a subsidy, the mirror of the Pigouvian tax  b) With clear property rights and low bargaining costs, the parties bargain to the efficient outcome — the Coase theorem  c) A Pigouvian tax charges the polluter the external cost so private cost rises to social cost
**Answer: a) Positive externalities are fixed with a subsidy, the mirror of the Pigouvian tax**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Coase — let them bargain"?
a) With clear property rights and low bargaining costs, the parties bargain to the efficient outcome — the Coase theorem  b) A Pigouvian tax charges the polluter the external cost so private cost rises to social cost  c) Positive externalities are fixed with a subsidy, the mirror of the Pigouvian tax
**Answer: a) With clear property rights and low bargaining costs, the parties bargain to the efficient outcome — the Coase theorem**
