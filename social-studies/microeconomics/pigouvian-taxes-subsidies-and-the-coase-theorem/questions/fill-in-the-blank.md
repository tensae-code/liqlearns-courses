# Pigouvian taxes, subsidies and the Coase theorem — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tax named after ____ charges the polluter the external cost.
**Answer: Pigou**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A positive externality is fixed with a ____.
**Answer: subsidy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The theorem that bargaining with clear property rights reaches efficiency is named after ____.
**Answer: Coase**
