# Pigouvian taxes, subsidies and the Coase theorem — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Pigouvian tax charges the polluter the external cost so private cost rises to social cost.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Positive externalities are fixed with a subsidy, the mirror of the Pigouvian tax.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With clear property rights and low bargaining costs, the parties bargain to the efficient outcome — the Coase theorem.
**Answer: True**
