# Pigouvian taxes, subsidies and the Coase theorem — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pigou's tax — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The subsidy mirror — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Coase — let them bargain — b

Statements:
a) Positive externalities are fixed with a subsidy, the mirror of the Pigouvian tax
b) With clear property rights and low bargaining costs, the parties bargain to the efficient outcome — the Coase theorem
c) A Pigouvian tax charges the polluter the external cost so private cost rises to social cost
