# Hands-on — Pigouvian taxes, subsidies and the Coase theorem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Set the tax
Give a factory MEC figures at three output levels. Set the Pigouvian tax, then say what output the firm chooses before and after the tax — and what happens if the tax is set twice too high.

## Bargain it out
In pairs, bargain the smoke case twice: once with the laundry holding the right to clean air, once with the factory holding the right to smoke. Compare the outcome and the payments.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
