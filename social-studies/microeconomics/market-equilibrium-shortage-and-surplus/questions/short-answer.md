# Market equilibrium — shortage and surplus — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Where the schedules meet" in your own words.
**Answer:** The shirts market is in equilibrium at Birr 20, where 3 shirts are demanded and supplied.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Shortage bids the price up" in your own words.
**Answer:** A shortage — excess demand — bids the price up toward equilibrium.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Surplus bids the price down" in your own words.
**Answer:** A surplus — excess supply — pushes the price down toward equilibrium.
