# Market equilibrium — shortage and surplus — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The shirts market is in equilibrium at Birr 20, where 3 shirts are demanded and supplied.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A shortage — excess demand — bids the price up toward equilibrium.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A surplus — excess supply — pushes the price down toward equilibrium.
**Answer: True**
