# Market equilibrium — shortage and surplus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Where the schedules meet"?
a) equilibrium at Birr 20 and 3 shirts, where the quantities are equal  b) shortage (excess demand) below equilibrium pushes price up  c) surplus (excess supply) above equilibrium pushes price down
**Answer: a) equilibrium at Birr 20 and 3 shirts, where the quantities are equal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Shortage bids the price up"?
a) equilibrium at Birr 20 and 3 shirts, where the quantities are equal  b) surplus (excess supply) above equilibrium pushes price down  c) shortage (excess demand) below equilibrium pushes price up
**Answer: c) shortage (excess demand) below equilibrium pushes price up**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Surplus bids the price down"?
a) shortage (excess demand) below equilibrium pushes price up  b) surplus (excess supply) above equilibrium pushes price down  c) equilibrium at Birr 20 and 3 shirts, where the quantities are equal
**Answer: b) surplus (excess supply) above equilibrium pushes price down**
