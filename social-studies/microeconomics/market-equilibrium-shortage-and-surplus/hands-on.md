# Hands-on — Market equilibrium — shortage and surplus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Run the adjustment with the class: set a price card too low, count hands up (demand) against shirts held (supply), name the shortage, then raise the price card step by step until hands and shirts match.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a crowded stall in shortage beside a stall with unsold goods in surplus — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
