# Information in economic decisions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Information is an _____ to economic decisions, like labour or materials.
**Answer: input**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Searching takes time and effort, so information has a _____.
**Answer: cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Decisions are taken on _____ knowledge, and some turn out wrong.
**Answer: partial / incomplete**
