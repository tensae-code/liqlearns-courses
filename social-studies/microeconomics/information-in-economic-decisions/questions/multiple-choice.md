# Information in economic decisions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, information is treated as —
a) a luxury
b) an input to economic decisions
c) a tax
**Answer: b) an input to economic decisions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cost of information includes —
a) only money fees
b) time, effort and bought expertise
c) nothing — information is free
**Answer: b) time, effort and bought expertise**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Real decisions are usually taken on —
a) perfect knowledge
b) partial, incomplete information
c) no information at all
**Answer: b) partial, incomplete information**
