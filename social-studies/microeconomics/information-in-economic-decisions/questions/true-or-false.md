# Information in economic decisions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A buyer who knows prices in three shops usually decides better than one who knows one.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** It can be rational to stop searching when more search costs more than it gains.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Waiting for perfect information before deciding is the lesson's advice.
**Answer: False — Perfect information never comes; the lesson advises gathering what is worth its cost and deciding.**
