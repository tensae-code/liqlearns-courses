# Information in economic decisions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does better information improve a decision?
**Answer:** It lets the decider pick the right good, at the right price, at the right time, before money is committed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When should a decider stop gathering information?
**Answer:** When the cost of more searching is bigger than the expected gain from a better decision.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson advise about incomplete information?
**Answer:** Gather the information whose cost is worth paying, then decide — do not wait for perfect knowledge.
