# From one buyer to the whole market — market demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market demand is found by adding every buyer's quantity at each price — quantities added, not prices.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Market demand is found by adding every buyer's quantity at each price — quantities added, not prices.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Adding at the same price); sub lesson 2 says: The market demand curve is the horizontal summation of all individual demand curves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Horizontal summation adds along the quantity axis; prices are never stacked.
**Answer: True**
