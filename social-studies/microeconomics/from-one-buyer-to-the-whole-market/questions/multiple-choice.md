# From one buyer to the whole market — market demand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Adding at the same price"?
a) quantities added at the same price, never prices added  b) the horizontal summation of all individual demand curves  c) addition runs sideways along the quantity axis
**Answer: a) quantities added at the same price, never prices added**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The market curve"?
a) quantities added at the same price, never prices added  b) addition runs sideways along the quantity axis  c) the horizontal summation of all individual demand curves
**Answer: c) the horizontal summation of all individual demand curves**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why 'horizontal' matters"?
a) the horizontal summation of all individual demand curves  b) addition runs sideways along the quantity axis  c) quantities added at the same price, never prices added
**Answer: b) addition runs sideways along the quantity axis**
