# From one buyer to the whole market — market demand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In market demand, _____ are added at the same price — never prices.
**Answer: quantities**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The market demand curve is the _____ summation of individual curves.
**Answer: horizontal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Horizontal summation adds along the _____ axis.
**Answer: quantity**
