# From one buyer to the whole market — market demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Adding at the same price" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market demand is found by adding every buyer's quantity at each price — quantities added, not prices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The market curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The market demand curve is the horizontal summation of all individual demand curves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why 'horizontal' matters" in your own words.
**Answer:** Horizontal summation adds along the quantity axis; prices are never stacked.
