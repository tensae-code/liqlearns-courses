# Hands-on — From one buyer to the whole market — market demand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two learners call their quantities at each price while a third writes only the sums on the board — the class watches a market schedule being built by addition.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two household curves side by side adding, quantity by quantity, into one wider market curve — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
