# Video script — Depreciation — spreading the cost of equipment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Depreciation spreads a price over a life. A machine costs 120,000,000 kip and serves five years — so its cost is counted 24,000,000 kip a year, says the Laos Grade 11 book. Divide the price by the years of use, and use the printed table: buildings twenty years, machinery eight to ten, vehicles five. That is how a business counts the cost of its equipment honestly, year by year.
