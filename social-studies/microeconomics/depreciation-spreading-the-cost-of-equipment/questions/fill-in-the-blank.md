# Depreciation — spreading the cost of equipment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An asset's price is spread over its _____ life.
**Answer: useful**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To calculate depreciation, divide the asset's _____ by its years of use.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Machinery lives 8–10 years at _____ % a year.
