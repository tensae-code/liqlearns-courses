# Depreciation — spreading the cost of equipment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The whole price of a machine is counted as a cost of the year it was bought.
**Answer: False — the price is spread over the asset's useful life, a part each year.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 120,000,000 kip divided by 5 years is 24,000,000 kip a year.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Vehicles and buildings have the same life in the table.
**Answer: False — vehicles live 5 years at 20% a year; buildings live 20 years at 5% a year.**
