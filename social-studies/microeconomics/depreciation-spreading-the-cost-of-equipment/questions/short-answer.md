# Depreciation — spreading the cost of equipment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is depreciation, as the books teach it?
**Answer:** Spreading an asset's price over its useful life — counting a part of the price as a cost in each year the asset serves, instead of all of it in the year it was bought.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Work the books' example: a machine at 120,000,000 kip over 5 years.
**Answer:** 120,000,000 ÷ 5 = 24,000,000 kip a year.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the table's life and yearly percentage for buildings and for vehicles.
**Answer:** Buildings: 20 years at 5% a year. Vehicles: 5 years at 20% a year.
