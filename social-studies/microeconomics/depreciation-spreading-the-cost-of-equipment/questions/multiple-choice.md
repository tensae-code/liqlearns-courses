# Depreciation — spreading the cost of equipment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Depreciation spreads an asset's price over —
a) its useful life  b) one month  c) the owner's lifetime only
**Answer: a) its useful life**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A machine bought for 120,000,000 kip over 5 years depreciates each year by —
a) 12,000,000 kip  b) 24,000,000 kip  c) 120,000,000 kip
**Answer: b) 24,000,000 kip**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the table, buildings live —
a) 5 years  b) 3–5 years  c) 20 years
**Answer: c) 20 years**
