# Hands-on — Depreciation — spreading the cost of equipment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the division by hand
Write the book's example on paper — 120,000,000 kip divided by 5 years — and work it out without a calculator. Then open lesson/lesson.md and check your answer against the book's 24,000,000 kip a year.

## Say it with things at home
Take one tool or machine at home. Say its price, guess its years of use from the lesson's table, divide, and say its depreciation cost for one year out loud.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
