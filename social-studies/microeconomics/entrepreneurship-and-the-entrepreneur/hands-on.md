# Hands-on — Entrepreneurship and the entrepreneur

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the marks
On paper, write the three marks of entrepreneurship from the lesson — independent, initiative, risk-bearing. Under each, write one real example from a business you know. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything missing.

## Say it with things at home
Put three stones on the table, one for each mark of entrepreneurship, and name what each stone is. Then take one stone away and say what the activity loses without it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
