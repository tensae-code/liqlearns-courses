# Entrepreneurship and the entrepreneur — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Entrepreneurship carries no risk for the person who does it.
**Answer: False — it is taught as risk-bearing activity; the entrepreneur bears the risk.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The individual entrepreneur and the businessman are among the first concepts of the course.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The subject, aims and tasks of entrepreneurship are set out at the start of Duisenkhanov's Part 1.
**Answer: True**
