# Entrepreneurship and the entrepreneur — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Entrepreneurship, as Kazakhstan's Grade 10 books teach it, is —
a) work done for a wage  b) independent, initiative, risk-bearing activity  c) trade with no risk
**Answer: b) independent, initiative, risk-bearing activity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good, in the books' opening lesson, is —
a) what meets a need  b) money in a bank  c) a kind of tax
**Answer: a) what meets a need**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Duisenkhanov's Lesson 1 sets out, for entrepreneurship, its —
a) prices only  b) subject, aims and tasks  c) export rules
**Answer: b) subject, aims and tasks**
