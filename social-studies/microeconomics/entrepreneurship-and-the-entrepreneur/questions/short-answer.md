# Entrepreneurship and the entrepreneur — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is entrepreneurship, as the Grade 10 books define it?
**Answer:** Independent, initiative, risk-bearing activity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two people-concepts, and which two starting concepts, does the opening lesson teach?
**Answer:** The individual entrepreneur and the businessman; and needs and goods — a need is what a person lacks and wants met, a good is what meets it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What frame does Duisenkhanov's Lesson 1 give the whole subject?
**Answer:** Its subject, aims and tasks — what entrepreneurship studies, what it aims at, and what tasks it sets.
