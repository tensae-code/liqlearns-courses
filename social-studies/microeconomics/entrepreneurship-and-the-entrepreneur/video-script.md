# Video script — Entrepreneurship and the entrepreneur

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan's Grade 10 books open their entrepreneurship course by defining the thing itself, says the narrator: entrepreneurship is independent, initiative, risk-bearing activity. The first concepts are the individual entrepreneur and the businessman — and the needs and goods the activity starts from. First say what it is and who does it; then study how it works.
