# Explicit costs and implicit costs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explicit costs are actual _____ outlays to outsiders who supply inputs.
**Answer: cash**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Implicit costs are the values of resources the firm _____ and uses itself.
**Answer: owns**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Implicit costs are valued at what the resource could earn in its best _____ use.
**Answer: alternative**
