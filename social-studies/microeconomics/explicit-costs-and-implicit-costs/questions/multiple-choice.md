# Explicit costs and implicit costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Explicit costs — cash out the door"?
a) actual cash outlays to outsiders — labour, land, capital, materials, fuel, transport, power  b) the values of resources the firm owns and uses itself  c) valued at what the resource could earn in its best alternative use
**Answer: a) actual cash outlays to outsiders — labour, land, capital, materials, fuel, transport, power**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Implicit costs — your own resources"?
a) actual cash outlays to outsiders — labour, land, capital, materials, fuel, transport, power  b) valued at what the resource could earn in its best alternative use  c) the values of resources the firm owns and uses itself
**Answer: c) the values of resources the firm owns and uses itself**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Valued at the best alternative"?
a) the values of resources the firm owns and uses itself  b) valued at what the resource could earn in its best alternative use  c) actual cash outlays to outsiders — labour, land, capital, materials, fuel, transport, power
**Answer: b) valued at what the resource could earn in its best alternative use**
