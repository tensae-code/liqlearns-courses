# Explicit costs and implicit costs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explicit costs are actual cash outlays to outsiders who supply inputs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explicit costs are actual cash outlays to outsiders who supply inputs.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Explicit costs — cash out the door); sub lesson 2 says: Implicit costs are the values of resources the firm owns and uses itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Implicit costs are valued at what the resources could earn in their best alternative use.
**Answer: True**
