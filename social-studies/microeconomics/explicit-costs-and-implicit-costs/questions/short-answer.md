# Explicit costs and implicit costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Explicit costs — cash out the door" in your own words.
**Answer:** Explicit costs are actual cash outlays to outsiders who supply inputs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Implicit costs — your own resources" in your own words.
**Answer:** Implicit costs are the values of resources the firm owns and uses itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Valued at the best alternative" in your own words.
**Answer:** Implicit costs are valued at what the resources could earn in their best alternative use.
