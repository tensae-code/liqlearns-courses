# Hands-on — Explicit costs and implicit costs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two bowls: 'cash out' and 'no cash — still a cost'. Deal cost cards into them; for every 'no cash' card, the dealer must name what the resource could have earned elsewhere.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — birr notes leaving the firm to suppliers on one side; the owner's own building and time greyed on the other with no cash paid — still a cost — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
