# Hands-on — Price discrimination — three degrees

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Run a market stall three ways: first, haggle each buyer to their own maximum; second, post a bulk ladder; third, post two prices for two named groups. The class names the degree after each round.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three panels — a doctor's sliding fee scale, a bulk-price ladder, two market segments with two prices — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
