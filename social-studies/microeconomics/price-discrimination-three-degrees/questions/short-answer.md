# Price discrimination — three degrees — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "First degree — every buyer's maximum" in your own words.
**Answer:** First-degree price discrimination charges every buyer their exact reservation price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Second degree — price by quantity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Second-degree discrimination varies the price according to the quantity bought, under one schedule for all buyers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Third degree — segments, the commonest" in your own words.
**Answer:** Third-degree discrimination, the commonest kind, charges different prices to different segments of the market.
