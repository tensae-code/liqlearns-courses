# Price discrimination — three degrees — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** First-degree discrimination charges each buyer their exact _____ — the most they would pay.
**Answer: reservation price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Second-degree discrimination varies price by the _____ bought — bulk discounts are its common form.
**Answer: quantity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Third-degree discrimination splits the market into _____ and charges each a different price.
**Answer: segments**
