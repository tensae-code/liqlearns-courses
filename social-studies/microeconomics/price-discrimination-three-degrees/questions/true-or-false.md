# Price discrimination — three degrees — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** First-degree price discrimination charges every buyer their exact reservation price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Second-degree discrimination varies the price according to the quantity bought, under one schedule for all buyers.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Third-degree discrimination, the commonest kind, charges different prices to different segments of the market.
**Answer: True**
