# Price discrimination — three degrees — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "First degree — every buyer's maximum"?
a) each buyer charged their exact reservation price — the doctor's sliding scale  b) one schedule for all, with price falling as quantity bought rises  c) the market split into groups at different prices — seniors' and students' discounts
**Answer: a) each buyer charged their exact reservation price — the doctor's sliding scale**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Second degree — price by quantity"?
a) each buyer charged their exact reservation price — the doctor's sliding scale  b) the market split into groups at different prices — seniors' and students' discounts  c) one schedule for all, with price falling as quantity bought rises
**Answer: c) one schedule for all, with price falling as quantity bought rises**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Third degree — segments, the commonest"?
a) one schedule for all, with price falling as quantity bought rises  b) the market split into groups at different prices — seniors' and students' discounts  c) each buyer charged their exact reservation price — the doctor's sliding scale
**Answer: b) the market split into groups at different prices — seniors' and students' discounts**
