# Surplus becomes capital — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only small farmers sell surplus wheat.
**Answer: False — medium and large farmers sell the surplus.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Surplus earnings are always spent at once.
**Answer: False — they are saved to finance next season's capital.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tejpal Singh hides his surplus at home unused.
**Answer: False — he banks it, lends it and plans a tractor.**
