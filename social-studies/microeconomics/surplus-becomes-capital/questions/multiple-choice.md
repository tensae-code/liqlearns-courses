# Surplus becomes capital — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Surplus wheat is sold at —
a) Raiganj market  b) no market  c) the village pond
**Answer: a) Raiganj market**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saved earnings finance —
a) next season's capital  b) a feast only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) next season's capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tejpal Singh plans to buy —
a) another tractor  b) a ship  c) a market
**Answer: a) another tractor**
