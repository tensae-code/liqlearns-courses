# Surplus becomes capital — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who sells surplus wheat, and where?
**Answer:** Medium and large farmers, at Raiganj market.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do farmers do with the earnings?
**Answer:** Save them to finance next season's capital.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things Tejpal Singh does with his surplus.
**Answer:** Any two of: banks it, lends it, plans another tractor.
