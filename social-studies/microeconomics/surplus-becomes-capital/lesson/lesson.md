# Surplus becomes capital

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — surplus becomes capital — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Selling surplus wheat at Raiganj market

**Medium and large farmers sell their surplus wheat at Raiganj market** — the part of the crop beyond their own needs becomes money.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saving the earnings finances next season's capital

They **save the earnings to finance next season's capital** — this year's surplus buys next year's seed, fertiliser and machines.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tejpal Singh — banks it, lends it, plans a tractor

**Tejpal Singh banks his surplus, lends it out, and plans another tractor** — one farmer's surplus at work in three forms.
