# For whom to produce? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The problem of distribution" in your own words.
**Answer:** For whom to produce is the problem of distribution of the national product among society's members.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The two directions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Distribution can lean toward goods for a few rich people, or necessities for the whole population first and luxuries after.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why it is an economic problem" in your own words.
**Answer:** Distribution is an economic problem because the sharing rule changes what gets produced.
