# For whom to produce? — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For whom to produce is the problem of distribution of the national product among society's members.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For whom to produce is the problem of distribution of the national product among society's members.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The problem of distribution); sub lesson 2 says: Distribution can lean toward goods for a few rich people, or necessities for the whole population first and luxuries after.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Distribution is an economic problem because the sharing rule changes what gets produced.
**Answer: True**
