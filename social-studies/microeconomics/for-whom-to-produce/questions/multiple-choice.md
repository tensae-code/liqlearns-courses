# For whom to produce? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The problem of distribution"?
a) the problem of distribution of the national product  b) for a few rich, or necessities for all first and luxuries after  c) the sharing rule steers what gets produced
**Answer: a) the problem of distribution of the national product**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The two directions"?
a) the problem of distribution of the national product  b) the sharing rule steers what gets produced  c) for a few rich, or necessities for all first and luxuries after
**Answer: c) for a few rich, or necessities for all first and luxuries after**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why it is an economic problem"?
a) for a few rich, or necessities for all first and luxuries after  b) the sharing rule steers what gets produced  c) the problem of distribution of the national product
**Answer: b) the sharing rule steers what gets produced**
