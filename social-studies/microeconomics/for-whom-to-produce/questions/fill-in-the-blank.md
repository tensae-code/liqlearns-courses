# For whom to produce? — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For whom to produce is the problem of _____ of the national product.
**Answer: distribution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One direction of sharing is _____ for the whole population first, and luxuries after.
**Answer: necessities**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The distribution rule matters economically because it changes what gets _____.
**Answer: produced**
