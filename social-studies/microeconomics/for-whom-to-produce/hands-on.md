# Hands-on — For whom to produce?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Divide 20 counters (the national product) twice: once to 2 learners, once across the whole group with basics first. The group discusses what changed and what it cost.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one pile of goods being divided two ways — a small group taking most, then the same pile spread across a crowd — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
