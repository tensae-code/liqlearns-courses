# Video script — For whom to produce?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

For whom to produce is also called the problem of distribution of the national product: how the output an economy makes is shared among society's members. Production is only half the story — the sharing decides who actually eats, learns and lives from it.

The sharing can lean two ways: output for a few rich people, or first necessities for the whole population and luxuries after. Every real economy sits somewhere between those two poles.

Distribution is an economic problem, not only a moral one, because the sharing rule changes what gets produced: producers follow the buying power of those the product is for. Answer 'for whom' and you have half-answered 'what'.
