# Cardinal utility and the consumer's optimum — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The extra satisfaction from one more unit is called _____ — the change in total utility over the change in quantity.
**Answer: marginal utility**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As consumption rises, marginal utility _____ — each extra unit adds less than the one before.
**Answer: diminishes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At the optimum, the marginal utility _____ is the same for every good, and the whole income is spent.
**Answer: per birr**
