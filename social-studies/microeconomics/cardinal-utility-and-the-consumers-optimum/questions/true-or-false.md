# Cardinal utility and the consumer's optimum — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marginal utility is the change in total utility divided by the change in quantity consumed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marginal utility diminishes as consumption of a good rises, other things equal.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At the cardinal optimum, marginal utility per birr is equal for every good and the whole income is spent.
**Answer: True**
