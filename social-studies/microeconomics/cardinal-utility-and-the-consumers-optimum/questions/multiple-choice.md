# Cardinal utility and the consumer's optimum — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Utility and marginal utility"?
a) satisfaction measured in utils; MU is the extra from one more unit  b) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before  c) MU per birr equal for every good, with the whole income spent
**Answer: a) satisfaction measured in utils; MU is the extra from one more unit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Diminishing marginal utility"?
a) satisfaction measured in utils; MU is the extra from one more unit  b) MU per birr equal for every good, with the whole income spent  c) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before
**Answer: c) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The optimum — equal MU per birr"?
a) each extra unit adds less satisfaction than the one before  b) MU per birr equal for every good, with the whole income spent  c) satisfaction measured in utils; MU is the extra from one more unit
**Answer: b) MU per birr equal for every good, with the whole income spent**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Total utility is 40 utils at four cups of tea and 50 utils at five cups. What is the marginal utility of the fifth cup?
a) 10 utils  b) 50 utils  c) 90 utils
**Answer: a) 10 utils** — MU is the change in total utility: 50 − 40 = 10 utils.
