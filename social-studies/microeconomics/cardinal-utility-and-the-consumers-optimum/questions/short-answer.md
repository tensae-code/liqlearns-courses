# Cardinal utility and the consumer's optimum — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Utility and marginal utility" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal utility is the change in total utility divided by the change in quantity consumed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Diminishing marginal utility" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal utility diminishes as consumption of a good rises, other things equal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The optimum — equal MU per birr" in your own words.
**Answer:** At the cardinal optimum, marginal utility per birr is equal for every good and the whole income is spent.
