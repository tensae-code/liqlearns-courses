# Hands-on — Cardinal utility and the consumer's optimum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Use stones as utils. Build a pile for each cup of tea from 1 to 5, each new pile smaller than the last, and say at each step how much the newest cup added.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a total-utility curve flattening while the marginal-utility steps beneath it shrink — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
