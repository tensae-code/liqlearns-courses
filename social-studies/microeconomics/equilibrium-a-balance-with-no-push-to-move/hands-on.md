# Hands-on — Equilibrium — a balance with no push to move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Balance two equal piles of stones on a plank over a book. Tip it, watch it settle, and narrate: the tipping is the push; the settling is the market mechanism.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a balance scale with demand weights on one pan and supply weights on the other, level — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
