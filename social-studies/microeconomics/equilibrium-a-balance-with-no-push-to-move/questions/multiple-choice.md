# Equilibrium — a balance with no push to move — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The borrowed picture"?
a) a state of balance with no tendency to change  b) no wish to re-allocate income; no wish to change output  c) the market's tendency toward the balancing price; real markets tend, not rest
**Answer: a) a state of balance with no tendency to change**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Consumers and firms at rest"?
a) a state of balance with no tendency to change  b) the market's tendency toward the balancing price; real markets tend, not rest  c) no wish to re-allocate income; no wish to change output
**Answer: c) no wish to re-allocate income; no wish to change output**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The market mechanism"?
a) no wish to re-allocate income; no wish to change output  b) the market's tendency toward the balancing price; real markets tend, not rest  c) a state of balance with no tendency to change
**Answer: b) the market's tendency toward the balancing price; real markets tend, not rest**
