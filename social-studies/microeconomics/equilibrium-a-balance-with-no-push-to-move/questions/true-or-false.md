# Equilibrium — a balance with no push to move — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Equilibrium is a state of balance with no tendency to change.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Equilibrium is a state of balance with no tendency to change.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The borrowed picture); sub lesson 2 says: A consumer in equilibrium has no wish to re-allocate income, and a firm in equilibrium has no wish to change output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market mechanism is the tendency of a market to move toward the price where opposing forces balance.
**Answer: True**
