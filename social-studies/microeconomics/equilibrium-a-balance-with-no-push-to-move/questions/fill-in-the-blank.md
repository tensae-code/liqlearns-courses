# Equilibrium — a balance with no push to move — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Equilibrium is a state of _____ with no tendency to change.
**Answer: balance**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A consumer in equilibrium has no wish to _____ income.
**Answer: re-allocate**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market's tendency toward the balancing price is the market _____.
**Answer: mechanism**
