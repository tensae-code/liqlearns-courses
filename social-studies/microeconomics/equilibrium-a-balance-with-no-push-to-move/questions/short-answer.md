# Equilibrium — a balance with no push to move — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The borrowed picture" in your own words.
**Answer:** Equilibrium is a state of balance with no tendency to change.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Consumers and firms at rest" in your own words.
**Answer:** A consumer in equilibrium has no wish to re-allocate income, and a firm in equilibrium has no wish to change output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The market mechanism" in your own words.
**Answer:** The market mechanism is the tendency of a market to move toward the price where opposing forces balance.
