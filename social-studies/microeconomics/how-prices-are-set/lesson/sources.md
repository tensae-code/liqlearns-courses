Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 12 Economics): the final-year course turns practical — the market is studied the way a Cambodian trader meets it (consumers, prices, banks, riel exchange), and macro policy is taught as coordination: monetary policy, exchange rates and public finance fitted together, closing with foreign trade and the world trading system. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Ch. 1, Lesson 5: Pricing — teaches: how a price is determined for a product, and what a seller weighs when setting it. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
