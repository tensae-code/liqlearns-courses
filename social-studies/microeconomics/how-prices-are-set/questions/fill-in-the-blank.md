# How prices are set — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price is _____ by a seller weighing real things.
**Answer: determined (set)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A price below _____ eats the business.
**Answer: cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Too low a price means the business sells itself _____.
**Answer: poor**
