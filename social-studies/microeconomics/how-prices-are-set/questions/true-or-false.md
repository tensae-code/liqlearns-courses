# How prices are set — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price is determined by weighing, and tested in the market.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A seller setting a price weighs only their own wishes.
**Answer: False — A seller weighs cost, buyers and rivals — not only their own wishes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Too high a price leaves goods unsold.
**Answer: True**
