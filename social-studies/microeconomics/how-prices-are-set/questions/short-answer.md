# How prices are set — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What tests a set price?
**Answer: The market — buyers accept it or walk away.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three things the weighed price must do at once.
**Answer: Cover cost, tempt buyers, and stand beside rivals' prices.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When should a seller re-weigh a price?
**Answer: When costs, buyers or rivals move.**
