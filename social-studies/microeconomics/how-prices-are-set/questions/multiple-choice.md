# How prices are set — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price, in this lesson, is —
a) guessed with no weighing at all  b) determined — set by weighing real things and tested in the market  c) always copied from a foreign price list
**Answer: b) determined — set by weighing real things and tested in the market**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A seller setting a price weighs —
a) cost, what buyers will pay, and rivals' prices  b) nothing but the day of the week  c) only their own wishes
**Answer: a) cost, what buyers will pay, and rivals' prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Too high a price risks —
a) rivals lowering their prices in thanks  b) instant riches for the seller  c) goods sitting unsold
**Answer: c) goods sitting unsold**
