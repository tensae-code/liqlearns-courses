# The three stages of production — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The production table" in your own words.
**Answer:** The production table holds capital fixed at 10 units, adds labour from 0 to 9, and output rises to 108 quintals then falls to 80.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Stages I and II" in your own words.
**Answer:** Stage II, where AP and MP fall but MP stays positive and TP still rises, is the sensible operating range.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Stage III — and the rules beneath" in your own words.
**Answer:** In Stage III, MP turns negative and TP itself falls because too much variable input crowds the fixed input.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Stage I and Stage III — the two bad ends" in your own words.
**Answer:** No sensible producer operates in Stage III, where marginal product is negative.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "Stage II — the rational stage" in your own words.
**Answer:** Stage II, where marginal product is positive but falling, is the rational stage of production.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Explain "The product-curve rules" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal product cuts average product at average product's maximum.
