# The three stages of production — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The production table holds capital fixed at 10 units, adds labour from 0 to 9, and output rises to 108 quintals then falls to 80.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The production table holds capital fixed at 10 units, adds labour from 0 to 9, and output rises to 108 quintals then falls to 80.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The production table); sub lesson 2 says: Stage II, where AP and MP fall but MP stays positive and TP still rises, is the sensible operating range.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Stage III, MP turns negative and TP itself falls because too much variable input crowds the fixed input.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** No sensible producer operates in Stage III, where marginal product is negative.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Stage II, where marginal product is positive but falling, is the rational stage of production.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Marginal product cuts average product at average product's maximum.
**Answer: True**
