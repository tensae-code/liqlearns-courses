# The three stages of production — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The production table"?
a) capital fixed at 10, labour 0 to 9, output rising to 108 quintals then falling to 80  b) Stage I: AP rising; Stage II: AP and MP falling, MP positive — the sensible range  c) MP negative and TP falling — too much variable input crowding the fixed input
**Answer: a) capital fixed at 10, labour 0 to 9, output rising to 108 quintals then falling to 80**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Stages I and II"?
a) capital fixed at 10, labour 0 to 9, output rising to 108 quintals then falling to 80  b) MP negative and TP falling — too much variable input crowding the fixed input  c) Stage I: AP rising; Stage II: AP and MP falling, MP positive — the sensible range
**Answer: c) Stage I: AP rising; Stage II: AP and MP falling, MP positive — the sensible range**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Stage III — and the rules beneath"?
a) Stage I: AP rising; Stage II: AP and MP falling, MP positive — the sensible range  b) MP negative and TP falling — too much variable input crowding the fixed input  c) capital fixed at 10, labour 0 to 9, output rising to 108 quintals then falling to 80
**Answer: b) MP negative and TP falling — too much variable input crowding the fixed input**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Stage I and Stage III — the two bad ends"?
a) Stage I MP rising; Stage III MP negative — neither is the working stage  b) MP positive but falling — the rational stage  c) MP cuts AP at AP's maximum; TP rises while MP is positive
**Answer: a) Stage I MP rising; Stage III MP negative — neither is the working stage**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which statement best matches "Stage II — the rational stage"?
a) Stage I MP rising; Stage III MP negative — neither is the working stage  b) MP cuts AP at AP's maximum; TP rises while MP is positive  c) MP positive but falling — the rational stage
**Answer: c) MP positive but falling — the rational stage**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Which statement best matches "The product-curve rules"?
a) MP positive but falling — the rational stage  b) MP cuts AP at AP's maximum; TP rises while MP is positive  c) Stage I MP rising; Stage III MP negative — neither is the working stage
**Answer: b) MP cuts AP at AP's maximum; TP rises while MP is positive**
