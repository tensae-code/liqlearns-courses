# The three stages of production — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the production table, output rises to _____ quintals, levels, then falls to 80.
**Answer: 108**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Stage II, MP falls but stays _____, and TP still rises.
**Answer: positive**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Stage III, MP turns _____ and TP itself falls.
**Answer: negative**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In Stage III, marginal product is _____ — extra workers cut total output.
**Answer: negative**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Stage II — MP positive but falling — is called the _____ stage.
**Answer: rational**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Marginal product cuts average product at average product's _____.
**Answer: maximum**
