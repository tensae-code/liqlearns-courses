# Hands-on — The three stages of production

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act the stages with a cup-stacking team on one small table: stage one, stacks fly up; stage two, slower gains; stage three, crowding knocks stacks down. Name the stage aloud at each change.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the TP curve with the three stages shaded, AP and MP curves underneath peaking and crossing zero in order — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Grade 10 — say it with things at home
Three learners hold stage banners over the TP curve drawing: rising MP, falling-but-positive MP, negative MP. The class moves a worker token along the curve and the right banner is raised at each stretch.
