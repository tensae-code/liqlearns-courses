# Property ownership and patent rights — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is property, and what are its two kinds? Give an example of each.
**Answer:** Property is a possession or asset. Movable property can be carried away — cars, furniture, livestock; immovable property cannot be moved — land and houses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the types of ownership the unit lists.
**Answer:** Freehold, leasehold, communal, state, customary, and joint ownership.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the unit say ideas should be protected?
**Answer:** Because protecting ideas — by patent rights, copyrights and trademarks — encourages creativity and innovation: makers create when they know their ideas cannot simply be taken.
