# Property ownership and patent rights — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Livestock is movable property.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Communal and customary ownership are among the types of ownership the unit names.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Protecting ideas discourages creativity, according to the unit's discussion.
**Answer: False — protecting ideas encourages creativity and innovation, because a maker whose idea cannot simply be taken will bother to create.**
