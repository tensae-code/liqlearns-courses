# Property ownership and patent rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is immovable property?
a) a cow  b) land  c) furniture
**Answer: b) land**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ownership of property is proven by —
a) a handshake only  b) documents such as title deeds and registration books  c) living on the land longest
**Answer: b) documents such as title deeds and registration books**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Patent rights, copyrights and trademarks protect —
a) ideas — inventions, works and makers' marks  b) only land  c) school buildings
**Answer: a) ideas — inventions, works and makers' marks**
