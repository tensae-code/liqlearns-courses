# Property ownership and patent rights — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cars, furniture and livestock are _____ property; land and houses are immovable property.
**Answer: movable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ownership is proven by documents such as title _____ and registration books.
**Answer: deeds**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A _____ right protects an invention, as copyright protects a work and a trademark protects a maker's mark.
**Answer: patent**
