# Hands-on — Property ownership and patent rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the property
List ten things owned in your home and community. Mark each M for movable or I for immovable, and write how its ownership could be proven. Check your work against lesson/lesson.md.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a house and its land with a title deed above them, and beside them a new tool with a patent seal. Label every part with the lesson's words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
