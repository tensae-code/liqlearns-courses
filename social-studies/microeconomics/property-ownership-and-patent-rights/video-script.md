# Video script — Property ownership and patent rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Property is a possession or asset. Some of it moves — cars, furniture, livestock. Some of it cannot — land and houses.

It can be owned in different ways: freehold, leasehold, communal, state, customary, joint — and ownership is proven by documents, title deeds and registration books.

Ideas are property too. Patents, copyrights and trademarks protect them — and that protection encourages people to create and innovate.
