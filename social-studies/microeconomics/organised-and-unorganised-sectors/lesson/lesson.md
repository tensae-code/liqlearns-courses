# Organised and unorganised sectors

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — organised and unorganised sectors — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kanta's organised-sector job

**Kanta's organised-sector office job** gives a **regular salary, provident fund, paid leave and laws protecting her** — work inside the rules.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kamal's unorganised daily-wage work

**Kamal's unorganised work** is **daily-wage, with no leave, no security, and dismissal at will** — work outside the rules' protection.

## Sub lesson 3 — Who needs protection

The lesson closes on **who the unorganised workers needing protection are** — the daily-wage and casual workers outside the organised sector's rules.
