# Video script — Organised and unorganised sectors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Organised and unorganised sectors — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: Kanta's organised-sector office job (regular salary, provident fund, paid leave, laws protecting her) against Kamal's unorganised daily-wage work (no leave, no security, can be dismissed at will) — and who the unorganised workers needing protection are.
