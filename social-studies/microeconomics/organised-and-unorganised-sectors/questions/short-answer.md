# Organised and unorganised sectors — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two protections in Kanta's job.
**Answer:** Any two of: regular salary, provident fund, paid leave, laws protecting her.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two marks of Kamal's unorganised work.
**Answer:** Any two of: daily wage, no leave, no security, dismissal at will.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who needs protection, on the lesson's showing?
**Answer:** The unorganised workers outside the rules' protection.
