# Organised and unorganised sectors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kanta's job is in the —
a) organised sector  b) unorganised sector  c) no sector
**Answer: a) organised sector**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kamal's work has —
a) no leave, no security, dismissal at will  b) paid leave and a pension  c) a regular salary
**Answer: a) no leave, no security, dismissal at will**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The workers needing protection are —
a) unorganised workers  b) office managers  c) employers
**Answer: a) unorganised workers**
