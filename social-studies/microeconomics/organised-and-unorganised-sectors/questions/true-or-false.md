# Organised and unorganised sectors — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kanta can be dismissed at will with no rules.
**Answer: False — laws protect her in the organised sector.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kamal's job is protected by the same laws as Kanta's.
**Answer: False — his work has no such protection.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Organised-sector workers are the unprotected ones.
**Answer: False — the unorganised workers are those needing protection.**
