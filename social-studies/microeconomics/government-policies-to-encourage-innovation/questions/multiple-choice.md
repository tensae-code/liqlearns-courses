# Government policies to encourage innovation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Patents encourage innovation by —
a) letting innovators keep their inventions' returns for a time  b) giving inventions away at once  c) banning research
**Answer: a) letting innovators keep their inventions' returns for a time**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government help with R&D works by —
a) sharing research costs firms would not bear alone  b) taxing research away  c) closing laboratories
**Answer: a) sharing research costs firms would not bear alone**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A cooperative research venture joins —
a) firms, universities and government  b) only rival firms in secret  c) no one
**Answer: a) firms, universities and government**
