# Government policies to encourage innovation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do IP rights encourage innovation?
**Answer:** They let the innovator keep the returns for a time, so innovation is worth its cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the second policy?
**Answer:** Help with research and development costs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three innovation policies in this lesson.
**Answer:** Intellectual property rights, help with R&D costs, and cooperative research ventures.
