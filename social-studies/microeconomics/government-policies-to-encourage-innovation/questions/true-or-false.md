# Government policies to encourage innovation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Innovators need no protection to profit from inventions.
**Answer: False — without protection, imitators take the returns; IP rights let innovators keep them for a time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** R&D support means government does all research itself.
**Answer: False — it helps with costs; firms and universities still do the research.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cooperative ventures forbid universities from taking part.
**Answer: False — universities are central partners in them.**
