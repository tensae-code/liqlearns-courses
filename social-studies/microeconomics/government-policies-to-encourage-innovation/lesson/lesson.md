# Government policies to encourage innovation

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Intellectual property rights

**Intellectual property rights** — patents and copyrights — let innovators **keep the returns** to their inventions for a time, making innovation worth its cost.

## Sub lesson 2 — Help with research and development costs

Government **helps with research and development (R&D) costs** — funding and support that share the expense private firms would not bear alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — Cooperative research ventures

**Cooperative research ventures** join **firms, universities and government** in shared research whose results no single party would fund alone.
