# Video script — Total expenditure and price changes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook defines the buyers' total expenditure on a good as the price multiplied by the quantity bought: TE = P × Q. It is the same sum seen from the other side as the sellers' total revenue.

Cut the price and two things happen together: each unit brings in less, and more units are sold. Total expenditure rises if the quantity gain outweighs the price loss, and falls if it does not.

A seller announcing 'prices down 20%' has said nothing yet about money taken. If quantity rises only 5%, total expenditure has fallen; if quantity doubles, it has soared.
