# Total expenditure and price changes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Total expenditure on a good equals —
a) price plus quantity
b) price times quantity
c) price divided by quantity
**Answer: b) price times quantity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After a price cut, total expenditure —
a) always rises
b) always falls
c) rises only if the quantity gain outweighs the price loss
**Answer: c) rises only if the quantity gain outweighs the price loss**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Prices fall 20% and quantity rises 5%. Total expenditure —
a) rises
b) falls
c) is unchanged
**Answer: b) falls**
