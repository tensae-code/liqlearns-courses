# Total expenditure and price changes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Total expenditure of buyers is the same sum as total revenue of sellers.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Comparing total expenditure before and after requires computing P × Q in both states.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A price announcement alone tells you the money taken.
**Answer: False — Quantity's response must be known too; total expenditure is the product of both.**
