# Total expenditure and price changes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a price change pull total expenditure two ways?
**Answer:** The price factor changes directly while quantity responds in the opposite direction; the product depends on which change is bigger.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the textbook settle what a price cut does to total expenditure?
**Answer:** It computes price times quantity before and after the change and compares the two products.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must be known besides a price change to know the money taken?
**Answer:** How quantity responds — total expenditure is price times quantity.
