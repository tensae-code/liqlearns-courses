# The economic principle — getting the most from scarce means — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the economic principle both ways.
**Answer:** Achieve the greatest result with given means; or achieve a given result with the least means.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one household example of each form.
**Answer:** Greatest result from given means: feeding the family best within a fixed budget. Given result for least means: feeding them the same for less.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the workbook teach the principle right after scarcity?
**Answer:** Because it is the working rule scarcity forces: with means scarce, what matters is how much result they yield.
