# The economic principle — getting the most from scarce means — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The principle has two forms: maximise result for given means, or minimise means for a given result.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Idle means that could have produced count as waste under the principle.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Firms and states, not only households, are judged by the principle.
**Answer: True**
