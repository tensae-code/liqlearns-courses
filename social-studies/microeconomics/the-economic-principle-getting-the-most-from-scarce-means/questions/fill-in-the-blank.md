# The economic principle — getting the most from scarce means — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greatest result with given means, or a given result with the least _____.
**Answer: means**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spending beyond what the result required is _____ under the principle.
**Answer: waste**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Because means are _____, every user of means is judged by the result they yield.
**Answer: scarce**
