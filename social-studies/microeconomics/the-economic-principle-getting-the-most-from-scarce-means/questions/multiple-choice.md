# The economic principle — getting the most from scarce means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The economic principle says —
a) spend as much as possible
b) greatest result from given means, or a given result from least means
c) needs are unlimited so planning is useless
**Answer: b) greatest result from given means, or a given result from least means**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fixed food budget spent to feed the family best is an example of —
a) the first form — greatest result from given means
b) ignoring scarcity
c) a shift of demand
**Answer: a) the first form — greatest result from given means**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The economic principle is the working rule forced by —
a) abundance
b) scarcity
c) taxation
**Answer: b) scarcity**
