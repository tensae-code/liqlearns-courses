# Video script — The economic principle — getting the most from scarce means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 9 workbook gives the rule its own section: the economic principle — achieve the greatest result with given means, or a given result with the least means.

A student with two study hours before a test applies the principle by spending them where they gain the most marks. A household with a fixed food budget applies it by feeding the family best within the budget — or feeding them the same for less.

The workbook places the principle right after scarcity and choice for a reason: it is the working rule scarcity forces. Because means are scarce, every user of means — person, firm, state — is judged by how much result the means yield.
