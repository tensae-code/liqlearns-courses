## Sub lesson 1 — The great deregulation experiment

The **US deregulation experiment** removed price and entry controls in whole industries — the book **judges its results** industry by industry.

## Sub lesson 2 — How effective are regulation and antitrust?

The chapter weighs the **effectiveness of price regulation and antitrust policy** — what they achieved, and where they fell short.

## Sub lesson 3 — Regulatory capture

**Regulatory capture** is when the **regulated industry gains influence over its own regulator**, bending the rules toward itself instead of the public.
