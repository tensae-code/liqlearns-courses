# Deregulation and regulatory capture — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book refuses to judge deregulation's results.
**Answer: False — the book judges the experiment's results.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Price regulation is always fully effective.
**Answer: False — the book weighs its effectiveness, including where it fell short.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Regulators can never be influenced by the industries they police.
**Answer: False — that influence is exactly what regulatory capture names.**
