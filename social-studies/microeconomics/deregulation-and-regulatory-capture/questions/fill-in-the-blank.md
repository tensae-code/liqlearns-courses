# Deregulation and regulatory capture — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Removing price and entry controls is _____.
**Answer: deregulation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book weighs the _____ of price regulation and antitrust policy.
**Answer: effectiveness**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When an industry bends its own regulator, that is regulatory _____.
**Answer: capture**
