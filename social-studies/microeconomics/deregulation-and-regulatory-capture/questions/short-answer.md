# Deregulation and regulatory capture — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book do with the US deregulation experiment?
**Answer:** It judges its results, industry by industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two policies' effectiveness are weighed?
**Answer:** Price regulation and antitrust policy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is regulatory capture?
**Answer:** The regulated industry gaining influence over its regulator, bending rules toward itself.
