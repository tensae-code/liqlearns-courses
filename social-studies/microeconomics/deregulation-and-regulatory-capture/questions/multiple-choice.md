# Deregulation and regulatory capture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Deregulation means —
a) removing price and entry controls  b) adding more controls  c) nationalising an industry
**Answer: a) removing price and entry controls**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The chapter weighs —
a) the effectiveness of price regulation and antitrust policy  b) only deregulation's slogans  c) no evidence
**Answer: a) the effectiveness of price regulation and antitrust policy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Regulatory capture means —
a) the regulated industry bends its regulator toward itself  b) the regulator captures a firm  c) voters capture an agency
**Answer: a) the regulated industry bends its regulator toward itself**
