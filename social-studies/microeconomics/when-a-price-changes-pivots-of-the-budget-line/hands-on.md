# Hands-on — When a price changes — pivots of the budget line

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pin one end of a string budget line at the sugar intercept with a finger. Swing the free end in and out as bread's price rises and falls — the pin is the pivot.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a line rotating around a fixed Y-intercept as the X-price falls and rises — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
