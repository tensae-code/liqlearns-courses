# When a price changes — pivots of the budget line — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A change in one price _____ the budget line around the other good's untouched intercept.
**Answer: pivots**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If both prices rise in the same _____, the line shifts inward parallel, because the price ratio is unchanged.
**Answer: proportion**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A student with 60 birr facing tea at 3 birr could buy at most _____ if he bought no coffee.
**Answer: 20 teas**
