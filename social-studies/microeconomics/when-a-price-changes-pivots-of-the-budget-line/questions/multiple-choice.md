# When a price changes — pivots of the budget line — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "One price changes — one intercept stays"?
a) the changed good's intercept moves; the other stays fixed and anchors the pivot  b) a Y-price change pivots around the X-intercept; both prices rising together shifts the line in parallel  c) 60 birr over tea at 3 and coffee at 6 gives intercepts of 20 and 10
**Answer: a) the changed good's intercept moves; the other stays fixed and anchors the pivot**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The same logic for Y, and for both prices together"?
a) the changed good's intercept moves; the other stays fixed and anchors the pivot  b) 60 birr over tea at 3 and coffee at 6 gives intercepts of 20 and 10  c) a Y-price change pivots around the X-intercept; both prices rising together shifts the line in parallel
**Answer: c) a Y-price change pivots around the X-intercept; both prices rising together shifts the line in parallel**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Bereket's 60 birr — the closing activity"?
a) a Y-price change pivots around the X-intercept; both prices rising together shifts the line in parallel  b) 60 birr over tea at 3 and coffee at 6 gives intercepts of 20 and 10  c) the changed good's intercept moves; the other stays fixed and anchors the pivot
**Answer: b) 60 birr over tea at 3 and coffee at 6 gives intercepts of 20 and 10**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A student has 60 birr; tea costs 3 birr and coffee 6 birr. What are the intercepts of his budget line (tea on the X-axis)?
a) 20 teas and 10 coffees  b) 10 teas and 20 coffees  c) 60 teas and 60 coffees
**Answer: a) 20 teas and 10 coffees** — 60 / 3 = 20 teas; 60 / 6 = 10 coffees.
