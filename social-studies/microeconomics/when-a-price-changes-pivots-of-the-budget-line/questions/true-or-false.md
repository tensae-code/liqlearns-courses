# When a price changes — pivots of the budget line — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A change in one good's price pivots the budget line around the other good's intercept, which stays fixed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If both prices rise in the same proportion, the budget line shifts inward in parallel, like an income fall.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With 60 birr, tea at 3 birr and coffee at 6 birr, the budget line's intercepts are 20 teas and 10 coffees.
**Answer: True**
