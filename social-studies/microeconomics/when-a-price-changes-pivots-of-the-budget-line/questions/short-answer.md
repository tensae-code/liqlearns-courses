# When a price changes — pivots of the budget line — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "One price changes — one intercept stays" in your own words.
**Answer:** A change in one good's price pivots the budget line around the other good's intercept, which stays fixed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The same logic for Y, and for both prices together" in your own words.
**Answer:** If both prices rise in the same proportion, the budget line shifts inward in parallel, like an income fall.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Bereket's 60 birr — the closing activity" in your own words.
**Answer:** With 60 birr, tea at 3 birr and coffee at 6 birr, the budget line's intercepts are 20 teas and 10 coffees.
