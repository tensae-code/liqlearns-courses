# Labour costs and productivity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "The price of labour as wages and salaries"?
**Answer:** The price of labour as wages and salaries, productivity measured as output per worker and per hour, what raises or lowers it (skills, technology, innovation, unemployment, competition), and how rising labour costs affect production, distribution and consumption.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "How employee actions move a firm's costs and"?
**Answer:** How employee actions move a firm's costs and profit — unions protecting workers' rights, working conditions and productivity, voluntary and involuntary turnover draining knowledge, morale and output, and the wage rate raising or lowering the cost of production.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG: Grade 10 Economics is the labour and capitalism year — two full units on labour (unions, division of labour, labour law, labour costs), then the market system judged by its costs and benefits, trade through television and the internet, and a closing unit interrogating the capitalist ideology itself.
