# Labour costs and productivity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The price of labour as wages and salaries, _____ measured as output per worker and per hour, what raises or lowers it (skills, technology, innovation, unemployment, competition), and how rising labour costs affect production, distribution and consumption.
**Answer: productivity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How employee actions move a firm's costs and profit — unions protecting workers' rights, working conditions and _____, voluntary and involuntary turnover draining knowledge, morale and output, and the wage rate raising or lowering the cost of production.
**Answer: productivity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG: Grade 10 Economics is the labour and capitalism year — two full units on labour (unions, division of labour, labour law, labour costs), then the market system judged by its costs and benefits, trade through television and the internet, and a closing unit _____ the capitalist ideology itself.
**Answer: interrogating**
