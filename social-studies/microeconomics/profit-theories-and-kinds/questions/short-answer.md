# Profit — theories and kinds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is profit?
**Answer:** Profit is what remains to the producer after the other factor rewards are paid; it rewards enterprise and risk.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is net profit built from, in the book's account?
**Answer:** The reward of the producer's own labour, interest on his own capital, windfall gains and speculative gains.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three factors that move profit.
**Answer:** Any three of: techniques, nature of the business, monopoly, substitutes, advertisement, trade policy.
