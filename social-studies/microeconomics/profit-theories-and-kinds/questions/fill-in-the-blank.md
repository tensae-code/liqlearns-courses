# Profit — theories and kinds — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What remains to the producer after other factor rewards are paid is _____.
**Answer: profit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sudden gain from luck in net profit's build-up is a _____ gain.
**Answer: windfall**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One factor that moves profit, named in the book, is _____ policy.
**Answer: trade**
