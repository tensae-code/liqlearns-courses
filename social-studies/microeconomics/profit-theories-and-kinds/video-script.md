# Video script — Profit — theories and kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Profit — theories and kinds — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: profit is what remains to the producer after the other factor rewards are paid; net profit is built from own-labour reward, interest on own capital, windfall and speculative gains; profit is moved by techniques, the business's nature, monopoly, substitutes, advertisement and trade policy.
