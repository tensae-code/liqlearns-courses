## Sub lesson 1 — Profit — definition and role

The Class 11 book closes distribution with **profit**: what remains to the producer after the other factor rewards are paid. Its **role** is the reward that draws enterprise into production and risk.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kinds of profit — what net profit is built from

The book builds **net profit** from its parts: the reward of the producer's **own labour**, **interest on his own capital**, **windfall gains** and **speculative gains**. Gross appearances deceive — net profit is what is left when the producer's own inputs are counted.

## Sub lesson 3 — What moves profit

Profit is moved by named factors: **techniques** of production, the **nature of the business**, **monopoly**, the availability of **substitutes**, **advertisement** and **trade policy**. Change any of them and the firm's profit changes with it.
