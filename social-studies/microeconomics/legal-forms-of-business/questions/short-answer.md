# Legal forms of business — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between a sole trader and a partnership?
**Answer:** A sole trader owns the business alone and takes the profit and risk alone; in a partnership, two or more people own it together and share the work, capital and profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a cooperative work in the module's example?
**Answer:** Maize farmers join together as members and owners, so their maize is collected, stored and sold together instead of each farmer selling a small amount alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four forms of business in the order the lesson teaches them.
**Answer:** Sole trader, partnership, cooperative and limited company.
