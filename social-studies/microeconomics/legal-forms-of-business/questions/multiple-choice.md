# Legal forms of business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mr Banda's grocery shop, owned by Mr Banda alone, is —
a) a cooperative  b) a sole trader business  c) a limited company
**Answer: b) a sole trader business**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A farmers' maize cooperative is owned by —
a) one shareholder  b) the government only  c) its members together
**Answer: c) its members together**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A limited company is owned by —
a) shareholders, each owning a part of it  b) one trader alone  c) two tailors
**Answer: a) shareholders, each owning a part of it**
