# Legal forms of business — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a partnership, two or more people own the business together and share the profit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a cooperative, one person alone owns the whole business.
**Answer: False** — a cooperative is owned by its members together; one person owning alone is a sole trader.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mary and John's tailoring business is the module's example of a limited company.
**Answer: False** — Mary and John's tailoring is the partnership example; the transport company owned by shareholders is the limited company.
