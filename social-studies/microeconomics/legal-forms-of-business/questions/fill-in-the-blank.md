# Legal forms of business — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One person who owns a business alone is a sole _____.
**Answer: trader**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Maize farmers who own and sell together form a _____.
**Answer: cooperative**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** People who each own a part of a limited company are called _____.
**Answer: shareholders**
