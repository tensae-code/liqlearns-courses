# Video script — Legal forms of business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Businesses come in four forms. A sole trader owns a business alone, like Mr Banda and his grocery shop.

A partnership is owned by people working together, like Mary and John and their tailoring business. A cooperative is owned by all its members together, like farmers who sell their maize through a maize cooperative.

A limited company is owned by shareholders — each shareholder owns a part of it — like a transport company owned by its shareholders.
