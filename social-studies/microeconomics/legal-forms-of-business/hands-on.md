# Hands-on — Legal forms of business

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Take four cards and write one business on each: grocery shop, tailoring, maize selling, transport. First work alone with one card (sole trader), then join with a partner for one card, then group all the cards together two ways — as a cooperative of members and as a company of shareholders — and say what changes each time.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's four businesses from memory and label each one's form. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the forms there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
