# Hands-on — Long-run equilibrium — why profits are competed away

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act entry and exit: learners stand as firms. While a 'profit' sign is held up, more firms walk in and the called price steps down; while 'loss' shows, firms walk out and price steps up — until only 'normal profit' remains and everyone stops moving.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a price line settling onto the bottom of the LAC bowl as new firms stream in — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
