# Long-run equilibrium — why profits are competed away — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The long run — everything can change" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the long run all inputs are variable and firms can freely enter or leave the industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Entry pushes price down, exit pushes it up" in your own words.
**Answer:** Supernormal profits attract entrants, expanding supply and pushing price down.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The resting point — normal profit only" in your own words.
**Answer:** Long-run equilibrium sits at the minimum of long-run average cost, where firms earn only normal profit.
