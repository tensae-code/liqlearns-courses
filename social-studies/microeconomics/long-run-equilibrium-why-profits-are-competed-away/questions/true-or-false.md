# Long-run equilibrium — why profits are competed away — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the long run all inputs are variable and firms can freely enter or leave the industry.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Supernormal profits attract entrants, expanding supply and pushing price down.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Long-run equilibrium sits at the minimum of long-run average cost, where firms earn only normal profit.
**Answer: True**
