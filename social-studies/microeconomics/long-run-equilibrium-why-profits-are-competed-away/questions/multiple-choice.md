# Long-run equilibrium — why profits are competed away — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The long run — everything can change"?
a) in the long run all inputs vary and firms can enter or exit  b) profits attract entry and lower price; losses drive exit and raise price  c) price at minimum LAC with LMC = LAC = P = MR and normal profit only
**Answer: a) in the long run all inputs vary and firms can enter or exit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Entry pushes price down, exit pushes it up"?
a) in the long run all inputs vary and firms can enter or exit  b) price at minimum LAC with LMC = LAC = P = MR and normal profit only  c) profits attract entry and lower price; losses drive exit and raise price
**Answer: c) profits attract entry and lower price; losses drive exit and raise price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The resting point — normal profit only"?
a) profits attract entry and lower price; losses drive exit and raise price  b) price at minimum LAC with LMC = LAC = P = MR and normal profit only  c) in the long run all inputs vary and firms can enter or exit
**Answer: b) price at minimum LAC with LMC = LAC = P = MR and normal profit only**
