# Long-run equilibrium — why profits are competed away — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the long run, firms are free to _____ the industry.
**Answer: enter or leave**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Supernormal profits attract _____, whose extra supply pushes the price down.
**Answer: entrants**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In long-run equilibrium competitive firms earn only _____ — price equals minimum LAC.
**Answer: normal profit**
