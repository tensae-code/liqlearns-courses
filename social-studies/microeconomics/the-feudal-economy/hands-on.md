# Hands-on — The feudal economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Tell the feudal bargain out loud, both sides of it: what the serf or tenant gives — work on the lord's land, taxes and rent in labour — and what he gets — the use of land, and protection. Finish with the book's turn: land in Europe, cattle in parts of Africa.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the estate: the lord's land, the tenants' plots, and arrows for what flows each way — labour and rent one way, land use and protection the other. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and add anything you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
