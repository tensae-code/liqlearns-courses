# The feudal economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the feudal economy of Eritrea's Grade 7 book, the land is held by —
a) the serfs  b) the landlords (lords)  c) the towns
**Answer: b) the landlords (lords)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Serfs and tenants paid their taxes and rent —
a) in money  b) in labour  c) in cattle in every country
**Answer: b) in labour**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book says the feudal order was land-based in Europe and, in parts of Africa, based on —
a) cattle  b) fishing  c) factories
**Answer: a) cattle**
