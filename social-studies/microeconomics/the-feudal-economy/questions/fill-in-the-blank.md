# The feudal economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the feudal economy, _____ hold the land and _____ and tenants work it.
**Answer: landlords (lords); serfs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The tenants paid taxes and rent in _____, in exchange for land use and _____.
**Answer: labour; protection**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Land-based in _____, the feudal order was _____-based in parts of Africa.
**Answer: Europe; cattle**
