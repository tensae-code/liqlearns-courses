# The feudal economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who holds the land in the feudal economy, and who works it?
**Answer:** The landlords (lords) hold the land; serfs and tenants work the lord's land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the feudal bargain — what each side gives and gets.
**Answer:** Serfs and tenants work the lord's land and pay taxes and rent in labour; in exchange they receive the use of land to live from and the lord's protection.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How did the feudal order in parts of Africa differ from the one in Europe, in the book?
**Answer:** In Europe it was land-based — counted in plots of land; in parts of Africa it was cattle-based — wealth, rank and obligation counted in cattle.
