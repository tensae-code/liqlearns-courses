# The feudal economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the feudal economy, serfs and tenants owned the land they worked.
**Answer: False** — the landlords held the land; serfs and tenants worked the lord's land and lived from the use of it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The rent serfs and tenants paid was mainly work — labour on the lord's land — not money.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In exchange for their labour-rent, serfs and tenants received the use of land and the lord's protection.
**Answer: True**
