# Lesson plan — The feudal economy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** The feudal economy
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Eritrea's Grade 7 telling, the feudal economy is a land order: landlords hold the land, and serfs and tenants work the lord's land, paying taxes and rent in labour in exchange for the use of land and for protection — land-based in Europe, cattle-based in parts of Africa.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the slave economy, the rung below it on Eritrea's systems ladder, and on the traditional economy before that: the pupil who knows how the communal and slave societies organised production can now see what the feudal order changed — and what it kept.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
