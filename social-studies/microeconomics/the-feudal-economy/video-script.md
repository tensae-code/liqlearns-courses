# Video script — The feudal economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climb one more rung of Eritrea's Grade 7 ladder. After the slave society comes the feudal society — and its economy is built on one thing: land.

The landlords hold the land. Serfs and tenants work it — the lord's land as well as the plots they live from — and what do they pay for the use of that land, and for the lord's protection? Not money. They pay in labour, and in taxes and rent. And the book ends with a turn worth remembering: in Europe the feudal order was based on land; in parts of Africa it was based on cattle.

Land held by lords, worked by serfs and tenants, paid for in labour — that is the feudal economy.
