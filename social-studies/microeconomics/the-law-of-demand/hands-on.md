# Hands-on — The law of demand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Price-tag relay: one learner calls prices from high to low; the group holds up the number of fingers (kilos) the schedule says. Swap roles and repeat from low to high.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the oranges schedule as a table with the price column falling and the quantity column rising — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
