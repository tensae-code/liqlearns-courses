**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Ethiopia, France, Kazakhstan, Jordan, Bangladesh, Yemen, Armenia, Nicaragua). Albania is teacher-guide evidence only — named here, counted nowhere.**

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 7 Guía del Emprendedor): entrepreneurship is taught as a government-guided route — idea, business plan, formalisation through the Ventanilla Única de Inversiones, and export registration — inside the "economía creativa, familiar, comunitaria, cooperativa y asociativa" model, with córdoba prices and Nicaraguan institutions throughout.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 Social Science student): Grade 10 is a full Economics course inside Social Science — demand and supply are taught with a school-canteen juice schedule priced in dram, the production-possibilities curve with duduks and shvis (Armenian instruments), and personal finance through Armenian institutions: Central-Bank-licensed lenders, the ACRA credit bureau, the Financial System Mediator and the Rate.am / Appa.am comparison sites, including a fake "Gazprom Armenia" investment-scam case.

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium.

Albania's own way (Albania Grade 12 Ekonomia 12 Teacher Guide): a full Grade 12 course that runs micro to macro and then turns practical — business ethics, success skills and personal finance close the course; Albanian anchors throughout (Bank of Albania videos for the personal-budget lesson, the Albanian government budget in the government chapter, the types of money in circulation in Albania). This Albania material stands on the teacher guide as evidence only.

France's own way (SES 2de, Manuels Libres): SES teaches economics, sociology and political science as one subject from Seconde; the economics is built from data and cases (INSEE, the France Relance plan read as a Keynesian model, housing and oil prices, the gender pay gap in spreadsheets) with a synthesis and exercise for nearly every notion.

Pairing note carried from the placements: the student textbook is the finished 2023 edition, while the teacher guide is ©2022 with an unfinished placeholder printing block — edition-mismatched companions; the guide's answer keys are partly incomplete/misaligned with this book's review questions, and no answer-key figure is taught here unchecked.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 10 Aganina): Economics is taught inside an entrepreneurship course — market theory arrives as the theory an entrepreneur needs, with tenge-denominated supply examples (a potato supply curve at 50-70 tenge), world oil prices deciding whether an Almaty-region furniture workshop profits, and Kazakhstan's own post-Soviet transition as the opening history.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 10 Duisenkhanov Part 1): the same national curriculum taught in a three-lesson rhythm — one theory lesson, one game, one Kazakhstani or world business case (Rocket Study, Yuframe, Shymbulak, Elon Musk) — so every Economics concept is immediately played and then applied.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 5 Term 2): Grade 5 gets the first formal market lesson in the whole set — supply and demand open with frost-damaged vegetables and Ramadan meat prices, trade is read off Aqaba port and Jordan's real export/import lists, and services are toured through Jordan's own schools platform, hospitals and tourist sites.
