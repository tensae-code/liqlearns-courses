# The law of demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The law stated" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ceteris paribus, price and quantity demanded move inversely — higher price, lower quantity demanded.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why the curve slopes down" in your own words.
**Answer:** The demand curve slopes down because a lower price raises real income and makes the good relatively cheaper than its substitutes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The salt exception that proves the shape" in your own words.
**Answer:** The quantity of salt demanded tends to be unresponsive to price, because people buy some salt irrespective of price.
