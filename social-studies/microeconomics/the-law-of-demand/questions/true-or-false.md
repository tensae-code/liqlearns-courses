# The law of demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ceteris paribus, price and quantity demanded move inversely — higher price, lower quantity demanded.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ceteris paribus, price and quantity demanded move inversely — higher price, lower quantity demanded.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The law stated); sub lesson 2 says: The demand curve slopes down because a lower price raises real income and makes the good relatively cheaper than its substitutes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The quantity of salt demanded tends to be unresponsive to price, because people buy some salt irrespective of price.
**Answer: True**
