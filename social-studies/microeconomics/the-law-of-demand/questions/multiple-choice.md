# The law of demand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The law stated"?
a) price and quantity demanded move inversely, other things equal  b) a fall in price raises real income and makes the good cheaper than substitutes  c) salt demand barely answers price — a necessity bought irrespective of price
**Answer: a) price and quantity demanded move inversely, other things equal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Why the curve slopes down"?
a) price and quantity demanded move inversely, other things equal  b) salt demand barely answers price — a necessity bought irrespective of price  c) a fall in price raises real income and makes the good cheaper than substitutes
**Answer: c) a fall in price raises real income and makes the good cheaper than substitutes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The salt exception that proves the shape"?
a) a fall in price raises real income and makes the good cheaper than substitutes  b) salt demand barely answers price — a necessity bought irrespective of price  c) price and quantity demanded move inversely, other things equal
**Answer: b) salt demand barely answers price — a necessity bought irrespective of price**
