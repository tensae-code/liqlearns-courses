# The law of demand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price and quantity demanded move _____, other things remaining the same.
**Answer: inversely**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When price falls, the same money buys more — _____ has risen.
**Answer: real income**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The quantity of _____ demanded barely answers price, because people buy it irrespective of price.
**Answer: salt**
