# Video script — Time and the elasticity of supply

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook adds the factor students forget: time. Quantity supplied cannot jump the moment price rises — fields must be planted, workers hired, machines installed. Supply is therefore less elastic in the short run than in the long.

The textbook's habit is to think in horizons: the immediate run, where output is nearly fixed and supply almost vertical; the short run, where some inputs adjust; and the long run, where everything — plant, workforce, even the number of firms — can change.

A sudden price spike for a crop or a room does not prove sellers are greedy or buyers foolish — it often just shows supply caught in its inelastic horizon, unable to answer yet.
