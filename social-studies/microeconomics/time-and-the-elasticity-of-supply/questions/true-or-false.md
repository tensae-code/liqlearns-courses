# Time and the elasticity of supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The same price rise can call forth much more output after a year than after a month.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the long run even the number of firms can change.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The same good has different supply elasticities at different horizons.
**Answer: True**
