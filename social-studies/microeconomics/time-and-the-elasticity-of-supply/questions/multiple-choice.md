# Time and the elasticity of supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Supply is more elastic when producers have —
a) less time
b) more time to adjust
c) fixed prices
**Answer: b) more time to adjust**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the immediate run, supply is —
a) perfectly elastic
b) nearly fixed, almost vertical
c) horizontal
**Answer: b) nearly fixed, almost vertical**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A price spike right after demand jumps often shows —
a) supply in its inelastic short horizon
b) that elasticity is infinite
c) that supply has fully adjusted
**Answer: a) supply in its inelastic short horizon**
