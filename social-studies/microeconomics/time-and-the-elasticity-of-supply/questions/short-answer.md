# Time and the elasticity of supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does time raise supply elasticity?
**Answer:** Because adjusting takes time — planting, hiring and installing cannot happen instantly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three horizons and supply's response in each.
**Answer:** Immediate — nearly fixed; short run — some inputs adjust; long run — everything adjusts, including firm numbers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must be asked before judging a market's supply response?
**Answer:** Which time horizon the measurement covers — elasticity differs by horizon for the same good.
