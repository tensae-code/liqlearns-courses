# Self-employment versus wage employment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Laos book weighs working for yourself against being a monthly-salaried employee in —
a) a poem  b) side-by-side advantage/disadvantage tables  c) a single examination question
**Answer: b) side-by-side advantage/disadvantage tables**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The self-employed person's return is —
a) a monthly salary from an employer  b) profit and income from her own business  c) a government grant
**Answer: b) profit and income from her own business**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches that part of each year's income should be —
a) spent at once  b) lent to friends  c) saved
**Answer: c) saved**
