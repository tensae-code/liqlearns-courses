# Self-employment versus wage employment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On what three points do the book's tables weigh the two ways of working?
**Answer:** Independence, income and security — set out as side-by-side advantages and disadvantages for working for yourself and for being a monthly-salaried employee.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the self-employed person's return, and how does it differ from an employee's?
**Answer:** Profit and income from her own business. An employee receives a monthly salary from an employer; the self-employed person's return comes from her own business and can change.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What rule does the book give for the self-employed person's income?
**Answer:** Part of each year's income is to be saved.
