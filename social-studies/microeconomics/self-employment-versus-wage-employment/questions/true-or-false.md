# Self-employment versus wage employment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tables weigh the two ways of working on independence, income and security.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A monthly-salaried employee's return is the profit of her own business.
**Answer: False — profit and income are the self-employed person's return; the employee receives a monthly salary.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches saving part of each year's income in the same section as profit and income.
**Answer: True**
