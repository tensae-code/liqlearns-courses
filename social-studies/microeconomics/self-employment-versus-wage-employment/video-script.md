# Video script — Self-employment versus wage employment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two ways of working stand side by side in the Laos Grade 10 book. Working for yourself, or being a monthly-salaried employee — the book weighs both in tables, point by point: independence, income, security. The self-employed person's return is profit and income — and part of each year's income is to be saved. Neither side is praised; both are laid out whole, so you can choose with your eyes open.
