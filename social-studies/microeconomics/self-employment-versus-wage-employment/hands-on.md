# Hands-on — Self-employment versus wage employment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Weigh it with two columns
Draw two columns on paper: "Working for myself" and "Monthly-salaried employee". Under each, write one advantage and one disadvantage from the lesson's three weighing points — independence, income, security. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every point you missed.

## Say it with things at home
Put ten small stones in a row for one year's income. Move some of them aside and say: this part is saved. Say out loud why the book teaches saving in the same section as profit and income.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
