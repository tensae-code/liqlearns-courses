Yemen's own way (Grade 4 Al-Tarbiya al-Ijtima'iya): the economy is taught as the homeland's wealth — crops, herds, fish, workshops and ports — with the state, the agricultural associations and the export harbour all named as actors a nine-year-old can point to. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 2, Lesson 1 — My homeland's agricultural resources (§agricultural associations) — teaches: Agricultural associations work for the farmers: they supply farm inputs and loans at low prices, market the farmers' crops, arrange transport, and sell the surplus abroad. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
