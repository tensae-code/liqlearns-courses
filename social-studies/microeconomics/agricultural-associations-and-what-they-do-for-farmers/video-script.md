# Video script — Agricultural associations — and what they do for farmers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Farmers do not have to face the market alone. Agricultural associations do the heavy work with them: they supply farm inputs and loans at low prices, so the season can start without dear borrowing.

When the harvest comes, the association markets the farmers' crops and arranges transport to get them there. And the surplus — what the homeland does not need — the association sells abroad. One association, four tasks, all for the farmers.
