# Agricultural associations — and what they do for farmers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the associations' tasks.
**Answer:** Supply farm inputs and loans at low prices; market the farmers' crops; arrange transport; sell the surplus abroad.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do low-price inputs and loans matter to a farmer?
**Answer:** They free him from buying and borrowing dear on his own at the season's start — the lone farmer's heaviest burden.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the association do with the surplus?
**Answer:** It sells the surplus abroad, turning the farmers' combined extra harvest into an export.
