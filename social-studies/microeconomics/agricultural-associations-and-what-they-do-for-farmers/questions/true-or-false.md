# Agricultural associations — and what they do for farmers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An association's loans are at low prices.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each farmer must arrange his own transport to market; the association never does.
**Answer: False — arranging transport is one of the association's tasks.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The association sells the surplus abroad.
**Answer: True**
