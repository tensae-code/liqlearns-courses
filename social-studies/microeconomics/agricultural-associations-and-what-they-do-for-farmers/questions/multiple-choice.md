# Agricultural associations — and what they do for farmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural associations supply farmers with —
a) inputs and loans at low prices  b) only advice  c) imported food
**Answer: a) inputs and loans at low prices**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marketing the crops means the association takes on —
a) the sowing  b) the selling — buyers and prices  c) the harvesting by hand
**Answer: b) the selling — buyers and prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The surplus is —
a) sold abroad by the association  b) thrown away  c) taxed away in full
**Answer: a) sold abroad by the association**
