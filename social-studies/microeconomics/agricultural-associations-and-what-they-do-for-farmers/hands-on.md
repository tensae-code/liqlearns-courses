# Hands-on — Agricultural associations — and what they do for farmers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with one harvest
Follow one farmer's year. Say aloud what the association does for him at sowing (inputs and a loan), at harvest (marketing and transport), and after (selling the surplus abroad).

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — an agricultural association at work — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
