# Hands-on — Monopoly equilibrium — MR equals MC, price from demand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Build the book's table with counters: stack TR and TC piles unit by unit to 5 units, measure the gap each time, and stop the class's chant at 3 units where the gap peaks.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the standard monopoly diagram with the per-unit profit strip and total-profit rectangle shaded — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
