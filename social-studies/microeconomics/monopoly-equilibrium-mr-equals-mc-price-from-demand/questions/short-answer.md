# Monopoly equilibrium — MR equals MC, price from demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Two routes to the same output" in your own words.
**Answer:** The monopolist's profit peaks where MR = MC, at three units in the book's worked table.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The price is read off demand" in your own words.
**Answer:** A monopolist reads its price off the demand curve above the MR = MC output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Three outcomes — but supernormal profit can stay" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unlike under competition, a monopolist's supernormal profit is not competed away, because entry is blocked.
