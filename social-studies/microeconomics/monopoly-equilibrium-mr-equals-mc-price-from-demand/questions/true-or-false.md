# Monopoly equilibrium — MR equals MC, price from demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The monopolist's profit peaks where MR = MC, at three units in the book's worked table.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A monopolist reads its price off the demand curve above the MR = MC output.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unlike under competition, a monopolist's supernormal profit is not competed away, because entry is blocked.
**Answer: True**
