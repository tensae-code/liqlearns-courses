# Monopoly equilibrium — MR equals MC, price from demand

## Sub lesson 1 — Two routes to the same output

The monopolist maximises profit by the same two routes as the competitive firm. The total approach finds the widest TR-over-TC gap — worked in the book's table and graph, peaking at three units of output. The marginal approach sets MR = MC with MC cutting from below. Both routes name the same profit-maximising quantity.

## Sub lesson 2 — The price is read off demand

The monopolist does not charge the MR = MC value — that equality only fixes the quantity. The price is read off the demand curve directly above the chosen output: the highest price buyers will pay for exactly that quantity. Price sits above marginal revenue at the optimum, which is the visible mark of monopoly power.

## Sub lesson 3 — Three outcomes — but supernormal profit can stay

Comparing average revenue with average cost at the optimum gives the same three outcomes as competition: normal profit, supernormal profit, or loss. The difference is what happens next: with entry blocked, nothing erases the supernormal case. A profitable monopoly can stay profitable in the long run, where a competitive firm's extra profit is competed away.
