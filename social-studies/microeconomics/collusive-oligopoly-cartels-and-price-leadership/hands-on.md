# Hands-on — Collusive oligopoly — cartels and price leadership

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three 'firms' first compete by shouting falling prices. Then they sit, sign one price card together, and hold it up. Finally one firm leads: it raises its card alone and the others raise theirs to match.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — firms signing one price agreement; then one leader firm moving its price tag while smaller tags copy it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
