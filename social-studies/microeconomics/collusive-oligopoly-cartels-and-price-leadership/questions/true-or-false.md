# Collusive oligopoly — cartels and price leadership — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Collusive oligopolists cooperate to minimise competition and maximise joint profit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cartel is formal collusion that fixes price and output for all its members.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Price leadership is informal collusion where one dominant firm initiates price changes and the rest follow.
**Answer: True**
