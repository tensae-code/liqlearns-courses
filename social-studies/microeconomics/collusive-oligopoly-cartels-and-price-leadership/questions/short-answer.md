# Collusive oligopoly — cartels and price leadership — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Why rivals cooperate" in your own words.
**Answer:** Collusive oligopolists cooperate to minimise competition and maximise joint profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The cartel — formal collusion" in your own words.
**Answer:** A cartel is formal collusion that fixes price and output for all its members.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Price leadership — informal collusion" in your own words.
**Answer:** Price leadership is informal collusion where one dominant firm initiates price changes and the rest follow.
