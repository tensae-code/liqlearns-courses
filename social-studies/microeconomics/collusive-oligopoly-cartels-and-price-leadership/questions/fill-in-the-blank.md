# Collusive oligopoly — cartels and price leadership — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Collusion aims to minimise competition and maximise the firms' _____ .
**Answer: joint profit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Formal collusion fixing price and output for all members is a _____.
**Answer: cartel**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Informal collusion where followers copy the dominant firm's price moves is called _____.
**Answer: price leadership**
