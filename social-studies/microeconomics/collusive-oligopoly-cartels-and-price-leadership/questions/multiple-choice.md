# Collusive oligopoly — cartels and price leadership — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Why rivals cooperate"?
a) cooperation to minimise competition and maximise joint profit at a monopoly-like price  b) a cartel fixes price and output for all members, sometimes via a common agency  c) one dominant firm initiates price changes and the rest follow — no signed agreement
**Answer: a) cooperation to minimise competition and maximise joint profit at a monopoly-like price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The cartel — formal collusion"?
a) cooperation to minimise competition and maximise joint profit at a monopoly-like price  b) one dominant firm initiates price changes and the rest follow — no signed agreement  c) a cartel fixes price and output for all members, sometimes via a common agency
**Answer: c) a cartel fixes price and output for all members, sometimes via a common agency**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Price leadership — informal collusion"?
a) a cartel fixes price and output for all members, sometimes via a common agency  b) one dominant firm initiates price changes and the rest follow — no signed agreement  c) cooperation to minimise competition and maximise joint profit at a monopoly-like price
**Answer: b) one dominant firm initiates price changes and the rest follow — no signed agreement**
