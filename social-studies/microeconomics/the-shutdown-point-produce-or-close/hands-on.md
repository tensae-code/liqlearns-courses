# Hands-on — The shutdown point — produce or close?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Play the owner for three called prices: above break-even (produce, profit), between the thresholds (produce, loss but open), below AVC (close the door). Mime each decision.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one diagram with the break-even and shutdown prices marked as two thresholds on the price axis — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
