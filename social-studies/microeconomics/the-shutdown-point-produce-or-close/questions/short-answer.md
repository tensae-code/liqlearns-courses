# The shutdown point — produce or close? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Why a losing firm stays open" in your own words.
**Answer:** A closed firm still loses its fixed costs, because they are already committed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The rule — cover AVC and keep going" in your own words.
**Answer:** If price covers average variable cost, the firm keeps producing in the short run despite losses.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The shutdown point" in your own words.
**Answer:** The shutdown point is where the marginal cost curve crosses the average variable cost curve.
