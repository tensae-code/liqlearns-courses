# The shutdown point — produce or close? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Why a losing firm stays open"?
a) closing saves variable costs only; committed fixed costs are lost either way  b) price at or above AVC: produce, because revenue covers variable cost and part of fixed cost  c) shutdown where MC crosses AVC; below it, close immediately
**Answer: a) closing saves variable costs only; committed fixed costs are lost either way**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The rule — cover AVC and keep going"?
a) closing saves variable costs only; committed fixed costs are lost either way  b) shutdown where MC crosses AVC; below it, close immediately  c) price at or above AVC: produce, because revenue covers variable cost and part of fixed cost
**Answer: c) price at or above AVC: produce, because revenue covers variable cost and part of fixed cost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The shutdown point"?
a) price at or above AVC: produce, because revenue covers variable cost and part of fixed cost  b) shutdown where MC crosses AVC; below it, close immediately  c) closing saves variable costs only; committed fixed costs are lost either way
**Answer: b) shutdown where MC crosses AVC; below it, close immediately**
