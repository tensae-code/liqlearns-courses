# The shutdown point — produce or close? — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shutting down does not save the _____ — the closed firm still loses them.
**Answer: fixed costs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A losing firm keeps producing while the price covers its average _____ cost.
**Answer: variable**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The point where MC crosses AVC — the minimum of AVC — is the _____ point.
**Answer: shutdown**
