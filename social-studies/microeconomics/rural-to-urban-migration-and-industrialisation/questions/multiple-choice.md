# Rural-to-urban migration and industrialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About migraciones campo–ciudad — the lesson teaches that —
a) Mechanisation pushed peasants off the land into.
b) Fast-growing industrial cities in search of work.
c) And the world split into industrialised countries and suppliers of raw materials.
**Answer: a) Mechanisation pushed peasants off the land into.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About migraciones campo–ciudad — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) And the world split into industrialised countries and suppliers of raw materials.
b) Fast-growing industrial cities in search of work.
c) Mechanisation pushed peasants off the land into.
**Answer: b) Fast-growing industrial cities in search of work.**
