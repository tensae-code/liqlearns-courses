# Hands-on — Fixed, variable and total cost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two money piles on the table: a rent pile that never changes as a partner calls outputs 0 to 7, and a materials pile that grows each time. Add the piles aloud at each output to get total cost.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a rent bill that stays fixed beside a flour bill that grows with output — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
