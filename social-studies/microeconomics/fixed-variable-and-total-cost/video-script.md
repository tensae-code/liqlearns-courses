# Video script — Fixed, variable and total cost

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Costs are derived from production: the physical input story of the unit's first half is now retold in money. In the short run the split follows the inputs — costs of the fixed inputs cannot change with output, costs of the variable input do. That one distinction builds the whole cost family.

Total fixed cost (TFC) is the cost of the fixed inputs — rent is the picture — and in the book's birr table it stays at Birr 100 at every output, including zero. Total variable cost (TVC) is the cost of the variable input: zero when nothing is made, climbing as output climbs.

Total cost is the sum: TC = TFC + TVC. In the table TC climbs with output — because only TVC climbs — reaching Birr 230 at 7 units, of which 100 is the unchanging fixed part and 130 the variable part. Every per-unit cost in the next lesson is one of these three totals divided, or differenced, per unit.
