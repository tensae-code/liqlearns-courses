# Fixed, variable and total cost — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Costs are production in birr" in your own words.
**Answer:** Short-run costs are production retold in money, split by fixed and variable inputs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Fixed and variable totals" in your own words.
**Answer:** Total fixed cost stays at Birr 100 at every output in the book's table, including zero output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Total cost — the sum" in your own words.
**Answer:** Total cost equals total fixed cost plus total variable cost.
