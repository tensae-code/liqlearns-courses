# Fixed, variable and total cost — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Costs are production in birr"?
a) short-run costs split by the fixed and variable inputs behind them  b) TFC steady at Birr 100 at every output; TVC climbing from zero  c) TC = TFC + TVC, reaching Birr 230 at 7 units
**Answer: a) short-run costs split by the fixed and variable inputs behind them**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Fixed and variable totals"?
a) short-run costs split by the fixed and variable inputs behind them  b) TC = TFC + TVC, reaching Birr 230 at 7 units  c) TFC steady at Birr 100 at every output; TVC climbing from zero
**Answer: c) TFC steady at Birr 100 at every output; TVC climbing from zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Total cost — the sum"?
a) TFC steady at Birr 100 at every output; TVC climbing from zero  b) TC = TFC + TVC, reaching Birr 230 at 7 units  c) short-run costs split by the fixed and variable inputs behind them
**Answer: b) TC = TFC + TVC, reaching Birr 230 at 7 units**
