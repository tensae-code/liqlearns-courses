# Fixed, variable and total cost — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Short-run costs are production retold in money, split by fixed and variable inputs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Total fixed cost stays at Birr 100 at every output in the book's table, including zero output.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Total cost equals total fixed cost plus total variable cost.
**Answer: True**
