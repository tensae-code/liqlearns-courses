# Video script — Entrepreneurship and its benefits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An entrepreneur is a person who starts a business. A business sells goods — things like maize and bread — or services — jobs done for other people, like sewing and repairing.

If the business earns more than it spent, that is profit. If it spent more than it earned, that is a loss. Capital is what the business starts with, savings are money put aside, and risk is the chance that things do not go as planned.

Entrepreneurs help everyone around them: they earn money for their families, they create jobs for other people, and they provide the goods and services the community needs.
