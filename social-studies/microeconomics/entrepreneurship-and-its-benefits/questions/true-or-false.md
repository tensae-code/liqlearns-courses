# Entrepreneurship and its benefits — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market sellers, maize farmers and shopkeepers are entrepreneurs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A loss means the business earned more than it spent.
**Answer: False** — a loss means the business spent more than it earned; earning more than was spent is a profit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Entrepreneurs provide goods and services for families and communities.
**Answer: True**
