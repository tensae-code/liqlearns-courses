# Entrepreneurship and its benefits — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three entrepreneurs the module uses.
**Answer:** A market seller, a maize farmer and a shopkeeper.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between profit and loss?
**Answer:** Profit is the money left over when a business earns more than it spent; a loss is when the business spent more than it earned.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three ways entrepreneurs help families and communities.
**Answer:** They earn money for themselves and their families, they create jobs for other people, and they provide goods and services for families and communities.
