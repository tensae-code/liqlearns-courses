# Entrepreneurship and its benefits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An entrepreneur is —
a) a person who starts and runs a business  b) a person who only buys things  c) a person who never works
**Answer: a) a person who starts and runs a business**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Profit is —
a) money spent  b) the money left over when a business earns more than it spent  c) money put aside in a bank
**Answer: b) the money left over when a business earns more than it spent**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One way entrepreneurs help their community is —
a) by closing all the shops  b) by creating jobs for other people  c) by keeping goods away from families
**Answer: b) by creating jobs for other people**
