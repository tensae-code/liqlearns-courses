# Hands-on — Entrepreneurship and its benefits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Set up a small pretend stall with things at home — fruit, cups, books. Be the entrepreneur: name your goods, set a price in kwacha, and say what your capital was and what profit you hope to make.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a market scene from memory — a market seller, a maize farmer and a shopkeeper — and label what each one sells. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
