# Entrepreneurship and its benefits

## Do it first

Think of the market near your home. Who sells the maize? Who keeps the shop? Who sews the clothes? Before you read on, say what each of those people does to earn their money.

## Sub lesson 1 — What an entrepreneur is

In Zambia's Grade 4 teaching module, Topic 4.8 is Entrepreneurship, and Sub-topic 4.8.1 is the Benefits of Entrepreneurship. An **entrepreneur** is a person who starts and runs a **business** — work done to sell things or to do jobs for other people and earn money from it.

The module's entrepreneurs are the people the child already knows: **market sellers, maize farmers and shopkeepers**. The market seller sells vegetables at her stall. The maize farmer grows maize and sells the harvest. The shopkeeper keeps a shop and sells what families need. Each one is an entrepreneur.

## Sub lesson 2 — The key words of business

The module teaches the key words of business first, because the whole topic is told in them:

- **Goods** — the things a business sells, like maize, bread or clothes.
- **Services** — the jobs a business does for people, like sewing, carrying or repairing.
- **Profit** — the money left over when what a business earns is more than what it spent.
- **Loss** — the other way round: the business spent more than it earned.
- **Capital** — the money or things a business starts with and works with.
- **Savings** — money put aside and kept, not spent at once.
- **Risk** — the chance that things may not go as planned; an entrepreneur takes a risk, because profit is not promised.

## Sub lesson 3 — How entrepreneurs help families and communities

Entrepreneurs help in three ways the module names. First, they **earn money** for themselves and their families. Second, they **create jobs** — a growing shop or farm needs other people to work in it, and those people earn money too. Third, they **provide goods and services** for families and communities — the food at the market, the clothes from the tailor, the things in the shop.

That is why entrepreneurship matters: one person's business feeds their own family, gives work to others, and puts needed goods and services where people live.

## Say it to your companion

Explain entrepreneurship to your companion in your own words: what an entrepreneur is, the seven key words, and the three ways entrepreneurs help families and communities. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
