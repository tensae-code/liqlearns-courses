# Video script — Price as an economic category — and pricing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan's Grade 10 books give price a lesson of its own, says the narrator: what a price is, and its functions. A price is built — production cost, profit, excise and VAT, into wholesale and retail prices — and the first rule is that price must cover production costs. Over the structure sit pricing policy and strategies, and behind them the state's own pricing policy.
