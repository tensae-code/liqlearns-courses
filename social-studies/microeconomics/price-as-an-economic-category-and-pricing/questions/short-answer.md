# Price as an economic category — and pricing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the pricing lesson teach first?
**Answer:** What a price is — the essence and functions of price as an economic category.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a price structured, as Aganina's book builds it?
**Answer:** From production cost, profit, excise and VAT, built into wholesale and retail prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What sits on top of the price structure?
**Answer:** Pricing policy and pricing strategies — the rules and plans for setting prices — and, on the public side, state pricing policy.
