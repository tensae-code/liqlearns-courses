# Price as an economic category — and pricing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price is treated in the books as an economic category with functions of its own.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A price may safely be set below production cost, as a rule of pricing.
**Answer: False — Duisenkhanov's lesson teaches that price must cover production costs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wholesale and retail prices are the same number at the same stage.
**Answer: False — they are different stages of the same build: cost, profit, excise and VAT build toward wholesale and then retail prices.**
