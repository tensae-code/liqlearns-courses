# Price as an economic category — and pricing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The pricing lesson opens with —
a) export rules  b) what a price is, and its functions  c) advertising
**Answer: b) what a price is, and its functions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A price is built from —
a) cost alone  b) production cost, profit, excise and VAT  c) the seller's wishes
**Answer: b) production cost, profit, excise and VAT**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pricing policy and strategies are —
a) the rules and plans by which a seller sets prices  b) a kind of tax  c) banned by the state
**Answer: a) the rules and plans by which a seller sets prices**
