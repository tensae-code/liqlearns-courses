# Hands-on — Price as an economic category — and pricing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a price
On paper, build a price the book's way: start with production cost, add profit, add excise and VAT, and write the wholesale price; then write the retail price beside it. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix anything missing.

## Say it with things at home
Stack four stones in a tower — cost at the bottom, then profit, excise and VAT — and name each stone as you stack it. The tower is the price; take the bottom stone away and say what happens.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
