# Video script — Final, intermediate and primary goods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **final good** is used directly by the person who has it.

A **secondary (intermediate) good** is sent into making a final product.

A **primary good** is used to make the intermediate goods.
