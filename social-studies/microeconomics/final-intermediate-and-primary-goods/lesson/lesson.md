# Final, intermediate and primary goods

## Do it first

Think of the school uniform you wear. Before it was clothes, it was cloth; before it was cloth, it was yarn. Which of the three is used directly, which is sent into making the final product, and which is used to make that one? Say your answer before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Final goods — used directly

A **final good** is used directly by the person who has it. In the book's chain, the clothes are the final good: they are worn, not sent on to make something else.

## Sub lesson 2 — Intermediate goods — sent into making final products

A **secondary (intermediate) good** is sent into making a final product. In the book's chain, the cloth is the intermediate good: it is not worn as it is — it is cut and sewn into clothes, the final product.

## Sub lesson 3 — Primary goods — used to make the intermediate goods

A **primary good** is used to make the intermediate goods. In the book's chain, the yarn is the primary good: the yarn is woven into cloth, and the cloth is then made into clothes.
