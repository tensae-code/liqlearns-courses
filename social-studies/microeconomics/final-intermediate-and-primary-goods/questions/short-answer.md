# Final, intermediate and primary goods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trace the book's chain from yarn to clothes, naming each kind of good.
**Answer:** Yarn is the primary good; it is woven into cloth, the secondary (intermediate) good; the cloth is made into clothes, the final good, used directly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a final good?
**Answer:** A good that is used directly, not sent on to make something else.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a primary good?
**Answer:** A good used to make the intermediate goods — in the book's chain, the yarn used to make the cloth.
