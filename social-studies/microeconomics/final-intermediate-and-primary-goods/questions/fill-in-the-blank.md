# Final, intermediate and primary goods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods used directly are _____ goods.
**Answer: final**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Goods sent into making final products are _____ (intermediate) goods.
**Answer: secondary**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Goods used to make the intermediate goods are _____ goods.
**Answer: primary**
