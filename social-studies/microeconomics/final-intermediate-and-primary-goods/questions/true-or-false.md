# Final, intermediate and primary goods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A final good is used directly.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The yarn is woven into cloth, so in this chain the yarn is the final good.
**Answer: False** — the yarn is the primary good; the clothes are the final good.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Intermediate goods are also called secondary goods in the book.
**Answer: True**
