# Final, intermediate and primary goods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's chain, the clothes are —
a) a primary good  b) an intermediate good  c) a final good
**Answer: c) a final good**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cloth in the book's chain is a secondary (intermediate) good because —
a) it is worn directly  b) it is sent into making the final product  c) it is used to make the yarn
**Answer: b) it is sent into making the final product**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's chain, the yarn is —
a) a primary good  b) a final good  c) a service
**Answer: a) a primary good**
