# Division of labour and specialisation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about la revolución neolítica?
**Answer:** The farm surplus frees part of the population from working the land to specialise in pottery.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about la revolución neolítica — more?
**Answer:** Weaving, baskets and priesthood.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about la revolución neolítica — more?
**Answer:** The beginning of the division of labour.
