# Division of labour and specialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About la revolución neolítica — the lesson teaches that —
a) The farm surplus frees part of the population from working the land to specialise in pottery.
b) Weaving, baskets and priesthood.
c) The beginning of the division of labour.
**Answer: a) The farm surplus frees part of the population from working the land to specialise in pottery.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About la revolución neolítica — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) The beginning of the division of labour.
b) Weaving, baskets and priesthood.
c) The farm surplus frees part of the population from working the land to specialise in pottery.
**Answer: b) Weaving, baskets and priesthood.**
