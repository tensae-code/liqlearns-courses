# Work produces goods and services to satisfy needs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Work is the activity by which people produce or _____ objects (goods) or provide a service
**Answer: transform**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To satisfy human needs and improve _____ including housework;
**Answer: wellbeing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Long ago one person did everything to survive, today work is _____
**Answer: specialised**
