# Enterprises and market concentration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An enterprise is a business organised to produce and sell.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Concentration means business drifts into more, smaller hands only.
**Answer: False — Concentration is fewer, larger hands — not more, smaller ones.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Concentration costs buyers the protection of competing sellers.
**Answer: True**
