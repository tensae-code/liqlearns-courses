# Enterprises and market concentration — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Enterprises differ in size, in what they do, and in how they are organised and _____.
**Answer: owned**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Big enterprises grow, buy or squeeze out _____ ones.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The few large firms gain power over _____ and over smaller firms and workers.
**Answer: prices**
