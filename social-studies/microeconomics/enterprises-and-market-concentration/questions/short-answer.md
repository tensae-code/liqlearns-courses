# Enterprises and market concentration — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why map the variety of enterprises first?
**Answer: Because concentration can only be seen against the variety it narrows.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How can pupils use this lesson outside the book?
**Answer: To spot concentration in a real market.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is structure not a dry map, in the lesson's phrase?
**Answer: Because how enterprises are organised decides how a market treats everyone in it.**
