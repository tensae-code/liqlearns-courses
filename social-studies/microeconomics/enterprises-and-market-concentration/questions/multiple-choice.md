# Enterprises and market concentration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An enterprise is —
a) a government ministry in every case  b) a business organised to produce and sell  c) a market with no sellers
**Answer: b) a business organised to produce and sell**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Concentration means business drifts into —
a) fewer, larger hands  b) no hands at all — business vanishes  c) more, smaller hands only
**Answer: a) fewer, larger hands**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When a market concentrates, buyers lose —
a) their need for goods  b) nothing at all  c) the protection of many competing sellers
**Answer: c) the protection of many competing sellers**
