# Monopolistic competition — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monopolistic competition has many sellers of differentiated products and free entry and exit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In monopolistic competition, firms compete through branding and advertising as much as price.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Monopolistic competition's demand curve is flatter than a monopolist's because close substitutes exist.
**Answer: True**
