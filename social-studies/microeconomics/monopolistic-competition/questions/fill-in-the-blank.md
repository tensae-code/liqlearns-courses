# Monopolistic competition — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pens, biscuits, soap and restaurants compete as _____ products — each brand its own.
**Answer: differentiated**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In monopolistic competition, firms compete through branding and _____ as much as price.
**Answer: advertising**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Because close substitutes exist, this firm's demand curve is _____ than a monopolist's.
**Answer: flatter**
