# Monopolistic competition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Many sellers, different products" in your own words.
**Answer:** Monopolistic competition has many sellers of differentiated products and free entry and exit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Branding and advertising as weapons" in your own words.
**Answer:** In monopolistic competition, firms compete through branding and advertising as much as price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Its demand curve — sloping but flatter" in your own words.
**Answer:** Monopolistic competition's demand curve is flatter than a monopolist's because close substitutes exist.
