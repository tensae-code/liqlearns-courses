# Monopolistic competition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Many sellers, different products"?
a) many sellers of differentiated products, with free entry and exit  b) competition by branding and advertising as much as by price  c) a downward-sloping but flatter demand curve, MR below AR
**Answer: a) many sellers of differentiated products, with free entry and exit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Branding and advertising as weapons"?
a) many sellers of differentiated products, with free entry and exit  b) a downward-sloping but flatter demand curve, MR below AR  c) competition by branding and advertising as much as by price
**Answer: c) competition by branding and advertising as much as by price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Its demand curve — sloping but flatter"?
a) competition by branding and advertising as much as by price  b) a downward-sloping but flatter demand curve, MR below AR  c) many sellers of differentiated products, with free entry and exit
**Answer: b) a downward-sloping but flatter demand curve, MR below AR**
