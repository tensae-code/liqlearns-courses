# Special interest politics — pork barrel and logrolling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Special interest benefits are typically —
a) concentrated on the few, with costs spread over everyone  b) spread evenly with no cost  c) paid only by the group
**Answer: a) concentrated on the few, with costs spread over everyone**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Logrolling is —
a) trading votes between legislators  b) rolling logs to a mill  c) a kind of tariff
**Answer: a) trading votes between legislators**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The case in this lesson is —
a) the 2009 US tire tariff  b) the Hoover Dam  c) the 1825 French indemnity
**Answer: a) the 2009 US tire tariff**
