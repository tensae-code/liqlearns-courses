# Special interest politics — pork barrel and logrolling — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lobbyists have no influence on campaigns.
**Answer: False — special interest groups and lobbyists influence campaigns and elections.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pork-barrel spending serves the whole country equally by design.
**Answer: False — pork-barrel spending directs money to a legislator's own district.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The tire tariff's costs fell only on tire makers.
**Answer: False — its costs spread over all tire buyers.**
