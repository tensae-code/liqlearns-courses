# Special interest politics — pork barrel and logrolling — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do special interests organise so effectively?
**Answer:** Their benefits are concentrated on the few, while the costs spread thinly over everyone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish pork barrel from logrolling.
**Answer:** Pork barrel: spending directed to one's own district. Logrolling: trading votes to pass each other's projects.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the tire tariff a special-interest case?
**Answer:** A concentrated industry gain was sought through politics, with costs spread over all buyers.
