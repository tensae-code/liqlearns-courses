## Sub lesson 1 — Special interests and lobbyists

**Special interest groups and lobbyists** press campaigns and elections for policies whose **benefits concentrate on the few** while **costs spread over everyone**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pork barrel and logrolling

**Pork-barrel spending** directs public money to a legislator's **own district**; **logrolling** is **trading votes** — 'support my project and I will support yours'.

## Sub lesson 3 — The 2009 US tire tariff as case

The book's case is the **2009 US tire tariff** — a concentrated industry gain sought through politics, with costs spread over all tire buyers.
