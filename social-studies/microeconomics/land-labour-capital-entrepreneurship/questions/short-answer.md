# Land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship — and what each is paid — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Land and labour" in your own words.
**Answer:** Land is all natural resources and its reward is rent; labour is human effort and its reward is wages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Capital and entrepreneurship" in your own words.
**Answer:** Capital is manufactured inputs and its reward is interest; entrepreneurship organises the other factors and its reward is profit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Four factors, four payments" in your own words.
**Answer:** The four payments are rent for land, wages for labour, interest for capital and profit for entrepreneurship.
