# Land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship — and what each is paid — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land's reward is _____ and labour's reward is wages.
**Answer: rent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital's reward is _____ and entrepreneurship's reward is profit.
**Answer: interest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The chain runs land–rent, labour–wages, capital–interest, entrepreneurship–_____.
**Answer: profit**
