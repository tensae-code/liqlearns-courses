# Land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship — and what each is paid — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land is all natural resources and its reward is rent; labour is human effort and its reward is wages.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Land is all natural resources and its reward is rent; labour is human effort and its reward is wages.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Land and labour); sub lesson 2 says: Capital is manufactured inputs and its reward is interest; entrepreneurship organises the other factors and its reward is profit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The four payments are rent for land, wages for labour, interest for capital and profit for entrepreneurship.
**Answer: True**
