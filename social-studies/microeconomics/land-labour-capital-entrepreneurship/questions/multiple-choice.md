# Land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship — and what each is paid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Land and labour"?
a) land — all natural resources, paid rent; labour — human effort, paid wages  b) capital — manufactured inputs, paid interest; entrepreneurship — organising talent, paid profit  c) land–rent, labour–wages, capital–interest, entrepreneurship–profit
**Answer: a) land — all natural resources, paid rent; labour — human effort, paid wages**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Capital and entrepreneurship"?
a) land — all natural resources, paid rent; labour — human effort, paid wages  b) land–rent, labour–wages, capital–interest, entrepreneurship–profit  c) capital — manufactured inputs, paid interest; entrepreneurship — organising talent, paid profit
**Answer: c) capital — manufactured inputs, paid interest; entrepreneurship — organising talent, paid profit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Four factors, four payments"?
a) capital — manufactured inputs, paid interest; entrepreneurship — organising talent, paid profit  b) land–rent, labour–wages, capital–interest, entrepreneurship–profit  c) land — all natural resources, paid rent; labour — human effort, paid wages
**Answer: b) land–rent, labour–wages, capital–interest, entrepreneurship–profit**
