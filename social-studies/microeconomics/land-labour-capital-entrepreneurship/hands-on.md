# Hands-on — Land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship — and what each is paid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Four learners hold cards — land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship. A fifth deals payment cards — rent, wages, interest, profit. Deal each payment to its factor and defend the match.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a four-row chart, each factor beside its payment — rent, wage, interest, profit — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
