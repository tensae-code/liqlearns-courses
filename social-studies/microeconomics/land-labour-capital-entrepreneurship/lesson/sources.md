Saudi Arabia's own way: a whole Economics unit in Grade 4, anchored in the Kingdom's own production — dates, milk and oil — and ending with a buy-Saudi-made activity at the supermarket. This placement keeps that way: the resources production depends on are met through the Kingdom's own oil, water and soil, and the people who build, heal and teach.
