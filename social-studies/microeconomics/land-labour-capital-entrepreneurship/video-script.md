# Video script — Land, labour, capital, entrepreneurship — and what each is paid

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Land is all natural resources, and its reward is rent. Labour is physical and mental human effort, and its reward is wages. The first factor is what nature gives; the second is what people do.

Capital is manufactured inputs — machinery, equipment, transport facilities — and its reward is interest. Entrepreneurship is the talent that organises the other factors and bears the risk of loss, and its reward is profit.

The set is worth memorising as one chain: land–rent, labour–wages, capital–interest, entrepreneurship–profit. Every income in the circular flow you will meet later is one of these four payments arriving at a household.
