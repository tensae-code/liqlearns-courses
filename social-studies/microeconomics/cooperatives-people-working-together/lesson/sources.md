**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Greece).**

Greece's own way: economics enters primary school as the town hall's money — where a municipality's revenue comes from and what it is spent on, told through two photos of the same Cretan harbour 42 years apart (greece-placements.md); the one Economics chapter teaches public finance at the scale a child can see — the municipality: where its money comes from and what it is spent on, with the child's own household electricity bill as the data source (greece-placements-b.md). This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit D Ch.4 The economic organisation of municipalities — teaches: cooperatives as groups of people who work together to advance common interests, e.g. an agricultural cooperative producing and selling its members' produce together. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
