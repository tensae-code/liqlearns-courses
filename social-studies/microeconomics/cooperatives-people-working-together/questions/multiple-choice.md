# Cooperatives — people working together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cooperative is —
a) a state office
b) people working together for their common interests
c) a single owner's shop
**Answer: b) people working together for their common interests**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's example cooperative is —
a) a shipping cooperative
b) an agricultural cooperative selling members' produce together
c) a bank
**Answer: b) an agricultural cooperative selling members' produce together**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The companion activity weighs a weaving cooperative's effects, including —
a) fewer jobs
b) more jobs
c) higher taxes
**Answer: b) more jobs**
