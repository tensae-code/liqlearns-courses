# Cooperatives — people working together — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a cooperative the members own the undertaking together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pooling the harvest lets small farms reach buyers none could command alone.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 6 book teaches cooperatives inside the municipality's economic organisation.
**Answer: True**
