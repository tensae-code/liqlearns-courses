# Cooperatives — people working together — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a cooperative.
**Answer:** A group of people who work together, owning and deciding together, to advance their common interests.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does an agricultural cooperative help small farmers?
**Answer:** It pools their produce so together they reach buyers, prices and transport beyond any single farm.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where does the Grade 6 book place cooperatives?
**Answer:** Inside the economic organisation of municipalities, as a way a community organises work together.
