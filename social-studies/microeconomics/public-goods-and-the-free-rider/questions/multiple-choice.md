# Public goods and the free rider — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Non-rival and non-excludable"?
a) A public good is non-rival and non-excludable  b) Everyone waiting for someone else to pay is the free-rider problem, and the market under-provides public goods  c) Ethiopia funds public goods through community collections and expressway tolls
**Answer: a) A public good is non-rival and non-excludable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The free-rider problem"?
a) Everyone waiting for someone else to pay is the free-rider problem, and the market under-provides public goods  b) Ethiopia funds public goods through community collections and expressway tolls  c) A public good is non-rival and non-excludable
**Answer: a) Everyone waiting for someone else to pay is the free-rider problem, and the market under-provides public goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's own mechanisms"?
a) Ethiopia funds public goods through community collections and expressway tolls  b) A public good is non-rival and non-excludable  c) Everyone waiting for someone else to pay is the free-rider problem, and the market under-provides public goods
**Answer: a) Ethiopia funds public goods through community collections and expressway tolls**
