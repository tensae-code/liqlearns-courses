# Public goods and the free rider — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A public good is non-rival and non-excludable.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Everyone waiting for someone else to pay is the free-rider problem, and the market under-provides public goods.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia funds public goods through community collections and expressway tolls.
**Answer: True**
