# Public goods and the free rider — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good that non-payers cannot be kept out of is non-____.
**Answer: excludable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Waiting for others to pay for a good you will use anyway is ____ riding.
**Answer: free**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tolls on the Addis Ababa–Adama ____ turn a road into a priced good.
**Answer: expressway**
