# Public goods and the free rider — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Non-rival and non-excludable" in your own words.
**Answer:** A public good is non-rival and non-excludable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The free-rider problem" in your own words.
**Answer:** Everyone waiting for someone else to pay is the free-rider problem, and the market under-provides public goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Ethiopia's own mechanisms" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopia funds public goods through community collections and expressway tolls.
