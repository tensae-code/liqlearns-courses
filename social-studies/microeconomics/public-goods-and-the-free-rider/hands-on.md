# Hands-on — Public goods and the free rider

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the collection
Stage a streetlight collection for a class fund with voluntary contributions only. Count the free riders, then rerun it as a small compulsory levy and compare what gets built.

## Rival or not
Sort six goods (a seat on a bus, a lighthouse beam, a sandwich, national defence, a toll road, a radio broadcast) into rival/non-rival and excludable/non-excludable, and pick out the true public goods.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
