# Video script — Public goods and the free rider

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A public good is non-rival — one person's use leaves as much for others — and non-excludable — non-payers cannot be kept out. A streetlight shines for the payer and the non-payer in exactly the same pool of light.

Because no one can be excluded, everyone waits for someone else to pay — the free-rider problem — and the market under-provides the good, or provides none. The good is valued; it is the paying that collapses.

The book's provision mechanisms are Ethiopian: community collections through churches and mosques raise the money socially, and tolls on the Addis Ababa–Adama and Addis Ababa–Hawassa expressways turn a near-public good — a road — into a priced one that only payers use.
