## Sub lesson 1 — Socialism as an economic-political system based on socialising

Socialism as an economic-political system based on socialising the systems of production and partial or total state control of economic sectors, aiming at a classless society;

## Sub lesson 2 — Against it capitalism public control of the private

Against it, capitalism — public control of the private sector should be minimal, competition makes economic activity regulate itself

## Sub lesson 3 — And government is needed only for national defence

And government is needed only for national defence, enforcing private property and guaranteeing contracts
