Capitalism versus socialism a classroom debate — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Socialism as an economic-political system based on socialising the systems of production and partial or total state control of economic sectors, aiming at a classless society; Against it, capitalism — public control of the private sector should be minimal, competition makes economic activity regulate itself And government is needed only for national defence, enforcing private property and guaranteeing contracts
