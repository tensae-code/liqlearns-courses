# Capitalism versus socialism a classroom debate — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Socialism as an _____ system based on socialising the systems of production and partial or total state control of economic sectors, aiming at a classless society;
**Answer: economic-political**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Against it, capitalism — public control of the private sector should be minimal, _____ makes economic activity regulate itself
**Answer: competition**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** And government is needed only for national defence, enforcing private property and _____ contracts
**Answer: guaranteeing**
