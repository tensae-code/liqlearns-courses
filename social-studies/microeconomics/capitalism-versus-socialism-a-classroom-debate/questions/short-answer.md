# Capitalism versus socialism a classroom debate — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about socialism as an economic-political system based on socialising?
**Answer:** Socialism as an economic-political system based on socialising the systems of production and partial or total state control of economic sectors, aiming at a classless society;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about against it capitalism public control of the private?
**Answer:** Against it, capitalism — public control of the private sector should be minimal, competition makes economic activity regulate itself

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about and government is needed only for national defence?
**Answer:** And government is needed only for national defence, enforcing private property and guaranteeing contracts
