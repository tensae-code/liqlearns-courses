# Hands-on — Elasticity of supply

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
One learner is a coffee storekeeper with sacks drawn on cards. Call price rises of 5%, 10% and 20%; the storekeeper releases sacks from stock, then explains why the response ends when the store empties.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — sacks of coffee held in a store while the price is watched — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
