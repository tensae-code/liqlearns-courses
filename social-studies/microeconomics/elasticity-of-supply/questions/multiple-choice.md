# Elasticity of supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The measure — always positive"?
a) point and arc measures of quantity supplied responding to price — always positive  b) stored coffee can be released at once when price rises  c) expectations, production time, factor substitution and number of sellers
**Answer: a) point and arc measures of quantity supplied responding to price — always positive**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Coffee in stock — the quick response"?
a) point and arc measures of quantity supplied responding to price — always positive  b) expectations, production time, factor substitution and number of sellers  c) stored coffee can be released at once when price rises
**Answer: c) stored coffee can be released at once when price rises**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Its determinants"?
a) stored coffee can be released at once when price rises  b) expectations, production time, factor substitution and number of sellers  c) point and arc measures of quantity supplied responding to price — always positive
**Answer: b) expectations, production time, factor substitution and number of sellers**
