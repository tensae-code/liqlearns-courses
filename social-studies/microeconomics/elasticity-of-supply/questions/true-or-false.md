# Elasticity of supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elasticity of supply is always positive, because quantity supplied rises with price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee held in stock lets a seller respond to a price rise immediately.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A longer production response time makes supply elasticity larger.
**Answer: True**
