# Elasticity of supply — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elasticity of supply is always _____ — quantity supplied and price move together.
**Answer: positive**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee held in _____ lets the seller respond to a price rise at once.
**Answer: stock**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The master determinant of supply elasticity is _____ — longer to respond means stretchier supply.
**Answer: time**
