# Elasticity of supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The measure — always positive" in your own words.
**Answer:** Elasticity of supply is always positive, because quantity supplied rises with price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Coffee in stock — the quick response" in your own words.
**Answer:** Coffee held in stock lets a seller respond to a price rise immediately.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Its determinants" in your own words.
**Answer:** A longer production response time makes supply elasticity larger.
