# Setting the price of a product

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Nicaragua book opens it — Setting the price of a product. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Price — the money value of a good or service

Price is the money value of a good or service — the number in córdobas a buyer pays for it, as the Guía del Emprendedor defines it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Setting a price by watching demand

A small business sets its price by analysing how demand behaves when prices change — what buyers do when the price rises or falls.

## Sub lesson 3 — Setting a price by objective and competition

The price is also set by its objective and by the competition — what the business aims at, and what competitors charge for the same thing.
