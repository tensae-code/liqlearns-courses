# Setting the price of a product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about price — the money value of a good or service?
**Answer:** Price is the money value of a good or service — the number in córdobas a buyer pays for it, as the Guía del Emprendedor defines it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about setting a price by watching demand?
**Answer:** A small business sets its price by analysing how demand behaves when prices change — what buyers do when the price rises or falls.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about setting a price by objective and competition?
**Answer:** The price is also set by its objective and by the competition — what the business aims at, and what competitors charge for the same thing.
