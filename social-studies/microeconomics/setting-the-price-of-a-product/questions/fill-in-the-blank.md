# Setting the price of a product — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price is _____ of a good or service — the number in córdobas a buyer pays for it, as the Guía del Emprendedor defines it.
**Answer: the money value**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A small business sets its price by analysing _____ — what buyers do when the price rises or falls.
**Answer: how demand behaves when prices change**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The price is also set _____ — what the business aims at, and what competitors charge for the same thing.
**Answer: by its objective and by the competition**
