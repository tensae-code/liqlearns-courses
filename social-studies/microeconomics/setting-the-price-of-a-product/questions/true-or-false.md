# Setting the price of a product — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price is the money value of a good or service — the number in córdobas a buyer pays for it, as the Guía del Emprendedor defines it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A small business sets its price by analysing how demand behaves when prices change — what buyers do when the price rises or falls.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The price is also set something else entirely — what the business aims at, and what competitors charge for the same thing.
**Answer: False** — The price is also set by its objective and by the competition — what the business aims at, and what competitors charge for the same thing.
