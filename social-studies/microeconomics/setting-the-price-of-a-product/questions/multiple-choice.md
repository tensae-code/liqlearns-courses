# Setting the price of a product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, “Price — the money value of a good or service” turns on —
a) the money value  b) how demand behaves when prices change  c) by its objective and by the competition
**Answer: a) the money value**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, “Setting a price by watching demand” turns on —
a) how demand behaves when prices change  b) the money value  c) by its objective and by the competition
**Answer: a) how demand behaves when prices change**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, “Setting a price by objective and competition” turns on —
a) by its objective and by the competition  b) the money value  c) how demand behaves when prices change
**Answer: a) by its objective and by the competition**
