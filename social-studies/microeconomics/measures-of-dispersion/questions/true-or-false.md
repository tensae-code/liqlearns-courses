# Measures of dispersion — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If two sets of data share an average, they must be spread out the same way.
**Answer: False** — Two sets of data can share the same average yet be spread out very differently, so the spread itself must be measured.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The range is the sum of the highest and lowest values.
**Answer: False** — The range is the difference between the highest and the lowest value — the simplest measure of dispersion.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Dispersion measures only ever describe the average itself.
**Answer: False** — Further measures of dispersion describe how far values typically lie from the average, completing the summary of a data set.
