# Measures of dispersion — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the average not enough to describe data?
**Answer:** Two sets of data can share the same average yet be spread out very differently, so the spread itself must be measured.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the range?
**Answer:** The range is the difference between the highest and the lowest value — the simplest measure of dispersion.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do further measures of dispersion describe?
**Answer:** Further measures of dispersion describe how far values typically lie from the average, completing the summary of a data set.
