# Measures of dispersion

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Why averages are not enough

Two sets of data can share the same average yet be spread out very differently, so the spread itself must be measured.

## Sub lesson 2 — The range

The range is the difference between the highest and the lowest value — the simplest measure of dispersion.

## Sub lesson 3 — Other measures of dispersion

Further measures of dispersion describe how far values typically lie from the average, completing the summary of a data set.
