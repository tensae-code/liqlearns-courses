Measures of dispersion — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: two sets of data can share the same average yet be spread out very differently, so the spread itself must be measured.; the range is the difference between the highest and the lowest value — the simplest measure of dispersion.; further measures of dispersion describe how far values typically lie from the average, completing the summary of a data set..
