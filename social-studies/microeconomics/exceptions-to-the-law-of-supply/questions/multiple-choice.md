# Exceptions to the Law of Supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** According to this lesson, sub lesson 1 teaches —
a) when the law of supply does not hold
b) a subject this lesson does not teach
c) the opposite of what Bhutan's book teaches

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** According to this lesson, sub lesson 2 teaches —
a) cases where a higher price brings no more supply
b) a subject this lesson does not teach
c) the opposite of what Bhutan's book teaches
**Answer: a) cases where a higher price brings no more supply**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** According to this lesson, sub lesson 3 teaches —
a) cases where supply does not rise with price
b) a subject this lesson does not teach
c) the opposite of what Bhutan's book teaches
**Answer: a) cases where supply does not rise with price**
