# Hands-on — Short-run equilibrium — the total approach

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Draw TR as a straight string and TC as a curving string on one board. Slide a ruler vertically along the output axis and stop where the gap between the strings is visibly widest.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — TR and TC curves with the widest gap bracketed and its output marked — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
