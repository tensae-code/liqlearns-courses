# Short-run equilibrium — the total approach — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the short run at least one input is fixed, so only output can be chosen.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the total approach, equilibrium output is where the vertical gap of TR over TC is greatest.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The total approach finds the equilibrium output but does not directly show price or per-unit profit.
**Answer: True**
