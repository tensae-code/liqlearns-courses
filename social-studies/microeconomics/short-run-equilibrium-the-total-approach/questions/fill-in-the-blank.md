# Short-run equilibrium — the total approach — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the short run at least one input is _____, so the firm chooses only its output.
**Answer: fixed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The total approach sets output where the vertical gap of TR over TC is _____.
**Answer: greatest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The total approach settles the equilibrium _____; the marginal approach adds price and per-unit profit.
**Answer: output**
