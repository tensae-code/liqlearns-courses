# Short-run equilibrium — the total approach — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The short run — at least one input fixed" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the short run at least one input is fixed, so only output can be chosen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The total approach — the widest gap" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the total approach, equilibrium output is where the vertical gap of TR over TC is greatest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "What the approach leaves open" in your own words.
**Answer:** The total approach finds the equilibrium output but does not directly show price or per-unit profit.
