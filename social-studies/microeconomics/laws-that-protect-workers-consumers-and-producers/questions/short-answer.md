# Laws that protect workers, consumers and producers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two protective laws from the table.
**Answer:** Any two of: workplace safety, quality standards, price control of essentials, anti-pollution, child-labour bans, union rights.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two limits the law sets on markets.
**Answer:** Any two of: price control of essentials, anti-pollution laws, child-labour bans.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the whole web of laws for?
**Answer:** To keep market relations from being exploitative.
