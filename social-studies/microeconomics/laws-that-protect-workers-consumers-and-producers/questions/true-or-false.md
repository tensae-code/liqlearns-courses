# Laws that protect workers, consumers and producers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** There are no laws on the quality of goods.
**Answer: False — quality standards are part of the web of laws.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Children may legally be put to factory labour.
**Answer: False — child-labour bans are part of the protective web.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Workers have no right to organise for fair wages.
**Answer: False — the right to form unions is in the table of laws.**
