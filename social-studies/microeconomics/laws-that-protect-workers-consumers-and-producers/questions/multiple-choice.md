# Laws that protect workers, consumers and producers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Quality standards protect —
a) buyers of goods  b) only sellers  c) no one
**Answer: a) buyers of goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Child labour is met by —
a) bans  b) bonuses  c) longer hours
**Answer: a) bans**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unions exist to —
a) demand fair wages  b) set prices of goods  c) ban all trade
**Answer: a) demand fair wages**
