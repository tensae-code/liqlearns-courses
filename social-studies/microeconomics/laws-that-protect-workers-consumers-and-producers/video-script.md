# Video script — Laws that protect workers, consumers and producers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Laws that protect workers, consumers and producers — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: the web of market laws — workplace safety, quality standards for goods, price control of essentials, anti-pollution, child-labour bans, and the right to form unions to demand fair wages — how law keeps market relations from being exploitative.
