# Laws that protect workers, consumers and producers

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — laws that protect workers, consumers and producers — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Safety and quality laws

Market laws protect people at work and at the shop: **workplace safety laws** and **quality standards for goods**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Price control, anti-pollution, child-labour bans

The web also holds **price control of essentials, anti-pollution laws and child-labour bans** — limits the market must trade inside.

## Sub lesson 3 — The right to form unions — law against exploitation

Workers hold the **right to form unions to demand fair wages**; together these laws **keep market relations from being exploitative**.
