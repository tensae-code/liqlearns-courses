# Hands-on — The command economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Plan a class picnic by central plan: one planner assigns every item and quantity. Then list what was easy (coordination) and what was lost (choice) — the system's trade in miniature.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a central planning office directing factories and farms from one plan board — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
