# The command economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "State ownership and the plan" in your own words.
**Answer:** In a command economy productive institutions are owned and controlled by the state under a central plan.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Its features" in your own words.
**Answer:** A command economy has collective ownership, central planning, a strong controlling government and no profit motive.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Its promise — equal shares" in your own words.
**Answer:** A command economy's income distribution is relatively equal.
