# The command economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a command economy productive institutions are owned and controlled by the state under a central plan.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a command economy productive institutions are owned and controlled by the state under a central plan.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (State ownership and the plan); sub lesson 2 says: A command economy has collective ownership, central planning, a strong controlling government and no profit motive.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A command economy's income distribution is relatively equal.
**Answer: True**
