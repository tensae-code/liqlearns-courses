# The command economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "State ownership and the plan"?
a) state ownership and control under a central plan  b) collective ownership, central planning, strong government, no profit motive  c) relatively equal income distribution
**Answer: a) state ownership and control under a central plan**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Its features"?
a) state ownership and control under a central plan  b) relatively equal income distribution  c) collective ownership, central planning, strong government, no profit motive
**Answer: c) collective ownership, central planning, strong government, no profit motive**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Its promise — equal shares"?
a) collective ownership, central planning, strong government, no profit motive  b) relatively equal income distribution  c) state ownership and control under a central plan
**Answer: b) relatively equal income distribution**
