# The command economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a command economy productive institutions are owned and controlled by the _____.
**Answer: state**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A command economy's ownership is _____, with no right to private property.
**Answer: collective**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A command economy offers relatively _____ income distribution.
**Answer: equal**
