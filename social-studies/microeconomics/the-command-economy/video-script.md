# Video script — The command economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In a command economy — presented also as a socialist economy — productive institutions are owned and controlled by the state and run under a central plan. The book traces it from Russia in 1917 and lists Ethiopia among the countries that adopted it.

Its features: collective ownership with no right to private property; central economic planning allocating resources by socio-economic goals; a strong, controlling role for government; and the profit motive absent. The guide's list adds shared ownership, specific social and economic goals and maximum social welfare (teacher guide — evidence).

Against capitalism's inequality, the command economy offers relatively equal income distribution: the plan shares output by its goals rather than by market success. Equality is the system's stated prize for surrendering private choice.
