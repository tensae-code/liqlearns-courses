# Surplus, deficit and balanced family budgets

## Sub lesson 1 — Saving and the surplus budget

**Saving = salary minus expenditure**. When the result is **positive**, the family budget is a **surplus budget** — money is left over.

## Sub lesson 2 — The deficit budget — spending more than the salary

When the result is **negative**, the budget is a **deficit**. The book's second worked family is the problem case: its **120,000 FRW spending exceeds its 100,000 FRW salary**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The balanced budget — the two sides equal

When salary and expenditure are **equal**, the budget is a **balanced budget** — neither surplus nor deficit.
