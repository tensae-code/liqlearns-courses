# Surplus, deficit and balanced family budgets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs to sub lesson 1 — Saving and the surplus budget?
a) Saving = salary minus expenditure  b) a thing from another lesson  c) none of these
**Answer: a) Saving = salary minus expenditure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs to sub lesson 2 — The deficit budget — spending more than the salary?
a) negative  b) a thing from another lesson  c) none of these
**Answer: a) negative**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these belongs to sub lesson 3 — The balanced budget — the two sides equal?
a) equal  b) a thing from another lesson  c) none of these
**Answer: a) equal**
