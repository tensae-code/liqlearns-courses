# Surplus, deficit and balanced family budgets — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about saving and the surplus budget?
**Answer:** Saving = salary minus expenditure. When the result is positive, the family budget is a surplus budget — money is left over.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about the deficit budget — spending more than the salary?
**Answer:** When the result is negative, the budget is a deficit. The book's second worked family is the problem case: its 120,000 FRW spending exceeds its 100,000 FRW salary.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about the balanced budget — the two sides equal?
**Answer:** When salary and expenditure are equal, the budget is a balanced budget — neither surplus nor deficit.
