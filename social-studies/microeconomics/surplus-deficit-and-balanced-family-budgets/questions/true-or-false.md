# Surplus, deficit and balanced family budgets — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving = salary minus expenditure.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When the result is negative, the budget is a deficit.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When salary and expenditure are equal, the budget is a balanced budget — neither surplus nor deficit.
**Answer: True**
