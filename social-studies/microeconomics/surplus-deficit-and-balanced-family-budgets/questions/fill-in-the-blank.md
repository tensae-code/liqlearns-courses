# Surplus, deficit and balanced family budgets — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is a key idea of sub lesson 1 (Saving and the surplus budget).
**Answer: Saving = salary minus expenditure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is a key idea of sub lesson 2 (The deficit budget — spending more than the salary).
**Answer: negative**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ is a key idea of sub lesson 3 (The balanced budget — the two sides equal).
**Answer: equal**
