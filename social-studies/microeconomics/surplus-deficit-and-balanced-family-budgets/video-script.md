# Video script — Surplus, deficit and balanced family budgets

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Surplus, deficit and balanced family budgets — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: Saving = salary minus expenditure. When the result is positive, the family budget is a surplus budget — money is left over. When the result is negative, the budget is a deficit. The book's second worked family is the problem case: its 120,000 FRW spending exceeds its 100,000 FRW salary. When salary and expenditure are equal, the budget is a balanced budget — neither surplus nor deficit.
