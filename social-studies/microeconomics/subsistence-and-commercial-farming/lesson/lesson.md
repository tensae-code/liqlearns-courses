# Subsistence and commercial farming

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Subsistence farming feeds the family

**Subsistence farming** grows food **for the family itself** — its kinds include **intensive subsistence, shifting cultivation and nomadic herding**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Commercial farming feeds the market

**Commercial farming** produces **for the market** — its kinds include **commercial grain farming, mixed farming and plantations**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The dividing question — who eats the produce?

The two are divided by one question: **who is the produce for — the family, or the market?**
