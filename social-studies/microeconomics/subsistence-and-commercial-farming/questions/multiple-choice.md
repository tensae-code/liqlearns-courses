# Subsistence and commercial farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsistence farming is —
a) farming for the family  b) farming only for export  c) city farming
**Answer: a) farming for the family**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Commercial farming produces for —
a) the market  b) the family only  c) no one
**Answer: a) the market**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The dividing question is —
a) who the produce is for  b) how big the farm is only  c) which country it is in
**Answer: a) who the produce is for**
