# Subsistence and commercial farming — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two kinds of subsistence farming.
**Answer:** Any two of: intensive subsistence, shifting cultivation, nomadic herding.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two kinds of commercial farming.
**Answer:** Any two of: commercial grain, mixed farming, plantations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What question divides the two kinds of farming?
**Answer:** Who the produce is for — family or market.
