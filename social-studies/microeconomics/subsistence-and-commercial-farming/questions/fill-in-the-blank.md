# Subsistence and commercial farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shifting cultivation is a kind of _____ farming.
**Answer: subsistence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Plantations are a kind of _____ farming.
**Answer: commercial**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Subsistence = for the family; commercial = for the _____.
**Answer: market**
