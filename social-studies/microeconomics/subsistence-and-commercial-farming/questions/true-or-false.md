# Subsistence and commercial farming — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsistence farmers grow mainly for distant markets.
**Answer: False — they farm for the family.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Plantations are subsistence farming.
**Answer: False — plantations are commercial farming.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Both kinds farm for the market.
**Answer: False — subsistence farms for the family.**
