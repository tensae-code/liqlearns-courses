# Hands-on — The budget line

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
With 100 counters as birr, 'buy' bread cards at 4 and sugar cards at 20 in different mixes. Keep every mix that spends exactly 100 — those mixes are your budget line.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the birr-100 budget line with its two intercepts and shaded feasible triangle — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
