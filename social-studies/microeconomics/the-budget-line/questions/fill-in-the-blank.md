# The budget line — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spending on X plus spending on Y equals income — that summary is the _____.
**Answer: budget equation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With 100 birr and bread at 4 birr, the bread intercept is _____ breads.
**Answer: 25**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Points outside the budget line are _____ — the income cannot buy them.
**Answer: unattainable**
