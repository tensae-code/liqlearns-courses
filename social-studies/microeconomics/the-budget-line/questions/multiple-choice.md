# The budget line — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A wish backed by purchasing power"?
a) every affordable combination at fixed income and prices, in one equation  b) intercepts of 25 breads and 5 sugars from 100 birr at prices 4 and 20  c) on the line spends all income; inside is feasible; outside is unattainable
**Answer: a) every affordable combination at fixed income and prices, in one equation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The worked example — 100 birr, bread and sugar"?
a) every affordable combination at fixed income and prices, in one equation  b) on the line spends all income; inside is feasible; outside is unattainable  c) intercepts of 25 breads and 5 sugars from 100 birr at prices 4 and 20
**Answer: c) intercepts of 25 breads and 5 sugars from 100 birr at prices 4 and 20**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "On, inside and outside the line"?
a) intercepts of 25 breads and 5 sugars from 100 birr at prices 4 and 20  b) on the line spends all income; inside is feasible; outside is unattainable  c) every affordable combination at fixed income and prices, in one equation
**Answer: b) on the line spends all income; inside is feasible; outside is unattainable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** With income of 100 birr, bread at 4 birr and sugar at 20 birr, what are the two intercepts of the budget line?
a) 25 breads and 5 sugars  b) 20 breads and 4 sugars  c) 25 breads and 20 sugars
**Answer: a) 25 breads and 5 sugars** — 100 / 4 = 25 breads; 100 / 20 = 5 sugars.
