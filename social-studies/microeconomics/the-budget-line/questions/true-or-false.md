# The budget line — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The budget equation says spending on X plus spending on Y equals income.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With 100 birr, bread at 4 birr and sugar at 20 birr, the intercepts are 25 breads and 5 sugars.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Points outside the budget line are unattainable at the given income and prices.
**Answer: True**
