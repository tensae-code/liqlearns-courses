# The budget line — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A wish backed by purchasing power" in your own words.
**Answer:** The budget equation says spending on X plus spending on Y equals income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The worked example — 100 birr, bread and sugar" in your own words.
**Answer:** With 100 birr, bread at 4 birr and sugar at 20 birr, the intercepts are 25 breads and 5 sugars.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "On, inside and outside the line" in your own words.
**Answer:** Points outside the budget line are unattainable at the given income and prices.
