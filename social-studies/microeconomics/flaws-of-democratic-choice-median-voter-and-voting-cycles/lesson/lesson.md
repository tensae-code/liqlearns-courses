## Sub lesson 1 — The median voter theory

The **median voter theory** says two competing parties are pulled toward the **position of the voter in the middle** — the median voter decides.

## Sub lesson 2 — The voting cycle

With three or more choices, majority voting can **cycle** — A beats B, B beats C, yet C beats A — so **no stable winner** exists: the **voting cycle**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Markets and government intertwined

The chapter closes on the **interrelationship between markets and government** — each shapes the other, so democratic flaws **spill into economic life**.
