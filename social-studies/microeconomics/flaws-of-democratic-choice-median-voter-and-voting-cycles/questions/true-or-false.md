# Flaws of democratic choice — median voter and voting cycles — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The median voter has no special importance.
**Answer: False — competing parties are pulled to the median voter's position.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Majority voting always produces one stable winner.
**Answer: False — with several choices it can cycle: A beats B, B beats C, C beats A.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Government choices never affect markets.
**Answer: False — markets and government are interrelated, each shaping the other.**
