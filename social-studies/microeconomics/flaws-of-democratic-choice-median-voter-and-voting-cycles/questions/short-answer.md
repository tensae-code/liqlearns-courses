# Flaws of democratic choice — median voter and voting cycles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the median voter theory say?
**Answer:** Two competing parties converge toward the median voter's position.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the cycle in this lesson.
**Answer:** A beats B, B beats C, yet C beats A — no stable winner.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do democratic flaws matter economically?
**Answer:** Because markets and government shape each other, so flaws in democratic choice spill into economic life.
