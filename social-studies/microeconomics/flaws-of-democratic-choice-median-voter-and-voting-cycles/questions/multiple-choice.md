# Flaws of democratic choice — median voter and voting cycles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Parties are pulled toward —
a) the median voter's position  b) the richest voter  c) no position
**Answer: a) the median voter's position**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A voting cycle means —
a) majority choices can circle with no stable winner  b) elections repeat every year  c) voters take turns
**Answer: a) majority choices can circle with no stable winner**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Markets and government are —
a) interrelated, each shaping the other  b) wholly separate  c) the same thing
**Answer: a) interrelated, each shaping the other**
