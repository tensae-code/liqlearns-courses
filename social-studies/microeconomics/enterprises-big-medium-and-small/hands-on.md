# Hands-on — Enterprises — big, medium and small

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Name four enterprises you know — a big company, a medium one, a small one and an informal one (a stall, a home workshop). Say each one's size rank out loud, from largest to smallest, in the book's order.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the book's pyramid with four layers and label them top to bottom: large companies, medium companies, small companies, small informal institutions. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
