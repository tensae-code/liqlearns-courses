# Enterprises — big, medium and small — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides sorting productive institutions by activity, Syria's Grade 6 book also sorts them by —
a) colour  b) size  c) age
**Answer: b) size**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the top of the size pyramid stand —
a) the small informal institutions  b) the medium companies  c) the large companies
**Answer: c) the large companies**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At the base of the pyramid stand —
a) the small informal institutions  b) the large companies  c) the ministries
**Answer: a) the small informal institutions**
