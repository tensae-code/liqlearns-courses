# Enterprises — big, medium and small — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two ways does the Institutions lesson sort productive institutions?
**Answer:** By their activity (agricultural, industrial, commercial, service) and by their size.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the four size ranks in the book's order.
**Answer:** Large companies, medium companies, small companies, and small informal institutions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the pyramid's shape teach besides the ranking itself?
**Answer:** That number grows as size shrinks — large companies are the fewest at the top, and the small informal institutions are the most numerous at the base.
