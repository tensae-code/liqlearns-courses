# Enterprises — big, medium and small — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A large company and a one-person informal stall are both productive institutions, but they do not work at the same scale.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The size pyramid is read from the bottom up, starting with the smallest institutions.
**Answer: False** — the pyramid is read top to bottom, starting with the large companies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** There are more large companies than small informal institutions.
**Answer: False** — the pyramid widens downward: the smaller the enterprise, the more of them there are, with the small informal institutions the most numerous at the base.
