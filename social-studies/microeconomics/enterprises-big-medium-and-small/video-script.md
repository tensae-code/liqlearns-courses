# Video script — Enterprises — big, medium and small

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Not every enterprise is the same size. Syria's Grade 6 book sorts productive institutions by size and draws them as a pyramid. Read it from the top: large companies first — the fewest and the biggest. Then medium companies. Then small companies. And at the base, the small informal institutions — the most numerous of all.

The pyramid's shape is the lesson: as the enterprises get smaller, there are more and more of them.
