# Enterprises — big, medium and small

## Do it first

Think of the businesses and workplaces you pass in a week — a factory, a company office, a small shop, a street stall. Before you read on, put them in order from the biggest to the smallest.

## Sub lesson 1 — Enterprises come in sizes

In Syria's Grade 6 Social Studies book, the lesson المؤسسات (Institutions) — inside the unit on the family and institutions — meets the state's **productive institutions**: the farms, factories, companies and services that produce what society needs. The same lesson that sorts them by their activity also sorts them a second way: **by size**.

An enterprise's size is a real difference. A large company and a one-person informal stall are both productive institutions, but they do not work at the same scale — and the book ranks them so the pupil can see the whole range at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — The size pyramid — large companies at the top

The book draws the ranking as a **pyramid**, read from the top down.

At the top stand the **large companies** — the biggest enterprises, and the fewest. Below them come the **medium companies**. The pyramid widens as it descends because each step down holds more enterprises than the step above it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading the pyramid — down to the small informal institutions

Below the medium companies come the **small companies**, and at the base of the pyramid stand the **small informal institutions** — the smallest productive units of all, and the most numerous.

So the whole ranking, top to bottom, is: **large companies, medium companies, small companies, small informal institutions**. The shape teaches the count as well as the size: the larger the enterprise, the fewer of them there are; the smaller the enterprise, the more of them fill the base.

## Say it to your companion

Explain the size pyramid to your companion in your own words: the four ranks in order, and what the pyramid's shape says about how many enterprises sit at each rank. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Take four enterprises you know and place each one in the pyramid's four layers; say what makes each belong in its layer.

2. Answer this lesson's questions from questions/ without the lesson open; re-read only the parts you missed.

3. Write the key words from memory — enterprise, productive institution, large company, medium company, small company, small informal institution, size pyramid — and write one sentence of your own for each.
