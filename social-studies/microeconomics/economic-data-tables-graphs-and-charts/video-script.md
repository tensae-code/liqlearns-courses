The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Economic data — tables, graphs and charts — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: economic data often shows a functional relationship: when one thing changes, another changes with it in a regular way.; a table sets economic data out in rows and columns so values can be read and compared exactly.; graphs and charts draw economic data as pictures, so patterns and changes can be seen at a glance..
