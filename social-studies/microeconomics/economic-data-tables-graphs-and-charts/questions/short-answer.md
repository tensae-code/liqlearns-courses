# Economic data — tables, graphs and charts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a functional relationship in economic data?
**Answer:** Economic data often shows a functional relationship: when one thing changes, another changes with it in a regular way.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a table do with economic data?
**Answer:** A table sets economic data out in rows and columns so values can be read and compared exactly.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are graphs and charts used for economic data?
**Answer:** Graphs and charts draw economic data as pictures, so patterns and changes can be seen at a glance.
