## Sub lesson 1 — Functional relationships in economics

Economic data often shows a functional relationship: when one thing changes, another changes with it in a regular way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Presenting data in tables

A table sets economic data out in rows and columns so values can be read and compared exactly.

## Sub lesson 3 — Presenting data in graphs and charts

Graphs and charts draw economic data as pictures, so patterns and changes can be seen at a glance.
