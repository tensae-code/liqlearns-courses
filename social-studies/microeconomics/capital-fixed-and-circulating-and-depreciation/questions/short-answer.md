# Capital — fixed and circulating, and depreciation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are fixed funds? Give two examples.
**Answer:** Funds used for years — buildings and equipment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe the turnover of a firm's money.
**Answer:** Money buys materials and labour, production makes goods, the goods are sold and the money returns.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is depreciation charged into?
**Answer:** A depreciation fund, for replacing what wears out.
