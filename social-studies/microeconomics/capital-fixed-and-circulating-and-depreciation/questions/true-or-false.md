# Capital — fixed and circulating, and depreciation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Raw materials are fixed funds.
**Answer: False** — Raw materials are circulating funds — they are used once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A firm's money turns over through production and sale.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Depreciation is charged yearly into a depreciation fund.
**Answer: True**
