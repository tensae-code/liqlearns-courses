# Capital — fixed and circulating, and depreciation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buildings and equipment are _____ funds; raw materials and fuel are _____ funds.
**Answer: fixed; circulating**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The firm's money _____ over: it buys materials and labour, goods are made and sold, and the money returns.
**Answer: turns**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Depreciation A = p/n, where p is the value of the fixed fund and n is its _____ of service.
**Answer: years**
