# Capital — fixed and circulating, and depreciation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fixed funds are —
a) used once  b) buildings and equipment, used for years  c) raw materials and fuel
**Answer: b) buildings and equipment, used for years**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Circulating funds are —
a) used once in production  b) used for years  c) never spent
**Answer: a) used once in production**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The depreciation formula is —
a) A = p/n  b) A = p · n  c) A = n/p
**Answer: a) A = p/n**
