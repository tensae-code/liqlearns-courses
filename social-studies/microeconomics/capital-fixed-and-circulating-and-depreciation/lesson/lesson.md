# Capital — fixed and circulating, and depreciation

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 6, The Firm's Funds. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Fixed funds — used for years

A firm's funds split in two. **Fixed funds** — buildings and equipment — are used for years, serving production again and again.

## Sub lesson 2 — Circulating funds — used once, and the turnover of money

**Working, or circulating, funds** — raw materials, fuel and wages — are used once in production. The firm's money turns over: money buys materials and labour, production makes goods, the goods are sold, and the money returns.

## Sub lesson 3 — Depreciation — A = p/n, into a depreciation fund

Fixed funds wear out, so the firm charges **depreciation** yearly: **A = p/n**, where p is the value of the fixed fund and n its years of service. The sums go into a **depreciation fund** for replacing what wears out.
