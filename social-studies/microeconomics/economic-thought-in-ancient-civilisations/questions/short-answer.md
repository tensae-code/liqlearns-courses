# Economic thought in ancient civilisations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Hammurabi's market laws do?
**Answer:** They fixed written rules for buying, selling and dealing, so exchange followed known rules instead of the stronger party's whim.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Confucius teach about distribution?
**Answer:** That distribution should be just — what a society produces should be shared fairly.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three Greek thinkers in the lesson and one idea of one of them.
**Answer:** Xenophon (managing a household and estate), Plato (the division of labour) and Aristotle (exchange).
