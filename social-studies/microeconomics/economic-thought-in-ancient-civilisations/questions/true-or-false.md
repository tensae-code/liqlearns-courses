# Economic thought in ancient civilisations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic ideas began only when economics got its name.
**Answer: False — the ancient civilisations, Pharaonic Egypt and the Greeks all reasoned about markets, distribution and exchange long before economics had a name.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Confucius taught that distribution should be just.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Aristotle reasoned about exchange.
**Answer: True**
