# Economic thought in ancient civilisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hammurabi is remembered in this lesson for —
a) market laws  b) inventing coins  c) building the first bank
**Answer: a) market laws**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kautilya's Arthashastra sets out —
a) the division of labour in a city  b) the state's economic role  c) the law of demand
**Answer: b) the state's economic role**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Plato described —
a) just distribution  b) paper money  c) the division of labour
**Answer: c) the division of labour**
