**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Yemen).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 1, Lesson 1 — Historical development of economic thought — teaches: Long before economics had a name, the ancient civilisations already thought about markets, fairness and the state's role: Hammurabi wrote market laws, Confucius taught just distribution, Kautilya's Arthashastra set out the state's economic role, and the Greeks — Xenophon, Plato and Aristotle — reasoned about the division of labour and exchange. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
