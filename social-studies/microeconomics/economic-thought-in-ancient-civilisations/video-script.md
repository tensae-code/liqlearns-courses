# Video script — Economic thought in ancient civilisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economic ideas are very old. In the ancient East, and in Pharaonic Egypt, people were already thinking about how a land should be run. Hammurabi wrote market laws. Confucius taught that distribution should be just. Kautilya, in the Arthashastra, set out the economic role of the state.

Then came the Greeks. Xenophon wrote about managing an estate. Plato described the division of labour — each person doing the work that fits them. Aristotle reasoned about exchange. Economics had no name yet, but its first questions were already being asked.
