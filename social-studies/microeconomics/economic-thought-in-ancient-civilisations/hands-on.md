# Hands-on — Economic thought in ancient civilisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with the timeline
Draw a line on paper. Put the thinkers in the lesson's order along it — the ancient East and Pharaonic Egypt, Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, then the Greeks — and say each one's idea out loud as you place him.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a timeline of early thinkers — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
