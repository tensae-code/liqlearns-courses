# The international division of labour — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production is increasingly _____ out to places offering lower costs.
**Answer: contracted**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Often developing countries with no _____ wage and few environmental rules;.
**Answer: minimum**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The garment industry left the developed West for _____ America, Eastern Europe, North Africa and Asia.
**Answer: Central**
