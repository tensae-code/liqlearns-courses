# Supply — schedules, curves and the function — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Qs = a + bP, quantity supplied _____ as price rises.
**Answer: rises**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Below the minimum price, the quantity offered is _____ — nothing is offered at all.
**Answer: zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** By the law of supply, a higher price brings a _____ quantity supplied.
**Answer: larger**
