# Supply — schedules, curves and the function — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The supply function" in your own words.
**Answer:** The supply function Qs = a + bP has quantity supplied rising as price rises.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The minimum price" in your own words.
**Answer:** Below the minimum price, the quantity supplied is zero — nothing is offered.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The supply schedule and curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The law of supply says a higher price brings a larger quantity supplied, other things equal.
