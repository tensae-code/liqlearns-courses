# Supply — schedules, curves and the function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The supply function"?
a) Qs = a + bP — quantity supplied rising with price  b) a minimum price below which the quantity offered is zero  c) an upward-sloping curve — higher price, larger quantity supplied
**Answer: a) Qs = a + bP — quantity supplied rising with price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The minimum price"?
a) Qs = a + bP — quantity supplied rising with price  b) an upward-sloping curve — higher price, larger quantity supplied  c) a minimum price below which the quantity offered is zero
**Answer: c) a minimum price below which the quantity offered is zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The supply schedule and curve"?
a) a minimum price below which the quantity offered is zero  b) an upward-sloping curve — higher price, larger quantity supplied  c) Qs = a + bP — quantity supplied rising with price
**Answer: b) an upward-sloping curve — higher price, larger quantity supplied**
