# Supply — schedules, curves and the function — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The supply function Qs = a + bP has quantity supplied rising as price rises.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Below the minimum price, the quantity supplied is zero — nothing is offered.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The law of supply says a higher price brings a larger quantity supplied, other things equal.
**Answer: True**
