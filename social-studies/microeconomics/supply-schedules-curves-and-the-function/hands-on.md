# Hands-on — Supply — schedules, curves and the function

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
One learner calls rising prices; the class, as sellers, holds up the number of paper shirts they would offer at each price. Plot the offers and find the price where the first shirt appears.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a supply schedule rising with price from a minimum price where nothing is offered — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
