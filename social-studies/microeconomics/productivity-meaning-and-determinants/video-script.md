# Video script — Productivity — meaning and determinants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Productivity — meaning and determinants — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: productivity measures how much output is gained from the inputs used — output per unit of input.; productivity rises with better skills, tools, technology and organisation of work.; higher productivity means more goods and services from the same resources, raising living standards..
