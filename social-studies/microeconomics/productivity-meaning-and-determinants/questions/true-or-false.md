# Productivity — meaning and determinants — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Productivity measures only the inputs a firm uses, never its output.
**Answer: False** — Productivity measures how much output is gained from the inputs used — output per unit of input.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Productivity rises when workers use worse tools.
**Answer: False** — Productivity rises with better skills, tools, technology and organisation of work.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Higher productivity lowers living standards.
**Answer: False** — Higher productivity means more goods and services from the same resources, raising living standards.
