# Productivity — meaning and determinants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does productivity measure?
**Answer:** Productivity measures how much output is gained from the inputs used — output per unit of input.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What raises productivity?
**Answer:** Productivity rises with better skills, tools, technology and organisation of work.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does productivity matter?
**Answer:** Higher productivity means more goods and services from the same resources, raising living standards.
