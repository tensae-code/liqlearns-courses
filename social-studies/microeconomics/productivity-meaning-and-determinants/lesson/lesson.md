## Sub lesson 1 — What productivity means

Productivity measures how much output is gained from the inputs used — output per unit of input.

## Sub lesson 2 — What raises productivity

Productivity rises with better skills, tools, technology and organisation of work.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why productivity matters

Higher productivity means more goods and services from the same resources, raising living standards.
