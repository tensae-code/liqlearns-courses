# Hands-on — Positive statements and normative statements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Read a partner four statements you make up about prices and schools. Your partner sorts each into fact or opinion and says how it could be settled.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two speech bubbles over the same market — one reporting a fact, one giving an opinion — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
