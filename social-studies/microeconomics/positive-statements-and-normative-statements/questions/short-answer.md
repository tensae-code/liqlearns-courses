# Positive statements and normative statements — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Positive — what is" in your own words.
**Answer:** Positive economics describes what is, was or will be, and can be settled by facts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Normative — what ought to be" in your own words.
**Answer:** Normative economics asks what ought to be and carries value judgements.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Telling them apart" in your own words.
**Answer:** A statement is positive if facts can settle it, and normative if only opinion or voting can.
