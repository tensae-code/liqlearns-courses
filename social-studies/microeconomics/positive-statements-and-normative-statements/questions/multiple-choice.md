# Positive statements and normative statements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Positive — what is"?
a) describing what is, settled by facts  b) asking what ought to be, settled by opinion or voting  c) the settlement test — facts, or opinion and voting
**Answer: a) describing what is, settled by facts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Normative — what ought to be"?
a) describing what is, settled by facts  b) the settlement test — facts, or opinion and voting  c) asking what ought to be, settled by opinion or voting
**Answer: c) asking what ought to be, settled by opinion or voting**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Telling them apart"?
a) asking what ought to be, settled by opinion or voting  b) the settlement test — facts, or opinion and voting  c) describing what is, settled by facts
**Answer: b) the settlement test — facts, or opinion and voting**
