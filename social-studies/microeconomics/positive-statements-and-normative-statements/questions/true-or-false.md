# Positive statements and normative statements — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Positive economics describes what is, was or will be, and can be settled by facts.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Positive economics describes what is, was or will be, and can be settled by facts.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Positive — what is); sub lesson 2 says: Normative economics asks what ought to be and carries value judgements.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A statement is positive if facts can settle it, and normative if only opinion or voting can.
**Answer: True**
