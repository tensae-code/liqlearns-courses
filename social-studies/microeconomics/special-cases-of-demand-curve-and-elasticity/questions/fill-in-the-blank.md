# Special cases of the demand curve and elasticity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Perfectly inelastic demand is drawn as a _____ curve.
**Answer: vertical**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Perfectly elastic demand is drawn as a _____ curve.
**Answer: horizontal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elasticity of exactly one is called _____ elasticity.
**Answer: unit**
