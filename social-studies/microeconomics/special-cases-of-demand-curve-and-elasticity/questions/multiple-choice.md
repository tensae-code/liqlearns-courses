# Special cases of the demand curve and elasticity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A vertical demand curve has elasticity —
a) infinity
b) one
c) zero
**Answer: c) zero**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A horizontal demand curve means elasticity is —
a) zero
b) infinite
c) one
**Answer: b) infinite**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unit elasticity means the percentage changes of price and quantity are —
a) opposite in size
b) equal in size
c) both zero
**Answer: b) equal in size**
