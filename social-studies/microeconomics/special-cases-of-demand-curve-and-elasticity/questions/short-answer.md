# Special cases of the demand curve and elasticity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a vertical demand curve say about buyers?
**Answer:** They take the same quantity at every price — quantity does not respond to price at all.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which seller faces a horizontal demand curve?
**Answer:** A single small seller in perfect competition, who loses every buyer by charging above the market price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three special cases and their values.
**Answer:** Vertical — zero; unit-elastic — one; horizontal — infinite.
