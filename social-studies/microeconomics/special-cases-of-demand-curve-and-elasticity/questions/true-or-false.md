# Special cases of the demand curve and elasticity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With vertical demand, quantity demanded is the same at every price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Above the going price, a horizontal demand curve's quantity falls to zero.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** With unit elasticity, a 10% price rise brings a 10% quantity fall.
**Answer: True**
