---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook closes its elasticity survey with the borderline shapes. A vertical demand curve means quantity demanded is the same at every price — perfectly inelastic demand, elasticity zero.

A horizontal demand curve is the opposite borderline: at the going price buyers will take any quantity, but above it they take none — perfectly elastic demand, elasticity without limit (infinite).

Between the borderlines sits unit elasticity: quantity responds in the same proportion as price — a 10% price rise, a 10% quantity fall — so elasticity is exactly one.
