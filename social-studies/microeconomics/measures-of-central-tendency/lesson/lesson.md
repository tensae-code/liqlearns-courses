# Measures of central tendency

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The mean

The mean adds all the values together and divides by how many values there are, giving one average value.

## Sub lesson 2 — The median

The median is the middle value when all the values are lined up in order.

## Sub lesson 3 — The mode

The mode is the value that appears most often in the data.
