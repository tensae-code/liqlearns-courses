The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Measures of central tendency — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: the mean adds all the values together and divides by how many values there are, giving one average value.; the median is the middle value when all the values are lined up in order.; the mode is the value that appears most often in the data..
