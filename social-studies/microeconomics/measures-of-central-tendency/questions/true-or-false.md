# Measures of central tendency — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mean is always the largest value in the data.
**Answer: False** — The mean adds all the values together and divides by how many values there are, giving one average value.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The median is the value that appears most often.
**Answer: False** — The median is the middle value when all the values are lined up in order.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The mode is the middle value of the data.
**Answer: False** — The mode is the value that appears most often in the data.
