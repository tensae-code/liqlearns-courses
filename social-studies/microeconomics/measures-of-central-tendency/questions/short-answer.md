# Measures of central tendency — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the mean worked out?
**Answer:** The mean adds all the values together and divides by how many values there are, giving one average value.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the median?
**Answer:** The median is the middle value when all the values are lined up in order.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the mode?
**Answer:** The mode is the value that appears most often in the data.
