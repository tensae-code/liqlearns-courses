# Measures of central tendency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the mean — the lesson teaches that —
a) the mean adds all the values together and divides by how many values there are, giving one average value.  b) the median is the middle value when all the values are lined up in order.  c) the mode is the value that appears most often in the data.
**Answer: a) the mean adds all the values together and divides by how many values there are, giving one average value.**
