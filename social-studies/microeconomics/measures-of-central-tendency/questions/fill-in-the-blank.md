# Measures of central tendency — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The _____ adds all the values and divides by how many there are.
**Answer: mean**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The _____ is the middle value when values are lined up in order.
**Answer: median**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ is the value that appears most often.
**Answer: mode**
