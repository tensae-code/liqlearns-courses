# Price ceilings and price floors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Ceilings — a cap that creates a shortage" in your own words.
**Answer:** A price ceiling below equilibrium creates a shortage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Floors — a prop that creates a surplus" in your own words.
**Answer:** A price floor above equilibrium creates a surplus and a deadweight loss.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Private floors — resale price maintenance" in your own words.
**Answer:** Resale price maintenance is a private floor between manufacturer and distributors.
