# Price ceilings and price floors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Ceilings — a cap that creates a shortage"?
a) A price ceiling below equilibrium creates a shortage  b) A price floor above equilibrium creates a surplus and a deadweight loss  c) Resale price maintenance is a private floor between manufacturer and distributors
**Answer: a) A price ceiling below equilibrium creates a shortage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Floors — a prop that creates a surplus"?
a) A price floor above equilibrium creates a surplus and a deadweight loss  b) Resale price maintenance is a private floor between manufacturer and distributors  c) A price ceiling below equilibrium creates a shortage
**Answer: a) A price floor above equilibrium creates a surplus and a deadweight loss**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Private floors — resale price maintenance"?
a) Resale price maintenance is a private floor between manufacturer and distributors  b) A price ceiling below equilibrium creates a shortage  c) A price floor above equilibrium creates a surplus and a deadweight loss
**Answer: a) Resale price maintenance is a private floor between manufacturer and distributors**
