# Price ceilings and price floors — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price ceiling below equilibrium creates a shortage.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A price floor above equilibrium creates a surplus and a deadweight loss.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Resale price maintenance is a private floor between manufacturer and distributors.
**Answer: True**
