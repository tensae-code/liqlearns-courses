# Hands-on — Price ceilings and price floors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two panels
Draw the two panels: a capped price with an empty shelf, and a floored price with a piled-up surplus. Label shortage, surplus and deadweight loss where they belong.

## Substitute watch
Pick one floored product and name its margarine — the substitute buyers flee to. Say what happens to the substitute's price and why.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
