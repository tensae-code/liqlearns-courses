# Video script — Price ceilings and price floors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A price ceiling caps a price below equilibrium, typically on food and energy staples. At the capped price, buyers want more than sellers bring: a shortage, with queues and empty shelves standing in for the missing price rise.

A price floor holds a price above equilibrium and creates a surplus: goods pile up unsold, surplus transfers from consumers to producers, and a deadweight loss is left over as consumers substitute — butter to margarine — or exit the market.

Floors can also be private: resale price maintenance, between a manufacturer and its distributors, holds retail prices up without any law. Same diagram, different author.
