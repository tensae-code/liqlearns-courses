Grade 10 teacher-guide note (placements 10 and 12): the two added features come from *Economics, Teacher Guide Book, Grade 10*, printed p. 47 (teacher guide — evidence). The same page's Activity 4.1 revenue key contains two errors as printed: total revenue computed as 'Birr 200/kg × 100 kg = Birr 2000' (correct: Birr 20,000), and marginal revenue stated as Birr 190 where the key's own AR = MR = P rule requires Birr 200. The added sub lesson teaches the corrected figures (TR = Birr 20,000; MR = Birr 200); the errors are named here only and are not reproduced in the teaching text.
