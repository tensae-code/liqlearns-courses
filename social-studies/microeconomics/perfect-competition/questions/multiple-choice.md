# Perfect competition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The features"?
a) many sellers, homogeneous product, free entry and exit, perfect information  b) a horizontal demand curve at the market price — the firm takes the price  c) TR = P × Q with AR = MR = P for every unit
**Answer: a) many sellers, homogeneous product, free entry and exit, perfect information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The firm as price taker"?
a) many sellers, homogeneous product, free entry and exit, perfect information  b) TR = P × Q with AR = MR = P for every unit  c) a horizontal demand curve at the market price — the firm takes the price
**Answer: c) a horizontal demand curve at the market price — the firm takes the price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Revenue — AR equals MR equals P"?
a) a horizontal demand curve at the market price — the firm takes the price  b) TR = P × Q with AR = MR = P for every unit  c) many sellers, homogeneous product, free entry and exit, perfect information
**Answer: b) TR = P × Q with AR = MR = P for every unit**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Two more features — mobility and no government hand (teacher guide)"?
a) perfect mobility of factors and absence of government intervention complete the feature list  b) TR = 200 x 100 = Birr 20,000 and MR = Birr 200, equal to the price  c) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches
**Answer: a) perfect mobility of factors and absence of government intervention complete the feature list**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which statement best matches "Revenue under perfect competition — the corrected key (teacher guide, corrected)"?
a) perfect mobility of factors and absence of government intervention complete the feature list  b) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches  c) TR = 200 x 100 = Birr 20,000 and MR = Birr 200, equal to the price
**Answer: c) TR = 200 x 100 = Birr 20,000 and MR = Birr 200, equal to the price**
