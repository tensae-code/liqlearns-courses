# Perfect competition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The features" in your own words.
**Answer:** Perfect competition has many buyers and sellers trading a homogeneous product.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The firm as price taker" in your own words.
**Answer:** The perfectly competitive firm's demand curve is horizontal at the market price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Revenue — AR equals MR equals P" in your own words.
**Answer:** In perfect competition, average revenue and marginal revenue both equal the price.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Two more features — mobility and no government hand (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** The teacher guide adds perfect mobility of factors and absence of government intervention to the features of perfect competition.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "Revenue under perfect competition — the corrected key (teacher guide, corrected)" in your own words.
**Answer:** At Birr 200 per kg and 100 kg sold, total revenue is Birr 20,000 and marginal revenue is Birr 200.
