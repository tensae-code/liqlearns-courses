# Perfect competition — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Salt and wheat are the book's examples of a _____ product — the same from every seller.
**Answer: homogeneous**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The price taker's demand curve is _____ at the market price.
**Answer: horizontal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In perfect competition, AR and MR both equal the _____.
**Answer: price**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher guide adds perfect _____ and absence of government intervention to the perfect-competition features.
**Answer: mobility of factors**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** At Birr 200 per kg, selling 100 kg gives total revenue of Birr _____ — 200 × 100.
**Answer: 20,000**
