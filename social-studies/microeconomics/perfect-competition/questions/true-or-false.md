# Perfect competition — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Perfect competition has many buyers and sellers trading a homogeneous product.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The perfectly competitive firm's demand curve is horizontal at the market price.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In perfect competition, average revenue and marginal revenue both equal the price.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher guide adds perfect mobility of factors and absence of government intervention to the features of perfect competition.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** At Birr 200 per kg and 100 kg sold, total revenue is Birr 20,000 and marginal revenue is Birr 200.
**Answer: True**
