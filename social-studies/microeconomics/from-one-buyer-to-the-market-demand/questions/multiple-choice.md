# From one buyer to the market demand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Adding quantities at each price"?
a) horizontal summation — quantities added at each price  b) 100 buyers of Q = 50 − 5P demand 5000 − 500P together  c) the market curve — downward-sloping like the individual curves, farther out
**Answer: a) horizontal summation — quantities added at each price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Scaling one buyer to a market"?
a) horizontal summation — quantities added at each price  b) the market curve — downward-sloping like the individual curves, farther out  c) 100 buyers of Q = 50 − 5P demand 5000 − 500P together
**Answer: c) 100 buyers of Q = 50 − 5P demand 5000 − 500P together**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Reading the market curve"?
a) 100 buyers of Q = 50 − 5P demand 5000 − 500P together  b) the market curve — downward-sloping like the individual curves, farther out  c) horizontal summation — quantities added at each price
**Answer: b) the market curve — downward-sloping like the individual curves, farther out**
