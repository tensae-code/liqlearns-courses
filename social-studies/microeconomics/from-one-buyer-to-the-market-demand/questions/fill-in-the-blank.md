# From one buyer to the market demand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market demand is built by _____ summation: quantities added at each price.
**Answer: horizontal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One hundred buyers of Q = 50 − 5P together demand _____ — the market demand function.
**Answer: 5000 − 500P**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market demand curve slopes _____, like every individual demand curve.
**Answer: downward**
