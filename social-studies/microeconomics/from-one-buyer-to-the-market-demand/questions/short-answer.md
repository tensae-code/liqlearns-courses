# From one buyer to the market demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Adding quantities at each price" in your own words.
**Answer:** Market demand is found by adding individual quantities at each price — horizontal summation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Scaling one buyer to a market" in your own words.
**Answer:** One hundred buyers each demanding Q = 50 − 5P give a market demand of 5000 − 500P.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reading the market curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The market demand curve slopes downward like individual demand curves, with larger quantities at each price.
