# From one buyer to the market demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market demand is found by adding individual quantities at each price — horizontal summation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One hundred buyers each demanding Q = 50 − 5P give a market demand of 5000 − 500P.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market demand curve slopes downward like individual demand curves, with larger quantities at each price.
**Answer: True**
