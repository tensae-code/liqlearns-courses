# Hands-on — From one buyer to the market demand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three learners hold quantity cards for prices Birr 5, 10 and 15. At each price the class adds the three cards aloud, sideways, and writes the market schedule on the board.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three shoppers' baskets merging into one market basket — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
