# Video script — Consumer rights — laws and standards

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's Grade 7 Social Studies book, in Lesson 8 "Consumption", teaches that **consumers have rights protected by laws and regulations**. The first of them is the right to buy **sound, quality goods** — goods that are what they claim to be and safe to use. The right is not a favour from the seller; it stands in law before any purchase is made. The same section names how the protection is enforced. Producers work under **production permits** — permission to make and sell what they make. A producer who breaks the rules faces **fines and punishment** for the violation. Rights, permits and penalties are one system: the law says what the consumer may claim, and backs the claim against the producer who ignores it. Beside the laws stands the **standard (استاندارد, defined in the book)**: the **acceptable specification** every producer must meet. The standard matters above all for the goods that touch people's **health and safety** — **food, hygiene goods, and electrical and heating appliances**. A thing that meets the standard has been measured against a written specification, not against the seller's promise.
