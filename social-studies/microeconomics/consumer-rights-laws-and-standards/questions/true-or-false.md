# Consumer rights — laws and standards — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A consumer's rights stand in law before any purchase is made.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A producer who breaks the consumer-protection rules faces no penalty if the goods were cheap.
**Answer: False — Violations are met with fines and punishment; the law backs the consumer's claim against the producer.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The standard is the seller's own promise about a good.
**Answer: False — The standard is a written, acceptable specification every producer must meet — measured against the specification, not the seller's promise.**
