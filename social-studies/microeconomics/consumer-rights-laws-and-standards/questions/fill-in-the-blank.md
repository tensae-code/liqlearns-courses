# Consumer rights — laws and standards — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Producers work under production _____ — permission to make and sell what they make.
**Answer: permits**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A producer who violates the rules faces fines and _____.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The _____ (استاندارد) is the acceptable specification every producer must meet.
