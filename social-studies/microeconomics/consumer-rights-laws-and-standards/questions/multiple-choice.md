# Consumer rights — laws and standards — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The consumer's right to sound, quality goods is protected by —
a) the seller's goodwill  b) laws and regulations  c) advertising
**Answer: b) laws and regulations**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A producer who violates the consumer-protection rules faces —
a) a warning only  b) fines and punishment  c) a prize
**Answer: b) fines and punishment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The standard matters above all for goods that touch —
a) fashion  b) health and safety — food, hygiene goods, electrical and heating appliances  c) sport
**Answer: b) health and safety — food, hygiene goods, electrical and heating appliances**
