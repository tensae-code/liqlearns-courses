# Consumer rights — laws and standards — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first consumer right this lesson teaches, and what protects it?
**Answer:** The right to buy sound, quality goods. It is protected by laws and regulations — it stands in law, not in the seller's goodwill.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the standard, and for which goods does it matter most?
**Answer:** The standard is the acceptable specification every producer must meet. It matters above all for food, hygiene goods, and electrical and heating appliances — the goods that touch people's health and safety.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three parts of the protection system this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The consumer's rights in law (sound, quality goods first), enforcement through production permits with fines and punishment for violations, and the standard — the specification every producer must meet, above all for food, hygiene goods and electrical and heating appliances.
