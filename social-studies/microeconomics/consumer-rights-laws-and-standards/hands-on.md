# Hands-on — Consumer rights — laws and standards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Check a standard
Take one packaged food and one electrical thing at home. Look for the standard mark and the production permit information on each, and write down what you find.

## Write the claim
A shop sold you a heater that does not meet the standard. Write a three-sentence claim: the right you stand on, the rule broken, and the penalty the law provides.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 3 aloud — the standard — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
