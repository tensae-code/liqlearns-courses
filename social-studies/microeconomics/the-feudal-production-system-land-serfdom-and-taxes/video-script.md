The feudal production system land serfdom and taxes — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: The feudal system rested on possessing land and extracting its resources; Vassalage (lords help the king in war, receive fiefs) and serfdom (peasants work the lord's land and pay taxes in exchange for protection); Technical advances — wheeled plough, better yokes, the water mill replacing the hand mill — raised agricultural production
