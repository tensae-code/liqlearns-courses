# The feudal production system land serfdom and taxes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the feudal system rested on possessing land and?
**Answer:** The feudal system rested on possessing land and extracting its resources;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about vassalage lords help the king in war receive?
**Answer:** Vassalage (lords help the king in war, receive fiefs) and serfdom (peasants work the lord's land and pay taxes in exchange for protection);

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about technical advances wheeled plough better yokes the water?
**Answer:** Technical advances — wheeled plough, better yokes, the water mill replacing the hand mill — raised agricultural production
