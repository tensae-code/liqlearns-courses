# The feudal production system land serfdom and taxes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The feudal system rested on _____ land and extracting its resources;
**Answer: possessing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vassalage (lords help the king in war, receive fiefs) and serfdom (peasants work the lord's land and pay taxes in exchange for _____
**Answer: protection**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Technical advances — wheeled plough, better yokes, the water mill replacing the hand mill — raised _____ production
**Answer: agricultural**
