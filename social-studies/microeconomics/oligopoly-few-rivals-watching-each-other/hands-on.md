# Hands-on — Oligopoly — few rivals watching each other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Pairs face off as duopolists with price cards hidden. Each round both reveal at once, and both must announce what they expected the other to do — the watching-each-other habit in play.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three large firms in a triangle of double-headed watching arrows — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
