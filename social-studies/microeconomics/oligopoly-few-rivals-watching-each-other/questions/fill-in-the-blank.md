# Oligopoly — few rivals watching each other — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oligopoly is a market with _____ dominant sellers, each holding a large share.
**Answer: few**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oligopoly's defining trait is strategic _____ — every move provokes rivals' reactions.
**Answer: interdependence**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book names _____, steel and photographic equipment as oligopolistic industries.
**Answer: pharmaceuticals**
