# Oligopoly — few rivals watching each other — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oligopoly means a few dominant sellers, as in cement and steel, or automobiles and soft drinks.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oligopoly's defining trait is strategic interdependence: each firm's decisions provoke rivals' reactions.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pharmaceuticals, steel and photographic equipment are among the book's examples of oligopoly.
**Answer: True**
