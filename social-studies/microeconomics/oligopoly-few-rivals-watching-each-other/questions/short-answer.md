# Oligopoly — few rivals watching each other — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Few firms, large shares" in your own words.
**Answer:** Oligopoly means a few dominant sellers, as in cement and steel, or automobiles and soft drinks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Strategic interdependence" in your own words.
**Answer:** Oligopoly's defining trait is strategic interdependence: each firm's decisions provoke rivals' reactions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Examples all around" in your own words.
**Answer:** Pharmaceuticals, steel and photographic equipment are among the book's examples of oligopoly.
