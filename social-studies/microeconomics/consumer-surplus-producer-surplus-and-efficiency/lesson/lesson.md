# Consumer surplus, producer surplus and efficiency

## Sub lesson 1 — Consumer surplus

**Consumer surplus** is the gain buyers get when they pay **less than the most they were willing to pay** — the area under demand and above the price.

## Sub lesson 2 — Producer surplus and social surplus

**Producer surplus** is the gain sellers get above their cost; **social surplus** is **consumer plus producer surplus** together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ceilings, floors and lost surplus

**Price ceilings and price floors can destroy surplus** — trades that would have benefited both sides no longer happen, so the policy is **inefficient**.
