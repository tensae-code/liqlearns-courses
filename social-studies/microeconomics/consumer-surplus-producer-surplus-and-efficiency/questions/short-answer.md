# Consumer surplus, producer surplus and efficiency — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is consumer surplus?
**Answer:** The gain to buyers from paying less than the most they were willing to pay.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is producer surplus?
**Answer:** The gain sellers get from receiving more than the minimum they would accept (above cost).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why can a price floor be inefficient?
**Answer:** It blocks trades that would have benefited both sides, destroying surplus.
