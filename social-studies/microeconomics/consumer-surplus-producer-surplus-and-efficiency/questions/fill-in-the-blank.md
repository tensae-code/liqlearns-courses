# Consumer surplus, producer surplus and efficiency — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buyers gain _____ surplus when they pay less than their maximum willingness to pay.
**Answer: consumer**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Social surplus = consumer surplus + _____ surplus.
**Answer: producer**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ceilings and floors can _____ surplus.
**Answer: destroy**
