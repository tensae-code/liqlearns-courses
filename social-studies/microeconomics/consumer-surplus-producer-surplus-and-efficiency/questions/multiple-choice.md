# Consumer surplus, producer surplus and efficiency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumer surplus is —
a) what buyers gain by paying less than their maximum willingness to pay  b) the firm's profit  c) a tax on buyers
**Answer: a) what buyers gain by paying less than their maximum willingness to pay**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Social surplus is —
a) consumer surplus plus producer surplus  b) consumer surplus minus producer surplus  c) the government's revenue
**Answer: a) consumer surplus plus producer surplus**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ceilings and floors can be inefficient because they —
a) destroy surplus by blocking beneficial trades  b) always raise social surplus  c) never change quantities
**Answer: a) destroy surplus by blocking beneficial trades**
