# Consumer surplus, producer surplus and efficiency — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumer surplus is a loss to buyers.
**Answer: False — it is a gain: buyers pay less than the most they were willing to pay.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Social surplus counts only the sellers' gain.
**Answer: False — social surplus is consumer plus producer surplus.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A price ceiling always increases social surplus.
**Answer: False — ceilings and floors can destroy surplus and so be inefficient.**
