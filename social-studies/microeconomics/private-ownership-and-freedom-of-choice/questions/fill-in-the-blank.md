# Private ownership and freedom of choice — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private ownership means _____ can belong to a person or a family, not only to the state.
**Answer: property**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Freedom of choice means a person may choose their work, their purchases and whether to start a _____.
**Answer: business**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ownership without choice would be property you cannot use as you _____ best.
**Answer: judge**
