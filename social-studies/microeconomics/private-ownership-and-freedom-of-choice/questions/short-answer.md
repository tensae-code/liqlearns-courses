# Private ownership and freedom of choice — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does private ownership push people to build and improve?
**Answer: Because what they build is theirs and its fruits are theirs — a family gains when its own workshop grows.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What steers a market economy, according to this lesson?
**Answer: Millions of free choices by workers, shoppers and businesses, added together.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What limits each person's ownership and choice?
**Answer: The law, and other people's same rights.**
