# Private ownership and freedom of choice — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private ownership means property can belong to a person or family, not only the state.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Freedom of choice in a market system means a person may choose nothing — an office assigns everything.
**Answer: False — An office does not assign each worker a job or each shopper a basket in a market system.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ownership and choice are twin pillars because each needs the other.
**Answer: True**
