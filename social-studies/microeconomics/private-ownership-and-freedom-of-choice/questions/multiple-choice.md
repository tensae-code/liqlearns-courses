# Private ownership and freedom of choice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private ownership means property can belong to —
a) nobody at all  b) a person or a family, not only to the state  c) only foreign companies
**Answer: b) a person or a family, not only to the state**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Freedom of choice in a market system means a person may choose —
a) their work, their purchases and whether to start a business  b) only the colour of their clothes  c) nothing — an office assigns everything
**Answer: a) their work, their purchases and whether to start a business**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are ownership and choice called twin pillars?
a) only one of them is really needed  b) they are two names for exactly the same thing  c) each needs the other — ownership without choice, or choice without ownership, cannot hold the system up
**Answer: c) each needs the other — ownership without choice, or choice without ownership, cannot hold the system up**
