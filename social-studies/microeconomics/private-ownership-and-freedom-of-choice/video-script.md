# Video script — Private ownership and freedom of choice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Private ownership means property — land, tools, a shop, a business — can belong to a person or a family, not only to the state. What you own, you may use, and its fruits are yours. This pillar gives people a reason to build and improve: a family that owns its workshop gains when the workshop grows.

The second pillar is freedom of choice: a person may choose their work, choose what to buy with their money, and choose to start a business. No office assigns each worker a job or each shopper a basket. These millions of free choices, added together, are what steer a market economy.

The book teaches the two as twin pillars of one system. Ownership without choice would be property you cannot use as you judge best; choice without ownership would be freedom with nothing of your own to choose with. Together they hold up the market system the book introduced — and each pillar limits the other only through the law and other people's same rights.
