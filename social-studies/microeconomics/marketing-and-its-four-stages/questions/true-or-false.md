# Marketing and its four stages — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marketing works through four research stages.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Studying the product is the first marketing stage.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Advertising is the fourth stage of marketing.
**Answer: False** — Advertising is the third stage; placing the good is the fourth.
