# Marketing and its four stages — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is marketing, in the book's words?
**Answer:** Studying consumers' demands and satisfying them in market conditions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the first two marketing stages.
**Answer:** Studying the product, and pricing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three channels for placing a good.
**Answer:** Direct, retail and wholesale.
