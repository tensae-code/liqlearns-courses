# Marketing and its four stages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ is studying consumers' demands and satisfying them in market conditions.
**Answer: Marketing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second stage is _____, following production, wholesale and retail prices.
**Answer: pricing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third stage is _____; the fourth places the good on the market.
**Answer: advertising**
