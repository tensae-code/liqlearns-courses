# Marketing and its four stages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marketing is —
a) studying consumers' demands and satisfying them  b) only advertising  c) setting taxes
**Answer: a) studying consumers' demands and satisfying them**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The pricing stage follows —
a) production, wholesale and retail prices  b) only the retail price  c) the tax rate
**Answer: a) production, wholesale and retail prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Placing the good happens through —
a) direct, retail and wholesale channels  b) the khokimiyat  c) the bank only
**Answer: a) direct, retail and wholesale channels**
