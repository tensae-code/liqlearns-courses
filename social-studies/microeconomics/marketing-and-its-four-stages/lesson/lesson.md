# Marketing and its four stages

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 11, Marketing. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Marketing — studying consumers' demands

**Marketing** is studying consumers' demands and satisfying them in market conditions. It works through four research stages.

## Sub lesson 2 — Stage one and two — studying the product, and pricing

The first stage **studies the product**. The second is **pricing** — the book follows production, wholesale and retail prices, the price rising as the good moves toward the buyer.

## Sub lesson 3 — Stage three and four — advertising, and placing the good

The third stage is **advertising**. The fourth **places the good on the market** through channels: direct, retail and wholesale.
