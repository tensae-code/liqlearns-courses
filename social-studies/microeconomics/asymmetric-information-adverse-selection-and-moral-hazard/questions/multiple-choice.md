# Asymmetric information — adverse selection and moral hazard — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "One side knows more"?
a) Asymmetric information is when one side of a deal knows more than the other  b) Adverse selection is the hidden type driving good risks out before the deal  c) Moral hazard is the protected party taking more risk after the contract
**Answer: a) Asymmetric information is when one side of a deal knows more than the other**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Adverse selection — before the deal"?
a) Adverse selection is the hidden type driving good risks out before the deal  b) Moral hazard is the protected party taking more risk after the contract  c) Asymmetric information is when one side of a deal knows more than the other
**Answer: a) Adverse selection is the hidden type driving good risks out before the deal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Moral hazard — after the deal"?
a) Moral hazard is the protected party taking more risk after the contract  b) Asymmetric information is when one side of a deal knows more than the other  c) Adverse selection is the hidden type driving good risks out before the deal
**Answer: a) Moral hazard is the protected party taking more risk after the contract**
