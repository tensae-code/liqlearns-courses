# Asymmetric information — adverse selection and moral hazard — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "One side knows more" in your own words.
**Answer:** Asymmetric information is when one side of a deal knows more than the other.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Adverse selection — before the deal" in your own words.
**Answer:** Adverse selection is the hidden type driving good risks out before the deal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Moral hazard — after the deal" in your own words.
**Answer:** Moral hazard is the protected party taking more risk after the contract.
