# Asymmetric information — adverse selection and moral hazard — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Asymmetric information is when one side of a deal knows more than the other.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Adverse selection is the hidden type driving good risks out before the deal.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Moral hazard is the protected party taking more risk after the contract.
**Answer: True**
