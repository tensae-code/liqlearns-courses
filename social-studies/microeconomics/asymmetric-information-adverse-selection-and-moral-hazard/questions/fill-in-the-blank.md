# Asymmetric information — adverse selection and moral hazard — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When one side knows more than the other, information is ____.
**Answer: asymmetric**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hidden types driving good risks out before a deal is ____ selection.
**Answer: adverse**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Taking more risk because a contract protects you is moral ____.
**Answer: hazard**
