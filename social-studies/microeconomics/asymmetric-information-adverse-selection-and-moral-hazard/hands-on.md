# Hands-on — Asymmetric information — adverse selection and moral hazard

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the stories
Write six deal stories, three of adverse selection and three of moral hazard, shuffle, and sort a partner's set — the test is always: hidden type before, or changed behaviour after?

## Design a defence
For one lending case and one insurance case, name what the uninformed side could check, ask or require before signing. Keep the defences legal and realistic.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
