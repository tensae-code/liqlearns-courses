# Hands-on — Ordinal utility — ranking bundles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay three pairs of food cards on the table. For each pair, a partner must say 'I prefer this', 'I prefer that' or 'I am indifferent' — never a number. Swap roles and repeat.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — three bundle pairs labelled prefers this, prefers that, indifferent — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
