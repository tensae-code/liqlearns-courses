# Ordinal utility — ranking bundles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If two bundles satisfy a consumer equally, the consumer is _____ between them.
**Answer: indifferent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The assumption that more is always preferred to less is called _____.
**Answer: non-satiation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indifference curves join bundles the consumer's _____ place at the same level of satisfaction.
**Answer: rankings**
