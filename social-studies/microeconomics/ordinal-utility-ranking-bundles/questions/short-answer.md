# Ordinal utility — ranking bundles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Ranking instead of measuring" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the ordinal approach a consumer can only prefer one bundle, prefer the other, or be indifferent between them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The assumptions of the ordinal approach" in your own words.
**Answer:** The ordinal assumptions include a diminishing marginal rate of substitution and non-satiation — more is always preferred to less.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why rankings are enough" in your own words.
**Answer:** Indifference curves are built from bundles the consumer ranks as equal in satisfaction.
