# Ordinal utility — ranking bundles — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the ordinal approach a consumer can only prefer one bundle, prefer the other, or be indifferent between them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ordinal assumptions include a diminishing marginal rate of substitution and non-satiation — more is always preferred to less.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indifference curves are built from bundles the consumer ranks as equal in satisfaction.
**Answer: True**
