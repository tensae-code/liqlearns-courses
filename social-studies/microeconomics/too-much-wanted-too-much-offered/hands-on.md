# Hands-on — Too much wanted, too much offered — excess demand and excess supply

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Deal price cards Birr 1–5. At each price, two groups hold up demand and supply quantity cards from the table; the class calls the difference and its name.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the five-row birr table with the middle row highlighted as equilibrium and the rows above and below shaded as shortage and surplus sides — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
