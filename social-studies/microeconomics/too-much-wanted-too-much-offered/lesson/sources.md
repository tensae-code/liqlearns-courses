This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (the guide's Answers to Activities, printed pp. 47–49): the student book's equilibrium section is purely graphical and contains no excess-demand/excess-supply table. The table's figures were verified before teaching: at Birr 1–5 per kg, demand 500, 400, 300, 200, 100 kg against supply 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 kg gives excess demand of 400 and 200 below Birr 3, excess supply of 200 and 400 above it, and equality at Birr 3 with 300 kg — internally consistent, so it is taught as computed here.

Pairing note carried from the placements: the student textbook is the finished 2023 edition, while the teacher guide is ©2022 with an unfinished placeholder printing block — edition-mismatched companions; the guide's answer keys are partly incomplete/misaligned with this book's review questions, and no answer-key figure is taught here unchecked.
