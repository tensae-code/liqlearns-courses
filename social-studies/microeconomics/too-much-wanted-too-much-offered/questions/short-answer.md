# Too much wanted, too much offered — excess demand and excess supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Excess demand — too much wanted" in your own words.
**Answer:** Excess demand is consumers wanting to buy more than producers will supply at that price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Excess supply — too much offered" in your own words.
**Answer:** Excess supply is consumers wanting less than producers offer at that price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The worked birr table" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the worked table, equilibrium is at Birr 3 with 300 kg traded.
