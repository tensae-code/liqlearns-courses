# Too much wanted, too much offered — excess demand and excess supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Excess demand — too much wanted"?
a) quantity demanded greater than quantity supplied, below the equilibrium price  b) quantity supplied greater than quantity demanded, above the equilibrium price  c) equilibrium at Birr 3 with 300 kg, excess demand below and excess supply above
**Answer: a) quantity demanded greater than quantity supplied, below the equilibrium price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Excess supply — too much offered"?
a) quantity demanded greater than quantity supplied, below the equilibrium price  b) equilibrium at Birr 3 with 300 kg, excess demand below and excess supply above  c) quantity supplied greater than quantity demanded, above the equilibrium price
**Answer: c) quantity supplied greater than quantity demanded, above the equilibrium price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The worked birr table"?
a) quantity supplied greater than quantity demanded, above the equilibrium price  b) equilibrium at Birr 3 with 300 kg, excess demand below and excess supply above  c) quantity demanded greater than quantity supplied, below the equilibrium price
**Answer: b) equilibrium at Birr 3 with 300 kg, excess demand below and excess supply above**
