# Too much wanted, too much offered — excess demand and excess supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Excess demand is consumers wanting to buy more than producers will supply at that price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Excess demand is consumers wanting to buy more than producers will supply at that price.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Excess demand — too much wanted); sub lesson 2 says: Excess supply is consumers wanting less than producers offer at that price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the worked table, equilibrium is at Birr 3 with 300 kg traded.
**Answer: True**
