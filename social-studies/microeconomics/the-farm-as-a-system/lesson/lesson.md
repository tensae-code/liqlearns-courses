# The farm as a system

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — the farm as a system — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Inputs — seed, fertiliser, machinery, labour

A farm is a **system**. Its **inputs** are **seed, fertiliser, machinery and labour** — what is put into the land.

## Sub lesson 2 — Processes — ploughing, sowing, spraying, harvesting

Its **processes** are the work done: **ploughing, sowing, spraying and harvesting** — inputs at work through the season.

## Sub lesson 3 — Outputs — crops, wool, dairy, poultry

Its **outputs** are what comes out: **crops, wool, dairy and poultry** — the produce the system yields.

## Say it to your companion
