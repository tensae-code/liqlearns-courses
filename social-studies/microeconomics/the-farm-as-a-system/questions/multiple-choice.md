# The farm as a system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farm inputs are —
a) seed, fertiliser, machinery and labour  b) crops and wool  c) ploughing and sowing
**Answer: a) seed, fertiliser, machinery and labour**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farm processes include —
a) ploughing, sowing, spraying and harvesting  b) only selling  c) only buying seed
**Answer: a) ploughing, sowing, spraying and harvesting**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Farm outputs include —
a) crops, wool, dairy and poultry  b) seed and fertiliser  c) machinery only
**Answer: a) crops, wool, dairy and poultry**
