# The farm as a system — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crops are a farm's inputs.
**Answer: False — crops are outputs; inputs are seed, fertiliser, machinery, labour.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Harvesting is an input.
**Answer: False — harvesting is a process.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Inputs, processes and outputs are the farm as a system.
**Answer: True**
