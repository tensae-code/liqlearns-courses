# Hands-on — Fair prices, costs and the basic basket

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work the Venezuelan case
Take the lesson's case on paper: a fair price the family budget can reach. Write its three parts in your own words, one line each, and check them against lesson/lesson.md. Fix any line the lesson does not support.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the chain from memory: A fair price the family budget can reach → The basic basket — what fair prices must cover → The State regulates costs — the Sundde and the fair-gains law. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
