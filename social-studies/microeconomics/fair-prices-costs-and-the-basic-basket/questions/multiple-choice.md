# Fair prices, costs and the basic basket — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fair price, in the book, is one —
a) accessible to the family budget  b) set as high as buyers can be forced to pay  c) the same for every product
**Answer: a) accessible to the family budget**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The basic basket (canasta básica) is —
a) the set of basics a family's fair prices must let it cover  b) a shopping basket sold in stores  c) a list of luxury goods
**Answer: a) the set of basics a family's fair prices must let it cover**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Sundde watches over the application of —
a) the Law of Fair Gains, Costs and Prices  b) the railway guarantee law  c) the school curriculum
**Answer: a) the Law of Fair Gains, Costs and Prices**
