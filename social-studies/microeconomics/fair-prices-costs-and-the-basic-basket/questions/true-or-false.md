# Fair prices, costs and the basic basket — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book judges a price's fairness against the family budget.
**Answer: True** — products must have a fair price accessible to it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fair prices matter because the basic basket can be covered by families.
**Answer: True** — that is why the standard is set.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Sundde's role includes watching over the Law of Fair Gains, Costs and Prices.
**Answer: True** — the State regulates costs through laws and the Sundde watches their application.
