# Fair prices, costs and the basic basket — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Against what is a price's fairness measured in the book?
**Answer:** Against the family budget — a fair price is one accessible to it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must prices be fair, in terms of the basic basket?
**Answer:** So that the basic basket — the basics a family needs — can be covered by the family budget.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is the fair-price standard enforced, according to the book?
**Answer:** The State regulates costs through laws, and the Sundde watches over the application of the Law of Fair Gains, Costs and Prices.
