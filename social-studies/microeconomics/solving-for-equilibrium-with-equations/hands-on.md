# Hands-on — Solving for equilibrium with equations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Deal price cards from Birr 6 to 14. At each price, two learners compute Qd and Qs aloud from the equations; the class shouts which is bigger until the card where they meet at 50.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the wheat equations set equal, solved step by step to price Birr 10 and quantity 50 kg — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
