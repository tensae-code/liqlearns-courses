# Solving for equilibrium with equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The equation method"?
a) set Qd equal to Qs and solve for the price  b) P = Birr 10 per kg and Q = 50 kg from 120 = 12P  c) supply at P = 10 also gives 50 — the two sides must agree
**Answer: a) set Qd equal to Qs and solve for the price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The wheat worked example"?
a) set Qd equal to Qs and solve for the price  b) supply at P = 10 also gives 50 — the two sides must agree  c) P = Birr 10 per kg and Q = 50 kg from 120 = 12P
**Answer: c) P = Birr 10 per kg and Q = 50 kg from 120 = 12P**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Checking with the second equation"?
a) P = Birr 10 per kg and Q = 50 kg from 120 = 12P  b) supply at P = 10 also gives 50 — the two sides must agree  c) set Qd equal to Qs and solve for the price
**Answer: b) supply at P = 10 also gives 50 — the two sides must agree**
