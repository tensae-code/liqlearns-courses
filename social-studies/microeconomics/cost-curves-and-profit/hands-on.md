# Hands-on — Cost curves and profit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Use two strings on graph paper: a U for ATC and a dipping-then-rising MC that must cross the U at its lowest point. A partner checks the crossing sits exactly at the bottom.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — U-shaped ATC with MC cutting it at its lowest point, the three rules captioned — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
