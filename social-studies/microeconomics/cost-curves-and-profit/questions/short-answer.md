# Cost curves and profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The shapes of the cost curves" in your own words.
**Answer:** The average total cost curve is U-shaped and marginal cost dips then rises.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The three marginal-average rules" in your own words.
**Answer:** Average cost is at its minimum where the marginal cost curve crosses it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The two profit formulas" in your own words.
**Answer:** Profit equals total revenue minus total cost, or price minus average cost, times quantity.
