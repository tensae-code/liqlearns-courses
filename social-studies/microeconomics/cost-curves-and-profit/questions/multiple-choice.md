# Cost curves and profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The shapes of the cost curves"?
a) ATC is U-shaped; MC dips then rises under the law of variable proportions  b) average falls while marginal is below it, rises while above, minimum where they cross  c) profit = TR − TC = (P − AC) × Q
**Answer: a) ATC is U-shaped; MC dips then rises under the law of variable proportions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The three marginal-average rules"?
a) ATC is U-shaped; MC dips then rises under the law of variable proportions  b) profit = TR − TC = (P − AC) × Q  c) average falls while marginal is below it, rises while above, minimum where they cross
**Answer: c) average falls while marginal is below it, rises while above, minimum where they cross**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The two profit formulas"?
a) average falls while marginal is below it, rises while above, minimum where they cross  b) profit = TR − TC = (P − AC) × Q  c) ATC is U-shaped; MC dips then rises under the law of variable proportions
**Answer: b) profit = TR − TC = (P − AC) × Q**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A firm sells 100 units at 50 birr each with an average cost of 42 birr. What is its total profit?
a) 800 birr  b) 8 birr  c) 4,200 birr
**Answer: a) 800 birr** — Profit = (P − AC) × Q = (50 − 42) × 100 = 800 birr.
