# Cost curves and profit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The average total cost curve is _____ — falling, bottoming out, then rising.
**Answer: U-shaped**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Average cost is at its _____ exactly where marginal cost crosses it.
**Answer: minimum**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit is total revenue minus _____; equivalently, price minus average cost, times quantity.
**Answer: total cost**
