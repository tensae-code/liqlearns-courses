# Cost curves and profit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The average total cost curve is U-shaped and marginal cost dips then rises.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Average cost is at its minimum where the marginal cost curve crosses it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit equals total revenue minus total cost, or price minus average cost, times quantity.
**Answer: True**
