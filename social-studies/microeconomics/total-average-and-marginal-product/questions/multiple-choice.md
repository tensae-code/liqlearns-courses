# Total, average and marginal product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Total product"?
a) the whole output from the factors employed  b) AP = TP/L — output per worker, the measure of labour productivity  c) MP = ΔTP/ΔL — the extra output from one more unit of input; MP crosses AP at AP's maximum
**Answer: a) the whole output from the factors employed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Average product — and productivity"?
a) the whole output from the factors employed  b) MP = ΔTP/ΔL — the extra output from one more unit of input; MP crosses AP at AP's maximum  c) AP = TP/L — output per worker, the measure of labour productivity
**Answer: c) AP = TP/L — output per worker, the measure of labour productivity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Marginal product — and the AP–MP rules"?
a) AP = TP/L — output per worker, the measure of labour productivity  b) MP = ΔTP/ΔL — the extra output from one more unit of input; MP crosses AP at AP's maximum  c) the whole output from the factors employed
**Answer: b) MP = ΔTP/ΔL — the extra output from one more unit of input; MP crosses AP at AP's maximum**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "The three product measures"?
a) APL = TP ÷ L; MPL = change in TP ÷ change in L, the slope of TP  b) TP peaking at 60 quintals with 10–11 workers on one hectare  c) extra output from each further worker eventually falls on fixed land
**Answer: a) APL = TP ÷ L; MPL = change in TP ÷ change in L, the slope of TP**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which statement best matches "The wheat hectare worked"?
a) APL = TP ÷ L; MPL = change in TP ÷ change in L, the slope of TP  b) extra output from each further worker eventually falls on fixed land  c) TP peaking at 60 quintals with 10–11 workers on one hectare
**Answer: c) TP peaking at 60 quintals with 10–11 workers on one hectare**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Which statement best matches "The law of diminishing marginal productivity"?
a) TP peaking at 60 quintals with 10–11 workers on one hectare  b) extra output from each further worker eventually falls on fixed land  c) APL = TP ÷ L; MPL = change in TP ÷ change in L, the slope of TP
**Answer: b) extra output from each further worker eventually falls on fixed land**
