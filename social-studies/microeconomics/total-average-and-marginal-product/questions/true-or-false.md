# Total, average and marginal product — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Total product is the whole output from the factors employed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Total product is the whole output from the factors employed.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Total product); sub lesson 2 says: Average product is TP divided by L, output per worker and the measure of labour productivity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Marginal product is the extra output from one additional unit of the variable input, and MP intersects AP where AP is at its maximum.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Marginal product is the change in total product divided by the change in labour.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** On one hectare, total product peaks at 60 quintals with 10 to 11 workers.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The law of diminishing marginal productivity says extra output per added worker eventually falls when other inputs are fixed.
**Answer: True**
