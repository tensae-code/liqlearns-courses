# Total, average and marginal product — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Total product is the _____ output from the factors employed.
**Answer: whole**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Average product is output _____, AP = TP/L.
**Answer: per worker**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Marginal product is the _____ output from one additional unit of the variable input.
**Answer: extra**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Marginal product can be read as the _____ of the total product curve.
**Answer: slope**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Total product on the hectare peaks at _____ with 10–11 workers.
**Answer: 60 quintals**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** On fixed land, the extra output from each further worker _____ — the law of diminishing marginal productivity.
**Answer: eventually falls**
