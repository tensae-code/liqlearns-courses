# Total, average and marginal product — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Total product" in your own words.
**Answer:** Total product is the whole output from the factors employed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Average product — and productivity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Average product is TP divided by L, output per worker and the measure of labour productivity.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Marginal product — and the AP–MP rules" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal product is the extra output from one additional unit of the variable input, and MP intersects AP where AP is at its maximum.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "The three product measures" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal product is the change in total product divided by the change in labour.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "The wheat hectare worked" in your own words.
**Answer:** On one hectare, total product peaks at 60 quintals with 10 to 11 workers.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Explain "The law of diminishing marginal productivity" in your own words.
**Answer:** The law of diminishing marginal productivity says extra output per added worker eventually falls when other inputs are fixed.
