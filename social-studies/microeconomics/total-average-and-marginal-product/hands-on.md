# Hands-on — Total, average and marginal product

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Add learners one by one to a 'harvest team' stacking cups; after each addition, the class computes TP (total cups), AP (per learner) and MP (cups the new learner added) aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a three-line mini-chart of TP, AP and MP from one small table, formulas written under each — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Grade 10 — say it with things at home
Add paper workers to a drawn hectare one by one. For each, the class calls that worker's extra quintals from the lesson's table and a learner writes the MPL on the board — watching the extras shrink, then turn negative.
