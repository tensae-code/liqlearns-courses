# Cross and income elasticity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Substitutes carry a _____ cross elasticity — one price rise lifts the other's sales.
**Answer: positive**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good bought less as income rises — negative income elasticity — is an _____ good.
**Answer: inferior**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Both measures are read by _____ first — plus or minus — then by size.
**Answer: sign**
