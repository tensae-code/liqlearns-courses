# Cross and income elasticity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Cross elasticity — the sign speaks"?
a) positive for substitutes, negative for complements, zero for independent goods  b) negative inferior, below 1 necessity, above 1 luxury  c) cross maps good against good; income maps good against prosperity
**Answer: a) positive for substitutes, negative for complements, zero for independent goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Income elasticity — sorting goods"?
a) positive for substitutes, negative for complements, zero for independent goods  b) cross maps good against good; income maps good against prosperity  c) negative inferior, below 1 necessity, above 1 luxury
**Answer: c) negative inferior, below 1 necessity, above 1 luxury**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Reading the two together"?
a) negative inferior, below 1 necessity, above 1 luxury  b) cross maps good against good; income maps good against prosperity  c) positive for substitutes, negative for complements, zero for independent goods
**Answer: b) cross maps good against good; income maps good against prosperity**
