# Cross and income elasticity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Cross elasticity — the sign speaks" in your own words.
**Answer:** A positive cross elasticity marks a pair of substitutes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Income elasticity — sorting goods" in your own words.
**Answer:** A negative income elasticity marks an inferior good.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reading the two together" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cross elasticity compares two goods, while income elasticity compares a good with buyers' income.
