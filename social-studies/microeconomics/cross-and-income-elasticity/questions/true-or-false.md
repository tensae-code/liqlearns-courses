# Cross and income elasticity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A positive cross elasticity marks a pair of substitutes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A negative income elasticity marks an inferior good.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cross elasticity compares two goods, while income elasticity compares a good with buyers' income.
**Answer: True**
