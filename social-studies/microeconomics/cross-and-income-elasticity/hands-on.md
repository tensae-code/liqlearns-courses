# Hands-on — Cross and income elasticity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Hold two cans — cola brands — and a phone with its SIM card. Act out a price rise for one of each pair and have the class call the cross-elasticity sign, plus or minus, before you reveal it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — cola cans with a plus sign for substitutes, and a phone and SIM card with a minus sign for complements — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
