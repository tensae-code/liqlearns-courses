## Sub lesson 1 — Where firms raise funds

Business enterprises raise funds from owners' savings, retained profit, loans and other sources matched to the firm's size and needs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Problems of business enterprises

Enterprises face problems in raising enough funds and in growing, which limit what they can do.

## Sub lesson 3 — What fixes a firm's size

The size of a firm is fixed by the capital it can raise, the size of its market and the ability of its management.
