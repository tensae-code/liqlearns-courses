# Sources of finance for business enterprises — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A firm can only ever raise funds from one source.
**Answer: False** — Business enterprises raise funds from owners' savings, retained profit, loans and other sources matched to the firm's size and needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Enterprises never face problems raising funds.
**Answer: False** — Enterprises face problems in raising enough funds and in growing, which limit what they can do.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A firm's size is fixed by chance alone.
**Answer: False** — The size of a firm is fixed by the capital it can raise, the size of its market and the ability of its management.
