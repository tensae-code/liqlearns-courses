# Sources of finance for business enterprises — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About where firms raise funds — the lesson teaches that —
a) business enterprises raise funds from owners' savings, retained profit, loans and other sources matched to the firm's size and needs.  b) enterprises face problems in raising enough funds and in growing, which limit what they can do.  c) the size of a firm is fixed by the capital it can raise, the size of its market and the ability of its management.
