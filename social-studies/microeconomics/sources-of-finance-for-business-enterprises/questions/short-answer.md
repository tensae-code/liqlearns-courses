# Sources of finance for business enterprises — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do business enterprises raise funds?
**Answer:** Business enterprises raise funds from owners' savings, retained profit, loans and other sources matched to the firm's size and needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What problems do enterprises face?
**Answer:** Enterprises face problems in raising enough funds and in growing, which limit what they can do.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What fixes the size of a firm?
**Answer:** The size of a firm is fixed by the capital it can raise, the size of its market and the ability of its management.
