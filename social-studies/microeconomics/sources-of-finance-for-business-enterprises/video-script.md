# Video script — Sources of finance for business enterprises

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sources of finance for business enterprises — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: business enterprises raise funds from owners' savings, retained profit, loans and other sources matched to the firm's size and needs.; enterprises face problems in raising enough funds and in growing, which limit what they can do.; the size of a firm is fixed by the capital it can raise, the size of its market and the ability of its management..
