# Video script — Kinds of goods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kinds of goods — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: economic goods are scarce and priced; non-economic goods are free like air; consumer goods are used directly; capital goods are used to produce other goods; goods are also split public against private, and ranked necessities, comforts, luxuries.
