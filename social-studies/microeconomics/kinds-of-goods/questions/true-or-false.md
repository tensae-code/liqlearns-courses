# Kinds of goods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Air is an economic good because it is scarce and priced.
**Answer: False** — air is the book's kind of free, non-economic good.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A machine used to make goods is a consumer good.
**Answer: False** — a machine that produces other goods is a capital good.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Luxuries are needed more urgently than necessities.
**Answer: False** — necessities come first in the ranking; luxuries are extras.
