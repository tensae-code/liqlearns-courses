# Kinds of goods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About economic and non-economic goods — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic goods are scarce and priced; non-economic goods are free like air  b) consumer goods are used directly; capital goods are used to produce other goods  c) goods are also split public against private, and ranked necessities, comforts, luxuries
**Answer: a) economic goods are scarce and priced; non-economic goods are free like air**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About consumer goods and capital goods — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic goods are scarce and priced; non-economic goods are free like air  b) consumer goods are used directly; capital goods are used to produce other goods  c) goods are also split public against private, and ranked necessities, comforts, luxuries
**Answer: b) consumer goods are used directly; capital goods are used to produce other goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About public and private goods; necessities, comforts and luxuries — the lesson teaches that —
a) economic goods are scarce and priced; non-economic goods are free like air  b) consumer goods are used directly; capital goods are used to produce other goods  c) goods are also split public against private, and ranked necessities, comforts, luxuries
**Answer: c) goods are also split public against private, and ranked necessities, comforts, luxuries**
