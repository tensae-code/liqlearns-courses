# Kinds of goods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods that are scarce and carry a price are _____ goods.
**Answer: economic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Goods used to produce other goods are _____ goods.
**Answer: capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's ranking, food is a necessity and extras are _____.
**Answer: luxuries**
