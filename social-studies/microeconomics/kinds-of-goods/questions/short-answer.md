# Kinds of goods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book split goods by scarcity and price?
**Answer:** Economic goods are scarce and priced; non-economic goods are freely available, like air.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between consumer goods and capital goods?
**Answer:** Consumer goods are used directly by the consumer; capital goods are used to produce other goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the book's last two ways of classifying goods?
**Answer:** Public against private goods, and the ranking necessities, comforts and luxuries.
