# Kinds of goods

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Economic and non-economic goods

The Class 11 book classifies goods first by whether they are scarce and priced. **Economic goods** are limited and carry a price; **non-economic goods** are freely available, like air, and carry none.

## Sub lesson 2 — Consumer goods and capital goods

Next, goods are split by their use: **consumer goods** are used directly by the consumer; **capital goods** are used to produce other goods. The same thing can change kind with its use — what matters is what it is used for.

## Sub lesson 3 — Public and private goods; necessities, comforts and luxuries

The classification closes two ways: **public goods** against **private goods**, by who can use them; and goods ranked as **necessities, comforts and luxuries**, by how urgently they are needed — food first, ease next, extras last.
