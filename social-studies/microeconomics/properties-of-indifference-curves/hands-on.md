# Hands-on — Properties of indifference curves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
With string on a table, lay out three curves that never touch. Then force two strings to cross and ask a partner to explain, pointing at the crossing bundle, why it breaks the rules.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — four small panels, one per property, with the crossing-curves panel crossed out — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
