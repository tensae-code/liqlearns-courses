# Properties of indifference curves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Downward sloping, and higher is better"?
a) downward slope balances more of one good with less of the other; higher curves are preferred  b) a crossing would give one bundle two satisfaction levels and break transitivity  c) convex because the MRS falls as the consumer moves down the curve
**Answer: a) downward slope balances more of one good with less of the other; higher curves are preferred**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Curves never intersect"?
a) downward slope balances more of one good with less of the other; higher curves are preferred  b) convex because the MRS falls as the consumer moves down the curve  c) a crossing would give one bundle two satisfaction levels and break transitivity
**Answer: c) a crossing would give one bundle two satisfaction levels and break transitivity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Convex to the origin"?
a) a crossing would give one bundle two satisfaction levels and break transitivity  b) convex because the MRS falls as the consumer moves down the curve  c) downward slope balances more of one good with less of the other; higher curves are preferred
**Answer: b) convex because the MRS falls as the consumer moves down the curve**
