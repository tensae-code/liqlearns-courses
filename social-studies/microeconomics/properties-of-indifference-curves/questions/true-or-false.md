# Properties of indifference curves — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An indifference curve slopes downward because more of one good must be balanced by less of the other to keep satisfaction constant.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Indifference curves never intersect, because a crossing would give one bundle two levels of satisfaction.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An indifference curve is convex to the origin because the marginal rate of substitution falls along it.
**Answer: True**
