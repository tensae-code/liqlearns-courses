# Properties of indifference curves — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A standard indifference curve slopes _____, because more of one good must be balanced by less of the other.
**Answer: downward**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Indifference curves never _____ — a crossing would give one bundle two satisfaction levels.
**Answer: intersect**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indifference curves are _____ to the origin, because the marginal rate of substitution falls along them.
**Answer: convex**
