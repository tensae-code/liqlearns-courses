# Properties of indifference curves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Downward sloping, and higher is better" in your own words.
**Answer:** An indifference curve slopes downward because more of one good must be balanced by less of the other to keep satisfaction constant.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Curves never intersect" in your own words.
**Answer:** Indifference curves never intersect, because a crossing would give one bundle two levels of satisfaction.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Convex to the origin" in your own words.
**Answer:** An indifference curve is convex to the origin because the marginal rate of substitution falls along it.
