# Regulating natural monopolies — cost-plus and price-cap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A natural monopoly is regulated because —
a) one firm serves the market cheapest, so price must be policed  b) it has too many rivals  c) it makes no profit
**Answer: a) one firm serves the market cheapest, so price must be policed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cost-plus regulation allows price = —
a) costs plus a set return  b) any price the firm likes  c) marginal cost exactly
**Answer: a) costs plus a set return**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under a price cap, a firm that cuts costs —
a) keeps the saving  b) must hand it all back at once  c) is fined
**Answer: a) keeps the saving**
