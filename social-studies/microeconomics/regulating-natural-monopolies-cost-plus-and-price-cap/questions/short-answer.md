# Regulating natural monopolies — cost-plus and price-cap — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why regulate rather than split a natural monopoly?
**Answer:** Because a single firm serves the whole market at lowest cost.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is cost-plus regulation's weakness?
**Answer:** The firm has little reason to cut costs, since costs are covered.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does a price cap encourage efficiency?
**Answer:** The firm keeps what it saves by cutting costs below the cap.
