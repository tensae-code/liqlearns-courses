# Regulating natural monopolies — cost-plus and price-cap — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Splitting a natural monopoly always lowers costs.
**Answer: False — one firm serves the market cheapest; that is what makes it natural.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cost-plus regulation strongly rewards cost-cutting.
**Answer: False — costs are covered anyway, so the incentive to cut them is weak.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A price cap removes any reason to cut costs.
**Answer: False — keeping the saving is exactly the incentive a cap creates.**
