# Regulating natural monopolies — cost-plus and price-cap — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One firm serving the market cheapest is a _____ monopoly.
**Answer: natural**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cost-_____ regulation covers costs plus a set return.
**Answer: plus**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Price-_____ regulation sets a ceiling price for a period.
**Answer: cap**
