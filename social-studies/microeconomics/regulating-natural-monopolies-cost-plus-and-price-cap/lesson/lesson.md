## Sub lesson 1 — The natural monopoly problem

A **natural monopoly** serves the whole market **cheapest alone** — competition policy cannot simply split it, so the state **regulates its price** instead.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cost-plus regulation

**Cost-plus regulation** lets the firm charge its **costs plus a set return** — safe for the firm, but it gives **little reason to cut costs**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Price-cap regulation

**Price-cap regulation** sets a **ceiling price** for a period — the firm keeps what it saves by cutting costs, so it has a **reason to be efficient**; the book reads both off a **graph of regulatory choices**.
