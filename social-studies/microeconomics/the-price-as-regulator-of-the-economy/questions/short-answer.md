# The price as regulator of the economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about price — the monetary expression of value?
**Answer:** Price is defined as the monetary expression of value, and Nicaragua's Grade 10 book gives it four regulating roles in the economy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about regulating production and resources?
**Answer:** Price regulates production, and it regulates the use and disposal of resources — producers follow prices when they decide what to make and what to use.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about regulating distribution and consumption — the balancing agent?
**Answer:** Price regulates distribution and consumption, acting as the balancing agent of the whole economic system.
