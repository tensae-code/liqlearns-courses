# The price as regulator of the economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price is defined as _____, and Nicaragua's Grade 10 book gives it four regulating roles in the economy.
**Answer: the monetary expression of value**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Price regulates production, and it regulates the use and disposal of resources — producers follow prices when they decide what to make and what to use.
**Answer: regulating production**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Price regulates distribution and consumption, acting as _____.
**Answer: the balancing agent of the whole economic system**
