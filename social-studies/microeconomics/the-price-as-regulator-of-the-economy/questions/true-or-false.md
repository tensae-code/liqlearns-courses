# The price as regulator of the economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Price is defined as the monetary expression of value, and Nicaragua's Grade 10 book gives it four regulating roles in the economy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Price regulates production, and it regulates the use and disposal of resources — producers follow prices when they decide what to make and what to use.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Price regulates distribution and consumption, acting as something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — Price regulates distribution and consumption, acting as the balancing agent of the whole economic system.
