Price is defined as the monetary expression of value, and Nicaragua's Grade 10 book gives it four regulating roles in the economy.

Price regulates production, and it regulates the use and disposal of resources — producers follow prices when they decide what to make and what to use.

Price regulates distribution and consumption, acting as the balancing agent of the whole economic system.
