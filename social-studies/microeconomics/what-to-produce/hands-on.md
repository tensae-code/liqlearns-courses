# Hands-on — What to produce?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Deal 12 product cards to a group acting as planners. They must drop 4. Each dropped card must be defended with a pair from the lesson (necessity vs luxury, and so on).

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a national shopping list being chosen between bread, machines, and luxuries — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
