# What to produce? — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What to produce is the problem of allocation of resources: deciding the type and quantity of goods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What to produce is the problem of allocation of resources: deciding the type and quantity of goods.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The problem of allocation); sub lesson 2 says: The what-to-produce choice runs between consumption and capital goods, civil and military goods, necessities and luxuries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Every economy must answer what to produce, whether by plan, by market or by custom.
**Answer: True**
