# What to produce? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The problem of allocation" in your own words.
**Answer:** What to produce is the problem of allocation of resources: deciding the type and quantity of goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The great either-ors" in your own words.
**Answer:** The what-to-produce choice runs between consumption and capital goods, civil and military goods, necessities and luxuries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "No economy escapes it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Every economy must answer what to produce, whether by plan, by market or by custom.
