# What to produce? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The problem of allocation"?
a) the problem of allocation of resources — deciding types and quantities  b) consumption vs capital, civil vs military, necessities vs luxuries  c) every economy answers it somehow — by plan, market or custom
**Answer: a) the problem of allocation of resources — deciding types and quantities**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The great either-ors"?
a) the problem of allocation of resources — deciding types and quantities  b) every economy answers it somehow — by plan, market or custom  c) consumption vs capital, civil vs military, necessities vs luxuries
**Answer: c) consumption vs capital, civil vs military, necessities vs luxuries**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "No economy escapes it"?
a) consumption vs capital, civil vs military, necessities vs luxuries  b) every economy answers it somehow — by plan, market or custom  c) the problem of allocation of resources — deciding types and quantities
**Answer: b) every economy answers it somehow — by plan, market or custom**
