| Bulgaria part — Economics expansion — "Advertising — necessary and useful" (Рекламата – необходима и полезна): advertising is a form of marketing communication whose aim is to inform clients about the offered products, awaken their interest and motivate them to buy; its messages reach consumers through special advertising materials (business cards, posters, catalogues), print media (newspapers, magazines, letters) and electronic media (radio, television, internet, telephone); the firm sign (trademark) is the graphic image that distinguishes one entrepreneur's goods and services from the others'; advertising's benefit is that it informs consumers and spurs competition between producers, which leads to improving quality and lowering the prices of goods and services — but advertising messages sometimes exaggerate and mislead, so in many countries strict requirements are set on advertising: it must not mislead consumers, promise easy profits, imitate established marks and products, claim unverifiable advantages, attract attention in socially unacceptable or offensive ways, or slander and offend competitors | Bulgaria, *Bulgaria Grade 7 Technology and Entrepreneurship*, "Advertising — necessary and useful" (Рекламата – необходима и полезна) — printed p. 67 |
