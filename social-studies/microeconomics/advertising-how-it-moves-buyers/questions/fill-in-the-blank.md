# Advertising — how it moves buyers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Advertising attracts the buyers' _____ and helps the spread of goods.
**Answer: attention**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Advertising is a form of marketing _____ whose aim is to inform, interest and motivate.
**Answer: communication**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Advertising messages sometimes exaggerate and _____, so strict requirements are set on advertising.
**Answer: mislead**
