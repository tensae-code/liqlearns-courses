# Advertising — how it moves buyers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six ways of advertising the Grade 3 lesson lists.
**Answer:** Leaflet, brochure, internet advertisement, catalogue, television advertisement and billboard.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two benefits of advertising does the Grade 7 lesson name?
**Answer:** It informs consumers, and it spurs competition between producers — which leads to improving quality and lowering the prices of goods and services.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four things advertising must not do.
**Answer:** Any four of: mislead consumers; promise easy profits; imitate established marks and products; claim unverifiable advantages; attract attention in socially unacceptable or offensive ways; slander and offend competitors.
