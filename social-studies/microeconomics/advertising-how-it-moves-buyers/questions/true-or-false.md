# Advertising — how it moves buyers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In nature, bright flowers win pollinators as customers — the Grade 3 lesson uses this to show how advertising attracts buyers' attention.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The firm sign (trademark) is the graphic image that distinguishes one entrepreneur's goods and services from the others'.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Advertising may claim advantages that cannot be verified.
**Answer: False — advertising must not claim unverifiable advantages.**
