# Advertising — how it moves buyers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a way of advertising named in the Grade 3 lesson?
a) a leaflet  b) a shopping list  c) a price label
**Answer: a) a leaflet**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Advertising is a form of marketing communication whose aim is to —
a) hide products  b) inform clients, awaken their interest and motivate them to buy  c) fix prices
**Answer: b) inform clients, awaken their interest and motivate them to buy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Advertising must not —
a) inform consumers  b) use posters  c) mislead consumers or promise easy profits
**Answer: c) mislead consumers or promise easy profits**
