# Video script — Advertising — how it moves buyers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Advertising is everywhere — leaflet, brochure, internet, catalogue, television, billboard. It is marketing communication: it informs clients, awakens their interest and motivates them to buy, and its trademark tells one firm from another. It helps buyers and spurs competition — but it sometimes exaggerates and misleads, so the rules bind it: no misleading, no easy profits, no unverifiable claims.
