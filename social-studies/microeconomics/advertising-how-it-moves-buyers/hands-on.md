# Hands-on — Advertising — how it moves buyers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the forms
On one walk or one evening of media, note every advertisement you meet and sort it by the Grade 3 forms — leaflet, brochure, internet, catalogue, television, billboard. Which form reached you most often?

## Make one honestly
Make an advertising poster or souvenir for a healthy snack, as the Grade 3 team project does — then check it against the Grade 7 rules: nothing misleading, no easy-profit promise, no unverifiable claim.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — inform, interest, motivate — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
