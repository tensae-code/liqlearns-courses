# Video script — Unfair competition and competition law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Competition has a dishonest twin, and Vietnam's Grade 11 book names it: unfair competition.

It is conduct against law, good faith, honesty and business customs — misleading indications, disparaging or disrupting a rival. The book's case is a mattress maker who advertised lies about rivals' products.

Vietnam's Competition Law 2018 forbids it because the harm falls on enterprises and consumers alike. Compete on price and quality — never on deception.
