# Unfair competition and competition law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Unfair competition is conduct contrary to —
a) law, good faith and honesty  b) low prices  c) advertising itself
**Answer: a) law, good faith and honesty**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The mattress maker's fault was —
a) baseless advertising denigrating rivals  b) selling too cheaply  c) employing too few workers
**Answer: a) baseless advertising denigrating rivals**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Vietnam's law naming unfair competition is the —
a) Competition Law 2018  b) State Budget Law  c) Labour Code
**Answer: a) Competition Law 2018**
