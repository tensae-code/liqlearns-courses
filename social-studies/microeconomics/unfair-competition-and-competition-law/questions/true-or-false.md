# Unfair competition and competition law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Competing on price and quality is unfair competition.
**Answer: False — unfair competition is conduct contrary to law, good faith, honesty and business customs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Disparaging another enterprise can be unfair competition.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unfair competition can harm consumers as well as enterprises.
**Answer: True**
