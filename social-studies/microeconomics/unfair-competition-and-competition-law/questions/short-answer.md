# Unfair competition and competition law — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define unfair competition.
**Answer:** Conduct contrary to law, to the principles of good faith and honesty, to business customs and other standards in business, that harms or may harm the lawful rights and interests of other enterprises or consumers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's example of unfair competition.
**Answer:** A mattress maker whose baseless advertising denigrated rivals' products.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whose rights does unfair competition harm?
**Answer:** The lawful rights and interests of other enterprises and of consumers.
