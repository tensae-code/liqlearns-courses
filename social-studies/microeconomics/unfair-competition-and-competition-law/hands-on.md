# Hands-on — Unfair competition and competition law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's own activity
Find one advertisement you have seen recently. Judge it by this lesson: does it compete on the product's own price and quality, or does it mislead, disparage or disrupt a rival? Write your verdict and the words in the advertisement that decided it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main sorting or chain from memory and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
