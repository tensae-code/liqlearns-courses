# Functions, variables and graphs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A quantity that can take different values, like income or price, is a _____.
**Answer: variable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saving written as depending on income is saving as a _____ of income.
**Answer: function**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Drawing the relation between variables so the eye can see it is _____ representation.
**Answer: graphic**
