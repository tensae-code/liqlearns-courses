# Management — functions and methods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Planning is one of the four management functions.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The manager is the person who does the work of management.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In autocracy, decisions are shared by all.
**Answer: False** — In autocracy one person decides; shared decisions are democracy.
