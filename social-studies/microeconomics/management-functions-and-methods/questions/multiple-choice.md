# Management — functions and methods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Management's functions are —
a) planning, organising, leading, controlling  b) buying, selling, advertising  c) hiring and firing only
**Answer: a) planning, organising, leading, controlling**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A firm's management structure is drawn —
a) as a price list  b) as schemes of who directs whom  c) as a map
**Answer: b) as schemes of who directs whom**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The three management methods are —
a) autocracy, bureaucracy, democracy  b) planning, leading, controlling  c) fixed, variable, total
**Answer: a) autocracy, bureaucracy, democracy**
