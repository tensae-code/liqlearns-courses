# Management — functions and methods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four functions of management.
**Answer:** Planning, organising, leading and controlling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a management-structure scheme show?
**Answer:** Who directs whom, level by level.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whose statecraft is the book's local-wisdom sidebar for this topic?
