# Management — functions and methods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Management is planning, organising, leading and _____.
**Answer: controlling**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book draws a firm's management _____ as schemes.
**Answer: structure**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One person deciding is _____; rules and papers deciding is bureaucracy; shared decisions are democracy.
**Answer: autocracy**
