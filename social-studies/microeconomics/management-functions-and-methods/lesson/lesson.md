# Management — functions and methods

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 10, Managing the Firm — Management. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Management — planning, organising, leading, controlling

**Management** is running a firm through four functions: **planning, organising, leading and controlling**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The manager's job and the firm's management structure

The **manager** is the person who does this work. The book draws a firm's **management structure** as schemes — who directs whom, level by level.

## Sub lesson 3 — Three methods — autocracy, bureaucracy, democracy

Management is done by one of three **methods**: **autocracy** (one person decides), **bureaucracy** (rules and papers decide) and **democracy** (decisions are shared). The book's local-wisdom sidebar is Amir Temur's statecraft.
