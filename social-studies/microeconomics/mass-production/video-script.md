# Video script — Mass production

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's Grade 6 Social Studies book, in Lesson 16 "Clothing, from production to consumption", defines **mass production (تولید انبوه)**: **making goods in large quantities in one uniform form**. The two marks belong together — the quantity is large, and every piece comes out in the same form as the piece before it. The book gives the cause. Once **population grew**, the old way broke: **one tailor taking measurements** for each person, one garment at a time, **could no longer meet many people's needs**. The need outgrew the single craftsman's hands, and production had to change its shape to answer it. The answer the book names is the **workshop** and **ready-made clothing bought from shops**. Instead of waiting for a tailor to measure and sew one garment, people buy clothing already made — in quantity, in uniform sizes — from the shop shelf. Mass production is what fills that shelf.
