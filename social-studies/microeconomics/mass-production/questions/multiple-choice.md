# Mass production — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass production is —
a) making one special garment for one person  b) making goods in large quantities in one uniform form  c) repairing old goods
**Answer: b) making goods in large quantities in one uniform form**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mass production came because —
a) tailors became too expensive to train  b) population grew and one tailor taking measurements could no longer meet many people's needs  c) people stopped wearing clothes
**Answer: b) population grew and one tailor taking measurements could no longer meet many people's needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the new way, people get clothing —
a) only by measuring with a tailor  b) as ready-made clothing bought from shops  c) by making it all at home
**Answer: b) as ready-made clothing bought from shops**
