# Mass production — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In mass production every piece comes out in the same form as the piece before it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mass production began because one tailor taking measurements could no longer meet many people's needs once population grew.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ready-made clothing is measured and sewn for one named person.
**Answer: False — Ready-made clothing is made in quantity, in uniform sizes, and bought from shops — no measuring of one person.**
