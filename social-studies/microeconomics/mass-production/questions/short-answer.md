# Mass production — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define mass production in the book's words, naming both of its marks.
**Answer:** Making goods in large quantities in one uniform form — the quantity is large, and every piece comes out in the same form.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did mass production come, in the book's account?
**Answer:** Population grew, and one tailor taking measurements, making one garment at a time, could no longer meet many people's needs — production had to change its shape.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What replaced the tailor's measuring, and where do people get their clothing in the new way?
**Answer:** Workshops making clothing in quantity replaced the single tailor's measuring, and people buy ready-made clothing from shops.
