# Mass production — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass _____ (تولید انبوه) is making goods in large quantities in one uniform form.
**Answer: production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Once _____ grew, one tailor could no longer meet many people's needs.
**Answer: population**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** People now buy _____ clothing, already made in uniform sizes, from the shop shelf.
**Answer: ready-made**
