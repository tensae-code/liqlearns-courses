# Mass production

## Do it first

Think of a shirt in a shop. Dozens hang there, all the same size, the same cut, the same cloth. Before you read on, say how shirts were made before shops could hang them like that.

## Sub lesson 1 — What mass production is — large quantities, one uniform form

Iran's Grade 6 Social Studies book, in Lesson 16 "Clothing, from production to consumption", defines **mass production (تولید انبوه)**: **making goods in large quantities in one uniform form**. The two marks belong together — the quantity is large, and every piece comes out in the same form as the piece before it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it came — one tailor could no longer meet many people's needs

The book gives the cause. Once **population grew**, the old way broke: **one tailor taking measurements** for each person, one garment at a time, **could no longer meet many people's needs**. The need outgrew the single craftsman's hands, and production had to change its shape to answer it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Workshops and ready-made clothing — the new way people dress

The answer the book names is the **workshop** and **ready-made clothing bought from shops**. Instead of waiting for a tailor to measure and sew one garment, people buy clothing already made — in quantity, in uniform sizes — from the shop shelf. Mass production is what fills that shelf.
