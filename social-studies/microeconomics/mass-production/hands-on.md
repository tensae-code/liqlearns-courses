# Hands-on — Mass production

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Uniform or made-for-one?
Take five things you wear or use. Sort them: made in one uniform form for everyone, or made for one person. Say which pile mass production filled.

## Count the shelf
In a shop or a picture of one, count how many identical pieces of one good you can see. Write the number — that is mass production's quantity made visible.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — why mass production came — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
