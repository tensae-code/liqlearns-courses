Why an educated workforce matters and its impact, and the role human resources play in how societies develop and change.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.

Putting it together: why an educated workforce matters and its impact, and the role human resources play in how societies develop and change — taught in Unit 1, Topics 5–6: Role of education and training on the workforce; Role of human resources in the development and changes of societies (Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG, printed p. 47).
