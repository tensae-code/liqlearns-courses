# Education and training build the workforce — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Why an educated workforce matters and its impact"?
**Answer:** Why an educated workforce matters and its impact, and the role human resources play in how societies develop and change.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG: the standalone Senior High Economics course re-runs the five-unit frame at full depth — human resources and human capital first, then price mechanism and inflation, economic ideologies (conservatism, liberalism, protectionism, free market), international trade, and a closing unit on the communist economy mirroring Grade 10's capitalism unit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Putting it together: why an educated workforce matters"?
**Answer:** Putting it together: why an educated workforce matters and its impact, and the role human resources play in how societies develop and change — taught in Unit 1, Topics 5–6: Role of education and training on the workforce; Role of human resources in the development and changes of societies (Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Economics TG, printed p. 47).
