# Profitability and bankruptcy of a firm

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 9, Analysis of Economic Activity. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Profitability — R = P/TC · 100%

**Profitability** measures profit against the costs that earned it: **R = P/TC · 100%**. The higher the percentage, the more profit each sum of costs brings in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nominal and real profit — and judging a firm from its report

**Nominal profit** is the money figure as it stands; **real profit** deflates it by the price-rise index, so inflation is stripped out. A firm is judged from its **profit-and-loss report**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bankruptcy — the insolvent firm's property is auctioned

A firm that cannot pay its debts is **bankrupt**. Its property is **auctioned** and its debts are paid **in legal order**. The book names Uzbekistan's **1994 bankruptcy law**.
