# Profitability and bankruptcy of a firm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Profitability R equals —
a) P/TC · 100%  b) P · TC  c) TC/P
**Answer: a) P/TC · 100%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Real profit is —
a) the money figure as it stands  b) nominal profit deflated by the price-rise index  c) profit before costs
**Answer: b) nominal profit deflated by the price-rise index**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A bankrupt firm's property is —
a) given to its workers  b) auctioned, and its debts paid in legal order  c) burned
**Answer: b) auctioned, and its debts paid in legal order**
