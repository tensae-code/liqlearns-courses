# Profitability and bankruptcy of a firm — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does profitability measure?
**Answer:** Profit against total costs, as a percentage: R = P/TC · 100%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is real profit got from nominal profit?
**Answer:** By deflating it by the price-rise index.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happens to a bankrupt firm's debts?
**Answer:** Its property is auctioned and the debts are paid in legal order.
