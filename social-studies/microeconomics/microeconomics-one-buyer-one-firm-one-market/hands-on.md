# Hands-on — Microeconomics — one buyer, one firm, one market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
At a real or imagined stall, list everything micro about it: its prices, its costs, its one seller's choices. Read the list aloud and have a partner challenge anything that sounds like the whole country instead.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a single market stall with its price tag picked out from a whole town behind it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
