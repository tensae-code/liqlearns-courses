# Microeconomics — one buyer, one firm, one market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Microeconomics studies single decision-making units such as a household, a firm or an industry.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Microeconomics studies single decision-making units such as a household, a firm or an industry.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The single units); sub lesson 2 says: Microeconomics' fundamental problem is resource allocation, and its tools are the demand and supply of particular goods and factors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Newer branches around micro and macro include development, environmental, labour, monetary and health economics.
**Answer: True**
