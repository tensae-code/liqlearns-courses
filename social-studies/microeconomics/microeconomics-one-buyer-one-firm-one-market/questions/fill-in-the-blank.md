# Microeconomics — one buyer, one firm, one market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Microeconomics studies _____ decision-making units — a household, a firm, an industry.
**Answer: single**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Microeconomics' main tools are the _____ of particular goods and factors.
**Answer: demand and supply**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The wider family of branches includes development, environmental, labour, monetary and _____ economics.
**Answer: health**
