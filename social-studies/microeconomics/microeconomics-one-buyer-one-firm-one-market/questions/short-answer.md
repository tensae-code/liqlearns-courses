# Microeconomics — one buyer, one firm, one market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The single units" in your own words.
**Answer:** Microeconomics studies single decision-making units such as a household, a firm or an industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Its questions and tools" in your own words.
**Answer:** Microeconomics' fundamental problem is resource allocation, and its tools are the demand and supply of particular goods and factors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The wider family of branches" in your own words.
**Answer:** Newer branches around micro and macro include development, environmental, labour, monetary and health economics.
