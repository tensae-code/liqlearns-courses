# Hands-on — Consumer equilibrium and the demand curve

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
With 22 counters as birr, buy bread and injera cards at Birr 2 and Birr 4. Try three different baskets, score each with the lesson's utility figures, and show that 3 bread and 4 injera wins.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the marginal utility curve relabelled as a demand curve, beside a Birr 22 basket of bread and injera — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
