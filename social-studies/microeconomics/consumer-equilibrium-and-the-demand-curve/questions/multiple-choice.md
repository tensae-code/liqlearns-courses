# Consumer equilibrium and the demand curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "One good — where MU equals price"?
a) equilibrium where marginal utility equals price, MUx = Px  b) MU per birr equal across all goods — 3 bread and 4 injera for 40 utils  c) the demand curve traced out as price falls and the MU-equals-price quantity rises
**Answer: a) equilibrium where marginal utility equals price, MUx = Px**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Many goods — equal MU per birr"?
a) equilibrium where marginal utility equals price, MUx = Px  b) the demand curve traced out as price falls and the MU-equals-price quantity rises  c) MU per birr equal across all goods — 3 bread and 4 injera for 40 utils
**Answer: c) MU per birr equal across all goods — 3 bread and 4 injera for 40 utils**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The demand curve derived"?
a) MU per birr equal across all goods — 3 bread and 4 injera for 40 utils  b) the demand curve traced out as price falls and the MU-equals-price quantity rises  c) equilibrium where marginal utility equals price, MUx = Px
**Answer: b) the demand curve traced out as price falls and the MU-equals-price quantity rises**
