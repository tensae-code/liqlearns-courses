# Consumer equilibrium and the demand curve — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "One good — where MU equals price" in your own words.
**Answer:** For one good, the consumer maximises satisfaction where marginal utility equals price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Many goods — equal MU per birr" in your own words.
**Answer:** With many goods, satisfaction is maximised when marginal utility per birr is equal across all goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The demand curve derived" in your own words.
**Answer:** As price falls, the quantity at which marginal utility equals the new price rises, tracing out the demand curve.
