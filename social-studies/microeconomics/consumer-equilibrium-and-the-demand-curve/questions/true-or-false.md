# Consumer equilibrium and the demand curve — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For one good, the consumer maximises satisfaction where marginal utility equals price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With many goods, satisfaction is maximised when marginal utility per birr is equal across all goods.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** As price falls, the quantity at which marginal utility equals the new price rises, tracing out the demand curve.
**Answer: True**
