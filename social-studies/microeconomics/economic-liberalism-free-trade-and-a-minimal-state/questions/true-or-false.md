# Economic liberalism — free trade and a minimal state — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Librecambismo was a doctrine of state control of trade.
**Answer: False** — the book defines it as liberalism — the free-market doctrine.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Liberalism proposes state planning as the best form of development.
**Answer: False** — it proposes the free market and private initiative.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The free-traders opposed private initiative.
**Answer: False** — librecambismo is liberalism — private initiative is its core.
