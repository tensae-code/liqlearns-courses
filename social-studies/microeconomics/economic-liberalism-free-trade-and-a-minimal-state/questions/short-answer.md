# Economic liberalism — free trade and a minimal state — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about librecambismo is liberalism?
**Answer:** What Bolivian history called librecambismo was liberalism, the economic doctrine of the free market.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about free market and private initiative?
**Answer:** Liberalism proposes the free market and private initiative as the best form of development.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the doctrine behind the trade fights?
**Answer:** The free-traders in Bolivia's trade fights were the party of economic liberalism.
