## Sub lesson 1 — Librecambismo is liberalism

What Bolivian history called librecambismo — free-trade-ism — was liberalism: the economic doctrine whose name fills the country's 19th-century trade fights, defined here in the Grade 10 student book.

## Sub lesson 2 — Free market and private initiative

The doctrine proposes the free market and private initiative as the best form of development: let buyers, sellers and owners decide, and the economy develops best.

## Sub lesson 3 — The doctrine behind the trade fights

Hold this definition and the century's fights come clear: the free-traders of the silver and quina battles were liberalism's party, the protectionists its opponents — doctrine first, tariffs after.
