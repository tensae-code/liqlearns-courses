# Hands-on — Basic needs and other needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Put real things — or cards naming them — in a row: food, water, clothing, a game, a computer picture, a picture of a trip. Sort them into two piles, basic needs and other needs, and say out loud why each one goes where it goes.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's two shelves from memory — basic needs on one, other needs on the other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the sorting there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
