# Basic needs and other needs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food, water, housing and clothing are basic needs — things we cannot do without.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Games are a basic need that a person cannot do without.
**Answer: False** — games are named as a need that is not basic; a person can do without them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches the sorting of needs through a family supermarket dialogue.
**Answer: True**
