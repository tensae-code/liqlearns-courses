# Basic needs and other needs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four basic needs.
**Answer:** Food, water, housing and clothing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three needs the book says are not basic.
**Answer:** Games, computers and tourism.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book teach the sorting of needs?
**Answer:** Through a family supermarket dialogue, sorting each thing at the shelf into basic needs and other needs, with the basics first.
