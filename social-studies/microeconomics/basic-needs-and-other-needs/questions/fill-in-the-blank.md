# Basic needs and other needs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food, water, housing and clothing are _____ needs — the things we cannot do without.
**Answer: basic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Games, computers and tourism are needs that are not _____.
**Answer: basic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The family in the book sorts its needs at the _____ shelf.
**Answer: supermarket**
