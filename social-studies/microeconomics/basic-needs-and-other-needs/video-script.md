# Video script — Basic needs and other needs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some needs are basic needs: the things we cannot do without. They are food, water, housing and clothing.

Other needs are not basic. Games, computers and tourism are needs a person can do without, even when they want them.

At the supermarket shelf, a family sorts its needs this way: the basics first, and the other needs after.
