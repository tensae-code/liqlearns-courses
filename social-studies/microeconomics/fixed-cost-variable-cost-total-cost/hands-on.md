# Hands-on — Fixed cost, variable cost, total cost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay a flat string (fixed cost) across a table and a rising string (variable cost) beside it; stack them to show TC riding parallel above TVC, and say why the gap never changes.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the Birr 60 flat line, the rising TVC curve, and TC running parallel above it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
