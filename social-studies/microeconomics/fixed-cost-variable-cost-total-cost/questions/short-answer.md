# Fixed cost, variable cost, total cost — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Fixed cost — paid even at zero" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fixed costs do not vary with output, and the worked table holds TFC at Birr 60 for every output 0 to 6.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Variable cost — zero at zero" in your own words.
**Answer:** Variable costs vary directly with output and are zero at zero output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Total cost and its graph" in your own words.
**Answer:** Total cost equals total fixed cost plus total variable cost, TC = TFC + TVC.
