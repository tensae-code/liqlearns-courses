# Fixed cost, variable cost, total cost — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Fixed cost — paid even at zero"?
a) Birr 60 at every output 0 to 6 — rent, interest, machinery wear, admin staff  b) zero at zero output, climbing to 120 — production wages and raw materials  c) TC = TFC + TVC; TC copies TVC's shape shifted up by the fixed amount
**Answer: a) Birr 60 at every output 0 to 6 — rent, interest, machinery wear, admin staff**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Variable cost — zero at zero"?
a) Birr 60 at every output 0 to 6 — rent, interest, machinery wear, admin staff  b) TC = TFC + TVC; TC copies TVC's shape shifted up by the fixed amount  c) zero at zero output, climbing to 120 — production wages and raw materials
**Answer: c) zero at zero output, climbing to 120 — production wages and raw materials**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Total cost and its graph"?
a) zero at zero output, climbing to 120 — production wages and raw materials  b) TC = TFC + TVC; TC copies TVC's shape shifted up by the fixed amount  c) Birr 60 at every output 0 to 6 — rent, interest, machinery wear, admin staff
**Answer: b) TC = TFC + TVC; TC copies TVC's shape shifted up by the fixed amount**
