# Fixed cost, variable cost, total cost — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fixed costs do not vary with output, and the worked table holds TFC at Birr 60 for every output 0 to 6.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fixed costs do not vary with output, and the worked table holds TFC at Birr 60 for every output 0 to 6.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Fixed cost — paid even at zero); sub lesson 2 says: Variable costs vary directly with output and are zero at zero output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Total cost equals total fixed cost plus total variable cost, TC = TFC + TVC.
**Answer: True**
