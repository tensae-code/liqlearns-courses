# Video script — Fixed cost, variable cost, total cost

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fixed costs do not vary with output and are incurred even at zero output — rent, interest on borrowed funds, machinery wear and administrative staff. The worked table holds total fixed cost at Birr 60 for every output from 0 to 6.

Variable costs vary directly with output and are zero at zero output — wages of production workers and raw materials. In the worked table TVC climbs from 0 to 120 as output rises to 6.

Total cost is the sum: TC = TFC + TVC. Graphed, TFC is flat, TVC rises first at a decreasing then at an increasing rate, and TC copies TVC's shape shifted up by the fixed amount — the Birr 60 rides on every point.
