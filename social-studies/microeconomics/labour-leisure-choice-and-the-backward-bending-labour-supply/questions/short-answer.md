# Labour–leisure choice and the backward-bending labour supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things does the labour–leisure constraint trade off?
**Answer:** Leisure and the consumption goods that wages buy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What mix does the worker choose?
**Answer:** The mix of leisure and goods that gives the most utility on the budget constraint.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why can supply bend backward?
**Answer:** At a high wage the worker can afford more leisure, and the income effect outweighs the pull to work more.
