# Labour–leisure choice and the backward-bending labour supply — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each hour of leisure costs the _____ that hour would have earned.
**Answer: wage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Utility comes from leisure as well as from _____.
**Answer: goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At high wages the labour supply curve can bend _____.
**Answer: backward**
