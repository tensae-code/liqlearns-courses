# Labour–leisure choice and the backward-bending labour supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An hour of leisure costs —
a) the wage that hour would have earned  b) nothing  c) the employer's profit
**Answer: a) the wage that hour would have earned**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Utility comes from —
a) leisure as well as goods  b) goods only  c) wages only
**Answer: a) leisure as well as goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A backward-bending labour supply means —
a) past a point, a higher wage brings fewer hours supplied  b) hours always rise with the wage  c) wages never affect hours
**Answer: a) past a point, a higher wage brings fewer hours supplied**
