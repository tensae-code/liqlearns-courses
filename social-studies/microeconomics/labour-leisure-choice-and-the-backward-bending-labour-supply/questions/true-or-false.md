# Labour–leisure choice and the backward-bending labour supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Leisure is free because it is not bought in a shop.
**Answer: False — leisure's cost is the forgone wage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Workers care only about the size of the wage.
**Answer: False — utility comes from leisure as well as from goods.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Higher wages always bring more hours at every wage level.
**Answer: False — at high wages the curve can bend backward.**
