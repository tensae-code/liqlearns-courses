## Sub lesson 1 — The labour–leisure budget constraint

A worker's **budget constraint** trades **leisure against the goods wages buy** — each hour of leisure costs the wage that hour would have earned.

## Sub lesson 2 — Utility from leisure and from goods

The worker seeks the best mix: **utility comes from leisure as well as from goods**, so the choice balances the two, not income alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — The backward-bending supply curve

At high wages the **supply curve of labour can bend backward** — beyond a point a higher wage leads the worker to take **more leisure and supply fewer hours**.
