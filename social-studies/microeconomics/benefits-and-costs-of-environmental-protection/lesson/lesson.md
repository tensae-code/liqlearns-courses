# Benefits and costs of environmental protection

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Weighing benefit against cost

US environmental laws are **weighed benefit against cost** — firms spend **over $200 billion a year** complying, against the health and environmental gains.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecotourism as a benefit

**Ecotourism** counts among the **benefits** — protected environments draw visitors whose spending rewards keeping nature intact.

## Sub lesson 3 — Marginal analysis of cutting pollution

**Marginal analysis** decides **how much** pollution to cut: cut while the **marginal benefit exceeds the marginal cost**, and stop where they meet.
