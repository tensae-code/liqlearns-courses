# Benefits and costs of environmental protection — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Environmental laws have benefits but no costs.
**Answer: False — compliance costs firms over $200 billion a year, weighed against the benefits.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Protecting nature can never bring economic benefit.
**Answer: False — ecotourism is one of its benefits.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The right amount of cleanup is always zero pollution at any cost.
**Answer: False — marginal analysis stops where marginal benefit meets marginal cost.**
