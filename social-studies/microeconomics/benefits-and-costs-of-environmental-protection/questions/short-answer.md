# Benefits and costs of environmental protection — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two sides are weighed for US environmental laws?
**Answer:** Their benefits against their costs, including over $200 billion a year in firm compliance costs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is ecotourism a benefit?
**Answer:** Visitors' spending rewards keeping protected environments intact.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does marginal analysis decide here?
**Answer:** How much pollution to cut — up to where marginal benefit equals marginal cost.
