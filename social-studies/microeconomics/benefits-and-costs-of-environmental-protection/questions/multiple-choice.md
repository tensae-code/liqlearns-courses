# Benefits and costs of environmental protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Firm compliance with US environmental laws costs —
a) over $200 billion a year  b) about $200 a year  c) nothing
**Answer: a) over $200 billion a year**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ecotourism is —
a) a benefit of environmental protection  b) a compliance cost  c) a kind of pollution
**Answer: a) a benefit of environmental protection**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cut pollution while —
a) marginal benefit exceeds marginal cost  b) any cost remains  c) marginal cost is zero
**Answer: a) marginal benefit exceeds marginal cost**
