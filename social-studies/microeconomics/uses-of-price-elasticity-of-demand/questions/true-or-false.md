# Uses of price elasticity of demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where demand is elastic, a rival's price cut steals quantity fast.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tax on an elastic good shrinks the taxed activity itself.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pricing, taxing and subsidising decisions are all bets on how quantity will respond.
**Answer: True**
