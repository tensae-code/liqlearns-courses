# Uses of price elasticity of demand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A firm uses elasticity estimates chiefly to —
a) guess prices by habit
b) predict what price changes do to revenue
c) avoid keeping accounts
**Answer: b) predict what price changes do to revenue**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For steady tax revenue, the state prefers taxing goods with —
a) elastic demand
b) inelastic demand
c) no demand
**Answer: b) inelastic demand**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elasticity's usefulness rests on it measuring —
a) how strongly quantity responds
b) the size of a firm
c) the number of sellers
**Answer: a) how strongly quantity responds**
