# Uses of price elasticity of demand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Firms use elasticity to price _____ instead of by habit.
**Answer: deliberately**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tax on an inelastic good raises steady _____ because quantity barely falls.
**Answer: revenue**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elasticity is a decision _____, not just a label.
