# Uses of price elasticity of demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two firm uses of elasticity from the lesson.
**Answer:** Predicting a price change's effect on revenue, and judging where price competition is dangerous / where making demand less elastic pays.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does elasticity guide the state's tax choice?
**Answer:** Inelastic goods yield steady revenue since quantity barely falls; elastic goods shrink under tax, yielding less and changing behaviour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What single mechanism do all the uses share?
**Answer:** Each decision bets on how strongly quantity demanded will respond — which is what elasticity measures.
