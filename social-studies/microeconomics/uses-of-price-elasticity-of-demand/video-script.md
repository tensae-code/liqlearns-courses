# Video script — Uses of price elasticity of demand

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook ends the elasticity chapters with their usefulness. A firm that knows its demand's elasticity knows what a price change does to its revenue — the previous lesson's rule — and can price deliberately instead of by habit.

The state uses elasticity too. A tax on a good with inelastic demand raises steady revenue because quantity barely falls; a tax on an elastic good shrinks the taxed activity itself.

The lesson's closing point: elasticity is not a number to memorise but a tool that firms and the state actually decide with — pricing, taxing, subsidising.
