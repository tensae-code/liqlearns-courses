Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG): Economics enters at Grade 3 as a "Trade" strand built from village life — needs and wants vary by place (a canoe is a need on the coast, not in the highlands), production is taught through making a food garden, and trade starts with traditional exchange and traditional money before modern money and EFTPOS. This lesson is built from that book's placement. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 4 Social Science TG): Grade 4 Trade is organised around meeting needs through exchange — barter role-plays in a pretend store, cash crops (coffee, cocoa, copra, vanilla) as PNG's revenue earners, and transport (including the Hiri trading voyages) as what makes trade possible.
