# Needs and wants basic needs versus wants

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of one case of this lesson's subject from your own life or town and say what you already know about it. Hold your answer — the lesson takes its subject in three parts, in order.

In Papua New Guinea's book (Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG), **Needs and Wants unit, Guided Lesson 46 "I need this but I want that"** — teaches: needs as things people must have to stay alive (water, food, house, clothes) versus wants, and that needs and wants differ by environment and group (family, community, church, orphans, NGOs). This lesson teaches that placement in the course's own words. (Teacher guide — evidence.)

## Sub lesson 1 — Needs as things people must have to stay

Needs as things people must have to stay alive (water, food, house, clothes) versus wants, and that needs and wants differ by environment and group (family, community, church, orphans, NGOs).

## Sub lesson 2 — The needs and wants of children, young people

The needs and wants of children, young people and adults, and how trade and exchange are the way people's needs get met.

## Sub lesson 3 — Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG: Economics enters at Grade 3 as a "Trade" strand built from village life — needs and wants vary by place (a canoe is a need on the coast, not in the highlands), production is taught through making a food garden, and trade starts with traditional exchange and traditional money before modern money and EFTPOS.
