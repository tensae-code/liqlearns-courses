# Needs and wants basic needs versus wants — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Needs as things people must have to stay alive (water, food, house, clothes) versus wants, and that needs and wants differ by _____ and group (family, community, church, orphans, NGOs).
**Answer: environment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The needs and wants of _____, young people and adults, and how trade and exchange are the way people's needs get met.
**Answer: children**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG: Economics enters at Grade 3 as a "Trade" strand built from village life — needs and wants vary by place (a canoe is a need on the coast, not in the highlands), production is taught through making a food garden, and trade starts with _____ exchange and traditional money before modern money and EFTPOS.
**Answer: traditional**
