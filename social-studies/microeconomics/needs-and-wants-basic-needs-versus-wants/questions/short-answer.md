# Needs and wants basic needs versus wants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Needs as things people must have to stay"?
**Answer:** Needs as things people must have to stay alive (water, food, house, clothes) versus wants, and that needs and wants differ by environment and group (family, community, church, orphans, NGOs).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "The needs and wants of children, young people"?
**Answer:** The needs and wants of children, young people and adults, and how trade and exchange are the way people's needs get met.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG: Economics enters at Grade 3 as a "Trade" strand built from village life — needs and wants vary by place (a canoe is a need on the coast, not in the highlands), production is taught through making a food garden, and trade starts with traditional exchange and traditional money before modern money and EFTPOS.
