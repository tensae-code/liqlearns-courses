# Video script — Types of demand

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Types of demand — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: demand comes in different kinds, distinguished by what drives it and what the good is wanted for.; some demand is direct, for the good itself; other demand is derived, because the good is needed to make something else.; goods can be demanded jointly, together with other goods, or for several purposes at once — composite demand..
