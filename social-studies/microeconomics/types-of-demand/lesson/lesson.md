# Types of demand

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Kinds of demand distinguished

Demand comes in different kinds, distinguished by what drives it and what the good is wanted for.

## Sub lesson 2 — Direct and derived demand

Some demand is direct, for the good itself; other demand is derived, because the good is needed to make something else.

## Sub lesson 3 — Joint and composite demand

Goods can be demanded jointly, together with other goods, or for several purposes at once — composite demand.
