# Types of demand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are the kinds of demand distinguished?
**Answer:** Demand comes in different kinds, distinguished by what drives it and what the good is wanted for.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is derived demand?
**Answer:** Some demand is direct, for the good itself; other demand is derived, because the good is needed to make something else.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is composite demand?
**Answer:** Goods can be demanded jointly, together with other goods, or for several purposes at once — composite demand.
