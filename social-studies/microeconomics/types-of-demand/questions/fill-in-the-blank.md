# Types of demand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand comes in different _____, distinguished by what drives it.
**Answer: kinds**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Demand needed to make something else is called _____ demand.
**Answer: derived**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Demand for several purposes at once is _____ demand.
**Answer: composite**
