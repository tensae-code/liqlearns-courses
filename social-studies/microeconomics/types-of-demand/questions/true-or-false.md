# Types of demand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All demand is of one single kind.
**Answer: False** — Demand comes in different kinds, distinguished by what drives it and what the good is wanted for.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Derived demand is demand for a good for its own sake.
**Answer: False** — Some demand is direct, for the good itself; other demand is derived, because the good is needed to make something else.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Composite demand means a good is wanted for one purpose only.
**Answer: False** — Goods can be demanded jointly, together with other goods, or for several purposes at once — composite demand.
