Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About kinds of demand distinguished — the lesson teaches that —
a) demand comes in different kinds, distinguished by what drives it and what the good is wanted for.  b) some demand is direct, for the good itself; other demand is derived, because the good is needed to make something else.  c) goods can be demanded jointly, together with other goods, or for several purposes at once — composite demand.
