# Cost per unit — ATC, AFC, AVC and marginal cost

## Do it first

A batch costs Birr 600 to make 100 units, of which Birr 200 was fixed. What did each unit cost in total, in fixed cost, in variable cost? Divide before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three averages

Average total cost is cost per unit, ATC = TC/Q, and it splits into average fixed cost (AFC = TFC/Q) plus average variable cost (AVC = TVC/Q) — so AFC + AVC = ATC at every output.

## Sub lesson 2 — Marginal cost

Marginal cost is the extra total cost of one more unit of output, MC = ΔTC/ΔQ — which in the short run equals ΔTVC/ΔQ, because fixed cost does not change when output grows by one.

## Sub lesson 3 — How MC steers the averages

The guide's relationship rules (teacher guide — evidence): where MC lies below ATC, ATC is falling; where MC lies above ATC, ATC is rising; the same holds against AVC; MC cuts both ATC and AVC at their minimum points, while rising; and ATC sits above AVC at every output because ATC = AVC + AFC.
