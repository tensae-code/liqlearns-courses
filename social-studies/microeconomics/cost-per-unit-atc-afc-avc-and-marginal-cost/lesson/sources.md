Worked-answer error corrected here, named only in this file: the teacher guide's answer block misdefines AVC as total cost divided by quantity; AVC is total VARIABLE cost divided by quantity (AVC = TVC/Q). This lesson teaches the corrected definition, and the check AFC + AVC = ATC (4 + 6 = 10 in the Do It Yourself) only balances with the corrected formula.

Pairing note carried from the placements: the student textbook is the finished 2023 edition, while the teacher guide is ©2022 with an unfinished placeholder printing block — edition-mismatched companions; the guide's answer keys are partly incomplete/misaligned with this book's review questions, and no answer-key figure is taught here unchecked.

Ecuador's own way (Ecuador Grade 13 Emprendimiento y Gestión): Economics enters the entrepreneurship course through the family and the small venture — costs are worked through a school-desk factory costed month by month in dollars, and every unit closes with a financial-education section that takes the same tools home to the family budget and the family's debts. The unit cost is built differently for each kind of firm — industrial, commercial, services and agricultural each count their own components.
