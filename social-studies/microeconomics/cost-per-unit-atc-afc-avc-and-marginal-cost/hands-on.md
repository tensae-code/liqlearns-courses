# Hands-on — Cost per unit — ATC, AFC, AVC and marginal cost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Slice a paper 'total cost' strip into a fixed slice and a variable slice for one unit; then add a '+1 unit' strip and price only the extra — that extra is MC.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one unit of output with its total cost being divided into a fixed slice and a variable slice, and a +1 unit arrow showing the extra cost — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
