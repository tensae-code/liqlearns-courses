# Cost per unit — ATC, AFC, AVC and marginal cost — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Average total cost is ATC = _____, and it splits into AFC plus AVC.
**Answer: TC/Q**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marginal cost is the _____ total cost of one more unit of output.
**Answer: extra**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** MC cuts both ATC and AVC at their _____ points, while rising.
**Answer: minimum**
