# Cost per unit — ATC, AFC, AVC and marginal cost — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The three averages" in your own words.
**Answer:** Average total cost is TC divided by Q, and AFC + AVC = ATC.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Marginal cost" in your own words.
**Answer:** Marginal cost is the extra total cost of one more unit, MC = ΔTC/ΔQ.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "How MC steers the averages" in your own words.
**Answer:** Where MC lies below ATC, ATC is falling; where MC lies above ATC, ATC is rising, and MC cuts ATC and AVC at their minimum points.
