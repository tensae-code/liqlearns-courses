# Cost per unit — ATC, AFC, AVC and marginal cost — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The three averages"?
a) ATC = TC/Q, splitting into AFC = TFC/Q plus AVC = TVC/Q  b) MC = ΔTC/ΔQ, equal to ΔTVC/ΔQ in the short run  c) MC below — the average falls; MC above — it rises; MC cuts both at their minimum
**Answer: a) ATC = TC/Q, splitting into AFC = TFC/Q plus AVC = TVC/Q**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Marginal cost"?
a) ATC = TC/Q, splitting into AFC = TFC/Q plus AVC = TVC/Q  b) MC below — the average falls; MC above — it rises; MC cuts both at their minimum  c) MC = ΔTC/ΔQ, equal to ΔTVC/ΔQ in the short run
**Answer: c) MC = ΔTC/ΔQ, equal to ΔTVC/ΔQ in the short run**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "How MC steers the averages"?
a) MC = ΔTC/ΔQ, equal to ΔTVC/ΔQ in the short run  b) MC below — the average falls; MC above — it rises; MC cuts both at their minimum  c) ATC = TC/Q, splitting into AFC = TFC/Q plus AVC = TVC/Q
**Answer: b) MC below — the average falls; MC above — it rises; MC cuts both at their minimum**
