# Cost per unit — ATC, AFC, AVC and marginal cost — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Average total cost is TC divided by Q, and AFC + AVC = ATC.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Average total cost is TC divided by Q, and AFC + AVC = ATC.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The three averages); sub lesson 2 says: Marginal cost is the extra total cost of one more unit, MC = ΔTC/ΔQ.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where MC lies below ATC, ATC is falling; where MC lies above ATC, ATC is rising, and MC cuts ATC and AVC at their minimum points.
**Answer: True**
