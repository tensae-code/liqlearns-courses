# The kibbutz communal work and equal pay — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Israel's kibbutz as a contrasting economic system — a voluntary society of communal property
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about israel's kibbutz as a contrasting economic system a.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about production and work where every member is paid; israel's kibbutz as a contrasting economic system a is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Food/housing/health/education are free, visitors get food and lodging in exchange for voluntary work, and the kibbutz sells most of its farm produce abroad
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
