# The kibbutz communal work and equal pay — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about israel's kibbutz as a contrasting economic system a?
**Answer:** Israel's kibbutz as a contrasting economic system — a voluntary society of communal property

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about production and work where every member is paid?
**Answer:** Production and work where every member is paid the same whatever their job

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about foodhousinghealtheducation are free visitors get food and lodging?
**Answer:** Food/housing/health/education are free, visitors get food and lodging in exchange for voluntary work, and the kibbutz sells most of its farm produce abroad
