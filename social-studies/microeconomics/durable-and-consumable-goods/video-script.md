# Video script — Durable and consumable goods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some goods stay with you for years. Some are gone the day you use them. Jordan's Grade 3 book teaches the split inside its Smart Consumer lesson.

Durable goods last a long time and are used continuously: electrical appliances, furniture, cars, phones. When they break, you repair them; you replace them only after long periods.

Consumable goods are used once, or for a short time: fuel, foods, medicines. Using them is what finishes them.

One question tells them apart: after I use it, is it still there?
