# Hands-on — Durable and consumable goods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort your home
Walk through your home and list ten goods. Mark each D (durable — it lasts and is used again and again) or C (consumable — using it finishes it). Check your list against lesson/lesson.md.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two shelves: a durable shelf and a consumable shelf. Put four goods on each, from the lesson's lists. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every shelf you filled wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
