# Durable and consumable goods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods that last a long time and are used continuously are called _____ goods.
**Answer: durable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Goods that are used once or for a short time are called _____ goods.
**Answer: consumable**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A durable good that breaks can be _____, and is replaced only after a long period.
**Answer: repaired**
