# Durable and consumable goods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the one question that tells a durable good from a consumable good?
**Answer:** After I use it, is it still there? If it is still there serving you again, it is durable; if using it finishes it, it is consumable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two durable goods and two consumable goods from the book.
**Answer:** Durable: any two of electrical appliances, furniture, cars, phones. Consumable: any two of fuel, foods, medicines.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does a family plan differently for the two kinds of goods?
**Answer:** Consumable goods must be bought again and again, so money for them must be there every week; a durable good is one big choice made rarely, judged by how long it will last and whether it can be repaired.
