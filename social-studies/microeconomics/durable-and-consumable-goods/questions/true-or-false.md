# Durable and consumable goods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foods and medicines are consumable goods, because using them finishes them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A car is a consumable good, because it uses fuel.
**Answer: False — the car is a durable good: it lasts a long time and is used continuously. The fuel it burns is the consumable good.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Electrical appliances and phones are durable goods.
**Answer: True**
