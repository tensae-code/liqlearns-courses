# Durable and consumable goods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A durable good is —
a) used once and finished  b) a good that lasts a long time and is used continuously, like furniture or a car  c) any good that is free
**Answer: b) a good that lasts a long time and is used continuously, like furniture or a car**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a consumable good?
a) fuel  b) a phone  c) a table
**Answer: a) fuel**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When a durable good breaks, the book says it —
a) is thrown away the same day  b) can be repaired, and is replaced only after a long period  c) turns into a consumable good
**Answer: b) can be repaired, and is replaced only after a long period**
