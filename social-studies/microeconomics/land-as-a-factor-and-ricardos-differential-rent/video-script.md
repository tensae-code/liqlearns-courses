# Video script — Land as a factor — and Ricardo's differential rent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Land, in economics, means all natural resources — not just fields, but everything nature provides for production. Land has two marks: its area is fixed — nobody can make more of it — and its fertility differs from plot to plot.

Ricardo explained rent from that difference. The better land yields more for the same work, and that extra yield is its differential rent: rent exists because land is not all alike, and the best land is scarce.
