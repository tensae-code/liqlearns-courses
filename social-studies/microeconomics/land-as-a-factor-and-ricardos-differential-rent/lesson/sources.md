**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Yemen).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 3, Lesson 1 — Land — teaches: Land, as a factor of production, is all natural resources: its area is fixed and its fertility differs from plot to plot — and Ricardo's differential rent theory explains rent from exactly that difference in fertility. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
