# Land as a factor — and Ricardo's differential rent

## Do it first

In Yemen's Grade 11 economics book (Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad), **Unit 3, Lesson 1 — Land** — takes the first element of production on its own: **land**.

## Sub lesson 1 — Land — all natural resources

In economics, **land** is wider than a field: it is **all natural resources** — the soil, and everything nature provides that production uses.

Land is the first element of production because every producing starts from something nature supplied: a place to stand, a material to work, a force to harness.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fixed area, differing fertility

Land has two marks. Its **area is fixed**: the supply of land cannot be increased by human effort — what exists is what there is.

And its **fertility differs**: plot by plot, land yields differently for the same work — one field is rich, the next poor. That difference, not the sameness, is what the rent theory is built on.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ricardo's differential rent

**Ricardo's differential rent theory** explains rent by the difference in fertility. Where better and worse land are both farmed, the better land yields an extra over the worst — and that extra is claimed as **rent** for the better land.

Rent, on this account, is not a payment for land in general: it is the differential — the advantage of the better plot over the poorer one worked at the same time.
