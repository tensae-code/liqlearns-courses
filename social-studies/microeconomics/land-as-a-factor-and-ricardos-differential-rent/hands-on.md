# Hands-on — Land as a factor — and Ricardo's differential rent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with three plots
Draw three plots and label them rich, middling and poor. The same seed and work go into each. Write the harvest you would expect from each, and mark the extra of the best plot as its differential rent. Say it aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — three fields side by side — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
