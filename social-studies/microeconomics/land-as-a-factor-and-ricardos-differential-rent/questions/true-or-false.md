# Land as a factor — and Ricardo's differential rent — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** More land can always be made when it is needed.
**Answer: False — land's area is fixed; its supply cannot be increased by human effort.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** All land yields the same for the same work.
**Answer: False — fertility differs from plot to plot.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Ricardo's theory, rent is the extra the better land yields over the poorer land.
**Answer: True**
