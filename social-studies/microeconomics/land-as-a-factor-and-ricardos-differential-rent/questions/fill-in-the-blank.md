# Land as a factor — and Ricardo's differential rent — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land as a factor is all _____ resources.
**Answer: natural**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The _____ of land differs from plot to plot.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ rent theory explains rent by the difference in fertility.
