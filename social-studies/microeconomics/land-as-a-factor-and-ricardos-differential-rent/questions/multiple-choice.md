# Land as a factor — and Ricardo's differential rent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In economics, 'land' means —
a) only farm fields  b) all natural resources  c) only building plots
**Answer: b) all natural resources**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Land's area is —
a) fixed  b) growing every year  c) set by the market
**Answer: a) fixed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ricardo's differential rent arises from —
a) differences in fertility between plots  b) the size of the farmhouse  c) the price of seed
**Answer: a) differences in fertility between plots**
