# Land as a factor — and Ricardo's differential rent — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is land called the first element of production here?
**Answer:** Because every producing starts from something nature supplied — a place, a material, a force.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State land's two marks.
**Answer:** Its area is fixed, and its fertility differs from plot to plot.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain differential rent in one sentence.
**Answer:** Rent is the extra yield the better land gives over the poorer land farmed at the same time, claimed for the better plot.
