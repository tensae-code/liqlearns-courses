# Video script — Traditional and modern agriculture in Nepal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal's two farming systems stand side by side: the traditional farm grows to live, the modern farm grows to sell.

The traditional farm is subsistence-oriented, working old methods with household manure and local seed and content with what it grows.

The modern commercial farm tests soil, uses tractors and improved seed, takes cooperative loans, subsidies and crop insurance and produces for the market.

Set side by side, the two systems differ in aim: one grows to live, the other grows to sell.
