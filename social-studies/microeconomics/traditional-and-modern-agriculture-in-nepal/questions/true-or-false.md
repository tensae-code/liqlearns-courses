# Traditional and modern agriculture in Nepal — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The traditional farm is subsistence-oriented, working old methods with household manure and local seed and content with what it grows.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The modern commercial farm tests soil, uses tractors and improved seed, takes cooperative loans, subsidies and crop insurance and produces for the market.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Set side by side, the two systems differ in aim: one grows to live, the other something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — Set side by side, the two systems differ in aim: one grows to live, the other grows to sell.
