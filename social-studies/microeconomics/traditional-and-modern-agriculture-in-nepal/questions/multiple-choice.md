# Traditional and modern agriculture in Nepal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, “The traditional farm” turns on —
a) household manure and local seed  b) produces for the market  c) grows to sell
**Answer: a) household manure and local seed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In this lesson, “The modern commercial farm” turns on —
a) produces for the market  b) grows to sell  c) household manure and local seed
**Answer: a) produces for the market**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In this lesson, “The two systems side by side” turns on —
a) grows to sell  b) household manure and local seed  c) produces for the market
**Answer: a) grows to sell**
