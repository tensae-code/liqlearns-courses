# Traditional and modern agriculture in Nepal — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The traditional farm is subsistence-oriented, working old methods with _____ and content with what it grows.
**Answer: household manure and local seed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The modern commercial farm tests soil, uses tractors and improved seed, takes cooperative loans, subsidies and crop insurance and _____.
**Answer: produces for the market**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Set side by side, the two systems differ in aim: one grows to live, the other _____.
**Answer: grows to sell**
