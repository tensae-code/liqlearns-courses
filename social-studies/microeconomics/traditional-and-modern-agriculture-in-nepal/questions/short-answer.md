# Traditional and modern agriculture in Nepal — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the traditional farm?
**Answer:** The traditional farm is subsistence-oriented, working old methods with household manure and local seed and content with what it grows.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the modern commercial farm?
**Answer:** The modern commercial farm tests soil, uses tractors and improved seed, takes cooperative loans, subsidies and crop insurance and produces for the market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the two systems side by side?
**Answer:** Set side by side, the two systems differ in aim: one grows to live, the other grows to sell.
