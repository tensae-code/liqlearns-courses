# Producer and consumer behaviour in a capitalist economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "How exchange works in a capitalist economy and"?
**Answer:** How exchange works in a capitalist economy and the behaviour patterns of producers and consumers within it, evaluated.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG: Grade 10 Economics is the labour and capitalism year — two full units on labour (unions, division of labour, labour law, labour costs), then the market system judged by its costs and benefits, trade through television and the internet, and a closing unit interrogating the capitalist ideology itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Putting it together: how exchange works in a"?
**Answer:** Putting it together: how exchange works in a capitalist economy and the behaviour patterns of producers and consumers within it, evaluated — taught in Unit 5, Topics 6 and 7: Exchanging goods and services in a capitalist economy; Producers and consumers behaviour in the capitalist state (Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG, printed p. 246).
