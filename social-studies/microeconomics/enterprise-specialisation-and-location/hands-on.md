# Hands-on — Enterprise specialisation and location

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your own map
List the enterprises of your settlement. For each, name its specialisation and the resource it secures nearby — or say what it must bring in from far away.

## Place the enterprise
On a blank map with a forest, a dam and a coal deposit marked, place a timber enterprise, a fish-breeding enterprise, a hydroelectric plant, a mine and a thermal plant. Defend each placing with the book's rule.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — continuously and cheaply — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
