# Enterprise specialisation and location

## Do it first

Think of one factory or workshop you know. Before you read on, write what it makes — and guess why it stands where it stands.

## Sub lesson 1 — Enterprises specialise — by kinds of goods and services

Bulgaria's Grade 6 book teaches that the **division and specialisation of labour by kinds of goods and services is a way of raising their efficiency**: instead of making everything, **every enterprise specialises in producing products** of one kind — the kind it is set up to produce well.

## Sub lesson 2 — Specialise where the resources can be secured — continuously and cheaply

The book gives the rule behind the choice: an enterprise specialises in products **for which it can secure the needed resources — raw materials, materials, energy, trained workers — continuously and cheaply**. A specialisation that cannot be fed, week after week and at a workable price, cannot last.

## Sub lesson 3 — Where enterprises arise — forests, dams and coal

The book then reads the map with that rule: **near the forests arise timber-felling and wood-processing enterprises, near the dams fish-breeding enterprises and hydroelectric plants, near the coal deposits mines and thermal power plants**. The enterprise sits where its resources are, because that is where they are secured most continuously and cheaply.
