# Lesson plan — Enterprise specialisation and location

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Microeconomics · **Lesson:** Enterprise specialisation and location
**Place in the curriculum:** Microeconomics (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Bulgaria, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Every enterprise specialises in producing the products for which it can secure the needed resources — raw materials, materials, energy, trained workers — continuously and cheaply, and that is why enterprises arise where those resources are: timber near forests, fish-breeding and hydro power near dams, mines and thermal plants near coal.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Bulgaria's Grade 6 "People's labour" lesson, beside its pair on the division of labour.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Enterprises specialise — by kinds of goods and services**
2. **Specialise where the resources can be secured — continuously and cheaply**
3. **Where enterprises arise — forests, dams and coal**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
