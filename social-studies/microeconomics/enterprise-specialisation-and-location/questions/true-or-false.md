# Enterprise specialisation and location — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The resources an enterprise must secure include raw materials, materials, energy and trained workers.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Near the coal deposits arise fish-breeding enterprises.
**Answer: False — near the coal deposits arise mines and thermal power plants; fish-breeding enterprises arise near the dams.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Specialisation means each enterprise produces every kind of product itself.
**Answer: False — specialisation means each enterprise produces the products of one kind, the kind it can resource well.**
