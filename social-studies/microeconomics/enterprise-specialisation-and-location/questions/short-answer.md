# Enterprise specialisation and location — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What four resources must an enterprise secure for its specialisation?
**Answer:** Raw materials, materials, energy and trained workers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's three location examples and the resource behind each.
**Answer:** Timber-felling and wood-processing near the forests (wood); fish-breeding enterprises and hydroelectric plants near the dams (water and its energy); mines and thermal power plants near the coal deposits (coal).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does an enterprise arise where its resources are?
**Answer:** Because there the resources it needs — raw materials, materials, energy, trained workers — can be secured continuously and cheaply, and specialisation on that basis raises efficiency.
