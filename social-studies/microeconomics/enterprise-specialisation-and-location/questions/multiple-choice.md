# Enterprise specialisation and location — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An enterprise specialises in products for which it can secure —
a) any resources at any price  b) the needed resources continuously and cheaply  c) only trained workers
**Answer: b) the needed resources continuously and cheaply**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Near the dams arise —
a) timber enterprises  b) fish-breeding enterprises and hydroelectric plants  c) mines
**Answer: b) fish-breeding enterprises and hydroelectric plants**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Specialisation by kinds of goods and services is a way of —
a) raising efficiency  b) avoiding work  c) ending trade
**Answer: a) raising efficiency**
