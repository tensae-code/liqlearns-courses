# Video script — Enterprise specialisation and location

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every enterprise specialises — it makes the products for which it can secure raw materials, materials, energy and trained workers, continuously and cheaply. That is why the map looks as it does: timber enterprises near the forests, fish-breeding and hydroelectric plants near the dams, mines and thermal power plants near the coal.
