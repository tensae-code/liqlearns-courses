# Hands-on — Small enterprises — strengths, weaknesses and competition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's own activity
Take one small enterprise you know — a shop, a workshop, a farm business. List its strengths in one column (how fast it can change, how closely it manages its people, how easily it renews its tools) and its difficulties in another (capital, market information, skills). Then say which rival — same industry or larger, home or abroad — presses it hardest, and why.
