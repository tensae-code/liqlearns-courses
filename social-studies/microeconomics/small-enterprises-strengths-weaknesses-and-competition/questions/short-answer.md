# Small enterprises — strengths, weaknesses and competition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two strengths of small enterprises.
**Answer:** Any two of: quickly changing product designs to meet market demand, closely managing labour, easily renewing technology.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two difficulties of small enterprises.
**Answer:** Any two of: little capital that is hard to borrow, lack of market information, low worker and management skill.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are small enterprises at risk of bankruptcy?
**Answer:** Because their competitiveness is limited against firms in the same industry and larger firms at home and abroad.
