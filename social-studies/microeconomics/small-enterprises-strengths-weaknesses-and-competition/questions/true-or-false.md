# Small enterprises — strengths, weaknesses and competition — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Small enterprises can closely manage labour.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Small enterprises never lack market information.
**Answer: False — lack of market information is one of their difficulties.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Limited competitiveness leaves small enterprises at risk of bankruptcy.
**Answer: True**
