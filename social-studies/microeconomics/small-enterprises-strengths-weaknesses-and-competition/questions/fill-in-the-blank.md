# Small enterprises — strengths, weaknesses and competition — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Small enterprises are _____ — quick to change product designs to meet market demand.
**Answer: flexible**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Little capital that is hard to _____ is a difficulty of small enterprises.
**Answer: borrow**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Against larger firms, a small enterprise's competitiveness is _____.
**Answer: limited**
