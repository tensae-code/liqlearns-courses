# Small enterprises — strengths, weaknesses and competition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A strength of a small enterprise is that it can —
a) change product designs quickly  b) borrow unlimited capital  c) ignore market demand
**Answer: a) change product designs quickly**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A difficulty small enterprises face with capital is that it is —
a) little and hard to borrow  b) always free  c) supplied by customers
**Answer: a) little and hard to borrow**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Small enterprises compete weakly against —
a) only their own workers  b) larger firms at home and abroad  c) nobody at all
**Answer: b) larger firms at home and abroad**
