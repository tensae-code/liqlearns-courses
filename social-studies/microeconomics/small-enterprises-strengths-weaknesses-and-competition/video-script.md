# Video script — Small enterprises — strengths, weaknesses and competition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam's Grade 10 study-topics book weighs the small enterprise honestly: its strengths on one side, its difficulties on the other.

The strengths are flexibility — product designs changed quickly for the market, labour managed closely, technology renewed easily.

The difficulties are little capital that is hard to borrow, thin market information and low skill — and against bigger firms at home and abroad, that leaves the small enterprise at risk of bankruptcy.
