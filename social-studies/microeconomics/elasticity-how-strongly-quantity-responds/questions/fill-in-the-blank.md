# Elasticity — how strongly quantity responds — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elasticity is built from _____ changes, so goods can be compared fairly.
**Answer: percentage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A price rise of _____ means the same thing to a matchbox and to a car — that is why percentages are used.
**Answer: 10%**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** All the elasticity measures share one skeleton: quantity's _____ to a change.
**Answer: responsiveness**
