# Elasticity — how strongly quantity responds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Responsiveness, measured" in your own words.
**Answer:** Elasticity compares the percentage change in quantity with the percentage change in its determinant.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why percentages, not units" in your own words.
**Answer:** Elasticity uses percentages because a birr change means different things for cheap and dear goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The elasticity block ahead" in your own words.
**Answer:** Price, cross, income and supply elasticity all share the same responsiveness skeleton.
