# Elasticity — how strongly quantity responds — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elasticity compares the percentage change in quantity with the percentage change in its determinant.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Elasticity uses percentages because a birr change means different things for cheap and dear goods.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Price, cross, income and supply elasticity all share the same responsiveness skeleton.
**Answer: True**
