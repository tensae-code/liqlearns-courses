# Hands-on — Elasticity — how strongly quantity responds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Stretch a real rubber band and a piece of string side by side with the same small pull. Use the two stretches to explain elastic and inelastic to a partner, with one market example each.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a rubber band stretched a little and a lot — weak and strong responses to the same pull — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
