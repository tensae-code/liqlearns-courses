# The mixed economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Two systems combined" in your own words.
**Answer:** A mixed economy combines capitalism and socialism, with private and public ownership coexisting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Who owns what" in your own words.
**Answer:** In a mixed economy, strategic industries like heavy industry, defence and power are public, while consumer-goods and small-scale industry are private.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Property with rules" in your own words.
**Answer:** A mixed economy allows private property but has rules to prevent wealth concentrating, with equal pay for equal work.
