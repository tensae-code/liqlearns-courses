# The mixed economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Two systems combined"?
a) a combination of capitalism and socialism, with ownership coexisting  b) strategic industries public; consumer goods and small industry private  c) private property allowed but rules prevent wealth concentrating; equal pay for equal work
**Answer: a) a combination of capitalism and socialism, with ownership coexisting**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Who owns what"?
a) a combination of capitalism and socialism, with ownership coexisting  b) private property allowed but rules prevent wealth concentrating; equal pay for equal work  c) strategic industries public; consumer goods and small industry private
**Answer: c) strategic industries public; consumer goods and small industry private**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Property with rules"?
a) strategic industries public; consumer goods and small industry private  b) private property allowed but rules prevent wealth concentrating; equal pay for equal work  c) a combination of capitalism and socialism, with ownership coexisting
**Answer: b) private property allowed but rules prevent wealth concentrating; equal pay for equal work**
