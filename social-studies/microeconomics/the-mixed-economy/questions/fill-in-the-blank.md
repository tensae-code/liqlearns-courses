# The mixed economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a mixed economy private and public ownership _____.
**Answer: coexist**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a mixed economy, heavy industry, defence and power sit in the _____ sector.
**Answer: public**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A mixed economy pairs private property with rules against wealth concentrating and _____ for equal work.
**Answer: equal pay**
