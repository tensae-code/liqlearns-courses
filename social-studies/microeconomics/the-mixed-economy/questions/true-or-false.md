# The mixed economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mixed economy combines capitalism and socialism, with private and public ownership coexisting.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A mixed economy combines capitalism and socialism, with private and public ownership coexisting.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Two systems combined); sub lesson 2 says: In a mixed economy, strategic industries like heavy industry, defence and power are public, while consumer-goods and small-scale industry are private.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A mixed economy allows private property but has rules to prevent wealth concentrating, with equal pay for equal work.
**Answer: True**
