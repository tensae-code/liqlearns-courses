# Hands-on — The mixed economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Sort ten enterprise cards (power plant, bakery, defence factory, café, farm, bus company…) into public, private or either, and defend each sort with the mixed economy's rule.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one street split between a state power plant and private shops, with a planning arrow over both — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
