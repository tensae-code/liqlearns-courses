# The mixed economy

## Do it first

One street: a state power plant at one end, private shops along the rest, and a town plan that coordinates both. Which single system is this — capitalist, command, or neither alone? Say your answer first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two systems combined

A mixed economy is a combination of capitalism and socialism: private and public ownership coexist in one economy. Neither pure system runs it — each sector covers what the other handles badly.

[Picture 24020 — one street split between a state power plant and private shops, with a planning arrow over both]

## Sub lesson 2 — Who owns what

Industries of national and strategic importance — heavy industry, defence, power — sit in the public sector, while consumer-goods and small-scale industry sit in the private sector. Government uses economic planning for coordinated development.

## Sub lesson 3 — Property with rules

Private property is allowed, but rules prevent wealth concentrating, with equal pay for equal work regardless of personal characteristics. The book ends by asking which system best fits Ethiopia today — the application question is part of the teaching.
