# Fishing family shares and seasonal borrowing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The catch is split into —
a) four shares  b) two shares  c) ten shares
**Answer: a) four shares**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One share goes to —
a) the boat and engine  b) the trader's house  c) the market tax only
**Answer: a) the boat and engine**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Lean monsoon months are survived by —
a) borrowing from the trader  b) extra fishing  c) selling the boat
**Answer: a) borrowing from the trader**
