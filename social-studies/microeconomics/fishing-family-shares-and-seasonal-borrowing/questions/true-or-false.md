# Fishing family shares and seasonal borrowing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The fishing family keeps its catch income undivided.
**Answer: False — it is split into four shares.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The boat and engine get no share of the catch.
**Answer: False — one of the four shares is theirs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The family fishes most in the monsoon lean months.
**Answer: False — those are lean months, survived by borrowing.**
