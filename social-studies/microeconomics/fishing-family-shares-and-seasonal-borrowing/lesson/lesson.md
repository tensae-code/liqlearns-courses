# Fishing family shares and seasonal borrowing

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The catch split into four shares

A fishing family splits its catch income into **four shares** — the family's earnings are shared out by rule, not kept whole.

## Sub lesson 2 — One share for the boat and engine

One of the four shares is **for the boat and engine** — the capital that makes the catch possible earns its share like a worker.

## Sub lesson 3 — Monsoon lean months survived by borrowing from the trader

In the **monsoon lean months**, when fishing stops, the family survives by **borrowing from the trader** — seasonal borrowing tied to the fishing year.
