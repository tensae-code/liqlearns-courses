# Sharecropping and token wages on the latifundio — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Export production happened in —
a) great latifundios, while subsistence production happened in conucos and minifundios  b) factories in the cities  c) cooperatives only
**Answer: a) great latifundios, while subsistence production happened in conucos and minifundios**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sharecroppers (aparceros) paid landowners —
a) with part of the harvest  b) with money wages they received  c) with imported goods
**Answer: a) with part of the harvest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Peons were generally paid in —
a) tokens (fichas) accepted only in the landowner's own store  b) gold coins  c) banknotes usable anywhere
**Answer: a) tokens (fichas) accepted only in the landowner's own store**
