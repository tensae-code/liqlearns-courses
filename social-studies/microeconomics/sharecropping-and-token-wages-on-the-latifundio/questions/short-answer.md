# Sharecropping and token wages on the latifundio — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which production served exports and which served subsistence?
**Answer:** Export production in great latifundios; subsistence production in conucos and minifundios — both with backward techniques.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did aparceros pay for the land they worked?
**Answer:** With part of the harvest they produced on it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are fichas a trap for the peon who is paid in them?
**Answer:** Because they are accepted only in the landowner's own store — the miserable wage can be spent only where the owner sells, so it returns to him.
