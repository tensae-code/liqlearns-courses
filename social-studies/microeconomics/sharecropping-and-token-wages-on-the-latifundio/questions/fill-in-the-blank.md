# Sharecropping and token wages on the latifundio — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsistence production happened in conucos and _____.
**Answer: minifundios**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sharecropper in Spanish is an _____.
**Answer: aparcero**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wage tokens accepted only at the owner's store are called _____.
**Answer: fichas**
