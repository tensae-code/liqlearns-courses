# Sharecropping and token wages on the latifundio — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The latifundios and the conucos both used backward techniques.
**Answer: True** — the book says both coexisted with backward techniques.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Aparceros owned the land they worked.
**Answer: False** — they worked the landowners' land and paid with part of the harvest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A peon could spend his fichas in any shop in the country.
**Answer: False** — they were accepted only in the landowner's own store.
