# Video script — Drawing demand — curve and function

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The demand curve graphs the demand schedule: each (price, quantity) pair becomes one point, and the points join into a line. A table and a curve are the same law in two scripts.

The curve slopes downward from left to right for the reason the law of demand gives: as price falls along the axis, quantity demanded rises. The slope is the inverse relationship made visible.

The demand function states the same relationship mathematically: Qd = f(P) — quantity demanded as a function of price, other things equal. Read it aloud as: the amount demanded depends on the price.
