# Drawing demand — curve and function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "From schedule to curve"?
a) the graph of the demand schedule — points joined into a line  b) downward from left to right, because price and quantity move inversely  c) Qd = f(P) — quantity demanded as a function of price
**Answer: a) the graph of the demand schedule — points joined into a line**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Why it slopes downward"?
a) the graph of the demand schedule — points joined into a line  b) Qd = f(P) — quantity demanded as a function of price  c) downward from left to right, because price and quantity move inversely
**Answer: c) downward from left to right, because price and quantity move inversely**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The demand function"?
a) downward from left to right, because price and quantity move inversely  b) Qd = f(P) — quantity demanded as a function of price  c) the graph of the demand schedule — points joined into a line
**Answer: b) Qd = f(P) — quantity demanded as a function of price**
