# Drawing demand — curve and function — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The demand curve graphs the demand schedule, joining its price–quantity points into a line.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The demand curve graphs the demand schedule, joining its price–quantity points into a line.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (From schedule to curve); sub lesson 2 says: The demand curve slopes downward from left to right because price and quantity demanded move inversely.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The demand function is Qd = f(P): quantity demanded as a function of price, other things equal.
**Answer: True**
