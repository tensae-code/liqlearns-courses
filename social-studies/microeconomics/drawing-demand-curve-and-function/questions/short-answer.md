# Drawing demand — curve and function — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "From schedule to curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The demand curve graphs the demand schedule, joining its price–quantity points into a line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why it slopes downward" in your own words.
**Answer:** The demand curve slopes downward from left to right because price and quantity demanded move inversely.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The demand function" in your own words.
**Answer:** The demand function is Qd = f(P): quantity demanded as a function of price, other things equal.
