# Hands-on — Drawing demand — curve and function

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Learners stand as points in a human graph: each holds a price card and stands at their quantity position. The class reads the slope the line of heads makes.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the oranges table turning into its downward-sloping curve, with Qd = f(P) written beneath — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
