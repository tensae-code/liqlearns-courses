# Video script — Producers and consumers who makes who uses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Producers as people, groups and organisations that make goods and services, consumers as the people who buy and use them, their mutual dependence, and choosing between a good-condition and a damaged product.

Local producers and their contributions and challenges, and consumers' contributions and the factors affecting them, with influence traced across local, provincial and national economies.

Local producers identified and classified by what they produce, the size and operation of their business, and the ways they contribute to the local community, ending in a profile of one local producer.
