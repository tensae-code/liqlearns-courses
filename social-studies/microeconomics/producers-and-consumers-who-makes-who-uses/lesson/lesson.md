# Producers and consumers who makes who uses

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of one case of this lesson's subject from your own life or town and say what you already know about it. Hold your answer — the lesson takes its subject in three parts, in order.

In Papua New Guinea's book (Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG), **Needs and Wants unit, Guided Lesson 46 "Which product do I choose?" and Lessons 47–52 (KSAV)** — teaches: producers as people, groups and organisations that make goods and services, consumers as the people who buy and use them, their mutual dependence, and choosing between a good-condition and a damaged product. This lesson teaches that placement in the course's own words. (Teacher guide — evidence.)

## Sub lesson 1 — Producers as people, groups and organisations that make

Producers as people, groups and organisations that make goods and services, consumers as the people who buy and use them, their mutual dependence, and choosing between a good-condition and a damaged product.

## Sub lesson 2 — Local producers and their contributions and challenges, and

Local producers and their contributions and challenges, and consumers' contributions and the factors affecting them, with influence traced across local, provincial and national economies.

## Sub lesson 3 — Local producers identified and classified by what they

Local producers identified and classified by what they produce, the size and operation of their business, and the ways they contribute to the local community, ending in a profile of one local producer.
