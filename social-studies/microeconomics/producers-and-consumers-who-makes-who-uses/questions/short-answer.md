# Producers and consumers who makes who uses — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "Producers as people, groups and organisations that make"?
**Answer:** Producers as people, groups and organisations that make goods and services, consumers as the people who buy and use them, their mutual dependence, and choosing between a good-condition and a damaged product.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Local producers and their contributions and challenges, and"?
**Answer:** Local producers and their contributions and challenges, and consumers' contributions and the factors affecting them, with influence traced across local, provincial and national economies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Local producers identified and classified by what they"?
**Answer:** Local producers identified and classified by what they produce, the size and operation of their business, and the ways they contribute to the local community, ending in a profile of one local producer.
