# Producers and consumers who makes who uses — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Producers as people, groups and organisations that make goods and services, consumers as the people who buy and use them, their mutual dependence, and choosing between a _____ and a damaged product.
**Answer: good-condition**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Local producers and their _____ and challenges, and consumers' contributions and the factors affecting them, with influence traced across local, provincial and national economies.
**Answer: contributions**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Local producers _____ and classified by what they produce, the size and operation of their business, and the ways they contribute to the local community, ending in a profile of one local producer.
**Answer: identified**
