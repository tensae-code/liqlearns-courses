# Business entities in the economy

## Sub lesson 1 — What a business entity is

A business entity (badan usaha) is an organisation that seeks profit and needs capital and labour to run.

## Sub lesson 2 — Types by ownership

By ownership, business entities in Indonesia are grouped as state-owned (BUMN), regionally-owned (BUMD) and private (BUMS).

## Sub lesson 3 — Types by activity

By activity, business entities work in fields such as extraction, agriculture, industry, trade and services.
