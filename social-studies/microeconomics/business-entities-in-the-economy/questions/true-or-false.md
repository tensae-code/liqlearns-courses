# Business entities in the economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A business entity (badan usaha) is an organisation that seeks profit and needs capital and labour to run.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By ownership, business entities in Indonesia are grouped as state-owned (BUMN), regionally-owned (BUMD) and private (BUMS).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** By activity, business entities work in fields such as extraction, agriculture, something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — By activity, business entities work in fields such as extraction, agriculture, industry, trade and services.
