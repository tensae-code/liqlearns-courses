# Business entities in the economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A business entity (badan usaha) is an organisation that seeks profit and needs _____ to run.
**Answer: capital and labour**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By ownership, business entities in Indonesia are grouped as state-owned (BUMN), regionally-owned (BUMD) and _____.
**Answer: private (BUMS)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** By activity, business entities work in fields such as extraction, agriculture, _____.
**Answer: industry, trade and services**
