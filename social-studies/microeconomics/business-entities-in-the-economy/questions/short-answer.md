# Business entities in the economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what a business entity is?
**Answer:** A business entity (badan usaha) is an organisation that seeks profit and needs capital and labour to run.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about types by ownership?
**Answer:** By ownership, business entities in Indonesia are grouped as state-owned (BUMN), regionally-owned (BUMD) and private (BUMS).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about types by activity?
**Answer:** By activity, business entities work in fields such as extraction, agriculture, industry, trade and services.
