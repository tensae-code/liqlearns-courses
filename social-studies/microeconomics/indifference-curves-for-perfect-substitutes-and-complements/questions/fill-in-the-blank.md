# Indifference curves for perfect substitutes and complements — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For perfect substitutes the MRS is _____ all along the straight-line indifference curve.
**Answer: constant**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Perfect complements, like left and right shoes, have _____ indifference curves.
**Answer: L-shaped**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For substitutes and complements alike, curves farther from the origin mean _____ satisfaction.
**Answer: higher**
