# Indifference curves for perfect substitutes and complements — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Perfect substitutes have straight-line indifference curves and a constant MRS.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Perfect complements have L-shaped indifference curves, and extra units of one good alone add nothing.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For both perfect substitutes and perfect complements, higher indifference curves mean higher satisfaction.
**Answer: True**
