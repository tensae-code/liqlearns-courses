# Hands-on — Indifference curves for perfect substitutes and complements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Hold one blue and one black pen: swap them freely at one-for-one in any mix to show a straight trade line. Then hold shoes: add left shoes alone to a pair and show that satisfaction does not move until a right shoe joins.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a straight-line curve family beside an L-shaped family, pens on one side, a pair of shoes on the other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
