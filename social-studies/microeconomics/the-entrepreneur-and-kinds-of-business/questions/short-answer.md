# The entrepreneur and kinds of business — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is an entrepreneur?
**Answer:** An entrepreneur is the person who starts and runs a business, finding customers and promoting it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a sole proprietor?
**Answer:** A sole proprietor is the single owner of a business, who decides and carries it alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a partnership?
**Answer:** A partnership is a business owned and run together by two or more people.
