# The entrepreneur and kinds of business — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An entrepreneur is an employee hired by a business.
**Answer: False** — the entrepreneur starts and runs the business itself; employees are hired by it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sole proprietor shares ownership with many partners.
**Answer: False** — sole means one owner alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A partnership has exactly one owner.
**Answer: False** — one owner is a sole proprietor; a partnership has two or more.
