# The entrepreneur and kinds of business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About who an entrepreneur is — the lesson teaches that —
a) an entrepreneur starts and runs a business, finding and keeping customers  b) a sole proprietor owns the business alone  c) a partnership is owned and run by two or more people together
**Answer: a) an entrepreneur starts and runs a business, finding and keeping customers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About sole proprietor — one owner — the lesson teaches that —
a) an entrepreneur starts and runs a business, finding and keeping customers  b) a sole proprietor owns the business alone  c) a partnership is owned and run by two or more people together
**Answer: b) a sole proprietor owns the business alone**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About partnership — owners together — the lesson teaches that —
a) an entrepreneur starts and runs a business, finding and keeping customers  b) a sole proprietor owns the business alone  c) a partnership is owned and run by two or more people together
**Answer: c) a partnership is owned and run by two or more people together**
