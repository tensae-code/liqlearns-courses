# The entrepreneur and kinds of business — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The person who starts and runs a business, finding customers and promoting it, is an _____.
**Answer: entrepreneur**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A business owned by one person alone is run by a sole _____.
**Answer: proprietor**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A business owned and run together by two or more people is a _____.
**Answer: partnership**
