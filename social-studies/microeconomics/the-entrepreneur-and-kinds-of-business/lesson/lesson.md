# The entrepreneur and kinds of business

## Sub lesson 1 — Who an entrepreneur is

An **entrepreneur** is the person who starts and runs a business — finding customers and promoting the business so it grows. The Class 4 book teaches the idea through a restaurant: someone sets it up, draws customers in, and keeps it going.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sole proprietor — one owner

The first form of business the book names is the **sole proprietor**: one person owns the business alone, decides alone, and carries it alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — Partnership — owners together

The second form is a **partnership**: two or more people own and run the business together, sharing the work and the results.
