# Long-run cost and the envelope curve — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the firm choose in the long run?
**Answer:** For each output, the plant size whose short-run cost is lowest at that output.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can the long-run curve not lie above a short-run curve?
**Answer:** Because the firm would choose that plant — the long-run curve is built from the cheapest plant at each output.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the envelope guide?
**Answer:** Expansion decisions — it shows achievable unit cost at each scale and where growing stops paying.
