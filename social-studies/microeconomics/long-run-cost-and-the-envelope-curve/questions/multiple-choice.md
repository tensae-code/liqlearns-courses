# Long-run cost and the envelope curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the long run, the firm can vary —
a) only labour
b) every input, including plant size
c) nothing
**Answer: b) every input, including plant size**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The long-run average cost curve is called the envelope because it —
a) sits above all short-run curves
b) wraps underneath, tangent to each short-run curve
c) is a straight line
**Answer: b) wraps underneath, tangent to each short-run curve**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A firm above the envelope for its output is —
a) in the cheapest plant
b) in the wrong plant for its output
c) breaking the law
**Answer: b) in the wrong plant for its output**
