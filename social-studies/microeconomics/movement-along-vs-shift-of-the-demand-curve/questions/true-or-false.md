# Movement along vs shift of the demand curve — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A movement along the curve leaves the curve itself in place.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After a shift, buyers want a different quantity at every price.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A movement along the curve can be caused by a change in income.
**Answer: False — Income changes shift the curve; only the good's own price moves buyers along it.**
