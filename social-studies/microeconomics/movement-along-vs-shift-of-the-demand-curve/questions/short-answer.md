# Movement along vs shift of the demand curve — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to the demand curve when only the price changes?
**Answer:** Nothing — buyers move along the same curve to a new quantity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two determinants that shift demand.
**Answer:** Income and tastes (also: prices of related goods).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the diagram test from the lesson.
**Answer:** If the good's own price changed, it is a movement along the curve; if another demand determinant changed, it is a shift of the curve.
