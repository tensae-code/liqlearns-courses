# Movement along vs shift of the demand curve — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An own-price change causes a _____ along the demand curve.
**Answer: movement**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When income or tastes change, the whole demand curve _____.
**Answer: shifts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ask what changed: own price means movement; anything else on the demand side means _____.
**Answer: shift**
