# Movement along vs shift of the demand curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If only the good's own price rises, quantity demanded —
a) shifts the whole curve right
b) moves along the same curve
c) leaves the curve meaningless
**Answer: b) moves along the same curve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A rise in buyers' income, price unchanged, causes —
a) a movement along the curve
b) a shift of the whole curve
c) no change of any kind
**Answer: b) a shift of the whole curve**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Demand grows because tastes change, and price rises as a result. The first event is —
a) a movement along the curve
b) a shift of the curve
c) a supply change
**Answer: b) a shift of the curve**
