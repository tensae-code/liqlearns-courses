# Video script — Movement along vs shift of the demand curve

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook separates two things students constantly mix. First: when the good's own price changes and nothing else does, quantity demanded changes along the same demand curve — up the curve when price rises, down it when price falls.

Second: when a determinant other than price changes — income, tastes, the price of a related good — the whole curve shifts. At every price, buyers now want more (shift right) or less (shift left) than before.

The test in any diagram: ask what changed. Price of the good itself → movement along. Anything else on the demand side → shift of the curve.
