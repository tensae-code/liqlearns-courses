**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Greece, Sri Lanka).**

Greece's own way: the national Lykeio textbook teaches micro first then macro in one volume, opening economics through Robinson Crusoe's one-man economy, and closes with the EU and the Greek economy itself; worked examples use euros, olive oil and peaches. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Ch.2 §7 Change in quantity demanded vs change in demand — teaches: a price change moves along the curve, other determinants shift it. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
