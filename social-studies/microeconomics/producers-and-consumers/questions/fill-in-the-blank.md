# Producers and consumers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The person who grows and produces the wheat is a _____.
**Answer: producer**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The person who buys and eats the bread is a _____.
**Answer: consumer**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Complete the book's sentence: "The child _____ water."
**Answer: consumes**
