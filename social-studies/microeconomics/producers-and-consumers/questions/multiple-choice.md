# Producers and consumers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The person who grows the wheat and the person who makes the bread are —
a) consumers  b) producers  c) neither
**Answer: b) producers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The person who buys and eats the bread is —
a) a producer  b) a consumer  c) a raw material
**Answer: b) a consumer**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's sentence "The worker ___ pottery", the right word is —
a) consumes  b) buys  c) produces
**Answer: c) produces**
