# Producers and consumers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A producer is the person who grows or makes a good.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The child who drinks water produces the water.
**Answer: False** — the child consumes the water; a consumer buys and uses a good.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One person can be a producer of one good and a consumer of another on the same day.
**Answer: True** — the baker produces bread and consumes water.
