# Producers and consumers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who are the producers in the bread chain, and what does each do?
**Answer:** The farmer who grows and produces the wheat, and the baker who makes the bread — each brings the good into being.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a consumer do with a good?
**Answer:** A consumer buys and uses a good — eating it, drinking it, wearing it or using it up.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book's test tell a producer from a consumer?
**Answer:** Ask, with that good: does the person make it, or use it? Making it means producer; using it means consumer — as in "the worker produces pottery" and "the child consumes water".
