# Hands-on — Producers and consumers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Take three things from your kitchen or your bag. For each one, say out loud: who produced it, and who consumes it. Finish with the book's own pair: "The child consumes water" and "The worker produces pottery" — say what makes each sentence true.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the bread chain: wheat field, baker, eater. Label each person PRODUCER or CONSUMER. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
