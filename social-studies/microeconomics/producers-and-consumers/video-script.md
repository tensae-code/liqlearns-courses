# Video script — Producers and consumers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Behind every loaf of bread stand two kinds of people. The farmer who grows the wheat and the baker who makes the bread are producers — they produce. The person who buys the bread and eats it is a consumer — he consumes.

It works for everything. The child drinks water — the child consumes. The worker makes pottery — the worker produces. Ask of anyone, with any good: does he make it, or does he use it? That answer tells you whether he is its producer or its consumer.
