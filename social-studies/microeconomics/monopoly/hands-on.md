# Hands-on — Monopoly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
One learner plays the only electricity seller, posting price cards; the class, as households, must buy or go without. Replay with five wheat sellers and compare how much power the single seller held.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a single power line feeding a whole town, beside a total revenue hill with MR crossing zero at its peak — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.

## Grade 11 deepening from the Grade 10 teacher guide — say it with things at home
Take the newest part of this lesson and act it out with things at home — objects, counters, or a partner — saying each key term aloud as you use it. Then teach the part to your partner without the lesson open.
