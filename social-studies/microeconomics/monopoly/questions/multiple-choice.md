# Monopoly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "One seller, the whole industry"?
a) a single seller with no close substitutes and restricted entry — ELPA electricity  b) no substitutes, scale, owned inputs, patents and licences, secret ingredients  c) MR below AR; TR peaking at 30 around 5–6 units, MR negative beyond

**Answer: c) no substitutes, scale, owned inputs, patents and licences, secret ingredients**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Revenue — MR below AR"?
