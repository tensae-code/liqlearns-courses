# Monopoly — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "One seller, the whole industry" in your own words.
**Answer:** A monopoly is a single seller that is the whole industry and acts as price maker.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The sources of monopoly power" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ambo mineral water's monopoly source is ownership of a key input — its spring.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Revenue — MR below AR" in your own words.
**Answer:** For a monopolist, marginal revenue falls below average revenue.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "One more source — exclusive low-cost knowledge (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** Exclusive knowledge of low-cost production or technology is a source of monopoly power.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "What determines monopoly power (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** Monopoly power depends on the number of sellers, the closeness of substitutes, product differentiation, and how possible entry is.
