# Monopoly — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The monopolist is a _____ — it sets the price, where the competitive firm took it.
**Answer: price maker**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ambo mineral water's power rests on owning a _____ — its spring.
**Answer: key input**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A monopolist's marginal revenue falls _____ average revenue, because price must drop to sell more.
**Answer: below**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Exclusive knowledge of _____ or technology is a source of monopoly the teacher guide adds.
**Answer: low-cost production**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Monopoly power grows stronger the fewer and weaker the close _____ for the firm's product.
**Answer: substitutes**
