# Monopoly — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A monopoly is a single seller that is the whole industry and acts as price maker.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ambo mineral water's monopoly source is ownership of a key input — its spring.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For a monopolist, marginal revenue falls below average revenue.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Exclusive knowledge of low-cost production or technology is a source of monopoly power.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Monopoly power depends on the number of sellers, the closeness of substitutes, product differentiation, and how possible entry is.
**Answer: True**
