# Consumerism and responsible consumption — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** _____ (مصرف‌گرایی) is buying what is not really needed.
**Answer: Consumerism**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Producers today produce in _____ for profit rather than for real needs, and push sales with advertising.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The consumer tests every purchase against the real _____.
