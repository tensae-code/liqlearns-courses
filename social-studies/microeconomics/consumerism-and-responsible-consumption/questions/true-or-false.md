# Consumerism and responsible consumption — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumer rights govern the consumer's own hand; responsibility protects the consumer against the seller.
**Answer: False — It is the other way round: rights protect the consumer against the seller; responsibility governs the consumer's own hand.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Buying a new sharpener while a sound one sits at home is the book's example of consumerism.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Consumerism is only a weakness in the buyer; sellers play no part in it.
**Answer: False — Producers produce in bulk for profit rather than real needs and push sales with advertising — the pressure is built by the seller's side too.**
