# Consumerism and responsible consumption — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What responsibility does this lesson set the consumer?
**Answer:** Correct consumption — consuming in the right way, in the right measure, for real needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's own example of consumerism, and say what makes it consumerism.
**Answer:** Buying a new sharpener while a sound one sits at home. The old thing works, so the purchase meets no real need — it adds nothing but the buying itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the book say consumerism is pushed, and what is the consumer's answer to the push?
**Answer:** Because producers produce in bulk for profit rather than for real needs and push sales with advertising. The consumer's answer is responsibility: test every purchase against the real need before buying.
