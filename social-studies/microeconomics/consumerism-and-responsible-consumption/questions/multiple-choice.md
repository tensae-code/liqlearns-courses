# Consumerism and responsible consumption — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Correct consumption means —
a) buying everything on sale  b) consuming in the right way, for real needs  c) never buying anything new
**Answer: b) consuming in the right way, for real needs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's own example of consumerism is —
a) buying bread when there is none at home  b) buying a new sharpener while a sound one sits at home  c) repairing a broken pencil
**Answer: b) buying a new sharpener while a sound one sits at home**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Producers push consumerism by —
a) producing less  b) producing in bulk for profit and pushing sales with advertising  c) closing their shops
**Answer: b) producing in bulk for profit and pushing sales with advertising**
