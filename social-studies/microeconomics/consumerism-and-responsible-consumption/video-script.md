# Video script — Consumerism and responsible consumption

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's Grade 7 Social Studies book, in Lesson 8 "Consumption", sets the consumer a responsibility beside the consumer's rights: **correct consumption** — consuming in the right way, in the right measure, for real needs. Rights protect the consumer against the seller; responsibility governs the consumer's own hand. Against correct consumption the book sets **consumerism (مصرف‌گرایی, defined in the book)**: **buying what is not really needed**. Its own example is small and exact — buying **a new sharpener while a sound one sits at home**. The old thing works; the purchase adds nothing but the buying itself. That is the pattern consumerism names, at every size of purchase. The book then asks why the push is so strong. **Producers today produce in bulk for profit rather than for real needs**, and they **push sales with advertising**. Consumerism is not only a weakness in the buyer; it is a pressure built by the seller's side of the market — which is why the consumer's responsibility is to test every purchase against the real need.
