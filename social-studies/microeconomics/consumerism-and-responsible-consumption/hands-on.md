# Hands-on — Consumerism and responsible consumption

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sharpener test
List your last five purchases. Mark each one: real need, or a sound one already at home. Count how many pass the book's test.

## Spot the push
Find one advertisement today. Write what real need it names — and what it actually pushes you to feel or want.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — consumerism — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
