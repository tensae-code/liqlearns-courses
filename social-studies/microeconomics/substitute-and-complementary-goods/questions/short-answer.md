# Substitute and complementary goods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes two goods substitutes?
**Answer:** Each can replace the other in meeting a need, so a buyer can switch between them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes two goods complements?
**Answer:** They are consumed together — each is wanted with the other, as a working pair.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A good's price rises. What happens to its substitute's demand, and to its complement's demand?
**Answer:** The substitute's demand rises (buyers switch to it); the complement's demand falls (buyers take less of the pair).
