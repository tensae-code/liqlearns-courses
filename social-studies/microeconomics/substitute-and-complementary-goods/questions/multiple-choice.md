# Substitute and complementary goods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Substitute goods are goods that —
a) are consumed together  b) can replace each other  c) are always free
**Answer: b) can replace each other**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If a complement's price rises, demand for its partner —
a) falls  b) rises  c) never changes
**Answer: a) falls**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If a substitute's price rises, demand for its partner —
a) falls  b) stays at zero  c) rises
**Answer: c) rises**
