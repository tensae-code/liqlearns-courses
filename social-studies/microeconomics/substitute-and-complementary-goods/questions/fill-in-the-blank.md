# Substitute and complementary goods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods that can replace each other are _____ goods.
**Answer: substitute**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Goods consumed together are _____ goods.
**Answer: complementary**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Substitutes and complements have _____ demand effects.
**Answer: opposite**
