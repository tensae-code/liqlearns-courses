# Substitute and complementary goods — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Complementary goods are consumed together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A rise in a substitute's price lowers demand for its partner.
**Answer: False — buyers switch to the partner, so its demand rises.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Substitutes and complements pull demand in opposite directions.
**Answer: True**
