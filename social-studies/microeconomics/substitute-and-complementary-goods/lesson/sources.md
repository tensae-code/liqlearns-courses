**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Yemen).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 2, Lesson 3 — Economic goods — teaches: Some goods can replace each other — substitute goods — and some are consumed together — complementary goods — and the two kinds pull demand in opposite directions: when a substitute's price rises, demand for its partner rises; when a complement's price rises, demand for its partner falls. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
