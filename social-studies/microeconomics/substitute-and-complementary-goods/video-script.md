# Video script — Substitute and complementary goods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some goods can replace each other. These are substitute goods: if the price of one rises, buyers switch to the other, and demand for the other rises. One good steps into the other's place.

Other goods are consumed together — complementary goods. They are wanted as a pair, so if the price of one rises, people buy less of it — and less of its partner too. Substitutes pull demand one way; complements pull it the opposite way.
