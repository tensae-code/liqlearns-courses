Grade 10 teacher-guide note (placements 5–6): the objective of the firm joins the supply determinants from *Economics, Teacher Guide Book, Grade 10*, printed pp. 18–19 (teacher guide — evidence). The guide's review key for the equilibrium exercise (demand Q = 250 − 3P, supply Q = 2P − 50) computes supply at P = 75 as 2(75) + 50 = 200 — a sign error in the key as printed. The build teaches the corrected arithmetic: 2(75) − 50 = 100, against demand of 250 − 3(75) = 25, so the surplus at P = 75 is 75 units and the equilibrium (P = 60, Q = 70) stands as the key gives it. The guide's activity keys (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 24–28) also give: price elasticity for Activity 2.3, income elasticity 0.6 for Activity 2.4, and supply elasticity 0.017 for Activity 2.5.

**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

Worked-answer error, taught corrected: the §2.2.3 supply-factor bullet on the price of other goods reads backwards as printed. Taught corrected here: if the price of another good a producer could make instead RISES, supply of this good falls (shifts left), because producers switch to the better-paying good — and the reverse when that other price falls.
