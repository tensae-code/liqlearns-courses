# What shifts supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Movements along the supply curve"?
a) own price: extension or contraction along the same curve  b) factor costs, technology, shocks, taxes, subsidies, transport and other goods' prices  c) rightward supply shift: price down, quantity up; leftward: price up, quantity down
**Answer: a) own price: extension or contraction along the same curve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The supply shifters"?
a) own price: extension or contraction along the same curve  b) rightward supply shift: price down, quantity up; leftward: price up, quantity down  c) factor costs, technology, shocks, taxes, subsidies, transport and other goods' prices
**Answer: c) factor costs, technology, shocks, taxes, subsidies, transport and other goods' prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Shift effects on equilibrium"?
a) factor costs, technology, shocks, taxes, subsidies, transport and other goods' prices  b) rightward supply shift: price down, quantity up; leftward: price up, quantity down  c) own price: extension or contraction along the same curve
**Answer: b) rightward supply shift: price down, quantity up; leftward: price up, quantity down**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "One more supply determinant — the objective of the firm (teacher guide)"?
a) what the firm aims at — profit, share or survival — also shifts its supply  b) at P = 75: demand 25, supply 100, surplus 75; equilibrium P = 60, Q = 70  c) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches
**Answer: a) what the firm aims at — profit, share or survival — also shifts its supply**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which statement best matches "The corrected surplus calculation (teacher guide key, corrected)"?
