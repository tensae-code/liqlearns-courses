# What shifts supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Movements along the supply curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** A change in own price moves you along the supply curve; it does not shift it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The supply shifters" in your own words.
**Answer:** Taxes shift supply left and subsidies shift supply right.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Shift effects on equilibrium" in your own words.
**Answer:** A rightward supply shift lowers equilibrium price and raises equilibrium quantity.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "One more supply determinant — the objective of the firm (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** The teacher guide adds the objective of the firm to the list of supply determinants.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Explain "The corrected surplus calculation (teacher guide key, corrected)" in your own words.
**Answer:** At P = 75 the exercise gives demand of 25 and supply of 100, a surplus of 75 units.
