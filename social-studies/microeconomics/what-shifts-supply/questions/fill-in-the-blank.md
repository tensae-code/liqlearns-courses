# What shifts supply — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When own price changes, quantity supplied moves _____ the same supply curve.
**Answer: along**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A subsidy shifts the supply curve _____ — more is offered at every price.
**Answer: right**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A rightward supply shift _____ equilibrium price and raises equilibrium quantity.
**Answer: lowers**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The supply determinant the teacher guide adds to the student list is the _____.
**Answer: objective of the firm**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** With supply Q = 2P − 50, the quantity supplied at P = 75 is 2(75) − 50 = _____.
**Answer: 100**
