# What shifts supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A change in own price moves you along the supply curve; it does not shift it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Taxes shift supply left and subsidies shift supply right.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A rightward supply shift lowers equilibrium price and raises equilibrium quantity.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher guide adds the objective of the firm to the list of supply determinants.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** At P = 75 the exercise gives demand of 25 and supply of 100, a surplus of 75 units.
**Answer: True**
