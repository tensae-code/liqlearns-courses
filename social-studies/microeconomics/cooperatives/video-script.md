# Video script — Cooperatives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cooperatives (koperasi) are member-owned businesses built on principles such as voluntary membership, democratic control and working together for members' welfare.

Cooperatives are typed by their field and membership; their capital comes from members' savings and their organisation runs through member meetings, managers and supervisors.

The cooperative's yearly surplus, the SHU, is shared among members by the rules the members set, and the Grade 11 book works the SHU calculation in rupiah exercises.
