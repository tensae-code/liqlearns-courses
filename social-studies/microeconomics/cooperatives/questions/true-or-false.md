# Cooperatives — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cooperatives (koperasi) are member-owned businesses built on principles such as voluntary membership, democratic control and working together for members' welfare.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cooperatives are typed by their field and membership; their capital comes from members' savings and their organisation runs through member meetings, managers and supervisors.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cooperative's yearly surplus, the something else entirely, is shared among members by the rules the members set, and the Grade 11 book works the SHU calculation in rupiah exercises.
**Answer: False** — The cooperative's yearly surplus, the SHU, is shared among members by the rules the members set, and the Grade 11 book works the SHU calculation in rupiah exercises.
