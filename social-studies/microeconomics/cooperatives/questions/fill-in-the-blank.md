# Cooperatives — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cooperatives (koperasi) are _____ businesses built on principles such as voluntary membership, democratic control and working together for members' welfare.
**Answer: member-owned**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cooperatives are typed by their field and membership; their capital comes from _____ and their organisation runs through member meetings, managers and supervisors.
**Answer: members' savings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cooperative's yearly surplus, the _____, is shared among members by the rules the members set, and the Grade 11 book works the SHU calculation in rupiah exercises.
**Answer: SHU**
