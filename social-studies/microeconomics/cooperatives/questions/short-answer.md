# Cooperatives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about history and principles?
**Answer:** Cooperatives (koperasi) are member-owned businesses built on principles such as voluntary membership, democratic control and working together for members' welfare.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about types, capital and organisation?
**Answer:** Cooperatives are typed by their field and membership; their capital comes from members' savings and their organisation runs through member meetings, managers and supervisors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the shu surplus share?
**Answer:** The cooperative's yearly surplus, the SHU, is shared among members by the rules the members set, and the Grade 11 book works the SHU calculation in rupiah exercises.
