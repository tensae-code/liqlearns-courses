# Campesinos produce for themselves and for the market

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — campesinos produce for themselves and for the market — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Campesino work brings agricultural products to market and

Campesino work brings agricultural products to market and feeds the whole country;

## Sub lesson 2 — Families eat part of what they grow and

Families eat part of what they grow and take the rest to market;

## Sub lesson 3 — In coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros

In coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros (sharecroppers) and jornaleros (day labourers) on big landowners' land for long days and scarce earnings, so coffee growth enriched landowners while smallholders could only grow for their own consumption
