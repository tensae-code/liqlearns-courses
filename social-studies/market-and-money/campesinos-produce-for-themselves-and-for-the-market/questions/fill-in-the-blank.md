# Campesinos produce for themselves and for the market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Campesino work brings _____ products to market and feeds the whole country;
**Answer: agricultural**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ eat part of what they grow and take the rest to market;
**Answer: Families**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros _____ and jornaleros (day labourers) on big landowners' land for long days and scarce earnings, so coffee growth enriched landowners while smallholders could only grow for their own consumption
**Answer: sharecroppers**
