# Campesinos produce for themselves and for the market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About campesino work brings agricultural products to market and — the lesson teaches that —
a) Campesino work brings agricultural products to market and feeds the whole country;  b) Families eat part of what they grow and take the rest to market;  c) In coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros (sharecroppers) and jornaleros (day labourers) on big landowners' land for long days and scarce earnings, so coffee growth enriched landowners while smallholders could only grow for their own consumption
