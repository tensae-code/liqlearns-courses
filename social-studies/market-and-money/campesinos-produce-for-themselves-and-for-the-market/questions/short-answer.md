# Campesinos produce for themselves and for the market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about campesino work brings agricultural products to market and?
**Answer:** Campesino work brings agricultural products to market and feeds the whole country;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about families eat part of what they grow and?
**Answer:** Families eat part of what they grow and take the rest to market;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about in coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros?
**Answer:** In coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros (sharecroppers) and jornaleros (day labourers) on big landowners' land for long days and scarce earnings, so coffee growth enriched landowners while smallholders could only grow for their own consumption
