# Campesinos produce for themselves and for the market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Campesino work brings agricultural products to market and feeds the whole country;
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about campesino work brings agricultural products to market and.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about families eat part of what they grow and; campesino work brings agricultural products to market and is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros (sharecroppers) and jornaleros (day labourers) on big landowners' land for long days and scarce earnings, so coffee growth enriched landowners while smallholders could only grow for their own consumption
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
