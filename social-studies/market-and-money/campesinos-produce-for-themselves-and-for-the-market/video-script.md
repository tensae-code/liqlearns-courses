# Video script — Campesinos produce for themselves and for the market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Campesinos produce for themselves and for the market — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Campesino work brings agricultural products to market and feeds the whole country; Families eat part of what they grow and take the rest to market; In coffee regions whole families worked as aparceros (sharecroppers) and jornaleros (day labourers) on big landowners' land for long days and scarce earnings, so coffee growth enriched landowners while smallholders could only grow for their own consumption
