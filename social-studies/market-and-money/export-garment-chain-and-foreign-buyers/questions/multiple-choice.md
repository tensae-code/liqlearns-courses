# Export garment chain and foreign buyers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign chains demand —
a) lowest prices, high quality and on-time delivery  b) high prices and late delivery  c) low quality
**Answer: a) lowest prices, high quality and on-time delivery**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The exporter in the chain is based in —
a) Delhi  b) Mumbai only  c) a foreign capital
**Answer: a) Delhi**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pressure is passed on as —
a) maximum work at the lowest wages  b) higher wages  c) shorter hours
**Answer: a) maximum work at the lowest wages**
