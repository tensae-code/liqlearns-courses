# Export garment chain and foreign buyers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign chains accept any price and any delay.
**Answer: False — they demand lowest prices, quality and on-time delivery.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The exporter sets the chains' terms freely.
**Answer: False — the chains dictate price, quality and timing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The exporter's workers gain most from the chain's pressure.
**Answer: False — they get maximum work at the lowest wages.**
