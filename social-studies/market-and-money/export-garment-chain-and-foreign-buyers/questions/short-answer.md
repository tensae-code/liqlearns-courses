# Export garment chain and foreign buyers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three demands foreign chains make.
**Answer:** Lowest prices, high quality and on-time delivery.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who stands between foreign chains and workers?
**Answer:** The Delhi garment exporter.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is the pressure passed on?
**Answer:** As maximum work at the lowest wages for the workers.
