# Export garment chain and foreign buyers

## Sub lesson 1 — Foreign chains demand low prices, quality, on-time delivery

**Foreign store chains demand the lowest prices, high quality and on-time delivery** from the garment exporter — all three at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Delhi exporter in the middle

The **Delhi garment exporter** stands between the foreign chains and the workers, squeezed by the chains' demands.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pressure passed on — maximum work, lowest wages

The exporter **passes the pressure on** to the workers as **maximum work at the lowest wages** — the chain's squeeze lands on those who sew.
