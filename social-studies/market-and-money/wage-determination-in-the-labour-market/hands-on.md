# Hands-on — Wage determination in the labour market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cross the curves
Draw labour demand and supply crossing at a market wage. Then show a wage offered below the crossing and describe, in market language, the queue that forms — or fails to.

## Cost of an hour
List what you would give up for three hours of evening work. Price the last hour: the wage below which you would refuse is your supply price for that hour.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
