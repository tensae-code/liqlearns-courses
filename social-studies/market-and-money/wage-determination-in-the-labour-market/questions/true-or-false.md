# Wage determination in the labour market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The wage settles where labour demand meets labour supply.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Many firms, many identical workers, wage-taking on both sides, free information and mobility.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The supply curve's height is the marginal opportunity cost of the last hour.
**Answer: True**
