# Wage determination in the labour market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The wage is a price"?
a) The wage settles where labour demand meets labour supply  b) Many firms, many identical workers, wage-taking on both sides, free information and mobility  c) The supply curve's height is the marginal opportunity cost of the last hour
**Answer: a) The wage settles where labour demand meets labour supply**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The perfectly competitive labour market"?
a) Many firms, many identical workers, wage-taking on both sides, free information and mobility  b) The supply curve's height is the marginal opportunity cost of the last hour  c) The wage settles where labour demand meets labour supply
**Answer: a) Many firms, many identical workers, wage-taking on both sides, free information and mobility**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Reading the supply curve"?
a) The supply curve's height is the marginal opportunity cost of the last hour  b) The wage settles where labour demand meets labour supply  c) Many firms, many identical workers, wage-taking on both sides, free information and mobility
**Answer: a) The supply curve's height is the marginal opportunity cost of the last hour**
