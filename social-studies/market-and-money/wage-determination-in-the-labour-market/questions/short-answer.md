# Wage determination in the labour market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The wage is a price" in your own words.
**Answer:** The wage settles where labour demand meets labour supply.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The perfectly competitive labour market" in your own words.
**Answer:** Many firms, many identical workers, wage-taking on both sides, free information and mobility.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reading the supply curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The supply curve's height is the marginal opportunity cost of the last hour.
