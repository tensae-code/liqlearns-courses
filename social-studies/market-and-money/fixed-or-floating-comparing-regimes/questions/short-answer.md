# Fixed or floating — comparing regimes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The case for fixed" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fixed rates give certainty, can suppress speculation if believed, and discipline policy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The price of fixed" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fixed rates cannot respond to shocks, need large reserves, and invite speculative runs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The case and price of floating" in your own words.
**Answer:** Floating rates absorb shocks and free policy, but are unstable and speculation-prone.
