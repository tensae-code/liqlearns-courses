# Fixed or floating — comparing regimes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fixed rates give certainty, can suppress speculation if believed, and discipline policy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fixed rates cannot respond to shocks, need large reserves, and invite speculative runs.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Floating rates absorb shocks and free policy, but are unstable and speculation-prone.
**Answer: True**
