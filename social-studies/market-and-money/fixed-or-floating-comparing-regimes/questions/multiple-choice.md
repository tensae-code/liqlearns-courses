# Fixed or floating — comparing regimes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The case for fixed"?
a) Fixed rates give certainty, can suppress speculation if believed, and discipline policy  b) Fixed rates cannot respond to shocks, need large reserves, and invite speculative runs  c) Floating rates absorb shocks and free policy, but are unstable and speculation-prone
**Answer: a) Fixed rates give certainty, can suppress speculation if believed, and discipline policy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The price of fixed"?
a) Fixed rates cannot respond to shocks, need large reserves, and invite speculative runs  b) Floating rates absorb shocks and free policy, but are unstable and speculation-prone  c) Fixed rates give certainty, can suppress speculation if believed, and discipline policy
**Answer: a) Fixed rates cannot respond to shocks, need large reserves, and invite speculative runs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The case and price of floating"?
a) Floating rates absorb shocks and free policy, but are unstable and speculation-prone  b) Fixed rates give certainty, can suppress speculation if believed, and discipline policy  c) Fixed rates cannot respond to shocks, need large reserves, and invite speculative runs
**Answer: a) Floating rates absorb shocks and free policy, but are unstable and speculation-prone**
