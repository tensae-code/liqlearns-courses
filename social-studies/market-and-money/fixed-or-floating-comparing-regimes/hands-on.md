# Hands-on — Fixed or floating — comparing regimes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Balance sheet
Build the two-column balance sheet of the two regimes from the lesson — every strength and weakness placed. Then mark the one item on each side you judge heaviest, and defend the marks.

## Advise a country
A small economy with thin reserves and volatile export prices asks your advice. Choose a regime in five sentences, naming the risk you accept.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
