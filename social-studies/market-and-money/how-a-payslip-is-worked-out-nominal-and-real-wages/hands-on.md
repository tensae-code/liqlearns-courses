# Hands-on — How a payslip is worked out — nominal and real wages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a slip
Copy the book's payslip lines onto paper: gross pay, social insurance, income tax, take-home pay. Beside each line write whether it is money in or money out.

## Say it with things at home
Put 85 stones in a bowl for the gross pay in tens of thousands of tögrög. Take stones out for the deductions until 695,500 tögrög's worth is left in tens — say each line aloud as you move the stones.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
