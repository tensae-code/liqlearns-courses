# Video script — How a payslip is worked out — nominal and real wages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How is a wage calculated? Mongolia's Grade 9 book works a real payslip, says the narrator. Gross pay: 850,000 tögrög. Less 10 percent social insurance and 10 percent income tax. Take-home pay: 695,500 tögrög. And remember the two wages: nominal is the money sum; real is nominal divided by the price level — what the money buys.
