# How a payslip is worked out — nominal and real wages — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The nominal wage is the money sum itself.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's payslip deducts 10% social insurance and 10% income tax.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Take-home pay is the gross pay before any deductions.
**Answer: False — take-home pay is what remains after deductions: 695,500 tögrög from the 850,000 tögrög gross.**
