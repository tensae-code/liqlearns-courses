# How a payslip is worked out — nominal and real wages — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book define the real wage?
**Answer:** The nominal wage divided by the price level — what the money sum is worth at the prices of the day.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Work the book's payslip in words: start, deductions, end.
**Answer:** Gross pay 850,000 tögrög, less 10% social insurance and 10% income tax, to a take-home pay of 695,500 tögrög.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the gross figure alone not enough to judge a wage?
**Answer:** Because deductions stand between gross and take-home pay, and the real wage depends on the price level — what the money actually buys.
