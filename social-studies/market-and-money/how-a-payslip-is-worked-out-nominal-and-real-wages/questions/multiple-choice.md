# How a payslip is worked out — nominal and real wages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The real wage, in the book, is —
a) the gross pay before deductions  b) the nominal wage divided by the price level  c) the take-home pay plus tax
**Answer: b) the nominal wage divided by the price level**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's payslip starts from a gross pay of —
a) 850,000 tögrög  b) 695,500 tögrög  c) 85,000 tögrög
**Answer: a) 850,000 tögrög**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The payslip's take-home pay is —
a) 850,000 tögrög  b) 765,000 tögrög  c) 695,500 tögrög
**Answer: c) 695,500 tögrög**
