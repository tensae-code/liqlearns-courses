Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about philosophers and economists read the luddite movement as?
**Answer:** Philosophers and economists read the Luddite movement as the start of the workers' class struggle;

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about by the late 19th century wages kept falling?

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about revolutionary unions aimed not at less exploitation but?
