# Sindicalismo unions luddites and the class struggle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Philosophers and economists read the _____ movement as the start of the workers' class struggle;
**Answer: Luddite**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By the late _____ century wages kept falling while unemployment rose;
**Answer: 19th**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Revolutionary unions aimed not at less exploitation but at eliminating it — collective ownership of the means of production (communism, with Marx as the named economist) — and strikes grew to _____ workers across the US, centred on Chicago
**Answer: 190,000**
