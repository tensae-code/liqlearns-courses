# The foreign exchange market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** To buy birr with dollars is also to ____ dollars.
**Answer: supply (sell)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An Ethiopian exporter supplies dollars and demands ____.
**Answer: birr**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ecuador, El Salvador and Panama are ____ economies.
**Answer: dollarised**
