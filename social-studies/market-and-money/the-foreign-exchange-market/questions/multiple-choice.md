# The foreign exchange market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Everyone both buys and sells"?
a) In the FX market every participant both demands one currency and supplies another  b) The four participants are trading firms, tourists, international investors and financial investors  c) Dollarised economies and shared currencies like the euro are the framing exceptions
**Answer: a) In the FX market every participant both demands one currency and supplies another**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The four participants"?
a) The four participants are trading firms, tourists, international investors and financial investors  b) Dollarised economies and shared currencies like the euro are the framing exceptions  c) In the FX market every participant both demands one currency and supplies another
**Answer: a) The four participants are trading firms, tourists, international investors and financial investors**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The exceptions that frame it"?
a) Dollarised economies and shared currencies like the euro are the framing exceptions  b) In the FX market every participant both demands one currency and supplies another  c) The four participants are trading firms, tourists, international investors and financial investors
**Answer: a) Dollarised economies and shared currencies like the euro are the framing exceptions**
