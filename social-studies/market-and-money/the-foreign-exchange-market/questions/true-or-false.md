# The foreign exchange market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the FX market every participant both demands one currency and supplies another.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four participants are trading firms, tourists, international investors and financial investors.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Dollarised economies and shared currencies like the euro are the framing exceptions.
**Answer: True**
