# The foreign exchange market — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everyone both buys and sells — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four participants — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The exceptions that frame it — b

Statements:
a) The four participants are trading firms, tourists, international investors and financial investors
b) Dollarised economies and shared currencies like the euro are the framing exceptions
c) In the FX market every participant both demands one currency and supplies another

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
