# The foreign exchange market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Everyone both buys and sells" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the FX market every participant both demands one currency and supplies another.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The four participants" in your own words.
**Answer:** The four participants are trading firms, tourists, international investors and financial investors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The exceptions that frame it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Dollarised economies and shared currencies like the euro are the framing exceptions.
