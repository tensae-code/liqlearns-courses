# Hands-on — The foreign exchange market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## At the counter
Write four counter scenes, one per participant group, each with two arrows showing the currency given and the currency demanded. The exporter scene must end in birr wages paid.

## Spot the pair
For six invented transactions, name the currency supplied and the currency demanded. Any answer naming only one currency is half an answer — say why.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
