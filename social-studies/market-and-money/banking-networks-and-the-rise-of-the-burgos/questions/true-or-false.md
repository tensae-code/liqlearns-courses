# Banking networks and the rise of the burgos — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Permanent contact between cities through banking networks made trade constant and ever more lucrative
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about permanent contact between cities through banking networks made.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about and wealth slowly left the fields and feudal; permanent contact between cities through banking networks made is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** And wealth slowly left the fields and feudal castles to take residence in the burgos (towns)
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
