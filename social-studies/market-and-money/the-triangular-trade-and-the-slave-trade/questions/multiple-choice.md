# The triangular trade and the slave trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About comercio triangular — the lesson teaches that —
a) The Atlantic circuit.
b) European goods (alcohol, arms, cloth) exchanged for enslaved people in Africa, sold in the Antilles.
c) Ships returning with sugar, cacao, coffee and indigo, a 15–18 month round trip.
**Answer: a) The Atlantic circuit.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About comercio triangular — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) Ships returning with sugar, cacao, coffee and indigo, a 15–18 month round trip.
b) European goods (alcohol, arms, cloth) exchanged for enslaved people in Africa, sold in the Antilles.
c) The Atlantic circuit.
**Answer: b) European goods (alcohol, arms, cloth) exchanged for enslaved people in Africa, sold in the Antilles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About comercio triangular — more — more — the lesson teaches that —
a) The Atlantic circuit.
b) European goods (alcohol, arms, cloth) exchanged for enslaved people in Africa, sold in the Antilles.
c) Ships returning with sugar, cacao, coffee and indigo, a 15–18 month round trip.
