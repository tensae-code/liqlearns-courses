# The triangular trade and the slave trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about comercio triangular?
**Answer:** The Atlantic circuit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about comercio triangular — more?
**Answer:** European goods (alcohol, arms, cloth) exchanged for enslaved people in Africa, sold in the Antilles.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about comercio triangular — more — more?
**Answer:** Ships returning with sugar, cacao, coffee and indigo, a 15–18 month round trip.
