# The triangular trade and the slave trade

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — the triangular trade and the slave trade — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Comercio triangular

The Atlantic circuit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Comercio triangular — more

European goods (alcohol, arms, cloth) exchanged for enslaved people in Africa, sold in the Antilles.

## Sub lesson 3 — Comercio triangular — more — more

Ships returning with sugar, cacao, coffee and indigo, a 15–18 month round trip.

## Say it to your companion

Explain the triangular trade and the slave trade to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
