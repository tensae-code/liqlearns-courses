# Simple and compound interest

## Do it first

Think of this topic the way the Uzbekistan Grade 9 book opens it — Topic 17, Interest and the Credit Rate. What do you already know about it before you read on?

## Sub lesson 1 — Simple interest — I = P · r · t, and S = P + I

**Credit interest** on the simple rule is **I = P · r · t** — the principal times the rate times the time. The sum to repay is **S = P + I**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Compound interest — Pn = P(1+r)^n

With **compound interest**, each period's interest itself earns interest: **Pn = P(1+r)^n** — the principal multiplied by (1 + the rate) raised to the number of periods.

## Sub lesson 3 — The practicum — Ipotekabank's two deposits for 100,000 sum

The book's Economic Practicum compares Ipotekabank's **'Shodlik'** deposit (simple interest) and **'Kafolat'** deposit (compound interest), each for **100,000 sum**, so pupils see the two rules give different results.
