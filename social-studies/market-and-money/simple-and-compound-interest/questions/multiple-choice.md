# Simple and compound interest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Simple interest I equals —
a) P · r · t  b) P(1+r)^n  c) P + r
**Answer: a) P · r · t**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Compound interest Pn equals —
a) P · r · t  b) P(1+r)^n  c) P + I
**Answer: b) P(1+r)^n**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The practicum compares deposits at —
a) the central bank  b) Ipotekabank  c) a foreign bank
**Answer: b) Ipotekabank**
