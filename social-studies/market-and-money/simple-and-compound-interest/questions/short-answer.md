# Simple and compound interest — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is simple interest worked out?
**Answer:** I = P · r · t — principal times rate times time.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes compound interest grow faster than simple interest?
**Answer:** Each period's interest itself earns interest in the next periods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which two deposits does the practicum compare, and for how much?
**Answer:** Ipotekabank's 'Shodlik' (simple) and 'Kafolat' (compound), each for 100,000 sum.
