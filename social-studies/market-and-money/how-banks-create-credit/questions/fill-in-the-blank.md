# How banks create credit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banks create _____ by lending most of a deposit while keeping part in reserve.
**Answer: credit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A loan spent lands as a _____ in another bank, which lends again.
**Answer: deposit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One limit on credit creation is the policy of the _____ bank.
**Answer: central**
