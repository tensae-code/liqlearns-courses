# How banks create credit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do banks begin creating credit?
**Answer:** A bank lends most of a first deposit, keeping only a part in reserve; the loan becomes new money.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does credit creation multiply?
**Answer:** Each loan becomes a new deposit in another bank, which lends most of it again — round by round.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three limitations of credit creation.
**Answer:** Any three of: quantity of money, demand for cash, shortage of borrowers, shortage of security assets, cooperation among banks, central-bank policy, economic fluctuations.
