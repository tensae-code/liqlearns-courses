# How banks create credit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About credit creation from a first deposit — the lesson teaches that —
a) banks create credit by lending most of a deposit and keeping only part in reserve  b) each loan becomes another bank's deposit, which is lent again, multiplying credit  c) credit creation is limited by cash demand, borrowers, security, central-bank policy and more
**Answer: a) banks create credit by lending most of a deposit and keeping only part in reserve**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About how the creation multiplies — the lesson teaches that —
a) banks create credit by lending most of a deposit and keeping only part in reserve  b) each loan becomes another bank's deposit, which is lent again, multiplying credit  c) credit creation is limited by cash demand, borrowers, security, central-bank policy and more
**Answer: b) each loan becomes another bank's deposit, which is lent again, multiplying credit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About the limitations of credit creation — the lesson teaches that —
a) banks create credit by lending most of a deposit and keeping only part in reserve  b) each loan becomes another bank's deposit, which is lent again, multiplying credit  c) credit creation is limited by cash demand, borrowers, security, central-bank policy and more
**Answer: c) credit creation is limited by cash demand, borrowers, security, central-bank policy and more**
