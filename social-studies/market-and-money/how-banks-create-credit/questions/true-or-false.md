# How banks create credit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bank lends nothing until every deposit is a year old.
**Answer: False** — banks lend most of each deposit, keeping part in reserve.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Credit is created once, in one bank, and stops there.
**Answer: False** — it multiplies round by round through the banking system.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Credit creation has no limits.
**Answer: False** — the book lists seven limitations.
