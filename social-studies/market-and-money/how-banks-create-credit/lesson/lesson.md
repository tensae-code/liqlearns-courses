## Sub lesson 1 — Credit creation from a first deposit

The Class 12 banking chapter teaches **credit creation**: a first deposit does not sit idle in the bank. The bank lends most of it, keeping only a part in reserve — and the loan becomes new money in the borrower's account.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the creation multiplies

The loan is spent and lands as a deposit in another bank, which lends most of it again. Round by round, the banking system builds a total of credit money many times the first deposit.

## Sub lesson 3 — The limitations of credit creation

The book lists what limits creation: the **quantity of money**, people's **demand for cash**, **shortage of borrowers**, **shortage of security assets**, **cooperation among banks**, the **central bank's policy** and **economic fluctuations**. Credit creation is powerful, never unlimited.
