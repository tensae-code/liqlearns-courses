# Video script — How banks create credit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How banks create credit — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: banks create credit by lending most of a deposit and keeping only part in reserve; each loan becomes another bank's deposit, which is lent again, multiplying credit; credit creation is limited by cash demand, borrowers, security, central-bank policy and more.
