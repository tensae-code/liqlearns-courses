# Present discounted value — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is present discounted value?
**Answer:** What a stream of future payments is worth today, at a given interest rate.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is a stock valued in the worked example?
**Answer:** By the present discounted value of its expected future payments.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is a bond valued by PDV?
**Answer:** Discount its future interest payments and final repayment back to today at the given interest rate.
