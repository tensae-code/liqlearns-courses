# Present discounted value — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A payment in ten years is worth its full face value today.
**Answer: False — it is discounted: money now can earn interest, so future money is worth less today.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A stock's value has nothing to do with future payments.
**Answer: False — the worked example values the stock by its expected future payments, discounted.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bonds cannot be valued by present discounted value.
**Answer: False — bonds are valued exactly so: future interest and repayment, discounted.**
