# Present discounted value — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Present discounted value is —
a) what future payments are worth today at a given interest rate  b) the sum of future payments undiscounted  c) tomorrow's price
**Answer: a) what future payments are worth today at a given interest rate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Babble, Inc. example, a stock's value is —
a) the present value of its expected future payments  b) its highest past price  c) the firm's building value
**Answer: a) the present value of its expected future payments**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A bond is valued by —
a) discounting its future interest and repayment to today  b) its face value in all cases  c) counting its coupons twice
**Answer: a) discounting its future interest and repayment to today**
