## Sub lesson 1 — What a future payment is worth today

**Present discounted value (PDV)** is **what a stream of future payments is worth today** at a given interest rate — future money is **discounted** because money now can earn interest.

## Sub lesson 2 — Applied to a stock — Babble, Inc.

The book values a **stock** by PDV in the **Babble, Inc. worked example** — the share is worth the **present value of its expected future payments** to the holder.

## Sub lesson 3 — Applied to a bond

The same method values a **bond**: its **future interest and repayment** are discounted back to **today's value** at the given rate.
