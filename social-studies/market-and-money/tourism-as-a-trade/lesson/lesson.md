# Tourism as a trade

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — tourism as a trade — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tourism employs more than 15 million directly

**Tourism as a trade employs more than 15 million people directly** — guides, hotels, transport, food and more.

## Sub lesson 2 — It supports handicrafts and cultural pursuits

Tourism **supports local handicrafts and cultural pursuits** — visitors buy the crafts and pay for the culture of the places they visit.

## Sub lesson 3 — It earns from foreign visitors

Tourism **earns from foreign visitors** — money from abroad spent inside the country, like an export that never leaves home.

## Say it to your companion

Explain tourism as a trade to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
