# Tourism as a trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many people does tourism employ directly?
**Answer:** More than 15 million.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two local activities tourism supports.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does tourism earn from abroad?
**Answer:** Foreign visitors spend their money inside the country.
