# Tourism as a trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tourism employs directly —
a) more than 15 million people  b) under a million people  c) no one directly
**Answer: a) more than 15 million people**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tourism supports —
a) local handicrafts and cultural pursuits  b) only airlines  c) only imports
**Answer: a) local handicrafts and cultural pursuits**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tourism earns from —
a) foreign visitors  b) only local taxes  c) nobody
**Answer: a) foreign visitors**
