# Tourism as a trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tourism employs almost nobody.
**Answer: False — it employs more than 15 million directly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tourism harms all local crafts.
**Answer: False — it supports handicrafts and cultural pursuits.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tourism is not a trade because nothing is sold.
**Answer: False — it is a trade employing millions and earning from visitors.**
