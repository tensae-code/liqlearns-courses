# Hands-on — Payment instruments — cheques and orders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Draw four blank specimen cards on paper and label them: cheque, money order, postal order, bill of exchange. On each card, write who is ordered to pay and who receives the money, using the lesson's specimens — the Kenya Commercial Bank cheque for KSh 50,000, the Garissa money order for Shs 800, the ten-shilling postal order, and the bill of exchange drawn on Sukuma Bank Ltd.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's cheque from memory — a written order to a bank to pay a named amount — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the specimens there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
