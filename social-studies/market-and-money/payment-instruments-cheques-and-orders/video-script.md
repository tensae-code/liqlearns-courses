# Video script — Payment instruments — cheques and orders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A business does not always pay in cash. It pays with instruments — written orders that move money from one person to another.

A cheque is an order to a bank: pay the amount written to the person named. Kenya's book shows a filled Kenya Commercial Bank cheque for KSh 50,000.

A money order and a postal order are bought and sent through the post. The book shows a Kenya inland money order from Garissa for Shs 800, and a Kenya postal order for ten shillings.

A bill of exchange is also a written order to pay — the book's specimen is drawn on Sukuma Bank Ltd.
