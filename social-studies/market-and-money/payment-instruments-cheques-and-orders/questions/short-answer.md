# Payment instruments — cheques and orders — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a cheque order, and what is the book's cheque specimen?
**Answer:** A cheque orders a bank to pay the amount written on it to the person named. The book's specimen is a Kenya Commercial Bank cheque for KSh 50,000.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do a money order and a postal order pay, and what are the book's two specimens?
**Answer:** They are bought and sent through the post — the paper itself carries the value. The specimens are a Kenya inland money order (Garissa, Shs 800) and a Kenya postal order for ten shillings.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a bill of exchange, and on whom is the book's specimen drawn?
**Answer:** A bill of exchange is a written order by one party telling another to pay a stated sum. The book's specimen is drawn on Sukuma Bank Ltd.
