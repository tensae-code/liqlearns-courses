# Payment instruments — cheques and orders — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's cheque specimen is a Kenya Commercial Bank cheque for KSh 50,000.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A money order and a postal order are bought and sent through the post.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A bill of exchange is a bag of cash sent to a bank.
**Answer: False** — a bill of exchange is a written order to pay; the book's specimen is drawn on Sukuma Bank Ltd.
