# Payment instruments — cheques and orders — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cheque is a written _____ to a bank to pay the amount written to the person named.
**Answer: order**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's postal order specimen is a Kenya postal order for _____ shillings.
**Answer: ten**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cheques, money orders, postal orders and bills of exchange are all payment _____ — paper orders that let a business pay without handing over cash.
**Answer: instruments**
