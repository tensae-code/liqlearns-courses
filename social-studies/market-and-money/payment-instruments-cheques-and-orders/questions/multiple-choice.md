# Payment instruments — cheques and orders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cheque is —
a) cash carried in an envelope  b) a written order to a bank to pay the amount written to the person named  c) a receipt for goods received
**Answer: b) a written order to a bank to pay the amount written to the person named**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's money order specimen is —
a) a Kenya inland money order, Garissa, Shs 800  b) a foreign money order for KSh 50,000  c) a postal order for ten shillings
**Answer: a) a Kenya inland money order, Garissa, Shs 800**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's bill of exchange specimen is drawn on —
a) Kenya Commercial Bank  b) the Garissa post office  c) Sukuma Bank Ltd
**Answer: c) Sukuma Bank Ltd**
