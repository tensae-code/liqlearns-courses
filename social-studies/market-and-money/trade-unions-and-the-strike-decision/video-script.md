# Video script — Trade unions and the strike decision

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When work moved into the towns, workers began to stand together. That is the labour movement, and its organisations are trade unions: united we stand.

A strike is when workers stop work together. Striking costs: wages are lost while the rent still has to be paid, and a worker can face eviction from the union and from housing.

But standing together can also win: in the Icelandic book, a neighbouring factory's four-day strike won higher pay. The strike decision is weighing those costs against that solidarity.
