# Lesson plan — Trade unions and the strike decision

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Trade unions and the strike decision
**Place in the curriculum:** Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Iceland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Workers who stand together in a trade union can strike — stop work together — and deciding to strike means weighing what it costs (lost wages, rent, eviction) against what solidarity can win.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own judgement — weighing real costs against a shared gain, the way the Icelandic pupils weigh the strike cards.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
