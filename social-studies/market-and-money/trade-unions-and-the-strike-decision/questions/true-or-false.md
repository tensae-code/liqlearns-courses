# Trade unions and the strike decision — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A trade union is an organisation of workers who stand together, and the book's part title is "United we stand!"
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Striking costs the strikers nothing.
**Answer: False** — wages are lost while rent still has to be paid, and a worker can face eviction from the union and from housing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The neighbouring factory's strike in the book lasted four days and won higher pay.
**Answer: True**
