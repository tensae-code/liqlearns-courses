# Trade unions and the strike decision — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do workers stand together in a trade union?
**Answer:** One worker alone has little power against a factory owner; workers standing together speak with one voice — united we stand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two costs a striker weighs, and one thing solidarity can win.
**Answer:** Costs: lost wages (with rent still to pay) and eviction from the union and from housing. Solidarity can win higher pay — the neighbouring factory's four-day strike won it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does Worksheet 15 teach the strike decision?
**Answer:** Pupils rank strike-decision cards — lost wages and rent, eviction from union and housing, against solidarity and the neighbouring factory's winning strike.
