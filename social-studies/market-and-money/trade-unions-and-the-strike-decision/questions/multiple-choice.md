# Trade unions and the strike decision — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Icelandic book, the labour movement rose together with —
a) farming alone  b) urbanisation — work and workers gathering in the towns  c) tourism
**Answer: b) urbanisation — work and workers gathering in the towns**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a cost of striking named in the book?
a) lost wages while the rent still has to be paid  b) higher pay at once  c) a shorter working day
**Answer: a) lost wages while the rent still has to be paid**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Worksheet 15, pupils —
a) copy a definition of a strike  b) rank strike-decision cards  c) draw a factory
**Answer: b) rank strike-decision cards**
