# Trade unions and the strike decision — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The workers' own organisations in the labour movement are called trade _____.
**Answer: unions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When workers stop work together until their demand is met, that is a _____.
**Answer: strike**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Worksheet 15 asks, in Icelandic, "Viltu fara í verkfall?" — "Do you want to go on _____?"
**Answer: strike**
