# Hands-on — Depreciation, devaluation and the trade balance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## See-saw it
Draw a see-saw: the currency side dips, exports rise; the currency side climbs, imports rise. Label each end with the mechanism, not just the result.

## Two firms
An exporter and an importer face the same devaluation. Write each firm's one-paragraph reaction — prices set, orders expected — then the trade balance's verdict on both together.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
