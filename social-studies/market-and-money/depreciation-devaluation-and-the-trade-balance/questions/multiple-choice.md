# Depreciation, devaluation and the trade balance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Cheaper exports, dearer imports"?
a) A weaker currency makes exports cheaper abroad and imports dearer at home  b) A weaker currency improves the current account and the overall balance of payments
**Answer: a) A weaker currency makes exports cheaper abroad and imports dearer at home**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The balances improve"?
a) A weaker currency improves the current account and the overall balance of payments  b) A weaker currency makes exports cheaper abroad and imports dearer at home
**Answer: a) A weaker currency improves the current account and the overall balance of payments**
