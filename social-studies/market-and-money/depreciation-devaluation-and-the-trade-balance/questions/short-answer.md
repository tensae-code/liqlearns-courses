# Depreciation, devaluation and the trade balance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Cheaper exports, dearer imports" in your own words.
**Answer:** A weaker currency makes exports cheaper abroad and imports dearer at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The balances improve" in your own words.
**Answer:** A weaker currency improves the current account and the overall balance of payments.
