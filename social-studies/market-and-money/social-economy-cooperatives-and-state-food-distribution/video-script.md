# Video script — Social economy — cooperatives and state food distribution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

It can meet local needs the market has not satisfied, organising the economy from solidarity.

A voluntary society of people who unite to resolve common needs, with equal duties and rights for all members.

They are state organisations that market basic foods at low prices; organised communities and cooperatives take part in production and distribution.
