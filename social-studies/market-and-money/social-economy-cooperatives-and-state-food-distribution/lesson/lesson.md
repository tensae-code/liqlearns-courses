## Sub lesson 1 — The social economy — meeting needs the market missed

The Grade 7 book presents the **social economy** as the part of economic life that can meet **local needs the market has not satisfied**.

Its organising principle is named in one word: **solidarity**. Where the market organises the economy by profit, the social economy organises it from solidarity — and its working form is the cooperative.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cooperatives — equal duties and rights

A **cooperative** is a voluntary society of people who unite to resolve common needs.

Its rule of membership is equality: **equal duties and rights for all members**. Nobody joins a cooperative as an owner over others; the form itself is the social economy's principle turned into an organisation.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mercal and Pdval — state food at low prices

The book connects the social economy to food. Community social-protection spokespeople link directly with **Mercal** and **Pdval** — the state organisations that market **basic foods at low prices**.

And the link is two-way: organised communities and cooperatives take part in the **production and distribution** of that food, not only in receiving it.
