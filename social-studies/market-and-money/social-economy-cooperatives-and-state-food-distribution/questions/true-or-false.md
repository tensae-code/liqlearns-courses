# Social economy — cooperatives and state food distribution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The social economy exists to meet needs the market has not satisfied.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's presentation of it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cooperatives are compulsory organisations people are forced to join.
**Answer: False** — they are voluntary societies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mercal and Pdval sell basic foods at the highest prices the market allows.
**Answer: False** — they market basic foods at low prices.
