# Social economy — cooperatives and state food distribution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What can the social economy do that the market has not, and from what principle?
**Answer:** It can meet local needs the market has not satisfied, organising the economy from solidarity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Define a cooperative as the book does.
**Answer:** A voluntary society of people who unite to resolve common needs, with equal duties and rights for all members.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do Mercal and Pdval do, and who takes part with them?
**Answer:** They are state organisations that market basic foods at low prices; organised communities and cooperatives take part in production and distribution.
