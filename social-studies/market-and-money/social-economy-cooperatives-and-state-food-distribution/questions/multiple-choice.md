# Social economy — cooperatives and state food distribution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The social economy meets —
a) local needs the market has not satisfied, organised from solidarity  b) only export markets  c) the needs of shareholders first
**Answer: a) local needs the market has not satisfied, organised from solidarity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a cooperative, all members have —
a) equal duties and rights  b) rights set by how much money they put in  c) no duties at all
**Answer: a) equal duties and rights**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mercal and Pdval are —
a) state organisations marketing basic foods at low prices  b) private supermarket chains  c) foreign food companies
**Answer: a) state organisations marketing basic foods at low prices**
