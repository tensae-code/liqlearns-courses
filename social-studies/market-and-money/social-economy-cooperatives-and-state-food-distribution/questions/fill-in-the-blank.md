# Social economy — cooperatives and state food distribution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The social economy organises the economy from _____.
**Answer: solidarity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cooperative is a voluntary society of people united to resolve common _____.
**Answer: needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Organised communities and cooperatives take part in the production and _____ of basic foods.
**Answer: distribution**
