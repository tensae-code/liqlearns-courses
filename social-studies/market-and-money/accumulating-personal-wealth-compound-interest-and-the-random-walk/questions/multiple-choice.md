# Accumulating personal wealth — compound interest and the random walk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Compound interest pays on —
a) the sum plus past interest  b) the original sum only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the sum plus past interest**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Random walk theory says stock prices —
a) move unpredictably; past prices cannot predict future ones  b) always rise steadily  c) follow last week's pattern exactly
**Answer: a) move unpredictably; past prices cannot predict future ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Capital markets transform savings into —
a) firms' investment  b) consumption only  c) government debt only
**Answer: a) firms' investment**
