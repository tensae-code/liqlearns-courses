# Accumulating personal wealth — compound interest and the random walk — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Compound interest never beats simple interest over time.
**Answer: False — compounding pulls ahead over time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Past prices reliably predict future stock prices.
**Answer: False — that is what the random walk theory denies.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Savings never reach firms' investment.
**Answer: False — channelling savings into investment is what capital markets do.**
