# Accumulating personal wealth — compound interest and the random walk — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between simple and compound interest?
**Answer:** Simple pays on the original sum only; compound pays also on past interest.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the random walk theory claim?
**Answer:** Stock price changes are unpredictable; past prices cannot predict future prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do capital markets transform financial capital?
**Answer:** They channel households' savings into firms' productive investment.
