## Sub lesson 1 — Simple and compound interest calculated

**Simple interest** pays on the original sum only; **compound interest** pays **on the sum plus past interest** — the book **calculates both**, and compounding pulls ahead over time.

## Sub lesson 2 — The random walk theory of stock prices

The **random walk theory** says **stock prices move unpredictably** — tomorrow's change does not follow from today's, so **past prices cannot predict future prices**.

## Sub lesson 3 — How capital markets transform financial capital

**Capital markets transform financial capital** — households' savings are channelled into **firms' investment**, turning small savings into productive capital.
