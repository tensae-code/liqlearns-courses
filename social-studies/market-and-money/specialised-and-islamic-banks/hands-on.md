# Hands-on — Specialised and Islamic banks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with three borrowers
Take the farmer, the factory owner and the home-builder from the Do it first task. Send each to his specialised bank and say why. Then say how an Islamic bank would treat the merchant who wants a partner, not a lender.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — three bank doors labelled farming, industry and housing, and a fourth door where bank and customer share profit and loss together — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
