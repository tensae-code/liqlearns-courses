# Video script — Specialised and Islamic banks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some banks serve one sector only. These are the specialised banks: an agricultural bank for farming, an industrial bank for industry, a housing bank for building homes. Each learns its sector's needs and lends for them.

Islamic banks work on a different principle. Instead of interest — a fixed charge on a loan — they share in the venture: profit is divided between bank and customer, and loss is shared too. Two kinds of 'other banks': one sorted by sector, one by principle.
