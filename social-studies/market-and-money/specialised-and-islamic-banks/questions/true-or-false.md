# Specialised and Islamic banks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An agricultural bank is a specialised bank.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An Islamic bank charges a fixed interest whatever the venture's result.
**Answer: False — it works on profit-and-loss sharing instead of interest: profit is divided, and loss is shared.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A specialised bank serves every sector equally.
**Answer: False — it serves one sector, such as agriculture, industry or housing.**
