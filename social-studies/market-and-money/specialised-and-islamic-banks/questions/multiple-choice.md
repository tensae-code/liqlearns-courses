# Specialised and Islamic banks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A specialised bank is one that —
a) serves one sector  b) lends only to the state  c) issues the currency
**Answer: a) serves one sector**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Islamic banks work on —
a) fixed interest  b) profit-and-loss sharing  c) barter
**Answer: b) profit-and-loss sharing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A housing bank serves the sector of —
a) farming  b) industry  c) building homes
**Answer: c) building homes**
