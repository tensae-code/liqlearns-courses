# Specialised and Islamic banks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three specialised banks in the lesson and the sector of each.
**Answer:** Agricultural bank (farming), industrial bank (industry), housing bank (building homes).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does an Islamic bank earn from a venture?
**Answer:** By sharing its outcome: profit is divided between bank and customer, and loss is shared too.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference between interest and profit-and-loss sharing?
**Answer:** Interest is a fixed charge owed whatever the result; sharing ties the bank's return to the venture's real profit or loss.
