# Specialised and Islamic banks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banks serving one sector — agricultural, industrial, housing — are _____ banks.
**Answer: specialised**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Islamic banks work on _____-and-loss sharing.
**Answer: profit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Islamic bank's principle stands instead of _____.
**Answer: interest**
