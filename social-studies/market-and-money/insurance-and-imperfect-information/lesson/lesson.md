## Sub lesson 1 — How insurance works

**Insurance** works by **pooling risk**: many pay premiums into a fund that **pays the few who suffer the loss**, so no one faces ruin alone.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moral hazard and adverse selection inside it

**Imperfect information** haunts insurance: **adverse selection** — the highest-risk people most seek cover — and **moral hazard** — the insured take more risk once covered.

## Sub lesson 3 — Government, social insurance and regulation

Because private insurance struggles with these problems, there is **government and social insurance**, and **insurance regulation** policing the market.
