# Insurance and imperfect information — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does insurance work?
**Answer:** Many pay premiums into a fund that pays the few who suffer the loss.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish adverse selection from moral hazard.
**Answer:** Adverse selection: the highest-risk most seek cover. Moral hazard: the insured take more risk once covered.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do government insurance and regulation exist here?
**Answer:** Because imperfect information strains private insurance, the state provides social insurance and regulates the market.
