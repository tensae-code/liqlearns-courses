# Insurance and imperfect information — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insurance works by —
a) pooling many people's risk to pay the few who suffer loss  b) each person facing ruin alone  c) banning risk
**Answer: a) pooling many people's risk to pay the few who suffer loss**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Moral hazard is —
a) taking more risk once insured  b) high-risk people seeking cover  c) paying premiums late
**Answer: a) taking more risk once insured**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Social insurance is provided by —
a) government  b) no one  c) only private firms
**Answer: a) government**
