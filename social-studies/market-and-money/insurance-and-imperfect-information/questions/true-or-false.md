# Insurance and imperfect information — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insurance means each person bears every loss alone.
**Answer: False — insurance pools risk across many people.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Adverse selection and moral hazard are the same thing.
**Answer: False — adverse selection is who seeks cover; moral hazard is behaviour once covered.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Government plays no role in insurance.
**Answer: False — government and social insurance exist, and insurance is regulated.**
