# Why people hold money 1 — the transaction motive — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keynes reframed the demand for money as liquidity preference — the demand for money to hold.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Keynes reframed the demand for money as liquidity preference — the demand for money to hold.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (From the old view to Keynes); sub lesson 2 says: The income motive bridges the gap between pay arriving weekly or monthly and spending happening daily.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the business motive, firms keep ready cash for materials, transport, wages and current expenses, and the amount depends largely on turnover.
**Answer: True**
