# Why people hold money 1 — the transaction motive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "From the old view to Keynes"?
a) Keynes reframed money demand as liquidity preference, split into motives  b) bridging pay arriving weekly or monthly against spending happening daily  c) firms' ready cash for materials, transport, wages and expenses, sized by turnover

**Answer: c) bridging pay arriving weekly or monthly against spending happening daily**
