# Why people hold money 1 — the transaction motive — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keynes reframed the demand for money as _____ — the demand for money to hold.
**Answer: liquidity preference**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The income motive bridges pay arriving monthly against spending happening _____.
**Answer: daily**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A firm's transaction cash depends largely on its _____.
