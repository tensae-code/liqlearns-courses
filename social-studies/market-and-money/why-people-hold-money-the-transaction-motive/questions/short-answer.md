# Why people hold money 1 — the transaction motive — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "From the old view to Keynes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Keynes reframed the demand for money as liquidity preference — the demand for money to hold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The income motive" in your own words.
**Answer:** The income motive bridges the gap between pay arriving weekly or monthly and spending happening daily.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The business motive" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under the business motive, firms keep ready cash for materials, transport, wages and current expenses, and the amount depends largely on turnover.
