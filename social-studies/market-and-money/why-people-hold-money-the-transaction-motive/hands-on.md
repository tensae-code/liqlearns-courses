# Hands-on — Why people hold money 1 — the transaction motive

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Calendar game: a learner receives 'pay' on day 1 and must make it cover daily spending cards for 30 days; the class watches the balance bridge the gap and names the motive.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a monthly pay line above daily spending dots, the gap bridged by a cash balance; a shop till beside it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
