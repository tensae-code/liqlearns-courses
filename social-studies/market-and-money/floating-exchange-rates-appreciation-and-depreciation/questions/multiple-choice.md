# Floating exchange rates — appreciation and depreciation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Set by the market, daily"?
a) Under a float, supply and demand set the rate daily  b) A market-driven weakening is depreciation; a market-driven strengthening is appreciation
**Answer: a) Under a float, supply and demand set the rate daily**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Depreciation and appreciation"?
a) A market-driven weakening is depreciation; a market-driven strengthening is appreciation  b) Under a float, supply and demand set the rate daily
**Answer: a) A market-driven weakening is depreciation; a market-driven strengthening is appreciation**
