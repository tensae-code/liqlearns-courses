# Floating exchange rates — appreciation and depreciation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Set by the market, daily" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under a float, supply and demand set the rate daily.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Depreciation and appreciation" in your own words.
**Answer:** A market-driven weakening is depreciation; a market-driven strengthening is appreciation.
