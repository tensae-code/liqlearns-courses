# Hands-on — Floating exchange rates — appreciation and depreciation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Decree or market
Write four rate-change headlines, two under a fixed regime and two under a float. Under each, write the correct word — devaluation, revaluation, depreciation or appreciation — and the clue that decided it.

## Ride the waves
Draw a currency price line floating on waves across a month, with two rises and two falls labelled appreciation and depreciation in the right places.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
