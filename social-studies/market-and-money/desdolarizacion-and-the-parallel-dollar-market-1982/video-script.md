Desdolarización and the parallel dollar market, 1982 — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: dedollarisation converted dollar contracts into bolivianos and banned foreign-currency transactions; dedollarisation dealt a hard blow to the financial system and provoked capital flight; the ban created a parallel black market where the dollar's price shot up and speculation hit people's savings.
