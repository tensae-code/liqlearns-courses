# Desdolarización and the parallel dollar market, 1982

## Sub lesson 1 — Dollar contracts converted by decree

Minister Aranibar's dedollarisation converted dollar contracts into bolivianos and banned foreign-currency transactions from then on: every debt, deposit and deal written in dollars became boliviano money by decree.

## Sub lesson 2 — A hard blow — capital flight

The measure dealt a hard blow to the national financial system and provoked capital flight: money that could leave, left, rather than be converted at the decree's terms.

## Sub lesson 3 — The parallel black market for dollars

Banned dollars did not disappear — they went underground: a parallel black market formed where the dollar's price shot up and speculation in exchange was common, hitting people's savings. The official money and the real money parted ways.
