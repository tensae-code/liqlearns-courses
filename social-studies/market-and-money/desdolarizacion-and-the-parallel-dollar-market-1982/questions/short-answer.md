# Desdolarización and the parallel dollar market, 1982 — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about dollar contracts converted by decree?
**Answer:** Dedollarisation converted dollar contracts into bolivianos and banned foreign-currency transactions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about a hard blow — capital flight?
**Answer:** Dedollarisation dealt a hard blow to the financial system and provoked capital flight.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the parallel black market for dollars?
**Answer:** The ban created a parallel black market where the dollar's price shot up and speculation hit people's savings.
