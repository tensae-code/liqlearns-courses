# Desdolarización and the parallel dollar market, 1982 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About dollar contracts converted by decree — the lesson teaches that —
a) dedollarisation converted dollar contracts into bolivianos and banned foreign-currency transactions  b) dedollarisation dealt a hard blow to the financial system and provoked capital flight  c) the ban created a parallel black market where the dollar's price shot up and speculation hit people's savings
**Answer: a) dedollarisation converted dollar contracts into bolivianos and banned foreign-currency transactions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About a hard blow — capital flight — the lesson teaches that —
a) dedollarisation converted dollar contracts into bolivianos and banned foreign-currency transactions  b) dedollarisation dealt a hard blow to the financial system and provoked capital flight  c) the ban created a parallel black market where the dollar's price shot up and speculation hit people's savings
**Answer: b) dedollarisation dealt a hard blow to the financial system and provoked capital flight**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About the parallel black market for dollars — the lesson teaches that —
a) dedollarisation converted dollar contracts into bolivianos and banned foreign-currency transactions  b) dedollarisation dealt a hard blow to the financial system and provoked capital flight  c) the ban created a parallel black market where the dollar's price shot up and speculation hit people's savings
**Answer: c) the ban created a parallel black market where the dollar's price shot up and speculation hit people's savings**
