# Desdolarización and the parallel dollar market, 1982 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dedollarisation converted boliviano contracts into dollars.
**Answer: False** — the conversion ran the other way — dollars into bolivianos.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dedollarisation attracted a flood of foreign capital.
**Answer: False** — it provoked capital flight, the book says.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ban ended all dollar trading in Bolivia.
**Answer: False** — it created the parallel black market instead.
