# Hands-on — Start-up capital and working capital

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the payments
Write these payments on slips of paper: land, a building, machinery, equipment, a licence, stock, wages, transport. Sort the slips into two piles — spent once before opening, and spent again and again. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every slip in the wrong pile.

## Say it with things at home
Put two bowls on the table. Into one put stones for investment money and name what each stone buys; into the other put stones for working capital and name what each stone pays for, again and again.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 3 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
