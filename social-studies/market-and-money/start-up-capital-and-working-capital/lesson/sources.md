Uzbekistan's own way: a post-transition primer that teaches the whole market economy through the firm first (entrepreneurship, business plans, Uzbek registration at the khokimiyat), then the state, then the nation — every formula is worked in sum on Uzbek cases (a dekhkan cotton farm's costs, the "Ziyo-LTD" family joint-stock company, Umid's photo studio, the Sadullaev family budget, Ipotekabank deposit offers), with Uzbek data throughout (2004 state budget and tax shares, 1995–2004 inflation, 2004 trade by category, the sum exchange rate) and Eastern-wisdom sidebars (Amir Temur, Sahibkiran) beside Fisher, Ricardo and Hume.

**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Laos, Uzbekistan).**

Laos' own way (Laos Grade 11 Business Combined student): costs and capital are taught with full kip arithmetic — a machine bought for 120,000,000 kip is depreciated over its 5-year life at 24,000,000 kip a year, inside a printed table of asset lives (buildings 20 years at 5%, machinery 8-10 years, vehicles 5 years).

Laos' own way (Laos Grade 11 Business student): the market is defined three ways at once — everyday (a place to buy and sell), economic (the group of buyers and sellers who exchange), and marketing (people with a need and the money to pay) — and costs are drilled with the SMOC sorting exercise (Salary, Material, Other, Capital).
