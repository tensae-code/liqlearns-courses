# Start-up capital and working capital — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Into what two kinds does start-up money split?
**Answer:** Investment money, spent once before opening, and working capital, spent again and again to run the business.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five things the Combined book lists for investment money.
**Answer:** Land, buildings, machinery, equipment and licences.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must working capital cover, beyond the running costs themselves?
**Answer:** The wait before income arrives — the business must be able to keep paying stock, wages and transport until its income starts coming in.
