# Start-up capital and working capital — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Both Laos Grade 11 books teach the capital needed to start a business.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Stock and wages are investment money, spent once before opening.
**Answer: False — stock, wages and transport are working capital, spent again and again; investment money buys land, buildings, machinery, equipment and licences.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A business needs no money left over once it has opened.
**Answer: False — working capital must cover the wait before income arrives.**
