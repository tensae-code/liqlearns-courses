# Start-up capital and working capital — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The capital needed to start a business splits into —
a) one kind only  b) investment money and working capital  c) wages and profit
**Answer: b) investment money and working capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Investment money is spent —
a) again and again on stock  b) once, before opening, on things like land, buildings and licences  c) only after the first year
**Answer: b) once, before opening, on things like land, buildings and licences**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Working capital must also cover —
a) the owner's holidays  b) the wait before income arrives  c) next year's taxes only
**Answer: b) the wait before income arrives**
