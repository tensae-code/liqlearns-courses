# Start-up capital and working capital — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Start-up money splits into investment money and _____ capital.
**Answer: working**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ money is spent once, before opening.
**Answer: Investment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Working capital is spent again and again on stock, _____ and transport.
**Answer: wages**
