# Video script — Start-up capital and working capital

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A new business needs two kinds of money, says the Laos Grade 11 book. Investment money — spent once, before opening: land, buildings, machinery, equipment, licences. And working capital — spent again and again on stock, wages and transport. Working capital must also cover the wait before income arrives. Split the money right, or the business cannot keep running.
