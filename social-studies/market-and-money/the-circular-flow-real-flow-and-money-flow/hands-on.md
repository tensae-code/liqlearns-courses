# Hands-on — The circular flow — real flow and money flow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two circles of learners pass tokens in opposite directions — goods cards one way, birr notes the other. Freeze and have each learner say which flow they are carrying.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the two-ring diagram — goods and services on the inner ring, birr payments on the outer ring, running opposite ways — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
