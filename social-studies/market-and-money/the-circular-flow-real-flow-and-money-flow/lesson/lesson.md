# The circular flow — real flow and money flow

## Do it first

A worker is paid wages on Friday and spends them at a shop on Saturday. Follow the work one way and the birr the other. Which flow is 'real' and which is 'money'? Say both before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — A model of money on the move

The circular flow of income is a visual model of how a currency such as the birr flows through markets among households, firms and government as they exchange goods, factor services and money. It is the economy drawn as a circle instead of a list.

[Picture 24047 — the two-ring diagram — goods and services on the inner ring, birr payments on the outer ring, running opposite ways]

## Sub lesson 2 — Two flows, opposite ways

Every exchange has two sides: a real flow — factor services from owners to producers, and goods and services from producers to buyers — matched by an equal and opposite money flow of factor incomes and spending. The guide specifies the drawing (teacher guide — evidence): the real flow runs clockwise and the money flow runs counter-clockwise.

## Sub lesson 3 — Markets do the coordinating

The diagram contains households, firms, markets for factors of production and markets for goods and services. The guide names the coordinator (teacher guide — evidence): the market system is the coordinating mechanism that aligns the decisions of producers and households.
