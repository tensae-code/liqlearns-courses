# The circular flow — real flow and money flow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A model of money on the move"?
a) a visual model of how currency flows among households, firms and government  b) real flow clockwise, money flow counter-clockwise, equal and opposite  c) the market system coordinates producers' and households' decisions
**Answer: a) a visual model of how currency flows among households, firms and government**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Two flows, opposite ways"?
a) a visual model of how currency flows among households, firms and government  b) the market system coordinates producers' and households' decisions  c) real flow clockwise, money flow counter-clockwise, equal and opposite
**Answer: c) real flow clockwise, money flow counter-clockwise, equal and opposite**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Markets do the coordinating"?
a) real flow clockwise, money flow counter-clockwise, equal and opposite  b) the market system coordinates producers' and households' decisions  c) a visual model of how currency flows among households, firms and government
**Answer: b) the market system coordinates producers' and households' decisions**
