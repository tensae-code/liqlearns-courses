# The circular flow — real flow and money flow — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A model of money on the move" in your own words.
**Answer:** The circular flow of income is a visual model of how a currency like the birr flows through markets among households, firms and government.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Two flows, opposite ways" in your own words.
**Answer:** Every exchange has a real flow matched by an equal and opposite money flow; the real flow runs clockwise and the money flow counter-clockwise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Markets do the coordinating" in your own words.
**Answer:** The market system is the coordinating mechanism that aligns the decisions of producers and households.
