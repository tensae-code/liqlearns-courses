# The circular flow — real flow and money flow — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The circular flow of income is a visual model of how a currency like the birr flows through markets among households, firms and government.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The circular flow of income is a visual model of how a currency like the birr flows through markets among households, firms and government.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (A model of money on the move); sub lesson 2 says: Every exchange has a real flow matched by an equal and opposite money flow; the real flow runs clockwise and the money flow counter-clockwise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market system is the coordinating mechanism that aligns the decisions of producers and households.
**Answer: True**
