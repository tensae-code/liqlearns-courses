# Money as a store of value — and liquidity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Storing purchasing power"?
a) storing purchasing power from when income is received until it is spent  b) liquidity — the ease and speed of turning an asset into a medium of exchange  c) money's purchasing power is subject to change, so it is a poor store of wealth
**Answer: a) storing purchasing power from when income is received until it is spent**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Why hold money then? Liquidity"?
a) storing purchasing power from when income is received until it is spent  b) money's purchasing power is subject to change, so it is a poor store of wealth  c) liquidity — the ease and speed of turning an asset into a medium of exchange
**Answer: c) liquidity — the ease and speed of turning an asset into a medium of exchange**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Its weakness — and two classic definitions"?
a) liquidity — the ease and speed of turning an asset into a medium of exchange  b) money's purchasing power is subject to change, so it is a poor store of wealth  c) storing purchasing power from when income is received until it is spent
**Answer: b) money's purchasing power is subject to change, so it is a poor store of wealth**
