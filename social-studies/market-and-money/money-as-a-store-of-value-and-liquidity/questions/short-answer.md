# Money as a store of value — and liquidity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Storing purchasing power" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money as a store of value keeps purchasing power from when income is received until it is spent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why hold money then? Liquidity" in your own words.
**Answer:** Liquidity is the relative ease and speed with which an asset can be turned into a medium of exchange, and money is unmatched in it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Its weakness — and two classic definitions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money is a poor store of wealth because its purchasing power is subject to change.
