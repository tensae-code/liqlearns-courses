# Money as a store of value — and liquidity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money as a store of value keeps purchasing power from when income is received until it is spent.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money as a store of value keeps purchasing power from when income is received until it is spent.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Storing purchasing power); sub lesson 2 says: Liquidity is the relative ease and speed with which an asset can be turned into a medium of exchange, and money is unmatched in it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money is a poor store of wealth because its purchasing power is subject to change.
**Answer: True**
