# Money as a store of value — and liquidity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money stores _____ from when income is received until it is spent.
**Answer: purchasing power**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** _____ is the ease and speed of turning an asset into a medium of exchange.
**Answer: liquidity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money is a poor store of wealth because its purchasing power is subject to _____.
**Answer: change**
