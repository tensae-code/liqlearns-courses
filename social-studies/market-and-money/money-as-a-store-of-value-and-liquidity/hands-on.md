# Hands-on — Money as a store of value — and liquidity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Auction game: each learner holds a different 'asset card' and must 'pay a bill' — only cash pays instantly; others must find a buyer first while a timer runs. Name liquidity as it bites.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a pay packet being held over time, beside a house and a bond each marked valuable but slow to turn into spending money — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
