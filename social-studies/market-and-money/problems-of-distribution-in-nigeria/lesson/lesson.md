# Problems of distribution in Nigeria

## Sub lesson 1 — Government agencies in distribution

Government agencies take part in distributing goods in Nigeria, trying to keep goods moving and prices fair.

## Sub lesson 2 — The problems of distribution

Poor roads, transport and storage make distribution in Nigeria costly and uneven.

## Sub lesson 3 — What the problems do

Distribution problems raise prices, waste perishable goods and leave some areas undersupplied.
