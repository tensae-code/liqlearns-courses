# Problems of distribution in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government agencies play no part in distribution in Nigeria.
**Answer: False** — Government agencies take part in distributing goods in Nigeria, trying to keep goods moving and prices fair.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distribution in Nigeria is costless and perfectly even.
**Answer: False** — Poor roads, transport and storage make distribution in Nigeria costly and uneven.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Distribution problems lower all prices.
**Answer: False** — Distribution problems raise prices, waste perishable goods and leave some areas undersupplied.
