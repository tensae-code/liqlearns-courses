# Problems of distribution in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government _____ take part in distributing goods in Nigeria.
**Answer: agencies**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Poor roads, transport and _____ make distribution costly and uneven.
**Answer: storage**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Distribution problems raise prices and leave some areas _____.
**Answer: undersupplied**
