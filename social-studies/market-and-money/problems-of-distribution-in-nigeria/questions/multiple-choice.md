# Problems of distribution in Nigeria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About government agencies in distribution — the lesson teaches that —
a) government agencies take part in distributing goods in Nigeria, trying to keep goods moving and prices fair.  b) poor roads, transport and storage make distribution in Nigeria costly and uneven.  c) distribution problems raise prices, waste perishable goods and leave some areas undersupplied.
**Answer: a) government agencies take part in distributing goods in Nigeria, trying to keep goods moving and prices fair.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About the problems of distribution — the lesson teaches that —
a) government agencies take part in distributing goods in Nigeria, trying to keep goods moving and prices fair.  b) poor roads, transport and storage make distribution in Nigeria costly and uneven.  c) distribution problems raise prices, waste perishable goods and leave some areas undersupplied.
**Answer: b) poor roads, transport and storage make distribution in Nigeria costly and uneven.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About what the problems do — the lesson teaches that —
a) government agencies take part in distributing goods in Nigeria, trying to keep goods moving and prices fair.  b) poor roads, transport and storage make distribution in Nigeria costly and uneven.  c) distribution problems raise prices, waste perishable goods and leave some areas undersupplied.
**Answer: c) distribution problems raise prices, waste perishable goods and leave some areas undersupplied.**
