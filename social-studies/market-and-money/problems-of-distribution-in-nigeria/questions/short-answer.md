# Problems of distribution in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What part do government agencies play in distribution?
**Answer:** Government agencies take part in distributing goods in Nigeria, trying to keep goods moving and prices fair.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes distribution in Nigeria costly?
**Answer:** Poor roads, transport and storage make distribution in Nigeria costly and uneven.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do distribution problems do?
**Answer:** Distribution problems raise prices, waste perishable goods and leave some areas undersupplied.
