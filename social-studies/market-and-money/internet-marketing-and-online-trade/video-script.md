# Video script — Internet marketing and online trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The significance and importance of buying and selling over the internet, and the range of goods and services actually traded online.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG: Grade 10 Economics is the labour and capitalism year — two full units on labour (unions, division of labour, labour law, labour costs), then the market system judged by its costs and benefits, trade through television and the internet, and a closing unit interrogating the capitalist ideology itself.

Putting it together: the significance and importance of buying and selling over the internet, and the range of goods and services actually traded online — taught in Unit 4, Topics 5 and 6: Internet marketing; Internet traded items (Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG, printed p. 231).
