# Internet marketing and online trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "The significance and importance of buying and selling"?
**Answer:** The significance and importance of buying and selling over the internet, and the range of goods and services actually traded online.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG: Grade 10 Economics is the labour and capitalism year — two full units on labour (unions, division of labour, labour law, labour costs), then the market system judged by its costs and benefits, trade through television and the internet, and a closing unit interrogating the capitalist ideology itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Putting it together: the significance and importance of"?
**Answer:** Putting it together: the significance and importance of buying and selling over the internet, and the range of goods and services actually traded online — taught in Unit 4, Topics 5 and 6: Internet marketing; Internet traded items (Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG, printed p. 231).
