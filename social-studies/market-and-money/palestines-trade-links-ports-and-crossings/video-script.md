# Video script — Palestine's trade links — ports and crossings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine trades from its map. A central location gave it trade relations across history — and Grade 5 names the gateways: Haifa port on the Mediterranean, the Hejaz railway, the Karama crossing to Jordan, the Rafah crossing to Egypt. And Gaza International Airport: opened in 1998, destroyed by the occupation in 2001. The gateways are Palestine's. The occupation controls the crossings.
