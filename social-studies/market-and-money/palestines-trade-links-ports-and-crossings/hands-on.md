# Hands-on — Palestine's trade links — ports and crossings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Draw a simple map with the sea on one side and Jordan and Egypt on the others. Mark Haifa, the railway, Karama and Rafah with four different markers, and a small plane for Gaza airport — then lay a hand over all of them and say who, in the book, controls the crossings.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the map again from memory with every link labelled and its direction written. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every link you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
