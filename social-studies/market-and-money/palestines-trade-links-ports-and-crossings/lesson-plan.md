# Lesson plan — Palestine's trade links — ports and crossings

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Palestine's trade links — ports and crossings
**Place in the curriculum:** Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Palestine, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Palestine's trade relations across history, from its central location: Haifa port on the Mediterranean, the Hejaz railway, the Karama crossing to Jordan, the Rafah crossing to Egypt, and Gaza International Airport (opened 1998, destroyed by the occupation in 2001) — under an occupation that controls its crossings and transport routes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on one country's own gateways named one by one — sea, rail, land crossings and air — and on who controls them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
