# Palestine's trade links — ports and crossings — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Palestine's central location gave it trade relations across history.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Karama crossing leads to Egypt.
**Answer: False — Karama leads to Jordan; Rafah leads to Egypt**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The occupation controls Palestine's crossings and transport routes.
**Answer: True**
