# Palestine's trade links — ports and crossings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why has Palestine had trade relations across history?
a) its central location  b) it has no neighbours  c) it is an island
**Answer: a) its central location**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names Palestine's trade links as the book gives them?
a) Haifa port, the Hejaz railway, the Karama crossing and the Rafah crossing  b) only airports  c) no links are named
**Answer: a) Haifa port, the Hejaz railway, the Karama crossing and the Rafah crossing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happened to Gaza International Airport?
a) opened 1998, destroyed by the occupation in 2001  b) it is still expanding  c) it was never built
**Answer: a) opened 1998, destroyed by the occupation in 2001**
