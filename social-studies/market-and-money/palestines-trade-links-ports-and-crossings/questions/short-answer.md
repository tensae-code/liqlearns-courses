# Palestine's trade links — ports and crossings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a country's first trade fact, in this lesson?
**Answer:** Where it sits — Palestine's central location put trade routes through it across history.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which link is by sea, and which by rail?
**Answer:** Haifa port, on the Mediterranean, is the sea link; the Hejaz railway is the rail link.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who controls Palestine's crossings and transport routes, as the book states it?
**Answer:** The occupation — the gateways are Palestine's, the control of them is not.
