# Channels of distribution and middlemen

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The channel from producer to consumer

Goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.

## Sub lesson 2 — The middleman's role

Middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.

## Sub lesson 3 — Criticisms of middlemen

Middlemen are criticised for raising prices and taking profit, yet the channel cannot easily do without their work.
