# Video script — Channels of distribution and middlemen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Channels of distribution and middlemen — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.; middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.; middlemen are criticised for raising prices and taking profit, yet the channel cannot easily do without their work..
