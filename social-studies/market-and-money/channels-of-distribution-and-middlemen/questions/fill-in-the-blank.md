# Channels of distribution and middlemen — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods reach consumers through a channel of _____.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Middlemen move, store and _____ goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Middlemen are criticised for raising _____.
