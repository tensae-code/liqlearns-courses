# Channels of distribution and middlemen — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do goods pass from producer to consumer?
**Answer:** Goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do middlemen do?
**Answer:** Middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are middlemen criticised?
**Answer:** Middlemen are criticised for raising prices and taking profit, yet the channel cannot easily do without their work.
