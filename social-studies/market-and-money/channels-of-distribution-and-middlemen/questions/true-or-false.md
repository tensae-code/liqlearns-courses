# Channels of distribution and middlemen — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods never pass through any channel to reach consumers.
**Answer: False** — Goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Middlemen manufacture all the goods they sell.
**Answer: False** — Middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Middlemen are never criticised for anything.
**Answer: False** — Middlemen are criticised for raising prices and taking profit, yet the channel cannot easily do without their work.
