# Channels of distribution and middlemen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the channel from producer to consumer — the lesson teaches that —
a) goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.  b) middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.  c) middlemen are criticised for raising prices and taking profit, yet the channel cannot easily do without their work.
**Answer: a) goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About the middleman's role — the lesson teaches that —
a) goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.  b) middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.  c) middlemen are criticised for raising prices and taking profit, yet the channel cannot easily do without their work.
**Answer: b) middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About criticisms of middlemen — the lesson teaches that —
a) goods pass from producer to consumer through a channel of distribution, either directly or through middlemen.  b) middlemen — wholesalers and retailers — move, store and sell goods, bringing them within the consumer's reach.  c) middlemen are criticised for raising prices and taking profit, yet the channel cannot easily do without their work.
