# Hands-on — What moves exchange rates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Eight winds
Draw one currency flag with eight labelled winds, arrows showing which push it up and which down. Every wind from the lesson must appear with its correct direction.

## Forecast a week
Invent a week of news for one country (inflation print, rate decision, debt warning, export boom). For each item, name the wind and the currency's likely move.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
