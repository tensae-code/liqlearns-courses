# What moves exchange rates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Prices, rates and the payments balance"?
a) Low inflation appreciates, high interest rates attract capital, and a current-account deficit depreciates  b) Government debt scares investors out; rising export prices strengthen the currency; instability repels capital  c) Recession repels capital through falling rates, and speculation feeds on itself
**Answer: a) Low inflation appreciates, high interest rates attract capital, and a current-account deficit depreciates**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Debt, trade terms and stability"?
a) Government debt scares investors out; rising export prices strengthen the currency; instability repels capital  b) Recession repels capital through falling rates, and speculation feeds on itself  c) Low inflation appreciates, high interest rates attract capital, and a current-account deficit depreciates
**Answer: a) Government debt scares investors out; rising export prices strengthen the currency; instability repels capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Recession and speculation"?
a) Recession repels capital through falling rates, and speculation feeds on itself  b) Low inflation appreciates, high interest rates attract capital, and a current-account deficit depreciates  c) Government debt scares investors out; rising export prices strengthen the currency; instability repels capital
**Answer: a) Recession repels capital through falling rates, and speculation feeds on itself**
