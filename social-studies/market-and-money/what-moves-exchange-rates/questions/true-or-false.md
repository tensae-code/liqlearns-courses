# What moves exchange rates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Low inflation appreciates, high interest rates attract capital, and a current-account deficit depreciates.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government debt scares investors out; rising export prices strengthen the currency; instability repels capital.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Recession repels capital through falling rates, and speculation feeds on itself.
**Answer: True**
