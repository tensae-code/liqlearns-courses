# What moves exchange rates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A current-account deficit tends to ____ the currency.
**Answer: depreciate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rising export prices improve the ____ of trade and strengthen the currency.
**Answer: terms**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An expected rise that causes the rise itself is ____.
**Answer: speculation**
