# What moves exchange rates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Prices, rates and the payments balance" in your own words.
**Answer:** Low inflation appreciates, high interest rates attract capital, and a current-account deficit depreciates.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Debt, trade terms and stability" in your own words.
**Answer:** Government debt scares investors out; rising export prices strengthen the currency; instability repels capital.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Recession and speculation" in your own words.
**Answer:** Recession repels capital through falling rates, and speculation feeds on itself.
