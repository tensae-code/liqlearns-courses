# Money as a standard of deferred payments

## Do it first

A farmer needs seed now and can pay after the harvest. In goats, the debt's size argues all year. In birr, what exactly does he owe in six months? Say why money settles the argument in advance.

## Sub lesson 1 — Buy now, pay later

As a standard of deferred payments, money lets people buy now and pay later, and lend now and collect later. Because a birr debt is stated in a stable unit, both sides know today what tomorrow's payment means.

## Sub lesson 2 — Savers to borrowers

Saved money is borrowed and channelled into investment, transferring spending power from savers to borrowers. The person who does not spend today funds the person who builds today — through money, not through finding each other.

## Sub lesson 3 — Resources to better uses

The flow moves resources from less productive to more productive uses: idle balances become machines, seed and workshops. Deferred payment is thus not only a convenience — it is one of the engines that rearranges an economy's resources.
