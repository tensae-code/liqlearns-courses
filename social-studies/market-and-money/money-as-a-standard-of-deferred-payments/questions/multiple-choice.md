# Money as a standard of deferred payments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Buy now, pay later"?
a) a stable unit in which future payments are stated  b) saved money channelled into investment, spending power moved from savers to borrowers  c) resources moved from less productive to more productive uses
**Answer: a) a stable unit in which future payments are stated**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Savers to borrowers"?
a) a stable unit in which future payments are stated  b) resources moved from less productive to more productive uses  c) saved money channelled into investment, spending power moved from savers to borrowers
**Answer: c) saved money channelled into investment, spending power moved from savers to borrowers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Resources to better uses"?
a) saved money channelled into investment, spending power moved from savers to borrowers  b) resources moved from less productive to more productive uses  c) a stable unit in which future payments are stated
**Answer: b) resources moved from less productive to more productive uses**
