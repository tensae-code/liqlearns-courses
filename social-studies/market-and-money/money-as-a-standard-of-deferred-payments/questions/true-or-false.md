# Money as a standard of deferred payments — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money as a standard of deferred payments lets people buy now and pay later, and lend now and collect later.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money as a standard of deferred payments lets people buy now and pay later, and lend now and collect later.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Buy now, pay later); sub lesson 2 says: Saved money is channelled into investment, transferring spending power from savers to borrowers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Lending through money moves resources from less productive to more productive uses.
**Answer: True**
