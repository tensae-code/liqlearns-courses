# Money as a standard of deferred payments — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Buy now, pay later" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money as a standard of deferred payments lets people buy now and pay later, and lend now and collect later.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Savers to borrowers" in your own words.
**Answer:** Saved money is channelled into investment, transferring spending power from savers to borrowers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Resources to better uses" in your own words.
**Answer:** Lending through money moves resources from less productive to more productive uses.
