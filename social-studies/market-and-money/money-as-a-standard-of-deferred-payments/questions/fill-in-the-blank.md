# Money as a standard of deferred payments — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money as a standard of _____ payments lets people buy now and pay later.
**Answer: deferred**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deferred payments transfer spending power from _____ to borrowers.
**Answer: savers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Deferred payments move resources from less _____ to more productive uses.
**Answer: productive**
