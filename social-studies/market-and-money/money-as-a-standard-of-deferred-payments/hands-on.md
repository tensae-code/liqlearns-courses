# Hands-on — Money as a standard of deferred payments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Chain game: savers hand play birr to a 'bank', which lends to a 'builder' who stacks blocks into a project, then repays with a small extra stack. Narrate each arrow with the lesson's words.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a loan arrow from a saver through a bank to a borrower building something, with a repay-later arrow returning — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
