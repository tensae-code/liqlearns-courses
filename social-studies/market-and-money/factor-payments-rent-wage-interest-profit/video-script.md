# Video script — Factor payments — rent, wage, interest, profit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 9 workbook closes its factors chapter with the rewards of the factors of production — each factor is paid in its own coin: rent for land, wage for labour, interest for capital, profit for entrepreneurship.

Rent is the payment for the use of land and natural resources — paid to the owner who lets others work what they own. Wage (and salary) is the payment for labour — for work done over a time or for a task.

Interest is the payment for capital — for the use of money or equipment over time, the price of waiting and of the risk of lending. Profit is the entrepreneur's reward: what remains after rent, wages and interest are paid — which can be large, small, or negative as a loss.
