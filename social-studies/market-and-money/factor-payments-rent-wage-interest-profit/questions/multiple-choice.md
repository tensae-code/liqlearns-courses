# Factor payments — rent, wage, interest, profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The payment for capital is —
a) rent
b) interest
c) profit
**Answer: b) interest**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A wage is the payment for —
a) land
b) labour
c) capital
**Answer: b) labour**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Profit is —
a) promised in advance like wages
b) what remains after the other payments — possibly a loss
c) a tax
**Answer: b) what remains after the other payments — possibly a loss**
