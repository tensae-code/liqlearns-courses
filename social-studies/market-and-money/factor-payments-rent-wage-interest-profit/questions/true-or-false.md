# Factor payments — rent, wage, interest, profit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One person can receive several factor payments at once.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rent and wage are contracted in advance, before production.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Interest is the price of the use of capital over time.
**Answer: True**
