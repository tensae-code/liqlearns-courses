# Factor payments — rent, wage, interest, profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four factor payments with their factors.
**Answer:** Land — rent; labour — wage; capital — interest; entrepreneurship — profit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do rent and wage differ from profit in timing?
**Answer:** They are contracted in advance; profit is what remains after, paid by result.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is profit called the uncertain payment?
**Answer:** Because it is only the residue after rent, wages and interest — it can be large, small or negative.
