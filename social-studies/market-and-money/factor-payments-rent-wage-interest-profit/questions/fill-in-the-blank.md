# Factor payments — rent, wage, interest, profit — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rent for land, wage for labour, _____ for capital, profit for entrepreneurship.
**Answer: interest**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rent pays for the use of _____ and natural resources.
**Answer: land**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The entrepreneur is paid _____, by result, and may make a loss.
**Answer: last**
