# Pakistan's banking system

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Commercial banks — the National Bank of Pakistan

The Class 12 banking chapter turns home: Pakistan's **commercial banks**, with the **National Bank of Pakistan** as the worked example — its **capital and management** set out as the model of a big state commercial bank.

## Sub lesson 2 — Specialised banks

Beside the commercial banks stand the **specialised banks**: the **Zarai Taraqiatiati Bank** for agriculture and the **Industrial Development Bank of Pakistan** for industry — each built for one sector, with the **functions of industrial development banks** taught in their own right.

## Sub lesson 3 — Banks in development; the State Bank's role

The chapter closes with the **role of banks in economic development** and the **role of the State Bank**: rehabilitation of the banking system, training of bank staff, formation of the capital market, balanced distribution of credit, control of inflation and deflation, promotion of exports, and provision of finance to the state.
