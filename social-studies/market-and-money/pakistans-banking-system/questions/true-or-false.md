# Pakistan's banking system — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The National Bank of Pakistan is a specialised agricultural bank in the book.
**Answer: False** — it is the worked example of a commercial bank.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Specialised banks serve every sector equally, with no focus.
**Answer: False** — each is built for one sector.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The State Bank plays no role in exports or credit distribution.
**Answer: False** — both are named among its roles.
