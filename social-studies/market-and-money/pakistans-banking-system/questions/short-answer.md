# Pakistan's banking system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which bank is the book's example of a commercial bank?
**Answer:** The National Bank of Pakistan, its capital and management set out.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Pakistan's specialised banks from the book.
**Answer:** The Zarai Taraqiatiati Bank (agriculture) and the Industrial Development Bank of Pakistan (industry).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three roles of the State Bank from the book.
**Answer:** Any three of: rehabilitation of the banking system, training of bank staff, formation of the capital market, balanced distribution of credit, control of inflation and deflation, promotion of exports, provision of finance to the state.
