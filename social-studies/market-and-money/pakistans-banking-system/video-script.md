# Video script — Pakistan's banking system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Pakistan's banking system — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: the National Bank of Pakistan is the book's worked example of a commercial bank; specialised banks serve one sector — Zarai Taraqiatiati for agriculture, IDBP for industry; the State Bank's roles include training bank staff, forming the capital market and promoting exports.
