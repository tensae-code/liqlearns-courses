# Video script — Saving and investment — where to put savings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's Grade 10 Economics book, in Lesson 14 "Saving and investment", names the first home for savings: **bank deposit accounts**. Money placed in the bank is kept by the banking system and put to work in the economy, instead of sitting idle in a drawer — the saver hands the money to an institution whose business is holding and using deposits. The second home the book names is **shares**: buying a share is becoming a **part-owner of a company**. The saver's money goes into a real business — its workshops, machines and trade — and the saver's return rises and falls with that company's fortune, because the saver now owns a piece of it. The lesson closes with a **class debate** the book sets its students: parking savings in **gold coins, currency (dollars), housing and cars** — things bought to hold value while producing nothing — against **keeping money in production**, where savings become deposits and shares that fund real making. The book's own running story has already shown the cost of the first road: money parked outside production is money the economy cannot use, and the lesson leaves the class to argue it out.
