# Saving and investment — where to put savings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to money placed in a bank deposit account?
**Answer:** The banking system keeps it and puts it to work in the economy, instead of the money sitting idle in a drawer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a share, and how does the share-owner's return behave?
**Answer:** A share is part-ownership of a company: the saver's money goes into a real business, and the return rises and falls with that company's fortune.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the class debate the book sets, with both sides.
**Answer:** Parking savings in gold coins, currency, housing and cars — bought to hold value while producing nothing — against keeping money in production, where savings become deposits and shares that fund real making.
