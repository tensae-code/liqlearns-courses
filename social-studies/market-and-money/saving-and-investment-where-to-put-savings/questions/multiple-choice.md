# Saving and investment — where to put savings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first home for savings the book names is —
a) a drawer  b) bank deposit accounts  c) a gold coin
**Answer: b) bank deposit accounts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Buying a share means —
a) lending the company a drawer  b) becoming a part-owner of a company  c) parking money in gold
**Answer: b) becoming a part-owner of a company**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The class debate sets gold coins, dollars, housing and cars against —
a) spending everything  b) keeping money in production  c) hiding money at home
**Answer: b) keeping money in production**
