# Saving and investment — where to put savings — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money in a bank deposit account sits idle and is used by no one.
**Answer: False — Deposits are kept by the banking system and put to work in the economy — that is the bank's business.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Buying a share makes the buyer a part-owner of a company.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Gold coins, dollars, housing and cars bought to hold value produce new goods while they wait.
**Answer: False — They are things bought to hold value while producing nothing — the debate's other side is keeping money in production, where savings fund real making.**
