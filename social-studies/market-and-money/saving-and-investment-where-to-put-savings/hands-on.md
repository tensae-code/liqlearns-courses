# Hands-on — Saving and investment — where to put savings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Place the savings
Take a pretend saved sum. Split it three ways — bank deposit, shares, gold coins — and write one sentence for each: what the money does while it waits.

## Argue both sides
Hold the book's debate in two voices, two sentences each: park it in gold, dollars, housing and cars — or keep it in production. Then say which voice the book's running story supports.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 2 aloud — shares as part-ownership — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
