# Forms of enterprise ownership in Venezuela — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private capital means capital belonging to one person, in the book's classification.
**Answer: True** — social = communities, private = one person, public = the State.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Indirect social-property enterprises are owned by the State in the community's name and transferable to communities.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's definition of the form.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Recovered or nationalised enterprises are controlled by their workers, in the book's classification.
**Answer: True** — one of the ownership forms the chapter lists.
