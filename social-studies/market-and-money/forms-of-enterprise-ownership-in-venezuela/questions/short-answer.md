# Forms of enterprise ownership in Venezuela — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three kinds of capital by owner in the book's classification.
**Answer:** Social (communities), private (one person), and public (the State).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish mixed enterprises from social distribution enterprises.
**Answer:** Mixed: capital shared public/private (Petrozumano S.A.). Social distribution: gains reinvested for social benefit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are self-management enterprises, and what does FONDEN do?
**Answer:** Self-management enterprises are cooperatives using their own resources; FONDEN provides resources for industrial development.
