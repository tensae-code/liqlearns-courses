# Forms of enterprise ownership in Venezuela — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An industry's capital in Venezuela may be —
a) social (communities), private (one person) or public (the State)  b) only foreign  c) only private
**Answer: a) social (communities), private (one person) or public (the State)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A mixed enterprise is one whose capital is —
a) shared public/private, like Petrozumano S.A. (PDVSA and China capital)  b) held by one person only  c) held by a foreign state only
**Answer: a) shared public/private, like Petrozumano S.A. (PDVSA and China capital)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Self-management enterprises are —
a) cooperatives using their own resources  b) firms run by foreign managers  c) firms with no workers
**Answer: a) cooperatives using their own resources**
