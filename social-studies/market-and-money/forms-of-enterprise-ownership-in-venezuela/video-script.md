# Video script — Forms of enterprise ownership in Venezuela

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Social (communities), private (one person), and public (the State).

Mixed: capital shared public/private (Petrozumano S.A.). Social distribution: gains reinvested for social benefit.

Self-management enterprises are cooperatives using their own resources; FONDEN provides resources for industrial development.
