# Cash crop farming — growing for sale — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about a crop grown for sale?
**Answer:** A cash crop is grown for sale or profit, not subsistence or barter. It is also called commercial farming.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about bank loans and the price?
**Answer:** Farmers borrow money from banks to buy seed and repay the loan only after selling the harvest. The price of cash crops depends on supply and demand.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about advantages of cash crop farming?
**Answer:** Cash crop farming brings profit and sustenance for farmers, employment in processing, economic diversification and revenue for government.
