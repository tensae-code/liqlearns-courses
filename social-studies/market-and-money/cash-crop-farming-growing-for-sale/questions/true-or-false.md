# Cash crop farming — growing for sale — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cash crop is grown for sale or profit, not subsistence or barter. It is also called commercial farming.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farmers borrow money from banks to buy seed and repay the loan only after selling the harvest. The price of cash crops depends on supply and demand.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cash crop farming brings profit and sustenance for farmers, employment in processing, economic diversification and revenue for government.
**Answer: True**
