## Sub lesson 1 — A crop grown for sale

A **cash crop is grown for sale or profit**, not subsistence or barter. It is also called **commercial farming**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bank loans and the price

Farmers **borrow money from banks to buy seed** and **repay the loan only after selling the harvest**. The **price of cash crops depends on supply and demand**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Advantages of cash crop farming

Cash crop farming brings **profit and sustenance for farmers**, **employment in processing**, **economic diversification** and **revenue for government**.
