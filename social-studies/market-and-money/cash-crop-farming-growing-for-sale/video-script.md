# Video script — Cash crop farming — growing for sale

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cash crop farming — growing for sale — as South Sudan's book teaches it, says the narrator: A cash crop is grown for sale or profit, not subsistence or barter (also called commercial farming). Farmers borrow money from banks to buy seed and repay the loan only after selling the harvest, and the price of cash crops depends on supply and demand. Its advantages are profit and sustenance for farmers, employment in processing, economic diversification and revenue for government.
