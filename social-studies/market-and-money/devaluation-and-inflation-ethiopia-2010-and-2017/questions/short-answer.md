# Devaluation and inflation — Ethiopia 2010 and 2017 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "August 2010 — 20% devaluation, 33% inflation" in your own words.
**Answer:** The 2010 devaluation took the birr from 13.63 to 16.35 per dollar, and about 33% inflation followed in 2011.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "October 2017 — 15% more" in your own words.
**Answer:** The 15% devaluation of 10 October 2017 aimed to encourage exports and relieve the FX shortage.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The general lesson" in your own words.
**Answer:** In Ethiopia devaluation reliably feeds inflation; CPI inflation rose from 2.8% in 2010 to 20.2% in 2020.
