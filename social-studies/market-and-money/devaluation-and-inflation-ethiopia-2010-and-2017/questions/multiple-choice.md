# Devaluation and inflation — Ethiopia 2010 and 2017 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "August 2010 — 20% devaluation, 33% inflation"?
a) The 2010 devaluation took the birr from 13.63 to 16.35 per dollar, and about 33% inflation followed in 2011  b) The 15% devaluation of 10 October 2017 aimed to encourage exports and relieve the FX shortage  c) In Ethiopia devaluation reliably feeds inflation; CPI inflation rose from 2.8% in 2010 to 20.2% in 2020
**Answer: a) The 2010 devaluation took the birr from 13.63 to 16.35 per dollar, and about 33% inflation followed in 2011**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "October 2017 — 15% more"?
a) The 15% devaluation of 10 October 2017 aimed to encourage exports and relieve the FX shortage  b) In Ethiopia devaluation reliably feeds inflation; CPI inflation rose from 2.8% in 2010 to 20.2% in 2020  c) The 2010 devaluation took the birr from 13.63 to 16.35 per dollar, and about 33% inflation followed in 2011
**Answer: a) The 15% devaluation of 10 October 2017 aimed to encourage exports and relieve the FX shortage**
