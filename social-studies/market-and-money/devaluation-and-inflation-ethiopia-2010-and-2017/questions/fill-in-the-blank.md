# Devaluation and inflation — Ethiopia 2010 and 2017 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The August 2010 devaluation moved the rate from 13.63 to ____ birr per dollar.
**Answer: 16.35**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The devaluation of 10 October 2017 was ____ percent.
**Answer: 15**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Measured CPI inflation in 2020 was ____ percent.
**Answer: 20.2**
