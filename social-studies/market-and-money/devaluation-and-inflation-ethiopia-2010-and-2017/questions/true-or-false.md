# Devaluation and inflation — Ethiopia 2010 and 2017 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 2010 devaluation took the birr from 13.63 to 16.35 per dollar, and about 33% inflation followed in 2011.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 15% devaluation of 10 October 2017 aimed to encourage exports and relieve the FX shortage.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Ethiopia devaluation reliably feeds inflation; CPI inflation rose from 2.8% in 2010 to 20.2% in 2020.
**Answer: True**
