# Hands-on — Devaluation and inflation — Ethiopia 2010 and 2017

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain the feeding
Draw the chain from devaluation to inflation in five links (rate, import prices in birr, costs, shop prices, CPI). Then mark the link where the export gain enters, going the other way.

## Check both percents
Verify the 20% of 2010 from 13.63 and 16.35 with your own division. Then compute what a 15% devaluation does to a rate of 20 birr per dollar (20 × 1.15 = 23).

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
