# Marketing strategies — the four Ps

## Do it first

A firm makes a good juice, but nobody buys it. Before you read on, say: what must the firm do besides making the product, so that clients get it, want it and buy it? Hold your answer — the lesson works it the Guatemalan book's way, through the four Ps.

## Sub lesson 1 — Marketing — bringing products and services to clients

In Guatemala's Grade 8 entrepreneurship guide, the "Mercadeo" reading defines **marketing** (*mercadeo*) as **everything needed to bring products and services to clients so they are satisfied and the firm gains**.

Marketing **investigates and analyses the market**, **plans strategies to position itself**, and **evaluates constantly**. It is not one act of selling: it is the firm's whole work of knowing its market, placing itself in it, and checking, again and again, whether its plan is working.

## Sub lesson 2 — Product and price — what the client is really buying

The reading works marketing through **four strategies, the "4P"**: **product, price, place (*plaza*) and promotion**.

The **product** strategy starts from what the client is really buying — which is not always the object on the shelf. **Phone companies sell closeness and safety**; **appliance shops sell small instalments, not the stove**. A firm that knows what its client is really buying can shape its product to that need.

**Price** is the second P: the strategy by which the firm fixes what the client pays for that product.

## Sub lesson 3 — Place and promotion — reaching the client

**Place** (*plaza*) is the third P: where and how the product reaches the client.

**Promotion** is the fourth P, and the reading names its parts: **publicidad** (advertising), **sales promotion**, **personal selling**, **publicity** and **public relations**. Promotion is how the firm makes its product known and wanted — by paid messages, by offers, by a seller face to face, and by the standing the firm builds with the public.
