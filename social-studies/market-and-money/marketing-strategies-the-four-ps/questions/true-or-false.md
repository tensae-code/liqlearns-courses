# Marketing strategies — the four Ps — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marketing investigates and analyses the market, plans strategies to position itself, and evaluates constantly.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Appliance shops, in the book's example, sell the stove itself and nothing else.
**Answer: False** — they sell small instalments, not the stove: what the client is really buying is the way of paying.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Place (plaza) is one of the four Ps.
**Answer: True**
