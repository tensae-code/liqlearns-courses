# Marketing strategies — the four Ps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marketing (mercadeo) is —
a) everything needed to bring products and services to clients so they are satisfied and the firm gains  b) only advertising  c) only the act of selling at the till
**Answer: a) everything needed to bring products and services to clients so they are satisfied and the firm gains**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's examples, phone companies sell —
a) handsets only  b) closeness and safety  c) telephone poles
**Answer: b) closeness and safety**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which list names promotion's parts as the reading gives them?
a) rent, wages, depreciation  b) publicidad, sales promotion, personal selling, publicity, public relations  c) wholesale, retail, export
**Answer: b) publicidad, sales promotion, personal selling, publicity, public relations**
