# Marketing strategies — the four Ps — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does marketing do, in the reading's three verbs?
**Answer:** It investigates and analyses the market, plans strategies to position itself, and evaluates constantly — all to bring products and services to clients so they are satisfied and the firm gains.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the product strategy start from what the client is really buying? Give the book's two examples.
**Answer:** Because the client buys a need met, not only an object: phone companies sell closeness and safety, and appliance shops sell small instalments, not the stove.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four Ps and the parts of promotion.
**Answer:** Product, price, place (plaza) and promotion; promotion's parts are publicidad (advertising), sales promotion, personal selling, publicity and public relations.
