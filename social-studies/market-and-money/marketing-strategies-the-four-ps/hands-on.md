# Hands-on — Marketing strategies — the four Ps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name what is really bought
Pick three products sold near you. For each, write the object — then write what the client is really buying, the book's way. Compare your answers with the lesson's two examples.

## Build a four-P plan
Take one product and write its four Ps on four pieces of paper — product, price, place, promotion. Under promotion, name which of its parts you would use: publicidad, sales promotion, personal selling, publicity or public relations, and why.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
