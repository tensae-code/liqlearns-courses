# Video script — Marketing strategies — the four Ps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Making a product is only half the work. Marketing is everything needed to bring it to clients so they are satisfied and the firm gains.

Guatemala's book works it through four strategies — the four Ps: product, price, place and promotion.

And the product is what the client is really buying: phone companies sell closeness and safety, and appliance shops sell small instalments — not the stove.
