# The Nigerian Stock Exchange and securities markets

## Sub lesson 1 — The Nigerian Stock Exchange

The Nigerian Stock Exchange is where shares and other securities are bought and sold.

## Sub lesson 2 — Second-tier and alternative markets

The second-tier securities market and ASeM give smaller companies a place to raise capital under lighter rules.

## Sub lesson 3 — Shares for the investor

For the investor, shares offer ownership, dividends and the chance of gain — with risk.
