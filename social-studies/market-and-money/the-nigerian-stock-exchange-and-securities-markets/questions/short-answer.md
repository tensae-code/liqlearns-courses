# The Nigerian Stock Exchange and securities markets — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens on the Nigerian Stock Exchange?
**Answer:** The Nigerian Stock Exchange is where shares and other securities are bought and sold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the second-tier market and ASeM give smaller companies?
**Answer:** The second-tier securities market and ASeM give smaller companies a place to raise capital under lighter rules.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do shares offer the investor?
**Answer:** For the investor, shares offer ownership, dividends and the chance of gain — with risk.
