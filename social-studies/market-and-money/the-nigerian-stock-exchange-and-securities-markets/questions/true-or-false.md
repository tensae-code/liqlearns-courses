# The Nigerian Stock Exchange and securities markets — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shares are never bought or sold on the Stock Exchange.
**Answer: False** — The Nigerian Stock Exchange is where shares and other securities are bought and sold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Smaller companies have no market where they can raise capital.
**Answer: False** — The second-tier securities market and ASeM give smaller companies a place to raise capital under lighter rules.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Shares carry no risk of any kind.
**Answer: False** — For the investor, shares offer ownership, dividends and the chance of gain — with risk.
