# The Nigerian Stock Exchange and securities markets — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Nigerian Stock Exchange is where _____ and securities are bought and sold.
**Answer: shares**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second-tier market and ASeM let smaller companies raise _____.
**Answer: capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Shares offer ownership, dividends and the chance of gain — with _____.
**Answer: risk**
