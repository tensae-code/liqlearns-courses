# The Nigerian Stock Exchange and securities markets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About the nigerian stock exchange — the lesson teaches that —
a) the Nigerian Stock Exchange is where shares and other securities are bought and sold.  b) the second-tier securities market and ASeM give smaller companies a place to raise capital under lighter rules.  c) for the investor, shares offer ownership, dividends and the chance of gain — with risk.
