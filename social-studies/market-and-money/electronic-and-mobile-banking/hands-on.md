# Hands-on — Electronic and mobile banking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Sort service cards — ATM withdrawal, salary landing by direct deposit, debit-card payment, credit-card payment, phone transfer — into 'my money' and 'bank's money' piles, then defend the mobile-banking cards' gains and risks aloud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a phone, an ATM and a card as three doors into the same account, with two cards labelled your money and borrowed money — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
