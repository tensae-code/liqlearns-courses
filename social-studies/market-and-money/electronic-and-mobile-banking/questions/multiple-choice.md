# Electronic and mobile banking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "E-banking and its three aspects"?
a) electronic transfer via ATMs, direct deposit and debit cards, plus mobile  b) debit spends your money and stops at the balance; credit spends the bank's, at interest  c) anywhere-anytime access set against security risks and limited capability
**Answer: a) electronic transfer via ATMs, direct deposit and debit cards, plus mobile**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Debit versus credit — whose money?"?
a) electronic transfer via ATMs, direct deposit and debit cards, plus mobile  b) anywhere-anytime access set against security risks and limited capability  c) debit spends your money and stops at the balance; credit spends the bank's, at interest
**Answer: c) debit spends your money and stops at the balance; credit spends the bank's, at interest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Mobile banking weighed"?
a) debit spends your money and stops at the balance; credit spends the bank's, at interest  b) anywhere-anytime access set against security risks and limited capability  c) electronic transfer via ATMs, direct deposit and debit cards, plus mobile
**Answer: b) anywhere-anytime access set against security risks and limited capability**
