# Electronic and mobile banking — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E-banking means electronic funds transfer — ATMs, direct deposit and debit cards are its key aspects.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A debit card spends your own money, while a credit card spends the bank's money at interest.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mobile banking trades anywhere-anytime access against security risks and limited capability.
**Answer: True**
