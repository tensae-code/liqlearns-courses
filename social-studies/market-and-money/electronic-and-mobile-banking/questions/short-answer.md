# Electronic and mobile banking — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "E-banking and its three aspects" in your own words.
**Answer:** E-banking means electronic funds transfer — ATMs, direct deposit and debit cards are its key aspects.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Debit versus credit — whose money?" in your own words.
**Answer:** A debit card spends your own money, while a credit card spends the bank's money at interest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Mobile banking weighed" in your own words.
**Answer:** Mobile banking trades anywhere-anytime access against security risks and limited capability.
