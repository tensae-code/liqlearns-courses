# Electronic and mobile banking — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E-banking is defined as _____ — money moved without paper or branch visits.
**Answer: electronic funds transfer**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A debit card spends _____ money and cannot overspend the account.
**Answer: your own**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mobile banking's gains come with two costs, the first being _____ — the price of access anywhere.
**Answer: security risks**
