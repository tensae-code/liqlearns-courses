Grade 10 teacher-guide note (placement 15): the Ethiopian T-bill maturities come from *Economics, Teacher Guide Book, Grade 10*, printed p. 69 (teacher guide — evidence). The two Grade 10 books disagree: the guide's key gives maturities of 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, while the student textbook says T-bill maturities run from three weeks to six months. The build teaches the guide's Ethiopia-specific list and records the student text's shorter range here, as the placements direct.

**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

Completeness note carried from the placements: §5.2 lists five money-market instruments but details only three — repos and bankers' acceptances are named, not explained. They are named here exactly as the book names them, and the sources record that the book does not detail them; nothing is invented for them.
