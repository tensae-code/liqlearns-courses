# Money markets and capital markets — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In financial markets, saving and investment are _____ — the two sides need never meet.
**Answer: decoupled**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The money market trades funds for up to _____ — short-term funds only.
**Answer: one year**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debt running over 10 years in the capital market is carried by _____ — notes run up to 10.
**Answer: bonds**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Ethiopian T-bills are issued at 1, 3, 6 and _____ maturities, auctioned at a discount to par.
**Answer: 12-month**
