# Money markets and capital markets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Saving and investment decoupled"?
a) the market standing between savers and investors who never meet  b) short-term instruments: T-bills, commercial paper, CDs, repos, bankers' acceptances  c) long-term funds: stock, syndicated loans, notes to 10 years, bonds over 10 years
**Answer: a) the market standing between savers and investors who never meet**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The money market — short-term funds"?
a) the market standing between savers and investors who never meet  b) long-term funds: stock, syndicated loans, notes to 10 years, bonds over 10 years  c) short-term instruments: T-bills, commercial paper, CDs, repos, bankers' acceptances
**Answer: c) short-term instruments: T-bills, commercial paper, CDs, repos, bankers' acceptances**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The capital market — long-term funds"?
a) short-term instruments: T-bills, commercial paper, CDs, repos, bankers' acceptances  b) long-term funds: stock, syndicated loans, notes to 10 years, bonds over 10 years  c) the market standing between savers and investors who never meet
**Answer: b) long-term funds: stock, syndicated loans, notes to 10 years, bonds over 10 years**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopian T-bill maturities (teacher guide)"?
a) Ethiopian T-bills at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, sold at a discount to par  b) a different part of this lesson, as stated in the earlier sub lessons  c) the opposite claim to the one this sub lesson teaches
**Answer: a) Ethiopian T-bills at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, sold at a discount to par**
