# Money markets and capital markets — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Saving and investment decoupled" in your own words.
**Answer:** Financial markets decouple saving from investment — savers and investors need never meet.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The money market — short-term funds" in your own words.
**Answer:** The money market trades short-term funds of up to one year.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The capital market — long-term funds" in your own words.
**Answer:** The capital market trades long-term funds — over one year, or perpetual like stock.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "Ethiopian T-bill maturities (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ethiopian treasury bills are issued at 1-month, 3-month, 6-month and 12-month maturities.
