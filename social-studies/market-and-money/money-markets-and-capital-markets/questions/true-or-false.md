# Money markets and capital markets — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Financial markets decouple saving from investment — savers and investors need never meet.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The money market trades short-term funds of up to one year.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The capital market trades long-term funds — over one year, or perpetual like stock.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Ethiopian treasury bills are issued at 1-month, 3-month, 6-month and 12-month maturities.
**Answer: True**
