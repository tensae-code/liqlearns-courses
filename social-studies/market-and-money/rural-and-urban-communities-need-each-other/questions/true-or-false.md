# Rural and urban communities need each other — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rural and urban communities need each other and exchange products and services — farm produce goes to city supermarkets, city products satisfy countryside needs
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about rural and urban communities need each other and.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about rural and urban communities need each other and — more; rural and urban communities need each other and is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rural and urban communities need each other and exchange products and services — farm produce goes to city supermarkets, city products satisfy countryside needs
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
