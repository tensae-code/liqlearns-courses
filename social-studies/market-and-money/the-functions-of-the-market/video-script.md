# Video script — The functions of the market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A market does three jobs at once, and Vietnam's Grade 10 book names them: recognition, information and regulation.

Recognition: a good proves its social usefulness only by selling, at a price. Information: sales and prices signal how demand is changing — a garment firm re-plans from its sales reports.

Regulation: everyone adjusts on those signals — as when disease first flooded, then emptied, Vietnam's pork supply and prices swung with it.
