# Hands-on — The functions of the market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's own activity
Pick one good sold near you. Follow it through the market's three functions: how the market recognised it (did it sell, at what price?), what information its sales send (rising or falling demand, quality, type), and how the seller adjusted in regulation (more stock, less stock, a new design). Write one line for each function.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main sorting or chain from memory and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
