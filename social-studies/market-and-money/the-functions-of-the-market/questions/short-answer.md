# The functions of the market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the recognition function recognise?
**Answer:** The social usefulness of a good and the labour spent on it — shown by whether the good sells and at what price.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the market's information function signal?
**Answer:** Changes in social demand — quantity, quality, types, supply–demand structure and prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does regulation follow from information?
**Answer:** Producers and consumers adjust on the information, so production and consumption are stimulated or restrained.
