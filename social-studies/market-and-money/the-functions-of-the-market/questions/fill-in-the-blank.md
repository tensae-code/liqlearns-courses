# The functions of the market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The market's three functions are recognition, _____ and regulation.
**Answer: information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Recognition is shown by whether the good _____ and at what price.
**Answer: sells**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** African swine fever swung pork prices through the market's _____ function.
**Answer: regulation**
