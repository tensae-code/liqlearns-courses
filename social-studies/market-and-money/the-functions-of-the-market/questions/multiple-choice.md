# The functions of the market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The market recognises a good's social usefulness through —
a) whether it sells and at what price  b) its colour  c) the maker's wishes
**Answer: a) whether it sells and at what price**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which function signals changes in social demand?
a) recognition  b) information  c) taxation
**Answer: b) information**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the regulation function, producers and consumers —
a) adjust on the market's information  b) stop trading  c) fix all prices by law
**Answer: a) adjust on the market's information**
