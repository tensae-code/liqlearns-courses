# The functions of the market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good that does not sell has not been recognised by the market.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sales reports are one way the market gives information to a firm.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Regulation means the State sets every price.
**Answer: False — regulation here is producers and consumers adjusting on the market's information, stimulating or restraining production and consumption.**
