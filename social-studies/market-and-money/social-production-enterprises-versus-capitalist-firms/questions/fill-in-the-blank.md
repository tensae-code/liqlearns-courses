# Social production enterprises versus capitalist firms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Large farm firms need much _____, provided by shareholders.
**Answer: capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A pig farm that makes _____ is the book's example of an agroindustry.
**Answer: sausages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Social production enterprises distribute _____ among their associates.
**Answer: surpluses**
