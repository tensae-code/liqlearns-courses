# Social production enterprises versus capitalist firms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The capital of large farm firms is provided by —
a) national or foreign shareholders  b) the workers' harvest shares only  c) nobody — big firms need no capital
**Answer: a) national or foreign shareholders**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An agroindustry is a firm that —
a) also processes the farm product, like a sugar mill or a pig farm making sausages  b) only sells raw crops abroad  c) farms without any processing
**Answer: a) also processes the farm product, like a sugar mill or a pig farm making sausages**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a social production enterprise, members —
a) do not enrich themselves; surpluses go to associates and social aid to the community  b) keep all the profit themselves  c) sell at the highest possible price
**Answer: a) do not enrich themselves; surpluses go to associates and social aid to the community**
