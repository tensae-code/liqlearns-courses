# Social production enterprises versus capitalist firms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shareholders in Venezuela's large farm firms can be foreign as well as national.
**Answer: True** — the book says the capital is provided by national or foreign shareholders.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sugar mill counts as an agroindustry because it processes what is grown.
**Answer: True** — agroindustries process the product, and the sugar mill is the book's example.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Social production enterprises sell more expensively than capitalist firms.
**Answer: False** — the book says they produce and sell more cheaply.
