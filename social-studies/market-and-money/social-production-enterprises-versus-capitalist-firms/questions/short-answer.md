# Social production enterprises versus capitalist firms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do large farm firms need shareholders?
**Answer:** Because farming at a large scale needs much capital, which shareholders — national or foreign — provide.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes a firm an agroindustry, with the book's two examples?
**Answer:** It processes the product as well as producing it — sugar mills processing cane, and pig farms making sausages.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three things a social production enterprise does with what it produces and earns.
**Answer:** It produces and sells more cheaply, distributes surpluses among associates, and gives social aid to the community.
