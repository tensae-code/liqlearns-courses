# Video script — Types of money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Money has worn more than one form. The first great type is metallic money — coins of gold and silver. People accepted them because the metal itself was precious: the coin carried its value in its own body.

Then came paper money. A note is worth little as paper — it works because people accept it, trusting it will be accepted onward and honoured by its issuer. Two types, two roads to the same thing: general acceptance.
