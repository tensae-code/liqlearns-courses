# Types of money — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A gold coin carries value in its own body.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Paper money is valuable because paper is precious.
**Answer: False — as paper a note is worth almost nothing; its value stands on general acceptance and trust.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** General acceptance is what makes either type money at all.
**Answer: True**
