# Types of money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Metallic money is —
a) paper notes  b) gold and silver coins  c) bank deposits only
**Answer: b) gold and silver coins**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Paper money's value stands on —
a) the cost of the paper  b) acceptance and trust  c) its weight in gold at home
**Answer: b) acceptance and trust**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Metallic money was accepted mainly because —
a) the metal itself was precious  b) the law punished refusal  c) it was light to carry
**Answer: a) the metal itself was precious**
