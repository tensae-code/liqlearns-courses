# Types of money — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coins of gold and silver are _____ money.
**Answer: metallic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money in note form is _____ money.
**Answer: paper**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What makes either type money at all is general _____.
**Answer: acceptance**
