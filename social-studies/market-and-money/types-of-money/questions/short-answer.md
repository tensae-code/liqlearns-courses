# Types of money — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two types of money in the lesson.
**Answer:** Metallic money (gold and silver coins) and paper money.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did metallic money gain acceptance?
**Answer:** Through the metal itself — gold and silver were already valued everywhere, so the coin carried value in its own body.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How did paper money gain acceptance?
**Answer:** Gradually — trusted first as a claim, then by habit and the issuer's standing, until notes passed as money in their own right.
