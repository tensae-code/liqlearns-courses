# Hands-on — Types of money

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with two objects
Find a coin and a paper note. For each, say aloud which type of money it is and why people accept it — the metal's own value, or trust in acceptance. Then say which type the lesson's second kind is.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — gold and silver coins on one side and paper notes on the other, both passing from hand to hand in a market — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
