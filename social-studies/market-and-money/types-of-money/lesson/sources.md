**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Yemen, Greece).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 11 Mabadi' Ilm al-Iqtisad): a compact principles course that starts from the history of economic thought — including Hammurabi, Confucius, Kautilya, Ibn Khaldun and Al-Maqrizi — and works every schedule and elasticity example in Yemeni rials on one good, oranges, from demand through equilibrium. This lesson is built from that book's placement: Unit 5, Lesson 1 — Money (§types of money) — teaches: Money has taken more than one form: metallic money — gold and silver coins — and paper money; and each kind had to gain people's general acceptance before it could work as money. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 12 Arches Oikonomikis Theorias): the national Lykeio textbook teaches micro first then macro in one volume, opening economics through Robinson Crusoe's one-man economy, and closes with the EU and the Greek economy itself; worked examples use euros, olive oil and peaches.
