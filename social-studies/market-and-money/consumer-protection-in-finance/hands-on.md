# Hands-on — Consumer protection in finance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Write the four rights — safety, information, complaint, compensation — on four slips of paper. Invent one bank problem for each slip and say out loud which right it breaks.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the complaint path as three boxes: the organisation itself, then the Financial System Mediator, then the court. Write under the Mediator's box "free — claims up to 10 million dram". Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
