# Video script — Consumer protection in finance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When you use a bank, you are a consumer — and in finance a consumer has rights: safety, information, complaint and compensation. The organisation must tell you the real terms, must not harm you, must hear your complaint, and must pay back what its wrong service cost you.

If the bank will not listen, Armenia gives you a free door: the Financial System Mediator, who settles disputes with financial organisations at no cost to you, for claims up to 10 million dram. And remember the other half of protection — your credit record at the ACRA credit bureau. What is written there decides your loans, so it must be true, and you can challenge it.
