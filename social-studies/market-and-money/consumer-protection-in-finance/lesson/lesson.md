# Consumer protection in finance

## Do it first

A bank charges your account a fee you never agreed to, and the branch will not listen. Before you read on, say what rights you think you have — and who, outside the bank, could make the bank answer.

## Sub lesson 1 — Consumer rights in finance — safety, information, complaint and compensation

Armenia's Grade 10 book, in 4.4 Protecting consumers' rights, teaches consumer protection **in finance** — the rights a person holds when the product is not bread or shoes but a deposit, a loan, a card or an insurance policy. The rights are four: **safety** — the service must not harm you; **information** — you must be told the real terms before you agree; **complaint** — you may formally complain when the organisation breaks those terms; and **compensation** — you may claim back what a wrong service cost you. A financial consumer is not a beggar at the bank's desk: the rights come first, and the organisations must answer to them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Financial System Mediator — free dispute resolution up to 10 million dram

Rights need a door to knock on. Armenia's book names its own: the **Financial System Mediator**, an independent office that resolves disputes between consumers and financial organisations **free of charge**. A person who has complained to the organisation itself and got nowhere can take the case to the Mediator instead of paying for a court fight — for claims **up to 10 million dram**. That ceiling and that free door are the lesson's Armenian core: protection is not only written in law, it is given an office, a limit and a price of zero.

## Sub lesson 3 — The ACRA credit bureau case — your credit record as protection

The same section works the **ACRA credit bureau case**. A credit bureau holds the record of how a person has borrowed and repaid — the credit history lenders read before they lend. The case shows why that record is part of consumer protection: what is written about you decides the loans and prices you are offered, so the record must be accurate, and a consumer who knows the bureau exists can check and challenge what it says. Protection in finance runs both ways — rights against the organisation, and a true record about yourself.
