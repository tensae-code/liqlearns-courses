# Consumer protection in finance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, the consumer's rights in finance are —
a) safety, information, complaint and compensation  b) speed, silence and obedience  c) only the right to pay
**Answer: a) safety, information, complaint and compensation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Financial System Mediator settles a consumer's dispute with a financial organisation —
a) only after a court orders it  b) free of charge, for claims up to 10 million dram  c) for a fee of 10 million dram
**Answer: b) free of charge, for claims up to 10 million dram**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ACRA credit bureau matters to consumer protection because —
a) it sells loans  b) it prints banknotes  c) it holds the credit record that decides the loans and prices a person is offered, so the record must be accurate
**Answer: c) it holds the credit record that decides the loans and prices a person is offered, so the record must be accurate**
