# Consumer protection in finance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four consumer rights in finance the book teaches.
**Answer:** Safety, information, complaint and compensation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the Financial System Mediator, and up to what claim size does it work?
**Answer:** An independent office that resolves disputes between consumers and financial organisations free of charge, for claims up to 10 million dram.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the ACRA credit bureau case part of consumer protection?
**Answer:** The bureau holds a person's credit history, which lenders read before lending; a wrong record harms the consumer, so the record must be accurate and open to challenge.
