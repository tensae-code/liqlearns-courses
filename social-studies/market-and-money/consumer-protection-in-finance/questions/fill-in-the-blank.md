# Consumer protection in finance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The consumer right to be told the real terms of a financial service before agreeing is the right to _____.
**Answer: information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Armenia, disputes with financial organisations can be taken free of charge to the Financial System _____.
**Answer: Mediator**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The credit bureau whose case the book works is called _____.
**Answer: ACRA**
