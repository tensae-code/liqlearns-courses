# Consumer protection in finance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A financial consumer holds rights — safety, information, complaint and compensation — when using deposits, loans, cards and insurance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Financial System Mediator charges the consumer a fee for settling a dispute.
**Answer: False — the Mediator's dispute resolution is free of charge, for claims up to 10 million dram**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What a credit bureau writes about a person has no effect on the loans they are offered.
**Answer: False — the credit record decides the loans and prices offered, which is why it must be accurate and can be challenged**
