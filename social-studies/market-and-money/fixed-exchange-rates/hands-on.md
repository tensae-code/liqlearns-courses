# Hands-on — Fixed exchange rates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Repaint the board
Draw an official rate board being repainted by a government clerk — devaluation day — with a shadow board glowing behind it. Label both prices and the gap.

## Tax or subsidy
Take one undervalued rate. Trace its effect on an exporter, an importer and a factory using imported inputs, one sentence each, using the words tax and subsidy where they fit.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
