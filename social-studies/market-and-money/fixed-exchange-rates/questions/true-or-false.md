# Fixed exchange rates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under fixed rates, a government move raising foreign money's price is devaluation; lowering it, revaluation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An undervalued rate acts as an import tax plus an export subsidy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A wrong official price opens a parallel (black) market beside it.
**Answer: True**
