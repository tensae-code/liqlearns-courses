# Fixed exchange rates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Set by decision — devaluation and revaluation"?
a) Under fixed rates, a government move raising foreign money's price is devaluation; lowering it, revaluation  b) An undervalued rate acts as an import tax plus an export subsidy  c) A wrong official price opens a parallel (black) market beside it
**Answer: a) Under fixed rates, a government move raising foreign money's price is devaluation; lowering it, revaluation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Undervalued and overvalued"?
a) An undervalued rate acts as an import tax plus an export subsidy  b) A wrong official price opens a parallel (black) market beside it  c) Under fixed rates, a government move raising foreign money's price is devaluation; lowering it, revaluation
**Answer: a) An undervalued rate acts as an import tax plus an export subsidy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The parallel market"?
a) A wrong official price opens a parallel (black) market beside it  b) Under fixed rates, a government move raising foreign money's price is devaluation; lowering it, revaluation  c) An undervalued rate acts as an import tax plus an export subsidy
**Answer: a) A wrong official price opens a parallel (black) market beside it**
