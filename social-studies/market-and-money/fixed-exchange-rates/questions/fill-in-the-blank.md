# Fixed exchange rates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A government decision lowering the price of foreign money is ____.
**Answer: revaluation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An undervalued rate works as an import tax plus an export ____.
**Answer: subsidy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market that grows beside a wrong official price is the ____ market.
**Answer: parallel (black)**
