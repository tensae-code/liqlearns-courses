# Fixed exchange rates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Set by decision — devaluation and revaluation" in your own words.
**Answer:** Under fixed rates, a government move raising foreign money's price is devaluation; lowering it, revaluation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Undervalued and overvalued" in your own words.
**Answer:** An undervalued rate acts as an import tax plus an export subsidy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The parallel market" in your own words.
**Answer:** A wrong official price opens a parallel (black) market beside it.
