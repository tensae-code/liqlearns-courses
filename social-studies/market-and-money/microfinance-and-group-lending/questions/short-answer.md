# Microfinance and group lending — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Yunus and lending without collateral" in your own words.
**Answer:** Microfinance began with Muhammad Yunus and the Grameen Bank, lending without collateral.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Group lending — the group is the security" in your own words.
**Answer:** In group lending, the borrowing group itself is the security for the loans.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Moneylenders, MFIs and Ethiopia today" in your own words.
**Answer:** On the book's 2020/21 figures, Ethiopia's 41 MFIs held about Birr 47 billion in deposits.
