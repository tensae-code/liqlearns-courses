# Microfinance and group lending — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Yunus and lending without collateral"?
a) Yunus and Grameen — savings, credit, insurance and transfers without collateral  b) the group as security, peer pressure, targeting the economically active poor  c) moneylenders dearer but simple and fast; 41 MFIs on 2020/21 figures

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Group lending — the group is the security"?

**Answer: c) the group as security, peer pressure, targeting the economically active poor**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Moneylenders, MFIs and Ethiopia today"?
