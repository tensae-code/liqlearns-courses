# Hands-on — Microfinance and group lending

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Five learners form a borrowing circle. One draws a 'cannot pay this week' card; the circle must cover the instalment together and explain to the class why covering protects all five, not just one.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a Grameen-style circle of borrowers, five members guaranteeing each other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
