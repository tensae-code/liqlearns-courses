# Prices carry information — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price carries no information.
**Answer: False — prices collect and combine the market's information.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Someone must direct every response to a price change.
**Answer: False — prices transmit the signal and people respond without a director.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wages and interest rates carry no information.
**Answer: False — wages and interest rates are prices and carry information too.**
