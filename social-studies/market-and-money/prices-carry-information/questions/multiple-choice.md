# Prices carry information — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price combines —
a) many buyers' and sellers' information into one number  b) only the seller's costs  c) government orders
**Answer: a) many buyers' and sellers' information into one number**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A rising price signals —
a) scarcity, calling forth more supply  b) that supply should stop  c) nothing
**Answer: a) scarcity, calling forth more supply**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Prices carry information in —
a) goods, labour and capital markets  b) goods markets only  c) no markets
**Answer: a) goods, labour and capital markets**
