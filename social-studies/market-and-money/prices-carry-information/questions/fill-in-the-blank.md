# Prices carry information — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A price _____ the separate facts of buyers and sellers into one number.
**Answer: combines (collects)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Prices _____ information across the market.
**Answer: transmit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wages and interest rates are _____ carrying information in labour and capital markets.
**Answer: prices**
