# Prices carry information — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a price do with information?
**Answer:** It collects and combines many people's separate information into one number.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do prices transmit information?
**Answer:** A price change signals scarcity or plenty, and buyers and sellers respond to the signal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three markets named in this lesson.
**Answer:** Goods markets, labour markets and financial capital markets.
