# Prices carry information

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — prices carry information — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prices collect information

A **price collects information** — thousands of buyers' and sellers' separate facts are **combined into one number** anyone can read.

## Sub lesson 2 — Prices transmit information

Prices **transmit** that information — a rising price **signals** scarcity and calls forth more supply, with no one in charge.

## Sub lesson 3 — In goods, labour and capital markets

This works **across goods, labour and financial capital markets** — wages and interest rates are prices carrying information too.
