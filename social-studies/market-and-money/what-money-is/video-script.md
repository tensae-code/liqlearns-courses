# Video script — What money is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Money is anything generally accepted in payment for goods and services or in repayment of debts; it is immediately exchangeable for all kinds of assets. Each person accepts it confident others will too — acceptance is a social convention, established by law or custom.

Money works because everyone agrees it works: I take your note because the next shop will take it from me. Law can declare it and custom can build it, but trust is the fuel either way.

To be accepted, a commodity must be standardised (value easy to ascertain), widely acceptable, divisible (easy to make change), portable, and durable. Fail one badly and the money limps — cattle fail divisibility, ice fails durability.
