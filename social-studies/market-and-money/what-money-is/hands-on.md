# Hands-on — What money is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Run a class shop twice: first paying with awkward 'commodity money' objects, then with uniform play notes. List which of the five qualities made the second run easy.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — five test badges — standardised, accepted, divisible, portable, durable — being checked against a coin and a banknote — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
