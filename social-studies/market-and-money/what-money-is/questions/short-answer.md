# What money is — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The definition" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money is anything generally accepted in payment for goods and services or in repayment of debts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Acceptance is a convention" in your own words.
**Answer:** Acceptance of money is a social convention, established by law or custom.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The five qualities" in your own words.
**Answer:** Good money must be standardised, widely acceptable, divisible, portable and durable.
