# What money is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money is anything generally _____ in payment for goods and services or repayment of debts.
**Answer: accepted**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Acceptance of money is a social _____, established by law or custom.
**Answer: convention**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Good money must be standardised, widely acceptable, _____, portable and durable.
**Answer: divisible**
