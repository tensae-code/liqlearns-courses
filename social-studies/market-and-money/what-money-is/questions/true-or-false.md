# What money is — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money is anything generally accepted in payment for goods and services or in repayment of debts.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money is anything generally accepted in payment for goods and services or in repayment of debts.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The definition); sub lesson 2 says: Acceptance of money is a social convention, established by law or custom.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Good money must be standardised, widely acceptable, divisible, portable and durable.
**Answer: True**
