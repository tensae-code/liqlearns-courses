# What money is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The definition"?
a) anything generally accepted in payment or repayment of debts  b) a social convention established by law or custom, resting on confidence  c) standardised, widely acceptable, divisible, portable, durable
**Answer: a) anything generally accepted in payment or repayment of debts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Acceptance is a convention"?
a) anything generally accepted in payment or repayment of debts  b) standardised, widely acceptable, divisible, portable, durable  c) a social convention established by law or custom, resting on confidence
**Answer: c) a social convention established by law or custom, resting on confidence**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The five qualities"?
a) a social convention established by law or custom, resting on confidence  b) standardised, widely acceptable, divisible, portable, durable  c) anything generally accepted in payment or repayment of debts
**Answer: b) standardised, widely acceptable, divisible, portable, durable**
