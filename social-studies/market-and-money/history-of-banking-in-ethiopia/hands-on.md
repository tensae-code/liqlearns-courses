# Hands-on — History of banking in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay date cards along a string timeline in the classroom. Learners hang bank-name cards under the right years and narrate the story walking from 1905 to 1994 without notes.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a banking timeline from the Bank of Abyssinia in 1905 flowering into many private banks after 1994 — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
