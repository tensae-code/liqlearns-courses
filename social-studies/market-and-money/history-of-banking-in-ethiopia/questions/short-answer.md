# History of banking in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "1905–1943 — the first banks" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Bank of Abyssinia was founded in 1905/06 under Menelik II.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "1943–1991 — one bank, a split, then three" in your own words.
**Answer:** The 1963 split created the National Bank of Ethiopia and the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "1991 onward — the private era opens" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Monetary and Banking Proclamation 83/1994 opened banking to private owners.
