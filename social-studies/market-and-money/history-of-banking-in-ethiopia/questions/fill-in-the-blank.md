# History of banking in Ethiopia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's first bank, founded 1905/06 under Menelik II, was the _____.
**Answer: Bank of Abyssinia**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The State Bank split into the NBE and the CBE in _____ — Proclamation 206 of that year.
**Answer: 1963**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Banking was opened to private owners by Proclamation _____ — the 1994 law.
**Answer: 83/1994**
