# History of banking in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "1905–1943 — the first banks"?
a) Bank of Abyssinia 1905 to Barclays 1941–43, via the Bank of Ethiopia and the Italian banks  b) the State Bank, the 1963 NBE–CBE split, then three banks under socialism  c) Proclamation 83/1994 opening banking to private owners
**Answer: a) Bank of Abyssinia 1905 to Barclays 1941–43, via the Bank of Ethiopia and the Italian banks**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "1943–1991 — one bank, a split, then three"?
a) Bank of Abyssinia 1905 to Barclays 1941–43, via the Bank of Ethiopia and the Italian banks  b) Proclamation 83/1994 opening banking to private owners  c) the State Bank, the 1963 NBE–CBE split, then three banks under socialism
**Answer: c) the State Bank, the 1963 NBE–CBE split, then three banks under socialism**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "1991 onward — the private era opens"?
a) the State Bank, the 1963 NBE–CBE split, then three banks under socialism  b) Proclamation 83/1994 opening banking to private owners  c) Bank of Abyssinia 1905 to Barclays 1941–43, via the Bank of Ethiopia and the Italian banks
**Answer: b) Proclamation 83/1994 opening banking to private owners**
