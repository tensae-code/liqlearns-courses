# History of banking in Ethiopia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bank of Abyssinia was founded in 1905/06 under Menelik II.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1963 split created the National Bank of Ethiopia and the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Monetary and Banking Proclamation 83/1994 opened banking to private owners.
**Answer: True**
