# Wages — nominal and real, piece-rate and time-rate — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wages are the price of labour.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The real wage is the goods and services the money wage buys at a given price level.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the time-rate form, pay is counted per unit produced.
**Answer: False** — time-rate pay is counted per hour actually worked; per unit produced is the piece-rate form.
