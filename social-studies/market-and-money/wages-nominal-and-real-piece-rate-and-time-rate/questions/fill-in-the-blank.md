# Wages — nominal and real, piece-rate and time-rate — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The _____ wage is the money sum a worker receives.
**Answer: nominal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The real wage is the goods and services the money wage buys at a given _____ level.
**Answer: price**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On top of the two main forms of pay, a worker's pay can also carry bonuses and a share in _____.
**Answer: profit**
