# Wages — nominal and real, piece-rate and time-rate — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a wage, and what is the nominal wage?
**Answer:** A wage is the price of labour. The nominal wage is the money sum the worker receives.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the real wage, and what happens to it when prices rise and the money wage does not change?
**Answer:** The real wage is the goods and services the money wage buys at a given price level. When prices rise and the money wage stays the same, the real wage falls — the pay buys less.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two main forms of wage and what each is counted by.
**Answer:** Piece-rate, counted per unit produced, and time-rate, counted per hour actually worked.
