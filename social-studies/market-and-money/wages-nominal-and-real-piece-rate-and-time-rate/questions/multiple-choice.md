# Wages — nominal and real, piece-rate and time-rate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The nominal wage is —
a) the goods a wage buys  b) the money sum a worker receives  c) the number of hours worked
**Answer: b) the money sum a worker receives**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If prices rise while the money wage stays the same, the real wage —
a) rises  b) stays the same  c) falls
**Answer: c) falls**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the piece-rate form, pay is counted —
a) per unit produced  b) per hour actually worked  c) per month, whatever is made
**Answer: a) per unit produced**
