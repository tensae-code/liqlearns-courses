# Hands-on — Wages — nominal and real, piece-rate and time-rate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Write a money sum on a card and label it "nominal wage". Next to it, put real things — or cards naming them — that the sum would buy today, and label them "real wage". Now cross out some of the things and say why: prices rose, so the same money sum buys less.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's two pay forms from memory — a worker paid per unit produced, and a worker paid per hour actually worked — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the two forms there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
