# Video script — Wages — nominal and real, piece-rate and time-rate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A wage is the price of labour — the payment a worker receives for their work.

The nominal wage is the money sum. The real wage is what that money buys at a given price level. When prices rise and the money sum stays the same, the real wage falls.

Pay is counted in two main forms. Piece-rate pays per unit produced. Time-rate pays per hour actually worked. Bonuses and a share in profit can come on top.
