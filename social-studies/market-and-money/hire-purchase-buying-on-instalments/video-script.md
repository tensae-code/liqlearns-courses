# Video script — Hire purchase — buying on instalments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hire purchase is a way of buying where you do not pay the whole price at once. You pay a first payment — the deposit — and then the rest in regular payments called instalments.

Kenya's book works it with a real shop example. At Akiba Hire-Purchase Shop, a colour TV is priced at Shs 19,000. The buyer pays a deposit of Shs 3,000.

Then come the instalments: Shs 1,166.90, paid twelve times. Deposit first, instalments after — that is the shape of every hire purchase.
