# Hands-on — Hire purchase — buying on instalments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Lay out thirteen cards in a row. Label the first card "deposit — Shs 3,000" and the other twelve "instalment — Shs 1,166.90". Walk along the row, turning each card over as its payment falls due, and say the running total aloud as you go.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's payment ladder from memory — price, deposit, then the twelve instalments — and label every part with its amount. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the Akiba example there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
