# Hire purchase — buying on instalments — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's hire-purchase example is a colour TV at Akiba Hire-Purchase Shop, priced at Shs 19,000.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The deposit is the last payment in a hire-purchase agreement.
**Answer: False** — the deposit is the first payment, made at the start; the instalments follow it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The twelve instalments of Shs 1,166.90 come to Shs 14,002.80 in all.
**Answer: True**
