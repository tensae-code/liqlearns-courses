# Hire purchase — buying on instalments — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hire _____ is buying by paying a first payment and the rest over time in regular payments.
**Answer: purchase**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first payment in hire purchase, paid at the start, is the _____.
**Answer: deposit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The regular payments that follow the deposit are called _____.
**Answer: instalments**
