# Hire purchase — buying on instalments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In hire purchase, the buyer —
a) pays the whole price in cash at once  b) takes the good and pays for it over time — a first payment, then regular payments  c) borrows the good and returns it unused
**Answer: b) takes the good and pays for it over time — a first payment, then regular payments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's Akiba example, the deposit on the colour TV is —
a) Shs 19,000  b) Shs 1,166.90  c) Shs 3,000
**Answer: c) Shs 3,000**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Akiba instalments are —
a) Shs 1,166.90 × 12  b) Shs 3,000 × 12  c) one payment of Shs 19,000
**Answer: a) Shs 1,166.90 × 12**
