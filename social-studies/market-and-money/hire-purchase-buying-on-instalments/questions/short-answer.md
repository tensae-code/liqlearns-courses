# Hire purchase — buying on instalments — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is hire purchase?
**Answer:** Hire purchase is buying by taking the good and paying for it over time — a first payment at the start, then regular payments — instead of paying the whole price at once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the deposit, and how big is it in the book's example?
**Answer:** The deposit is the first payment, paid at the start of the agreement. In the Akiba example it is Shs 3,000 on a colour TV priced at Shs 19,000.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the instalments in the book's example, and what do they total?
**Answer:** Twelve equal instalments of Shs 1,166.90 each. They total Shs 14,002.80.
