## Sub lesson 1 — Commercial banks

Commercial banks take deposits and make loans for everyday business, with the central bank at the head of the system.

## Sub lesson 2 — Merchant and development banks

Merchant banks serve companies' large finance, and development banks lend long-term for industry and agriculture.

## Sub lesson 3 — Savings banks

Savings banks gather small savings from ordinary people and encourage the habit of saving.
