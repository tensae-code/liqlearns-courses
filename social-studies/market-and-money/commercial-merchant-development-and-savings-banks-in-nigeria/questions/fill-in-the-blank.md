# Commercial, merchant, development and savings banks in Nigeria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Commercial banks take deposits and make _____ for everyday business.
**Answer: loans**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Development banks lend long-term for industry and _____.
**Answer: agriculture**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Savings banks gather small _____ from ordinary people.
**Answer: savings**
