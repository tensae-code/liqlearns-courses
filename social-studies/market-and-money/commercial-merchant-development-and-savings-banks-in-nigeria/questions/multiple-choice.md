# Commercial, merchant, development and savings banks in Nigeria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About commercial banks — the lesson teaches that —
a) commercial banks take deposits and make loans for everyday business, with the central bank at the head of the system.  b) merchant banks serve companies' large finance, and development banks lend long-term for industry and agriculture.  c) savings banks gather small savings from ordinary people and encourage the habit of saving.
**Answer: a) commercial banks take deposits and make loans for everyday business, with the central bank at the head of the system.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About merchant and development banks — the lesson teaches that —

**Answer: b) merchant banks serve companies' large finance, and development banks lend long-term for industry and agriculture.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About savings banks — the lesson teaches that —
a) commercial banks take deposits and make loans for everyday business, with the central bank at the head of the system.  b) merchant banks serve companies' large finance, and development banks lend long-term for industry and agriculture.  c) savings banks gather small savings from ordinary people and encourage the habit of saving.
