# Commercial, merchant, development and savings banks in Nigeria — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do commercial banks do?
**Answer:** Commercial banks take deposits and make loans for everyday business, with the central bank at the head of the system.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do merchant and development banks do?
**Answer:** Merchant banks serve companies' large finance, and development banks lend long-term for industry and agriculture.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do savings banks do?
**Answer:** Savings banks gather small savings from ordinary people and encourage the habit of saving.
