# Commercial, merchant, development and savings banks in Nigeria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Commercial banks print the country's money.
**Answer: False** — Commercial banks take deposits and make loans for everyday business, with the central bank at the head of the system.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Development banks lend only for one week at a time.
**Answer: False** — Merchant banks serve companies' large finance, and development banks lend long-term for industry and agriculture.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Savings banks discourage people from saving.
**Answer: False** — Savings banks gather small savings from ordinary people and encourage the habit of saving.
