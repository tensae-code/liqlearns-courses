# Video script — Commercial, merchant, development and savings banks in Nigeria

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Commercial, merchant, development and savings banks in Nigeria — as Nigeria's book teaches it, says the narrator: commercial banks take deposits and make loans for everyday business, with the central bank at the head of the system.; merchant banks serve companies' large finance, and development banks lend long-term for industry and agriculture.; savings banks gather small savings from ordinary people and encourage the habit of saving..
