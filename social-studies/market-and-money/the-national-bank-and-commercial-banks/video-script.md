# Video script — The National Bank and commercial banks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The National Bank of Ethiopia's role: issuing currency, bonds and treasury bills; keeping prices stable; authorising microfinance institutions; holding government deposits; lending to banks; and regulating money supply, interest, credit, reserves and foreign exchange. It referees the game the other banks play.

The Commercial Bank of Ethiopia does the everyday work: taking deposits, granting loans, safe custody of valuables, foreign-exchange services, letters of credit and traveller's cheques. Where the NBE faces the banks and the government, the CBE faces households and businesses across its branch network.

The Development Bank of Ethiopia took its present name in the 1994 reform. It gives project-based development loans to agriculture and industry, and supervises the projects it finances — lending tied to a plan it then watches, unlike the CBE's general lending.
