# Hands-on — The National Bank and commercial banks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three desks as three banks. Learners bring task cards — issue currency, hold a deposit, finance an irrigation project, keep government deposits — and the class routes each card to the right desk with a reason.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the central bank as referee and rule-maker, beside a CBE branch counter with its services listed — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
