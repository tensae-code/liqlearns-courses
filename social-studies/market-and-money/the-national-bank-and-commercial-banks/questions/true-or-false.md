# The National Bank and commercial banks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The National Bank of Ethiopia issues currency and regulates money supply, interest and credit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Commercial Bank of Ethiopia takes deposits and grants loans.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Development Bank gives project-based development loans and supervises the projects it finances.
**Answer: True**
