# The National Bank and commercial banks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The National Bank — referee and issuer" in your own words.
**Answer:** The National Bank of Ethiopia issues currency and regulates money supply, interest and credit.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The Commercial Bank — the public's bank" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Commercial Bank of Ethiopia takes deposits and grants loans.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The Development Bank — projects" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Development Bank gives project-based development loans and supervises the projects it finances.
