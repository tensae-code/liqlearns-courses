# The National Bank and commercial banks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The National Bank's first-listed role is issuing _____ — bonds and T-bills with it.
**Answer: currency**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The CBE's everyday work begins with taking _____ and granting loans.
**Answer: deposits**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The DBE lends for projects — and then _____ the projects it finances.
**Answer: supervises**
