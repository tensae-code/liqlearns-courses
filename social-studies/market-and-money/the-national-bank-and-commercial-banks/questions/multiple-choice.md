# The National Bank and commercial banks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The National Bank — referee and issuer"?
a) issuing currency, guarding stability, regulating money, credit and foreign exchange  b) deposits, loans, safe custody, foreign exchange, letters of credit  c) project-based development loans to agriculture and industry, with supervision
**Answer: a) issuing currency, guarding stability, regulating money, credit and foreign exchange**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The Commercial Bank — the public's bank"?
a) issuing currency, guarding stability, regulating money, credit and foreign exchange  b) project-based development loans to agriculture and industry, with supervision  c) deposits, loans, safe custody, foreign exchange, letters of credit
**Answer: c) deposits, loans, safe custody, foreign exchange, letters of credit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The Development Bank — projects"?
a) deposits, loans, safe custody, foreign exchange, letters of credit  b) project-based development loans to agriculture and industry, with supervision  c) issuing currency, guarding stability, regulating money, credit and foreign exchange
**Answer: b) project-based development loans to agriculture and industry, with supervision**
