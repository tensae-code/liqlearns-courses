# Actors of the economy and their roles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three choices must a producer make?
**Answer:** What to produce, how much to produce, and how to produce it — for maximum profit within limited resources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two kinds of intermediary in the lesson.
**Answer:** Any two of: distributors, securities brokers, real-estate brokers, job-introduction centres.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do consumers orient production?
**Answer:** By buying to satisfy their needs — their purchases signal what production should supply, as in the shift to green consumption.
