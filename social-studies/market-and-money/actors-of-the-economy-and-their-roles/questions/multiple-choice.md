# Actors of the economy and their roles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who invests capital and supplies goods and services?
a) producers  b) consumers  c) intermediaries only
**Answer: a) producers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intermediaries bridge production and —
a) consumption  b) taxation  c) saving
**Answer: a) consumption**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The fourth actor of the economy, besides producers, consumers and intermediaries, is —
a) the bank clerk  b) the State  c) the tourist
**Answer: b) the State**
