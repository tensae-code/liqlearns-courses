# Actors of the economy and their roles — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consumers orient and motivate production by what they buy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intermediaries produce all the goods themselves.
**Answer: False — intermediaries bridge production and consumption and supply market information.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Producers must choose what to produce, how much and how, within limited resources.
**Answer: True**
