# Lesson plan — Actors of the economy and their roles

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Actors of the economy and their roles
**Place in the curriculum:** Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The economy's actors are producers (who invest capital, produce and supply goods and services, choosing what, how much and how to produce for maximum profit within limited resources), consumers (who buy to satisfy needs and so orient and motivate production, including the shift to green consumption), intermediaries (distributors, brokers and job-introduction centres who bridge production and consumption and supply market information) and the State.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Vietnam book's own teaching of this thing (see lesson/sources.md for the book and pages).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
