# Video script — Actors of the economy and their roles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every economy has a cast of four, and Vietnam's Grade 10 book names them in one lesson: producers, consumers, intermediaries and the State.

Producers choose what, how much and how to produce. Consumers, by buying, tell them whether they chose well. Intermediaries — distributors, brokers, job centres — bridge the two and carry the market's information.

Learn the four actors and you can place every person and office in the economy on the stage where they act.
