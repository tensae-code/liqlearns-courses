# Video script — Advertising a product

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A four-lesson project to advertise a product, planning how a producer presents goods to consumers.

How goods and services are advertised on television and what advertising does to consumers' values, choices and consumption behaviour.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG: Grade 5 Trade is built on producers and consumers in the local community — consumer rights, a project to advertise a product, formal versus informal business activities, a fund-raising production project, and communication in trade traced from conch shell and kundu drum to mobile phone, internet and media advertising.
