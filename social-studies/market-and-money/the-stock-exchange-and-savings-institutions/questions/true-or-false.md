# The stock exchange and savings institutions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Companies never save; only persons do.
**Answer: False** — corporate savings are a named form in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Insurance companies hold no funds at all.
**Answer: False** — their policyholders' payments build a large fund.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The stock exchange is a bank that takes deposits.
**Answer: False** — it is a market for shares and securities, not a deposit bank.
