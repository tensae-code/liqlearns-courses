# The stock exchange and savings institutions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What forms of savings does the book name?
**Answer:** Individual savings and corporate savings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do insurance companies act as savings institutions?
**Answer:** Many policyholders' regular payments build a large fund, part of which can be invested.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the stock exchange?
**Answer:** The stock exchange is the market where shares and securities are bought and sold — where savings meet investment.
