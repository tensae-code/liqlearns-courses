# The stock exchange and savings institutions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Savings kept back by companies from their earnings are _____ savings.
**Answer: corporate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The savings institution built from policyholders' payments is the _____ company.
**Answer: insurance**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The market where savings meet investment is the _____ exchange.
**Answer: stock**
