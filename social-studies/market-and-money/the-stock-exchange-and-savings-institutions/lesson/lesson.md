# The stock exchange and savings institutions

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Savings — individual and corporate

The Class 12 book treats **savings** in their institutional forms: **individual savings** set aside by persons, and **corporate savings** kept back by companies from their earnings. Savings are the pool from which investment is fed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Insurance companies as savings institutions

**Insurance companies** gather savings too: the small regular payments of many policyholders build a large fund, part of which can be invested while the promises to pay claims are kept.

## Sub lesson 3 — The stock exchange — where savings meet investment

The **stock exchange** is the market where savings meet investment: shares and securities are bought and sold there, so a saver's money can become a company's capital and the saver can sell on when they wish.
