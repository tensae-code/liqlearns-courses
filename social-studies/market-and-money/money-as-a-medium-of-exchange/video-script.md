# Video script — Money as a medium of exchange

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Money's most basic function is to be the go-between in almost every market transaction: goods are sold for money, and money is spent for goods. Exchange no longer needs a double coincidence of wants at all.

The time spent exchanging is a transaction cost; money lowers it. Hours once burned searching for the right barter partner are freed for work — the whole economy gains those hours back every day.

By lowering that cost, money encourages specialisation and the division of labour: people dare to make one thing well, trusting money to connect them to everything else. Money is called the lubricant of the economy.
