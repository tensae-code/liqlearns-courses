# Money as a medium of exchange — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money's most basic function is to be the _____ in almost every transaction.
**Answer: go-between**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The time spent exchanging is a _____ cost, and money lowers it.
**Answer: transaction**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money is called the _____ of the economy.
**Answer: lubricant**
