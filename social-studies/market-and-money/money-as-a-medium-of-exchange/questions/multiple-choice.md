# Money as a medium of exchange — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The go-between"?
a) the go-between in almost every market transaction  b) money lowers the transaction cost of exchanging  c) money encourages specialisation — the lubricant of the economy
**Answer: a) the go-between in almost every market transaction**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Lowering transaction costs"?
a) the go-between in almost every market transaction  b) money encourages specialisation — the lubricant of the economy  c) money lowers the transaction cost of exchanging
**Answer: c) money lowers the transaction cost of exchanging**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The lubricant"?
a) money lowers the transaction cost of exchanging  b) money encourages specialisation — the lubricant of the economy  c) the go-between in almost every market transaction
**Answer: b) money encourages specialisation — the lubricant of the economy**
