# Money as a medium of exchange — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As a medium of exchange, money is the go-between in almost every market transaction.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As a medium of exchange, money is the go-between in almost every market transaction.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The go-between); sub lesson 2 says: Money lowers transaction costs — the time and effort spent exchanging.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money encourages specialisation and the division of labour, and is called the lubricant of the economy.
**Answer: True**
