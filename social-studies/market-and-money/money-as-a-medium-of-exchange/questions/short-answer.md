# Money as a medium of exchange — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The go-between" in your own words.
**Answer:** As a medium of exchange, money is the go-between in almost every market transaction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Lowering transaction costs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money lowers transaction costs — the time and effort spent exchanging.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The lubricant" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money encourages specialisation and the division of labour, and is called the lubricant of the economy.
