# Hands-on — Money as a medium of exchange

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Run the two swaps side by side with real learners: Team Barter must find a double coincidence; Team Money trades through a 'money desk'. Compare times and name the function at work.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a shoemaker and a farmer swapping directly and slowly, then the same swap done instantly through money in the middle — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
