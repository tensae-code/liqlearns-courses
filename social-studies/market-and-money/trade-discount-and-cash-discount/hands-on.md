# Hands-on — Trade discount and cash discount

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Write "Shs 10,000" on a card. Below it, write the trade-discount step — "less 10% trade discount = Shs 1,000 off → payable Shs 9,000" — then two cash-discount cards, one at 10% and one at 5%, and work out what is actually paid on each card.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's two-step price ladder from memory — the starting price, the trade discount, the payable price, then the cash discount — and label every amount. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the worked calculation there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
