# Trade discount and cash discount — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A 10% trade discount on Shs 10,000 brings the payable price to —
a) Shs 10,000  b) Shs 9,000  c) Shs 1,000
**Answer: b) Shs 9,000**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cash discount is given for —
a) buying in bulk  b) prompt cash payment  c) paying late
**Answer: b) prompt cash payment**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which discount is worked first?
a) The cash discount  b) They are worked together as one  c) The trade discount
**Answer: c) The trade discount**
