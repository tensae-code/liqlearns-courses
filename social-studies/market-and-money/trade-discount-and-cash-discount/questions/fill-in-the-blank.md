# Trade discount and cash discount — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After a 10% trade discount on Shs 10,000, the _____ price is Shs 9,000.
**Answer: payable**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cash discount reduces what is actually paid, as the reward for _____ cash payment.
**Answer: prompt**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade discount sets the price to pay; _____ discount rewards paying it promptly.
**Answer: cash**
