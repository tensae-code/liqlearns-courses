# Trade discount and cash discount — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trade discount is worked on the full price and sets the payable price.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's worked lines, the cash discount appears at 10% in one line and 5% in the other.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The cash discount is worked before the trade discount.
**Answer: False** — the trade discount is worked first, on the full price; the cash discount follows, on the payable price, for prompt cash payment.
