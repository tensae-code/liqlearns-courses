# Trade discount and cash discount — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the trade discount do in the book's example?
**Answer:** It takes 10% off the Shs 10,000 price — Shs 1,000 — bringing the payable price down to Shs 9,000.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a cash discount, and at what rates does the book work it?
**Answer:** A cash discount is a further reduction of what is owed, given for prompt cash payment. The book works it at 10% in one worked line and 5% in the other.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do the two discounts differ in their jobs?
**Answer:** The trade discount is worked first and sets the payable price. The cash discount is worked second and reduces what is actually paid, rewarding prompt cash payment.
