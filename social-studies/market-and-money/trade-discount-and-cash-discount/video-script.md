# Video script — Trade discount and cash discount

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A price can be knocked down twice, for two different reasons. Kenya's book works both discounts on the same price: Shs 10,000.

First comes the trade discount. Ten per cent off Shs 10,000 is Shs 1,000, so the payable price becomes Shs 9,000.

Then comes the cash discount — a further reduction for paying promptly in cash. The book works it at 10% in one line and at 5% in the other.

Trade discount sets the price to pay. Cash discount rewards paying it promptly. Two discounts, two jobs.
