**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Kenya).**

Kenya's own way: Business Studies is Kenya's carrier for Economics at Form 1: trade and payment are taught through Kenyan specimens — a Kenya Commercial Bank cheque, a Kenya inland money order and postal order, and shilling-priced worked examples from a Nairobi publisher (Ubora Publishers, P.O. Box 52013, Nairobi). This lesson keeps that way: the two discounts are met as the book works them, in shillings on one shared Shs 10,000 price. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
