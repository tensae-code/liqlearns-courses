# Video script — Expansive and contractive monetary policy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Expansive and contractive monetary policy — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: expansive monetary policy increases the money quantity and/or lowers the interest rate to stimulate investment and production; contractive monetary policy reduces the money quantity and/or raises the interest rate against inflationary pressures; monetary policy's two orientations sit alongside exchange-rate policy and international reserves in the Central Bank's kit.
