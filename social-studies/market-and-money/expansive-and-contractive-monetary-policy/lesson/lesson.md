# Expansive and contractive monetary policy

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — expansive and contractive monetary policy — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Expansive — more money, lower interest

Monetary policy has two orientations. Expansive monetary policy increases the quantity of money and/or reduces the interest rate, to stimulate access to the financial system, investment, production and lower unemployment.

## Sub lesson 2 — Contractive — less money, higher interest

Contractive monetary policy runs the other way: reduce the quantity of money and/or raise the interest rate, to reduce inflationary pressures of internal origin — cooling demand before prices run.

## Sub lesson 3 — Alongside — exchange-rate policy and reserves

The Grade 12 book teaches the two orientations alongside exchange-rate policy and international reserves: the Central Bank's full kit — money, the rate, the exchange rate and the reserves — for steering the economy.
