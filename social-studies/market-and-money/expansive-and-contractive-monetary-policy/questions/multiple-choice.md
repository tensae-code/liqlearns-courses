# Expansive and contractive monetary policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About expansive — more money, lower interest — the lesson teaches that —
a) expansive monetary policy increases the money quantity and/or lowers the interest rate to stimulate investment and production  b) contractive monetary policy reduces the money quantity and/or raises the interest rate against inflationary pressures  c) monetary policy's two orientations sit alongside exchange-rate policy and international reserves in the Central Bank's kit
**Answer: a) expansive monetary policy increases the money quantity and/or lowers the interest rate to stimulate investment and production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About contractive — less money, higher interest — the lesson teaches that —
a) expansive monetary policy increases the money quantity and/or lowers the interest rate to stimulate investment and production  b) contractive monetary policy reduces the money quantity and/or raises the interest rate against inflationary pressures  c) monetary policy's two orientations sit alongside exchange-rate policy and international reserves in the Central Bank's kit
**Answer: b) contractive monetary policy reduces the money quantity and/or raises the interest rate against inflationary pressures**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About alongside — exchange-rate policy and reserves — the lesson teaches that —
a) expansive monetary policy increases the money quantity and/or lowers the interest rate to stimulate investment and production  b) contractive monetary policy reduces the money quantity and/or raises the interest rate against inflationary pressures  c) monetary policy's two orientations sit alongside exchange-rate policy and international reserves in the Central Bank's kit
**Answer: c) monetary policy's two orientations sit alongside exchange-rate policy and international reserves in the Central Bank's kit**
