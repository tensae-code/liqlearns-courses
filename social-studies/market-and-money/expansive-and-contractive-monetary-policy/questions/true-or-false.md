# Expansive and contractive monetary policy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Expansive policy reduces the money quantity.
**Answer: False** — it increases it — that is what makes it expansive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contractive policy lowers interest rates to fight inflation.
**Answer: False** — it raises them — cheaper money would feed the inflation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Central Bank has no role in monetary policy.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches the policy as the Central Bank's own kit.
