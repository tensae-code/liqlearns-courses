# Expansive and contractive monetary policy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about expansive — more money, lower interest?
**Answer:** Expansive monetary policy increases the money quantity and/or lowers the interest rate to stimulate investment and production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about contractive — less money, higher interest?
**Answer:** Contractive monetary policy reduces the money quantity and/or raises the interest rate against inflationary pressures.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about alongside — exchange-rate policy and reserves?
**Answer:** Monetary policy's two orientations sit alongside exchange-rate policy and international reserves in the Central Bank's kit.
