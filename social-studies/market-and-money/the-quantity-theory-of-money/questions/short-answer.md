# The quantity theory of money — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write Fisher's equation and say what M and V stand for.
**Answer:** M · V = P · Q; M is the money mass and V its velocity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the goods side of Fisher's equation stand for?
**Answer:** The price level times the quantity of goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must prices rise when too much money chases the same goods?
**Answer:** The money side of M · V = P · Q grows while the goods side does not, so the price level must rise to balance it.
