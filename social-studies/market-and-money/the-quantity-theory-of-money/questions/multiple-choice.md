# The quantity theory of money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fisher's equation is —
a) M · V = P · Q  b) TR = p · q  c) A = p/n
**Answer: a) M · V = P · Q**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Fisher's equation, P · Q is —
a) the money mass  b) the price level times the quantity of goods  c) the velocity
**Answer: b) the price level times the quantity of goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If too much money chases the same goods —
a) prices must rise  b) prices must fall  c) nothing changes
**Answer: a) prices must rise**
