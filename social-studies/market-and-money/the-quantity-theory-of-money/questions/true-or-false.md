# The quantity theory of money — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Velocity is how fast money circulates.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The equation is called the law of monetary circulation.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** More money against the same goods leaves prices unchanged.
**Answer: False** — Prices must rise — the money side grows while the goods side does not.
