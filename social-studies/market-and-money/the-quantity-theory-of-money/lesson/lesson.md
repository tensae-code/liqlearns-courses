# The quantity theory of money

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The velocity of money — how fast money circulates

Money does its work by moving: the **velocity of money** is how fast a unit of money circulates, passing from hand to hand in a period.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fisher's equation — M · V = P · Q

The law of monetary circulation is written as **Fisher's equation: M · V = P · Q** — the money mass times its velocity equals the price level times the quantity of goods.

## Sub lesson 3 — Too much money — prices must rise

From the equation: if **too much money** chases **the same goods**, prices must rise — the money side grows while the goods side does not.
