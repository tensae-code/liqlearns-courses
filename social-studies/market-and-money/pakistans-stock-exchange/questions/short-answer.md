# Pakistan's stock exchange — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the capital market?
**Answer:** The market where long-term money is raised — savers' funds becoming companies' capital through shares.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where are company shares traded in Pakistan, in the book's account?
**Answer:** On the Karachi Stock Exchange.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the KSE-100?
**Answer:** An index summarising the prices of the leading shares on the Karachi Stock Exchange.
