# Pakistan's stock exchange — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The market where long-term money is raised through shares is the _____ market.
**Answer: capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Shares of companies are traded on the Karachi Stock _____.
**Answer: Exchange**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The index summarising Pakistan's leading shares is the _____-100.
**Answer: KSE**
