# Pakistan's stock exchange — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The capital market is where vegetables are sold daily.
**Answer: False** — it is the market for long-term capital, through shares.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Shares cannot be sold once bought.
**Answer: False** — they are bought and sold on the exchange.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The KSE-100 is the price of one company's share.
**Answer: False** — it is an index over the leading shares, not one price.
