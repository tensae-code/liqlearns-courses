## Sub lesson 1 — Pakistan's capital market

The Class 12 book teaches Pakistan's **capital market** — the market where long-term money is raised: savers' funds become companies' capital through shares, instead of through bank loans alone.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Karachi Stock Exchange and shares

Its home is the **Karachi Stock Exchange**, where **shares of companies** are bought and sold. A share is a part-ownership of a company, and its price moves with what buyers and sellers judge the company to be worth.

## Sub lesson 3 — The KSE-100 index family

The exchange's health is read through the **KSE-100 index family**: an index number that summarises the prices of the leading shares, rising and falling with the market as a whole.
