# Video script — What counts as the money supply

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The supply of money is the total of all forms of money held by the community at a moment: coins, currency notes issued by the currency authority, and chequable bank deposits. 'At a moment' matters — supply is a snapshot, not a flow.

In the book's Ethiopian accounting, currency with the public is built from notes in circulation plus birr notes and coins and small coins, minus cash held by banks — the bulk of it notes issued by the National Bank of Ethiopia — plus deposit money in current accounts.

Currency dominates in developing countries because most transactions are cash rather than cheque. Overall, supply depends on the credit-control policies of the banking system — the supply of money is steered, not accidental.
