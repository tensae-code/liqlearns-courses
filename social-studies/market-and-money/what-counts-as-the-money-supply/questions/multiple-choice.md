# What counts as the money supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The total, at a moment"?
a) coins, currency notes and chequable bank deposits held by the community at a moment  b) notes in circulation minus cash held by banks, plus deposit money in current accounts  c) currency dominates where most transactions are cash; supply depends on banking credit-control policies
**Answer: a) coins, currency notes and chequable bank deposits held by the community at a moment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Ethiopia's accounting"?
a) coins, currency notes and chequable bank deposits held by the community at a moment  b) currency dominates where most transactions are cash; supply depends on banking credit-control policies  c) notes in circulation minus cash held by banks, plus deposit money in current accounts
**Answer: c) notes in circulation minus cash held by banks, plus deposit money in current accounts**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Why currency dominates — and who steers supply"?
a) notes in circulation minus cash held by banks, plus deposit money in current accounts  b) currency dominates where most transactions are cash; supply depends on banking credit-control policies  c) coins, currency notes and chequable bank deposits held by the community at a moment
**Answer: b) currency dominates where most transactions are cash; supply depends on banking credit-control policies**
