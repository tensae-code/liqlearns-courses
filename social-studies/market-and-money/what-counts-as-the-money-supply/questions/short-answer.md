# What counts as the money supply — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The total, at a moment" in your own words.
**Answer:** The money supply is the total of all forms of money held by the community at a moment: coins, currency notes and chequable bank deposits.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Ethiopia's accounting" in your own words.
**Answer:** Currency with the public is notes in circulation minus cash held by banks, the bulk being notes issued by the National Bank of Ethiopia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why currency dominates — and who steers supply" in your own words.
**Answer:** Currency dominates in developing countries because most transactions are cash, and money supply depends on the banking system's credit-control policies.
