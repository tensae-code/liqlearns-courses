# What counts as the money supply — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The money supply is the total of all forms of money held by the community at a moment: coins, currency notes and chequable bank deposits.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The money supply is the total of all forms of money held by the community at a moment: coins, currency notes and chequable bank deposits.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The total, at a moment); sub lesson 2 says: Currency with the public is notes in circulation minus cash held by banks, the bulk being notes issued by the National Bank of Ethiopia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Currency dominates in developing countries because most transactions are cash, and money supply depends on the banking system's credit-control policies.
**Answer: True**
