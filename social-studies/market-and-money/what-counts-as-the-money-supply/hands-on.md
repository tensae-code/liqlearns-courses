# Hands-on — What counts as the money supply

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Basket game: drop item cards (public's coins, NBE notes, current-account balances, banks' vault cash) into or out of a 'money supply' basket; the class rules on each with the lesson's test — held by the community?

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a basket labelled money supply holding coins, NBE notes and a current-account balance, with bank vault cash shown outside the basket — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
