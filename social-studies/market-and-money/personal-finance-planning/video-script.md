# Video script — Personal finance planning

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Personal finance is one plan, not five separate habits. Earning is the inflow. Saving sets part of it aside first. Investing puts savings to work, in the right order.

Credit is a tool with a price, matched to what it buys. Risk management protects the plan — an emergency reserve, insurance, nothing staked on one outcome.

And the plan's biggest early decision is education, because study choices shape a lifetime of income.
