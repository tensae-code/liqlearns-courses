# Personal finance planning — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the order earn, save, then invest matter?
**Answer:** Investing before any saving exists, or with money you cannot afford to lose, reverses the plan: saving first builds the emergency fund and the sum investment starts from.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two protections in risk management.
**Answer:** An emergency reserve, insurance against losses that would break the plan, and not placing everything on one outcome (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a personal financial plan join together?
**Answer:** Goals in order, deliberately grown earning, automatic saving, investing matched to goal and risk, credit used at a known price, and protection against shocks — reviewed as life changes.
