# Personal finance planning — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving means what is left after spending, if anything.
**Answer: False** — saving is part of income set aside before spending, not what is left after.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An emergency reserve is part of risk management.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A personal financial plan joins earning, saving, investing, credit and risk in one plan.
**Answer: True**
