# Personal finance planning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the plan's order, saving comes —
a) after investing  b) before investing — part of income set aside before spending  c) never
**Answer: b) before investing — part of income set aside before spending**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Credit in a personal plan is —
a) free money  b) a tool with a price, matched to what it buys and what income can carry  c) always forbidden
**Answer: b) a tool with a price, matched to what it buys and what income can carry**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Education choices matter to the plan because —
a) they change lifetime income, the inflow the whole plan is built on  b) they are the only spending allowed  c) they remove all risk
**Answer: a) they change lifetime income, the inflow the whole plan is built on**
