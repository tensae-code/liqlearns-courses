# Hands-on — Personal finance planning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draft your own plan
On one page, write a first-year plan: your expected earning, the saving you set aside first, one investing step, the credit you would refuse and the credit you might accept, and your protection against one shock. Keep the page — it is the lesson made personal.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the plan as a chain from memory — earning → saving → investing, with credit and risk marked where they touch it. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
