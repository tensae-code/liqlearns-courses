# Offshore banking and insurance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bahamas is an offshore banking centre for the _____.
**Answer: Americas**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When a hurricane damages a home, the payment the insurance company makes is a hurricane _____.
**Answer: claim**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurance is _____ bought in advance, so the company pays when the covered harm happens.
**Answer: cover**
