# Offshore banking and insurance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are financial services, as the spread teaches them?
**Answer:** The work of banks and insurance companies — holding, moving and lending money and covering risks with money; in The Bahamas they form the country's second largest industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do hurricane claims matter so much in The Bahamas' insurance teaching?
**Answer:** The Bahamas is an island country in the hurricane path; when a hurricane damages homes and property, insurance claims are the money that rebuilds.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the five kinds of cover the book lists.
**Answer:** Life, health, home, vehicle and marine cover.
