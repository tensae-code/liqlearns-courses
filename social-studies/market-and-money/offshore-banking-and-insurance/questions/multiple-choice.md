# Offshore banking and insurance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Financial services in The Bahamas are —
a) the largest industry  b) the second largest industry  c) not counted as an industry
**Answer: b) the second largest industry**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An offshore bank mainly does business for —
a) customers from outside its own country  b) only the government  c) fishermen only
**Answer: a) customers from outside its own country**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is NOT one of the covers the book names?
a) marine cover  b) home cover  c) homework cover
**Answer: c) homework cover**
