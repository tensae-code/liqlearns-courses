# Hands-on — Offshore banking and insurance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Put five cards on the table and label them life, health, home, vehicle, marine. For each card, say one thing that could go wrong and what the insurance company would pay for.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the country's two biggest industries as two pillars — tourism first, financial services second. Under the second pillar write: offshore banks, insurance companies. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
