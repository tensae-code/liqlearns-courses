# Video script — Offshore banking and insurance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After tourism comes finance. The Bahamas' Grade 5 book teaches that financial services are the country's second largest industry — and that The Bahamas is an offshore banking centre for the Americas, a place where banks hold business that comes from abroad.

Next to the banks stand the insurance companies. They sell cover for life, health, home, vehicle and marine risks — and when a hurricane hits, it is the insurance companies that pay the claims that rebuild the islands. Banks and insurance together: that is the second pillar of the Bahamian economy.
