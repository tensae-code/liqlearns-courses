# Hands-on — Iqub, Iddir and SACCOs — Ethiopia's own finance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Run a paper Iqub: ten learners contribute tokens monthly, a draw names the taker, and the circle repeats. Afterwards the class lists why nobody defaulted — solidarity, contract, the draw — before any bank is mentioned.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a circle of neighbours paying into one pot, one member taking it home, beside an Iddir's stored tents and chairs — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
