# Iqub, Iddir and SACCOs — Ethiopia's own finance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Iqub — the rotating pot" in your own words.
**Answer:** Iqub members contribute by capacity and take the pooled pot in turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Why people prefer Iqub" in your own words.
**Answer:** People prefer Iqub for solidarity, forced contractual saving, flexibility and low default, among Aredo's reasons.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Iddir and SACCOs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Iddir is a funeral-focused mutual-aid association with monthly contributions and written by-laws.
