# Iqub, Iddir and SACCOs — Ethiopia's own finance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iqub is an indigenous _____ savings club — the pot goes to one member in turn.
**Answer: rotating**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first of Aredo's reasons people prefer Iqub is _____ — members standing together.
**Answer: solidarity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Iddir is the _____-focused mutual-aid association — also helping with medical costs, fire and theft.
**Answer: funeral**
