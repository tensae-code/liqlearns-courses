# The Four Jobs of Money

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of one case of this lesson's subject from your own life or town and say what you already know about it. Hold your answer — the lesson takes its subject in three parts, in order.

In Sri Lanka's book (Grade 10 G10 Business and Accounting Studies 1998), **Ch. 1 functions of money** — teaches: medium of exchange, measure of value, store of value and standard of deferred payment

In Sri Lanka's book (Grade 10 Commerce Introductory Book 1974), **Ch. XV functions of money** — teaches: medium of exchange, measure of value, store of value and standard of deferred payment

In Sri Lanka's book (Grade 12 Economics Part II 1979), **Ch. 5 the four functions** — teaches: measure of value, medium of exchange (ending the double coincidence of wants), store of value, and standard for deferred payments that makes credit possible

In Sri Lanka's book (Grade 12 Economics Source Book Part I), **Ch. 13 Money and Banks** — teaches: money's functions, taught after the barter-to-money story — medium of exchange, measure of value, store of value, standard of deferred payment

In Sri Lanka's book (Grade 13 Economics Teacher Guide 2018), **Level 7.1 Money** — teaches: money defined, the qualities of good money (general acceptability, durability, homogeneity, divisibility, portability, stable value, legal recognition, scarcity, recognisability), the four functions money performs, the barter defects money removes, and the kinds — legal-tender money, bank money, near money, paper notes and e-money (debit cards, e-banking, Bitcoin named) This lesson teaches those placements in the course's own words.
