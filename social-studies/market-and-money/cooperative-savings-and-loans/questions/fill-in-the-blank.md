# Cooperative savings and loans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's village, some 2,100 households save a compulsory _____ in their cooperative and lend to whichever member needs it at a set interest, until members are running goat, buffalo, vegetable and chicken businesses.
**Answer: Rs. 500 a month**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cooperative principles are voluntary and open membership, _____, members' economic participation, autonomy, education and information, cooperation among cooperatives, and concern for the community.
**Answer: democratic control**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cooperatives carry about 4 percent of GDP, a fifth of financial services and some 62,000 direct jobs, _____ beside the state and the private sector.
**Answer: a third pillar of the economy**
