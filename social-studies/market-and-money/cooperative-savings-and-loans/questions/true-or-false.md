# Cooperative savings and loans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's village, some 2,100 households save a compulsory Rs. 500 a month in their cooperative and lend to whichever member needs it at a set interest, until members are running goat, buffalo, vegetable and chicken businesses.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The cooperative principles are voluntary and open membership, democratic control, members' economic participation, autonomy, education and information, cooperation among cooperatives, and concern for the community.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cooperatives carry about 4 percent of GDP, a fifth of financial services and some 62,000 direct jobs, something else entirely beside the state and the private sector.
**Answer: False** — Cooperatives carry about 4 percent of GDP, a fifth of financial services and some 62,000 direct jobs, a third pillar of the economy beside the state and the private sector.
