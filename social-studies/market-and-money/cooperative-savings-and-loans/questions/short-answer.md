# Cooperative savings and loans — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about the village cooperative at work?
**Answer:** In the book's village, some 2,100 households save a compulsory Rs. 500 a month in their cooperative and lend to whichever member needs it at a set interest, until members are running goat, buffalo, vegetable and chicken businesses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the cooperative principles?
**Answer:** The cooperative principles are voluntary and open membership, democratic control, members' economic participation, autonomy, education and information, cooperation among cooperatives, and concern for the community.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the cooperative's weight in the economy?
**Answer:** Cooperatives carry about 4 percent of GDP, a fifth of financial services and some 62,000 direct jobs, a third pillar of the economy beside the state and the private sector.
