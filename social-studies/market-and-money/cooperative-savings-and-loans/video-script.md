---

A cooperative's members save together and lend to each other — in Nepal a third pillar of the economy beside the state and the private sector.

In the book's village, some 2,100 households save a compulsory Rs. 500 a month in their cooperative and lend to whichever member needs it at a set interest, until members are running goat, buffalo, vegetable and chicken businesses.

The cooperative principles are voluntary and open membership, democratic control, members' economic participation, autonomy, education and information, cooperation among cooperatives, and concern for the community.

Cooperatives carry about 4 percent of GDP, a fifth of financial services and some 62,000 direct jobs, a third pillar of the economy beside the state and the private sector.
