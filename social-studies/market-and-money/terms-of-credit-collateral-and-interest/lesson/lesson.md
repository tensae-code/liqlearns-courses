## Sub lesson 1 — The four terms of credit

Every loan has **terms of credit: the interest rate, collateral, documentation and the mode of repayment** — agreed before the money moves.

## Sub lesson 2 — Collateral — what the lender may sell on default

**Collateral** is security like **land, buildings, vehicles, livestock or bank deposits** that the **lender may sell if the borrower defaults**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Megha's bank house loan

The terms are worked through **Megha's bank house loan** — interest, documents, collateral and repayment instalments all written down before she borrows.
