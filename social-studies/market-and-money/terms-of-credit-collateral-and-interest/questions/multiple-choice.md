# Terms of credit — collateral and interest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The terms of credit are —
a) interest rate, collateral, documentation and mode of repayment  b) only the loan's size  c) only the borrower's name
**Answer: a) interest rate, collateral, documentation and mode of repayment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Collateral can be —
a) land, buildings, vehicles, livestock or bank deposits  b) only cash in hand  c) a promise alone
**Answer: a) land, buildings, vehicles, livestock or bank deposits**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The worked example is —
a) Megha's bank house loan  b) a moneylender's crop loan  c) a shop's barter deal
**Answer: a) Megha's bank house loan**
