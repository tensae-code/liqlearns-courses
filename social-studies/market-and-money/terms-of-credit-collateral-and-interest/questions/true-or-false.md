# Terms of credit — collateral and interest — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A loan has no terms if the borrower is trusted.
**Answer: False — every loan carries terms of credit.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Collateral is a gift to the lender.
**Answer: False — it is security the lender may sell only on default.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Megha's loan had no documentation.
**Answer: False — documentation is one of its terms.**
