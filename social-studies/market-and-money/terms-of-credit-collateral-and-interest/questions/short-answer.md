# Terms of credit — collateral and interest — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four terms of credit.
**Answer:** Interest rate, collateral, documentation and mode of repayment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is collateral, and when can the lender sell it?
**Answer:** Security such as land or vehicles; the lender may sell it if the borrower defaults.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whose loan works through the terms of credit?
**Answer:** Megha's bank house loan.
