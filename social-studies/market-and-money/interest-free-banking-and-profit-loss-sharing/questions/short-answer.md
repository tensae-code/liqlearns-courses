# Interest-free banking and profit–loss sharing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is interest-free banking?
**Answer:** Banking without interest: the bank earns a share in what its finance earns, not a fixed charge on loans.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are mudarabah and musharakah?
**Answer:** Partnership forms of finance where profit and loss are shared by agreement instead of interest being fixed in advance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two examples of interest-free banking does the book give?
**Answer:** The National Commercial Bank of Saudi Arabia and the central banking experience of Malaysia.
