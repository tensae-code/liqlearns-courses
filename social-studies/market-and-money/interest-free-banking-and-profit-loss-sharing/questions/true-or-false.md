# Interest-free banking and profit–loss sharing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Interest-free banking charges a higher fixed interest.
**Answer: False** — it charges no interest at all — it shares earnings instead.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mudarabah fixes the lender's return in advance like interest.
**Answer: False** — its return is shared by agreement, not fixed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book gives no real examples of Islamic banking.
**Answer: False** — it gives two — a Saudi bank and Malaysia's central bank experience.
