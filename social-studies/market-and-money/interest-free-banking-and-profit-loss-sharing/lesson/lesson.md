# Interest-free banking and profit–loss sharing

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Banking without interest

The Class 12 book first states the **meaning of interest**, then teaches banking without it: **interest-free banking**, where the bank earns not a fixed charge on loans but a share in what its finance actually earns.

## Sub lesson 2 — Profit-and-loss-sharing accounts; mudarabah and musharakah

Its working forms are **profit-and-loss-sharing accounts** and the partnership contracts **mudarabah** and **musharakah**: money and work join in a venture, and profit — or loss — is shared by agreement instead of being fixed in advance.

## Sub lesson 3 — Two working examples

The book gives two working Islamic banking examples: the **National Commercial Bank of Saudi Arabia** and the **central banking experience of Malaysia** — proof the system runs in practice, in a bank and in a central bank.
