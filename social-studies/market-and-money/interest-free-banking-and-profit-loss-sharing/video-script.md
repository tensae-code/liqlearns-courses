# Video script — Interest-free banking and profit–loss sharing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Interest-free banking and profit–loss sharing — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: interest-free banking earns a share in earnings instead of a fixed charge on loans; mudarabah and musharakah share profit and loss by agreement instead of fixing interest; the book's examples are Saudi Arabia's National Commercial Bank and Malaysia's central banking experience.
