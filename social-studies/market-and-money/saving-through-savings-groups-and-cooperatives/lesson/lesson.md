# Saving through savings groups and cooperatives

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — saving through savings groups and cooperatives — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Saving together in savings groups — ibimina

Adults also save **together**, through **savings groups** — the book names them **ibimina**. Group saving is one of the ways of saving the book teaches, beside the coin box and the bank.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saving together in cooperatives

Adults also save together through **cooperatives** — people joining their saving in one shared organisation.

## Sub lesson 3 — Group saving beside saving alone

A child saves alone in a coin box or with small livestock; adults, the book teaches, can also save **in a group** — in ibimina and cooperatives — so saving is both a personal and a shared way.
