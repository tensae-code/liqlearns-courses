# Video script — Saving through savings groups and cooperatives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saving through savings groups and cooperatives — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: Adults also save together, through savings groups — the book names them ibimina. Group saving is one of the ways of saving the book teaches, beside the coin box and the bank. Adults also save together through cooperatives — people joining their saving in one shared organisation. A child saves alone in a coin box or with small livestock; adults, the book teaches, can also save in a group — in ibimina and cooperatives — so saving is both a personal and a shared way.
