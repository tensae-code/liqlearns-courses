# Saving through savings groups and cooperatives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about saving together in savings groups — ibimina?
**Answer:** Adults also save together, through savings groups — the book names them ibimina. Group saving is one of the ways of saving the book teaches, beside the coin box and the bank.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about saving together in cooperatives?
**Answer:** Adults also save together through cooperatives — people joining their saving in one shared organisation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about group saving beside saving alone?
**Answer:** A child saves alone in a coin box or with small livestock; adults, the book teaches, can also save in a group — in ibimina and cooperatives — so saving is both a personal and a shared way.
