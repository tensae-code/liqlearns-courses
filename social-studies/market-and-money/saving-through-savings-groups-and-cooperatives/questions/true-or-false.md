# Saving through savings groups and cooperatives — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adults also save together, through savings groups — the book names them ibimina.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Adults also save together through cooperatives — people joining their saving in one shared organisation.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A child saves alone in a coin box or with small livestock; adults, the book teaches, can also save in a group — in ibimina and cooperatives — so saving is both a personal and a shared way.
**Answer: True**
