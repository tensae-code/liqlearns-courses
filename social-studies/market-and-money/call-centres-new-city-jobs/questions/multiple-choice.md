# Call centres — new city jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Call centres are —
a) a new form of city employment  b) a kind of farm work  c) a type of factory
**Answer: a) a new form of city employment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Call centres serve customers of —
a) Indian and foreign firms  b) only local shops  c) only farmers
**Answer: a) Indian and foreign firms**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Call centres locate in India because —
a) English-speaking staff work for lower wages  b) nobody speaks English  c) wages are the world's highest
**Answer: a) English-speaking staff work for lower wages**
