# Call centres — new city jobs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is new about call-centre work?
**Answer:** It is a new form of city employment serving distant customers by phone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose customers do call centres serve?
**Answer:** Customers of Indian and foreign firms.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do call centres locate in India?
**Answer:** English-speaking staff are available at lower wages.
