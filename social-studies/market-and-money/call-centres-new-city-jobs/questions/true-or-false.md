# Call centres — new city jobs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Call centres are an old village occupation.
**Answer: False — they are a new form of city employment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Call centres serve only their own city's customers.
**Answer: False — they serve Indian and foreign firms' customers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Call centres come to India for its high wages.
**Answer: False — for lower wages with English-speaking staff.**
