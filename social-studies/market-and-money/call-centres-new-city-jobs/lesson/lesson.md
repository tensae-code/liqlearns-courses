# Call centres — new city jobs

## Sub lesson 1 — A new form of city employment

**Call centres** are a **new form of city employment** — offices where workers answer customers by phone for firms far away.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serving Indian and foreign firms' customers

Call-centre staff serve the **customers of Indian and foreign firms** — the customer may be on another continent while the worker sits in an Indian city.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why India — English-speaking staff at lower wages

The industry locates in India because **English-speaking staff work for lower wages** than in the firms' home countries.
