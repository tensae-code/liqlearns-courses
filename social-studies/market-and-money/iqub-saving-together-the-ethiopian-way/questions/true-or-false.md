# Iqub — saving together the Ethiopian way — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iqub is a savings product offered by commercial banks.
**Answer: False — Iqub sits entirely outside the formal banking system — it is an indigenous community practice older than modern banking.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In an iqub, the pot is divided equally among all members at every meeting.
**Answer: False — Nothing is divided: the whole pooled sum goes to ONE member each meeting, in turn.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Iqub matters only for consumption spending.
**Answer: False — The case study names both consumption and investment — especially for small and medium businesses.**
