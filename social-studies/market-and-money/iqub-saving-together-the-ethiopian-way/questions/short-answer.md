# Iqub — saving together the Ethiopian way — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What iqub is" in your own words.
**Answer:** Iqub is an indigenous Ethiopian revolving-savings practice outside the formal banking system.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "How a round runs" in your own words.
**Answer:** Each meeting's pooled sum goes to one member, by lottery or urgency, until every member has received it once.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Why iqub matters" in your own words.
**Answer:** Iqub remains an important source of finance for consumption and investment, especially for small and medium businesses.
