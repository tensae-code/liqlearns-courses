# Video script — Exports and imports move through ports

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is exported and imported generally leaves and arrives through ports.

Cacao — La Guaira (Guipuzcoana company); coffee — Maracaibo; oil — ports dedicated only to oil trade.

They have machinery and road links to production and consumption areas, instead of the fragmented structure of the past.
