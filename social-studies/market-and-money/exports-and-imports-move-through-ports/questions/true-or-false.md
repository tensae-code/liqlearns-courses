# Exports and imports move through ports — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venezuela's exports mostly left by land across its borders.
**Answer: False** — the book's rule is that exports and imports generally move through ports.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oil was shipped from the same general ports as everything else, with no dedicated ports.
**Answer: False** — the book names ports dedicated only to oil trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The agro-export structure of the past was as integrated as today's ports.
**Answer: False** — the book calls the past structure fragmented, unlike today's linked ports.
