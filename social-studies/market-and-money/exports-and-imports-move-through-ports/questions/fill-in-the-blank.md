# Exports and imports move through ports — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is exported and imported generally sale y llega por _____ (leaves and arrives through ports).
**Answer: puertos**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 19th-century coffee was exported through _____.
**Answer: Maracaibo**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Modern ports are linked by _____ to production and consumption areas.
**Answer: roads**
