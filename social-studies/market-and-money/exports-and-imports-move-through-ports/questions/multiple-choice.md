# Exports and imports move through ports — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Exports and imports generally leave and arrive through —
a) ports  b) airports only  c) mountain passes
**Answer: a) ports**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Colonial cacao was shipped from La Guaira by —
a) the Guipuzcoana company  b) PDVSA  c) the Maracaibo coffee board
**Answer: a) the Guipuzcoana company**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Today's international ports have —
a) machinery and road links to production and consumption areas  b) no machinery, only sailing ships  c) no links inland
**Answer: a) machinery and road links to production and consumption areas**
