# Exports and imports move through ports — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the book's rule about exports, imports and ports.
**Answer:** What is exported and imported generally leaves and arrives through ports.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match the era to its port: colonial cacao, 19th-century coffee, oil.
**Answer:** Cacao — La Guaira (Guipuzcoana company); coffee — Maracaibo; oil — ports dedicated only to oil trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do today's ports differ from the agro-export ports of the past?
**Answer:** They have machinery and road links to production and consumption areas, instead of the fragmented structure of the past.
