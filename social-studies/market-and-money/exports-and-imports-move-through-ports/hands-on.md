# Hands-on — Exports and imports move through ports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work the Venezuelan case
Take the lesson's case on paper: trade leaves and arrives by sea. Write its three parts in your own words, one line each, and check them against lesson/lesson.md. Fix any line the lesson does not support.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the chain from memory: Trade leaves and arrives by sea → Three eras, three ports — La Guaira, Maracaibo, the oil ports → Today's ports — machinery and road links. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
