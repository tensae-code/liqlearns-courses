# Video script — How banks began — goldsmiths to banknotes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Banks began in the goldsmiths' workshops. Goldsmiths worked with precious metal and kept strong chests and safes — so people began leaving their valuables with them for safekeeping.

For everything left with him, the goldsmith issued a paper receipt. People discovered the receipts themselves could pass in payment — lighter and safer than fetching the valuables back. The receipts became banknotes, and the goldsmiths' safekeeping trade became banking.
