# Hands-on — How banks began — goldsmiths to banknotes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with a receipt
Write a receipt as a goldsmith would: who left what, and when. Then say aloud the three steps of the lesson — valuables left, receipt issued, receipt passing as a banknote — using your receipt as the middle step.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a goldsmith's workshop — and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
