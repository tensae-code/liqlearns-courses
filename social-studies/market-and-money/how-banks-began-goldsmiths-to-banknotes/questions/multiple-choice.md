# How banks began — goldsmiths to banknotes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People first left valuables with goldsmiths because —
a) goldsmiths paid the highest prices  b) their keeping was safe  c) the law required it
**Answer: b) their keeping was safe**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The goldsmith issued, for what he held, a —
a) coin  b) law  c) paper receipt
**Answer: c) paper receipt**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Receipts became banknotes when —
a) they were printed in colour  b) people began passing them in payment  c) gold ran out
**Answer: b) people began passing them in payment**
