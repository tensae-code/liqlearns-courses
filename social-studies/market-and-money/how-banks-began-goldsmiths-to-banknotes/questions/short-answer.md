# How banks began — goldsmiths to banknotes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did people leave valuables with goldsmiths?
**Answer:** Because goldsmiths' workshops held strong chests and safes — their keeping was safe.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the goldsmith's receipt acknowledge?
**Answer:** The valuables in his keeping, which the owner could present the receipt to reclaim.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell the three steps from goldsmith to bank.
**Answer:** People left valuables with goldsmiths; goldsmiths issued paper receipts; the receipts passed in payment and became banknotes — and the goldsmith became a banker.
