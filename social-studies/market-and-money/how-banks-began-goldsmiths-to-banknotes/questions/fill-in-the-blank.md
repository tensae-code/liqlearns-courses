# How banks began — goldsmiths to banknotes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banks grew from the workshops of _____.
**Answer: goldsmiths**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For valuables left with him, the goldsmith issued a paper _____.
**Answer: receipt**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Receipts circulating as money became _____.
**Answer: banknotes**
