# How banks began — goldsmiths to banknotes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Banks began as safekeeping — holding people's valuables.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The goldsmith's receipt could only ever be used by the person who left the valuables.
**Answer: False — receipts passed from hand to hand in payment, which is how they became banknotes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A receipt turned stored valuables into a document that could travel.
**Answer: True**
