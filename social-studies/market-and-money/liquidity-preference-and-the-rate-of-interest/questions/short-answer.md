# Liquidity preference and the rate of interest — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book do first with interest?
**Answer:** It sorts interest into its kinds — interest being the payment for the use of money.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is liquidity preference, and what are its three motives?
**Answer:** Liquidity preference is the demand for money itself, held for transaction, precautionary and speculative motives.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where does the rate of interest settle in the book's account?
**Answer:** Where the demand for money (liquidity preference) meets the supply of money, with the marginal efficiency of capital guiding investment demand.
