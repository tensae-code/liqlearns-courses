# Liquidity preference and the rate of interest — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Interest is a tax on money.
**Answer: False** — interest is the payment for the use of money, not a tax.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Keynes's motives for holding money are only the transaction motive.
**Answer: False** — the book names three motives, not one.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The rate of interest is fixed by firms alone, with money supply playing no part.
**Answer: False** — the book sets it where money demand meets money supply.
