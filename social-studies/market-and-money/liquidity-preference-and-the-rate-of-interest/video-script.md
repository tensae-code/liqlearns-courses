Liquidity preference and the rate of interest — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: interest is the payment for the use of money, and the book first sorts its kinds; liquidity preference is the demand for money itself, from transaction, precautionary and speculative motives; the rate of interest settles where the demand for money meets its supply.
