# Free land or wage work why the poor avoided jornalero labour — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When the poorest had free access to baldío (unclaimed) land
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about when the poorest had free access to baldío.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about they were less interested in accepting wage work; when the poorest had free access to baldío is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Colombia's first export crops were made by big entrepreneurs on private property, helped by transport improvements
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
