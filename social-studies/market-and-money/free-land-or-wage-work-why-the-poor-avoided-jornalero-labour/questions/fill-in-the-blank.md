# Free land or wage work why the poor avoided jornalero labour — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When the poorest had free access to baldío _____ land
**Answer: unclaimed**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** They were less _____ in accepting wage work as tenants or day labourers;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Colombia's first export crops were made by big _____ on private property, helped by transport improvements
