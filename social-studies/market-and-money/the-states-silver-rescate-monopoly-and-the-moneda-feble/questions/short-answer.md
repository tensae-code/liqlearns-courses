# The state's silver rescate monopoly and the moneda feble — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the rescate — only the state may buy the silver?
**Answer:** Under the rescate, miners had to sell all their silver to the state, the only buyer, for minting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about a camouflaged tax — 26% and 18% below the world price?
**Answer:** The state paid 26% (1829) and 18% (1850) below the international price — a camouflaged tax — alongside the debased moneda feble.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about 1873 — free export ends the rescate?
**Answer:** Free export of silver pastes in 1873 abolished the rescate monopoly and drew in foreign investment.
