# The state's silver rescate monopoly and the moneda feble — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the rescate, miners sold silver to any buyer they chose.
**Answer: False** — only the state could buy it — that was the monopoly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The state paid miners above the international price.
**Answer: False** — it paid 26% and then 18% below it — a hidden tax.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The rescate monopoly was abolished in 1825.
**Answer: False** — free export came in 1873, opening Mitre's boom phase 1873–95.
