# The state's silver rescate monopoly and the moneda feble — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the rescate, miners had to sell all their silver to the _____.
**Answer: state**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The rescate's below-price payment was a camouflaged _____.
**Answer: tax**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Free export of silver pastes, decreed in _____, abolished the rescate.
**Answer: 1873**
