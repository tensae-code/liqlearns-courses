# Video script — The state's silver rescate monopoly and the moneda feble

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The state's silver rescate monopoly and the moneda feble — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: under the rescate, miners had to sell all their silver to the state, the only buyer, for minting; the state paid 26% (1829) and 18% (1850) below the international price — a camouflaged tax — alongside the debased moneda feble; free export of silver pastes in 1873 abolished the rescate monopoly and drew in foreign investment.
