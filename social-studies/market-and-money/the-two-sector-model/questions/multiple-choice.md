# The two-sector model — households and firms only — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Who is in the model"?
a) a private closed economy of households and firms only  b) households spend all income; no saving, no government, no exports or imports  c) firms' total receipts equal total household income
**Answer: a) a private closed economy of households and firms only**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Its explicit assumptions"?
a) a private closed economy of households and firms only  b) firms' total receipts equal total household income  c) households spend all income; no saving, no government, no exports or imports
**Answer: c) households spend all income; no saving, no government, no exports or imports**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "What the model proves"?
a) households spend all income; no saving, no government, no exports or imports  b) firms' total receipts equal total household income  c) a private closed economy of households and firms only
**Answer: b) firms' total receipts equal total household income**
