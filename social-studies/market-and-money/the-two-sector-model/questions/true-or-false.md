# The two-sector model — households and firms only — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two-sector model is a private, closed economy of households and firms only.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The two-sector model is a private, closed economy of households and firms only.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Who is in the model); sub lesson 2 says: The two-sector model assumes households spend all their income, with no saving, no government and no trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the two-sector model, total money receipts of firms equal total household income.
**Answer: True**
