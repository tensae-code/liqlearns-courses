# The two-sector model — households and firms only — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Who is in the model" in your own words.
**Answer:** The two-sector model is a private, closed economy of households and firms only.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Its explicit assumptions" in your own words.
**Answer:** The two-sector model assumes households spend all their income, with no saving, no government and no trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "What the model proves" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the two-sector model, total money receipts of firms equal total household income.
