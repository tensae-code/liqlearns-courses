# Hands-on — The two-sector model — households and firms only

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Run the island with play money: households spend everything each round; after three rounds, count — the money all returns. Then remove one assumption (allow saving) and watch the circle leak.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the two-box flow diagram with its assumption list pinned beside it — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
