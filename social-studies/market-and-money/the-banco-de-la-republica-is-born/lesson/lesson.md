## Sub lesson 1 — The banco de la república began as a

The Banco de la República began as a sociedad anónima (a company whose members hold capital through shares — the term is defined in the margin) with $10 million oro of capital, 50% from the government, the rest from national and foreign commercial banks and individuals;

## Sub lesson 2 — Its creation brought a stable monetary and banking

Its creation brought a stable monetary and banking organisation, a single currency and a convertible bill, with functions to issue Colombia's legal money, set the discount rate and intervene to control interest rates, and watch the exchange market
