# BRICS — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A multipolar world system is one of BRICS' goals.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The BRICS development bank funds infrastructure in developing countries.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** BRICS wants the international organisations left exactly as they are.
**Answer: False — it calls for deep reform of the World Bank, IMF and UN.**
