# BRICS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** BRICS seeks a share in managing —
a) the world economy  b) one country's economy  c) a single city's trade
**Answer: a) the world economy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** BRICS calls for deep reform of which organisations?
a) the World Bank, the IMF and the UN  b) only local councils  c) only private banks
**Answer: a) the World Bank, the IMF and the UN**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The BRICS development bank's capital is —
a) 100 billion dollars  b) 100 million dollars  c) 10 billion dollars
**Answer: a) 100 billion dollars**
