# BRICS — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What share does BRICS seek, and beside whom?
**Answer:** A share in managing the world economy, beside the other groupings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which three international organisations does BRICS want deeply reformed?
**Answer:** The World Bank, the IMF and the UN.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the BRICS development bank for?
**Answer:** It holds 100 billion dollars of capital to fund infrastructure in developing countries.
