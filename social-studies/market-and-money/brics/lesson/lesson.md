## Sub lesson 1 — A share in managing the world economy — and a multipolar world

**BRICS** seeks **a share in managing the world economy** beside the other groupings, and works toward a **multipolar world system** — a world with several centres of power, not one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Deep reform of the international organisations

BRICS calls for **deep reform of the international organisations** — the **World Bank, the IMF and the UN**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The BRICS development bank — 100 billion dollars for infrastructure

BRICS set up a **development bank** with **100 billion dollars of capital**, to fund **infrastructure in developing countries**.
