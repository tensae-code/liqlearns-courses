# Video script — How commercial banks create money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece's Grade 12 textbook gives money creation its own section. When a commercial bank receives a deposit, it keeps a part as reserve and lends the rest. The loan is spent and lands as a new deposit in another bank — which lends again.

Round by round the chain runs: deposit, reserve kept, loan made, new deposit, smaller loan — each round smaller than the last, because each bank keeps its reserve slice.

Because commercial banks together create money, the system's total is not left to chance: the central bank — the bank of issue of the previous lessons — steers it, through the reserves banks must hold and the terms on which it lends to them.
