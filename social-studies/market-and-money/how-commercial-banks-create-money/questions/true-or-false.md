# How commercial banks create money — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A loan spent lands as a new deposit in another bank.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A smaller reserve share means a larger total multiplication of deposits.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The central bank steers money creation through required reserves and its lending terms to banks.
**Answer: True**
