# How commercial banks create money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What multiplies through lending is —
a) banknotes printed by shops
b) deposit money
c) the reserves themselves
**Answer: b) deposit money**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each round of lending is —
a) bigger than the last
b) smaller than the last, by the reserve slice kept
c) equal forever
**Answer: b) smaller than the last, by the reserve slice kept**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money creation by commercial banks is bounded by —
a) shopkeepers
b) the central bank
c) borrowers alone
**Answer: b) the central bank**
