# How commercial banks create money — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to a loan after it is spent?
**Answer:** It lands as a new deposit in another bank, which can lend from it again.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What governs the chain's total size?
**Answer:** The reserve share banks keep — smaller slices kept, larger total multiplication.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the two roles in money creation.
**Answer:** Commercial banks create deposit money by lending from deposits; the central bank bounds and steers the total.
