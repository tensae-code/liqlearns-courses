# Remittance services and their costs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Remittance (bipreshan) is _____ from one place to another, for example money sent home by a worker abroad.
**Answer: sending or bringing money**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Remittance is safe and trustworthy only through channels licensed by the government; money sent through unregistered agents is _____, untrustworthy and illegal.
**Answer: hundi**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cryptocurrency is a virtual currency secured by code with no physical existence — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Litecoin and Monero are named — and such currencies are _____ as in most countries.
**Answer: banned in Nepal**
