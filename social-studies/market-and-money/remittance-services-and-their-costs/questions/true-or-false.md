# Remittance services and their costs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Remittance (bipreshan) is sending or bringing money from one place to another, for example money sent home by a worker abroad.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Remittance is safe and trustworthy only through channels licensed by the government; money sent through unregistered agents is hundi, untrustworthy and illegal.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Cryptocurrency is a virtual currency secured by code with no physical existence — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Litecoin and Monero are named — and such currencies are something else entirely as in most countries.
**Answer: False** — Cryptocurrency is a virtual currency secured by code with no physical existence — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Litecoin and Monero are named — and such currencies are banned in Nepal as in most countries.
