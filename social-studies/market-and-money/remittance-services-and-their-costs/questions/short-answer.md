# Remittance services and their costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about what remittance is?
**Answer:** Remittance (bipreshan) is sending or bringing money from one place to another, for example money sent home by a worker abroad.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about licensed channels and hundi?
**Answer:** Remittance is safe and trustworthy only through channels licensed by the government; money sent through unregistered agents is hundi, untrustworthy and illegal.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about cryptocurrency — banned in nepal?
**Answer:** Cryptocurrency is a virtual currency secured by code with no physical existence — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Litecoin and Monero are named — and such currencies are banned in Nepal as in most countries.
