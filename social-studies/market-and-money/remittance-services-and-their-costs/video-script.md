Remittance is money sent home from another place — safe only through licensed channels; the unregistered hundi is illegal.

Remittance (bipreshan) is sending or bringing money from one place to another, for example money sent home by a worker abroad.

Remittance is safe and trustworthy only through channels licensed by the government; money sent through unregistered agents is hundi, untrustworthy and illegal.

Cryptocurrency is a virtual currency secured by code with no physical existence — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Litecoin and Monero are named — and such currencies are banned in Nepal as in most countries.
