# Business finance — sources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between a share and a bond?
**Answer:** A share is part-ownership: the buyer shares the company's profit and is not repaid a fixed sum. A bond is a loan: the buyer is paid interest and must be repaid at the end of the term.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two internal sources of finance and one limit of internal finance.
**Answer:** Any two of: retained profit, the owner's own savings put in, money from selling assets no longer needed. The limit: internal finance is limited by what the business has.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which document answers “is the business earning or losing”, and which answers “what does it stand on today”?
**Answer:** The income statement answers earning or losing (revenue against costs over a period); the balance sheet answers what it stands on today (assets against liabilities and capital at one moment).
