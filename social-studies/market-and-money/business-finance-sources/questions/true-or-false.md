# Business finance — sources — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Selling shares gives the buyers a part of the ownership of the company.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** External finance never costs the business anything.
**Answer: False** — external finance carries a price — interest, repayment, or a share of ownership given away.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The income statement tells the story of a period: revenue, costs, and the profit or loss between them.
**Answer: True**
