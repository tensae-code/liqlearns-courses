# Business finance — sources — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money a business raises from outside itself is called _____ finance.
**Answer: external**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A _____ is a part of ownership in a company, sold to an investor.
**Answer: share**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a balance sheet, assets balance against liabilities plus _____.
**Answer: capital**
