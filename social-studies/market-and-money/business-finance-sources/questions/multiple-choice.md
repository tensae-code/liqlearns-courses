# Business finance — sources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A business's retained profit is —
a) external finance, because the bank holds it  b) internal finance — money the business itself provides  c) a bond
**Answer: b) internal finance — money the business itself provides**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A bondholder is —
a) a part-owner of the company  b) a lender who is paid interest and repaid  c) a manager of the company
**Answer: b) a lender who is paid interest and repaid**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A balance sheet shows —
a) one moment: what a business owns, what it owes, and what is left for the owners  b) a whole year of parties  c) only the wages bill
**Answer: a) one moment: what a business owns, what it owes, and what is left for the owners**
