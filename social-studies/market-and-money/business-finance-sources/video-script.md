# Video script — Business finance — sources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A business needs money, and the money comes from inside or outside. Inside money is the business's own: profit it keeps, the owner's savings, assets it sells.

Outside money has two great forms. A share makes the buyer a part-owner who shares the profit. A bond makes the buyer a lender who is paid interest and repaid at the end.

And two documents tell the truth about a business: the balance sheet, a snapshot of what it owns and owes, and the income statement, the story of what it earned and spent.
