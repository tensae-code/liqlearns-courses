# Hands-on — Business finance — sources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the sources
Take six cards: retained profit, owner's savings, selling an old machine, selling shares, issuing bonds, a bank loan. Sort them into two piles — internal and external — and say for each card what the business gives in return for the money.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a simple balance sheet from memory — assets on one side, liabilities and capital on the other, balancing. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
