# Hands-on — Transshipment and the container port

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Take two toy boats or two boxes as ships and one small box as a container. Move the container from the first ship to the second and say out loud why it could not sail straight to its last stop.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a route with a break in the middle: port A, the container port, port B. Mark where the cargo changes vessel and write the word for that change. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
