# Transshipment and the container port — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Transshipment happens when a direct export–import route exists and is cheap.
**Answer: False — transshipment is used when there is no direct export–import route.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Over 10% of the world's container traffic passes through the Grand Bahama container port.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The container port pays no wages to local people.
**Answer: False — it pays $25–30 million a year in local wages.**
