# Transshipment and the container port — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Transshipment is —
a) unloading cargo to another vessel when there is no direct export–import route  b) sinking cargo at sea  c) selling cargo at the port market
**Answer: a) unloading cargo to another vessel when there is no direct export–import route**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grand Bahama container port handles —
a) under 1% of global container traffic  b) exactly half of global container traffic  c) over 10% of global container traffic
**Answer: c) over 10% of global container traffic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The port pays in local wages each year —
a) $25–30 million  b) $2,500  c) nothing — the work is unpaid
**Answer: a) $25–30 million**
