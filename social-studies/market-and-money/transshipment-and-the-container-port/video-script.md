# Video script — Transshipment and the container port

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some cargo cannot travel straight from where it is made to where it is used — there is no direct route. So it is unloaded from one vessel and loaded onto another. That is transshipment.

The Bahamas' Grade 5 book takes the idea to the Grand Bahama container port: a port handling over ten percent of all the container traffic in the world. And the book counts what that means at home — twenty-five to thirty million dollars a year paid in local wages. A harbour where the world's cargo changes ships is also a pay packet for the islands.
