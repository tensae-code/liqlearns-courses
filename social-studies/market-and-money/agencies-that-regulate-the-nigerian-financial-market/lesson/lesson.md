## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Regulating the money market

Nigeria's money market is regulated by agencies that license and supervise its institutions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Regulating the capital market

The capital market has its own regulators watching shares, bonds and those who issue and trade them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why regulation matters

Regulation keeps the financial market honest and safe, protecting savers and investors.
