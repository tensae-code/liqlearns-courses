# Agencies that regulate the Nigerian financial market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About regulating the money market — the lesson teaches that —
a) nigeria's money market is regulated by agencies that license and supervise its institutions.  b) the capital market has its own regulators watching shares, bonds and those who issue and trade them.  c) regulation keeps the financial market honest and safe, protecting savers and investors.
**Answer: a) nigeria's money market is regulated by agencies that license and supervise its institutions.**

**Answer: b) the capital market has its own regulators watching shares, bonds and those who issue and trade them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About why regulation matters — the lesson teaches that —
a) nigeria's money market is regulated by agencies that license and supervise its institutions.  b) the capital market has its own regulators watching shares, bonds and those who issue and trade them.  c) regulation keeps the financial market honest and safe, protecting savers and investors.
