# Agencies that regulate the Nigerian financial market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who regulates Nigeria's money market?
**Answer:** Nigeria's money market is regulated by agencies that license and supervise its institutions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do capital market regulators watch?
**Answer:** The capital market has its own regulators watching shares, bonds and those who issue and trade them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does financial regulation matter?
**Answer:** Regulation keeps the financial market honest and safe, protecting savers and investors.
