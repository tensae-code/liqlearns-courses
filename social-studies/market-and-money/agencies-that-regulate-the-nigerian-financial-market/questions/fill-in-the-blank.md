# Agencies that regulate the Nigerian financial market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agencies license and _____ the money market's institutions.
**Answer: supervise**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Regulators watch shares, _____ and those who issue and trade them.
**Answer: bonds**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Regulation protects savers and _____.
**Answer: investors**
