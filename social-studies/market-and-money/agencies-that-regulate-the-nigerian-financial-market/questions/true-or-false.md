# Agencies that regulate the Nigerian financial market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nobody licenses or supervises Nigeria's money market institutions.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's money market is regulated by agencies that license and supervise its institutions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The capital market has no regulators at all.
**Answer: False** — The capital market has its own regulators watching shares, bonds and those who issue and trade them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Regulation exists to make markets unsafe.
**Answer: False** — Regulation keeps the financial market honest and safe, protecting savers and investors.
