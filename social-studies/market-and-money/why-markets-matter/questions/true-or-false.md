# Why markets matter — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A market is a framework for meeting, price-forming and exchange.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Markets can be sorted by the colour of the stalls only.
**Answer: False — Markets are not all identical — they come in kinds.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Forces around a market include incomes, tastes, laws, taxes and other prices.
**Answer: True**
