# Why markets matter — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book ask why markets matter before studying single markets?
**Answer: So pupils understand the framework every single market works inside.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one way markets are sorted in this lesson.
**Answer: By what is sold (goods, labour, money), or by where (local, national, world), or by how sellers stand to each other.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the framework matter, in the lesson's closing line?
**Answer: Because the forces around a market work through the framework — it is how they reach every buyer and seller.**
