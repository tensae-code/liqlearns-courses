# Why markets matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A market, in this lesson, is —
a) only a building with a roof  b) a framework in which buyers and sellers meet, prices form and goods move  c) a list of prices fixed by law
**Answer: b) a framework in which buyers and sellers meet, prices form and goods move**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Markets can be sorted by —
a) what is sold, where they are, and how sellers stand to each other  b) nothing — all markets are identical  c) the colour of the stalls only
**Answer: a) what is sold, where they are, and how sellers stand to each other**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The forces around a market include —
a) only the weather on market day  b) nothing outside the stall at all  c) buyers' incomes and tastes, laws and taxes, other markets' prices
**Answer: c) buyers' incomes and tastes, laws and taxes, other markets' prices**
