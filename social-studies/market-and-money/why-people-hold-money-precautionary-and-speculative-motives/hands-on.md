# Hands-on — Why people hold money 2 — precautionary and speculative motives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Scenario cards: sickness, job loss, a bond price dip, a sudden bargain. Learners sort each card to the wallet it belongs to and defend the sort.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two wallets — one labelled for emergencies, one labelled waiting for the right moment — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
