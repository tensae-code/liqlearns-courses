# Video script — Why people hold money 2 — precautionary and speculative motives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The precautionary motive is cash held against unforeseen contingencies — unemployment, sickness, accidents. Its size depends on the person and their circumstances, and it also acts as a store of value: safety, held liquid.

The speculative motive is resources kept liquid to take advantage of expected movements in interest rates — bond prices. It, too, is a store of value, but held for a different, gain-seeking purpose: to strike when prices move the expected way.

Together the three motives — transaction, precautionary, speculative — make up the demand for money. Every birr held is held for one of the three: to spend, to be safe, or to strike.
