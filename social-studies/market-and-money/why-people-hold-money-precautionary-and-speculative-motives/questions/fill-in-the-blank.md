# Why people hold money 2 — precautionary and speculative motives — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The precautionary motive holds cash against _____ contingencies.
**Answer: unforeseen**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The speculative motive watches expected movements in _____ and bond prices.
**Answer: interest rates**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The demand for money is made of _____ motives: transaction, precautionary, speculative.
**Answer: three**
