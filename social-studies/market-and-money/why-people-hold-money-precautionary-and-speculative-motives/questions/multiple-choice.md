# Why people hold money 2 — precautionary and speculative motives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The precautionary motive"?
a) cash held against unemployment, sickness and accidents  b) resources kept liquid to profit from expected interest-rate and bond-price movements  c) transaction, precautionary and speculative motives together make the demand for money
**Answer: a) cash held against unemployment, sickness and accidents**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The speculative motive"?
a) cash held against unemployment, sickness and accidents  b) transaction, precautionary and speculative motives together make the demand for money  c) resources kept liquid to profit from expected interest-rate and bond-price movements
**Answer: c) resources kept liquid to profit from expected interest-rate and bond-price movements**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Three motives, one demand"?
a) resources kept liquid to profit from expected interest-rate and bond-price movements  b) transaction, precautionary and speculative motives together make the demand for money  c) cash held against unemployment, sickness and accidents
**Answer: b) transaction, precautionary and speculative motives together make the demand for money**
