# Why people hold money 2 — precautionary and speculative motives — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The precautionary motive is holding cash against unforeseen contingencies like unemployment, sickness and accidents.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The precautionary motive is holding cash against unforeseen contingencies like unemployment, sickness and accidents.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The precautionary motive); sub lesson 2 says: The speculative motive keeps resources liquid to take advantage of expected movements in interest rates and bond prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The transaction, precautionary and speculative motives together make up the demand for money.
**Answer: True**
