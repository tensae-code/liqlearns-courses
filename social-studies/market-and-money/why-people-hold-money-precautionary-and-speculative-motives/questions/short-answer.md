# Why people hold money 2 — precautionary and speculative motives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The precautionary motive" in your own words.
**Answer:** The precautionary motive is holding cash against unforeseen contingencies like unemployment, sickness and accidents.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The speculative motive" in your own words.
**Answer:** The speculative motive keeps resources liquid to take advantage of expected movements in interest rates and bond prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Three motives, one demand" in your own words.
**Answer:** The transaction, precautionary and speculative motives together make up the demand for money.
