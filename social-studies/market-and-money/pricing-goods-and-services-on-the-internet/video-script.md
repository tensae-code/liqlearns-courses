# Video script — Pricing goods and services on the internet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Economic conditions; government; price elasticity of demand; prices of substitutes); how government regulations affect internet pricing.
