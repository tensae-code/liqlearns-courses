# Pricing goods and services on the internet — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "How goods and services are priced on the"?
**Answer:** How goods and services are priced on the internet compared with normal markets; price regulation and the ICCC as PNG's principal economic regulator and consumer watchdog; the internal and external factors influencing pricing (costs; demand; competition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Economic conditions; government; price elasticity of demand; prices"?
**Answer:** Economic conditions; government; price elasticity of demand; prices of substitutes); how government regulations affect internet pricing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "How national and global influences (supply and demand"?
**Answer:** How national and global influences (supply and demand; currency exchange rates; distribution; handling and shipping costs) move internet prices.
