# Video script — When water is sold for profit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When water is sold for profit — as India's book teaches it, says the narrator: Chennai's shortage is filled by private tanker companies buying water rights from farmers while the poor get a fraction of the standard supply — against Porto Alegre's public water department, which achieved universal access at low prices, and privatisations elsewhere that brought steep price rises and protests.
