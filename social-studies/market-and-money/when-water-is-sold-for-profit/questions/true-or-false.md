# When water is sold for profit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Private tankers give the poor the full standard supply.
**Answer: False — the poor get a fraction of it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Public supply in Porto Alegre reached only a few people.
**Answer: False — it achieved universal access at low prices.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Privatisation always lowers water prices.
**Answer: False — elsewhere it brought steep rises and protests.**
