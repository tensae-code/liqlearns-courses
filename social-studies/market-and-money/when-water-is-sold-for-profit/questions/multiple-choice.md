# When water is sold for profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Chennai, tanker companies buy water rights from —
a) farmers  b) the sea  c) schools
**Answer: a) farmers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Porto Alegre's public department achieved —
a) universal access at low prices  b) supply for the rich only  c) the highest prices
**Answer: a) universal access at low prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Privatisation elsewhere brought —
a) steep price rises and protests  b) universal cheap access  c) no change
**Answer: a) steep price rises and protests**
