# When water is sold for profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is Chennai's shortage filled, and who loses?
**Answer:** Private tankers buy water rights from farmers; the poor get a fraction of the standard supply.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Porto Alegre's public water department achieve?
**Answer:** Universal access at low prices.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What followed privatisation elsewhere?
**Answer:** Steep price rises and protests.
