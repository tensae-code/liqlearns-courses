# When water is sold for profit

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — when water is sold for profit — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chennai — tankers buy water rights, the poor get a fraction

In **Chennai's shortage**, **private tanker companies buy water rights from farmers**, while **the poor get a fraction of the standard supply**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Porto Alegre — public supply, universal access, low prices

Against it, **Porto Alegre's public water department achieved universal access at low prices** — public supply reaching everyone.

## Sub lesson 3 — Privatisation elsewhere — steep rises and protests

In other places, **privatisation brought steep price rises and protests** — water for profit can price out the people who need it most.
