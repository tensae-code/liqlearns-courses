Sindicatos workers organise to defend wages and conditions — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: A sindicato (union) is the organisation of people in the same trade or company who group to defend their working conditions and wages; Colombia's first strikes (Magdalena river porters, 1910, ten-hour days for low pay) won wage rises, while later strikes were violently repressed (Tropical Oil, Barrancabermeja) the union movement (movimiento sindical) represents workers politically and in labour matters, defending their interests and improving working… Unions' functions include meeting affiliates and carrying out collective negotiations over salaries and working conditions, their main objective being members' wellbeing (same NEW slug first proposed at Book 14)
