## Sub lesson 1 — A sindicato union is the organisation of people

A sindicato (union) is the organisation of people in the same trade or company who group to defend their working conditions and wages;

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's first strikes magdalena river porters 1910 ten-hour

Colombia's first strikes (Magdalena river porters, 1910, ten-hour days for low pay) won wage rises, while later strikes were violently repressed (Tropical Oil, Barrancabermeja) the union movement (movimiento sindical) represents workers politically and in labour matters, defending their interests and improving working conditions before employers, business organisations and governments;

## Sub lesson 3 — Unions' functions include meeting affiliates and carrying out

Unions' functions include meeting affiliates and carrying out collective negotiations over salaries and working conditions, their main objective being members' wellbeing (same NEW slug first proposed at Book 14)
