# Wage systems and the minimum wage — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A wage system is the way pay is set and paid; the book works through wage systems and the _____ workers receive.
**Answer: types of wages**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The minimum wage is the _____ an employer may legally pay, set to protect workers' basic needs.
**Answer: lowest pay**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A wage council advises on setting the minimum wage, and in Indonesia each province has its own _____, worked with real provincial data in the teacher guide.
**Answer: provincial minimum wage (UMP)**
