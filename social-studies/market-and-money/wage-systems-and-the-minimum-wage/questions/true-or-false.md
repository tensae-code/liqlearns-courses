# Wage systems and the minimum wage — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A wage system is the way pay is set and paid; the book works through wage systems and the types of wages workers receive.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The minimum wage is the lowest pay an employer may legally pay, set to protect workers' basic needs.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A wage council advises on setting the minimum wage, and in Indonesia each province has its own something else entirely, worked with real provincial data in the teacher guide.
**Answer: False** — A wage council advises on setting the minimum wage, and in Indonesia each province has its own provincial minimum wage (UMP), worked with real provincial data in the teacher guide.
