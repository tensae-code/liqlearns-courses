# Wage systems and the minimum wage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about wage systems and types of wages?
**Answer:** A wage system is the way pay is set and paid; the book works through wage systems and the types of wages workers receive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about the minimum wage?
**Answer:** The minimum wage is the lowest pay an employer may legally pay, set to protect workers' basic needs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about the wage council and the ump?
**Answer:** A wage council advises on setting the minimum wage, and in Indonesia each province has its own provincial minimum wage (UMP), worked with real provincial data in the teacher guide.
