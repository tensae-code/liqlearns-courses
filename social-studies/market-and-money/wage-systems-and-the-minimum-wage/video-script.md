# Video script — Wage systems and the minimum wage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A wage system is the way pay is set and paid; the book works through wage systems and the types of wages workers receive.

The minimum wage is the lowest pay an employer may legally pay, set to protect workers' basic needs.

A wage council advises on setting the minimum wage, and in Indonesia each province has its own provincial minimum wage (UMP), worked with real provincial data in the teacher guide.
