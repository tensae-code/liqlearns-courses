# The three-sector model — adding government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Government enters the circle"?
a) government activity splits into revenue and expenditure  b) buys from firms, subsidies to firms, transfer payments to households  c) saving flows via the capital market to firms as investment
**Answer: a) government activity splits into revenue and expenditure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What government does with it"?
a) government activity splits into revenue and expenditure  b) saving flows via the capital market to firms as investment  c) buys from firms, subsidies to firms, transfer payments to households
**Answer: c) buys from firms, subsidies to firms, transfer payments to households**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Saving joins through the capital market"?
a) buys from firms, subsidies to firms, transfer payments to households  b) saving flows via the capital market to firms as investment  c) government activity splits into revenue and expenditure
**Answer: b) saving flows via the capital market to firms as investment**
