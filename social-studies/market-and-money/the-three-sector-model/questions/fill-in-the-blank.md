# The three-sector model — adding government — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Government activity splits into _____ and expenditure.
**Answer: revenue**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Government gives subsidies to firms and _____ payments to households.
**Answer: transfer**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Household saving flows through the capital market to firms as _____.
**Answer: investment**
