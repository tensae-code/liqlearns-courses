# The three-sector model — adding government — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the three-sector model, government activity splits into revenue and expenditure.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the three-sector model, government activity splits into revenue and expenditure.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Government enters the circle); sub lesson 2 says: Government buys goods and services from firms, gives subsidies to firms and transfer payments to households.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Household saving flows into the capital market and on to firms as investment.
**Answer: True**
