# The three-sector model — adding government — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Government enters the circle" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the three-sector model, government activity splits into revenue and expenditure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What government does with it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Government buys goods and services from firms, gives subsidies to firms and transfer payments to households.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Saving joins through the capital market" in your own words.
**Answer:** Household saving flows into the capital market and on to firms as investment.
