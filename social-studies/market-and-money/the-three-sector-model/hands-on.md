# Hands-on — The three-sector model — adding government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Replay the island game with a government learner taxing each round and spending it back three different ways; the class names each arrow as it happens.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the three-box diagram with tax arrows in and spending, subsidy and transfer arrows out of the government box — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
