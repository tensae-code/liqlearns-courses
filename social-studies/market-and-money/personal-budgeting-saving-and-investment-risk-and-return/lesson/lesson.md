# Personal budgeting, saving and investment — risk and return

## Sub lesson 1 — Planning a personal budget

A **personal budget (belanjawan peribadi)** is a **plan for your income**: you set out what you will spend, sorting your spending into **needs and wants** before the money is spent.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saving the surplus

What is left after the planned spending is the **surplus** — and the budget's point is to **save the surplus** instead of letting it leak away unplanned.

## Sub lesson 3 — Investment — risk against return

Saved money can be **invested**, and every investment is weighed the same way: **risk against return (risiko dan pulangan pelaburan)** — the chance of losing against the gain hoped for.
