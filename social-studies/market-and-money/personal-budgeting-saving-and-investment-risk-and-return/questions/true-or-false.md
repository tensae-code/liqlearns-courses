# Personal budgeting, saving and investment — risk and return — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A personal budget (belanjawan peribadi) is a plan for your income: you set out what you will spend, sorting your spending into needs and wants before the money is spent.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is left after the planned spending is the surplus — and the budget's point is to save the surplus instead of letting it leak away unplanned.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Saved money can be invested, and every investment is weighed the same way: risk against return (risiko dan pulangan pelaburan) — the chance of losing against the gain hoped for.
**Answer: True**
