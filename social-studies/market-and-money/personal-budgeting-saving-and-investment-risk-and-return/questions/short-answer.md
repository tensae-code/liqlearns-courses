# Personal budgeting, saving and investment — risk and return — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about planning a personal budget?
**Answer:** A personal budget (belanjawan peribadi) is a plan for your income: you set out what you will spend, sorting your spending into needs and wants before the money is spent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about saving the surplus?
**Answer:** What is left after the planned spending is the surplus — and the budget's point is to save the surplus instead of letting it leak away unplanned.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about investment — risk against return?
**Answer:** Saved money can be invested, and every investment is weighed the same way: risk against return (risiko dan pulangan pelaburan) — the chance of losing against the gain hoped for.
