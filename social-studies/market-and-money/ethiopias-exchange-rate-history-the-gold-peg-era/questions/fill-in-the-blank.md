# Ethiopia's exchange rate history — the gold peg era — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The birr was introduced in ____ at 2.48 per dollar.
**Answer: 1945**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1971 move to 2.30 birr per dollar was a ____.
**Answer: revaluation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The peg set in February 1973 was ____ birr per dollar.
**Answer: 2.07**
