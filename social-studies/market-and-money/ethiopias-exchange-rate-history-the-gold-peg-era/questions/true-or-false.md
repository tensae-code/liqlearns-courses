# Ethiopia's exchange rate history — the gold peg era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The birr was introduced in 1945 at 2.48 per dollar, defined as 0.36 grams of fine gold.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Devaluation to 2.50 in 1964, then revaluation to 2.30 in 1971.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1973 revaluation to 2.07 set a peg that held for almost two decades.
**Answer: True**
