# Ethiopia's exchange rate history — the gold peg era — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "1945 — the birr is born at 2.48"?
a) The birr was introduced in 1945 at 2.48 per dollar, defined as 0.36 grams of fine gold  b) Devaluation to 2.50 in 1964, then revaluation to 2.30 in 1971  c) The 1973 revaluation to 2.07 set a peg that held for almost two decades
**Answer: a) The birr was introduced in 1945 at 2.48 per dollar, defined as 0.36 grams of fine gold**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Small moves — 1964 and 1971"?
a) Devaluation to 2.50 in 1964, then revaluation to 2.30 in 1971  b) The 1973 revaluation to 2.07 set a peg that held for almost two decades  c) The birr was introduced in 1945 at 2.48 per dollar, defined as 0.36 grams of fine gold
**Answer: a) Devaluation to 2.50 in 1964, then revaluation to 2.30 in 1971**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "1973 — the 2.07 peg that held"?
a) The 1973 revaluation to 2.07 set a peg that held for almost two decades  b) The birr was introduced in 1945 at 2.48 per dollar, defined as 0.36 grams of fine gold  c) Devaluation to 2.50 in 1964, then revaluation to 2.30 in 1971
**Answer: a) The 1973 revaluation to 2.07 set a peg that held for almost two decades**
