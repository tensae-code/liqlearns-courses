# Ethiopia's exchange rate history — the gold peg era — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "1945 — the birr is born at 2.48" in your own words.
**Answer:** The birr was introduced in 1945 at 2.48 per dollar, defined as 0.36 grams of fine gold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Small moves — 1964 and 1971" in your own words.
**Answer:** Devaluation to 2.50 in 1964, then revaluation to 2.30 in 1971.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "1973 — the 2.07 peg that held" in your own words.
**Answer:** The 1973 revaluation to 2.07 set a peg that held for almost two decades.
