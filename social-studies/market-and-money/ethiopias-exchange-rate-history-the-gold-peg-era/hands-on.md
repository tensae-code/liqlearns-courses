# Hands-on — Ethiopia's exchange rate history — the gold peg era

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Timeline it
Draw a timeline of birr notes against the dollar: 2.48, 2.50, 2.30, 2.07, each dated and labelled devaluation or revaluation. Check the direction of every label — fewer birr per dollar means a stronger birr.

## Peg arithmetic
At the 1945 definition, how much gold stood behind 100 birr? (100 × 0.36 g = 36 g.) Then say in one sentence what 'defined as gold' promised a holder of birr.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
