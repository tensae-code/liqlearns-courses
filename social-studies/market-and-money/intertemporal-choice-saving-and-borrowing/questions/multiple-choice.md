# Intertemporal choice — saving and borrowing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving moves consumption —
a) into the future  b) out of existence  c) into the past
**Answer: a) into the future**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The interest rate is the price of —
a) consuming now rather than later  b) leisure  c) a house
**Answer: a) consuming now rather than later**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The saving data in this lesson come from —
a) the Bureau of Economic Analysis  b) a single household's diary  c) the Bureau of Labor Statistics
**Answer: a) the Bureau of Economic Analysis**
