# Intertemporal choice — saving and borrowing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Borrowing moves future spending into the present.
**Answer: True — borrowing pulls consumption into the present, saving pushes it into the future.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The interest rate plays no part in the now-or-later choice.
**Answer: False — the interest rate is the price of consuming now rather than later.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** US saving rates are among the world's highest.
**Answer: False — on BEA data, US saving rates are low.**
