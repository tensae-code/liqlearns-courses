# Intertemporal choice — saving and borrowing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the intertemporal budget constraint set against each other?
**Answer:** Consumption now and consumption later.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What decides the household's chosen point?
**Answer:** Its preferences together with the interest rate.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What fact does the book examine about US saving?
**Answer:** That US saving rates are low, and why households choose that point.
