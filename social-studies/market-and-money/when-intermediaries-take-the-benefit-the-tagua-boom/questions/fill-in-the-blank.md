# When intermediaries take the benefit the tagua boom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tagua seed jewellery is prized on the _____ market;
**Answer: international**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The _____ Wounam, on whose land the palms grow, benefited first
**Answer: Emberá**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** But as demand rose big _____ accumulated the benefits for themselves and expanded cultivation, deteriorating the natural forest, until governments moved to rationalise the exploitation
**Answer: intermediaries**
