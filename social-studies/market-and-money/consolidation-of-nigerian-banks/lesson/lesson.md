## Sub lesson 1 — What consolidation means

Consolidation joins financial institutions into fewer, larger and stronger ones through mergers and takeovers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nigeria's bank consolidation

Nigeria's consolidation raised the capital banks were required to hold and merged many banks into stronger groups.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why consolidate

Consolidation aimed at banks strong enough to finance real development and withstand shocks.
