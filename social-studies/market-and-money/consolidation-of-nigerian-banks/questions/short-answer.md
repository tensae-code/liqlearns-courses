# Consolidation of Nigerian banks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does consolidation mean?
**Answer:** Consolidation joins financial institutions into fewer, larger and stronger ones through mergers and takeovers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Nigeria's bank consolidation do?
**Answer:** Nigeria's consolidation raised the capital banks were required to hold and merged many banks into stronger groups.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why did Nigeria consolidate its banks?
**Answer:** Consolidation aimed at banks strong enough to finance real development and withstand shocks.
