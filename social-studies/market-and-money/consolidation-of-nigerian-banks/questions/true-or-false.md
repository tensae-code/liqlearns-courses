# Consolidation of Nigerian banks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consolidation splits banks into more, smaller and weaker ones.
**Answer: False** — Consolidation joins financial institutions into fewer, larger and stronger ones through mergers and takeovers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria's consolidation lowered banks' required capital to nothing.
**Answer: False** — Nigeria's consolidation raised the capital banks were required to hold and merged many banks into stronger groups.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Consolidation aimed at banks too weak to finance anything.
**Answer: False** — Consolidation aimed at banks strong enough to finance real development and withstand shocks.
