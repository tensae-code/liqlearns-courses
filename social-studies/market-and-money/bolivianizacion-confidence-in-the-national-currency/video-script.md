# Video script — Bolivianización — confidence in the national currency

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivianización — confidence in the national currency — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: bolivianización is the population's confidence in the national currency — using the boliviano more often than the US dollar; bolivianización shows in transactions, loans and saving done in bolivianos more often than in dollars; bolivianización recovers monetary sovereignty, strengthens the financial system and shields the economy against external crises.
