# Bolivianización — confidence in the national currency — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bolivianización means abandoning the boliviano for the dollar.
**Answer: False** — it is the opposite — confidence in the boliviano over the dollar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bolivianización covers only cash purchases.
**Answer: False** — the book names transactions, loans and saving alike.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bolivianización weakens the financial system.
**Answer: False** — the book lists strengthening it among the gains.
