# Bolivianización — confidence in the national currency — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about bolivianización defined — confidence in the boliviano?
**Answer:** Bolivianización is the population's confidence in the national currency — using the boliviano more often than the US dollar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about transactions, loans and saving in bolivianos?
**Answer:** Bolivianización shows in transactions, loans and saving done in bolivianos more often than in dollars.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about what it makes possible — sovereignty, strength, stability?
**Answer:** Bolivianización recovers monetary sovereignty, strengthens the financial system and shields the economy against external crises.
