## Sub lesson 1 — Bolivianización defined — confidence in the boliviano

Bolivianización is the population's confidence in the national currency: using the boliviano for buying, selling, borrowing and saving in the financial system more frequently than the US dollar. The compendium teaches it beside the electronic payment instruments (IEP).

## Sub lesson 2 — Transactions, loans and saving in bolivianos

The Grade 12 book lists the fields of that confidence: the boliviano used for transactions, for loans and for saving, more often than the dollar — the currency of daily life, of credit and of the future, all in the nation's own money.

## Sub lesson 3 — What it makes possible — sovereignty, strength, stability

The student book spells out the gains: recovering monetary sovereignty, strengthening the financial system, shielding the national economy against external crises, easing daily transactions and allowing greater price stability.
