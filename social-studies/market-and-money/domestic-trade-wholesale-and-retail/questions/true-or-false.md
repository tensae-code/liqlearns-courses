# Domestic trade — wholesale and retail — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Domestic trade means trade between two countries.
**Answer: False — Trade between countries is international trade. Domestic trade stays inside one border.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wholesalers sell single items directly to final consumers.
**Answer: False — Selling in small quantities to consumers is retail. Wholesale moves goods in bulk, often on credit.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Domestic trade has no limits at all.
**Answer: False — Its hard limit is selection: only what the country produces can be traded at home.**
