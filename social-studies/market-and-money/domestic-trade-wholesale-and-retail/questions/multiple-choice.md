# Domestic trade — wholesale and retail — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Trade inside one border"?
a) Domestic trade is exchange inside one country's borders, split into wholesale and retail.  b) Wholesalers supply goods in bulk, often on credit, and form the backbone of domestic trade.  c) Retail is the final small-quantity sale to consumers; domestic trade's hard limit is that a country can only trade what it produces.
**Answer: a) Domestic trade is exchange inside one country's borders, split into wholesale and retail.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Wholesale — the backbone"?
a) Retail is the final small-quantity sale to consumers; domestic trade's hard limit is that a country can only trade what it produces.  b) Domestic trade is exchange inside one country's borders, split into wholesale and retail.  c) Wholesalers supply goods in bulk, often on credit, and form the backbone of domestic trade.
**Answer: c) Wholesalers supply goods in bulk, often on credit, and form the backbone of domestic trade.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Retail — and domestic trade's limit"?
a) Wholesalers supply goods in bulk, often on credit, and form the backbone of domestic trade.  b) Retail is the final small-quantity sale to consumers; domestic trade's hard limit is that a country can only trade what it produces.  c) Domestic trade is exchange inside one country's borders, split into wholesale and retail.
**Answer: b) Retail is the final small-quantity sale to consumers; domestic trade's hard limit is that a country can only trade what it produces.**
