# Domestic trade — wholesale and retail — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Trade inside one border" in your own words.
**Answer:** Domestic trade is exchange inside one country's borders, split into wholesale and retail.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Wholesale — the backbone" in your own words.
**Answer:** Wholesalers supply goods in bulk, often on credit, and form the backbone of domestic trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Retail — and domestic trade's limit" in your own words.
**Answer:** Retail is the final small-quantity sale to consumers; domestic trade's hard limit is that a country can only trade what it produces.
