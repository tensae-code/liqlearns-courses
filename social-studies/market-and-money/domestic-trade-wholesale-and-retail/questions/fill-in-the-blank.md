# Domestic trade — wholesale and retail — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade inside one country's borders is ____ trade.
**Answer: domestic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wholesale trade supplies goods in ____.
**Answer: bulk**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Retailers sell in ____ quantities to consumers.
**Answer: small**
