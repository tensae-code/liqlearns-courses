# Video script — Domestic trade — wholesale and retail

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Domestic (internal) trade exchanges goods and services inside one country's borders. It splits into wholesale trade and retail trade — the two levels every traded good passes through on the way to its user.

Wholesale trade is the backbone of domestic trade: wholesalers supply goods in bulk, often on credit, gathering produce and feeding it onward to the shops that face the public.

Retail trade is the final sale to consumers in small quantities, by retailers spread where consumers live. Domestic trade's advantages are low transaction and transport costs (no tariffs or customs), money kept circulating at home, insulation from foreign political shocks, and better distribution of resources. Its hard limit is selection: a country can only trade what it can produce — which pushes every country toward international trade.
