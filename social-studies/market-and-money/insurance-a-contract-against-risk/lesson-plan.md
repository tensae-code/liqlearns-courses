# Lesson plan — Insurance — a contract against risk

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Insurance — a contract against risk
**Place in the curriculum:** Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Iran, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Iran's Grade 7 Social Studies book, in Lesson 6 "Insurance and coping with accidents", defines **insurance (بیمه)**: a **contract** in which the **insurer**, in return for a **premium (حق بیمه, defined in the book)**, **undertakes to compensate specified losses**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Iran's Grade 7 Social Studies Lesson 6, on insurance and coping with accidents.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What insurance is — a contract, a premium, a promised compensation**
2. **Why it exists — losses too big for one person to cover**
3. **Insurance in Iran — student, workers' accident and third-party vehicle cover**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
