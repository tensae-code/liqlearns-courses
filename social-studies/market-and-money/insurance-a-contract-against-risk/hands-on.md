# Hands-on — Insurance — a contract against risk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Price a pretend premium
A village of 100 households expects one fire a year costing 10,000. Work out the premium each household pays if all share the cost — insurance's arithmetic in one line.

## Name your covers
List the insurances your household or school actually has. Match each to the book's named forms — student, workers' accident, third-party vehicle — and name any loss on the book's list that no cover meets.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach sub lesson 1 aloud — the contract, the premium, the compensation — as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
