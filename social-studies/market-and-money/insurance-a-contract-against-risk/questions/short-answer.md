# Insurance — a contract against risk — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define insurance in the book's words, naming its three parts.
**Answer:** A contract (the binding agreement) in which the insurer, in return for a premium, undertakes to compensate specified losses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does insurance exist, in the book's account?
**Answer:** Because illness, disability, unemployment, retirement, accidents and fire can bring losses too big for one person to cover; insurance spreads that cost across everyone who pays the premium.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three forms of insurance the book names for Iran, and say why the vehicle one is compulsory.
**Answer:** Student insurance, workers' accident insurance, and compulsory annual third-party vehicle insurance. The vehicle cover is compulsory so that harm a driver does to others is compensated whether or not the driver could pay.
