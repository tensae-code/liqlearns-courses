# Insurance — a contract against risk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insurance is —
a) a gift from the state  b) a contract in which the insurer, for a premium, undertakes to compensate specified losses  c) a savings box at home
**Answer: b) a contract in which the insurer, for a premium, undertakes to compensate specified losses**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Insurance exists because —
a) losses are always small  b) illness, disability, unemployment, retirement, accidents and fire can bring losses too big for one person to cover  c) the state needs income
**Answer: b) illness, disability, unemployment, retirement, accidents and fire can bring losses too big for one person to cover**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Third-party vehicle insurance in Iran is —
a) optional  b) compulsory and annual  c) only for trucks
**Answer: b) compulsory and annual**
