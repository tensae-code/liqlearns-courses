# Insurance — a contract against risk — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The premium (حق بیمه) is what the insurer pays the insured every month.
**Answer: False — The premium is what is paid to the insurer; in return the insurer undertakes to compensate the specified losses.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Illness, disability, unemployment, retirement, accidents and fire can each bring a loss too big for one person to cover.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Third-party vehicle insurance in Iran is optional for careful drivers.
**Answer: False — It is compulsory and annual — every vehicle must carry it, so a victim's compensation does not wait on the driver's purse.**
