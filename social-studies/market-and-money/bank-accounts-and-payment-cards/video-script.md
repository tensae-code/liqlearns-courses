# Video script — Bank accounts and payment cards

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A bank account comes in three kinds — current, savings and fixed — and cards and e-banking are the tools that move its money.

A bank account is opened at a bank, and the account is the home from which deposits, withdrawals and payments are made.

A current account allows deposit and withdrawal freely with no interest and suits traders and offices; a savings account pays interest with withdrawals within a set limit; a fixed deposit locks money for a term from three months to five years at higher interest.

E-banking works by SMS, internet and mobile, and the payment cards are the ATM/debit card and the credit card.
