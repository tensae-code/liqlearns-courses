# Bank accounts and payment cards — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bank account is opened at a bank, and the account is the home from which _____ are made.
**Answer: deposits, withdrawals and payments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A current account allows deposit and withdrawal freely with no interest and suits traders and offices; a savings account pays interest with withdrawals within a set limit; a fixed deposit locks money for a term from three months to five years at _____.
**Answer: higher interest**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** E-banking works by SMS, internet and mobile, and the payment cards are _____.
**Answer: the ATM/debit card and the credit card**
