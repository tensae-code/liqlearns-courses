# Bank accounts and payment cards — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about opening a bank account?
**Answer:** A bank account is opened at a bank, and the account is the home from which deposits, withdrawals and payments are made.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the book teach about current, savings and fixed accounts?
**Answer:** A current account allows deposit and withdrawal freely with no interest and suits traders and offices; a savings account pays interest with withdrawals within a set limit; a fixed deposit locks money for a term from three months to five years at higher interest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the book teach about cards and electronic tools?
**Answer:** E-banking works by SMS, internet and mobile, and the payment cards are the ATM/debit card and the credit card.
