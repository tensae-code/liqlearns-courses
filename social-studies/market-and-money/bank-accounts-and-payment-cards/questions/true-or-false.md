# Bank accounts and payment cards — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A bank account is opened at a bank, and the account is the home from which deposits, withdrawals and payments are made.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A current account allows deposit and withdrawal freely with no interest and suits traders and offices; a savings account pays interest with withdrawals within a set limit; a fixed deposit locks money for a term from three months to five years at higher interest.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** E-banking works by SMS, internet and mobile, and the payment cards are something else entirely.
**Answer: False** — E-banking works by SMS, internet and mobile, and the payment cards are the ATM/debit card and the credit card.
