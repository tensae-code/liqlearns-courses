# Hands-on — Collective bargaining and wage setting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Whisper one sentence alone. Then say the same sentence together with everyone in the room, out loud. Say which attempt a listener across the yard would hear — and what that shows about a collective voice.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a table with an employer on one side. First draw one worker alone on the other side; then draw a union group. Label what changed: voice and bargaining power. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 2 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
