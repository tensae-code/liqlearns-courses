# Video script — Collective bargaining and wage setting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A single worker asking for a better wage speaks with one small voice. The Bahamas' Grade 6 book teaches what changes when workers join together: a union.

The book's reason is exact. Unions are effective for bargaining and for handling worker disputes because they give workers a collective voice — many workers speaking as one — and with that voice, more bargaining power. The wage bargain and the workplace dispute are both settled at a table; the union is what brings the workers to that table as equals.
