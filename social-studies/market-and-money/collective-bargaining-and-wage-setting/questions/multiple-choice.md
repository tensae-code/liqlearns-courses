# Collective bargaining and wage setting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A union gives workers —
a) a collective voice and more bargaining power  b) a new employer  c) no voice at all
**Answer: a) a collective voice and more bargaining power**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book calls unions effective for —
a) farming and fishing  b) bargaining and handling worker disputes  c) printing money
**Answer: b) bargaining and handling worker disputes**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One worker bargaining alone is weak because —
a) one voice can be ignored, waited out or replaced  b) workers may not speak  c) employers never bargain
**Answer: a) one voice can be ignored, waited out or replaced**
