# Collective bargaining and wage setting — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Many workers speaking as one is a _____ voice.
**Answer: collective**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A disagreement at work that the two sides cannot settle alone is a _____.
**Answer: dispute**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Workers join together in a _____.
**Answer: union**
