# Collective bargaining and wage setting — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a collective voice carry more bargaining power than a single voice?
**Answer:** A single voice can be ignored, waited out or replaced; a collective voice speaks for the whole workforce, so the employer bargains with the workforce, not with one replaceable person.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two jobs the spread gives unions.
**Answer:** Bargaining (over wages) and handling worker disputes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a dispute, as this lesson teaches it?
**Answer:** A disagreement at work — over pay, conditions or treatment — that the two sides cannot settle alone.
