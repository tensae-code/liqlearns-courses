# Collective bargaining and wage setting — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Unions give workers more bargaining power.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A union weakens workers by making them speak together.
**Answer: False — speaking together is exactly what gives workers a collective voice and more bargaining power.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Handling worker disputes is one of the union's two works in the spread.
**Answer: True**
