# Video script — Money as a unit of account

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Money provides the common measure in which values are expressed — as weight is measured in one unit and distance in another. Prices in birr let unlike things — goats, grain, labour — be compared at a glance.

Without money, a supermarket of a thousand items would need up to 999 relative prices on every item — each good priced against every other. With money, one price tag each makes pricing possible at all.

One price tag each makes orderly pricing, rational calculation and the transmission of economic information possible: costs can be added, profits computed, and prices compared across a whole country in one shared language of value.
