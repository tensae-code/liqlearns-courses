# Money as a unit of account — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The common measure" in your own words.
**Answer:** As a unit of account, money provides the common measure in which values are expressed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The thousand-item problem" in your own words.
**Answer:** Without money, a supermarket of a thousand items would need up to 999 relative prices on every item.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "What the measure makes possible" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money as a unit of account makes orderly pricing, rational calculation and the transmission of economic information possible.
