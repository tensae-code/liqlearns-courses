# Money as a unit of account — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As a unit of account, money provides the common measure in which values are expressed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** As a unit of account, money provides the common measure in which values are expressed.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (The common measure); sub lesson 2 says: Without money, a supermarket of a thousand items would need up to 999 relative prices on every item.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money as a unit of account makes orderly pricing, rational calculation and the transmission of economic information possible.
**Answer: True**
