# Hands-on — Money as a unit of account

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Label five classroom objects twice: first with barter ratios on sticky notes (watch the notes explode), then with single birr prices. Photograph both label sets and compare.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — one shelf label showing a single birr price, beside the same label exploding into hundreds of barter ratios — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
