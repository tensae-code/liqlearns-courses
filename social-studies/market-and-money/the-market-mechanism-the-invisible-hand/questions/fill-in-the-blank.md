# The market mechanism — the invisible hand — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The market mechanism directs all actors' activity like an _____ hand.
**Answer: invisible**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The economic laws governing the mechanism are competition, supply–demand, price and _____.
**Answer: profit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The mechanism _____ economic resources to meet needs better.
**Answer: reallocates**
