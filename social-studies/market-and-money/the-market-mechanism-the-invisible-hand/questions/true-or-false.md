# The market mechanism — the invisible hand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The market mechanism is a self-adjusting system.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A central office directs the market mechanism step by step.
**Answer: False — it is self-adjusting, governed by the economic laws, directing actors like an invisible hand.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The mechanism can stimulate actors' dynamism and creativity.
**Answer: True**
