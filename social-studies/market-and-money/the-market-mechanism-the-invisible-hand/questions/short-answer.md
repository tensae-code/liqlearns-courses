# The market mechanism — the invisible hand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the market mechanism?
**Answer:** The self-adjusting system of economic relations governed by the economic laws (competition, supply–demand, price, profit), directing all actors' activity like an invisible hand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two strengths of the market mechanism.
**Answer:** Any two of: stimulates dynamism and creativity; drives productive-force development and growth; reallocates resources to meet needs better; satisfies human needs ever better.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the hand called invisible?
**Answer:** Because no office directs it — the economic laws themselves guide actors' decisions through prices, competition and profit.
