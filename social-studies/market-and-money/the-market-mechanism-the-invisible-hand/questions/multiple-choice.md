# The market mechanism — the invisible hand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The market mechanism is governed by the economic laws of —
a) competition, supply–demand, price and profit  b) rainfall and seasons  c) custom only
**Answer: a) competition, supply–demand, price and profit**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The mechanism directs actors' activity like —
a) a written plan  b) an invisible hand  c) a tax office
**Answer: b) an invisible hand**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One strength of the mechanism is that it reallocates resources to —
a) meet needs better  b) freeze them in place  c) hide them
**Answer: a) meet needs better**
