## Sub lesson 1 — A self-adjusting system governed by economic laws

The **market mechanism** is the **self-adjusting system of economic relations** governed by the **economic laws — competition, supply–demand, price and profit**. No office runs it; the laws direct **all actors' activity like an invisible hand**.

## Sub lesson 2 — It stimulates dynamism, creativity and growth

Its first strengths: the mechanism **stimulates actors' dynamism and creativity**, and **drives productive-force development and growth** — because competition and profit reward whoever produces better, the whole economy's capacity grows.

## Sub lesson 3 — It reallocates resources and satisfies needs better

And it **reallocates economic resources to meet needs better**, so it **satisfies human needs ever better**: resources move toward what people actually want, guided by the same laws — the invisible hand's everyday work.
