# Video script — The market mechanism — the invisible hand

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam's Grade 10 book gives the market mechanism its classic image: an invisible hand.

The mechanism is a self-adjusting system governed by the laws of competition, supply and demand, price and profit — nobody runs it, yet it directs everyone.

Its strengths: dynamism and creativity stimulated, productive forces and growth driven forward, resources reallocated toward needs — human needs satisfied ever better.
