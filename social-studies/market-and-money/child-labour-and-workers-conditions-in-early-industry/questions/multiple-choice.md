# Child labour and workers' conditions in early industry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Icelandic book's part begins with which two things together?
a) employment and housing  b) trade and ships  c) farming and fishing only
**Answer: a) employment and housing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Child labour means —
a) children learning a trade at school  b) children working in industry  c) children playing at factories
**Answer: b) children working in industry**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book's source work compares —
a) how different factories were built  b) how different towns grew  c) how different workers were paid
**Answer: c) how different workers were paid**
