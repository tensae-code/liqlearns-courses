# Child labour and workers' conditions in early industry — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do employment and housing belong together in this lesson?
**Answer:** A worker's condition is not only the job, but also the home the job's pay must hold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does "workers' conditions" cover in this lesson?
**Answer:** The whole working life of early industry's people — employment, housing, child labour and pay — not the factory machines alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book teach workers' pay?
**Answer:** Through source work: pupils compare how different workers were paid and see the differences for themselves.
