# Child labour and workers' conditions in early industry — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book, a worker's condition is only the job — housing has nothing to do with it.
**Answer: False** — employment and housing belong together: a worker's condition is also the home the job's pay must hold.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Children were among early industry's workers.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's source work, all workers turn out to have been paid exactly the same.
**Answer: False** — different workers were not paid the same; comparing their pay is the lesson.
