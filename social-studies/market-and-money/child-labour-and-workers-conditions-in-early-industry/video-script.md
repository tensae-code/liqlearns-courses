# Video script — Child labour and workers' conditions in early industry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

At the end of the 19th century, industry was young and its workers' lives were hard. Employment and housing are where the Icelandic book starts: where workers worked, and where they lived.

Children worked too. Child labour was part of early industry, and workers' conditions — the work, the housing, the pay — were the conditions this lesson names.

The book does not only tell this: it gives sources. Pupils compare how different workers were paid, and read the differences for themselves.
