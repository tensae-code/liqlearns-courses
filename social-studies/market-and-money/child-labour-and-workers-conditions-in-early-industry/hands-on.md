# Hands-on — Child labour and workers' conditions in early industry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compare the pay
Write three worker cards — three different workers of early industry — and under each, a pay line you invent for the exercise, clearly your own numbers. Compare them the book's way: who is paid most, who least, and what in each worker's work might explain the difference. Say it out loud.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — employment, housing, child labour and workers' conditions in early industry — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the parts there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
