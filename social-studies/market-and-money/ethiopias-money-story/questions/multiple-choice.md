# Ethiopia's money story — from Axumite coins to the birr — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Axum, then salt"?
a) Axumite coins from the 3rd century AD; then amole salt bars, cloth and beads  b) the Maria Theresa thaler serving until 1945; Menelik II's first national coin in 1893  c) paper money 1914 rejected and 1932 gold-backed; the birr issued in July 1945
**Answer: a) Axumite coins from the 3rd century AD; then amole salt bars, cloth and beads**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The thaler and the national coins"?
a) Axumite coins from the 3rd century AD; then amole salt bars, cloth and beads  b) paper money 1914 rejected and 1932 gold-backed; the birr issued in July 1945  c) the Maria Theresa thaler serving until 1945; Menelik II's first national coin in 1893
**Answer: c) the Maria Theresa thaler serving until 1945; Menelik II's first national coin in 1893**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Paper, chaos, and the birr"?
a) the Maria Theresa thaler serving until 1945; Menelik II's first national coin in 1893  b) paper money 1914 rejected and 1932 gold-backed; the birr issued in July 1945  c) Axumite coins from the 3rd century AD; then amole salt bars, cloth and beads
**Answer: b) paper money 1914 rejected and 1932 gold-backed; the birr issued in July 1945**
