# Ethiopia's money story — from Axumite coins to the birr — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Axum, then salt" in your own words.
**Answer:** Axumite kings Endybis and Aphilas minted Ethiopia's own coins from the 3rd century AD, and after Axum fell, amole salt bars became the most widespread money.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The thaler and the national coins" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Maria Theresa thaler served as a medium of exchange in Ethiopia until 1945, and Menelik II minted the first national coin in 1893.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Paper, chaos, and the birr" in your own words.
**Answer:** Paper money was issued in 1914 and rejected, issued again in 1932 gold-backed, and the birr became the national currency in July 1945.
