# Ethiopia's money story — from Axumite coins to the birr — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Axumite kings Endybis and Aphilas minted Ethiopia's own coins from the 3rd century AD, and after Axum fell, amole salt bars became the most widespread money.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Axumite kings Endybis and Aphilas minted Ethiopia's own coins from the 3rd century AD, and after Axum fell, amole salt bars became the most widespread money.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Axum, then salt); sub lesson 2 says: The Maria Theresa thaler served as a medium of exchange in Ethiopia until 1945, and Menelik II minted the first national coin in 1893.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Paper money was issued in 1914 and rejected, issued again in 1932 gold-backed, and the birr became the national currency in July 1945.
**Answer: True**
