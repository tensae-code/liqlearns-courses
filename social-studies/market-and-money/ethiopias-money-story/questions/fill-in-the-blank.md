# Ethiopia's money story — from Axumite coins to the birr — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Axum fell, bars of salt called _____ became the most widespread money.
**Answer: amole**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Menelik II minted the first national coin in _____.
**Answer: 1893**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Ethiopian government issued the birr as national currency in July _____.
**Answer: 1945**
