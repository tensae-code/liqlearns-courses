# Hands-on — Ethiopia's money story — from Axumite coins to the birr

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Five learners hold money-object cards and must arrange themselves in the book's order while the class reads dates aloud; swap one card wrongly and let the class catch it.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — an Ethiopia timeline — Axumite coin, amole salt bar, Maria Theresa thaler, Menelik coin, 1945 birr note — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
