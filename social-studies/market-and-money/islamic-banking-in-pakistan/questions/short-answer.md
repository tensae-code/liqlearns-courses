# Islamic banking in Pakistan — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the book teach about Islamic banking in Pakistan?
**Answer:** Its promotion — banking without interest, on profit-and-loss sharing, encouraged inside Pakistan's system.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does Islamic banking stand in the book's account?
**Answer:** Inside Pakistan's banking system, as one of its working forms.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is e-commerce in banking?
**Answer:** Banking done electronically — accounts and payments moving online.
