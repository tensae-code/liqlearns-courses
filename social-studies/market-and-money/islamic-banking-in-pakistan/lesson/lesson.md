## Sub lesson 1 — The promotion of Islamic banking in Pakistan

Within Pakistan's banking chapter, the book teaches the **promotion of Islamic banking in Pakistan**: banking on profit-and-loss sharing, without interest, deliberately encouraged inside the country's own system.

## Sub lesson 2 — Islamic banking inside the system

The promotion is taught as part of the banking system itself — not a side experiment: Islamic banking stands in the same chapter as the commercial and specialised banks, as one of the system's working forms.

## Sub lesson 3 — E-commerce arrives in banking

The same section records a second arrival: **e-commerce in the banking system** — banking done electronically, payments and accounts moving online. The old system and the new technology are taught in one breath.
