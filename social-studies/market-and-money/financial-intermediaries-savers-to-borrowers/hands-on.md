# Hands-on — Financial intermediaries — savers to borrowers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Three learners: a saver with birr cards, a bank in the middle, a borrower with a machine card. Pass the cards saver to bank to borrower, then back as repayment, while the class names the benefit each hand receives.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — savers on one side, borrowers on the other, a bank in the middle — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
