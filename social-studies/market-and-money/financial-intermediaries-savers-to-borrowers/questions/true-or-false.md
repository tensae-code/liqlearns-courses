# Financial intermediaries — savers to borrowers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Financial intermediaries stand between surplus units (savers) and deficit units (borrowers).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Depositors gain safety plus interest, while borrowers gain access to funds.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book calls finance the blood of the economy.
**Answer: True**
