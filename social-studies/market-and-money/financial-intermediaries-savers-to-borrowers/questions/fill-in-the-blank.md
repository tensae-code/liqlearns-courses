# Financial intermediaries — savers to borrowers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Savers are _____ units — they hold more than they spend — and borrowers are deficit units.
**Answer: surplus**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Depositors get _____ plus interest for their money — two benefits of intermediation.
**Answer: safety**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Finance is the _____ of the economy — the unit's organising image.
**Answer: blood**
