# Financial intermediaries — savers to borrowers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Standing between surplus and deficit" in your own words.
**Answer:** Financial intermediaries stand between surplus units (savers) and deficit units (borrowers).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The benefits to each side" in your own words.
**Answer:** Depositors gain safety plus interest, while borrowers gain access to funds.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Finance, the blood of the economy" in your own words.
**Answer:** The book calls finance the blood of the economy.
