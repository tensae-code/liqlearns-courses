# The Islamic Development Bank — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Islamic Development Bank is an institution of the Islamic world.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Its programmes avoid poorer countries.
**Answer: False — they support development projects and infrastructure in poorer Islamic countries.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Bank is taught as a development-finance answer.
**Answer: True**
