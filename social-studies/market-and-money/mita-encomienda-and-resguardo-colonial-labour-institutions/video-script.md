# Video script — Mita encomienda and resguardo colonial labour institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mita encomienda and resguardo colonial labour institutions — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: The colony organised labour through the mita (obligatory work turns in mines and works), the encomienda (indigenous people assigned to Spaniards) and the resguardo (indigenous towns organised to manage labour and collect tribute); When the indigenous population was decimated, labour grew scarce and expensive and mining productivity fell, reaching crisis by 1630 the mita obliged a set number of indigenous people to work six-month turns in agriculture, domestic service and mining; The encomienda handed an encomendero a group of indigenous families who were forced into farm and mine work to pay tribute to him and the king, and whose labour enriched hacendados and landowners;
