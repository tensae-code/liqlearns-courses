## Sub lesson 1 — The colony organised labour through the mita obligatory

The colony organised labour through the mita (obligatory work turns in mines and works), the encomienda (indigenous people assigned to Spaniards) and the resguardo (indigenous towns organised to manage labour and collect tribute);

## Sub lesson 2 — When the indigenous population was decimated labour grew

When the indigenous population was decimated, labour grew scarce and expensive and mining productivity fell, reaching crisis by 1630 the mita obliged a set number of indigenous people to work six-month turns in agriculture, domestic service and mining;

## Sub lesson 3 — The encomienda handed an encomendero a group of

The encomienda handed an encomendero a group of indigenous families who were forced into farm and mine work to pay tribute to him and the king, and whose labour enriched hacendados and landowners; the resguardo was the political-social system of reserved indigenous towns
