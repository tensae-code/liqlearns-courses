# Mita encomienda and resguardo colonial labour institutions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The colony organised labour through the mita (obligatory work turns in mines and works), the encomienda (indigenous people assigned to _____ and the resguardo (indigenous towns organised to manage labour and collect tribute);
**Answer: Spaniards**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When the indigenous population was decimated, labour grew scarce and expensive and mining productivity fell, reaching crisis by _____ the mita obliged a set number of indigenous people to work six-month turns in agriculture, domestic service and mining;
**Answer: 1630**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The encomienda handed an _____ a group of indigenous families who were forced into farm and mine work to pay tribute to him and the king, and whose labour enriched hacendados and landowners;
**Answer: encomendero**
