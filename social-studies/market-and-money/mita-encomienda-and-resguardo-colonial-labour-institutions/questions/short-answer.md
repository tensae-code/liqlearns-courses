# Mita encomienda and resguardo colonial labour institutions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about the colony organised labour through the mita obligatory?
**Answer:** The colony organised labour through the mita (obligatory work turns in mines and works), the encomienda (indigenous people assigned to Spaniards) and the resguardo (indigenous towns organised to manage labour and collect tribute);

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about when the indigenous population was decimated labour grew?
**Answer:** When the indigenous population was decimated, labour grew scarce and expensive and mining productivity fell, reaching crisis by 1630 the mita obliged a set number of indigenous people to work six-month turns in agriculture, domestic service and mining;

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the encomienda handed an encomendero a group of?
**Answer:** The encomienda handed an encomendero a group of indigenous families who were forced into farm and mine work to pay tribute to him and the king, and whose labour enriched hacendados and landowners;
