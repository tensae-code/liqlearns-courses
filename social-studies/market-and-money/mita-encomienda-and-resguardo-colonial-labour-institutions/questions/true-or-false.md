# Mita encomienda and resguardo colonial labour institutions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The colony organised labour through the mita (obligatory work turns in mines and works), the encomienda (indigenous people assigned to Spaniards) and the resguardo (indigenous towns organised to manage labour and collect tribute);
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sub lesson 2 is mainly about the colony organised labour through the mita obligatory.
**Answer: False** — sub lesson 2 is about when the indigenous population was decimated labour grew; the colony organised labour through the mita obligatory is sub lesson 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The encomienda handed an encomendero a group of indigenous families who were forced into farm and mine work to pay tribute to him and the king, and whose labour enriched hacendados and landowners;
**Answer: True** — this is what Colombia's book teaches in this part.
