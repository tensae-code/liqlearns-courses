# Buying without a marketplace

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — buying without a marketplace — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Orders by phone and Internet, delivered home

Buying and selling also happen **by phone and Internet orders delivered home** — no visit to a market place at all.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hidden markets between factories

There are **hidden markets** where **factories buy fertiliser, engines and parts from other factories** — trade the ordinary shopper never sees.

## Sub lesson 3 — Markets beyond the marketplace

A market is **wherever buying and selling happen** — a street, a phone line, the Internet, or between factories — not only a marketplace with stalls.

## Say it to your companion

Explain buying without a marketplace to your companion in your own words, part by part, from the 3 sub lessons. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
