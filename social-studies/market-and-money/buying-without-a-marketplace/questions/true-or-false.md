# Buying without a marketplace — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All buying requires a visit to a marketplace.
**Answer: False — phone and Internet orders are delivered home.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hidden markets are markets the public shops in daily.
**Answer: False — they are factory-to-factory markets, hidden from shoppers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A market must have stalls and a roof.
**Answer: False — markets also exist by phone, Internet and between factories.**
