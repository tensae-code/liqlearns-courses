# Buying without a marketplace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Phone and Internet orders are —
a) delivered home  b) never delivered  c) only for factories
**Answer: a) delivered home**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hidden markets are where —
a) factories buy from other factories  b) children buy sweets  c) farmers sell vegetables
**Answer: a) factories buy from other factories**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A market exists —
a) wherever buying and selling happen  b) only where there are stalls  c) only in villages
**Answer: a) wherever buying and selling happen**
