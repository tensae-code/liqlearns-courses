# Buying without a marketplace — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How can you buy without going to a market?
**Answer:** By phone or Internet orders delivered home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a hidden market in the lesson?
**Answer:** Factories buying inputs like fertiliser, engines and parts from other factories.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the lesson's wider idea of a market?
**Answer:** Anywhere buying and selling happen, not only a physical marketplace.
