# Hands-on — Electronic money — cards, ATMs and transfers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Mock ATM: one learner 'banks' at a cardboard terminal at odd hours (the class calls times — 2 a.m., holiday, payday) while a cash-only learner must wait for 'bank hours'. Compare and list e-money's advantages as they appear.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — an ATM, a debit card at a shop terminal and a phone transfer on one panel; a warning sign for cybercrime on the other — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
