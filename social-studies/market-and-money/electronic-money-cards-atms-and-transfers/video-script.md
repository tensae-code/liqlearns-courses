# Video script — Electronic money — cards, ATMs and transfers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

E-money is an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments — debit, prepaid and credit cards at point-of-sale terminals. In practice for many people it means 24-hour ATM access and direct deposit of pay.

As electronic fund transfer (EFT), e-money replaces cheques and paper with cards, codes and PINs. In Ethiopia, ATMs are presented as terminals letting you bank at almost any time — pupils are sent to visit local banks and ask why people prefer ATMs, and that field task is part of the teaching.

Advantages: speed, better record-keeping, global transfers, and moving large sums with no physical burden. Disadvantages: cybercrime and digital money laundering, the training and knowledge users need, some forms (cryptocurrencies are named) being linked to criminal activity, and the hardware and software transfers require.
