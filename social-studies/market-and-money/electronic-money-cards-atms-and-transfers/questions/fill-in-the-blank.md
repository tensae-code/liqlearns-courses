# Electronic money — cards, ATMs and transfers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E-money is an _____ store of monetary value on a technical device.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** EFT replaces cheques and paper with cards, codes and _____.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** One named disadvantage of e-money is _____ and digital money laundering.
