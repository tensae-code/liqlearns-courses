# Electronic money — cards, ATMs and transfers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What e-money is" in your own words.
**Answer:** E-money is an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "EFT — paper replaced" in your own words.
**Answer:** Electronic fund transfer replaces cheques and paper with cards, codes and PINs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Advantages and disadvantages" in your own words.
**Answer:** E-money's advantages are speed, better record-keeping, global transfers and moving large sums without physical burden; its disadvantages include cybercrime and the training users need.
