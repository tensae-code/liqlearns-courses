# Electronic money — cards, ATMs and transfers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E-money is an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** E-money is an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (What e-money is); sub lesson 2 says: Electronic fund transfer replaces cheques and paper with cards, codes and PINs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** E-money's advantages are speed, better record-keeping, global transfers and moving large sums without physical burden; its disadvantages include cybercrime and the training users need.
**Answer: True**
