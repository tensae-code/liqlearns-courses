# Electronic money — cards, ATMs and transfers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What e-money is"?
a) an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments  b) cards, codes and PINs replacing cheques and paper; ATMs banking at almost any time  c) speed and records against cybercrime, training needs and equipment costs
**Answer: a) an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "EFT — paper replaced"?
a) an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments  b) speed and records against cybercrime, training needs and equipment costs  c) cards, codes and PINs replacing cheques and paper; ATMs banking at almost any time
**Answer: c) cards, codes and PINs replacing cheques and paper; ATMs banking at almost any time**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Advantages and disadvantages"?
a) cards, codes and PINs replacing cheques and paper; ATMs banking at almost any time  b) speed and records against cybercrime, training needs and equipment costs  c) an electronic store of monetary value on a technical device, used widely for payments
**Answer: b) speed and records against cybercrime, training needs and equipment costs**
