# Markets — and the market for goods and services — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A market is an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange, physically or digitally online.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A market is an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange, physically or digitally online.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (What a market is); sub lesson 2 says: In the goods and services market households buy consumable goods and services and businesses sell them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the goods and services market, money flows continuously from consumers to firms.
**Answer: True**
