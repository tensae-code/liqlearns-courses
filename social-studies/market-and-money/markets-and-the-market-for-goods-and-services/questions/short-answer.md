# Markets — and the market for goods and services — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What a market is" in your own words.
**Answer:** A market is an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange, physically or digitally online.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The goods and services market" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the goods and services market households buy consumable goods and services and businesses sell them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The money flow in it" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the goods and services market, money flows continuously from consumers to firms.
