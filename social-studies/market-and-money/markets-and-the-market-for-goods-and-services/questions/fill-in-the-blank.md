# Markets — and the market for goods and services — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A market is an institution where buyers and sellers meet to _____.
**Answer: exchange**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the goods and services market _____ buy and businesses sell.
**Answer: households**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Money flows continuously from _____ to firms in this market.
**Answer: consumers**
