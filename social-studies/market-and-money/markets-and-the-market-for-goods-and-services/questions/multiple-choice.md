# Markets — and the market for goods and services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What a market is"?
a) an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange, physically or online  b) households buy, businesses sell, end products are traded  c) money flows continuously from consumers to firms
**Answer: a) an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange, physically or online**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The goods and services market"?
a) an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange, physically or online  b) money flows continuously from consumers to firms  c) households buy, businesses sell, end products are traded
**Answer: c) households buy, businesses sell, end products are traded**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The money flow in it"?
a) households buy, businesses sell, end products are traded  b) money flows continuously from consumers to firms  c) an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange, physically or online
**Answer: b) money flows continuously from consumers to firms**
