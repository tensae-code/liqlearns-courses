# Markets — and the market for goods and services

## Do it first

This week you will 'go to market' without leaving your chair at least once, and walk to a real stall at least once. Name both. What makes both of them markets?

## Sub lesson 1 — What a market is

A market is an institution where buyers and sellers meet to exchange — physically, face to face in a shop, or digitally online with no physical contact. 'Meet' can mean a square, a stall, or a screen.

## Sub lesson 2 — The goods and services market

In the goods and services market, households buy consumable goods and services and businesses sell them. This is the market of daily life — food, clothes, haircuts, transport — where end products are what is traded.

## Sub lesson 3 — The money flow in it

Money flows continuously from consumers to firms through this market — every purchase is birr leaving a household and arriving at a business. Later lessons trace this flow around the whole circular economy.
