# Hands-on — Markets — and the market for goods and services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Two stalls: one physical (goods on a mat), one 'online' (a phone passed hand to hand). Run the same purchase both ways and list what stayed the same.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a physical market stall and a phone shopping screen side by side, money arrows pointing to the sellers — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
