# Free trade treaties what they promise — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Free trade treaties are bilateral _____ agreements;
**Answer: commercial**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** From _____ theory they are held to benefit a country's and the world's economy — eliminating protectionist barriers gives exports wider, faster access to foreign markets and consumers better access to quality goods and services, raising wellbeing;
**Answer: international-trade**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Colombia expected to grow economically by signing its FTA with the _____ States
**Answer: United**
