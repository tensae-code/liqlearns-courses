The service providers that aid economic activities — communication providers, banks and transport companies — and how each supports business and economic development.

Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG: Grade 7 Trade profiles the economic activities that contribute to PNG's national development — primary, secondary and tertiary industries, the banks, transport and communication firms that aid business, development projects, and direct government aid to local producers including SMEC loans through the National Development Bank.

Putting it together: the service providers that aid economic activities — communication providers, banks and transport companies — and how each supports business and economic development — taught in Economic Activities unit, Lessons 129–131 (KSAV) (Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG, printed p. 91).
