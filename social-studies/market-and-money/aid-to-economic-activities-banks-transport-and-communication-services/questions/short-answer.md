# Aid to economic activities banks transport and communication services — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach in "The service providers that aid economic activities —"?
**Answer:** The service providers that aid economic activities — communication providers, banks and transport companies — and how each supports business and economic development.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach in "Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this"?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's own way of teaching this, from the Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG: Grade 7 Trade profiles the economic activities that contribute to PNG's national development — primary, secondary and tertiary industries, the banks, transport and communication firms that aid business, development projects, and direct government aid to local producers including SMEC loans through the National Development Bank.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach in "Putting it together: the service providers that aid"?
**Answer:** Putting it together: the service providers that aid economic activities — communication providers, banks and transport companies — and how each supports business and economic development — taught in Economic Activities unit, Lessons 129–131 (KSAV) (Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG, printed p. 91).
