# Cooperatives and SACCOs in Rwanda

## Sub lesson 1 — A cooperative — working together for a common goal

A **co-operative** is a **group working together for a common goal**. Its types include **producer**, **worker** and **savings-and-credit** co-operatives.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rwanda's SACCOs — Umwalimu, Umurenge and Zigama CSS

Rwanda's named savings-and-credit co-operatives are **Umwalimu SACCO**, **Umurenge SACCO** and **Zigama CSS**.

## Sub lesson 3 — What cooperatives do

Co-operatives **give loans to members**, **provide tools, seeds and fertiliser**, **help farmers sell produce** and **promote development**.
