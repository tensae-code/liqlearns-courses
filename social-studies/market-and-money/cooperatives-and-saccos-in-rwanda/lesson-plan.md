# Lesson plan — Cooperatives and SACCOs in Rwanda

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Cooperatives and SACCOs in Rwanda
**Place in the curriculum:** Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Rwanda, 2026-11-16).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A co-operative is a group working together for a common goal. Its types include producer, worker and savings-and-credit co-operatives. Rwanda's named savings-and-credit co-operatives are Umwalimu SACCO, Umurenge SACCO and Zigama CSS. Co-operatives give loans to members, provide tools, seeds and fertiliser, help farmers sell produce and promote development.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Rwanda's own Grade book teaching — the learner meets the thing as Rwanda's primary course teaches it.
