# Cooperatives and SACCOs in Rwanda — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A co-operative is a group working together for a common goal.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rwanda's named savings-and-credit co-operatives are Umwalimu SACCO, Umurenge SACCO and Zigama CSS.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Co-operatives give loans to members, provide tools, seeds and fertiliser, help farmers sell produce and promote development.
**Answer: True**
