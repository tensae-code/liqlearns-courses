# Cooperatives and SACCOs in Rwanda — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 1 teach about a cooperative — working together for a common goal?
**Answer:** A co-operative is a group working together for a common goal. Its types include producer, worker and savings-and-credit co-operatives.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 2 teach about rwanda's saccos — umwalimu, umurenge and zigama css?
**Answer:** Rwanda's named savings-and-credit co-operatives are Umwalimu SACCO, Umurenge SACCO and Zigama CSS.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words, what does sub lesson 3 teach about what cooperatives do?
**Answer:** Co-operatives give loans to members, provide tools, seeds and fertiliser, help farmers sell produce and promote development.
