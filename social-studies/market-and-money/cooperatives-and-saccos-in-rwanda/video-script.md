Cooperatives and SACCOs in Rwanda — as Rwanda's book teaches it, says the narrator: A co-operative is a group working together for a common goal. Its types include producer, worker and savings-and-credit co-operatives. Rwanda's named savings-and-credit co-operatives are Umwalimu SACCO, Umurenge SACCO and Zigama CSS. Co-operatives give loans to members, provide tools, seeds and fertiliser, help farmers sell produce and promote development.
