Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 10 Social Studies): Grade 10 economics is the money-and-state unit, taught as documents a pupil could meet in life — a student's study report for energy, a first-person story for foreign employment, a central-bank officer's school visit for financial education, a mother's letter for insurance, a street-corner conversation with a businessman for revenue, and the Sixteenth Plan's own target table for planning.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 10 Teacher's Guide G10): the guide teaches the same Grade 10 money-and-state unit through classroom artefacts — charts of Nepal's hydro production figures, photographs of workers in foreign employment, an ATM card, a mobile banking app, a clean-note chart and a cheque specimen, cooperative brochures and principles, insurance company advertisements and news, and metacards of the taxes in force in Nepal. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 11 Business Studies): the optional Business Studies book carries the two most technical economics units in the whole Nepal set — insurance taught through its seven principles with a burnt cloth factory's compensation worked in rupees, and the tax system taught down to the current rates and the PAN registration clicks.
