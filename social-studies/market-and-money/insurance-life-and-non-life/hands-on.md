# Hands-on — Insurance — life and non-life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
List the risks in your family's life and work, and sort them: which could life insurance cover, which non-life, and which neither?

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main sorting or chain from memory and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
