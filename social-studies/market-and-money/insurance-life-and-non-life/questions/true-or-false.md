# Insurance — life and non-life — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insurance is the means by which the financial loss from an uncertain risk is made good: the insurer and the insured agree, the written agreement is the policy (bimalekh), and the company bears the insured's losses for a premium.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Life insurance pays the nominee after death, or pays a lump sum at the end of a term — itself a kind of compulsory saving, with loans available against it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Non-life insurance values damaged property and pays compensation, and insurance works by principles that include something else entirely, insurable interest and indemnity, from the burnt cloth factory compensated with Rs. 50 lakh for the property and Rs. 25 lakh under business-interruption cover.
**Answer: False** — Non-life insurance values damaged property and pays compensation, and insurance works by principles that include utmost good faith, insurable interest and indemnity, from the burnt cloth factory compensated with Rs. 50 lakh for the property and Rs. 25 lakh under business-interruption cover.
