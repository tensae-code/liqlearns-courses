Saudi Arabia's own way: a whole Economics unit in Grade 4, anchored in the Kingdom's own production — dates, milk and oil — and ending with a buy-Saudi-made activity at the supermarket. This placement keeps that way: barter is met in the Kingdom's own goods — dried yoghurt swapped for dates, ghee for wheat — before exchange moves to money in shops, markets and online.

Syria's own way: barter is met in old Damascus, inside the Grade 4 Pillars of the Economy unit's trade lesson — the oil producer giving part of his oil for eggs and wheat — until, as life developed, peoples began to mint money and whoever produces a good or does a work receives money in return.
