# Hands-on — Barter — and why it fails

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Barter fair: each learner holds one goods card and one 'want' card; trade only by direct swap. After five minutes, count completed trades and name the limitation that blocked each failed one.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — two traders circling each other, each holding what the other does not want, with the five problems as small signs around them — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
