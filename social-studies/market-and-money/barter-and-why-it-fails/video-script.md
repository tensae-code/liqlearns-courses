# Video script — Barter — and why it fails

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before money, exchange was barter — goods directly for goods, each producer swapping a surplus for what was needed. Its first difficulty is the double coincidence of wants: finding someone who wants your surplus and has what you want. Its second: no common measure of value — how much wheat for one sheep?

Third: indivisibility — a horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes without destroying its value. Fourth: difficulty storing and transferring wealth — goods perish, or cost much to store and move, so riches rot or crawl.

Fifth: difficulty with deferred payments — barter goods have unstable values and quality over time, so 'pay me in goats next year' invites argument about which goats. Five failures, one cure: money, whose evolution comes next.
