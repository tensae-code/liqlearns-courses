# Barter — and why it fails — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barter requires a double _____ of wants — each side wanting what the other has.
**Answer: coincidence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes — barter's problem of _____.
**Answer: indivisibility**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Barter's fifth failure is difficulty with _____ payments.
**Answer: deferred**
