# Barter — and why it fails — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barter's first difficulties are the double coincidence of wants and having no common measure of value.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Barter's first difficulties are the double coincidence of wants and having no common measure of value.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Barter and its first two failures); sub lesson 2 says: Barter fails on indivisibility — a horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes — and on the difficulty of storing and transferring wealth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Barter makes deferred payments difficult because goods' values and quality are unstable over time.
**Answer: True**
