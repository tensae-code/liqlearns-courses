# Barter — and why it fails — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Barter and its first two failures" in your own words.
**Answer:** Barter's first difficulties are the double coincidence of wants and having no common measure of value.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Indivisibility and storing wealth" in your own words.
**Answer:** Barter fails on indivisibility — a horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes — and on the difficulty of storing and transferring wealth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Deferred payments fail too" in your own words.
**Answer:** Barter makes deferred payments difficult because goods' values and quality are unstable over time.
