# Barter — and why it fails — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Barter and its first two failures"?
a) double coincidence of wants; no common measure of value  b) a horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes; goods perish and cost much to store and move  c) unstable values and quality over time make paying later unreliable
**Answer: a) double coincidence of wants; no common measure of value**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Indivisibility and storing wealth"?
a) double coincidence of wants; no common measure of value  b) unstable values and quality over time make paying later unreliable  c) a horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes; goods perish and cost much to store and move
**Answer: c) a horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes; goods perish and cost much to store and move**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Deferred payments fail too"?
a) a horse cannot be cut to pay for shoes; goods perish and cost much to store and move  b) unstable values and quality over time make paying later unreliable  c) double coincidence of wants; no common measure of value
**Answer: b) unstable values and quality over time make paying later unreliable**
