# The labour market

## Do it first

At a town corner, workers wait hoping someone hires them for the day. Who is supplying what in that scene — and who is doing the 'buying'?

## Sub lesson 1 — Who supplies, who demands

The labour market is the market in which employees supply labour services and employers supply the jobs. Note the wording: workers are the suppliers here — what they sell is their labour.

[Picture 24045 — workers of several trades on one side, employers with vacancies on the other]

## Sub lesson 2 — Who is in it

Day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and professionals all supply their labour here. The labour market is not one corner or office — it is every hiring, at every skill level, in the economy.

## Sub lesson 3 — Two levels of view

The labour market can be viewed at the individual level — one person's search for one job — and at the whole-economy level, where all hiring together decides employment. Both views study the same market at different heights.
