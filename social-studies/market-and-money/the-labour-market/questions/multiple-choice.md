# The labour market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Who supplies, who demands"?
a) employees supply labour services; employers supply the jobs  b) day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and professionals  c) viewed at the individual level and the whole-economy level
**Answer: a) employees supply labour services; employers supply the jobs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Who is in it"?
a) employees supply labour services; employers supply the jobs  b) viewed at the individual level and the whole-economy level  c) day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and professionals
**Answer: c) day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and professionals**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Two levels of view"?
a) day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and professionals  b) viewed at the individual level and the whole-economy level  c) employees supply labour services; employers supply the jobs
**Answer: b) viewed at the individual level and the whole-economy level**
