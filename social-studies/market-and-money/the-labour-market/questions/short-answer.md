# The labour market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Who supplies, who demands" in your own words.
**Answer:** In the labour market, employees supply labour services and employers supply the jobs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Who is in it" in your own words.
**Answer:** Day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and professionals all supply their labour in the labour market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Two levels of view" in your own words.
**Answer:** The labour market can be viewed at both the individual and the whole-economy level.
