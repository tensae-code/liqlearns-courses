# The labour market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the labour market, employees supply _____ services.
**Answer: labour**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and _____ all supply labour here.
**Answer: professionals**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The labour market can be viewed at the individual and the _____ level.
**Answer: whole-economy**
