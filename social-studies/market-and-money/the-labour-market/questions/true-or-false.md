# The labour market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the labour market, employees supply labour services and employers supply the jobs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the labour market, employees supply labour services and employers supply the jobs.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Who supplies, who demands); sub lesson 2 says: Day labourers, domestic workers, skilled workers and professionals all supply their labour in the labour market.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The labour market can be viewed at both the individual and the whole-economy level.
**Answer: True**
