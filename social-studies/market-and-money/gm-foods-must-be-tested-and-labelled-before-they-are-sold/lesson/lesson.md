# GM foods must be tested and labelled before they are sold

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — gm foods must be tested and labelled before they are sold — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Before gm foods may be commercialised they must

Before GM foods may be commercialised they must pass an evaluation proving no danger to health or environment

## Sub lesson 2 — And labels must state the origin and effects

And labels must state the origin and effects of the chemical substances used;

## Sub lesson 3 — Some scientists warn modified organisms may produce mutations

Some scientists warn modified organisms may produce mutations or toxic substances
