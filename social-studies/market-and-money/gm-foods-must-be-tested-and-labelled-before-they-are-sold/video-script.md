# Video script — GM foods must be tested and labelled before they are sold

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

GM foods must be tested and labelled before they are sold — as Colombia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Before GM foods may be commercialised they must pass an evaluation proving no danger to health or environment And labels must state the origin and effects of the chemical substances used; Some scientists warn modified organisms may produce mutations or toxic substances
