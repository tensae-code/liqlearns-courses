# GM foods must be tested and labelled before they are sold — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about before gm foods may be commercialised they must?
**Answer:** Before GM foods may be commercialised they must pass an evaluation proving no danger to health or environment

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about and labels must state the origin and effects?
**Answer:** And labels must state the origin and effects of the chemical substances used;
