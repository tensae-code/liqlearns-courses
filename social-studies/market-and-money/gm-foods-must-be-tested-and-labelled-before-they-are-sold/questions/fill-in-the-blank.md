# GM foods must be tested and labelled before they are sold — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before GM foods may be _____ they must pass an evaluation proving no danger to health or environment
**Answer: commercialised**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** And labels must state the origin and effects of the chemical _____ used;
**Answer: substances**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Some _____ warn modified organisms may produce mutations or toxic substances
**Answer: scientists**
