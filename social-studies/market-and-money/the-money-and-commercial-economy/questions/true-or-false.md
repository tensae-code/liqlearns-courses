# The money and commercial economy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture, mining, industry and services are among the money economy's diverse activities.
**Answer: True** — the book lists them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the money economy, each person produces everything they use.
**Answer: False** — they buy food and objects produced by other people.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The subsistence economy was said to cause grave environmental damage, unlike the money economy.
**Answer: False** — it is the money economy that harms nature in many cases; the subsistence economy does not cause grave damage.
