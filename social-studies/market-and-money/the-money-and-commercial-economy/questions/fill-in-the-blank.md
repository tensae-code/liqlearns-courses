# The money and commercial economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The money economy in Spanish is the economía comercial or _____.
**Answer: dineraria**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the money economy a person buys objects produced by _____ people.
**Answer: other**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In many countries the subsistence and monetary economies _____.
**Answer: coexist**
