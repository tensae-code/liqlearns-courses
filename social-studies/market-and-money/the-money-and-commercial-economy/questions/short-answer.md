# The money and commercial economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do people obtain what they need in the money and commercial economy?
**Answer:** They work in diverse activities, receive money for their work, and with it pay for services and buy food and objects produced by others.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does money let people in this economy do?
**Answer:** Pay for services and buy food and objects produced by other people, instead of making everything themselves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the money economy do to nature, and how do the two economies relate in many countries?
**Answer:** It harms nature in many cases, exploiting it exaggeratedly to obtain many products to sell; and in many countries the subsistence and monetary economies coexist.
