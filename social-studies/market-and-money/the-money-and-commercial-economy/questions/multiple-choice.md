# The money and commercial economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the money economy, people receive —
a) money in exchange for their work in diverse activities  b) only goods in barter  c) nothing for their work
**Answer: a) money in exchange for their work in diverse activities**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** With their money, people in this economy —
a) pay for services and buy food and objects produced by other people  b) must still make everything themselves  c) can buy nothing
**Answer: a) pay for services and buy food and objects produced by other people**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The money economy harms nature, in the book, because —
a) nature is exploited exaggeratedly to obtain many products to sell  b) money itself is poisonous  c) it uses no natural resources at all
**Answer: a) nature is exploited exaggeratedly to obtain many products to sell**
