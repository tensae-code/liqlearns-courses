# Hands-on — Exchange rates — the price of a currency

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Price it both ways
Take one Ethiopian product with a birr price. Price it for a foreign buyer at two exchange rates, then say in one sentence what each rate does to export demand.

## Flip the quote
Convert three rates both ways (birr per dollar and dollars per birr), showing the reciprocal each time, and label which currency each quote prices.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
