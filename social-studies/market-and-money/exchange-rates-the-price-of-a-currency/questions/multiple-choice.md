# Exchange rates — the price of a currency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The jacket at two rates"?
a) At 2 rupees per birr the 1,500 birr jacket costs 3,000 rupees; at 3 rupees per birr, 4,500 rupees  b) A stronger birr makes exports dearer abroad and shifts AD left; a depreciation shifts AD right  c) 1 dollar = 30 birr is the same price as 1 birr ≈ 0.033 dollars
**Answer: a) At 2 rupees per birr the 1,500 birr jacket costs 3,000 rupees; at 3 rupees per birr, 4,500 rupees**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What a stronger birr does to demand"?
a) A stronger birr makes exports dearer abroad and shifts AD left; a depreciation shifts AD right  b) 1 dollar = 30 birr is the same price as 1 birr ≈ 0.033 dollars  c) At 2 rupees per birr the 1,500 birr jacket costs 3,000 rupees; at 3 rupees per birr, 4,500 rupees
**Answer: a) A stronger birr makes exports dearer abroad and shifts AD left; a depreciation shifts AD right**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Two ways to quote one rate"?
a) 1 dollar = 30 birr is the same price as 1 birr ≈ 0.033 dollars  b) At 2 rupees per birr the 1,500 birr jacket costs 3,000 rupees; at 3 rupees per birr, 4,500 rupees  c) A stronger birr makes exports dearer abroad and shifts AD left; a depreciation shifts AD right
**Answer: a) 1 dollar = 30 birr is the same price as 1 birr ≈ 0.033 dollars**
