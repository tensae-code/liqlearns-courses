# Exchange rates — the price of a currency — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At 2 rupees per birr the 1,500 birr jacket costs 3,000 rupees; at 3 rupees per birr, 4,500 rupees.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A stronger birr makes exports dearer abroad and shifts AD left; a depreciation shifts AD right.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 1 dollar = 30 birr is the same price as 1 birr ≈ 0.033 dollars.
**Answer: True**
