# Exchange rates — the price of a currency — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The jacket at two rates" in your own words.
**Answer:** At 2 rupees per birr the 1,500 birr jacket costs 3,000 rupees; at 3 rupees per birr, 4,500 rupees.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What a stronger birr does to demand" in your own words.
**Answer:** A stronger birr makes exports dearer abroad and shifts AD left; a depreciation shifts AD right.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Two ways to quote one rate" in your own words.
**Answer:** 1 dollar = 30 birr is the same price as 1 birr ≈ 0.033 dollars.
