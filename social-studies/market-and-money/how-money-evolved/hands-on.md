# Hands-on — How money evolved — commodity, paper, cheques, electronic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Relay race of payment: pass 'value' across the room as a heavy sack, a coin pouch, a note, a cheque slip, then a phone tap — the class times each and names the stage.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a five-step timeline — salt bar, metal coin, paper note, cheque book, phone payment — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
