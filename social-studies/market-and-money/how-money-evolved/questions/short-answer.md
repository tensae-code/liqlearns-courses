# How money evolved — commodity, paper, cheques, electronic — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Commodity money and paper" in your own words.
**Answer:** Money evolved from commodity money to paper currency, first convertible and then fiat money decreed legal tender.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Cheques" in your own words.
**Answer:** A cheque is drawn on a bank account and payable on demand, removing the need to carry bulk currency.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Electronic payment" in your own words.
**Answer:** Electronic payment is the stage where paperwork disappears and value moves as signals between accounts.
