# How money evolved — commodity, paper, cheques, electronic — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Money evolved from commodity money to paper currency, first convertible and then fiat money decreed legal tender.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money evolved from commodity money to paper currency, first convertible and then fiat money decreed legal tender.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (Commodity money and paper); sub lesson 2 says: A cheque is drawn on a bank account and payable on demand, removing the need to carry bulk currency.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Electronic payment is the stage where paperwork disappears and value moves as signals between accounts.
**Answer: True**
