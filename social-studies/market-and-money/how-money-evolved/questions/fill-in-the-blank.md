# How money evolved — commodity, paper, cheques, electronic — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Paper money decreed legal tender by government is _____ money.
**Answer: fiat**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cheque is payable on _____ and drawn on a bank account.
**Answer: demand**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In _____ payment, paperwork disappears altogether.
**Answer: electronic**
