# How money evolved — commodity, paper, cheques, electronic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Commodity money and paper"?
a) commodity money, then convertible paper, then fiat money decreed legal tender  b) drawn on a bank account, payable on demand, safer, and giving a receipt  c) electronic means of payment, where paperwork disappears
**Answer: a) commodity money, then convertible paper, then fiat money decreed legal tender**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Cheques"?
a) commodity money, then convertible paper, then fiat money decreed legal tender  b) electronic means of payment, where paperwork disappears  c) drawn on a bank account, payable on demand, safer, and giving a receipt
**Answer: c) drawn on a bank account, payable on demand, safer, and giving a receipt**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Electronic payment"?
a) drawn on a bank account, payable on demand, safer, and giving a receipt  b) electronic means of payment, where paperwork disappears  c) commodity money, then convertible paper, then fiat money decreed legal tender
**Answer: b) electronic means of payment, where paperwork disappears**
