# Financial institutions and the rules of the game — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Institutions are defined as the _____ — formal and informal — under which finance operates.
**Answer: rules of the game**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Insurance and pensions belong to the _____ institutions — one of the three types.
**Answer: contractual**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Non-bank institutions are bodies without a full _____ — pawn shops and payday lenders among them.
**Answer: banking licence**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The five components are institutions, markets, _____, services, and money.
**Answer: financial instruments**
