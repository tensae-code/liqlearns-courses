# Financial institutions and the rules of the game — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Institutions are the rules of the game, split into formal and informal.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The three types are depository, contractual and investment institutions.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Non-bank financial institutions operate without a full banking licence.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The financial system has five components: financial institutions, financial markets, financial instruments, financial services, and money.
**Answer: True**
