# Financial institutions and the rules of the game — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Institutions — rules, formal and informal" in your own words.
**Answer:** Institutions are the rules of the game, split into formal and informal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Three types, and banks' work in development" in your own words.
**Answer:** The three types are depository, contractual and investment institutions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Non-bank financial institutions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Non-bank financial institutions operate without a full banking licence.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Explain "The financial system in five components (teacher guide)" in your own words.
**Answer:** The financial system has five components: financial institutions, financial markets, financial instruments, financial services, and money.
