# Kinds of money — paper and credit money — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Paper money acceptable for payments of any size is _____ legal tender.
**Answer: unlimited**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cheque paid only through a bank account is a _____ cheque.
**Answer: crossed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A written promise to pay is a _____ note.
**Answer: promissory**
