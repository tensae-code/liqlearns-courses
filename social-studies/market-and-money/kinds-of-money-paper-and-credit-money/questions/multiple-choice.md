# Kinds of money — paper and credit money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About paper money — the lesson teaches that —
a) paper money is convertible or inconvertible, and limited or unlimited legal tender  b) the cheque kinds are traveller's, bearer, order and crossed  c) credit instruments include bills of exchange, promissory notes, bank drafts and credit cards
**Answer: a) paper money is convertible or inconvertible, and limited or unlimited legal tender**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About cheques — traveller's, bearer, order and crossed — the lesson teaches that —
a) paper money is convertible or inconvertible, and limited or unlimited legal tender  b) the cheque kinds are traveller's, bearer, order and crossed  c) credit instruments include bills of exchange, promissory notes, bank drafts and credit cards
**Answer: b) the cheque kinds are traveller's, bearer, order and crossed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About bills of exchange, promissory notes, bank drafts and credit cards — the lesson teaches that —
a) paper money is convertible or inconvertible, and limited or unlimited legal tender  b) the cheque kinds are traveller's, bearer, order and crossed  c) credit instruments include bills of exchange, promissory notes, bank drafts and credit cards
**Answer: c) credit instruments include bills of exchange, promissory notes, bank drafts and credit cards**
