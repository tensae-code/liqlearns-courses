# Kinds of money — paper and credit money — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All paper money is convertible into gold on demand.
**Answer: False** — inconvertible paper money exists and is a named kind.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A crossed cheque is paid in cash to anyone holding it.
**Answer: False** — a crossed cheque is paid only through a bank account.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A promissory note is a coin.
**Answer: False** — it is a written promise to pay — credit money, not a coin.
