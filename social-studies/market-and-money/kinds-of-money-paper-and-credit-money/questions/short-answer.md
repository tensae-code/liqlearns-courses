# Kinds of money — paper and credit money — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the kinds of paper money?
**Answer:** Convertible and inconvertible paper money; and limited against unlimited legal tender.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four kinds of cheque in the book.
**Answer:** Traveller's, bearer, order and crossed cheques.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the credit instruments that close the book's list.
**Answer:** Bills of exchange, promissory notes, bank drafts and credit cards.
