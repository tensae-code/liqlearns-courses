Kinds of money — paper and credit money — as Pakistan's book teaches it, says the narrator: paper money is convertible or inconvertible, and limited or unlimited legal tender; the cheque kinds are traveller's, bearer, order and crossed; credit instruments include bills of exchange, promissory notes, bank drafts and credit cards.
