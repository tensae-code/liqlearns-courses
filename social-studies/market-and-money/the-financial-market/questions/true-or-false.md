# The financial market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The financial market is where securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets are traded.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The financial market is where securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets are traded.
**Answer: False** — that statement belongs to sub lesson 1 (What is traded there); sub lesson 2 says: The financial market provides capital formation and liquidity for businesses.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The three markets are for goods and services, labour, and financial assets, divided by what is traded.
**Answer: True**
