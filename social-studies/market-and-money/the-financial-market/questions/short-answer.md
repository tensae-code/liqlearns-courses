# The financial market — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "What is traded there" in your own words.
**Answer:** The financial market is where securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets are traded.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "What it does for business" in your own words.
**Answer:** The financial market provides capital formation and liquidity for businesses.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The three markets together" in your own words.
**Answer:** The three markets are for goods and services, labour, and financial assets, divided by what is traded.
