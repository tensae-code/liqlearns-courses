# The financial market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "What is traded there"?
a) securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets — bond, stock and share markets  b) capital formation and liquidity for businesses  c) three markets divided by what is traded — products, work, financial claims
**Answer: a) securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets — bond, stock and share markets**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "What it does for business"?
a) securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets — bond, stock and share markets  b) three markets divided by what is traded — products, work, financial claims  c) capital formation and liquidity for businesses
**Answer: c) capital formation and liquidity for businesses**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The three markets together"?
a) capital formation and liquidity for businesses  b) three markets divided by what is traded — products, work, financial claims  c) securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets — bond, stock and share markets
**Answer: b) three markets divided by what is traded — products, work, financial claims**
