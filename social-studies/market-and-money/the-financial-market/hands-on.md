# Hands-on — The financial market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Act a tiny financial market: 'share' and 'bond' cards are traded between savers and a firm for play money; the firm explains what the raised capital will build.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — shares, bonds and currency changing hands over a trading screen — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
