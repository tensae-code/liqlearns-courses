# Video script — The financial market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The financial market is any place, physical or digital, where securities, currencies, bonds and other financial assets are traded — bond, stock and share markets are its named parts. Nothing you can eat or wear changes hands; claims and money do.

It provides capital formation — gathering savings into funds businesses can build with — and liquidity, the ability to turn assets back into money. The book presents it as the basis of capitalist societies for exactly this gathering work.

Goods and services, labour, financial: the three markets of this unit divide all exchange by what is traded — products, work, and financial claims. Pupils are sent to classify markets in their own surroundings into the three types — the local-survey task is part of the teaching.
