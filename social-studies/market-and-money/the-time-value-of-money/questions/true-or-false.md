# The time value of money — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The time value of money is the difference in worth between the same amount of money at different times.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A loan is repaid with exactly the amount borrowed, with nothing on top.
**Answer: False** — a loan must be repaid with interest on top of the amount borrowed; money has a cost.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Future cash flows can be compared directly with today's money, with no adjustment.
**Answer: False** — future cash flows cannot be compared directly; they are brought to present value first.
