# The time value of money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dollar received today is worth —
a) less than a dollar received in the future  b) exactly the same as a dollar received in the future  c) more than a dollar received in the future
**Answer: c) more than a dollar received in the future**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inflation makes future money worth less because —
a) it erodes what the same money buys  b) it makes coins heavier  c) it stops people from saving
**Answer: a) it erodes what the same money buys**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Future cash flows are brought to present value using —
a) a selling price  b) a discount rate that represents the opportunity cost of money  c) the venture's name
**Answer: b) a discount rate that represents the opportunity cost of money**
