# The time value of money — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dollar received _____ is worth more than a dollar received in the future.
**Answer: today**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A general rise in prices, which erodes what the same money buys, is called _____.
**Answer: inflation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Present value is the basis for net present value, called _____ in the book.
**Answer: VAN**
