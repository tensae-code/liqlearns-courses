# The time value of money — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the book's first rule about money over time.
**Answer:** A dollar received today is worth more than a dollar received in the future.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two reasons future money is worth less.
**Answer:** Inflation erodes what the same money buys, and money has a cost — a loan must be repaid with interest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the discount rate, and what is present value used for?
**Answer:** The discount rate represents the opportunity cost of money; future cash flows are brought to present value with it, which is the basis for net present value (VAN).
