# Hands-on — The time value of money

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Put a coin on the table for "today". Next to it, put a smaller pile of beans for "the same coin in a year, after prices have risen". Say out loud why the second pile buys less, even though the coin is the same.

## Draw it and check it
Draw a timeline with three points — today, one year from now, two years from now. Write a future amount at the last point and draw an arrow bringing it back to its present value today. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
