# Video script — The time value of money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A dollar today is worth more than a dollar next year. Why? Because prices rise — that is inflation — so the same dollar buys less later. And because money has a cost: if you borrow a dollar, you pay it back with interest on top.

So you cannot compare money from different years directly. You bring future money back to what it is worth today — its present value — using a discount rate, the opportunity cost of money. That is the first step to net present value, the VAN: compare everything in today's dollars, then decide.
