# The History of Money in Samoa — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Samoa's money story — fine mats and tapa (still ceremonial money), German marks.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** New Zealand pounds and shillings, the first Samoan money of 1959.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that its subject has nothing to do with the history of money in samoa.
**Answer: False — The lesson is built on Samoa's book teaching decimal tala and sene in 1967, the Central Bank in charge since 1984.**
