# The History of Money in Samoa

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of one case of this lesson's subject from your own life or town and say what you already know about it. Hold your answer — the lesson takes its subject in three parts, in order.

In Samoa's book (Year 9 Business Studies Book 1), **Unit 6, History of Money** — teaches: Samoa's money story — fine mats and tapa (still ceremonial money), German marks, New Zealand pounds and shillings, the first Samoan money of 1959, decimal tala and sene in 1967, the Central Bank in charge since 1984. This lesson teaches that placement in the course's own words.
