# Lesson plan — Saving and rationalising consumption

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Market and Money · **Lesson:** Saving and rationalising consumption
**Place in the curriculum:** Market and Money (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Economics expansion (Saudi Arabia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A producer makes goods and services and a consumer uses them; saving (الادخار) is setting aside part of one's money for future needs, and rationalising consumption (ترشيد الاستهلاك) is using resources and money moderately — switching off unused devices, using water as needed, and choosing moderately priced goods.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the book's own worked choice — a laptop at 1,500 riyals now against the same laptop at 1,100 riyals on sale next month.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
