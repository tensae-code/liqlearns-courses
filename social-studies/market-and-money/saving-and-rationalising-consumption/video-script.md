# Video script — Saving and rationalising consumption

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A producer makes goods and services. A consumer uses them. Every one of us is a consumer — and the book teaches two habits for consuming well.

The first habit is saving: setting aside part of your money, not spending it all, so it is there for a future need. The second habit is rationalising consumption: using resources and money moderately. Switch off a device nobody is using. Use water as needed, no more. And choose moderately priced goods — a laptop at 1,500 riyals today, or the same laptop at 1,100 riyals on sale next month? Waiting is also a way of spending well.
