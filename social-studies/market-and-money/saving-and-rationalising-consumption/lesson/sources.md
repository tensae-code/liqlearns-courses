Saudi Arabia's own way: a whole Economics unit in Grade 4, anchored in the Kingdom's own production — dates, milk and oil — and ending with a buy-Saudi-made activity at the supermarket. This lesson keeps that way: saving and moderate consumption are taught through the book's own riyal-priced laptop choice and the devices and water of the child's own home. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
