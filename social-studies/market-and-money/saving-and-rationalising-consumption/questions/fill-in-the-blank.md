# Saving and rationalising consumption — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The one who makes goods and provides services is the _____.
**Answer: producer**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Setting aside part of one's money for future needs is called _____.
**Answer: saving (الادخار)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's example, the laptop costs 1,500 riyals now and _____ riyals on sale next month.
**Answer: 1,100**
