# Saving and rationalising consumption — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A consumer is —
a) the one who makes goods  b) the one who uses goods and services  c) only a shopkeeper
**Answer: b) the one who uses goods and services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saving is —
a) spending all your money at once  b) setting aside part of your money for future needs  c) borrowing money
**Answer: b) setting aside part of your money for future needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rationalising consumption means —
a) using resources and money moderately  b) never buying anything  c) always buying the most expensive good
**Answer: a) using resources and money moderately**
