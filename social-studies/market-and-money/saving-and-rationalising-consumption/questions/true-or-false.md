# Saving and rationalising consumption — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The family that eats the dates is the consumer, and the dates grower is the producer.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Money that is saved is lost.
**Answer: False** — saved money waits for a future need; it turns part of today's money into tomorrow's security.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Leaving unused devices switched on is rationalising consumption.
**Answer: False** — rationalising consumption means using resources moderately, including switching off unused devices.
