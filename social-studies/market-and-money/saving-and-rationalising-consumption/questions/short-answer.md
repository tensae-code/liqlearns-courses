# Saving and rationalising consumption — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define producer and consumer.
**Answer:** The producer makes goods and provides services; the consumer uses those goods and services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is saving, and why does a consumer save?
**Answer:** Saving is setting aside part of one's money for future needs, so that part of today's money becomes tomorrow's security.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the book's three examples of rationalising consumption.
**Answer:** Switching off unused devices, using water as needed, and choosing moderately priced goods — like waiting for the laptop at 1,100 riyals instead of buying it at 1,500 riyals now.
