# Hands-on — Saving and rationalising consumption

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Walk through one room at home. Find one device switched on that nobody is using, and one place where water or light is being used beyond need. Say out loud what rationalising consumption would do in each place.

## Draw it and check it
Draw two purses from memory — one labelled "spent now" and one labelled "saved for a future need" — and split ten coins between them as you think best. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the book's laptop choice, and fix your reasoning where it differs.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
