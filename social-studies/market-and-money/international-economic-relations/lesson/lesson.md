# International economic relations

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — international economic relations — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Exchanging goods and services — trade and integrating markets

States relate economically first by **exchanging goods and services through imports, exports and international trade**, with **markets integrating** — joining together — as they trade.

## Sub lesson 2 — Monetary dealings — central banks, currency exchange, capital

The second form is money itself: **monetary dealings between central banks**, **currency exchange**, and the **free movement of capital** between states.

## Sub lesson 3 — Loans and aid between states

The third form is support: **loans and aid between states** — one state lending to, or aiding, another, as part of their economic relations.
