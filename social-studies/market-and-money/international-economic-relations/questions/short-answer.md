# International economic relations — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do states exchange goods and services?
**Answer:** Through imports, exports and international trade, with integrating markets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two monetary dealings.
**Answer:** Dealings between central banks and currency exchange (with free movement of capital).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What support passes between states?
**Answer:** Loans and aid.
