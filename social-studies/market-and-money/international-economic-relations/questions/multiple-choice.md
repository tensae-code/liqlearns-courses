# International economic relations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Goods and services move between states as imports and —
a) exports  b) central  c) aid
**Answer: a) exports**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Monetary dealings run between — banks
a) exports  b) central  c) aid
**Answer: b) central**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third form named is loans and — between states
a) exports  b) central  c) aid
**Answer: c) aid**
