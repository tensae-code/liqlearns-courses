# International economic relations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** International trade is a form of economic relations between states.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Capital is taught as unable to move between states.
**Answer: False — the free movement of capital is taught.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Loans and aid between states are part of the lesson.
**Answer: True**
