# Ethiopia's exchange rate reforms — auctions and the interbank market

## Do it first

A rate has been frozen at 2.07 for nearly twenty years while the world moved on. Do you jump to a market rate overnight, or walk there? Ethiopia chose — guess how, before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — October 1992 — the great devaluation

After the Derg's fall, the EPRDF devalued massively in October 1992 — by 140%, from 2.07 to 5 birr per dollar. Check the figure: (5 − 2.07) / 2.07 ≈ 1.42, about 140%. One decision undid two frozen decades.

## Sub lesson 2 — Auctions beside the official rate

In 1993 a fortnightly foreign-exchange auction began, run beside the official rate, which still covered fertilizer, petroleum, pharmaceuticals, international contributions and debt service. Two prices coexisted while the market price was discovered — gradualism as the stated principle.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unification to the interbank market

The two rates were unified in August 1995; a weekly wholesale auction followed in July 1996; and the interbank foreign-exchange market was established in 1998. Step by step, the price of foreign currency moved from decree toward the market.
