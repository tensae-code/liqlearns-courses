# Ethiopia's exchange rate reforms — auctions and the interbank market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "October 1992 — the great devaluation" in your own words.
**Answer:** In October 1992 the birr was devalued by 140%, from 2.07 to 5 per dollar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Auctions beside the official rate" in your own words.
**Answer:** A fortnightly FX auction began in 1993 beside the official rate.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Unification to the interbank market" in your own words.
**Answer:** Rates unified in 1995, weekly wholesale auction in 1996, interbank market in 1998.
