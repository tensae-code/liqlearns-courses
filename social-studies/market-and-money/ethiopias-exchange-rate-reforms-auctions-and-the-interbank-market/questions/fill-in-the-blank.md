# Ethiopia's exchange rate reforms — auctions and the interbank market — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The October 1992 devaluation took the rate from 2.07 to ____ birr per dollar.
**Answer: 5**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The foreign-exchange auction introduced in 1993 ran every ____.
**Answer: fortnight**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The interbank foreign-exchange market was established in ____.
**Answer: 1998**
