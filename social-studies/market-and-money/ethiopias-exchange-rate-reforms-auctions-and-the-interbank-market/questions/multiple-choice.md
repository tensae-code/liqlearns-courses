# Ethiopia's exchange rate reforms — auctions and the interbank market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "October 1992 — the great devaluation"?
a) In October 1992 the birr was devalued by 140%, from 2.07 to 5 per dollar  b) A fortnightly FX auction began in 1993 beside the official rate  c) Rates unified in 1995, weekly wholesale auction in 1996, interbank market in 1998
**Answer: a) In October 1992 the birr was devalued by 140%, from 2.07 to 5 per dollar**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Auctions beside the official rate"?
a) A fortnightly FX auction began in 1993 beside the official rate  b) Rates unified in 1995, weekly wholesale auction in 1996, interbank market in 1998  c) In October 1992 the birr was devalued by 140%, from 2.07 to 5 per dollar
**Answer: a) A fortnightly FX auction began in 1993 beside the official rate**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Unification to the interbank market"?
a) Rates unified in 1995, weekly wholesale auction in 1996, interbank market in 1998  b) In October 1992 the birr was devalued by 140%, from 2.07 to 5 per dollar  c) A fortnightly FX auction began in 1993 beside the official rate
**Answer: a) Rates unified in 1995, weekly wholesale auction in 1996, interbank market in 1998**
