# Ethiopia's exchange rate reforms — auctions and the interbank market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In October 1992 the birr was devalued by 140%, from 2.07 to 5 per dollar.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fortnightly FX auction began in 1993 beside the official rate.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rates unified in 1995, weekly wholesale auction in 1996, interbank market in 1998.
**Answer: True**
