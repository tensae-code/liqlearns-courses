# Hands-on — Ethiopia's exchange rate reforms — auctions and the interbank market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Steps in order
Put the five steps (1992 devaluation, 1993 auction, 1995 unification, 1996 weekly auction, 1998 interbank market) on a staircase and write one line per step on what changed for a buyer of dollars.

## Why gradual
Write three sentences defending gradualism against a 'jump overnight' critic, using the reserved official-rate purchases (fertilizer, petroleum, pharmaceuticals) as evidence.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
