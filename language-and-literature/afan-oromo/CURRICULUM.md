# Afan Oromo course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Afan Oromo is Ethiopia's most widely spoken language, and in the
Language and Literature structure it stands as its own course
inside that folder, beside Amharic (his architecture ruling,
2026-11-11). The path climbs by the size of the thing —
**Letters → Reading → Word → Sentence → Speaking → Writing →
Listening** (see TITLES.md).

**Levels, not grades:** a learner climbs when they are ready,
not when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books
come from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance),
never as the course's organisation.

**The first page (what learners are told) — and this course's
honest footing:** no Afan Oromo student textbook exists in any
record we hold. Everything in this course is founded on
Ethiopia's own **teacher guides** for learning Afan Oromo — the
Addis Ababa City Administration Education Bureau guides named in
each lesson's sources. The Grade 3 and Grade 4 guides teach
Afan Oromo as a **second language**; the Grade 7 guide teaches
it as a **mother tongue**, and the course keeps those two
framings apart, exactly as the guides do. Because the student
books themselves were never held, every lesson's acceptance
count stands at 0 countries — teacher-guide evidence only. The
one exception to "the lessons are the books'" is the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns.
Tips are ours; the lessons are the guides'.
