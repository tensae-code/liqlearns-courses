# Video script — Reading Everyday Sentences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A sentence tells a whole thing. **Muuzii ni mi'aawa** — a banana is sweet.
**Dammi fayyaa namaaf gaariidha** — honey is good for health.
A family lives in one house. Mother and father share the leading.
True — **dhugaa**. False — **soba**. The reading itself is the judge.
