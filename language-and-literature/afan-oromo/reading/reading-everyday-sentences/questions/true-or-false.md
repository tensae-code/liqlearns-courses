# Reading Everyday Sentences — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Bishaan dhugaatiif oola' — water is used for drinking.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The reading says a friend must be of good character.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The reading says children help only during study time.
**Answer: False** — children help outside their study time

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 'Where there is love and caring, the family has happiness' — the reading says this.
**Answer: True**
