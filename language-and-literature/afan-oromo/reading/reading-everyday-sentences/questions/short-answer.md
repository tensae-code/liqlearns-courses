# Reading Everyday Sentences — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is honey good for, in the sentence 'Dammi fayyaa namaaf gaariidha'?
**Answer: A person's health**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things the reading asks of children.
**Answer: Any two of: respect their family; help with what they can outside study time**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Correct this false statement from the reading: 'The father alone leads the family.'
**Answer: Father and mother share the responsibility of leading the family**
