# Reading Everyday Sentences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Muuzii ni mi'aawa' tells us a banana is —
a) Sour  b) Sweet  c) Big  d) Fast
**Answer: b) Sweet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Konkolaataan saffisaan deema' — the car goes —
a) Slowly  b) Fast  c) Home  d) Uphill
**Answer: b) Fast** — saffisaan — fast

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the reading, a family is —
a) People who study together  b) People who come together and live in one house  c) People who work in one office  d) People who travel together
**Answer: b) People who come together and live in one house**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who leads the family, in the reading?
a) The father alone  b) The mother alone  c) Father and mother share the leading  d) The oldest child
**Answer: c) Father and mother share the leading**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A statement disagrees with the reading. It is —
a) dhugaa  b) soba  c) gaalee  d) hima
**Answer: b) soba** — false — and the reading corrects it
