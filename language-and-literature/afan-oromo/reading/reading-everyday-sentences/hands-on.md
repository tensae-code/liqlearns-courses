# Hands-on — Reading Everyday Sentences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sentence walk

Read the four sentences, then go and find their things in your home: a banana or another fruit, honey if there is any, water in its cup. Say each sentence standing by its thing.

## Family in one house

Draw your own house and the people who live in it. Label each person — abbaa, haadha, obboleessa, obboleettii, akkoo — and say one true sentence about your drawing.
