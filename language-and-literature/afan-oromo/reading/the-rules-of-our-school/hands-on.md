# Hands-on — The Rules of Our School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pack for rule two

Tonight, pack your school bag by the rule: uniform ready, every learning material in the bag. Tomorrow, check yourself against rule one — arrive before the lesson starts.

## Care for one thing

Choose one piece of school property this week — a bench, a book, a tap — and care for it deliberately: straighten it, clean it, report it if it is broken. Rule five, done with hands.
