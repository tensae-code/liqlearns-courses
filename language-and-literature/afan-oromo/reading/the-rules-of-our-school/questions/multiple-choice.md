# The Rules of Our School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the first rule say?
a) Leave early  b) Do not be late  c) Run to school  d) Skip homework
**Answer: b) Do not be late**

**2.** At lesson time, a learner wears the uniform and brings —
a) Only a pencil  b) All learning materials  c) Food for the class  d) A ball
**Answer: b) All learning materials**

**3.** Which rule protects safety?
a) Do classwork  b) Do not fight or throw stones  c) Follow other rules  d) Wear the uniform
**Answer: b) Do not fight or throw stones** — with staying inside the compound

**4.** School property must be —
a) Sold  b) Hidden  c) Cared for  d) Broken
**Answer: c) Cared for**
