# The Rules of Our School — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** A learner may leave the school compound before lessons end.
**Answer: False** — the rules say the opposite

**2.** The rules ask learners to keep free of disturbance.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Homework is not part of the rules.
**Answer: False** — do classwork and homework is rule three
