# Video script — The Rules of Our School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The rules of our school. Read them like law.
Do not be late. Wear the uniform. Bring your materials.
Stay inside. Care for the school's things. Do not fight.
Obey your teachers. A rule understood is a rule half kept.
