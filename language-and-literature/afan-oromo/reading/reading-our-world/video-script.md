# Video script — Reading Our World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before you read — guess. While you read — gather. After you read — answer.
Water: rain and springs. Lakes: **Xaanaa. Abijaataa. Hara Danbal.**
The rainbow — **sabbata waaqaa** — wears the colour words you know.
A hero's life: scan it. Places, years, facts — your eyes hunt them.
Read the world. It is all one book.
