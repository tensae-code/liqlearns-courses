# Hands-on — Reading Our World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rainbow after rain

Next time rain ends and the sun returns, look for sabbata waaqaa. If it stands, name its colours in Afaan Oromoo aloud, top band to bottom. If it does not come, draw it from the picture and say the colours anyway.

## One free good

Volunteering is answered with a life: do one free good this week — carry water for a neighbour, sweep a shared path, help a younger child read — and say afterwards, in Afaan Oromoo if you can: tola ooltummaa.
A memory circle (sub lesson 7, G7 supplement): in a circle, each learner answers one childhood question in the first person — "When I was small, I…" — as the remembered reading's narrator answers hers; the rope-family words (hidhaa, wadaroo, funyoo, tushaa) are mimed and named as the circle warms up.
