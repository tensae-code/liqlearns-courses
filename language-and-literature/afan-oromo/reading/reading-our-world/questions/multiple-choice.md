# Reading Our World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water is found in rain and in —
a) The moon  b) Springs rising from the ground  c) Books  d) Clouds only
**Answer: b) Springs rising from the ground**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The rainbow in Afaan Oromoo is —
a) sabbata waaqaa  b) tola ooltummaa  c) amaleeffannaa  d) bidiruu
**Answer: a) sabbata waaqaa**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Volunteering — tola ooltummaa — means —
a) Working only for pay  b) Doing good freely, with no pay asked  c) Travelling abroad  d) Studying at night
**Answer: b) Doing good freely, with no pay asked**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Scanning a life story means —
a) Reading every word slowly  b) Hunting single facts — places, years, ages  c) Skipping the story  d) Guessing the hero's name
**Answer: b) Hunting single facts — places, years, ages** — the hero is never guessed

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Amaleeffannaa means —
a) Habit-forming  b) A kind of boat  c) A lake  d) A colour
**Answer: a) Habit-forming** — learned from its context in the reading

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In the work reading, the reader names the reading's words and —
a) Sings them  b) Answers questions from the reading  c) Forgets them  d) Draws a boat
**Answer: b) Answers questions from the reading**
