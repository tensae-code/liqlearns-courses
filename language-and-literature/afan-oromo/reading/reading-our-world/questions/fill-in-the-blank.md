# Reading Our World — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or sound.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The hippo in the water reading is ____.
**Answer: roobii**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Diimaa, keelloo, magariisa, cuquliisa — these are ____ words.
**Answer: colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** The work reading is called dubbisa ____.
**Answer: hojii**

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** The play at the heart of the remembered childhood is called ______ in Afaan Oromoo.
**Answer: tapha** — the games of the village yard.
