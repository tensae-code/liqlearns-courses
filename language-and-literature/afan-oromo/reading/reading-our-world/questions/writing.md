# Reading Our World — writing

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write three uses of water from the reading.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the hero's facts you scanned: the places, the years, the age.

**3. (Sub lesson 7)** Write four first-person sentences remembering your own childhood play — "When I was small, I…" — answering as the reading's narrator answers.
**Answer guidance: first person throughout; the play named and placed (where, with whom); the guide's key answers the reading this way (printed pp. 134–135).**
