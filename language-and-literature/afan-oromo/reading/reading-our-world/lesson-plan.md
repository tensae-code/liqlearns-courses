# Lesson plan — Reading Our World

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Reading · **Lesson:** Reading Our World
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Reading (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** reading about the world — water and its lakes, the rainbow, volunteering, a hero's life found by scanning, the harms of addiction, and work — each reading met before, during and after, the mother-tongue guide's own way.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Reading Everyday Sentences: the learner reads sentences and a short reading; now longer readings are worked the Grade 7 way — predict before, work during, respond after.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Water — sources, uses and lakes** — the G7 Ch. 5 reading by its answer keys: where water is found, what it serves, the great lakes, water animals and plants, and the boats.
2. **The rainbow and its colours** — the G4 Ch. 5 rainbow reading: its colours listed; learners draw the rainbow.
3. **What volunteering is** — the G7 Ch. 3 reading: Henerii Duunaa (Henry Dunant) named, and the meaning of volunteering (tola ooltummaa) answered from the text.
4. **A patriot hero's life — scanning for facts** — the G7 Ch. 9 biography read for specific information (scanning): Wallagga and Najjoo, Hoolataa Gannatii, 1912, Captain Juuliyoo, 1970, fighting at 18 — the hero unnamed in the key and unnamed here.
5. **Addiction and its harms** — the G7 Ch. 8 reading Araada: the main idea reported orally; its words explained from context (amaleeffannaa — habit-forming; dhugaatii nama macheessu — an intoxicating drink).
6. **Reading — work** — the G4 Ch. 8 reading about work (dubbisa hojii): its words named and questions answered.
7. **A childhood remembered** — the Grade 7 oral-poetry chapter's reading, evidenced by its answer keys: its words worked before reading (dawoo; muka/cufaa; hidhaa/wadaroo/funyoo/tushaa; qaabachuu), then the reading followed by its answers — a famous girl's childhood, her beautiful teeth, the play (tapha) — answered in the first person; the reading's text is not printed in the guide and is not reconstructed here (G7 Ch. 10 keys; G7 supplement, built 2026-11-12 from the completed re-read).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Reading Everyday Sentences — sentences and true/false, the tools this lesson uses on longer readings. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Work any reading the three-stage way: before (predict), during (gather), after (respond).
2. Name water's sources, uses, the three great lakes, and the boats bidiruu, doonii, yabala.
3. List the rainbow's colours and say its name: sabbata waaqaa.
4. Say what volunteering (tola ooltummaa) is and who Henerii Duunaa is in the reading.
5. Scan a life story for single facts — places, years, an age.
6. Report the main idea of the addiction reading and explain amaleeffannaa from context.
7. Work a reading that reaches you by its keys alone: settle its words beforehand, follow its answers, and answer in the first person, as its narrator answers.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Coloured pencils if you draw the rainbow.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner predicts what a reading about water will say before meeting its facts.
2. **The explanation** — seven readings worked the before/during/after way: water, the rainbow, volunteering, a hero scanned for facts, addiction, work, and (the supplement) a childhood remembered — the last evidenced by its keys alone (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9888]: a picture of a lake with a small boat on it and a hippo at its shore, trees behind — the student names the lake things in Afaan Oromoo — qurxummii, naacha, roobii — and the boats bidiruu, doonii, yabala.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9903]: a picture of a rainbow's bands appearing one by one over green fields, each band filling from the same foot of the bow — the student names each colour in Afaan Oromoo as its band appears.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9862]: a picture of a rainbow over fields with its bands labelled in the colour words diimaa, keelloo, magariisa, cuquliisa — the student reads the labels and lists the rainbow's colours from the picture.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9889]: a picture of flat figures of volunteers helping at a community event — carrying, sweeping, serving food — the student points to one free good in the picture and says who it helps.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9890]: a picture of a young patriot in traditional dress beside fact cards: Wallagga, Najjoo, 1912, 1970, 18 — the student scans the cards and reads out four facts without reading anything else.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9891]: a picture of a round sign: a cigarette and a bottle, each under a red bar — no smoking, no drinking — the student says what the sign forbids and one harm addiction brings.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9895]: a picture of a strip of people at different kinds of work — a farmer, a builder, a teacher, a trader — the student names each kind of work and one thing it gives other people.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the water facts slowly — sources, uses, lakes — the student ticks each fact in the air as it is heard, then repeats the list.
3. **Say it to your companion** — work one reading the three-stage way together: predict, gather, respond.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, true or false, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — scan the hero's facts, list water's uses from memory, and report the addiction reading's main idea aloud.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
