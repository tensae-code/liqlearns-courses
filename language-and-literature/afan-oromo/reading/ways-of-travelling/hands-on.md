# Hands-on — Ways of Travelling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ways I have travelled

Think of every way you have ever travelled — on foot, by bus, by boat if you have been on water. Say which road each used: dachii (land), bishaan (water) or samii (sky). The aeroplane in the sky is the one most learners have only read about — the sentence still stands.

## Paper boat, paper plane

Fold a paper boat and a paper plane. Float the boat on a bowl of water — bishaan irra deema. Throw the plane gently — samii irra balali'a. Say each sentence as its traveller moves.
