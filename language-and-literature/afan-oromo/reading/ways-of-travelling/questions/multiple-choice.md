# Ways of Travelling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The boat travels —
a) On land  b) On water  c) In the sky  d) Under the ground
**Answer: b) On water** — Dooniin bishaan irra deema

**2.** Which traveller flies?
a) doonii  b) baabura  c) xiyyaara  d) konkolaataa
**Answer: c) xiyyaara** — balali'a — it flies

**3.** 'Baaburri dachii irra deema' — the train travels on —
a) bishaan (water)  b) samii (sky)  c) dachii (land)  d) a bridge
**Answer: c) dachii (land)**
