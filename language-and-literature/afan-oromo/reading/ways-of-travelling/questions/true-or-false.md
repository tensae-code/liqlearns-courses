# Ways of Travelling — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Dooniin samii irra balali'a — the boat flies in the sky.
**Answer: False** — the boat travels on water; the aeroplane flies

**2.** Baaburri dachii irra deema — the train travels on land.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Xiyyaarri bishaan irra deema — the aeroplane travels on water.
**Answer: False**
