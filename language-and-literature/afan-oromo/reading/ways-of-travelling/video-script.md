# Video script — Ways of Travelling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three travellers. Three roads.
**Dooniin bishaan irra deema.** The boat — on the water.
**Baaburri dachii irra deema.** The train — on the land.
**Xiyyaarri samii irra balali'a.** The aeroplane — it flies in the sky!
Boat, water. Train, land. Plane, sky. Say them with me.
