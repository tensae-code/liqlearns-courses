# Lesson plan — Ways of Travelling

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Reading · **Lesson:** Ways of Travelling
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Reading (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the three ways of travelling and where each one goes: the boat on water, the train on land, the aeroplane in the sky.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Words by Their Sounds and the everyday sentences: short reading sentences, answered true or false.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Words by Their Sounds; Reading Everyday Sentences. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read the three travelling sentences in Afaan Oromoo.
2. Match doonii (boat) to water, baabura (train) to land, xiyyaara (aeroplane) to sky.
3. Answer true-or-false questions from the three sentences.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner matches boat, train and aeroplane to water, land and sky before reading.
2. **The explanation** — the transport reading's three sentences, one for each way of travelling, with true/false answers (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9863]: a picture of a strip of three: a boat on blue water, a train on rails across land, an aeroplane in the sky with clouds — the student reads each sentence and points to its traveller.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9907]: a picture of three panels in one frame: a boat crossing water, a train rolling on rails, an aeroplane flying through sky — each moving in its own medium — the student names each traveller and its road as it moves.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the three travelling sentences slowly — the student repeats each sentence and says where that traveller goes.
3. **Say it to your companion** — one names a traveller; the other answers with its full sentence.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, true or false, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write the three sentences and answer three true/false judgements.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
