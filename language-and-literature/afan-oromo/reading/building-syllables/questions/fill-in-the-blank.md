# Building Syllables — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or sound.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** ____ Te Ti To Tu: the chart row's first syllable is ____.
**Answer: Ta**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** La + ____ = Lafa: the missing syllable is ____.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** A-dur-ree: Adurree has ____ syllables.
