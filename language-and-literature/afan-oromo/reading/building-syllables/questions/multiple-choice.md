# Building Syllables — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A syllable is —
a) A whole sentence  b) One beat of a word — a consonant joined to a vowel  c) Any two letters  d) The last letter of a word
**Answer: b) One beat of a word — a consonant joined to a vowel**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Read the chart: the long row for T is —
a) Ta Te Ti To Tu  b) Taa Tee Tii Too Tuu  c) Tsa Tse Tsi Tso Tsu  d) Wa We Wi Wo Wu
**Answer: b) Taa Tee Tii Too Tuu** — the vowels doubled and held

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Mala splits into —
a) m-al-a  b) ma-la  c) mal-a  d) malaa
**Answer: b) ma-la** — two beats

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Na + ma joins into —
a) nama  b) mana  c) naan  d) namaa
**Answer: a) nama** — a person

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** How many syllables stand in Bulbulaa?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four  d) Five
**Answer: b) Three** — Bul-bu-laa
