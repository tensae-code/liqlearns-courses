# Video script — Building Syllables

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One consonant. One vowel. They join. **Ca!** A syllable.
Short row: **Ta. Te. Ti. To. Tu.** Long row: **Taa. Tee. Tii. Too. Tuu.**
Split it: **ma-la.** Join it: **mala.**
Count the beats: **Ga-moo — two! Bul-bu-laa — three!**
Every word is beats in a row. Now you can build them all.
