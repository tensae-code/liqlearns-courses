# Hands-on — Building Syllables

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Block building

Cut paper into small blocks. Write consonants on some and vowels on others. Build syllables on the table — push a consonant block against a vowel block and read what stands there. Build a whole chart row for T.

## Clap the word

Say words from your home — mana, buna, saree — and clap once per beat. Write each word with dashes where your claps fell: ma-na, bu-na, sa-ree.
