# Video script — Words by Their Sounds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Short word. Long word. Listen: **lafa — laafaa.**
Words in columns: four letters, five letters, six letters. Count them!
A word with a hole: **A_a…** The sound comes home: **Ala!**
Read the words whole. Mend the broken ones. They are yours.
