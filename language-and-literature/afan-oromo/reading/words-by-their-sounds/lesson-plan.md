# Lesson plan — Words by Their Sounds

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Reading · **Lesson:** Words by Their Sounds
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Reading (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** reading words by their sounds — short words against long words, words sorted by how many letters they carry, and broken words mended with their missing sound.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Building Syllables: the learner builds and counts syllables; now whole words are read, sorted and mended by sound.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Words with short and long vowels** — the book's word sets read whole: lama, muka, lafa, fala, jala, bofa, mana, buna, kuma; then eelee, lukaa, hoolaa, gaala; and the food-chapter pairs laga/laagaa, lafa/laafaa, mala/maala, bona/boonaa, gara/gaara.
2. **Words by their letter count** — words of 4, 5 and 6 letters built and sorted (G4 Ch. 7 answer key).
3. **Completing words with the missing sound** — A_a→Ala, Baal_→Baala, and the answer set Aduu, Buna, Harka, Quba, Lama — the missing sound restored.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Building Syllables. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read the short word set whole: lama, muka, lafa, fala, jala, bofa, mana, buna, kuma.
2. Tell short words from long words in the pairs laga/laagaa, lafa/laafaa, mala/maala, bona/boonaa, gara/gaara.
3. Sort words into 4-letter, 5-letter and 6-letter columns.
4. Complete a word with its missing sound: A_a → Ala, Baal_ → Baala.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner reads lafa against laafaa and mends A_a.
2. **The explanation** — the short and long word sets read whole; words sorted by letter count; gapped words completed with their missing sound (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9858]: a picture of a coffee cup with the word buna under it beside a house with the word mana under it — the student reads the two labels, then the whole short word set aloud.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9861]: a picture of three columns of word cards sorted by letter count — 4 letters, 5 letters, 6 letters — a hill card labelled tulluu and an orange labelled loomii among them — the student counts the letters of two words in each column aloud.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9902]: a picture of the word A_a with an empty glowing gap; the letter l slides in and the word stands whole as Ala — the student says the word before the letter lands, then reads Ala whole.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9859]: a picture of a hand with the word harka under it beside one raised finger with the word quba under it — the student names the hand and the finger in Afaan Oromoo and spells each word.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the pair row — laga/laagaa, lafa/laafaa, mala/maala — the student says 'short' or 'long' for the second word of each pair.
3. **Say it to your companion** — read the pairs to each other; then gap a word and let your companion mend it.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — read both sets, sort six words by letter count, and mend A_a and Baal_.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
