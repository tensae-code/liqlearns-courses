# Words by Their Sounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is the long partner of lafa?
a) lafaa  b) laafaa  c) laffa  d) lafi
**Answer: b) laafaa** — the aa is doubled and held

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the pair bona / boonaa, the long word is —
a) bona  b) boonaa  c) both are long  d) neither
**Answer: b) boonaa**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which word carries 6 letters?
a) lama  b) gamoo  c) tulluu  d) banaa
**Answer: c) tulluu** — t-u-l-l-u-u

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Loomii (an orange) stands in the —
a) 4-letter column  b) 5-letter column  c) 6-letter column  d) 3-letter column
**Answer: c) 6-letter column** — l-o-o-m-i-i

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A_a becomes Ala. The missing sound is —
a) a  b) l  c) m  d) s
**Answer: b) l**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Baal_ becomes —
a) Baala  b) Baalu  c) Baale  d) Baali
**Answer: a) Baala** — the missing sound is a
