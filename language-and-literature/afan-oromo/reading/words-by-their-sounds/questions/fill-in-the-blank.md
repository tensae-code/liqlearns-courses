# Words by Their Sounds — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or sound.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Laga is short; its long partner is ____.
**Answer: laagaa**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saree has ____ letters.
**Answer: 5 (five) — s-a-r-e-e**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Qu__a: the mended finger word is ____.
**Answer: Quba**
