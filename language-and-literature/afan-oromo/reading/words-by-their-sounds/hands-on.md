# Hands-on — Words by Their Sounds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pair cards

Write the five pairs on cards, one word per card. Shuffle, then lay each pair side by side, short word left, long word right. Read down the pairs holding the long sounds.

## Gap words at home

Write three words you know — mana, buna, lama — each with one sound replaced by a line. Give the page to someone at home to mend, or mend it yourself tomorrow morning from memory.
