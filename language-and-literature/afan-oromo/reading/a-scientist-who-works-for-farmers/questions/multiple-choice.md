# A Scientist Who Works for Farmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The college in the key is —
a) Jimma College of Agriculture and Technology  b) A law college in Finfinnee  c) A medical school  d) A teachers' college only
**Answer: a) Jimma College of Agriculture and Technology**

**2.** Mishingaa is —
a) Coffee  b) Sorghum  c) Teff  d) A river
**Answer: b) Sorghum**

**3.** The scientist's work stands at —
a) A market  b) An agricultural research centre  c) A port  d) A mine
**Answer: b) An agricultural research centre**

**4.** The key carries a reference to —
a) The Nobel name  b) A football cup  c) A railway  d) A dam
**Answer: a) The Nobel name**
