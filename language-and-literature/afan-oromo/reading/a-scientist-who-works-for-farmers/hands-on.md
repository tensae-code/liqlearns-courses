# Hands-on — A Scientist Who Works for Farmers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Know your sorghum

Find mishingaa (sorghum) if it grows or is sold near you — a stalk, a grain basket at market, flour at home. Look at the head of grains the scientist in the picture holds. Sorghum feeds people and animals and stands through dry weather: that is why a research centre works on it.

## Fact hunt at home

Ask an elder at home: who is a person from your area who studied and now serves farmers or the land? Gather five facts only — places, school, work — and write them as a fact list, the way this lesson reads a life.
