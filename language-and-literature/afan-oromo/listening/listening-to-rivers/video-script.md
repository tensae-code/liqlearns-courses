# Video script — Listening to Rivers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Listen — a great river!

**Abbayyaa!** It makes electricity! It waters farms!
People play and travel on it! Water falling — beautiful!
Its helpers: Daabbus! Dhidheessaa! Fincaa'aa! Gudar! Mogor! Jamaa!

And **Hawaas** — the Awash! From Ginchi, far, far —
and it never leaves home! Sugarcane! Falls! Parks!

Two rivers. Listen well!
