# Listening to Rivers — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Abbayyaa's benefits in the text are —
a) electricity, irrigation, recreation, transport  b) roads, schools, markets  c) rain, snow, wind
**Answer: a) electricity, irrigation, recreation, transport.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A country, says the text, has the recognised right to use —
a) other countries' rivers  b) its own soil, water and minerals  c) nothing
**Answer: b) its own soil, water and minerals** — to improve its people's lives.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a tributary of the Abbayyaa, per the gap-fill answers?
a) Jamaa  b) Awash  c) Xaanaa
**Answer: a) Jamaa** — with Daabbus, Dhidheessaa, Fincaa'aa, Gudar and Mogor.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Awash rises at —
a) Bekoji  b) Ginchi  c) Haramaayaa
**Answer: b) Ginchi** — 90 km west of Finfinnee.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Awash is about how long?
a) 120 km  b) 1,200 km  c) 12,000 km
**Answer: b) 1,200 km** — the second longest of the country's rivers.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Awash's strange honour is that it —
a) is the widest  b) never leaves its own country  c) flows uphill
**Answer: b) never leaves its own country.**
