# Listening to Rivers — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Abbayyaa's scenery — falls, trees, gorge — is part of what the text admires.
**True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Awash flows out of Ethiopia into another country.
**False** — it never leaves its own country; it spends its soil and water on its own people.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Awash is the longest of the country's rivers.
**False** — it is the second longest, about 1,200 km.
