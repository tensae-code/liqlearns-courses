# Listening to Rivers — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three stages of the guide's listening frame.
**Answer:** before (duraa — predict), during (yeroo — work while listening), after (boodaa — respond).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the Abbayyaa's six tributaries.
**Answer:** Daabbus, Dhidheessaa, Fincaa'aa, Gudar, Mogor, Jamaa.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What must citizens do with the Awash, per the text?
**Answer:** together protect it, admire it, use it — and teach those who do not understand it.
