# The Kinds of Afwalaloo — hands-on

Served by this build: the lesson's picture strip (12513) — one panel per kind — is the sorting board for the whole lesson.

- **Name that occasion (sub lessons 1–3).** The teacher describes an occasion without naming the kind — a wedding dance, a baby's cradle, a hero coming home, a blessing at a journey's start, a muttered grievance. Learners hold up the matching panel of the strip and say the kind's name.
- **Sort the strip (sub lesson 2).** In pairs, learners re-tell the strip from loudest to quietest and defend their order: where does the blessing sit? Where the murmured complaint?
- **One kind each (sub lesson 3).** Each learner picks the kind they know best from home and tells the class: who makes it, when, and — if it can be shared — a line or its tune's shape. The class names the kind before the teller confirms it.
