# The Kinds of Afwalaloo — short answer

Write a short answer for each. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do weedduu and sirba differ in the body of the maker?
**Answer: a weedduu is sung sitting with the feeling — a song of longing; a sirba is sung AND danced — the whole body joins in.**
*Why it matters: the kinds are told apart by their occasion and their making, not by their words alone.*

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Eebba has two faces. Name both, and say against what the dark face is turned.
**Answer: eebba blesses; abaarsa curses — and the curse is turned against wrongdoing.**
*Why it matters: blessing and cursing are one spoken power with two directions.*

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the kind for each moment: (a) a baby's cradle; (b) a hero's return; (c) a wronged worker muttering alone.
**Answer: (a) hurursa daa'immanii; (b) faaruu gootaa (or geerarsa); (c) gumgummii.**
*Why it matters: naming the kind for the moment is the whole skill of this lesson.*
