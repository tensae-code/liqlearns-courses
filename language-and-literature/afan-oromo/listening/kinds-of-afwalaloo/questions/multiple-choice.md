# The Kinds of Afwalaloo — multiple choice

Answer each question. Try them in any order you like — the number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A *weedduu* is —
a) a song of longing and love
b) a blessing spoken over a child
c) a chant for battle
d) a lullaby for a baby
**Answer: a) a song of longing and love** — the heart reaching for someone far away.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** *Sirba* is the kind that is —
a) only whispered, never sung
b) sung and danced
c) made only at funerals
d) written on walls
**Answer: b) sung and danced** — the body joins the voice.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The kind spoken as a blessing over a person is —
a) gumgummii
b) eebba
c) weedduu
d) hurursa
**Answer: b) eebba** — good spoken over a person's road, by an elder's mouth.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A mother singing her baby down into sleep is singing a —
a) geerarsa
b) faaruu gootaa
c) hurursa daa'immanii
d) sirba
**Answer: c) hurursa daa'immanii** — the lullaby, the family's gentlest kind.
