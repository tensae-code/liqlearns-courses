# The Kinds of Afwalaloo — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the right word. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The dark other face of eebba — a curse instead of a blessing — is ______.
**Answer: abaarsa** — the same spoken power turned against wrong.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The heroic chant that stirs courage and names great deeds is ______.
**Answer: geerarsa** — sung for heroes and their victories.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A complaint murmured low, where only the heart hears it, is a ______.
**Answer: gumgummii** — poetry made alone, out of grievance.
