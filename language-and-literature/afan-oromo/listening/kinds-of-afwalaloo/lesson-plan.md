# The Kinds of Afwalaloo

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Listening · **Lesson:** The Kinds of Afwalaloo
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Listening (CURRICULUM.md).

**What this lesson teaches:** the Grade 7 guide's own walk through the kinds of oral poetry, as its Chapter 10 inventory names them: *weedduu* (the song of longing), *sirba* (song-and-dance), *eebba* (blessing — with *abaarsa*, cursing, as its other face), *geerarsa* (the heroic chant), *faaruu gootaa* (praise of a hero), *gumgummii* (the murmured complaint) and *hurursa daa'immanii* (the lullaby). Each kind is taught by its occasion: where you would hear it, and what it is for. It follows Afwalaloo — Oral Poetry, which named the family and its features.

**What the learner will be able to do:**
- Name the seven kinds the guide names.
- Say where each kind is heard and what it does.
- Hear a kind and name it, or name the kind a moment asks for.

## Sub lessons
1. Songs of the heart — one list, in this order, in `lesson/lesson.md`; each opens by proving the one before, by the 2026-11-08 rule stated there.
2. Blessing and heroes
3. The quiet kinds

**Gate pool:** Sub lesson 2's prove-it check is drawn from sub lesson 1's whole question set (mixed with earlier parts of this lesson), and sub lesson 3's check from the whole set before it — every check at most 5 questions, picked from the whole set, never a fixed list.

**How the lesson runs:** By occasions, loud to quiet: the singing and dancing kinds first, then the gathering kinds (blessing, heroes), then the kinds one person makes alone (the murmured complaint, the lullaby). The picture strip carries one panel per kind.

**Level of the language:** Full Afaan Oromoo sentences, with each kind's name glossed in English as it comes.
