# The Kinds of Afwalaloo — levels

This lesson is a step: doing it fully is what "knowing it" means. Depth, in learning order — the sub lessons of this lesson, which are its levels:

- **Sub lesson 1 (the base level) — Songs of the heart.** *Weedduu* — the song of longing and love; *sirba* — the kind that is sung and danced.
- **Sub lesson 2 — Blessing and heroes.** *Eebba* — blessing spoken over a person, with *abaarsa* (cursing) as its other face; *geerarsa* — the heroic chant; *faaruu gootaa* — praise sung for a hero.
- **Sub lesson 3 (the top level) — The quiet kinds.** *Gumgummii* — the complaint murmured low; *hurursa daa'immanii* — the lullaby that rocks a baby to sleep.

A learner "has" this lesson when all three are proved: given any occasion — a cradle, a gathering, a hero's return, a lonely grievance — they can name the kind that belongs to it.
