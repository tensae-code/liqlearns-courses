# Farruu Qotiyyoo — hands-on

Served by this build: the lesson's picture (12508) shows the farmer and his oxen at the plough — the song's whole world in one frame — and the GIF (12509) walks the team across the field, the furrow lengthening behind the share. Do them in the lesson's order.

- **Riddle warm-up (sub lesson 1).** The guide's two pre-listening riddles are read aloud; the class guesses in pairs before any answer is given. The song's hard words are then chorused: *uffata — kafana — wayyaa; dhiisuu; fiixee gaanfaa; qonnaan bulaa*.
- **Listen and catch (sub lesson 2).** The teacher sings (or reads) the song from the guide while the class listens with pencils down the first time — refrain only, joined aloud — then again with the gap-words to catch: furdaa, miiccaa, lafee. The picture (12508) is pointed at as each thing is sung: the oxen, the harness, the furrow, the farmer.
- **Answer back (sub lesson 3).** In groups, learners answer the song with reasons (why is the ox praised? what are its clothes?) and then each group speaks two praise-lines of its own for a working animal they know — the class answers with the refrain.
