# Farruu Qotiyyoo — the praise song of the plough-ox

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Listening · **Lesson:** Farruu Qotiyyoo
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Listening (CURRICULUM.md).

**What this lesson teaches:** the Grade 7 guide's Chapter 10 listening lesson, whole: the class prepares with the guide's own word-riddles, hears the praise song of the plough-ox (*Walaloo Farruu Qotiyyoo*) with its listening tasks, and answers it afterwards with reasons. The song is carried as the read recorded it — a summary with its teaching points; the song's own full text stays in the guide, where the teacher sings it from. This lesson opens the guide's oral-poetry chapter and stands before Afwalaloo — Oral Poetry, where the kind this song belongs to is named.

**What the learner will be able to do:**
- Work out the song's hard words from the guide's riddles before listening.
- Follow the song: catch its missing words, join its refrain, and say what it praises.
- Answer the song afterwards, giving reasons from what they heard.

## Sub lessons
1. The words before the song — one list, in this order, in `lesson/lesson.md`; each opens by proving the one before, by the 2026-11-08 rule stated there.
2. The praise song heard
3. Answering the song

**Gate pool:** Sub lesson 2's prove-it check is drawn from sub lesson 1's whole question set (mixed with earlier parts of this lesson), and sub lesson 3's check from the whole set before it — every check at most 5 questions, picked from the whole set, never a fixed list.

**How the lesson runs:** Before → during → after, the guide's own frame for every listening lesson: riddles first, the song with ears open for the missing words and the refrain, then answers with reasons. The video script is a performance script — the praise spoken aloud, the class joining the refrain.

**Level of the language:** Full Afaan Oromoo sentences, with the song's hard words glossed in English as they come.
