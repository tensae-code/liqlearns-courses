# Farruu Qotiyyoo — levels

This lesson is a step: doing it fully is what "knowing it" means. Depth, in learning order — the sub lessons of this lesson, which are its levels:

- **Sub lesson 1 (the base level) — The words before the song.** The guide's pre-listening riddles worked out, and the song's hard words ready: *uffata/kafana/wayyaa*, *dhiisuu*, *fiixee gaanfaa*, *qonnaan bulaa*.
- **Sub lesson 2 — The praise song heard.** The song followed whole: its praise understood, its missing words caught, its refrain (*Ushururuu*) joined.
- **Sub lesson 3 (the top level) — Answering the song.** Questions on the song answered with reasons, and praise-lines of the learner's own spoken in the song's spirit.

A learner "has" this lesson when all three are proved: they can hear this song — or the next praise song — prepared, follow it, and answer it.
