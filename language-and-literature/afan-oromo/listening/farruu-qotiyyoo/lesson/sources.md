# Farruu Qotiyyoo — sources

**Course:** Afan Oromo — as named in README.md, Unit 7 (Listening).

This course is built ONLY from the Afan Oromo teacher guides in hand — no pupil book was in hand for this course. Everything in this lesson traces to the book below: what the guide itself teaches is stocked; nothing is invented, and no other book or outside material is used. Placements were written during the 2026-11-12 Ethiopia Wave 2 read (the Grade 7 file completed by re-read the same day), never reconstructed afterwards.

**Book 1:** *Barnoota Afaan Oromoo, Qajeelcha Barsiisaa Kutaa 7ffaa* (teacher guide — evidence; Afaan Oromoo as the learners' mother tongue) — Ethiopia, Addis Ababa (Finfinnee) City Administration Education Bureau, 2014 E.C. / 2022, printed pp. 1–170. Page rule: printed page = PDF page − 3. Read in full, page by page, in the 2026-11-12 Ethiopia Wave 2 read. Placements at liqlearns-courses-work/ethiopia-2026-11-11/afan-oromo/g07-teacher-placements.md.

**How the lesson maps to the book.** Chapter 10 of Afaan Oromoo's Grade 7 guide — the oral-poetry chapter — opens with this listening lesson, run in the guide's own before/during/after frame. This lesson carries that frame whole:

| Lesson part | Source |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — The words before the song | Printed p. 132 — the pre-listening word-riddles (*walitti hidhata lafarraa*; the branch-of-afoola riddle); the song's vocabulary prepared |
| Sub lesson 2 — The praise song heard | Printed pp. 133–134 — the listening text *Walaloo Farruu Qotiyyoo* (the guide prints the full song for the teacher) and its during-listening keys: *uffata* (with its synonyms *kafana*, *wayyaa*), *dhiisuu*, *fiixee gaanfaa*, *qonnaan bulaa*; the gap-words (*furdaa*, *miiccaa*, *lafee*…); the refrain *Ushururuu* |
| Sub lesson 3 — Answering the song | Printed p. 134 — the after-listening answers: the song answered with reasons |
| Picture 12508 + GIF 12509 | Drawn for LiqLearns for this lesson — the farmer ploughing a straight furrow behind his oxen; the ox team ploughing, the furrow extending |

**The song itself is not reproduced in this lesson.** The guide prints its full text (printed p. 133) for the teacher to sing from, and the read recorded it as a summary with its teaching points — this lesson stocks the song exactly so: its summary, its words, its listening tasks and its refrain. Nothing of the song's own lines is quoted here beyond the one-word refrain.

No picture from the book is reproduced in this lesson, and no book text is copied beyond the guide's own riddles, word keys and exercise material named in the table, which are the teaching content itself.
