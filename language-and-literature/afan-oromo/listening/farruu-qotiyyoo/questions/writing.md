# Farruu Qotiyyoo — writing

Write for each one. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the song in three sentences of your own: what is praised, what the ox's "clothes" are, and what the refrain is.
**Answer: any true three-sentence retelling** — the ox praised for its beauty and strength; its clothes the gear of its work; the refrain "Ushururuu."
**Why:** hearing a song whole means being able to carry it to someone who has not heard it.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write three praise-lines of your own for an animal that works for your family — its beauty, its strength, and what its work gives you — with "Ushururuu" between them, as the song does.
**Answer: the learner's own lines.** No set text — this is the lesson's doing made writing.
