# Farruu Qotiyyoo — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** *Walaloo Farruu Qotiyyoo* is a praise song for the plough-ox.
**Answer: True** — a *farruu*, sung for the ox.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The refrain "Ushururuu" is a cry of sadness and loss.
**Answer: False** — it is a cry of wonder and admiration, the listeners' joining-place in the song.
