# Farruu Qotiyyoo — multiple choice

Answer each question. Try them in any order you like — the number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the song's words, *uffata* (clothing) also goes by the names —
a) kafana and wayyaa
b) lafee and miiccaa
c) dhiisuu and dhaabuu
d) gaafa and farda
**Answer: a) kafana and wayyaa** — three words, one meaning: the song's own synonym set.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** *Dhiisuu* means the same as —
a) jalqabuu (to begin)
b) dhaabuu (to stop, to leave)
c) waamuu (to call)
d) guddisuu (to raise)
**Answer: b) dhaabuu** — the guide's own pairing for the song's word.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The cry that keeps coming back through the song — its refrain of wonder — is —
a) Ushururuu
b) Eebba
c) Weedduu
d) Sirba
**Answer: a) Ushururuu** — the listeners join on it every time it returns.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The song praises the plough-ox with feeling above all because —
a) it is the fastest animal on the farm
b) its work feeds the farmer's family
c) it can sing along with the farmer
d) it is the biggest animal in the village
**Answer: b) its work feeds the farmer's family** — the ploughing is the family's living; that is the song's reason.
