# Farruu Qotiyyoo — short answer

Write a short answer for each. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the class do BEFORE the song is heard, and why?
**Answer: it works out the song's hard words — the guide opens with word-riddles — so the ears are ready for the song's words when they come.**
*Why it matters: every listening lesson in the guide begins this way: words first, then listening.*

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Give one reason, from the song, why the farmer loves his ox.
**Answer: because the ox's ploughing feeds the family (any reason the song itself gives — its strength, its beauty in its work, the living it makes).**
*Why it matters: answering a song means answering with its own reasons.*
