# Farruu Qotiyyoo — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the right word. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The farmer in the song's words is the ______.
**Answer: qonnaan bulaa** — the one who ploughs and stays with the land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The very tip of the ox's horn is ______ gaanfaa.
**Answer: fiixee** — *fiixee gaafa sangaa*, the point of the horn.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The song dresses the ox in the ______ of its work — its clothing is its harness, not cloth.
**Answer: gear (hidhaa)** — the song's surprise: the ox's *uffata* is its work-gear.
