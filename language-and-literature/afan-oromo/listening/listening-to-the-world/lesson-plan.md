# Lesson plan — Listening to the World

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Listening · **Lesson:** Listening to the World
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Listening (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** listening to the world spoken about: a weather report (oduu haala qilleensaa) — cold (qorraa), hot (ho'aa), cloudy (duumessa) — with clothes sorted for the weather, and instructions about time, heard and carried out.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Word lessons' weather and time words. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The weather report** — listening to a weather report (oduu haala qilleensaa) and reporting the day's weather — cold (qorraa), hot (ho'aa), cloudy (duumessa) (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 8).
2. **Clothes for the weather** — clothes for cold weather vs hot weather sorted, riding with the report (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 8).
3. **Following instructions about time** — listening and carrying out instructions; talking about what you have watched and seen (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 12 — Yeroo, Time).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Word lesson Days, Months and Seasons — seasons and weather words live there; times of the day are in the same unit. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Listen to a weather report and report the day's weather: qorraa (cold), ho'aa (hot), duumessa (cloudy).
2. Sort clothes for cold weather against hot weather, from what you heard.
3. Listen to instructions about time and carry them out.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A clock for the time work. Your own clothes knowledge — what you wear when it is cold.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner listens to the teacher say today's real weather in one sentence, and must choose the right clothes from a pile before any teaching (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the weather report as the Grade 4 guide listens to it; clothes sorted by the heard weather; then the time chapter's listening-and-doing instructions, with the clock (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9926] in Sub lesson 3: clock hands sweeping round the face and stopping at a named time — 9:00, the play time — as the instruction lands — the student listens to the instruction, says the time aloud as the hands stop, and names what happens then.
   - ADD IMAGE [IMAGE 9927] in Sub lesson 2: a picture of a child in warm clothes in cold weather beside a child in light clothes in heat, a cloudy sky panel between them, the words qorraa, ho'aa and duumessa as a chart head — the student points to the panel a heard weather word names and says the report back in a full sentence.
   - ADD AUDIO in Sub lesson 1: a voice giving a weather report for three invented days, slowly — the student listens and writes the weather word of each day before checking.
3. **Say it to your companion** — give each other weather reports for invented days and dress a drawn figure for each report heard (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — clock hands moving to a named time as the instruction is spoken, in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching, short-answer, true-or-false — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — listen to one real weather word today — from family, radio or the sky — and report it at home in Afaan Oromoo (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Hears the report but reports the window → the task is reporting what was *heard*. Cover the window if you must; the ear, not the eye, is the organ being trained.
- Sorts clothes by habit, not by report → start every choice from the heard word: say qorraa aloud, then pick. The word leads; the hand follows.
- Clock hands reversed → the short hand is the hour's; drill hour-only instructions first ("at 9:00"), then add the activity.
