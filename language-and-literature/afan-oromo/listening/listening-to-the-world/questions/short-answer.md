# Listening to the World — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two listens does the weather work ask for?
**Answer:** one for the whole sky (the gist), and one again for the exact weather word.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why sort clothes from the heard word rather than from the window?
**Answer:** the ear is the organ being trained — the report, not the eye's weather, must dress the person.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the chapter the time-instruction listening rides in.
**Answer:** Yeroo — Time (Ch. 12).
