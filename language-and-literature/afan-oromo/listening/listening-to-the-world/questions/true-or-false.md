# Listening to the World — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reporting the day's weather back is the proof the listening landed.
**True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hot weather (ho'aa) calls for heavy covering clothes.
**False** — ho'aa calls for light, loose clothes; qorraa is the warm-clothes word.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After carrying out instructions, learners talk about what they watched and saw.
**True** — the chapter joins doing and telling.
