# Listening to the World — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oduu haala qilleensaa is —
a) a weather report  b) a market list  c) a song
**Answer: a) a weather report** — news of the sky's state.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Qorraa means —
a) hot  b) cloudy  c) cold
**Answer: c) cold.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Duumessa means —
a) rainy season  b) cloudy  c) windy
**Answer: b) cloudy.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The report says qorraa. Your hands reach for —
a) light loose clothes  b) heavy warm clothes  c) no clothes change
**Answer: b) heavy warm clothes.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the time chapter, listening is proved by —
a) repeating the words  b) carrying out the instructions  c) drawing the teacher
**Answer: b) carrying out the instructions.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The strictest checker of a heard time instruction is —
a) the clockface  b) a neighbour  c) the window
**Answer: a) the clockface** — wrong hearing puts the hands wrong for all to see.
