# Hands-on — Listening to the World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The dressing race
A pile of real clothes (or drawn cards) in the middle. The teacher gives a weather report for an invented place — very cold, very hot, cloudy-cool. Pairs dress a volunteer or a drawn figure from the pile, justifying each piece by the heard word: "qorraa — so the thick jacket."

## Clock hands
A big drawn clock with movable hands (a split pin through card hands). Instructions are given about times — lessons from 2:00 to 9:00, play 9:00 to 12:00, as the guide's time answers give them — and learners set the hands and name the activity. Wrong hands are visible to all: the clock is the checker.

## Report the sky
Each morning for a week, one learner is the reporter: they step outside, feel the air, look at the sky, and deliver the day's oduu haala qilleensaa to the class in one sentence. The class repeats it back — yesterday's reporter checks today's.
