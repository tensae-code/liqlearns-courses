# Video script — Listening to the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Listen! The weather report!
**Qorraa** — cold! Warm clothes!
**Ho'aa** — hot! Light clothes!
**Duumessa** — cloudy!

Now listen and DO.
The clock says — move the hands! Two o'clock! Nine o'clock!
Your ears heard it. Your hands did it!
