# Listening to History and Heroes — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At Adwa, Ethiopia defeated —
a) France  b) Italy  c) Britain
**Answer: b) Italy** — the invasion of 1896, as the guide prints the year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Adwa's deepest lesson, per the text, is —
a) one people's strength  b) national unity — many peoples and faiths together  c) better weapons
**Answer: b) national unity.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia remained —
a) a colony for a short time  b) the one African country never under European colonial rule  c) divided
**Answer: b) the one African country never under European colonial rule.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Derartu Tulu was the first African woman to —
a) run a marathon  b) win Olympic gold (1992)  c) coach a team
**Answer: b) win Olympic gold (1992).**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What interrupted her winning in 1993–94?
a) a knee injury  b) a flood  c) a lost shoe
**Answer: a) a knee injury** — she returned to win in 1995 and take a second Olympic gold in 2000.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** At 37, in 2009, she won —
a) the London marathon  b) the New York City Marathon  c) the Tokyo marathon
**Answer: b) the New York City Marathon.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In 1982, on the famine road, Abbabach Goobanaa —
a) bought a shop  b) carried home infants whose mothers had died  c) opened a school at once
**Answer: b) carried home infants whose mothers had died** — and chose the children when made to choose.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** She is called —
a) the mother of Fiche  b) the "Mother Teresa of Africa"  c) the runner of Wollo
**Answer: b) the "Mother Teresa of Africa".**
