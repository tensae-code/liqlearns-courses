# Listening to History and Heroes — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Put the events in the order they happened.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Derartu Tulu: the 2009 New York City Marathon win — the first Olympic gold — the knee injury years — the 1995 return title — birth at Bekoji — the second Olympic gold.
**Answer:** birth at Bekoji (1972) → the first Olympic gold (1992) → the knee injury years (1993–94) → the 1995 return title → the second Olympic gold (2000) → the 2009 New York City Marathon win.
