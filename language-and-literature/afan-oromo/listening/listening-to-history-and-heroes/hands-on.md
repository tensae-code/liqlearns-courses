# Hands-on — Listening to History and Heroes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Finish-line tape
Stretch a ribbon across the classroom door as a finish tape. Each learner, retelling Derartu's life in order at the front, breaks the tape at the exact sentence of the second gold — 2000 — and the class calls the year aloud. The tape is re-strung for every teller; the life must be in order before the tape may break.

## Date lines on the floor
Chalk two date lines on the floor — Derartu's and Abbabach's. Learners stand on the year being told and step forward as the life advances: 1992, 1995, 2000, 2009 — 1936, 1982, 1984. A wrong step is visible to everyone; the line corrects the telling.

## Heroes' roll
The five Adwa heroes' names are called as a roll, one by one, and the class answers each with the same response: present. A roll-call of honour — Koloneel Abdiisaa Aagaa, Jeneraal Taaddasaa Birruu, Jeneraal Waaqoo Guutuu, Koloneel Aliyyii Cirrii, Gabayyoo Gurmuu — until the names are held by all.
