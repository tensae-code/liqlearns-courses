# Video script — Listening to History and Heroes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Listen to the heroes!

**Adwa!** The people together — and the invader falls! Ethiopia free!
**Derartu Tulu!** From Bekoji — gold! Hurt — and back — gold again! Run!
**Abbabach Goobanaa!** She carried the orphans home. A mother to thousands!

Three lives. Hear them. Tell them. Remember them.
