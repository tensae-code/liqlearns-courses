# Video script — Listening to Stories and Talk

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
A hungry traveller. Beans in a field.
He eats them with the pod. He shells them. He eats the inside too!
And a voice — the owner was watching!

Two friends talk: a doctor! A teacher!
A teacher warns: do not go the dangerous way!

**Namni namaaf qoricha** — a person is medicine for a person.
Listen. Sing. Remember.
