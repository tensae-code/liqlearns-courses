# Listening to Stories and Talk — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The traveller knew the owner was watching.
**False** — she was hidden; her call reveals the watching at the end.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** In Hawaasummaa, a person can live entirely alone and needs no one.
**False** — no one can live alone; one person is a prop (dagalee) to another.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** After hearing the poem, learners prepare a similar text on Tamboo Xuuxuu (smoking tobacco).
**True** — the poem is a model for their own self-questioning text.
