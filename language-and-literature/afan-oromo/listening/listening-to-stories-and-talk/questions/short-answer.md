# Listening to Stories and Talk — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does Beektuu want to become a teacher?
**Answer:** because a country grows when everyone learns — an educated people can create work and change themselves and their country.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things that make Taliilee's brokers untrustworthy, from the dialogue.
**Answer:** any two: she has spoken to them only by phone; their promise (dollars easily earned) is the bait; she doubts them herself; the way they offer is irregular.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two of the Hawaasummaa values from the answers.
**Answer:** any two of: haqummaa (justice), amanamummaa (trustworthiness), walqixxummaa (equality), nageenya (peace).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two harms the poem's smoker lists against himself.
**Answer:** any two: money wasted; bad smell; carbon monoxide ruining lungs and dirtying blood; teeth like roasted maize; no pleasure in food; coughing stealing sleep; burnt lips; eyes blinded.
