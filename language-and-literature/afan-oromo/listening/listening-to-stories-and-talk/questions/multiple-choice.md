# Listening to Stories and Talk — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The traveller eats the beans first —
a) shelled  b) with the pod  c) cooked
**Answer: b) with the pod** — then shelled, then the inner part.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who watches the traveller the whole time?
a) a hunter  b) the woman who owns the beans, hidden  c) nobody
**Answer: b) the woman who owns the beans, hidden.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Beekaa wants to become a doctor to —
a) travel  b) find a cure for Covid-19 and keep the people's health  c) teach
**Answer: b) find a cure for Covid-19 and keep the people's health** — teaching is Beektuu's wish.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bilisee's answer to Taliilee's plan ends with —
a) a punishment  b) "we will arrange things for you here"  c) silence
**Answer: b) "we will arrange things for you here"** — a warning and a promise together.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Namni namaaf qoricha means —
a) a person is medicine for a person  b) a person is a stranger to a person  c) a person eats alone
**Answer: a) a person is medicine for a person.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Per the text, spacing births by planning is —
a) a burden  b) hope for children's growth, health and life  c) forbidden
**Answer: b) hope for children's growth, health and life.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The poem's speaker is —
a) a doctor warning patients  b) a smoker questioning himself  c) a teacher
**Answer: b) a smoker questioning himself** — "why do I smoke tobacco?"

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In the song, houses are —
a) taxes  b) the pillar of life — shelter from cold and heat  c) markets
**Answer: b) the pillar of life — shelter from cold and heat.**
