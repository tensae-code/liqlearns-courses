Honesty: there is no Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' contents, objectives, syllabus tables and their full answer keys (the guides print the answers to the student book's exercises, which is the evidence of what learners do). Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented. Framing split, kept visible inside the sub lessons: the story, the careers dialogue and the song are Grade 4 second-language guide evidence; the migration dialogue, the two living-together texts and the poem are Grade 7 mother-tongue guide evidence. Every full text named here is printed in its guide because the teacher reads it aloud; all are taught as summaries only, never transcribed.
