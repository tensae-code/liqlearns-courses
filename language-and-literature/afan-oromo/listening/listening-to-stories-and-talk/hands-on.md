# Hands-on — Listening to Stories and Talk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the story
The traveller's story is heard; its pictures lie shuffled on the floor. Learners match and order them — field, pod, shelling, inner part, the hidden watcher — as the story is heard a second time, then one teller retells walking along the line.

## Dialogue in chairs
Two chairs: Beektuu and Beekaa's talk is played in pairs, then Taliilee and Bilisee's. The class listens for the turn: in the first, the wishes; in the second, the promise — "we will arrange things for you here." Actors swap chairs and play the other dialogue.

## Medicine for a person
The class collects true small cases of namni namaaf qoricha from their own streets — a carried load, a visited sick neighbour, a quarrel reconciled — and tells them in a circle, each ending with the saying. The text's values get faces.
