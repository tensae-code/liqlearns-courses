LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the ear's full range: first the story, then the two dialogues, then the two texts of living together, then the poem and the song.
