# Afwalaloo — multiple choice

Answer each question. Try them in any order you like — the number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Afwalaloo is —
a) poetry that lives only in printed books
b) poetry presented by mouth — a branch of afoola
c) a kind of dance step
d) a story told without words
**Answer: b) poetry presented by mouth — a branch of afoola** — the guide's own definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** *Qusannaa jechootaa* means —
a) many words carrying little meaning
b) economy of words — few words, heavy with meaning
c) words arranged in alphabetical order
d) words spoken in a whisper
**Answer: b) economy of words** — the poem travels light because memory carries it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The feature that makes the ends of lines close with the same sound is —
a) yeedaloo
b) walqixxummaa
c) rukuttaa manaa
d) filannoo jechootaa
**Answer: c) rukuttaa manaa** — end-rhyme: the ear hears each line land.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Lines gathered together into one group make a —
a) bo'oo
b) buufata
c) yeedaloo
d) afoola
**Answer: b) buufata** — a stanza; a single line is a bo'oo.
