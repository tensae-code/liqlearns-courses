# Afwalaloo — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the right word. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Everything passed from mouth to mouth is ______; its poetry branch is afwalaloo.
**Answer: afoola** — the heritage carried in voices.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The melody that carries an oral poem is its ______.
**Answer: yeedaloo** — one of the poem's two handles, with the words.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Making pictures in the listener's mind is ______.
**Answer: suur-sammuu uumuu** — imagery: a picture for the mind.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** One single line of a poem is a ______.
**Answer: bo'oo** — and lines gathered make a buufata.
