# Afwalaloo — levels

This lesson is a step: doing it fully is what "knowing it" means. Depth, in learning order — the sub lessons of this lesson, which are its levels:

- **Sub lesson 1 (the base level) — Poetry by mouth.** Afwalaloo defined: poetry presented by mouth (*afaan*), a branch of *afoola* — all that passes from mouth to mouth — carried by melody and belonging to everyone.
- **Sub lesson 2 — The six features.** *Qusannaa jechootaa* (economy of words), *filannoo jechootaa* (choice of words), *walqixxummaa* (equal measure), *rukuttaa manaa* (end-rhyme), *yeedaloo* (melody), *suur-sammuu uumuu* (imagery) — each named and understood.
- **Sub lesson 3 (the top level) — The shape of a poem.** Line (*bo'oo*) and stanza (*buufata*); the capital start; the poem's freedoms — breaking grammar, borrowing and coining words.

A learner "has" this lesson when all three are proved: they can hear any oral poem and point at its features, its shape, and its kind's home inside afoola.
