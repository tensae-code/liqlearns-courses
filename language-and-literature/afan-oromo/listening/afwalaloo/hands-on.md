# Afwalaloo — hands-on

Served by this build: the lesson's picture (12512) — an elder reciting to a circle of listeners at dusk — is the lesson's whole idea in one frame. Do them in the lesson's order.

- **The circle (sub lesson 1).** The class sits in a circle, as in the picture. One short poem or praise the learners know is spoken by a volunteer; the class says afterwards: where did that poem live before it was spoken? (In memory and mouth — afoola.)
- **Feature hunt (sub lesson 2).** The praise song of the last lesson is heard again (teacher singing from the guide) — this time with six feature-cards on the floor: economy, choice, measure, rhyme, melody, imagery. Each time the class hears a feature at work, a learner raises its card and says where it heard it.
- **Take it apart (sub lesson 3).** A short poem the class knows is counted together: how many lines (*bo'oo*)? How many stanzas (*buufata*)? Where does a line begin with a capital? Did the poem bend any rule of ordinary talk — and did the bending make it stronger?
