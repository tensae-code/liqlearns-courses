# Hands-on — Barii Ganama

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beat keepers
Half the class sings; half keeps the beat only — claps, knee-taps, a stick on a tin — the guide's own division of ear work. Midway, swap sides: the beat-keepers must find the words, the singers the floor under them.

## Dawn watch
Sing the song once facing east at a real morning lesson, or at the window at the day's start: the class names everything the dawn is doing outside — light, birds, people starting — in Afaan Oromoo words they know, then sings the song to the real morning.

## Word stones
The song's explained words are written on stones or cards. As the class sings, a learner holds up each word's stone at the moment its meaning is sung — the song made visible, word by word.
