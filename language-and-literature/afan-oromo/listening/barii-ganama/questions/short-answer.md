# Barii Ganama — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the chapter of the Grade 4 guide the song rides in.
**Answer:** the plants chapter (Biqiloota), Ch. 3 — the song is at printed p. 13.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What stays steady while the song's sounds lengthen and shorten over it?
**Answer:** the beat — the rukuttaa.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Say the two dawn words and their meanings.
**Answer:** barii — the dawn; ganama — morning.
