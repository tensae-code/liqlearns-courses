# Barii Ganama — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barii Ganama means —
a) Good Evening  b) Early Morning  c) The Market Day
**Answer: b) Early Morning.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The song is sung —
a) to its tune  b) without a tune  c) only in writing
**Answer: a) to its tune** — a song is listening you can hold.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rukuttaa is —
a) the song's title  b) the song's beat  c) the singer's name
**Answer: b) the song's beat** — the steady steps under the words.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When the words are gone, the class should still be able to —
a) keep the beat  b) name the chapter  c) close the book
**Answer: a) keep the beat.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ganama means —
a) evening  b) morning  c) night
**Answer: b) morning** — barii is the dawn.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** After its tune is held, the guide does what with the song's words?
a) explains them  b) erases them  c) counts them only
**Answer: a) explains them** — a song understood is speech with wings.
