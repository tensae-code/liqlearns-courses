# Video script — Barii Ganama

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
The sun is coming up. **Barii ganama** — early morning!

Sing with me. Sing to the tune!
Clap the beat — **rukuttaa** — steady, steady.

What do the words mean? Dawn! Morning! A new day!
Sing it again — now you know!

**Barii Ganama!**
