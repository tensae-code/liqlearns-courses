# Barii Ganama — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — a song learned by ear: first the tune met, then the beat under it, then the words opened.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meeting the song
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sing Barii Ganama with the class to its tune, keeping your part without following a neighbour.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its beat
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: keep the song's beat with hands or taps for a whole verse, without the words.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its words explained
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what Barii Ganama means and explain the dawn words barii and ganama.
