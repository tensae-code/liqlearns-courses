# Video script — Things in the House

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The things of the house — **meeshaalee manaa**.
Name the thing. Say what it is for. That is knowing it whole.
And the houses: **mana citaa**, thatched. **Mana qorqorroo**, iron roof. **Gamoo dheeraa**, tall building.
Your home has words for everything in it. Go and name them.
