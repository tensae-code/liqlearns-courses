Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented; where a guide refers to a reading, song or listening text without printing it, this lesson teaches from the guide's own lists and answer keys and says so.

Framing: the Grade 3 and Grade 4 guides behind this lesson are **second-language** guides (Afaan Oromoo as a Second Language, Addis Ababa).

The Grade 4 placements name the household-things-and-uses placement by its chapter topic and pages (pp. 61, 64) without printing an item list; this lesson teaches the pairing method (name + use) from the guide's own wording and does not invent an item list for it.
