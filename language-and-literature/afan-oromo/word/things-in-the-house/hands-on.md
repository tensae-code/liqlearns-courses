# Hands-on — Things in the House

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The labelled house

On small pieces of paper, write the names of five household things in Afaan Oromoo words you know from this course — mana (house), buna (coffee) and its jebena, the cup, the door. Stick each label on its thing for a day. Read them at every pass.

## House-kind walk

Walk your street or compound and count the house kinds standing there: how many mana qorqorroo? Any gamoo dheeraa? Say each kind's name as you pass it.
