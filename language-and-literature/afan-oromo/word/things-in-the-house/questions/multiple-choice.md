# Things in the House — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Meeshaalee manaa' means —
a) Kinds of food  b) Things of the house  c) Family members  d) School rules
**Answer: b) Things of the house**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Xiyyaara split into syllables is —
a) xiy-yaa-ra  b) xi-yyaara  c) xiyya-ara  d) x-iyyaara
**Answer: a) xiy-yaa-ra**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 4 book pairs each household thing with —
a) Its colour  b) Its use — what it is for  c) Its price  d) Its owner
**Answer: b) Its use — what it is for** — tajaajila isaanii

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Mana qorqorroo is —
a) A thatched hut  b) A corrugated-iron house  c) A villa  d) A tall building
**Answer: b) A corrugated-iron house**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A tall building is —
a) mana xiqqaa  b) mana waliinii  c) gamoo dheeraa  d) mana citaa
**Answer: c) gamoo dheeraa**
