# Animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hoolaa is —
a) A dog  b) A sheep  c) A cow  d) A cat
**Answer: b) A sheep**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The donkey is —
a) harree  b) sa'a  c) adurree  d) saree
**Answer: a) harree**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Bineensota bosonaa' means —
a) Farm animals  b) Wild animals — animals of the bush  c) Young animals  d) Water animals
**Answer: b) Wild animals — animals of the bush**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The line between the two families is —
a) Size  b) The fence: living with people, or living wild  c) Colour  d) Speed
**Answer: b) The fence: living with people, or living wild**
