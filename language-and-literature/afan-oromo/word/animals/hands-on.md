# Hands-on — Animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compound census

Count the domestic animals around your home or on your street for one day: every saree, adurree, harree, hoolaa, sa'a you see. Write the census in words — 'sadii saree' (three dogs) if your numbers are ready.

## Bush from the window

Look at the furthest wild place you can see — a field edge, a river bank, trees. Say: bineensota bosonaa — the animals whose home is there, not here.
