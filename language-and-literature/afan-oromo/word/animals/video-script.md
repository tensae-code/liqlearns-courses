# Video script — Animals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The farmyard five. **Saree** — dog. **Adurree** — cat.
**Harree** — donkey. **Hoolaa** — sheep. **Sa'a** — cow.
And far away, the bush family: **bineensota bosonaa** — the wild animals.
Two families. You know them both.
