# My Body and the Senses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jilba is —
a) The neck  b) The knee  c) The chest  d) The toe
**Answer: b) The knee** — morma is the neck

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Quba miillaa is —
a) A finger  b) A toe — the foot's quba  c) An ankle  d) A thigh
**Answer: b) A toe — the foot's quba** — quba harkaa is the finger

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You taste with —
a) ija  b) arraba  c) gurra  d) harka
**Answer: b) arraba** — the tongue

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Gurra kootiin nan dhaggeeffadha' — I ____ with my ears.
a) See  b) Smell  c) Hear  d) Taste
**Answer: c) Hear**
