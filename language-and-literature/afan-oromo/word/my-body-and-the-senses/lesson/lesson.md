# My Body and the Senses

## Do it first

Touch your ear. Your eye. Your nose. Your tongue — say 'arraba' as you feel it move. Your hand. Five parts, five ways the world reaches you. This lesson names your whole body, fifteen parts, and crowns the five that sense.

## Sub lesson 1 — Parts of the body — the fifteen

The book names the body from a **numbered picture** — fifteen parts, top to toe:

**mataa** (head) · **gurra** (ear) · **ija** (eye) · **funyaan** (nose) · **afaan** (mouth) · **morma** (neck) · **laphee** (chest) · **harma** (breast) · **irree** (arm) · **garaa** (belly) · **quba harkaa** (finger — the hand's quba) · **sarbaa** (thigh) · **jilba** (knee) · **koronyoo** (ankle/heel) · **quba miillaa** (toe — the foot's quba)

[Picture 9881 — a child with the fifteen body parts named]

Read the picture, then read your own body: touch each part as you say its word, head first, toes last. Your body is the one picture you always carry.

## Sub lesson 2 — Body parts and the senses

Five of the body's parts are the **sense organs** — the parts the world enters by:

**gurra** (the ear — hearing) · **ija** (the eye — seeing) · **arraba** (the tongue — tasting) · **funyaan** (the nose — smelling) · **harka** (the hand — touching)

[Picture 9882 — the five sense organs]

The Grade 4 book asks the next question of each part — *what do you do with it?* — and answers in sentences: 'Ija kootiin nan ilaala' (I see with my eyes), 'Gurra kootiin nan dhaggeeffadha' (I hear with my ears), 'Funyaan kootiin nan fuunfadha' (I smell with my nose), 'Arraba kootiin nan dhandhama' (I taste with my tongue), 'Harka kootiin nan qaqqaba' (I touch with my hands), 'Luka kootiin nan deema' (I walk with my legs). Those sentences are taught in full in the Speaking lessons; here, own the organs and their senses.

## Say it to your companion

The touching game: one touches a body part on themselves; the other names it in Afaan Oromoo. Fifteen parts, no repeats. Then senses: one mimes a sense — sniffing, listening — the other names the organ: funyaan! gurra!

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the fifteen parts in order, head to toe.
2. Write the five sense organs and beside each, its sense in English.
3. Complete: 'Ija kootiin nan ____' (I see with my eyes) — ilaala. Then write the ear sentence the same way.
