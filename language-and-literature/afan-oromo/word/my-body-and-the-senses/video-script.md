# Video script — My Body and the Senses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your body is the picture. Touch and name.
**Mataa** — head. **Ija** — eye. **Gurra** — ear. **Funyaan** — nose. **Afaan** — mouth.
Down we go: **morma, laphee, irree, garaa, jilba** — to the toes, **quba miillaa**.
Five senses: hear, see, taste, smell, touch. **Gurra, ija, arraba, funyaan, harka.**
Fifteen parts. Five senses. All yours.
