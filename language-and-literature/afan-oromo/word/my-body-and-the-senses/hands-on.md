# Hands-on — My Body and the Senses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your body

Stand and read yourself like the numbered picture: touch and name, mataa to quba miillaa, three times — slower, then faster, then with eyes closed. Your hands must find each part by its word alone.

## Sense walk

Take a short walk using one sense at a time. Ears only for ten steps: what did gurra gather? Eyes only: what did ija gather? At home, name each sense's harvest in its word.
