# Hands-on — Food and Plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## At the market

At a market or shop, find the vegetables of this lesson in real piles: kaarota, timaatima, dinnicha, qullubbii diimaa. Say each word standing by its pile — the word sticks where it was earned.

## Name the meal

For one meal today, name every food on the table in Afaan Oromoo before eating: buddeena, foon, marqa, damma — whatever stands there. Missing words go on a list to ask about.
