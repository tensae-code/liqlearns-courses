# Video script — Food and Plants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The table speaks. **Buddeena** — injera. **Foon** — meat. **Damma** — honey.
**Keekii. Marqa. Daabboo. Ruuzii.**
The garden speaks too: **kaarota**, carrot. **Timaatima**, tomato. **Dinnicha**, potato.
Name your food before you eat it. The words are delicious.
