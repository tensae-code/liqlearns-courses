# Food and Plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buddeena is —
a) Cake  b) Injera / flatbread  c) Porridge  d) Honey
**Answer: b) Injera / flatbread**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Damma is —
a) Rice  b) Pasta  c) Honey  d) Bread
**Answer: c) Honey** — the reading's health sentence uses it

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Timaatima is —
a) Potato  b) Cabbage  c) Tomato  d) Lettuce
**Answer: c) Tomato**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Qullubbii diimaa' is built as —
a) Colour first, thing after  b) Thing first, colour after  c) Two colours  d) A verb
**Answer: b) Thing first, colour after** — onion, then red — the phrase rule
