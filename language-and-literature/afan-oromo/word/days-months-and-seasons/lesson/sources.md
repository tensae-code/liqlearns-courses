Honesty: there is NO Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' objectives, contents lists, syllabus tables and their full answer keys, which print the answers to the student book's exercises and so evidence what learners do. Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented; where a guide refers to a reading, song or listening text without printing it, this lesson teaches from the guide's own lists and answer keys and says so.

Framing: the Grade 3 and Grade 4 guides behind this lesson are **second-language** guides (Afaan Oromoo as a Second Language, Addis Ababa).

Two season tables, named honestly: the Grade 3 key names the four seasons without month sets; the Grade 4 key (p. 11) prints its own grouping of months into Birraa, Bona and Arfaasaa and lists Ganna in the season row without a printed month set in the placements. This lesson prints the Grade 4 grouping exactly as the placements record it and does not complete Ganna's set from outside knowledge.

The two songs (months song; the days are also recalled through song in Grade 4) are named in the guides; their full wording lives in the missing student book and is not invented — this lesson sings the day and month rows themselves, which are the keys' content.
