# Days, Months and Seasons — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Days of the week
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say the seven days in order and name today, yesterday-by-name and tomorrow-by-name in the row.

## Sub lesson 2 — Months of the year — the song
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sing or say the twelve months in order and find the present month in the row.

## Sub lesson 3 — The seasons
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the four seasons and say one thing the land or the sky does in each.

## Sub lesson 4 — Seasons, months and weather together
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the month set of two seasons from the Grade 4 table and say today's weather in a word from the lesson.

## Sub lesson 5 — Times of the day
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: match studying, sleeping and playing to their times of the day and use galgala and halkan in a sentence each.
