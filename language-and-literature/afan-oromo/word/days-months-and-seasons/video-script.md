# Video script — Days, Months and Seasons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The week: **Wiixata. Kibxata. Roobii. Kamisa. Jimaata. Sanbata. Dilbata.**
The year sings: **Fulbaana, Onkoloolessa, Sadaasa, Muddee, Amajjii, Guraandhala…**
Four seasons: **Birraa. Bona. Arfaasaa. Ganna.**
And the day: **ganama** morning, **galgala** evening, **halkan** night.
Day, month, season — now time speaks Afaan Oromoo.
