# Hands-on — Days, Months and Seasons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The week's walk

For one whole week, each morning say the day's name aloud as you wake: 'Har'a Wiixata' — today is Monday (use the true day). By Dilbata the row will own itself.

## Season watch

Draw the view from your door today — the sky, the land, the trees. Write the season's name and the month under it. Keep the drawing; draw the same view next season and compare.
