# Days, Months and Seasons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which day comes after Roobii (Wednesday)?
a) Kamisa  b) Kibxata  c) Jimaata  d) Wiixata
**Answer: a) Kamisa**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first day of the week row is —
a) Dilbata  b) Sanbata  c) Wiixata  d) Jimaata
**Answer: c) Wiixata** — Monday

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The months song begins with —
a) Hagayya  b) Fulbaana  c) Caamsa  d) Muddee
**Answer: b) Fulbaana** — and ends with Hagayya

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How many seasons (waqtiilee) are named?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four  d) Seven
**Answer: c) Four** — Birraa, Bona, Arfaasaa, Ganna

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In the Grade 4 table, Fulbaana stands inside —
a) Bona  b) Birraa  c) Arfaasaa  d) No season
**Answer: b) Birraa** — with Muddee, Bitootessa, Waxabajjii

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Halkan is —
a) Morning  b) Midday  c) Evening  d) Night
**Answer: d) Night** — galgala is the evening
