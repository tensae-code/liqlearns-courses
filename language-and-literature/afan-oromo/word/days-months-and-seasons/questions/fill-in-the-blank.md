# Days, Months and Seasons — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or sound.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wiixata, ____, Roobii — the missing day is ____.
**Answer: Kibxata**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Birraa, Bona, ____, Ganna — the missing season is ____.
**Answer: Arfaasaa**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Cold weather is ____; hot is ho'aa; cloudy is duumessa.
**Answer: qorraa**
