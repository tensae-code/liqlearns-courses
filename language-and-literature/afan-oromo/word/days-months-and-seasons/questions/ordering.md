# Days, Months and Seasons — ordering

Put the items in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the days in their true order.
Mixed up: Jimaata · Wiixata · Kamisa · Roobii · Kibxata
**Answer: Wiixata → Kibxata → Roobii → Kamisa → Jimaata**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put these months in the song's order.
Mixed up: Caamsa · Fulbaana · Muddee · Sadaasa
**Answer: Fulbaana → Sadaasa → Muddee → Caamsa**
