# Lesson plan — Days, Months and Seasons

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Days, Months and Seasons
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the seven days, the twelve months (learned by their song), the four seasons, and the times of the day — the words that place everything in time.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Number Words: the learner counts and orders; now days, months and seasons give counting its calendar.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Days of the week** — the seven days read from the Guyyoota Torbanii reading and answered orally: Wiixata … Dilbata (G3 Ch. 7).
2. **Months of the year — the song** — the twelve months learned through the months song (faaruu ji'oota waggaa), G3 Ch. 7 answer key.
3. **The seasons** — the four seasons (waqtiilee) named: Birraa, Bona, Arfaasaa, Ganna (G3 Ch. 7).
4. **Seasons, months and weather together** — the Grade 4 guide's grouping: seasons with their month sets, and weather talked about (G4 Ch. 2, answer key p. 11).
5. **Times of the day** — time-of-day words in use: evening (galgala), night (halkan), the day's hours matched to their activities (G4 Ch. 12).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Number Words — order and counting carry the calendar. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the seven days in order: Wiixata, Kibxata, Roobii, Kamisa, Jimaata, Sanbata, Dilbata.
2. Sing the twelve months in order, Fulbaana to Hagayya.
3. Name the four seasons: Birraa, Bona, Arfaasaa, Ganna — and the Grade 4 month sets inside them.
4. Name the day's parts — ganama, guyyaa, galgala, halkan — and match activities to their times.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A wall calendar if there is one at home.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner names today, this month and this season in their first language, ready to own the Afaan Oromoo words.
2. **The explanation** — the seven days in order; the twelve months by their song; the four seasons; the Grade 4 month-in-season table with weather words; the times of the day matched to their activities (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9868]: a picture of a weekly calendar strip: seven day boxes in a row, each with its day name — Wiixata to Dilbata — the student reads the row across, then points to today and reads from today onward.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9906]: a picture of a calendar week whose seven boxes light up one by one in order as the day names appear beneath them — the student says each day name as its box lights, one beat per box.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9869]: a picture of a year wheel of twelve segments, each segment carrying one month name — Fulbaana at the top, running round to Hagayya — the student turns a finger slowly round the wheel, saying each month in the song's order.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9870]: a picture of a strip of the same farm scene drawn four times — dry, wet with rain, green, harvest-gold — one for each season — the student names each season over its picture and says what the land is doing.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9871]: a picture of four panels of one day: sunrise (ganama), high sun (guyyaa), sunset (galgala), moon and stars (halkan) — the student names the part of the day in each panel and one thing done then.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice singing the twelve months in order, the months song's own list — the student sings along with the wheel, then alone.
3. **Say it to your companion** — run the day row and the month row to each other; quiz the season table and the times of the day.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, ordering, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write the seven days, the twelve months, the four seasons, and a day matched to its activities.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
