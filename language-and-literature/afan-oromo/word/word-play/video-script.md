# Video script — Word Play

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Opposites! **Furdaa — qallaa.** Fat — thin. **Dheeraa — gabaabaa.** Tall — short.
Same meaning? Two words, one meaning — synonyms, joining like friends.
Build the whole word: **mammaaksa** — a proverb!
And the school web hangs on **meeshaalee barnootaa**.
Play with words, and the words are yours forever.

And the great chapter's words go to work — idioms finished whole:
**…hinfuudhani! Ollaan birmata!** — meanings settled where they stand: **hiika galumsaa**.
And the poetry words join hands — **sakaaluu** is **takaaluu** — same meaning, friends forever.
