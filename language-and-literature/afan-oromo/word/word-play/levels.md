# Word Play — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Opposites
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give the opposites of furdaa, dheeraa and adii, and say what hiika faallaa means.

## Sub lesson 2 — Idioms and synonyms
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a synonym is and match two word cards of like meaning; say what an idiom adds to plain words.

## Sub lesson 3 — Meanings in context
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: match three verbs to their meaning partners from the sets and explain how context settles a word's meaning.

## Sub lesson 4 — Building whole words
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read the eight built words aloud and split two of them into syllables.

## Sub lesson 5 — The school word-web and everyday actions
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the seven school materials under their web-word and act out three hygiene actions by name.

Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: complete the chapter's idiom-phrases, use its present-tense verb forms, sort its synonym sets, and settle two word-meanings from their sentences (hiika galumsaa).
