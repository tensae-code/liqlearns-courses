# Word Play — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the opposites of dheeraa and adii.
**Answer: gabaabaa (short), gurraacha (black)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does an idiom add beyond its plain words?
**Answer: A bigger meaning carried in use — ibsoota jechamoota are read in use, inside real reading, and the sentence teaches them**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** From the verb sets: what two verbs stand as a pair for reading and writing?
**Answer: dubbisuu / barreessuu — reading / writing**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Read and gloss alaabaa and qalama.
**Answer: alaabaa — a flag; qalama — a pen/pencil**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name three materials hanging on the school web.
**Answer: Any three of: qubeessaa, dabtara, maarkarii, haqxuu, gabatee gurraacha, kobbee, sarartuu**
