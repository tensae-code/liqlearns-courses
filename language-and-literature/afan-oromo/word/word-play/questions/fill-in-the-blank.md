# Word Play — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or sound.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Words of like meaning are jechoota hiika ____.
**Answer: walfakkii**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Dubbisuu / ____ — reading / writing.
**Answer: barreessuu**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Cutting nails is qeensa ____.
**Answer: qorachuu**
