# Word Play — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or sound.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Words of like meaning are jechoota hiika ____.
**Answer: walfakkii**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Dubbisuu / ____ — reading / writing.
**Answer: barreessuu**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Cutting nails is qeensa ____.
**Answer: qorachuu**

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** A word's meaning settled from the sentence it stands in is its hiika ______.
**Answer: galumsaa** — the chapter review's sixteen-sentence skill.
**5. (Sub lesson 7)** Sakaaluu jechuun ______ jechuudha (sakaaluu means the same as —).
**Answer: takaaluu** — the same-meaning pair.
