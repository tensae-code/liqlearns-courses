# Word Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The opposite of furdaa (fat) is —
a) dheeraa  b) qallaa  c) gabaabaa  d) adii
**Answer: b) qallaa** — thin

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Synonyms are words —
a) Of opposite meaning  b) Of like meaning  c) With double letters  d) From the bush
**Answer: b) Of like meaning** — jechoota hiika walfakkii

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A word's meaning is settled by —
a) Guessing fast  b) Its context — the sentence around it  c) Its length  d) Its first letter
**Answer: b) Its context — the sentence around it**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Mammaaksa is —
a) A flag  b) A proverb  c) A pen  d) A sign
**Answer: b) A proverb** — alaabaa is the flag

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The centre of the school word-web is —
a) meeshaalee manaa  b) meeshaalee barnootaa  c) gosoota manaa  d) dubbisa hojii
**Answer: b) meeshaalee barnootaa** — learning materials

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Kubbaa taphachuu is —
a) Washing clothes  b) Cutting nails  c) Playing ball  d) Washing shoes
**Answer: c) Playing ball**
