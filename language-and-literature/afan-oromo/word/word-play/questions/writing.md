# Word Play — writing

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the five big built words: bineensa, qalama, mallattoo, mammaaksa, alaabaa.

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** Write three hygiene action phrases with their meanings.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Write three short sentences of your own, each carrying one of the chapter's completed idiom-phrases (choose from: …hinfuudhani; ollaan birmata; "bishaan ol hinyaa'u," jette harreen).
**Answer guidance: the phrase used whole and used truly — the scene must fit the saying's sense (the guide's Exercise pattern, Ch. 9 keys, printed pp. 123–124).**

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** Write two sentences: one with **gatii**, one with **maraachaa**, from the poetry chapter's synonym set.
**Answer guidance: each set word used in a true sentence of the learner's own; the set is Exercise 8's, printed pp. 135–136.**
