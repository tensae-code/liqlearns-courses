# Hands-on — Word Play

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Real hand-washing

The hygiene set ends in water: wash your hands and body the thorough way — qaama dhiqachuu — saying the action words as you go: qeensa qorachuu is for nail day, kophee miiccuu for shoe day. The words live in the doing.

## Web on the wall

Draw the school web big on paper: meeshaalee barnootaa in the middle, its seven materials around. Hang it where you study; add a new card whenever a new school word is learned.
Vocabulary in use (sub lessons 6–7, G7 supplement): saying-halves are dealt as cards — one learner holds "Bishaan ol hinyaa'u…", the table must answer "…jette harreen" before the card is laid. Then synonym snap: when sakaaluu is called, the takaaluu card must slap down first.
