# Lesson plan — Word Play

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Word Play
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** playing with words until they open — opposites, synonyms, idioms, meanings found from context, whole words built from parts, and the word-webs and action words that finish the Word title.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on all the Word lessons: every word-family met so far returns here as material to play with.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Opposites** — antonyms (hiika faallaa): furdaa ↔ qallaa, dheeraa ↔ gabaabaa, adii ↔ gurraacha (G3 Ch. 6); synonyms and antonyms matched inside the work reading (G4 Ch. 8).
2. **Idioms and synonyms** — idioms in use read and used; synonyms matched (jechoota hiika walfakkii) (G7 Ch. 7 contents/objectives).
3. **Meanings in context** — word-meaning matching and verb-pair sets from the reading (G7 Ch. 1 answers): nyaachuu/qopheessuu, uffachuu/miiccuu/bitachuu, dhuguu/dhaabuu, dubbisuu/barreessuu…
4. **Building whole words** — word-building answers (G3 Ch. 5): bineensa, qalama, mallattoo, mammaaksa, alaabaa; gaara, naacha, raacha, saree.
5. **The school word-web and everyday actions** — school things word-web (G4 Ch. 10 answer key): qubeessaa, dabtara, maarkarii, haqxuu, gabatee gurraacha, kobbee, sarartuu under meeshaalee barnootaa; hygiene action words (G3 Ch. 8): qaama dhiqachuu, qeensa qorachuu, rifeensa murachuu, kophee miiccuu, kubbaa taphachuu, huccuu miccachuu.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: all the Word lessons — this is their playground and their proof. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the opposites: furdaa ↔ qallaa, dheeraa ↔ gabaabaa, adii ↔ gurraacha.
2. Match synonyms and read idioms in use, the mother-tongue book's way.
3. Settle a word's meaning from its context, using the verb sets.
4. Read whole built words: bineensa, qalama, mallattoo, mammaaksa, alaabaa.
5. Name the school word-web (meeshaalee barnootaa) and the hygiene action set.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Soap and water for the hand-washing that ends the lesson honestly.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner guesses the opposite of dheeraa and reads mammaaksa whole.
2. **The explanation** — opposites and synonym/antonym matching; idioms and synonyms in use; meanings from context; whole words built; the school word-web and the hygiene action set (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9883]: a picture of a strip of three opposite pairs: a fat figure beside a thin one, a tall tree beside a short one, a white sheet beside a black one — the student reads each pair aloud — word and opposite — then gives the three opposites from memory.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9884]: a picture of two word cards sliding together, their edges meeting, for words of like meaning — the student says what joins the cards: the same meaning.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9886]: a picture of a word-web: the centre card meeshaalee barnootaa with seven cards around it — a pencil, an exercise book, a marker, an eraser, a blackboard, a ruler and one more — the student reads the web from the centre out, naming each material.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9887]: a picture of a strip of action pictures: a child washing hands and body, a child cutting nails, shoes being washed, a ball being kicked — the student names each action as its picture is pointed to.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the opposite pairs and the school web slowly — the student answers each opposite before the voice gives it.
3. **Say it to your companion** — fire opposites at each other; read a web from its centre; mime an action for its word.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write the opposite pairs, the school web, and three hygiene actions with their meanings.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
