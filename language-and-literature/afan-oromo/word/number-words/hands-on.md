# Hands-on — Number Words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the house

Walk through your home counting real things in Afaan Oromoo: cups, chairs, doors, spoons, people. Write each count as a word — 'shan' beside five cups. Nothing in the house is too small to count.

## Stair stones

Lay twenty stones in a row. Count them in Afaan Oromoo, touching each. Then make decade piles of ten and count the piles: digdama… no — ten stones is kudhan; two piles counted stone by stone reach digdama. Feel the teens happen.
