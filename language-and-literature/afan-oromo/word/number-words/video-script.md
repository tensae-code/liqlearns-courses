# Video script — Number Words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Count with me. **Tokko. Lama. Sadii. Afur. Shan.**
**Jaha. Torba. Saddeet. Sagal. Kudhan.**
The teens climb by kudha. The decades climb by stairs: **digdamii tokko… soddoma!**
Up and up — **afurtama, shantama, jahatama, torbaatama, saddeettama, sagaltama** —
And the top word of all: **dhibba** — one hundred!
