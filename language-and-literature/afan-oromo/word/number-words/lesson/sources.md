**Acceptance count: taught in 0 countries — this lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (Ethiopia), named here, counted nowhere.**

Honesty: there is NO Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' objectives, contents lists, syllabus tables and their full answer keys, which print the answers to the student book's exercises and so evidence what learners do. Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented; where a guide refers to a reading, song or listening text without printing it, this lesson teaches from the guide's own lists and answer keys and says so.

Framing: the Grade 3 and Grade 4 guides behind this lesson are **second-language** guides (Afaan Oromoo as a Second Language, Addis Ababa). No mother-tongue (Grade 7) evidence is used in this lesson.

Pattern honesty: each decade's answer key prints the decade's first and last number words (e.g. digdamii tokko 21 … soddoma 30). The numbers between are written here by the book's own pattern — the tens word in its -ii form plus the unit words tokko…sagal that the keys themselves print for 1–9 — and no other source. For 71–80 the Grade 4 guide's Chapter 8 gives the decade by its contents page only (p. 40), with no answer-key words; its words are written by the same pattern.

Placements conflict, taught corrected: the Grade 4 placements record Chapter 9's decade (pp. 52–53) as 'torbaatamii tokko 81 … saddeettama 90'. That clashes with the book's own system everywhere else — torbaatama is 70 (Ch. 7 key), so torbaatamii tokko is 71 and saddeettama is the round 80, exactly as sagaltamii tokko 91 … dhibba 100 (Ch. 10) requires sagaltama to be 90. This lesson teaches 71–80 as torbaatamii tokko … saddeettama and 81–90 as saddeettamii tokko … sagaltama, following the pattern the other eight decades prove, and flags the Chapter 9 line here rather than teaching a shifted decade.

Zero: the Grade 3 chapter is headed 'numbers 0–20' but its answer key lists the words from tokko 1 to digdama 20 and gives no word for 0; this lesson teaches 1–100 and invents no zero word.

The grammar companions (sub lesson 10) are stocked here because the number chapters carry them, exactly as the structure map directs; each is taught in full in its own Sentence lesson (Persons and Pronouns; One and Many; He, She and They).
