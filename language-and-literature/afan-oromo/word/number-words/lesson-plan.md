# Lesson plan — Number Words

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Number Words
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the number words from tokko 1 to dhibba 100 — every decade its own stair — with the grammar companions the number chapters carry.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Reading lessons: the learner reads words whole; numbers are the first big word-family to master end to end.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Numbers 1–20** — numbers 1–20 read, matched word↔figure and written in words (G3 Ch. 6 answer keys): tokko 1 … digdama 20.
2. **Numbers 21–30** — the decade written in words: digdamii tokko 21 … soddoma 30 (the running numbers spine).
3. **Numbers 31–40** — the decade written in words: soddomii tokko 31 … afurtama 40 (the running numbers spine).
4. **Numbers 41–50** — the decade afurtamii tokko 41 … shantama 50; relatives' names spelled and written stand beside it in the same chapter.
5. **Numbers 51–60** — the decade written in words: shantamii tokko 51 … jahatama 60 (the running numbers spine).
6. **Numbers 61–70** — the decade written in words: jahatamii tokko 61 … torbaatama 70 (the running numbers spine).
7. **Numbers 71–80** — the decade written in words: torbaatamii tokko 71 … saddeettama 80 (the running numbers spine).
8. **Numbers 81–90** — the decade written in words: saddeettamii tokko 81 … sagaltama 90 (the running numbers spine).
9. **Numbers 91–100 — and dhibba** — the last decade: sagaltamii tokko 91 … dhibba 100, with the chapter's plurals and pronoun sentences carried as its grammar companions.
10. **The grammar companions of the number road** — the grammar parts the number chapters carry, stocked here as parts and taught in full in the Sentence lessons: plurals and pronoun sentences (Ch. 10), the verb changes with the person (Ch. 9), singular and plural both ways (Ch. 6).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Building Syllables and Words by Their Sounds — the learner reads every word here already. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and write the numbers 1–20 in words: tokko … digdama.
2. Climb every decade stair 21–100 by the -ii pattern, up to dhibba 100.
3. Match any figure to 100 with its word and any word with its figure.
4. Make the companion plurals, run a verb through its persons, and turn plurals back to singulars.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Twenty small stones or seeds for counting.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner counts to five in Afaan Oromoo and wonders at thirteen, seventy-seven and a hundred.
2. **The explanation** — ten sub lessons: the numbers to 20, then one decade stair per sub lesson to dhibba 100, then the grammar companions the number chapters carry (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9865]: a picture of a number-word chart: the figures 1 to 20 in a grid, each with its Afaan Oromoo word under it — tokko 1, lama 2 … digdama 20 — the student points to a figure and says its word, then points to a word and says its figure.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9904]: a picture of stones appearing one by one in a row while the number words tokko, lama, sadii … appear under them up to kudhan — the student counts aloud with the stones, touching the table for each one.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9866]: a picture of a tall number chart of the decades: each row a decade stair from 21–30 up to 91–100, the round tens in bold — soddoma, afurtama … dhibba — the student reads one full decade row, then finds a named number anywhere on the chart.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice counting slowly from tokko 1 to digdama 20, then the round tens to dhibba — the student counts along, then counts the tens alone.
3. **Say it to your companion** — count together to 20; then quiz each other across the decades — a figure for a word, a word for a figure.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, matching, ordering, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write the teens, two decades of your choice, and the round tens to dhibba.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
