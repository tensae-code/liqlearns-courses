# Number Words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Seven in Afaan Oromoo is —
a) jaha  b) torba  c) sagal  d) shan
**Answer: b) torba** — jaha is six

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Digdamii tokko is —
a) 20  b) 21  c) 12  d) 30
**Answer: b) 21**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Soddoma is —
a) 20  b) 30  c) 40  d) 13
**Answer: b) 30**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Shantama is —
a) 40  b) 45  c) 50  d) 60
**Answer: c) 50** — the top of the 41–50 decade

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Shantamii tokko opens the decade —
a) 41–50  b) 51–60  c) 61–70  d) 91–100
**Answer: b) 51–60**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Torbaatama is —
a) 60  b) 70  c) 17  d) 71
**Answer: b) 70** — the round ten at the decade's top

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Torbaatamii tokko is —
a) 71  b) 81  c) 70  d) 17
**Answer: a) 71** — the 70-stair's first step

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Saddeettamii tokko is —
a) 80  b) 81  c) 18  d) 88
**Answer: b) 81** — the 80-stair's first step

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Dhibba means —
a) Ninety  b) Ninety-nine  c) One hundred  d) One thousand
**Answer: c) One hundred**

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** Mana (house) becomes plural as —
a) manoota  b) manneen  c) manaa  d) manneena
**Answer: b) manneen** — the book's own pair
