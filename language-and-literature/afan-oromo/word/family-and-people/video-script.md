# Video script — Family and People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The family. **Abbaa** — father. **Haadha** — mother.
**Obboleessa** — brother. **Obboleettii** — sister. **Akkoo** — grandmother. **Akaakayyuu** — grandfather.
The jobs: **barsiisaa**, teacher. **Doktoora**, doctor. **Daldalaa**, trader.
And character: **walgargaaruu** — helping each other. Good. **Soba** — lying. Bad.
Name your people. They are your first words.
