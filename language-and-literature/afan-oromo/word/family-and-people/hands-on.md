# Hands-on — Family and People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the household

At the next family meal, name everyone at the table with their word — abbaa, haadha, obboleettii — quietly or aloud, as your house allows. The words become real when they land on real people.

## Jobs on the street

On your next walk, spot the jobs: a daldalaa at a stall, a konkolaachisaa at a wheel. Count how many of the four jobs you can find, and say each word as you find its person.
