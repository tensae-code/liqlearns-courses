# Family and People — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Akkoo is —
a) Father  b) Sister  c) Grandmother  d) Aunt
**Answer: c) Grandmother** — akaakayyuu is grandfather

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A baby or child is —
a) daa'ima  b) jaarsa  c) dhalaa  d) adaadaa
**Answer: a) daa'ima**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A woman teacher is —
a) barsiisaa  b) barsiistuu  c) doktoora  d) daldalaa
**Answer: b) barsiistuu** — the ending changes for a woman

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A trader is —
a) konkolaachisaa  b) daldalaa  c) barsiisaa  d) doktoora
**Answer: b) daldalaa**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Walgargaaruu — helping each other — stands in —
a) The bad set  b) The good set  c) Neither set  d) The jobs
**Answer: b) The good set**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which word is in the bad set?
a) ajajamuu  b) obsaan dubbachuu  c) hanna — stealing  d) maatii kabajuu
**Answer: c) hanna — stealing**
