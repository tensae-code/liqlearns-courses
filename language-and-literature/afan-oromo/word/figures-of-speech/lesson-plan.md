# Figures of Speech

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Figures of Speech
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).

**What this lesson teaches:** the *malleen dubbii* — the figures of speech — one by one, exactly as the Grade 7 guide teaches them across its oral-poetry chapter: *iddeessa* (metaphor), *akkasaa* (simile), *nameessa* (personification), *arbeessa* (exaggeration, upward and downward), *habalaka* (irony), *eemitii/fuggisoo* (antithesis), *fakkoomii* (symbol, with the guide's own symbol stock), *hinjirree dubbisuu* (apostrophe) and *dubbiilee qoolaa* (witty sayings) — closed by the book's own sorting exercise, hearing examples and naming their figure. This lesson is the toolbox the poem-writing of Writing — Kinds of Writing asks for, and its last sub lesson IS the guide's Exercise 8 sorting.

**What the learner will be able to do:**
- Name each figure and say what it does.
- Tell metaphor from simile, and exaggeration-up from exaggeration-down.
- Recite the guide's symbol stock (odaa, dove, snake, scales, red).
- Sort heard or read sayings by their figure.

## Sub lessons
1. Comparing — one list, in this order, in `lesson/lesson.md`; each opens by proving the one before, by the 2026-11-08 rule stated there.
2. Giving life, and stretching size
3. Opposite, standing-for, and calling out
4. Name that figure

**Gate pool:** Each later sub lesson's prove-it check is drawn from the whole question set of the sub lessons before it in this lesson (mixed with earlier parts), every check at most 5 questions, picked from the whole set, never a fixed list.

**How the lesson runs:** Two figures at a time, each with the guide's own examples where it gives them (the bee-swarm metaphor, the symbol stock), the picture strip opening the lesson, and the book's sorting exercise as the summit: first you learn the figures, then you catch them.

**Level of the language:** Full Afaan Oromoo sentences, with each figure's name glossed in English as it comes.
