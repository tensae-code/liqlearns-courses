# Figures of Speech — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the right word. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saying one thing IS another, with no "like" in it, is ______.
**Answer: iddeessa** — the metaphor names outright.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Saying the opposite of what you mean, so the hearer feels the truth bite, is ______.
**Answer: habalaka** — irony.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Speaking to something absent as if it stood before you is ______ dubbisuu.
**Answer: hinjirree** — apostrophe: "O rain, why do you delay?"

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The witty, playful sayings are called dubbiilee ______.
**Answer: qoolaa** — the play of a quick tongue.
