# Figures of Speech — multiple choice

Answer each question. Try them in any order you like — the number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Oromoon kaannisa" — the Oromo are a swarm of bees — is —
a) akkasaa
b) iddeessa
c) nameessa
d) habalaka
**Answer: b) iddeessa** — a metaphor: it names them bees outright, with no "like" — the guide's own example.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A simile (*akkasaa*) is known by words like —
a) fi, garuu
b) akka, amma, fakkaata
c) yoo, osoo
d) hanga, erga
**Answer: b) akka, amma, fakkaata** — the comparison shows itself in these words.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When a poem makes the river hurry and decide, that is —
a) nameessa
b) iddeessa
c) fakkoomii
d) arbeessa
**Answer: a) nameessa** — personification: the river acts with a person's intentions.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Making a thing much SMALLER than it is, to cut it down, is —
a) arbeessuu
b) kineessuu
c) iddeessa
d) eemitii
**Answer: b) kineessuu** — exaggeration downward; arbeessuu stretches upward.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's symbol stock, the odaa tree stands for —
a) peace
b) cunning
c) Oromo identity and unity
d) justice
**Answer: c) Oromo identity and unity** — the dove is peace, the snake cunning, the scales justice.
