# Figures of Speech — levels

This lesson is a step: doing it fully is what "knowing it" means. Depth, in learning order — the sub lessons of this lesson, which are its levels:

- **Sub lesson 1 (the base level) — Comparing.** *Iddeessa* (metaphor: saying a thing IS another) and *akkasaa* (simile: saying it is LIKE another, with akka/amma/fakkaata/caala/gaha).
- **Sub lesson 2 — Giving life, and stretching size.** *Nameessa* (personification: a thing or animal acting with a person's intentions) and *arbeessa* (exaggeration: *arbeessuu* upward, *kineessuu* downward).
- **Sub lesson 3 — Opposite, standing-for, and calling out.** *Habalaka* (irony), *eemitii/fuggisoo* (antithesis), *fakkoomii* (symbol — the guide's stock: odaa, dove, snake, scales, red), *hinjirree dubbisuu* (apostrophe), and *dubbiilee qoolaa* (witty sayings).
- **Sub lesson 4 (the top level) — Name that figure.** The guide's sorting exercise done: sayings heard and sorted by their figure.

A learner "has" this lesson when all four are proved: they can hear any saying, poem or speech and point at the figures working inside it — the tools every walaloo is built with.
