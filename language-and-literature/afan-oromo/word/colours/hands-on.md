# Hands-on — Colours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Colour hunt

Walk your home or compound and gather one small thing of each colour — a leaf for magariisa, a stone, a cup, a cloth. Lay them in the song's order and say the row over real things.

## Draw the phrases

Draw the yellow flower and the red car. Write each phrase under its drawing, thing first, colour after — daraaraa keelloo, konkolaataa diimaa.
