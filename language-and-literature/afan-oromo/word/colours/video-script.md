# Video script — Colours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six colours. Sing them with me.
**Diimaa** — red. **Gurraacha** — black. **Magariisa** — green.
**Cuquliisa** — blue. **Keelloo** — yellow. **Adii** — white.
Now join them: **daraaraa keelloo** — a yellow flower! **Samii cuquliisa** — a blue sky!
Thing first, colour after. That is the way.
