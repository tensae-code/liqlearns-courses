# Colours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keelloo is —
a) Red  b) Blue  c) Yellow  d) White
**Answer: c) Yellow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The blue of the sky is —
a) magariisa  b) cuquliisa  c) diimaa  d) adii
**Answer: b) cuquliisa** — samii cuquliisa — a blue sky

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Daraaraa keelloo' means —
a) A red car  b) A yellow flower  c) White teff  d) A black ox
**Answer: b) A yellow flower** — thing first: daraaraa (flower), keelloo (yellow)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A red car, as a phrase, is —
a) diimaa konkolaataa  b) konkolaataa diimaa  c) konkolaataa keelloo  d) samii diimaa
**Answer: b) konkolaataa diimaa** — the colour rides behind its thing
