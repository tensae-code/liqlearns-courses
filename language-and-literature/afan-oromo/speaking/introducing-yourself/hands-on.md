# Hands-on — Introducing Yourself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The introduction line
The whole class stands in a line. Each learner steps forward, gives the full introduction — three facts and one experience — and steps back. The class answers each introduction with a greeting. Everyone is met, by name and by family.

## Whose child?
In pairs: one introduces only with mother's and father's names and age, hiding their own name to the last word. The partner listens and must place the speaker: whose child, how old — before the name lands. It teaches why the parents' names carry weight.

## Back-row reading
Write the introduction, then read it aloud standing at the front while a partner stands at the very back with a thumb: up only when every word arrives clear. Adjust voice, not speed, until the thumb stays up.
