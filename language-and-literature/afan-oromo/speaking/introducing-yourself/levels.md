# Introducing Yourself — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — standing and saying who you are: first the three facts, then the telling, then the written text read aloud.

## Sub lesson 1 — Saying who you are
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: introduce yourself standing, with all three parts — name, mother's and father's names, age — without notes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Telling your experiences
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: add a short true telling — one personal experience or one group experience — after the three facts.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading your introduction aloud
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read your written introduction aloud, eyes up at sentence ends, so the back of the room hears every name.
