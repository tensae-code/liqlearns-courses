# Video script — Introducing Yourself

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Stand tall. Smile. Greet!

Say your name. Big and clear.
Say your mother's name. Say your father's name.
Say your age.

Now — one true thing about you. Something you did. Something your group did.

That is you, introduced. **Of ibsuu** — introducing yourself!
