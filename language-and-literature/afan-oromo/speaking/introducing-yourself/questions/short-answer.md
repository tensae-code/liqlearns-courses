# Introducing Yourself — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say the three facts of the introduction, in order.
**Answer:** your own name; your mother's and father's names; your age.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between a fact of the introduction and the telling that follows it?
**Answer:** a fact places you (name, parents, age); the telling shows you — a short true experience, personal or of your group.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why write the introduction before reading it aloud?
**Answer:** writing steadies the speaking — names spelled right, order set, nothing forgotten standing up.
