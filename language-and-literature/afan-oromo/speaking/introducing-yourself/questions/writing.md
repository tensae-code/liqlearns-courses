# Introducing Yourself — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write your own three facts as a list: name, mother's name, father's name, age.
**A good answer:** the four lines written truly, with the names spelled as your family spells them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write two sentences of telling: one personal experience, one group experience.
**A good answer:** two short true sentences — one "I…" happening, one "we…" happening.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write your whole introduction as one short text, ready to read aloud.
**A good answer:** greeting, the three facts, the telling — in order, in full sentences, nothing missing.
