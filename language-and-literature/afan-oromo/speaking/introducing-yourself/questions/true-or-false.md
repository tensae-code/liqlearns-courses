# Introducing Yourself — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this introduction, your age is not part of what you say.
**False** — age is the third of the three facts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A group experience is something that happened to your group, not only to you.
**True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Reading your introduction aloud means mumbling into the page.
**False** — eyes up at sentence ends, voice to the back of the room; the text is a script for speaking.
