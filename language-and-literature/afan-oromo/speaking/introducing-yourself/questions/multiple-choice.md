# Introducing Yourself — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 introduction names three things. They are —
a) name, school, village  b) name, mother's and father's names, age  c) name, job, house
**Answer: b) name, mother's and father's names, age.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the parents' names belong in the introduction?
a) they fill time  b) they place you — whose child you are  c) they are a song
**Answer: b) they place you — whose child you are.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 4 step adds, after the facts —
a) a dance  b) telling personal and group experiences  c) a list of foods
**Answer: b) telling personal and group experiences.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A personal experience is —
a) something that happened to you  b) something you read  c) a rule of the school
**Answer: a) something that happened to you.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Grade 4 way, the introduction is finished by —
a) singing it  b) writing it as a short text and reading it aloud  c) drawing it
**Answer: b) writing it as a short text and reading it aloud.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** When reading your introduction aloud, your eyes should —
a) stay on the page  b) be closed  c) come up at the ends of sentences
**Answer: c) come up at the ends of sentences** — the text is a script for speaking.
