# Buying and Selling — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What passes each way in a sale?
**Answer:** goods pass from seller to buyer; their worth (money) passes from buyer to seller.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two daily activities — one at home, one at school — that buying serves.
**Answer:** any two true ones — e.g. cooking the evening food at home; carrying exercise books and pencils at school.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What three skills meet in the sequencing work?
**Answer:** listening (the heard story), ordering (the pictures in time order), and retelling (the order spoken aloud).
