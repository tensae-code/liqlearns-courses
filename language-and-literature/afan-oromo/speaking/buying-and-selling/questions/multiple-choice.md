# Buying and Selling — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bittaafi Gurgurtaa means —
a) Time  b) Buying and Selling  c) Directions
**Answer: b) Buying and Selling.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Buying is —
a) giving goods and taking their worth  b) taking goods and giving their worth  c) looking only
**Answer: b) taking goods and giving their worth** — selling is the other side: giving goods, taking their worth.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A daldalaa is —
a) a teacher  b) a driver  c) a trader
**Answer: c) a trader** — many: daldaltoota.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Around the market work, the chapter also talks about —
a) the weather at sea  b) daily activities at home and at school  c) wild animals
**Answer: b) daily activities at home and at school.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** After hearing a story, learners —
a) sequence its pictures in time order  b) close the book  c) sing the alphabet
**Answer: a) sequence its pictures in time order** — then retell the order aloud.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** If the pictures land in the story's order, it shows —
a) the drawing was good  b) the listening landed  c) the story was short
**Answer: b) the listening landed.**
