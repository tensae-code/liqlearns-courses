# Hands-on — Buying and Selling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The classroom market
Set up stalls with real or drawn goods — fruit, grain, cups, cloth. Learners circulate with play money or stones: every purchase must be talked, not mimed — greeting, asking, agreeing, paying, thanking — before goods change hands. Sellers rotate so everyone stands on both sides.

## Hear it, lay it, tell it
The teacher tells a short market story. Learners hold its pictures shuffled; as a class they lay the pictures in time order on the floor, then one teller retells the whole story walking along the line of pictures.

## The weekly basket
Each learner lists aloud what their house buys in a week, stall by stall, while a partner tallies the goods on paper. The pair then reports the fullest basket to the class — in Afaan Oromoo, goods and sellers named.
