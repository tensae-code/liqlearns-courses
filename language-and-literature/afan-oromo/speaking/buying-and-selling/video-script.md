# Video script — Buying and Selling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
The market! Stalls and goods everywhere.

You buy — you take the goods, you give their worth.
You sell — you give the goods, you take their worth.
**Daldalaa** — the trader — sells. You buy.

Greet! Ask! Agree! Thank!
That is **Bittaafi Gurgurtaa** — buying and selling!
