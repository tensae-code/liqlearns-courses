# Video script — Describing Pictures and Places

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Look at the picture. What do you see?
Fields — flat and wide. A river — bending.
Hills — rising behind. Trees along the water.

Say it near to far. Say it low to high.
Your words build the place in my head.

That is describing — **haala teessuma lafaa**, the shape of the land!
