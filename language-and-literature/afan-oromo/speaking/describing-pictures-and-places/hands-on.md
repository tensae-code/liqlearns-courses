# Hands-on — Describing Pictures and Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw my words
In pairs: one describes a simple scene while the other draws it without seeing it. Compare drawing and picture. Every miss is a missing sentence — add it and describe again. This is the guides' own check: a description works when a listener can rebuild the scene.

## Compound walk
Walk the real school compound slowly, in a line. At each stop, a different learner describes what stands there and what it stands against. By the gate, the class has spoken a whole map.

## Land from the door
Stand at the school door or a window with a real view. Each learner gives one land sentence — a hill, the river, a field, the sky — in near-to-far order, until the whole view has been built aloud.
