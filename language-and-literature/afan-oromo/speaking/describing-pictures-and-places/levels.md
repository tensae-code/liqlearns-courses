# Describing Pictures and Places — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — describing: first a picture, then your own school ground, then the land itself, each ring wider than the last.

## Sub lesson 1 — Describing a picture
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe a picture aloud in at least five connected sentences, drawing on your own experience.

## Sub lesson 2 — Describing my school and surroundings
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe your school compound so a listener could walk it — at least six things, each placed against another.

## Sub lesson 3 — Describing the land in a picture
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe a landscape picture along one clear road for the eye — near to far or low to high — naming hills, water and flat land.
