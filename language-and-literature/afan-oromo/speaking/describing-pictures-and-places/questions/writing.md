# Describing Pictures and Places — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a five-sentence description of a picture you can see (a scene from a book or a wall).
**A good answer:** five connected sentences — what the scene is, what stands where, what is happening — using your own experience of such a place.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a description of your school compound in four sentences.
**A good answer:** at least four things of the compound, each placed against another, so a reader could walk it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write a land description: fields, a river, hills, sky — in near-to-far order.
**A good answer:** the four parts in order, with the river's bend and the hills' rise named.
