# Describing Pictures and Places — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A description is done in which two ways in the Grade 3 chapter?
**Answer:** orally and in writing — the mouth first, the hand after.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things of a school compound a description should place.
**Answer:** any three truly placed — e.g. the classrooms, the flag, the gate, the trees, the road beyond — each against another.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three land parts the lesson's landscape holds.
**Answer:** flat fields, a river, and hills (trees along the water, sky over all).
