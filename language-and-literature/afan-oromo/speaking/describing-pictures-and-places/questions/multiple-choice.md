# Describing Pictures and Places — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 chapter Fakkiiwwan Ibsuu is about —
a) counting syllables  b) describing pictures  c) the alphabet
**Answer: b) describing pictures.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's method for describing a picture is to use —
a) only the picture's title  b) your prior experience of the world it shows  c) a dictionary
**Answer: b) your prior experience of the world it shows.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A surroundings description works when —
a) it names things with no placing  b) a listener could walk the place from your words  c) it is very long
**Answer: b) a listener could walk the place from your words** — each thing placed against another.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 4 chapter Naannoo Ibsuu means —
a) Describing Your Surroundings  b) Buying and Selling  c) Time
**Answer: a) Describing Your Surroundings.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Haala teessuma lafaa is —
a) a kind of food  b) the shape/lay of the land  c) a greeting
**Answer: b) the shape/lay of the land** — its hills, river and flat fields.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A land description gives the eye a road, for example —
a) in any order at all  b) backwards only  c) near to far, or low to high
**Answer: c) near to far, or low to high.**
