# Greetings — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 guide's first chapter, Nagaa Walgaafachuu, means —
a) Counting Numbers  b) Greeting Each Other  c) The Qubee Letters
**Answer: b) Greeting Each Other.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A greeting is practised the guide's way —
a) silently, from a book  b) aloud, to real people — the teacher and each other  c) only in writing
**Answer: b) aloud, to real people** — greeting is speaking work.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ganama is —
a) the evening  b) the morning  c) midday
**Answer: b) the morning.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Galgala names —
a) the evening  b) the morning  c) the whole day
**Answer: a) the evening.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The sun stands high. The division is —
a) ganama  b) galgala  c) guyyaa
**Answer: c) guyyaa** — midday, the day.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Shaakala waliin dubbii means —
a) dialogue practice  b) writing practice  c) counting practice
**Answer: a) dialogue practice** — greeting in pairs, in turns.
