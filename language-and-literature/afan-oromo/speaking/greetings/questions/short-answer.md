# Greetings — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the day's three divisions, in order.
**Answer:** ganama (morning), guyyaa (midday/day), galgala (evening).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** After the mouth knows the greetings, what does the chapter do with the hand?
**Answer:** learners read and write the greeting words.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Who are the first two greeters of the chapter?
**Answer:** the teacher and each other — learners greet the teacher, and greet one another.
