# Video script — Greetings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Morning! The sun is low. **Ganama.** Greet!
Midday! The sun is high. **Guyyaa.** Greet!
Evening! The sun is going down. **Galgala.** Greet!

One day. Three greetings.
Now — you greet me, I greet you. **Shaakala waliin dubbii** — talk together!
