Honesty: there is no Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' contents, objectives, syllabus tables and their full answer keys (the guides print the answers to the student book's exercises, which is the evidence of what learners do). Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented. Framing: the Grade 3 guide is a second-language guide — this is its first chapter, the year's first speaking work. The guide evidences the greeting practice and the day's three divisions through its contents and answer key; the greeting phrases themselves live in the missing student book, so this lesson names and practises the greetings by their times rather than quoting wording the guide does not print.
