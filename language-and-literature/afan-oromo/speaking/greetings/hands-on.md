# Hands-on — Greetings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Gate greetings
For one week, the lesson begins at the gate or the door: every learner greets the teacher on entry, with the greeting of the real hour. No greeting, no seat — kindly, and without exception, until the habit holds.

## Sun clock
Draw a big day-circle on paper: ganama at the rising side, guyyaa at the top, galgala at the setting side. A learner points to an hour; the class calls the division and its greeting together.

## Dialogue chairs
Two chairs face each other. Pairs sit, greet in dialogue for an announced time of day, then stand and swap chairs and partners. Five partners each — greetings are short; give them many mouths.
