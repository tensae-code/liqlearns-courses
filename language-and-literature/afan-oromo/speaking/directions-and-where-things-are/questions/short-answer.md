# Directions and Where Things Are — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the school a good anchor for saying where your house is?
**Answer:** everyone in the conversation knows where the school is; the house is placed against the shared anchor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say, in your own words for your real ground, where the shop is from your home.
**Answer:** any true placing that anchors on home and sets the suuqii against it, with a direction and a landmark where possible.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three places of the lesson's route, in order.
**Answer:** school → home → shop.
