# Directions and Where Things Are — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write where your house is relative to your school, in two sentences.
**A good answer:** the house placed against the school with a direction word and a landmark.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the whole route — school, home, shop — as directions a visitor could follow.
**A good answer:** three legs in order, each leg starting from the place the last leg reached, with turns and landmarks named.
