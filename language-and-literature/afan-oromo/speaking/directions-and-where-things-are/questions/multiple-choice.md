# Directions and Where Things Are — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 4 directions chapter is called —
a) Yeroo  b) Kallattiiwwan  c) Hojii
**Answer: b) Kallattiiwwan** — Directions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Where is your house?" is answered in speech relative to —
a) nowhere  b) the school  c) the moon
**Answer: b) the school** — the shared anchor everyone knows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Suuqii means —
a) school  b) shop  c) house
**Answer: b) shop.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Sub lesson 2 the anchor changes to —
a) home  b) the market  c) the river
**Answer: a) home** — the shop is placed from home.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A route in words is complete when —
a) it is very long  b) it has no turns  c) a listener could walk it from the words
**Answer: c) a listener could walk it from the words.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In speech, a place is always placed —
a) from somewhere the listener knows  b) by pointing only  c) by its colour
**Answer: a) from somewhere the listener knows** — directions are relative.
