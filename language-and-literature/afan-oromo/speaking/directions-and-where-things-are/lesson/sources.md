Honesty: there is no Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' contents, objectives, syllabus tables and their full answer keys (the guides print the answers to the student book's exercises, which is the evidence of what learners do). Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented. Framing: the Grade 4 guide is a second-language guide; its directions chapter is Kallattiiwwan (printed pp. 34–39), and the direction-word speech work is evidenced at p. 36. The guide evidences the tasks — saying where the house is relative to the school, and where the shop is from home — and does not print a phrase list, so this lesson teaches the tasks and the anchor method without inventing a direction-word list.
