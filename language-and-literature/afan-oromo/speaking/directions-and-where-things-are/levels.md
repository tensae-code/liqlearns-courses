This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — placing places aloud: first home against school, then the shop against home, then the whole route set walking.

Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say where your house is relative to the school, with at least one direction word and one landmark.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where the shop is
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say where the shop is from your home, anchoring on home and placing the suuqii against it.
