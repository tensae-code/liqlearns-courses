# Hands-on — Talking Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Coffee ceremony telling
Lay out the coffee sequence as real things or drawn cards — beans, roasting pan, mortar, jebena, cups. A learner stands at each station and tells its step; the class moves along the line as the ceremony is told, start to poured cup, without a step repeated or missed.

## Morning line
Six learners stand as the morning sequence — waking, toilet, washing hands, breakfast, dressing, school. Shuffle them; a teller must put the line back in order by telling each step aloud to the person who holds it.

## Mini debate
The class splits on a small question — should the school day start earlier? Each side gets two speakers, one reason each, under the rule: answer the other side's point before adding your own. The rest judge who listened best.
