# Talking Together — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things does a longer conversation do with information?
**Answer:** it gives information and receives it — each speaker does both, turn by turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the guide place talking about traditional games here?
**Answer:** learners talk most freely about a game they know and love — explaining its play and rules builds fluency on ground the speaker owns.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What does "under debate rules" add to ordinary talk?
**Answer:** turns by rule, set sides, and the duty to listen — you must answer the other side's argument, which you cannot do without hearing it.
