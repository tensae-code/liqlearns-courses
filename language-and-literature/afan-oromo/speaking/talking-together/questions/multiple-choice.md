# Talking Together — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Waliin dubbii dheeraa is —
a) a greeting  b) an extended/longer conversation  c) a song
**Answer: b) an extended/longer conversation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A longer conversation lives when each turn —
a) repeats the first turn  b) ignores the partner  c) builds on the information the last turn gave
**Answer: c) builds on the information the last turn gave.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Duraa duuba means —
a) before and after (in order)  b) loud and soft  c) yours and mine
**Answer: a) before and after (in order).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the morning sequence, washing hands comes —
a) after dressing in uniform  b) before eating breakfast  c) after arriving at school
**Answer: b) before eating breakfast** — waking, toilet, washing hands, breakfast, dressing, school.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Taphoota aadaa are —
a) new video games  b) traditional/cultural games  c) board exams
**Answer: b) traditional/cultural games.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** In a falmii (debate), before adding your own point you must —
a) shout louder  b) answer the other side's point  c) repeat your first point
**Answer: b) answer the other side's point** — listening is a duty under debate rules.
