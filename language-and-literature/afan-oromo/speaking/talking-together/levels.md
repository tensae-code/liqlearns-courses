# Talking Together — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — real talk: first the long conversation, then the ordered telling, then game talk, then the debate, talk under rules.

## Sub lesson 1 — Longer conversations
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: hold a conversation of at least eight turns, each turn building on the information the last one gave.

## Sub lesson 2 — Telling a sequence from pictures
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell both sequences from their pictures, in order, with order words holding the steps.

## Sub lesson 3 — Playing and talking about traditional games
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain one traditional game aloud — how it is played and its rules — so a listener who never played it could join in.

## Sub lesson 4 — Debate
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: take one side of a class debate: give your side's reason in your turn, and answer the other side's last point, not your own echo.
