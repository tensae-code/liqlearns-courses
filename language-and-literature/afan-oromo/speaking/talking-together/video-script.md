# Video script — Talking Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
First — the coffee is roasted. Smell it!
Then — it is ground. Pound, pound.
Then — water boils, the coffee brews in the jebena.
Last — it is poured. Drink!

First. Then. After that. Last.
Tell it **duraa duuba** — before and after, in order!
