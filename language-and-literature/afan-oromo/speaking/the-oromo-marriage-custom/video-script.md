# Video script — The Oromo Marriage Custom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
A wedding! Families gathering. Beautiful clothes.

This is **sirna fuudhaafi heerumaa aadaa Oromoo** —
the Oromo marriage custom.

Watch the order. Who stands where? Who is honoured?
Now — we act it. You take this part. I take that part.
And we tell each other our relatives' names — **maqaa firoota**.
