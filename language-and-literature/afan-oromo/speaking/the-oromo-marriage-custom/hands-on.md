# Hands-on — The Oromo Marriage Custom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ceremony walked
Before any acting, walk the ceremony: the class lays out the order of their area's marriage ceremony as stations around the room, and a narrator tells each station as the group walks it. Only when the walk is right does the role-play begin.

## Name circle
Stand in a circle. One learner names a relative and where they stand — "my aunt, my mother's sister" — and throws a soft ball to the next, who names one of theirs. A name may not be repeated with the same standing twice; the circle must listen.

## Picture to stage
From the lesson's picture, the class lists everything they can see — gathering, clothes, order signs — then stages one frozen moment of the ceremony as a living picture, and one learner describes the frozen scene aloud as if it were the picture.
