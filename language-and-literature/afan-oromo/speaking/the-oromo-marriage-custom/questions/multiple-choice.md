# The Oromo Marriage Custom — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sirna fuudhaafi heerumaa aadaa Oromoo names —
a) a harvest festival  b) the Oromo marriage custom  c) a school rule
**Answer: b) the Oromo marriage custom** — the order of taking and giving in marriage as the culture keeps it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's way into the custom is —
a) a written test  b) describing it from pictures  c) a song
**Answer: b) describing it from pictures.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the role-play, learners act out —
a) any wedding from a film  b) the marriage ceremony of their own area  c) a market scene
**Answer: b) the marriage ceremony of their own area.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Role-play in these guides is —
a) a game-break  b) a serious learning tool — order, parts and words learned by acting  c) punishment
**Answer: b) a serious learning tool.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Maqaa firoota means —
a) names of foods  b) relatives' names  c) names of days
**Answer: b) relatives' names.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** At a wedding, a relative's name is told with —
a) its price  b) where the relative stands in the family  c) nothing else
**Answer: b) where the relative stands in the family.**
