# The Oromo Marriage Custom — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things may differ between areas, as part of the lesson rather than as errors?
**Answer:** the details of the custom as each area keeps it — its order and ways can differ, and the differences are content.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What must you know to act a ceremony properly?
**Answer:** its order, its people's parts, and its words — said at the right moments, to the right faces.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the Writing lesson that teaches spelling relatives' names.
**Answer:** First Written Pieces — this chapter's writing twin.
