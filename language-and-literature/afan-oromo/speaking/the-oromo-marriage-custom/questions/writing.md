# The Oromo Marriage Custom — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a five-sentence description of the lesson's wedding picture.
**A good answer:** the gathering, the people, their order and dress — described from the picture, as the chapter teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write six of your relatives' names, each with where they stand in the family.
**A good answer:** six true names, spelled as your family spells them, each placed — whose child/sister/brother, which side, elder or younger.
