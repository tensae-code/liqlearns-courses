# The Oromo Marriage Custom — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The marriage custom joins two people only, with families taking no part.
**False** — in the custom, families meet and are gathered; that is why relatives' names belong to the chapter.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ceremony acted is the one from the learners' own area.
**True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Names of relatives are told carelessly, any way they come.
**False** — names matter exactly and are pronounced carefully, with each relative's standing.
