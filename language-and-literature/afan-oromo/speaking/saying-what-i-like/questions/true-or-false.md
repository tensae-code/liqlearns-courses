# Saying What I Like — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the pair talk, only likes are spoken; dislikes stay silent.
**False** — hin jaallanne, what you don't like, is half the talk.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Luka kootiin nan deema" is something you say about your ears.
**False** — it is the legs' sentence: I walk with my legs.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** All six body sentences wear the same shape: (part) kootiin nan (doing).
**True.**
