# Saying What I Like — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Waan jaallatan / hin jaallanne means —
a) what you eat / drink  b) what you like / don't like  c) what you see / hear
**Answer: b) what you like / don't like.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the pair talk, a like should come with —
a) a song  b) a reason  c) a picture
**Answer: b) a reason** — a list is not talk.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Ija kootiin nan ilaala" means —
a) I hear with my ears  b) I see with my eyes  c) I taste with my tongue
**Answer: b) I see with my eyes.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** You smell with your nose: "Funyaan kootiin nan ______".
a) deema  b) fuunfadha  c) dhandhama
**Answer: b) fuunfadha.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "Harka kootiin nan qaqqaba" — the hands —
a) walk  b) touch/hold  c) hear
**Answer: b) touch/hold.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The word that means "with my" in every sentence is —
a) nan  b) kootiin  c) ija
**Answer: b) kootiin** — it straps the part to its work.
