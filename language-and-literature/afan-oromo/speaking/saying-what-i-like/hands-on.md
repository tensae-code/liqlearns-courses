# Hands-on — Saying What I Like

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Like it or not
Hold up real things or picture cards — foods, a ball, a book, a broom. Learners sort them into two hoops on the floor: like / don't like, saying the sentence as each thing lands. The class's biggest like and biggest dislike get a tally on the board.

## Body pointing game
The teacher calls a doing — smelling! seeing! tasting! — and learners touch the part and say its whole sentence. Wrong part touched? The sentence cannot be said — the body must agree with the mouth. Faster and faster rounds.

## Smell and listen walk
A short walk outside: stop and smell (funyaan), stop and listen (gurra), stop and look far (ija). At each stop the group says the part's sentence together, about the real thing in front of them.
