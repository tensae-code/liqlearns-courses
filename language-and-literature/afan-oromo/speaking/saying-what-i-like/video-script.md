# Video script — Saying What I Like

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
What do you like? Tell me! What don't you like? Tell me why!

Now your body. Your legs?
**Luka kootiin nan deema** — I walk!
Your nose? **Funyaan kootiin nan fuunfadha** — I smell!
Your eyes? **Ija kootiin nan ilaala** — I see!
Your ears? **Gurra kootiin nan dhaggeeffadha** — I hear!
Your hands? **Harka kootiin nan qaqqaba** — I touch!
Your tongue? **Arraba kootiin nan dhandhama** — I taste!

Six parts. Six jobs. Say them all!
