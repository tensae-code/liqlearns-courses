# One and Many — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Fill each blank with the right shape of the word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** One book is kitaaba; many books are ______.
**Answer: kitaabota.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One building is gamoo; many buildings are ______.
**Answer: gamoowwan.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The one inside manneen is ______.
**Answer: mana.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one inside firoota is ______.
**Answer: fira.**
