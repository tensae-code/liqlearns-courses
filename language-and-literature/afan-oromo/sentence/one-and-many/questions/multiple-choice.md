# One and Many — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Qeenxee means —
a) many  b) one  c) a person
**Answer: b) one** — a singular noun; danuu is many.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word is danuu (plural)?
a) kitaaba  b) kitaabota  c) mana
**Answer: b) kitaabota** — books, many.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Nama (person) becomes many as —
a) manneen  b) namoota  c) namaa
**Answer: b) namoota.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The many of mana (house) is —
a) manoota  b) manawwan  c) manneen
**Answer: c) manneen** — mana takes the -neen tail.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tail did gamoo take to become gamoowwan?
a) -oota  b) -neen  c) -wwan
**Answer: c) -wwan.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Manneen stripped back to one is —
a) mana  b) manaa  c) manne
**Answer: a) mana.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The one inside barsiistota is —
a) barsiis  b) barsiisaa  c) barsiist
**Answer: b) barsiisaa** — a teacher.
