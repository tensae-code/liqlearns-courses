# Hands-on — One and Many

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One handful, two handfuls
With real things — stones, cups, pencils: lay one down and say its qeenxee word; sweep many together and say its danuu word. Ten things from the room, one and many, one and many.

## Tail cards
Write each noun pair on a card, one-shape on the front, many-shape on the back. Shuffle the deck, test each other, and sort the cards by their tails: the -oota pile, the -toota pile, the -neen pile, the -wwan pile.

## Strip it back
The teacher writes a danuu word big on paper — barattoota. A learner covers the tail with a hand and reads the one hiding inside: barataa. Five words, five strippings.
