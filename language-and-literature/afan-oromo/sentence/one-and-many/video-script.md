# Video script — One and Many

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
One book. **Kitaaba.**
Many books! **Kitaabota.**

One person. **Nama.**
Many people! **Namoota.**

One house. **Mana.**
Many houses! **Manneen.**

One is **qeenxee**. Many is **danuu**.
Each word grows its own tail. Learn the tail with the word.
