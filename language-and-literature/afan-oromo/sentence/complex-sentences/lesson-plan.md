# Complex Sentences

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Complex Sentences
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).

**What this lesson teaches:** what a clause is (a subject with its predicate), the two kinds of clause — the one that stands alone (*ciroo of danda'aa*) and the one that leans (*ciroo hirkataa*) — and how the two join into a complex sentence (*himoota xaxamoo*), exactly as the Grade 7 guide's grammar lesson teaches it: the halves matched, the clauses counted, and ten complex sentences of your own at the end. From the Grade 7 guide alone.

**What the learner will be able to do:**
- Say what a clause is, and name its two parts.
- Tell a clause that stands alone from a clause that leans, and name the joining words that start the leaning ones (*hirkisiistota*).
- Join two halves into the book's six complex sentences.
- Count the clauses in a sentence, and write ten complex sentences of their own.

## Sub lessons
1. Standing alone and leaning — one list, in this order, in `lesson/lesson.md`; each opens by proving the one before, by the 2026-11-08 rule stated there.
2. Joining two halves into one sentence
3. Counting clauses, and writing your own

**Gate pool:** Sub lesson 2's prove-it check is drawn from sub lesson 1's whole question set (mixed with earlier parts of this lesson), and sub lesson 3's check from the whole set before it — every check at most 5 questions, picked from the whole set, never a fixed list.

**How the lesson runs:** Stand and Lean first (the words are sorted into standing sentences and leaning beginnings), then the halves game, then counting — the book's own order. The lesson's picture shows the standing clause carrying the leaning clause on a gold link word, and the GIF slides the two clause cards together into one long sentence.

**Level of the language:** Full Afaan Oromoo sentences, with the grammar words glossed in English as they come.
