# Complex Sentences — short answer

Write a short answer for each. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "Caalaan barataa cimaadha," which word is the subject, and which words are the predicate?
**Answer: "Caalaan" is the subject; "barataa cimaadha" is the predicate** — the guide's own worked table.
*Why it matters: every clause is found by finding its subject and its predicate.*

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Join into one complex sentence: the standing clause "Mana homaa hin dhaqne" and the leaning clause "sababa roobni roobeef."
**Answer: "Sababa roobni roobeef, mana homaa hin dhaqne."**
*Why it matters: the leaning clause comes first, with a comma at the joint.*

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write two complex sentences of your own — one beginning with *yoo*, one beginning with *waan …-eef*.
**Answer: any two true complex sentences** — each must hold a clause that stands and a clause that leans (the book's review task).
*Why it matters: this is the book's finishing test for the whole grammar lesson.*
