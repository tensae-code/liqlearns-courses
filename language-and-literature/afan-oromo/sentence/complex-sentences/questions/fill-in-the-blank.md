# Complex Sentences — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the right word. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A clause that cannot stand alone is called ciroo ______.
**Answer: hirkataa** — the leaning clause.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A complex sentence holds one clause that stands and one or more clauses that ______.
**Answer: lean (hirkataa)** — standing clause + leaning clause(s) = *hima xaxamaa*.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ inni dhufutti, ani hojiin qaba." (until)
**Answer: Hanga** — "Until he comes, I have work."

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Waan bakka sana jiraatteef, mucicha arguu hin dandeenye" — this sentence has ______ clauses.
**Answer: lama (two)** — one leaning, one standing.
