# Complex Sentences — multiple choice

Answer each question. Try them in any order you like — the number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A clause (*ciroo*) always has —
a) a subject and a predicate
b) two full stops
c) a picture beside it
d) a question mark
**Answer: a) a subject and a predicate** — that is the guide's definition of a clause.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these can stand alone as a complete sentence?
a) Yoo inni dhufe…
b) Caalaan barataa cimaadha.
c) Waan bakka sana jiraatteef…
d) Osoo isheen hin du'in…
**Answer: b) Caalaan barataa cimaadha.** — the others all begin with joining words, so they lean.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Yeroon mana oolu," joins which ending?
a) qorumsa kufte
b) ani hojiin qaba
c) summuugaa cufeera
d) mucicha arguu hin dandeenye
**Answer: c) summuugaa cufeera** — "When I am at home, I hang the mosquito net" (the book's Exercise 9, pair 1).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How many clauses are in "Sababa roobni roobeef, mana homaa hin dhaqne"?
a) one
b) two
c) three
d) four
**Answer: b) two** — one leaning clause and one standing clause, as the book's counting table works it.
