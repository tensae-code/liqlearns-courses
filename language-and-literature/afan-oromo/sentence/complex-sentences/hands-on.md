# Complex Sentences — hands-on

Served by this build: the lesson's picture (12504) gives the shape to copy onto the board, and the GIF (12505) shows the two clause cards sliding together. Do them in the lesson's order.

- **Stand or lean (sub lesson 1).** The teacher reads sentence-parts aloud from the guide's examples — some whole sentences, some leaning beginnings. Learners stand up for a clause that stands alone and lean sideways for a clause that leans. A wrong call is talked through on the spot: does it begin with a joining word? Does it finish the thought?
- **The halves game (sub lesson 2).** Pairs get the book's six first halves (A) and six second halves (B) as loose cards. They match and join all six, write each whole sentence, and read it aloud — leaning half first, standing half second.
- **Count and write (sub lesson 3).** In groups: count the clauses in the book's worked sentences (answers: 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 2), then each learner writes the book's review task — ten complex sentences — and the group shares them, checking each one really has a clause that stands and a clause that leans.
