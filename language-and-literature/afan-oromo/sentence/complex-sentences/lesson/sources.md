**How the lesson maps to the book.** Afaan Oromoo's Grade 7 guide teaches grammar chapter by chapter, and its Chapter 9 grammar lesson is complex sentences: clauses defined and sorted, halves joined, clauses counted. This lesson carries that teaching whole:

| Lesson part | Source |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Standing alone and leaning | Printed pp. 125–126 — *Himoota Xaxamaa*: the clause (*ciroo*) defined by its subject and predicate; the two clause kinds named (*ciroo of danda'aa* / *ciroo hirkataa*); the joining words (*hirkisiistota*: akka, waan, osoo, yoo, yemmuu, erga); the worked table on "Caalaan barataa cimaadha" and the because-it-rained example |
| Sub lesson 2 — Joining two halves into one sentence | Printed pp. 126–127 — Exercise 9: six leaning halves (A) matched to six standing halves (B); the guide's printed key (1e, 2d, 3b, 4a, 5f, 6c); the six joined sentences stocked as printed |
| Sub lesson 3 — Counting clauses, and writing your own | Printed p. 126 — the worked clause-counting table (six sentences with their counts, the last carrying three clauses); printed pp. 126–127 — the chapter review's tasks: join the cut halves and write ten complex sentences |
