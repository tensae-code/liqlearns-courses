# Doubled Letters in Words — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The doubled letter in biyya is —
a) b  b) y  c) a
**Answer: b) y** — bi-yy-a; the yy is held.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word carries -qq-?
a) keessa  b) buqqee  c) tabba
**Answer: b) buqqee.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Waggaa's doubled letter is —
a) gg  b) aa only — there is no double  c) w
**Answer: a) gg** — wa-gg-aa.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cluster in jilba is —
a) ji  b) lb  c) ba
**Answer: b) lb** — two consonants with no vowel between them.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which word carries the -rq- cluster?
a) marga  b) marqaa  c) gamna
**Answer: b) marqaa** — marga carries -rg-.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A doubled letter in speech is —
a) skipped  b) one sound held longer  c) two different sounds
**Answer: b) one sound held longer.**
