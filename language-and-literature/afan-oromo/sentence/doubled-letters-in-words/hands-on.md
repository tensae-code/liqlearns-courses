# Hands-on — Doubled Letters in Words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hold it with your hands
Read the doubled words aloud together. At every doubled letter, clap twice — once for each twin — while holding the sound: kee-(clap clap)-a. The hands teach the mouth how long the hold is.

## Cluster stepping stones
Chalk or lay out stepping stones on the floor, one per letter of gamna, jilba and marqaa. A learner steps stone to stone saying each letter's sound — the cluster stones stand close together, with no vowel-stone between them.

## Ring and read
On paper, ring every doubled letter and underline every cluster in the twenty words. Then read the page aloud to a partner, who ticks each ring only when the hold is truly heard.
