# Video script — Doubled Letters in Words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Look: **biyya**. Two y's! Hold it — bi-**yy**-a. Country!

**Keessa** — hold the ss.
**Waggaa** — hold the gg.
**Bittaa** — hold the tt.

A doubled letter is a sound held long.
And tight corners — **gamna**, **jilba**, **marqaa** — step through, no stopping.

Read what is written. Hold what is doubled.
