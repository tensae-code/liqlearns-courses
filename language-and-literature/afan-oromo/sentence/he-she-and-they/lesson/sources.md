Honesty: there is no Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' contents, objectives, syllabus tables and their full answer keys (the guides print the answers to the student book's exercises, which is the evidence of what learners do). Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented. Framing: the Grade 4 guide is a second-language guide; its grammar spine teaches gender alkallattiin — through examples, not head-on — and this lesson keeps that way. The third-person set qo'atte / qu'ate / qu'atan is copied exactly as the guide's answer key prints it (p. 71), including the middle form's spelling.
