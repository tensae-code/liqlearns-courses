# Video script — He, She and They

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
The boy runs. **Fiige.**
The girl runs. **Fiigde.**
Hear it? Her word has an extra beat!

The boy ate. **Nyaate.**
The girl ate. **Nyaatte.**

He — one short ending.
She — the extra **t** beat.
They — **baratan**, **taphatan** — the many ending!

Listen to the end of the word. It tells you who.
