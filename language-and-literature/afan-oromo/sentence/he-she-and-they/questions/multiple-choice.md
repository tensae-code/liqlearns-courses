# He, She and They — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Koornayaa is —
a) the numbers one and many  b) the masculine/feminine sort  c) a kind of sentence
**Answer: b) the masculine/feminine sort.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Arrabni diimaadha" means —
a) the teeth are white  b) the tongue is red  c) the tongue is white
**Answer: b) the tongue is red** — ilkaan adiidha is "the teeth are white".

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "She went" is —
a) deeme  b) deemte  c) deeman
**Answer: b) deemte** — the feminine form carries the extra t-beat.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which form did a he do?
a) nyaatte  b) fiigde  c) bite
**Answer: c) bite** — he bought; nyaatte and fiigde are she-forms.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The she-form of dhufe (came) is —
a) dhufte  b) dhufan  c) dhufee
**Answer: a) dhufte.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In baratte / barate / baratan, the they-form is —
a) baratte  b) barate  c) baratan
**Answer: c) baratan** — the -tan ending is the many ending.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** "They played" is —
a) taphate  b) taphatte  c) taphatan
**Answer: c) taphatan.**
