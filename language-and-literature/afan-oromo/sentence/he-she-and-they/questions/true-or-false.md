# He, She and They — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 4 guide teaches koornayaa head-on with a rule first.
**False** — it teaches it alkallattiin, indirectly, from a picture of a man and a woman.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deemte and deeme mean the same doer.
**False** — deemte is she went; deeme is he went.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The they-forms end in -tan or -an.
**True** — baratan, taphatan, dhugan, qu'atan, ciman.
