# He, She and They — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Fill each blank with the verb form that fits.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The girl ran: isheen ______ (fiige / fiigde).
**Answer: fiigde** — the she-form.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The man ate: inni ______ (nyaate / nyaatte).
**Answer: nyaate** — the he-form.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** They learned: isaan ______ (barate / baratan).
**Answer: baratan** — the they-ending.
