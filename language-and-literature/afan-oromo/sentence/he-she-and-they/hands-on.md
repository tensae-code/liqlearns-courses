# Hands-on — He, She and They

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## His turn, her turn
A boy and a girl take turns doing the same action in front of the class — run in place, mime eating, mime drinking. After each turn the class calls the right form together: fiige! fiigde! nyaate! nyaatte!

## Three footsteps
Three learners stand in a row: she, he, they. The teacher calls a verb — barachuu (learn), taphachuu (play), dhuguu (drink) — and each learner answers with their own form: baratte! barate! baratan!

## Ending sort
Write the eleven third-person forms from Sub lesson 3 on cards. Sort the cards into three piles — she, he, they — by their endings alone, then read each pile aloud.
