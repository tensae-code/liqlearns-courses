# Lesson plan — Persons and Pronouns

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Persons and Pronouns
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the little words that stand for people — ana/ani (I), ati (you), isa (he), ishee (she), nuyi/nuti (we), isaan (they) — and how the verb changes its ending to agree with the person.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Word lessons — the learner can already read and write whole Afaan Oromoo words and short sentences. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The pronouns** — the six little words that stand for people, as the Grade 3 guide's answer key gives them: ana/ani, ati, isa, ishee, isaan, nuyi.
2. **The persons — first, second, third** — persons (ramaddii) as the Grade 4 guide tables them: 1st person ani/nuti, 2nd person ati — across masculine/feminine (Dhiira/Dubara) and number (Baay'ina): one (qeenxee) and many (danuu).
3. **Pronouns in sentences — the verb agrees** — pronoun-verb sentences from the Grade 4 guide's answer keys: isheen dhufte / ani dhufe / nuti dhufne / ati dhufte / isaan dhufan, and trade-word pronoun sentences.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is
the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Word lessons — the learner reads whole words and knows the family and people words (abbaa, haadha, obboleessa). Recommended, never forced.
- The verb-changing-with-the-person work in this lesson is met again, one step deeper, in He, She and They.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the pronouns: ana/ani (I), ati (you), isa (he/him), ishee (she/her), nuyi/nuti (we), isaan (they).
2. Say which person each pronoun is: first person (the speaker), second person (the one spoken to), third person (the one spoken about).
3. Tell one (qeenxee) from many (danuu) in the pronoun table.
4. Read and build sentences where the verb's ending agrees with the person: isheen dhufte, ani dhufe, nuti dhufne, ati dhufte, isaan dhufan.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A partner helps for the pointing game in Say it to your companion.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes isa and ishee → point again: isa is the boy's word, ishee the girl's. The extra -ee in ishee is the girl's mark — you will meet it again in He, She and They.
- Gives every verb the same ending → cover the pronoun and read the ending alone: -e, -ne, -te, -an are the person's footprints. The guide teaches this through examples (alkallattiin), so give more examples, not a rule lecture.
- Confuses nuyi and nuti → both are "we"; the Grade 3 book writes nuyi, the Grade 4 book writes nuti. Neither is wrong.
