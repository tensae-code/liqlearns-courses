# Persons and Pronouns — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Isa means "she".
**False** — isa is "he"; ishee is "she".

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ati is the third person.
**False** — ati (you) is the second person, the one spoken to. The third person is the one spoken about.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In Afaan Oromoo the verb's ending can change with the person.
**True** — dhufe, dhufne, dhufte, dhufan: the ending agrees with the person.
