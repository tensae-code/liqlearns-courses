# Persons and Pronouns — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word means "I"?
a) ati  b) ani  c) isa  d) isaan
**Answer: b) ani** — ana is its other shape; ati is "you", isa is "he", isaan is "they".

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Ishee" means —
a) he  b) we  c) she  d) you
**Answer: c) she.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair are both shapes of the same pronoun?
a) ana / ani  b) isa / ishee  c) ati / isaan
**Answer: a) ana / ani** — both mean "I".

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The first person is —
a) the one spoken about  b) the one speaking  c) the one listening
**Answer: b) the one speaking** — ani and nuti sit in the first person's chair.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pronoun is second person?
a) nuti  b) isaan  c) ati
**Answer: c) ati** — the one spoken to.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Qeenxee means —
a) many  b) one  c) a question
**Answer: b) one** — danuu is many.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** "Nuti ______" — we came. The ending that fits is —
a) dhufan  b) dhufne  c) dhufte
**Answer: b) dhufne** — the -ne ending agrees with nuti (we).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In "isaan dhufan", the verb ending -an tells you the doers are —
a) I  b) you  c) they
**Answer: c) they.**
