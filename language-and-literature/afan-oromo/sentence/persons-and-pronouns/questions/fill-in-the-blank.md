# Persons and Pronouns — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Fill each blank with the right pronoun or verb form.

**Box:** ani · ati · isa · ishee · isaan · nuti · dhufne · dhufan

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point at a girl and say: ______ (she).
**Answer: ishee.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Point at your whole group, yourself inside it, and say: ______ (we — the Grade 3 book's shape).
**Answer: nuyi** (the Grade 4 book writes nuti; both are "we").

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ is the second person — the one spoken to.
**Answer: ati.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ is first person and danuu (many).
**Answer: nuti.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Isaan ______ — they came.
**Answer: dhufan.**
