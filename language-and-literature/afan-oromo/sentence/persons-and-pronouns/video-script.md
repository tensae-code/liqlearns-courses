# Video script — Persons and Pronouns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Point to yourself. **Ana.** I.
Point to your friend. **Ati.** You.
Point to the boy. **Isa.** He.
Point to the girl. **Ishee.** She.
Point to all of us. **Nuyi.** We.
Point to them, over there. **Isaan.** They.

Six little words. They stand for people.

Now listen. The doing word changes!
**Isheen dhufte** — she came.
**Ani dhufe** — I came.
**Nuti dhufne** — we came.
**Isaan dhufan** — they came.

The end of the word tells you who!
