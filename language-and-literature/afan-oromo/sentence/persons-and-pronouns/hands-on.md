# Hands-on — Persons and Pronouns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The pointing round
Stand in a circle. One learner points — at self, at one other, at a boy, at a girl, at the whole circle, at people outside — and everyone calls the pronoun together: ana! ati! isa! ishee! nuyi! isaan! Ten pointings each, faster and faster.

## Person chairs
Put three chairs in a row and label them with paper: first, second, third. The teacher says a pronoun; a learner runs and sits in its chair. Ani — first chair. Ati — second. Isaan — third. Wrong chair? Say the right person out loud and try again.

## Ending detectives
Write the five "came" sentences on cards, cut each card between the pronoun and the verb, shuffle, and rebuild the sentences so every verb ending sits with its right person.
