Honesty: there is no Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' contents, objectives, syllabus tables and their full answer keys (the guides print the answers to the student book's exercises, which is the evidence of what learners do). Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented. Framing: both guides behind this lesson are Afaan Oromoo as a Second Language guides (Addis Ababa learners whose first language is another language). The two shapes of "we" — nuyi in the Grade 3 guide (p. 46) and nuti in the Grade 4 guide (pp. 13, 52) — are both the books' own spellings and are kept side by side, not merged.
