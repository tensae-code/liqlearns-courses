# Video script — Saying What Is Happening Now

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
The dog! **Sareen dutaa jira.** The dog is barking — now!
The man! **Namichi deemaa jira.** Walking — now!
The hen! **Lukkuun iyyaa jira.**
The goat! **Re'een ni mar'atti.**
The children! **Ijoolleen taphachaa jiru.** Playing — now!

One doer — **jira**. Many doers — **jiru**.
This is **hennaa ammeennaa** — the time of now, and of every day.
