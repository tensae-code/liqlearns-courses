# Saying What Is Happening Now — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Fill each blank with the right word.

**Box:** jira · jiru · mar'atti · ammeennaa

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Namichi deemaa ______ — the man is walking.
**Answer: jira.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ijoolleen taphachaa ______ — the children are playing.
**Answer: jiru** — many doers.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Re'een ni ______ — the goat is bleating.
**Answer: mar'atti.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The present/habitual time is hennaa ______.
**Answer: ammeennaa.**
