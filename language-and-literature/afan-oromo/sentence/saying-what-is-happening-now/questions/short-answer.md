# Saying What Is Happening Now — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say, in Afaan Oromoo, "the man is walking".
**Answer:** Namichi deemaa jira.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which animal says its sentence with ni -atti?
**Answer:** the goat — Re'een ni mar'atti.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Ijoolleen taphachaa jiru", why jiru and not jira?
**Answer:** the doers are many (the children); many doers take jiru, one doer takes jira.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the Grade 7 guide's grammar name for the present/habitual time?
**Answer:** hennaa ammeennaa.
