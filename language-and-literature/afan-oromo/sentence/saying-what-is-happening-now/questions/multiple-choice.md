# Saying What Is Happening Now — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Sareen dutaa jira" means —
a) the dog barked  b) the dog is barking  c) the dog will bark
**Answer: b) the dog is barking** — happening now.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sentence is about children playing, now?
a) Ijoolleen taphachaa jiru  b) Namichi deemaa jira  c) Lukkuun iyyaa jira
**Answer: a) Ijoolleen taphachaa jiru.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "Lukkuun iyyaa jira" — the hen is —
a) eating  b) sleeping  c) crowing
**Answer: c) crowing.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The many-doers present ending is —
a) jira  b) jiru  c) ni
**Answer: b) jiru** — Ijoolleen taphachaa jiru; one doer takes jira.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The goat's sentence, "Re'een ni mar'atti", wears which present shape?
a) -aa jira  b) ni -atti  c) -tan
**Answer: b) ni -atti.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Hennaa ammeennaa is —
a) the past time  b) the present/habitual time  c) a punctuation mark
**Answer: b) the present/habitual time.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Hennaa ammeennaa covers —
a) only this second  b) only long ago  c) happening now and happening as a habit
**Answer: c) happening now and happening as a habit.**
