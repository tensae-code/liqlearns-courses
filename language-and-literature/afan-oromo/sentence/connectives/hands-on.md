# Connectives — hands-on

Served by this build: the lesson's picture (12506) holds the two-cards-and-a-link-word shape, and the GIF (12507) shows the connective clicking into place. Do them in the lesson's order.

- **Complete the season (sub lesson 1).** The book's nine rainy-season sentences are read with their gaps; learners call out the connective for each gap before the teacher shows the book's printed key. A miss is argued from the meaning: does this gap want a choice, a turn, or a reason?
- **Sort the join-words (sub lesson 2).** Word cards — fi, akkasumas, dabalees, garuu, haata'u malee, yookiin, moo, kanaa achi, kanaaf, malee — are sorted into the book's four work-piles: adders, adversatives, choosers, reason-givers. The piles are checked against the guide's table.
- **Build with every kind (sub lesson 3).** Each learner writes four sentences about the rain: one with an adder, one with an adversative, one with a chooser, one with a subordinating connective — then reads them to a partner, who names each connective's kind and work.
