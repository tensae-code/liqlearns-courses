**How the lesson maps to the book.** Chapter 10 of Afaan Oromoo's Grade 7 guide teaches connectives (*walqabsiistota*) as its grammar: a completing exercise inside a rainy-season text, then a full classified inventory of the connectives. This lesson carries both whole:

| Lesson part | Source |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — The join-words at work | Printed p. 137 — Exercise 9: nine rainy-season sentences to complete with connectives, with the guide's printed key (moo, hin qabdu with malee, kanaa achi, akkuma, kanaaf, yookiin, haata'u malee, yoo, haata'u malee); printed pp. 145–147 — the note: a connective joins word to word, phrase to phrase, clause to clause, sentence to sentence |
| Sub lesson 2 — Joining equals | Printed pp. 145–147 — the coordinating kind (*qofgaleessaa*) divided by its work into adders (*gichisiistuu*: fi, akkasumas, dabalees, kanamalees…), adversatives (*faallessituu*: garuu, haata'u malee, ta'us…), choosers (*filachiistuu*: yookiin, moo, kanaa achi, kanas ta'ee sana…) and reason-givers (*sababaati*: kanaaf, malee, erga akkas ta'ee, kanaaf jecha…) — taught as the book classifies them |
| Sub lesson 3 — Joining a dependent clause | Printed pp. 145–147 — the subordinating kind (*hirkatoo*: akkuma, erga, yoo, osoo, yemmuu, hanga…tti, waan…tti, akka…tti); the bridge to this course's Complex Sentences lesson is the guide's own joining-word list (*hirkisiistota*) from its Chapter 9 grammar, printed pp. 125–126 |
