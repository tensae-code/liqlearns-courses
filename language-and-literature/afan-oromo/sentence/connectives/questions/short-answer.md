# Connectives — short answer

Write a short answer for each. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Complete the book's sentence with its connective, and name the family the guide puts that connective in: "Tapha kubbaa miilaa qopheessanii jiran; ______ kan kubbaa miilaa qofa hin taane."
**Answer: "kanaa achi" — and the guide places kanaa achi with the choosers (filachiistuu).**
*Why it matters: every connective both joins and does a job — naming the family is naming the job.*

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four works of the coordinating connectives.
**Answer: adding (gichisiistuu), opposing (faallessituu), choosing (filachiistuu), and reason-giving (sababaati).**
*Why it matters: the four families are the whole map of the coordinating kind.*

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference between *qofgaleessaa* and *hirkatoo* connectives?
**Answer: qofgaleessaa join equals side by side, so both sides can stand; hirkatoo tie a clause under another one, so the tied clause leans and cannot stand alone.**
*Why it matters: it is the same stand-or-lean test as in Complex Sentences.*
