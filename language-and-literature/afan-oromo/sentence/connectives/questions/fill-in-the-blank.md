# Connectives — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the right word. The number shows which sub lesson each question belongs to.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Ani qabaa malee ati ______." (I have, but you —)
**Answer: hin qabdu** — "…you don't have" — the guide's printed key.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Connectives that join equals, side by side, are called ______.
**Answer: qofgaleessaa** — the coordinating kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The word *fi* belongs to the family called ______, the adders.
**Answer: gichisiistuu** — fi, akkasumas, dabalees and kanamalees pile things of the same kind together.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "______ inni ka'eefi hojii jalqabe." (as soon as)
**Answer: Akkuma** — "As soon as he got up, he started his work" — the guide's Exercise 9 key.
