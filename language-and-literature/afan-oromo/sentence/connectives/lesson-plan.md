# Connectives

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Connectives
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).

**What this lesson teaches:** the join-words — *walqabsiistota* — what they join (a word, a phrase, a clause, a sentence), and the Grade 7 guide's own sorting of them: the two great kinds, coordinating (*qofgaleessaa*) and subordinating (*hirkatoo*), and inside the coordinating kind its four works — adding (*gichisiistuu*), opposing (*faallessituu*), choosing (*filachiistuu*) and reason-giving (*sababaati*). The book's own completing exercise (the rainy-season sentences) is worked through with its printed answers. This lesson stands beside Complex Sentences: its subordinating join-words are the very words that make a clause lean there.

**What the learner will be able to do:**
- Say what a connective joins, and complete the book's nine rainy-season sentences with the right connective.
- Name the two great kinds of connective.
- Sort the common connectives by their work: adders, adversatives, choosers, reason-givers.
- Build sentences with a connective of each kind.

## Sub lessons

3. Joining a dependent clause — the subordinating connectives

**Gate pool:** Sub lesson 2's prove-it check is drawn from sub lesson 1's whole question set (mixed with earlier parts of this lesson), and sub lesson 3's check from the whole set before it — every check at most 5 questions, picked from the whole set, never a fixed list.

The GIF shows two sentence cards sliding together as the gold connective clicks into place between them.

**Level of the language:** Full Afaan Oromoo sentences, with the grammar words glossed in English as they come.
