# Hands-on — Cause and Effect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arrow chains
On paper, draw the four lesson pairs as arrow cards: cause card, arrow, effect card. Shuffle all eight cards, spread them, and rebuild the four chains. Then deal the effect cards only, face up, and race to name each cause.

## Because at home
Walk home in your mind: the fire, the water jar, the animals, the field. Find three real cause-and-effect pairs in your own house and yard and say each as one sentence — because…, so… — to the class.

## Idiom plain-meaning game
The teacher reads a saying from the readings; the class first guesses what its words say one by one, then what the whole saying really means. The gap between the two answers is the lesson: that gap is what makes it a jechamaa.
