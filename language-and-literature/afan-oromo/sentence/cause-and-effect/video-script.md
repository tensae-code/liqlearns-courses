# Video script — Cause and Effect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Why is the harvest good?
The rain was enough. That is the **cause** — sababaa.
The harvest is good. That is the **effect** — bu'aa.

No clean body, no clean yard — sickness comes.
No hard work — troubles grow.

Cause. Effect. One sentence can carry both.
And some sayings — **jechamoota** — mean more than their words!
