# Cause and Effect — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "enough rain → the harvest is good", the cause is —
a) the harvest  b) the rain  c) the year
**Answer: b) the rain** — the rain makes the harvest good; that is the bu'aa, the effect.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Not keeping body and environment clean leads to —
a) a good harvest  b) exposure to disease  c) more rain
**Answer: b) exposure to disease.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A cause can be —
a) only a thing done  b) only a thing missing  c) a thing done or a thing not done
**Answer: c) a thing done or a thing not done** — two of the guide's four causes are nots.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** "Not working hard" has which effect, in the guide's working?
a) problems multiply  b) the country fits life  c) the harvest is good
**Answer: a) problems multiply.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Jechoota ce'umsaa are —
a) plural endings  b) transition words  c) names of rivers
**Answer: b) transition words** — they carry the reader across the sentence.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A gaalee maqaa is —
a) a noun phrase  b) a question mark  c) a whole story
**Answer: a) a noun phrase** — a group of words built on a noun, doing a noun's work.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A jechamaa (idiom) means —
a) exactly the sum of its words  b) one whole meaning, not the sum of its words  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) one whole meaning, not the sum of its words** — the guide gives each idiom's plain meaning.
