# Cause and Effect — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the cause and the effect: "Enough water and air → a country fit for life."
**Answer:** cause — enough water and air; effect — a country fit for life.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two of the lesson's causes are absences — things not done?
**Answer:** cleanliness not kept (body and environment) and work not done (not working hard).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a transition word do in a sentence?
**Answer:** it carries the reader across from one part to the next — from cause over to effect.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the Grade 7 guide handle an idiom in its answers?
**Answer:** it gives the idiom's plain meaning — what the whole saying really means — rather than its word-by-word sum.
