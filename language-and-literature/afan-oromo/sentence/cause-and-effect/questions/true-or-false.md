# Cause and Effect — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The harvest being good is the cause of the rain.
**False** — the arrow points the other way: enough rain is the cause; the good harvest is its effect.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A noun phrase can pack a cause into a sentence.
**True** — gaalee maqaa do a noun's work and often carry the cause or the effect as a bundle.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** To understand an idiom, add up its words one by one.
**False** — the whole saying carries one meaning; the guide gives its plain meaning instead.
