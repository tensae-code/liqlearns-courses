# Hands-on — Joining Sentences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Breath marks
Read a page of any book aloud as a group. Every time the reader breathes at a mark, the class taps the table: one tap for a comma, two for a full stop, a raised hand for a question. Feel how the marks conduct the breathing.

## Joinery bench
Write six plain sentences on six cards. On the bench, join cards two by two into compound sentences — read each join aloud and test both halves: could each stand alone? Then build one leaning join and show the half that cannot.

## Job sort
The teacher says sentences — telling, asking, ordering, exclaiming — in mixed order. Learners hold up the mark card that finishes each: full stop, question mark, exclamation mark. The order-sentences get a firm full stop and a firm voice.
