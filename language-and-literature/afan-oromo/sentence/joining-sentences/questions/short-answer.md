# Joining Sentences — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Punctuate this list sentence: "I carry a pencil a book and a ruler."
**Answer:** "I carry a pencil, a book and a ruler." — commas separate the items of the list.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Join as a compound: "The rain came down." + "The harvest stood ready."
**Answer:** the two whole sentences joined into one compound sentence (himoota dachaa), both halves kept whole.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In "Because the rain was enough, the harvest was good", which part leans?
**Answer:** "Because the rain was enough" — it cannot stand alone; the sentence is himoota ceelaa.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What job does "Did you see it?" do?
**Answer:** it asks — so it ends with a question mark.
