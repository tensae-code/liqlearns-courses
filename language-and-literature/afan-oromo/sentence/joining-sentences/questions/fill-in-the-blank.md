# Joining Sentences — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Fill each blank with the right word or mark.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Did you see it" is asking, so it ends with a ______.
**Answer: question mark.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two whole sentences joined as equals make a ______ sentence (himoota dachaa).
**Answer: compound.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Himoota ______ are joined sentences where one part leans on the other.
**Answer: ceelaa.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** "What a day" does the job of ______, so it takes an exclamation mark.
**Answer: exclaiming.**
