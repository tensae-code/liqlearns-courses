# Video script — Joining Sentences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Words, words, words — no stop! Out of breath!

Stop. **.** The thought ends here.
Ask? **?** The mark asks with you.
A list — one, two, three — commas between!

Two sentences, joined as equals — **himoota dachaa**.
One sentence leaning on another — **himoota ceelaa**.

Tell. Ask. Order. Exclaim.
Every sentence does a job. Give it its mark.
