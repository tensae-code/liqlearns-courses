# Joining Sentences — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the sentence finished and joined: first its marks, then the equal join, then the leaning join, then the jobs sentences do.

## Sub lesson 1 — Punctuation marks — sirna tuqaalee
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: punctuate a question, a three-item list and one line of quoted speech correctly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Compound sentences — himoota dachaa
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: join two plain sentences into one compound sentence, and point to the two halves that could each stand alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — Joined sentences — himoota ceelaa
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell a compound sentence from a joined one: could both halves stand alone, or does one lean?

## Sub lesson 4 — Kinds of sentences by what they do
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort four sentences by their jobs — telling, asking, ordering, exclaiming — and name the mark each one wears.
