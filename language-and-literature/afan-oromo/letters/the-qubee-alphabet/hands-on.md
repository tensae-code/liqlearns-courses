# Hands-on — The Qubee Alphabet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The letter hunt

Walk around your home or classroom with paper and pencil. Every time you find a Qubee letter — on a packet, a book, a sign — write it down. Five letters minimum; ten is a good hunt. Say each letter's name as you write it.

## Big letters, small letters

Write the capitals big on a page. Under each, write its small partner small. The pairs that change shape — A–a, G–g, Q–q, R–r — get a second try underneath.
