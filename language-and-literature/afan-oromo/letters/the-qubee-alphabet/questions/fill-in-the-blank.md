# The Qubee Alphabet — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or sound.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The alphabet of Afaan Oromoo is called ____.
**Answer: Qubee**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A ____ B: the letter that stands between A and C in reading order is CH's neighbour ____.
**Answer: B**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the pair G–g, the small letter is ____.
**Answer: g**
