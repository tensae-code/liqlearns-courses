# The Qubee Alphabet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which row is the Qubee alphabet in the book's order?
a) A B C CH D DH E F G  b) A B C D E F G H  c) CH DH NY A B C D E  d) A B C D CH DH E F
**Answer: a) A B C CH D DH E F G** — CH stands right after C, as its own letter

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many double letters stand in the Qubee row?
a) Five  b) Six  c) Seven  d) Nine
**Answer: c) Seven** — CH, DH, NY, PH, SH, TS and ZY

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The small partner of Q is —
a) g  b) q  c) p  d) o
**Answer: b) q**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pair changes its shape most when it shrinks?
a) B–b  b) D–d  c) A–a  d) S–s
**Answer: c) A–a**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** When you write the letter B, what do you draw first?
a) The two bellies  b) A straight line down  c) A curve at the bottom  d) The cross line
**Answer: b) A straight line down** — then the two round bellies against it
