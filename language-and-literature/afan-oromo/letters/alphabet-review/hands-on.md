# Hands-on — Alphabet Review — Consonants and Vowels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Flash cards

Cut paper into small cards, one letter per card, capitals on one side and small letters on the back. Shuffle, deal, and read — the book's own tool for this review is the flash card (*kaardii libsuu*). A card you miss goes to the bottom of the pile and comes back.

## Sort the cards

Lay the vowel cards in one row and the consonant cards in another. The double letters each get ONE card of their own — two shapes drawn on a single card, because they are one letter.
