# Alphabet Review — Consonants and Vowels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which letter is a vowel?
a) b  b) k  c) o  d) t
**Answer: c) o** — one of the five dubbachiiftuu

**2.** Mend the sequence: A B C __ D DH
a) D  b) CH  c) E  d) B
**Answer: b) CH** — the double letter stands after C

**3.** How many vowels are there?
a) Seven  b) Five  c) Ten  d) Two
**Answer: b) Five** — a e i o u

**4.** Which of these counts as ONE letter in the sorting?
a) s and h apart  b) sh  c) a  d) b c
**Answer: b) sh** — a double letter is one letter, one card

**5.** The consonants are called —
a) dubbachiiftuu  b) bamaqaalee  c) dubbifamaa  d) qubee dachaa
**Answer: c) dubbifamaa**
