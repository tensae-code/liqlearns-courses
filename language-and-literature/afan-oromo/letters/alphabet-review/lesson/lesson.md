# Alphabet Review — Consonants and Vowels

## Do it first

Here is a broken row: **A B C __ D DH E F**. One sign is missing. Say the alphabet in your head until the gap fills itself. And here is a mixed handful: **a, b, e, k, o, t** — can you pick out the vowels? This review lesson does both, until both are easy.

## The alphabet whole, once more

Stand in front of the chart and read the whole row, capitals then small letters:

**A B C CH D DH E F G H I J K L M N NY O P PH Q R S SH T TS U V W X Y Z ZY**

[Picture 9850 — the Qubee alphabet chart]

This is the Grade 4 year's opening review: the alphabet read from flash cards and from the board, letter by letter, until the row is firm. If a letter trips you, that letter — not the whole row — is what you practise.

## Consonants and vowels, sorted

Now sort the row into its two families.

The **vowels** (*dubbachiiftuu*) are five: **a e i o u**.

Everything else is a **consonant** (*dubbifamaa*) — the single letters **b c d f g h j k l m n p q r s t v w x y z** and the seven double letters **ch dh ny ph sh ts zy**, each one letter in the sorting, never two.

Say any letter; ask: does it carry the voice by itself? Then it is a vowel. Does it need a vowel beside it to be said in a word? Then it is a consonant.

## Mending broken sequences

A sequence with a hole in it is finished by saying the row until the gap speaks:

[Picture 9853 — a letter sequence with one letter missing]

**A B C __ D** — the missing letter is the double letter **CH**, standing in its own place after C. **M N __ O** — **NY**. **S __ T TS** — **SH**.

The rule is the same in words: a word with a missing sound is completed by the sound that truly belongs there, and the missing-letter work of the first lessons grows up into the word work of the Reading lessons.

## Say it to your companion

Read the alphabet aloud to your companion, once through, no stopping. Then take turns: one writes a broken sequence — three letters, a gap, two letters — the other mends it and says the whole row of five.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the alphabet in capitals, then write the vowels again underneath in a different colour or underlined twice.
2. Mend these: A B C __ D · M N __ O · P __ Q R · S __ T TS.
3. Write ten letters in a mixed row. Sort them into two columns: dubbachiiftuu and dubbifamaa.
