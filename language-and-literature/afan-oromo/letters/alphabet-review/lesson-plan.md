# Lesson plan — Alphabet Review — Consonants and Vowels

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Letters · **Lesson:** Alphabet Review — Consonants and Vowels
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Letters (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the alphabet reviewed whole — consonants (dubbifamaa) told from vowels (dubbachiiftuu), and letter sequences completed with their missing letter.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the three Letters lessons before it; it is the review that proves them.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Qubee Alphabet; Vowels — Short and Long; Double Letters. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read the whole alphabet from the chart without stopping.
2. Tell consonants (dubbifamaa) from vowels (dubbachiiftuu) in any mixed row.
3. Complete a letter sequence with its missing letter.
4. Treat every double letter as one letter when you sort and count.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner completes the broken row A B C __ D DH E F and picks the vowels from a mixed handful.
2. **The explanation** — the alphabet read whole from the chart; consonants sorted from vowels; sequences completed with their missing letter (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9850]: a picture of the complete Qubee alphabet chart, each capital beside its small letter — the student reads the whole row twice — once fast, once pointing at each letter.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9853]: a picture of a letter sequence card — A B C, then an empty box with a question mark, then D DH E — the gap waiting for its letter — the student says the sequence aloud and names the missing letter before turning the card over in the mind.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading a mixed handful of letters — vowels and consonants shuffled — the student calls 'vowel' or 'consonant' for each letter heard.
3. **Say it to your companion** — read the alphabet to each other, then break sequences for each other to mend.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, true or false, matching, ordering, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — read the chart, sort twelve letters, and mend three broken sequences.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
