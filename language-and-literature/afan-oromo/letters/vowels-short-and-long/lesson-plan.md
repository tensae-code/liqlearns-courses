# Lesson plan — Vowels — Short and Long

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Letters · **Lesson:** Vowels — Short and Long
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Letters (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the five vowels (dubbachiiftuu) a e i o u, and how each one sounds short and long — Ca against Caa.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Qubee Alphabet — the learner can read the letters; now the vowel sounds inside them come alive.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The five vowels** — the vowels a e i o u (dubbachiiftuu) picked out from the consonants (dubbifamaa).
2. **Short syllables** — a consonant with each short vowel: Ca Ce Ci Co Cu, read across the chart.
3. **Long syllables — the vowel stretches** — the same syllables with the vowel doubled and held: Caa Cee Cii Coo Cuu; short against long side by side.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Qubee Alphabet — the learner reads the letters this lesson puts to work. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five vowels — a e i o u — and call them by their book name, dubbachiiftuu.
2. Read short syllables: Ca Ce Ci Co Cu for any consonant.
3. Read long syllables: Caa Cee Cii Coo Cuu, holding the sound.
4. Hear and write the difference: one vowel letter short, two vowel letters long.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner says Ca and Caa and feels the long one take longer.
2. **The explanation** — the five vowels named (dubbachiiftuu); short syllables Ca Ce Ci Co Cu; long syllables Caa Cee Cii Coo Cuu, with word pairs whose meaning turns on length (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9851]: a picture of a vowel chart: the short row Ca Ce Ci Co Cu above the long row Caa Cee Cii Coo Cuu, the long vowels doubled and underlined — the student reads across the short row, then across the long row, holding the long sounds.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9898]: a picture of one syllable, Ca, on screen; the a stretches into aa and the voice bar under it grows twice as long — the student says Ca as it shows, then stretches the voice with the picture to Caa — three times.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading short and long pairs — Ca/Caa, Te/Tee, Mi/Mii — the student points to short or long on the chart for each pair heard.
3. **Say it to your companion** — trade short and long syllables; the listener says 'short' or 'long' for each.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, true or false, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — read the chart rows, write lafa and laafaa, and say three short/long pairs aloud.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
