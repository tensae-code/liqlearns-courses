# Hands-on — Vowels — Short and Long

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Clap the length

Say syllables with a partner or a family member. Short syllable — one sharp clap. Long syllable — hands slide apart slowly while you hold the sound, like stretching an elastic band. Ten syllables, mixed up.

## Stretch the elastic

Hold a scarf, a belt or a piece of string loosely. For a short vowel, give it one small tug. For a long vowel, pull it wide and hold while the sound lasts. Your hands learn what your ear hears.
