# Video script — Vowels — Short and Long

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five letters are the voice letters. **a. e. i. o. u.**
Short: **Ca. Ce. Ci. Co. Cu.** Quick. Clean. Done.
Long: **Caa. Cee. Cii. Coo. Cuu.** Hold it. Stretch it.
**Lafa** — land. **Laafaa** — soft. Hear it? Short, long.
One letter — short. Two letters — long. Say it with me.
