# Vowels — Short and Long — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which row holds only vowels?
a) b c d f g  b) a e i o u  c) k l m n p  d) ch dh ny sh ts
**Answer: b) a e i o u** — the five dubbachiiftuu

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The vowels are called —
a) dubbifamaa  b) dubbachiiftuu  c) qubee dachaa  d) bamaqaalee
**Answer: b) dubbachiiftuu** — dubbifamaa is the consonants' name

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Read the row: Ba Be Bi Bo Bu. These syllables are —
a) Long  b) Short  c) Double letters  d) Names
**Answer: b) Short** — one vowel letter each, one beat each

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which syllable is long?
a) Ta  b) Te  c) Tii  d) Tu
**Answer: c) Tii** — the vowel is doubled and held

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How is a long vowel written?
a) With a capital letter  b) With the vowel written twice  c) With a line over it  d) With two consonants
**Answer: b) With the vowel written twice** — aa, ee, ii, oo, uu
