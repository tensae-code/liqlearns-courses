# Vowels — Short and Long — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the short row for the consonant m.
**Answer: Ma Me Mi Mo Mu**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the long row for the consonant t.
**Answer: Taa Tee Tii Too Tuu**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Say lafa and laafaa. Which one means 'soft'?
**Answer: Laafaa — the long one**
