# Video script — Double Letters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two shapes. One sound. These are the double letters.
**ch. dh. ny. ph. sh. ts. zy.** Seven of them.
**Dh — Dhadhaa.** Butter.
**Ny — Nyaata.** Food.
**Sh — Shan.** Five.
Never split them. One letter, one sound. Say them with me.
