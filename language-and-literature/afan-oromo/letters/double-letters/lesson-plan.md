# Lesson plan — Double Letters

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Letters · **Lesson:** Double Letters
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Letters (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the seven double letters (qubee dachaa) — ch, dh, ny, ph, sh, ts, zy — two letter-shapes making one sound, each matched to the book's own word.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Qubee Alphabet and Vowels — Short and Long: the learner reads single letters and syllables; now two shapes that walk as one.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The seven double letters** — ch, dh, ny, sh, ph, ts, zy named as qubee dachaa — identified and read as single sounds.
2. **Double letters in words** — each digraph matched to the Grade 4 guide's own word: Ch Chaappaa, Dh Dhadhaa, Ny Nyaata, Ph Buuphaa, Sh Shan, Ts Tsahaay, Zy Televizyiinii.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Qubee Alphabet; Vowels — Short and Long. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the seven double letters: ch, dh, ny, ph, sh, ts, zy.
2. Read each digraph as one sound, never as two letters.
3. Match each digraph to its book word: Ch Chaappaa, Dh Dhadhaa, Ny Nyaata, Ph Buuphaa, Sh Shan, Ts Tsahaay, Zy Televizyiinii.
4. Complete a word that is missing its double letter.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner reads Shan, then tries s+h separately and hears the word break.
2. **The explanation** — the seven digraphs named and read as single sounds; each matched to the book's own word (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9852]: a picture of seven cards for the double letters — ch, dh, ny, ph, sh, ts, zy — each card showing the two shapes joined by a link — the student reads each card as ONE sound, never as two letters.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9899]: a picture of the letters c and h sliding together until they touch and stand as one letter, ch, with its word Chaappaa under it — the student says c, then h, then — as they join — the one sound ch.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the seven digraph words slowly — Chaappaa, Dhadhaa, Nyaata, Buuphaa, Shan, Tsahaay, Televizyiinii — the student repeats each word and names its double letter.
3. **Say it to your companion** — name a digraph; your companion answers with its word, and back again.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, fill in the blank, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write the seven digraphs, match each to its word, and read the words aloud.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
