# Hands-on — Double Letters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Linked cards

Write c and h on two small pieces of paper. Slide them together on the table until they touch and read ch — one sound. Do the same for s+h and t+s. Your hands learn the join before your eye speeds up.

## Butter and food at home

At the next meal, look for dhadhaa (butter) and nyaata (food) in your own kitchen. Say both words, naming the double letter at the front of each.
