# Hands-on — Letter, Picture and Word Matching

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make the card set

On seven pieces of paper write the big letters A B C D E F G. On seven more, draw or write the seven words: Abaaboo, Baabura, Cumboo, Daakiyyee, Eelee, Filaa, Gamoo. Mix the word cards, then lay each under its true letter.

## First-sound walk

Walk around your home naming things you see. For each thing, say its Afaan Oromoo word if you know it from this course — **mana** (house), **buna** (coffee), **saree** (dog) — and say its starting letter out loud.
