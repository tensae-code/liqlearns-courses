# Letter, Picture and Word Matching

## Do it first

Here are three starting letters and three words in a muddle: **A — B — C**, and **Cumboo, Baabura, Abaaboo**. One word starts with the train sound B… no, listen: **Baabura** — hear the B at its front? Match each word to its starting letter before you read on. This lesson is that game, played with the book's own seven words.

## The seven pairs

The book teaches letters by tying each one to a picture and a word. Here are its seven pairs — learn them as triples: the letter, the thing, the word.

- **A — Abaaboo** — a flower
- **B — Baabura** — a train
- **C — Cumboo**
- **D — Daakiyyee** — a duck
- **E — Eelee** — an electric frying pan
- **F — Filaa** — a comb
- **G — Gamoo** — a building

[Picture 9854 — the seven letter, picture and word cards]

Say each triple the same way every time: letter first, then the word — **A, Abaaboo. B, Baabura.** The letter you say first is the letter the word starts with. That is the whole skill: hear the first sound, name its letter, and the word has a home.

## Reading the words whole

Now cover the letters and read only the words: **Abaaboo, Baabura, Cumboo, Daakiyyee, Eelee, Filaa, Gamoo**.

Read them by their syllables when one is long: **Baa-bu-ra. Daa-kiy-yee. Ee-lee. Ga-moo.** Every word you have met in this course is built the same way — letters, then syllables, then the whole word standing on its feet.

## Say it to your companion

Shuffle seven letter cards and seven word cards face down. Turn two up at a time: a letter and a word that starts with it is a match you keep. Play until the table is empty, saying each triple aloud as you win it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the seven words in a column. Beside each, write its starting letter big.
2. Draw one of the seven things — the flower, the duck or the comb — and write its word and letter under your drawing.
3. Find three more words in this course that start with B, D or G, and write each under its letter.
