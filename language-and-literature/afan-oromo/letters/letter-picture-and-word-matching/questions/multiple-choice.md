# Letter, Picture and Word Matching — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which word starts with the letter B?
a) Abaaboo  b) Baabura  c) Cumboo  d) Gamoo
**Answer: b) Baabura** — the train

**2.** Abaaboo — a flower — starts with —
a) B  b) C  c) A  d) G
**Answer: c) A**

**3.** The duck, Daakiyyee, is filed under —
a) E  b) D  c) F  d) C
**Answer: b) D**

**4.** Which pair is right?
a) E — Filaa  b) F — Filaa  c) G — Eelee  d) C — Gamoo
**Answer: b) F — Filaa** — Filaa, a comb, starts with F

**5.** Gamoo, a building, starts with the letter —
a) F  b) G  c) A  d) D
**Answer: b) G** — the first sound of Gamoo
