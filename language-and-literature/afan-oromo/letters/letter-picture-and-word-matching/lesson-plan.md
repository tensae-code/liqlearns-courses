# Lesson plan — Letter, Picture and Word Matching

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Letters · **Lesson:** Letter, Picture and Word Matching
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Letters (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** matching a starting letter to its picture and its word — A to Abaaboo, B to Baabura, and five more of the book's own pairs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Qubee Alphabet — the learner knows the letters; now each letter earns a word.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and

shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Qubee Alphabet. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Match A to Abaaboo (flower) and B to Baabura (train).
2. Match C to Cumboo, D to Daakiyyee (duck), E to Eelee (electric frying pan).
3. Match F to Filaa (comb) and G to Gamoo (building).
4. Say the rule: a word is filed by its first letter, the first sound you hear.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Scissors if you make the cards for the hands-on game.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner matches A, B and C to Cumboo, Baabura and Abaaboo by their starting sounds.
2. **The explanation** — the book's seven letter–picture–word pairs, matched and read; the starting-letter rule spoken aloud (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9854]: a picture of seven picture-word cards in a row: a train (Baabura), the word Cumboo, a flower (Abaaboo), an electric frying pan (Eelee), a duck (Daakiyyee), a tall building (Gamoo) and a comb (Filaa), each card with its big starting letter — the student points to each card, says the letter, then reads the word.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the seven words slowly, letter first: 'A — Abaaboo' — the student says the letter before the voice says the word.
3. **Say it to your companion** — deal the seven pairs as a memory game — letter card, picture card, word card.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple choice, short answer, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — match the seven pairs from memory and write each word under its letter.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread mid-lesson → go back to the Do it first at the top of lesson/lesson.md and do it again with real things in hand; the explanation lands the second time.
- Reads the Afaan Oromoo words but cannot say what they mean → the English gloss beside each word set is the bridge; say word, gloss, word again, in that order, three times.
