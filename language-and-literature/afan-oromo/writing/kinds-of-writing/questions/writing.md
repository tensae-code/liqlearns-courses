# Kinds of Writing — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a three-paragraph narrative of a real journey or happening, with time markers.
**A good answer:** three paragraphs, the order carried by its markers — ago, later, then, last.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a descriptive paragraph of a person you know.
**A good answer:** features chosen exactly — face, hair, build, dress, manner — so a stranger could recognise them.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write your own life story so far in one paragraph.
**A good answer:** parents, where born, how you grew, where you are now — in that order.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write a persuasive paragraph: learners should read every day.
**A good answer:** at least two reasons standing in a row, and the ask last.
