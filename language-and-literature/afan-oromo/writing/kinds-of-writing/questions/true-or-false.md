# Kinds of Writing — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A narrative may scramble time if its paragraphs are neat.
**False** — its time markers carry an order; scrambling breaks the kind.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A biography can only be written by the person whose life it is.
**False** — the guide's two kinds include another person's life story, written by someone else.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** In the model, the ask comes before the reasons.
**False** — reasons stand first (health, money, community); the ask lands last.
