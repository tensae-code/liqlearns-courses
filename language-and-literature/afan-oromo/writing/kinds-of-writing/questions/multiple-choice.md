# Kinds of Writing — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barruu seenessaa is —
a) descriptive writing  b) narrative writing  c) a letter
**Answer: b) narrative writing** — a happening or life told in order.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The model's narrative is the life of —
a) Mooneet  b) Boontuu  c) Jireenyaa
**Answer: c) Jireenyaa** — the tragic life the guide prints.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Barruu addeessaa describes —
a) a person  b) a recipe  c) a debate
**Answer: a) a person** — the model subject is Mooneet.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The personal-data table names Boontuu's —
a) age 15, grade 7, born in Finfinnee  b) age 20, grade 12  c) village only
**Answer: a) age 15, grade 7, born in Finfinnee** — father Irranaa Buusii, mother Magartuu Tolaa.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The two kinds of seenaa jireenyaa are —
a) long and short  b) your own life (written by you) and another's life (written by someone)  c) true and false
**Answer: b) your own life and another's life.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide's biography checklist begins with —
a) spelling  b) order of ideas  c) handwriting
**Answer: b) order of ideas** — then punctuation, word choice, spelling, legible handwriting.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Barruu amansiisaa works to —
a) describe  b) convince  c) narrate
**Answer: b) convince** — persuasive writing.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The model's argument is against —
a) group study  b) smoking tobacco  c) coffee
**Answer: b) smoking tobacco** — health, money, community, then the ask.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The book's poem task (Exercise 7) asks you to write a walaloo —
a) with no figures of speech at all
b) using figures of speech (malleen dubbii), then present it and take the teacher's feedback
c) by copying a printed poem exactly
d) that nobody is allowed to hear
**Answer: b)** — the poem is written with the figures, presented aloud, and answered with feedback.
