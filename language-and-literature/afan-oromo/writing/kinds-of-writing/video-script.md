# Video script — Kinds of Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
Four doors!

Door one — a story: **barruu seenessaa**. What happened, in order!
Door two — a person you can see: **barruu addeessaa**. Describe!
Door three — a life: **seenaa jireenyaa**. Yours — or another's!
Door four — change a mind: **barruu amansiisaa**. Reasons — then the ask!

Four kinds of writing. You can write them all!
