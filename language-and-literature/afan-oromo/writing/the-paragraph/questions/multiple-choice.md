# The Paragraph — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A keeyyata (paragraph) is —
a) the longest unit of writing  b) the smallest unit of writing carrying one idea  c) a single word
**Answer: b) the smallest unit of writing carrying one idea.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hima ijoo is —
a) the concluding sentence  b) a supporting sentence  c) the topic sentence
**Answer: c) the topic sentence** — the sentence that carries the idea.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A paragraph's three parts are —
a) title, story, song  b) topic sentence, supporting sentences, concluding sentence  c) beginning, middle, moral
**Answer: b) topic sentence, supporting sentences, concluding sentence.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The topic sentence may stand —
a) only at the start  b) at the start, middle, end, or start-and-end  c) only at the end
**Answer: b) at the start, middle, end, or start-and-end.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's model, the elephants greet each other by —
a) trumpeting  b) entwining trunks  c) stamping
**Answer: b) entwining trunks** — and they mourn and bury their dead, and draw water with their trunks.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A three-paragraph text on one topic holds —
a) one idea in all three  b) three ideas, one per paragraph  c) no ideas
**Answer: b) three ideas, one per paragraph** — the paragraph rule scales without changing.
