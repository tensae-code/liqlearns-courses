# The Paragraph — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A paragraph may carry two ideas if the sentences are good.
**False** — by the guide's definition, one paragraph carries one idea; two ideas are two paragraphs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The topic sentence must always stand first.
**False** — it may stand at the start, middle, end, or start-and-end.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Himoota tajaajila are functional sentences — sentences that do their jobs.
**True.**
