# Video script — The Paragraph

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
One idea. One block. A **paragraph** — keeyyata!

Its heart: the **topic sentence** — hima ijoo!
Helpers around it — supporting sentences!
Last: the concluding sentence. The idea lands.

Where does the heart stand? At the start! In the middle!
At the end! Or start — and end again!
You choose. You are the writer!
