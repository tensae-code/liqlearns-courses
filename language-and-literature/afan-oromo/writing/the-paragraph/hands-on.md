# Hands-on — The Paragraph

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cut the wall
A long text is written as one wall on a big sheet. Learners read it with scissors in hand and cut it where the ideas change, pinning the pieces as separate paragraphs. Each cut must be defended: "the idea changes here."

## Topic-sentence hunt
Paragraphs from the class's own writing are pinned around the room, their topic sentences in different standings. Learners hunt in pairs, marking each hima ijoo and naming its standing — start, middle, end, start-and-end — on a tally sheet.

## Elephant watch
From the guide's model as told in the lesson — elephants greeting with entwined trunks, mourning their dead, drawing water — pairs write their own one-idea paragraph about an animal they know, then swap and hunt each other's three parts.
