# Video script — Building Sentences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Words lying flat: sareen — foon — nyaatti.
Stand them up! **Sareen foon nyaatti.** The dog eats meat!

Three columns — pick, pick, pick: a sentence!
A phrase — give it a verb: a sentence!
A verb — build around it: a sentence!

You are a builder. Build!
