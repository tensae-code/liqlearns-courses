# Lesson plan — Building Sentences

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Writing · **Lesson:** Building Sentences
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Writing (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a sentence is built, not found: from three word columns, from word groups, from phrases (gaalee → hima), around a verb, and by completing a started sentence — the Grade 4 guide's five building benches.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Sentence unit — the learner knows persons, plurals, verb forms and joins. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **From three word columns** — sentence building from three word columns (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 12 answer key): "Borsumaan barataa cimaadha" (Borsumaa is a strong student), "Faayoon uffata miiccite" (Faayoo washed clothes).
2. **From word groups** — sentence-building from word cards/word groups (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 10 answer key): "Furtuu balbalaa qabdi", "Sareen foon nyaatti", "Re'een baala nyaatti", "Lukkuun dheedhii guurratti".
3. **From phrases — gaalee into hima** — phrases → sentences (gaalee → hima), with favourite dishes named as phrases first (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 9 answer key): "Maatii kabajuun nurraa eegama" — respecting family is expected of us.
4. **Around a verb** — building sentences from verbs; completing sentences (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 4 answer keys): "Boontuun dhufte", "Boonaa konkolaataa bite".
5. **Completing sentences** — completing sentences with the missing subject word, and completing started sentences (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 2 and Ch. 9 answer keys): "…nyaatte. Muuzii nyaatte."

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Sentence lessons — Persons and Pronouns and Joining Sentences above all. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Build sentences from three word columns: "Borsumaan barataa cimaadha", "Faayoon uffata miiccite".
2. Build sentences from word groups and word cards: "Sareen foon nyaatti", "Re'een baala nyaatti".
3. Grow a phrase (gaalee) into a sentence (hima): "Maatii kabajuun nurraa eegama".
4. Build a sentence around a verb, and complete a started sentence with its missing subject word.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Word cards help the column work — paper strips with words on them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Builds a pile, not a sentence → check the three legs: doer, thing, doing — and the verb's agreement with the doer. A sentence missing a leg does not stand.
- Phrase stays a phrase → a gaalee has no verb of its own. Ask: what does it do, or what is done to it? The answer is its verb; add a doer and it is a hima.
- Completion doesn't agree → read the started half aloud and listen to its verb ending before choosing the subject: the ending has already chosen the person for you.
