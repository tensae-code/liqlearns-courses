# Building Sentences — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Columns: (Borsumaan / Faayoon) — (barataa / uffata) — (cimaadha / miiccite). Write the two sentences the columns build.
**A good answer:** Borsumaan barataa cimaadha. Faayoon uffata miiccite. — each verb agreeing with its doer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Order into sentences: (a) nyaatti / sareen / foon; (b) baala / re'een / nyaatti.
**A good answer:** (a) Sareen foon nyaatti. (b) Re'een baala nyaatti. — doer, thing, doing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Grow into a sentence: ittoo lukkuu (chicken stew).
**A good answer:** the phrase given a doer and a verb — e.g. a sentence saying who eats or cooks the chicken stew — standing as a full hima.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Build a sentence around the verb dhufte (came, she).
**A good answer:** a sentence with a she-doer the form agrees with — Boontuun dhufte is the guide's own.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Complete: "Isheen ______" with dhufte or dhufan — and one more started sentence of your own, completed.
**A good answer:** Isheen dhufte (she came — the -te form agrees); the second completion's verb also agreeing with its subject.
