# Building Sentences — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Faayoon uffata miiccite" means —
a) Faayoo bought clothes  b) Faayoo washed clothes  c) Faayoo folded money
**Answer: b) Faayoo washed clothes** — built from the three columns.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Re'een baala nyaatti" — the goat eats —
a) meat  b) leaves  c) grain
**Answer: b) leaves** — Sareen foon nyaatti is the dog and meat.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Lukkuun dheedhii guurratti" — the hen —
a) gathers grain  b) holds the door  c) eats leaves
**Answer: a) gathers grain.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A gaalee (phrase) has no —
a) words  b) verb of its own  c) meaning
**Answer: b) verb of its own** — give it a verb and a doer and it becomes a hima, a sentence.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ittoo misiraa is —
a) chicken stew  b) meat stew  c) lentil stew
**Answer: c) lentil stew** — ittoo lukkuu is chicken, ittoo foonii is meat.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** "Boonaa konkolaataa bite" — Boonaa —
a) sold a car  b) bought a car  c) washed a car
**Answer: b) bought a car** — the sentence is built around the verb bite.

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** A completion is right only when —
a) it is long  b) its verb agrees with the subject supplied  c) it uses new words
**Answer: b) its verb agrees with the subject supplied.**
