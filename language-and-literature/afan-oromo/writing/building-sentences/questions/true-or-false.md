# Building Sentences — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In column building, any verb fits any doer.
**False** — the verb must agree with its doer: Faayoon… miiccite, the she-form.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A phrase and a sentence are the same thing.
**False** — a gaalee (phrase) has no verb of its own; a hima (sentence) stands whole.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** A started sentence can be completed without reading what already stands.
**False** — the standing half, and its verb ending, decide what completion is right.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In "Boontuun dhufte", the verb dhufte was chosen because its doer is a she.
**True** — building around a verb still obeys agreement: the form must fit the doer supplied.
