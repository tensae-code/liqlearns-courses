# Hands-on — Building Sentences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The column bench
Pin three columns of word strips on the wall. Learners come up one at a time, pick one strip from each column, pin them in a row and read the sentence aloud. The class gives the agreement check before the next builder steps up.

## Card building
Each pair gets a shuffled set of word cards from the guide's own sentences — Furtuu balbalaa qabdi, Re'een baala nyaatti. Race, kindly: first pair to lay all four sentences and read them aloud wins the round; then the cards are re-shuffled for new sentences the pairs invent.

## Complete the wall
Started sentences are pinned around the room, each missing its subject or its ending. Learners walk the wall with pencils, completing each one in writing as they pass, and initial their completions. The class reads the finished wall together.
