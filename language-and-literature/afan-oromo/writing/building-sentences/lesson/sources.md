Honesty: there is no Afan Oromo student book in hand for any grade — no student book exists in the library records. This lesson is built from teacher-guide evidence only: the guides' contents, objectives, syllabus tables and their full answer keys (the guides print the answers to the student book's exercises, which is the evidence of what learners do). Nothing here is presented as pupil-book content, and no student-book wording has been invented. Framing: the Grade 4 guide is a second-language guide; every built sentence in this lesson is its printed answer key, copied exactly (pp. 70, 58, 52, 21–22, 11). The Ch. 2 completion key is quoted in the placements in short form ("…nyaatte. Muuzii nyaatte."); the lesson teaches the completion act from it without expanding the fragment into invented sentences.
