# Lesson plan — First Written Pieces

**Course:** Afan Oromo · **Title:** Writing · **Lesson:** First Written Pieces
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Writing (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first things a writer writes whole: the greeting words, a four-sentence story about a friend, a short text introducing yourself, sentences about eating and cutting food, and the spelled names of relatives.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Building Sentences — the learner already builds sentences that stand. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **First words — writing the greetings** — the greeting chapter's second half: learners read and write the greeting words (Grade 3 guide, Ch. 1).
2. **A four-sentence story about a friend** — writing a friend's story (seenaa hiriyyaa) in not more than four sentences (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 7).
3. **Introducing yourself in a short text** — writing a short text introducing yourself — and reading it aloud (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 6).
4. **Sentences about eating and cutting food** — describing food actions in sentences (Grade 3 guide, Ch. 9 answer key): "buddeena nyaataa jira" (is eating injera), "foon murataa jira" (is cutting meat).
5. **Spelling names of relatives** — relatives' names (maqaa firoota) spelled and written (Grade 4 guide, Ch. 5 answer keys).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is
the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Building Sentences — every piece here is sentences stood up together. Recommended, never forced.
- Greetings and Introducing Yourself in the Speaking unit are these pieces' spoken twins.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Write the greeting words from the speaking work (first words).
2. Write a friend's story (seenaa hiriyyaa) in not more than four sentences.
3. Write a short text introducing yourself.
4. Write sentences about eating and cutting food: "buddeena nyaataa jira", "foon murataa jira".
5. Spell and write the names of your relatives (maqaa firoota).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Friend story runs to ten sentences → the frame is four, and the frame is the teaching. Find the placing, the happening, the close; the rest is cut, kindly but completely.
- Introduction text pads with extras → the parts are named: name, mother's and father's names, age, one telling. Anything else waits for a later piece.
- Relatives' names spelled by ear → names are not sounded out fresh; they are spelled as the family spells them. Ask home, write, check.
