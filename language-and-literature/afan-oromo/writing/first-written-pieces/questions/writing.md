# First Written Pieces — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the greeting words for the three divisions of the day.
**A good answer:** the greeting words of ganama, guyyaa and galgala written whole and spelled carefully.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a friend's story in not more than four sentences.
**A good answer:** four sentences or fewer — a placing, a happening, a close — about a real friend.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write your introduction as a short text.
**A good answer:** name, mother's and father's names, age and one telling, in full sentences, in order.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write two sentences about a meal: one eating, one cutting.
**A good answer:** the key's shape — …nyaataa jira for the eating, …murataa jira for the cutting.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write six relatives' names, spelled as your family spells them.
**A good answer:** six true names, exact spellings.
