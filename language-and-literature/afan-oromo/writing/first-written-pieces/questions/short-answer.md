**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What three beats must the four sentences hold?
**Answer:** a placing (who/where the friend is), a happening, and a close.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the guide join the introduction's writing to reading aloud?
**Answer:** the written text steadies the speaking, and the reading proves the writing — the two skills check each other.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Give the cutting sentence from the key.
**Answer:** foon murataa jira — (he/she) is cutting meat.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which chapter's work ends with reading and writing the greeting words?
**Answer:** the Grade 3 guide's first chapter — Nagaa Walgaafachuu, Greeting Each Other.
