# First Written Pieces — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 3 greeting chapter ends with learners —
a) singing the months  b) reading and writing the greeting words  c) counting to twenty
**Answer: b) reading and writing the greeting words.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Seenaa hiriyyaa is —
a) a friend's story  b) a song  c) a letter to a teacher
**Answer: a) a friend's story.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The friend story's frame is —
a) at least ten sentences  b) not more than four sentences  c) exactly one sentence
**Answer: b) not more than four sentences.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The introduction text, once written, is —
a) thrown away  b) read aloud  c) only drawn
**Answer: b) read aloud** — the guide joins the writing and the speaking.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** "Foon murataa jira" means —
a) is eating injera  b) is cutting meat  c) is drinking coffee
**Answer: b) is cutting meat** — buddeena nyaataa jira is the eating one.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Maqaa firoota are —
a) names of relatives  b) names of foods  c) names of days
**Answer: a) names of relatives** — spelled and written in this chapter's work.
