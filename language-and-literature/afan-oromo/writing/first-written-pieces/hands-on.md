# Hands-on — First Written Pieces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The four-sentence challenge
Pairs write friend stories, then swap and count with tallies on the page: any story over four sentences goes back for cutting. Cutting is the skill — the class reads the best cut aloud and names what was kept and what was let go.

## Name wall
Each learner writes their relatives' names on cards, spelled with care, and pins them on a family wall in the classroom — grouped by family. The wall is checked by its owners: a name misspelled is taken down, corrected by its owner, and pinned again.

## A meal, watched and written
At a real or mimed meal — injera torn, meat cut, coffee poured — learners write happening-now sentences live, as each action happens, in the key's shape: nyaataa jira, murataa jira. The writing must keep up with the eating.
