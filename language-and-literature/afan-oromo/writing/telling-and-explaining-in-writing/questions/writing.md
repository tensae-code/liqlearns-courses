# Telling and Explaining in Writing — writing

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Write what each prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Narrate a small real event in five sentences, in order, in the present.
**A good answer:** five steps, first to last, no step out of place, the present forms carrying it alive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write an explanatory paragraph of steps for making tea or washing clothes.
**A good answer:** every step in order, none missing that the doing needs — a reader could do it from your paragraph.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Make a gumee for "The Benefits of Group Study" with three branches, then write one branch's paragraph.
**A good answer:** an outline of heads and points only (no sentences), then a paragraph that grows from one branch and stays on it.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Continue the hyena opening in four sentences.
**A good answer:** the tale's road kept — the hiding, the hunters, the trick turning — heading for the close such tales earn.
