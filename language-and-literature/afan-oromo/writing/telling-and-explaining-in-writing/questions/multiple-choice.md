# Telling and Explaining in Writing — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Taatee seenessuu means —
a) outlining a topic  b) narrating an event  c) spelling names
**Answer: b) narrating an event** — told in its own order.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The grammar paired with narrating in the chapter is —
a) plural nouns  b) hennaa ammeennaa — present/habitual time forms  c) punctuation
**Answer: b) hennaa ammeennaa.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A keeyyata ibsaa is —
a) an explanatory paragraph  b) a letter  c) a debate
**Answer: a) an explanatory paragraph.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The coffee model explains by —
a) reasons  b) steps in order  c) questions
**Answer: b) steps in order** — the group-study model explains by reasons.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the group-study model, one benefit is —
a) boiling water  b) exchanging ideas  c) washing cups
**Answer: b) exchanging ideas** — the cups belong to the coffee steps.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A gumee is —
a) a final text  b) an outline made before writing  c) a kind of tale
**Answer: b) an outline made before writing.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The worked outline's topic, Faayidaa Barnootaa, means —
a) The Benefits of Education  b) The Benefits of Family Planning  c) The Awash River
**Answer: a) The Benefits of Education.**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In the folk-tale opening, the fleeing hyena begs —
a) the two hunters  b) a man driving a donkey, to hide it on the donkey  c) the cow's owner
**Answer: b) a man driving a donkey, to hide it on the donkey.**
