# Telling and Explaining in Writing — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A narration may put the last step first if the sentences are fine.
**False** — order is the whole law of narrating; an event out of order is mis-told.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A gumee is written in full sentences.
**False** — it holds heads and points only; sentences belong to the text built from it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the worked outline, one branch of the text becomes one paragraph.
**True** — the three-paragraph text is built branch by branch.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A continued folk tale may leave its opening's road and become a market list.
**False** — continuing keeps the tale's road and order, heading for the close such tales earn.
