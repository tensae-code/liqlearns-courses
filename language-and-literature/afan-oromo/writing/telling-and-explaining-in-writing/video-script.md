# Video script — Telling and Explaining in Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---
An event! Tell it **in order**. First, then, last. **Taatee seenessuu.**

Explaining? Two roads!
Steps — like brewing coffee: roast, pound, boil, pour!
Reasons — like group study: ideas, understanding, sharing!

But first — the **gumee**! The outline! The tree before the leaves.
Plan the branches. Then write. The text cannot wander!
