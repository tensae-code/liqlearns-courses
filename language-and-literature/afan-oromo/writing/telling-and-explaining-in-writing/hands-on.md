# Hands-on — Telling and Explaining in Writing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Brew it on paper
The class brews coffee in words before anyone touches a bean: each learner writes one step of the brewing on a card, the cards are laid in order on the floor and read as one paragraph, and missing steps (who washed the cups?) are caught by the readers who would have to drink.

## Gumee tree
Draw the outline as a real tree on a big sheet: trunk = the topic, branches = the gumee heads, twigs = their points. Learners add twigs for a new topic, then each takes one branch and writes its paragraph standing at the tree.

## Tale relay
The folk-tale opening is read aloud. Learners continue it in a relay — each writes two sentences and passes the page — with one rule: read everything before your part, and keep the road. The finished tale is read aloud whole.
