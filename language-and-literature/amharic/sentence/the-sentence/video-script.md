# Video script — The Sentence and Its Marks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words together can carry one whole idea.
That is a **sentence** — ዓረፍተነገር.

**ኃይለኛ ዝናብ ዘነበ።**
Heavy rain fell. The idea is whole. The mark closes it: **።**

Now the same words — but asking:
**አባትህ መጣ?**
Did your father come? A question ends with **?**

A strong feeling ends with **!**
። — telling. ? — asking. ! — strong feeling.

Now join two sentences.
"I want to finish first." **1ኛ መወጣት እፈልጋለሁ።**
"I study all the time." **ሁልጊዜ አጠናለሁ።**
Join them with **ስለምፈልግ** — because I want:
**1ኛ መወጣት ስለምፈልግ ሁልጊዜ አጠናለሁ።**
One sentence now. Two verbs inside — that makes it **ውስብስብ**, complex.

Your turn. One idea. One mark. Say it whole.

---

Now take a sentence apart.
**ከድር ከትምህርትቤት መጣ።**
Who is it about? **ከድር** — the subject, **ባለቤት**, at the start.
What is said? **መጣ** — the predicate, **ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ**, at the end.

A sentence can do four things.
It states: **ልጁ ብዙ ሥራ ሠራ።** — ሀተታዊ.
It asks: **ከሥራ ለምን ቀረሽ?** — ጥያቄያዊ.
It orders: **የቤት ሥራችሁን ሥሩ።** — ትዕዛዛዊ.
It exclaims: **ነፍሴ ምንጥቅ!** — አጋናኝ.

It can say yes: **ልጁ መጣ።** — አዎንታዊ.
It can say no: **ልጁ አልመጣም።** — አሉታዊ. See the **አል- … -ም** around the verb.

And it can say what a thing is:
**እነዚህ ዋልያዎች ናቸው።**
Pointer — complement — being-verb. **ናቸው** means *are*.

One verb alone: **ነጠላ**, simple.
A bound verb waiting — **ሲመጣ** — and its binder — **ሄደ**:
**ውስብስብ**, complex.

Your turn. Take a sentence apart. Name its two sides.
