# Lesson plan — The Sentence and Its Marks

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** The Sentence and Its Marks
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a sentence (ዓረፍተነገር) carries one complete idea; it ends with a mark — ። for a full idea, ? for a question, ! for a strong feeling — and two sentences can be joined into one compound (ድርብ) or complex (ውስብስብ) sentence.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Word unit — the learner can already read whole words and knows the little pieces on words (ቅጥያ). It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a sentence is** — a sentence carries one complete idea; the book's first example "ኃይለኛ ዝናብ ዘነበ።" (heavy rain fell).
2. **The end marks — ። ? !** — the full stop (አራት ነጥብ ።) shows the sentence has ended; a question-feeling sentence ends with ? ("አባትህ መጣ። / አባትህ መጣ?"); ! joins them in the trio.
3. **Joining two sentences with ስለ** — the scripted dialogue: "1ኛ መወጣት እፈልጋለሁ።" + "ሁልጊዜ አጠናለሁ።" become one sentence joined by ስለምፈልግ (because I want).
4. **Compound and complex sentences** — ድርብ ዓረፍተነገር: two simple sentences joined by a conjunction (ስለዚህ, ነገርግን, ወይም, እና, ምክንያቱም); ውስብስብ ዓረፍተነገር: a sentence containing two or more verbs.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: My First Sentences (reading whole sentences) and Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a sentence is: words carrying one complete idea.
2. End a statement with ።, a question with ?, a strong-feeling sentence with !.
3. Read the same words as a statement and as a question and hear the difference.
4. Join two short sentences into one with ስለምፈልግ / ስለ.
5. Build a compound sentence with እና, ወይም, ነገርግን, ስለዚህ or ምክንያቱም, and spot a complex sentence by its two or more verbs.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner reads "ኃይለኛ ዝናብ ዘነበ" with no mark, then with ።, and says what the mark does (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — what a sentence is; the three end marks; joining with ስለ; compound and complex sentences (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8391]: a picture of the three end marks in three boxes — ። "a full idea ends", ? "a question ends", ! "a strong feeling ends" — the student points to the mark that ends each sentence they read.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 8390]: a GIF of two short sentences sliding together with ስለምፈልግ highlighted in red joining them — the student watches the join, then reads the joined sentence aloud.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading "አባትህ መጣ።" then "አባትህ መጣ?" — the student says which one is the question and why.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain what a sentence is and join two sentences for your companion (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the joining dialogue in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank, matching, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write your own statement, question and joined sentence (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks any line of words is a sentence → ask: does it carry one complete idea? "ዝናብ" alone is only a word; "ኃይለኛ ዝናብ ዘነበ።" carries the whole idea.
- Puts ። after a question → read it aloud in a questioning voice; a question-feeling sentence ends with ?, as the book's own pair shows.
- Cannot join two sentences → start from the dialogue: say what you want (1ኛ መወጣት እፈልጋለሁ), say what you do (ሁልጊዜ አጠናለሁ), then let ስለምፈልግ carry the reason between them.
