# The Sentence and Its Marks — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a sentence is
Pass mark: say that a sentence carries one complete idea, and pick the complete sentence out of a word, a part-idea and a full sentence.

## Sub lesson 2 — The end marks — ። ? !
Pass mark: end a statement with ።, a question with ?, a strong-feeling sentence with !, and read "አባትህ መጣ። / አባትህ መጣ?" two ways.

## Sub lesson 3 — Joining two sentences with ስለ
Pass mark: join two short sentences into one with ስለምፈልግ / ስለ, on the dialogue's model.

## Sub lesson 4 — Compound and complex sentences
Pass mark: build a ድርብ compound with እና, ወይም, ነገርግን, ስለዚህ or ምክንያቱም, and call a sentence ውስብስብ when it holds two or more verbs.

## Sub lesson 5 — Subject and predicate: ባለቤት and ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ
Pass mark: split a sentence into its subject side and predicate side — ባለቤት at the start, ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ at the end — and name each side's attachments in ትልቁ ልጅ እየሮጠ መጣ.

## Sub lesson 6 — The four things a sentence can do
Pass mark: name a sentence's function — ሀተታዊ, ጥያቄያዊ, ትዕዛዛዊ or አጋናኝ — from what it does, and build one sentence of each function.

## Sub lesson 7 — Affirmative and negative sentences
Pass mark: tell አዎንታዊ from አሉታዊ, say the affirmative carries no special marker, and turn an affirmative into a negative by wrapping its verb in አል- … -ም.

## Sub lesson 8 — The being-verb and its complement
Pass mark: in እነዚህ ዋልያዎች ናቸው, name the pointer (እነዚህ), the complement (ዋልያዎች, ስም መሙያ) and the being-verb (ናቸው, መሆን ግስ), and unscramble a mixed "is / are" sentence into that order.

## Sub lesson 9 — Simple and complex sentences, grown
Pass mark: call a one-clause sentence ነጠላ, and in a ውስብስብ sentence point to the bound verb (ሲመጣ) and the binding verb that completes it (ሄደ).

## Sub lesson 10 — The sentence's constituents, grown (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: open a sentence into ባለቤት (subject), ተሳቢ (object — the one the action falls on) and ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ (verb phrase), on the worked models ቶፊቅ ጻፈ and ታላቁ የህዳሴ ግድብ የኤሌክትሪክ ሀይል ማመንጨት ጀመረ.

## Sub lesson 11 — Morphemes: the free and the bound (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: tell a ነፃ ምዕላድ (stands alone — ላም, ሴት) from a ጥገኛ ምዕላድ (prefixes ስለ-, ከ-, በ-, መ-; suffixes -ኦች, -ኤ, -ኣቸው, -ዋ), split ላም + ኦች + ኣቸው, and build a new word with -ነት or -አዊ.

## Sub lesson 12 — Affirmative to negative, grown (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: convert ገሰፀ to አልገሰፁም exactly as keyed, and choose between the verb wrap, አይደለም (is not) and የላቸውም (they do not have) for an action, a being, or a having.
