# The Sentence and Its Marks — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 5)** In ትልቁ ልጅ እየሮጠ መጣ, which words are attachments (ጓዝ), and what do they attach to?
**Answer: ትልቁ attaches to the subject ልጅ; እየሮጠ attaches to the predicate መጣ.**

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the four sentence functions.
**Answer: ሀተታዊ (declarative — states), ጥያቄያዊ (interrogative — asks), ትዕዛዛዊ (imperative — orders), አጋናኝ (exclamatory — exclaims).**

**3. (Sub lesson 8)** Unscramble this sentence into its right order: ናቸው እነዚህ በሬዎች
**Answer: እነዚህ በሬዎች ናቸው።** — pointer first, then the complement noun, then the being-verb.

**4. (Sub lesson 10)** Name the three constituents a sentence is opened into in the grown analysis.
**Answer: ባለቤት (subject), ተሳቢ (object — the one the action falls on), ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ (verb phrase).**

**5. (Sub lesson 12)** Convert ገሰፀ to its negative, and say what happens to the verb inside the wrap.
**Answer: አልገሰፁም** — አል- stands before and -ም after, and the verb's own shape changes inside the wrap (ገሰፀ → ገሰፁ), exactly as the keyed pair gives it.

**6. (Sub lesson 16)** What passage is rewritten at p.32?
**Answer:** The opening passage of የወፏ ዝማሬ (The Bird's Song).

**7. (Sub lesson 17)** What is the omitted mark called?
**Answer:** ትዕምርተ ጥያቄ — the question mark.

**8. (Sub lesson 18)** How many affirmative and negative sentences does the Expert's Advice chapter ask for?
**Answer:** Five affirmative and five negative.

**9. (Sub lesson 19)** What is the asking function called?
**Answer:** Interrogative — the sentence that asks (ጥያቄያዊ).

**10. (Sub lesson 20)** What must stay untouched in a conversion?
**Answer:** The sentence's content — only the named form changes.

**11. (Sub lesson 21)** Name three error kinds the p.46 work corrects.
**Answer:** Subject–verb number agreement, possessives, and word choice.

**12. (Sub lesson 22)** How many sentences does the Grade 9 guide set for simple/complex classification?
**Answer:** Ten sentences, against the guide's key.

**13. (Sub lesson 23)** From what kinds of text are the phrases picked, per the chapter's materials?
**Answer:** Real standing text — the chapter lists short and long fiction, poems, articles and research articles.
