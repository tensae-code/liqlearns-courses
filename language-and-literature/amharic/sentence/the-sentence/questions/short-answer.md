# The Sentence and Its Marks — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 5)** In ትልቁ ልጅ እየሮጠ መጣ, which words are attachments (ጓዝ), and what do they attach to?
**Answer: ትልቁ attaches to the subject ልጅ; እየሮጠ attaches to the predicate መጣ.**

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the four sentence functions.
**Answer: ሀተታዊ (declarative — states), ጥያቄያዊ (interrogative — asks), ትዕዛዛዊ (imperative — orders), አጋናኝ (exclamatory — exclaims).**

**3. (Sub lesson 8)** Unscramble this sentence into its right order: ናቸው እነዚህ በሬዎች
**Answer: እነዚህ በሬዎች ናቸው።** — pointer first, then the complement noun, then the being-verb.
