# The Sentence and Its Marks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a sentence (ዓረፍተነገር)?
a) one single word  b) words carrying one complete idea  c) any long line of words
**Answer: b) words carrying one complete idea.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which one is a complete sentence?
a) ዝናብ  b) ኃይለኛ ዝናብ  c) ኃይለኛ ዝናብ ዘነበ።
**Answer: c) ኃይለኛ ዝናብ ዘነበ።** — it carries the whole idea and is closed by ።.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The mark ። shows —
a) a question  b) the sentence has ended  c) a strong feeling
**Answer: b) the sentence has ended** — ። is the full stop (አራት ነጥብ).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "አባትህ መጣ?" ends with ? because —
a) it tells something  b) it carries a question feeling  c) it is a shout
**Answer: b) it carries a question feeling.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In "1ኛ መወጣት ስለምፈልግ ሁልጊዜ አጠናለሁ።", the joining word is —
a) ሁልጊዜ  b) ስለምፈልግ  c) አጠናለሁ
**Answer: b) ስለምፈልግ** — "because I want"; it joins the two sentences.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** A ድርብ (compound) sentence is —
a) one word with a suffix  b) two simple sentences joined by a conjunction  c) a sentence with no verb
**Answer: b) two simple sentences joined by a conjunction.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** A ውስብስብ (complex) sentence holds —
a) two or more verbs  b) no verbs  c) only one word
**Answer: a) two or more verbs.**

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** In ከድር ከትምህርትቤት መጣ።, the ባለቤት (subject) is —
a) መጣ  b) ከድር  c) ከትምህርትቤት
**Answer: b) ከድር** — the subject stands at the start; መጣ is the ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ, the predicate, at the end.

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** "የቤት ሥራችሁን ሥሩ።" — Do your house work. This sentence's function is —
a) ሀተታዊ — it states  b) ጥያቄያዊ — it asks  c) ትዕዛዛዊ — it orders
**Answer: c) ትዕዛዛዊ** — an imperative sentence gives an order.

**10. (Sub lesson 7)** A negative (አሉታዊ) sentence is known by —
a) a question mark  b) its verb carrying አል- … -ም  c) having no verb
**Answer: b) its verb carrying አል- … -ም** — the affixes break/deny the idea; the affirmative carries no special marker.

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** In እነዚህ ዋልያዎች ናቸው, the ስም መሙያ (complement) is —
a) እነዚህ  b) ናቸው  c) ዋልያዎች
**Answer: c) ዋልያዎች** — the noun completing the being-verb ናቸው; እነዚህ is the pointer.

**12. (Sub lesson 9)** In ልጁ ከትምህርትቤት ሲመጣ አባቱ ወደቢሮ ሄደ, the bound verb that waits for its binder is —
a) ሄደ  b) ሲመጣ  c) there is none
**Answer: b) ሲመጣ** — "while coming" gives no full sense alone; ሄደ binds and completes it.

**13. (Sub lesson 10)** In ቶፊቅ ጻፈ።, the ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ (verb phrase) is —
a) ቶፊቅ  b) ጻፈ  c) the whole sentence
**Answer: b) ጻፈ** — ቶፊቅ is the ባለቤት, the subject; ጻፈ is the doing part.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** The constituent named ተሳቢ is —
a) the subject the sentence is about  b) the object — the one the action falls on  c) the verb phrase
**Answer: b) the object** — ተሳቢ receives what is done; ባለቤት is the subject, ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ the verb phrase.

**15. (Sub lesson 11)** Which of these is a ነፃ ምዕላድ — a free morpheme?
a) -ኦች  b) ስለ-  c) ላም
**Answer: c) ላም** — it stands alone as a word; -ኦች and ስለ- are bound and must lean on a stem.

**16. (Sub lesson 12)** The negative of ገሰፀ, as the keyed pair gives it, is —
a) ገሰፀም  b) አልገሰፁም  c) አይገሰፅም
**Answer: b) አልገሰፁም** — አል- before, -ም after, and the verb's own shape changes inside the wrap.

**17. (Sub lesson 13)** ሥርዓተነጥብ means…
a) Correct punctuation — the order of the marks  b) A kind of sentence  c) Handwriting speed
**Answer: a) Correct punctuation — the order of the marks** — Grade 2 p.61.

**18. (Sub lesson 13)** A sentence that asks goes with…
a) ?  b) ። only  c) ! only
**Answer: a) ?** — the three marks of sub lesson 2, at work.

**19. (Sub lesson 13)** The p.61 sentences come from which chapter's world?
a) Cleanliness and disease  b) Music  c) Wild animals
**Answer: a) Cleanliness and disease** — the cleanliness chapter, Grade 2 p.61.

**20. (Sub lesson 14)** What is the ዋና ግስ of a sentence?
a) Its main, finite verb — the action word that carries the time  b) Its longest noun  c) Its first word always
**Answer: a) Its main, finite verb — the action word that carries the time** — the sentence's engine.

**21. (Sub lesson 14)** How can you prove which word is the main verb?
a) Change the sentence's time — the word that must change is the main verb  b) Count its letters  c) It is always last in every language
**Answer: a) Change the sentence's time — the word that must change is the main verb** — the review's own test.

**22. (Sub lesson 14)** A sentence stripped to its engine keeps what?
a) Subject and main verb  b) Only adverbs  c) Only adjectives
**Answer: a) Subject and main verb** — who/what + the main action.


**23. (Sub lesson 15)** በሌላ በኩል means…?
a) Therefore  b) On the other hand  c) Because
**Answer: b) On the other hand** — it turns the sentence to the thing's other face; ስለዚህ is therefore.

**24. (Sub lesson 15)** A ድርብ (compound) sentence is…?
a) One simple sentence alone  b) Two simple sentences joined by a conjunction  c) A sentence with no verb
**Answer: b) Two simple sentences joined by a conjunction** — joined by እና, ነገርግን, ወይም, ስለዚህ or በሌላ በኩል.

**25. (Sub lesson 15)** "ትምህርት አስቸጋሪ ነው፤ ____ ደስ የሚል ነው።" (Study is hard; ____ it is joyful.) The turn to the other face takes —
a) በሌላ በኩል  b) ምክንያቱም  c) እንደ
**Answer: a) በሌላ በኩል** — hard on one face, joyful on the other.
