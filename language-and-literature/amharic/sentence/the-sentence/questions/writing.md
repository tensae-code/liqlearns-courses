# The Sentence and Its Marks — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write one complete sentence about rain, on the model ኃይለኛ ዝናብ ዘነበ። — close it with ።.
**Answer: a full sentence carrying one complete idea, ending with ። (the model itself is a right answer).**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Take two sentences that can stand alone and join them with ነገርግን into one ድርብ sentence.
**Answer: the two standing sentences in one line, joined by ነገርግን.**
