# The Sentence and Its Marks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or mark.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** A question-feeling sentence ends with the mark ___.
**Answer: ?**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** "1ኛ መወጣት ______ ሁልጊዜ አጠናለሁ።" — the joining word from the dialogue.
**Answer: ስለምፈልግ**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Two simple sentences joined by a conjunction make a ______ ዓረፍተነገር.
**Answer: ድርብ** (compound).

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** In ትልቁ ልጅ እየሮጠ መጣ, the word ልጅ is the ______ and መጣ is the ______.
**Answer: ባለቤት (subject); ማሰሪያ አንቀጽ (predicate)** — ትልቁ and እየሮጠ are their attachments.

**5. (Sub lesson 7)** To make መጣ negative, wrap it in the affixes ______ … ______.
**Answer: አል- … -ም** — አልመጣም, "did not come".

**6. (Sub lesson 8)** ናቸው is a ______ ግስ — the verb of being — and the noun that completes it is called ስም ______.
**Answer: መሆን; መሙያ.**

**7. (Sub lesson 9)** A sentence with one clause unit and one whole sense is ______; a sentence whose verb is bound and waits for a binder is ______.
**Answer: ነጠላ (simple); ውስብስብ (complex).**

**8. (Sub lesson 11)** A morpheme that can stand alone is a ______ ምዕላድ; one that must lean on a stem is a ______ ምዕላድ.
**Answer: ነፃ (free); ጥገኛ (bound).**

**9. (Sub lesson 11)** Bound morphemes that enter before the stem are called ቅድመ አምድ ______; those that enter after it are ድህረ አምድ ______.
**Answer: ቅጥያ (prefixes, e.g. ስለ-, ከ-, በ-, መ-); ቅጥያ (suffixes, e.g. -ኦች, -ኤ, -ኣቸው, -ዋ).**

**10. (Sub lesson 12)** "Is not" as a negative shape is ______; "they do not have" is ______.
**Answer: አይደለም; የላቸውም.**
