# The Sentence and Its Marks — matching

Match each mark or joining word to what it does.

1. (Sub lesson 2) ። — a) joins with a reason: "because"
2. (Sub lesson 2) ? — b) shows the sentence has ended
3. (Sub lesson 2) ! — c) ends a question
4. (Sub lesson 4) ወይም — d) ends a strong-feeling sentence
5. (Sub lesson 3) ስለምፈልግ — e) joins with a choice: "or"

**Answer key:** 1 — b, 2 — c, 3 — d, 4 — e, 5 — a.
