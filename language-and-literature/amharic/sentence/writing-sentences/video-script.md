# Video script — Writing Sentences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here are three words. Loose words.

**ልጅ** — a child. **ወደ** — to. **ሔደ** — went.

Now join them. **ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት ሔደ።**
The child went to school. One whole saying. That is a sentence YOU built.

Now listen. I say a word. You write it. **ተዘጋ።** Write it.
**ተማረ።** Write it. **አወጣ። ጠላት። ኩረት።**
Now read your words back to me.

Last game. These words are mixed up: **ጨዋታ እወዳለሁ መጫወት**.
Hard to read! Every word has its place.
Find the places. Write the sentence whole again.

Build it. Hear it. Order it. You can write sentences.

## Added 2026-11-12 — the Grade 1–2 student-book parts
Sub lesson 4, Grade 1–2: the books' own sentence sets: present the part in a simple voice, model the reading or listening once, let the learner answer the book's own questions, then prove it with the sub lesson's questions.
