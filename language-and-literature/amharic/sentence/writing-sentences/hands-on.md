# Hands-on — Writing Sentences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Word cards on the table
Write six words you know on six small papers — a person word, a
place word, a doing word, and three more. Mix them face up on the
table. Build the best sentence you can. Read it aloud. Then mix
and build a different one.

## Dictation with a partner
One of you reads five words slowly from a book page; the other
writes without looking. Swap. Check each other's writing letter
by letter — a wrong fidel is found kindly and fixed together.

## The mixed-up line
Write a sentence on a strip of paper. Cut it into its words.
A partner mixes the pieces; you put them back in their places
and glue or copy the sentence whole. Then do theirs.
