# Writing Sentences — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** In dictation you write exactly what you hear, not what you think the word should be.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A sentence reads the same however you mix its words.
**Answer: False** — mixed-up words make even good words hard to read; every word has its place.
