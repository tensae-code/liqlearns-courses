# Writing Sentences — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Build one whole sentence from the given word ውኃ (water).
**Answer guide: any one whole saying carrying ውኃ — the books' own model is ካምግብ በኋላ ውኃ እጠጣለሁ። (After food, I drink water.)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Build one whole sentence using two of these given words: ፀጉር (hair), ማበጠር (combing), ጤናማ (healthy).
**Answer guide: one whole saying about hair/combing/health, every word in its place, end mark ። in place.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dictation: your teacher or the audio reads the five words — write them: ተዘጋ, ተማረ, አወጣ, ጠላት, ኩረት.
**Answer: the five words written exactly as listed.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Copy the mixed-up words ጨዋታ እወዳለሁ መጫወት, then write under them the numbers 1, 2, 3 showing the places you would try first, and say aloud why the mixed order is hard to read.
**Answer guide: the three words copied exactly; the reason given in words — the words are not standing in their places.**
