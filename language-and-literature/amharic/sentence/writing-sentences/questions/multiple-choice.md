# Writing Sentences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You are given the word ሄደ (went). Which finished work is a built sentence?
a) ሄደ ሄደ ሄደ  b) ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት ሔደ።  c) ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት
**Answer: b) ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት ሔደ።** — the given word joined with the others says one whole thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A built sentence must —
a) use every word you know  b) say one whole thing  c) be as long as possible
**Answer: b) say one whole thing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In dictation, what do you do FIRST?
a) write quickly before you forget  b) listen in silence, then hear it again  c) read the word list aloud
**Answer: b) listen in silence, then hear it again** — the books' way: listen, hear again, write, read back.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "ጨዋታ እወዳለሁ መጫወት" is hard to read because —
a) the words are too long  b) the words are not in their places  c) it has no picture
**Answer: b) the words are not in their places.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** ፍሬን means…
a) A brake  b) Advice  c) Danger
**Answer: a) A brake** — the given-word set, Grade 1 p.90.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** ምክር means…
a) Advice  b) A brake  c) Yourselves
**Answer: a) Advice** — Grade 1 p.90.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Jumbled-sentence repair puts the words…
a) In their right order  b) In alphabetical order  c) In reverse
**Answer: a) In their right order** — Grade 2 pp.85–86.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** In unscrambling, the finished sentence must have what?
a) Its words in correct order  b) The most words possible  c) No verbs
**Answer: a) Its words in correct order** — jumbled order repaired.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Whose dialogue rides with the Grade 6 unscrambling?
a) Demle and Berihun, the herders  b) Fatuma and her friends  c) Asrat and Lubaba
**Answer: a) Demle and Berihun, the herders** — stocked whole in More Punctuation.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In dictation, what must be correct besides the words?
a) The punctuation  b) The handwriting colour  c) The page number
**Answer: a) The punctuation** — hear it, write it — marks and all.
