# Writing Sentences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You are given the word ሄደ (went). Which finished work is a built sentence?
a) ሄደ ሄደ ሄደ  b) ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት ሔደ።  c) ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት
**Answer: b) ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት ሔደ።** — the given word joined with the others says one whole thing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A built sentence must —
a) use every word you know  b) say one whole thing  c) be as long as possible
**Answer: b) say one whole thing.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In dictation, what do you do FIRST?
a) write quickly before you forget  b) listen in silence, then hear it again  c) read the word list aloud
**Answer: b) listen in silence, then hear it again** — the books' way: listen, hear again, write, read back.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "ጨዋታ እወዳለሁ መጫወት" is hard to read because —
a) the words are too long  b) the words are not in their places  c) it has no picture
**Answer: b) the words are not in their places.**
