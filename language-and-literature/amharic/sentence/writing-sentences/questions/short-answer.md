# Writing Sentences — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the model sentence ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት ሔደ።, which word was the GIVEN word the sentence was built from?
**Answer: ሄደ (went).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After writing a dictation, what do you do with your writing?
**Answer: read it back and check it fidel by fidel.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When you read a mixed-up sentence aloud and your voice trips, what does the trip tell you?
**Answer: a word is out of its place.**
