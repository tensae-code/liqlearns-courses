# Writing Sentences — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the dictation steps in the books' order: write the word — listen in silence — read your writing back — hear the word again.
**Answer: listen in silence → hear the word again → write the word → read your writing back.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A sentence's parts are mixed: the doing word (ሔደ) — who went (ልጅ) — where to (ወደ ትምህርት ቤት). Put them in the order of the books' model sentence.
**Answer: ልጅ — ወደ ትምህርት ቤት — ሔደ (ልጅ ወደ ትምህርት ቤት ሔደ።).**
