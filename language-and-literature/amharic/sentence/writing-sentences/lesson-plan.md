# Lesson plan — Writing Sentences

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Writing Sentences
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** writing sentences of your own three ways: build one from given words, write one you only hear (dictation), and put mixed-up words back in their places (word order).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on My First Sentences and the Word unit — the learner can already read whole sentences and single words; this lesson turns reading into writing.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Build a sentence from the given words** — given single words, join them into one whole sentence that says something true — the Grade 3 teacher guide's sentence-building task, with the books' own model sentences.
2. **Dictation — hear it, write it** — the teacher (or the audio) reads words and sentences aloud; you write exactly what you hear, then read it back.
3. **Word order — putting the words in their places** — a sentence whose words are mixed up is hard to read; find each word's place and write the sentence whole again.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is
the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: My First Sentences (sentence/first-sentences) — reading whole sentences. Recommended, never forced.
- The Fidel lesson helps the dictation part: every word is written with the fidel the learner already knows.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Build a whole sentence from given words, following the books' model sentences.
2. Write the five dictation words — ተዘጋ, ተማረ, አወጣ, ጠላት, ኩረት — exactly as heard, and read them back.
3. Take the mixed-up words ጨዋታ እወዳለሁ መጫወት and say why the mixed order is hard to read.
4. Put the words of a mixed-up sentence back in their places and write the sentence whole, with its end mark.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A partner or the audio voice for the dictation.

## If the learner is stuck
- The built sentence is only words in a row → ask: does it SAY one whole thing? The books' models all say one whole thing about somebody.
- Dictation words come out with wrong fidel → hear the word again slowly, fidel by fidel, and write it again; the word list is short on purpose.
- Word order feels like guessing → read the mixed sentence aloud: the place where your voice trips is the word that is out of its place.
