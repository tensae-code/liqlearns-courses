Honesty notes: (1) The placements record the p.47, p.82, p.101, p.118 and p.140 sentence sets in English (the read's own rendering of the Amharic reading pages); the lesson teaches those sets in English exactly as placed and does not invent Amharic versions for them — only the p.16 set, whose Amharic the placements quote, is taught in Amharic script. (2) The market sentence "its price is five…" is unfinished on the book's page as placed; the lesson keeps it as a starter to finish and marks it as one. (3) The p.101 page also carries a "Yosef…" (rocket) fragment, incomplete as placed; it is not taught. (4) In Sub lesson 2, the joined sentences ገበሬው በሬ ሸጠ / ገበሬው ፈረስ ሸጠ are built from the book's own four given words (p.142) — the book gives the words and the making task, not a model joining. (5) ዓባይ is spelled as the p.119 placement spells it; the water pages spell the river አባይ — both spellings are the book's own, kept per page. (6) Picture 8213 (child planting a tree, captioned ዛፍ ተከልኩ) is a drawn card made for this lesson from the p.16 sentence.
