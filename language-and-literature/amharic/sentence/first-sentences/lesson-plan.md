# Lesson plan — My First Sentences

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** My First Sentences
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a sentence is a whole
saying — you read it whole, and then you can build your own from
single words.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Word unit — the
learner can already read single words; this lesson joins them
into whole sayings about the child's own world: trees, washing,
water, the market, the farm.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

2. **Making my own sentences** — given one word (ውሃ, ሃይቅ, ዓባይ; then በሬ, ሸጠ, ገበሬው, ፈረስ), build a whole sentence out of it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read the three first sentences whole — ዛፍ ተከልኩ, አበባ
   እወዳለሁ, ብርቱካን በላሁ — and say what each one tells.
2. Read the washing, water, movement, sky, market and farm
   sentence sets whole, without stopping at every word.
3. Hear a sentence read whole, then point to its words one by one.
4. Given a single word — ውሃ, ሃይቅ, ዓባይ — say a whole sentence
   that carries it.
5. Join given words — ገበሬው, በሬ, ሸጠ — into a whole sentence,
   and change it with ፈረስ.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A cup of water helps the making part feel real.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads word… by… word… and loses the saying → cover the sentence
  and say it from the picture first; a sentence is one breath,
  not a row of stops.
- Cannot make a sentence from one word → give the frame "… is /
  … does" with the word in hand: ውሃ — what does water do? Who
  needs it? Answer in a whole saying and that is the sentence.
- Mixes up word order in the making part → lay the word cards in
  a row and read the row with a finger; move one card and read
  again — the row you can say whole is the sentence.
