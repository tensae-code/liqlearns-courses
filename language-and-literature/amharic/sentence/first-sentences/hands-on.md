# Hands-on — My First Sentences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sentence cards on the floor
Write the three first sentences on three big cards — one sentence
per card, words spaced wide. Put the cards on the floor, walk
along each one, and read it whole as you walk: one step per word,
no stopping between the steps of one sentence. The sentence is
the whole walk.

## Wash and say
At the washing place, do the actions and say the sentences as
you do them: washing your face, brushing your teeth, washing
your hands and feet. Doing and saying together fixes the whole
sentence in the body, not only on the page.

## One word, one sentence — the home hunt
A partner names one thing at home — water, the cup, the door.
You must answer with a whole sentence carrying that word, never
the word alone. "Water" is not an answer; "We need water to keep
clean" is. Five things each, then swap.

## If it is hard
Start with one sentence only — ዛፍ ተከልኩ, with its picture card
8213 — and practise it three ways: hear it whole, say it whole,
point to its two words. Add one new sentence only when the first
is steady. Small steps, a picture with every sentence, and no
hurrying (SNE toolkit — SUPPORTING; see sources.md).
