# My First Sentences — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the two halves of
sentence work: first reading whole sentences, then making your
own from single words.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reading whole sentences
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read each
set's sentences whole — the first sentences (ዛፍ ተከልኩ, አበባ
እወዳለሁ, ብርቱካን በላሁ), washing, water, movement, sky, market
and farm — and say what a given sentence tells.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making my own sentences
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given one
word (ውሃ, ሃይቅ, ዓባይ) say a whole sentence carrying it, and join
ገበሬው + በሬ/ፈረስ + ሸጠ into a whole sentence in a workable order.
