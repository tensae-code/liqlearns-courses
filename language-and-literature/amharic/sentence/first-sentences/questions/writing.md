# My First Sentences — writing

Write or say each one. A whole sentence is the only right shape —
a single word alone is not finished work.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a whole sentence carrying the word
ውሃ (wuha, water).
**Answer guide:** any whole saying with water in it, e.g. "Water
is useful for people" — the book's own water sentence is one
right shape.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a whole sentence carrying the word
ሃይቅ (hayq, lake).
**Answer guide:** any whole saying with a lake in it — what is
on it, who goes there, what you see there.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a whole sentence carrying the word
ዓባይ (Abay).
**Answer guide:** any whole saying about the great river — where
it runs, or the dam that was built on it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Use all three given words — ገበሬው (the
farmer), በሬ (ox), ሸጠ (sold) — and write the sentence they make
together.
**Answer guide:** ገበሬው በሬ ሸጠ — the farmer sold an ox.
