# My First Sentences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ዛፍ ተከልኩ (Zaf tekilku) tells us —
a) I ate an orange  b) I planted a tree  c) I like flowers
**Answer: b) I planted a tree.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which one is a whole sentence?
a) water  b) We need water to keep clean  c) the cow
**Answer: b)** — it tells a whole thing; the others are single
words or bits of sayings.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "The cow gives milk" is a sentence about —
a) the market  b) the sky  c) farm life
**Answer: c) farm life.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sentence belongs to the washing set?
a) I brushed my teeth  b) The sun gives heat  c) I bought bread
**Answer: a) I brushed my teeth.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** You are given the word ውሃ (wuha, water).
Your job is to —
a) copy the word ten times  b) say a whole sentence carrying it
c) spell it backwards
**Answer: b)** — the word alone is not the answer; the whole
saying is.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** ገበሬው (the farmer) — በሬ (ox) — ሸጠ (sold).
Joined into a sentence, they say —
a) The ox sold the farmer  b) The farmer sold an ox  c) Sold ox
farmer the
**Answer: b) The farmer sold an ox.**
