# My First Sentences — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word from the box, so the
sentence stands whole again.

**Box:** tree · teeth · water · milk · lake · horse

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ዛፍ ተከልኩ — I planted a ______.
**Answer: tree.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** I brushed my ______.
**Answer: teeth.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** We need ______ to keep clean.
**Answer: water.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The cow gives ______.
**Answer: milk.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Make the sentence whole: "I can see a
______" — using the word ሃይቅ.
**Answer: lake** — ሃይቅ is the lake word; any whole sentence
carrying it is right.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** ገበሬው ______ ሸጠ — the farmer sold a ______.
Use ፈረስ.
**Answer: ፈረስ (horse)** — the farmer sold a horse.
