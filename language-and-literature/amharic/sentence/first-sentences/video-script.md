# Video script — My First Sentences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A sentence is a whole saying. Listen to it whole.

**ዛፍ ተከልኩ** (Zaf tekilku). I planted a tree.
Say it whole. Zaf tekilku.

**አበባ እወዳለሁ** (Abeba iwodalehu). I like flowers.
**ብርቱካን በላሁ** (Birtukan belahu). I ate an orange.

Whole first. Then word by word. Zaf — tekilku. Two words, one
saying.

Now make your own. Here is one word: **ውሃ** (wuha) — water.
Water is useful for people. That is a sentence!

Here is one word: **ፈረስ** (feres) — horse.
You try. Say a whole sentence with feres in it.

Words join hands. That is a sentence.
