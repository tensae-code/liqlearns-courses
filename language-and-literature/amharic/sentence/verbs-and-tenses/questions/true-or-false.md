# Verbs and Tenses — true or false

Say true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** መኪናው ቆመ (the car stopped) has a transitive (ሳቢ) verb.
**Answer: False** — ቆመ takes no object; the stopping ends on the car itself, so it is ኢሳቢ.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ጠብታለች is the recent-perfect form in ሕፃኗ ጡት ጠባች → ጠብታለች.
**Answer: True** — the past sentence is re-dressed in the ቅርብ ኃላፊ form.

**3. (Sub lesson 7)** The p.31 work writes sentences in past, present and future time.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 10)** The Grade 8 guide's tense note names the past tense የኅላፊ ጊዜ, as printed.
**Answer: True**
