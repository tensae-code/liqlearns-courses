# Verbs and Tenses — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of ሰበረ and ይሰብራል is the ኢሀላፊ (non-past) form, and why?
**Answer: ይሰብራል — it covers today and tomorrow (present + future as one block); ሰበረ is ሀላፊ, the past.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** In ሰውየው ለልጁ ደብተር ገዛለት, name the direct object and the indirect object.
**Answer: direct object (ቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ): ደብተር; indirect object (ኢቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ): ለልጁ.**
