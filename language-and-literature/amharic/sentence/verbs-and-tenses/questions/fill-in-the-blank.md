# Verbs and Tenses — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or form.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ትናንት (yesterday) points to the ______ time.
**Answer: past — የሀላፊ ጊዜ.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In ጀሚላ በሚገባ አጥንታ ነበር፤ ስለዚህ ፈተናውን አለፈች, the form አጥንታ ነበር ("had studied") is the ______ past.
**Answer: remote — ሩቅ ኃላፊ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A verb whose action passes from the doer onto another thing is a ______ verb.
**Answer: ሳቢ ግስ (ተሻጋሪ ግስ)** — like ቀደደች in ልጅቱ ልብሷን ቀደደች.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** አንቺ ______ — "you ate", said to a girl.
**Answer: በላሽ.**
