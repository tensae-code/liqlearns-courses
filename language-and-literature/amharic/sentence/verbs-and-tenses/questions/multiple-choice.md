# Verbs and Tenses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An action happening as it is spoken is in —
a) የሀላፊ ጊዜ  b) የአሁን ጊዜ  c) የትንቢት ጊዜ
**Answer: b) የአሁን ጊዜ** — the present; its marker word is አሁን.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Amharic divides time into which two blocks?
a) ሀላፊ and ኢሀላፊ  b) ትናንት and ነገ  c) ሳቢ and ኢሳቢ
**Answer: a) ሀላፊ and ኢሀላፊ** — past, and non-past (present + future as one).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The recent-perfect (ቅርብ ኃላፊ) form of ቆረጡ is —
a) ቆረጡ ነበር  b) ይቆርጣል  c) ቆርጠዋል
**Answer: c) ቆርጠዋል** — "they have cut": finished, with its result still here.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In ልጁ ተኛ (the child slept), the verb ተኛ is —
a) ሳቢ ግስ — it takes an object  b) ኢሳቢ ግስ — it takes no object  c) a noun
**Answer: b) ኢሳቢ ግስ** — the sleeping lands on nothing.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In አዜብ ለእናቷ ብር ላከች, the direct object (ቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ) is —
a) ለእናቷ  b) ላከች  c) ብር
**Answer: c) ብር** — ask "what did she send?" — money. ለእናቷ is the indirect object.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** "We ate" in the በላ family is —
a) በላን  b) በላችሁ  c) በሉ
**Answer: a) በላን** — እኛ በላን.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** In ወደቀች, what does the ending -ች mark?
a) That 'she' did it  b) That many did it  c) That it will happen tomorrow
**Answer: a) That 'she' did it** — Grade 2 pp.28–29.

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** ቀረበ in its doing-now form is…
a) እየቀረበች  b) ቀረበች  c) ይቀርባል
**Answer: a) እየቀረበች** — Grade 2 pp.36–38.

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** ይጨምራል comes from which base verb?
a) ጨመረ  b) ጨረሰ  c) መጣ
**Answer: a) ጨመረ** — Grade 2 p.69.

**10. (Sub lesson 7)** Writing a verb for a different person changes —
a) only its translation  b) the verb's own form  c) nothing
**Answer: b) the verb's own form** — person lives in the verb's form — ሰበርክ, ሰበራችሁ, ሰበሩ.
