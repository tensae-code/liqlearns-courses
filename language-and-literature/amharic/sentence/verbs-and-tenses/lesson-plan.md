# Lesson plan — Verbs and Tenses

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Verbs and Tenses
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a verb carries time and company — its three times (present የአሁን ጊዜ, past የሀላፊ ጊዜ, future የትንቢት ጊዜ) and Amharic's own two blocks (ሀላፊ / ኢሀላፊ); the recent perfect (ቅርብ ኃላፊ) and the remote past (ሩቅ ኃላፊ); verbs that take an object (ሳቢ ግስ) and verbs that do not (ኢሳቢ ግስ); the two objects (ቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ / ኢቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ); and one verb changing its ending for every person, on the paradigm of በላ.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Word Kinds — the learner already knows a ግስ shows the action and stands at the sentence's end, and knows the past-tense verb (የኃላፊ ጊዜ ግስ). It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
1. **The verb's three times — and Amharic's two blocks** — present (የአሁን ጊዜ), past (የሀላፊ ጊዜ) and future (የትንቢት ጊዜ), marked by አሁን, ትናንት, ነገ; then the deeper cut: Amharic divides time into ሀላፊ (past) and ኢሀላፊ (non-past), with ሰበረ against ይሰብራል.
2. **Just done, and long done** — the recent perfect (ቅርብ ኃላፊ): ቆረጡ → ቆርጠዋል, ሕፃኗ ጡት ጠባች → ጠብታለች; the remote past (ሩቅ ኃላፊ): ጀሚላ በሚገባ አጥንታ ነበር፤ ስለዚህ ፈተናውን አለፈች.
3. **Verbs that take an object, verbs that do not** — ሳቢ ግስ (also ተሻጋሪ ግስ): ልጅቱ ልብሷን ቀደደች, ልጁ መጽሐፍ ገዛ; ኢሳቢ ግስ (also ኢተሻጋሪ ግስ): ልጁ ተኛ, መጣ, ቆመ, ተቀመጠ.
4. **The two objects** — ቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ (direct object) and ኢቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ (indirect object): አዜብ ለእናቷ ብር ላከች; ሰውየው ለልጁ ደብተር ገዛለት.
5. **One verb, many persons** — the full paradigm of በላ: በላሁ, በላህ, በላሽ, በላች, በላን, በላችሁ, በሉ — and the same endings walking on ሰበረ.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the verb's three times and say which time each of አሁን, ትናንት, ነገ points to.
2. Sort a verb form into ሀላፊ or ኢሀላፊ, and change ሰበረ into ይሰብራል.
3. Turn a past form into its recent-perfect form (ቆረጡ → ቆርጠዋል) and read a remote-past sentence joined with ነበር.
4. Tell a ሳቢ verb from an ኢሳቢ verb in a sentence, and say why.
5. Find the direct object and the indirect object in a sentence.
6. Say the whole በላ paradigm and put the right ending on በላ for each person.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes present and future → in Amharic's own cut they are one block, ኢሀላፊ (non-past): ዛሬ and ነገ both take ይሰብራል. Only the past stands apart as ሀላፊ.
- Cannot see the recent perfect → look at the ending: ቆረጡ says "they cut, finished"; ቆርጠዋል says "they have cut" — the doing is past, its result is still here.
- Mixes ሳቢ and ኢሳቢ → ask the verb "what?" If the sentence answers with a thing that takes the action (ልብሷን, መጽሐፍ), the verb is ሳቢ. If nothing answers — ተኛ, ቆመ — it is ኢሳቢ.
- Mixes the two objects → the direct object answers "what was done to what?"; the indirect object carries ለ- and answers "to whom?"
