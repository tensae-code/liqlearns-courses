# Verbs and Tenses — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The verb's three times — and Amharic's two blocks
Pass mark: name the three times (የአሁን ጊዜ, የሀላፊ ጊዜ, የትንቢት ጊዜ), say which time አሁን, ትናንት and ነገ each point to, and sort ሰበረ / ይሰብራል, ቀነሰ / ይቀንሳል, መከረ / ይመክራል into ሀላፊ and ኢሀላፊ.

## Sub lesson 2 — Just done, and long done
Pass mark: pick the past forms (ሠራ, ተናገረ, ሄደ, ቆረጡ, ጋገረች) out of a list, change ቆረጡ into ቆርጠዋል and ሕፃኗ ጡት ጠባች into ጠብታለች, and say which action happened first in ጀሚላ በሚገባ አጥንታ ነበር፤ ስለዚህ ፈተናውን አለፈች.

## Sub lesson 3 — Verbs that take an object, verbs that do not
Pass mark: sort ልጅቱ ልብሷን ቀደደች, ልጁ መጽሐፍ ገዛ, ሰላም እንጀራ በወጥ በላች (ሳቢ) against ልጁ ተኛ, መኪናው ቆመ, ውሻ ሞተ (ኢሳቢ), and give the -ን test for a ሳቢ verb.

## Sub lesson 4 — The two objects
Pass mark: in አዜብ ለእናቷ ብር ላከች and ሰውየው ለልጁ ደብተር ገዛለት, name the ቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ by asking "what?" and the ኢቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ by asking "to whom?"

## Sub lesson 5 — One verb, many persons
Pass mark: say the whole በላ family — በላሁ, በላህ, በላሽ, በላች, በላን, በላችሁ, በሉ — from the pronouns alone, and put the matching ending on ሰበረ for እኔ and እርሷ.
