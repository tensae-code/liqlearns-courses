# Video script — Verbs and Tenses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A verb carries time.

Three times.
**የአሁን ጊዜ** — now. It is happening.
**የሀላፊ ጊዜ** — past. It is finished.
**የትንቢት ጊዜ** — future. It is yet to come.

But Amharic cuts time in two.
**ሀላፊ** — past: **ሰበረ** — he broke.
**ኢሀላፊ** — not past: **ይሰብራል** — he breaks, he will break.

Just done? **ቆረጡ** becomes **ቆርጠዋል** — they have cut.
Long done, before another past thing? **አጥንታ ነበር** — she had studied. Then she passed.

Some verbs land on a thing. **ሳቢ ግስ.**
**ልጅቱ ልብሷን ቀደደች** — the girl tore her dress.
Some verbs land nowhere. **ኢሳቢ ግስ.**
**ልጁ ተኛ** — the child slept.

Two objects. Ask: what? Ask: to whom?
**አዜብ ለእናቷ ብር ላከች.**
What? **ብር**. To whom? **ለእናቷ**.

Now the family of **በላ** — he ate.
**በላሁ. በላህ. በላሽ. በላች. በላን. በላችሁ. በሉ.**
One verb. Every person. Only the tail changes.
