# Verbs and Tenses

## Do it first

Read these two sentences:

**ትናንት እንጨት ሰበረ።** — Yesterday he broke wood.
**ዛሬ / ነገ እንጨት ይሰብራል።** — Today / tomorrow he breaks / will break wood.

The wood is the same. The man is the same. Only the verb changed its shape — ሰበረ became ይሰብራል — and with that one change, the action jumped in time. This lesson is about everything a verb carries: its time, its company, and its persons.

## Sub lesson 1 — The verb's three times — and Amharic's two blocks

A verb's action sits in time. There are **three times**:

- **Present — የአሁን ጊዜ** — an action happening as it is spoken. Its marker word is **አሁን** (now).
- **Past — የሀላፊ ጊዜ** — an action completed. Its marker word is **ትናንት** (yesterday).
- **Future — የትንቢት ጊዜ** — an action yet to happen. Its marker word is **ነገ** (tomorrow).

[Picture 8660 — the verb's times chart]

[GIF 8663 — travelling from yesterday to tomorrow]

Watch the dot travel: ትናንት, ዛሬ, ነገ. Three stops, three times.

Now the deeper cut. Time can be divided into three — but a language does not have to divide it by three. Amharic treats the present and the future as **one**, and divides time into **two blocks**:

- **ሀላፊ** — the past. Model: ትናንት እንጨት **ሰበረ** (yesterday he broke wood).
- **ኢሀላፊ** — the non-past: today and tomorrow together. Model: ዛሬ / ነገ እንጨት **ይሰብራል** (today / tomorrow he breaks, will break wood).

Learn the pairs the books drill: **ሰበረ / ይሰብራል**, **ቀነሰ / ይቀንሳል**, **መከረ / ይመክራል**. The first of each pair is ሀላፊ; the second is ኢሀላፊ. And the changing works both ways: a past sentence can be rewritten in the non-past — አስማረች ነገ ትመጣለች becomes, in past dress, አስማረች ትናንትና መጣች — and a non-past sentence can be rewritten back into the past.

## Sub lesson 2 — Just done, and long done

Inside the past, the books sort two more shapes.

**The recent perfect — ቅርብ ኃላፊ.** The action is finished, but it stands close — its result is still here with us. Watch a past form change its ending:

- **ቆረጡ** (they cut) → **ቆርጠዋል** (they have cut).
- A whole sentence: **ሕፃኗ ጡት ጠባች** (the baby suckled at the breast) → **ጠብታለች** (she has suckled).

First learn to pick the plain past forms out of a list — ሠራ, ተናገረ, ሄደ, ቆረጡ, ጋገረች are past — then change each one into its recent-perfect dress, the way ቆረጡ became ቆርጠዋል.

**The remote past — ሩቅ ኃላፊ.** The action was already done **before** another past thing happened. The books build it by joining two sentences into one, with the earlier action wearing **ነበር**:

**ጀሚላ በሚገባ አጥንታ ነበር፤ ስለዚህ ፈተናውን አለፈች።** — Jemila **had studied** well; therefore she passed the exam.

Read it in order: the studying (አጥንታ ነበር — had studied) happened first, deep in the past; the passing (አለፈች) came after. That "had done, before the other past thing" is the remote past.

## Sub lesson 3 — Verbs that take an object, verbs that do not

Ask a verb one question: **what did the action land on?**

Some verbs throw their action onto another thing. These are **transitive verbs — ሳቢ ግስ** (the Grade 5 and Grade 6 books call them ተሻጋሪ ግስ). You can know them by the **-ን** marker at the end of the word they land on:

**ልጅቱ ልብሷን ቀደደች።** — The girl tore her dress. The tearing landed on ልብሷን (her dress) — the -ን shows it.
**ልጁ መጽሐፍ ገዛ።** — The boy bought a book. Two nouns stand in the sentence: the doer, and the receiver of the action.
**ሰላም እንጀራ በወጥ በላች።** — Selam ate injera with wot. The eating landed on the injera.

Other verbs keep their action on their own doer and take **no object**. These are **intransitive verbs — ኢሳቢ ግስ** (also called ኢተሻጋሪ ግስ):

**ልጁ ተኛ።** — The child slept. Nothing receives the sleeping.
**መጣ** (came), **ቆመ** (stood), **ተቀመጠ** (sat), **ተኛ** (lay down) — and in sentences: **በረዶው ቀለጠ** (the ice melted), **መኪናው ቆመ** (the car stopped), **ውሻ ሞተ** (the dog died).

The test never changes: if the action passes from the doer to another thing, the verb is ሳቢ; if it ends on its own doer, it is ኢሳቢ.

## Sub lesson 4 — The two objects

A ሳቢ sentence can hold **two** objects, and the books give each its own name:

- **ቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ — the direct object**: the thing the action lands on directly. Ask: **what?**
- **ኢቀጥተኛ ተሳቢ — the indirect object**: the one it is done **to** or **for**, and it carries **ለ-**. Ask: **to whom?**

[Picture 8662 — the two objects]

Work the models:

**አዜብ ለእናቷ ብር ላከች።** — Azeb sent money to her mother. What did she send? **ብር** (money) — the direct object. To whom? **ለእናቷ** (to her mother) — the indirect object.

**ሰውየው ለልጁ ደብተር ገዛለት።** — The man bought a notebook for his child. **ደብተር** = direct; **ለልጁ** = indirect.

Keep going with the same two questions: **አብዱ ለእናቱ ቤት ሠራ** (Abdu built a house for his mother — ቤት direct, ለእናቱ indirect); **ዳዊት ለፋጡማ እስክርቢቶ አቀበላት** (Dawit handed a pen to Fatuma — እስክርቢቶ direct, ለፋጡማ indirect).

## Sub lesson 5 — One verb, many persons

One verb changes its ending for every person who does it. Here is the full paradigm of **በላ** (he ate) — learn it as one family:

[Picture 8661 — the በላ paradigm]

- **እኔ በላሁ** — I ate
- **አንተ በላህ** — you ate (to a boy / man)
- **አንቺ በላሽ** — you ate (to a girl / woman)
- **እርሱ በላ** — he ate
- **እርሷ በላች** — she ate
- **እኛ በላን** — we ate
- **እናንተ በላችሁ** — you all ate
- **እነርሱ በሉ** — they ate

The stem never moves: በላ stays በላ. Only the tail changes — ሁ, ህ, ሽ, ች, ን, ችሁ, ኡ — and the tail tells you who ate. The same endings walk onto any verb: the books apply this paradigm to መጣ, ሠራ, ለበሰ, ደረሰ, ዘመረ, አነበበ — and here it is walking on ሰበረ: እኔ **ሰበርኩ**, አንተ **ሰበርክ**, አንቺ **ሰበርሽ**, እርሱ **ሰበረ**, እርሷ **ሰበረች**, እኛ **ሰበርን**, እናንተ **ሰበራችሁ**, እነርሱ **ሰበሩ**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Amharic divides time into ሀላፊ and ኢሀላፊ — ሰበረ against ይሰብራል. Then walk the በላ family from እኔ to እነርሱ without stopping. Last, read አዜብ ለእናቷ ብር ላከች and let your companion ask "what?" and "to whom?" — you answer ብር and ለእናቷ.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sort these forms into ሀላፊ and ኢሀላፊ: ሰበረ, ይሰብራል, ቀነሰ, ይቀንሳል, መከረ, ይመክራል.
2. Change ቆረጡ into its recent-perfect form, and change the sentence ሕፃኗ ጡት ጠባች the same way.
3. Read ጀሚላ በሚገባ አጥንታ ነበር፤ ስለዚህ ፈተናውን አለፈች and say which action happened first, and which word shows it.
4. Sort these sentences: ልጅቱ ልብሷን ቀደደች / ልጁ ተኛ / ሰላም እንጀራ በወጥ በላች / መኪናው ቆመ — which verbs are ሳቢ, which ኢሳቢ? Say why for each.
5. In ሰውየው ለልጁ ደብተር ገዛለት and ዳዊት ለፋጡማ እስክርቢቶ አቀበላት, name the direct object and the indirect object.
6. Write the በላ paradigm from memory — all eight persons — then check it against Picture 8661.
