# Hands-on — Verbs and Tenses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the timeline
Lay a long string on the floor. Put a stone at the left end and write ትናንት beside it, a stone in the middle for ዛሬ, a stone at the right end for ነገ. Stand at each stone and say one true sentence about yourself in that time: one thing you did, one thing you are doing, one thing you will do. Then step back and sort your three verbs: which are ሀላፊ, which ኢሀላፊ?

## Pass the verb
Sit in a circle. The first person says እኔ በላሁ and mimes eating. The next must say አንተ በላህ to the person beside them, and so on round the circle — አንቺ በላሽ, እርሱ በላ, እርሷ በላች, እኛ በላን, እናንተ በላችሁ, እነርሱ በሉ. If anyone drops the right tail, the family starts again from እኔ.

## What? To whom?
Take a cup and a book. Act: give the book to your partner. Say the sentence aloud with ለ- before your partner's place in it, on the model ሰውየው ለልጁ ደብተር ገዛለት. Your partner asks "ምን?" (what?) and "ለማን?" (to whom?) — you point: the book is the direct object, your partner is the indirect object. Swap roles with the cup.

## Object or no object?
One person mimes an action from the lesson: ቀደደ (tearing), ገዛ (buying), በላ (eating) with something in hand — then ተኛ (sleeping), ቆመ (standing), መጣ (coming) with empty hands. The watchers call out ሳቢ! or ኢሳቢ! for each mime, and say what the action landed on — or that it landed on nothing.
