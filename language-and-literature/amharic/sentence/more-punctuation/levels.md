# More Punctuation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The exclamation mark and the seven feelings
Pass mark: name the feeling behind a ! — ድንጋጤ, መገረም, ደስታ, ትካዜ, ዛቻ, ማሞገስ or ማበረታት — from its cry, and read each of the seven cries with its own feeling.

## Sub lesson 2 — Quotation marks, grown
Pass mark: fence a speaker's exact words with ትዕምርተጥቅስ " ", and set a quotation inside a quotation with ነጠላ ትዕምርተጥቅስ ' ', saying whose words are inside whose.

## Sub lesson 3 — The colon and the slash
Pass mark: place the colon ፡- where a list of points follows inside a sentence, and use እዝባር three ways — compound shortening (ት/ቤት), dates, reference numbers — while refusing to slash a single word.

## Sub lesson 4 — Dash, parentheses, ellipsis
Pass mark: break a thought with ሠረዝ, pocket an explanation inside ቅንፍ, and use ሦስት ነጠብጣቦች both ways — words left out of a proverb, and speech trailing off.

## Sub lesson 5 — Abbreviations with one dot
Pass mark: shorten a word with አንድ ነጥብ by the first two letters and by the first and last letters, shorten a long name by its initials, and name the guard rule — only common, accepted shortenings.

## Sub lesson 6 — Quotation marks first met, and the Corona paragraph
Pass mark: say what “ ” first means — speech taken from another person or book — and punctuate a paragraph about ኮረና using ? ። ! ፣ and “ ” together, each mark where the reading-aloud ear puts it.

## Sub lesson 8 — The Grade 4 book's new marks: . , (፡-), እዝባር, ሦስት ነጠብጣብ
Pass mark: build one correct sentence with each of the four marks — dot abbreviation, colon announcing more, slash እዝባር, ellipsis ሦስት ነጠብጣብ trailing off — and name each mark's work.
