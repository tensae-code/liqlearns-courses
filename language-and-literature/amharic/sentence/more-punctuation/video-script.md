# Video script — More Punctuation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You know four marks. Here are the grown ones.

First — the shout with seven faces. **!**
Shock: **ዋይ!** Surprise: **ጉድ!** Joy: **የምሥራች!**
Despair: **እህህ…!** Threat: **ቆይ አሳይሃለሁ!**
Admiration: **ጎሽ!** Encouragement: **አይዞህ!**
One mark — **ትዕምርተአንክሮ** — seven feelings. Name the feeling, then read.

Someone's exact words wear these: **" "**
**መምህር አካሉ "ነገ በኹለት ሰዓት እንድትገኙ" አሉ።**
A quote inside a quote wears the small pair: **' '**

A list is coming inside the sentence: **፡-**
**መግቢያ፣ ሀተታ፣ መደምደሚያ።**

The slash — **እዝባር / **
**ትምህርትቤት → ት/ቤት.** A date: **18/11/2013.**
Only compounds. Never a single word.

A broken thought: **–** An aside in a pocket: **( )**
Words left out, speech trailing away: **…**

And one dot shortens a word:
**መስተዳድር → መስ.** **ወይዘሮ → ወሮ.**

More marks. Same job: the reader is never lost.
