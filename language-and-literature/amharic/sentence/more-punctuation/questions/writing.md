# More Punctuation — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the cry for the feeling ዛቻ (threat), and one for ማሞገስ (admiration).
**Answer: threat — ቆይ አሳይሃለሁ! (or እንገናኛለን!); admiration — ጎሽ! (or አበጀህ! / ጎበዝ!).**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Write this speech so it trails off brokenly, with the ellipsis: ደ…ደ… ደመላሽ ተው
**Answer: ደ…ደ… ደመላሽ ተው!…** — ሦስት ነጠብጣቦች inside the stammer and at the trail-off.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Shorten የእርዳታ ማስተባበሪያና ማቋቋሚያ ኮሚሽን to its initials.
**Answer: እ.ማ.ማ.ኮ** — one dot after each initial.

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** Punctuate a short paragraph about ኮረና that your teacher reads without marks, using ? ። ! ፣ and “ ” — including one quoted sentence inside “ ”.
**Answer: every question ends ?; statements close ።; a cry takes !; pauses rest on ፣; the quoted speech is fenced in “ ”.**
