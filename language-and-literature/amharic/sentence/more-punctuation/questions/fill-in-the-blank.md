# More Punctuation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right mark or name.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** መምህር አካሉ ______ነገ በኹለት ሰዓት እንድትገኙ______ አሉ። — fence the teacher's exact words.
**Answer: " " (ትዕምርተጥቅስ)** — "ነገ በኹለት ሰዓት እንድትገኙ".

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A complete text has three parts, namely ______ መግቢያ፣ ሀተታ፣ መደምደሚያ — the mark that announces the list of points.
**Answer: ፡- (ኹለት ነጥብ ከሰረዝ, the colon).**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** A small explanation sitting inside a sentence, in a lowered voice, is pocketed by the mark called ______.
**Answer: ቅንፍ — parentheses ( ).**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Shorten መስተዳድር by the first-two-letters way: ______.
**Answer: መስ.** — closed with አንድ ነጥብ, one dot.
