# More Punctuation — matching

Match each mark to its job.

1. (Sub lesson 1) ! — a) pockets a small explanation inside the sentence
2. (Sub lesson 2) ' ' — b) follows speech carrying a strong feeling
3. (Sub lesson 3) / — c) holds a quotation inside a quotation
4. (Sub lesson 4) ( ) — d) separates the parts of a date; shortens compound words only
5. (Sub lesson 5) . — e) closes a shortened word: መስ., ወሮ.

**Answer key:** 1 — b, 2 — c, 3 — d, 4 — a, 5 — e.
