# More Punctuation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 9)** What four kinds of sentence does the p.22 repair set hold?
**Answer:** A quotation, an exclamation, a shop-hours line, and a surgery sentence.

**2. (Sub lesson 10)** From which set of marks does p.117 ask you to choose?
**Answer:** Quotation marks, !, ፤ (the Amharic semicolon-like mark), ? and ፣ (comma).

**3. (Sub lesson 13)** In which chapter's writing part does the Grade 9 guide place its punctuation explanation?
**Answer:** Chapter 7 — the part on explaining concepts and phrases in one's own words.
