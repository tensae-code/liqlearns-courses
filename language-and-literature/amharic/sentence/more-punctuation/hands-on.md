# Hands-on — More Punctuation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seven faces
Seven cards, one for each feeling: ድንጋጤ, መገረም, ደስታ, ትካዜ, ዛቻ, ማሞገስ, ማበረታት. A partner reads a cry from the lesson — ዋይ!, ጉድ!, አይዞህ! — with its full feeling. Hold up the feeling's card. Then swap: you read, they name. Finish by making each face with your own face as you read its cry.

## Boxes inside boxes
Draw a big speech box on paper and a small one inside it. In the small box write a short quoted saying — መማር፣ መማር፣ አሁንም መማር! In the big box, write the speech that reports it, with " " around the whole and ' ' around the inner saying. Read it aloud, pointing to whose words are whose.

## Slash or not?
Cards: ትምህርትቤት, መዝገብቤት, መጽሐፍ, መሥሪያቤት, ዛፍ. For each card, decide: may it be slashed? Write the slashed form for the compounds (ት/ቤት…) and write "ነጠላ ቃል ነው — አይሆንም" (a single word — not allowed) for the rest. Say the rule aloud each time.

## The whisper pocket
Write a sentence with a hard word in it. Now write it again with the word's small explanation pocketed inside ቅንፍ. Read both to a partner: the second time, drop your voice inside the brackets, the way a sentence whispers an aside.
