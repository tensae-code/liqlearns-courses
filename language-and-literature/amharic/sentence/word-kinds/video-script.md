# Video script — The Word Kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every word has a job. Six jobs. In order.

One: **ስም** — the noun. It names a thing.
**ሰው** — a person. **ዛፍ** — a tree. **ድመት** — a cat.

Two: **ግስ** — the verb. It shows the action. It stands at the end.
**ሄደ** — went. **ተማረ** — learned.

Three: **ቅጽል** — the adjective. It describes the noun.
**ቀይ በሬ** — a red ox. **ወፍራም በግ** — a fat sheep.

Four: **አያያዥ ቃላት** — the conjunction. It joins.
**እና** — and. **ወይም** — or.

Five: **ተውሳከግስ** — the adverb. When? Where? How?
**ቶሎ** — quickly. **ትናንት** — yesterday.

Six: **መስተዋድድ** — the preposition. It stands before a noun.
**ወደ** — to. **ከ** — from. **ለ** — for.

ስም, ግስ, ቅጽል, አያያዥ, ተውሳከግስ, መስተዋድድ.
Six kinds. Now you name them.

---

And inside the noun, more families.

**ወል ስም** — a common noun. Any one of a kind.
**መምህር** — any teacher. **ዛፍ** — any tree.
**የተጸውኦ ስም** — a proper noun. One named one.
**ጣና. ተከዜ. ጎንደር. ማህሌት.**

**ጥቅል ስም** — one word, a whole group.
**መንጋ** — the herd. **ሕዝብ** — the people. **ደን** — the forest.

Words that stand in a noun's place: **ተውላጠስም**.
Three persons — **መደብ**.
**እኔ** — I. **አንተ, አንቺ** — you. **እሱ, እሷ** — he, she.

Pointing words: **ይህ** — this, near. **ያ** — that, far.
Question words: **ማን** — who? **ምን** — what? **ወዴት** — where? **መቼ** — when? **እንዴት** — how? **ለምን** — why?

Last split. Can you count it?
**በሬ** — one, two, three: **የሚቆጠሩ**.
**ዉኃ, ስኳር** — no number: **የማይቆጠሩ**.

---

Now stand in the places of person.
You speak: **እኔ** — first person.
You listen: **አንተ, አንቺ** — second person.
You are talked about: **እሱ, እሷ** — third person.
**መደብ** is the place you stand in. Change places — the word changes.

And the adjective, grown.
It walks **before** its noun.
**ረጅም ቀሚስ** — a long dress.
**አዲስ መኪና** — a new car.
**ጎጂ ልማድ** — a harmful habit.
Find the noun. Look just before it. There stands the **ቅጽል**.
