# Video script — The Word Kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every word has a job. Six jobs. In order.

One: **ስም** — the noun. It names a thing.
**ሰው** — a person. **ዛፍ** — a tree. **ድመት** — a cat.

Two: **ግስ** — the verb. It shows the action. It stands at the end.
**ሄደ** — went. **ተማረ** — learned.

Three: **ቅጽል** — the adjective. It describes the noun.
**ቀይ በሬ** — a red ox. **ወፍራም በግ** — a fat sheep.

Four: **አያያዥ ቃላት** — the conjunction. It joins.
**እና** — and. **ወይም** — or.

Five: **ተውሳከግስ** — the adverb. When? Where? How?
**ቶሎ** — quickly. **ትናንት** — yesterday.

Six: **መስተዋድድ** — the preposition. It stands before a noun.
**ወደ** — to. **ከ** — from. **ለ** — for.

ስም, ግስ, ቅጽል, አያያዥ, ተውሳከግስ, መስተዋድድ.
Six kinds. Now you name them.
