# The Word Kinds — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the two types of መስታጽምር (conjunction).
**Answer: coordinating (ወደረኛ) — joins equals; subordinating (ጥገኛ) — one verb depends on another.**

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** Give three prepositions from the books' list.
**Answer: any three of ስለ, ወደ, በ, ከ, እንደ, ለ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 7)** Give one ወል ስም and one የተጸውኦ ስም from the lesson.
**Answer: any one of ደም, መምህር, ዛፍ; and any one of ጣና, ተከዜ, ጎንደር, ማህሌት.**

**4. (Sub lesson 8)** Name two collective nouns besides መንጋ.
**Answer: any two of ሕዝብ, ደን, እጽዋት, ከተማ.**

**5. (Sub lesson 9)** Name the three persons (መደብ) of pronouns and give one pronoun for each.
**Answer: 1st — the speaker (እኔ / እኛ); 2nd — the one spoken to (አንተ / አንቺ / እናንተ); 3rd — the one spoken about (እሱ / እሷ / እነሱ).**

**6. (Sub lesson 10)** What does each question word ask about: ማን, ወዴት, እንዴት?
**Answer: ማን — a person; ወዴት — a place; እንዴት — a manner (how).**

**7. (Sub lesson 11)** How do you test whether a noun is የሚቆጠሩ or የማይቆጠሩ?
**Answer: try to state its number — if the number can be stated (one በሬ, two በሬ), it is የሚቆጠሩ; if not (ዉኃ, ስኳር), it is የማይቆጠሩ.**

**8. (Sub lesson 12)** In the staged way of learning person, which three places do the learners stand in?
**Answer: the speaker's place (first person), the listener's place (second person), and the place of the one talked about (third person)** — the pronoun changes as you change places.

**9. (Sub lesson 16)** What question sorts adjective from adverb?
**Answer:** What does the word describe — a thing (noun) or an action (verb)?

**10. (Sub lesson 17)** Name the three types of adverb in the Grade 7 table.
**Answer:** Of time (የጊዜ), of place (የቦታ), of manner (የሁኔታ).

**11. (Sub lesson 18)** What are the two modifier kinds named?
**Answer:** የስም ገላጭ (noun-modifier) and የግስ ገላጭ (verb-modifier).

**12. (Sub lesson 22)** From what are the bold-underlined words classified in Chapter 7?
**Answer:** From the context they occur in — the word's work in its sentence.
