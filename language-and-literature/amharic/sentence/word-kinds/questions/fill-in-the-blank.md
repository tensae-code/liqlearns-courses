# The Word Kinds — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right kind or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ድመት names an animal, so ድመት is a ______.
**Answer: ስም** (noun).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** In ወፍራም በግ, the word ወፍራም shows the sheep's size, so it is a ______.
**Answer: ቅጽል** (adjective).

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** ትናንት answers "when?", so ትናንት is a ______.
**Answer: ተውሳከግስ** (adverb).

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** መምህር names any teacher, so መምህር is a ______ ስም.
**Answer: ወል** (common noun).

**5. (Sub lesson 8)** Many trees standing as one word — ______ (a forest).
**Answer: ደን** — a ጥቅል ስም.

**6. (Sub lesson 9)** እኛ (we) stands in the ______ person.
**Answer: first** — the speakers; እኔ / እኛ.

**7. (Sub lesson 10)** For a thing near you, the pointing word (masculine) is ______.
**Answer: ይህ** — ያ is far from both.

**8. (Sub lesson 11)** ተማሪ can be counted — one, two, three — so ተማሪ is ______.
**Answer: የሚቆጠሩ** (countable).

**9. (Sub lesson 12)** Words that stand in a noun's place are called ______ ስሞች; their persons, in three places, are called ______.
**Answer: ተውላጠ; መደብ.**

**10. (Sub lesson 13)** A noun's modifier stands ______ the noun and describes its kind, size, type or origin.
**Answer: before** — as in ረጅም ቀሚስ and አዲስ መኪና.
