# The Word Kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ስም (noun) is a word that —
a) shows an action  b) names a thing  c) joins two sentences
**Answer: b) names a thing** — ሰው, ዛፍ, ድመት.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a sentence, the ግስ (verb) stands —
a) at the start  b) in the middle  c) at the end
**Answer: c) at the end** — the verb shows the action and comes last.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which word is a past-tense verb (የኃላፊ ጊዜ ግስ)?
a) ሰበረች  b) በግ  c) ቶሎ
**Answer: a) ሰበረች** — "she broke": an action completed in past time.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In ቀይ በሬ, the adjective (ቅጽል) is —
a) በሬ  b) ቀይ  c) neither word
**Answer: b) ቀይ** — it shows the ox's kind/colour.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** እና and ወይም are which type of መስታጽምር?
a) subordinating (ጥገኛ)  b) coordinating (ወደረኛ) — they join equals  c) prepositions
**Answer: b) coordinating (ወደረኛ).**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** An adverb (ተውሳከግስ) shows —
a) a name  b) when, where and how an action is done  c) a joining
**Answer: b) when, where and how an action is done** — ቶሎ, ሁልጊዜ, ትናንት, ክፉኛ.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** A preposition (መስተዋድድ) stands —
a) at the sentence's end doing the action  b) before a noun  c) alone with no meaning
**Answer: b) before a noun** — ስለ, ወደ, በ, ከ, እንደ, ለ.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** ጎንደር is a የተጸውኦ ስም (proper noun) because it —
a) names any town  b) names one named town  c) names a group
**Answer: b) names one named town** — like ጣና, ተከዜ, ማህሌት.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** Which word is a ጥቅል ስም (collective noun)?
a) መንጋ  b) በሬ  c) ዛፍ
**Answer: a) መንጋ** — one word, a whole herd inside it.

**10. (Sub lesson 9)** እናንተ stands in which person (መደብ)?
a) first  b) second  c) third
**Answer: b) second** — "you all": the ones spoken to.

**11. (Sub lesson 10)** To ask about a reason, the መጠይቅ word is —
a) መቼ  b) እንዴት  c) ለምን
**Answer: c) ለምን** — why? መቼ asks time; እንዴት asks manner.

**12. (Sub lesson 11)** ዉኃ (water) is —
a) የሚቆጠሩ — countable  b) የማይቆጠሩ — uncountable  c) a verb
**Answer: b) የማይቆጠሩ** — with ስኳር, ዘይት, አሸዋ: no number is stated.

**13. (Sub lesson 12)** In the staged lesson on person, the learner standing in the speaker's place uses —
a) third-person words  b) first-person words: እኔ, እኛ  c) pointing words only
**Answer: b) first-person words** — person (መደብ) is the place you stand in: speaker, listener, or the one talked about.

**14. (Sub lesson 12)** The written work for personal pronouns asks for —
a) one sentence in each person  b) at least five sentences in the first person and five in the second  c) sentences only in the third person
**Answer: b) at least five in the first person and five in the second** — drawn from what was read.

**15. (Sub lesson 13)** In the pair ረጅም ቀሚስ, the adjective (ቅጽል) is —
a) ቀሚስ  b) ረጅም  c) neither word
**Answer: b) ረጅም** — the modifier stands before its noun and tells its size; ቀሚስ is the noun.

**16. (Sub lesson 13)** Which list is all adjectives, as the keyed answers name them?
a) ትላልቅ, ጎበዝ, ሰፊ, ሩቅ  b) ቤት, ልጅ, መኪና, ልማድ  c) ሄደ, መጣ, ጻፈ, ሠራ
**Answer: a) ትላልቅ, ጎበዝ, ሰፊ, ሩቅ** — big, clever, wide, far; the b list is nouns and the c list is verbs.
