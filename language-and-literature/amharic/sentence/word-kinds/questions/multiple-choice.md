# The Word Kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ስም (noun) is a word that —
a) shows an action  b) names a thing  c) joins two sentences
**Answer: b) names a thing** — ሰው, ዛፍ, ድመት.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a sentence, the ግስ (verb) stands —
a) at the start  b) in the middle  c) at the end
**Answer: c) at the end** — the verb shows the action and comes last.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which word is a past-tense verb (የኃላፊ ጊዜ ግስ)?
a) ሰበረች  b) በግ  c) ቶሎ
**Answer: a) ሰበረች** — "she broke": an action completed in past time.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In ቀይ በሬ, the adjective (ቅጽል) is —
a) በሬ  b) ቀይ  c) neither word
**Answer: b) ቀይ** — it shows the ox's kind/colour.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** እና and ወይም are which type of መስታጽምር?
a) subordinating (ጥገኛ)  b) coordinating (ወደረኛ) — they join equals  c) prepositions
**Answer: b) coordinating (ወደረኛ).**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** An adverb (ተውሳከግስ) shows —
a) a name  b) when, where and how an action is done  c) a joining
**Answer: b) when, where and how an action is done** — ቶሎ, ሁልጊዜ, ትናንት, ክፉኛ.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** A preposition (መስተዋድድ) stands —
a) at the sentence's end doing the action  b) before a noun  c) alone with no meaning
**Answer: b) before a noun** — ስለ, ወደ, በ, ከ, እንደ, ለ.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** ጎንደር is a የተጸውኦ ስም (proper noun) because it —
a) names any town  b) names one named town  c) names a group
**Answer: b) names one named town** — like ጣና, ተከዜ, ማህሌት.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** Which word is a ጥቅል ስም (collective noun)?
a) መንጋ  b) በሬ  c) ዛፍ
**Answer: a) መንጋ** — one word, a whole herd inside it.

**10. (Sub lesson 9)** እናንተ stands in which person (መደብ)?
a) first  b) second  c) third
**Answer: b) second** — "you all": the ones spoken to.

**11. (Sub lesson 10)** To ask about a reason, the መጠይቅ word is —
a) መቼ  b) እንዴት  c) ለምን
**Answer: c) ለምን** — why? መቼ asks time; እንዴት asks manner.

**12. (Sub lesson 11)** ዉኃ (water) is —
a) የሚቆጠሩ — countable  b) የማይቆጠሩ — uncountable  c) a verb
**Answer: b) የማይቆጠሩ** — with ስኳር, ዘይት, አሸዋ: no number is stated.

**13. (Sub lesson 12)** In the staged lesson on person, the learner standing in the speaker's place uses —
a) third-person words  b) first-person words: እኔ, እኛ  c) pointing words only
**Answer: b) first-person words** — person (መደብ) is the place you stand in: speaker, listener, or the one talked about.

**14. (Sub lesson 12)** The written work for personal pronouns asks for —
a) one sentence in each person  b) at least five sentences in the first person and five in the second  c) sentences only in the third person
**Answer: b) at least five in the first person and five in the second** — drawn from what was read.

**15. (Sub lesson 13)** In the pair ረጅም ቀሚስ, the adjective (ቅጽል) is —
a) ቀሚስ  b) ረጅም  c) neither word
**Answer: b) ረጅም** — the modifier stands before its noun and tells its size; ቀሚስ is the noun.

**16. (Sub lesson 13)** Which list is all adjectives, as the keyed answers name them?
a) ትላልቅ, ጎበዝ, ሰፊ, ሩቅ  b) ቤት, ልጅ, መኪና, ልማድ  c) ሄደ, መጣ, ጻፈ, ሠራ
**Answer: a) ትላልቅ, ጎበዝ, ሰፊ, ሩቅ** — big, clever, wide, far; the b list is nouns and the c list is verbs.

**17. (Sub lesson 14)** ክፉኛ (badly) is which kind of adverb?
a) A derived adverb (ምሥርት) — from the adjective ክፉ  b) A native adverb (ነባር)  c) A noun
**Answer: a) A derived adverb (ምሥርት) — from the adjective ክፉ** — native words stand as they are; derived ones are built.

**18. (Sub lesson 14)** ዛሬ (today) belongs to which of the four kinds?
a) የጊዜ — time  b) የቦታ — place  c) የመጠን — degree
**Answer: a) የጊዜ — time** — when, where, how, how much.

**19. (Sub lesson 14)** In the night-story cloze, adverbs are chosen to fit what?
a) The story's sense — hour, sound, fear, movement  b) The alphabet  c) The longest word wins
**Answer: a) The story's sense — hour, sound, fear, movement** — the review's own task.

**20. (Sub lesson 14)** ቶሎ (quickly) answers which adverb question?
a) How? — manner  b) Where?  c) How much?
**Answer: a) How? — manner** — ቶሎ tells how the action is done.

**21. (Sub lesson 15)** In ታሪካዊ ቀን, which word is the adjective?
a) ታሪካዊ — historic  b) ቀን  c) ነው
**Answer: a) ታሪካዊ — historic** — the describing word before the noun.

**22. (Sub lesson 15)** In ሐሰተኛ መረጃ, the adjective ሐሰተኛ means what?
a) False, lying  b) True  c) Written
**Answer: a) False, lying** — ሐሰተኛ መረጃ against እውነተኛ መረጃ — false against true.

**23. (Sub lesson 15)** An adjective's job is to do what?
a) Describe a noun  b) Do an action  c) Name a person
**Answer: a) Describe a noun** — ቅጽል — the describer.


**24. (Sub lesson 16)** ቶሎ in ቶሎ መጣ is an adverb because it describes —
a) a noun  b) the verb መጣ — how he came  c) a bag
**Answer: b) the verb መጣ — how he came** — ቶሎ tells HOW he came — it describes the verb.

**25. (Sub lesson 17)** ትናንት in 'ዘርፌ ትናንት ደስተኛ ነበረች' is an adverb of —
a) place  b) time  c) manner
**Answer: b) time** — ትናንት — yesterday — tells WHEN: an adverb of time.

**26. (Sub lesson 18)** In ጎበዟ ልጅ, ጎበዟ is a —
a) verb-modifier  b) noun-modifier  c) conjunction
**Answer: b) noun-modifier** — ጎበዟ (clever) describes the girl — a noun-modifier (የስም ገላጭ).

**27. (Sub lesson 19)** ሰበረ belongs with —
a) the nouns (ስሞች)  b) the verbs (ግሶች)  c) neither
**Answer: b) the verbs (ግሶች)** — ሰበረ is a doing word — a verb.

**28. (Sub lesson 19)** በቀሎ belongs with —
a) the nouns (ስሞች)  b) the verbs (ግሶች)  c) the sounds only
**Answer: a) the nouns (ስሞች)** — በቀሎ names a thing — a noun.

**29. (Sub lesson 19)** The sorting question is —
a) 'How long is it?'  b) 'Does it name a thing, or do something?'  c) 'What colour is it?'
**Answer: b) 'Does it name a thing, or do something?'** — Name or do — that sorts nouns from verbs.

**30. (Sub lesson 20)** አባይ (Abay) is —
a) a common noun  b) a proper noun  c) a verb
**Answer: b) a proper noun** — Proper — one particular river.

**31. (Sub lesson 20)** ወንዝ (river) is —
a) a proper noun  b) a common noun  c) an adjective
**Answer: b) a common noun** — Common — any river.

**32. (Sub lesson 20)** ነብር (leopard) is keyed as —
a) proper  b) common  c) neither
**Answer: b) common** — Common.

**33. (Sub lesson 21)** The sorting aid is —
a) a mixed-noun chart  b) two tidy lists  c) a song
**Answer: a) a mixed-noun chart** — የተወያየ ስንጠረዥ — mixed chart.

**34. (Sub lesson 21)** The sorting rule asks —
a) 'How is it spelled?'  b) 'One particular thing, or any of its kind?'  c) 'How long is it?'
**Answer: b) 'One particular thing, or any of its kind?'** — Particular vs any-of-kind.

**35. (Sub lesson 21)** Special-needs learners get —
a) no tasks  b) extra tasks  c) the same single task only
**Answer: b) extra tasks** — Extra tasks.

**36. (Sub lesson 22)** The Chapter 7 classification key runs —
a) verb, adverb, verb, noun, adjective, verb, pronoun  b) noun, noun, verb, verb, pronoun, adjective, adverb  c) adjective, noun, pronoun, verb, adverb, verb, verb
**Answer: a) verb, adverb, verb, noun, adjective, verb, pronoun**
