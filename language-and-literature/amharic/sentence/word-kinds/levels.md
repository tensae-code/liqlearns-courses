# The Word Kinds — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Noun — ስም
Pass mark: pick the naming words (ሰው, ዛፍ, ድመት) out of a mixed list and say what each names.

## Sub lesson 2 — Verb — ግስ
Pass mark: find the verb at the sentence's end, and tell a past-tense verb (ገደለ, ሰበረች) by its finished action.

## Sub lesson 3 — Adjective — ቅጽል
Pass mark: spot the adjective in የዋህ ልጅ / ቀይ በሬ / ወፍራም በግ and say whether it tells kind, quality or size.

## Sub lesson 4 — Conjunction — አያያዥ ቃላት
Pass mark: join two equals with እና / ወይም (coordinating) and say why ስለ / ከ are subordinating.

## Sub lesson 5 — Adverb — ተውሳከግስ
Pass mark: find the word answering when, where or how (ቶሎ, ሁልጊዜ, ትናንት, ክፉኛ).

## Sub lesson 6 — Preposition — መስተዋድድ
Pass mark: find the word standing before a noun showing time, place, instrument, reason or purpose (ስለ, ወደ, በ, ከ, እንደ, ለ).

## Sub lesson 7 — Common and proper nouns
Pass mark: sort ደም, መምህር, ዛፍ (ወል ስም) against ጣና, ተከዜ, ጎንደር, ማህሌት (የተጸውኦ ስም) and say the test — any one of a kind, or one named one.

## Sub lesson 8 — Collective nouns — ጥቅል ስም
Pass mark: pick መንጋ out of a word list, then name ሕዝብ, ደን, እጽዋት, ከተማ as collective and say what group lives inside each word.

## Sub lesson 9 — Pronouns and their persons — ተውላጠስም, መደብ
Pass mark: give all three persons — እኔ / እኛ; አንተ, አንቺ, እናንተ; እሱ, እሷ, እነሱ / እነርሱ — and classify a pronoun in a sentence by person, gender and number.

## Sub lesson 10 — Pointing pronouns and question pronouns
Pass mark: choose ይህ / ይቺ / እነዚህ for near and ያ / ያቺ / እነዚያ for far, and match each መጠይቅ word (ማን, ምን, ወዴት, መቼ, እንዴት, ለምን) to what it asks about.

## Sub lesson 11 — Countable and uncountable nouns
Pass mark: sort ሰው, እንስሳ, ቤት, ተማሪ, በሬ (የሚቆጠሩ) against ስኳር, ዘይት, አሸዋ, ዉኃ, ቡና, ብርሃን, ጨለማ, ኃይል (የማይቆጠሩ), using the number-stating test.

## Sub lesson 12 — Personal pronouns and person, grown (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: name the place each pronoun stands in — speaker (1st: እኔ / እኛ), listener (2nd: አንተ, አንቺ, እናንተ), talked-about (3rd: እሱ, እሷ, እነሱ) — and write at least five sentences in the first person and five in the second.

## Sub lesson 13 — Adjectives, grown (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: find the modifier standing before its noun in ረጅም ቀሚስ / አዲስ መኪና / ጎጂ ልማድ, say whether it tells kind, size, type or origin, and pick the keyed adjectives (ትላልቅ, አዲስ, ጎበዝ, ወፍራም, ሰፊ, አጭሩ, ሩቅ, ትኩስ, ጥቁር, ረዥም) out of sentences.
