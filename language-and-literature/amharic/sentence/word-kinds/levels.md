# The Word Kinds — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Noun — ስም
Pass mark: pick the naming words (ሰው, ዛፍ, ድመት) out of a mixed list and say what each names.

## Sub lesson 2 — Verb — ግስ
Pass mark: find the verb at the sentence's end, and tell a past-tense verb (ገደለ, ሰበረች) by its finished action.

## Sub lesson 3 — Adjective — ቅጽል
Pass mark: spot the adjective in የዋህ ልጅ / ቀይ በሬ / ወፍራም በግ and say whether it tells kind, quality or size.

## Sub lesson 4 — Conjunction — አያያዥ ቃላት
Pass mark: join two equals with እና / ወይም (coordinating) and say why ስለ / ከ are subordinating.

## Sub lesson 5 — Adverb — ተውሳከግስ
Pass mark: find the word answering when, where or how (ቶሎ, ሁልጊዜ, ትናንት, ክፉኛ).

## Sub lesson 6 — Preposition — መስተዋድድ
Pass mark: find the word standing before a noun showing time, place, instrument, reason or purpose (ስለ, ወደ, በ, ከ, እንደ, ለ).
