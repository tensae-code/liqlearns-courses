# Lesson plan — The Word Kinds

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** The Word Kinds
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** words come in kinds, in the books' own order — noun (ስም), verb (ግስ), adjective (ቅጽል), conjunction (አያያዥ ቃላት), adverb (ተውሳከግስ), preposition (መስተዋድድ) — and each kind does its own job in the sentence. Grown in the G5–G8 pass: the noun family goes deeper (common, proper and collective nouns; countable and uncountable nouns) and the pronouns get their own sub lessons (persons, pointing words, question words).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Sentence and Its Marks — the learner already knows a sentence carries one complete idea. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Noun — ስም** — words that name things (ሰው, ዛፍ, ድመት).
2. **Verb — ግስ** — the word that shows the action and comes at the end of the sentence (ተማረ, ሄደ, ተኛ); with past-tense verbs (የኃላፊ ጊዜ ግስ): ገደለ, ሰበረች.
3. **Adjective — ቅጽል** — a word that shows a noun's kind, quality or size (የዋህ ልጅ, ቀይ በሬ, ወፍራም በግ); the teacher guide calls adjectives አይነግሮች.
4. **Conjunction — አያያዥ ቃላት** — words that join word to word, phrase to phrase, sentence to sentence; the two kinds of መስታጽምር: coordinating (እና, ወይም) and subordinating (ስለ, ከ).
5. **Adverb — ተውሳከግስ** — words that show when, where and how an action is done (ቶሎ, ሁልጊዜ, ትናንት, ክፉኛ).
6. **Preposition — መስተዋድድ** — words that come before a noun and show time, place, instrument, reason, purpose (ስለ, ወደ, በ, ከ, እንደ, ለ).
7. **Common and proper nouns** — inside ስም: ወል ስም names any one of a kind (ደም, መምህር, ዛፍ); የተጸውኦ ስም names one named one (ጣና, ተከዜ, ጎንደር, ማህሌት).
8. **Collective nouns — ጥቅል ስም** — one word standing for a whole group: መንጋ, ሕዝብ, ደን, እጽዋት, ከተማ.
9. **Pronouns and their persons — ተውላጠስም, መደብ** — words that stand in a noun's place, in three persons: 1st እኔ / እኛ; 2nd አንተ, አንቺ, እናንተ; 3rd እሱ, እሷ, እነሱ / እነርሱ.
10. **Pointing pronouns and question pronouns** — demonstratives (ጠቋሚ): near ይህ, ይቺ, እነዚህ; far ያ, ያቺ, እነዚያ; and interrogatives (መጠይቅ ተውላጠስም): ማን, ምን, ወዴት, መቼ, እንዴት, ለምን.
11. **Countable and uncountable nouns** — የሚቆጠሩ ስሞች, whose number can be stated (ሰው, እንስሳ, ቤት, ተማሪ, በሬ), against የማይቆጠሩ ስሞች (ስኳር, ዘይት, አሸዋ, ዉኃ, ቡና, ብርሃን, ጨለማ, ኃይል).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Sentence and Its Marks. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the six word kinds in the books' order and say each kind's job.
2. Pick the noun and the verb out of a sentence; find the verb at the sentence's end.
3. Spot an adjective and say what it tells about its noun.
4. Tell a coordinating conjunction from a subordinating one.
5. Find the adverb and the preposition in a sentence.
6. Sort nouns into ወል ስም and የተጸውኦ ስም, and pick out a ጥቅል ስም in a word list.
7. Give the pronouns of all three persons (መደብ) and say whose place each stands in.
8. Choose the right pointing word for a thing near or far, and ask a question with each መጠይቅ word.
9. Sort nouns into የሚቆጠሩ and የማይቆጠሩ and say how the test works.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes noun and verb → the verb shows the action and stands at the sentence's end; the noun only names.
- Mixes adjective and adverb → an adjective tells about a noun (kind, quality, size); an adverb tells when, where or how the action is done.
- Mixes conjunction and preposition → a conjunction joins equals or sentences; a preposition stands before a noun (ወደ ቤት).
- Mixes common and proper → ask: does the word name any one of a kind (መምህር — any teacher), or one named one (ማህሌት — this one person)? Named one = የተጸውኦ ስም.
- Mixes pronoun persons → point: at yourself for እኔ (1st), at your listener for አንተ / አንቺ (2nd), over your shoulder for እሱ / እሷ (3rd).
- Counts the uncountable → try to state the number: three በሬ works; "three ዉኃ" does not — the number cannot be stated, so the noun is የማይቆጠሩ.
