# The Marks That Help Us Read — matching

Match each mark to its name.

1. : — a) የጥያቄ ምልክት (question mark)
2. ፣ — b) ሁለት ነጥብ (two dots)
3. ? — c) the four dots — the saying is finished
4. ። — d) ነጠላ ሰረዝ (comma)

**Answer key:** 1 — b) ሁለት ነጥብ · 2 — d) ነጠላ ሰረዝ · 3 — a)
የጥያቄ ምልክት · 4 — c) the four dots.
