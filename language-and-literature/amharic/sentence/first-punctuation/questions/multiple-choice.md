# The Marks That Help Us Read — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which mark is the ሁለት ነጥብ — the two dots?
a) ፣  b) :  c) ?
**Answer: b) :** — two dots, one over the other. It opens a
list.

**2.** Which mark closes a saying — the full stop of our
writing?
a) the four dots (።)  b) the comma (፣)  c) the two dots (:)
**Answer: a) the four dots** — the sentence is finished.

**3.** The ነጠላ ሰረዝ (comma) tells the reader to —
a) stop for good  b) ask a question  c) rest a moment, then go
on
**Answer: c)** — a comma is a small resting place inside the
sentence.

**4.** You read: ብርቱካን በላሁ? — "Did I eat an orange?" The mark
at the end is —
a) የጥያቄ ምልክት, the question mark  b) ሁለት ነጥብ, the two dots
c) ነጠላ ሰረዝ, the comma
**Answer: a)** — the sentence is asking, so the voice climbs.

**5.** A list is coming — "ቃላት: አበባ ዛፍ" (Words: flower,
tree). Which mark opens the list?
a) the comma  b) the question mark  c) the two dots
**Answer: c) the two dots** — they say "here they come."
