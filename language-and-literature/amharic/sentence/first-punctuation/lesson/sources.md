Honesty notes: (1) The placement records the four dots mark (::) with no Amharic name of its own; the lesson therefore names it in English — "the four dots" — and Picture 8214 labels it አራት ነጥብ, a descriptive label built from the book's own number word አራት (four, KG3 printed p.31), not a name the p.141 page gives. (2) The placement writes the two-dots and four-dots marks in the Latin forms ":" and "::"; in Amharic print the four dots are the single glyph ።, which is how the lesson and the picture draw them. (3) What each mark *does* (opens a list, closes a saying, a small rest, shows a question) is the ordinary working of these marks, taught so the names have something to hold; the book's own p.141 work is the marks and writing their names, which is the lesson's pass mark. (4) Picture 8214 is a drawn card made for this lesson.
