# Video script — The Marks That Help Us Read

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Little marks help us read. Four marks. Watch.

Two dots. **ሁለት ነጥብ** (hulet net'ib).
ቃላት: አበባ ዛፍ — Words: flower, tree.
The two dots open the list.

Four dots. The saying is finished.
ዛፍ ተከልኩ። — I planted a tree. Full stop. Done.

One comma. **ነጠላ ሰረዝ** (net'ela serez).
አበባ፣ ዛፍ እወዳለሁ — a small rest… then go on.

A question mark. **የጥያቄ ምልክት** (yet'iyaqé milik't).
ብርቱካን በላሁ? Hear my voice climb? The sentence is asking!

Two dots open. Four dots close. A comma rests. A question asks.
Those are the marks that help us read.
