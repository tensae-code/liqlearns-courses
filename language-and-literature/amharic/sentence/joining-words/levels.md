# Joining Words — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Coordinating joiners: አያያዦች
Pass mark: join two simple sentences into a ድርብ compound with እና, ወይም, ግን, ነገርግን, እንጂ, ቢሆንም, ስለዚህ, በመሆኑም or ምክንያቱም, and say what the chosen joiner makes the pair mean — adding, choosing, pushing, or following.

## Sub lesson 2 — Subordinate conjunctions: ጥገኛ መስተፃምር
Pass mark: spot the subordinate conjunction (ስለ, እንደ, ከ, ለ, ቢ, ሲ, ሊ) binding a leaning clause, and finish an unfinished leaning half (ሱሰኛነት ሰዎችን ስለሚጎዳ …) with a main clause that completes it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Transitional words: መሸጋገሪያ ቃላት
Pass mark: read a short paragraph and name the transitional word at each crossing and its direction (forward — ስለዚህ; against — ይሁንእንጂ; back to the reason — ምክንያቱም), and plant one transitional word of each direction in writing of your own.
