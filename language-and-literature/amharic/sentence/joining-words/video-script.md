# Video script — Joining Words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two sentences. Two stops. Two breaths.
**የሒሣብ ትምህርት እወዳለሁ። ጥሩ ውጤት አላመጣሁም።**

Now join them. One word does the joining:
**የሒሣብ ትምህርት እወዳለሁ፤ ይሁን እንጂ ጥሩ ውጤት አላመጣሁም።**
Feel the push? **ይሁን እንጂ** — however.

The joiners are a family.
**እና** adds. **ወይም** offers a choice.
**ነገርግን** pushes back. **ስለዚህ** shows the result.
**ምክንያቱም** gives the reason.

Some joiners make a clause lean.
**አህመድ ቶሎ ስለመጣ ደብተሩን አገኘ።**
*Because Ahmed came early* — that half leans. It waits.
*He found his exercise book* — that half stands.
**ስለ, እንደ, ከ, ለ, ቢ, ሲ, ሊ** — the leaning joiners. **ጥገኛ መስተፃምር.**

And in a whole text, the same words carry you across.
**ስለዚህ** — forward. **ይሁንእንጂ** — against. **ምክንያቱም** — back to the reason.
These are the **መሸጋገሪያ ቃላት** — transitional words.

One family. Three sizes of joining.
Your turn: join two sentences — and choose the word that joins them true.
