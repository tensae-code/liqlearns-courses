# Hands-on — Joining Words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Joining cards
Write eight joiner cards: እና, ወይም, ግን, ነገርግን, ስለዚህ, በመሆኑም, ምክንያቱም, ይሁን እንጂ. Write two sentences on two big cards. A partner holds up a joiner card; you read the joined sentence aloud and say what the joiner did — added, pushed, or showed the result. Go through all eight cards.

## The leaning rope
Tie a string between two chairs. Hang a card with a leaning half on it — ሱሰኛነት ሰዎችን ስለሚጎዳ … — and let it droop. It cannot stand. Now hang the main-clause card beside it — ከሱስ ነፃ እንሁን — and pull the string tight. Say it whole: the leaning half needed the standing half. Make two more leaning halves of your own and finish them the same way.

## Crossing stones
Lay five paper stones in a path. On stone 1, 3 and 5 write an idea each (ሐሳብ). On stones 2 and 4 write a transitional word — ስለዚህ on one, ይሁንእንጂ on the other. Walk the path, stepping and reading: idea — crossing — idea — crossing — idea. Then write the whole path as a paragraph.
