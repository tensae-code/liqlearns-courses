# Lesson plan — Joining Words

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Joining Words
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Sentence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** joining words work at three sizes — coordinating joiners (አያያዦች) tie two standing sentences into one compound (ድርብ); subordinate conjunctions (ጥገኛ መስተፃምር — ስለ, እንደ, ከ, ለ, ቢ, ሲ, ሊ) bend one clause so it leans on another; and transitional words (መሸጋገሪያ ቃላት) carry a reader across a whole text from idea to idea.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Word Kinds (whose Sub lesson 4 met conjunctions — አያያዥ ቃላት — and sorted መስታጽምር into coordinating ወደረኛ and subordinating ጥገኛ) and on The Sentence and Its Marks (ድርብ and ውስብስብ sentences). This lesson grows both: the full joiner family, the leaning clause finished and unfinished, and the same words at work across a text.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
1. **Coordinating joiners — አያያዦች** — the grown family (እና, ግን, ነገርግን, እንጂ, ወይም, ቢሆንም, ስለዚህ, በመሆኑም, ምክንያቱም, ይሁን እንጂ) tying two simple sentences into one ድርብ compound; the work is in choosing the joiner that matches what the second sentence does to the first.
2. **Subordinate conjunctions — ጥገኛ መስተፃምር** — ስለ, እንደ, ከ, ለ, ቢ, ሲ, ሊ make one clause lean on another (አህመድ ቶሎ ስለመጣ ደብተሩን አገኘ); a leaning half can stand unfinished, waiting for its main clause (ሱሰኛነት ሰዎችን ስለሚጎዳ …).
3. **Transitional words — መሸጋገሪያ ቃላት** — the same family at the head of sentences in a text, carrying the reader across: forward (ስለዚህ), against (ይሁንእንጂ), back to the reason (ምክንያቱም). *(This sub lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence — see sources.md.)*

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Word Kinds (Sub lesson 4 — conjunctions) and The Sentence and Its Marks (Sub lessons 3–4 — joining with ስለ; compound and complex sentences). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Join two simple sentences into a ድርብ compound with each coordinating joiner, and say what each joiner makes the pair mean.
2. Choose the right joiner from a bank for a given pair of sentences.
3. Spot a subordinate conjunction (ስለ, እንደ, ከ, ለ, ቢ, ሲ, ሊ) and name the leaning clause it binds.
4. Finish an unfinished leaning half with a main clause that completes its sense.
5. Plant transitional words at the crossings of a paragraph so a reader can follow the path of ideas.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Picks እና for every join → ask what the second sentence does to the first: adds? pushes? follows? The doing picks the word — ነገርግን pushes, ስለዚህ follows.
- Thinks a leaning half is a full sentence → read it alone and ask "…and so what?" A ስለ- or ሲ- clause always leaves that question hanging; the main clause answers it.
- Writes a paragraph of separate stones → read it back and stop at each full stop: "How do we get to the next idea — forward, against, or back to the reason?" Plant the word that walks the reader there.
