# Joining Words — matching

Match each joining word to what it does.

1. (Sub lesson 1) እና — a) carries the reader forward to the result: "therefore"
2. (Sub lesson 1) ነገርግን — b) adds the second beside the first: "and"
3. (Sub lesson 2) ስለ — c) pushes against the first: "but"
4. (Sub lesson 2) ሲ — d) binds a leaning clause of reason: "because / since"
5. (Sub lesson 3) ስለዚህ — e) binds a leaning clause of time: "when / while"

**Answer key:** 1 — b, 2 — c, 3 — d, 4 — e, 5 — a.
