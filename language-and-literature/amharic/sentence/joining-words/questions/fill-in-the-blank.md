# Joining Words — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right joining word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "የሒሣብ ትምህርት እወዳለሁ፤ ______ ጥሩ ውጤት አላመጣሁም።" — the second sentence pushes against the first.
**Answer: ይሁን እንጂ** (however) — ግን or ነገርግን also push, and both are right answers here.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "አህመድ ቶሎ ______ ደብተሩን አገኘ።" — the leaning verb carries the subordinate conjunction meaning "because".
**Answer: ስለመጣ** — ስለ (because) bound inside the verb መጣ.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "ዝናቡ አልዘነበም። ______ አርሶአደሩ ዘሩን አቋረጠ።" ("The rain did not fall. ______, the farmer held back his seed.") — a result crossing at the head of the sentence.
**Answer: ስለዚህ** (therefore).
