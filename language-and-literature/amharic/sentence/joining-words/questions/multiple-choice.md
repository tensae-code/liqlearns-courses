# Joining Words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A coordinating joiner (አያያዥ) joins —
a) two simple sentences into one compound (ድርብ) sentence  b) a noun to its adjective  c) a verb to its ending
**Answer: a) two simple sentences into one compound sentence** — each side could stand alone; the joiner ties them.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To join a pair where the second sentence is the *result* of the first, the joiner to choose is —
a) ወይም  b) ስለዚህ  c) እንጂ
**Answer: b) ስለዚህ** — "therefore": the second sentence follows from the first. ወይም offers a choice; እንጂ turns back.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In አህመድ ቶሎ ስለመጣ ደብተሩን አገኘ, the subordinate conjunction is —
a) ቶሎ  b) ስለ (inside ስለመጣ)  c) ደብተሩን
**Answer: b) ስለ** — it rides inside the verb and makes that clause lean on the main clause.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A clause bound by ጥገኛ መስተፃምር —
a) always stands alone  b) leans on another clause and cannot give a full sense alone  c) is not part of the sentence
**Answer: b)** — the leaning clause waits for its main clause, exactly as ሲመጣ waits for its binder.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A transitional word (መሸጋገሪያ ቃል) does its work —
a) inside one word  b) at a crossing between ideas in a text, often at the head of a sentence  c) only at the end of a book
**Answer: b)** — it carries the reader from the idea before to the idea after.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "The rain failed. ____, the farmers stored what grain they had." The crossing is a result — the word is —
a) ይሁንእንጂ (however)  b) ስለዚህ (therefore)  c) ወይም (or)
**Answer: b) ስለዚህ** — the storing follows from the failed rain; ይሁንእንጂ would push against it.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** "ዝናብ አልዘነበም፤ ____ ሰዎች ውኃ አጠጡ።" (It did not rain; ____ people fetched water.) The second half follows as a result — the word is —
a) ወይም  b) ስለሆነም  c) እና only
**Answer: b) ስለሆነም** — the fetching follows from the failed rain: therefore.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Which conjunction warns what happens if the first part fails?
a) አለበለዚያ — otherwise  b) እና  c) ወይም
**Answer: a) አለበለዚያ** — otherwise carries the warning; ወይም offers a choice, እና adds.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** "ሻይ ____ ቡና ትጠጣለህ?" (Will you drink tea ____ coffee?) offers a choice — the word is —
a) ስለሆነም  b) አለበለዚያ  c) ወይም
**Answer: c) ወይም** — tea or coffee: one of two, chosen.
