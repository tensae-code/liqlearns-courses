# Joining Words — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Join this pair with ነገርግን into one ድርብ sentence: ልጁ ትምህርቱን ይወዳል። / ውጤቱ ዝቅ ብሏል።
**Answer: the two standing sentences in one line, joined by ነገርግን (or ግን / ይሁን እንጂ — any pushing joiner, with the push named).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Finish this leaning half with a main clause of your own: ሱሰኛነት ሰዎችን ስለሚጎዳ …
**Answer: a standing main clause completing the sense — the book's own completion is ከሱስ ነፃ እንሁን ("let us be free of addiction"); any true completion counts.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write three sentences on one topic. Begin the second with ስለዚህ and the third with ይሁንእንጂ.
**Answer: three sentences where ስለዚህ truly carries a result forward and ይሁንእንጂ truly pushes against the sentence before it.**
