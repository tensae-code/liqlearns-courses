# Speaking and Performing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the speaking slots of the Ethiopian books, in their own climb: the tale's two roles, then scenes, then argument, then the judged read-aloud.

## Sub lesson 1 — Storytelling roles — ተራች and መላሽ
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: hold either role for a whole tale: the teller keeps the tale moving with its opening and closing; the responder answers and reacts so the telling stays a two-person performance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Role play
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: speak in character for a whole scene, in pairs and in groups, answering the other parts as they come.

## Sub lesson 3 — Debate
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: support or oppose a set claim from prepared notes, in turns, answering the other side's points.

## Sub lesson 4 — Reading aloud well
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read a text aloud meeting the trio — accuracy, speed, expression — in each of the three modes: paired, group, echo.
