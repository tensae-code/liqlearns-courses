# Speaking and Performing — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two storytelling roles, in Amharic?
**Answer: ተራች (the teller) and መላሽ (the responder).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one debate motion from the books.
**Answer: any one of — farming or teaching, which is better; chicken raising or vegetable growing; radio better than television; traditional sport vs modern sport; agriculture more useful than trade; creating your own work vs government employment; should bamboo work be modernised.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What are the three modes of reading aloud practice?
**Answer: paired reading (ጥንደ ንባብ), group/choral reading (የህብረት ንባብ), echo reading (ማማዱ ንባብ).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Give one of the student book's own fluency rules.
**Answer: any one of — don't count letters one by one; don't turn back; pause only at phrase/sentence ends; read questions in a questioning tone.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In role play, why do you put the book down instead of reading at each other?
**Answer: a role play is talking TO each other as the parts — knowing what your part wants and answering as they come; reading lines aloud is not playing the part.**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two of the debaters' rules in a formal debate.
**Answer: any two of — prepare well on the topic; greet teacher and students respectfully; introduce the topic; present points in order; listen exactly and note the opponent's points for the rebuttal; support points with evidence; keep the time limit; keep eye contact; thank the audience at the end.**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the two roles in a panel discussion, in Amharic, and say what each does.
**Answer: አወያይ — the moderator, who runs the discussion; ተወያይ — the participants, who bring the ideas.**

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** What is the refrain of the harvest work-poem, and what does it say?
**Answer: "ሆ ይላለ ገበሬ" — "Ho — sing, farmer!"**

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** What are የቅኔ አፈታት ዘዴዎች?
**Answer: the methods of qene interpretation — the named methods by which a qene poem is untied to reach its hidden meaning.**
