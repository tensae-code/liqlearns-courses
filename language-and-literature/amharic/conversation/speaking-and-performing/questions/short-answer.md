# Speaking and Performing — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two storytelling roles, in Amharic?
**Answer: ተራች (the teller) and መላሽ (the responder).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one debate motion from the books.
**Answer: any one of — farming or teaching, which is better; chicken raising or vegetable growing; radio better than television; traditional sport vs modern sport; agriculture more useful than trade; creating your own work vs government employment; should bamboo work be modernised.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What are the three modes of reading aloud practice?
**Answer: paired reading (ጥንደ ንባብ), group/choral reading (የህብረት ንባብ), echo reading (ማማዱ ንባብ).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Give one of the student book's own fluency rules.
**Answer: any one of — don't count letters one by one; don't turn back; pause only at phrase/sentence ends; read questions in a questioning tone.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In role play, why do you put the book down instead of reading at each other?
**Answer: a role play is talking TO each other as the parts — knowing what your part wants and answering as they come; reading lines aloud is not playing the part.**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two of the debaters' rules in a formal debate.
**Answer: any two of — prepare well on the topic; greet teacher and students respectfully; introduce the topic; present points in order; listen exactly and note the opponent's points for the rebuttal; support points with evidence; keep the time limit; keep eye contact; thank the audience at the end.**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the two roles in a panel discussion, in Amharic, and say what each does.
**Answer: አወያይ — the moderator, who runs the discussion; ተወያይ — the participants, who bring the ideas.**

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** What is the refrain of the harvest work-poem, and what does it say?
**Answer: "ሆ ይላለ ገበሬ" — "Ho — sing, farmer!"**

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** What are የቅኔ አፈታት ዘዴዎች?
**Answer: the methods of qene interpretation — the named methods by which a qene poem is untied to reach its hidden meaning.**

**10. (Sub lesson 12)** Name two of the p.9 nature talk topics.
**Answer:** Any two of: rivers, mountains, forests, wild animals, waterfalls, valleys.

**11. (Sub lesson 13)** What topic does the p.42 conversation treat?
**Answer:** The benefit of mutual help.

**12. (Sub lesson 14)** Name two athletes in the book's list.
**Answer:** Any two of: Haile Gebrselassie, Abebe Bikila, Derartu Tulu.

**13. (Sub lesson 15)** Name two of the four speech tips.
**Answer:** Any two of: support with evidence; fit to the listener; follow the listener's feelings; persuade with body movement and varied expression.

**14. (Sub lesson 16)** What two harms do the p.119 field talks research?
**Answer:** The harm of cigarette smoking, and the harm of chat chewing.

**15. (Sub lesson 17)** In the verse-court role play, who speaks the pleas?
**Answer:** The accuser and the accused, in verse, before the judges and crowd.

**16. (Sub lesson 18)** At which level is a work done with neighbours, in the talk's terms?
**Answer:** The kebele level — the first rung.

**17. (Sub lesson 19)** From one declarative sentence, the grammar partner generates questions using —
**Answer:** Different question words — who, when, how, by what, why, from where.

**18. (Sub lesson 20)** Name one of the four set speech titles.
**Answer:** Any of: the amazing bird; the day I won't forget; the cart-owner and his mule; our school's protection success.

**19. (Sub lesson 21)** What does the p.46 set title say?
**Answer:** አርበኞች የሚኖሩት ለሀገርና ለሕዝብ ነው — patriots live for country and people.

**20. (Sub lesson 22)** State the mothers-vs-fathers debate's two sides.
**Answer:** 'Mothers are decisive in raising children in order' vs 'fathers are decisive.'

**21. (Sub lesson 23)** What second talk shares pp.90–91?
**Answer:** A talk on the horror of war, comparing the verses' ideas with recent destruction.

**22. (Sub lesson 24)** Name the two groups of working children the p.108 talks treat.
**Answer:** Shoe-shining children in town and cattle-herding children in the country.

**23. (Sub lesson 25)** How does Eli introduce herself?
**Answer:** 'ኤሊ በትዕግስቱ' — Eli by-her-patience — of መዳብቸ village, celebrating her 10th year.

**24. (Sub lesson 26)** Name three heads of the reactions bigar.
**Answer:** Any three of: what astonished me, what saddened me, what angered me, what pleased me, what excited me.

**25. (Sub lesson 27)** How does she answer the cut-heel verse?
**Answer:** 'If people knew, what grieves you? Come to me, and I'll hold you night and day.'
