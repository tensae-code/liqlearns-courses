# Speaking and Performing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tale told with a silent responder is still a full two-person telling.
**Answer: False** — the responder answers; a silent መላሽ makes it a lecture, not a telling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the judge role play, each speaker stays in character — judge, plaintiff, defendant.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** If your reading-aloud words keep going wrong, the trio says your speed is right and accuracy must catch up.
**Answer: False** — the trio balances all three; words going wrong means speed was too high for accuracy.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** A good debater may ignore the other side's points and only repeat their own.
**Answer: False** — the debaters' rules say the opposite: listen exactly, note the opponent's points, and build the rebuttal from them.

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** The moderator's work includes introducing the topic and its purpose at the start, and summarising at the end.
**Answer: True.**

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** In the oral-poetry work, learners first read the poem silently, and only later hear it performed.
**Answer: False** — the teacher performs the poem aloud first; learners listen for what makes it a poem, then chant it.

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** A qene poem carries only the meaning on its surface.
**Answer: False** — a qene poem carries two meanings: the surface meaning and a hidden second meaning under it.

**8. (Sub lesson 12)** Nature talk topics include rivers, waterfalls and valleys.
**Answer: True**

**9. (Sub lesson 13)** An interview report presents the professional's answers in order, from prepared questions and notes.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 14)** Catherine Hamlin and Derartu Tulu are both among the book's famous-lives choices.
**Answer: True**

**11. (Sub lesson 15)** The water-source talk's web holds sources, uses, services, harms and causes of harm.
**Answer: True**

**12. (Sub lesson 16)** Field-research talks name their source — the health institution or people consulted.
**Answer: True**

**13. (Sub lesson 17)** Iddir and equb are researched by asking parents and local people.

**14. (Sub lesson 18)** The works named should grow with the rung — from neighbours to the whole country.

**17. (Sub lesson 21)** True-story speaking tells of real people who worked diligently and changed their poverty life.
**Answer: True**

**18. (Sub lesson 22)** The judges' decision must be respected by both sides.

**23. (Sub lesson 27)** His 'chaff of your door' verse is answered by 'be the fruit, and I'll taste you'.

**24. (Sub lesson 30)** In the Grade 8 guide's Chapter 3, groups present through their representatives as an informative speech (በመረጃ ሰጪ ንግግር መልክ).

**25. (Sub lesson 30)** The Grade 7 teacher guide carries its own help-note for running a debate and rules of debate presentation (የክርክር አቀራረብ).

**26. (Sub lesson 31)** In the industry speaking part, groups of five research industrial villages (የኢንዱስትሪ መንደሮች) and report in a speech.
