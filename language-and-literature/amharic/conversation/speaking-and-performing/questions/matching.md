# Speaking and Performing — matching

Match each Amharic term (1–5) to its meaning (a–e).

1. ተራች — ?
2. መላሽ — ?
3. ትክክለኛነት — ?
4. ፍጥነት — ?
5. ገላጭነት — ?

a) speed — a good, even pace
b) the responder in a tale
c) expression — a voice that shows meaning
d) the teller of a tale
e) accuracy — every word right

**Answer key: 1 — d, 2 — b, 3 — e, 4 — a, 5 — c.** (Sub lessons 1 and 4)

Match each term of the formal spoken forms (1–4) to its meaning (a–d).

1. ክርክር — ?
2. ተወያይ — ?
3. ቃላዊ ግጥም — ?
4. ቅኔ — ?

a) a poem whose hidden meaning must be untied
b) a debate — an ordered argument held before judges
c) oral poetry — a poem performed aloud and chanted
d) the participants in a panel discussion

**Answer key: 1 — b (Sub lesson 5), 2 — d (Sub lesson 6), 3 — c (Sub lesson 7), 4 — a (Sub lesson 8).**
