# Speaking and Performing — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a tale opening exchange: the ተራች's first line and the መላሽ's answering line, for a tale you know.
**Answer guide: two lines, clearly one teller and one responder; the responder's line answers the teller's.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Choose the motion "radio is better than television." Write three notes for the side you support, and one note answering the other side.
**Answer guide: three supporting notes from what you know/have read; one note that answers an opposing point, not just repeats your own.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the fluency trio in Amharic and English, and under each, what it watches in a reading.
**Answer guide: ትክክለኛነት accuracy — every word right; ፍጥነት speed — a good even pace; ገላጭነት expression — voice showing meaning.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Write the judges' part of a formal debate as a checklist, in the order the judges do the work.
**Answer guide: greet; introduce the left and right debaters and the topic; remind about the time; ask the class to follow quietly; summarise the debate's substance; announce the winner.**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** Choose the panel topic "why mutual help is needed." Write the moderator's opening: the thanks, the topic, and its purpose — three or four sentences.
**Answer guide: respectful thanks to the participants; the topic named plainly; its purpose stated (why the audience should listen); the talking then opens with an opening idea.**
