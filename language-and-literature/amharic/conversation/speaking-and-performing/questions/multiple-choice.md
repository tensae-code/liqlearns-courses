# Speaking and Performing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a tale told the Ethiopian way, the መላሽ (melash) is —
a) the listener who must stay silent  b) the responder, who answers the teller  c) the judge of the tale
**Answer: b) the responder, who answers the teller.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In role play, your first job is to —
a) read your lines loudly  b) know what your part wants, and speak as the part  c) finish before the other part speaks
**Answer: b) know what your part wants, and speak as the part.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In a debate, you win listeners by —
a) speaking loudest  b) answering the other side's points, in turns, from prepared notes  c) never letting the other side speak
**Answer: b) answering the other side's points, in turns, from prepared notes.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The fluency trio is —
a) accuracy, speed, expression  b) loud, louder, loudest  c) first, second, third reading
**Answer: a) accuracy, speed, expression** — ትክክለኛነት, ፍጥነት, ገላጭነት.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In a formal debate (ክርክር), who announces the winner at the end?
a) the louder debater  b) the judges  c) the audience, by clapping
**Answer: b) the judges** — announcing the winner is the last of the judges' rules.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In a panel discussion (ውይይት), the አወያይ is —
a) a participant who brings ideas  b) the moderator, who runs the discussion  c) a judge who announces a winner
**Answer: b) the moderator, who runs the discussion** — a panel has no winner; the ተወያይ are the participants.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** An oral poem (ቃላዊ ግጥም) is first met by —
a) reading it silently from the book  b) hearing it performed aloud, then chanting it  c) copying it into a notebook
**Answer: b) hearing it performed aloud, then chanting it** — the teacher performs it first; the voice comes before the page.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Interpreting a qene (ቅኔ) poem means —
a) memorising it by heart  b) untying it, to find the hidden meaning under the surface meaning  c) reading it as fast as you can
**Answer: b) untying it, to find the hidden meaning under the surface meaning** — by the methods of qene interpretation, የቅኔ አፈታት ዘዴዎች.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** ባትሪ is the name of…
a) A battery  b) A camera  c) A radio
**Answer: a) A battery** — Grade 2 pp.122–124.

**10. (Sub lesson 9)** የልብስ ማጠቢያ ማሽን means…
a) The clothes-washing machine  b) The television room  c) A telephone box
**Answer: a) The clothes-washing machine** — the device-phrase model, Grade 2 pp.122–124.

**11. (Sub lesson 9)** When you complete a telephone conversation, the last thing is…
a) The greeting  b) Saying goodbye properly  c) The message
**Answer: b) Saying goodbye properly** — greeting, message, thanks, goodbye — in that order.

**12. (Sub lesson 10)** In the one-minute fluency check, what comes first?
a) Accuracy — every word read as it stands  b) Speed at any cost  c) Shouting
**Answer: a) Accuracy — every word read as it stands** — the book's timed check.

**13. (Sub lesson 10)** The Wise Girl's fluent-reading practice teaches the saying that friends are what?
a) Wealth that time does not erase  b) A burden  c) A game
**Answer: a) Wealth that time does not erase** — the story's own moral, read fluently.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** In the river-crossing puzzle, what may the goat never be left alone with?
a) The leopard — and the leaves  b) The boatman  c) The river
**Answer: a) The leopard — and the leaves** — the puzzle's two dangers.

**15. (Sub lesson 11)** The house-or-friends debate grows out of which story?
a) The Wise Girl  b) The Scale  c) Fear
**Answer: a) The Wise Girl** — the mother who told her children to build houses.

**16. (Sub lesson 11)** Under which heading do ORNAMENTS go in the dress debate's writing task?
a) ጌጣጌጥ  b) ቀሚስ  c) አልባሳት
**Answer: a) ጌጣጌጥ** — dresses / clothes / ornaments — the three headings.

**17. (Sub lesson 11)** In the accidents debate, which two sides face each other?
a) Pedestrians' fault against drivers' fault  b) Drivers against conductors  c) Police against passengers
**Answer: a) Pedestrians' fault against drivers' fault** — the review's own motion.

**18. (Sub lesson 11)** What phrase names tourism's cultural harm in the Grade 6 debate?
a) ባህል ወረራ — cultural invasion  b) ጭስ አልባ ኢንዱስትሪ  c) የቱሪስት መስህብ
**Answer: a) ባህል ወረራ — cultural invasion** — the harm side's key term.


**19. (Sub lesson 12)** A research talk on a rare animal covers —
a) only its name  b) its life, feeding and breeding  c) its price
**Answer: b) its life, feeding and breeding** — life, feeding, breeding — the book's three heads.

**20. (Sub lesson 13)** A solution-seeking conversation differs from chat because —
a) it is longer  b) it raises opposing views to reach a solution or conclusion  c) it is quieter
**Answer: b) it raises opposing views to reach a solution or conclusion** — the book's own distinction: idea development towards a conclusion, vs time-passing chat.

**21. (Sub lesson 14)** A spoken biography should keep —
a) every written detail  b) the order of years and the turning points  c) only the birth date
**Answer: b) the order of years and the turning points** — the ear needs order and turning points; eye-only detail is cut.

**22. (Sub lesson 15)** The speech-tips note asks you to fit the speech to —
a) the clock only  b) the listener, with evidence and expression  c) the teacher's book
**Answer: b) the listener, with evidence and expression** — listener, evidence, feelings, body movement and varied expression — the note's four helps.

**23. (Sub lesson 16)** A group report presents —
a) the representative's private opinion  b) the group's agreed conclusion  c) the longest speech
**Answer: b) the group's agreed conclusion** — through the representative, the GROUP speaks.

**24. (Sub lesson 17)** In the doctor role play, the doctor's advice is built from —
a) general sayings  b) the chat reading's named harms  c) the patient's wishes
**Answer: b) the chat reading's named harms** — health, economy and social harms from the reading arm the advice.

**25. (Sub lesson 18)** The talk's levels are —
a) family, clan, town  b) kebele, woreda, zone, region, nation  c) school, district, city
**Answer: b) kebele, woreda, zone, region, nation** — the five administrative rungs of the organiser.

**26. (Sub lesson 19)** አውቆ አበድ means —
a) going mad unknowingly  b) going mad KNOWINGLY — deliberately  c) pretending to sleep
**Answer: b) going mad KNOWINGLY — deliberately** — the discussion asks whether such deliberate madness profits or harms.

**27. (Sub lesson 20)** Speech preparation in the book means —
a) memorising another's speech  b) writing your speech text first, then presenting it  c) speaking without any text
**Answer: b) writing your speech text first, then presenting it** — the prepared text is the speaking's backbone at this grade.

**28. (Sub lesson 21)** A speech supporting a saying must carry —
a) only the saying, repeated  b) examples — one for each claim  c) a song
**Answer: b) examples — one for each claim** — examples are the support; the saying alone is a poster, not a speech.

**29. (Sub lesson 22)** In debate, you attack —
a) the opponent  b) the opponent's IDEA, never the person  c) the judges
**Answer: b) the opponent's IDEA, never the person** — the note is explicit: the idea, not the debater.

**30. (Sub lesson 23)** A two-sided topic is set so the class argues —
a) one side only  b) both sides, in two groups  c) neither side
**Answer: b) both sides, in two groups** — felling's harm AND restoration's benefit — both heard, both weighed.

**31. (Sub lesson 24)** The p.108 talks close with —
a) a song  b) what you would do for such children if you had power and responsibility  c) a vote
**Answer: b) what you would do for such children if you had power and responsibility** — the personal pledge is the talks' appointed ending.

**32. (Sub lesson 25)** In the protocol, the questions are —
a) asked without preparation  b) prepared by one student and reviewed/corrected by another before asking  c) written by the teacher only
**Answer: b) prepared by one student and reviewed/corrected by another before asking** — prepare, review, then ask — points 1 and 2.

**33. (Sub lesson 26)** A role speech stays inside —
a) every topic  b) its brief — the peace ambassador speaks peace  c) no topic
**Answer: b) its brief — the peace ambassador speaks peace** — each ambassador speaks their own brief: development, peace, or culture.

**34. (Sub lesson 27)** In the exchange, each of her verses —
a) repeats his  b) answers his image and turns it  c) changes the subject
**Answer: b) answers his image and turns it** — chaff becomes fruit; the wound becomes an invitation — each answer turns the image offered.

**35. (Sub lesson 28)** ግብረ መልስ means —
a) a riddle  b) feedback  c) a timetable
**Answer: b) feedback** — Feedback.

**36. (Sub lesson 28)** In this talk, the teacher —
a) only listens silently  b) checks the questions, corrects, and gives feedback  c) reads a new story
**Answer: b) checks the questions, corrects, and gives feedback** — Check, correct, feedback.

**37. (Sub lesson 28)** A full answer to a story question is given —
a) in one word  b) in a full sentence  c) by pointing only
**Answer: b) in a full sentence** — Answer in a full sentence.

**38. (Sub lesson 29)** Marta's Diary is retold —
a) in your own words, in its order  b) backwards  c) by copying it
**Answer: a) in your own words, in its order** — Own words, in order.

**39. (Sub lesson 29)** Marta's father took her to —
a) a market only  b) Hamle 19 Park — a special green place  c) the airport
**Answer: b) Hamle 19 Park — a special green place** — To Hamle 19 Park.

**40. (Sub lesson 29)** New words from the diary are practised —
a) in sentences about real places  b) by silence  c) by tearing the page
**Answer: a) in sentences about real places** — In your own sentences.

**41. (Sub lesson 30)** The Grade 7 guide's speaking note tells the teacher to give special attention to —
a) speaking speed  b) word choice (ለቃላት ምርጫ ልዩ ትኩረት)  c) loudness
**Answer: b) word choice (ለቃላት ምርጫ ልዩ ትኩረት)**

**42. (Sub lesson 31)** The Grade 9 guide's homework variant of the successful-women speech is a speech about —
a) a chosen woman athlete  b) a famous general  c) a market trader
**Answer: a) a chosen woman athlete**

**43. (Sub lesson 32)** A Grade 9 guide discussion group of five appoints —
a) five speakers and no officers  b) a chair and four discussants  c) a chair, a secretary and three discussants
**Answer: c) a chair, a secretary and three discussants**

**44. (Sub lesson 33)** The hospital role-play «ወደ ሆስፒታል መሄድ» is performed in groups of —
a) two  b) five  c) the whole class at once
**Answer: b) five**

**45. (Sub lesson 34)** Which of these is one of the guide's quoted conversation extenders?
a) «ከዚያስ?» — and then?  b) «ዝም በል» — be quiet  c) «አልቅሷል» — it is finished
**Answer: a) «ከዚያስ?» — and then?**
