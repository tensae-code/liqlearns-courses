# Video script — Speaking and Performing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A tale needs TWO people.

I am the **ተራች** — the teller. I tell the tale.
You are the **መላሽ** — the responder. You answer me.
Teller… responder… teller… responder… NOW it is a tale!

Now we play a scene. I am the judge. You bring your case.
Speak as your part. Stay as your part.

Now a debate! This side says YES. That side says NO.
Notes ready. Turns only. Answer their point!

Last — read aloud. Three dials:
**ትክክለኛነት** — every word right.
**ፍጥነት** — a good speed.
**ገላጭነት** — a voice with feeling.
In pairs… in a group… echo after me…

Your voice is an instrument. Play it well.

---

Now the debate — the WHOLE debate. **ክርክር**.
Debaters: prepare… greet… introduce…
points in order…
LISTEN. Write their points down. Answer THOSE.
Evidence. Watch the time. Eyes up. Thank you.
Judges: introduce the left… the right… the topic.
Quiet, please. At the end — the summary. The winner.

Now a panel. **ውይይት**.
You are the **አወያይ** — the moderator. You hold the talking.
You are a **ተወያይ** — a participant. You bring an idea.
Topic first. Gather your facts.
Start on time. Ideas in order. Reasons strong.
Respect every idea. Finish on time. Summarise. Thank you.

Now — no book. LISTEN.
The teacher sings the work-poem. **ቃላዊ ግጥም**.
ሆ ይላለ ገበሬ — Ho, sing, farmer!
Again — all together! Now YOUR poem. Voice UP.

Last, a small poem with a secret. **ቅኔ**.
On top — one meaning. Underneath — another.
Untie it slowly. What ELSE is it saying?
That untying has methods — **የቅኔ አፈታት ዘዴዎች** —
and you learn them poem by poem.
