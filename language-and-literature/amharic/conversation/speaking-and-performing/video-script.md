# Video script — Speaking and Performing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A tale needs TWO people.

I am the **ተራች** — the teller. I tell the tale.
You are the **መላሽ** — the responder. You answer me.
Teller… responder… teller… responder… NOW it is a tale!

Now we play a scene. I am the judge. You bring your case.
Speak as your part. Stay as your part.

Now a debate! This side says YES. That side says NO.
Notes ready. Turns only. Answer their point!

Last — read aloud. Three dials:
**ትክክለኛነት** — every word right.
**ፍጥነት** — a good speed.
**ገላጭነት** — a voice with feeling.
In pairs… in a group… echo after me…

Your voice is an instrument. Play it well.
