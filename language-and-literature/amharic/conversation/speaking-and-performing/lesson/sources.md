Honesty notes: (1) The tale openings'/closings' actual formula words are not quoted in the placements (the student book names the formulas and the roles); the lesson names them exactly as placed and invents no formula text. (2) The fluency trio and the three reading modes rest on teacher-guide evidence and are labelled so in the table; the lesson as a whole is pupil-taught through its student-book parts. (3) Both teacher guides are partial scans (see book lines); every guide page cited is inside the secured pages. (4) Transliterations terach/melash are this lesson's reading aids; the placements give the Amharic terms without transliteration.
