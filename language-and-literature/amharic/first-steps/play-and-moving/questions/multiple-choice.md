# Play and Moving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cart is going away from you. Your hands are —
a) pushing  b) pulling  c) lifting
**Answer: a) pushing** — it moves away from you.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The cart is coming towards you. You are —
a) pushing  b) pulling  c) lifting
**Answer: b) pulling** — it comes to you.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The box comes up off the ground. You are —
a) pushing  b) pulling  c) lifting
**Answer: c) lifting** — up off the ground.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which movement uses your legs to go fast?
a) throwing  b) running  c) carrying
**Answer: b) running**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the exercise, what comes after arms up?
a) arms down  b) arms on the chest  c) arms at the waist
**Answer: c) arms at the waist** — the order is: up, waist, chest, down.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Playing and cleaning up together is called —
a) cooperation  b) sleeping  c) hiding
**Answer: a) cooperation** — መተባበር — working together.
