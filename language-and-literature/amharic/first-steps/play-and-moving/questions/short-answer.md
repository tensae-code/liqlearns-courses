# Play and Moving — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line. For a young child, answering out loud is a full answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point to the child who is pushing. What is the child doing?
**Answer:** pushing — መግፋት.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four arm positions in order.
**Answer:** arms up, arms at the waist, arms on the chest, arms down.
