# Play and Moving — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulling a cart makes it come towards you.
**Answer: true**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pushing a table sends it away from you.
**Answer: true**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first arm position is arms down.
**Answer: false** — arms up is first.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Jumping is a way our bodies move.
**Answer: true**
