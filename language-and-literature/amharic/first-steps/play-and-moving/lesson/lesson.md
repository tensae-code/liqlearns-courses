# Play and Moving

## Do it first

Push a cart or a box across the floor. Now pull it back to you. Now run to the door and back, and jump up high once. You just did the whole lesson with your body. Now we learn the words for what you did.

## Sub lesson 1 — Push, pull, lift

When we move a thing, our hands do one of three things.

**Push — መግፋት.** Your hands send the thing away from you. Children pushing a loaded cart together, pushing a table to its place — that is pushing.

[GIF 8160 — push, then pull the cart]

**Pull — መጎተት.** The thing comes towards you. Pulling a cart behind you is pulling.

**Lift — ማንሳት.** The thing comes up off the ground. Lifting a box is lifting.

Push. Pull. Lift. Three ways to move the things around us.

## Sub lesson 2 — Run, jump, move

Our own bodies move in many ways too:

- **Run — መሮጥ**
- **Jump — መዝለል**
- **Throw — መወርወር**
- **Carry — መሸከም**
- **Push — መግፋት** and **pull — መጎተት**, the same words as before, now with our whole body in the game.

And exercise has its own order. Watch, then do it with me:

[GIF 8161 — the four arm positions in order]

1. Arms up — እጅ ወደላይ.
2. Arms at the waist.
3. Arms on the chest.
4. Arms down.

Playing together is moving together too: children cleaning up together, building blocks together, eating together. That togetherness is መተባበር እና አንድነት — cooperation and unity.

## Say it to your companion

Do a movement. Your companion names it: push, pull, lift, run, jump, throw, carry. Then swap. End with the four arm positions, in order, together.

## Do It Yourself

1. Push one thing, pull one thing, lift one thing at home. Say the word each time.
2. Run a short way and jump over a line on the ground.
3. Do the four arm positions in order, without stopping: up, waist, chest, down.
4. Help someone move or clean something — together.
