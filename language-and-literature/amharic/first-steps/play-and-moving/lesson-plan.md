# Lesson plan — Play and Moving

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Play and Moving
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** your body moves in many ways — you can push, pull and lift things, and you can run, jump and exercise — and playing and working together is how we move with other people.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Push, pull, lift** — pushing a cart and a table, pulling a cart, lifting a box — the three ways we move things.
2. **Run, jump, move** — running, jumping, throwing, carrying and the exercise arm positions — the ways our own bodies move.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This lesson stands on the child's own body.
- Next door: the safety lessons — moving is fun, and some things must never be touched.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Push a cart, pull a cart and lift a light box, and name each one: መግፋት (push), መጎተት (pull), ማንሳት (lift).
2. Run, jump, throw and carry, and name each movement.
3. Do the four exercise arm positions in order: arms up, arms at the waist, arms on the chest, arms down.
4. Play and work together with other children — cleaning up together, building together.

## What you need
A small cart or box, a light box to lift, and open space to run and jump. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes push and pull → stand behind the cart: if your hands push it away from you, it is a push; if it comes towards you, it is a pull.
- Cannot stand on one leg yet → that is fine and normal: hold the wall, let go for one second, hold again — the support way in hands-on.md (## If it is hard). No child is failed for needing the wall.
- This lesson's next door: Safety — some things we never touch.
