# Professions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who helps the sick?
a) the tailor  b) the doctor  c) the pilot
**Answer: b) the doctor** — ሐኪም.

**2.** Who sews clothes?
a) the tailor  b) the weaver  c) the farmer
**Answer: a) the tailor** — ልብስ ሰፊ — the weaver makes the cloth; the tailor sews it.

**3.** መምህር (memhir) is the —
a) soldier  b) teacher  c) trader
**Answer: b) teacher**

**4.** Who flies the aeroplane?
a) the pilot  b) the farmer  c) the doctor
**Answer: a) the pilot** — አውሮፕላን አብራሪ.

**5.** Who grows our food?
a) the soldier  b) the weaver  c) the farmer
**Answer: c) the farmer** — አርሶ አደር.
