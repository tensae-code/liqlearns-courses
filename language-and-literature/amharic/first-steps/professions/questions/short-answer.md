# Professions — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line. For a young child, answering out loud is a full answer.

**1.** Point to the weaver on the board. What is the weaver doing?
**Answer:** weaving cloth at the loom — ሸማኔ.

**2.** Say the work of one person in your family.
**Answer:** any true answer, in the shape: "My father's / mother's work is ___."
