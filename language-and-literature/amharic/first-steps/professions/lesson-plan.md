# Lesson plan — Professions

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Professions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the people who work around us — doctor, tailor, teacher, soldier, pilot, farmer, weaver, trader — and what each one's work is.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. These are the people of the child's own street.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the workers: ሐኪም (doctor), ልብስ ሰፊ (tailor), መምህር (teacher), ወታደር (soldier), አውሮፕላን አብራሪ (pilot), አርሶ አደር (farmer), ሸማኔ (weaver), ነጋዴ (trader).
2. Say what each worker does and where they work.
3. Say what work the people in your own family do.

## What you need
The board picture in this lesson. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner looks at the board and points to the worker they know best — a teacher, a farmer — before the names are taught (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the seven named workers on the board, then the trader, then the workers of the child's own family (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes tailor and weaver → the tailor (ልብስ ሰፊ) sews clothes together; the weaver (ሸማኔ) makes the cloth itself at the loom.
- Knows a face but not the work → ask what the person holds: the doctor holds a healer's bag, the pilot wears the flying cap, the farmer carries the hoe.
