# Professions

## Do it first

Look at the board. Point to a worker you know. Point to the one who teaches. Point to the one who grows food. Now we learn all their names.

## The workers around us

[Picture 8171 — the professions board]

People around us (በአካባቢያችን የሚገኙ የተለያዩ ባለሙያዎች) do many kinds of work:

- **ሐኪም (hakim) — the doctor**, who helps the sick
- **ልብስ ሰፊ (libs sefi) — the tailor**, who sews clothes
- **መምህር (memhir) — the teacher**, who teaches children
- **ወታደር (wetader) — the soldier**
- **አውሮፕላን አብራሪ (awroplan abrari) — the pilot**, who flies the aeroplane
- **አርሶ አደር (arso ader) — the farmer**, who grows our food
- **ሸማኔ (shemane) — the weaver**, who weaves cloth at the loom

Around them work many more: the carpenter who makes things of wood, and the **ነጋዴ (negade) — the trader** who buys and sells.

Every work helps everybody. The farmer grows, the weaver weaves, the tailor sews, the doctor heals, the teacher teaches.

## Say it to your companion

Point to a worker on the board. Your companion names the worker and says the work. Then swap. Finish with your own family: "My father's work is ___; my mother's work is ___." — fill in your family's real work.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name all seven workers on the board without help.
2. Match in your head: who sews? Who heals? Who flies? Who weaves?
3. Say the work of two people in your family.
4. Say one work you would like to do when you grow up.
