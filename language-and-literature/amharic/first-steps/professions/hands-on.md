# Hands-on — Professions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Who works here?
On a walk with a grown-up, point to the workers you pass — at the shop, the field, the school — and name their work.

## Dress the work
Act it out: one person mimes a work (sewing, teaching, flying, digging). The others guess the worker.

## My family's work
Ask at home what work each grown-up does. Say it in one line each: "My father's work is ___; my mother's work is ___."
