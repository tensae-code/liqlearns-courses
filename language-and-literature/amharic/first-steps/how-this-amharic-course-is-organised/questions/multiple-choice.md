# How This Amharic Course Is Organised — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many chapters does the Grade 7 book have?
a) five  b) ten  c) fifteen
**Answer: b) ten** — ten themed chapters, five in each semester.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 8's final chapter is —
a) ድራማ (Drama)  b) ቃላዊ ግጥሞች (Oral Poems)  c) ሰላም (Peace)
**Answer: a) ድራማ (Drama)** — chapter 10 is ድራማ — the play chapter.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 7's Chapter 8 theme is —
a) ሱስ (Addiction)  b) ድራማ (Drama)  c) ሰላም (Peace)
**Answer: a) ሱስ (Addiction)** — Chapter 8 of Grade 7 is ሱስ; drama is Grade 8's Chapter 10.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Grade 8's Chapter 5 is —
a) አጭር ልቦለድ (Short Fiction)  b) ሥነሕዝብ (Demography)  c) ባህላዊ ቅርሶች (Heritage)
**Answer: b) ሥነሕዝብ (Demography)** — ሥነሕዝብ is Chapter 5; short fiction is Chapter 4.
