# How This Amharic Course Is Organised

## Do it first

Try the first task in Sub lesson 1 before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Grade 7: ten chapters, ten themes

The Grade 7 book has ten chapters, each a theme, each planned for three weeks: 1 ተፈጥሮን ማድነቅ (Appreciating Nature); 2 የቤተሰብ ምጣኔ (Family Planning); 3 በጎፈቃድ (Volunteering); 4 የታዋቂ ሰዎች የሕይወት ታሪክ (Biographies of Famous People); 5 ዉኃና ጥቅሙ (Water and Its Uses); 6 ሕገወጥ የሰዎች ዝውውር (Illegal Human Trafficking); 7 ማኅበራዊ ግንኙነት (Social Relations); 8 ሱስ (Addiction); 9 የሀገር ፍቅር (Love of Country); 10 ቃላዊ ግጥሞች (Oral Poems). The first five chapters are semester 1, the last five semester 2. References (ዋቢዎች) close the book.

## Sub lesson 2 — Grade 8: ten chapters, from work to drama

The Grade 8 book follows the same design — ten chapters, three weeks each, five per semester: 1 የሥራ ሥነምግባር (Work Ethics); 2 ባህላዊ ቅርሶች (Cultural Heritage); 3 ሰብዓዊ እሴቶች (Human Values); 4 አጭር ልቦለድ (Short Fiction); 5 ሥነሕዝብ (Demography); 6 በረሃማነት (Desertification); 7 የሕፃናት ጉልበት ብዝበዛ (Child Labour Exploitation); 8 ሰላም (Peace); 9 የአየር ንብረት (Climate); 10 ድራማ (Drama). Inside every chapter the same strands return: reading, vocabulary, writing, speaking, listening, fluent reading and grammar — so a learner who knows one chapter's shape knows every chapter's shape.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the main thing each sub lesson taught, in your own words, in order. Your companion asks one question about each part, and you answer from the lesson. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the key Amharic words of this lesson in your notebook, each with its meaning or its job.
2. Do the last sub lesson's task once more on your own, without looking, then check against the lesson.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, in your own words.
