# Hands-on — Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The week in your life
Say what you do on each day — school days, ቅዳሜ (Saturday), እሁድ (Sunday). Draw one day: today. What day is tomorrow? What day was yesterday?

## The year in your family
Ask at home: in which month is your birthday, in Ethiopia's calendar? In which month does the New Year (እንቁጣጣሽ) come — which month is first?
