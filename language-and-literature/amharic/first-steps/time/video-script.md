# Video script — Time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The days! ሰኞ. ማክሰኞ. ረቡዕ.
ሀሙስ. አርብ. ቅዳሜ. እሁድ.
Seven days. One week.

Now the months — sing them!
መስከረም. ጥቅምት. ህዳር. ታህሳስ.
ጥር. የካቲት. መጋቢት. ሚያዝያ.
ግንቦት. ሰኔ. ሐምሌ. ነሐሴ.
And ጳጉሜን — thirteen!

Ethiopia's year has thirteen months.

## Added 2026-11-12 — the Grade 1–2 student-book parts
Sub lesson 3, The Grade 2 book's month drills: present the part in a simple voice, model the reading or listening once, let the learner answer the book's own questions, then prove it with the sub lesson's questions.
