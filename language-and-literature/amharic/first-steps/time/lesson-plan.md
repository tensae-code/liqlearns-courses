# Lesson plan — Time

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Time
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's time words — the seven days of the week (ሰኞ to እሁድ) and Ethiopia's thirteen months (መስከረም to ነሐሴ, and ጳጉሜን).
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **The days of the week** — the seven days in Ethiopia's order: ሰኞ, ማክሰኞ, ረቡዕ, ሀሙስ, አርብ, ቅዳሜ, እሁድ — to say, sing and copy-write.
2. **The months of Ethiopia** — all thirteen months in order — መስከረም, ጥቅምት, ህዳር, ታህሳስ, ጥር, የካቲት, መጋቢት, ሚያዝያ, ግንቦት, ሰኔ, ሐምሌ, ነሐሴ, ጳጉሜን — with before-and-after questions.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Counting — the months are numbered 1 to 13. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the seven days in order: ሰኞ (Monday), ማክሰኞ (Tuesday), ረቡዕ (Wednesday), ሀሙስ (Thursday), አርብ (Friday), ቅዳሜ (Saturday), እሁድ (Sunday).
2. Say all thirteen months in order, መስከረም first and ጳጉሜን last.
3. Write the months in order, 1 to 13.
4. Answer before-and-after questions about the months.

## What you need
The week strip and calendar strip in this lesson. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — the learner says any day or month names they already know — most children know some — before the full lists are taught (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the month order in the middle → go back to the strip and point: the finger remembers what the voice forgets. Learn the year in two halves: መስከረም to የካቲት, then መጋቢት to ጳጉሜን.
- Forgets ጳጉሜን → it is the little month at the very end, number 13 — Ethiopia's own extra month. Other calendars stop at 12; ours has 13.
