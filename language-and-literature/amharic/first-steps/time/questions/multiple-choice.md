# Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which day comes after ረቡዕ (Wednesday)?
a) ሰኞ  b) ሀሙስ  c) ቅዳሜ
**Answer: b) ሀሙስ** — Thursday follows Wednesday.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many days are in the week?
a) five  b) seven  c) thirteen
**Answer: b) seven** — thirteen is the months!

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The first day in this lesson's order is —
a) እሁድ  b) ቅዳሜ  c) ሰኞ
**Answer: c) ሰኞ** — Monday, as printed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How many months does Ethiopia's year have?
a) twelve  b) thirteen  c) seven
**Answer: b) thirteen** — with ጳጉሜን last.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The first month of the year is —
a) ጥር  b) መስከረም  c) ነሐሴ
**Answer: b) መስከረም** — month 1.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which month comes before ታህሳስ?
a) ህዳር  b) ጥር  c) ሰኔ
**Answer: a) ህዳር** — month 3 comes before month 4.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The last, thirteenth month is —
a) ነሐሴ  b) ጳጉሜን  c) ሐምሌ
**Answer: b) ጳጉሜን**
