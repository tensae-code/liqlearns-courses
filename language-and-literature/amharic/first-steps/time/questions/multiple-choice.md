# Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which day comes after ረቡዕ (Wednesday)?
a) ሰኞ  b) ሀሙስ  c) ቅዳሜ
**Answer: b) ሀሙስ** — Thursday follows Wednesday.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many days are in the week?
a) five  b) seven  c) thirteen
**Answer: b) seven** — thirteen is the months!

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The first day in this lesson's order is —
a) እሁድ  b) ቅዳሜ  c) ሰኞ
**Answer: c) ሰኞ** — Monday, as printed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How many months does Ethiopia's year have?
a) twelve  b) thirteen  c) seven
**Answer: b) thirteen** — with ጳጉሜን last.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The first month of the year is —
a) ጥር  b) መስከረም  c) ነሐሴ
**Answer: b) መስከረም** — month 1.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which month comes before ታህሳስ?
a) ህዳር  b) ጥር  c) ሰኔ
**Answer: a) ህዳር** — month 3 comes before month 4.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The last, thirteenth month is —
a) ነሐሴ  b) ጳጉሜን  c) ሐምሌ
**Answer: b) ጳጉሜን**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Which month begins the thirteen, in the book's review writing?
a) መስከረም  b) ሰኔ  c) ጳጉሜን
**Answer: a) መስከረም** — Grade 2 pp.28–29.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** ሐምሌ in the drills is sounded…
a) Fidel by fidel, then whole  b) Backwards  c) By singing only
**Answer: a) Fidel by fidel, then whole** — Grade 2 pp.16–19.

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** How many Ethiopian months does the review ask you to write?
a) Thirteen  b) Twelve  c) Seven
**Answer: a) Thirteen** — the thirteen months in order.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** What do you see in the sky in the day?
a) the moon and stars  b) the sun and clouds  c) only darkness
**Answer: b) the sun and clouds** — draw and colour them in the day sky.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** What do you draw in the night sky?
a) the moon and the stars  b) the sun  c) rain only
**Answer: a) the moon and the stars** — the night sky holds the moon and stars.

**13. (Sub lesson 4)** The መንኮራኩር (menk'orakur) you colour has how many wheels?
a) two  b) three  c) one
**Answer: c) one** — a unicycle has only one big wheel.
