# Time — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ቅዳሜ (Saturday) comes after አርብ (Friday).
**Answer: true**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's year has twelve months, like every other year.
**Answer: false** — thirteen — ጳጉሜን is the thirteenth.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** መስከረም is the first month.
**Answer: true**
