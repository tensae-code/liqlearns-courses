# Time — writing

Write or copy-write. For a young child, tracing and copying count in full.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copy-write the seven days in order: ሰኞ — ማክሰኞ — ረቡዕ — ሀሙስ — አርብ — ቅዳሜ — እሁድ.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the thirteen months in order, numbered 1 to 13.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Copy-write: መስከረም — ጳጉሜን.
