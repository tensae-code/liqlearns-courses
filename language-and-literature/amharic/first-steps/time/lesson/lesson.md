# Time

## Do it first

Say every day name you know. Say every month name you know. Grown-ups use these words every day — today we learn them all, in order.

## Sub lesson 1 — The days of the week

[Picture 8177 — the week strip]

The week has seven days (የሳምንቱ ቀናት). Say them in order:

1. **ሰኞ** (señ) — Monday
2. **ማክሰኞ** (makseñ) — Tuesday
3. **ረቡዕ** (rebu') — Wednesday
4. **ሀሙስ** (hamus) — Thursday
5. **አርብ** (arb) — Friday
6. **ቅዳሜ** (q'idame) — Saturday
7. **እሁድ** (ehud) — Sunday

Say them again. Copy-write them. The week goes round and round: after እሁድ comes ሰኞ again.

## Sub lesson 2 — The months of Ethiopia

[Picture 8176 — the calendar strip]

Ethiopia's year has **thirteen** months (የዓመቱ ወራቶች). Read them, then copy-write them in order, 1 to 13:

1. **መስከረም** (Meskerem)
2. **ጥቅምት**
3. **ህዳር**
4. **ታህሳስ**
5. **ጥር**
6. **የካቲት**
7. **መጋቢት**
8. **ሚያዝያ**
9. **ግንቦት**
10. **ሰኔ**
11. **ሐምሌ**
12. **ነሐሴ**
13. **ጳጉሜን**

And the before-and-after game: which month comes **before** ጥቅምት? መስከረም. Which comes **after** የካቲት? መጋቢት. Which comes before ነሐሴ? ሐምሌ.

## Say it to your companion

Say a day; your companion says the next day. Go all the way round the week. Then months: say one; your companion says the next — all the way to ጳጉሜን.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the seven days in order.
2. Write the thirteen months in order, numbered 1 to 13.
3. Which month comes before ታህሳስ? After የካቲት? Before ግንቦት?
4. Say today's day name, and the month we are in now.
