# Counting — fill in the blank

Fill the missing number. Say the whole line out loud when you finish.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** 0, _, 2, 3, 4, 5
**Answer: 1** — the line climbs one by one from zero.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** 0, _, 2, _, 4, _, _, 7
**Answer: 1, 3, 5, 6** — read the line whole to check: 0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7. (The start of the book's own broken line, Level Two p.30.)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The number that comes just after 19 is _.
**Answer: 20** — the line runs 16, 17, 18, 19, 20.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** ፩, _, ፫, _, ፭, _, ፯, _, ፱, _
**Answer: ፪, ፬, ፮, ፰, ፲** — the Ge'ez line climbs the same way: 1 to 10.
