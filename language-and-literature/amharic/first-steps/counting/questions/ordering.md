# Counting — ordering

Put the numbers in order, smallest first. Say the line out loud.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** 4, 0, 2, 5, 1, 3
**Answer: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5** — zero first, then one by one.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** 14, 20, 11, 17, 9, 12
**Answer: 9, 11, 12, 14, 17, 20.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** ፭, ፩, ፲, ፫, ፮
**Answer: ፩, ፫, ፭, ፮, ፲** — that is 1, 3, 5, 6, 10.
