# Counting — matching

Match each numeral to its number name. Say each pair out loud.

1. (Sub lesson 1) 3 — a) ዘጠኝ
2. (Sub lesson 1) 7 — b) ሶስት
3. (Sub lesson 1) 9 — c) ሰባት
4. (Sub lesson 3) 2 — d) አስር
5. (Sub lesson 3) 10 — e) ሁለት
6. (Sub lesson 4) ፬ — f) 6
7. (Sub lesson 4) ፮ — g) 4

**Answer key:** 1–b (3 = ሶስት), 2–c (7 = ሰባት), 3–a (9 = ዘጠኝ),
4–e (2 = ሁለት), 5–d (10 = አስር), 6–g (፬ = 4), 7–f (፮ = 6).
