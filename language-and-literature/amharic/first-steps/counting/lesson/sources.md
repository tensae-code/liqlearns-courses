**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

GAP, recorded plainly: oral counting to 100 is NOT in the three workbooks — they write numbers 0–20 only. The SNE toolkit's KG3-exit list (printed pp.30–31, SUPPORTING) expects a child leaving pre-primary to orally memorise numbers to 100 and orally count, so the system's expectation runs past its own workbooks. Following the brief for this build, the lesson does NOT invent the 100-count: it teaches the workbooks' written 0–20 and names the gap here. Whether an audio-only counting-to-100 track belongs in this lesson is the merger's call, flagged in the supports placements.

The number-card pictures (8110–8118) show star counters, not the books' own sets (banana, cups, strawberries, pencils, spoons, lions, apples, cars, lemons): a drawn lion or strawberry at this size would mislead more than it teaches, so the cards say the sets are counters, and lesson.md names the books' own set for every number. No book errors were flagged in the placements for these pages, and no reading on them is marked uncertain.
