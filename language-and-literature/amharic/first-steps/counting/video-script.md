# Video script — Counting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Touch it. Count it.

**አንድ** — one. One stone.
**ሁለት** — two. Two stones.
**ሶስት** — three. **አራት** — four. **አምስት** — five.

Keep going. **ስድስት** — six. **ሰባት** — seven.
**ስምንት** — eight. **ዘጠኝ** — nine. **አስር** — ten!

Now look. The jar is empty. Nothing inside.
**ዜሮ** — zero. Zero is the number for nothing there.

And Ethiopia's own numbers:
**፩** is one. **፪** is two. **፲** is ten.

Now you. Count your fingers. Count the cups.
What is the last number you say? That is how many.
