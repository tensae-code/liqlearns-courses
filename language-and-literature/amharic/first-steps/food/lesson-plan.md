# Lesson plan — Food

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Food
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the foods the child eats — the fruits, the vegetables, and the food that keeps us healthy, including the foods we get from animals.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own world — the food on the child's own table every day. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Fruits** — the ten named fruits, telling fruits from everything else, and writing their names.
2. **Vegetables** — the garden vegetables, and telling vegetables from fruits.
3. **Food that keeps us healthy** — the balanced diet, the healthy foods and drinks, and the foods we get from animals.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This is a First Steps lesson — a child can start here. The plants lesson next door helps, because fruits and vegetables are the plant's gifts. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the ten fruits: ፖም, አቮካዶ, ማንጎ, ሀባብ, ብርቱካን, አናናስ, ፓፓያ, ሎሚ, ሙዝ, እንጆሪ — and pick fruits out of a mixed picture.
2. Name the garden vegetables: ጥቅልጎመን, ቲማቲም, ካሮት, ቀይ ስር, ቀይ ሽንኩርት, ድንች, ቃሪያ, ጎመን — and pick vegetables out of a mixed picture.
3. Say what makes a meal balanced, and name healthy foods and drinks.
4. List the foods we get from animals: meat, egg, chicken meat, milk.

## What you need
The pictures in the lesson. Real fruits and vegetables from the home kitchen for the hands-on work, if there are any.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls a tomato a fruit → in this lesson the books sort by the kitchen, not the seed: ቲማቲም (tomato) sits with the garden vegetables, with the cabbage and the carrot.
- Cannot tell fruit from vegetable in the mixed picture → ask: "Sweet, and we eat it after the meal, or it grows sweet on the tree?" — fruit. "We cook it with the meal?" — vegetable. (The tomato is the one hard case; the books put it with the vegetables.)
- Thinks healthy food is one thing only → a full meal holds many things together: grains, vegetables, egg or meat or fish, milk, fruit. One thing alone is not a balanced diet.
