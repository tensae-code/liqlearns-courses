# Video script — Food

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The target
words are in Amharic, with the transliteration the books give.

---

Yum! Food!

Fruits! ፖም (pom) — apple. ማንጎ (mango). ሙዝ (muz) — banana.
ብርቱካን (birtukan) — orange. ሀባብ (habab) — watermelon. Sweet fruits!

Vegetables! ቲማቲም (tomato). ካሮት (carrot). ድንች (potato).
ጥቅልጎመን (cabbage). We cook vegetables with our meal.

A full meal has many things.
Grains. Vegetables. An egg — እንቁላል. Milk — ወተት. Fruit.
Many good foods together — that keeps us healthy!

From animals, too: meat — ስጋ. Fish — ዓሣ. Eggs and milk.

Now you. Name a fruit. Name a vegetable. What is on your plate today?
