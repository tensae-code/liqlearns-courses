# Food

## Do it first

Think of the last meal you ate. What was on the plate? Name every food you can remember. Some of those foods grow on plants. Some come from animals. This lesson names them all.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fruits

Fruits are sweet, and many of them grow on trees and plants. Here are the ten fruits, one by one. Say each name after you hear it.

[Picture 8063 — the fruits]

- ፖም (pom) — apple
- አቮካዶ — avocado
- ማንጎ — mango
- ሀባብ (habab) — watermelon, big and green outside, red inside
- ብርቱካን (birtukan) — orange
- አናናስ (ananas) — pineapple
- ፓፓያ — papaya
- ሎሚ (lomi) — lemon, small and sour
- ሙዝ (muz) — banana
- እንጆሪ (injori) — strawberry

Grapes — ወይን (weyn) — belong here too; the reading list of fruits names them with the apple, the orange, the banana, the watermelon and the mango.

Now the sorting game of the book: in a mixed picture of fruits and vegetables, circle only the fruits. Sweet, from the tree or the plant, eaten as they are — that is a fruit.

One fruit gets its own drawing work: the ማንጎ, the mango. Look at
the mango picture well — its round, heavy shape, its smooth
skin. Now draw the mango picture again, yourself, and colour
your drawing.

[Picture 21009 — a mango, coloured, and the same mango in outline to draw again and colour]

## Sub lesson 2 — Vegetables

Vegetables grow in the garden — the garden vegetables, የጓሮ አትክልቶች. We cook them and eat them with our meal.

[Picture 8069 — the vegetables]

- ጥቅልጎመን — cabbage, round and green, leaf on leaf
- ቲማቲም — tomato, round and red
- ካሮት — carrot, long and orange, growing under the ground
- ቀይ ስር — red beetroot
- ቀይ ሽንኩርት — red onion
- ድንች — potato, growing under the ground
- ቃሪያ — pepper
- ጎመን (gomen) — greens, the leafy ones we cook

The book's game again, the other way: in a mixed picture, circle only the vegetables.

## Sub lesson 3 — Food that keeps us healthy

One food alone does not keep us healthy. A full meal contains all the food contents together — eating a balanced diet is good for health.

Look at the full meal:

[Picture 8070 — a balanced meal]

On the plate: grains, vegetables, lentils, and an egg እንቁላል. Beside it: milk ወተት to drink, and fruit. Grains and legumes — ጥራጥሬዎች — belong in the meal too.

The foods good for health, from the book's own lists: fruits — banana, strawberry, watermelon, mango; and garden vegetables — cabbage, carrot, tomato, beetroot.

Healthy foods and drinks:

- ወተት — milk
- እንቁላል — egg
- ዓሣ — fish
- ስጋ — meat
- Vegetables and fruits
- ጥራጥሬዎች — grains and legumes

And some foods come from animals. The foods we get from animals: meat, egg, chicken meat, and milk. The cow gives the milk; the hen gives the egg and the chicken meat.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: two fruits, two vegetables, and what a full meal must hold. Then your companion names a food, and you say: fruit, vegetable, or from an animal?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the fruits' names under their pictures, like the book: ማንጎ, ፓፓያ, ብርቱካን, ወይን.
2. Copy-write the vegetables' names: ጥቅልጎመን, ቲማቲም, ቀይ ስር, ቀይ ሽንኩርት, ድንች, ቃሪያ.
3. List the foods from animals, 1 to 5: meat, egg, chicken meat, milk — and one more you know.
4. At your next meal, look at the plate. Is it a full meal? What is missing?
