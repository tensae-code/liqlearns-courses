# Food — matching

Match each food to its kind: fruit, vegetable, or from an animal.

**Set 1 (Sub lessons 1, 2 and 3)**
1. ሙዝ (muz, banana) — fruit
2. ቲማቲም (tomato) — vegetable
3. ወተት (milk) — from an animal
4. እንጆሪ (injori, strawberry) — fruit
5. ድንች (potato) — vegetable
6. እንቁላል (egg) — from an animal

**Answer key:** 1 fruit · 2 vegetable · 3 from an animal · 4 fruit · 5 vegetable · 6 from an animal.
