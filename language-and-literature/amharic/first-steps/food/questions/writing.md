# Food — writing

Copy-write each name slowly, the book's own writing work.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copy the fruits' names: ማንጎ (mango), ፓፓያ (papaya), ብርቱካን (orange), ወይን (grapes).
**Done when:** each word is copied whole, letter by letter.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Copy the vegetables' names: ጥቅልጎመን (cabbage), ቲማቲም (tomato), ድንች (potato), ቃሪያ (pepper).
**Done when:** each word is copied whole, letter by letter.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the foods from animals in a list, 1 to 4: meat, egg, chicken meat, milk — in Amharic where you can: ስጋ, እንቁላል, ወተት.
**Done when:** the list holds at least three foods that come from animals.
