# Food — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ሎሚ (lomi, the lemon) is a fruit.
**True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The carrot ካሮት grows under the ground.
**True** — like the potato ድንች.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Eating only one food, and nothing else, is a balanced diet.
**False** — a balanced diet is a full meal: all the food contents together.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Milk ወተት comes from the cow, and the egg እንቁላል comes from the hen.
**True** — they are foods we get from animals.
