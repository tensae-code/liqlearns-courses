# Food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a fruit?
a) ድንች (potato)  b) ማንጎ (mango)  c) ቲማቲም (tomato)
**Answer: b) ማንጎ (mango)** — the mango is a fruit; the potato and the tomato sit with the garden vegetables in the books.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ሀባብ (habab) is —
a) the lemon  b) the banana  c) the watermelon
**Answer: c) the watermelon** — big and green outside, red inside. The lemon is ሎሚ (lomi); the banana is ሙዝ (muz).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a garden vegetable?
a) ካሮት (carrot)  b) ፖም (pom, apple)  c) አናናስ (ananas, pineapple)
**Answer: a) ካሮት (carrot)** — the carrot grows in the garden, under the ground.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Point to the ጥቅልጎመን in the vegetable picture. What is it?
a) the pepper ቃሪያ  b) the cabbage  c) the potato ድንች
**Answer: b) the cabbage** — round and green, leaf on leaf.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A balanced diet is —
a) eating only one food, a lot of it  b) a full meal with all the food contents together  c) eating nothing at all
**Answer: b)** — a full meal holds grains, vegetables, egg or meat or fish, milk and fruit together.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which food do we get from an animal?
a) እንቁላል (egg)  b) ማንጎ (mango)  c) ድንች (potato)
**Answer: a) እንቁላል (egg)** — the hen gives the egg; the mango and the potato grow on plants.

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 1 review at p.72 asks you to write how many kinds of fruit?
a) Five  b) Two  c) Ten
**Answer: a) Five** — five kinds of fruit, after the two planting stories.
