# Hands-on — Animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The animals of my street
Walk slowly around your home with a grown-up. Every animal you see, stop, point, and say its name in Amharic: ድመት, ውሻ, ዶሮ… How many animals did you find? Which ones live with people?

## Draw the animal you like
On paper, draw the animal you like best — the book's own work. Colour it. Write its name under it, or say its name out loud and let a grown-up write it.

## Who sleeps where?
With small stones or bottle tops as animals, and boxes or cups as homes: put the hen in the hen house ጉሮኖ, the cow and the ox in the pen ቆጥ and the barn በረት. Say each home's name as the animal goes in.

## Mother and young
Match pairs with real things or drawings: cow → calf ጥጃ, dog → puppy ቡችላ. Say: "the cow's young is the calf."
