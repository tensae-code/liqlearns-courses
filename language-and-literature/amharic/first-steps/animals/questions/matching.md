# Animals — matching

Match each animal to the right answer.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1 and Sub lesson 2)** — home or wild?
1. ድመት (dimet, cat) — home
2. አንበሳ (anbesa, lion) — wild
3. ፍየል (fiyel, goat) — home
4. ዝሆን (zihon, elephant) — wild
5. ግመል (gimel, camel) — home
6. ነብር (nebr, tiger) — wild

**Answer key:** 1 home · 2 wild · 3 home · 4 wild · 5 home · 6 wild.

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 4)** — match the animal to its home.
1. ዶሮ (doro, hen) — c
2. በሬ (bere, ox) — a
3. ላም (cow) — a

a) ቆጥ (qot', cattle pen) and በረት (beret, barn) — the cattle's homes
b) the river
c) ጉሮኖ (gurono, chicken house)

**Answer key:** 1–c · 2–a · 3–a.

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 4)** — match the mother to her young.
1. cow — ጥጃ (ṭija, calf)
2. dog — ቡችላ (buchila, puppy)

**Answer key:** cow–calf · dog–puppy.
