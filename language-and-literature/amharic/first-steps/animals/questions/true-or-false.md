# Animals — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The camel ግመል lives at home and carries loads for people.
**True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The lion አንበሳ sleeps in the house with the children.
**False** — the lion is a wild animal; it lives far from people.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The walia ዋሊያ has two big curved horns and a beard ፂም.
**True** — and strong legs እግር for climbing the mountain rocks.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The walia and the cat are the same size.
**False** — the walia is big; the cat is small.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The hen ዶሮ hatches chicks from her eggs.
**True.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The hippopotamus ጉማሬ lives only on the dry land, never in water.
**False** — the hippopotamus, the crocodile አዞ and the frog እንቁራሪት live in the water and on the land.
