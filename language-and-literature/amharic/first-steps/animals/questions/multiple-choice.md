# Animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which animal lives at home and gives us eggs?
a) the hen ዶሮ  b) the lion አንበሳ  c) the zebra
**Answer: a) the hen ዶሮ** — the hen is a home and farm animal; the lion and the zebra are wild.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Point to the ግመል (gimel) in the home-animals picture. Which animal is it?
a) the goat  b) the camel  c) the sheep
**Answer: b) the camel** — ግመል is the camel, the animal that carries loads in dry places.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a wild animal?
a) the dog ውሻ  b) the elephant ዝሆን  c) the cat ድመት
**Answer: b) the elephant ዝሆን** — the elephant lives far from people; the dog and the cat live at home.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The animal with black and white stripes all over its body is —
a) the giraffe  b) the monkey  c) the zebra
**Answer: c) the zebra.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Where does the walia ዋሊያ live?
a) in the house with people  b) high in the mountains, on the rocks  c) in the river
**Answer: b) high in the mountains, on the rocks** — the walia is Ethiopia's own mountain animal.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which body part does the walia have that a cat does not have?
a) legs እግር  b) a tail ጭራ  c) horns ቀንድ
**Answer: c) horns ቀንድ** — the walia has two big curved horns, and a beard ፂም.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The hen ዶሮ sleeps in the —
a) ጉሮኖ (gurono, chicken house)  b) ቆጥ (qot', cattle pen)  c) በረት (beret, barn)
**Answer: a) ጉሮኖ** — the chicken house is the hen's home.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** ጥጃ (ṭija) is the young of the —
a) dog  b) cow  c) hen
**Answer: b) cow** — the calf is the cow's young; the dog's young is the puppy ቡችላ.
