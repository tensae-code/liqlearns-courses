# Video script — Animals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The target
words are in Amharic, with the transliteration the books give.

---

Look! Animals!

This is a cat. ድመት (dimet).
This is a dog. ውሻ (wusha).
A horse — ፈረስ (feres). A donkey — አህያ (ahya).
A goat — ፍየል (fiyel). A sheep — በግ (beg).
An ox — በሬ (bere). A hen — ዶሮ (doro). A camel — ግመል (gimel).
These animals live with us, at home.

Now — far away animals!
A lion — አንበሳ (anbesa). An elephant — ዝሆን (zihon).
A tiger — ነብር (nebr). A giraffe. A zebra. A monkey.
These animals live far from people. Wild animals.

And this one — look! Big horns! A beard!
This is the walia. ዋሊያ.
The walia lives high in Ethiopia's mountains.

The ox works on the farm.
The cow gives milk.
The hen gives eggs.

Now you. Point. Name the animal. Home, or wild?
