# Transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which one travels on water?
a) the bus  b) the ship  c) the bicycle
**Answer: b) the ship** — መርከብ.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** አውሮፕላን is the —
a) aeroplane  b) cart  c) bus
**Answer: a) aeroplane** — it travels by air.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which one is pulled by an animal?
a) the aeroplane  b) the bus  c) the cart
**Answer: c) the cart** — ጋሪ.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cart is —
a) modern  b) traditional  c) from the sky
**Answer: b) traditional** — the way of long ago.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The aeroplane is —
a) traditional  b) modern  c) pulled by horses
**Answer: b) modern**
