# Transport — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ship travels on the road.
**Answer: false** — a ship travels on water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** People travelled long ago, before aeroplanes.
**Answer: true** — by cart and on foot.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A bus is traditional transport from long ago.
**Answer: false** — the bus is modern.
