# Hands-on — Transport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Travel I have done
Tell or draw one real journey you made: where you went, and what you travelled by. Underline the transport's name — aeroplane, car, bicycle, train, bus, cart.

## Sort the street
With a grown-up, watch the road and name everything that passes. Sort your list: traditional or modern?
