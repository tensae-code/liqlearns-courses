# Transport

## Do it first

Look at the board. Name everything on it that can carry people. Which one flies? Which one floats? Which ones roll on the road? Now we learn their Amharic names.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ways we travel

[Picture 8173 — ways we travel]

The kinds of transport (የመጓጓዣ ዓይነት):

- **አውሮፕላን** — the aeroplane, travelling by air
- **መርከብ** — the ship, travelling on water
- **አውቶብስ** — the bus, travelling on the road
- **ሳይክል** — the bicycle, travelling on the road
- **ጋሪ** — the cart, pulled along the road

And one question that is yours alone: **which transport have you travelled by?** Aeroplane, car, bicycle, train — underline the one you used, and tell its story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Long ago and now

People have always travelled, but the ways change. Sort every picture: is it **traditional** (ባህላዊ) — the way of long ago — or **modern** (ዘመናዊ) — the way of now?

The cart is traditional: animals pull it, as they did long ago. The aeroplane and the bus are modern. The bicycle stands between: people's own legs made it go then, and still do now.

## Say it to your companion

Point to one way on the board. Your companion names it, says where it travels — air, water, or road — and says traditional or modern. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name all five ways from the board without help.
2. Say which one you have travelled by, and where you went.
3. Sort these: cart, aeroplane, bus — traditional or modern?
