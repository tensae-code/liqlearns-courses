# Lesson plan — Transport

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Transport
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the ways we travel — አውሮፕላን aeroplane, መርከብ ship, አውቶብስ bus, ሳይክል bicycle, ጋሪ cart — and how travel was long ago and how it is now.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Ways we travel** — the five named ways: by air, by water, by road — and which one you have travelled by.
2. **Long ago and now** — sorting transport into traditional and modern — the cart of long ago, the aeroplane and bus of now.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. The child has already travelled — on foot, by bus, by cart.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five: አውሮፕላን (aeroplane), መርከብ (ship), አውቶብስ (bus), ሳይክል (bicycle), ጋሪ (cart).
2. Say which travel in the air, on water, and on the road.
3. Say which transport you yourself have travelled by.
4. Sort transport pictures: traditional or modern.

## What you need
The board picture in this lesson. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes ship and bus → ask where it travels: the ship (መርከብ) goes on water; the bus (አውቶብስ) goes on the road.
- Mixes traditional and modern → ask: did grandmother travel this way when she was small? The cart — yes, long ago and still today in the countryside. The aeroplane — only now.
