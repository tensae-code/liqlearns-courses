# Lesson plan — Basic Needs

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Basic Needs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the three things every person needs: food ምግብ, clothing አልባሳት, and shelter መጠለያ.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This lesson starts from the child's own day: eating, dressing, home.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three basic needs: ምግብ (food), አልባሳት (clothing), መጠለያ (shelter).
2. List them in order: 1, 2, 3.
3. Point to each need in the picture and at home.

## What you need
The three-part picture in this lesson, and the child's own home to look around.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner thinks of their own morning — eating, dressing, the house around them — before the three needs are named (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the three needs, one by one, each with its Amharic name, then listing them 1, 2, 3 (lesson/lesson.md).

3. **Say it to your companion** — name a need; your companion points to it in the picture and at home — then swap (lesson/lesson.md).
