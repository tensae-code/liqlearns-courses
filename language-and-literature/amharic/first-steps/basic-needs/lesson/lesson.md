# Basic Needs

## Do it first

Think of this morning. You woke up inside a house. You put on clothes. You ate food. Three things you needed before the day even started. This lesson names them.

## The three things people need

Every person needs three basic things (መሰረታዊ ፍላጎቶች):

[Picture 8172 — the three basic needs]

1. **ምግብ — food.** We must eat.
2. **አልባሳት — clothing.** We must dress.
3. **መጠለያ — shelter.** We must have a house, a place to be safe and dry.

Food, clothing, shelter. One, two, three. Nobody can live without these three.

## Say it to your companion

Name one need. Your companion points to it in the picture, then points to it in the room — real food, real clothing, the real house. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. List the three needs in order: 1, 2, 3 — without looking.
2. Draw the three: a plate, clothes, a house.
3. At home, show a grown-up one of each and say its name.
