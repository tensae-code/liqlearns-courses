# Basic Needs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a basic need?
a) a toy car  b) food  c) a balloon
**Answer: b) food** — ምግብ — everyone must eat.

**2.** How many basic needs are there?
a) two  b) three  c) ten
**Answer: b) three** — food, clothing, shelter.

**3.** መጠለያ means —
a) clothing  b) food  c) shelter
**Answer: c) shelter** — the house that keeps us safe and dry.
