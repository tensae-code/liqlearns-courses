# Hands-on — Basic Needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the three at home
Walk through your home with a grown-up. Touch one food, one clothing, one part of your shelter. Say the name of the need each time.

## List them
Say the three needs as a list — 1, 2, 3 — to someone at home. Then draw the three: a plate, a shirt, a house.
