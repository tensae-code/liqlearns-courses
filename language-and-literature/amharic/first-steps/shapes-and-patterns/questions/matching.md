# Shapes and Patterns — matching

Match each shape name to the thing that wears it. Say each pair out loud.

1. (Sub lesson 1) ክብ — circle — a) a slice of watermelon
2. (Sub lesson 1) ካሬ — square — b) an egg
3. (Sub lesson 1) ጎነ ሶስት — triangle — c) round bread
4. (Sub lesson 1) አራት ማዕዘን — rectangle — d) a window
5. (Sub lesson 1) ሞላላ — oval — e) a TV

**Answer key:** 1–c (circle ↔ round bread), 2–d (square ↔
window), 3–a (triangle ↔ watermelon slice), 4–e (rectangle ↔
TV), 5–b (oval ↔ egg). These are the books' own matches
(Level One, printed p.46).
