# Shapes and Patterns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An egg has the shape called —
a) ክብ — circle  b) ሞላላ — oval  c) ካሬ — square
**Answer: b) ሞላላ — oval** — a circle stretched out.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which shape has three sides and three corners?
a) ጎነ ሶስት — triangle  b) አራት ማዕዘን — rectangle  c) ክብ — circle
**Answer: a) ጎነ ሶስት — triangle.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A TV has the shape of —
a) a square  b) a circle  c) a rectangle — አራት ማዕዘን
**Answer: c) a rectangle** — two long sides, two short sides.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Circle, square, circle, square, circle — what comes next?
a) circle  b) square  c) triangle
**Answer: b) square** — the row takes turns.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Star, star, circle, star, star — what comes next?
a) star  b) circle  c) square
**Answer: b) circle** — the rule is two stars, then one circle.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Which shape is a window in the books' matching?
a) ካሬ — square  b) ሞላላ — oval  c) ጎነ ሶስት — triangle
**Answer: a) ካሬ — square** — four straight sides, all the same.
