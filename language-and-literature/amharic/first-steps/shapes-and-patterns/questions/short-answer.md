# Shapes and Patterns — short answer

Answer out loud. Point at the shape or the thing as you answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between a square (ካሬ) and a rectangle (አራት ማዕዘን)?
**Answer: A square's four sides are all the same; a rectangle has two long sides and two short sides.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A row of fish is coloured: red, blue, red, blue, red — and the last fish is not coloured. What colour should it be, and why?
**Answer: Blue** — the colours take turns, and blue follows red in the row. (The books' fish pattern rows, Level One p.160.)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Say this row aloud and finish it: triangle, circle, triangle, circle, triangle, _
**Answer: Circle** — triangle and circle take turns.
