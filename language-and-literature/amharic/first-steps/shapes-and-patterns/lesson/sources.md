Name variants, carried as the books give them: rectangle appears as አራት ጎን (KG2 p.144) and አራት ማዕዘን (KG3 p.34), and triangle as ጎነ ሶስት / ሶስት ጎን / ጎነ አራት for the family of sided shapes; the lesson teaches አራት ማዕዘን and ጎነ ሶስት as the head names and shows the variants in this file rather than silently choosing — a learner meeting the other form in a workbook will still recognise it. The star (ኮከብ) enters through KG2 p.46's shape-to-world matching (star ↔ star); it has no name-writing page in the placements, and the lesson says it is pointed to, not written. KG2 p.93 (colour the heart shapes and circle the number of the count) is placed under counting/shapes and is used by the Counting lesson, not here.
