# Hands-on — Shapes and Patterns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The shape hunt
Walk through your home and street with the shape list: ክብ, ካሬ,
ጎነ ሶስት, አራት ማዕዘን, ሞላላ. Find each shape on a real thing — a
plate, a window, a roof corner, a door, an egg — and say the
shape's name and the thing's name together.

## Build with shapes
Cut or draw shapes and join them: a house (square + triangle),
a car (rectangle + two circles), a tree (triangle + rectangle).
Joining shapes is the books' own building work — say each shape
as you place it.

## A pattern on the ground
Lay stones, leaves and bottle tops in a pattern row on the
ground: stone, leaf, stone, leaf… Ask someone at home what comes
next. Then make a harder row: two stones, one leaf, two stones,
one leaf.

## Trace it big
Draw a big circle, square and triangle in the air with your
whole arm, then on paper with dotted lines first and a full line
after — the way the GIF's pencil goes.
