# Video script — Shapes and Patterns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Round and round… **ክብ** — a circle. Like the bread.
Four same sides… **ካሬ** — a square. Like the window.
Three sides, three corners… **ጎነ ሶስት** — a triangle.
Long and short sides… **አራት ማዕዘን** — a rectangle. Like the TV.
A stretched circle… **ሞላላ** — an oval. Like the egg.

Now a row: circle, square, circle, square, circle…
What comes next? A square! The row takes turns.

Now you. Find a shape in your room. Say its name.
Make a row. Ask: what comes next?
