# Lesson plan — Shapes and Patterns

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Shapes and Patterns
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the shape names — ክብ, ካሬ,
ጎነ ሶስት, አራት ማዕዘን, ሞላላ and the star — where they live in the
world, and how a pattern row grows: what comes next.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own
world — a round bread, a window, an egg, the things on their table.
It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The shapes** — ክብ (kib, circle), ካሬ (kare, square), ጎነ ሶስት (gone sost, triangle), አራት ማዕዘን (rectangle), ሞላላ (molala, oval), and the star: colouring them, tracing them on the dots, matching them to the things they live in (circle ↔ round bread, square ↔ window, triangle ↔ watermelon slice, oval ↔ egg, rectangle ↔ TV), joining shapes together, drawing them, and writing their names.
2. **Patterns — what comes next** — colour-and-shape pattern rows to copy, continue and complete: circle-rectangle…, triangle-heart…, rows of fish in colour patterns with the last one left empty — the learner reads the row and says what comes next.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Colours (this unit) — the pattern rows run on
  shape AND colour together. Recommended, never forced.
- Counting (this unit) counts the shapes on several of these pages;
  the counting of shapes lives there and here together.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the shapes in Amharic: ክብ, ካሬ, ጎነ ሶስት, አራት ማዕዘን, ሞላላ —
   and point to the star.
2. Colour, trace on the dots, copy and draw each shape.
3. Match each shape to a thing in the world: circle to round
   bread, square to a window, triangle to a watermelon slice,
   oval to an egg, rectangle to a TV.
4. Write the shapes' names.
5. Read a pattern row and say — then draw or colour — what comes
   next.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Colour pencils. Things around the home with
shapes to find: a plate, a window, an egg, a book.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes square and rectangle → a square's sides are all the same;
  a rectangle has two long sides and two short ones. Hold a book
  (rectangle) beside a square window frame.
- Calls every round shape a circle → an egg is a stretched circle:
  that stretch is what makes it ሞላላ, an oval.
- Cannot see the pattern → say the row aloud as words — "circle,
  square, circle, square" — the ear hears the pattern the eye
  missed.
- This lesson's next door: Size and quantity (same unit) — the
  shapes get compared and counted there.
