Honesty notes. (1) Book 1's printed p.4 carries no instruction line and no content picture in the book's text layer; the placements record that visually it is the zigzag/wave tracing page completing the printed pp.2–4 tracing set (toucan to perch, monkey to banana), and the lesson teaches it as such, exactly as placed — nothing was invented to fill the silent page. (2) The pencil grip itself is NOT taught in this lesson beyond "a hold that works": the grip belongs to the shared Pencil and Lines course, as the build brief rules, and Book 1's own two hand pictures (printed p.1) are described, not redrawn — no grip GIF was made here, by design. (3) The two travel GIFs (7988/7989) and the dotted-paths picture (7990) are drawn renders of the placements' own media need ("GIF for pp.2–4: a pencil travelling slowly along a dotted wavy/zigzag path, left to right"), frame-checked before filing; they are renders, not scans of book pages. (4) The fine-motor support activities rest on SUPPORTING teacher evidence only (Book 2) and are labelled as such wherever they appear; they are help for the hand, never a label on the child.
