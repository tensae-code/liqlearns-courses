# Video script — Ready Hands — Before the Pencil

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take the pencil. Hold it. Soft. Ready.

Now — scribble! Round and round. Up and down.
Fast. Slow. Your hand moves. The pencil follows.
Good hand.

Now a road. A road of dots.
Pencil on the first dot. Go slow. Do not lift.

Straight road. Dot, dot, dot. You did it.

Wavy road. Up… down… up… down. Like a river.
Slow is smooth.

Zigzag road. Up! Corner. Turn. Down! Corner. Turn.
One side at a time. Finish the road.

Your hand is ready.
The letters are waiting.
