# Ready Hands — Before the Pencil — writing

Do it with your pencil. (A teacher or the app watches the doing, not just the page.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fill one big empty box with free scribbles — across, bouncing, and one big round scribble like a ball of string.
**Answer: a box full of steered scribble lines** — checked by watching: the hand holds the pencil ready and sends the lines where it wants them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Follow the dotted wavy road on your practice page from the first dot to the last, without lifting the pencil.
**Answer: one unbroken line riding the wave** — checked by watching: left to right, tip on the dots, no lifting.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Follow the dotted zigzag road, stopping and turning at every corner.
**Answer: one unbroken zigzag line with a turn at each corner** — checked by watching: each side climbed in one run, corners turned, not rounded off.
