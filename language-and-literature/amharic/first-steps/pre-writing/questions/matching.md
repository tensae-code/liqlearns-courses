# Ready Hands — Before the Pencil — matching

Match each road to the trip the pencil takes on it.

**Set 1. (Sub lesson 2) Road — how the pencil travels it**

1. the straight dotted road — b
2. the wavy dotted road — a
3. the zigzag dotted road — c

a) up and down like a river, never lifting
b) dot to dot in one straight run
c) climb, stop at the corner, turn, climb again

**Answer key:** 1 — b · 2 — a · 3 — c.

**Set 2. (Sub lesson 1) Start of the lesson — what the hand does**

1. pick up the pencil — c
2. scribble in the big empty box — a
3. steer the scribble slowly — b

a) make free marks — none of them can be wrong
b) send the lines where you want them to go
c) hold it ready, pinched near its front

**Answer key:** 1 — c · 2 — a · 3 — b.
