# Ready Hands — Before the Pencil — ordering

Put the steps in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the roads in the order this lesson teaches them: zigzag road, straight road, wavy road.
**Answer: straight road, wavy road, zigzag road** — straight first, then the bending road, then the road with corners.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the start of the lesson in order: steer slow scribbles, pick up the pencil, scribble freely.
**Answer: pick up the pencil, scribble freely, steer slow scribbles** — hold first, free marks second, steering third.
