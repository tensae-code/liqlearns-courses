# Ready Hands — Before the Pencil — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In free scribbling, a mark can be wrong.
**Answer: False** — መሞነጫጨር is free; the marks are yours and none of them can be wrong.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** You may lift the pencil whenever you like while following a dotted road.
**Answer: False** — the road is travelled in one trip: tip on the dots, no lifting, until the road ends.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On a wavy road, going slow makes the line smoother.
**Answer: True** — slow is smooth; the pencil follows the wave up and down without rushing.
