# Ready Hands — Before the Pencil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is free scribbling (መሞነጫጨር) for?
a) Making marks that can be wrong  b) Moving the pencil freely so the hand learns to steer it  c) Filling the page as fast as you can
**Answer: b)** — no scribble mark can be wrong; the point is a hand that moves the pencil on purpose.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When you follow a dotted line, which way do you travel?
a) Right to left  b) From the middle out  c) Left to right
**Answer: c) left to right** — the way reading and writing will go.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Your pencil reaches a corner on the zigzag road. What do you do?
a) Lift the pencil and jump to the next side  b) Stop for a breath, turn, and climb the next side  c) Draw through the corner and round it off
**Answer: b)** — corners are part of the road; stop, turn, continue — without lifting.
