# Hands-on — Feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mirror faces
Stand at a mirror. A friend says a feeling — happy, surprised,
sad, angry — and you make that face. Four feelings, then swap.

## Feelings stones
Draw a different face on four stones or four pieces of paper:
happy, surprised, sad, angry. Hold up the stone that matches how
you feel at breakfast, at play, and at bedtime. Say the name each
time.

## Same feeling hunt
Two people each draw a secret feeling face on paper. Show them at
the same time — if the feelings match, both of you say the name
together.

## If it is hard
Be a smiling, calm model: make the face yourself first, talk about
your own feelings plainly, and when a child shows a feeling the
"wrong" way, show the right face without emphasising the mistake
(SNE toolkit support, printed pp.27–30 — written as help, never as
a label on the child).
