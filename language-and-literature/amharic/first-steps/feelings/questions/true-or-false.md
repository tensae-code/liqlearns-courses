# Feelings — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Two different children can show the same feeling on their faces.
**Answer: True** — match by the mouth and the eyebrows.

**2.** ማዘን means happy.
**Answer: False** — ማዘን is sad; መደሰት is happy.

**3.** A sulking face (ማኩረፍ) is turned away and not speaking.
**Answer: True.**

**4.** An angry face and a happy face look the same.
**Answer: False.**
