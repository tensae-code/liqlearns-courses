# Feelings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A face is smiling with closed, joyful eyes. The feeling is —
a) sad  b) happy  c) angry
**Answer: b) happy** — መደሰት.

**2.** A face has tears and a mouth turned down. The feeling is —
a) sad  b) surprised  c) happy
**Answer: a) sad** — ማዘን.

**3.** Eyebrows pulled down hard, mouth tight. The feeling is —
a) surprised  b) angry  c) sulking
**Answer: b) angry** — መናደድ.

**4.** Wide eyes and a round open mouth show —
a) fear only  b) surprise  c) happiness
**Answer: b) surprise.**

**5.** መፍራት means —
a) happy  b) afraid  c) angry
**Answer: b) afraid.**
