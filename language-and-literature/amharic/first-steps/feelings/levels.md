# Feelings — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons, so this file
is its depth record for the whole lesson. Pass mark for the whole
lesson: name the feeling on each of the four big faces, match two
faces showing the same feeling, and write or say the wider feeling
words (መከፋት upset, መፍራት afraid) when a face shows them. Practice
deepens by adding faces — sulking and afraid join the first four —
and by harder corners: two feelings that look close (sad and
sulking, angry and upset) told apart by the mouth and eyebrows.
