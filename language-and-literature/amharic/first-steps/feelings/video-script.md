# Video script — Feelings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at my face.

I smile. I am **happy** — መደሰት.
Oh! My mouth is round. I am **surprised**.
Now tears. I am **sad** — ማዘን.
Eyebrows down! I am **angry** — መናደድ.

Happy. Surprised. Sad. Angry.
Feelings live on faces.

Now you. Make a happy face. Make a sad face.
What are you feeling today?
