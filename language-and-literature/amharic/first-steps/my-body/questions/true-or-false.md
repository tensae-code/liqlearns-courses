# My Body — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The እግር is the leg and foot.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ጥርስ (teeth) are in the hand.
**Answer: False** — the teeth are in the mouth.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The tongue is for hearing.
**Answer: False** — ምላስ ለመቅመስ, the tongue is for tasting; the ear is for hearing.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** We have five senses.
**Answer: True** — seeing, smelling, tasting, hearing, feeling.
