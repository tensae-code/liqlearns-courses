# My Body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point to the አይን. Which part is it?
a) the ear  b) the eye  c) the nose
**Answer: b) the eye.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word names the hair?
a) ፀጉር  b) እግር  c) ሆድ
**Answer: a) ፀጉር.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The ጉልበት is —
a) the shoulder  b) the belly  c) the knee
**Answer: c) the knee** — the leg bends there.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is NOT a part of the body?
a) ደረት (chest)  b) ኩባያ (cup)  c) አንገት (neck)
**Answer: b) ኩባያ** — a cup is a thing in the home, not a body part.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** አይን ለማየት — the eye is for —
a) hearing  b) tasting  c) seeing
**Answer: c) seeing.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sense do you use to hear a song?
a) the ear  b) the tongue  c) the hand
**Answer: a) the ear** — ጆሮ ለመስማት, the ear is for hearing.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** You smell coffee with your —
a) eye  b) nose  c) knee
**Answer: b) nose** — አፍንጫ ለማሽት.

**8. (Sub lesson 1)** The wide, round person in the picture is —
a) ቀጭን (thin)  b) ወፍራም (fat)  c) ረዥም (long)
**Answer: b) ወፍራም** — ወፍራም is fat; ቀጭን is thin.

**9. (Sub lesson 1)** The child whose ፀጉር grows down past the shoulders has —
a) short hair  b) no hair  c) long hair
**Answer: c) long hair** — short hair stops close to the head; long hair grows down.

**10. (Sub lesson 1)** When two children meet and shake hands, ሰላምታ ሲለዋወጡ, they are —
a) exchanging greetings  b) exchanging shoes  c) counting
**Answer: a) exchanging greetings** — they face each other, smile, shake hands and say ሰላም.
