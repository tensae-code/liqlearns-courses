# My Body — short answer

Answer out loud, in one short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three parts of the head.
**Answer: any three of — hair (ፀጉር), forehead (ግንባር), eyebrow (ቅንድብ), eye (አይን), ear (ጆሮ), nose (አፍንጫ), teeth (ጥርስ).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Point to your ትከሻ. What is it in English?
**Answer: the shoulder.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the nose do?
**Answer: it smells** — አፍንጫ ለማሽት.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sense tells you a stone is rough?
**Answer: feeling** — the hand and skin.
