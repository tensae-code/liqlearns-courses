# My Body

## Do it first

Stand up. Touch your head. Touch your eyes. Touch your ears, your
nose, your hands, your legs. You already know this body — it is
yours. Now learn the name of every part, and what the special
helpers — your senses — do for you.

## Sub lesson 1 — The parts of my body

Here is a child, standing still, so we can name every part.

[Picture 8010 — the labelled body figure]

Start at the top. **ፀጉር** (ts'egur) is the hair. Under the hair is
the **ግንባር** (ginbar), the forehead. Under the forehead are the
**ቅንድብ** (qindib), the eyebrows. Then the **አይን** (ayn), the eye —
you have two. At the side of the head is the **ጆሮ** (joro), the ear
— two of those too. In the middle of the face is the **አፍንጫ**
(afinch'a), the nose. Inside the mouth are the **ጥርስ** (t'irs), the
teeth. Under the head is the **አንገት** (anget), the neck.

Now the body. Where the arm joins the body is the **ትከሻ** (tikesha),
the shoulder. The front of the body is the **ደረት** (deret), the
chest, and under it the **ሆድ** (hod), the belly. At the end of the
arm is the **እጅ** (ij), the hand. The top of the leg is the **ጭን**
(ch'in), the thigh. The leg bends at the **ጉልበት** (gulibet), the
knee. The whole leg and the foot at its end are the **እግር** (igir).

Point to each part on the picture. Then point to it on your own
body. Say its name out loud.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five senses

Five parts of your body are helpers. They bring the world to you.

[Picture 8011 — the five senses]

**አይን ለማየት** — the eye is for seeing. You see colours, faces, and
the road in front of you.

**አፍንጫ ለማሽት** — the nose is for smelling. You smell coffee, rain,
and smoke.

**ምላስ ለመቅመስ** — the tongue is for tasting. You taste sweet, sour,
and salty.

**ጆሮ ለመስማት** — the ear is for hearing. You hear voices, songs, and
someone calling your name.

Your **እጅ**, your hand — and your skin — are for feeling. You feel
hot and cold, rough and smooth, soft and hard.

Watch the pointing dot. Where it stops, touch the same part on
your own body and say what it does.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: "This is my አይን — my eye — it is for seeing."
Do the same for the nose, the tongue, the ear and the hand. Then
let your companion name any body part, and you point to it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Stand in front of a mirror. Point to ten parts of your body and
   say each name in Amharic.
2. Close your eyes. A friend puts a safe thing in your hand — a
   stone, a cup, a cloth. Feel it and say what it is. Your hand
   did that.
3. At your next meal, name the sense you use for each thing: you
   see the food, you smell it, you taste it. Say the three sense
   sentences aloud.
