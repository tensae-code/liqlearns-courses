# Hands-on — My Body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Touch and name
A grown-up or a friend says a body part in Amharic — አይን, ጆሮ, እጅ —
and you touch that part on your own body as fast as you can. Ten
parts. Then swap: you say, they touch.

## Draw me
Draw a person on paper — head, body, arms, legs. Then point to each
part of your drawing and say its name. Add the eyes, the ears, the
nose and the teeth.

## The senses walk
Walk around your home. Find one thing to look at closely, one
smell, one sound, one taste (only food a grown-up gives you), and
one thing to feel with your hand — rough or smooth? Say which sense
you used for each one.

## If it is hard
Work with one part at a time, with the picture and the spoken name
together, and let the child point before naming. Break the naming
into small steps and praise each step before the next one
(SNE toolkit support, printed pp.26–27 — written as help, never as
a label on the child).
