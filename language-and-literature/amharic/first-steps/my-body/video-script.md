# Video script — My Body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The second
half carries the "All about me" mezmur's body-part count as a
sing-along (SUPPORTING — Training module, Session 3.1 Activity 1):
the song is named and its count is given; its full words are not
transcribed.

---

This is my body. Touch it. Name it.

**ፀጉር** — hair. **ግንባር** — forehead. **አይን** — eye.
**አፍንጫ** — nose. **ጆሮ** — ear. **እጅ** — hand. **እግር** — leg.

Now the five helpers.

My **eye** sees. አይን ለማየት.
My **nose** smells. አፍንጫ ለማሽት.
My **tongue** tastes. ምላስ ለመቅመስ.
My **ear** hears. ጆሮ ለመስማት.
My **hand** feels.

Sing with me — count my body:
hair — one! forehead — two! eye — three! nose — four! ear — five!
cheek — six! chin — seven! neck — eight! hand — nine! leg — ten!

Now you. Point. Name it. What does it do?
That is your body.
