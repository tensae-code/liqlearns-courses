# Hands-on — Water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Where does our water come from?
At home, walk with a grown-up to where your drinking water comes from — the tap, the container, the well. Say its name: የቧንቧ ውሃ, የጉድጓድ ውሃ, የታሸገ ውሃ. Pour a cup and drink: መጠጣት.

## Water sorting walk
Outside with a grown-up, find water or signs of water: a tap, a bottle, a puddle, a river if one is near. For each one say: natural, or man-made? Never go into water alone — swimming ዋና is only with a teacher or a grown-up watching, like the children in the picture.

## Paper boat
Fold a small paper boat and float it in a basin of water. Say ጀልባ — boat — as it sails. The real traditional boat of Lake Tana is made of papyrus reeds.
