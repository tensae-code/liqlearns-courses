# Lesson plan — Water

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Water
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** where water comes from — the natural sources (lake, river, waterfall, cave) and the sources people bring (dam, well, tap, bottle) — and the water we play in: swimming and boats.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own world — the water the child drinks, washes in and sees in rivers and lakes. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This is a First Steps lesson — a child can start here. The nature and man-made lesson next door helps, because this lesson sorts water the same way: natural, or made by people. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the natural water sources: ሓይቅ (lake), cave water, ፏፏቴ (waterfall), ወንዝ (river).
2. Name the water words: ውሃ (water), ሓይቅ (lake), አባይ (Abay), ወንዝ (river), መጠጣት (drinking).
3. Say the two boats: the traditional boat ባህላዊ ጀልባ on Lake Tana, the modern boat ዘመናዊ ጀልባ on Lake Langano.
4. Name the man-made water sources: የግድብ ውሃ (dam), የታሸገ ውሃ (bottled), የጉድጓድ ውሃ (well), የቧንቧ ውሃ (tap) — and sort any source into natural or man-made.

## What you need
The pictures in the lesson. A cup of real water for the hands-on work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls tap water natural because water is natural → the water is nature's, but the tap የቧንቧ is people's work — the source is man-made, the way the dam and the well are. Sort by how the water reaches us.
- Mixes the two lakes and boats → Lake Tana holds the traditional boat ባህላዊ ጀልባ; Lake Langano holds the modern boat ዘመናዊ ጀልባ. Old boat, Tana; new boat, Langano.
- Thinks a waterfall is man-made because it is big and loud → ፏፏቴ falls by itself; nobody built it. Natural.
