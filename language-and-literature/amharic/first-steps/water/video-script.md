# Video script — Water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The target
words are in Amharic, with the transliteration the books give.

---

Water! ውሃ!

Here is a lake. ሓይቅ.
Children are learning to swim. ዋና!
A boat is crossing — ባህላዊ ጀልባ, the traditional boat.
This is Lake Tana.

A river — ወንዝ. The big one is አባይ — Abay!
Water falls — ፏፏቴ, a waterfall!
Lake, river, waterfall — nature's water.

And water people bring:
a dam — ግድብ. A well — ጉድጓድ. A tap — ቧንቧ. A bottle — የታሸገ ውሃ.
We drink — መጠጣት.

Now you. Name a water place. Natural, or man-made?
