# Water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a natural water source?
a) a tap ቧንቧ  b) a waterfall ፏፏቴ  c) a bottle
**Answer: b) a waterfall ፏፏቴ** — the waterfall falls by itself; nobody built it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The traditional boat ባህላዊ ጀልባ sails on —
a) Lake Langano  b) Lake Tana  c) the Abay river only
**Answer: b) Lake Tana** — the modern boat ዘመናዊ ጀልባ is the one on Lake Langano.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** መጠጣት means —
a) swimming  b) drinking  c) raining
**Answer: b) drinking.** Swimming is ዋና.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** የጉድጓድ ውሃ is —
a) well water  b) river water  c) waterfall water
**Answer: a) well water** — people dig the well ጉድጓድ down to the water.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a man-made water source?
a) lake water  b) cave water  c) dam water የግድብ ውሃ
**Answer: c) dam water የግድብ ውሃ** — people build the dam ግድብ.
