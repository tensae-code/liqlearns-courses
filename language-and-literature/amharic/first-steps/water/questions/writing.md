# Water — writing

Copy-write, the book's own writing work.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copy the water words: ውሃ (water), ሓይቅ (lake), አባይ (Abay), ወንዝ (river), መጠጣት (drinking).
**Done when:** each word is copied whole, letter by letter.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Copy the man-made sources: የግድብ ውሃ (dam water), የጉድጓድ ውሃ (well water), የቧንቧ ውሃ (tap water).
**Done when:** each name is copied whole, word by word.
