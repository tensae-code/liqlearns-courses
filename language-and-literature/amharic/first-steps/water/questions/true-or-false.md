# Water — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Children learn to swim ዋና with a teacher watching them.
**True** — that is the book's swimming picture, and the safe way.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tap water የቧንቧ ውሃ is a natural water source.
**False** — the tap is people's work; tap water is a man-made source, like the dam, the well and the bottle.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** አባይ (Abay) is the name of a great river.
**True.**
