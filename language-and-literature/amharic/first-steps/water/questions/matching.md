# Water — matching

Match each water source to its kind: natural or man-made.

**Set 1 (Sub lessons 1 and 2)**
1. ሓይቅ (lake) — natural
2. ቧንቧ (tap) — man-made
3. ፏፏቴ (waterfall) — natural
4. ጉድጓድ (well) — man-made
5. ወንዝ (river) — natural
6. የታሸገ ውሃ (bottled water) — man-made

**Answer key:** 1 natural · 2 man-made · 3 natural · 4 man-made · 5 natural · 6 man-made.
