# Water

## Do it first

Have a drink of water. Now think: where did that water come from before it reached your cup? A river? A tap? A bottle? This lesson follows the water back to all its homes.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water from nature

Some water is simply in the world, where nature put it. These are the natural water sources — ተፈጥሮአዊ የውሃ መገኛ:

- Lake water — the water of a ሓይቅ (lake)
- Cave water
- Waterfall water — ፏፏቴ, water falling from the rocks
- River water — the water of a ወንዝ (river)

Copy-write the sources: river, lake, cave, waterfall.

And we play in this water. Look at the lake:

- አባይ — Abay, the great river
- ወንዝ — river
- መጠጣት — drinking

## Sub lesson 2 — Water people bring

Other water reaches us because people did the work. These are the man-made water sources — ሰው ሰራሽ የውሃ መገኛ መንገዶች:

[Picture 8074 — the water sources]

- የግድብ ውሃ — dam water: people build a dam, ግድብ, and hold the river's water.
- የታሸገ ውሃ — bottled water: water put into bottles.
- የጉድጓድ ውሃ — well water: people dig a well, ጉድጓድ, down to the water under the ground.
- የቧንቧ ውሃ — tap water: water that comes through the tap, ቧንቧ, in the house.

The sorting test is the same one you know: did people make this way of getting water? A lake — no: natural. A tap — yes: man-made. The water itself is nature's gift either way; it is the source we are sorting.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: two natural water sources and two man-made ones. Then tell them where your own drinking water comes from, and say its name.

## Do It Yourself

1. Copy-write the water sources: river ወንዝ, lake ሓይቅ, cave, waterfall ፏፏቴ.
2. Copy-write the man-made sources: የግድብ ውሃ, የታሸገ ውሃ, የጉድጓድ ውሃ, የቧንቧ ውሃ.
3. Draw a lake with a boat on it. Say which boat yours is: ባህላዊ ጀልባ or ዘመናዊ ጀልባ.
4. At home, find the water you drink. Say its source's name.
