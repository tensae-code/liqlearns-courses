# Money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which picture shows a note, not a coin?
a) a round hard 10 santim  b) a paper 100 birr  c) a round 50 santim
**Answer: b) a paper 100 birr** — notes are paper.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is the most money?
a) 10 birr  b) 50 birr  c) 200 birr
**Answer: c) 200 birr** — the biggest number.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** አስር ብር means —
a) 10 birr  b) 100 birr  c) 50 birr
**Answer: a) 10 birr**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Santim are —
a) paper notes  b) round coins  c) stones
**Answer: b) round coins**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which coin is the most?
a) 5 santim  b) 25 santim  c) 50 santim
**Answer: c) 50 santim**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** ሀያ አምስት ሳንቲም means —
a) 10 santim  b) 25 santim  c) 50 santim
**Answer: b) 25 santim**
