# Money — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A 200 birr note is more money than a 10 birr note.
**Answer: true**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Santim coins are made of paper.
**Answer: false** — coins are round and hard.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 10 santim is more than 5 santim.
**Answer: true**
