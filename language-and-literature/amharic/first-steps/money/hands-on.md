# Hands-on — Money

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the money
Lay out play notes and coins (or cut-out pictures). Match each one to its twin — the same note with the same note, the same coin with the same coin.

## The little shop
With a grown-up, play shop: one bread, one egg. Pay with play money and name what you hand over: አምስት ብር — five birr.

## Sort big and small
Sort a mixed pile into two piles: notes (ብር) and coins (ሳንቲም). Then put each pile in order, smallest first.
