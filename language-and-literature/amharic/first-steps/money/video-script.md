# Video script — Money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is money. ብር.

Ten birr. አስር ብር.
Fifty birr. ሃምሳ ብር.
One hundred birr. መቶ ብር.
Two hundred birr. ሁለት መቶ ብር.

And the small round coins. ሳንቲም.
Five. Ten. Twenty-five. Fifty.

Big money, small money.
All of it is Ethiopia's money.
