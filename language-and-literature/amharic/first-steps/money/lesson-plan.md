# Lesson plan — Money

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Money
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's money — the birr notes (ብር) and the santim coins (ሳንቲም) — what each one looks like and what it is called.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Birr notes** — the paper money: 1, 5, 10, 50, 100 and 200 birr — big number, name, and colour.
2. **Santim coins** — the small round money: 1, 5, 10, 25 and 50 santim.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Counting — money is counting with real things. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the birr notes: አስር ብር (10), ሃምሳ ብር (50), መቶ ብር (100), ሁለት መቶ ብር (200), and the smaller አንድ ብር (1) and አምስት ብር (5).
2. Name the santim coins: አምስት ሳንቲም (5), አስር ሳንቲም (10), ሀያ አምስት ሳንቲም (25), ሃምሳ ሳንቲም (50), አንድ ሳንቲም (1).
3. Match the same note to the same note, and the same coin to the same coin.
4. Say which is bigger money: a note or a coin; 200 birr or 10 birr.

## What you need
Play money or the pictures in this lesson. Real coins a grown-up lends for looking are best of all.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes notes and coins → notes are paper and flat and big; coins are round and hard and small. Birr are the big money; santim are the small money.
- Mixes 50 and 5 → look at the whole number: 50 has two digits and is a note of ሃምሳ ብር; 5 santim is a small round coin.
