The pictures in this lesson are drawn teaching pictures standing in for the fronts of the notes and coins (the placements ask for "a clear front picture" of each); the 10 birr note is drawn blue-ish and the others in approximating colours — they are named as drawings in the lesson and the plan, never as photographs. The market sentences on KG3 printed p.43 ("I went to the market", "I bought bread", "its price is five…") belong to the first-sentences lesson and are cross-referenced there.
