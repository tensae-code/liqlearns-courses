# Safety

## Do it first

Look at the warning picture in this lesson. Point to everything in it that could hurt a child. Count them. Now we learn their names and the rules that keep us safe.

## Sub lesson 1 — Things that can hurt us

[Picture 8167 — the warning set]

Some things are not for playing. Learn their names so you can stay away from them:

- **ቢላዋ** — a knife
- **ምላጭ** — a razor
- **መኮትኮቻ** — a sharp poker
- **የሙዝ ልጣጭ** — a banana peel (it makes feet slide and fall)

And three rules for home:

1. **Don't play with sharp things.**
2. **Never touch fire.**
3. **Don't play near electricity.** Touching an electric wire is never proper — that sentence is FALSE, always.

Sharp things, fire, and wires are for grown-ups only.

## Sub lesson 2 — Disasters — from nature or from people

Some dangers are very big. They are called disasters — አደጋዎች. They come in two kinds.

**From nature:**

- **ሰደድ እሳተ** — wildfire, fire running through dry grass and trees
- **እሳተ ጎመራ** — a volcano
- **ጎርፍ** — a flood, too much water

**Made by people:**

- Car accidents
- Factory smoke
- Waste that never rots away (non-biodegradable waste)

One question sorts them all: did nature make it, or did people make it?

## Say it to your companion

Name a danger thing; your companion says its rule. Name a disaster; your companion says its kind — from nature, or made by people. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name all four things in the warning picture, with the rule for each.
2. Say the three home rules without stopping.
3. Sort these: flood, car accident, volcano, factory smoke — nature, or people?
4. Put today's fruit peels in the bin. Every time.
