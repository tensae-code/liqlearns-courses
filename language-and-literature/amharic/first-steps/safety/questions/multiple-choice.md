# Safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which thing must a child never play with?
a) a ball  b) a knife  c) a book
**Answer: b) a knife** — ቢላዋ — sharp things are for grown-ups.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A banana peel on the floor is —
a) a toy  b) a danger — feet slide and fall  c) food for later
**Answer: b) a danger — feet slide and fall** — peels go in the bin.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Near electricity, we —
a) play  b) never play  c) touch the wires
**Answer: b) never play**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A flood (ጎርፍ) comes from —
a) people  b) nature  c) school
**Answer: b) nature**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Factory smoke is a disaster made by —
a) nature  b) people  c) animals
**Answer: b) people**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A volcano (እሳተ ጎመራ) comes from —
a) people  b) nature  c) the market
**Answer: b) nature**
