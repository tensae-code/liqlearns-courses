# Safety — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Touching an electric wire is proper.
**Answer: false** — never proper — the book's own true/false item.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** We never touch fire.
**Answer: true**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A car accident is a disaster from nature.
**Answer: false** — car accidents are made by people.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A wildfire can come from nature.
**Answer: true**
