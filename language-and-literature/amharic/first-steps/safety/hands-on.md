# Hands-on — Safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The danger hunt (pictures only)
Look through the warning picture and around your home WITH a grown-up. Point at the things a child must never touch. The grown-up moves or covers real dangers — children only point, never touch.

## Peel in the bin
Every banana peel you meet today goes straight in the bin. A peel on the floor is a fall waiting.

## Nature or people?
Name the day's happenings: rain, a flood, smoke, a crash far away. Sort each one: from nature, or made by people?
