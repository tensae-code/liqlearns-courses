# Lesson plan — Safety

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Safety
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** some things can hurt us — sharp things, fire, electric wires — and some big events bring danger, from nature or from people; we stay safe by never touching the first and by knowing the second.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Things that can hurt us** — the danger words — knife ቢላዋ, razor ምላጭ, banana peel የሙዝ ልጣጭ — and the home rules: never play with sharp things, never touch fire, never play near electricity.
2. **Disasters — from nature or from people** — wildfire ሰደድ እሳተ, volcano እሳተ ጎመራ and flood ጎርፍ come from nature; car accidents, factory smoke and waste that never rots away are made by people.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Road Safety — the road half of staying safe. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the danger things: መኮትኮቻ, ቢላዋ (knife), የሙዝ ልጣጭ (banana peel), ምላጭ (razor).
2. Say the three home rules: don't play with sharp things; never touch fire; don't play near electricity.
3. Say why "touching an electric wire is proper" is false.
4. Sort disasters: wildfire, volcano and flood from nature; car accidents, factory smoke and non-biodegradable waste made by people.

## What you need
The warning picture in this lesson. Nothing real and sharp is ever used for teaching.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a banana peel is harmless → a peel on the floor makes feet slide; we drop, we fall, we hurt. Peels go in the bin, not on the ground.
- Mixes the two kinds of disaster → ask one question: did nature make it, or did people make it? Fire in the wild grass, the volcano and the flood — nature. The car crash and the factory smoke — people.
