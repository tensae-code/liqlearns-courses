# Hands-on — Caring for Books and Shared Things

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Cover a real book
Take one school book. Wrap it in paper or plastic, neatly, corners folded. Stand it upright on a shelf or table edge.

## The hand-on check
Pass your covered book to a partner. Your partner checks: cover clean, pages whole, nothing written inside. Then swap.
