# Caring for Books and Shared Things — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where should you write instead of in the book?
**Answer:** In your notebook — the book itself stays clean.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you do when you find a small tear?
**Answer:** Repair it at once, gently, before it grows bigger.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What should you do when a shared thing is damaged?
**Answer:** Report the damage at once so it can be repaired.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things a book should be kept away from.
**Answer:** Food and small children (also rain/damp) — the care rules name them.
