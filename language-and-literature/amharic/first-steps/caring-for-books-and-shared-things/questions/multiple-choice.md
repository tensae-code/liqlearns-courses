# Caring for Books and Shared Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who does a school textbook belong to?
a) one child alone  b) the school — it is shared by many students  c) the teacher's house
**Answer: b) the school — it is shared by many students** — the school owns it; each learner keeps it for a time and passes it on.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How should a book stand on a shelf?
a) lying flat under heavy things  b) upright, not bent  c) open, face down
**Answer: b) upright, not bent** — upright standing keeps its spine and pages unharmed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A shared thing lasts longest when —
a) everyone uses it as if it were theirs alone  b) everyone uses it gently and leaves it clean  c) no one ever uses it
**Answer: b) everyone uses it gently and leaves it clean** — gentle, clean use by everyone is what makes shared things last.
