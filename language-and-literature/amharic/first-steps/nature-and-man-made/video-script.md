# Video script — Nature and Man-Made

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The target
words are in Amharic, with the transliteration the books give.

---

Look around. Who made all these things?

A tree! Did people make the tree? No!
The tree grows by itself. Natural. ተፈጥሮአዊ.
The sea. The soil. The rocks. Natural!

A house! Did people make the house? Yes!
ሰው ሰራሽ — man-made.
A car. A chair. A bed. People made them. Man-made!

Look — big mountains! ሰሜን ተራራ — the Simien Mountains.
And a big river! የአባይ ወንዝ — the Abay river.
Nobody made them. Natural. Ethiopia's own!

Now — living, or not living?
Animals — living! Plants — living! People — living!
A car… does it grow? No. Not living. A balloon — not living.

Now you. Point at a thing. Did people make it? Does it grow?
