# Hands-on — Nature and Man-Made

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-pile walk
Walk around your home with a grown-up. Touch ten things, one by one, and for each one ask out loud: "Did people make it?" Put a small stone next to the natural things and a leaf next to the man-made things, or sort them into two piles on paper.

## Keep our world clean
The caring work of the book: with a grown-up, pick up litter in a green place near home and put it in the right place. People made the litter; the green place is natural. We care for the environment — የአካባቢ እንክብካቤ.

## Living, not living
Point at things outside and sort them with your voice: tree — living; stone — not living; dog — living; cup — not living. How many of each can you find?
