# Nature and Man-Made

## Do it first

Look at the things around you right now. Touch one of them. Now answer this: did people make that thing, or was it already in the world before people? Keep your answer — this lesson is that question, for the whole world.

## Sub lesson 1 — Made by people, or not?

Some things are in the world by themselves. Nobody made them. They are natural things — ተፈጥሮአዊ ነገሮች:

- The sea
- The soil
- A tree
- The rocks

And look at Ethiopia's own natural places:

[Picture 8064 — the Simien Mountains and the Abay river]

- ሰሜን ተራራ — the Simien Mountains, big and green and high.
- የአባይ ወንዝ — the Abay river, the great river of Ethiopia.

Nobody built the mountains. Nobody poured the river. They are natural.

Other things exist because people made them. They are man-made things — ሰው ሰራሽ ነገሮች:

- A jumper
- A house — and the little round gojo hut, ጎጆ ቤት
- A bed
- A chair
- An aeroplane
- A car

And a farmer ploughing his field: the field work is people's work, in the middle of the natural world.

[Picture 8071 — natural or man-made?]

The test question is always the same: **did people make it?** Yes — man-made. No — natural.

One more piece of this part, and it matters: we care for the natural world. Children picking up litter in a green park — that is caring for the environment, የአካባቢ እንክብካቤ. The park is natural; the litter is man-made; we take the litter away.

## Sub lesson 2 — Living or not living

Now a second sort, a different question: **does it grow and breathe?**

Living things — ህይወት ያላቸው ነገሮች:

- Animals
- Plants
- People

Living things grow. A baby animal grows big. A small plant grows tall. You grow.

Non-living things — ህይወት የሌላቸው ነገሮች:

- A car
- A balloon

A car does not grow. A balloon does not breathe. However big a car is, it will never grow bigger by itself.

Watch out — the two sorts are not the same sort! A rock is natural, but it is not living. A wooden chair is made of a tree, but the chair is man-made, and it is not living. Ask both questions, one at a time.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two test questions. Then name a thing, and let your companion answer both questions: did people make it? Does it grow and breathe?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the names of 3 living things and 3 non-living things, like the book's own writing work.
2. Go through your home and name five man-made things. Say who made each one, if you know.
3. Name two natural things you can see from your door.
4. Draw one natural thing and one man-made thing on one page. Say which is which.
