Honesty notes. No reading in the placements used here is marked uncertain, and no page was unreadable. The lesson keeps the books' two sorts apart on purpose: the natural/man-made sort (KG1–KG3) and the living/non-living sort (KG3 pp.154–155) are taught as two different test questions, and the stuck notes name the two crossings (a natural non-living rock; a man-made wooden chair) that children mix up.
