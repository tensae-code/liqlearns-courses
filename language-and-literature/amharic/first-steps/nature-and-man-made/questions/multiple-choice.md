# Nature and Man-Made — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a natural thing?
a) a car  b) a tree  c) a chair
**Answer: b) a tree** — nobody made the tree; it grows by itself. People made the car and the chair.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Abay river የአባይ ወንዝ is —
a) man-made  b) natural
**Answer: b) natural** — nobody poured the river; it is Ethiopia's own natural place, like the Simien Mountains ሰሜን ተራራ.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is man-made?
a) the sea  b) the rocks  c) an aeroplane
**Answer: c) an aeroplane** — people built it. The sea and the rocks were in the world before people.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a living thing?
a) a balloon  b) a plant  c) a car
**Answer: b) a plant** — plants grow. A balloon and a car do not grow or breathe.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A rock is —
a) natural and living  b) natural but not living  c) man-made and living
**Answer: b) natural but not living** — the two sorts are different sorts; a rock does not grow or breathe.
