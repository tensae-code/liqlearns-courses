# Nature and Man-Made — matching

Match each thing to its sort.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — natural or man-made?
1. soil — natural
2. bed — man-made
3. sea — natural
4. jumper — man-made
5. rocks — natural
6. house — man-made

**Answer key:** 1 natural · 2 man-made · 3 natural · 4 man-made · 5 natural · 6 man-made.

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — living or not living?
1. a dog — living
2. a car — not living
3. a tree — living
4. a balloon — not living
5. a child — living

**Answer key:** 1 living · 2 not living · 3 living · 4 not living · 5 living.
