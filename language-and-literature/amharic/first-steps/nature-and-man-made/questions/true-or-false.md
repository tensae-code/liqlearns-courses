# Nature and Man-Made — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People built the Simien Mountains ሰሜን ተራራ.
**False** — the mountains are natural; nobody made them.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A gojo hut ጎጆ ቤት is man-made.
**True** — people build the hut, even though it stands in the natural world.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A car grows bigger by itself when it is old.
**False** — a car is a non-living thing; it does not grow.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Plants are living things.
**True** — a small plant grows into a big plant.
