# Hands-on — Plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Water a real plant
Take a cup of water to a flower or a small plant at home, like the book's watering picture. Pour slowly at the bottom, not on the leaves. Say: አበባ ውሃ ማጠጣት — I am watering the flower.

## Find the six parts
Stand by a real tree or plant with a grown-up. Touch each part as you name it: root ስር (point at the ground where it hides), trunk ግንድ, branch ቅርንጫፍ, leaf ቅጠል, flower አበባ, fruit ፍሬ. Not every plant has all six showing — which ones can you find?

## Small, medium, big
Find three plants or three leaves outside and line them up: small, medium, big. Say the order out loud. Then draw the three sizes on paper.

## Draw and colour a plant
Draw a tree and a rose, like the book's colouring pages. Colour them. Say each part's name as you colour it.
