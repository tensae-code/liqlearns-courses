# Plants — matching

Match each part of the plant to where it is.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)**
1. ስር (sir) — under the ground
2. ግንድ (gind) — the strong middle that stands up
3. ቅርንጫፍ (q'irinch'af) — grows out of the trunk
4. ቅጠል (q'itel) — flat and green, on the branch
5. አበባ (abeba) — the coloured, pretty part
6. ፍሬ (fire) — the part we pick and eat

**Answer key:** root–under the ground · trunk–the strong middle · branch–out of the trunk · leaf–flat and green · flower–the coloured part · fruit–the part we eat.
