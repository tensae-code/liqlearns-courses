# Plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which part of the plant hides under the ground?
a) the flower አበባ  b) the root ስር  c) the leaf ቅጠል
**Answer: b) the root ስር** — the root holds the plant and drinks water from the soil.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The fruit ፍሬ comes after the —
a) root  b) trunk  c) flower አበባ
**Answer: c) flower አበባ** — first the flower, then the fruit grows from it.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Point to the ቅርንጫፍ in the plant picture. What is it?
a) the branch  b) the root  c) the fruit
**Answer: a) the branch** — branches grow out of the trunk ግንድ.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How do we care for a flower?
a) we pull it up  b) we water it — አበባ ውሃ ማጠጣት  c) we hide it from the sun all day
**Answer: b) we water it** — water at the bottom, where the root can drink.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tool carries water to the plants?
a) ውሃ ማጠጫ (the watering can)  b) መኮትኮቻ (the hoe)  c) መቆፈሪያ (the digging tool)
**Answer: a) ውሃ ማጠጫ** — the watering can; the hoe and the digging tool work the soil.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A plant grows in this order —
a) big, small, medium  b) medium, big, small  c) small, medium, big
**Answer: c) small, medium, big** — a plant is small first; it cannot be big before it is small.

**7. (Sub lesson 1)** In the first plant picture, what stands tall over the flower beds?
a) a house  b) a big tree  c) a car
**Answer: b) a big tree** — the big tree and the flower beds are the first plant picture of all.

**8. (Sub lesson 1)** The tree picture with no colours in it is for —
a) cutting out  b) colouring — colour the tree  c) counting only
**Answer: b) colouring** — colour the trunk and the leaves, staying in the lines.

**9. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the flower in the colouring picture grow?
a) in a pot  b) in a river  c) on a roof
**Answer: a) in a pot** — colour the potted flower: petals, leaves and pot.
