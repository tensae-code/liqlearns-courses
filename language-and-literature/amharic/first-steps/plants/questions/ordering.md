# Plants — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the plant's growing in order: big plant — small plant — medium plant.
**Answer: small plant → medium plant → big plant.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the parts in order from the bottom of the plant to the top: fruit ፍሬ — root ስር — flower አበባ — trunk ግንድ.
**Answer: root ስር → trunk ግንድ → flower አበባ → fruit ፍሬ.**
