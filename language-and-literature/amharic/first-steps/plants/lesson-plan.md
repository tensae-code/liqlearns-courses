# Lesson plan — Plants

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Plants
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the parts of a plant — root, trunk, branch, leaf, flower, fruit — and how we care for plants and watch them grow from small to big.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own world — the trees, flowers and gardens the child already sees every day. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This is a First Steps lesson — a child can start here. The animals lesson next door helps, because plants and animals are the two living halves of the child's world. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the six parts of a plant: ስር (sir, root), ግንድ (gind, trunk), ቅርንጫፍ (q'irinch'af, branch), ቅጠል (q'itel, leaf), አበባ (abeba, flower), ፍሬ (fire, fruit).
2. Say how we care for plants: water the flower — አበባ ውሃ ማጠጣት.
3. Name the garden tools: መቆፈሪያ, ውሃ ማጠጫ, መቧጠጫ, መኮትኮቻ.
4. Put growth in order: small plant, medium plant, big plant.

## What you need
The pictures in the lesson. A real plant or flower near the home, and water in a cup, for the hands-on work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Points at the trunk for the root → the root ስር hides under the ground; follow the label line down into the soil in Picture 8067.
- Mixes leaf ቅጠል and flower አበባ → the leaf is flat and green and feeds the plant; the flower is the coloured part that comes before the fruit.
- Puts growth in the wrong order → watch GIF 8068 again and stop it: which plant is smallest? A plant cannot be big before it is small.
