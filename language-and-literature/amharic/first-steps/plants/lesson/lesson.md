# Plants

## Do it first

Look out of the door, or think of the nearest tree. A plant has parts, like you have parts. Point in the air: where is the plant's bottom part, the part under the ground? Where is its top part? Now let us name them all.

## Sub lesson 1 — Parts of a plant

[Picture 8067 — parts of a plant]

Follow each line in the picture, from the bottom to the top.

- ስር (sir) — the root. The root hides under the ground. It holds the plant and drinks water for it.
- ግንድ (gind) — the trunk, the plant's strong middle. It stands up and holds everything.
- ቅርንጫፍ (q'irinch'af) — the branch. Branches grow out of the trunk, this way and that way.
- ቅጠል (q'itel) — the leaf. Flat and green, it grows on the branch.
- አበባ (abeba) — the flower. The coloured, pretty part.
- ፍሬ (fire) — the fruit. After the flower comes the fruit — the part we pick and eat.

Say them in order, bottom to top: ስር, ግንድ, ቅርንጫፍ, ቅጠል, አበባ, ፍሬ. Root, trunk, branch, leaf, flower, fruit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Caring for plants and watching them grow

A plant is alive, like an animal. It needs care.

How do we care for plants? We water them: አበባ ውሃ ማጠጣት — watering a flower. We pour water gently at the bottom of the plant, where the root can drink it.

The tools that help us care for the garden:

- መቆፈሪያ — the digging tool, for opening the soil.
- ውሃ ማጠጫ — the watering can, for carrying water to the plants.
- መቧጠጫ — a garden tool for the soil.
- መኮትኮቻ — the hoe, for working the ground.

And a plant does not start big. Watch it grow:

[GIF 8068 — a plant growing]

First a small plant, just out of the soil. Then a medium plant, taller, with leaves. Then a big plant — and a flower opens at the top. Small, medium, big: that is the growth order, always in that order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the six parts of a plant, in order from the root to the fruit. Then tell them: how do we care for a plant, and what are the three sizes of its growing?

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw a plant and draw lines to its parts. Write or say each part's name: ስር, ግንድ, ቅርንጫፍ, ቅጠል, አበባ, ፍሬ.
2. Copy-write the parts of a plant, the book's own writing work: ቅርንጫፍ, ግንድ, ቅጠል, አበባ, ፍሬ, ስር.
3. Water one real plant at home today.
4. Find a small plant, a medium plant and a big plant, and point at them in order.
