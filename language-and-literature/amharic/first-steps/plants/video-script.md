# Video script — Plants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The target
words are in Amharic, with the transliteration the books give.

---

Look at this plant. It has parts!

Under the ground — the root. ስር (sir).
The root drinks water.

Up stands the trunk. ግንድ (gind).
On the trunk — a branch. ቅርንጫፍ (q'irinch'af).
On the branch — a leaf. ቅጠል (q'itel). Green and flat.

Then — a flower! አበባ (abeba). So pretty!
And after the flower — fruit! ፍሬ (fire). We eat the fruit!

We care for plants. We give water. አበባ ውሃ ማጠጣት — watering the flower.
Small plant… medium plant… big plant! It grows!

Now you. Point to a plant. Name its parts.
