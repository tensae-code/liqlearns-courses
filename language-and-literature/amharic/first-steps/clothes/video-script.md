# Video script — Clothes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The target
words are in Amharic, with the transliteration the books give.

---

Clothes! What are you wearing?

ቲሸርት — a T-shirt.
ሸሚዝ — a shirt, with buttons.
ቁምጣ — shorts.
ጉርድ ቀሚስ — a short dress.
ነጠላ ጫማ — sandals on your feet.

Where do clothes come from?
Cotton grows. We spin the thread. We weave the cloth. We sew the clothes!

Hot day? Light clothes!
Cold, rainy day? ሹራብ — a sweater. ጃኬት — a jacket. ቦቲ ጫማ — boots!

Now you. Point at your clothes. Name them!
