# Clothes — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A leather jacket and boots can be made from leather.
**True** — the making-clothes page shows leather, the leather jacket and the boots.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sandals ነጠላ ጫማ are the best shoes for a cold, rainy day.
**False** — sandals are summer clothes; for the cold rain we wear boots ቦቲ ጫማ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** We choose our clothes by the season — light clothes in በጋ, warm clothes in ክረምት.
**True.**
