# Clothes — matching

Match each piece of clothing to its name and its season.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — match the picture to the name.
1. the T-shirt — ቲሸርት
2. the shirt with buttons — ሸሚዝ
3. the sweater — ሹራብ
4. the boots — ቦቲ ጫማ

**Answer key:** T-shirt–ቲሸርት · shirt–ሸሚዝ · sweater–ሹራብ · boots–ቦቲ ጫማ.

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — match the clothing to its season.
1. ቁምጣ (shorts) — የበጋ ልብስ (summer)
2. ጃኬት (jacket) — የክረምት ልብስ (winter)
3. ነጠላ ጫማ (sandals) — የበጋ ልብስ (summer)
4. ሹራብ (sweater) — የክረምት ልብስ (winter)

**Answer key:** 1 summer · 2 winter · 3 summer · 4 winter.
