# Clothes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point to the ሹራብ in the clothes picture. What is it?
a) the sweater  b) the sandals ነጠላ ጫማ  c) the shorts ቁምጣ
**Answer: a) the sweater.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Clothes are made in this order —
a) cloth, cotton, thread, clothes  b) cotton, thread, cloth, clothes  c) clothes, cloth, thread, cotton
**Answer: b) cotton, thread, cloth, clothes** — the cotton is spun into thread, the thread is woven into cloth, the cloth becomes clothes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On a cold, rainy ክረምት day, which clothes keep you warm and dry?
a) ቲሸርት and ቁምጣ  b) ሹራብ, ጃኬት and ቦቲ ጫማ  c) ነጠላ ጫማ only
**Answer: b) ሹራብ, ጃኬት and ቦቲ ጫማ** — the winter clothes, የክረምት ልብስ.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ጉርድ ቀሚስ (the short dress) belongs to —
a) የበጋ ልብስ, the summer clothes  b) የክረምት ልብስ, the winter clothes
**Answer: a) የበጋ ልብስ** — with the T-shirt, the sandals, the shorts and the shirt.
