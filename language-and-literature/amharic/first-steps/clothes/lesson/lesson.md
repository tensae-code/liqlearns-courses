# Clothes

## Do it first

Look down at yourself. Touch each thing you are wearing, one by one, and try to say its name. How many things are you wearing? By the end of this lesson you will name them all.

And where do clothes come from? Making clothes — የልብስ መስሪያ — goes step by step: first the cotton; the cotton is spun into thread; the thread is woven into cloth; the cloth becomes clothes. Leather takes its own road: leather, then a leather jacket, and boots.

## Sub lesson 2 — Clothes for the season

Clothes follow the sky. What we wear in the heat is not what we wear in the cold rain.

Summer clothes — የበጋ ልብስ — for the dry season's heat:

- ቲሸርት — T-shirt
- ጉርድ ቀሚስ — short dress
- ነጠላ ጫማ — sandals
- ቁምጣ — shorts
- ሸሚዝ — shirt

Winter clothes — የክረምት ልብስ — for the rainy season's cold:

- ሹራብ — sweater
- ጃኬት — jacket
- ቦቲ ጫማ — boots

So before you dress, ask the season: is it በጋ, hot and dry, or ክረምት, cold and rainy? The clothes answer the sky.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: everything you are wearing today, by name — and say why: which season are those clothes for? Then your companion names a piece of clothing, and you say its season.

## Do It Yourself

1. Match the clothes to their names, like the book: draw a line from each picture to its word.
2. Trace one piece of clothing on dots and colour it.
3. At home, lay out tomorrow's clothes. Say each name, and say the season they are for.
