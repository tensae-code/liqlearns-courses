# Lesson plan — Clothes

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Clothes
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the clothes the child wears — their names, where clothes come from, and which clothes belong to which season.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own world — the clothes on the child's own body right now. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This is a First Steps lesson — a child can start here. The weather and seasons lesson next door pairs with it, because clothes follow the season. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the clothes: ቲሸርት, ጉርድ ቀሚስ, ነጠላ ጫማ, ቁምጣ, ሸሚዝ, ሹራብ, ጃኬት, ቦቲ ጫማ.
2. Match each piece of clothing to its name.
3. Say where clothes come from: cotton → spun thread → woven cloth → clothes; and leather → jacket and boots.
4. Sort clothes into dry-season clothes የበጋ ልብስ and rainy-season clothes የክረምት ልብስ.

## If the learner is stuck
- Names the clothes but cannot sort by season → go back to the sky: in ክረምት it is cold and rainy, so the body needs covering — sweater ሹራብ, jacket ጃኬት, boots ቦቲ ጫማ. In በጋ it is hot — light clothes.
- Mixes ሸሚዝ (shirt) and ቲሸርት (T-shirt) → both cover the top of the body; the shirt ሸሚዝ in the picture has buttons down the front. Point at the buttons.
- Thinks clothes just come from a shop → walk the making chain slowly: cotton grows, the cotton is spun into thread, the thread is woven into cloth, the cloth is cut and sewn into clothes.
