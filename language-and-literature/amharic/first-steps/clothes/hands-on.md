# Hands-on — Clothes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name what you wear
Stand up and touch each piece of clothing you are wearing today, one by one, and say its name in Amharic. Count them: how many pieces are you wearing?

## Two piles of clothes
With a grown-up, take clothes from home and make two piles: clothes for the hot dry season በጋ, clothes for the cold rainy season ክረምት. Say the season's name as each piece goes down.

## Trace and colour
Draw a sweater, a dress, a shirt and shorts on paper — or trace them on dotted lines like the book — and colour each one. Say its name as you colour.
