# How Families Can Help with Amharic at Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Amharic books are built mainly on —
a) copying long passages  b) learning by doing activities  c) memorising without questions
**Answer: b) learning by doing activities** — the prefaces describe activity-based learning: thinking, questioning, summarising and deciding.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the eleven home activities?
a) doing the child's homework for them  b) reading together, taking turns  c) hiding the textbook
**Answer: b) reading together, taking turns** — reading together in turns is activity 2 in the prefaces' list.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Fluent reading (አቀላጥፎ ማንበብ) means —
a) reading smoothly, without dropping letters  b) reading as fast as possible, skipping hard words  c) reading silently only
**Answer: a) reading smoothly, without dropping letters** — fluency is smooth, complete reading — no letters dropped, no words cut.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The books were prepared under —
a) the 2013 E.C. curriculum change and the Fenote Karta framework  b) a foreign syllabus  c) no framework
**Answer: a) the 2013 E.C. curriculum change and the Fenote Karta framework** — the prefaces name the 2013 E.C. change and Fenote Karta.
