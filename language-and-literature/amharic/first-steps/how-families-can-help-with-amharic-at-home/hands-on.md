# Hands-on — How Families Can Help with Amharic at Home

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Read together tonight
Take turns reading one page aloud — one paragraph each. Then ask the preface's questions: what kind of text is it? What do we learn? What is our opinion?

## Notebook together
Open the child's notebook together. Find one piece of writing. Check its punctuation together and praise one thing done well.
