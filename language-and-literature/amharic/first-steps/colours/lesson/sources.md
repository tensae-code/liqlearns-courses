Placements honesty: the KG3 p.166 placement line writes the third mix as "ሰማዊ + ቀይ = ወይንጠጅ" — ሰማዊ read as ሰማያዊ (semayawi, blue), the base colour the same book names on p.17; the lesson teaches the mix with the book's own base-colour name and flags the shortened form here rather than silently fixing it. The six first colours in Sub lesson 1 are assembled from all three books: red/yellow/blue/black from KG1 pp.25, 103 and KG3 p.17, white and green added from the KG3 mixing page (አረንጓዴ, p.166) and the colour-matching work — no page teaches a six-colour list in one place, and sources.md says so plainly. Traffic-light colouring pages (KG2 p.124, KG3 p.80) are placed under road safety, not here. No book errors were flagged in the placements for these pages beyond the shortened form above.
