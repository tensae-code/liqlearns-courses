# Colours

## Do it first

Look at three things near you: your shirt, a cup, the sky through
the window. For each one, do not say what it IS — say what COLOUR
it is. Colours have names, and this lesson teaches them: first the
colours themselves, then what happens when they mix.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first colours

Here are the first colours, each on a thing that wears it:

[Picture 8126 — the six colour panels: red tomato, yellow rose, blue sky, black, white, green leaf]

- **ቀይ (qey) — red.** The tomato is red.
- **ቢጫ (bicha) — yellow.** The rose is yellow.
- **ሰማያዊ (semayawi) — blue.** The sky is blue.
- **ጥቁር — black.** The dark circle is black.
- **ነጭ — white.** The egg is white.
- **አረንጓዴ — green.** The leaf is green.

Colour a picture with the same colours as the picture shown:
the rose yellow, the sky blue, the tomato red. Then match the
colours — red to red, blue to blue, yellow to yellow, black to
black, circle to circle.

Three of these colours stand at the start of all the others.
They are the base colours (መሰረታዊ ቀለማት): **ቀይ, ቢጫ, ሰማያዊ** —
red, yellow, blue. Colour them and write their names.

Now use the names on the real world. Look at the objects around
you — a plate, a jug, a pillow, a pot, a cup, a bucket — and say
each one's colour. Colour the stars the same colours as the
stars in the picture. And colours live two ways in the world:
some are nature's own colours (ተፈጥሮአዊ ቀለሞች) — the green of a
leaf, the blue of the sky — and some are man-made colours
(ሰው ሰራሽ ቀለሞች) — the colour a person paints a pot or dyes a cloth.

Last question of this part, and it is yours to answer: what is
your favourite colour? Colour a big pencil in it, and say its
name.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mixing colours — the second colours

When the base colours are mixed, they give new colours — the
second colours (ሁለተኛ ደረጃ ቀለማት). There are three mixes to know:

[Picture 8127 — the mixing chart: the three mixes with their names]

- **ቀይ + ቢጫ = ብርቱካናማ** — red + yellow = orange.
- **ቢጫ + ሰማያዊ = አረንጓዴ** — yellow + blue = green.
- **ሰማያዊ + ቀይ = ወይንጠጅ** — blue + red = purple.

Watch the blobs run together — each pair of colours melts into
its child colour:

[GIF 8128 — paint blobs mixing: red+yellow into orange, yellow+blue into green, blue+red into purple]

Say each mix like a little sum: the two parents, then the child.
Notice አረንጓዴ, green: it was already in the first colours on
the leaf — now you know where it comes from. It is a second
colour that nature uses everywhere.

Finish by matching the colours to their names — every colour in
this lesson, first and second together.

## Say it to your companion

Point at five things around you, one by one. Your companion
names each colour in Amharic. Then tell each other: "My favourite
colour is…" — and each of you says one mix and its answer:
"ቢጫ + ሰማያዊ = አረንጓዴ."

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw a tomato, a rose and the sky. Colour each one its own
   colour and write the colour's name under it: ቀይ, ቢጫ, ሰማያዊ.
2. Find one thing at home for each first colour — red, yellow,
   blue, black, white, green — and say its colour name.
3. Mix the three mixes with paints, crayons layered hard, or
   coloured water. Name each child colour: ብርቱካናማ, አረንጓዴ,
   ወይንጠጅ.
4. Look outside: name two of nature's colours and two man-made
   colours you can see.
5. Colour a star picture: colour each star the colour of the
   star beside it, matching exactly.
