# Hands-on — Colours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The colour walk
Walk around your home and street. Find one thing for each first
colour — ቀይ, ቢጫ, ሰማያዊ, ጥቁር, ነጭ, አረንጓዴ — touch it, and say
its colour name out loud.

## Mix it yourself
With paints, crayons pressed hard in layers, or water coloured
with food colouring: mix red and yellow and watch orange come.
Mix yellow and blue, then blue and red. Say each mix as a sum
before you mix: "ቀይ + ቢጫ = ?" — then name the child colour.

## Same colours as the picture
Draw a small picture — a rose, the sky, a tomato. Put it beside
you, draw it again, and colour the copy with exactly the same
colours as the first picture, the books' own matching work.

## Nature or man-made?
Collect five coloured things or point at five coloured things
outside. For each one say: nature's colour, or man-made colour?
The leaf is nature's. The painted pot is man-made.
