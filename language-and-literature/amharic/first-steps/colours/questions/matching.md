# Colours — matching

Match each colour to its name. Say each pair out loud.

1. (Sub lesson 1) the colour of the tomato — a) ቢጫ
2. (Sub lesson 1) the colour of the rose — b) ሰማያዊ
3. (Sub lesson 1) the colour of the sky — c) ቀይ
4. (Sub lesson 1) the colour of the leaf — d) ጥቁር
5. (Sub lesson 1) the dark colour, black — e) አረንጓዴ
6. (Sub lesson 2) the colour red + yellow makes — f) ወይንጠጅ
7. (Sub lesson 2) the colour blue + red makes — g) ብርቱካናማ

**Answer key:** 1–c (ቀይ, red), 2–a (ቢጫ, yellow), 3–b (ሰማያዊ,
blue), 4–e (አረንጓዴ, green), 5–d (ጥቁር, black), 6–g (ብርቱካናማ,
orange), 7–f (ወይንጠጅ, purple).
