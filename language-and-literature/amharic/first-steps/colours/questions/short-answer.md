# Colours — short answer

Answer out loud, in Amharic where you can.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point to something white near you. What is its colour's name?
**Answer: ነጭ** — white, like the egg in the lesson picture.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Is the green of a leaf a natural colour or a man-made colour?
**Answer: A natural colour (ተፈጥሮአዊ)** — nature made it; nobody painted the leaf.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is your favourite colour? Answer in a whole sentence: "My favourite colour is…"
**Answer: the child's own colour, named in Amharic** — the book asks this on both the Level One pencil page and the Level Two base-colours page.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** You have only red and blue paint. Which second colour can you make?
**Answer: ወይንጠጅ — purple** (ሰማያዊ + ቀይ = ወይንጠጅ).
