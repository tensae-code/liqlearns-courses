# Colours — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Blue is one of the base colours.
**Answer: True** — the base colours are ቀይ, ቢጫ, ሰማያዊ (red, yellow, blue).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mixing yellow and blue gives orange.
**Answer: False** — yellow + blue gives green (አረንጓዴ); orange comes from red + yellow.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Green can be a first colour on a leaf and a second colour from a mix.
**Answer: True** — the leaf wears it in the first colours, and ቢጫ + ሰማያዊ = አረንጓዴ makes it again.
