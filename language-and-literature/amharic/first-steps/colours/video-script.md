# Video script — Colours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the tomato. **ቀይ** — red.
Look at the rose. **ቢጫ** — yellow.
Look at the sky. **ሰማያዊ** — blue.
Red, yellow, blue. These are the base colours.

Now mix!
Red and yellow run together… **ብርቱካናማ** — orange!
Yellow and blue run together… **አረንጓዴ** — green!
Blue and red run together… **ወይንጠጅ** — purple!

Now you. Point. Name the colour.
What is YOUR favourite colour?
