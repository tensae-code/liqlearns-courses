# Lesson plan — Colours

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Colours
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the colour names — the first
colours ቀይ, ቢጫ, ሰማያዊ, ጥቁር, ነጭ, አረንጓዴ — and what happens when
the base colours mix: the second colours ብርቱካናማ, አረንጓዴ, ወይንጠጅ.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own
world — a tomato, the sky, a rose, the colours on their own clothes.
It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The first colours** — ቀይ (qey, red), ቢጫ (bicha, yellow), ሰማያዊ (semayawi, blue), ጥቁር (black), ነጭ (white), አረንጓዴ (green): colouring things the colours of the picture (a yellow rose, a blue sky, a red tomato), matching colours to colours, saying the colours of real objects, colouring stars to match, and the colours nature makes against the colours people make. The first three — red, yellow, blue — are the base colours (መሰረታዊ ቀለማት).
2. **Mixing colours — the second colours** — when the base colours mix they give the second colours (ሁለተኛ ደረጃ ቀለማት): ቀይ + ቢጫ = ብርቱካናማ (orange), ቢጫ + ሰማያዊ = አረንጓዴ (green), ሰማያዊ + ቀይ = ወይንጠጅ (purple) — then matching the colours to their names.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing — this is a first lesson; the child
  already lives among colours. Recommended, never forced.
- Shapes and patterns, next door, uses these colour names in its
  pattern rows.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the first colours in Amharic: ቀይ, ቢጫ, ሰማያዊ, ጥቁር, ነጭ,
   አረንጓዴ — and point to each one.
2. Colour a picture with the same colours as the picture shown
   (yellow rose, blue sky, red tomato) and match colour to colour.
3. Look at real objects — a plate, a jug, a cup — and say their
   colours.
4. Say which colours are nature's and which are man-made, and name
   their own favourite colour.
5. Mix the base colours and name what comes out: red + yellow =
   ብርቱካናማ, yellow + blue = አረንጓዴ, blue + red = ወይንጠጅ — and
   match the second colours to their names.

## What you need
Colour pencils or crayons. Real things around the home to name the
colours of. Paints or coloured water for mixing, if you have them.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner names the colours of three things
   in front of them — their shirt, a cup, the sky through the
   window (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the first colours on the books' own
   objects, matching and naming; then mixing, and the second
   colours (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8126]: a picture of six colour panels — a red tomato (ቀይ), a yellow rose (ቢጫ), a blue sky (ሰማያዊ), a black circle (ጥቁር), a white egg (ነጭ), a green leaf (አረንጓዴ) — the student points and names each colour.
   - ADD AUDIO in the first-colours part: a voice reading the colour names — ቀይ … ቢጫ … ሰማያዊ … ጥቁር … ነጭ … አረንጓዴ — the student repeats each name and finds the colour in the room. (Audio slot — described only, made later.)
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8127]: a picture of the mixing chart — red + yellow = orange (ብርቱካናማ), yellow + blue = green (አረንጓዴ), blue + red = purple (ወይንጠጅ) — the student reads each mix and names the new colour.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 8128]: a picture of paint blobs mixing — red and yellow blobs run together into orange, then yellow and blue into green, then blue and red into purple — the student names the new colour as each mix lands.
3. **Say it to your companion** — point at things around you; your
   companion names the colour; then each of you tells the other
   your favourite colour (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the
     colours and the mixes in the super-simple voice (made later,
     when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching,
   short-answer, true-or-false — served at the learner's level and
   teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — colour a page with the picture's own
   colours, and mix two colours and name what comes out
   (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Names the object, not the colour → ask "not what is it — what
  colour is it?" and hold the tomato beside Picture 8126's red panel.
- Mixes up blue and green → green is the leaf's colour and comes
  from a mix (yellow + blue); blue is the sky's own base colour.
- Cannot remember a mix → walk the chart row by row on Picture
  8127, saying the two parents and the child colour aloud.
- This lesson's next door: Shapes and patterns (same unit) — its
  pattern rows run on these colours.
