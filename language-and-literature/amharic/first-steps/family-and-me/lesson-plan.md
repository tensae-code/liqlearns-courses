# Lesson plan — Family and Me

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Family and Me
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** who I am and who my family
is — my name, my family members (ቤተሰብ), and telling others about
myself.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's
own world — the child is the world's first expert on themselves.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **All about me** — my name on the cover page, my school's name;
   write about yourself: name (ስም), age (እድሜ), sex (ፆታ), the fruit
   I like; telling the teacher about myself — name, age, class.
2. **My family** — the family members (የቤተሰብ አባላት): mother (እናት),
   father (አባት), brother (ወንድም), the children — together at the
   family meal; writing my family members' names.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the
gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: My Home — the home this family lives in.
  Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and write their own name, age, and school.
2. Name the family members in Amharic and write their names.
3. Tell a teacher or a new friend about themselves in three lines.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil for the name page. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner says their own name out loud and
   introduces themselves before any writing (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the name page first (me), then the family
   at the meal (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8020]: a picture of an Ethiopian family —
     father, mother and children — at a mesob meal — the student
     names each person in the picture: who is the አባት, who is the
     እናት, who are the children.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the about-me lines slowly — ስም …
     እድሜ … — the student answers each one about themselves.
     Described only in this build; no audio file is made.
3. **Say it to your companion** — introduce yourself and your
   family (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — "this is
     me, this is my family" in the super-simple voice (made later).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   writing — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — your own name page and family list
   (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot yet write their name → trace it first, the books' own way
  (the name page starts every workbook), then copy-write it.
- Mixes the family words → anchor them on the picture: point at
  the father, say አባት; point at the mother, say እናት.
- This lesson's next door: Manners and Living Together — how this
  family lives kindly with everyone else.
