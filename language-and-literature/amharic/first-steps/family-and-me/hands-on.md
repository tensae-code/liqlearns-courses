# Hands-on — Family and Me

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My name page
Make your own cover page, like the workbook's first page: your
school's name at the top, your own name big under it. Keep it at
the front of your papers.

## Family hands
Draw your family: father, mother, the children, and you. Put the
mesob in the middle if your family eats at one. Say each person's
name in Amharic as you draw them.

## Introduce me
Practise with a real person: stand up straight, say your name,
your age, and your class, the way children tell their teacher.
Three lines, no more. Then let the other person do theirs.
