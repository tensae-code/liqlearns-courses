# Family and Me

## Do it first

Say your name out loud. Say it again, proudly. Your name is the
first writing in your book — before every lesson, the book asks:
whose book is this? This lesson is you, and the people you belong
to.

## Sub lesson 1 — All about me

Every workbook opens with a name page: the child writes the name
of their school, and their own name. That page says: this book
belongs to me.

About yourself, you can write four things:

**ስም** (sim) — my name.
**እድሜ** (idime) — my age.
**ፆታ** (ts'ota) — whether I am a boy or a girl.
The fruit I like is ______.

And you can tell your teacher about yourself the same way: my
name, my age, my class. Three true lines, said clearly — that is
introducing yourself.

## Sub lesson 2 — My family

[Picture 8020 — my family at the mesob meal]

Here is a family — የቤተሰብ አባላት, the family members — eating
together at the መሶብ.

The **father** — አባት (abat).
The **mother** — እናት (inat).
A **brother** — ወንድም (wendim).
And the children — of which you are one.

Your family members have their own names, and you can write them:
my mother's name, my father's name, my brother's name. A family
is the people who eat with you, live with you, and call you by
your name.

## Say it to your companion

Introduce yourself and your family: "My name is … I am … years
old. This is my አባት, my father. This is my እናት, my mother."
Your companion listens and says one thing back about their own
family. Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make your name page: your school's name, then your own name,
   big and clear.
2. Write about yourself: ስም, እድሜ, ፆታ, and the fruit you like.
3. Write your family members' names: mother, father, brother —
   and every member of your real family.
4. Practise telling your teacher about yourself: name, age,
   class. Say it three times until it is easy.
