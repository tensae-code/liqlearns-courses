# Family and Me — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What goes on the very first page of your book?
a) a shopping list  b) your name and your school's name  c) a song
**Answer: b) your name and your school's name** — the name page.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** እድሜ means —
a) name  b) age  c) fruit
**Answer: b) age.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** እናት is —
a) father  b) brother  c) mother
**Answer: c) mother.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the meal picture, the family eats together at the —
a) መሶብ  b) bed  c) door
**Answer: a) መሶብ** — the woven basket-table.
