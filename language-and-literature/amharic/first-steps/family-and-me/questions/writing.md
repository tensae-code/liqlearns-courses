# Family and Me — writing

Write, in your best letters.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write your own name.
**Answer: the child's own name, written as on their name page.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Write about yourself: ስም ___, እድሜ ___.
**Answer: the child's own name and age filled in.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write your mother's name and your father's name.
**Answer: the two names of the child's own family members.**
