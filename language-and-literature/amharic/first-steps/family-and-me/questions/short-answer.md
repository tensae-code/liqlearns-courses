# Family and Me — short answer

Answer out loud, in one short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the teacher three things about yourself.
**Answer: my name, my age, and my class** — said clearly, in that order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three family members in Amharic.
**Answer: እናት (mother), አባት (father), ወንድም (brother).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who eats at the mesob with you?
**Answer: my family** — the members of my ቤተሰብ.
