# Video script — Manners and Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An elder is coming. I stand. I greet him. I hold the door.

I did a wrong thing. I say: **I am sorry.**
You did a good thing for me. I say: **thank you.**
I ask for something. I say: **እባክዎ** — please.

We go together. We play together. We help each other.
That is living together.

Now you. Who will you help today?
