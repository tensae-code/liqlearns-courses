# Lesson plan — Manners and Living Together

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Manners and Living Together
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how we live kindly together —
respect and humility (ትህትና), saying sorry, thank you and please
(እባክዎ), helping and respecting each other.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's
own world — the child already lives with family and friends.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short,
single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09). Respect, the kind
words, helping and togetherness are one way of living, practised
in scenes, not a sequence of separate skills.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Feelings and Family and Me — the feelings we
  manage and the family we live with. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Show respect to an elder: greet them, help them, hold a door.
2. Use the three kind sentences: "I am sorry", "thank you",
   እባክዎ — "please".
3. Name the togetherness acts: going together, playing together,
   studying together, helping a friend, eating together; helping
   elders, respecting each other, caring for parents.
4. Say why helping people with disabilities and supporting the
   elderly belong to living together.

## What you need
People to be kind to. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — the learner shows how they greet an elder at

2. **The explanation** — the respect picture scene by scene, the
   manners true/false sayings, the two morals pages

   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: true-or-false, multiple-choice
   — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — one real act of help today
   (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- "Please is wrong to say" traps them → the manners page itself
  marks that saying FALSE: እባክዎ is a good and right word.
- Cannot think of a helping act → start from the picture: hold a
  door, carry a bag, greet first.
- This lesson's next door: School Things — the kind child, ready
  for school.
