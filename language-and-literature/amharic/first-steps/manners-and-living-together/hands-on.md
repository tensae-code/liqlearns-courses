# Hands-on — Manners and Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The door game
Take turns: one person is the elder coming through a door with a
walking stick or a heavy bag; the other greets them and holds the
door. Say one kind word each time. Then swap.

## Three kind sentences hunt
Use each kind sentence once for real today: say sorry when you do
wrong, thank you when good is done for you, and እባክዎ when you ask
for something. Count them on your fingers as you use them.

## Together tasks
Do one thing TOGETHER that is slower alone: carry something with a
friend, clean one corner with a sibling, or study one page with a
partner. Together is the lesson.

## If it is hard
Be a smiling, calm model: talk about your own friends, sharing and
supporting; show the correct way when a child does wrong without
emphasising the mistake; teach the social skills — friendship,
loving, supporting, respecting each other and elders — in small
real acts (SNE toolkit support, printed pp.27–30 and p.27 —
written as help, never as a label on the child).
