# Manners and Living Together — true or false

Say true or false. (The books' own manners exercise.)

**1.** When we do wrong, we must say sorry.
**Answer: True.**

**2.** When good is done for us, we must say thank you.
**Answer: True.**

**3.** "እባክዎ (please) is a wrong thing to say."
**Answer: False** — please is a good, kind word.

**4.** Respecting our elders is part of living together.
**Answer: True.**

**5.** Helping a friend is NOT part of togetherness.
**Answer: False** — helping a friend is one of its pictures.
