# Manners and Living Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An elder comes to the door carrying something heavy. The respectful thing is to —
a) run away  b) greet him and hold the door  c) laugh
**Answer: b) greet him and hold the door** — ትህትና, respect.

**2.** You broke a friend's pencil. You say —
a) nothing  b) "it was not me"  c) "I am sorry"
**Answer: c) "I am sorry."**

**3.** Which of these is a togetherness act?
a) playing alone and hiding the ball  b) studying together  c) pushing in line
**Answer: b) studying together.**

**4.** Supporting the elderly and helping people with disabilities are —
a) not our business  b) part of living together  c) only for grown-ups
**Answer: b) part of living together.**

**5.** ትህትና (tihitina) means —
a) humility and respect  b) running fast  c) eating together
**Answer: a) humility and respect.**
