# Technology

## Do it first

Look at the board. Point to the one you talk into. Point to the one with keys you type on. Point to the big one the whole family watches. Now we learn their names.

## The three technology words

[Picture 8174 — technology]

Three things of our time, to know and to copy-write:

- **ተንቀሳቀሽ ስልክ** — the mobile phone, the phone that moves with you
- **ላፕ ቶፕ** — the laptop
- **ቴሌቪዢን** — the television

Say each name. Point to each thing. Then copy-write each name slowly and carefully.

## Say it to your companion

Point to one thing on the board. Your companion names it. Then swap. Finish by naming all three in order without looking at the words — only at the pictures.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name all three things from the pictures alone.
2. Copy-write: ተንቀሳቀሽ ስልክ.
3. Copy-write: ላፕ ቶፕ — ቴሌቪዢን.
4. At home, point to the real things and say their names to a grown-up.
