# Technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which one do we talk into?
a) the television  b) the mobile phone  c) the laptop
**Answer: b) the mobile phone** — ተንቀሳቀሽ ስልክ.

**2.** ላፕ ቶፕ is the —
a) television  b) phone  c) laptop
**Answer: c) laptop**

**3.** Which one does the whole family watch together?
a) the television  b) the mobile phone  c) the laptop
**Answer: a) the television** — ቴሌቪዢን.
