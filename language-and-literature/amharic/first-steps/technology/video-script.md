# Video script — Technology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three new words.

Mobile phone. ተንቀሳቀሽ ስልክ.
Laptop. ላፕ ቶፕ.
Television. ቴሌቪዢን.

Point. Say it. Write it.
Now you know the three.

## Added 2026-11-12 — the older book's device words
Show each device, say its Amharic name, let the learner repeat and point: ሬዲዮ, ቴሌቪዥን, ስልክ, ባትሪ, ካሜራ.
