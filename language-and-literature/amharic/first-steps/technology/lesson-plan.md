# Lesson plan — Technology

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three technology words to know and copy-write: ተንቀሳቀሽ ስልክ mobile phone, ላፕ ቶፕ laptop, ቴሌቪዢን television.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. These things are in the child's own home.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three: ተንቀሳቀሽ ስልክ (mobile phone), ላፕ ቶፕ (laptop), ቴሌቪዢን (television).
2. Point to each one in the picture and at home.
3. Copy-write each name.

## What you need
The board picture in this lesson. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — the learner points to every one of the three things they have at home — before the names are taught (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the three things and their names, one by one, then copy-writing them (lesson/lesson.md).
