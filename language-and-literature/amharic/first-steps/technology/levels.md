# Technology — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: name the three technology things from the board and copy-write each name. Practice deepens by using more items, mixing the items up, and asking the same thing in a new place — at home, on the street, with real things in the hand.

## The older book's device words
