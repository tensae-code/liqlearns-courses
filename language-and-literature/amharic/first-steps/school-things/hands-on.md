# Hands-on — School Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Out of the bag
Empty your school bag onto the floor or a mat. Pick up one thing
at a time, say its name, and put it in a line. Eight things, eight
names. Then pack them back, naming each one again.

## Sharpen and rub
Sharpen a dull pencil with the sharpener, draw a line with the
ruler, colour it with the colour pencils, then rub out one end
with the eraser. Say the name of each thing as your hand picks it
up.

## Colour the board
Draw the eight school things on paper — your own board, like the
picture — and colour it, the books' own way (their school-things
work is name it, then colour it).
