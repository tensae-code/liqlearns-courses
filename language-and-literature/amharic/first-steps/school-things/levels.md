# School Things — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons, so this file
is its depth record for the whole lesson. Pass mark for the whole
lesson: name all eight school things — pen, school bag, pencil,
eraser, colour pencils, pencil case, ruler, sharpener — from the
picture board and from a real bag. Practice deepens by mixing the
order, by asking for the thing by its job ("show me the thing that
rubs out"), and by colouring and packing routines that use every
name more than once.
