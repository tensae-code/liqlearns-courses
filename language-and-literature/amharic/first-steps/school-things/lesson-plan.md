# Lesson plan — School Things

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** School Things
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the names of the eight
things found at school (ትምህርት ቤት የሚገኙ ቁሳቁሶች).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's
own world — these are the things in the child's own school bag.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short,
single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: any First Steps lesson — the child meets
  these things at school beside all the others. Recommended,
  never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the eight school things: pen, school bag, pencil, eraser,
   colour pencils, pencil case, ruler, sharpener.
2. Point to each one in a picture and in their own bag.
3. Say what each thing is for, in one short line.

## What you need
Your own school things, if you have them. The picture carries the
lesson without them.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner empties (or imagines emptying)
   their school bag and names what comes out (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the eight things one by one on the board
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8022]: a picture board of the eight school
     things — pen, bag, pencil, eraser, colour pencils, pencil
     case, ruler, sharpener — the student names each thing and
     points to the real one in their bag.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice naming the eight things slowly — the
     student repeats and holds each thing up. Described only in
     this build; no audio file is made.
3. **Say it to your companion** — "In my bag I have …"
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching —
   served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — pack your bag by name (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes pencil and pen → a pencil can be rubbed out and sharpened;
  a pen cannot. Hold the two side by side.
- Forgets a name → go back to the bag: take the thing out, hold it,
  say its name, put it back.
- This lesson's next door: the rest of First Steps — the school
  day these things are used in.
