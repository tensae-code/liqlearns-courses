# School Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which thing rubs out a pencil mistake?
a) the ruler  b) the eraser  c) the sharpener
**Answer: b) the eraser.**

**2.** Which thing makes a dull pencil sharp again?
a) the sharpener  b) the pencil case  c) the pen
**Answer: a) the sharpener.**

**3.** The pencils and the pen sleep inside the —
a) school bag's pocket only  b) pencil case  c) ruler
**Answer: b) the pencil case.**

**4.** Which thing makes lines straight?
a) the colour pencils  b) the eraser  c) the ruler
**Answer: c) the ruler.**

**5.** How many school things are on the board?
a) six  b) seven  c) eight
**Answer: c) eight** — pen, bag, pencil, eraser, colour pencils, pencil case, ruler, sharpener.
