# School Things — matching

Match each school thing to its job.

1. pen — d
2. pencil — a
3. eraser — b
4. ruler — e
5. sharpener — c

Jobs: a) writes and draws, and can be rubbed out  b) rubs out mistakes  c) makes a dull pencil sharp  d) writes in ink  e) makes lines straight

**Answer key: 1–d, 2–a, 3–b, 4–e, 5–c.**
