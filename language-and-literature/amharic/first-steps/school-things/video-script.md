# Video script — School Things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open my bag. What is inside?

A **pen**. A **pencil**. An **eraser** — rub, rub, gone!
**Colour pencils** — many colours. A **pencil case** to keep them.
A **ruler** — straight lines. A **sharpener** — pencil sharp again!

Eight things. One bag.
Now you. Open your bag. Name everything.
