# School Things

## Do it first

Tip out your school bag — in real life, or in your mind. Things
fall out: things for writing, things for drawing, things for
fixing mistakes. Every one of them has a name. This lesson names
the eight things found at school — ትምህርት ቤት የሚገኙ ቁሳቁሶች.

## The eight school things

[Picture 8022 — the eight school things]

A **pen** — for writing in ink.
A **school bag** — everything travels in it.
A **pencil** — for writing and drawing; it can be sharpened and
rubbed out.
An **eraser** — it rubs out pencil mistakes.
**Colour pencils** — pencils in many colours, for colouring.
A **pencil case** — the pencils and pen sleep inside it.
A **ruler** — it makes lines straight.
A **sharpener** — it makes a dull pencil sharp again.

Eight things. Point to each one on the board and say its name.
Then find it in your own bag and hold it up.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: "In my bag I have a pencil, an eraser, a
ruler …" — name everything in your bag. Your companion counts:
did you reach eight? Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw the eight school things on paper and colour the picture.
2. Pack your bag for tomorrow, saying each thing's name as it
   goes in.
3. Ask a friend: "show me the thing that rubs out." Trade five
   riddles like this, naming things by their jobs.
