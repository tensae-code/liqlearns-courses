# Lesson plan — My Home

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** My Home
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the rooms of my home and the
things in it, by their Amharic names (ቤት / ቤተሰብ strand — the home).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's
own world — the child lives in these rooms every day.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The rooms of my home** — guest room (የእንግዳ መቀበያ ቤት / እንግዳ
   መቀበያ), bedroom (መኝታ ቤት), toilet/bathroom (መፀዳጃ ቤት), store
   room (እቃ ቤት) — and the house itself.
2. **The things in my home** — cup, plate with fork/knife/spoon,
   table, sofa, wardrobe, and the eight copy-written names: ጀበና
   (jebena), ኩባያ (kubaya), ጭልፋ (ch'ilfa), ቢለዋ (bilewa), ሰሀን
   (sahan), ድስት (dist), ሲኒ (sini), መሶብ (mesob) — plus helping at
   home: cleaning the house, washing things, cooking food.
3. **Colour it, draw it, write its name** — colour a home picture
   and write its name; colour the ሲኒ, draw it again yourself,
   colour the drawing, and write ሲኒ under it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the
gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cleanliness — the clean hands that help at
  home. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four rooms and say what each room is for.
2. Name the household things, including the eight Amharic names.
3. Match a thing to the room where it is found.
4. Name one job of helping at home.

## What you need
Your own home. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner walks (or imagines walking) from
   room to room at home, naming each one (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the four rooms on one board, then the
   things board name by name, then helping at home
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8018]: a picture board of the four rooms —
     guest room, bedroom, bathroom, store room — the student names
     each room and says what happens in it.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8019]: a picture board of eight household
     things — jebena, cup, ladle, knife, plate, pot, sini, mesob —
     the student says each thing's Amharic name.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice naming the rooms and the eight things
     slowly — the student repeats and points at the real thing at
     home. Described only in this build; no audio file is made.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 21010]: a picture of a ሲኒ (sini cup),
     coloured, beside the same ሲኒ in outline — the student
     colours the cup, draws it again, colours the drawing and
     writes ሲኒ under it.
3. **Say it to your companion** — "In my home, the … is in the …"
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the walk
     through the home in the super-simple voice (made later).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching,
   writing — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — draw your own house and name its rooms
   (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the rooms → go room by room at home for real: stand in the
  room, say its name, say what you do there.
- The eight names blur → take two at a time (ጀበና and ሲኒ belong to
  coffee; ድስት and ጭልፋ belong to cooking).
- This lesson's next door: Family and Me — the people who live in
  this home.
