# My Home

## Do it first

Think of your home. Walk through it in your mind: the door, the
room where guests sit, the room where you sleep. How many rooms
does your home have? This lesson names them all — and the things
that live in them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rooms of my home

[Picture 8018 — the four rooms of my home]

A home (ቤት) has rooms, and each room has its work.

The **guest room** — የእንግዳ መቀበያ ቤት — is where guests are received
and sit. There is a sofa and chairs, and coffee is brought here.

The **bedroom** — መኝታ ቤት — is where we sleep. The bed is here.

The **toilet and bathroom** — መፀዳጃ ቤት — is where we wash and use
the toilet.

The **store room** — እቃ ቤት — is where things are kept: food, sacks
and boxes, safe until they are needed.

And the house itself stands with its tree and its path — draw it,
and you have drawn your home.

## Sub lesson 2 — The things in my home

[Picture 8019 — the things in my home]

Every home is full of things, and the things have names. A cup and
a plate, with a fork, a knife and a spoon. A table and a sofa. A
wardrobe where clothes are kept.

Eight names to say and to write:

**ጀበና** (jebena) — the coffee pot.
**ኩባያ** (kubaya) — a cup.
**ጭልፋ** (ch'ilfa) — a ladle.
**ቢለዋ** (bilewa) — a knife.
**ሰሀን** (sahan) — a plate.
**ድስት** (dist) — a cooking pot.
**ሲኒ** (sini) — a small cup, for coffee.
**መሶብ** (mesob) — the woven basket-table the family eats from.

Things belong in rooms: the bed sleeps in the bedroom, the መሶብ
stands where the family eats, the ድስት cooks where food is made.

And a home is kept by helping: cleaning the house, washing
things, cooking food. Your hands can do one of these jobs today.

## Sub lesson 3 — Colour it, draw it, write its name

Now the home things get drawing work of their own. Take a
picture of a thing in your home: colour the picture, and write
the thing's name under it.

The ሲኒ gets the full work. First, colour the ሲኒ — the little
coffee cup on its saucer.

[Picture 21010 — a ሲኒ, coloured, and the same ሲኒ in outline to draw again and colour]

Then draw the ሲኒ again, yourself, on your own paper — the round
cup, the coffee inside, the saucer under it — and colour your
drawing. Under it, write its name: ሲኒ.

Colour it. Draw it again. Write its name. A thing you have drawn
and named is a thing you know.

Tell your companion about your home: "My home has a መኝታ ቤት. The
መሶብ is in our home. I help by …" — name one room, one thing, and
one job. Your companion does the same for their home.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw your house with its tree and its path, like the picture
   books do.
2. Write the eight names — ጀበና, ኩባያ, ጭልፋ, ቢለዋ, ሰሀን, ድስት, ሲኒ, መሶብ —
   saying each one as you write it.
3. Find each of the eight things in your real home. Touch it and
   say its name.
4. Do one helping job at home today: clean one room, wash one
   thing, or help with the cooking.
