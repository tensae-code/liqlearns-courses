# Hands-on — My Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The home walk
Walk through your home with a friend. In each room, stop, say the
room's name in Amharic, and name one thing in it. Four rooms if
your home has them — name the rooms your home really has.

## Coffee things
With a grown-up's permission, bring the coffee things together:
the ጀበና, the ሲኒ cups, the መሶብ. Set them out, name each one as you
lay it down, then put each one back where it lives.

## Draw my house
Draw your house: the outside with a tree and a path, then the
inside — its rooms. Say the rooms' names as you draw them, like
the book's draw-a-house page.

## Helping hands
Choose one helping job today — sweeping one room, washing one cup,
carrying one thing to the store room. Say the job's name when it
is done: "I cleaned the house" / "I washed the things."
