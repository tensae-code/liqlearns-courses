# Video script — My Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Come in. This is my home — ቤት.

This is the **guest room** — የእንግዳ መቀበያ ቤት. Guests sit here.
This is the **bedroom** — መኝታ ቤት. We sleep here.
This is the **bathroom** — መፀዳጃ ቤት. We wash here.
This is the **store room** — እቃ ቤት. Things are kept here.

Now the things. **ጀበና** — the coffee pot. **ሲኒ** — the little cup.
**ድስት** — the pot for cooking. **መሶብ** — the mesob, our table.

Now you. Walk with me. Name your rooms.
