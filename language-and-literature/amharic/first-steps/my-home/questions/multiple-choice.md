# My Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which room do we sleep in?
a) the store room  b) the bedroom (መኝታ ቤት)  c) the guest room
**Answer: b) the bedroom.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The እቃ ቤት is —
a) where guests sit  b) where we wash  c) the store room, where things are kept
**Answer: c) the store room.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Guests are received in the —
a) guest room  b) toilet  c) store room
**Answer: a) the guest room.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ጀበና (jebena) is —
a) a plate  b) the coffee pot  c) a knife
**Answer: b) the coffee pot.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The woven basket-table the family eats from is the —
a) መሶብ (mesob)  b) ሲኒ (sini)  c) ድስት (dist)
**Answer: a) መሶብ.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a helping-at-home job?
a) breaking things  b) hiding the broom  c) cleaning the house
**Answer: c) cleaning the house.**
