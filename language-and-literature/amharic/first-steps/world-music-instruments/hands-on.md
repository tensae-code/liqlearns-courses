# Hands-on — Music Instruments of the World

The lesson with your hands and your ears, not only your eyes.

## Make a drum
Stretch a cloth or a plastic sheet tight over an empty tin or a
pot, tie it firm, and tap it with your fingers, then with two
sticks. That is the drum's way: a tight skin, struck. Say its
name as you play.

## The five mimes
With a partner: mime one instrument — pluck the air guitar, tap
the drum, press the piano keys, blow the saxophone, bow the
violin. Your partner names the instrument. Five mimes, five
names, then swap.

## Listen and point
When music plays at home, at church or in the street, listen
hard. Point in the air at each instrument you hear, and whisper
its name. The ear learns the instruments one sound at a time.
