# Video script — Music Instruments of the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Music! የሙዚቃ መሳሪያዎች — the tools of music.

This is the **guitar**. Pluck the strings. It sings.

This is the **drum**. Strike the skin. Boom, boom — the beat!

This is the **piano**. Press the keys. White, black, white.

This is the **saxophone**. Blow, and press. Out comes the sound.

This is the **violin**. The bow slides. High and sweet.

Now you. Point. Name it. How does it sing?
