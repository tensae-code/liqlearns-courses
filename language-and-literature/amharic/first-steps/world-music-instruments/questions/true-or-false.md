# Music Instruments of the World — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The drum keeps the beat for everyone.
**Answer: True** — hands or sticks strike its tight skin.

**2.** The saxophone is played with a bow.
**Answer: False** — the saxophone is blown; the violin is the one played with a bow.

**3.** The piano has black and white keys.
**Answer: True** — fingers press the keys, and each key makes its note.
