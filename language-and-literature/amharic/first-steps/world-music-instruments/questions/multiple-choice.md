# Music Instruments of the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which instrument has a tight skin that hands or sticks strike?
a) the piano  b) the drum  c) the violin
**Answer: b) the drum** — the drum keeps the beat: boom, boom.

**2.** Which instrument's black and white keys do the fingers press?
a) the piano  b) the guitar  c) the saxophone
**Answer: a) the piano** — each key makes its note.

**3.** The golden, curving horn the player blows into is the —
a) violin  b) drum  c) saxophone
**Answer: c) saxophone** — blow in the top, and the sound pours out of the wide bell.

**4.** The small wooden instrument held under the chin, played with a bow, is the —
a) violin  b) guitar  c) piano
**Answer: a) violin** — the bow slides across its strings.

**5.** How does the guitar make its sound?
a) its skin is struck  b) its strings are plucked and pressed  c) air is blown into it
**Answer: b) its strings are plucked and pressed** — the fingers work the strings along its long neck.

**6.** የሙዚቃ መሳሪያዎች means —
a) school things  b) farm tools  c) music instruments
**Answer: c) music instruments** — the name of them all.
