# Music Instruments of the World — matching

Match each instrument to how its sound is made.

1. guitar — c
2. drum — a
3. piano — e
4. saxophone — b
5. violin — d

Ways of making sound: a) skin struck with hands or sticks
b) air blown in, keys pressed, sound out of a wide bell
c) strings plucked and pressed
d) strings bowed under the chin
e) black and white keys pressed

**Answer key: 1–c, 2–a, 3–e, 4–b, 5–d.**
