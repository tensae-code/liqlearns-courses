# Music Instruments of the World

## Do it first

Clap your hands. Tap the table like a drum. Hum a tune. You just
made music. Music is made with hands and voices — and with
instruments, የሙዚቃ መሳሪያዎች — the tools of music. Today we meet
the instruments the whole world plays.

## The big picture of instruments

Open the big picture. Here are the instruments together:

[Picture 21007 — the instruments of the world: guitar, drum, piano, saxophone, violin]

**The guitar.** It has a round, hollow body and a long neck, with
strings stretched along it. The fingers pluck and press the
strings, and it sings.

**The drum.** A hollow body with a tight skin stretched over the
top. Hands or sticks strike the skin — boom, boom — and the drum
keeps the beat for everyone.

**The piano.** A big box of black and white keys. Fingers press
the keys — white, black, white — and each key makes its note.

**The saxophone.** A golden, curving horn. The player blows into
its top, fingers press its keys, and the sound pours out of its
wide bell.

**The violin.** A small wooden body held under the chin. A bow —
a stick with hair — slides across its strings, and it sings high
and sweet.

Five instruments, five ways of making sound: strings plucked,
skin struck, keys pressed, air blown, strings bowed. And there
are more in the world than these five — the big picture is the
start, not the end.

These are the instruments of the world. Ethiopia has its own
instruments too — the masenqo, the washint, the krar and the
kebero — and they have their own lesson, next door.

## Say it to your companion

Name an instrument: "The drum!" Your companion says how its
sound is made: "Hands strike the skin!" Then swap. Name them
all: guitar, drum, piano, saxophone, violin.

## Do It Yourself

1. Point at each instrument in the big picture and say its name.
2. Make each instrument's sound with your mouth and hands: pluck
   the air guitar, tap the drum, press the piano keys in the air,
   blow the saxophone, bow the violin.
3. Draw one instrument — the one you would most like to play —
   and say its name under your drawing.
4. Listen for music today. Which instrument do you hear first?
