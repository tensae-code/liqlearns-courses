Honesty notes. The placement names the instruments in English (guitars, drums, piano, saxophone, violin, with an ellipsis for more) and gives the Amharic heading የሙዚቃ መሳሪያዎች; the lesson teaches the heading in Amharic and the five named instruments by the placement's own names, and says plainly that the world holds more instruments than the five shown. Picture 21007 is a drawn board of the five named instruments (guitar, drum, piano keys, saxophone, violin), made in this build and looked at before filing; the book's own page is a photograph, and the drawn board stands in its place until a photograph is filed. Ethiopia's own traditional instruments (masenqo, washint, krar, kebero, sistrum) are NOT taught here — they are the ethiopian-culture lesson's content (KG1 printed p.61 and KG3 printed pp.51–52), and this lesson says so.
