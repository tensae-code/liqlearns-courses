# Music Instruments of the World — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short,
single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09). Pass mark for the
whole lesson: name the five instruments in the big picture —
guitar, drum, piano, saxophone, violin — and say how each one
makes its sound. Practice deepens by speed and by ear: first
point-and-name on the picture, then name from the mimed action
(pluck, strike, press, blow, bow), then name from the described
audio sound alone.
