# Lesson plan — Music Instruments of the World

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Music Instruments of the World
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the world's music instruments (የሙዚቃ መሳሪያዎች) by sight and by how each one makes its sound — guitar, drum, piano, saxophone, violin.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: songs they have heard, drums they have
seen, music at home and in the street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. Recommended, never forced.
- Next door: Ethiopian Culture — Ethiopia's own instruments (masenqo, washint, krar, kebero) live there.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five instruments in the big picture: guitar, drum, piano, saxophone, violin.
2. Say how each one makes its sound: strings plucked, skin struck, keys pressed, air blown, strings bowed.
3. Say የሙዚቃ መሳሪያዎች — music instruments — and know it is the name of them all.

## What you need
The big picture in the lesson. Ears. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes saxophone and violin → the saxophone is blown (golden horn, wide bell); the violin is bowed (small wooden body under the chin).
- Names them but cannot say how the sound is made → mime it: pluck, strike, press, blow, bow — the hand remembers the way before the mouth does.
