# Hands-on — Adding and Taking Away

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two piles become one
Make a pile of 3 stones and a pile of 2 stones. Say the sum,
push the piles together, and count the one big pile: 3 + 2 = 5.
Do it again with other counts — bottle tops, sticks, spoons.

## The disappearing stones
Start with 7 stones. A partner closes their eyes; take some
away. The partner counts what is left and says the whole
taking-away: "7 − 3 = 4." Swap turns.

## Tally sticks
Use small sticks or drawn lines as tallies. Lay 4 tallies, add 5
tallies, count the row: 4 + 5 = 9. Then cross tallies out to
take away: 9 − 5 = 4.

## Which hand is greater?
Hold stones in both hands, hidden. Open both hands, count each
side, and say the comparing sentence with its sign: "6 > 4 —
ስድስት ይበልጣል." Play until an equal pair comes — እኩል ይሆናል.
