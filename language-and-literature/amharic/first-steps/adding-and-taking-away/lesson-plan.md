# Lesson plan — Adding and Taking Away

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Adding and Taking Away
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first meeting with
adding (መደመር, +), taking away (መቀነስ, −), and the comparing signs
(> < =) — all by counting real pictures and things.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Counting (this unit) —
the learner can count sets to 20 and say the whole count. It needs
nothing else. **This is the pre-primary first meeting only: the
Maths course owns the full version of adding, taking away and
comparing. Nothing here replaces it.**

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Adding — putting together** — መደመር means adding to what we have; the + sign; picture additions counted out (1+1=2, 3+2, 5+5…), tallies included. The book introduces addition first, and this lesson keeps its order.
2. **Taking away** — መቀነስ means taking away from what we have; the − sign (የመቀነስ ምልክት); the picture example 3−1=2, then sums and tally-mark taking away.
3. **Comparing — bigger, smaller, equal** — the comparing signs (የማወዳደሪያ ምልክቶች): ይበልጣል (is greater) >, ያንሳል (is less) <, እኩል ይሆናል (is equal) =, put between two counted picture sets. In the book, comparing signs come right after subtraction — the lesson keeps that order.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Counting (this unit) — every sum here is

- The full adding, subtracting and comparing lessons live in the

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what መደመር means — adding to what we have — and use the
   + sign in a picture sum (3 + 2 = 5).
2. Answer small additions by counting the pictures: the 3-family
   (3+1 … 3+6), doubles and tens (5+5, 6+4, 3+7), and tally
   additions.
3. Say what መቀነስ means — taking away from what we have — and use
   the − sign (3 − 1 = 2).
4. Answer small subtractions, tallies included (5 − 2 = 3,
   9 − 5 = 4).
5. Put the right comparing sign between two counts: > when the
   first is greater (ይበልጣል), < when it is less (ያንሳል), = when
   they are equal (እኩል ይሆናል).

## What you need
Stones, sticks or bottle tops to add and take away. Paper and a
pencil. Tally sticks or drawn tally lines.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner puts 3 stones in a pile, adds 2
   more, and counts the whole pile; then takes 2 away and counts
   what is left (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — adding first, then taking away, then the
   comparing signs, each by counting pictures (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8123]: a picture of 3 stones and 2 stones joined, with 3 + 2 = 5 — the student counts the stones, then reads the sum.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8124]: a picture of 5 stones with 2 crossed out and leaving, with 5 − 2 = 3 — the student counts what is left.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8125]: a picture of a balance three ways — 5 stars against 3 (5 > 3), 2 against 4 (2 < 4), 3 against 3 (3 = 3) — the student says each sign's name: ይበልጣል, ያንሳል, እኩል ይሆናል.
   - ADD AUDIO in the adding part: a voice reading sums aloud slowly — "3 + 2 = 5", counting each side first — the student counts along on their fingers. (Audio slot — described only, made later.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — make a sum with stones for your
   companion to answer, then a taking-away, then hold two handfuls
   and say which is greater (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — stones
     joining and leaving in the super-simple voice (made later,
     when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false — served at the learner's level
   and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — three additions, three taking-aways and
   three comparings with things at home (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Counts the two piles but not together → push the piles into one
  pile first, then count the whole thing; adding IS the pushing
  together.
- Takes away and counts the crossed ones → count only what is
  left standing; the crossed ones have gone.
- Mixes > and < → the sign's open mouth faces the bigger count:
  5 > 3 opens to the 5.
- This lesson's next door: the Maths course's own adding and
  taking away lessons — the full version lives there.
