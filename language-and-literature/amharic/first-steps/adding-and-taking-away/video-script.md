# Video script — Adding and Taking Away

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three stones. Count: one, two, three.
Two more stones come. **መደመር** — we add.
Now count them all: five! **3 + 2 = 5.**

Five stones. Take two away. **መቀነስ** — we take away.
Count what is left: three. **5 − 2 = 3.**

Two piles now. This pile has five. That pile has three.
Five **ይበልጣል** — is greater. **5 > 3.**
Three **ያንሳል** — is less. **3 < 5.**
Three and three? **እኩል ይሆናል** — equal. **3 = 3.**

Now you. Add with stones. Take away with stones.
Say the whole sum, every time.
