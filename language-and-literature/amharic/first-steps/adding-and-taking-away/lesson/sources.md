This lesson is the pre-primary first meeting only, exactly as the placements direct: addition is a NEW LESSON at this level and the Maths course owns the full version. The lesson keeps the book's own order, which the placements flag as deliberate: addition is introduced early (printed p.30, on the same page as number sequences), subtraction much later (p.110), and the comparing signs come right after subtraction (p.111). The number-side comparing pages (biggest/smallest number and number-before work, printed pp.92–94, and the size-ordering and sideways-reading pages pp.160–162) are taught inside Sub lesson 3 as the comparing eye at work, as the build brief assigns them to this lesson; the comparing WORDS themselves (ትልቅ/ትንሽ and family) are taught in the Size and Quantity lesson and are only used here. No book errors were flagged in the placements for these pages, and no reading on them is marked uncertain.
