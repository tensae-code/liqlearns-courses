# Adding and Taking Away — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** መደመር (medemer) means —
a) taking away from what we have  b) adding to what we have  c) comparing two counts
**Answer: b) adding to what we have.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 3 + 2 = _
a) 4  b) 5  c) 6
**Answer: b) 5** — count the two sets together: 3 stones and 2 more make 5.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sum makes 10?
a) 5 + 5  b) 3 + 5  c) 6 + 2
**Answer: a) 5 + 5** — the book's make-ten sums are 5+5, 6+4 and 3+7.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** መቀነስ (meqenes) means —
a) adding to what we have  b) writing numbers as words  c) taking away from what we have
**Answer: c) taking away from what we have.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 5 − 2 = _
a) 2  b) 3  c) 7
**Answer: b) 3** — count what is left, not what went away.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sign goes between 5 and 3? 5 _ 3
a) >  b) <  c) =
**Answer: a) >** — 5 ይበልጣል, is greater than, 3; the open mouth faces the bigger count.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** እኩል ይሆናል (ikul yihonal) means —
a) is greater  b) is less  c) is equal
**Answer: c) is equal** — its sign is =.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In the row 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, the BIGGEST number is —
a) 16  b) 19  c) 20
**Answer: c) 20** — the book's own row (printed p.92).
