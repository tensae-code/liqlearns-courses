# Adding and Taking Away — fill in the blank

Fill the missing sign or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1 _ 1 = 2 — which sign makes this an adding sum?
**Answer: +** — the adding sign; 1 + 1 = 2 is the book's first sum.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 3 _ 1 = 2 — which sign makes this a taking-away sum?
**Answer: −** — the taking-away sign (የመቀነስ ምልክት); 3 − 1 = 2 is the book's first example.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** 2 _ 4 — ያንሳል (is less) — which sign?
**Answer: <** — 2 < 4, and the open mouth faces the 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** 6 + 4 = _
**Answer: 10** — one of the book's make-ten sums.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the number that comes BEFORE: _ 13
**Answer: 12** — from the book's before-line: _11 _13 _15 _17 _19 (printed p.94).
