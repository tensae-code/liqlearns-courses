# Adding and Taking Away — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When you add, the count grows.
**Answer: True** — መደመር adds to what we have.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In 5 − 2, you count the 2 that went away to get the answer.
**Answer: False** — you count what is left: 3.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In 4 > 6, the sign is right.
**Answer: False** — 4 is less than 6, so the sign is <: 4 < 6.
