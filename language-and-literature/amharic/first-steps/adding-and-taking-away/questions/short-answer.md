# Adding and Taking Away — short answer

Answer by counting the pictures in your head, or with real stones. Say the whole sum.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 3 + 4 = _
**Answer: 7** — from the book's 3-family (3+1 … 3+6).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 8 + 1 = _ (count the tallies: eight lines and one more)
**Answer: 9.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 7 − 6 = _
**Answer: 1** — take the smaller from the bigger and count what is left.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 9 − 5 = _
**Answer: 4.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 4 − 4 = _
**Answer: 0 — ዜሮ.** Everything taken away leaves the empty count.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A set has 6 stones and another has 6 stones. Which sign goes between them, and what does it say in Amharic?
**Answer: = — እኩል ይሆናል, is equal.**
