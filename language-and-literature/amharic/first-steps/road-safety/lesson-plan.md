# Lesson plan — Road Safety

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Road Safety
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the traffic light's three colours — red ቁም stop, yellow ተዘጋጅ get ready, green ሂድ go — and how to cross the road safely at the zebra crossing.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **The traffic light** — red means stop, yellow means get ready, green means go — in that order, every time.
2. **Crossing the road** — cross at the zebra crossing, on green, walking on the footpath and looking left and right.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Colours — the light is read by its colours. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what each light means: ቀይ (red) = ቁም stop; ቢጫ (yellow) = ተዘጋጅ get ready; አረንጓዴ (green) = ሂድ go.
2. Cross only at the zebra crossing, and only on green.
3. Walk on the footpath, never play on the road.
4. Look left and right before and while crossing a car road.

## What you need
Nothing but the pictures and GIF in this lesson — and a real street to watch, with a grown-up.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes yellow and green → yellow is the middle light and it never means go; it means get ready. Only the bottom light, green, means go.
- Wants to run across → we walk across, looking left and right the whole way. Running on the road is never safe.
