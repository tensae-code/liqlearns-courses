# Hands-on — Road Safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The crossing game
Draw a zebra crossing with chalk or sticks. One person is the light and calls ቁም / ተዘጋጅ / ሂድ (or holds up red, yellow, green paper). The others wait, get ready, and cross — walking, looking left and right.

## Watch a real road
With a grown-up, stand on the footpath and watch a crossing. Say what the light tells the cars and the people. Never step onto the road for this game.

## Footpath walk
Walk a short way staying only on the footpath. If there is no footpath, walk at the very edge, facing the cars, holding a grown-up's hand.
