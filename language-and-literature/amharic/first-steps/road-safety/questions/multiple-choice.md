# Road Safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The light is red. What do we do?
a) go  b) stop  c) run
**Answer: b) stop** — ቁም — red means stop.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The yellow light means —
a) go fast  b) get ready  c) sleep
**Answer: b) get ready** — ተዘጋጅ.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which colour means ሂድ — go?
a) red  b) yellow  c) green
**Answer: c) green** — አረንጓዴ.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do we cross the road?
a) anywhere  b) at the zebra crossing  c) behind a car
**Answer: b) at the zebra crossing** — the white lines.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Before crossing, we look —
a) up and down  b) left and right  c) only ahead
**Answer: b) left and right**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** On the road we —
a) play  b) run across  c) walk on the footpath
**Answer: c) walk on the footpath**
