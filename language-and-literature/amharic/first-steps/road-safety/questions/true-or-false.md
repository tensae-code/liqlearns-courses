# Road Safety — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yellow means go.
**Answer: false** — yellow means get ready.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Green means go.
**Answer: true**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** It is fine to play on the road.
**Answer: false** — never play on the road.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** We walk across the road; we do not run.
**Answer: true**
