# Road Safety

## Do it first

Watch the light in this lesson. When it is red, freeze. When it is yellow, stand ready. When it is green — walk. You already know the game. Now we learn it properly, because on a real road it keeps you safe.

## Sub lesson 1 — The traffic light

The traffic light has three colours, and each colour is an order:

[Picture 8165 — the traffic light]

- **Red — ቀይ — means ቁም (qum): STOP.**
- **Yellow — ቢጫ — means ተዘጋጅ (tezagaj): GET READY.**
- **Green — አረንጓዴ — means ሂድ (hid): GO.**

Always in that order: red, yellow, green. Stop, get ready, go.

## Sub lesson 2 — Crossing the road

[GIF 8166 — the light changes, the child crosses on green]

Cross the road at the **zebra crossing** — the white lines on the road. Wait on the footpath until the light is green. Look left and right. Then walk across — do not run — looking left and right as you go.

And every day, on every road:

- Walk only on the footpath.
- Do not play on the road.
- Look left and right when you cross a car road.

## Say it to your companion

Name a colour — red, yellow, or green. Your companion gives its order: stop, get ready, or go. Then swap. Finish by saying the whole crossing in order: wait, look left, look right, cross on green.

## Do It Yourself

1. Say the three colours and their orders, in order, three times.
2. Colour a traffic light: red on top, yellow in the middle, green at the bottom.
3. On your next walk with a grown-up, be the one who says when it is safe to cross — and say why.
