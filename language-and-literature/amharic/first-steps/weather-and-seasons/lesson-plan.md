# Lesson plan — Weather and Seasons

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Weather and Seasons
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the words for the weather in the sky today — sunny, rainy, cloudy — and Ethiopia's four seasons: በጋ, ክረምት, ፀደይ, በልግ.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own world — the sky the child can look at right now, and the seasons the child has already lived through. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say today's weather: ፀሐያማ (sunny), ዝናባማ (rainy) or ደመናማ (cloudy).
2. Name the four seasons: በጋ, ክረምት, ፀደይ, በልግ.
3. Say what በጋ brings (sun and heat) and what ክረምት brings (rain and cold).

## If the learner is stuck
- Says ዝናባማ (rainy) on a day with clouds but no rain → clouds alone are ደመናማ (cloudy); rainy is only when the rain itself falls. Look together: is rain falling?
- Mixes up በጋ and ክረምት → በጋ is the dry season — sun and heat; ክረምት is the rainy season — rain and cold. The two are opposites; say them as a pair.
- Thinks the weather and the season are the same → the weather is today's sky; a season is many days together. Today can be cloudy inside the dry season.
