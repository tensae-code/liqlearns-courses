# Weather and Seasons

## Do it first

Go to the door or the window and look up. What is the sky doing right now? Is the sun out? Is rain falling? Are there only clouds? Hold your answer in your head — now let us learn the words for it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The weather today

The weather — የአየር ፀባይ — is what the sky is doing today. There are three weather words to learn:

[Picture 8072 — sunny, rainy, cloudy]

- ፀሐያማ (tsehayama) — sunny. The sun is out and the sky is bright.
- ዝናባማ (zinabama) — rainy. Rain is falling from the clouds.
- ደመናማ (demenama) — cloudy. Clouds cover the sky, but no rain is falling.

Look at the picture: it is the same hill and the same tree three times. Only the sky changes. That is the whole lesson of this part — the land stays, the sky changes, and each sky has its word.

So: what is the weather today, where you are?

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's seasons

A season — ወቅት — is not one day. A season is many days together, all with the same kind of sky. Ethiopia has four seasons. Read them and copy them:

- በጋ (bega) — the dry season: sun and heat.
- ክረምት (kiremt) — the rainy season: rain and cold.
- ፀደይ (tsedey) — spring.
- በልግ (belg) — the belg season.

Two of them are opposites, and the book teaches them as a pair: በጋ brings the sun and the heat, and ክረምት brings the rain and the cold. When ክረምት comes, we colour the rainy-season picture: grey clouds over the hills and rain falling on the land.

The weather is today's sky. The season is the long stretch of days. A day inside ክረምት is usually ዝናባማ — rainy. A day inside በጋ is usually ፀሐያማ — sunny. But a single day can always surprise you.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: today's weather, the season we are in now, and what that season usually brings. Then ask your companion: what is the weather at your home today?

## Do It Yourself

1. Read and copy-write the four seasons: ክረምት, በጋ, ፀደይ, በልግ.
2. Colour the rainy-season picture: clouds, a hill, and rain.
3. Look at the sky every day this week and say the weather word each morning: ፀሐያማ, ዝናባማ or ደመናማ.
