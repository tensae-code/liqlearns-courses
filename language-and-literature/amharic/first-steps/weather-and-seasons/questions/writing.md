# Weather and Seasons — writing

Copy-write, the book's own writing work.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Copy the four seasons: ክረምት, በጋ, ፀደይ, በልግ.
**Done when:** each word is copied whole, letter by letter.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Write today's weather word: ፀሐያማ, ዝናባማ or ደመናማ — the true one for today.
**Done when:** the word written is the weather that is really in the sky today.
