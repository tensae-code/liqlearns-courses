# Weather and Seasons — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If clouds cover the sky and no rain falls, the weather is ዝናባማ (rainy).
**False** — that is ደመናማ (cloudy). Rainy is only when rain falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** በጋ and ክረምት are the same season.
**False** — they are opposites: በጋ is dry, with sun and heat; ክረምት is rainy, with rain and cold.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A season is one single day.
**False** — the weather is one day's sky; a season is many days together.
