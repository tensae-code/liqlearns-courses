# Weather and Seasons — matching

Match each word to what it means.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)**
1. ፀሐያማ — sunny: the sun is out
2. ዝናባማ — rainy: rain is falling
3. ደመናማ — cloudy: clouds, no rain

**Answer key:** ፀሐያማ–sunny · ዝናባማ–rainy · ደመናማ–cloudy.

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)**
1. በጋ — the dry season: sun and heat
2. ክረምት — the rainy season: rain and cold
3. ፀደይ — spring
4. በልግ — the belg season

**Answer key:** በጋ–dry · ክረምት–rainy · ፀደይ–spring · በልግ–belg.
