# Weather and Seasons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rain is falling from the clouds. The weather is —
a) ፀሐያማ (sunny)  b) ዝናባማ (rainy)  c) ደመናማ (cloudy)
**Answer: b) ዝናባማ (rainy)** — rainy is when the rain itself falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Clouds cover the whole sky, but no rain falls. The weather is —
a) ደመናማ (cloudy)  b) ዝናባማ (rainy)  c) ፀሐያማ (sunny)
**Answer: a) ደመናማ (cloudy).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** በጋ (bega) is the season of —
a) rain and cold  b) sun and heat  c) snow
**Answer: b) sun and heat** — በጋ is the dry season.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ክረምት (kiremt) brings —
a) rain and cold  b) only sun  c) no weather at all
**Answer: a) rain and cold** — ክረምት is the rainy season, the opposite of በጋ.
