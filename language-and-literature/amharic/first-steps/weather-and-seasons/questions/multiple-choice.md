# Weather and Seasons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rain is falling from the clouds. The weather is —
a) ፀሐያማ (sunny)  b) ዝናባማ (rainy)  c) ደመናማ (cloudy)
**Answer: b) ዝናባማ (rainy)** — rainy is when the rain itself falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Clouds cover the whole sky, but no rain falls. The weather is —
a) ደመናማ (cloudy)  b) ዝናባማ (rainy)  c) ፀሐያማ (sunny)
**Answer: a) ደመናማ (cloudy).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** በጋ (bega) is the season of —
a) rain and cold  b) sun and heat  c) snow
**Answer: b) sun and heat** — በጋ is the dry season.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ክረምት (kiremt) brings —
a) rain and cold  b) only sun  c) no weather at all
**Answer: a) rain and cold** — ክረምት is the rainy season, the opposite of በጋ.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ወቅት means…
a) A season  b) A month only  c) Rain only
**Answer: a) A season** — Grade 2 pp.23–24.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** የጥቅምት እሸት joins a month to…
a) What grows in it  b) A river  c) A school
**Answer: a) What grows in it** — the compound-phrase model.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** ፈነዳ goes with መስከረም because in Meskerem the adey flower…
a) Bursts open  b) Is planted  c) Is sold
**Answer: a) Bursts open** — the book's sentence words.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The task matches —
a) clothes to materials  b) seasons to their months  c) stories to tellers
**Answer: b) seasons to their months** — Seasons to months.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** The letter key runs —
a) 1. ለ 2. ሀ 3. መ 4. ሐ  b) 1. ሀ 2. ሀ 3. ሀ 4. ሀ  c) there is no key
**Answer: a) 1. ለ 2. ሀ 3. መ 4. ሐ** — 1. ለ 2. ሀ 3. መ 4. ሐ.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** መጋቢት and ግንቦት appear in —
a) the month sets on the charts  b) the dictation list  c) the fox story
**Answer: a) the month sets on the charts** — They are chart month sets.
