# Hands-on — Weather and Seasons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Today's sky
Every morning for a week, look at the sky with a grown-up and say the weather word out loud: ፀሐያማ, ዝናባማ or ደመናማ. Draw the sky of the day on paper — sun, rain or clouds over the same hill, like the picture.

## Colour the rainy season
Draw the rainy-season picture, like the book's colouring page: a hill, clouds, and rain falling. Colour the clouds grey and the rain blue. Say ዝናባማ as you colour.

## Season clothes check
When the season changes, stand at the clothes at home and sort with your voice: this one for በጋ, the dry season; this one for ክረምት, the rainy season. The clothes lesson next door teaches the clothes themselves.
