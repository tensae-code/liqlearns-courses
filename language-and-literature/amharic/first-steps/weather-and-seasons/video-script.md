# Video script — Weather and Seasons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The target
words are in Amharic, with the transliteration the books give.

---

Look up! The sky!

Sun! ፀሐያማ (tsehayama) — sunny!
Rain falling! ዝናባማ (zinabama) — rainy!
Clouds, no sun, no rain. ደመናማ (demenama) — cloudy!

What is the sky today?

And the seasons — ወቅቶች!
በጋ (bega) — the dry season. Sun and heat.
ክረምት (kiremt) — the rainy season. Rain and cold.
ፀደይ (tsedey) — spring.
በልግ (belg).

Now you. Look at the sky. Say the weather!
