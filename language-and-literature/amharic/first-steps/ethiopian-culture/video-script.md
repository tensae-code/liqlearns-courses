# Video script — Ethiopian Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dance! Let us dance.

The Oromo dance. The Amhara dance.
The Afar dance. The Wolayta dance.
The Tigrigna dance. The Gurage dance.

Shoulders move. Feet move.
Every people dances its own dance.
All of them are Ethiopia's dances.

Watch. Then dance with me.
