# Hands-on — Ethiopian Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Listen and name
A grown-up plays or sings a sound like an instrument — a drum beat on a table (kebero), a hum through a tube (washint). Name the instrument it is copying.

## A celebration at home
Ask at home: which celebration comes next — እንቁጣጣሽ, ቡሄ, አሸንዳ? What food will there be? Tell the class or your companion one thing your family does.

## Dance steps
Copy a traditional dance step from the video or from a family member: shoulders, then feet. Dance together — dancing is also how we practise moving together.
