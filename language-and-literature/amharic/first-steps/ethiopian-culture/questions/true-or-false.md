# Ethiopian Culture — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The kebero is a drum.
**Answer: true**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Genfo is a porridge with a well of butter in its middle.
**Answer: true**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Only one people in Ethiopia has a traditional dance.
**Answer: false** — every people named has its own dance.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A mesob (መሶብ) is a mark of Ethiopia's identity.
**Answer: true**
