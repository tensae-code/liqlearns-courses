# Ethiopian Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which instrument is a drum?
a) washint  b) kebero  c) krar
**Answer: b) kebero** — ከበሮ — you beat it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which instrument is the biggest of all?
a) masenqo  b) tsenatsil  c) begena
**Answer: c) begena** — በገና — the big harp.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The washint (ዋሽንት) is a —
a) drum  b) flute  c) harp
**Answer: b) flute** — you blow it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** እንቁጣጣሽ (Enqutatash) is —
a) a dish  b) the New Year  c) a dance
**Answer: b) the New Year**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which dish is the chicken stew with an egg?
a) genfo  b) kitfo  c) doro wat
**Answer: c) doro wat** — ዶሮ ወጥ.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which people have their own traditional dance in this lesson?
a) only one people  b) many peoples of Ethiopia  c) no peoples
**Answer: b) many peoples of Ethiopia** — Oromo, Amhara, Afar, Wolayta, Tigrigna, Gurage.
