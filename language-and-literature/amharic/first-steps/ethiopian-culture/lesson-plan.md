# Lesson plan — Ethiopian Culture

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Ethiopian Culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's own culture — its traditional instruments and their sounds, its celebrations and traditional foods, its marks of identity, and the dances of its peoples.
**This lesson's own level:** pre-primary — it stands on nothing but
the child's own world: their body, their home, their street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Ethiopia's music — the instruments** — the six instruments: masenqo ማሲንቆ, washint ዋሽንት, krar ክራር, kebero ከበሮ, begena በገና, tsenatsil ፅናፅል — what each looks like, and that each must be heard.
2. **Celebrations and traditional food** — ቡሄ, እንቁጣጣሽ (New Year), ሽምግልና, ፍቼ ጨንበላላ, አሸንዳ/ሻደይ — and the dishes ዶሮ ወጥ, ቁርጥ, ክትፎ, ገንፎ.
3. **Dances of the peoples** — the traditional dances of the Oromo, Amhara, Afar, Wolayta, Tigrigna and Gurage peoples, and the marks of identity — traditional dress, mesob መሶብ, clay pot.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing. This is the child's own country and home.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the six instruments: ማሲንቆ, ዋሽንት, ክራር, ከበሮ, በገና, ፅናፅል.
2. Name the celebrations: ቡሄ, እንቁጣጣሽ, ፍቼ ጨንበላላ, አሸንዳ/ሻደይ, and the elders' mediation ሽምግልና.
3. Name the four dishes: ዶሮ ወጥ (doro wat), ቁርጥ (q'urt), ክትፎ (kitfo), ገንፎ (genfo).
4. Name the peoples whose dances are taught: Oromo, Amhara, Afar, Wolayta, Tigrigna, Gurage.
5. Point out Ethiopia's marks of identity: traditional dress, the mesob (መሶብ), the clay pot.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes krar and begena → both have strings, but the begena is the big one — the biggest instrument on the board; the krar is the small lyre you can hold.
- Mixes the dishes → doro wat is the chicken stew with an egg; genfo is the porridge with the well of butter in its middle.
