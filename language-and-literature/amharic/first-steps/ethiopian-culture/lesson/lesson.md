# Ethiopian Culture

## Do it first

Look at the board of instruments. Point to one you have seen or heard before. Point to the biggest one. Point to the drum. Now we learn all their names.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ethiopia's music — the instruments

[Picture 8168 — the six instruments]

Ethiopia's traditional instruments (ባህላዊ የሙዚቃ መሳሪያዎች):

- **Masenqo — ማሲንቆ** — the one-string fiddle, played with a bow
- **Washint — ዋሽንት** — the flute
- **Krar — ክራር** — the small lyre
- **Kebero — ከበሮ** — the drum
- **Begena — በገና** — the big harp, the biggest of all
- **Tsenatsil — ፅናፅል** — the sistrum that shakes and rings

This is a lesson that must be HEARD: every instrument has its own sound, and the audio that rides on this lesson plays each one. Listen, then name what you heard. "I play the washint" — say it, and point to the washint.

## Sub lesson 2 — Celebrations and traditional food

Ethiopia's celebrations (ባህላዊ ገጽታዎች):

- **ቡሄ — Buhe**
- **እንቁጣጣሽ — Enqutatash, the New Year**
- **የሽምግልና ሥርዓት — shimgelina, the elders' mediation**
- **ፍቼ ጨንበላላ — Fichee Chambalaalla**
- **አሸንዳ/ሻደይ — Ashenda / Shadey**

And the traditional foods (የኢትዮጵያ ባህላዊ ምግቦች):

[Picture 8170 — the four dishes]

- **ዶሮ ወጥ — doro wat**, the chicken stew with an egg
- **ቁርጥ — q'urt**, raw meat
- **ክትፎ — kitfo**, the minced meat dish
- **ገንፎ — genfo**, the hot porridge with a well of spiced butter in its middle

## Sub lesson 3 — Dances of the peoples

[Picture 8169 — a traditional dance]

Every people of Ethiopia has its own traditional dance (ባህላዊ ውዝዋዜ):

- the **Oromo** dance — የኦሮምኛ
- the **Amhara** dance — የአማርኛ
- the **Afar** dance — የአፋርኛ
- the **Wolayta** dance — የወላይታ
- the **Tigrigna** dance — የትግርኛ
- the **Gurage** dance — የጉራጊኛ

And Ethiopia shows who she is in her marks of identity (ባህላዊ መገለጫዎች): traditional dress, the **mesob — መሶብ** we eat from together, the clay pot, the tanning of hide.

## Say it to your companion

Point to an instrument on the board; your companion names it. Point to a dish; name it. Name a people; your companion says "their dance!" Then swap.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name all six instruments from the board, in any order, without help.
2. Name the four dishes from their board.
3. Name three celebrations.
4. Show a dance step from your own people's dance — or from one you learned today.
