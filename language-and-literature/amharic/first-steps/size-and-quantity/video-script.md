# Video script — Size and Quantity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a lion. **ትልቅ** — big!
Here is a mouse. **ትንሽ** — small.

This jar is full — **ሙሉ**. Many!
This jar is empty — **ባዶ**. Zero inside.
This jar has a few — **ጥቂት**.

Now lift. Ugh! **ከባድ** — heavy!
And this one floats up. **ቀላል** — light!

Now you. Find two things. Which is big? Which is small?
Lift them. Which is heavy? Which is light?
