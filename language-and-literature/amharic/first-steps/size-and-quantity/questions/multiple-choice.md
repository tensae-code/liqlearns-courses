# Size and Quantity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the picture, the lion stands beside the mouse. The lion is —
a) ትንሽ — small  b) ትልቅ — big  c) ባዶ — empty
**Answer: b) ትልቅ — big** — the mouse is the ትንሽ one.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A jar holds three stones only, with lots of room left. The jar holds —
a) ብዙ — many  b) ሙሉ — a full load  c) ጥቂት — few
**Answer: c) ጥቂት — few.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair compares how wide a thing opens?
a) ሰፊ / ጠባብ — wide / narrow  b) ረዥም / አጭር — long / short  c) ብዙ / ጥቂት — many / few
**Answer: a) ሰፊ / ጠባብ** — the wide vase against the narrow vase.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ከባድ (kebad) means —
a) light  b) heavy  c) empty
**Answer: b) heavy** — the box that fights your arms.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A big empty box floats up in one hand. Its weight is —
a) ቀላል — light  b) ከባድ — heavy  c) ሰፊ — wide
**Answer: a) ቀላል — light** — a thing can be big and light; weight is felt, not seen.
