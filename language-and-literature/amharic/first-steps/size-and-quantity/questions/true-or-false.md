# Size and Quantity — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Many (ብዙ) and few (ጥቂት) are found by counting.
**Answer: True** — how-many is a count compared; that is why this lesson stands on Counting.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A small jar can hold many small stones.
**Answer: True** — big/small is the room a thing takes; many/few is how many things there are. The two pairs are different ideas.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** If a box is big, it must be heavy.
**Answer: False** — a big empty box is ቀላል (light); ከባድ is decided by lifting, not by looking.
