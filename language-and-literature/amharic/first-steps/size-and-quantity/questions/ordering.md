# Size and Quantity — ordering

Put the things in order and say the comparing words as you go.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order by size, smallest first: a big tree, a small stone, a medium plant.
**Answer: small stone, medium plant, big tree** — the size order the Level Two book teaches on its picture pages (and on the plant growth page, smallest → medium → biggest).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the jars by how much they hold, emptiest first: a full jar (ሙሉ), a jar with few (ጥቂት), an empty jar (ባዶ).
**Answer: ባዶ (empty), ጥቂት (few), ሙሉ (full).**
