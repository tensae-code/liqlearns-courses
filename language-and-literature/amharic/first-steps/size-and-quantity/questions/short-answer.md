# Size and Quantity — short answer

Answer out loud, pointing at the picture or the real thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two trees stand in the picture: one reaches high, one stays low. Give the comparing word for each.
**Answer: ረዥም (long/tall) for the high tree; አጭር (short) for the low one** — the book's own tall vs short trees.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A bead jar is full to the top, and another has almost nothing in it. Say both jars' words.
**Answer: ሙሉ (full) for the full jar; the nearly-empty jar holds ጥቂት (few) — and a jar with nothing at all is ባዶ (empty).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You lift a stone and a leaf. Which is ከባድ and which is ቀላል? How do you know?
**Answer: The stone is ከባድ (heavy), the leaf ቀላል (light) — you know by lifting: weight is what the arms feel.**
