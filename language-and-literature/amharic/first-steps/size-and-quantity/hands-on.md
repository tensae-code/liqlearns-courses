# Hands-on — Size and Quantity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Big hand, small hand
Fill both hands with stones: a big handful and a small handful.
Say ብዙ for the many and ጥቂት for the few, then count both
handfuls to prove the words.

## Jar filling
With three jars or cups: fill one to the top (ሙሉ), leave one
empty (ባዶ), drop three stones in the last (ጥቂት). A partner
closes their eyes, feels or hears each jar, and names it.

## The lift test
Pack a box with books until it is ከባድ — you can barely lift
it. Take books out one by one, lifting after each, until it is
ቀላል. Say the word at every lift. Weight is learned by arms,
not eyes.

## Order by size
Line up three shoes, three sticks or three stones from smallest
to biggest. Say the comparing sentence for the whole line:
"ትንሽ, then bigger, then ትልቅ." Shuffle and order them again.

## If it is hard
Sort real objects smallest → biggest and sort by shape with
your hands, the SNE toolkit's own ordering and sorting checks
(printed pp.24–25): real things you can hold, one step at a
time, with the right way shown, never a failure label.
