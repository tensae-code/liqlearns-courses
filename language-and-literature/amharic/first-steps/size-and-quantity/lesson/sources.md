Cross-reference only, not taught as this lesson's content: KG3 printed p.49 (plant growth order — small plant, medium plant, big plant, matched by size) belongs to the plants lesson; it is named here because the brief for this build assigns it there, and this lesson's size-order work proper comes from KG3 pp.160–161 above. The comparing-words copy-writing on KG3 p.96 and the biggest/smallest animal pictures on p.97 sit with the Adding and taking away / animals lessons. Sub lessons decision: the lesson carries two sub lessons (how big / how heavy) because the Level One book teaches them as two separate pages with two separate word pairs (p.53 size, p.77 weight) — two distinct sequential parts under the 2026-11-09 ruling, not one merged "comparing" block. The drawn lion and mouse in Picture 8132 are simple flat figures; the words ትልቅ/ትንሽ carry the teaching, as the placements' own pictured pair (lion vs mouse) does.
