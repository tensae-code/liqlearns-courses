# Lesson plan — Cleanliness

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Cleanliness
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how to keep my body clean —
wash your hands, brush your teeth, keep clean every day (ንጽሕና).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's
own world — the child washes and eats at home every day already.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Wash your hands** — the four steps in order: apply soap (ሳሙና
   መቀባት), rub well with soap, rinse with clean water, dry with a
   towel (በፎጣ ማድረቅ) — and when: before eating, after the toilet.
2. **Brush your teeth** — the brushing routine: across, up-and-down,
   the tongue, then rinse — every day.
3. **Keep clean every day** — washing the face, the body, the feet
   and the hair, combing the hair; covering a sneeze; the hygiene
   things (ሳሙና soap, ጥፍር መቁረጫ nail cutter, የጥርስ ቡርሽ toothbrush,
   የጫማ ቡሩሽ shoe brush, ማበጠሪያ comb); and the story of Gobez Meseret,
   the girl the whole village copied.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the
gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: My Body — the hands and teeth this lesson
  washes. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Do and say the four hand-washing steps in order.
2. Brush teeth the book's way — across, up-and-down, tongue, rinse.
3. Name the daily cleanliness jobs and the five hygiene things.
4. Cover a sneeze or cough with the elbow, like Meseret.
5. Tell the Gobez Meseret story's habits from memory.

## What you need
Soap, water and a towel if you have them. The lesson works by
mime and picture without them.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner shows, by mime, how they wash
   their hands now, before the steps are taught (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the four steps, the brushing routine, the
   daily jobs and the hygiene things, and Meseret's story told as a
   summary (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD GIF [GIF 8015]: hands rubbing under a running tap, soap

3. **Say it to your companion** — teach your companion the four

6. **Do It Yourself** — wash for real, brush for real, and check a

## If the learner is stuck
- Steps out of order → go back to the four numbered pictures in the
  head: soap, rub, rinse, dry. Say them as a chant while miming.
- Forgets when to wash → Meseret's rule: before eating, always;
  after the toilet, always.
- This lesson's next door: My Home — the clean home these clean
  hands live in.
