# Hands-on — Cleanliness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wash for real
At a tap or a bowl of water, wash your hands with soap the real
way: soap, rub the palms, the backs and between the fingers,
rinse, dry. Count slowly to twenty while you rub.

## The brushing mime — then real
First mime the brushing with a finger: across, up-and-down,
tongue, rinse. Then do it for real with your own toothbrush,
morning and night.

## Meseret's day
Play Meseret for one day: wash before every meal, wash after the
toilet, keep your nails short and clean, cover every sneeze and
cough with your elbow. At bedtime, count how many of her habits
you kept.

## If it is hard
Break the job into small steps and show each step separately:
watch step 1 and praise it before step 2. Teach the life skills —
washing the face and hands, dressing, eating properly — step by
step, with pictures backing up the short words (SNE toolkit
support, printed pp.26–27 — written as help, never as a label on
the child).
