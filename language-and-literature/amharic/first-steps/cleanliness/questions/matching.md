# Cleanliness — matching

Match each hygiene thing to its job.

1. ሳሙና (soap) — c
2. የጥርስ ቡርሽ (toothbrush) — a
3. ማበጠሪያ (comb) — d
4. ጥፍር መቁረጫ (nail cutter) — b
5. የጫማ ቡሩሽ (shoe brush) — e

Jobs: a) brushes the teeth  b) cuts the nails short  c) washes hands and body  d) combs the hair  e) cleans the shoes

**Answer key: 1–c, 2–a, 3–d, 4–b, 5–e.**
