# Cleanliness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the FIRST step of hand washing?
a) dry with a towel  b) apply soap  c) rinse with water
**Answer: b) apply soap** — ሳሙና መቀባት.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When must you always wash your hands?
a) before eating  b) before sleeping only  c) never
**Answer: a) before eating** — and after the toilet, always.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After brushing across and up-and-down, you brush —
a) your hair  b) the tongue  c) your feet
**Answer: b) the tongue** — then rinse with a cup of water.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How often do we brush our teeth?
a) once a month  b) every day  c) never
**Answer: b) every day.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ሳሙና is —
a) a comb  b) a toothbrush  c) soap
**Answer: c) soap.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A sneeze comes! You cover your mouth and nose with —
a) your elbow  b) nothing  c) your friend's hand
**Answer: a) your elbow** — like Meseret.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** How much toothpaste does the science lesson set?
a) A pea-sized drop  b) A full brush-load  c) None
**Answer: a) A pea-sized drop** — the story's own dental steps.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** How often should teeth be brushed, in the story's lesson?
a) Twice a day, and after sweets  b) Once a week  c) Only when they hurt
**Answer: a) Twice a day, and after sweets** — brush every tooth, and the tongue gently.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** What brings Momina's smile back?
a) The dentist's treatment, then cleaning and instruction a month later  b) A new dress  c) Nothing — it never returns
**Answer: a) The dentist's treatment, then cleaning and instruction a month later** — the story's ending.
