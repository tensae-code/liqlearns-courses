# Cleanliness — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The hand-washing steps: rinse with clean water · apply soap · dry with a towel · rub well with soap.
**Answer: apply soap → rub well with soap → rinse with clean water → dry with a towel.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The teeth-brushing steps: rinse with a cup of water · brush across · brush the tongue · brush up and down.
**Answer: brush across → brush up and down → brush the tongue → rinse with a cup of water.**
