# Cleanliness — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** You dry your hands before you rinse them.
**Answer: False** — rinse third, dry fourth, last.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Meseret brushes her teeth.
**Answer: True** — it is one of her habits.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ማበጠሪያ (mabet'eriya) is a comb.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Meseret eats without washing her hands.
**Answer: False** — she NEVER eats without washing her hands first.
