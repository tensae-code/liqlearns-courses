# Video script — Cleanliness

The super-simple voice, for the videos that ride on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the scripts are the slots' written form until then. This
lesson carries TWO full scripts — both routines were named
VIDEO-SCRIPT YES in the placements.

---

## Script 1 — Wash your hands (የእጅ አስተጣጠብ ቅደም ተከተል)

Water on. Hands wet.

**One** — soap. ሳሙና መቀባት. Soap on both hands.
**Two** — rub, rub, rub. Palms. Backs. Between the fingers.
**Three** — rinse. Clean water takes the soap away.
**Four** — dry. በፎጣ ማድረቅ. Dry with a clean towel.

Soap. Rub. Rinse. Dry.
Before you eat — wash. After the toilet — wash.
Clean hands, healthy child.

## Script 2 — Brush your teeth (የጥርስ አፋፋቅ)

Brush ready. A little paste.

Across, across — the brush walks across the teeth.
Up and down, up and down — like rain falling.
Now the tongue — brush it gently.
Cup of water — rinse, and spit.

Across. Up and down. Tongue. Rinse.
Every day. Morning and night.
Clean teeth smile.
