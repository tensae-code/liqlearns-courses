# Video script — Position Words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a box. Here is a ball.
In it goes — **ውስጥ** — in!
On top — **ላይ** — on!
Underneath — **ስር** — under!
At its side — **ጎን** — beside!

Now your hands. This hand — **ቀኝ እጅ** — right hand. Up!
This hand — **ግራ እጅ** — left hand. Up!
In front — **ከፊት**. Behind — **ከኋላ**.

Now you. Put the ball. Say where it is.
Raise the hand I name. Ready?
