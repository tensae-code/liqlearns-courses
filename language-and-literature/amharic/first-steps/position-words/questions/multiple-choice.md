# Position Words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ball is hidden by the box's sides, inside it. The word is —
a) ላይ — on  b) ውስጥ — in  c) ጎን — beside
**Answer: b) ውስጥ — in.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The dog is ___ the table, hidden underneath it.
a) ስር — under  b) ላይ — on  c) ውስጥ — in
**Answer: a) ስር — under.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book is ___ the table, sitting on its top.
a) ጎን — beside  b) ስር — under  c) ላይ — on
**Answer: c) ላይ — on.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ቀኝ እጅ means —
a) left hand  b) right hand  c) in front
**Answer: b) right hand.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** You stand where the chair hides your middle. You are —
a) ከፊት — in front of the chair  b) ጎን — beside the chair  c) ከኋላ — behind the chair
**Answer: c) ከኋላ — behind.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which word says where your nose is, on your body?
a) ከኋላ — behind  b) ከፊት — in front  c) ስር — under
**Answer: b) ከፊት — in front** — your back is the behind one.
