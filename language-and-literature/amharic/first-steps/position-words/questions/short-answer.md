# Position Words — short answer

Answer out loud, and show the position with the ball and box or your own body.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How can you tell the ball is IN (ውስጥ) the box and not ON (ላይ) it?
**Answer: In means the box's sides hide it; on means it sits on top where you can see it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The banana is in the box, the dog is under the table, the book is on the table. Say the three words in order.
**Answer: ውስጥ (in), ስር (under), ላይ (on)** — the Level One book's own scenes (printed p.21).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one body part that is in front (ከፊት) and one that is on the right (በቀኝ).
**Answer: the nose or the chest is in front; the right ear or the right hand is on the right** — any correct pair; the Level One book asks exactly this of body parts (printed p.38).
