# Position Words — fill in the blank

Fill the missing direction word. Act it out as you answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The children are ___ the blackboard. (ፊት ለፊት — face to face with it, in front)
**Answer: ፊት ለፊት** — the Level Two book's own direction sentence (printed p.83).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The cat is ___ the table — it sits on top of it.
**Answer: ላይ** — on top.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The donkey is ___ the tree — it stands at the tree's side.
**Answer: ጎን** — beside.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Raise your ___ እጅ — the hand that is not the left one.
**Answer: ቀኝ** — ቀኝ እጅ, the right hand.
