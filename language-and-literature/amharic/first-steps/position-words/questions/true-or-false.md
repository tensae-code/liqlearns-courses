# Position Words — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If the ball is beside (ጎን) the box, it is inside the box.
**Answer: False** — beside means at its side, outside it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your right hand is the ግራ እጅ.
**Answer: False** — ግራ እጅ is the left hand; the right hand is ቀኝ እጅ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A figure facing you raises its own right hand on your left side.
**Answer: True** — its right is your left when it faces you — which is why you name your OWN hands, not the viewer's.
