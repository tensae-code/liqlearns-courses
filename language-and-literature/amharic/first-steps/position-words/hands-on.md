# Hands-on — Position Words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hide the ball
With a ball and a box: a partner closes their eyes, you place
the ball in, on, under or beside the box. They open their eyes
and must say the whole sentence: "The ball is ___ the box." Ten
rounds, then swap.

## The whole house is a lesson
Walk room to room and collect wheres: the pot is on the stove,
the shoes are under the bed, the picture is beside the door,
the grain is in the sack. Say each one with its Amharic word.

## Left, right, move
A caller names a movement — ቀኝ እጅ (right hand up), ግራ እጅ
(left hand up), ከፊት (step in front of the chair), ከኋላ (step
behind it) — and everyone moves. Wrong movers sit one round
out; the caller swaps every five calls.

## Turn and look back
From the SNE toolkit's gross-motor supports (printed
pp.13–15): walk along a rope laid on the ground — rope on the
left, rope on the right, rope between the legs — turn the body
left and right standing still, walk around a chair, and turn to
look back (ከኋላ) when your name is called. Position words
learned by the whole body.
