# Position Words — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. First the ball-and-box words, then the words
on the child's own body — front, behind, and the two hands.

## Sub lesson 1 — In, on, under, beside
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say and
show ውስጥ, ላይ, ስር, ጎን with a ball and a box, use them on real
scenes (dog under the table, book on the table), and choose the
right word to finish a direction sentence.
