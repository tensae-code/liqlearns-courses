**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

The Level One book's own summary names position words (in front / behind / left / right) as new at that level, which is why this lesson exists at pre-primary at all. ውስጥ (in) is the word the lesson panel uses for the in-position of KG2 p.21; the placement gives the four positions in English (in / on / under / beside) with Amharic for the other three carried from KG3 p.83 (ላይ, ስር, ጎን) — ውስጥ is the standard Amharic word for "in/inside" and is used here as the gloss for that position, named as a gloss here rather than as a placement quote. In the pictures and GIF, the figure raises its OWN right hand (ቀኝ እጅ), which appears on the viewer's left when the figure faces you — the plan's stuck-note tells the teacher to name each child's own hands, not the viewer's.
