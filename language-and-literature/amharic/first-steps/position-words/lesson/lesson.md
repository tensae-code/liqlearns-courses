# Position Words

## Do it first

Take a ball and a box. Put the ball inside the box. Take it out
and put it on top of the box. Now put it underneath. Now put it
right next to the box. You just made four different wheres — and
each where has a word. This lesson teaches the where-words: first
the box words, then the words for your own body — front, behind,
left, right.

## Sub lesson 1 — In, on, under, beside

One ball, one box, four words:

[Picture 8135 — the ball and the box, four ways]

- **ውስጥ — in.** The ball is IN the box. The box's sides hide it.
- **ላይ — on.** The ball is ON the box, sitting on its top.
- **ስር — under.** The ball is UNDER the box, hidden beneath it.
- **ጎን — beside.** The ball is BESIDE the box, at its side.

Watch the ball travel — in, on, under:

[GIF 8137 — the ball going in, on and under the box]

The same four words work on the whole world. The dog is under
the table. The book is on the table. The banana is in the box.
Whenever you say where a thing is, one of these words does the
work — and the Level Two book tests it as choosing the word that
finishes the sentence: the children are ___ the blackboard
(ፊት ለፊት — face to face, in front); the cat is ___ the table;
the ball is ___ the table; the donkey is ___ the tree. The words
it offers are ላይ (on top), ስር (under), ፊት ለፊት (in front) and
ጎን (beside) — you pick the one the picture shows.

## Sub lesson 2 — In front, behind, left, right

Now the words stand on your own body.

[Picture 8136 — right hand, left hand, behind the box, in front of the box]

- **ቀኝ እጅ — right hand.** Raise your right hand.
- **ግራ እጅ — left hand.** Raise your left hand.
- **ከፊት — in front.** Stand in front of a chair: we see you
  first, then the chair.
- **ከኋላ — behind.** Stand behind the chair: the chair hides
  your middle.

[GIF 8138 — the figure raising right hand, left hand, standing in front, then behind]

It starts smaller still, on body parts: say where the body part
is — in front (ከፊት), behind (ከኋላ), on the left (በግራ), on the
right (በቀኝ). Your nose is in front. Your back is behind. One
ear is on the left, one on the right.

And the writing step: look at each picture and write the
direction word for it — ቀኝ እጅ, ግራ እጅ, ከፊት, ከኋላ.

## Say it to your companion

One of you closes their eyes. The other puts the ball somewhere —
in, on, under or beside the box. Eyes open: "Where is the ball?"
Answer with the whole sentence: "The ball is under the box —
ስር." Then hands: one calls "ግራ እጅ!" and the other must raise
the left hand, fast. Swap turns.

## Do It Yourself

1. With a ball and a box, show all four positions and say each
   word: ውስጥ, ላይ, ስር, ጎን.
2. Walk through your home and say five wheres: the book is on
   the table; the shoes are under the bed; the cup is beside
   the pot.
3. Stand in front of a door (ከፊት), then behind it (ከኋላ), and
   say each word while you stand there.
4. Raise the hand your companion names — ቀኝ እጅ, ግራ እጅ — ten
   times, faster and faster, no mistakes.
5. Draw a table with a cat: draw the cat under the table, then
   again on the table, and write ስር and ላይ under the right
   drawings.
