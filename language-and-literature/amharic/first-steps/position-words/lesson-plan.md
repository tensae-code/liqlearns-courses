# Lesson plan — Position Words

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Position Words
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the words that say where a
thing is — ውስጥ (in), ላይ (on), ስር (under), ጎን (beside), ከፊት (in
front), ከኋላ (behind), ቀኝ (right), ግራ (left).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's own
world — a ball and a box, a book on a table, their own two hands.
It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **In, on, under, beside** — the ball and the box (ውስጥ in, ላይ on, ስር under, ጎን beside), then the same words on the world: a dog under the table, a book on the table, a banana in the box; and the choose-the-word direction sentences (the children are ___ the blackboard; the cat is ___ the table).
2. **In front, behind, left, right** — ከፊት (in front), ከኋላ (behind), ቀኝ እጅ (right hand), ግራ እጅ (left hand): first on the child's own body (say where a body part is: in front, behind, on the left, on the right), then writing the direction word for each picture.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the child's own body lesson in this unit —
  the body-part position page (Level One p.38) stands on naming
  body parts. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say where the ball is, all four ways: ውስጥ the box, ላይ the box,
   ስር the box, ጎን the box — and put the ball there when asked.
2. Use the same words on real scenes: the dog under the table,
   the book on the table, the banana in the box.
3. Choose the right direction word to finish a sentence: the cat
   is ___ the table; the donkey is ___ the tree.
4. Raise the right hand (ቀኝ እጅ) and the left hand (ግራ እጅ) when
   named, and step ከፊት (in front) and ከኋላ (behind) a chair.
5. Say where a body part is: in front, behind, on the left, on
   the right.

## What you need
A ball (or a stone) and a box (or a basket). A table and a book.
Your own two hands.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner puts a ball in a box, on the box,
   under the box and beside the box, one after another, before any
   word is named (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the four box words with the ball; then
   front/behind/left/right on the child's own body
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8135]: a picture of the ball and box four ways — the ball inside the box (ውስጥ), on the box (ላይ), under the box (ስር), beside the box (ጎን) — the student points at each panel and says its word.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 8137]: a picture of a ball travelling — rolling into the box (ውስጥ), jumping on top of it (ላይ), then sliding under it (ስር) — the student says each word as the ball lands.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8136]: a picture of a child raising the right hand (ቀኝ እጅ) and the left hand (ግራ እጅ), and standing behind a box (ከኋላ) and in front of it (ከፊት) — the student copies each pose.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 8138]: a picture of a child figure raising the right hand, then the left hand, then standing in front of a box, then behind it — the student does each movement with the figure.
   - ADD AUDIO in both parts: a voice naming a position — "ላይ!", "ግራ እጅ!" — the student puts the ball, or raises the hand, that matches. (Audio slot — described only, made later.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — one hides the ball somewhere on,
   in, under or beside the box; the other says where it is
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the ball
     and the two hands in the super-simple voice (made later, when
     videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, short-answer,
   fill-in-the-blank, true-or-false — served at the learner's level
   and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — position words all over the house: the book
   on the table, the shoes under the bed, you in front of the door
   (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes on and in → the ball ON the box can be seen sitting on top;
  the ball IN the box is hidden by the box's sides. Hide and show.
- Mixes left and right → tie the words to the hands themselves:
  raise the hand you draw with — name it — then the other one.
  (Each child names their own hands; the GIF's figure raises its
  own right hand, on the viewer's left.)
- Mixes in front and behind → stand the child by a chair: between
  the chair and the door is ከፊት; hidden by the chair is ከኋላ.
- This lesson's next door: the movement lessons in this unit —
  arms up, down, in front use these same words on the whole body.
