# Hands-on — Farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The tools and grains discussion
In a group, with real things or pictures:
1. Name the traditional farm tools and say what each is used for — the plough and its parts among them.
2. Look at the grains — wheat, beans, chickpeas, corn, peas — and compare their sizes, biggest to smallest.
3. Talk: ox, donkey or horse ploughing against tractor ploughing — which yields more? Why?

## City and countryside
Ask at home or look around: what farm work happens in the city, and what in the countryside? Name one of each.
