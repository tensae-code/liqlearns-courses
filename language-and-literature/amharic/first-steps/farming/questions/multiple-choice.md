# Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the picture, the field is ploughed with —
a) a tractor  b) oxen  c) a bus
**Answer: b) oxen** — most farmers in our country plough with oxen.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ox-drawn plough is —
a) modern farm work  b) traditional farm work  c) not farm work
**Answer: b) traditional farm work** — ባህላዊ.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Wide fields belong most to —
a) the city farm  b) the countryside farm  c) the road
**Answer: b) the countryside farm**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a grain?
a) wheat  b) a yoke  c) a plough
**Answer: a) wheat** — the yoke and plough are tools.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A yoke is —
a) a grain  b) a farm tool  c) a kind of rain
**Answer: b) a farm tool** — it joins the oxen to the plough.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the discussion we compare grains by —
a) their colour only  b) their size  c) their names only
**Answer: b) their size**
