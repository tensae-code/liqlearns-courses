# Farming

## Do it first

Look at the picture of the field. Tell everything you see: who is there, what the animals are doing, what is happening to the earth. You are already reading the farm. Now we learn its words.

## Sub lesson 1 — Farm work then and now

[Picture 8175 — ploughing with oxen]

The field is ploughed with oxen. The farmer walks behind the plough; the oxen pull; the earth opens in lines, ready for seed. Most farmers in our country plough with oxen.

Farm work (የግብርና ስራ) can be sorted two ways:

- **Traditional and modern** (ባህላዊ እና ዘመናዊ): the ox-drawn plough is traditional farm work; the tractor is modern farm work. Sort each picture the same way.
- **City and countryside** (የከተማ እና የገጠር): farm work in the city is not the same as farm work in the countryside — the countryside has the wide fields; the city farms small and close.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tools and grains — the discussion

This part is a discussion — the class talks together:

1. **Farm tools.** Name the traditional farm tools — the mofer, the yoke, the plough among them — and say the name, amount and use of each, and the parts of the tools.
2. **Grains.** Look at the grain types in pictures — wheat, beans, chickpeas, corn, peas — and compare their sizes.
3. **Ox or tractor?** Ox, donkey and horse ploughing against tractor ploughing — which yields more? Talk it through together.

Then: word cards, drawing farm animals, and forming words from the farm tools' first letters.

## Say it to your companion

Name a tool; your companion says what it does. Name a grain; your companion says whether it is big or small beside the others. Finish with one sentence each: ox ploughing is ___; tractor ploughing is ___.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sort these: ox-plough, tractor, yoke — traditional or modern?
2. Put the grains in size order: corn, wheat, peas, beans, chickpeas — your own true order from looking at them.
3. Say one thing the countryside farm has that the city farm does not.
