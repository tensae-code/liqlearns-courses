# Amharic course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Amharic is Ethiopia's own language, and in the Language and
Literature structure it stands as its own course inside that
folder (his architecture ruling, 2026-11-11). The path climbs by
the size of the thing — **First Steps → Fidel → Word → Sentence →
Paragraph → Essay → Story** (see TITLES.md).

**Levels, not grades:** a learner climbs when they are ready,
not when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books
come from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance),
never as the course's organisation. The pre-primary books are
this course's earliest level — First Steps — not a separate
course.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from Ethiopia's own government books for learning
Amharic — the pre-primary workbooks of the Addis Ababa City
Administration Education Bureau and, as the higher levels are
built, the Amharic student textbooks and teacher guides named in
each lesson's sources. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a
tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'.

## Unit 2 — Fidel

1. **The Fidel** (`fidel/the-fidel`) — the full chart; the first
   letters (KG1's sixteen first letters, traced and copied); all
   33 families and their seven forms; first sounds, matching and
   the odd one out; first reading; a word for every form;
   building words. Grown in Band 2 with the whole chart in the
   G1 book's own teaching order, the -ዋ (labialised) forms, and
   the derived forms ቈ ኈ ኰ ጐ. The sounds that get mixed up
   (ለ/ረ/ጠ/ተ) are carried in the lesson's support material from
   the special-needs toolkit, labelled SUPPORTING.
2. **Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs** (`fidel/fidel-shapes`)
   — which fidel stand on legs; the look-alikes (ሞክሸ) and their
   keyword names; the compound fidel (ውሰብሰብ). Teacher-guide
   evidence only, labelled in its sources.
3. **Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል**
   (`fidel/blending-and-segmenting`) — joining fidel into words
   and splitting words back; taking away the first fidel; moving
   a fidel to make a new word (the guides' own keys).

   word carries in its sentence (አውዳዊ ፍቺ); words with more
   than one meaning; the figurative table.

   antonyms as systems; word webs (እርዝ); swapping a word for a
   better one.
6. **Building Words** (`word/building-words`) — compound words;
   doubled words; -ኛ and -ነት nouns; the loanwords and where

7. **Using the Dictionary** (`word/using-the-dictionary`) —
   finding a word, in the book's own order of teaching.
8. **Study Skills** (`word/study-skills`) — KWL charts; concept

   guide), labelled in its sources and counted nowhere.
10. **Dictionary Meaning vs Meaning in the Text**
    (`word/dictionary-meaning-vs-meaning-in-the-text`) — a word

    2026-11-12).
11. **Children's Rights in a Table**

12. **Reading Habits and Speed** (`word/reading-habits-and-speed`)

## Unit 4 — Sentence

1. **My First Sentences** (`sentence/first-sentences`) — reading
   whole sentences; making sentences of your own.
2. **The Marks That Help Us Read**
   (`sentence/first-punctuation`) — ሁለት ነጥብ (colon), the
   four-dot mark, ነጠላ ሰረዝ (comma), የጥያቄ ምልክት (question
   mark).
3. **The Sentence** (`sentence/the-sentence`) — what makes a
   sentence; the full stop (።); the question mark; word order.
   Grown in Band 3: subject ባለቤት + predicate; the four sentence
   functions; yes and no sentences (አል-…-ም); the copula and its
   complement; simple and complex grown.
4. **Word Kinds** (`sentence/word-kinds`) — the kinds in the
   books' own order: noun (ስም), verb (ግስ), adjective (ቅጽል),
   conjunction, adverb, preposition. Grown in Band 3: common and
   proper nouns; collective nouns; pronouns and their persons
   (መደብ); demonstratives and question pronouns; countable and
   uncountable nouns.
5. **Writing Sentences** (`sentence/writing-sentences`) —
   dictation; building a sentence piece by piece; the compound
   sentence.
6. **Verbs and Tenses** (`sentence/verbs-and-tenses`) — the
   three times (የአሁን / የሀላፊ / የትንቢት); recent perfect and
   remote past; ሳቢ and ኢሳቢ verbs; direct and indirect objects;
   the በላ paradigm.
7. **Joining Words** (`sentence/joining-words`) — coordinating
   joiners (አያያዦች); subordinate conjunctions (ጥገኛ መስተፃምር);
   transitional words (መሸጋገሪያ ቃላት).
8. **More Punctuation** (`sentence/more-punctuation`) — the
   exclamation mark and the seven feelings; quotation marks and
   single quotes; colon and slash (እዝባር); dash, parentheses
   (ቅንፍ), ellipsis; abbreviations grown.
