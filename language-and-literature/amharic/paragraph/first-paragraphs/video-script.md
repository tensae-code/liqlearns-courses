# Video script — First Paragraphs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One sentence says one thing.

Now — three sentences. Listen. Are they one family?
They all talk about the animals I like. One idea!
Sentences together around one idea — that is a **paragraph**.

Here is a paragraph that stops. It is not finished.
I read the given part. I keep its idea. I write the ending.
Same idea. Same flow. Now it is whole.

And here is one small starter sentence.
Watch it grow. One line. Two lines. Five lines!
Every new line stays with the same idea.

One idea. Sentences together. That is a paragraph — YOUR paragraph.
