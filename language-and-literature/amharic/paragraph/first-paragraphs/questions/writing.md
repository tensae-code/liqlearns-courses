# First Paragraphs — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a short paragraph about the wild animals you like. Then read it aloud.
**Answer guide: every sentence about the animals you like — which ones, where they live, what they do; no stranger sentence.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Complete this starter in at least two more sentences, keeping its idea: "Forests keep nature's balance, but people cut them for temporary gain…"
**Answer guide: the added sentences stay about forests and cutting; punctuation correct.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Complete this starter: "Our school had no trees. The science teacher had a pit dug, and we fetched seedlings…"
**Answer guide: the completion keeps the planting idea and its flow to a rounded ending.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Grow a paragraph of at least five lines from the starter "good behaviour has many benefits."
**Answer guide: five or more lines, every line about the starter's idea, the last line rounding it off.**
