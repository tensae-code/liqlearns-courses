# First Paragraphs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Any sentences standing near each other make a paragraph.
**Answer: False** — a paragraph's sentences must all carry one idea.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When completing a starter, you may change the given sentences to your own idea.
**Answer: False** — you keep the given idea and flow, and write the part that finishes it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A paragraph can be grown from given points as well as from a starter sentence.
**Answer: True** — the books grow paragraphs both ways (the civet points; the starters).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** One paragraph keeps to ONE chosen topic.
**Answer: True**
