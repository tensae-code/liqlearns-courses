# First Paragraphs — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the one question to ask of any paragraph?
**Answer: what is the one idea, and does every sentence carry it?**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When you complete a starter paragraph, what must your next sentence stay about?
**Answer: the same idea as the given part — never a new one.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In growing a paragraph, what does the last sentence do?
**Answer: it rounds the paragraph off — it does not wander away to a new idea.**
