# First Paragraphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What holds a paragraph together?
a) its sentences are the same length  b) its sentences all carry one idea  c) it has exactly three sentences
**Answer: b) its sentences all carry one idea.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a paragraph about the wild animals you like, which sentence is the stranger?
a) The elephant eats leaves.  b) My shoe is under the bed.  c) The lion is strong.
**Answer: b) My shoe is under the bed** — it carries a different idea; it starts another paragraph.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before completing a starter paragraph, you should first —
a) choose a new idea  b) read the given part twice and say its idea in your own words  c) change the given sentences
**Answer: b) read the given part twice and say its idea in your own words.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 3 book's measure for a grown paragraph is —
a) exactly two lines  b) at least five lines  c) one line per pupil
**Answer: b) at least five lines.**
