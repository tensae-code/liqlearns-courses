# Hands-on — First Paragraphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sentence strips
Write four sentences on four strips of paper — three about your
home, one about the market. Mix them. A partner picks the three
that belong together and names their one idea. Then the odd one
becomes the starter of a new paragraph — grow it together.

## Finish my paragraph
Write two sentences of a paragraph and stop. Swap papers.
Your partner completes your paragraph, keeping your idea.
Read both halves aloud: does it sound like one voice?

## The five-line grow
Take the starter "good behaviour has many benefits" (the books'
own starter). Each person in a small group adds one sentence,
always about the same idea, until the paragraph has five lines.
Read the whole paragraph aloud in turns.
