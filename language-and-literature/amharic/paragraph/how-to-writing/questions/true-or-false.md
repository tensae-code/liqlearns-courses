# How-To Writing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A how-to still works if two steps change places.
**Answer: False** — order is the how-to's whole strength; a swapped step breaks it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The outline comes after the full writing, as a summary of it.
**Answer: False** — the outline (ዝርዝር) is drafted BEFORE composing; the writing grows from it.
