# How-To Writing — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the kebero steps in order: lace the hide over both openings — soak the hide — hollow the wanza-wood body — sun it for a good sound — scrape the hide.
**Answer: soak the hide → scrape the hide → hollow the wanza-wood body → lace the hide over both openings → sun it for a good sound.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the salad steps in order: serve with bread or injera — chop — wash — mix with oil and salt, then lemon juice.
**Answer: wash → chop → mix with oil and salt, then lemon juice → serve with bread or injera.**
