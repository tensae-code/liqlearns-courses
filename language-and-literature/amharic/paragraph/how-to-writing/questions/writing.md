# How-To Writing — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the steps of preparing ጥቅል ጎመን (cabbage) for eating, in sequence, numbering your steps. (Ask at home first, the way the book sets it.)
**Answer guide: steps numbered, in the order they are done, nothing skipped.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Make an outline (ዝርዝር) for a how-to about how coffee is brewed (የቡና አፈላል): a topic line and at least four point-lines in order.
**Answer guide: topic first; the points short, ordered, one per line.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Now write the full how-to from your outline in Question 2.
**Answer guide: the writing follows the outline point by point; steps in order.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Write traditional mediation (ባህላዊ ሽምግልና) as a process text: five numbered steps, one or two lines each, in their true order.
**Answer guide: 1 preliminary inquiry; 2 consent to reconcile; 3 mediators for both sides; 4 the mediation judge; 5 conducting the mediation — each step preparing the next.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Choose a real process you know (settling, building, harvesting, cooking) and write it as a process text of at least four numbered steps. Then test it: could any two steps change places and still work?
**Answer guide: steps numbered and in doing order; the test is answered honestly — in a true process, swapped steps break it.**
