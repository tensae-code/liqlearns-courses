# How-To Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the whole strength of a how-to text?
a) long words  b) the steps standing in their true order  c) a surprise ending
**Answer: b) the steps standing in their true order.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the salad how-to, which step comes FIRST?
a) mix in a deep bowl  b) serve with bread or injera  c) wash the ingredients
**Answer: c) wash the ingredients** — then chop, mix, oil and salt, lemon, serve.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book test that you hold a how-to?
a) you retell the steps in sequence  b) you draw the finished thing  c) you name the ingredients only
**Answer: a) you retell the steps in sequence.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ዝርዝር (zirzir) is —
a) the final fair copy  b) the outline you draft before composing  c) a list of new words
**Answer: b) the outline you draft before composing.**
