# How-To Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the whole strength of a how-to text?
a) long words  b) the steps standing in their true order  c) a surprise ending
**Answer: b) the steps standing in their true order.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the salad how-to, which step comes FIRST?
a) mix in a deep bowl  b) serve with bread or injera  c) wash the ingredients
**Answer: c) wash the ingredients** — then chop, mix, oil and salt, lemon, serve.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book test that you hold a how-to?
a) you retell the steps in sequence  b) you draw the finished thing  c) you name the ingredients only
**Answer: a) you retell the steps in sequence.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ዝርዝር (zirzir) is —
a) the final fair copy  b) the outline you draft before composing  c) a list of new words
**Answer: b) the outline you draft before composing.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In traditional mediation, what must be obtained before mediators are chosen for both sides?
a) the judge's verdict  b) both sides' consent to reconcile  c) the parties' oath
**Answer: b) both sides' consent to reconcile** — step 2, before mediators (step 3) and the judge (step 4).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The LAST step of the mediation process is —
a) the preliminary inquiry  b) choosing the mediation judge  c) conducting the mediation
**Answer: c) conducting the mediation** — the sitting itself, where the parties swear an oath confirming the decision.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In a process chain, each arrow is…
a) One step, in order  b) A new food  c) A question
**Answer: a) One step, in order** — Grade 2 p.108.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The model chain ends with…
a) ቅቤ ይወጣል — butter comes out  b) ወተት ይታለባል  c) The buying
**Answer: a) ቅቤ ይወጣል — butter comes out** — the milk chain, Grade 2 p.108.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** If a step is missing from your chain, the chain…
a) Breaks  b) Gets shorter only  c) Still works the same
**Answer: a) Breaks** — that is how you check it.
