# Video script — How-To Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

We are making a salad. But my steps are all mixed!

Serve it. Wash it. Chop it. — No! Wrong order.
First: **wash**. Second: **chop**. Third: **mix**.
Then oil and salt and lemon. Last: **serve**.

A how-to text is steps in order. Skip a step — it breaks.
Swap two steps — it breaks.

And before I write? I make my **ዝርዝር** — my outline.
My topic. My points. Short lines.
Then the writing grows from the plan.

Plan first. Steps in order. That is how-to writing.

And a whole real process can be written this way.
**ባህላዊ ሽምግልና** — traditional mediation. Five steps.

One: ask first. Find out what happened.
Two: get consent. Both sides agree to reconcile.
Three: choose mediators — for both sides.
Four: choose the judge.
Five: hold the mediation.

Consent before the sitting. The judge before the hearing.
Each step prepares the next. That is a process.
