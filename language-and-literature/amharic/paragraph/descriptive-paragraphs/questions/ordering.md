# Descriptive Paragraphs — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the process of writing a paragraph in its right order: write the paragraph from the plan — choose your own topic — prepare a ቢጋር (outline) — check the paragraph's topic sentence and points against the outline.
**Answer: choose your own topic → prepare a ቢጋር (outline) → write the paragraph from the plan → check the paragraph's topic sentence and points against the outline.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A ተራኪ paragraph tells a journey. Put these events in the order a narrative paragraph must keep them: they reached the river at noon — they rose before dawn and set out — they came home as the light failed — they crossed the river and rested.
**Answer: they rose before dawn and set out → they reached the river at noon → they crossed the river and rested → they came home as the light failed** — ተራኪ keeps the order of time.
