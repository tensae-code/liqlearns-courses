# Descriptive Paragraphs — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the መዘርዝሪያ ዓረፍተነገሮች of a paragraph?
**Answer: its supporting sentences — the sentences that spread out and hold up the topic sentence (ኃይል ቃል).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a ቢጋር, and when is it made?
**Answer: an outline — a plan drawn BEFORE writing, which keeps the paragraph on its main idea and in order.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a ገላጭ paragraph explain? (Name the three things.)
**Answer: what a thing is, its use, and its working/handling — as the model paragraph defines ባህል (culture).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the descriptive prose model, how is Wehni Amba's rock described, and where is its "nose"?
**Answer: the rock is like bone; the mountain's nose is in the clouds — imagery words, not flat telling.**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** How can the two paragraphs be divided?
**Answer:** By near view and far view, or by the place's look and its life — one subject, two aspects.

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** Name two traditional household items from the chapter's word-stock lists.
**Answer:** E.g. ሸክላ ድስት (a clay pot) and ብረት-ማጣድ (an iron implement).
