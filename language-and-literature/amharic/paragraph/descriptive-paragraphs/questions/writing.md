# Descriptive Paragraphs — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Here is a paragraph: "A saddle is the rider's seat. It is strapped on the horse's back. It holds the rider steady on rough ground. Riders clean and oil it so the leather lasts." Underline its ኃይል ቃል (topic sentence) and bracket one መዘርዝሪያ ዓረፍተነገር (supporting sentence).
**Answer guide: the topic sentence is "A saddle is the rider's seat" — it carries the whole idea; any of the other three sentences is a supporting sentence.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Choose a topic and prepare its ቢጋር in በዓረፍተ ነገር መልክ (sentence outline): a title line and four numbered points, each a full sentence, in the shape of "ከራምት ሲጠባ".
**Answer guide: four full-sentence points in order under the title; no point is a bare word-list (that would be ብርዕስ መልክ).**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Write a ሥዕላዊ አንቀጽ (descriptive paragraph) about a mountain, a river or a market you know, using ምስልከሳች ቃላት (imagery words) so a reader sees it — in the shape of the Tarmaber model.
**Answer guide: every sentence shows a seeable thing (shape, colour, height, mist, sound); no sentence only tells ("it is big", "it is beautiful") without showing.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Do the books' own task: write a two-paragraph descriptive text titled ብርሃን (Light).
**Answer guide: two paragraphs, each with its own topic sentence, both painted with imagery words about light.**
