# Descriptive Paragraphs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every sentence in a paragraph carries the main idea in exactly the same way.
**Answer: False** — one sentence, the topic sentence (ኃይል ቃል / መንደርደሪያ), carries the whole idea; the rest are supporting sentences (መዘርዝሪያ ዓረፍተነገሮች).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ቢጋር is written after the paragraph, as a summary of it.
**Answer: False** — the ቢጋር is prepared BEFORE writing; the paragraph grows from it. (Unpacking a finished paragraph into an outline is a checking move, not the plan's place.)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "First he rose, then he saddled the horse, then he rode out" is a ገላጭ paragraph opening.
**Answer: False** — it recounts events in time order, so it is ተራኪ; ገላጭ explains what a thing is.

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** Editing comes after the two paragraphs are composed.
**Answer: True**
