# Descriptive Paragraphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ኃይል ቃል of a paragraph is —
a) its longest sentence  b) the sentence that carries the paragraph's main idea  c) always its last sentence
**Answer: b) the sentence that carries the paragraph's main idea** — the "power sentence"; the others support it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** መንደርደሪያ ዓረፍተነገር (Grade 7's name) is —
a) a different thing from the topic sentence  b) the topic sentence that carries the paragraph's whole idea  c) a sentence that joins two paragraphs
**Answer: b) the topic sentence that carries the paragraph's whole idea** — the same sentence the Grade 5 teaching calls ኃይል ቃል.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ብርዕስ መልክ (the topic outline) holds —
a) full sentences  b) short points, not sentences  c) only pictures
**Answer: b) short points, not sentences** — as in "የአየር ብክለትና መንስኤዎቹ": 1. የአንዱስትሪዎች ጭስ, 2. ጠረንች…

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A paragraph that recounts an event in order of time, place and circumstance is built in the —
a) ገላጭ ስልት  b) ተራኪ ስልት  c) ቢጋር ስልት
**Answer: b) ተራኪ ስልት** — the narrative way; ገላጭ explains what a thing is.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A ሥዕላዊ አንቀጽ works by —
a) imagery words (ምስልከሳች ቃላት) that make the reader see the scene in the mind  b) the shortest words possible  c) listing numbers and dates
**Answer: a) imagery words (ምስልከሳች ቃላት) that make the reader see the scene in the mind** — the Tarmaber mountain way.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** In ነጭ ሸሚዝ, the describing word is…
a) ነጭ — white  b) ሸሚዝ — shirt  c) Both are things
**Answer: a) ነጭ — white** — Grade 2 p.15.

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** A describing-word phrase puts the describing word…
a) First, before the thing  b) After the thing  c) At the end of the sentence
**Answer: a) First, before the thing** — the book's model phrase.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** ካልሲ are…
a) Socks  b) Shoes  c) A towel
**Answer: a) Socks** — the book's clothing row, Grade 2 p.15.

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** The two paragraphs describe —
a) two different countries  b) the attractiveness of your school or home area  c) a story's ending
**Answer: b) the attractiveness of your school or home area** — your own school or area — described so a stranger can stand there.
