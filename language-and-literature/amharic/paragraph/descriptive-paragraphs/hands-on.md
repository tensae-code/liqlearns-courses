# Hands-on — Descriptive Paragraphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hunt the power sentence
Take any page of a book at home. Choose one paragraph. With a
pencil, underline its ኃይል ቃል (topic sentence) once and its
supporting sentences twice. Read only the underlined-once
sentence aloud: does it carry the whole paragraph?

## Outline on two cards
Pick a topic — air pollution, harvest time, your own walk to
school. On one card write its ቢጋር in ብርዕስ መልክ (short points
only). On a second card write the same plan in በዓረፍተ ነገር መልክ
(full sentences). Swap cards with a partner: can they tell
which card is which, and why?

## Unpack a paragraph
Take a paragraph you wrote last week. On a fresh page, unpack
it into a ቢጋር: ዋና ሀሳብ at the top, its points numbered 1.–4.
under it. A point you cannot find in the paragraph is a point
the paragraph never made — fix the paragraph or fix the plan.

## Paint it with words
Stand at your door and look at one scene — a hill, a market
stall, the school gate. Say three flat sentences about it
("it is big", "it is far"). Now trade each flat sentence for a
seeing sentence with ምስልከሳች ቃላት (imagery words) until a
listener who shuts their eyes can see your scene.
