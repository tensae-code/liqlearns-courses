# Video script — Descriptive Paragraphs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a paragraph. Four sentences.

One sentence carries them all. This one!
It is the **ኃይል ቃል** — the power sentence.
The Grade 7 name is **መንደርደሪያ**.
The other sentences? They hold it up. They are its supporters.

But wait — before I write, I plan.
I make a **ቢጋር** — my outline.
Short points: that is **ብርዕስ መልክ**.
Full sentences: that is **በዓረፍተ ነገር መልክ**.
Now watch the outline grow into the paragraph.

Two ways to build. **ገላጭ** explains: what is it? How does it work?
**ተራኪ** recounts: first this, then this, in the order of time.

And the finest explaining paints a picture.
**ሥዕላዊ አንቀጽ** — words that show.
The mountain. The mist. The rock like bone.
I do not tell you. I show you — and you see it.

Find the power sentence. Plan with a ቢጋር.
Choose your way. Then write so the reader sees.
