# Lesson plan — Descriptive Paragraphs

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Paragraph · **Lesson:** Descriptive Paragraphs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Paragraph (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the paragraph grown up — the topic sentence that carries the whole idea (ኃይል ቃል / መንደርደሪያ), the outline (ቢጋር) you plan before writing, the two ways a paragraph is built (ገላጭ explaining, ተራኪ recounting), and the descriptive paragraph (ሥዕላዊ አንቀጽ) that paints a picture in the reader's mind.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on First Paragraphs and How-To Writing — the learner can already write a paragraph around one idea and draft a simple outline (ዝርዝር); this lesson names the paragraph's power sentence, gives the outline its grown-up form (ቢጋር) in two formats, and sorts paragraphs by how they are built.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The power sentence — ኃይል ቃል and መንደርደሪያ** — one sentence in every paragraph carries its whole idea: the Grade 5 guide's ኃይል ቃል ("power word"/topic sentence) with its supporting sentences (መዘርዝሪያ ዓረፍተነገሮች), and the Grade 7 name for the same sentence, መንደርደሪያ ዓረፍተነገር.
2. **Plan first — the outline (ቢጋር)** — before the paragraph, prepare a ቢጋር: the Grade 6 guide's two formats, ብርዕስ መልክ (topic outline) and በዓረፍተ ነገር መልክ (sentence outline), and how a finished paragraph unpacks back into its outline (ዋና ሀሳብ + supporting points).
3. **Two ways to build a paragraph — ገላጭ and ተራኪ** — ገላጭ ስልት explains what a thing is, its use and working; ተራኪ ስልት recounts an event in order of time, place and circumstance — and how to tell one from the other.
4. **The descriptive paragraph — ሥዕላዊ አንቀጽ** — a paragraph written with imagery words (ምስልከሳች ቃላት) so the reader sees the scene in the mind: the Tarmaber mountain worked example, the task of writing ብርሃን (Light) in two paragraphs, and the bare Wehni Amba as a second model.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is
the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: First Paragraphs (paragraph/first-paragraphs) and How-To Writing (paragraph/how-to-writing). Recommended, never forced.
- The expository essay's topic-sentence work in the wider course grows out of Sub lesson 1's መንደርደሪያ.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Find the ኃይል ቃል / መንደርደሪያ of a given paragraph and name the supporting sentences (መዘርዝሪያ ዓረፍተነገሮች) that hold it up.
2. Prepare a ቢጋር before writing — in ብርዕስ መልክ (topic outline) or በዓረፍተ ነገር መልክ (sentence outline) — and unpack a finished paragraph back into its outline.
3. Say whether a paragraph is ገላጭ (explaining) or ተራኪ (recounting), and say why.
4. Write a ሥዕላዊ አንቀጽ — a descriptive paragraph whose imagery words (ምስልከሳች ቃላት) make the reader see the scene.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A paragraph from any book you are reading, to hunt topic sentences in.

## If the learner is stuck
- Every sentence looks equal → ask: "if I may keep only ONE sentence, which one still tells me what the whole paragraph is about?" That one is the power sentence.
- The outline turns into the whole paragraph → shrink it: a topic outline holds short points, not sentences; if a point has a full stop and a verb doing a full job, it belongs to the sentence outline — or to the paragraph itself.
- ገላጭ and ተራኪ blur → ask what the paragraph DOES: does it tell you what a thing is and how it works (ገላጭ), or does it walk you through what happened, in time order (ተራኪ)?
- Description comes out flat ("the mountain is big") → trade telling words for seeing words: what does it look like, what stands where, what would your eye meet first? That is the ምስልከሳች ቃላት work.
