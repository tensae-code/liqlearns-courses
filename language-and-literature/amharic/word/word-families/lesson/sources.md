Honesty notes: (1) The webs and the swap method rest on teacher-guide evidence (the guides print the webs' word sets and the method); the synonym/antonym sets themselves are placed in the student textbooks of Grades 2, 3 and 4, so the lesson is pupil-taught in Ethiopia through its sets and teacher-evidenced in its webs and method — each part is labelled in the table. (2) The G1 guide's file is partial (it ends at printed p.35); only its placed Unit 1 and Unit 3 pages are used. (3) The እርዝ web's satellite words are taught exactly as the G4 guide prints them (ታታሪ, ትንህ, ጠንካራ, ብርቱ); the placements give no English gloss for ትንህ, so the lesson prints none. (4) Pictures 8453 (እርዝ web) and 8454 (ቤተሰብ web) are drawn webs made for this lesson from the placed word sets and were looked at before filing. (5) This lesson grows — and does not replace — word/same-and-opposite-words (Words That Go Together), which teaches the pairs one at a time from the pre-primary workbook.
