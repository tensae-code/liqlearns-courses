# Word Families and Webs — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four words that hang off እርዝ in its web.
**Answer:** ታታሪ, ትንህ, ጠንካራ, ብርቱ.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A sentence reads "ቁርሌን በላሁ" with በላሁ underlined. Do the swap and write the whole sentence.
**Answer:** ቁርሌን ተመገብኩ። — በላሁ is replaced by its synonym
ተመገብኩ; nothing else moves.
