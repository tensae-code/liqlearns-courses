# Word Families and Webs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ተመሳሳይ ፍቺ (synonyms) are —
a) words carrying the same idea  b) words carrying opposite ideas  c) words in alphabetical order
**Answer: a)** — two words, one idea (ወረፋ/ተራ).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The antonym (ተቃራኒ ፍቺ) of ቅርብ (near) is —
a) አለመጠራጠር  b) ሩቅ  c) ክፍት
**Answer: b) ሩቅ** — near ↔ far, from the Grade 2 set.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The synonym of መተማመን (to trust) is —
a) ተነሳ  b) አለመጠራጠር  c) ሞኝ
**Answer: b) አለመጠራጠር** — "not to doubt", from the Grade 3 set.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** መጉዳት (to harm) has the antonym —
a) መበደል  b) መጥቀም  c) ብልህ
**Answer: b) መጥቀም** — to benefit. (መበደል is its synonym.)

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the እርዝ web, the word at the CENTRE is —
a) ጠንካራ  b) እርዝ  c) ብርቱ
**Answer: b) እርዝ** — length itself sits in the middle;
ጠንካራ and ብርቱ hang off it.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Swap በላሁ for its synonym in "ቁርሌን በላሁ" and the sentence becomes —
a) ቁርሌን ሄድኩ  b) ቁርሌን ተመገብኩ  c) ቁርሌን አልበላሁም
**Answer: b)** — only the word changes; the sentence stands
and means the same.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** ወዳጅ is the synonym of which word?
a) ጓደኛ — friend  b) ሰንሰለት  c) ጥላቻ
**Answer: a) ጓደኛ — friend** — the Wise Girl matching set.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** What does ዘመን የማይሽረው mean?
a) "That time does not erase"  b) "That time builds"  c) "Quickly forgotten"
**Answer: a) "That time does not erase"** — the phrase at the heart of The Wise Girl.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** ጥላቻ (hatred) matches which antonym?
a) ፍቅር — love  b) ያናጋል  c) ውጫዊ
**Answer: a) ፍቅር — love** — the Littering essay's antonym set.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** ጨዋታዎች means —
a) school books  b) playthings and games  c) cooking pots
**Answer: b) playthings and games** — ጨዋታዎች — playthings/games.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** መጫወቻ are —
a) play materials  b) winter clothes  c) food items
**Answer: a) play materials** — መጫወቻ — play materials.

**12. (Sub lesson 5)** The words are learned by —
a) using them in talk about your own friends and play  b) copying them once, silently  c) translating them away
**Answer: a) using them in talk about your own friends and play** — Use them about your own life.
