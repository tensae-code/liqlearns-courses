# Word Families and Webs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ተመሳሳይ ፍቺ (synonyms) are —
a) words carrying the same idea  b) words carrying opposite ideas  c) words in alphabetical order
**Answer: a)** — two words, one idea (ወረፋ/ተራ).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The antonym (ተቃራኒ ፍቺ) of ቅርብ (near) is —
a) አለመጠራጠር  b) ሩቅ  c) ክፍት
**Answer: b) ሩቅ** — near ↔ far, from the Grade 2 set.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The synonym of መተማመን (to trust) is —
a) ተነሳ  b) አለመጠራጠር  c) ሞኝ
**Answer: b) አለመጠራጠር** — "not to doubt", from the Grade 3 set.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** መጉዳት (to harm) has the antonym —
a) መበደል  b) መጥቀም  c) ብልህ
**Answer: b) መጥቀም** — to benefit. (መበደል is its synonym.)

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the እርዝ web, the word at the CENTRE is —
a) ጠንካራ  b) እርዝ  c) ብርቱ
**Answer: b) እርዝ** — length itself sits in the middle;
ጠንካራ and ብርቱ hang off it.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Swap በላሁ for its synonym in "ቁርሌን በላሁ" and the sentence becomes —
a) ቁርሌን ሄድኩ  b) ቁርሌን ተመገብኩ  c) ቁርሌን አልበላሁም
**Answer: b)** — only the word changes; the sentence stands
and means the same.
