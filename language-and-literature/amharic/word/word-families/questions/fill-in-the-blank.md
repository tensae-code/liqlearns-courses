# Word Families and Webs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** In the family web, the centre word is ______.
**Answer: ቤተሰብ** — family itself, with አባት, እናት, ልጅ and the
rest hanging off it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two school words from the Grade 1 school set are መጽሐፍ (book) and ______ (blackboard).
**Answer: ጥቁር ሰሌዳ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the swap method, you change ONLY the ______ word, and the rest of the sentence stays where it was.
**Answer: underlined** (the one word marked for swapping).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** አስተዋይ (wise) has the synonym ብልህ and the antonym ______.
**Answer: ሞኝ** — foolish.
