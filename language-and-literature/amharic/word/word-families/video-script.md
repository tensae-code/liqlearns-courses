# Video script — Word Families and Webs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words have families.

Some words mean the same. **በላ** — he ate. **ተመገበ** — he ate.
Same idea, two ways. These are **ተመሳሳይ ፍቺ** — synonyms.

Some words face away. **ቅርብ** — near. **ሩቅ** — far.
**ዝግ** — closed. **ክፍት** — open. These are **ተቃራኒ ፍቺ** —
antonyms.

A big family is drawn as a web.
In the middle: **ቤተሰብ** — family.
Around it: **አባት** father, **እናት** mother, **ልጅ** child,
**ወንድም** brother, **እህት** sister, **አያት** grandmother,
**አክስት** aunt, **ሰራተኛ** worker.
Start in the middle. Walk out on each line. Say the word.

Now the swap. A sentence: **ቁርሌን በላሁ።**
Take out በላሁ. Put in its partner: ተመገብኩ.
**ቁርሌን ተመገብኩ።**
The sentence stands. The meaning stands. Only the word changed.

Own the family, and you can say everything twice.
