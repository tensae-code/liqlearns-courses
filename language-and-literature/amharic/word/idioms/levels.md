# Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What an idiom is
Pass mark: define ፈሊጣዊ አነጋገር in your own words — words combined gain a meaning different from the dictionary meaning of each word — and explain why ሆድና ጀርባ cannot be read word by word.

## Sub lesson 2 — Idioms of the body
Pass mark: give both meanings of ሆድና ጀርባ (belly and back / people who do not get along) and of እጅ አጠረው (his hand shortened / he fell into difficulty), and use each saying about the right situation.

## Sub lesson 3 — Idioms that describe a person
Pass mark: give the real meaning of ጥራዝ ነጠቅ (superficial, lacking real knowledge) and ጥቁር እንግዳ (a complete stranger) and match each saying to the person it describes.
