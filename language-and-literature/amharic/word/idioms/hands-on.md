# Hands-on — Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two cards, one saying
Write ሆድና on one card and ጀርባ on another. Hold them apart and read them word by word: belly, back. Now slide the cards together — the same join as GIF 9019 — and say the saying's real meaning aloud: people who do not get along. Do the same with two cards for እጅ አጠረው: apart, the words; together, the meaning.

## Act the picture, say the meaning
With a partner, stand back to back, arms folded, not speaking: you are the picture of ሆድና ጀርባ. Then one of you reaches for a cup placed just too far away, hand falling short: እጅ አጠረው. The watcher names the saying each time before you change pose.

## Who fits the saying?
Write the four sayings on four cards. A partner describes a person or a pair — two neighbours who never agree; a man whose work collapsed; a student who only shines on the surface; a visitor nobody knows at all. Lay the fitting card down for each description and say the saying aloud.
