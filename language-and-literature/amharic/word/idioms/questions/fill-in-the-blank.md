# Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or saying.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When words combine and gain a meaning different from the dictionary meaning of each word, the saying is called ______ አነጋገር.
**Answer: ፈሊጣዊ**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A person whose learning is only a surface shine, with no real knowledge under it, is described by the saying ______.
**Answer: ጥራዝ ነጠቅ**
