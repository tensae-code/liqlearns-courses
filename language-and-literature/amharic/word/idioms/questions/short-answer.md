# Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Say እጅ አጠረው word by word, then say what the saying really means.
**Answer:** Word by word: "his hand shortened". The saying really means: he fell into difficulty.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A learner shines in talk but, tested, knows almost nothing underneath. Which saying of this lesson fits him, and what does it mean?
**Answer:** ጥራዝ ነጠቅ — superficial, lacking real knowledge.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A visitor arrives whom nobody knows at all — not a friend of a friend, wholly unknown. Which saying fits him, and what does it mean?
**Answer:** ጥቁር እንግዳ — a complete stranger.
