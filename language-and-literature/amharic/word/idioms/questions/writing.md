# Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New — writing

Write what is asked.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Write one sentence about a person who fell into difficulty, using እጅ አጠረው. Then write one sentence about a complete stranger, using ጥቁር እንግዳ.
**Answer (model shape):** the first sentence must use እጅ አጠረው to mean "fell into difficulty" (not a shortened hand); the second must use ጥቁር እንግዳ to mean a wholly unknown person.
