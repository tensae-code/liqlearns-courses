# Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If you read an idiom word by word, you always reach its real meaning.
**Answer: False** — word by word, ሆድና ጀርባ is only "belly and back"; the real meaning (people who do not get along) belongs to the whole saying.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** ጥራዝ ነጠቅ praises a person for deep, real knowledge.
**Answer: False** — ጥራዝ ነጠቅ means superficial, lacking real knowledge.
