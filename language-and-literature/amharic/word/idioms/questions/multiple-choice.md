# Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An idiom (ፈሊጣዊ አነጋገር) is a saying where —
a) every word keeps its own dictionary meaning  b) words combine and gain a meaning different from the dictionary meaning of each word  c) the longest word carries the meaning
**Answer: b) words combine and gain a meaning different from the dictionary meaning of each word.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To understand an idiom, you must read —
a) the whole saying  b) only the first word  c) each word alone, one by one
**Answer: a) the whole saying** — the meaning lives in the combination.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ሆድና ጀርባ really means —
a) a pain in the belly and back  b) people who do not get along  c) two parts of one body
**Answer: b) people who do not get along** — word by word it only says "belly and back".

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** እጅ አጠረው really means —
a) his hand became shorter by measure  b) he fell into difficulty  c) he cut his hand
**Answer: b) he fell into difficulty** — word by word it only says "his hand shortened".

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ጥቁር እንግዳ is said of —
a) a complete stranger  b) a close friend  c) a dark night
**Answer: a) a complete stranger** — someone wholly unknown to you.
