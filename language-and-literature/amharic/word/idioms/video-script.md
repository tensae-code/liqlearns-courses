# Video script — Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some sayings are a trap for careful readers.

**ሆድና ጀርባ.**
Word by word: belly and back.
But the saying is not about a body.
It means: **people who do not get along.**

Words together can gain a new meaning —
a meaning no single word carries.
That is an idiom: **ፈሊጣዊ አነጋገር**.

**እጅ አጠረው.**
Word by word: his hand shortened.
The saying means: **he fell into difficulty.**

**ጥራዝ ነጠቅ** — a person whose knowledge is only surface.
**ጥቁር እንግዳ** — a complete stranger.

So read the whole saying.
Never word by word.
The meaning lives in the combination.

Your turn. Say one idiom. Say what it really means.
