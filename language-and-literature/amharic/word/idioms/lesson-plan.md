# Lesson plan — Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Idioms — When Words Together Mean Something New
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an idiom (ፈሊጣዊ አነጋገር) is a saying whose words, combined, gain a meaning different from the dictionary meaning of each word — ሆድና ጀርባ is not a belly and a back but people who do not get along — so a saying must be read whole, never word by word.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What a Word Means — the learner already knows a word can carry a figurative meaning (ፍካሬያዊ ፍቺ) beyond its direct one. It needs nothing else. This lesson rests on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What an idiom is** — ፈሊጣዊ አነጋገሮች: when words combine they gain a meaning different from the dictionary meaning of each word; the test case ሆድና ጀርባ.
2. **Idioms of the body** — ሆድና ጀርባ (lit. "belly and back" = people who do not get along) and እጅ አጠረው ("his hand shortened" = he fell into difficulty).
3. **Idioms that describe a person** — ጥራዝ ነጠቅ (superficial, lacking real knowledge) and ጥቁር እንግዳ (a complete stranger).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What a Word Means (figurative meanings, ፍካሬያዊ ፍቺ). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what an idiom is: words combined, a meaning different from each word's dictionary meaning.
2. Give the real meaning of ሆድና ጀርባ and እጅ አጠረው, and say what the words say word by word.
3. Give the real meaning of ጥራዝ ነጠቅ and ጥቁር እንግዳ and use each about the right kind of person.
4. Read a saying whole instead of word by word, and say why the word-by-word reading fails.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — the learner reads ሆድና ጀርባ word by word and guesses what it could mean about two people (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — what an idiom is; the two body idioms; the two person idioms (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 9020]: a picture of the idioms chart — each saying, its word-by-word meaning where the guide gives one, and its real meaning — the student covers the right column and says each saying's real meaning from memory.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 9039]: a GIF of the word-cards ሆድና and ጀርባ sliding together into one saying, over two people with a crack between them — the student says the saying's real meaning as the words join.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the four sayings one by one — ሆድና ጀርባ, እጅ አጠረው, ጥራዝ ነጠቅ, ጥቁር እንግዳ (slot only — no audio file is made in this build) → the student gives each saying's real meaning after hearing it.
3. **Say it to your companion** — define an idiom, split ሆድና ጀርባ into its two meanings, and describe people for your companion to match with ጥራዝ ነጠቅ / ጥቁር እንግዳ (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — "Idioms: when words together mean something new" in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank, matching, short-answer, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write each saying with its two meanings and use two sayings in sentences (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads an idiom word by word → go back to ሆድና ጀርባ: ask what a belly and a back have to do with two people who quarrel — nothing; the meaning lives in the whole saying.
- Mixes up the two body idioms → use the picture: ሆድና ጀርባ is two people turned away from each other; እጅ አጠረው is one person's hand that can no longer reach.
- Thinks ጥቁር እንግዳ is about colour → the saying measures how unknown a person is: a complete stranger, wholly foreign to you.
