# Children's Rights in a Table

## Do it first

Try the first task in Sub lesson 1 before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rights table — መብት

In the child-labour chapter's vocabulary work, Grade 8 builds a rights bigar around the word መብት (a right): ሰብዓዊ (human rights), የመኖር (the right to live), ቅጣት (punishment — and its limits), የህሊና (of conscience), የገንዘብ (of property/money), መታደግ (being rescued/helped), አረጋዊያንን… (elders' rights) — each right named, and beside it what upholds it. A right written in a table is a duty made visible: for every right in the left column, somebody in the right column must act.

## Sub lesson 2 — The influence table — ተፅዕኖ

The second bigar maps ተፅዕኖ — influence — on a child: the influence of family, of a peer friend, of culture, of a teacher and of a neighbour, each in its own cell, with what each influence does written beside it; and a final column for what government upholds (ያስከብራል). The two tables belong together: rights say what a child must have; influences say what actually shapes the child. Where an influence harms a right — a neighbour who sets a child to heavy work, a friend who leads a child from school — the tables show the clash plainly, and plain sight is the start of remedy.

## Say it to your companion
