# Children's Rights in a Table — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two rights from the table.
**Answer:** Any two of: human rights (ሰብዓዊ), the right to live (የመኖር), of conscience (የህሊና), of property (የገንዘብ), being helped/rescued (መታደግ).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the government's column in the table say?
**Answer:** What government upholds (ያስከብራል) — the rights it enforces.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does መታደግ name in the table?
**Answer:** Being rescued / helped — the right to be helped out of harm.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What verb names what government does for rights in the table?
**Answer:** ያስከብራል — it upholds / enforces them.
