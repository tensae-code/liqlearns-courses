# Children's Rights in a Table — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** መብት means —
a) a punishment  b) a right  c) a wage
**Answer: b) a right** — መብት is a right — the table lists a child's and others' rights.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ተፅዕኖ means —
a) influence  b) punishment  c) a school fee
**Answer: a) influence** — ተፅዕኖ is influence — what family, friends, culture, teachers and neighbours do to shape a child.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** የመኖር in the rights table is —
a) the right to live  b) the right to travel  c) a duty
**Answer: a) the right to live** — የመኖር መብት — the right to live — stands in the table.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is NOT one of the five influences in the table?
a) family  b) the weather  c) culture
**Answer: b) the weather** — family, peer friend, culture, teacher, neighbour — the weather is not among them.
