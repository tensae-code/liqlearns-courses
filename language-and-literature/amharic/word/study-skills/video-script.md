# Video script — Study Skills

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before I read, I make my chart. Three columns.

**K** — what do I KNOW already?
**W** — what do I WANT to know?
Then I read… and **L** — what did I LEARN?

Now a web! One idea in the middle.
Its words hang around it, like branches. That is a **ቢጋር**.

And a chain: the rain fell — **ዝናብ ዘነበ** —
so the flood came — **ጎርፍ ተከሰተ**.
Cause… effect… cause… effect…

Last, a game! One fidel in each box.
Across… or down… Fill the crossword!

Charts, webs, chains, games — tools that make reading yours.
