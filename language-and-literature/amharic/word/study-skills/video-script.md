# Video script — Study Skills

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before I read, I make my chart. Three columns.

**K** — what do I KNOW already?
**W** — what do I WANT to know?
Then I read… and **L** — what did I LEARN?

Now a web! One idea in the middle.
Its words hang around it, like branches. That is a **ቢጋር**.

And a chain: the rain fell — **ዝናብ ዘነበ** —
so the flood came — **ጎርፍ ተከሰተ**.
Cause… effect… cause… effect…

Last, a game! One fidel in each box.
Across… or down… Fill the crossword!

Now a table! Rows… and columns…
The question names a row. The question asks a column.
Where they cross — that cell is the answer!
Kora needs five hundred to eight hundred of rain.
Negus is ready in one hundred and five days.

And when I LISTEN — my notebook is ready BEFORE the voice.
Not every word! Main points only.
One word… a short phrase… one big sentence.

And my chart grows up with me.
K… W… L… — for farmers, for palaces, for the lives of writers.

Charts, webs, chains, games, tables, notes — tools that make reading yours.

And every skill has three stages.
Listening: **ቅድመ** — before. **በማዳመጥ ጊዜ** — during. **ድህረ** — after.
Reading: warm up — two to five minutes. Never give the message away!
Then read silently — your own book — ten to fifteen minutes.
Then the longest stage: questions.
Speaking: **መግቢያ** — begin. **ማለማመጃ** — practise.
**ማፍለቂያ** — produce your own words.

One book. Two weeks. A little every day.
Then a test — ten marks, fifteen marks. **መጠነ ሰፊ ንባብ**.

Listen well — seven ways. Decide to listen.
Drop your agenda. Be curious. Eyes on the speaker.
Hear the WHOLE message. Be patient. Respect a different idea.

And at the end of every chapter — questions! **የክለሳ ጥያቄዎች**.
Ask them aloud. Answer in full.
Where the answer will not come — read there again.
