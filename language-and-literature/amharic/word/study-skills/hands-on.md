# Hands-on — Study Skills

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The door chart
Put a big KWL chart on the wall for one week. Every text anyone
reads at home gets a row: title on the chart before reading
(K, W), and the L filled after. At the week's end, read the L
column aloud together — it is the week's learning, in your own
words.

## Web of our street
Draw a concept web with your street or village at the centre.
Branches: the people, the work, the water, the roads, the
animals. Each person adds one branch word and says one sentence
from it. A word that joins nothing is questioned kindly: where
does it truly belong?

## Cause hunt
On a walk or in the kitchen, hunt causes: the pot boiled
because…; the path is muddy because… Each hunter must build a
chain of three: rain → mud → late to school. Chains are read
aloud and tested: did each link MAKE the next happen?

## Paper crossword
Copy the lesson's crossword grid onto a big sheet, empty.
In pairs, fill it from the clue answers (across and down lists
kept beside you). Swap sheets and check each other against the
book's filled grid — every fidel in its box.
