# Study Skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a KWL chart, when is the L column filled?
a) before reading  b) after reading — what I learned  c) never; it stays empty
**Answer: b) after reading — what I learned.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The W column holds —
a) what I want to know — my questions before reading  b) words I cannot read  c) what the teacher wants
**Answer: a) what I want to know — my questions before reading.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A concept web (ቢጋር) is NOT just a list because —
a) it is drawn in a circle  b) every branch must join the centre idea  c) it uses Amharic words
**Answer: b) every branch must join the centre idea.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "ዝናብ ዘነበ → ጎርፍ ተከሰተ" is a cause-and-effect chain because —
a) the flood is bigger than the rain  b) the rain falling MADE the flood come  c) both are about water
**Answer: b) the rain falling MADE the flood come.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Rule 1 of the fidel crossword is —
a) one word per box  b) one fidel per box  c) one answer per row
**Answer: b) one fidel per box.**
