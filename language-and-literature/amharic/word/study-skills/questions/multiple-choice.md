# Study Skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a KWL chart, when is the L column filled?
a) before reading  b) after reading — what I learned  c) never; it stays empty
**Answer: b) after reading — what I learned.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The W column holds —
a) what I want to know — my questions before reading  b) words I cannot read  c) what the teacher wants
**Answer: a) what I want to know — my questions before reading.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A concept web (ቢጋር) is NOT just a list because —
a) it is drawn in a circle  b) every branch must join the centre idea  c) it uses Amharic words
**Answer: b) every branch must join the centre idea.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "ዝናብ ዘነበ → ጎርፍ ተከሰተ" is a cause-and-effect chain because —
a) the flood is bigger than the rain  b) the rain falling MADE the flood come  c) both are about water
**Answer: b) the rain falling MADE the flood come.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Rule 1 of the fidel crossword is —
a) one word per box  b) one fidel per box  c) one answer per row
**Answer: b) one fidel per box.**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** From the teff table, ኮራ (Kora) needs rainfall of —
a) 1000–1200 mm  b) 500–800 mm  c) 150–700 mm
**Answer: b) 500–800 mm.**

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** The headache table's questions (who used most in 2008, lowest in 2011) are answered —
a) from the table's cells  b) from your own experience  c) from the story before the reading
**Answer: a) from the table's cells.**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** Good listening notes hold —
a) every word that was said  b) main points only, at word, phrase and sentence level  c) only the first sentence
**Answer: b) main points only, at word, phrase and sentence level.**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** The higher books use the KWL chart to open —
a) only the civet reading  b) bigger readings too — the farmer essay, Fasilides' palace, a biography  c) no readings after Grade 4
**Answer: b) bigger readings too — the farmer essay, Fasilides' palace, a biography.**

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** In the three stages of reading, the during-reading stage is —
a) the teacher reading the text aloud to the class  b) silent reading, each learner with their own book, for 10–15 minutes  c) questions and correction
**Answer: b)** — if one voice reads aloud, the class has listened, not read; questions and correction belong to the post stage.

**11. (Sub lesson 9)** In extensive reading (መጠነ ሰፊ ንባብ), the test on the book's content is worth —
a) 2 marks  b) 10 or 15 marks  c) no marks at all
**Answer: b) 10 or 15 marks** — counted like a monthly or semester test: readers gain them, non-readers lose them.

**12. (Sub lesson 10)** Which one IS one of the seven strategies of effective listening?
a) answer before the speaker finishes, to save time  b) avoid eye contact  c) hear the whole message before answering
**Answer: c)** — never interrupt; the whole message first.

**13. (Sub lesson 11)** In the higher classes, every chapter ends with —
a) oral revision questions (የክለሳ ጥያቄዎች)  b) a song  c) the next chapter's first page
**Answer: a)** — the closing check, worked through before anyone moves on.

**14. (Sub lesson 12)** ድንች (potato) goes in which column?
a) Plants eaten for their root  b) Plants eaten for their fruit  c) Plants not eaten
**Answer: a) Plants eaten for their root** — Grade 1 p.106.

**15. (Sub lesson 12)** ፌስታል (plastic) waste goes with things that…
a) Turn back to soil  b) Do not turn back to soil  c) Are eaten
**Answer: b) Do not turn back to soil** — Grade 2 p.69.

**16. (Sub lesson 12)** In the Grade 2 food web at p.108, እርጎ and አይብ are…
a) Animal products  b) Plant products  c) Not foods
**Answer: a) Animal products** — the classifying web, Grade 2 p.108.

**17. (Sub lesson 13)** What comes FIRST in the three-part reading frame?
a) Prediction from the title and picture  b) The post-reading questions  c) The retelling
**Answer: a) Prediction from the title and picture** — predict → text → questions.

**18. (Sub lesson 13)** In guess-the-word, the explainer may use all of these EXCEPT what?
a) Saying the word itself  b) A synonym  c) An action
**Answer: a) Saying the word itself** — synonym, antonym, picture, action — never the word.

**19. (Sub lesson 13)** On the attraction web, Sof Omar cave goes on which branch?
a) Natural  b) Cultural  c) Historical
**Answer: a) Natural** — caves and lakes are natural attractions.


**20. (Sub lesson 14)** In a cloze, each gap is decided by —
a) the word's length  b) grammar and sense together  c) the bank's order
**Answer: b) grammar and sense together** — grammar and the paragraph's sense decide each gap.

**21. (Sub lesson 15)** The child-labour cloze paragraph argues that —
a) exploitation cannot be stopped by anyone  b) government and people must rise together to rescue children  c) children should work longer hours
**Answer: b) government and people must rise together to rescue children** — its bank builds the argument for joint rescue — እርምጃ (measures) must be taken.

**22. (Sub lesson 16)** ውቅያኖስ is —
a) a pond  b) an ocean  c) a well
**Answer: b) an ocean** — the ocean is the ladder's top — it holds the seas.
