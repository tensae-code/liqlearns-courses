# Study Skills — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Draw a KWL chart for a text you are about to read. Fill K and W now (at least two entries each).
**Answer guide: K holds only true prior knowledge; W holds real questions the text might answer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Build a concept web around ጎረቤት (neighbour) with at least four branches, then write two sentences from the web.
**Answer guide: every branch joins the centre; the sentences use branch words.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write a cause → problem → solution web for a dirty water point near home: three boxes, arrows between.
**Answer guide: the cause truly makes the problem; the solution answers the cause.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the crossword's across list and down list from the lesson, with their numbers.
**Answer guide: across — 1 ጀግና, 3 ንብ, 4 አክሱም, 7 ሰር, 8 ሳሙና, 9 ትራስ; down — 2 ግንብኛ, 4 አክስት, 5 ኬራር, 6 መቀጨ, 10 ሱሪ.**
