# Study Skills — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Draw a KWL chart for a text you are about to read. Fill K and W now (at least two entries each).
**Answer guide: K holds only true prior knowledge; W holds real questions the text might answer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Build a concept web around ጎረቤት (neighbour) with at least four branches, then write two sentences from the web.
**Answer guide: every branch joins the centre; the sentences use branch words.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write a cause → problem → solution web for a dirty water point near home: three boxes, arrows between.
**Answer guide: the cause truly makes the problem; the solution answers the cause.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the crossword's across list and down list from the lesson, with their numbers.
**Answer guide: across — 1 ጀግና, 3 ንብ, 4 አክሱም, 7 ሰር, 8 ሳሙና, 9 ትራስ; down — 2 ግንብኛ, 4 አክስት, 5 ኬራር, 6 መቀጨ, 10 ሱሪ.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Using Picture 8860's teff table, write a comparison paragraph about two varieties (for example ኬና and ኮራ): compare their rainfall needs, days to maturity and farmer-field yields.
**Answer guide: every figure in the paragraph comes from a cell of the table (Kena 1000–1200 mm, 110–134 days, 13–23 q/ha; Kora 500–800 mm, 110–117 days, 20–22 q/ha).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Have a short text read to you once. Write your notes for it at the three levels: one key word, two phrases, one sentence.
**Answer guide: the word is a true key word; the phrases hold main points in a few words; the sentence carries the biggest point only.**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Draw a KWL chart for a hard text you are about to read — on farmers, a palace, or a person's life. Fill K and W now, with at least two hard W questions.
**Answer guide: K holds true prior knowledge; W holds questions the text must work to answer; L is left ready to fill after reading.**
