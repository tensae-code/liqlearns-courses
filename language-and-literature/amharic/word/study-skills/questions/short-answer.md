# Study Skills — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What should the L column do to your W questions?
**Answer: answer them — from the reading.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one centre word the books build a concept web around.
**Answer: any one of — ጎረቤት (neighbour); good/bad behaviour; cotton → clothes; the foods; the forest words.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the test question for a cause arrow?
**Answer: did this MAKE that happen?**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What do shaded boxes in the crossword take?
**Answer: no fidel — they stay empty (rule 3).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** From the teff table: how many days does ንጉሥ (Negus) take to mature, and what is ዱከም (Dukem)'s yield on a research station?
**Answer: Negus — 105 days. Dukem — 24–34 quintals per hectare on the research station.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What must be prepared BEFORE the listening starts?
**Answer: notebook and pen.**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** In the farmer reading's KWL chart, the essay contrasts which two farmers?
**Answer: the diligent farmer (ምስጉን አርሶአደር) and the lazy farmer (ሰነፍ አርሶአደር).**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Name the three stages of a speaking lesson.
**Answer: introduction (መግቢያ) — set the context, prepare the mind; practice (ማለማመጃ) — practise in the context, individually, in pairs, in groups; production (ማፍለቂያ) — learners produce their own words and grammar for their own ideas.**

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** How long are learners given to read the extensive-reading book, and how is that time decided?
**Answer: two weeks or more, as homework — the time is adjusted to the learners' language background and to how reachable the book is.**

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** Name any three of the seven strategies of effective listening.
**Answer: any three of — decide to listen; drop your own agenda and prejudice; be curious and ask open questions; keep eye contact and read visual cues; hear the whole message before answering; be patient while a speaker finds words; respect the right to express a differing idea.**

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** When do the revision questions happen, and what are they for?
**Answer: at the end of every chapter, before moving on — they check what the chapter taught while there is still time to re-read where an answer will not come.**
