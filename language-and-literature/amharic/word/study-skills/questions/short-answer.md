# Study Skills — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What should the L column do to your W questions?
**Answer: answer them — from the reading.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one centre word the books build a concept web around.
**Answer: any one of — ጎረቤት (neighbour); good/bad behaviour; cotton → clothes; the foods; the forest words.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the test question for a cause arrow?
**Answer: did this MAKE that happen?**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What do shaded boxes in the crossword take?
**Answer: no fidel — they stay empty (rule 3).**
