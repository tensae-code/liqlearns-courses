# Study Skills — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Guesses about a topic belong in the K column of a KWL chart.
**Answer: False** — K holds what you truly know; guesses and hopes go in W as questions.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** If one event only happened AFTER another, the arrow between them is a cause arrow.
**Answer: False** — that is a sequence; a cause arrow needs the first to MAKE the second happen.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** In the crossword, letters already placed are starting points for other answers.
**Answer: True** — rule 4, exactly as the guide prints it.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** A data-table answer may come from general knowledge, as long as it sounds right.
**Answer: False** — table questions are answered from the cells: named row, asked column, crossing cell.

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** A note at sentence level should be used for every point heard.
**Answer: False** — sentence level is for the biggest points only; most points are held at word or phrase level.

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** A pre-reading chart with only "what I know / what I want to know" is the K and W of the same KWL chart.
**Answer: True** — the two columns are the chart's first two columns, filled before reading.

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** The pre-reading stage should tell learners the text's message in advance, so the reading is easier.
**Answer: False** — the message is never revealed in advance; pre-reading is a 2–5 minute warm-up (a question, joke, story, poem or mini-drama) only.

**8. (Sub lesson 9)** In extensive reading, readers and non-readers finish with the same marks.
**Answer: False** — the book test is worth 10 or 15 marks, counted like a monthly or semester test: readers gain the marks, non-readers lose them.

**9. (Sub lesson 10)** A good listener answers quickly, before the speaker finishes, to keep the talk moving.
**Answer: False** — strategy 5: hear the whole message before answering; never interrupt. Strategy 6 adds patience while a speaker finds words.

**10. (Sub lesson 11)** Revision questions are asked only once, at the end of the school year.
**Answer: False** — they close every chapter (የክለሳ ጥያቄዎች), before moving on to the next one.

**11. (Sub lesson 14)** You read the whole paragraph before filling any gap.
**Answer: True**

**12. (Sub lesson 15)** አደብ ገዛ in the ጥሪ bank is the poem's call — 'take heed / discipline yourself'.
**Answer: True**

**13. (Sub lesson 16)** A ጉድጓድ (well) is dug; a ምንጭ (spring) rises by itself.
**Answer: True**

**14. (Sub lesson 18)** In the Grade 7 guide's principles, learners are taught to work out a word's meaning from the text itself.
**Answer: True**

**15. (Sub lesson 19)** አመዛዛኝ ድርሰት is the guide's term for the comparison/contrast essay.
**Answer: True**
