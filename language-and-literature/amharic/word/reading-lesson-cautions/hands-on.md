# Hands-on — Reading-Lesson Cautions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Caution cards
Write the seven cautions on seven cards. After any reading lesson at home or school, draw one card and check honestly: did today's lesson keep this caution, or break it? Write the date and one line. The cards turn a guide's list into a habit of noticing.

## The short-answer game
In pairs: one reads a short text, the other asks three questions — one true/false, one 'find the fact', one 'what do you think'. Answers may be short and spoken, and no answer may be stopped in the middle. Afterwards, say which caution was hardest to keep, and why.
