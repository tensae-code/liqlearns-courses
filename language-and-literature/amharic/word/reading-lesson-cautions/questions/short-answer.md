# Reading-Lesson Cautions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three cautions that guard the reading itself.
**Answer:** Do not read aloud or make learners read aloud; do not let the reading lesson become a vocabulary lesson; do not rely only on true/false and multiple choice.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four cautions that guard how answers are received.
**Answer:** Encourage guessing word meanings; do not stop learners mid-answer for grammar faults; accept short spoken answers; do not force complete sentences.
