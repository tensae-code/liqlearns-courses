# Video script — Reading-Lesson Cautions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A reading lesson can be spoiled.
Here is how NOT to spoil it.

Do not read the text aloud.
Do not make the learners read aloud.
Their eyes must do the work.

Do not teach every word.
That is a vocabulary lesson — not reading.

Not only true or false!
Vary the questions.

And when a learner answers —
let the answer live.
Short is fine. A guess is work.
Grammar can wait its turn.
