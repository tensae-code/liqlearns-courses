# Video script — Building Words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words are built. Watch.

Two words walk together. **ፍኖተ** — a path. **ሰላም** — peace.
They join! **ፍኖተሰላም** — one word!
**አዲስ** plus **አበባ** — **አዲስአበባ**!
Two words join into one. This is a compound word.

Some words double. **ፍሬ… ፍሬ… ፍራፍሬ** — fruit, fruit everywhere!
**ቅጠል… ቅጠላቅጠል** — leaves everywhere!
Say the part twice, and the word grows.

Endings build people. **ነገር** — talk. Add **-ኛ**: **ነገረኛ** —
the talkative person!
Endings build states. **ታማኝ** — faithful. Add **-ነት**:
**ታማኝነት** — faithfulness!
Person, or state? -ኛ is the person. -ነት is the state.

And some words travelled from far away.
**ቆጮ** — from Gurage. **ሞባይል** — from English.
**ሱቅ** — from Arabic. **ቢሮ** — from French. **ፖስታ** — from
Italian.
They travelled. They stayed. Now they are Amharic words.

Join. Double. Add an ending. Welcome a traveller.
That is how words are built.
