# Lesson plan — Building Words

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Building Words
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** new words are BUILT —
two words join into one compound (ውስብስብ ሆሄያት / ጥምር ቃላት), a
sound doubles to build reduplicated words (ፍራፍሬ, ቅጠላቅጠል),
-ኛ builds the person who does a thing and -ነት builds the
state of being, and some words in Amharic travelled here from
other languages (የውስጠ ቃላት, loanwords) and keep their home
countries in their story.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Word lessons
before it — the learner reads whole words and knows fidel
joining (blending) from the Fidel title; building BIGGER words
out of whole words and endings is this lesson's own work, in
the order the Grade 2–4 books teach it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Compound words** — ውስብስብ ሆሄያት / ጥምር ቃላት: two words
   join and live as one — ፍኖተ-ሰላም → ፍኖተሰላም, አዲስአበባ,
   ባሕርዳር, ደብረማርቆስ, ሥነ-ምግባር → ሥነምግባር — and the Grade 3
   guide's placed sets (ጨፍ, ጨሪ, ብልጫ; ጭንገጠ, ጭንቅጥ, ቁጭቻ…).
2. **Words that double — reduplication** — a part of the word
   said twice builds a new word: ፍራፍሬ, ቅጠላቅጠል, as placed in
   the Grade 2 garden chapter.
3. **Doer nouns in -ኛ and state nouns in -ነት** — ነገር →
   ነገረኛ (the person full of talk) with ተንኮል, ትዕቢት, ትዕግስት,
   ቅናት; ታማኝ → ታማኝነት (faithfulness) with ኃላፊ, ወጣት, ልጅ,
   ሰው — both from the Grade 2 student book.
4. **Words that travelled — loanwords** — የውስጠ ቃላት: words
   Amharic took in from other languages, with the Grade 4
   guide's 12-row table: ቆጮ ← ጉራግኛ (Gurage), ሞባይል ←
   እንግሊዝኛ (English), ሱቅ ← አረብኛ (Arabic), ቢሮ ← ፈረንሳይኛ
   (French), ፖስታ ← ጣሊያንኛ (Italian), and the rest.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is
the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Fidel lessons on joining fidel into
  words (ማባመር, blending) — compounds join whole words the
  same way fidel join into words. Recommended, never forced.
- Words That Go Together helps: a compound's two parts are
  words the learner already owns.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a compound word is and split ፍኖተሰላም and ሥነምግባር
   back into their two words.
2. Read the placed compound sets and use one compound in a
   sentence of their own.
3. Spot a reduplicated word (ፍራፍሬ, ቅጠላቅጠል) and say how it
   is built.
4. Build a -ኛ person-word (ነገር → ነገረኛ) and a -ነት
   state-word (ታማኝ → ታማኝነት) and say what each ending does.
5. Name at least five loanwords and the language each one
   travelled from (ቆጮ, ሞባይል, ሱቅ, ቢሮ, ፖስታ…).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Two small cards or stones help for the
joining game in hands-on.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner takes the city name አዲስአበባ
   and pulls it apart into the two words hiding inside it,
   before any explanation (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — compounds and their sets; reduplicated
   words; the -ኛ and -ነት builders; loanwords and their home
   languages (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8455]: a picture of three compound
     joins — አዲስ + አበባ = አዲስአበባ, ፍኖተ + ሰላም = ፍኖተሰላም,
     ሥነ + ምግባር = ሥነምግባር — the student reads each join,
     then covers the right side and rebuilds it.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8456]: a picture of the two builders —
     ነገር → ነገረኛ under -ኛ "the person who…", ታማኝ → ታማኝነት
     under -ነት "the state of being…" — the student says which
     ending builds a person and which builds a state.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 8461]: loanwords travelling along arrows from
     their home languages (Gurage, English, Arabic, French,
     Italian) into an Amharic box — the student names each word
     and its home language as it lands.
   - ADD AUDIO in Sub lesson 4: a voice reading each loanword
     and its source language as a pair — ቆጮ, from Gurage;
     ሞባይል, from English — the student beats the voice to the
     home language on the second hearing.
3. **Say it to your companion** — building talk: one names two
   words, the other joins them; one names a base word, the
   other builds its -ኛ or -ነት form (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — words
     joining, doubling, growing endings and travelling in the
     super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching,
   fill-in-the-blank, writing — served at the learner's level
   and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — split three place names, build two
   ending-words, and hunt one loanword at home (lesson/
   lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot split a compound → read it slowly and listen for a
  word you already know hiding inside: ሰላም (peace) inside
  ፍኖተሰላም, ምግባር (behaviour) inside ሥነምግባር.
- Mixes -ኛ and -ነት → ask "person or state?" -ኛ always lands
  on a PERSON (ነገረኛ is someone); -ነት lands on a STATE no
  one can touch (ታማኝነት, faithfulness).
- Thinks a loanword is "not real Amharic" → it IS Amharic now;
  it travelled and stayed. ሱቅ is the word every shopper uses —
  its Arabic birth is its story, not a fault.
