# Hands-on — Building Words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two stones join
Write two words on two stones or bottle tops — ፍኖተ on one,
ሰላም on the other. Slide them together until they touch, and
say the compound: ፍኖተሰላም. Do it with ሥነ + ምግባር and
አዲስ + አበባ. Then reverse: start joined, pull the stones
apart, and name the two words inside.

## Echo words
Clap the building of doubled words: ፍሬ (clap) — ፍራፍሬ (two
claps). ቅጠል (clap) — ቅጠላቅጠል (two claps). Walk around the
house or garden naming things that are plentiful — fruit on a
tree, leaves on a branch — and try the doubling rhythm on
their names.

## Person or state?
Make two hoops or two circles on the ground: PERSON and
STATE. Call out built words — ነገረኛ, ታማኝነት, and more you
build from ተንኮል, ትዕግስት, ወጣት, ልጅ. The player steps into
the right circle and says why: "-ኛ — a person!" or "-ነት — a
state!"

## Travellers at the market
On your next market or shop visit, listen for loanwords:
ሱቅ, ሞባይል, ቢሮ, ፖስታ, ሰዓት. Count how many travellers you
hear in one visit, and tell someone at home where each one
came from.

## Added 2026-11-12 — change one fidel
Take ልብስ and change its first fidel the book's way; then take a word of your own and do the same, saying each new word aloud.
