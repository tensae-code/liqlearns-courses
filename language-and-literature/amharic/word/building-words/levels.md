# Building Words — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons run through the four ways
Amharic builds words: joining, doubling, endings, and borrowing.

## Sub lesson 1 — Compound words
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what
a compound word is, join two given words into their compound,
and split ፍኖተሰላም and ሥነምግባር back into their two words.

## Sub lesson 2 — Words that double — reduplication
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: spot
ፍራፍሬ and ቅጠላቅጠል as doubled words and say how each is built
(a part of the word said twice).

## Sub lesson 3 — Doer nouns in -ኛ and state nouns in -ነት
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build
ነገረኛ from ነገር and ታማኝነት from ታማኝ, and tell the endings
apart — -ኛ builds the person, -ነት builds the state.

## Sub lesson 4 — Words that travelled — loanwords
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name at
least five loanwords with the language each travelled from
(ቆጮ ← Gurage, ሞባይል ← English, ሱቅ ← Arabic, ቢሮ ← French,
ፖስታ ← Italian).
