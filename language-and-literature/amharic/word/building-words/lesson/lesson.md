# Building Words

## Do it first

Here is a word you have known all your life: **አዲስአበባ** —
Addis Ababa, the capital city.

Now pull it apart. Somewhere inside it, two smaller words are
hiding. Say the name slowly. Can you hear **አዲስ** (new) and
**አበባ** (flower) standing inside it, joined?

አዲስ + አበባ = **አዲስአበባ** — "new flower".

You have just built — and unbuilt — a compound word. This
lesson is about all the ways Amharic builds its words: by
joining, by doubling, by endings, and by borrowing travellers
from other languages.

## Sub lesson 1 — Compound words

A **compound word** — **ውስብስብ ሆሄያት**, also called **ጥምር
ቃላት** — is one word built by joining two words together. The
two words move in, and from then on they live as one.

The books build place names this way:

- **ፍኖተ + ሰላም → ፍኖተሰላም** — Finote Selam, "path of peace"
- **አዲስ + አበባ → አዲስአበባ** — Addis Ababa
- **ባሕርዳር** — Bahir Dar, by the sea/lake
- **ደብረማርቆስ, ደብረብርሃን, ደብረታቦር** — the ደብረ (mountain)
  towns
- **ቀይባሕር** — the Red Sea

And everyday words join the same way:

- **ሥነ + ምግባር → ሥነምግባር** — ethics, good behaviour
- **ሥነአዕምሮ, ሥነሕይወት, ሥነጽሑፍ** — the ሥነ family (the study
  of…)

[Picture 8455 — three compound joins]

In Grade 3 your teacher picks compound words out of real
sentences and you use them in sentences of your own. The
placed sets run like music: **ጨፍ, ጨሪ, ብልጫ** — **ጭንገጠ,
ጭንቅጥ, ቁጭቻ** — **ትኋኋ, ሹኋ, ሿሚ** — **ዓኒተ, አባኒተ, ልጅኒተ** —
**ዓሌ, ቃሌ, የማሌ**. Read them aloud; hear how the joined words
ring.

Sometimes the join wears a little bridge: the books write
**የሥራ-ድርሻ** and then join it smooth as **የሥራ ድርሻ**, and
**ሥነ-ምግባር** smooths into **ሥነምግባር**. Joined with a dash,
joined with a space, joined tight — it is the same building:
two words, one word.

## Sub lesson 2 — Words that double — reduplication

Some words are built by saying a part of the word TWICE.

- **ፍራፍሬ** — fruit, fruits of every kind: ፍሬ said with its
  own echo.
- **ቅጠላቅጠል** — leafy, full of leaves: ቅጠል doubled and
  spread.

Listen to them: ፍራ-ፍሬ, ቅጠላ-ቅጠል. The doubling is not a
mistake and not a stutter. It is a building tool — the echo
makes the word mean MORE of the thing, the thing spread out
and plentiful: fruit everywhere, leaves everywhere. When you
meet a word whose inside repeats itself, you have found a
doubled word.

## Sub lesson 3 — Doer nouns in -ኛ and state nouns in -ነት

Endings build people and states.

**-ኛ builds the PERSON.** Take **ነገር** (talk, a matter) and
add -ኛ: **ነገረኛ** — the talkative person, the one full of
talk. The Grade 2 book builds the family the same way from
**ተንኮል** (mischief), **ትዕቢት** (pride), **ትዕግስት** (patience),
**ቅናት** (envy) — each one names the person who carries that
quality.

**-ነት builds the STATE.** Take **ታማኝ** (faithful) and add
-ነት: **ታማኝነት** — faithfulness, the state of being faithful.
The book's builders run from **ኃላፊ** (responsible), **ወጣት**
(young), **ልጅ** (child), **ሰው** (person) the same way: -ነት
turns the word into the state or condition it names.

[Picture 8456 — the -ኛ and -ነት builders]

Hold the two apart with one question: **person or state?**
ነገረኛ is a person you can point at. ታማኝነት is a state no
hand can touch. -ኛ points at people; -ነት names states.

## Sub lesson 4 — Words that travelled — loanwords

Not every Amharic word was born in Amharic. Some words
**travelled** — Amharic took them in from other languages,
and they stayed and became Amharic words. These are called
**የውስጠ ቃላት** — loanwords, words from inside other
languages.

The Grade 4 guide prints the table — each traveller with the
home it came from:

| Loanword | It travelled from |
|---|---|
| ቆጮ | ጉራግኛ — Gurage |
| ሞባይል | እንግሊዝኛ — English |
| አምባሽ | ትግርኛ — Tigrinya |
| ሴንጋ | ኦሮምኛ — Oromo |
| ሱቅ | አረብኛ — Arabic |
| ሰዓት | አረብኛ — Arabic |
| ቢሮ | ፈረንሳይኛ — French |
| ባቡር | ፈረንሳይኛ — French |
| ፖስታ | ጣሊያንኛ — Italian |
| መርከብ | ጣሊያንኛ — Italian (as placed in the table) |
| ጉድፈቻ / አባሪ | ኦሮምኛ — Oromo |

And the guide's own pages add two more travellers in use:
**ኮምፒውተር** from English, and everyday words like
**ቴሌቪዥን, ሬድዮ, ማግኔት** that came with the things themselves.

[GIF 8461 — loanwords travelling into Amharic]

Think about ሱቅ: every market day, every shopper says it —
and it was born in Arabic. ቢሮ was born in French, ፖስታ in
Italian, ቆጮ close to home in Gurage. A loanword is not a
broken word. It is a word with a journey — and knowing its
home language is part of knowing the word.

## Sub lesson 6 — Grade 5 and 6: compounds — ቤተመንግሥት and the compound web; doubling — the reduplicated sets


### Compound words — the Grade 5 book's own joins

Join two words into one compound, as the Grade 5 book sets it: ቤት + መንግሥት → ቤተመንግሥት; ትምህርት + ቤት → ትምህርትቤት (school). Compounds stand in sub lesson 1 of this lesson from other books; this part stocks the Grade 5 book's own joins at its own page.

### Compound words web — the Grade 6 book's joins

Build compound words in a web, as the Grade 6 book sets it: አየር + አደር = አየርመንገድ (airline); ብረት + አጥ; እንጀራ + ሰራሽ; ወዝ + መንገድ; ሥራ + ምጣድ; ሰው + እናት. Each web line is two word-blocks snapping into one new word.

### Reduplicated words — the Grade 5 review set

The reduplication pattern ("Xs of all kinds"): ቅጠላቅጠል, ብረታብረት, ጨርቃጨርቅ — then write six more of your own. Reduplication stands in sub lesson 2 of this lesson from the Grade 2 book; this part stocks the Grade 5 book's own review set at its own page.

### Word-building by doubling — the Grade 6 set

Make new, intensive words by the doubling pattern: ሸቀጥ → ሸቀጣሸቀጥ; then work ቅመም, ትል, ጥቅም, ቅርጽ, ቁስ the same way. Doubling stands in sub lesson 2 from the Grade 2 book; this part stocks the Grade 6 book's own set at its own page.


## Sub lesson 7 — Grade 7: compound families — ሥነ, ፀረ, ቤተ, ሕገ

Grade 7's volunteering chapter builds compound words (የተዋሃዱ ቃላት) from four roots. Each root joins a grid of second elements to make real compounds: ሥነ- (as in ሥነሕዝብ, ሥነምግባር), ፀረ- (against — ፀረወባ, ፀረሕዝብ), ቤተ- (house of — ቤተመጽሐፍት the library, ቤተክርስቲያን the church), ሕገ- (law — ሕገመንግሥት the constitution, ሕገወጥ unlawful). The book's grid of partners runs: ወባ, ሕዝብ, መስጊድ, ደንብ, ሥርዓት, ሕዋስ, አረም, ዕድገት, ክህነት, ልቡና, ዜጋ, ሰብል, ሰላም, መዘክር, ጥበብ, መጻሕፍት, ተባይ, ሥዕል, አንድነት, ግጥም, መንግሥት, ሙስና, ወጥ, ምግባር, ፀሎት, ምህዳር. ቤተ at the root of a word-family tree carries ቤተመጽሐፍት and ቤተክርስቲያን on its branches. Grade 8's justice chapter adds compound pairs of its own (model: ጥራዝ ነጠቅ, ወሬ ነጋሪ, ነገር አዋቂ) — pairs whose shared meaning is more than either half.

## Say it to your companion

Play builders. One of you names two words — ፍኖተ and ሰላም,
ሥነ and ምግባር — and the other joins them and says the
compound. Then endings: one names a base (ነገር, ታማኝ), the
other builds with -ኛ or -ነት and says "person!" or "state!".
Finish with travellers: one says a loanword, the other names
the language it travelled from.

## Do It Yourself

1. Split these three back into their two words: ፍኖተሰላም,
   ሥነምግባር, አዲስአበባ. Write each join out with a + sign.
2. Build: from ነገር write the -ኛ person-word; from ታማኝ
   write the -ነት state-word. Say what each ending did.
3. Write five loanwords from the table, each with its home
   language beside it.
4. At home or at the market, listen for one word you think
   travelled into Amharic. Ask where it came from, and write
   the word and its home.
