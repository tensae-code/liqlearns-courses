# Building Words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ፍኖተ + ሰላም join into —
a) ሰላምፍኖተ  b) ፍኖተሰላም  c) ፍኖተዎች
**Answer: b) ፍኖተሰላም** — "path of peace", a compound word.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Inside ሥነምግባር, the two joined words are —
a) ሥነ and ምግባር  b) ሥራ and ድርሻ  c) ፍኖተ and ሰላም
**Answer: a)** — the compound is written joined smooth from
ሥነ-ምግባር.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ፍራፍሬ is built by —
a) borrowing it from Arabic  b) saying a part of the word twice  c) adding -ነት
**Answer: b)** — reduplication: ፍሬ doubled, fruit everywhere.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** ነገር + -ኛ gives ነገረኛ, which names —
a) a state  b) a place  c) a person — the talkative one
**Answer: c)** — -ኛ builds the person who carries the quality.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ታማኝነት (faithfulness) is built with —
a) -ኛ  b) -ነት  c) -ኦች
**Answer: b) -ነት** — the ending that builds a state.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** ሱቅ travelled into Amharic from —
a) French  b) Italian  c) Arabic
**Answer: c) Arabic** — from the guide's loanword table.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** ቆጮ is close to home — it came from —
a) Gurage (ጉራግኛ)  b) English  c) Tigrinya
**Answer: a) Gurage.**

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** ልብስ with its first fidel changed, the book's model, becomes…
a) ጥብስ  b) ልብሶች  c) ቤት
**Answer: a) ጥብስ** — Grade 2 p.5.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** ጧት ጧት is an example of…
a) A doubled (reduplicated) word  b) A compound word  c) A plural
**Answer: a) A doubled (reduplicated) word** — Grade 1 p.120.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** ፎካሪ goes with which verb pattern?
a) ይፎክራል  b) ይበላል  c) ይተኛል
**Answer: a) ይፎክራል** — the agent-noun pattern, Grade 2 pp.85–86.

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** ቤት + መንግሥት joins into what?
a) ቤተመንግሥት  b) ቤትመንግሥትቤት  c) መንግሥትቤት
**Answer: a) ቤተመንግሥት** — the Grade 5 book's own compound.

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** አየር + አደር makes which compound?
a) አየርመንገድ — airline  b) አየርአደርቤት  c) መንገድአየር
**Answer: a) አየርመንገድ — airline** — the Grade 6 web's first line.

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** ሸቀጥ doubled the book's way becomes what?
a) ሸቀጣሸቀጥ  b) ሸቀጥሸቀጥጥ  c) አሸቀጠ
**Answer: a) ሸቀጣሸቀጥ** — the doubling pattern: the word, then itself with -አ- between.


**14. (Sub lesson 7)** ቤተመጽሐፍት is built from ቤተ- + መጻሕፍት and means —
a) a church  b) a library  c) a court
**Answer: b) a library** — ቤተ- 'house of' + books = the library, house of books.
