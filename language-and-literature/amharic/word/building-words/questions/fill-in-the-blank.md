# Building Words — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** አዲስ + አበባ = ______.
**Answer: አዲስአበባ** — "new flower", the capital's name is a
compound.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ቅጠል doubled and spread builds the word ______.
**Answer: ቅጠላቅጠል** — leafy, full of leaves.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** To build the STATE of being faithful, ታማኝ takes the ending ______.
**Answer: -ነት** — giving ታማኝነት.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Words Amharic took in from other languages are called ______ ቃላት.
**Answer: የውስጠ** — የውስጠ ቃላት, loanwords.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Building a word by saying a part of it twice — as ፍሬ becomes ፍራፍሬ — is called ______.
**Answer: reduplication** (doubling).
