# One and Many — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The plural of ቀሚስ is —
a) ቀሚስህ  b) ቀሚሶች  c) ቀሚስአች
**Answer: b) ቀሚሶች** — the piece -ኦች makes it many.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Strip -ዎች off ገበሬዎች and the singular is —
a) ገበ  b) ገበሬዎች stays whole  c) ገበሬ
**Answer: c) ገበሬ** — the guide's key: ገበሬ-ዎች = ገበሬ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Strip the piece off መጽሐፍአች and the singular is —
a) መጽሐፍ  b) አች  c) መጽ
**Answer: a) መጽሐፍ** — the key: መጽሐፍ-አች = መጽሐፍ.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A plural word (ብዙ ቁጥር) —
a) names one and takes no suffix  b) names two or more and takes a plural suffix  c) is always the longest word
**Answer: b) names two or more and takes a plural suffix.**
