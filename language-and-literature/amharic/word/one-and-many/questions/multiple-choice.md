# One and Many — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The plural of ቀሚስ is —
a) ቀሚስህ  b) ቀሚሶች  c) ቀሚስአች
**Answer: b) ቀሚሶች** — the piece -ኦች makes it many.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Strip -ዎች off ገበሬዎች and the singular is —
a) ገበ  b) ገበሬዎች stays whole  c) ገበሬ
**Answer: c) ገበሬ** — the guide's key: ገበሬ-ዎች = ገበሬ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Strip the piece off መጽሐፍአች and the singular is —
a) መጽሐፍ  b) አች  c) መጽ
**Answer: a) መጽሐፍ** — the key: መጽሐፍ-አች = መጽሐፍ.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A plural word (ብዙ ቁጥር) —
a) names one and takes no suffix  b) names two or more and takes a plural suffix  c) is always the longest word
**Answer: b) names two or more and takes a plural suffix.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In the re-taught system, a singular noun —
a) carries the suffix -ኦች  b) carries no marker at all  c) always ends in -ዎች
**Answer: b) carries no marker at all** — the plural is the one that is formed, with -ኦች.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** When a plural subject enters a sentence, the verb phrase must agree with it in —
a) spelling only  b) number, gender and person (መደብ)  c) length
**Answer: b) number, gender and person** — the two sides of the sentence match each other.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** መምህራን, the plural of መምህር, is —
a) made with -ኦች  b) an older Ge'ez plural (-አን), named here with its teaching deferred  c) a singular word
**Answer: b) an older Ge'ez plural** — named exactly as the teaching names it; no Ge'ez rule is built in this lesson.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** ክራር in its plural is…
a) ክራሮች  b) ክራርኦች only  c) ክራርዎች only — never ክራሮች
**Answer: a) ክራሮች** — the review's own plural, Grade 2 pp.85–86.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The pp.85–86 review belongs to which chapter?
a) Traditional music  b) Digital devices  c) Seasons
**Answer: a) Traditional music** — the instruments review.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** A plural in this review is made by…
a) Adding the many-ending to the word  b) Changing the first fidel  c) Doubling the word
**Answer: a) Adding the many-ending to the word** — the -ኦች/-ዎች work of sub lessons 1–2, at the review page.

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** እነሙኒት means what?
a) Munit and her group  b) Many Munits  c) Munit's house
**Answer: a) Munit and her group** — the እነ- collective plural: the named person and their people.

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** Which is an -ኦች plural?
a) ተማሪዎች  b) እነዳዊት  c) ሙኒት
**Answer: a) ተማሪዎች** — farmers, students, bags — ገበሬዎች, ተማሪዎች, ቦርሳዎች.

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** The እነ- plural names a person and what?
a) Their group — the people with them  b) Their belongings  c) Their village only
**Answer: a) Their group — the people with them** — a collective, not a count.


**14. (Sub lesson 7)** Which of these is already a PLURAL form?
a) ሴት  b) ነገረኞች  c) በሬ
**Answer: b) ነገረኞች** — ነገረኞች carries the plural piece -ኦች; ሴት and በሬ are singular.
