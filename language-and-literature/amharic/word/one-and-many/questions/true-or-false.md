# One and Many — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every plural is made with the same piece.
**Answer: False** — words take different pieces: -ኦች, -ዎች, -አች (ቀሚሶች, ጫማዎች, ቅርስአች).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** ገበሬ is a plural word because it is about a person.
**Answer: False** — ገበሬ names one and carries no plural suffix: it is singular (ነጠላ ቁጥር).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The Ge'ez plurals named in this lesson (መምህራን, አልባሳት, አብያት, ሊቃውንት) are taught with a full rule for building new ones.
**Answer: False** — they are named exactly as the teaching names them and their teaching is deferred; the lesson builds no Ge'ez rule and does not complete the list.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A plural subject can sit beside a verb phrase of any number — agreement does not matter.
**Answer: False** — subject and verb phrase must agree in number, gender and person (መደብ).
