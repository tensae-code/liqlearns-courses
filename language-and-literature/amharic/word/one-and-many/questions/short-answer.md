# One and Many — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 7)** What is the singular of ምሁራን?
**Answer:** ምሁር — a scholar; ምሁራን is its plural.

**2. (Sub lesson 8)** Write the Amharic pair for singular and plural number as the Grade 7 guide heads the part.
**Answer:** ነጠላና ብዙ ቁጥር.
