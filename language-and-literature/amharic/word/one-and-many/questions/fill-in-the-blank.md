# One and Many — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right piece or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ቀሚስ + ______ = ቀሚሶች.
**Answer: ኦች**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A singular word names ______ referent and takes no plural suffix.
**Answer: one**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** መልሶች stripped of its plural piece is ______.
**Answer: መልስ**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Subject and verb phrase must agree in number, gender and ______.
**Answer: person (መደብ)**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The older plural suffixes borrowed from Ge'ez, named in the teaching, are ______ and ______.
**Answer: -አን; -አት** — as in መምህር → መምህራን and ልብስ → አልባሳት; their teaching is deferred.
