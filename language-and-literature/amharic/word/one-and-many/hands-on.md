# Hands-on — One and Many

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One cup, many cups
Put one cup on the table and say its word in the singular. Add more cups one by one and say the plural each time. Then take cups away and say the singular again when only one is left. Your hands count; your mouth names one or many.

## The dropping piece
Write ገበሬዎች on a strip of paper, with ዎች on a part you can tear. Tear the piece off, hold up what is left, and read it: ገበሬ. Do the same with ቅርስአች — tear off አች, read ቅርስ. This is GIF 8395 with your own hands.

## Two boxes
Label two boxes ONE and MANY. Write word cards: ገበሬ, ገበሬዎች, ቅርስ, ቅርስአች, ጫማ, ጫማዎች, ወላጅ, ወላጆች, መልስ, መልሶች. Sort every card into its box, saying the box's two questions aloud for each: how many does it name? does it carry a plural piece?
