# One and Many

## Do it first

Look at this word: **ገበሬዎች**.

It means *farmers* — many of them. But hiding inside it is the word for one farmer. Can you find it? Cover the last piece with your finger… there it is: **ገበሬ** — one farmer. The piece you covered, **ዎች**, is what made one into many.

This lesson is about that piece: how words become many, and how you strip the piece back off to find the one.

## Sub lesson 1 — Making many with -ኦች

One dress: **ቀሚስ**. Many dresses: **ቀሚሶች**.

The piece **-ኦች** turns one into many. The books teach this plural form early, in the clothing lessons: ቀሚስ → ቀሚሶች.

Its sister piece **-ዎች** does the same work in other words: ጫማ → ጫማዎች (shoe → shoes). Keep both pieces in your hand — different words take different pieces, and the books give each word its own.

## Sub lesson 2 — Making many with -ዎች, and stripping it back

Here is the teacher guide's own key — plurals on the left, and what you get when you strip the piece off:

- **ቅርስ-አች = ቅርስ**
- **መጽሐፍ-አች = መጽሐፍ**
- **ገበሬ-ዎች = ገበሬ**
- **በሬ-ዎች = በሬ**
- **ጫማ-ዎች = ጫማ**

Read the key both ways. Going right: the plural loses its piece — **-አች** or **-ዎች** — and the singular stands alone. Going left: the singular takes its piece and becomes many.

[GIF 8395 — the plural piece drops off]

Watch ዎች fall away from ገበሬዎች. What is left standing is the one: ገበሬ. Stripping is a reading power: when a long word stops you, take its plural piece off first and read the word inside.

The student books practise the same two-way street: ጥያቄ → ጥያቄዎች going up, and ወላጆች → ወላጅ, መልሶች → መልስ coming back down.

## Sub lesson 3 — One or many? Sorting words

Now the definition box, in the teacher guide's own words, made plain:

- A **singular** word (ነጠላ ቁጥር) names **one** referent and takes **no** plural suffix.
- A **plural** word (ብዙ ቁጥር) names **two or more** and takes **plural suffixes** (ብዙ ቁጥር አመልካች ቅጥያዎች).

So sorting is two questions: *How many does it name?* and *Does it carry a plural piece?*

- ገበሬ — one farmer, no piece: singular.
- ገበሬዎች — many farmers, carries ዎች: plural.
- ቅርስ — one, no piece: singular.
- ቅርስአች — many, carries አች: plural.

Sort every word you meet this way, and one-and-many will never confuse you.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: a plural word names two or more and carries a plural piece — -ኦች, -ዎች or -አች. Make ቀሚስ many for them (ቀሚሶች), then strip ገበሬዎች back to one (ገበሬ) and let them name the piece that fell off.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make these many: ቀሚስ (with -ኦች), ጫማ (with -ዎች), ገበሬ (with -ዎች).
2. Strip these back to one: ቅርስአች, መጽሐፍአች, በሬዎች.
3. Sort these into ONE and MANY columns: ወላጅ, ወላጆች, መልስ, መልሶች, ጥያቄ, ጥያቄዎች.
4. Look around your room: name one thing in the singular, then say its plural. Write both.
5. Read ገበሬዎች aloud, then cover ዎች with your finger and read what is left.
