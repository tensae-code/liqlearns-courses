# Lesson plan — One and Many

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** One and Many
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a singular word names one; a plural word names two or more and takes a plural suffix — -ኦች, -ዎች or -አች — and you can strip the suffix off a plural to find the singular (ገበሬዎች = ገበሬ + ዎች).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ: the learner already knows a word can carry a little piece at its end. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Making many with -ኦች** — ቀሚስ → ቀሚሶች; plural forms drilled across the books (ጫማ→ጫማዎች pattern beside it).
2. **Making many with -ዎች, and stripping it back** — the teacher guide's key: ቅርስ-አች = ቅርስ, መጽሐፍ-አች = መጽሐፍ, ገበሬ-ዎች = ገበሬ, በሬ-ዎች = በሬ, ጫማ-ዎች = ጫማ.
3. **One or many? Sorting words** — the definition box: a singular word names one referent and takes no plural suffix; a plural word names two or more and takes plural suffixes (ብዙ ቁጥር አመልካች ቅጥያዎች).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Make the plural of a word with -ኦች and with -ዎች.
2. Strip -አች or -ዎች off a plural and give the singular.
3. Sort a list of words into singular and plural, and say why each belongs where it is.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Adds -ኦች to every word → the books use more than one plural piece (-ኦች, -ዎች, -አች); learn each word with its own piece, the way the key gives them: ገበሬ-ዎች, ቅርስ-አች.
- Cannot find the singular → do what the key does: strip the piece off the end — ገበሬዎች minus ዎች is ገበሬ.
- Sorts a plural as singular → ask the box's question: does it name one, or two or more? Does it carry a plural suffix?
