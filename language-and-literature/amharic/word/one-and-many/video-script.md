# Video script — One and Many

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One word can name one thing. Or many.

One: **ቀሚስ**. Many: **ቀሚሶች**.
The piece **-ኦች** made it many.

One: **ገበሬ** — one farmer.
Many: **ገበሬዎች** — the piece is **-ዎች**.

Now take the piece off.
**ገበሬዎች** — drop **-ዎች** — **ገበሬ**. One again.
**ቅርስአች** — drop **-አች** — **ቅርስ**. One again.

One names one. Many names two or more — and wears its piece.
Strip the piece, find the one. That is the game.

---

Now the whole system, stated once.
A singular noun carries **no marker at all**.
The plural is formed with one suffix: **-ኦች**.
And in a sentence, subject and verb phrase agree —
in number, in gender, in person — **መደብ**.

Older plurals live beside it, borrowed from Ge'ez:
**መምህር → መምህራን. ቤት → አብያት. ቀን → ቀናት.**
Named here. Their teaching comes later.
Recognise them. Build with **-ኦች**.
