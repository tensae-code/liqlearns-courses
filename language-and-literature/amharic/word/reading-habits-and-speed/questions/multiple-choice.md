# Reading Habits and Speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As you read, your eyes travel along the line of words —
a) from right to left  b) from left to right  c) in circles
**Answer: b) from left to right**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reading speed is counted in —
a) words per minute  b) pages per hour only  c) lines per book
**Answer: a) words per minute**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide's note says people gain the great bulk of their knowledge from —
a) being told  b) watching  c) reading
**Answer: c) reading**
