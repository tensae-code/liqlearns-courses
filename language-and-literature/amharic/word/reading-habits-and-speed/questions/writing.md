# Reading Habits and Speed — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Read a page for one timed minute, mark where you stopped, count the words, and write down your words-per-minute with today's date.
**Answer guide: A dated number, honestly counted from the learner's own timed reading.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write three lines explaining to a younger learner how their eyes read a line.
**Answer guide: Left to right; the eyes move and fix; at each fixation words are taken in.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** For three days, keep the daily-ten record: the text, and one thing it taught you, each day.
**Answer guide: Three dated lines, each naming a real text and a real thing learned.**
