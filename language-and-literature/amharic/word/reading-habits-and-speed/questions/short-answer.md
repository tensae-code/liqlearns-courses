# Reading Habits and Speed — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens at a fixation?
**Answer:** The vision settles on the words, and the words are taken in.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this lesson count reading speed at all?
**Answer:** So it can be grown — speed is a measure of the taking-in, counted alongside understanding.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a good reading habit, in this lesson's terms?
**Answer:** Reading daily, on purpose — fixing on words, taking them in, and knowing and growing your own speed.
