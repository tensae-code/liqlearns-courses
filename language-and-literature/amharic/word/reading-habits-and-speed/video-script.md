# Video script — Reading Habits and Speed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How do your eyes read?

They do not slide. They step.
Move… stop… take the words in.
Move… stop… take the words in.
From the left of the line — to the right.

Count your words in one minute.
That is your speed.
Next month, count again.
Is the number growing?

And read every day.
Most of what a person knows —
comes from reading.
Make it a habit. A good one.
