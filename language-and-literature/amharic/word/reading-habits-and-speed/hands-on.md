# Hands-on — Reading Habits and Speed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch your own eyes
Read one page aloud to a partner — no: silently, while your partner watches your eyes, not the page. Afterwards your partner describes what your eyes did: where they stopped, how they crossed each line. Then swap. Fixation is easier to believe once you have seen it on a face you know.

## Your number this month
Pick a page of plain text. Read for exactly one minute (a partner watches the clock), mark the word you reached, and count the words. Write the date and your words-per-minute in your notebook. Repeat on the first day of next month with a fresh page of the same kind. Two numbers, honestly counted, show a habit growing.

## The daily ten
For one week, read ten minutes before sleep — any text you choose. Each night, write one line: what you read, and one thing it taught you. At the week's end, read your seven lines: that is the note's promise, in your own hand — knowledge arriving by reading.
