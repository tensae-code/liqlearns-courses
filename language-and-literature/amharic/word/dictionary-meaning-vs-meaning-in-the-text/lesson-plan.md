# Lesson plan — Dictionary Meaning vs Meaning in the Text

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Dictionary Meaning vs Meaning in the Text
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every word has two addresses: its dictionary meaning, and the meaning it carries inside this text — a good reader checks both.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What a Word Means and Using the Dictionary — the learner knows contextual meaning; this lesson sets the two layers side by side the Grade 8 way.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
