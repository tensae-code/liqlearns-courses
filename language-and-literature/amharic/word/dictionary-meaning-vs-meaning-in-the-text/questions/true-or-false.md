# Dictionary Meaning vs Meaning in the Text — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The same word can carry a dictionary meaning and a different working meaning in a text.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When dictionary and text meanings disagree, the text's meaning governs that reading.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A phrase's meaning in a play can be a whole situation, not just its dictionary meaning.
**Answer: True**
