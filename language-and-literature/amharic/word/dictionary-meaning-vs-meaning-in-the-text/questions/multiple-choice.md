# Dictionary Meaning vs Meaning in the Text — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A word's meaning IN THE TEXT is found by —
a) the dictionary alone  b) reading what the word does in its sentence  c) guessing from its first letter
**Answer: b) reading what the word does in its sentence** — the text layer is the meaning the sentence gives the word.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's method, which layer do you check FIRST?
a) the text layer  b) the dictionary layer  c) neither — skip both
**Answer: b) the dictionary layer** — dictionary first, then the meaning in the text.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ማጋዝ in the play's vocabulary work means —
a) dancing  b) storing / putting into store  c) singing
**Answer: b) storing / putting into store** — ማጋዝ is storing — the chairs stored since Friday in Act 1.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** ትውፊት belongs to which layer's list in the first work?
a) the text layer only  b) the dictionary layer  c) neither
**Answer: b) the dictionary layer** — ትውፊት is in the first list — dictionary definitions.
