# Dictionary Meaning vs Meaning in the Text — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two layers this lesson compares?
**Answer:** The dictionary meaning and the meaning in the text.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ጥበብ is one of the heritage words defined in this work — what layer comes first for it?
**Answer:** The dictionary layer — its home meaning — before its meaning in the reading.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the model pair, what is the SIMILAR meaning given for ደልደል ያለ?
**Answer:** ወፈር ያለ (its opposite is ከሳ ያለ).
