# Hands-on — How New Words Are Taught

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Collect from a real text
Read one chapter or story this week. Underline every word that is new to you — your own collection, as the guide orders. Write each on a card: the word on one side, its sentence from the text on the other.

## Build one network
Take one word from your cards. Draw it at the centre of a page. Around it, write its meaning-relations (words linked by the message it carries) on one side, and its context-relations (the words that line up with it in the passage) on the other. Say one sentence using three of the network's words together — that is active use (ማውጣጣት), done with your own hands.
