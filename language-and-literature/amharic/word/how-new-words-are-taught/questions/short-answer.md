# How New Words Are Taught — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five ways of working a new word, in the guide's order.
**Answer:** Dictionaries; context with examples; networks (ዝምድና); active use (ማውጣጣት); guessing from context.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between a meaning relation and a context relation?
**Answer:** Meaning relations link words by the message they carry; context relations link words that line up together in a passage about one thing.
