# How New Words Are Taught — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Collect five new words from a text you read, and work each one two ways: its dictionary meaning, and your guess from its context before you looked it up.
**Answer guide: Five words, each with a context guess recorded before its dictionary meaning.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Draw the network of one of your collected words and write a sentence using three of its network words.
**Answer guide: A network drawing with both relation kinds, and one sentence using three of its words.**
