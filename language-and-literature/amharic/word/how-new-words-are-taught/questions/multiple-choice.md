# How New Words Are Taught — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The method begins with learners —
a) memorising a dictionary page  b) collecting new words from the chapter's texts  c) copying the teacher's list
**Answer: b) collecting new words from the chapter's texts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ዝምድና names —
a) a word's network of relations  b) a word's spelling  c) a word's plural
**Answer: a) a word's network of relations**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Active use of a new word is —
a) hiding it in a list  b) translating it once  c) ማውጣጣት — using the word, spoken and written, until it is yours
**Answer: c) ማውጣጣት — using the word, spoken and written, until it is yours**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Context relations are words that —
a) sound alike  b) line up together in a passage about one thing  c) share a first letter
**Answer: b) line up together in a passage about one thing**
