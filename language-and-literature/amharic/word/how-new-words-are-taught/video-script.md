# Video script — How New Words Are Taught

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A new word! How do we learn it?

First — we FIND our new words.
In the chapter. In the text we read.

Then — five ways.
The dictionary tells us.
The context shows us, with examples.
The network holds it — ዝምድና —
words holding hands.
We USE it — ማውጣጣት — speak it, write it.
And we GUESS it from its passage.

Five ways. One word — learned.
