# Video script — What Reading Practice Builds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why do we learn to read?

One — to reach information.
Nobody has to be there to tell you.
The text tells you.

Two — reading builds the others.
Listening. Speaking. Writing.
They all stand on what you have read.

Three — confidence.
A person who can read — stands sure.

And reading builds twelve abilities.
Predicting. Finding facts. Guessing words.
The main idea. The author's feeling.
Twelve. Grow them all.
