# Hands-on — What Reading Practice Builds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Purpose hunt
Take any text you read this week. Before reading, write which of the three purposes you are reading for — information, foundation, or confidence. After reading, write one line on whether it served that purpose. Three texts, three purposes, tried in turn.

## Ability of the day
Write the twelve abilities on twelve slips. Each reading day, draw one slip and read *for that ability*: predict before opening; or hunt one specific fact; or guess three words from context and check them after. Keep the slips you have practised in a pile — the pile is your growth, visible.
