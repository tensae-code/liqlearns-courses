# What Reading Practice Builds — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three purposes of teaching reading.
**Answer:** Access to information; a foundation for listening, speaking and writing; self-confidence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name any four of the twelve abilities.
**Answer:** Any four of: predicting; extracting specific information; general understanding; guessing words from context; text organisation; the author's feeling; main idea; linking prior knowledge; leaving aside irrelevant detail; indirect messages; detailed information; context of presentation.
