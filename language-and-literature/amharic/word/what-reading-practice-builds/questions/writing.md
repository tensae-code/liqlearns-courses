# What Reading Practice Builds — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before reading a text of your choice, write your purpose (one of the three) and the one ability you will practise; after reading, write two lines on how each went.
**Answer guide: A named purpose and ability before, and an honest two-line account after.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the twelve abilities from memory, then check against the lesson and repair any you missed.
**Answer guide: All twelve present after checking; misses repaired, not hidden.**
