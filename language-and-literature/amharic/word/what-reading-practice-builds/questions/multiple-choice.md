# What Reading Practice Builds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the guide's three purposes of teaching reading?
a) access to information  b) filling the timetable  c) punishing slow readers
**Answer: a) access to information**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is among the twelve abilities?
a) copying quickly  b) memorising every word  c) leaving aside irrelevant detail
**Answer: c) leaving aside irrelevant detail**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The second purpose says reading is a foundation for —
a) only handwriting  b) listening, speaking and writing  c) only examinations
**Answer: b) listening, speaking and writing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Understanding what is meant, not only what is said, is the ability of —
a) understanding indirectly stated messages  b) predicting  c) extracting specific information
**Answer: a) understanding indirectly stated messages**
