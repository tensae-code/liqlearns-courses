# Video script — Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words carry little pieces. The pieces are **ቅጥያ**.

Look: **ክፍያውን**. Peel the end: **ውን**.
The word inside is ክፍያ. The piece rides on its back.

A piece can stand in front, too.
The stem is **በላ** — ate.
**እንደበላ**. **ስለበላ**. **እስበላ**. **ሊበላ**. **ቢበላ**.
The stem never changes. The rider changes.

Now the house. **ቤት**.
**ቤቴ** — my house.
**ቤትህ** — your house, boy.
**ቤትሽ** — your house, girl.
**ቤታችን** — our house.

One stem. Little pieces. Whose? The piece tells you.

Pieces can build describing words, too.

**ኢትዮጵያ** plus **-አዊ**: **ኢትዮጵያዊ**.
**ድንጋይ** plus **-አማ**: **ድንጋይአማ** — full of stones.
**ሆድ** plus **-ኣም**: **ሆዳም**.
**ሕመም** plus **-ተኛ**: **ሕመምተኛ** — the one with the illness.

And a verb root grows a family. **ሥርወቃል**.
**ሰፋ** grows: **አሰፋ, ተሰፋ, ተስፋፋ, አስፋፋ**.
**ቆረጠ** grows: **ተቆረጠ, አሰቆረጠ, አቆራረጠ, ተቆራረጠ**.
However big the word, the root stands inside it. Find the root.

Now name the whole system.
**ቅድመ ገብ ቅጥያ** — the prefix. It enters BEFORE.
**ድህረ ገብ ቅጥያ** — the suffix. It enters AFTER.
**ኢ-** — before. A prefix. **መ-** — before. A prefix.
**-አዊ** — after. A suffix. **-ነት** — after. **-ኦሽ** — after.
And one word can carry both!
**ከ-** in front… **ሴት** in the middle… **-ኦች** behind: **ከሴቶች**.
Peel from the front. Peel from the back. The word stands in the middle.
