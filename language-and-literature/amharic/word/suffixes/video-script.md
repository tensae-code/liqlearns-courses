# Video script — Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words carry little pieces. The pieces are **ቅጥያ**.

Look: **ክፍያውን**. Peel the end: **ውን**.
The word inside is ክፍያ. The piece rides on its back.

A piece can stand in front, too.
The stem is **በላ** — ate.
**እንደበላ**. **ስለበላ**. **እስበላ**. **ሊበላ**. **ቢበላ**.
The stem never changes. The rider changes.

Now the house. **ቤት**.
**ቤቴ** — my house.
**ቤትህ** — your house, boy.
**ቤትሽ** — your house, girl.
**ቤታችን** — our house.

One stem. Little pieces. Whose? The piece tells you.
