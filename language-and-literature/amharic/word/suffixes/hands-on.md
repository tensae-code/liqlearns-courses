# Hands-on — Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Peel with a finger
Write ክፍያውን, ቤትህ, ልጅህ, ዝንጀሮን big on paper. Cover the word with your hand and slide it slowly from the left: read the stem first, then let the suffix appear. Say the two pieces apart, then the whole word together.

## Snap-on pieces
Write በላ on a big card. Write እንደ, ስለ, እስ, ሊ, ቢ on five small cards. Lay each small card in front of the big card, one at a time, and read the whole word aloud as it forms — እንደበላ, ስለበላ, እስበላ, ሊበላ, ቢበላ. This is GIF 8393 with your own hands.

## Whose house is it?
Draw a small house on four cards. Under them write ቤቴ, ቤትህ, ቤትሽ, ቤታችን. Stand by each card in turn: point to yourself for ቤቴ, to a boy for ቤትህ, to a girl for ቤትሽ, and gather everyone for ቤታችን. A partner shuffles the cards; you read and point.

## Adjective chips
Write the stems ኢትዮጵያ, ድንጋይ, ሆድ, ዝናብ, ሕመም on big cards. Write the suffixes -አዊ, -አማ, -ኣም, -ተኛ on small chips. Lay each chip after the stem it fits, and read the whole adjective aloud as it forms — ኢትዮጵያዊ, ድንጋይአማ, ሆዳም, ዝናባማ, ሕመምተኛ. This is GIF 8682 with your own hands. A partner shuffles the chips; you rebuild.

## Grow the family tree
Draw a tree on a big paper: the root ቆረጠ in the trunk. On four branches write ተቆረጠ, አሰቆረጠ, አቆራረጠ, ተቆራረጠ. Under the tree, plant a second root — ሰፋ — and grow its family in the soil line: አሰፋ, ተሰፋ, ተስፋፋ, አስፋፋ. Read each family from the root outward.
