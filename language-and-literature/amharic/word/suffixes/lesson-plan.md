# Lesson plan — Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** words carry little pieces (ቅጥያ) — pieces before the stem (prefixes እንደ-, ስለ-) and pieces after it (suffixes -አች, -ምች, and the person pieces on ቤት: ቤትህ, ቤትሽ, ቤቴ, ቤታችን) — and you can peel a piece off to find the word inside. Grown in the G5–G8 pass: suffixes that build adjectives (-አዊ, -አማ / -ኣም, -ኛ families) and whole verb families grown from one root (ሥርወቃል — the ተ- and አስ- families).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Fidel and Word lessons — the learner can already read whole words. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Suffixes on verbs** — separating suffixes (ቅጥያ) from words: ክፍያውን → ውን; ቤትህ, ልጅህ, ጠላች, ዝንጀሮን; and ይብሉኝ, ሰጠችው, ሰፈርህ.
2. **Prefixes and suffixes** — a word split as prefix + stem + suffix: እንደ-, ስለ- before; -አች, -ምች after; the በላ family: እንደበላ, ስለበላ, እስበላ, ሊበላ, ቢበላ.
3. **Whose house?** — person suffixes on ቤት: ቤትህ (your house, to a boy/man), ቤትሽ (your house, to a girl/woman), ቤቴ (my house), ቤታችን (our house).
4. **Suffixes that make adjectives** — -አዊ for origin (ኢትዮጵያ → ኢትዮጵያዊ, ኤንያ → ኤንያዊ); -አማ / -ኣም for full-of (ድንጋይ → ድንጋይአማ, እሸዋ → እሸዋማ, ሆድ → ሆዳም, ዝናብ → ዝናባማ); the -ኛ family ( -ኧኛ in the word web; ሕመም + -ተኛ → ሕመምተኛ; ታሪካዊ ቀን, አመለኛ በቅሎ met in sentences).
5. **Verb families from one root — ሥርወቃል** — one root grows a family: ሰፋ → አሰፋ, ተሰፋ, ተስፋፋ, አስፋፋ; the ቆረጠ table: ተቆረጠ, አሰቆረጠ, አቆራረጠ, ተቆራረጠ — the ተ- family, the አስ-/አ- family, and the doubled-middle forms.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Words That Go Together (word level). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Peel a suffix off a word and name both pieces (ክፍያውን → ክፍያ + ውን).
2. Split a word into prefix + stem + suffix and build the በላ family.
3. Say whose house ቤትህ, ቤትሽ, ቤቴ and ቤታችን each is.
4. Build an adjective from a stem with -አዊ, -አማ / -ኣም or a -ኛ-family suffix, and name the suffix you used.
5. Grow the family of a verb root: give its ተ- form and its አስ-/አ- form, on the models ሰፋ and ቆረጠ.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot see the piece → cover the stem with a finger: in ቤትህ, cover ቤት — the ህ left standing is the piece; it tells you whose.
- Mixes prefix and suffix → pieces before the stem stand at the front (እንደ-, ስለ-); pieces after it ride at the back (-አች, -ምች, -ህ, -ሽ, -ኤ, -አችን).
- Reads እንደበላ as a new word to memorise → build it: እንደ + በላ. The stem stays በላ; only the rider changes.
- Meets ኢትዮጵያዊ as a new word → peel it: ኢትዮጵያ + -አዊ. The suffix is the adjective-maker; the stem is the place.
- Cannot find the root inside ተቆራረጠ → peel from the front (ተ-) and look at the doubled middle: the root ቆረጠ is still standing inside its family.
