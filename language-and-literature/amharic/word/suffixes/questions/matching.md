# Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ — matching

Match each house word to its owner.

1. (Sub lesson 3) ቤቴ — a) your house — said to a girl or woman
2. (Sub lesson 3) ቤትህ — b) our house
3. (Sub lesson 3) ቤትሽ — c) my house
4. (Sub lesson 3) ቤታችን — d) your house — said to a boy or man

**Answer key:** 1 — c, 2 — d, 3 — a, 4 — b.
