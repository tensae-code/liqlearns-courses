# Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ — short answer

Answer in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peel ዝንጀሮን into its word and its suffix, on the model ክፍያውን → ውን.
**Answer: ዝንጀሮ + ን — the suffix is ን.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one piece that goes before a stem and one that goes after it.
**Answer: before: እንደ- or ስለ-; after: -አች or -ምች.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Split ዝናባማነት into its three pieces.
**Answer: ዝናብ + -ኣማ + -ነት.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the four grown forms of ቆረጠ in the review table.
**Answer: ተቆረጠ, አሰቆረጠ, አቆራረጠ, ተቆራረጠ.**
