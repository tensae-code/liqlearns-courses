# Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In ክፍያውን, the suffix (ቅጥያ) is —
a) ክፍ  b) ውን  c) ያ
**Answer: b) ውን** — peel it off and ክፍያ stands clear.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When you strip the suffixes off አህያቸው, the plain word left is —
a) አህያ  b) ቸው  c) አህያቸው stays whole
**Answer: a) አህያ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the split prefix + stem + suffix, እንደ- and ስለ- stand —
a) after the stem  b) before the stem  c) inside the stem
**Answer: b) before the stem** — they are prefixes; -አች and -ምች come after.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The stem of እንደበላ, ስለበላ, እስበላ, ሊበላ, ቢበላ is —
a) እንደ  b) ሊ  c) በላ
**Answer: c) በላ** — the stem never changes; only the riders change.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ቤታችን means —
a) my house  b) your house  c) our house
**Answer: c) our house** — the piece -አችን says "ours".
