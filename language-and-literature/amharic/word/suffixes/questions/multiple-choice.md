# Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In ክፍያውን, the suffix (ቅጥያ) is —
a) ክፍ  b) ውን  c) ያ
**Answer: b) ውን** — peel it off and ክፍያ stands clear.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When you strip the suffixes off አህያቸው, the plain word left is —
a) አህያ  b) ቸው  c) አህያቸው stays whole
**Answer: a) አህያ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the split prefix + stem + suffix, እንደ- and ስለ- stand —
a) after the stem  b) before the stem  c) inside the stem
**Answer: b) before the stem** — they are prefixes; -አች and -ምች come after.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The stem of እንደበላ, ስለበላ, እስበላ, ሊበላ, ቢበላ is —
a) እንደ  b) ሊ  c) በላ
**Answer: c) በላ** — the stem never changes; only the riders change.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ቤታችን means —
a) my house  b) your house  c) our house
**Answer: c) our house** — the piece -አችን says "ours".

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** ኢትዮጵያ + -አዊ builds —
a) ኢትዮጵያዊ  b) ኢትዮጵያማ  c) ኢትዮጵያተኛ
**Answer: a) ኢትዮጵያዊ** — the -አዊ family: of / from a place.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In ሕመምተኛ (the patient), the adjective-making suffix is —
a) -አዊ  b) -ተኛ  c) -ዎች
**Answer: b) -ተኛ** — ሕመም + -ተኛ: the one with the illness.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** Which form belongs to the ተ- family of ቆረጠ?
a) አሰቆረጠ  b) ተቆረጠ  c) አቆራረጠ
**Answer: b) ተቆረጠ** — the ተ- family; አሰቆረጠ is the አስ-/አ- family.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The root standing inside ተቆራረጠ is —
a) ቆረጠ  b) ተቆ  c) ራረጠ
**Answer: a) ቆረጠ** — however big the grown form, the root stands inside it.

**10. (Sub lesson 6)** A prefix (ቅድመ ገብ ቅጥያ) is a piece that enters —
a) after the word  b) before the word  c) between the word's fidel
**Answer: b) before the word** — a suffix (ድህረ ገብ ቅጥያ) is the piece that enters after.

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** In the guide's keyed answers, ኢ- is —
a) a suffix  b) a whole word on its own  c) a prefix — standing together with -አዊ in one keyed word
**Answer: c) a prefix** — one word can carry a piece at each end: ኢ- before, -አዊ after.

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** Which of these pieces is a SUFFIX in the guide's key?
a) መ-  b) -ኦሽ  c) ኢ-
**Answer: b) -ኦሽ** — መ- and ኢ- are prefixes; -ኦሽ, like -አዊ and -ነት, enters after.
