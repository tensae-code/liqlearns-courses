# Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right piece or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ልጅ + ______ = ልጅህ.
**Answer: ህ**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ + በላ = ስለበላ.
**Answer: ስለ**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "My house" is ቤት______.
**Answer: ኤ** — ቤቴ.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** ድንጋይ + ______ = ድንጋይአማ.
**Answer: -አማ** — the full-of family.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** ሆድ + ______ = ሆዳም.
**Answer: -ኣም.**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** ሰፋ → አሰፋ, ______, ተስፋፋ, አስፋፋ.
**Answer: ተሰፋ** — the ተ- form of the family.

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** Growing a family of verbs from one root is called ______.
**Answer: ሥርወቃል.**
