# Little Pieces on Words — ቅጥያ — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Suffixes on verbs
Pass mark: peel the suffix off ክፍያውን, ቤትህ, ልጅህ, ዝንጀሮን and name what is left, on the books' model.

## Sub lesson 2 — Prefixes and suffixes
Pass mark: split a word into prefix + stem + suffix and build all five በላ forms (እንደበላ, ስለበላ, እስበላ, ሊበላ, ቢበላ).

## Sub lesson 3 — Whose house?
Pass mark: give ቤቴ, ቤትህ, ቤትሽ, ቤታችን for my house, your house (boy), your house (girl), our house — and name the piece that carries the person.
