# Video script — What a Word Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at this word. **ደረሰ**.

A boy walks. He comes to his school.
**አበል ትምህርትቤት ደረሰ።** Abel ARRIVED.
Here, ደረሰ means: he came. He arrived.

Now look. A man sits. He writes a book.
**ደራሲው መጽሐፍ ደረሰ።** The author WROTE.
Here, ደረሰ means: he wrote.

Same word. Two meanings.
How do you know? Read the whole sentence.
The words around it tell you. This is **አውዳዊ ፍቺ** —
the meaning from the sentence.

And some words wear a second coat.
**ኤርግብ** — a dove. A quiet bird.
Call a person ኤርግብ, and he is gentle. Innocent.
**አንበሳ** — a lion.
Call a person አንበሳ, and he is brave. Strong.
That extra meaning is **ፍካሬያዊ ፍቺ**.

Last thing. What is a word?
Sounds, in order, with a meaning.
**መስጠል** — sounds in order. A real word.
**ስለጠል** — sounds all mixed. No meaning. NOT a word.

A word is sounds in order, carrying a meaning.
Now you know what a word means — and what a word is.

And the higher classes name the two coats.
**እማሬያዊ ፍች** — the direct meaning. The dictionary meaning.
**መሬት** — earth, land. In every sentence, the same.
**ፍካሬያዊ ፍች** — the layered meaning. It leans on the context.
**መብረቅ** — lightning. Flash and thunder — that is the direct one.
But call an event መብረቅ… and it is powerfully shocking.
That is the layer.
Two coats. One word. Now you can sort them.
