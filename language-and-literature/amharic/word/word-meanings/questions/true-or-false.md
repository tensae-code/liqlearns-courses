# What a Word Means — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A word's meaning is always the same, in every sentence.
**Answer: False** — ደረሰ means "arrived" in one sentence and
"wrote" in another; the sentence around the word decides.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A figurative meaning is an extra meaning a word carries beyond its direct meaning.
**Answer: True** — that is the printed definition of ፍካሬያዊ ፍቺ.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** ስለጠል is a word because it is made of letters.
**Answer: False** — letters alone are not enough; its sounds
stand out of order and give no meaning, so it is not a word.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** A denotative meaning changes from sentence to sentence, because it depends on the context.
**Answer: False** — the denotative (direct) meaning does NOT
depend on the context; the connotative (layered) meaning is
the one that does.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** መሬት's denotative meaning is "earth / land".
**Answer: True** — that is the guide's printed worked example
of a direct, dictionary meaning.

**6. (Sub lesson 9)** Dictation in this lesson means writing heard words without dropping letters.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 10)** ነፍሰጡር means pregnant, defined dictionary-style in this lesson.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 11)** This Grade 7 work stands beside the 'how to write a biography' lesson on the same page.
**Answer: True**

**9. (Sub lesson 12)** እማሬያዊ ፍቺ is the direct, dictionary meaning.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 13)** Each job of a two-job word must be proved by its own sentence.
