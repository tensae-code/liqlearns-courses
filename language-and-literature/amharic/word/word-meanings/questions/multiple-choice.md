# What a Word Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "አበል ትምህርትቤት በሰዓቱ ደረሰ" (Abel ደረሰ at school on time), ደረሰ means —
a) he wrote  b) he arrived  c) he ate
**Answer: b) he arrived** — the boy and the school show the
"arrived" meaning (መጣ).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "ደራሲው መጽሐፍ ደረሰ" (The author ደረሰ a book), ደረሰ means —
a) he arrived  b) he slept  c) he wrote
**Answer: c) he wrote** — the author and the book show the
"wrote" meaning (ጽፈ).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** አውዳዊ ፍቺ (contextual meaning) is —
a) the meaning a word has in the dictionary only
b) the meaning a word takes from the sentence around it
c) the opposite of a word
**Answer: b)** — a word's exact meaning is known when it stands
with other words.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ድምጽ can mean "voice". Its other meaning is —
a) vote  b) hand  c) road
**Answer: a) vote.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** When a person is called አንበሳ (a lion), the figurative meaning is —
a) he has four legs  b) he is brave and strong  c) he lives in the forest
**Answer: b)** — the lion's strength travels onto the person.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Which one IS a word (ቃል)?
a) ስለጠል  b) መስጠል  c) both of them
**Answer: b) መስጠል** — its sounds stand in order and give a
meaning; ስለጠል is a shuffle with no meaning.

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** እማሬያዊ ፍች (denotative meaning) is —
a) the meaning a word takes from its context  b) a word's direct, dictionary meaning  c) a word's opposite meaning
**Answer: b)** — the direct meaning does not depend on the
context; it is what the word says wherever it stands.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** መብረቅ (lightning) — its connotative meaning is —
a) flash and thunder  b) rain on the roof  c) powerfully shocking
**Answer: c) powerfully shocking** — flash and thunder is its
denotative meaning; the shocking layer is the connotative one.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** A connotative meaning (ፍካሬያዊ ፍች) depends on —
a) the context — the words around the word and the world around the words  b) nothing at all  c) the dictionary alone
**Answer: a)** — the layered meaning leans on the linguistic
and the non-linguistic context.

**10. (Sub lesson 6)** What does እግረኛ mean?
a) One who goes on foot — a pedestrian  b) A taxi driver  c) A shopkeeper
**Answer: a) One who goes on foot — a pedestrian** — ዜብራው's word set, Grade 1 p.82.

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** What does ኮሚቴ mean?
a) A committee  b) A market  c) A committee's chair only
**Answer: a) A committee** — Grade 1 p.105.

**12. (Sub lesson 7)** Which verb cooks ቂጣ?
a) ይጋገራል — it is baked  b) ይገነፋል  c) ይጠመቃል
**Answer: a) ይጋገራል — it is baked** — the cooking-verb matching, Grade 2 p.116.

**13. (Sub lesson 8)** What does ምህረት mean in the Loyal Friend's word set?
a) Mercy  b) Boldness  c) A public square
**Answer: a) Mercy** — ይቅርታ ማድረግ is apologising; ድፍረት is boldness.

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** ቤት in its FIGURATIVE meaning can mean what?
a) Family, life  b) Only a building  c) A roof
**Answer: a) Family, life** — the organiser's own two columns.

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** ጋሻ — 'shield' — used figuratively means what?
a) A protector  b) A weapon only  c) A door
**Answer: a) A protector** — the review's figurative sentences.


**16. (Sub lesson 9)** ጥላ can mean a hat — and also —
a) a river  b) shade  c) a song
**Answer: b) shade** — ጥላ is a hat, and also the shade under a tree.

**17. (Sub lesson 10)** In the organiser, a word gets —
a) only its dictionary meaning  b) both its text meaning and its dictionary meaning  c) only a picture
**Answer: b) both its text meaning and its dictionary meaning** — the organiser contrasts the two meanings side by side.

**18. (Sub lesson 11)** When a known word appears in a new sentence, you should —
a) keep its old sense automatically  b) read the new sentence and let it choose the sense  c) skip the word
**Answer: b) read the new sentence and let it choose the sense** — a new sentence can choose a different sense — read it afresh.

**19. (Sub lesson 12)** ጎጆ figuratively means —
a) a grass roof  b) marriage / home  c) a river bank
**Answer: b) marriage / home** — literally a grass-roofed hut; figuratively marriage and home.

**20. (Sub lesson 13)** መለመለ in 'he plucked the leaves' means —
a) recruited  b) plucked  c) marched
**Answer: b) plucked** — with the tree's leaves, መለመለ is plucking — not recruiting.

**21. (Sub lesson 14)** ፈነዳ used of a man bursting into laughter is —
a) its literal use  b) its figurative use  c) a spelling error
**Answer: b) its figurative use** — a ball bursts literally; laughter 'bursts' figuratively.

**22. (Sub lesson 15)** ተሟላ in the model means —
a) his desire was satisfied  b) what was lacking was completed  c) he repented
**Answer: b) what was lacking was completed** — ተሟላ: the lacking money was completed today; ረካ is desire reaching its measure.

**23. (Sub lesson 16)** A sentence starter must be completed so that —
a) it changes the subject  b) sense and grammar both continue the half given  c) it ends at once
**Answer: b) sense and grammar both continue the half given** — the completion continues the starter's thought in sense and grammar.

**24. (Sub lesson 17)** መዘዝ in this set belongs with —
a) a celebration  b) a consequence / aftermath  c) a harvest
**Answer: b) a consequence / aftermath** — መዘዝ is a consequence — as in ጫት መቃምና መዘዙ, chat chewing and its consequences.

**25. (Sub lesson 18)** ሰማይ ጠቀስ — 'sky-touching' — means —
a) a tall mountain  b) a skyscraper  c) a storm cloud
**Answer: b) a skyscraper** — in the diaspora essay it names America's sky-touching buildings.

**26. (Sub lesson 19)** A word's meaning in this work is taken from —
a) its first letter  b) the sentence it stands in  c) its length
**Answer: b) the sentence it stands in** — context decides — each word is read inside its own sentence.

**27. (Sub lesson 20)** ልቧ ተሰበረ should be read —
a) word by word only  b) as a whole phrase — her heart broke / she was grieved  c) as a spelling exercise
**Answer: b) as a whole phrase — her heart broke / she was grieved** — phrases carry their meaning whole; splitting them loses it.

**28. (Sub lesson 21)** ፍትህ means —
a) a proclamation  b) justice  c) a pack animal
**Answer: b) justice** — ፍትህ is justice — the chapter's king-and-donkey story is built on it.

**29. (Sub lesson 22)** 'The lamps did not shine by themselves' means —
a) lamps need oil  b) hidden work by others makes a person shine  c) generators are expensive
**Answer: b) hidden work by others makes a person shine** — the lamp-and-generator saying: the mother, unseen, made the son's shining possible.

**30. (Sub lesson 23)** ወረርሽኝ means —
a) a harvest  b) an epidemic  c) a wedding
**Answer: b) an epidemic** — ወረርሽኝ is an epidemic — one of the decline causes in the demography reading.

**31. (Sub lesson 24)** የገበታ መዓት — 'a table-full crowd' — is Belayhun's complaint about —
a) filming costs  b) the crowd a return feast must feed  c) the iddir
**Answer: b) the crowd a return feast must feed** — he asks what a table-full crowd will DO — the return feast's cost is his despair.

**32. (Sub lesson 25)** In the Nuru chapter, a bigar collects a cat's —
a) prices  b) describing words (ጸጉራም, ቆንጆ…)  c) owners
**Answer: b) describing words (ጸጉራም, ቆንጆ…)** — the cat bigar gathers describing words and facts about the cat.

**33. (Sub lesson 26)** መተባበር means —
a) competing  b) working together / mutual cooperation  c) celebrating
**Answer: b) working together / mutual cooperation** — the volunteers' story is built on መተባበር — working together.

**34. (Sub lesson 27)** ሞዛይክ is —
a) a painter  b) a mosaic — a picture built from coloured pieces  c) a stamp
**Answer: b) a mosaic — a picture built from coloured pieces** — ሞዛይክ names the art Afewerk studied abroad — pieces forming a picture.

**35. (Sub lesson 28)** የደም እንባ — 'tears of blood' — means —
a) joy  b) grief so deep it is figured as blood  c) anger
**Answer: b) grief so deep it is figured as blood** — the journey story's grief vocabulary: tears figured as blood.
