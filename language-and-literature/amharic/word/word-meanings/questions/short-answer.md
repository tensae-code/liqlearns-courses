# What a Word Means — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the two meanings of ወጥቼ.
**Answer:** "I went out" and "I came first in class" — one
word, two meanings, told apart by the sentence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** አዕምሮ means "the mind". What is its second meaning in the books' sentence pair?
**Answer:** ripened / matured.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Say the two tests a thing must pass to be a word (ቃል).
**Answer:** (1) its sounds stand in order; (2) together they
give a meaning.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the two kinds of meaning, and say which one depends on the context.
**Answer:** denotative (እማሬያዊ ፍች) — the direct, dictionary
meaning, which does NOT depend on context; and connotative
(ፍካሬያዊ ፍች) — the layered meaning, which DOES depend on the
context, in the words and beyond the words.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A word in a poem can carry two meanings at once. Which two?
**Answer:** its denotative meaning (the direct one) and its
connotative meaning (the layered one) — the reader holds the
two apart.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Give the meaning of ምክር and use it in a sentence.
**Answer:** ምክር — advice; any sentence using it as advice given or taken (Grade 1, pp.115–116).

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Match: ወጥ and ጠላ to their verbs.
**Answer:** ወጥ ይሠራል (wat is made); ጠላ ይጠመቃል (tela is brewed) (Grade 2, p.116).

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** Say what a camera is used for, the book's pattern.
**Answer:** በካሜራ ፊልም ይታያል — with a camera, pictures/films are taken and seen (Grade 2, pp.127–128).

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Name two of the p.4 two-meaning words.
**Answer:** Any two of: ጥላ, የሰፈሩ, ፈጀ, አብረው, ጣለች.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** What two meanings does the organiser compare for each word?
**Answer:** The contextual (text) meaning and the dictionary meaning.

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** What changes when the same word takes a different sense?
**Answer:** The sentence around it — the new context selects the new meaning.

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** Name the Grade 7 pair of terms for the two kinds of meaning.
**Answer:** እማሬያዊ ፍቺ (direct/literal) and ፍካሬያዊ ፍቺ (figurative).

**13. (Sub lesson 13)** What are the two jobs of መለመለ in the model?
**Answer:** Recruiting (youths for the army) and plucking (leaves from a tree).

**14. (Sub lesson 14)** What two uses must you show for each word in this set?
**Answer:** A literal use and a figurative use, each in its own sentence.

**15. (Sub lesson 15)** What does ረካ mean in the model?
**Answer:** His desire was satisfied — it reached its measure.

**16. (Sub lesson 16)** What do you write after learning each word's contextual meaning here?
**Answer:** A new sentence of your own built from that word.

**17. (Sub lesson 17)** Which chapter's reading do these words come from?
**Answer:** The addiction chapter — the chat-chewing reading and its set.

**18. (Sub lesson 18)** Explain 'breathing, I suffocate' in the essay's sense.
**Answer:** Though he breathes freely abroad, he feels suffocated — Ethiopianness in him finds the foreign place too narrow.

**19. (Sub lesson 19)** Name three words of the pp.53–54 set.
**Answer:** Any three of: ጥልቅ, ቀጠለ, ተማረ, ተቀበሉ, አረፉ, አቀኑ, በቃ, ቅንብር.

**20. (Sub lesson 20)** Name three words of the pp.8–9 set.
**Answer:** Any three of: እምነተቢስ, ካዝና, እየመዘዘ, ባለፀጋ, ደመወዝ, ዘመድ አዝማድ, ቋሚ ንብረት, ማኅበራዊ ጉዳይ.

**21. (Sub lesson 21)** What is አዋጅ?
**Answer:** A proclamation — the king's cried order after the donkey's case.

**22. (Sub lesson 22)** Explain 'these scars are his scars'.
**Answer:** The mother's rough, wounded hands were damaged by the washing work that paid for his education — her scars were earned for him, so they are his.

**23. (Sub lesson 23)** What does ማሽቆልቆል mean in this chapter?
**Answer:** Decline / shrinking — a population falling in number.

**24. (Sub lesson 24)** Name four trees from the book's tree-name list.
**Answer:** Any four of: ጓሣ, ጅብራ, ጦስኝ, አስታ, ፅድ, ኮሶ, ዋንዛ, ዝግባ, ወይራ, አጣጥ.

**25. (Sub lesson 25)** Name three words of the Chapter 9 proving set.
**Answer:** Any three of: ውህድ, ነውጥ, መንስኤ, ጎጂ, ብክለት, ይራቆታል, ንፁህ, ጠጣር, መፈራቀቅ, ዑደት.

**26. (Sub lesson 26)** Name three words of the p.41 set.
**Answer:** Any three of: መከፈል, መተቃቀፍ, ጠባይ, አስተዋፅዖ, ማስተናበር, ዋዜማ, ሥፍራ, መተባበር, የሚተም.

**27. (Sub lesson 27)** What is a ሎሬት?
**Answer:** A laureate — one publicly honoured for excellence, as in Afewerk's world title.

**28. (Sub lesson 28)** What must your built sentence preserve from the reading?
**Answer:** The word's contextual meaning — the same meaning, in your own sentence.

**29. (Sub lesson 36)** What material does the Grade 7 guide name for teaching common and uncommon words, and what do learners do with the uncommon ones?
**Answer:** A chart (ሰንጠረዥ); learners identify the uncommon words and write them.

**30. (Sub lesson 37)** The Grade 8 guide's vocabulary work is bound to what, chapter by chapter?
**Answer:** To each chapter's own text — its words are taken from the text just read.

**31. (Sub lesson 38)** Give the Grade 9 guide's keyed meaning of መጋጨት.
**Answer:** መጣላት / አለመስማማት — conflict, disagreement.

**32. (Sub lesson 39)** Why does the Grade 9 guide print no fixed antonym key for Chapter 8?
**Answer:** Because acceptable antonyms vary — the guide leaves them open, to be confirmed with the learners.

**33. (Sub lesson 40)** How are the technology and IT profession words learned in the Grade 10 guide?
**Answer:** Their meanings are found from websites, presented in class, and used in sentences.
