# What a Word Means — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the two meanings of ወጥቼ.
**Answer:** "I went out" and "I came first in class" — one
word, two meanings, told apart by the sentence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** አዕምሮ means "the mind". What is its second meaning in the books' sentence pair?
**Answer:** ripened / matured.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Say the two tests a thing must pass to be a word (ቃል).
**Answer:** (1) its sounds stand in order; (2) together they
give a meaning.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the two kinds of meaning, and say which one depends on the context.
**Answer:** denotative (እማሬያዊ ፍች) — the direct, dictionary
meaning, which does NOT depend on context; and connotative
(ፍካሬያዊ ፍች) — the layered meaning, which DOES depend on the
context, in the words and beyond the words.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** A word in a poem can carry two meanings at once. Which two?
**Answer:** its denotative meaning (the direct one) and its
connotative meaning (the layered one) — the reader holds the
two apart.
