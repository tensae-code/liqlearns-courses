# What a Word Means — matching

Match each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

1. (Sub lesson 3) ኤርግብ — a) brave, strong
2. (Sub lesson 3) አንበሳ — b) watchful, alert
3. (Sub lesson 3) ቆቅ — c) innocent, gentle
4. (Sub lesson 2) መሪ (in a sentence about a country) — d) a leader

**Answer key:** 1 — c (the dove → an innocent, gentle person);
2 — a (the lion → a brave, strong person); 3 — b (the
francolin → a watchful, alert person); 4 — d (መሪ of a country
is its leader; መሪ holding a blind person's hand is a guide).

More matching — the two kinds of meaning:

5. (Sub lesson 5) መሬት, denotative — a) powerfully shocking
6. (Sub lesson 5) መብረቅ, denotative — b) earth / land
7. (Sub lesson 5) መብረቅ, connotative — c) flash and thunder

**Answer key (new rows):** 5 — b (መሬት's direct meaning is
earth / land); 6 — c (መብረቅ's direct meaning is flash and
thunder); 7 — a (መብረቅ's layered meaning is powerfully
shocking).
