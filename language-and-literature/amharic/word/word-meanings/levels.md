# What a Word Means — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons run from the sentence around
the word, to the word's several lives, to its extra figurative
coat, to what makes a word a word at all.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meaning from the sentence around it
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: given a
sentence with ደረሰ (or another placed two-meaning word), say
which meaning is alive and name the words in the sentence that
decide it.

## Sub lesson 2 — One word, many meanings
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give two
meanings for ድምጽ and for one more word from the student books'
sets (ወጥቼ, መሪ, አዕምሮ, ማረኩ, ጥማት), each inside its own
sentence.

## Sub lesson 3 — When a word means more — figurative meanings
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what
ፍካሬያዊ ፍቺ is and give the figurative meanings of ኤርግብ and
አንበሳ (and ቆቅ or ቡግ) from the table.

## Sub lesson 4 — What makes a word a word
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state
the two tests of ቃል (sounds in order; the sounds give meaning)
and judge መስጠል (a word) against ስለጠል (not a word) with them.

## Sub lesson 5 — Two kinds of meaning: denotative and connotative
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define
እማሬያዊ ፍች (denotative — direct, dictionary, not depending on
context) and ፍካሬያዊ ፍች (connotative — layered, depending on
context), and sort መሬት and መብረቅ's meanings with them —
including መብረቅ's two coats, flash-and-thunder and powerfully
shocking. This sub lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only.
