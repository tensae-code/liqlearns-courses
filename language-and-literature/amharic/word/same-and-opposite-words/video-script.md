# Video script — Words That Go Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some words are friends. They say the same thing.

**በላ** (bela). **ተመገበ** (temegebe). Both say: ate!
**ሄደ** (hede). **ተራመደ** (teramede). Both say: went!
**ትልቅ** (tilq). **ግዙፍ** (gizuf). Both say: big!

Same meaning. Two ways to say it.

Some words face away.

**መጣ** (met'a) — came. **ሄደ** (hede) — went. Opposite!
**ጥቁር** (t'iqur) — black. **ነጭ** (nech') — white. Opposite!
**ትልቅ** (tilq) — big. **ትንሽ** (tinsh) — small. Opposite!

See? ትልቅ has two partners. ግዙፍ says the same. ትንሽ says the
opposite.

Now you. I say a word. You say its partner.
Same? Or opposite? You tell me.
