# Words That Go Together — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the two halves of one
thing — words that pair up: first the pairs that mean the same,
then the pairs that mean the opposite.

## Sub lesson 1 — Words that mean the same
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read the
eight same-meaning pairs and, given one word, say or pick the word
that means the same — በላ → ተመገበ, ሄደ → ተራመደ, ትልቅ → ግዙፍ.

## Sub lesson 2 — Words that mean the opposite
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: read the
ten opposite pairs and, given one word, say or pick its opposite —
መጣ → ሄደ, ጥቁር → ነጭ, ትልቅ → ትንሽ, ፈጣን → ቀርፋፋ — including
telling ትልቅ's opposite (ትንሽ) apart from its same-word (ግዙፍ).
