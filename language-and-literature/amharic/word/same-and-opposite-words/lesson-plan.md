# Lesson plan — Words That Go Together

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Words That Go Together
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Word (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** some words mean the same as
each other, and some words mean the opposite of each other — and a
reader who knows both can say one thing in two ways.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own world —
the learner already speaks these words at home (ate, went, big,
black, fast); this lesson only pairs them up in print. It needs
nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Words that mean the same** — pairs that say the same thing two ways: በላ–ተመገበ, ሄደ–ተራመደ, ትልቅ–ግዙፍ and their friends.
2. **Words that mean the opposite** — pairs that face away from each other: መጣ–ሄደ, ጥቁር–ነጭ, ትልቅ–ትንሽ, ፈጣን–ቀርፋፋ and their friends.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is
the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Fidel lessons — the learner can read the
  words themselves. Recommended, never forced.
- The word lessons next door (naming words, doing words) help, but
  this lesson stands on spoken words the child already owns.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a same-meaning pair is and give one: በላ and ተመገበ
   both tell about eating.
2. Read the eight same-meaning pairs and match a word to the word
   that means the same.
3. Say what an opposite pair is and give one: ጥቁር (black) faces
   ነጭ (white).
4. Read the ten opposite pairs and match a word to its opposite.
5. Notice that ትልቅ (big) lives in both halves: its same-word is
   ግዙፍ (huge) and its opposite is ትንሽ (small).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Two small stones or bottle tops help for the
hands-on sorting game.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner says three words for eating,
   going, and looking, in any way they know, then hears that the
   lesson pairs up the two ways of saying each one
   (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — same-meaning pairs first, read and
   copy-written; then opposite pairs, read and matched
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8210]: a picture of a big tree and a small
     tree side by side, the big one labelled ትልቅ and the small
     one ትንሽ — the student points to the tree each word names.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8211]: a picture of a big black circle and
     a big white circle, labelled ጥቁር and ነጭ — the student
     covers the word and says it from the colour, then checks.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8212]: a picture of two runners, one
     leaning forward fast with motion lines (ፈጣን) and one walking
     slowly upright (ቀርፋፋ) — the student says which runner each
     word fits, and why.
   - ADD AUDIO in both sub lessons: a voice reading each pair
     aloud, word — pause — its partner — the student says the
     partner before the voice does on the second hearing.
3. **Say it to your companion** — trade pairs: one says a word,
   the other answers with its same-word or its opposite
   (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the
     pairing game in the super-simple voice (made later, when
     videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching,
   true-or-false — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix
   (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — find one same-pair and one opposite-pair
   in your own home talk today (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes same and opposite → ask: "Do the two words shake hands
  (same) or face away (opposite)?" Say both words in a sentence
  about one picture; if both fit the same picture, they are the
  same kind.
- Gives ትንሽ as the same-word for ትልቅ → ትንሽ is its opposite;
  the same-word is ግዙፍ. ትልቅ is the lesson's two-way word —
  walk it both ways slowly.
- Reads the pairs but cannot recall partners → cover one side of
  the pair with a finger and say the hidden word from memory,
  then slide the finger away to check.
