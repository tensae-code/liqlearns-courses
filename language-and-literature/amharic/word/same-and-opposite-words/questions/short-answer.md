# Words That Go Together — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 5)** Name three words of the Grade 7 synonyms set.
**Answer:** Any three of: ላሂ, ትዕይንት, ግዙፍ, አምባ, ባልጩት, ሀሴት, መቅረዝ, ኩልል, ሰጠ, ፍንትው.

**2. (Sub lesson 6)** Name three words of the antonyms set.
**Answer:** Any three of: ሰውሠራሽ, መስህብ, ግንዛቤ, ጉጉት, መርሐግብር, ቀስተ ደመና.

**3. (Sub lesson 7)** Name three words of the p.34 synonym set.
**Answer:** Any three of: አፍጥጦ, ይሸሻል, ቀበጥ, ለግላጋ, የተጨማደደ, እያንጎራጎረ, ላሰጣ, ተረዳ.

**4. (Sub lesson 8)** Name two antonym pairs from the bat chapter's set.
**Answer:** Any two of: ጦርነት/እርቅ, ድል/መጥፋት-side pairs as listed — war–reconciliation, victory–loss, sky–earth (ዳር), standing–fleeing (ቆመ/ፈራ).

**5. (Sub lesson 10)** Against what are the homework answers checked?
**Answer:** Against the mediation passage itself.
