# Words That Go Together — matching

Match each word to its partner.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 1)** — match each word to the word that means
the same.

1. በላ — a) ተናገረ
2. አወራ — b) ማዳመጥ
3. መስማት — c) ተመገበ
4. ትልቅ — d) ግዙፍ

**Answer key:** 1 — c) ተመገበ · 2 — a) ተናገረ · 3 — b) ማዳመጥ ·
4 — d) ግዙፍ.

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — match each word to its opposite.

1. መጣ — a) ነጭ
2. ጥቁር — b) ሩቅ
3. ጣፍጭ — c) ሄደ
4. ቅርብ — d) ቀጭን
5. ወፍራም — e) መራራ

**Answer key:** 1 — c) ሄደ (came–went) · 2 — a) ነጭ (black–white) ·
3 — e) መራራ (sweet–bitter) · 4 — b) ሩቅ (near–far) · 5 — d) ቀጭን
(thick–thin).
