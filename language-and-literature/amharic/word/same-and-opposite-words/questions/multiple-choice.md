# Words That Go Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word means the same as በላ (bela, ate)?
a) ሄደ  b) ተመገበ  c) መጣ
**Answer: b) ተመገበ** — both words tell about eating.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ሄደ (hede, went) — which word means the same?
a) ተራመደ  b) ቆመ  c) ሳቅ
**Answer: a) ተራመደ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair means the same thing?
a) ማየት – መመልከት  b) ጥቁር – ነጭ  c) ቅርብ – ሩቅ
**Answer: a) ማየት – መመልከት** — seeing and looking. The other two
pairs are opposites.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the opposite of ጥቁር (t'iqur, black)?
a) ትልቅ  b) ነጭ  c) ሰፊ
**Answer: b) ነጭ** — white faces away from black.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A runner who is ፈጣን is —
a) slow  b) fast  c) tired
**Answer: b) fast** — its opposite is ቀርፋፋ, slow.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** ትልቅ (tilq, big) has two partners in this
lesson. Its opposite is —
a) ግዙፍ  b) ትንሽ  c) ወፍራም
**Answer: b) ትንሽ** — small. ግዙፍ is its same-word, not its
opposite.
