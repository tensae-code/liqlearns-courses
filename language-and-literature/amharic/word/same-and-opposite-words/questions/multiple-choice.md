# Words That Go Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word means the same as በላ (bela, ate)?
a) ሄደ  b) ተመገበ  c) መጣ
**Answer: b) ተመገበ** — both words tell about eating.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ሄደ (hede, went) — which word means the same?
a) ተራመደ  b) ቆመ  c) ሳቅ
**Answer: a) ተራመደ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair means the same thing?
a) ማየት – መመልከት  b) ጥቁር – ነጭ  c) ቅርብ – ሩቅ
**Answer: a) ማየት – መመልከት** — seeing and looking. The other two
pairs are opposites.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the opposite of ጥቁር (t'iqur, black)?
a) ትልቅ  b) ነጭ  c) ሰፊ
**Answer: b) ነጭ** — white faces away from black.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A runner who is ፈጣን is —
a) slow  b) fast  c) tired
**Answer: b) fast** — its opposite is ቀርፋፋ, slow.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** ትልቅ (tilq, big) has two partners in this
lesson. Its opposite is —
a) ግዙፍ  b) ትንሽ  c) ወፍራም
**Answer: b) ትንሽ** — small. ግዙፍ is its same-word, not its
opposite.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** ሰፊ is the opposite of…
a) ጠባብ  b) ደስታ  c) ፍሬን
**Answer: a) ጠባብ** — ጠባብ ↔ ሰፊ, Grade 1 p.90.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** ሀዘን (sorrow) is the opposite of…
a) ደስታ (joy)  b) ሰላም  c) ምክር
**Answer: a) ደስታ (joy)** — ደስታ ↔ ሀዘን, Grade 1 p.90.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Opposite matching pairs a word with…
a) Its opposite meaning  b) Its plural  c) Its picture only
**Answer: a) Its opposite meaning** — the book's matching task.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** ያናጋል (stirs up) — its opposite?
a) ያረጋጋል — calms  b) ጥላቻ  c) ውስጣዊ
**Answer: a) ያረጋጋል — calms** — the book's matching pair.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** ውጫዊ (outer) — its opposite?
a) ውስጣዊ — inner  b) ፍቅር  c) ያረጋጋል
**Answer: a) ውስጣዊ — inner** — outer against inner.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** The antonym set is drawn from which essay?
a) "Littering Is Forbidden!" (ቆሻሻ መጣል ክልክል ነው!)  b) The Scale  c) Salt
**Answer: a) "Littering Is Forbidden!" (ቆሻሻ መጣል ክልክል ነው!)** — the hate-speech-as-litter essay.


**13. (Sub lesson 5)** አምባ is —
a) a waterfall  b) a flat-topped mountain  c) a songbird
**Answer: b) a flat-topped mountain** — አምባ is the flat-topped mountain of the 'Life on an Amba' reading.

**14. (Sub lesson 6)** ቀስተ ደመና means —
a) a bridge  b) a rainbow  c) a waterfall
**Answer: b) a rainbow** — ቀስተ ደመና is the rainbow — 'bow of the cloud' — seen over Tis Abay.

**15. (Sub lesson 7)** An opposite chosen in context must fit —
a) any meaning of the word  b) the meaning the word has in THIS text  c) the longest meaning
**Answer: b) the meaning the word has in THIS text** — context first: the opposite answers the meaning alive in the reading.

**16. (Sub lesson 8)** The opposite of ጦርነት (war) in this set is —
a) ድል  b) እርቅ (reconciliation/peace)  c) ሰማይ
**Answer: b) እርቅ (reconciliation/peace)** — the set pairs war with እርቅ — reconciliation, the hen's work in the fable.

**17. (Sub lesson 9)** Task 2 fills gaps with —
a) synonyms  b) antonyms of the bracketed words  c) any word
**Answer: b) antonyms of the bracketed words** — Antonyms.

**18. (Sub lesson 9)** After the tasks, learners —
a) exchange work and mark each other's  b) throw work away  c) only self-mark
**Answer: a) exchange work and mark each other's** — Swap and mark.

**19. (Sub lesson 9)** The synonym-match key has —
a) six answers  b) two answers  c) no answers
**Answer: a) six answers** — Six keyed answers.

**20. (Sub lesson 10)** The Grade 9 guide's sample synonym pair from the mediation reading is —
a) ቅደም ተከተል = የአሰራር ቅደም ተከተል  b) ቅደም ተከተል = የቤት ቅደም ተከተል  c) መጋጨት = መጣላት
**Answer: a) ቅደም ተከተል = የአሰራር ቅደም ተከተል**
