# Words That Go Together — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** በላ and ተመገበ mean the same thing.
**True** — both tell about eating.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ሳቅ (laughter) and ደስታ (joy) are opposites.
**False** — they are partners that mean the same; they sit in the
same-meaning list.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** መጣ (came) and ሄደ (went) are opposites.
**True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The opposite of ትልቅ (big) is ግዙፍ (huge).
**False** — ግዙፍ means the same as ትልቅ. Its opposite is ትንሽ
(small).
