# Hands-on — Words That Go Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Word cards, two piles
Write each word of the lesson's pairs on its own small card or
stone — one word per card. Shuffle them face up on the floor.
First round: find the pairs that mean the same and put them
together, hand in hand. Second round: start again and find the
pairs that mean the opposite, standing back to back. Say each
pair aloud as you lay it down.

## Big and small hunt
Walk around your home and find the biggest thing and the smallest
thing you can carry. Hold one in each hand and say: ትልቅ (tilq)
for the big one, ትንሽ (tinsh) for the small one — then say the
big one's other name too: ግዙፍ (gizuf).

## Fast feet, slow feet
In an open space: when your partner calls ፈጣን (fet'an), run
fast; when they call ቀርፋፋ (qerfafa), walk as slowly as a turtle.
Five calls each way, then swap. The words live in your legs now,
not only on the page.

## If it is hard
Work with only three pairs at a time — one same pair and two
opposite pairs — with the picture cards 8210, 8211 and 8212 in
front of you. Say each word while you point at its picture; add
more pairs only when these three are easy. Breaking the work
into small steps, with a picture for every word, is the support
the course builds in (SNE toolkit — SUPPORTING; see sources.md).
