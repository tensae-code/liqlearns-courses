Honesty notes: (1) Cross-reference decision (the brief's condition): the English course's dictionary lesson (english/word/using-the-dictionary) is built from Bangladesh, India and Mongolia books, and nothing in the Ethiopian placements draws that parallel — so this lesson carries NO cross-reference tip and stays Ethiopia-only, as the brief directs when the placements do not support the parallel. (2) The G4 book places the look-up tasks and the words to look up, but does not itself print a step-by-step finding method or an order rule. The lesson's one method sentence — that the dictionary's words stand in fidel order (ሀ ለ ሐ መ…) — is the standard working order of Amharic dictionaries, stated as the tool's own order so the placed task ("look up and write") can be done; it is flagged here as the lesson's single method addition, and no placed page is claimed for it. (3) The p.67 placement glosses four of its five words and leaves ማህደር unglossed; the lesson prints ማህደር without a gloss rather than inventing one. (4) Glosses for the p.107 set beyond ስጦታ (gift) and ትጋት (diligence — the reading's own theme, placement 147) are not printed in the placements, so the lesson names those two words as look-up words without glossing ማሳመን and መዘከር. (5) Picture 8457 is a drawn dictionary card made for this lesson from the placed look-up sets (p.80 set on the left page; ክፍለዘመን with the p.107 set's glossed words on the right) and was looked at before filing.
