# Hands-on — Using the Dictionary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cover, write, check
On a card, write ሰገነት, መደዳ, ደርብ, ክፍለዘመን down one side.
Cover the meanings in the lesson. Write your best meaning for
each word. Uncover and check. Any word you missed, say it
aloud three times with its meaning and write it once more.

## The five-step walk
Take one real page from any book at home. Read it with a
pencil in hand. Every word you cannot explain gets a small
mark. Then walk the five steps for the first marked word:
stop — visit the dictionary (or ask a person, as the books
also allow) — write the meaning — read on. Feel how the page
opens after the visit.

## My own word book
Start a small word book: a few folded pages. Every day, add
the words you looked up, each with its meaning in your own
writing. By the end of the month, read your word book to
someone — every entry is a word that once stopped you and
never will again.

## Word hunt at the library
At the school or public library, ask to hold the dictionary.
Find one word from this lesson inside it — ገዳም or ክፍለዘመን —
and show a friend where it stands and what stands beside it.
