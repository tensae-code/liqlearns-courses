# Video script — Using the Dictionary

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You are reading. A word stops you. You do not know it.
Do not sit stuck. There is a tool.

This is the **መዝገበቃላት** — the dictionary.
It is not a story. You do not read it from the start.
You visit it. One word. You take the meaning. You go back.

A hard word: **ሰገነት**. Look it up. It means: a balcony.
**መደዳ** — a row. **ደርብ** — a storey. **ክፍለዘመን** — a
century, one hundred years.
And write the meaning! A meaning you write is yours.

Old words from a monastery story:
**ገዳም** — a monastery. **ብራና** — parchment. **አምድ** — a
pillar. **ተዓምር** — a miracle.
Look them up first, and the story flows.

Read. Stop. Visit. Write. Read on.
Five steps. No word can bar your road.
