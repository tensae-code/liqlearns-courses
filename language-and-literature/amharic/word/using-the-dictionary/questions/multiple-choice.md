# Using the Dictionary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** መዝገበቃላት (the dictionary) is —
a) a story book you read from the first page to the last
b) a tool book you visit for one word's meaning
c) a book of songs
**Answer: b)** — you come for one word, take its meaning, and
go back to your reading.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grade 4 book, dictionary work is —
a) one of the new skills of Grade 4  b) not taught at all  c) only for teachers
**Answer: a)** — the book names it among the year's new
skills, with multiple-choice, true/false, summary and news
writing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ሰገነት means —
a) a row  b) a balcony  c) a century
**Answer: b) a balcony** — from the book's look-up set,
printed p.80.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ክፍለዘመን means —
a) a storey  b) a pillar  c) a century
**Answer: c) a century** — a hundred years.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ገዳም, from the monastery listening, means —
a) monastery  b) parchment  c) miracle
**Answer: a) monastery.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** ተዓምር means —
a) a gift  b) a miracle  c) diligence
**Answer: b) a miracle.**
