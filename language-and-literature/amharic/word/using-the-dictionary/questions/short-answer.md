# Using the Dictionary — short answer

Answer in a word or a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is using a dictionary different from reading a story book?
**Answer:** You do not read it front to back; you visit it
for one word, take the meaning, and return to your reading.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the four words of the book's visiting-chapter look-up set, with their meanings.
**Answer:** ሰገነት — balcony; መደዳ — row; ደርብ — storey;
ክፍለዘመን — century.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Say the five steps of the dictionary habit.
**Answer:** Read → stop at the unknown word → visit the
dictionary → write the meaning → read on.
