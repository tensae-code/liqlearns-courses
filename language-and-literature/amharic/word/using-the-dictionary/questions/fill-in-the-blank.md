# Using the Dictionary — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Amharic word for the dictionary is ______.
**Answer: መዝገበቃላት.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's dictionary task has two halves: look the word up, and ______ its definition.
**Answer: write** — a meaning you have written is a meaning
you keep.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the river reading, the village honours the expert for her ትጋት — her ______.
**Answer: diligence.**
