# Problem-and-Solution Essays — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first link of the book's solution chain?
**Answer:** Planting seedlings.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What links drought to poverty in the problem chain?
**Answer:** Soil erosion before it and failed harvests with it — the chain as the bigar gives it: erosion → drought → poverty and exile.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the solution chain's first two links.
**Answer:** Planting seedlings → forest expansion.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must the two chains match in shape?
**Answer:** So each harm is answered — a chain of causes is undone link by link, and the reader can check the answering.
