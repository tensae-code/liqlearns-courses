# Writing Essays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An expository essay (አስረጂ ድርሰት) —
a) tells a story in time order  b) explains a thing's nature, working or application  c) defends one side of an argument
**Answer: b) explains a thing's nature, working or application.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A paragraph's topic sentence (መንደርደሪያ ዓረፍተነገር) is —
a) its longest sentence  b) its last sentence, always  c) the sentence that carries the paragraph's whole idea
**Answer: c) the sentence that carries the paragraph's whole idea.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A narrative essay (ተራኪ ድርሰት) keeps —
a) the time sequence of its events  b) the alphabet order of its words  c) one paragraph only
**Answer: a) the time sequence of its events.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the volunteers organiser, "the elderly are kept from living in hardship" is —
a) the cause  b) an effect of volunteers existing  c) the topic sentence of the whole essay
**Answer: b) an effect of volunteers existing.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The argumentative essay the guides model is written in —
a) one paragraph  b) three paragraphs (ባለ ሦስት አንቀፅ ድርሰት)  c) ten paragraphs
**Answer: b) three paragraphs (ባለ ሦስት አንቀፅ ድርሰት).**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** In the organised essay, the essay is written from —
a) one web that holds the whole topic  b) the last page of the book  c) a list copied from a friend
**Answer: a) one web that holds the whole topic.**
