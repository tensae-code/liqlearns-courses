# Writing Essays — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The narrator of a narrative essay may be a participant in the events, an observer, or one who heard the story from another.
**Answer: True** — all three narrator places are named in the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A sentence that only says one thing happened after another is already a cause-and-effect sentence.
**Answer: False** — the cause must MAKE the effect happen; mere order is sequence, not cause.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** An argumentative essay is a quarrel on paper, won by the loudest words.
**Answer: False** — it states a position and defends it with reasons in order, following the model.
