# Writing Essays — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The narrator of a narrative essay may be a participant in the events, an observer, or one who heard the story from another.
**Answer: True** — all three narrator places are named in the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A sentence that only says one thing happened after another is already a cause-and-effect sentence.
**Answer: False** — the cause must MAKE the effect happen; mere order is sequence, not cause.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** An argumentative essay is a quarrel on paper, won by the loudest words.
**Answer: False** — it states a position and defends it with reasons in order, following the model.

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** The explanatory paragraph (አስረጅ አንቀጽ) and the explanatory essay do the same explaining craft at two sizes — paragraph and essay.
**Answer: True** — both make a thing clear to a reader; the paragraph does it for one process or part, the essay at full size.

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** In the higher classes, the explanatory essay is written alone, on any topic, with no prompts and no review first.
**Answer: False** — the craft and the essay types are reviewed first, three prompts are given, and the writing is done in pairs or groups.

**6. (Sub lesson 8)** The family-planning essay is written from gathered information, not from opinion alone.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 9)** The Advice to Mothers essay is written as if by a health professional.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 10)** The set passage edited at pp.70–71 is a patriotism passage taken from the Grade 8 book.
**Answer: True**

**9. (Sub lesson 11)** The professions web holds eight professions, from agriculture to industry.
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 12)** In the tortoise outline, the hare sleeps under a tree in the last race and the tortoise passes quietly.
**Answer: True**

**11. (Sub lesson 13)** The narrator's story may be heard from another person — the book says so.
**Answer: True**

**12. (Sub lesson 14)** The bigar frame holds title, introduction, four paragraph boxes and conclusion.
**Answer: True**

**13. (Sub lesson 15)** A cause-and-effect essay is a chain, not a list — each link pulls the next.
**Answer: True**

**14. (Sub lesson 16)** The Grade 7 guide sets persuasive writing as two tasks with assessment.
**Answer: True**

**15. (Sub lesson 17)** A descriptive essay on a traditional game, written in groups of five, is set inside the Grade 9 guide's Chapter 7 grammar part.
**Answer: True**

**16. (Sub lesson 18)** The season essay is written from an outline tool (ቢጋር).
**Answer: True**

**17. (Sub lesson 19)** The correction repertoire has four hands: self, peer, group and teacher correction, alternated as needed.
**Answer: True**

**18. (Sub lesson 20)** The unity-and-diversity writing sequence builds sentences of different sizes first, then a paragraph organised around one idea.
**Answer: True**
