# Writing Essays — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a four-paragraph expository essay (አስረጂ ድርሰት) explaining how something is made or done near home. Then pick out and underline each paragraph's topic sentence.
**Answer guide: one organiser part per paragraph; each underlined sentence carries its paragraph's whole idea.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write a three-paragraph cause-and-effect essay from the organiser "የበጎፈቃደኖች መኖር" (volunteers exist), or from your own one-cause organiser.
**Answer guide: every paragraph's key sentences are built as cause and effect — each cause truly makes its effect happen.**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Build one web around a big topic with at least five branches, then write a complete essay from it, branch by branch.
**Answer guide: every branch on the web appears in the essay; nothing is in the essay that is not on the web.**

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** With a partner, write an explanatory paragraph (አስረጅ አንቀጽ) about one technology product, the model bank's way: name the product, then say what it is for, in order, for a reader who may never have seen it.
**Answer guide: the product is named; its uses are given in order; nothing is assumed the reader already knows.**
