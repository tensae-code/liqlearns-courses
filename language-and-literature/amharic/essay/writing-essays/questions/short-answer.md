# Writing Essays — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four organiser parts of the expository essay "የቡና አፈላል" (brewing coffee).
**Answer: prepare the materials; lighting the fire; from washing to pounding-grinding; serving and concluding.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the set narrative-essay topic?
**Answer: a river in your area, told in its time order.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the improved climate paragraph, what do plants take in, and what do they release?
**Answer: plants take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What do you read and study first, before writing your own argumentative essay?
**Answer: the given model of how an argumentative essay is written.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name any three branches of the web around ኤች አይ ቪ/ኤድስ.
**Answer: any three of — ምንነት (what it is), አመጣጥ (origin), አሁን ያለበት ሁኔታ (present state), ምልክቶች (signs), መተላለፊያ መንገዶች (transmission routes), ህክምና (treatment).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The higher-class explanatory essay is written from how many prompts, and with whom?
**Answer: from three given prompts, written in pairs or groups — after reviewing essay techniques and the essay types.**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Name four products from the technology model bank, and say what the computer is for.
**Answer: any four of — computer, photocopier, iron, laundry, hair-dryer (ካስክ), combine harvester, tractor, fridge. The computer is for writing, reading, film and internet.**
