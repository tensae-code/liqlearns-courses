# Writing Essays — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four organiser parts of the expository essay "የቡና አፈላል" (brewing coffee).
**Answer: prepare the materials; lighting the fire; from washing to pounding-grinding; serving and concluding.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the set narrative-essay topic?
**Answer: a river in your area, told in its time order.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the improved climate paragraph, what do plants take in, and what do they release?
**Answer: plants take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What do you read and study first, before writing your own argumentative essay?
**Answer: the given model of how an argumentative essay is written.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name any three branches of the web around ኤች አይ ቪ/ኤድስ.
**Answer: any three of — ምንነት (what it is), አመጣጥ (origin), አሁን ያለበት ሁኔታ (present state), ምልክቶች (signs), መተላለፊያ መንገዶች (transmission routes), ህክምና (treatment).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The higher-class explanatory essay is written from how many prompts, and with whom?
**Answer: from three given prompts, written in pairs or groups — after reviewing essay techniques and the essay types.**

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Name four products from the technology model bank, and say what the computer is for.
**Answer: any four of — computer, photocopier, iron, laundry, hair-dryer (ካስክ), combine harvester, tractor, fridge. The computer is for writing, reading, film and internet.**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** What are the spacing organiser's three boxes?
**Answer:** What spacing is; its benefit; the conclusion.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Name two social problems the p.80 essay offers.
**Answer:** Any two of: child labour exploitation, illegal trade, migration, unemployment.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** What is the p.86 cause-and-effect essay's title?
**Answer:** የሰው ሀገር መናፈቅ — longing for foreign lands.

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** Name three professions of the web.
**Answer:** Any three of: agriculture, engineering, trade, teaching, medicine, politics, the military, industry.

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** What was the clinic patient's test result in the outline?
**Answer:** Pneumonia (ሳንባ ምች) — found by blood, urine, stool and x-ray tests.

**13. (Sub lesson 13)** Name the four sources of a narrative in the definition.
**Answer:** The narrator's own experience; learned by practice; thought out; heard from another.

**14. (Sub lesson 14)** What essay is planned in the frame and later written?
**Answer:** የሕፃናት ጉልበት ብዝበዛ ዓይነቶች — Kinds of Child Labour Exploitation.

**15. (Sub lesson 15)** What story does the አውቆ አበድ essay build on?
**Answer:** The embezzlement story — the cash worker who feigned madness in court.

**16. (Sub lesson 16)** What does the Grade 8 guide's essay assessment check?
**Answer:** That all learners can write — the essay kept the language's proper structure (የቋንቋውን ተገቢ መዋቅር).

**17. (Sub lesson 17)** In Chapter 5's setting, what does the teacher organise before learners write the descriptive essay?
**Answer:** The starter ideas, as an outline (አስተዋጽኦ) — and every learner must attempt the essay.

**18. (Sub lesson 18)** What is paraphrase, as the Grade 9 guide sets it?
**Answer:** Rewriting phrases and sentences so they express the same content in one's own words.

**19. (Sub lesson 19)** Which writing approach does the Grade 10 guide recommend, and what is its Amharic name?
**Answer:** The process approach — ሂደት ተኮር.

**20. (Sub lesson 20)** What does the Grade 10 guide tell teachers about learners' handwriting?
**Answer:** Monitor its improvement and give practice where needed — do not leave handwriting as a small thing.
