# Writing Essays — ordering

Put each set in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the narrative essay's events in the time order the essay must keep:
a) the river reaches the plain  b) the rain falls on the hill  c) the stream joins the river  d) the water flows down the slope

**Answer: b) → d) → c) → a)** — first the rain, then the flow down the slope, then the joining stream, at last the river on the plain: "this happened, then this was done."
