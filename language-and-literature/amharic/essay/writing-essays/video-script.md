# Video script — Writing Essays

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An essay is not one kind. It is five shapes.

First — explain. **አስረጂ ድርሰት**. The expository essay.
It explains a thing: what it is, how it works, how it is used.
Four parts — four paragraphs. And every paragraph stands on one
sentence: **መንደርደሪያ ዓረፍተነገር** — the topic sentence.

Second — tell. **ተራኪ ድርሰት**. The narrative essay.
This happened… then this… then this. Keep the time order.

Third — join. A cause… and its effect.
Volunteers exist — so children are raised,
so the old are not left in hardship.

Fourth — argue. Say your side. Give your reasons, in order.
Three paragraphs that hold one position.

Fifth — organise. One web holds everything.
Six branches… and the essay grows from the web, branch by branch.

Explain, tell, join, argue, organise — five shapes, one writer.

And the explaining grows up.
Before you write — go back. Review the craft.
Study the types of essay: **የድርሰት ዓይነቶች**.
Three prompts are given. Take one.
Write with a partner. Write in a group.

And explain for the reader you have.
A computer — for writing, for reading, for film, for the internet.
A hair-dryer — **ካስክ**. A tractor. A fridge.
Name the thing. Say what it is for.
That is explaining.
