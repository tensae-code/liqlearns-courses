## Sub lesson 1 — The five-step summary method
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: apply the five steps in order and produce a one-paragraph summary; also the book's shorter measures — a summary in three sentences, and in four sentences.

## Sub lesson 2 — Writing the news
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write a two-paragraph news item (ዜና) and a one-day news report (ዘገባ) answering what, who, where and when, and read the report aloud.
