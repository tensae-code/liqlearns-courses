# Video script — Summary Writing and News

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This text is long. I must tell it SHORT — but true.

Step one: read it again.
Step two: pick out only the main points.
Step three: write short and clear. No repetition.
Step four: keep the flow. Keep the order.
Step five: join with linking words.

There! One short paragraph. The whole text, in my own words.
That is a **summary**.

Now the news. Something happened at our school.
**What** happened? **Who** was in it? **Where?** **When?**
Four answers — and I have a news report. Read it aloud!

Summarise it short. Report it plain. Two crafts, one writer.
