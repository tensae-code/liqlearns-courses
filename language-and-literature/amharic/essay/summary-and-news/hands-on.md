# Summary Writing and News — hands-on

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shrink the story
Take a story everyone knows. The first person tells it in five
sentences. The next must tell the same story in three. The next
in one. At each shrinking, the group checks: are the main points
still there, in order? That checking IS the summary method.

## The five-step wall chart
On one big sheet, write the five steps in order. Pin it where
you write. For one week, every summary anyone writes is checked
against the chart by a partner before it is finished.

## Yesterday's newsroom
In pairs: one is the reporter, one the editor. The reporter
writes a news report (ዘገባ) of what they did yesterday. The
editor may only ask the four questions — what? who? where?
when? — and the reporter must fix every gap the questions find.
Then the reporter reads the report aloud, like the book's class
reading.
