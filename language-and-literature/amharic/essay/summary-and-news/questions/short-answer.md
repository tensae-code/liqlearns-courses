# Summary Writing and News — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the five summary steps in order, in short form.
**Answer: re-read; main points only; short and clear, no repetition; keep flow and order; linking words.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the story of the woman who came home empty-handed, name two main points a summary must keep.
**Answer: any two of — she worked years abroad and was injured/thrown from a window; she came home empty-handed; her village collected money, treated her, and opened her a hair salon; today she lives thanking them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the Amharic word the book uses for a news report of your day?
**Answer: ዘገባ (zegeba).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** How is the COVID report produced, in the guide's stages?
**Answer:** Groups of 3–5 collect information together, then write the report and present it.
