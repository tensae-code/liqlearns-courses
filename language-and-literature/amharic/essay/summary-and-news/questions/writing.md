# Summary Writing and News — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Summarise this story in one paragraph, by the five steps: "Five years in an Arab country chasing a money dream; she worked day and night without rest, was thrown by her employer from an upper window, and her small savings were spent on treatment; she came home empty-handed; her village collected money house to house, treated her, and opened her a hair salon — today she lives thanking them."
**Answer guide: one paragraph, own words, main points only, the story's order kept, linking words used.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Summarise the same story again in exactly three sentences.
**Answer guide: three sentences carrying the main points — the years abroad and the injury; the empty-handed return; the village's help and the salon.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a two-paragraph news item (ዜና) about your school's activities this week.
**Answer guide: two paragraphs; the what, who, where and when all answerable from the item.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a news report (ዘገባ) of what you did yesterday, then read it aloud.
**Answer guide: a plain report in order; read aloud at the end, as the book sets.**
