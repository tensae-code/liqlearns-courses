# Summary Writing and News — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the five steps in the method's order: use linking words — pick out only the main points — re-read the text — keep the flow and the order — write short and clear without repetition.
**Answer: re-read the text → pick out only the main points → write short and clear without repetition → keep the flow and the order → use linking words.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A news report's facts are mixed: (when) yesterday morning — (what) the school garden was cleaned — (who) the pupils of two classes — (where) at the school compound. Write them in the report's question order: what, who, where, when.
**Answer: what — the school garden was cleaned; who — the pupils of two classes; where — at the school compound; when — yesterday morning.**
