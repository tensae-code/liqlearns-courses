# Summary Writing and News — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A good summary is nearly as long as the text it summarises.
**Answer: False** — step 2 keeps only the main points and step 3 writes short and clear.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A summary may change the order of the text's points to a better order.
**Answer: False** — step 4: keep the flow and the order.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A news report of yesterday must be read aloud as part of the book's task.
**Answer: True** — write the ዘገባ and read it in class (p.151).
