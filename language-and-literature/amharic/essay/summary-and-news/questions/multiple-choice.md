# Summary Writing and News — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is step 2 of the summary method?
a) write short and clear  b) pick out only the main points  c) use linking words
**Answer: b) pick out only the main points.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A summary must be written —
a) in the book's exact words  b) in your own words, short and clear  c) with your own opinions added
**Answer: b) in your own words, short and clear.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 4 of the method says: keep —
a) every detail  b) the flow and the order  c) the longest sentences
**Answer: b) the flow and the order.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set does a news report answer?
a) what happened, who, where, when  b) beginning, middle, end  c) setting, characters, problem, outcome
**Answer: a) what happened, who, where, when.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 4 book's first news task is —
a) a two-paragraph news item about your school's activities this week  b) a poem about the school  c) a letter to the newspaper
**Answer: a) a two-paragraph news item about your school's activities this week.**
