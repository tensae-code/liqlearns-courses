# Summary Writing and News

## Do it first

Read this paragraph (the books' own story, in short):

"A woman tells her own story: five years in an Arab country
chasing a money dream; she worked day and night without rest,
was thrown by her employer from an upper window, and her small
savings were spent on treatment; she came home empty-handed; her
village collected money house to house, treated her, and opened
her a hair salon — today she lives thanking them."

Now tell that whole story in ONE sentence. Try before you read
on. It is harder than it looks — Sub lesson 1 teaches the books'
own method for it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The five-step summary method

A summary (የማጠቃለያ አጻጻፍ) tells a text again — short, in your
own words, with nothing lost that matters. The Grade 4 book
teaches it as a method box of **five steps**:

[Picture 8521 — summary writing: the five steps]

1. **Re-read the text.**
2. **Pick out only the main points.**
3. **Write short and clear, without repetition.**
4. **Keep the flow and the order.**
5. **Use linking words.**

Then — the book's own task — summarise the story above in **one
paragraph**. Watch what the method does: the five years, the
fall, the empty-handed return, the village's help, the salon —
those are the main points. The day-and-night detail, the window,
the savings — they carry the points, but the summary lets them
go.

[GIF 8523 — a long text shrinking to its main points]

The book practises the craft at every size: summarise the story
of the woman mayor in **three sentences**; summarise a listening
text in **four sentences**; summarise a reconciliation story, an
honoured father's story, a journalist's story — each in **one
paragraph**. The size changes; the five steps do not.

## Sub lesson 2 — Writing the news

News writing answers for an event, plainly and in order. The
Grade 4 book sets two news tasks:

[Picture 8522 — writing the news: the four questions]

- **Write a two-paragraph news item (ዜና)** about your school's
  activities this week.
- **Write a news report (ዘገባ)** of what you did yesterday —
  and read it in class.

Every news piece is built on the same four answers:

- **What** happened?
- **Who** was in it?
- **Where** did it happen?
- **When** did it happen?

Answer the four, in a clear order, with no wandering, and the
report stands. The book also maps the kinds of writing a
newspaper carries — ዜና (news) first among them — so you know
the craft's name and its family. News is a summary's cousin:
both keep the main points and drop the rest; news adds the
four questions and tells them fresh, as a report.


## Sub lesson 3 — Grade 5 and 6: the main-idea paragraph, a poem's message in prose, and the school-week news item


### Writing: the main idea of the Fatuma reading in one paragraph

Summarise the main idea of Fatuma and Her Friends — the greetings dialogue — in one paragraph of single sentences. The five-step summary method stands in sub lesson 1 of this lesson from the Grade 4 book; this part stocks the Grade 5 book's own main-idea writing task at its own page.

### A poem's message in prose — 'Life Is a Pair of Legs'

Read the poem ሕይወት ጥንድ እግር ("Life Is a Pair of Legs"): life is like two legs on a long journey — the front leg should not boast, the back leg should not be shamed, for positions keep changing. Write its main message in one paragraph of prose. Summarising stands in sub lesson 1 from other books; this poem and its prose task are the Grade 6 book's own, at its own page.

### A news item (ዜና) on the school's week

The writing that rides with the Grade 6 dictation: write a ዜና — a news item — reporting your school's one week of activities: what happened, where, when, and who took part, in news order. The school-week news task stands in sub lesson 2 of this lesson from the Grade 4 book (the same task in a different book); this part stocks the Grade 6 book's own setting of it at its own page. The dictation half of the placement stands in the Writing Sentences lesson.


## Say it to your companion

Read your companion your one-paragraph summary. Your companion
checks it step by step: did you re-read? main points only? short
and clear? flow and order kept? linking words used? Five checks —
then swap, and check their news report against the four
questions.

## Do It Yourself

1. Take a reading you know. Summarise it in one paragraph by the five steps. Write the steps down the side of your page as you use them.
2. Summarise the same reading again — this time in exactly three sentences.
3. Write a two-paragraph news item (ዜና) about your school's activities this week.
4. Write a news report (ዘገባ) of what you did yesterday. Read it aloud to someone at home, as the book's class reading does.
