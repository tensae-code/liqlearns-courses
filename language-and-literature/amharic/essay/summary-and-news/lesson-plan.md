# Lesson plan — Summary Writing and News

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Essay · **Lesson:** Summary Writing and News
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Essay (CURRICULUM.md) — this lesson OPENS the Essay unit.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** two Grade 4 writing crafts: the summary — a long text told again in your own short, clear words by the book's five-step method — and the news report (ዜና / ዘገባ), built on what happened, who, where and when.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on First Paragraphs and Writing About Stories — the learner can write paragraphs and walk a story's shape; this lesson teaches them to shrink a text faithfully and to report an event plainly.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The five-step summary method** — the Grade 4 book's own method box: re-read; pick out only the main points; write short and clear without repetition; keep the flow and order; use linking words — then summarise in one paragraph.
2. **Writing the news** — the news item (ዜና) and the news report (ዘገባ): what happened, who was in it, where, when — written as the book sets it: a two-paragraph news item about your school, and a report of your own day, read aloud.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: First Paragraphs (paragraph/first-paragraphs) and Writing About Stories (story/writing-about-stories). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Summarise a text in one paragraph by the five steps, in order.
2. Say what a summary must never do (repeat, wander, add your own new ideas).
3. Summarise a reading in three or four sentences, as the book sets.
4. Write a two-paragraph news item about your school's week.
5. Write a news report of what you did yesterday and read it aloud.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A reading text to summarise.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner reads a long paragraph and tries to tell it in one sentence before any explanation (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the five steps, then the news (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- The summary is nearly as long as the text → go back to step 2: main points only. Say each paragraph of the original in five words before writing anything.
- The summary adds the learner's own opinion → a summary carries the TEXT's points, in the text's order; opinions live in other writing.
- The news item wanders → return to the four questions; a sentence that answers none of them does not belong in the report.
