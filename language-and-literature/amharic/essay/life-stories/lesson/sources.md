| Sub lesson 1 — how to write a life history (የሕይወት ታሪክ): two kinds — የግል (autobiography: a person writes their own) and የግለሰብ (biography: another writes it); whichever kind, it must contain the full name, the father's and mother's names, birthplace and time, upbringing, level of education, the main contributions, and — if the person is no longer alive — the date of death; learners complete a graphic organiser of their own life history from these points | Ethiopia, *Amharic Student Textbook, Grade 5*, printed pp.172–173 |
| Sub lesson 2 — how to write a biography: gather written sources about the person, ask people who know them, gather true evidence, arrange it in order, state birthplace and time (and death time if deceased), write about upbringing, education level and contributions; task: write the biography of a family member or neighbour and check/fix its chronological order | Ethiopia, *Amharic Student Textbook, Grade 7*, printed p.47 |
| Sub lesson 2 — autobiography: write your own life story expanding an organiser wheel (birth time and place, primary-school stay, current education life, the happiest event of my life, the saddest event, conclusion), main ideas first, then expanded | Ethiopia, *Amharic Student Textbook, Grade 7*, printed p.58 |
| Sub lesson 3 (teacher guide — evidence) — the life of Abebe Bikila (አበበ ቢቂላ), summary as placed: born 30 Nehase 1925 E.C. at Jato, North Shewa; a genna player as a boy; joined the Imperial Bodyguard (1934 E.C.); married W/ro Webdar Wolde Giorgis (1936 E.C.); inspired watching athletes depart for the Olympics; beat Wami Biratu in army races and broke his records; Rome Olympics: won the marathon barefoot in 2:15:16.2, a world record; Tokyo 1964: 2:12:11.2, breaking his own record; received rank and a car from the Emperor; paralysed in a car accident; died 1966 E.C., buried at St. Joseph's Church. Guide's printed source: Henok Yared's article in Reporter newspaper, Nehase 5, 2005 E.C., adapted | Ethiopia, *Grade 6 Amharic Teacher Guide*, printed pp.12–13 |
