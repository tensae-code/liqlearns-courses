# Life Stories — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons run: the shape of a life
history, the method for writing one, then three worked lives —
a runner, a writer told from a table, and a doctor.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two kinds of life history, and its seven points
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name both kinds (የግል / የግለሰብ) and all seven points, and fill an organiser of your own life from them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Writing a biography: gather, order, tell
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: gather from written sources and from people, take only true evidence, and deliver a biography whose chronological order has been checked and fixed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Abebe Bikila: a worked life (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell the life in order as a summary, with Rome 2:15:16.2 and Tokyo 2:12:11.2 exact and in the right races.

## Sub lesson 4 — Dr Haddis Alemayehu: a life told from a table (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: compile a shortened life from a table and present it in sequence, following the biography guidelines.

## Sub lesson 5 — Dr Catherine Hamlin: a worked life (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell the life as a summary — hospitals founded, mothers treated free and recovering, the "Right Livelihood" award, and the name የኢትዮጵያ እናቶች እናት.

## Sub lesson 6 — Yetnebersh Nigussie: a trial turned to good fortune (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell the life by the points the listening truly gives — a disabled woman of history, her strength, a trial turned to good fortune — and say where the guide's presentation names its source (its reference list's two web biographies).

## Sub lesson 7 — Dr Haddis Alemayehu, heard from a recording (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run the recorded-life method in order — prior knowledge stated, main information from the first playing, detailed information from the second, the life told whole from notes — and say how it differs from Sub lesson 4's table method.
