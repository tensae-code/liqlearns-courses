# Life Stories — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** After writing a family member's or neighbour's biography, what check must you make — and fix?
**Answer: check and fix its chronological order.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** The table task's work is checked on two things. Name them.
**Answer: that the biography guidelines were understood, and that the sequence was followed.**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** What award was Dr Catherine Hamlin honoured with?
**Answer: the "Right Livelihood" award (‹‹ራይት ላይብሊሁድ›› ሽልማት).**

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the Sub lesson 6 listening ask learners to note, and about whom?
**Answer:** Detailed information about a disabled woman of history and her strength — the life the guide's reference list sources to two web biographies of Yetnebersh Nigussie.

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** Why does this lesson invent no further facts about Yetnebersh Nigussie's life?
**Answer:** Because the guide's presentation gives only the woman, her disability, her strength and the listening's title — a summary tells the points it truly has, in order, and invents none.

**6. (Sub lesson 7)** What happens BEFORE the recording of Haddis Alemayehu's life is played?
**Answer:** Pre-listening: the class says who Haddis Alemayehu is and what they know of his life and works — the hearing is measured against what they already hold.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Name the recording's own materials, as the guide lists them.
**Answer:** A tape or a computer, a CD, a flash disk, and a small monitor.

**8. (Sub lesson 9)** Give the researched profile's steps in order.
**Answer:** Choose a well-known person in the locality; gather information by asking people; organise it; present it in writing.
