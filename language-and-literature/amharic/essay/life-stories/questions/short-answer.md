# Life Stories — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** After writing a family member's or neighbour's biography, what check must you make — and fix?
**Answer: check and fix its chronological order.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** The table task's work is checked on two things. Name them.
**Answer: that the biography guidelines were understood, and that the sequence was followed.**
