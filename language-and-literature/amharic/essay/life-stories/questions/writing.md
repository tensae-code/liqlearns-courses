# Life Stories — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Complete a graphic organiser of your own life history from the seven points: full name; father's and mother's names; birthplace and time; upbringing; education level; main contributions; date of death only if it applies.
**Answer guide: all applicable points filled; anything unknown is marked as a question to ask, never guessed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the biography of a family member or a neighbour by the method: written sources, asking people who know them, true evidence only, arranged in order. Then check and fix the chronological order.
**Answer guide: the seven points are present; the life runs forwards without jumps.**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Draw the table (ሰንጠረዥ) of a life you know — dates on one side, events on the other — then compile a shortened told version of that life from your table and present it in sequence.
**Answer guide: the telling follows the table's rows in order and covers the biography guidelines.**
