# Life Stories — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A life history of a person who is still alive must still include the date of their death.
**Answer: False** — the date of death belongs in the life history only if the person is no longer alive.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** At Tokyo in 1964, Abebe Bikila broke his own Rome record.
**Answer: True** — Tokyo 2:12:11.2 beat Rome's 2:15:16.2.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Dr Catherine Hamlin is called የኢትዮጵያ እናቶች እናት — "Mother of Ethiopia's Mothers."
**Answer: True** — the name is given for her service to Ethiopian women.

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** The Grade 10 guide prints Yetnebersh Nigussie's full biography as a pupil text in the chapter.
**Answer: False** — there is no pupil text; the life is a listening, and the guide's reference list cites two web biographies of her as its source. The sub lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence.

**5. (Sub lesson 7)** Sub lesson 7's Haddis Alemayehu is the same presentation as Sub lesson 4's.
**Answer: False** — Sub lesson 4 works from the Grade 7 guide's table (ሰንጠረዥ); Sub lesson 7 is the Grade 10 guide's recorded biography, played twice for main, then detailed, information.

**6. (Sub lesson 9)** The Grade 10 guide names sample biographies among the materials for life-history writing.
**Answer: True**
