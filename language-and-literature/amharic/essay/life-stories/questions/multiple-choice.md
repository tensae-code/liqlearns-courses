# Life Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** የግል (autobiography) means —
a) another person writes your life  b) you write your own life  c) a life with no dates in it
**Answer: b) you write your own life.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first steps of writing a biography are —
a) write first, ask later  b) gather written sources and ask people who know the person  c) copy a famous life and change the name
**Answer: b) gather written sources and ask people who know the person.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At the Rome Olympics, Abebe Bikila won the marathon —
a) in shoes, in 2:12:11.2  b) barefoot, in 2:15:16.2  c) barefoot, in 2:12:11.2
**Answer: b) barefoot, in 2:15:16.2 — a world record.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the table task, Dr Haddis Alemayehu's shortened life is compiled from —
a) a given table (ሰንጠረዥ)  b) a dream  c) the last chapter of a novel
**Answer: a) a given table (ሰንጠረዥ).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In Dr Catherine Hamlin's hospitals, women suffering fistula are —
a) treated for a high fee  b) treated free, and recover  c) only given advice
**Answer: b) treated free, and recover.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The listening of Sub lesson 6 is titled ፈተናን ወደ መልካም ዕድል መለወጥ. Its title means —
a) "Running From a Trial"  b) "Turning a Trial into Good Fortune"  c) "The Trial of the Judges"
**Answer: b) "Turning a Trial into Good Fortune"** — the listening gives detailed information about a disabled woman of history and her strength.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Where does the guide name the source of the Yetnebersh Nigussie listening?
a) On its cover  b) In its reference list, which cites two web biographies of her  c) In the student's own book
**Answer: b)** — the reference list (printed p.92) cites two web biographies of Yetnebersh Nigussie, accessed Tahsas 25, 2014 E.C.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** How is Dr Haddis Alemayehu's life presented in Sub lesson 7?
a) In a printed table  b) Played from a recording, twice  c) As a timeline of his works
**Answer: b)** — the table is Sub lesson 4's Grade 7 way, and the timeline is the student book's; this Grade 10 way is a recording.

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** What is taken from the SECOND playing of the recorded biography?
a) The main information  b) The detailed information  c) The marks for the lesson
**Answer: b) The detailed information** — the first playing gives the life in outline: its main information.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** What is Haile Gebrselassie's motto on the card?
a) ይቻላል! — It is possible!  b) Never give up  c) Run alone
**Answer: a) ይቻላል! — It is possible!** — the card's own word.

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** Where was he born, by the card?
a) In Arsi — Chialo, Tiyo woreda, 1966 E.C.  b) In Gondar, 1970 E.C.  c) In Addis Ababa, 1960 E.C.
**Answer: a) In Arsi — Chialo, Tiyo woreda, 1966 E.C.** — the card's birth row.

**12. (Sub lesson 8)** What does the paragraph task do with the card?
a) Turns its rows of facts into one flowing paragraph  b) Copies the rows as they stand  c) Shortens it to one sentence
**Answer: a) Turns its rows of facts into one flowing paragraph** — facts in rows → a life in order.
