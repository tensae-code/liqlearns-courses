# Life Stories — ordering

Put each set in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Put Abebe Bikila's life events in their true order:
a) he won at Tokyo in 2:12:11.2  b) he was born at Jato, North Shewa  c) he won at Rome, barefoot, in 2:15:16.2  d) he joined the Imperial Bodyguard  e) he died, in 1966 E.C.

**Answer: b) → d) → c) → a) → e)** — born 1925 E.C.; the Bodyguard from 1934 E.C.; Rome first, then Tokyo; his death closes the life.

**2. (Sub lesson 7)** The recorded-life method, in its order:
a) Give the main and detailed information from your notes  b) Say what you know of the person before anything plays  c) Hear the whole life — the main information  d) Hear it again — the detailed information
**Answer: b, c, d, a.**
