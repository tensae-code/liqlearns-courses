# Writing One Essay Together as a Group — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Disagreements in a group essay are settled with evidence, not by who speaks loudest.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each member writes an individual essay BEFORE the group work begins.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Settling disagreements by who speaks loudest is the book's method.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The group essay must be rewritten in one voice, not left in four voices.
**Answer: True**
