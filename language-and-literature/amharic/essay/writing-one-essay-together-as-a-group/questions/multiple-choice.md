# Writing One Essay Together as a Group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A group essay is made by —
a) stapling the individual essays together  b) joining the clearest points into one essay in one voice  c) copying the longest draft
**Answer: b) joining the clearest points into one essay in one voice** — the drafts are improved INTO one essay — one voice, one flow.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The group essay of Chapter 9 grows out of essays on —
a) the bat and the hen  b) man-made climate change  c) the Hero Donkey
**Answer: b) man-made climate change** — the individually written climate essays are improved into one.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The finished group essay is judged —
a) per member  b) as one essay — one title, one flow, one voice  c) by its length
**Answer: b) as one essay — one title, one flow, one voice** — one essay is the product, however many hands wrote it.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Laying drafts side by side helps the group to —
a) copy one whole  b) find the clearest statement of each point  c) avoid reading
**Answer: b) find the clearest statement of each point** — side by side, the best wording of each point can be seen and chosen.
