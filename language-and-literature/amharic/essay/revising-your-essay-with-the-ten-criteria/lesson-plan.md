# Lesson plan — Revising Your Essay with the Ten Criteria

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Essay · **Lesson:** Revising Your Essay with the Ten Criteria
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Essay (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an essay is revised (ከልሳችሁ) against the book's own ten assessment criteria — title, main idea, detail, order, words, sentences, flow, punctuation, unity.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Writing Essays — the learner can write an essay; this lesson teaches judging and mending it the book's own way.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The ten criteria — የድርሰት መገምገሚያ መስፈርት** — the book's correction checklist, all ten
2. **Revising in practice — three essays, one checklist** — the criteria used again in Chapters 7 and 9

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
