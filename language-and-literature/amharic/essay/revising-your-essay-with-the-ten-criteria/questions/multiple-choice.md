# Revising Your Essay with the Ten Criteria — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many criteria has the book's essay checklist?
a) five  b) ten  c) seven
**Answer: b) ten** — ten criteria, from keeping to the title to paragraph unity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The same ten criteria are used to revise essays in —
a) only Chapter 6  b) Chapters 6, 7 and 9  c) only the final exam
**Answer: b) Chapters 6, 7 and 9** — the book returns to them for the comparative, child-labour and climate essays.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Criterion 9 concerns —
a) the title  b) punctuation in its proper place  c) word choice
**Answer: b) punctuation in its proper place** — criterion 9: proper punctuation in its proper place.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The 'ተማሪ' essay revised in Chapter 6 is a —
a) narrative essay  b) comparative essay  c) letter
**Answer: b) comparative essay** — the comparative essay 'The Student' is the first revision task.
