# Letters

## Do it first

A letter has been taken apart. Its pieces are:

- the signature (ፊርማ)
- the greeting — "ውድ ወንድሜ/ጓደኛዬ…" (my dear brother / my dear friend…)
- the sender's name, town and P.O. box
- the body — the news itself
- the date
- the close

Put the pieces back in the order they stand on a real letter
page. Which piece must be first? Which one seals the letter at
the end? Hold your order — Sub lesson 1 names every piece and
its place.

## Sub lesson 1 — The personal letter (የግል ደብዳቤ)

The first letter kind is the one you write to a person you
know: the **personal letter**. Its form, from the model:

1. At the top: the **sender's name, town and P.O. box**
   (የመ.ሣ.ቁ).
2. The **date**.
3. Then the **recipient's name, town and box**.
4. The **greeting** — ውድ ወንድሜ/ጓደኛዬ… ("my dear brother /
   my dear friend…").
5. The **body** — the letter's news.
6. The **close**, and the **signature** (ፊርማ).

[Picture 8785 — the personal letter: its shape]

[GIF 8791 — a letter assembling, then folding into its envelope]

The model's story, in summary: Fozia Mohammed of Woldia (Box
1754, writing on 30/11/2013 E.C.) writes to Alemush Tesfaw in
Dabat. She asks after Alemush's health; she passes on school
news — a friend wrote that Alemush came first, and Fozia
herself came first in her own class and was showered with
prizes: a laptop from the school, a bicycle from her father,
dictionaries and books from her mother; she writes with
self-confidence, and promises they will talk fully when they
meet. Health, news, confidence, a promise — that is what a
personal letter's body carries. Your own letter is written in
the same form: same shape, your own news.

## Sub lesson 2 — The greeting letter (የሰላምታ ደብዳቤ) and its parts

The **greeting letter** is the personal letter with its parts
named and labelled. The model is dated 10/10/2013 E.C.; its
sender is ተማሪ ሰዓዳ ሰይድ, of አፄ ፋሲል 1ኛ ደረጃ ትምህርትቤት in
ጎንደር, P.O. box መ.ሳ.ቁ. 1200. Its three parts:

[Picture 8786 — the greeting letter: its parts]

1. **የሰላምታ ክፍል — the greeting part.** "To my dear friend
   Aster Kebede — how is your health since we parted? I, thank
   the Creator, am well."
2. **የጉዳይ ክፍል — the body part.** The matter itself: she is
   studying hard for the second-semester exam; she is sure
   Aster will come first as before, and she aims to rise from
   third to first; she is grateful for the maths book Aster
   sent, which is easing her maths difficulty.
3. **የመዝጊያ ክፍል — the closing part.** "You too, do your
   best for me; when school closes I will come to Bahir Dar to
   visit relatives and we will meet; stay well until then."

Then the signature: ጓደኛሽ ሰዓዳ ሰይድ — "your friend, Saada
Seyid". Greeting, body, closing: a greeting letter that misses
one part is a visit cut short. Write your own on the model.

## Sub lesson 3 — The congratulatory letter (የደስታ መግለጫ ደብዳቤ)

Some letters carry joy officially. The **congratulatory
letter** model is a real official letter: the Prime Minister's
Office writes on 22 Meskerem 1993 E.C. (its reference:
ጠ80-አጠ88/07/03) to the Olympic Committee and the athletes in
Sydney, congratulating them on the marathon victory that
followed Abebe Bikila's and Mamo Wolde's, and praising
teamwork over personal glory.

[Picture 8787 — an official letter: every part in its place]

Its anatomy — the official skeleton every formal letter in
this lesson wears: **heading, number, date, addressee,
subject line (ጉዳዩ), body, closing (ከሠላምታ ጋር — "with
greetings"), signature.**

The task mirrors the model exactly: **as school principal,
write a congratulatory letter to the school football team
that won an inter-school competition** — the same joy, your
own office, the same shape.

## Sub lesson 4 — Official and request letters

An **official letter** travels between offices, or from an
office to a person, and its head carries its identity: the
letterhead, its abbreviations, and its reference number built
from parts (office abbreviation, subject code, file number,
year) with its date — as in the official letter from Hote
General Secondary School in Dessie, reference
ሆ/አ/2ኛ/ደ/ት/ቤት/305/2095/13, dated 15/11/13 E.C., written about
selecting students for science and innovation.

The **request letter** wears that frame to ask for something.
The model's errand: as the school minimedia club coordinator,
write to the principal asking for materials to be bought. Its
frame:

[Picture 8788 — the request letter frame]

- **Letterhead, left side:** the school name, the club name,
  the town.
- **Letterhead, right side:** the office name abbreviated,
  the subject code (ደብዳቤዎች — letters — is code **112**), the
  file number **315**, and the date.
- Then the **subject line: ጉዳዩ** — and the request itself,
  stated plainly.

The **office letter** (የመስሪያቤት ደብዳቤ) is the fullest form:
the kind of letter one government office writes to another
office, to a private organisation or to an individual, written
by the office head or by one seated in his place. Its form,
in order: **letterhead stamp (የራስጌ ማህተም), number (ቁጥር),
date (ቀን), to (ለ…), subject (ጉዳዩ), the detailed idea
(ዝርዝር ሐሳብ), copy (ግልባጭ), closing (ከሠላምታ ጋር), the office
head, signature, name and title, round stamp.** Imagine
yourself an office head, write one on this form — then edit
and rewrite it.

## Sub lesson 5 — The appreciation letter (የአድናቆት ደብዳቤ)

An **appreciation letter** thanks, with evidence. The model:
a parent — Woyzero Birqe Tafese, mother of the Grade 6 "A"
student Tigist — writes to the principal after the school's
annual celebration.

[Picture 8792 — the appreciation letter: greeting, body, warm closing]

Her body says what she **saw**: she was there from the
morning; everything was well organised; the teachers cared
for every student, worried over their dress and readiness;
her own very quiet daughter sang and danced — proof, she
writes, of the teachers' diligence and love of work. She is
sure her daughter will succeed under such follow-up, and she
expresses her appreciation. Appreciation is not flattery: it
names the good thing, plainly.

Write your own appreciation letter on the model — to a
parent, a teacher, a friend or a club coordinator — then
edit it and read it aloud.

## Sub lesson 6 — The invitation letter (የግብዣ ጥሪ ደብዳቤ)

An **invitation letter** changes its clothes with the
occasion: invitation letters are written for a wedding, a
baptism (ክርስትና), a tsebel (ፀበል) gathering, a memorial
(ተዝካር); government and private offices also write them to
officials and concerned bodies. Most people use them for
weddings — and though there is no single fixed shape, a
wedding invitation is mostly written in this form:

[Picture 8789 — the invitation letter: the wedding form]

"**ለ____** (to ____), presenting our respectful greetings
beforehand — since the marriage ceremony of our child
Ato/W/ro ____ will be performed on ____, we have honourably
invited you to come at 6:00 with ____ and share our joy.
Your respecters: W/ro ____, Ato ____."

Every blank is a question: to whom? whose child? which day?
with whom? Fill the form for a wedding invitation, exchange
it with a friend, correct each other's, and rewrite — and say
what is written in each of the model's blanks.

## Sub lesson 7 — The application letter (የማመልከቻ ደብዳቤ)

The last kind asks an office for a thing to be done. An
**application letter** is the letter in which an individual
states or requests the matter he wants from a private
organisation or a government office. Though it is a personal
letter, **because it enters an office it must be written
using formal words**; its writing varies by writer, but it
must include the main matters.

[Picture 8790 — the application letter: subject, body, humble request]

The model: dated 16/11/2013 E.C., addressed to ዳግማዊ ምኒልክ
ክፍለከተማ ጽ/ቤት in ባሕር ዳር (the Dagmawi Menelik Sub-City
Office, Bahir Dar). Its subject line stands in the heading:

**ጉዳዩ፡- የነዋሪነት መታወቂያ ካርድ እንዲሰጠኝ ስለመጠየቅ** — "Re:
requesting that a residency ID card be given to me."

The body: "As stated in the heading above, I, ሥራውብዙ ታዬ, am
known to have lived registered as a resident in this kebele
for about two years. Therefore I humbly request the sub-city
to give me an ID card stating my residency." Then the
closing — ከሠላምታ ጋር — the signature, and the name ሥራውብዙ
ታዬ.

Your task mirrors it: write an application to an office about
a matter you want fulfilled — subject first, the facts, the
humble request — then exchange, correct, and rewrite.

## Say it to your companion

Name the letter kind for each errand, and why: (1) thanking
your teacher at the year's end; (2) asking a sub-city office
for a residency ID card; (3) calling your uncle to your
sister's wedding; (4) congratulating the school team that won
the cup; (5) telling your far-away friend your school news.
Your companion checks each kind against its shape.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write a personal letter (የግል ደብዳቤ) to a real friend: sender block, date, recipient, greeting, your news in the body, close and ፊርማ.
2. Write a greeting letter (የሰላምታ ደብዳቤ) and label its three parts in the margin: የሰላምታ ክፍል, የጉዳይ ክፍል, የመዝጊያ ክፍል.
3. As school principal, write the congratulatory letter to the school football team that won an inter-school competition — heading, number, date, addressee, ጉዳዩ, body, ከሠላምታ ጋር, signature.
4. As minimedia club coordinator, write the request letter to your principal asking for materials to be bought, on the two-sided letterhead frame (subject code 112, file number 315).
5. Write an appreciation letter (የአድናቆት ደብዳቤ) to a teacher, parent, friend or club coordinator; edit it; read it aloud.
6. Fill the wedding invitation form for a real or imagined wedding, and fill every blank.
7. Write an application letter (የማመልከቻ ደብዳቤ) to an office about a matter you want fulfilled — formal words, subject line, humble request.
