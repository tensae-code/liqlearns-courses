Honesty notes: (1) No model letter is transcribed in full anywhere in this lesson: the personal-letter, greeting-letter, appreciation-letter and application-letter models are taught as shape + the placements' own summaries of their content, exactly as the brief requires. The greeting letter's three parts are quoted only in the short fragments the placements themselves quote (the greeting's health enquiry, the closing's promise), never as a whole letter. (2) The wedding invitation form is given in the placements in English rendering with its blanks; lesson.md and Picture 8789 reproduce that rendering and invent no Amharic letter text for it — the Amharic the placements give for the invitation is its name (የግብዣ ጥሪ ደብዳቤ), the occasions (ክርስትና, ፀበል, ተዝካር) and ሠርግ on the card, and those are the Amharic words used. (3) The application model's signature is illegible in the book as placed; the lesson teaches the closing and the printed name (ሥራውብዙ ታዬ) and claims nothing about the signature's form. (4) Sub lesson 4 joins three placements that teach one family — the Grade 6 official letter's frame (pp.52–53), the Grade 6 request letter built on that frame (p.60), and the Grade 7 office letter's full part list (pp.135–136); each frame is credited to its own book and page in the table, and no part of one frame is attributed to another. (5) The "Do it first" pieces are the personal letter's parts as listed in the Grade 5 placement, shuffled; no book page beyond that placement is claimed for them.
