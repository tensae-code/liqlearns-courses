# Hands-on — Letters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a letter from pieces
Write a personal letter's parts on six slips of paper — sender
block, date, recipient, greeting, body, close + signature.
Mix them, and a partner rebuilds the letter in its true order
on the table. Then they read it top to bottom: does it visit
well?

## The real envelope
Write one real personal letter, fold it, and put it in an
envelope. On the envelope write the recipient's name, town and
box — the same three things the letter's own head carries.
Give it or send it for real.

## Fill the wedding form
Copy the wedding invitation form onto a big sheet with its
blanks wide. In pairs, fill it for a wedding you invent —
names, day, hour 6:00, the companion's name. Exchange sheets:
can your partner read every blank's answer without asking?

## Office for a day
Set up a table as an office desk. One learner is the office
head and writes an office letter on the full form (stamp,
number, date, ጉዳዩ, ዝርዝር ሐሳብ, ግልባጭ, signature, round
stamp — a bottle-cap makes the stamp). The other brings an
application letter asking that office for something, and the
two letters are checked side by side: does each carry every
part its kind demands?
