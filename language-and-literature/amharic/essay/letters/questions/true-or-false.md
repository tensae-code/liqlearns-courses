# Letters — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 9)** A follow-on task tests whether you can use a learned form without the model in front of you.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 10)** Informal writing, as the guide teaches it, must carry a subject line (ጉዳዩ) and a round stamp.
**Answer: False — it is writing without the formal frame**

**3. (Sub lesson 11)** የሥራ ደብዳቤ is the job-application letter.
**Answer: True**
