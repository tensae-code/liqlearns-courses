# Letters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a personal letter (የግል ደብዳቤ), what stands at the very top of the page?
a) the signature (ፊርማ)  b) the sender's name, town and P.O. box (የመ.ሣ.ቁ)  c) the greeting
**Answer: b) the sender's name, town and P.O. box (የመ.ሣ.ቁ)** — then the date, then the recipient.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The middle part of a greeting letter — the part that carries the matter itself — is the —
a) የሰላምታ ክፍል  b) የመዝጊያ ክፍል  c) የጉዳይ ክፍል
**Answer: c) የጉዳይ ክፍል** — the body part; the greeting part opens, the closing part ends.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The congratulatory letter model was written by —
a) a student to a friend  b) the Prime Minister's Office to the Olympic Committee and the athletes in Sydney  c) a parent to a principal
**Answer: b) the Prime Minister's Office to the Olympic Committee and the athletes in Sydney** — 22 Meskerem 1993 E.C., on the marathon victory.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the request letter's frame, the right side of the letterhead holds —
a) the school name, club name and town  b) the office name abbreviated, the subject code, the file number and the date  c) the closing and signature
**Answer: b) the office name abbreviated, the subject code, the file number and the date** — ደብዳቤዎች (letters) is subject code 112, and the model's file number is 315. The left side holds the school, club and town.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The appreciation letter's model is written by —
a) Woyzero Birqe Tafese, a parent, to the school principal  b) the principal to the teachers  c) a student to a friend
**Answer: a) Woyzero Birqe Tafese, a parent, to the school principal** — after the annual celebration, thanking the teachers through what she saw.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Invitation letters (የግብዣ ጥሪ ደብዳቤ) are written for all of these EXCEPT —
a) a wedding  b) a baptism (ክርስትና) and a memorial (ተዝካር)  c) asking an office for an ID card
**Answer: c) asking an office for an ID card** — that errand belongs to the application letter; invitations also serve tsebel (ፀበል) gatherings and offices inviting officials.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Though an application letter (የማመልከቻ ደብዳቤ) is a personal letter, it must be written in formal words because —
a) it is longer than other letters  b) it enters an office  c) it has no greeting
**Answer: b) it enters an office** — it requests a matter from a private organisation or a government office, so formal words and the main matters are required.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** ሐምሌ አስር, ኹለት ሺህ አሥራ ሦስት written in figures is what?
a) 10/11/2013  b) 11/10/2013  c) 10/12/2013
**Answer: a) 10/11/2013** — day/month/year in register form.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** In the reference number 101/3055/13, what does 101 stand for?
a) The subject code — ሪፖርቶች (reports)  b) The year  c) The file number
**Answer: a) The subject code — ሪፖርቶች (reports)** — office + subject code + file number + year.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** What are a reference number's parts, in order?
a) Office abbreviation, subject code, file number, year  b) Year only  c) Subject, greeting, body
**Answer: a) Office abbreviation, subject code, file number, year** — the parts click together in that order.


**11. (Sub lesson 9)** A greeting letter's parts, in order, are —
a) body, date, signature  b) date and sender, greeting part, body part, closing part, signature  c) subject, body, stamp
**Answer: b) date and sender, greeting part, body part, closing part, signature** — the model's order: heading, greeting, body, closing, signature.
