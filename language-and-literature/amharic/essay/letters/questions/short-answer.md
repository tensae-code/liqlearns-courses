# Letters — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the personal-letter model, who writes to whom — and what three kinds of thing does her body carry?
**Answer: Fozia Mohammed of Woldia writes to Alemush Tesfaw in Dabat; her body carries health enquiries, school news (both girls came first; Fozia's prizes), and a promise to talk fully when they meet.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the greeting letter's three parts in order.
**Answer: የሰላምታ ክፍል (the greeting part), የጉዳይ ክፍል (the body part), የመዝጊያ ክፍል (the closing part).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the congratulatory letter's anatomy in order, from the top of the page.
**Answer: heading, number, date, addressee, subject line (ጉዳዩ), body, closing (ከሠላምታ ጋር), signature.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the first and the last parts of an office letter (የመስሪያቤት ደብዳቤ).
**Answer: first, the letterhead stamp (የራስጌ ማህተም); last, the round stamp — after the office head, signature, name and title.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What makes the appreciation model's thanks strong instead of empty?
**Answer: it names what she actually saw — everything well organised, teachers caring for every student, her quiet daughter singing and dancing — appreciation with evidence, not flattery.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In the wedding invitation form, what four things do the blanks ask for?
**Answer: to whom it is written; whose child (Ato/W/ro ____) is marrying; on which day the ceremony is performed; and with whom the guest should come (at 6:00) — the inviters' names (W/ro ____, Ato ____) close the form.**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** What does the application model's subject line (ጉዳዩ) ask for?
**Answer: ጉዳዩ፡- የነዋሪነት መታወቂያ ካርድ እንዲሰጠኝ ስለመጠየቅ — that a residency ID card be given to the writer (ሥራውብዙ ታዬ, a registered resident of the kebele for about two years).**
