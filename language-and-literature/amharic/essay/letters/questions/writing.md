# Letters — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a personal letter (የግል ደብዳቤ) to a friend in another town: your name, town and box at the top, the date, your friend's name and town, the greeting ውድ ወንድሜ/ጓደኛዬ…, a body with your news, the close and your ፊርማ.
**Answer guide: the six parts in the model's order; the body carries real news in the model's spirit — health, school or home news, a promise or plan to meet.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a greeting letter (የሰላምታ ደብዳቤ) to a friend you have not seen since last term, and label its three parts in the margin.
**Answer guide: የሰላምታ ክፍል asks after health and says you are well; የጉዳይ ክፍል carries the matter (study, news, thanks); የመዝጊያ ክፍል closes with good wishes and a meeting hoped for; signed as a friend signs.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** As school principal, write a congratulatory letter (የደስታ መግለጫ ደብዳቤ) to the school football team that won an inter-school competition.
**Answer guide: heading, number, date, addressee (the team), ጉዳዩ subject line, a body congratulating and praising teamwork, closing ከሠላምታ ጋር, signature — the model's anatomy, the school's own joy.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** As the school minimedia club coordinator, write the request letter to your principal asking for materials to be bought.
**Answer guide: letterhead left (school name, club name, town), right (office name abbreviated, subject code ደብዳቤዎች = 112, file number 315, date), then ጉዳዩ, then the request stated plainly.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write an appreciation letter (የአድናቆት ደብዳቤ) to a teacher, parent, friend or club coordinator. Then edit it and read it aloud.
**Answer guide: greeting, a body naming what you saw and value (as the model names the organised celebration and the teachers' care), a warm closing; thanks with evidence, not flattery.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Write a wedding invitation on the model's form, filling every blank: ለ____, the child Ato/W/ro ____, the day ____, come at 6:00 with ____, signed by the respecters W/ro ____ and Ato ____.
**Answer guide: every blank filled; the respectful greetings come before the invitation; the form's order is kept.**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Write an application letter (የማመልከቻ ደብዳቤ) to an office about a matter you want fulfilled.
**Answer guide: date, the office addressed, a ጉዳዩ subject line saying the errand in one line, a body stating the facts and humbly requesting, closing ከሠላምታ ጋር, signature and name — formal words throughout.**
