# Video script — Letters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A letter is a visit on paper. But every visit has its shape.

To my friend, I write a **የግል ደብዳቤ** — a personal letter.
My name, my town, my box. The date. Her name.
**ውድ ወንድሜ/ጓደኛዬ…** — my dear friend.
My news. My close. My **ፊርማ**.

The greeting letter names its parts:
**የሰላምታ ክፍል** — I greet you. **የጉዳይ ክፍል** — my matter.
**የመዝጊያ ክፍል** — I close, and I promise: we will meet.

For joy in an office shape: the **የደስታ መግለጫ ደብዳቤ**.
Heading. Number. Date. **ጉዳዩ** — the subject. Congratulations!
**ከሠላምታ ጋር** — with greetings. Signature.

To ask an office, I keep the frame: letterhead left, letterhead
right, file number, code — then my request, plain.
To thank, I write **የአድናቆት ደብዳቤ**: I name the good I saw.
To invite, I fill the blanks: to whom, whose wedding, which day.
To apply, **የማመልከቻ ደብዳቤ**: formal words, my subject line,
my humble request.

Seven letters. Seven shapes. Choose the shape — then write.
