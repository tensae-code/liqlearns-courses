# Compare-and-Contrast Essays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A compare-and-contrast paragraph must —
a) describe only the first thing  b) carry both things, compared point by point  c) list dates in order
**Answer: b) carry both things, compared point by point** — comparison means both sides in every part — not two separate descriptions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the bat and the hen SHARE in the book's bigar?
a) eggs  b) wings  c) night activity
**Answer: b) wings** — both have wings — the shared point comes first, then the differences.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do good and bad neighbours SHARE, in the book's bigar?
a) good thoughts  b) being area residents, nearby, iddir members  c) arriving quickly to help
**Answer: b) being area residents, nearby, iddir members** — the shared column: resident, nearby, association member, sharing the area's fortunes.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Full citizenship stands in which column of the first bigar?
a) the foreign country's  b) one's own country's  c) neither
**Answer: b) one's own country's** — own country: full citizenship, pride, equality.
