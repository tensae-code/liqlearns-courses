# Compare-and-Contrast Essays — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What organiser is the essay built from?
**Answer:** A comparison bigar — two columns of shared and different points.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which figure is the lamp in that essay?
**Answer:** The youth — the one who shines, made to shine by another.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In 'ተማሪ', what does the high-desire student do that the low-desire one does not?
**Answer:** Any of: treats the teacher as a starting-point, is never absent, asks many questions.
