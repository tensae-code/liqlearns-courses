# Judging a Character in an Essay — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An evaluative essay gives its judgement —
a) in the first sentence, before any evidence  b) last, after the general ground and both cases  c) never
**Answer: b) last, after the general ground and both cases** — judgement comes fourth, after ground, case and counter-case.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The essay's second paragraph treats children who —
a) leave home early  b) do not want to part from their parents  c) hide their parents
**Answer: b) do not want to part from their parents** — part 2 of the hint bigar: children who cling to parents.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An evaluative essay is also called —
a) a narrative essay  b) a judging essay — it weighs a character  c) a letter
**Answer: b) a judging essay — it weighs a character** — its work is judgement, fairly earned.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The mother's proof in the essay is —
a) her photograph and her fire story  b) her wealth  c) her neighbours' words
**Answer: a) her photograph and her fire story** — the photo of the beautiful young woman and the fire account carry the proof.
