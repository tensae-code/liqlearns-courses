# Judging a Character in an Essay — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What evidence from the story proves the mother's case?
**Answer:** She ran through fire and carried her baby out wrapped in a blanket, and was scarred by it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the FIRST part of the hint bigar?
**Answer:** Children and parents in general — the general ground before the cases.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the son make his daily work after forgiveness?
**Answer:** Teaching youths to honour their parents.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does judgement come last in this form?
**Answer:** Because a fair judgement must rest on the general ground and both cases, with the story's evidence heard.
