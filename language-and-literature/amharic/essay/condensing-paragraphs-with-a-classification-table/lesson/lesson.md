# Condensing Paragraphs with a Classification Table

## Do it first

Read any two paragraphs of a lesson. Now try to say both in four sentences without looking back. Hard to choose what to keep? Sub lesson 1 gives you the sieve.

## Sub lesson 1 — Classify, then condense

Grade 8's heritage chapter teaches a new writing move. Take paragraphs 4, 5 and 6 of the heritage reading — three paragraphs about the sites. Before writing anything, sort their facts into a classification bigar with five columns: name / service / place / nature / time built. Every fact must find its cell; a fact that fits no cell is either repeated or decoration. Then write ONE paragraph from the table alone, never looking back at the three paragraphs. The table is the sieve: what passes through it is the condensed paragraph — complete, ordered, and a third of the length.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the main thing each sub lesson taught, in your own words, in order. Your companion asks one question about each part, and you answer from the lesson. Then swap.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, in your own words.
