# Grade 3 Health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the small icon at a task tell you?
a) The page number  b) The skill — listening, reading, writing or speaking  c) The answer
**Answer: b) The skill — listening, reading, writing or speaking** — The icon/skill key marks each task's skill.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the lesson of ሀሊማና ያሬድ?
a) Prevention is better than cure  b) Cure is better than prevention  c) Sickness cannot be helped
**Answer: a) Prevention is better than cure** — The story teaches that stopping sickness first is best.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In ህመምተኞችን መንከባከብ, who teach and care for the sick?
a) Young people  b) Only doctors  c) Nobody
**Answer: a) Young people** — Young people teach and care for the sick.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Weyinshet advise Fatuma to do?
a) Stay in bed with no help  b) Go to hospital  c) Stop taking medicine
**Answer: b) Go to hospital** — Weyinshet advises hospital; malaria is diagnosed there.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What cures Fatuma?
a) Taking her medicine properly  b) Skipping the medicine  c) Drinking only water
**Answer: a) Taking her medicine properly** — Taking medicine properly cures her.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which things must never be shared, in ከኤችአይቪ ኤድስ እንጠንቀቅ?
a) Books and pencils  b) Razors, nail cutters, scissors and needles  c) Cups and plates
**Answer: b) Razors, nail cutters, scissors and needles** — Sharp things that can carry blood are never shared.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the trumpet announce in የጽዳት አባት ጥሩንባ?
a) A wedding  b) Dry-waste collection day  c) A market
**Answer: b) Dry-waste collection day** — The trumpet announces the waste collection day.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the visitor see in ትዝብቴ?
a) People dumping waste carelessly, and the harm it brings back  b) A clean river  c) A new school
**Answer: a) People dumping waste carelessly, and the harm it brings back** — The observation essay is about careless dumping and its harm.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** What reading habit harms the eyes, in Weyinshet's story?
a) Reading at night  b) Reading in bright sun  c) Reading aloud
**Answer: b) Reading in bright sun** — Reading in bright sun harms the eyes.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** What does ታካሚ mean?
a) Doctor  b) Patient  c) Medicine
**Answer: b) Patient** — ታካሚ is the patient; ሃኪም is the doctor.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the opposite of ዘወትር (always)?
a) አልፎ አልፎ — once in a while  b) ዘወትር itself  c) በፍጹም
**Answer: a) አልፎ አልፎ — once in a while** — The book's antonym pair is ዘወትር → አልፎ አልፎ.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** In the balanced-diet web, አትክልት is which branch?
a) Vegetables  b) Meat  c) Fat
**Answer: a) Vegetables** — አትክልት means vegetables.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** How does the paragraph say HIV/AIDS spreads?
a) Through blood contact via shared sharp items  b) Through shaking hands  c) Through food
**Answer: a) Through blood contact via shared sharp items** — Blood contact via shared sharp items is the paragraph's teaching.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** What do you ask people to help with, in the typhoid paragraph?
a) Filling the gaps about its symptoms  b) Cooking  c) Reading aloud
**Answer: a) Filling the gaps about its symptoms** — You ask people to fill the symptom gaps.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** Which word means caution/care?
a) ቫይረስ  b) ጥንቃቄ  c) ምላጭ
**Answer: b) ጥንቃቄ** — ጥንቃቄ is care/caution; ቫይረስ is virus, ምላጭ is razor blade.

**16. (Sub lesson 6)** Grade 3 Unit 1 is —
a) Health — ጤና  b) Market — ገበያ  c) Culture — ባህል
**Answer: a) Health — ጤና** — ጤና — health.

**17. (Sub lesson 6)** The unit opens with —
a) road safety  b) dental hygiene — የጥርስ ንፅህና  c) farming
**Answer: b) dental hygiene — የጥርስ ንፅህና** — Dental hygiene.

**18. (Sub lesson 6)** አይነግሮች are —
a) verbs  b) adjectives — describing words  c) place names
**Answer: b) adjectives — describing words** — Adjectives.

**19. (Sub lesson 7)** The check verifies you can —
a) report the heard text  b) only draw  c) only sing
**Answer: a) report the heard text** — Report the heard text.

**20. (Sub lesson 7)** Word meanings in the check must be —
a) guessed  b) exact  c) skipped
**Answer: b) exact** — Give exact meanings.

**21. (Sub lesson 7)** The check's four verifications match —
a) the unit's objectives  b) another unit  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the unit's objectives** — They mirror the objectives.
