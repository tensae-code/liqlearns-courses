# Grade 3 Health — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two sentences: one thing you do to prevent sickness, and one thing you do to care for a sick person at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the safety rule from ከኤችአይቪ ኤድስ እንጠንቀቅ in your own words, and name the four sharp things.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write a concept web for your own reading habits: where, when, and in what light you read.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Make the waste table: three liquid wastes and three dry wastes, each in its right column.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write your three eating-routine sentences, then check each one begins with a capital fidel and ends with ።.
