# Lesson plan — Writing a Play

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Story · **Lesson:** Writing a Play
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** write a play in three parts — beginning, middle, end — listing its elements in the table as you go, the way the Grade 8 book builds one.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Elements of a Play and Reading a Play — the learner has analysed a real play before writing one.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The first part — set the stage** — choosing a title, opening the stage, entering the problem
2. **The middle part — let it grow and complicate** — writing and performing the play's middle
3. **Finishing the play — the final part** — the end, the full table, and the summary organiser

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
