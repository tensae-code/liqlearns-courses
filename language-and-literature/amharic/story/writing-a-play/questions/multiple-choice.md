# Writing a Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A play's first part should open with —
a) a long explanation of the ending  b) stage directions — place, people, how they look  c) the author's biography
**Answer: b) stage directions — place, people, how they look** — stage directions set the scene before dialogue begins.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In a play's middle, the action should —
a) end at once  b) grow and complicate  c) repeat the beginning exactly
**Answer: b) grow and complicate** — the middle is where the conflict sharpens — the action's growth and complication.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A play's ending should —
a) introduce a brand-new problem  b) answer the problem the beginning set  c) stop in the middle of a quarrel
**Answer: b) answer the problem the beginning set** — the end resolves what the beginning raised — as the debt is answered by the family's decision.
