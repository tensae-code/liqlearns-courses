# Writing a Play — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What four things go in the 'beginning' column of the elements table?
**Answer:** Characters, setting, action so far, and the problem.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What must you enter in the elements table after writing the middle?
**Answer:** The middle part's elements — its characters, action's growth, setting, problem and movement towards the climax.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In የሠርጉ መዘዝ, how is the debt problem answered?
**Answer:** By a decision, not by money — cancel the return feast and live within the family's means.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What must a play's final speech carry?
**Answer:** The play's message — as Mhret's closing speech carries the wedding play's.
