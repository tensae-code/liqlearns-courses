# Writing a Play — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The problem should already appear in the play's first part.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ይሁን እንጂ means 'however' and joins two sentences in the play's grammar work.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After finishing, you summarise beginning, middle and end elements in one organiser.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Performing your middle part in class is part of the book's writing work, not an extra.
**Answer: True**
