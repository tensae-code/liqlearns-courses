# Grade 3 Farming — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two farm tools you heard and what each is for.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the garden steps in order, numbered 1 to 6.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Make the three columns — root, leaf, fruit — and put two vegetables in each.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write your forest paragraph from the starter, at least five sentences.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write one line of a farm work chant you collected, and say what work it was sung over.
