# Grade 3 Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Those who plough cleverly do what?
a) Harvest as they wish  b) Work less  c) Buy more land
**Answer: a) Harvest as they wish** — በብልሀት ያረሱ እንደልብ ጎረሱ — clever ploughing, wished-for harvest.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The diligent-farmers listening asks about which two seasons?
a) ክረምት and በጋ  b) Spring and autumn  c) Day and night
**Answer: a) ክረምት and በጋ** — Is the work heavier in the rains or the dry season?

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** ዶሮ ርባታ is about raising what?
a) Goats  b) Chickens  c) Bees
**Answer: b) Chickens** — ዶሮ ርባታ is chicken raising.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For every tree cut, what should be planted?
a) Nothing  b) A new seedling  c) Grass only
**Answer: b) A new seedling** — A new seedling for every tree cut — and be an example first.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Forests hold what, stopping desertification?
a) The rain  b) The soil  c) The wind
**Answer: b) The soil** — Forests hold the soil.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the FIRST step to start a garden, in the dialogue?
a) Buy seed  b) Learn  c) Plant
**Answer: b) Learn** — Learn first — then want, prepare, ask, buy, plant.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** ቀንበር is what?
a) A plough  b) A yoke  c) A sickle
**Answer: b) A yoke** — ቀንበር is the yoke; ማረሻ is the plough.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** ክረምት is which season?
a) The dry season  b) The rains  c) Autumn
**Answer: b) The rains** — ክረምት is the rainy season; በጋ is dry.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** ቲማቲም — tomato — is eaten as which part?
a) Root  b) Leaf  c) Fruit
**Answer: c) Fruit** — Tomato is the fruit column; carrot and potato are roots.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** The starter paragraph begins from which sentence-idea?
a) Forests are far away  b) Caring for the forest is useful  c) Trees are tall
**Answer: b) Caring for the forest is useful** — 'Caring for the forest is useful' is the starter.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** The research paragraph is about what?
a) The harms of deforestation  b) How to plant  c) Farm tools
**Answer: a) The harms of deforestation** — Gather information on deforestation's harms, then write.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** After writing, the group speaking is about what?
a) Prevention  b) Prices  c) Seasons
**Answer: a) Prevention** — Group speaking follows on preventing deforestation.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** ካልተንከባከብን comes from which dialogue?
a) The garden dialogue  b) The forest dialogue — የደን ጥቅም  c) The market dialogue
**Answer: b) The forest dialogue — የደን ጥቅም** — The long words are picked from የደን ጥቅም.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** Oral farm poems are collected from whom?
a) The radio  b) Your parents  c) A shop
**Answer: b) Your parents** — Ask your parents — ቃላዊ ግጥም carried at home.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** Work chants about ploughing are which kind of poem?
a) Written poems  b) Oral poems — ቃላዊ ግጥም  c) Riddles
**Answer: b) Oral poems — ቃላዊ ግጥም** — They are oral poems, carried mouth to mouth.
