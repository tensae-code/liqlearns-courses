# Grade 3 Market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are markets named ሰኞ ገበያ and ቅዳሜ ገበያ?
a) After people  b) After their weekday  c) After goods
**Answer: b) After their weekday** — The market is named by the weekday it is held.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Halima wants what sewn, in የገበያ ጭውውት?
a) A shirt  b) A dress  c) A bag
**Answer: b) A dress** — Halima wants a dress sewn; the ቀጠሮ is not kept.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** የገበያዎች ወግ compares which markets?
a) Village and town  b) Traditional and modern  c) Morning and evening
**Answer: b) Traditional and modern** — Market customs sets traditional beside modern.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern market halls trade when?
a) One day a week  b) All week  c) Only on holidays
**Answer: b) All week** — Halls (አዳራሽ) trade all week; open markets meet on their day.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the buyer leave without the school bag?
a) It is too dear  b) The seller is rude, distracted and finally closing  c) It is lost
**Answer: b) The seller is rude, distracted and finally closing** — Rudeness loses the sale — manners are part of trade.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** How much berbere do they buy, and why?
a) 5 kilos — it is cheap  b) 2 kilos — the price has 'reached the sky'  c) None
**Answer: b) 2 kilos — the price has 'reached the sky'** — Price high, so 2 kilos instead of 5.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** ሳሙና in the web is the example of what?
a) A non-good  b) Merchandise — ሸቀጣሸቀጥ  c) A spice
**Answer: b) Merchandise — ሸቀጣሸቀጥ** — Soap is the yes-example of merchandise.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** ገዢ means what?
a) Seller  b) Buyer  c) Judge
**Answer: b) Buyer** — ገዢ buys; ሻጭ sells, ዳኛ judges.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** አይገኝም can also mean what?
a) Is not cooked  b) Doesn't keep time  c) Is not sold
**Answer: b) Doesn't keep time** — 'Is not found' and 'doesn't keep time' — two meanings.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** A reasoned paragraph mainly gives what?
a) Only a choice  b) Its reasons  c) A story
**Answer: b) Its reasons** — Reasons carry a reasoned paragraph.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** The debate is spoken from what?
a) Memory only  b) Prepared notes  c) Nothing
**Answer: b) Prepared notes** — Groups debate from prepared notes.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** The seller–buyer role play is done how?
a) Alone  b) In pairs  c) By the teacher
**Answer: b) In pairs** — Pairs: one sells, one buys.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** ገበየች splits into what?
a) ገበየ - ች  b) ገ - በየች  c) ገበየች - ት
**Answer: a) ገበየ - ች** — ገበየች → ገበየ - ች: stem and suffix.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** ዋቢዎች is what page of the book?
a) The contents  b) The references — where its readings came from  c) The cover
**Answer: b) The references — where its readings came from** — ዋቢዎች lists the sources of the book's readings.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** Kebede Mikael (1999) gave the book which reading you know?
a) The spice row  b) The honest judge — ሀቀኛው ዳኛ  c) The train
**Answer: b) The honest judge — ሀቀኛው ዳኛ** — ታሪክና ምሳሌ (1999) is the honest judge's source, named in ዋቢዎች.
