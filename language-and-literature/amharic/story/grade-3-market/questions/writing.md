# Grade 3 Market — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write three weekday markets with their days.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the spice row's five spices in order, as the mother points them out.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write two sentences with አይገኝም, one for each meaning.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write your clothing paragraph: your choice, then three reasons.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Split three words of your own into stem and suffix, on the ገበየች model.
