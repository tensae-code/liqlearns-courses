# Grade 4 Helping Each Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does ድር ቢያብር teach?
a) One thread is enough  b) Many threads together hold — people without money can still help  c) Never help anyone
**Answer: b) Many threads together hold — people without money can still help** — Thread joins thread: hands, time and kindness are wealth too.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** የገንዘብ ድጋፍ means…?
a) House repair  b) Money support  c) A harvest party
**Answer: b) Money support** — ገንዘብ is money, ድጋፍ is support — a word partnership.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How long does the harvest take when the neighbourhood works together?
a) A week  b) One day  c) A month
**Answer: b) One day** — Both fields finished in a day — alone it would take a week.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Whom does Tesfaye care for?
a) His teacher  b) His blind grandmother of eighty-eight  c) A neighbour's child
**Answer: b) His blind grandmother of eighty-eight** — He washes her feet and light clothes and guides her everywhere.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** እጦት means…?
a) Plenty  b) Shortage  c) Harvest
**Answer: b) Shortage** — እጦት is shortage — the fitting meaning in its sentence.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What does Ato Libs tell the needle and thread?
a) Work apart  b) Only together do you mend my tears  c) The thread is useless
**Answer: b) Only together do you mend my tears** — Neither mends alone; together they mend the garment's tears.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Who shelters the dove?
a) The hare  b) Her neighbour the kite  c) A farmer
**Answer: b) Her neighbour the kite** — The kite takes the dove into her cave; they share the housework happily.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** How do the rowdies reform in አርኣያነት?
a) They are punished  b) Well-behaved friends befriend them and they copy the example  c) They move school
**Answer: b) Well-behaved friends befriend them and they copy the example** — Example teaches where scolding fails — little by little they reform.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** By nine o'clock, what does the uncle's house wear?
a) Paint  b) Its corrugated roof  c) A fence
**Answer: b) Its corrugated roof** — Many hands raise the house; by nine it wears its roof.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** In the poet and azmari tale, what do the two friends do?
a) Compete  b) Lend each other their gifts  c) Stop working
**Answer: b) Lend each other their gifts** — The voiceless poet and the composing-less azmari lend each other their gifts.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** ተማሪ takes which plural?
a) ተማሪዎች / ተማሪኦች — its fitting tail  b) ተማሪኣት only  c) No plural
**Answer: a) ተማሪዎች / ተማሪኦች — its fitting tail** — Each word takes its fitting plural tail, -ኦች or -ዎች.

**12. (Sub lesson 5)** አያት means…?
a) Blessing  b) Grandmother  c) A wise person
**Answer: b) Grandmother** — አያት is grandmother; በረከት is blessing.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** ስደት means…?
a) Harvest  b) Migration — leaving family and area for a foreign country  c) A market
**Answer: b) Migration — leaving family and area for a foreign country** — ስደት is migration, with its hopes and its costs.

**14. (Sub lesson 6)** Unit 3 is —
a) Mutual Help — መረዳዳት  b) Water — ውሃ  c) Sport
**Answer: a) Mutual Help — መረዳዳት** — መረዳዳት.

**15. (Sub lesson 6)** This unit's grammar target is —
a) suffixes — ቅጥያዎች  b) only punctuation  c) only spelling of names
**Answer: a) suffixes — ቅጥያዎች** — ቅጥያዎች.

**16. (Sub lesson 6)** The listening text is —
a) እህትማማችች — Siblings  b) a market text  c) a road song
**Answer: a) እህትማማችች — Siblings** — Siblings.

**17. (Sub lesson 7)** The fluency trio is —
a) accuracy, speed, expression  b) loud, louder, loudest  c) read, copy, draw
**Answer: a) accuracy, speed, expression** — ትክክለኛነት, ፍጥነት, ገላጭነት.

**18. (Sub lesson 7)** ቤታችን means —
a) your house (m)  b) my house  c) our house
**Answer: c) our house** — ቤታችን — our house.

**19. (Sub lesson 7)** Words were split into —
a) prefix, stem, suffix  b) beginning only  c)  rhymes
**Answer: a) prefix, stem, suffix** — Prefix + stem + suffix.
