# Grade 4 Helping Each Other — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write your support web as three sentences: who needs support, and two kinds of support.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write three sentences about how you help at home in your spare time.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the dove and kite story-flow: beginning action, next action, final outcome — one sentence each.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the first paragraph of the merchant and swimmer story.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write two sentences on ስደት: one hope and one cost of migration.
