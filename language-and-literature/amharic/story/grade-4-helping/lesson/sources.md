Honesty notes: Listening texts (ማዳመጥ) are not printed in this student book — they are teacher-read; the book prints titles, pictures and questions only. Every listening part in this lesson therefore teaches exactly the title, the questions and the plot the book's own questions and placement summary give, and invents no further plot. Stories and readings are plot summaries only, never the book's text. Songs and poems are named and summarised, never transcribed. Sensitive content is filed as the book files it. No hardness numbers are written anywhere in this lesson — the book gives none.
