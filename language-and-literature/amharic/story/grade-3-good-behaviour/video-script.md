# Video script — Grade 3 Good Behaviour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ሥነምግባር** — behaviour.

Character is what a person does. This chapter names the marks of good behaviour, honours an honest judge, and learns the rules that make a school and a library work for everyone.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: ታማኝነት — ምግባር — ቤተመጻሕፍት — ደንብ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
