# Grade 3 Good Behaviour — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two sentences: one rule that is only for school, and one rule that is for everywhere.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write what the judge could say to his friend, kindly and firmly, in two sentences.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Grow the good-behaviour web in words: honesty in the middle, four marks around it.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write your five sentences, one for each mark: . ? ! ፣ “ ”.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Say እንዳያስተምርብን aloud in parts three times, then whole once, and write it correctly.
