# Grade 3 Good Behaviour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In ምስጉኗ መምህር, what question does the class think about?
a) Do all good workers want prizes?  b) Who is the teacher?  c) Where is the school?
**Answer: a) Do all good workers want prizes?** — Whether all good workers want prizes is the thinking question.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Momina and Marta are chosen as what?
a) The fastest runners  b) Exemplary students  c) Librarians
**Answer: b) Exemplary students** — They are chosen exemplary students.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** የቤተመጻሕፍት ውሎዬ is a day spent where?
a) At the market  b) At the library  c) At home
**Answer: b) At the library** — The day is spent at the library.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Ababa Mgbaru find when he investigates?
a) The boy is lazy  b) The boy is a new tenant's child with a real problem  c) The boy has left town
**Answer: b) The boy is a new tenant's child with a real problem** — The boy is new, with a real problem — and is counselled back.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the honest judge refuse?
a) To hear the case  b) To do his friend a favour in the case  c) To sit in court
**Answer: b) To do his friend a favour in the case** — Office held in trust must not serve friend or kin.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ababa Mgbaru's way is to do what first?
a) Punish  b) Find the truth, then judge  c) Tell the neighbours
**Answer: b) Find the truth, then judge** — Investigate first, judge after.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** ታማኝነት means what?
a) Speed  b) Honesty  c) Silence
**Answer: b) Honesty** — ታማኝነት is honesty — first in the good-behaviour web.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** አንባቢ means what?
a) Rule  b) Reader  c) Library
**Answer: b) Reader** — አንባቢ is a reader; ደንብ is a rule, ቤተመጻሕፍት the library.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** In the saying, work is honey to whom?
a) Its doer  b) Its idler  c) Its teacher
**Answer: b) Its idler** — Work is a thorn to its doer, honey to its idler.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** Direct speech is written inside what?
a) Parentheses  b) Quotation marks  c) A concept web
**Answer: b) Quotation marks** — A person's exact words wear quotation marks.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** The comparison paragraph sets what against what?
a) Two schools  b) Bad behaviour's marks against good behaviour's marks  c) Two libraries
**Answer: b) Bad behaviour's marks against good behaviour's marks** — Bad against good, mark by mark.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** Which mark ends a question?
a) ።  b) ?  c) ፣
**Answer: b) ?** — A question ends with ? in this exercise's set.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** How is a very long word read?
a) Skipped  b) In parts, then whole, until smooth  c) Only by the teacher
**Answer: b) In parts, then whole, until smooth** — Parts first, then whole — እንዳያስተምርብን read carefully.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** A proverb-tale carries what at its end?
a) A question  b) A saying  c) A song
**Answer: b) A saying** — Tell the story, then land the saying it carries.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** The sorting review has how many word-kinds?
a) Two  b) Three — noun, adjective, verb  c) Five
**Answer: b) Three — noun, adjective, verb** — Noun, adjective and verb — the three kinds sorted.
