# Lesson plan — Grade 3 Good Behaviour

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Story · **Lesson:** Grade 3 Good Behaviour
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Character is what a person does. This chapter names the marks of good behaviour, honours an honest judge, and learns the rules that make a school and a library work for everyone.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 3 learner's fluent reading and listening: the texts are read twice the fluent way, listened to from the teacher's voice, and answered from what was read and heard.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order:

1. **Listening — good workers and good students** — Listen to **ምስጉኗ መምህር ተሸለመች** — The Exemplary Teacher Is Awarded.
2. **Reading — an elder who fathers a neighbourhood, and an honest judge** — Read **አባባ ምግባሩ** — Ababa Mgbaru.
3. **Behaviour words — sayings and webs** — Discuss the sayings: goodness is for oneself — the good you do comes back to you; and work is a thorn to its doer, honey to its idler — the one who works feels the thorn, the lazy one only dreams of honey.
4. **Writing — punctuation, comparison and library rules** — Re-write sentences adding the correct punctuation, including direct speech inside quotation marks — the exact words a person said, wearing their marks.
5. **Reading aloud and speaking — long words and proverb-tales** — Read very long words aloud carefully, with the teacher: **እንዳያስተምርብን, ለማፈላለግ** and their kin.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Reading Stories and Listening Stories — this lesson's Grade 3 texts are read and heard the same way, on the chapter theme of behaviour. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Retell this chapter's listening and reading texts in sequence, in their own words.
2. Use this chapter's word sets in sentences and in role play.
3. Do this chapter's writing tasks — sentences, paragraphs, webs and tables — with correct punctuation.
4. Do this chapter's grammar review work (suffixes, plurals and word-kinds as the chapter names them).

## If the learner is stuck
- A story will not retell → go back to its sequence: first, then, last — three steps carry every tale in this lesson.
- A word set will not stick → say it in a pair, use it in a sentence, then write it: ear, mouth, hand.
- A writing task feels big → the web or table first, sentences after: the book's own order in every chapter.
