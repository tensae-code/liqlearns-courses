# Listening to a Play

## Do it first

Read the title ያልተፈረደባት ነፍስ — The Soul Not Yet Judged. Guess what secret such a play might hold. Keep your guess; Sub lesson 1 tests it.

## Sub lesson 1 — ያልተፈረደባት ነፍስ — The Soul Not Yet Judged

The Grade 8 book sets a play to be heard, not read — its text is not printed in full. Before listening, think about the title: what can 'the soul not yet judged' mean? In the play, Mesfin holds that 'one day suffices for a person to know a person', yet lives in hide-and-seek (ድብብቆሽ) with Haymanot; Ato Kinfe wants Haymanot to form a relation with Mesfin quickly; and Mesfin is shocked when Buzuye tells him she is living with HIV/AIDS — he had not known that Buzuye and Haymanot are sisters. The lesson of the listening is the play's own: people carry what you do not know, and one day does not suffice to know a person. After listening, pick out the dependent clauses (ጥገኛ ሐረጎች) in sentences taken from the play — 'እኔ ላይ ሲዘንብብኝ' is one.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the main thing each sub lesson taught, in your own words, in order. Your companion asks one question about each part, and you answer from the lesson. Then swap.
