# Listening to a Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mesfin is shocked to learn that Buzuye —
a) is leaving the town  b) is living with HIV/AIDS  c) has married Haymanot
**Answer: b) is living with HIV/AIDS** — Buzuye tells him she is living with HIV/AIDS — a thing he did not know.
