# Listening to a Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mesfin is shocked to learn that Buzuye —
a) is leaving the town  b) is living with HIV/AIDS  c) has married Haymanot
**Answer: b) is living with HIV/AIDS** — Buzuye tells him she is living with HIV/AIDS — a thing he did not know.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ato Kinfe wants Haymanot to —
a) leave Mesfin  b) form a relation with Mesfin quickly  c) tell Buzuye's secret
**Answer: b) form a relation with Mesfin quickly** — his haste is one of the listening's questions.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 'እኔ ላይ ሲዘንብብኝ' is given as an example of —
a) a simple sentence  b) a dependent clause (ጥገኛ ሐረግ)  c) a question
**Answer: b) a dependent clause (ጥገኛ ሐረግ)** — the grammar work picks dependent clauses from the play's sentences.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Listening to a play, you follow the story mainly by —
a) the stage furniture  b) the voices — who speaks, wants and hides  c) the page numbers
**Answer: b) the voices — who speaks, wants and hides** — a heard play is carried by voices alone.
