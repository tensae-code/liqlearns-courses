# Listening to a Play — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mesfin had known all along that Buzuye and Haymanot are sisters.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Buzuye and Haymanot are sisters — a fact Mesfin did not know.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The play's text is printed in full in the student book.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Pre-listening in this lesson begins from the title phrase's meaning.
**Answer: True**
