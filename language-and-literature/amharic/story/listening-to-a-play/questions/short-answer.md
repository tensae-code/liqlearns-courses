# Listening to a Play — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the play teach about Mesfin's saying that one day suffices to know a person?
**Answer:** It shows the saying is false — people carry much that one day cannot reveal.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What phrase does Mesfin use for the pair's hidden relation?
**Answer:** ድብብቆሽ — hide-and-seek: 'until when shall we live in hide-and-seek?'

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Mesfin believe about knowing a person?
**Answer:** That one day suffices for a person to know a person — the belief the play overturns.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one question the book asks after the listening.
**Answer:** Any of: Mesfin's 'one day' view; living in hide-and-seek; Kinfe's haste; Haymanot's love; Mesfin's shock at Buzuye's news; the unknown sisterhood; what was learned.
