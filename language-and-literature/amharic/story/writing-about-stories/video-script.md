# Video script — Writing About Stories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every story has a shape. Five parts. Listen.

**መቼት** — the setting. Where? When?
**ገፀባሕርያት** — the characters. Who is in it?
**ችግር** — the problem. What went wrong?
**ድርጊት** — the actions. What did they do?
**ውጤት** — the outcome. How did it end?

And when you are bigger, two more:
**ሴራ** — the plot. Why did it all happen?
**ጭብጥ** — the theme. What is the story teaching us?

Now a story stops in the middle… YOU write the end.
Now the sentences are all mixed… YOU put them in order.
First. Next. Last.

Name the shape. Finish the story. Order the events.
That is writing about stories.

---

Now the same five parts — in the bigger books' words.

**መቼት** — the setting. Time and place.
**ገፀባሕርያት** — the characters.
**ችግሮች** — the problems. The trouble that PUSHES them to act.
**ድርጊት** — the action they take.
**ልቀት/ውጤት** — the resolution. The problem… solved.

And now their formal names — the literary elements.
**ግጭት** — conflict. A clash! Character against character.
Character against nature.
**ትልም/ሴራ** — plot. Not just "then, then, then" —
BECAUSE. This happened BECAUSE that happened.

Last — a small, whole story. **አጭር ልቦለድ** — short fiction.
One room. One person, awake in the night.
Hunger… or thought — which tires him more?
A whole story, in one small room.
List its people. Sum it in ONE page. No more.

---

Last — what is fiction itself? **ልቦለድ.**

A work the writer brings from his own mind, his own heart.
Made up? Yes. Cut off from truth? NO.

Its people behave like real people.
Its world is made — but it has its own place, its own time.

Small fiction: one story. Few people. One place. One line.
Big fiction: many faces of life. Many people. A wide world.
Plots inside plots. Conflicts — more than two!

And the way to love it? One whole book.
Two weeks. Finish it. That is how readers are grown.
