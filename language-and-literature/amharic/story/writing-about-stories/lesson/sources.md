Honesty notes: (1) Stories are summarised and their structure taught — no story text is copied out of the books, per the course rule. (2) The Grade 4 note box spells the characters term ገፀባህርይ (p.143 placement) where Grade 3 prints ገፀባሕርያት (pp.33–34); the lesson teaches the Grade 3 plural form as the placements' headline term and the difference is the books' own. (3) The "Do it first" wrong-order story is this lesson's own four-sentence practice piece built on the farming-year order of Book 3, p.96; no book page is claimed for its wording.
