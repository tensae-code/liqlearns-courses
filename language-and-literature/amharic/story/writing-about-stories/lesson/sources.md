Honesty notes: (1) Stories are summarised and their structure taught — no story text is copied out of the books, per the course rule. (2) The Grade 4 note box spells the characters term ገፀባህርይ (p.143 placement) where Grade 3 prints ገፀባሕርያት (pp.33–34); the lesson teaches the Grade 3 plural form as the placements' headline term and the difference is the books' own. (3) The "Do it first" wrong-order story is this lesson's own four-sentence practice piece built on the farming-year order of Book 3, p.96; no book page is claimed for its wording. (4) Band 3 growth — Sub lesson 4: the Grade 5 naming (Book 5, p.20) genuinely differs from the Grade 3 naming of Sub lesson 1 in two places: the problem is plural (ችግሮች, defined as the problem that pushes the characters to act) and the outcome is named ልቀት/ውጤት (resolution when the problem is solved). The lesson teaches the difference as growth, not as a correction of the earlier naming. (5) Sub lesson 5: the Grade 7 elements note (Book 6, pp.104–106) is bound into the verse-court reading (በላ ልበልሃ); the lesson teaches the note's five definitions exactly as placed, including the plot's double name ትልም/ሴራ. (6) Sub lesson 6 stands partly on teacher-guide evidence and is labelled so: the Grade 8 guide file in hand ENDS at printed p.38, inside Chapter 4's listening part — the chapter's own printed definition and components list for short fiction lie beyond the secured pages (the chapter runs to printed p.46) and are NOT claimed here. What the sub lesson teaches is what the placements give: the chapter's aims (state what short fiction is; list its components; use transitional words), the read-aloud excerpt the guide describes, and the Grade 8 student book's own short-fiction tasks (characters with roles, p.50; one-page summary, p.55). The line "a complete fiction in a small compass" is this lesson's plain-English summary of that placed material, not a quotation of a definition. (7) Transliterations chigiroch, gichit, liboled are this lesson's reading aids; the placements give the Amharic terms.
