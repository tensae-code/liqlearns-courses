# Writing About Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** መቼት (mechet) names which part of a story?
a) the characters  b) the setting — place and time  c) the outcome
**Answer: b) the setting — place and time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ድርጊት (dirgit) — the actions — are —
a) what characters do about the problem  b) the place of the story  c) the story's message
**Answer: a) what characters do about the problem.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** At the Grade 4 level, which two parts join the five?
a) the title and the author  b) ሴራ (plot) and ጭብጥ (theme)  c) the beginning and the end
**Answer: b) ሴራ (plot) and ጭብጥ (theme).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A story frame asks five questions. Which set is the frame?
a) who, where, when, why, how  b) what colour, how big, how many, whose, which  c) first, second, third, fourth, fifth
**Answer: a) who, where, when, why, how.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the farming year, what comes right after ዘር ይዘራል (the seed is sown)?
a) አዝመራ ይሰበሰባል (the harvest)  b) ቡቃያ ይበቅላል (the sprout comes up)  c) እርሻ ይታረሳል (the ploughing)
**Answer: b) ቡቃያ ይበቅላል.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** In the higher books' naming, the story's problem part is written —
a) ችግሮች — the problem that pushes the characters to act  b) መቼት  c) ልቀት
**Answer: a) ችግሮች** — plural now, and defined by what it does: it pushes the characters to act.

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** ግጭት (gichit), conflict, is —
a) the place and time of the story  b) the clash the characters create with other characters or with natural conditions  c) the message the story conveys
**Answer: b) the clash the characters create with other characters or with natural conditions.**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The set work on a short fiction (አጭር ልቦለድ) includes —
a) listing its characters with their roles, and summarising it in at most one page  b) memorising the whole story by heart  c) copying the story out word for word
**Answer: a) listing its characters with their roles, and summarising it in at most one page.**

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** By the guides' definitions, fiction (ልቦለድ) is —
a) A true report with the names changed  b) A creative work the author brings from his own mind and heart — not cut off from truth  c) Any story that is not about real places
**Answer: b)** — its characters wear the behaviour of real people, in a made world with its own place and time.

**10. (Sub lesson 7)** In fiction, the made world has —
a) No place and no time  b) Its own place and its own time  c) Only the reader's place and time
**Answer: b)** — a made world, standing somewhere and somewhen.

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** Which set belongs to LONG fiction?
a) One story, few characters, narrow setting  b) A single narrative line only  c) Many life-faces, many characters, wide setting, main and sub-plots
**Answer: c)** — and possibly more than two conflicts.

**12. (Sub lesson 9)** In extensive reading (መጠነ ሰፊ ንባብ), the test on the chosen book is worth —
a) 2 or 3 marks  b) 10 or 15 marks, counted like a monthly or semester test  c) No marks at all
**Answer: b)** — readers gain the marks; the book is read as homework over two weeks or more.

**13. (Sub lesson 10)** What writing follows the review questions on ዱባና ድንች?
a) Draw one of them and label the drawing  b) Copy the story twice  c) Write a letter
**Answer: a) Draw one of them and label the drawing** — Grade 1 p.79.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** In the Grade 2 classification, the night bird (የሌሊት ወፍ) goes with animals that…
a) Lay eggs  b) Suckle their young  c) Live in water
**Answer: b) Suckle their young** — it suckles its young like a human — Grade 2 pp.137–138.

**15. (Sub lesson 10)** When you complete a started dialogue, what must each new line do?
a) Answer the line before it  b) Repeat the first line  c) Change the story
**Answer: a) Answer the line before it** — the horse/mule completion, Grade 1 p.137.
