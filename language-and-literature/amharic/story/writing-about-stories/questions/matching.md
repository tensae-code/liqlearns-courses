# Writing About Stories — matching

Match each Amharic name (1–5) to its meaning (a–e).

1. መቼት — ?
2. ገፀባሕርያት — ?
3. ችግር — ?
4. ድርጊት — ?
5. ውጤት — ?

a) the problem
b) the characters
c) the outcome — how it ends
d) the setting — place and time
e) the actions — what the characters do

**Answer key: 1 — d, 2 — b, 3 — a, 4 — e, 5 — c.** (Sub lesson 1)

Match each formal term (1–4) to its meaning (a–d).

1. ግጭት — ?
2. ትልም — ?
3. ልቀት — ?
4. አጭር ልቦለድ — ?

a) short fiction — a complete story in a small compass
b) the resolution — the story's part when the problem is solved
c) conflict — the clash characters create
d) plot — the cause-and-effect connection

**Answer key: 1 — c (Sub lesson 5), 2 — d (Sub lesson 5), 3 — b (Sub lesson 4), 4 — a (Sub lesson 6).**
