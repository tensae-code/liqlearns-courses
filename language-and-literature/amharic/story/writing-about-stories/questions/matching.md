# Writing About Stories — matching

Match each Amharic name (1–5) to its meaning (a–e).

1. መቼት — ?
2. ገፀባሕርያት — ?
3. ችግር — ?
4. ድርጊት — ?
5. ውጤት — ?

a) the problem
b) the characters
c) the outcome — how it ends
d) the setting — place and time
e) the actions — what the characters do

**Answer key: 1 — d, 2 — b, 3 — a, 4 — e, 5 — c.** (Sub lesson 1)
