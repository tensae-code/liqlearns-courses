# Writing About Stories — short answer

Answer in a few words, or one sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is ጭብጥ (chibt), the theme?
**Answer: the message the story carries (conveys).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When you retell a story in writing, whose words must the retelling use?
**Answer: your own words — from a starter sentence, not copied from the book.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the three order questions for any story?
**Answer: what is the beginning, what is the middle, what is the end?**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** How is the story's outcome named in the higher books' wording, and what does the name say?
**Answer: ልቀት/ውጤት — the resolution/outcome: ልቀት (the release) stands beside ውጤት, and the part comes when the problem is solved.**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What is ትልም/ሴራ, the plot?
**Answer: the cause-and-effect connection in the fiction or drama — each event happening because of the one before it.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In the short-fiction chapter, what two things must a learner be able to do with short fiction itself, before the story work begins?
**Answer: state what short fiction is, and list its components.**
