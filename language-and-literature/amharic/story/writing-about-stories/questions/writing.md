# Writing About Stories — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Take a story you know. Write its frame: who, where, when, why, how — one line for each.
**Answer guide: five lines, each answering its frame question from the story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A story stops here: "The monkey visited Goshu the buffalo late, and was reproached…" Write how the story could finish — at least three sentences that grow out of the story so far.
**Answer guide: the ending follows from the given problem and characters; it does not start a new story.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the farming year in its order, numbering the steps 1–6: አዝመራ ይሰበሰባል, ዘር ይዘራል, አረም ይታረማል, እርሻ ይታረሳል, አዝመራው ያሽታል, ቡቃያ ይበቅላል.
**Answer: 1 እርሻ ይታረሳል, 2 ዘር ይዘራል, 3 ቡቃያ ይበቅላል, 4 አረም ይታረማል, 5 አዝመራው ያሽታል, 6 አዝመራ ይሰበሰባል.**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Take a story you know. Write its five literary elements as a list — one line each: characters, setting, conflict (ግጭት), theme (ጭብጥ), plot (ትልም/ሴራ).
**Answer guide: characters named as persons of the story; setting as place and time; conflict as a clash (against a character or against nature); theme in one sentence; plot as a chain of causes, not a bare list of events.**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** Write a summary of a short story you know — at most one page — joining its parts with transitional words (መሸጋገሪያ ቃላት).
**Answer guide: the whole story in one page or less; all five elements present; transitional words visibly carrying the reader from part to part; no retelling in the story's own words.**

**6. (Sub lesson 8)** Write a short text on the short-fiction pattern: one story, few characters, a narrow setting, a single narrative line. Then, under it, write three lines proving it is short fiction by the five marks.
**Answer guide: one narrative line only; characters few and named; the setting one small place in a short time; the proof names lines, characters, setting, plot and conflict — each matched to the short-fiction side of the list.**

**7. (Sub lesson 9)** Choose a fiction you know. Organise paragraphs into a short essay about it: what fiction is, which size this one is, and one place where its characters behave like real people though its world is made.
**Answer guide: the definition in the guides' wording (a creative work from the author's mind/heart, not cut off from truth); the size proved by the five marks; the real-behaviour example drawn from the fiction itself, not invented.**
