# Writing About Stories — writing

Write each one on paper.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Take a story you know. Write its frame: who, where, when, why, how — one line for each.
**Answer guide: five lines, each answering its frame question from the story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A story stops here: "The monkey visited Goshu the buffalo late, and was reproached…" Write how the story could finish — at least three sentences that grow out of the story so far.
**Answer guide: the ending follows from the given problem and characters; it does not start a new story.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the farming year in its order, numbering the steps 1–6: አዝመራ ይሰበሰባል, ዘር ይዘራል, አረም ይታረማል, እርሻ ይታረሳል, አዝመራው ያሽታል, ቡቃያ ይበቅላል.
**Answer: 1 እርሻ ይታረሳል, 2 ዘር ይዘራል, 3 ቡቃያ ይበቅላል, 4 አረም ይታረማል, 5 አዝመራው ያሽታል, 6 አዝመራ ይሰበሰባል.**
