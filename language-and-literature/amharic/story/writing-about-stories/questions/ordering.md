# Writing About Stories — ordering

Put the things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Put the story's shape in the order you walk it when you write about a story: ውጤት — መቼት — ድርጊት — ችግር — ገፀባሕርያት.
**Answer: መቼት → ገፀባሕርያት → ችግር → ድርጊት → ውጤት.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A tiny story is jumbled: "The farmer ate the harvest." / "The farmer sowed the seed." / "The farmer ploughed the field." / "The farmer reaped the crop." Put it in order.
**Answer: ploughed the field → sowed the seed → reaped the crop → ate the harvest.**
