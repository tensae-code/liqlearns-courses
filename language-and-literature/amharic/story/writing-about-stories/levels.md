# Writing About Stories — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons follow the books' climb: the five-part shape of Grade 3, the frame and completion work of Grades 1–3, and the sequencing of Grade 4.

## Sub lesson 1 — The story's shape
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the five parts in Amharic and say what each asks; at the higher level add ሴራ (plot) and ጭብጥ (theme) and say how they differ from the five.

## Sub lesson 2 — Frames, completion and retelling in writing
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fill a story frame in writing, complete a started story, write an ending, and retell a story in your own words from a starter sentence.

## Sub lesson 3 — Putting events in order
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: put jumbled story sentences and jumbled events into the story's own order and say the beginning, middle and end.

## Sub lesson 4 — The five parts, named again higher up
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the five parts in the higher books' wording — ችግሮች (the problem that pushes the characters to act) and ልቀት/ውጤት (the resolution, when the problem is solved) included — and map a whole story onto the frame, part by part.

## Sub lesson 5 — The literary elements, grown formal
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define the five literary elements (የሥነጽሑፍ አላባውያን) formally — characters, setting, ግጭት conflict, ጭብጥ theme, ትልም/ሴራ plot as the cause-and-effect connection — and fill an elements organiser for a read text.

## Sub lesson 6 — Short fiction: what makes a short story
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state what short fiction (አጭር ልቦለድ) is, list its components, list a short fiction's characters with their roles, and summarise a short fiction in at most one page with transitional words joining the parts (the chapter's aims rest on teacher-guide evidence).

## Sub lesson 7 — Fiction itself: what ልቦለድ is (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define fiction in the guides' wording — a creative work from the author's own mind/heart, not cut off from truth — and say how its characters and its made world carry that truth.

## Sub lesson 8 — Short fiction and long fiction (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: set the two sizes side by side by the five marks — narrative lines, characters, setting, plots, conflicts — and prove one known fiction's size from them; write a short text on the single-line pattern.

## Sub lesson 9 — Fiction met whole: heard, read, and one whole book (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run Enatitu's full stretch in order — predict, timed silent reading, answers in a group table, report; name ሰመመን and ሸክም and their kinds of work; and state the extensive-reading system whole — one book, two weeks or more, a test of 10 or 15 marks.
