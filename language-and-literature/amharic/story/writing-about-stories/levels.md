# Writing About Stories — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons follow the books' climb: the five-part shape of Grade 3, the frame and completion work of Grades 1–3, and the sequencing of Grade 4.

## Sub lesson 1 — The story's shape
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the five parts in Amharic and say what each asks; at the higher level add ሴራ (plot) and ጭብጥ (theme) and say how they differ from the five.

## Sub lesson 2 — Frames, completion and retelling in writing
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fill a story frame in writing, complete a started story, write an ending, and retell a story in your own words from a starter sentence.

## Sub lesson 3 — Putting events in order
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: put jumbled story sentences and jumbled events into the story's own order and say the beginning, middle and end.
