# Video script — Grade 4 Water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ዉኃ** — water.

The water chapter: water as medicine, water wasted and water saved, the river that feeds a village twice a year, and the rain cycle that carries water round the world.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: ወንዝ, ምንጭ, ትነት, ቁጠባ, ዝናብ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
