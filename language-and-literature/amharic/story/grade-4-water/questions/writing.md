# Grade 4 Water — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the water-medicine matching as three sentences: ice, cold water, hot water — one hurt each.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write three sentences on saving wash-water in your own house.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the water web as four sentences, with three supporting details.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the black stranger story web: beginning, middle, end — one sentence each.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write your compare paragraph's first four sentences: two likenesses, two differences.
