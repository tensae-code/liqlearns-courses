# Grade 4 Water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For a fall or collision injury, the matching treatment is…?
a) Hot water only  b) Ice / cold water on the hurt  c) No water at all
**Answer: b) Ice / cold water on the hurt** — The listening matches water kinds to hurts — cold for knocks and falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the uncle leap up when wash-water is poured away?
a) He is startled  b) He strains that water for his yard plants — less than a litre is precious  c) He dislikes guests
**Answer: b) He strains that water for his yard plants — less than a litre is precious** — His secret is saving: strained wash-water keeps his plants green.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the lake paragraph, what happens at the end?
a) The lake grows  b) The lake dries, the greenness goes, the people deeply regret  c) Nothing changes
**Answer: b) The lake dries, the greenness goes, the people deeply regret** — Carelessness unfolds point by point to drying and regret.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the expert teach the village in ወንዛችን?
a) To leave the river  b) To lift the river's water for irrigation — harvesting twice and three times a year  c) To sell the river
**Answer: b) To lift the river's water for irrigation — harvesting twice and three times a year** — Irrigation from their own river changed one harvest into two or three.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What gift does the village give her?
a) A house  b) Ten thousand birr — 'even this is too little'  c) A medal only
**Answer: b) Ten thousand birr — 'even this is too little'** — Ten thousand birr, and the narrator says even that is too little for her diligence.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** ትነት means…?
a) Rain  b) Vapour — what plants and water bodies give off  c) A well
**Answer: b) Vapour — what plants and water bodies give off** — ትነት is vapour, the cycle's rising step.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** How does the black stranger repay the couple's kindness?
a) With money  b) He digs a well with them in ten days and refuses their gift — 'the return for your good deed'  c) He leaves at once
**Answer: b) He digs a well with them in ten days and refuses their gift — 'the return for your good deed'** — Eight cubits in ten days — kindness returned as water.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In the rain cycle, after vapour rises…?
a) It vanishes  b) Clouds gather and rain falls  c) The sun stops
**Answer: b) Clouds gather and rain falls** — Vapour → cloud → rain → water again — the loop never stops.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The speaking task asks you to support the claim with…?
a) Volume  b) Evidence — water's benefits against our carelessness  c) A song
**Answer: b) Evidence — water's benefits against our carelessness** — Name the benefits and the carelessness; let the lists argue.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Why did father fence the spring?
a) To keep cattle from spoiling it  b) For decoration  c) To hide it
**Answer: a) To keep cattle from spoiling it** — A fence of wood keeps the cattle out and the spring clean.

**11. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the water-medicine listening ask you to do?
a) Memorise a poem  b) Match each water kind — ice, cold, hot — to the hurt it treats  c) Draw a river
**Answer: b) Match each water kind — ice, cold, hot — to the hurt it treats** — Migraine, colds and flu, fall injuries: each takes its fitting water.
