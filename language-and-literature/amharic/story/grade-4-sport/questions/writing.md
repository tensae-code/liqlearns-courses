# Grade 4 Sport — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two sentences naming two ball games and one athletics event.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write three sentences: one traditional sport, one modern sport, one benefit of exercise.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the poem's bridge picture in your own words, in two sentences.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write three sentences about your own sports period, using ግብ ጠባቂ, ተከላካይ or አጥቂ.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write a four-line gebeta rules paragraph: board, moves, winning, fair play.
