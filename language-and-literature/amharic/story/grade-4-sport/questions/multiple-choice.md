# Grade 4 Sport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sport is Ethiopia world-famous for?
a) Swimming  b) Running  c) Wrestling
**Answer: b) Running** — Ethiopia's world fame is in running — the listening asks and answers it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a TRADITIONAL sport?
a) Basketball  b) ትግል — wrestling  c) Volleyball
**Answer: b) ትግል — wrestling** — ትግል, horse-gugs, Genna, gebeta and swimming are the traditional set.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does exercise do for the heart and breathing?
a) Disorders them  b) Orders the heart-rate and breathing, brings oxygen  c) Nothing
**Answer: b) Orders the heart-rate and breathing, brings oxygen** — Strength, ordered heart and breathing, oxygen, weight control — the reading's list.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The poem calls sport…?
a) A strong bridge that joins everyone in love and unity  b) A wall  c) A race only
**Answer: a) A strong bridge that joins everyone in love and unity** — Sport as a bridge — the poem's own picture.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What feeling does exercise create, in the listening?
a) Tiredness only  b) Happiness  c) Anger
**Answer: b) Happiness** — Exercise creates a feeling of happiness, gathered into the web.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Who plays the first fifteen minutes in የስፖርት ክፍለጊዜያችን?
a) The boys  b) The girls  c) The teachers
**Answer: b) The girls** — Girls first fifteen minutes, boys the second — teams of eleven.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** ተከላካይ means…?
a) Goalkeeper  b) Defender  c) Attacker
**Answer: b) Defender** — ግብ ጠባቂ goalkeeper, ተከላካይ defender, አጥቂ attacker.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** ሴቶቹ carries which suffix?
a) -ኣቸው  b) -ኦች  c) -ኡ
**Answer: b) -ኦች** — ሴቶች + -ኡ written as one: the -ኦች tail shows on ሴቶቹ — the book's own pick-out example.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** በአጠቃላይ means…?
a) For example  b) In general  c) However
**Answer: b) In general** — በአጠቃላይ = in general; ለምሳሌ ያህል = for example; ይሁን እንጂ = however.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** A gebeta rules paragraph is mainly…?
a) A story  b) Order writing — board, moves, how to win  c) A poem
**Answer: b) Order writing — board, moves, how to win** — Rules are written in order: board first, then moves, then winning.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** Which pair builds a word at both ends?
a) Only a suffix  b) Prefix አስ- before and suffix -ች after the stem  c) Only a prefix
**Answer: b) Prefix አስ- before and suffix -ች after the stem** — አስ-/ተ-/እየ- before, -ች/-ኡ/-ኣቸው after — clothes at both ends.

**12. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these counts as a ball game in the listening?
a) Wrestling  b) Football  c) Swimming
**Answer: b) Football** — Ball games are named in the listening; wrestling and swimming are sport, but not ball games.
