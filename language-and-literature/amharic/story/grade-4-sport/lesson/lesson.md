# Grade 4 Sport

## Do it first

Say the chapter word aloud: **ስፖርት** — sport. The sport chapter: Ethiopia's traditional games and modern sports side by side, what exercise does for the body, and a sports period where girls and boys both play. Before any teaching, try this: name one thing you already know about this chapter's theme in Amharic, and one word you want this lesson to teach you.

## Sub lesson 1 — Listening — modern sport and its supporters

Listen to **ዘመናዊ ስፖርትና ደጋፊዎቹ** — modern sport and its supporters. Which games count as ball games? What does athletics include? And in which sport is Ethiopia world-famous? Listen for the answers the crowd itself would give.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading — sport old and new, and the benefits of exercise

Read **ስፖርት** — sport joins people and introduces them to each other. TRADITIONAL sports: **ትግል** wrestling, **ፈረስ ጉግስ** horse-gugs, **የገና ጨዋታ** the Genna game, **ገበጣ** the gebeta board game, **ዉኃ ዋና** swimming — with traditional dress and equipment. MODERN sports: football, volleyball, basketball, running, jumping, shot put, discus, javelin. Both kinds strengthen love, peace, unity and health.

Read **ስፖርትና ጥቅሙ** — sport and its benefit. Exercise gives strength and resistance to disease, orders the muscles, the heart-rate and the breathing, brings clean air and oxygen, controls weight, keeps liver, pancreas and kidneys healthy — and builds friendship between countries. Build the web: the main idea of each of the four paragraphs.

## Sub lesson 3 — Listening — the sport poem and the benefits of moving

Listen to the poem **ስፖርት ለጤንነት** — sport for health. The poem calls sport a strong bridge that joins everyone, binding them in love, creating unity. Hear also what harms health — the poem names both sides.

Listen to **የአካል ብቃት እንቅስቃሴ ጠቀሜታዎች** — the benefits of physical exercise — and gather them into a concept web. Hear how exercise creates a feeling of happiness, not only strength.

## Sub lesson 4 — Reading — our sports period

Read **የስፖርት ክፍለጊዜያችን** — our sports period — told by a girl. The uniforms are changed under the warka trees; there is a warm-up; then football — the girls play the first fifteen minutes, the boys the second. Teams of eleven; she plays defender. Today she learned the names: **ግብ ጠባቂ** goalkeeper, **ተከላካይ** defender, **አጥቂ** attacker — and the game's laws. The teacher referees with a long whistle blast.

Pick the suffixes out of words: **ሴቶቹ → -ኦች**; hunt the tails in **አንዳችንም, ልብሳችንን, ጓደኞቻችን**. A suffix found is a word understood twice.

## Sub lesson 5 — Writing — gebeta rules, verses, punctuation and joining words

Write a short paragraph on the rules of playing **ገበጣ** — the gebeta board game. Rules writing is order writing: first the board, then the moves, then how a player wins.

Punctuate: insert **፣ ፤ ? “ ” .** into the given sentences. Each mark has its own work — the sentence tells you which it needs.

Complete the given verse openings — ጀምር ስንኞች — into lines about sickness, excess and healthy living. A verse opening is a door; your line walks through it.

Use the TRANSITION WORDS — መሸጋገሪያ ቃላት: complete a paragraph with **ለምሳሌ ያህል** (for example), **ይሁን እንጂ** (however), **በአጠቃላይ** (in general), **በተጨማሪም** (in addition), **ወይም** (or). Transitions are the paragraph's joints.

Review all the marks together: insert **! ፤ “ ” ፣ ? .** into unpunctuated sentences — the full set at work.

Build words with prefixes AND suffixes together: **አስ-, ተ-, እየ-** before the stem; **-ች, -ኡ, -ኣቸው** after it. A word can wear clothes at both ends.

Complete the starter: today's period is the running race; children from three classes have met at the running ground; my heart is pounding; I, from the side, … Your sentences run the race to its end.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the chapter word — **ስፖርት** — and the one part of this lesson you liked most, in Amharic where you can. Your companion retells one story or rule from the lesson back to you, in their own words.

2. Write five sentences using this lesson's own words.

4. Teach one saying, rule or word set from this lesson to someone at home.
