# Video script — Grade 3 Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ሥራ** — work.

Work divided is work done: the carpenter's two branches, the bees' three kinds, a healer's old knowledge and a lawyer who cannot see — this chapter honours the people who do a country's work.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: አናጺ — ንብ — መምህር — ሥራ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
