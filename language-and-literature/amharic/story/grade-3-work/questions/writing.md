# Grade 3 Work — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write Yetnebersh's life in order: five years old, blind school, university, work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write two sentences: how a teacher has shaped you.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Build three 'like…' similes of your own, for three animals.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write አባት with three possessive endings and say whose father each one is.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Give your speech on the house-work motion in four sentences: your side, two reasons, your close.
