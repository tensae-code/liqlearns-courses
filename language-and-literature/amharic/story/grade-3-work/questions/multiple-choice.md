# Grade 3 Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Carpentry's two branches are what?
a) Indoor and outdoor  b) Big construction and house work  c) Old and new
**Answer: b) Big construction and house work** — Big construction; house work — the reading's own two branches.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How do people take the bees' honey?
a) With bare hands  b) With smoke and covered hands  c) At night only
**Answer: b) With smoke and covered hands** — Smoke and covered hands, in the bees' own narration.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Yetnebersh Nigussie studied what at Addis Ababa University?
a) Medicine  b) Law  c) Teaching
**Answer: b) Law** — She is a law graduate and rights advocate.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A teacher shapes what, in የመምህርነት ክብር?
a) A classroom  b) A generation  c) A school building
**Answer: b) A generation** — The honour of teaching is shaping a generation.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Abebech Shifera is a traditional what?
a) Carpenter  b) Healer  c) Judge
**Answer: b) Healer** — She is a traditional healer — ሃኪም.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** ሥነፈለግ is the study of what?
a) Stars — astronomy  b) Soil  c) Water
**Answer: a) Stars — astronomy** — ሥነፈለግ is astronomy, which she also studies.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** እንደንብ ታታሪ means what?
a) As lazy as a bee  b) As diligent as a bee  c) As loud as a bee
**Answer: b) As diligent as a bee** — The bee simile praises diligence — ታታሪ.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The carpenter web has how many branches?
a) Two  b) Four — tools, products, actions, skills  c) Six
**Answer: b) Four — tools, products, actions, skills** — Tools, products, actions, skills: four branches.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** ሁልጊዜ — always — is which kind?
a) Adjective  b) Adverb  c) Noun
**Answer: b) Adverb** — ሁልጊዜ tells how often — an adverb in this sorting.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** ወደቤትሽ corrects ወደቤት how?
a) It adds the suffix that fits the person  b) It changes the verb  c) It makes it plural
**Answer: a) It adds the suffix that fits the person** — The possessive ending must agree with its person.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** -ኤ on a noun says the thing is whose?
a) Yours  b) Mine  c) Theirs
**Answer: b) Mine** — -ኤ is 'mine' — the first possessive drilled.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** The possessive drill words include which?
a) ወንድም and አባት  b) መኪና and ባቡር  c) ገበያ and ትምህርት
**Answer: a) ወንድም and አባት** — ወንድም, ግድብ, ባህል, አባት carry the drills.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** The future-job paragraph needs what first?
a) A drawing  b) Gathered information  c) A title only
**Answer: b) Gathered information** — Gather information, then write the paragraph.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** ሀገርበቀል እውቀት means what?
a) Foreign knowledge  b) Indigenous knowledge — grown in this country  c) School knowledge
**Answer: b) Indigenous knowledge — grown in this country** — It is the country's own grown knowledge.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** In the bee role play, the three roles are what?
a) Queen, worker, drone  b) King, soldier, cook  c) Mother, father, child
**Answer: a) Queen, worker, drone** — Queen, worker and drone — the hive's three kinds.
