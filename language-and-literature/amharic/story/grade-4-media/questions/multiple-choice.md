# Grade 4 Newspapers Radio and Television — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first newspaper matched to its years distributed how many copies?
a) 200 copies  b) 2,000 copies  c) 20 copies
**Answer: a) 200 copies** — Two hundred copies — the listening's matching fact.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1929 newspaper format was…?
a) All adverts  b) Three pages news, two pages adverts  c) One page only
**Answer: b) Three pages news, two pages adverts** — Three pages of news and two of adverts — the format the listening dates.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pawlos Gnogno's schooling stopped…?
a) At university  b) After Grade 4, when the Italian invasion came  c) He never went to school
**Answer: b) After Grade 4, when the Italian invasion came** — Stopped at Grade 4 — yet he wrote nineteen books.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** His famous Addis Zemen column was called…?
a) አንድ ጥያቄ አለኝ — I have one question  b) የድምጽ ጋዜጣ  c) አዕምሮ
**Answer: a) አንድ ጥያቄ አለኝ — I have one question** — The 'I have one question' column made his name.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Who names the mass media for the questioning student?
a) Her teacher  b) Her father  c) A journalist
**Answer: b) Her father** — Her father names them; print and voice media are then compared.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which word belongs on the media map?
a) ሬዲዮ — radio  b) ገበጣ  c) ርከን
**Answer: a) ሬዲዮ — radio** — ሬዲዮ, ቴሌቪዥን, ጋዜጣ — the media family.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** What happens to the hawker in the café?
a) He is arrested  b) His bundle slips, glasses smash — the manager counsels him and forgives him  c) He sells everything
**Answer: b) His bundle slips, glasses smash — the manager counsels him and forgives him** — The manager cleans up, counsels, and lets him go with forgiveness.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Which of these is a preposition in the text?
a) ጋዜጣ  b) ወደ — to/towards  c) አዟሪ
**Answer: b) ወደ — to/towards** — በ, ወደ, ከ, ለ, ስለ stand before nouns showing place, direction, purpose.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Why does radio serve even the poor of the town?
a) It has pictures  b) It is cheap and runs on a battery  c) It is printed
**Answer: b) It is cheap and runs on a battery** — Voice only, battery, cheap — radio reaches everyone.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Who teaches Sisaynesh most?
a) Her listeners  b) Weyzero Tihitna, her supervisor of 35 years  c) A book
**Answer: b) Weyzero Tihitna, her supervisor of 35 years** — The veteran supervisor's experience shapes the young journalist.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** Sisaynesh is named at the festival…?
a) Best singer  b) Exemplary and diligent journalist of the year  c) Manager
**Answer: b) Exemplary and diligent journalist of the year** — Fact-based reports and many listeners earn her the year's name.
