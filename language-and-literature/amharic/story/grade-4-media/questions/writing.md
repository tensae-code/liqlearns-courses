# Grade 4 Newspapers Radio and Television — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write three sentences from the first-newspaper listening: one event and its year, twice over.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write a four-sentence summary of Pawlos Gnogno's life.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the media word map as three sentences.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write three sentences retelling the hawker's café moment — first, then, last.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write your school-layout composition's first paragraph.
