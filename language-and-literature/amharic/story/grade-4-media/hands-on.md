# Hands-on — Grade 4 Newspapers Radio and Television

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Retell with stones
Lay five stones in a row. One stone, one step of the story. Retell this chapter's main tale, touching a stone for each step — first, then, then, then, last.

## Make the web on paper
Draw this chapter's concept web or table big, on a full page. Put the chapter word — ብዙኃን መገናኛ — in the middle, and grow its branches from the lesson.

## Role-play in pairs
Take one dialogue or speaking task from this lesson and play both parts with a partner at home — then swap parts and play it again.

## Teach it at home
Teach one saying, one safety rule or one word set from this chapter to someone at home. Teaching is the last step of learning it.
