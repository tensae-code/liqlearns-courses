# Video script — Grade 4 Newspapers Radio and Television

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ብዙኃን መገናኛ** — mass communication — media for everyone.

The media chapter: the first newspapers, a journalist who crossed ages, the hawker and the woman journalist, and how radio and television carry news to everyone — rich and poor alike.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: ጋዜጣ, ሬዲዮ, ቴሌቪዥን, ዜና, ጋዜጠኛ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
