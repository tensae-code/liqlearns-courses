# The Elements of a Play — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** መቼት in a play is —
a) the people who act  b) the place and time where it happens  c) the clash between characters
**Answer: b) the place and time where it happens** — መቼት is the setting — place and time.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the elements table, ጡዘት is —
a) the setting  b) the climax — the highest point  c) a character's name
**Answer: b) the climax — the highest point** — ጡዘት is the climax, the peak of the action.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What pressure does Act 2 name as the cause of the debt?
a) a failed harvest  b) custom (ወግ) — the expectation to feast and spend  c) a stolen purse
**Answer: b) custom (ወግ) — the expectation to feast and spend** — Demena's speech names custom: people will not recognise you if you do not feast.
