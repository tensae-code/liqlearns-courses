# The Elements of a Play — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the people who speak and act in a play called?
**Answer:** ገፀባሕርያት — the characters.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What problem appears at the play's beginning?
**Answer:** Belayhun's debt — creditors phoning for rented goods and money after the wedding feast.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the play's problem resolve at the end?
**Answer:** The family cancels the return feast and decides to live within its means.
