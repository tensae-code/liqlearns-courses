# Hands-on — Poetry's Double Meaning — Wax and Gold (ሰምና ወርቅ)

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Wax, then gold
Take a verse your teacher gives you. Write its wax meaning in one column and hunt its gold meaning in the other.

## Make a small one
Write two lines about a friend where one word carries a kind secret meaning. Read the wax to the class; let them dig for the gold.
