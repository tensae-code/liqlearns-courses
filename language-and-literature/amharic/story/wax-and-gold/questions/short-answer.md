# Poetry's Double Meaning — Wax and Gold (ሰምና ወርቅ) — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the worked verse, what is በቅሎ's gold meaning?
**Answer:** 'One like you has never yet been born.'

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How many verses does the book set for the wax-and-gold exercises?
**Answer:** Five.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the worked example, what is በቅሎ's WAX meaning?
**Answer:** The household animal used for transport — the mule of the poem's surface.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one of the five exercise verse openings.
**Answer:** Any of: ሞጣ ቀራንዮ…, በጎጃም ያካፋል በሸዋ ፀሐይ…, ለምን ገለበጡት ወተቱን በጋን…, አዲስ አበባ ላይ ቡና ቢወደድ…, የጥቁር በሬ ስባቱ…
