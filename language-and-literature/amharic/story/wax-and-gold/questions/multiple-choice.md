# Poetry's Double Meaning — Wax and Gold (ሰምና ወርቅ) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In ሰምና ወርቅ, the GOLD meaning is —
a) the meaning on the surface  b) the secret meaning hidden under the surface  c) the rhyme at the line's end
**Answer: b) the secret meaning hidden under the surface** — gold is the hidden meaning; wax is the surface one.
