# Poetry's Double Meaning — Wax and Gold (ሰምና ወርቅ) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In ሰምና ወርቅ, the GOLD meaning is —
a) the meaning on the surface  b) the secret meaning hidden under the surface  c) the rhyme at the line's end
**Answer: b) the secret meaning hidden under the surface** — gold is the hidden meaning; wax is the surface one.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The three steps for each exercise verse are —
a) gold, wax, rhyme  b) ኅብረቃል first, then wax, then gold  c) translation, then copying
**Answer: b) ኅብረቃል first, then wax, then gold** — find the double word, read its surface, then dig its secret.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Before digging for gold, you must first read —
a) another poem  b) the wax — the surface meaning  c) the title only
**Answer: b) the wax — the surface meaning** — wax first: the gold grows out of the surface.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If your gold meaning does not grow out of the verse's surface, it is —
a) a reading  b) a guess  c) a rhyme
**Answer: b) a guess** — a gold that ignores the wax is a guess, not a reading — the verse itself must judge.
