# Poetry's Double Meaning — Wax and Gold (ሰምና ወርቅ) — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word carrying both meanings is called an ኅብረቃል.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A gold meaning must grow out of the verse's surface — it is never a free guess.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** ሰም is the wax — the meaning plainly seen on the surface.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where partners' gold meanings differ, the verse itself decides between them.
**Answer: True**
