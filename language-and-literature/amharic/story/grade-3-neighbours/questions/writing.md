# Grade 3 Neighbours — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell ዶሮ ለምን ትጭራለች? in three steps — first, then, last — and say whose fault the quarrel was.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write what the cat should have done to keep the peace, in two sentences.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the elephant story's three helpers and what each one did.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** With a partner, play caller and answerer: call three words, and answer each with its opposite.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write the three criticising sentences about the fox, each ending with ።.
