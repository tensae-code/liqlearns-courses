# Grade 3 Neighbours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What beats haste, in ከመቸኮል ማስተዋል?
a) Patience  b) Running  c) Shouting
**Answer: a) Patience** — The fable teaches patience over haste.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What saves the ox in the fable?
a) The flood filling the pit  b) A rope  c) The farmer's son
**Answer: a) The flood filling the pit** — The flood filling the pit saves the ox.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the answerer's answer in የሰፈር ልጆች?
a) The rain took me  b) The wind took me  c) I was sleeping
**Answer: b) The wind took me** — The answerer says the wind took me.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What do Ato Aynalem and Ato Anbaye promise?
a) To sell their fields  b) To marry their children to each other, sealing their friendship  c) To build a fence
**Answer: b) To marry their children to each other, sealing their friendship** — They promise their children in marriage to seal the friendship.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the cat lure the mouse out?
a) By calling her name  b) By imitating a dog's bark  c) By bringing food
**Answer: b) By imitating a dog's bark** — The cat imitates a dog's bark to lure the mouse.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the cat and mouse first agree?
a) To live in peace  b) To share a house  c) To hunt together
**Answer: a) To live in peace** — They agreed to live in peace — and the cat broke it.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the fox smear on the sleeping hyena's mouth?
a) Mud  b) Blood  c) Milk
**Answer: b) Blood** — The fox smears blood to frame the hyena.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Who takes all the cattle at the end?
a) The lion  b) The hyena  c) The fox
**Answer: c) The fox** — The fox takes all the cattle.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Which neighbour croaks from the deep pit?
a) The fly  b) The frog  c) The woodpecker
**Answer: b) The frog** — The frog croaks from the pit the elephant leaps into.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the opposite pair taught here?
a) የዋህ → ተንኮለኛ  b) ጫጩት → ክንፍ  c) ጎረቤት → ሰፈር
**Answer: a) የዋህ → ተንኮለኛ** — የዋህ (gentle) answers to ተንኮለኛ (sly).

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** ጫጩት means what?
a) Wing  b) Chick  c) Kite
**Answer: b) Chick** — ጫጩት is a chick; ክንፍ is wing, ጭልፊት is kite.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** In the role table, who checks the sentences?
a) The word-caller  b) The sentence-maker  c) The corrector
**Answer: c) The corrector** — The corrector is the third role in the table.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** አባል → አባላት uses which suffix?
a) -ኦች  b) -ኣት  c) እነ-
**Answer: b) -ኣት** — አባላት is made with -ኣት.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** Which small word hides inside ብርቅ, in the book's set?
a) ቅርብ  b) መጽሐፍ  c) ውኃ
**Answer: a) ቅርብ** — ቅርብ is one of the words found inside ብርቅ.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** እነ- makes a plural by standing where?
a) At the end  b) In front of names  c) In the middle
**Answer: b) In front of names** — እነ- stands in front of names to make the plural.

**16. (Sub lesson 6)** Unit 2 is —
a) Neighbourliness — ጉርብትና  b) Use of Time  c) Work — ስራ
**Answer: a) Neighbourliness — ጉርብትና** — ጉርብትና — neighbourliness.

**17. (Sub lesson 6)** Among its objectives is giving —
a) antonyms for words from a text  b) directions to the market  c) a weather report
**Answer: a) antonyms for words from a text** — Antonyms from the text.

**18. (Sub lesson 6)** Sentences in this unit are written using —
a) punctuation  b) no marks at all  c) only capital letters
**Answer: a) punctuation** — ስርዓተ ኅፁቦች — punctuation.

**19. (Sub lesson 7)** The listening task's main purpose is to —
a) measure how much was understood  b) fill time  c) test handwriting
**Answer: a) measure how much was understood** — Measure understanding.

**20. (Sub lesson 7)** The task links first to —
a) learners' own experience  b) a foreign country  c) the market
**Answer: a) learners' own experience** — Own experience first.

**21. (Sub lesson 7)** The word task's second part builds —
a) speaking skill on the same words  b) a crossword  c) a song
**Answer: a) speaking skill on the same words** — የንግግር ክሂል — speaking items.

**22. (Sub lesson 8)** The summary names which marks?
a) Full stop, question mark and quotation  b) Only the colon  c) Only the slash
**Answer: a) Full stop, question mark and quotation** — አራት ነጥብ, ጥያቄ ምልክት, ጥቅስ.

**23. (Sub lesson 8)** Compound words in this unit were —
a) ignored  b) picked out and commented on  c) only counted
**Answer: b) picked out and commented on** — Picked out and commented.

**24. (Sub lesson 8)** The verification bullets mirror —
a) the four objectives  b) the contents page  c) another book
**Answer: a) the four objectives** — The four objectives.
