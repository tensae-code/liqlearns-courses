# Video script — Grade 3 Neighbours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ጉርብትና** — neighbourliness.

Neighbours are the people whose fields touch yours and whose children eat at your table. This chapter teaches patience between neighbours, help given and returned, and what a bad neighbour — in the animal tales — does to everyone.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: ጎረቤት — መፋቀር — መተማመን — አባላት.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
