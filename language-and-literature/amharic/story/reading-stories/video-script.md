# Video script — Reading Stories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is the full script the placements name for this lesson: the
Rooster and the Fox told whole, with the guide's own prediction
pause.

---

A fox met a rooster.
The fox said: "Your voice is sweet. Sing for me!"

The fox seized the rooster. The fox ran.
The shepherds saw. The shepherds chased!

The rooster said: "Shout to them!
Shout: this rooster is not yours!"

***Will the rooster escape the fox?***

Guess. …

The fox opened his mouth to shout —
and the rooster flew out! Free!

The rooster saved himself — with the fox's own trick.
---

Read this story with me. A father speaks to his two children.

"Build houses," he says.

One child builds with stone. Walls. A roof.
The other child builds friends. Greetings. Help. Laughter.

Years pass. The stone house stands still.
But when trouble comes — who is carried? Who is fed?
The one with friends.

Guess before you read on. Which house lasts longest? …

Read it twice. First for the story. Then for fluency.
Then say its meaning in one sentence of your own.
