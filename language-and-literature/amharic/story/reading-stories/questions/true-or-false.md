# Reading Stories — true or false

Say if the sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fikirte can see well, and Kirubel is blind.
**Answer: False** — Fikirte is blind; Kirubel walks with her.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The director took the club members' name list.
**Answer: True** — the reading's marked question asks why.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Holiday Clothes stands beside the materials cotton, leather and wool.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In Protect Our Environment, learners stand and read aloud to classmates.
**Answer: True** — that is the guide's fluency run.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Eyoel and Amira care for their surroundings.
**Answer: True.**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In the vine and the trunk, the trunk says the vine can stand alone easily.
**Answer: False** — the trunk answers that the vine cannot stand alone.
**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Tirngo, in A Dream — Or Not?, wakes relieved and vows to stay in school.
**Answer: True.**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Kedir takes his brother's medicines openly, with the family's knowledge.
**Answer: False** — he takes them secretly, and only the hospital exposes the cause of his sickness.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Asefa and Tirngo, who walked 12 km to school, end as international athletes.
**Answer: True** — and Andarge ends as a language teacher.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** Ato Altaye's wife and children enter his own tavern, and he realises his fault.
**Answer: True.**

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** The returned emigrant in What Regret Bore builds an elderly-care centre in his parents' compound.
**Answer: True.**

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** The bat keeps the friendship of both sides by joining whichever is winning.
**Answer: False** — both sides drive him off; the hen counsels peace.

**13. (Sub lesson 25)** The story's teller became a health extension worker after persevering to Grade 10.
**Answer: True**

**14. (Sub lesson 26)** The expert advised her to attend a health station for continuing education and to choose a method fitting her circumstances.
**Answer: True**

**15. (Sub lesson 27)** Tebejje built his grass-roofed house himself and was proud of it.
**Answer: True**

**16. (Sub lesson 28)** The wife borrowed from neighbours and bought what the holiday needed, before she knew what had happened.
**Answer: True**

**17. (Sub lesson 29)** Captured, he answers his interrogators with pride, without lowering his eyes.
**Answer: True**

**18. (Sub lesson 30)** Before learning the truth, the son introduced his mother to friends as his servant.
**Answer: True**

**19. (Sub lesson 31)** The king fed the donkey with his own mules until it recovered.
**Answer: True**

**20. (Sub lesson 32)** The assembly pledges to protect protected forests and restore the destroyed ones.
**Answer: True**

**21. (Sub lesson 33)** Auntie Altaye promises to speak for Mimi and, if that fails, to return her to her family.
**Answer: True**

**22. (Sub lesson 34)** His mother says useful messages and education also come by television.
**Answer: True**

**23. (Sub lesson 35)** Sheikh Abdela went house to house asking whose found money it was.
**Answer: True**

**24. (Sub lesson 36)** The elder told Efrem that hitting animals is a disgrace (ነውር).
**Answer: True**
