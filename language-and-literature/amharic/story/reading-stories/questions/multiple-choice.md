# Reading Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is Fikirte?
a) Kirubel's teacher  b) Kirubel's sister, who is blind  c) Kirubel's mother
**Answer: b)** — his sister; he plays, walks to school and shops with her.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the environment club do?
a) It waters the school's plants and keeps the compound clean  b) It cooks lunch  c) It sells books
**Answer: a)** — watering morning and evening by rota, and cleaning.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How is Holiday Clothes read, in the guide's way?
a) Once only, silently  b) Read and re-read for fluency  c) Only by the teacher
**Answer: b)** — re-reading until it flows; weaker readers are timed on a chart.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the main idea of Protect Our Environment?
a) Let us protect our environment  b) Let us build more roads  c) Let us read faster
**Answer: a)** — it is in the title: አካባቢያችን እንጠብቅ.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What is the post-reading work for Eyoel and Amira?
a) Copy the story word for word  b) Write the main idea in your own sentences  c) Draw the school
**Answer: b)** — the main idea in the reader's own words, read aloud.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** How does the rooster escape the fox?
a) The shepherds catch the fox  b) The fox opens his mouth to shout, and the rooster flies out  c) The rooster digs a hole
**Answer: b)** — he used the fox's own trick.
**7. (Sub lesson 7)** In The Wise Girl, what does "build houses" truly mean?
a) Build with stone quickly  b) Make lasting friends  c) Build a tall house
**Answer: b)** — the friend-maker is honoured above the stone-builder.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** What does Fasikaw do when he meets the lion in Fear?
a) He climbs a tree  b) He runs home  c) He plays dead
**Answer: c)** — he plays dead, and the story's end warns against friends who betray in a day of trouble.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** How does Senait weigh the butter in The Scale?
a) Against Zebiba's own bread  b) Against a stone  c) By counting handfuls
**Answer: a)** — measure for measure: the butter against the buyer's own bread.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** What teaches the father the truth in Salt?
a) A dream  b) A meal served without salt  c) A neighbour's letter
**Answer: b)** — the cook serves unsalted food, and the father learns what his youngest daughter meant.

**11. (Sub lesson 9)** Why does the guest feed his clothes in "Feed My Clothes!"?
a) He is not hungry  b) The feast honoured his clothes, not him  c) The food is cold
**Answer: b)** — shunned in rags, honoured in fine clothes: "It was for them you made it."

**12. (Sub lesson 9)** In Blessing and Curse, why is the bee blessed?
a) She kills the queen quickly  b) She refuses to kill her queen  c) She flies fastest
**Answer: b)** — the bee refuses the order and is blessed; the fly obeys and is cursed.

**13. (Sub lesson 10)** What does the village council resolve in One Does Not Choose One's Lineage?
a) To stand together against hate  b) To build a wall  c) To move the village
**Answer: a)** — the village resolves to stand together against hate.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** What does Be'emnet found in I Will Not Give In?
a) A school  b) A free legal-aid centre for women  c) A market stall
**Answer: b)** — she flees early marriage, studies law, and founds the centre.

**15. (Sub lesson 11)** In The Bird's Song, what do the birds decide after the third nest is destroyed?
a) To leave the river  b) To build in stone  c) To live together, cooperatively
**Answer: c)** — together is how the fourth building stands.

**16. (Sub lesson 11)** Which two holy grounds do the volunteers clean in Friendship and Volunteering?
a) A church and a school  b) The Meskel Demera site and the Eid prayer place  c) Two markets
**Answer: b)** — Mansur and Admasu's volunteers clean both.

**17. (Sub lesson 12)** How long does Asrebeb guard the dropped money in Trustworthiness Is for Oneself?
a) Two hours  b) Two days  c) A week
**Answer: a)** — he guards it two hours, until the owner comes, and returns it.

**18. (Sub lesson 12)** How is the false madman found out in You Shall Not Go Mad Without Paying Me?
a) He laughs in court  b) The examination confirms he is sane  c) A witness sees him working
**Answer: b)** — examined and found sane, he receives a twofold judgement.

**19. (Sub lesson 13)** Why does the clay pot break in They Heard a Shout?
a) It walks on the right side by mistake and falls when the car comes  b) It is dropped at home  c) It is washed too hard
**Answer: a) It walks on the right side by mistake and falls when the car comes** — the right side is dangerous; the clay pot falls and shatters.

**20. (Sub lesson 14)** What did Ayele see on the shola tree?
a) A huge python coiled there  b) A beehive  c) His lost ball
**Answer: a) A huge python coiled there** — part 4 of the serial story, Grade 1 pp.95–96.

**21. (Sub lesson 15)** In The Election, which food mediates when the groups quarrel?
a) The lemon  b) The potato  c) The egg
**Answer: a) The lemon** — ምርጫው, Grade 1 p.107.

**22. (Sub lesson 16)** How is Aklilu's white shirt made white again?

**Answer: b) It is soaked again with bleach (በረኪና) and Omo** — Grade 2 p.2.

**23. (Sub lesson 17)** What is the clever woman's cure in ብልኋ ሴት?

**Answer: b) Patience — feeding the lion day by day until it is tame** — she brings the lion's gofer by patience, Grade 2 p.36.

**24. (Sub lesson 18)** What does the dog promise in the cat-and-dog dialogue?

**25. (Sub lesson 19)** How does the blind student's phone speak callers' names?

**26. (Sub lesson 13)** The clay pot and the iron pot are frightened by…

**27. (Sub lesson 13)** Where should the pots cross the road?

**28. (Sub lesson 14)** In part 2, the children run because…

**Answer: a) Ayele slid down stammering and fled — they follow without knowing why** — ልጆችዬ እናምልጥ!, Grade 1 p.92.

**29. (Sub lesson 14)** The children decide at the end never to go to the bush…

**30. (Sub lesson 15)** The lemon's work in The Election is to…

**31. (Sub lesson 15)** The doctor orders Goshu to be…

**32. (Sub lesson 16)** “Washed mud” means…

**33. (Sub lesson 16)** In The Circle Game, “ሰኞ” makes the players…

**34. (Sub lesson 17)** The monkey judge ends the jackal quarrel by…

**35. (Sub lesson 17)** The two-year-old's sickness in Oh, Ignorance! comes from…

**37. (Sub lesson 18)** Nejim and Hana help the old man by…

**40. (Sub lesson 20)** In COVID-19 times, what replaces hand-kissing and handshake greetings in Fatuma's dialogue?

**41. (Sub lesson 20)** Who is Setotaw revealed to be at Dangila?

**42. (Sub lesson 20)** Why does Azalu weep in the taxi?

**43. (Sub lesson 21)** What does Wubit do with the bundle the truck driver slips toward her pocket?

**44. (Sub lesson 21)** What is the wedding car's fault when it is stopped?

**45. (Sub lesson 21)** Where does Arega finally read with appetite?

**46. (Sub lesson 21)** What does የውኃ ጡር mean?

**47. (Sub lesson 22)** Who keeps the minutes at the animals' assembly?

**48. (Sub lesson 22)** How many times does the released buffalo find its way back home?

**49. (Sub lesson 22)** What is new in the Genna game today, in the son's words?

**50. (Sub lesson 22)** In Ete Mete, what happens to the tempo as the song goes on?

**51. (Sub lesson 23)** Why does the servant refuse to lift his fallen master?
