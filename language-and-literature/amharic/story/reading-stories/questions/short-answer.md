# Reading Stories — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 25)** Why are spaced children healthier, in the worker's teaching?
**Answer:** Each gets proper feeding and care with no rival, and the mother is not exhausted.

**2. (Sub lesson 26)** What does ልጅ በልጅነት mean?
**Answer:** 'A child in childhood' — a child bearing children while still almost a child herself.

**3. (Sub lesson 27)** Why did Tebejje decide he must quit drink?
**Answer:** For his new house's honour — passers-by would name him 'that drunkard of Gulele' beside his own house.

**4. (Sub lesson 28)** What does the husband vow at the end?
**Answer:** To quit drink and gambling, which made him grieve his wife and children.

**5. (Sub lesson 29)** What does his country write his name in?
**Answer:** In gold colour — history remembers the ploughman-and-shooter forever.

**6. (Sub lesson 30)** How old was the son when the fire happened?
**Answer:** Ten months — a baby asleep on the bed.

**7. (Sub lesson 31)** What did the proclamation punish?
**Answer:** Beating, improperly loading or under-feeding a horse, mule or donkey.

**8. (Sub lesson 32)** What began the area's destruction, in his teaching?
**Answer:** Our own felling of our forests and ploughing even sloping places.

**9. (Sub lesson 33)** How have Mimi's school results changed?
**Answer:** She was first in first grade; now, overworked and underfed, she will not pass.

**10. (Sub lesson 34)** What distinction does his mother teach him to make?
**Answer:** Each matter's benefit and harm, truth and falsehood — without tormenting his mind.

**11. (Sub lesson 35)** How do the elders answer the claim?
**Answer:** Since he confirms five thousand lost, the found four thousand must be another's; his five will come when a finder of five thousand appears.

**12. (Sub lesson 36)** What did Nuru argue about cats?
**Answer:** Cats raised not to steal are lovable house animals that catch rats.
