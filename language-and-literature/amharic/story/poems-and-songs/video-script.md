# Video script — Poems and Songs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is the full script the placements name for this lesson: the
class performing ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ exactly as the guide scripts it —
songs prepared, sung together, turns given to join the melody,
then sung loudly following the tune.

---

Stand by the line. This is our road.

Sing with me. **ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ** — children, be careful!

Going to school — keep to the left side.
Look — **ግራ ቀኝ ግራ**. Left. Right. Left.
Again — ግራ ቀኝ ግራ. Left. Right. Left.

Green light — the cars' turn. Wait!
Red light — my turn. The cars stop.
Now I cross. ግራ ቀኝ ግራ.

Sing it again, loudly, all together —
and your feet stay on the safe side.
