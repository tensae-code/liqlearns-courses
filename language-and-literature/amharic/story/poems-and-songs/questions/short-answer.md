# Poems and Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the poem or song.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two roles does the flower poem's group have?
**Answer:** One leader (መሪ) and three receivers, taking the verse in turns.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which side of the road does the song say to keep, going to school?
**Answer:** The left side.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three handles of the poem ሮዛ.
**Answer:** Rosa, the river, and the crocodile (ዐዞ).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the wedding songs, what is the bride praised as being like?
**Answer:** Like the sun.
**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the six kinds of oral poem and one occasion for each.
**Answer:** የሠርግ (a wedding), የእርሻ (field work), የሙሾ (mourning), የውጊያ (battle), የፍቅር (love), የአጨዳ (harvesting).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the poem ዘር teach a farmer?
**Answer:** Sow in Ginbot and Sene, or perish — sowing beats borrowing grain.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Tell ሽለላ and ፉከራ apart by their targets.
**Answer:** ሽለላ warns the evil-hearted; ፉከራ mocks the coward.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Why are a wedding poem's refrains sung twice?
**Answer:** So the company can join the second time — the doubling is the invitation to sing together.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Define ምጣኔ as the books' margin teaches it.
**Answer:** ምጣኔ, metre, is the time a ሐረግ takes to read; lines of equal time give a regular melody.
