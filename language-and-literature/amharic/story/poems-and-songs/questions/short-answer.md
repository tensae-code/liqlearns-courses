# Poems and Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the poem or song.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two roles does the flower poem's group have?
**Answer:** One leader (መሪ) and three receivers, taking the verse in turns.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which side of the road does the song say to keep, going to school?
**Answer:** The left side.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three handles of the poem ሮዛ.
**Answer:** Rosa, the river, and the crocodile (ዐዞ).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the wedding songs, what is the bride praised as being like?
**Answer:** Like the sun.
**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the six kinds of oral poem and one occasion for each.
**Answer:** የሠርግ (a wedding), የእርሻ (field work), የሙሾ (mourning), የውጊያ (battle), የፍቅር (love), የአጨዳ (harvesting).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the poem ዘር teach a farmer?
**Answer:** Sow in Ginbot and Sene, or perish — sowing beats borrowing grain.

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Tell ሽለላ and ፉከራ apart by their targets.
**Answer:** ሽለላ warns the evil-hearted; ፉከራ mocks the coward.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Why are a wedding poem's refrains sung twice?
**Answer:** So the company can join the second time — the doubling is the invitation to sing together.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Define ምጣኔ as the books' margin teaches it.
**Answer:** ምጣኔ, metre, is the time a ሐረግ takes to read; lines of equal time give a regular melody.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** What does the Poetry chapter say is NOT presented in a poem?
**Answer:** Explained, detailed matter — a poem compresses instead: a wide idea with a deep subject, in few words.

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** What two works does the reading of የግጥም ጥግ set?
**Answer:** Explain what was grasped from the poem; and analyse the poems presented — their themes (ጭብጥ).

**12. (Sub lesson 11)** Name the two meanings a poem's words are worked in, and say each in a phrase.
**Answer:** The denotative meaning — what the word says outright — and the connotative meaning — what it suggests, layered on by context.

**13. (Sub lesson 12)** In what three ways do poetry's words differ from prose words, by the chapter's teaching?
**Answer:** In form, in meaning, and in use.

**14. (Sub lesson 17)** What was the Abay before its change, in the poem?
**Answer:** A stream of weeping carrying black soil and tears, making the country grieve.

**15. (Sub lesson 18)** Name two topics the book offers for your poem.
**Answer:** Any two of: our cities, our lakes, our peoples, our school, our friends, our parents.

**16. (Sub lesson 19)** Give the refrain as the placements quote it.
**Answer:** 'ዓባይ ዓባይ, ዓባይ ወንዛወንዙ, ብዙ ነው መዘዙ' — Abay, river of rivers, its tribute is much.

**17. (Sub lesson 20)** What is the writing follow-on task?
**Answer:** Write down another Abay song's lyrics and correct them into proper poem form.

**18. (Sub lesson 21)** What did neighbours do when a house burned in the old era?
**Answer:** They rebuilt it by rallying together (and pooled money for a dead ox).

**19. (Sub lesson 22)** What is a ደቦ?
**Answer:** A communal work party — neighbours working one person's field together.

**20. (Sub lesson 23)** What two writing tasks does p.26 set?
**Answer:** Summarise given poems in 2–3 sentences each; and collect oral poems from people in your area, in writing.

**21. (Sub lesson 24)** What does አደብ ገዛ call the countryman to do?
**Answer:** Take heed — discipline himself — and not fell the forest, seeing its benefits.

**22. (Sub lesson 25)** Name two warnings from the poem's close.
**Answer:** Any two of: insulting the great, glaring at the neighbour, spending time in bad places, nagging parents, wasting time in jest.
