# Poems and Songs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the poem or song.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two roles does the flower poem's group have?
**Answer:** One leader (መሪ) and three receivers, taking the verse in turns.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which side of the road does the song say to keep, going to school?
**Answer:** The left side.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three handles of the poem ሮዛ.
**Answer:** Rosa, the river, and the crocodile (ዐዞ).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the wedding songs, what is the bride praised as being like?
**Answer:** Like the sun.
