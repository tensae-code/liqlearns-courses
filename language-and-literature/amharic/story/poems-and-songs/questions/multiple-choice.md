# Poems and Songs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is አበባ አየሁሽ performed, in the guide's way?
a) By one child alone, silently  b) By a group — one leader and three receivers, in turns  c) Only by the teacher
**Answer: b)** — መሪ and receivers, the verse taken in turns.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the refrain of ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ?
a) ቀኝ ግራ ቀኝ  b) ግራ ቀኝ ግራ — left, right, left  c) ቀጥ ቀጥ ቀጥ
**Answer: b)** — look left, right, left.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At the traffic light, in the song, whose turn is green?
a) The pedestrians'  b) The vehicles'  c) Nobody's
**Answer: b)** — green is the cars' turn; red is mine.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which set rhymes?
a) መናጩ ዋናጩ ቂናጩ  b) መጽሐፍ ወንበር ሰሌዳ  c) ውኃ እሳት ዝናብ
**Answer: a)** — the book's printed rhyme set, chanted like music.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** How many days has Pagumen, from ክረምትና በጋ?
a) Thirty  b) Five — six in the fourth year  c) Thirteen
**Answer: b)** — the short month at the year's end.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** What does መጽሐፌ መካሪዬ praise?
a) The market  b) The book — "my book, my counsellor"  c) The river
**Answer: b)** — always new, never tires, a market of knowledge.
**7. (Sub lesson 5)** Why are oral poems called "oral"?
a) They are very old  b) They are carried mouth to mouth, on the tongue  c) They are short
**Answer: b)** — an oral poem lives on the tongue before it lives on a page; its metre is ምጣኔ and it is heritage, ቅርስ.

**8. (Sub lesson 5)** Which kind of oral poem is sung for the dead?
a) የሠርግ  b) የፍቅር  c) የሙሾ
**Answer: c)** — የሙሾ, mourning poems, composed differently for a woman, a man, a hero or a farmer.

**9. (Sub lesson 6)** Which of these is a task the work songs sing over?
a) Grinding  b) Sleeping  c) Reading letters
**Answer: a)** — work songs carry building, fencing, ploughing, weeding, guarding, harvesting, threshing, grinding and spinning.

**10. (Sub lesson 6)** In the ደቦ reading, whom do the work-songs mock?
a) The diligent farmer  b) The lazy one  c) The sickle
**Answer: b)** — they praise the diligent farmer (and even the sickle), and mock the lazy one.

**11. (Sub lesson 7)** What is ቀረርቶ?
a) A lullaby  b) The heroism-boast that enrages and provokes the enemy  c) A mourning poem
**Answer: b)** — the boast of the hero's deeds, sung to challenge.

**12. (Sub lesson 7)** Which boast poem mocks the coward?
a) ፉከራ  b) ሽለላ  c) ቀረርቶ
**Answer: a)** — ፉከራ mocks the coward; ሽለላ warns the evil-hearted.

**13. (Sub lesson 8)** Which of these is NOT one of a wedding song's three works?
a) Dignify the ceremony  b) Praise the bride, groom and families  c) Count the wedding gifts for tax
**Answer: c)** — the songs dignify, praise, and bring out the dancing.

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** What rises above the kebero, masinqo and clapping at a wedding?
a) ዕልልታ — the women's ululation  b) A trumpet  c) Bells
**Answer: a)** — ዕልልታ, the ululation, crowns the wedding music.

**15. (Sub lesson 9)** What does the proverb የወጋ ቢረሳ የተወጋ አይረሳ teach?
a) The striker may forget; the struck never forgets  b) Strike first  c) Forget every blow
**Answer: a)** — it stands in the heard story of the humble rich man and the lazy herdsman.

**16. (Sub lesson 9)** In the parts of a poem, what is the ቤት?
a) The first word  b) The rhyme — the final letter at a verse's end  c) The title
**Answer: b)** — ሐረግ is read in one breath; ስንኝ takes a full pause; አርኬ is where one thought ends; ቤት is the rhyme.
