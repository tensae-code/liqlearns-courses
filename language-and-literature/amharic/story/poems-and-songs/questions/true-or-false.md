# Poems and Songs — true or false

Say if the sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the flower poem's performance, the group has one leader and three receivers.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The song says children should cross without looking, to be quick.
**Answer: False** — look ግራ ቀኝ ግራ, and cross when the vehicles stop.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The poem ሮዛ is about Rosa, a river and a crocodile.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Hoya Hoye is sung house to house at Buhe, and the singers are rewarded with mulmul bread or gifts.
**Answer: True.**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** ጠንቃቃ teaches that a careful pupil may play in the road.
**Answer: False** — never plays in the road; crosses only at the zebra.
