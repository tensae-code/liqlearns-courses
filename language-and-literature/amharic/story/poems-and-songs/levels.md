# Poems and Songs — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — አበባ አየሁሽ — I Saw a Flower
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: perform the poem in a group of four, leader and receivers, the guide's way.

## Sub lesson 2 — ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ — Children, Be Careful!
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sing the song's teaching and act its refrain — look left, right, left — at a real crossing line.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rhymes and Rosa
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: chant a rhyme set in rhythm, keeping the beat, and retell Rosa's poem by its handles.

## Sub lesson 4 — More poems and songs of the books
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name each poem's subject and sing or chant one named refrain or line exactly as the placements print it.
## Sub lesson 5 — The kinds of oral poem
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: match the six kinds to their occasions without a miss; say why the poems are called oral; and say for whom mourning poems are composed differently.

## Sub lesson 6 — Work songs — the song that carries the work
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name five tasks the work songs sing over; say whom the debo songs praise and mock; and chant ዘር with its months in the rhythm.

## Sub lesson 7 — Qererto, shilela and fuqera — the boast poems
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell the three boast poems apart by their targets — provoke the enemy, warn the evil-hearted, mock the coward — and perform one standing.

## Sub lesson 8 — Wedding songs and love verses
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name a wedding song's three works and its instruments and voices; say why refrains are sung twice; and name a love poem's images.

## Sub lesson 9 — Proverbs in stories, and the parts of a poem
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: complete a half-given proverb from the heard story; and mark a known poem's ሐረግ, ስንኝ, አርኬ and ቤት without help.

## Sub lesson 10 — A poem heard — My Country Is a Warka Tree (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say the chapter's definition of poetry — a wide idea with a deep subject, compressed, no explained detailed matter — and what the warka stands for in the title.

## Sub lesson 11 — Poems read, presented, and opened (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain your grasp of የግጥም ጥግ, name a poem's theme (ጭብጥ), present a poem aloud standing, and split one poem-word into its denotative and connotative meanings.

## Sub lesson 12 — Write a poem of your own (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write the short poem የመማሪያ ክፍላችን and read it to the class; and name one difference each in form, meaning and use between your poem's words and prose words.
