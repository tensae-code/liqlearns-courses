# Poems and Songs

## Do it first

Listen to the flower poem once. On its second hearing, join
in where it repeats. You have just done what this lesson
teaches: a poem is not only read — it is **performed**:
chanted, sung, acted, in a group. In this lesson every poem
and song is taught as a summary and its named refrain — you
learn what it is about, you perform it, and you carry its
refrain by heart.

## Sub lesson 1 — አበባ አየሁሽ (I Saw a Flower)

The poem አበባ አየሁሽ is chanted — and the guide's way of
performing it is part of the lesson: a group performs it
before the class, one leader (መሪ/አውጫ) and three receivers
taking the ጭራፍ (the verse) in turns. The teacher may adapt
the performance to the classroom. Form your group of four,
give the leader's part and the receivers' parts, and perform
it so.

## Sub lesson 2 — ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ (Children, Be Careful!)

This children's song is the listening text of the road-safety
unit — sung together, the class joining the melody (ዘይማ) in
turns, then sung loudly following the tune. Its teaching,
summarised: children who cross asphalt carelessly, who do not
look left and right, are hit by cars — so, *children, be
careful to save yourselves* (ራሳን ለማገን ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ). Going
to school, keep to the left side. Look **ግራ ቀኝ ግራ** —
left, right, left — the refrain, repeated. At the traffic
light: green is the vehicles' turn, red is mine; when the
vehicles stop, I cross.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rhymes and Rosa

**Rhyme sets.** The book chants rhyming words like music,
with rhythm: **መናጩ ዋናጩ ቂናጩ** is its printed set. Chant it
— the sounds rhyme, the beat carries them. The same shelf
holds the short rhyming reading built on the አ and ከ fidel,
an early poem-reading about going into school.

**ሮዛ (Rosa).** A short rhyming poem printed in full in the
book, read aloud: Rosa, the river, and a crocodile (ዐዞ).
Retell it by its three handles — a girl, a river, a crocodile
in it — and read it aloud the poem's way, in rhythm.

## Sub lesson 4 — More poems and songs of the books

**ክረምትና በጋ (Rainy Season and Dry Season).** A poem that
tours the Ethiopian year month by month — Yekatit, Megabit,
Miyazya, Hamle, Nehase, and Pagumen, the short month of five
days (six in the fourth year). Read it fluently; it is also
the calendar carried in verse.

**መጽሐፌ መካሪዬ (My Book, My Counsellor).** A praise poem for
the book itself: my book is always new, never tires, teaches
compassion, knowledge, faith and truth — "my book is a market
of knowledge". Read it in turns, by phrases.

**Wedding songs (የሠርግ ዘፈኖች).** The songs sung at the
bride's house and the groom's house — praising the bride
"like the sun", and the groom — with ululation: an anthology
of songs read as a reading text.

**ሆያ ሆዬ (Hoya Hoye).** The Buhe festival chant children sing
house to house, danced, rewarded with mulmul bread or gifts,
and ending with blessing verses — its printed blessing runs
"ዓመት ዓውደዓመት ድገምና…".

**ጠንቃቃ (Careful).** The road-safety song of the traffic
chapter: the careful pupil keeps the left line, never plays
in the road, and crosses only at the zebra. Its first two
lines are the book's copying task — sing it first, then copy.

**On the same shelf.** ተመጣጣኝ ምግብ, the balanced-diet poem
(vegetables, fruit, beans and meat, a little fat);
የሰው ሀገሩ ምግባሩ — "a person's country is their character";
አለኝታው አርሶአደር, the praise-poem of the generous farmer;
and ገበያና እናት — "what the market gives, a mother does not
give", the old saying sung as a poem.

## Say it to your companion

Perform your group's piece — poem, song or chant — to another
group. They listen and name its subject: the flower, the road,
the year, the book. Then their group performs, and yours
listens and names. A performance you can name is a performance
you understood.

## Do It Yourself

1. Teach the refrain ግራ ቀኝ ግራ to someone at home, with
   the head movements.
2. In a group of four, perform አበባ አየሁሽ once more — with a
   different leader.
3. Chant መናጩ ዋናጩ ቂናጩ three times: slow, normal, fast —
   never losing the beat.
4. Name the months Pagumen has, and how many days — from
   ክረምትና በጋ.
5. Say, in one sentence, what መጽሐፌ መካሪዬ praises — and
   whether you agree.
