# Lesson plan — Poems and Songs

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Story · **Lesson:** Poems and Songs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a poem or song can be performed — chanted, sung and acted in a group — and its subject and refrain carried by heart.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on First Stories and Reading Stories: the learner already hears, reads and retells; here the text is verse — performed the books' ways (group performance, chorus singing, chant with rhythm) — taught as summaries and named refrains only, never transcribed whole.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **አበባ አየሁሽ — I Saw a Flower** — the flower poem, performed by a group: one leader (መሪ) and three receivers take the verse in turns before the class.
2. **ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ — Children, Be Careful!** — the road-safety song: keep left, look ግራ ቀኝ ግራ, and know whose turn the traffic light says it is.
3. **Rhymes and Rosa** — the rhyme sets chanted like music (መናጩ ዋናጩ ቂናጩ), the rhyming reading, and the poem ሮዛ — Rosa, the river and the crocodile.
4. **More poems and songs of the books** — ክረምትና በጋ's tour of the year; መጽሐፌ መካሪዬ, my book my counsellor; the wedding songs; Hoya Hoye at Buhe; the careful pupil's song ጠንቃቃ; and the books' praise and food poems.
5. **The kinds of oral poem** — what makes a poem "oral"; its metre ምጣኔ and heritage ቅርስ; the six kinds matched to their occasions; and mourning poems composed for a woman, a man, a hero, a farmer.
6. **Work songs — the song that carries the work** — የሥራ ዘፈኖች for debo and every heavy task; the praise-song for the sickle; the poem ዘር; ploughing and crop-guarding chants; and the guide's harvest work-poem.
7. **Qererto, shilela and fuqera — the boast poems** — ቀረርቶ, the heroism-boast that provokes the enemy; ሽለላ warns the evil-hearted; ፉከራ mocks the coward; all performed standing.
8. **Wedding songs and love verses** — የሠርግ ዘፈኖች dignify, praise and bring out the dancing, to kebero, masinqo, clapping and ዕልልታ; refrains sung twice; love poems known by their images.
9. **Proverbs in stories, and the parts of a poem** — a heard story carrying proverbs, completed from halves; and the poem's parts named: ሐረግ, ስንኝ, አርኬ, ቤት — with ምጣኔ, the time a ሐረግ takes to read.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is
written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is
the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Listening Stories — a song is first
  heard, like a story. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Perform አበባ አየሁሽ in a group of four — one leader and three receivers, taking the verse in turns.
2. Sing ልጆች ተጠንቅቁ's teaching: keep to the left side, look ግራ ቀኝ ግራ, cross when the vehicles stop.
3. Say whose turn it is at the traffic light, from the song: green is the cars' turn; red is mine.
4. Chant the rhyme set መናጩ ዋናጩ ቂናጩ in rhythm.
5. Retell the poem ሮዛ by its handles — Rosa, the river, the crocodile.
6. Name what each shelf poem praises or teaches: the year (ክረምትና በጋ), the book (መጽሐፌ መካሪዬ), care on the road (ጠንቃቃ).
7. Match the six oral-poem kinds to their occasions — wedding, farming, mourning, battle, love, harvesting.
8. Name five tasks the work songs sing over, and say whom the debo songs praise and whom they mock.
9. Tell ቀረርቶ, ሽለላ and ፉከራ apart by their targets.
10. Name the three works of a wedding song, and its instruments and voices.
11. Complete a half-given proverb from the heard story.
12. Mark a poem's parts: breathe its ሐረግ, pause at its ስንኝ, show its አርኬ, find its ቤት.

## What you need
Room to stand in a group of four. A crossing line marked on the ground (chalk or a rope) for the song's acting.

## If the learner is stuck
- The group performance tangles → the guide's shape: one
  leader (መሪ) says the verse, three receivers answer; turns
  are given, not grabbed. Re-set the four roles and go again.
- A learner will not sing → chanting in rhythm counts; the
  books chant rhymes like music before anyone must sing.
- Refrain forgotten → it is three words: ግራ ቀኝ ግራ. Clap
  them: left (clap), right (clap), left (clap).
