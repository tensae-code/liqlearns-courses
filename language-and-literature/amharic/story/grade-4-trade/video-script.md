# Video script — Grade 4 Honest Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ንግድ** — trade.

The trade chapter: success through effort and honest scales, the licence that makes trade legal, the fraud that ruins a family, and a graduate who grows rich on chickens — plus the book's own list of the books behind it.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: ገበያ, ሚዛን, ትርፍ, ፈቃድ, ደንበኛ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
