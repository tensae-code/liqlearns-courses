# Grade 4 Honest Trade — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two sentences about the caravan trader's homecomings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write three sentences: the couple's goods, their scales, their blessing-measure.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the legal/illegal trade web as four sentences.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write three sentences on the chicken trader's way: licence, rounds, incubator.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write your ነጋዴ ብሆን essay's first paragraph.
