# Grade 4 Honest Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the caravan-trader grandfather bring home?
a) Nothing  b) Goods from Addis Ababa, after long absences  c) Only stories
**Answer: b) Goods from Addis Ababa, after long absences** — Goods from the capital — and the listening mourns the love and respect of those days.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the honest couple known for at the scale?
a) Cheating it  b) Adding a blessing-measure — መመረቅ — never cheating  c) Closing early
**Answer: b) Adding a blessing-measure — መመረቅ — never cheating** — Fresh goods, honest scales, extra measure, free advice — their profit tops the others'.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** አብሽ belongs on which branch?
a) Vegetables  b) Spices  c) Neither
**Answer: b) Spices** — አብሽ is a spice; ካሮት and ድንች are vegetables.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Where does the money for schools, clinics and roads come from?
a) Licences only  b) Taxes — the listening's answer  c) Foreign gifts only
**Answer: b) Taxes — the listening's answer** — Legal trade pays taxes; taxes build schools, clinics and roads.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What ruins the wealthy trader?
a) Paying taxes  b) Air trade — he hands over everything without a receipt  c) His shop burns
**Answer: b) Air trade — he hands over everything without a receipt** — አየርባየር fraud: stock sold, house mortgaged, friend vanished like the smell of water.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** የጧት ጤዛ — morning dew — means…?
a) Lasting wealth  b) Wealth that vanishes  c) A tax
**Answer: b) Wealth that vanishes** — Morning dew vanishes by sunrise — so does fraud-gotten wealth.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the chicken trader do first?
a) Waits for a government job  b) Takes out a trade licence  c) Borrows a house
**Answer: b) Takes out a trade licence** — Licence first, in Mekane Iyesus town — then chicks from Bahir Dar.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** After seven rounds, what has he bought?
a) A car  b) An incubator — to hatch his own  c) A second shop
**Answer: b) An incubator — to hatch his own** — Seven profitable rounds, then his own incubator.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** ዕቁብ is…?
a) A tax  b) A savings circle  c) A licence
**Answer: b) A savings circle** — The country girl joins an ዕቁብ to save toward her plot and house.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** ዋቢዎች are…?
a) The book's chapters  b) The book's own references — the books behind its lessons  c) Its pictures
**Answer: b) The book's own references — the books behind its lessons** — Baye Yimam, Kebede Mikael and the rest — a book naming its sources.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** Someone asks you for money to 'trade together'. The chapter teaches…?
a) Hand it over fast  b) Ask questions, want receipts — remember the ruined trader  c) Run away
**Answer: b) Ask questions, want receipts — remember the ruined trader** — Receipts, patience and questions first: the fraud story is the evidence.

**12. (Sub lesson 1)** The caravan trader returned home…?
a) Every week  b) Only after long absences, bringing goods  c) Never
**Answer: b) Only after long absences, bringing goods** — Long absences, goods from Addis Ababa, and a house full of respect.
