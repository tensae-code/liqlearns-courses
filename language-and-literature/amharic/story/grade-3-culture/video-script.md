# Video script — Grade 3 Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ባህል** — culture.

Culture is what a people hands down: games in a circle, riddles at dusk, the Ashendye grass at dancing girls' waists, and the eskista's shoulders telling a story no words can.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: አሸንድዬ — እስክስታ — እንቆቅልሽ — ቀርቀሃ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
