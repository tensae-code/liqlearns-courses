# Grade 3 Culture — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two things bamboo is made into, from what you know at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write Ashendye in order: fields, cut, tie, dance, hospitality.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Describe a game from your own street completely — who, when, where, how it ends.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the Ashendye steps as a numbered list in the right order.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Build three doer-nouns of your own and say what work each doer does.
