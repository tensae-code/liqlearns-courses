# Grade 3 Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forgetting culture is forgetting what?
a) School  b) Identity  c) Songs only
**Answer: b) Identity** — Forgetting culture is forgetting identity — the show's own sentence.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bamboo is not used when?
a) On market day  b) On the day it is cut  c) In the rains
**Answer: b) On the day it is cut** — Not the day it is cut — the listening explains why.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** የቀርቀሃ ሥራ is work with what?
a) Iron  b) Bamboo  c) Clay
**Answer: b) Bamboo** — ቀርቀሃ is bamboo.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** መሐረቤን ያያችሁ asks what?
a) Have you seen my book?  b) Have you seen my handkerchief?  c) Have you seen my home?
**Answer: b) Have you seen my handkerchief?** — The circle game calls: have you seen my handkerchief?

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** At Ashendye, girls tie what round their waists?
a) A belt  b) አሸንዳ/እንግጫ grass  c) Flowers
**Answer: b) አሸንዳ/እንግጫ grass** — Cut grass tied at the waist, dancing house to house.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Eniye Takele now does what in Gondar?
a) Runs a cultural evening house, training young dancers  b) Owns a shop  c) Teaches school
**Answer: a) Runs a cultural evening house, training young dancers** — She trains young dancers at her cultural evening house.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** A game description is complete when it says what?
a) Only its name  b) Who plays, when, where, how it ends  c) Only who wins
**Answer: b) Who plays, when, where, how it ends** — Who, when, where, and how it ends — the four parts.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** What do you do after writing your favourite game?
a) Forget it  b) Demonstrate how it is played  c) Copy it
**Answer: b) Demonstrate how it is played** — Write it, then demonstrate it.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Traditional house building writing begins by doing what?
a) Gathering information  b) Buying materials  c) Drawing
**Answer: a) Gathering information** — Gather information first, then write the paragraph.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** እንካስላንትያ is played how?
a) Alone  b) In small groups, taking turns  c) Only by teachers
**Answer: b) In small groups, taking turns** — Small groups, turns for everyone.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** How many እንካስላንትያ do you collect from your parents?
a) Two  b) Five  c) Ten
**Answer: b) Five** — Five, written down, then competed in pairs.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** The Ashendye sequencing puts what in order?
a) Songs  b) The celebration's steps  c) Names
**Answer: b) The celebration's steps** — The steps of the celebration, in their right order.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** ሸማ ሠሪ is a what?
a) Goldsmith  b) Weaver  c) Embroiderer
**Answer: b) Weaver** — ሸማ ሠሪ weaves; ወርቅ ሠሪ works gold, ጥልፍ ሠሪ embroiders.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** The BINGO tournament uses what grid?
a) Numbers  b) A mixed word grid  c) Pictures
**Answer: b) A mixed word grid** — A mixed grid of the chapter's vocabulary.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** In the eskista reading you pick out which two kinds?
a) Nouns and verbs  b) Pronouns and adjectives  c) Adverbs and conjunctions
**Answer: b) Pronouns and adjectives** — Pronouns (መስተዋድድ) and adjectives, inside the reading.
