# Grade 3 Transport — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write your accidents web in words: two causes, two solutions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write Hana's two lists: transport on land, transport on water.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the four resolutions of Jemila and Tilaye as a numbered list.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Draw the mule/car web in words: two branches for the mule, two for the car.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write your bicycle precautions paragraph in at least four sentences.
