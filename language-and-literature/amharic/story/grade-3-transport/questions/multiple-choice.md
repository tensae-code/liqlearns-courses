# Grade 3 Transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the accidents web, what is one solution?
a) More speed  b) Train and test drivers  c) No licences
**Answer: b) Train and test drivers** — Training and testing drivers answers untrained drivers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In ባቡር, the question to ask is what?
a) How fast is the train?  b) Who does 'us' mean?  c) Who built it?
**Answer: b) Who does 'us' mean?** — 'The train is precious to us' — who is 'us'?

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** ሽርሽር በጣና ሐይቅ is an excursion where?
a) On Lake Tana  b) In Addis Ababa  c) On a mountain
**Answer: a) On Lake Tana** — The excursion is on Lake Tana.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which animal is NOT in Hana's land list?
a) Camel  b) Mule  c) Elephant
**Answer: c) Elephant** — Land pack animals: mule, donkey, horse, camel.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** ታንኳ is what?
a) A reed boat  b) A train  c) A saddle
**Answer: a) A reed boat** — The ታንኳ is the reed boat used on water.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** How long is the mother's flight to Addis Ababa?
a) One day  b) One hour  c) Ten hours
**Answer: b) One hour** — One hour — then she phones home.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the mule boast?
a) That she is fastest  b) That the car's wealth came from her ancient service  c) That roads are hers
**Answer: b) That the car's wealth came from her ancient service** — The mule claims the car's wealth grew from her service.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** ቃሬዛ means what?
a) Ambulance  b) Stretcher  c) Mud
**Answer: b) Stretcher** — A ቃሬዛ is the stretcher they carry her on.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** What do Jemila and Tilaye resolve to do?
a) Avoid roads  b) Learn the road rules and teach others  c) Buy a car
**Answer: b) Learn the road rules and teach others** — Learn the rules, teach family and neighbours, ask for crossings, serve as traffic police.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** ጥንታዊ means what?
a) Modern  b) Traditional  c) Expensive
**Answer: b) Traditional** — ጥንታዊ is traditional; ዘመናዊ is modern.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** ጎማ is which car word?
a) Motor  b) Tyre  c) Smoke
**Answer: b) Tyre** — ጎማ is the tyre; ሞተር the motor, ጪስ the smoke.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** The air-travel web collects what?
a) Farm words  b) Words and phrases belonging to air travel  c) Market words
**Answer: b) Words and phrases belonging to air travel** — It gathers the vocabulary of flying.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** ጀሚላ → እነጀሚላ makes the plural how?
a) With -ኦች  b) With እነ- in front  c) With -ኣት
**Answer: b) With እነ- in front** — እነ- in front of the name makes this plural.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** The table work pulls out which two word-kinds?
a) Nouns and verbs  b) Adjectives and adverbs  c) Names only
**Answer: a) Nouns and verbs** — Noun and verb, each into its column.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** Pedestrian precautions protect whom?
a) Drivers  b) Walkers — pedestrians  c) Cyclists only
**Answer: b) Walkers — pedestrians** — Pedestrian precautions are the walker's safety.
