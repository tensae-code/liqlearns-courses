# Grade 4 Women and Men Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the sorting task, ማርገዝ — carrying a child — belongs to…?
a) Nature — only a woman's body can do it  b) Men only  c) Nobody
**Answer: a) Nature — only a woman's body can do it** — Some tasks are nature's; most house and field tasks are sorted as shared.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The division of work between men and women comes from…?
a) Nature only  b) Custom and culture — true in the book's check  c) The law only
**Answer: b) Custom and culture — true in the book's check** — True: custom and culture, not nature — so people can re-make it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do Dr Aweke and Dr Birke ask about fathers?
a) Why don't fathers help with carrying, washing and feeding?  b) Why do fathers work?  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Why don't fathers help with carrying, washing and feeding?** — Nature gives mothers pregnancy and breastfeeding — not all the carrying and washing.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In ሴቶች ባደባባይ, what happens to the old belittling sayings?
a) They are praised  b) They are quoted and rejected as unacceptable in this age  c) They are hidden
**Answer: b) They are quoted and rejected as unacceptable in this age** — Quoted, then rejected; new sayings replace them.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the little son ask in እኔና ልጆቼ?
a) Can I play?  b) Then why does mother work alone in our house?  c) What is for lunch?
**Answer: b) Then why does mother work alone in our house?** — His question reproaches his father, who slips away ashamed.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What does father do this week in እማዬና አባዬ?
a) Rests  b) Makes breakfast, dresses the children, cooks, washes clothes  c) Travels
**Answer: b) Makes breakfast, dresses the children, cooks, washes clothes** — Father carries the house for a week — and it stands, carried by two.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is an example of women's equality?
a) ልማዳዊ አባባሎች  b) ጉልህ ተሳትፎ — full participation  c) በወንድ ያልቅ
**Answer: b) ጉልህ ተሳትፎ — full participation** — Full participation is an example; customary belittling sayings are not.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** How was Selamnesh's mother married?
a) To a man she chose at school  b) To a man she had never seen — their parents arranged it  c) She was not married
**Answer: b) To a man she had never seen — their parents arranged it** — Married to a stranger by her parents' love-match — a harmful custom examined.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** What did the woman mayor do?
a) Raised taxes only  b) Cut unemployment, gave equal service, kept the peace — and won first place  c) Closed the schools
**Answer: b) Cut unemployment, gave equal service, kept the peace — and won first place** — Wise and tireless, she leads well and wins first place among mayors.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** ዘመናት splits into…?
a) ዘመን - ኣት  b) ዘ - መናት  c) ዘመና - ት
**Answer: a) ዘመን - ኣት** — Stem ዘመን plus the tail -ኣት.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** True or false: women can be nurses but not doctors.
a) True  b) False  c) Only in towns
**Answer: b) False** — False — there is no field of work women do not enter.

**12. (Sub lesson 5)** What did the doctor order for the overworked mother?
a) More work  b) A week's rest — heart strain from overwork  c) Medicine only
**Answer: b) A week's rest — heart strain from overwork** — Heart strain from overwork; a week's rest — the family finally sees her burden.

**13. (Sub lesson 6)** Unit 4 is —
a) Gender — ስርዓተ ያታ  b) Trade — ንግድ  c) Sport
**Answer: a) Gender — ስርዓተ ያታ** — ስርዓተ ያታ.

**14. (Sub lesson 6)** Its listening text concerns —
a) the division of labour  b) river travel  c) weaving
**Answer: a) the division of labour** — Division of labour.

**15. (Sub lesson 6)** The unit runs —
a) 6 periods  b) 18 periods  c) 40 periods
**Answer: b) 18 periods** — 18 periods.
