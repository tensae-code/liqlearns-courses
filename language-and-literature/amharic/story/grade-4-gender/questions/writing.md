# Grade 4 Women and Men Together — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the sorting as three sentences: one nature task, one shared task, one task you will share at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write one old saying replaced: write the new saying from the chapter in your own words.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write three sentences: what father did in እማዬና አባዬ, and one thing you will do at home.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the mayor's three-sentence summary: who she is, what she did, what she won.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write your two paragraphs' first lines for የገጠር እናቶች ድካም — morning and night.
