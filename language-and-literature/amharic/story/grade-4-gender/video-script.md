# Video script — Grade 4 Women and Men Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ሥርዓተፆታ** — gender — women and men together.

The gender chapter: equal participation, equal share — mothers' work seen clearly, old sayings that belittle women rejected, and fathers and children sharing the work of the house.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: እኩልነት, እናት, ተሳትፎ, ሥራ, ክብር.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
