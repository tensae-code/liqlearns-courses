# Grade 4 Making Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Abagar order, when do the elders rise?
a) At midday  b) Only when peace is concluded  c) When they are tired
**Answer: b) Only when peace is concluded** — Once seated for peace, they do not rise until it is concluded.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is punishment in the Abagar order?
a) Imprisonment  b) Not imprisonment — the order itself, submitted to by both sides  c) A fine only
**Answer: b) Not imprisonment — the order itself, submitted to by both sides** — Offender and victim alike submit to the order; prison is not its way.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How long did the compound feud last?
a) Four months  b) Four years  c) Four days
**Answer: b) Four years** — Four years over yard, parking and the guard's pay — ended by chosen elders.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who did the neutral residents choose as elders?
a) The police  b) Three women and three men  c) The guard
**Answer: b) Three women and three men** — Three women and three men hear both sides and name the faults.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ሽምግልና is…?
a) A court building  b) Elder mediation — the traditional peace system  c) A festival
**Answer: b) Elder mediation — the traditional peace system** — Elder mediation: if the sky is torn, an elder sews it.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** ቁርሾ means…?
a) Forgiveness spoken aloud  b) A wrong kept in the heart awaiting its time  c) An agreement
**Answer: b) A wrong kept in the heart awaiting its time** — ቁርሾ is the kept wrong; ይቅርታ is repentance spoken; እርቅ is agreement.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** What did the two teachers agree in ትዝታዬ?
a) To move house  b) He would drink coffee, she would eat goat — talked through  c) To stop speaking
**Answer: b) He would drink coffee, she would eat goat — talked through** — Talking it through settled coffee and goat — sixty birr, both glad.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** How long does Ato Mgbaru take to make peace?
a) A week  b) One hour  c) A year
**Answer: b) One hour** — He hears, names the fault, divides equally — peace in one hour.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** In ገንዘብ ለሕጻናት, one side argues children…?
a) Are rich  b) Cannot yet tell bad from good  c) Need no food
**Answer: b) Cannot yet tell bad from good** — The debate is whether children can yet judge — you take a side, with reasons.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** The ሽምግልና essay's paragraphs grow from…?
a) A story  b) The web — what it is, its benefit, its process, the elders  c) A poem
**Answer: b) The web — what it is, its benefit, its process, the elders** — Web branches become essay paragraphs.
