Every listening part in this lesson therefore teaches exactly the title, the questions and the plot the book's own questions and placement summary give, and invents no further plot. Sensitive content is filed as the book files it (health safety in Chapter 1, the cancelled early marriage in Chapter 4, disability inclusion in Chapters 4, 6 and 8). No hardness numbers are written anywhere in this lesson — the book gives none.
