# Grade 3 Using Time — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write whose mistake you think was biggest in አለማወቅ, and why, in two sentences.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write Haset's day as a list in order: clothes, homework, help, play.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write two sentences: what Alemnesh's teacher might have said to her parents.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write one saying about time that you collected at home, and what it means.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Draw your daily timetable for tomorrow, with study, help, play and rest all in it.
