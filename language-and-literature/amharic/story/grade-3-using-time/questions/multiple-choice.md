# Grade 3 Using Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In አለማወቅ, whose mistake do you judge?
a) Only the teacher's  b) The teacher's, the student's or the friends' — whose was biggest  c) Nobody's
**Answer: b) The teacher's, the student's or the friends' — whose was biggest** — All three are weighed: teacher, student, friends.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Human desire is compared to what, in የሰው ፍላጎት?
a) A river  b) The deep ocean  c) A mountain
**Answer: b) The deep ocean** — Desire is deep as the ocean.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Leading time by plan is compared to what?
a) Gems and gold  b) Rain  c) A fast horse
**Answer: a) Gems and gold** — A planned use of time is likened to gems and gold.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the main thing, for the student on crutches?
a) Walking faster  b) Using time properly  c) Having crutches
**Answer: b) Using time properly** — Using time properly is the main thing; disability does not stop excellence.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Haset does everything by what?
a) Luck  b) Plan — ዕቅድ  c) Her mother's orders only
**Answer: b) Plan — ዕቅድ** — Haset works by plan; Ikram regrets having none.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the ants teach?
a) To sleep in winter  b) To store food in harvest season, with no overseer  c) To work only when watched
**Answer: b) To store food in harvest season, with no overseer** — The ants store in season, unprompted.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** When does 'time is gold' mean something, in the dialogue?
a) When time is divided among works and used  b) When it is only said  c) When you are rich
**Answer: a) When time is divided among works and used** — Divided among works and used — that is the dialogue's agreement.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Who persuades Alemnesh's parents?
a) Her friend  b) Her teacher  c) A neighbour
**Answer: b) Her teacher** — Her teacher persuades the parents.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** What happens to the marriage?
a) It happens early  b) It is cancelled, and she continues learning  c) It is delayed a week
**Answer: b) It is cancelled, and she continues learning** — The marriage is cancelled and Alemnesh stays in school.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** ሰዓት ማክበር means what?
a) Being late  b) Keeping time  c) Wasting time
**Answer: b) Keeping time** — ሰዓት ማክበር is keeping time; ማርፈድ is being late.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** ገመተ → ሸመተ changes what?
a) The last letter  b) The first letter  c) Nothing
**Answer: b) The first letter** — Changing the first letter builds the new word.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** 'Time is gold' is what kind of saying?
a) A literal fact  b) A figurative, proverb-like saying — ምሣሌያዊ አባባል  c) A command
**Answer: b) A figurative, proverb-like saying — ምሣሌያዊ አባባል** — It is figurative: time is precious like gold.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** What do friends do with your timetable?
a) Copy it  b) Evaluate it  c) Hide it
**Answer: b) Evaluate it** — Friends evaluate the timetable you draw up.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** Your ክረምት paragraph is about what?
a) Last year's rains  b) How you will spend the coming rainy season  c) A story about rain
**Answer: b) How you will spend the coming rainy season** — It plans the coming rainy season.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** -ኣም and -ነት in the review are what?
a) Prefixes  b) Suffixes words can take  c) Punctuation
**Answer: b) Suffixes words can take** — They are suffixes, matched to the words that take them.
