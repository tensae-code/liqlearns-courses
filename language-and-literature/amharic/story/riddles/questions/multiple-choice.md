# Riddles — እንቆቅልሽ — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the riddle game as taught, how many sit in a group?
a) Two  b) Four  c) The whole class in one group
**Answer: b) Four** — taking turns asking and answering.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the game's five printed answers?
a) ወፍጮ — a mill  b) መኪና — a car  c) ጀልባ — a boat
**Answer: a) ወፍጮ** — with ባህር, እሳት, ጀበናና ስኒ and ሰዓት.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "A red snake lying in a cave" — the answer is:
a) እሳት, fire  b) ምላስ, the tongue  c) ባህር, the sea
**Answer: b) ምላስ** — red, and it lies in the mouth's cave.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The orange riddle-passage describes a fruit with how many segments?
a) Two or three  b) Eight to ten  c) Fifty
**Answer: b)** — 8–10 segments, with seeds.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the house riddle's answer?
a) ትምህርትቤት, a school  b) ቤት, a house  c) ገበያ, a market
**Answer: b) ቤት** — rooms to sleep, eat and receive guests.
