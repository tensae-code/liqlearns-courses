# Riddles — እንቆቅልሽ — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the riddle game played — who asks, who guesses?
**Answer:** In groups of four, learners take turns asking riddles and guessing the answers, following the riddle procedure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the book's two sorting piles for riddles?
**Answer:** ተቀማጭ — things that sit; ተንቀጥቃጭ — things that move.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What can a wrong answer cost, in the student book's riddle lesson?
**Answer:** A forfeit.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the lemon riddle-story, who is the speaker?
**Answer:** The lemon (ሎሚ) — it tells why it is honoured.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things the traffic policeman in the Who-Am-I reading does.
**Answer:** Any two of: teaches pedestrians to keep left; fines speeding drivers; fines overloading drivers; stands in frost and heat at the roadside.
