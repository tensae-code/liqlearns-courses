# Hands-on — Riddles — እንቆቅልሽ

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Play the game in fours
Sit in a group of four — the guide's group, mixed — and play
properly: one asks a riddle, the others guess in turn, the
answer is confirmed only when it is guessed or the group gives
up. Keep the turns. Change the asker each round.

## Make riddle cards
On paper cards, draw (do not name) the five printed answers —
sea, fire, coffee pot and cup, clock, mill — one per card.
Shuffle them face down. A partner draws a card and must ask
YOU a riddle for it: clues first, the card flipped only at the
end, like GIF 8331.

## Sit or move?
Take the book's sorting game home: name ten things in your
house and yard, and sort them into ተቀማጭ (things that sit)
and ተንቀጥቃጭ (things that move). Say one riddle for one thing
in each pile.

## Riddle your family
Ask the house riddle at home, one room-clue at a time, exactly
as in Listening Stories. Note who guessed it and after which
clue — bring the count back to your companion.

## If it is hard
Guess with the answer cards face up in front of you (choosing
is guessing, too); hear the riddle twice; take clues one at a
time. The game is played in a group precisely so no one
guesses alone.
