# Video script — Riddles — እንቆቅልሽ

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A game-show script for the riddle game, the way the student
book's riddle lesson is performed: a questioner, guessers, and
the answer held back to the end.

---

**እንቆቅልሽ — ምን አውቅልሽ?**
A riddle for you. Listen to all of it. Do not shout!

I am round. When I am ripe, I am yellow.
Peel me to eat me.
Inside me are parts — eight, nine, ten — with seeds.

Who am I? Guess! …

Yes! I am the orange.

One more. This one sits in your mouth and never sits still…
Guess in the pause. The answer comes last — that is the game.
