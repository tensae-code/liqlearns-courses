# Grade 4 Visiting Our Heritage — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two sentences: one thing you learned about the Goze mosque, one about Ysma the King.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write three sentences about Ayiteyef: its size, its builder, and why it has its name.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the falls web as four sentences — place, phenomena, width and depth, feeling.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write two table rows for Ankober: a king, his reign, his deeds.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write a paragraph of four sentences on the benefits of visiting a historical place near you.
