# Grade 4 Visiting Our Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who lived below the Goze mosque?
a) The Argoba communities  b) Nobody  c) Traders only
**Answer: a) The Argoba communities** — The Argoba communities lived below the mosque the listening describes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Adwa Day is celebrated because of…?
a) A harvest  b) Ethiopia's victory — the pioneer of black people's freedom  c) A king's birthday
**Answer: b) Ethiopia's victory — the pioneer of black people's freedom** — Adwa stands for freedom; the listening names its pioneer.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Captain Silenat fly?
a) Only local routes  b) International routes — and she tells tourists about the region  c) She does not fly
**Answer: b) International routes — and she tells tourists about the region** — A famous woman pilot on international routes who brings tourists to the region.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the hall called አይጠየፍ?
a) It is very big  b) Poor and rich shared equally there — no one was asked or wanting  c) A king slept there
**Answer: b) Poor and rich shared equally there — no one was asked or wanting** — Equality at the feast gives the hall its name.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How wide are the Tis Abay falls?
a) 40 metres  b) About 400 metres  c) 4 kilometres
**Answer: b) About 400 metres** — About 400 m wide, plunging 40–50 m into the gorge, spray like smoke.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the writer see Lake Tana in ጣናን በጨረፍታ?
a) By boat for a week  b) From a Bahir Dar hotel balcony — only half a day  c) From a plane
**Answer: b) From a Bahir Dar hotel balcony — only half a day** — Half a day only: no boat — the lake admired from a balcony.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Who built the Guzara palace?
a) Sahle Selassie  b) Atse Serse Dingil, in the mid-16th century  c) King Mikael
**Answer: b) Atse Serse Dingil, in the mid-16th century** — Stone wall, three gates, ten rooms — built by Serse Dingil near Gondar.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** Ankober was the seat of how many Shewa kings?
a) Two  b) Six  c) Twelve
**Answer: b) Six** — Seat of six Shewa kings, founded 1733 E.C. by Negasi Amha Iyesus.

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Which phrase shows an adjective of size?
a) ነጩ በግ  b) ብልሁ ተማሪ  c) ትልቅ ዳቦ — the big bread
**Answer: c) ትልቅ ዳቦ — the big bread** — ትልቅ (big) describes by size; ነጩ by kind, ብልሁ by quality.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Lake Zengena is ringed by…?
a) Desert  b) Forest  c) Houses
**Answer: b) Forest** — Green-looking, ringed by forest, in the Awi zone.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** መንግሥት -ኣት makes…?
a) መንግሥታት  b) መንግሥትኦች  c) አመንግሥት
**Answer: a) መንግሥታት** — The suffix joins the stem: መንግሥታት.
