# Lesson plan — Reading a Play — The Wedding's Aftermath

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Story · **Lesson:** Reading a Play — The Wedding's Aftermath
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** የሠርጉ መዘዝ (The Wedding's Aftermath): a family crushed by wedding debt learns to live within its means — read act by act, as stage directions, dialogue and curtain.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Elements of a Play — the learner knows the five elements before meeting this play.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Act 1 — I and my debt alone remained** — the salon, the calculator and the creditors' calls
2. **Act 2 — It's custom, no?** — the video bill, and Demena's speech on ወግ
3. **Act 3 — Knowing our capacity is not shame** — the cancelled return and the family's decision

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
