# Reading a Play — The Wedding's Aftermath — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who reports the creditors' phone demands in Act 1?
**Answer:** Demena, the house guard (and Meseret brings further demands).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much does the cameraman ask for filming alone?
**Answer:** 600 birr (up to 1,600 with editing and the return).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does Mhret say not feasting a return really is?
**Answer:** Knowing your capacity — not shame; an example to others.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does Belayhun climb on the table in Act 3?
**Answer:** In despair at his debt — Demena holds his legs and he descends; the scene shows how far the debt has crushed him.
