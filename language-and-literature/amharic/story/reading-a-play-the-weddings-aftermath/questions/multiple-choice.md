# Reading a Play — The Wedding's Aftermath — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At the opening, Belayhun is —
a) dancing at the feast  b) alone, working a calculator, worried by debt  c) asleep in the salon
**Answer: b) alone, working a calculator, worried by debt** — he sits alone with pen, paper and calculator — 'I and my debt alone remained.'

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Demena's speech blames the debt mainly on —
a) the video cameraman  b) custom (ወግ) — the pressure to feast and spend to be recognised  c) the guard's wages
**Answer: b) custom (ወግ) — the pressure to feast and spend to be recognised** — custom is his answer: feast or be scorned as stingy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The play ends with the family deciding to —
a) feast the return even bigger  b) cancel the return feast and live within its means  c) borrow more money
**Answer: b) cancel the return feast and live within its means** — Mhret's closing speech: knowing our capacity is not shame.
