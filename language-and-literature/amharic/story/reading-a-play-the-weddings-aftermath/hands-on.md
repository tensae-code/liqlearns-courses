# Hands-on — Reading a Play — The Wedding's Aftermath

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Read it in parts
In a group, each person takes a character — Belayhun, Ayelech, Mhret, Demena, Meseret. Read one act aloud in your parts.

## The silent phone
Act the Act 3 moment: the phone rings, and Belayhun signs 'I am not here!' — without words.
