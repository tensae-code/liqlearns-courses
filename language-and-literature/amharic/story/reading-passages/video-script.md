# Video script — Reading Passages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A short read-and-answer script for the proverb passage, run the
guide's way: look at the picture first, read, then answer.

---

Look at the picture. Two houses.
This house is near. This house is far.

Now listen to the passage's name:
**ከሩቅ ዘመድ የቅርብ ጎረቤት.**
A near neighbour — beats a far relative.

A neighbour is beside you, every day.
When trouble comes, who runs first?
The one who is near.

Read the passage now, in your own voice.
Then answer — in full sentences, like the book asks.
