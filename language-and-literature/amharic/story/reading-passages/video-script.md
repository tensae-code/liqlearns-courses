# Video script — Reading Passages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A short read-and-answer script for the proverb passage, run the
guide's way: look at the picture first, read, then answer.

---

Look at the picture. Two houses.
This house is near. This house is far.

Now listen to the passage's name:
**ከሩቅ ዘመድ የቅርብ ጎረቤት.**
A near neighbour — beats a far relative.

A neighbour is beside you, every day.
When trouble comes, who runs first?
The one who is near.

Read the passage now, in your own voice.
Then answer — in full sentences, like the book asks.
---

Read this passage with me. It is about a forest.

In 1994, the forest stood wide. Ninety-two thousand hectares.
Trees and trees and trees.

Then read on. In 2002 — only eight thousand.
In 2005 — eight thousand again, and a little less.

Stop. Ask the passage: why?
A passage that explains must give its cause.
Find it. Say it in one sentence.

Numbers are part of the story.
Carry them away in order: 92,200 — 8,290 — 8,222.

---

Now read about a village where work is honour.

Nobody stands idle. The weaver weaves. The farmer digs.
The child helps. The basket is carried.

Ask before you read: what holds this village together?
Read. Then say the whole passage in ONE sentence.
That sentence is its main idea.

And read about time itself. **ዓውድ** — a cycle. A circle.
The old scholars watched the stars,
and the years went round, and round, and round.

Read first for what it says. Then for what it means.
