# Reading Passages — ordering

Put the steps or events in the right order. Write the numbers in order.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** The kebero-making steps:
a) Lace the hide over both openings  b) Soak and scrape the hide  c) Rub with embway fruit and sun it  d) Hollow the wanza-wood body
**Answer: b, d, a, c.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** The salad steps:
a) Mix in a deep bowl  b) Wash  c) Lemon juice  d) Chop  e) Oil and salt
**Answer: b, d, a, e, c.**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** The Finote Selam events:
a) Haile Selassie returns via Sudan  b) Italy invades in 1928 E.C.  c) ወጀት is renamed ፍኖተሰላም  d) The patriots resist for five years
**Answer: b, d, a, c.**
