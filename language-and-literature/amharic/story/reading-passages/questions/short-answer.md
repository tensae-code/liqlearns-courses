# Reading Passages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the passage.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are the passage's comprehension answers given?
**Answer:** Some in full sentences, some short, some chosen by letter — and underlined words' meanings are taken from the passage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Serving the Food, name two dairy things on the table.
**Answer:** Ergo and ayib.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** From the cleanliness completion key, which words fill item 4?
**Answer:** የግል ንፅህና — personal hygiene.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two fast crops from Growing a Backyard Garden Wisely.
**Answer:** Any two of: onion, lettuce, kale, cabbage, tomato, pepper.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In the salad passage, what is served with the salad?
**Answer:** Bread or injera.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** How long did the patriots resist, and from where?
**Answer:** Five years, from the forests and mountains.
**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the order of a visit passage using A Visit: what does the club go to, and what kind of place is it?
**Answer:** The "Know Your Country" club visits the culture and research institute named after Dr Haddis Alemayehu — a descriptive passage that moves from place to place.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** From the climate tables: give the four zones' shares and the lowest and highest temperatures.
**Answer:** ቆላ 6%, ወይና ደጋ 60%, ደጋ 31%, ውርጭ 3%; lowest 12.4 °C, highest 27.8 °C.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** Name the five kinds of source Mulunesh compiled her report from.
**Answer:** Television, newspapers, magazines, books and websites.

**10. (Sub lesson 9)** How many novels and plays did Berhanu Zerihun write, from his passage?
**Answer:** Eleven novels and three plays.

**11. (Sub lesson 10)** Say the proverb the littering passage carries, and its teaching in one sentence.
**Answer:** ለነገረኛ ሰው ጀርባህን ስጠው — to a quarrelsome person, give your back: walk away from quarrels rather than trade rubbish words.

**12. (Sub lesson 11)** From the climate change passage: how high is the ozone layer, as the passage gives it?
**Answer:** 19–48 kilometres up.
