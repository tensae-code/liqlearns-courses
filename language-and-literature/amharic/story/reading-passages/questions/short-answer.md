# Reading Passages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the passage.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are the passage's comprehension answers given?
**Answer:** Some in full sentences, some short, some chosen by letter — and underlined words' meanings are taken from the passage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Serving the Food, name two dairy things on the table.
**Answer:** Ergo and ayib.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** From the cleanliness completion key, which words fill item 4?
**Answer:** የግል ንፅህና — personal hygiene.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two fast crops from Growing a Backyard Garden Wisely.
**Answer:** Any two of: onion, lettuce, kale, cabbage, tomato, pepper.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In the salad passage, what is served with the salad?
**Answer:** Bread or injera.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** How long did the patriots resist, and from where?
**Answer:** Five years, from the forests and mountains.
**7. (Sub lesson 6)** Name the order of a visit passage using A Visit: what does the club go to, and what kind of place is it?
**Answer:** The "Know Your Country" club visits the culture and research institute named after Dr Haddis Alemayehu — a descriptive passage that moves from place to place.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** From the climate tables: give the four zones' shares and the lowest and highest temperatures.
**Answer:** ቆላ 6%, ወይና ደጋ 60%, ደጋ 31%, ውርጭ 3%; lowest 12.4 °C, highest 27.8 °C.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** Name the five kinds of source Mulunesh compiled her report from.
**Answer:** Television, newspapers, magazines, books and websites.

**10. (Sub lesson 9)** How many novels and plays did Berhanu Zerihun write, from his passage?
**Answer:** Eleven novels and three plays.

**11. (Sub lesson 10)** Say the proverb the littering passage carries, and its teaching in one sentence.
**Answer:** ለነገረኛ ሰው ጀርባህን ስጠው — to a quarrelsome person, give your back: walk away from quarrels rather than trade rubbish words.

**12. (Sub lesson 11)** From the climate change passage: how high is the ozone layer, as the passage gives it?
**Answer:** 19–48 kilometres up.

**13. (Sub lesson 12)** Give the Awramba reading's main idea in one sentence, as the reading asks.
**Answer:** A community where work is honoured by everyone — a strong work culture — is what the passage holds up; any one-sentence statement of that main idea, from the passage, counts.

**14. (Sub lesson 13)** What is Tebechisa in its chapter, and what work is done with it?
**Answer:** A cultural practice of one of the peoples of the land, treated as the diversity case study; its comprehension questions are answered orally, in detail.

**15. (Sub lesson 14)** Who were the Bahre Hasab scholars' named models, and from which century?
**Answer:** Foreign historians, above all Abu Shakir and George ibn al-Amid, of the thirteenth century. The scholars recorded the history of the kings in their books.

**16. (Sub lesson 15)** Name the three challenges to inclusive education that Tsehay's reading keys.
**Answer:** People's attitudes; failure to identify some children's difficulties; and the assumption that disabled people cannot reach high levels.

**17. (Sub lesson 16)** Say the difference between the migration reading's two closing works.
**Answer:** Explaining what was grasped retells the passage's content in your own words; writing its themes names what the passage is about — its subjects — apart from its details.

**18. (Sub lesson 17)** What two instruments does the Grade 1 short passage at p.46 join?
**Answer:** The kebero drum (ከበሮ) and the krar lyre (ክራር).

**19. (Sub lesson 18)** Use ሽልማት in a sentence about the awarded friend.
**Answer:** Any sentence placing ሽልማት (a prize) with the friend who won the quiz competition and prizes for the school (Grade 1, p.119).

**20. (Sub lesson 19)** What does Weyinitu offer in Accepting Responsibility?
**Answer:** To help Kemal's group when her own group finishes (Grade 2, p.94).

**21. (Sub lesson 27)** How does the last speaker define family planning?
**Answer:** The method by which we decide how many children to have, from our income and living conditions — husband and wife sharing the responsibility.

**22. (Sub lesson 28)** What two aims has the recent youth volunteer service?
**Answer:** Serving community segments, and strengthening national unity and social values.

**23. (Sub lesson 29)** How much electric power does the text name?
**Answer:** 6,000 units of electric power.

**24. (Sub lesson 30)** Name two health harms the text lists.
**Answer:** Any two of: depression/agitation, stomach and intestine dryness, reduced appetite, TB/hepatitis exposure, spoiled teeth, raised heart rate and blood pressure.

**25. (Sub lesson 31)** What do the hourly wages seem to the narrator, instead of the price of labour?
**Answer:** Compensation paid for the failed yesterday of the one who left his home country.

**26. (Sub lesson 32)** What two kinds of heroes does the essay call for?
**Answer:** Heroes of work and heroes of thought — word and deed one.

**27. (Sub lesson 33)** How large is the Fasil palace compound?
**Answer:** 70,000 m², with six kings' palaces.

**28. (Sub lesson 34)** What form does this reading take?
**Answer:** An interview — a student questioning her teacher, the answers in parts.

**29. (Sub lesson 38)** What does the Grade 7 guide print at printed p.24 for the teacher's checking work?
**Answer:** Model answers (በናሙናነት የተመረጡ ጥያቄዎች መልስ) for selected questions — the chapter's answer key.

**30. (Sub lesson 39)** Name the substance the epidemics reading uses to explain fever-like feelings under fear and stress.
**Answer:** Serotonin.

**31. (Sub lesson 40)** To what is the Paying Tax passage credited in the Grade 9 guide?
**Answer:** A Walta Media & Communication corporate report, Hamle 24, 2009 E.C.

**32. (Sub lesson 41)** In the recovery reading, what happens after the class has read two paragraphs?
**Answer:** Reading stops; predictions are checked against the pre-reading answers, the rest is predicted, and then the text is finished.
