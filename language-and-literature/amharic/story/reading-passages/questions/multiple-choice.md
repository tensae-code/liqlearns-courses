# Reading Passages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the proverb in the passage's title say?
a) A far relative beats a near neighbour  b) A near neighbour beats a far relative  c) Relatives should live far away
**Answer: b)** — ከሩቅ ዘመድ የቅርብ ጎረቤት.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which trees stand in the school compound passage?
a) Coffee, mango and olive trees  b) Only palm trees  c) No trees at all
**Answer: a)** — and warka, weyra and tid besides.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Draining stagnant pools helps stop which disease's mosquitoes breeding?
a) Eye disease  b) Malaria  c) Flu
**Answer: b) Malaria** — from Preventing Disease.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is done to the kebero's hide first?
a) It is painted  b) It is soaked and scraped  c) It is cut into straps
**Answer: b)** — soak, scrape, then the body is hollowed and laced.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does ፍኖተሰላም mean?
a) Path of peace  b) Town of gold  c) River of stone
**Answer: a)** — ፍኖተ-ሰላም, path of peace.
**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What is Wehni Amba like, from its description?
a) Low and forested  b) Bare, steep and flat-topped  c) Round like a dome
**Answer: b)** — the passage describes a bare steep amba, and paints it with similes.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** In As I Saw Tis Abay, why is the falls' name tied to smoke?
a) A fire burns nearby  b) The spray rises like smoke — ጢስ  c) Mist hides the road
**Answer: b)** — the spray rises like smoke; that is the name's origin in the passage.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** What is the rule for answering the Headache passage's questions?
a) Answer from memory  b) Answer from the passage's table  c) Ask a friend
**Answer: b)** — every question is answered from the data table.

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** Which climate zone covers the largest share of the region?
a) ቆላ — 6%  b) ደጋ — 31%  c) ወይና ደጋ — 60%
**Answer: c)** — ወይና ደጋ covers 60 of every 100 parts; ውርጭ is 3% and ቆላ 6%.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** How does Eskinder research the first driver?
a) He asks one elder  b) He searches newspapers, magazines and books, noting authors and years  c) He guesses
**Answer: b)** — library research, notes with authors' names and publication years, written up as an essay.

**11. (Sub lesson 8)** How many steps does Fasilides' Palace have, from the passage?
a) 132  b) 20  c) 32
**Answer: a)** — 132 steps; it is 32 metres high, with three floors and 20 rooms.

**12. (Sub lesson 9)** How many trees did Wangari Maathai's Green Belt plant, from the passage?
a) Over 3 million  b) Over 30 million  c) 300,000
**Answer: b)** — over 30 million trees, and she received the Nobel Prize in 1996 E.C.

**13. (Sub lesson 9)** What did Tirunesh Dibaba do at Beijing 2008, from her passage?
a) Won double gold — the first woman ever  b) Carried the flag  c) Set a walking record
**Answer: a)** — double gold, the first woman ever to do it.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** What does "Littering Is Forbidden!" say hate speech is?
a) A small fault  b) Rubbish thoughts thrown at people  c) A joke
**Answer: b)** — the passage widens "rubbish": hate speech is rubbish thoughts.

**15. (Sub lesson 10)** What rule does the Social Media passage teach?
a) Believe the most-shared post  b) Separate the true and the useful before believing  c) Never read posts
**Answer: b)** — true and useful are separated out before anything is believed.

**16. (Sub lesson 11)** What happened to the Wof Washa forest between 1994 and 2002 E.C.?
a) It grew  b) It shrank from 92,200 to 8,290 hectares  c) It stayed the same
**Answer: b)** — and by 2005 E.C. it stood at 8,222 hectares.

**17. (Sub lesson 11)** Which of these is one of the passage's three causes of child labour exploitation?
a) ድህነት — poverty  b) Rain  c) Big schools
**Answer: a)** — poverty; parents' distorted attitude; and illegal people-movers.

**18. (Sub lesson 12)** The Awramba community is known above all for —
a) Its strong work culture  b) Its high mountains  c) Its fishing boats
**Answer: a) Its strong work culture** — the reading begins with pre-reading questions on the community, then silent reading.

**19. (Sub lesson 13)** In Nature's Twin Faces, unity and diversity are presented as —
a) Enemies that can never meet  b) Two seemingly opposite concepts that belong together  c) The same word said twice
**Answer: b)** — the passage's themes are the twin points themselves, held together as nature holds them.

**20. (Sub lesson 14)** What is ዓውድ (awd) in the reckoning-of-time reading?
a) A king's throne  b) A period marked by recurring natural signs — a cycle, a circle  c) A kind of manuscript ink
**Answer: b)** — the Bahre Hasab scholars linked the change of eras to the movement of the stars and planets.

**21. (Sub lesson 15)** In Tsehay and Her Challenges, exams can be reached by disabled learners through —
a) Braille and tape-recording  b) Guessing the questions  c) Sitting a different, easier exam only
**Answer: a) Braille and tape-recording** — and help begins, the answers key, with attitude change.

**22. (Sub lesson 16)** What two works does the migration reading set?
a) Copy the passage and learn it by heart  b) Explain what was grasped, and write the themes presented  c) Draw a map and label it
**Answer: b)** — grasp explained in your own words; themes written.
