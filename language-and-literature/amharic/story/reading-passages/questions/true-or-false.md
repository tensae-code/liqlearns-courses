# Reading Passages — true or false

Say if the sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The near-neighbour reading is opened from its picture, before the reading itself.
**Answer: True** — the guide's picture-first opening.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the lion and the mouse passage, the mouse gnaws the trap rope and frees the lion.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The school compound passage names flowers at the gate and coffee, mango and olive trees inside.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Serving the Food, the lunch things are thrown onto the table in any order.
**Answer: False** — they are laid out in order: bread, injera, wats, dairy, vegetables, fruit.

**5. (Sub lesson 12)** The Awramba reading is read first aloud by the teacher, so the class hears it before seeing it.
**Answer: False** — it begins with pre-reading questions; the reading itself is silent and individual.

**6. (Sub lesson 13)** Tebechisa is a cultural practice of one of the peoples of the land, used as the chapter's diversity case study.
**Answer: True.**

**7. (Sub lesson 14)** The hospitality reading's work includes writing a summary and comparing the text with your own experience in writing.
**Answer: True** — gist (ጭምቅ ሀሳብ), summary, and comparison are its three works.

**8. (Sub lesson 15)** In the growth sequences inside Tsehay's answers, movement grows: walk → creep → crawl → roll.
**Answer: False** — the order is roll → crawl → creep → walk (and speech grows sound → words → sentences).

**9. (Sub lesson 16)** The migration reading ends by asking for a copied-out paragraph of the passage.
**Answer: False** — it asks for what was grasped, explained, and the themes, written — not copying.

**10. (Sub lesson 27)** The closing speaker says family planning does NOT mean not bearing children.
**Answer: True**

**11. (Sub lesson 28)** The summer volunteer service has run since 2011 E.C.
**Answer: True**

**12. (Sub lesson 29)** Besides power, the stored water is expected to green a formerly desert area.
**Answer: True**

**13. (Sub lesson 30)** Chat chewing is linked in the text to lost work time and falling income.
**Answer: True**

**14. (Sub lesson 31)** In the shop, iddir and equb money is collected among the talk and coffee.
**Answer: True**

**15. (Sub lesson 32)** The essay counts science and technology among the foreign things that WOULD profit us if chosen sorted.
**Answer: True**

**16. (Sub lesson 33)** Most of Lalibela's churches were carved from a single rock.
**Answer: True**

**17. (Sub lesson 34)** The sun's light is said to vary in an 11-year cycle.
**Answer: True**
