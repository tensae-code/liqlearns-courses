# Reading Passages — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Comprehension passages — people near us
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer comprehension questions from the passage — full sentence or short answer, as asked — and give the meaning of underlined words from the passage itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Descriptive passages — places and tables
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a descriptive passage does — it shows a place or a thing part by part — and list the school compound's trees from the passage.

## Sub lesson 3 — Expository passages — how things are
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pull a passage's main idea out in one sentence, and complete sentences with words from the passage's word box.

## Sub lesson 4 — Procedural passages — steps in order
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell a procedure's steps in their right order, first step to last.

## Sub lesson 5 — History passages — Finote Selam
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell the history passage in time order and say what ፍኖተሰላም means.
## Sub lesson 6 — Descriptive passages — mountains and waterfalls
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: describe Wehni Amba from the passage's words alone, its similes named; and retell the Tis Abay visit in its order.

## Sub lesson 7 — Passages with tables — read the numbers
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer from the table, cell pointed to first; say the climate shares and the seed table's use.

## Sub lesson 8 — Research-style readings — how a report is made
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name Eskinder's method whole — search, note with author and year, write up; and give the palace's four numbers back with the passage closed.

## Sub lesson 9 — Lives in prose — biography readings
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell one life by its dates and deeds in order — Maathai's trees and prize, Dibaba's double gold, Zerihun's eleven novels.

## Sub lesson 10 — Argue and advise — passages that take a side
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state one argue-passage's side fairly; name the five counsels of the rubbish-dump passage; and apply social media's true-and-useful rule to a claim.

## Sub lesson 11 — Explain the world — causes in prose
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: carry the forest's three numbers in order and name its cause; name the three causes of child labour exploitation as the passage orders them.

## Sub lesson 12 — Work culture — the Awramba reading (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer the pre-reading questions, read silently, and give the Awramba passage's main idea in one sentence.

## Sub lesson 13 — Unity in diversity — two readings (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 13 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what Tebechisa is a case study of, and name Nature's Twin Faces' themes — unity and diversity held together.

## Sub lesson 14 — Hospitality and the reckoning of time (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 14 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the hospitality gist (ጭምቅ ሀሳብ) and write its summary; explain ዓውድ, the Bahre Hasab scholars and their models; and sort the medicine reading's general from its detailed information.

## Sub lesson 15 — Inclusive education — two readings (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 15 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the three challenges Tsehay's reading names, the two exam accesses, and both growing sequences in order; and list the perseverance passage's detailed information.

## Sub lesson 16 — Migration — the reading (teacher guide — evidence)
Sub lesson 16 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain what was grasped in your own words and write the migration passage's themes, with the passage closed.
