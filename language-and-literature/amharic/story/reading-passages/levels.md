# Reading Passages — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Comprehension passages — people near us
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer comprehension questions from the passage — full sentence or short answer, as asked — and give the meaning of underlined words from the passage itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Descriptive passages — places and tables
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what a descriptive passage does — it shows a place or a thing part by part — and list the school compound's trees from the passage.

## Sub lesson 3 — Expository passages — how things are
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pull a passage's main idea out in one sentence, and complete sentences with words from the passage's word box.

## Sub lesson 4 — Procedural passages — steps in order
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell a procedure's steps in their right order, first step to last.

## Sub lesson 5 — History passages — Finote Selam
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell the history passage in time order and say what ፍኖተሰላም means.
