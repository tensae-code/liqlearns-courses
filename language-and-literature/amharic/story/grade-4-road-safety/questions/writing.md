# Grade 4 Road Safety — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two sentences: why we face the cars, and the antonym pair you like best with its meanings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the blind man's story ending: who helps him, and how he crosses safely.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the traffic-light web as three sentences — one for each colour.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write three sentences warning a friend about speed, using the village children's story.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write your own crossing story in order — first, then, then, last — with one double-suffix word underlined.
