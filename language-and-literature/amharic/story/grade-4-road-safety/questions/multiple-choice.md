# Grade 4 Road Safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do we walk on the left side, facing the cars?
a) So we see the cars coming, and they see us  b) Because the right side is forbidden  c) No reason
**Answer: a) So we see the cars coming, and they see us** — Facing the cars, you see them coming — the school guest's teaching.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the antonym of ብልህ (clever)?
a) ጠርዝ  b) ሞኝ — foolish  c) መሀል
**Answer: b) ሞኝ — foolish** — ብልህ → ሞኝ: clever and foolish stand at opposite ends.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Mirtinesh never do on the road?
a) Cross at a crossing  b) Look left and right  c) Hurry
**Answer: c) Hurry** — She uses the pedestrian way, crosses at crossings, looks both ways — and never hurries.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How does little Smret teach the boys?
a) She shouts at them  b) She carries the ball away without a word  c) She calls a policeman
**Answer: b) She carries the ball away without a word** — Without a word she carries the ball off the road; the boys understand and move their game.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** When may pedestrians cross?
a) When their light is green  b) When their light is red  c) Any time
**Answer: a) When their light is green** — Pedestrians cross on their green; red for cars means green for pedestrians.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** ቸኮለ + -ች makes…?
a) ቸኮሎች  b) ቸኮለች  c) ቸኮለኧኛ
**Answer: b) ቸኮለች** — The fitting suffix joins cleanly: ቸኮለች.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: a drunk driver who causes no accident has no problem.
a) True  b) False  c) Only on holidays
**Answer: b) False** — False — drunk driving is a problem before any accident happens.

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In ፍጥነትና መዘዙ, how does the father drive?
a) Slowly and carefully  b) Like a storm, ignoring pleas to slow down  c) He does not drive
**Answer: b) Like a storm, ignoring pleas to slow down** — Drunk with joy, he drives like a storm and ignores the family.

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** Yellow on the traffic light means…?
a) Go  b) Stop  c) Beware
**Answer: c) Beware** — Red stop, yellow beware, green go — for cars and pedestrians in turn.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** What do the village children do when the bus comes?
a) Dance eskista in the middle of the road  b) Wait at the side  c) Run home
**Answer: a) Dance eskista in the middle of the road** — They dance in the road; the driver stops and teaches them — a road is not for dancing.

**11. (Sub lesson 5)** መንገደኞች splits into…?
a) መንገድ - ኧኛ - ኦች  b) መንገ - ደኞች  c) መንገደኞ - ች
**Answer: a) መንገድ - ኧኛ - ኦች** — One stem, two tails: መንገድ + ኧኛ + ኦች.

**12. (Sub lesson 5)** ደርቅ + ኦሽ makes…?
a) ደርቃማ  b) ድርቆሽ  c) ደርቅነት
**Answer: b) ድርቆሽ** — The book's own example: ድርቅ + ኦሽ = ድርቆሽ.

**13. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a zebra crossing show?
a) Where cars park  b) Where pedestrians cross — black-and-white lines  c) A bus stop
**Answer: b) Where pedestrians cross — black-and-white lines** — Zebra lines mark where crossing belongs — Gizish's father shows her at the junction.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** Red, yellow and green on Ethiopia's flag are also…?
a) Traffic colours only  b) Colours the colour web compares with the traffic light  c) Unrelated to the web
**Answer: b) Colours the colour web compares with the traffic light** — The colour web sets the light's meanings beside the flag's.
