# Lesson plan — Figures of Speech (ዘይቤ)

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Story · **Lesson:** Figures of Speech (ዘይቤ)
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ዘይቤ is deliberately stepping away from a word's ordinary meaning to give expression strength — in three kinds at this grade: comparison, substitution and personification.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Poems and Songs — the learner has met figures inside poems; this lesson names and sorts them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **ዘይቤ — saying it stronger** — what a figure of speech is, and why poets use it
2. **The three kinds — ተነፃፃሪ, ተለዋጭ, ሰውኛ** — comparison, substitution, personification with the book's examples
3. **Naming figures in real poems** — the mourning-verse and love-poem figure tables

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
