# Figures of Speech (ዘይቤ) — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A figure of speech makes an idea strike harder than plain speech.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A bed that 'wept' is ሰውኛ ዘይቤ — personification.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After naming a figure, you should also say what the figure does — what it praises or makes felt.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'The boy fell like a cut tree' compares two things — ተነፃፃሪ.
**Answer: True**
