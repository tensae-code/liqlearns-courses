# Figures of Speech (ዘይቤ) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ዘይቤ is —
a) a spelling rule  b) deliberately leaving ordinary meaning to strengthen expression  c) a kind of letter
**Answer: b) deliberately leaving ordinary meaning to strengthen expression** — the book's definition: departing from the ordinary to give expression strength and height.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 'ልጅቷ ፀሐይ ናት' — the girl is a sun — is which kind?
a) ተነፃፃሪ (comparison)  b) ተለዋጭ (substitution)  c) ሰውኛ (personification)
**Answer: b) ተለዋጭ (substitution)** — she IS the sun in the verse — one thing's character given to another: substitution.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the model table, 'her heel is a lemon' is which kind of ዘይቤ?
a) ተነፃፃሪ  b) ተለዋጭ  c) ሰውኛ
**Answer: b) ተለዋጭ** — the heel IS a lemon in the verse — substitution, praising the heel.
