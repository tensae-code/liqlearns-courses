# Figures of Speech (ዘይቤ) — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do poets use ዘይቤ?
**Answer:** To give the idea greater strength, information and height than ordinary wording gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you tell comparison from substitution?
**Answer:** Comparison says 'like/as' between two things; substitution gives one thing's character to another outright — it IS the other thing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the set scrambled poem about?
**Answer:** Envy (ቅናት) — the disease that destroys the eyes of conscience.
