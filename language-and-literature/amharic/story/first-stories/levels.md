# First Stories — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the lesson's four
stories, in the order the child meets them — two workbook
stories known by their questions, the first real reading
passage, and one supporting story from the teachers' training.

## Sub lesson 1 — Butiye and the flower
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell the
story in your own words and answer its questions — the flower
grew because Butiye watered it, and we plant flowers at home
too.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ali and Hawa
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer
the story's true/false questions from what the questions
themselves tell — father went to town by horse; Hawa's car is
red; the donkey belongs in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mamo and his brother Gobeze
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say who
is who (Mamo, six, the big brother; Gobeze, the little brother)
and what Mamo does for him — sings him mezmur, plays with him,
teaches him how to eat, helps him dress — including catching
that "Mamo is 7" is false.

## Sub lesson 4 — Gobez Meseret, who washes her hands
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name
Meseret's clean habits (hands washed with soap and water before
eating and after the toilet, nails cut, teeth brushed, nose and
mouth covered when sneezing or coughing) and pick one to copy.
This sub lesson stands on SUPPORTING teacher material — named
as evidence in sources.md; the workbook stories above are the
pupil-taught core.
