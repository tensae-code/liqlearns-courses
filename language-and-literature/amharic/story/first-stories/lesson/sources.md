**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).** The three workbook stories are pupil-taught; the Meseret story and the three-pass method are SUPPORTING teacher material and do not add to the count.

Honesty notes: (1) All stories are told as summaries in the lesson's own words — never the books' text, per the build rules. (2) For Ali and Hawa, the book gives questions, not the tale's text: the lesson's summary is built only from the facts those questions carry (father went to town by horse; Hawa's car is red; a donkey figures in the story), and nothing beyond those facts is invented — no journey details, no other events. (3) For Butiye, the book's page is a fill-the-gaps question page; the summary states only what the placement records (her flower grew because she watered it; plant flowers at home too). (4) The Mamo passage is summarised from the placement's own summary (p.146) and its questions (p.147), including the planted falsehood the book itself tests — "Mamo is 7" — which the lesson teaches as false (he is 6). (5) Meseret's age (ten) and her habits list are as the supports placement records them; the placement summarises the training-module story rather than quoting it, and the lesson does the same. (6) Pictures 8215 (girl watering her flower) and 8216 (boy singing to his little brother) are generated pictures made for this lesson; both were looked at before filing — modest dress, right actions, no text in either image.
