# Video script — First Stories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is the full script the placements name for this lesson:
the three-pass treatment of one story — hear it, say it, act it
(the teachers' own story method) — worked on the Mamo story.

---

**Pass one — hear it.**

Sit down. Eyes here. Listen to the whole story.

Mamo is six years old.
This year, Mamo finishes pre-primary school. He is the year's
graduating pupil!
Mamo has a little brother. His name is Gobeze.
Mamo loves his little brother.
Mamo sings mezmur to Gobeze. Sing, sing.
Mamo plays with Gobeze.
Mamo teaches Gobeze how to eat.
Mamo helps Gobeze get dressed.

That is the story. You heard it.

**Pass two — say it.**

Now you say it. One by one.
Who is six years old? — Mamo!
Who is the little brother? — Gobeze!
What does Mamo sing to Gobeze? — Mezmur!
What does Mamo teach Gobeze? — How to eat!
What does Mamo help Gobeze do? — Get dressed!

Good. The story is in your mouth now.

**Pass three — act it.**

Stand up. You are Mamo. Your friend is Gobeze.
Sing to your little brother. Softly. Sing, sing.
Play with him.
Show him how to eat. Like this. Slowly.
Help him put on his shirt. Arm in. Other arm in.

You did what Mamo does. That is the story — in your hands now.

Hear it. Say it. Act it. That is how a story becomes yours.
