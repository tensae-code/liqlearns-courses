# First Stories — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the Butiye story tell us to do
at home, too?
**Answer:** plant flowers at home — and water them, like Butiye.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What colour is Hawa's car?
**Answer:** red.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How old is Mamo?
**Answer:** six years old — he is this year's graduating
pre-primary pupil.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two of Meseret's clean habits.
**Answer:** any two of — washing hands before eating; washing
with soap and water after the toilet; keeping nails cut;
brushing teeth; covering nose and mouth when sneezing or
coughing.
