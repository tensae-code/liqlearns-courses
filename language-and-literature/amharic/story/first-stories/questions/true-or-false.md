# First Stories — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Butiye's flower grew because she watered
it.
**True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Butiye never gave her flower any water.
**False** — she watered it, and that is why it grew.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ali and Hawa's father went to town by
horse.
**True** — that is what the story's questions tell us.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hawa's car is blue.
**False** — Hawa's car is red.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Mamo is seven years old.
**False** — Mamo is six. (The book's own question plants this
one — catch it.)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Mamo sings mezmur to his little brother
Gobeze.
**True.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Meseret eats her food without washing her
hands.
**False** — Meseret never eats without washing her hands first.
