# First Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Butiye give her flower?
a) bread  b) water  c) a stone
**Answer: b) water** — and the flower grew because of it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did Ali and Hawa's father go to town?
a) by bus  b) on foot  c) by horse
**Answer: c) by horse.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who is Gobeze?
a) Mamo's little brother  b) Mamo's teacher  c) Mamo's father
**Answer: a) Mamo's little brother.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** What does Mamo do for Gobeze?
a) He hides his shoes  b) He sings to him, plays with him,
teaches him how to eat and helps him dress  c) He leaves him
alone all day
**Answer: b)** — all four helps are in the passage.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** What does Meseret wash her hands with?
a) only dust  b) soap and water  c) she does not wash them
**Answer: b) soap and water.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** When does Meseret wash her hands?
a) before eating, and after the toilet  b) only on holidays
c) never
**Answer: a)** — every time, before she eats.
