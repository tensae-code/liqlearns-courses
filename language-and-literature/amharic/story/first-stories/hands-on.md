# Hands-on — First Stories

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Act the story (the third pass)
With a partner, act one story's best moment: pour real water
for Butiye's flower; be Mamo singing softly and helping your
"little brother" put on a jacket; be Meseret washing hands with
real soap and water, step by step. The acting is not extra — it
is the story's third pass, the way the teachers themselves run
a story.

## Story stones
Put four stones in a row for a story: who — where — what
happened — how it ends. Point at each stone as you retell. If
you get stuck mid-story, the stones tell you where you are.

## Draw the best moment
Draw the one moment of a story you would keep if you could keep
only one: the flower standing tall after watering, Mamo singing,
Meseret at the water with her soap. Under it, say — or write —
one sentence about your picture.

## Meseret's week
Pick one of Meseret's habits — hands before eating, nails cut,
teeth brushed, covering a sneeze — and really do it every day
for a week. Each day, tell someone: "Today I did what Meseret
does."

## If it is hard
Retell with the picture cards (8215, 8216) in front of you and
the story stones doing the remembering. Hear the story twice
before you must say it, say it with a partner before you say it
alone, and act it before you answer questions about it — small
steps, in the story method's own order (SNE toolkit and the
training module's three passes — SUPPORTING; see sources.md).

Two memory games a teacher can play, from the toolkit's own
checks (SUPPORTING — teacher checks, played as games, never as
tests):
- **The red marker game:** show the child a red marker for three
  or four seconds. Hide it among two other markers. After ten
  minutes of other play, ask: "Which one did I show you?" The
  child picks out the red marker they saw before.
- **The story card game:** show the child one of the story
  picture cards (8215, 8216). Put it back among the other cards,
  face up together, and ask the child to pick out the card they
  were shown earlier. Change the card and play again.
