# Measuring Your Reading Speed — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the timed passage about?
**Answer:** The impact of global warming — rising temperature, drought, melting ice and rising seas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is correct reading speed worked out?
**Answer:** Speed (words reached minus unread words) minus the number of error marks.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What mark do you put at the word where the minute ends?
**Answer:** A slash (/).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If you reach the 120 line with 5 unread words and 5 errors, what are speed and correct speed?
**Answer:** Speed 115 words per minute; correct speed 110.
