# Measuring Your Reading Speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The numbers down the passage's margin are —
a) page numbers  b) cumulative word counts  c) temperature readings
**Answer: b) cumulative word counts** — they count the words read up to each line — 6, 11, 17 … 136.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The example student's correct reading speed was —
a) 98 words per minute  b) 92 words per minute  c) 102 words per minute
**Answer: b) 92 words per minute** — 98 speed minus 6 errors = 92 correct words per minute.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The passage's six columns are for —
a) six readers  b) six attempts  c) six questions
**Answer: b) six attempts** — the record is attempt by attempt — six tries on one passage.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Comprehension 4/5 is written as —
a) 40%  b) 80%  c) 92%
**Answer: b) 80%** — 4 of 5 questions = 80%.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In timed reading, the time is —
a) ignored  b) set by the teacher and kept  c) chosen by each reader mid-read
**Answer: b) set by the teacher and kept** — The teacher sets the time.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A struggling reader —
a) stops forever  b) repeats until fluent  c) skips the text
**Answer: b) repeats until fluent** — Repeat until fluent.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Fluent reading here holds together —
a) time, voice, words and punctuation  b) only speed  c) only loudness
**Answer: a) time, voice, words and punctuation** — All four together.
