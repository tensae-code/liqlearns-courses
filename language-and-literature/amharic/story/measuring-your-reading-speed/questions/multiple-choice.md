# Measuring Your Reading Speed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The numbers down the passage's margin are —
a) page numbers  b) cumulative word counts  c) temperature readings
**Answer: b) cumulative word counts** — they count the words read up to each line — 6, 11, 17 … 136.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The example student's correct reading speed was —
a) 98 words per minute  b) 92 words per minute  c) 102 words per minute
**Answer: b) 92 words per minute** — 98 speed minus 6 errors = 92 correct words per minute.
