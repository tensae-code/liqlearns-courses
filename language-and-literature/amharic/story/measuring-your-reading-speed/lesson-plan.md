# Lesson plan — Measuring Your Reading Speed

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Story · **Lesson:** Measuring Your Reading Speed
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** read for exactly one minute, slash where you stop, subtract the unread words and the errors — that is your speed and your correct speed, the Grade 8 book's own method.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Reading Aloud Like an Actor — accuracy first; this lesson adds the clock and the scoring.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
