# Grade 4 Our Environment — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write two sentences: one thing the care rules ask, and one way parents help learning at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the hare's story in three sentences — first, then, last — ending each with ።.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the diligent-farmer side of the chart paragraph: three habits and their result.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Write your forest web as four sentences: two benefits of forest, two harms of deforestation.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Write a paragraph naming three fruit-bearing plants and one use for each.
