# Grade 4 Our Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the care message የጥንቃቄ መልዕክት ask of you?
a) Keep the book clean and whole for next year's children  b) Write your name in the book  c) Hide the book at home
**Answer: a) Keep the book clean and whole for next year's children** — The book is school property, kept clean and whole for the children who come after.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In አካባቢያችን, what can waste become?
a) Only poison  b) Useful products  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) Useful products** — The listening teaches turning waste into products, not only throwing it away.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does ቀዬ mean?
a) A river  b) Your home area  c) A kind of tree
**Answer: b) Your home area** — ቀዬ is your home area — the place you belong to.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the hare migrate in ጥንቸሏ?
a) The forest was cut down — springs, shade and food vanished  b) She wanted a holiday  c) A lion chased her
**Answer: a) The forest was cut down — springs, shade and food vanished** — When the dense forest is cut, her spring, shade and food all vanish.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the man find at his childhood river in ቁጭት?
a) Clean water and fish  b) Felled trees, plastic bottles and cloth, waste flowing in  c) A new bridge
**Answer: b) Felled trees, plastic bottles and cloth, waste flowing in** — He weeps: native trees felled, bottles and cloth in the water, waste flowing in.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** ቁጭት matches which synonym?
a) ፀፀት — regret  b) ሽታ — smell  c) መጋለጥ — exposure
**Answer: a) ፀፀት — regret** — ቁጭት → ፀፀት, both mean regret.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What do Aster and Sofiya resolve to do?
a) Cut more trees  b) Be examples and mobilise friends to plant seedlings  c) Leave their village
**Answer: b) Be examples and mobilise friends to plant seedlings** — They agree deforestation drives heat and drought, and resolve to plant and mobilise.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** What do the neighbours build in ውሏችን ለአካባቢያችን?
a) A house  b) Terraces — ርከን — on flood-damaged land  c) A fence
**Answer: b) Terraces — ርከን — on flood-damaged land** — With spade and shovel they terrace the flood-damaged land all day.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** How do the diligent farmers plough?
a) Straight down the hill  b) On the contour, and they terrace against floods  c) They do not plough
**Answer: b) On the contour, and they terrace against floods** — Contour ploughing and terraces hold the soil against floods.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** What endangers Lake Tana in the listening?
a) እንቦጭ — water hyacinth, an invasive weed  b) Too many fish  c) A drought only
**Answer: a) እንቦጭ — water hyacinth, an invasive weed** — The dangerous invasive weed እንቦጭ spreads over Tana's water.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** What do Sindu's mango trees give her?
a) Fruit, shade, clean air and a windbreak  b) Only fruit  c) Timber
**Answer: a) Fruit, shade, clean air and a windbreak** — Daily fruit, cooling shade, clean air and a windbreak for the house.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** In ሳይቃጠል በቅጠል, what does irresponsible cutting and burning bring?
a) More rain  b) Desertification, food and water shortage  c) Bigger forests
**Answer: b) Desertification, food and water shortage** — Cutting and burning brings desertification and shortage — the text's warning.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** Which mark asks a question?
a) !  b) ?  c) ፣
**Answer: b) ?** — ? is the question mark; ! carries strong feeling, ፣ pauses.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** ማንጎዎች splits into…?
a) ማንጎ - ዎች  b) ማ - ንጎዎች  c) ማን - ጎዎች
**Answer: a) ማንጎ - ዎች** — The stem is ማንጎ and -ዎች is the plural suffix.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** What does ንጹህ mean?
a) Dirty  b) Clean, pure  c) Freedom
**Answer: b) Clean, pure** — ንጹህ means clean/pure; ነጻነት is freedom.
