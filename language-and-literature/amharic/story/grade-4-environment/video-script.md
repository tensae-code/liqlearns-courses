# Video script — Grade 4 Our Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **አካባቢ ጥበቃ** — protecting the environment.

Our environment chapter: how air and water are spoiled, how forests hold the land together, and what people do together — terraces, seedlings, clean rivers — to protect the place they live in.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: አካባቢ, ዛፍ, ወንዝ, ቆሻሻ, ችግኝ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
