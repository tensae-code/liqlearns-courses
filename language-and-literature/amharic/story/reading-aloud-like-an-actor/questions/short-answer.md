# Reading Aloud Like an Actor — the Four Levels — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things does a Level 4 reader do besides not stopping?
**Answer:** Follows the punctuation fully, imitates the characters' speech, and reads the words correctly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three errors the book's fluency note lists.
**Answer:** Any three of: stumbling pronunciation, omission, substitution, skipping, leaving words out.
