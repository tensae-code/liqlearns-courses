# Reading Aloud Like an Actor — the Four Levels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Level 4 expressive reading means —
a) reading fastest  b) following punctuation fully, imitating the characters, correct words, no stopping  c) reading loudest
**Answer: b) following punctuation fully, imitating the characters, correct words, no stopping** — the top level joins all four: punctuation, character, correctness, flow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs to reading WITHOUT error?
a) omitting hard words  b) self-correcting when you slip  c) skipping lines
**Answer: b) self-correcting when you slip** — self-correction is in the clean list; omission and skipping are errors.
