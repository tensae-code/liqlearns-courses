# Video script — Listening Stories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This is the full script the placements name for this lesson: the
house riddle እኔ ማን ነኝ? told as a guessing game, exactly the way
the guide runs it — read in parts, the learners predict, the
answer is confirmed at the end.

---

Close your eyes. Only listen.

I have a room where people sleep.
I have a room where people eat.
I have a room where guests sit and talk.
People live inside me. Every day. Every night.

***Who am I?***

Say it. Guess!

Yes — I am a **ቤት**. A house.

Now open your eyes. Look at the house.
Room for sleeping. Room for eating. Room for guests.
And the people live inside. That is a house.

Listen again to a story this week — and at the question,
stop. Guess. Then listen on.
---

Hear this story. A man stands before a king.

The man's words reached the king. The king is angry.
The man kneels. He asks for mercy.

Guess. Will the king listen? …

A friend stands with him. A loyal friend does not run away.
Not in the good days only. In the worst day too.

Now hear the counting story. Nine hyenas hunt together.
They take one buffalo. One lion hunts alone. He takes nine.

Count with me. One for the many. Nine for the one.
Is it fair? Say why.

Listen this week. Stop at the question. Guess.
Then listen on.

---

Now hear how people settle a quarrel — the old way, the fair way.

Two sides. Angry. Apart.
Between them sit the elders. That is the law called **መድዓ**.

Step one — ask: what happened?
Step two — both sides agree to make peace.
Step three — choose the mediators.
Step four — choose the judge.
Step five — hold the mediation. Speak. Listen. Decide.

And at the end — an oath. The decision stands.

Count the steps with me: one, two, three, four, five.

This week, when you listen — listen like an elder.
Hear the whole message. Never interrupt. Then answer.
