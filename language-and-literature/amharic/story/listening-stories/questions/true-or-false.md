# Listening Stories — true or false

Say if the sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The story's own questions about Ato Nuradin ask what he looks like and what he does.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In The Carpenter, Samuel hurries past his friend without helping.
**Answer: False** — he stops and helps his friend.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** At the end of The Late Student, Abel apologises.
**Answer: True** — ይቅርታ ጠይቃት.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Marta's father took her to Hamle 19 Park, called the Green Paradise.
**Answer: True** — አረንጓዴው ገነት.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Ato Amha and Woizero Hait celebrated their family events alone, without neighbours.
**Answer: False** — the neighbours organised and celebrated together.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The housework story ends with the rule that work is shared by ability, not by gender.
**Answer: True.**
**7. (Sub lesson 7)** In The Town, the town stays dirty because nobody cleans it.
**Answer: True** — the listening's questions ask who dirties it and who should clean it.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** The proverb ድር ቢያብር አንበሳ ያሥር teaches that joined threads can bind a lion.
**Answer: True** — it is one of the cooperation proverbs the listening carries.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** The Bride and Groom listening is a fast adventure story with a chase.
**Answer: False** — it is a description heard word by word: the wedding dress, the ጫጉላ ቤት, who organises what.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** In the guide's story of two school friends, both friends' paths end at the same place.
**Answer: False** — one comes home drunk; Hibist's and Zemzem's paths divide and end differently.

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** The wane bird in The Migrant Wane talks with a herdsman.
**Answer: True** — the travelling bird and the herdsman each know what the other does not.

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** At the Dawro New Year, only the people eat their fill; the animals are forgotten.
**Answer: False** — on New Year's Eve the household and the animals eat their fill, and then the youth sing house to house.
