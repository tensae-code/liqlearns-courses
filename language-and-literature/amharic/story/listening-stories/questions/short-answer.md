# Listening Stories — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the story you heard.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Our Family, where does the child live, and with whom?
**Answer:** In one house, with their parents.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three kinds of room the house riddle names.
**Answer:** A room for sleeping, a room for eating, and a room for receiving guests.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happened to Kaleab in Two Friends, and who helped him?
**Answer:** He fell during the jumping contest; the school health club gave him first aid.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which months does ክረምት hold?
**Answer:** ሰኔ, ሐምሌ, ነሐሴ and ጳጉሜ.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two things the good neighbour Ato Amha does.
**Answer:** Any two of: visits the sick, helps the needy, reconciles quarrels.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In Siblings Who Share the Work, what do the siblings do when guests arrive?
**Answer:** They serve the guests properly, having finished the shared housework together.
**7. (Sub lesson 7)** In The Goat and the Leopard, what stands between the two animals, and what does the story say of the one who breaks it?
**Answer:** An oath — ቃለመሐላ. The story tells what befalls an oath-breaker; an oath is a promise that binds.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Give one point from each side of the traditional-medicine debate between Seyid and Siraj.
**Answer:** From the story's debate: where traditional medicine helps, and where it can harm — one point for each side, in the listener's own words.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Say Abebe Bikila's biography in order: where he was born, and his two great wins with their times.
**Answer:** Born at Jato, 30 Nehase 1925 E.C.; Rome — won barefoot, 2:15:16.2; Tokyo 1964 — won again, 2:12:11.2.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** In the guide's first-person life story, name two things the narrator achieved.
**Answer:** Any two of: won a school speaking competition; became vice-president of the student association; writes poems (one begins «የማታ እንጀራ ስጠኝ»).

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** From Man Is for Work: what is the difference between merely working and አውቆ መስራት?
**Answer:** አውቆ መስራት is knowing how to work — working with knowledge, not busy hands alone.

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** Name the three cultural dresses the guide's listening tells apart.
**Answer:** The ሽብሽባ ቀሚስ, the ጉርድ ቀሚስ and the ጉንፋ ቀሚስ.

**13. (Sub lesson 13)** Say the five steps of a traditional mediation, in order.
**Answer:** (1) Preliminary inquiry — finding out what happened; (2) obtaining the two sides' consent to reconcile; (3) choosing mediators for both sides; (4) choosing the mediation judge; (5) conducting the mediation. The parties then swear an oath confirming the decision.

**14. (Sub lesson 14)** From the assistive-devices table: which device does a blind walker feel ahead on the ground, and which device writes the Braille dots?
**Answer:** The white cane — ኬን — is felt ahead on the ground; the stylus — ስታይለስ — writes the dots on Braille paper.

**15. (Sub lesson 15)** Name malaria's two parasites, and say which is the dangerous one.
**Answer:** Plasmodium falciparum — the dangerous one — and P. vivax, which hides in the body and relapses. The female Anopheles mosquito carries them.

**16. (Sub lesson 16)** In the Abdisa Aga listening, what two kinds of work do the learners do with the biography after hearing it?
**Answer:** They extract detailed information from it in order; and they work its exercises — defining words from the text and building sentences with them, and matching causes to their effects.

**17. (Sub lesson 17)** List the first three of the seven strategies of effective listening.
**Answer:** (1) Decide to listen — listening is a choice; (2) drop your own agenda and prejudice; (3) be curious about how others see the world, and ask open questions.

**18. (Sub lesson 18)** From the drug-consequences listening: name two costs of drug use that the guide keys.
**Answer:** Any two of: the health-care costs of users; the productivity (work) users lose; and, when use spreads, crime and pressure on safety and order.

**19. (Sub lesson 20)** Say the four words the book teaches with The Clever Hare, and what each means.
**Answer:** ሾለከች — she slipped away; ወጥመድ — a trap; ችኩል — hurried; እግሬ አውጪኝ — “let my leg out!” (Grade 1, pp.76–79).

**20. (Sub lesson 23)** Name the four seasons the listening passage የዓመቱ ወቅቶች teaches.
**Answer:** ክረምት (rainy), በጋ (dry), ጸደይ and በልግ (Grade 2, pp.20–21).

**21. (Sub lesson 25)** Which dance does the Grade 2 listening lesson እስክስታ teach, and how does it move?
**Answer:** Eskista, the shoulder dance — the shoulders rise and fall, men's and women's styles differing (Grade 2, p.81).

**22. (Sub lesson 26)** Name two rare Ethiopian wild animals from the listening story at Grade 2 p.139.
**Answer:** The walia (ዋልያ) and the red fox (ቀይ ቀበሮ).

**23. (Sub lesson 20)** What do Sara and Mohammed's follow-up questions ask about?
**Answer:** Plants and trees, and which plants we eat (Grade 1, p.73).

**24. (Sub lesson 21)** Why must children not play ball on the road, in ኳሳችን's world?
**Answer:** Cars pass there; the ball belongs on the field, not the road (Grade 1, p.87).

**25. (Sub lesson 21)** Who is ሯጯ, the runner, about?
**Answer:** The athlete Aynalem — her results, and the flag and anthem at her victory (Grade 1, p.94).

**26. (Sub lesson 22)** What does the polluted-river story's title say happened to the river?
**Answer:** It was polluted — የተበከለው ወንዝ (Grade 1, p.114).

**27. (Sub lesson 22)** In The Dog and the Rooster, what does the rooster give the dog?
**Answer:** Gold (Grade 1, p.134).

**28. (Sub lesson 23)** Name the festival clothes in Qenubsh at the Genna Festival.
**Answer:** ኩታ, ጥልፍ, ጃኖ (Grade 2, p.6).

**29. (Sub lesson 24)** What does the bird in The Bird's Complaint find in the fouled neighbourhood?
**Answer:** No clean resting place (Grade 2, p.62).

**30. (Sub lesson 24)** In Cleanliness for Health, name two of the book's words.
**Answer:** ሳሙና (soap), ጉድጓድ, ማቃጠል, መሸፈን — any two (Grade 2, p.58).

**31. (Sub lesson 25)** Say 'excuse me / forgiveness' and 'thank you' the way Zehara's book teaches.
**Answer:** ይቅርታ — አመሰግናለሁ (Grade 2, p.87).

**32. (Sub lesson 26)** What does ታዳሚ mean, from Radio and Television?
**Answer:** The audience — those who listen and watch (Grade 2, p.119).

**33. (Sub lesson 33)** Which families does the listening ask you to name?
**Answer:** Local families admired as good examples of spacing.

**34. (Sub lesson 34)** What must your talk answer about the widow?
**Answer:** Where she sat before entering the new house, and the sad thing found at her old house.

**35. (Sub lesson 35)** What organiser orders the making-safe of water?
**Answer:** A sequence organiser — first / then / next / at last.

**36. (Sub lesson 36)** Why could the heroine not live in agreement with people, in the story?
**Answer:** Her anger kept her at odds with people — the temper the bee parallel names.

**37. (Sub lesson 37)** Which age group does the book say is most hit by addiction, and why?
**Answer:** Adolescents and youths — they take up habits as signs of maturity and fear losing friends' love.

**38. (Sub lesson 38)** Name three questions the table asks.
**Answer:** Any three of: his birth; his father's killing and his age then; where he went; the invasion year and his age; his governorship; his sentence.

**39. (Sub lesson 39)** What five things does the listening ask about the mosque?
**Answer:** Its building, its century, what is in and around it, its festival times, and its owner (with two benefits).

**40. (Sub lesson 40)** What gap would open without iddir, in the text's question?
**Answer:** A gap in social relations — members would face death, loss and hardship unaccompanied.

**41. (Sub lesson 41)** Why does the mother laugh when the narrator receives a gift, in the story's telling?
**Answer:** Because the gift matter had come out on the giver in childhood like measles — the family knows the pattern the gift belongs to.

**42. (Sub lesson 42)** What does አዋጅ mean in the king's part of the story?
**Answer:** A proclamation — the king's announced award/order.

**43. (Sub lesson 43)** Explain 'my death is your death'.
**Answer:** When the trees die, the air, shade and rain they guarded die with them — people's lives are bound to the trees' lives.

**44. (Sub lesson 44)** Name two benefits of forests from the hearing.
**Answer:** Any two of: cleaning man-made polluted air, cooling the earth, holding biodiversity resources, protecting soil and water.

**45. (Sub lesson 45)** In the volunteer's life story, what did she search for after returning from America, and what did she do when she did not find it?
**Answer:** She searched for a school able to serve children affected by autism; finding none, she began serving her community herself.

**46. (Sub lesson 46)** What is the Amharic term both guides use for blind learners in these notes?
**Answer:** አይነ ስውራን.

**47. (Sub lesson 47)** Name the two sources the Grade 8 guide prints for its heritage texts.
**Answer:** The Ethiopia Tourism book and the former Grade 11 student book.

**48. (Sub lesson 48)** What do learners do with the marriage types after hearing the Zeyse listening text?
**Answer:** They classify them — sorting the regular traditional marriage from the chapter's other forms, against the guide's key.

**49. (Sub lesson 49)** In «አንዱ ለሌላው ምንድን ነው?», how are unity and diversity presented?
**Answer:** As twin concepts — seemingly opposite, but belonging together.
