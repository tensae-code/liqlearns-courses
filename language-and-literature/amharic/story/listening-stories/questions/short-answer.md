# Listening Stories — short answer

Answer in a short sentence, from the story you heard.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Our Family, where does the child live, and with whom?
**Answer:** In one house, with their parents.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three kinds of room the house riddle names.
**Answer:** A room for sleeping, a room for eating, and a room for receiving guests.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happened to Kaleab in Two Friends, and who helped him?
**Answer:** He fell during the jumping contest; the school health club gave him first aid.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which months does ክረምት hold?
**Answer:** ሰኔ, ሐምሌ, ነሐሴ and ጳጉሜ.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name two things the good neighbour Ato Amha does.
**Answer:** Any two of: visits the sick, helps the needy, reconciles quarrels.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** In Siblings Who Share the Work, what do the siblings do when guests arrive?
**Answer:** They serve the guests properly, having finished the shared housework together.
**7. (Sub lesson 7)** In The Goat and the Leopard, what stands between the two animals, and what does the story say of the one who breaks it?
**Answer:** An oath — ቃለመሐላ. The story tells what befalls an oath-breaker; an oath is a promise that binds.

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Give one point from each side of the traditional-medicine debate between Seyid and Siraj.
**Answer:** From the story's debate: where traditional medicine helps, and where it can harm — one point for each side, in the listener's own words.

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Say Abebe Bikila's biography in order: where he was born, and his two great wins with their times.
**Answer:** Born at Jato, 30 Nehase 1925 E.C.; Rome — won barefoot, 2:15:16.2; Tokyo 1964 — won again, 2:12:11.2.

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** In the guide's first-person life story, name two things the narrator achieved.
**Answer:** Any two of: won a school speaking competition; became vice-president of the student association; writes poems (one begins «የማታ እንጀራ ስጠኝ»).

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** From Man Is for Work: what is the difference between merely working and አውቆ መስራት?
**Answer:** አውቆ መስራት is knowing how to work — working with knowledge, not busy hands alone.

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** Name the three cultural dresses the guide's listening tells apart.
**Answer:** The ሽብሽባ ቀሚስ, the ጉርድ ቀሚስ and the ጉንፋ ቀሚስ.

**13. (Sub lesson 13)** Say the five steps of a traditional mediation, in order.
**Answer:** (1) Preliminary inquiry — finding out what happened; (2) obtaining the two sides' consent to reconcile; (3) choosing mediators for both sides; (4) choosing the mediation judge; (5) conducting the mediation. The parties then swear an oath confirming the decision.

**14. (Sub lesson 14)** From the assistive-devices table: which device does a blind walker feel ahead on the ground, and which device writes the Braille dots?
**Answer:** The white cane — ኬን — is felt ahead on the ground; the stylus — ስታይለስ — writes the dots on Braille paper.

**15. (Sub lesson 15)** Name malaria's two parasites, and say which is the dangerous one.
**Answer:** Plasmodium falciparum — the dangerous one — and P. vivax, which hides in the body and relapses. The female Anopheles mosquito carries them.

**16. (Sub lesson 16)** In the Abdisa Aga listening, what two kinds of work do the learners do with the biography after hearing it?
**Answer:** They extract detailed information from it in order; and they work its exercises — defining words from the text and building sentences with them, and matching causes to their effects.

**17. (Sub lesson 17)** List the first three of the seven strategies of effective listening.
**Answer:** (1) Decide to listen — listening is a choice; (2) drop your own agenda and prejudice; (3) be curious about how others see the world, and ask open questions.

**18. (Sub lesson 18)** From the drug-consequences listening: name two costs of drug use that the guide keys.
**Answer:** Any two of: the health-care costs of users; the productivity (work) users lose; and, when use spreads, crime and pressure on safety and order.
