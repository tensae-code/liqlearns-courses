# Listening Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the Our Family story say the parents do in the evening?
a) They go to market  b) They tell stories  c) They mend clothes
**Answer: b) They tell stories** — ማታ ማታ, evening by evening.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the answer to the riddle እኔ ማን ነኝ?
a) A school  b) A house — ቤት  c) A tree
**Answer: b) A house — ቤት** — rooms to sleep, eat and receive guests, and people live inside it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why did Abel miss the first lesson?
a) He was late and the school gate was closed  b) He was ill  c) He went to market
**Answer: a)** — he arrived after the gate closed.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Which of these is made from leather (ቆዳ)?
a) ጋቢ  b) ኮት, a coat  c) ነጠላ
**Answer: b) ኮት** — ጋቢ and ነጠላ are cotton.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What did the doctor order Abel to do?
a) Eat no fruit  b) Brush his teeth morning and evening  c) Miss school for a week
**Answer: b)** — ጠዋትና ማታ, morning and evening, or the tooth would be lost.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Which of these is a green area?
a) An asphalt road  b) A public park  c) A stone plaza
**Answer: b) A public park** — green areas are land not covered by asphalt or stone.
**7. (Sub lesson 7)** In The Loyal Friend, what does Ayalew face when his words reach the king?
a) A fine  b) A death sentence  c) Exile from the town
**Answer: b)** — the king sentences him to death, and the story turns on his plea and his friend's loyalty.

**8. (Sub lesson 7)** In A Friend Is Like a Mirror, why is Seada nicknamed የዉኃ እናት?
a) She carries water for the village  b) She loves washing and advises Abdu to wash  c) She lives beside a river
**Answer: b)** — "the mother of water": she advises Abdu, who hates washing, and he changes.

**9. (Sub lesson 8)** At the Qoti betrothal ceremony, who sits in honour with the Sultan?
a) The elders (ሽማግሌዎች)  b) The children  c) The merchants
**Answer: a)** — the elders take their part with the gifts, the አደስ incense and the ceremony foods.

**10. (Sub lesson 8)** In The Lion and the Hyenas, how is the kill divided?
a) The hyenas take nine, the lion one  b) All share equally  c) The lion takes nine, the hyenas one
**Answer: c)** — nine hyenas together took one buffalo; the one lion took nine.

**11. (Sub lesson 9)** What pattern is heard described on the bride's ነጠላ in The Bride and Groom?
a) ጥለት  b) A stripe of gold thread only  c) Flowers
**Answer: a)** — the listening describes the ጥለት on her ነጠላ, word by word.

**12. (Sub lesson 9)** How did Abebe Bikila run the Rome marathon?
a) In new shoes  b) Barefoot  c) Carrying the flag
**Answer: b)** — he won at Rome barefoot, in 2:15:16.2.

**13. (Sub lesson 10)** In The Wonderful Mountains, what cry do the listeners remember?
a) "ፍሬን ያዝ! ፍሬን ያዝ!"  b) "ዝም በል!"  c) "ትንሽ ቆይ!"
**Answer: a)** — "Hold the fruit! Hold the fruit!" — the heard text itself is not printed; its questions are.

**14. (Sub lesson 10)** In The Unsuspecting One Got Ensnared, what were the children made to do?
a) Herd cattle  b) Beg  c) Carry water
**Answer: b)** — moved from place to place and made to beg; the story says their childhood was stolen.

**15. (Sub lesson 11)** In The Expert's Advice, what does Molalign the farmer hear?
a) A weather song  b) Cause-and-effect counsel from an agricultural expert  c) A market price list
**Answer: b)** — the listening is worked through a cause-and-effect organiser.

**16. (Sub lesson 11)** What name was Dr Catherine Hamlin given?
a) የኢትዮጵያ እናቶች እናት — the mother of Ethiopia's mothers  b) The desert doctor  c) The queen of hospitals
**Answer: a)** — for her fistula hospitals and her life's work for injured mothers.

**17. (Sub lesson 12)** In The Reading Children, where are the two boys lost?
a) In a market  b) In the deep forest, gone hunting  c) On a river
**Answer: b)** — the king's son and the poor woman's son slip away to hunt and are lost in the deep forest.

**18. (Sub lesson 12)** In the city-farming story, what do the young people do with their surplus?
a) Give it all away  b) Save it in a bank, and teach their neighbours  c) Buy a car
**Answer: b)** — day labour, then የከተማ ግብርና: they save the surplus and teach others.
