# Listening Stories — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Family at home
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell both family stories and answer what Ato Nuradin looks like and what he does, from the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The house riddle and the carpenter
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: guess the riddle's answer from its clues before it is given, and answer the carpenter story's prediction question in your own words.

## Sub lesson 3 — School and friends
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what happens when Abel is late, what the teacher is expected to say, and how the jumping story ends — care while playing.

## Sub lesson 4 — Clothes, seasons and a park diary
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the three clothing materials, match each season to its months, and retell Marta's park visit in order.

## Sub lesson 5 — Teeth, neighbours and a mother
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say why Abel's teeth hurt and what the doctor ordered; say what good neighbours do; and retell the mother's story with care, in your own words.

## Sub lesson 6 — Green areas and work shared at home
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the kinds of green area and one benefit of each; and say who should do housework — everyone, by ability, not by gender.
