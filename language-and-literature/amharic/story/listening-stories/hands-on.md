# Hands-on — Listening Stories

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the riddle
Stand in your own home (or classroom corner) and give the house
riddle as clues, one room at a time, the way GIF 8333 does: "I
have a place where we sleep…" Let your partner guess after each
clue. Then swap — your partner picks another thing at home and
riddles you.

## Retell with stones
Four stones in a row for one heard story: who — where — what
happened — how it ends. Point at each stone as you retell the
Late Student or Two Friends. If you get stuck, the stones say
where you are.

## Brush like Abel learned
For one week, brush morning and evening — ጠዋትና ማታ — and each
time say the doctor's order from Abel's story in your own words.
Mark each brushing on paper with a sun or a moon.

## Share the work at home
Pick one house job this week that is usually done by someone
else in your house, and do it yourself — the siblings' story and
the housework story both say work is shared by ability. Tell
your companion which job you took.

## If it is hard
Hear the story twice before you must retell it; retell with the
story stones and the lesson's pictures in front of you; answer
the prediction question with two choices given, not from
nothing. Hearing-impaired learners are given the listening text
to read and answer in writing, and blind learners answer orally
— the guides' own inclusion directions, kept here as help, never
as a label on the child.
