# Video script — Grade 3 More Stories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is our chapter. **ታሪኮች** — stories.

More tales: cows who stand together, a clever man and a hyena, a lion asking a dangerous question — and the games readers play with story words.

Listen first. Then read — twice, the fluent way.
Answer from what you heard and read. Nothing else.

Now the words. Say them with me: አራት ላሞች — ዝሆን — አንበሳ — ወፍ.

Retell one story. First… then… last.
Write your sentences. End each one with ።.

You did it. Teach one thing from this chapter at home tonight.
