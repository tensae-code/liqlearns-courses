# Grade 3 More Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens to the cows after they separate?
a) They drive off the tiger  b) They are eaten one by one  c) They find a new field
**Answer: b) They are eaten one by one** — Apart, the tiger eats them one by one.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** According to the wisest tree, who is the real enemy?
a) The axe  b) The bent trees who become its handle  c) The wind
**Answer: b) The bent trees who become its handle** — The handle comes from the bent trees among them.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the mice dig a hole by the wedding tree?
a) To store food  b) As an escape, suspecting a trick  c) To sleep in
**Answer: b) As an escape, suspecting a trick** — They dig an escape hole, suspecting a trick — and use it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the son do when his father falls?
a) Runs away  b) Quotes his father's own rule back to him  c) Lifts him at once
**Answer: b) Quotes his father's own rule back to him** — The son quotes the rule back; the father says it doesn't apply to him.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the clever man stall the hyena?
a) He runs  b) He tells him to call all his kin to share the meal  c) He gives him food
**Answer: b) He tells him to call all his kin to share the meal** — Calling all the kin buys the time to escape up the tree.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the monkey escape the lion's question?
a) She flatters him  b) She pleads a cold that blocks her nose  c) She runs fastest
**Answer: b) She pleads a cold that blocks her nose** — A blocked nose means she cannot answer — and she escapes.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What must you judge in የጥንቸል ፍርሃት?
a) Whether the hare's fears were reasonable  b) Whether the hare is fast  c) Whether it will rain
**Answer: a) Whether the hare's fears were reasonable** — The listening asks whether her fears were reasonable.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the bird in ወፍና ሰው have, besides advice?
a) A nest of gold  b) An escape trick  c) A song
**Answer: b) An escape trick** — The bird's escape trick and her advice are the listening's two catches.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Listening tales are heard how many times before retelling, in this lesson's way?
a) Once, then retell from the hearing  b) Never  c) Only in writing
**Answer: a) Once, then retell from the hearing** — Hear it, then retell it in your own words.

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** In the chart for ብልሁ ሰው, what stands on the left?
a) The solutions only  b) Problem + problem  c) The characters' names
**Answer: b) Problem + problem** — Problems on the left, the solution they lead to on the right.

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** In your own lion tale, how many animals do you question?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four
**Answer: c) Four** — Your composed tale questions four animals.

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** A chart for a story shows mainly what?
a) Its bones — problems and their solution  b) Its pictures  c) Its page numbers
**Answer: a) Its bones — problems and their solution** — The chart lays out problems leading to the solution.

**13. (Sub lesson 5)** How many words must you build from the fidel grid?
a) At least five  b) At least ten  c) Two
**Answer: b) At least ten** — At least ten 3–4-letter words from the grid.

**14. (Sub lesson 5)** BINGO here is played with what on the boards?
a) Numbers  b) Story vocabulary  c) Pictures only
**Answer: b) Story vocabulary** — The two boards carry story vocabulary.

**15. (Sub lesson 5)** Joining suffixes is the mirror of what?
a) Splitting them off  b) Reading them  c) Copying them
**Answer: a) Splitting them off** — Joining puts together what splitting took apart.

**16. (Sub lesson 6)** Unit 3 is —
a) Tales — ተረት  b) Road — መንገድ  c) Morals
**Answer: a) Tales — ተረት** — ተረት — tales.

**17. (Sub lesson 6)** ዋና ገፀ ባህሪያት are —
a) the main characters  b) the page numbers  c) the morals only
**Answer: a) the main characters** — Main characters.

**18. (Sub lesson 6)** Part 1 opens with —
a) a test  b) pre-listening questions  c) a song
**Answer: b) pre-listening questions** — ቅድመ ማዳመጥ ጥያቄዎች.
