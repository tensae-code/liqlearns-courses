# Grade 3 More Stories — writing

Write in full sentences, with correct punctuation.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the four cows' story in two sentences: together, then apart.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write why both the honest goat and the flattering dik-dik were eaten, and what that teaches about the lion's question.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell the bird's escape in sequence: first, then, last.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Fill your own problem + problem → solution chart for a tale you know.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Build ten words from letters your teacher gives you, and write them in a row.
