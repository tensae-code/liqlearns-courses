# Why Populations Grow and Shrink

## Do it first

Try the first task in Sub lesson 1 before you read any explanation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Causes of population decline

The Grade 8 demography chapter defines its terms first: a population declines when its count falls below what it was, or fails to rise to the expected level. Then the causes, in the study's order. War — fighting with war weapons; over 1,000 deaths it may be called ጦርነት (war), under 1,000 ግጭት (conflict); the Second World War and Ethiopia's wars are the examples. Epidemics — HIV/AIDS, Ebola, malaria, cholera and COVID-19 — raise deaths and hold back births. Urbanisation and late marriage shorten childbearing time and spread family-planning knowledge, so the birth rate falls. Economic growth can lower growth too, while high birth rates and low growth feed each other in poor areas. Genocide, migration (ፍልሰት), government-led birth-rate reduction and poverty complete the list.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conditions that determine the birth rate

The second reading asks what sets the birth rate (ውልደት መጠን). It is healthy when parents' income matches their children's number. Culture raises it: child marriage (የልጅነት ጋብቻ) — girls married before marriage age bear early, stay long in marriage, and their average birth rate is high; the remedy is educating parents, who then send children to school. The attitude counting childbearing as a blessing (ፀጋ) raises it too — a son to ward off attack, a daughter to bring an in-law (አማች) who is an 'iron gate' (ብረት መዝጊያ). Religion can count birth control as breaking its law, and continuous bearing harms mothers' health and children's raising. The solution the text gives: awareness and training, so mothers can space births (አራርቆ መውለድ) — bearing in measure protects children's health and growth.
