# Why Populations Grow and Shrink — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the text, fighting with over 1,000 deaths may be called —
a) ግጭት (conflict)  b) ጦርነት (war)  c) ፍልሰት (migration)
**Answer: b) ጦርነት (war)** — the book's own line: over 1,000 deaths — war; under — conflict.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The text's remedy for child marriage's high birth rate is —
a) earlier marriage  b) educating parents, who then send children to school  c) larger families
**Answer: b) educating parents, who then send children to school** — educating parents is the remedy named — schooled children marry later.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A population decline means its count —
a) doubles  b) falls below what it was, or fails to rise to the expected level  c) stays written the same
**Answer: b) falls below what it was, or fails to rise to the expected level** — the chapter's own definition opens the reading.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A daughter bringing an in-law is figured as bringing —
a) a harvest  b) an iron gate (ብረት መዝጊያ)  c) a debt
**Answer: b) an iron gate (ብረት መዝጊያ)** — the አማች as iron gate — protection married into the family.
