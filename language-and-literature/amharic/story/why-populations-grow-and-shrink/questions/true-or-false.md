# Why Populations Grow and Shrink — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Epidemics both raise deaths and hold back births.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spacing births (አራርቆ መውለድ) is presented as protecting mothers' and children's health.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Urbanisation and late marriage shorten childbearing time.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Continuous bearing is said to harm mothers' health and children's raising.
**Answer: True**
