# Why Populations Grow and Shrink — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three epidemics the text lists.
**Answer:** Any three of: HIV/AIDS, Ebola, malaria, cholera, COVID-19.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is an አማች called in the text's saying?
**Answer:** ብረት መዝጊያ — an iron gate (the in-law as protection).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two push factors the text groups with poverty.
**Answer:** Unemployment and lack of options (with economic crisis) — the text's economic causes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What attitude counts childbearing as ፀጋ?
**Answer:** Counting childbearing as a blessing/grace — a son against attack, a daughter for an in-law.
