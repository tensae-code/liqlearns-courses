# Lesson plan — Why Populations Grow and Shrink

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Story · **Lesson:** Why Populations Grow and Shrink
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a population falls when deaths, epidemics, late marriage, city life, migration and poverty outrun births — and rises or steadies when births are spaced and mothers educated.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the reading lessons — the learner can read an expository text and follow its causes in order.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Causes of population decline** — war, epidemics, city life and late marriage, migration and poverty
2. **Conditions that determine the birth rate** — child marriage, blessing attitudes, religion — and the remedy of spacing

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
