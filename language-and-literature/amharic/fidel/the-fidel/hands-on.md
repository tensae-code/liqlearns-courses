# Hands-on — The Fidel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fidel in the air and on the ground
Write a big letter in the air with your whole arm — በ first, then
ሰ. Then draw it big on the ground outside with a stick, or in
flour or sand spread on a tray at home. Big first, small later:
the hand learns the shape at body size before paper size.

## The family line
Write one family on seven stones, seven bottle caps, or seven
scraps of paper — one form on each. Mix them up. Now put the
family back in order: በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ. Read the line aloud when
it stands right. Do a new family every day.

## First-sound hunt at home
Walk around your home. Find a thing, say its name, and say the
fidel its name starts with: ጀበና — ጀ. መኪና — መ. Touch the thing
as you say it. Ten things. If you find a word for a form you
learned in Sub lesson 5 (a word starting with ቡ, or with ሺ),
shout it out — those are the rare ones.

## Build-a-word stones
Write single fidel on stones or caps — በ ለ ጠ ሰ ሸ ዳ ቦ. Build
words by pushing the stones together: በ+ለ+ጠ = በለጠ. Read every
word you build out loud. A word nobody can read gets taken apart
and built again.

## If it is hard
Help for ears and eyes that mix similar letters up — written as
help, never as a label on the child (support from Ethiopia's own
Special Needs toolkit for pre-primary schools).

- **Listen and point (ለ / ረ / ጠ / ተ):** some fidel sound close —
  ለ and ረ, ጠ and ተ. A grown-up or the audio slot says one sound;
  the child points to the letter they hear, choosing between just
  two big letter cards at a time. Ears first, eyes second. Two
  cards, many turns, no score kept.
- **Look-alike forms:** put the two mixed-up family cards side by
  side. Read both rows aloud slowly, pointing. Say what is
  different about the shapes — where the tail sits, which way it
  turns. Then cover one row and write it from memory.
- **Small steps:** a whole family at once is a lot. Learn three
  forms, say them, get them checked, then the next three, then
  the last one. A check after each small step, not one big test
  at the end.
- **See it, say it, touch it:** every letter is met three ways at
  once — the picture card to see, the spoken sound to hear, and
  the air-tracing and stone-writing to feel. Keep all three
  together; that triple is itself the support.
