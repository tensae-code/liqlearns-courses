# Lesson plan — The Fidel

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Fidel · **Lesson:** The Fidel
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fidel (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the fidel (ፊደል) — Amharic's
letters: every letter lives in a family (ቤተሰብ, beteseb) of seven
forms, and words are built by joining those forms.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the child's
own world and a ready hand — the child can hold a pencil and
follow a dotted line (Ready Hands — Before the Pencil, Unit 1).
The chart, the families and first reading all grow inside this
one lesson, step by step.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The fidel chart and the first letters** — the whole chart (የፊደል ገበታ) as one big table the child can see at once, then the first letters one at a time: colour the big letter, join the dots to make it, and meet its keyword picture (በ with a sheep በግ, ሰ with a clock ሰዓት…). The second-pass letters are coloured and copy-written.
2. **The families — seven forms of one letter** — every letter's family (በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ): read the family aloud in order, copy-write the family, fill the family member that is missing. All 33 families.
3. **First sounds, matching and the odd one out** — using the families: circle or write the first fidel of a picture's name, join the same forms to each other, and find the one fidel in a row that is different.
4. **First reading — words made of learned letters** — short words built only from letters already learned, read aloud slowly, then whole (በሰበሰ, ዳቦ…).
5. **A word for every form** — each form of a family starts its own word (በ–በር, ቡ–ቡና, ቢ–ቢራቢሮ…): read the family, then read its seven words.
6. **Building words from fidel** — join fidel together to make words (በ+ለ+ጠ = በለጠ), build a word from a given fidel, and fill the missing fidel inside a whole word.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ready Hands — Before the Pencil (Unit 1 —
  First Steps) — the hand holds the pencil and follows a dotted
  line before it is asked to trace letters. Recommended, never
  forced.
- Nothing else is needed: this lesson starts from the chart
  itself and the child's own spoken Amharic.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Find any letter on the fidel chart and say its sound.
2. Say and write the seven forms of a family in order.
3. Fill the missing form inside a family.
4. Give the first fidel of a pictured word's name.
5. Find the odd fidel out in a row of one family's forms.
6. Read short words built only from learned letters.
7. Say a word that starts with each form of the በ, ሰ and ሸ
   families.
8. Join fidel together to build a word, and fill a missing
   fidel inside a word.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. The chart picture (Picture 7950) where the
child can see it. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up two forms of one family → go back to the family card:
  read the row aloud slowly, in order, pointing at each form;
  the order of the row is the memory hook. Then cover the row
  and write it from memory.
- Cannot hear the difference between ለ / ረ or ጠ / ተ → use the
  minimal-pairs AUDIO slot and the listen-and-point games in
  hands-on.md ("If it is hard") — ears first, eyes second; never
  mark the child, only practise the pair again.
- Reads a word letter by letter and loses it → read it the
  book's way: slowly, syllable by syllable, then whole, twice;
  the whole word is the goal, the slow pass is the road.
- Finds a family too long to copy → copy three forms, say them,
  then the next three, then the last one — small steps, a check
  after each step.
