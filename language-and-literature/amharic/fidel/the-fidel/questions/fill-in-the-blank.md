# The Fidel — fill in the blank

Write the missing fidel.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** በ ቡ _ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ — the missing form of the በ family is ___.
**Answer: ቢ** — the third form, between ቡ and ባ.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ሰ ሱ ሲ ሳ _ ስ ሶ — the missing form of the ሰ family is ___.
**Answer: ሴ** — the fifth form, between ሳ and ስ.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** The word for coffee starts with ቡ: ___ና.
**Answer: ቡና** — ቡ + ና; ቡ is the second form of the በ family.

**4. (Sub lesson 6)** ዛ_ is the word for tree — the missing fidel is ___.
**Answer: ፍ** — ዛፍ (zaf, tree).

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** ተ_ሪ is the word for student — the missing fidel is ___.
**Answer: ማ** — ተማሪ (temari, student).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** _በቶ is the word for belt — the missing first fidel is ___.
**Answer: ቀ** — ቀበቶ (qebeto, belt).

**7. (Sub lesson 8)** አ_ት — the missing fidel is a -ዋ form of ለ. The missing fidel is ___.
**Answer: ሏ** — አሏት; ሏ is ለ with the ዋ tail.

**8. (Sub lesson 9)** _ንቋ is the word for language — the missing first fidel is ___.
**Answer: ቋ** — ቋንቋ; ቋ is the fourth form of the derived ቈ family.
