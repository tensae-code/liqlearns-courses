# The Fidel — matching

Match each item on the left to the one it belongs with on the right.

**Set 1. (Sub lesson 1) Keyword picture — its starting letter**

1. sheep, በግ — b
2. clock, ሰዓት — a
3. lion, አንበሳ — d
4. bell, ደወል — c

a) ሰ  b) በ  c) ደ  d) አ

**Answer key:** 1 — b) በ · 2 — a) ሰ · 3 — d) አ · 4 — c) ደ.

**Set 2. (Sub lesson 3) The same form — find its twin**

1. ቢ — c
2. ሱ — a
3. ላ — d
4. ዝ — b

a) ሱ  b) ዝ  c) ቢ  d) ላ

**Answer key:** 1 — c) ቢ · 2 — a) ሱ · 3 — d) ላ · 4 — b) ዝ.

**Set 3. (Sub lesson 5) Form of the በ family — the word that starts with it**

1. በ — d
2. ቡ — a
3. ቢ — c
4. ቤ — b

a) ቡና (buna, coffee)  b) ቤት (bet, house)  c) ቢራቢሮ (birabiro, butterfly)  d) በር (ber, door)

**Answer key:** 1 — d) በር · 2 — a) ቡና · 3 — c) ቢራቢሮ · 4 — b) ቤት.

**Set 4. (Sub lesson 7) Family — its keyword word on the teaching road**

1. ገ — c
2. ቀ — a

**Set 5. (Sub lesson 9) Derived family — a word that starts with it**
