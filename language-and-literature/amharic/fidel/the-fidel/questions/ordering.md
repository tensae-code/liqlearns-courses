# The Fidel — ordering

Put the forms in their family order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the ከ family in order: ኬ ከ ኮ ኩ ክ ካ ኪ.
**Answer: ከ ኩ ኪ ካ ኬ ክ ኮ.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the ለ family in order: ሎ ለ ሌ ሊ ል ሉ ላ.
**Answer: ለ ሉ ሊ ላ ሌ ል ሎ.**

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Join the fidel in order to make the word they build: ጠ, ለ, በ (first በ, then ለ, then ጠ).
**Answer: በለጠ** — በ + ለ + ጠ, joined in that order.

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** Put these families in the book's teaching order: በ, ሰ, ሀ, ለ.
**Answer: ሀ, ለ, ሰ, በ** — the road runs ሀ first, then ለ; ሰ comes after ረ; በ comes after ቀ.

**5. (Sub lesson 9)** Put the ቈ family in order: ቋ, ቈ, ቌ, ቊ, ቍ.
**Answer: ቈ ቊ ቍ ቋ ቌ** — the family's five forms, in the book's order.
