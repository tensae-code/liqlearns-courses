# The Fidel — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Every letter on the fidel chart has seven forms in its family.
**Answer: True** — each row of the chart is one letter with its seven forms.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** In the row ሸ ሹ ሺ ሻ ሼ ሽ ሾ, the form ሱ belongs to the family.
**Answer: False** — ሱ belongs to the ሰ family; the ሸ family's third form is ሺ.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The word ሸበሸበ can be read using only the letters በ, ሰ and ሸ.
**Answer: True** — it is built from ሸ, በ and ሸ alone, fidel by fidel.

**4. (Sub lesson 8)** ሏ is the -ዋ (labialised) form of ለ.
**Answer: True** — ለ with the ዋ tail is read ሏ; it stands first in the book's first -ዋ set.

**5. (Sub lesson 9)** The derived families ቈ ኈ ኰ ጐ have seven forms each, like the families on the main chart.
**Answer: False** — as the book prints them, each has five forms (ቈ ቊ ቍ ቋ ቌ…).
