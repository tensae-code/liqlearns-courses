# The Fidel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which letter does በግ (beg, sheep) start with?
a) ሰ  b) በ  c) ሸ
**Answer: b) በ** — the sheep is በ's keyword picture.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which picture's name starts with ሰ?
a) the clock, ሰዓት  b) the lion, አንበሳ  c) the bell, ደወል
**Answer: a) the clock, ሰዓት** — አንበሳ starts with አ and ደወል with ደ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which row is the whole በ family, in order?
a) በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ  b) በ ቢ ቡ ባ ቤ ቦ ብ  c) በ ቡ ቢ ባ ሰ ብ ቦ
**Answer: a) በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ** — seven forms of በ, in the chart's order; row c) smuggles in ሰ, which belongs to another family.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the row አ ኡ ኢ በ ኤ እ ኦ, one form does not belong to the አ family. The odd one out is —
a) ኢ  b) ኤ  c) በ
**Answer: c) በ** — every other form belongs to the አ family; በ lives in its own row.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Which word is built only from በ, ሰ and ሸ?
a) በሰበሰ  b) ከለከለ  c) ዳቦ
**Answer: a) በሰበሰ** — ከለከለ needs ከ and ለ; ዳቦ needs ዳ and ቦ.

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** In the ሰ family, which form starts the word ሲኒ (sini, small cup)?
a) ሰ  b) ሲ  c) ሱ
**Answer: b) ሲ** — ሰ starts ሰዓት and ሱ starts ሱሪ.
