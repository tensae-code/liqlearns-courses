# The Fidel — writing

Write with your pencil. (A teacher or the app checks the writing against the chart.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copy-write the letter አ three times, big and slow, joining the dots in your mind the way the trace GIF showed for በ and ሰ. Then write it once from memory.
**Answer: አ አ አ** — checked against Picture 7950 (the chart) and the አ family card (Picture 7957).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Copy-write the word ዳቦ (dabo, bread) twice, saying each fidel as you write it, then read the whole word aloud.
**Answer: ዳቦ** — ዳ + ቦ; checked against the lesson's reading list.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Build a word that holds the fidel ሹ, and write it. Then build one that holds ኮ.
**Answer: any true word the chart supports, e.g. ሹካ (shuka, fork) for ሹ** — checked form by form against the chart; the word must really hold the given fidel.

**4. (Sub lesson 7)** Write the ሐ family from memory, in order. Then write the family that the book teaches right after it.
**Answer: ሐ ሑ ሒ ሓ ሔ ሕ ሖ, then መ ሙ ሚ ማ ሜ ም ሞ** — checked against Picture 8250 (the chart in teaching order).

**5. (Sub lesson 8)** Copy-write the first -ዋ set twice: ሏ ሷ ሟ ሿ ሯ ቧ. Say each form aloud as you write it.
**Answer: ሏ ሷ ሟ ሿ ሯ ቧ** — checked against Picture 8251; each form carries the ዋ tail.

**6. (Sub lesson 9)** Write the word ቋንቋ, then write the word ጐላ. Read both aloud.
**Answer: ቋንቋ · ጐላ** — checked against Picture 8252; ቋ is from the ቈ family and ጐ heads its own family.
