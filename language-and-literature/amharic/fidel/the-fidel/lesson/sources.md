Honesty notes. (1) The chart picture (7950) is a drawn render of the standard fidel table — the same table all three workbooks print in their front matter — checked letter by letter against the placements' family list (ሀ ለ ሐ መ ሠ ረ ሰ ሸ ቀ በ ተ ቸ ኀ ነ ኘ አ ከ ኸ ወ ዐ ዘ ዠ የ ደ ጀ ገ ጠ ጨ ጰ ጸ ፀ ፈ ፐ, each with its seven forms) before filing; it is a render, not a scan of any book page. (2) The bell word is printed ደወል (dewel) in Book 1 (printed p.62) and ደውል in Book 3's family word set (printed p.60); the lesson keeps each book's own spelling in its own sub lesson (Sub lesson 1: ደወል; Sub lesson 5: ደውል) and invents neither. (3) Book 3's word-per-form sets name five of the seven forms for the በ family and five for the ሰ and ሸ families (the placements list them with "…"); the lesson teaches only the forms the placements name and turns the rest into a spoken hunt, never inventing the missing words. (4) Book 1's ከ (sweets), ኸ (baby), ለ (football), ዠ and ጰ entries name a keyword picture but no Amharic keyword word in the placements; the lesson names the picture and the letter only, exactly as placed. (5) The KG3 fidel strand's sentences and stories items (Book 3, printed pp.16, 18, 68, 118–119, 142, 146–147) are NOT in this lesson — they belong to the Sentence and Story titles, as the build brief rules; only the strand's word-building, family-word, fill-missing and match items are here. (6) The confusable-sounds audio slot rests on SUPPORTING teacher evidence only (Book 4) and is labelled as such wherever it appears; it teaches no new letters. (7) The generated keyword boards (Pictures 7986/7987) carry no text, as required; on board 7986 the ሸሚዝ (shirt) panel shows folded clothes with a visible collar — it reads as folded clothing rather than a single shirt, and is described that way in the build log.

(8) Book 5 prints the ቸ family row with ቡ in the second place (item 23, printed pp.21–22) — the placements flag it ("read ቹ"); Sub lesson 7 teaches the corrected ቹ, as the placements direct. (9) Three families on the teaching road — ኸ, ጰ and ቨ — are given no keyword word anywhere in the Book 5 placements; Sub lesson 7 reads those families aloud with no keyword word rather than inventing one. The keyword word ሀበል for the ሀ family comes from the Grade 1 teacher guide's worked segmenting table (ሀ+በ+ል = ሀበል), not from Book 5 itself, and the table above says so. (10) Pictures 8250–8252 and GIFs 8253–8254 are drawn renders made for this build, checked letter by letter against the placements' family lists before filing; they are renders, not scans of book pages. GIF 8253's join (ለ + ዋ → ሏ) is a drawn model of the labialised build; the book itself prints the finished forms and their words, cited above.
