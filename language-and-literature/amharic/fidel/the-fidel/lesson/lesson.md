## Sub lesson 2 — The families — seven forms of one letter

Here is the secret of the whole chart: **one letter is not one shape. One letter is a family** — ቤተሰብ (beteseb, family) — **of seven forms.** The letter stays itself; its ending changes, and each ending makes a new sound.

Read the first family aloud, slowly, in order: **በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ** — ba, bu, bi, baa, be, b, bo. Hear how the start stays በ's sound and the tail changes? That is every family on the chart.

Last step in this sub lesson: **the missing member.** A family is written with one form left out — በ ቡ _ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ. Which one is missing? ቢ. Do it for every family: the form's place in the row tells you its name. And when you can, do it the big way: from one letter alone, write out its whole family — በ, and then all seven.

## Sub lesson 3 — First sounds, matching and the odd one out

Now use the families.

**First sounds.** A word starts with one fidel — the first sound you hear. Look at a picture: a sheep, በግ. The first fidel is በ. Circle it, then later write it yourself. Try the ሰ family's pictures the same way: look, hear the first sound, write the first fidel.

**Matching.** The same form hides in two places — join them with a line. Join the same fidel in the በ family; match a picture to its first letter (ሰ, ሸ, በ — which picture starts with which?); join letters to their pictures by line — አ ለ ዠ ዘ, then ጰ ደ ጸ ጀ, then ሐ ጠ ጨ. Slow looking wins this game: form by form, not by guessing.

**The odd one out.** Here is a row of the አ family with one stranger hiding in it: አ ኡ ኢ **በ** ኤ እ ኦ. One of these does not belong to the family. Which one? በ — it lives in another row of the chart. Circle the different one, every time: that is how your eye learns each family's true face.

## Sub lesson 4 — First reading — words made of learned letters

Letters join, and joined letters make words. These first words are built **only** from letters you have already learned — that is why you can read them today.

Read slowly first, fidel by fidel, then whole:

- በሰበሰ · ሰበሰበ · ሸበሸበ — built from በ, ሰ and ሸ alone.
- ከለከለ · አሰበ · ከለለ · ለበሰ — from the next letters.
- Then: በሶ · ባሻ · ሽቦ · ሰባ · ሸሸ · ኪስ · ካባ · ኬክ · ክብ · ኩስ · ክክ · ኮባ.
- And words you can also copy-write: በላ · ሸሸ · ዳቦ (dabo, bread) · ቡላ.
- Longer ones, from more letters: ቀበጠ · አኘከ · አበጀ · ከበደ · ተነነ · ቸከለ.
- The big reading list: አለ · ተወ · በተነ · ሰጠ · ቀረ · ሰቀለ · ሰበረ · በሰለ · ሸለመ · አሰበ · ሸመተ · ከተበ · ጀመረ · ለበሰ · አለቀ.

Say each word out loud. Reading aloud is reading twice — once with the eyes, once with the ears.

## Sub lesson 5 — A word for every form

A family is not only shapes — **every form starts its own words.** Read the family, then read the word that begins with each form.

**በ family:** በ — በር (ber, door) · ቡ — ቡና (buna, coffee) · ቢ — ቢራቢሮ (birabiro, butterfly) · ባ — ባልዲ (baldi, bucket) · ቤ — ቤት (bet, house).

**ሰ family:** ሰ — ሰዓት (se'at, clock) · ሱ — ሱሪ (suri, trousers) · ሲ — ሲኒ (sini, small cup) · ሳ — ሳፋ (safa) · ሴ — ሴት (set, woman).

**ሸ family:** ሸ — ሸሚዝ (shemiz, shirt) · ሹ — ሹካ (shuka, fork) · ሺ — ሺበሺ (shibeshi) · ሻ — ሻማ (shama) · ሼ — ሼኪ (sheki).

More families carry a word set the same way — one word to find for each form: the ለ family (ለ — ለምለም, lemlam), the አ family (አ — አበባ, abeba, flower), the ከ family (ከ — ከበሮ, kebero, drum), and then ዘ, ዠ, ደ (ደ — ደውል, bell), ጀ (ጀ — ጀበና, jebena) and ጸ (ጸ — ጸሎት, tselot, prayer). For these, play it as a hunt: say the form, find a word in your home or street that starts with it.

## Sub lesson 6 — Building words from fidel

Last step: **you** build the words.

**Join fidel to make a word.** Take the fidel in the box and join them: በ + ለ + ጠ = **በለጠ**. The word was hiding in the pieces.

**Build from a given fidel.** You are given one fidel — ቢ, ሹ, ሊ, እ, ኮ — build a whole word that holds it. Or given መ, ሞ, ሳ — what words can you make? Or ወ, መ, ሐ, ው?

**Words in tables.** Build and read: ተ — ተማሪ (temari, student) · ቸ — ቸኮሌት (chokolet, chocolate) · ገ — ገመድ (gemed, rope) · ቱ — ቱታ (tuta) · ቹ — ቹቹ (chuchu) · ጉ — ጉንዳን (gundan, ant) · ነ — ነብር (nebr, tiger) · ኘ — ዋኛ (waña, swimmer) · ኀ — ኀፍረት (ḥafret, shame) · ኑ — ኑኑ (nunu) · ኙ — ጎበኙ (gobeñu) · ቀ — ቀበቶ (qebeto, belt) · የ — የኔ (yene, mine).

**Fill the missing fidel inside a word.** A word is written with one fidel gone — ዛ_ for ዛፍ (zaf, tree). The missing fidel hides in the box of choices; the word itself tells you which one it needs. Work these: ዛፍ (tree) · ፍሬ (fire, fruit) · ግንብ (ginb) · ስንዴ (sinde) · ቅጠል (q'itel, leaf). And inside whole family grids too — the ተ ቸ ገ families, the ነ ኘ ኀ families, then ቀ የ ፐ and ሐ ጠ ጨ, and at the end ወ ፈ ረ መ ሠ ዐ ፀ: the missing one always has a home row, and the row tells you its name.

Match the same forms to each other one last time, and match the same words to their twins: ዘንቢል (zenbil) · ላጲስ (lapis) · ጅብ (jib, hyena) · ዣንጥላ (zhanṭila, umbrella) · ዶማ (doma). If you can build it, match it and read it — the fidel are yours.

## Say it to your companion

Read one whole family aloud to your companion — all seven forms, in order, no stopping. Then give a word for three of its forms. Then build a word from loose fidel while your companion watches, and let your companion read your word aloud. Swap: your companion builds, you read.

## Do It Yourself

1. Put the chart picture where you eat or sleep. Every day, point to one row and read its family aloud before you do anything else.
2. Hunt fidel at home: on a coffee jar, a calendar, a shop sign, a book cover — find five fidel in the wild and write down which family each one belongs to.
3. Write the በ family and the ሰ family from memory. Check them against the chart. Fix only the form that is wrong, and write that one three times.
4. Build three words of your own from fidel you know. Write each word, read it aloud, and say what it means.
5. Teach one family to someone at home — read it, let them read it, and tell them one word for its first form.
