# Video script — The Fidel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the
lesson the whole Amharic course stands on, so the script is full:
the chart, one family, and one built word, all read aloud.

---

Look. This is the chart. የፊደል ገበታ.
Many letters. Do not fear. We go slow.

This is **በ** (ba). በ is for በግ — a sheep.
This is **ሰ** (sa). ሰ is for ሰዓት — a clock.
This is **ሸ** (sha). ሸ is for ሸሚዝ — a shirt.
This is **አ** (a). አ is for አንበሳ — a lion.

Now the secret. One letter has seven shapes.
One letter, one family. ቤተሰብ.

**በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ.**
Say it with me. በ. ቡ. ቢ. ባ. ቤ. ብ. ቦ.
Again. Slow. በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ.
You read a family!

One form is missing. በ ቡ — ባ ቤ ብ ቦ.
What is missing? … **ቢ**! Yes.

Now we build a word.
**በ** + **ለ** + **ጠ** = **በለጠ**.
Three letters. One word. You built it.

One more. **ዳቦ** — bread. ዳቦ.
Read it slow. Then read it whole. ዳቦ.

Now you. Point to a letter. Say its sound.
Read its family. Build a word.
That is the fidel.

---

Now the whole road. The book's own order.
Not the chart's order. The teaching order.

**ሀ** first. ሀ ሁ ሂ ሃ ሄ ህ ሆ. ሀበል.
Then **ለ**. ለ ሉ ሊ ላ ሌ ል ሎ. ለማ.
Then **ሐ**. ሐ ሑ ሒ ሓ ሔ ሕ ሖ. ሕመም.

On and on. Family by family.
**መ** — መላ. **ረ** — ሬት. **ሰ** — ሰላ. **ሸ** — ሻማ.
**ቀ** — ቀበቶ. **በ** — በራ. **ተ** — ተማሪ.
**ነ** — ንብ. **አ** — አወቀ. **ከ** — ከበሮ.
**ወ** — ወርቅ. **ዘ** — ዘር. **የ** — ዮሐንስ.
**ደ** — ዱር. **ገ** — ገንቦ. **ጠ** — ጣና.
**ጨ** — ጫማ. **ጸ** — ጸጉር. **ፈ** — ፊደል.
Last, **ቨ**. Read its family twice.

Walk the road slow. When no family stops you,
the whole chart is yours.

---

Some letters carry a little tail. **ዋ**.
ለ with the tail is **ሏ**. The sound rounds: wa.

Three sets. Read them with me.
ሏ ሷ ሟ ሿ ሯ ቧ — አሏት. ቧልት.
ቷ ኗ ኟ ዟ ዧ ቿ — ላቷ. አባቶች.
ዷ ጇ ጧ ጯ ፏ — እጇ. ፏፏቴ.

Watch. ለ and ዋ come together. **ሏ**.
Now the long words, slow, then whole:
አሯሯጯ. ተጧጧፈ. ባለሟል.

---

Four small families come last.
Five forms each. Not seven. Five.

**ቈ** — ቈ ቊ ቍ ቋ ቌ. ቋንቋ — a language!
**ኈ** — ኈ ኊ ኍ ኋ ኌ. ኋለኛ.
**ኰ** — ኰ ኵ ኲ ኳ ኴ. ኰራ.
**ጐ** — ጐ ጕ ጒ ጓ ጔ. ጕልበት.

Now the chart is truly complete.
Thirty-three families. The ዋ forms. These four.
Every letter the book teaches. Yours.
