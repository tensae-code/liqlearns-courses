# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል

## Do it first

Here are three fidel on three cards: **ገ · በ · ረ**. Push the cards together in your mind until they touch. Now read what they make — one word, in one go: **ገበረ**.

Now pull the word apart again: ገበረ → ገ · በ · ረ.

You just did the two moves this whole lesson is about. Joining is **ማባመር** (blending). Pulling apart is **መነጠል** (splitting). Every word you will ever read is built — and can be unbuilt — this way.

## Sub lesson 1 — Blending fidel into words (ማባመር)

**ማባመር** means: join the fidel a word is built from, and read the word in one go. Not letter… letter… letter — one smooth word.

Watch the core move:

[GIF 8260 — ገ + በ + ረ slide together into ገበረ, hold, then split apart again]

Now work the blending table. Read the parts, push them together, read the word:

[Picture 8258 — the blending table]

- ገ + በ + ረ = **ገበረ**
- ወ + ሰ + ደ = **ወሰደ**
- ከ + በ + በ = **ከበበ**
- ተ + ገ + ኝ = **ተገኝ**
- ጨ + መ + ረ = **ጨመረ**
- ሀ + በ + ል = **ሀበል**

Longer words blend the same way, piece by piece: ቅጠረ · ረጠበ · በረት · ተመለከተ. Blend on the standard ruled line (መደበኛ መስመር), the way the class does — the line holds the fidel while your voice joins them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Splitting words into fidel (መነጠል)

**መነጠል** is the road back: split a word into its fidel, one by one. Splitting is how you read a word you have never seen, and how you write a word you have only heard.

[Picture 8259 — the splitting table]

- ወፈረ → ወ · ፈ · ረ
- ከበረ → ከ · በ · ረ
- ገበረ → ገ · በ · ረ
- ዋንጨ → ዋ · ን · ጨ
- ኈኅት → ኈ · ኅ · ት

Split, then blend back. Split ዋንጨ: ዋ · ን · ጨ. Blend it: ዋንጨ. A word you can split and re-join is a word you own.

## Sub lesson 3 — Take away the first fidel, make a new word

Here is a word game with sharp edges: **take away the first fidel** of a word, and what is left is a new word. Put a different fidel in its place, and you have a third word. These five words were dictated for writing first — write them, then play the game with each one:

- ተዘጋ — take away ተ → **ዜጋ** — put አ in its place → **አዘጋ**
- ተማረ — take away ተ → **ማረ** — put አ in its place → **አማረ**
- አወጣ — take away አ → **ወጣ** — put ተ in its place → **ተወጣ**
- ጠላት — take away ጠ → **ላት** — put አ in its place → **አላት**
- ኩረት — take away ኩ → **ሬት** — put አ in its place → **አረት**

Watch the first one happen:

[GIF 8261 — ተ lifts away from ተዘጋ leaving ዜጋ, then አ takes its place: አዘጋ]

The missing fidel can always be replaced by another one — that is the rule of the game. One word becomes three, and you built two of them yourself.

## Sub lesson 4 — Move the fidel, make a new word

Now shuffle instead of taking away: **move a word's fidel to new places** (የፊደላትን ቦታ በመቀየር) and a new word grows. Work this table — given word, fidel moved, new word built:

- ሰበሰበ → በሰሰበ → **ሰበሰበች**
- ረዘመ → መረዘ → **ተመረዘ**
- ሰላጠ → ሳለጠ → **ሳለጣው**
- ዶማ → ማደ → **የማደ**
- ቀጠቀጠ → ጠቀጠቀ → **ጠቀጠቀው**
- ደለበ → ዴለበ → **የደለበ**

These are examples — other moves and other built words are possible. The skill is not memorising this table; it is seeing that a word's fidel are pieces you are allowed to move.

## Sub lesson 5 — Sorting by first and last sounds, and clap the letters

Last, sort words **by their sounds**.

**Same first sound:** ከበበ · ከበረ · ከበደ · ከደነ — every one starts with ከ. Words that start with ጠ: ጠቡ · ጠባ · ጠጠ. Words that begin with ቅ, as printed: ቅሌ · ጭልፉ · ዌጋና.

**By last sound:** words ending with ረ: ቀረ · ናረ · ማረ. Words ending with ኘ: ዋርኘ · ሰበኘ · በረኘ. Words ending with ገ: አገኘ · ተመኘ · ተገኘ. And a mixed set with different ending sounds to sort out: ሰበኘ · በለል · ዘገየ · በቋል · ሴረዘ.

[Picture 8262 — sorting words by their sounds, clap the letters, words by fidel count]

**Find the middle.** A three-fidel word has a middle fidel: ከበበ — the middle is በ.

**Clap the letters.** A three-fidel word gets three claps — one clap per fidel: ጨጨት — clap, clap, clap. Count the fidel of every word this way before you sort it.

**Sort by how many fidel.** ባለ 2 ፊደል (two-fidel words): ስራ · ፈራ · ደራ · ጣራ · ሰጠ. ባለ 3 ፊደል: እስራ · ስራች · በለጠ · ባለገ · ጠብቆ. ባለ 4 ፊደል — a word built from four fidel: ወንበር · እበደረ · አጣራች · ወስደች · ተራበች.

**Rhyme to finish.** Some word sets sing: መናጩ · ዋናጩ · ቂናጩ. Chant them like music — rhyming words are sorting by sound, done by ear.

## Sub lesson 7 — The Grade 4 book's own method page: icons, and ማጣመር/መነጠል

The Grade 4 book opens by explaining its own way of working. Every task wears a small picture mark — an icon — that names the skill before you begin: listening, speaking, reading, writing, or grammar. Look at the icon first, and you know how the task wants to be done.

The same front pages state the fidel method in the book's own two words. **ማጣመር** — blending: read a word's parts, then blend them into the whole word. **መነጠል** — splitting: read the whole word, then split it into its fidel units. You have done both through this whole lesson — ገ + በ + ረ blended into ገበረ, and ገበረ split back into ገ በ ረ. The Grade 4 book names the same two moves at its front, because every chapter that follows leans on them.

## Say it to your companion

Blend three fidel your companion names, and read the word in one go. Then split a word your companion says, fidel by fidel. Take away its first fidel and say the new word. Finish together: clap the letters of ጨጨት — three claps — and chant መናጩ, ዋናጩ, ቂናጩ.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write ገበረ, ወሰደ and ከበበ. Under each, write its fidel split apart. Then cover the split row and blend each word aloud.
2. Play take-away with ተማረ and ኩረት: write the word, cross out the first fidel, write the new word, then put አ in front and write the third word.
3. Move the fidel of ዶማ and ደለበ the way the table does, and write the words you build.
4. Clap-count five words at home — a name, a food, a place — one clap per fidel, and write how many fidel each one has.
5. Sort six words from your day into two piles: same first sound, different first sounds. Read each pile aloud.
