# Lesson plan — Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Fidel · **Lesson:** Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fidel (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ማባመርና መነጠል — blending fidel into words and segmenting words back into fidel, Ethiopia's core phonics mechanic, with its word games: take away the first fidel, move the fidel, sort by sounds, clap the letters.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Fidel (the learner reads the families) — blending is what the families are for.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Blending fidel into words** — ማባመር defined and worked: ገ+በ+ረ = ገበረ and the guide's blend tables, on the standard line.
2. **Splitting words into fidel** — መነጠል defined and worked: ወፈረ, ከበረ, ገበረ, ዋንጨ split fidel by fidel, then re-blended.
3. **Take away the first fidel, make a new word** — the dictated five (ተዘጋ ተማረ አወጣ ጠላት ኩረት) and the guide's answer table: remove the first fidel, then replace it.
4. **Move the fidel, make a new word** — rearranging a word's fidel positions (ሰበሰበ → በሰሰበ → ሰበሰበች and the guide's table).
5. **Sorting by first and last sounds, and clap the letters** — same-start and different-end sorts, the middle fidel, clapping one clap per fidel (ጨጨት = 3), sorting words by fidel count, and rhyme sets.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Fidel (Unit 2) and Fidel Shapes —
  Letters With Legs (Unit 2). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what ማባመር and መነጠል mean, in their own words.
2. Blend three given fidel into a word and read it in one go.
3. Split a three-fidel word into its fidel and blend it back.
4. Take away a word's first fidel to make a new word, then replace it to make a third.
5. Rearrange a word's fidel to build a new word.
6. Sort words by first sound and by last sound, find a word's middle fidel, and clap-count a word's fidel.
7. Sort words into 2-, 3- and 4-fidel groups.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Three loose cards or stones for the fidel
ገ በ ረ. The blending table (Picture 8258) where the child can
see it. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads letter by letter and loses the word → go back to GIF
  8260: push real cards together slowly while saying the sounds
  without gaps — the blend is a slide, not three stops.
- Splits a word but cannot re-blend it → split on paper with a
  space between fidel, then rub out one space at a time while
  reading, until the word stands whole again.
- Take-away game confuses → keep the three words in a row on
  paper (ተዘጋ · ዜጋ · አዘጋ) and point: the middle word is the
  first word with its head off; the third is the middle word
  with a new head on.
- Loses count when clapping → lay one stone per fidel as you
  clap; the stones hold the count.
