# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል — ordering

Put the items in order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** These fidel are out of order. Put them in the order that blends into ከበበ: በ, ከ, በ (the word starts with ከ).
**Answer: ከ, በ, በ** — ከ + በ + በ = ከበበ.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Put the guide's row in its order: the built word ሰበሰበች, the given word ሰበሰበ, the moved word በሰሰበ.
**Answer: ሰበሰበ → በሰሰበ → ሰበሰበች** — given word first, fidel moved second, new word built last.
