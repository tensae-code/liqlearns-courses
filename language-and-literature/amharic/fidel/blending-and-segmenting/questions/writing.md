# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል — writing

Write with your pencil. (A teacher or the app checks the writing against the lesson's tables.)

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the word አወጣ. Cross out its first fidel and write the word that is left. Then put ተ in the empty place and write the third word.
**Answer: አወጣ → ወጣ → ተወጣ** — checked against the guide's take-away table (Picture/GIF 8261 shows the same move for ተዘጋ).

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** Write these words and sort them into fidel-count groups: ስራ, በለጠ, ወንበር. Write how many fidel each word has.
**Answer: ስራ — 2 fidel (ባለ 2 ፊደል) · በለጠ — 3 fidel (ባለ 3 ፊደል) · ወንበር — 4 fidel (ባለ 4 ፊደል)** — checked against Picture 8262.
