# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ገ + በ + ረ blended in one go makes —
a) ገበ  b) ገበረ  c) በረገ
**Answer: b) ገበረ** — join the three fidel in order and read the word whole.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Splitting ከበረ into its fidel gives —
a) ከ በ ረ  b) ከበ ረ  c) ከ በረ
**Answer: a) ከ በ ረ** — መነጠል splits a word into its single fidel, one by one.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Take away the first fidel of ጠላት and you get —
a) ጠላ  b) ላት  c) አላት
**Answer: b) ላት** — አላት is what you get when you then put አ in the empty place.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the guide's table, moving the fidel of ዶማ builds —
a) ማደ  b) ዶማች  c) አዶም
**Answer: a) ማደ** — ዶማ → ማደ → የማደ, as the table prints it.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which word starts with the same sound as ከበበ and ከበረ?
a) ሰበኘ  b) ከደነ  c) በለል
**Answer: b) ከደነ** — all three start with ከ; ሰበኘ starts with ሰ and በለል with በ.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** ቦቢ ቀረ። is read in which blending set?
a) The ቀ/በ set, p.20  b) The ዐ/ዘ/ዠ set, p.39  c) The labialised set
**Answer: a) The ቀ/በ set, p.20** — Grade 1 p.20.

**7. (Sub lesson 6)** ዕርቅ and ዥግራ belong to which families' blending?
a) ዐ/ዘ/ዠ  b) የ/ደ/ጀ  c) ቀ/በ
**Answer: a) ዐ/ዘ/ዠ** — Grade 1 p.39.

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The p.71 drill splits which kind of words?
a) The stories' long words  b) Only two-fidel words  c) Names only
**Answer: a) The stories' long words** — እንደምናሸንፍ and its brothers, Grade 1 p.71.

**9. (Sub lesson 7)** In the Grade 4 book, what does a task's icon tell you?
a) The page number  b) The skill the task trains — listening, speaking, reading, writing or grammar  c) The answer
**Answer: b) The skill the task trains** — Look at the icon first, and you know how the task wants to be done.

**10. (Sub lesson 7)** ማጣመር means…?
a) Splitting a word into fidel units  b) Blending — reading a word's parts and joining them into the whole word  c) Copying a word
**Answer: b) Blending** — ማጣመር blends the parts; መነጠል splits the whole.

**11. (Sub lesson 7)** መነጠል on ገበረ gives…?
a) ገበረ whole only  b) ገ በ ረ — its fidel units  c) በገረ
**Answer: b) ገ በ ረ** — Splitting reads the whole word into its fidel units, one by one.

**12. (Sub lesson 8)** Three-letter words in the checklist are called —
a) ባሉስት ሆሄ  b) ተዛውታሪ ቃላት  c) የምዕራፉ ማጠቃለያ
**Answer: a) ባሉስት ሆሄ** — ባሉስት ሆሄ — three-letter words.

**13. (Sub lesson 8)** Fidel are practised —
a) in one place only  b) on slate, blackboard, coloured paper and cards  c) never aloud
**Answer: b) on slate, blackboard, coloured paper and cards** — Many surfaces, and read aloud.

**14. (Sub lesson 8)** This page is —
a) the guide's first page  b) the last page of the secured file (printed p.35)  c) from Unit 10
**Answer: b) the last page of the secured file (printed p.35)** — Printed p.35 — the file ends here.

**15. (Sub lesson 9)** ምእላድ is —
a) a paragraph  b) a syllable  c) a punctuation mark
**Answer: b) a syllable**
