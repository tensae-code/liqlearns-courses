# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** When you take away a word's first fidel, the empty place can never be filled by another fidel.
**Answer: False** — the guide's rule is the opposite: the missing fidel may be replaced by another (ተዘጋ → ዜጋ → አዘጋ).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** The rearrange table's built words are the only words that can ever be built from those fidel.
**Answer: False** — the guide says the table gives examples only; other moves and other built words are possible.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** ጨጨት gets three claps because it is built from three fidel.
**Answer: True** — one clap per fidel: ጨ · ጨ · ት.
