# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል — fill in the blank

Write the missing fidel or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ወ + ሰ + ___ = ወሰደ — the missing fidel is ___.
**Answer: ደ** — blend ወ, ሰ and ደ in order and read ወሰደ whole.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ዋንጨ splits into ዋ · ___ · ጨ — the missing middle fidel is ___.
**Answer: ን** — ዋ ን ጨ, one fidel at a time.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ተማረ — take away the first fidel and the new word is ___.
**Answer: ማረ** — then put አ in its place and you get አማረ.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** The middle fidel of ከበበ is ___.
**Answer: በ** — ከ (first), በ (middle), በ (last).
