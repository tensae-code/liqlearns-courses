# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል — matching

Match each item on the left to the one it belongs with on the right.

**Set 1. (Sub lesson 2) Word — its fidel, split**

1. ወፈረ — c
2. ከበረ — a
3. ዋንጨ — b

a) ከ በ ረ  b) ዋ ን ጨ  c) ወ ፈ ረ

**Answer key:** 1 — c) ወ ፈ ረ · 2 — a) ከ በ ረ · 3 — b) ዋ ን ጨ.

**Set 2. (Sub lesson 4) Given word — the new word built by moving its fidel**

1. ረዘመ — b
2. ሰላጠ — c
3. ደለበ — a

a) የደለበ  b) ተመረዘ  c) ሳለጣው

**Answer key:** 1 — b) ተመረዘ · 2 — c) ሳለጣው · 3 — a) የደለበ.
