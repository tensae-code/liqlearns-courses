# Hands-on — Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Card pushing
Write ገ, በ and ረ on three stones, caps or scraps of paper.
Push them together slowly, saying the sounds without gaps,
until they touch: ገበረ. Pull them apart and do it again with
ወ ሰ ደ and ከ በ በ. The slide of the hands teaches the slide
of the voice.

## Take-away game with cards
Write ተዘጋ on three cards. Lift the ተ card away and read what
is left: ዜጋ. Put an አ card in its place: አዘጋ. Play it with
ተማረ, አወጣ, ጠላት and ኩረት. A partner checks each new word by
reading it aloud.

## Shuffle words
Write ዶማ on two cards. Swap their places and read the new
word: ማደ. Try ደለበ on three cards — move one fidel and read
what you built. A move that makes no word gets moved again.

## Clap the letters
Say a word and clap once for every fidel: ጨጨት — three claps.
Clap the names of people at home, foods, and places. Write the
number beside each word and sort your words: 2-fidel pile,
3-fidel pile, 4-fidel pile.

## If it is hard
Help for learners who lose the word while blending — written as
help, never as a label on the child.

- **No-gap sounds:** say the fidel sounds as one slide (ገበረ
  with no stops), pushing the cards at the same speed as the
  voice. Stops in the hands make stops in the voice.
- **Stones hold the count:** one stone per fidel when counting
  or clapping, so a lost count can be re-counted from the
  stones, not from memory.
- **Small words first:** master 2-fidel splits before 3-fidel,
  and 3-fidel before 4-fidel words like ተመለከተ.
