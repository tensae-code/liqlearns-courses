# Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb the mechanic itself:
blend, split, take away, move, sort.

## Sub lesson 1 — Blending fidel into words
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: blend ገ+በ+ረ and two more table rows into words read in one go, and say what ማባመር means.

## Sub lesson 2 — Splitting words into fidel
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: split ወፈረ, ከበረ and ዋንጨ into their fidel in order, and blend each back into its word.

## Sub lesson 3 — Take away the first fidel, make a new word
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: for at least three of the five dictated words, give the word left after removing the first fidel and the word made by replacing it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Move the fidel, make a new word
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: work one row of the rearrange table (given word → moved → built word) and say that other moves are possible.

## Sub lesson 5 — Sorting by first and last sounds, and clap the letters
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pick the words that start with a named sound from a mixed set, name the middle fidel of ከበበ, clap-count a three-fidel word, and sort words into 2-, 3- and 4-fidel groups.
