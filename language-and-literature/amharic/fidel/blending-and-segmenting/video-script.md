# Video script — Blending and Splitting — ማባመርና መነጠል

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three cards. **ገ**. **በ**. **ረ**.
Push them together. Slow… slow… together!
**ገበረ**. One word. That is ማባመር — blending.

Now pull them apart. ገ… በ… ረ.
That is መነጠል — splitting.

Again, with a new word. **ወ** + **ሰ** + **ደ** = **ወሰደ**.
You blend it. Say it whole. ወሰደ!

Now a game. **ተዘጋ**. Take away the first one — ተ goes!
**ዜጋ**. A new word!
Put አ in its place. **አዘጋ**. Another new word! You made two words from one.

Clap with me. **ጨጨት** — 👏 👏 👏. Three letters, three claps.

Now you. Blend it. Split it. Take one away. Clap it.
The words are yours.
