# Hands-on — Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four houses
Draw four big houses on paper, or mark four corners of the floor
with stones. Name them: one leg, two legs, three legs, no legs.
Write fidel on scraps of paper and carry each one to its house.
Read the whole house aloud when it is full.

## Legs with your body
Stand like a letter: one foot down and arms still — one leg.
Two feet apart — two legs. Two feet and one hand touching the
ground — three legs. Sit down round — no legs. A partner guesses
which group you are.

## Look-alike detective
Write a look-alike pair big — ሀ and ሐ. Circle the one stroke
that is different. Do the same for ሰ/ሠ, አ/ዐ and ጸ/ፀ. Then hide
one letter inside a word and let a partner find it and name its
family by its keyword name.

## If it is hard
Help for eyes that mix similar shapes up — written as help, never
as a label on the child.

- **One shape at a time:** sort single cards, never whole rows.
  One card, one house, one check — then the next card.
- **Touch the legs:** trace the letter's legs with a finger as
  you count them aloud. The count comes from the finger, not
  from memory.
- **Pairs before crowds:** for look-alikes, work with just two
  families side by side until they stay told apart, then add
  one more pair.
