# Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ጠ stands on three legs (ባለ ሦስት እግር).
**Answer: True** — ጠ heads the three-legged set, with ጨ and ሐ.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ሸ can be found in only one leg group, because a family has only one shape.
**Answer: False** — as the guide prints it, ሸ stands in both the one-legged and the two-legged sets; the form in front of you is what you sort, since a family's seven forms do not all stand the same way.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The keyword name of the ሀ family is ሀሌታው.
**Answer: True** — ሀ–ሀሌታው, as the Grade 2 guide's table prints it.
