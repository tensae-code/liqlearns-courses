# Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs — matching

Match each item on the left to the one it belongs with on the right.

**Set 1. (Sub lesson 1) Fidel — its leg group**

1. ተ — b
2. ለ — a
3. ጨ — d
4. ወ — c

a) ባለ ሁለት እግር (two legs)  b) ባለ አንድ እግር (one leg)  c) እግር አልባ (no legs)  d) ባለ ሦስት እግር (three legs)

**Answer key:** 1 — b) one leg · 2 — a) two legs · 3 — d) three legs · 4 — c) no legs.

**Set 2. (Sub lesson 2) Family — its keyword family name**

1. ሐ — c
2. ዐ — a

4. ፀ — b

a) ዐይኑ  b) ፃፍቁ  c) ሐመሩ  d) ጸሎቱ
