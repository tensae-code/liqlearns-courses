# Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs — fill in the blank

Write the missing word or name.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fidel with no legs at all is called እግር ___. 
**Answer: አልባ** — እግር አልባ, the legless group (ሠ መ ወ ዐ ዑ ሀ ፈ ረ).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The keyword family name of ኘ is ___.
**Answer: ብዝኘኙ** — as the Grade 2 guide's look-alike table prints it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fidel built from two joined parts, like ሏ and ቋ, are called ___ ፊደላት.
**Answer: ውሰብሰብ** — ውሰብሰብ ፊደላት, the compound fidel.
