# Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which group does ቀ stand in?
a) ባለ ሁለት እግር (two legs)  b) ባለ አንድ እግር (one leg)  c) እግር አልባ (no legs)
**Answer: b) ባለ አንድ እግር** — ቀ heads the guide's one-legged set.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which letter is legless (እግር አልባ)?
a) መ  b) ጠ  c) ተ
**Answer: a) መ** — ጠ has three legs and ተ has one.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the keyword family name of ሰ?
a) ኛንው  b) እሳቱ  c) አልፋው
**Answer: b) እሳቱ** — ኛንው names ሠ and አልፋው names አ.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A learner keeps mixing up አ and ዐ inside words. What does the guide's way say to do?
a) skip those words  b) compare the two families side by side and learn their keyword names, አልፋው and ዐይኑ  c) read faster so the shapes blur less
**Answer: b)** — look-alike fidel are told apart by their shapes, side by side, with the keyword names as hooks.
