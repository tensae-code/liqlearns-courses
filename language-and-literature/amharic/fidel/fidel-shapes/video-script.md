# Video script — Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at the letters. They stand on legs.

**ቀ** — one leg. ባለ አንድ እግር.
**ለ** — two legs. ባለ ሁለት እግር.
**ጠ** — three legs. ባለ ሦስት እግር.
**መ** — no legs! እግር አልባ. It sits round on the line.

Your turn. **ተ**? … One leg!
**በ**? … Two legs!
**ጨ**? … Three legs!
**ወ**? … No legs!

Now the tricksters. Letters that look the same.
**ሀ** — ሀሌታው. **ሐ** — ሐመሩ. Look at the small stroke. That stroke is the difference.
**ሰ** — እሳቱ. **ሠ** — ኛንው.
**አ** — አልፋው. **ዐ** — ዐይኑ.
**ጸ** — ጸሎቱ. **ፀ** — ፃፍቁ.

Slow looking wins. Name the family, then read the word.
