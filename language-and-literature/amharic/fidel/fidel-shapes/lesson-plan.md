# Lesson plan — Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs

**Course:** Amharic · **Title:** Fidel · **Lesson:** Fidel Shapes — Letters With Legs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Fidel (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fidel shapes — sorting fidel by their "legs" (ባለ አንድ እግር, ባለ ሁለት እግር, ባለ ሦስት እግር, እግር አልባ), and telling the look-alike fidel (ሞክሸ ፊደላት) apart by their shapes and keyword family names.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Fidel (the learner can read the families) and on nothing else — this lesson is the eye-training that goes with the chart.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Sorting by legs** — the four shape groups (one-legged, two-legged, three-legged, legless) with the teacher guide's worked sets; the same family can visit two groups because its forms stand differently.
2. **Look-alike fidel** — ሞክሸ ፊደላት: telling look-alike families apart inside words, the keyword family names (ሀሌታው, ሐመሩ, ብዝኘኙ, ኛንው, እሳቱ, አልፋው, ዐይኑ, ጸሎቱ, ፃፍቁ), and compound fidel (ውሰብሰብ) — read in order, build words with four of them.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Fidel (Unit 2) — the learner reads the
  families before sorting their shapes. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four leg groups in Amharic and English.
2. Sort a given fidel into its leg group by its shape.
3. Explain why ሸ and ሐ can each stand in two groups.
4. Say the keyword family name for ሀ, ሐ, ኘ, ሠ, ሰ, አ, ዐ, ጸ and ፀ.
5. Tell a look-alike pair apart inside a word by its differing stroke.
6. Build a word with a compound fidel and say its meaning.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. The leg-groups chart (Picture 8255) where the
child can see it. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sorts by the family's name instead of the shape in front of
  them → cover the family row; show one form alone on a card and
  ask only: how many legs does THIS one stand on?
- Mixes up a look-alike pair → put the two family cards side by
  side (Picture 8256), read both keyword names aloud, and trace
  the one stroke that differs with a finger before reading on.
- Cannot remember the keyword names → chant them in pairs
  (ሀሌታው–ሐመሩ, እሳቱ–ኛንው…) — the names are memory hooks, not
  tests; the shapes are the lesson.
