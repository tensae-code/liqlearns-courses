Honesty notes. (1) TEACHER GUIDE evidence throughout, SECOND-LANGUAGE throughout. (2) The placements name the two-fidel-word work (Ch.2) and the splitting/joining operation (Ch.6) without quoting a list of example words; the lesson's one worked word is መጣ, which the guide itself works at printed p.145, and the only other words it builds are built by the learner from learned fidels, as the guide directs ("form two-fidel words"). No invented word list is presented as the book's. (3) For commands, the placements name the practice and the guide's media names the acted examples "stand, sit, open the book" (in English); the lesson teaches those three as actions with the spoken commands carried on the audio slot, and claims no further command vocabulary. (4) The placements present singular/plural teaching without quoting example pairs; the lesson teaches the operation and the one/many pointing, and sends the written conversion to the learner's notebook exactly as the guide does — it does not print invented plural pairs as the book's own. (5) The grammar block (printed pp.145–151) has no chapter heading in the book, and the book's numbering later jumps to Chapter 14 with no Chapter 13 — the book's own inconsistency, flagged in the placements. (6) The placements give no publication year for the guide; none is claimed.
