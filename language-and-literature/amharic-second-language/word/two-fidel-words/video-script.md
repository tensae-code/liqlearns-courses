# Video script — Two-Fidel Words (Second Language)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. (The guide names no separate video need for this lesson's chapters — the Ch.1 script pattern covers it; this short script follows that pattern.)

---

Two letters. One word.

**መ** … **ጣ** … listen: **መጣ**.
Apart: መ — ጣ. Together: መጣ. He came.

Again, slower. Slide them together. No gap.
መጣ. Now split them. መ — ጣ. And back. መጣ.

Now — a word that tells you to DO something.
You hear it — you do it. Stand! Sit! Open the book!
Doing it means you understood it.

One more trick. Add **ች**: መጣ**ች**.
He came — she came. One little tail changes the word.

Two fidels. Split them. Join them. Obey them. Change them.
Words are yours now.
