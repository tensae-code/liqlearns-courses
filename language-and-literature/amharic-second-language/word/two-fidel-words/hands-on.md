# Hands-on — Two-Fidel Words (Second Language)

The lesson with your hands, your feet and your notebook, not only your eyes.

## Slide the cards
Write መ on one card and ጣ on another. Lay them apart on the table. Say each fidel. Now slide the cards together until they touch — and say the word: መጣ. Slide them apart again. Together, apart, together — faster each round, the sound never breaking when they join.

## Command chain
With a partner or family: one person gives a command — stand, sit, open the book, close the book, come, go — and the other does it at once, no translating. Five commands, then swap. Doing it fast and right is the win; the guide checks understanding by the action itself.

## One becomes many
Put one stone on the table. Say "one". Now put down five more stones. Say "many". Do the same with cups, sticks, books. Then in your notebook, write a singular word and turn it into its plural, the way the guide's block teaches.

## He came, she came
Write መጣ big in your notebook. Now add ች at its tail: መጣች. Read both aloud. Cover the ች with your thumb — he came. Lift your thumb — she came. Thumb down, thumb up, five times.
