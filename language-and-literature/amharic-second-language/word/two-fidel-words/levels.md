# Two-Fidel Words (Second Language) — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons climb from the whole word, to the word taken apart and rejoined, to words obeyed, to words changed.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two-fidel words
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: spot the two-fidel words among others, read one aloud fidel by fidel then whole, and form one from given fidels.

## Sub lesson 2 — Splitting and joining fidels
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: split a two-fidel word into its two fidels and join it back without a gap in the sound — መነጠልና ማጣመር, both directions.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hear it, do it (commands)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: carry out the three classroom commands (stand, sit, open the book) on hearing them — the doing is the answer.

## Sub lesson 4 — The word changes: -ች and one-and-many
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: write መጣች from መጣ and say what the -ች changed; and turn a given singular word into its plural in the notebook, as the guide's block teaches.
