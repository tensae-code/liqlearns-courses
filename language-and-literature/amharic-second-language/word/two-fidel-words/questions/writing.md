# Two-Fidel Words (Second Language) — writing

Write in your notebook, keeping the line.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write መጣ. Under it, write its two fidels apart, with a space between them. Under that, write the word whole again.
**Answer: መጣ / መ ጣ / መጣ** — whole, split, rejoined (Picture 8586).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Write መጣ, then write it again with the -ች tail added.
**Answer: መጣ → መጣች** — checked against Picture 8587.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The teacher (or companion) gives you a singular word from the lesson's work. Write its plural next to it.
**Answer: the singular word rewritten in its plural form, as taught from the blackboard/flash-card pairs in class** — the guide's own exercise (printed pp.147–148): read after the teacher first, then convert in the notebook.
