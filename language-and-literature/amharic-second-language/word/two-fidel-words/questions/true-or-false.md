# Two-Fidel Words (Second Language) — true or false

Say "true" or "false" out loud, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A two-fidel word can be split in many places.
**Answer: False** — it splits in exactly one place: between its two fidels.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** To show you understood a command, you must first translate it into your own language.
**Answer: False** — you show it by doing it. The action is the answer; no translation is asked.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Adding -ች to a verb can change it from "he" to "she".
**Answer: True** — መጣ → መጣች: "he came" becomes "she came".
