# Two-Fidel Words (Second Language) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** መጣ is a two-fidel word. Its two fidels are —
a) መ and ጠ  b) መ and ጣ  c) ማ and ጣ
**Answer: b) መ and ጣ** — count them: መ … ጣ. Two fidels, one word.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ማጣመር (joining) means —
a) Taking a word apart into its fidels  b) Putting the fidels back together into the word  c) Reading a word silently
**Answer: b)** — መነጠል is the splitting apart; ማጣመር is the joining back.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The teacher gives a command and you carry it out at once. What has the teacher checked?
a) Your handwriting  b) That you understood — the action itself is the check  c) Your singing
**Answer: b)** — ትዕዛዝ መፈጸም: carrying out the command IS the comprehension check.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** መጣች means —
a) "He came"  b) "She came"  c) "They came"
**Answer: b) "She came"** — መጣ ("he came") + -ች turns the word toward the female (ወደ ሴት).

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Chapter 4's listening asks learners to hear a story and then —
a) retell it (ደግመው ይናገራሉ)  b) copy it silently  c) forget it
**Answer: a) retell it (ደግመው ይናገራሉ)**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** The guide's closing notes say grammar should be taught with —
a) rule recitation only  b) practical exercises and practical observation  c) translation lists
**Answer: b) practical exercises and practical observation**
