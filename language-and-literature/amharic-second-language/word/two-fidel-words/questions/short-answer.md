# Two-Fidel Words (Second Language) — short answer

Answer out loud, or by doing the thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Read this word the lesson's way — fidel, fidel, whole word: መጣ.
**Answer: መ … ጣ … መጣ** — two fidels, then the word in one breath ("he came").

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Split መጣ apart, saying each part, then join it back and say the word.
**Answer: መ — ጣ (split) … መጣ (joined)** — መነጠልና ማጣመር, both directions, no gap in the sound when it joins.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** (Do it.) The command is given: **stand up**. Then: **sit down**. Then: **open the book**.
**Answer: the learner stands, sits, and opens the book as each command is heard** — the doing is the answer (the commands ride on the audio slot in the app; a teacher or companion can speak them here).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What single thing was added to መጣ to make መጣች — and what did it change?
**Answer: the fidel ች (the -ች tail)** — it changed the meaning from "he came" to "she came".
