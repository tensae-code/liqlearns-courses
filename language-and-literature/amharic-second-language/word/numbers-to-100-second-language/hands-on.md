# Hands-on — Numbers to 100 (Second Language)

The lesson with real things in your hands, not only numbers on a page.

## Count real things
Gather stones, sticks or bottle caps. Count out ten. Then twenty. Make piles of ten until you reach one hundred things — or as many as your yard gives you. Say each ten aloud as its pile is finished: the tens are the handrails.

## Colour hunt
Walk around your home and yard. Find one thing for each patch on the colour board (Picture 8583) and say its colour in Amharic as you touch it. Wrong colour word? Say it again right — the guide's way is correct-and-repeat, kindly.

## Who is it?
With family: point at yourself and say እኔ. Point at a brother or father: እሱ. At a sister or mother: እሷ. At everyone together: እኛ. Then build the sentences: point, say the pronoun, add መጣ or መጣች — እሱ መጣ! እሷ መጣች!

## Write the tens from memory
Close the chart. Write 0, 10, 20 … 100 in your notebook from memory. Open the chart and check yourself, row by row.
