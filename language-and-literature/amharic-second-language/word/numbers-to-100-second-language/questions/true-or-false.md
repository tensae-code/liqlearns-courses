# Numbers to 100 (Second Language) — true or false

Say "true" or "false" out loud, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** If a learner names a colour wrongly, the guide's way is to scold them so they remember.
**Answer: False** — correct naming is praised and wrong naming is corrected kindly, then said again right — and the teacher brings in still more colours (printed pp.136–137).
