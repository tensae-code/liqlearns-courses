# Numbers to 100 (Second Language) — short answer

Answer out loud, or by doing the thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** (Point at 86 on the chart, Picture 8582.) Read this number aloud — handrail first, then the walk.
**Answer: the learner finds the 80 handrail and walks: 80 … 86, and reads "86" aloud** — the spoken Amharic number rides on the lesson's audio; the chart work is what is checked here.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** (Point at one patch on Picture 8583.) Say this colour in Amharic.
**Answer: the learner names the pointed patch's colour aloud; a wrong naming is corrected kindly and said again right** — the guide's way (printed pp.136–137). The patches are unlabelled on purpose: the word must come from the learner.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Look at Lesson 1's picture (Picture 8570): two children at a school gate. Say what the people in the picture are doing.
**Answer: they are standing at the gate, waving and greeting each other** — any true saying of what the people are doing (የተግባራቸውን ይናገራሉ), in the learner's own sentence.
