# Numbers to 100 (Second Language) — fill in the blank

Say (or write) what belongs in the gap.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 50, 51, __, 53 — which number is missing?
**Answer: 52** — walk the 50s row by ones from its handrail.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** እሱ __ — finish the sentence so it says "He came."
**Answer: መጣ** — እሱ (he) takes መጣ; the -ች form belongs with እሷ.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** እኛ means __.
**Answer: "we"** — the pronoun for yourself together with others (Picture 8584).
