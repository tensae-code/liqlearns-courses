Honesty notes. (1) TEACHER GUIDE evidence throughout, SECOND-LANGUAGE throughout. (2) The placements place the numbers as ከ 0–100 at printed p.67 without quoting Amharic number words; the chart (Picture 8582) therefore teaches the digits 0–100 — the figures Ethiopian schoolbooks print — and the spoken Amharic counting rides on the audio slot. No Amharic number word is printed as the book's. (3) The placements name the colours work (Ch.12) without listing colour words; the colour patches (8583) are deliberately unlabelled, the colour names are carried by the audio slot and corrected in the guide's praise-and-correct manner, and the lesson text names the patches only by their English colour descriptions of what is drawn. (4) The placements name the pronouns by their heading (ተውላጠ ስሞች, printed pp.136–137) without listing each pronoun; the eight taught — እኔ አንተ አንቺ እሱ እሷ እኛ እናንተ እነሱ — are the standard Amharic pronoun set the guide's sentence work uses, and the lesson's example sentences (እሱ መጣ / እሷ መጣች) are built only from words this course itself teaches (መጣ/መጣች, Lesson 3, from printed p.145) under the placements' own direction "sentences formed with pronouns". This is stated here so no one mistakes the set for a quoted list. (5) Chapter 7 also teaches ገ ነ ኘ, ተ ቸ and opposites (ተቃራኒ ቃላት, placement 20); the families are in Lesson 2 of this course, and the opposites set is not placed in this lesson — see the build log. (6) The placements give no publication year for the guide; none is claimed.
