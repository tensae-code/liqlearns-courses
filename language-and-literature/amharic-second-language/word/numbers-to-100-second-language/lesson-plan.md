# Lesson plan — Numbers to 100 (Second Language)

**Course:** Amharic as a Second Language (አማርኛ እንደ ሁለተኛ ቋንቋ) · **Title:** Word · **Lesson:** Numbers to 100 (Second Language)
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Numbers to 100 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the numbers 0–100 in the new language — reading and writing them — with the colours, and the little standing-in words (pronouns, ተውላጠ ስሞች) that let a learner build real sentences.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Fidel for New Speakers and Two-Fidel Words — the learner reads fidels and two-fidel words. The numbers themselves start from zero, exactly as the guide places them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Numbers 0–100** — reading and writing the numbers from 0 to 100 (ከ 0–100 ያሉ ቁጥሮች), row by row on the chart, the tens standing lit at each row's head (Chapter 7).
2. **Colours** — identifying and saying the colours on flash cards; right naming praised, wrong naming corrected, and more colours brought in beyond the printed ones (Chapter 12).
3. **Pronouns and sentences with them** — ተውላጠ ስሞች: reading and writing the pronouns, and reading and writing sentences formed with them; looking at pictures and saying what the people in them are doing (Chapter 12).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Two-Fidel Words — the learner reads and builds words. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read any number from 0 to 100 off the chart, and write a number that is said.
2. Count up a row by ones and jump by tens (0, 10, 20 … 100).
3. Point at a colour and say it — and accept a correction and say it again right.
4. Read and write the pronouns (እኔ, አንተ, አንቺ, እሱ, እሷ, እኛ, እናንተ, እነሱ).
5. Build and read a sentence with a pronoun: እሱ መጣ — እሷ መጣች.

## What you need
A pencil and notebook; some small things to count (stones, sticks, bottle caps); colour cards if you have them.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner counts the fingers of both hands in the new language as far as they can, then finds the number 100 on the chart with a finger (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the chart and how to walk it; colours on flash cards, said aloud; the pronouns and the sentences they build, with the lesson picture used the guide's way: say what the people in it are doing (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD AUDIO: a voice counting 0–100 in Amharic, tens first, then a whole row — the student counts along, then counts alone.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8583]: eight colour patches with no labels — the student points at each patch and says its colour aloud; the voice confirms or corrects.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice naming each colour patch — the student listens after answering, and repeats the corrected ones.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 8584]: the pronouns board (ተውላጠ ስሞች), eight cards with their people marks — the student reads each pronoun and points at who it stands for.

3. **Say it to your companion** — count to 100 together by tens, then by ones up a row; ask your companion a colour and answer theirs; build a pronoun sentence each (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the guide names a video need for this chapter set: "Counting to 100 in Amharic" (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, ordering, fill-in-the-blank, short-answer, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — write the tens from memory; count real things at home; write the pronouns and one sentence with እሷ (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the row on the chart → the tens are the handrails: find the ten that starts your row (it is lit), then walk by ones.
- Mixes the order of digits when writing → say the number aloud first, then write what you said, tens before ones.
- Says a colour wrong → the guide's way: correct it kindly, say it right together, and bring in one more colour — correction here is praise-plus-repair, never scolding.
- Pronoun sentences tangle → build them only from parts already owned: pronoun + መጣ / መጣች (Lesson 3's pair), one swap at a time.
