# Video script — Greetings and Introductions (Second Language)

The super-simple voice, for the videos that ride on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the scripts are the slots' written form until then. The guide names two video needs for this lesson's chapters: "Your first Amharic greetings" (Chapter 1) and "Saying who I am" (Chapter 3). Both scripts are here.

---

## Script 1 — Your first Amharic greetings

Stand up. Yes — you. Stand up tall.

This is a new language. It is okay to feel shy.
Everyone feels shy. We speak anyway. Together.

First, listen. ሰላምታ መለዋወጥ — exchanging greetings.
One voice greets. One voice answers.
Listen again. Now say it with me.

The teacher speaks first. The teacher says who he is.
Then you. You stand. You say who you are.

Say your name. Loud and clear. That is enough.
Well done. Sit down. Tomorrow, we say more.

## Script 2 — Saying who I am

Two friends stand up. A boy. A girl.
He asks. She answers. She asks. He answers.
That is a dialogue — ጭውውት.

The class listens. Quiet. Kind.
Now it is your turn. Stand up.
Say who you are. Say it like you mean it.

Your voice in a new language — that is a brave sound.
ሎታ — confidence. It grows every time you stand.
