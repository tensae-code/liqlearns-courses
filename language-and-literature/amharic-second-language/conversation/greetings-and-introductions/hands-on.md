# Hands-on — Greetings and Introductions (Second Language)

The lesson with your feet on the floor and your voice in the air, not only your eyes on a page.

## Stand and say your name
Stand up at home, the way you would in class. Greet the room. Say your name out loud, clearly, once. Sit down. Do it three times — it gets easier every time, and easier is the point.

## The greeting round
With family or friends, make a circle. The oldest person greets and introduces himself or herself first — the teacher's part. Then go around the circle: each person stands, greets, and says who they are. Anyone who feels shy gets the whole circle speaking with them first, then says it alone.

## Ask and answer
In pairs: one asks, one answers — a tiny dialogue (ጭውውት). Three questions each, then swap sides. The rest of the group listens quietly and kindly, the way the class listens.

## Greet three people
Today, greet three real people in Amharic — at the door, at the shop, on the road. A greeting given is the lesson finished.
