# Greetings and Introductions (Second Language) — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — speaking your first Amharic in front of people: first the meeting and the introduction, then the dialogue where you hold your side of a talk.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meeting and introducing yourself
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner stands, greets, and introduces himself or herself to a teacher figure and to the class — and can say who goes first in the class's way (the teacher) and why shy learners are encouraged, not pushed past.

## Sub lesson 2 — Standing and saying who you are
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner holds both sides of the short dialogue (ጭውውት) — asking and answering — standing, while a partner or the class listens; and can say what the listening side is doing while two others speak.
