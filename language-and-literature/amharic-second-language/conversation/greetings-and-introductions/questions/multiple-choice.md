# Greetings and Introductions (Second Language) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer. (Say your answer out loud — this is a speaking lesson.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the first day, who introduces himself or herself first?
a) The youngest learner  b) The teacher  c) Nobody — everyone speaks at once
**Answer: b) The teacher** — the teacher goes first, so the learners hear how it is done before they stand.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A learner feels shy about standing up and speaking the new language. What does the class's way say about that?
a) Shy learners should skip the turn  b) Shyness is wrong and must be punished  c) Shy learners are encouraged — feeling afraid in a new language is normal, and they speak a little at a time
**Answer: c)** — the guide stresses encouraging shy learners; shyness is where everybody starts.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the dialogue (ጭውውት), the boy and the girl are standing and talking. What is the rest of the class doing?
a) Listening quietly  b) Talking among themselves  c) Writing in their notebooks
**Answer: a) Listening quietly** — listening is the class's job while two learners hold the dialogue.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is ሎታ (lota), the thing this lesson grows?
a) A new word list  b) The spoken confidence to present yourself in the new language  c) A prize for the best speaker
**Answer: b)** — confidence grows each time a learner stands and speaks.
