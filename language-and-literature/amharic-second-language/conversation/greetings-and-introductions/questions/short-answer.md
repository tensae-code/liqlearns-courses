# Greetings and Introductions (Second Language) — short answer

Answer out loud, in a short sentence or by doing the thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stand up and introduce yourself: greet the class and say who you are.
**Answer: the learner stands, greets, and says his or her name clearly** — greeting + name is a complete introduction for this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your partner has just asked you a question in the dialogue. What two things do you do?
**Answer: listen to the question, then answer it standing** — and afterwards the partners swap, so the answerer becomes the asker.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does ሰላምታ መለዋወጥ mean — the name of the chapter this lesson starts from?
**Answer: exchanging greetings** — meeting people and greeting them in the new language.
