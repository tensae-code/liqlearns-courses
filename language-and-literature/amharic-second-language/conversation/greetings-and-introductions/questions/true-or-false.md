# Greetings and Introductions (Second Language) — true or false

Say "true" or "false" out loud, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Each learner stands in turn and introduces himself or herself to the teacher and the classmates.
**Answer: True** — one by one, standing where they are, each learner greets and says who they are.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A learner must introduce himself in Amharic before he has heard anyone do it.
**Answer: False** — the teacher introduces himself or herself first; learners follow what they have heard.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the dialogue, one learner asks the questions and the other only ever answers — they never swap.
**Answer: False** — the two question and answer each other, and in this lesson's practice every learner takes both sides.
