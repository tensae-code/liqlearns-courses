Honesty notes. (1) TEACHER GUIDE evidence throughout, SECOND-LANGUAGE throughout. (2) Vocabulary naming: the placements name the themes and the pictured sets (body parts, pp.79–80; family words and household things, p.92; foods as pictured — "injera, bread, banana, milk…", Ch.10 media; house types — "tukul, corrugated-roof house, apartment", Ch.12/14 media) but do not print Amharic word lists for them. The lesson therefore names the pictured things in English (the placements' own naming), keeps the Amharic category words the placements do print (የሰውነት ክፍሎች, ቤተሰብ, የቤት ቁሳቁሶች, የመማሪያ ቁሳቁሶች, የምግብ አይነቶች, የቤት አይነቶች, አካባቢን መግለጽ), and carries the spoken Amharic names on the audio slots. No Amharic vocabulary is printed as the book's beyond what the placements give. (3) The household set in the placements' media lists a bed as well as pot, chair and lamp; the generated picture 8575 shows pot, chair and lamp on the mat, and the lesson names the bed only as one of the things in the learner's own home, not as pictured. (4) Chapter 14 is the book's own numbering — the body has no Chapter 13 (the TOC lists 12 chapters; an unheaded grammar/assessment block also appears) — flagged here exactly as the placements flag it. (5) Chapter 11 also carries the year's fidel consolidation; that work is taught in this course's Lesson 2, Sub lesson 4, and only the describing and body-parts-speaking of Ch.11 are used here, so nothing is double-counted between lessons. (6) The placements give no publication year for the guide; none is claimed.
