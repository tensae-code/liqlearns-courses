# Describing My World (Second Language) — ordering

Put things in their right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the family in Picture 8575 in age order, oldest first: boy, grandmother, girl, father, mother.
**Answer: grandmother, mother, father, girl, boy** — the family as it stands, eldest to youngest.

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** The story strip (Picture 8589) has been shuffled. Put its pictures in the order of the story: the open book — the house in the morning — the school — the road.
**Answer: the house in the morning, the road, the school, the open book** — ስዕሎችን በቅደም ተከተል: the pictures follow what was heard, beat by beat.
