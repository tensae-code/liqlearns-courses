# Describing My World (Second Language) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer. (These are picture questions — look, point, then answer.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** (Point at the dot on the figure's hand, Picture 8585.) The part under the dot is —
a) The foot  b) The hand  c) The ear
**Answer: b) The hand** — then name it in Amharic and touch your own hand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Picture 8575, the household things on the mat are —
a) A pot, a chair and a lamp  b) A book, a pencil and a bag  c) A drum, a ball and a rope
**Answer: a) A pot, a chair and a lamp** — የቤት ቁሳቁሶች, the things of the house, beside the family.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is one of the foods named in Picture 8576, as the guide's chapter pictures name them?
a) Injera  b) Pizza  c) Cake
**Answer: a) Injera** — with bread, milk, eggs and banana, the foods of the chapter's own picture set.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In Picture 8577, the round house with the thatched roof is —
a) The apartment  b) The tukul  c) The corrugated-roof house
**Answer: b) The tukul** — the corrugated-roof house stands in the middle; the apartment is the tall one.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In Picture 8579, the animal eating grass by the river is —
a) A cow  b) A horse  c) A lion
**Answer: a) A cow** — and the goat grazes on the other side of the path.
