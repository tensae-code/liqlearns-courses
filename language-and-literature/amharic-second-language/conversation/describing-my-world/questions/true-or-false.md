# Describing My World (Second Language) — true or false

Say "true" or "false" out loud, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The body parts you name in this lesson are spoken again later, inside the describing-the-environment chapter.
**Answer: True** — Chapter 11 speaks the body parts again while describing (printed p.127).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The foods in this lesson are named from a picture set — the foods are pointed at and named one by one.
**Answer: True** — injera, bread, banana, milk and the rest, as pictured in the chapter (printed pp.106–107 and its media).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** In the houses chapter, if you name a room or a house part wrongly, the wrong name is left alone so you do not feel bad.
**Answer: False** — wrong naming is corrected with the right term, and right naming is praised (printed pp.152–154). Correction, kindly given, is how the names stick.
