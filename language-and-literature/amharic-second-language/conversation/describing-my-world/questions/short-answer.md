# Describing My World (Second Language) — short answer

Answer out loud, pointing as you speak.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** (Point in turn at the dots on Picture 8585.) Name each part — head, eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand, body, leg, foot — in Amharic, then touch the same part on your own body.
**Answer: the learner names every dotted part and touches it on himself or herself** — the names ride on the lesson's audio; the pointing and touching are what is checked (printed pp.79–80).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five people in Picture 8575, pointing at each.
**Answer: grandmother, mother, father, girl, boy** — the family (ቤተሰብ), oldest to youngest or in pointing order; then the pot, the chair and the lamp on the mat.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the foods on the table in Picture 8576 as they are pointed at.
**Answer: injera, bread, milk, eggs, bananas and greens** — the chapter's pictured foods, named one by one.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Describe one house in Picture 8577: its type, and two rooms or parts it holds.
**Answer: any one house truly described** — e.g. "the tukul: round, thatched roof; a door and a small round window; rooms for sleeping and eating" — with a wrong term corrected kindly and said again right, the chapter's way (printed pp.152–154).

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Describe Picture 8579: name five things in it, then say one whole sentence about it.
**Answer: any five of — the big tree, the small house, the river, the cow, the goat, the two children, the path — plus one true sentence** (e.g. "The children are walking on the path"), the describing chapter's work (አካባቢን መግለጽ).
