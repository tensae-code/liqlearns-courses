# Video script — Describing My World (Second Language)

The super-simple voice, for the videos that ride on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the scripts are the slots' written form until then. The guide names four video needs for this lesson's chapters: "Parts of my body" (Ch.8), "My family" (Ch.9), "Foods we eat" (Ch.10) and "Houses and their rooms" (Ch.14). The four short scripts follow the chapters' own pattern.

---

## Script 1 — Parts of my body

Touch your head. Say its name.
Touch your eye. Your ear. Your mouth.
Hand. Leg. Foot.

Your body, in Amharic. Point — say — point — say.
Now faster. You lead. I follow.

## Script 2 — My family

This is my family — ቤተሰብ.
Grandmother. Mother. Father. Sister. Brother. Me.

And the things of our house: the pot, the chair, the lamp.
Names for the people I love. Names for the things we use.

## Script 3 — Foods we eat

Look at the table. Injera. Bread. Milk. Eggs. Banana.
Say each one. Point, and say.

Food has names in every language.
Now these foods have Amharic names in your mouth too.

## Script 4 — Houses and their rooms

Three houses. A tukul — round, with its grass roof.
A house with an iron roof. A tall apartment house.

Doors. Windows. Rooms for sleeping, rooms for eating.
Say the house. Say its rooms. Every home has names.
