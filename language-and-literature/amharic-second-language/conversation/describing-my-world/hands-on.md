# Hands-on — Describing My World (Second Language)

The lesson in your own home and on your own street, not only on a page.

## Touch and name
Stand in front of a mirror if you have one. Touch each body part the lesson names — head, eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand, leg, foot — and say its Amharic name as you touch it. Then have a brother, sister or friend point: you name. Then swap.

## Name your own house
Walk through your own home slowly. In each room, name the room and three things in it, in Amharic, touching each thing as you name it. The pot, the chair, the bed, the lamp — your own ones, not a picture's.

## Set the table
At the next meal, before anyone eats: name every food on the table in Amharic. Miss one? Someone who knows says it, you say it again — kindly, the guide's way.

## Tell the strip's story outside
Look out of your door or walk your street. Describe it the way you described the picture: the tree, the houses, the animals, the people, one true sentence each. Your world, in the new language, out loud.
