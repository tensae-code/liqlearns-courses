# Hands-on — Fidel for New Speakers

The lesson with your hands and your voice, not only your eyes.

## Write the family on the line
In a lined notebook, write today's family across the page — all seven orders, keeping the line (መስመር ጠብቀው). Check your pencil grip first (የእርሳስ አያያዝ): pinch the pencil near the front, rest it on the middle finger. Write the family three times, slower each time. This is the guide's own practice, and its homework too: re-read at home what you wrote.

## Flash cards from scrap paper
Cut or fold paper into cards. One fidel on each card. Hold up a card — your partner says the sound and the family it belongs to. Then shuffle and go faster. Make the confusable pairs sit side by side (በ next to ሰ, ደ next to ጀ) so your eyes learn the difference.

## Match the picture
Draw three simple pictures on cards: a drum (ከበሮ), and two more things whose Amharic names you know. Put the fidel cards under the pictures they begin with. ከ goes under the drum — the guide's own match.

## Sing the row
Sing the family's seven orders to any tune you know — the መዝሙር way. Sing it while you walk, while you wash, while you wait. The song carries the orders into your memory without a book.
