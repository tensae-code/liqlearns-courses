# Fidel for New Speakers — fill in the blank

Say (or write) the fidel that belongs in the gap.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** ዘ ዙ ዚ ዛ __ ዝ ዞ — which order is missing?
**Answer: ዜ** — the fifth order of the ዘ family.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** ተ ቱ ቲ ታ __ ት ቶ — which order is missing?
**Answer: ቴ** — the fifth order of the ተ family.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The double-sound partner of the ሰ family, printed in the guide's series ሏ/ሷ/ሿ/ኳ, is __.
**Answer: ሷ** — ሰ's labialised form, one shape with a w inside the sound.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** On the full chart, the family that comes right after ቀ is __.
**Answer: በ** — read down Picture 8580: …ሸ, ቀ, በ, ተ…
