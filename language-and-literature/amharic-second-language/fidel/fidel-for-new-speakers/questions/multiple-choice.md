# Fidel for New Speakers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which row is the በ family, in order?
a) በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ  b) በ ባ ቢ ቡ ቦ ብ ቤ  c) ሰ ሱ ሲ ሳ ሴ ስ ሶ
**Answer: a) በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ** — the seven orders, in their order (printed p.10 of the guide).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The fidel ከ is matched to which picture?
a) A person  b) A drum — ከበሮ  c) A house
**Answer: b) A drum — ከበሮ** — the word ከበሮ begins with ከ (the guide's own match, printed p.15). A person's picture is ሰ's match.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do ደ and ጀ get a whole chapter to themselves after they are first taught?
a) They are the last letters  b) They are easy to mix up, so they are practised again on their own  c) They have no sounds
**Answer: b)** — the guide introduces ደ/ጀ inside Chapter 3's set, then gives them Chapter 4 alone to consolidate.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which one is a double-sound (labialised) fidel?
a) ለ  b) ሏ  c) ሎ
**Answer: b) ሏ** — one shape with a double sound, a little w inside it (ባለ ድርብ ድምጽ). ሎ is an ordinary order of ለ.
