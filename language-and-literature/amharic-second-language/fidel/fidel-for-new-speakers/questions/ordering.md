# Fidel for New Speakers — ordering

Put the forms in their family order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the ሰ family in order: ሳ ሰ ሶ ሱ ስ ሲ ሴ.
**Answer: ሰ ሱ ሲ ሳ ሴ ስ ሶ.**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Put these family heads in the chart's order: መ ሀ ሰ ለ ሐ.
**Answer: ሀ ለ ሐ መ ሰ** — the first five rows of the full chart (Picture 8580).
