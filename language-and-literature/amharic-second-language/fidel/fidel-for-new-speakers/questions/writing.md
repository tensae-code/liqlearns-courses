# Fidel for New Speakers — writing

Write with your pencil, keeping the line (መስመር ጠብቀው). Check your grip first (የእርሳስ አያያዝ).

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copy-write the ሰ family twice, all seven orders in order, keeping the line. Then read what you wrote aloud.
**Answer: ሰ ሱ ሲ ሳ ሴ ስ ሶ** — checked against Picture 8572, row 2.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Copy-write the ጀ family twice, keeping the line, saying each order as you write it.
**Answer: ጀ ጁ ጂ ጃ ጄ ጅ ጆ** — checked against Picture 8573, the ጀ row.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** From memory: write the whole ከ family. Then check yourself against the chart.
**Answer: ከ ኩ ኪ ካ ኬ ክ ኮ** — checked against Picture 8580, the ከ row (and the guide's printed row, p.15).
