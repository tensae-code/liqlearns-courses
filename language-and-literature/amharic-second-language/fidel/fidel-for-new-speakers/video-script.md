# Video script — Fidel for New Speakers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look. A row of letters. One family.

**በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ.**
Same letter. Seven sounds. Sing them with me.

በ — ቡ — ቢ — ባ — ቤ — ብ — ቦ.

Again. A song helps. መዝሙር — we sing the fidel.
Sing it until your mouth knows the road.

Now this one. **ከ.** What begins with ከ?
ከበሮ! A drum. *(beat the air like a drum)* ከ — ከበሮ.

Write it now. On the line. Keep the line.
Pencil like this. *(show the grip)* Slow is good.

One family. Then the next. Pair by pair.
Soon — the whole chart. All yours.
