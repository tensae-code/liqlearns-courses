# Fidel for New Speakers

## Do it first

Look at this row: **በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ**.

It is one letter with seven faces. Point at each face and guess its sound — go on, guess. Then point at the last one and the first one: same letter, different sound. How can one letter sing seven notes?

That is the secret of the fidel, and this lesson teaches it the way new speakers learn it: family by family, two families at a time, sung out loud, matched to pictures, and written on the line.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first pairs: በ with ሰ, ሸ with ከ

A **fidel family** (ፊደል) is one letter and its seven orders — its ዝርያ/እርባታ, its forms. The letter stays itself; its ending changes, and each ending is a new sound.

In this course the families do not come one lonely at a time. They come in **pairs that are easy to mix up** — because learning the pair together is how you learn to tell them apart.

**The first pair: በ (bä) and ሰ (sä).**

- በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ
- ሰ ሱ ሲ ሳ ሴ ስ ሶ

**The second pair: ሸ (shä) and ከ (kä).**

- ሸ ሹ ሺ ሻ ሼ ሽ ሾ
- ከ ኩ ኪ ካ ኬ ክ ኮ

[Picture 8572 — the first pairs board: the በ, ሰ, ሸ and ከ families]

Here is how every family is learned — the same road, every time:

1. **Look and listen.** The family is written big. The teacher reads it aloud. You just listen, carefully.
2. **Repeat together.** The whole class reads after the teacher, chorally — many voices, one row.
3. **Lead.** A volunteer comes to the front and leads the reading; the others follow.
4. **Sing it.** A song — መዝሙር (mezmur) — is made for the family's orders, sung with its tune (በግጥምና ዜማ) until the orders are fixed in your head. Watch the በ row light up as it is sung, and sing with it:

[GIF 8595 — the በ family lights up order by order as it is sung]

5. **Match it to a picture.** Each fidel is matched to a picture whose word begins with it. ሰ is matched to a person's picture. And ከ is matched to this:

[Picture 8571 — a kebero drum]

**ከ → ከበሮ** (kebero, a drum). Say it: ከ … ከበሮ. The word begins with the letter. Your eye learns the letter by meeting its word.

6. **Write it, keeping the line.** In your notebook, write the family keeping the line — መስመር ጠብቀው. Hold the pencil right (የእርሳስ አያያዝ — the grip matters; if the grip is wrong, the hand tires and the letters wander). Good writing gets praised; if you struggle, help comes to your desk, not a scolding.

And the day's work ends with the guide's homework: **re-read at home what you wrote.**

## Sub lesson 2 — The middle pairs

Same road, new pairs:

**ለ (lä) with አ (a)** — ለ ሉ ሊ ላ ሌ ል ሎ · አ ኡ ኢ ኣ ኤ እ ኦ

**ዘ (zä) with ዠ (zhä)** — ዘ ዙ ዚ ዛ ዜ ዝ ዞ · ዠ ዡ ዢ ዣ ዤ ዥ ዦ

**ደ (dä) with ጀ (jä)** — ደ ዱ ዲ ዳ ዴ ድ ዶ · ጀ ጁ ጂ ጃ ጄ ጅ ጆ. These two get a whole second chapter to themselves later in the guide — they are that easy to confuse, and that important.

**ጸ (ts'ä) with ጰ (p'ä)** — ጸ ጹ ጺ ጻ ጼ ጽ ጾ · ጰ ጱ ጲ ጳ ጴ ጵ ጶ

**ጠ (t'ä) with ጨ (ch'ä)** — ጠ ጡ ጢ ጣ ጤ ጥ ጦ · ጨ ጩ ጪ ጫ ጬ ጭ ጮ. Look closely at ጠ's fifth form, ጤ: it wears a shape close to ቤ's family, but it is ጠ's own fifth order — one of the look-alike traps this lesson exists to teach. Read it off the board, exactly as drawn:

[Picture 8573 — the middle pairs board: ለ አ ዘ ዠ ደ ጀ ጸ ጰ ጠ ጨ]

Read each row off the board, slowly. The board is the truth, here and in the full chart at the end of this lesson, where every row is drawn the same careful way.

For each pair: listen, repeat together, sing it, match fidels to pictures, read words built from them, and write the families keeping the line. The road does not change; only the families do.

## Sub lesson 3 — The last families and the double sounds

The remaining families, in the guide's chapter order:

- **ገ (gä), ነ (nä), ኘ (nyä)** — ገ ጉ ጊ ጋ ጌ ግ ጎ · ነ ኑ ኒ ና ኔ ን ኖ · ኘ ኙ ኚ ኛ ኜ ኝ ኞ
- **ተ (tä) with ቸ (chä)** — ተ ቱ ቲ ታ ቴ ት ቶ · ቸ ቹ ቺ ቻ ቼ ች ቾ — another confusable pair, learned together.
- **ቀ (qä) with ኀ (h'ä)**, and **ፐ (pä), የ (yä)** — ቀ ቁ ቂ ቃ ቄ ቅ ቆ · ኀ ኁ ኂ ኃ ኄ ኅ ኆ · ፐ ፑ ፒ ፓ ፔ ፕ ፖ · የ ዩ ዪ ያ ዬ ይ ዮ
- **ዐ (’ä), ፀ (ts'ä), ወ (wä), መ (mä)** — ዐ ዑ ዒ ዓ ዔ ዕ ዖ · ፀ ፁ ፂ ፃ ፄ ፅ ፆ · ወ ዉ ዊ ዋ ዌ ው ዎ · መ ሙ ሚ ማ ሜ ም ሞ
- **ሀ (hä), ሠ (s'ä), ረ (rä), ፈ (fä)** — ሀ ሁ ሂ ሃ ሄ ህ ሆ · ሠ ሡ ሢ ሣ ሤ ሥ ሦ · ረ ሩ ሪ ራ ሬ ር ሮ · ፈ ፉ ፊ ፋ ፌ ፍ ፎ

(ሐ — ሐ ሑ ሒ ሓ ሔ ሕ ሖ — also appears in the guide's Chapter 6 work, beside ጠ and ጨ.)

[Picture 8574 — the last families board]

And now a new kind of fidel: the **double-sound fidels** — ባለ ድርብ ድምጽ ፊደላት, the labialised ones. One shape, but the sound carries a little **w** inside it:

[Picture 8581 — the double-sound fidel cards: ሏ ሷ ሿ ኳ]

**ሏ ሷ ሿ ኳ** — say ሏ slowly: it is not ለ + ወ. It is one letter with a double sound. Read and write these, and words built from them, the same way as every family.

## Sub lesson 4 — All the fidels in order

Now put it all together. The guide's consolidation point: **read and write ALL the fidels in their order** — ሁሉንም ፊደላት በቅደም ተከተላቸው.

[Picture 8580 — the full fidel chart, all the families in order]

Read down the chart: ሀ ለ ሐ መ ሠ ረ ሰ ሸ ቀ በ ተ ቸ ኀ ነ ኘ አ ከ ኸ ወ ዐ ዘ ዠ የ ደ ጀ ገ ጠ ጨ ጰ ጸ ፀ ፈ ፐ. Then read across: pick any row and sing its seven orders. Then write: a family is named, and you write its row from memory, keeping the line.

This chart is yours now. Every word in the next lessons is built from these rows.

## Say it to your companion

Take one family each. Read your row aloud to your companion — they point at each fidel as you say it, and catch you if an order is skipped. Then swap. Finish by singing one family's መዝሙር together, twice: once slow, once at singing speed.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the በ and ሰ families in your notebook, keeping the line. Check your grip before you start.
2. Draw a drum. Write ከ under it, and the word ከበሮ. Put it where you will see it tomorrow.
3. Read the full chart (Picture 8580) aloud, top to bottom, first letters only. Then read three whole rows of your choosing.
4. Tonight, re-read at home what you wrote — the guide's own homework.
