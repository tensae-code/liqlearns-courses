# Keeyyata — dhugaa yookiin soba

Tokkoon tokkoo dubbii dhugaa yookiin soba jedhi.

**1. (Barnoota xiqqaa 1)** Keeyyati yaada lama qabu keeyyata lama uffata tokko uffate dha.
**Deebii: Dhugaa**

**2. (Barnoota xiqqaa 2)** Himi ijoo yeroo hunda jalqaba irratti dhaabbata.
**Deebii: Soba** — jalqaba, gidduu, dhuma, yookiin jalqabaafi dhuma irratti dhaabbachuu danda'a
