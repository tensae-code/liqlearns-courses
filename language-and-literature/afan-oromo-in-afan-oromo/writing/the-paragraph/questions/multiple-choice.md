# Keeyyata — filannoo hedduu

Deebii sirrii tokko filadhu.

**1. (Barnoota xiqqaa 1)** Keeyyati —
a) Hima tokko  b) Qaama barreessuu isa xiqqaa yaada tokko baatu  c) Barreeffama guutuu  d) Gaalee tokko
**Deebii: b) Qaama barreessuu isa xiqqaa yaada tokko baatu**

**2. (Barnoota xiqqaa 1)** 'Hima ijoo' jechuun —
a) Hima cufuu  b) Hima yaada baatu  c) Hima gaaffii  d) Hima jalqabaa yeroo hunda
**Deebii: b) Hima yaada baatu**

**3. (Barnoota xiqqaa 2)** Himi ijoo dhaabata meeqa qaba?
a) Lama  b) Sadii  c) Afur  d) Shan
**Deebii: c) Afur** — jalqaba, gidduu, dhuma, jalqabaafi dhuma
