# Birsaga Ijaaruu — deebii gabaabaa

Jecha tokkoon yookiin hima gabaabaa dhaan deebisi.

**1. (Barnoota xiqqaa 2)** Tarree gabaabaa W dhaan barreessi.
**Deebii: Wa We Wi Wo Wu**

**2. (Barnoota xiqqaa 3)** "waada" birsagaan qoqoodi.
**Deebii: waa-da**

**3. (Barnoota xiqqaa 4)** Birsaga meeqatu "Gamoo" keessa jira?
**Deebii: Lama** — Ga-moo
