# Qeenxee fi Danuu — filannoo hedduu

Deebii sirrii tokko filadhu.

**1. (Barnoota xiqqaa 1)** Maqaan tokko maqaa dhahu —
a) Danuu  b) Qeenxee  c) Tishoo  d) Ramaddii
**Deebii: b) Qeenxee**

**2. (Barnoota xiqqaa 2)** Danuun 'gamoo' —
a) Gamoowwan  b) Gamootoota  c) Gamoneen  d) Gamoo
**Deebii: a) Gamoowwan**

**3. (Barnoota xiqqaa 3)** Qeenxeen 'haadholii' —
a) Haadha  b) Haadhaa  c) Haadhoo  d) Haadhol
**Deebii: a) Haadha**
