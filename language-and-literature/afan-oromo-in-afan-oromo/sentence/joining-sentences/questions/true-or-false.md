# Himoota Walitti Makaa — dhugaa yookiin soba

Tokkoon tokkoon himootaa dhugaa yookiin soba jedhi.

**1. (Barnoota xiqqaa 3)** Qaamni hima ceelaa kophaa dhaabbachuu danda'a.
**Deebii: Soba** — qaamni ceelaa hima guddaa irratti irkata

**2. (Barnoota xiqqaa 1)** Dubbiin waraabame waraabbii dhaan marfama.
**Deebii: Dhugaa**
