# Himoota Xaxamoo — filannoo hedduu

Deebii sirrii tokko filadhu.

**1. (Barnoota xiqqaa 1)** Kamtu ciroo hirkataa ti?
a) Caalaan barataa cimaadha  b) Yoo inni dhufe…  c) Ani hojiin qaba  d) Midhaanni gaarii ture
**Deebii: b) Yoo inni dhufe…** — jecha walitti makaa 'yoo' tiin jalqaba

**2. (Barnoota xiqqaa 2)** 'Hanga inni dhufutti, ani hojiin qaba' keessatti ciroo irkatu —
a) Ani hojiin qaba  b) Hanga inni dhufutti  c) Lamaan isaanii  d) Hin jiru
**Deebii: b) Hanga inni dhufutti**

**3. (Barnoota xiqqaa 3)** Himni xaxamaan yoo xiqqaate ciroo meeqa barbaada?
a) Tokko  b) Lama  c) Kudhan  d) Afur
**Deebii: b) Lama** — tokko dhaabbatu, tokko irkatu
