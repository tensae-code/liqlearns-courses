# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: Early Civilisations 2 — Egypt”?
**Answer:** On the Nile, the river Herodotus called Egypt's gift, farmers grow rich on the flood; pharaohs raise the pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, priests embalm mummies for the life after death, and the kingdom lasts three thousand years.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 1: the Egyptian (River Nile) civilisation” end?
**Answer:** A civilisation the book dates from about 4000 BCE grows along one river — the Nile gives water, papyrus and flax, a highway of boats, and the flood-calendar by which Egypt lives.
