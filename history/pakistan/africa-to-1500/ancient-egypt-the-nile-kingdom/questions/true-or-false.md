# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   On the Nile, the river Herodotus called Egypt's gift, farmers grow rich on the flood; pharaohs raise the pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, priests embalm mummies for the life after death, and the kingdom lasts three thousand years.
**Answer: True**
