## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 4: Early Civilisations 2 — Egypt

On the Nile, the river Herodotus called Egypt's gift, farmers grow rich on the flood; pharaohs raise the pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, priests embalm mummies for the life after death, and the kingdom lasts three thousand years.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 1: the Egyptian (River Nile) civilisation

A civilisation the book dates from about 4000 BCE grows along one river — the Nile gives water, papyrus and flax, a highway of boats, and the flood-calendar by which Egypt lives.
