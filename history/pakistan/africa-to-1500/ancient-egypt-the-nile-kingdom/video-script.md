# Video script — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

On the Nile, the river Herodotus called Egypt's gift, farmers grow rich on the flood; pharaohs raise the pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, priests embalm mummies for the life after death, and the kingdom lasts three thousand years.

A civilisation the book dates from about 4000 BCE grows along one river — the Nile gives water, papyrus and flax, a highway of boats, and the flood-calendar by which Egypt lives.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Pakistan.
