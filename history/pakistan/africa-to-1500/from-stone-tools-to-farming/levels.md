# From Stone Tools to Farming: Mehrgarh — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — On the Kacchi plain near Bolan, farmers settle from about 7000 to 2500 BCE — among the earliest farming villages on earth; the French mission of Jean-François Jarrige uncovers them from 1974, mud-brick houses, pottery and burials proving the Indus story starts as a farming story. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
