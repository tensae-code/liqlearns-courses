# From Stone Tools to Farming: Mehrgarh — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  On the Kacchi plain near Bolan, farmers settle from about 7000 to 2500 BCE — among the earliest farming villages on earth; the French mission of Jean-François Jarrige uncovers them from 1974, mud-brick houses, pottery and burials proving the Indus story starts as a farming story.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: early settlements in Mehrgarh (Balochistan)” is mainly about this: Cyrus founds the Achaemenid Empire in 550 BCE and takes Babylon in 539; Cambyses adds Egypt in 525; Darius the Great organises twenty satrapies, builds the Royal Road from Sardis to Susa and a postal relay to hold it together; Zoroaster's faith burns underneath it all — until Alexander ends the empire in 331 BCE.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: On the Kacchi plain near Bolan, farmers settle from about 7000 to 2500 BCE — among the earliest farming villages on earth; the French mission of Jean-François Jarrige uncovers them from 1974, mud-brick houses, pottery and burials proving the Indus story starts as a farming story.
