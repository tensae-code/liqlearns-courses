# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming: Mehrgarh


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

On the Kacchi plain near Bolan, farmers settle from about 7000 to 2500 BCE — among the earliest farming villages on earth; the French mission of Jean-François Jarrige uncovers them from 1974, mud-brick houses, pottery and burials proving the Indus story starts as a farming story.
