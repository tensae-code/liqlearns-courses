# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Society stands as a pyramid drawn on the page — pharaoh, vizier and officials, nobles and priests, scribes and soldiers, artisans and merchants, peasants and slaves; below the pharaoh-god, a world of many gods, hieroglyphic writing, and pyramids whose three inner chambers guard the king's road to the afterlife.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: Egypt's social structure, religion, scripts and pyramids” is mainly about this: Emperors build palaces and the Great Wall, commoners their courtyard houses; caravans carry silk west; Confucianism, Taoism and Buddhism order the inner life; and the script grows from oracle bones (Jiaguwen) toward the characters still written today.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Society stands as a pyramid drawn on the page — pharaoh, vizier and officials, nobles and priests, scribes and soldiers, artisans and merchants, peasants and slaves; below the pharaoh-god, a world of many gods, hieroglyphic writing, and pyramids whose three inner chambers guard the king's road to the afterlife.
