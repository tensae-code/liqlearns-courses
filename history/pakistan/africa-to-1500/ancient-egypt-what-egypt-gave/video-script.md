# Video script — Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Society stands as a pyramid drawn on the page — pharaoh, vizier and officials, nobles and priests, scribes and soldiers, artisans and merchants, peasants and slaves; below the pharaoh-god, a world of many gods, hieroglyphic writing, and pyramids whose three inner chambers guard the king's road to the afterlife.
