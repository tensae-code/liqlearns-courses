# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Society stands as a pyramid drawn on the page — pharaoh, vizier and officials, nobles and priests, scribes and soldiers, artisans and merchants, peasants and slaves; below the pharaoh-god, a world of many gods, hieroglyphic writing, and pyramids whose three inner chambers guard the king's road to the afterlife. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
