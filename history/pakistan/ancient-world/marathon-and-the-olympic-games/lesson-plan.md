# Lesson plan — Marathon and the Olympic Games

**Course:** History — Pakistan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Marathon and the Olympic Games
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: Persia, Greece, Rome and India's Empires (CURRICULUM.md).
