# Marathon and the Olympic Games — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   When Persia lands at Marathon in 490 BCE, Pheidippides runs the road to Athens with the news and gives every later marathon its name and distance; and at Olympia the Greeks' games of 776 BCE sleep for fifteen centuries and wake again in 1896 as the modern Olympics.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 2: Greek facts and timeline” is mainly about this: In Pakistan's own north-west, around Peshawar, Swat and Taxila, a kingdom stands where empires collide — Persian satraps, Alexander's march of 327–326 BCE, Ashoka turning it Buddhist and raising stupas, Kanishka spreading the faith, and Greek sculptors carving the Buddha's face — until the White Huns burn it down around 500 CE.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The book's fact-box gives Pheidippides his run — 240 kilometres to Sparta in two days to beg help against Persia — and its timeline runs the Greek story from the first Olympic Games of 776 BCE to the modern revival of 1896.
