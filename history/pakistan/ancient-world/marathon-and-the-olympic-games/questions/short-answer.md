# Marathon and the Olympic Games — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: Marathon and the Olympic Games”?
**Answer:** When Persia lands at Marathon in 490 BCE, Pheidippides runs the road to Athens with the news and gives every later marathon its name and distance; and at Olympia the Greeks' games of 776 BCE sleep for fifteen centuries and wake again in 1896 as the modern Olympics.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 2: Greek facts and timeline” end?
**Answer:** The book's fact-box gives Pheidippides his run — 240 kilometres to Sparta in two days to beg help against Persia — and its timeline runs the Greek story from the first Olympic Games of 776 BCE to the modern revival of 1896.
