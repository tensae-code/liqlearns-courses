# Magna Carta 1215: The King Under Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  In 1215 England's barons corner King John and make him sign the Great Charter — for the first time a king's power is written down as less than the law, a seed from which parliaments grow.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: the Magna Carta” is mainly about this: The Abbasids move the capital to Al-Mansur's new round city of Baghdad (762); under Haroon-al-Rashid (786–809) the Bait-ul-Hikmah — House of Wisdom — gathers the learning of Greece, Persia and India; Al-Kindi philosophises, Ibn Sina writes the Canon of Medicine; and in 1258 the Mongols drown the golden age in the Tigris.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: In 1215 England's barons corner King John and make him sign the Great Charter — for the first time a king's power is written down as less than the law, a seed from which parliaments grow.
