# The Abbasid Golden Age: Baghdad and the House of Wisdom

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Abbasid Golden Age: Baghdad and the House of Wisdom** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Abbasids move the capital to Al-Mansur's new round city of Baghdad (762); under Haroon-al-Rashid (786–809) the Bait-ul-Hikmah — House of Wisdom — gathers the learning of Greece, Persia and India; Al-Kindi philosophises, Ibn Sina writes the Canon of Medicine; and in 1258 the Mongols drown the golden age in the Tigris.
