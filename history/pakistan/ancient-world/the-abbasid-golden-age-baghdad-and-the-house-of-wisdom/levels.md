# The Abbasid Golden Age: Baghdad and the House of Wisdom — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Abbasids move the capital to Al-Mansur's new round city of Baghdad (762); under Haroon-al-Rashid (786–809) the Bait-ul-Hikmah — House of Wisdom — gathers the learning of Greece, Persia and India; Al-Kindi philosophises, Ibn Sina writes the Canon of Medicine; and in 1258 the Mongols drown the golden age in the Tigris. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
