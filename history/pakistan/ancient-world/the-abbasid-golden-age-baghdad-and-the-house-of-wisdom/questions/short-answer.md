# The Abbasid Golden Age: Baghdad and the House of Wisdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 2: the Abbasid Dynasty (750–1258)”?
**Answer:** The Abbasids move the capital to Al-Mansur's new round city of Baghdad (762); under Haroon-al-Rashid (786–809) the Bait-ul-Hikmah — House of Wisdom — gathers the learning of Greece, Persia and India; Al-Kindi philosophises, Ibn Sina writes the Canon of Medicine; and in 1258 the Mongols drown the golden age in the Tigris.
