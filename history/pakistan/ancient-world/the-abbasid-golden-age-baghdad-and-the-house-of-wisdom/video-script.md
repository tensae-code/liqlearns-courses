# Video script — The Abbasid Golden Age: Baghdad and the House of Wisdom


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The Abbasids move the capital to Al-Mansur's new round city of Baghdad (762); under Haroon-al-Rashid (786–809) the Bait-ul-Hikmah — House of Wisdom — gathers the learning of Greece, Persia and India; Al-Kindi philosophises, Ibn Sina writes the Canon of Medicine; and in 1258 the Mongols drown the golden age in the Tigris.
