# Lesson plan — The Delhi Sultanate: Five Dynasties

**Course:** History — Pakistan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Delhi Sultanate: Five Dynasties
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — India: Sultanate, Mughals and the British Advance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Mahmood of Ghazni raids India seventeen times (1000–1027), but Shahab-ud-Din Ghori plants rule — Tarain 1192 — and his slave-general Qutb-ud-Din Aibak founds the Sultanate in 1206; five dynasties follow — Mamluk (Iltutmish the real organiser, Razia the queen, Balban), Khilji (Ala-ud-Din's market reforms and Mongol-proof armies), Tughlaq (Firoz Shah's canals and employment bureau), Sayyid, and Lodhi, until Babar ends it at Panipat.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — India: Sultanate, Mughals and the British Advance (recommended, never forced).
