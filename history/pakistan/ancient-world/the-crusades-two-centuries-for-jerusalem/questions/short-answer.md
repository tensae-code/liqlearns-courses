# The Crusades: Two Centuries for Jerusalem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 3: the Crusades”?
**Answer:** After Manzikert, Byzantium begs the West for help and Pope Urban II answers with holy war — the First Crusade (1095–1099) storms Jerusalem; Nuruddin Zengi and then Salahuddin unite the Muslims; the Third Crusade pits Salahuddin against Richard; the Fourth (1202–1204) shames itself by sacking Christian Constantinople; two centuries of war end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour.
