# The Crusades: Two Centuries for Jerusalem — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  After Manzikert, Byzantium begs the West for help and Pope Urban II answers with holy war — the First Crusade (1095–1099) storms Jerusalem; Nuruddin Zengi and then Salahuddin unite the Muslims; the Third Crusade pits Salahuddin against Richard; the Fourth (1202–1204) shames itself by sacking Christian Constantinople; two centuries of war end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 3: the Crusades” is mainly about this: Sher Shah drives Humayun into exile and in five years builds the machine the Mughals will inherit — the Grand Trunk Road from Bengal to Peshawar, the silver rupiya, the dak postal system and a measured land revenue — before dying at Kalinjar in 1545.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After Manzikert, Byzantium begs the West for help and Pope Urban II answers with holy war — the First Crusade (1095–1099) storms Jerusalem; Nuruddin Zengi and then Salahuddin unite the Muslims; the Third Crusade pits Salahuddin against Richard; the Fourth (1202–1204) shames itself by sacking Christian Constantinople; two centuries of war end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour.
