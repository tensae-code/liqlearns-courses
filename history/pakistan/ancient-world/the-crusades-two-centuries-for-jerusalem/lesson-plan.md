# Lesson plan — The Crusades: Two Centuries for Jerusalem

**Course:** History — Pakistan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Crusades: Two Centuries for Jerusalem
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Rise of Islam and the Medieval Worlds (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After Manzikert, Byzantium begs the West for help and Pope Urban II answers with holy war — the First Crusade (1095–1099) storms Jerusalem; Nuruddin Zengi and then Salahuddin unite the Muslims; the Third Crusade pits Salahuddin against Richard; the Fourth (1202–1204) shames itself by sacking Christian Constantinople; two centuries of war end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour.
