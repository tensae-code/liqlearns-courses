# The Crusades: Two Centuries for Jerusalem — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After Manzikert, Byzantium begs the West for help and Pope Urban II answers with holy war — the First Crusade (1095–1099) storms Jerusalem; Nuruddin Zengi and then Salahuddin unite the Muslims; the Third Crusade pits Salahuddin against Richard; the Fourth (1202–1204) shames itself by sacking Christian Constantinople; two centuries of war end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
