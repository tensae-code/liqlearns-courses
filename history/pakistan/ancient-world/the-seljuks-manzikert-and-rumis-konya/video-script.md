# Video script — The Seljuks: Manzikert and Rumi's Konya


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Tughril Beg's Turks take Baghdad and become Islam's sword-arm; Alp Arslan crushes Byzantium at Manzikert in 1071 and opens Anatolia; Malik Shah and his vizier Nizam-ul-Mulk hold the empire together; and in Seljuk Konya the poet Rumi (1207–1273) sings — the dynasty's other conquest.
