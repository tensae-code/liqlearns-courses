# The Seljuks: Manzikert and Rumi's Konya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 3: the Seljuk Dynasty (1037–1194)”?
**Answer:** Tughril Beg's Turks take Baghdad and become Islam's sword-arm; Alp Arslan crushes Byzantium at Manzikert in 1071 and opens Anatolia; Malik Shah and his vizier Nizam-ul-Mulk hold the empire together; and in Seljuk Konya the poet Rumi (1207–1273) sings — the dynasty's other conquest.
