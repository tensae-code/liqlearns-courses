# The Seljuks: Manzikert and Rumi's Konya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Tughril Beg's Turks take Baghdad and become Islam's sword-arm; Alp Arslan crushes Byzantium at Manzikert in 1071 and opens Anatolia; Malik Shah and his vizier Nizam-ul-Mulk hold the empire together; and in Seljuk Konya the poet Rumi (1207–1273) sings — the dynasty's other conquest.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 3: the Seljuk Dynasty (1037–1194)” is mainly about this: Shah Ismail founds a Shi'a empire in Persia in 1501; under Shah Abbas (1587–1629) Isfahan is rebuilt into one of the world's most beautiful capitals — "Isfahan is half the world" — before Afghan revolt ends the dynasty.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Tughril Beg's Turks take Baghdad and become Islam's sword-arm; Alp Arslan crushes Byzantium at Manzikert in 1071 and opens Anatolia; Malik Shah and his vizier Nizam-ul-Mulk hold the empire together; and in Seljuk Konya the poet Rumi (1207–1273) sings — the dynasty's other conquest.
