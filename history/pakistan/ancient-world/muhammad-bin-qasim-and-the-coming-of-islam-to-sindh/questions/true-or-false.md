# Muhammad bin Qasim and the Coming of Islam to Sindh — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  In 712 the young Umayyad general Muhammad bin Qasim lands in Sindh, defeats Raja Dahir, and opens the door through which Islam enters South Asia — the book gives his conquest its own section because it is where Pakistan's own story begins.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 2: the importance of Muhammad bin Qasim in spreading Islam in the Subcontinent” is mainly about this: The Fatimids claim the caliphate from North Africa, conquer Egypt, found Cairo and its Al-Azhar mosque-university in 972, and rule the richest trade crossroads of the Mediterranean until Salahuddin ends their line.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: In 712 the young Umayyad general Muhammad bin Qasim lands in Sindh, defeats Raja Dahir, and opens the door through which Islam enters South Asia — the book gives his conquest its own section because it is where Pakistan's own story begins.
