# Muhammad bin Qasim and the Coming of Islam to Sindh — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In 712 the young Umayyad general Muhammad bin Qasim lands in Sindh, defeats Raja Dahir, and opens the door through which Islam enters South Asia — the book gives his conquest its own section because it is where Pakistan's own story begins. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
