# Muhammad bin Qasim and the Coming of Islam to Sindh

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Muhammad bin Qasim and the Coming of Islam to Sindh** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

In 712 the young Umayyad general Muhammad bin Qasim lands in Sindh, defeats Raja Dahir, and opens the door through which Islam enters South Asia — the book gives his conquest its own section because it is where Pakistan's own story begins.
