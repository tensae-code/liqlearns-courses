# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: the Roman timeline”?
**Answer:** The book's own timeline tells the fall in dates — republic in 509 BCE, empire from 27 BCE, the empire split in two in 395 CE, and the western throne empty in 476 CE.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 2: the fall of Rome” end?
**Answer:** Constantine legalises the Christians' faith and moves the capital east; the empire is split between Latin West and Greek East; and in 476 CE the western empire falls — while the eastern, Byzantine half rules on for a thousand years, as the book's closing note records.
