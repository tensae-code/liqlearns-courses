# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   The book's own timeline tells the fall in dates — republic in 509 BCE, empire from 27 BCE, the empire split in two in 395 CE, and the western throne empty in 476 CE.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 2: the fall of Rome” is mainly about this: Money has a biography — first barter, swapping goods for goods; then metal coins, struck in China around 1000 BCE and in Lydia around 500 BCE, with leather notes in China by 118 BCE; then paper money in China around 806 CE, spreading until notes, and finally plastic cards, carry the world's trade.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Constantine legalises the Christians' faith and moves the capital east; the empire is split between Latin West and Greek East; and in 476 CE the western empire falls — while the eastern, Byzantine half rules on for a thousand years, as the book's closing note records.
