## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 4: the Roman timeline

The book's own timeline tells the fall in dates — republic in 509 BCE, empire from 27 BCE, the empire split in two in 395 CE, and the western throne empty in 476 CE.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 2: the fall of Rome

Constantine legalises the Christians' faith and moves the capital east; the empire is split between Latin West and Greek East; and in 476 CE the western empire falls — while the eastern, Byzantine half rules on for a thousand years, as the book's closing note records.
