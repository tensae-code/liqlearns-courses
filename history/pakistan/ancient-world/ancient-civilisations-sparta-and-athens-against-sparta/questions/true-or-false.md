# Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Two cities, two ways of life — Athens of ships, assemblies and schools, Sparta of barracks and discipline; allies against Persia, they turn on each other in the Peloponnesian War of 431 BCE and exhaust the Greek world between them.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 2: Athens and Sparta” is mainly about this: From 320 CE the Guptas reunite northern India — Chandragupta I, Samudragupta the conqueror-poet, Chandragupta II at the height — an age the book presents as a golden age of learning, art and gold coinage, before the empire fragments after 550 CE.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Two cities, two ways of life — Athens of ships, assemblies and schools, Sparta of barracks and discipline; allies against Persia, they turn on each other in the Peloponnesian War of 431 BCE and exhaust the Greek world between them.
