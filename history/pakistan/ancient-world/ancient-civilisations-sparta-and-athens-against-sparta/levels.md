# Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Two cities, two ways of life — Athens of ships, assemblies and schools, Sparta of barracks and discipline; allies against Persia, they turn on each other in the Peloponnesian War of 431 BCE and exhaust the Greek world between them. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
