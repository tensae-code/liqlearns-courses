# The Gupta Golden Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  From 320 CE the Guptas reunite northern India — Chandragupta I, Samudragupta the conqueror-poet, Chandragupta II at the height — an age the book presents as a golden age of learning, art and gold coinage, before the empire fragments after 550 CE.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 3: the Gupta Empire” is mainly about this: From the 8th to the 10th century the Norsemen pour out of Scandinavia — raiders, traders and settlers at once — frightening every coast of Europe and leaving their names from Normandy to Iceland.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From 320 CE the Guptas reunite northern India — Chandragupta I, Samudragupta the conqueror-poet, Chandragupta II at the height — an age the book presents as a golden age of learning, art and gold coinage, before the empire fragments after 550 CE.
