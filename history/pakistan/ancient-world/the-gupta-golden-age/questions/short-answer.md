# The Gupta Golden Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 3: the Gupta Empire”?
**Answer:** From 320 CE the Guptas reunite northern India — Chandragupta I, Samudragupta the conqueror-poet, Chandragupta II at the height — an age the book presents as a golden age of learning, art and gold coinage, before the empire fragments after 550 CE.
