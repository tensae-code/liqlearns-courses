# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Romulus's little city on the Tiber learns to rule the whole Mediterranean — the chapter tours its Latin tongue, its baths and amphitheatres, the Colosseum holding fifty thousand, and an empire map that by 117 CE touches three continents.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 2: the Roman civilisation” is mainly about this: After the catastrophe of 1857, Sir Syed argues the Muslims of India must learn the new knowledge or be left behind — he opens schools at Muradabad and Ghazipur, founds the Scientific Society in 1863, raises the college at Aligarh that becomes a university, and plants the two-nation idea from which Pakistan grows.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Rome begins as an Etruscan-ruled village in 753 BCE, throws off its kings for a Republic in 509 ruled by Senate and assemblies, conquers Italy and then the Mediterranean through the Punic wars, survives Spartacus's slave revolt, and is remade as an empire when Augustus takes power in 27 BCE.
