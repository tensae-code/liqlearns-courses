# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Romulus's little city on the Tiber learns to rule the whole Mediterranean — the chapter tours its Latin tongue, its baths and amphitheatres, the Colosseum holding fifty thousand, and an empire map that by 117 CE touches three continents.

Rome begins as an Etruscan-ruled village in 753 BCE, throws off its kings for a Republic in 509 ruled by Senate and assemblies, conquers Italy and then the Mediterranean through the Punic wars, survives Spartacus's slave revolt, and is remade as an empire when Augustus takes power in 27 BCE.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Pakistan.
