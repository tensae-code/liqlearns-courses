## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 4: the Roman civilisation (753 BCE–476 CE)** — Romulus's little city on the Tiber learns to rule the whole Mediterranean — the chapter tours its Latin tongue, its baths and amphitheatres, the Colosseum holding fifty thousand, and an empire map that by 117 CE touches three continents.
2. **Chapter 2: the Roman civilisation** — Rome begins as an Etruscan-ruled village in 753 BCE, throws off its kings for a Republic in 509 ruled by Senate and assemblies, conquers Italy and then the Mediterranean through the Punic wars, survives Spartacus's slave revolt, and is remade as an empire when Augustus takes power in 27 BCE.
