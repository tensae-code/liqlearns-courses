# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Emperors build palaces and the Great Wall, commoners their courtyard houses; caravans carry silk west; Confucianism, Taoism and Buddhism order the inner life; and the script grows from oracle bones (Jiaguwen) toward the characters still written today. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
