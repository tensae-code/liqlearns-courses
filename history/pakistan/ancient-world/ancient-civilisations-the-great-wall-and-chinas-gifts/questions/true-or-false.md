# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Emperors build palaces and the Great Wall, commoners their courtyard houses; caravans carry silk west; Confucianism, Taoism and Buddhism order the inner life; and the script grows from oracle bones (Jiaguwen) toward the characters still written today.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: China's architecture, trade, religions and script” is mainly about this: Alexander (356–323 BCE), king of Macedon at twenty, destroys the Persian Empire, and in 327 BCE turns toward India — the book asks why he wanted India at all: its wealth, and the Greek belief that the world's edge lay just beyond the Indus.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Emperors build palaces and the Great Wall, commoners their courtyard houses; caravans carry silk west; Confucianism, Taoism and Buddhism order the inner life; and the script grows from oracle bones (Jiaguwen) toward the characters still written today.
