# Ancient Civilisations: India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   In the learners' own soil, the Indus Valley people build planned cities like Mohenjo-daro around 2500 BCE, stamp their goods with animal seals and trade far away; the cities fade, Aryan newcomers arrive around 1500 BCE, and the buried cities sleep until excavators uncover them in 1921–22.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 1: the Indus Valley civilisation” is mainly about this: Before the stories, the chapter teaches the clock they are told by — years counted backwards in BCE from the birth of Christ and forwards in CE, so a child can place Greece, Rome and Gandhara on one line of time.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From 3300 to 1300 BCE the Indus cities — Mohenjo-daro and Harappa first among them — plan their streets, baths and drains, farm the floodplain, carve animal seals in a logo-syllabic script no one has yet read, trade with Mesopotamia, and then, for reasons still argued (river shifts, floods, drying), fade away.
