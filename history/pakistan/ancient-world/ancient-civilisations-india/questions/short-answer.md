# Ancient Civilisations: India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: Early Civilisations 3 — the Indus Valley”?
**Answer:** In the learners' own soil, the Indus Valley people build planned cities like Mohenjo-daro around 2500 BCE, stamp their goods with animal seals and trade far away; the cities fade, Aryan newcomers arrive around 1500 BCE, and the buried cities sleep until excavators uncover them in 1921–22.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 1: the Indus Valley civilisation” end?
**Answer:** From 3300 to 1300 BCE the Indus cities — Mohenjo-daro and Harappa first among them — plan their streets, baths and drains, farm the floodplain, carve animal seals in a logo-syllabic script no one has yet read, trade with Mesopotamia, and then, for reasons still argued (river shifts, floods, drying), fade away.
