# Ancient Civilisations: China — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  China's 4,000-year story opens on its rivers — the Shang kingdom (the book's map: 1028–1523 BCE), the Zhou who follow, silk weaving, oracle-bone writing, and in 221 BCE Qin Shi Huang welding the warring states into one China.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: the Chinese civilisation” is mainly about this: Two cities, two ways of life — Athens of ships, assemblies and schools, Sparta of barracks and discipline; allies against Persia, they turn on each other in the Peloponnesian War of 431 BCE and exhaust the Greek world between them.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: China's 4,000-year story opens on its rivers — the Shang kingdom (the book's map: 1028–1523 BCE), the Zhou who follow, silk weaving, oracle-bone writing, and in 221 BCE Qin Shi Huang welding the warring states into one China.
