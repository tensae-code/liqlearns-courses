# Salahuddin and the Ayyubids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Salahuddin ibn Ayyub, a Kurdish soldier from Tikrit, unites Egypt and Syria, retakes Jerusalem in 1187, and faces Richard the Lionheart in the Third Crusade — the book's sultans list runs his house to 1260.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 3: the Ayyubid Dynasty (1171–1260)” is mainly about this: After Manzikert, Byzantium begs the West for help and Pope Urban II answers with holy war — the First Crusade (1095–1099) storms Jerusalem; Nuruddin Zengi and then Salahuddin unite the Muslims; the Third Crusade pits Salahuddin against Richard; the Fourth (1202–1204) shames itself by sacking Christian Constantinople; two centuries of war end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Salahuddin ibn Ayyub, a Kurdish soldier from Tikrit, unites Egypt and Syria, retakes Jerusalem in 1187, and faces Richard the Lionheart in the Third Crusade — the book's sultans list runs his house to 1260.
