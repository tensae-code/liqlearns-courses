# Salahuddin and the Ayyubids

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Salahuddin and the Ayyubids** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Salahuddin ibn Ayyub, a Kurdish soldier from Tikrit, unites Egypt and Syria, retakes Jerusalem in 1187, and faces Richard the Lionheart in the Third Crusade — the book's sultans list runs his house to 1260.
