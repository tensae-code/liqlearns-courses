# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Babylon's king Hammurabi (the book dates him 1750 BCE) carves his laws on stone so all can read them; around the law the chapter builds the whole economy — farmers, weavers, metal-workers, merchants sailing the Gulf — the gifts of writing, law and trade that Mesopotamia hands on. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
