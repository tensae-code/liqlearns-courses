# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Babylon's king Hammurabi (the book dates him 1750 BCE) carves his laws on stone so all can read them; around the law the chapter builds the whole economy — farmers, weavers, metal-workers, merchants sailing the Gulf — the gifts of writing, law and trade that Mesopotamia hands on.
