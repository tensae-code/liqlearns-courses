# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Babylon's king Hammurabi (the book dates him 1750 BCE) carves his laws on stone so all can read them; around the law the chapter builds the whole economy — farmers, weavers, metal-workers, merchants sailing the Gulf — the gifts of writing, law and trade that Mesopotamia hands on.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: the Code of Hammurabi and Mesopotamia's working life” is mainly about this: China's 4,000-year story opens on its rivers — the Shang kingdom (the book's map: 1028–1523 BCE), the Zhou who follow, silk weaving, oracle-bone writing, and in 221 BCE Qin Shi Huang welding the warring states into one China.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Babylon's king Hammurabi (the book dates him 1750 BCE) carves his laws on stone so all can read them; around the law the chapter builds the whole economy — farmers, weavers, metal-workers, merchants sailing the Gulf — the gifts of writing, law and trade that Mesopotamia hands on.
