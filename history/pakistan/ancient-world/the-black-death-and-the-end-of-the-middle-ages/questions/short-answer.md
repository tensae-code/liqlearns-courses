# The Black Death and the End of the Middle Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 1: the end of the Middle Ages”?
**Answer:** The old world dies of three blows — the Hundred Years War between England and France (1337–1453), the Black Death of 1347–1351 that empties whole towns, and Gutenberg's printing press of about 1440 that makes books cheap and ideas unstoppable.
