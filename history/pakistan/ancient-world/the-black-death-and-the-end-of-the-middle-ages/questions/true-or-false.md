# The Black Death and the End of the Middle Ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The old world dies of three blows — the Hundred Years War between England and France (1337–1453), the Black Death of 1347–1351 that empties whole towns, and Gutenberg's printing press of about 1440 that makes books cheap and ideas unstoppable.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: the end of the Middle Ages” is mainly about this: Tughril Beg's Turks take Baghdad and become Islam's sword-arm; Alp Arslan crushes Byzantium at Manzikert in 1071 and opens Anatolia; Malik Shah and his vizier Nizam-ul-Mulk hold the empire together; and in Seljuk Konya the poet Rumi (1207–1273) sings — the dynasty's other conquest.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The old world dies of three blows — the Hundred Years War between England and France (1337–1453), the Black Death of 1347–1351 that empties whole towns, and Gutenberg's printing press of about 1440 that makes books cheap and ideas unstoppable.
