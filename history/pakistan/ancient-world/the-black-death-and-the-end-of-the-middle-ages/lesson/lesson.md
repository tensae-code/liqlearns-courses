# The Black Death and the End of the Middle Ages

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Black Death and the End of the Middle Ages** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The old world dies of three blows — the Hundred Years War between England and France (1337–1453), the Black Death of 1347–1351 that empties whole towns, and Gutenberg's printing press of about 1440 that makes books cheap and ideas unstoppable.
