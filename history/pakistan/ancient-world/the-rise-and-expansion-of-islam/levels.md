# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 4: the rise of Islamic civilisation — the Prophet's life
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Muhammad, born in Makkah in 571, orphaned young, known as Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin, marries Khadija and in 610 receives the first revelation in Cave Hira; a decade of persecution, the boycott and the Year of Sorrow follow; the migration to Abyssinia in 615 and the Hijra to Madinah in 622 — with Abu Bakr, hiding in Cave Thawr — open the Hijri era; then Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627), Hudaybiyyah (628), the peaceful conquest of Makkah (630), and the Farewell Pilgrimage and his death in 632. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 4: the Khulfa-e-Rashideen (632–661)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The four rightly-guided caliphs carry the new civilisation outward — Abu Bakr (632–634) unites Arabia and has the Quran compiled; Umar (634–644) defeats Persia and Byzantium and builds the Bait-ul-Mal and the administration; Usman (644–656) gives the Quran its standard text and is martyred; Ali (656–661) rules from Kufa and is martyred in 661. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 2: the Umayyad Dynasty (661–750)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Muawiyah makes the caliphate a kingdom at Damascus and builds its post and Diwan; under Abd-ul-Malik the Dome of the Rock rises in Jerusalem (691–692) and Arabic coinage is struck; Al-Walid raises the great Umayyad Mosque; Sulaiman and the pious Umar II follow — an empire of some 62 million people from Spain to Sindh. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
