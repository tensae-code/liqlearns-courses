# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Muhammad, born in Makkah in 571, orphaned young, known as Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin, marries Khadija and in 610 receives the first revelation in Cave Hira; a decade of persecution, the boycott and the Year of Sorrow follow; the migration to Abyssinia in 615 and the Hijra to Madinah in 622 — with Abu Bakr, hiding in Cave Thawr — open the Hijri era; then Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627), Hudaybiyyah (628), the peaceful conquest of Makkah (630), and the Farewell Pilgrimage and his death in 632.

Muhammad, born in Makkah in 571, orphaned young, known as Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin, marries Khadija and in 610 receives the first revelation in Cave Hira; a decade of persecution, the boycott and the Year of Sorrow follow; the migration to Abyssinia in 615 and the Hijra to Madinah in 622 — with Abu Bakr, hiding in Cave Thawr — open the Hijri era; then Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627), Hudaybiyyah (628), the peaceful conquest of Makkah (630), and the Farewell Pilgrimage and his death in 632.

The four rightly-guided caliphs carry the new civilisation outward — Abu Bakr (632–634) unites Arabia and has the Quran compiled; Umar (634–644) defeats Persia and Byzantium and builds the Bait-ul-Mal and the administration; Usman (644–656) gives the Quran its standard text and is martyred; Ali (656–661) rules from Kufa and is martyred in 661.

Muawiyah makes the caliphate a kingdom at Damascus and builds its post and Diwan; under Abd-ul-Malik the Dome of the Rock rises in Jerusalem (691–692) and Arabic coinage is struck; Al-Walid raises the great Umayyad Mosque; Sulaiman and the pious Umar II follow — an empire of some 62 million people from Spain to Sindh.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Pakistan.
