# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: the rise of Islamic civilisation — the Prophet's life”?
**Answer:** Muhammad, born in Makkah in 571, orphaned young, known as Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin, marries Khadija and in 610 receives the first revelation in Cave Hira; a decade of persecution, the boycott and the Year of Sorrow follow; the migration to Abyssinia in 615 and the Hijra to Madinah in 622 — with Abu Bakr, hiding in Cave Thawr — open the Hijri era; then Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627), Hudaybiyyah (628), the peaceful conquest of Makkah (630), and the Farewell Pilgrimage and his death in 632.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 4: the Khulfa-e-Rashideen (632–661)” end?
**Answer:** The four rightly-guided caliphs carry the new civilisation outward — Abu Bakr (632–634) unites Arabia and has the Quran compiled; Umar (634–644) defeats Persia and Byzantium and builds the Bait-ul-Mal and the administration; Usman (644–656) gives the Quran its standard text and is martyred; Ali (656–661) rules from Kufa and is martyred in 661.
