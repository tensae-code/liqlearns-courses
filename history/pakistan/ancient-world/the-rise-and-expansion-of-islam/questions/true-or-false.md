# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Muhammad, born in Makkah in 571, orphaned young, known as Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin, marries Khadija and in 610 receives the first revelation in Cave Hira; a decade of persecution, the boycott and the Year of Sorrow follow; the migration to Abyssinia in 615 and the Hijra to Madinah in 622 — with Abu Bakr, hiding in Cave Thawr — open the Hijri era; then Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627), Hudaybiyyah (628), the peaceful conquest of Makkah (630), and the Farewell Pilgrimage and his death in 632.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 4: the Khulfa-e-Rashideen (632–661)” is mainly about this: In 1215 England's barons corner King John and make him sign the Great Charter — for the first time a king's power is written down as less than the law, a seed from which parliaments grow.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The four rightly-guided caliphs carry the new civilisation outward — Abu Bakr (632–634) unites Arabia and has the Quran compiled; Umar (634–644) defeats Persia and Byzantium and builds the Bait-ul-Mal and the administration; Usman (644–656) gives the Quran its standard text and is martyred; Ali (656–661) rules from Kufa and is martyred in 661.
