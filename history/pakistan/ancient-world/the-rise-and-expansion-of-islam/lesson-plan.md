## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 4: the rise of Islamic civilisation — the Prophet's life** — Muhammad, born in Makkah in 571, orphaned young, known as Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin, marries Khadija and in 610 receives the first revelation in Cave Hira; a decade of persecution, the boycott and the Year of Sorrow follow; the migration to Abyssinia in 615 and the Hijra to Madinah in 622 — with Abu Bakr, hiding in Cave Thawr — open the Hijri era; then Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627), Hudaybiyyah (628), the peaceful conquest of Makkah (630), and the Farewell Pilgrimage and his death in 632.
2. **Chapter 4: the Khulfa-e-Rashideen (632–661)** — The four rightly-guided caliphs carry the new civilisation outward — Abu Bakr (632–634) unites Arabia and has the Quran compiled; Umar (634–644) defeats Persia and Byzantium and builds the Bait-ul-Mal and the administration; Usman (644–656) gives the Quran its standard text and is martyred; Ali (656–661) rules from Kufa and is martyred in 661.
3. **Chapter 2: the Umayyad Dynasty (661–750)** — Muawiyah makes the caliphate a kingdom at Damascus and builds its post and Diwan; under Abd-ul-Malik the Dome of the Rock rises in Jerusalem (691–692) and Arabic coinage is struck; Al-Walid raises the great Umayyad Mosque; Sulaiman and the pious Umar II follow — an empire of some 62 million people from Spain to Sindh.
