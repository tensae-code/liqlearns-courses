## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 4: the Greek civilisation (1600–146 BCE)

Greece is a necklace of rival city-states — Athens, Sparta, Thebes, Olympia — worshipping Zeus's family, dressing in the chiton, and thinking with Socrates, Plato and Aristotle, until Alexander carries Greek ways across the world and Rome finally swallows Greece itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 2: the Greek civilisation and its periods

Greek history is told in three acts — the Archaic age of Homer's Iliad and Odyssey, the Classical age of Pericles, Euripides and Plato's Academy, and the Hellenistic age after Alexander's death in 323 BCE, when Greek culture becomes a world culture.
