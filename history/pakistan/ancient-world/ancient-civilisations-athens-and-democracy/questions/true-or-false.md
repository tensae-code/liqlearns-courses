# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Greece is a necklace of rival city-states — Athens, Sparta, Thebes, Olympia — worshipping Zeus's family, dressing in the chiton, and thinking with Socrates, Plato and Aristotle, until Alexander carries Greek ways across the world and Rome finally swallows Greece itself.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 2: the Greek civilisation and its periods” is mainly about this: The book's own timeline tells the fall in dates — republic in 509 BCE, empire from 27 BCE, the empire split in two in 395 CE, and the western throne empty in 476 CE.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Greek history is told in three acts — the Archaic age of Homer's Iliad and Odyssey, the Classical age of Pericles, Euripides and Plato's Academy, and the Hellenistic age after Alexander's death in 323 BCE, when Greek culture becomes a world culture.
