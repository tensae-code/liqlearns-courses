# Medieval World: The Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 3: the Ottoman Dynasty (1299–1923)”?
**Answer:** Osman I's little Anatolian beylik grows for six centuries — Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, Selim I takes Egypt and the Hijaz in 1517, Suleiman besieges Vienna; the Janissaries rise and rot; the empire shrinks through Karlowitz (1699) and Greek independence (1821) until the Turkish Republic replaces it in 1923.
