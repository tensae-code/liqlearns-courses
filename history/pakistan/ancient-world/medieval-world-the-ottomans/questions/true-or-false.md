# Medieval World: The Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Osman I's little Anatolian beylik grows for six centuries — Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, Selim I takes Egypt and the Hijaz in 1517, Suleiman besieges Vienna; the Janissaries rise and rot; the empire shrinks through Karlowitz (1699) and Greek independence (1821) until the Turkish Republic replaces it in 1923.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 3: the Ottoman Dynasty (1299–1923)” is mainly about this: Zahir-ud-Din Babar (1483–1530), heir of Timur and Genghis Khan, loses Samarkand and Ferghana and turns to India; at Panipat in 1526 his small organised army, cannon and tulughma tactics destroy Ibrahim Lodhi's host; Khanwa (1527) breaks the Rajputs; his Babarnama remains the empire's founding diary.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Osman I's little Anatolian beylik grows for six centuries — Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, Selim I takes Egypt and the Hijaz in 1517, Suleiman besieges Vienna; the Janissaries rise and rot; the empire shrinks through Karlowitz (1699) and Greek independence (1821) until the Turkish Republic replaces it in 1923.
