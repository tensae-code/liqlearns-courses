# Medieval World: The Ottomans — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Osman I's little Anatolian beylik grows for six centuries — Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, Selim I takes Egypt and the Hijaz in 1517, Suleiman besieges Vienna; the Janissaries rise and rot; the empire shrinks through Karlowitz (1699) and Greek independence (1821) until the Turkish Republic replaces it in 1923. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
