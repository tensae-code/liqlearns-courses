# Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Around 1500 BCE Aryan tribes migrate through the Khyber into the Punjab — pushed, the book says, by cold, hunger and shrinking pastures; they transform the local civilisation, order society into Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas and Shudras, and worship Indra, Agni and Varuna in the hymns of the Vedas.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 3: the Aryans” is mainly about this: When Rome's western empire falls, Germanic tribes carve Europe into little kingdoms; the Pope crowns Charlemagne and calls his realm a Holy Roman Empire; land becomes the only wealth, and society stacks into kings, lords, knights and serfs — the feudal pyramid the chapter draws in full, with daily life, manorial agriculture, trade, guild schools and inventions (spinning wheel, compass, printing press, gunpowder) as its texture.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Around 1500 BCE Aryan tribes migrate through the Khyber into the Punjab — pushed, the book says, by cold, hunger and shrinking pastures; they transform the local civilisation, order society into Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas and Shudras, and worship Indra, Agni and Varuna in the hymns of the Vedas.
