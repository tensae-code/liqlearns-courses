# Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 3: the Aryans”?
**Answer:** Around 1500 BCE Aryan tribes migrate through the Khyber into the Punjab — pushed, the book says, by cold, hunger and shrinking pastures; they transform the local civilisation, order society into Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas and Shudras, and worship Indra, Agni and Varuna in the hymns of the Vedas.
