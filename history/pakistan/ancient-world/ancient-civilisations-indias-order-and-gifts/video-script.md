# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Around 1500 BCE Aryan tribes migrate through the Khyber into the Punjab — pushed, the book says, by cold, hunger and shrinking pastures; they transform the local civilisation, order society into Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas and Shudras, and worship Indra, Agni and Varuna in the hymns of the Vedas.
