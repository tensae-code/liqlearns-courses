# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Cyrus founds the Achaemenid Empire in 550 BCE and takes Babylon in 539; Cambyses adds Egypt in 525; Darius the Great organises twenty satrapies, builds the Royal Road from Sardis to Susa and a postal relay to hold it together; Zoroaster's faith burns underneath it all — until Alexander ends the empire in 331 BCE.
