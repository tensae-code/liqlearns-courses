# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Cyrus founds the Achaemenid Empire in 550 BCE and takes Babylon in 539; Cambyses adds Egypt in 525; Darius the Great organises twenty satrapies, builds the Royal Road from Sardis to Susa and a postal relay to hold it together; Zoroaster's faith burns underneath it all — until Alexander ends the empire in 331 BCE.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 2: the ancient Persian civilisation” is mainly about this: Around 1500 BCE Aryan tribes migrate through the Khyber into the Punjab — pushed, the book says, by cold, hunger and shrinking pastures; they transform the local civilisation, order society into Brahmins, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas and Shudras, and worship Indra, Agni and Varuna in the hymns of the Vedas.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Cyrus founds the Achaemenid Empire in 550 BCE and takes Babylon in 539; Cambyses adds Egypt in 525; Darius the Great organises twenty satrapies, builds the Royal Road from Sardis to Susa and a postal relay to hold it together; Zoroaster's faith burns underneath it all — until Alexander ends the empire in 331 BCE.
