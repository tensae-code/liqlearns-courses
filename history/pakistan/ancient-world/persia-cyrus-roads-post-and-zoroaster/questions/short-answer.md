# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 2: the ancient Persian civilisation”?
**Answer:** Cyrus founds the Achaemenid Empire in 550 BCE and takes Babylon in 539; Cambyses adds Egypt in 525; Darius the Great organises twenty satrapies, builds the Royal Road from Sardis to Susa and a postal relay to hold it together; Zoroaster's faith burns underneath it all — until Alexander ends the empire in 331 BCE.
