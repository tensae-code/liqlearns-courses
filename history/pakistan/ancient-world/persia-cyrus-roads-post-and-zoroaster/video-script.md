# Video script — Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Cyrus founds the Achaemenid Empire in 550 BCE and takes Babylon in 539; Cambyses adds Egypt in 525; Darius the Great organises twenty satrapies, builds the Royal Road from Sardis to Susa and a postal relay to hold it together; Zoroaster's faith burns underneath it all — until Alexander ends the empire in 331 BCE.
