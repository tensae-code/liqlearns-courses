# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Between the Tigris and Euphrates the Sumerians, Assyrians and Babylonians raise the first cities in the Fertile Crescent, press writing into clay tablets, build temples like Babel and govern by written laws.

From about 4500 BCE the land between the rivers fills with cities; the Sumerians press cuneiform into clay, sing of Gilgamesh, raise ziggurats to their gods and trade from the Gulf to the Indus.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Pakistan.
