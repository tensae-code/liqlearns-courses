# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Between the Tigris and Euphrates the Sumerians, Assyrians and Babylonians raise the first cities in the Fertile Crescent, press writing into clay tablets, build temples like Babel and govern by written laws.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 1: the Mesopotamian civilisation” is mainly about this: After the failed war of 1857 the Muslims of India organise; the Lahore Resolution of 23 March 1940 demands a separate homeland; and each province plays its part — Punjab passes the resolution's spirit at the Minar-e-Pakistan's ground, Sindh, the land where Islam first entered with Muhammad bin Qasim in 712 and which the book calls Bab-ul-Islam, becomes the first province to vote for Pakistan in 1943, Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa join by their own decisions and referendum, until 14 August 1947.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From about 4500 BCE the land between the rivers fills with cities; the Sumerians press cuneiform into clay, sing of Gilgamesh, raise ziggurats to their gods and trade from the Gulf to the Indus.
