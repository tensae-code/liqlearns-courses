# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: Early Civilisations 1 — Mesopotamia”?
**Answer:** Between the Tigris and Euphrates the Sumerians, Assyrians and Babylonians raise the first cities in the Fertile Crescent, press writing into clay tablets, build temples like Babel and govern by written laws.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 1: the Mesopotamian civilisation” end?
**Answer:** From about 4500 BCE the land between the rivers fills with cities; the Sumerians press cuneiform into clay, sing of Gilgamesh, raise ziggurats to their gods and trade from the Gulf to the Indus.
