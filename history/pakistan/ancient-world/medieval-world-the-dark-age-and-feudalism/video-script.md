# Video script — Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

When Rome's western empire falls, Germanic tribes carve Europe into little kingdoms; the Pope crowns Charlemagne and calls his realm a Holy Roman Empire; land becomes the only wealth, and society stacks into kings, lords, knights and serfs — the feudal pyramid the chapter draws in full, with daily life, manorial agriculture, trade, guild schools and inventions (spinning wheel, compass, printing press, gunpowder) as its texture.
