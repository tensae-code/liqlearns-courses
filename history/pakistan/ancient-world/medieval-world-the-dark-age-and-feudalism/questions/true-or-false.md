# Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  When Rome's western empire falls, Germanic tribes carve Europe into little kingdoms; the Pope crowns Charlemagne and calls his realm a Holy Roman Empire; land becomes the only wealth, and society stacks into kings, lords, knights and serfs — the feudal pyramid the chapter draws in full, with daily life, manorial agriculture, trade, guild schools and inventions (spinning wheel, compass, printing press, gunpowder) as its texture.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 1: Middle Ages in Europe (500–1500 CE) — the early centuries and the feudal order” is mainly about this: The old world dies of three blows — the Hundred Years War between England and France (1337–1453), the Black Death of 1347–1351 that empties whole towns, and Gutenberg's printing press of about 1440 that makes books cheap and ideas unstoppable.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: When Rome's western empire falls, Germanic tribes carve Europe into little kingdoms; the Pope crowns Charlemagne and calls his realm a Holy Roman Empire; land becomes the only wealth, and society stacks into kings, lords, knights and serfs — the feudal pyramid the chapter draws in full, with daily life, manorial agriculture, trade, guild schools and inventions (spinning wheel, compass, printing press, gunpowder) as its texture.
