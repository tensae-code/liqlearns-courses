# Gandhara: Where Greece Met the Buddha — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: the Gandhara civilisation (600 BCE–5th century CE)”?
**Answer:** In Pakistan's own north-west, around Peshawar, Swat and Taxila, a kingdom stands where empires collide — Persian satraps, Alexander's march of 327–326 BCE, Ashoka turning it Buddhist and raising stupas, Kanishka spreading the faith, and Greek sculptors carving the Buddha's face — until the White Huns burn it down around 500 CE.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 3: the Kushan Empire” end?
**Answer:** The Kushans, Central Asian nomads turned kings — Kadphises I, Vima Kadphises with his gold coins, and Kanishka (127–151 CE), whose empire stitches the Silk Road from China to Rome; under them Gandhara's Buddhism travels to China and Sanskrit learning flourishes.
