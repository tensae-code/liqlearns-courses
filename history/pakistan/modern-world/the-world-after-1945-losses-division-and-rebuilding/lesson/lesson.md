# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

From the ruins come two armed blocs and a new world body meant to keep the peace.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: From the ruins come two armed blocs and a new world body meant to keep the peace. Give one real example: From the ruins come two armed blocs and a new world body meant to keep the peace. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: From the ruins come two armed blocs and a new world body meant to keep the peace.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
