# Muslim Reform Movements After 1857 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: Historical Personalities — Sir Syed Ahmad Khan (1817–1898)”?
**Answer:** After the catastrophe of 1857, Sir Syed argues the Muslims of India must learn the new knowledge or be left behind — he opens schools at Muradabad and Ghazipur, founds the Scientific Society in 1863, raises the college at Aligarh that becomes a university, and plants the two-nation idea from which Pakistan grows.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Ch.2 Reform Movements: after 1857 Muslims are blamed as rebels, lose jobs and land, fall behind in education as Persian gives way to English” end?
**Answer:** A defeated community is punished and shut out, and its leaders must decide whether to fight the British or learn from them.
