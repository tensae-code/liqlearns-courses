# Muslim Reform Movements After 1857 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   After the catastrophe of 1857, Sir Syed argues the Muslims of India must learn the new knowledge or be left behind — he opens schools at Muradabad and Ghazipur, founds the Scientific Society in 1863, raises the college at Aligarh that becomes a university, and plants the two-nation idea from which Pakistan grows.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Ch.2 Reform Movements: after 1857 Muslims are blamed as rebels, lose jobs and land, fall behind in education as Persian gives way to English” is mainly about this: Babylon's king Hammurabi (the book dates him 1750 BCE) carves his laws on stone so all can read them; around the law the chapter builds the whole economy — farmers, weavers, metal-workers, merchants sailing the Gulf — the gifts of writing, law and trade that Mesopotamia hands on.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A defeated community is punished and shut out, and its leaders must decide whether to fight the British or learn from them.
