# The East India Company: Plassey and Buxar

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The East India Company: Plassey and Buxar** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 4: the British took over the Subcontinent

After the failed rising of 1857 the East India Company's rule passes to the Crown; the book closes the chapter with the challenges the Muslims of the Subcontinent then faced — political eclipse, economic ruin and an education gap.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch.1: the East India Company gets a Mughal farman, beats the Portuguese, then wins Bengal at Plassey (1757) through Mir Jafar's betrayal and Buxar (1764)

A trading company turns political, Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar hands it Bengal, and Buxar makes it master of northern India.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch.1: the Doctrine of Lapse under Dalhousie annexes state after state whose ruler dies without a natural heir

A legal rule becomes a land-grab, Indian rulers learn adoption no longer protects their thrones, and resentment builds toward 1857.
