## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 4: the British took over the Subcontinent** — After the failed rising of 1857 the East India Company's rule passes to the Crown; the book closes the chapter with the challenges the Muslims of the Subcontinent then faced — political eclipse, economic ruin and an education gap.
2. **Ch.1: the East India Company gets a Mughal farman, beats the Portuguese, then wins Bengal at Plassey (1757) through Mir Jafar's betrayal and Buxar (1764)** — A trading company turns political, Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar hands it Bengal, and Buxar makes it master of northern India.
3. **Ch.1: the Doctrine of Lapse under Dalhousie annexes state after state whose ruler dies without a natural heir** — A legal rule becomes a land-grab, Indian rulers learn adoption no longer protects their thrones, and resentment builds toward 1857.
