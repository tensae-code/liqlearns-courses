# The East India Company: Plassey and Buxar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 4: the British took over the Subcontinent”?
**Answer:** After the failed rising of 1857 the East India Company's rule passes to the Crown; the book closes the chapter with the challenges the Muslims of the Subcontinent then faced — political eclipse, economic ruin and an education gap.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Ch.1: the East India Company gets a Mughal farman, beats the Portuguese, then wins Bengal at Plassey (1757) through Mir Jafar's betrayal and Buxar (1764)” end?
**Answer:** A trading company turns political, Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar hands it Bengal, and Buxar makes it master of northern India.
