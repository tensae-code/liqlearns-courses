# The East India Company: Plassey and Buxar — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   After the failed rising of 1857 the East India Company's rule passes to the Crown; the book closes the chapter with the challenges the Muslims of the Subcontinent then faced — political eclipse, economic ruin and an education gap.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Ch.1: the East India Company gets a Mughal farman, beats the Portuguese, then wins Bengal at Plassey (1757) through Mir Jafar's betrayal and Buxar (1764)” is mainly about this: Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519), painter-engineer of the Mona Lisa; Michelangelo (1475–1564), sculptor of the Pieta and painter of the Last Judgment; Raphael (1483–1520) of the Madonnas; Shakespeare (1564–1616); Galileo (1564–1642) with his telescope; and Thomas More (1478–1535), the Utopia writer executed by his king.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A trading company turns political, Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar hands it Bengal, and Buxar makes it master of northern India.
