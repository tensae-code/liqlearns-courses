# Pakistan 1947: Early Problems, Refugees and State-Building — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   A state born in partition must house, feed and govern millions before it has offices, money or staff.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Ch.5: the Objectives Resolution (12 March 1949), moved by Liaquat Ali Khan, fixes the Islamic-democratic frame every later constitution follows” is mainly about this: A country breaks in two in 1971, and a generation later its western border makes it a frontline state again.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Before any constitution, the founding assembly agrees sovereignty belongs to Allah and power is a trust from the people.
