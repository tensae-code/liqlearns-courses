# Pakistan 1947: Early Problems, Refugees and State-Building — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Ch.5 Pakistan a New Country: unfair boundary awards (Ferozepur), millions of refugees arrive, canal water is cut and administration starts from almost nothing in Karachi”?
**Answer:** A state born in partition must house, feed and govern millions before it has offices, money or staff.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Ch.5: the Objectives Resolution (12 March 1949), moved by Liaquat Ali Khan, fixes the Islamic-democratic frame every later constitution follows” end?
**Answer:** Before any constitution, the founding assembly agrees sovereignty belongs to Allah and power is a trust from the people.
