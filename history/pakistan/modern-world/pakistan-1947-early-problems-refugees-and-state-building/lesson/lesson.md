## Sub lesson 1 — Ch.5 Pakistan a New Country: unfair boundary awards (Ferozepur), millions of refugees arrive, canal water is cut and administration starts from almost nothing in Karachi

A state born in partition must house, feed and govern millions before it has offices, money or staff.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch.5: the Objectives Resolution (12 March 1949), moved by Liaquat Ali Khan, fixes the Islamic-democratic frame every later constitution follows

Before any constitution, the founding assembly agrees sovereignty belongs to Allah and power is a trust from the people.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch.5: the constitutions of 1956, 1962 and 1973 compared — parliamentary, presidential, then federal-parliamentary with Islamic provisions

It takes three constitutions and twenty-six years for Pakistan to settle the rules its founders argued over.
