## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch.5 Pakistan a New Country: unfair boundary awards (Ferozepur), millions of refugees arrive, canal water is cut and administration starts from almost nothing in Karachi** — A state born in partition must house, feed and govern millions before it has offices, money or staff.
2. **Ch.5: the Objectives Resolution (12 March 1949), moved by Liaquat Ali Khan, fixes the Islamic-democratic frame every later constitution follows** — Before any constitution, the founding assembly agrees sovereignty belongs to Allah and power is a trust from the people.
3. **Ch.5: the constitutions of 1956, 1962 and 1973 compared — parliamentary, presidential, then federal-parliamentary with Islamic provisions** — It takes three constitutions and twenty-six years for Pakistan to settle the rules its founders argued over.
