# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — short answer

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 5: the Industrial Revolution and the steam engine”?
**Answer:** England, 1760–1840: steam is tamed step by step — Savery's pump of 1698, Newcomen's engine of 1712 draining mines, Watt's engine of 1776 — then steam takes to rails (the 1829 locomotive trials) and water (Fulton's Clermont of 1807, Trevithick's first railway engine of 1804).
