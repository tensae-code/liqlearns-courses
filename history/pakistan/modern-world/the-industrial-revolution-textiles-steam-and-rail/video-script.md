# Video script — The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

England, 1760–1840: steam is tamed step by step — Savery's pump of 1698, Newcomen's engine of 1712 draining mines, Watt's engine of 1776 — then steam takes to rails (the 1829 locomotive trials) and water (Fulton's Clermont of 1807, Trevithick's first railway engine of 1804).
