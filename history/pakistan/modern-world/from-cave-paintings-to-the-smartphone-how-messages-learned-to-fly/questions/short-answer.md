# From Cave Paintings to the Smartphone: How Messages Learned to Fly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 2 (Culture): Means of Communication”?
**Answer:** The first messages are paintings on cave walls; then come letters and the post card, Marconi's wireless of 1901, Baird's television (Pakistan's own TV starts in 1964), the telephone in the pocket and Babbage's computer — every generation's message travels faster and farther than the last.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 2 (Culture): Means of Communication” end?
**Answer:** The Class 4 story continues into the age of mass media — newspapers and magazines, radio and television, then the computer, the internet and social media shrink the world into what the book, quoting Marshall McLuhan, calls a global village.
