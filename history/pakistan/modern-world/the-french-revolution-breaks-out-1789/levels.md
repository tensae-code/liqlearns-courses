# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In 1789 France's revolution proclaims the rights of man against the old regime; the book's portrait box gives its heir — Napoleon Bonaparte (1769–1821), general, emperor from 1804, fallen by 1815. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
