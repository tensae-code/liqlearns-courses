# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Columbus reaches America in 1492 seeking India; John Cabot claims North America's coast for England in 1497; Cabral lands in Brazil in 1500; and Captain Cook (1728–1779) charts the Pacific — Europe's map of the world is redrawn in a generation.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 5: the Age of Exploration” is mainly about this: In the 17th and 18th centuries reason itself becomes the banner — thinkers argue that knowledge, law and government must answer to evidence and the "value of reason", preparing the ground for revolution.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Columbus reaches America in 1492 seeking India; John Cabot claims North America's coast for England in 1497; Cabral lands in Brazil in 1500; and Captain Cook (1728–1779) charts the Pacific — Europe's map of the world is redrawn in a generation.
