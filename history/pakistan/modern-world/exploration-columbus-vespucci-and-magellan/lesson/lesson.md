# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.
