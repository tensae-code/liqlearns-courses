# Video script — Babar and Panipat: The Founding of the Mughal Empire


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Zahir-ud-Din Babar (1483–1530), heir of Timur and Genghis Khan, loses Samarkand and Ferghana and turns to India; at Panipat in 1526 his small organised army, cannon and tulughma tactics destroy Ibrahim Lodhi's host; Khanwa (1527) breaks the Rajputs; his Babarnama remains the empire's founding diary.
