# Babar and Panipat: The Founding of the Mughal Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 4: Babar, the first Mughal ruler”?
**Answer:** Zahir-ud-Din Babar (1483–1530), heir of Timur and Genghis Khan, loses Samarkand and Ferghana and turns to India; at Panipat in 1526 his small organised army, cannon and tulughma tactics destroy Ibrahim Lodhi's host; Khanwa (1527) breaks the Rajputs; his Babarnama remains the empire's founding diary.
