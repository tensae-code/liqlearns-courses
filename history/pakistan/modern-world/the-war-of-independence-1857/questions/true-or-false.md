# The War of Independence, 1857 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Ch.1: sepoys refuse greased cartridges and rise in 1857; the war fails, the Company is abolished and Queen Victoria's Proclamation of 1858 puts India under the Crown” is mainly about this: The peace punishes Germany so hard it stores up the anger of the 1930s.
