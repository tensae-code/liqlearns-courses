# The War of Independence, 1857

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The War of Independence, 1857** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule. Give one real example: A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
