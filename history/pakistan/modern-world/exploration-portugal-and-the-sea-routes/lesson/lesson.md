## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 5: the Portuguese sea road

Vasco da Gama (1460–1524) rounds Africa and reaches India by sea in 1498, breaking the land routes' monopoly and opening the ocean road that brings Europe — and eventually empire — to Asia's ports.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch.1 Arrival of the British: Arab traders, Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut 1498, sea route opens

Arab merchants profit carrying Indian goods to Europe, da Gama's landing shows Europeans the sea road and the race for India begins.
