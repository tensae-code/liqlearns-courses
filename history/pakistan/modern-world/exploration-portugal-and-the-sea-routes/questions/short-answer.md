# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 5: the Portuguese sea road”?
**Answer:** Vasco da Gama (1460–1524) rounds Africa and reaches India by sea in 1498, breaking the land routes' monopoly and opening the ocean road that brings Europe — and eventually empire — to Asia's ports.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Ch.1 Arrival of the British: Arab traders, Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut 1498, sea route opens” end?
**Answer:** Arab merchants profit carrying Indian goods to Europe, da Gama's landing shows Europeans the sea road and the race for India begins.
