# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Vasco da Gama (1460–1524) rounds Africa and reaches India by sea in 1498, breaking the land routes' monopoly and opening the ocean road that brings Europe — and eventually empire — to Asia's ports.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Ch.1 Arrival of the British: Arab traders, Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut 1498, sea route opens” is mainly about this: In 1789 France's revolution proclaims the rights of man against the old regime; the book's portrait box gives its heir — Napoleon Bonaparte (1769–1821), general, emperor from 1804, fallen by 1815.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Arab merchants profit carrying Indian goods to Europe, da Gama's landing shows Europeans the sea road and the race for India begins.
