# Shah Waliullah and the Faraizi Movement: Revival Before 1857 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  As Mughal power fades, renewal comes from scholars — Shah Waliullah (1703–1762) translates the Quran into Persian and Urdu and writes fifty-one books to revive Muslim society; in Bengal, Haji Shariatullah's Faraizi Movement (1781–1840) calls peasants back to the faraiz, the core duties of the faith.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 4: Shah Waliullah Dehlavi and Haji Shariatullah” is mainly about this: Columbus reaches America in 1492 seeking India; John Cabot claims North America's coast for England in 1497; Cabral lands in Brazil in 1500; and Captain Cook (1728–1779) charts the Pacific — Europe's map of the world is redrawn in a generation.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: As Mughal power fades, renewal comes from scholars — Shah Waliullah (1703–1762) translates the Quran into Persian and Urdu and writes fifty-one books to revive Muslim society; in Bengal, Haji Shariatullah's Faraizi Movement (1781–1840) calls peasants back to the faraiz, the core duties of the faith.
