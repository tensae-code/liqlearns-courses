# Video script — Shah Waliullah and the Faraizi Movement: Revival Before 1857


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

As Mughal power fades, renewal comes from scholars — Shah Waliullah (1703–1762) translates the Quran into Persian and Urdu and writes fifty-one books to revive Muslim society; in Bengal, Haji Shariatullah's Faraizi Movement (1781–1840) calls peasants back to the faraiz, the core duties of the faith.
