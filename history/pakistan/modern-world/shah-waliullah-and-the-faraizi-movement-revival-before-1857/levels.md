# Shah Waliullah and the Faraizi Movement: Revival Before 1857 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — As Mughal power fades, renewal comes from scholars — Shah Waliullah (1703–1762) translates the Quran into Persian and Urdu and writes fifty-one books to revive Muslim society; in Bengal, Haji Shariatullah's Faraizi Movement (1781–1840) calls peasants back to the faraiz, the core duties of the faith. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
