# Kashmir and the Indus Waters Dispute — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  A Muslim-majority princely state's fate is frozen by war and a promised vote that never happens.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Ch.5: the Kashmir war of 1947–48 — Kashmiri Muslims rise, India claims the state, the UN Security Council calls for a plebiscite India refuses to hold” is mainly about this: Pakistan is introduced as a house of many cultures — languages, dress, foods and festivals differing from valley to valley; the chapter sends children to a museum and sets them collecting old coins, stamps and postcards, learning that a people's past is a thing you can hold and must keep.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A Muslim-majority princely state's fate is frozen by war and a promised vote that never happens.
