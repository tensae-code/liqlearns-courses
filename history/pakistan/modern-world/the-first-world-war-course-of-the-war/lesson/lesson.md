# The First World War: Course of the War

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The First World War: Course of the War** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Germany wins in the east but bleeds in the trenches of the west until revolution knocks Russia out.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Germany wins in the east but bleeds in the trenches of the west until revolution knocks Russia out. Give one real example: Germany wins in the east but bleeds in the trenches of the west until revolution knocks Russia out. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Germany wins in the east but bleeds in the trenches of the west until revolution knocks Russia out.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
