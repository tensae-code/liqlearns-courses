# Lesson plan — The Founders: Jinnah, Iqbal and Fatima Jinnah

**Course:** History — Pakistan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Founders: Jinnah, Iqbal and Fatima Jinnah
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The Pakistan Movement and Pakistan Since 1947 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Three lives carry the founding — Muhammad Ali Jinnah, born in Karachi in 1876, who joins the Muslim League in 1913, presides over the 1940 Lahore session and becomes the new state's first Governor-General before his death in 1948; Allama Iqbal, born in Sialkot in 1877, whose 1930 Allahabad address first dreams the separate homeland aloud; and Fatima Jinnah, the Quaid's sister, a dentist who stands beside the movement and is remembered as Madar-e-Millat, Mother of the Nation.
