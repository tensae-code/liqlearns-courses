# Aurangzeb, the Marathas and the Long Mughal Decline — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Aurangzeb stretches the empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas (1630–1680), revolts from Punjab to the south, and after his death a parade of weak emperors until the last, Bahadur Shah Zafar, is exiled to Rangoon after 1857 and dies there in 1862.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 4: Aurangzeb Alamgir (1658–1707) and the decline” is mainly about this: In the 15th and 16th centuries Europe is "reborn" — the Crusades' contacts, Constantinople's fall, and wealthy patrons turn minds back to Greece and Rome and outward to experiment; the chapter names it a cultural, social and intellectual revolution.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Aurangzeb stretches the empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas (1630–1680), revolts from Punjab to the south, and after his death a parade of weak emperors until the last, Bahadur Shah Zafar, is exiled to Rangoon after 1857 and dies there in 1862.
