# Aurangzeb, the Marathas and the Long Mughal Decline — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 4: Aurangzeb Alamgir (1658–1707) and the decline”?
**Answer:** Aurangzeb stretches the empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas (1630–1680), revolts from Punjab to the south, and after his death a parade of weak emperors until the last, Bahadur Shah Zafar, is exiled to Rangoon after 1857 and dies there in 1862.
