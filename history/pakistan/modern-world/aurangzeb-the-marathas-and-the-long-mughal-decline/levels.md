# Aurangzeb, the Marathas and the Long Mughal Decline — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Aurangzeb stretches the empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas (1630–1680), revolts from Punjab to the south, and after his death a parade of weak emperors until the last, Bahadur Shah Zafar, is exiled to Rangoon after 1857 and dies there in 1862. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
