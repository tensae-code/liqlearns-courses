# Video script — Aurangzeb, the Marathas and the Long Mughal Decline


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Aurangzeb stretches the empire to its greatest size and breaks it doing so — endless Deccan wars against Shivaji's Marathas (1630–1680), revolts from Punjab to the south, and after his death a parade of weak emperors until the last, Bahadur Shah Zafar, is exiled to Rangoon after 1857 and dies there in 1862.
