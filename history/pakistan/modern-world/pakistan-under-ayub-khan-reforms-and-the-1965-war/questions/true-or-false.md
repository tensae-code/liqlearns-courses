# Pakistan Under Ayub Khan: Reforms and the 1965 War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   A general promises development instead of politics, builds dams and factories, and settles the water war on paper.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Ch.5: the Indo-Pak War of 1965 — causes, the fighting, national unity and the ceasefire” is mainly about this: The first messages are paintings on cave walls; then come letters and the post card, Marconi's wireless of 1901, Baird's television (Pakistan's own TV starts in 1964), the telephone in the pocket and Babbage's computer — every generation's message travels faster and farther than the last.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Kashmir sparks a seventeen-day war that ends where it began but lives on as a national epic.
