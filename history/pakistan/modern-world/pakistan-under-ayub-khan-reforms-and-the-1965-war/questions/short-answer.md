# Pakistan Under Ayub Khan: Reforms and the 1965 War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Ch.5: Ayub Khan's regime — presidential system, monetary policy, five-year plans, industrialisation, administrative reforms and a new capital at Islamabad; the Indus Basin Treaty (1960) divides the rivers with India”?
**Answer:** A general promises development instead of politics, builds dams and factories, and settles the water war on paper.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Ch.5: the Indo-Pak War of 1965 — causes, the fighting, national unity and the ceasefire” end?
**Answer:** Kashmir sparks a seventeen-day war that ends where it began but lives on as a national epic.
