# The Second World War: Course in Europe

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Second World War: Course in Europe** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Blitzkrieg wins the Axis an empire in months, then overreach and Allied strength reverse it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
