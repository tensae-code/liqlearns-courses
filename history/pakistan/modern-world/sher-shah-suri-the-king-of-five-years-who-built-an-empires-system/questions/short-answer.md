# Sher Shah Suri: The King of Five Years Who Built an Empire's System — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 4: Sher Shah Suri and the Suri interregnum (1540–1555)”?
**Answer:** Sher Shah drives Humayun into exile and in five years builds the machine the Mughals will inherit — the Grand Trunk Road from Bengal to Peshawar, the silver rupiya, the dak postal system and a measured land revenue — before dying at Kalinjar in 1545.
