# Lesson plan — Sher Shah Suri: The King of Five Years Who Built an Empire's System

**Course:** History — Pakistan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Sher Shah Suri: The King of Five Years Who Built an Empire's System
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — India: Sultanate, Mughals and the British Advance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sher Shah drives Humayun into exile and in five years builds the machine the Mughals will inherit — the Grand Trunk Road from Bengal to Peshawar, the silver rupiya, the dak postal system and a measured land revenue — before dying at Kalinjar in 1545.
