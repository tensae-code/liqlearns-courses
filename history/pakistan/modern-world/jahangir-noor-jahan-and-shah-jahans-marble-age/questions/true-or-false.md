# Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan's Marble Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Jahangir (1605–1627) rules through art and justice while his queen Noor Jahan (1577–1645) — poet, patron and power behind the throne — shapes the court; his son Shah Jahan turns the empire's wealth into marble: the Taj Mahal, Delhi's Jama Masjid, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 4: Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan” is mainly about this: As Mughal power fades, renewal comes from scholars — Shah Waliullah (1703–1762) translates the Quran into Persian and Urdu and writes fifty-one books to revive Muslim society; in Bengal, Haji Shariatullah's Faraizi Movement (1781–1840) calls peasants back to the faraiz, the core duties of the faith.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Jahangir (1605–1627) rules through art and justice while his queen Noor Jahan (1577–1645) — poet, patron and power behind the throne — shapes the court; his son Shah Jahan turns the empire's wealth into marble: the Taj Mahal, Delhi's Jama Masjid, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens.
