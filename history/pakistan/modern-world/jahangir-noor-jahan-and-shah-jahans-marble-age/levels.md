# Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan's Marble Age — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Jahangir (1605–1627) rules through art and justice while his queen Noor Jahan (1577–1645) — poet, patron and power behind the throne — shapes the court; his son Shah Jahan turns the empire's wealth into marble: the Taj Mahal, Delhi's Jama Masjid, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
