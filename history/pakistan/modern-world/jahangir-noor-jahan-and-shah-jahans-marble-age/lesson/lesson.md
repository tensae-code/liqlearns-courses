# Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan's Marble Age

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan's Marble Age** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Jahangir (1605–1627) rules through art and justice while his queen Noor Jahan (1577–1645) — poet, patron and power behind the throne — shapes the court; his son Shah Jahan turns the empire's wealth into marble: the Taj Mahal, Delhi's Jama Masjid, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens.
