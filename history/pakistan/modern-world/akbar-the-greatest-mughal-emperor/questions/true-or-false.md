# Akbar: The Greatest Mughal Emperor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Crowned at fourteen after the Second Battle of Panipat (1556), Akbar welds northern India into one empire, abolishes the jizya, marries Rajput princesses, debates all faiths in the Ibadat Khana and proclaims Din-e-Ilahi in 1582 — the book names him the greatest Mughal emperor.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 4: Akbar (1556–1605)” is mainly about this: After the failed rising of 1857 the East India Company's rule passes to the Crown; the book closes the chapter with the challenges the Muslims of the Subcontinent then faced — political eclipse, economic ruin and an education gap.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Crowned at fourteen after the Second Battle of Panipat (1556), Akbar welds northern India into one empire, abolishes the jizya, marries Rajput princesses, debates all faiths in the Ibadat Khana and proclaims Din-e-Ilahi in 1582 — the book names him the greatest Mughal emperor.
