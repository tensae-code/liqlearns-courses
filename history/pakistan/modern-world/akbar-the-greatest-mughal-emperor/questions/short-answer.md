# Akbar: The Greatest Mughal Emperor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 4: Akbar (1556–1605)”?
**Answer:** Crowned at fourteen after the Second Battle of Panipat (1556), Akbar welds northern India into one empire, abolishes the jizya, marries Rajput princesses, debates all faiths in the Ibadat Khana and proclaims Din-e-Ilahi in 1582 — the book names him the greatest Mughal emperor.
