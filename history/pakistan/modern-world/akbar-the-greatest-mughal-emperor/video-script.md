# Video script — Akbar: The Greatest Mughal Emperor


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Crowned at fourteen after the Second Battle of Panipat (1556), Akbar welds northern India into one empire, abolishes the jizya, marries Rajput princesses, debates all faiths in the Ibadat Khana and proclaims Din-e-Ilahi in 1582 — the book names him the greatest Mughal emperor.
