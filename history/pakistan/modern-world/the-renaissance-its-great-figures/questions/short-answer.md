# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 5: famous people of the Renaissance”?
**Answer:** Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519), painter-engineer of the Mona Lisa; Michelangelo (1475–1564), sculptor of the Pieta and painter of the Last Judgment; Raphael (1483–1520) of the Madonnas; Shakespeare (1564–1616); Galileo (1564–1642) with his telescope; and Thomas More (1478–1535), the Utopia writer executed by his king.
