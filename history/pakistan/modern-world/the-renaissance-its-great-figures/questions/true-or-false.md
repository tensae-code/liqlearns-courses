# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519), painter-engineer of the Mona Lisa; Michelangelo (1475–1564), sculptor of the Pieta and painter of the Last Judgment; Raphael (1483–1520) of the Madonnas; Shakespeare (1564–1616); Galileo (1564–1642) with his telescope; and Thomas More (1478–1535), the Utopia writer executed by his king.
