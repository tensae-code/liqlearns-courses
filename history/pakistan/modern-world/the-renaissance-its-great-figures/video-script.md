# Video script — The Renaissance: Its Great Figures


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519), painter-engineer of the Mona Lisa; Michelangelo (1475–1564), sculptor of the Pieta and painter of the Last Judgment; Raphael (1483–1520) of the Madonnas; Shakespeare (1564–1616); Galileo (1564–1642) with his telescope; and Thomas More (1478–1535), the Utopia writer executed by his king.
