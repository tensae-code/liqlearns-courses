# The Safavids: Shah Abbas and Isfahan — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Shah Ismail founds a Shi'a empire in Persia in 1501; under Shah Abbas (1587–1629) Isfahan is rebuilt into one of the world's most beautiful capitals — "Isfahan is half the world" — before Afghan revolt ends the dynasty. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
