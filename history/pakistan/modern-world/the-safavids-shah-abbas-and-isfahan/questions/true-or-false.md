# The Safavids: Shah Abbas and Isfahan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Shah Ismail founds a Shi'a empire in Persia in 1501; under Shah Abbas (1587–1629) Isfahan is rebuilt into one of the world's most beautiful capitals — "Isfahan is half the world" — before Afghan revolt ends the dynasty.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Chapter 3: the Safavid Dynasty (1501–1736)” is mainly about this: Mahmood of Ghazni raids India seventeen times (1000–1027), but Shahab-ud-Din Ghori plants rule — Tarain 1192 — and his slave-general Qutb-ud-Din Aibak founds the Sultanate in 1206; five dynasties follow — Mamluk (Iltutmish the real organiser, Razia the queen, Balban), Khilji (Ala-ud-Din's market reforms and Mongol-proof armies), Tughlaq (Firoz Shah's canals and employment bureau), Sayyid, and Lodhi, until Babar ends it at Panipat.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Shah Ismail founds a Shi'a empire in Persia in 1501; under Shah Abbas (1587–1629) Isfahan is rebuilt into one of the world's most beautiful capitals — "Isfahan is half the world" — before Afghan revolt ends the dynasty.
