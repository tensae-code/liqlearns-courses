**1.**  What happens in “Chapter 3: the Safavid Dynasty (1501–1736)”?
**Answer:** Shah Ismail founds a Shi'a empire in Persia in 1501; under Shah Abbas (1587–1629) Isfahan is rebuilt into one of the world's most beautiful capitals — "Isfahan is half the world" — before Afghan revolt ends the dynasty.
