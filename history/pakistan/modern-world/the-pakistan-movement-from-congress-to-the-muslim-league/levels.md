# The Pakistan Movement: From Congress to the Muslim League — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 4: the contribution of the provinces and regions in the creation of Pakistan
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: After the failed war of 1857 the Muslims of India organise; the Lahore Resolution of 23 March 1940 demands a separate homeland; and each province plays its part — Punjab passes the resolution's spirit at the Minar-e-Pakistan's ground, Sindh, the land where Islam first entered with Muhammad bin Qasim in 712 and which the book calls Bab-ul-Islam, becomes the first province to vote for Pakistan in 1943, Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa join by their own decisions and referendum, until 14 August 1947. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
