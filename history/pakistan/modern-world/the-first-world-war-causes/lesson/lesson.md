# The First World War: Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The First World War: Causes** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Armed camps and an arms race leave Europe one spark away from a general war.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Armed camps and an arms race leave Europe one spark away from a general war. Give one real example: Armed camps and an arms race leave Europe one spark away from a general war. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Armed camps and an arms race leave Europe one spark away from a general war.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
