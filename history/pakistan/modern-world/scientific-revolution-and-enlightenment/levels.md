# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In the 17th and 18th centuries reason itself becomes the banner — thinkers argue that knowledge, law and government must answer to evidence and the "value of reason", preparing the ground for revolution. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
