# Video script — Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This lesson tells one story.

In the 17th and 18th centuries reason itself becomes the banner — thinkers argue that knowledge, law and government must answer to evidence and the "value of reason", preparing the ground for revolution.
