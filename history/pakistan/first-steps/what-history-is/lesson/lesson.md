# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **What History Is** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

History is the record of what humans did; the chapter starts the film at the very beginning — wandering hunters learn tools, then farming, then settle by rivers, and settled life grows into civilisation, the word itself coming from the Latin for a city.
