# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Pakistan is introduced as a house of many cultures — languages, dress, foods and festivals differing from valley to valley; the chapter sends children to a museum and sets them collecting old coins, stamps and postcards, learning that a people's past is a thing you can hold and must keep.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 2 (Culture): the cultures of Pakistan” is mainly about this: Between the Tigris and Euphrates the Sumerians, Assyrians and Babylonians raise the first cities in the Fertile Crescent, press writing into clay tablets, build temples like Babel and govern by written laws.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: One country, many inheritances — the chapter walks through the dresses, foods, fairs and festivals, folk songs and arts and crafts of every province, and the national days (23 March, 14 August, 6 September, 25 December) that the whole country keeps together.
