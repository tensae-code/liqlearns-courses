# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 2 (Culture): the diverse culture of Pakistan and the museum activity”?
**Answer:** Pakistan is introduced as a house of many cultures — languages, dress, foods and festivals differing from valley to valley; the chapter sends children to a museum and sets them collecting old coins, stamps and postcards, learning that a people's past is a thing you can hold and must keep.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 2 (Culture): the cultures of Pakistan” end?
**Answer:** One country, many inheritances — the chapter walks through the dresses, foods, fairs and festivals, folk songs and arts and crafts of every province, and the national days (23 March, 14 August, 6 September, 25 December) that the whole country keeps together.
