# Video script — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Pakistan is introduced as a house of many cultures — languages, dress, foods and festivals differing from valley to valley; the chapter sends children to a museum and sets them collecting old coins, stamps and postcards, learning that a people's past is a thing you can hold and must keep.

One country, many inheritances — the chapter walks through the dresses, foods, fairs and festivals, folk songs and arts and crafts of every province, and the national days (23 March, 14 August, 6 September, 25 December) that the whole country keeps together.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Pakistan.
