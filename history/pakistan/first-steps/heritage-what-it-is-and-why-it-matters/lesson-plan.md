## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 2 (Culture): the diverse culture of Pakistan and the museum activity** — Pakistan is introduced as a house of many cultures — languages, dress, foods and festivals differing from valley to valley; the chapter sends children to a museum and sets them collecting old coins, stamps and postcards, learning that a people's past is a thing you can hold and must keep.
2. **Chapter 2 (Culture): the cultures of Pakistan** — One country, many inheritances — the chapter walks through the dresses, foods, fairs and festivals, folk songs and arts and crafts of every province, and the national days (23 March, 14 August, 6 September, 25 December) that the whole country keeps together.
