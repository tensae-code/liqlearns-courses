# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Dating the Past** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Before the stories, the chapter teaches the clock they are told by — years counted backwards in BCE from the birth of Christ and forwards in CE, so a child can place Greece, Rome and Gandhara on one line of time.
