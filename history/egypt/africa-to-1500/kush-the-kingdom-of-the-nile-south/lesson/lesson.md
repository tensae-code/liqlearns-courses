# Kush: The Kingdom of the Nile South

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Kush: The Kingdom of the Nile South** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan. Kush trades ivory, incense, iron and gold with Egypt, raises its own pyramids — a UNESCO site since 2011 — and worships Amun, Ra, Osiris and Isis.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan. Give one real example: Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
