# Kush: The Kingdom of the Nile South — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What happens in “The civilisation of Kush”?
**Answer:** Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan; Kush trades ivory, incense, iron and gold with Egypt, raises its own pyramids — a UNESCO site since 2011 — and worships Amun, Ra, Osiris and Isis

**2.** Name one person or place in “The civilisation of Kush” and say what they did or what happened there.
