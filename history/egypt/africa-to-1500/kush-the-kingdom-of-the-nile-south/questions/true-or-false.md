# Kush: The Kingdom of the Nile South — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “The civilisation of Kush” is mainly about this: England wraps the Gulf in treaties — Oman in 1798, Perim in 1799, the General Peace of 1820 against piracy and the slave trade, protectorates over Bahrain's rulers, Muscat in 1891 and Kuwait in 1899 — and seizes Aden in 1839 as Muhammad Ali's shadow falls on Arabia.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan.
