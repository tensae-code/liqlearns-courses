# Kush: The Kingdom of the Nile South — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.**  ____, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan; Kush trades ivory, incense, iron and gold with Egypt, raises its own pyramids — a UNESCO site since 2011 — and worships Amun, Ra, Osiris and Isis
**Answer:** Napata

**2.**  Napata, then ____, rule northern Sudan; Kush trades ivory, incense, iron and gold with Egypt, raises its own pyramids — a UNESCO site since 2011 — and worships Amun, Ra, Osiris and Isis
**Answer:** Meroe
