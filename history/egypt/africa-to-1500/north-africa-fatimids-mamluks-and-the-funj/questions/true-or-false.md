# North Africa: Fatimids, Mamluks and the Funj — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Cairo is founded over a thousand years ago under the Fatimids, al-Azhar rises as a beacon of Islam, and the city collects names across the centuries — Jewel of the East, City of a Thousand Minarets.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Egypt in the Fatimid age” is mainly about this: Egypt stands among the founders of the Organisation of African Unity in 1963 and of its successor, the African Union of 2002 seated in Addis Ababa, and works through Africa's regional blocs, COMESA among them, for the continent's peace, security and development.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: the Fatimids make Egypt the seat of a rival caliphate and raise Cairo — Egypt's medieval golden age begins.
