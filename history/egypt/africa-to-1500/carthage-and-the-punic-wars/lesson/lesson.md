# Carthage and the Punic Wars

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Carthage and the Punic Wars** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages. It grows into an independent kingdom ruling most of the North African coast from the Mediterranean's mid-route.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages. Give one real example: the Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
