# Carthage and the Punic Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “The civilisation of Carthage”?
a) the Fatimids make Egypt the seat of a rival caliphate and raise Cairo — Egypt's medieval golden age begins  b) Mentuhotep II reunites Egypt from Thebes  c) the Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages  d) Khalid ibn al-Walid opens Iraq — the Chains, al-Buwayb, al-Qadisiyya — and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas besieges al-Mada'in…
**Answer: c) the Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens later in “The civilisation of Carthage”?
a) fire, farming and copper carry the Egyptian down from the plateaus to the river  b) Mentuhotep II reunites Egypt from Thebes  c) it grows into an independent kingdom ruling most of the North African coast from the Mediterranean's mid-route  d) Abbas I closes most schools and factories
**Answer: c) it grows into an independent kingdom ruling most of the North African coast from the Mediterranean's mid-route** — That is what this lesson teaches.
