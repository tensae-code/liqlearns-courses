# Carthage and the Punic Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “The civilisation of Carthage” is mainly about this: the Fatimids make Egypt the seat of a rival caliphate and raise Cairo — Egypt's medieval golden age begins.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: the Phoenicians plant Carthage on the Tunisian coast to guard their Spain-bound trade voyages.
