# Lesson plan — What Egypt Gave: Life in Pharaonic Egypt

**Course:** History — Egypt · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** What Egypt Gave: Life in Pharaonic Egypt
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Egypt and Its African Neighbours (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before imagining future cities, the class looks back at Akhetaten, the planned city of the ancient Egyptians, as proof Egyptians were city planners thousands of years ago.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Egypt and Its African Neighbours (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2 Lesson 4: our city in the future** — before imagining future cities, the class looks back at Akhetaten, the planned city of the ancient Egyptians, as proof Egyptians were city….
2. **Factors for the rise of the ancient Egyptian civilisation: the human gifts** — the Egyptians answer the river with work — the world's first political unity under one government, a strong army among the oldest on earth….
3. **Economic life in Pharaonic Egypt** — the state organises the flood — land survey, irrigation offices, canals — and Amenemhat III throws his dam across the Faiyum's mouth to….
4. **Social life** — mud-brick rooms for the poor, gardens for the rich, palaces for kings.
5. **Religious life** — many gods for one ordered world, and beneath them the belief that shaped everything — resurrection and immortality.
6. **Cultural and intellectual life** — the written, reckoned and healed world the Egyptians built — the intellectual harvest of the Pharaonic civilisation.
