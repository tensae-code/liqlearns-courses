# What Egypt Gave: Life in Pharaonic Egypt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Before imagining future cities, the class looks back at Akhetaten, the planned city of the ancient Egyptians, as proof Egyptians were city planners thousands of years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Factors for the rise of the ancient Egyptian civilisation: the human gifts” is mainly about this: Rome inherits the Mediterranean world Greece opened — the last of the ancient civilisations in the course's westward arc.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: the Egyptians answer the river with work — the world's first political unity under one government, a strong army among the oldest on earth….
