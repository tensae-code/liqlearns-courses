# Egypt and Africa in the Middle Ages

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Egypt and Africa in the Middle Ages** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Cairo shelters the caliphate after Baghdad falls. African kings seek Mamluk recognition and Egyptian weapon-craft. Ethiopia's church receives its bishop from Alexandria. Al-Azhar houses African students in their own riwaqs with stipends. And Sahara caravans make Egypt the middleman of the continent's trade.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Cairo shelters the caliphate after Baghdad falls. Give one real example: Cairo shelters the caliphate after Baghdad falls. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Cairo shelters the caliphate after Baghdad falls.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
