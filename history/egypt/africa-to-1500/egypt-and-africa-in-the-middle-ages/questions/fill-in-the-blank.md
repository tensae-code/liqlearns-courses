# Egypt and Africa in the Middle Ages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.**  ____ shelters the caliphate after Baghdad falls; African kings seek Mamluk recognition and Egyptian weapon-craft; Ethiopia's church receives its bishop from Alexandria; al-Azhar houses…
**Answer:** Cairo

**2.**  Cairo shelters the caliphate after ____ falls; African kings seek Mamluk recognition and Egyptian weapon-craft; Ethiopia's church receives its bishop from Alexandria; al-Azhar houses…
