# Egypt: The Tulunids and Ikhshidids — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Ahmad ibn Tulun, the Turkish officer who came as a deputy in 868, gathers Egypt's reins, wins her people's love with justice and generosity, and stretches his state into the Levant as its shield. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
