# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  The ancient Egyptians settle on the banks of the Nile, farming villages appear, and the villages grow into Egypt's first cities.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Unit 3 Lesson 5: historical landmarks in Egypt” is mainly about this: a land of tribes and their wars, ruled by custom and the shaykh's word.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Djoser raises the first great stone building, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara, the Giza pyramids follow, and Karnak's temples at Thebes grow….
