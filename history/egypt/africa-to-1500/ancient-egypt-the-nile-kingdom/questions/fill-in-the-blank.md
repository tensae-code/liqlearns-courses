# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  the ancient ____ settle on the banks of the Nile, farming villages appear, and the villages grow into Egypt's first cities
**Answer:** Egyptians

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  ____ raises the first great stone building, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara, the Giza pyramids follow, and Karnak's temples at Thebes grow column by column for a thousand years
**Answer:** Djoser
