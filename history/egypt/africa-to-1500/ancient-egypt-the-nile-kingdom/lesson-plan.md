## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2 Lesson 1: how our city looks** — the ancient Egyptians settle on the banks of the Nile, farming villages appear, and the villages grow into Egypt's first cities.
2. **Unit 3 Lesson 5: historical landmarks in Egypt** — Djoser raises the first great stone building, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara, the Giza pyramids follow, and Karnak's temples at Thebes grow….
3. **The ancient Egyptian civilisation among Africa's civilisations** — on the Nile's banks farming, villages and unity grow into the dynastic state, which trades south with Kush and Punt.
4. **The Old Kingdom, era of the pyramid builders** — Djoser's minister Imhotep raises the first stone pyramid at Saqqara.
5. **The Middle Kingdom, era of economic prosperity** — Mentuhotep II reunites Egypt from Thebes.
6. **The New Kingdom, era of military glory** — Ahmose expels the Hyksos and founds an empire from the Euphrates to the Fourth Cataract.
7. **Factors for the rise of the ancient Egyptian civilisation: the natural gifts** — a river that teaches a whole people to farm, settle and cooperate on its banks.
8. **Life of the ancient Egyptian: the ages before history** — fire, farming and copper carry the Egyptian down from the plateaus to the river.
9. **Life of the ancient Egyptian: the Old Kingdom** — the pyramid age opens — Djoser's step pyramid, Khufu's great pyramid, Khafre and the Sphinx — while the commander Weni's tomb inscription….
10. **Life of the ancient Egyptian: the Middle Kingdom** — the age the book calls economic prosperity — Senusret III digs his canal, Amenemhat III tames the Faiyum's waters behind the Lahun dam, and….
11. **Life of the ancient Egyptian: the New Kingdom** — the warrior age — Thutmose III at Megiddo, Ramesses II at Kadesh and the first written peace treaty in history, and the twin temples of Abu….
12. **Features of the history of ancient Egypt** — the long arc of the Pharaonic state from the first unification through the ages of strength and decline — the frame on which the whole unit….
13. **Political and administrative life** — how the Pharaonic state was governed — king, ministers and the administrations that ran a united kingdom for three thousand years.
