# Video script — Egypt from the Arab Conquest to the Independent States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

For two centuries Egypt is a province governed from abroad — under the Umayyads Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, the best and justest of her governors, builds the Nilometer of the year 69 AH and makes Helwan his seat. Under the Abbasids the governors change so fast, farmed out to Turkish amirs and their deputies, that Egypt learns to dream of ruling herself.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
