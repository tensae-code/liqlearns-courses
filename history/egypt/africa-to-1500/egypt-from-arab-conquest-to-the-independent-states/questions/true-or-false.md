# Egypt from the Arab Conquest to the Independent States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  For two centuries Egypt is a province governed from abroad — under the Umayyads Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, the best and justest of her governors, builds the Nilometer of the year 69 AH and makes Helwan his seat.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “Egypt from the Islamic conquest until the rise of the independent states” is mainly about this: Alexander, Aristotle's pupil, is welcomed in 332 BC as a liberator from Persia, kneels to Amun at Siwa and plans Alexandria (331 BC).
**Answer: False** — It is about this: for two centuries Egypt is a province governed from abroad — under the Umayyads Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, the best and justest of her….
