# Egypt from the Arab Conquest to the Independent States — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — For two centuries Egypt is a province governed from abroad — under the Umayyads Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, the best and justest of her governors, builds the Nilometer of the year 69 AH and makes Helwan… — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
