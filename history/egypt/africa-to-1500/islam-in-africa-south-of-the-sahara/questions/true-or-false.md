# Islam in Africa South of the Sahara — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Islam crosses into Africa by Sinai, the Red Sea and the ocean roads — by preachers, by honest traders whose markets become centres like Kano and Timbuktu, by intermarriage and by migrations from the Companions' flight to Abyssinia onward.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “The spread of Islam in Africa” is mainly about this: Khalid ibn al-Walid opens Iraq — the Chains, al-Buwayb, al-Qadisiyya — and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas besieges al-Mada'in for two months and prays in Kisra's White Palace.
