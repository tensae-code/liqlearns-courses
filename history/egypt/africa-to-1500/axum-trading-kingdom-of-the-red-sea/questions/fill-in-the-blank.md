# Axum: Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.**  ____, in today's Eritrea and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and spices, and is famed for its farming, pottery and glass
**Answer:** Axum

**2.**  Axum, in today's ____ and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and spices, and is famed for its farming, pottery and glass
