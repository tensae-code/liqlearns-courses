# Axum: Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “The civilisation of Axum”?
a) Axum, in today's Eritrea and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and…  b) the sea-people of the Levant coast take their place beside river-born Egypt and Iraq — a civilisation of ships…  c) the Egyptians answer the river with work — the world's first political unity under one government, a strong army…  d) Khalid ibn al-Walid opens Iraq — the Chains, al-Buwayb, al-Qadisiyya — and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas besieges al-Mada'in…
**Answer: a) Axum, in today's Eritrea and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
