# Axum: Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Axum: Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Axum, in today's Eritrea and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and spices, and is famed for its farming, pottery and glass.

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
