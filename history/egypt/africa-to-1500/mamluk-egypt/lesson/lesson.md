# Mamluk Egypt

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Mamluk Egypt** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Hulagu's letter demands submission. Qutuz beheads the envoys, hangs their heads on Bab Zuweila, and marches out to meet the Mongols at Ain Jalut in 1260 — crying "O Islam!" he throws himself into the battle, the Mongol army breaks, and Egypt's Mamluks — the slave-soldiers of the Rawda island barracks — become the shield of the whole Islamic world against Mongols and Crusaders alike.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Hulagu's letter demands submission. Give one real example: Hulagu's letter demands submission. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Hulagu's letter demands submission.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
