# Mamluk Egypt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.**  ____'s letter demands submission; Qutuz beheads the envoys, hangs their heads on Bab Zuweila, and marches out to meet the Mongols at Ain Jalut in 1260 — crying "O Islam!" he throws himself…
**Answer:** Hulagu

**2.**  Hulagu's letter demands submission; ____ beheads the envoys, hangs their heads on Bab Zuweila, and marches out to meet the Mongols at Ain Jalut in 1260 — crying "O Islam!" he throws himself…
**Answer:** Qutuz
