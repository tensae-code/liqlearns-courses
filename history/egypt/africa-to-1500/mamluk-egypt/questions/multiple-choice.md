# Mamluk Egypt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Egypt in the Mamluk age: Ain Jalut”?
a) Hulagu's letter demands submission  b) Sumerian city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu  c) Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens  d) mud-brick rooms for the poor, gardens for the rich, palaces for kings
**Answer: a) Hulagu's letter demands submission** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens later in “Egypt in the Mamluk age: Ain Jalut”?
a) ancient Egyptians draw some of the first maps, Arab scholars refine map-making, Maryam al-Asturlabi's astrolabes…  b) the Arab states learn to act together first over Palestine — the Bludan conference of 1937 gathers delegates from…  c) Qutuz beheads the envoys, hangs their heads on Bab Zuweila, and marches out to meet the Mongols at Ain Jalut in…  d) the Abbasid age carries the same story forward — caliphs patronising the sciences, cities rising in an urban…
**Answer: c) Qutuz beheads the envoys, hangs their heads on Bab Zuweila, and marches out to meet the Mongols at Ain Jalut in…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
