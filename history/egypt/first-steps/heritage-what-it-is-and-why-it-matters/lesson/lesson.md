# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The landmarks carry Egypt's identity and world standing, so guarding them is every Egyptian's duty and each child is an ambassador. The Grand Egyptian Museum gathers the treasures in one place.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
