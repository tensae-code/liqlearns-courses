# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Unit 3 Lesson 5: historical landmarks in Egypt”?
a) the state organises the flood — land survey, irrigation offices, canals — and Amenemhat III throws his dam across…  b) Napata, then Meroe, rule northern Sudan  c) the landmarks carry Egypt's identity and world standing, so guarding them is every Egyptian's duty and each child…  d) Abbas I closes most schools and factories
**Answer: c) the landmarks carry Egypt's identity and world standing, so guarding them is every Egyptian's duty and each child…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens later in “Unit 3 Lesson 5: historical landmarks in Egypt”?
a) before imagining future cities, the class looks back at Akhetaten, the planned city of the ancient Egyptians, as…  b) for two centuries Egypt is a province governed from abroad — under the Umayyads Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, the best…  c) the Grand Egyptian Museum gathers the treasures in one place  d) Djoser raises the first great stone building, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara, the Giza pyramids follow, and Karnak's…
**Answer: c) the Grand Egyptian Museum gathers the treasures in one place** — That is what this lesson teaches.
