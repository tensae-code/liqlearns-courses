# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **What History Is** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The course begins by asking what a civilisation is and what history is — the doorway every later lesson walks through.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
