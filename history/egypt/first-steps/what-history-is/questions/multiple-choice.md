# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Civilisation and history”?
a) Djoser's minister Imhotep raises the first stone pyramid at Saqqara  b) the course begins by asking what a civilisation is and what history is — the doorway every later lesson walks…  c) in the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states…  d) the ancient Egyptians settle on the banks of the Nile, farming villages appear, and the villages grow into Egypt's…
**Answer: b) the course begins by asking what a civilisation is and what history is — the doorway every later lesson walks…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
