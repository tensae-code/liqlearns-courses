# Fact or Opinion

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Fact or Opinion** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The class sorts statements about a school trip into fact, opinion and rumour before trusting any of them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The class sorts statements about a school trip into fact, opinion and rumour before trusting any of them. Give one real example: the class sorts statements about a school trip into fact, opinion and rumour before trusting any of them. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The class sorts statements about a school trip into fact, opinion and rumour before trusting any of them.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
