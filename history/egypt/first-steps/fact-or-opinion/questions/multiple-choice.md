# Fact or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 4: telling fact from opinion and rumour when gathering information”?
a) crushed by Khurshid Pasha's taxes and looting soldiers, Egypt's sheikhs and people choose Muhammad Ali as wali in…  b) the class sorts statements about a school trip into fact, opinion and rumour before trusting any of them  c) ancient Egyptians draw some of the first maps, Arab scholars refine map-making, Maryam al-Asturlabi's astrolabes…  d) the Arab states learn to act together first over Palestine — the Bludan conference of 1937 gathers delegates from…
**Answer: b) the class sorts statements about a school trip into fact, opinion and rumour before trusting any of them** — That is what this lesson teaches.
