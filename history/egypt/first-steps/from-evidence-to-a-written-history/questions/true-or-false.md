# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Papyrus rolls and the inscriptions on Karnak's temple walls record events as they happen, while historians' books retell them later, and children learn to sort first-hand evidence from second-hand accounts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Sources for studying civilisations” is mainly about this: the sea-people of the Levant coast take their place beside river-born Egypt and Iraq — a civilisation of ships, ports and letters rather than of one river valley.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: before any story is told, the pupil learns where stories come from — the remains, writings and witnesses from which the past is rebuilt.
