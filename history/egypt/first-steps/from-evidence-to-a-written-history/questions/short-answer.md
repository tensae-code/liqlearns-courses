# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What happens in “Unit 1 Lesson 4: sources of information, primary and secondary”?
**Answer:** papyrus rolls and the inscriptions on Karnak's temple walls record events as they happen, while historians' books retell them later, and children learn to sort first-hand evidence from second-hand accounts

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What happens in “Sources for studying civilisations”?
**Answer:** before any story is told, the pupil learns where stories come from — the remains, writings and witnesses from which the past is rebuilt
