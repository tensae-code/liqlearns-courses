# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Europe's states race to build empires while its merchants hunt a sea road to India free of Ottoman and Mamluk tolls, and dream of gold, silver, spices and silk. Better ships and instruments carry Columbus in 1492, Vasco da Gama in 1497 and Magellan in 1519 across the oceans.
