# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “The geographic discoveries of the New World continents”?
a) Tawfiq restores the foreign Dual Control and Riyad Pasha's absolutism  b) ancient Egyptians draw some of the first maps, Arab scholars refine map-making, Maryam al-Asturlabi's astrolabes…  c) under the occupation the British Agent rules, the army is capped at six thousand men under an English commander…  d) Europe's states race to build empires while its merchants hunt a sea road to India free of Ottoman and Mamluk…
**Answer: d) Europe's states race to build empires while its merchants hunt a sea road to India free of Ottoman and Mamluk…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens later in “The geographic discoveries of the New World continents”?

**Answer: d) better ships and instruments carry Columbus in 1492, Vasco da Gama in 1497 and Magellan in 1519 across the oceans** — That is what this lesson teaches.
