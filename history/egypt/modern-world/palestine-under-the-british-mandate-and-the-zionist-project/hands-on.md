# Hands-on — Palestine Under the British Mandate and the Zionist Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Palestine Under the British Mandate and the Zionist Project — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Britain's Balfour promise of 1917 — "a promise from one who does not own, to one who does not deserve" — is carried out through the San Remo mandate of 1920, and the lesson follows the question… Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
