# The Scramble for Africa: Origins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  After the Industrial Revolution Europe hunts raw materials, markets, investment outlets, land for its surplus people, national glory and military bases.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Chapter 5: Colonial expansion in the Arab countries before the First World War, until independence” is mainly about this: on the Nile's banks farming, villages and unity grow into the dynastic state, which trades south with Kush and Punt.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: England wraps the Gulf in treaties — Oman in 1798, Perim in 1799, the General Peace of 1820 against piracy and the slave trade….
