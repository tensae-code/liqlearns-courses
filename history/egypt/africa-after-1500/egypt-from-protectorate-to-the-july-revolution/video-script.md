# Video script — Egypt: From Protectorate to the July Revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul, the declaration of 28 February 1922 and the constitutions of 1923 and 1930, with Egyptian women marching in 1919.

The 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul, the declaration of 28 February 1922 and the constitutions of 1923 and 1930, with Egyptian women marching in 1919. The 1936 treaty turns occupation into alliance. British tanks surround Abdeen Palace on 4 February 1942 to impose a ministry. Nahhas abrogates the treaty in 1951, the Ismailia police battle of 25 January 1952 and the Cairo Fire follow — and the monarchy falls to the July Revolution.

From the 1919 revolution through the treaty age to the eve of 1952 — Egypt's interwar struggle for full independence.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
