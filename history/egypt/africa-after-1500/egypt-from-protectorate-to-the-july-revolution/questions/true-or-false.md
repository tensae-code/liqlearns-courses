# Egypt: From Protectorate to the July Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  The 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul, the declaration of 28 February 1922 and the constitutions of 1923 and 1930, with Egyptian women marching in 1919.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Chapter 4: Egypt after the First World War” is mainly about this: the civilisation at work and at prayer — its markets, farms and classes, its scholars, and the architecture its faith raised — the harvest of the Islamic centuries.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: from the 1919 revolution through the treaty age to the eve of 1952 — Egypt's interwar struggle for full independence.
