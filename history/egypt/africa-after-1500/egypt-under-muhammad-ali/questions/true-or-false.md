# Egypt Under Muhammad Ali — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Crushed by Khurshid Pasha's taxes and looting soldiers, Egypt's sheikhs and people choose Muhammad Ali as wali in 1805 — "we accept only you, on our conditions".
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Muhammad Ali builds the modern state” is mainly about this: a land of tribes and their wars, ruled by custom and the shaykh's word.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: seven directorates under Cairo and advisory councils.
