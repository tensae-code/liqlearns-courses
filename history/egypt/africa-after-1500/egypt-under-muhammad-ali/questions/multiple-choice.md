# Egypt Under Muhammad Ali — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What is the heart of the story in “Muhammad Ali takes power in Egypt”?
a) crushed by Khurshid Pasha's taxes and looting soldiers, Egypt's sheikhs and people choose Muhammad Ali as wali in…  b) ancient Egyptians draw some of the first maps, Arab scholars refine map-making, Maryam al-Asturlabi's astrolabes…  c) the written, reckoned and healed world the Egyptians built — the intellectual harvest of the Pharaonic civilisation  d) three powers collide over the Islamic world — a Mamluk state ruined by the Cape of Good Hope route of 1498, the…
**Answer: a) crushed by Khurshid Pasha's taxes and looting soldiers, Egypt's sheikhs and people choose Muhammad Ali as wali in…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What is the heart of the story in “Muhammad Ali builds the modern state”?
a) the army council dissolves parliament in February 2011, the Freedom and Justice Party sweeps the elections, and…  b) seven directorates under Cairo and advisory councils  c) Alexander enters in 332 BC, honours Amun, treats Egyptians well and keeps their administration, and founds…  d) in the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states…
**Answer: b) seven directorates under Cairo and advisory councils** — That is what this lesson teaches.
