# Video script — Egypt Between the Mamluks and the Ottomans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Three powers collide over the Islamic world — a Mamluk state ruined by the Cape of Good Hope route of 1498, the fleet's destruction at Diu in 1509 and its own infighting.

Three powers collide over the Islamic world — a Mamluk state ruined by the Cape of Good Hope route of 1498, the fleet's destruction at Diu in 1509 and its own infighting. Safavid Persia. And Selim's Ottomans, fresh from Chaldiran in 1514. At Marj Dabiq in 1516 and al-Raydaniyya in 1517 the Mamluks fall, Egypt becomes an Ottoman province and the caliphate's seat moves to the Ottoman state — four stagnant centuries follow, broken only by Ali Bey al-Kabir's bid for independence in 1768.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
