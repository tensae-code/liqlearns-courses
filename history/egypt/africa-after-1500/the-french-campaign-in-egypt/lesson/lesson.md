# The French Campaign in Egypt

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The French Campaign in Egypt** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The French Campaign on Egypt, 1798–1801

Napoleon lands and Egyptians resist in Cairo's two revolts. The Levant campaign ends in the Jaffa massacre and failure before Acre. Kléber is assassinated by the Azhar student Sulayman al-Halabi in 1800, Menou dreams of a colony, and Britain and the Ottomans force the evacuation of September 1801 — but the campaign's scholars found the Scientific Institute, write the Description de l'Égypte and discover the Rosetta Stone in 1799, and the invasion wakes Egypt's national spirit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 1: The French Campaign on Egypt and the Levant, 1798–1801

France dreams of Egypt for a century first — Louis XIV in 1672 and Louis XV in 1769 both reach for her to wound Dutch and British trade. In 1798 the causes ripen — cut the Cape route, strike England's Indian empire, replace the lost American colonies with an eastern empire. And the Egypt it strikes is an Ottoman province of tax-farmers and decayed crafts, two societies — Turkish-Mamluk rulers and Egyptian ruled — governed by wali, diwan and beys.
