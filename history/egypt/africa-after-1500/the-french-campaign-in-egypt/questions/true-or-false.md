# The French Campaign in Egypt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Napoleon lands and Egyptians resist in Cairo's two revolts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Chapter 1: The French Campaign on Egypt and the Levant, 1798–1801” is mainly about this: the rigged elections of 2010 that gave the ruling party ninety-seven per cent, the emergency law in force since 1981, rising prices and jobless graduates ignite a revolution organised through social media.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: France dreams of Egypt for a century first — Louis XIV in 1672 and Louis XV in 1769 both reach for her to wound Dutch and British trade.
