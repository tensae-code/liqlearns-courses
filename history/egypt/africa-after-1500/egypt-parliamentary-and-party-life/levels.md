# Egypt: Parliamentary and Party Life — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — From Muhammad Ali's advisory councils and Ismail's Shura Council of 1866, through the occupation's sham councils and the party age of the 1923 constitution, to the single organisation after 1952… — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
