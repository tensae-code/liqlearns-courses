# Egypt: The July 1952 Revolution — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The revolution abolishes feudalism — agrarian reform from September 1952 caps ownership at fifty feddans — makes education free at every stage, builds the High Dam and Helwan's steelworks and gives… — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
