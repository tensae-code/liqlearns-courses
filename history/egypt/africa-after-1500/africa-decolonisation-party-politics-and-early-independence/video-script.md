# Video script — Africa: Decolonisation, Party Politics and Early Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Wilson's Fourteen Points and the Atlantic Charter of 1941 promise peoples the right to choose their governments.

Wilson's Fourteen Points and the Atlantic Charter of 1941 promise peoples the right to choose their governments. Africans answer with strikes, boycotts and arms — Mandela endures twenty-seven years in prison, takes the Nobel Peace Prize in 1993 and leads South Africa from 1994 — while Egypt funds, trains and broadcasts for the liberation movements from Cairo.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
