# Egypt: The Urabi Revolution and the British Occupation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  ____ restores the foreign Dual Control and Riyad Pasha's absolutism; Egyptian officers, passed over for Turks by war minister Uthman Rifqi, rally to Ahmed Urabi — freed by his own soldiers…
**Answer:** Tawfiq

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  ____ Kamel founds the newspaper al-Liwa in 1900 and the National Party in 1907 and campaigns for an Egyptian university, opened in 1908; the Dinshaway hangings of 1906 shame Britain before…
