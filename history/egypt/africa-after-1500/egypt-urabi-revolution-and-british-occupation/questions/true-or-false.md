# Egypt: The Urabi Revolution and the British Occupation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Tawfiq restores the foreign Dual Control and Riyad Pasha's absolutism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Mustafa Kamel and Muhammad Farid” is mainly about this: on the Yellow River the Qin unify China's warring states under one central rule.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Mustafa Kamel founds the newspaper al-Liwa in 1900 and the National Party in 1907 and campaigns for an Egyptian university, opened in 1908.
