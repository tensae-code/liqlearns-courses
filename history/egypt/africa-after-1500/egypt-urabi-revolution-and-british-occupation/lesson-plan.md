## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Urabi Revolution and the start of the British occupation** — Tawfiq restores the foreign Dual Control and Riyad Pasha's absolutism.
2. **Mustafa Kamel and Muhammad Farid** — Mustafa Kamel founds the newspaper al-Liwa in 1900 and the National Party in 1907 and campaigns for an Egyptian university, opened in 1908.
3. **The national movement and the Urabi Revolution** — Sharif Pasha's Chamber of December 1881 fights for the budget.
4. **The national struggle against the British occupation** — under the occupation the British Agent rules, the army is capped at six thousand men under an English commander, cotton is king and….
5. **Chapter 3: Egypt from the Urabi Revolution until the First World War** — the revolution, the occupation it fails to prevent, and the national movement's long rebuilding under British rule down to 1914.
