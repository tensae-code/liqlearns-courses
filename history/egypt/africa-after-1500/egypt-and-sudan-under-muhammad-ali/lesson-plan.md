## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Egypt's civilisational role in Africa in Muhammad Ali's era** — Muhammad Ali drives into Sudan to widen Egypt's borders, crush the Mamluks who fled there and recruit soldiers.
2. **Egypt's role under Muhammad Ali's successors** — Khedive Ismail pushes the Egyptian flag to Lakes Albert and Victoria and the Somali coast, maps the continent in the Khedivial map of 1877….

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
