# Egypt and Sudan Under Muhammad Ali — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  ____ Ali drives into Sudan to widen Egypt's borders, crush the Mamluks who fled there and recruit soldiers; Ismail Pasha takes Sennar in 1821, his father inspects the gold country in 1838…
**Answer:** Muhammad

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  ____ Ismail pushes the Egyptian flag to Lakes Albert and Victoria and the Somali coast, maps the continent in the Khedivial map of 1877, and builds schools, railways, telegraph lines and…
**Answer:** Khedive
