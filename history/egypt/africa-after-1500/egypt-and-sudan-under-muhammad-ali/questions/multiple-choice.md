# Egypt and Sudan Under Muhammad Ali — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What is the heart of the story in “Egypt's civilisational role in Africa in Muhammad Ali's era”?
a) Islam appears in seventh-century Arabia and turns scattered tribes into one nation  b) Khalid ibn al-Walid opens Iraq — the Chains, al-Buwayb, al-Qadisiyya — and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas besieges al-Mada'in…  c) the course begins by asking what a civilisation is and what history is — the doorway every later lesson walks…  d) Muhammad Ali drives into Sudan to widen Egypt's borders, crush the Mamluks who fled there and recruit soldiers
**Answer: d) Muhammad Ali drives into Sudan to widen Egypt's borders, crush the Mamluks who fled there and recruit soldiers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What is the heart of the story in “Egypt's role under Muhammad Ali's successors”?
a) on the Nile's banks farming, villages and unity grow into the dynastic state, which trades south with Kush and Punt  b) Khedive Ismail pushes the Egyptian flag to Lakes Albert and Victoria and the Somali coast, maps the continent in…  c) in Medina the state is built on firm pillars — and at once beset by dangers from inside and outside, which the…  d) Abbas I closes most schools and factories
**Answer: b) Khedive Ismail pushes the Egyptian flag to Lakes Albert and Victoria and the Somali coast, maps the continent in…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
