# Egypt and Sudan Under Muhammad Ali — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Muhammad Ali drives into Sudan to widen Egypt's borders, crush the Mamluks who fled there and recruit soldiers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Egypt's role under Muhammad Ali's successors” is mainly about this: Khalid ibn al-Walid opens Iraq — the Chains, al-Buwayb, al-Qadisiyya — and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas besieges al-Mada'in for two months and prays in Kisra's White Palace.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Khedive Ismail pushes the Egyptian flag to Lakes Albert and Victoria and the Somali coast, maps the continent in the Khedivial map of 1877….
