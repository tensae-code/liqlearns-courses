# Egypt and Sudan Under Muhammad Ali

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Egypt and Sudan Under Muhammad Ali** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt's civilisational role in Africa in Muhammad Ali's era

Muhammad Ali drives into Sudan to widen Egypt's borders, crush the Mamluks who fled there and recruit soldiers. Ismail Pasha takes Sennar in 1821, his father inspects the gold country in 1838, and Salim Bey sails the White Nile toward the equatorial lakes. Khartoum rises as a capital with shipyard, hospital and factories and Sudanese students come to al-Azhar — until the Treaty of London in 1840 and the firman of 1841 fence the expansion in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Egypt's role under Muhammad Ali's successors

Khedive Ismail pushes the Egyptian flag to Lakes Albert and Victoria and the Somali coast, maps the continent in the Khedivial map of 1877, and builds schools, railways, telegraph lines and the Khartoum post office — but debt, crisis and foreign officials such as Gordon hollow the project out and open the road to Britain.
