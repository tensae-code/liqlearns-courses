# Egypt and Africa's Regional Organisations — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Egypt stands among the founders of the Organisation of African Unity in 1963 and of its successor, the African Union of 2002 seated in Addis Ababa, and works through Africa's regional blocs, COMESA… — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
