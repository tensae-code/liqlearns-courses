# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Rome defeats Carthage, takes Macedonia and Syria, and annexes Egypt last, in 30 BCE after Actium — the Mediterranean becomes a Roman lake.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “The Roman civilisation” is mainly about this: Sumerian city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Rome inherits the Mediterranean world Greece opened — the last of the ancient civilisations in the course's westward arc.
