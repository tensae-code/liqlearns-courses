# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Rome defeats Carthage, takes Macedonia and Syria, and annexes Egypt last, in 30 BCE after Actium — the Mediterranean becomes a Roman lake. Augustus keeps Egypt under his own direct control, Trajan re-digs the Nile–Red Sea canal, Diocletian's persecution gives Egypt its Era of Martyrs and the Coptic calendar from 284, and Constantine legalises Christianity at Milan in 313 and founds Constantinople. St Mark brings the faith to Alexandria and St Anthony founds monasticism in Egypt's eastern desert.

Rome inherits the Mediterranean world Greece opened — the last of the ancient civilisations in the course's westward arc.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
