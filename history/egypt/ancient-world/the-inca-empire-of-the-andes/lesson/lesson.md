# The Inca Empire of the Andes

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Inca Empire of the Andes** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Inca build South America's strongest empire on mountain terraces and aqueducts, store grain against famine, hang bridges of plant fibre across the gorges, mummify their dead and keep their records on knotted quipu cords — and Machu Picchu's mortarless stones still defy earthquakes.
