# The Inca Empire of the Andes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The Inca build South America's strongest empire on mountain terraces and aqueducts, store grain against famine, hang bridges of plant fibre across the gorges, mummify their dead and keep their records on knotted quipu cords — and Machu Picchu's mortarless stones still defy earthquakes.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The part “The ancient civilisations of the Americas: the Inca” is mainly about this: Abbas I closes most schools and factories.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: the Inca build South America's strongest empire on mountain terraces and aqueducts, store grain against famine, hang bridges of plant fibre….
