# Video script — The South Arabian Kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf.

Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf. Saba builds the great dam of Marib and Queen Bilqis visits Solomon, then fades as the dam breaks and trade takes to the sea. Himyar unites Yemen from Zafar until Abyssinia and Persia invade. Yemen writes in the twenty-nine letters of the Musnad, terraces its mountains and grows rich on incense — its myrrh reaches even Egypt's temples and embalmers.

In the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states grown rich on the incense roads.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
