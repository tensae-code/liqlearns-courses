# The South Arabian Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What happens in “Models of the civilisations of ancient Yemen”?
**Answer:** Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf; Saba builds the great dam of Marib and Queen Bilqis visits Solomon, then fades as the dam breaks and trade takes to the sea; Himyar unites Yemen from Zafar until Abyssinia…

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What happens in “Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the southern kingdoms”?
**Answer:** in the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states grown rich on the incense roads
