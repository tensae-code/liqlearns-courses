# The South Arabian Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the southern kingdoms” is mainly about this: the state organises the flood — land survey, irrigation offices, canals — and Amenemhat III throws his dam across the Faiyum's mouth to store the water and win twenty-seven thousand new feddans.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: in the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states grown rich on the incense….
