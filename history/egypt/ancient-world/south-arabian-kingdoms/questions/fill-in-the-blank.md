# The South Arabian Kingdoms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Ma'in trades from ____ in the fertile Jawf; Saba builds the great dam of Marib and Queen Bilqis visits Solomon, then fades as the dam breaks and trade takes to the sea; Himyar unites Yemen…
**Answer:** Qarnaw

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  in the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, ____ of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states grown rich on the incense roads
**Answer:** Saba
