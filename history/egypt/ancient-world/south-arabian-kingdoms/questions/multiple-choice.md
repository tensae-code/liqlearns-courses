# The South Arabian Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What is the heart of the story in “Models of the civilisations of ancient Yemen”?
a) Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf  b) mud-brick rooms for the poor, gardens for the rich, palaces for kings  c) on the Nile's banks farming, villages and unity grow into the dynastic state, which trades south with Kush and Punt  d) Rome defeats Carthage, takes Macedonia and Syria, and annexes Egypt last, in 30 BCE after Actium — the…
**Answer: a) Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf** — That is what this lesson teaches.
