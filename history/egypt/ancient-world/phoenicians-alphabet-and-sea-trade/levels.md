# The Phoenicians: Alphabet and Sea Trade — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The sea-people of the Levant coast take their place beside river-born Egypt and Iraq — a civilisation of ships, ports and letters rather than of one river valley. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
