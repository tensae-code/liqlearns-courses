# Arab-Islamic Civilisation: Science, Literature and Art — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Islam appears in seventh-century Arabia and turns scattered tribes into one nation.
**Answer: True**
