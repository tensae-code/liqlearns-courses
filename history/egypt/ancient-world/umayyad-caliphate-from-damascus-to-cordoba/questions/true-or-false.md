# The Umayyad Caliphate: From Damascus to Cordoba — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  After Ali's martyrdom a new age opens — the Umayyad state rules for about ninety-one years, the conquests widen, government and administration develop, and building and learning flourish — until the causes of its fall gather and the caliphate passes on.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “The Islamic conquests in the Umayyad age” is mainly about this: the civilisation at work and at prayer — its markets, farms and classes, its scholars, and the architecture its faith raised — the harvest of the Islamic centuries.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: under the Umayyads the paused movement of conquest rolls again to the state's widest reach.
