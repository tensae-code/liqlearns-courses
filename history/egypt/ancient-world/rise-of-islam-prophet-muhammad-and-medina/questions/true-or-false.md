# The Rise of Islam: The Prophet Muhammad and Medina — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  The Prophet's upbringing and mission in Mecca, and the hardships he and his followers face as Quraysh turns on the new call — the ground the next lesson's escape is built on.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “The Hijra and the universality of the call” is mainly about this: the landmarks carry Egypt's identity and world standing, so guarding them is every Egyptian's duty and each child is an ambassador.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: persecution drives the Muslims to leave their wealth and homes behind.
