# The Rise of Islam: The Prophet Muhammad and Medina — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What is the heart of the story in “The Arabian Peninsula after the appearance of Islam”?
a) Ahmose expels the Hyksos and founds an empire from the Euphrates to the Fourth Cataract  b) the Prophet's upbringing and mission in Mecca, and the hardships he and his followers face as Quraysh turns on the…  c) under the Umayyads the paused movement of conquest rolls again to the state's widest reach  d) Mecca of the Kaaba and the Quraysh caravans between Yemen and the Levant, farming Yathrib of the Aws and Khazraj…
**Answer: b) the Prophet's upbringing and mission in Mecca, and the hardships he and his followers face as Quraysh turns on the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What is the heart of the story in “The Hijra and the universality of the call”?
a) the landmarks carry Egypt's identity and world standing, so guarding them is every Egyptian's duty and each child…  b) the revolution, the occupation it fails to prevent, and the national movement's long rebuilding under British rule…  c) papyrus rolls and the inscriptions on Karnak's temple walls record events as they happen, while historians' books…  d) persecution drives the Muslims to leave their wealth and homes behind
**Answer: d) persecution drives the Muslims to leave their wealth and homes behind** — That is what this lesson teaches.
