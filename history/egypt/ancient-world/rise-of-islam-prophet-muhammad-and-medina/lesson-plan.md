## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Arabian Peninsula after the appearance of Islam** — the Prophet's upbringing and mission in Mecca, and the hardships he and his followers face as Quraysh turns on the new call — the ground….
2. **The Hijra and the universality of the call** — persecution drives the Muslims to leave their wealth and homes behind.
3. **The appearance of Islam** — the call begins in Mecca and a new community forms around the Prophet — the second unit's story opens here.
4. **Pillars of building the Islamic state, and the dangers it faced in Medina** — in Medina the state is built on firm pillars — and at once beset by dangers from inside and outside, which the young state survives.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
