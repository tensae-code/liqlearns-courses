## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Egypt in the Ptolemaic and Roman eras** — Alexander, Aristotle's pupil, is welcomed in 332 BC as a liberator from Persia, kneels to Amun at Siwa and plans Alexandria (331 BC).
2. **Egypt under the rule of the Ptolemies** — Alexander enters in 332 BC, honours Amun, treats Egyptians well and keeps their administration, and founds Alexandria on the village of….
3. **Egypt under Roman rule, 30 BC – 641 AD** — Rome wants Egypt for her wheat.
