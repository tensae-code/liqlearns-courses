# Egypt: Alexander, the Ptolemies and Rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  ____, Aristotle's pupil, is welcomed in 332 BC as a liberator from Persia, kneels to Amun at Siwa and plans Alexandria (331 BC); his general Ptolemy founds a Greek dynasty that lasts three…
**Answer:** Alexander

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  Alexander enters in ____, honours Amun, treats Egyptians well and keeps their administration, and founds Alexandria on the village of Raqoda; dying at Babylon in 323 BC he leaves Egypt to…
**Answer:** 332 BC
