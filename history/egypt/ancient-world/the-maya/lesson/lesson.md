# The Maya

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Maya** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens. Their kings join political and religious power and are buried in pyramids with their treasures for the next life. The Maya write in glyphs like Egypt's, split the year into 365 days, watch the stars from observatories and raise stepped pyramids like Kukulcan's.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens. Give one real example: Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
