# Video script — The Maya

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens.

Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens. Their kings join political and religious power and are buried in pyramids with their treasures for the next life. The Maya write in glyphs like Egypt's, split the year into 365 days, watch the stars from observatories and raise stepped pyramids like Kukulcan's.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
