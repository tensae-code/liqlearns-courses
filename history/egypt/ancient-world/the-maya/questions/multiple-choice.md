# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “The ancient civilisations of the Americas: the Maya”?
a) the long arc of the Pharaonic state from the first unification through the ages of strength and decline — the…  b) papyrus rolls and the inscriptions on Karnak's temple walls record events as they happen, while historians' books…  c) Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens  d) Mecca of the Kaaba and the Quraysh caravans between Yemen and the Levant, farming Yathrib of the Aws and Khazraj…
**Answer: c) Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens later in “The ancient civilisations of the Americas: the Maya”?

**Answer: c) the Maya write in glyphs like Egypt's, split the year into 365 days, watch the stars from observatories and raise…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
