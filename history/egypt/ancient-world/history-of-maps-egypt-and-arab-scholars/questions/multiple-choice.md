Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 3: the development of the map”?
a) ancient Egyptians draw some of the first maps, Arab scholars refine map-making, Maryam al-Asturlabi's astrolabes…  b) Khalid ibn al-Walid opens Iraq — the Chains, al-Buwayb, al-Qadisiyya — and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas besieges al-Mada'in…  c) on the Nile's banks farming, villages and unity grow into the dynastic state, which trades south with Kush and Punt  d) on the desert's edge two Arab kingdoms face each other as the shields of two empires — the Ghassanids for Rome…
