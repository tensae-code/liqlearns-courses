# What Is a Civilisation? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Factors for the rise of civilisations”?
a) Axum, in today's Eritrea and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and…  b) the revolution abolishes feudalism — agrarian reform from September 1952 caps ownership at fifty feddans — makes…  c) Europe's states race to build empires while its merchants hunt a sea road to India free of Ottoman and Mamluk…  d) rivers that force cooperation, meeting-places of continents, minerals in the hills, safety from invasion, and…
**Answer: d) rivers that force cooperation, meeting-places of continents, minerals in the hills, safety from invasion, and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
