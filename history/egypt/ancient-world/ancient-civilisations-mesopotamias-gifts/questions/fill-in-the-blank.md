# Mesopotamia's Gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.**  Ur-____'s code is the first written law, protecting the weak and ordering trade, and Hammurabi's laws follow, even guarding women's rights; the Sumerians invent cuneiform and the…
**Answer:** Nammu

**2.**  Ur-Nammu's code is the first written law, protecting the weak and ordering trade, and ____'s laws follow, even guarding women's rights; the Sumerians invent cuneiform and the solar-lunar…
**Answer:** Hammurabi
