# Mesopotamia's Gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Features of ancient Iraq's civilisations”?
a) Ur-Nammu's code is the first written law, protecting the weak and ordering trade, and Hammurabi's laws follow…  b) the long arc of the Pharaonic state from the first unification through the ages of strength and decline — the…  c) the revolution, the occupation it fails to prevent, and the national movement's long rebuilding under British rule…  d) Khedive Ismail pushes the Egyptian flag to Lakes Albert and Victoria and the Somali coast, maps the continent in…
**Answer: a) Ur-Nammu's code is the first written law, protecting the weak and ordering trade, and Hammurabi's laws follow…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens later in “Features of ancient Iraq's civilisations”?
a) and Iraq's trade reaches Egypt, Persia, India and the Levant  b) persecution drives the Muslims to leave their wealth and homes behind  c) the class sorts statements about a school trip into fact, opinion and rumour before trusting any of them  d) Djoser's minister Imhotep raises the first stone pyramid at Saqqara
**Answer: a) and Iraq's trade reaches Egypt, Persia, India and the Levant** — That is what this lesson teaches.
