# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What is the heart of the story in “The Greek civilisation”?
a) Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens  b) many gods for one ordered world, and beneath them the belief that shaped everything — resurrection and immortality  c) a land of tribes and their wars, ruled by custom and the shaykh's word  d) Crete grows rich on sea trade, exchanging goods with Middle and New Kingdom Egypt
**Answer: d) Crete grows rich on sea trade, exchanging goods with Middle and New Kingdom Egypt** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What is the heart of the story in “The Greek civilisation”?
a) Axum, in today's Eritrea and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and…  b) Greece takes the ancient world's story westward — the civilisation Egypt's pupils meet just before Alexander…  c) in Medina the state is built on firm pillars — and at once beset by dangers from inside and outside, which the…  d) fire, farming and copper carry the Egyptian down from the plateaus to the river
**Answer: b) Greece takes the ancient world's story westward — the civilisation Egypt's pupils meet just before Alexander…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
