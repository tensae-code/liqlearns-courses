# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Crete grows rich on sea trade, exchanging goods with Middle and New Kingdom Egypt.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “The Greek civilisation” is mainly about this: Alexander, Aristotle's pupil, is welcomed in 332 BC as a liberator from Persia, kneels to Amun at Siwa and plans Alexandria (331 BC).
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Greece takes the ancient world's story westward — the civilisation Egypt's pupils meet just before Alexander carries it back east.
