# The Pre-Islam Arab States of the North — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — On the desert's edge two Arab kingdoms face each other as the shields of two empires — the Ghassanids for Rome, whose last king Jabala ibn al-Ayham meets the Muslims at Yarmouk in 634, and the… — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
