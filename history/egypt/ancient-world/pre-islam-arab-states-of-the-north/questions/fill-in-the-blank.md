# The Pre-Islam Arab States of the North — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.**  on the desert's edge two ____ kingdoms face each other as the shields of two empires — the Ghassanids for Rome, whose last king Jabala ibn al-Ayham meets the Muslims at Yarmouk in 634, and…
**Answer:** Arab
