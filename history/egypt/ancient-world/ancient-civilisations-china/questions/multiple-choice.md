# Ancient Civilisations: China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Models of the ancient civilisations of the Far East: China”?
a) Islam appears in seventh-century Arabia and turns scattered tribes into one nation  b) on the Yellow River the Qin unify China's warring states under one central rule  c) Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens  d) Djoser raises the first great stone building, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara, the Giza pyramids follow, and Karnak's…
**Answer: b) on the Yellow River the Qin unify China's warring states under one central rule** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens later in “Models of the ancient civilisations of the Far East: China”?
a) Islam appears in seventh-century Arabia and turns scattered tribes into one nation  b) Cai Lun invents paper, and printing, the compass and gunpowder follow, while Buddhism arrives from India  c) ancient Egyptians draw some of the first maps, Arab scholars refine map-making, Maryam al-Asturlabi's astrolabes…  d) between the two rivers the city-states of Sumer give way to Sargon of Akkad, unifier of Iraq in 2350 BC
**Answer: b) Cai Lun invents paper, and printing, the compass and gunpowder follow, while Buddhism arrives from India** — That is what this lesson teaches.
