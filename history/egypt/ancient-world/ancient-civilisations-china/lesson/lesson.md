# Ancient Civilisations: China

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Civilisations: China** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

On the Yellow River the Qin unify China's warring states under one central rule. The Great Wall guards the northern border. Silk, porcelain and bronze travel the Silk Road to Central Asia and Europe. Cai Lun invents paper, and printing, the compass and gunpowder follow, while Buddhism arrives from India.

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: On the Yellow River the Qin unify China's warring states under one central rule. Give one real example: on the Yellow River the Qin unify China's warring states under one central rule. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: On the Yellow River the Qin unify China's warring states under one central rule.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
