# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  When the Prophet dies, the Companions gather at the Saqifa of Bani Sa'ida and choose Abu Bakr as the first caliph.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “The Islamic state after the Prophet's death” is mainly about this: the long arc of the Pharaonic state from the first unification through the ages of strength and decline — the frame on which the whole unit hangs.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Abu Bakr holds the state together through the apostasy wars and secures its borders.
