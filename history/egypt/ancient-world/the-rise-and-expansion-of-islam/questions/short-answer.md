# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What happens in “Achievements of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs”?
**Answer:** when the Prophet dies, the Companions gather at the Saqifa of Bani Sa'ida and choose Abu Bakr as the first caliph; four caliphs in turn — Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali — spread Islam, judge between people with justice…

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What happens in “The Islamic state after the Prophet's death”?
**Answer:** Abu Bakr holds the state together through the apostasy wars and secures its borders; Umar ibn al-Khattab takes up the work — the caliphate survives its founder
