# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What is the heart of the story in “Achievements of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs”?
a) when the Prophet dies, the Companions gather at the Saqifa of Bani Sa'ida and choose Abu Bakr as the first caliph  b) India's ancients plan their cities with set quarters for homes, markets, trade and ritual, carve the caves of…  c) rivers that force cooperation, meeting-places of continents, minerals in the hills, safety from invasion, and…  d) after Ali's martyrdom a new age opens — the Umayyad state rules for about ninety-one years, the conquests widen…
**Answer: a) when the Prophet dies, the Companions gather at the Saqifa of Bani Sa'ida and choose Abu Bakr as the first caliph** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What is the heart of the story in “The Islamic state after the Prophet's death”?
a) Abu Bakr holds the state together through the apostasy wars and secures its borders  b) Rome defeats Carthage, takes Macedonia and Syria, and annexes Egypt last, in 30 BCE after Actium — the…  c) Djoser raises the first great stone building, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara, the Giza pyramids follow, and Karnak's…  d) the 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul, the declaration of 28 February 1922 and the constitutions of 1923 and 1930…
**Answer: a) Abu Bakr holds the state together through the apostasy wars and secures its borders** — That is what this lesson teaches.
