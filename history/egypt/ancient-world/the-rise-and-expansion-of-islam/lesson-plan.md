**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Arabia, Islam and Medieval Egypt (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Achievements of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs** — when the Prophet dies, the Companions gather at the Saqifa of Bani Sa'ida and choose Abu Bakr as the first caliph.
2. **The Islamic state after the Prophet's death** — Abu Bakr holds the state together through the apostasy wars and secures its borders.
3. **The Islamic conquests in the age of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs** — Khalid ibn al-Walid opens Iraq — the Chains, al-Buwayb, al-Qadisiyya — and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas besieges al-Mada'in for two months and prays….

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
