# The Crusades and the Muslim Response — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.**  ____ restores Jerusalem and lifts Muslim hearts; Richard's crusade ends in the peace of 1192 and Saladin dies the next year; his brother al-Adil sees that the Crusaders now aim at Egypt…
**Answer:** Hattin

**2.**  Hattin restores ____ and lifts Muslim hearts; Richard's crusade ends in the peace of 1192 and Saladin dies the next year; his brother al-Adil sees that the Crusaders now aim at Egypt…
**Answer:** Jerusalem
