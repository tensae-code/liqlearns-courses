# The Crusades and the Muslim Response — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What is the heart of the story in “Egypt in the Ayyubid age: Saladin and the Crusades”?
a) Hattin restores Jerusalem and lifts Muslim hearts  b) the Inca build South America's strongest empire on mountain terraces and aqueducts, store grain against famine…  c) homes, services and transport change within living memory, from simple houses and animal carts to modern roads…  d) many gods for one ordered world, and beneath them the belief that shaped everything — resurrection and immortality
**Answer: a) Hattin restores Jerusalem and lifts Muslim hearts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
