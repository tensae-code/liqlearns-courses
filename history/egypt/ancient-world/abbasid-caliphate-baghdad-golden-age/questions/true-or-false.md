# The Abbasid Caliphate: Baghdad's Golden Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  The Abbasid age carries the same story forward — caliphs patronising the sciences, cities rising in an urban renaissance, and an empire whose own weaknesses finally bring it down.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “The Islamic conquests in the Abbasid age” is mainly about this: Maya city-states in the far south of North America ally when danger threatens.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: the Abbasid age inherits the conquests' frontier and holds it — the expansion story's last chapter in this unit.
