# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What is the heart of the story in “Models of the ancient civilisations of the Near East: Iraq”?
a) Sumerian city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu  b) Hattin restores Jerusalem and lifts Muslim hearts  c) after Ali's martyrdom a new age opens — the Umayyad state rules for about ninety-one years, the conquests widen…  d) the Aztecs raise a centralised military empire on farming in the south of North America, one of the last great…
**Answer: a) Sumerian city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What is the heart of the story in “Civilisation of ancient Iraq”?
a) on the desert's edge two Arab kingdoms face each other as the shields of two empires — the Ghassanids for Rome…  b) between the two rivers the city-states of Sumer give way to Sargon of Akkad, unifier of Iraq in 2350 BC  c) in the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states…  d) Tawfiq restores the foreign Dual Control and Riyad Pasha's absolutism
**Answer: b) between the two rivers the city-states of Sumer give way to Sargon of Akkad, unifier of Iraq in 2350 BC** — That is what this lesson teaches.
