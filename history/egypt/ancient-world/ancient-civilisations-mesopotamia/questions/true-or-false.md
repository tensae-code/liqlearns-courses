# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Sumerian city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Civilisation of ancient Iraq” is mainly about this: out of the campaign's wreckage Muhammad Ali and his house build the modern Egyptian state — the nineteenth-century transformation the book's introduction promises: its economy, army, schools and society remade.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: between the two rivers the city-states of Sumer give way to Sargon of Akkad, unifier of Iraq in 2350 BC.
