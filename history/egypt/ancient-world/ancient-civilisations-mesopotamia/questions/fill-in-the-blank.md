# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  ____ city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu; Sargon of Akkad conquers and unifies them into the first kingdom; Babylon's Hammurabi issues his famous laws; Assyria's…
**Answer:** Sumerian

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  between the two rivers the city-states of ____ give way to Sargon of Akkad, unifier of Iraq in 2350 BC; Hammurabi rebuilds Babylon, raises Marduk's temple and carves his 282 laws on stone…
**Answer:** Sumer
