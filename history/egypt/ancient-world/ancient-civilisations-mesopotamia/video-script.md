# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Sumerian city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu.

Sumerian city-states rise on the Euphrates, Ur under King Ur-Nammu. Sargon of Akkad conquers and unifies them into the first kingdom. Babylon's Hammurabi issues his famous laws. Assyria's Sargon II rules by military might until repeated revolts weaken the state.

Between the two rivers the city-states of Sumer give way to Sargon of Akkad, unifier of Iraq in 2350 BC. Hammurabi rebuilds Babylon, raises Marduk's temple and carves his 282 laws on stone, received — he says — from the god of justice. The Assyrians build an empire on a strong army and lose it to their own cruelty. Nebuchadnezzar II stretches Babylon from the Gulf to the Mediterranean. Then Cyrus takes the city in 538 BC, Alexander and the Seleucids and Rome and Persia pass over the land, until the Arab conquest of 632 opens a new flourishing age.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
