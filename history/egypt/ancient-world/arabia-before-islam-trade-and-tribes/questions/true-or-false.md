# Arabia Before Islam: Trade and Tribes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  A land of tribes and their wars, ruled by custom and the shaykh's word.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The part “Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the Hijaz and the tribal world” is mainly about this: the written, reckoned and healed world the Egyptians built — the intellectual harvest of the Pharaonic civilisation.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Mecca of the Kaaba and the Quraysh caravans between Yemen and the Levant, farming Yathrib of the Aws and Khazraj, and Taif in its hills.
