# Arabia Before Islam: Trade and Tribes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  a land of tribes and their wars, ruled by custom and the shaykh's word; herders on the steppe, sword- and spear-makers in the towns, and ____'s merchants organising the caravans of the…
**Answer:** Mecca

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  Mecca of the ____ and the Quraysh caravans between Yemen and the Levant, farming Yathrib of the Aws and Khazraj, and Taif in its hills; a world ruled by the shaykh and the tribal council…
**Answer:** Kaaba
