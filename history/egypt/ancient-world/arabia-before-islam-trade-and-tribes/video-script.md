# Video script — Arabia Before Islam: Trade and Tribes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
A land of tribes and their wars, ruled by custom and the shaykh's word.

A land of tribes and their wars, ruled by custom and the shaykh's word. Herders on the steppe, sword- and spear-makers in the towns, and Mecca's merchants organising the caravans of the winter and summer journeys. A society of rich merchants, small craftsmen and slaves, whose courage, generosity and loyalty sit beside buried daughters, blood feuds, wine and gambling.

Mecca of the Kaaba and the Quraysh caravans between Yemen and the Levant, farming Yathrib of the Aws and Khazraj, and Taif in its hills. A world ruled by the shaykh and the tribal council, torn by wars like al-Basus yet capable of the Fudul pact for the wronged — and in religion a mixture of Abraham's Hanifs, Jews, Christians, and the idol-worship of Hubal, al-Lat and al-Uzza.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Egypt.
