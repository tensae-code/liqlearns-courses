# Ancient India

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient India** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

India's ancients plan their cities with set quarters for homes, markets, trade and ritual, carve the caves of Ajanta, chart the moving earth and planets, and practise surgery and herbal medicine.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: India's ancients plan their cities with set quarters for homes, markets, trade and ritual, carve the caves of Ajanta, chart the moving earth and planets, and practise surgery and herbal medicine.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
