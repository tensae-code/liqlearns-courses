# History — Egypt course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Egypt's own — built from Egypt's Ministry of Education and Technical Education student books (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside Levels 1–6, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': learn the historian's craft first, the way the youngest books teach it; walk Pharaonic Egypt whole inside Africa; measure the ancient world against Egypt; follow Arabia, Islam and medieval Egypt; watch the Ottomans fall, Napoleon land and Muhammad Ali build; and end with occupation, liberation and the modern Arab world. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
