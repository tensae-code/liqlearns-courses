# Carthage and the Punic Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 1: Rome against Carthage”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Hannibal leads elephants over the Alps and destroys a Roman army at Cannae, yet cannot take Rome; Scipio carries the war to Africa and wins at Zama, and a half-century later Rome burns Carthage to the ground and enslaves its people.
