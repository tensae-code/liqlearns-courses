# The Bogomils Macedonias Heretics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: Bogomilism”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** In a 10th-century Macedonia squeezed by war taxes and rich monasteries, priest Bogomil of the village Bogomila preaches a dualist faith — the visible world is the Devil's work, so reject cross, icons, marriage and masters; presbyter Kozma writes against them, Byzantium burns their leader Basil, yet the teaching travels — to Bosnia's Patarenes and France's Cathars.
