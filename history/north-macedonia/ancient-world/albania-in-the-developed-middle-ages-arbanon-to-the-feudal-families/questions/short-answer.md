# Albania in the Developed Middle Ages Arbanon to the Feudal Families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: Albania in the developed Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The nobleman Progon carves out the principality of Arbanon at Krujë; Epirus's Theodore takes Thessalonica and a crown, then falls at Klokotnitsa; Charles of Anjou proclaims a Kingdom of Albania in 1272; Dušan takes all but Durrës — and after his empire cracks, the feudal families rule: Topia, Balsha, Shpata, Muzaki, Kastrioti, Dukagjini, until Karl Topia calls in the Ottomans at Savra in 1385.
