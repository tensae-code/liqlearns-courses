# Video script — Albania in the Developed Middle Ages Arbanon to the Feudal Families

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of albania in the Developed Middle Ages Arbanon to the Feudal Families — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The nobleman Progon carves out the principality of Arbanon at Krujë; Epirus's Theodore takes Thessalonica and a crown, then falls at Klokotnitsa; Charles of Anjou proclaims a Kingdom of Albania in 1272; Dušan takes all but Durrës — and after his empire cracks, the feudal families rule: Topia, Balsha, Shpata, Muzaki, Kastrioti, Dukagjini, until Karl Topia calls in the Ottomans at Savra in 1385.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
