# The Great Schism of 1054 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 1: the Church in the early Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Five patriarchates guard the faith and councils settle its quarrels, but Rome's pope and Constantinople's patriarch drift apart over doctrine and pride until, in 1054, mutual excommunication splits Christianity into Catholic West and Orthodox East — a divorce never healed.
