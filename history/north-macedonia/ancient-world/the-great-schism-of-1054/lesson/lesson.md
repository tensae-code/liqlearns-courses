# The Great Schism of 1054

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Five patriarchates guard the faith and councils settle its quarrels, but Rome's pope and Constantinople's patriarch drift apart over doctrine and pride until, in 1054, mutual excommunication splits Christianity into Catholic West and Orthodox East — a divorce never healed.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Great Schism of 1054 — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Great Schism of 1054. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
