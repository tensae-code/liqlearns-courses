# Video script — King Marko and the Last Macedonian Kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of king Marko and the Last Macedonian Kingdoms — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Dušan's death frees the brothers Volkašin, crowned king at Prilep in 1365, and Ugleša, despot at Serres; they defeat the tsar's army at Kosovo Field yet fall together at the Maritsa in 1371; Volkašin's son Marko keeps only Pelagonia as the sultan's vassal — and dies fighting for the Ottomans at Rovine in 1395, beside Konstantin Dejanović of Velbužd, even as epic song makes him the greatest hero of the Balkans.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
