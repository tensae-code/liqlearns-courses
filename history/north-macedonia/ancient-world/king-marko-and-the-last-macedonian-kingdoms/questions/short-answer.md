# King Marko and the Last Macedonian Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: feudal rulers in Macedonia in the 14th century”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Dušan's death frees the brothers Volkašin, crowned king at Prilep in 1365, and Ugleša, despot at Serres; they defeat the tsar's army at Kosovo Field yet fall together at the Maritsa in 1371; Volkašin's son Marko keeps only Pelagonia as the sultan's vassal — and dies fighting for the Ottomans at Rovine in 1395, beside Konstantin Dejanović of Velbužd, even as epic song makes him the greatest hero of the Balkans.
