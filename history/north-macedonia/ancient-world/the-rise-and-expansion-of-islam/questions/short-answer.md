# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: the appearance of Islam and the state of the Arabs”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** On Arabia's incense road, between desert Bedouin and the trading city of Mecca, Muhammad preaches one God; driven out in the Hijra of 622, he returns to unite the Arabs, and his caliph successors conquer from India to Spain within a century — until the caliphate splits into Baghdad, Cairo and Cordoba.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: Islam and the empires of the East”?
**Answer:** Among Mecca's 300 idols Muhammad preaches one God; the Hijra to Medina founds the umma, and within a century his caliph successors rule from Syria to Spain, checked only at Poitiers in 732 — while the succession quarrel splits Islam into Sunni and Shia forever.
