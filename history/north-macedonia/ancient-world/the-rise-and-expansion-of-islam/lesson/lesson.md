# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 1: the appearance of Islam and the state of the Arabs

On Arabia's incense road, between desert Bedouin and the trading city of Mecca, Muhammad preaches one God; driven out in the Hijra of 622, he returns to unite the Arabs, and his caliph successors conquer from India to Spain within a century — until the caliphate splits into Baghdad, Cairo and Cordoba.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2: Islam and the empires of the East

Among Mecca's 300 idols Muhammad preaches one God; the Hijra to Medina founds the umma, and within a century his caliph successors rule from Syria to Spain, checked only at Poitiers in 732 — while the succession quarrel splits Islam into Sunni and Shia forever.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Rise and Expansion of Islam — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Rise and Expansion of Islam. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
