# Video script — Samuils Empire Macedonias Medieval Tsardom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of samuils Empire Macedonias Medieval Tsardom — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

When Byzantium devours Bulgaria in 971, the four Komitopuli brothers raise Macedonia in revolt; Samuil alone survives to build an empire from Prespa and then Ohrid, humbles Basil II at Trajan's Gate in 986 and takes a tsar's crown with Rome's blessing — until at Belasica in 1014 his army is trapped and, the chronicler says, 15,000 prisoners are blinded; Samuil dies of grief within days, and by 1018 his empire is gone.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
