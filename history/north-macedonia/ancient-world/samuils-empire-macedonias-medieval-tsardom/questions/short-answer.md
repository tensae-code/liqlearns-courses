# Samuils Empire Macedonias Medieval Tsardom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: Samuil's state”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** When Byzantium devours Bulgaria in 971, the four Komitopuli brothers raise Macedonia in revolt; Samuil alone survives to build an empire from Prespa and then Ohrid, humbles Basil II at Trajan's Gate in 986 and takes a tsar's crown with Rome's blessing — until at Belasica in 1014 his army is trapped and, the chronicler says, 15,000 prisoners are blinded; Samuil dies of grief within days, and by 1018 his empire is gone.
