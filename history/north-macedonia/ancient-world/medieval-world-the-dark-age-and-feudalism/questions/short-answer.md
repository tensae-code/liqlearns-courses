# Medieval World the Dark Age and Feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Introduction: the concept and periodisation of the Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Renaissance humanists name the age between antiquity and themselves the "middle" age; historians open it with the fall of the Western Roman Empire in 476 and close it at the discovery of America (1492) or the English Revolution, dividing it into early, developed and late periods as feudalism rises and decays.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 1: society in the early Middle Ages”?
**Answer:** Western society stacks into a feudal hierarchy — the ruler grants fiefs to great nobles, they to lesser ones, each owing loyalty and war-service upward, while at the bottom free peasants slide into serfdom; Byzantium runs the opposite way, a centralised state of salaried officials, theme armies and free tax-paying farmers.
