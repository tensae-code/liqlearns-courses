# Capetian France and the Hundred Years War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 3: the Kingdom of France”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Hugh Capet begins in 987 with little more than Paris; Philip II Augustus and Saint Louis build a real kingdom; when the Capetian line fails, England's king claims the throne and the Hundred Years War (1337–1453) begins — English longbowmen win the early battles, French cannon the last, and France emerges a great power (the book sets Joan of Arc as the pupils' own research).
