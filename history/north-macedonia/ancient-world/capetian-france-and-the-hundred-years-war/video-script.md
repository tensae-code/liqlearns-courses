# Video script — Capetian France and the Hundred Years War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of capetian France and the Hundred Years War — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Hugh Capet begins in 987 with little more than Paris; Philip II Augustus and Saint Louis build a real kingdom; when the Capetian line fails, England's king claims the throne and the Hundred Years War (1337–1453) begins — English longbowmen win the early battles, French cannon the last, and France emerges a great power (the book sets Joan of Arc as the pupils' own research).

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
