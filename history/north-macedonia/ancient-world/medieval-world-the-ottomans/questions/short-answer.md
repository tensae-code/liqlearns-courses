# Medieval World the Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 4: the Ottomans and their conquests in the Balkans”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Seljuk Turks fleeing the Mongols settle Anatolia in beyliks; Emir Osman unites them and his heirs cross at Gallipoli in 1352, take Adrianople as Edirne, destroy the Serbian and Bulgarian kingdoms between Maritsa (1371), Kosovo (1389) and Nicopolis (1396), and leave Macedonia wholly theirs when Thessalonica finally falls in 1430.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 5: the Ottoman Empire — its organisation and army”?
**Answer:** A centralised empire under an absolute sultan, advised by the divan and served by the grand vizier; sharia law in kadi courts; Rumelia and Anatolia as first eyalets; and an army of timar-holding sipahi cavalry plus the janissaries — Christian boys taken by the devshirme, raised as the sultan's own, some rising to grand vizier — at its height under Suleiman the Lawgiver, whose fleets and armies press Vienna.
