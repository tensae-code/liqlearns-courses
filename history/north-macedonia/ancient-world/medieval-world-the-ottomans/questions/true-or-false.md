# Medieval World the Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Seljuk Turks fleeing the Mongols settle Anatolia in beyliks; Emir Osman unites them and his heirs cross at Gallipoli in 1352, take Adrianople as Edirne, destroy the Serbian and Bulgarian kingdoms between Maritsa (1371), Kosovo (1389) and Nicopolis (1396), and leave Macedonia wholly theirs when Thessalonica finally falls in 1430.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A centralised empire under an absolute sultan, advised by the divan and served by the grand vizier; sharia law in kadi courts; Rumelia and Anatolia as first eya
