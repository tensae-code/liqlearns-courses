# Video script — The Great Migration and the Fall of the West

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Great Migration and the Fall of the West — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Climate change, hunger and the Huns push tribe after tribe across Rome's frontiers; Attila's host is broken at the Battle of the Nations in 451, Rome is sacked twice, and in 476 the last western emperor is deposed — on the ruins rise the kingdoms of Visigoths, Ostrogoths, Franks, Vandals and Anglo-Saxons, then Avars, Lombards and Slavs.

Cold, dry steppes and growing tribes set the dominoes falling; the Huns burst through the Gate of Peoples, Attila is checked at the Catalaunian Plains in 451, and Germanic kingdoms — Visigoth, Ostrogoth, Frankish, Vandal, Anglo-Saxon — inherit the West, with Avars, Lombards and Slavs following in the sixth century.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
