# The Great Migration and the Fall of the West — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: the Great Migration of the peoples and the early European kingdoms”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Climate change, hunger and the Huns push tribe after tribe across Rome's frontiers; Attila's host is broken at the Battle of the Nations in 451, Rome is sacked twice, and in 476 the last western emperor is deposed — on the ruins rise the kingdoms of Visigoths, Ostrogoths, Franks, Vandals and Anglo-Saxons, then Avars, Lombards and Slavs.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: the Great Migration of peoples”?
**Answer:** Cold, dry steppes and growing tribes set the dominoes falling; the Huns burst through the Gate of Peoples, Attila is checked at the Catalaunian Plains in 451, and Germanic kingdoms — Visigoth, Ostrogoth, Frankish, Vandal, Anglo-Saxon — inherit the West, with Avars, Lombards and Slavs following in the sixth century.
