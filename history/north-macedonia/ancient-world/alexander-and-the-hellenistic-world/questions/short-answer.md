# Alexander and the Hellenistic World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 1: Alexander of Macedon”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Tutored by Aristotle and king at twenty after Philip II's murder, Alexander breaks Persia at Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela, is crowned pharaoh in Egypt, marries his army to Asia at Susa, and dies at Babylon in 323 BCE — his generals carve the wreck into Hellenistic kingdoms.
