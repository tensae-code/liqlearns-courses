# Everyday Life in the Middle Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 3: life in the Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Nobles feast, play chess and joust in castle tournaments while peasants live by the sun and the church calendar and crowd to fairs on feast days; towns are filthy and plague kills a third of Europe in the 14th century; and women — subordinate in law — still leave marks as the poetess Kassia, the historian Anna Komnina, Christine de Pizan and Empresses Theodora and Irene.
