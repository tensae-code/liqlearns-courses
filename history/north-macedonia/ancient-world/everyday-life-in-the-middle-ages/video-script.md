# Video script — Everyday Life in the Middle Ages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of everyday Life in the Middle Ages — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Nobles feast, play chess and joust in castle tournaments while peasants live by the sun and the church calendar and crowd to fairs on feast days; towns are filthy and plague kills a third of Europe in the 14th century; and women — subordinate in law — still leave marks as the poetess Kassia, the historian Anna Komnina, Christine de Pizan and Empresses Theodora and Irene.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
