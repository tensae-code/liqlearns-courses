# Medieval World Byzantium and Justinian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: the Eastern Roman Empire (Byzantium) under Justinian I”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Constantinople, Constantine's New Rome, outlives the West by a thousand years; Justinian's generals win back lands on three continents, his lawyers codify Roman law for all later Europe, and his builders raise Hagia Sophia — while the Nika revolt nearly ends him and Empress Theodora's nerve saves the throne.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the Eastern Roman Empire”?
**Answer:** Constantinople, founded in 330, calls itself the second Rome and its people Romaioi; Justinian codifies the law, raises Hagia Sophia and reconquers Italy, Africa and Spain — with Theodora beside him on the mosaic wall.
