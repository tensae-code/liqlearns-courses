# Video script — Medieval World Byzantium and Justinian

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of medieval World Byzantium and Justinian — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Constantinople, Constantine's New Rome, outlives the West by a thousand years; Justinian's generals win back lands on three continents, his lawyers codify Roman law for all later Europe, and his builders raise Hagia Sophia — while the Nika revolt nearly ends him and Empress Theodora's nerve saves the throne.

Constantinople, founded in 330, calls itself the second Rome and its people Romaioi; Justinian codifies the law, raises Hagia Sophia and reconquers Italy, Africa and Spain — with Theodora beside him on the mosaic wall.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
