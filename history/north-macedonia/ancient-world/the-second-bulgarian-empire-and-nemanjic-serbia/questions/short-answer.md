# The Second Bulgarian Empire and Nemanjic Serbia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: the rise of Bulgaria and Serbia”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The brothers Petar and Asen raise Bulgaria in 1185 and Byzantium concedes the Second Empire at Tarnovo in 1187; Kaloyan takes a crown from the pope's legate and captures the Latin emperor himself; Ivan Asen II crushes Epirus at Klokotnitsa in 1230 — meanwhile Stefan Nemanja founds Serbia, his son is crowned king in 1217, Saint Sava wins an autocephalous church, Milutin takes Skopje, and Dušan — crowned tsar in Skopje in 1346 — gives his empire a law code before it shatters under Uroš V.
