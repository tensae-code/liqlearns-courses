# Video script — The Second Bulgarian Empire and Nemanjic Serbia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Second Bulgarian Empire and Nemanjic Serbia — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The brothers Petar and Asen raise Bulgaria in 1185 and Byzantium concedes the Second Empire at Tarnovo in 1187; Kaloyan takes a crown from the pope's legate and captures the Latin emperor himself; Ivan Asen II crushes Epirus at Klokotnitsa in 1230 — meanwhile Stefan Nemanja founds Serbia, his son is crowned king in 1217, Saint Sava wins an autocephalous church, Milutin takes Skopje, and Dušan — crowned tsar in Skopje in 1346 — gives his empire a law code before it shatters under Uroš V.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
