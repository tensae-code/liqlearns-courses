# Video script — Cyril and Methodius and the First Slavic Alphabet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of cyril and Methodius and the First Slavic Alphabet — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Slavs cannot hear a gospel preached in Greek, so the Thessalonica brothers Cyril and Methodius build the 38-letter Glagolitic alphabet from their own region's Slavic speech; sent to Moravia in 863, they defy the "trilingual" clergy — "does the rain not fall on all alike?" — win the pope's blessing in Rome, and after Methodius dies in 885 their disciples are driven out, carrying the letters south.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
