# Cyril and Methodius and the First Slavic Alphabet — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: the appearance and development of the Slavonic alphabet and literacy”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Slavs cannot hear a gospel preached in Greek, so the Thessalonica brothers Cyril and Methodius build the 38-letter Glagolitic alphabet from their own region's Slavic speech; sent to Moravia in 863, they defy the "trilingual" clergy — "does the rain not fall on all alike?" — win the pope's blessing in Rome, and after Methodius dies in 885 their disciples are driven out, carrying the letters south.
