# Albania in the Early Middle Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: Albania in the early Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Goths, Huns, Avars and Slavs sweep the Illyrians' Romanised descendants into coastal cities and high mountains; Byzantium organises the land into the themes of Dyrrachion and Nikopolis, with Durrës guarding the start of the Via Egnatia; Bulgaria and Samuil's empire take turns ruling it, the people rise under Tihomir in 1040, and Norman invaders close the period.
