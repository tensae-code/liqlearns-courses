**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 1: Sparta, and the two cities against Persia and each other”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Sparta raises its boys in the agoge from age seven under two kings, 28 elders and five all-powerful ephors, its mothers bidding sons come back "with your shield or on it"; Athens and Sparta together break Persia at Salamis and Plataea, then ruin each other in the Peloponnesian War.
