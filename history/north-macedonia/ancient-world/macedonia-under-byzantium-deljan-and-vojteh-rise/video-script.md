# Video script — Macedonia under Byzantium Deljan and Vojteh Rise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of macedonia under Byzantium Deljan and Vojteh Rise — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Basil II rules the conquered land gently, but his successors raise taxes and import Greek bishops; in 1040 Petar Deljan, claiming Samuil's blood, is proclaimed tsar and sweeps the Balkans — until his own cousin Alusian blinds him at a feast and the rising collapses; in 1072 Skopje's nobles under Gjorgji Vojteh crown Duklja's Konstantin Bodin and die at Kosovo Field.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
