# Macedonia under Byzantium Deljan and Vojteh Rise — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: the uprisings in Macedonia in the 11th century”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Basil II rules the conquered land gently, but his successors raise taxes and import Greek bishops; in 1040 Petar Deljan, claiming Samuil's blood, is proclaimed tsar and sweeps the Balkans — until his own cousin Alusian blinds him at a feast and the rising collapses; in 1072 Skopje's nobles under Gjorgji Vojteh crown Duklja's Konstantin Bodin and die at Kosovo Field.
