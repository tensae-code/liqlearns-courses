# Video script — The First Bulgarian Empire Asparuh Krum Boris and Simeon

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the First Bulgarian Empire Asparuh Krum Boris and Simeon — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Khan Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and in 681 force Byzantium to pay tribute to their new state at Pliska; Krum ambushes and kills Emperor Nikephoros I in 811; Boris I accepts baptism in 864 to bind Bulgars and Slavs into one people, crushes the boyars' pagan revolt and, when his son Vladimir Rasate tries to restore Tangra, returns from his monastery to blind him and crown Simeon — under whom Preslav's golden age makes Bulgaria an empire from the Black Sea to the Adriatic, until John Tzimiskes ends it in 971.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
