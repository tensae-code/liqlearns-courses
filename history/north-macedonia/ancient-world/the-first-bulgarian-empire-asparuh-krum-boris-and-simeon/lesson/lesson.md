# The First Bulgarian Empire Asparuh Krum Boris and Simeon

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Khan Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and in 681 force Byzantium to pay tribute to their new state at Pliska; Krum ambushes and kills Emperor Nikephoros I in 811; Boris I accepts baptism in 864 to bind Bulgars and Slavs into one people, crushes the boyars' pagan revolt and, when his son Vladimir Rasate tries to restore Tangra, returns from his monastery to blind him and crown Simeon — under whom Preslav's golden age makes Bulgaria an empire from the Black Sea to the Adriatic, until John Tzimiskes ends it in 971.
