# The First Bulgarian Empire Asparuh Krum Boris and Simeon — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: the Bulgarian state in the early Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Khan Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and in 681 force Byzantium to pay tribute to their new state at Pliska; Krum ambushes and kills Emperor Nikephoros I in 811; Boris I accepts baptism in 864 to bind Bulgars and Slavs into one people, crushes the boyars' pagan revolt and, when his son Vladimir Rasate tries to restore Tangra, returns from his monastery to blind him and crown Simeon — under whom Preslav's golden age makes Bulgaria an empire from the Black Sea to the Adriatic, until John Tzimiskes ends it in 971.
