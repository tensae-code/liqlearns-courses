# Ancient Civilisations Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 1: the Roman Republic”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** From the wolf-twins' legend and the expelled last king, Rome builds a republic of consuls who can veto each other, a Senate, plebeian tribunes and the written Twelve Tables; the Gracchi die for land reform, Caesar crosses the Rubicon and falls to daggers, and Octavian's victory at Actium ends the republic in all but name.
