**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 1: the Roman Empire and its fall”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Augustus's Principate spreads citizenship and reaches its widest under Trajan; crisis brings Diocletian's Dominate, Theodosius splits the empire in 395, and in 476 Odoacer deposes the last western emperor — while the East lives on for another thousand years.
