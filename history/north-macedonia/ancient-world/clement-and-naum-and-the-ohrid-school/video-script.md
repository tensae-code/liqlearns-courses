# Video script — Clement and Naum and the Ohrid School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of clement and Naum and the Ohrid School — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Welcomed by Boris I and sent to the Kutmichevica region, Clement founds the Ohrid Literary School — called the first Slavic university — teaching some 3,500 pupils letters, farming and crafts, and becomes the first Slavic bishop in 893; Naum succeeds him at the school and builds his monastery on Lake Ohrid's southern shore, where the folk still say his heart beats in his tomb.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
