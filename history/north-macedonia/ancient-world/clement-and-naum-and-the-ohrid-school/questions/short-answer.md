# Clement and Naum and the Ohrid School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: the work of Saints Clement and Naum”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Welcomed by Boris I and sent to the Kutmichevica region, Clement founds the Ohrid Literary School — called the first Slavic university — teaching some 3,500 pupils letters, farming and crafts, and becomes the first Slavic bishop in 893; Naum succeeds him at the school and builds his monastery on Lake Ohrid's southern shore, where the folk still say his heart beats in his tomb.
