# Video script — Raska and Duklja the First Serb States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of raska and Duklja the First Serb States — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Serb tribes organised in župe build two first states — Raška around the fortress of Ras, where Knez Vlastimir unites the districts and holds off Bulgaria; and coastal Duklja, where the love story of captured prince Jovan Vladimir and Samuil's daughter Kosara softens an empire, Stefan Vojislav wins independence, and Mihailo receives a royal crown from the pope in 1077.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
