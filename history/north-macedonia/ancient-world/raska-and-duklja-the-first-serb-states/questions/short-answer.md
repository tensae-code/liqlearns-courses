# Raska and Duklja the First Serb States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: Raška and Duklja”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Serb tribes organised in župe build two first states — Raška around the fortress of Ras, where Knez Vlastimir unites the districts and holds off Bulgaria; and coastal Duklja, where the love story of captured prince Jovan Vladimir and Samuil's daughter Kosara softens an empire, Stefan Vojislav wins independence, and Mihailo receives a royal crown from the pope in 1077.
