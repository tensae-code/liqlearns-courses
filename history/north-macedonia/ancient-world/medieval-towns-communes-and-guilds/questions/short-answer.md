# Medieval Towns Communes and Guilds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 3: medieval towns”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Rome's cities die into natural economy, then from the 11th century crafts and trade revive them — first Venice, Genoa and Marseille, then the Rhine and Danube towns; citizens fight, buy or are granted freedom from their feudal lords, organise communes with their own courts and militias, and the boldest — Genoa, Venice, Dubrovnik, Florence, Pisa — become city-states.
