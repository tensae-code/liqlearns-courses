# The Holy Roman Empire and the Road to Canossa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 3: European monarchies — the Holy Roman Empire”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Otto I, crowned emperor in 962, binds the Church to his crown; two centuries of empire-versus-papacy explode when Gregory VII and Henry IV fight over who appoints bishops, and the emperor stands barefoot in the snow at Canossa begging pardon — yet by the Golden Bull of 1356 the empire is a league of near-independent princes.
