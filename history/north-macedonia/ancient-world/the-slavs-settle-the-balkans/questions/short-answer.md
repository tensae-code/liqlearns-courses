# The Slavs Settle the Balkans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: the settlement of the Slavs in the Balkans”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Pressed by cold winters, hunger and the Huns, the Slavs leave their forest homeland and pour south in dugout boats (monoxyla); plague-emptied Byzantium cannot stop them — they besiege Thessalonica again and again without taking it, organise into self-governing sklaviniai (Dragovites, Berzites, Rinhini, Sagudates, Strumians), worship Perun and Veles, and rename the land itself: Lychnid becomes Ohrid, Axios the Vardar, Scupi Skopje.
