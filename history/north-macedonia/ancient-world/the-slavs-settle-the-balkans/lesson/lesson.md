# The Slavs Settle the Balkans

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Pressed by cold winters, hunger and the Huns, the Slavs leave their forest homeland and pour south in dugout boats (monoxyla); plague-emptied Byzantium cannot stop them — they besiege Thessalonica again and again without taking it, organise into self-governing sklaviniai (Dragovites, Berzites, Rinhini, Sagudates, Strumians), worship Perun and Veles, and rename the land itself: Lychnid becomes Ohrid, Axios the Vardar, Scupi Skopje.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Slavs Settle the Balkans — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Slavs Settle the Balkans. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
