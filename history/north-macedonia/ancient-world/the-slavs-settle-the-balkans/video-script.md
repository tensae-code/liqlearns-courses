# Video script — The Slavs Settle the Balkans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Slavs Settle the Balkans — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Pressed by cold winters, hunger and the Huns, the Slavs leave their forest homeland and pour south in dugout boats (monoxyla); plague-emptied Byzantium cannot stop them — they besiege Thessalonica again and again without taking it, organise into self-governing sklaviniai (Dragovites, Berzites, Rinhini, Sagudates, Strumians), worship Perun and Veles, and rename the land itself: Lychnid becomes Ohrid, Axios the Vardar, Scupi Skopje.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
