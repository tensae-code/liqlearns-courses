# Macedonias Feudal Lords Prosek and Melnik — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: independent feudal rulers in Macedonia in the 13th century”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** As empires falter, strongmen seize rock fortresses — Dobromir Hrs at Prosek above the Vardar, who ambushes Emperor Alexios III's army by night and marries his way to peace; Strez, who holds Prosek after him and is murdered in his tent in 1214 (folk said Saint Sava's prayers killed him); and Alexiy Slav at Melnik, who survives twenty years by marrying into the Latin emperor's family, then switching to Epirus.
