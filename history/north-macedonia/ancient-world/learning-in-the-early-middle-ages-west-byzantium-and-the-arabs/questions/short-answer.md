# Learning in the Early Middle Ages West Byzantium and the Arabs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 1: science and culture in the early Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Three worlds keep learning alive differently — western monks copy manuscripts in scriptoria and Charlemagne's court revives letters; Byzantium's Magnaura school teaches emperors' children, and its engineers wield Greek fire; the Arabs, freer of dogma, found algebra and trigonometry, adopt Indian numerals and zero, print Avicenna's Canon of medicine and carry paper and gunpowder from China toward Europe.
