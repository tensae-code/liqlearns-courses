# Christianity from Persecuted Sect to State Religion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: Judaism and the rise of Christianity”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The Jews keep one God through two destroyed Temples; Jesus's followers are persecuted and martyred, then Constantine's Edict of Milan (313) legalises their faith and Theodosius makes it the empire's religion (380).
