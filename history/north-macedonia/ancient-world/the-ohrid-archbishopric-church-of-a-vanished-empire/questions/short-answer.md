# The Ohrid Archbishopric Church of a Vanished Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 2: the Ohrid Archbishopric”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Conquering Samuil's empire in 1018, Basil II keeps its church alive as an autocephalous archbishopric seated at Ohrid, first under the Slav Jovan of Debar; for centuries it crowns Balkan rulers — Demetrios Chomatenos crowns an Epirote despot emperor — and guards Slavic letters while empires come and go around it.
