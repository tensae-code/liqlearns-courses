# Byzantium after Justinian Heraclius and the Macedonian Renaissance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: reforms and the empire's second rise”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** With Avars at the walls, Heraclius rebuilds the state into soldier-farmer themes and Greek replaces Latin; three centuries later the Macedonian dynasty brings a Byzantine renaissance — Basil I, a peasant from the theme of Macedonia, seizes the throne, and Basil II extends the empire again.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: Byzantium survives and renews itself”?
**Answer:** Heraclius answers siege and collapse with soldier-farmer themes; the empire tears itself over icons, splits from Rome for good in 1054, flowers again under the Macedonian dynasty — then eats its own strength by granting pronoia lands to magnates.
