# Video script — Magna Carta 1215 the King under Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of magna Carta 1215 the King under Law — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Angles and Saxons, Alfred, then William of Normandy's conquest of 1066 make one kingdom; the Plantagenets hold half of France too — until King John's taxes and seizures drive his barons to force the Magna Carta on him in 1215: no free man imprisoned without lawful judgment, justice neither sold nor delayed — and England's first parliament follows.

William wins Hastings in 1066 and makes every lord swear to the king himself; his descendant John squeezes the barons too hard and is forced at Runnymede to seal Magna Carta, and Edward I's Model Parliament of 1295 seats towns and counties beside the lords.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
