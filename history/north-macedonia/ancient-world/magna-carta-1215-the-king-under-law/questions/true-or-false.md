# Magna Carta 1215 the King under Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Angles and Saxons, Alfred, then William of Normandy's conquest of 1066 make one kingdom; the Plantagenets hold half of France too — until King John's taxes and seizures drive his barons to force the Magna Carta on him in 1215: no free man imprisoned without lawful judgment, justice neither sold nor delayed — and England's first parliament follows.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: William wins Hastings in 1066 and makes every lord swear to the king himself; his descendant John squeezes the barons too hard and is forced at Runnymede to sea
