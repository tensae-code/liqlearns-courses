# Magna Carta 1215 the King under Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: medieval England”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Angles and Saxons, Alfred, then William of Normandy's conquest of 1066 make one kingdom; the Plantagenets hold half of France too — until King John's taxes and seizures drive his barons to force the Magna Carta on him in 1215: no free man imprisoned without lawful judgment, justice neither sold nor delayed — and England's first parliament follows.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: England”?
**Answer:** William wins Hastings in 1066 and makes every lord swear to the king himself; his descendant John squeezes the barons too hard and is forced at Runnymede to seal Magna Carta, and Edward I's Model Parliament of 1295 seats towns and counties beside the lords.
