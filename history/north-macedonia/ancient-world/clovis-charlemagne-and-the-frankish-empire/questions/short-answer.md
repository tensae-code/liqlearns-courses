# Clovis Charlemagne and the Frankish Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: the state of the Franks”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Clovis unites the Franks, accepts baptism at Reims and wins the Church for his dynasty; as Merovingian kings fade into "do-nothing" rulers, their mayors of the palace take power — Charles Martel stops the Arabs in 732, Pepin founds the Carolingian line and carves out the Papal State, and Charlemagne, crowned emperor in Rome in 800, builds an empire whose court school at Aachen sparks a Carolingian Renaissance — before Verdun splits it into France, Italy and Germany.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the Franks and the Holy Roman Empire”?
**Answer:** Clovis's baptism wins the Church for the Franks; Charles Martel halts the Arabs, Pepin takes a crown, and Charlemagne's Aachen court sparks a renaissance before Verdun splits his empire three ways — Otto I's imperial coronation in 962 refounds an "empire" that is really a loose league of princes.
