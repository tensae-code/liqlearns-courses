# Hands-on — Clovis Charlemagne and the Frankish Empire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a quick map from memory: mark where this story happens, who moves, and where the turn happens. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the story of clovis Charlemagne and the Frankish Empire — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Arrange the objects as the places and people of the story, move them as the story moves, and say the turn aloud when it comes.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this story's pattern in real life — a road, a market, a border, a new rule, a leader's choice. Note what happened and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
