# Video script — Clovis Charlemagne and the Frankish Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of clovis Charlemagne and the Frankish Empire — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Clovis unites the Franks, accepts baptism at Reims and wins the Church for his dynasty; as Merovingian kings fade into "do-nothing" rulers, their mayors of the palace take power — Charles Martel stops the Arabs in 732, Pepin founds the Carolingian line and carves out the Papal State, and Charlemagne, crowned emperor in Rome in 800, builds an empire whose court school at Aachen sparks a Carolingian Renaissance — before Verdun splits it into France, Italy and Germany.

Clovis's baptism wins the Church for the Franks; Charles Martel halts the Arabs, Pepin takes a crown, and Charlemagne's Aachen court sparks a renaissance before Verdun splits his empire three ways — Otto I's imperial coronation in 962 refounds an "empire" that is really a loose league of princes.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
