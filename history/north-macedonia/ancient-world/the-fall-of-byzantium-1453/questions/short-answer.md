# The Fall of Byzantium 1453 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the twilight and fall of the Byzantine Empire”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Michael VIII Palaiologos wins Constantinople back in 1261, but the restored empire never recovers its trade or strength; civil wars invite the Ottomans in, and by the 1400s Byzantium is a city with an empire's memories — in 1453 Mehmed II's great army breaks the Theodosian walls and the last emperor, Constantine XI Dragash, dies in the defence; its scholars flee west, Moscow claims its mantle, and its fall pushes Europe to seek new sea roads.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the fall of Byzantium and its legacy”?
**Answer:** Manzikert opens Anatolia to the Seljuks, the Fourth Crusade sacks Constantinople itself in 1204, and though Nicaea restores the empire in 1261 it shrinks to a city — Mehmed II takes it in 1453 and the last emperor dies on the walls; what survives is law, letters and the mission of Cyril and Methodius, while Moscow dreams of being the Third Rome.
