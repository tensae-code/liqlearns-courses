# Al Andalus Islamic Spain Cordoba to Granada

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

One Umayyad prince escapes the Abbasid massacre and rebuilds his dynasty in Cordoba, whose caliphate counts seventy libraries and a wary tolerance — when it shatters into taifa kingdoms in 1031, the Reconquista begins its long march to 1492.

Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
