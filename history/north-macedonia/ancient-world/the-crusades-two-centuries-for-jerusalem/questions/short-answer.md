# The Crusades Two Centuries for Jerusalem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the Crusades and Byzantium”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** After the Seljuks take Anatolia and Jerusalem, Urban II's call at Clermont (1095) sends eight crusades east; the first takes Jerusalem in 1099, but the Fourth, hijacked by Venice's Enrico Dandolo, sacks Christian Constantinople in 1204 and plants a Latin Empire — Acre, the last crusader port, falls in 1291, and the real winners are Venice and Genoa.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: the Crusades”?
**Answer:** Eight great expeditions under the popes' banner march to free Jerusalem between 1096 and 1270, mixing faith, land-hunger and profit, massacring Muslims, Jews and Orthodox on the way — and crusaders come home carrying the crafts, foods and irrigation of the Islamic cities they fought.
