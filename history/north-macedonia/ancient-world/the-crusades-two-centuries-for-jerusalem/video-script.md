# Video script — The Crusades Two Centuries for Jerusalem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Crusades Two Centuries for Jerusalem — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

After the Seljuks take Anatolia and Jerusalem, Urban II's call at Clermont (1095) sends eight crusades east; the first takes Jerusalem in 1099, but the Fourth, hijacked by Venice's Enrico Dandolo, sacks Christian Constantinople in 1204 and plants a Latin Empire — Acre, the last crusader port, falls in 1291, and the real winners are Venice and Genoa.

Eight great expeditions under the popes' banner march to free Jerusalem between 1096 and 1270, mixing faith, land-hunger and profit, massacring Muslims, Jews and Orthodox on the way — and crusaders come home carrying the crafts, foods and irrigation of the Islamic cities they fought.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
