# Video script — Balkan Economic and Cultural Life 12th to 15th Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of balkan Economic and Cultural Life 12th to 15th Century — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Peasants work the three-field system, Vlach shepherds drive flocks between mountain and plain, and mines at Zletovo, Sase and Novo Brdo feed the mints; new capitals raise literary schools at Skopje, Tarnovo and Hilandar, chronicles and the Alexander romance circulate, ktitor portraits stare from frescoed walls at Dečani and Marko's Monastery, and folk song begins the making of King Marko's legend.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
