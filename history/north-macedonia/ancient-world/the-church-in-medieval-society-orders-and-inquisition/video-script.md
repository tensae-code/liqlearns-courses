# Video script — The Church in Medieval Society Orders and Inquisition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Church in Medieval Society Orders and Inquisition — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

After 1054 pope and patriarch head rival churches; Innocent III teaches that royal power borrows light from papal power as the moon from the sun; crusading breeds monk-knights — Templars, Hospitallers, Teutons — and preaching friars, Franciscans and Dominicans; against heretics like the Cathars, heirs of Bogomil ideas, the Church answers with crusade and the Inquisition's courts.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
