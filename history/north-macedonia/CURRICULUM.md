# History — North Macedonia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for North Macedonia inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade a book comes from appears only in ABOUT.md and each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The ladder derives from North Macedonia's own books' progression as filed: the placements file's book order is the teaching order — the Grade 7 Middle Ages first (Europe and the Balkans swinging theme by theme, closing under the sultans), then the Grade 8 new age (absolutism and revolution in Europe, then Macedonia's Ottoman-era awakening, Ilinden and the Balkan Wars), then the Grade 10 gymnasium survey, which returns thematically to the foundations, to ideas and power, to a connecting world of wars and freedom struggles, and closes with history in today's world. A lesson sits at the level of its first appearance in that progression; later books' passes of the same lesson deepen it there. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — North Macedonia's own history books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns, and is marked exactly "Tip — from us, not from the books". Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
