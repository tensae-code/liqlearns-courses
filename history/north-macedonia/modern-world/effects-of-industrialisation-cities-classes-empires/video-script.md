# Video script — Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The factory makes goods cheap and lives hard, and the first workers answer by smashing looms.

Twelve-to-sixteen-hour days, women and children cheapest of all, Luddites smashing the machines that ate their trades — then unions, strikes and laws claw back the working day, Marx calls the workers of all countries to unite, and the smoke begins to poison air, water and soil.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
