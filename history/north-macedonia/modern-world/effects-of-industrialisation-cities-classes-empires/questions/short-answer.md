# Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: consequences of, and resistance to, the First Industrial Revolution — hand crafts die, workers resist the machines”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The factory makes goods cheap and lives hard, and the first workers answer by smashing looms.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: what industrialisation did to people”?
**Answer:** Twelve-to-sixteen-hour days, women and children cheapest of all, Luddites smashing the machines that ate their trades — then unions, strikes and laws claw back the working day, Marx calls the workers of all countries to unite, and the smoke begins to poison air, water and soil.
