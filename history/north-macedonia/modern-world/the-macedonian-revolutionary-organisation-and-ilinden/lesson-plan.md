# Lesson plan — The Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden

**Course:** History — North Macedonia · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of the Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Theme 5: Macedonian organisations and societies in emigration — émigrés in Sofia form the Macedonian Committee (1895); Stefan Dedov and Mihail Gerdžikov lead rival currents** — exiles who cannot go home organise the homeland's liberation from abroad, and argue over how
2. **Theme 5: the Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation fights for political autonomy for Macedonia and Adrianople for every Macedonian regardless of nationality, facing down Ottoman power** — teachers found a secret state within the state, with its own courts, post and taxes
3. **Theme 5: the organisation's leading figures — Gotse Delčev and his comrades, who teach peasants to use the organisation's courts instead of Turkish ones** — an apostle-like organiser rides from village to village turning grievance into a network
4. **Theme 5: the Ilinden Uprising (1903) — the Salonika bombings echo worldwide, congresses fix the uprising's rules and districts rise** — on St Elijah's Day a whole people rises at once, knowing the empire's answer will be terrible
5. **Theme 5: the Kruševo Republic — the liberated town organises defence, sanitation and a workshop repairing weapons, and some 1,200 insurgents defend it** — for ten days a mountain town runs itself as a republic of all its peoples before the troops return
