# The Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 5: Macedonian organisations and societies in emigration — émigrés in Sofia form the Macedonian Committee (1895); Stefan Dedov and Mihail Gerdžikov lead rival currents”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Exiles who cannot go home organise the homeland's liberation from abroad, and argue over how.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 5: the Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation fights for political autonomy for Macedonia and Adrianople for every Macedonian regardless of nationality, facing down Ottoman power”?
**Answer:** Teachers found a secret state within the state, with its own courts, post and taxes.
