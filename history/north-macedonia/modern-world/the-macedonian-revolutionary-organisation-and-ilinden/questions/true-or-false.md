# The Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Exiles who cannot go home organise the homeland's liberation from abroad, and argue over how.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Teachers found a secret state within the state, with its own courts, post and taxes.
