# Video script — The Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Exiles who cannot go home organise the homeland's liberation from abroad, and argue over how.

Teachers found a secret state within the state, with its own courts, post and taxes.

An apostle-like organiser rides from village to village turning grievance into a network.

On St Elijah's Day a whole people rises at once, knowing the empire's answer will be terrible.

For ten days a mountain town runs itself as a republic of all its peoples before the troops return.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
