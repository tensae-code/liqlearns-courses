# The Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation and Ilinden

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 5: Macedonian organisations and societies in emigration — émigrés in Sofia form the Macedonian Committee (1895); Stefan Dedov and Mihail Gerdžikov lead rival currents

Exiles who cannot go home organise the homeland's liberation from abroad, and argue over how.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 5: the Macedonian Revolutionary Organisation fights for political autonomy for Macedonia and Adrianople for every Macedonian regardless of nationality, facing down Ottoman power

Teachers found a secret state within the state, with its own courts, post and taxes.

## Sub lesson 3 — Theme 5: the organisation's leading figures — Gotse Delčev and his comrades, who teach peasants to use the organisation's courts instead of Turkish ones

An apostle-like organiser rides from village to village turning grievance into a network.

## Sub lesson 4 — Theme 5: the Ilinden Uprising (1903) — the Salonika bombings echo worldwide, congresses fix the uprising's rules and districts rise

On St Elijah's Day a whole people rises at once, knowing the empire's answer will be terrible.

## Sub lesson 5 — Theme 5: the Kruševo Republic — the liberated town organises defence, sanitation and a workshop repairing weapons, and some 1,200 insurgents defend it

For ten days a mountain town runs itself as a republic of all its peoples before the troops return.
