# Video script — The Second World War the Pacific and the Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Second World War the Pacific and the Results — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Pearl Harbor (7 December 1941) brings America in; after Midway the US hops island by island toward Japan, and chooses the new weapon — Hiroshima on 6 August, Nagasaki on 9 August 1945 — over an invasion's feared millions; Japan capitulates on 2 September, leaving sixty million dead worldwide and the Nuremberg judges inventing the law to try the guilty.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
