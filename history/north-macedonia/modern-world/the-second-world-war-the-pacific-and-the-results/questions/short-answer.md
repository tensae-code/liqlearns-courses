# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: the Pacific, the atomic bombs and the reckoning”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Pearl Harbor (7 December 1941) brings America in; after Midway the US hops island by island toward Japan, and chooses the new weapon — Hiroshima on 6 August, Nagasaki on 9 August 1945 — over an invasion's feared millions; Japan capitulates on 2 September, leaving sixty million dead worldwide and the Nuremberg judges inventing the law to try the guilty.
