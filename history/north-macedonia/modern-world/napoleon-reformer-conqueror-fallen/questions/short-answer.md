# Napoleon Reformer Conqueror Fallen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: Napoleon's wars and the Congress of Vienna — his rise, conquests redrawing Europe's map, the Continental Blockade, and the 1814 settlement after his exile”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** One general's armies carry revolution across Europe, and the victors at Vienna try to put the old world back.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 4: the Napoleonic Wars”?
**Answer:** The artillery officer who beat Austria in Italy and chased glory to Egypt (where his men find the Rosetta Stone) crowns himself emperor in 1804 — Austerlitz, Jena and Friedland humble three empires, Trafalgar bars him from Britain, his Continental Blockade leaks everywhere, and his Code carries property rights and equality before the law across Europe on French bayonets.
