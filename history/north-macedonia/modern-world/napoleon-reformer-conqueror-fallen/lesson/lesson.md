# Napoleon Reformer Conqueror Fallen

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 1: Napoleon's wars and the Congress of Vienna — his rise, conquests redrawing Europe's map, the Continental Blockade, and the 1814 settlement after his exile

One general's armies carry revolution across Europe, and the victors at Vienna try to put the old world back.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 4: the Napoleonic Wars

The artillery officer who beat Austria in Italy and chased glory to Egypt (where his men find the Rosetta Stone) crowns himself emperor in 1804 — Austerlitz, Jena and Friedland humble three empires, Trafalgar bars him from Britain, his Continental Blockade leaks everywhere, and his Code carries property rights and equality before the law across Europe on French bayonets.

## Sub lesson 3 — Theme 4: Russia, Waterloo and Vienna

600,000 men march into Russia and fewer than 100,000 come back through the snow; Leipzig and Waterloo finish him, St Helena keeps him, and the Congress of Vienna restores the old dynasties and redraws the map — planting, in its ignored nations, the nationalism that will dominate the century.
