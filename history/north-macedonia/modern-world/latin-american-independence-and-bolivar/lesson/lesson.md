# Latin American Independence and Bolivar

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Bolívar welds Gran Colombia, San Martín crosses the Andes for Argentina, Chile and Peru, Mexico rises in 1810 and Brazil slips free of Portugal in 1822 — but the dream of one united Latin America shatters into quarrelling republics, and President Monroe warns Europe to keep its hands off the hemisphere.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Latin American Independence and Bolivar. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
