# Lesson plan — Absolutism in Europe Louis Xiv and Peter the Great

**Course:** History — North Macedonia · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Absolutism in Europe Louis Xiv and Peter the Great
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of absolutism in Europe Louis Xiv and Peter the Great — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Theme 1: absolutism in early-modern Europe — Versailles court life, and Peter the Great's reforms building a modern army, fleet and St Petersburg as Russia's window on Europe** — kings gather all power into one palace, and Peter drags Russia toward Europe by decree
2. **Theme 2: absolutism** — Bossuet preaches that the throne is God's own; Louis XIV, the Sun King, cages his nobles in Versailles' golden routines and calls himself the state, while Peter the Great shaves his boyars' beards, taxes the rest, and builds St Petersburg as Russia's window on the West

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
