# Absolutism in Europe Louis Xiv and Peter the Great

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 1: absolutism in early-modern Europe — Versailles court life, and Peter the Great's reforms building a modern army, fleet and St Petersburg as Russia's window on Europe

Kings gather all power into one palace, and Peter drags Russia toward Europe by decree.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2: absolutism

Bossuet preaches that the throne is God's own; Louis XIV, the Sun King, cages his nobles in Versailles' golden routines and calls himself the state, while Peter the Great shaves his boyars' beards, taxes the rest, and builds St Petersburg as Russia's window on the West.
