# Video script — Absolutism in Europe Louis Xiv and Peter the Great

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of absolutism in Europe Louis Xiv and Peter the Great — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Kings gather all power into one palace, and Peter drags Russia toward Europe by decree.

Bossuet preaches that the throne is God's own; Louis XIV, the Sun King, cages his nobles in Versailles' golden routines and calls himself the state, while Peter the Great shaves his boyars' beards, taxes the rest, and builds St Petersburg as Russia's window on the West.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
