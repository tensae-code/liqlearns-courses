# Absolutism in Europe Louis Xiv and Peter the Great — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: absolutism in early-modern Europe — Versailles court life, and Peter the Great's reforms building a modern army, fleet and St Petersburg as Russia's window on Europe”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Kings gather all power into one palace, and Peter drags Russia toward Europe by decree.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: absolutism”?
**Answer:** Bossuet preaches that the throne is God's own; Louis XIV, the Sun King, cages his nobles in Versailles' golden routines and calls himself the state, while Peter the Great shaves his boyars' beards, taxes the rest, and builds St Petersburg as Russia's window on the West.
