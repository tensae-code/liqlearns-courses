# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: the creation of the United States — Britain taxes its colonies after costly wars, Jefferson drafts the Declaration that all men are created equal, independence is won and a constitution follows”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Colonies taxed without a voice declare themselves a new nation and write its rules.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the American War of Independence”?
**Answer:** Taxed without a seat in Parliament, Boston's Sons of Liberty dump the tea in the harbour disguised as Native Americans; London closes the port, the colonies meet at Philadelphia, and the first shots at Lexington and Concord turn a tax revolt into a war.
