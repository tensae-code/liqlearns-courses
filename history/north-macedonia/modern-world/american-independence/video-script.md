# Video script — American Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of american Independence — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Colonies taxed without a voice declare themselves a new nation and write its rules.

Taxed without a seat in Parliament, Boston's Sons of Liberty dump the tea in the harbour disguised as Native Americans; London closes the port, the colonies meet at Philadelphia, and the first shots at Lexington and Concord turn a tax revolt into a war.

Jefferson writes Locke into the Declaration of 1776; Saratoga brings France in, Yorktown ends it, and the Constitution of 1787 builds Montesquieu's three powers with checks and balances, crowned by a Bill of Rights — a freedom that still leaves slavery standing and women, like Molly Pitcher who served the guns at Monmouth, without the vote.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
