# Germany Unifies Bismarck Blood and Iron — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: Germany's unification led by Prussia, with the Prussian leadership directing events”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Where 1848's talkers failed, Prussia's armies and diplomacy weld the German states into an empire.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 4: Germany unifies”?
**Answer:** Bismarck's Realpolitik — interests and power before ideals — beats Austria in 1866, humbles France in 1870, and has Wilhelm I proclaimed Kaiser in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles itself.
