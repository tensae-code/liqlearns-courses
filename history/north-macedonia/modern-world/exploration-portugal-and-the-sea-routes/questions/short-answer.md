# Exploration Portugal and the Sea Routes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the sea road to India and the first circumnavigation”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Dias rounds the Cape of Good Hope, Vasco da Gama reaches India in 1498, and Magellan's five ships set out in 1519 — he dies in the Philippines, but the Victoria returns in 1522 with 18 men, proving the Earth round.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: Portugal leads”?
**Answer:** Dias rounds Africa's southern tip in 1488 and da Gama drops anchor in India in 1498 — the sea road is open, and Lisbon grows rich on pepper.
