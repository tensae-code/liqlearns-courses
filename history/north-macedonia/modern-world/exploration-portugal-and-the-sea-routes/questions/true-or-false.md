# Exploration Portugal and the Sea Routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dias rounds the Cape of Good Hope, Vasco da Gama reaches India in 1498, and Magellan's five ships set out in 1519 — he dies in the Philippines, but the Victoria returns in 1522 with 18 men, proving the Earth round.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Dias rounds Africa's southern tip in 1488 and da Gama drops anchor in India in 1498 — the sea road is open, and Lisbon grows rich on pepper.
