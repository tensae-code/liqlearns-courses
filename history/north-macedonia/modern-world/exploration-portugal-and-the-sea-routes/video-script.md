# Video script — Exploration Portugal and the Sea Routes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of exploration Portugal and the Sea Routes — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Dias rounds the Cape of Good Hope, Vasco da Gama reaches India in 1498, and Magellan's five ships set out in 1519 — he dies in the Philippines, but the Victoria returns in 1522 with 18 men, proving the Earth round.

Dias rounds Africa's southern tip in 1488 and da Gama drops anchor in India in 1498 — the sea road is open, and Lisbon grows rich on pepper.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
