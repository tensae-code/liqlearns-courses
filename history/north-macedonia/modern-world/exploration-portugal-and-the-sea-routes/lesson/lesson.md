# Exploration Portugal and the Sea Routes

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: the sea road to India and the first circumnavigation

Dias rounds the Cape of Good Hope, Vasco da Gama reaches India in 1498, and Magellan's five ships set out in 1519 — he dies in the Philippines, but the Victoria returns in 1522 with 18 men, proving the Earth round.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 3: Portugal leads

Dias rounds Africa's southern tip in 1488 and da Gama drops anchor in India in 1498 — the sea road is open, and Lisbon grows rich on pepper.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of exploration Portugal and the Sea Routes — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Exploration Portugal and the Sea Routes. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
