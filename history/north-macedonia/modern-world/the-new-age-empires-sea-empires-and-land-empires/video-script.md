# Video script — The New Age Empires Sea Empires and Land Empires

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the New Age Empires Sea Empires and Land Empires — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Gunpowder and ocean fleets replace feudal patchworks with centralised empires — Spain rich on American silver, Britain and France racing for colonies, Russia walking to the Pacific, the Habsburgs and Ottomans ruling many peoples at once; East India Companies govern like states, mercantilism counts gold in and goods out, and a cold table records which empires kept slaves, and where.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
