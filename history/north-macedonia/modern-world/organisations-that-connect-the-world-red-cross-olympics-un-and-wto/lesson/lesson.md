# Organisations That Connect the World Red Cross Olympics Un and Wto

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

A knitted world needs referees — the Red Cross (1863) for war's wounded, the revived Olympics (1896) for peaceful rivalry, then the UN, UNESCO, UNICEF and the WTO for a planet that trades and argues in minutes.
