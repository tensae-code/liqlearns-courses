# The Industrial Revolution its Two Stages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the Second Industrial Revolution — electricity, steel, the telegraph and lit streets change safety, work and evenings”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Steel and electric light remake the city, and night itself becomes usable time.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: the second industrial revolution”?
**Answer:** Electricity and oil succeed steam — Tesla and Edison fight the War of the Currents, Stephenson's Rocket opens the railway age, the telegraph and telephone shrink the world, and Ford's assembly line puts a Model T within a worker's reach.
