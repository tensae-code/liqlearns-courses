# The Industrial Revolution its Two Stages

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: the Second Industrial Revolution — electricity, steel, the telegraph and lit streets change safety, work and evenings

Steel and electric light remake the city, and night itself becomes usable time.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 3: the second industrial revolution

Electricity and oil succeed steam — Tesla and Edison fight the War of the Currents, Stephenson's Rocket opens the railway age, the telegraph and telephone shrink the world, and Ford's assembly line puts a Model T within a worker's reach.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Industrial Revolution its Two Stages — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Industrial Revolution its Two Stages. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
