# Macedonia after Ilinden Reforms and the Young Turks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 5: Macedonia after the Ilinden Uprising — reforms to resettle the displaced, Dame Gruev's congress plans future work, and neighbouring states send their own četas in”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** A crushed uprising brings European reform officers, and with them the neighbours' armed bands.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 5: Macedonia in the Young Turk coup and regime — Macedonia is the centre of the 1908 coup, pluralism blooms, then the regime hardens after the counter-coup attempt”?
**Answer:** Macedonian revolutionaries help restore the constitution, then watch the Young Turks turn nationalist.
