# Macedonia after Ilinden Reforms and the Young Turks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A crushed uprising brings European reform officers, and with them the neighbours' armed bands.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Macedonian revolutionaries help restore the constitution, then watch the Young Turks turn nationalist.
