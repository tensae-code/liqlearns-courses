# The Eastern Question Nationalism in the Balkans

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 2: the European powers and the Balkans in the 19th century — Napoleon in the western Balkans, the sultan forced to treat with Muhammad Ali of Egypt, Austria-Hungary's Benjamin Kállay

The sick man's neighbours circle, each great power shopping for its share of the Balkans.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 4: the Eastern Question

A weakening Ottoman Empire becomes the Great Powers' chessboard — the Crimean War, then the Eastern Crisis of 1875–78, each round fought in the name of Balkan Christians and settled in London, Paris and Berlin drawing rooms.
