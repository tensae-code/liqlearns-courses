# The Eastern Question Nationalism in the Balkans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The sick man's neighbours circle, each great power shopping for its share of the Balkans.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A weakening Ottoman Empire becomes the Great Powers' chessboard — the Crimean War, then the Eastern Crisis of 1875–78, each round fought in the name of Balkan C
