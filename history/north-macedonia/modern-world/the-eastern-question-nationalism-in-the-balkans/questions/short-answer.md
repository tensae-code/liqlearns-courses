# The Eastern Question Nationalism in the Balkans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 2: the European powers and the Balkans in the 19th century — Napoleon in the western Balkans, the sultan forced to treat with Muhammad Ali of Egypt, Austria-Hungary's Benjamin Kállay”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The sick man's neighbours circle, each great power shopping for its share of the Balkans.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 4: the Eastern Question”?
**Answer:** A weakening Ottoman Empire becomes the Great Powers' chessboard — the Crimean War, then the Eastern Crisis of 1875–78, each round fought in the name of Balkan Christians and settled in London, Paris and Berlin drawing rooms.
