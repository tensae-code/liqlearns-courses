# Universities and Chivalric Culture of Medieval Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 3: culture and science — the first universities and chivalric culture”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Towns need educated men, so Bologna opens Europe's first university in the late 11th century, Barbarossa's charter of 1158 frees scholars from every other power, and Paris, Oxford and Cambridge follow — 80 universities by 1500; beside them, knighthood sings itself in the Song of Roland, the Nibelungenlied, Arthur's legends and the troubadours' love poetry.
