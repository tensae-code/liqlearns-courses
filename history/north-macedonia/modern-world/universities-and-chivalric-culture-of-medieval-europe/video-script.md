# Video script — Universities and Chivalric Culture of Medieval Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of universities and Chivalric Culture of Medieval Europe — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Towns need educated men, so Bologna opens Europe's first university in the late 11th century, Barbarossa's charter of 1158 frees scholars from every other power, and Paris, Oxford and Cambridge follow — 80 universities by 1500; beside them, knighthood sings itself in the Song of Roland, the Nibelungenlied, Arthur's legends and the troubadours' love poetry.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
