# China the Opium Wars and Forced Opening — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: China and the Opium Wars”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Britain pays for tea with opium until millions are addicted; Commissioner Lin Zexu writes to Queen Victoria that a drug banned at home cannot honestly be sold abroad — China bans the trade, loses two wars, and signs the unequal treaties.
