# Macedonian Uprisings Razlovtsi and Kresna — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A teacher turns rebel, raises a village, and pays for it in ash.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: As the great powers redraw the map at Berlin, Macedonians rise to say they are a people too.
