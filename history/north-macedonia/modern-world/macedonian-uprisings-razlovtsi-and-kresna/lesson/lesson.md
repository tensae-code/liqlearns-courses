# Macedonian Uprisings Razlovtsi and Kresna

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 2: the Razlovtsi Uprising (1876) — Dimitar Berovski returns to Maleševo with a small četa, Razlovtsi is twice burned and looted in reprisal

A teacher turns rebel, raises a village, and pays for it in ash.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of macedonian Uprisings Razlovtsi and Kresna — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Macedonian Uprisings Razlovtsi and Kresna. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
