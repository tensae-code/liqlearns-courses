# India the Sepoy Uprising of 1857 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: India under the Company, and the Sepoy rising”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The East India Company forces peasants to plant cotton and indigo instead of food and ruins India's crafts; when a rumour says the new cartridges are greased with pig and cow fat, Hindu and Muslim sepoys rise together — a year of the bloodiest anti-colonial revolt of the century ends Company rule and puts India under the Crown.
