# Video script — The Renaissance What It Was

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Renaissance What It Was — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Rich Italian cities breed citizens who reject church dogma and put man at the centre; humanists revive antiquity, and the "rebirth" spreads from Florence, Rome, Venice and Milan across Europe between the 14th and early 17th centuries.

Rich Florentine bankers pay men to think like Romans again — Petrarch fathers humanism, and the studia humanitatis (grammar, rhetoric, history, poetry, ethics) challenge the monkish schoolroom.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
