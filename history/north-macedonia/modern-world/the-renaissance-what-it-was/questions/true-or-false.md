# The Renaissance What It Was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rich Italian cities breed citizens who reject church dogma and put man at the centre; humanists revive antiquity, and the "rebirth" spreads from Florence, Rome, Venice and Milan across Europe between the 14th and early 17th centuries.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Rich Florentine bankers pay men to think like Romans again — Petrarch fathers humanism, and the studia humanitatis (grammar, rhetoric, history, poetry, ethics)
