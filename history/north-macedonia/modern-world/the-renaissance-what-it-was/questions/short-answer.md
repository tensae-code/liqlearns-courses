# The Renaissance What It Was — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: Humanism and the Renaissance”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Rich Italian cities breed citizens who reject church dogma and put man at the centre; humanists revive antiquity, and the "rebirth" spreads from Florence, Rome, Venice and Milan across Europe between the 14th and early 17th centuries.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: humanism and the Renaissance”?
**Answer:** Rich Florentine bankers pay men to think like Romans again — Petrarch fathers humanism, and the studia humanitatis (grammar, rhetoric, history, poetry, ethics) challenge the monkish schoolroom.
