# 1917 Twice February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: revolutions in Russia”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Petrograd's women textile workers march for bread and peace on 8 March 1917 and the tsar falls within days; the Provisional Government shares power uneasily with the Soviet until Lenin's Bolsheviks, promising peace, land and factories, storm the Winter Palace in October.
