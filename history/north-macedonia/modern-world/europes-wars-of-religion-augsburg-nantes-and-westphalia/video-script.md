# Video script — Europes Wars of Religion Augsburg Nantes and Westphalia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of europes Wars of Religion Augsburg Nantes and Westphalia — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Charles V cannot crush the Protestant princes, so Augsburg lets each ruler choose his land's faith — "cuius regio, eius religio"; France's wars end with Henry IV's Edict of Nantes; and the Thirty Years War (1618–1648) burns Germany until Westphalia lets Protestants worship freely.

Charles V cannot shoot Europe back into one faith — Augsburg settles for "whose land, his religion", France massacres its Huguenots on St Bartholomew's Day yet later shelters them at Nantes, and only after the Thirty Years' War does Westphalia (1648) let every state choose its own faith.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
