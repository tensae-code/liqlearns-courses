# Europes Wars of Religion Augsburg Nantes and Westphalia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: religious conflicts in Europe”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Charles V cannot crush the Protestant princes, so Augsburg lets each ruler choose his land's faith — "cuius regio, eius religio"; France's wars end with Henry IV's Edict of Nantes; and the Thirty Years War (1618–1648) burns Germany until Westphalia lets Protestants worship freely.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: Europe's wars of religion”?
**Answer:** Charles V cannot shoot Europe back into one faith — Augsburg settles for "whose land, his religion", France massacres its Huguenots on St Bartholomew's Day yet later shelters them at Nantes, and only after the Thirty Years' War does Westphalia (1648) let every state choose its own faith.
