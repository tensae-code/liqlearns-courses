# The Second World War Course in Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: the war in Europe”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Blitzkrieg eats Poland, Denmark, Norway and France in months; Britain survives its bombing and the invasion is called off; Barbarossa drives to Moscow's suburbs, Stalingrad turns the tide, El Alamein clears Africa, partisans and resistance movements bleed the occupiers, and on 6 June 1944 the Allies storm Normandy — a year later the Red Army takes Berlin and Hitler kills himself in its ruins.
