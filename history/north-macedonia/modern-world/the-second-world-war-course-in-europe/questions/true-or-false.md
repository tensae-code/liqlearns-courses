# The Second World War Course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Blitzkrieg eats Poland, Denmark, Norway and France in months; Britain survives its bombing and the invasion is called off; Barbarossa drives to Moscow's suburbs, Stalingrad turns the tide, El Alamein clears Africa, partisans and resistance movements bleed the occupiers, and on 6 June 1944 the Allies storm Normandy — a year later the Red Army takes Berlin and Hitler kills himself in its ruins.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Blitzkrieg eats Poland, Denmark, Norway and France in months; Britain survives its bombing and the invasion is called off; Barbarossa drives to Moscow's suburbs
