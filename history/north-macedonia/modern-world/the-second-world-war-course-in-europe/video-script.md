# Video script — The Second World War Course in Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Second World War Course in Europe — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Blitzkrieg eats Poland, Denmark, Norway and France in months; Britain survives its bombing and the invasion is called off; Barbarossa drives to Moscow's suburbs, Stalingrad turns the tide, El Alamein clears Africa, partisans and resistance movements bleed the occupiers, and on 6 June 1944 the Allies storm Normandy — a year later the Red Army takes Berlin and Hitler kills himself in its ruins.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
