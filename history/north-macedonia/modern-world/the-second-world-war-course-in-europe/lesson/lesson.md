# The Second World War Course in Europe

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Blitzkrieg eats Poland, Denmark, Norway and France in months; Britain survives its bombing and the invasion is called off; Barbarossa drives to Moscow's suburbs, Stalingrad turns the tide, El Alamein clears Africa, partisans and resistance movements bleed the occupiers, and on 6 June 1944 the Allies storm Normandy — a year later the Red Army takes Berlin and Hitler kills himself in its ruins.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Second World War Course in Europe — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Second World War Course in Europe. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
