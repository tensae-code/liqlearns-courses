# The Balkan Wars and the End of Ottoman Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 4: the Ottoman Empire to the Bucharest Peace Treaty — Abdülhamid II, the restored parliament, and the four Balkan states (Serbia, Greece, Bulgaria, Montenegro) preparing joint attack”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Four small kingdoms stop fighting each other long enough to throw the empire out of Europe.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 5: Macedonia in the Balkan Wars — Macedonian volunteers like vojvoda Petar Čaulev take towns, the Second Balkan War divides the land at Bucharest”?
**Answer:** Macedonians fight for liberation and are rewarded with partition among three kingdoms.
