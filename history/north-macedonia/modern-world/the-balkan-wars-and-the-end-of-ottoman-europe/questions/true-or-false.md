# The Balkan Wars and the End of Ottoman Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Four small kingdoms stop fighting each other long enough to throw the empire out of Europe.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Macedonians fight for liberation and are rewarded with partition among three kingdoms.
