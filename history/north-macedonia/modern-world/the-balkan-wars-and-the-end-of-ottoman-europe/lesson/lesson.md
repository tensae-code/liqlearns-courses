# The Balkan Wars and the End of Ottoman Europe

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 4: the Ottoman Empire to the Bucharest Peace Treaty — Abdülhamid II, the restored parliament, and the four Balkan states (Serbia, Greece, Bulgaria, Montenegro) preparing joint attack

Four small kingdoms stop fighting each other long enough to throw the empire out of Europe.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 5: Macedonia in the Balkan Wars — Macedonian volunteers like vojvoda Petar Čaulev take towns, the Second Balkan War divides the land at Bucharest

Macedonians fight for liberation and are rewarded with partition among three kingdoms.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Balkan Wars and the End of Ottoman Europe — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Balkan Wars and the End of Ottoman Europe. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
