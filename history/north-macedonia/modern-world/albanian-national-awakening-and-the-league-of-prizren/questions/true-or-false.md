# Albanian National Awakening and the League of Prizren — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Before Albanians have a state, teachers give them an alphabet and books of their own.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Faced with partition among neighbours, Albanian beys and teachers form a league to keep Albanian lands together.
