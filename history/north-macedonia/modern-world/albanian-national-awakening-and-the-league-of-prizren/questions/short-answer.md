# Albanian National Awakening and the League of Prizren — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 2: Albania from the early 19th century to the Great Eastern Crisis, and the Albanian enlightenment — primers and translations teach children to read Albanian as the empire crumbles”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Before Albanians have a state, teachers give them an alphabet and books of their own.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the League of Prizren (1878) and the rise of the Albanian liberation movement — Abdyl Frashëri and the League demand one united Albanian vilayet with wide autonomy and plead the Albanian case in Europe's capitals”?
**Answer:** Faced with partition among neighbours, Albanian beys and teachers form a league to keep Albanian lands together.
