# The Colonisation of the Americas Conquistadors Disease and Slavery

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: the colonisation of the New World

Conquistadors overthrow the Maya, Aztec and Inca worlds for gold and silver; European diseases finish what swords began; labour hunger drags enslaved Africans across the Atlantic, while potatoes, maize and tobacco travel the other way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 3: the conquest of the New World

Cortés and Pizarro topple the Aztec and Inca empires, but the deadliest conquerors are smallpox, measles and flu, which empty whole peoples; Europe answers its labour hunger by shipping millions of enslaved Africans across the Atlantic triangle.
