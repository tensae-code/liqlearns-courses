# The Colonisation of the Americas Conquistadors Disease and Slavery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the colonisation of the New World”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Conquistadors overthrow the Maya, Aztec and Inca worlds for gold and silver; European diseases finish what swords began; labour hunger drags enslaved Africans across the Atlantic, while potatoes, maize and tobacco travel the other way.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: the conquest of the New World”?
**Answer:** Cortés and Pizarro topple the Aztec and Inca empires, but the deadliest conquerors are smallpox, measles and flu, which empty whole peoples; Europe answers its labour hunger by shipping millions of enslaved Africans across the Atlantic triangle.
