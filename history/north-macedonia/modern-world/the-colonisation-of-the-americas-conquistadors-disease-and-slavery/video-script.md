# Video script — The Colonisation of the Americas Conquistadors Disease and Slavery

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Colonisation of the Americas Conquistadors Disease and Slavery — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Conquistadors overthrow the Maya, Aztec and Inca worlds for gold and silver; European diseases finish what swords began; labour hunger drags enslaved Africans across the Atlantic, while potatoes, maize and tobacco travel the other way.

Cortés and Pizarro topple the Aztec and Inca empires, but the deadliest conquerors are smallpox, measles and flu, which empty whole peoples; Europe answers its labour hunger by shipping millions of enslaved Africans across the Atlantic triangle.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
