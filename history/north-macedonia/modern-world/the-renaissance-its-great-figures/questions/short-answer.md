# The Renaissance its Great Figures — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the great figures”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Dante writes the Divine Comedy in the people's Italian, Petrarch and Boccaccio follow; Leonardo paints the Mona Lisa, Michelangelo the Sistine frescoes and David, Raphael and Titian complete the school; Shakespeare and Cervantes carry it north and west — all floated on patrons like Florence's Medici.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the great figures”?
**Answer:** Leonardo draws flying machines and paints the Mona Lisa, Michelangelo frees David from the marble and covers the Sistine ceiling, Raphael fills the School of Athens — and the new spirit travels north to Shakespeare's stage, Cervantes's knight and Erasmus's mocking pen.
