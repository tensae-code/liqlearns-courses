# 1848 the Spring of Peoples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: the Spring of Nations, 1848”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Revolution blooms in Paris, Vienna, Berlin and the Italian states in a single spring — constitutions demanded here, feudal dues abolished there, nations claimed everywhere; the empires shoot it down, but absolutism never sits as safely again.
