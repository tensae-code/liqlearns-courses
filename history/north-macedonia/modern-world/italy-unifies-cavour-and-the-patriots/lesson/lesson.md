# Italy Unifies Cavour and the Patriots

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Mazzini preaches, Cavour manoeuvres Piedmont into war beside France, Garibaldi's thousand Redshirts liberate the south in a summer — the Kingdom of Italy is proclaimed in 1861, Venetia follows, and Rome, taken in 1870, becomes the capital.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Italy Unifies Cavour and the Patriots. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
