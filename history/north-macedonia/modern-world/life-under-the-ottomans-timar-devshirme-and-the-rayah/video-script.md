# Video script — Life under the Ottomans Timar Devshirme and the Rayah

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of life under the Ottomans Timar Devshirme and the Rayah — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The conquerors call the land Rumelia and its Christians Rumi; society divides into asker and rayah under the timar system, taxed by tithe and the jizya head-tax; Anatolian settlers and Yürük herdsmen colonise the plains, Sephardic Jews make Thessalonica a "mother of Israel", Islam spreads — by choice, advantage and the devshirme's force — and Kratovo's mint, new crops (cotton, rice, tobacco, maize) and the great Doljani fair remake daily life around the sofra table.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
