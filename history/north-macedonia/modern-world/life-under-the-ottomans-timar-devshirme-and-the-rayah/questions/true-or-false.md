# Life under the Ottomans Timar Devshirme and the Rayah — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The conquerors call the land Rumelia and its Christians Rumi; society divides into asker and rayah under the timar system, taxed by tithe and the jizya head-tax; Anatolian settlers and Yürük herdsmen colonise the plains, Sephardic Jews make Thessalonica a "mother of Israel", Islam spreads — by choice, advantage and the devshirme's force — and Kratovo's mint, new crops (cotton, rice, tobacco, maize) and the great Doljani fair remake daily life around the sofra table.
