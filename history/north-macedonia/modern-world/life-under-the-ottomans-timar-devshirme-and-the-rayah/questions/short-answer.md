# Life under the Ottomans Timar Devshirme and the Rayah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 5: life in the Balkans and in Macedonia under Ottoman rule”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The conquerors call the land Rumelia and its Christians Rumi; society divides into asker and rayah under the timar system, taxed by tithe and the jizya head-tax; Anatolian settlers and Yürük herdsmen colonise the plains, Sephardic Jews make Thessalonica a "mother of Israel", Islam spreads — by choice, advantage and the devshirme's force — and Kratovo's mint, new crops (cotton, rice, tobacco, maize) and the great Doljani fair remake daily life around the sofra table.
