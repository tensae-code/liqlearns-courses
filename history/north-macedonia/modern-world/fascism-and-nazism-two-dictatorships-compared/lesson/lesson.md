# Fascism and Nazism Two Dictatorships Compared

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Mussolini's March on Rome (1922) makes the state everything and the Duce its face; Hitler, Chancellor in 1933 and Führer within a year, builds on antisemitism, the myth of an Aryan master race and a hunger for Lebensraum in the east.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of fascism and Nazism Two Dictatorships Compared — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Fascism and Nazism Two Dictatorships Compared. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
