# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the rise of science, technology and thought”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Gutenberg prints the Bible in 1455 and ideas outrun censors; Copernicus moves the Earth and Galileo's telescope confirms him — both persecuted by the Church; Machiavelli writes The Prince as a handbook of power, and Francis Bacon founds knowledge on observation and experiment, the road to Hobbes, Locke, Kant and Newton.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 1: the Enlightenment and enlightened absolutism — thinkers attack unchecked rule, demand human rights, and rulers like Frederick II of Prussia pose as reforming monarchs”?
**Answer:** Reason is turned against absolute kings, and some kings try to rule with reason before it rules them out.
