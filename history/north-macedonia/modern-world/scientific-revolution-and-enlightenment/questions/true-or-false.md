# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gutenberg prints the Bible in 1455 and ideas outrun censors; Copernicus moves the Earth and Galileo's telescope confirms him — both persecuted by the Church; Machiavelli writes The Prince as a handbook of power, and Francis Bacon founds knowledge on observation and experiment, the road to Hobbes, Locke, Kant and Newton.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Reason is turned against absolute kings, and some kings try to rule with reason before it rules them out.
