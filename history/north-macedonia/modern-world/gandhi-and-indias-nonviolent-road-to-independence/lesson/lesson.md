# Gandhi and Indias Nonviolent Road to Independence

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Gandhi answers an empire with marches, boycotts and disobedience — the Salt March of 1930 walks 380 kilometres against the salt monopoly — and in 1947 India wins freedom, partitioned at birth into India and Pakistan.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of gandhi and Indias Nonviolent Road to Independence — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Gandhi and Indias Nonviolent Road to Independence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
