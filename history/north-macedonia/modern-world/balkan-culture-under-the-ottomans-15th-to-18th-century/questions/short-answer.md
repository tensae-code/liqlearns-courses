# Balkan Culture under the Ottomans 15th to 18th Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 5: culture and cultural heritage of the Balkans and Macedonia, 15th–18th centuries”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Monasteries at Lesnovo, Treskavec and Slepče keep Slavic letters alive in scriptoria; damaskini retell saints' lives in near-vernacular; printing happens abroad — Jakov of Kamena Reka in Venice, Žefarović's Stemmatographia (Vienna 1741) with its Macedonian coat of arms, Buzuku's Albanian Meshari (1555); cell schools and medreses (Skopje's Isa-beg) teach the two faiths; hidden cave churches ring Lake Ohrid while Ottoman towns take their lasting shape — čaršija and mahala, türbe, hamam, han and bezisten.
