# The League of Prizren and the Albanian National Movement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: the League of Prizren”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** When Berlin starts handing Albanian-inhabited lands to the neighbours, Albanian leaders gather at Prizren in June 1878 to stop the partition — at first backed by the sultan, dissolved by his soldiers in 1881 when it dares demand autonomy; the road from Prizren leads to the Albanian state of 1912.
