# The League of Prizren and the Albanian National Movement

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

When Berlin starts handing Albanian-inhabited lands to the neighbours, Albanian leaders gather at Prizren in June 1878 to stop the partition — at first backed by the sultan, dissolved by his soldiers in 1881 when it dares demand autonomy; the road from Prizren leads to the Albanian state of 1912.

## Say it to your companion

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The League of Prizren and the Albanian National Movement. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
