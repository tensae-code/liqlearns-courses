# Karposhs Uprising and the Great Turkish War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 5: the Karposh uprising”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The Great Turkish War (1683–1699) breaks Ottoman power at Vienna; Austria's Piccolomini storms down to Skopje in 1689, finds it plague-ridden and burns it for two days; in the vacuum the hajduk leader Karposh — once the Ottomans' own hajduk-hunter — raises north-eastern Macedonia, holds a free territory around Kriva Palanka, Kumanovo and Skopje for six weeks as a buffer both empires court (Leopold I writes to the "Macedonian people"), then falls at Kumanovo; the Ottomans kill him at Skopje's Stone Bridge, and thousands of Christians emigrate north with the retreating Austrians.
