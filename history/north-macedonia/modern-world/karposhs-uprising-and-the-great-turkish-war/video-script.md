# Video script — Karposhs Uprising and the Great Turkish War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of karposhs Uprising and the Great Turkish War — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The Great Turkish War (1683–1699) breaks Ottoman power at Vienna; Austria's Piccolomini storms down to Skopje in 1689, finds it plague-ridden and burns it for two days; in the vacuum the hajduk leader Karposh — once the Ottomans' own hajduk-hunter — raises north-eastern Macedonia, holds a free territory around Kriva Palanka, Kumanovo and Skopje for six weeks as a buffer both empires court (Leopold I writes to the "Macedonian people"), then falls at Kumanovo; the Ottomans kill him at Skopje's Stone Bridge, and thousands of Christians emigrate north with the retreating Austrians.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
