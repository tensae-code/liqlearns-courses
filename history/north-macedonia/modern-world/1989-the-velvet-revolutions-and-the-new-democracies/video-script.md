# Video script — 1989 the Velvet Revolutions and the New Democracies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of 1989 the Velvet Revolutions and the New Democracies — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Wałęsa's Solidarity makes Poland first, Havel's Velvet Revolution changes Czechoslovakia without a shot, Romania shoots its dictator after a show trial, the two Germanys unite in 1990, Czechoslovakia later divides in peace — while Yugoslavia breaks apart in blood, in the diary of twelve-year-old Zlata Filipović writing through Sarajevo's siege.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
