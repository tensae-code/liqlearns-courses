# Nationalism the Feeling That Made Nation States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Belonging shifts from the king to the nation, and states set out to manufacture national feeling.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In one spring, peoples across Europe demand nations, and by autumn the old regimes shoot their way back.
