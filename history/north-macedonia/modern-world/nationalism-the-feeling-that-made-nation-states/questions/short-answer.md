# Nationalism the Feeling That Made Nation States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: nation and national identity in Europe — people, nation and the state's role in making citizens”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Belonging shifts from the king to the nation, and states set out to manufacture national feeling.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: the Spring of Nations (1848) and the creation of national states in Europe — banquets in Paris become barricades, Vienna rises, then counter-revolution wins by summer”?
**Answer:** In one spring, peoples across Europe demand nations, and by autumn the old regimes shoot their way back.
