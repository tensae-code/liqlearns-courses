# Video script — Nationalism the Feeling That Made Nation States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of nationalism the Feeling That Made Nation States — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Belonging shifts from the king to the nation, and states set out to manufacture national feeling.

In one spring, peoples across Europe demand nations, and by autumn the old regimes shoot their way back.

Two kinds of nation contend — the civic nation of shared laws and rights (France, America) and the ethnic nation of shared language and blood (Germans, Poles) — while Paris punishes Breton and Occitan children for speaking their own tongues at school.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
