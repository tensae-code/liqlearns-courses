# Nationalism the Feeling That Made Nation States

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 1: nation and national identity in Europe — people, nation and the state's role in making citizens

Belonging shifts from the king to the nation, and states set out to manufacture national feeling.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 3: the Spring of Nations (1848) and the creation of national states in Europe — banquets in Paris become barricades, Vienna rises, then counter-revolution wins by summer

In one spring, peoples across Europe demand nations, and by autumn the old regimes shoot their way back.

## Sub lesson 3 — Theme 4: revolutions and wars in the 19th century — the idea of the nation

Two kinds of nation contend — the civic nation of shared laws and rights (France, America) and the ethnic nation of shared language and blood (Germans, Poles) — while Paris punishes Breton and Occitan children for speaking their own tongues at school.
