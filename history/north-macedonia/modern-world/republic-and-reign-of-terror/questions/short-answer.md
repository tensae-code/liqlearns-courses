# Republic and Reign of Terror — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: from monarchy to republic, and the Reign of Terror and the Revolution's meaning”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The republic that promised liberty guillotines its own children before the terror burns out.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: from monarchy to republic and Terror”?
**Answer:** The 1791 constitution keeps a king and a rich man's vote; the king's flight destroys trust, the republic is proclaimed, and Robespierre's Jacobins guillotine king, queen and finally each other — the corrupt Directory that follows is swept away by Napoleon's coup of 1799.
