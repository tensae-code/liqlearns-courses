# Women Win the Vote — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Florence Nightingale remakes nursing in the Crimea's hospitals; suffragettes hunger-strike their way from New Zealand's first victory (1893) to America's (1920); Marie Curie takes two Nobel Prizes, Rosa Luxemburg binds the workers' cause to women's, Simone de Beauvoir names how society "makes" women — and a missionary from Skopje, Mother Teresa, serves the poorest of the poor.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Florence Nightingale remakes nursing in the Crimea's hospitals; suffragettes hunger-strike their way from New Zealand's first victory (1893) to America's (1920)
