# Video script — The Reformation Luther and Protestantism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Reformation Luther and Protestantism — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

A Church grown rich on indulgences meets Martin Luther, whose 95 theses of 1517 deny that pardon can be bought; excommunicated, he gathers "Protestants" across Germany, Switzerland, France, England and Scandinavia — Lutheran, Calvinist and Anglican churches split western Christianity a second time.

Indulgences sold like tickets to heaven and Gutenberg's press spreading every argument — Luther nails his 95 Theses in 1517, is excommunicated and sheltered by princes, and Calvin builds a disciplined Geneva while Müntzer dies for the poor.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
