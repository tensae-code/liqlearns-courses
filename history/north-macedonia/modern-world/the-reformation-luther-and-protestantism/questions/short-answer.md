# The Reformation Luther and Protestantism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the Reformation”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** A Church grown rich on indulgences meets Martin Luther, whose 95 theses of 1517 deny that pardon can be bought; excommunicated, he gathers "Protestants" across Germany, Switzerland, France, England and Scandinavia — Lutheran, Calvinist and Anglican churches split western Christianity a second time.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the Reformation”?
**Answer:** Indulgences sold like tickets to heaven and Gutenberg's press spreading every argument — Luther nails his 95 Theses in 1517, is excommunicated and sheltered by princes, and Calvin builds a disciplined Geneva while Müntzer dies for the poor.
