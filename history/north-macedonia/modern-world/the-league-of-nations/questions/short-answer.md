# The League of Nations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: the world between the wars — the League and the peace that wasn't”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Wilson's League of Nations opens in Geneva in 1920 without America in it; it mediates, feeds refugees and fights the drug trade, the Washington Conference caps navies, Locarno guarantees borders, and in 1928 the world's states solemnly renounce war in the Briand-Kellogg Pact — and go on preparing it.
