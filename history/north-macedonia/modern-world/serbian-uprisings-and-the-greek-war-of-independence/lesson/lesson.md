# Serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 2: the uprisings in Serbia and Greece — Serbian rebels free towns, Miloš Obrenović raises the Second Uprising (1815), and the Greek struggle is decided at Navarino

Two Christian peoples shoot their way out of the empire, Serbia by bargaining and Greece by great-power fleets.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2: autonomous Serbia builds its first state and educational institutions, Ilija Garašanin and Prince Mihailo Obrenović shape the principality (1830–1878)

Autonomy becomes a working state with schools, laws and a plan for all Serbs.

## Sub lesson 3 — Theme 2: Greece, the first independent state in the Balkans — after independence it must decide government, education and laws, holds its first elections and passes from King Otto to George I

The first Balkan state freed from the sultan learns to govern itself under imported kings.

## Sub lesson 4 — Theme 4: Serbia rises

Karađorđe's first uprising (1804) against renegade janissaries grows into a national struggle and is crushed; Miloš Obrenović's second (1815), fought with negotiation as much as muskets, wins Serbia its autonomy by 1830 — the first Balkan people to govern themselves inside the empire.
