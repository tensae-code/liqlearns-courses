# Video script — Serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Two Christian peoples shoot their way out of the empire, Serbia by bargaining and Greece by great-power fleets.

Autonomy becomes a working state with schools, laws and a plan for all Serbs.

The first Balkan state freed from the sultan learns to govern itself under imported kings.

Karađorđe's first uprising (1804) against renegade janissaries grows into a national struggle and is crushed; Miloš Obrenović's second (1815), fought with negotiation as much as muskets, wins Serbia its autonomy by 1830 — the first Balkan people to govern themselves inside the empire.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
