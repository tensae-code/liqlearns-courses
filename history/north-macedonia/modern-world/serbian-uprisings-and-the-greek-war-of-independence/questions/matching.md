# Serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Theme 2: the uprisings in Serbia and Greece — Serbian rebels free towns, Miloš Obrenović raises the Second Uprising (1815), and the Greek struggle is decided at Navarino — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Theme 2: autonomous Serbia builds its first state and educational institutions, Ilija Garašanin and Prince Mihailo Obrenović shape the principality (1830–1878) — b)

a) Two Christian peoples shoot their way out of the empire, Serbia by bargaining and Greece by great-power fleets.
b) Autonomy becomes a working state with schools, laws and a plan for all Serbs.
