# Serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 2: the uprisings in Serbia and Greece — Serbian rebels free towns, Miloš Obrenović raises the Second Uprising (1815), and the Greek struggle is decided at Navarino”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Two Christian peoples shoot their way out of the empire, Serbia by bargaining and Greece by great-power fleets.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: autonomous Serbia builds its first state and educational institutions, Ilija Garašanin and Prince Mihailo Obrenović shape the principality (1830–1878)”?
**Answer:** Autonomy becomes a working state with schools, laws and a plan for all Serbs.
