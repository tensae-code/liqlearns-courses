# Serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two Christian peoples shoot their way out of the empire, Serbia by bargaining and Greece by great-power fleets.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Autonomy becomes a working state with schools, laws and a plan for all Serbs.
