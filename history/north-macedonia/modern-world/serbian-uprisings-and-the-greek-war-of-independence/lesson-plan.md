# Lesson plan — Serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence

**Course:** History — North Macedonia · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of serbian Uprisings and the Greek War of Independence — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Theme 2: the uprisings in Serbia and Greece — Serbian rebels free towns, Miloš Obrenović raises the Second Uprising (1815), and the Greek struggle is decided at Navarino** — two Christian peoples shoot their way out of the empire, Serbia by bargaining and Greece by great-power fleets
2. **Theme 2: autonomous Serbia builds its first state and educational institutions, Ilija Garašanin and Prince Mihailo Obrenović shape the principality (1830–1878)** — autonomy becomes a working state with schools, laws and a plan for all Serbs
3. **Theme 2: Greece, the first independent state in the Balkans — after independence it must decide government, education and laws, holds its first elections and passes from King Otto to George I** — the first Balkan state freed from the sultan learns to govern itself under imported kings
4. **Theme 4: Serbia rises** — Karađorđe's first uprising (1804) against renegade janissaries grows into a national struggle and is crushed; Miloš Obrenović's second (1815), fought with negotiation as much as muskets, wins Serbia its autonomy by 1830 — the first Balkan people to govern themselves inside the empire
