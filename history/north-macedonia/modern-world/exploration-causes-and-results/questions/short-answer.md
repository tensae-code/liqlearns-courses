# Exploration Causes and Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the Age of Discoveries — causes”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Ottoman tolls after 1453 strangle the spice roads, so Europe hunts new routes; Ptolemy's recovered map, the compass, the astrolabe, the caravel and gunpowder make the hunt possible.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: the great geographical discoveries — causes”?
**Answer:** Spices, gold, rivalry and the missionary urge, armed with compass, astrolabe and better ships — and Constantinople's fall making the old land routes dear and dangerous — send Europe looking for the sea road to Asia.
