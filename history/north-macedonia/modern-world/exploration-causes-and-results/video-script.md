# Video script — Exploration Causes and Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of exploration Causes and Results — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Ottoman tolls after 1453 strangle the spice roads, so Europe hunts new routes; Ptolemy's recovered map, the compass, the astrolabe, the caravel and gunpowder make the hunt possible.

Spices, gold, rivalry and the missionary urge, armed with compass, astrolabe and better ships — and Constantinople's fall making the old land routes dear and dangerous — send Europe looking for the sea road to Asia.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
