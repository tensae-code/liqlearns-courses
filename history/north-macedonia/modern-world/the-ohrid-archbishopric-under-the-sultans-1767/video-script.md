# Video script — The Ohrid Archbishopric under the Sultans 1767

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Ohrid Archbishopric under the Sultans 1767 — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The sultans keep the Archbishopric as a loyal, privileged church — its flock grows to Bulgaria, Wallachia, Moldavia and even Orthodox communities in Italy — but favour shifts to Constantinople's patriarchate; St Sophia and St Pantelejmon become mosques and the seat moves to Perivlepta; Archbishop Athanasius I tours Europe begging for an uprising that never comes; squeezed by debt and Phanariot intrigue, the last archbishop, Arsenij, is forced to resign on 16 January 1767, and the sultan abolishes the Archbishopric — the story Grigor Prlićev later sings in "1762 leto".

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
