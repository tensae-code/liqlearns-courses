# Video script — Skanderbeg Albanias Quarter Century of Freedom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of skanderbeg Albanias Quarter Century of Freedom — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Gjergj Kastrioti, a hostage prince raised at Edirne and named Iskender for Alexander, deserts the Ottoman army in 1443, tricks his way into Krujë with a forged letter, and binds Albania's feudal families in the League of Lezhë (1444); for twenty-five years his mountain war defeats sultans in person — Murad II dies before Krujë's walls — the pope names him Athleta Christi, and only his death in 1468 ends it; Durrës, the last Venetian holdout, falls in 1501.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
