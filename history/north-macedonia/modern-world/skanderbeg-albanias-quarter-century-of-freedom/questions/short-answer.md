# Skanderbeg Albanias Quarter Century of Freedom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 5: the uprising of Skanderbeg”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Gjergj Kastrioti, a hostage prince raised at Edirne and named Iskender for Alexander, deserts the Ottoman army in 1443, tricks his way into Krujë with a forged letter, and binds Albania's feudal families in the League of Lezhë (1444); for twenty-five years his mountain war defeats sultans in person — Murad II dies before Krujë's walls — the pope names him Athleta Christi, and only his death in 1468 ends it; Durrës, the last Venetian holdout, falls in 1501.
