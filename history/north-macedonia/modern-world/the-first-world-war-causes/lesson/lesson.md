# The First World War Causes

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: international relations 1870–1914 — the rivalry of the Great Powers, the race for colonies and armed camps form the road to 1914

Alliances harden and empires collide in the colonies until Europe stands armed and waiting.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 4: the First World War — causes

Colonial rivalry, naval races and revenge for 1871 arm two alliance blocs; a nineteen-year-old's shots in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 give Austria-Hungary its excuse, and the ultimatum, the alliances and the timetables do the rest.
