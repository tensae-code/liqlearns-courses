# The First World War Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: international relations 1870–1914 — the rivalry of the Great Powers, the race for colonies and armed camps form the road to 1914”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Alliances harden and empires collide in the colonies until Europe stands armed and waiting.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 4: the First World War — causes”?
**Answer:** Colonial rivalry, naval races and revenge for 1871 arm two alliance blocs; a nineteen-year-old's shots in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 give Austria-Hungary its excuse, and the ultimatum, the alliances and the timetables do the rest.
