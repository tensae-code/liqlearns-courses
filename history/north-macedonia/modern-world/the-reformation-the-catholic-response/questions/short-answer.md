# The Reformation the Catholic Response — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the Counter-Reformation”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Rome answers with new orders — above all the Jesuits, whose schools win back Poland and southern Germany — a renewed Inquisition, an Index of forbidden books (even Bible translations), and the splendour of Baroque art as propaganda.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the Counter-Reformation”?
**Answer:** Rome answers at Trent with trained priests and an end to indulgence abuses, but also with the Inquisition, an Index of forbidden books and Loyola's Jesuits marching schools and missions across three continents.
