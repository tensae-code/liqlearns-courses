# The Reformation the Catholic Response

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: the Counter-Reformation

Rome answers with new orders — above all the Jesuits, whose schools win back Poland and southern Germany — a renewed Inquisition, an Index of forbidden books (even Bible translations), and the splendour of Baroque art as propaganda.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2: the Counter-Reformation

Rome answers at Trent with trained priests and an end to indulgence abuses, but also with the Inquisition, an Index of forbidden books and Loyola's Jesuits marching schools and missions across three continents.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Reformation the Catholic Response — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Reformation the Catholic Response. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
