# Video script — The Reformation the Catholic Response

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the Reformation the Catholic Response — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Rome answers with new orders — above all the Jesuits, whose schools win back Poland and southern Germany — a renewed Inquisition, an Index of forbidden books (even Bible translations), and the splendour of Baroque art as propaganda.

Rome answers at Trent with trained priests and an end to indulgence abuses, but also with the Inquisition, an Index of forbidden books and Loyola's Jesuits marching schools and missions across three continents.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
