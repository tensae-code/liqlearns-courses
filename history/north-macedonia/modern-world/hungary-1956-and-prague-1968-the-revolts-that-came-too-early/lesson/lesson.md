# Hungary 1956 and Prague 1968 the Revolts That Came Too Early

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Budapest pulls down Stalin's statue in 1956 and Soviet tanks answer; Prague's "socialism with a human face" in 1968 is ended the same way — the bloc holds, but everyone has seen it can crack.

Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
