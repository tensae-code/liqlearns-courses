# The Road to War Manchuria Ethiopia Spain and Munich

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

The League watches Japan seize Manchuria (1931) and Italy swallow Ethiopia (1935) and does nothing that matters; Spain's civil war becomes the dictators' rehearsal, Hitler takes the Rhineland, Austria and — with London and Paris signing at Munich — the Sudetenland, then signs his pact with Stalin.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the Road to War Manchuria Ethiopia Spain and Munich — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Road to War Manchuria Ethiopia Spain and Munich. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
