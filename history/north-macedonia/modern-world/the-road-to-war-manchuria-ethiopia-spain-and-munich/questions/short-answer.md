# The Road to War Manchuria Ethiopia Spain and Munich — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: the road to the Second World War”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The League watches Japan seize Manchuria (1931) and Italy swallow Ethiopia (1935) and does nothing that matters; Spain's civil war becomes the dictators' rehearsal, Hitler takes the Rhineland, Austria and — with London and Paris signing at Munich — the Sudetenland, then signs his pact with Stalin.
