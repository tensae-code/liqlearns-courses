# Video script — Bulgaria 1876 1878 April Uprising San Stefano and Berlin

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of bulgaria 1876 1878 April Uprising San Stefano and Berlin — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The April Uprising is drowned in blood and the Batak massacre shocks Europe's newspapers; Russia declares war in 1877 and the Treaty of San Stefano draws a great Bulgaria — which frightens London and Vienna into the Congress of Berlin, cutting it into a tributary principality and an autonomous Eastern Rumelia still under the sultan.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
