# Bulgaria 1876 1878 April Uprising San Stefano and Berlin — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: Bulgaria 1876–1878”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The April Uprising is drowned in blood and the Batak massacre shocks Europe's newspapers; Russia declares war in 1877 and the Treaty of San Stefano draws a great Bulgaria — which frightens London and Vienna into the Congress of Berlin, cutting it into a tributary principality and an autonomous Eastern Rumelia still under the sultan.
