# Americas Conquest and Columbian Exchange — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 3: the Columbian Exchange”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The two worlds swap their pantries and their plagues — wheat, horses and cattle sail west; potatoes, maize, tomatoes, cacao and tobacco sail east; and the book names the frame that justified it all: Eurocentrism, the belief that Europe was the world's centre and measure.
