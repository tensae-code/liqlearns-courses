# The American Civil War Union Against Slavery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the US Civil War (1861–1865) — the South defends its way of life against federal power, in one of the century's bloodiest wars, until the South is defeated by spring 1865”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** A republic built on liberty fights itself over slavery, and the Union survives.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 4: the American Civil War”?
**Answer:** Lincoln writes that his paramount object is to save the Union, not slavery — yet his Emancipation Proclamation of 1863 gives the war its moral cause; four years and 600,000 dead later the Union holds and slavery is abolished.
