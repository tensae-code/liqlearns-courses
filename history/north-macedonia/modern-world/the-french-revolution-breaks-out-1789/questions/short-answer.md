# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 1: the French Revolution — causes and outbreak, the estates system and the storm that spreads across France”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** A bankrupt kingdom summons the estates and unleashes a revolution it cannot control.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 2: the French Revolution breaks out”?
**Answer:** A bankrupt crown, bread prices doubled after failed harvests (an Icelandic volcano's ash helped chill Europe), and a Third Estate of 98% paying all the taxes — the Estates-General meets, the Tennis Court swears its oath, Paris storms the Bastille, the Great Fear burns the châteaux, and the Assembly proclaims the Rights of Man while Parisian women march the king back from Versailles.
