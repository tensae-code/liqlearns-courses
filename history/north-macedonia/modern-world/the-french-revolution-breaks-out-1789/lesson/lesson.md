# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 1: the French Revolution — causes and outbreak, the estates system and the storm that spreads across France

A bankrupt kingdom summons the estates and unleashes a revolution it cannot control.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2: the French Revolution breaks out

A bankrupt crown, bread prices doubled after failed harvests (an Icelandic volcano's ash helped chill Europe), and a Third Estate of 98% paying all the taxes — the Estates-General meets, the Tennis Court swears its oath, Paris storms the Bastille, the Great Fear burns the châteaux, and the Assembly proclaims the Rights of Man while Parisian women march the king back from Versailles.

Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
