# Cold War Crises 1948 1962

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

The Berlin blockade is beaten by an airlift of coal and candy; Korea splits at the 38th parallel in a war that never formally ends; the Wall goes up in 1961; and in October 1962 Soviet missiles in Cuba bring Kennedy and Khrushchev — and the world — to the edge, before the quiet bargain over Turkey steps it back.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of cold War Crises 1948 1962 — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Cold War Crises 1948 1962. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
