# Cold War Crises 1948 1962 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: Cold War crises”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The Berlin blockade is beaten by an airlift of coal and candy; Korea splits at the 38th parallel in a war that never formally ends; the Wall goes up in 1961; and in October 1962 Soviet missiles in Cuba bring Kennedy and Khrushchev — and the world — to the edge, before the quiet bargain over Turkey steps it back.
