# Video script — Migration Diasporas and the Making of a Global Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of migration Diasporas and the Making of a Global Culture — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Famine, poverty and persecution push millions to America, Canada, Argentina and Australia; a Danish emigrant's recorded voice remembers that the hardest thing was not understanding a single word; empires meanwhile ship Indians to Caribbean plantations and Chinese labour across Asia — and the mixing brews new cultures: jazz, Haitian Creole, Rio's carnival, the Day of the Dead.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
