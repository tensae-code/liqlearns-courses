# Lesson plan — Exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan

**Course:** History — North Macedonia · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Middle Ages: Europe, the Balkans and Macedonia Under the Sultans (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Middle Ages: Europe, the Balkans and Macedonia Under the Sultans — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Theme 3: the discovery of America** — Genoa's Columbus, twice refused by Portugal, sails for Spain in 1492 and names San Salvador's people "Indians", sure he has reached Asia; Amerigo Vespucci proves it a new continent, and the mapmakers name it America after him
2. **Theme 3: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan** — Columbus reaches the Caribbean in 1492 still believing in India; Vespucci argues it is a whole new continent and lends it his name; Magellan's ships circle the Earth though he dies on the way — and the book asks whether a man can "discover" a land where millions already live, and whether the Vikings were there first

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **Exploration Causes and Results** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: the story of exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
2. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, what came of it — without looking back.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of the region in the story if you have one. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Retells names but not the turn → ask: what changed in this story, and for whom? The turn is the proof.
- Go back to the one thing: the story of exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
