# Exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the discovery of America”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Genoa's Columbus, twice refused by Portugal, sails for Spain in 1492 and names San Salvador's people "Indians", sure he has reached Asia; Amerigo Vespucci proves it a new continent, and the mapmakers name it America after him.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 3: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan”?
**Answer:** Columbus reaches the Caribbean in 1492 still believing in India; Vespucci argues it is a whole new continent and lends it his name; Magellan's ships circle the Earth though he dies on the way — and the book asks whether a man can "discover" a land where millions already live, and whether the Vikings were there first.
