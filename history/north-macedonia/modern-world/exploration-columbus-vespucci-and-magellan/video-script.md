# Video script — Exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of exploration Columbus Vespucci and Magellan — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Genoa's Columbus, twice refused by Portugal, sails for Spain in 1492 and names San Salvador's people "Indians", sure he has reached Asia; Amerigo Vespucci proves it a new continent, and the mapmakers name it America after him.

Columbus reaches the Caribbean in 1492 still believing in India; Vespucci argues it is a whole new continent and lends it his name; Magellan's ships circle the Earth though he dies on the way — and the book asks whether a man can "discover" a land where millions already live, and whether the Vikings were there first.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
