# Video script — Young People in History Victims Rebels and Change Makers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of young People in History Victims Rebels and Change Makers — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

The young are used — Hitler Youth drilled for a regime, child workers consumed by factories — and the young resist: pupils in the civil-rights struggle, hippies against a war, a generation inventing Woodstock; pupils role-play factory children, 1960s students and festival organisers and argue whether today's youth hold more power than any before them.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
