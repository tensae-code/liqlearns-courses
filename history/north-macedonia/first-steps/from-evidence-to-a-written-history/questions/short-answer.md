# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Introduction: sources for the history of the Middle Ages”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The medieval past survives in two kinds of trace — written sources (rulers' charters, chronicles that mix legend with fact, saints' lives) and material ones (coins, weapons, frescoes, fortresses) — and the historian must cross-examine both.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Introduction: sources for the history of the new age, worked through Napoleon's entry into history as a source exercise”?
**Answer:** Pupils open a new era by reading what a conqueror's contemporaries wrote about him and learning to question it.
