# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The medieval past survives in two kinds of trace — written sources (rulers' charters, chronicles that mix legend with fact, saints' lives) and material ones (coins, weapons, frescoes, fortresses) — and the historian must cross-examine both.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Pupils open a new era by reading what a conqueror's contemporaries wrote about him and learning to question it.
