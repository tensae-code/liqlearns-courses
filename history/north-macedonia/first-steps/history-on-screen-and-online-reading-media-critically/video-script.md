# Video script — History on Screen and Online Reading Media Critically

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of history on Screen and Online Reading Media Critically — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Documentaries colourise the trenches, social networks let anyone publish "history", and polished lies outrun footnoted truths — Hitler escaping to Argentina, Napoleon shrunk to 150 cm by a units mix-up and British mockery; meanwhile nations argue over Confederate statues and over how German, British and French textbooks tell empire — the pupil's defence is critical reading.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
