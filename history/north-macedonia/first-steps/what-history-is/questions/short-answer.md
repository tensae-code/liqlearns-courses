# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Introduction: what the "new age" (early-modern/modern era) is and how life, power and society changed as nations formed”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Centuries of slow life give way to new states built on the idea that peoples sharing language and culture deserve their own country.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Introduction to history”?
**Answer:** History begins as the Greek word istoria, inquiry; the book separates history (what happened) from historiography (how it is written), lays out the great periods from prehistory to the newest age, and ends with the old saying that history is life's teacher.
