# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Centuries of slow life give way to new states built on the idea that peoples sharing language and culture deserve their own country.
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: History begins as the Greek word istoria, inquiry; the book separates history (what happened) from historiography (how it is written), lays out the great period
