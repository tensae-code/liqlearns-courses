# Video script — Historical Memory in the Digital Age Archives Ai and Deepfakes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of historical Memory in the Digital Age Archives Ai and Deepfakes — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Manuscripts and French-Revolution newspapers go online, VR walks you through Pompeii and Machu Picchu, AI translates damaged texts — and also invents facts and fake images; paper survives centuries while unmaintained data rots in years, Chernobyl teaches why hidden information kills, Fukushima answers faster because history was remembered, and multiperspectivity becomes the citizen's core skill.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
