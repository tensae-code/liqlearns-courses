# Video script — History and Identity Who We Think We Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of history and Identity Who We Think We Are — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Identity is also built against an enemy — Nazi posters draw Slavs as Untermenschen and the image prepares the crime; against that, Belgian pupils learn how Flemish and Walloon identities grew inside one shared history, and German textbooks teach two Germanys' different pasts inside one state — history builds bridges or walls depending on how it is told.

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
