# Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Theme 3: the era of colonialism and imperialism — Cecil Rhodes as the symbol of imperialism in Africa, and the map of African colonies by 1913”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Industrial empires race to own whole continents, and one diamond magnate dreams of a red line from Cape to Cairo.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Theme 4: colonialism and imperialism”?
**Answer:** Industrial empires hunt markets, raw materials and cheap labour, and dress the hunt as a duty to "enlighten" peoples they declare inferior — the ideology that made exploitation feel like generosity.
