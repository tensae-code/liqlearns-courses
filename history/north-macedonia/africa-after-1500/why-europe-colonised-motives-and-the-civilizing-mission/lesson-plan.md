# Lesson plan — Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission

**Course:** History — North Macedonia · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The New Age: Revolution, Industry and the Balkan Awakening — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Theme 3: the era of colonialism and imperialism — Cecil Rhodes as the symbol of imperialism in Africa, and the map of African colonies by 1913** — industrial empires race to own whole continents, and one diamond magnate dreams of a red line from Cape to Cairo
2. **Theme 4: colonialism and imperialism** — industrial empires hunt markets, raw materials and cheap labour, and dress the hunt as a duty to "enlighten" peoples they declare inferior — the ideology that made exploitation feel like generosity

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: the story of why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
2. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, what came of it — without looking back.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of the region in the story if you have one. Nothing else.
