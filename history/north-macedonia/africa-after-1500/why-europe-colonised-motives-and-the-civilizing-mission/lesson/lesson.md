# Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: the era of colonialism and imperialism — Cecil Rhodes as the symbol of imperialism in Africa, and the map of African colonies by 1913

Industrial empires race to own whole continents, and one diamond magnate dreams of a red line from Cape to Cairo.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 4: colonialism and imperialism

Industrial empires hunt markets, raw materials and cheap labour, and dress the hunt as a duty to "enlighten" peoples they declare inferior — the ideology that made exploitation feel like generosity.
