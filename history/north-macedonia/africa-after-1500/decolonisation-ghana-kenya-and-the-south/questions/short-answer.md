# Decolonisation Ghana Kenya and the South — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell the story of “Theme 4: Africa freed — and the Caribbean”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya and the Congo (under its first premier, Lumumba) break from Britain and Belgium, Angola and Mozambique from Portugal — and across the Atlantic Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago and Barbados take independence while keeping their ties to the old metropole.
