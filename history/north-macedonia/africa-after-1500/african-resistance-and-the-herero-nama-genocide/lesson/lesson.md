# African Resistance and the Herero Nama Genocide

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Africa fights back — the Zulu kingdom bloodies Britain, the Mahdi's Sudan holds its independence for over a decade — and in German South-West Africa the Herero and Nama are systematically destroyed in 1904–08, the twentieth century's first genocide.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of african Resistance and the Herero Nama Genocide — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: African Resistance and the Herero Nama Genocide. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
