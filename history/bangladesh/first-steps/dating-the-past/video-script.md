# Video script — Dating the Past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The past is placed in order with centuries and decades on either side of the BC/AD line; economies and eras can be periodised too — but the lines between ancient, medieval and modern are human choices, and Bengal's own facts show where the labels fail.

Before any story can be told in order, the class learns the measuring frame.

The working past can be divided too.

In Class Nine the frame itself is put on trial.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
