# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Liberation War of 1971, the Language Movement of 1952, Bangabandhu's birth in 1920 and Plassey in 1757 are placed by century and decade on either side of the BC/AD line.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — The past is placed in order with centuries and decades on either side of the BC/AD line; economies and eras can be periodised too — but the lines between ancient, medieval and modern are human choices, and Bengal's own facts show where the labels fail.
