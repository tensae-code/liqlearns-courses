# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “Centuries, decades, BC and AD” is right?
a) The Liberation War of 1971, the Language Movement of 1952, Bangabandhu's birth in 1920 and Plassey in 1757 are placed by century and decade on either side of the BC/AD line  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Bangladesh's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The past is placed in order with centuries and decades on either side of the BC/AD line; economies and eras can be periodised too — but the lines between ancient, medieval and modern are human choices, and Bengal's own facts show where the labels fail.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Bangladesh has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
