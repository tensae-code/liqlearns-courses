# Dating the Past — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Centuries, decades, BC and AD — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Periodisation in economic history — b)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The problem of dividing eras — c)


Meanings:
a) The Liberation War of 1971, the Language Movement of 1952, Bangabandhu's birth in 1920 and Plassey in 1757 are placed by century and decade on either side of the BC/AD line.
b) The human economy is divided from hunting-and-gathering through the Stone, Copper, Iron and Bronze ages to agriculture, cities and global trade.
c) Dividing the past into ancient, medieval and modern eras is itself a problem, and Bengal examples from the 7th century onward show where the labels fail.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
