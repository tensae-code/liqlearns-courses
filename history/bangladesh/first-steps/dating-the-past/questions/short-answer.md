# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Centuries, decades, BC and AD; Periodisation in economic history; The problem of dividing eras.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because the past is placed in order with centuries and decades on either side of the BC/AD line; economies and eras can be periodised too — but the lines between ancient, medieval and modern are human choices, and Bengal's own facts show where the labels fail. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
