# Lesson plan — Dating the Past

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Dating the Past
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — History by Inquiry: Your Locality, Bengal and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The past is placed in order with centuries and decades on either side of the BC/AD line; economies and eras can be periodised too — but the lines between ancient, medieval and modern are human choices, and Bengal's own facts show where the labels fail.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — History by Inquiry: Your Locality, Bengal and the Deep Past (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Centuries, decades, BC and AD** — The Liberation War of 1971, the Language Movement of 1952, Bangabandhu's birth in 1920 and Plassey in 1757 are placed by century and decade on either side of the BC/AD line.
2. **Periodisation in economic history** — The human economy is divided from hunting-and-gathering through the Stone, Copper, Iron and Bronze ages to agriculture, cities and global trade.
3. **The problem of dividing eras** — Dividing the past into ancient, medieval and modern eras is itself a problem, and Bengal examples from the 7th century onward show where the labels fail.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a family photograph dated by an elder's memory — placing it by decade and century is this lesson's skill at home.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up centuries and decades → a decade is ten years, a century a hundred; 1971 sits in the twentieth century, in its eighth decade.
- Go back to the one thing: The past is placed in order with centuries and decades on either side of the BC/AD line; economies and eras can be periodised too — but the lines between ancient, medieval and modern are human choices, and Bengal's own facts show where the labels fail. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
