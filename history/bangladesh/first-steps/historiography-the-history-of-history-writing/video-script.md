# Video script — Historiography: The History of History Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History itself has a history: scripts change century by century and must be deciphered before old texts speak, historians assemble the decoded sources — with reference works like Banglapedia — into written cultural history, and their methods and era schemes are human inventions argued over in print.

An old text does not speak on its own.

Class Nine widens the point: historians' methods, and the era schemes built with them, are human inventions — argued over in print like everything else humans make.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
