# Historiography: The History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Scripts change century by century and must be decoded before old texts speak; the historian assembles deciphered sources, and reference works like Banglapedia, into a written cultural history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — History itself has a history: scripts change century by century and must be deciphered before old texts speak, historians assemble the decoded sources — with reference works like Banglapedia — into written cultural history, and their methods and era schemes are human inventions argued over in print.
