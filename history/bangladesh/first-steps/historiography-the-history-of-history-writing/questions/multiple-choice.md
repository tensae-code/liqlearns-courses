# Historiography: The History of History Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “Deciphering and writing cultural history” is right?
a) Scripts change century by century and must be decoded before old texts speak; the historian assembles deciphered sources, and reference works like Banglapedia, into a written cultural history  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Bangladesh's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) History itself has a history: scripts change century by century and must be deciphered before old texts speak, historians assemble the decoded sources — with reference works like Banglapedia — into written cultural history, and their methods and era schemes are human inventions argued over in print.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Bangladesh has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
