# Historiography: The History of History Writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Deciphering and writing cultural history; Methods of learning history.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because history itself has a history: scripts change century by century and must be deciphered before old texts speak, historians assemble the decoded sources — with reference works like Banglapedia — into written cultural history, and their methods and era schemes are human inventions argued over in print. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
