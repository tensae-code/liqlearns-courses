# Video script — From Evidence to a Written History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A written history is built from sources that are questioned first: a lost ball can lead to a ruined building, and coins, inscriptions, travellers' accounts, manuscripts and buildings each testify — differently — about the past.

Renu loses a cricket ball, and the search leads the team off the field and into the green, to a mound nobody had questioned before.

For the working life of the past, the witnesses have their own characters.

For the social and cultural past, the witnesses divide into two great kinds.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
