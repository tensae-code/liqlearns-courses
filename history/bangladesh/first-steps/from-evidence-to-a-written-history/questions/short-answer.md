# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The ruined building in the jungle; Sources of economic history; Two kinds of sources.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because a written history is built from sources that are questioned first: a lost ball can lead to a ruined building, and coins, inscriptions, travellers' accounts, manuscripts and buildings each testify — differently — about the past. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
