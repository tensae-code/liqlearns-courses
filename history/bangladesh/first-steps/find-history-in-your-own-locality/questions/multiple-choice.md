# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “Explore the history of our neighbourhood” is right?
a) Pupil teams interview 4–5 elders about their own village, under rules of permission, confidentiality and no pressure to answer  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Bangladesh's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) History begins where you live: pupil teams interview elders about their own village — with permission, confidentiality and no pressure to answer — and plan real inquiries, like the class investigation of what happened in their locality in 1971.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Bangladesh has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
