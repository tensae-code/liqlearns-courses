# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History begins where you live: pupil teams interview elders about their own village — with permission, confidentiality and no pressure to answer — and plan real inquiries, like the class investigation of what happened in their locality in 1971.

Khushi Apa sends the class out to explore the history of its own neighbourhood.

Then the locality inquiry turns to the hardest chapter of all: 1971.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
