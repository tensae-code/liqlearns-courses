## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The earliest humans are rebuilt from hardened bones dug from the ground: palaeontologists and historians read the fossils together in laboratories, each science lending the other its eyes.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a stone picked up on a riverbank — asking whose hands shaped it, and who could tell you, is this lesson's question.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
