# Who Studies Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hardened bones dug from the ground are read in laboratories by palaeontologists and historians working together to rebuild the earliest humans.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — The earliest humans are rebuilt from hardened bones dug from the ground: palaeontologists and historians read the fossils together in laboratories, each science lending the other its eyes.
