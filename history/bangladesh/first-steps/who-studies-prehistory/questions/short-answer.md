# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Fossils and palaeontologists.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because the earliest humans are rebuilt from hardened bones dug from the ground: palaeontologists and historians read the fossils together in laboratories, each science lending the other its eyes. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
