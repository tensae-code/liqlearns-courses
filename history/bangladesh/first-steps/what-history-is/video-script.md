# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is the logical, source-based account of how people lived, farmed, built kingdoms and reached the present — and a people without it has no identity; read through work and trade, it becomes economic history, the story of how people earned, ate and were governed.

In Khushi Apa's Class Six classroom, the question comes first: what is history? It is not a pile of old tales.

A year later the same class looks at the past through a new lens.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
