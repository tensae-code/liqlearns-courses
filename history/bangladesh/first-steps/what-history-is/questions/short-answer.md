# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** What history is; What economic history is.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because history is the logical, source-based account of how people lived, farmed, built kingdoms and reached the present — and a people without it has no identity; read through work and trade, it becomes economic history, the story of how people earned, ate and were governed. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
