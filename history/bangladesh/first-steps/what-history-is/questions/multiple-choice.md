# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “What history is” is right?
a) History is the logical, source-based account of how people lived, farmed, built kingdoms and reached the present  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Bangladesh's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) History is the logical, source-based account of how people lived, farmed, built kingdoms and reached the present — and a people without it has no identity; read through work and trade, it becomes economic history, the story of how people earned, ate and were governed.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Bangladesh has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
