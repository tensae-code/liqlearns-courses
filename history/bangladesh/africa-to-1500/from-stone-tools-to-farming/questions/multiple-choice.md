# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “Stone Age to Copper-Stone Age” is right?
a) From the first stone tools 3.3 million years ago, cave life and hunting give way to copper worked alongside stone  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Bangladesh's book

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) From the first stone tools 3.3 million years ago, cave life and hunting give way to copper worked alongside stone — and then to Gordon Childe's 'Neolithic Revolution', when people stopped wandering for food, farmed, reared animals and settled for good, reaching Bengal about seven thousand years ago.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Bangladesh has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
