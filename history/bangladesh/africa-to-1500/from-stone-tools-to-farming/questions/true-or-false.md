# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) From the first stone tools 3.3 million years ago, cave life and hunting give way to copper worked alongside stone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — From the first stone tools 3.3 million years ago, cave life and hunting give way to copper worked alongside stone — and then to Gordon Childe's 'Neolithic Revolution', when people stopped wandering for food, farmed, reared animals and settled for good, reaching Bengal about seven thousand years ago.
