# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Stone Age to Copper-Stone Age; The Agricultural Revolution.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because from the first stone tools 3.3 million years ago, cave life and hunting give way to copper worked alongside stone — and then to Gordon Childe's 'Neolithic Revolution', when people stopped wandering for food, farmed, reared animals and settled for good, reaching Bengal about seven thousand years ago. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
