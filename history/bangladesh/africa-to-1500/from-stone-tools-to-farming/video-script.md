# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From the first stone tools 3.3 million years ago, cave life and hunting give way to copper worked alongside stone — and then to Gordon Childe's 'Neolithic Revolution', when people stopped wandering for food, farmed, reared animals and settled for good, reaching Bengal about seven thousand years ago.

The human story of tools begins 3.3 million years ago with the first shaped stone.

The scholar Gordon Childe named what happened next a revolution — the Neolithic Revolution.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
