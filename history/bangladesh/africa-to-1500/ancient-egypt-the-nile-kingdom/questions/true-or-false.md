# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A three-class society under god-king Pharaohs, landless farmers and sold prisoners below, yet women like Nefertiti near the throne, and a people who give the world writing, religion and mathematics.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Ancient Egypt is a three-class society under god-king Pharaohs — from Menes to Tutankhamun, rulers as descendants of the sun god Re, owning all wealth and lives, mummified in pyramids to rule after death — with landless farmers and sold prisoners below, women like Nefertiti near the throne, and a people who give the world writing, religion and mathematics.
