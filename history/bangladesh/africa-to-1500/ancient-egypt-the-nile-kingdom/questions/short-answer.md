# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient Egypt: society and culture; The Pharaohs.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because ancient Egypt is a three-class society under god-king Pharaohs — from Menes to Tutankhamun, rulers as descendants of the sun god Re, owning all wealth and lives, mummified in pyramids to rule after death — with landless farmers and sold prisoners below, women like Nefertiti near the throne, and a people who give the world writing, religion and mathematics. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
