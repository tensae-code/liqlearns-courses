# Lesson plan — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — History Through Society and Culture: How People Lived (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ancient Egypt is a three-class society under god-king Pharaohs — from Menes to Tutankhamun, rulers as descendants of the sun god Re, owning all wealth and lives, mummified in pyramids to rule after death — with landless farmers and sold prisoners below, women like Nefertiti near the throne, and a people who give the world writing, religion and mathematics.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — History Through Society and Culture: How People Lived (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Ancient Egypt is a three-class society under god-king Pharaohs — from Menes to Tutankhamun, rulers as descendants of the sun god Re, owning all wealth and lives, mummified in pyramids to rule after death — with landless farmers and sold prisoners below, women like Nefertiti near the throne, and a people who give the world writing, religion and mathematics.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any ranked order you know — a school, a team, a workplace — Egypt's three classes are the ancient shape of the same question: who stands where, and why.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
