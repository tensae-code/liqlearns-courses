# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a pyramid. Divide it into three layers and write who you think stood at the top, the middle and the bottom of Egypt's society. This lesson fills your drawing with the real names and the real order.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Ancient Egypt is a three-class society under god-king Pharaohs — from Menes to Tutankhamun, rulers as descendants of the sun god Re, owning all wealth and lives, mummified in pyramids to rule after death — with landless farmers and sold prisoners below, women like Nefertiti near the throne, and a people who give the world writing, religion and mathematics. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
