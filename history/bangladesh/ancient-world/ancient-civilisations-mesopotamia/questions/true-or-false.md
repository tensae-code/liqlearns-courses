# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sumerian and Babylonian city civilisations build a class society between two rivers, with their own laws, gods and learning.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Between two rivers, the Sumerian and Babylonian city civilisations build a class society with its own laws, gods and learning — Mesopotamia orders life by written rule at the dawn of cities.
