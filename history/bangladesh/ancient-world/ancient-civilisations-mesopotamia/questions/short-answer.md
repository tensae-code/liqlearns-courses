# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient Mesopotamia.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because between two rivers, the Sumerian and Babylonian city civilisations build a class society with its own laws, gods and learning — Mesopotamia orders life by written rule at the dawn of cities. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
