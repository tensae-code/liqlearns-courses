# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the two rivers of your own land that matter most, and one rule your locality lives by that is written down somewhere. Mesopotamia's cities grew the same way — water first, then written law to hold the city together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Between two rivers, the Sumerian and Babylonian city civilisations build a class society with its own laws, gods and learning — Mesopotamia orders life by written rule at the dawn of cities. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
