# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — History Through Society and Culture: How People Lived (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Between two rivers, the Sumerian and Babylonian city civilisations build a class society with its own laws, gods and learning — Mesopotamia orders life by written rule at the dawn of cities.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a written rule that orders your own school day — law as a city's memory and backbone begins in worlds like this one.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
