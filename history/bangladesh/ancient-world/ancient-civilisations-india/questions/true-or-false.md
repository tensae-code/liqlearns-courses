# Ancient Civilisations: India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peoples of many languages and cultures enter the subcontinent over the ages and struggle, mix and settle into one shared, diverse history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Peoples of many languages and cultures entered South Asia over the ages, and through struggle, mixing and settling they made one shared, diverse history — the subcontinent's past belongs to all of them together.
