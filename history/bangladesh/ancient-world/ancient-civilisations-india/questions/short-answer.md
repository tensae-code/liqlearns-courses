# Ancient Civilisations: India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Peoples of South Asia.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because peoples of many languages and cultures entered South Asia over the ages, and through struggle, mixing and settling they made one shared, diverse history — the subcontinent's past belongs to all of them together. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
