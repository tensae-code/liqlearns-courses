# Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sparta tests newborns and raises seven-year-olds as soldiers; Athens invents democracy; their thirty-year Peloponnesian War exhausts both, and Philip of Macedon swallows the Greek world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Sparta tests its newborns and raises seven-year-olds as soldiers; Athens invents democracy; their thirty-year Peloponnesian War exhausts both — and Philip of Macedon's rising power swallows the Greek world.
