# Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Sparta, Athens, and the fall of the city-states.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because sparta tests its newborns and raises seven-year-olds as soldiers; Athens invents democracy; their thirty-year Peloponnesian War exhausts both — and Philip of Macedon's rising power swallows the Greek world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
