# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one story you know with a god or hero in it, and one question you have asked that has no easy answer. Myths answer the first way, philosophy the second — Greece gave the world both, and this lesson shows the unequal society they grew in.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Ancient Greece is a divided society ruled by a privileged class — yet its gods, myths and thinkers shape mathematics, philosophy and science for everyone who comes after. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
