# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient Greece.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because ancient Greece is a divided society ruled by a privileged class — yet its gods, myths and thinkers shape mathematics, philosophy and science for everyone who comes after. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
