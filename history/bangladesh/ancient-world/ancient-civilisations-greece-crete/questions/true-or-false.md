# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A privileged class rules a divided society while Greek gods, myths and thinkers shape mathematics, philosophy and science for everyone after.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Ancient Greece is a divided society ruled by a privileged class — yet its gods, myths and thinkers shape mathematics, philosophy and science for everyone who comes after.
