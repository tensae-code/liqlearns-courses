# Lesson plan — Bengal: A River Land and Its Old Names

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Bengal: A River Land and Its Old Names
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — History by Inquiry: Your Locality, Bengal and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bengal is a river land ringed by the Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal, known across time as Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela; its people settled beside the great rivers, farmed the flood-fed soil, lived with flood, storm and tiger — and grew from Austric, Dravidian and Arya peoples mixing into one diverse society.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bengal as a region** — Ringed by the Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal, this river land was known as Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela.
2. **Landforms shape settlement** — People settle beside the Ganges, Padma, Brahmaputra and Meghna, farm the flood-fed soil and live with flood, storm and tiger.
3. **The peoples who made Bengal's culture** — Austric, Dravidian and then Arya language peoples enter Bengal in turn, and their mixing lays the diverse foundation of Bengali society, language and custom.
