# Video script — Bengal: A River Land and Its Old Names

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bengal is a river land ringed by the Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal, known across time as Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela; its people settled beside the great rivers, farmed the flood-fed soil, lived with flood, storm and tiger — and grew from Austric, Dravidian and Arya peoples mixing into one diverse society.

Look at the land first, the book says, before any king.

The land then shapes the people.

Who, then, are Bengal's people? The Class Eight book answers with a procession.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
