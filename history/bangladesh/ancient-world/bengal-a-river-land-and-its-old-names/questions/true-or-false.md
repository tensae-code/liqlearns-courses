# Bengal: A River Land and Its Old Names — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ringed by the Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal, this river land was known as Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Bengal is a river land ringed by the Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal, known across time as Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela; its people settled beside the great rivers, farmed the flood-fed soil, lived with flood, storm and tiger — and grew from Austric, Dravidian and Arya peoples mixing into one diverse society.
