# Bengal: A River Land and Its Old Names — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Bengal as a region; Landforms shape settlement; The peoples who made Bengal's culture.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because bengal is a river land ringed by the Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal, known across time as Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela; its people settled beside the great rivers, farmed the flood-fed soil, lived with flood, storm and tiger — and grew from Austric, Dravidian and Arya peoples mixing into one diverse society. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
