# Lesson plan — The Urban Revolution: The First Cities

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Urban Revolution: The First Cities
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — History by Inquiry: Your Locality, Bengal and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Gordon Childe's second revolution raises the first cities — with writing, science and art — and the river-valley civilisations of the Indus, Egypt, Mesopotamia and China, and the Greek city world, show what rivers and seas made possible.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the nearest town's market and signboards — writing, trade and special jobs in one place are the city's ancient signature.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
