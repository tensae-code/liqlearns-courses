# The Urban Revolution: The First Cities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Childe's second revolution raises cities with writing, science and art, touring the Indus, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Chinese and Greek city worlds.
**Answer: True**

**Answer: False** — Gordon Childe's second revolution raises the first cities — with writing, science and art — and the river-valley civilisations of the Indus, Egypt, Mesopotamia and China, and the Greek city world, show what rivers and seas made possible.
