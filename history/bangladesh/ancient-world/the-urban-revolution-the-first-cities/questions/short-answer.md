# The Urban Revolution: The First Cities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Urban Revolution and river-valley civilisations.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because gordon Childe's second revolution raises the first cities — with writing, science and art — and the river-valley civilisations of the Indus, Egypt, Mesopotamia and China, and the Greek city world, show what rivers and seas made possible. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
