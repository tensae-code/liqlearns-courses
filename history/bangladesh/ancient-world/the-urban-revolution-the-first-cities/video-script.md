# Video script — The Urban Revolution: The First Cities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Gordon Childe's second revolution raises the first cities — with writing, science and art — and the river-valley civilisations of the Indus, Egypt, Mesopotamia and China, and the Greek city world, show what rivers and seas made possible.

Childe's second revolution is the Urban Revolution: farming's surplus raises something the world had never seen — the city, with writing, science and art inside its walls.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
