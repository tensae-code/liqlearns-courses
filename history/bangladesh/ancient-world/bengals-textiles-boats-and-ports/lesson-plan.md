# Lesson plan — Bengal's Textiles, Boats and Ports

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Bengal's Textiles, Boats and Ports
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — History Through Work and Trade: Bengal's Economy and the Road to 1971 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bengal's famed fine textiles — praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted by Europeans into the 1600s — and its river-forged boatbuilding clothed and carried a trading people who moved from barter to punch-marked coins and traded through Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar with the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia 2,000 years ago.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — History Through Work and Trade: Bengal's Economy and the Road to 1971 (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Industry: textiles and boats** — Bengal's famed fine textiles, praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted by Europeans into the 1600s, and its river-forged boatbuilding industry.
2. **Trade, commerce and money** — From barter to punch-marked and Gupta, Gauda and Harikela coins, Bengal traded through Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar with the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia 2,000 years ago.

1.
