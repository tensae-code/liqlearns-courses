# Video script — Bengal's Textiles, Boats and Ports

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bengal's famed fine textiles — praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted by Europeans into the 1600s — and its river-forged boatbuilding clothed and carried a trading people who moved from barter to punch-marked coins and traded through Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar with the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia 2,000 years ago.

Bengal worked as beautifully as it farmed.

Making led to trading, and trading grew its tools.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
