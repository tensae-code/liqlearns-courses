# Bengal's Textiles, Boats and Ports — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bengal's famed fine textiles, praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted by Europeans into the 1600s, and its river-forged boatbuilding industry.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Bengal's famed fine textiles — praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted by Europeans into the 1600s — and its river-forged boatbuilding clothed and carried a trading people who moved from barter to punch-marked coins and traded through Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar with the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia 2,000 years ago.
