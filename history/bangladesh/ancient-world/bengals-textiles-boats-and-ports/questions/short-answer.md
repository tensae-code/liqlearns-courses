# Bengal's Textiles, Boats and Ports — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Industry: textiles and boats; Trade, commerce and money.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because bengal's famed fine textiles — praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted by Europeans into the 1600s — and its river-forged boatbuilding clothed and carried a trading people who moved from barter to punch-marked coins and traded through Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar with the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia 2,000 years ago. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
