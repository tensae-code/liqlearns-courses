# Bengal's Political History: From the Mauryas to the Palas

## Do it first

Draw Bengal as a house with a door. First, great emperors from outside hold the keys; then a king of Bengal's own takes them. This lesson tells who held the keys first, and who took them back.

Bengal's political history opens under other people's empires. Maurya emperors reach into the river land, and the Gupta emperor Samudra Gupta captures Banga and Pundra outright. But empires fade. As Gupta power weakens, a king of Bengal's own rises: Shashanka of Karnasubarna, Bengal's first great independent king. After him comes the Pala dynasty — and Bengal's long political story, told in Class Nine reign by reign, has begun.
