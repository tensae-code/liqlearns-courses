# Bengal's Political History: From the Mauryas to the Palas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Empires and the first Bengali kings.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because maurya and Gupta emperors reach into Bengal — Samudra Gupta captures Banga and Pundra — and as Gupta power fades, Shashanka of Karnasubarna becomes Bengal's first great independent king, with the Pala dynasty following him. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
