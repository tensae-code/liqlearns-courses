# Bengal's Political History: From the Mauryas to the Palas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Maurya and Gupta emperors reach into Bengal; as Gupta power fades, Shashanka of Karnasubarna becomes Bengal's first great independent king, and the Pala dynasty follows.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Maurya and Gupta emperors reach into Bengal — Samudra Gupta captures Banga and Pundra — and as Gupta power fades, Shashanka of Karnasubarna becomes Bengal's first great independent king, with the Pala dynasty following him.
