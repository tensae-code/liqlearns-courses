# Lesson plan — Bengal's Political History: From the Mauryas to the Palas

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Bengal's Political History: From the Mauryas to the Palas
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — History Through Power and Politics: Rulers, Resistance and the Nation-State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Maurya and Gupta emperors reach into Bengal — Samudra Gupta captures Banga and Pundra — and as Gupta power fades, Shashanka of Karnasubarna becomes Bengal's first great independent king, with the Pala dynasty following him.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — History Through Power and Politics: Rulers, Resistance and the Nation-State (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Maurya and Gupta emperors reach into Bengal — Samudra Gupta captures Banga and Pundra — and as Gupta power fades, Shashanka of Karnasubarna becomes Bengal's first great independent king, with the Pala dynasty following him.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a team or class that first follows others' captains, then chooses its own — Shashanka's turn is that moment at a kingdom's scale.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
