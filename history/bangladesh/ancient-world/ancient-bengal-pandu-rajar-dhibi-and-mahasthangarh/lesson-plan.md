# Lesson plan — Ancient Bengal: Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Bengal: Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — History by Inquiry: Your Locality, Bengal and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bengal's first settlements grow into its first city at Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh (Pundranagar) on the Karatoya, where Bengal's oldest Brahmi stone inscription (c. 300 BCE), granary rice 3,500 years old, crop lists and copper-plate land grants prove a rich farming land.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — History by Inquiry: Your Locality, Bengal and the Deep Past (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh** — A copper-and-stone-age mound at Pandu Rajar Dhibi and the walled city of Mahasthangarh (Pundranagar) on the Karatoya, with Bengal's oldest Brahmi stone inscription (c. 300 BCE).
2. **The agricultural economy** — 3,500-year-old rice from a granary near Pandu Rajar Dhibi, the Mahasthan Brahmi inscription's crop lists, and copper-plate land grants prove a rich farming Bengal.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
