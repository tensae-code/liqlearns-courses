# Ancient Bengal: Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh; The agricultural economy.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because bengal's first settlements grow into its first city at Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh (Pundranagar) on the Karatoya, where Bengal's oldest Brahmi stone inscription (c. 300 BCE), granary rice 3,500 years old, crop lists and copper-plate land grants prove a rich farming land. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
