# Ancient Bengal: Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A copper-and-stone-age mound at Pandu Rajar Dhibi and the walled city of Mahasthangarh (Pundranagar) on the Karatoya, with Bengal's oldest Brahmi stone inscription (c. 300 BCE).
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Bengal's first settlements grow into its first city at Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh (Pundranagar) on the Karatoya, where Bengal's oldest Brahmi stone inscription (c. 300 BCE), granary rice 3,500 years old, crop lists and copper-plate land grants prove a rich farming land.
