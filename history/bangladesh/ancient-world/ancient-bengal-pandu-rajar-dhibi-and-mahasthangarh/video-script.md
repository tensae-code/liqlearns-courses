# Video script — Ancient Bengal: Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bengal's first settlements grow into its first city at Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh (Pundranagar) on the Karatoya, where Bengal's oldest Brahmi stone inscription (c. 300 BCE), granary rice 3,500 years old, crop lists and copper-plate land grants prove a rich farming land.

At Pandu Rajar Dhibi, the diggers found a mound of the copper-and-stone age — a settlement from Bengal's deep past.

The Class Seven book returns to the same ground and asks what its people ate and grew.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
