# Lesson plan — Bengal's Social and Cultural History

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Bengal's Social and Cultural History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — History Through Society and Culture: How People Lived (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bengal's culture diversifies century by century: harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, and changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace a society forever becoming more varied.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — History Through Society and Culture: How People Lived (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Bengal's culture diversifies century by century: harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, and changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace a society forever becoming more varied.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a family festival's food and dress — small, living pieces of the same cultural history this lesson traces.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
