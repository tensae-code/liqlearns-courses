# Bengal's Social and Cultural History

## Do it first

Name one festival your family keeps, one food that belongs to it, and one thing people wear for it. That small list is social and cultural history at home size — this lesson watches it grow across centuries of Bengal.

How does a culture grow? The book watches Bengal's ordinary centuries: harvest festivals filling the villages with dance and music; temples rising over the rooftops as faith takes built form; food, dress and ritual shifting generation by generation; sculpture changing its face. None of it stands still. Century by century, Bengal's culture diversifies — more peoples, more tastes, more ways of celebrating and believing woven into one society.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bengal's culture diversifies century by century: harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, and changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace a society forever becoming more varied. Give one real example from your own life — a family festival's food and dress — small, living pieces of the same cultural history this lesson traces — and name the lesson's key words: Bengal's Social and Cultural History, festivals, faith, food. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
