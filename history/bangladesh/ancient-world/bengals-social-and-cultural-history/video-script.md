# Video script — Bengal's Social and Cultural History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bengal's culture diversifies century by century: harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, and changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace a society forever becoming more varied.

How does a culture grow? The book watches Bengal's ordinary centuries: harvest festivals filling the villages with dance and music; temples rising over the rooftops as faith takes built form; food, dress and ritual shifting generation by generation; sculpture changing its face.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
