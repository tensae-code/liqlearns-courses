# Hands-on — Bengal's Social and Cultural History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one festival your family keeps, one food that belongs to it, and one thing people wear for it. That small list is social and cultural history at home size — this lesson watches it grow across centuries of Bengal.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Bengal's culture diversifies century by century: harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, and changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace a society forever becoming more varied. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
