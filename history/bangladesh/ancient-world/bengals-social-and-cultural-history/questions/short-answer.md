# Bengal's Social and Cultural History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Festivals, faith, food and building.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because bengal's culture diversifies century by century: harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, and changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace a society forever becoming more varied. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
