# Bengal's Social and Cultural History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace Bengal's culture diversifying century by century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Bengal's culture diversifies century by century: harvest festivals with dance and music, temples rising over villages, and changing food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace a society forever becoming more varied.
