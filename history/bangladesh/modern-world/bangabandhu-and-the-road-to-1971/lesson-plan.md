**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — History Through Work and Trade: Bengal's Economy and the Road to 1971 (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bangabandhu leads: 1966 to 7 March** — Mujib leads the 6-Point movement of 1966, the mass uprising of 1969 and the 1970 election victory, and declares on 7 March that 'the struggle this time is the struggle for our emancipation'.
2. **Mujib's early struggle** — Born in Tungipara in 1920, Sheikh Mujib fights the Urdu-only policy from 1948, is expelled and jailed, and hunger-strikes in prison in February 1952.
3. **Humanitarian ideals and secular spirit** — Mujib's love for all people regardless of language, religion or caste, and his rule of nation before self, are traced to Bengal's thousand-year humanitarian tradition.
4. **From Pakistan to the Language Movement** — Pakistan tries to impose Urdu; Dhaka University students protest from December 1947 and Dhirendranath Dutta defies the Constituent Assembly.
