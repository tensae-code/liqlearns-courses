# Bangabandhu and the Road to 1971 — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bangabandhu leads: 1966 to 7 March — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Mujib's early struggle — b)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Humanitarian ideals and secular spirit — c)

**4.** (Sub lesson 4) From Pakistan to the Language Movement — d)


Meanings:
a) Mujib leads the 6-Point movement of 1966, the mass uprising of 1969 and the 1970 election victory, and declares on 7 March that 'the struggle this time is the struggle for our emancipation'.
b) Born in Tungipara in 1920, Sheikh Mujib fights the Urdu-only policy from 1948, is expelled and jailed, and hunger-strikes in prison in February 1952.
c) Mujib's love for all people regardless of language, religion or caste, and his rule of nation before self, are traced to Bengal's thousand-year humanitarian tradition.
d) Pakistan tries to impose Urdu; Dhaka University students protest from December 1947 and Dhirendranath Dutta defies the Constituent Assembly.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c; 4. d
