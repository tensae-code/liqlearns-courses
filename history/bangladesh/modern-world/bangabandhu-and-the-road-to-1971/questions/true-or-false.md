# Bangabandhu and the Road to 1971 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Mujib leads the 6-Point movement of 1966, the mass uprising of 1969 and the 1970 election victory, and declares on 7 March that 'the struggle this time is the struggle for our emancipation'.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Sheikh Mujibur Rahman — Bangabandhu — fights the Urdu-only policy from 1948, is expelled and jailed, hunger-strikes in prison in February 1952, then leads the 6-Point movement of 1966, the mass uprising of 1969 and the 1970 election victory, declaring on 7 March that 'the struggle this time is the struggle for our emancipation' — a road rooted in Bengal's thousand-year humanitarian and secular spirit, opening when Dhaka's students first defied Pakistan's language policy.
