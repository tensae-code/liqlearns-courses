# Bangabandhu and the Road to 1971 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Bangabandhu leads: 1966 to 7 March; Mujib's early struggle; Humanitarian ideals and secular spirit; From Pakistan to the Language Movement.

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because sheikh Mujibur Rahman — Bangabandhu — fights the Urdu-only policy from 1948, is expelled and jailed, hunger-strikes in prison in February 1952, then leads the 6-Point movement of 1966, the mass uprising of 1969 and the 1970 election victory, declaring on 7 March that 'the struggle this time is the struggle for our emancipation' — a road rooted in Bengal's thousand-year humanitarian and secular spirit, opening when Dhaka's students first defied Pakistan's language policy. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
