# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The nation-state.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because nation-states rise in England, Spain, France, Russia and Prussia; the French Revolution of 1789 defines the idea through Renan — and nationalism then multiplies independent states across the world, Bengal's own included. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
