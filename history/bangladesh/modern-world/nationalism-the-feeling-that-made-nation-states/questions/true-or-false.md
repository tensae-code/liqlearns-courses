# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nation-states rise in England, Spain, France, Russia and Prussia, the French Revolution of 1789 defines the idea through Renan, and nationalism then multiplies independent states across the world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Nation-states rise in England, Spain, France, Russia and Prussia; the French Revolution of 1789 defines the idea through Renan — and nationalism then multiplies independent states across the world, Bengal's own included.
