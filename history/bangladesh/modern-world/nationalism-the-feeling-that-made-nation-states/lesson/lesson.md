# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

## Do it first

Write down what makes your country one country in your own words: a language, a flag, a shared story? You have just touched the feeling this lesson studies — the feeling that built the modern map.

A new kind of state enters history. Nation-states rise in England, Spain, France, Russia and Prussia — states claiming to embody one people. The French Revolution of 1789 gives the idea its sharp definition, carried in Renan's famous account of what a nation is. From Europe the feeling spreads and multiplies: across the world, peoples who feel themselves nations demand states of their own — the same demand that, in time, raises Bangladesh.
