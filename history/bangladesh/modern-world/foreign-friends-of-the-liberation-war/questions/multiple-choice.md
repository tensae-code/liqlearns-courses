# Foreign Friends of the Liberation War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “The journalists” is right?
a) 26-year-old Simon Dring hides in Dhaka's Hotel Intercontinental after foreign reporters are expelled on 25 March 1971 and cables the massacre to The Daily Telegraph  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Bangladesh's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Bangladesh's Liberation War was carried to the world by foreign friends: Simon Dring's cables from a Dhaka hotel, the BBC and Akashvani, India's shelter for about one crore refugees under Indira Gandhi, Soviet diplomatic backing, Senator Edward Kennedy's camp visits, and George Harrison singing for Bangladesh.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Bangladesh has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
