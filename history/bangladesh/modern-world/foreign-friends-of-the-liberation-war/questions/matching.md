# Foreign Friends of the Liberation War — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The journalists — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Refugees, diplomacy and song — b)


Meanings:
a) 26-year-old Simon Dring hides in Dhaka's Hotel Intercontinental after foreign reporters are expelled on 25 March 1971 and cables the massacre to The Daily Telegraph.
b) About one crore refugees flood into India, which shelters and speaks for them under Indira Gandhi; the Soviet Union backs Bangladesh, Edward Kennedy visits the camps, and George Harrison sings for Bangladesh.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
