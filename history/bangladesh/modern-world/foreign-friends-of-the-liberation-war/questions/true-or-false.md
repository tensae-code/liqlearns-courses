# Foreign Friends of the Liberation War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) 26-year-old Simon Dring hides in Dhaka's Hotel Intercontinental after foreign reporters are expelled on 25 March 1971 and cables the massacre to The Daily Telegraph.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Bangladesh's Liberation War was carried to the world by foreign friends: Simon Dring's cables from a Dhaka hotel, the BBC and Akashvani, India's shelter for about one crore refugees under Indira Gandhi, Soviet diplomatic backing, Senator Edward Kennedy's camp visits, and George Harrison singing for Bangladesh.
