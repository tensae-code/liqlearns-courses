# Foreign Friends of the Liberation War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The journalists; Refugees, diplomacy and song.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because bangladesh's Liberation War was carried to the world by foreign friends: Simon Dring's cables from a Dhaka hotel, the BBC and Akashvani, India's shelter for about one crore refugees under Indira Gandhi, Soviet diplomatic backing, Senator Edward Kennedy's camp visits, and George Harrison singing for Bangladesh. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
