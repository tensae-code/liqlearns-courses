# Video script — Foreign Friends of the Liberation War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bangladesh's Liberation War was carried to the world by foreign friends: Simon Dring's cables from a Dhaka hotel, the BBC and Akashvani, India's shelter for about one crore refugees under Indira Gandhi, Soviet diplomatic backing, Senator Edward Kennedy's camp visits, and George Harrison singing for Bangladesh.

When the Pakistan army struck on 25 March 1971, foreign reporters were expelled from Dhaka.

The war drove about one crore refugees into India, which sheltered, fed and spoke for them under Indira Gandhi.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
