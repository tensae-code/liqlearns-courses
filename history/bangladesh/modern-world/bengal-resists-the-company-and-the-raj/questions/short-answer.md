# Bengal Resists the Company and the Raj — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Resistance to British rule.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because bengal resists British rule with arms and with ideas: Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, the indigo ryots, Titumir's 1831 rising and the sepoys of 1857 fight the Company and the Raj, while Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fight with ideas — until the British are forced to leave in 1947. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
