# Bengal Resists the Company and the Raj — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, indigo ryots, Titumir's 1831 rising and the sepoys of 1857 fight the Company and the Raj, while Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fight with ideas, until the British leave in 1947.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Bengal resists British rule with arms and with ideas: Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, the indigo ryots, Titumir's 1831 rising and the sepoys of 1857 fight the Company and the Raj, while Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fight with ideas — until the British are forced to leave in 1947.
