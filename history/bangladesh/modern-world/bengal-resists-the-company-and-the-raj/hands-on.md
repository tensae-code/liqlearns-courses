# Hands-on — Bengal Resists the Company and the Raj

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two columns: 'fought with arms' and 'fought with ideas'. Before reading, put any resistance you already know of into a column. Bengal used both columns for two centuries — this lesson fills them in.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Bengal resists British rule with arms and with ideas: Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, the indigo ryots, Titumir's 1831 rising and the sepoys of 1857 fight the Company and the Raj, while Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fight with ideas — until the British are forced to leave in 1947. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
