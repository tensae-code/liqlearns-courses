# Video script — Bengal Resists the Company and the Raj

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bengal resists British rule with arms and with ideas: Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, the indigo ryots, Titumir's 1831 rising and the sepoys of 1857 fight the Company and the Raj, while Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fight with ideas — until the British are forced to leave in 1947.

British rule in Bengal is resisted from its first century to its last.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
