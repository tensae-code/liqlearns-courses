# Lesson plan — Bengal Resists the Company and the Raj

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bengal Resists the Company and the Raj
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — History Through Power and Politics: Rulers, Resistance and the Nation-State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bengal resists British rule with arms and with ideas: Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, the indigo ryots, Titumir's 1831 rising and the sepoys of 1857 fight the Company and the Raj, while Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fight with ideas — until the British are forced to leave in 1947.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — History Through Power and Politics: Rulers, Resistance and the Nation-State (recommended, never forced).
