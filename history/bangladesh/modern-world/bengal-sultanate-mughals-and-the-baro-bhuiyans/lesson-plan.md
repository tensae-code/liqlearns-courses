# Lesson plan — Bengal Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bengal Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — History Through Power and Politics: Rulers, Resistance and the Nation-State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate — 'Shah-i Bangalah' — and centuries later, though Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576, the Baro Bhuiyan zamindars under Isa Khan keep resisting Mughal subadars river by river.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate — 'Shah-i Bangalah' — and centuries later, though Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576, the Baro Bhuiyan zamindars under Isa Khan keep resisting Mughal subadars river by river.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a home team that knows every lane of its own ground — local knowledge against a great empire is the Baro Bhuiyans' story.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
