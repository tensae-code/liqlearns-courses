# Bengal Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Sultanate and Mughal Bengal.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate — 'Shah-i Bangalah' — and centuries later, though Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576, the Baro Bhuiyan zamindars under Isa Khan keep resisting Mughal subadars river by river. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
