# Bengal Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate ('Shah-i Bangalah'); Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576, yet the Baro Bhuiyans under Isa Khan keep resisting Mughal subadars river by river.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate — 'Shah-i Bangalah' — and centuries later, though Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576, the Baro Bhuiyan zamindars under Isa Khan keep resisting Mughal subadars river by river.
