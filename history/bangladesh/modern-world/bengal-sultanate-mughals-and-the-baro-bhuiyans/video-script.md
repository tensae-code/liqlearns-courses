# Video script — Bengal Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate — 'Shah-i Bangalah' — and centuries later, though Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576, the Baro Bhuiyan zamindars under Isa Khan keep resisting Mughal subadars river by river.

Bengal becomes a sultanate of its own when Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate and takes the title 'Shah-i Bangalah' — King of Bengal.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
