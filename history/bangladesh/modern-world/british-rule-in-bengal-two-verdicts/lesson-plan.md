# Lesson plan — British Rule in Bengal: Two Verdicts

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** British Rule in Bengal: Two Verdicts
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — History Through Society and Culture: How People Lived (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** British rule in Bengal carries two verdicts side by side: a 1911 British weekly boasts of the peace and security given to India, while economist Angus Maddison counts India's share of world wealth collapsing to about 3 percent by the time the British left.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — History Through Society and Culture: How People Lived (recommended, never forced).
