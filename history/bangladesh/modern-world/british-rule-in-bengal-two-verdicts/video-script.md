# Video script — British Rule in Bengal: Two Verdicts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
British rule in Bengal carries two verdicts side by side: a 1911 British weekly boasts of the peace and security given to India, while economist Angus Maddison counts India's share of world wealth collapsing to about 3 percent by the time the British left.

The Class Eight book ends its Bengal survey by putting two verdicts on British rule face to face.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
