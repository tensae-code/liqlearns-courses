# British Rule in Bengal: Two Verdicts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A 1911 British weekly boasts of peace and security given to India, while Angus Maddison counts India's share of world wealth collapsing to about 3 percent by the time the British left.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — British rule in Bengal carries two verdicts side by side: a 1911 British weekly boasts of the peace and security given to India, while economist Angus Maddison counts India's share of world wealth collapsing to about 3 percent by the time the British left.
