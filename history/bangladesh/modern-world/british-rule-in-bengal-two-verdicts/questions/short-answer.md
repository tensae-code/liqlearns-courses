# British Rule in Bengal: Two Verdicts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Some views on British rule.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because british rule in Bengal carries two verdicts side by side: a 1911 British weekly boasts of the peace and security given to India, while economist Angus Maddison counts India's share of world wealth collapsing to about 3 percent by the time the British left. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
