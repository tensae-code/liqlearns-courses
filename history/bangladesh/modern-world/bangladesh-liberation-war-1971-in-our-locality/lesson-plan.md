# Lesson plan — The Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 in Our Locality

**Course:** History — Bangladesh · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 in Our Locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — History by Inquiry: Your Locality, Bengal and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Liberation War of 1971 is first met not as a date but as a place: the killing field in the children's own village, where 46 local men and women were killed, and the story of Azad, captured by the Pakistani forces, whose mother never stopped waiting — one martyr standing for thousands unknown.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Liberation War of 1971 is first met not as a date but as a place: the killing field in the children's own village, where 46 local men and women were killed, and the story of Azad, captured by the Pakistani forces, whose mother never stopped waiting — one martyr standing for thousands unknown.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a memorial, a graveyard corner or an elder's story in your own locality — the war's history stands in places like these.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
