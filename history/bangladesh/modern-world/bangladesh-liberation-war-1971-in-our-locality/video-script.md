# Video script — The Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 in Our Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Liberation War of 1971 is first met not as a date but as a place: the killing field in the children's own village, where 46 local men and women were killed, and the story of Azad, captured by the Pakistani forces, whose mother never stopped waiting — one martyr standing for thousands unknown.

The class's inquiry into its own locality reaches the village's 1971 killing field — the ground where 46 local men and women were killed in the Liberation War.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
