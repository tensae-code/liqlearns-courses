# The Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 in Our Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The killing field and the story of Azad.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because the Liberation War of 1971 is first met not as a date but as a place: the killing field in the children's own village, where 46 local men and women were killed, and the story of Azad, captured by the Pakistani forces, whose mother never stopped waiting — one martyr standing for thousands unknown. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
