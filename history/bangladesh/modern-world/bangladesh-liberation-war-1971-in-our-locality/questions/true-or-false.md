# The Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 in Our Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils find their village's 1971 killing field where 46 local men and women were killed, and hear of Azad, whose mother never stopped waiting.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — The Liberation War of 1971 is first met not as a date but as a place: the killing field in the children's own village, where 46 local men and women were killed, and the story of Azad, captured by the Pakistani forces, whose mother never stopped waiting — one martyr standing for thousands unknown.
