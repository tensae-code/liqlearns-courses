Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The killing field and the story of Azad” is right?
a) Pupils find their village's 1971 killing field where 46 local men and women were killed, and hear of Azad, whose mother never stopped waiting  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Bangladesh's book

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Liberation War of 1971 is first met not as a date but as a place: the killing field in the children's own village, where 46 local men and women were killed, and the story of Azad, captured by the Pakistani forces, whose mother never stopped waiting — one martyr standing for thousands unknown.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Bangladesh has no way of its own
