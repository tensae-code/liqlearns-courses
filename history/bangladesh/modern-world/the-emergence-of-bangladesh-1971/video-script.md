# Video script — The Emergence of Bangladesh 1971

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bangladesh emerges at the end of a journey of millennia — from the ancient janapadas through Pala, Sena, Muslim and Mughal rulers, British extraction, and the 1947 partition that made East Pakistan a colony of the West — until Bangabandhu's movement wins independence in 1971, the war's spirit is carried into rebuilding, and the land's name grows from Banga to Bangladesh.

Class Six closes with the whole road in one view.

Class Seven walks the same road asking what the land itself was called.

The war won, its spirit had to be carried home.

Class Nine binds the land's name to its leader's.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
