# The Emergence of Bangladesh 1971 — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The journey of millennia — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The long arc — b)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The spirit, the homecoming, the rebuilding — c)

**4.** (Sub lesson 4) From Banga to Bangladesh, from Bangabandhu to Bishwabandhu — d)


Meanings:
a) From the ancient janapadas through Pala, Sena, Muslim and Mughal rulers, British extraction and the 1947 partition, to Bangabandhu's movement and independence in 1971.
b) Banga janapada becomes Bangal, Bangala, Bengal under Deva, Chandra, Pala and Sena dynasties, Mauryas, Guptas, Sultanate and Mughals, then Europeans, and finally Pakistani exploitation from 1947 — all ending in 1971.
c) Bangabandhu lands in independent Bangladesh on 10 January 1972 and turns the war's spirit into students farming, building and teaching literacy.
d) The land's name grows from Banga to Bangladesh as Bangabandhu becomes a symbol of all oppressed peoples and receives the Joliot-Curie Medal for Peace on 23 May 1973.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c; 4. d
