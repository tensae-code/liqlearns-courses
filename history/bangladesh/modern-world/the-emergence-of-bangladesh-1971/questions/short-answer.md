# The Emergence of Bangladesh 1971 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The journey of millennia; The long arc; The spirit, the homecoming, the rebuilding; From Banga to Bangladesh, from Bangabandhu to Bishwabandhu.

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Bangladesh?
**Answer:** Because bangladesh emerges at the end of a journey of millennia — from the ancient janapadas through Pala, Sena, Muslim and Mughal rulers, British extraction, and the 1947 partition that made East Pakistan a colony of the West — until Bangabandhu's movement wins independence in 1971, the war's spirit is carried into rebuilding, and the land's name grows from Banga to Bangladesh. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
