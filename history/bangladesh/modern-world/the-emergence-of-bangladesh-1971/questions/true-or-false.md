# The Emergence of Bangladesh 1971 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) From the ancient janapadas through Pala, Sena, Muslim and Mughal rulers, British extraction and the 1947 partition, to Bangabandhu's movement and independence in 1971.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Bangladesh.
**Answer: False** — Bangladesh emerges at the end of a journey of millennia — from the ancient janapadas through Pala, Sena, Muslim and Mughal rulers, British extraction, and the 1947 partition that made East Pakistan a colony of the West — until Bangabandhu's movement wins independence in 1971, the war's spirit is carried into rebuilding, and the land's name grows from Banga to Bangladesh.
