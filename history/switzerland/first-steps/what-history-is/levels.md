# What History Is — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — History, in this course, is read the way this schoolbook was built: like a public history magazine — you step into the victims' place, you also hear the authorities and the bystanders, you work the original sources yourself, and in the end you must reach your own judgement. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
