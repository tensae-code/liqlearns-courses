# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How was the schoolbook behind this course built?
a) like a public history magazine, so pupils work the sources themselves  b) as a list of dates to memorise  c) as a verdict pupils must copy  d) as a story with only one voice
**Answer: a) like a public history magazine, so pupils work the sources themselves** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What must the reader do at the end of each case?
a) reach their own judgement  b) repeat the official's judgement  c) wait for the teacher's verdict  d) forget the case
**Answer: a) reach their own judgement** — That is what this lesson teaches.
