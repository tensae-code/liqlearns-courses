# Video script — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Heritage is what a country chooses to remember in public: Graubünden opened a place of remembrance above Chur in 2018, the Rätisches Museum mounted a special exhibition, a special stamp funded emergency aid — and this schoolbook itself was made as an act of remembrance, so that the victims of the welfare measures are not forgotten twice.

The five fates in this course happened in one canton, Graubünden — and that canton has begun, publicly, to remember them. In 2018 it opened a place of remembrance above Chur, its capital: a place in the landscape itself where the victims of the welfare measures are named and honoured, open to anyone who climbs to it.

The remembering took other forms too. The Rätisches Museum, the canton's museum, mounted a special exhibition. A special postage stamp carried the remembrance into every post office and funded emergency aid for victims in need. And the last sign is the one in the pupil's own hands: this schoolbook itself was made as an act of remembrance — history teaching as a memorial. That is what heritage is, in this lesson: not only castles and old songs, but a country's decision about what must not be forgotten — including, hardest of all, what the country itself did wrong.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Switzerland.
