# Lesson plan — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

**Course:** History — Switzerland · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Remembering: Signs of Remembrance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Heritage is what a country chooses to remember in public: Graubünden opened a place of remembrance above Chur in 2018, the Rätisches Museum mounted a special exhibition, a special stamp funded emergency aid — and this schoolbook itself was made as an act of remembrance, so that the victims of the welfare measures are not forgotten twice.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Remembering: Signs of Remembrance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks remembrance is only about old buildings → here heritage is a country's public memory of its own wrongdoing: a place, an exhibition, a stamp, and a schoolbook itself.
- Go back to the one thing: Heritage is what a country chooses to remember in public: Graubünden opened a place of remembrance above Chur in 2018, the Rätisches Museum mounted a special exhibition, a special stamp funded emergency aid — and this schoolbook itself was made as an act of remembrance, so that the victims of the welfare measures are not forgotten twice. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
