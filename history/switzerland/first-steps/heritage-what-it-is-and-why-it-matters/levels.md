# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Heritage is what a country chooses to remember in public: Graubünden opened a place of remembrance above Chur in 2018, the Rätisches Museum mounted a special exhibition, a special stamp funded emergency aid — and this schoolbook itself was made as an act of remembrance, so that the victims of the welfare measures are not forgotten twice. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
