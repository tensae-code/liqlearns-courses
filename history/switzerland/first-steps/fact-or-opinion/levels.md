# Fact or Opinion — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each is proved in order, the one before opening the next. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Words written as fact can carry opinion inside them: home reports praised Ursula Waser as a nice girl, then blamed her wandering on her 'blood' — and those same judgements, treated as fact, decided a court case against her twenty years later; a home's glowing reports and a survivor's memoir can describe the same house in opposite ways. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
