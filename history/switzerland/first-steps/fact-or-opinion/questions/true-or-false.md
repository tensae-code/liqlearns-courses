# Fact or Opinion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The court believed Ursula over the old home reports. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The court believed the reports — the judgements written as fact — against her.

**2.** In 2016, the victim, her daughter, a researcher and the home's director each drew a different balance of the home. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
