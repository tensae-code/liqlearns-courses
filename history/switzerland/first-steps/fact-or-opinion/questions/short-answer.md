# Fact or Opinion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a facade, in this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The face an institution shows the world — here, the happy house of the newsletter and inspections — which may hide what stands behind it.

**2.** Why does it matter whether a sentence in a report is fact or opinion? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Because written judgements were treated as fact and decided real outcomes — a court case against Ursula twenty years later.
