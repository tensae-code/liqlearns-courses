# Fact or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the reports blame Ursula's wandering on? (Sub lesson 1)
a) her 'blood' — a judgement about her people, written as if it were fact  b) a broken leg  c) a long journey home  d) her school lessons
**Answer: a) her 'blood' — a judgement about her people, written as if it were fact** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the 'Gott hilft' home appear in its donor newsletter and inspection reports? (Sub lesson 2)
a) as a happy house with glowing reports  b) as a closed building  c) as an empty farm  d) as a prison
**Answer: a) as a happy house with glowing reports** — That is what this lesson teaches.
