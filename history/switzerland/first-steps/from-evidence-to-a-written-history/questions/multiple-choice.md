# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What survives of the Albin family? (Sub lesson 1)
a) two home-visit reports, a psychiatric report and a lawyer's appeal  b) the family's own diary  c) the children's letters home  d) a film of the family
**Answer: a) two home-visit reports, a psychiatric report and a lawyer's appeal** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does Cornelia Studer admit at the start of her memoir? (Sub lesson 2)
a) her lived and merely heard episodes have merged in her memory  b) that she invented everything  c) that she never lived in a home  d) that the files are all false
**Answer: a) her lived and merely heard episodes have merged in her memory** — That is what this lesson teaches.
