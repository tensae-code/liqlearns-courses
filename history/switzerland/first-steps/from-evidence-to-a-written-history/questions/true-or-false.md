# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The cantonal government refused the Albin family's dissolution in 1953. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — It approved the dissolution in 1953, on that thin paper evidence.

**2.** A memoir can prove what a childhood felt like, even where it cannot prove every date. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
