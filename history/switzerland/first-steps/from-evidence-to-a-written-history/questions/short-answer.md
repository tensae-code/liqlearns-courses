# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who wrote the appeal for the Albin family? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The lawyer Gaudenz Canova.

**2.** Why must a witness account be judged carefully? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Because memory merges lived and merely heard episodes, and the official files differ from both — each source proves some things and not others.
