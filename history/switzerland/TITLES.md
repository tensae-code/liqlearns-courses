# History — Switzerland course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Switzerland's
own — built from the canton of Graubünden's teaching package
*Sorge oder Zwang?* ("Care or Coercion?"): a pupil reader
telling five true life-stories, with a teacher companion at
Sekundarstufe I and another at Sekundarstufe II (German
originals; Italian and Romansh parallel editions placed once
under them). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside
Levels 1–3, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grades the books come from live only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the package's: first learn how this
history is read — voices, evidence, fact against opinion —
then walk the five fates in the reader's order, each deepened
by the companions, and close with the canton's own signs of
remembrance. That ladder IS the path order (recommended,
never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — first steps: how this history is read**, in
`first-steps/`:

- What History Is (a schoolbook built like a public history
  magazine: step into the victims' place, hear authorities
  and bystanders, work the original sources, reach your own
  judgement) · From Evidence to a Written History (the Albin
  family in four documents; what Cornelia Studer's memoir can
  and cannot prove) · Fact or Opinion (reports that blamed a
  girl's wandering on her "blood" decided a court case; a
  home's facade against its memoir).

**Level 2 — care or coercion: the five fates**, in
`modern-world/`:

- Switzerland's Compulsory Welfare Measures: An Overview
  (the poor sorted "worthy" and "unworthy"; the 1912 Civil
  Code; the peak before the AHV 1948; the 1974 human-rights
  convention) · The Albin Family Dissolved (eight children
  taken in stages 1949–1953; Canova's appeal answered point
  by point) · Uschi Waser and the Yenish Children of the Road
  (taken at seven months; moved 22 times; 586 children,
  1926–1973) · Cornelia Studer and the 'Gott Hilft' Children's
  Home (eight years behind a praised facade; the 1955
  inspection's top marks) · Florian Branger and Administrative
  Detention (detention by office decision, without a court;
  Realta; Loosli's 1938 charge) · Ruedi Hofer, the Hired-Out
  Children, and Whether Amends Can Be Made (over thirty
  farms; over 100,000 hired-out children; the 2017 law and
  the "solidarity contribution").

**Level 3 — remembering: signs of remembrance**, in
`first-steps/`:

- Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters (the place of
  remembrance above Chur, 2018; the Rätisches Museum's
  exhibition; the special stamp; the schoolbook itself as an
  act of remembrance).
