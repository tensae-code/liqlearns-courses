# Uschi Waser and the Yenish Children of the Road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The scheme's aim was to help travelling families keep travelling together. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — Its aim was to end the travelling way of life by taking the children and making them settled.

**2.** Journalist Hans Caprez's 1972 articles forced the scheme to close. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
