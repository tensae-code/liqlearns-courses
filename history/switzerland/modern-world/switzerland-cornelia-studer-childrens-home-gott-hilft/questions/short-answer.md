# Cornelia Studer and the 'Gott Hilft' Children's Home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two punishments or harms Cornelia describes in the home. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pre-dawn labour, beatings, hunger used as punishment, unreported abuse (any two).

**2.** Who was Werner Würmli? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A carer in the home — untrained and tubercular like other overwhelmed staff, who later confessed a castration as a terrible misjudgement.
