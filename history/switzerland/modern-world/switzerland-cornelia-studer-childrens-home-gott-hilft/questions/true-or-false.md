# Cornelia Studer and the 'Gott Hilft' Children's Home — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 'Gott hilft' homes were financed without the children's labour. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The network was self-financing and ran on children's farm labour and unpaid carers.

**2.** Graubünden counted 121 children's homes in 1955. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
