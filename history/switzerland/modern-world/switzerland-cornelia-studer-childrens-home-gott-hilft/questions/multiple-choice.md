# Cornelia Studer and the 'Gott Hilft' Children's Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why were Cornelia and her brother sent to the home, as the reason was given? (Sub lesson 1)
a) for 'rest and order'  b) to learn farming as a trade  c) for a summer holiday  d) to visit relatives
**Answer: a) for 'rest and order'** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the 1955 cantonal inspection judge the 'Gott hilft' home? (Sub lesson 2)
a) it gave the home top marks  b) it closed the home at once  c) it freed all the children  d) it never took place
**Answer: a) it gave the home top marks** — That is what this lesson teaches.
