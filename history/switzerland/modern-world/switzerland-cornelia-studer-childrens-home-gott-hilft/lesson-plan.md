## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Behind the facades of a home** — Seven-year-old Cornelia and her brother are sent to the 'Gott hilft' home at Zizers for 'rest and order' and find eight years of pre-dawn labour, beatings, hunger punishments and unreported abuse, which she only writes down fifty years later.
2. **A network of homes, inspected and praised** — A Salvation-Army couple founds a self-financing home network in 1916 that runs on children's farm labour and unpaid carers, Graubünden counts 121 homes in 1955, and cantonal inspection arrives that year but still gives 'Gott hilft' top marks.
3. **The 1955 inspection, memory, and a carer's life** — The full 1955 inspection praises the home's people and passes over its punishments, Studer's own preface warns how memory mixes with others' tales, and carer Werner Würmli's life shows untrained, tubercular staff overwhelmed — including a castration he later confessed as a terrible misjudgement.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
