# Cornelia Studer and the 'Gott Hilft' Children's Home — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each is proved in order, the one before opening the next. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Seven-year-old Cornelia Studer and her brother were sent to the 'Gott hilft' home at Zizers 'for rest and order' and found eight years of pre-dawn labour, beatings, hunger punishments and unreported abuse — inside a self-financing home network, founded in 1916, that ran on children's farm labour and still won top marks from cantonal inspectors in 1955. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
