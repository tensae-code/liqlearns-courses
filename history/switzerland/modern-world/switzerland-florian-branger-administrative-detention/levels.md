# Florian Branger and Administrative Detention — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each is proved in order, the one before opening the next. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Sailor Florian Branger came home to Chur and collected small convictions; the guardianship authority answered not with trials but with ever longer administrative stays — in the Realta work institution, prison and a psychiatric clinic — under a system that, from 1840, locked the 'dissolute' and 'work-shy' into institutions by office decision, without a court, some ten years of his life in detention on top of his sentences. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
