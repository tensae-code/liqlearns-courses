# Florian Branger and Administrative Detention — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Branger's administrative detention came on top of his short prison sentences — some ten years of his life. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Realta refused to defend chaining inmates who ran away. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Realta defended its practice of chaining inmates who ran away — and refused to take Branger back.
