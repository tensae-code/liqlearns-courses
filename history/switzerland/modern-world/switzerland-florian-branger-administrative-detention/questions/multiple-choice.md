# Florian Branger and Administrative Detention — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How were Branger's long stays ordered? (Sub lesson 1)
a) administratively — by office decision, without a court  b) by juries after long trials  c) by his own request  d) by the sailors' union
**Answer: a) administratively — by office decision, without a court** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Carl Albert Loosli call Swiss administrative detention in 1938? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the country's own concentration camps  b) a model welfare system  c) a sailor's holiday  d) a school for workers
**Answer: a) the country's own concentration camps** — That is what this lesson teaches.
