# Florian Branger and Administrative Detention — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three kinds of place Branger was administratively sent to. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Realta work institution, prison, and a psychiatric clinic.

**2.** What did archaeologists read in the Realta institution cemetery in 2016? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The skeletons of 103 inmates buried there.
