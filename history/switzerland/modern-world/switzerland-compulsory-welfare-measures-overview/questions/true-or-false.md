# Switzerland's Compulsory Welfare Measures: An Overview — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The fight stayed aimed at poverty itself, never at the poor. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The fight turned from poverty onto the poor themselves — families judged 'unworthy' could lose their children.

**2.** Switzerland joined the European human-rights convention in 1974. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
