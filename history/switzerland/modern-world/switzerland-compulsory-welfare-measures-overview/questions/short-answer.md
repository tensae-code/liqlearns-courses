# Switzerland's Compulsory Welfare Measures: An Overview — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What powers did the 1912 Civil Code give the guardianship offices? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Wide preventive powers — to step in before harm was done, stretchable enough to reach almost anywhere poverty showed.

**2.** Name two things that helped end the era. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The post-war boom and work; social insurance (AHV 1948); women's suffrage; the 1968 generation; the 1974 human-rights convention (any two).
