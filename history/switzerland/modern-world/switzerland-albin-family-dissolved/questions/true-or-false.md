# The Albin Family Dissolved — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the children were taken, the parents were allowed regular contact with them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — All contact was blocked; both parents died without the family ever reuniting.

**2.** The Grisons government answered Canova's appeal point by point and rejected it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
