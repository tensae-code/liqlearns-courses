# The Albin Family Dissolved — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what years were the Albin children taken away? (Sub lesson 1)
a) in stages, 1949–1953  b) all on one day in 1949  c) in 1912  d) after 1974
**Answer: a) in stages, 1949–1953** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did other poor families, judged salvageable, receive instead of dissolution? (Sub lesson 2)
a) beds, furniture and a 'family rescue'  b) a court trial  c) a one-way ticket  d) nothing at all
**Answer: a) beds, furniture and a 'family rescue'** — That is what this lesson teaches.
