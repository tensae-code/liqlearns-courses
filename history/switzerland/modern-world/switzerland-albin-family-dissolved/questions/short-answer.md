# The Albin Family Dissolved — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who appealed against taking the Albins' last four children? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The lawyer Gaudenz Canova.

**2.** Against what were the Albins measured and found failing? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The model family built into the 1912 Civil Code — husband as head, orderly household — with 'neglect' as the stretchable word used against them.
