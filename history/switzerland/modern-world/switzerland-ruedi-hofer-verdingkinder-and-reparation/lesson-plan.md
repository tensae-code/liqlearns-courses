## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Passed to over thirty farms** — Ruedi Hofer is passed to over 30 farm places as a child labourer, loses half a hand at eight in an unexplained explosion, is exploited and abused, never gets a steady job, and finds his gift instead in rescuing people with his dogs.
2. **When does a bad time end?** — Hofer's own drawings show the scythe strapped to his ruined hand, he writes that money cannot undo a childhood, and the victims' counsellor explains why the state deliberately calls its 25,000 francs a 'solidarity contribution', not compensation.
3. **Over 100,000 hired-out children** — Over 100,000 Swiss children are hired out, Grisons children herd seasonally or walk a week to labour in Upper Swabia, the 1955 foster ordinance leaves the seasonal children out, and only the 2017 federal law (AFZFG) names the wrong and pays the solidarity contribution.
4. **The initiative against the law** — Guido Fluri's initiative demands a 500-million fund, parliament answers with the 2017 law and a 300-million fund paying at most 25,000 francs, minister Sommaruga apologises in 2013 saying looking away is also an act, and a 2016 postage stamp carries the remembrance into every post office.
