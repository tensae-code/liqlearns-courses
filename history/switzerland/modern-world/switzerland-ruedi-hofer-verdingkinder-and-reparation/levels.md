# Ruedi Hofer, the Hired-Out Children, and Whether Amends Can Be Made — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each is proved in order, the one before opening the next. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Ruedi Hofer was passed to over 30 farm places as a Verdingkind — a child hired out as labour — lost half a hand at eight in an unexplained explosion, was exploited and abused, and never got a steady job; he stands for over 100,000 hired-out Swiss children, and his case asks the course's closing question: can such a wrong ever be made good — the state answers with a 2017 law and a payment it deliberately calls a 'solidarity contribution', not compensation. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
