# Ruedi Hofer, the Hired-Out Children, and Whether Amends Can Be Made — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many farm places was Ruedi Hofer passed to as a child? (Sub lesson 1)
a) over 30  b) three  c) eight  d) exactly one, for eight years
**Answer: a) over 30** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the state deliberately call its payment of up to 25,000 francs? (Sub lesson 2)
a) a 'solidarity contribution' — not compensation  b) compensation in full  c) a wage for the work done  d) a fine on the farms
**Answer: a) a 'solidarity contribution' — not compensation** — That is what this lesson teaches.
