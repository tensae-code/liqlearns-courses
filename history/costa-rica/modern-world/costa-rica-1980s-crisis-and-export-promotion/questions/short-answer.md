# Costa Rica: The 1980s Crisis and Export Promotion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The crisis of the 1980s (the CONED telling); Costa Rica since the crisis of the 1980s (the Porras y Gamboa telling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the 1980s crisis breaks the welfare model: debt and structural adjustment shrink the state, and Costa Rica turns to export promotion and free trade — a turn the people themselves later judge in the CAFTA referendum. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
