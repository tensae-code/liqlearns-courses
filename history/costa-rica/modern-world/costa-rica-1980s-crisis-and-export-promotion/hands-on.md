# Hands-on — Costa Rica: The 1980s Crisis and Export Promotion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your family owes more than it earns. Write the painful choices: earn more, spend less, sell something. A country in debt crisis faces the same list, larger.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The 1980s crisis breaks the welfare model: debt and structural adjustment shrink the state, and Costa Rica turns to export promotion and free trade — a turn the people themselves later judge in the CAFTA referendum. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
