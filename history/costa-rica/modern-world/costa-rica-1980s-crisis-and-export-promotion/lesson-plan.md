# Lesson plan — Costa Rica: The 1980s Crisis and Export Promotion

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica: The 1980s Crisis and Export Promotion
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The 1980s crisis breaks the welfare model: debt and structural adjustment shrink the state, and Costa Rica turns to export promotion and free trade — a turn the people themselves later judge in the CAFTA referendum.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The crisis of the 1980s (the CONED telling)** — The 1980s economic crisis breaks the welfare model; Costa Rica turns to export promotion and trade opening.
2. **Costa Rica since the crisis of the 1980s (the Porras y Gamboa telling)** — Debt crisis, structural adjustment (PAES), state shrinking and free-trade treaties — the CAFTA referendum decides by popular vote.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
