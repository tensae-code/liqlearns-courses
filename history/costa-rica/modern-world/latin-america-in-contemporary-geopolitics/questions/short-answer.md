# Latin America in Contemporary Geopolitics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** El contexto geopolítico latinoamericano.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the world story lands at home: Latin America has its own place and its own problems in today's geopolitical context — and Costa Rica reads the world from there. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
