# Video script — Latin America in Contemporary Geopolitics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The world story lands at home: Latin America has its own place and its own problems in today's geopolitical context — and Costa Rica reads the world from there.

The Grade 10 book ends its world unit the way the Grade 9 book ended its own: at home. Latin America takes its place in the contemporary geopolitical context — a region courted and pressured by the great poles, carrying its own problems of inequality, migration, resources and sovereignty, and its own weight in the multipolar world. Costa Rica reads the planet from here. The next course level turns fully homeward: Costa Rica's own 20th century.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
