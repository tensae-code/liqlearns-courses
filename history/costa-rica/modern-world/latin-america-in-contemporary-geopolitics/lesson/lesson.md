# Latin America in Contemporary Geopolitics

## Do it first

Write where your country sits: which big powers care about your region, and what they want from it.

The Grade 10 book ends its world unit the way the Grade 9 book ended its own: at home. Latin America takes its place in the contemporary geopolitical context — a region courted and pressured by the great poles, carrying its own problems of inequality, migration, resources and sovereignty, and its own weight in the multipolar world. Costa Rica reads the planet from here. The next course level turns fully homeward: Costa Rica's own 20th century.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The world story lands at home: Latin America has its own place and its own problems in today's geopolitical context — and Costa Rica reads the world from there. Give one real example from your own life — your own country's trade and migration in the news — Latin America's geopolitical place is your place — and name the lesson's key words: Latin America, geopolitics, place and problems. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
