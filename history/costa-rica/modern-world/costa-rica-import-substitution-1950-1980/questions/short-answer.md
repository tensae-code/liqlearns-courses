# Costa Rica: Import Substitution, 1950–1980 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Productive diversification and import substitution (the CONED telling); The import-substitution model (the Porras y Gamboa telling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because from 1950 to 1980 Costa Rica tries to make at home what it used to import — diversifying production and building industry — until the import-substitution model exhausts itself. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
