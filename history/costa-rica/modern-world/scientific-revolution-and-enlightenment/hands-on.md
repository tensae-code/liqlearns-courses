# Hands-on — Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one thing 'everyone has always done' in your town. Then write the question a reasoner would ask about it: why, and who says so?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Enlightenment turns reason and science against inherited authority: if kings and customs can be questioned, political revolution is being prepared. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
