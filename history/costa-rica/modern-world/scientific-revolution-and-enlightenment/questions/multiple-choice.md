# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “La Ilustración” is right?
a) Enlightenment scientific-rational thought attacks inherited authority and prepares political revolution.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Enlightenment turns reason and science against inherited authority: if kings and customs can be questioned, political revolution is being prepared.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
