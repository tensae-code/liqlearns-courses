# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Enlightenment scientific-rational thought attacks inherited authority and prepares political revolution.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Enlightenment turns reason and science against inherited authority: if kings and customs can be questioned, political revolution is being prepared.
