# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Enlightenment turns reason and science against inherited authority: if kings and customs can be questioned, political revolution is being prepared. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
