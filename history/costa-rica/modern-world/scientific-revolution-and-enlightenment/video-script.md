# Video script — Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Enlightenment turns reason and science against inherited authority: if kings and customs can be questioned, political revolution is being prepared.

A new way of thinking spreads through Europe: the Enlightenment. Its weapon is reason, sharpened by the scientific revolution — observe, question, test, conclude. Turned on society, that weapon attacks inherited authority: why should kings rule by birth, why should custom command without reasons, why should old books settle new questions? The book draws the line forward plainly: thought like this prepares political revolution. Before thrones fall in the street, they fall in the mind.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
