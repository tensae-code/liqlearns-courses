# Hands-on — Costa Rica Building the Nation-State, 1821–1848

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your town had to make its own laws, money and army, separate from the next town. Write two things that would go well and two that would go badly.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After 1821 Costa Rica is a scatter of towns pulling their own way; the road from localisms to centralisation ends in 1848 with the founding of the Republic. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
