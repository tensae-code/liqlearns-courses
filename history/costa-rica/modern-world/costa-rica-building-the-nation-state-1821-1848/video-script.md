# Video script — Costa Rica Building the Nation-State, 1821–1848

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After 1821 Costa Rica is a scatter of towns pulling their own way; the road from localisms to centralisation ends in 1848 with the founding of the Republic.

Independence in 1821 leaves Costa Rica as a scatter of towns — Cartago, San José, Heredia, Alajuela — each pulling its own way, localisms stronger than any nation. The book walks the road out of that scatter: the slow centralisation of power, the fights and settlements between the towns, the building of common institutions. The road's end is a date: 1848, the founding of the Republic. A nation-state is not declared in a day; Costa Rica's took a generation to build.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
