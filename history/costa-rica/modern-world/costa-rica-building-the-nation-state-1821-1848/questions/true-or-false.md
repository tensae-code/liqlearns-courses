# Costa Rica Building the Nation-State, 1821–1848 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From town localisms after 1821 Costa Rica walks the road of centralisation to the founding of the Republic in 1848.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — After 1821 Costa Rica is a scatter of towns pulling their own way; the road from localisms to centralisation ends in 1848 with the founding of the Republic.
