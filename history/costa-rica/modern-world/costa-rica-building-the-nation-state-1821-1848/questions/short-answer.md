# Costa Rica Building the Nation-State, 1821–1848 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** De los localismos a la fundación de la República.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because after 1821 Costa Rica is a scatter of towns pulling their own way; the road from localisms to centralisation ends in 1848 with the founding of the Republic. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
