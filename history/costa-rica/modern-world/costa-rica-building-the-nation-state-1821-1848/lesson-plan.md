## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After 1821 Costa Rica is a scatter of towns pulling their own way; the road from localisms to centralisation ends in 1848 with the founding of the Republic.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — rivalries between towns in your own province — the localisms of 1821 in a milder, living form.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
