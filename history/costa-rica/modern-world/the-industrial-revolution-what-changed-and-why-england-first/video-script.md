# Video script — The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Industrial Revolution births a new society in 19th-century Europe — machines, factories and cities — the change Costa Rica's agro-export economy will soon plug into.

In 19th-century Europe a new kind of society is born: industrial society. Steam and machines gather workers into factories; factories gather people into fast-growing cities; railways and ships tie it all into world markets hungry for food and raw materials. The book plants this lesson in Costa Rica's story on purpose: that hungry industrial world is exactly what Costa Rica's agro-export economy — coffee, then bananas — will plug into. The next lessons are unintelligible without this one.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
