Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Industrial Revolution births a new society in 19th-century Europe — machines, factories and cities — the change Costa Rica's agro-export economy will soon plug into. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
