# Hands-on — Costa Rica: The Agro-Export Model in Crisis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your family's income comes from selling one thing to buyers far away, and suddenly they stop buying. Write the first three problems that arrive at your door.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The agro-export model that built Costa Rica — coffee and bananas sold to the world — enters repeated crisis in the first half of the 20th century, and the country must answer: taxes reformed, workers organising, a president facing 1929. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
