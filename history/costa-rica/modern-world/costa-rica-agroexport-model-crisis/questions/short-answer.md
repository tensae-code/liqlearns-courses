# Costa Rica: The Agro-Export Model in Crisis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The crises of the agro-export model (the CONED telling); The crisis of the agro-export model (the Porras y Gamboa telling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the agro-export model that built Costa Rica — coffee and bananas sold to the world — enters repeated crisis in the first half of the 20th century, and the country must answer: taxes reformed, workers organising, a president facing 1929. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
