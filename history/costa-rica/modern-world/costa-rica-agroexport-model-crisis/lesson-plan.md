# Lesson plan — Costa Rica: The Agro-Export Model in Crisis

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica: The Agro-Export Model in Crisis
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The agro-export model that built Costa Rica — coffee and bananas sold to the world — enters repeated crisis in the first half of the 20th century, and the country must answer: taxes reformed, workers organising, a president facing 1929.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The crises of the agro-export model (the CONED telling)** — The coffee-and-banana agro-export model that built the nation-state enters repeated crisis in the first half of the 20th century.
2. **The crisis of the agro-export model (the Porras y Gamboa telling)** — Coffee-and-banana dependence breaks: WWI's consequences for Costa Rica, González Flores' tax reforms, workers organising, and the actions taken against the 1929 crisis — learners role-play the president facing it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
