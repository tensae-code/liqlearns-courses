# Hands-on — Costa Rica: The Electoral System After 1949

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the rules a fair election needs: who votes, who counts, who accepts the result. Then write what happens to a party the rules shut out.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After 1949 Costa Rica's electoral system becomes the frame of its democratic decades — an order of elections and institutions, with the Communist Party left outlawed outside it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
