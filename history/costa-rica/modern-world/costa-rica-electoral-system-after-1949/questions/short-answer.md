**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because after 1949 Costa Rica's electoral system becomes the frame of its democratic decades — an order of elections and institutions, with the Communist Party left outlawed outside it. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
