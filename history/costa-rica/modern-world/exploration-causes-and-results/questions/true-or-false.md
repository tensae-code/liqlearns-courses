# Exploration: Causes and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Spain's overseas exploration of the 15th–16th centuries opens the Atlantic world that the next unit's conquest fills.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Spain's overseas exploration of the 15th and 16th centuries opens the Atlantic: two worlds that did not know each other are joined by sea — and conquest follows.
