# Exploration: Causes and Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La exploración ultramarina de España.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because spain's overseas exploration of the 15th and 16th centuries opens the Atlantic: two worlds that did not know each other are joined by sea — and conquest follows. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
