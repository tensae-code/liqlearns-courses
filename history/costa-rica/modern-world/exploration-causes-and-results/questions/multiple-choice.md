# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “La exploración ultramarina de España” is right?
a) Spain's overseas exploration of the 15th–16th centuries opens the Atlantic world that the next unit's conquest fills.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Spain's overseas exploration of the 15th and 16th centuries opens the Atlantic: two worlds that did not know each other are joined by sea — and conquest follows.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
