# Video script — Exploration: Causes and Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Spain's overseas exploration of the 15th and 16th centuries opens the Atlantic: two worlds that did not know each other are joined by sea — and conquest follows.

Spain turns its ships outward. In the 15th and 16th centuries Spanish exploration crosses the Atlantic and opens a world: Europe and America, strangers to each other, are joined by sea routes, and everything changes — maps, trade, plants, peoples, power. The book ends its first unit here on purpose: exploration is the door, and conquest is what walks through it in the next unit. The Atlantic world is open; what fills it is the next lesson's story.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
