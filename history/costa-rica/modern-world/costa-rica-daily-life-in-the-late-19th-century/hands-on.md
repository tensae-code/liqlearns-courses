# Hands-on — Costa Rica: Daily Life in the Late 19th Century

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write your own day's timetable, hour by hour. Then cross out everything that needs electricity, motors or telephones — what is left is close to a day in 1880.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: History ends at home: this lesson lives one ordinary day with Costa Ricans of the late 19th century — their work, food, homes and feasts. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
