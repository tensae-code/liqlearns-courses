# Costa Rica: Daily Life in the Late 19th Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La vida cotidiana costarricense.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because history ends at home: this lesson lives one ordinary day with Costa Ricans of the late 19th century — their work, food, homes and feasts. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
