# Video script — Costa Rica: Daily Life in the Late 19th Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History ends at home: this lesson lives one ordinary day with Costa Ricans of the late 19th century — their work, food, homes and feasts.

The Grade 9 book ends where its readers live: at home. After empires and revolutions, the last theme is la vida cotidiana — how ordinary Costa Ricans lived in the second half of the 19th century. Their houses and clothes, their work in field and kitchen, their food, their feasts and faith, their days measured by sun and church bell rather than clock and screen. It is the right ending: the nation-state and the coffee motor of the earlier lessons were built, in the end, out of these ordinary days.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
