## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Today's geopolitical tensions carry a Cold War background: old rivalries still move under new forms, and the book teaches you to read them so.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a headline in this week's news — read it again after the lesson and find its Cold War shadow.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
