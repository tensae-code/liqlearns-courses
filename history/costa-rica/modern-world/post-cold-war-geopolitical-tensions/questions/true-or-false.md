# Post-Cold-War Geopolitical Tensions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Today's geopolitical tensions are read against their Cold War background — old rivalries in new forms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Today's geopolitical tensions carry a Cold War background: old rivalries still move under new forms, and the book teaches you to read them so.
