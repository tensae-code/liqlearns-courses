# Post-Cold-War Geopolitical Tensions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Tensiones geopolíticas con trasfondo de la guerra fría.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because today's geopolitical tensions carry a Cold War background: old rivalries still move under new forms, and the book teaches you to read them so. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
