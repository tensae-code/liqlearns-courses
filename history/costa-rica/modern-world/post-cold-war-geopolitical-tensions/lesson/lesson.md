# Post-Cold-War Geopolitical Tensions

## Do it first

Take one tension in today's news. Write what part of it might be older than the people now arguing it.

The Cold War ended; its shadows did not. The book reads today's geopolitical tensions against their background (trasfondo) in the Cold War: borders drawn by the blocs, alliances inherited from them, rivalries between the same powers wearing new names. Old rivalries move in new forms — new weapons, new economies, new flags — but the lines underneath are often the old lines. A reader who knows the background cannot be fooled by the headline.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Today's geopolitical tensions carry a Cold War background: old rivalries still move under new forms, and the book teaches you to read them so. Give one real example from your own life — a headline in this week's news — read it again after the lesson and find its Cold War shadow — and name the lesson's key words: geopolitical tensions, Cold War background, old rivalries. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
