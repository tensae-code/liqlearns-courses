# Hands-on — Post-Cold-War Geopolitical Tensions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one tension in today's news. Write what part of it might be older than the people now arguing it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Today's geopolitical tensions carry a Cold War background: old rivalries still move under new forms, and the book teaches you to read them so. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
