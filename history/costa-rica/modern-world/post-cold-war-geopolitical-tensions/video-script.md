# Video script — Post-Cold-War Geopolitical Tensions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Today's geopolitical tensions carry a Cold War background: old rivalries still move under new forms, and the book teaches you to read them so.

The Cold War ended; its shadows did not. The book reads today's geopolitical tensions against their background (trasfondo) in the Cold War: borders drawn by the blocs, alliances inherited from them, rivalries between the same powers wearing new names. Old rivalries move in new forms — new weapons, new economies, new flags — but the lines underneath are often the old lines. A reader who knows the background cannot be fooled by the headline.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
