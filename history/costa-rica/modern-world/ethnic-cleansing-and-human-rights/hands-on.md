# Hands-on — Ethnic Cleansing and Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the difference between winning an argument with a group and trying to remove that group from a place entirely.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Ethnic cleansing and human-rights violations are the dark face of contemporary geopolitics — the lesson names them plainly so they can be recognised and refused. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
