# Ethnic Cleansing and Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Limpiezas étnicas y violación de los derechos humanos.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because ethnic cleansing and human-rights violations are the dark face of contemporary geopolitics — the lesson names them plainly so they can be recognised and refused. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
