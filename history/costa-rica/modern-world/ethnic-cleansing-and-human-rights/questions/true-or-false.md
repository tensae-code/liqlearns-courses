# Ethnic Cleansing and Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ethnic cleansings and human-rights violations are studied as the dark face of contemporary geopolitics.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Ethnic cleansing and human-rights violations are the dark face of contemporary geopolitics — the lesson names them plainly so they can be recognised and refused.
