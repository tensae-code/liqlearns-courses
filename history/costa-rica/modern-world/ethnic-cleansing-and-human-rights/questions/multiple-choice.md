# Ethnic Cleansing and Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Limpiezas étnicas y violación de los derechos humanos” is right?
a) Ethnic cleansings and human-rights violations are studied as the dark face of contemporary geopolitics.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Ethnic cleansing and human-rights violations are the dark face of contemporary geopolitics — the lesson names them plainly so they can be recognised and refused.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
