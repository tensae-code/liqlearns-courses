# Video script — Ethnic Cleansing and Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ethnic cleansing and human-rights violations are the dark face of contemporary geopolitics — the lesson names them plainly so they can be recognised and refused.

Contemporary geopolitics has a dark face, and the book looks at it directly: ethnic cleansings — whole peoples driven from their homes by violence — and the violation of human rights that travels with them. These are studied not as distant horrors but as the extreme end of the tensions of the previous lessons: what happens when rivalry and hatred are let loose on civilians. Naming them plainly is the lesson's work — they can only be refused by people who can recognise them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
