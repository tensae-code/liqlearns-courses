# Video script — China in a Multipolar World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the new multipolar world China rises as one of its poles — a centre of power the whole planet must reckon with.

The world after the Cold War is not a one-pole world for long. The book's contemporary unit begins with China: an ancient country become an industrial giant, rising as a pole — a centre of economic and political gravity — in a new multipolar world. Trade routes, factories, loans and diplomacy now bend toward Beijing as once they bent only toward Washington or Moscow. To read today's map, the lesson says, start with the poles — and count China among the first.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
