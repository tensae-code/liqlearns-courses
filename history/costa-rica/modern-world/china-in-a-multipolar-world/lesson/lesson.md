# China in a Multipolar World

## Do it first

List three things in your home made in China. Then write what it means that the country that made them is now a world power.

The world after the Cold War is not a one-pole world for long. The book's contemporary unit begins with China: an ancient country become an industrial giant, rising as a pole — a centre of economic and political gravity — in a new multipolar world. Trade routes, factories, loans and diplomacy now bend toward Beijing as once they bent only toward Washington or Moscow. To read today's map, the lesson says, start with the poles — and count China among the first.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In the new multipolar world China rises as one of its poles — a centre of power the whole planet must reckon with. Give one real example from your own life — the phone or shoes in your own home made in China — the multipolar world on your shelf — and name the lesson's key words: China, multipolar world, pole. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
