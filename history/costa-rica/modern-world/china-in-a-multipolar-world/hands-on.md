# Hands-on — China in a Multipolar World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List three things in your home made in China. Then write what it means that the country that made them is now a world power.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the new multipolar world China rises as one of its poles — a centre of power the whole planet must reckon with. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
