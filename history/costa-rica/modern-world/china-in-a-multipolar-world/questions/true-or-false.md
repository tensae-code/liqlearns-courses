# China in a Multipolar World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** China rises as a pole of the new multipolar world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — In the new multipolar world China rises as one of its poles — a centre of power the whole planet must reckon with.
