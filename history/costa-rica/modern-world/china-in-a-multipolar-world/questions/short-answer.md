# China in a Multipolar World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** El papel de China en un mundo multipolar.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because in the new multipolar world China rises as one of its poles — a centre of power the whole planet must reckon with. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
