# China in a Multipolar World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “El papel de China en un mundo multipolar” is right?
a) China rises as a pole of the new multipolar world.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) In the new multipolar world China rises as one of its poles — a centre of power the whole planet must reckon with.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
