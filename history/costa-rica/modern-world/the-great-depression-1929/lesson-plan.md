## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The crash of 1929 and the depression of the 1930s expose the capitalist model's crises — felt in everyday life and in whole national economies.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a closed shop or a lost job in your own family — the depression's everyday face in small.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
