# Video script — The Great Depression, 1929

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The crash of 1929 and the depression of the 1930s expose the capitalist model's crises — felt in everyday life and in whole national economies.

In 1929 the capitalist world's machinery seizes. The crash on the stock market becomes the Great Depression of the 1930s: factories close, banks fail, harvests rot unsold, and millions lose work. The book studies the crisis where it lands — in everyday life, in kitchens and queues — and at the level of national economies, Costa Rica's coffee economy among the struck. The lesson's judgement is structural: 1929 exposes the crises the capitalist model carries inside itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
