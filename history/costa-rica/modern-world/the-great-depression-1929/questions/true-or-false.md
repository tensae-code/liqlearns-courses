# The Great Depression, 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1929 crash and the 1930s depression expose the capitalist model's crises in everyday life and national economies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The crash of 1929 and the depression of the 1930s expose the capitalist model's crises — felt in everyday life and in whole national economies.
