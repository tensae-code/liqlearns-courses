# The Great Depression, 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “La crisis económica de 1929” is right?
a) The 1929 crash and the 1930s depression expose the capitalist model's crises in everyday life and national economies.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The crash of 1929 and the depression of the 1930s expose the capitalist model's crises — felt in everyday life and in whole national economies.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
