# The Great Depression, 1929 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La crisis económica de 1929.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the crash of 1929 and the depression of the 1930s expose the capitalist model's crises — felt in everyday life and in whole national economies. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
