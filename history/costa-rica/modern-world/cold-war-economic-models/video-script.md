# Video script — Cold War Economic Models

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Cold War was also two ways of organising a whole life: the capitalist and socialist economic models arranged rival societies and their everyday days differently.

Under the flags, the Cold War was a contest of kitchens. Two economic models organised the rival societies: the capitalist model, where markets and private property arrange production, and the socialist model, where the state plans and owns. The book studies them as lived systems — what work, shops, housing and everyday life looked like under each — because the blocs were not only armies and parties; they were two answers to the question of how a whole society should eat, work and live.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
