# Lesson plan — Cold War Economic Models

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Cold War Economic Models
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The World Wars, the Cold War and the World Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Cold War was also two ways of organising a whole life: the capitalist and socialist economic models arranged rival societies and their everyday days differently.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The World Wars, the Cold War and the World Today (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Cold War was also two ways of organising a whole life: the capitalist and socialist economic models arranged rival societies and their everyday days differently.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — who owns the shops and buses in your own town — the model question answered around you every day.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
