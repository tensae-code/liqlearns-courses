# Cold War Economic Models — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Modelos económicos y sociedad durante la guerra fría.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Cold War was also two ways of organising a whole life: the capitalist and socialist economic models arranged rival societies and their everyday days differently. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
