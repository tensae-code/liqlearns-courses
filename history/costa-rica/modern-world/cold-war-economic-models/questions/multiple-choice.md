# Cold War Economic Models — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Modelos económicos y sociedad durante la guerra fría” is right?
a) Capitalist and socialist economic models organise rival societies and everyday life during the Cold War.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Cold War was also two ways of organising a whole life: the capitalist and socialist economic models arranged rival societies and their everyday days differently.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
