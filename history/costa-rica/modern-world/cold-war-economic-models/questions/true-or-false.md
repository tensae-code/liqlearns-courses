# Cold War Economic Models — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Capitalist and socialist economic models organise rival societies and everyday life during the Cold War.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Cold War was also two ways of organising a whole life: the capitalist and socialist economic models arranged rival societies and their everyday days differently.
