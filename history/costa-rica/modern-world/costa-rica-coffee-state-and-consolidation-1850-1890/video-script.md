# Video script — Costa Rica: The Coffee State and Consolidation, 1850–1890

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From 1850 to 1890 coffee and the military hold and then consolidate the Costa Rican state: coffee interests and soldiers control it first, and the following decades make the nation-state firm.

Who holds the young Republic? The book answers with two hands: coffee and the barracks. From 1850 to 1870 coffee interests and military men control the state between them — the crop pays, the soldiers guard, and governments rise and fall inside that bargain. Then, from 1870 to 1890, the grip firms into structure: the nation-state consolidates, its institutions thickening around the coffee order. Costa Rica's modern state is built by the crop that paid for it and the army that guarded it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
