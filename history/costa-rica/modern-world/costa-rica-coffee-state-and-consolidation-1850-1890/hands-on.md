# Hands-on — Costa Rica: The Coffee State and Consolidation, 1850–1890

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write who gains most when one crop pays for a whole country — and what that gives them a say in.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From 1850 to 1890 coffee and the military hold and then consolidate the Costa Rican state: coffee interests and soldiers control it first, and the following decades make the nation-state firm. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
