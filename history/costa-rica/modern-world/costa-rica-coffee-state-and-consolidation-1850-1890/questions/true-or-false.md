# Costa Rica: The Coffee State and Consolidation, 1850–1890 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Coffee interests and military men control the state (1850–1870); the following decades consolidate the Costa Rican nation-state (1870–1890).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — From 1850 to 1890 coffee and the military hold and then consolidate the Costa Rican state: coffee interests and soldiers control it first, and the following decades make the nation-state firm.
