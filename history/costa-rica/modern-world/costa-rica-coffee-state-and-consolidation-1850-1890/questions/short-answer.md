# Costa Rica: The Coffee State and Consolidation, 1850–1890 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Control cafetalero-militar y consolidación.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because from 1850 to 1890 coffee and the military hold and then consolidate the Costa Rican state: coffee interests and soldiers control it first, and the following decades make the nation-state firm. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
