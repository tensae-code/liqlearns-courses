# Lesson plan — The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The World Wars, the Cold War and the World Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After 1945 the world splits into two rival blocs, and their rivalry is fought out in proxy conflicts across the globe — other people's countries become the chessboard.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The World Wars, the Cold War and the World Today (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After 1945 the world splits into two rival blocs, and their rivalry is fought out in proxy conflicts across the globe — other people's countries become the chessboard.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a small country caught between two big powers' arguments — the proxy pattern, still seen in today's news.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
