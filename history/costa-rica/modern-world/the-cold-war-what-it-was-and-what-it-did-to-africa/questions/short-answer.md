# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Hacia el mundo bipolar y expresiones de la rivalidad.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because after 1945 the world splits into two rival blocs, and their rivalry is fought out in proxy conflicts across the globe — other people's countries become the chessboard. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
