# Video script — The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After 1945 the world splits into two rival blocs, and their rivalry is fought out in proxy conflicts across the globe — other people's countries become the chessboard.

The second war ends, and the world does not get one peace — it gets two camps. After 1945 the planet splits into rival blocs, East and West, armed against each other yet never fighting directly. Instead, the book shows, their geopolitical rivalry is expressed in proxy conflicts across the globe: Korea, Vietnam, Africa, Latin America — other people's countries become the chessboard of a war called cold only in the capitals. Tema I-A walks toward the bipolar world; Tema I-B reads the rivalry's expressions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
