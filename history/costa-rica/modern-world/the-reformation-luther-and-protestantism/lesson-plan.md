1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In the 16th century the Protestant Reformation breaks the unity of Western Christianity, and Europe's religious map is redrawn for good.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the different churches on your own street — their variety begins in this break.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
