# Hands-on — The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the churches in your town. Write down how many different ones there are, and ask: was it always so?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the 16th century the Protestant Reformation breaks the unity of Western Christianity, and Europe's religious map is redrawn for good. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
