# Video script — The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the 16th century the Protestant Reformation breaks the unity of Western Christianity, and Europe's religious map is redrawn for good.

For a thousand years the West had one Church. In the 16th century that unity breaks. The Protestant Reformation — Luther its famous name — challenges the old Church's authority and teachings, and princes, cities and peoples take sides. Western Christianity splits, and Europe's map is redrawn in two faiths, with wars and settlements following for generations. The book marks the break plainly: after the Reformation, 'the Christian world' is never one again.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
