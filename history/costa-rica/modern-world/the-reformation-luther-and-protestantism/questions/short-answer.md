# The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La reforma protestante.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because in the 16th century the Protestant Reformation breaks the unity of Western Christianity, and Europe's religious map is redrawn for good. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
