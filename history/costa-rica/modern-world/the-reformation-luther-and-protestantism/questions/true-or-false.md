# The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Protestant Reformation breaks Western Christian unity in 16th-century Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — In the 16th century the Protestant Reformation breaks the unity of Western Christianity, and Europe's religious map is redrawn for good.
