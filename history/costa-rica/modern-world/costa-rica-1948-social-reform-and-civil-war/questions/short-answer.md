# Costa Rica 1948: Social Reform and Civil War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Social reform and the civil war of 1948 (the CONED telling); Costa Rica in the 1940s and the Civil War of 1948 (the Porras y Gamboa telling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the 1940s bring Costa Rica its great social reforms — social guarantees, the labour code, the University and the Caja — and then the civil war of 1948, the founding rupture from which modern Costa Rica and the 1949 Constitution come. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
