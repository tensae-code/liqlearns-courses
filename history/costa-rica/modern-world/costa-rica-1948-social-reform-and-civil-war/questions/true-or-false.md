# Costa Rica 1948: Social Reform and Civil War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The 1940s social reforms (social guarantees, labour code) end in the 1948 civil war, the founding rupture of modern Costa Rica.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Calderón Guardia's reforms — the University of Costa Rica (1940) and the Caja Costarricense de Seguro Social (1941) — the Bloque de la Victoria, Picado's government, the 1948 elections and civil war, and the 1949 Constitution.
**Answer: True**
