# Hands-on — Costa Rica 1948: Social Reform and Civil War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what a worker's family gains from a labour code and a health Caja. Then write what could still split such a country into war.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The 1940s bring Costa Rica its great social reforms — social guarantees, the labour code, the University and the Caja — and then the civil war of 1948, the founding rupture from which modern Costa Rica and the 1949 Constitution come. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
