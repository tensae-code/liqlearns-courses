# Lesson plan — Costa Rica 1948: Social Reform and Civil War

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica 1948: Social Reform and Civil War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The 1940s bring Costa Rica its great social reforms — social guarantees, the labour code, the University and the Caja — and then the civil war of 1948, the founding rupture from which modern Costa Rica and the 1949 Constitution come.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Social reform and the civil war of 1948 (the CONED telling)** — The 1940s social reforms (social guarantees, labour code) end in the 1948 civil war, the founding rupture of modern Costa Rica.
2. **Costa Rica in the 1940s and the Civil War of 1948 (the Porras y Gamboa telling)** — Calderón Guardia's reforms — the University of Costa Rica (1940) and the Caja Costarricense de Seguro Social (1941) — the Bloque de la Victoria, Picado's government, the 1948 elections and civil war, and the 1949 Constitution.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
