# Lesson plan — Cold War: Social and Political Struggles

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Cold War: Social and Political Struggles
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The World Wars, the Cold War and the World Today (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Inside the Cold War frame, peoples fight their own battles: movements press social and political demands that belong to them, while the blocs try to capture them.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a community protest in your own province — its demands are its own, whoever tries to claim them.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
