# Video script — Cold War: Social and Political Struggles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Inside the Cold War frame, peoples fight their own battles: movements press social and political demands that belong to them, while the blocs try to capture them.

The Cold War frame is not the whole picture. Inside it, peoples fight their own battles: workers for wages, students for universities, peasants for land, citizens for rights — social and political demands (luchas y reivindicaciones) that belong to the people pressing them. The blocs try to capture each struggle for their side, and the book's long section (printed pp. 84–125) teaches the reader to tell the difference: a people's own claim is not a superpower's pawn, even when the superpowers reach for it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
