# Hands-on — Cold War: Social and Political Struggles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write about a demand in your own town — water, a road, a wage. Then write how an outsider might try to use that demand for their own fight.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Inside the Cold War frame, peoples fight their own battles: movements press social and political demands that belong to them, while the blocs try to capture them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
