# Cold War: Social and Political Struggles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Luchas y reivindicaciones sociales y políticas en la guerra fría.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because inside the Cold War frame, peoples fight their own battles: movements press social and political demands that belong to them, while the blocs try to capture them. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
