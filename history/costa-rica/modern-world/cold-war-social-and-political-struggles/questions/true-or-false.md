# Cold War: Social and Political Struggles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Inside the Cold War frame, peoples fight their own battles — social and political demands movements press claims the blocs try to capture (a long section, printed pp. 84–125).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Inside the Cold War frame, peoples fight their own battles: movements press social and political demands that belong to them, while the blocs try to capture them.
