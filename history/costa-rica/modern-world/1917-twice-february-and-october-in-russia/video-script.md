# Video script — 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In 1917 revolution remakes Russia — its society at home and the geopolitical map of the whole first half of the 20th century abroad.

While the war still rages, Russia breaks. Revolution in 1917 — twice in one year, February and October — overthrows the old order and begins remaking Russian society from its foundations: land, factories, power itself change hands. The book's eye is geopolitical as well as social: the new Russia redraws the map of the first half of the 20th century, and the contest between its model and the capitalist West will organise the century that follows.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
