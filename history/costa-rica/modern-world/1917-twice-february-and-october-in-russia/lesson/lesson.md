# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia

## Do it first

Write what a country would need to change if its rulers fell twice in one year — who governs, who owns, who obeys.

While the war still rages, Russia breaks. Revolution in 1917 — twice in one year, February and October — overthrows the old order and begins remaking Russian society from its foundations: land, factories, power itself change hands. The book's eye is geopolitical as well as social: the new Russia redraws the map of the first half of the 20th century, and the contest between its model and the capitalist West will organise the century that follows.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In 1917 revolution remakes Russia — its society at home and the geopolitical map of the whole first half of the 20th century abroad. Give one real example from your own life — a year of sudden change in your own country's news — revolutions remake maps the way this one did — and name the lesson's key words: Russian Revolution, 1917, society remade, geopolitical map. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
