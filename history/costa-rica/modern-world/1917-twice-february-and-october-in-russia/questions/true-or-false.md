# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Revolution in Russia remakes its society and the geopolitical map of the first half of the 20th century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — In 1917 revolution remakes Russia — its society at home and the geopolitical map of the whole first half of the 20th century abroad.
