# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La revolución rusa.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because in 1917 revolution remakes Russia — its society at home and the geopolitical map of the whole first half of the 20th century abroad. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
