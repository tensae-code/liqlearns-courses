## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In 1917 revolution remakes Russia — its society at home and the geopolitical map of the whole first half of the 20th century abroad.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a year of sudden change in your own country's news — revolutions remake maps the way this one did.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
