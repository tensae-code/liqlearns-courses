# The First World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The world goes to war in 1914; the section traces the war's causes and the social, economic and political changes it forced.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — In 1914 the world goes to war: the lesson traces why it came, and the social, economic and political changes the war forced on everyone.
