# The First World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La Primera Guerra Mundial.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because in 1914 the world goes to war: the lesson traces why it came, and the social, economic and political changes the war forced on everyone. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
