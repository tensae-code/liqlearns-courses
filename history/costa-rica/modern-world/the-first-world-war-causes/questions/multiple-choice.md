# The First World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “La Primera Guerra Mundial” is right?
a) The world goes to war in 1914; the section traces the war's causes and the social, economic and political changes it forced.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) In 1914 the world goes to war: the lesson traces why it came, and the social, economic and political changes the war forced on everyone.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
