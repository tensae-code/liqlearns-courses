# Video script — The First World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In 1914 the world goes to war: the lesson traces why it came, and the social, economic and political changes the war forced on everyone.

In 1914 the imperial world turns its weapons on itself. The book traces the war's causes — the rivalries, alliances and arms races that made Europe a loaded trap — and then follows what the war forced: societies mobilised whole, economies bent to the front, politics shaken to their roots. The First World War is the hinge of the century: everything after it, from revolution in Russia to crisis in Costa Rica's coffee trade, hangs on this door.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
