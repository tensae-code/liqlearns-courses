# Costa Rica: Democracy and Social Struggles, 1900–1948 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The construction of democracy and the social struggles (the CONED telling); The construction of democracy and the social struggles (the Porras y Gamboa telling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because costa Rican democracy was built by struggle: workers, parties and reformers fight the social battles of 1900–1948 — electoral reform won, coffee workers and producers organised — that widen it before 1948. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
