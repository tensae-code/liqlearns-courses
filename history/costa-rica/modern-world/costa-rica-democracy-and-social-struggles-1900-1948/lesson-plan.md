# Lesson plan — Costa Rica: Democracy and Social Struggles, 1900–1948

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica: Democracy and Social Struggles, 1900–1948
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Costa Rican democracy was built by struggle: workers, parties and reformers fight the social battles of 1900–1948 — electoral reform won, coffee workers and producers organised — that widen it before 1948.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (recommended, never forced).

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Costa Rican democracy was built by struggle: workers, parties and reformers fight the social battles of 1900–1948 — electoral reform won, coffee workers and producers organised — that widen it before 1948.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
