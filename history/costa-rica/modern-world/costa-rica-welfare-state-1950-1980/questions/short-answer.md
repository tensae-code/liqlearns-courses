# Costa Rica: The Welfare State, 1950–1980 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The welfare state (the CONED telling); The welfare state and Costa Rican society, 1950–1980 (the Porras y Gamboa telling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because for three decades the welfare state — the Estado benefactor — shapes Costa Rican society: state banking, the Estado empresario, health and education build a broad middle class, until the model's own crisis gathers. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
