# Lesson plan — Costa Rica: The Welfare State, 1950–1980

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica: The Welfare State, 1950–1980
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** For three decades the welfare state — the Estado benefactor — shapes Costa Rican society: state banking, the Estado empresario, health and education build a broad middle class, until the model's own crisis gathers.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The welfare state (the CONED telling)** — A welfare (benefactor) state shapes Costa Rican society for three decades — until its own crisis gathers (the section even carries a "Crisis del Estado Benefactor" crossword).
2. **The welfare state and Costa Rican society, 1950–1980 (the Porras y Gamboa telling)** — The welfare state — state banking, the Estado empresario (CODESA), health and education — builds a broad middle class, with urban problems and the Guanacaste land seizures at its edges.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
