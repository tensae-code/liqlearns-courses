# The Spanish Conquest of America and Costa Rica — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Spanish conquest overwhelms the indigenous societies of America, Costa Rica's included, by war, disease and alliance.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Spanish conquest overwhelms America's indigenous societies — Costa Rica's among them — not by soldiers alone, but by war, disease and alliances together.
