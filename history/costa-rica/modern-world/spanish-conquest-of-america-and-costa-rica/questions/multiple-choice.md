# The Spanish Conquest of America and Costa Rica — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “La conquista española en el continente americano” is right?
a) Spanish conquest overwhelms the indigenous societies of America, Costa Rica's included, by war, disease and alliance.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Spanish conquest overwhelms America's indigenous societies — Costa Rica's among them — not by soldiers alone, but by war, disease and alliances together.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
