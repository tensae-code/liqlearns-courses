# The Spanish Conquest of America and Costa Rica — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La conquista española en el continente americano.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Spanish conquest overwhelms America's indigenous societies — Costa Rica's among them — not by soldiers alone, but by war, disease and alliances together. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
