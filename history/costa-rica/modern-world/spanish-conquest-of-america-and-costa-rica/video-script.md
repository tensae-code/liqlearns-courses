# Video script — The Spanish Conquest of America and Costa Rica

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Spanish conquest overwhelms America's indigenous societies — Costa Rica's among them — not by soldiers alone, but by war, disease and alliances together.

The ships were few; the peoples of America were many. Yet within generations the Spanish conquest overwhelms the continent's indigenous societies, Costa Rica's included. The book refuses the simple story of braver soldiers: the conquest works by three forces together — war, with steel, horses and guns; disease, the invisible army that emptied towns before any battle; and alliance, as indigenous peoples divided and some joined the newcomers against their own rivals. Great societies fall, and a colonial world is built on their land.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
