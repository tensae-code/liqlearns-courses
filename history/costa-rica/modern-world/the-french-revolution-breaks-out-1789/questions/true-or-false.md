# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world whose ideas cross the Atlantic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — In 1789 the French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world — and its ideas cross the Atlantic to America.
