# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “La Revolución Francesa” is right?
a) The French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world whose ideas cross the Atlantic.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) In 1789 the French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world — and its ideas cross the Atlantic to America.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
