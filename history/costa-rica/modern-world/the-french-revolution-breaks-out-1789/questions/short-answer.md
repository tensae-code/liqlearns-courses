# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La Revolución Francesa.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because in 1789 the French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world — and its ideas cross the Atlantic to America. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
