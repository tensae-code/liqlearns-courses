## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In 1789 the French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world — and its ideas cross the Atlantic to America.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — voting and rights in your own country — the Atlantic crossing of those ideas ends at your own ballot.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
