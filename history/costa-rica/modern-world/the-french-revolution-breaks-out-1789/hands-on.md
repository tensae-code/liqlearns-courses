# Hands-on — The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the three words of the Revolution's famous promise — liberty, equality, fraternity — and beside each, one place in your life where it is kept and one where it is not.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In 1789 the French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world — and its ideas cross the Atlantic to America. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
