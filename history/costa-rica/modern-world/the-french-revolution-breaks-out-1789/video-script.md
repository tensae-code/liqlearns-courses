# Video script — The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In 1789 the French Revolution breaks the absolute order and proclaims a new political world — and its ideas cross the Atlantic to America.

In 1789 the absolute order cracks in France itself. The French Revolution breaks the old world of king, privilege and estates, and proclaims a new political world: citizens, rights, liberty, equality. The book does not leave the story in Paris: those ideas board ships. Across the Atlantic they travel to America, where colonists read them and begin to ask why they should be ruled from across an ocean. The next unit's independences are lit by this fire.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
