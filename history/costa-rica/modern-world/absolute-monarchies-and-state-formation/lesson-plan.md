# Lesson plan — Absolute Monarchies and State Formation

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Absolute Monarchies and State Formation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and the Making of Costa Rica (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Early-modern kings gather all power into their own hands and form absolute monarchies — the state form the Enlightenment will soon challenge.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and the Making of Costa Rica (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Early-modern kings gather all power into their own hands and form absolute monarchies — the state form the Enlightenment will soon challenge.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any group where one voice decides all — the absolute pattern in small, and the wish to challenge it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
