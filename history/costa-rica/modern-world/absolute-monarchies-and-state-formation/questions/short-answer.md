# Absolute Monarchies and State Formation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La formación de monarquías absolutas.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because early-modern kings gather all power into their own hands and form absolute monarchies — the state form the Enlightenment will soon challenge. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
