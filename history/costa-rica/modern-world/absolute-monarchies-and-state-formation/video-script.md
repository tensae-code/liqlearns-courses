# Video script — Absolute Monarchies and State Formation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Early-modern kings gather all power into their own hands and form absolute monarchies — the state form the Enlightenment will soon challenge.

Out of the feudal scatter of lords, early-modern kings build something tighter: the absolute monarchy. Power is concentrated — armies, taxes, courts and law gathered into the king's own hands, and around that single centre the modern state is formed. The book sets this state form carefully in place, because the rest of the unit leans on it: the Enlightenment will challenge exactly this — one unquestionable will ruling all — and revolution will break it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
