# Video script — Colonial Costa Rica: Institutions and Daily Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The colony is a system: divisions and institutions rule from above, and below them a daily life of cities and streets goes on — studied with Costa Rica at the centre.

Conquest is an event; colony is a system. The book organises it: America divided into administrative divisions, ruled through institutions — councils, courts, church and crown officials — and Costa Rica studied inside that frame with emphasis on its own case. Then the lens drops from the offices to the streets: colonial cities with their grid and square, their urban structure, and the everyday life of the people who lived in them — markets, houses, feasts and work. A colony is both the institution and the day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
