# Hands-on — Colonial Costa Rica: Institutions and Daily Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw your town as the colony would organise it: the main square, the church, the government house. Who sits where, and who is left outside?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The colony is a system: divisions and institutions rule from above, and below them a daily life of cities and streets goes on — studied with Costa Rica at the centre. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
