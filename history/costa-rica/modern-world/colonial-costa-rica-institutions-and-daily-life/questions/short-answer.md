# Colonial Costa Rica: Institutions and Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Sociedades coloniales en América y Costa Rica.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the colony is a system: divisions and institutions rule from above, and below them a daily life of cities and streets goes on — studied with Costa Rica at the centre. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
