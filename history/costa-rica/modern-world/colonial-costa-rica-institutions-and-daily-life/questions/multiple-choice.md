# Colonial Costa Rica: Institutions and Daily Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Sociedades coloniales en América y Costa Rica” is right?
a) The colony is organised in administrative divisions and institutions; its cities, urban structure and everyday life are studied with emphasis on Costa Rica.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The colony is a system: divisions and institutions rule from above, and below them a daily life of cities and streets goes on — studied with Costa Rica at the centre.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
