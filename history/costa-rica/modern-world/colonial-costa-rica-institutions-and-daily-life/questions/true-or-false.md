# Colonial Costa Rica: Institutions and Daily Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The colony is organised in administrative divisions and institutions; its cities, urban structure and everyday life are studied with emphasis on Costa Rica.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The colony is a system: divisions and institutions rule from above, and below them a daily life of cities and streets goes on — studied with Costa Rica at the centre.
