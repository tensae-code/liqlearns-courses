# The Renaissance: What It Was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Renaissance humanism revalues classical antiquity and the human being at the close of the medieval world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Renaissance revalues two things at the close of the medieval world: the classical ancient world, and the human being at the centre of it.
