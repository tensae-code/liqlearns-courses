# The Renaissance: What It Was — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** El Renacimiento.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Renaissance revalues two things at the close of the medieval world: the classical ancient world, and the human being at the centre of it. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
