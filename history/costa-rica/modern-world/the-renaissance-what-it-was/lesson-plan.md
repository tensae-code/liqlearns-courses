# Lesson plan — The Renaissance: What It Was

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Renaissance: What It Was
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Medieval World and the Turn to Modern Times (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Renaissance revalues two things at the close of the medieval world: the classical ancient world, and the human being at the centre of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Medieval World and the Turn to Modern Times (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Renaissance revalues two things at the close of the medieval world: the classical ancient world, and the human being at the centre of it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — an old building restored in your own town — the Renaissance gesture in small: the past valued again.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
