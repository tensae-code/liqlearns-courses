# Video script — The Renaissance: What It Was

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Renaissance revalues two things at the close of the medieval world: the classical ancient world, and the human being at the centre of it.

At the close of the medieval world, European eyes turn backward — to go forward. The Renaissance is a revaluing: scholars and artists lift up classical antiquity again, its Greek and Roman books, statues and buildings, and with them a new valuation of the human being — humanism — the person as capable, curious and central. Old manuscripts are hunted, old statues dug up, and in their light new art and new thought are made. The modern age begins by remembering.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
