# Costa Rica: Women's Suffrage Struggle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La situación social y laboral de la mujer; las luchas de las mujeres por el sufragio.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because costa Rican women worked the society's work and were refused its vote — this lesson tells their long fight for suffrage. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
