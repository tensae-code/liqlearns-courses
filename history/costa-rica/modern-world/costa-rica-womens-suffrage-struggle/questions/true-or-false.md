# Costa Rica: Women's Suffrage Struggle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Women's work and the long fight for the vote.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Costa Rican women worked the society's work and were refused its vote — this lesson tells their long fight for suffrage.
