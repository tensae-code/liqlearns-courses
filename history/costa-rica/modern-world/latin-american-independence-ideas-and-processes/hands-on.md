# Hands-on — Latin American Independence: Ideas and Processes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one idea that could make a colony want to rule itself, and one hard problem it would face the morning after independence.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Across Spanish America, independence ideas become independence processes — colony by colony, new nations emerge, Costa Rica among them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
