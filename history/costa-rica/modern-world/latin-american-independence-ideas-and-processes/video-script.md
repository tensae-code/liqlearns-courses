# Video script — Latin American Independence: Ideas and Processes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Across Spanish America, independence ideas become independence processes — colony by colony, new nations emerge, Costa Rica among them.

Nations are born twice: first in ideas, then in events. The book's Tema 1-A (printed p. 132) gathers the independence ideas — Enlightenment reason, the French and North American examples, creole grievances — and Tema 1-B (printed p. 140) follows them becoming processes: declarations, wars, congresses, the long unmaking of the Spanish empire in America. Colony by colony, new nations emerge — and Central America, Costa Rica inside it, takes its own road in the next lessons.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
