# Latin American Independence: Ideas and Processes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Independence ideas (Tema 1-A, printed p. 132) become independence processes across Spanish America (Tema 1-B, printed p. 140), and new nations emerge.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Across Spanish America, independence ideas become independence processes — colony by colony, new nations emerge, Costa Rica among them.
