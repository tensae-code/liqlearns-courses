# Latin American Independence: Ideas and Processes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** El surgimiento de naciones en América.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because across Spanish America, independence ideas become independence processes — colony by colony, new nations emerge, Costa Rica among them. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
