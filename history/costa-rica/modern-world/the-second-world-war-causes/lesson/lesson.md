# The Second World War: Causes

## Do it first

Write how an idea — 'our people are above yours' — can move from words on a page to danger for real families.

Twenty years after the first, the world goes to war again. The book tells the second global war whole — its fronts across Europe, Africa, Asia and the oceans — and refuses to leave out its darkest centre: under ideologies of racial superiority, whole ethnic groups are persecuted and exterminated. A war of armies is also a crime against peoples, and the lesson keeps both in view, because the world built after 1945 — the world of the next lessons — is built in answer to both.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A second global war follows the first — and inside it, ideologies of racial superiority drive the persecution and extermination of whole ethnic groups. Give one real example from your own life — cruel words about a group in your own school or town — the first step of the road this lesson follows to its end — and name the lesson's key words: Second World War, racial superiority, persecution, extermination. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
