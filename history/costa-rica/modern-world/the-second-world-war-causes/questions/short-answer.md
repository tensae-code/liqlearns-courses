# The Second World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La Segunda Guerra Mundial.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because a second global war follows the first — and inside it, ideologies of racial superiority drive the persecution and extermination of whole ethnic groups. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
