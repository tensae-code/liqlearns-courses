# The Second World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A second global war follows, including the persecution and extermination of ethnic groups under racial-superiority ideologies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — A second global war follows the first — and inside it, ideologies of racial superiority drive the persecution and extermination of whole ethnic groups.
