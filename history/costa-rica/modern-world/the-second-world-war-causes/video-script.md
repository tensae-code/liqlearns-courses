# Video script — The Second World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A second global war follows the first — and inside it, ideologies of racial superiority drive the persecution and extermination of whole ethnic groups.

Twenty years after the first, the world goes to war again. The book tells the second global war whole — its fronts across Europe, Africa, Asia and the oceans — and refuses to leave out its darkest centre: under ideologies of racial superiority, whole ethnic groups are persecuted and exterminated. A war of armies is also a crime against peoples, and the lesson keeps both in view, because the world built after 1945 — the world of the next lessons — is built in answer to both.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
