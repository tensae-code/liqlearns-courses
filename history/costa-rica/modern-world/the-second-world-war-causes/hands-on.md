# Hands-on — The Second World War: Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write how an idea — 'our people are above yours' — can move from words on a page to danger for real families.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A second global war follows the first — and inside it, ideologies of racial superiority drive the persecution and extermination of whole ethnic groups. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
