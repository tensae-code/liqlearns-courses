# Video script — The End of Real Socialism and the Cold War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
'Real socialism' collapses, the bipolar world ends, and the contemporary geopolitical age — the world of today — opens.

The bipolar world does not fade — it falls. Around 1989–1991 'real socialism', the actually existing socialist system of the East, collapses: its parties lose power, its bloc dissolves, its union breaks apart. With it the Cold War ends and the two-colour map is finished. The book marks the threshold plainly: after this ending, the contemporary geopolitical age opens — the age of the next lessons, and of the reader's own lifetime.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
