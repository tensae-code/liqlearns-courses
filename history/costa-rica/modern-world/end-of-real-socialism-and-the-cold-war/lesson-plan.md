## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: 'Real socialism' collapses, the bipolar world ends, and the contemporary geopolitical age — the world of today — opens.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the world news of your own lifetime — it all happens in the age that opened when the bipolar world ended.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
