# The End of Real Socialism and the Cold War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** “Real socialism” collapses and the bipolar world ends, opening the contemporary geopolitical age.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — 'Real socialism' collapses, the bipolar world ends, and the contemporary geopolitical age — the world of today — opens.
