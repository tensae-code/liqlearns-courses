# The End of Real Socialism and the Cold War

## Do it first

Write what changes in a game when one of two teams leaves the field: who wins, who is lost, what new game begins?

The bipolar world does not fade — it falls. Around 1989–1991 'real socialism', the actually existing socialist system of the East, collapses: its parties lose power, its bloc dissolves, its union breaks apart. With it the Cold War ends and the two-colour map is finished. The book marks the threshold plainly: after this ending, the contemporary geopolitical age opens — the age of the next lessons, and of the reader's own lifetime.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example from your own life — the world news of your own lifetime — it all happens in the age that opened when the bipolar world ended — and name the lesson's key words: real socialism collapses, bipolar world ends, contemporary age. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
