# Hands-on — Costa Rica: State and Social Movements Since 1980

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write about a protest, strike or community movement you have seen or heard of in your town. What did it ask for, and who answered?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From 1980 to today the national story closes — for now — with the state, elections and social movements answering the neoliberal turn, and two-party rule and its discontents under judgement. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
