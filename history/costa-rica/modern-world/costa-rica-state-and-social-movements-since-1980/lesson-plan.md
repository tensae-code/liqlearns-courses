# Lesson plan — Costa Rica: State and Social Movements Since 1980

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica: State and Social Movements Since 1980
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 1980 to today the national story closes — for now — with the state, elections and social movements answering the neoliberal turn, and two-party rule and its discontents under judgement.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Costa Rica's Own Twentieth Century (recommended, never forced).
