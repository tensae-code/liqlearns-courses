# Costa Rica: State and Social Movements Since 1980 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** State, electoral system and social movements since the 1980s (the CONED telling); The role of the state and the social movements since 1980 (the Porras y Gamboa telling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because from 1980 to today the national story closes — for now — with the state, elections and social movements answering the neoliberal turn, and two-party rule and its discontents under judgement. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
