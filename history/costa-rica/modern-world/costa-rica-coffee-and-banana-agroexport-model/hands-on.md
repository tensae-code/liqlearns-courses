# Hands-on — Costa Rica: The Coffee and Banana Agro-Export Model

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow one bag of coffee or one bunch of bananas from a Costa Rican field to a foreign breakfast table. Write every hand and ship it passes through.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Coffee becomes the motor of Costa Rica's economy, society and culture, and bananas rise on the Caribbean coast: the country joins the world market as an agro-exporter. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
