# Costa Rica: The Coffee and Banana Agro-Export Model — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Coffee becomes the motor of economic, social and cultural change, and banana production rises on the Caribbean coast — Costa Rica joins the world market as an agro-exporter.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Coffee becomes the motor of Costa Rica's economy, society and culture, and bananas rise on the Caribbean coast: the country joins the world market as an agro-exporter.
