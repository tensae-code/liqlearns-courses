# Costa Rica: The Coffee and Banana Agro-Export Model — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La inserción al mercado mundial.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because coffee becomes the motor of Costa Rica's economy, society and culture, and bananas rise on the Caribbean coast: the country joins the world market as an agro-exporter. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
