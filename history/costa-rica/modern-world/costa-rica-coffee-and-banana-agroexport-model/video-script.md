# Video script — Costa Rica: The Coffee and Banana Agro-Export Model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Coffee becomes the motor of Costa Rica's economy, society and culture, and bananas rise on the Caribbean coast: the country joins the world market as an agro-exporter.

Costa Rica plugs into the world. Coffee — grown in the central valley, carried by rail to the ports — becomes the motor of the whole country: not only its economy but its society and its culture turn around the harvest. Then a second motor starts on the other coast: banana production rises on the Caribbean, with its railways, ports and foreign companies. The book names the result: the agro-export model — a country inserted into the world market by what its fields sell abroad. The 20th century will test that model to breaking point.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
