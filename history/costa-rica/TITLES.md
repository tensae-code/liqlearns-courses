# History — Costa Rica course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Costa Rica's
own — built from Costa Rica's Estudios Sociales books
(Spanish): the CONED distance-education anthologies for
Grades 7, 9, 10 and 11 (UNED / CONED), which run one long
arc from the first humans to today's Costa Rica, and the
Porras y Gamboa *Estudios Sociales 11*, a private-publisher
twin of the Grade 11 programme. Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below
sit inside the levels, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grade each book comes from lives
only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the human story opens
in Africa and the ancient world and reaches Costa Rica's
indigenous peoples before any national narrative; the
medieval and early-modern world follows; then every world
process is landed in Costa Rica — conquest, colony, coffee,
banana; the Grade 10 book reads the world's wars and the
Cold War from Costa Rica outward; and the last level turns
fully homeward to Costa Rica's own 20th century, told twice
by its two Grade 11 books. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
