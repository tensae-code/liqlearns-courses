# Video script — Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Industrial Europe expands overseas and seizes other peoples' lands; the book asks what imperialism did to the societies it seized — socially, economically, environmentally, politically.

Industrial Europe, squeezed by its late-century 'great depression', looks outward for markets, materials and land — and expands overseas, seizing societies across Africa, Asia and the Pacific. The Grade 10 book does not stop at the map of empires: it asks the harder question — how did imperialism change the societies it seized? Socially, economically, environmentally, politically: whole worlds are rearranged around Europe's needs. That question, asked from Costa Rica, opens the 20th century's story.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
