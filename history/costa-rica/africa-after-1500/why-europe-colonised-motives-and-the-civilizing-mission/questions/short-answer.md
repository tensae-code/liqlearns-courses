# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La expansión imperialista europea.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because industrial Europe expands overseas and seizes other peoples' lands; the book asks what imperialism did to the societies it seized — socially, economically, environmentally, politically. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
