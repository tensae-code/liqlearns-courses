# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Industrial Europe, in its late-century "great depression", expands overseas; the anthology asks how imperialism changed the social, economic, environmental and political conditions of the societies it seized.
