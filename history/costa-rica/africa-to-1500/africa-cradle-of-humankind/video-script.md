# Video script — Africa: Cradle of Humankind

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every human story starts in Africa: there our ancestors stood upright, made the first tools and tamed fire, on the long road to Homo sapiens — which is why the book calls Africa the cradle of humankind.

The story of everyone begins in one place: Africa. Between seven and five million years ago, one line of apes took a road no other animal had taken — they came down from the trees and walked upright, on two feet, freeing their hands. Much later came Homo habilis, the handy one, who struck stones into the first tools. Then fire was tamed: warmth, cooked food, light against the dark, and a circle to gather around. Step by slow step the road runs to Homo sapiens — to us. That is why the book calls Africa the cradle of humankind: every people on earth, Costa Rica's included, traces its first steps there.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
