# Africa: Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Los primeros pasos del ser humano.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because every human story starts in Africa: there our ancestors stood upright, made the first tools and tamed fire, on the long road to Homo sapiens — which is why the book calls Africa the cradle of humankind. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
