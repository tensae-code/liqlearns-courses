# Africa: Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Humanity evolves in Africa, the "cradle of humankind": bipedal hominids separate from the chimpanzee line 7–5 million years ago, Homo habilis makes tools, and fire is tamed on the way to Homo sapiens.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Every human story starts in Africa: there our ancestors stood upright, made the first tools and tamed fire, on the long road to Homo sapiens — which is why the book calls Africa the cradle of humankind.
