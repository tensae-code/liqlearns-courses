# Video script — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Egypt is a civilisation built by a river: the Nile's floods order the farming, the pharaoh's state orders the people, and its legacy stands beside Mesopotamia's as one of the first civilisations.

Egypt grows along one river. The Nile floods on its yearly rhythm, lays down black soil, and a desert land becomes a garden — if the work is organised. Around that organisation a whole world rises: the pharaoh at the top, officials and priests and scribes beneath, farmers in the fields, and a culture of temples, tombs and writing that still astonishes. The book studies Egypt beside Mesopotamia as one of the first civilisations: a society whose order — river, state and belief together — left a legacy the world still reads.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
