# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Las primeras civilizaciones — Egipto.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because egypt is a civilisation built by a river: the Nile's floods order the farming, the pharaoh's state orders the people, and its legacy stands beside Mesopotamia's as one of the first civilisations. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
