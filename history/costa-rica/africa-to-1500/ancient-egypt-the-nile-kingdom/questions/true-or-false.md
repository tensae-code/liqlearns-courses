# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On the Nile a pharaonic civilisation organises its world around the river, its social order and cultural legacy studied beside Mesopotamia's.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Egypt is a civilisation built by a river: the Nile's floods order the farming, the pharaoh's state orders the people, and its legacy stands beside Mesopotamia's as one of the first civilisations.
