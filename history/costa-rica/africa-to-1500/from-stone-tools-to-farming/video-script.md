# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Neolithic revolution is the turn from wandering and hunting to settling and farming: people plant, keep animals, and stay in one place for good.

For most of the human story, people moved: bands of hunters and gatherers followed the animals and the seasons, carrying everything they owned. Then, in the Neolithic revolution, the pattern breaks. People settle. They plant seeds and wait for them; they tame animals and keep them close; they store grain against the hungry months. A band that plants cannot wander — so villages rise, and human beings begin to occupy space permanently. Everything later — towns, cities, states — stands on this turn: food grown in one place feeds people who stay in one place.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
