# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nomadic hunters settle, farm and domesticate, and the Neolithic revolution turns mobile bands into agricultural societies occupying space permanently.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Neolithic revolution is the turn from wandering and hunting to settling and farming: people plant, keep animals, and stay in one place for good.
