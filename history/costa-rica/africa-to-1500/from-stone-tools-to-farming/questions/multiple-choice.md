# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “La Revolución Neolítica” is right?
a) Nomadic hunters settle, farm and domesticate, and the Neolithic revolution turns mobile bands into agricultural societies occupying space permanently.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Neolithic revolution is the turn from wandering and hunting to settling and farming: people plant, keep animals, and stay in one place for good.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
