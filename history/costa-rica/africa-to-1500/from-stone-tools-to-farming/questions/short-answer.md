# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La Revolución Neolítica.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Neolithic revolution is the turn from wandering and hunting to settling and farming: people plant, keep animals, and stay in one place for good. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
