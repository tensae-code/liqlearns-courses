# Video script — Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the medieval centuries Christianity expands and takes firm hold across Europe, coming to structure its society and its way of thinking.

Across the medieval centuries one faith spreads over a whole continent and settles deep. Christianity expands into every kingdom and consolidates — churches in every town, monasteries copying books, feast days ordering the year. The book studies what that means beyond belief: the Church comes to structure medieval society itself — who teaches, who records, who judges, who cares for the poor — and to shape the way Europe thinks about the world, time and the soul. A religion becomes the frame of a civilisation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
