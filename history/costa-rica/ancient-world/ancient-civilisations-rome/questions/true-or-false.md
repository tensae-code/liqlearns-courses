# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rome's social organisation and cultural contributions complete the classical pair from which Western societies are traced.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Rome completes the classical pair: its way of organising society and its cultural contributions are the second root from which Western societies are traced.
