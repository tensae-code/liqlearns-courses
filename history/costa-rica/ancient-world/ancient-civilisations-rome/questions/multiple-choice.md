# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Roma” is right?
a) Rome's social organisation and cultural contributions complete the classical pair from which Western societies are traced.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Rome completes the classical pair: its way of organising society and its cultural contributions are the second root from which Western societies are traced.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
