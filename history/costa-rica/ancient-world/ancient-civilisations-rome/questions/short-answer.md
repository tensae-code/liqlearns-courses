# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Roma.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because rome completes the classical pair: its way of organising society and its cultural contributions are the second root from which Western societies are traced. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
