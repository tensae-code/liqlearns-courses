# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rome completes the classical pair: its way of organising society and its cultural contributions are the second root from which Western societies are traced.

Rome begins as one city and becomes a world. The book studies how Roman society was organised — its orders and ranks, its citizens and its law — and the cultural contributions it spread across an empire: roads and aqueducts, a language, Latin, that became Spanish and its sister tongues, and ideas of law and citizenship that outlived the empire itself. With Greece, Rome completes the classical pair: two ancient societies from which Western societies, Costa Rica's among them, trace their descent.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
