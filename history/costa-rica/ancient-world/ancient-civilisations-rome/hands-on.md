# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at your own town: name one thing organised 'the Roman way' — a road, a law, a calendar month, a word in Spanish that comes from Latin.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Rome completes the classical pair: its way of organising society and its cultural contributions are the second root from which Western societies are traced. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
