**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Rome completes the classical pair: its way of organising society and its cultural contributions are the second root from which Western societies are traced.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the Spanish language you speak every day — its Latin roots are Rome's contribution living in your mouth.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
