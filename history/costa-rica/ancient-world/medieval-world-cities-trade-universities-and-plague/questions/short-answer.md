# The Medieval World: Cities, Trade, Universities and Plague — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** El tránsito a la Edad Moderna (siglos XII–XV).

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because from the 12th to the 15th century the medieval world turns: cities revive, trade reaches far, universities are founded — and great epidemics shake the old order toward modern times. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
