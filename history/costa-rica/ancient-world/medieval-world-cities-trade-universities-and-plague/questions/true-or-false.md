# The Medieval World: Cities, Trade, Universities and Plague — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cities revive, new social actors and long-distance trade grow, universities are founded — and great epidemics shake the old order toward modernity.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — From the 12th to the 15th century the medieval world turns: cities revive, trade reaches far, universities are founded — and great epidemics shake the old order toward modern times.
