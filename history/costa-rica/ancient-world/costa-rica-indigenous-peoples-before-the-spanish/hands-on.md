# Hands-on — Costa Rica's Indigenous Peoples Before the Spanish

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one place near your home with an indigenous name, or one food your family eats that the first peoples grew. Write what it tells you about who lived there first.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Costa Rica's story does not begin with the Spanish: it begins with its own indigenous peoples, living in their territories, with societies of their own. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
