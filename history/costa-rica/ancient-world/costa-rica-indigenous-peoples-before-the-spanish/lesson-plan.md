# Lesson plan — Costa Rica's Indigenous Peoples Before the Spanish

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica's Indigenous Peoples Before the Spanish
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Costa Rica's story does not begin with the Spanish: it begins with its own indigenous peoples, living in their territories, with societies of their own.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Costa Rica's story does not begin with the Spanish: it begins with its own indigenous peoples, living in their territories, with societies of their own.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — maize, beans and cacao on your own table — foods of the first peoples, still feeding Costa Rica.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
