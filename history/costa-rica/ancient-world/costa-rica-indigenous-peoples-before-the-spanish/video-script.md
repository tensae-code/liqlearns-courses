# Video script — Costa Rica's Indigenous Peoples Before the Spanish

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Costa Rica's story does not begin with the Spanish: it begins with its own indigenous peoples, living in their territories, with societies of their own.

Before any ship, this land was already a home. The book introduces the pueblos originarios — the original peoples of the territory that is today Costa Rica — in their own territories: peoples who farmed, fished, traded, made gold and pottery and stone spheres, and ordered their societies in their own ways. This is where the national past begins in Costa Rica's own telling: not with colonisation, but with the indigenous societies who were here first, and whose descendants are part of the country still.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
