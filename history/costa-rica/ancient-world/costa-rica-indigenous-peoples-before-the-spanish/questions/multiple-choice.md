# Costa Rica's Indigenous Peoples Before the Spanish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Los pueblos originarios del territorio que hoy es Costa Rica” is right?
a) Costa Rica's own first peoples are introduced in their territories before the Spanish — the national past begins with its indigenous societies, not with colonisation.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Costa Rica's story does not begin with the Spanish: it begins with its own indigenous peoples, living in their territories, with societies of their own.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
