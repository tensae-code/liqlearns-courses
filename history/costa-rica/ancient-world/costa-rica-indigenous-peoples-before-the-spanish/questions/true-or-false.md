# Costa Rica's Indigenous Peoples Before the Spanish — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Costa Rica's own first peoples are introduced in their territories before the Spanish — the national past begins with its indigenous societies, not with colonisation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Costa Rica's story does not begin with the Spanish: it begins with its own indigenous peoples, living in their territories, with societies of their own.
