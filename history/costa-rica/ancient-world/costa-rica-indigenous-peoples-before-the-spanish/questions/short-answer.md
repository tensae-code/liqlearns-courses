# Costa Rica's Indigenous Peoples Before the Spanish — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Los pueblos originarios del territorio que hoy es Costa Rica.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because costa Rica's story does not begin with the Spanish: it begins with its own indigenous peoples, living in their territories, with societies of their own. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
