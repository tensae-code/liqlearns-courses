# Hands-on — The Inca: Andean Civilisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine ruling a land of high mountains where the valleys are a week's walk apart. Write how you would move food and orders up and down.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Inca state organised the Andes — mountains, roads and storehouses — into one great society, studied whole: its organisation, its legacy and its living descendants. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
