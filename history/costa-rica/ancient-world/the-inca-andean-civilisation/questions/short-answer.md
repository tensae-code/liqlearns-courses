# The Inca: Andean Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Sociedades azteca, maya e inca (Inca part).

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Inca state organised the Andes — mountains, roads and storehouses — into one great society, studied whole: its organisation, its legacy and its living descendants. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
