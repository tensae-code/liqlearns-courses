# The Inca: Andean Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Sociedades azteca, maya e inca (Inca part)” is right?
a) The Inca state of the Andes is studied whole — space and time, economic/social/political organisation, cultural legacy and living descendants.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Inca state organised the Andes — mountains, roads and storehouses — into one great society, studied whole: its organisation, its legacy and its living descendants.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
