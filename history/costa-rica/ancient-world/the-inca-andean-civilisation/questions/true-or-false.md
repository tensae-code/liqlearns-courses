# The Inca: Andean Civilisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Inca state of the Andes is studied whole — space and time, economic/social/political organisation, cultural legacy and living descendants.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Inca state organised the Andes — mountains, roads and storehouses — into one great society, studied whole: its organisation, its legacy and its living descendants.
