# Lesson plan — The Inca: Andean Civilisation

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Inca: Andean Civilisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Inca state organised the Andes — mountains, roads and storehouses — into one great society, studied whole: its organisation, its legacy and its living descendants.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Inca state organised the Andes — mountains, roads and storehouses — into one great society, studied whole: its organisation, its legacy and its living descendants.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — mountain roads in your own country — joining steep places into one land is still the same problem the Inca solved.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
