# Video script — The Inca: Andean Civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Inca state organised the Andes — mountains, roads and storehouses — into one great society, studied whole: its organisation, its legacy and its living descendants.

In the Andes, where the land stands on edge, the Inca build a state that turns mountains into a system: terraces climbing the slopes, storehouses full against lean years, roads of stone joining valley to valley. The book studies the Inca whole — their space and time along the cordillera, their economic, social and political organisation, the cultural legacy of their building and weaving, and their living descendants in the Andes today. The hardest landscape in the Americas carried one of its greatest states.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
