# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Sociedades azteca, maya e inca (Aztec part).

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Aztecs built an imperial society centred on Tenochtitlan, studied beside the Maya and Inca: how it was organised, what it left, and where its descendant peoples stand today. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
