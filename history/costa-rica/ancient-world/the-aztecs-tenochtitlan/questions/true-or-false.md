# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Aztecs' imperial society is set beside the Maya and Inca — organisation, legacy and the present situation of its descendant peoples.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Aztecs built an imperial society centred on Tenochtitlan, studied beside the Maya and Inca: how it was organised, what it left, and where its descendant peoples stand today.
