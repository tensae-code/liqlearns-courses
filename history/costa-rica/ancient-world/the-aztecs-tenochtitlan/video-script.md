# Video script — The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Aztecs built an imperial society centred on Tenochtitlan, studied beside the Maya and Inca: how it was organised, what it left, and where its descendant peoples stand today.

On a lake in the high valley of Mexico the Aztecs raise Tenochtitlan, a capital of canals and causeways, and around it an empire. The book sets their imperial society beside the Maya and the Inca and studies it the same way: how it was organised — its rulers, its tribute, its markets and its wars; the legacy it left in Mexico's memory and land; and the present situation of its descendant peoples. An empire is more than its armies: it is a whole society, and it is studied whole.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
