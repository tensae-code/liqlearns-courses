# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Greek civilisation of classical antiquity (5th c. BC–5th c. AD) is presented as an origin of Western societies — its space, social structure and cultural contributions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Greece of classical antiquity is studied as an origin of Western societies: its land and cities, its social structure, and the culture it handed down.
