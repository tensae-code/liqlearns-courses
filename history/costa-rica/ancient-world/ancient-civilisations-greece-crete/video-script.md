# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Greece of classical antiquity is studied as an origin of Western societies: its land and cities, its social structure, and the culture it handed down.

On a rocky land cut by the sea, the Greeks build small cities and a civilisation out of proportion to their numbers. The book presents classical Greece — from the 5th century BC onward — as an origin of Western societies: it studies the Greek space of sea and city-states, the structure of their society, and the contributions they handed down — ways of governing, of arguing, of building, of asking what is true. When later societies in the West look for their roots, one root runs back to Greece.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
