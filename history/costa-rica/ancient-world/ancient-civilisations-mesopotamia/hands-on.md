# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a town of ten thousand people with no writing and no written laws. Write down three problems that would appear in the first week.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Mesopotamia, the land between the rivers, raises the first cities — and a city needs organisers, laws and writing, which are its lasting legacy. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
