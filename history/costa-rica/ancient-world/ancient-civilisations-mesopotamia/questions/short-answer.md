# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Las primeras civilizaciones — Mesopotamia.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because mesopotamia, the land between the rivers, raises the first cities — and a city needs organisers, laws and writing, which are its lasting legacy. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
