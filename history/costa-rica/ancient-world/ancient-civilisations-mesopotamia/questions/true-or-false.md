# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Between the rivers the first cities organise economy, society and culture, leaving laws and writing as their legacy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Mesopotamia, the land between the rivers, raises the first cities — and a city needs organisers, laws and writing, which are its lasting legacy.
