# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mesopotamia, the land between the rivers, raises the first cities — and a city needs organisers, laws and writing, which are its lasting legacy.

Between two rivers — the Tigris and the Euphrates — people first did something new under the sun: they built cities. A city is not just many houses. Grain must be counted, canals shared, quarrels judged, goods weighed and traded. So Mesopotamia organises an economy, a society and a culture: kings and priests, merchants and farmers, temples rising over the plain. And it leaves two tools the world never put down again — laws written for all to see, and writing itself, pressed into clay. The first cities stand between the rivers, and we still live in their invention.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
