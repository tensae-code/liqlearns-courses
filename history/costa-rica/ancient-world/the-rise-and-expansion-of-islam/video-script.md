# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Islam is born in Arabia and expands swiftly, so the medieval world becomes a meeting — and a contest — between the Christian and Muslim worlds.

In Arabia, in the 7th century, a new religion is born: Islam. It does not stay home. Within generations it expands across Arabia and far beyond — to Persia, to North Africa, to Spain — carried by armies, traders and teachers. The medieval world is now two great worlds of faith facing each other, and the book follows their long meeting across the centuries (Tema 2-C, printed p. 38): trading, borrowing, fighting, and shaping each other. The map of the Middle Ages cannot be read without both.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
