# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A new religion, Islam, is born in Arabia and expands, meeting the Christian world across the medieval centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — Islam is born in Arabia and expands swiftly, so the medieval world becomes a meeting — and a contest — between the Christian and Muslim worlds.
