# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “El nacimiento del Islam” is right?
a) A new religion, Islam, is born in Arabia and expands, meeting the Christian world across the medieval centuries.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Islam is born in Arabia and expands swiftly, so the medieval world becomes a meeting — and a contest — between the Christian and Muslim worlds.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
