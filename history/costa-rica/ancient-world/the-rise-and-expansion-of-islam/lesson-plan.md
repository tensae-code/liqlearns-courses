# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Medieval World and the Turn to Modern Times (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Islam is born in Arabia and expands swiftly, so the medieval world becomes a meeting — and a contest — between the Christian and Muslim worlds.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Medieval World and the Turn to Modern Times (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Islam is born in Arabia and expands swiftly, so the medieval world becomes a meeting — and a contest — between the Christian and Muslim worlds.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — words in Spanish from Arabic — the al- words in your own language are footprints of that medieval meeting.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
