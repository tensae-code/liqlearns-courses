# Hands-on — The Maya

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture a map of Central America and Mexico in your mind: write where you think the Maya lived, and one thing you have heard they built or invented.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Maya are studied whole: a great civilisation of this continent — its lands and times, how its people worked, lived and were ruled, the culture it left, and its descendants living today. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
