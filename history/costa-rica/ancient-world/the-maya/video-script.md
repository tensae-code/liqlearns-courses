# Video script — The Maya

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Maya are studied whole: a great civilisation of this continent — its lands and times, how its people worked, lived and were ruled, the culture it left, and its descendants living today.

The Maya are this continent's own great civilisation, and the book studies them whole. Where and when: the forests and highlands of what is now Mexico and Central America, across many centuries. How they lived: farmers of maize, traders, builders of cities with pyramids and calendars, organised in an economy, a society and a politics of their own. What they left: writing, astronomy, art and architecture — a cultural legacy the world still studies. And the story does not end in ruins: Maya descendant peoples live in the region today, heirs of that civilisation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
