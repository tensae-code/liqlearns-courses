# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Sociedades azteca, maya e inca (Maya part)” is right?
a) The Maya are studied whole — where and when they flourished, their economic, social and political organisation, their cultural legacy and their descendants today.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Costa Rica's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Maya are studied whole: a great civilisation of this continent — its lands and times, how its people worked, lived and were ruled, the culture it left, and its descendants living today.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Costa Rica has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
