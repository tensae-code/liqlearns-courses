# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Sociedades azteca, maya e inca (Maya part).

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because the Maya are studied whole: a great civilisation of this continent — its lands and times, how its people worked, lived and were ruled, the culture it left, and its descendants living today. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
