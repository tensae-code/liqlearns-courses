# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Maya are studied whole — where and when they flourished, their economic, social and political organisation, their cultural legacy and their descendants today.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The Maya are studied whole: a great civilisation of this continent — its lands and times, how its people worked, lived and were ruled, the culture it left, and its descendants living today.
