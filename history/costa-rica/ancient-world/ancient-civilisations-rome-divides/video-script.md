# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The western Roman Empire declines and falls — and the medieval world begins in its ruins.

The empire that organised the West does not end with a single blow. The book tells a decline: the western Roman Empire weakens — its frontiers pressed, its unity strained, its power thinning — until it falls. But an ending is also a beginning: out of the empire's ruins, new peoples, new kingdoms and new ways of living assemble themselves, and the medieval world begins. Every age in this course is born from the one before it, and this is the first great birth.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
