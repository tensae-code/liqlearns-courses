## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The western Roman Empire declines and falls — and the medieval world begins in its ruins.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any great organisation in your own town that grew too big to hold together — empires face the same strain.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
