# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rome's western empire declines and falls, and out of its ruins the medieval world begins.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — The western Roman Empire declines and falls — and the medieval world begins in its ruins.
