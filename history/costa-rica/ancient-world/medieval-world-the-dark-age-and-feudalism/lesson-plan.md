# Lesson plan — The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

**Course:** History — Costa Rica · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** When invasion makes life unsafe, Europe reorganises into feudalism: lords protect, vassals serve, peasants work the land — each tied to the others and to the soil.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: When invasion makes life unsafe, Europe reorganises into feudalism: lords protect, vassals serve, peasants work the land — each tied to the others and to the soil.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a bargain of protection for work — the feudal tie in small, still recognisable wherever safety is exchanged for service.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
