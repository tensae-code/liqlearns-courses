# The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Invasion and insecurity reorganise Europe into feudal societies of lords, vassals and peasants tied to the land.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Costa Rica.
**Answer: False** — When invasion makes life unsafe, Europe reorganises into feudalism: lords protect, vassals serve, peasants work the land — each tied to the others and to the soil.
