# The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La organización de las sociedades feudales.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Costa Rica?
**Answer:** Because when invasion makes life unsafe, Europe reorganises into feudalism: lords protect, vassals serve, peasants work the land — each tied to the others and to the soil. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
