# Video script — The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When invasion makes life unsafe, Europe reorganises into feudalism: lords protect, vassals serve, peasants work the land — each tied to the others and to the soil.

After Rome falls, danger rides the roads: invasions come wave after wave, and no distant emperor can protect a village. So Europe reorganises itself around protection. A lord with a castle and armed men offers safety; a vassal swears service in return for land; peasants work that land and give its produce, tied to the soil and to the lord above them. Feudal society is a ladder of promises — protection downward, service and food upward — built because insecurity demanded it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
