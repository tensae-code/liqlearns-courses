# Hands-on — Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the “Civilizing Mission”

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A speaker claims his country has the right to rule yours: it will bring trade, schools, and 'civilisation'. Write down two questions you would ask him. Iceland's book puts exactly this case to its pupils — Europe's arguments for empire, laid out to be weighed, not swallowed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Europeans justified their empires as profit, duty and destiny — a 'civilizing mission' — and Iceland's pupils must weigh those arguments themselves, because the weighing is the lesson. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
