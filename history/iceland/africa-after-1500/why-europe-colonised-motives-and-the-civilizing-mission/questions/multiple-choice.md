# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the “Civilizing Mission” — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three justifications did Europeans give for empire?
a) profit, duty and destiny  b) faith, hope and charity  c) ships, guns and gold alone  d) fear, hunger and cold
**Answer: a) profit, duty and destiny** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What must pupils do with the empire's justifications in worksheet 22?
a) memorise them  b) weigh them — who profits, who is judged, what the ruled would say  c) copy them into the proclamation  d) translate them
**Answer: b) weigh them — who profits, who is judged, what the ruled would say** — That is what this lesson teaches.
