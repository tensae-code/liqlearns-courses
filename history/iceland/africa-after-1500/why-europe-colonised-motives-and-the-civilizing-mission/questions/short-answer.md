# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the “Civilizing Mission” — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the civilizing mission?
**Answer:** The claimed duty to carry law, medicine, schools and faith to peoples deemed not yet 'civilised' — the moral banner under which profit and conquest marched.

**2.** Why are Icelandic pupils well placed to weigh these arguments?
**Answer:** Because Iceland itself was ruled from Copenhagen — the course soon asks outright whether Iceland was a colony, turning the same weighing on their own country.
