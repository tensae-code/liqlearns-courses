# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the “Civilizing Mission” — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 'civilizing mission' claimed Europeans had a duty to rule and remake other peoples.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Europe's powers admitted openly that empire was simply theft.
**Answer: False** — Europeans justified their empires as profit, duty and destiny — a 'civilizing mission' — and Iceland's pupils must weigh those arguments themselves, because the weighing is the lesson.
