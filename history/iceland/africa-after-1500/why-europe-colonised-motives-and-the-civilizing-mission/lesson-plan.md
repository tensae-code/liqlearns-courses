# Lesson plan — Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the “Civilizing Mission”

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the “Civilizing Mission”
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Nations and Empires (CURRICULUM.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the lesson asks pupils to admire the empire-builders → The book's method is the opposite: the justifications are laid out as arguments to be weighed — profit dressed as duty, destiny claimed over other peoples' homes. Weighing them is the worksheet's work.
- Go back to the one thing: Europeans justified their empires as profit, duty and destiny — a 'civilizing mission' — and Iceland's pupils must weigh those arguments themselves, because the weighing is the lesson. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
