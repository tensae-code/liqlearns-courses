# The Scramble and Berlin, 1884–85 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did 'effective occupation' mean under the Berlin rules?
**Answer:** A power's claim to African land counted when it actually occupied the place and notified the other powers — paper claims alone no longer sufficed.

**2.** Who was missing from the Berlin room, and why does it matter?
**Answer:** Every African ruler and people: the continent was divided by Europeans among themselves, so its borders were drawn without the consent of those who lived inside them.
