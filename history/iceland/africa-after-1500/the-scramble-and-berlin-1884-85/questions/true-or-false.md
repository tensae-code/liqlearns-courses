# The Scramble and Berlin, 1884–85 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** African rulers sat at the Berlin table and agreed the rules.
**Answer: False** — Europe's powers raced to seize Africa — the Scramble — and at Berlin in 1884–85 they set the rules of the race among themselves, with men like Cecil Rhodes grabbing for a continent no African had offered them.

**2.** Cecil Rhodes dreamed of a British Africa from the Cape to Cairo.
**Answer: True**
