# The Scramble and Berlin, 1884–85 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the Scramble for Africa?
a) a famous horse race  b) Europe's powers racing to seize African land  c) African peoples uniting  d) a gold rush in Iceland
**Answer: b) Europe's powers racing to seize African land** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Berlin Conference of 1884–85 do?
a) freed the colonies  b) set the rules of the race — claims counted when effectively occupied and notified  c) gave Africa its independence  d) banned all colonies
**Answer: b) set the rules of the race — claims counted when effectively occupied and notified** — That is what this lesson teaches.
