# Lesson plan — The Scramble and Berlin, 1884–85

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The Scramble and Berlin, 1884–85
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Nations and Empires (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Europe's powers raced to seize Africa — the Scramble — and at Berlin in 1884–85 they set the rules of the race among themselves, with men like Cecil Rhodes grabbing for a continent no African had offered them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Africa's borders grew naturally from its peoples and rivers → The book's account is of lines drawn in Europe: the Berlin rules let a power claim land by planting occupation on the coast and notifying the others — the map was cut first, the continent fitted to it after.
- Go back to the one thing: Europe's powers raced to seize Africa — the Scramble — and at Berlin in 1884–85 they set the rules of the race among themselves, with men like Cecil Rhodes grabbing for a continent no African had offered them.
