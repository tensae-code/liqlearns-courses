# The Viking Age and the Norse World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every Norse person in the Viking Age was a Viking. (Sub lesson 2) (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Between about 800 and 1050 the Norse people of the North — Iceland's own ancestors — built ships that opened the whole northern world: they raided, traded and settled from Russia to America, governed themselves at the Althing, and told their story in the sagas.

**2.** At the Althing, free farmers governed themselves and the allsherjargoði opened the assembly. (Sub lesson 5) (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer: True**
