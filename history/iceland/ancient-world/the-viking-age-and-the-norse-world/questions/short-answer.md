# The Viking Age and the Norse World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Norse sailors navigate without maps? (Sub lesson 3) (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** By the sun, by birds flying toward land, by the sea's swell and colour, and by memory — sailing directions carried in the head and handed down.

**2.** What happens in the saga scene with the seeress Þorbjörg? (Sub lesson 4) (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** In a hungry winter in Greenland, Þorbjörg is feasted as an honoured guest and questioned about the future — when the famine will end and what comes next.
