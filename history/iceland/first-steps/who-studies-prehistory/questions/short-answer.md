# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What do the objects in the Fjallkona's grave tell us?
**Answer:** They are evidence: her ornaments, goods and burial tell who she was, how she lived and what her people believed — the dead were dressed with care and believed to travel on.

**2.** How does an archaeologist's evidence differ from a written page?
**Answer:** A written page tells what its writer chose to say; objects in the ground tell what a life actually held, whether anyone meant to record it or not.
