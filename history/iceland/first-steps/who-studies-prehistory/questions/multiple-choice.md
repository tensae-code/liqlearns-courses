# Who Studies Prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who studies the ages before writing, reading the past from things left in the ground?
a) the archaeologist  b) the skald  c) the lawspeaker  d) the map-maker
**Answer: a) the archaeologist** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was found and excavated in Iceland's own opening story of this lesson?
a) a Viking ship under a church  b) a woman's pagan grave — the Fjallkona's grave  c) a chest of silver coins  d) the first written law book
**Answer: b) a woman's pagan grave — the Fjallkona's grave** — That is what this lesson teaches.
