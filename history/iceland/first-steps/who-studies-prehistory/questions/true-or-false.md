# Who Studies Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A grave with no writing in it can still tell us about the person buried there.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Archaeologists only study things that have words written on them.
**Answer: False** — The people who study the ages before writing are archaeologists, and they read the past from things left in the ground — as Iceland's children do with a real pagan grave, the grave of the Fjallkona.
