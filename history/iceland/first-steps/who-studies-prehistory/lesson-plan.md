# Lesson plan — Who Studies Prehistory

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Who Studies Prehistory
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Reading Iceland's Ground (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the people who study the ages before writing are archaeologists, and they read the past from things left in the ground — as Iceland's children do with a real pagan grave, the grave of the Fjallkona.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Reading Iceland's Ground — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the past before writing is simply unknown and unknowable → Objects are evidence: a grave's brooch, beads and bones tell who was buried, how she lived and what she believed — that reading is the archaeologist's craft.
- Go back to the one thing: the people who study the ages before writing are archaeologists, and they read the past from things left in the ground — as Iceland's children do with a real pagan grave, the grave of the Fjallkona. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
