# Fact or Opinion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the borderline between fact and opinion matter?
**Answer:** Because opinions often dress as facts, and judgements hide inside plain-looking statements; a reader who cannot sort them can be led by any writer or speaker.

**2.** What does the worksheet title Þjálfaðu hugann tell pupils to do?
**Answer:** Train your mind — the sorting of fact from opinion is presented as exercise for the mind, done in pairs with the hard cases defended aloud.
