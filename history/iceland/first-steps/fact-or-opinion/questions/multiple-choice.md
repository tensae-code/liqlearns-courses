# Fact or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is an opinion?
a) the assembly met at Þingvellir  b) the Spanish flu reached Iceland in 1918  c) the assembly was the finest gathering in the world  d) the census was taken in 1703
**Answer: c) the assembly was the finest gathering in the world** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What do pupils do with the borderline statements in this exercise?
a) throw them away  b) defend their sorting of them, in pairs  c) memorise them  d) copy them out
**Answer: b) defend their sorting of them, in pairs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
