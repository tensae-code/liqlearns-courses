# Fact or Opinion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A fact is a statement that can be checked against evidence.
**Answer: True**

**2.** If a statement is printed in a book, it must be a fact.
**Answer: False** — A statement of fact can be checked, but an opinion is what someone thinks — and a reader of history must be able to sort the one from the other, especially where the borderline is defended.
