# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the three questions to ask of any source?
a) who made it, when, and why  b) how long, how heavy, how old  c) who won, who lost, who fled  d) where, what colour, what price
**Answer: a) who made it, when, and why** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does Iceland's older history book begin with source analysis instead of a story?
a) the first chapter was lost  b) every written history is built from sources, and they must be judged first  c) stories are forbidden in it  d) to make the book longer
**Answer: b) every written history is built from sources, and they must be judged first** — That is what this lesson teaches.
