# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** A source was written to praise a ruler. How should a historian use it?
**Answer:** Judge it first: its maker's purpose was praise, so its claims need checking against other sources — and the praise itself is evidence of what the ruler wanted believed.

**2.** What is Greining heimilda?
**Answer:** The analysis of sources — the worksheet that opens the book, teaching pupils to judge who made a source, when and why, before any story is built from it.
