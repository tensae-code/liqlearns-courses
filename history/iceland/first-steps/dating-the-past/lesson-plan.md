## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks 'long ago' is all one blur with no order inside it → The timeline gives the blur an order: the Viking Age runs about 800–1050, the ages after it follow one by one, and the class's own line carries the story from 800 to the learners themselves.
- Go back to the one thing: the past is put in order on a timeline — and Iceland's own class builds one that runs from the Viking Age all the way to their own day, so every story can be hung in its place. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
