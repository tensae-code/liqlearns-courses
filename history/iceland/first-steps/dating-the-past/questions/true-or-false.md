# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dating the past means hanging each event on a timeline in its order.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Viking Age ended because Icelanders disappeared.
**Answer: False** — The past is put in order on a timeline — and Iceland's own class builds one that runs from the Viking Age all the way to their own day, so every story can be hung in its place.
