# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the class build at the end of Iceland's Viking Age book?
a) a model longhouse  b) a timeline from the Viking Age to their own day  c) a map of Greenland  d) a list of kings
**Answer: b) a timeline from the Viking Age to their own day** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** About when does the Viking Age end, as this book teaches it?
a) around 800  b) around 1050  c) in 1944  d) it never ended
**Answer: b) around 1050** — That is what this lesson teaches.
