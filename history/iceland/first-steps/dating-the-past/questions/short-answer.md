# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the Viking Age end when it does?
**Answer:** Because Europe around the Norse world changed — the raiding and settling age gave way to a different age, not because the people vanished.

**2.** What is the Tímalína project?
**Answer:** The class's own timeline, built by the pupils, running from the Viking Age to their own day, with every story hung in its place on the line.
