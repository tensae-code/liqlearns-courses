# Lesson plan — Germany Unifies: Bismarck, Blood and Iron

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Germany Unifies: Bismarck, Blood and Iron
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Nations and Empires (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Germany's many states were unified under Prussia by Bismarck, who said the great questions of the day would be decided by blood and iron — and in 1871 an emperor was proclaimed in the palace of Versailles.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks German unification was a popular rising like Garibaldi's → The book's account is Bismarck's: unification from above, by Prussia's army and a minister's engineered wars — 'blood and iron', his own words — not a people's volunteer campaign.
- Go back to the one thing: Germany's many states were unified under Prussia by Bismarck, who said the great questions of the day would be decided by blood and iron — and in 1871 an emperor was proclaimed in the palace of Versailles. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
