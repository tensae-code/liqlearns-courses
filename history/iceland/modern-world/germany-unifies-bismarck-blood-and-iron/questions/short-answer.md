# Germany Unifies: Bismarck, Blood and Iron — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Bismarck's wars gather Germany under Prussia?
**Answer:** By engineered wars, each gathering more states: Austria pushed out of German affairs, then France defeated and occupied — unification by Prussia's army, step by step.

**2.** Why was Versailles chosen for the proclamation of the emperor?
**Answer:** For the sting: proclaiming Germany's emperor in the palace of France's kings, in conquered France, announced the new great power to all Europe.
