# Germany Unifies: Bismarck, Blood and Iron — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** By what method did Bismarck say the great questions would be decided?
a) speeches and votes  b) blood and iron  c) trade and treaties  d) prayer and patience
**Answer: b) blood and iron** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where was the German emperor proclaimed in 1871?
a) in Berlin cathedral  b) in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles, in conquered France  c) in Vienna  d) in Rome
**Answer: b) in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles, in conquered France** — That is what this lesson teaches.
