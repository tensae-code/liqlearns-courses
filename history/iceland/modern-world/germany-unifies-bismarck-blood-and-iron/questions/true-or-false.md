# Germany Unifies: Bismarck, Blood and Iron — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** German unification was led from above by Prussia and Bismarck.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Austria led the German unification and Prussia was pushed out.
**Answer: False** — Germany's many states were unified under Prussia by Bismarck, who said the great questions of the day would be decided by blood and iron — and in 1871 an emperor was proclaimed in the palace of Versailles.
