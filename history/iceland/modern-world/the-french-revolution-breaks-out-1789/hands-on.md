# Hands-on — The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A country where the king and the nobles pay almost no tax, and the poorest pay nearly all of it, runs out of bread and money at the same time. Write down what you think happens next. In France in 1789, what happened next changed Europe forever — this lesson tells it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: in 1789 France rose against its old order, declared the rights of man, and shook every throne in Europe — the old world of kings by birth was challenged in the name of the citizen. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
