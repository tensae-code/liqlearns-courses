# Lesson plan — The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Old Iceland and the Age of Revolutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in 1789 France rose against its old order, declared the rights of man, and shook every throne in Europe — the old world of kings by birth was challenged in the name of the citizen.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Old Iceland and the Age of Revolutions — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the French Revolution was only riots and chaos → The book teaches its double face: a rising against an unjust order AND a declaration — the rights of man — that set the terms every later European struggle, Iceland's included, argued in.
- Go back to the one thing: in 1789 France rose against its old order, declared the rights of man, and shook every throne in Europe — the old world of kings by birth was challenged in the name of the citizen. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
