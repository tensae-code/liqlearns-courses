# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the Third Estate claim to speak for the nation?
**Answer:** Because it was everyone outside the privileged clergy and nobles — the vast majority who paid the taxes and carried the state's costs.

**2.** What was the declaration of the rights of man aimed at?
**Answer:** Every throne in Europe: it claimed men are born free and equal in rights and that law is the people's will, challenging rule by birth everywhere.
