# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the old order, the Third Estate carried most of the state's costs.
**Answer: True**

**2.** After 1789, Europe's kings felt more secure than before.
**Answer: False** — In 1789 France rose against its old order, declared the rights of man, and shook every throne in Europe — the old world of kings by birth was challenged in the name of the citizen.
