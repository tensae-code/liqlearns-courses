# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did the French Revolution break out?
a) 1703  b) 1789  c) 1809  d) 1918
**Answer: b) 1789** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the revolution declare?
a) the rights of man  b) the divine right of kings  c) the end of all taxes forever  d) France an empire
**Answer: a) the rights of man** — That is what this lesson teaches.
