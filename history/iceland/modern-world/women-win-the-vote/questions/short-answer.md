# Women Win the Vote — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the course end with this lesson?
**Answer:** Because it completes the course's quiet theme: every earlier lesson widened who counts — citizens, workers, nations — and this last one widens 'the people' itself to include women.

**2.** What claim had been answered for generations with 'custom dressed as nature'?
**Answer:** Women's claim to vote and hold office — half the nation stood outside both, told their exclusion was natural rather than chosen.
