# Women Win the Vote — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How does the book teach the question of women's votes and office?
a) as a date to memorise  b) as a role-play debate in period voices  c) as a map exercise  d) it does not teach it
**Answer: b) as a role-play debate in period voices** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the worksheet's question, exactly?
a) should women work in factories?  b) should women be allowed to hold office?  c) should women emigrate?  d) should women attend the Althing as guests?
**Answer: b) should women be allowed to hold office?** — That is what this lesson teaches.
