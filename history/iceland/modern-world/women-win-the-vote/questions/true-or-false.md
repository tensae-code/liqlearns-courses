# Women Win the Vote — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Icelandic women were among the earliest in the world to win the vote.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The vote was granted to women without any argument or struggle.
**Answer: False** — The democracy Iceland built was unfinished while half its people could not vote: the book closes by making the class debate, in period voices, whether women may vote and hold office — the question the century answered yes.
