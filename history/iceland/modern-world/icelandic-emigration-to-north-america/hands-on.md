# Hands-on — Icelandic Emigration to North America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Meet Sigurður, born in 1853 to poor tenant parents in Skagafjörður, in Iceland's north. The worksheet asks one question: why would Sigurður want to move to Canada? Write down his reasons before you open the lesson — then check yours against his world.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: in the late nineteenth century thousands of Icelanders — the poor of the old society, like Sigurður of Skagafjörður — weighed leaving everything for Canada, and many went forever. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
