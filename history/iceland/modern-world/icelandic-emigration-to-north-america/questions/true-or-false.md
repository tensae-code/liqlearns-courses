# Icelandic Emigration to North America — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Most Icelanders who emigrated to Canada in this period later returned home.
**Answer: False** — In the late nineteenth century thousands of Icelanders — the poor of the old society, like Sigurður of Skagafjörður — weighed leaving everything for Canada, and many went forever.

**2.** The emigrants founded a settlement called New Iceland in Canada.
**Answer: True**
