# Icelandic Emigration to North America — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why would Sigurður want to leave Iceland?
**Answer:** He was a poor tenant's son with no land and no prospect but service in the old society; Canada offered land of his own and a future he could build himself.

**2.** What does the emigration measure about the old society?
**Answer:** Its failure: when a country cannot feed its children's hopes — thousands leaving forever — the old order of tenancy and the vistarband stands judged by its own people.
