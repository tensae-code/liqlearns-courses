# Icelandic Emigration to North America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who is Sigurður, in the book's worksheet?
a) a Danish governor  b) a poor tenant farmer's son from Skagafjörður, born 1853, weighing emigration to Canada  c) a ship's captain  d) a Canadian agent
**Answer: b) a poor tenant farmer's son from Skagafjörður, born 1853, weighing emigration to Canada** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Canada offer that old Iceland could not?
a) warmer seas for fishing only  b) land a man could own and a future built by his own hands  c) a university education  d) nothing — it was a trick
**Answer: b) land a man could own and a future built by his own hands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
