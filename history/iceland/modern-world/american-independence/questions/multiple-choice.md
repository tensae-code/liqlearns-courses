# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Britain's American colonies do?
a) asked for a new king  b) rebelled and won independence, then built a democratic republic  c) joined France  d) moved back to Britain
**Answer: b) rebelled and won independence, then built a democratic republic** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** On what claim did the new American republic rest?
a) government rests on the people's consent  b) kings rule by God's grant alone  c) the richest should rule  d) armies make law
**Answer: a) government rests on the people's consent** — That is what this lesson teaches.
