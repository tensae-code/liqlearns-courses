# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the colonies say Britain's rule was tyranny?
**Answer:** Because they were taxed and governed without representation — laws were made for them in a Parliament where they had no voice.

**2.** Why does Iceland's book teach the American story to Icelandic pupils?
**Answer:** Because the idea it carries — a people founding a state on consent — is the same idea Iceland's own independence struggle would later use.
