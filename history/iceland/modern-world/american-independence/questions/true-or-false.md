# American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The American colonies had seats in the Parliament that taxed them.
**Answer: False** — Britain's American colonies rebelled against their king and invented something new — a democratic republic, built on the claim that government rests on the people's consent.

**2.** Instead of choosing a new king, the Americans built a republic.
**Answer: True**
