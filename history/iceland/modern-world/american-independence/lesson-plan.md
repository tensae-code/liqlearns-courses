# Lesson plan — American Independence

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** American Independence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Old Iceland and the Age of Revolutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain's American colonies rebelled against their king and invented something new — a democratic republic, built on the claim that government rests on the people's consent.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Old Iceland and the Age of Revolutions — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Americans rebelled only to pay less tax → The taxes lit the fire, but the claim underneath was bigger: no government is lawful without the governed people's consent — and the republic they built was the claim made into a state.
- Go back to the one thing: Britain's American colonies rebelled against their king and invented something new — a democratic republic, built on the claim that government rests on the people's consent. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
