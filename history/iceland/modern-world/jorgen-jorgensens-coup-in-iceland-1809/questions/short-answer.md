# Jørgen Jørgensen's Coup in Iceland, 1809 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How long did Jørgensen's rule last, and how did it end?
**Answer:** Only months: he had no army and no recognition, and a British warship's captain had him removed and carried away; Danish rule resumed.

**2.** Why does the book call this event a valdarán?
**Answer:** Because it was a coup — power seized by arresting the officials and taking rule into his own hands, not granted by any law or assembly.
