# Jørgen Jørgensen's Coup in Iceland, 1809 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who seized power in Iceland in 1809?
a) Jón Sigurðsson  b) Jørgen Jørgensen  c) Napoleon  d) the British king
**Answer: b) Jørgen Jørgensen** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Jørgensen proclaim?
a) Iceland a British colony  b) Iceland independent  c) war on Denmark  d) the end of the Althing
**Answer: b) Iceland independent** — That is what this lesson teaches.
