# Jørgen Jørgensen's Coup in Iceland, 1809 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jørgensen's rule lasted for many years and was recognised abroad.
**Answer: False** — In the summer of 1809, with Iceland cut off by war, the adventurer Jørgen Jørgensen seized power, proclaimed Iceland independent — and was removed again within months.

**2.** The coup was possible because war had cut Iceland off and authority in Reykjavík stood empty.
**Answer: True**
