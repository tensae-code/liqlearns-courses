# Hands-on — The 1918 Spanish Flu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
1918 was Iceland's year of celebration: sovereignty at last. It was also the year ships brought a sickness that emptied streets within weeks. Write down how a country can hold triumph and disaster in the same year. This lesson is that year's shadow — the Spanish flu in Iceland.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: in 1918, the year of sovereignty, the Spanish flu reached Iceland and killed in a few weeks more Icelanders than any war had ever touched — a nation's triumph and its mourning in the same year. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
