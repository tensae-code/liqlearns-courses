# The 1918 Spanish Flu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the book set the flu lesson in the year of sovereignty?
**Answer:** Because 1918 holds both: sovereignty celebrated and the flu's graves dug in the same year — history that keeps only the flags tells half the story.

**2.** Who fell hardest in the flu, and how fast did it move?
**Answer:** The young and strong fell hardest, and within weeks the sickness was through towns and farms, striking whole households.
