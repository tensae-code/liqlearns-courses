# The 1918 Spanish Flu — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The flu killed in a few weeks more Icelanders than any war had touched.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Iceland's remoteness kept the flu out entirely.
**Answer: False** — In 1918, the year of sovereignty, the Spanish flu reached Iceland and killed in a few weeks more Icelanders than any war had ever touched — a nation's triumph and its mourning in the same year.
