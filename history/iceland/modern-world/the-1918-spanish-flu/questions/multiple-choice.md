# The 1918 Spanish Flu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did the Spanish flu reach Iceland?
a) 1809  b) 1918  c) 1944  d) 1703
**Answer: b) 1918** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the flu reach Iceland?
a) overland from Norway  b) by ship, as everything reached Iceland  c) it began in Iceland  d) by aeroplane
**Answer: b) by ship, as everything reached Iceland** — That is what this lesson teaches.
