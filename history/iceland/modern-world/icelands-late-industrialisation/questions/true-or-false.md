# Iceland's Late Industrialisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Steam trawlers could catch far more than sail and oar ever could.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Iceland's industrial revolution strengthened the old farm society and emptied the towns.
**Answer: False** — The industrial revolution came late to Iceland — not through coal and cotton, but through fish: steam trawlers, processing and new towns rising around the harbours.
