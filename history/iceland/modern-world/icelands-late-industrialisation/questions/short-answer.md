# Iceland's Late Industrialisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did new towns rise in Iceland during its late industrialisation?
**Answer:** The trawlers' catches needed hands ashore — gutting, salting, drying, packing — so workers gathered where the ships landed, and towns grew around the harbours.

**2.** How did the change move the centre of Icelandic life?
**Answer:** From the isolated turf farm to the fishing town: within a generation Icelanders left the farms for wages, streets and shops, and the old vistarband world began to dissolve.
