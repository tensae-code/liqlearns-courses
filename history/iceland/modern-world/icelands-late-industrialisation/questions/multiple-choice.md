# Iceland's Late Industrialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Through what did the industrial revolution arrive in Iceland?
a) coal mining  b) fish — steam trawlers, processing and harbour towns  c) cotton mills  d) gold rushes
**Answer: b) fish — steam trawlers, processing and harbour towns** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the chapter title Síðbúin mean about Iceland's industrial revolution?
a) it never happened  b) it came late  c) it came first in the world  d) it was forbidden
**Answer: b) it came late** — That is what this lesson teaches.
