# Lesson plan — Iceland's Late Industrialisation

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Iceland's Late Industrialisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Machines, Emigration and the New Ideas (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the industrial revolution came late to Iceland — not through coal and cotton, but through fish: steam trawlers, processing and new towns rising around the harbours.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Machines, Emigration and the New Ideas — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Iceland simply missed the industrial revolution → The book's word is Síðbúin — late-coming, not absent: Iceland's industrial revolution arrived through fish, steam trawlers and harbour towns, later than England's and shaped by the sea.
- Go back to the one thing: the industrial revolution came late to Iceland — not through coal and cotton, but through fish: steam trawlers, processing and new towns rising around the harbours. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
