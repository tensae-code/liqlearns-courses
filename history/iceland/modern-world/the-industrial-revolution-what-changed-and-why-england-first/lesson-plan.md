## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the industrial revolution was just new inventions → Inventions were the spark; the revolution was the reordering of life — populations growing, work leaving the home for the factory, towns exploding, and children put to machine-work. The book's chapter title pairs the two: population growth AND technology.
