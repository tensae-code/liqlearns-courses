# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the factory system, the workers owned the machines they worked.
**Answer: False** — Machines, factories and fast-growing populations remade work itself — beginning in England — and even children's work was swallowed by the factory system.

**2.** Children worked in the new factories, tending machines for long days.
**Answer: True**
