# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What changed when work moved from the cottage to the factory?
**Answer:** Work was gathered into buildings of machines owned by one man; workers sold their labour by the hour the machine set, and towns swelled as country people poured in for wages.

**2.** Why does the book pair population growth with technology in one title?
**Answer:** Because the two drove each other: more people than ever needed work and goods, and machines could suddenly do the work of many hands at once.
