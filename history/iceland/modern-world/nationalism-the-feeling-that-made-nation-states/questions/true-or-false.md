# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nationalism redrew Europe's map in the nineteenth century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The book teaches that nationalism is simply and always good.
**Answer: False** — Nationalism — the feeling that a people is a nation and needs its own state — redrew the map of Europe in the nineteenth century; and the book makes pupils weigh it honestly: a feeling that can build a state can also turn on its neighbours.
