# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did nationalism change Europe's map?
**Answer:** Peoples ruled as provinces demanded their own states, and peoples sharing one language joined their small states into nations — the map began to be drawn by peoples, not only by kings.

**2.** Give the two sides pupils must weigh about nationalism.
**Answer:** Positive: it can free a people to build its own state. Negative: it can harden into contempt for neighbours and for a nation's own minorities.
