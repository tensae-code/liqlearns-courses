# Liberalism and Socialism — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the factory age bred two great answering ideas — liberalism, trusting the free individual and the free market, and socialism, demanding that workers join together; Iceland's pupils learn them by playing factory hands who must decide whether to found a union and strike — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
