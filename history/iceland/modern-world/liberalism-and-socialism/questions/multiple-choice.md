# Liberalism and Socialism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the worksheet 'Viltu fara í verkfall?' ask?
a) do you want a holiday?  b) do you want to go on strike?  c) do you want to buy a factory?  d) do you want to emigrate?
**Answer: b) do you want to go on strike?** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is socialism's answer to the lone worker's weakness?
a) work harder alone  b) workers must join together to hold real power  c) ask the owner kindly  d) wait for the king
**Answer: b) workers must join together to hold real power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
