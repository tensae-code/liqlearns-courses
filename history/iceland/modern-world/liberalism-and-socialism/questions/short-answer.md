# Liberalism and Socialism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does the book teach liberalism and socialism?
**Answer:** By role-play: pupils play factory hands at the century's start and must decide, in role, whether to found a union and strike — the ideas are learned as decisions, not definitions.

**2.** What risks does the strike decision carry inside the role-play?
**Answer:** The sack and the empty table if it fails — set against the hope of winning better wages and hours together if it succeeds.
