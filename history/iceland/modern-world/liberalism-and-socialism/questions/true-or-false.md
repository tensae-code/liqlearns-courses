# Liberalism and Socialism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Liberalism trusted the free individual and the free market, with the state keeping out of the way.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In the role-play, pupils play factory owners deciding workers' wages.
**Answer: False** — The factory age bred two great answering ideas — liberalism, trusting the free individual and the free market, and socialism, demanding that workers join together; Iceland's pupils learn them by playing factory hands who must decide whether to found a union and strike.
