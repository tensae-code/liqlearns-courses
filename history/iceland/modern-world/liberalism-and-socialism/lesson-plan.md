# Lesson plan — Liberalism and Socialism

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Liberalism and Socialism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Machines, Emigration and the New Ideas (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the factory age bred two great answering ideas — liberalism, trusting the free individual and the free market, and socialism, demanding that workers join together; Iceland's pupils learn them by playing factory hands who must decide whether to found a union and strike.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Machines, Emigration and the New Ideas — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
