# Iceland's Road to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the road's last three steps, in order. (Sub lesson 2) (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Home rule, with Icelanders governing their own affairs; sovereignty in 1918, a free state in personal union with the Danish king; and the republic proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944.

**2.** Why do the worksheets ask whether Jón Sigurðsson was a freedom hero, instead of simply calling him one? (Sub lesson 1) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Because the book teaches pupils to judge, not copy: they must weigh his peaceful, legal struggle — pen, argument, restored Althing — and defend their verdict, as with every hero and source in the course.
