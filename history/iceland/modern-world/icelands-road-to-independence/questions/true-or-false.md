# Iceland's Road to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Iceland won its independence by a war against Denmark. (Sub lesson 2) (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Iceland walked out of Danish rule by peaceful steps: Jón Sigurðsson led the independence struggle with pen and argument, the Fjallkona became the nation's face — and the road ended with home rule, sovereignty in 1918, and the republic proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944.

**2.** The Fjallkona — the Mountain Woman — became the national symbol of Iceland. (Sub lesson 1) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**
