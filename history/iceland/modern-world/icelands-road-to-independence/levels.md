# Iceland's Road to Independence — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Jón Sigurðsson and the Fjallkona
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the struggle had a leader, and the worksheets name the question about him: **Var Jón Sigurðsson frelsishetja?** — was Jón Sigurðsson a freedom hero? His weapons were the pen and the argument: from Copenhagen he edited, wrote and organised, proving from Iceland's old laws and history that Iceland was a nation with rights Denmark itself had once recognised — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
