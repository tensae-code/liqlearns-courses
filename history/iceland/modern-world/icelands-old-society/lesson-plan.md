# Lesson plan — Iceland's Old Society

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Iceland's Old Society
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Old Iceland and the Age of Revolutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in old Iceland most people owned nothing: tenant farmers worked other men's land, servants were bound to farms by the vistarband, and the census of 1703 counted them all, name by name.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Old Iceland and the Age of Revolutions — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Iceland was always a land of free, equal farmers → The book's old society is a ladder of dependence: most people owned nothing, tenants held land they could lose, and the vistarband tied servants to farms by law.
- Go back to the one thing: in old Iceland most people owned nothing: tenant farmers worked other men's land, servants were bound to farms by the vistarband, and the census of 1703 counted them all, name by name. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
