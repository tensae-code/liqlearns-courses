# Iceland's Old Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What bound servants to farms in old Iceland?
a) the vistarband  b) the Althing  c) a sailors' oath  d) the church tithe
**Answer: a) the vistarband** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is the census of 1703 so valuable to historians of Iceland?
a) it was written in verse  b) it counts the whole people farm by farm — owners, tenants, servants, paupers  c) it lists only kings  d) it was the first book printed in Iceland
**Answer: b) it counts the whole people farm by farm — owners, tenants, servants, paupers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
