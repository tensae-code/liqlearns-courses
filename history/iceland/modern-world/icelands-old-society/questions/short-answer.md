# Iceland's Old Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Describe the ladder of old Icelandic society.
**Answer:** Landowners — king, church and wealthy farmers — held the land; tenant farmers rented farms they could lose; servants at the bottom were bound to farms by the vistarband.

**2.** How do pupils use the 1703 census in this lesson?
**Answer:** As a source: they read its farm-by-farm columns — owners, tenants, servants, paupers — and see the old society itself, name by name.
