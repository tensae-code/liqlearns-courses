# Iceland's Old Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In old Iceland, most people owned nothing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Tenant farmers owned the farms they worked and could never be moved off them.
**Answer: False** — In old Iceland most people owned nothing: tenant farmers worked other men's land, servants were bound to farms by the vistarband, and the census of 1703 counted them all, name by name.
