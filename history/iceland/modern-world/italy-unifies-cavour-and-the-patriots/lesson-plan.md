# Lesson plan — Italy Unifies: Cavour and the Patriots

**Course:** History — Iceland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Italy Unifies: Cavour and the Patriots
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Nations and Empires (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy, long a patchwork of kingdoms, dukedoms and foreign-ruled provinces, was stitched into one kingdom by Cavour's statecraft and Garibaldi's volunteers — nationalism with a flag and an army.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Nations and Empires — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
