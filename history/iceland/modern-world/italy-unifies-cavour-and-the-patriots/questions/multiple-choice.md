# Italy Unifies: Cavour and the Patriots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was Italy before unification?
a) one kingdom since Roman times  b) a patchwork of kingdoms, dukedoms and foreign-ruled provinces  c) a French province  d) an empty land
**Answer: b) a patchwork of kingdoms, dukedoms and foreign-ruled provinces** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Garibaldi hand to the king after his southern campaign?
a) his red shirt  b) the conquered south, for the new kingdom  c) a peace treaty with Austria  d) the Papal States' treasury
**Answer: b) the conquered south, for the new kingdom** — That is what this lesson teaches.
