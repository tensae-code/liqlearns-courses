# Italy Unifies: Cavour and the Patriots — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What were Cavour's and Garibaldi's different parts in unification?
**Answer:** Cavour, Piedmont's minister, worked by statecraft — alliances and wars chosen to expel Austria and gather the north; Garibaldi's red-shirted volunteers conquered the south by fire and handed it to the king.

**2.** Why does Iceland's book teach Italy's unification?
**Answer:** As the pattern of the age: nationalism's feeling, a statesman's plan and volunteers' fighting remaking a map — a recipe small nations, Iceland included, studied closely.
