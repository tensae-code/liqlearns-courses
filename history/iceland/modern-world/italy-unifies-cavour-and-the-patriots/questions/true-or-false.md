# Italy Unifies: Cavour and the Patriots — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cavour unified Italy by diplomacy and well-chosen wars from Piedmont-Sardinia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Italy was unified peacefully, without any fighting.
**Answer: False** — Italy, long a patchwork of kingdoms, dukedoms and foreign-ruled provinces, was stitched into one kingdom by Cavour's statecraft and Garibaldi's volunteers — nationalism with a flag and an army.
