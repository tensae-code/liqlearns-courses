# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did Napoleon's wars reach Iceland?
a) Napoleon invaded Iceland  b) Denmark was dragged into the wars and Iceland was cut off from Copenhagen  c) Iceland sent armies to France  d) they never reached the North in any way
**Answer: b) Denmark was dragged into the wars and Iceland was cut off from Copenhagen** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was cut when the wars reached the North?
a) the sea-road between Copenhagen and Iceland  b) the road to Þingvellir  c) trade with Norway only  d) the fishing grounds
**Answer: a) the sea-road between Copenhagen and Iceland** — That is what this lesson teaches.
