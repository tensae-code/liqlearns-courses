# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book's Napoleon chapter is about his wars in the Nordic countries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** For Iceland, the great war was a famous land battle.
**Answer: False** — Napoleon's wars redrew Europe — and reached even the far North, cutting Iceland off from Denmark and setting the stage for the strangest summer in Iceland's history.
