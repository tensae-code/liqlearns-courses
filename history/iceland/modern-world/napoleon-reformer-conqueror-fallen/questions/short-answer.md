# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the wars mean for ordinary Icelanders?
**Answer:** An absence: ships that did not come and officials who could not be reached — Iceland was left suddenly alone when its lifeline to Denmark was cut.

**2.** Why does this lesson lead directly to the summer of 1809?
**Answer:** Because the cut-off colony's seat of power stood empty — the stage was set for an adventurer to seize Iceland, as the next lesson tells.
