1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Napoleon's wars redrew Europe — and reached even the far North, cutting Iceland off from Denmark and setting the stage for the strangest summer in Iceland's history.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Napoleon's wars were a southern affair that never touched the North → The book's chapter is 'Napoleon's wars in the Nordic countries': Denmark was dragged in, its fleet and trade struck, and Iceland — fed and governed from Copenhagen — was cut off.
- Go back to the one thing: Napoleon's wars redrew Europe — and reached even the far North, cutting Iceland off from Denmark and setting the stage for the strangest summer in Iceland's history. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
