# Hands-on — Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find Iceland on a map, then find France. Between them lie thousands of kilometres of sea and several kingdoms. Write down how a war started in France could possibly reach a farm in Iceland. This lesson follows Napoleon's wars north — all the way to Iceland's shore.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Napoleon's wars redrew Europe — and reached even the far North, cutting Iceland off from Denmark and setting the stage for the strangest summer in Iceland's history. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
