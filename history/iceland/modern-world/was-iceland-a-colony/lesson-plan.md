## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the question has one obvious answer the teacher will supply → The worksheet is built as an argument, not an answer: Iceland was no plantation colony, yet it was ruled, monopolised and treated as a dependency. Pupils must weigh the definition against the facts — that weighing IS the learning.
- Go back to the one thing: after studying empire abroad, Iceland's pupils turn the definition homeward: was Iceland itself a colony of Denmark? — the question is the lesson, and the class must argue it from the evidence of their own history.
