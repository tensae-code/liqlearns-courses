# Was Iceland a Colony? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give one argument against calling Iceland a colony.
**Answer:** Icelanders were never enslaved or displaced, kept their language and legal tradition, and no settler class replaced them on their own land — unlike the plantation and settler colonies of the Scramble.

**2.** How must the class answer the worksheet's question?
**Answer:** By defining 'colony' from the empire chapters, testing Iceland's facts against that definition, and defending a judgement — the book supplies no verdict.
