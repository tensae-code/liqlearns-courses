# Was Iceland a Colony? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book gives pupils a ready-made verdict to copy for this question.
**Answer: False** — After studying empire abroad, Iceland's pupils turn the definition homeward: was Iceland itself a colony of Denmark? — the question is the lesson, and the class must argue it from the evidence of their own history.

**2.** Icelanders kept their language and the Althing's tradition under Danish rule.
**Answer: True**
