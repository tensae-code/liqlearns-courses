# Was Iceland a Colony? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the exact question of worksheet 24?
a) was Denmark a colony?  b) was Iceland a colony?  c) was Africa a colony?  d) was Greenland a colony?
**Answer: b) was Iceland a colony?** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which fact argues FOR calling Iceland a colony?
a) Icelanders kept their language  b) Iceland's trade was a Danish monopoly and it was ruled from Copenhagen  c) the Althing tradition survived  d) no settlers replaced Icelanders
**Answer: b) Iceland's trade was a Danish monopoly and it was ruled from Copenhagen** — That is what this lesson teaches.
