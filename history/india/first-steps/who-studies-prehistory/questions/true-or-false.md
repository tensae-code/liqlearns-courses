# Who Studies Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Archaeologists divide prehistory into the Palaeolithic (2 million to 12,000 years ago), the Mesolithic — the age of tiny microliths — and the Neolithic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Archaeologists organise the deep past before writing into three ages: the Palaeolithic (2 million to 12,000 years ago), the Mesolithic of the tiny microliths, and the Neolithic — labels that are themselves a lesson in how prehistory is studied.
