# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.2 / Names and dates.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because archaeologists organise the deep past before writing into three ages: the Palaeolithic (2 million to 12,000 years ago), the Mesolithic of the tiny microliths, and the Neolithic — labels that are themselves a lesson in how prehistory is studied. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
