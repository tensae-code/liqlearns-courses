# Lesson plan — Dating the Past

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Dating the Past
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Dates are counted on a grid the whole world shares: years run forward from the birth of Christ (AD, also called CE) and backwards before it (BC, also called BCE), with BP — before present — for the deep past beyond counting.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Dates are counted on a grid the whole world shares: years run forward from the birth of Christ (AD, also called CE) and backwards before it (BC, also called BCE), with BP — before present — for the deep past beyond counting.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — your own family timeline — exact dates for the living, 'about' dates for the long dead, exactly like the historian's grid.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
