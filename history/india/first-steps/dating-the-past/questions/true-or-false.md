# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Years are counted forward from the birth of Christ — AD, also called CE — and backwards before it — BC, also called BCE — with BP meaning 'before present'.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Dates are counted on a grid the whole world shares: years run forward from the birth of Christ (AD, also called CE) and backwards before it (BC, also called BCE), with BP — before present — for the deep past beyond counting.
