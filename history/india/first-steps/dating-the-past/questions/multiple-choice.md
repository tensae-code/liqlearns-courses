# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “What do dates mean?” is right?
a) Years are counted forward from the birth of Christ — AD, also called CE — and backwards before it — BC, also called BCE — with BP meaning 'before present'.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in India's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Dates are counted on a grid the whole world shares: years run forward from the birth of Christ (AD, also called CE) and backwards before it (BC, also called BCE), with BP — before present — for the deep past beyond counting.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and India has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
