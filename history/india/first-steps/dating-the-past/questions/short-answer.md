# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.1 / What do dates mean?.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because dates are counted on a grid the whole world shares: years run forward from the birth of Christ (AD, also called CE) and backwards before it (BC, also called BCE), with BP — before present — for the deep past beyond counting. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
