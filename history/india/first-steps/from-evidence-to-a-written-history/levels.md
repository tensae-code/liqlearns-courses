# From Evidence to a Written History — depth

This lesson has 5 sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: A written history is built from evidence that must be read critically: manuscripts and inscriptions, the archaeologist's dig, a hymn mined for the life inside it, a foreign ambassador's amazed account, a flattering praise-poem checked against the map, and even a made-up tale used carefully beside sculpture and travellers' reports.
