# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “Rasheeda's question” is right?
a) Rasheeda reads a headline 'One Hundred Years Ago' and wonders how anyone can know what happened then; historians work like detectives, following the clues the past left behind.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in India's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) History is detective work on the past: nobody alive saw it happen, so we rebuild it from the clues it left behind — and there was never just one past, because kings and herders, farmers and fishers each lived a different one.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and India has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
