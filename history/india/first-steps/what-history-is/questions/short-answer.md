# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Rasheeda's question; One past or many?.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because history is detective work on the past: nobody alive saw it happen, so we rebuild it from the clues it left behind — and there was never just one past, because kings and herders, farmers and fishers each lived a different one. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
