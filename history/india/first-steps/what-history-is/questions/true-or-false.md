# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rasheeda reads a headline 'One Hundred Years Ago' and wonders how anyone can know what happened then; historians work like detectives, following the clues the past left behind.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — History is detective work on the past: nobody alive saw it happen, so we rebuild it from the clues it left behind — and there was never just one past, because kings and herders, farmers and fishers each lived a different one.
