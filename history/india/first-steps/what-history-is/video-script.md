# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is detective work on the past: nobody alive saw it happen, so we rebuild it from the clues it left behind — and there was never just one past, because kings and herders, farmers and fishers each lived a different one.

Rasheeda reads a headline 'One Hundred Years Ago' and wonders how anyone can know what happened then; historians work like detectives, following the clues the past left behind.

The past of herders and farmers was not the past of kings; kings kept records of their victories and ordinary people kept none, so archaeology must speak for them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
