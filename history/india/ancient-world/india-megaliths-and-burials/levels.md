Pass mark: From about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials — megaliths — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir: the dead laid with pots, iron tools and horse gear, and one Brahmagiri grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots tells of rich and poor, chiefs and followers.
