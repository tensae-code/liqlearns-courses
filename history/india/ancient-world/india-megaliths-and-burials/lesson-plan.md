# Lesson plan — India: Megaliths and Burials

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Megaliths and Burials
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials — megaliths — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir: the dead laid with pots, iron tools and horse gear, and one Brahmagiri grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots tells of rich and poor, chiefs and followers.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths** — From about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir, the dead laid with Black and Red Ware pots, iron tools and horse gear — Brahmagiri's one grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots betrays rich and poor.
2. **A special burial at Inamgaon; occupations at Inamgaon** — A man is buried cross-legged in a huge four-legged clay jar in the courtyard of the settlement's largest, five-roomed house with its own granary — likely a chief; seeds and cut-marked bones reconstruct the whole village's work.
