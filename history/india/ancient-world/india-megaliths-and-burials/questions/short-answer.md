# India: Megaliths and Burials — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths; A special burial at Inamgaon; occupations at Inamgaon.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because from about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials — megaliths — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir: the dead laid with pots, iron tools and horse gear, and one Brahmagiri grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots tells of rich and poor, chiefs and followers. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
