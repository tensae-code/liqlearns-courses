# India: Megaliths and Burials — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A special burial at Inamgaon; occupations at Inamgaon — b)


Meanings:
a) From about 3000 years ago, megaliths — big-stone burials — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir; the dead were laid with Black and Red Ware pots, iron tools and horse gear, and at Brahmagiri one grave held 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots..
b) At Inamgaon a man was buried cross-legged in a huge four-legged clay jar in the courtyard of the settlement's largest, five-roomed house with its own granary — likely a chief — and seeds and cut-marked bones reconstruct the village's farming, herding, hunting and fishing..

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
