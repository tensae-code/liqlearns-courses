# India: Megaliths and Burials — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) From about 3000 years ago, megaliths — big-stone burials — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir; the dead were laid with Black and Red Ware pots, iron tools and horse gear, and at Brahmagiri one grave held 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — From about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials — megaliths — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir: the dead laid with pots, iron tools and horse gear, and one Brahmagiri grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots tells of rich and poor, chiefs and followers.
