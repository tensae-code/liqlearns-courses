# Video script — India: Megaliths and Burials

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials — megaliths — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir: the dead laid with pots, iron tools and horse gear, and one Brahmagiri grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots tells of rich and poor, chiefs and followers.

From about 3000 years ago, megaliths — big-stone burials — spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir; the dead were laid with Black and Red Ware pots, iron tools and horse gear, and at Brahmagiri one grave held 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots.

At Inamgaon a man was buried cross-legged in a huge four-legged clay jar in the courtyard of the settlement's largest, five-roomed house with its own granary — likely a chief — and seeds and cut-marked bones reconstruct the village's farming, herding, hunting and fishing.
