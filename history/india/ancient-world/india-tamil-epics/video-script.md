# Video script — India: The Tamil Epics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In Ilango's Silappadikaram, the merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Sattanar's sequel follows Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, and both epics were lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago.
