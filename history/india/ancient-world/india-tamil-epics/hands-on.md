# Hands-on — India: The Tamil Epics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A wronged wife confronts a king who has killed her innocent husband — and her rage sets his city on fire. This lesson tells the Tamil epics' blazing story, and how the books themselves were lost and found.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In Ilango's Silappadikaram, the merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Sattanar's sequel follows Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, and both epics were lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
