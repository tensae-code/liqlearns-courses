# India: The Tamil Epics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the Silappadikaram, the merchant Kovalan abandoned his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, was falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burned the city down; the epic and its sequel were lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — In Ilango's Silappadikaram, the merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Sattanar's sequel follows Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, and both epics were lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago.
