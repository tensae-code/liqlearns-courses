# India: The Tamil Epics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.11 / The world of books: Silappadikaram and Manimekalai.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because in Ilango's Silappadikaram, the merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Sattanar's sequel follows Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, and both epics were lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
