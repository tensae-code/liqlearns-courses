# Lesson plan — India: The Tamil Epics

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Tamil Epics
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Ilango's Silappadikaram, the merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Sattanar's sequel follows Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, and both epics were lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (recommended, never forced).
