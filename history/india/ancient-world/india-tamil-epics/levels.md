Pass mark: In Ilango's Silappadikaram, the merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Sattanar's sequel follows Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, and both epics were lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago.
