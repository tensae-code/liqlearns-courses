# India: How the New Kingdoms Were Run — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.10 / How were these kingdoms administered?; a new kind of army; assemblies in the south.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because offices turned hereditary and piled up in one man — Harishena was judge, minister and war-and-peace minister at once — unpaid samantas held land grants and owed troops, growing independent whenever the king weakened, while in the south the sabha, ur and nagaram assemblies ran irrigation, roads and temples for centuries. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
