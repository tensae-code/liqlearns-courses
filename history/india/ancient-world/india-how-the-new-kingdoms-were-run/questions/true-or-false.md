# India: How the New Kingdoms Were Run — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Offices turned hereditary and piled up in one man — Harishena was judge, minister and war-and-peace minister at once; unpaid samantas held land grants and owed troops, growing independent whenever the king weakened; in the south, sabha, ur and nagaram assemblies ran irrigation, roads and temples for centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Offices turned hereditary and piled up in one man — Harishena was judge, minister and war-and-peace minister at once — unpaid samantas held land grants and owed troops, growing independent whenever the king weakened, while in the south the sabha, ur and nagaram assemblies ran irrigation, roads and temples for centuries.
