# Lesson plan — India: How the New Kingdoms Were Run

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: How the New Kingdoms Were Run
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Offices turned hereditary and piled up in one man — Harishena was judge, minister and war-and-peace minister at once — unpaid samantas held land grants and owed troops, growing independent whenever the king weakened, while in the south the sabha, ur and nagaram assemblies ran irrigation, roads and temples for centuries.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (recommended, never forced).
