# Video script — India: How the New Kingdoms Were Run

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Offices turned hereditary and piled up in one man — Harishena was judge, minister and war-and-peace minister at once — unpaid samantas held land grants and owed troops, growing independent whenever the king weakened, while in the south the sabha, ur and nagaram assemblies ran irrigation, roads and temples for centuries.
