# Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** About 2000 years ago, in West Asia, the teaching of Jesus began the Christian faith, which would travel the same trade routes the chapter has been tracing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — About 2000 years ago in West Asia the teaching of Jesus began the faith that would travel the same trade routes this course has been tracing — from a persecuted sect toward a state religion of empires.
