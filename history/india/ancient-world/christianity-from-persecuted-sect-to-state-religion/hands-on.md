# Hands-on — Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A teaching starts in one small province of an empire that will try to crush it. Within a few centuries the empire itself adopts it. This lesson opens that astonishing reversal — the chapter's 'Elsewhere' window.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: About 2000 years ago in West Asia the teaching of Jesus began the faith that would travel the same trade routes this course has been tracing — from a persecuted sect toward a state religion of empires. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
