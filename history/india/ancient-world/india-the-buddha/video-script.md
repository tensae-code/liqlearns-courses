# Video script — India: The Buddha

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, left home comfort, debated other thinkers for years, then meditated under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment made him the Buddha; he taught first at Sarnath and walked and taught until his death at Kusinara.
