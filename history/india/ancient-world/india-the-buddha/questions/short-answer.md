# India: The Buddha — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.6 New Questions and Ideas / The story of the Buddha.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, left home comfort, debated other thinkers for years, then meditated under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment made him the Buddha; he taught first at Sarnath and walked and taught until his death at Kusinara. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
