# India: The Buddha — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, left home comfort, debated other thinkers for years, then meditated under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment made him the Buddha; he taught first at Sarnath and died at Kusinara.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, left home comfort, debated other thinkers for years, then meditated under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment made him the Buddha; he taught first at Sarnath and walked and taught until his death at Kusinara.
