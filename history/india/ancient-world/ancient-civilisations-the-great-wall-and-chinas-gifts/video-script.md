# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

This lesson is about one thing.
Somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raised a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples and kept adding to it for 2,000 years — a wall against neighbours, set against Ashoka's messengers to them.
