# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two empires face the same problem — powerful neighbours beyond the border. One builds a wall thousands of kilometres long. The other sends messengers with a teaching. This lesson sets the two answers side by side.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raised a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples and kept adding to it for 2,000 years — a wall against neighbours, set against Ashoka's messengers to them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
