# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raised a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples and kept adding to it for 2,000 years — a wall against neighbours, set against Ashoka's messengers to them.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raised a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples and kept adding to it for 2,000 years — a wall against neighbours, set against Ashoka's messengers to them.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a fence and a friendship — every border in history is managed by some mix of the two.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
