# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.7 Elsewhere / China's Great Wall.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raised a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples and kept adding to it for 2,000 years — a wall against neighbours, set against Ashoka's messengers to them. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
