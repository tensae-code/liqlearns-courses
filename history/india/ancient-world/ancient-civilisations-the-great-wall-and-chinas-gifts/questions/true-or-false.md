# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raised a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples, and kept adding to it for 2,000 years.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raised a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples and kept adding to it for 2,000 years — a wall against neighbours, set against Ashoka's messengers to them.
