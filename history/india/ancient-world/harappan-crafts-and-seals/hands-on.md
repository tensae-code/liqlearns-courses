# Hands-on — Harappan Crafts and Seals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Hold a bead, a button or a coin from your home. How many skilled steps did it take to make? This lesson meets the specialists whose hands made a civilisation's small, perfect things.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Harappan specialists cut, polished and bored carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
