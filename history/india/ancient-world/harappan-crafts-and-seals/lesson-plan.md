# Lesson plan — Harappan Crafts and Seals

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Harappan Crafts and Seals
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Harappan specialists cut, polished and bored carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (recommended, never forced).
