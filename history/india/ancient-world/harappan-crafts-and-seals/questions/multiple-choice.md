# Harappan Crafts and Seals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “New crafts in the city; faience” is right?
a) Specialists cut, polished and bored carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in India's book

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Harappan specialists cut, polished and bored carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and India has no way of its own
