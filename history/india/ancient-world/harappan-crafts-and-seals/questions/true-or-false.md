# Harappan Crafts and Seals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Specialists cut, polished and bored carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Harappan specialists cut, polished and bored carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found.
