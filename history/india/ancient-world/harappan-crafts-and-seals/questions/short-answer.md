# Harappan Crafts and Seals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.3 / New crafts in the city; faience.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because harappan specialists cut, polished and bored carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
