# Video script — India: The Ashvamedha and the New Rajas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A new kind of raja proves his power with the ashvamedha: a sacrificial horse wanders a year under guard — kings who stop it must fight, kings who let it pass admit his power and come bearing gifts to the sacrifice, where he sits enthroned while his charioteer sings his glory.

For the ashvamedha, a raja let a sacrificial horse wander a year under guard; kings who stopped it had to fight, and kings who let it pass admitted his power and came bearing gifts, while he sat enthroned and his charioteer sang his glory.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
