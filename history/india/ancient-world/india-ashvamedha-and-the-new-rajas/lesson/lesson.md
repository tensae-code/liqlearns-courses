# India: The Ashvamedha and the New Rajas

## Do it first

Imagine proving you are the strongest leader around without fighting a single battle — by sending one horse walking through everyone's land. This lesson explains how that strange, brilliant test worked.

How did some men become rulers over other rulers? One answer is a horse. For the ashvamedha — the horse sacrifice — a raja released a special horse to wander for a full year, followed by his armed men. The horse was a walking challenge. A king whose land it entered had two choices: stop it, and fight the raja's army — or let it pass, and thereby admit, before everyone, that the raja was stronger. Those who let it pass came to the great sacrifice bearing gifts, and watched the raja enthroned in splendour while his charioteer sang his glory. Power, performed as ritual.
