# India: The Ashvamedha and the New Rajas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.5 Kingdoms, Kings and an Early Republic / How some men became rulers.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because a new kind of raja proves his power with the ashvamedha: a sacrificial horse wanders a year under guard — kings who stop it must fight, kings who let it pass admit his power and come bearing gifts to the sacrifice, where he sits enthroned while his charioteer sings his glory. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
