# India: The Ashvamedha and the New Rajas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** For the ashvamedha, a raja let a sacrificial horse wander a year under guard; kings who stopped it had to fight, and kings who let it pass admitted his power and came bearing gifts, while he sat enthroned and his charioteer sang his glory.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — A new kind of raja proves his power with the ashvamedha: a sacrificial horse wanders a year under guard — kings who stop it must fight, kings who let it pass admit his power and come bearing gifts to the sacrifice, where he sits enthroned while his charioteer sings his glory.
