# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** In search of raw materials; A closer look — Arikamedu; A poem about trade; sea routes and monsoon winds; The story of the Silk Route.

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because from Harappan ports reaching to Oman to the Roman warehouse at Arikamedu, from monsoon sailors to the Silk Route the Kushanas guarded, India's trade always ran on the same fuel: raw materials and luxuries moving between people who had different things. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
