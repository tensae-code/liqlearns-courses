# India: Vedic Battles, Cattle and Rajas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.4 / Cattle, horses and chariots.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because in the Rigvedic world, prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils feed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
