# India: Vedic Battles, Cattle and Rajas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Prayers begged for cattle, sons and horses; battles were fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils fed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies chose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — In the Rigvedic world, prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils feed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes.
