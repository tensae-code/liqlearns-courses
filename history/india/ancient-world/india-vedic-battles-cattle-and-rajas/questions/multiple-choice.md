Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Cattle, horses and chariots” is right?
a) Prayers begged for cattle, sons and horses; battles were fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils fed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies chose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in India's book

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) In the Rigvedic world, prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils feed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and India has no way of its own
