# India: Vedic Battles, Cattle and Rajas — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: In the Rigvedic world, prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils feed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes.
