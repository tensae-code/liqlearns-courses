# Lesson plan — India: Vedic Battles, Cattle and Rajas

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Vedic Battles, Cattle and Rajas
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the Rigvedic world, prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils feed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (recommended, never forced).

1. 3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
