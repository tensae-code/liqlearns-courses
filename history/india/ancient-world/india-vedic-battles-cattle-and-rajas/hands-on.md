# Hands-on — India: Vedic Battles, Cattle and Rajas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
If wealth walked on four legs and could be stolen in a night raid, how would your whole society organise itself? This lesson enters a world where cattle were money, and prayer asked for herds.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the Rigvedic world, prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils feed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
