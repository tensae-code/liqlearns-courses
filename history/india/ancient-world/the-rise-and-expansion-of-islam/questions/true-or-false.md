# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** About 1400 years ago, desert Arabia — hub of the sea trade between India and Europe — heard Prophet Muhammad's faith of equality before one God; within a century Islam reached North Africa, Spain, Iran and India, and Arab soldiers conquered Sind about 1300 years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Desert Arabia, hub of the sea trade between India and Europe, heard Prophet Muhammad's new faith of equality before one God about 1400 years ago; within a century Islam reached North Africa, Spain, Iran and India, and Arab soldiers conquered Sind about 1300 years ago.
