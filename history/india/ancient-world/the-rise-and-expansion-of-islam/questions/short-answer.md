# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.10 Elsewhere / Arabia and Islam.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because desert Arabia, hub of the sea trade between India and Europe, heard Prophet Muhammad's new faith of equality before one God about 1400 years ago; within a century Islam reached North Africa, Spain, Iran and India, and Arab soldiers conquered Sind about 1300 years ago. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
