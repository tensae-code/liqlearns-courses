# Hands-on — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A desert land of caravans and sea-traders hears a message of equality before one God — and within a hundred years that message maps half the world. This lesson traces the fastest expansion in this course.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Desert Arabia, hub of the sea trade between India and Europe, heard Prophet Muhammad's new faith of equality before one God about 1400 years ago; within a century Islam reached North Africa, Spain, Iran and India, and Arab soldiers conquered Sind about 1300 years ago. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
