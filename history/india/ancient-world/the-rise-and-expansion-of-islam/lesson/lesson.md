# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

A desert land of caravans and sea-traders hears a message of equality before one God — and within a hundred years that message maps half the world. This lesson traces the fastest expansion in this course.

The chapter's final 'Elsewhere' is Arabia — and Arabia was never elsewhere for India. The desert peninsula sat at the hub of the sea trade between India and Europe; its merchants knew Indian ports well. About 1400 years ago, Arabia heard a new faith: Prophet Muhammad's teaching of one God, before whom all believers stand equal. The expansion that followed is one of history's fastest. Within a century, Islam had reached North Africa and Spain in the west, Iran in the east — and India itself: about 1300 years ago, Arab soldiers conquered Sind, at the subcontinent's north-western door. A faith born in the desert had become a world power in a single lifetime's echo.
