# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Desert Arabia, hub of the sea trade between India and Europe, heard Prophet Muhammad's new faith of equality before one God about 1400 years ago; within a century Islam reached North Africa, Spain, Iran and India, and Arab soldiers conquered Sind about 1300 years ago.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1.
