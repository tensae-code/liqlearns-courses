# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: China

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: China
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** China enters this course twice as India's mirror: around 3500 years ago the Chinese write on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere — and about 1900 years ago Cai Lun invents paper, the secret that leaks to Korea, Japan, Baghdad and then the world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Writing in China** — Around 3500 years ago the Chinese write on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere, a parallel beginning to the subcontinent's book story.
2. **The invention of paper** — About 1900 years ago Cai Lun beats plant fibre, cloth, rope and bark into pulp and dries it into paper in China; the guarded secret leaks to Korea, Japan, Baghdad and then Europe, Africa and India.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
