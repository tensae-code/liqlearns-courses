# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: China

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a message two ways: scratch it on something hard, then write it on paper. Which would you trust to travel the world? This lesson follows China's two gifts to every later student — early writing, and paper itself.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: China enters this course twice as India's mirror: around 3500 years ago the Chinese write on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere — and about 1900 years ago Cai Lun invents paper, the secret that leaks to Korea, Japan, Baghdad and then the world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
