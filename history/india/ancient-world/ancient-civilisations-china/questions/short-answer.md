# Ancient Civilisations: China — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Writing in China; The invention of paper.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because china enters this course twice as India's mirror: around 3500 years ago the Chinese write on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere — and about 1900 years ago Cai Lun invents paper, the secret that leaks to Korea, Japan, Baghdad and then the world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
