# Ancient Civilisations: China — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Around 3500 years ago the Chinese wrote on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere, a parallel beginning to the subcontinent's own book story.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — China enters this course twice as India's mirror: around 3500 years ago the Chinese write on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere — and about 1900 years ago Cai Lun invents paper, the secret that leaks to Korea, Japan, Baghdad and then the world.
