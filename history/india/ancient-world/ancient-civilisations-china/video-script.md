# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: China

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
China enters this course twice as India's mirror: around 3500 years ago the Chinese write on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere — and about 1900 years ago Cai Lun invents paper, the secret that leaks to Korea, Japan, Baghdad and then the world.

Around 3500 years ago the Chinese wrote on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere, a parallel beginning to the subcontinent's own book story.

About 1900 years ago Cai Lun beat plant fibre, cloth, rope and bark into pulp and dried it into paper in China; the guarded secret leaked to Korea, Japan, Baghdad and then Europe, Africa and India.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
