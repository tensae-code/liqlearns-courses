# Lesson plan — India: Craft Guilds and Women's Work

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Craft Guilds and Women's Work
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Craft and merchant guilds — shrenis — trained workers, bought raw material and even banked deposits whose interest endowed monasteries, while the Arthashastra's workshop rules show widows and retired temple women spinning for wages under a superintendent forbidden even to talk to them of anything but work.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).
