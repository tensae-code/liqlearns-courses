# India: Craft Guilds and Women's Work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Potters fired mirror-black Northern Black Polished Ware and Varanasi and Madurai wove famous cloth; craft and merchant shrenis trained workers, bought raw material and banked deposits whose interest endowed monasteries, while the Arthashastra's rules show widows and retired temple women spinning for wages.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Craft and merchant guilds — shrenis — trained workers, bought raw material and even banked deposits whose interest endowed monasteries, while the Arthashastra's workshop rules show widows and retired temple women spinning for wages under a superintendent forbidden even to talk to them of anything but work.
