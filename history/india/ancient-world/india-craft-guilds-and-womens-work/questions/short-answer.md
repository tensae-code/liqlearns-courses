# India: Craft Guilds and Women's Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.8 / Crafts persons; shrenis; rules for spinning and weaving.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because craft and merchant guilds — shrenis — trained workers, bought raw material and even banked deposits whose interest endowed monasteries, while the Arthashastra's workshop rules show widows and retired temple women spinning for wages under a superintendent forbidden even to talk to them of anything but work. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
