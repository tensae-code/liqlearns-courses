# Video script — India: Craft Guilds and Women's Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Craft and merchant guilds — shrenis — trained workers, bought raw material and even banked deposits whose interest endowed monasteries, while the Arthashastra's workshop rules show widows and retired temple women spinning for wages under a superintendent forbidden even to talk to them of anything but work.
