# India: Stories Ordinary People Told — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jataka and Panchatantra tales were written down and carved on stupa railings; a monkey king of 80,000 followers made his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escaped a human king, who, watching him die, mourned and honoured him.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Jataka and Panchatantra tales were written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him.
