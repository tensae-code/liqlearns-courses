# India: Stories Ordinary People Told — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.11 / Stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because jataka and Panchatantra tales were written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
