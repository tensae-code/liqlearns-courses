# India: Stories Ordinary People Told — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king” is right?
a) Jataka and Panchatantra tales were written down and carved on stupa railings; a monkey king of 80,000 followers made his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escaped a human king, who, watching him die, mourned and honoured him.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in India's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Jataka and Panchatantra tales were written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and India has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
