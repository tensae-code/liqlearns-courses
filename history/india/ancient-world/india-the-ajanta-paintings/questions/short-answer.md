# India: The Ajanta Paintings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.11 / Painting.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because in Ajanta's dark rock-cut monasteries, unknown artists painted vivid scenes by torchlight in colours ground from plants and minerals that still glow after 1500 years. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
