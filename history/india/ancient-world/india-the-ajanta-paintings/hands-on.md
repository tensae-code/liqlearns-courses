# Hands-on — India: The Ajanta Paintings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Paint a picture in a dark cave, by torchlight, on a rock wall — and make it glow for 1,500 years. This lesson enters Ajanta, where artists did exactly that and signed nothing.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In Ajanta's dark rock-cut monasteries, unknown artists painted vivid scenes by torchlight in colours ground from plants and minerals that still glow after 1500 years. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
