# Lesson plan — India: The Ajanta Paintings

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Ajanta Paintings
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Ajanta's dark rock-cut monasteries, unknown artists painted vivid scenes by torchlight in colours ground from plants and minerals that still glow after 1500 years.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In Ajanta's dark rock-cut monasteries, unknown artists painted vivid scenes by torchlight in colours ground from plants and minerals that still glow after 1500 years.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a mural in a public building today — Ajanta's painters were its masters, working without light or fame.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
