# Video script — India: Janapadas and Mahajanapadas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rajas of peoples become rajas of lands — janapadas, where 'the jana set its foot' — ringed by fortified capitals, fed by Painted Grey Ware feasts, and soon needing salaried year-round armies paid by real taxes: bhaga, a sixth of the crop, as iron ploughshares and transplanted paddy multiply the harvest on the bent backs of dasas and landless kammakaras.

Rajas of peoples became rajas of lands — janapadas, where 'the jana set its foot'; excavated settlements at Purana Qila, Hastinapura and Atranjikhera yield huts, cattle and fine Painted Grey Ware for special feasts, and the greatest janapadas ringed their capitals with massive fortification walls.

Kings who built forts and kept salaried, year-round armies had to tax — bhaga, a sixth of the crop, labour from craftsmen, animals from herders — while iron ploughshares bit heavy clay and transplanted paddy multiplied the harvest, on the bent backs of dasas and landless kammakaras.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
