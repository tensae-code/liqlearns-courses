# India: Janapadas and Mahajanapadas — depth

This lesson has 2 sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Rajas of peoples become rajas of lands — janapadas, where 'the jana set its foot' — ringed by fortified capitals, fed by Painted Grey Ware feasts, and soon needing salaried year-round armies paid by real taxes: bhaga, a sixth of the crop, as iron ploughshares and transplanted paddy multiply the harvest on the bent backs of dasas and landless kammakaras.
