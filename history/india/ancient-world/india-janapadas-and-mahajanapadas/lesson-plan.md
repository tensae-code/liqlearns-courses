## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware** — Rajas of peoples become rajas of lands where 'the jana set its foot'; excavated settlements yield huts, cattle and fine Painted Grey Ware for special feasts; the greatest janapadas ring their capitals with massive fortification walls.
2. **Armies, taxes and changes in agriculture** — Kings who build forts and keep salaried, year-round armies must tax — bhaga, a sixth of the crop, labour from craftsmen, animals from herders; iron ploughshares bite heavy clay and transplanted paddy multiplies the harvest, on the bent backs of dasas and landless kammakaras.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
