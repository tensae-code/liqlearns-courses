# India: Janapadas and Mahajanapadas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rajas of peoples became rajas of lands — janapadas, where 'the jana set its foot'; excavated settlements at Purana Qila, Hastinapura and Atranjikhera yield huts, cattle and fine Painted Grey Ware for special feasts, and the greatest janapadas ringed their capitals with massive fortification walls.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Rajas of peoples become rajas of lands — janapadas, where 'the jana set its foot' — ringed by fortified capitals, fed by Painted Grey Ware feasts, and soon needing salaried year-round armies paid by real taxes: bhaga, a sixth of the crop, as iron ploughshares and transplanted paddy multiply the harvest on the bent backs of dasas and landless kammakaras.
