# India: Janapadas and Mahajanapadas — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Armies, taxes and changes in agriculture — b)


Meanings:
a) Rajas of peoples became rajas of lands — janapadas, where 'the jana set its foot'; excavated settlements at Purana Qila, Hastinapura and Atranjikhera yield huts, cattle and fine Painted Grey Ware for special feasts, and the greatest janapadas ringed their capitals with massive fortification walls..
b) Kings who built forts and kept salaried, year-round armies had to tax — bhaga, a sixth of the crop, labour from craftsmen, animals from herders — while iron ploughshares bit heavy clay and transplanted paddy multiplied the harvest, on the bent backs of dasas and landless kammakaras..

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
