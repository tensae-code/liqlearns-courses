# India: Janapadas and Mahajanapadas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware; Armies, taxes and changes in agriculture.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because rajas of peoples become rajas of lands — janapadas, where 'the jana set its foot' — ringed by fortified capitals, fed by Painted Grey Ware feasts, and soon needing salaried year-round armies paid by real taxes: bhaga, a sixth of the crop, as iron ploughshares and transplanted paddy multiply the harvest on the bent backs of dasas and landless kammakaras. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
