# Lesson plan — India: The Iron Pillar and Metallurgy

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Iron Pillar and Metallurgy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Delhi's Mehrauli iron pillar — 7.2 m tall, over 3 tonnes, raised about 1500 years ago for a Gupta king named Chandra — has not rusted in fifteen centuries, a standing riddle from the heirs of Harappan bronze-masters.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Delhi's Mehrauli iron pillar — 7.2 m tall, over 3 tonnes, raised about 1500 years ago for a Gupta king named Chandra — has not rusted in fifteen centuries, a standing riddle from the heirs of Harappan bronze-masters.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a nail left in the rain for one season — compare it with fifteen centuries; the pillar is a materials-science riddle.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
