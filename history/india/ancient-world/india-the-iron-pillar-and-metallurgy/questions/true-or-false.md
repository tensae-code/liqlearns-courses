# India: The Iron Pillar and Metallurgy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Delhi's Mehrauli iron pillar — 7.2 metres tall, over 3 tonnes, raised about 1500 years ago for a Gupta king named Chandra — has not rusted in fifteen centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Delhi's Mehrauli iron pillar — 7.2 m tall, over 3 tonnes, raised about 1500 years ago for a Gupta king named Chandra — has not rusted in fifteen centuries, a standing riddle from the heirs of Harappan bronze-masters.
