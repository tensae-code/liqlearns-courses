# India: The Iron Pillar and Metallurgy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.11 Buildings, Paintings and Books / Marutasami and the iron pillar.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because delhi's Mehrauli iron pillar — 7.2 m tall, over 3 tonnes, raised about 1500 years ago for a Gupta king named Chandra — has not rusted in fifteen centuries, a standing riddle from the heirs of Harappan bronze-masters. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
