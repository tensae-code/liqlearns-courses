# Çatal Höyük: A Neolithic Town — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.2 Elsewhere / A Neolithic site in Turkey.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because at Çatal Höyük in Turkey, a Neolithic farming town was already plugged into long-distance exchange: flint arrived from Syria, cowries from the Red Sea and shells from the Mediterranean, carried on pack animals' backs. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
