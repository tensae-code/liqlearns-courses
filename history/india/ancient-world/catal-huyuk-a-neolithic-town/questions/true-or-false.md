# Çatal Höyük: A Neolithic Town — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Çatal Höyük in Turkey, flint from Syria, cowries from the Red Sea and shells from the Mediterranean arrived on pack animals' backs — a farming town already plugged into long-distance exchange.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — At Çatal Höyük in Turkey, a Neolithic farming town was already plugged into long-distance exchange: flint arrived from Syria, cowries from the Red Sea and shells from the Mediterranean, carried on pack animals' backs.
