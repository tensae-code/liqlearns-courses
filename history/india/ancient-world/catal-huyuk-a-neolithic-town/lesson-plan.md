# Lesson plan — Çatal Höyük: A Neolithic Town

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Çatal Höyük: A Neolithic Town
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** At Çatal Höyük in Turkey, a Neolithic farming town was already plugged into long-distance exchange: flint arrived from Syria, cowries from the Red Sea and shells from the Mediterranean, carried on pack animals' backs.
