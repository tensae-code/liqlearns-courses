# Hands-on — Çatal Höyük: A Neolithic Town

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at three objects in your home that came from far away. How did they get to you? This lesson's town had the same puzzle — 9,000 years ago, with no trucks, no ships' containers, only pack animals.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: At Çatal Höyük in Turkey, a Neolithic farming town was already plugged into long-distance exchange: flint arrived from Syria, cowries from the Red Sea and shells from the Mediterranean, carried on pack animals' backs. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
