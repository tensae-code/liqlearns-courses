# India: Pilgrims Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.9 / The quest of the pilgrims.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because fa Xian, caught in a 13-day storm on the voyage home, threw his belongings overboard but clung to his books and Buddha statues, taking 90 days to Java; Xuan Zang lost 50 manuscripts when his Indus boat capsized yet hauled 600 texts home on 20 horses — both studied at Nalanda, where the gatekeeper's hard questions turned away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
