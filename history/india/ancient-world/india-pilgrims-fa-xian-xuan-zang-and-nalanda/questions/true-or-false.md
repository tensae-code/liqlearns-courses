# India: Pilgrims Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fa Xian, in a 13-day storm, threw his belongings overboard but clung to his books and Buddha statues; Xuan Zang lost 50 manuscripts in the Indus yet hauled 600 texts home on 20 horses; both studied at Nalanda, whose gatekeeper's questions turned away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Fa Xian, caught in a 13-day storm on the voyage home, threw his belongings overboard but clung to his books and Buddha statues, taking 90 days to Java; Xuan Zang lost 50 manuscripts when his Indus boat capsized yet hauled 600 texts home on 20 horses — both studied at Nalanda, where the gatekeeper's hard questions turned away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls.
