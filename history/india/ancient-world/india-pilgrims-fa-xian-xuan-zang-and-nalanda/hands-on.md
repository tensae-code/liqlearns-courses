# Hands-on — India: Pilgrims Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your boat is sinking in a storm. You can save your luggage or your books — not both. Two Chinese pilgrims faced that choice for real. This lesson sails and walks with them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Fa Xian, caught in a 13-day storm on the voyage home, threw his belongings overboard but clung to his books and Buddha statues, taking 90 days to Java; Xuan Zang lost 50 manuscripts when his Indus boat capsized yet hauled 600 texts home on 20 horses — both studied at Nalanda, where the gatekeeper's hard questions turned away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
