# Lesson plan — India: Pilgrims Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Pilgrims Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Fa Xian, caught in a 13-day storm on the voyage home, threw his belongings overboard but clung to his books and Buddha statues, taking 90 days to Java; Xuan Zang lost 50 manuscripts when his Indus boat capsized yet hauled 600 texts home on 20 horses — both studied at Nalanda, where the gatekeeper's hard questions turned away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).
