# India: Pilgrims Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Fa Xian, caught in a 13-day storm on the voyage home, threw his belongings overboard but clung to his books and Buddha statues, taking 90 days to Java; Xuan Zang lost 50 manuscripts when his Indus boat capsized yet hauled 600 texts home on 20 horses — both studied at Nalanda, where the gatekeeper's hard questions turned away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls.
