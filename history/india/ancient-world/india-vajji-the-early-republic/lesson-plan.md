# Lesson plan — India: Vajji, the Early Republic

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Vajji, the Early Republic
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas ruled together by assembly, discussion and debate — and when Ajatasattu asked the Buddha how to conquer them, the answer was that they would prosper while they met fully and often, acted together, honoured elders and protected their women and shrines.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks kingship was the only system in ancient India → Vajji was a gana, a republic of many rajas ruling by assembly — and such sanghas outlasted kingdoms for centuries.
