# India: Vajji, the Early Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.5 / A closer look — Vajji; Ajatasattu and the Vajjis.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because in the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas ruled together by assembly, discussion and debate — and when Ajatasattu asked the Buddha how to conquer them, the answer was that they would prosper while they met fully and often, acted together, honoured elders and protected their women and shrines. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
