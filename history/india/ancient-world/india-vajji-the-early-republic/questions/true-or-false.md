# India: Vajji, the Early Republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas ruled together by assembly, discussion and debate; the Buddha told Ajatasattu's minister the Vajjis would prosper while they met fully and often, acted together, honoured elders and protected their women and shrines.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas ruled together by assembly, discussion and debate — and when Ajatasattu asked the Buddha how to conquer them, the answer was that they would prosper while they met fully and often, acted together, honoured elders and protected their women and shrines.
