# Video script — India: Vajji, the Early Republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas ruled together by assembly, discussion and debate — and when Ajatasattu asked the Buddha how to conquer them, the answer was that they would prosper while they met fully and often, acted together, honoured elders and protected their women and shrines.

In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas ruled together by assembly, discussion and debate; the Buddha told Ajatasattu's minister the Vajjis would prosper while they met fully and often, acted together, honoured elders and protected their women and shrines.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
