# India: Names of the Land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Names of the land” is right?
a) Iranians and Greeks met the river Sindhu and called it Hindos or Indos, so the land to its east became 'India'; the Bharata people named in the Rigveda gave the land its other name, Bharat.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in India's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The land has two great names with two different stories: 'India' comes from the river Sindhu, heard by Iranians and Greeks as Hindos and Indos, while 'Bharat' comes from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and India has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
