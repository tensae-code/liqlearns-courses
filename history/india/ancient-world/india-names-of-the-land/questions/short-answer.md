# India: Names of the Land — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.1 / Names of the land.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because the land has two great names with two different stories: 'India' comes from the river Sindhu, heard by Iranians and Greeks as Hindos and Indos, while 'Bharat' comes from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
