# India: Names of the Land — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Iranians and Greeks met the river Sindhu and called it Hindos or Indos, so the land to its east became 'India'; the Bharata people named in the Rigveda gave the land its other name, Bharat.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — The land has two great names with two different stories: 'India' comes from the river Sindhu, heard by Iranians and Greeks as Hindos and Indos, while 'Bharat' comes from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda.
