# Lesson plan — India: Names of the Land

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Names of the Land
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The land has two great names with two different stories: 'India' comes from the river Sindhu, heard by Iranians and Greeks as Hindos and Indos, while 'Bharat' comes from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The land has two great names with two different stories: 'India' comes from the river Sindhu, heard by Iranians and Greeks as Hindos and Indos, while 'Bharat' comes from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — your own name — it was given by somebody, in some language, for some reason, and it carries a story.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
