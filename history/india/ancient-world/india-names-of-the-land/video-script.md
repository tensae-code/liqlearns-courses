# Video script — India: Names of the Land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The land has two great names with two different stories: 'India' comes from the river Sindhu, heard by Iranians and Greeks as Hindos and Indos, while 'Bharat' comes from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda.

Iranians and Greeks met the river Sindhu and called it Hindos or Indos, so the land to its east became 'India'; the Bharata people named in the Rigveda gave the land its other name, Bharat.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
