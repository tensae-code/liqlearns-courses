# Hands-on — India: Names of the Land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the names of your own country, town or street out loud. Where did each name come from — who gave it, and in what language? This lesson tells how one great land got two names from two directions.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The land has two great names with two different stories: 'India' comes from the river Sindhu, heard by Iranians and Greeks as Hindos and Indos, while 'Bharat' comes from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
