# India: Early Cities and Ring Wells — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rows of ceramic rings sunk in house plots served as toilets, drains and rubbish pits; an unnamed Greek sailor's port-guide lists Barygaza's imports — wine, copper, coral, gold and silver coins — and exports — ivory, agate, cotton, silk, perfumes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Rows of ceramic rings sunk in house plots served as toilets, drains and rubbish pits — the humble ring well is the archaeologist's sign of a city house — while a Greek sailor's port-guide lists Barygaza's imports and exports and the singing boys and fine cloth its merchants gifted to the king.
