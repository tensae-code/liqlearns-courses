# India: Early Cities and Ring Wells — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.8 / Ring wells; the story of Barygaza.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because rows of ceramic rings sunk in house plots served as toilets, drains and rubbish pits — the humble ring well is the archaeologist's sign of a city house — while a Greek sailor's port-guide lists Barygaza's imports and exports and the singing boys and fine cloth its merchants gifted to the king. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
