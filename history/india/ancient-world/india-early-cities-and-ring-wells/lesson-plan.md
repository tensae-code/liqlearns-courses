# Lesson plan — India: Early Cities and Ring Wells

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Early Cities and Ring Wells
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
