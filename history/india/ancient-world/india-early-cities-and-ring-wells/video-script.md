# Video script — India: Early Cities and Ring Wells

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

This lesson is about one thing.
Rows of ceramic rings sunk in house plots served as toilets, drains and rubbish pits — the humble ring well is the archaeologist's sign of a city house — while a Greek sailor's port-guide lists Barygaza's imports and exports and the singing boys and fine cloth its merchants gifted to the king.

Rows of ceramic rings sunk in house plots served as toilets, drains and rubbish pits; an unnamed Greek sailor's port-guide lists Barygaza's imports — wine, copper, coral, gold and silver coins — and exports — ivory, agate, cotton, silk, perfumes.
