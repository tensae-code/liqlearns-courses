# India: Sangam Literature — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Poems composed around 2300 years ago were gathered in assemblies — sangams — of poets at Madurai, preserving the Tamil words for the village's people: a historian's source in verse.
