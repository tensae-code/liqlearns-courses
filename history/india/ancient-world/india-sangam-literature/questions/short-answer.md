# India: Sangam Literature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.8 / The earliest Tamil compositions.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because poems composed around 2300 years ago were gathered in assemblies — sangams — of poets at Madurai, preserving the Tamil words for the village's people and becoming a historian's source in verse. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
