# India: Ashoka's Dhamma — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ashoka's dhamma needed no god or sacrifice: gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions; dhamma mahamattas toured teaching it, messengers carried it to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and the emperor planted roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Ashoka's dhamma needs no god or sacrifice: gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions — taught by touring dhamma mahamattas, carried by messengers to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and built into roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals.
