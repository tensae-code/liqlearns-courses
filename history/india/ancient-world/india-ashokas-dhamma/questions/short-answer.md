# India: Ashoka's Dhamma — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.7 / What was Ashoka's dhamma?; Ashoka's messages to his subjects.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because ashoka's dhamma needs no god or sacrifice: gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions — taught by touring dhamma mahamattas, carried by messengers to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and built into roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
