# Video script — India: Ashoka's Dhamma

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ashoka's dhamma needs no god or sacrifice: gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions — taught by touring dhamma mahamattas, carried by messengers to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and built into roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals.

Ashoka's dhamma needed no god or sacrifice: gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions; dhamma mahamattas toured teaching it, messengers carried it to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and the emperor planted roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
