Pass mark: Ashoka's dhamma needs no god or sacrifice: gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions — taught by touring dhamma mahamattas, carried by messengers to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and built into roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals.
