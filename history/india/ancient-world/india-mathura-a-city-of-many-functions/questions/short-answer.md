# India: Mathura, a City of Many Functions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.8 / Cities with many functions: Mathura.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because at the crossroads of the north-west–east and north–south routes, walled Mathura fed itself from its hinterland, carved superb sculpture, became the Kushanas' second capital, and hosted Buddhist monasteries, Jaina shrines and Krishna worship — its gift-inscriptions naming the city's goldsmiths, weavers, basket makers and perfumers. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
