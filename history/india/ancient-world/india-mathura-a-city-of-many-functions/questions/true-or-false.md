# India: Mathura, a City of Many Functions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mathura stood at the crossroads of the north-west–east and north–south routes; walled and fed by its hinterland, it carved superb sculpture, became the Kushanas' second capital, and hosted Buddhist monasteries, Jaina shrines and Krishna worship.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — At the crossroads of the north-west–east and north–south routes, walled Mathura fed itself from its hinterland, carved superb sculpture, became the Kushanas' second capital, and hosted Buddhist monasteries, Jaina shrines and Krishna worship — its gift-inscriptions naming the city's goldsmiths, weavers, basket makers and perfumers.
