# Video script — India: Mathura, a City of Many Functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
At the crossroads of the north-west–east and north–south routes, walled Mathura fed itself from its hinterland, carved superb sculpture, became the Kushanas' second capital, and hosted Buddhist monasteries, Jaina shrines and Krishna worship — its gift-inscriptions naming the city's goldsmiths, weavers, basket makers and perfumers.
