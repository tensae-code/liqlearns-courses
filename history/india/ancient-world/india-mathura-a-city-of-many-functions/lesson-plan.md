# Lesson plan — India: Mathura, a City of Many Functions

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Mathura, a City of Many Functions
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** At the crossroads of the north-west–east and north–south routes, walled Mathura fed itself from its hinterland, carved superb sculpture, became the Kushanas' second capital, and hosted Buddhist monasteries, Jaina shrines and Krishna worship — its gift-inscriptions naming the city's goldsmiths, weavers, basket makers and perfumers.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).
