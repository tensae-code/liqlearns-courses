# Video script — India: Upanishadic Thinkers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Upanishadic teachers hunted what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul — while a hungry beggar shamed two sages with their own doctrine, women like Gargi debated in royal courts, the slave's son Satyakama Jabala became a famed thinker, and Panini wrote Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae.
