# India: Upanishadic Thinkers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.6 / Upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because upanishadic teachers hunted what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul — while a hungry beggar shamed two sages with their own doctrine, women like Gargi debated in royal courts, the slave's son Satyakama Jabala became a famed thinker, and Panini wrote Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
