# India: Upanishadic Thinkers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Upanishadic teachers sought atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul; a hungry beggar shamed two sages with their own teaching, Gargi debated in royal courts, Satyakama Jabala the slave's son became a famed thinker, and Panini wrote Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Upanishadic teachers hunted what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul — while a hungry beggar shamed two sages with their own doctrine, women like Gargi debated in royal courts, the slave's son Satyakama Jabala became a famed thinker, and Panini wrote Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae.
