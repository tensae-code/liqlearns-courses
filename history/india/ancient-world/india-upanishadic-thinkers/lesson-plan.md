# Lesson plan — India: Upanishadic Thinkers

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Upanishadic Thinkers
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Upanishadic teachers hunted what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul — while a hungry beggar shamed two sages with their own doctrine, women like Gargi debated in royal courts, the slave's son Satyakama Jabala became a famed thinker, and Panini wrote Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (recommended, never forced).
