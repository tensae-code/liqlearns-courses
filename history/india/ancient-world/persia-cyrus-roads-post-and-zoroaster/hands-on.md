# Hands-on — Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Three short rules for a whole life: think good, speak good, do good. Could a faith stand on just that? This lesson crosses to Iran, India's old cousin, to meet the prophet who taught it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Iran's prophet Zoroaster teaches 'Good thoughts, Good Words and Good Deeds' in an Avesta whose language echoes the Vedas; a thousand years Iran's faith, it later sails with migrants to Gujarat and Maharashtra, ancestors of today's Parsis. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
