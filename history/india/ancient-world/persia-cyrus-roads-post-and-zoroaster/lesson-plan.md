## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Iran's prophet Zoroaster teaches 'Good thoughts, Good Words and Good Deeds' in an Avesta whose language echoes the Vedas; a thousand years Iran's faith, it later sails with migrants to Gujarat and Maharashtra, ancestors of today's Parsis.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
