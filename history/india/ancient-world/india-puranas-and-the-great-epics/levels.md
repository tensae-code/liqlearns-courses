# India: Puranas and the Great Epics — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas — while the Mahabharata's cousin-war for Hastinapura, with the Gita inside it, and the Ramayana's exiled Rama, abducted Sita and victorious return were written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago.
