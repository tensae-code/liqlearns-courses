# India: Puranas and the Great Epics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.11 / Recording and preserving old stories.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because the Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas — while the Mahabharata's cousin-war for Hastinapura, with the Gita inside it, and the Ramayana's exiled Rama, abducted Sita and victorious return were written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
