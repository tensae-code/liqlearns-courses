# India: Puranas and the Great Epics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas; the Mahabharata and Ramayana were written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — The Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas — while the Mahabharata's cousin-war for Hastinapura, with the Gita inside it, and the Ramayana's exiled Rama, abducted Sita and victorious return were written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago.
