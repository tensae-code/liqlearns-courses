# Video script — India: Puranas and the Great Epics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas — while the Mahabharata's cousin-war for Hastinapura, with the Gita inside it, and the Ramayana's exiled Rama, abducted Sita and victorious return were written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago.
