# Lesson plan — India: Puranas and the Great Epics

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Puranas and the Great Epics
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas — while the Mahabharata's cousin-war for Hastinapura, with the Gita inside it, and the Ramayana's exiled Rama, abducted Sita and victorious return were written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (recommended, never forced).
