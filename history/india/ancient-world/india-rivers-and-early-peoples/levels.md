# India: Rivers and Early Peoples — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: People have lived along India's rivers for hundreds of thousands of years: gatherers on the Narmada, the first wheat and barley farmers in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago, then cities on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago.
