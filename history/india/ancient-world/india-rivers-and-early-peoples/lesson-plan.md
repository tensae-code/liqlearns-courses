# Lesson plan — India: Rivers and Early Peoples

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Rivers and Early Peoples
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** People have lived along India's rivers for hundreds of thousands of years: gatherers on the Narmada, the first wheat and barley farmers in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago, then cities on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: People have lived along India's rivers for hundreds of thousands of years: gatherers on the Narmada, the first wheat and barley farmers in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago, then cities on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the river nearest your own home — people have always settled where water, food and routes meet.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
