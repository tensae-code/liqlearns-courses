# Video script — India: Rivers and Early Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
People have lived along India's rivers for hundreds of thousands of years: gatherers on the Narmada, the first wheat and barley farmers in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago, then cities on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago.

Gatherers lived along the Narmada for hundreds of thousands of years; wheat and barley were first grown in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago; cities rose on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
