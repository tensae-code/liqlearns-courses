# India: Rivers and Early Peoples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.1 / Where did people live?.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because people have lived along India's rivers for hundreds of thousands of years: gatherers on the Narmada, the first wheat and barley farmers in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago, then cities on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
