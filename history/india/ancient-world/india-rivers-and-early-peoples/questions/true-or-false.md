# India: Rivers and Early Peoples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gatherers lived along the Narmada for hundreds of thousands of years; wheat and barley were first grown in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago; cities rose on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — People have lived along India's rivers for hundreds of thousands of years: gatherers on the Narmada, the first wheat and barley farmers in the Sulaiman and Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago, then cities on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago.
