# Video script — India's Order and Gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The hymns sort people into orders — brahmin priests and rajas, jana and vish communities, Aryas against Dasas — and the later Vedic books harden the sorting into four varnas by birth, branding some people untouchable; but many pushed back against the idea that birth should fix a life.

The hymns sorted people as brahmin priests and rajas, as jana and vish communities, and as Aryas against Dasas, who sacrificed differently and spoke differently — dasa sliding in time into the word for slave.

Priests of the later Vedic books divided society by birth into brahmins, kshatriyas, vaishyas and shudras, and branded some people untouchable — and many pushed back against it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
