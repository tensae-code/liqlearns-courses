# India's Order and Gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Words to describe people; Varnas.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because the hymns sort people into orders — brahmin priests and rajas, jana and vish communities, Aryas against Dasas — and the later Vedic books harden the sorting into four varnas by birth, branding some people untouchable; but many pushed back against the idea that birth should fix a life. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
