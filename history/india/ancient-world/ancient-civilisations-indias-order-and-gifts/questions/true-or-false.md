# India's Order and Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The hymns sorted people as brahmin priests and rajas, as jana and vish communities, and as Aryas against Dasas, who sacrificed differently and spoke differently — dasa sliding in time into the word for slave.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — The hymns sort people into orders — brahmin priests and rajas, jana and vish communities, Aryas against Dasas — and the later Vedic books harden the sorting into four varnas by birth, branding some people untouchable; but many pushed back against the idea that birth should fix a life.
