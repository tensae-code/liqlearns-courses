# Lesson plan — India's Order and Gifts

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India's Order and Gifts
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The hymns sort people into orders — brahmin priests and rajas, jana and vish communities, Aryas against Dasas — and the later Vedic books harden the sorting into four varnas by birth, branding some people untouchable; but many pushed back against the idea that birth should fix a life.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Words to describe people** — The hymns sort people as brahmin priests and rajas, as jana and vish communities, and as Aryas against Dasas who sacrifice differently and speak differently — dasa sliding, in time, into the word for slave.
2. **Varnas** — Priests of the later Vedic books divide society by birth into brahmins, kshatriyas, vaishyas and shudras, then brand some people untouchable — and many push back.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
