# India: Magadha Becomes Great — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.5 / A closer look — Magadha.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because magadha rose on its advantages — rivers for transport and fertility, forests full of war-elephants and timber, iron mines for weapons — as Bimbisara and Ajatasattu conquered janapada after janapada, Mahapadma Nanda pushed to the north-west, the capital moved to Pataliputra, and Alexander turned back at the Beas before India's vast armies. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
