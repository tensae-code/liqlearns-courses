# India: Magadha Becomes Great — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Magadha rose on rivers for transport and fertility, forests full of war-elephants and timber, and iron mines for weapons; Bimbisara and Ajatasattu conquered janapada after janapada, and Alexander turned back at the Beas because his soldiers feared India's vast armies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Magadha rose on its advantages — rivers for transport and fertility, forests full of war-elephants and timber, iron mines for weapons — as Bimbisara and Ajatasattu conquered janapada after janapada, Mahapadma Nanda pushed to the north-west, the capital moved to Pataliputra, and Alexander turned back at the Beas before India's vast armies.
