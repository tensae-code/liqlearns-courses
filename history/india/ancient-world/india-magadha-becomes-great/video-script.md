# Video script — India: Magadha Becomes Great

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Magadha rose on its advantages — rivers for transport and fertility, forests full of war-elephants and timber, iron mines for weapons — as Bimbisara and Ajatasattu conquered janapada after janapada, Mahapadma Nanda pushed to the north-west, the capital moved to Pataliputra, and Alexander turned back at the Beas before India's vast armies.

Magadha rose on rivers for transport and fertility, forests full of war-elephants and timber, and iron mines for weapons; Bimbisara and Ajatasattu conquered janapada after janapada, and Alexander turned back at the Beas because his soldiers feared India's vast armies.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
