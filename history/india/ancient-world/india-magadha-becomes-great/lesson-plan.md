# Lesson plan — India: Magadha Becomes Great

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Magadha Becomes Great
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Magadha rose on its advantages — rivers for transport and fertility, forests full of war-elephants and timber, iron mines for weapons — as Bimbisara and Ajatasattu conquered janapada after janapada, Mahapadma Nanda pushed to the north-west, the capital moved to Pataliputra, and Alexander turned back at the Beas before India's vast armies.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. 3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

- Go back to the one thing: Magadha rose on its advantages — rivers for transport and fertility, forests full of war-elephants and timber, iron mines for weapons — as Bimbisara and Ajatasattu conquered janapada after janapada, Mahapadma Nanda pushed to the north-west, the capital moved to Pataliputra, and Alexander turned back at the Beas before India's vast armies.
