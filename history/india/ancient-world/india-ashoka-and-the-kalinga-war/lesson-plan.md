# Lesson plan — India: Ashoka and the Kalinga War

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Ashoka and the Kalinga War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ashoka, the first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquered coastal Kalinga — then read his own casualty figures, a lakh and a half captured and more than a lakh killed, and horrified, renounced war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1.
