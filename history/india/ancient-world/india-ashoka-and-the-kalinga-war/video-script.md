# Video script — India: Ashoka and the Kalinga War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ashoka, the first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquered coastal Kalinga — then read his own casualty figures, a lakh and a half captured and more than a lakh killed, and horrified, renounced war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning.
