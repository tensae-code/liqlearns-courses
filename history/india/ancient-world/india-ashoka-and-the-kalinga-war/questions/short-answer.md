# India: Ashoka and the Kalinga War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.7 / Ashoka, a unique ruler; Ashoka's war in Kalinga.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because ashoka, the first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquered coastal Kalinga — then read his own casualty figures, a lakh and a half captured and more than a lakh killed, and horrified, renounced war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
