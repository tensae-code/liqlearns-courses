# India: Ashoka and the Kalinga War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ashoka, the first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquered Kalinga, then read his own casualty figures — a lakh and a half captured, more than a lakh killed — and horrified, renounced war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Ashoka, the first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquered coastal Kalinga — then read his own casualty figures, a lakh and a half captured and more than a lakh killed, and horrified, renounced war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning.
