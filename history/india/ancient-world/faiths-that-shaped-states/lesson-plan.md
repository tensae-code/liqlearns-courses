# Lesson plan — Faiths That Shaped States

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Faiths That Shaped States
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kanishka's Buddhist council and Ashvaghosha's Sanskrit life of the Buddha mark a turn: Mahayana Buddhism now carves the Buddha's image instead of symbols, and Bodhisattvas who postpone their own release to teach others carry the faith across Central Asia to China, Korea and Japan, while Theravada sails to Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).
