# Faiths That Shaped States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.9 / The spread of Buddhism.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because kanishka's Buddhist council and Ashvaghosha's Sanskrit life of the Buddha mark a turn: Mahayana Buddhism now carves the Buddha's image instead of symbols, and Bodhisattvas who postpone their own release to teach others carry the faith across Central Asia to China, Korea and Japan, while Theravada sails to Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
