# Faiths That Shaped States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kanishka's Buddhist council and Ashvaghosha's Sanskrit life of the Buddha mark Mahayana's turn: the Buddha's image carved at Mathura and Taxila instead of symbols, Bodhisattvas carrying the faith across Central Asia to China, Korea and Japan, while Theravada sailed to Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Kanishka's Buddhist council and Ashvaghosha's Sanskrit life of the Buddha mark a turn: Mahayana Buddhism now carves the Buddha's image instead of symbols, and Bodhisattvas who postpone their own release to teach others carry the faith across Central Asia to China, Korea and Japan, while Theravada sails to Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia.
