Pass mark: A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise — Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan, its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara tower with a mandapa hall, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks — all paid for by kings, queens and hundreds of ordinary devotees whose names survive carved on railings.
