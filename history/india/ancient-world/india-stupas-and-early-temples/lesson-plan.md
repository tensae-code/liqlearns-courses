# Lesson plan — India: Stupas and Early Temples

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Stupas and Early Temples
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise — Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan, its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara tower with a mandapa hall, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks — all paid for by kings, queens and hundreds of ordinary devotees whose names survive carved on railings.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Buildings in brick and stone** — A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise; Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan, its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara tower with a mandapa hall, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks.
2. **How were stupas and temples built?** — Find and quarry good stone, haul it, shape pillars and panels, set them exactly — paid for by kings and queens, and decorated by gifts from devotees: an association of ivory workers pays for a whole Sanchi gateway, and hundreds of donors survive only as names carved on railings.
