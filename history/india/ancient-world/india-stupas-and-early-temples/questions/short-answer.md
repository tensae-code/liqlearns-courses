# India: Stupas and Early Temples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Buildings in brick and stone; How were stupas and temples built?.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because a stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise — Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan, its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara tower with a mandapa hall, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks — all paid for by kings, queens and hundreds of ordinary devotees whose names survive carved on railings. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
