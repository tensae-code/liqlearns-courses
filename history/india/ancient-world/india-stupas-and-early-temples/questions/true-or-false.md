# India: Stupas and Early Temples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path walked clockwise; Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan and its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise — Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan, its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara tower with a mandapa hall, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks — all paid for by kings, queens and hundreds of ordinary devotees whose names survive carved on railings.
