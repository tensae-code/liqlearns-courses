# India: Stupas and Early Temples — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Buildings in brick and stone — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How were stupas and temples built? — b)


Meanings:
a) A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path walked clockwise; Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan and its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks..
b) Stupas and temples were built by quarrying good stone, hauling it, shaping pillars and panels and setting them exactly — paid for by kings and queens and decorated by devotees' gifts; an association of ivory workers paid for a whole Sanchi gateway..

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
