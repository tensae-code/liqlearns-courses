# Video script — India: Stupas and Early Temples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise — Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan, its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara tower with a mandapa hall, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks — all paid for by kings, queens and hundreds of ordinary devotees whose names survive carved on railings.

A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path walked clockwise; Sanchi's brick core is Ashokan and its gateways later gifts; early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara, and at Mahabalipuram whole temples are cut top-down from single rocks.

Stupas and temples were built by quarrying good stone, hauling it, shaping pillars and panels and setting them exactly — paid for by kings and queens and decorated by devotees' gifts; an association of ivory workers paid for a whole Sanchi gateway.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
