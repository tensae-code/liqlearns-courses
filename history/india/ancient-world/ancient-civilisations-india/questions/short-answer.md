# Ancient Civilisations: India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** How Harappa was found; What was special about these cities?; Houses, drains and streets; Life in the city; Food for people in the cities.

**2.** (Sub lesson 5) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because the Harappan cities were planned before they were lived in: a raised citadel and a larger lower town behind baked-brick walls, courtyard houses with private baths and wells, covered drains with inspection holes — while farmers with ploughs and stored water fed them all. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
