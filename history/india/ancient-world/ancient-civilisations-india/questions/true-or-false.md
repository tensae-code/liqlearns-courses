# Ancient Civilisations: India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Railway builders carried off thousands of ancient bricks for track ballast and destroyed whole buildings; eighty years ago archaeologists realised Harappa was among the subcontinent's oldest cities, and every sister site takes its name — Harappan.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 5) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — The Harappan cities were planned before they were lived in: a raised citadel and a larger lower town behind baked-brick walls, courtyard houses with private baths and wells, covered drains with inspection holes — while farmers with ploughs and stored water fed them all.
