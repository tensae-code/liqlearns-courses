## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **How Harappa was found** — Railway builders carry off thousands of ancient bricks for track ballast and destroy whole buildings; eighty years ago archaeologists realise the ruin is among the subcontinent's oldest cities, and every sister site takes its name — Harappan.
2. **What was special about these cities?** — Each city splits into a raised citadel and a larger lower town behind interlocked baked-brick walls; Mohenjodaro's Great Bath, fire altars at Kalibangan and Lothal, and great storehouses crown the citadel.
3. **Houses, drains and streets** — Courtyard houses of one or two storeys, private bathing areas and wells, and covered street drains with inspection holes are all planned and built together.
4. **Life in the city** — Rulers plan and hoard gold and beads, scribes cut the seals whose script we still cannot read, craftspeople work at home and in workshops, travellers return with raw materials and stories, and children play with terracotta toys.
5. **Food for people in the cities** — Countryside farmers grow wheat, barley, pulses, rice, sesame and mustard with a new tool, the plough, store water against the dry months, and drive herds far for summer pasture so the cities can eat.
