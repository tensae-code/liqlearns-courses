# Hands-on — India: The Harappan Cities End

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine everyone in your town quietly leaving over a few years — houses emptied, markets silent. What could make that happen? This lesson is honest about a mystery historians still cannot solve.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Around 3900 years ago people walked away from the Harappan cities: writing, seals and weights fell out of use, long-distance raw materials stopped coming — and nobody yet knows why. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
