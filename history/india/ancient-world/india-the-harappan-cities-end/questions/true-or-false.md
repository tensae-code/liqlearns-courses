# India: The Harappan Cities End — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Around 3900 years ago people walked away from the Harappan cities; writing, seals and weights fell out of use and long-distance raw materials stopped coming — and nobody yet knows why.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Around 3900 years ago people walked away from the Harappan cities: writing, seals and weights fell out of use, long-distance raw materials stopped coming — and nobody yet knows why.
