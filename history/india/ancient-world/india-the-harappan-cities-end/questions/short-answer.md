# India: The Harappan Cities End — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.3 / The mystery of the end.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because around 3900 years ago people walked away from the Harappan cities: writing, seals and weights fell out of use, long-distance raw materials stopped coming — and nobody yet knows why. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
