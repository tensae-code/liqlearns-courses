# Hands-on — India: The Vedas and the Rigveda

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Learn a song by heart — really by heart, every word exact. Now imagine a thousand songs, carried in memory alone for longer than most countries have existed. This lesson is about the people who did exactly that.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma were composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
