# India: The Vedas and the Rigveda

## Do it first

Learn a song by heart — really by heart, every word exact. Now imagine a thousand songs, carried in memory alone for longer than most countries have existed. This lesson is about the people who did exactly that.

The Rigveda is one of the oldest books in the world — and for most of its life it was not a book you could hold. About 3500 years ago, sages composed more than a thousand hymns: to Agni, the fire; to Indra, the storm and war god; to Soma, the sacred drink. These hymns were not written down. They were memorised — syllable by syllable, accent by accent — and passed from teacher to student by voice alone, for centuries. The Rigveda was heard before it was read: a library carried in human memory, guarded by precision.
