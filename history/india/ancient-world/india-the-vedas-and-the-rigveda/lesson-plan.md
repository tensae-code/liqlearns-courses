# Lesson plan — India: The Vedas and the Rigveda

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Vedas and the Rigveda
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma were composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma were composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a song or prayer your family knows by heart and never wrote down — memory was the first library.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
