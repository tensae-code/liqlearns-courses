# India: The Vedas and the Rigveda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.4 What Books and Burials Tell Us / One of the oldest books in the world.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because the Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma were composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
