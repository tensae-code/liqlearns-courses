# India: The Vedas and the Rigveda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma were composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries, heard and recited long before they were written or printed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma were composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed.
