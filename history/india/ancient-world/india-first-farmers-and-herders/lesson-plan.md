## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Storing grain and 'storing' animals** — Surplus grain forces invention — clay pots, woven baskets, storage pits; tended animals become a living store of milk and meat; burnt grain and animal bones identify the first farming sites.
2. **Towards a settled life** — At Burzahom in Kashmir people dig pit-houses with steps and cook indoors or out by the weather; polished Neolithic tools, mortars, cooking pots and woven cotton cloth mark the settled turn.
3. **A closer look — living and dying in Mehrgarh** — In one of the earliest villages, by the Bolan Pass route to Iran, square houses store the barley and wheat people first grew here; a dead person is buried with goats — food, the mourners believed, for the next world.
