# India: First Farmers and Herders — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Surplus grain forced invention — clay pots, woven baskets and storage pits; tended animals became a living store of milk and meat; burnt grain and animal bones are how scientists identify the first farming sites.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Farming forced invention and a new kind of life: surplus grain needed pots, baskets and storage pits, tended animals became a living store of milk and meat, and at Burzahom and Mehrgarh the settled village appears — houses, grinding stones, and the dead buried with goats for the next world.
