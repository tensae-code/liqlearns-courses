# India: First Farmers and Herders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Storing grain and 'storing' animals; Towards a settled life; A closer look — living and dying in Mehrgarh.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because farming forced invention and a new kind of life: surplus grain needed pots, baskets and storage pits, tended animals became a living store of milk and meat, and at Burzahom and Mehrgarh the settled village appears — houses, grinding stones, and the dead buried with goats for the next world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
