# India: First Farmers and Herders — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Storing grain and 'storing' animals — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Towards a settled life — b)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A closer look — living and dying in Mehrgarh — c)


Meanings:
a) Surplus grain forced invention — clay pots, woven baskets and storage pits; tended animals became a living store of milk and meat; burnt grain and animal bones are how scientists identify the first farming sites..
b) At Burzahom in Kashmir people dug pit-houses with steps and cooked indoors or out by the weather; polished Neolithic tools, mortars for grinding, cooking pots and woven cotton cloth mark the settled turn..
c) At Mehrgarh, by the Bolan Pass route to Iran, square houses stored the barley and wheat people first grew there; a dead person was buried with goats — food, the mourners believed, for the next world..

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
