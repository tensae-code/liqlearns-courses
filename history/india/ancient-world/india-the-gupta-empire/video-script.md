# Video script — India: The Gupta Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Samudragupta conquered with four policies — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours bound by tribute and court attendance, and distant Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters — while prashasti genealogies show the family climbing from maha-raja to maharaj-adhiraja, and Chandragupta II finished the Shakas in the west.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
