# Lesson plan — India: The Gupta Empire

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Gupta Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Samudragupta conquered with four policies — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours bound by tribute and court attendance, and distant Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters — while prashasti genealogies show the family climbing from maha-raja to maharaj-adhiraja, and Chandragupta II finished the Shakas in the west.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (recommended, never forced).
