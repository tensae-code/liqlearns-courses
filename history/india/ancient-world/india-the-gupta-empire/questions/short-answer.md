# India: The Gupta Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.10 / Samudragupta's four policies; genealogies; Chandragupta II.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because samudragupta conquered with four policies — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours bound by tribute and court attendance, and distant Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters — while prashasti genealogies show the family climbing from maha-raja to maharaj-adhiraja, and Chandragupta II finished the Shakas in the west. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
