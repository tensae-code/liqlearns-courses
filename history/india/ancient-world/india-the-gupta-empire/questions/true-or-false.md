# India: The Gupta Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Samudragupta used four policies: Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours bound by tribute and court attendance, and Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters; Chandragupta II finished the Shakas in the west.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Samudragupta conquered with four policies — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours bound by tribute and court attendance, and distant Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters — while prashasti genealogies show the family climbing from maha-raja to maharaj-adhiraja, and Chandragupta II finished the Shakas in the west.
