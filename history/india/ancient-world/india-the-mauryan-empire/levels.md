# India: The Mauryan Empire — depth

This lesson has 2 sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Chandragupta Maurya founded an empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by Chanakya, whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; around Pataliputra the emperor ruled directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies, while provinces answered from Taxila and Ujjain under royal princes and forest peoples stayed near-independent, sending elephants, timber, honey and wax.
