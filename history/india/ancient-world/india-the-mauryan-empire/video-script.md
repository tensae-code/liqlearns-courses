# Video script — India: The Mauryan Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Chandragupta Maurya founded an empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by Chanakya, whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; around Pataliputra the emperor ruled directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies, while provinces answered from Taxila and Ujjain under royal princes and forest peoples stayed near-independent, sending elephants, timber, honey and wax.

Chandragupta Maurya founded the empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by Chanakya, whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; the dynasty runs Chandragupta, Bindusara, Ashoka, and an empire needs bigger armies and more officials than a kingdom.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
