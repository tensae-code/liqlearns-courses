# India: The Mauryan Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Chandragupta Maurya founded the empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by Chanakya, whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; the dynasty runs Chandragupta, Bindusara, Ashoka, and an empire needs bigger armies and more officials than a kingdom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Chandragupta Maurya founded an empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by Chanakya, whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; around Pataliputra the emperor ruled directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies, while provinces answered from Taxila and Ujjain under royal princes and forest peoples stayed near-independent, sending elephants, timber, honey and wax.
