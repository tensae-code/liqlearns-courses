# India: The Mauryan Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** A very big kingdom = an empire; Ruling the empire.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because chandragupta Maurya founded an empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by Chanakya, whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; around Pataliputra the emperor ruled directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies, while provinces answered from Taxila and Ujjain under royal princes and forest peoples stayed near-independent, sending elephants, timber, honey and wax. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
