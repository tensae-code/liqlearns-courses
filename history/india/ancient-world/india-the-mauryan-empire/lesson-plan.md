## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A very big kingdom = an empire** — Chandragupta Maurya founds the empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by the wise Chanakya whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; the Maurya dynasty runs Chandragupta, Bindusara, Ashoka — and an empire needs bigger armies and more tax-collecting officials than a kingdom.
2. **Ruling the empire** — Around Pataliputra the emperor rules directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies; provinces answer from Taxila and Ujjain under royal princes; roads and rivers are gripped for tax and tribute, while forest peoples stay near-independent, sending elephants, timber, honey and wax.
