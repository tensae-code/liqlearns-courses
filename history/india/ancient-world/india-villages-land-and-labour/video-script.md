# Video script — India: Villages, Land and Labour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Kings tax farmers, farmers demand surer crops, irrigation follows; and the village stacks its own order — in Tamil country vellalar landowners, uzhavar ploughmen and landless kadaisiyar and adimai labourers, while in the north the hereditary grama bhojaka is landlord, tax-collector, judge and policeman in one.
