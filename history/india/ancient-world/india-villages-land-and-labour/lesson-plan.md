# Lesson plan — India: Villages, Land and Labour

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Villages, Land and Labour
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kings tax farmers, farmers demand surer crops, irrigation follows; and the village stacks its own order — in Tamil country vellalar landowners, uzhavar ploughmen and landless kadaisiyar and adimai labourers, while in the north the hereditary grama bhojaka is landlord, tax-collector, judge and policeman in one.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).
