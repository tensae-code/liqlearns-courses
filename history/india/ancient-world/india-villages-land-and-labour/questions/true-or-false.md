# India: Villages, Land and Labour — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kings taxed farmers, farmers demanded surer crops, and irrigation followed; in Tamil villages vellalar landowners, uzhavar ploughmen and landless kadaisiyar and adimai labourers stacked the order, while in the north the hereditary grama bhojaka was landlord, tax-collector, judge and policeman in one.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Kings tax farmers, farmers demand surer crops, irrigation follows; and the village stacks its own order — in Tamil country vellalar landowners, uzhavar ploughmen and landless kadaisiyar and adimai labourers, while in the north the hereditary grama bhojaka is landlord, tax-collector, judge and policeman in one.
