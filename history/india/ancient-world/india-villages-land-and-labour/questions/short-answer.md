# India: Villages, Land and Labour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.8 Vital Villages, Thriving Towns / irrigation and who lived in the villages.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because kings tax farmers, farmers demand surer crops, irrigation follows; and the village stacks its own order — in Tamil country vellalar landowners, uzhavar ploughmen and landless kadaisiyar and adimai labourers, while in the north the hereditary grama bhojaka is landlord, tax-collector, judge and policeman in one. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
