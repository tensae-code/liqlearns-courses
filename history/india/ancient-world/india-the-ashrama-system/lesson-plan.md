# Lesson plan — India: The Ashrama System

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Ashrama System
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Brahmins answered the new religions with a life-map in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Brahmins answered the new religions with a life-map in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — school, work, retirement — modern lives still fall into stages; the ashrama map made the pattern sacred.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
