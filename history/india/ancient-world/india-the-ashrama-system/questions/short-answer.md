# India: The Ashrama System — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.6 / The system of ashramas.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because brahmins answered the new religions with a life-map in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
