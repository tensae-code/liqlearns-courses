# India: The Ashrama System — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The ashrama system mapped a life in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Brahmins answered the new religions with a life-map in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages.
