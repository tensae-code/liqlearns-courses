# Video script — India: The Ashrama System

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Brahmins answered the new religions with a life-map in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages.

The ashrama system mapped a life in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages.
