# Hands-on — India: The Ashrama System

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw your life as four chapters. What belongs in each? This lesson shows the four-chapter map the brahmins drew — and asks who was allowed to walk the whole map.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Brahmins answered the new religions with a life-map in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women were to follow their husbands' stages. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
