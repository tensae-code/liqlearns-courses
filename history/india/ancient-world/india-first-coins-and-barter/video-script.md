# Video script — India: First Coins and Barter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Punch-marked coins, stamped with symbols and uninscribed, circulated for 500 years across the subcontinent — while a Sangam poem's salt merchants still carted white paddy to swap for coastal salt, whole families travelling roads 'through sands white as moonlight'.
