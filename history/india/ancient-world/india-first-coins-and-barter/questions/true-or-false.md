# India: First Coins and Barter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Punch-marked coins, stamped with symbols and uninscribed, circulated for 500 years across the subcontinent — while Sangam salt merchants still carted white paddy to swap for coastal salt, whole families travelling roads 'through sands white as moonlight'.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Punch-marked coins, stamped with symbols and uninscribed, circulated for 500 years across the subcontinent — while a Sangam poem's salt merchants still carted white paddy to swap for coastal salt, whole families travelling roads 'through sands white as moonlight'.
