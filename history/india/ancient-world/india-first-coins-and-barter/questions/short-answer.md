# India: First Coins and Barter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.8 / Coins; other means of exchange.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because punch-marked coins, stamped with symbols and uninscribed, circulated for 500 years across the subcontinent — while a Sangam poem's salt merchants still carted white paddy to swap for coastal salt, whole families travelling roads 'through sands white as moonlight'. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
