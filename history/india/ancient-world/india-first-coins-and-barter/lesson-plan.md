# Lesson plan — India: First Coins and Barter

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: First Coins and Barter
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Punch-marked coins, stamped with symbols and uninscribed, circulated for 500 years across the subcontinent — while a Sangam poem's salt merchants still carted white paddy to swap for coastal salt, whole families travelling roads 'through sands white as moonlight'.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).
