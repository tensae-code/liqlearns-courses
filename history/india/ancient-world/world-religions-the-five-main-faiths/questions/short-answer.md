# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Buddha's teaching; the story of Kisagotami; Jainism; The beginning of Bhakti.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because from India's sixth-century-BCE ferment come teachings the whole world still lives by: the Buddha's path out of suffering through moderation and kindness, taught in the people's Prakrit; Mahavira's Jain ahimsa so strict it spares even insects; and, centuries later, bhakti — devotion to a chosen deity, open to rich and poor, high caste and low, men and women alike. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
