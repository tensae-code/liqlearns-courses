# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Buddha taught that suffering comes from tanha — an unquenchable thirst for more — and ends in moderation and kindness; he taught in Prakrit so all could understand, and Kisagotami, sent for mustard seed from a house where no one had died, learned that every house knows death.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — From India's sixth-century-BCE ferment come teachings the whole world still lives by: the Buddha's path out of suffering through moderation and kindness, taught in the people's Prakrit; Mahavira's Jain ahimsa so strict it spares even insects; and, centuries later, bhakti — devotion to a chosen deity, open to rich and poor, high caste and low, men and women alike.
