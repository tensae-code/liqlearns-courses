# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Buddha's teaching; the story of Kisagotami — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Jainism — b)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The beginning of Bhakti — c)


Meanings:
a) The Buddha taught that suffering comes from tanha — an unquenchable thirst for more — and ends in moderation and kindness; he taught in Prakrit so all could understand, and Kisagotami, sent for mustard seed from a house where no one had died, learned that every house knows death..
b) Vardhamana Mahavira, the 24th tirthankara, taught ahimsa so strict that followers must not kill even insects; traders embraced it, farmers struggled, and the teachings, carried orally for centuries, were written down at Valabhi about 1500 years ago..
c) Bhakti — devotion to a chosen deity such as Shiva, Vishnu or Durga — opened worship to rich and poor, high caste and low, men and women alike, without elaborate sacrifice; the Gita's counsel and the Tamil poet Appar's hymn carried it across the country..

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
