# Hands-on — World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Three teachers, three doors into the sacred: one says end your thirst for more; one says harm nothing that lives; one says love your god directly, whoever you are. This lesson walks through all three doors.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From India's sixth-century-BCE ferment come teachings the whole world still lives by: the Buddha's path out of suffering through moderation and kindness, taught in the people's Prakrit; Mahavira's Jain ahimsa so strict it spares even insects; and, centuries later, bhakti — devotion to a chosen deity, open to rich and poor, high caste and low, men and women alike. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
