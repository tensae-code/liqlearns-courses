# Lesson plan — World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** World Religions: The Five Main Faiths
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From India's sixth-century-BCE ferment come teachings the whole world still lives by: the Buddha's path out of suffering through moderation and kindness, taught in the people's Prakrit; Mahavira's Jain ahimsa so strict it spares even insects; and, centuries later, bhakti — devotion to a chosen deity, open to rich and poor, high caste and low, men and women alike.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Buddha's teaching; the story of Kisagotami** — Suffering comes from tanha, an unquenchable thirst for more, and ends in moderation, kindness and karma understood by all because he teaches in Prakrit; grieving Kisagotami is sent for mustard seed from a house where no one has died — and learns every house knows death.
2. **Jainism** — Vardhamana Mahavira, 24th tirthankara, teaches ahimsa so strict that followers must not kill even insects — traders embrace it, farmers who must kill crop pests struggle; carried orally for centuries, the teachings are written down at Valabhi about 1500 years ago.
3. **The beginning of Bhakti** — Devotion to a chosen deity — Shiva, Vishnu, Durga — opens worship to rich and poor, high caste and low, men and women alike, without elaborate sacrifice; Krishna's counsel to Arjuna in the Gita and the Tamil poet Appar's hymn carry bhakti across the country.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
