# Harappan Cities of Gujarat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.3 / A closer look — Dholavira and Lothal; seals and sealings.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because dholavira splits into three walled parts and displays giant letters of the Harappan script in white stone, while Lothal's great tank may be a dockyard, its warehouse full of seals and clay sealings that proved a goods-bag had arrived untampered. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
