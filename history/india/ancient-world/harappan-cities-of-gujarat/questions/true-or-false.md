# Harappan Cities of Gujarat — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dholavira split into three walled parts and displayed giant letters of the script in white stone; Lothal's great tank may have been a dockyard, and its warehouse held seals and clay sealings that proved a goods-bag had arrived untampered.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Dholavira splits into three walled parts and displays giant letters of the Harappan script in white stone, while Lothal's great tank may be a dockyard, its warehouse full of seals and clay sealings that proved a goods-bag had arrived untampered.
