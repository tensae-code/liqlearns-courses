# Hands-on — Harappan Cities of Gujarat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write your name as big as you can on a board. Who is it meant for — people near, or people far away? This lesson visits a city that wrote its letters giant-sized, and a port that stamped its cargo shut.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Dholavira splits into three walled parts and displays giant letters of the Harappan script in white stone, while Lothal's great tank may be a dockyard, its warehouse full of seals and clay sealings that proved a goods-bag had arrived untampered. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
