# Lesson plan — Harappan Cities of Gujarat

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Harappan Cities of Gujarat
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Dholavira splits into three walled parts and displays giant letters of the Harappan script in white stone, while Lothal's great tank may be a dockyard, its warehouse full of seals and clay sealings that proved a goods-bag had arrived untampered.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
