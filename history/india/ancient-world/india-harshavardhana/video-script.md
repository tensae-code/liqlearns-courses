# Video script — India: Harshavardhana

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Not the eldest son, Harsha inherited Thanesar on two deaths, avenged his murdered brother-in-law by taking Kanauj and conquering east to Bengal — but at the Narmada the Chalukya Pulakeshin II stopped him cold; his court poet Banabhatta's biography and Xuan Zang's stay at his court give us the reign twice over.
