# Lesson plan — India: Harshavardhana

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Harshavardhana
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Not the eldest son, Harsha inherited Thanesar on two deaths, avenged his murdered brother-in-law by taking Kanauj and conquering east to Bengal — but at the Narmada the Chalukya Pulakeshin II stopped him cold; his court poet Banabhatta's biography and Xuan Zang's stay at his court give us the reign twice over.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (recommended, never forced).
