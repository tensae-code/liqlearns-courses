# India: Harshavardhana — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.10 / Harshavardhana and the Harshacharita.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because not the eldest son, Harsha inherited Thanesar on two deaths, avenged his murdered brother-in-law by taking Kanauj and conquering east to Bengal — but at the Narmada the Chalukya Pulakeshin II stopped him cold; his court poet Banabhatta's biography and Xuan Zang's stay at his court give us the reign twice over. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
