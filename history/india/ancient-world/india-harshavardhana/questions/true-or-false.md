# India: Harshavardhana — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Harsha, not the eldest son, inherited Thanesar after two deaths, avenged his murdered brother-in-law by taking Kanauj and conquering east to Bengal, but Pulakeshin II stopped him at the Narmada; Banabhatta's biography and Xuan Zang's account give us the reign twice over.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Not the eldest son, Harsha inherited Thanesar on two deaths, avenged his murdered brother-in-law by taking Kanauj and conquering east to Bengal — but at the Narmada the Chalukya Pulakeshin II stopped him cold; his court poet Banabhatta's biography and Xuan Zang's stay at his court give us the reign twice over.
