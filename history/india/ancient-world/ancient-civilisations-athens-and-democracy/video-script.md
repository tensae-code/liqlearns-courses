# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Around 2500 years ago Athens let all free men over 30 sit in an assembly meeting forty times a year and filled offices by lottery — yet women, foreigners and thousands of slaves were shut out, and the book asks: do you think this was a true democracy?
