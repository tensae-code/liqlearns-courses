# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Around 2500 years ago Athens let all free men over 30 sit in an assembly meeting forty times a year and filled offices by lottery — yet women, foreigners and thousands of slaves were shut out, and the book asks: do you think this was a true democracy?
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (recommended, never forced).
