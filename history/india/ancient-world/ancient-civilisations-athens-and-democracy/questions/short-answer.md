# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.5 Elsewhere / Athens and democracy.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because around 2500 years ago Athens let all free men over 30 sit in an assembly meeting forty times a year and filled offices by lottery — yet women, foreigners and thousands of slaves were shut out, and the book asks: do you think this was a true democracy? — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
