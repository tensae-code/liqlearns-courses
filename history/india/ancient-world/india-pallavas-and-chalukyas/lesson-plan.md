# Lesson plan — India: Pallavas and Chalukyas

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Pallavas and Chalukyas
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raided each other's rich capitals for generations; Ravikirti's praise-poem boasts Pulakeshin II checked Harsha and drove the Pallava king behind his walls — yet both dynasties soon gave way to Rashtrakutas and Cholas.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (recommended, never forced).
