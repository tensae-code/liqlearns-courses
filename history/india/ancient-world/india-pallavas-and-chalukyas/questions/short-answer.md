# India: Pallavas and Chalukyas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.10 / The Pallavas, Chalukyas and Pulakeshin's prashasti.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raided each other's rich capitals for generations; Ravikirti's praise-poem boasts Pulakeshin II checked Harsha and drove the Pallava king behind his walls — yet both dynasties soon gave way to Rashtrakutas and Cholas. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
