# India: Pallavas and Chalukyas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raided each other's capitals for generations; Ravikirti's prashasti boasts that Pulakeshin II checked Harsha and drove the Pallava king behind his walls, yet both dynasties soon gave way to Rashtrakutas and Cholas.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raided each other's rich capitals for generations; Ravikirti's praise-poem boasts Pulakeshin II checked Harsha and drove the Pallava king behind his walls — yet both dynasties soon gave way to Rashtrakutas and Cholas.
