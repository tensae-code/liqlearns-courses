# Video script — India: Pallavas and Chalukyas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raided each other's rich capitals for generations; Ravikirti's praise-poem boasts Pulakeshin II checked Harsha and drove the Pallava king behind his walls — yet both dynasties soon gave way to Rashtrakutas and Cholas.
