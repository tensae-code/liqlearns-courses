# Hands-on — India: Hunter-Gatherers on the Move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your home has no fridge, no shop and no stored food — only what the land around you offers this week. When would you have to move? This lesson follows the people who lived exactly that way.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Hunter-gatherers could never settle: they moved to follow ripe plants, migrating herds, seasonal fruit and dry-season water, sheltered in rock caves like Bhimbetka, painted the animals they hunted, and had already tamed fire. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
