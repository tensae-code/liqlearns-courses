# Video script — India: Hunter-Gatherers on the Move

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Hunter-gatherers could never settle: they moved to follow ripe plants, migrating herds, seasonal fruit and dry-season water, sheltered in rock caves like Bhimbetka, painted the animals they hunted, and had already tamed fire.

Hunter-gatherers moved because they ate up one place's plants, followed migrating herds, chased seasonal fruit and dry-season water; their surviving stone tools cut meat, scraped hides and tipped spears and arrows.

Bands sheltered in Bhimbetka's caves near the Narmada and painted wild animals on the walls; ash in the Kurnool caves proves they had tamed fire for light, roasting and scaring beasts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
