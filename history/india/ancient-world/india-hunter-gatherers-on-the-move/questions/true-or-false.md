# India: Hunter-Gatherers on the Move — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hunter-gatherers moved because they ate up one place's plants, followed migrating herds, chased seasonal fruit and dry-season water; their surviving stone tools cut meat, scraped hides and tipped spears and arrows.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Hunter-gatherers could never settle: they moved to follow ripe plants, migrating herds, seasonal fruit and dry-season water, sheltered in rock caves like Bhimbetka, painted the animals they hunted, and had already tamed fire.
