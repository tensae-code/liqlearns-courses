# India: Hunter-Gatherers on the Move — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why hunter-gatherers moved, and their stone tools — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Choosing a place to live in; rock paintings; finding out about fire — b)


Meanings:
a) Hunter-gatherers moved because they ate up one place's plants, followed migrating herds, chased seasonal fruit and dry-season water; their surviving stone tools cut meat, scraped hides and tipped spears and arrows..
b) Bands sheltered in Bhimbetka's caves near the Narmada and painted wild animals on the walls; ash in the Kurnool caves proves they had tamed fire for light, roasting and scaring beasts..

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
