# India: Hunter-Gatherers on the Move — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Why hunter-gatherers moved, and their stone tools; Choosing a place to live in; rock paintings; finding out about fire.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because hunter-gatherers could never settle: they moved to follow ripe plants, migrating herds, seasonal fruit and dry-season water, sheltered in rock caves like Bhimbetka, painted the animals they hunted, and had already tamed fire. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
