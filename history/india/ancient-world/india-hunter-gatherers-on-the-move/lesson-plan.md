# Lesson plan — India: Hunter-Gatherers on the Move

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Hunter-Gatherers on the Move
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Hunter-gatherers could never settle: they moved to follow ripe plants, migrating herds, seasonal fruit and dry-season water, sheltered in rock caves like Bhimbetka, painted the animals they hunted, and had already tamed fire.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Why hunter-gatherers moved, and their stone tools** — People who eat up one place's plants, follow migrating herds, chase seasonal fruit and dry-season water can never settle; their stone tools cut meat, scraped hides and tipped spears and arrows.
2. **Choosing a place to live in; rock paintings; finding out about fire** — Bands shelter in Bhimbetka's caves near the Narmada and paint wild animals on the walls; ash in the Kurnool caves proves they had tamed fire.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
