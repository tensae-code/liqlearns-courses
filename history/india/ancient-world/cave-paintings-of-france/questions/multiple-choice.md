# Cave Paintings of France — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Cave paintings in France” is right?
a) Four schoolchildren stumbled on a French cave painted 20,000 to 10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours, perhaps for rituals before the hunt.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in India's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) In a cave in France, painted 20,000 to 10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours — perhaps for rituals before the hunt — hunter-gatherer art matches the paintings of Bhimbetka across the world.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and India has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
