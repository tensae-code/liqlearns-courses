# Cave Paintings of France — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Four schoolchildren stumbled on a French cave painted 20,000 to 10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours, perhaps for rituals before the hunt.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — In a cave in France, painted 20,000 to 10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours — perhaps for rituals before the hunt — hunter-gatherer art matches the paintings of Bhimbetka across the world.
