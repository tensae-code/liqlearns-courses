# Cave Paintings of France — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.2 Elsewhere / Cave paintings in France.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because in a cave in France, painted 20,000 to 10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours — perhaps for rituals before the hunt — hunter-gatherer art matches the paintings of Bhimbetka across the world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
