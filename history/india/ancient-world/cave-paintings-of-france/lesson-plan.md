# Lesson plan — Cave Paintings of France

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Cave Paintings of France
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In a cave in France, painted 20,000 to 10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours — perhaps for rituals before the hunt — hunter-gatherer art matches the paintings of Bhimbetka across the world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know, and the First Farmers (recommended, never forced).
