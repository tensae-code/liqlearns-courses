# Hands-on — Cave Paintings of France

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Four schoolchildren with a dog explore a hillside and find a hole in the ground. What would you do? This lesson is the book's 'Elsewhere' window: what those children found, and why it matters to India's story.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In a cave in France, painted 20,000 to 10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours — perhaps for rituals before the hunt — hunter-gatherer art matches the paintings of Bhimbetka across the world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
