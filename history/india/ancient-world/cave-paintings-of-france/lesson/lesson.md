# Cave Paintings of France

## Do it first

Four schoolchildren with a dog explore a hillside and find a hole in the ground. What would you do? This lesson is the book's 'Elsewhere' window: what those children found, and why it matters to India's story.

The book now looks elsewhere — to France. There, four schoolchildren stumbled into a cave whose walls were covered with paintings: horses, aurochs — great wild cattle — and bison, drawn in mineral colours 20,000 to 10,000 years ago, in the same deep ages as the paintings of Bhimbetka. Why paint animals deep underground, where no one lived? Perhaps, the book suggests, for rituals before the hunt — a way of meeting the animals in spirit before meeting them in the field. On two sides of the world, hunting peoples answered the same life with the same art.
