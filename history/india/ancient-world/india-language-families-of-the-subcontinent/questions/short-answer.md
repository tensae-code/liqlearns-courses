# India: Language Families of the Subcontinent — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.4 / Sanskrit and other languages.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because the subcontinent's languages fall into great families: Sanskrit, Hindi and English share one Indo-European family — matr, ma, mother — while Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam are Dravidian, and the north-east and central India hold Tibeto-Burman and Austro-Asiatic tongues. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
