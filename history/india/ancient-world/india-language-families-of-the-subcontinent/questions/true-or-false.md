# India: Language Families of the Subcontinent — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sanskrit's matr, Hindi's ma and English's mother betray one Indo-European family; Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam form the Dravidian family, with Tibeto-Burman languages in the north-east and Austro-Asiatic in central India.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — The subcontinent's languages fall into great families: Sanskrit, Hindi and English share one Indo-European family — matr, ma, mother — while Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam are Dravidian, and the north-east and central India hold Tibeto-Burman and Austro-Asiatic tongues.
