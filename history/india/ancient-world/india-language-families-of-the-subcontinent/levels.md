# India: Language Families of the Subcontinent — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The subcontinent's languages fall into great families: Sanskrit, Hindi and English share one Indo-European family — matr, ma, mother — while Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam are Dravidian, and the north-east and central India hold Tibeto-Burman and Austro-Asiatic tongues.
