# Video script — India: Language Families of the Subcontinent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The subcontinent's languages fall into great families: Sanskrit, Hindi and English share one Indo-European family — matr, ma, mother — while Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam are Dravidian, and the north-east and central India hold Tibeto-Burman and Austro-Asiatic tongues.

Sanskrit's matr, Hindi's ma and English's mother betray one Indo-European family; Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam form the Dravidian family, with Tibeto-Burman languages in the north-east and Austro-Asiatic in central India.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
