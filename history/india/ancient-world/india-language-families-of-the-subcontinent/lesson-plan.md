# Lesson plan — India: Language Families of the Subcontinent

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Language Families of the Subcontinent
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The subcontinent's languages fall into great families: Sanskrit, Hindi and English share one Indo-European family — matr, ma, mother — while Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam are Dravidian, and the north-east and central India hold Tibeto-Burman and Austro-Asiatic tongues.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First Cities and the Vedic World (recommended, never forced).
