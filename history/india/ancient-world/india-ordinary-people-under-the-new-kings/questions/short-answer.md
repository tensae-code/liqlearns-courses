# India: Ordinary People Under the New Kings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.10 / Ordinary people in the kingdoms.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because in Kalidasa's play a poor fisherman who returns the king's ring found in a fish is accused of theft by the gatekeepers, then rewarded — and the officials demand a cut; Fa Xian records untouchables striking wood to warn others away in markets; Banabhatta's royal army on the march swallows the world in dust, trampling villagers' huts as it passes. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
