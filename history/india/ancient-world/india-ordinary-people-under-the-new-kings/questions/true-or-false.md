# India: Ordinary People Under the New Kings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Kalidasa's play, a poor fisherman who returned the king's ring found in a fish was accused of theft by the gatekeepers, then rewarded — and the officials demanded a cut; Fa Xian recorded untouchables striking wood to warn others away in markets, and Banabhatta's royal army on the march trampled villagers' huts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — In Kalidasa's play a poor fisherman who returns the king's ring found in a fish is accused of theft by the gatekeepers, then rewarded — and the officials demand a cut; Fa Xian records untouchables striking wood to warn others away in markets; Banabhatta's royal army on the march swallows the world in dust, trampling villagers' huts as it passes.
