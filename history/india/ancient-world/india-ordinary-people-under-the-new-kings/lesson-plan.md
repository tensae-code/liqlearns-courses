# Lesson plan — India: Ordinary People Under the New Kings

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Ordinary People Under the New Kings
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Kalidasa's play a poor fisherman who returns the king's ring found in a fish is accused of theft by the gatekeepers, then rewarded — and the officials demand a cut; Fa Xian records untouchables striking wood to warn others away in markets; Banabhatta's royal army on the march swallows the world in dust, trampling villagers' huts as it passes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — New Empires and Kingdoms (recommended, never forced).
