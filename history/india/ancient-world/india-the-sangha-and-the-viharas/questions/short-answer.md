# India: The Sangha and the Viharas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.6 / The sangha; monasteries.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because buddha and Mahavira held that true knowledge needs leaving home, so monks and nuns lived simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours — and their rainy-season shelters hardened into permanent viharas of wood, brick and rock-cut cave, on land gifted by kings and merchants. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
