# India: The Sangha and the Viharas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Buddha and Mahavira held that true knowledge needs leaving home; their monks and nuns lived simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours, and rainy-season shelters hardened into permanent viharas of wood, brick and rock-cut cave, on land gifted by kings and merchants.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Buddha and Mahavira held that true knowledge needs leaving home, so monks and nuns lived simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours — and their rainy-season shelters hardened into permanent viharas of wood, brick and rock-cut cave, on land gifted by kings and merchants.
