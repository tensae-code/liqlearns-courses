# Video script — India: The Sangha and the Viharas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Buddha and Mahavira held that true knowledge needs leaving home, so monks and nuns lived simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours — and their rainy-season shelters hardened into permanent viharas of wood, brick and rock-cut cave, on land gifted by kings and merchants.
