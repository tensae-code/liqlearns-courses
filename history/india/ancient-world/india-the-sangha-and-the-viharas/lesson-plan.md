# Lesson plan — India: The Sangha and the Viharas

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Sangha and the Viharas
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Buddha and Mahavira held that true knowledge needs leaving home, so monks and nuns lived simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours — and their rainy-season shelters hardened into permanent viharas of wood, brick and rock-cut cave, on land gifted by kings and merchants.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Kingdoms, Republics and New Questions (recommended, never forced).
