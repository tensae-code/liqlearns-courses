# Video script — India: Ancient Science and Medicine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause and a near-modern value for a circle's circumference; Indian mathematicians invented the symbol for zero, which travelled via the Arabs to Europe and the world; Charaka and Sushruta founded Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas.
