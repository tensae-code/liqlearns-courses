# India: Ancient Science and Medicine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause; Indian mathematicians invented the symbol for zero, which travelled via the Arabs to Europe; Charaka and Sushruta founded Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — Aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause and a near-modern value for a circle's circumference; Indian mathematicians invented the symbol for zero, which travelled via the Arabs to Europe and the world; Charaka and Sushruta founded Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas.
