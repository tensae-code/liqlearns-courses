# India: Ancient Science and Medicine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.11 / Writing books on science; zero; Ayurveda.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause and a near-modern value for a circle's circumference; Indian mathematicians invented the symbol for zero, which travelled via the Arabs to Europe and the world; Charaka and Sushruta founded Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
