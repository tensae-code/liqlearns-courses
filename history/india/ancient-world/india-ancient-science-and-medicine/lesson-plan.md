# Lesson plan — India: Ancient Science and Medicine

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: Ancient Science and Medicine
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause and a near-modern value for a circle's circumference; Indian mathematicians invented the symbol for zero, which travelled via the Arabs to Europe and the world; Charaka and Sushruta founded Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Buildings, Paintings and Books (recommended, never forced).
