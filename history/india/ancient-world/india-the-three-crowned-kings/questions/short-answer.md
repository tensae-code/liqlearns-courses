# India: The Three Crowned Kings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.9 / New kingdoms along the coasts.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because the muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — held an inland and a coastal capital each, took gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and rewarded praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rose in the west, Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni styling himself lord of the dakshinapatha and marching to three coasts. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
