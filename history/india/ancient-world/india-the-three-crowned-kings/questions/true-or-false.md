# India: The Three Crowned Kings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The muvendar — the Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — held an inland and a coastal capital each, took gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and rewarded praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rose in the west under Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni, lord of the dakshinapatha.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — held an inland and a coastal capital each, took gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and rewarded praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rose in the west, Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni styling himself lord of the dakshinapatha and marching to three coasts.
