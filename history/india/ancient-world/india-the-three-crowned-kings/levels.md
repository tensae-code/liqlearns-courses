Pass mark: The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — held an inland and a coastal capital each, took gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and rewarded praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rose in the west, Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni styling himself lord of the dakshinapatha and marching to three coasts.
