# Video script — India: The Three Crowned Kings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — held an inland and a coastal capital each, took gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and rewarded praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rose in the west, Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni styling himself lord of the dakshinapatha and marching to three coasts.
