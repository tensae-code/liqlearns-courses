# Lesson plan — India: The Three Crowned Kings

**Course:** History — India · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** India: The Three Crowned Kings
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — held an inland and a coastal capital each, took gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and rewarded praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rose in the west, Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni styling himself lord of the dakshinapatha and marching to three coasts.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empire and Exchange: Ashoka to the Pilgrims (recommended, never forced).
