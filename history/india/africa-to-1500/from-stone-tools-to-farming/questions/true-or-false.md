# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** About 12,000 years ago the world warmed and grasslands spread; hunters learned the habits of deer, sheep and cattle until they were herding them, and grain-collectors became the first farmers; the dog was tamed first.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in India.
**Answer: False** — About 12,000 years ago the world warmed, grasslands spread, and hunters who had learned the habits of deer, sheep and cattle began herding them, while grain-collectors became the first farmers — the dog was tamed first of all.
