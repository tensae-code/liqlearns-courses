# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ch.2 / A changing environment; the beginning of farming and herding; domestication.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in India?
**Answer:** Because about 12,000 years ago the world warmed, grasslands spread, and hunters who had learned the habits of deer, sheep and cattle began herding them, while grain-collectors became the first farmers — the dog was tamed first of all. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
