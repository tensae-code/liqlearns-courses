# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
About 12,000 years ago the world warmed, grasslands spread, and hunters who had learned the habits of deer, sheep and cattle began herding them, while grain-collectors became the first farmers — the dog was tamed first of all.

About 12,000 years ago the world warmed and grasslands spread; hunters learned the habits of deer, sheep and cattle until they were herding them, and grain-collectors became the first farmers; the dog was tamed first.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
