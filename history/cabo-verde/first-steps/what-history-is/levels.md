# What History Is — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — History is the science that studies people in time and space — it works from traces of the past, like maps, old texts, photographs, monuments and oral tradition, so we can understand Cabo Verde's present through its past — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
