# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is History, in this lesson?
a) the science that studies people in time and space  b) a list of kings' names only  c) stories invented for fun  d) the study of the future
**Answer: a) the science that studies people in time and space** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is one of the traces History works from?
a) oral tradition  b) tomorrow's weather  c) a dream  d) a shopping list for next week
**Answer: a) oral tradition** — That is what this lesson teaches.
