# A Creole Society Is Born — depth

This lesson has 2 sub lessons, each opening when the one before it is proved; the gate pool for each is that sub lesson's whole question set (no hardness ranking is written in questions/). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — on the islands, peoples and cultures from Europe and Africa met and made something new — a creole society, creole in its language, food, music and custom — and that creole culture is the Cabo Verdean people's own wealth — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
