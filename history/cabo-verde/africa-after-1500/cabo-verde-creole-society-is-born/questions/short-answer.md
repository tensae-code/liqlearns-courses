# A Creole Society Is Born — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In what four things was the new society creole, as the first book tells it? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Language, food, music and custom.

**2.** Name three parts of Cabo Verdean culture as the workbook presents them. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any three of: language, oral traditions, typical dishes, religious festivals, music and dance, literature, crafts, housing.
