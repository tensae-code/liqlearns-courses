# A Creole Society Is Born — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was born when peoples and cultures from Europe and Africa met on the islands? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a new creole society  b) a copy of Portugal  c) a copy of one African kingdom  d) nothing — the peoples never mixed
**Answer: a) a new creole society** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In Cabral's words, what is Cabo Verdean culture for its people? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the people's own wealth, to defend and develop  b) something to be ashamed of  c) something borrowed that must be returned  d) only a tourist show
**Answer: a) the people's own wealth, to defend and develop** — That is what this lesson teaches.
