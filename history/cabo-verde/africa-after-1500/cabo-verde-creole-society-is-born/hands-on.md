# Hands-on — A Creole Society Is Born

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Listen to the language spoken around you in Cabo Verde — or think of a Cabo Verdean dish, song or festival you know. Now say out loud: "This did not come from one place. It was born here, from peoples who met." This lesson tells how that meeting made a new society — and a new culture.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: on the islands, peoples and cultures from Europe and Africa met and made something new — a creole society, creole in its language, food, music and custom — and that creole culture is the Cabo Verdean people's own wealth. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, at the harbour. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
