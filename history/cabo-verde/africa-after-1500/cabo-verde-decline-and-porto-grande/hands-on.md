# Hands-on — Decline and Porto Grande

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a shop on a road that the traffic no longer uses — the road moves, and the shop fades. Say out loud: "A port lives from the routes that call at it." This lesson tells how the islands' first great port faded — and how a new one rose on a different island.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: when the old sailing routes faded, Ribeira Grande decayed and the islands were neglected — until steam shipping made Mindelo's Porto Grande a great coaling port, and São Vicente took Santiago's place. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, at the harbour. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
