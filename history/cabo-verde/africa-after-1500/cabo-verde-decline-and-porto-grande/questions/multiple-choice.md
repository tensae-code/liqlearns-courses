# Decline and Porto Grande — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened to Ribeira Grande when the old routes faded?
a) it decayed, and the islands were neglected  b) it became bigger than ever  c) it moved to another island  d) it became the coaling port
**Answer: a) it decayed, and the islands were neglected** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What made Porto Grande great?
a) steam shipping — it became a great coaling port at Mindelo  b) cotton farming  c) a gold mine  d) the old sailing routes returning
**Answer: a) steam shipping — it became a great coaling port at Mindelo** — That is what this lesson teaches.
