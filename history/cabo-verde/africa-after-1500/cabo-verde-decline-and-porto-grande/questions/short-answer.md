# Decline and Porto Grande — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why do steam ships need a port like Porto Grande?
**Answer:** Steam ships crossing the Atlantic must take on coal mid-voyage; Porto Grande was a great coaling port in the right place.

**2.** What faded first, before Ribeira Grande decayed?
**Answer:** The old (sailing) routes that had called at the islands.
