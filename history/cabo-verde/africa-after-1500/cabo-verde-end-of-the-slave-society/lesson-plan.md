# Lesson plan — The End of the Slave Society

**Course:** History — Cabo Verde · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The End of the Slave Society
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Past of the Islands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** slavery was abolished, and in the second half of the 1800s Cabo Verdean society, culture and economy were remade without it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Past of the Islands — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **Popular Revolts in the Islands** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: slavery was abolished, and in the second half of the 1800s Cabo Verdean society, culture and economy were remade without it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or island — or, for this book's lessons, from the Cabo Verde they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Cabo Verde and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the islands in the second half of the 1800s — people working the land, the ports and the towns as free people, rebuilding society, culture and economy — the student names three things in the picture — society, culture, economy — and says each one had to be remade.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's one thing and gives a real example (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).
