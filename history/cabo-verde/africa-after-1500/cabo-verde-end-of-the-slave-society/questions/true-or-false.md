# The End of the Slave Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The creole society had grown up with slavery at the base of its strict structure.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Abolition remade the islands' society by itself, in a single day, with no work after it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
