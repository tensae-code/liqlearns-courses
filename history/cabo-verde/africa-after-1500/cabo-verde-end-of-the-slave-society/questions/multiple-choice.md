# The End of the Slave Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What ended in the islands in the course of the 1800s?
a) slavery — the slave-owning society  b) all farming  c) the creole language  d) trade with the coast
**Answer: a) slavery — the slave-owning society** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** After abolition, what was remade in the second half of the 1800s?
a) Cabo Verdean society, culture and economy  b) only the harbour at Mindelo  c) nothing at all  d) the islands' position in the Atlantic
**Answer: a) Cabo Verdean society, culture and economy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
