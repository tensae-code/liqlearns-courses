# The End of the Slave Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the islands' old society rest on, from the peopling onward?
**Answer:** Slavery — enslaved Africans farmed, built, served and laboured under a strict social structure.

**2.** Name the three things the lesson says were remade without slavery.
**Answer:** Society, culture and economy.
