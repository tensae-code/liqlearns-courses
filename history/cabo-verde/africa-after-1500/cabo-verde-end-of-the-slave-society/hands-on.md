# Hands-on — The End of the Slave Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember how the islands were peopled: many Africans were brought enslaved, and the creole society grew up inside a strict order with slavery at its base. Say out loud: "One day, that order ended." This lesson tells what changed when slavery was abolished — and how the islands had to remake themselves without it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: slavery was abolished, and in the second half of the 1800s Cabo Verdean society, culture and economy were remade without it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, at the harbour. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
