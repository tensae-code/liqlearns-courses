# Lesson plan — Discovery and Settlement of Cabo Verde

**Course:** History — Cabo Verde · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Discovery and Settlement of Cabo Verde
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Past of the Islands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in the age of European expansion, Portuguese sailors found the islands of Cabo Verde with nobody living on them — and Europeans and Africans, many of them enslaved, then peopled Santiago and Fogo first, and the remaining islands after.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Past of the Islands — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Discovery of the islands of Cabo Verde** — In the age of European expansion, Portuguese sailors find the uninhabited islands — and the book asks who really "discovered" land no one lived on.
2. **Occupation and peopling of the islands** — Settling empty, dry islands is hard; Europeans and Africans (many enslaved) people Santiago and Fogo first, then the remaining islands — farming cotton and maize, raising cattle, fishing and trading.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: in the age of European expansion, Portuguese sailors found the islands of Cabo Verde with nobody living on them — and Europeans and Africans, many of them enslaved, then peopled Santiago and Fogo first, and the remaining islands after.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or island — or, for this book's lessons, from the Cabo Verde they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Cabo Verde and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.

- Mixes up "found" and "lived on" → the islands were found empty; people came afterwards, and peopling them was the hard part.
- Go back to the one thing: in the age of European expansion, Portuguese sailors found the islands of Cabo Verde with nobody living on them — and Europeans and Africans, many of them enslaved, then peopled Santiago and Fogo first, and the remaining islands after. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
