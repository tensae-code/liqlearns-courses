# Hands-on — Discovery and Settlement of Cabo Verde

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand at a harbour or look at a map of the Atlantic. Point to the islands of Cabo Verde, far out at sea off West Africa. Now say out loud: "When the first ships reached these islands, nobody lived there at all." This lesson tells how empty islands were found — and how people then came to live on them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: in the age of European expansion, Portuguese sailors found the islands of Cabo Verde with nobody living on them — and Europeans and Africans, many of them enslaved, then peopled Santiago and Fogo first, and the remaining islands after. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, at the harbour. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
