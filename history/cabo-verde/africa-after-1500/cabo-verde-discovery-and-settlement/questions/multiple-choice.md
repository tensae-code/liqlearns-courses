# Discovery and Settlement of Cabo Verde — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Portuguese sailors find when they reached the islands of Cabo Verde? (Sub lesson 1)
a) islands with nobody living on them  b) a great city  c) islands full of farms  d) another fleet already settled there
**Answer: a) islands with nobody living on them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which islands were peopled first? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Santiago and Fogo  b) São Vicente and Sal  c) Maio and Boa Vista  d) all ten at the same time
**Answer: a) Santiago and Fogo** — That is what this lesson teaches.
