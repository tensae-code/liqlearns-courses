# Discovery and Settlement of Cabo Verde — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What question does the book ask about the word "discovered"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Who really "discovered" the islands — can land that no one lived on be discovered?

**2.** Name two things the settlers did to live on the islands. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any two of: farming cotton and maize, raising cattle, fishing, trading.
