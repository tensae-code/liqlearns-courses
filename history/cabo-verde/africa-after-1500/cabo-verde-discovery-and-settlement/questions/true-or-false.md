# Discovery and Settlement of Cabo Verde — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** People were already living on the islands when the Portuguese sailors arrived. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.

**2.** Many of the Africans brought to people the islands were enslaved. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
