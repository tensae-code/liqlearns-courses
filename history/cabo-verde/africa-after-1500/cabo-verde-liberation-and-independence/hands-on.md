# Hands-on — Liberation and Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember the risings of the poor — 1811, 1822, and on to 1910. Say out loud: "The resistance never disappeared; in the 1900s it became a liberation struggle." This lesson tells how that struggle was led, how it fought, and how it ended: with independence.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the PAIGC under Amílcar Cabral first tried negotiation with Portugal and, refused, launched the armed struggle in Guinea — and after Portugal's Carnation Revolution of 25 April 1974, Cabo Verde's independence was proclaimed on 5 July 1975, in unity with Guinea-Bissau. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, at the harbour. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
