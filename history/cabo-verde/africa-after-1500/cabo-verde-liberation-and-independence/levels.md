# Liberation and Independence — depth

This lesson has 2 sub lessons, each opening when the one before it is proved; the gate pool for each is that sub lesson's whole question set (no hardness ranking is written in questions/). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the PAIGC under Amílcar Cabral first tried negotiation with Portugal and, refused, launched the armed struggle in Guinea — and after Portugal's Carnation Revolution of 25 April 1974, Cabo Verde's independence was proclaimed on 5 July 1975, in unity with Guinea-Bissau — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
