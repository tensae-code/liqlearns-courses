# Liberation and Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** When negotiation was refused, the PAIGC launched the armed struggle in Guinea. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Carnation Revolution of 25 April 1974 happened in Cabo Verde and ended the monarchy there. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
