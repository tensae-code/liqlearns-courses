# Liberation and Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who led the PAIGC, and what two roads did the party walk, in order? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Amílcar Cabral; first negotiation with Portugal, then — refused — the armed struggle in Guinea.

**2.** With whom was Cabo Verde's independence proclaimed in unity? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Guinea-Bissau.
