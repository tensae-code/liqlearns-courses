# The First and Second Republics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the difference between the First and Second Republics' way of ruling?
**Answer:** The First Republic ruled one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV; the Second Republic, from 1991, is a multiparty democracy.

**2.** What did the Second Republic take, to mark the change?
**Answer:** New symbols — and the country itself was changed.
