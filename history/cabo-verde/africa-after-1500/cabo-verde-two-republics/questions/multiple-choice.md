# The First and Second Republics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How was the First Republic ruled?
a) one-party, under the PAIGC/PAICV  b) by many parties from the first day  c) by Portugal  d) by the Church
**Answer: a) one-party, under the PAIGC/PAICV** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What began in 1991?
a) the Second Republic, with multiparty democracy  b) the First Republic  c) the armed struggle  d) the peopling of Santiago
**Answer: a) the Second Republic, with multiparty democracy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
