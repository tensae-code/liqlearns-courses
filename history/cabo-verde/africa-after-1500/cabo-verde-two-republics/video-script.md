# Video script — The First and Second Republics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The first republic ruled one-party under the paigc/paicv, with its first leaders and symbols — and in 1991 multiparty democracy installed the second republic, with new symbols and a changed country.

On 5 July 1975 the struggle ended in independence. Now the new state had to be **formed** — and its first form was the **First Republic**. The country was ruled **one-party** under the **PAIGC**, and after it, the **PAICV**: one party leading the state, with its **first leaders** and its own **symbols** — the flag and emblem under which the young country presented itself to the world.

One-party rule lasted a decade and a half. Then the country changed its answer. In **1991**, **multiparty democracy** was installed: more than one party, elections the people could choose in — and with it the **Second Republic** began.

A new republic marks itself. The Second Republic took **new symbols**, and the country itself was **changed** — in how it was governed, in who could speak and organise, in the choices open to its citizens. The workbook teaches the political words inside this story — one-party rule, multiparty rule, the electoral system — as part of the story itself: first one party ruled, then the people gained a choice among many. That change, from the First Republic to the Second, is the last turn of the islands' story as Cabo Verde's own books tell it — from empty islands to a creole society, from slavery's end to liberation, and from liberation to a democracy its citizens can change.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, on your island.
