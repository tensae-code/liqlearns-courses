# Hands-on — The First and Second Republics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Independence day ends one story and starts another: a new country must now govern itself. Say out loud: "First, who rules, and how?" This lesson tells how Cabo Verde answered that question twice — in its First Republic, and again in its Second.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the First Republic ruled one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV, with its first leaders and symbols — and in 1991 multiparty democracy installed the Second Republic, with new symbols and a changed country. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, at the harbour. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
