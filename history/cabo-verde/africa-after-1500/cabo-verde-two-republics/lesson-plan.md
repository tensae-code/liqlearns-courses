# Lesson plan — The First and Second Republics

**Course:** History — Cabo Verde · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The First and Second Republics
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Liberation and the Two Republics (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the First Republic ruled one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV, with its first leaders and symbols — and in 1991 multiparty democracy installed the Second Republic, with new symbols and a changed country.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Liberation and the Two Republics — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
