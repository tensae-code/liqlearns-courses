# Hands-on — Cabo Verde on the World Routes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find Cabo Verde on a map of the Atlantic, between Europe, Africa and America. Put your finger on the sailing routes that pass it. Say out loud: "A ship crossing this ocean needs a place to stop — and these islands are that place." This lesson tells what that position did for the islands' first great city.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the islands became a strategic stopping point on the Atlantic routes, and Ribeira Grande on Santiago rose fast on the trade with the nearby West African coast, with the Church at its centre. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, at the harbour. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
