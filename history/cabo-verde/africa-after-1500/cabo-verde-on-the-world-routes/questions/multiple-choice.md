# Cabo Verde on the World Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did ships stop at the islands of Cabo Verde?
a) the islands were a strategic stopping point on the Atlantic routes  b) ships were forbidden to sail past them  c) the islands had the only harbour in the world  d) sailors were lost and stopped by accident every time
**Answer: a) the islands were a strategic stopping point on the Atlantic routes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which city rose fast on the trade with the nearby West African coast?
a) Ribeira Grande, on Santiago  b) Mindelo, on São Vicente  c) Praia, on Santiago  d) Santa Maria, on Sal
**Answer: a) Ribeira Grande, on Santiago** — That is what this lesson teaches.
