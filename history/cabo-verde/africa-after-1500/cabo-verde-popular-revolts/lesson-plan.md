# Lesson plan — Popular Revolts in the Islands

**Course:** History — Cabo Verde · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Popular Revolts in the Islands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Past of the Islands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** again and again the poor of the islands rose — Ribeira Grande 1811, the Engenhos 1822, Monte Agarro 1835, Achada Falcão 1841, Casa Martins 1847, Ribeirão Manuel 1910 — against taxes, hunger and abuse.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Past of the Islands — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
