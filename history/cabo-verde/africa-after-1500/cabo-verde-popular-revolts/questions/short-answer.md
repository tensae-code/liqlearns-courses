# Popular Revolts in the Islands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two of the risings the book lists, with their years.
**Answer:** Any two of: Ribeira Grande 1811; the Engenhos 1822; Monte Agarro 1835; Achada Falcão 1841; Casa Martins 1847; Ribeirão Manuel 1910.

**2.** Why did the risings keep happening, generation after generation?
**Answer:** Because the same causes remained: taxes the poor could not pay, hunger, and abuse by those in power.
