# Popular Revolts in the Islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Against what did the poor of the islands rise?
a) taxes, hunger and abuse  b) the building of ports  c) the arrival of steam ships  d) the teaching of history in schools
**Answer: a) taxes, hunger and abuse** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which rising came last, in 1910?
a) Ribeirão Manuel  b) Ribeira Grande  c) the Engenhos  d) Casa Martins
**Answer: a) Ribeirão Manuel** — That is what this lesson teaches.
