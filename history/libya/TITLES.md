# History — Libya course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Libya's own — built from Libya's Centre for Educational Curricula and Educational Research books (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': first steps and the ancient Arab world, Arabia and the rise of Islam, the caliphates and Islamic civilisation, the Maghreb, al-Andalus and Islam in Africa, Ottoman Libya and the Karamanlis, and Europe transformed with Libya's struggle to independence. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
