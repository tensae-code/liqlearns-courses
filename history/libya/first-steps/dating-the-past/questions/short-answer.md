# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Part 1: dividing history and reckoning time; Dividing history into eras.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Dividing history into eras”?
**Answer:** Historians cut the human story into eras — the ages before writing, then the historical ages — and each era is known by its own mark.
