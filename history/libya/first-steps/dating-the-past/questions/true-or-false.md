# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Part 1: dividing history and reckoning time”.
**Answer: False** — The past is cut into ages and eras and dated by centuries and by before/after turning points, so a Libyan, an Egyptian and a Greek event can be set on one timeline.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Historians cut the human story into eras — the ages before writing, then the historical ages — and each era is known by its own mark.
**Answer: True**
