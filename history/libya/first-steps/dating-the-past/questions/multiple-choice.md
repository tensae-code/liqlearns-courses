# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Part 1: dividing history and reckoning time”?
a) The past is cut into ages and eras and dated by centuries and by before/after turning points, so a Libyan, an Egyptian and a Greek event can be set on one timeline.  b) Quarrelling Sumerian cities weaken; Sargon of Akkad builds the first empire; Assyria rises and falls in 612 BCE; Nebuchadnezzar's Babylon destroys the Temple in Jerusalem before Cyrus takes it in 539 BCE.  c) Mongol armies sweep the east and sack Baghdad, until the Mamluks of Egypt stop them and the Islamic world survives its darkest blow.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The past is cut into ages and eras and dated by centuries and by before/after turning points, so a Libyan, an Egyptian and a Greek event can be set on one timeline.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Dividing history into eras”?
a) Historians cut the human story into eras — the ages before writing, then the historical ages — and each era is known by its own mark.  b) Rustamids at Tahert, Idrisids in Morocco and Aghlabids in Ifriqiya break away from Baghdad and rule the Maghreb as their own states.  c) From the first battles of 1911 at Abu Meliana and al-Shatt, Libyans resist Italy for twenty years through desert war and Italian detention camps until independence comes after the second war.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Historians cut the human story into eras — the ages before writing, then the historical ages — and each era is known by its own mark.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
