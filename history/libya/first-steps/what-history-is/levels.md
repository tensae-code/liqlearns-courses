# What History Is — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — human memory is too short for the past, so people invent writing; the historian gathers remains and documents, puts events in time order and cleans out errors to reach the truth — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
