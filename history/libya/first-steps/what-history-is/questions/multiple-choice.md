# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Part 1: the historian and history”?
a) Human memory is too short for the past, so people invent writing; the historian gathers remains and documents, puts events in time order and cleans out errors to reach the truth.  b) Driven by faith and pressure at home, Muslim armies defeat both Persia and Byzantium, and the book asks why the two great empires fell so fast.  c) European powers pick at the Ottoman Empire's edges — Algeria, Egypt, Tunisia — while diplomacy in Europe decides the sick man's fate.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Human memory is too short for the past, so people invent writing; the historian gathers remains and documents, puts events in time order and cleans out errors to reach the truth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
