# Central Arabia: Kinda and the Desert Tribes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (Arabian Peninsula): civilisation in the centre”?
a) The desert tribes of Najd accept kings from Yemen's choice and Hujr ibn Amr founds Kinda, the one kingdom of central Arabia before Islam.  b) Britain takes Palestine in the war, the Balfour promise opens it to Zionist settlement, and Palestinian Arabs organise a national movement against both.  c) A kind climate, open land where peoples can meet and trade, enough food, and safety from attack — where these gather, villages grow into civilisations.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The desert tribes of Najd accept kings from Yemen's choice and Hujr ibn Amr founds Kinda, the one kingdom of central Arabia before Islam.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
