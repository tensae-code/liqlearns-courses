## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit: the concept of civilisation and the factors that raised the ancient civilisations of the Arab world** — Great rivers, fertile land, climate and security gather people into the first civilisations, and the Arab world's valleys are where it happens first.
2. **Factors in the rise of ancient civilisation** — A kind climate, open land where peoples can meet and trade, enough food, and safety from attack — where these gather, villages grow into civilisations.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
