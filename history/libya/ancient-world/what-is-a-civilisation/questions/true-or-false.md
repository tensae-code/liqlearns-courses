# What Is a Civilisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit: the concept of civilisation and the factors that raised the ancient civilisations of the Arab world”.
**Answer: False** — Great rivers, fertile land, climate and security gather people into the first civilisations, and the Arab world's valleys are where it happens first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** A kind climate, open land where peoples can meet and trade, enough food, and safety from attack — where these gather, villages grow into civilisations.
**Answer: True**
