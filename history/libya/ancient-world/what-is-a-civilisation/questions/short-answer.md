# What Is a Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Unit: the concept of civilisation and the factors that raised the ancient civilisations of the Arab world; Factors in the rise of ancient civilisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Factors in the rise of ancient civilisation”?
**Answer:** A kind climate, open land where peoples can meet and trade, enough food, and safety from attack — where these gather, villages grow into civilisations.
