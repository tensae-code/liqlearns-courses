# What Is a Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit: the concept of civilisation and the factors that raised the ancient civilisations of the Arab world”?
a) Great rivers, fertile land, climate and security gather people into the first civilisations, and the Arab world's valleys are where it happens first.  b) Through Spain, Sicily and translation, Arabic science and philosophy reach Europe and help wake its Renaissance.  c) Spain seizes Tripoli in 1510 and hands it to the Knights of St John, who oppress it; the people appeal to Suleiman the Magnificent, and in August 1551 Sinan Pasha's fleet takes the city — Murad Agha becomes the first Ottoman wali and builds his mosque and school.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Great rivers, fertile land, climate and security gather people into the first civilisations, and the Arab world's valleys are where it happens first.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Factors in the rise of ancient civilisation”?
a) A kind climate, open land where peoples can meet and trade, enough food, and safety from attack — where these gather, villages grow into civilisations.  b) Aramaic becomes the East's common tongue and mother of the Arabic script; Nabataeans carve Petra and guard its water by law until Rome annexes them in 106 CE and the caravans move to Palmyra; Ghassanids and Lakhmids hold the desert edges.  c) Narmer (Mena) joins Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom, and the Nile Valley becomes a single state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A kind climate, open land where peoples can meet and trade, enough food, and safety from attack — where these gather, villages grow into civilisations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
