# Phoenicians: Alphabet and Sea Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit: features of Levantine civilisation”?
a) The Phoenician cities of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos sail the whole Mediterranean, sell purple dye and cedar, and hand every later people their alphabet.  b) The fleet shells Tripoli on 3 October 1911 against barely 7,000 Ottoman troops, and the garrison refuses Istanbul's surrender order; at Ouchy on 18 October 1912 the Sultan withdraws — but his firman grants Libyans self-rule, so Libyans reject the treaty and fight on: Derna's Friday battle in May 1913, Janduba, and Mahruqa in Fezzan.  c) Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Phoenician cities of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos sail the whole Mediterranean, sell purple dye and cedar, and hand every later people their alphabet.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
