# Phoenicians: Alphabet and Sea Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Unit: features of Levantine civilisation; The Phoenicians of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Phoenicians of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos”?
**Answer:** Cedar ships steered by the pole star carry purple dye and glass to Cadiz and Carthage, and the 22-letter alphabet they invent travels via Greece and Rome into Europe's letters.
