# Lesson plan — Phoenicians: Alphabet and Sea Trade

**Course:** History — Libya · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Phoenicians: Alphabet and Sea Trade
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Phoenician cities of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos sail the whole Mediterranean, sell purple dye and cedar, and hand every later people their alphabet.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit: features of Levantine civilisation** — The Phoenician cities of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos sail the whole Mediterranean, sell purple dye and cedar, and hand every later people their alphabet.
2. **The Phoenicians of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos** — Cedar ships steered by the pole star carry purple dye and glass to Cadiz and Carthage, and the 22-letter alphabet they invent travels via Greece and Rome into Europe's letters.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
