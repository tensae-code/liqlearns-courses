# Video script — Phoenicians: Alphabet and Sea Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The Phoenician cities of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos sail the whole Mediterranean, sell purple dye and cedar, and hand every later people their alphabet. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Cedar ships steered by the pole star carry purple dye and glass to Cadiz and Carthage, and the 22-letter alphabet they invent travels via Greece and Rome into Europe's letters. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
