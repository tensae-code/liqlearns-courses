# Medieval World: The Ottomans

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Medieval World: The Ottomans** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 1: the Arab world at the opening of the 16th century

The Arab lands matter for their place between three continents and their holy cities just as a new Turkish power, the Ottoman state, rises and turns toward them. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 1: the rise and development of the Ottoman state and its coming to the Arab region

The Ottomans grow from a frontier emirate into an empire, defeat the Mamluks, and take Syria, Egypt and the holy cities into their rule. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 2: the Arab region under Ottoman rule

Sultans govern the Arab provinces through appointed governors, tax farmers and local notables, and four centuries of Ottoman rule reshape the region's cities and countryside. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
