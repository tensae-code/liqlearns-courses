# Medieval World: The Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 1: the Arab world at the opening of the 16th century”.
**Answer: False** — The Arab lands matter for their place between three continents and their holy cities just as a new Turkish power, the Ottoman state, rises and turns toward them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Ottomans grow from a frontier emirate into an empire, defeat the Mamluks, and take Syria, Egypt and the holy cities into their rule.
**Answer: True**
