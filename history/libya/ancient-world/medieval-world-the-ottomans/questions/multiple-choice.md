# Medieval World: The Ottomans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 1: the Arab world at the opening of the 16th century”?
a) The Arab lands matter for their place between three continents and their holy cities just as a new Turkish power, the Ottoman state, rises and turns toward them.  b) Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and migrates to Medina, where the first Muslim community is born.  c) Umayyad armies carry Islam to the Atlantic in the west and to Sindh and Transoxiana in the east, the widest reach of the early conquests.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Arab lands matter for their place between three continents and their holy cities just as a new Turkish power, the Ottoman state, rises and turns toward them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 1: the rise and development of the Ottoman state and its coming to the Arab region”?

**Answer: a) The Ottomans grow from a frontier emirate into an empire, defeat the Mamluks, and take Syria, Egypt and the holy cities into their rule.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
