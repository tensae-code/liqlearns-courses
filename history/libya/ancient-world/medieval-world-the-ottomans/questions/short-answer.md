# Medieval World: The Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 1: the Arab world at the opening of the 16th century; Chapter 1: the rise and development of the Ottoman state and its coming to the Arab region; Chapter 2: the Arab region under Ottoman rule.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Chapter 1: the rise and development of the Ottoman state and its coming to the Arab region”?
**Answer:** The Ottomans grow from a frontier emirate into an empire, defeat the Mamluks, and take Syria, Egypt and the holy cities into their rule.
