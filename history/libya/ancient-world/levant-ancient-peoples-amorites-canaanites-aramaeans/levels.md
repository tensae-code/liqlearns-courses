# Levant Ancient Peoples: Amorites, Canaanites, Aramaeans — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Amorites, Canaanites and Aramaeans build rival kingdoms and city-states across Syria and Palestine, trading and fighting between Egypt and Mesopotamia — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
