Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit: the eras of ancient civilisation in the Levant”?
a) Amorites, Canaanites and Aramaeans build rival kingdoms and city-states across Syria and Palestine, trading and fighting between Egypt and Mesopotamia.  b) The Ottomans enter the world war beside Germany and lose their Arab provinces and then the empire itself.  c) The war grows out of Franco-German hatred, the Moroccan crises and the shot at Sarajevo on 28 June 1914; when Italy joins the Allies in May 1915 it pulls its troops back, and at al-Qardabiya on 29 April 1915 a united Libyan force crushes its column — by 1916 Italy holds only Tripoli and Khums.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Amorites, Canaanites and Aramaeans build rival kingdoms and city-states across Syria and Palestine, trading and fighting between Egypt and Mesopotamia.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
