# Islam Expands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Expansion under the Rashidun”.
**Answer: False** — At al-Qadisiyya in 636 Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas breaks Persia's army and Rustum falls; Ajnadayn and Yarmouk open the Levant; Nahavand in 642 is the "conquest of conquests", and Persia's last king dies in 31 AH.

**2. ** At al-Qadisiyya in 636 Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas breaks Persia's army and Rustum falls; Ajnadayn and Yarmouk open the Levant; Nahavand in 642 is the "conquest of conquests", and Persia's last king dies in 31 AH.
**Answer: True**
