# Islam Expands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Expansion under the Rashidun”?
a) At al-Qadisiyya in 636 Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas breaks Persia's army and Rustum falls; Ajnadayn and Yarmouk open the Levant; Nahavand in 642 is the "conquest of conquests", and Persia's last king dies in 31 AH.  b) In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united.  c) Cavour and the patriots stitch Italy's fragments into one kingdom, the same Italy that will later seize Libya.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) At al-Qadisiyya in 636 Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas breaks Persia's army and Rustum falls; Ajnadayn and Yarmouk open the Levant; Nahavand in 642 is the "conquest of conquests", and Persia's last king dies in 31 AH.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
