# Islam Expands

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islam Expands** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

At al-Qadisiyya in 636 Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas breaks Persia's army and Rustum falls; Ajnadayn and Yarmouk open the Levant; Nahavand in 642 is the "conquest of conquests", and Persia's last king dies in 31 AH. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
