# Arabia before Islam: Trade and Tribes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Arabs of the Hijaz before Islam”.
**Answer: False** — Quraysh run the winter and summer caravans under treaty, pilgrims crowd Mecca's fairs and poets compete at Ukaz, while the Hanifs — Quss, Umayya and Waraqa — already seek Abraham's one God.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** A peninsula of tribes, idols, poetry and caravan fairs, squeezed between the Persian and Roman empires and waiting for a unifying call.
**Answer: True**
