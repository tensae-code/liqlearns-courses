# Arabia before Islam: Trade and Tribes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Arabs of the Hijaz before Islam”?
a) Quraysh run the winter and summer caravans under treaty, pilgrims crowd Mecca's fairs and poets compete at Ukaz, while the Hanifs — Quss, Umayya and Waraqa — already seek Abraham's one God.  b) Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community.  c) Rustamids at Tahert, Idrisids in Morocco and Aghlabids in Ifriqiya break away from Baghdad and rule the Maghreb as their own states.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Quraysh run the winter and summer caravans under treaty, pilgrims crowd Mecca's fairs and poets compete at Ukaz, while the Hanifs — Quss, Umayya and Waraqa — already seek Abraham's one God.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Arabia before Islam”?
a) A peninsula of tribes, idols, poetry and caravan fairs, squeezed between the Persian and Roman empires and waiting for a unifying call.  b) Nabataeans carve Petra and tax the caravans, Palmyra grows rich between Rome and Persia, and Ghassanids and Lakhmids guard the desert frontiers for Byzantium and Persia.  c) Europe receives Islamic science and learning through Spain, Sicily and translation, and its own awakening follows.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A peninsula of tribes, idols, poetry and caravan fairs, squeezed between the Persian and Roman empires and waiting for a unifying call.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
