# Arabia before Islam: Trade and Tribes

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Arabia before Islam: Trade and Tribes** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Arabs of the Hijaz before Islam

Quraysh run the winter and summer caravans under treaty, pilgrims crowd Mecca's fairs and poets compete at Ukaz, while the Hanifs — Quss, Umayya and Waraqa — already seek Abraham's one God. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Arabia before Islam

A peninsula of tribes, idols, poetry and caravan fairs, squeezed between the Persian and Roman empires and waiting for a unifying call. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Arabs before Islam

Arabia's tribes, beliefs and markets on the eve of Islam, between the Persian and Roman worlds. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
