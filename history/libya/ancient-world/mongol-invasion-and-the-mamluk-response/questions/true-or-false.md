# Mongol Invasion and the Mamluk Response — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 4: the Mongol invasion and the Islamic response”.
**Answer: False** — Mongol armies sweep the east and sack Baghdad, until the Mamluks of Egypt stop them and the Islamic world survives its darkest blow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** After Baghdad's fall, Mongol Ilkhans rule Iraq while the rest of the Arab world lives under other masters until the Ottomans come.
**Answer: True**
