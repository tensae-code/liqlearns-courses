# Video script — South Arabian Kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Ma'in's merchants run the incense roads from about 1200 BCE; Saba settles at Marib about 800 BCE — Bilqis visits Solomon — and swallows Ma'in about 650 BCE; Abraha's elephants march in 571, Sayf ibn Dhi Yazan brings Persia in, and its last governor Badhan accepts Islam in 628. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Kings ruling by council, incense and gold, ships to India, the 20-storey Ghamdan palace, more than a hundred dams with Marib the greatest, and the 29 letters of the Musnad script. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
