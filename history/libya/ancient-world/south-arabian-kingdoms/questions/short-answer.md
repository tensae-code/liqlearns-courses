# South Arabian Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The ancient states of Yemen: Ma'in and Saba; Features of ancient Yemeni civilisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Features of ancient Yemeni civilisation”?
**Answer:** Kings ruling by council, incense and gold, ships to India, the 20-storey Ghamdan palace, more than a hundred dams with Marib the greatest, and the 29 letters of the Musnad script.
