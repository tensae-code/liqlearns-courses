# South Arabian Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The ancient states of Yemen: Ma'in and Saba”?
a) Ma'in's merchants run the incense roads from about 1200 BCE; Saba settles at Marib about 800 BCE — Bilqis visits Solomon — and swallows Ma'in about 650 BCE; Abraha's elephants march in 571, Sayf ibn Dhi Yazan brings Persia in, and its last governor Badhan accepts Islam in 628.  b) Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman.  c) Sultans govern the Arab provinces through appointed governors, tax farmers and local notables, and four centuries of Ottoman rule reshape the region's cities and countryside.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Ma'in's merchants run the incense roads from about 1200 BCE; Saba settles at Marib about 800 BCE — Bilqis visits Solomon — and swallows Ma'in about 650 BCE; Abraha's elephants march in 571, Sayf ibn Dhi Yazan brings Persia in, and its last governor Badhan accepts Islam in 628.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Kings ruling by council, incense and gold, ships to India, the 20-storey Ghamdan palace, more than a hundred dams with Marib the greatest, and the 29 letters of the Musnad script.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
