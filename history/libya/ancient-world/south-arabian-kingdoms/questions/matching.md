# South Arabian Kingdoms — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The ancient states of Yemen: Ma'in and Saba — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Features of ancient Yemeni civilisation — b)

Meanings:
a) Ma'in's merchants run the incense roads from about 1200 BCE; Saba settles at Marib about 800 BCE — Bilqis visits Solomon — and swallows Ma'in about 650 BCE; Abraha's elephants march in 571, Sayf ibn Dhi Yazan brings Persia in, and its last governor Badhan accepts Islam in 628.
b) Kings ruling by council, incense and gold, ships to India, the 20-storey Ghamdan palace, more than a hundred dams with Marib the greatest, and the 29 letters of the Musnad script.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
