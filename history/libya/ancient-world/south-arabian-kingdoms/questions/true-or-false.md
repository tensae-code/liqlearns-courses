# South Arabian Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The ancient states of Yemen: Ma'in and Saba”.
**Answer: False** — Ma'in's merchants run the incense roads from about 1200 BCE; Saba settles at Marib about 800 BCE — Bilqis visits Solomon — and swallows Ma'in about 650 BCE; Abraha's elephants march in 571, Sayf ibn Dhi Yazan brings Persia in, and its last governor Badhan accepts Islam in 628.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Kings ruling by council, incense and gold, ships to India, the 20-storey Ghamdan palace, more than a hundred dams with Marib the greatest, and the 29 letters of the Musnad script.
**Answer: True**
