# North Arabia: Nabataeans, Palmyra, Ghassanids, Lakhmids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (Arabian Peninsula): civilisation in the north”?
a) Nabataeans carve Petra and tax the caravans, Palmyra grows rich between Rome and Persia, and Ghassanids and Lakhmids guard the desert frontiers for Byzantium and Persia.  b) Arab Muslim missionaries and the Sufi orders, above all the Qadiriyya, carry Islam peacefully across the Sahara into West and Central Africa.  c) Kings ruling by council, incense and gold, ships to India, the 20-storey Ghamdan palace, more than a hundred dams with Marib the greatest, and the 29 letters of the Musnad script.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Nabataeans carve Petra and tax the caravans, Palmyra grows rich between Rome and Persia, and Ghassanids and Lakhmids guard the desert frontiers for Byzantium and Persia.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
