# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Romans”.
**Answer: False** — Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Rome takes the Three Cities in 104 BCE and Cyrenaica in 96 BCE; Libyans lose farms and labour while grain sails to Rome — yet Leptis-born Septimius Severus rises to emperor, honours his home city and builds the desert defence line.
**Answer: True**
