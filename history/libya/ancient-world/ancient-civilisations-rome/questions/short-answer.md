# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Romans; The Romans in Libya.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Romans in Libya”?
**Answer:** Rome takes the Three Cities in 104 BCE and Cyrenaica in 96 BCE; Libyans lose farms and labour while grain sails to Rome — yet Leptis-born Septimius Severus rises to emperor, honours his home city and builds the desert defence line.
