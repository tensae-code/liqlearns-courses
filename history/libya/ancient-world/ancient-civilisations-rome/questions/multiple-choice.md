# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Romans”?
a) Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman.  b) Aramaic becomes the East's common tongue and mother of the Arabic script; Nabataeans carve Petra and guard its water by law until Rome annexes them in 106 CE and the caravans move to Palmyra; Ghassanids and Lakhmids hold the desert edges.  c) The Phoenician cities of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos sail the whole Mediterranean, sell purple dye and cedar, and hand every later people their alphabet.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Romans in Libya”?
a) Rome takes the Three Cities in 104 BCE and Cyrenaica in 96 BCE; Libyans lose farms and labour while grain sails to Rome — yet Leptis-born Septimius Severus rises to emperor, honours his home city and builds the desert defence line.  b) The Almohad movement unites the Maghreb and Muslim Spain into one empire, the last great western Islamic state.  c) Weak caliphs, foreign guards who seize power, an over-wide state and failing revenues break the caliphate into pieces.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Rome takes the Three Cities in 104 BCE and Cyrenaica in 96 BCE; Libyans lose farms and labour while grain sails to Rome — yet Leptis-born Septimius Severus rises to emperor, honours his home city and builds the desert defence line.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
