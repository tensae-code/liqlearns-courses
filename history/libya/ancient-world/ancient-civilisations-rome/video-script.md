# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Rome takes the Three Cities in 104 BCE and Cyrenaica in 96 BCE; Libyans lose farms and labour while grain sails to Rome — yet Leptis-born Septimius Severus rises to emperor, honours his home city and builds the desert defence line. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
