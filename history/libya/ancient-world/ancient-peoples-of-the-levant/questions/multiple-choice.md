# Ancient Peoples of the Levant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “The ancient peoples of the Levant”?
a) Amorites build Mari on the Euphrates; Canaanites raise walled city-kingdoms — Jericho, Megiddo, Jerusalem — each with its own king, until Assyria and Babylon swallow them all.  b) The Almohad movement unites the Maghreb and Muslim Spain into one empire, the last great western Islamic state.  c) Uqba ibn Nafi founds Kairouan in 670 and the conquest runs west to the Atlantic under Musa ibn Nusayr; Tariq ibn Ziyad crosses in 711 and gives Gibraltar his name, while eastward the armies reach Sind and the borders of China.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Amorites build Mari on the Euphrates; Canaanites raise walled city-kingdoms — Jericho, Megiddo, Jerusalem — each with its own king, until Assyria and Babylon swallow them all.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
