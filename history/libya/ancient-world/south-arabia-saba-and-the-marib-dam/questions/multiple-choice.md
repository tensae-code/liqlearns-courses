# South Arabia: Saba and the Marib Dam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (Arabian Peninsula): civilisation in the south”?
a) Saba, Ma'in, Qataban and Himyar grow rich on frankincense, and the Marib Dam waters Yemen's terraces until its breaches undo the kingdoms.  b) Muawiya founds a hereditary caliphate at Damascus, builds its administration and fleets, fights Byzantium for a century, and the dynasty falls when its Arab tribal base splits.  c) Cheated of Tunisia in 1881, Italy wins Europe's consent, then creeps in peacefully — Banco di Roma branches in Tripoli and Benghazi, schools, clinics, doctors — before an ultimatum over the Edirne ship affair gives Istanbul 24 hours; war is declared on 29 September 1911.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Saba, Ma'in, Qataban and Himyar grow rich on frankincense, and the Marib Dam waters Yemen's terraces until its breaches undo the kingdoms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
