# The Arab World's Gifts to the World — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — one family of faiths from Babylon to Yemen — Marduk, Amun, Baal, Hadad, Ishtar in every land, the sun-moon-Venus triad, Osiris echoed in Adonis — and everywhere a belief in life after death — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
