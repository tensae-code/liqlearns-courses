# The Arab World's Gifts to the World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “What the ancient Arab civilisations shared”.
**Answer: False** — One family of faiths from Babylon to Yemen — Marduk, Amun, Baal, Hadad, Ishtar in every land, the sun-moon-Venus triad, Osiris echoed in Adonis — and everywhere a belief in life after death.

**2. ** One family of faiths from Babylon to Yemen — Marduk, Amun, Baal, Hadad, Ishtar in every land, the sun-moon-Venus triad, Osiris echoed in Adonis — and everywhere a belief in life after death.
**Answer: True**
