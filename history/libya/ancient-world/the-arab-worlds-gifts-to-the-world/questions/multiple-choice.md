Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “What the ancient Arab civilisations shared”?
a) One family of faiths from Babylon to Yemen — Marduk, Amun, Baal, Hadad, Ishtar in every land, the sun-moon-Venus triad, Osiris echoed in Adonis — and everywhere a belief in life after death.  b) Portuguese and Spanish sailors round Africa and cross the Atlantic, and the sea routes turn discovery straight into colonies and empire.  c) Libya becomes an Ottoman province in 1551; walis such as the Saqizlis raise mosques, schools and fortresses — though the era's later years slide into corruption and disorder as the empire weakens after Suleiman.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) One family of faiths from Babylon to Yemen — Marduk, Amun, Baal, Hadad, Ishtar in every land, the sun-moon-Venus triad, Osiris echoed in Adonis — and everywhere a belief in life after death.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
