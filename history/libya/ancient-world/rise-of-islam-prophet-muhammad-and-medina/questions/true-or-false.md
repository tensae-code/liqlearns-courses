# Rise of Islam: Prophet Muhammad and Medina — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Islamic call”.
**Answer: False** — Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and migrates to Medina, where the first Muslim community is born.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Medina's charter binds Muhajirun and Ansar; Badr, Uhud and the Trench defend the young state; Hudaybiyya's truce opens the road to Mecca's peaceful conquest.
**Answer: True**
