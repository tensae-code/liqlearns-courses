# Rise of Islam: Prophet Muhammad and Medina — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Islamic call”?
a) Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and migrates to Medina, where the first Muslim community is born.  b) The caliphate moves to Baghdad and the Islamic state takes its classical shape — viziers, diwans, judges and a world city of learning.  c) On 21 November 1949 the UN votes Libya independent by 1 January 1952; the Committee of Sixty writes a constitution of 213 articles and chooses a federal monarchy under King Idris; at ten in the morning on 24 December 1951 independence is proclaimed at al-Manar Palace in Benghazi.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and migrates to Medina, where the first Muslim community is born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Medina's charter binds Muhajirun and Ansar; Badr, Uhud and the Trench defend the young state; Hudaybiyya's truce opens the road to Mecca's peaceful conquest.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
