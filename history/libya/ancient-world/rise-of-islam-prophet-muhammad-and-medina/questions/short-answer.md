# Rise of Islam: Prophet Muhammad and Medina — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Islamic call; The formation of the Islamic state; The appearance of Islam.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The formation of the Islamic state”?
**Answer:** Medina's charter binds Muhajirun and Ansar; Badr, Uhud and the Trench defend the young state; Hudaybiyya's truce opens the road to Mecca's peaceful conquest.
