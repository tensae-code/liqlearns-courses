## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Islamic call** — Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and migrates to Medina, where the first Muslim community is born.
2. **The formation of the Islamic state** — Medina's charter binds Muhajirun and Ansar; Badr, Uhud and the Trench defend the young state; Hudaybiyya's truce opens the road to Mecca's peaceful conquest.
3. **The appearance of Islam** — Muhammad is born in the Year of the Elephant (571), orphaned, and raised by his grandfather then Abu Talib; he settles the Black Stone quarrel with his cloak and receives the first revelation at Hira at forty; the call goes public, followers are tortured, some flee to the Negus in Abyssinia — then comes the Hijra to Medina.
