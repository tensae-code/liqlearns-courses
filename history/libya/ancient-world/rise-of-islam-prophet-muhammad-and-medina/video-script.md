# Video script — Rise of Islam: Prophet Muhammad and Medina

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and migrates to Medina, where the first Muslim community is born. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Medina's charter binds Muhajirun and Ansar; Badr, Uhud and the Trench defend the young state; Hudaybiyya's truce opens the road to Mecca's peaceful conquest. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Muhammad is born in the Year of the Elephant (571), orphaned, and raised by his grandfather then Abu Talib; he settles the Black Stone quarrel with his cloak and receives the first revelation at Hira at forty; the call goes public, followers are tortured, some flee to the Negus in Abyssinia — then comes the Hijra to Medina. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
