**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “The perished Arabs: Ad, Thamud, Tasm and Jadis”?
a) Ad reject the prophet Hud and perish; Thamud carve palaces at Madain Salih and fall; a Yemeni king's army wipes out Jadis — Arabia's oldest cautionary tales.  b) Ubayd Allah al-Mahdi founds the Fatimid state in North Africa in 909 and builds Mahdia; al-Mu'izz makes Tripoli's port his naval base for the Sicily jihad, Jawhar takes Egypt in 969 and founds Cairo and al-Azhar — then the Banu Hilal invasion ruins Ifriqiya and the state fades away in 1171.  c) Tehenu, Temehu, Libu, Meshwesh and Nasamones face Egypt across the desert, while the Garamantes build Garama in Fezzan, drive war chariots, run the Sahara caravans to the Three Cities and defy Rome.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Ad reject the prophet Hud and perish; Thamud carve palaces at Madain Salih and fall; a Yemeni king's army wipes out Jadis — Arabia's oldest cautionary tales.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
