# The Lost Arabs: Ad, Thamud and Madain Salih — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The perished Arabs: Ad, Thamud, Tasm and Jadis”.
**Answer: False** — Ad reject the prophet Hud and perish; Thamud carve palaces at Madain Salih and fall; a Yemeni king's army wipes out Jadis — Arabia's oldest cautionary tales.

**2. ** Ad reject the prophet Hud and perish; Thamud carve palaces at Madain Salih and fall; a Yemeni king's army wipes out Jadis — Arabia's oldest cautionary tales.
**Answer: True**
