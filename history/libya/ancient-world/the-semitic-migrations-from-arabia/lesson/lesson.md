# The Semitic Migrations from Arabia

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Semitic Migrations from Arabia** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Arabia slowly dries, and from about 3500 BCE Semitic tribes wave out toward Palestine, Sinai, the Nile Delta and North Africa, mixing with the peoples they find. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
