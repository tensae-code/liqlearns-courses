# The Semitic Migrations from Arabia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “The Semitic migrations”?
a) Arabia slowly dries, and from about 3500 BCE Semitic tribes wave out toward Palestine, Sinai, the Nile Delta and North Africa, mixing with the peoples they find.  b) Britain promises Hussein an Arab kingdom for revolting against the Turks while secretly signing Sykes-Picot to divide the same lands with France.  c) Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and migrates to Medina, where the first Muslim community is born.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Arabia slowly dries, and from about 3500 BCE Semitic tribes wave out toward Palestine, Sinai, the Nile Delta and North Africa, mixing with the peoples they find.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
