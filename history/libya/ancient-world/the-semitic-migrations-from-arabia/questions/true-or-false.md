# The Semitic Migrations from Arabia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Semitic migrations”.
**Answer: False** — Arabia slowly dries, and from about 3500 BCE Semitic tribes wave out toward Palestine, Sinai, the Nile Delta and North Africa, mixing with the peoples they find.

**2. ** Arabia slowly dries, and from about 3500 BCE Semitic tribes wave out toward Palestine, Sinai, the Nile Delta and North Africa, mixing with the peoples they find.
**Answer: True**
