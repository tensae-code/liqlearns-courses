# The Crusades and the Muslim Response — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 4: the Islamic world's jihad against the Crusaders”.
**Answer: False** — Crusader states seize the Levant coast, then Zengi, Nur al-Din and Saladin reunite the Muslims and retake Jerusalem.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Saladin founds the Ayyubid state, unites Egypt and the Levant, and turns the Muslim world toward recovering Jerusalem.
**Answer: True**
