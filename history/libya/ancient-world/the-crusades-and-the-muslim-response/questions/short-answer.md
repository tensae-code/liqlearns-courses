# The Crusades and the Muslim Response — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 4: the Islamic world's jihad against the Crusaders; The Ayyubids; The Crusader invasion; Saladin and the power of unity; The Ayyubid state; The Crusades against the Arab homeland; The Mamluks expel the Crusaders.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Ayyubids”?
**Answer:** Saladin founds the Ayyubid state, unites Egypt and the Levant, and turns the Muslim world toward recovering Jerusalem.
