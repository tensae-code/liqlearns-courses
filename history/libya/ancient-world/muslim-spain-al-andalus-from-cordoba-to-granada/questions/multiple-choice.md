# Muslim Spain: Al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: al-Andalus”?
a) A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain.  b) Saladin founds the Ayyubid state, unites Egypt and the Levant, and turns the Muslim world toward recovering Jerusalem.  c) Fascist rule wars on the Libyan personality itself — gatherings and clubs banned, education strangled, Libya cut off from the Islamic world and its history distorted, fertile land seized by force for Italian settlers — and the resistance answers by moving abroad, to committees in Damascus, Egypt and the Levant.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: the fall of Granada and the expulsion of the Muslims from al-Andalus”?
a) In 1492 Granada surrenders, eight centuries of Muslim Spain end, and its people are expelled across the sea to North Africa.  b) The Ottomans enter the world war beside Germany and lose their Arab provinces and then the empire itself.  c) The war grows out of Franco-German hatred, the Moroccan crises and the shot at Sarajevo on 28 June 1914; when Italy joins the Allies in May 1915 it pulls its troops back, and at al-Qardabiya on 29 April 1915 a united Libyan force crushes its column — by 1916 Italy holds only Tripoli and Khums.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In 1492 Granada surrenders, eight centuries of Muslim Spain end, and its people are expelled across the sea to North Africa.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
