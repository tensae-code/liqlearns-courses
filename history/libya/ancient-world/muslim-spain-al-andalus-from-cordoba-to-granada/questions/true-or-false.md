# Muslim Spain: Al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 5: al-Andalus”.
**Answer: False** — A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** In 1492 Granada surrenders, eight centuries of Muslim Spain end, and its people are expelled across the sea to North Africa.
**Answer: True**
