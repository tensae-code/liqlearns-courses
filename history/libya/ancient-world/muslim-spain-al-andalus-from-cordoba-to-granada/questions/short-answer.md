# Muslim Spain: Al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 5: al-Andalus; Chapter 5: the fall of Granada and the expulsion of the Muslims from al-Andalus.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Chapter 5: the fall of Granada and the expulsion of the Muslims from al-Andalus”?
**Answer:** In 1492 Granada surrenders, eight centuries of Muslim Spain end, and its people are expelled across the sea to North Africa.
