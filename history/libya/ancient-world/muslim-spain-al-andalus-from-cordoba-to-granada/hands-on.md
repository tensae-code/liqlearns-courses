# Hands-on — Muslim Spain: Al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Muslim Spain: Al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
