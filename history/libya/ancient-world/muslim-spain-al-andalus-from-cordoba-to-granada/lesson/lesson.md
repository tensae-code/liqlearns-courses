# Muslim Spain: Al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Muslim Spain: Al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 5: al-Andalus

A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 5: the fall of Granada and the expulsion of the Muslims from al-Andalus

In 1492 Granada surrenders, eight centuries of Muslim Spain end, and its people are expelled across the sea to North Africa. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
