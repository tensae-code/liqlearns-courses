# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Rise and Expansion of Islam** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 1: the emergence of Islam, Mecca

Muhammad preaches first in secret then openly in Mecca, Quraysh persecute the believers, and the weakest find refuge with the just king of Abyssinia before the migration to Yathrib turns a persecuted band into a community. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 1: the Prophet's state in Medina

In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 2: the Rashidun caliphs

Abu Bakr holds Arabia together, Umar organises the conquering state, Uthman standardises the Qur'an, and Ali's caliphate ends in the first great split. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 4 — Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in the Levant, Iraq and Persia

Driven by faith and pressure at home, Muslim armies defeat both Persia and Byzantium, and the book asks why the two great empires fell so fast.
