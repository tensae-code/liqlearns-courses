# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 1: the emergence of Islam, Mecca
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Muhammad preaches first in secret then openly in Mecca, Quraysh persecute the believers, and the weakest find refuge with the just king of Abyssinia before the migration to Yathrib turns a persecuted band into a community — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 1: the Prophet's state in Medina
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 2: the Rashidun caliphs
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Abu Bakr holds Arabia together, Umar organises the conquering state, Uthman standardises the Qur'an, and Ali's caliphate ends in the first great split — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in the Levant, Iraq and Persia
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Driven by faith and pressure at home, Muslim armies defeat both Persia and Byzantium, and the book asks why the two great empires fell so fast — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Chapter 3: Islamic conquests in the Umayyad era
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Umayyad armies carry Islam to the Atlantic in the west and to Sindh and Transoxiana in the east, the widest reach of the early conquests — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
