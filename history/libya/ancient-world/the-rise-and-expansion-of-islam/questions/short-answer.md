# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 1: the emergence of Islam, Mecca; Chapter 1: the Prophet's state in Medina; Chapter 2: the Rashidun caliphs; Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in the Levant, Iraq and Persia; Chapter 3: Islamic conquests in the Umayyad era.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Chapter 1: the Prophet's state in Medina”?
**Answer:** In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united.
