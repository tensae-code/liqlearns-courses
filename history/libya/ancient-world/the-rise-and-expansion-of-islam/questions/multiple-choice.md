# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 1: the emergence of Islam, Mecca”?
a) Muhammad preaches first in secret then openly in Mecca, Quraysh persecute the believers, and the weakest find refuge with the just king of Abyssinia before the migration to Yathrib turns a persecuted band into a community.  b) Sumerians raise the first city-states between the Tigris and Euphrates, Akkad unites them, Babylon and then Assyria turn the valley into empires in turn.  c) The Fatimids rise in Ifriqiya, conquer Egypt and found Cairo, then leave the Zirids holding the Maghreb coast as their deputies.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Muhammad preaches first in secret then openly in Mecca, Quraysh persecute the believers, and the weakest find refuge with the just king of Abyssinia before the migration to Yathrib turns a persecuted band into a community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 1: the Prophet's state in Medina”?
a) In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united.  b) Uqba ibn Nafi founds Kairouan in 670 and the conquest runs west to the Atlantic under Musa ibn Nusayr; Tariq ibn Ziyad crosses in 711 and gives Gibraltar his name, while eastward the armies reach Sind and the borders of China.  c) Fascist Italy tears up the agreements in 1922 and Graziani grinds through the Western Mountain — Khalifa ibn Askar is hanged after a show trial — while in the Green Mountain Omar Mukhtar, Senussi sheikh since 1902 and veteran of the Chad fighting, takes command in 1923 and defeats the Italians at Bir Bilal and Marsa Brega.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
