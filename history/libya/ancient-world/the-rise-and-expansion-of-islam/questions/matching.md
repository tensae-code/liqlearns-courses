# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Chapter 1: the emergence of Islam, Mecca — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Chapter 1: the Prophet's state in Medina — b)

Meanings:
a) Muhammad preaches first in secret then openly in Mecca, Quraysh persecute the believers, and the weakest find refuge with the just king of Abyssinia before the migration to Yathrib turns a persecuted band into a community.
b) In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
