## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 1: the emergence of Islam, Mecca** — Muhammad preaches first in secret then openly in Mecca, Quraysh persecute the believers, and the weakest find refuge with the just king of Abyssinia before the migration to Yathrib turns a persecuted band into a community.
2. **Chapter 1: the Prophet's state in Medina** — In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united.
3. **Chapter 2: the Rashidun caliphs** — Abu Bakr holds Arabia together, Umar organises the conquering state, Uthman standardises the Qur'an, and Ali's caliphate ends in the first great split.
4. **Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in the Levant, Iraq and Persia** — Driven by faith and pressure at home, Muslim armies defeat both Persia and Byzantium, and the book asks why the two great empires fell so fast.
5. **Chapter 3: Islamic conquests in the Umayyad era** — Umayyad armies carry Islam to the Atlantic in the west and to Sindh and Transoxiana in the east, the widest reach of the early conquests.
