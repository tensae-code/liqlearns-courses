# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (Arabian Peninsula): features of Arabian civilisation”?
a) Incense and spice caravans cross Arabia from Yemen to the Levant, joining the Indian Ocean to the Mediterranean in one trade web.  b) Through Spain, Sicily and translation, Arabic science and philosophy reach Europe and help wake its Renaissance.  c) Spain seizes Tripoli in 1510 and hands it to the Knights of St John, who oppress it; the people appeal to Suleiman the Magnificent, and in August 1551 Sinan Pasha's fleet takes the city — Murad Agha becomes the first Ottoman wali and builds his mosque and school.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Incense and spice caravans cross Arabia from Yemen to the Levant, joining the Indian Ocean to the Mediterranean in one trade web.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
