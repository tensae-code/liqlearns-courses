## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 7: cultural life — sciences, literature, arts and architecture** — Scholars translate and then out-create the ancients in science and letters, and mosques, palaces and cities rise in a new Islamic art.
2. **Chapter 7: the civilisation centres** — Baghdad, Damascus, Cairo, Kairouan, Fez and Cordoba shine in turn as capitals of learning, each drawing scholars from the whole Islamic world.
3. **Origins and landmarks of Islamic civilisation** — Faith in knowledge drives translation, medicine, mathematics, astronomy and great cities from Baghdad to Cordoba.
4. **Islamic civilisation's effect on Europe** — Europe receives Islamic science and learning through Spain, Sicily and translation, and its own awakening follows.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
