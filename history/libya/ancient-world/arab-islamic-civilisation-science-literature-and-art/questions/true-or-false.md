# Arab-Islamic Civilisation: Science, Literature and Art — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 7: cultural life — sciences, literature, arts and architecture”.
**Answer: False** — Scholars translate and then out-create the ancients in science and letters, and mosques, palaces and cities rise in a new Islamic art.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Baghdad, Damascus, Cairo, Kairouan, Fez and Cordoba shine in turn as capitals of learning, each drawing scholars from the whole Islamic world.
**Answer: True**
