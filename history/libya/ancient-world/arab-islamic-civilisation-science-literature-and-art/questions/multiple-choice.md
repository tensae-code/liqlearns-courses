**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 7: cultural life — sciences, literature, arts and architecture”?
a) Scholars translate and then out-create the ancients in science and letters, and mosques, palaces and cities rise in a new Islamic art.  b) Elected kings checked by 300 elders and a court of 104 judges; ships to Egypt and Greece, caravans to Garama, the dam on Wadi Kaam, new crops and six-storey houses.  c) Caliph, minister, emir, diwan registers and the barid post knit a continent-wide state together, paid for by an organised treasury.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Scholars translate and then out-create the ancients in science and letters, and mosques, palaces and cities rise in a new Islamic art.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
