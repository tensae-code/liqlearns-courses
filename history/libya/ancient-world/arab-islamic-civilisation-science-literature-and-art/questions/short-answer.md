# Arab-Islamic Civilisation: Science, Literature and Art — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 7: cultural life — sciences, literature, arts and architecture; Chapter 7: the civilisation centres; Origins and landmarks of Islamic civilisation; Islamic civilisation's effect on Europe.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Chapter 7: the civilisation centres”?
**Answer:** Baghdad, Damascus, Cairo, Kairouan, Fez and Cordoba shine in turn as capitals of learning, each drawing scholars from the whole Islamic world.
