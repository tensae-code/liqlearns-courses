# Video script — Arab-Islamic Civilisation: Science, Literature and Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Scholars translate and then out-create the ancients in science and letters, and mosques, palaces and cities rise in a new Islamic art. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Baghdad, Damascus, Cairo, Kairouan, Fez and Cordoba shine in turn as capitals of learning, each drawing scholars from the whole Islamic world. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Faith in knowledge drives translation, medicine, mathematics, astronomy and great cities from Baghdad to Cordoba. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Europe receives Islamic science and learning through Spain, Sicily and translation, and its own awakening follows. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
