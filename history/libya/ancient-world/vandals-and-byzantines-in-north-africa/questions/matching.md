# Vandals and Byzantines in North Africa — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Vandals in Libya — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Byzantines in Libya — b)

Meanings:
a) Genseric's Vandals cross from Spain in 429, take Carthage in 439 and western Libya in 455, and tear down Rome's walls at Tripoli, Leptis and Sabratha — until Libyan tribes defeat them in the battles of 527–533.
b) Belisarius lands in 533 with 35,000 men and 600 ships, captures King Gelimer and restores North Africa to Justinian; forts rise along the coast manned by Libyan soldiers, but corrupt clergy and quarrelling sects hollow the empire out.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
