# Vandals and Byzantines in North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Vandals in Libya; The Byzantines in Libya.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Byzantines in Libya”?
**Answer:** Belisarius lands in 533 with 35,000 men and 600 ships, captures King Gelimer and restores North Africa to Justinian; forts rise along the coast manned by Libyan soldiers, but corrupt clergy and quarrelling sects hollow the empire out.
