# Umayyad Caliphate: From Damascus to Cordoba — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 3: the Umayyad state in the Levant”.
**Answer: False** — Muawiya founds a hereditary caliphate at Damascus, builds its administration and fleets, fights Byzantium for a century, and the dynasty falls when its Arab tribal base splits.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Muawiya turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship at Damascus; Karbala and Ibn al-Zubayr's revolt shake it; Abd al-Malik Arabises the diwans and strikes the first Islamic dinar, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz briefly restores the Rashidun way.
**Answer: True**
