# Umayyad Caliphate: From Damascus to Cordoba — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 3: the Umayyad state in the Levant; The Umayyad state; Expansion under the Umayyads; The Umayyad state and its expansion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Umayyad state”?
**Answer:** Muawiya turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship at Damascus; Karbala and Ibn al-Zubayr's revolt shake it; Abd al-Malik Arabises the diwans and strikes the first Islamic dinar, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz briefly restores the Rashidun way.
