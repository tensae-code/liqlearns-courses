## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 3: the Umayyad state in the Levant
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Muawiya founds a hereditary caliphate at Damascus, builds its administration and fleets, fights Byzantium for a century, and the dynasty falls when its Arab tribal base splits — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Umayyad state
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Muawiya turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship at Damascus; Karbala and Ibn al-Zubayr's revolt shake it; Abd al-Malik Arabises the diwans and strikes the first Islamic dinar, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz briefly restores the Rashidun way — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Expansion under the Umayyads
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sind is secured under al-Walid, armies cross the Oxus toward China, frontier forts face Byzantium in summer and winter campaigns — and Constantinople holds out, to fall only in 1453 — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Umayyad state and its expansion
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Uqba ibn Nafi founds Kairouan in 670 and the conquest runs west to the Atlantic under Musa ibn Nusayr; Tariq ibn Ziyad crosses in 711 and gives Gibraltar his name, while eastward the armies reach Sind and the borders of China — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
