## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 3: the Umayyad state in the Levant** — Muawiya founds a hereditary caliphate at Damascus, builds its administration and fleets, fights Byzantium for a century, and the dynasty falls when its Arab tribal base splits.
2. **The Umayyad state** — Muawiya turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship at Damascus; Karbala and Ibn al-Zubayr's revolt shake it; Abd al-Malik Arabises the diwans and strikes the first Islamic dinar, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz briefly restores the Rashidun way.
3. **Expansion under the Umayyads** — Sind is secured under al-Walid, armies cross the Oxus toward China, frontier forts face Byzantium in summer and winter campaigns — and Constantinople holds out, to fall only in 1453.
4. **The Umayyad state and its expansion** — Uqba ibn Nafi founds Kairouan in 670 and the conquest runs west to the Atlantic under Musa ibn Nusayr; Tariq ibn Ziyad crosses in 711 and gives Gibraltar his name, while eastward the armies reach Sind and the borders of China.
