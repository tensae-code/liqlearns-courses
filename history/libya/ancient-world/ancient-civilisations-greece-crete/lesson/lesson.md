# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Greeks

Alexander the Great sweeps through the Levant and Egypt in the 330s BCE and Greek cities and kings follow in his wake. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Greeks and the Five Cities of Cyrenaica

Drought and crowding push the Greeks across the sea; in the Green Mountain they found Cyrene, Apollonia, Barca, Euesperides and Taucheira, grow rich on silphium, fix borders with the Phoenicians, and pass under Alexander's heirs to Rome in 96 BCE. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
