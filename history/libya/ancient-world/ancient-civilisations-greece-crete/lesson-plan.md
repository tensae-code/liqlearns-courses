## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Greeks** — Alexander the Great sweeps through the Levant and Egypt in the 330s BCE and Greek cities and kings follow in his wake.
2. **The Greeks and the Five Cities of Cyrenaica** — Drought and crowding push the Greeks across the sea; in the Green Mountain they found Cyrene, Apollonia, Barca, Euesperides and Taucheira, grow rich on silphium, fix borders with the Phoenicians, and pass under Alexander's heirs to Rome in 96 BCE.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
