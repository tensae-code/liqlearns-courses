# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Greeks; The Greeks and the Five Cities of Cyrenaica.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Greeks and the Five Cities of Cyrenaica”?
**Answer:** Drought and crowding push the Greeks across the sea; in the Green Mountain they found Cyrene, Apollonia, Barca, Euesperides and Taucheira, grow rich on silphium, fix borders with the Phoenicians, and pass under Alexander's heirs to Rome in 96 BCE.
