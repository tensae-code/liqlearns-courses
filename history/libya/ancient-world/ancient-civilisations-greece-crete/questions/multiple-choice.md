# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Greeks”?
a) Alexander the Great sweeps through the Levant and Egypt in the 330s BCE and Greek cities and kings follow in his wake.  b) Britain promises Hussein an Arab kingdom for revolting against the Turks while secretly signing Sykes-Picot to divide the same lands with France.  c) The United Kingdom of Libya builds its institutions — a parliament elected in February 1952, a Senate of twenty-four — and takes its seat in the Arab League and the United Nations.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Alexander the Great sweeps through the Levant and Egypt in the 330s BCE and Greek cities and kings follow in his wake.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Drought and crowding push the Greeks across the sea; in the Green Mountain they found Cyrene, Apollonia, Barca, Euesperides and Taucheira, grow rich on silphium, fix borders with the Phoenicians, and pass under Alexander's heirs to Rome in 96 BCE.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
