# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit: attempts to control the Arab world — the Greeks”.
**Answer: False** — Alexander the Great sweeps through the Levant and Egypt in the 330s BCE and Greek cities and kings follow in his wake.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Drought and crowding push the Greeks across the sea; in the Green Mountain they found Cyrene, Apollonia, Barca, Euesperides and Taucheira, grow rich on silphium, fix borders with the Phoenicians, and pass under Alexander's heirs to Rome in 96 BCE.
**Answer: True**
