# Pre-Islam Arab States of the North — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Aramaeans and the Arab states before Islam”.
**Answer: False** — Aramaic becomes the East's common tongue and mother of the Arabic script; Nabataeans carve Petra and guard its water by law until Rome annexes them in 106 CE and the caravans move to Palmyra; Ghassanids and Lakhmids hold the desert edges.

**2. ** Aramaic becomes the East's common tongue and mother of the Arabic script; Nabataeans carve Petra and guard its water by law until Rome annexes them in 106 CE and the caravans move to Palmyra; Ghassanids and Lakhmids hold the desert edges.
**Answer: True**
