# Pre-Islam Arab States of the North

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Pre-Islam Arab States of the North** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Aramaic becomes the East's common tongue and mother of the Arabic script; Nabataeans carve Petra and guard its water by law until Rome annexes them in 106 CE and the caravans move to Palmyra; Ghassanids and Lakhmids hold the desert edges. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
