# Pre-Islam Arab States of the North — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Aramaic becomes the East's common tongue and mother of the Arabic script; Nabataeans carve Petra and guard its water by law until Rome annexes them in 106 CE and the caravans move to Palmyra; Ghassanids and Lakhmids hold the desert edges — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
