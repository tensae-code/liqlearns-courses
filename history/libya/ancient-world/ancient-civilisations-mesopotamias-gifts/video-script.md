# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Scribes press cuneiform into clay, Hammurabi carves his laws, Gudea of Lagash builds and prays in diorite stone, and the valley gives writing, law and the wheel to the world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Kings ruling with councils of elders, siege engines and iron, Hammurabi's carved laws, irrigation canals — and cuneiform, the wedge writing pressed into clay. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
