# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit: features of Mesopotamian civilisation”.
**Answer: False** — Scribes press cuneiform into clay, Hammurabi carves his laws, Gudea of Lagash builds and prays in diorite stone, and the valley gives writing, law and the wheel to the world.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Kings ruling with councils of elders, siege engines and iron, Hammurabi's carved laws, irrigation canals — and cuneiform, the wedge writing pressed into clay.
**Answer: True**
