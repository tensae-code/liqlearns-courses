# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit: features of Mesopotamian civilisation”?
a) Scribes press cuneiform into clay, Hammurabi carves his laws, Gudea of Lagash builds and prays in diorite stone, and the valley gives writing, law and the wheel to the world.  b) Muawiya turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship at Damascus; Karbala and Ibn al-Zubayr's revolt shake it; Abd al-Malik Arabises the diwans and strikes the first Islamic dinar, and Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz briefly restores the Rashidun way.  c) The fleet shells Tripoli on 3 October 1911 against barely 7,000 Ottoman troops, and the garrison refuses Istanbul's surrender order; at Ouchy on 18 October 1912 the Sultan withdraws — but his firman grants Libyans self-rule, so Libyans reject the treaty and fight on: Derna's Friday battle in May 1913, Janduba, and Mahruqa in Fezzan.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Scribes press cuneiform into clay, Hammurabi carves his laws, Gudea of Lagash builds and prays in diorite stone, and the valley gives writing, law and the wheel to the world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Kings ruling with councils of elders, siege engines and iron, Hammurabi's carved laws, irrigation canals — and cuneiform, the wedge writing pressed into clay.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
