# Abbasid Caliphate: Baghdad Golden Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 4: the rise of the Abbasid state”.
**Answer: False** — A secret then open call topples the Umayyads, al-Mansur builds Baghdad and becomes the state's true founder, and the caliphate turns eastward and patronises civilisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Weak caliphs, foreign guards who seize power, an over-wide state and failing revenues break the caliphate into pieces.
**Answer: True**
