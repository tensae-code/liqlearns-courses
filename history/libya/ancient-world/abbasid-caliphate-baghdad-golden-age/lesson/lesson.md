# Abbasid Caliphate: Baghdad Golden Age

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Abbasid Caliphate: Baghdad Golden Age** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 4: the rise of the Abbasid state

A secret then open call topples the Umayyads, al-Mansur builds Baghdad and becomes the state's true founder, and the caliphate turns eastward and patronises civilisation. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 4: the second Abbasid era, retreat and collapse

Weak caliphs, foreign guards who seize power, an over-wide state and failing revenues break the caliphate into pieces. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Abbasid state and its first age

The caliphate moves to Baghdad and the Islamic state takes its classical shape — viziers, diwans, judges and a world city of learning. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Abbasid state

The Abbasids overthrow the Umayyads in 750; al-Mansur builds Baghdad, and under Harun al-Rashid the city fills with translated books and libraries. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
