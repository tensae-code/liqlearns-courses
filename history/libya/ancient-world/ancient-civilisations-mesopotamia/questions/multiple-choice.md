# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit: the eras of ancient civilisation in Mesopotamia”?
a) Sumerians raise the first city-states between the Tigris and Euphrates, Akkad unites them, Babylon and then Assyria turn the valley into empires in turn.  b) Old Stone Age hunters learn fire, settle by water, farm and grind grain, until pottery, copper and the first villages end the long prehistory.  c) Europe's kings march for Jerusalem and the trade roads while Baghdad is weak and Egypt decays; Crusader kingdoms plant themselves at Jerusalem and Tripoli — until Saladin unites the Muslims and crushes them at Hattin in 1187, freeing Jerusalem.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Sumerians raise the first city-states between the Tigris and Euphrates, Akkad unites them, Babylon and then Assyria turn the valley into empires in turn.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “The peoples of Mesopotamia”?
a) Quarrelling Sumerian cities weaken; Sargon of Akkad builds the first empire; Assyria rises and falls in 612 BCE; Nebuchadnezzar's Babylon destroys the Temple in Jerusalem before Cyrus takes it in 539 BCE.  b) One faith and one state unite the Arab tribes, and a new era of Muslim history begins.  c) A broken Europe arms, dictators rise, and the settlement of the first war prepares the second.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Quarrelling Sumerian cities weaken; Sargon of Akkad builds the first empire; Assyria rises and falls in 612 BCE; Nebuchadnezzar's Babylon destroys the Temple in Jerusalem before Cyrus takes it in 539 BCE.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
