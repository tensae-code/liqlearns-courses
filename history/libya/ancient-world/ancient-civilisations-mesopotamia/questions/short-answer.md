# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Unit: the eras of ancient civilisation in Mesopotamia; The peoples of Mesopotamia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The peoples of Mesopotamia”?
**Answer:** Quarrelling Sumerian cities weaken; Sargon of Akkad builds the first empire; Assyria rises and falls in 612 BCE; Nebuchadnezzar's Babylon destroys the Temple in Jerusalem before Cyrus takes it in 539 BCE.
