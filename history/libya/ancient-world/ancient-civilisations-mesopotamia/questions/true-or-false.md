# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit: the eras of ancient civilisation in Mesopotamia”.
**Answer: False** — Sumerians raise the first city-states between the Tigris and Euphrates, Akkad unites them, Babylon and then Assyria turn the valley into empires in turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Quarrelling Sumerian cities weaken; Sargon of Akkad builds the first empire; Assyria rises and falls in 612 BCE; Nebuchadnezzar's Babylon destroys the Temple in Jerusalem before Cyrus takes it in 539 BCE.
**Answer: True**
