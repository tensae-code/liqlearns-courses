# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit: the eras of ancient civilisation in Mesopotamia

Sumerians raise the first city-states between the Tigris and Euphrates, Akkad unites them, Babylon and then Assyria turn the valley into empires in turn. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — The peoples of Mesopotamia

Quarrelling Sumerian cities weaken; Sargon of Akkad builds the first empire; Assyria rises and falls in 612 BCE; Nebuchadnezzar's Babylon destroys the Temple in Jerusalem before Cyrus takes it in 539 BCE. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
