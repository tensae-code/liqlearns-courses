# Spain and the Knights in Tripoli

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Spain and the Knights in Tripoli** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
