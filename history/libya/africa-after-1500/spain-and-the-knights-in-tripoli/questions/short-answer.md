# Spain and the Knights in Tripoli — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.
