# Spain and the Knights in Tripoli — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Spanish and the Knights of St John”.
**Answer: False** — After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.

**2. ** After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.
**Answer: True**
