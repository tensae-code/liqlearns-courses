# Spain and the Knights in Tripoli — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “The Spanish and the Knights of St John”?
a) After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.  b) Quraysh run the winter and summer caravans under treaty, pilgrims crowd Mecca's fairs and poets compete at Ukaz, while the Hanifs — Quss, Umayya and Waraqa — already seek Abraham's one God.  c) Carthaginian farmers write farming treatises, its ports and fleets run the sea trade, and Libyan, Punic and Greek cities share one coast.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
