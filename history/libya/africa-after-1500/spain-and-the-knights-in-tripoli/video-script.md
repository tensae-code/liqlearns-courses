# Video script — Spain and the Knights in Tripoli

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
