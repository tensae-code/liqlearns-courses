# Decolonisation: North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: the national movements — the Maghreb peoples”?
a) Tunisians, Algerians, Moroccans and Mauritanians organise, negotiate and fight against France, Spain and Italy until the flags change.  b) The Zionist movement seeks a national home in Palestine and survives by riding European colonial power, above all Britain's mandate.  c) Sumerians raise the first city-states between the Tigris and Euphrates, Akkad unites them, Babylon and then Assyria turn the valley into empires in turn.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Tunisians, Algerians, Moroccans and Mauritanians organise, negotiate and fight against France, Spain and Italy until the flags change.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
