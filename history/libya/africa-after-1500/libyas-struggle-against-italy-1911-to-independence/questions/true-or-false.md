# Libya's Struggle against Italy, 1911 to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 5: the national movements in the Arab world — Libya”.
**Answer: False** — From the first battles of 1911 at Abu Meliana and al-Shatt, Libyans resist Italy for twenty years through desert war and Italian detention camps until independence comes after the second war.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Cheated of Tunisia in 1881, Italy wins Europe's consent, then creeps in peacefully — Banco di Roma branches in Tripoli and Benghazi, schools, clinics, doctors — before an ultimatum over the Edirne ship affair gives Istanbul 24 hours; war is declared on 29 September 1911.
**Answer: True**
