**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: the national movements — Sudan and the Mashriq peoples”?
a) Syria resists the French mandate by arms and politics, Sudan's movement grows through the 1922–24 crisis years, and each Arab people finds its own road out of foreign rule.  b) Baybars rebuilds the army and the fleet and takes Jaffa and Antioch; the pressure never lets up until the last Crusaders are expelled in 1291 — a danger that had lasted since 1097.  c) Britain takes Palestine in the war, the Balfour promise opens it to Zionist settlement, and Palestinian Arabs organise a national movement against both.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Syria resists the French mandate by arms and politics, Sudan's movement grows through the 1922–24 crisis years, and each Arab people finds its own road out of foreign rule.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
