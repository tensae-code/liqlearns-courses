# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Syria resists the French mandate by arms and politics, Sudan's movement grows through the 1922–24 crisis years, and each Arab people finds its own road out of foreign rule. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
