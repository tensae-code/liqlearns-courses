# Libya under the Ottomans and Karamanlis — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Ottomans come to Libya”.
**Answer: False** — Spain seizes Tripoli in 1510 and hands it to the Knights of St John, who oppress it; the people appeal to Suleiman the Magnificent, and in August 1551 Sinan Pasha's fleet takes the city — Murad Agha becomes the first Ottoman wali and builds his mosque and school.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Libya joins a great Islamic empire; Andalusian refugees fleeing Spain settle and rebuild Derna, and by the mid-1600s Ottoman authority reaches Benghazi, Awjila and southern Cyrenaica.
**Answer: True**
