# Libya under the Ottomans and Karamanlis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Ottomans come to Libya”?
a) Spain seizes Tripoli in 1510 and hands it to the Knights of St John, who oppress it; the people appeal to Suleiman the Magnificent, and in August 1551 Sinan Pasha's fleet takes the city — Murad Agha becomes the first Ottoman wali and builds his mosque and school.  b) Muhammad is born in the Year of the Elephant (571), orphaned, and raised by his grandfather then Abu Talib; he settles the Black Stone quarrel with his cloak and receives the first revelation at Hira at forty; the call goes public, followers are tortured, some flee to the Negus in Abyssinia — then comes the Hijra to Medina.  c) One faith and one state unite the Arab tribes, and a new era of Muslim history begins.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Spain seizes Tripoli in 1510 and hands it to the Knights of St John, who oppress it; the people appeal to Suleiman the Magnificent, and in August 1551 Sinan Pasha's fleet takes the city — Murad Agha becomes the first Ottoman wali and builds his mosque and school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
