# Libya under the Ottomans and Karamanlis — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Ottomans come to Libya — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The first Ottoman era, 1551–1711 — b)

Meanings:
a) Spain seizes Tripoli in 1510 and hands it to the Knights of St John, who oppress it; the people appeal to Suleiman the Magnificent, and in August 1551 Sinan Pasha's fleet takes the city — Murad Agha becomes the first Ottoman wali and builds his mosque and school.
b) Libya joins a great Islamic empire; Andalusian refugees fleeing Spain settle and rebuild Derna, and by the mid-1600s Ottoman authority reaches Benghazi, Awjila and southern Cyrenaica.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
