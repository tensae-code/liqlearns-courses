# The Renaissance: What It Was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 7: the effect of Arab-Islamic civilisation on the European Renaissance”.
**Answer: False** — Through Spain, Sicily and translation, Arabic science and philosophy reach Europe and help wake its Renaissance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Europe wakes from its medieval sleep, revives learning and art, and becomes the power that will soon press on the Arab world.
**Answer: True**
