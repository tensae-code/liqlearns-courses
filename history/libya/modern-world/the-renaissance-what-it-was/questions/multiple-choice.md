# The Renaissance: What It Was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 7: the effect of Arab-Islamic civilisation on the European Renaissance”?
a) Through Spain, Sicily and translation, Arabic science and philosophy reach Europe and help wake its Renaissance.  b) Old Stone Age hunters learn fire, settle by water, farm and grind grain, until pottery, copper and the first villages end the long prehistory.  c) Saladin's victory shows the book's moral — divided, the Muslims fell; united under one leadership, they defeat the West.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Through Spain, Sicily and translation, Arabic science and philosophy reach Europe and help wake its Renaissance.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 3: the Renaissance and the making of modern Europe”?
a) Europe wakes from its medieval sleep, revives learning and art, and becomes the power that will soon press on the Arab world.  b) Umayyad armies carry Islam to the Atlantic in the west and to Sindh and Transoxiana in the east, the widest reach of the early conquests.  c) A broken Europe arms, dictators rise, and the settlement of the first war prepares the second.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Europe wakes from its medieval sleep, revives learning and art, and becomes the power that will soon press on the Arab world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
