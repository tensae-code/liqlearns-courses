# The Renaissance: What It Was

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Renaissance: What It Was** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 7: the effect of Arab-Islamic civilisation on the European Renaissance

Through Spain, Sicily and translation, Arabic science and philosophy reach Europe and help wake its Renaissance. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 3: the Renaissance and the making of modern Europe

Europe wakes from its medieval sleep, revives learning and art, and becomes the power that will soon press on the Arab world. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
