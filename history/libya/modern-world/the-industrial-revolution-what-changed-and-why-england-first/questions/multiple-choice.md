**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 3: the Industrial Revolution and its effect on European colonial expansion”?
a) Factories hunger for raw materials and markets, and industrial Europe turns its machines outward into a new scramble for colonies.  b) Persia swallows Egypt and the Levant into its empire and Arab lands become provinces of an empire run from the east.  c) Europe wakes from its medieval sleep, revives learning and art, and becomes the power that will soon press on the Arab world.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Factories hunger for raw materials and markets, and industrial Europe turns its machines outward into a new scramble for colonies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
