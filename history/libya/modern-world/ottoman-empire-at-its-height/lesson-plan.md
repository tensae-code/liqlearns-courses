# Lesson plan — Ottoman Empire at Its Height

**Course:** History — Libya · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Ottoman Empire at Its Height
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Ottoman Libya and the Karamanlis (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Arab world before the Ottomans** — Baghdad's fall in 1258 ends the Abbasid age; Mamluk Egypt weakens as Portuguese ships round the Cape in 1498 and strangle its trade; Safavid Iran rises — and the Ottomans, fresh from Constantinople in 1453 and Chaldiran in 1514, turn toward the Arab lands.
2. **The rise and expansion of the Ottoman state** — Turkish tribes flee the Mongol storm westward; Osman founds the state at Bursa; Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, and the empire turns toward the Arab lands.
