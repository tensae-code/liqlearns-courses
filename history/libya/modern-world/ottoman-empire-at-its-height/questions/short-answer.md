# Ottoman Empire at Its Height — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Arab world before the Ottomans; The rise and expansion of the Ottoman state.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The rise and expansion of the Ottoman state”?
**Answer:** Turkish tribes flee the Mongol storm westward; Osman founds the state at Bursa; Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, and the empire turns toward the Arab lands.
