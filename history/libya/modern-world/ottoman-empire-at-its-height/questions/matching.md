# Ottoman Empire at Its Height — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Arab world before the Ottomans — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The rise and expansion of the Ottoman state — b)

Meanings:
a) Baghdad's fall in 1258 ends the Abbasid age; Mamluk Egypt weakens as Portuguese ships round the Cape in 1498 and strangle its trade; Safavid Iran rises — and the Ottomans, fresh from Constantinople in 1453 and Chaldiran in 1514, turn toward the Arab lands.
b) Turkish tribes flee the Mongol storm westward; Osman founds the state at Bursa; Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, and the empire turns toward the Arab lands.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
