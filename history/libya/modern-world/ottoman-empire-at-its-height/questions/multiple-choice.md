# Ottoman Empire at Its Height — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Arab world before the Ottomans”?
a) Baghdad's fall in 1258 ends the Abbasid age; Mamluk Egypt weakens as Portuguese ships round the Cape in 1498 and strangle its trade; Safavid Iran rises — and the Ottomans, fresh from Constantinople in 1453 and Chaldiran in 1514, turn toward the Arab lands.  b) Tyrian settlers found Carthage on the Tunisian coast and the trading city grows into the western Mediterranean's sea power.  c) Arab traders and settlers carry Islam down the East African coast and the Swahili cities grow on the monsoon trade.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Baghdad's fall in 1258 ends the Abbasid age; Mamluk Egypt weakens as Portuguese ships round the Cape in 1498 and strangle its trade; Safavid Iran rises — and the Ottomans, fresh from Constantinople in 1453 and Chaldiran in 1514, turn toward the Arab lands.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “The rise and expansion of the Ottoman state”?
a) Turkish tribes flee the Mongol storm westward; Osman founds the state at Bursa; Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, and the empire turns toward the Arab lands.  b) Europe's kings march for Jerusalem and the trade roads while Baghdad is weak and Egypt decays; Crusader kingdoms plant themselves at Jerusalem and Tripoli — until Saladin unites the Muslims and crushes them at Hattin in 1187, freeing Jerusalem.  c) Tunisians, Algerians, Moroccans and Mauritanians organise, negotiate and fight against France, Spain and Italy until the flags change.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Turkish tribes flee the Mongol storm westward; Osman founds the state at Bursa; Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, and the empire turns toward the Arab lands.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
