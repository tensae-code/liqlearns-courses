# Video script — Ottoman Empire at Its Height

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Baghdad's fall in 1258 ends the Abbasid age; Mamluk Egypt weakens as Portuguese ships round the Cape in 1498 and strangle its trade; Safavid Iran rises — and the Ottomans, fresh from Constantinople in 1453 and Chaldiran in 1514, turn toward the Arab lands. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Turkish tribes flee the Mongol storm westward; Osman founds the state at Bursa; Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, and the empire turns toward the Arab lands. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
