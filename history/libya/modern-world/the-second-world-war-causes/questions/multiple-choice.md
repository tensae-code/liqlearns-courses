Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: the Second World War and its reflections on the Arab world”?
a) The second war is fought partly on Arab soil, above all in North Africa, and leaves the colonial powers weakened.  b) Rome takes the Three Cities in 104 BCE and Cyrenaica in 96 BCE; Libyans lose farms and labour while grain sails to Rome — yet Leptis-born Septimius Severus rises to emperor, honours his home city and builds the desert defence line.  c) Military defeats, capitulations and corrupt tax farming hollow the empire out, and Arab provinces begin to push against Istanbul's rule.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The second war is fought partly on Arab soil, above all in North Africa, and leaves the colonial powers weakened.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
