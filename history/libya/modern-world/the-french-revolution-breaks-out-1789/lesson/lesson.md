# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

England's revolution, France's 1789 revolution and Italy's unification teach Europe's peoples that nations should rule themselves — an idea that will travel to the Arab world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
