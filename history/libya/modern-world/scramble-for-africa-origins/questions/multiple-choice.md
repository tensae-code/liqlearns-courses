# Scramble for Africa: Origins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Modern European colonialism”?
a) Africa's forests, unnavigable rivers and diseases long delay the colonisers, but conferences carve the continent between them — by 1936 France, Britain, Belgium, Portugal and Italy hold it, driven by wealth, markets, surplus people and rivalry.  b) Tunisians, Algerians, Moroccans and Mauritanians organise, negotiate and fight against France, Spain and Italy until the flags change.  c) Great rivers, fertile land, climate and security gather people into the first civilisations, and the Arab world's valleys are where it happens first.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Africa's forests, unnavigable rivers and diseases long delay the colonisers, but conferences carve the continent between them — by 1936 France, Britain, Belgium, Portugal and Italy hold it, driven by wealth, markets, surplus people and rivalry.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
