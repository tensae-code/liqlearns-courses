# Scramble for Africa: Origins

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Scramble for Africa: Origins** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Africa's forests, unnavigable rivers and diseases long delay the colonisers, but conferences carve the continent between them — by 1936 France, Britain, Belgium, Portugal and Italy hold it, driven by wealth, markets, surplus people and rivalry. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
