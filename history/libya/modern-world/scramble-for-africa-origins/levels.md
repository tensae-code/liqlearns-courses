# Scramble for Africa: Origins — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Africa's forests, unnavigable rivers and diseases long delay the colonisers, but conferences carve the continent between them — by 1936 France, Britain, Belgium, Portugal and Italy hold it, driven by wealth, markets, surplus people and rivalry — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
