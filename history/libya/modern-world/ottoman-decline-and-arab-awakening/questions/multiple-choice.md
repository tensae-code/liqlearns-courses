# Ottoman Decline and Arab Awakening — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 2: factors of Ottoman weakness and the movements against Ottoman rule”?
a) Military defeats, capitulations and corrupt tax farming hollow the empire out, and Arab provinces begin to push against Istanbul's rule.  b) Belisarius lands in 533 with 35,000 men and 600 ships, captures King Gelimer and restores North Africa to Justinian; forts rise along the coast manned by Libyan soldiers, but corrupt clergy and quarrelling sects hollow the empire out.  c) Libyan, Kushite, Persian, Greek and Roman rulers take Egypt in turn, and the pharaonic story fades under foreign crowns.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Military defeats, capitulations and corrupt tax farming hollow the empire out, and Arab provinces begin to push against Istanbul's rule.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
