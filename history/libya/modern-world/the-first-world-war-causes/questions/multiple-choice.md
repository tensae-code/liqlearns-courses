# The First World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 4: the First World War and its effect on the Ottoman state”?
a) The Ottomans enter the world war beside Germany and lose their Arab provinces and then the empire itself.  b) Europe wakes from its medieval sleep, revives learning and art, and becomes the power that will soon press on the Arab world.  c) The Kouloughli officer Ahmad Karamanli takes Tripoli in 1711 with the people's backing and the Sultan confirms him as Pasha in 1714; over 34 years he unifies the country to Fezzan, rebuilds the fleet and shipyard, secures the caravan roads and patronises Ibn Ghalbun, who writes Libya's history.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Ottomans enter the world war beside Germany and lose their Arab provinces and then the empire itself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
