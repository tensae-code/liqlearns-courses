# World War I: Alliances and Outbreak — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The First World War and the Libyan jihad”.
**Answer: False** — The war grows out of Franco-German hatred, the Moroccan crises and the shot at Sarajevo on 28 June 1914; when Italy joins the Allies in May 1915 it pulls its troops back, and at al-Qardabiya on 29 April 1915 a united Libyan force crushes its column — by 1916 Italy holds only Tripoli and Khums.

**2. ** The war grows out of Franco-German hatred, the Moroccan crises and the shot at Sarajevo on 28 June 1914; when Italy joins the Allies in May 1915 it pulls its troops back, and at al-Qardabiya on 29 April 1915 a united Libyan force crushes its column — by 1916 Italy holds only Tripoli and Khums.
