# World War I: Alliances and Outbreak

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **World War I: Alliances and Outbreak** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The war grows out of Franco-German hatred, the Moroccan crises and the shot at Sarajevo on 28 June 1914; when Italy joins the Allies in May 1915 it pulls its troops back, and at al-Qardabiya on 29 April 1915 a united Libyan force crushes its column — by 1916 Italy holds only Tripoli and Khums. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
