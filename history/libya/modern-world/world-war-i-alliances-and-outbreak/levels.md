# World War I: Alliances and Outbreak — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the war grows out of Franco-German hatred, the Moroccan crises and the shot at Sarajevo on 28 June 1914; when Italy joins the Allies in May 1915 it pulls its troops back, and at al-Qardabiya on 29 April 1915 a united Libyan force crushes its column — by 1916 Italy holds only Tripoli and Khums — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
