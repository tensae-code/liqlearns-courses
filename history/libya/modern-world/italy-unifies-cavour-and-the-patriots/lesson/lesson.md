# Italy Unifies: Cavour and the Patriots

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Italy Unifies: Cavour and the Patriots** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Cavour and the patriots stitch Italy's fragments into one kingdom, the same Italy that will later seize Libya. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
