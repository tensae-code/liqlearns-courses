# The First World War: Versailles and the Results

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The First World War: Versailles and the Results** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Britain promises Hussein an Arab kingdom for revolting against the Turks while secretly signing Sykes-Picot to divide the same lands with France. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
