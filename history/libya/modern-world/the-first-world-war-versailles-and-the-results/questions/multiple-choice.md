Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 4: Sharif Hussein, Britain, and the Sykes-Picot agreement of 1916”?
a) Britain promises Hussein an Arab kingdom for revolting against the Turks while secretly signing Sykes-Picot to divide the same lands with France.  b) Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman.  c) Sultans govern the Arab provinces through appointed governors, tax farmers and local notables, and four centuries of Ottoman rule reshape the region's cities and countryside.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Britain promises Hussein an Arab kingdom for revolting against the Turks while secretly signing Sykes-Picot to divide the same lands with France.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
