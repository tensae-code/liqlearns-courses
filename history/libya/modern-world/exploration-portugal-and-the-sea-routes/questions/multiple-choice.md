# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 3: the geographical discoveries and their effect on colonialism”?
a) Portuguese and Spanish sailors round Africa and cross the Atlantic, and the sea routes turn discovery straight into colonies and empire.  b) Hafsid Tripoli decays into chaos and Spain storms its port in 1510; the Knights of St John then hold the walled city from 1530, while Cyrenaica obeys its tribal sheikhs and Fezzan its Awlad Muhammad rulers at Murzuq.  c) Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Portuguese and Spanish sailors round Africa and cross the Atlantic, and the sea routes turn discovery straight into colonies and empire.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
