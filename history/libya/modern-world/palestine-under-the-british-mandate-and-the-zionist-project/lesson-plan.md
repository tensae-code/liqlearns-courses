## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 6: the Arab–Zionist conflict — Britain occupies Palestine and the Palestinian national movement** — Britain takes Palestine in the war, the Balfour promise opens it to Zionist settlement, and Palestinian Arabs organise a national movement against both.
2. **Chapter 6: Zionist ambitions in Palestine and the Zionist movement's tie to European colonialism** — The Zionist movement seeks a national home in Palestine and survives by riding European colonial power, above all Britain's mandate.
3. **Chapter 6: Arab resistance to the Zionists and the British — the 1936 revolt** — Palestinians rise in the great revolt of 1936, strike and fight for three years, and force Britain to rethink its Palestine policy.
4. **Chapter 6: Palestine during and after the Second World War, and Arab resistance after 1948** — The war ends, Britain hands Palestine to the UN, Israel is proclaimed in 1948, and Arab resistance continues after the catastrophe.
