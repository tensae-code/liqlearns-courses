# Video script — Palestine under the British Mandate and the Zionist Project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Britain takes Palestine in the war, the Balfour promise opens it to Zionist settlement, and Palestinian Arabs organise a national movement against both. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

The Zionist movement seeks a national home in Palestine and survives by riding European colonial power, above all Britain's mandate. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Palestinians rise in the great revolt of 1936, strike and fight for three years, and force Britain to rethink its Palestine policy. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

The war ends, Britain hands Palestine to the UN, Israel is proclaimed in 1948, and Arab resistance continues after the catastrophe. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Libya.
