# Islam Arrives in North Africa — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Abu Bakr and Umar — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Uthman and Ali — b)

Meanings:
a) Abu Bakr holds Arabia together through the Ridda wars and sends the armies out; Umar organises the growing state with diwans, garrison cities and a calendar.
b) Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
