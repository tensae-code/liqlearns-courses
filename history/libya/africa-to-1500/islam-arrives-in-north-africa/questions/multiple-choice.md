# Islam Arrives in North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Abu Bakr and Umar”?
a) Abu Bakr holds Arabia together through the Ridda wars and sends the armies out; Umar organises the growing state with diwans, garrison cities and a calendar.  b) The second war is fought partly on Arab soil, above all in North Africa, and leaves the colonial powers weakened.  c) Medina's charter binds Muhajirun and Ansar; Badr, Uhud and the Trench defend the young state; Hudaybiyya's truce opens the road to Mecca's peaceful conquest.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Abu Bakr holds Arabia together through the Ridda wars and sends the armies out; Umar organises the growing state with diwans, garrison cities and a calendar.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Uthman and Ali”?
a) Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community.  b) Baghdad's fall in 1258 ends the Abbasid age; Mamluk Egypt weakens as Portuguese ships round the Cape in 1498 and strangle its trade; Safavid Iran rises — and the Ottomans, fresh from Constantinople in 1453 and Chaldiran in 1514, turn toward the Arab lands.  c) Nabataeans carve Petra and tax the caravans, Palmyra grows rich between Rome and Persia, and Ghassanids and Lakhmids guard the desert frontiers for Byzantium and Persia.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
