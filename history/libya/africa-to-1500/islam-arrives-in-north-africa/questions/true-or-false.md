# Islam Arrives in North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Abu Bakr and Umar”.
**Answer: False** — Abu Bakr holds Arabia together through the Ridda wars and sends the armies out; Umar organises the growing state with diwans, garrison cities and a calendar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community.
**Answer: True**
