# Islam Arrives in North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Abu Bakr and Umar; Uthman and Ali; The Rashidun Caliphs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Uthman and Ali”?
**Answer:** Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community.
