# Islam Arrives in North Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islam Arrives in North Africa** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Abu Bakr and Umar

Abu Bakr holds Arabia together through the Ridda wars and sends the armies out; Umar organises the growing state with diwans, garrison cities and a calendar. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Uthman and Ali

Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Rashidun Caliphs

Abu Bakr crushes the apostasy wars, kills the false prophet Musaylima and orders the Quran gathered into one book; Umar wins at Yarmouk, receives Jerusalem, breaks Persia at al-Qadisiyya in 635 and sends Amr ibn al-As to take Egypt and found Fustat. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
