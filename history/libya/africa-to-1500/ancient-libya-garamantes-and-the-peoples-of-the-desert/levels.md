# Ancient Libya: Garamantes and the Peoples of the Desert — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the Libu and Rebu tribes face Egypt to the east, Greek settlers under Battus plant Cyrene, Barqa and Apollonia on the coast, and deep in Fezzan the Garamantes farm the Sahara and race four-horse chariots — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
