# Almoravids and Almohads of the Maghreb — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “The Almohads”?
a) The Almohad movement unites the Maghreb and Muslim Spain into one empire, the last great western Islamic state.  b) After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.  c) A secret then open call topples the Umayyads, al-Mansur builds Baghdad and becomes the state's true founder, and the caliphate turns eastward and patronises civilisation.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Almohad movement unites the Maghreb and Muslim Spain into one empire, the last great western Islamic state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. ** What is the heart of the story in “The Almohads”?
a) The Almohad movement unites the Maghreb and Muslim Spain into one empire, the last great western Islamic state.  b) Rome annexes Syria, Egypt and North Africa province by province until the whole southern Mediterranean shore is Roman.  c) Sultans govern the Arab provinces through appointed governors, tax farmers and local notables, and four centuries of Ottoman rule reshape the region's cities and countryside.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Almohad movement unites the Maghreb and Muslim Spain into one empire, the last great western Islamic state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
