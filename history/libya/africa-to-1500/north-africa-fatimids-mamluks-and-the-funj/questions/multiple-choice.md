# North Africa: Fatimids, Mamluks and the Funj — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: the Fatimid state and the Zirids”?
a) The Fatimids rise in Ifriqiya, conquer Egypt and found Cairo, then leave the Zirids holding the Maghreb coast as their deputies.  b) The Fatimid call rises in Ifriqiya, destroys the Aghlabids, then takes Egypt and founds Cairo, ruling the southern Mediterranean.  c) A green land of elephants and giraffes dries into the Sahara; dinosaur bones at Nalut, giraffe carvings in the Acacus and pottery at Murzuq are the evidence its first peoples left.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Fatimids rise in Ifriqiya, conquer Egypt and found Cairo, then leave the Zirids holding the Maghreb coast as their deputies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
