# Libyan Kingdoms after Carthage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Numidians”.
**Answer: False** — Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician.

**2. ** Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician.
**Answer: True**
