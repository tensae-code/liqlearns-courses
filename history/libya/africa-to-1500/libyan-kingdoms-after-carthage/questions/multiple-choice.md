# Libyan Kingdoms after Carthage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “The Numidians”?
a) Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician.  b) The war ends, Britain hands Palestine to the UN, Israel is proclaimed in 1948, and Arab resistance continues after the catastrophe.  c) Kings ruling with councils of elders, siege engines and iron, Hammurabi's carved laws, irrigation canals — and cuneiform, the wedge writing pressed into clay.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
