# Libyan Kingdoms after Carthage

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Libyan Kingdoms after Carthage** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
