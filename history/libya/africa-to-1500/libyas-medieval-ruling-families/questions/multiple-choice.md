**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: ruling families inside Libyan lands in the medieval era”?
a) Inside what is now Libya, local families and dynasties govern the coast and the oases between the great empires, keeping Libyan towns alive between Fatimids, Zirids and Hafsids.  b) Baghdad, Damascus, Cairo, Kairouan, Fez and Cordoba shine in turn as capitals of learning, each drawing scholars from the whole Islamic world.  c) One family of faiths from Babylon to Yemen — Marduk, Amun, Baal, Hadad, Ishtar in every land, the sun-moon-Venus triad, Osiris echoed in Adonis — and everywhere a belief in life after death.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
