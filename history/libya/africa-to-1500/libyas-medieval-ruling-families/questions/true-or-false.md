# Libya's Medieval Ruling Families — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 5: ruling families inside Libyan lands in the medieval era”.
**Answer: False** — Inside what is now Libya, local families and dynasties govern the coast and the oases between the great empires, keeping Libyan towns alive between Fatimids, Zirids and Hafsids.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Harun al-Rashid appoints Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab over Ifriqiya in 800; his line builds al-Abbasiyya and a great fleet, takes Sicily in 827 under the judge Asad ibn al-Furat, and makes the Maghreb flourish — until the Fatimids end the state in 909.
**Answer: True**
