# Libya's Medieval Ruling Families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 5: ruling families inside Libyan lands in the medieval era; The Aghlabids; The Fatimids; The Fatimid state.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Aghlabids”?
**Answer:** Harun al-Rashid appoints Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab over Ifriqiya in 800; his line builds al-Abbasiyya and a great fleet, takes Sicily in 827 under the judge Asad ibn al-Furat, and makes the Maghreb flourish — until the Fatimids end the state in 909.
