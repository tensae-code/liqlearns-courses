## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit (Nile Valley): the formation of the unified state** — Narmer (Mena) joins Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom, and the Nile Valley becomes a single state.
2. **Unit (Nile Valley): the Old Kingdom age** — Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure raise the Giza pyramids and the pyramid age shows what a united Egypt can organise.
3. **Unit (Nile Valley): the Middle Kingdom, the age of economic prosperity** — After collapse, reunified kings drain the Faiyum, extend trade and bring a second golden age of prosperity.
4. **Unit (Nile Valley): the New Kingdom, the age of empire** — Warrior pharaohs drive out the Hyksos and carry Egypt's borders to the Euphrates and into Nubia, making Egypt an empire.
5. **Unit (Nile Valley): the late periods** — Libyan, Kushite, Persian, Greek and Roman rulers take Egypt in turn, and the pharaonic story fades under foreign crowns.
6. **Egypt and its civilisation** — 42 districts gather into two kingdoms and Narmer unites them about 3245 BCE; 30 dynasties follow — Khufu's pyramid, Abu Simbel, Karnak, Akhenaten's one god, and the mummies prepared for the next life.
