# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (Nile Valley): the formation of the unified state”?
a) Narmer (Mena) joins Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom, and the Nile Valley becomes a single state.  b) The Phoenician cities of Tyre, Sidon and Byblos sail the whole Mediterranean, sell purple dye and cedar, and hand every later people their alphabet.  c) A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Narmer (Mena) joins Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom, and the Nile Valley becomes a single state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (Nile Valley): the Old Kingdom age”?
a) Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure raise the Giza pyramids and the pyramid age shows what a united Egypt can organise.  b) Saladin founds the Ayyubid state, unites Egypt and the Levant, and turns the Muslim world toward recovering Jerusalem.  c) Idris al-Senussi governs Cyrenaica from Ajdabiya and wins the Akrama agreement of 1917 — prisoners exchanged, Senussi zawiyas reopened, seized property restored — while the western leaders proclaim the Tripolitanian Republic on 18 November 1918, the Arab homeland's first republic, with its governing council, shura council and scholars' assembly.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure raise the Giza pyramids and the pyramid age shows what a united Egypt can organise.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
