# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit (Nile Valley): the formation of the unified state”.
**Answer: False** — Narmer (Mena) joins Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom, and the Nile Valley becomes a single state.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure raise the Giza pyramids and the pyramid age shows what a united Egypt can organise.
**Answer: True**
