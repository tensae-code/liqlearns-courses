**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Unit (Nile Valley): the formation of the unified state; Unit (Nile Valley): the Old Kingdom age; Unit (Nile Valley): the Middle Kingdom, the age of economic prosperity; Unit (Nile Valley): the New Kingdom, the age of empire; Unit (Nile Valley): the late periods; Egypt and its civilisation.
