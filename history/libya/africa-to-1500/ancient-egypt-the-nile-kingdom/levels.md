Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Narmer (Mena) joins Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom, and the Nile Valley becomes a single state — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Warrior pharaohs drive out the Hyksos and carry Egypt's borders to the Euphrates and into Nubia, making Egypt an empire — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Libyan, Kushite, Persian, Greek and Roman rulers take Egypt in turn, and the pharaonic story fades under foreign crowns — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
