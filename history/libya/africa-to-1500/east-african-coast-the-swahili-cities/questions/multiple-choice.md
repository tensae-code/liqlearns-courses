# East African Coast: The Swahili Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: the spread of Islam in East Africa”?
a) Arab traders and settlers carry Islam down the East African coast and the Swahili cities grow on the monsoon trade.  b) Amorites build Mari on the Euphrates; Canaanites raise walled city-kingdoms — Jericho, Megiddo, Jerusalem — each with its own king, until Assyria and Babylon swallow them all.  c) Amorites, Canaanites and Aramaeans build rival kingdoms and city-states across Syria and Palestine, trading and fighting between Egypt and Mesopotamia.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Arab traders and settlers carry Islam down the East African coast and the Swahili cities grow on the monsoon trade.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
