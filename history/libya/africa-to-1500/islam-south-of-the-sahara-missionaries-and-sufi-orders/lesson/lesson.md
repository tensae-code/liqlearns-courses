# Islam South of the Sahara: Missionaries and Sufi Orders

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islam South of the Sahara: Missionaries and Sufi Orders** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Arab Muslim missionaries and the Sufi orders, above all the Qadiriyya, carry Islam peacefully across the Sahara into West and Central Africa. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
