# Islam in Africa South of the Sahara — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Islam crosses Africa by conquest, trade and the Sufi orders — Qadiriyya scholars make Timbuktu a beacon, Tijaniyya spreads in Senegal and the Niger, Mirghaniyya in Sudan, and the Senussiyya of Muhammad ibn Ali al-Senussi plants 121 zawiyas from al-Bayda in 1841 across Libya and the Sahara.
