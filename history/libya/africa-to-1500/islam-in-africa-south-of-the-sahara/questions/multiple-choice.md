Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “The spread of Islam in Africa”?
a) Islam crosses Africa by conquest, trade and the Sufi orders — Qadiriyya scholars make Timbuktu a beacon, Tijaniyya spreads in Senegal and the Niger, Mirghaniyya in Sudan, and the Senussiyya of Muhammad ibn Ali al-Senussi plants 121 zawiyas from al-Bayda in 1841 across Libya and the Sahara.  b) After collapse, reunified kings drain the Faiyum, extend trade and bring a second golden age of prosperity.  c) Ubayd Allah al-Mahdi founds the Fatimid state in North Africa in 909 and builds Mahdia; al-Mu'izz makes Tripoli's port his naval base for the Sicily jihad, Jawhar takes Egypt in 969 and founds Cairo and al-Azhar — then the Banu Hilal invasion ruins Ifriqiya and the state fades away in 1171.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Islam crosses Africa by conquest, trade and the Sufi orders — Qadiriyya scholars make Timbuktu a beacon, Tijaniyya spreads in Senegal and the Niger, Mirghaniyya in Sudan, and the Senussiyya of Muhammad ibn Ali al-Senussi plants 121 zawiyas from al-Bayda in 1841 across Libya and the Sahara.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
