# Islam in Africa South of the Sahara — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Islam crosses Africa by conquest, trade and the Sufi orders — Qadiriyya scholars make Timbuktu a beacon, Tijaniyya spreads in Senegal and the Niger, Mirghaniyya in Sudan, and the Senussiyya of Muhammad ibn Ali al-Senussi plants 121 zawiyas from al-Bayda in 1841 across Libya and the Sahara — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
