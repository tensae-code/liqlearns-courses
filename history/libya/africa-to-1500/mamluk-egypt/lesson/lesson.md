# Mamluk Egypt

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Mamluk Egypt** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Slave-soldier sultans rule Egypt and Syria for over two centuries, defeat Mongols and Crusaders, and fill Cairo with mosques and schools. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Give one real example: Slave-soldier sultans rule Egypt and Syria for over two centuries, defeat Mongols and Crusaders, and fill Cairo with mosques and schools. ## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Slave-soldier sultans rule Egypt and Syria for over two centuries, defeat Mongols and Crusaders, and fill Cairo with mosques and schools.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
