# Mamluk Egypt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 6: Mamluk rule in Egypt and the Levant”?
a) Slave-soldier sultans rule Egypt and Syria for over two centuries, defeat Mongols and Crusaders, and fill Cairo with mosques and schools.  b) Turkish tribes flee the Mongol storm westward; Osman founds the state at Bursa; Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 1453, and the empire turns toward the Arab lands.  c) Saba, Ma'in, Qataban and Himyar grow rich on frankincense, and the Marib Dam waters Yemen's terraces until its breaches undo the kingdoms.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Slave-soldier sultans rule Egypt and Syria for over two centuries, defeat Mongols and Crusaders, and fill Cairo with mosques and schools.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 6: Mamluk rule in Egypt and the Levant”?
a) Slave-soldier sultans rule Egypt and Syria for over two centuries, defeat Mongols and Crusaders, and fill Cairo with mosques and schools.  b) Amorites, Canaanites and Aramaeans build rival kingdoms and city-states across Syria and Palestine, trading and fighting between Egypt and Mesopotamia.  c) The Mongols sack Baghdad in 1258 and march on the Levant and Egypt; the Mamluks destroy their army at Ain Jalut in 1260 and clear them from the Levant.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Slave-soldier sultans rule Egypt and Syria for over two centuries, defeat Mongols and Crusaders, and fill Cairo with mosques and schools.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
