# Maghreb after the Almohads: Hafsids, Zayyanids, Marinids

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Maghreb after the Almohads: Hafsids, Zayyanids, Marinids** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 5: the successor states of the Maghreb

When the Almohads fade, Hafsids take Ifriqiya, Zayyanids Tlemcen and Marinids Morocco, and the Maghreb settles into three rival kingdoms. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 6: the Maghreb and al-Andalus from Baghdad's fall to the Ottomans

The western lands drift between Hafsid, Zayyanid and Marinid rulers and a shrinking Granada while the east waits for a new unifier. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
