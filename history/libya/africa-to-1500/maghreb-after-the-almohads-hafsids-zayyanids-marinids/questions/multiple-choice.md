# Maghreb after the Almohads: Hafsids, Zayyanids, Marinids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 5: the successor states of the Maghreb”?
a) When the Almohads fade, Hafsids take Ifriqiya, Zayyanids Tlemcen and Marinids Morocco, and the Maghreb settles into three rival kingdoms.  b) The United Kingdom of Libya builds its institutions — a parliament elected in February 1952, a Senate of twenty-four — and takes its seat in the Arab League and the United Nations.  c) Driven by faith and pressure at home, Muslim armies defeat both Persia and Byzantium, and the book asks why the two great empires fell so fast.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) When the Almohads fade, Hafsids take Ifriqiya, Zayyanids Tlemcen and Marinids Morocco, and the Maghreb settles into three rival kingdoms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The western lands drift between Hafsid, Zayyanid and Marinid rulers and a shrinking Granada while the east waits for a new unifier.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
