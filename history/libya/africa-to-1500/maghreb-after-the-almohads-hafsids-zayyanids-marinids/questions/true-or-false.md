# Maghreb after the Almohads: Hafsids, Zayyanids, Marinids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 5: the successor states of the Maghreb”.
**Answer: False** — When the Almohads fade, Hafsids take Ifriqiya, Zayyanids Tlemcen and Marinids Morocco, and the Maghreb settles into three rival kingdoms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The western lands drift between Hafsid, Zayyanid and Marinid rulers and a shrinking Granada while the east waits for a new unifier.
**Answer: True**
