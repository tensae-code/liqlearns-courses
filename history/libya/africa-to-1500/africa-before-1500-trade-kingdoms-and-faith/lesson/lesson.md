# Africa before 1500: Trade, Kingdoms and Faith

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Africa before 1500: Trade, Kingdoms and Faith** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The book tells the conquests as liberation — peoples crushed by Persian and Byzantine taxes and persecution welcome the new order. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
