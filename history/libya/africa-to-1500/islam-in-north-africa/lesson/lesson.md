# Islam in North Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islam in North Africa** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in North Africa and the conquest of Libya

Under Umar the Muslim armies cross from Egypt into Libya and North Africa, and the region begins its turn to Islam. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 5: the Maghreb — the independent emirates

Rustamids at Tahert, Idrisids in Morocco and Aghlabids in Ifriqiya break away from Baghdad and rule the Maghreb as their own states. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
