# Islam in North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in North Africa and the conquest of Libya”.
**Answer: False** — Under Umar the Muslim armies cross from Egypt into Libya and North Africa, and the region begins its turn to Islam.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Rustamids at Tahert, Idrisids in Morocco and Aghlabids in Ifriqiya break away from Baghdad and rule the Maghreb as their own states.
**Answer: True**
