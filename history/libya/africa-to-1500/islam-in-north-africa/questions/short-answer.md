# Islam in North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in North Africa and the conquest of Libya; Chapter 5: the Maghreb — the independent emirates.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Chapter 5: the Maghreb — the independent emirates”?
**Answer:** Rustamids at Tahert, Idrisids in Morocco and Aghlabids in Ifriqiya break away from Baghdad and rule the Maghreb as their own states.
