# Islam in North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 2: the spread of Islam in North Africa and the conquest of Libya”?
a) Under Umar the Muslim armies cross from Egypt into Libya and North Africa, and the region begins its turn to Islam.  b) Beaten to Tunisia in 1881, Italy prepares Libya quietly — Banco di Roma branches, Italian schools, study missions — wins Britain's and France's consent, then shells Tripoli and lands on 5 October 1911 against barely 7,000 Ottoman troops, meeting a coast-wide resistance of named battles from Abu Meliana and al-Shatt to al-Hawari.  c) Islam crosses Africa by conquest, trade and the Sufi orders — Qadiriyya scholars make Timbuktu a beacon, Tijaniyya spreads in Senegal and the Niger, Mirghaniyya in Sudan, and the Senussiyya of Muhammad ibn Ali al-Senussi plants 121 zawiyas from al-Bayda in 1841 across Libya and the Sahara.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Under Umar the Muslim armies cross from Egypt into Libya and North Africa, and the region begins its turn to Islam.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Rustamids at Tahert, Idrisids in Morocco and Aghlabids in Ifriqiya break away from Baghdad and rule the Maghreb as their own states.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
