# Carthage and the Punic Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit (North Africa): the Phoenicians and the founding of Carthage”.
**Answer: False** — Tyrian settlers found Carthage on the Tunisian coast and the trading city grows into the western Mediterranean's sea power.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Carthaginian farmers write farming treatises, its ports and fleets run the sea trade, and Libyan, Punic and Greek cities share one coast.
**Answer: True**
