# Carthage and the Punic Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (North Africa): the Phoenicians and the founding of Carthage”?
a) Tyrian settlers found Carthage on the Tunisian coast and the trading city grows into the western Mediterranean's sea power.  b) After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.  c) Crusader states seize the Levant coast, then Zengi, Nur al-Din and Saladin reunite the Muslims and retake Jerusalem.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Tyrian settlers found Carthage on the Tunisian coast and the trading city grows into the western Mediterranean's sea power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (North Africa): civilisational features in North Africa”?
a) Carthaginian farmers write farming treatises, its ports and fleets run the sea trade, and Libyan, Punic and Greek cities share one coast.  b) In Medina the Prophet organises a state — administration, covenants, an army — fights Badr, Uhud and the Trench, makes the Hudaybiyya truce, then enters Mecca in conquest and dies leaving Arabia united.  c) Cavour and the patriots stitch Italy's fragments into one kingdom, the same Italy that will later seize Libya.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Carthaginian farmers write farming treatises, its ports and fleets run the sea trade, and Libyan, Punic and Greek cities share one coast.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
