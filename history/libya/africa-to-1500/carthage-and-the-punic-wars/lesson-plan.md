## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit (North Africa): the Phoenicians and the founding of Carthage** — Tyrian settlers found Carthage on the Tunisian coast and the trading city grows into the western Mediterranean's sea power.
2. **Unit (North Africa): civilisational features in North Africa** — Carthaginian farmers write farming treatises, its ports and fleets run the sea trade, and Libyan, Punic and Greek cities share one coast.
3. **The Phoenicians in North Africa** — Dido flees Tyre and founds Carthage in 814 BCE; its empire runs from Sirte to Spain until the Punic Wars from 264 BCE end with Carthage destroyed in 146 BCE — in Libya the Phoenicians plant Oea, Leptis and Sabratha.
4. **Features of Carthaginian civilisation** — Elected kings checked by 300 elders and a court of 104 judges; ships to Egypt and Greece, caravans to Garama, the dam on Wadi Kaam, new crops and six-storey houses.
