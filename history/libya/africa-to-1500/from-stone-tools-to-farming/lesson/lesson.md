# From Stone Tools to Farming

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **From Stone Tools to Farming** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Part 2: a brief look at the prehistoric ages

Stone-age hunters chip tools in the Old, Middle and New Stone Ages, tame animals and learn farming, then metals replace stone and settled civilisation becomes possible. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — How human communities developed before history

Old Stone Age hunters learn fire, settle by water, farm and grind grain, until pottery, copper and the first villages end the long prehistory. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
