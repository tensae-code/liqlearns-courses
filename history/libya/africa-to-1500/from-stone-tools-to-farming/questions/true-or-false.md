# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Part 2: a brief look at the prehistoric ages”.
**Answer: False** — Stone-age hunters chip tools in the Old, Middle and New Stone Ages, tame animals and learn farming, then metals replace stone and settled civilisation becomes possible.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Old Stone Age hunters learn fire, settle by water, farm and grind grain, until pottery, copper and the first villages end the long prehistory.
**Answer: True**
