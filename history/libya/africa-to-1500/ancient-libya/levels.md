## Sub lesson 1 — North Africa before history
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A green land of elephants and giraffes dries into the Sahara; dinosaur bones at Nalut, giraffe carvings in the Acacus and pottery at Murzuq are the evidence its first peoples left — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ancient Libyans
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tehenu, Temehu, Libu, Meshwesh and Nasamones face Egypt across the desert, while the Garamantes build Garama in Fezzan, drive war chariots, run the Sahara caravans to the Three Cities and defy Rome — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Features of ancient Libyan civilisation
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Hereditary chiefs rule with tribal councils and unite under one leader against Rome; a Libyan script of thirty letters is carved in columns; the great god rides a bull carrying the sun disc, and caravans have traded into Africa since about 1520 BCE — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
