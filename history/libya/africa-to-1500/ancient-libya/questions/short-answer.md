# Ancient Libya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** North Africa before history; The ancient Libyans; Features of ancient Libyan civilisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The ancient Libyans”?
**Answer:** Tehenu, Temehu, Libu, Meshwesh and Nasamones face Egypt across the desert, while the Garamantes build Garama in Fezzan, drive war chariots, run the Sahara caravans to the Three Cities and defy Rome.
