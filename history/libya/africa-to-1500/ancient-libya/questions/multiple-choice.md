# Ancient Libya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “North Africa before history”?
a) A green land of elephants and giraffes dries into the Sahara; dinosaur bones at Nalut, giraffe carvings in the Acacus and pottery at Murzuq are the evidence its first peoples left.  b) Narmer (Mena) joins Upper and Lower Egypt into one kingdom, and the Nile Valley becomes a single state.  c) Harun al-Rashid appoints Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab over Ifriqiya in 800; his line builds al-Abbasiyya and a great fleet, takes Sicily in 827 under the judge Asad ibn al-Furat, and makes the Maghreb flourish — until the Fatimids end the state in 909.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A green land of elephants and giraffes dries into the Sahara; dinosaur bones at Nalut, giraffe carvings in the Acacus and pottery at Murzuq are the evidence its first peoples left.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “The ancient Libyans”?
a) Tehenu, Temehu, Libu, Meshwesh and Nasamones face Egypt across the desert, while the Garamantes build Garama in Fezzan, drive war chariots, run the Sahara caravans to the Three Cities and defy Rome.  b) Weak caliphs, foreign guards who seize power, an over-wide state and failing revenues break the caliphate into pieces.  c) Britain rules Cyrenaica and Tripolitania separately and France holds Fezzan; the Bevin–Sforza plan of 1948 to split Libya among the three powers leaks, the streets explode in protest, a four-power committee hears one demand everywhere — independence — and the Arab League carries Libya's case to the United Nations.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Tehenu, Temehu, Libu, Meshwesh and Nasamones face Egypt across the desert, while the Garamantes build Garama in Fezzan, drive war chariots, run the Sahara caravans to the Three Cities and defy Rome.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
