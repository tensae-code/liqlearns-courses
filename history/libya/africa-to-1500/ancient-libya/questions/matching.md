# Ancient Libya — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** North Africa before history — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The ancient Libyans — b)

Meanings:
a) A green land of elephants and giraffes dries into the Sahara; dinosaur bones at Nalut, giraffe carvings in the Acacus and pottery at Murzuq are the evidence its first peoples left.
b) Tehenu, Temehu, Libu, Meshwesh and Nasamones face Egypt across the desert, while the Garamantes build Garama in Fezzan, drive war chariots, run the Sahara caravans to the Three Cities and defy Rome.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
