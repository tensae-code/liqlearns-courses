# Libyan Kingdoms after Carthage: Numidia to Rome — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
