# Libyan Kingdoms after Carthage: Numidia to Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Unit (North Africa): the ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage”?
a) Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE.  b) Cedar ships steered by the pole star carry purple dye and glass to Cadiz and Carthage, and the 22-letter alphabet they invent travels via Greece and Rome into Europe's letters.  c) Harun al-Rashid appoints Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab over Ifriqiya in 800; his line builds al-Abbasiyya and a great fleet, takes Sicily in 827 under the judge Asad ibn al-Furat, and makes the Maghreb flourish — until the Fatimids end the state in 909.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
