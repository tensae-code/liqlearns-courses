# Libyan Kingdoms after Carthage: Numidia to Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit (North Africa): the ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage”.
**Answer: False** — Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE.

**2. ** Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE.
**Answer: True**
