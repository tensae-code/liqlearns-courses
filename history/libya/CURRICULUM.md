# History — Libya course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Libya inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Libya's books in hand are eight Arabic student books from the Centre for Educational Curricula and Educational Research, Libya (2019–2020 / 1440–1441 AH): three secondary histories of the Arab world (ancient, medieval, and modern and contemporary), a Grade 7 History of Libya and the Ancient World, a Grade 9 Modern and Contemporary History of Libya, a Grade 8 Islamic History, and the History parts of the combined Grade 5 and Grade 6 Social Studies (History and Geography) books — all eight COMPLETE as filed (the two combined books COMPLETE for their History parts). The placements file's book order is the teaching order — earliest books first. Inside that order, the ladder below groups the books the way Libya's own story divides in them: first steps and the ancient Arab world, Arabia before Islam and the rise of Islam, the caliphates and Islamic civilisation, the Maghreb, al-Andalus and Islam in Africa, Ottoman Libya and the Karamanlis, and Europe transformed, the world wars and Libya's struggle to independence. Each lesson sits at the level of the first book that teaches it. Levels, NOT grades: the grade numbers live only in ABOUT.md and sources.md. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Libya's own Centre for Educational Curricula and Educational Research books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps and the Ancient World** — the historian's craft first — what history is and how the past is dated — then the ancient world as Libya's books tell it as one Arab-world story: what a civilisation is, Mesopotamia and its gifts, the Levant and the Phoenicians, Egypt on the Nile, ancient Libya itself — the Garamantes, Cyrenaica and Carthage — Arabia south, centre and north, the trade routes, the foreign powers that tried to seize the region, and the gifts the ancient Arab world handed back to civilisation — 30 lessons.

2. **Arabia before Islam and the Rise of Islam** — Arabia on the eve of Islam — its tribes, trade and fairs — then the call in Mecca, the state built in Medina, the faith that reshaped life around one community, the Rashidun expansion, and the telling of the conquests as the freeing of peoples under Persia and Rome — 7 lessons.

3. **The Caliphates and Islamic Civilisation** — the Umayyads from Damascus to Cordoba, the Abbasids and Baghdad's golden age and fall, how a caliphate was actually run — diwan, post and ministers — the civilisation of science, literature and art, and the two great invasions answered: the Crusades and the Mongols, with Mamluk Egypt holding the line — 7 lessons.

4. **The Maghreb, Al-Andalus and Islam in Africa** — Islam's road west into North Africa and Libya itself — the independent emirates, the Fatimids and Zirids, the ruling families inside Libyan lands, the Almoravid and Almohad empires, the three successor kingdoms, Muslim Spain from Cordoba to Granada's fall, the Swahili cities of the east coast, and Islam carried south of the Sahara by missionaries and Sufi orders — 10 lessons.

5. **Ottoman Libya and the Karamanlis** — Spain and the Knights in Tripoli first — the occupation that opens the door — then the Ottoman state rising and ruling the Arab lands, its long decline and the Arab awakening against it, and Libya's own four centuries in full: the first Ottoman era, Ahmad Pasha al-Karamanli and his family's state, its treaties and its war with America, and the second Ottoman era to 1912 — 5 lessons.

6. **Europe Transformed, the World Wars and Libya's Struggle to Independence** — Europe remade — the Renaissance, the sea discoveries, the revolutions, Italy unified and industry hungry for colonies — then the scramble, the two world wars fought partly on Arab soil, and the national struggles country by country: Libya's twenty-year war against Italy from 1911 through the Tripolitanian Republic, Omar Mukhtar, liberation and UN independence in 1951, the Maghreb and Sudan roads beside it, independent Libya's first institutions, and Palestine under the mandate as the open wound that closes the course — 17 lessons.

**Totals: 76 lessons** (Level 1: 30 · Level 2: 7 · Level 3: 7 · Level 4: 10 · Level 5: 5 · Level 6: 17 — 161 placements stocked; all 76 lessons stand on student books, so those lessons are taught in 1 country (Libya); 33 lessons carry sub lessons).
