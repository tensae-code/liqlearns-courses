# Ancient Civilisations China — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) along the Yellow River a civilisation of silk, characters and emperors grows in the far east of the ancient world

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
