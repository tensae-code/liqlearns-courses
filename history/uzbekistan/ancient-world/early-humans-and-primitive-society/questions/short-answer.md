# Early Humans and Primitive Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “5. Primitive man and 6. The development of the ancient human community”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** the first people learn fire, tools and speech, and the lone family grows into clan and tribe — the long childhood of humankind before any state

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “§2 The early stages of the earliest man and §3 Clan society”?
**Answer:** from the first tool-makers to settled clan communities with elders, custom and shared work — how society exists before kings
