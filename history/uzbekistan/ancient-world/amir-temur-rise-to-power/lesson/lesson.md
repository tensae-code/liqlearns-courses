# Amir Temur Rise to Power

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

a minor lord from Kesh fights his way up through Transoxiana's chaos, takes Samarkand and by 1370 wears the crown as Sahibkiran, lord of the fortunate conjunction

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of amir temur rise to power — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Amir Temur Rise to Power. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
