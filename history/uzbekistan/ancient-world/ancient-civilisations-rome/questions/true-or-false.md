# Ancient Civilisations Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) a city on the Tiber grows into an empire that rules the Mediterranean and leaves its law and roads to Europe
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Rome grows from a Tiber village of kings into a republic of senate, consuls and citizen legions
