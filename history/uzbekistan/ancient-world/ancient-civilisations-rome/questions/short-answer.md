# Ancient Civilisations Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “10. Ancient Greece and Ancient Rome (Rome part)”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** a city on the Tiber grows into an empire that rules the Mediterranean and leaves its law and roads to Europe

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “§47 Italy and its inhabitants and §48 The Roman Republic”?
**Answer:** Rome grows from a Tiber village of kings into a republic of senate, consuls and citizen legions
