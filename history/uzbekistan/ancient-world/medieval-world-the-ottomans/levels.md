# Medieval World the Ottomans — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the story of medieval world the ottomans — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it — in their own words, retell the story's turn, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain story as taught, then cases where a wrong neighbour-story tempts the wrong answer, then the story's pattern applied to a new place or time the learner has not seen in the lesson.
