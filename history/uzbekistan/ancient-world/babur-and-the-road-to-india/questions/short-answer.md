# Babur and the Road to India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “30. India and Babur”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Temur's heir Babur loses Samarkand three times, takes Kabul, and at Panipat (1526) founds the Mughal empire in India, writing his Baburnama on the way

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. I §4 Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur — a great statesman and thinker”?
**Answer:** the Fergana prince who lost Samarkand to Shaybani three times becomes the book's model ruler-writer; his road leads through Kabul to India and the Baburnama
