# Babur and the Road to India

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — 30. India and Babur

Temur's heir Babur loses Samarkand three times, takes Kabul, and at Panipat (1526) founds the Mughal empire in India, writing his Baburnama on the way

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. I §4 Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur — a great statesman and thinker

the Fergana prince who lost Samarkand to Shaybani three times becomes the book's model ruler-writer; his road leads through Kabul to India and the Baburnama

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of babur and the road to india — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Babur and the Road to India. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
