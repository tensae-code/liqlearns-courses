# Ancient Civilisations India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) on the Indus and Ganges, cities, castes and the Buddha's teaching shape a civilisation unlike any other

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
