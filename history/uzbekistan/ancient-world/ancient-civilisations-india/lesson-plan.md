**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of ancient civilisations india — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **Ancient Civilisations China** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: the story of ancient civilisations india — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
2. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, what came of it — without looking back.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of the region in the story if you have one. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Retells names but not the turn → ask: what changed in this story, and for whom? The turn is the proof.
- Go back to the one thing: the story of ancient civilisations india — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
