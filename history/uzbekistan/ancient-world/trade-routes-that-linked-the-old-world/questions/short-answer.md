# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “16. The Great Silk Road”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** caravans link China to the Mediterranean through Samarkand and Bukhara; silk, paper and ideas travel the same road and make the homeland the world's crossroads

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “§46 The Great Silk Road”?
**Answer:** Zhang Qian's journey opens the road; through Kushan and Sogdian hands silk, glass, horses and religions travel between China, India, Iran and Rome
