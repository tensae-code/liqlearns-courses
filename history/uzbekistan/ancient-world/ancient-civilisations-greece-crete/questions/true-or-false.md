# Ancient Civilisations Greece Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Greek city-states of sailors, athletes and thinkers invent the polis world the Romans will inherit
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Crete and Mycenae fall, the polis rises from the dark age, and Greek colonists ring the Mediterranean with new cities
