# The Xinhai Revolution and Sun Yat Sen — short answer

**1.**  Retell the story of “Ch. V §29 The socio-economic and political position of China”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Sun Yat-sen's Tongmenghui (1905) programmes the republic — down with the Qing, equal land rights, a people's livelihood; in December 1911 he returns, Nanjing proclaims the Republic with him as provisional president, but the revolution stalls into dual power with Yuan Shikai's Beiyang north
