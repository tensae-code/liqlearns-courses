# Reform and Fall of the Bukhara Emirate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  Retell the story of “Ch. 5 §26 The attempt to carry out reforms and the fall of the Bukhara Emirate”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Young Bukharan reformers beg schools and a constitution; the Emir answers with pogroms, and in 1920 the Red Army ends the emirate altogether
