# The Dreyfus Affair and France Before 1914

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Captain Dreyfus is framed on forged papers and sent to lifelong exile (1894); Zola's published letter forces the real spy Esterhazy into the open, and France splits for a decade before Dreyfus is fully cleared (1906) — while iron output quadruples and the Eiffel Tower rises over the republic

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the dreyfus affair and france before 1914 — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Dreyfus Affair and France Before 1914. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
