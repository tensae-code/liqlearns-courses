# Afghanistan Between Britain and Russia

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Sher Ali refuses a British mission and the Second Anglo-Afghan War follows; the Gandamak treaty (1879) costs Kabul its foreign policy, the Kabul residency is stormed, and Abdur Rahman keeps a guarded internal independence — Britain leaves Kandahar beaten by Afghan resistance at Maiwand (1880)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of afghanistan between britain and russia — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Afghanistan Between Britain and Russia. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
