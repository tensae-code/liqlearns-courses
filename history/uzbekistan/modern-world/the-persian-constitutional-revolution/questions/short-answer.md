# The Persian Constitutional Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  Retell the story of “Ch. V §32 Iran at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Persians take bast in mosques and cemeteries where no punishment can touch them, demanding a "House of Justice"; the shah concedes a constitution and the first Majlis (1906) takes the budget and foreign loans into its own hands — then a new shah fights the revolution his father signed
