# Britain Loses Its Industrial Lead — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  Retell the story of “Ch. II §11 Britain at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Disraeli's reforms (the 54-hour week, no factory children under ten) cannot hide the turn — the "workshop of the world" falls to third in industrial output as its capital flows to the colonies and German and American goods chase British ones out of world markets, London keeping only the money crown
