# Video script — The Eastern Question Nationalism in the Balkans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the eastern question nationalism in the balkans — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Russia fights Turkey (1877–78), Osman Pasha surrenders at Plevna, and the San Stefano treaty draws a big Bulgaria — until Britain's fleet and the Berlin Congress cut the gains back, teaching Russia that Balkan victories are settled in Europe's chancelleries

Bulgaria's April rising (1876) is drowned in blood — some 30,000 killed — and poet Hristo Botev falls fighting; the Tarnovo Constitution (1878) makes the freed principality a constitutional monarchy, which then quarrels with its Russian liberator over Prince Battenberg

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
