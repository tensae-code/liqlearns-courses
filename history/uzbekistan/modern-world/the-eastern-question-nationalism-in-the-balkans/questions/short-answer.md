# The Eastern Question Nationalism in the Balkans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Ch. IV §20 The foreign policy of Russia”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Russia fights Turkey (1877–78), Osman Pasha surrenders at Plevna, and the San Stefano treaty draws a big Bulgaria — until Britain's fleet and the Berlin Congress cut the gains back, teaching Russia that Balkan victories are settled in Europe's chancelleries

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. IV §25 The Balkan states at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century”?
**Answer:** Bulgaria's April rising (1876) is drowned in blood — some 30,000 killed — and poet Hristo Botev falls fighting; the Tarnovo Constitution (1878) makes the freed principality a constitutional monarchy, which then quarrels with its Russian liberator over Prince Battenberg
