# Science At the Turn of the Century

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Faraday and Maxwell build the theory of electromagnetism, the Curies, Becquerel and Rutherford open the atom, Tsiolkovsky proves rockets can reach space — and Einstein's relativity (1905, 1916) redraws the universe the century thought it knew

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of science at the turn of the century — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Science At the Turn of the Century. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
