# Meiji Japan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  Retell the story of “Ch. V §§26–27 Japan at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** the Meiji state abolishes the feudal domains, frees the land market and builds 1,300 factories, then sells them cheap to the houses that become Mitsui and Mitsubishi — the first Eastern country to take Europe's industrial technique ready-made, its monopoly capitalism still woven with feudal leftovers and a landlord-bourgeois monarchy
