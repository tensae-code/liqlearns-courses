## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§3 Socio-economic life in the countries of Western Europe at the beginning of the new period** — manufactories gather workers under one roof, merchants become capitalists and peasants become wage-hands — a new economy inside the old order
2. **Ch. I §1 Changes occurring in the epoch of monopoly capitalism at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century** — free competition gives way to cartels, syndicates and trusts (the Rhine-Westphalia coal syndicate holds 86.7% of Germany's coal), banks merge with industry into a financial oligarchy, capital is exported and the world is divided between the great monopolies
3. **Ch. III §14 The United States of America** — trusts swallow whole industries — Morgan's 1901 Steel Corporation, the first billion-dollar company, and Rockefeller's Standard Oil with 90% of oil output — until two banks control 112 more and 2% of Americans own 60% of the nation's wealth
