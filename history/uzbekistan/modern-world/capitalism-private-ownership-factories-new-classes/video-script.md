# Video script — Capitalism Private Ownership Factories New Classes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of capitalism private ownership factories new classes — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

manufactories gather workers under one roof, merchants become capitalists and peasants become wage-hands — a new economy inside the old order

free competition gives way to cartels, syndicates and trusts (the Rhine-Westphalia coal syndicate holds 86.7% of Germany's coal), banks merge with industry into a financial oligarchy, capital is exported and the world is divided between the great monopolies

trusts swallow whole industries — Morgan's 1901 Steel Corporation, the first billion-dollar company, and Rockefeller's Standard Oil with 90% of oil output — until two banks control 112 more and 2% of Americans own 60% of the nation's wealth

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
