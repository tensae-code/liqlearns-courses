# Capitalism Private Ownership Factories New Classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “§3 Socio-economic life in the countries of Western Europe at the beginning of the new period”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** manufactories gather workers under one roof, merchants become capitalists and peasants become wage-hands — a new economy inside the old order

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. I §1 Changes occurring in the epoch of monopoly capitalism at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century”?
**Answer:** free competition gives way to cartels, syndicates and trusts (the Rhine-Westphalia coal syndicate holds 86.7% of Germany's coal), banks merge with industry into a financial oligarchy, capital is exported and the world is divided between the great monopolies
