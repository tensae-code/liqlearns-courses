# Bismarcks Germany and the Young Wilhelm Ii

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Bismarck's anti-socialist laws sit beside Europe's first social insurance, but Wilhelm II will share power with no one — the old chancellor resigns (1890), the SPD works legally again, and Germany, late to colonies, demands its equal place in the sun and builds the Triple Alliance

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of bismarcks germany and the young wilhelm ii — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Bismarcks Germany and the Young Wilhelm Ii. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
