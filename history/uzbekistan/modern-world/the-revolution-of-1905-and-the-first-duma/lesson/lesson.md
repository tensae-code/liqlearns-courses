# The Revolution of 1905 and the First Duma

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Plekhanov's Marxists, the RSDLP and Lenin's Bolsheviks organise while the tsar concedes nothing; on 9 January 1905 troops fire on workers marching peacefully to petition him, and the revolution that follows forces the Duma into being — Jadidism is named as Central Asia's own answer in the same pages

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of the revolution of 1905 and the first duma — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Revolution of 1905 and the First Duma. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
