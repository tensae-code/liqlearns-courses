# Video script — The Jadids and the New Method Schools

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the jadids and the new method schools — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Munavvar Qari, Behbudiy and Fitrat demand new-method schools, newspapers and a nation awake — reform, not revolt, is the Jadid wager

usul-i jadid classrooms, the 'Taraqqiy' and 'Khurshid' presses and theatre troupes carry the argument for a modern nation into every mahalla

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.
