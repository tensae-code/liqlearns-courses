# The Jadids and the New Method Schools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “Ch. 7 §32 The programme goals and tasks of the Jadids”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Munavvar Qari, Behbudiy and Fitrat demand new-method schools, newspapers and a nation awake — reform, not revolt, is the Jadid wager

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. 7 §33 The influence of Jadid ideology on the socio-economic and cultural life of the krai”?
**Answer:** usul-i jadid classrooms, the 'Taraqqiy' and 'Khurshid' presses and theatre troupes carry the argument for a modern nation into every mahalla
