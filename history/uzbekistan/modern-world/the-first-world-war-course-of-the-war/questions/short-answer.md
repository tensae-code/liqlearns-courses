# The First World War Course of the War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “40. The First World War”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Europe's empires collide in 1914; the war reaches the homeland as requisitions, prices and the 1916 call-up of Central Asians for rear labour

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. VII §37 The beginning and course of the First World War”?
**Answer:** Schlieffen's plan to crush France through Belgium before turning east collapses when Russia attacks early; at the Marne (September 1914) two million men fight and the Anglo-French line holds — the lightning war fails and the trenches begin, with Turkey bought into the German alliance
