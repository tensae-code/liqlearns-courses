# The First World War Course of the War

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — 40. The First World War

Europe's empires collide in 1914; the war reaches the homeland as requisitions, prices and the 1916 call-up of Central Asians for rear labour

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. VII §37 The beginning and course of the First World War

Schlieffen's plan to crush France through Belgium before turning east collapses when Russia attacks early; at the Marne (September 1914) two million men fight and the Anglo-French line holds — the lightning war fails and the trenches begin, with Turkey bought into the German alliance

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch. VII §38 Military actions in 1916–1917

Verdun and the Somme kill over 2.26 million men for a few kilometres — the Somme sees the first tanks, Brusilov's offensive tears the Austro-Hungarian front open and saves Italy, and Romania joins the Entente for Transylvania
