# Video script — The First World War Course of the War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of the first world war course of the war — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Europe's empires collide in 1914; the war reaches the homeland as requisitions, prices and the 1916 call-up of Central Asians for rear labour

Schlieffen's plan to crush France through Belgium before turning east collapses when Russia attacks early; at the Marne (September 1914) two million men fight and the Anglo-French line holds — the lightning war fails and the trenches begin, with Turkey bought into the German alliance

Verdun and the Somme kill over 2.26 million men for a few kilometres — the Somme sees the first tanks, Brusilov's offensive tears the Austro-Hungarian front open and saves Italy, and Romania joins the Entente for Transylvania

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
