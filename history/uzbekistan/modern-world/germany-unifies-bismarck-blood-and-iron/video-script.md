This lesson is about one thing.
The story of germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Bismarck unifies Germany by blood and iron through three wars, ending the princes' Germany of §17

Bismarck edits the Ems Dispatch to bait France into declaring war (17 July 1870); Napoleon III is trapped at Sedan and surrenders — the war that finishes German unification begins with a doctored telegram

the 1871 Constitution makes the Prussian king emperor and Bismarck, the Iron Chancellor, its real ruler for twenty years; unification creates one market, and French milliards in reparations plus Lorraine's iron fire Germany's industrial leap
