## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§27 Germany in the first half and 1860s of the 19th century (German part)** — Bismarck unifies Germany by blood and iron through three wars, ending the princes' Germany of §17
2. **Ch. II §3 The Franco-Prussian War and its results** — Bismarck edits the Ems Dispatch to bait France into declaring war (17 July 1870); Napoleon III is trapped at Sedan and surrenders — the war that finishes German unification begins with a doctored telegram
3. **Ch. II §8 Germany at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century: the formation of the Empire** — the 1871 Constitution makes the Prussian king emperor and Bismarck, the Iron Chancellor, its real ruler for twenty years; unification creates one market, and French milliards in reparations plus Lorraine's iron fire Germany's industrial leap
