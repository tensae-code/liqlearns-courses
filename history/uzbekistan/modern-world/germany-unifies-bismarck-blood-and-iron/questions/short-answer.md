# Germany Unifies Bismarck Blood and Iron — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “§27 Germany in the first half and 1860s of the 19th century (German part)”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Bismarck unifies Germany by blood and iron through three wars, ending the princes' Germany of §17

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. II §3 The Franco-Prussian War and its results”?
**Answer:** Bismarck edits the Ems Dispatch to bait France into declaring war (17 July 1870); Napoleon III is trapped at Sedan and surrenders — the war that finishes German unification begins with a doctored telegram
