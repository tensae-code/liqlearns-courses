# The Young Turks and the Ottoman Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  Retell the story of “Ch. V §34 Turkey at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** the Young Ottomans win a constitution (1876), Abdulhamid II dissolves the parliament and rules by terror for thirty years; the officers' Union and Progress society — the Young Turks — rises in the army, and in July 1908 the Monastir garrison's revolt forces the constitution back to life
