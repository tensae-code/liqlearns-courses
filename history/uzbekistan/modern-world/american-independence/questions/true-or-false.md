# American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  thirteen colonies rebel against George III, declare independence (1776) and write a republic's constitution
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.**  In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: thirteen colonies rebel against George III, declare independence (1776) and write a republic's constitution
