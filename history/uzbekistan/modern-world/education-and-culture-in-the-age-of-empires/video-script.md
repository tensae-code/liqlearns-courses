# Video script — Education and Culture in the Age of Empires

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The story of education and culture in the age of empires — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

schooling spreads but bends to nationalism and racism — segregated American schools answer with Uncle Tom's Cabin — while Nietzsche's Zarathustra, Freud's psychoanalysis and the Impressionists (Manet, Monet, Pissarro, then Cézanne, Gauguin and Van Gogh) teach Europe to see the inner world, and China's Ren Bonian and the sculptor Rodin join the same story

Watch the map as you listen: find where the story starts, follow what moves, and stop where the turn happens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, what came of it. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself.
