# Anti Colonial Resistance in Russian Turkestan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “39. The national-liberation movement against colonialism”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** from the 1892 cholera riot to the 1916 uprising against the labour draft, Turkestan answers colonial rule with revolts the empire drowns in blood

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. 3 §§20–21 The national-liberation movement in the Turkestan krai at the end of the 19th century”?
**Answer:** the 1892 Tashkent 'cholera riot' and Dukchi Ishan's Andijan rising (1898) — desperate, local, and answered with gallows
