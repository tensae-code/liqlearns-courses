# Anti Colonial Resistance in Russian Turkestan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) from the 1892 cholera riot to the 1916 uprising against the labour draft, Turkestan answers colonial rule with revolts the empire drowns in blood
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: the 1892 Tashkent 'cholera riot' and Dukchi Ishan's Andijan rising (1898) — desperate, local, and answered with gallows
