# The Paris Commune — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  Retell the story of “Ch. II §4 The state coup in France and its consequences (the Paris Commune)”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** defeated Paris refuses Thiers' Versailles government, elects the Commune Council (26 March 1871) and runs a workers' city-state — free schools, abandoned factories handed to their workers — before Versailles' army retakes the city street by street
