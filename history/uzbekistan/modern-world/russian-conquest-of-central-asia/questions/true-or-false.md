# Russian Conquest of Central Asia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Russia advances fortress by fortress from the steppe; Tashkent, Samarkand and Khiva fall despite desperate local defence, and the khanates become a colony's borderland
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: fortress lines creep down the Syr Darya; Ak-Mechet, Chimkent and Aulie-Ata fall as the empire closes on Tashkent
