# Russian Conquest of Central Asia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “37. The dream of conquering Central Asia and 38. Defence of the Homeland”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Russia advances fortress by fortress from the steppe; Tashkent, Samarkand and Khiva fall despite desperate local defence, and the khanates become a colony's borderland

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “Ch. 2 §§6–7 The beginning of the Russian Empire's conquests in Central Asia”?
**Answer:** fortress lines creep down the Syr Darya; Ak-Mechet, Chimkent and Aulie-Ata fall as the empire closes on Tashkent
