# Europes Struggle For Colonies 16Th to 18Th Centuries

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## The story

Spain, Portugal, Holland, France and England fight over America, India and the sea lanes for two centuries — colonies as the prize of European rivalry
