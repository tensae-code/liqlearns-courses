# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — 32. The development of science in Europe (16th–17th centuries) and 33. The great scientific discoveries of the 18th – early 20th centuries

from Copernicus and Galileo to steam, electricity and Darwin, European science accelerates and changes how the whole world, the homeland included, must live

## Sub lesson 2 — §10 The development of science

Copernicus, Galileo and Newton take the earth from the centre and give the age a new picture of nature

## Sub lesson 3 — §39 The development of science (17th – 1860s of the 19th century)

Darwin's evolution (1859), Pasteur's microbes and Mendeleev's table cap three centuries of science remaking the world-picture
