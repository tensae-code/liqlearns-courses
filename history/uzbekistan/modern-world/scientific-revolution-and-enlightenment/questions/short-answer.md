# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “32. The development of science in Europe (16th–17th centuries) and 33. The great scientific discoveries of the 18th – early 20th centuries”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** from Copernicus and Galileo to steam, electricity and Darwin, European science accelerates and changes how the whole world, the homeland included, must live

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “§10 The development of science”?
**Answer:** Copernicus, Galileo and Newton take the earth from the centre and give the age a new picture of nature
