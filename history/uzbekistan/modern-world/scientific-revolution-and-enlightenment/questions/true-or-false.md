# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) from Copernicus and Galileo to steam, electricity and Darwin, European science accelerates and changes how the whole world, the homeland included, must live
**Answer: True** — as taught in this lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In this lesson's story, nothing turned and nothing came of what happened.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Copernicus, Galileo and Newton take the earth from the centre and give the age a new picture of nature
