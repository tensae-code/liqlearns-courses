# Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom

## Do it first

On a blank page, draw the map of this story as you imagine it — the places, the people, the road between them. Keep the page; the story will fill it in.

## Sub lesson 1 — 8. Ancient Egypt and Babylon (Egypt part)

on the Nile a farming kingdom of pharaohs, pyramids and scribes rises while Babylon does the same between two rivers — the first states the child meets

## Sub lesson 2 — §7 The Nile valley and its inhabitants and §8 Egypt and neighbouring peoples

the Nile's flood makes one kingdom of two lands; Egypt trades and wars with Nubia, Libya and Asia from its river fortress

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of ancient egypt the nile kingdom — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: draw the story's map once from memory after you close the lesson; the places you cannot draw are the parts to read again.
