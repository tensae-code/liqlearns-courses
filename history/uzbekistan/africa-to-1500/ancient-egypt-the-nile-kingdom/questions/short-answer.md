# Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell the story of “8. Ancient Egypt and Babylon (Egypt part)”: who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** on the Nile a farming kingdom of pharaohs, pyramids and scribes rises while Babylon does the same between two rivers — the first states the child meets

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What came of the story in “§7 The Nile valley and its inhabitants and §8 Egypt and neighbouring peoples”?
**Answer:** the Nile's flood makes one kingdom of two lands; Egypt trades and wars with Nubia, Libya and Asia from its river fortress
