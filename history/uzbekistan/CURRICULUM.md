# History — Uzbekistan course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Uzbekistan inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade a book comes from appears only in ABOUT.md and each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The ladder derives from Uzbekistan's own books' progression as filed: the placements file's book order is the teaching order — the Grade 5 stories survey first (homeland, then the whole world story), the Grade 6 ancient-world course in depth, then the homeland alone in Grades 8–9 (the khanates, conquest and colonial Turkestan) with the parallel world-history books running beside it (early modern world, then the world to 1918). The levels below group that progression by the eras the books themselves present. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Uzbekistan's own history books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns, and is marked exactly "Tip — from us, not from the books". Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
