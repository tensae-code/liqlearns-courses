# Lesson plan — Trade Routes That Linked the Old World

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Trade Routes That Linked the Old World
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Spice World and the Coming of Europe (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Long before Europeans arrived, Nusantara sailors traded across Asia — Barus camphor crossed the seas, Ptolemy's map named the Barussae islands, and Borobudur's stone ships show the archipelago's own maritime world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Spice World and the Coming of Europe (recommended, never forced).
