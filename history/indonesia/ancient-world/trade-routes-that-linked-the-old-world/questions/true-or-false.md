# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ptolemy's 2nd-century map names the Barussae islands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Nusantara's trade began when European ships arrived.
**Answer: False** — Nusantara sailors traded Barus camphor across Asia long before Europeans, and Borobudur's stone ships show its own maritime world.
