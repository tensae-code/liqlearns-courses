# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What prized goods did Barus trade across Asia?
a) Camphor  b) Ptolemy's  c) On Borobudur's reliefs  d) Yes — its sailors traded across Asia long before
**Answer: a) Camphor** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Whose 2nd-century map names the Barussae islands?
a) Camphor  b) Ptolemy's  c) On Borobudur's reliefs  d) Yes — its sailors traded across Asia long before
**Answer: b) Ptolemy's** — That is what this lesson teaches.
