# Ancient Civilisations: India

## Do it first

Write the number 205. Now try to write it with no zero — only a gap. Feel how the gap breaks the number. That feeling is this lesson: why zero matters.

This short lesson is an inspiring story the method book tells inside its research chapter, and its hero is a number: zero. In India, zero was known and used long before Europe had it — not as an empty gap, but as a real number you could count and calculate with. When the idea travelled west, it changed what numbers could do: place value, big sums, and in time all of modern calculation rest on it. The book tells the story to make a point about evidence and pride alike — great ideas have homes and journeys, and one of the greatest began in Asia.

Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
