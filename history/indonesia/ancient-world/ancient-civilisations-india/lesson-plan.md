## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The number zero was known and used in India long before Europe — and when it travelled west, it made modern calculation possible.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Every price, score and phone number you use today runs on a counting system that zero makes possible — and zero's home in this story is India.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
