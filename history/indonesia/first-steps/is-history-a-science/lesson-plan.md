# Lesson plan — Is History a Science?

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Is History a Science?
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How History Works (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History broke away from the social sciences in the 19th century, then rejoined them — and used well, social theory helps the historian explain the past, not just list it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: How History Works (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History broke away from the social sciences in the 19th century, then rejoined them — and used well, social theory helps the historian explain the past, not just list it.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Women's voices in the magazine Dunia Wanita become evidence a historian can explain with social theory — a magazine turns into a window on a whole society.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
