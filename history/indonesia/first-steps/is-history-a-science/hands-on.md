# Hands-on — Is History a Science?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pick one thing in your town that changed in your lifetime — a market, a road, a phone in every hand. Write one sentence explaining the change using an idea about how society works. That explaining is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: History broke away from the social sciences in the 19th century, then rejoined them — and used well, social theory helps the historian explain the past, not just list it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
