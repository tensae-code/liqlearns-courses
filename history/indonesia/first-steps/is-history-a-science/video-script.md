# Video script — Is History a Science?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
History broke away from the social sciences in the 19th century, then rejoined them — and used well, social theory helps the historian explain the past, not just list it.

For a long time history stood among the social sciences, then in the 19th century it broke away and went its own road, proud of its own method. Later the two rejoined: historians found that ideas about how societies work could help them explain what their sources showed. The book presents Kuntowijoyo's Indonesian synthesis — history written with social theory as a tool of explanation. His cases are Indonesian: among them, women's voices in the magazine Dunia Wanita, read not as gossip but as evidence of how a society was changing. The lesson's answer is balanced: history keeps its own craft of evidence, and borrows theory to explain — neither a science in a laboratory coat, nor a story cut off from thought.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
