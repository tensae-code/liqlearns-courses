# Is History a Science? — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — History broke away from the social sciences in the 19th century, then rejoined them — and used well, social theory helps the historian explain the past, not just list it. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
