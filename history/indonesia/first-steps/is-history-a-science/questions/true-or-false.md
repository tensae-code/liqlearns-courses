# Is History a Science? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History rejoined the social sciences after breaking away from them in the 19th century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** History has never had anything to do with the social sciences.
**Answer: False** — It broke from them in the 19th century and then rejoined them; Kuntowijoyo's synthesis uses social theory to explain cases like Dunia Wanita.
