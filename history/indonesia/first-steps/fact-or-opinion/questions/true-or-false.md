# Fact or Opinion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A fact about the past is a claim that survives checking against credible references.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Once a claim is printed in a book, it no longer needs checking.
**Answer: False** — The guide treats every textbook claim as checkable and corrects wrong versions against credible references and new findings.
