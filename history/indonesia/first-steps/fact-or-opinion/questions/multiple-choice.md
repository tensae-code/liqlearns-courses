# Fact or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What must teachers hunt down among pupils?
a) Credible references and new findings  b) Straightening out misconceived concepts (meluruskan konsep yang salah kaprah)  c) Yes — every claim is treated as checkable  d) The wrong versions of events circulating among them
**Answer: d) The wrong versions of events circulating among them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Against what are wrong versions corrected?
a) Credible references and new findings  b) The wrong versions of events circulating among them  c) Straightening out misconceived concepts (meluruskan konsep yang salah kaprah)  d) Yes — every claim is treated as checkable
**Answer: a) Credible references and new findings** — That is what this lesson teaches.
