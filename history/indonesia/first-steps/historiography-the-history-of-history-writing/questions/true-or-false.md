# Historiography: The History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Colonial historiography tells the past from the coloniser's seat.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Modern historiography is palace-centred and written to glorify the ruler.
**Answer: False** — That is traditional historiography; modern historiography is written for nation-building.
