# Historiography: The History of History Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What are the three kinds of Indonesian historiography?
a) Bias  b) Traditional (palace-centred), colonial, and modern (nation-building)  c) As a fellow researcher who must keep revising the story  d) The palace — the ruler's throne
**Answer: b) Traditional (palace-centred), colonial, and modern (nation-building)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What trap must every history writer avoid?
a) Traditional (palace-centred), colonial, and modern (nation-building)  b) As a fellow researcher who must keep revising the story  c) Bias  d) The palace — the ruler's throne
**Answer: c) Bias** — That is what this lesson teaches.
