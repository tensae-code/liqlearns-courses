# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What tool pins events in time and sets them in order?
a) Chronos, meaning time  b) Change and continuity  c) Chronology  d) Bank Indonesia, from sovereignty recognition to the nationalisation of De Javasche Bank
**Answer: c) Chronology** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) From which Greek word does chronology come?
a) Chronology  b) Change and continuity  c) Bank Indonesia, from sovereignty recognition to the nationalisation of De Javasche Bank  d) Chronos, meaning time
**Answer: d) Chronos, meaning time** — That is what this lesson teaches.
