# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cutting time into periods lets historians measure change and continuity.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Chronology means guessing the order of events from their importance.
**Answer: False** — Chronology, from Greek chronos, is the historian's ordering tool — events are pinned by dates and set in order.
