# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) History is a reconstruction of real past events, not myth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) History is mainly a list of dates to memorise by heart.
**Answer: False** — The guide says the core is process — how an event happened and what it meant — explained by cause and effect.
