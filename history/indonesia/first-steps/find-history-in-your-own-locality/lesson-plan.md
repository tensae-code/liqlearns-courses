# Lesson plan — Find History in Your Own Locality

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Find History in Your Own Locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: How History Works (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History lives where you live: your own family's story, and local practices with centuries behind them — like sasi in Maluku — are real research subjects, and museums and heritage sites are primary classrooms.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: How History Works (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History lives where you live: your own family's story, and local practices with centuries behind them — like sasi in Maluku — are real research subjects, and museums and heritage sites are primary classrooms.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Sasi, the Maluku custom that closes the lompa fish harvest until the community opens it, has run since the 16th century — a pupil's own islands hold five hundred years of history.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Indonesia, as the books themselves work.
