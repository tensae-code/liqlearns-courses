# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sasi closes the lompa fish harvest until the community opens it again.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sasi is a custom invented recently for tourists.
**Answer: False** — Sasi is a Maluku custom running since the 16th century, closing the lompa harvest until the community opens it.
