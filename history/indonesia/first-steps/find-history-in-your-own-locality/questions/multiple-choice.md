# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What local practice does the book name as a research subject?
a) Since the 16th century  b) Writing your own family history  c) Museums, archaeological sites and heritage-preservation offices  d) Sasi, the Maluku custom that closes the lompa fish harvest
**Answer: d) Sasi, the Maluku custom that closes the lompa fish harvest** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Since when has sasi been practised?
a) Since the 16th century  b) Sasi, the Maluku custom that closes the lompa fish harvest  c) Writing your own family history  d) Museums, archaeological sites and heritage-preservation offices
**Answer: a) Since the 16th century** — That is what this lesson teaches.
