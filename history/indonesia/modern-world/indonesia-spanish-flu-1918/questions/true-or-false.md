# The Spanish Flu, 1918 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Spanish flu swept the Indies in 1918 along the shipping routes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The colonial state answered the flu with medicine alone and no publicity.
**Answer: False** — It printed pamphlets, campaigned in newspapers against hoaxes, and vaccinated on the rubber estates.
