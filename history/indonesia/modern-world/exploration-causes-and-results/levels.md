# Exploration: Causes and Results — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The West came east for wealth, glory and gospel — the three motives that launched the voyages — and 'found' a Nusantara that was already trading with Chinese ships; the pearl of the East became a prize to seize. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
