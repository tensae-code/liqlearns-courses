# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three motives launched the Western voyages?
a) The pearl of the East  b) Chinese ships, and Nusantara's own sailors  c) Wealth, glory and gospel  d) No — it was already a busy trading crossroads
**Answer: c) Wealth, glory and gospel** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was Nusantara called by those who hunted it?
a) Wealth, glory and gospel  b) Chinese ships, and Nusantara's own sailors  c) No — it was already a busy trading crossroads  d) The pearl of the East
**Answer: d) The pearl of the East** — That is what this lesson teaches.
