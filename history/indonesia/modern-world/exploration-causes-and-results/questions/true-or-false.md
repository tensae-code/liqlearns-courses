# Exploration: Causes and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Wealth, glory and gospel were the three motives of the voyages.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Western voyages were launched mainly for adventure and curiosity.
**Answer: False** — The book names the motives as wealth, glory and gospel — and Nusantara, already trading, became the prize to seize.
