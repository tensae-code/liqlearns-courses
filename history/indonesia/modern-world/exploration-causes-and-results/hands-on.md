# Hands-on — Exploration: Causes and Results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of something precious in your region. Write down who would want it, and what they might do to get it. That wanting — and what it causes — is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The West came east for wealth, glory and gospel — the three motives that launched the voyages — and 'found' a Nusantara that was already trading with Chinese ships; the pearl of the East became a prize to seize. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
