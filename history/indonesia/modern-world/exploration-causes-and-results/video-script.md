# Video script — Exploration: Causes and Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The West came east for wealth, glory and gospel — the three motives that launched the voyages — and 'found' a Nusantara that was already trading with Chinese ships; the pearl of the East became a prize to seize.

The Kurikulum 2013 book opens its hunt with a name: the 'pearl of the East', the prize the West crossed oceans to seize. It names the motives without decoration — wealth, glory and gospel: gold and spices to take, fame and empire to win, and a faith to spread. Those three wants launched the voyages. But the book is careful about the word 'found': the Nusantara the Western ships reached was already trading, with Chinese ships in its harbours and its own sailors on every route. Exploration, in this telling, is not discovery of an empty place. It is the arrival of hunters at a busy crossroads — and everything in the chapters after this one follows from that arrival.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
