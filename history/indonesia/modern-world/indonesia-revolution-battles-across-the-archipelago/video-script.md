# Video script — Revolution Battles Across the Archipelago

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Allies and NICA arrived, and the young Republic fought everywhere at once — Medan Area, Bandung Lautan Api, Palagan Ambarawa, Palembang, Puputan Margarana in Bali and Makassar.

When the Allies landed after Japan's surrender, the Dutch civil administration — NICA — rode in behind them, expecting to resume an empire. The Republic's answer was not one battle but a season of them, across the whole map. Medan Area became a byword for street-by-street fighting in Sumatra. Bandung's defenders made their city a sea of fire. At Palagan Ambarawa, the young army fought a set-piece battle in Central Java. Palembang rose in the south. In Bali, the Puputan Margarana carried the old island tradition of refusal into the revolution's war. Makassar fought in the east. The Grade 12 book teaches the upheavals of the early revolution as an archipelago-wide fact: the Republic survived its first year because everywhere, at once, people fought for it locally.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
