# Revolution Battles Across the Archipelago — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Palagan Ambarawa was a set-piece battle of the young army in Central Java.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The early revolution's fighting was confined to Jakarta.
**Answer: False** — It was fought everywhere at once — Medan, Bandung, Ambarawa, Palembang, Bali and Makassar.
