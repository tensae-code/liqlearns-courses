# Mohammad Hatta — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where and when was Hatta born?
a) Vice-President of the Republic  b) He handed it back  c) Integrity in public office  d) In Bukittinggi, on 12 August 1902
**Answer: d) In Bukittinggi, on 12 August 1902** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What office did he rise to?
a) Vice-President of the Republic  b) In Bukittinggi, on 12 August 1902  c) He handed it back  d) Integrity in public office
**Answer: a) Vice-President of the Republic** — That is what this lesson teaches.
