# Mohammad Hatta — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hatta was born in Bukittinggi on 12 August 1902.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Hatta used his office to enrich himself after retiring.
**Answer: False** — Offered state money as ex-vice-president, he handed it back — his life teaches integrity in public office.
