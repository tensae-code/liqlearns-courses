# Hands-on — Mohammad Hatta

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one rule you would keep even if breaking it cost you money and no one would ever know. That rule, kept, is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Born in Bukittinggi on 12 August 1902, Mohammad Hatta rose to Vice-President of the Republic — and, offered state money as an ex-vice-president, handed it back: a life used to teach integrity in public office. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
