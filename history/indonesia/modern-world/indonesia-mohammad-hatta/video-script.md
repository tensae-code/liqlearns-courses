# Video script — Mohammad Hatta

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Born in Bukittinggi on 12 August 1902, Mohammad Hatta rose to Vice-President of the Republic — and, offered state money as an ex-vice-president, handed it back: a life used to teach integrity in public office.

The second inspiring life in the method book is Mohammad Hatta's. He was born in Bukittinggi, in West Sumatra, on 12 August 1902, and rose through the movement and the exile years to become the Republic's first Vice-President, at Sukarno's side in the Proclamation. But the book does not teach his life for the office. It teaches it for a gesture: offered state money as an ex-vice-president — money that was his to take — Hatta handed it back. In a course that will soon reach the struggles over the Republic's character, the method book sets this standard early: public office is a trust, and integrity is what a leader does with money when no one could blame him for keeping it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
