# Budi Utomo and the Early Movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Budi Utomo was founded in 1908 by STOVIA students.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Budi Utomo was a military rebellion like the Java War.
**Answer: False** — It was an association founded by medical students — organisation replaced rebellion as the weapon.
