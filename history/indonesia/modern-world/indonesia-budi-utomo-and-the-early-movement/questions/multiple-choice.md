# Budi Utomo and the Early Movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who founded Budi Utomo?
a) Sarekat Islam  b) Students of STOVIA, the medical school  c) Tjipto Mangunkusumo, Douwes Dekker and Suwardi Suryaningrat  d) Organisation
**Answer: b) Students of STOVIA, the medical school** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What mass movement gathered at Kaliwungu?
a) Students of STOVIA, the medical school  b) Tjipto Mangunkusumo, Douwes Dekker and Suwardi Suryaningrat  c) Sarekat Islam  d) Organisation
**Answer: c) Sarekat Islam** — That is what this lesson teaches.
