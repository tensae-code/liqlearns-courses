# Lesson plan — Budi Utomo and the Early Movement

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Budi Utomo and the Early Movement
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Awakening: Schools, Press and Organisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Organisation replaced rebellion as the weapon: STOVIA students founded Budi Utomo, Sarekat Islam gathered the masses, and Tjipto, Douwes Dekker and Suwardi launched the Indische Partij — the movement organised in every key, from associations and unions to the PKI and Taman Siswa's schools.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Awakening: Schools, Press and Organisation (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab II.B Kemunculan Embrio Kebangsaan dan Nasionalisme Indonesia** — In 1908 the weapon changed.
2. **Bab 3.B Perjuangan Organisasi Pergerakan Kebangsaan** — The Kurikulum 2013 book hears the movement in every key at once: early associations; religious organisations; youth and women's organisations; labour unions; the PKI, the communist party; and Taman Siswa's schools, educating outside the colonial system altogether.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
