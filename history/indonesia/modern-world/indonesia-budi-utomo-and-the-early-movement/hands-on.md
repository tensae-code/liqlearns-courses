# Hands-on — Budi Utomo and the Early Movement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the agenda of a first meeting: who is invited, what is decided, what happens next week. That agenda — not a battle plan — is this lesson's weapon.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Organisation replaced rebellion as the weapon: STOVIA students founded Budi Utomo, Sarekat Islam gathered the masses, and Tjipto, Douwes Dekker and Suwardi launched the Indische Partij — the movement organised in every key, from associations and unions to the PKI and Taman Siswa's schools. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
