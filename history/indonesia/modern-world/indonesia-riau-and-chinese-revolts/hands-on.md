# Hands-on — Riau and the Chinese Revolts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a rumour spreading through a frightened city quarter: 'they are coming to ship us away.' Write what fear does to a community in one week. That fear is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Riau's people took up arms against the company, and in 1740 Batavia's Chinese community — fearing deportation after a fire was blamed on them — revolted in the Geger Pacinan. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
