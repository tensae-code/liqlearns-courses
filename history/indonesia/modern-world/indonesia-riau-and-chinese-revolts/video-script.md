# Video script — Riau and the Chinese Revolts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Riau's people took up arms against the company, and in 1740 Batavia's Chinese community — fearing deportation after a fire was blamed on them — revolted in the Geger Pacinan.

The resistance atlas widens here beyond palaces. In Riau, the island people took up arms against the company on their own shores. And in Batavia itself, the company's own capital, the Chinese community rose in 1740. The spark was fear: a fire in the city was blamed on the Chinese, and word spread that the authorities meant to deport them — to ship the community away across the sea. Fearing deportation, the community revolted: the Geger Pacinan, the Chinese tumult. The company's capital discovered that the people it ruled included whole communities who, pushed far enough, would fight inside its own walls.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
