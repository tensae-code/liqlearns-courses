# Riau and the Chinese Revolts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Geger Pacinan was Batavia's Chinese community revolting in 1740.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Batavia's Chinese community revolted over spice prices.
**Answer: False** — They revolted fearing deportation, after a fire in the city was blamed on them — the Geger Pacinan of 1740.
