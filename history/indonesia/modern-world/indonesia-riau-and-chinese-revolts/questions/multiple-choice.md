# Riau and the Chinese Revolts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who took up arms in Riau?
a) 1740  b) Deportation, after a fire was blamed on them  c) Riau's people, against the company  d) The Geger Pacinan
**Answer: c) Riau's people, against the company** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In what year did Batavia's Chinese community revolt?
a) Riau's people, against the company  b) Deportation, after a fire was blamed on them  c) The Geger Pacinan  d) 1740
**Answer: d) 1740** — That is what this lesson teaches.
