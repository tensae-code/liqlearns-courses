1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Riau's people took up arms against the company, and in 1740 Batavia's Chinese community — fearing deportation after a fire was blamed on them — revolted in the Geger Pacinan.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
