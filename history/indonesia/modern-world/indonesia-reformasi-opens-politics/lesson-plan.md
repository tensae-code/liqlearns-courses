# Lesson plan — Reformasi Opens Politics

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Reformasi Opens Politics
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Civil-service recruitment opened, state institutions were reformed, labour law tilted toward workers, regional autonomy devolved power, parties multiplied, presidents came to be directly elected, and a free press with citizen journalism replaced the licensed press.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Civil-service recruitment opened, state institutions were reformed, labour law tilted toward workers, regional autonomy devolved power, parties multiplied, presidents came to be directly elected, and a free press with citizen journalism replaced the licensed press.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Almost everything the New Order had closed was opened at once: jobs, regions, parties, ballots and newspapers — Reformasi was an unlocking of the whole state.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
