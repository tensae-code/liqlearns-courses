# Video script — Reformasi Opens Politics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Civil-service recruitment opened, state institutions were reformed, labour law tilted toward workers, regional autonomy devolved power, parties multiplied, presidents came to be directly elected, and a free press with citizen journalism replaced the licensed press.

The Reformasi chapters itemise the opening, institution by institution. Civil-service recruitment opened — state jobs by merit and advertisement, not connection. State institutions were reformed, and labour law tilted toward the workers the New Order had disciplined. Regional autonomy devolved real power from Jakarta to the regions — the centre–region wound of the 1950s answered at last by sharing the state. Parties multiplied beyond counting after decades of the three-colour fusion. Presidents came to be directly elected by the people, not chosen by an assembly. And the licensed press — newspapers that existed by government permit — was replaced by a free press, joined in time by citizen journalism: anyone could publish. Read together, the chapters describe a single event with many doors: the unlocking of Indonesia.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
