# Reformasi Opens Politics — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Civil-service recruitment opened, state institutions were reformed, labour law tilted toward workers, regional autonomy devolved power, parties multiplied, presidents came to be directly elected, and a free press with citizen journalism replaced the licensed press. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
