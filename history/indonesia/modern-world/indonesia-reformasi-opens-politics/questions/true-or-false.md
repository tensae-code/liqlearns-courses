# Reformasi Opens Politics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Parties multiplied after the three-colour fusion ended.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Reformasi kept the licensed press and the fused party system.
**Answer: False** — It replaced the licensed press with a free one, parties multiplied, and presidents came to be directly elected.
