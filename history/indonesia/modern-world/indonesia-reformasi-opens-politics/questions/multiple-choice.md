# Reformasi Opens Politics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What opened in civil-service recruitment?
a) Devolved power from Jakarta to the regions  b) Directly elected by the people  c) State jobs — by merit and open recruitment  d) A free press, with citizen journalism
**Answer: c) State jobs — by merit and open recruitment** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did regional autonomy do?
a) State jobs — by merit and open recruitment  b) Directly elected by the people  c) A free press, with citizen journalism  d) Devolved power from Jakarta to the regions
**Answer: d) Devolved power from Jakarta to the regions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
