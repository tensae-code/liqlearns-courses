# Hands-on — Reformasi Opens Politics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List five doors a state can lock: jobs, regions, parties, elections, newspapers. Write what changes when each is unlocked. That unlocking is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Civil-service recruitment opened, state institutions were reformed, labour law tilted toward workers, regional autonomy devolved power, parties multiplied, presidents came to be directly elected, and a free press with citizen journalism replaced the licensed press. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
