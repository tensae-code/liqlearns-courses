## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In the days after the Proclamation the PPKI wrote a constitution, elected Sukarno and Hatta, and built ministries and regions — a state assembled while the Japanese still held the arms depots.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A constitution, a president, ministries, provinces — assembled in days, while the defeated occupier still guarded the guns.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
