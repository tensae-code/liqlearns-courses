# Hands-on — Forming the Republic, 1945

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List what a brand-new state needs in its first week. Order your list. Then compare it with what the PPKI did, in the order it did it. That ordering is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the days after the Proclamation the PPKI wrote a constitution, elected Sukarno and Hatta, and built ministries and regions — a state assembled while the Japanese still held the arms depots. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
