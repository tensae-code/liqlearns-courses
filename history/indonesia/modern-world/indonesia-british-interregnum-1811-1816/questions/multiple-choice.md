# The British Interregnum, 1811–1816 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did Britain take Java?
a) Thomas Stamford Raffles  b) 1811  c) Five years (1811–1816)  d) The colony was handed back to the Dutch
**Answer: b) 1811** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who governed Java in the interregnum?
a) 1811  b) Five years (1811–1816)  c) Thomas Stamford Raffles  d) The colony was handed back to the Dutch
**Answer: c) Thomas Stamford Raffles** — That is what this lesson teaches.
