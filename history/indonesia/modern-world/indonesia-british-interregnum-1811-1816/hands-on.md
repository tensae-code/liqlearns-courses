# Hands-on — The British Interregnum, 1811–1816

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what changes in a town when its rulers change flags but the tax collector stays. Then what changes when the old flag returns. That swapping is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Britain took Java in 1811, and Raffles's five-year interregnum interrupted Dutch rule before the colony was handed back in 1816. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
