# The Women's Congress and the Nationalist Press — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The First Indonesian Women's Congress met in Yogyakarta in December 1928.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Indonesian women joined the movement only after independence.
**Answer: False** — They held their own national congress in 1928, and women like Ruhana Kuddus and Rasuna Said led in print and on the platform.
