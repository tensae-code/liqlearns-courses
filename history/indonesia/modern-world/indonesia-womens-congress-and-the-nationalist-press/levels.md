# The Women's Congress and the Nationalist Press — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The First Indonesian Women's Congress met in Yogyakarta on 22–25 December 1928, while Ruhana Kuddus edited Soenting Melayoe, Tirto Adhi Soerjo invented the native press, Rasuna Said rose toward parliament, and Max Havelaar still indicted the system. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
