# Lesson plan — The VOC and the Founding of Batavia

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The VOC and the Founding of Batavia
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The VOC grew from spice trader into a territorial empire: Houtman reached Banten in 1596, Keumalahayati fought the Dutch at Aceh, and the company built Batavia as its Asian capital while crushing Banda.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab I.B Perlawanan Bangsa Indonesia terhadap Kolonialisme (opening)** — The story starts with four ships: Cornelis de Houtman reached Banten in 1596, the first Dutch fleet to arrive — behind him stood the logic of the Treaty of Tordesillas, which had divided the whole world between Spain and Portugal, and which the Dutch now challenged.
2. **Bab I.B Menganalisis Kemaharajaan VOC / Keserakahan Kongsi Dagang** — The older Kurikulum 2013 book puts the company itself on trial.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The VOC grew from spice trader into a territorial empire: Houtman reached Banten in 1596, Keumalahayati fought the Dutch at Aceh, and the company built Batavia as its Asian capital while crushing Banda.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A trading company ended up with a capital city, an army and conquered islands — Batavia is where a shop turned into a state.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Indonesia, as the books themselves work.
