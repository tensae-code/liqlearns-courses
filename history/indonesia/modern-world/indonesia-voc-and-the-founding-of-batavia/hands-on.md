# Hands-on — The VOC and the Founding of Batavia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a shop. Now add, one by one, a fort, soldiers, a harbour and conquered islands around it. When does a shop become a state? That question is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The VOC grew from spice trader into a territorial empire: Houtman reached Banten in 1596, Keumalahayati fought the Dutch at Aceh, and the company built Batavia as its Asian capital while crushing Banda. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
