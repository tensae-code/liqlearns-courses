# The VOC and the Founding of Batavia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who reached Banten in 1596?
a) Admiral Keumalahayati  b) Cornelis de Houtman  c) Batavia  d) Fort Nassau's
**Answer: b) Cornelis de Houtman** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who fought the Dutch at Aceh?
a) Cornelis de Houtman  b) Batavia  c) Admiral Keumalahayati  d) Fort Nassau's
**Answer: c) Admiral Keumalahayati** — That is what this lesson teaches.
