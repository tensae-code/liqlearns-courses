# The VOC and the Founding of Batavia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The VOC built Batavia into its Asian capital.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The VOC stayed a simple trading partnership and never ruled territory.
**Answer: False** — It grew into a territorial empire — capital, monopolies and violence — as the K2013 book's trial of its greed shows.
