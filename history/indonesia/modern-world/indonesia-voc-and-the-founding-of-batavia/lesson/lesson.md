# The VOC and the Founding of Batavia

## Do it first

Draw a shop. Now add, one by one, a fort, soldiers, a harbour and conquered islands around it. When does a shop become a state? That question is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab I.B Perlawanan Bangsa Indonesia terhadap Kolonialisme (opening)

The story starts with four ships: Cornelis de Houtman reached Banten in 1596, the first Dutch fleet to arrive — behind him stood the logic of the Treaty of Tordesillas, which had divided the whole world between Spain and Portugal, and which the Dutch now challenged. The Dutch were not welcomed everywhere: at Aceh, Admiral Keumalahayati fought them at sea. But the traders organised themselves into the VOC, a chartered company with powers no shop should have — and it built Batavia on Java's coast as its Asian capital. From Batavia the company reached back to Banda and crushed the islands that would not accept its nutmeg monopoly; the ruins of Fort Nassau still stand there as the receipt.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab I.B Menganalisis Kemaharajaan VOC / Keserakahan Kongsi Dagang

The older Kurikulum 2013 book puts the company itself on trial. Its chapter analyses the VOC's empire — the keserakahan, the greed of the trading company — and how a kongsi dagang, a merchant partnership, grew into a territorial empire. The weapons were monopolies: whole islands forced to sell one spice to one buyer. The method was violence where monopoly was refused. By the end of the chapter the verdict is the lesson: the VOC was never only trading. It was ruling — and Indonesians had been fighting it, at Aceh and Banda and a dozen other coasts, from the very first years.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The VOC grew from spice trader into a territorial empire: Houtman reached Banten in 1596, Keumalahayati fought the Dutch at Aceh, and the company built Batavia as its Asian capital while crushing Banda. Give one real example: A trading company ended up with a capital city, an army and conquered islands — Batavia is where a shop turned into a state. and name the lesson's key words: Batavia, Keumalahayati. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
