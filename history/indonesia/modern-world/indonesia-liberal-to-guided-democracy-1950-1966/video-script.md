# Video script — From Liberal to Guided Democracy, 1950–1966

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Indonesia inside the Cold War constellation polarised by party and identity; centre–region imbalance spawned DI/TII, PRRI/Permesta and the RMS — and the dominoes ran to the 30 September 1965 event.

This lesson stands on the Grade 12 teacher guide's overview of the years between the revolution and the New Order. The guide sets Indonesia inside the new Cold War constellation, and watches the young democracy polarise: parties and identities hardened against each other, cabinets rose and fell, and the imbalance between the centre and the regions broke into open rebellion — DI/TII's Islamic state, Sumatra's PRRI, Sulawesi's Permesta, and the RMS in the Moluccas. Liberal democracy gave way to Sukarno's Guided Democracy, an attempt to rule the polarisation from above. The guide's image is dominoes: each instability tipping the next, running in a line to the event of 30 September 1965, where the next lessons begin.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
