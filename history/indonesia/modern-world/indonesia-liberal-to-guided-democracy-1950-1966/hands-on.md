# Hands-on — From Liberal to Guided Democracy, 1950–1966

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Line up six dominoes and label them: parties, regions, army, economy, president, parties again. Tip the first in your mind. That falling line is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Indonesia inside the Cold War constellation polarised by party and identity; centre–region imbalance spawned DI/TII, PRRI/Permesta and the RMS — and the dominoes ran to the 30 September 1965 event. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
