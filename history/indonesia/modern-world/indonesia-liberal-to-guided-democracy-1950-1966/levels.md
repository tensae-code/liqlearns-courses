# From Liberal to Guided Democracy, 1950–1966 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Indonesia inside the Cold War constellation polarised by party and identity; centre–region imbalance spawned DI/TII, PRRI/Permesta and the RMS — and the dominoes ran to the 30 September 1965 event. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
