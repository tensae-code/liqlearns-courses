# Diplomacy and Guerrilla War, 1946–1949 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Linggarjati (1946) recognise?
a) On an American warship  b) General Sudirman  c) The Round Table Conference  d) The Republic over Java, Madura and Sumatra
**Answer: d) The Republic over Java, Madura and Sumatra** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where was Renville (1948) signed?
a) On an American warship  b) The Republic over Java, Madura and Sumatra  c) General Sudirman  d) The Round Table Conference
