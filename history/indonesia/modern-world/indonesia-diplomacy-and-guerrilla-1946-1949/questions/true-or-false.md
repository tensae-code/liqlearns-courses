# Diplomacy and Guerrilla War, 1946–1949 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dutch aggressions broke both Linggarjati and Renville.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Dutch honoured Linggarjati and withdrew in 1946.
**Answer: False** — They broke it with a 'police action', and broke Renville too; sovereignty came only in 1949, after guerrilla war and world pressure.
