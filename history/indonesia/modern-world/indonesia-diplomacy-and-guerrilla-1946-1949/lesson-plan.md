## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: At the table, Linggarjati (1946) and Renville (1948) shrank the Republic while Dutch aggressions broke them; the answer was Sudirman's guerrilla war plus Roem–Royen and the Round Table Conference, which finally transferred sovereignty.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Every agreement the Dutch signed, they broke with an army — so the Republic fought with the other hand, and negotiated with the smoking one, until the world made the Dutch sign for real.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
