# Video script — The Aceh War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Aceh answered the Dutch invasion with jihad and fought for thirty years — the longest and costliest of all the colonial wars.

The last and greatest of the atlas's wars is Aceh's. When the Dutch invaded, Aceh answered with jihad — a holy war proclaimed in defence of land and faith — and fought for thirty years. Thirty years made it the longest and costliest of all the colonial wars: costliest in Dutch money and soldiers' lives, and in Acehnese suffering beyond counting. Empires changed their generals, their tactics, their whole approach, and still the war ran on, village by village, valley by valley. The atlas ends its resistance survey here for a reason: after Aceh, no one could say the archipelago had been taken easily, or quickly, or cheaply. It had been resisted at every step — and nowhere longer than at its western gate.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
