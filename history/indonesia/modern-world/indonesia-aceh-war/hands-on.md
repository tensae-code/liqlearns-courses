# Hands-on — The Aceh War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a timeline of your own life, then stretch it to thirty years of war. What does a war longer than a childhood do to a people? That weight is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Aceh answered the Dutch invasion with jihad and fought for thirty years — the longest and costliest of all the colonial wars. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
