# The People of the Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did women run for the fighters?
a) Public kitchens (and they stood on the front line too)  b) The revolution's artists and writers  c) Student militias — school students who left class to fight  d) The revolution's ordinary people — women, artists, students
**Answer: a) Public kitchens (and they stood on the front line too)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who were the Generation of '45?
a) Public kitchens (and they stood on the front line too)  b) The revolution's artists and writers  c) Student militias — school students who left class to fight  d) The revolution's ordinary people — women, artists, students
**Answer: b) The revolution's artists and writers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
