# The People of the Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Women served in public kitchens and on the front line.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Only trained soldiers took part in the revolution.
**Answer: False** — Women, the Generation of '45's artists and writers, and student militias all made it — a whole people defended the Republic.
