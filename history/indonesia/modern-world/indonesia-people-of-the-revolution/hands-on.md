# Hands-on — The People of the Revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List three jobs a revolution needs besides soldiers. For each, write who did it in your own town's terms. That list is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The revolution's other armies: women in public kitchens and on the front line, artists and writers of the Generation of '45, and school students who left class for the laskar pelajar. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
