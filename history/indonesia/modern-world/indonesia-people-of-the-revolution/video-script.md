# Video script — The People of the Revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The revolution's other armies: women in public kitchens and on the front line, artists and writers of the Generation of '45, and school students who left class for the laskar pelajar.

The Grade 12 book closes its revolution with the people the battle chapters leave out. Women ran the public kitchens that fed the fighters — an army marches on what those kitchens cooked — and women also stood on the front line itself. Artists and writers fought with the revolution's other weapons: the Generation of '45, the poets and authors who gave the struggle its language and its conscience while it was still being fought. And school students left their classrooms for the laskar pelajar, the student militias — children who decided their schooling could wait and their country could not. The chapter's argument is the level's completion: the Republic was defended by a whole people, and its history belongs to all of them.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
