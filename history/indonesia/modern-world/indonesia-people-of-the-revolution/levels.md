# The People of the Revolution — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The revolution's other armies: women in public kitchens and on the front line, artists and writers of the Generation of '45, and school students who left class for the laskar pelajar. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
