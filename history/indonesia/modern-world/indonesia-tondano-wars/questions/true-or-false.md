# The Tondano Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Minahasa people of Tondano fought the colonial state in North Sulawesi.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Tondano's people welcomed colonial rule without a fight.
**Answer: False** — They fought the colonial state in a long, little-told war around Lake Tondano.
