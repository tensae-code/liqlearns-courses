# The Tondano Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who fought the Tondano wars?
a) In North Sulawesi, around Lake Tondano  b) The colonial state  c) The Minahasa people of Tondano  d) Long, and little-told beside the famous wars
**Answer: c) The Minahasa people of Tondano** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where is Tondano?
a) The Minahasa people of Tondano  b) The colonial state  c) Long, and little-told beside the famous wars  d) In North Sulawesi, around Lake Tondano
**Answer: d) In North Sulawesi, around Lake Tondano** — That is what this lesson teaches.
