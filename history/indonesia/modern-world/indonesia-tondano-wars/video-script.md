# Video script — The Tondano Wars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Minahasa people of Tondano, in North Sulawesi, fought the colonial state in a long, little-told war.

Not every war in the atlas is famous, and the book keeps this one on purpose. Around Lake Tondano in North Sulawesi, the Minahasa people fought the colonial state in a long war — little-told beside Java's great campaigns, but no shorter in courage or cost for the people who lived it. The Tondano wars stand in the atlas as proof of the pattern's reach: resistance was not a Javanese affair, nor only sultans' work. In the far north of Sulawesi, farming people around a lake took on the colonial state and kept fighting. The atlas restores their war to the national story, where it belongs.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
