# Hands-on — Reformasi: Development and Disasters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two report cards for a government: building, and disaster response. Give each a comment from this lesson. That judging is the lesson's work.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Reformasi-era governments built transport infrastructure to the border regions and digital and social infrastructure at home, while the era's disasters tested the new state's capacity to respond. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
