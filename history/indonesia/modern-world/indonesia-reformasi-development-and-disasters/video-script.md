# Video script — Reformasi: Development and Disasters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Reformasi-era governments built transport infrastructure to the border regions and digital and social infrastructure at home, while the era's disasters tested the new state's capacity to respond.

The last Reformasi chapters judge the open state on its works. Reformasi-era governments built: transport infrastructure pushed out to the border regions, binding the archipelago's edges to its centre with roads and ports; and at home, digital and social infrastructure — the networks and services of a connected society. Against the building stands the era's other test: disasters. Earthquakes, tsunamis, eruptions and floods struck the Reformasi decades, and each one examined the new state's capacity to respond — its speed, its honesty, its reach into the regions autonomy had empowered. The book closes the national story there: a state opened in 1998, still being graded — by its building, and by its answering — in the pupils' own lifetime.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
