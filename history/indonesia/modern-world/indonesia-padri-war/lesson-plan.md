# Lesson plan — The Padri War

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Padri War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In West Sumatra the Padri movement's religious war became a war against the Dutch: a civil war over faith drew the colonial army in, and Imam Bonjol's Padris ended fighting the Dutch instead.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab I.B** — In West Sumatra, the Padri movement — religious reformers demanding a stricter Islam — went to war with the defenders of the old customs.
2. **Bab 2.B.3 Perang Padri** — The Kurikulum 2013 book tells the turn plainly: West Sumatra's religious civil war draws the Dutch in, and Imam Bonjol's Padris end fighting the colonial army instead of their neighbours.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In West Sumatra the Padri movement's religious war became a war against the Dutch: a civil war over faith drew the colonial army in, and Imam Bonjol's Padris ended fighting the Dutch instead.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A war that began as an argument about religion inside one people ended as a war against a foreign army — the Padri War changed its enemy mid-course.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
