# Hands-on — The Padri War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two columns: what a community argues about among itself, and what happens when an outside power joins one side. Watch the argument change. That change is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In West Sumatra the Padri movement's religious war became a war against the Dutch: a civil war over faith drew the colonial army in, and Imam Bonjol's Padris ended fighting the Dutch instead. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
