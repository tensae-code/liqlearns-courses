# The Padri War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where was the Padri War fought?
a) In West Sumatra (the Minangkabau highlands)  b) Imam Bonjol  c) A war against the Dutch  d) Justus Pieter de Veer, a Dutch officer-illustrator
**Answer: a) In West Sumatra (the Minangkabau highlands)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who led the Padris?
a) In West Sumatra (the Minangkabau highlands)  b) Imam Bonjol  c) A war against the Dutch  d) Justus Pieter de Veer, a Dutch officer-illustrator
**Answer: b) Imam Bonjol** — That is what this lesson teaches.
