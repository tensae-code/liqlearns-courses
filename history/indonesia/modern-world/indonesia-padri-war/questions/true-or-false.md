# The Padri War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Padri War began as a religious civil war and became a war against the Dutch.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Padris fought only their neighbours and never the Dutch.
**Answer: False** — Once the Dutch were drawn in, Imam Bonjol's Padris ended fighting the colonial army instead.
