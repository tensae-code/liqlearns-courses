# Hands-on — Asian Networks Awaken Nationalism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw lines on a map from your town to three far places ideas could arrive from. Label each line with what travels on it. That web is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Hajj pilgrims in batik sarongs, Nawawi al-Bantani's Qur'an commentary written in Mecca, Gandhi, Sun Yat-sen and Rizal, and Indonesian students founding Perhimpunan Indonesia in the Netherlands wired the archipelago into Asia's anti-colonial awakening. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
