# Asian Networks Awaken Nationalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Indonesian students founded Perhimpunan Indonesia in the Netherlands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Indonesian nationalism developed in isolation from the rest of Asia.
**Answer: False** — It was wired into Asia's awakening through pilgrims, scholars like Nawawi al-Bantani, students, and the examples of Gandhi, Sun Yat-sen and Rizal.
