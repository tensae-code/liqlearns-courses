# Lesson plan — Asian Networks Awaken Nationalism

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Asian Networks Awaken Nationalism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Awakening: Schools, Press and Organisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Hajj pilgrims in batik sarongs, Nawawi al-Bantani's Qur'an commentary written in Mecca, Gandhi, Sun Yat-sen and Rizal, and Indonesian students founding Perhimpunan Indonesia in the Netherlands wired the archipelago into Asia's anti-colonial awakening.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Awakening: Schools, Press and Organisation (recommended, never forced).
