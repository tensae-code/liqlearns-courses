# Hands-on — Indonesia Under Japanese Occupation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Make two lists: what the occupier promised, and what the occupier took. Compare their lengths. That comparison is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Occupation meant propaganda posters and Asia Raya newspapers, internment camps where Dutch women bowed to the Emperor, occupation money, Fujinkai women's labour, the Selokan Mataram works and romusa forced labour. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
