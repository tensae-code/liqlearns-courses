# Indonesia Under Japanese Occupation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What newspapers preached Japan's message?
a) Internees — Dutch women among them  b) The organisation of women's labour under the occupation  c) Forced labour, as on the Selokan Mataram works  d) The Asia Raya newspapers
**Answer: d) The Asia Raya newspapers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who were made to bow to the Emperor in the camps?
a) Internees — Dutch women among them  b) The Asia Raya newspapers  c) The organisation of women's labour under the occupation  d) Forced labour, as on the Selokan Mataram works
**Answer: a) Internees — Dutch women among them** — That is what this lesson teaches.
