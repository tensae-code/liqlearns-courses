# Hands-on — Daendels and the Great Post Road

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Trace Java's north coast on a map from Anyer to Panarukan. Now write what it costs to build that road in a year, and who pays. That bill is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Governor-General Daendels (1808–1811), sent by the Batavian Republic to hold Java against Britain, quoted the French Revolution and ruled as a dictator — driving the Anyer–Panarukan Great Post Road out of the people by kerja rodi forced labour. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
