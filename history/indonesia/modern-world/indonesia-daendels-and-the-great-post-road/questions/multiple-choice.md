# Daendels and the Great Post Road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who was Daendels?
a) The Governor-General sent by the Batavian Republic (1808–1811)  b) The Great Post Road, from Anyer to Panarukan  c) Kerja rodi — forced labour  d) He quoted the French Revolution, and ruled as a dictator
**Answer: a) The Governor-General sent by the Batavian Republic (1808–1811)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What road did he drive across Java?
a) The Governor-General sent by the Batavian Republic (1808–1811)  b) The Great Post Road, from Anyer to Panarukan  c) Kerja rodi — forced labour  d) He quoted the French Revolution, and ruled as a dictator
**Answer: b) The Great Post Road, from Anyer to Panarukan** — That is what this lesson teaches.
