# Daendels and the Great Post Road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Great Post Road ran from Anyer to Panarukan along Java's north coast.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Great Post Road was built by paid volunteers at a gentle pace.
**Answer: False** — It was driven out of the people by kerja rodi forced labour under Daendels' dictatorship.
