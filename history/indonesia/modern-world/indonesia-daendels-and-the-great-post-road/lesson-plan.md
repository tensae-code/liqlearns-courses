# Lesson plan — Daendels and the Great Post Road

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Governor-General Daendels (1808–1811), sent by the Batavian Republic to hold Java against Britain, quoted the French Revolution and ruled as a dictator — driving the Anyer–Panarukan Great Post Road out of the people by kerja rodi forced labour.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A road a thousand kilometres long, built in a year by forced labour — the Great Post Road is Daendels' monument and his indictment in one.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
