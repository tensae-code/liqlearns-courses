## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Shamed by the poverty colonialism created, the Netherlands proclaimed an Ethical Policy of irrigation, emigration and education — and the schools it funded bred its own opposition.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: The empire announced it would repay its debt to the colony — and paid part of the bill in schools, which taught the colony to demand the rest.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
