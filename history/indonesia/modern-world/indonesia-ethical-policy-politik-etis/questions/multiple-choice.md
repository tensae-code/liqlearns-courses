# The Ethical Policy: Politik Etis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three arms did the Ethical Policy have?
a) Out of shame at the poverty colonialism had created  b) The empire's own opposition  c) Irrigation, emigration and education  d) The Ethical Policy
**Answer: c) Irrigation, emigration and education** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why was the policy proclaimed?
a) Irrigation, emigration and education  b) The empire's own opposition  c) The Ethical Policy  d) Out of shame at the poverty colonialism had created
**Answer: d) Out of shame at the poverty colonialism had created** — That is what this lesson teaches.
