# Hands-on — The Ethical Policy: Politik Etis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a promise a powerful person makes to people they have wronged. Then write what those people learn by watching whether it is kept. That watching is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Shamed by the poverty colonialism created, the Netherlands proclaimed an Ethical Policy of irrigation, emigration and education — and the schools it funded bred its own opposition. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
