# The Ethical Policy: Politik Etis

## Do it first

Write a promise a powerful person makes to people they have wronged. Then write what those people learn by watching whether it is kept. That watching is this lesson.

By 1901 the Netherlands had a guilty conscience, and the Kurikulum 2013 book tells why: the poverty colonialism had created in the Indies shamed the coloniser at home. The answer proclaimed was the Ethical Policy — Politik Etis — a promise that the empire owed its colony a debt of honour. Its programme had three arms: irrigation, to water the fields; emigration, to move people from crowded Java to emptier islands; and education, to school the colony's children. The book draws the irony without blinking: the schools the policy funded bred the empire's own opposition. The ethical gift educated the generation that judged the giver — and found the gift far smaller than the debt.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Shamed by the poverty colonialism created, the Netherlands proclaimed an Ethical Policy of irrigation, emigration and education — and the schools it funded bred its own opposition. Give one real example: The empire announced it would repay its debt to the colony — and paid part of the bill in schools, which taught the colony to demand the rest. and name the lesson's key words: Politik Etis, Emigration. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
