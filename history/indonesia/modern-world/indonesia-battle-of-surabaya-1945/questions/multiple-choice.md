# The Battle of Surabaya, 1945 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Whose death preceded the ultimatum?
a) That Surabaya surrender its weapons  b) The city's youth and its ulama  c) Brigadier Mallaby's  d) Heroes' Day
**Answer: c) Brigadier Mallaby's** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the British ultimatum demand?
a) Brigadier Mallaby's  b) The city's youth and its ulama  c) Heroes' Day  d) That Surabaya surrender its weapons
**Answer: d) That Surabaya surrender its weapons** — That is what this lesson teaches.
