# The Battle of Surabaya, 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Surabaya fought the British on 10 November 1945.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Surabaya obeyed the ultimatum and handed over its weapons.
**Answer: False** — Its youth and ulama refused, and fought the 10 November 1945 battle remembered as Heroes' Day.
