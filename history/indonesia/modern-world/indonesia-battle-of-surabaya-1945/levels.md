# The Battle of Surabaya, 1945 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After Brigadier Mallaby's death, Surabaya's youth and ulama refused the British ultimatum and fought the 10 November 1945 battle that became Heroes' Day. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
