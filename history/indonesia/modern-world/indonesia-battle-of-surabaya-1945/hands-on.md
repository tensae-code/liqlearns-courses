# Hands-on — The Battle of Surabaya, 1945

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the ultimatum as a note: hand over your weapons by morning. Then write the city's answer, as its youth and religious teachers framed it. That answer is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After Brigadier Mallaby's death, Surabaya's youth and ulama refused the British ultimatum and fought the 10 November 1945 battle that became Heroes' Day. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
