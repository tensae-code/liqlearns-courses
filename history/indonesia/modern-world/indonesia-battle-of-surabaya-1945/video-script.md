# Video script — The Battle of Surabaya, 1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
After Brigadier Mallaby's death, Surabaya's youth and ulama refused the British ultimatum and fought the 10 November 1945 battle that became Heroes' Day.

Surabaya's battle grew from a death and an ultimatum. Brigadier Mallaby, the British commander in the city, was killed in the confused fighting of late October 1945. The British answer was an ultimatum: Surabaya must surrender its weapons. The city's answer was made by its youth and its ulama — the religious teachers whose call to defend the proclaimed Republic turned refusal into duty. On 10 November 1945 the British attacked with ships, planes and tanks, and Surabaya fought. The city could not win against that weight, and did not; but the stand fixed itself in the nation's memory as the revolution's purest proof of will. Indonesia keeps 10 November as Heroes' Day — the battle's date, not a victory's.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
