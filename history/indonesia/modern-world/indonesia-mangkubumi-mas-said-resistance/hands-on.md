# Hands-on — Mangkubumi and Mas Said Resist

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what an insult at court can do when the insulted man can raise an army. List who joins him and why. That gathering is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Insulted by VOC interference at the Surakarta court, Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said raised 13,000 men from Sukowati and fought the company and the Sunan together. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
