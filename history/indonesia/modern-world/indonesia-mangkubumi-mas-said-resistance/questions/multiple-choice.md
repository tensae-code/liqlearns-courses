# Mangkubumi and Mas Said Resist — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who raised the army from Sukowati?
a) 13,000  b) The VOC and the Sunan together  c) VOC interference and insult at the Surakarta court  d) Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said
**Answer: d) Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How many men did they raise?
a) 13,000  b) Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said  c) The VOC and the Sunan together  d) VOC interference and insult at the Surakarta court
**Answer: a) 13,000** — That is what this lesson teaches.
