# Mangkubumi and Mas Said Resist — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mangkubumi and Mas Said raised 13,000 men from Sukowati.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Mangkubumi and Mas Said helped the VOC control the Surakarta court.
**Answer: False** — Insulted by VOC interference, they rebelled and fought the company and the Sunan together.
