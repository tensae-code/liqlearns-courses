# Mangkubumi and Mas Said Resist

## Do it first

Write what an insult at court can do when the insulted man can raise an army. List who joins him and why. That gathering is this lesson.

At the Surakarta court, the VOC's interference finally insulted the wrong men. Prince Mangkubumi — a prince of the blood — and his son-in-law Mas Said walked out of the palace world and into rebellion. From Sukowati they raised an army the book numbers at 13,000 men, and they fought on two fronts at once: against the company, and against the Sunan, their own ruler, who stood with the company. Their war is the atlas's lesson in what interference breeds: the company reached into a court's inner quarrels to control it, and instead produced the most dangerous enemies it faced in Java — princes who knew the court, the country and the company's methods from the inside.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Insulted by VOC interference at the Surakarta court, Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said raised 13,000 men from Sukowati and fought the company and the Sunan together. Give one real example: Two princes, insulted in their own palace, walked out and built an army — and Java's map was redrawn by their war. and name the lesson's key words: Mas Said, Sunan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
