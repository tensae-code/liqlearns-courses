# Video script — Mangkubumi and Mas Said Resist

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Insulted by VOC interference at the Surakarta court, Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said raised 13,000 men from Sukowati and fought the company and the Sunan together.

At the Surakarta court, the VOC's interference finally insulted the wrong men. Prince Mangkubumi — a prince of the blood — and his son-in-law Mas Said walked out of the palace world and into rebellion. From Sukowati they raised an army the book numbers at 13,000 men, and they fought on two fronts at once: against the company, and against the Sunan, their own ruler, who stood with the company. Their war is the atlas's lesson in what interference breeds: the company reached into a court's inner quarrels to control it, and instead produced the most dangerous enemies it faced in Java — princes who knew the court, the country and the company's methods from the inside.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
