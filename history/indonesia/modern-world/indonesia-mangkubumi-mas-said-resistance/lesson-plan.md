## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Insulted by VOC interference at the Surakarta court, Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said raised 13,000 men from Sukowati and fought the company and the Sunan together.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Two princes, insulted in their own palace, walked out and built an army — and Java's map was redrawn by their war.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
