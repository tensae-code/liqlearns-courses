# Ki Hadjar Dewantara — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ki Hadjar Dewantara founded Taman Siswa, a national education under colonial rule.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ki Hadjar kept his title and served the colonial school system.
**Answer: False** — He dropped his noble title and founded Taman Siswa, building national education outside the colonial system.
