# Ki Hadjar Dewantara — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Ki Hadjar Dewantara give up?
a) Taman Siswa — the Garden of Learners  b) Cipto Mangunkusumo and Douwes Dekker  c) His noble title (he was born Suwardi Suryaningrat)  d) 'Nothing but a happy heart'
**Answer: c) His noble title (he was born Suwardi Suryaningrat)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What schools did he found?
a) His noble title (he was born Suwardi Suryaningrat)  b) Cipto Mangunkusumo and Douwes Dekker  c) 'Nothing but a happy heart'  d) Taman Siswa — the Garden of Learners
**Answer: d) Taman Siswa — the Garden of Learners** — That is what this lesson teaches.
