# Hands-on — Ki Hadjar Dewantara

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what you would teach first if you opened a school for people told they were born to obey. Your first lesson plan is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Javanese aristocrat who dropped his noble title, founded Taman Siswa, and with Cipto Mangunkusumo and Douwes Dekker from 1912 built national education under colonial rule — choosing 'nothing but a happy heart' over wealth. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
