# Lesson plan — Ki Hadjar Dewantara

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Ki Hadjar Dewantara
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Awakening: Schools, Press and Organisation (CURRICULUM.md).
