# Perhimpunan Indonesia and the 1925 Manifesto — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Perhimpunan Indonesia issue in 1925?
a) Indonesia — not the Indies  b) In the Netherlands  c) A Political Manifesto  d) They carried it home
**Answer: c) A Political Manifesto** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What name did the manifesto give the nation?
a) A Political Manifesto  b) In the Netherlands  c) They carried it home  d) Indonesia — not the Indies
**Answer: d) Indonesia — not the Indies** — That is what this lesson teaches.
