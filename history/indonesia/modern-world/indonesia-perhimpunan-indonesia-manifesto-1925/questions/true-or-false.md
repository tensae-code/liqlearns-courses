# Perhimpunan Indonesia and the 1925 Manifesto — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1925 manifesto named Indonesia, not the Indies, as the nation to be freed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The 1925 manifesto asked for reforms inside the Dutch Indies under that name.
**Answer: False** — It named a new nation — Indonesia — to be freed, and students carried the word home.
