# Hands-on — Perhimpunan Indonesia and the 1925 Manifesto

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two names for the same place: the name an outsider gave it, and the name its own people choose. Say what changes when the second name is used. That change is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Students in the Netherlands issued the 1925 Political Manifesto, naming Indonesia — not the Indies — as the nation to be freed, and carried the word home. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
