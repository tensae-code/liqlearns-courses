1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Students in the Netherlands issued the 1925 Political Manifesto, naming Indonesia — not the Indies — as the nation to be freed, and carried the word home.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: One word did the work of an army: the students stopped saying 'the Indies', the coloniser's name, and named the nation Indonesia.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
