# Video script — Perhimpunan Indonesia and the 1925 Manifesto

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Students in the Netherlands issued the 1925 Political Manifesto, naming Indonesia — not the Indies — as the nation to be freed, and carried the word home.

In the Netherlands, the coloniser's own capital, Indonesian students organised in Perhimpunan Indonesia — and in 1925 they issued a Political Manifesto that did one enormous thing with words. It named the nation. Not 'the Indies', the administrative label of the colony; not a list of islands and peoples; but Indonesia — one nation, to be freed as one. A manifesto is paper, but the paper carried the word home on the student ships, and the word reorganised the movement: every association, every congress after 1925 could now say what it was for in a single name. The Youth Pledge of 1928, three years later, pledges exactly this name.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
