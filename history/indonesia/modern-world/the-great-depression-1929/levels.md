# The Great Depression, 1929 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Chicago's unemployed queued at a free soup kitchen in 1931 while the Depression gutted Indies export prices — and Hitler's rise in Europe signalled the war that would end Dutch rule. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
