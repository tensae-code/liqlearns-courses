# Lesson plan — The Banten War and the 1684 Treaty

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Banten War and the 1684 Treaty
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Banten tore itself apart in the 1682–84 war between Sultan Ageng and his son Sultan Haji — and Haji's treaty with the VOC on 17 April 1684 handed the company the kingdom.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab I.B** — Banten, the pepper sultanate of Java's western end, was not conquered from the sea.
2. **Bab 2.A.4 Perlawanan Banten** — The Kurikulum 2013 atlas tells the same fall in its resistance order: Banten resisted company encroachment for years — and then succession war and treaty together delivered it to the VOC.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Banten tore itself apart in the 1682–84 war between Sultan Ageng and his son Sultan Haji — and Haji's treaty with the VOC on 17 April 1684 handed the company the kingdom.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A father and a son fought for one throne, and the company collected the kingdom as the price of helping the winner.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Indonesia, as the books themselves work.
