# Hands-on — The Banten War and the 1684 Treaty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a short scene: two claimants to one throne, and a powerful neighbour offering help to one of them. What should the neighbour's help cost? That price is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Banten tore itself apart in the 1682–84 war between Sultan Ageng and his son Sultan Haji — and Haji's treaty with the VOC on 17 April 1684 handed the company the kingdom. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
