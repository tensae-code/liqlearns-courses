# The Banten War and the 1684 Treaty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who fought whom in Banten's 1682–84 war?
a) Sultan Ageng against his son Sultan Haji  b) A treaty on 17 April 1684  c) The kingdom — Banten's trade and freedom of action  d) No — it was delivered by succession war and treaty
**Answer: a) Sultan Ageng against his son Sultan Haji** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What did Haji sign with the VOC, and when?
a) Sultan Ageng against his son Sultan Haji  b) A treaty on 17 April 1684  c) The kingdom — Banten's trade and freedom of action  d) No — it was delivered by succession war and treaty
**Answer: b) A treaty on 17 April 1684** — That is what this lesson teaches.
