# The Banten War and the 1684 Treaty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sultan Haji's treaty of 17 April 1684 handed Banten to the VOC.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Banten fell when Dutch troops stormed its palace in battle.
**Answer: False** — Banten tore itself apart in the Ageng–Haji succession war, and Haji's 1684 treaty handed the company the kingdom.
