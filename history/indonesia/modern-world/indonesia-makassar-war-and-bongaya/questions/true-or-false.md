# The Makassar War and Bongaya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Treaty of Bongaya opened South Sulawesi to company rule.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Makassar won the war and expelled the VOC from the east.
**Answer: False** — Makassar held out until 1669, then the imposed Treaty of Bongaya opened South Sulawesi to company rule.
