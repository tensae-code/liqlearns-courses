# The Fall of Suharto, May 1998 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Suharto resigned on 21 May 1998.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Suharto was overthrown in a military coup in 1998.
**Answer: False** — After the crisis, the Trisakti shootings and Jakarta's burning, he read his resignation at the State Palace on 21 May 1998.
