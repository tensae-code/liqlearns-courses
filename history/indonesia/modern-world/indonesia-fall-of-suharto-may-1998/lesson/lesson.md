# The Fall of Suharto, May 1998

## Do it first

Write the timetable of a regime's last week: crisis, protest, tragedy, fire, resignation. Mark the hour it ends. That timetable is this lesson.

The end came fast, and the book times it. The Asian monetary crisis struck first: the currency collapsed, prices soared, and the shops emptied — the New Order's founding promise, prosperity, failed in a season. Students filled the streets, and at Trisakti University they were shot — students killed by the state they were petitioning, the era's moral breaking point. Jakarta burned in riots. And then the timetable stops: at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998, in the Istana Negara, the State Palace, Suharto read his resignation. The man who had taken power through a controversial letter in 1966 surrendered it in a short speech thirty-two years later — and the crowd's era, Reformasi, began in the same hour.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Asian monetary crisis emptied the shops, Trisakti students were shot, Jakarta burned — and at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998, in the Istana Negara, Suharto read his resignation. Give one real example: Thirty-two years ended at nine o'clock on a Thursday morning, with one short speech — after a crisis, a shooting and a burning capital made the order untenable. and name the lesson's key words: Monetary crisis, Istana Negara. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
