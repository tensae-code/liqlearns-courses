# Video script — The Fall of Suharto, May 1998

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Asian monetary crisis emptied the shops, Trisakti students were shot, Jakarta burned — and at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998, in the Istana Negara, Suharto read his resignation.

The end came fast, and the book times it. The Asian monetary crisis struck first: the currency collapsed, prices soared, and the shops emptied — the New Order's founding promise, prosperity, failed in a season. Students filled the streets, and at Trisakti University they were shot — students killed by the state they were petitioning, the era's moral breaking point. Jakarta burned in riots. And then the timetable stops: at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998, in the Istana Negara, the State Palace, Suharto read his resignation. The man who had taken power through a controversial letter in 1966 surrendered it in a short speech thirty-two years later — and the crowd's era, Reformasi, began in the same hour.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
