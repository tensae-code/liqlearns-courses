# Hands-on — The Fall of Suharto, May 1998

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the timetable of a regime's last week: crisis, protest, tragedy, fire, resignation. Mark the hour it ends. That timetable is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Asian monetary crisis emptied the shops, Trisakti students were shot, Jakarta burned — and at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998, in the Istana Negara, Suharto read his resignation. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
