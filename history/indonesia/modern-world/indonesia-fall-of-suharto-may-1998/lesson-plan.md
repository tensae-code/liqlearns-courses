## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Asian monetary crisis emptied the shops, Trisakti students were shot, Jakarta burned — and at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998, in the Istana Negara, Suharto read his resignation.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Thirty-two years ended at nine o'clock on a Thursday morning, with one short speech — after a crisis, a shooting and a burning capital made the order untenable.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
