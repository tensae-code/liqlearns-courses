# Hands-on — Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find Constantinople and Malacca on a map. Draw the land road that was closed, then the sea road that replaced it. That rerouting is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: When Constantinople fell and the spice roads closed, Portugal answered at sea — da Gama's route and Albuquerque's seizure of Malacca brought the European age to Nusantara's door. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
