# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Constantinople fell in 1453, closing the old spice roads.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Portugal took Malacca before Constantinople fell.
**Answer: False** — Constantinople fell in 1453 first; Portugal's sea answer came after, ending with Albuquerque's seizure of Malacca.
