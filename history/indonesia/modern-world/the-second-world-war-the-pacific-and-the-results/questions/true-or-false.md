# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Dutch East Indies surrendered unconditionally at Kalijati in March 1942.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Dutch East Indies surrendered to Indonesian forces in 1942.
**Answer: False** — It surrendered to Japan at Kalijati, after Japan struck for resources like Tarakan's oil.
