# Hands-on — The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write how long three hundred years is in generations. Then write how many weeks it took to end. The contrast is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Japan sold its Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere, struck Tarakan's oil, and at Kalijati airfield in March 1942 received the unconditional surrender of the Dutch East Indies from Ter Poorten and Governor-General Tjarda. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
