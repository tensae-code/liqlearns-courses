# The Revolution, 1945–1950: Defending Independence — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The 1945–1950 revolution was state-forming under fire: a government built in weeks, early-revolution upheavals, diplomacy and guerrilla war side by side — Bandung Lautan Api, the Linggarjati talks — on the road from RIS back to the unitary NKRI. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
