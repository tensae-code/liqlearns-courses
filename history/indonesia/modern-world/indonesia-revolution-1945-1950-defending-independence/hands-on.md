# Hands-on — The Revolution, 1945–1950: Defending Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two diaries for the same month of 1946: one of an official building a ministry, one of a fighter in the hills. Where do they meet? That meeting is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The 1945–1950 revolution was state-forming under fire: a government built in weeks, early-revolution upheavals, diplomacy and guerrilla war side by side — Bandung Lautan Api, the Linggarjati talks — on the road from RIS back to the unitary NKRI. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
