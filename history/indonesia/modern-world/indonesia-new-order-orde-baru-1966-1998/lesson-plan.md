# Lesson plan — The New Order: Orde Baru, 1966–1998

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The New Order: Orde Baru, 1966–1998
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The New Order fused parties into a three-colour politics, made the economy the commander of development through the Pelita plans, and enforced hegemony through social and cultural life — stability with a price the chapter itemises; the guide demands it be read not black-and-white, but as state strengthening and development set against weak policies, human-rights abuses and resistance.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (recommended, never forced).
