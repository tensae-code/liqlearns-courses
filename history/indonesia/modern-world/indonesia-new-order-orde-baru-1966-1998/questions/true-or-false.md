# The New Order: Orde Baru, 1966–1998 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The New Order ruled from 1966 to 1998.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The New Order may be taught as simply a golden age.
**Answer: False** — The guide demands a non-black-and-white reading — development set against human-rights abuses, weak policies and resistance.
