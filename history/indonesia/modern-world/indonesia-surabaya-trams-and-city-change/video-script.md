# Video script — Surabaya's Trams and City Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Surabaya's OJS electric tramway, built 1911–1924, quietly moved the city's economic centre and beat the steam tram — a case study in how technology rewrites urban life.

This case study, an activity sheet in the method book, follows a tram. The OJS — the East Java Steam Tramway company, in its electric age — built Surabaya's electric tramway between 1911 and 1924. The new line did something no decree had planned: it quietly moved the city's economic centre, drawing shops, offices and crowds toward the electric routes, and it beat the older steam tram that had served the city before. The book sets the case to teach change over time in its purest form: a technology arrives, and a city's life rearranges itself around it. Not all history marches or signs treaties. Some of it runs on rails, on schedule, every fifteen minutes.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
