# Surabaya's Trams and City Change

## Do it first

Draw your town's centre. Now draw a new fast road or railway through it, and move the centre to where people would gather along it. That moving centre is this lesson.

This case study, an activity sheet in the method book, follows a tram. The OJS — the East Java Steam Tramway company, in its electric age — built Surabaya's electric tramway between 1911 and 1924. The new line did something no decree had planned: it quietly moved the city's economic centre, drawing shops, offices and crowds toward the electric routes, and it beat the older steam tram that had served the city before. The book sets the case to teach change over time in its purest form: a technology arrives, and a city's life rearranges itself around it. Not all history marches or signs treaties. Some of it runs on rails, on schedule, every fifteen minutes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Surabaya's OJS electric tramway, built 1911–1924, quietly moved the city's economic centre and beat the steam tram — a case study in how technology rewrites urban life. Give one real example: No battle, no treaty: a tramline moved a city's heart. Surabaya's trams teach how technology rewrites a city without firing a shot. and name the lesson's key words: OJS, Change over time. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
