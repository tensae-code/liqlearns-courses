# Surabaya's Trams and City Change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Surabaya's electric tramway was built between 1911 and 1924.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Surabaya's economic centre never moved in the tram age.
**Answer: False** — The OJS electric tramway quietly moved it, drawing the city's life toward its lines.
