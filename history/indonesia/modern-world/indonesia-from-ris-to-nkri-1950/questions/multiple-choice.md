# From RIS to NKRI, 1950 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the RIS?
a) They dissolved themselves into the Republic, one by one  b) The federal Republic of the United States of Indonesia, imposed at the Round Table  c) 1950  d) The NKRI
**Answer: b) The federal Republic of the United States of Indonesia, imposed at the Round Table** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did its member states do?
a) The federal Republic of the United States of Indonesia, imposed at the Round Table  b) 1950  c) They dissolved themselves into the Republic, one by one  d) The NKRI
**Answer: c) They dissolved themselves into the Republic, one by one** — That is what this lesson teaches.
