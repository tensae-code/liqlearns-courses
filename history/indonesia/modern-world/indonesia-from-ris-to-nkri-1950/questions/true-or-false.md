# From RIS to NKRI, 1950 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The RIS dissolved itself within months of the sovereignty transfer.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The federal RIS lasted for decades as Indonesia's form.
**Answer: False** — Its own states dissolved it within months, merging back into the unitary NKRI in 1950.
