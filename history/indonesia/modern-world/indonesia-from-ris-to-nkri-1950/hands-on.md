# Hands-on — From RIS to NKRI, 1950

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a puzzle cut into many pieces by someone else's hand. Now show the pieces sliding back together by their own choice. That sliding is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The federal Republic of the United States of Indonesia, imposed at the Round Table, dissolved itself within months as its states merged back into the unitary Republic in 1950. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
