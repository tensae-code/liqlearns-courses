1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The federal Republic of the United States of Indonesia, imposed at the Round Table, dissolved itself within months as its states merged back into the unitary Republic in 1950.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: The Dutch left behind a federation designed to divide. Its own member states voted it out of existence within the year.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
