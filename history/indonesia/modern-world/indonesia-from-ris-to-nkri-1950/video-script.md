# Video script — From RIS to NKRI, 1950

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The federal Republic of the United States of Indonesia, imposed at the Round Table, dissolved itself within months as its states merged back into the unitary Republic in 1950.

The sovereignty transferred in 1949 came in a shape the Dutch had designed: not the unitary Republic proclaimed in 1945, but a federation — the Republik Indonesia Serikat, the Republic of the United States of Indonesia — a crown of Dutch-built states around the original Republic, meant to keep the archipelago divided and dependent. It did not survive its first year. One by one, the federal states themselves chose to dissolve into the Republic; their peoples and parliaments wanted the one nation the Youth Pledge had promised. Within months of the transfer, the federation had merged itself away, and in August 1950 the unitary Republic — the NKRI — stood restored: the state of 1945, whole at last in law as it had always been in intention.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
