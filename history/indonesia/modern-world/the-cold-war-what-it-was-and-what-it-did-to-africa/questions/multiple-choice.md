# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What doctrine guided Indonesia's Cold War policy?
a) The Asia-Africa Conference, at Bandung  b) The Non-Aligned Movement  c) Both Cold War blocs — West and East  d) Bebas Aktif — free and active
**Answer: d) Bebas Aktif — free and active** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What conference did Indonesia host in 1955, and where?
a) The Asia-Africa Conference, at Bandung  b) Bebas Aktif — free and active  c) The Non-Aligned Movement  d) Both Cold War blocs — West and East
**Answer: a) The Asia-Africa Conference, at Bandung** — That is what this lesson teaches.
