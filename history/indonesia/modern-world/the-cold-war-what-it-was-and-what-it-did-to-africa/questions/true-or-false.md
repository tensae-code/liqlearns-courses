# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Asia-Africa Conference met at Bandung in 1955.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Indonesia joined the Western bloc for protection.
**Answer: False** — Under Bebas Aktif it refused both blocs, hosted Bandung, and helped found the Non-Aligned Movement.
