# Hands-on — Colonial Education and the New Elites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what a school is for, as its builders intend it. Then write what its students might do with what they learn. When the two differ, history moves. That difference is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Colonial schools — STOVIA's medical students, the Kartini schools, Padang Panjang's Madrasah Diniyah Putri — were built to serve the colony, and instead produced the educated generation that would end it; the Volksraad opened as a limited council. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
