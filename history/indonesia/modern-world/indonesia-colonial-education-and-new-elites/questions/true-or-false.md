# Colonial Education and the New Elites — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Volksraad opened in Batavia as a limited council.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Colonial schools produced loyal clerks and nothing else.
**Answer: False** — They produced the educated generation that ended the colony — STOVIA's students, the Kartini schools and Diniyah Putri among them.
