# Colonial Education and the New Elites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was STOVIA?
a) The Kartini schools  b) The colonial medical school  c) The Madrasah Diniyah Putri  d) The educated generation that would end colonial rule
**Answer: b) The colonial medical school** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which schools carried girls' education in Kartini's name?
a) The colonial medical school  b) The Madrasah Diniyah Putri  c) The Kartini schools  d) The educated generation that would end colonial rule
**Answer: c) The Kartini schools** — That is what this lesson teaches.
