# Video script — The Proclamation Spreads and Is Defended

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Printed proclamations travelled to the regions, crowds filled Jakarta's Ikada field for Sukarno's rally, graffiti repeated the pledge on walls, and Sultan Hamengku Buwono IX's amanat bound Yogyakarta to the Republic.

News of 17 August had to travel, and the book follows it. Printed proclamations were carried to the regions and posted where people gathered, so the short text could be read far from Jakarta. In the capital itself, crowds filled Ikada field for Sukarno's rally — the new state's first mass showing of itself to itself. On the walls, graffiti repeated the pledge of independence in paint, the city's surfaces speaking. And in Yogyakarta, Sultan Hamengku Buwono IX issued his amanat, his royal mandate, binding his sultanate to the Republic — a king placing his throne inside the new state, and giving the Republic the city that would become its wartime capital. The proclamation had been read in a morning. Making it real took a nation repeating it, each in its own way.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
