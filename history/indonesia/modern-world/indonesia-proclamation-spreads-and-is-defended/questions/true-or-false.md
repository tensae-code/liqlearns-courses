# The Proclamation Spreads and Is Defended — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sultan Hamengku Buwono IX's amanat bound Yogyakarta to the Republic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The regions ignored the proclamation and waited for the Dutch.
**Answer: False** — Printed proclamations travelled to them, crowds rallied at Ikada, and Yogyakarta's sultan bound his kingdom to the Republic.
