# Hands-on — The Proclamation Spreads and Is Defended

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one piece of news and list every way it could travel in 1945: print, wall, word of mouth, a ruler's decree. That travelling is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Printed proclamations travelled to the regions, crowds filled Jakarta's Ikada field for Sukarno's rally, graffiti repeated the pledge on walls, and Sultan Hamengku Buwono IX's amanat bound Yogyakarta to the Republic. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
