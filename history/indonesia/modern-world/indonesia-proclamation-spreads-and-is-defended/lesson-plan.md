## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Printed proclamations travelled to the regions, crowds filled Jakarta's Ikada field for Sukarno's rally, graffiti repeated the pledge on walls, and Sultan Hamengku Buwono IX's amanat bound Yogyakarta to the Republic.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A proclamation in one city is paper. It became a nation when printed copies reached the regions, crowds filled Ikada field, and Yogyakarta's sultan bound his kingdom to it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
