# The Batak War: Sisingamangaraja — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sisingamangaraja XII was a priest-king of the Batak lands around Lake Toba.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Batak resistance ended with the Aceh War in the 19th century.
**Answer: False** — Sisingamangaraja XII sustained it into the 20th century.
