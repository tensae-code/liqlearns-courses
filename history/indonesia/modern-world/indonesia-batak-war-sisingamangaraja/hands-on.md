# Hands-on — The Batak War: Sisingamangaraja

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture a lake inside an ancient volcano, with villages around it. Write how its priest-king could rally them all. That rallying is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Around Lake Toba, the priest-king Sisingamangaraja XII sustained Batak resistance against Dutch columns into the 20th century. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
