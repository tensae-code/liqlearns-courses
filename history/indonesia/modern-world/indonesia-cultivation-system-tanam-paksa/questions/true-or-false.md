# The Cultivation System: Tanam Paksa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tanam Paksa forced villages to grow export crops for the colonial state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Tanam Paksa let villages choose freely what to plant.
**Answer: False** — It forced villages to grow sugar, coffee and tobacco for export — the colonial state's engine.
