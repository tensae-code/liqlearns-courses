# The Cultivation System: Tanam Paksa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does Tanam Paksa mean?
a) Sugar, coffee and tobacco  b) Deli Serdang and Malang  c) Forced planting — the Cultivation System (Cultuurstelsel)  d) The Tanam Paksa cultivation system
**Answer: c) Forced planting — the Cultivation System (Cultuurstelsel)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What export crops were villages forced to grow?
a) Forced planting — the Cultivation System (Cultuurstelsel)  b) Deli Serdang and Malang  c) The Tanam Paksa cultivation system  d) Sugar, coffee and tobacco
**Answer: d) Sugar, coffee and tobacco** — That is what this lesson teaches.
