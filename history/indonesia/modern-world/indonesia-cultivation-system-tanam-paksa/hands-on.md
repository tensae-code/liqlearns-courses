# Hands-on — The Cultivation System: Tanam Paksa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take a village field. Write what the family would plant to eat, then what they are ordered to plant for export. Compare the two harvests. That comparison is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Cultuurstelsel — Tanam Paksa — forced Javanese villages to grow sugar, coffee and tobacco for export; the book weighs its exploitation against the plantation towns, like Deli Serdang and Malang, that grew up around it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
