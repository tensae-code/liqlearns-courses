# Lesson plan — The Cultivation System: Tanam Paksa

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Cultivation System: Tanam Paksa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Cultuurstelsel — Tanam Paksa — forced Javanese villages to grow sugar, coffee and tobacco for export; the book weighs its exploitation against the plantation towns, like Deli Serdang and Malang, that grew up around it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Cultuurstelsel — Tanam Paksa — forced Javanese villages to grow sugar, coffee and tobacco for export; the book weighs its exploitation against the plantation towns, like Deli Serdang and Malang, that grew up around it.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: The system turned whole villages into the colony's workforce — and the wealth it grew sailed away, while new plantation towns rose where the crops were worked.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
