# Lesson plan — Sultan Agung Attacks Batavia

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab I.B** — Sultan Agung of Mataram was the strongest Javanese ruler of his age, and he would not leave the Dutch company sitting in Batavia on his island's coast.
2. **Bab 2.A.3 Sultan Agung Versus J.P. Coen** — The Kurikulum 2013 book titles its telling as a duel: Sultan Agung versus J.P.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
