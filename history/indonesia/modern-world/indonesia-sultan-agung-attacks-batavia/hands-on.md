# Hands-on — Sultan Agung Attacks Batavia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand at a city wall in your mind. Write what the attackers need to win, and what the defenders need. Then read whose needs ran out first. That weighing is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Mataram's Sultan Agung twice besieged VOC Batavia, measuring his kingdom directly against Jan Pieterszoon Coen's company capital — a war later reconstructed on canvas from the archives. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
