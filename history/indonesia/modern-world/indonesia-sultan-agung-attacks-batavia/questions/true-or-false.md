# Sultan Agung Attacks Batavia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sultan Agung besieged VOC Batavia twice.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sultan Agung captured Batavia and expelled the VOC.
**Answer: False** — His two sieges failed — Batavia's walls, ships and supplies held, and Mataram's supply lines broke.
