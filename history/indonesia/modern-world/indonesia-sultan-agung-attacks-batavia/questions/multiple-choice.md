# Sultan Agung Attacks Batavia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who twice besieged Batavia?
a) Jan Pieterszoon Coen  b) No — the walls, ships and supplies held  c) The painter Sudjojono  d) Sultan Agung of Mataram
**Answer: d) Sultan Agung of Mataram** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who commanded the VOC's side?
a) Jan Pieterszoon Coen  b) Sultan Agung of Mataram  c) No — the walls, ships and supplies held  d) The painter Sudjojono
**Answer: a) Jan Pieterszoon Coen** — That is what this lesson teaches.
