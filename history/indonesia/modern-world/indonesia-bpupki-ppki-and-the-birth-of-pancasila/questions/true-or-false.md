# BPUPKI, PPKI and the Birth of Pancasila — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pancasila was spoken by Sukarno in the BPUPKI on 1 June 1945.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pancasila was written after the revolution ended in 1950.
**Answer: False** — Sukarno spoke it in the BPUPKI on 1 June 1945, before the PPKI convened that August.
