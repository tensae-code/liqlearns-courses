# Hands-on — BPUPKI, PPKI and the Birth of Pancasila

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write five principles you would set under a new country — short enough to fit on one hand. Compare the work of choosing them with this lesson's committee.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Working inside Japan's own institutions, Indonesians trained in PETA and, in the BPUPKI, Sukarno spoke Pancasila on 1 June 1945 — with Maria Ulfah and Siti Sukaptinah among its members — before the PPKI convened on 18 August 1945. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
