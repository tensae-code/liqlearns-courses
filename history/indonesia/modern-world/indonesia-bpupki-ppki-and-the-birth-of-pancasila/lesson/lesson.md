# BPUPKI, PPKI and the Birth of Pancasila

## Do it first

Write five principles you would set under a new country — short enough to fit on one hand. Compare the work of choosing them with this lesson's committee.

Indonesia's strategy under the tyranny was to work inside Japan's own institutions and turn them. Indonesians trained as soldiers in PETA, the Japanese-raised auxiliary army — training that would soon serve the Republic. And in the BPUPKI, the body Japan convened to investigate independence, the future state's foundations were debated in the open. There, on 1 June 1945, Sukarno spoke Pancasila — the five principles — as the proposed basis of the coming state. The book notes the body's breadth: Maria Ulfah and Siti Sukaptinah sat among its members, women at the founding table. When Japan fell, the work passed to the PPKI, the preparatory committee, which convened on 18 August 1945 — the day after the Proclamation — to give the proclaimed state its government.
