## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The hunt for spices that began when Constantinople fell ends in Banda, where the VOC forced the islanders to sell their nutmeg and mace to no one else — a monopoly enforced on the islands that grew them.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Nutmeg grew almost nowhere else on earth but Banda — and the company that seized the trade made selling it to anyone else a crime.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
