# Hands-on — Banda and the VOC Spice Monopoly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your village grows the only supply of something the world wants. Write what a fair trade would look like, then what a forced monopoly would look like. The difference is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The hunt for spices that began when Constantinople fell ends in Banda, where the VOC forced the islanders to sell their nutmeg and mace to no one else — a monopoly enforced on the islands that grew them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
