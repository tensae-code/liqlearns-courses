# Video script — Banda and the VOC Spice Monopoly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The hunt for spices that began when Constantinople fell ends in Banda, where the VOC forced the islanders to sell their nutmeg and mace to no one else — a monopoly enforced on the islands that grew them.

Follow the chain the book builds as a case study. Constantinople falls in 1453. Europe, cut off from its spices, hunts for sea routes around the world. The routes are found, the ships come east — and the chain ends in the Banda islands, the tiny volcanic specks where nutmeg and its red lace, mace, grew almost nowhere else on earth. There the Dutch East India Company, the VOC, forced the islanders to sell their nutmeg and mace to no one else. That is a monopoly with soldiers behind it: the growers kept the trees, but lost the right to choose their buyer. The spice that flavoured Europe's food was gathered under compulsion, at the far end of a chain that started with a fallen city.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
