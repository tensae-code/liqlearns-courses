# Banda and the VOC Spice Monopoly — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Banda's nutmeg and mace grew almost nowhere else on earth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Banda's islanders were free to sell nutmeg to any buyer.
**Answer: False** — The VOC forced them to sell their nutmeg and mace to no one else — a monopoly enforced on the growers.
