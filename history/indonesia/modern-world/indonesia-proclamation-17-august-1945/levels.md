# The Proclamation, 17 August 1945 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — On 17 August 1945 Sukarno read the short proclamation text, the red-and-white sewn by Fatmawati rose, IPPHOS photographers fixed the moment, and the Domei news agency carried the words to the world. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
