# The Proclamation, 17 August 1945 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On what date was independence proclaimed?
a) Sukarno (with Hatta beside him)  b) Fatmawati  c) 17 August 1945  d) Domei
**Answer: c) 17 August 1945** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who read the proclamation?
a) 17 August 1945  b) Fatmawati  c) Domei  d) Sukarno (with Hatta beside him)
**Answer: d) Sukarno (with Hatta beside him)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
