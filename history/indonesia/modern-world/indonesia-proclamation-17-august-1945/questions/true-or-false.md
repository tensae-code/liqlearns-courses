# The Proclamation, 17 August 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** IPPHOS photographers fixed the proclamation moment for history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The proclamation was read on 17 August 1949 after the Round Table Conference.
**Answer: False** — It was read on 17 August 1945, as Japan fell — four years before sovereignty was transferred.
