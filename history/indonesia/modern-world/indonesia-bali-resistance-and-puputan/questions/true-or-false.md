# Bali's Resistance and the Puputan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The puputan was a court's ritual march into gunfire rather than surrender.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bali's kingdoms surrendered quickly to the first Dutch landing.
**Answer: False** — They resisted Dutch landings for decades, and the resistance ended in the puputan marches.
