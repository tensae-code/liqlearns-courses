# Hands-on — Bali's Resistance and the Puputan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what a people might value more than survival itself. Then think of a court choosing that value in front of an army. That choice is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Bali's kingdoms resisted Dutch landings for decades — a resistance that ended in the puputan, the ritual marches of whole courts into gunfire rather than surrender. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
