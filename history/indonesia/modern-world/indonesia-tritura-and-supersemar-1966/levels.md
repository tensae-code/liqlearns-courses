# Tritura and Supersemar, 1966 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Students raised Tritura on 10 January 1966 — dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices — the 11 March 1966 Supersemar order, whose original text remains controversial, empowered Suharto, and a dualism of two presidents followed. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
