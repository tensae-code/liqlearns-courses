# Hands-on — Tritura and Supersemar, 1966

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write three demands for a government in crisis, each five words or fewer. Then write what one signature on one letter can transfer. That pairing is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Students raised Tritura on 10 January 1966 — dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices — the 11 March 1966 Supersemar order, whose original text remains controversial, empowered Suharto, and a dualism of two presidents followed. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
