# Lesson plan — Tritura and Supersemar, 1966

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Tritura and Supersemar, 1966
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Students raised Tritura on 10 January 1966 — dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices — the 11 March 1966 Supersemar order, whose original text remains controversial, empowered Suharto, and a dualism of two presidents followed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (recommended, never forced).
