# Video script — Tritura and Supersemar, 1966

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Students raised Tritura on 10 January 1966 — dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices — the 11 March 1966 Supersemar order, whose original text remains controversial, empowered Suharto, and a dualism of two presidents followed.

The transfer of power was made in the street and on paper. On 10 January 1966 the students raised Tritura — the Three Demands of the People: dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, and lower prices. The demands named the old order's failures in a slogan a crowd could carry. Then, on 11 March 1966, came Supersemar: the Surat Perintah Sebelas Maret, the Order of 11 March, by which Sukarno authorised Suharto to restore order — the letter that empowered Suharto's rise. The book notes the document's strange afterlife: its original text remains controversial, argued over ever since. What followed the letter was a dualism of two presidents — Sukarno still in the palace in name, Suharto gathering the state in fact — until the dualism resolved into the New Order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
