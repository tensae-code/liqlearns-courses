# Tritura and Supersemar, 1966 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tritura was raised by students on 10 January 1966.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Supersemar's original text is preserved and uncontested.
**Answer: False** — The book notes its original text remains controversial — the letter empowered Suharto, and a dualism of two presidents followed.
