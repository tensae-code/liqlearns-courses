# Tritura and Supersemar, 1966 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What were Tritura's three demands?
a) 10 January 1966  b) Dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices  c) The Order of 11 March 1966 empowering Suharto  d) Its original text
**Answer: b) Dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When was Tritura raised?
a) Dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices  b) The Order of 11 March 1966 empowering Suharto  c) 10 January 1966  d) Its original text
**Answer: c) 10 January 1966** — That is what this lesson teaches.
