# Resistance to the New Order — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three forms of resistance does the chapter name?
a) Real personal risk under a supervising regime  b) Students, in wave after wave  c) Across the New Order's three decades  d) Petitions, student movements and opposition figures
**Answer: d) Petitions, student movements and opposition figures** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did petitions risk their signers?
a) Real personal risk under a supervising regime  b) Petitions, student movements and opposition figures  c) Students, in wave after wave  d) Across the New Order's three decades
**Answer: a) Real personal risk under a supervising regime** — That is what this lesson teaches.
