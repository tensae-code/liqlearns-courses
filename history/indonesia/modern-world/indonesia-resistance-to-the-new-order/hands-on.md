# Hands-on — Resistance to the New Order

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a petition of three lines against one policy you dislike. Note what it risks its signers, in a state that supervises speech. That risk, taken, is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Petitions, student movements and opposition figures answered three decades of Suharto's economic and political policy. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
