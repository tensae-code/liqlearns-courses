# Hands-on — The Youth Pledge, 1928

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a pledge of three lines for a group you belong to: one land, one people, one shared tongue — in your own case. Saying it together is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: At the Second Youth Congress in Jakarta in 1928, young delegates from rival regional associations pledged one motherland, one nation, one language — Indonesia. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
