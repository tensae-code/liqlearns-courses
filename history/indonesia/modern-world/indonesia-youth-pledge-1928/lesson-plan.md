## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: At the Second Youth Congress in Jakarta in 1928, young delegates from rival regional associations pledged one motherland, one nation, one language — Indonesia.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Young people from associations that competed with each other stood in one hall and promised to be one people — the pledge is the nation's birth certificate written by its youth.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Indonesia, as the books themselves work.
