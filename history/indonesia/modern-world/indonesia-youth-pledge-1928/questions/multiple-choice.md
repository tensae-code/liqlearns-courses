# The Youth Pledge, 1928 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What did the 1928 Youth Pledge promise?
a) The Second Youth Congress in Jakarta  b) Young members of rival regional associations  c) Mass organisation outside, and speeches inside the Volksraad  d) One motherland, one nation, one language — Indonesia
**Answer: d) One motherland, one nation, one language — Indonesia** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At what meeting was it pledged?
a) The Second Youth Congress in Jakarta  b) One motherland, one nation, one language — Indonesia  c) Young members of rival regional associations  d) Mass organisation outside, and speeches inside the Volksraad
**Answer: a) The Second Youth Congress in Jakarta** — That is what this lesson teaches.
