# The Youth Pledge, 1928 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Youth Pledge promised one motherland, one nation and one language.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Youth Pledge asked each region to keep its own separate nationhood.
**Answer: False** — Delegates from rival regional associations pledged the opposite: one motherland, one nation, one language — Indonesia.
