# The DI/TII, PRRI and Permesta Rebellions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** PRRI rose in Sumatra and Permesta in Sulawesi.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The rebellions were Dutch attempts to retake the colony.
**Answer: False** — They were Indonesian movements — DI/TII's Islamic state and the PRRI/Permesta regional revolts against Jakarta.
