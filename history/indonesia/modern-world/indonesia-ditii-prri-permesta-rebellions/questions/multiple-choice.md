# The DI/TII, PRRI and Permesta Rebellions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who proclaimed the Darul Islam?
a) An Islamic state  b) PRRI (Sumatra) and Permesta (Sulawesi)  c) Kartosuwiryo  d) Neglect and centre–region imbalance
**Answer: c) Kartosuwiryo** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did DI/TII proclaim?
a) Kartosuwiryo  b) PRRI (Sumatra) and Permesta (Sulawesi)  c) Neglect and centre–region imbalance  d) An Islamic state
**Answer: d) An Islamic state** — That is what this lesson teaches.
