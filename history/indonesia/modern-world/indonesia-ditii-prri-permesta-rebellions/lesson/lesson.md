# The DI/TII, PRRI and Permesta Rebellions

## Do it first

Draw the archipelago and shade three regions in revolt. Write, for each, one sentence on what the rebels said Jakarta had failed to be. That map is this lesson.

The Republic's second war for itself was against its own regions. In West Java, Kartosuwiryo proclaimed the Darul Islam — an Islamic state — and his TII army fought Jakarta for years: the revolution's promise, in his reading, had been an Islamic Indonesia, and the secular Republic was the betrayal. A decade on, the grievance changed its language but not its map: Sumatra's PRRI and Sulawesi's Permesta rose against Jakarta together, regional officers and politicians charging the centre with neglect and imbalance — the centre–region wound the teacher guide names as the era's engine. Islamic state, regional revolt: different banners, one lesson. The unitary state proclaimed in one morning in 1945 had to be fought for, region by region, against Indonesians who wanted a different Indonesia.
