1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Kartosuwiryo's Darul Islam/TII proclaimed an Islamic state, and Sumatra's PRRI with Sulawesi's Permesta rose against Jakarta — the unitary state fighting for its map.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
