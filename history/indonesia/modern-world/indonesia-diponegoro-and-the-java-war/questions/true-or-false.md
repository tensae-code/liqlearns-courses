# Diponegoro and the Java War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Diponegoro was raised outside the court yet became its leader in the war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Diponegoro was defeated and captured in one great pitched battle.
**Answer: False** — He fought a five-year guerrilla war and was captured under a flag of negotiation.
