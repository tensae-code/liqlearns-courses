# The Press and Islamic Modernism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what language did the native press publish?
a) Progress — news, argument and new ideas  b) Faith, schools and charity  c) Readers, schools and habits of association for the movement  d) Malay
**Answer: d) Malay** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the press carry into homes?
a) Progress — news, argument and new ideas  b) Malay  c) Faith, schools and charity  d) Readers, schools and habits of association for the movement
**Answer: a) Progress — news, argument and new ideas** — That is what this lesson teaches.
