## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The native press carried progress into Malay-reading homes, while Islamic modernism and reform movements reorganised faith, schools and charity for a new age.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Two printing-age forces moved together: newspapers that taught a people to read its own affairs, and a reformed faith that rebuilt its schools and charities.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
