# Hands-on — The Press and Islamic Modernism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a front page for an imaginary newspaper of your town, in your own language. What would you put first? That choosing is what the native press learned to do. This is the lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The native press carried progress into Malay-reading homes, while Islamic modernism and reform movements reorganised faith, schools and charity for a new age. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
