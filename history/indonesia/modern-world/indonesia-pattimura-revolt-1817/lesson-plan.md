# Lesson plan — Pattimura's Revolt, 1817

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Pattimura's Revolt, 1817
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the Moluccas, Pattimura — an ex-sergeant — led the 1817 rising against the returning Dutch, took Fort Duurstede on Saparua, and was executed at Fort Victoria in Ambon.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance (recommended, never forced).
