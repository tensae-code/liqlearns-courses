# Hands-on — Pattimura's Revolt, 1817

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what changes when an empire leaves and then comes back years later, expecting everything as it was. Who refuses first? That refusal is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the Moluccas, Pattimura — an ex-sergeant — led the 1817 rising against the returning Dutch, took Fort Duurstede on Saparua, and was executed at Fort Victoria in Ambon. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
