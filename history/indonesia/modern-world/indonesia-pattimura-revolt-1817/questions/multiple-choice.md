# Pattimura's Revolt, 1817 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who led the 1817 rising in the Moluccas?
a) An ex-sergeant in the colonial army  b) Fort Duurstede, on Saparua  c) At Fort Victoria in Ambon  d) Pattimura
**Answer: d) Pattimura** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What had Pattimura been before the rising?
a) An ex-sergeant in the colonial army  b) Pattimura  c) Fort Duurstede, on Saparua  d) At Fort Victoria in Ambon
**Answer: a) An ex-sergeant in the colonial army** — That is what this lesson teaches.
