# Pattimura's Revolt, 1817 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pattimura's rising answered the Dutch return to the Moluccas in 1817.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pattimura led the rising as a reigning sultan of Ternate.
**Answer: False** — He was an ex-sergeant; he took Fort Duurstede on Saparua and was executed at Fort Victoria in Ambon.
