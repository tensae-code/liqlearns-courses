# Lesson plan — Sukarno-Era Games and Mercusuar Projects

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Sukarno-Era Games and Mercusuar Projects

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
