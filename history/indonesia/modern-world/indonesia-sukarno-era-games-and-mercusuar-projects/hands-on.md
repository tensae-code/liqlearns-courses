# Hands-on — Sukarno-Era Games and Mercusuar Projects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List what you would build first to make the world respect a new country, and what you would build first for its villages. If the lists fight over money, you have this lesson's tension.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Sukarno built prestige in concrete and sport — the 1962 Asian Games, his rival GANEFO games, and the 'mercusuar' lighthouse projects — while health and education policy reached ordinary villages. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
