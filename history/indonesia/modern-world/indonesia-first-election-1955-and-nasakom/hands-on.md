# Hands-on — The First Election, 1955, and Nasakom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Count a class vote on paper, by hand, into the evening. Then write what you do when the winners are four camps that distrust each other. That problem is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Ballot boxes were counted into the night in Kemayoran on 29 September 1955 in Indonesia's first election, the women's movement organised — and Sukarno answered the fragmentation with Nasakom: nationalism, religion and communism in one formula. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
