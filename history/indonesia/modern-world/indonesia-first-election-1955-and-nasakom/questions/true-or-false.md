# The First Election, 1955, and Nasakom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Indonesia's first election was held on 29 September 1955.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The 1955 election produced one clear majority party.
**Answer: False** — The vote scattered among rival streams; Sukarno answered the fragmentation with the Nasakom formula.
