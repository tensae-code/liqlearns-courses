# The First Election, 1955, and Nasakom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does Nasakom stand for?
**Answer:** Nationalism, religion and communism (Nasionalisme, Agama, Komunisme)

**2.** What problem was Nasakom Sukarno's answer to?
**Answer:** The fragmentation of politics among rival streams
