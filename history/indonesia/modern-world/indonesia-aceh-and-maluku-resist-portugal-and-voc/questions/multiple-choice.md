# Aceh and Maluku Resist Portugal and the VOC — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Aceh fight to guard?
a) The Portuguese, then the Dutch (the VOC)  b) Cloves and nutmeg  c) The pepper coast  d) The first two centuries of contact
**Answer: c) The pepper coast** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two foreign powers did Aceh fight?
a) The pepper coast  b) Cloves and nutmeg  c) The first two centuries of contact  d) The Portuguese, then the Dutch (the VOC)
**Answer: d) The Portuguese, then the Dutch (the VOC)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
