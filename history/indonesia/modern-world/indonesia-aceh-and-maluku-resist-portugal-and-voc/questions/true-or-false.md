# Aceh and Maluku Resist Portugal and the VOC — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aceh fought first the Portuguese and then the Dutch for its pepper coast.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Aceh and Maluku accepted company rule without fighting.
**Answer: False** — Both resisted — Aceh for the pepper coast, Maluku for its cloves and nutmeg — across the first two centuries.
