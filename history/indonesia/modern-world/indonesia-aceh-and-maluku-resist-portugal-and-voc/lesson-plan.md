## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Aceh fought the Portuguese and then the Dutch for the pepper coast, while the Moluccas rose for their cloves and nutmeg — the first two centuries of armed refusal.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Pepper in the west, cloves and nutmeg in the east — at both ends of the archipelago, people fought the companies for the spices growing in their own soil.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
