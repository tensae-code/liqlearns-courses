# Hands-on — Aceh and Maluku Resist Portugal and the VOC

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Put two dots on a map: Aceh in the far west, Maluku in the far east. Write one word under each for what grew there that empires wanted. That map is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Aceh fought the Portuguese and then the Dutch for the pepper coast, while the Moluccas rose for their cloves and nutmeg — the first two centuries of armed refusal. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
