# Video script — Aceh and Maluku Resist Portugal and the VOC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Aceh fought the Portuguese and then the Dutch for the pepper coast, while the Moluccas rose for their cloves and nutmeg — the first two centuries of armed refusal.

The resistance atlas of the Kurikulum 2013 book opens at the two ends of the archipelago. In the west, Aceh guarded the pepper coast: it fought the Portuguese first, and then the Dutch company that came after them, refusing for generations to let foreigners own the trade of its own shore. In the east, the Moluccas — the Spice Islands themselves — rose for their cloves and nutmeg, the treasures that grew nowhere else. Aceh versus Portugal and the VOC, Maluku taking up arms: these are the first two centuries of armed refusal, and the book sets them first for a reason. Before there was a nation to defend, there were already coasts that would not be owned.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
