# The Road to the Proclamation, August 1945 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What city lay in ruins in August 1945?
a) The Indonesian flag and anthem  b) Hiroshima  c) At Admiral Maeda's house  d) A vacuum — Japanese authority finished, the Dutch not yet returned
**Answer: b) Hiroshima** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Tokyo now permit Indonesians?
a) Hiroshima  b) At Admiral Maeda's house  c) The Indonesian flag and anthem  d) A vacuum — Japanese authority finished, the Dutch not yet returned
**Answer: c) The Indonesian flag and anthem** — That is what this lesson teaches.
