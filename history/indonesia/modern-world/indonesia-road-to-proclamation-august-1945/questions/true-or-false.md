# The Road to the Proclamation, August 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The draft proclamation was formulated at Admiral Maeda's house.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The proclamation text was written in Tokyo by Japanese officials.
**Answer: False** — It was formulated by Indonesians at Admiral Maeda's house in Jakarta as Japan collapsed.
