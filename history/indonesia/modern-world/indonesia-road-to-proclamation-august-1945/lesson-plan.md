1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Japan's defeats mounted, Hiroshima lay in ruins, Tokyo permitted the Indonesian flag and anthem — and the draft proclamation was formulated at Admiral Maeda's house as the vacuum opened.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
