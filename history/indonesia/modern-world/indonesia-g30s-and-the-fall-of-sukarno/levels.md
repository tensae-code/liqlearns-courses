# G30S and the Fall of Sukarno — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — On 30 September 1965 a movement murdered six generals; the book lays out the competing accounts of the 'Percobaan Kup G30S/PKI' — the attempted-coup question — and the dominoes fall toward a transfer of power. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
