# Hands-on — G30S and the Fall of Sukarno

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one event and write two honest accounts of it that disagree about who caused it. List what evidence would decide between them. That discipline is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On 30 September 1965 a movement murdered six generals; the book lays out the competing accounts of the 'Percobaan Kup G30S/PKI' — the attempted-coup question — and the dominoes fall toward a transfer of power. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
