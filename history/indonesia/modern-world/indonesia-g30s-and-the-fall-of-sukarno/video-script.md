# Video script — G30S and the Fall of Sukarno

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
On 30 September 1965 a movement murdered six generals; the book lays out the competing accounts of the 'Percobaan Kup G30S/PKI' — the attempted-coup question — and the dominoes fall toward a transfer of power.

The chapter title is a storm warning: Kemelut Pergantian Kekuasaan, the turmoil of the transfer of power. On the night of 30 September 1965, a movement — G30S — seized and murdered six generals of the army's high command. What followed decided Indonesia for three decades. But the Grade 12 book does something careful with the event itself: it lays out the competing accounts of the 'Percobaan Kup G30S/PKI', the question of the attempted coup — who organised the movement, who knew, who used it — as accounts to be weighed, not as one slogan to be repeated. The dominoes the teacher guide traced through the polarised 1950s had reached their last tile. What fell, over the months after that night, was Sukarno himself: power drained from the proclaimer of independence toward the general who would rule the New Order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Indonesia.
