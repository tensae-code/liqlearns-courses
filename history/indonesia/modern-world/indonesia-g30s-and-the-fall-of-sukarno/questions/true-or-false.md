# G30S and the Fall of Sukarno — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Six generals were murdered by the G30S movement on 30 September 1965.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The book presents G30S with one official story that may not be questioned.
**Answer: False** — It lays out the competing accounts of the attempted-coup question for pupils to weigh.
