# The Banjar War

## Do it first

List three reasons a people might go to war at once: a resource, a ruler, a belief. Then braid them into one sentence. That braid is this lesson.

The Banjar War, in South Kalimantan, is the atlas's study in braided causes. Under the ground lay coal, which the Dutch wanted and began to dig. Above it stood a succession: the Banjar throne's inheritance was interfered with, and Prince Antasari emerged as the leader of the refusal. And through the people ran faith, which felt the colonial pressure as an attack on Islam itself. Coal, succession and faith — Antasari led the Banjar War against the Dutch with all three causes in one banner. The atlas places his war among the great regional resistances: Borneo's answer to the company-state, fought along the rivers of the south.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In South Kalimantan, Prince Antasari led the Banjar War against the Dutch over coal, succession and faith. Give one real example: Coal under the ground, a throne in dispute, and faith under pressure — the Banjar War had three causes braided into one. and name the lesson's key words: Antasari, Banjar. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
