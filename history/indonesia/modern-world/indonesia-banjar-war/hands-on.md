# Hands-on — The Banjar War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List three reasons a people might go to war at once: a resource, a ruler, a belief. Then braid them into one sentence. That braid is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In South Kalimantan, Prince Antasari led the Banjar War against the Dutch over coal, succession and faith. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
