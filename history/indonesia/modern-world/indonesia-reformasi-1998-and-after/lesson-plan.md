# Lesson plan — Reformasi, 1998 and After

**Course:** History — Indonesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Reformasi, 1998 and After
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Reformasi opened politics and expression, reformed the bureaucracy and state enterprises, widened education and built infrastructure — studied partly through pupils' own oral-history interviews with parents who lived it; the book first asks what separates revolution from reform, then replays the seconds before B.J. Habibie was sworn in as president.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 4, Indonesia pada Masa Reformasi (Gambaran Umum)** — The student book opens its Reformasi chapter with a distinction: what separates revolution from reform? A revolution replaces a world at a stroke; a reform reopens and rebuilds one piece by piece.
2. **Bab 4.A Reformasi: Awal Demokrasi Politik yang Terbuka** — The teacher guide gives the era's full ledger and its method.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
