# Reformasi, 1998 and After — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Habibie was Suharto's vice-president before being sworn in as president.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Reformasi replaced the state in one revolutionary stroke.
**Answer: False** — The book distinguishes reform from revolution: Reformasi reopened and rebuilt the state piece by piece, beginning with Habibie's swearing-in.
