# History — Indonesia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Indonesia's
own — built from Indonesia's Sejarah books for Kelas X–XII
(Kemendikbudristek, Jakarta, in Bahasa Indonesia). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below sit inside Levels 1–6, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from
live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': learn the historian's
craft first, as the Grade 10 book teaches it; meet the spice
world that drew Europe east; walk the wars of resistance
region by region; watch the awakening organise a nation;
endure occupation and proclaim the Republic; defend it in
revolution; and live its own lifetime — democracy, the New
Order and Reformasi — to the pupils' own century. That ladder
IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).
