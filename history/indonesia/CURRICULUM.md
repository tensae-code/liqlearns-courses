# History — Indonesia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Indonesia inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade
each book comes from appears only in each lesson's sources
(provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Indonesia's books in hand are four
student books and three teacher guides, all in Bahasa Indonesia
— the Grade 10 SMK method book and its guide (Kurikulum Merdeka,
2021), the Grade 11 book in its Edisi Revisi (2024) with its 2021
guide, the older Kurikulum 2013 Grade 11 Semester 1 book (2014),
and the Grade 12 book (2022) with its guide. The placements
file's book order is the teaching order, earliest books first.
Inside that order, the ladder follows the eras as Indonesia's
own books present them: the historian's craft first, exactly as
the Grade 10 book opens (Level 1); the spice world and Europe's
coming (Level 2); colonial rule and the war-by-war resistance
atlas (Level 3); the awakening — schools, press, organisation
(Level 4); occupation, proclamation and revolution (Level 5);
and the Republic's own lifetime, from Bandung to Reformasi
(Level 6). The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Sejarah books (Kemendikbudristek, Jakarta) — except
the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps: How History Works** — Indonesia's own method book opens the course — what history is, chronology and periods, fact against opinion, the research craft from evidence to a written history, historiography, history and social science, and history found in your own locality, with the Grade 10 and Grade 11 teacher guides' discipline stocked as parts 7 lessons.

2. **The Spice World and the Coming of Europe** — the archipelago's own maritime world first — Barus camphor and Borobudur's stone ships, India's zero travelling west — then the West's motives (wealth, glory, gospel), Constantinople's fall, Portugal's sea routes, and the chain ending in Banda's forced nutmeg monopoly 5 lessons.

3. **Colonial Rule and the Wars of Resistance** — the VOC's empire and Batavia, then the resistance atlas war by war — Aceh and Maluku, Sultan Agung, Banten, Makassar, Riau and the Geger Pacinan, Mangkubumi and Mas Said, Daendels and the British interregnum, Tondano, Pattimura, the Padri, Diponegoro, Bali's puputan, Banjar, Aceh's thirty years and the Batak war — closing with the system the wars produced: Tanam Paksa, Krakatau's dated catastrophe, and Surabaya's trams remaking a city 20 lessons.

4. **The Awakening: Schools, Press and Organisation** — the colony's own schools breeding its gravediggers — colonial education and the Ethical Policy, the native press and Islamic modernism, Asia's networks, Budi Utomo and the organised movement in every key, Ki Hadjar Dewantara and Mohammad Hatta, the 1925 manifesto's name, the Youth Pledge and the Women's Congress of 1928 — with the world's storms (the Great War's settlement, the 1918 flu, the Depression) read from Batavia 13 lessons.

5. **Occupation, Proclamation and Revolution** — Japan's arrival and the Dutch surrender at Kalijati, the occupation itemised, the Republic prepared inside Japan's own institutions (PETA, BPUPKI, Pancasila), the road through Hiroshima's ruins to the draft at Maeda's house, the Proclamation of 17 August 1945 and how it spread and was defended, then the revolution: a state formed in days and under fire, battles across the archipelago, Surabaya's Heroes' Day, diplomacy and guerrilla war together, the people's revolution, and the road from RIS back to the unitary NKRI 13 lessons.

6. **Democracy, the New Order and Reformasi** — the Cold War refused under Bebas Aktif at Bandung, liberal democracy polarising into Guided Democracy, the first election of 1955 and Nasakom, the DI/TII, PRRI and Permesta rebellions, Sukarno's games and lighthouse projects, G30S as an evidence problem, Tritura and Supersemar, the New Order's ledger read not black-and-white, its resistance, its fall in May 1998, and Reformasi — politics opened, the state unlocked, and a building nation tested by disasters 13 lessons.

**Totals: 71 lessons** (Level 1: 7 · Level 2: 5 · Level 3: 20 · Level 4: 13 · Level 5: 13 · Level 6: 13 — 90 placements stocked: four student books and three teacher guides; 17 lessons carry sub lessons; 3 lessons stand on teacher-guide evidence only, acceptance 0, as filed in ABOUT.md).
