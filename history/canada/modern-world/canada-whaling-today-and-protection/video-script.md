# Video script — Whaling Today and Protecting the Whales

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The whales are protected and counted now — and the Inuit hunt goes on, small and community-run, under shared rules.

Look at the map one last time. The same Arctic waters of Nunavut. The same bowhead whales. But the story has turned again.

The ships are gone. Their kind of hunting — whale after whale, in large numbers — has been stopped, because the whales grew too few. Now the whales are protected: no one may take them freely, in any number. And the whales are counted — watched from the shore and the boats, numbered, checked — so everyone knows how they are doing.

But watch the shore. A small boat is setting out. The Inuit hunt did not end. It continues today — small, the way the old hunt was small. Community-run: the community itself organises it. And under shared rules, agreed together, so the hunt and the whales both last.

The oldest story in this course and the newest day in the Arctic are the same story: people and whales, going on together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
