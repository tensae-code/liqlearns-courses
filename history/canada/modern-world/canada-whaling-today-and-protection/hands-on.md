# Hands-on — Whaling Today and Protecting the Whales

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's three holding words out loud: protected, counted, shared rules. Now say what each one does: the whales are guarded, the whales are numbered and watched, and the hunt runs under rules agreed together. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw a whale in the sea, people on the shore counting it, and one small boat setting out from the community. Under the drawing write the hunt's three marks today: small, community-run, under shared rules. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
