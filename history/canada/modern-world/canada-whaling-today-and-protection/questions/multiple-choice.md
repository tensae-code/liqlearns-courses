# Whaling Today and Protecting the Whales — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is done today to guard the whales' future?
a) they are protected and counted  b) they are hunted in large numbers  c) they are sold for baleen  d) nothing — no one watches them
**Answer: a) they are protected and counted** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does Inuit whaling continue today?
a) in tall ships from across the sea  b) it does not — it ended completely  c) as a small, community-run hunt under shared rules  d) with no rules at all
**Answer: c) as a small, community-run hunt under shared rules** — That is what this lesson teaches.
