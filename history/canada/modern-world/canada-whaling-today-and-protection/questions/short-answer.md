# Whaling Today and Protecting the Whales — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why are the whales counted?
**Answer:** So people know how the whales are doing — watched and numbered, to see whether their numbers are recovering under protection.

**2.** Name the three marks of the Inuit hunt today.
**Answer:** It is small, it is run by the community itself, and it runs under shared rules agreed together.
