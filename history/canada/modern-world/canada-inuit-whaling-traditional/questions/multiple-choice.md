# Inuit Whaling, the Traditional Way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did Inuit hunters hunt whales? (Sub lesson 1)
a) one whale fed and supplied a whole community  b) whales were easy to catch  c) whale bones were thrown away  d) only one family ate from a whale
**Answer: a) one whale fed and supplied a whole community** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the crew carry that told them where the whales travel and when to strike? (Sub lesson 2)
a) a machine  b) the elders' knowledge  c) a big ship  d) a map from across the sea
**Answer: b) the elders' knowledge** — That is what this lesson teaches.
