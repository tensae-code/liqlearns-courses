# Inuit Whaling, the Traditional Way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three kinds of gifts one whale gave a community. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Food (maktaq and meat), oil for the lamps, and bones for tools and sleds.

**2.** What happened when the bowhead was brought in? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The whole camp shared the catch — the whale's food and gifts spread out to everyone, not only the crew.
