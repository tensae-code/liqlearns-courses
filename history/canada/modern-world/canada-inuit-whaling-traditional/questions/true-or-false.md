# Inuit Whaling, the Traditional Way — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A whale's bones were used to make tools and sleds. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In the old way, one hunter caught a bowhead alone, with a machine. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the crew worked together in open boats with hand harpoons, and the whole camp shared the catch.
