# Lesson plan — Whalers from Across the Sea

**Course:** History — Canada · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Whalers from Across the Sea
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Whaling in the Arctic: the Inuit Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Foreign commercial whaling ships came into Arctic waters and hunted whales in large numbers for oil and baleen — and Inuit life was changed by the trade and by the depletion that followed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Whaling in the Arctic: the Inuit Story (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the foreign ships hunted the Inuit way, a whale at a time for the community → set the two hunts side by side: the old way took what a community needed and shared it; the ships hunted in LARGE numbers, for oil and baleen to sell, and the whales grew fewer — that growing-fewer is the depletion.
- Go back to the one thing: Foreign commercial whaling ships came into Arctic waters and hunted whales in large numbers for oil and baleen — and Inuit life was changed by the trade and by the depletion that followed. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
