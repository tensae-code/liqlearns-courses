# Whalers from Across the Sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The foreign whaling ships hunted whales in large numbers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The ships came to share one whale with the whole camp, the old way.
**Answer: False** — they hunted as a business, for oil and baleen to sell, and Inuit life was changed by the trade and the depletion.
