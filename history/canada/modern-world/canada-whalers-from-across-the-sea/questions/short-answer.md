# Whalers from Across the Sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How was the ships' hunting different from the traditional Inuit hunt?
**Answer:** The Inuit hunted from open boats for what a community needed and shared the catch; the ships hunted whales in large numbers as a business, for oil and baleen to sell.

**2.** Name two ways Inuit life was changed by the ships' coming.
**Answer:** Trade came — new goods and dealing with outsiders — and depletion followed: the whales grew fewer, so the sea held less than before.
