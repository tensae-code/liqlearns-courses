# Whalers from Across the Sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the foreign commercial whaling ships hunt whales for?
a) oil and baleen  b) sleds and tools  c) lamp light for one camp  d) maktaq for the whole camp
**Answer: a) oil and baleen** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did hunting whales in large numbers leave behind in the sea?
a) more whales than before  b) depletion — the whales grew fewer  c) new open boats  d) nothing at all
**Answer: b) depletion — the whales grew fewer** — That is what this lesson teaches.
