# Hands-on — Whalers from Across the Sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the ships' two prizes out loud — oil and baleen — and then say the two things their coming left in Inuit life: trade, and depletion, the whales growing fewer. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the small open boat on one side of the page and a tall ship on the other; under the boat write "one whale, shared by the camp", and under the ship write "many whales, for oil and baleen". Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
