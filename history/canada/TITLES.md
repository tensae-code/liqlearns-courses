# History — Canada course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Canada's own —
built from Nunavut's Inuktitut Level 13 reader, *History of
Whaling* (student book, Inuktitut, in syllabics). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below sit inside Level 1, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grade the book comes from
lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: one long Arctic story in
three turns — the traditional hunt first, then the foreign
whalers who changed it, then the protected whales and the
small community hunt of today. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — whaling in the Arctic: the Inuit story** (History
of Whaling, Uqalimaariuqsaniq Level 13, student book):

- `modern-world/` — Level 1, the course's one title.
  Inuit Whaling, the Traditional Way (why the hunt mattered —
  one whale fed and supplied a whole community — and how the
  old way worked, with elders' knowledge, open boats and hand
  harpoons); Whalers from Across the Sea (foreign commercial
  ships hunting for oil and baleen, and the trade and
  depletion that changed Inuit life); and Whaling Today and
  Protecting the Whales (whales protected and counted, the
  Inuit hunt continuing small and community-run under shared
  rules).
