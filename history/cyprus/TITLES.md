# History — Cyprus course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Cyprus's own —
built from Cyprus's Grade 3 teaching proposals and work cards
and its Grade 4 student book and work cards (Greek). Inside
the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what
the learner proves, step by step. The grade each book comes
from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the four enquiry
questions asked of the child's own life and of every age,
then Cyprus's own ancient story period by period — Geometric,
Archaic, Classical, Hellenistic — with the comparison grid
running across all of it. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — Becoming a Historian: Your Own Past and the Evidence**:

- `first-steps/` — What History Is; From Evidence to a Written History; Find History in Your Own Locality; Dating the Past; Who Studies Prehistory; Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together.

**Level 2 — Stone, Farming and Bronze: Cyprus's First Ages**:

- `africa-to-1500/` — From Stone Tools to Farming.
- `ancient-world/` — Palaeolithic Minds and Cave Art; Cyprus Neolithic: Choirokoitia and the First Villages; Neolithic Beliefs and Symbols; Cyprus Bronze Age: Copper and the First Cities; Bronze Age: Myth and History.

**Level 3 — The City-Kingdoms: Geometric and Archaic Cyprus**:

- `ancient-world/` — Cyprus Geometric Age: The City-Kingdoms Are Founded; Phoenicians in Cyprus; Cyprus Archaic Age: Kingdoms and Conquerors; Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos.

**Level 4 — Classical Cyprus, Alexander, the Ptolemies and Rome**:

- `ancient-world/` — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete; Cyprus Classical Age: Evagoras of Salamis; World Religions: The Five Main Faiths; Alexander the Great and Cyprus; Cyprus Under the Ptolemies; Ancient Civilisations: Rome.
