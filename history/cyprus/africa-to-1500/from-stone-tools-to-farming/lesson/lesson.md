# From Stone Tools to Farming

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: people in every age move for food, safety and opportunity, and settling down changes everything: Palaeolithic hunter-gatherers follow game and seasons with nothing stored for tomorrow, then Neolithic farmers invent the working day of fields, animals and stored food, with polished stone, pottery and woven things as their new tools.
