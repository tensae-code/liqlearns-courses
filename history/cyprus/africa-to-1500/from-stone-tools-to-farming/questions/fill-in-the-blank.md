# From Stone Tools to Farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Hunter-gatherers follow game and seasons, so home is wherever food is tonight — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Palaeolithic

**2.** Farming pins people to one place, and fields, animals and stored food fill the first farmers' day — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Neolithic
