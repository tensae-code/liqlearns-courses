# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) hunter-gatherers follow game and seasons, so home is wherever food is tonight  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) hunter-gatherers follow game and seasons, so home is wherever food is tonight** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) farming pins people to one place, and fields, animals and stored food fill the first farmers' day  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) farming pins people to one place, and fields, animals and stored food fill the first farmers' day** — That is what this lesson teaches.
