# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hunter-gatherers follow game and seasons, so home is wherever food is tonight. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing ever changed in Cyprus and nothing stayed the same. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is spotting what changed and what stayed the same.
