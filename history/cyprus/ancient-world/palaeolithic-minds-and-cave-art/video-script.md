# Video script — Palaeolithic Minds and Cave Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Palaeolithic people thought deeply but wrote nothing: their burials, images and objects are the only voice they left, and that voice is read as evidence of what they believed and imagined.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Burials, images and objects are the only voice left by people who thought deeply but wrote nothing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
