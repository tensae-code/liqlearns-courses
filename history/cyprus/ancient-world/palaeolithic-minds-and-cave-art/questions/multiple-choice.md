# Palaeolithic Minds and Cave Art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) burials, images and objects are the only voice left by people who thought deeply but wrote nothing  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) burials, images and objects are the only voice left by people who thought deeply but wrote nothing** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Palaeolithic people expressed what they thought in images and objects  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Palaeolithic people expressed what they thought in images and objects** — That is what this lesson teaches.
