# Palaeolithic Minds and Cave Art — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Burials, images and objects are the only voice left by people who thought deeply but wrote nothing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing ever changed in Cyprus and nothing stayed the same.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is spotting what changed and what stayed the same.
