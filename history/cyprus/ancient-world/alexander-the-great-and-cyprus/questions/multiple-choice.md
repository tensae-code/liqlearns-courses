# Alexander the Great and Cyprus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Cypriot kings bring their fleets to Alexander at Tyre  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Cypriot kings bring their fleets to Alexander at Tyre** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Cyprus trades Persian masters for a Macedonian conqueror marching to India  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Cyprus trades Persian masters for a Macedonian conqueror marching to India** — That is what this lesson teaches.
