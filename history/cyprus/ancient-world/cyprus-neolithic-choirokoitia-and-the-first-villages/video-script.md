# Video script — Cyprus Neolithic: Choirokoitia and the First Villages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Farming pins people to one place, and in Cyprus the first farmers raise the round-house village of Choirokoitia (Khirokitia): stone, mud and timber become round houses, and Cypriot settlements like Choirokoitia and Sotira show the shared Neolithic pattern found across the world.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Farming pins people to one place; in Cyprus they raise the round-house village of Choirokoitia (Khirokitia).

Stone, mud and timber become round houses at Choirokoitia, whose disputed roof shape shows evidence changing minds.

Choirokoitia, Sotira and other Cypriot settlements are compared with villages abroad to find the shared Neolithic pattern.
