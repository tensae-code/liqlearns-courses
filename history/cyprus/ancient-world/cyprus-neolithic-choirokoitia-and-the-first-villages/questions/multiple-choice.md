# Cyprus Neolithic: Choirokoitia and the First Villages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) farming pins people to one place, and in Cyprus they raise the round-house village of Choirokoitia  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
