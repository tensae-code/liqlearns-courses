# Lesson plan — Cyprus Neolithic: Choirokoitia and the First Villages

**Course:** History — Cyprus · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Cyprus Neolithic: Choirokoitia and the First Villages
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Stone, Farming and Bronze: Cyprus's First Ages (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** farming pins people to one place, and in Cyprus the first farmers raise the round-house village of Choirokoitia (Khirokitia): stone, mud and timber become round houses, and Cypriot settlements like Choirokoitia and Sotira show the shared Neolithic pattern found across the world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Stone, Farming and Bronze: Cyprus's First Ages (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Neolithic proposal 1: Why Neolithic people moved less, and where they built villages** — Farming pins people to one place; in Cyprus they raise the round-house village of Choirokoitia (Khirokitia).
2. **Neolithic proposal 2: How Neolithic people built their houses** — Stone, mud and timber become round houses at Choirokoitia, whose disputed roof shape shows evidence changing minds.
3. **Neolithic proposal 3: Neolithic settlements in Cyprus and the rest of the world** — Choirokoitia, Sotira and other Cypriot settlements are compared with villages abroad to find the shared Neolithic pattern.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
