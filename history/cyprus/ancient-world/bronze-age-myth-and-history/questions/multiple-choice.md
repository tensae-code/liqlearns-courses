# Bronze Age: Myth and History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) myths are read as a people's memory and imagination, tested against what the finds actually show  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) myths are read as a people's memory and imagination, tested against what the finds actually show** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) a myth is not simply believed or dismissed: it is checked against the evidence of the finds  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) a myth is not simply believed or dismissed: it is checked against the evidence of the finds** — That is what this lesson teaches.
