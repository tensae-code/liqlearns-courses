# Bronze Age: Myth and History

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: myths are read as a people's memory and imagination — and then tested against what the finds actually show, so story and evidence check each other.

Myths are read as a people's memory and imagination, tested against what the finds actually show.

That is the whole story of this lesson, in one part. Hold it against the four questions on your page: which of them does it answer — movement, the day, thought, or organisation — and what changed here, and what stayed the same?
