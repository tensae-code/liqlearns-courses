# Video script — Cyprus Under the Ptolemies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province of an Egyptian empire (325–30 BC): Cypriot copper, timber, wine and harbours feed Ptolemaic power — a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story — while Hellenistic Cypriots live in Greek-style cities, watch plays in stone theatres, and blend their old cults with Greek gods and ruler worship.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province of an Egyptian empire.

Cypriot copper, timber, wine and harbours feed Ptolemaic power, and a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story.

Hellenistic Cypriots live in Greek-style cities, watch plays in stone theatres and fill houses with new fashions.

Old Cypriot cults blend with Greek gods and ruler worship under Ptolemaic rule.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
