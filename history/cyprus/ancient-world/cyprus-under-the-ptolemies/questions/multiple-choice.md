# Cyprus Under the Ptolemies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story of copper, timber, wine and harbours feeding Ptolemaic power  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story of copper, timber, wine and harbours feeding Ptolemaic power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
