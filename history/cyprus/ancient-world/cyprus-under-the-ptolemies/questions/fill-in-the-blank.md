# Cyprus Under the Ptolemies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ptolemies

**2.** A merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story of copper, timber, wine and harbours feeding Ptolemaic power — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Kyrenia ship
