# Cyprus Under the Ptolemies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story of copper, timber, wine and harbours feeding Ptolemaic power.
