# Cyprus Under the Ptolemies

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province of an Egyptian empire (325–30 BC): Cypriot copper, timber, wine and harbours feed Ptolemaic power — a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story — while Hellenistic Cypriots live in Greek-style cities, watch plays in stone theatres, and blend their old cults with Greek gods and ruler worship.
