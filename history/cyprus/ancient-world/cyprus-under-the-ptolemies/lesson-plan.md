# Lesson plan — Cyprus Under the Ptolemies

**Course:** History — Cyprus · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Cyprus Under the Ptolemies
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Classical Cyprus, Alexander, the Ptolemies and Rome (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province of an Egyptian empire (325–30 BC): Cypriot copper, timber, wine and harbours feed Ptolemaic power — a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story — while Hellenistic Cypriots live in Greek-style cities, watch plays in stone theatres, and blend their old cults with Greek gods and ruler worship.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Classical Cyprus, Alexander, the Ptolemies and Rome (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 4 ch. 22–23: The end of the Cypriot kingdoms; administration — the koina (325–30 BC)** — Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province of an Egyptian empire.
2. **Unit 4 ch. 24: The importance of Cyprus for the Ptolemaic kingdom — the Kyrenia ship** — Cypriot copper, timber, wine and harbours feed Ptolemaic power, and a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story.
3. **Unit 4 ch. 25–26: Daily life; art — theatre of the Hellenistic age** — Hellenistic Cypriots live in Greek-style cities, watch plays in stone theatres and fill houses with new fashions.
4. **Unit 4 ch. 28: The religion of the Cypriots in the Hellenistic age** — Old Cypriot cults blend with Greek gods and ruler worship under Ptolemaic rule.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
