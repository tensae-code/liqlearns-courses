# Cyprus Classical Age: Evagoras of Salamis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the trireme, rowed by free crews, becomes the weapon every king must have  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) the trireme, rowed by free crews, becomes the weapon every king must have** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Evagoras of Salamis fights the Great King for a decade before a negotiated peace leaves him king  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Evagoras of Salamis fights the Great King for a decade before a negotiated peace leaves him king** — That is what this lesson teaches.
