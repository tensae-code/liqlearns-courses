# Video script — Cyprus Classical Age: Evagoras of Salamis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Sea power decides Classical Cyprus's fate: the trireme, rowed by free crews, is the weapon every king must have, and Evagoras of Salamis rebuilds his city, courts Athens, then fights the Persian Great King for a decade before a negotiated peace leaves him king.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Sea power decides Cyprus's fate, and the trireme, rowed by free crews, becomes the weapon every king must have.

Evagoras of Salamis rebuilds his city, courts Athens, then fights the Great King for a decade before a negotiated peace leaves him king.

Cypriot ships and sailors serve, and sometimes defy, the great powers contesting the eastern Mediterranean.

Clothes, banquets and everyday food show a Greek-style city life lived Cypriot-fashion.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
