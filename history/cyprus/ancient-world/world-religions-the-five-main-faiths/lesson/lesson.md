## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: in Classical Cyprus, Aphrodite above all — with Greek and Phoenician gods beside her — receives Cypriot worship in sanctuaries across the island: the island's faith is its own blend of the gods of its peoples.
