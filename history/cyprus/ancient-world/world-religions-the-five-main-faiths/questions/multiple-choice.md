# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Aphrodite above all, with Greek and Phoenician gods beside her, receives Cypriot worship in sanctuaries across the island  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Aphrodite above all, with Greek and Phoenician gods beside her, receives Cypriot worship in sanctuaries across the island** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Cypriot worship blends the gods of the island's own peoples  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Cypriot worship blends the gods of the island's own peoples** — That is what this lesson teaches.
