# Hands-on — World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud, in your own words: in Classical Cyprus, Aphrodite above all — with Greek and Phoenician gods beside her — receives Cypriot worship in sanctuaries across the island: the island's faith is its own blend of the gods of its peoples. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson (a Cypriot sanctuary with worshippers bringing offerings to Aphrodite's shrine), and say each part aloud as you draw it: who is there, where it happens, and what turned.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see something that answers one of this course's four enquiry questions — how and why do we move? how do we spend our day? what do we think? how do we organise ourselves? — note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
