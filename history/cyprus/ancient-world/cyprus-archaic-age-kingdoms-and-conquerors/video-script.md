# Video script — Cyprus Archaic Age: Kingdoms and Conquerors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the Archaic age (750–475 BC) Cyprus is split into kingdoms, each with its walled city, king, farmers and craftspeople — and Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians take the island in turn, so Cypriot kings pay tribute to survive each new master.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Cyprus is split into kingdoms, each with its walled city, king, farmers and craftspeople living under foreign shadows.

Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians take the island in turn, and Cypriot kings pay tribute to survive each new master.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
