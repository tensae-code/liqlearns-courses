# Cyprus Archaic Age: Kingdoms and Conquerors — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Cyprus is split into kingdoms, each with its walled city, king, farmers and craftspeople — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** tribute

**2.** Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians take the island in turn, and Cypriot kings pay tribute to survive — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Archaic age
