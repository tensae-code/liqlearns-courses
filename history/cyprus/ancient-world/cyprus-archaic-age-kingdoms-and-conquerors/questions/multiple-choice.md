# Cyprus Archaic Age: Kingdoms and Conquerors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Cyprus is split into kingdoms, each with its walled city, king, farmers and craftspeople  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Cyprus is split into kingdoms, each with its walled city, king, farmers and craftspeople** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians take the island in turn, and Cypriot kings pay tribute to survive  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians take the island in turn, and Cypriot kings pay tribute to survive** — That is what this lesson teaches.
