# Phoenicians in Cyprus

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: Phoenician traders settle in Cyprus, build colonies and temples, and change Cypriot life, while taking copper and goods east.

Phoenician traders settle on the island, build colonies and temples, and change Cypriot life while taking copper and goods east.

That is the whole story of this lesson, in one part. Hold it against the four questions on your page: which of them does it answer — movement, the day, thought, or organisation — and what changed here, and what stayed the same?
