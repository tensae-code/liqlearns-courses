# Phoenicians in Cyprus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Phoenician traders settle on the island, build colonies and temples, and change Cypriot life  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Phoenician traders settle on the island, build colonies and temples, and change Cypriot life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) the Phoenicians take copper and goods east from Cyprus  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the Phoenicians take copper and goods east from Cyprus** — That is what this lesson teaches.
