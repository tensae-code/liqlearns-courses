# Phoenicians in Cyprus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Phoenician traders settle on the island, build colonies and temples, and change Cypriot life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing ever changed in Cyprus and nothing stayed the same.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is spotting what changed and what stayed the same.
