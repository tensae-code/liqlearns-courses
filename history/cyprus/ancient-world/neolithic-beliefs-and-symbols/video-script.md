# Video script — Neolithic Beliefs and Symbols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Neolithic people left their beliefs unexplained: figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots are the hints from which their thoughts and symbols are read.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots hint at beliefs the makers never explained.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
