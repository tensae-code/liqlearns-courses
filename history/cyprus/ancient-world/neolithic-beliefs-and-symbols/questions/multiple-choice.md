# Neolithic Beliefs and Symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots hint at beliefs the makers never explained  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots hint at beliefs the makers never explained** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Neolithic people expressed their thoughts in figurines and decorated pots  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Neolithic people expressed their thoughts in figurines and decorated pots** — That is what this lesson teaches.
