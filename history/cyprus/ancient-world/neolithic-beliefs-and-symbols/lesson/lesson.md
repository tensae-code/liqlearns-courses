# Neolithic Beliefs and Symbols

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: Neolithic people left their beliefs unexplained: figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots are the hints from which their thoughts and symbols are read.

Figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots hint at beliefs the makers never explained.

That is the whole story of this lesson, in one part. Hold it against the four questions on your page: which of them does it answer — movement, the day, thought, or organisation — and what changed here, and what stayed the same?

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Neolithic people left their beliefs unexplained: figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots are the hints from which their thoughts and symbols are read. Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: figurine, burial under the house floor. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
