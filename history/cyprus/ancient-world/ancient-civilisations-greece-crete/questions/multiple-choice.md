# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Cypriots write Greek in their own syllabic script, a local invention that outlasts alphabets around it  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Cypriots write Greek in their own syllabic script, a local invention that outlasts alphabets around it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Zeno of Kition leaves Cyprus to found Stoicism in Athens  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Zeno of Kition leaves Cyprus to found Stoicism in Athens** — That is what this lesson teaches.
