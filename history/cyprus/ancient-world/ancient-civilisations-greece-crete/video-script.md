# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cyprus lives inside the Greek world in its own way: Cypriots write Greek in their own syllabic script, their artists mix Greek, Phoenician and eastern styles in sculpture and pottery, gymnasia school the island's youth in Greek letters, and Zeno of Kition leaves Cyprus to found Stoicism in Athens.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Cypriots write Greek in their own syllabic script, a local invention that outlasts alphabets around it.

Stone votaries, painted pots and terracottas show Cypriot artists mixing Greek, Phoenician and eastern styles.

Athenian influence refines Cypriot sculpture and pottery while local workshops keep their own hand.

Gymnasia school the island's youth in Greek letters, and Zeno of Kition leaves Cyprus to found Stoicism in Athens.

Geometric farmers, classical symposiasts and Hellenistic townsfolk eat, dress and work differently on the same island.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
