# Lesson plan — Cyprus Geometric Age: The City-Kingdoms Are Founded

**Course:** History — Cyprus · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Cyprus Geometric Age: The City-Kingdoms Are Founded
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The City-Kingdoms: Geometric and Archaic Cyprus (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after collapse and migration, settlers found Cyprus's city-kingdoms (1050–750 BC): farmers, herders, smiths and potters fill the new kingdoms, iron arrives, geometric-patterned pots mark the age, and colonists, traders and conquerors keep arriving across the ages that follow.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The City-Kingdoms: Geometric and Archaic Cyprus (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 1 ch. 1: The founding of new kingdoms in Cyprus (1050–750 BC)** — After collapse and migration, settlers found Cyprus's city-kingdoms, and myth and history together explain where each kingdom came from.
2. **Unit 1 ch. 2: The life of the people** — Farmers, herders, smiths and potters fill the new kingdoms, iron arrives, and geometric-patterned pots mark the age.
3. **Unit 1 ch. 3: Relations with other peoples** — A sea people dominating Mediterranean trade draws little Cyprus into a wide web of exchange.
4. **Cards 1–3, Movement strand: why people move; how they move and where to; where they build their dwelling** — Colonists, traders and conquerors keep arriving in Cyprus across four ages, each wave building its homes in new places.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
