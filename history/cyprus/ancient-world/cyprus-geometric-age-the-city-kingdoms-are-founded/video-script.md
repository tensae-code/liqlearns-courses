# Video script — Cyprus Geometric Age: The City-Kingdoms Are Founded

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
After collapse and migration, settlers found Cyprus's city-kingdoms (1050–750 BC): farmers, herders, smiths and potters fill the new kingdoms, iron arrives, geometric-patterned pots mark the age, and colonists, traders and conquerors keep arriving across the ages that follow.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

After collapse and migration, settlers found Cyprus's city-kingdoms, and myth and history together explain where each kingdom came from.

Farmers, herders, smiths and potters fill the new kingdoms, iron arrives, and geometric-patterned pots mark the age.

A sea people dominating Mediterranean trade draws little Cyprus into a wide web of exchange.

Colonists, traders and conquerors keep arriving in Cyprus across four ages, each wave building its homes in new places.
