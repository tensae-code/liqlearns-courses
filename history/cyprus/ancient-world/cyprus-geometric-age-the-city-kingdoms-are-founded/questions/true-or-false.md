# Cyprus Geometric Age: The City-Kingdoms Are Founded — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After collapse and migration, settlers found Cyprus's city-kingdoms. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing ever changed in Cyprus and nothing stayed the same. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is spotting what changed and what stayed the same.
