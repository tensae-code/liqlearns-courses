# Cyprus Geometric Age: The City-Kingdoms Are Founded — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) after collapse and migration, settlers found Cyprus's city-kingdoms  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) after collapse and migration, settlers found Cyprus's city-kingdoms** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) iron arrives, and geometric-patterned pots mark the Geometric age  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) iron arrives, and geometric-patterned pots mark the Geometric age** — That is what this lesson teaches.
