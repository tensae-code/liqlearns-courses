# Video script — Cyprus Bronze Age: Copper and the First Cities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Copper and bronze change Cyprus: the island's copper becomes the treasure outsiders sail for, ships and trade routes tie Cyprus to Egypt and the Levant, coastal cities rise where the boats land, and cities need rulers, laws, gods and sanctuaries of their own.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Copper and bronze reach Cyprus, and the island's copper becomes the treasure outsiders sail for.

Richer houses, new foods and finer clothes mark a world of farmers, smiths and traders.

Ships and trade routes tie Cyprus to Egypt and the Levant, and coastal cities rise where the boats land.

Gods, sanctuaries, figurines and early signs show belief and communication growing more elaborate.

Cities need rulers and laws, and Cyprus's kingdoms deal with great powers abroad as partners and prizes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
