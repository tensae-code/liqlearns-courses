# Cyprus Bronze Age: Copper and the First Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) copper and bronze reach Cyprus, and the island's copper becomes the treasure outsiders sail for  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) copper and bronze reach Cyprus, and the island's copper becomes the treasure outsiders sail for** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) ships and trade routes tie Cyprus to Egypt and the Levant, and coastal cities rise where the boats land  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) ships and trade routes tie Cyprus to Egypt and the Levant, and coastal cities rise where the boats land** — That is what this lesson teaches.
