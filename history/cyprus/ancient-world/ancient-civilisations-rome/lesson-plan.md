**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Classical Cyprus, Alexander, the Ptolemies and Rome (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Rome annexes Cyprus in 58 BC, ending the Hellenistic age and starting the island's Roman centuries.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — or, for this course's lessons, from the Cyprus story itself as the books tell it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Cyprus's books themselves work, asking the same enquiry questions of every age.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the story in the names and dates → go back to the one turn: who wanted what, what changed, and what came of it. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: Rome annexes Cyprus in 58 BC, ending the Hellenistic age and starting the island's Roman centuries. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
