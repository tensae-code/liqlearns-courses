# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Rome annexes the island in 58 BC, ending the Hellenistic age  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Rome annexes the island in 58 BC, ending the Hellenistic age** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) the annexation of 58 BC starts Cyprus's Roman centuries  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the annexation of 58 BC starts Cyprus's Roman centuries** — That is what this lesson teaches.
