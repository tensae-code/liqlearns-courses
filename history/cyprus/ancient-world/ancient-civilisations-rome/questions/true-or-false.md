# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rome annexes the island in 58 BC, ending the Hellenistic age.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing ever changed in Cyprus and nothing stayed the same.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is spotting what changed and what stayed the same.
