# Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Salamis strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Onesilos of Salamis leads Cyprus into the Ionian revolt, dies in battle against the Persians, and is honoured as a hero.
