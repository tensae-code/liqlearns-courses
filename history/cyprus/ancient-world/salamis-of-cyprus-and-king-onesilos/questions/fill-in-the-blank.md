# Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Salamis

**2.** ____ of Salamis leads Cyprus into the Ionian revolt, dies in battle against the Persians, and is honoured as a hero. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Onesilos
