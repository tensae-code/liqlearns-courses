# Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Salamis strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) Salamis strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) Onesilos of Salamis leads Cyprus into the Ionian revolt, dies in battle against the Persians, and is honoured as a hero  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Onesilos of Salamis leads Cyprus into the Ionian revolt, dies in battle against the Persians, and is honoured as a hero** — That is what this lesson teaches.
