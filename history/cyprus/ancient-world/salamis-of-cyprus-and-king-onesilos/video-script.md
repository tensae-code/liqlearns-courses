# Video script — Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Salamis grows rich and powerful, strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs — then Onesilos of Salamis leads Cyprus into revolt against the Persians (499–498 BC), dies in battle, and is honoured after death as a hero, while Greek fleets campaign to free Cyprus but never hold it.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Salamis grows rich and powerful, strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs.

Onesilos of Salamis leads Cyprus into the Ionian revolt, dies in battle against the Persians, and the island falls back under Persian rule, his tomb later honoured as a hero's.

Greek fleets and generals campaign to free Cyprus from Persia, winning battles but never holding the island.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
