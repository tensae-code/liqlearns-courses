# Lesson plan — Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos

**Course:** History — Cyprus · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The City-Kingdoms: Geometric and Archaic Cyprus (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Salamis grows rich and powerful, strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs — then Onesilos of Salamis leads Cyprus into revolt against the Persians (499–498 BC), dies in battle, and is honoured after death as a hero, while Greek fleets campaign to free Cyprus but never hold it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The City-Kingdoms: Geometric and Archaic Cyprus (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2 ch. 7: Salamis, an important city — how coins were minted; burial customs** — Salamis grows rich and powerful, strikes its own coins and buries its kings with horses and chariots in royal tombs.
2. **Unit 2 ch. 10–12: The Cypriot revolt against the Persians (499–498 BC); the battle and death of Onesilos; what happened after** — Onesilos of Salamis leads Cyprus into the Ionian revolt, dies in battle against the Persians, and the island falls back under Persian rule, his tomb later honoured as a hero's.
3. **Unit 2 ch. 13: A panhellenic attempt to liberate Cyprus** — Greek fleets and generals campaign to free Cyprus from Persia, winning battles but never holding the island.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
