# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a name carries a family story, traced by asking elders and checking records  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) a name carries a family story, traced by asking elders and checking records** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) old school photos and elders' testimony show schools changing — boys', girls' and mixed schools giving way to today's school — while some things endure  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) old school photos and elders' testimony show schools changing — boys', girls' and mixed schools giving way to today's school — while some things endure** — That is what this lesson teaches.
