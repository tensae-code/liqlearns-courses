# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History begins at home: a name carries a family story, a family photograph can be read and re-read as new information arrives, the class itself is an archive, and old school photos and elders' testimony show what changed in your own school and what stayed the same.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

A name turns out to carry a family story, traced by asking elders and checking records.

A family photo is read closely, first guesses are revised when relatives supply new information.

Children curate their own family objects into an exhibition, becoming the museum-makers of their own past.

The class itself becomes the archive, its members interviewed as living sources.

Old school photos and elders' testimony show boys'/girls'/mixed schools giving way to today's school, while some things endure.

Comparing schools across places tests whether change happened the same way everywhere.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
