# Find History in Your Own Locality

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: history begins at home: a name carries a family story, a family photograph can be read and re-read as new information arrives, the class itself is an archive, and old school photos and elders' testimony show what changed in your own school and what stayed the same.

## Sub lesson 1 — Intro unit proposal 2: How do I learn about my name?

A name turns out to carry a family story, traced by asking elders and checking records.

That is the turn of this part. Hold it against the four questions on your page: which of them does this part answer — movement, the day, thought, or organisation — and what changed here, and what stayed the same?
