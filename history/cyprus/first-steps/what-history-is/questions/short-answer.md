# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A child becomes a historian by asking questions of traces, not by receiving a finished past.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Each age thinks its own thoughts and leaves its own marks, from cave images to writing to today's screens.
