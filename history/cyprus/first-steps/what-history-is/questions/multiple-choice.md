# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a child becomes a historian by asking questions of traces, not by receiving a finished past  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) a child becomes a historian by asking questions of traces, not by receiving a finished past** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) each age thinks its own thoughts and leaves its own marks, from cave images to writing to today's screens  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) each age thinks its own thoughts and leaves its own marks, from cave images to writing to today's screens** — That is what this lesson teaches.
