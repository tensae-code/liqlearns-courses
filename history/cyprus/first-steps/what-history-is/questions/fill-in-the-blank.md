# What History Is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A child becomes a historian by asking questions of traces, not by receiving a finished past — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** historical literacy

**2.** Each age thinks its own thoughts and leaves its own marks, from cave images to writing to today's screens — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the four enquiry questions
