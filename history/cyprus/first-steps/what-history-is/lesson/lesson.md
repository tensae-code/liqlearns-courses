# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: a child becomes a historian by asking questions of traces — starting with their own name, family and life — and every age is asked the same four questions: how and why do we move, how do we spend our day, what do we think, and how do we organise ourselves; across every age some things change and some stay the same.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theoretical framework: developing historical literacy

Teachers learn that a child becomes a historian by asking questions of traces, not by receiving a finished past.
