# Who Studies Prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) archaeologists dig, record and reason from finds  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) archaeologists dig, record and reason from finds** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) children try the archaeologist's job before meeting prehistory  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) children try the archaeologist's job before meeting prehistory** — That is what this lesson teaches.
