# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Every group must decide who leads, who belongs and how disputes end, from band to city — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Bands become villages become cities, and leaders appear where groups grow — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
