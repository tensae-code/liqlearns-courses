# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) every group must decide who leads, who belongs and how disputes end, from band to city  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) every group must decide who leads, who belongs and how disputes end, from band to city** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) bands become villages become cities, and leaders appear where groups grow  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) bands become villages become cities, and leaders appear where groups grow** — That is what this lesson teaches.
