# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Past, present and future selves are placed on one line, and a single life is seen changing over time.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Across every age, some things change and some stay the same, and spotting both is the historian's job.
