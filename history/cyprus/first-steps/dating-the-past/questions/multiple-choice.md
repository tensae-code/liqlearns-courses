# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) past, present and future selves are placed on one line, and a single life is seen changing over time  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) past, present and future selves are placed on one line, and a single life is seen changing over time** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) across every age, some things change and some stay the same, and spotting both is the historian's job  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) across every age, some things change and some stay the same, and spotting both is the historian's job** — That is what this lesson teaches.
