# Dating the Past — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Past, present and future selves are placed on one line, and a single life is seen changing over time — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** timeline

**2.** Across every age, some things change and some stay the same, and spotting both is the historian's job — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** comparison grid
