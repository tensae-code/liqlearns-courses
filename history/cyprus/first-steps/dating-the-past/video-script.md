# Video script — Dating the Past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Past, present and future are placed on one line: a single life changes over time, and the historian's master question — what changes and what stays the same — is asked of every age on one comparison grid, from the Palaeolithic to today and even to an imagined future.

Look at the map. Here is Cyprus, an island in the eastern Mediterranean Sea.

Past, present and future selves are placed on one line, and the child sees a single life changing over time.

Children learn the year's master question — across every age, some things change and some stay the same, and spotting both is the historian's job.

The grid's columns are whole ages of Cyprus, and the child's task is to watch one island change across a thousand years and into an imagined future.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
