# From Evidence to a Written History — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A claim about the past stands or falls on its evidence — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** source

**2.** An object or photo is cross-questioned like a witness until it gives up what it can and cannot prove — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** graded certainty
