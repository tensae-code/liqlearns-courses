# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A claim about the past stands or falls on its evidence.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An object or photo is cross-questioned like a witness until it gives up what it can and cannot prove.
