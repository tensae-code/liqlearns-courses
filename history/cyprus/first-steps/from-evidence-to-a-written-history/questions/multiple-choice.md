# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a claim about the past stands or falls on its evidence  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in Cyprus ever changed  d) only kings have a past
**Answer: a) a claim about the past stands or falls on its evidence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against evidence  b) an object or photo is cross-questioned like a witness until it gives up what it can and cannot prove  c) Cyprus's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) an object or photo is cross-questioned like a witness until it gives up what it can and cannot prove** — That is what this lesson teaches.
