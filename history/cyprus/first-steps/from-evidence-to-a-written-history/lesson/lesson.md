# From Evidence to a Written History

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: take a blank page and write the four questions Cyprus's books ask of every age — How and why do we move? How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise ourselves? Keep the page beside you. This lesson answers them for its own story: a claim about the past stands or falls on its evidence: sources are cross-questioned like witnesses, questions are framed carefully, and certainty is graded in honest words from 'impossible' to 'certainly' — new evidence can overturn an old conclusion.
