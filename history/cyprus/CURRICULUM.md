# History — Cyprus course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Cyprus inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school
grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Cyprus's books in hand are primary
books in the country's own teaching order, and the ladder
follows it. The Grade 3 material (a teacher guide of teaching
proposals with its student work cards) builds history through
four recurring enquiry questions — How and why do we move?
How do we spend our day? What do we think? How do we organise
ourselves? — asked first of the child's own life and then of
the Palaeolithic, Neolithic and Bronze Age. The Grade 4
student book then walks Cyprus's own ancient story period by
period — Geometric, Archaic, Classical, Hellenistic — with its
work cards running the same change-and-continuity grid across
the ages. Four levels, in the books' own order: becoming a
historian, the first ages, the city-kingdoms, and Cyprus
inside the Greek, Macedonian and Roman worlds. The ladder is
a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — Cyprus's own
Ministry of Education and Culture / Pedagogical Institute of
Cyprus History books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a
box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours;
the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book
list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — Becoming a Historian: Your Own Past and the Evidence** — the historian's craft learned on the child's own life first — what history is, evidence cross-questioned and certainty graded, history found in your own name, family photographs, class and school, the past dated on one line, the archaeologists who study prehistory, and how groups organise themselves from band to city. 6 lessons, 22 placements: What History Is; From Evidence to a Written History; Find History in Your Own Locality; Dating the Past; Who Studies Prehistory; Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together.

2. **Level 2 — Stone, Farming and Bronze: Cyprus's First Ages** — the four enquiry questions asked of the deep past — Palaeolithic hunter-gatherers moving after food and thinking in images, Neolithic farmers settling at Choirokoitia and burying their dead under house floors, and Bronze Age Cyprus whose copper draws the world's ships and raises its first cities, with myth tested against the finds. 6 lessons, 23 placements: From Stone Tools to Farming; Palaeolithic Minds and Cave Art; Cyprus Neolithic: Choirokoitia and the First Villages; Neolithic Beliefs and Symbols; Cyprus Bronze Age: Copper and the First Cities; Bronze Age: Myth and History.

3. **Level 3 — The City-Kingdoms: Geometric and Archaic Cyprus** — Cyprus's kingdoms founded after collapse and migration — the Geometric city-kingdoms and their geometric pots, the Phoenicians settling and building temples, the Archaic kingdoms paying tribute to Assyria, Egypt and Persia in turn, and Salamis rich on its own coins until Onesilos falls in revolt. 4 lessons, 10 placements: Cyprus Geometric Age: The City-Kingdoms Are Founded; Phoenicians in Cyprus; Cyprus Archaic Age: Kingdoms and Conquerors; Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos.

4. **Level 4 — Classical Cyprus, Alexander, the Ptolemies and Rome** — the island inside the Greek and then the Macedonian and Roman worlds — its own syllabic script and mixed arts, Evagoras and the trireme, Aphrodite's sanctuaries, Alexander's fleets at Tyre, three centuries as one Ptolemaic province, and Rome's annexation in 58 BC. 6 lessons, 16 placements: Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete; Cyprus Classical Age: Evagoras of Salamis; World Religions: The Five Main Faiths; Alexander the Great and Cyprus; Cyprus Under the Ptolemies; Ancient Civilisations: Rome.

**Totals: 22 lessons, 71 placements stocked** (Level 1: 6 lessons, Level 2: 6, Level 3: 4, Level 4: 6).
