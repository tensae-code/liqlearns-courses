# Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Egyptian hieroglyphic writing”?
a) Egypt develops its own picture writing alongside Mesopotamia's wedges.  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Egypt develops its own picture writing alongside Mesopotamia's wedges.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Egypt develops its own picture writing alongside Mesopotamia's wedges.  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Egypt develops its own picture writing alongside Mesopotamia's wedges.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
