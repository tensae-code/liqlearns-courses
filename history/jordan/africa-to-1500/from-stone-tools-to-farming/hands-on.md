# Hands-on — From Stone Tools to Farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Prehistoric ages, Old/Middle/New Stone Age and Copper Age; Once Upon a Time (كان يا ما كان); Development of housing in my homeland. Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: Cave-dwelling hunters discover fire, settle, farm and grind grain, and copper tools found at Abu Ghosh north of the Dead Sea mark the metal turn.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Cave-dwelling hunters discover fire, settle, farm and grind grain, and copper tools found at Abu Ghosh north of the Dead Sea mark the metal turn. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
