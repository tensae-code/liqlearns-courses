# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cave-dwelling hunters discover fire, settle, farm and grind grain, and copper tools found at Abu Ghosh north of the Dead Sea mark the metal turn. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Once Upon a Time (كان يا ما كان)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Rashid finds grandfather Mustafa's old oil lamp; long ago people lived in caves, struck stones to discover fire, made tools of stone and bone, and hunted and gathered.
