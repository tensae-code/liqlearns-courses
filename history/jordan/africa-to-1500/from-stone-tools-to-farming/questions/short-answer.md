# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Prehistoric ages, Old/Middle/New Stone Age and Copper Age”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** cave-dwelling hunters discover fire, settle, farm and grind grain, and copper tools found at Abu Ghosh north of the Dead Sea mark the metal turn.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Once Upon a Time (كان يا ما كان)”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** rashid finds grandfather Mustafa's old oil lamp; long ago people lived in caves, struck stones to discover fire, made tools of stone and bone, and hunted and gathered.
