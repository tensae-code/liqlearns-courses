# Carthage and the Punic Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Roman rule and the Punic Wars”?
a) Hannibal leads Carthage against Rome and inflicts heavy losses, but short of supplies he falls at Zama in 202…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Carthage and the Punic Wars”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Hannibal leads Carthage against Rome and inflicts heavy losses, but short of supplies he falls at Zama in 202…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Hannibal leads Carthage against Rome and inflicts heavy losses, but short of supplies he falls at Zama in 202…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
