# Carthage and the Punic Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Roman rule and the Punic Wars”.
**Answer:** hannibal leads Carthage against Rome and inflicts heavy losses, but short of supplies he falls at Zama in 202 BCE; Rome takes Bilad al-Sham in 63 BCE and rules for centuries, planting the Decapolis cities.
