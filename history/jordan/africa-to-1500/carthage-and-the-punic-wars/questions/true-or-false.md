# Carthage and the Punic Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hannibal leads Carthage against Rome and inflicts heavy losses, but short of supplies he falls at Zama in 202 BCE; Rome takes Bilad al-Sham in 63 BCE and rules for centuries, planting the Decapolis cities.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Carthage and the Punic Wars” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Hannibal leads Carthage against Rome and inflicts heavy losses, but short of supplies he falls at Zama in 202 BCE; Rome takes Bilad al-Sham in 63 BCE and rules for centuries, planting the Decapolis cities.
