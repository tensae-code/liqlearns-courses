# Carthage and the Punic Wars

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Roman rule and the Punic Wars. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Roman rule and the Punic Wars

Hannibal leads Carthage against Rome and inflicts heavy losses, but short of supplies he falls at Zama in 202 BCE; Rome takes Bilad al-Sham in 63 BCE and rules for centuries, planting the Decapolis cities.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
