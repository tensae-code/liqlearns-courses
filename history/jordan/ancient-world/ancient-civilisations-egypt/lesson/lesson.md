# Ancient Civilisations Egypt

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The collapse phase; Ancient Egyptian civilisation — location and rise; Aspects of life in ancient Egypt; Achievements of ancient Egyptian civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The collapse phase

Egypt rules Bilad al-Sham for some twelve centuries after the Battle of Megiddo in 1468 BCE, where the Canaanites surrender — foreign rule begins the long decline.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ancient Egyptian civilisation — location and rise

The Nile valley unites into one kingdom by 3150 BCE; the Hyksos "shepherd kings" seize the north in the 16th century BCE, and the New Kingdom answers with a strong army, Nubia, campaigns into Bilad al-Sham, Karnak and Hatshepsut.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Aspects of life in ancient Egypt

An absolute hereditary king rules, canals and seasons order the farming year, barter gives way to copper, silver and gold money, and society stands in three classes with women holding a marked place.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 4 — Achievements of ancient Egyptian civilisation

Hieroglyphs, "the words of the gods", carry some 700 signs on walls and papyrus; physicians specialise and embalm the dead for the life after death; the Giza pyramids crown the builders' art.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
