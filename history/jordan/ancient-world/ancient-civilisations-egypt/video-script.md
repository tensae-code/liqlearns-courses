Ancient Egyptian civilisation — location and rise.

The Nile valley unites into one kingdom by 3150 BCE; the Hyksos "shepherd kings" seize the north in the 16th century BCE, and the New Kingdom answers with a strong army, Nubia, campaigns into Bilad al-Sham, Karnak and Hatshepsut.

Aspects of life in ancient Egypt.

An absolute hereditary king rules, canals and seasons order the farming year, barter gives way to copper, silver and gold money, and society stands in three classes with women holding a marked place.

Achievements of ancient Egyptian civilisation.

Hieroglyphs, "the words of the gods", carry some 700 signs on walls and papyrus; physicians specialise and embalm the dead for the life after death; the Giza pyramids crown the builders' art.
