## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The collapse phase** — Egypt rules Bilad al-Sham for some twelve centuries after the Battle of Megiddo in 1468 BCE, where the Canaanites surrender — foreign rule begins the long decline
2. **Ancient Egyptian civilisation — location and rise** — The Nile valley unites into one kingdom by 3150 BCE; the Hyksos "shepherd kings" seize the north in the 16th century BCE, and the New Kingdom answers with a strong army, Nubia, campaigns into Bilad al-Sham, Karnak and Hatshepsut
3. **Aspects of life in ancient Egypt** — An absolute hereditary king rules, canals and seasons order the farming year, barter gives way to copper, silver and gold money, and society stands in three classes with women holding a marked place
4. **Achievements of ancient Egyptian civilisation** — Hieroglyphs, "the words of the gods", carry some 700 signs on walls and papyrus; physicians specialise and embalm the dead for the life after death; the Giza pyramids crown the builders' art
