# Ancient Civilisations Egypt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Egypt rules Bilad al-Sham for some twelve centuries after the Battle of Megiddo in 1468 BCE, where the Canaanites surrender — foreign rule begins the long decline. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ancient Egyptian civilisation — location and rise” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Nile valley unites into one kingdom by 3150 BCE; the Hyksos "shepherd kings" seize the north in the 16th century BCE, and the New Kingdom answers with a strong army, Nubia, campaigns into Bilad al-Sham, Karnak and Hatshepsut.
