# Ancient Civilisations Egypt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The collapse phase”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** egypt rules Bilad al-Sham for some twelve centuries after the Battle of Megiddo in 1468 BCE, where the Canaanites surrender — foreign rule begins the long decline.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Ancient Egyptian civilisation — location and rise”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Nile valley unites into one kingdom by 3150 BCE; the Hyksos "shepherd kings" seize the north in the 16th century BCE, and the New Kingdom answers with a strong army, Nubia, campaigns into Bilad al-Sham, Karnak and Hatshepsut.
