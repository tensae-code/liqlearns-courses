# Ancient Civilisations Egypt — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The collapse phase
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Egypt rules Bilad al-Sham for some twelve centuries after the Battle of Megiddo in 1468 BCE, where the Canaanites surrender — foreign rule begins the long decline — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ancient Egyptian civilisation — location and rise
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Nile valley unites into one kingdom by 3150 BCE; the Hyksos "shepherd kings" seize the north in the 16th century BCE, and the New Kingdom answers with a strong army, Nubia, campaigns into Bilad al-Sham, Karnak and Hatshepsut — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Aspects of life in ancient Egypt
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: An absolute hereditary king rules, canals and seasons order the farming year, barter gives way to copper, silver and gold money, and society stands in three classes with women holding a marked place — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Achievements of ancient Egyptian civilisation
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Hieroglyphs, "the words of the gods", carry some 700 signs on walls and papyrus; physicians specialise and embalm the dead for the life after death; the Giza pyramids crown the builders' art — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
