# The Aztecs Tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Aztec part and the fall of the American civilisations”.
**Answer:** tenochtitlan, a city on a lake, rules an empire; then Columbus sails in 1492 hunting India and names the peoples "Red Indians", Amerigo follows in 1499, and European extermination campaigns kill millions — the book puts the dead at five million.
