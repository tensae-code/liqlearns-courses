# The Aztecs Tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tenochtitlan, a city on a lake, rules an empire; then Columbus sails in 1492 hunting India and names the peoples "Red Indians", Amerigo follows in 1499, and European extermination campaigns kill millions — the book puts the dead at five million.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Aztecs Tenochtitlan” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Tenochtitlan, a city on a lake, rules an empire; then Columbus sails in 1492 hunting India and names the peoples "Red Indians", Amerigo follows in 1499, and European extermination campaigns kill millions — the book puts the dead at five million.
