# The Aztecs Tenochtitlan — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Tenochtitlan, a city on a lake, rules an empire; then Columbus sails in 1492 hunting India and names the peoples "Red Indians", Amerigo follows in 1499, and European extermination campaigns kill millions — the book puts the dead at five million — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
