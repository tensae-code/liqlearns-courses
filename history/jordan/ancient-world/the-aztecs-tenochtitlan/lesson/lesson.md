# The Aztecs Tenochtitlan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Aztec part and the fall of the American civilisations. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Aztec part and the fall of the American civilisations

Tenochtitlan, a city on a lake, rules an empire; then Columbus sails in 1492 hunting India and names the peoples "Red Indians", Amerigo follows in 1499, and European extermination campaigns kill millions — the book puts the dead at five million.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
