# Arab and Islamic Philosophy al Kindi al Farabi Ibn Rushd — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Arab-Islamic philosophy”.
**Answer:** al-Kindi of Kufa, "philosopher of the Arabs" in the Abbasid age; al-Farabi's Virtuous City; and Ibn Rushd, born in Cordoba in 1126, judge of Seville and Cordoba, who carried Aristotle and Plato across the Islamic world.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Arab and Islamic Philosophy al Kindi al Farabi Ibn Rushd”, where does it happen, and what turns?
