# Arab and Islamic Philosophy al Kindi al Farabi Ibn Rushd — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Al-Kindi of Kufa, "philosopher of the Arabs" in the Abbasid age; al-Farabi's Virtuous City; and Ibn Rushd, born in Cordoba in 1126, judge of Seville and Cordoba, who carried Aristotle and Plato across the Islamic world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Arab and Islamic Philosophy al Kindi al Farabi Ibn Rushd” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Al-Kindi of Kufa, "philosopher of the Arabs" in the Abbasid age; al-Farabi's Virtuous City; and Ibn Rushd, born in Cordoba in 1126, judge of Seville and Cordoba, who carried Aristotle and Plato across the Islamic world.
