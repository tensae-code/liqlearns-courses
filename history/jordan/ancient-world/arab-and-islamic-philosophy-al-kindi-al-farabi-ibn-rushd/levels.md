# Arab and Islamic Philosophy al Kindi al Farabi Ibn Rushd — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Al-Kindi of Kufa, "philosopher of the Arabs" in the Abbasid age; al-Farabi's Virtuous City; and Ibn Rushd, born in Cordoba in 1126, judge of Seville and Cordoba, who carried Aristotle and Plato across the Islamic world — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
