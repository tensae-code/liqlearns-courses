# Video script — Arab and Islamic Philosophy al Kindi al Farabi Ibn Rushd

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Al-Kindi of Kufa, "philosopher of the Arabs" in the Abbasid age; al-Farabi's Virtuous City; and Ibn Rushd, born in Cordoba in 1126, judge of Seville and Cordoba, who carried Aristotle and Plato across the Islamic world.
