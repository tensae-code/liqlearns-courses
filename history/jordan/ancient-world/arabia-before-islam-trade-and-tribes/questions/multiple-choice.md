# Arabia Before Islam Trade and Tribes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Arabian Peninsula, its place and importance”?
a) Desert tribes and oasis towns grow rich as middlemen carrying frankincense and spices, poets hang the Muallaqa…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Arabia Before Islam Trade and Tribes”?
