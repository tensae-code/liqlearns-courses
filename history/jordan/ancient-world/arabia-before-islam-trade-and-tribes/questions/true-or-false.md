# Arabia Before Islam Trade and Tribes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Desert tribes and oasis towns grow rich as middlemen carrying frankincense and spices, poets hang the Muallaqat odes as Arabia's memory.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Arabia Before Islam Trade and Tribes” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
