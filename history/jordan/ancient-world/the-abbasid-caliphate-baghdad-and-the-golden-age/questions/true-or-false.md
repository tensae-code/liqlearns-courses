# The Abbasid Caliphate Baghdad and the Golden Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The secret call runs from al-Humayma, the Umayyads fall at the Zab, Baghdad rises, and under Harun al-Rashid the state peaks — the House of Wisdom makes the capital a centre of learning, run through diwans and the new office of vizier. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Civilisational aspects of the Abbasid state” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Sinbad's tales open an age of translation — Hunayn ibn Ishaq is paid his book's weight in gold — paper spreads, observatories rise, and Maryam al-Astrulabi perfects the astrolabe at Aleppo.
