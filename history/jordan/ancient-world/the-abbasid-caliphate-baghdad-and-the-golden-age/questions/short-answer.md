# The Abbasid Caliphate Baghdad and the Golden Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Abbasid state — rise and development”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the secret call runs from al-Humayma, the Umayyads fall at the Zab, Baghdad rises, and under Harun al-Rashid the state peaks — the House of Wisdom makes the capital a centre of learning, run through diwans and the new office of vizier.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Civilisational aspects of the Abbasid state”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** sinbad's tales open an age of translation — Hunayn ibn Ishaq is paid his book's weight in gold — paper spreads, observatories rise, and Maryam al-Astrulabi perfects the astrolabe at Aleppo.
