# Yemen Kingdoms Saba Himyar and the Marib Dam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yemeni kings dam the floods at Marib, write in Musnad script and raise Ghumdan palace, until the dam's collapse ends the kingdoms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Yemen Kingdoms Saba Himyar and the Marib Dam” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Yemeni kings dam the floods at Marib, write in Musnad script and raise Ghumdan palace, until the dam's collapse ends the kingdoms.
