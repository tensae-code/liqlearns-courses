# Yemen Kingdoms Saba Himyar and the Marib Dam

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Old kingdoms of Yemen (Ma'in, Saba, Qataban, Himyar). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Old kingdoms of Yemen (Ma'in, Saba, Qataban, Himyar)

Yemeni kings dam the floods at Marib, write in Musnad script and raise Ghumdan palace, until the dam's collapse ends the kingdoms.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
