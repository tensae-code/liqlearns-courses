# Medieval World the Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The rise of the Ottoman state”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a Turkish frontier tribe in Anatolia becomes a state: Orhan (1326–1360) takes Bursa as capital and founds the new army — the Janissaries raised through the devshirme.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The conquest of Constantinople (Istanbul)”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** mehmed II takes Constantinople in 857 AH / 1453 CE; the Byzantine Empire falls, the city becomes the Ottoman capital and a great trading centre, and the state turns to building fleets — historians mark it the end of the middle ages.
