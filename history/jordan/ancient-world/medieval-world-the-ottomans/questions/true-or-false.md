# Medieval World the Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A Turkish frontier tribe in Anatolia becomes a state: Orhan (1326–1360) takes Bursa as capital and founds the new army — the Janissaries raised through the devshirme. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The conquest of Constantinople (Istanbul)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Mehmed II takes Constantinople in 857 AH / 1453 CE; the Byzantine Empire falls, the city becomes the Ottoman capital and a great trading centre, and the state turns to building fleets — historians mark it the end of the middle ages.
