# Ancient Civilisations Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jerash in the north keeps its colonnaded street and south and north theatres, with antiquities from the Greek, Roman, Byzantine and Islamic ages. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Journey in Irbid Governorate” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: At Umm Qais (Gadara) and Abila the children walk Roman theatres of basalt stone seating thousands and a Byzantine church with mosaic floors, wondering how their builders raised the columns.
