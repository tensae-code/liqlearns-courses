# Ancient Civilisations Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Jerash”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** jerash in the north keeps its colonnaded street and south and north theatres, with antiquities from the Greek, Roman, Byzantine and Islamic ages.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Journey in Irbid Governorate”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** at Umm Qais (Gadara) and Abila the children walk Roman theatres of basalt stone seating thousands and a Byzantine church with mosaic floors, wondering how their builders raised the columns.
