# Jordan in the Crusader Ayyubid and Mamluk Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Jordan under the Frankish invasion of Bilad al-Sham”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** europe's kings exploit a divided Islamic world and take Jerusalem in 1099, planting Edessa, Antioch, Tripoli and the Kingdom of Jerusalem; Baldwin raises Montreal castle at Shobak and seizes Ayla (Aqaba) to command the Syria–Egypt road, and the Franks rebuild Karak — until Nur al-Din Zangi reads Jordan's importance and moves, setting the stage for Hattin (1187).

**2.** Who is in the story of “Jordan in the Ayyubid age”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** saladin, founder of the Ayyubid state (1173–1229 in the book's dating), treats Jordan as the link between Egypt and Syria, the muster-ground and the Hajj road; having freed Karak he grants it to his brother al-Adil, and al-Mu'azzam Isa rebuilds its walls, imports craftsmen and makes the city feed itself; al-Nasir Dawud follows; Ayyubid mosques rise at Rimoun, Irbid, Tabaqat Fahl, Harthas and Bayt Ras, and Jordanian scholars — from Ayla, Shobak, Husban, Ajloun, Ba'un and Karak — teach as far as the Ba'uniyya dome in Damascus, before the Mongols enter Damascus in 1260.
