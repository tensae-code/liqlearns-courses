# Persia Cyrus Roads Post and Zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cyrus (560–529 BCE) defeats the Babylonians and takes Jordan in 539 BCE; Darius reaches into the Greek cities, and Alexander of Macedon ends the Achaemenid state before the Parthians inherit the east.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Persia Cyrus Roads Post and Zoroaster” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Cyrus (560–529 BCE) defeats the Babylonians and takes Jordan in 539 BCE; Darius reaches into the Greek cities, and Alexander of Macedon ends the Achaemenid state before the Parthians inherit the east.
