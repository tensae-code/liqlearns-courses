# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Trade then and now”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) People once bartered wheat for oil, peddlers carried goods on donkeys and camels from village to village, then…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) People once bartered wheat for oil, peddlers carried goods on donkeys and camels from village to village, then…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Industries and trade”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Purple dye from sea shells, glass and cedar ships make the Phoenicians famous across the ancient world, while…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Purple dye from sea shells, glass and cedar ships make the Phoenicians famous across the ancient world, while…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
