# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** People once bartered wheat for oil, peddlers carried goods on donkeys and camels from village to village, then fixed shops, malls and e-commerce took over. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Industries and trade” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Purple dye from sea shells, glass and cedar ships make the Phoenicians famous across the ancient world, while Amorite and Aramean caravans cross the desert guided by men who know its wells.
