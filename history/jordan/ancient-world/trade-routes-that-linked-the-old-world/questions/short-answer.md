# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Trade then and now”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** people once bartered wheat for oil, peddlers carried goods on donkeys and camels from village to village, then fixed shops, malls and e-commerce took over.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Industries and trade”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** purple dye from sea shells, glass and cedar ships make the Phoenicians famous across the ancient world, while Amorite and Aramean caravans cross the desert guided by men who know its wells.
