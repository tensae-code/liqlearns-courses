# Ancient Civilisations China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “What civilisation is, its factors and collapse, Chinese civilisation example”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Civilisations rise on social, economic and cultural factors and can fall to war, disease and climate.  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Civilisations rise on social, economic and cultural factors and can fall to war, disease and climate.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The civilisation of China”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A river civilisation sealed off by mountains and deserts grows through dynasty after dynasty; Tang China meets…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A river civilisation sealed off by mountains and deserts grows through dynasty after dynasty; Tang China meets…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
