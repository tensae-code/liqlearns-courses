# Ancient Civilisations China — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Civilisations rise on social, economic and cultural factors and can fall to war, disease and climate. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The civilisation of China” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A river civilisation sealed off by mountains and deserts grows through dynasty after dynasty; Tang China meets the Arabs at the Battle of Talas in 751; gunpowder-age China is forced open by the First Opium War and the unequal Treaty of Nanking in 1843, until Mao Zedong proclaims the People's Republic in 1949.
