# Ancient Civilisations China — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “What civilisation is, its factors and collapse, Chinese civilisation example”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** civilisations rise on social, economic and cultural factors and can fall to war, disease and climate.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The civilisation of China”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a river civilisation sealed off by mountains and deserts grows through dynasty after dynasty; Tang China meets the Arabs at the Battle of Talas in 751; gunpowder-age China is forced open by the First Opium War and the unequal Treaty of Nanking in 1843, until Mao Zedong proclaims the People's Republic in 1949.
