The civilisation of China.

A river civilisation sealed off by mountains and deserts grows through dynasty after dynasty; Tang China meets the Arabs at the Battle of Talas in 751; gunpowder-age China is forced open by the First Opium War and the unequal Treaty of Nanking in 1843, until Mao Zedong proclaims the People's Republic in 1949.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
