# Aksum Kingdom and its Stelae — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aksum in Ethiopia grows rich trading between Africa, the Middle East and India, and its stone stelae and churches still stand.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Aksum Kingdom and its Stelae” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Aksum in Ethiopia grows rich trading between Africa, the Middle East and India, and its stone stelae and churches still stand.
