# Ancient Civilisations Greece Crete

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Greek civilisation; Macedonian rule; Greek philosophy; Foreign relations of ancient Egypt. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Greek civilisation

The Greeks excel in science, mathematics, philosophy and building, leaving theatres like Dionysus.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Macedonian rule

Alexander of Macedon seizes the Phoenician cities and Bilad al-Sham passes under Greek rule, 336–323 BCE.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Greek philosophy

Socrates questions Athens in its squares, Plato writes his thought as dialogue, and Aristotle — born in Macedonia in 384 BCE, Plato's pupil — founds logic and the Peripatetic school.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 4 — Foreign relations of ancient Egypt

Assyrians, Persians and Abyssinians invade a weakening Egypt until Alexander the Macedonian takes it in 332 BCE and founds Alexandria; the Ptolemies and Cleopatra follow, then Rome holds Egypt until 640 CE.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
