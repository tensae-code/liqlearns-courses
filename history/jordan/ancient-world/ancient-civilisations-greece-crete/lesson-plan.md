## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Greek civilisation** — The Greeks excel in science, mathematics, philosophy and building, leaving theatres like Dionysus
2. **Macedonian rule** — Alexander of Macedon seizes the Phoenician cities and Bilad al-Sham passes under Greek rule, 336–323 BCE
3. **Greek philosophy** — Socrates questions Athens in its squares, Plato writes his thought as dialogue, and Aristotle — born in Macedonia in 384 BCE, Plato's pupil — founds logic and the Peripatetic school
4. **Foreign relations of ancient Egypt** — Assyrians, Persians and Abyssinians invade a weakening Egypt until Alexander the Macedonian takes it in 332 BCE and founds Alexandria; the Ptolemies and Cleopatra follow, then Rome holds Egypt until 640 CE

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
