# Video script — Ancient Civilisations Greece Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Greek civilisation.

The Greeks excel in science, mathematics, philosophy and building, leaving theatres like Dionysus.

Macedonian rule.

Alexander of Macedon seizes the Phoenician cities and Bilad al-Sham passes under Greek rule, 336–323 BCE.

Greek philosophy.

Socrates questions Athens in its squares, Plato writes his thought as dialogue, and Aristotle — born in Macedonia in 384 BCE, Plato's pupil — founds logic and the Peripatetic school.

Foreign relations of ancient Egypt.

Assyrians, Persians and Abyssinians invade a weakening Egypt until Alexander the Macedonian takes it in 332 BCE and founds Alexandria; the Ptolemies and Cleopatra follow, then Rome holds Egypt until 640 CE.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
