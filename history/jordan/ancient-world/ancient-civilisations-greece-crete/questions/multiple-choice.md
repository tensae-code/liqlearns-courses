# Ancient Civilisations Greece Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Greek civilisation”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The Greeks excel in science, mathematics, philosophy and building, leaving theatres like Dionysus.  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Greeks excel in science, mathematics, philosophy and building, leaving theatres like Dionysus.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Macedonian rule”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Alexander of Macedon seizes the Phoenician cities and Bilad al-Sham passes under Greek rule, 336–323 BCE.  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Alexander of Macedon seizes the Phoenician cities and Bilad al-Sham passes under Greek rule, 336–323 BCE.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
