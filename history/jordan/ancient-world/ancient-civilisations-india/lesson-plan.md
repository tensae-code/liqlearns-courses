## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The civilisation of India** — Harappa and Mohenjo-daro raise planned cities in the Indus valley; Islamic kingdoms follow in Bengal, Sindh and the Deccan and the Qutb Minar towers over Delhi; the East India Company creeps from Madras, Bombay and Calcutta (1700) into conquest, until Britain partitions the subcontinent in 1947 into Nehru's India and Jinnah's Pakistan — and Bengal breaks away as Bangladesh in 1971
2. **Aspects of Indian civilisation** — The Vedas, "knowledge" in four parts, shape a society where religion touches everything; women study, sing, dance and even rule; smiths forge famous steel and mint gold coins; healers practise surgery and yoga, and mathematicians give the world the decimal numbers
