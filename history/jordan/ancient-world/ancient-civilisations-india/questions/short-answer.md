# Ancient Civilisations India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The civilisation of India”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** harappa and Mohenjo-daro raise planned cities in the Indus valley; Islamic kingdoms follow in Bengal, Sindh and the Deccan and the Qutb Minar towers over Delhi; the East India Company creeps from Madras, Bombay and Calcutta (1700) into conquest, until Britain partitions the subcontinent in 1947 into Nehru's India and Jinnah's Pakistan — and Bengal breaks away as Bangladesh in 1971.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Aspects of Indian civilisation”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Vedas, "knowledge" in four parts, shape a society where religion touches everything; women study, sing, dance and even rule; smiths forge famous steel and mint gold coins; healers practise surgery and yoga, and mathematicians give the world the decimal numbers.
