# Ancient Civilisations India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Harappa and Mohenjo-daro raise planned cities in the Indus valley; Islamic kingdoms follow in Bengal, Sindh and the Deccan and the Qutb Minar towers over Delhi; the East India Company creeps from Madras, Bombay and Calcutta (1700) into conquest, until Britain partitions the subcontinent in 1947 into Nehru's India and Jinnah's Pakistan — and Bengal breaks away as Bangladesh in 1971. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Aspects of Indian civilisation” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Vedas, "knowledge" in four parts, shape a society where religion touches everything; women study, sing, dance and even rule; smiths forge famous steel and mint gold coins; healers practise surgery and yoga, and mathematicians give the world the decimal numbers.
