**1.** What happens in the part called “The civilisation of India”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Harappa and Mohenjo-daro raise planned cities in the Indus valley; Islamic kingdoms follow in Bengal, Sindh an…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
