# Ancient Civilisations India

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The civilisation of India; Aspects of Indian civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The civilisation of India

Harappa and Mohenjo-daro raise planned cities in the Indus valley; Islamic kingdoms follow in Bengal, Sindh and the Deccan and the Qutb Minar towers over Delhi; the East India Company creeps from Madras, Bombay and Calcutta (1700) into conquest, until Britain partitions the subcontinent in 1947 into Nehru's India and Jinnah's Pakistan — and Bengal breaks away as Bangladesh in 1971.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aspects of Indian civilisation

The Vedas, "knowledge" in four parts, shape a society where religion touches everything; women study, sing, dance and even rule; smiths forge famous steel and mint gold coins; healers practise surgery and yoga, and mathematicians give the world the decimal numbers.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
