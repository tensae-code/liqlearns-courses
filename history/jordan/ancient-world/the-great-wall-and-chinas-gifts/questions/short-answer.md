# The Great Wall and Chinas Gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Aspects of Chinese civilisation”.
**Answer:** an emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam walks the Silk Roads into China and the Great Mosque of Xi'an rises in 742; farmers stand honoured above traders; silk, porcelain and bronze sail the maritime Silk Road from Quanzhou — the ancestor of today's Belt and Road.
