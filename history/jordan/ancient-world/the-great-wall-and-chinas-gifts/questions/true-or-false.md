# The Great Wall and Chinas Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam walks the Silk Roads into China and the Great Mosque of Xi'an rises in 742; farmers stand honoured above traders; silk, porcelain and bronze sail the maritime Silk Road from Quanzhou — the ancestor of today's Belt and Road.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Great Wall and Chinas Gifts” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: An emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam walks the Silk Roads into China and the Great Mosque of Xi'an rises in 742; farmers stand honoured above traders; silk, porcelain and bronze sail the maritime Silk Road from Quanzhou — the ancestor of today's Belt and Road.
