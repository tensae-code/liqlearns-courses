# The Great Wall and Chinas Gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Aspects of Chinese civilisation”?
a) An emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam wal…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) An emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam wal…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Great Wall and Chinas Gifts”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) An emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam wal…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) An emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam wal…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
