# The Great Wall and Chinas Gifts

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Aspects of Chinese civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Aspects of Chinese civilisation

An emperor-son-of-heaven rules a hereditary empire; scribes write in picture-signs with no alphabet; Islam walks the Silk Roads into China and the Great Mosque of Xi'an rises in 742; farmers stand honoured above traders; silk, porcelain and bronze sail the maritime Silk Road from Quanzhou — the ancestor of today's Belt and Road.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
