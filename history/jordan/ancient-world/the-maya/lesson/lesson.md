# The Maya

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Maya civilisation; The ancient American civilisations, the Maya part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Maya civilisation

In Central America the Maya raise stepped pyramids and master astronomy and the calendar.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ancient American civilisations, the Maya part

In the forests of Central America the Maya raise temple cities, write in picture glyphs on stone, pottery and bark-paper books, and send their children to temple schools.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In Central America the Maya raise stepped pyramids and master astronomy and the calendar. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Maya. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
