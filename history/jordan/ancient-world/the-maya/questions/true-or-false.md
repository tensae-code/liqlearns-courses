# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Central America the Maya raise stepped pyramids and master astronomy and the calendar. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The ancient American civilisations, the Maya part” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In the forests of Central America the Maya raise temple cities, write in picture glyphs on stone, pottery and bark-paper books, and send their children to temple schools.
