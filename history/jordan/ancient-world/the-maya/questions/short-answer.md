# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Maya civilisation”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** in Central America the Maya raise stepped pyramids and master astronomy and the calendar.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The ancient American civilisations, the Maya part”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** in the forests of Central America the Maya raise temple cities, write in picture glyphs on stone, pottery and bark-paper books, and send their children to temple schools.
