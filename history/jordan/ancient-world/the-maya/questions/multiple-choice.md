# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Maya civilisation”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) In Central America the Maya raise stepped pyramids and master astronomy and the calendar.  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In Central America the Maya raise stepped pyramids and master astronomy and the calendar.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The ancient American civilisations, the Maya part”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In the forests of Central America the Maya raise temple cities, write in picture glyphs on stone, pottery and…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In the forests of Central America the Maya raise temple cities, write in picture glyphs on stone, pottery and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
