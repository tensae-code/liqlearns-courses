# Phoenicians Canaanites and the Cities of the Levant Coast — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Canaanite peoples migrate north from Arabia and raise the Phoenician kingdoms — Ugarit, Byblos, Arwad, Sidon and Tyre — then plant trading colonies across the Mediterranean, Carthage among them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Civilisational aspects and achievements” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Phoenicians cut writing down to a 22-letter alphabet, written right to left — the mother from which the world's alphabets descend.
