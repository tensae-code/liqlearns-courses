# Phoenicians Canaanites and the Cities of the Levant Coast — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The ancient civilisations of Bilad al-Sham (their rise)”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** canaanite peoples migrate north from Arabia and raise the Phoenician kingdoms — Ugarit, Byblos, Arwad, Sidon and Tyre — then plant trading colonies across the Mediterranean, Carthage among them.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Civilisational aspects and achievements”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Phoenicians cut writing down to a 22-letter alphabet, written right to left — the mother from which the world's alphabets descend.
