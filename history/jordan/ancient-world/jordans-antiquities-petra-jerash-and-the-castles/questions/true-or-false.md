# Jordans Antiquities Petra Jerash and the Castles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jordan's sites tell its long history — rock-carved Petra, black-stone Umm al-Jimal, Qasr al-Abd with its carved animals, Amman's Citadel hill, and the defence castles of Tafila, Karak and Ajloun guarding the trade roads. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Umm al-Rasas” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: South of the capital, Umm al-Rasas keeps its castle and mosaic-floored churches, a world-heritage site.
