# Jordans Antiquities Petra Jerash and the Castles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Our beautiful antiquities”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** jordan's sites tell its long history — rock-carved Petra, black-stone Umm al-Jimal, Qasr al-Abd with its carved animals, Amman's Citadel hill, and the defence castles of Tafila, Karak and Ajloun guarding the trade roads.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Umm al-Rasas”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** south of the capital, Umm al-Rasas keeps its castle and mosaic-floored churches, a world-heritage site.
