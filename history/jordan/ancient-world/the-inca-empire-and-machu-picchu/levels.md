# The Inca Empire and Machu Picchu — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — High in the Andes the Inca organise whole villages in the collective ayllu system, speak Quechua, count in decimals, watch the sun and stars, divide the year into twelve months of thirty days, and build Machu Picchu, the stone city in the clouds — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
