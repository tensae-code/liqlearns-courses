# Lesson plan — The Inca Empire and Machu Picchu

**Course:** History — Jordan · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Inca Empire and Machu Picchu
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World and Jordan's Own Ancient Ground (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** High in the Andes the Inca organise whole villages in the collective ayllu system, speak Quechua, count in decimals, watch the sun and stars, divide the year into twelve months of thirty days, and build Machu Picchu, the stone city in the clouds.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World and Jordan's Own Ancient Ground — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
