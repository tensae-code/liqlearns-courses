# The Inca Empire and Machu Picchu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Inca part”.
**Answer:** high in the Andes the Inca organise whole villages in the collective ayllu system, speak Quechua, count in decimals, watch the sun and stars, divide the year into twelve months of thirty days, and build Machu Picchu, the stone city in the clouds.
