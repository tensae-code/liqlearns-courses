# The Inca Empire and Machu Picchu — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** High in the Andes the Inca organise whole villages in the collective ayllu system, speak Quechua, count in decimals, watch the sun and stars, divide the year into twelve months of thirty days, and build Machu Picchu, the stone city in the clouds.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Inca Empire and Machu Picchu” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: High in the Andes the Inca organise whole villages in the collective ayllu system, speak Quechua, count in decimals, watch the sun and stars, divide the year into twelve months of thirty days, and build Machu Picchu, the stone city in the clouds.
