# Ancient Civilisations Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Cuneiform writing”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Around 3500 BCE Mesopotamian scribes press wedge marks into wet clay tablets and writing is born.  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Around 3500 BCE Mesopotamian scribes press wedge marks into wet clay tablets and writing is born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Bronze Age and Iron Age”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Bronze (copper + tin) makes stronger weapons, then iron farming tools feed more people and world trade stirs.  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Bronze (copper + tin) makes stronger weapons, then iron farming tools feed more people and world trade stirs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
