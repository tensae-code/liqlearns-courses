# Ancient Civilisations Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Cuneiform writing”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** around 3500 BCE Mesopotamian scribes press wedge marks into wet clay tablets and writing is born.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Bronze Age and Iron Age”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** bronze (copper + tin) makes stronger weapons, then iron farming tools feed more people and world trade stirs.
