# Ancient Civilisations Mesopotamia — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cuneiform writing
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Around 3500 BCE Mesopotamian scribes press wedge marks into wet clay tablets and writing is born — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bronze Age and Iron Age
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Bronze (copper + tin) makes stronger weapons, then iron farming tools feed more people and world trade stirs — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Writing and means of communication
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: People draw things on walls, clay and stone, write on bark then paper with pens, and today on computers; messages once travelled by smoke and carrier pigeon (zagil) — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Sumerian civilisation
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: On the Euphrates' bank the Sumerians invent irrigation canals, the plough and cuneiform writing; King Gilgamesh of Uruk becomes an epic, half man and half god, and the ziggurat of Ur climbs over the flood plain — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — The Akkadian civilisation
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Before Naram-Sin's Victory Stele the learner meets the first empire: Akkad's kings bind the city-states, spread trade roads and security — then tyranny, priestly quarrels and Sumerian revolts wear the empire down — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — The Babylonian civilisation
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In the Louvre's halls, Hammurabi's Babylon — the king who united the two river lands under law and justice; his people carve the Lion of Babylon, draw land maps on clay, chart the stars and compound medicines — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — The Assyrian civilisation
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In a library of clay tablets, Assyria the military civilisation: Sargon II builds Khorsabad, Ashurbanipal gathers the Nineveh library, and carved palaces record every conquest — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 8 — The Chaldean civilisation
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: An excavator reads Babylon's second rise: Nebuchadnezzar II makes Chaldean Babylon flourish in arms, building and art — the Hanging Gardens among its wonders — until weak kings and inner wars open the gate to Cyrus of Persia — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
