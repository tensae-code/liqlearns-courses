# Jordans Rock Inscriptions Safaitic Thamudic Nabataean — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The badia from north to south is an open museum of scratched stones, a register of every people and caravan that passed; the Safaitic herders of the north-eastern desert leave their names, tribes, sheep, camels and horses; the Thamudic tribes known to Assyrian records draw people and animals across Wadi Rum; the Nabataeans write an Aramaic script that grows, in time, into the Arabic script itself — the Turkmaniyya tomb text at Petra is its longest inscription — and the Bedouin wasm brand-marks on camels still echo the ancient letters.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Jordans Rock Inscriptions Safaitic Thamudic Nabataean” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The badia from north to south is an open museum of scratched stones, a register of every people and caravan that passed; the Safaitic herders of the north-eastern desert leave their names, tribes, sheep, camels and horses; the Thamudic tribes known to Assyrian records draw people and animals across Wadi Rum; the Nabataeans write an Aramaic script that grows, in time, into the Arabic script itself — the Turkmaniyya tomb text at Petra is its longest inscription — and the Bedouin wasm brand-marks on camels still echo the ancient letters.
