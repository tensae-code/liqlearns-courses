# Jordans Rock Inscriptions Safaitic Thamudic Nabataean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The ancient rock inscriptions of Jordan”.
**Answer:** the badia from north to south is an open museum of scratched stones, a register of every people and caravan that passed; the Safaitic herders of the north-eastern desert leave their names, tribes, sheep, camels and horses; the Thamudic tribes known to Assyrian records draw people and animals across Wadi Rum; the Nabataeans write an Aramaic script that grows, in time, into the Arabic script itself — the Turkmaniyya tomb text at Petra is its longest inscription — and the Bedouin wasm brand-marks on camels still echo the ancient letters.
