# Hijaz Cities Mecca Yathrib and Taif Before Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mecca thrives on the Kaaba, Zamzam and Quraysh caravans, Yathrib farms, Taif walls itself, and Ukaz fair mixes trade with poetry contests.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Hijaz Cities Mecca Yathrib and Taif Before Islam” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Mecca thrives on the Kaaba, Zamzam and Quraysh caravans, Yathrib farms, Taif walls itself, and Ukaz fair mixes trade with poetry contests.
