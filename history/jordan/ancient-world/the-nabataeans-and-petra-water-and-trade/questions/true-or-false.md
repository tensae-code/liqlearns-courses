# The Nabataeans and Petra Water and Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Arab Nabataeans adapt to the desert, harvesting rainwater with dams, channels and rock-cut cisterns, protecting Petra from floods and trading widely. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Petra” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Samir and Salma enter the rose-red city through the Siq rock corridor and see the Treasury, the Court and the Monastery the Nabataeans carved.
