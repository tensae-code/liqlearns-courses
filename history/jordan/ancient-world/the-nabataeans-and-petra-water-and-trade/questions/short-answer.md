# The Nabataeans and Petra Water and Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Nabataean kingdom”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Arab Nabataeans adapt to the desert, harvesting rainwater with dams, channels and rock-cut cisterns, protecting Petra from floods and trading widely.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Petra”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** samir and Salma enter the rose-red city through the Siq rock corridor and see the Treasury, the Court and the Monastery the Nabataeans carved.
