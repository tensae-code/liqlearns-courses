# Medieval World Byzantium and Justinian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Byzantine Jordan (395–636)”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** theodosius splits the empire between Honorius in the West (Rome falls to the Goths in 476) and Arcadius in the East at Constantinople; Jordan's churches multiply, and Madaba lays the oldest mosaic map of the Holy Land (560 CE), still on its church floor today; south-east of Amman at al-Raqim, the Cave of the Sleepers recalls the Emperor Decius, who persecuted the Christians who hid there.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Byzantine Jordan”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** to the Madaba map and the Cave of the Sleepers this telling adds al-Maghtas at Wadi al-Kharrar north of the Dead Sea, where Christ was baptised — a UNESCO-listed pilgrimage site drawing thousands — and Madaba's own Mosaic Institute, teaching the Byzantine craft as a living Jordanian livelihood.
