# Medieval World Byzantium and Justinian — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Theodosius splits the empire between Honorius in the West (Rome falls to the Goths in 476) and Arcadius in the East at Constantinople; Jordan's churches multiply, and Madaba lays the oldest mosaic map of the Holy Land (560 CE), still on its church floor today; south-east of Amman at al-Raqim, the Cave of the Sleepers recalls the Emperor Decius, who persecuted the Christians who hid there. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Byzantine Jordan” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: To the Madaba map and the Cave of the Sleepers this telling adds al-Maghtas at Wadi al-Kharrar north of the Dead Sea, where Christ was baptised — a UNESCO-listed pilgrimage site drawing thousands — and Madaba's own Mosaic Institute, teaching the Byzantine craft as a living Jordanian livelihood.
