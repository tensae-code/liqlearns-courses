## Sub lesson 1 — Byzantine Jordan (395–636)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Theodosius splits the empire between Honorius in the West (Rome falls to the Goths in 476) and Arcadius in the East at Constantinople; Jordan's churches multiply, and Madaba lays the oldest mosaic map of the Holy Land (560 CE), still on its church floor today; south-east of Amman at al-Raqim, the Cave of the Sleepers recalls the Emperor Decius, who persecuted the Christians who hid there — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Byzantine Jordan
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: To the Madaba map and the Cave of the Sleepers this telling adds al-Maghtas at Wadi al-Kharrar north of the Dead Sea, where Christ was baptised — a UNESCO-listed pilgrimage site drawing thousands — and Madaba's own Mosaic Institute, teaching the Byzantine craft as a living Jordanian livelihood — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
