# Video script — Medieval World Byzantium and Justinian

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Theodosius splits the empire between Honorius in the West (Rome falls to the Goths in 476) and Arcadius in the East at Constantinople; Jordan's churches multiply, and Madaba lays the oldest mosaic map of the Holy Land (560 CE), still on its church floor today; south-east of Amman at al-Raqim, the Cave of the Sleepers recalls the Emperor Decius, who persecuted the Christians who hid there.
