# Jordan Iron Age Kingdoms Ammon Moab and Edom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Ancient civilisations in Jordan”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the kingdoms of Edom, Moab and Ammon rise on Jordan's land; Moab's King Mesha records his victories on the basalt Mesha Stele from his capital Dhiban.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Journey in the Capital Governorate”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** amman, among the oldest inhabited cities in the world, was the Ammonites' capital Rabbat Ammon from the 7th century BCE, and its Citadel still overlooks the city.
