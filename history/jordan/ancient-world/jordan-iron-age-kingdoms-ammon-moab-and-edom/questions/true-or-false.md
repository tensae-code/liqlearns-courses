# Jordan Iron Age Kingdoms Ammon Moab and Edom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The kingdoms of Edom, Moab and Ammon rise on Jordan's land; Moab's King Mesha records his victories on the basalt Mesha Stele from his capital Dhiban. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Journey in the Capital Governorate” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Amman, among the oldest inhabited cities in the world, was the Ammonites' capital Rabbat Ammon from the 7th century BCE, and its Citadel still overlooks the city.
