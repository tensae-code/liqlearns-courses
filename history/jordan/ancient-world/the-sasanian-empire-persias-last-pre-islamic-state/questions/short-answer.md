# The Sasanian Empire Persias Last Pre Islamic State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Sasanian state (226–651 CE)”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from Istakhr the Sasanians raise Persia's last pre-Islamic empire and seat it at al-Mada'in (Ctesiphon), near the Roman frontier and Mesopotamia's stored learning.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Government, institutions and public life”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** ardashir I, crowned 226, makes Zoroastrianism the state religion and centralises rule under the Shahanshah, "King of Kings"; Khosrow I Anushirvan the Just raises the state to its peak of cities, bridges, learning and four-class society.
