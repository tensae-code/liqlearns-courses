# The Sasanian Empire Persias Last Pre Islamic State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Istakhr the Sasanians raise Persia's last pre-Islamic empire and seat it at al-Mada'in (Ctesiphon), near the Roman frontier and Mesopotamia's stored learning. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Government, institutions and public life” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Ardashir I, crowned 226, makes Zoroastrianism the state religion and centralises rule under the Shahanshah, "King of Kings"; Khosrow I Anushirvan the Just raises the state to its peak of cities, bridges, learning and four-class society.
