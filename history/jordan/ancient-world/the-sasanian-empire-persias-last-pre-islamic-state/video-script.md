The Sasanian state (226–651 CE).

From Istakhr the Sasanians raise Persia's last pre-Islamic empire and seat it at al-Mada'in (Ctesiphon), near the Roman frontier and Mesopotamia's stored learning.

Government, institutions and public life.

Ardashir I, crowned 226, makes Zoroastrianism the state religion and centralises rule under the Shahanshah, "King of Kings"; Khosrow I Anushirvan the Just raises the state to its peak of cities, bridges, learning and four-class society.

The Sasanian state's foreign relations.

The Arab Muntherids of al-Hira, titled "King of the Arabs", shield Persia's desert flank; rivalry with Byzantium never sleeps, while silk-road trade binds Ctesiphon to China's ports.
