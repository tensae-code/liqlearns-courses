# Hands-on — The Sasanian Empire Persias Last Pre Islamic State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: The Sasanian state (226–651 CE); Government, institutions and public life; The Sasanian state's foreign relations. Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: From Istakhr the Sasanians raise Persia's last pre-Islamic empire and seat it at al-Mada'in (Ctesiphon), near the Roman frontier and Mesopotamia's stored learning.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From Istakhr the Sasanians raise Persia's last pre-Islamic empire and seat it at al-Mada'in (Ctesiphon), near the Roman frontier and Mesopotamia's stored learning. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
