# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Muhammad, respected in Mecca before the call, receives revelation; Khadijah, Ali, Abu Bakr and Zayd believe first; Quraysh persecute and boycott Banu Hashim, some Muslims flee to Abyssinia, and the Hijra to Yathrib follows. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Society in Madinah al-Munawwarah” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: After the Hijra (622) the Prophet writes the Madinah Charter, the first constitutional document, binding Muhajirun, Ansar of Aws and Khazraj, and the Jews into one just community, and Madinah becomes a trading centre.
