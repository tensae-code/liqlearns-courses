# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Beginning of the Islamic call”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** muhammad, respected in Mecca before the call, receives revelation; Khadijah, Ali, Abu Bakr and Zayd believe first; Quraysh persecute and boycott Banu Hashim, some Muslims flee to Abyssinia, and the Hijra to Yathrib follows.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Society in Madinah al-Munawwarah”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** after the Hijra (622) the Prophet writes the Madinah Charter, the first constitutional document, binding Muhajirun, Ansar of Aws and Khazraj, and the Jews into one just community, and Madinah becomes a trading centre.
