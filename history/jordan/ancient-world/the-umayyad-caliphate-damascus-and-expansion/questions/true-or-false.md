# The Umayyad Caliphate Damascus and Expansion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Al-Hasan yields the caliphate to Mu'awiya in 41 AH and Damascus rules 661–750; the armies besiege Constantinople, cross Transoxiana under al-Walid, found Kairouan, take Tangier, and Tariq ibn Ziyad carries Islam into Iberia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Umayyad civilisational achievements” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A school visit to Qasr Amra's painted walls opens the age of the Umayyad Mosque of Damascus (built 705–715 in ten years), bimaristan hospitals, courts of grievances, and trade exporting grain, sugar and cotton to India and China.
