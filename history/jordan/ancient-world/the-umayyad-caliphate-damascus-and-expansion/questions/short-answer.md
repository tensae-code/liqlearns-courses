# The Umayyad Caliphate Damascus and Expansion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Umayyad state — rise and expansion”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** al-Hasan yields the caliphate to Mu'awiya in 41 AH and Damascus rules 661–750; the armies besiege Constantinople, cross Transoxiana under al-Walid, found Kairouan, take Tangier, and Tariq ibn Ziyad carries Islam into Iberia.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Umayyad civilisational achievements”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a school visit to Qasr Amra's painted walls opens the age of the Umayyad Mosque of Damascus (built 705–715 in ten years), bimaristan hospitals, courts of grievances, and trade exporting grain, sugar and cotton to India and China.
