# Hands-on — The Umayyad Caliphate Damascus and Expansion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: The Umayyad state — rise and expansion; Umayyad civilisational achievements; The end of the Umayyad state; The Umayyad state in al-Andalus; Jordan in the Umayyad age; Jordan in the Umayyad age. Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: Al-Hasan yields the caliphate to Mu'awiya in 41 AH and Damascus rules 661–750; the armies besiege Constantinople, cross Transoxiana under al-Walid, found Kairouan, take Tangier, and Tariq ibn Ziyad carries Islam into Iberia.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Al-Hasan yields the caliphate to Mu'awiya in 41 AH and Damascus rules 661–750; the armies besiege Constantinople, cross Transoxiana under al-Walid, found Kairouan, take Tangier, and Tariq ibn Ziyad carries Islam into Iberia. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
