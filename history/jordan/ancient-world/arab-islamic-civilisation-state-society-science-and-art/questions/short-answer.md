# Arab Islamic Civilisation State Society Science and Art — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Government and administration”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** abu Bakr's first sermon sets the contract — "I have been put over you though I am not the best of you; if I do well help me, if I do wrong set me straight" — then choosing the caliph slides from shura to hereditary succession under Umayyads, Abbasids and Ottomans; wazirs of delegation and of execution, governors and the diwans (army, land-tax, post) run the machine.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The judiciary”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Prophet judges disputes himself; Umar separates the judges from the governors; the qadi settles quarrels, watches the markets and guards the endowments; under the Abbasids a chief judge, qadi al-qudat, oversees them all, the schools of law multiply, and a court of grievances, the mazalim, hears complaints against the powerful.
