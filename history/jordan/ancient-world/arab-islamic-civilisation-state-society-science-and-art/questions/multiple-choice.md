**1.** What happens in the part called “Government and administration”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Abu Bakr's first sermon sets the contract — "I have been put over you though I am not the best of you; if I do…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
