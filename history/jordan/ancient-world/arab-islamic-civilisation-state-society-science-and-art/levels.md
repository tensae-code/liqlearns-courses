# Arab Islamic Civilisation State Society Science and Art — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Government and administration
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Abu Bakr's first sermon sets the contract — "I have been put over you though I am not the best of you; if I do well help me, if I do wrong set me straight" — then choosing the caliph slides from shura to hereditary succession under Umayyads, Abbasids and Ottomans; wazirs of delegation and of execution, governors and the diwans (army, land-tax, post) run the machine — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The judiciary
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Prophet judges disputes himself; Umar separates the judges from the governors; the qadi settles quarrels, watches the markets and guards the endowments; under the Abbasids a chief judge, qadi al-qudat, oversees them all, the schools of law multiply, and a court of grievances, the mazalim, hears complaints against the powerful — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — The army, the fleet and the police
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Volunteers with swords and spears win Badr in 624; Mu'awiya begs Umar for a fleet and is refused, yet within a generation Muslim ships fight Byzantium at the Battle of the Masts; Abbasid fleets reach the Indian Ocean, Andalusi fleets guard the Mediterranean, and in 1533 Khayr al-Din Barbarossa makes the Ottoman fleet master of the sea — while in every city the sahib al-shurta walks the night streets with his lantern-men — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Economic life
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Farmers share-crop by muzara'a and mugharasa, terrace the hillsides, drain the marshes and lift river water with the noria wheels of Hama and the shaduf; Damascus weaves and bladesmiths, Fez tans leather, guilds train boy to journeyman to master; caravans and ships stitch the Islamic world into one market, Abd al-Malik arabises the coinage, and the waqf endowment pays for schools, roads and hospitals — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Social life
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Arabs, mawali, dhimmis and freed slaves live in one society; Islam urges freeing slaves and lets a slave buy his own freedom by mukataba; houses cluster round mosque and market, wardrobes run from the jubba to the turban, a quoted etiquette text teaches the witty not to overfill their mouths, and the feasts — Fitr, Adha, the Mawlid, even Nayruz and Mahrajan — gather everyone — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Scientific and cultural life
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A boy finishes the kuttab and joins a circle in the mosque; Ibn Sina of Afshana writes the medicine of centuries, Abu Hanifa founds a school of law; Baghdad's Bayt al-Hikma and a thousand libraries store the world's books; Hunayn ibn Ishaq and the translators carry Plato, Hippocrates, Euclid and Archimedes into Arabic, and bimaristan hospitals heal the sick — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — Arab Islamic arts
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Abd al-Malik crowns Jerusalem with the Dome of the Rock; Saladin's commander Izz al-Din Usama builds Ajloun Castle to watch the Crusader roads; in Granada the Alhambra's Court of the Lions pours water from stone lions' mouths; the Mamluk Sultan Hassan raises his mosque-school in Cairo; and everywhere arabesque geometry, plant scrolls, mashrabiyya screens and Kufic letters turn craft into art — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
