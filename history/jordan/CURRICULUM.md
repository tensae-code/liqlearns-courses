# History — Jordan course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Jordan inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path follows Jordan's own books' progression as filed — the placements file's book order is the teaching order, earliest books first — grouped into levels by the turns of Jordan's own story as its books present them: first the historian's craft and heritage at home; then the ancient world with Jordan's own ground as its spine; then Islam's rise and the Islamic ages anchored to Jordanian places; then empires, revolutions, colonialism and liberation; then Jordan made — revolt, Emirate, kingdom; and last Jordan in the world today. A lesson sits in the title folder the placements file filed it under, and its parts run in file order inside the lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Jordan's own Social Studies and History books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know the Past and Heritage at Home** — Jordan's books open with the method itself and with heritage at home: what history is, how the past is dated on two calendars, how history is found in your own locality and in your grandparents' memories, what heritage is and why it matters, how a fact is told from an opinion, and how coins, documents and museum objects prove the past — with transport's then-to-now story as the child's first change-over-time story. 7 lessons, 16 placements.
2. **Level 2 — The Ancient World and Jordan's Own Ancient Ground** — The long ancient story, Jordan's own soil as its spine: stone tools to farming, the river civilisations (Mesopotamia, Egypt, China, India), Greece, Rome and Carthage, Arabia before Islam with Yemen's dam kingdoms and the Hijaz cities, Aksum and the Maya — and Jordan itself age by age: its Stone-Age villages and Ain Ghazal's statues, its Iron Age kingdoms Ammon, Moab and Edom, the Nabataeans and Petra, its rock inscriptions, its coins and mints, and the antiquities tour of Petra, Jerash and the castles, with Persia and the Sasanian empire at the level's edge. 27 lessons, 72 placements.
3. **Level 3 — Islam Rises: The Caliphates and Jordan in the Islamic Ages** — The Islamic ages whole, anchored to Jordanian ground: the Prophet's life and the Madinah Charter, the Rashidun era opening at Yarmouk, the Umayyads met at Qasr Amra in the Jordanian desert, the Abbasids and Baghdad's golden age kindled at Humayma in Jordan's south, Arab Islamic civilisation taught as a working system of state, society, science and art, Arab-Islamic philosophy, and Jordan under Crusaders, Ayyubids and Mamluks, with Byzantium beside it. 7 lessons, 37 placements.
4. **Level 4 — Empires, Revolutions, Colonialism and Liberation** — The wider modern world as Jordan's books chain it: the Ottoman state from Osman to Atatürk with Jordan under its rule, industry first and what it changed, America, France and Napoleon, Russia 1917, the two world wars and the Cold War, Muhammad Ali's Egypt and Gandhi's India — then Europe's colonialism named and judged, its motives and forms, its seizure of the Arab homeland before and after the First World War, the Arab Nahda awakening, and the liberation movements of the Levant and Iraq, the Maghreb, South Yemen and the Gulf. 20 lessons, 38 placements.
5. **Level 5 — Jordan Made: From the Great Arab Revolt to the Modern Kingdom** — Jordan's own modern story whole: the Hijaz Railway and the Great Arab Revolt that made the country, independence and the national days, the Arab Army at Latrun, Bab al-Wad and Karameh, the poet Arar, the founding of the Emirate and the road to independence, political development 1947–1999 and political life since 1999 under Abdullah II, and the kingdom's economic, social, educational and cultural life reign by reign — closing with Jordan and the Palestinian cause and the Hashemite custodianship of Jerusalem. 16 lessons, 33 placements.
6. **Level 6 — Jordan in the World Today: Organisations, Issues and Rising Countries** — The world Jordan acts in today: the Arab League, the United Nations with Jordan's three Security Council turns and its peacekeepers, the Non-Aligned Movement, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, the European Union, the world economic organisations, and the Red Cross and Red Crescent with humanitarian law; Arab unity projects from Greater Syria to the Gulf Council, the dialogue of civilisations, refuge and migration with Jordan as a great refugee host — and the rising countries Jordan learns from: Japan's two renaissances, South Korea's miracle on the Han River, and Malaysia's rise. 12 lessons, 13 placements.

**Totals: 89 lessons, 209 placements** (Level 1: 7 lessons; Level 2: 27 lessons; Level 3: 7 lessons; Level 4: 20 lessons; Level 5: 16 lessons; Level 6: 12 lessons).
