# History — Jordan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Jordan's history belongs to Jordan: this course is Jordan's own — built from Jordan's Social Studies and History student books (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the historian's craft and heritage at home first, then the ancient world walked on Jordan's own ground — Ain Ghazal, the Iron Age kingdoms, Petra — then Islam's rise landing at Yarmouk and Qasr Amra, the empires and revolutions, the Great Arab Revolt and the kingdom it made, and last Jordan acting in the world today. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
