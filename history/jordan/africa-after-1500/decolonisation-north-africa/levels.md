# Decolonisation North Africa — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Liberation in the Maghreb, the Libya part
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Italy holds Libya from 1912; Omar Mukhtar leads the popular resistance for nine years until, wounded and thrown from his horse, he is captured and hanged after a show trial in 1931, aged eighty — and the poet Ahmad Shawqi elegises him — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Tunisia and Morocco parts
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: France imposes her protectorate on the Bey of Tunis in 1881, fights Arabic and crushes every national movement, yet cannot stop the struggle even when Axis troops land in 1942; in Morocco the Treaty of Fes (1912) makes a protectorate, Spain takes the Rif, Tangier goes international — until France and Spain must combine against al-Khattabi's Rif revolution, the Berber Dahir backfires, and Muhammad V returns as king of a united Morocco — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Algeria part
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Messali Hadj's Algerian People's Party (1937) demands separation from France and Arabic as the official language, while Ferhat Abbas offers wartime loyalty for rights; the answer comes in an eight-year revolution (1954–1962) that unites every Algerian force, wins Africa and the UN to its side, and costs more than a million martyrs before France negotiates — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Liberation in the Nile Valley, the Egypt part
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Ahmad Urabi's revolution of 1881 rallies all Egypt against foreign interference and fails; two days after the 1918 armistice Saad Zaghlul asks to plead Egypt's case in Paris, is refused, arrested and exiled to Malta — and Egypt erupts in the revolution of 1919; King Farouk succeeds Fuad in 1936, Britain forces the Wafd's Nahhas on him in wartime, and the demand to cancel the 1936 treaty rolls toward the Free Officers — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — The Sudan part
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Muhammad Ali's Egypt rules Sudan until Britain takes Egypt in 1882 and the two impose the joint Condominium of 1899; the Mahdist revolution of 1880 had already risen against Egyptian rule; after the Second World War Ismail al-Azhari's National Unionist Party wins the elections, parliament votes self-determination, and the British and Egyptian armies withdraw — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
