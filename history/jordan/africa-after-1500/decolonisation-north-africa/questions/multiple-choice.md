# Decolonisation North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Liberation in the Maghreb, the Libya part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Italy holds Libya from 1912; Omar Mukhtar leads the popular resistance for nine years until, wounded and throw…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Italy holds Libya from 1912; Omar Mukhtar leads the popular resistance for nine years until, wounded and throw…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Tunisia and Morocco parts”? (Sub lesson 2)

**Answer: b) France imposes her protectorate on the Bey of Tunis in 1881, fights Arabic and crushes every national movement…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
