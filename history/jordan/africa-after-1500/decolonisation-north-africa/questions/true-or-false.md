# Decolonisation North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Italy holds Libya from 1912; Omar Mukhtar leads the popular resistance for nine years until, wounded and thrown from his horse, he is captured and hanged after a show trial in 1931, aged eighty — and the poet Ahmad Shawqi elegises him. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Tunisia and Morocco parts” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: France imposes her protectorate on the Bey of Tunis in 1881, fights Arabic and crushes every national movement, yet cannot stop the struggle even when Axis troops land in 1942; in Morocco the Treaty of Fes (1912) makes a protectorate, Spain takes the Rif, Tangier goes international — until France and Spain must combine against al-Khattabi's Rif revolution, the Berber Dahir backfires, and Muhammad V returns as king of a united Morocco.
