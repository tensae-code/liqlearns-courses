# Decolonisation North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Liberation in the Maghreb, the Libya part”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** italy holds Libya from 1912; Omar Mukhtar leads the popular resistance for nine years until, wounded and thrown from his horse, he is captured and hanged after a show trial in 1931, aged eighty — and the poet Ahmad Shawqi elegises him.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Tunisia and Morocco parts”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** france imposes her protectorate on the Bey of Tunis in 1881, fights Arabic and crushes every national movement, yet cannot stop the struggle even when Axis troops land in 1942; in Morocco the Treaty of Fes (1912) makes a protectorate, Spain takes the Rif, Tangier goes international — until France and Spain must combine against al-Khattabi's Rif revolution, the Berber Dahir backfires, and Muhammad V returns as king of a united Morocco.
