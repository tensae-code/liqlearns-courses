# Video script — Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Strong states reach for weaker ones out of political rivalry, hunger for markets and raw materials, and the "white man's mission"; the forms run from direct occupation (Algeria 1830, Aden 1839, Tunisia 1881, Egypt 1882, Libya 1911, Morocco 1912) through settler colonialism — the Americas emptied, Palestine seized by the Zionist movement — to protectorate, mandate, trusteeship and the quiet invasion of culture and language.
