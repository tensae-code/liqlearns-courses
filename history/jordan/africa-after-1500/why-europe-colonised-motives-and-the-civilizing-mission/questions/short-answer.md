# Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Colonialism — its concept, motives and forms”.
**Answer:** strong states reach for weaker ones out of political rivalry, hunger for markets and raw materials, and the "white man's mission"; the forms run from direct occupation (Algeria 1830, Aden 1839, Tunisia 1881, Egypt 1882, Libya 1911, Morocco 1912) through settler colonialism — the Americas emptied, Palestine seized by the Zionist movement — to protectorate, mandate, trusteeship and the quiet invasion of culture and language.
