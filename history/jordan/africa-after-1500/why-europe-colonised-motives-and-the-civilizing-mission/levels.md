# Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Strong states reach for weaker ones out of political rivalry, hunger for markets and raw materials, and the "white man's mission"; the forms run from direct occupation (Algeria 1830, Aden 1839, Tunisia 1881, Egypt 1882, Libya 1911, Morocco 1912) through settler colonialism — the Americas emptied, Palestine seized by the Zionist movement — to protectorate, mandate, trusteeship and the quiet invasion of culture and language — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
