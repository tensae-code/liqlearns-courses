# Fact Or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Fact and opinion”?
a) Classmates answer questions about a picture; where answers agree the matter is a proven fact no one disputes,…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Classmates answer questions about a picture; where answers agree the matter is a proven fact no one disputes,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Fact Or Opinion”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Classmates answer questions about a picture; where answers agree the matter is a proven fact no one disputes,…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Classmates answer questions about a picture; where answers agree the matter is a proven fact no one disputes,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
