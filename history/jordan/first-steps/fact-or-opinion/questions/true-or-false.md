# Fact Or Opinion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Classmates answer questions about a picture; where answers agree the matter is a proven fact no one disputes, where they differ it is an opinion shaped by feeling and experience.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Fact Or Opinion” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Classmates answer questions about a picture; where answers agree the matter is a proven fact no one disputes, where they differ it is an opinion shaped by feeling and experience.
