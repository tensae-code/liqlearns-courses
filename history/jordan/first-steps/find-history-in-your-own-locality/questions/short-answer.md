# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Interviewing grandparents”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** pupils interview a grandfather or grandmother about the nature of life long ago and send it to the teacher.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Home activity”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** pupils visit an archaeological site in their own governorate with their family and write three facts they learned.
