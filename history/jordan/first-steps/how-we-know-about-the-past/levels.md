# How We Know About the Past — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Old coins
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A Nabataean coin of King Obodas II, an Umayyad dinar minted in Amman in 79 AH / 699 CE, and the first Jordanian dinar of 1949 — coins are evidence that names kings, cities and dates — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Umariyya Covenant as a document
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The class reads Umar ibn al-Khattab's covenant to the people of Iliya (Jerusalem) word by word — safety for their lives, property, churches and crosses — a primary text analysed in class — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Primary and secondary historical sources
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In a museum the learner separates first-hand evidence — inscriptions, Islamic coins, an Umayyad papyrus dated 24 January 722, Qur'an manuscripts — from later accounts, and tests both against the Umm Qais Treaty document of 20 September 1920 — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
