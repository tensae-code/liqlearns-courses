# How We Know About the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A Nabataean coin of King Obodas II, an Umayyad dinar minted in Amman in 79 AH / 699 CE, and the first Jordanian dinar of 1949 — coins are evidence that names kings, cities and dates. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Umariyya Covenant as a document” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The class reads Umar ibn al-Khattab's covenant to the people of Iliya (Jerusalem) word by word — safety for their lives, property, churches and crosses — a primary text analysed in class.
