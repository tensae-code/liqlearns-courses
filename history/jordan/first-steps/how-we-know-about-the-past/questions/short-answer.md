# How We Know About the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Old coins”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a Nabataean coin of King Obodas II, an Umayyad dinar minted in Amman in 79 AH / 699 CE, and the first Jordanian dinar of 1949 — coins are evidence that names kings, cities and dates.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Umariyya Covenant as a document”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the class reads Umar ibn al-Khattab's covenant to the people of Iliya (Jerusalem) word by word — safety for their lives, property, churches and crosses — a primary text analysed in class.
