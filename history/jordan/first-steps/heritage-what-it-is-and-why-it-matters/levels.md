Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 3 — Culture
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Culture's elements include history itself — Jordan's identity is layered from the civilisations that lived on its land: Ammonite, Moabite, Nabataean and more — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Jordanian cultural heritage
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The dabka dance, inscribed by UNESCO, folk costumes that change from region to region, wedding songs and the manqala game carry Jordan's intangible heritage — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
