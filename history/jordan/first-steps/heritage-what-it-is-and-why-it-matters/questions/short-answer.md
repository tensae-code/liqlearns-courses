# Heritage What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Our customs and traditions”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** jordanian society keeps its authentic customs — welcoming guests, helping the needy, wearing traditional dress — and children visit a folk-life museum to see them.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Customs and traditions; folk costumes”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a girl finds a photo of her grandmother's old dress; Jordan's folk dress (thobe, shemagh and egal) differs between desert and countryside and is part of Jordanian identity kept across centuries.
