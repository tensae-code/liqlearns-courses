# Heritage What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jordanian society keeps its authentic customs — welcoming guests, helping the needy, wearing traditional dress — and children visit a folk-life museum to see them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Customs and traditions; folk costumes” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A girl finds a photo of her grandmother's old dress; Jordan's folk dress (thobe, shemagh and egal) differs between desert and countryside and is part of Jordanian identity kept across centuries.
