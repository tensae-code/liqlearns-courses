# Heritage What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Our customs and traditions”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Jordanian society keeps its authentic customs — welcoming guests, helping the needy, wearing traditional dress…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Jordanian society keeps its authentic customs — welcoming guests, helping the needy, wearing traditional dress…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Customs and traditions; folk costumes”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A girl finds a photo of her grandmother's old dress; Jordan's folk dress (thobe, shemagh and egal) differs bet…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A girl finds a photo of her grandmother's old dress; Jordan's folk dress (thobe, shemagh and egal) differs bet…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
