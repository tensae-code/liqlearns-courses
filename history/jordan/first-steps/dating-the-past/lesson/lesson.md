# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Unit 6 unit-end timeline exercise; Unit 2 closing skills lesson: the calendar. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 6 unit-end timeline exercise

Pupils order the Prophet's birth, the revelation, the two Hijras and a battle on a timeline.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2 closing skills lesson: the calendar

Pupils compare the Gregorian (solar, from Christ's birth) and Hijri calendars and place Jordan's events — 1921, 1956, Independence Day, Karameh 1968, 1999 — on a timeline.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Pupils order the Prophet's birth, the revelation, the two Hijras and a battle on a timeline. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Dating the Past. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
