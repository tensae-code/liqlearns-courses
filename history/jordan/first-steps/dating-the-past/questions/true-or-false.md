# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils order the Prophet's birth, the revelation, the two Hijras and a battle on a timeline. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 2 closing skills lesson: the calendar” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Pupils compare the Gregorian (solar, from Christ's birth) and Hijri calendars and place Jordan's events — 1921, 1956, Independence Day, Karameh 1968, 1999 — on a timeline.
