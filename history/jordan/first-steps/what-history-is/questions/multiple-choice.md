# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “What history and dating the past mean”?
a) A child at a lecture on why studying the past matters learns history explains the present and guards heritage.  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A child at a lecture on why studying the past matters learns history explains the present and guards heritage.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “What History Is”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A child at a lecture on why studying the past matters learns history explains the present and guards heritage.  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A child at a lecture on why studying the past matters learns history explains the present and guards heritage.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
