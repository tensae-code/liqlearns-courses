# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A child at a lecture on why studying the past matters learns history explains the present and guards heritage.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “What History Is” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A child at a lecture on why studying the past matters learns history explains the present and guards heritage.
