# The Industrial Revolution What Changed and Why England First

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Industrial Revolution. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Industrial Revolution

Feudal Europe of nobles, clergy and commons gives way to machines; the revolution starts in England first, fattened on capital gathered from colonies in Asia, Africa and the New World.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
