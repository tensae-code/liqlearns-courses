# The Industrial Revolution What Changed and Why England First — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Industrial Revolution”?
a) Feudal Europe of nobles, clergy and commons gives way to machines; the revolution starts in England first, fat…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Feudal Europe of nobles, clergy and commons gives way to machines; the revolution starts in England first, fat…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Industrial Revolution What Changed and Why England First”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Feudal Europe of nobles, clergy and commons gives way to machines; the revolution starts in England first, fat…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Feudal Europe of nobles, clergy and commons gives way to machines; the revolution starts in England first, fat…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
