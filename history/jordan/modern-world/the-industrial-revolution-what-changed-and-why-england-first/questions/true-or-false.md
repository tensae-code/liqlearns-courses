# The Industrial Revolution What Changed and Why England First — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Feudal Europe of nobles, clergy and commons gives way to machines; the revolution starts in England first, fattened on capital gathered from colonies in Asia, Africa and the New World.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Industrial Revolution What Changed and Why England First” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Feudal Europe of nobles, clergy and commons gives way to machines; the revolution starts in England first, fattened on capital gathered from colonies in Asia, Africa and the New World.
