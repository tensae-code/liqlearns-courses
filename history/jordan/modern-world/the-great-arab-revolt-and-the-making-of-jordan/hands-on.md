# Hands-on — The Great Arab Revolt and the Making of Jordan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Opening story; Journey in Ma'an Governorate; The Great Arab Revolt; The Great Arab Revolt (the Arab Nahda); Jordan under the Syrian Arab Kingdom and the local governments; The Great Arab Revolt (the Arab Nahda); Jordan under the Syrian Arab Kingdom and the local governments. Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: In the municipal library a child finds the Hashemite medal of the Great Battle of Ma'an, struck for the forces of the Great Arab Revolt that helped make modern Jordan.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the municipal library a child finds the Hashemite medal of the Great Battle of Ma'an, struck for the forces of the Great Arab Revolt that helped make modern Jordan. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
