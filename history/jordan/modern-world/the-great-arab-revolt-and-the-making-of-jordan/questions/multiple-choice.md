# The Great Arab Revolt and the Making of Jordan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Opening story”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) In the municipal library a child finds the Hashemite medal of the Great Battle of Ma'an, struck for the forces…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In the municipal library a child finds the Hashemite medal of the Great Battle of Ma'an, struck for the forces…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Journey in Ma'an Governorate”? (Sub lesson 2)
