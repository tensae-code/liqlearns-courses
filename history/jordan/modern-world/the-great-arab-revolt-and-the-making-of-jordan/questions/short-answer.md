# The Great Arab Revolt and the Making of Jordan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Opening story”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** in the municipal library a child finds the Hashemite medal of the Great Battle of Ma'an, struck for the forces of the Great Arab Revolt that helped make modern Jordan.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Journey in Ma'an Governorate”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a grandfather recalls Ma'an's part in the Great Arab Revolt and its great battle against the Turks; Emir Abdullah took the Ma'an palace as his residence in 1920, and it is now a national museum.
