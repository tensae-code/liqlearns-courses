# The Great Arab Revolt and the Making of Jordan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the municipal library a child finds the Hashemite medal of the Great Battle of Ma'an, struck for the forces of the Great Arab Revolt that helped make modern Jordan. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Journey in Ma'an Governorate” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A grandfather recalls Ma'an's part in the Great Arab Revolt and its great battle against the Turks; Emir Abdullah took the Ma'an palace as his residence in 1920, and it is now a national museum.
