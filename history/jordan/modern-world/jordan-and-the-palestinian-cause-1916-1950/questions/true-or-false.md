# Jordan and the Palestinian Cause 1916 1950 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zionism organises at Basel in 1897; Britain plants settlements and arms the newcomers, and Palestine answers with the revolts of 1920, al-Buraq 1929 and the Great Revolt of 1936–39; Sharif Hussein refuses to sign Palestine away ("I do not accept…"); Amir Abdullah protests Balfour to Churchill in Jerusalem in 1921 and demands Palestine's and Transjordan's unity and Britain's wartime promises kept; the Peel map of 1937 proposes partition; 1948 brings war, the armistices of 1949, the West Bank under Jordan and Gaza under Egypt.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Jordan and the Palestinian Cause 1916 1950” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Zionism organises at Basel in 1897; Britain plants settlements and arms the newcomers, and Palestine answers with the revolts of 1920, al-Buraq 1929 and the Great Revolt of 1936–39; Sharif Hussein refuses to sign Palestine away ("I do not accept…"); Amir Abdullah protests Balfour to Churchill in Jerusalem in 1921 and demands Palestine's and Transjordan's unity and Britain's wartime promises kept; the Peel map of 1937 proposes partition; 1948 brings war, the armistices of 1949, the West Bank under Jordan and Gaza under Egypt.
