# Lesson plan — Jordan and the Palestinian Cause 1916 1950

**Course:** History — Jordan · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Jordan and the Palestinian Cause 1916 1950
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Jordan Made: From the Great Arab Revolt to the Modern Kingdom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zionism organises at Basel in 1897; Britain plants settlements and arms the newcomers, and Palestine answers with the revolts of 1920, al-Buraq 1929 and the Great Revolt of 1936–39; Sharif Hussein refuses to sign Palestine away ("I do not accept…"); Amir Abdullah protests Balfour to Churchill in Jerusalem in 1921 and demands Palestine's and Transjordan's unity and Britain's wartime promises kept; the Peel map of 1937 proposes partition; 1948 brings war, the armistices of 1949, the West Bank under Jordan and Gaza under Egypt.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Jordan Made: From the Great Arab Revolt to the Modern Kingdom — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
