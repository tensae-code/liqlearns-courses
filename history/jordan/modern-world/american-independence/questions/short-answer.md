# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The American Revolution”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** europeans flood the New World and displace its native peoples; after the Seven Years' War Britain taxes the colonies, and Boston answers by dumping the tea ships' cargo in the harbour.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Stages of the American Revolution”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** bunker Hill, 17 June 1775; the Second Continental Congress at Philadelphia raises an army under Washington; Saratoga turns the war; the Peace of Paris in 1783 recognises the first state to throw off European colonialism.
