# American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europeans flood the New World and displace its native peoples; after the Seven Years' War Britain taxes the colonies, and Boston answers by dumping the tea ships' cargo in the harbour. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Stages of the American Revolution” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Bunker Hill, 17 June 1775; the Second Continental Congress at Philadelphia raises an army under Washington; Saratoga turns the war; the Peace of Paris in 1783 recognises the first state to throw off European colonialism.
