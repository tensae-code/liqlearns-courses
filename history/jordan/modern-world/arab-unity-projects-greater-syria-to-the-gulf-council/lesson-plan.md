# Lesson plan — Arab Unity Projects Greater Syria to the Gulf Council

**Course:** History — Jordan · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Arab Unity Projects Greater Syria to the Gulf Council
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Jordan Made: From the Great Arab Revolt to the Modern Kingdom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The dream is as old as the nation and was a goal of the Great Arab Revolt; in 1913 thirty-five Arab deputies of the Ottoman parliament authorise Sharif Hussein to speak for the Arabs; King Abdullah I proposes Greater Syria in 1943 as the revolt's continuation; Jordan and Iraq unite in the Arab Federation of 1958 under the two Hashemite cousins, Hussein and Faysal II; Egypt and Syria merge as the United Arab Republic (1958) until Syrian officers, chafing under nationalisation, secede in 1961; the Emirates unite on the Trucial Coast, six Gulf states found the GCC in 1981, and in 1989 the Arab Cooperation Council rises as Egypt returns from its Camp David isolation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Jordan Made: From the Great Arab Revolt to the Modern Kingdom — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
