# Arab Unity Projects Greater Syria to the Gulf Council — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Arab unity and its projects”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the dream is as old as the nation and was a goal of the Great Arab Revolt; in 1913 thirty-five Arab deputies of the Ottoman parliament authorise Sharif Hussein to speak for the Arabs; King Abdullah I proposes Greater Syria in 1943 as the revolt's continuation; Jordan and Iraq unite in the Arab Federation of 1958 under the two Hashemite cousins, Hussein and Faysal II; Egypt and Syria merge as the United Arab Republic (1958) until Syrian officers, chafing under nationalisation, secede in 1961; the Emirates unite on the Trucial Coast, six Gulf states found the GCC in 1981, and in 1989 the Arab Cooperation Council rises as Egypt returns from its Camp David isolation.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Jordan and Arab relations”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** eden's declarations (May 1941, February 1943) smile on Arab unity if the Arabs move first, and Abdullah drafts Greater Syria; the Arab League's charter is read in class (Article 1); the Arab Federation with Iraq dies in Qasim's coup of 14 July 1958, leaving Jordan besieged while Hussein holds the kingdom together; the Arab Cooperation Council of 1989 dies in the invasion of Kuwait — and under Abdullah II the Jordan–Egypt–Iraq cooperation of 2021 renews the pattern.
