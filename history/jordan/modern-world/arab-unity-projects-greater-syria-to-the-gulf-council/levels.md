## Sub lesson 1 — Arab unity and its projects
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The dream is as old as the nation and was a goal of the Great Arab Revolt; in 1913 thirty-five Arab deputies of the Ottoman parliament authorise Sharif Hussein to speak for the Arabs; King Abdullah I proposes Greater Syria in 1943 as the revolt's continuation; Jordan and Iraq unite in the Arab Federation of 1958 under the two Hashemite cousins, Hussein and Faysal II; Egypt and Syria merge as the United Arab Republic (1958) until Syrian officers, chafing under nationalisation, secede in 1961; the Emirates unite on the Trucial Coast, six Gulf states found the GCC in 1981, and in 1989 the Arab Cooperation Council rises as Egypt returns from its Camp David isolation — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
