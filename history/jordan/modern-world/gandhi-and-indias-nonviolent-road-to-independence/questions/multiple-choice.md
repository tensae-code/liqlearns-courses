# Gandhi and Indias Nonviolent Road to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Mahatma Gandhi (1869–1948)”?
a) "nonviolence is the weapon of the strong": twenty years fighting discrimination in South Africa, then home to…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) "nonviolence is the weapon of the strong": twenty years fighting discrimination in South Africa, then home to…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Gandhi and Indias Nonviolent Road to Independence”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) "nonviolence is the weapon of the strong": twenty years fighting discrimination in South Africa, then home to…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) "nonviolence is the weapon of the strong": twenty years fighting discrimination in South Africa, then home to…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
