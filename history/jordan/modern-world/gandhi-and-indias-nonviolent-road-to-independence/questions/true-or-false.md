# Gandhi and Indias Nonviolent Road to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** "nonviolence is the weapon of the strong": twenty years fighting discrimination in South Africa, then home to lead the Congress Party's marches and protests until Britain's rule is resisted into independence.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Gandhi and Indias Nonviolent Road to Independence” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: "nonviolence is the weapon of the strong": twenty years fighting discrimination in South Africa, then home to lead the Congress Party's marches and protests until Britain's rule is resisted into independence.
