# Arab Liberation Movements the Levant and Iraq — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Syria answers partition with revolts at Deir ez-Zor (1921), Jabal Druze (1922) and the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925; Jordanians reject the 1928 treaty with Britain as domination and hold five national congresses (1928–1933) under the Jordanian Arab Party of Muhammad Subhi Abu Ghanima; Iraq explodes in the 1920 Revolution against British exploitation, and King Ghazi (1933–1939) defies Britain with his own radio station defending Palestine before dying in a car crash; after 1941 Lebanon wins independence in 1943 and the last French soldier leaves Syria in 1946.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Arab Liberation Movements the Levant and Iraq” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Syria answers partition with revolts at Deir ez-Zor (1921), Jabal Druze (1922) and the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925; Jordanians reject the 1928 treaty with Britain as domination and hold five national congresses (1928–1933) under the Jordanian Arab Party of Muhammad Subhi Abu Ghanima; Iraq explodes in the 1920 Revolution against British exploitation, and King Ghazi (1933–1939) defies Britain with his own radio station defending Palestine before dying in a car crash; after 1941 Lebanon wins independence in 1943 and the last French soldier leaves Syria in 1946.
