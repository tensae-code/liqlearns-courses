# Video script — Arab Liberation Movements the Levant and Iraq

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Syria answers partition with revolts at Deir ez-Zor (1921), Jabal Druze (1922) and the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925; Jordanians reject the 1928 treaty with Britain as domination and hold five national congresses (1928–1933) under the Jordanian Arab Party of Muhammad Subhi Abu Ghanima; Iraq explodes in the 1920 Revolution against British exploitation, and King Ghazi (1933–1939) defies Britain with his own radio station defending Palestine before dying in a car crash; after 1941 Lebanon wins independence in 1943 and the last French soldier leaves Syria in 1946.
