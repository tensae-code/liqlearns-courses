# Hands-on — Arab Liberation Movements the Levant and Iraq

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Liberation movements in Bilad al-Sham and Iraq. Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: Syria answers partition with revolts at Deir ez-Zor (1921), Jabal Druze (1922) and the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925; Jordanians reject the 1928 treaty with Britain as domination and hold five national congresses (1928–1933) under the Jordanian Arab Party of Muhammad Subhi Abu Ghanima; Iraq explodes in the 1920 Revolution against British exploitation, and King Ghazi (1933–1939) defies Britain with his own radio station defending Palestine before dying in a car crash; after 1941 Lebanon wins independence in 1943 and the last French soldier leaves Syria in 1946.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Syria answers partition with revolts at Deir ez-Zor (1921), Jabal Druze (1922) and the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925; Jordanians reject the 1928 treaty with Britain as domination and hold five national congresses (1928–1933) under the Jordanian Arab Party of Muhammad Subhi Abu Ghanima; Iraq explodes in the 1920 Revolution against British exploitation, and King Ghazi (1933–1939) defies Britain with his own radio station defending Palestine before dying in a car crash; after 1941 Lebanon wins independence in 1943 and the last French soldier leaves Syria in 1946. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
