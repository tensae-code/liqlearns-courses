# Hands-on — Jordans Social Development Tribes Refugees and Cities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Social life 1921–1999; Social life since 1999. Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: The Emirate's society is tribal to its roots — landowners, merchants, officials, clergy and craftsmen in the qasabas, Circassians and Chechens among them since the 1800s; a mobile medical unit (one doctor, two nurses) tours the Badia from 1937 while mukhtars report malaria, typhoid, smallpox and measles, until the first Health Ministry in 1950; women's public life begins in charity — the first women's association, the Women's Social Solidarity Association, in 1926; after 1950 the society counts four peoples — city, countryside, badia and the Palestinian refugee camps — Jordan grants the refugees citizenship, UNRWA serves the camps, the nuclear family spreads with the move to the cities, and the Royal Medical Services crown the age with King Hussein Medical City (1973) and the Youth Welfare Organisation (1966).

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Emirate's society is tribal to its roots — landowners, merchants, officials, clergy and craftsmen in the qasabas, Circassians and Chechens among them since the 1800s; a mobile medical unit (one doctor, two nurses) tours the Badia from 1937 while mukhtars report malaria, typhoid, smallpox and measles, until the first Health Ministry in 1950; women's public life begins in charity — the first women's association, the Women's Social Solidarity Association, in 1926; after 1950 the society counts four peoples — city, countryside, badia and the Palestinian refugee camps — Jordan grants the refugees citizenship, UNRWA serves the camps, the nuclear family spreads with the move to the cities, and the Royal Medical Services crown the age with King Hussein Medical City (1973) and the Youth Welfare Organisation (1966). Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
