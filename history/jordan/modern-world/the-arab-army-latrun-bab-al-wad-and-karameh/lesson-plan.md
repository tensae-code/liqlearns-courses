## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Heroisms of the Arab Army** — On a school trip to the Ghor the class sees the Unknown Soldier memorial; Jordan's army fought at Latrun and Bab al-Wad for Palestine and won at Karameh, and 15 February honours the veterans
2. **The Jordanian Armed Forces — the Arab Army and the security services** — Abdullah builds the army from the revolt's nucleus; Britain's Transjordan Frontier Force (1926) fails, and the Desert (Badia) Force follows under the Arab Army in the late 1930s; the army's honour roll runs through 1948 to Karameh's 86 martyrs; the National Service Law of 1976 takes every eighteen-year-old for two years until its suspension in 1991 — and Crown Prince Hussein announces its return in 2025; Public Security is merged into the army in 1927 and separated again in 1956, the Civil Defence and Gendarmerie guard the home front, the General Intelligence Department dates to 1964, and under Abdullah II the army adds a special-operations training centre, a counter-terrorism centre, a cyber-security directorate and electronic border surveillance
