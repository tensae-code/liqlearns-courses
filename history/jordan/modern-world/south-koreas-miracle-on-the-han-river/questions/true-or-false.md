# South Koreas Miracle on the Han River — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Cold War splits Korea in 1945; the North invades in 1950; from the ashes — and from 80% illiteracy in 1945, after Japanese rule forbade Koreans their own Hangul letters — Seoul plans, borrows and builds for three decades into the "Miracle on the Han River"; Jordan's ties date to 1962, a Korean embassy opens in Amman in 1975, and King Abdullah II's 2015 visit seals more than twenty-four agreements.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “South Koreas Miracle on the Han River” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Cold War splits Korea in 1945; the North invades in 1950; from the ashes — and from 80% illiteracy in 1945, after Japanese rule forbade Koreans their own Hangul letters — Seoul plans, borrows and builds for three decades into the "Miracle on the Han River"; Jordan's ties date to 1962, a Korean embassy opens in Amman in 1975, and King Abdullah II's 2015 visit seals more than twenty-four agreements.
