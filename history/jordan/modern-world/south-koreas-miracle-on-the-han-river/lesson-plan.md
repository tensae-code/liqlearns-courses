# Lesson plan — South Koreas Miracle on the Han River

**Course:** History — Jordan · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** South Koreas Miracle on the Han River
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Jordan in the World Today: Organisations, Issues and Rising Countries (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Cold War splits Korea in 1945; the North invades in 1950; from the ashes — and from 80% illiteracy in 1945, after Japanese rule forbade Koreans their own Hangul letters — Seoul plans, borrows and builds for three decades into the "Miracle on the Han River"; Jordan's ties date to 1962, a Korean embassy opens in Amman in 1975, and King Abdullah II's 2015 visit seals more than twenty-four agreements.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Jordan in the World Today: Organisations, Issues and Rising Countries — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
