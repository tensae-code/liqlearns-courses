# South Koreas Miracle on the Han River

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: South Korea. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## South Korea

The Cold War splits Korea in 1945; the North invades in 1950; from the ashes — and from 80% illiteracy in 1945, after Japanese rule forbade Koreans their own Hangul letters — Seoul plans, borrows and builds for three decades into the "Miracle on the Han River"; Jordan's ties date to 1962, a Korean embassy opens in Amman in 1975, and King Abdullah II's 2015 visit seals more than twenty-four agreements.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
