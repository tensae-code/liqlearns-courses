# Jordan Independence and National Days — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Our national occasions”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan became independent on 25 May 1946, and pupils celebrate Independence Day, the…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
