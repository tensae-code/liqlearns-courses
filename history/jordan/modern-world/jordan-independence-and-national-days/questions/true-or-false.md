# Jordan Independence and National Days — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan became independent on 25 May 1946, and pupils celebrate Independence Day, the King's Accession Day (9 June) and Flag Day. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “King Abdullah II ibn al-Hussein” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Abdullah, eldest son of King Hussein, is born in Amman in 1962, marries Rania in 1993, and takes his constitutional powers as King on 7 February 1999.
