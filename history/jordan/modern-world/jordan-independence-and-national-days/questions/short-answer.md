# Jordan Independence and National Days — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Our national occasions”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan became independent on 25 May 1946, and pupils celebrate Independence Day, the King's Accession Day (9 June) and Flag Day.

**2.** Who is in the story of “King Abdullah II ibn al-Hussein”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** abdullah, eldest son of King Hussein, is born in Amman in 1962, marries Rania in 1993, and takes his constitutional powers as King on 7 February 1999.
