# The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity, defends the Palestinian cause and Jerusalem's holy places, and works through the Islamic Development Bank and ISESCO; Jordan is an active member from the start, its voice rooted in the Hashemite custodianship — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
