# Video script — The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity, defends the Palestinian cause and Jerusalem's holy places, and works through the Islamic Development Bank and ISESCO; Jordan is an active member from the start, its voice rooted in the Hashemite custodianship.

The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
