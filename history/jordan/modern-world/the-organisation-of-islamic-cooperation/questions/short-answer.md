# The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation”.
**Answer:** founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity, defends the Palestinian cause and Jerusalem's holy places, and works through the Islamic Development Bank and ISESCO; Jordan is an active member from the start, its voice rooted in the Hashemite custodianship.
