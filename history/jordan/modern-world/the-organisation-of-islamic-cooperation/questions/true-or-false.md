# The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity, defends the Palestinian cause and Jerusalem's holy places, and works through the Islamic Development Bank and ISESCO; Jordan is an active member from the start, its voice rooted in the Hashemite custodianship.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity, defends the Palestinian cause and Jerusalem's holy places, and works through the Islamic Development Bank and ISESCO; Jordan is an active member from the start, its voice rooted in the Hashemite custodianship.
