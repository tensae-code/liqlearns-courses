# The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation”?
a) Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
