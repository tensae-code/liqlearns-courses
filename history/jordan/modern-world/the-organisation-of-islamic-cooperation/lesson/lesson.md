## The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation

Founded in 1969, seated in Jeddah after the first Rabat summit, the OIC binds the Islamic states in solidarity, defends the Palestinian cause and Jerusalem's holy places, and works through the Islamic Development Bank and ISESCO; Jordan is an active member from the start, its voice rooted in the Hashemite custodianship.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
