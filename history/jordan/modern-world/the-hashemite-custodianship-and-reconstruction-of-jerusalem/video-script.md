# Video script — The Hashemite Custodianship and Reconstruction of Jerusalem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Palestine's muftis, judges and notables pledge allegiance to Sharif Hussein in 1924, and he opens the first reconstruction with 25,000 gold pounds of his own; five Hashemite reconstructions follow — Abdullah I tends al-Aqsa and the Dome; the third (1992–94) re-clads al-Aqsa in marble and re-gilds the Dome of the Rock at King Hussein's personal expense; the fifth, under Abdullah II, wins the Security Council's use of al-Aqsa's name, launches the Holy Sepulchre's restoration in 2016 and gathers Jerusalem's churches in a Jordanian council — while the Jerusalem Awqaf Department, under Jordan's Awqaf Ministry, administers the sanctuaries day by day.
