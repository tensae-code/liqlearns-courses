# The Hashemite Custodianship and Reconstruction of Jerusalem — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Palestine's muftis, judges and notables pledge allegiance to Sharif Hussein in 1924, and he opens the first reconstruction with 25,000 gold pounds of his own; five Hashemite reconstructions follow — Abdullah I tends al-Aqsa and the Dome; the third (1992–94) re-clads al-Aqsa in marble and re-gilds the Dome of the Rock at King Hussein's personal expense; the fifth, under Abdullah II, wins the Security Council's use of al-Aqsa's name, launches the Holy Sepulchre's restoration in 2016 and gathers Jerusalem's churches in a Jordanian council — while the Jerusalem Awqaf Department, under Jordan's Awqaf Ministry, administers the sanctuaries day by day — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
