# The Hashemite Custodianship and Reconstruction of Jerusalem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Hashemite custodianship and reconstruction of Jerusalem's holy sites”.
**Answer:** palestine's muftis, judges and notables pledge allegiance to Sharif Hussein in 1924, and he opens the first reconstruction with 25,000 gold pounds of his own; five Hashemite reconstructions follow — Abdullah I tends al-Aqsa and the Dome; the third (1992–94) re-clads al-Aqsa in marble and re-gilds the Dome of the Rock at King Hussein's personal expense; the fifth, under Abdullah II, wins the Security Council's use of al-Aqsa's name, launches the Holy Sepulchre's restoration in 2016 and gathers Jerusalem's churches in a Jordanian council — while the Jerusalem Awqaf Department, under Jordan's Awqaf Ministry, administers the sanctuaries day by day.
