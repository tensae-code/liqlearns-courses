# The Hashemite Custodianship and Reconstruction of Jerusalem — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Palestine's muftis, judges and notables pledge allegiance to Sharif Hussein in 1924, and he opens the first reconstruction with 25,000 gold pounds of his own; five Hashemite reconstructions follow — Abdullah I tends al-Aqsa and the Dome; the third (1992–94) re-clads al-Aqsa in marble and re-gilds the Dome of the Rock at King Hussein's personal expense; the fifth, under Abdullah II, wins the Security Council's use of al-Aqsa's name, launches the Holy Sepulchre's restoration in 2016 and gathers Jerusalem's churches in a Jordanian council — while the Jerusalem Awqaf Department, under Jordan's Awqaf Ministry, administers the sanctuaries day by day.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Hashemite Custodianship and Reconstruction of Jerusalem” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Palestine's muftis, judges and notables pledge allegiance to Sharif Hussein in 1924, and he opens the first reconstruction with 25,000 gold pounds of his own; five Hashemite reconstructions follow — Abdullah I tends al-Aqsa and the Dome; the third (1992–94) re-clads al-Aqsa in marble and re-gilds the Dome of the Rock at King Hussein's personal expense; the fifth, under Abdullah II, wins the Security Council's use of al-Aqsa's name, launches the Holy Sepulchre's restoration in 2016 and gathers Jerusalem's churches in a Jordanian council — while the Jerusalem Awqaf Department, under Jordan's Awqaf Ministry, administers the sanctuaries day by day.
