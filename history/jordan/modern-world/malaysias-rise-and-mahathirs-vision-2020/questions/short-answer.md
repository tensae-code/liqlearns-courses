# Malaysias Rise and Mahathirs Vision 2020 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Malaysia”.
**Answer:** a trading crossroads that welcomed Arab and Indian merchants for centuries outgrows colonial rule, Singapore's separation and the 1997 Asian crisis by diversifying — cars, electronics, steel, textiles; Mahathir Mohamad, doctor and prime minister, sets a thirty-year plan (1990–2020) to stand level with the West on world-class education and social justice, planned by a committee of national and foreign experts; Jordan and Malaysia exchange embassies from 1965, Malaysia's first in Amman in 1994.
