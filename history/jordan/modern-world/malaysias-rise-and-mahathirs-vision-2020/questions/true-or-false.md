# Malaysias Rise and Mahathirs Vision 2020 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A trading crossroads that welcomed Arab and Indian merchants for centuries outgrows colonial rule, Singapore's separation and the 1997 Asian crisis by diversifying — cars, electronics, steel, textiles; Mahathir Mohamad, doctor and prime minister, sets a thirty-year plan (1990–2020) to stand level with the West on world-class education and social justice, planned by a committee of national and foreign experts; Jordan and Malaysia exchange embassies from 1965, Malaysia's first in Amman in 1994.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Malaysias Rise and Mahathirs Vision 2020” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A trading crossroads that welcomed Arab and Indian merchants for centuries outgrows colonial rule, Singapore's separation and the 1997 Asian crisis by diversifying — cars, electronics, steel, textiles; Mahathir Mohamad, doctor and prime minister, sets a thirty-year plan (1990–2020) to stand level with the West on world-class education and social justice, planned by a committee of national and foreign experts; Jordan and Malaysia exchange embassies from 1965, Malaysia's first in Amman in 1994.
