# Malaysias Rise and Mahathirs Vision 2020 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A trading crossroads that welcomed Arab and Indian merchants for centuries outgrows colonial rule, Singapore's separation and the 1997 Asian crisis by diversifying — cars, electronics, steel, textiles; Mahathir Mohamad, doctor and prime minister, sets a thirty-year plan (1990–2020) to stand level with the West on world-class education and social justice, planned by a committee of national and foreign experts; Jordan and Malaysia exchange embassies from 1965, Malaysia's first in Amman in 1994 — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
