## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Cold War** — NATO (1949) faces the Warsaw Pact; the Bandung Conference of 1955 answers with non-alignment — Nasser, Tito and Nehru refuse both camps
2. **The Cold War (1945–1991)** — Two victors glare across a divided world — capitalist democratic America and her NATO allies against communist Soviet Russia and the Warsaw Pact — fighting by proxy: Korea (1950–1953), Vietnam (1955–1975), the Cuban missile crisis, and the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan (1979–1989); Gorbachev answers with glasnost and perestroika, the Berlin Wall falls in November 1989, and the cold war ends without the hot one
