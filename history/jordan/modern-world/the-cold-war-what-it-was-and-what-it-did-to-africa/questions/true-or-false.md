# The Cold War What It Was and What It Did to Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** NATO (1949) faces the Warsaw Pact; the Bandung Conference of 1955 answers with non-alignment — Nasser, Tito and Nehru refuse both camps. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Cold War (1945–1991)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Two victors glare across a divided world — capitalist democratic America and her NATO allies against communist Soviet Russia and the Warsaw Pact — fighting by proxy: Korea (1950–1953), Vietnam (1955–1975), the Cuban missile crisis, and the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan (1979–1989); Gorbachev answers with glasnost and perestroika, the Berlin Wall falls in November 1989, and the cold war ends without the hot one.
