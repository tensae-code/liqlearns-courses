# The Cold War What It Was and What It Did to Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Cold War”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** nATO (1949) faces the Warsaw Pact; the Bandung Conference of 1955 answers with non-alignment — Nasser, Tito and Nehru refuse both camps.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Cold War (1945–1991)”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** two victors glare across a divided world — capitalist democratic America and her NATO allies against communist Soviet Russia and the Warsaw Pact — fighting by proxy: Korea (1950–1953), Vietnam (1955–1975), the Cuban missile crisis, and the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan (1979–1989); Gorbachev answers with glasnost and perestroika, the Berlin Wall falls in November 1989, and the cold war ends without the hot one.
