# Muhammad Ali Pasha and the Building of Modern Egypt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Muhammad Ali Pasha (1769–1849)”.
**Answer:** an Albanian soldier from Macedonia rises in the Ottoman army, ends the Mamluks at the Cairo Citadel feast of 1811, and builds modern Egypt — self-sufficient economy, factories, schools, a European-style army and fleet, campaigns in Arabia, Greece and the Levant — until the London Treaty of 1840 and the firman of 1841 fix his house in Egypt and Sudan.
