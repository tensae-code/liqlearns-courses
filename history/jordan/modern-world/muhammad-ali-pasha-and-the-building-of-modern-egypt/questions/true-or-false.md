# Muhammad Ali Pasha and the Building of Modern Egypt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An Albanian soldier from Macedonia rises in the Ottoman army, ends the Mamluks at the Cairo Citadel feast of 1811, and builds modern Egypt — self-sufficient economy, factories, schools, a European-style army and fleet, campaigns in Arabia, Greece and the Levant — until the London Treaty of 1840 and the firman of 1841 fix his house in Egypt and Sudan.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Muhammad Ali Pasha and the Building of Modern Egypt” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: An Albanian soldier from Macedonia rises in the Ottoman army, ends the Mamluks at the Cairo Citadel feast of 1811, and builds modern Egypt — self-sufficient economy, factories, schools, a European-style army and fleet, campaigns in Arabia, Greece and the Levant — until the London Treaty of 1840 and the firman of 1841 fix his house in Egypt and Sudan.
