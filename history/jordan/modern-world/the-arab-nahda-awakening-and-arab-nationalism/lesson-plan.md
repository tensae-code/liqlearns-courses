# Lesson plan — The Arab Nahda Awakening and Arab Nationalism

**Course:** History — Jordan · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Arab Nahda Awakening and Arab Nationalism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Empires, Revolutions, Colonialism and Liberation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After centuries of Ottoman isolation, the French campaign of 1798–1801, Muhammad Ali's reforms, student missions and missionary schools carry European ideas of freedom, constitution and nation into Arabic; Tahtawi preaches liberty and equality, al-Shidyaq social justice; the Sanusiyya in Libya and the Mahdiyya in Sudan renew Islam as resistance; and Sharif Hussein of Mecca, Amir from 1908, becomes the pioneer of Arab nationalism — "the Arabs are Arabs before they are Muslims or Christians or Jews" — whose Great Arab Revolt founds the kingdoms of the Hijaz, Syria and Iraq and the Emirate of Transjordan.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Empires, Revolutions, Colonialism and Liberation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
