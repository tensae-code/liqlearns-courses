# The Arab Nahda Awakening and Arab Nationalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After centuries of Ottoman isolation, the French campaign of 1798–1801, Muhammad Ali's reforms, student missions and missionary schools carry European ideas of freedom, constitution and nation into Arabic; Tahtawi preaches liberty and equality, al-Shidyaq social justice; the Sanusiyya in Libya and the Mahdiyya in Sudan renew Islam as resistance; and Sharif Hussein of Mecca, Amir from 1908, becomes the pioneer of Arab nationalism — "the Arabs are Arabs before they are Muslims or Christians or Jews" — whose Great Arab Revolt founds the kingdoms of the Hijaz, Syria and Iraq and the Emirate of Transjordan.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Arab Nahda Awakening and Arab Nationalism” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: After centuries of Ottoman isolation, the French campaign of 1798–1801, Muhammad Ali's reforms, student missions and missionary schools carry European ideas of freedom, constitution and nation into Arabic; Tahtawi preaches liberty and equality, al-Shidyaq social justice; the Sanusiyya in Libya and the Mahdiyya in Sudan renew Islam as resistance; and Sharif Hussein of Mecca, Amir from 1908, becomes the pioneer of Arab nationalism — "the Arabs are Arabs before they are Muslims or Christians or Jews" — whose Great Arab Revolt founds the kingdoms of the Hijaz, Syria and Iraq and the Emirate of Transjordan.
