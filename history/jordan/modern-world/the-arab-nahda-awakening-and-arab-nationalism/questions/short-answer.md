# The Arab Nahda Awakening and Arab Nationalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The modern Arab renaissance (Nahda)”.
**Answer:** after centuries of Ottoman isolation, the French campaign of 1798–1801, Muhammad Ali's reforms, student missions and missionary schools carry European ideas of freedom, constitution and nation into Arabic; Tahtawi preaches liberty and equality, al-Shidyaq social justice; the Sanusiyya in Libya and the Mahdiyya in Sudan renew Islam as resistance; and Sharif Hussein of Mecca, Amir from 1908, becomes the pioneer of Arab nationalism — "the Arabs are Arabs before they are Muslims or Christians or Jews" — whose Great Arab Revolt founds the kingdoms of the Hijaz, Syria and Iraq and the Emirate of Transjordan.
