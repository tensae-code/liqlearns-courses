# Video script — The Arab Nahda Awakening and Arab Nationalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After centuries of Ottoman isolation, the French campaign of 1798–1801, Muhammad Ali's reforms, student missions and missionary schools carry European ideas of freedom, constitution and nation into Arabic; Tahtawi preaches liberty and equality, al-Shidyaq social justice; the Sanusiyya in Libya and the Mahdiyya in Sudan renew Islam as resistance; and Sharif Hussein of Mecca, Amir from 1908, becomes the pioneer of Arab nationalism — "the Arabs are Arabs before they are Muslims or Christians or Jews" — whose Great Arab Revolt founds the kingdoms of the Hijaz, Syria and Iraq and the Emirate of Transjordan.

The modern Arab renaissance (Nahda).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
