# The European Union from Coal and Steel to the Euro

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The European Union. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The European Union

A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union of — after Britain's exit in 2020 — 27 states, with a parliament, a commission, a development fund and a common market, and one money: the euro, launched in bank dealings in 1999 and in pockets in 2002; Jordan's ties run from diplomatic relations in 1977 to the 2002 Association Agreement aiming at a free-trade area.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
