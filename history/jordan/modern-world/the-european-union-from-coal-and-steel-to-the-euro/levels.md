# The European Union from Coal and Steel to the Euro — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union of — after Britain's exit in 2020 — 27 states, with a parliament, a commission, a development fund and a common market, and one money: the euro, launched in bank dealings in 1999 and in pockets in 2002; Jordan's ties run from diplomatic relations in 1977 to the 2002 Association Agreement aiming at a free-trade area — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
