# The European Union from Coal and Steel to the Euro — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The European Union”.
**Answer:** a ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union of — after Britain's exit in 2020 — 27 states, with a parliament, a commission, a development fund and a common market, and one money: the euro, launched in bank dealings in 1999 and in pockets in 2002; Jordan's ties run from diplomatic relations in 1977 to the 2002 Association Agreement aiming at a free-trade area.
