# The European Union from Coal and Steel to the Euro — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The European Union”?
a) A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The European Union from Coal and Steel to the Euro”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
