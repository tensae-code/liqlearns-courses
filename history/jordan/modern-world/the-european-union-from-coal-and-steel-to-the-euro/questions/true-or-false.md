# The European Union from Coal and Steel to the Euro — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union of — after Britain's exit in 2020 — 27 states, with a parliament, a commission, a development fund and a common market, and one money: the euro, launched in bank dealings in 1999 and in pockets in 2002; Jordan's ties run from diplomatic relations in 1977 to the 2002 Association Agreement aiming at a free-trade area.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The European Union from Coal and Steel to the Euro” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A ruined Europe is rescued by America's Marshall Plan (1947); the Coal and Steel Community grows into a union of — after Britain's exit in 2020 — 27 states, with a parliament, a commission, a development fund and a common market, and one money: the euro, launched in bank dealings in 1999 and in pockets in 2002; Jordan's ties run from diplomatic relations in 1977 to the 2002 Association Agreement aiming at a free-trade area.
