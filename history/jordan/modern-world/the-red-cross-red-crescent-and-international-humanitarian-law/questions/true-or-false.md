# The Red Cross Red Crescent and International Humanitarian Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The International Committee founded in 1863 protects war's victims and visits prisoners; after the First World War the federation of societies follows, the Arab Red Crescent organisation settles in Riyadh in 1975, and Jordan's own Red Crescent volunteers serve their country; behind them stands humanitarian law — the rules that shield everyone not fighting, or no longer fighting, in a war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Red Cross Red Crescent and International Humanitarian Law” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The International Committee founded in 1863 protects war's victims and visits prisoners; after the First World War the federation of societies follows, the Arab Red Crescent organisation settles in Riyadh in 1975, and Jordan's own Red Crescent volunteers serve their country; behind them stands humanitarian law — the rules that shield everyone not fighting, or no longer fighting, in a war.
