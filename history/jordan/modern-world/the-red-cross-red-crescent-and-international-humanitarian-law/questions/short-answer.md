# The Red Cross Red Crescent and International Humanitarian Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Humanitarian organisations and international humanitarian law”.
**Answer:** the International Committee founded in 1863 protects war's victims and visits prisoners; after the First World War the federation of societies follows, the Arab Red Crescent organisation settles in Riyadh in 1975, and Jordan's own Red Crescent volunteers serve their country; behind them stands humanitarian law — the rules that shield everyone not fighting, or no longer fighting, in a war.
