# The Red Cross Red Crescent and International Humanitarian Law — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The International Committee founded in 1863 protects war's victims and visits prisoners; after the First World War the federation of societies follows, the Arab Red Crescent organisation settles in Riyadh in 1975, and Jordan's own Red Crescent volunteers serve their country; behind them stands humanitarian law — the rules that shield everyone not fighting, or no longer fighting, in a war — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
