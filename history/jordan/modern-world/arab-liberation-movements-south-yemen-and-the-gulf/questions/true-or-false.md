# Arab Liberation Movements South Yemen and the Gulf — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sinan Pasha takes Aden for the Ottomans in 1538 but the interior stays its own; the National Front forms in 1963, a bomb at Aden airport wounds the British High Commissioner, and Britain cannot hold the South; in the Gulf, Britain brands the Trucial Coast the "Pirate Coast", binds the sheikhdoms with perpetual protection treaties and forbids oil concessions without her consent — then, days before the Emirates unite in 1971, Iran seizes the Greater and Lesser Tunbs and lands on Abu Musa, as Prime Minister Hoveyda announces it to his parliament; Qatar and Bahrain nurse their own national movements around the British Political Resident's base, and Oman — Ya'ariba imams, Sultan bin Ahmad's treaty of 1798, the Dhofar revolution — fights its long road out of the Portuguese and British shadow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Arab Liberation Movements South Yemen and the Gulf” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
