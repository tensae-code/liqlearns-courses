# The Arab League Founding Aims and Challenges — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Founded in 1945 with its seat in Cairo under the Alexandria Protocol, first secretary-general the Egyptian Abdul Rahman Azzam; its council, secretariat and technical committees guard members' sovereignty, settle Arab disputes peacefully and help Arab states to independence — yet, the book admits, no binding consensus, rival national interests and unhealed crises limit it; Jordan hosts its summits again and again.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Arab League Founding Aims and Challenges” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Founded in 1945 with its seat in Cairo under the Alexandria Protocol, first secretary-general the Egyptian Abdul Rahman Azzam; its council, secretariat and technical committees guard members' sovereignty, settle Arab disputes peacefully and help Arab states to independence — yet, the book admits, no binding consensus, rival national interests and unhealed crises limit it; Jordan hosts its summits again and again.
