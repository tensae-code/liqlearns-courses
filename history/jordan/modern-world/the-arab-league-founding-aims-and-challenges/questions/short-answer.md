# The Arab League Founding Aims and Challenges — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The League of Arab States”.
**Answer:** founded in 1945 with its seat in Cairo under the Alexandria Protocol, first secretary-general the Egyptian Abdul Rahman Azzam; its council, secretariat and technical committees guard members' sovereignty, settle Arab disputes peacefully and help Arab states to independence — yet, the book admits, no binding consensus, rival national interests and unhealed crises limit it; Jordan hosts its summits again and again.
