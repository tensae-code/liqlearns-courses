## The League of Arab States

Founded in 1945 with its seat in Cairo under the Alexandria Protocol, first secretary-general the Egyptian Abdul Rahman Azzam; its council, secretariat and technical committees guard members' sovereignty, settle Arab disputes peacefully and help Arab states to independence — yet, the book admits, no binding consensus, rival national interests and unhealed crises limit it; Jordan hosts its summits again and again.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
