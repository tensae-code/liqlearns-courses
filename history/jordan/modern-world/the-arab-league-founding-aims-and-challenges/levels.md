# The Arab League Founding Aims and Challenges — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Founded in 1945 with its seat in Cairo under the Alexandria Protocol, first secretary-general the Egyptian Abdul Rahman Azzam; its council, secretariat and technical committees guard members' sovereignty, settle Arab disputes peacefully and help Arab states to independence — yet, the book admits, no binding consensus, rival national interests and unhealed crises limit it; Jordan hosts its summits again and again — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
