# European Colonialism in the Arab World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pope Alexander VI splits the world between Spain and Portugal in 1493; the Arab navigator Ibn Majid, born at Ras al-Khaimah in 1432, pilots Vasco da Gama to India in 1498 and the Portuguese seize the Arabian coasts; Napoleon storms Egypt and exposes Ottoman weakness; foreign capitulations and Orientalist scholarship pry the empire open; Britain garrisons Kuwait in 1793 while the Qawasim fight her in the Gulf; Dey Hussein's fly-whisk insult hands France her excuse, and Algiers falls in 1830. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Colonialism in the Arab homeland after the First World War” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: France works to erase Arabic in the Maghreb, seizes the awqaf and fans Arab against Berber; Italy whispers "peaceful penetration" over Libya; in the East, Britain issues the Balfour Declaration on 2 November 1917 promising a Jewish national home in a Palestine it does not own; Damascus answers with the Syrian General Congress of March 1920 crowning Faysal king of a united Syria — and San Remo tears the country into mandates, France splitting Syria into statelets and handing Alexandretta to Turkey in 1939.
