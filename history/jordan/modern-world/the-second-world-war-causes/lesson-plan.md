## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Second World War** — The harsh Paris peace of 1919–1920 breeds grievance in Germany and Austria; Axis and Allies form, and the war ends with atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in 1945
2. **The Second World War (1939–1945), the causes** — Versailles humiliates Germany and Hitler's Nazi party rides the grievance to power by election; the Great Depression of 1929 throws millions out of work and lifts the extremists; Spain's civil war (1936–1939) rehearses the clash, Franco's right against the left; then Germany demands Danzig and a corridor through Poland, Poland refuses under British and French promises, and in September 1939 Germany invades
