# The Second World War Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Second World War”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The harsh Paris peace of 1919–1920 breeds grievance in Germany and Austria; Axis and Allies form, and the war…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The harsh Paris peace of 1919–1920 breeds grievance in Germany and Austria; Axis and Allies form, and the war…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Second World War (1939–1945), the causes”? (Sub lesson 2)
