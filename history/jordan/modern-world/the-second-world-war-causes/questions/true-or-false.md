# The Second World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The harsh Paris peace of 1919–1920 breeds grievance in Germany and Austria; Axis and Allies form, and the war ends with atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in 1945. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Second World War (1939–1945), the causes” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Versailles humiliates Germany and Hitler's Nazi party rides the grievance to power by election; the Great Depression of 1929 throws millions out of work and lifts the extremists; Spain's civil war (1936–1939) rehearses the clash, Franco's right against the left; then Germany demands Danzig and a corridor through Poland, Poland refuses under British and French promises, and in September 1939 Germany invades.
