# The Second World War Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Second World War”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the harsh Paris peace of 1919–1920 breeds grievance in Germany and Austria; Axis and Allies form, and the war ends with atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in 1945.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Second World War (1939–1945), the causes”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** versailles humiliates Germany and Hitler's Nazi party rides the grievance to power by election; the Great Depression of 1929 throws millions out of work and lifts the extremists; Spain's civil war (1936–1939) rehearses the clash, Franco's right against the left; then Germany demands Danzig and a corridor through Poland, Poland refuses under British and French promises, and in September 1939 Germany invades.
