# The Second World War Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Second World War; The Second World War (1939–1945), the causes. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Second World War

The harsh Paris peace of 1919–1920 breeds grievance in Germany and Austria; Axis and Allies form, and the war ends with atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in 1945.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Second World War (1939–1945), the causes

Versailles humiliates Germany and Hitler's Nazi party rides the grievance to power by election; the Great Depression of 1929 throws millions out of work and lifts the extremists; Spain's civil war (1936–1939) rehearses the clash, Franco's right against the left; then Germany demands Danzig and a corridor through Poland, Poland refuses under British and French promises, and in September 1939 Germany invades.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
