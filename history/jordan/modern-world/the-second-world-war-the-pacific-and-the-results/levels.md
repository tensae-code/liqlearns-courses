# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In 1941 Germany invades the Soviet Union, Japan seizes Hong Kong, the Philippines and Singapore and bombs Pearl Harbor, dragging America in; El Alamein turns the desert war in 1942–43; the Arab lands fight under mandates — Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's Iraq revolts against Britain in May 1941 with German support, Allied forces take Syria and Lebanon from Vichy France, and Jordanian soldiers march in London's Victory Parade on 8 June 1946 on the road to Jordan's independence the same year; the toll: the totalitarian regimes fall, some 60 million die, crimes are committed against millions of Jews, Roma, Russians and Japanese, and women's public role is transformed — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
