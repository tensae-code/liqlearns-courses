# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The course, the Arabs' war, and the results”.
**Answer:** in 1941 Germany invades the Soviet Union, Japan seizes Hong Kong, the Philippines and Singapore and bombs Pearl Harbor, dragging America in; El Alamein turns the desert war in 1942–43; the Arab lands fight under mandates — Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's Iraq revolts against Britain in May 1941 with German support, Allied forces take Syria and Lebanon from Vichy France, and Jordanian soldiers march in London's Victory Parade on 8 June 1946 on the road to Jordan's independence the same year; the toll: the totalitarian regimes fall, some 60 million die, crimes are committed against millions of Jews, Roma, Russians and Japanese, and women's public role is transformed.
