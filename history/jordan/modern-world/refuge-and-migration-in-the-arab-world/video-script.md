# Video script — Refuge and Migration in the Arab World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A refugee flees a well-founded fear of persecution and cannot go home; the UNHCR guards the right of asylum; the Arab world's waves are traced to colonial legacies and wars — Israel keeps displacing Palestinians after 1967, barring return and building on the West Bank; Iraqis flee their decades of crises; and Jordan and Turkey become the states most affected by Syria's war, hosting millions of registered Syrian refugees.
