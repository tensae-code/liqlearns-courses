# Refuge and Migration in the Arab World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A refugee flees a well-founded fear of persecution and cannot go home; the UNHCR guards the right of asylum; the Arab world's waves are traced to colonial legacies and wars — Israel keeps displacing Palestinians after 1967, barring return and building on the West Bank; Iraqis flee their decades of crises; and Jordan and Turkey become the states most affected by Syria's war, hosting millions of registered Syrian refugees.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Refuge and Migration in the Arab World” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A refugee flees a well-founded fear of persecution and cannot go home; the UNHCR guards the right of asylum; the Arab world's waves are traced to colonial legacies and wars — Israel keeps displacing Palestinians after 1967, barring return and building on the West Bank; Iraqis flee their decades of crises; and Jordan and Turkey become the states most affected by Syria's war, hosting millions of registered Syrian refugees.
