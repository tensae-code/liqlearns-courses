# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The French Revolution”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** bourbon absolutism, three estates and Rousseau's Social Contract arm the Third Estate; the Bastille falls and the revolution begins.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Results of the French Revolution”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** feudalism is abolished, customs barriers fall, and the class reads the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (1789) as a primary text — liberty and equality go out to Europe and the world.
