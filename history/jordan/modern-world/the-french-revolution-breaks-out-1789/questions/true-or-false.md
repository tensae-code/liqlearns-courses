# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bourbon absolutism, three estates and Rousseau's Social Contract arm the Third Estate; the Bastille falls and the revolution begins. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Results of the French Revolution” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Feudalism is abolished, customs barriers fall, and the class reads the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (1789) as a primary text — liberty and equality go out to Europe and the world.
