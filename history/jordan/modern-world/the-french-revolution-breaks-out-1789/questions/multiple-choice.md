# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The French Revolution”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Bourbon absolutism, three estates and Rousseau's Social Contract arm the Third Estate; the Bastille falls and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Bourbon absolutism, three estates and Rousseau's Social Contract arm the Third Estate; the Bastille falls and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Results of the French Revolution”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Feudalism is abolished, customs barriers fall, and the class reads the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Feudalism is abolished, customs barriers fall, and the class reads the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
