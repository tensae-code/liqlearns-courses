# Arar Mustafa Wahbi al Tal Jordans National Poet

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The poet Arar. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The poet Arar

Mustafa Wahbi al-Tal "Arar", born in Irbid in 1899, teacher and state employee, becomes Jordan and the Arab world's famous poet of the diwan "Ashiyyat Wadi al-Yabis".

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
