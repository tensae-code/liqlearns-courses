# Arar Mustafa Wahbi al Tal Jordans National Poet — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mustafa Wahbi al-Tal "Arar", born in Irbid in 1899, teacher and state employee, becomes Jordan and the Arab world's famous poet of the diwan "Ashiyyat Wadi al-Yabis".
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Arar Mustafa Wahbi al Tal Jordans National Poet” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Mustafa Wahbi al-Tal "Arar", born in Irbid in 1899, teacher and state employee, becomes Jordan and the Arab world's famous poet of the diwan "Ashiyyat Wadi al-Yabis".
