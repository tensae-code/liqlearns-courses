# Jordans Cultural Life Press Theatre and Festivals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The founder-king is a poet who fills his court with writers; Jordan's first newspaper, al-Haqq Ya'lu, is printed at Ma'an in 1920–21 carrying the revolt's principles, the press reaches Amman in 1923 and the official al-Sharq al-Arabi follows (renamed 1926); a Culture and Arts Department (1966) becomes a Ministry of Culture and Youth (1977); Jordan Television opens in black and white at Umm al-Hiran in 1968; Prince Hassan founds the Arab Thought Forum in 1981; Amman is Arab Capital of Culture in 2002; and the Jerash, Azraq and Fuheis festivals and the Reading-for-All Family Library carry culture to every town.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Jordans Cultural Life Press Theatre and Festivals” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The founder-king is a poet who fills his court with writers; Jordan's first newspaper, al-Haqq Ya'lu, is printed at Ma'an in 1920–21 carrying the revolt's principles, the press reaches Amman in 1923 and the official al-Sharq al-Arabi follows (renamed 1926); a Culture and Arts Department (1966) becomes a Ministry of Culture and Youth (1977); Jordan Television opens in black and white at Umm al-Hiran in 1968; Prince Hassan founds the Arab Thought Forum in 1981; Amman is Arab Capital of Culture in 2002; and the Jerash, Azraq and Fuheis festivals and the Reading-for-All Family Library carry culture to every town.
