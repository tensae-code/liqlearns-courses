# Video script — Jordans Cultural Life Press Theatre and Festivals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The founder-king is a poet who fills his court with writers; Jordan's first newspaper, al-Haqq Ya'lu, is printed at Ma'an in 1920–21 carrying the revolt's principles, the press reaches Amman in 1923 and the official al-Sharq al-Arabi follows (renamed 1926); a Culture and Arts Department (1966) becomes a Ministry of Culture and Youth (1977); Jordan Television opens in black and white at Umm al-Hiran in 1968; Prince Hassan founds the Arab Thought Forum in 1981; Amman is Arab Capital of Culture in 2002; and the Jerash, Azraq and Fuheis festivals and the Reading-for-All Family Library carry culture to every town.
