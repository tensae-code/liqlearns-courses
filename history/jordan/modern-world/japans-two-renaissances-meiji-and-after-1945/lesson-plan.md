# Lesson plan — Japans Two Renaissances Meiji and After 1945

**Course:** History — Jordan · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Japans Two Renaissances Meiji and After 1945
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Jordan in the World Today: Organisations, Issues and Rising Countries (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** An archipelago of thousands of islands, 378,000 km² and some 127 million people; samurai warlords sideline the emperors into a dual rule until the Meiji movement under Mutsuhito — slogan "rich country, strong army" — abolishes feudalism and centralises the state; destroyed in 1945, three million dead and its cities in ruins, Japan rises a second time on American aid, the world's most productive and disciplined workers, care for inventors, and an education system — six compulsory primary years, 9.4% of government spending in 2014 — that guards Japanese values while it modernises.
