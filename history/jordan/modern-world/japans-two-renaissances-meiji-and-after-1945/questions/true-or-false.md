# Japans Two Renaissances Meiji and After 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An archipelago of thousands of islands, 378,000 km² and some 127 million people; samurai warlords sideline the emperors into a dual rule until the Meiji movement under Mutsuhito — slogan "rich country, strong army" — abolishes feudalism and centralises the state; destroyed in 1945, three million dead and its cities in ruins, Japan rises a second time on American aid, the world's most productive and disciplined workers, care for inventors, and an education system — six compulsory primary years, 9.4% of government spending in 2014 — that guards Japanese values while it modernises.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Japans Two Renaissances Meiji and After 1945” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: An archipelago of thousands of islands, 378,000 km² and some 127 million people; samurai warlords sideline the emperors into a dual rule until the Meiji movement under Mutsuhito — slogan "rich country, strong army" — abolishes feudalism and centralises the state; destroyed in 1945, three million dead and its cities in ruins, Japan rises a second time on American aid, the world's most productive and disciplined workers, care for inventors, and an education system — six compulsory primary years, 9.4% of government spending in 2014 — that guards Japanese values while it modernises.
