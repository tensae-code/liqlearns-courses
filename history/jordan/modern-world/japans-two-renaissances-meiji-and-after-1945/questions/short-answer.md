# Japans Two Renaissances Meiji and After 1945 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Japan”.
**Answer:** an archipelago of thousands of islands, 378,000 km² and some 127 million people; samurai warlords sideline the emperors into a dual rule until the Meiji movement under Mutsuhito — slogan "rich country, strong army" — abolishes feudalism and centralises the state; destroyed in 1945, three million dead and its cities in ruins, Japan rises a second time on American aid, the world's most productive and disciplined workers, care for inventors, and an education system — six compulsory primary years, 9.4% of government spending in 2014 — that guards Japanese values while it modernises.
