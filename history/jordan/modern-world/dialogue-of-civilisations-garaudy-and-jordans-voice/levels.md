# Dialogue of Civilisations Garaudy and Jordans Voice — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A civilisation is everything humans make, in science, art, building and rule; the French Muslim thinker Roger Garaudy coins "dialogue of civilisations" for peaceful rivalry and bridge-building instead of clash; Jordan carries the idea through Prince Hassan's Arab Thought Forum and the Royal Institute for Inter-Faith Studies — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
