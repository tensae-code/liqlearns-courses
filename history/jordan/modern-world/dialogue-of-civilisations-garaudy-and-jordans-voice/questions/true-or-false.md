# Dialogue of Civilisations Garaudy and Jordans Voice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A civilisation is everything humans make, in science, art, building and rule; the French Muslim thinker Roger Garaudy coins "dialogue of civilisations" for peaceful rivalry and bridge-building instead of clash; Jordan carries the idea through Prince Hassan's Arab Thought Forum and the Royal Institute for Inter-Faith Studies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Dialogue of Civilisations Garaudy and Jordans Voice” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A civilisation is everything humans make, in science, art, building and rule; the French Muslim thinker Roger Garaudy coins "dialogue of civilisations" for peaceful rivalry and bridge-building instead of clash; Jordan carries the idea through Prince Hassan's Arab Thought Forum and the Royal Institute for Inter-Faith Studies.
