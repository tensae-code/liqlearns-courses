## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The dialogue of civilisations. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The dialogue of civilisations

A civilisation is everything humans make, in science, art, building and rule; the French Muslim thinker Roger Garaudy coins "dialogue of civilisations" for peaceful rivalry and bridge-building instead of clash; Jordan carries the idea through Prince Hassan's Arab Thought Forum and the Royal Institute for Inter-Faith Studies.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
