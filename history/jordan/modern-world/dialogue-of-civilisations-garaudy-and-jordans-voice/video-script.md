# Video script — Dialogue of Civilisations Garaudy and Jordans Voice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
A civilisation is everything humans make, in science, art, building and rule; the French Muslim thinker Roger Garaudy coins "dialogue of civilisations" for peaceful rivalry and bridge-building instead of clash; Jordan carries the idea through Prince Hassan's Arab Thought Forum and the Royal Institute for Inter-Faith Studies.

The dialogue of civilisations.

A civilisation is everything humans make, in science, art, building and rule; the French Muslim thinker Roger Garaudy coins "dialogue of civilisations" for peaceful rivalry and bridge-building instead of clash; Jordan carries the idea through Prince Hassan's Arab Thought Forum and the Royal Institute for Inter-Faith Studies.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
