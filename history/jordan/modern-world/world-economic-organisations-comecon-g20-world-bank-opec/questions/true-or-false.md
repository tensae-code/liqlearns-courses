# World Economic Organisations Comecon G20 World Bank Opec — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Soviet answer to the West, COMECON (1949), organises the socialist economies; after the financial crises of the 1990s the G7 fathers the G20 (1999), nineteen states plus the EU; the World Bank in Washington lends against poverty; and when the oil companies cut posted prices in 1959 without asking, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait, Iran and Venezuela found OPEC in 1960 — while the Arab exporters' own OAPEC struggles to match its weight.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “World Economic Organisations Comecon G20 World Bank Opec” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Soviet answer to the West, COMECON (1949), organises the socialist economies; after the financial crises of the 1990s the G7 fathers the G20 (1999), nineteen states plus the EU; the World Bank in Washington lends against poverty; and when the oil companies cut posted prices in 1959 without asking, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait, Iran and Venezuela found OPEC in 1960 — while the Arab exporters' own OAPEC struggles to match its weight.
