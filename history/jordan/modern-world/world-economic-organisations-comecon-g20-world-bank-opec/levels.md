# World Economic Organisations Comecon G20 World Bank Opec — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Soviet answer to the West, COMECON (1949), organises the socialist economies; after the financial crises of the 1990s the G7 fathers the G20 (1999), nineteen states plus the EU; the World Bank in Washington lends against poverty; and when the oil companies cut posted prices in 1959 without asking, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Kuwait, Iran and Venezuela found OPEC in 1960 — while the Arab exporters' own OAPEC struggles to match its weight — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
