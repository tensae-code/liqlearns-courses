# Jordan Since 1999 Abdullah Ii and the Reform Papers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Political life since 1999”.
**Answer:** abdullah II takes the oath on 7 February 1999; the 14th parliament (2003–2007) seats the first women's quota of six; the National Centre for Human Rights opens in 2002 and the National Agenda launches in 2005; the king's Discussion Papers invite the nation to argue its democratic road; after the royal committee of 26 April 2011 the constitution gains a Constitutional Court and an Independent Election Commission, and the 2015 Decentralisation Law elects governorate councils.
