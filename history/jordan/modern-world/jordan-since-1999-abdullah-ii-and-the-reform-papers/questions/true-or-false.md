# Jordan Since 1999 Abdullah Ii and the Reform Papers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Abdullah II takes the oath on 7 February 1999; the 14th parliament (2003–2007) seats the first women's quota of six; the National Centre for Human Rights opens in 2002 and the National Agenda launches in 2005; the king's Discussion Papers invite the nation to argue its democratic road; after the royal committee of 26 April 2011 the constitution gains a Constitutional Court and an Independent Election Commission, and the 2015 Decentralisation Law elects governorate councils.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Jordan Since 1999 Abdullah Ii and the Reform Papers” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Abdullah II takes the oath on 7 February 1999; the 14th parliament (2003–2007) seats the first women's quota of six; the National Centre for Human Rights opens in 2002 and the National Agenda launches in 2005; the king's Discussion Papers invite the nation to argue its democratic road; after the royal committee of 26 April 2011 the constitution gains a Constitutional Court and an Independent Election Commission, and the 2015 Decentralisation Law elects governorate councils.
