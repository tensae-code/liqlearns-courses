# Jordan Since 1999 Abdullah Ii and the Reform Papers — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Abdullah II takes the oath on 7 February 1999; the 14th parliament (2003–2007) seats the first women's quota of six; the National Centre for Human Rights opens in 2002 and the National Agenda launches in 2005; the king's Discussion Papers invite the nation to argue its democratic road; after the royal committee of 26 April 2011 the constitution gains a Constitutional Court and an Independent Election Commission, and the 2015 Decentralisation Law elects governorate councils — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
