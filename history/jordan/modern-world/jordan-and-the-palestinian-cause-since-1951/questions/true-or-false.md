# Jordan and the Palestinian Cause Since 1951 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Israel raids the West Bank's frontline villages through the 1950s–60s and diverts the Jordan River, driving the Arabs to the Cairo summit; Resolution 242 (22 November 1967) sets the land-for-peace terms Jordan still invokes; after Israel's 1982 invasion of Lebanon scatters the PLO to Tunis, Algiers and South Yemen, Amman and the PLO talk (1983–86) over Resolutions 242 and 338; Oslo follows in 1993; the second intifada brings Gaza's withdrawal, blockade and wars — and Jordan answers with field hospitals in the West Bank and Gaza under the Jordan Hashemite Charity Organisation and Abdullah II's diplomacy in every forum.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Jordan and the Palestinian Cause Since 1951” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
