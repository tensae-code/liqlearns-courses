## Jordan's stance since 1951

Israel raids the West Bank's frontline villages through the 1950s–60s and diverts the Jordan River, driving the Arabs to the Cairo summit; Resolution 242 (22 November 1967) sets the land-for-peace terms Jordan still invokes; after Israel's 1982 invasion of Lebanon scatters the PLO to Tunis, Algiers and South Yemen, Amman and the PLO talk (1983–86) over Resolutions 242 and 338; Oslo follows in 1993; the second intifada brings Gaza's withdrawal, blockade and wars — and Jordan answers with field hospitals in the West Bank and Gaza under the Jordan Hashemite Charity Organisation and Abdullah II's diplomacy in every forum.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
