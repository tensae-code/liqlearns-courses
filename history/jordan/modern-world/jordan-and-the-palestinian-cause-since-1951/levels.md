# Jordan and the Palestinian Cause Since 1951 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Israel raids the West Bank's frontline villages through the 1950s–60s and diverts the Jordan River, driving the Arabs to the Cairo summit; Resolution 242 (22 November 1967) sets the land-for-peace terms Jordan still invokes; after Israel's 1982 invasion of Lebanon scatters the PLO to Tunis, Algiers and South Yemen, Amman and the PLO talk (1983–86) over Resolutions 242 and 338; Oslo follows in 1993; the second intifada brings Gaza's withdrawal, blockade and wars — and Jordan answers with field hospitals in the West Bank and Gaza under the Jordan Hashemite Charity Organisation and Abdullah II's diplomacy in every forum — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
