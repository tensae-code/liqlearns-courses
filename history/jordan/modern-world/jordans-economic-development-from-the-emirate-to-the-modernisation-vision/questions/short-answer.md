# Jordans Economic Development from the Emirate to the Modernisation Vision — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Economic life 1921 to today”.
**Answer:** a poor Emirate founds an agricultural bank in 1922 and a Supreme Economic Committee in 1924, living on Ghawr and plateau grain, hand mills and olive presses until independence (1946) brings factories — textiles, mills, metals, paper; 1948 floods the country with refugees and closes Palestine's ports, so Jordan builds Aqaba's port and digs the East Ghawr (King Abdullah) Canal from Adasiya to South Shuna, raises the Central Bank in 1964 and a Tourism Authority in 1960 (a ministry by 1967); the dinar halves amid military spending and subsidy cuts, the 1990–91 Gulf crisis sends the expatriates home — then the US free-trade agreement (2001), the Aqaba Special Economic Zone, the Abdali project and the Investment Commission (2014, a ministry by 2021) carry the story to the economic-modernisation vision.
