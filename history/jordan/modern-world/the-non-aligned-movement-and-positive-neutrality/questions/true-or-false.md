# The Non Aligned Movement and Positive Neutrality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Refusing both Cold War camps, Nehru, Zhou Enlai, Nasser and Tito forge "positive neutrality" at Bandung and the Cairo preparatory conference: independent policies, peaceful settlement, support for liberation movements, no great-power military alliances, no foreign bases; Jordan takes part as a founder and attends every summit.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Non Aligned Movement and Positive Neutrality” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Refusing both Cold War camps, Nehru, Zhou Enlai, Nasser and Tito forge "positive neutrality" at Bandung and the Cairo preparatory conference: independent policies, peaceful settlement, support for liberation movements, no great-power military alliances, no foreign bases; Jordan takes part as a founder and attends every summit.
