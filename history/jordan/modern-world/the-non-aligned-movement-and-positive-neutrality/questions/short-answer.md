# The Non Aligned Movement and Positive Neutrality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Non-Aligned Movement”.
**Answer:** refusing both Cold War camps, Nehru, Zhou Enlai, Nasser and Tito forge "positive neutrality" at Bandung and the Cairo preparatory conference: independent policies, peaceful settlement, support for liberation movements, no great-power military alliances, no foreign bases; Jordan takes part as a founder and attends every summit.
