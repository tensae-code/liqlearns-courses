# The Non Aligned Movement and Positive Neutrality — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Refusing both Cold War camps, Nehru, Zhou Enlai, Nasser and Tito forge "positive neutrality" at Bandung and the Cairo preparatory conference: independent policies, peaceful settlement, support for liberation movements, no great-power military alliances, no foreign bases; Jordan takes part as a founder and attends every summit — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
