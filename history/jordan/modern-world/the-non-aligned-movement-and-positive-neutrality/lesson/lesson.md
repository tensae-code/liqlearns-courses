# The Non Aligned Movement and Positive Neutrality

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Non-Aligned Movement. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Non-Aligned Movement

Refusing both Cold War camps, Nehru, Zhou Enlai, Nasser and Tito forge "positive neutrality" at Bandung and the Cairo preparatory conference: independent policies, peaceful settlement, support for liberation movements, no great-power military alliances, no foreign bases; Jordan takes part as a founder and attends every summit.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
