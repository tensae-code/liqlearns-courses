# Transport Through Time from Feet to Electric Vehicles

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Development of transport; Transport development strand. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Development of transport

People walk, ride horses and camels, pull carts, then drive fuel cars, trains, ships and planes, and now electric vehicles.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transport development strand

Developing transport solves the distance problem between countries and opens and lights Jordan's roads.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
