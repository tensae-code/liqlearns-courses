# Transport Through Time from Feet to Electric Vehicles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Development of transport”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) People walk, ride horses and camels, pull carts, then drive fuel cars, trains, ships and planes, and now elect…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) People walk, ride horses and camels, pull carts, then drive fuel cars, trains, ships and planes, and now elect…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Transport development strand”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Developing transport solves the distance problem between countries and opens and lights Jordan's roads.  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Developing transport solves the distance problem between countries and opens and lights Jordan's roads.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
