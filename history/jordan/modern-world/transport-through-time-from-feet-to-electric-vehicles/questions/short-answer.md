# Transport Through Time from Feet to Electric Vehicles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Development of transport”. (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Who is in the story of “Transport development strand”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
