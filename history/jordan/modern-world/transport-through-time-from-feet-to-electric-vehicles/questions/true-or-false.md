# Transport Through Time from Feet to Electric Vehicles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** People walk, ride horses and camels, pull carts, then drive fuel cars, trains, ships and planes, and now electric vehicles. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Transport development strand” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Developing transport solves the distance problem between countries and opens and lights Jordan's roads.
