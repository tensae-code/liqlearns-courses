# Jordan Founding the Emirate and the Road to Independence — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — France seizes Damascus and Syria's leaders beg Sharif Hussein to send a son; Abdullah reaches Amman and on 11 April 1921 forms the Council of Advisors under Rashid Tali', staffed by Syrian Istiqlal men; the 1928 Jerusalem agreement promises a Basic Law and hands legislation and executive power to the Amir, yet keeps British troops and an annual grant; the People's Party and the national conferences answer with a charter — reject Balfour, refuse to pay for Britain's Frontier Force, demand real elections and a government answerable to parliament; the 1935 Defence Law muzzles the opposition, until the March 1946 treaty ends the mandate; the state emblem, designed in 1934 at the founder's wish, is set over Raghadan Palace — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
