# Video script — Jordan Founding the Emirate and the Road to Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
France seizes Damascus and Syria's leaders beg Sharif Hussein to send a son; Abdullah reaches Amman and on 11 April 1921 forms the Council of Advisors under Rashid Tali', staffed by Syrian Istiqlal men; the 1928 Jerusalem agreement promises a Basic Law and hands legislation and executive power to the Amir, yet keeps British troops and an annual grant; the People's Party and the national conferences answer with a charter — reject Balfour, refuse to pay for Britain's Frontier Force, demand real elections and a government answerable to parliament; the 1935 Defence Law muzzles the opposition, until the March 1946 treaty ends the mandate; the state emblem, designed in 1934 at the founder's wish, is set over Raghadan Palace.
