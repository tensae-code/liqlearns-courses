# The United Nations Founding Organs and Jordans Role — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The League of Nations (1919, Geneva) fails — America never joins and no war is prevented — so in 1945 the UN rises in New York with 193 members and six official languages; the Security Council's fifteen members include five with the veto; agencies fan out — ILO in Geneva, FAO in Rome, IAEA in Vienna, UNRWA headquartered in Amman for Palestine's refugees; Jordan, a member since 1955, sends peacekeepers worldwide, sits on the Security Council in 1965–66, 1982–83 and 2014–15, and King Abdullah II addresses the General Assembly's 79th session in 2024. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Jordan in the United Nations” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A member since 1955, Jordan works the UN's three pillars — sustainable development, peace and security, human rights — sends its soldiers as peacekeepers, and carries the Palestinian cause into the Security Council it has thrice sat on.
