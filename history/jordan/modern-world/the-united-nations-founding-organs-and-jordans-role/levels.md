## Sub lesson 1 — The United Nations
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The League of Nations (1919, Geneva) fails — America never joins and no war is prevented — so in 1945 the UN rises in New York with 193 members and six official languages; the Security Council's fifteen members include five with the veto; agencies fan out — ILO in Geneva, FAO in Rome, IAEA in Vienna, UNRWA headquartered in Amman for Palestine's refugees; Jordan, a member since 1955, sends peacekeepers worldwide, sits on the Security Council in 1965–66, 1982–83 and 2014–15, and King Abdullah II addresses the General Assembly's 79th session in 2024 — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Jordan in the United Nations
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A member since 1955, Jordan works the UN's three pillars — sustainable development, peace and security, human rights — sends its soldiers as peacekeepers, and carries the Palestinian cause into the Security Council it has thrice sat on — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
