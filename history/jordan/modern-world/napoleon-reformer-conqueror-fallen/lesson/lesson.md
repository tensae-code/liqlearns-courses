# Napoleon Reformer Conqueror Fallen

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The revolution's stages. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The revolution's stages

Louis XVI is executed in 1793 and the republic proclaimed under the Committee of Public Safety; in 1795 Napoleon Bonaparte seizes the stage, then carries the revolution's wars to Egypt and Bilad al-Sham (1798–1801) and against Russia.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
