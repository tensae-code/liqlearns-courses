# Napoleon Reformer Conqueror Fallen — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Louis XVI is executed in 1793 and the republic proclaimed under the Committee of Public Safety; in 1795 Napoleon Bonaparte seizes the stage, then carries the revolution's wars to Egypt and Bilad al-Sham (1798–1801) and against Russia — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
