# Napoleon Reformer Conqueror Fallen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Louis XVI is executed in 1793 and the republic proclaimed under the Committee of Public Safety; in 1795 Napoleon Bonaparte seizes the stage, then carries the revolution's wars to Egypt and Bilad al-Sham (1798–1801) and against Russia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Napoleon Reformer Conqueror Fallen” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Louis XVI is executed in 1793 and the republic proclaimed under the Committee of Public Safety; in 1795 Napoleon Bonaparte seizes the stage, then carries the revolution's wars to Egypt and Bilad al-Sham (1798–1801) and against Russia.
