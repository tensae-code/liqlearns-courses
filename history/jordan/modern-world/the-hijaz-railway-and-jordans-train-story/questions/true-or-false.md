# The Hijaz Railway and Jordans Train Story — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Jordan under Ottoman rule” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: East Jordan stays in Damascus's orbit from first to last; Abdülhamid II drives the Hijaz Railway from Damascus toward Madinah across Jordanian stations, and the Shami Hajj road makes the land an economic lifeline.
