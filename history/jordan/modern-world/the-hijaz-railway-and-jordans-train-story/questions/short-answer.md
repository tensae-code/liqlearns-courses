# The Hijaz Railway and Jordans Train Story — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Development of trade in my homeland, opening story”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations.

**2.** Who is in the story of “Jordan under Ottoman rule”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** east Jordan stays in Damascus's orbit from first to last; Abdülhamid II drives the Hijaz Railway from Damascus toward Madinah across Jordanian stations, and the Shami Hajj road makes the land an economic lifeline.
