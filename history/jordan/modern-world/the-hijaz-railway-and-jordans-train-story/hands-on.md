# Hands-on — The Hijaz Railway and Jordans Train Story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Development of trade in my homeland, opening story; Jordan under Ottoman rule; Unit 3 Lesson 2, the railway part; Unit 3 Lesson 2, the railway part. Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
