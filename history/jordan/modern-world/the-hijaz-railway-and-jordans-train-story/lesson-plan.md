# Lesson plan — The Hijaz Railway and Jordans Train Story

**Course:** History — Jordan · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Hijaz Railway and Jordans Train Story
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Jordan Made: From the Great Arab Revolt to the Modern Kingdom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Jordan Made: From the Great Arab Revolt to the Modern Kingdom — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Development of trade in my homeland, opening story** — Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations
2. **Jordan under Ottoman rule** — East Jordan stays in Damascus's orbit from first to last; Abdülhamid II drives the Hijaz Railway from Damascus toward Madinah across Jordanian stations, and the Shami Hajj road makes the land an economic lifeline
3. **Unit 3 Lesson 2, the railway part** — The class traces the Hijaz Railway on the map through Jordan's stations — Damascus, Daraa, Mafraq, Zarqa, Amman, Qatrana, Ma'an, Batn al-Ghul, Aqaba's branch, al-Mudawara, Tabuk, Mada'in Salih, al-Ula, Medina — the iron road that replaced the forty-day Hajj caravan
4. **Unit 3 Lesson 2, the railway part** — The Hijaz Railway map is traced through Jordan as in the vocational book — Qatrana, Ma'an, Batn al-Ghul and the Aqaba branch among its stations — the line along which the Circassian and Chechen villages of the previous lesson were planted

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
