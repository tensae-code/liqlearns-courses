# The First World War Versailles and the Results

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The course and settlement of the First World War. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The course and settlement of the First World War

Allies (France, Britain, Tsarist Russia, then the USA in 1917 despite its isolationism) face the Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Bulgaria and the Ottoman state); Germany collapses in the autumn of 1918; at Paris the Big Four impose Versailles on Weimar Germany and Sèvres on the Ottomans (later replaced by Lausanne) — a peace so vindictive, the book warns, that it prepares the second war and the totalitarian regimes, communist, Nazi and Fascist; the class itself re-runs Versailles as a simulation, each group pleading a nation's case.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
