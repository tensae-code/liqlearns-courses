# The First World War Versailles and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The course and settlement of the First World War”.
**Answer:** allies (France, Britain, Tsarist Russia, then the USA in 1917 despite its isolationism) face the Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Bulgaria and the Ottoman state); Germany collapses in the autumn of 1918; at Paris the Big Four impose Versailles on Weimar Germany and Sèvres on the Ottomans (later replaced by Lausanne) — a peace so vindictive, the book warns, that it prepares the second war and the totalitarian regimes, communist, Nazi and Fascist; the class itself re-runs Versailles as a simulation, each group pleading a nation's case.
