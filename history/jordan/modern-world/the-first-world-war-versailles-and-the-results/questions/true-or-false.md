# The First World War Versailles and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Allies (France, Britain, Tsarist Russia, then the USA in 1917 despite its isolationism) face the Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Bulgaria and the Ottoman state); Germany collapses in the autumn of 1918; at Paris the Big Four impose Versailles on Weimar Germany and Sèvres on the Ottomans (later replaced by Lausanne) — a peace so vindictive, the book warns, that it prepares the second war and the totalitarian regimes, communist, Nazi and Fascist; the class itself re-runs Versailles as a simulation, each group pleading a nation's case.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The First World War Versailles and the Results” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Allies (France, Britain, Tsarist Russia, then the USA in 1917 despite its isolationism) face the Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Bulgaria and the Ottoman state); Germany collapses in the autumn of 1918; at Paris the Big Four impose Versailles on Weimar Germany and Sèvres on the Ottomans (later replaced by Lausanne) — a peace so vindictive, the book warns, that it prepares the second war and the totalitarian regimes, communist, Nazi and Fascist; the class itself re-runs Versailles as a simulation, each group pleading a nation's case.
