# The Russian Revolution 1917 and the Soviet Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Russian Revolution”.
**Answer:** tsarist tyranny jails its writers — Pushkin, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Gorky — until war and hunger raise the soviets; Lenin returns to Petrograd, the Bolsheviks take power in 1917, Russia leaves the Great War and the Soviet Union is born.
