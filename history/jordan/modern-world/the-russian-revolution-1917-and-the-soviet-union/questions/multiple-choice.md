# The Russian Revolution 1917 and the Soviet Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Russian Revolution”?
a) Tsarist tyranny jails its writers — Pushkin, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Gorky — until war and hunger raise the sovie…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Tsarist tyranny jails its writers — Pushkin, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Gorky — until war and hunger raise the sovie…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Russian Revolution 1917 and the Soviet Union”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Tsarist tyranny jails its writers — Pushkin, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Gorky — until war and hunger raise the sovie…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Tsarist tyranny jails its writers — Pushkin, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Gorky — until war and hunger raise the sovie…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
