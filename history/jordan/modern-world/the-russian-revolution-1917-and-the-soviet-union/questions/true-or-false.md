# The Russian Revolution 1917 and the Soviet Union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tsarist tyranny jails its writers — Pushkin, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Gorky — until war and hunger raise the soviets; Lenin returns to Petrograd, the Bolsheviks take power in 1917, Russia leaves the Great War and the Soviet Union is born.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Russian Revolution 1917 and the Soviet Union” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Tsarist tyranny jails its writers — Pushkin, Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Gorky — until war and hunger raise the soviets; Lenin returns to Petrograd, the Bolsheviks take power in 1917, Russia leaves the Great War and the Soviet Union is born.
