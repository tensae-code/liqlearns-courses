# Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Abraham Darby smelts iron with coke in 1709, James Watt patents the steam engine in 1769, Edison lights the bulb in 1879 — and society splits into a capitalist class and a proletariat that answers with unions and parties.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Abraham Darby smelts iron with coke in 1709, James Watt patents the steam engine in 1769, Edison lights the bulb in 1879 — and society splits into a capitalist class and a proletariat that answers with unions and parties.
