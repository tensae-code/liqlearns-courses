# Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Abraham Darby smelts iron with coke in 1709, James Watt patents the steam engine in 1769, Edison lights the bulb in 1879 — and society splits into a capitalist class and a proletariat that answers with unions and parties — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
