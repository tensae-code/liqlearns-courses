# The First World War Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The world in the twentieth century — the First World War”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two alliance blocs face off, a Serbian student assassinates Archduke Franz Ferdinand, and in August 1914 Europe is at war; Prince Faysal, the Sharif's son, leads the Arab Revolt's northern army into Damascus.

**2.** Who is in the story of “The First World War (1914–1918), the causes”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** colonial rivalry, the alliance blocs, and nationalism (Italian and German unity achieved, Balkan nationalisms pulling Russia, protector of the Slavs, against Austria-Hungary) load the gun through the Morocco crises and the Balkan crises of 1908–1913; a Serbian student fires it, assassinating Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo in June 1914.
