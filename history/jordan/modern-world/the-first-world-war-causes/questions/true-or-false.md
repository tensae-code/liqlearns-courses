# The First World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two alliance blocs face off, a Serbian student assassinates Archduke Franz Ferdinand, and in August 1914 Europe is at war; Prince Faysal, the Sharif's son, leads the Arab Revolt's northern army into Damascus. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The First World War (1914–1918), the causes” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Colonial rivalry, the alliance blocs, and nationalism (Italian and German unity achieved, Balkan nationalisms pulling Russia, protector of the Slavs, against Austria-Hungary) load the gun through the Morocco crises and the Balkan crises of 1908–1913; a Serbian student fires it, assassinating Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo in June 1914.
