# The First World War Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The world in the twentieth century — the First World War; The First World War (1914–1918), the causes. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The world in the twentieth century — the First World War

Two alliance blocs face off, a Serbian student assassinates Archduke Franz Ferdinand, and in August 1914 Europe is at war; Prince Faysal, the Sharif's son, leads the Arab Revolt's northern army into Damascus.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The First World War (1914–1918), the causes

Colonial rivalry, the alliance blocs, and nationalism (Italian and German unity achieved, Balkan nationalisms pulling Russia, protector of the Slavs, against Austria-Hungary) load the gun through the Morocco crises and the Balkan crises of 1908–1913; a Serbian student fires it, assassinating Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo in June 1914.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
