# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Arqueoastronomía y calendarios”?
**Answer:** The oldest civilisations read the sky to build calendars; archaeoastronomy shows how their temples and stones kept time. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: Arqueo astronomía” end?
**Answer:** Archaeoastronomy reads ancient sky-watching and calendars as evidence of how peoples dated their world. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
