# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The oldest civilisations read the sky to build calendars; archaeoastronomy shows how their temples and stones kept time.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: Arqueo astronomía” is mainly about this: The first ancient cultures organise cities, writing and states between the rivers
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Archaeoastronomy reads ancient sky-watching and calendars as evidence of how peoples dated their world.
