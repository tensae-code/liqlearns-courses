# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **What History Is** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Introducción a las Ciencias Sociales

The social sciences are defined as the disciplines that study the human being whole — Sociology, History, Geography, Anthropology and more — through Bolivia's nine departments and their peoples. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 1: La ciencia de la historia (with Introducción a las Ciencias Sociales)

History is presented as a science of the human past that must serve the present, not merely academic memory. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
