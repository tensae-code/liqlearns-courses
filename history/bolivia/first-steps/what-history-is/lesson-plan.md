# Lesson plan — What History Is

**Course:** History — Bolivia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What History Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Beginnings and the First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The social sciences are defined as the disciplines that study the human being whole — Sociology, History, Geography, Anthropology and more — through Bolivia's nine departments and their peoples.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Beginnings and the First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).
