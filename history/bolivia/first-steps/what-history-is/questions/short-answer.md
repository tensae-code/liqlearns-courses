# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Introducción a las Ciencias Sociales”?
**Answer:** The social sciences are defined as the disciplines that study the human being whole — Sociology, History, Geography, Anthropology and more — through Bolivia's nine departments and their peoples. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: La ciencia de la historia (with Introducción a las Ciencias Sociales)” end?
**Answer:** History is presented as a science of the human past that must serve the present, not merely academic memory. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
