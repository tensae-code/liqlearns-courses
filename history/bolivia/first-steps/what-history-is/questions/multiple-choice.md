# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the heart of the story in “Introducción a las Ciencias Sociales”?
a) The social sciences are defined as the disciplines that study the human being whole — Sociology, History, Geog…  b) History is built from primary, secondary and tertiary sources, worked by the historical research method  c) Climate crisis may have driven hominid evolution; language-rich groups out-compete others, and Homo sapiens ma…  d) Sedentarisation and the village community emerge as farming replaces the wandering life
**Answer: a) The social sciences are defined as the disciplines that study the human being whole — Sociology, History, Geog…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
