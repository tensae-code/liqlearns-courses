# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The social sciences are defined as the disciplines that study the human being whole — Sociology, History, Geography, Anthropology and more — through Bolivia's nine departments and their peoples.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: La ciencia de la historia (with Introducción a las Ciencias Sociales)” is mainly about this: History is built from primary, secondary and tertiary sources, worked by the historical research method
**Answer: False** — It is about this: History is presented as a science of the human past that must serve the present, not merely academic memory.
