## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **El debate sobre la colonia española en América** — Students weigh the theses for and against Spain's "Black Legend", reading Bartolomé de las Casas (1542) against its deniers.
2. **Año 3: Revisionistas en la historia de la independencia** — Revisionist historians re-argue the independence — students learn the national story itself is debated.
3. **Año 5: La historia crítica** — Traditional-positivist history is set against critical, reflexive history — the method unit of the fifth year.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
