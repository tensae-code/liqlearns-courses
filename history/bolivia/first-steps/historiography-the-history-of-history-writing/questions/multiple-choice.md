# Historiography: The History of History Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the heart of the story in “El debate sobre la colonia española en América”?
a) Students weigh the theses for and against Spain's "Black Legend", reading Bartolomé de las Casas (1542) agains…  b) Islam's rise is the door into the medieval world process  c) Medieval Europe organises itself feudally after Rome  d) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being
**Answer: a) Students weigh the theses for and against Spain's "Black Legend", reading Bartolomé de las Casas (1542) agains…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: Revisionistas en la historia de la independencia”?
a) Revisionist historians re-argue the independence — students learn the national story itself is debated  b) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being  c) Enlightenment reason challenges throne and altar and arms the independence generation  d) From 1760 industry transforms production and society, first in England
**Answer: a) Revisionist historians re-argue the independence — students learn the national story itself is debated** — That is what this lesson teaches.
