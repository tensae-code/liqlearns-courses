# Historiography: The History of History Writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “El debate sobre la colonia española en América”?
**Answer:** Students weigh the theses for and against Spain's "Black Legend", reading Bartolomé de las Casas (1542) against its deniers. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 3: Revisionistas en la historia de la independencia” end?
**Answer:** Revisionist historians re-argue the independence — students learn the national story itself is debated. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
