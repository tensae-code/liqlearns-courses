# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Egypt's kingdoms rise on the Nile to the New Kingdom of Thebes (1580–1100 BC).
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: Formación de las culturas en el mundo antiguo (Egypt part)” is mainly about this: The Greek world of Crete, Cyprus, Rhodes and Sicily, with Prometheus and the gift of fire
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Egypt's Nile civilisation stands beside Mesopotamia in the formation of the ancient world.
