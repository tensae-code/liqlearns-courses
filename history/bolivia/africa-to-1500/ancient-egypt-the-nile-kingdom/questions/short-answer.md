# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Egipto antiguo”?
**Answer:** Egypt's kingdoms rise on the Nile to the New Kingdom of Thebes (1580–1100 BC). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: Formación de las culturas en el mundo antiguo (Egypt part)” end?
**Answer:** Egypt's Nile civilisation stands beside Mesopotamia in the formation of the ancient world. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
