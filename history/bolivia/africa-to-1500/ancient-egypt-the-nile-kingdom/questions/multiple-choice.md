# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the heart of the story in “Egipto antiguo”?
a) Egypt's kingdoms rise on the Nile to the New Kingdom of Thebes (1580–1100 BC)  b) The Greek world of Crete, Cyprus, Rhodes and Sicily, with Prometheus and the gift of fire  c) Rome's legacy runs from the Julian calendar of Julius Caesar (46 BC) to the aqueducts  d) The first Americans arrive over the Bering land bridge, and perhaps also by coastal sea routes, peopling a new…
**Answer: a) Egypt's kingdoms rise on the Nile to the New Kingdom of Thebes (1580–1100 BC)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     What is the heart of the story in “Año 1: Formación de las culturas en el mundo antiguo (Egypt part)”?
a) Egypt's Nile civilisation stands beside Mesopotamia in the formation of the ancient world  b) The first Americans arrive over the Bering land bridge, and perhaps also by coastal sea routes, peopling a new…  c) Tenochtitlán's pyramids, temples and palaces rise under the Tlatoani, the hereditary supreme ruler of the Azte…  d) Maya and Aztec number systems are compared, and the Popol Vuh is read as the summit of Maya and Native America…
**Answer: a) Egypt's Nile civilisation stands beside Mesopotamia in the formation of the ancient world** — That is what this lesson teaches.
