# Lesson plan — Africa, Cradle of Humankind

**Course:** History — Bolivia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Africa, Cradle of Humankind
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Beginnings and the First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Climate crisis may have driven hominid evolution; language-rich groups out-compete others, and Homo sapiens masters fire for protection, light, warmth and cooking.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Beginnings and the First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Proceso de hominización** — Climate crisis may have driven hominid evolution; language-rich groups out-compete others, and Homo sapiens masters fire for protection, light, warmth and cooking.
2. **Año 1: La evolución de la especie humana** — Homo ergaster and its kin evolve toward modern humans — "it is practically a miracle we are still here".

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
