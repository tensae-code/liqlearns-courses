# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Climate crisis may have driven hominid evolution; language-rich groups out-compete others, and Homo sapiens masters fire for protection, light, warmth and cooking.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: La evolución de la especie humana” is mainly about this: Sedentarisation and the village community emerge as farming replaces the wandering life
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Homo ergaster and its kin evolve toward modern humans — "it is practically a miracle we are still here".
