# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Proceso de hominización”?
**Answer:** Climate crisis may have driven hominid evolution; language-rich groups out-compete others, and Homo sapiens masters fire for protection, light, warmth and cooking. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: La evolución de la especie humana” end?
**Answer:** Homo ergaster and its kin evolve toward modern humans — "it is practically a miracle we are still here". Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
