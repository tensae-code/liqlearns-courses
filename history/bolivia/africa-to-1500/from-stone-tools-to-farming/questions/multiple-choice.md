# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the heart of the story in “Del Paleolítico al Neolítico”?
a) Sedentarisation and the village community emerge as farming replaces the wandering life  b) The Shang dynasty (c. 1600–1046 BC), first historically certain dynasty, writing's origins and the Great Wall'…  c) Egypt's kingdoms rise on the Nile to the New Kingdom of Thebes (1580–1100 BC)  d) The Greek world of Crete, Cyprus, Rhodes and Sicily, with Prometheus and the gift of fire
**Answer: a) Sedentarisation and the village community emerge as farming replaces the wandering life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     What is the heart of the story in “Año 1: El surgimiento de las sociedades humanas”?
a) From the Upper Palaeolithic (Aurignacian, 30,000–25,000 years ago) human societies form and settle  b) The Greek world of Crete, Cyprus, Rhodes and Sicily, with Prometheus and the gift of fire  c) Rome's legacy runs from the Julian calendar of Julius Caesar (46 BC) to the aqueducts  d) The first Americans arrive over the Bering land bridge, and perhaps also by coastal sea routes, peopling a new…
**Answer: a) From the Upper Palaeolithic (Aurignacian, 30,000–25,000 years ago) human societies form and settle** — That is what this lesson teaches.
