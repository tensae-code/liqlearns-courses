# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Del Paleolítico al Neolítico”?
**Answer:** Sedentarisation and the village community emerge as farming replaces the wandering life. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: El surgimiento de las sociedades humanas” end?
**Answer:** From the Upper Palaeolithic (Aurignacian, 30,000–25,000 years ago) human societies form and settle. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
