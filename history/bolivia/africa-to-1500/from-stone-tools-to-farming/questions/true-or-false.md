# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Sedentarisation and the village community emerge as farming replaces the wandering life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: El surgimiento de las sociedades humanas” is mainly about this: The Shang dynasty (c. 1600–1046 BC), first historically certain dynasty, writing's origins and the Great Wall's curiosities
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From the Upper Palaeolithic (Aurignacian, 30,000–25,000 years ago) human societies form and settle.
