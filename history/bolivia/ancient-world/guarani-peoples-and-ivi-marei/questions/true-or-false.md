# Guaraní Peoples and Ivi Marei — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The Guaraní seek Ivy Marei, the Land Without Evil; their tenta communities are led by a Mburuvicha, and the Tenta Guasu by the Mburuvicha guasu, under the law of freedom (yeyora).
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 2: Los Guaraníes” is mainly about this: The invasion reaches the Inca world; adelantados found cities and then fight each other — Almagro against Pizarro's men — over the spoils
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher-compendium unit on the Guaraní, same arc as the student text.
