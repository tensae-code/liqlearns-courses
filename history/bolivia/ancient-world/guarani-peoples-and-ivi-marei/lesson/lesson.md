# Guaraní Peoples and Ivi Marei

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Guaraní Peoples and Ivi Marei** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Los Guaraníes

The Guaraní seek Ivy Marei, the Land Without Evil; their tenta communities are led by a Mburuvicha, and the Tenta Guasu by the Mburuvicha guasu, under the law of freedom (yeyora). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 2: Los Guaraníes

Teacher-compendium unit on the Guaraní, same arc as the student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
