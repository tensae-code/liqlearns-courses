# The Aymara Señoríos: Kallawaya and Carangas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     After Tiwanaku the Aymara lordships rule the Altiplano; the Kallawaya healers keep their Machaj Juyay language, and Carangas declines only with the Spanish arrival in the 16th century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: Los Aymaras” is mainly about this: The oldest civilisations read the sky to build calendars; archaeoastronomy shows how their temples and stones kept time
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Aymara people and their lordships carry the story from Tiwanaku toward the Inca.
