# The Aymara Señoríos: Kallawaya and Carangas

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Aymara Señoríos: Kallawaya and Carangas** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Los señoríos aymaras, Kallawaya y Carangas

After Tiwanaku the Aymara lordships rule the Altiplano; the Kallawaya healers keep their Machaj Juyay language, and Carangas declines only with the Spanish arrival in the 16th century. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 1: Los Aymaras

The Aymara people and their lordships carry the story from Tiwanaku toward the Inca. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
