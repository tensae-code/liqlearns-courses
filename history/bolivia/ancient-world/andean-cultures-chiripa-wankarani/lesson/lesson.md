# Andean Cultures: Chiripa and Wankarani

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Culturas altiplánicas tempranas: Chiripa

Chiripa, on Lake Titicaca's shore, is one of the Altiplano's first civilisations, with its sunken temple and early farming villages. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 1: Las primeras culturas en el territorio que hoy es Bolivia

The first cultures of today's Bolivian territory, Chiripa among them, open the national deep past. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
