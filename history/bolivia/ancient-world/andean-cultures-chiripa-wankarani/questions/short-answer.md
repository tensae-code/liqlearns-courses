# Andean Cultures: Chiripa and Wankarani — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Culturas altiplánicas tempranas: Chiripa”?
**Answer:** Chiripa, on Lake Titicaca's shore, is one of the Altiplano's first civilisations, with its sunken temple and early farming villages. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: Las primeras culturas en el territorio que hoy es Bolivia” end?
**Answer:** The first cultures of today's Bolivian territory, Chiripa among them, open the national deep past. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
