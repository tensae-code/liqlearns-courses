# Andean Cultures: Chiripa and Wankarani — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Chiripa, on Lake Titicaca's shore, is one of the Altiplano's first civilisations, with its sunken temple and early farming villages.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: Las primeras culturas en el territorio que hoy es Bolivia” is mainly about this: Tiwanaku builds the Akapana pyramid and the Puerta del Sol and trades camelid textiles, meat and wool across long-distance networks
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The first cultures of today's Bolivian territory, Chiripa among them, open the national deep past.
