# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Los Mayas”?
**Answer:** Maya and Aztec number systems are compared, and the Popol Vuh is read as the summit of Maya and Native American literature. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: Civilizaciones originarias del Abya Yala (Maya part)” end?
**Answer:** The original civilisations of Abya Yala are taught as civilisations in full, the Maya among them. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
