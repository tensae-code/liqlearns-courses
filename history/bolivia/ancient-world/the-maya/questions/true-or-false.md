# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Maya and Aztec number systems are compared, and the Popol Vuh is read as the summit of Maya and Native American literature.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: Civilizaciones originarias del Abya Yala (Maya part)” is mainly about this: Chiripa, on Lake Titicaca's shore, is one of the Altiplano's first civilisations, with its sunken temple and early farming villages
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The original civilisations of Abya Yala are taught as civilisations in full, the Maya among them.
