# The Maya

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Maya** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Los Mayas

Maya and Aztec number systems are compared, and the Popol Vuh is read as the summit of Maya and Native American literature. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 1: Civilizaciones originarias del Abya Yala (Maya part)

The original civilisations of Abya Yala are taught as civilisations in full, the Maya among them. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
