# The Inca: Andean Civilisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Before the Spanish, the Inca empire organises its territory in suyos, its people in ayllus working land communally, under the Inca and his heir the Auqui.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 2: El Tawantinsuyu” is mainly about this: In the Llanos de Moxos a hydraulic culture of raised fields and lomas settles from the 4th c. BC to the 13th c. AD — Moxos, Baures, Casarabes, Takanas, Mosetenes and Tsimanes purify water with the tap…
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher-compendium unit on the Inca state, same arc as the Secundaria 2 student text.
