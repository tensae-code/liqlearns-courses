# The Inca: Andean Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “El Tawantinsuyo”?
**Answer:** Before the Spanish, the Inca empire organises its territory in suyos, its people in ayllus working land communally, under the Inca and his heir the Auqui. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 2: El Tawantinsuyu” end?
**Answer:** Teacher-compendium unit on the Inca state, same arc as the Secundaria 2 student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
