# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: Desarrollo del proceso histórico en el mundo: el Islam”?
a) Islam's rise is the door into the medieval world process  b) Medieval Europe organises itself feudally after Rome  c) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being  d) Enlightenment reason challenges throne and altar and arms the independence generation
**Answer: a) Islam's rise is the door into the medieval world process** — That is what this lesson teaches.
