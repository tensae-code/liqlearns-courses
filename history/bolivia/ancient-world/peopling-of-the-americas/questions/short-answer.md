# The Peopling of the Americas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Poblamiento de América”?
**Answer:** The first Americans arrive over the Bering land bridge, and perhaps also by coastal sea routes, peopling a new continent. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: Poblamiento del Abya Yala” end?
**Answer:** The peopling of Abya Yala — the Americas under their indigenous name — opens America's own history. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
