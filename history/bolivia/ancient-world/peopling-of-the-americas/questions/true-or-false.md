# The Peopling of the Americas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The first Americans arrive over the Bering land bridge, and perhaps also by coastal sea routes, peopling a new continent.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: Poblamiento del Abya Yala” is mainly about this: Tenochtitlán's pyramids, temples and palaces rise under the Tlatoani, the hereditary supreme ruler of the Aztec empire
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The peopling of Abya Yala — the Americas under their indigenous name — opens America's own history.
