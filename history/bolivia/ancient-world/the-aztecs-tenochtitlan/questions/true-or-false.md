# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Tenochtitlán's pyramids, temples and palaces rise under the Tlatoani, the hereditary supreme ruler of the Aztec empire.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: Civilizaciones originarias del Abya Yala (Aztec part)” is mainly about this: Maya and Aztec number systems are compared, and the Popol Vuh is read as the summit of Maya and Native American literature
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Aztec world of Tenochtitlán stands among Abya Yala's original civilisations.
