# Tiwanaku: State and Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “La cultura Tiahuanaco”?
**Answer:** Tiwanaku builds the Akapana pyramid and the Puerta del Sol and trades camelid textiles, meat and wool across long-distance networks. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 1: La civilización Tiwanakota” end?
**Answer:** The Tiwanaku civilisation is given its own unit as the Altiplano's great state. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
