# Tiwanaku: State and Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Tiwanaku builds the Akapana pyramid and the Puerta del Sol and trades camelid textiles, meat and wool across long-distance networks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 1: La civilización Tiwanakota” is mainly about this: After Tiwanaku the Aymara lordships rule the Altiplano; the Kallawaya healers keep their Machaj Juyay language, and Carangas declines only with the Spanish arrival in the 16th century
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Tiwanaku civilisation is given its own unit as the Altiplano's great state.
