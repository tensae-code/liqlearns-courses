# The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: La Edad Media y el Feudalismo”?
a) Medieval Europe organises itself feudally after Rome  b) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being  c) Enlightenment reason challenges throne and altar and arms the independence generation  d) From 1760 industry transforms production and society, first in England
**Answer: a) Medieval Europe organises itself feudally after Rome** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Medieval Europe organises itself feudally after Rome  b) From 1760 industry transforms production and society, first in England  c) North America's independence shows Spanish America that colonies can break away  d) The French Revolution proclaims rights that cross the Atlantic into the independence wars
**Answer: a) Medieval Europe organises itself feudally after Rome** — That is what this lesson teaches.
