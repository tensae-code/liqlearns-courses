**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “China antigua”?
a) The Shang dynasty (c. 1600–1046 BC), first historically certain dynasty, writing's origins and the Great Wall'…  b) Egypt's kingdoms rise on the Nile to the New Kingdom of Thebes (1580–1100 BC)  c) The Greek world of Crete, Cyprus, Rhodes and Sicily, with Prometheus and the gift of fire  d) Rome's legacy runs from the Julian calendar of Julius Caesar (46 BC) to the aqueducts
