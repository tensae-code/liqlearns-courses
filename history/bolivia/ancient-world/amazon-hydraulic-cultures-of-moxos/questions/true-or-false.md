# Amazon Hydraulic Cultures of Moxos — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     In the Llanos de Moxos a hydraulic culture of raised fields and lomas settles from the 4th c. BC to the 13th c. AD — Moxos, Baures, Casarabes, Takanas, Mosetenes and Tsimanes purify water with the tapore plant.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 2: Culturas hidráulicas amazónicas” is mainly about this: The Guaraní seek Ivy Marei, the Land Without Evil; their tenta communities are led by a Mburuvicha, and the Tenta Guasu by the Mburuvicha guasu, under the law of freedom (yeyora)
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher-compendium unit on the Moxos hydraulic cultures, same arc as the student text.
