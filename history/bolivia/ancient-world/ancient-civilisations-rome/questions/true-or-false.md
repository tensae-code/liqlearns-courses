# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Rome's legacy runs from the Julian calendar of Julius Caesar (46 BC) to the aqueducts.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Roma antigua” is mainly about this: The first Americans arrive over the Bering land bridge, and perhaps also by coastal sea routes, peopling a new continent
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Rome's legacy runs from the Julian calendar of Julius Caesar (46 BC) to the aqueducts.
