# History — Bolivia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Bolivia inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Bolivia's books in hand are the Ministerio de Educación's Ciencias Sociales learning texts — combined History, Geography and Civics books — for the 1st, 2nd, 4th and 6th years of Secundaria (each held PARTIAL, the later portion of the year's text only, as filed), together with the teachers' compendium that binds all six years in one volume (COMPLETE as filed). The placements file's book order is the teaching order — earliest books first. Inside that order, the ladder follows the six-year arc as Bolivia's own books present it: each level below is one year of that arc, re-expressed as a level — the first year's first steps and ancient world, the second year's Abya Yala and colony, the third year's medieval-to-independence arc (held only in the compendium), the fourth year's founding and nineteenth-century republic, the fifth year's crisis century and road to 1952 (held only in the compendium), and the sixth year's world war, 1952 Revolution and Bolivia to today. Levels, NOT grades: the year numbers live only in ABOUT.md and sources.md. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Bolivia's own Ministerio de Educación Ciencias Sociales books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps, Human Beginnings and the First Civilisations** — the historian's craft first (what history is, its sources and method, dating the past by sky and calendar), then humanity's beginnings (hominisation, stone tools to farming) and the first civilisations whole — Mesopotamia, Egypt, China, Greece and Crete, Rome — then the peopling of the Americas and the Altiplano's own first civilisations: Chiripa, Tiwanaku and the Aymara señoríos — 16 lessons.

2. **Abya Yala Whole — Then the Colonial World** — Abya Yala whole before the fall — the Inca Tawantinsuyo, the Amazon hydraulic cultures of Moxos and the Guaraní seeking Ivi Marei — then the European invasion, the encomienda and the distorted mita, tribute and coinage, caste society and colonial government, hacienda against ayllu, the Moxos missions, the Afro-Bolivian people, the Bourbon reforms, and the colonial conflicts closing in the great indigenous rebellions of 1780–1781 and the Black Legend debate — 15 lessons.

3. **The Medieval World, New Ideas and Independence** — the medieval world opened by Islam's rise and feudalism, the Renaissance, the Enlightenment, the first Industrial Revolution, American and French revolutions and Napoleon's wars — then independence itself: its external influences and internal determinants, the juntas of Charcas in 1809, the republiquetas' guerrilla war, the emancipation of the new states, the revisionist debate and the women of the libertarian deed (this level stands on the teachers' compendium only, as filed) — 13 lessons.

4. **Bolivia Founded — Republic, Land, Silver and the Sea** — Bolivia founded — Sucre at Chuquisaca, the early republic of tribute, Church and invasion, Santa Cruz and the Confederation secured at Ingavi, the oligarchic republic and caudillismo, latifundio, Linares and Ibáñez, the War of the Pacific and the lost sea, silver mining's phases, the Federal Revolution of 1899 with Zárate Willka, the rubber boom and Acre, and women's rights from Olympe de Gouges to Bolivian democracy — 10 lessons.

5. **The World in Crisis and Bolivia Toward 1952** — critical history first, then the world's crisis century — the American Civil War, the second Industrial Revolution, Europe's colonial empires, the Mexican Revolution, the First World War, Russia 1917, the interwar years, the 1929 Depression and Latin America's populist governments — landing on Bolivia: the mining-feudal oligarchic republic, the Chaco War, military socialism and the emergent revolutionary nationalism that gathers toward 1952 (this level stands on the teachers' compendium only, as filed) — 13 lessons.

6. **World War, Cold War and Bolivia's Revolution to Today** — the Second World War, the Cold War and its crises (Cuba and the missiles, oil 1973 and Afghanistan), the end of real socialism, decolonisation and the OAU against apartheid — then Bolivia's own hinge: the National Revolution of 1952 and universal vote, Latin America's and Bolivia's dictatorships, neoliberalism and capitalización, the Gas War of 2003, referendums, autonomies and the democratic recovery of 2020, Latin American integration, and the democratic cultural revolution — 15 lessons.

**Totals: 82 lessons** (Level 1: 16 · Level 2: 15 · Level 3: 13 · Level 4: 10 · Level 5: 13 · Level 6: 15 — 123 placements stocked; 53 lessons stand on student books and 29 lessons stand on teacher-guide evidence only, acceptance 0, labelled in every sources.md; 36 lessons carry sub lessons).
