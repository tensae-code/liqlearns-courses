# History — Bolivia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Bolivia's own — built from Bolivia's Ciencias Sociales learning texts for Secundaria (student books, Spanish, each PARTIAL as filed — held portions only) and the teachers' compendium of all six years (COMPLETE as filed). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the six-year Ciencias Sociales arc — first steps and the ancient world, Abya Yala and the colony, the medieval world to independence, the founding republic, the crisis century, and the world war to today's Bolivia. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
