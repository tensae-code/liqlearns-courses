# The Spanish Conquest of the Andes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Invasión europea al Abya Yala”?
**Answer:** The invasion reaches the Inca world; adelantados found cities and then fight each other — Almagro against Pizarro's men — over the spoils. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Invasión europea al Abya Yala” end?
**Answer:** The invasion reaches the Inca world; adelantados found cities and then fight each other — Almagro against Pizarro's men — over the spoils. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
