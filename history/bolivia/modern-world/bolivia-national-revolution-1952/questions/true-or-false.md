# Bolivia: The National Revolution 1952 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     On 9–10 April 1952 an armed mobilisation of soldiers and worker leaders defeats the army; worker control (control obrero) follows in the mines, and the Agrarian Reform remakes the countryside, though spontaneously and insufficiently.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “El Voto Universal y el MNR” is mainly about this: Argentina's Onganía falls in 1970 amid violent repression; sectors radicalise toward the armed struggle Cuba exemplified; Pinochet's Chile imposes neoliberal reforms and privatisation, trained in the…
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The revolution's deepest change is universal suffrage, first exercised in the 1956 elections, while the MNR's two wings pull the National Revolution into internal conflict.
