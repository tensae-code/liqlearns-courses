# Bolivia: The National Revolution 1952 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “La Revolución Nacional de 1952”?
**Answer:** On 9–10 April 1952 an armed mobilisation of soldiers and worker leaders defeats the army; worker control (control obrero) follows in the mines, and the Agrarian Reform remakes the countryside, though spontaneously and insufficiently. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “El Voto Universal y el MNR” end?
**Answer:** The revolution's deepest change is universal suffrage, first exercised in the 1956 elections, while the MNR's two wings pull the National Revolution into internal conflict. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
