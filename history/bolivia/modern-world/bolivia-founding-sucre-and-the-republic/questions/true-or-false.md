# Bolivia's Founding: Sucre and the Republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Sucre arrives crowned by Ayacucho; Peru and Argentina press colonial-era claims on Alto Perú, and at Chuquisaca's Casa de la Libertad the new republic is decided.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 4: La creación de Bolivia” is mainly about this: Colonial landowners, miners and officials cross into the republic; the indigenous tribute and servitude are consolidated instead of abolished, Sucre moves against the Church's economic power, and Peru…
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher-compendium unit on the creation of Bolivia, same arc as the Secundaria 4 student text.
