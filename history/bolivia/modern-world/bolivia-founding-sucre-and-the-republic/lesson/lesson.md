# Bolivia's Founding: Sucre and the Republic

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bolivia's Founding: Sucre and the Republic** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — La llegada del Mariscal de Ayacucho y la fundación

Sucre arrives crowned by Ayacucho; Peru and Argentina press colonial-era claims on Alto Perú, and at Chuquisaca's Casa de la Libertad the new republic is decided. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 4: La creación de Bolivia

Teacher-compendium unit on the creation of Bolivia, same arc as the Secundaria 4 student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
