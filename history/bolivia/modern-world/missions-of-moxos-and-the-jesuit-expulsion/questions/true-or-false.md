# The Missions of Moxos and the Jesuit Expulsion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Jesuit missions in Moxos found ordered towns of four sectors; in 1767 Carlos III expels the Company — some 2,500 priests leave the colonies — a message that the crown, not the Vatican, rules.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 2: Las misiones coloniales españolas” is mainly about this: Africans are brought in chains — how many died will never be known; escapees flee east from bandeirantes, and communities settle in Nor and Sud Yungas, Inquisivi, Caranavi, Murillo and Larecaja, keepi…
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text.
