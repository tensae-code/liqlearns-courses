# Latin American Independence: Ideas and Processes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     External influences — Enlightenment, the US and French revolutions, Napoleon's invasion of Spain — converge on Spanish America.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 3: Determinantes internas para la Guerra Independentista” is mainly about this: In 1809 Chuquisaca and La Paz raise the first juntas of Spanish America — the libertarian revolutions begin on Bolivian soil
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Internal determinants — colonial exploitation, caste grievance, creole ambition — make the independence war necessary.
