# Latin American Independence: Ideas and Processes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Año 3: Influencias externas de la Guerra Independentista”?
**Answer:** External influences — Enlightenment, the US and French revolutions, Napoleon's invasion of Spain — converge on Spanish America. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 3: Determinantes internas para la Guerra Independentista” end?
**Answer:** Internal determinants — colonial exploitation, caste grievance, creole ambition — make the independence war necessary. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
