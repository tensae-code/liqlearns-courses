# Bolivia: Silver Mining Phases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “La minería de la plata”?
**Answer:** Silver mining runs in phases — colonial labour regimes, decline, and the great boom from 1873 that reshapes the republic's economy and oligarchy. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “La minería de la plata” end?
