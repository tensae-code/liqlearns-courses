# Bolivia: Silver Mining Phases — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Silver mining runs in phases — colonial labour regimes, decline, and the great boom from 1873 that reshapes the republic's economy and oligarchy.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “La minería de la plata” is mainly about this: North against south, tin against silver, federalists against unitarians, liberals against conservatives; Pablo Zárate Willka's indigenous uprising joins the 1899 revolution, decided at the Segundo Cru…
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Silver mining runs in phases — colonial labour regimes, decline, and the great boom from 1873 that reshapes the republic's economy and oligarchy.
