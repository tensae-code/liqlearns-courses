# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: La Independencia Norteamericana”?
a) North America's independence shows Spanish America that colonies can break away  b) The French Revolution proclaims rights that cross the Atlantic into the independence wars  c) Napoleon's wars shake Spain's throne — the crack through which Spanish America's juntas rise  d) External influences — Enlightenment, the US and French revolutions, Napoleon's invasion of Spain — converge on…
**Answer: a) North America's independence shows Spanish America that colonies can break away** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) North America's independence shows Spanish America that colonies can break away** — That is what this lesson teaches.
