# Women's Rights: From Gouges to Bolivia

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Women's Rights: From Gouges to Bolivia** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

From the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the Female Citizen against patriarchal colonialism, through important women in politics, education, literature and economics, to women's participation in Bolivian democracy. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
