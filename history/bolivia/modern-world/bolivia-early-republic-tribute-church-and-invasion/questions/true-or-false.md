# Bolivia's Early Republic: Tribute, Church and Invasion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Colonial landowners, miners and officials cross into the republic; the indigenous tribute and servitude are consolidated instead of abolished, Sucre moves against the Church's economic power, and Peru's invasion ends in the Treaty of Piquiza.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “La república temprana” is mainly about this: Santa Cruz lays the legal bases of the state and builds the Peru-Bolivian Confederation; Chile attacks it, and after its fall the Battle of Ingavi (1841) secures Bolivia's independence
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Colonial landowners, miners and officials cross into the republic; the indigenous tribute and servitude are consolidated instead of abolished, Sucre moves against the Church's economic power, and Peru's invasion ends in the Treaty of Piquiza.
