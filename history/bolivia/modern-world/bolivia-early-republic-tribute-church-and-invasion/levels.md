# Bolivia's Early Republic: Tribute, Church and Invasion — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Colonial landowners, miners and officials cross into the republic; the indigenous tribute and servitude are consolidated instead of abolished, Sucre moves against the Church's economic power, and Peru's invasion ends in the Treaty of Piquiza. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
