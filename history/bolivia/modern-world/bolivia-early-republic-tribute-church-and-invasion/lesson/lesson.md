# Bolivia's Early Republic: Tribute, Church and Invasion

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bolivia's Early Republic: Tribute, Church and Invasion** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Colonial landowners, miners and officials cross into the republic; the indigenous tribute and servitude are consolidated instead of abolished, Sucre moves against the Church's economic power, and Peru's invasion ends in the Treaty of Piquiza. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
