# Hands-on — Bolivia's Early Republic: Tribute, Church and Invasion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Bolivia's Early Republic: Tribute, Church and Invasion — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Colonial landowners, miners and officials cross into the republic; the indigenous tribute and servitude are consolidated instead of abolished, Sucre moves against the Church's economic power, and Peru's invasion ends in the Treaty of Piquiza. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
