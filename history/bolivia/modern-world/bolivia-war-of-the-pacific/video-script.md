# Video script — Bolivia: The War of the Pacific


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The 1866 and 1874 treaties fail; Chile occupies Calama where Eduardo Abaroa immolates himself, wins at Tacna's Alto de la Alianza and takes Arica; Bolivia leaves the war landlocked, its rear-guard fights at Canchas Blancas and Tambillo remembered. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bolivia.
