# Bolivia: The War of the Pacific — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “La Guerra del Pacífico”?
**Answer:** The 1866 and 1874 treaties fail; Chile occupies Calama where Eduardo Abaroa immolates himself, wins at Tacna's Alto de la Alianza and takes Arica; Bolivia leaves the war landlocked, its rear-guard fights at Canchas Blancas and Tambillo remembered. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 4: La Guerra del Pacífico” end?
**Answer:** Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
