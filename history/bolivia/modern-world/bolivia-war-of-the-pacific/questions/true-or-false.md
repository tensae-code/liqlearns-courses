# Bolivia: The War of the Pacific — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The 1866 and 1874 treaties fail; Chile occupies Calama where Eduardo Abaroa immolates himself, wins at Tacna's Alto de la Alianza and takes Arica; Bolivia leaves the war landlocked, its rear-guard fights at Canchas Blancas and Tambillo remembered.
