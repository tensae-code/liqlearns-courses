**1.**   What happens in “Las dictaduras militares en América Latina”?
**Answer:** Argentina's Onganía falls in 1970 amid violent repression; sectors radicalise toward the armed struggle Cuba exemplified; Pinochet's Chile imposes neoliberal reforms and privatisation, trained in the School of the Americas' Cold War doctrine.
