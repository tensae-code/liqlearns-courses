# Latin American Military Dictatorships — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Argentina's Onganía falls in 1970 amid violent repression; sectors radicalise toward the armed struggle Cuba exemplified; Pinochet's Chile imposes neoliberal reforms and privatisation, trained in the School of the Americas' Cold War doctrine. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
