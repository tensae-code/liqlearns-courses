# Cold War Crises: Oil 1973 and Afghanistan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Crisis del petróleo y Afganistán”?
**Answer:** The 1973 Arab-Israeli war triggers the oil crisis; in Afghanistan ethnic and religious groups unite to resist the Soviet occupation and overthrow Kabul's communist government. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
