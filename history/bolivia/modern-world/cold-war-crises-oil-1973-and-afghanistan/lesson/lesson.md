# Cold War Crises: Oil 1973 and Afghanistan

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Cold War Crises: Oil 1973 and Afghanistan** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The 1973 Arab-Israeli war triggers the oil crisis; in Afghanistan ethnic and religious groups unite to resist the Soviet occupation and overthrow Kabul's communist government. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
