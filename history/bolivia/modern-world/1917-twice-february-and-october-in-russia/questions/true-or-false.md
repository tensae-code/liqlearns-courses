# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   February and October 1917 carry Russia from tsar to soviets.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Año 5: La Revolución Rusa” is mainly about this: The interwar years — democracies strained, dictatorships rising — lead toward 1939
**Answer: False** — It is about this: February and October 1917 carry Russia from tsar to soviets.
