# Bolivia: Military Socialism

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bolivia: Military Socialism** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

After the Chaco defeat, military socialism (Toro, Busch) turns the army toward nationalism and labour rights. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: After the Chaco defeat, military socialism (Toro, Busch) turns the army toward nationalism and labour rights. Give one real example: After the Chaco defeat, military socialism (Toro, Busch) turns the army toward nationalism and labour rights. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
