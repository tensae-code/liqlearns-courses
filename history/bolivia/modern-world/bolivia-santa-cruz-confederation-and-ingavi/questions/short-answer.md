# Bolivia: Santa Cruz, the Confederation and Ingavi — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Santa Cruz y la Confederación Perú-Boliviana”?
**Answer:** Santa Cruz lays the legal bases of the state and builds the Peru-Bolivian Confederation; Chile attacks it, and after its fall the Battle of Ingavi (1841) secures Bolivia's independence. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 4: La Confederación Perú Boliviana” end?
**Answer:** Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
