# Bolivia: Santa Cruz, the Confederation and Ingavi

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bolivia: Santa Cruz, the Confederation and Ingavi** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Santa Cruz y la Confederación Perú-Boliviana

Santa Cruz lays the legal bases of the state and builds the Peru-Bolivian Confederation; Chile attacks it, and after its fall the Battle of Ingavi (1841) secures Bolivia's independence. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
