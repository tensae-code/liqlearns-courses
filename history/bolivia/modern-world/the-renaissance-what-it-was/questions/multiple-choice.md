# The Renaissance: What It Was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: El Renacimiento”?
a) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being  b) Enlightenment reason challenges throne and altar and arms the independence generation  c) From 1760 industry transforms production and society, first in England  d) North America's independence shows Spanish America that colonies can break away
**Answer: a) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: El Renacimiento”?
a) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being  b) North America's independence shows Spanish America that colonies can break away  c) The French Revolution proclaims rights that cross the Atlantic into the independence wars  d) Napoleon's wars shake Spain's throne — the crack through which Spanish America's juntas rise
**Answer: a) The Renaissance rebirths classical learning and a new image of the human being** — That is what this lesson teaches.
