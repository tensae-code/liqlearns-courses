# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “El mundo bipolar y sus guerrillas”?
**Answer:** Cold War policies breed popular movements and guerrilla groups seeking armed struggle; the UN's "police action" in Korea shows the new world order's machinery. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 6: La Guerra Fría” end?
**Answer:** Teacher-compendium unit, same arc as the student text. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
