# Colonial Taxes: Tribute and Coinage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Tributo indigenal, repartimiento y numismática”?
**Answer:** The indigenous tribute is framed as a right of conquest, the repartimiento of merchandise breaks captive community economies, the alcabala taxes trade, and Potosí's macuquina coins carry the story into Bolivian numismatics. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 2: Otras instituciones económicas de la colonia” end?
**Answer:** Tribute, reducciones, obrajes and the commercial monopoly complete the colony's economic machinery. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
