# Colonial Taxes: Tribute and Coinage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The indigenous tribute is framed as a right of conquest, the repartimiento of merchandise breaks captive community economies, the alcabala taxes trade, and Potosí's macuquina coins carry the story into Bolivian numismatics.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 2: Otras instituciones económicas de la colonia” is mainly about this: A caste pyramid disgregates society — peninsulares, criollos, then the indigenous strata of curacas, comunarios, agregados and yanaconas, with African communities as labour force
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Tribute, reducciones, obrajes and the commercial monopoly complete the colony's economic machinery.
