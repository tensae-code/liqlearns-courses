# Bolivia: Referendums, Autonomies and the 2020 Recovery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Referéndums, autonomías y recuperación democrática”?
**Answer:** Recall referendums follow the 2008 autonomist conflicts; after the crisis of 2019, the elections of 18 October 2020 restore Bolivia's democratic state. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**Answer:** Recall referendums follow the 2008 autonomist conflicts; after the crisis of 2019, the elections of 18 October 2020 restore Bolivia's democratic state.
