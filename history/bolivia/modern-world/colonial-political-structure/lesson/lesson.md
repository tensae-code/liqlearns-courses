# Colonial Political Structure

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Colonial Political Structure** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Estructura política de la colonia

Gobernaciones, viceroyalties (New Spain 1535), captaincies, corregimientos, the Real Audiencia and the cabildo govern the colony, co-opting caciques as intermediaries. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
