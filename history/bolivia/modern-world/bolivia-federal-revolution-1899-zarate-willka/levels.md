# Bolivia: The Federal Revolution 1899 and Zárate Willka — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — North against south, tin against silver, federalists against unitarians, liberals against conservatives; Pablo Zárate Willka's indigenous uprising joins the 1899 revolution, decided at the Segundo Crucero. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
