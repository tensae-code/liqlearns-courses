# Bolivia: The Federal Revolution 1899 and Zárate Willka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “La Revolución Federal”?
**Answer:** North against south, tin against silver, federalists against unitarians, liberals against conservatives; Pablo Zárate Willka's indigenous uprising joins the 1899 revolution, decided at the Segundo Crucero. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “La Revolución Federal” end?
**Answer:** North against south, tin against silver, federalists against unitarians, liberals against conservatives; Pablo Zárate Willka's indigenous uprising joins the 1899 revolution, decided at the Segundo Crucero. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
