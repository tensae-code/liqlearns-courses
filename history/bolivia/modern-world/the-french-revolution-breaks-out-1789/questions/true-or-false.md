# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The French Revolution proclaims rights that cross the Atlantic into the independence wars.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Año 3: La Revolución Francesa” is mainly about this: Napoleon's wars shake Spain's throne — the crack through which Spanish America's juntas rise
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The French Revolution proclaims rights that cross the Atlantic into the independence wars.
