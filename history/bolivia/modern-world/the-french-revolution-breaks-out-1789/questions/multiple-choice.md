# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: La Revolución Francesa”?
a) The French Revolution proclaims rights that cross the Atlantic into the independence wars  b) Napoleon's wars shake Spain's throne — the crack through which Spanish America's juntas rise  c) External influences — Enlightenment, the US and French revolutions, Napoleon's invasion of Spain — converge on…  d) In 1809 Chuquisaca and La Paz raise the first juntas of Spanish America — the libertarian revolutions begin on…
