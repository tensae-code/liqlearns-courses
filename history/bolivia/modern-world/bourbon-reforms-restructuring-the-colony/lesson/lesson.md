## Sub lesson 1 — Reformas borbónicas

The Bourbon reforms restructure the colonial system — new captaincies such as Venezuela, tighter royal control, and growing creole resentment. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
