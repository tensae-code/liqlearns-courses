# Bolivia: The Gas War 2003 and Nationalisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “La Guerra del Gas”?
**Answer:** Tariff protests become street mobilisation; on 17 October 2003 President Gonzalo Sánchez de Lozada resigns and flees — the Gas War's dead lead to the nationalisation of hydrocarbons. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “La Guerra del Gas” end?
**Answer:** Tariff protests become street mobilisation; on 17 October 2003 President Gonzalo Sánchez de Lozada resigns and flees — the Gas War's dead lead to the nationalisation of hydrocarbons. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
