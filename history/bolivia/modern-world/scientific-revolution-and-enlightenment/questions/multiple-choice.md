# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 3: La Ilustración”?
a) Enlightenment reason challenges throne and altar and arms the independence generation  b) From 1760 industry transforms production and society, first in England  c) North America's independence shows Spanish America that colonies can break away  d) The French Revolution proclaims rights that cross the Atlantic into the independence wars
**Answer: a) Enlightenment reason challenges throne and altar and arms the independence generation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Enlightenment reason challenges throne and altar and arms the independence generation** — That is what this lesson teaches.
