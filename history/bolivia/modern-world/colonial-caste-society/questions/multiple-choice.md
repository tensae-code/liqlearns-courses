# Colonial Caste Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the heart of the story in “La estructura social impuesta por la colonia”?
a) A caste pyramid disgregates society — peninsulares, criollos, then the indigenous strata of curacas, comunario…  b) Gobernaciones, viceroyalties (New Spain 1535), captaincies, corregimientos, the Real Audiencia and the cabildo…  c) The hacienda grows beside the surviving ayllu and marka; yanaconas stay on usurped ayllu land, and the latifun…  d) Jesuit missions in Moxos found ordered towns of four sectors; in 1767 Carlos III expels the Company — some 2,5…
**Answer: a) A caste pyramid disgregates society — peninsulares, criollos, then the indigenous strata of curacas, comunario…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
