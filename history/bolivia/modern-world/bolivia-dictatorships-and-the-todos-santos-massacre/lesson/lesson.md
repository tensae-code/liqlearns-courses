# Bolivia: Dictatorships and the Todos Santos Massacre

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bolivia: Dictatorships and the Todos Santos Massacre** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Coletazos de las dictaduras en Bolivia

Bolivia's dictatorships end in spasms — Natusch Busch's coup and the All Saints' Day (Todos Santos) massacre, then Lidia Gueiler's transitional government. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 6: El ciclo de las dictaduras militares

The military-dictatorship cycle (1964–1982) is taught whole. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
