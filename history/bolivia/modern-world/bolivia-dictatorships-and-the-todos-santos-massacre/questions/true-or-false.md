# Bolivia: Dictatorships and the Todos Santos Massacre — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Bolivia's dictatorships end in spasms — Natusch Busch's coup and the All Saints' Day (Todos Santos) massacre, then Lidia Gueiler's transitional government.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 6: El ciclo de las dictaduras militares” is mainly about this: Neoliberal doctrine — individualism, private property — arrives through the UDP's economic packages and de-dollarisation, then the capitalización of ENDE, ENTEL, ENFE, YPFB and LAB
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The military-dictatorship cycle (1964–1982) is taught whole.
