# Bolivia: Dictatorships and the Todos Santos Massacre — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the heart of the story in “Coletazos de las dictaduras en Bolivia”?
a) Bolivia's dictatorships end in spasms — Natusch Busch's coup and the All Saints' Day (Todos Santos) massacre,…  b) Neoliberal doctrine — individualism, private property — arrives through the UDP's economic packages and de-dol…  c) Tariff protests become street mobilisation; on 17 October 2003 President Gonzalo Sánchez de Lozada resigns and…  d) Recall referendums follow the 2008 autonomist conflicts; after the crisis of 2019, the elections of 18 October…
**Answer: a) Bolivia's dictatorships end in spasms — Natusch Busch's coup and the All Saints' Day (Todos Santos) massacre,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     What is the heart of the story in “Año 6: El ciclo de las dictaduras militares”?
a) The military-dictatorship cycle (1964–1982) is taught whole  b) Recall referendums follow the 2008 autonomist conflicts; after the crisis of 2019, the elections of 18 October…  c) Free-trade areas are only the first stage: ALBA-TCP and MERCOSUR carry Latin America's integration wave  d) The democratic and cultural revolution of the Plurinational era closes the six-year course
