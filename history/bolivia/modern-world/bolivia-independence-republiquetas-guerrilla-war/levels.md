# Bolivia's Independence: Republiquetas and Guerrilla War — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — River Plate auxiliary armies come and go, but the republiquetas — Vallegrande under Álvarez de Arenales, Santa Cruz under Ignacio Warnes (Sierra) — hold the guerrilla war for sixteen years. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
