# Bolivia: The Oligarchic Republic and Caudillismo — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The oligarchic republic consolidates on tribute, land and mining wealth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Año 4: La república oligárquica y el caudillismo militar” is mainly about this: The US Civil War is fought over union and slavery
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Military caudillismo rotates power inside the oligarchic republic.
