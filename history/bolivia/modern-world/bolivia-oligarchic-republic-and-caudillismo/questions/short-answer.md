# Bolivia: The Oligarchic Republic and Caudillismo — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Año 4: La consolidación de la república oligárquica”?
**Answer:** The oligarchic republic consolidates on tribute, land and mining wealth. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Año 4: La república oligárquica y el caudillismo militar” end?
**Answer:** Military caudillismo rotates power inside the oligarchic republic. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
