# Bolivia: The Oligarchic Republic and Caudillismo

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bolivia: The Oligarchic Republic and Caudillismo** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Año 4: La consolidación de la república oligárquica

The oligarchic republic consolidates on tribute, land and mining wealth. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Año 4: La república oligárquica y el caudillismo militar

Military caudillismo rotates power inside the oligarchic republic. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
