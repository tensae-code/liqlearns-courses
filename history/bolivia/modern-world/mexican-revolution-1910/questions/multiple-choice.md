Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Año 5: La Revolución Mexicana”?
a) Mexico's revolution opens Latin America's 20th-century social revolutions  b) The Great War's causes and course remake the world map  c) February and October 1917 carry Russia from tsar to soviets  d) The interwar years — democracies strained, dictatorships rising — lead toward 1939
