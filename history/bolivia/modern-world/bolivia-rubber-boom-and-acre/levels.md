# Bolivia: The Rubber Boom and Acre — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The rubber boom enriches the east; rubber barons twice attempt secession — the Acre "revolutions" and an "independent state" — pulling Bolivia toward losing the Acre. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
