# Bahamas: Loyalists and the Plantation Islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did the Loyalists come to The Bahamas? (Sub lesson 1)
a) They stayed loyal to the English king and fled American independence  b) They were hunting pirates  c) They came for tourism  d) They were shipwrecked whalers
**Answer: a) They stayed loyal to the English king and fled American independence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Loyalists plant with enslaved labour? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Cotton  b) Rice  c) Tea  d) Coffee only
**Answer: a) Cotton** — That is what this lesson teaches.
