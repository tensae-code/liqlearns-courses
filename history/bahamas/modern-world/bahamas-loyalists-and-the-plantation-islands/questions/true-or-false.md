# Bahamas: Loyalists and the Plantation Islands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** John Wells started a newspaper in the Loyalist Bahamas. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Loyalists raked salt during the cotton season's busiest weeks only, never in the off-season. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False**
