# Bahamas: Loyalists and the Plantation Islands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two lasting things the Loyalists built or started. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: Christ Church Cathedral; St. Matthew's church; the first schools; the first newspaper (John Wells's paper / the Gazette); organised law and order.

**2.** What did Loyalists do in the off-season from cotton? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** They raked salt.
