# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did Columbus land in The Bahamas? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 1492  b) 1648  c) 1834  d) 1973
**Answer: a) 1492** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened to many Lucayans after the landfall? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Many were killed or enslaved, and soon very few were left  b) They all sailed to Spain  c) They built Nassau at once  d) Nothing changed
**Answer: a) Many were killed or enslaved, and soon very few were left** — That is what this lesson teaches.
