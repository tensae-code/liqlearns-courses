# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the two sides of 1492 as this lesson teaches them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** For Europe it was a landfall — three ships and a flag planted on a new shore; for the Lucayans it turned their world upside down, as many were killed or enslaved and soon very few were left.

**2.** Where does the meeting of the old world and the new begin, in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On a Bahamian shore, with Columbus's 1492 landfall.
