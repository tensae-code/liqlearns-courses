# Lesson plan — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In 1492 Columbus landed in The Bahamas — three ships, a flag planted on a Bahamian shore — and the meeting of the old world and the new turned the Lucayans' world upside down: many were killed or enslaved, and soon very few Lucayans were left.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats 1492 as only a discovery celebration → hold both sides the books teach: a landfall for Europe, and catastrophe for the Lucayans, who were killed, enslaved and soon very few.
- Go back to the one thing: In 1492 Columbus landed in The Bahamas — three ships, a flag planted on a Bahamian shore — and the meeting of the old world and the new turned the Lucayans' world upside down: many were killed or enslaved, and soon very few Lucayans were left. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
