# Bahamas: English Settlers and Enslaved Africans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two routes by which Africans reached The Bahamas. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: direct from Africa; with the Loyalists from America; freed in America and settling; freed by shipwreck.

**2.** What did the freed Africans who chose The Bahamas carry with them? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Junkanoo, asue, cooking ways, and the Anansi and Brer Bookie and Rabbie stories.
