# Bahamas: The Contract — Wartime Workers in America — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What was The Contract, in your own words?
**Answer:** The wartime agreement under which Bahamian men and women left home to work American farms and factories emptied by the war, sending their earnings back to their families.

**2.** Which war emptied the American farms and factories?
**Answer:** The Second World War (1939–1945).
