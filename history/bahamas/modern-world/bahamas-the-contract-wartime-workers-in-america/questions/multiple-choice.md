# Bahamas: The Contract — Wartime Workers in America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did American farms and factories need Bahamian workers?
a) Their own men had left for the troops in the Second World War  b) The farms had burned down  c) No Americans wanted wages  d) The factories were in Nassau
**Answer: a) Their own men had left for the troops in the Second World War** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Contract workers send back to their families?
a) Their earnings  b) Cotton  c) Liquor  d) Pirate gold
**Answer: a) Their earnings** — That is what this lesson teaches.
