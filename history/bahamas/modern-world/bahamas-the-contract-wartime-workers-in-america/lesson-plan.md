# Lesson plan — Bahamas: The Contract — Wartime Workers in America

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bahamas: The Contract — Wartime Workers in America
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Freedom, Booms and War Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the Second World War (1939–1945), American farms and factories emptied as men left for the troops, and under 'The Contract' Bahamian men and women left home to work those farms and factories and send their earnings back to their families.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Freedom, Booms and War Work (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks The Contract was about soldiers → it was about workers: Bahamians filled the farm and factory jobs America's own men had left for the troops, and sent earnings home.
- Go back to the one thing: In the Second World War (1939–1945), American farms and factories emptied as men left for the troops, and under 'The Contract' Bahamian men and women left home to work those farms and factories and send their earnings back to their families. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
