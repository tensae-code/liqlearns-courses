# Bahamas: Independence 1973 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On what date did The Bahamas become independent? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 10 July 1973  b) 10 January 1967  c) 27 April 1965  d) 26 May 1966
**Answer: a) 10 July 1973** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who was the first Prime Minister of the independent Bahamas? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Sir Lynden Pindling  b) Hubert Ingraham  c) Perry Christie  d) Philip Davis
**Answer: a) Sir Lynden Pindling** — That is what this lesson teaches.
