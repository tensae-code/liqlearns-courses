# Bahamas: Independence 1973 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Read the flag and coat of arms: what do they stand for? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The black, gold and aquamarine flag stands for a united people's strength and their sun and sea; the coat of arms puts Columbus's Santa Maria on the shield under the motto 'Forward, Upward, Onward, Together'.

**2.** What did the 1972 constitution do? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Agreed by the British and Bahamian governments, it became the basis of all Bahamian law, sets out who is a Bahamian, and protects citizens' rights and freedoms.
