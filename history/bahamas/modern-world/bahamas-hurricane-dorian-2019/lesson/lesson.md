# Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019

## Do it first

Before any explanation, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already know or can guess about this — Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019. One sentence is enough. This lesson will give you the full story: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

## The story

On 1 September 2019, the islands met the worst storm in their recorded story. Hurricane Dorian — the most devastating hurricane ever to hit The Bahamas — tore across Abaco and Grand Bahama, destroying homes and businesses, and many lives were lost. The same sea and sky that shape island life can, in a single day, remake it. Dorian is the modern Bahamas' date of grief — and of rebuilding.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: hold this lesson as a story with a turn — ask: who is here, where are we, and what changed?

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: Hurricane Dorian, 1 September 2019, Abaco, Grand Bahama, devastation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
