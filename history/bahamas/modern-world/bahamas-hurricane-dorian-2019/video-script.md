# Video script — Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
On 1 September 2019 Hurricane Dorian, the most devastating hurricane to hit The Bahamas, destroyed homes and businesses across Abaco and Grand Bahama, and many lives were lost.

Look at the map. Here are the islands of The Bahamas, scattered in the Atlantic where the Caribbean begins — Nassau on New Providence, Eleuthera, Exuma, Abaco, Grand Bahama and the Family Islands around them.

On 1 September 2019, the islands met the worst storm in their recorded story. Hurricane Dorian — the most devastating hurricane ever to hit The Bahamas — tore across Abaco and Grand Bahama, destroying homes and businesses, and many lives were lost. The same sea and sky that shape island life can, in a single day, remake it. Dorian is the modern Bahamas' date of grief — and of rebuilding.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
