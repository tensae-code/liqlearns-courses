# Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did Hurricane Dorian destroy?
**Answer:** Homes and businesses across Abaco and Grand Bahama — and many lives were lost.

**2.** Why is Dorian taught inside a history course?
**Answer:** Because it is a turning point in the nation's own story — the most devastating hurricane to hit The Bahamas, a date of grief and rebuilding in the modern Bahamas.
