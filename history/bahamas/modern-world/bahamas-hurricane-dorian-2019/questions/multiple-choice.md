# Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On what date did Hurricane Dorian hit?
a) 1 September 2019  b) 10 July 1973  c) 27 April 1965  d) 1 August 1790
**Answer: a) 1 September 2019** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which islands did Dorian devastate?
a) Abaco and Grand Bahama  b) Eleuthera and Andros only  c) San Salvador and Cuba  d) Exuma and Inagua only
**Answer: a) Abaco and Grand Bahama** — That is what this lesson teaches.
