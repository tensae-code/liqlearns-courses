# Lesson plan — Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Majority Rule, Independence and the Nation Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On 1 September 2019 Hurricane Dorian, the most devastating hurricane to hit The Bahamas, destroyed homes and businesses across Abaco and Grand Bahama, and many lives were lost.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Majority Rule, Independence and the Nation Today (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats Dorian as an ordinary storm → the book's own words fix its scale: the most devastating hurricane to hit The Bahamas, with homes, businesses and many lives lost.
- Go back to the one thing: On 1 September 2019 Hurricane Dorian, the most devastating hurricane to hit The Bahamas, destroyed homes and businesses across Abaco and Grand Bahama, and many lives were lost. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
