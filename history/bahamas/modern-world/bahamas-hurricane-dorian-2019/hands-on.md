# Hands-on — Bahamas: Hurricane Dorian, 2019

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud, in your own words: On 1 September 2019 Hurricane Dorian, the most devastating hurricane to hit The Bahamas, destroyed homes and businesses across Abaco and Grand Bahama, and many lives were lost. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the story's turning point, and say each part aloud as you place it. Use the lesson's own key words: Hurricane Dorian, 1 September 2019, Abaco, Grand Bahama, devastation.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see a place, a name, a day or a thing this lesson names, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
