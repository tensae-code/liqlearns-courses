# Bahamas: Emancipation and Life After Slavery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which settlements did freed people build, in this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Adelaide, Gambier and Fox Hill  b) Eleuthera, Andros and Exuma only  c) Freeport, Nassau and London  d) San Salvador, Cuba and Haiti
**Answer: a) Adelaide, Gambier and Fox Hill** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why was Government High School founded? (Sub lesson 2)
a) To give Black Bahamians schooling beyond the elementary level  b) To train pirates  c) To teach only tourists  d) To replace all churches
**Answer: a) To give Black Bahamians schooling beyond the elementary level** — That is what this lesson teaches.
