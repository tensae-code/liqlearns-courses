# Bahamas: Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the two grants in this lesson.
**Answer:** In 1629 Charles I granted The Bahamas and the Carolinas to Sir Robert Heath; in 1670 Charles II handed the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina, whose absentee rule ended in 1718 with Woodes Rogers.

**2.** What surprising gift did the Adventurers make in this period?
**Answer:** They donated money to help build Harvard College.
