# Bahamas: Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who received The Bahamas and the Carolinas in the 1629 grant?
a) Sir Robert Heath  b) Woodes Rogers  c) Blackbeard  d) Captain Sayle
**Answer: a) Sir Robert Heath** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why did the Proprietors' rule fail?
a) They wanted profit but showed little interest, and their rents were refused  b) They built too many schools  c) The islands sank  d) They freed all the slaves
**Answer: a) They wanted profit but showed little interest, and their rents were refused** — That is what this lesson teaches.
