# Lesson plan — Bahamas: Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bahamas: Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In 1629 Charles I granted The Bahamas and the Carolinas to Sir Robert Heath, and in 1670 Charles II handed the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina — owners who wanted profit, showed little interest, and were refused their rents, until their rule ended in 1718 with Woodes Rogers.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (recommended, never forced).

- Go back to the one thing: In 1629 Charles I granted The Bahamas and the Carolinas to Sir Robert Heath, and in 1670 Charles II handed the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina — owners who wanted profit, showed little interest, and were refused their rents, until their rule ended in 1718 with Woodes Rogers.
