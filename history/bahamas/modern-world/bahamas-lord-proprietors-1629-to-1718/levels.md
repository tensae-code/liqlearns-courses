# Bahamas: Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: In 1629 Charles I granted The Bahamas and the Carolinas to Sir Robert Heath, and in 1670 Charles II handed the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina — owners who wanted profit, showed little interest, and were refused their rents, until their rule ended in 1718 with Woodes Rogers. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
