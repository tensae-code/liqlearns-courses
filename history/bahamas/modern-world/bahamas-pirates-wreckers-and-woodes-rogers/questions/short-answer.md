# Bahamas: Pirates, Wreckers and Woodes Rogers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three pirates named in this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any three of: Blackbeard, Anne Bonny, Mary Read, Calico Jack, Stede Bonnet, Henry Morgan, Charles Vane.

**2.** What two firsts belong to Woodes Rogers' time in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** He was the first Royal Governor who cleared out the pirates, and under him the islands organised their first parliament in 1729, with John Colebrook as first Speaker and 24 members.
