# Bahamas: Pirates, Wreckers and Woodes Rogers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was a boucan? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The slow fire hunters cured meat over  b) A pirate ship  c) A kind of coin  d) A Bahamian fort
**Answer: a) The slow fire hunters cured meat over** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Woodes Rogers do to the pirates? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Pardoned some and hanged the rest  b) Joined them  c) Paid them to stay  d) Sent them to school
**Answer: a) Pardoned some and hanged the rest** — That is what this lesson teaches.
