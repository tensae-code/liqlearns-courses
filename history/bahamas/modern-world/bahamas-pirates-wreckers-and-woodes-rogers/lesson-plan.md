# Lesson plan — Bahamas: Pirates, Wreckers and Woodes Rogers

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bahamas: Pirates, Wreckers and Woodes Rogers
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pirates — Blackbeard, Anne Bonny, Mary Read, Calico Jack, Stede Bonnet, Henry Morgan and Charles Vane — infested The Bahamas, and wreckers lured ships onto reefs to strip them, until Woodes Rogers, first Royal Governor, pardoned some pirates and hanged the rest, cleared the islands out, and under him the first parliament met in 1729.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Merges pirates and wreckers into one trade → pirates took ships at sea; wreckers waited on shore and lured ships onto reefs, sometimes with false lights, then stripped the wrecks.
- Go back to the one thing: Pirates — Blackbeard, Anne Bonny, Mary Read, Calico Jack, Stede Bonnet, Henry Morgan and Charles Vane — infested The Bahamas, and wreckers lured ships onto reefs to strip them, until Woodes Rogers, first Royal Governor, pardoned some pirates and hanged the rest, cleared the islands out, and under him the first parliament met in 1729. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
