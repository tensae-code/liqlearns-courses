# Bahamas: Tourism and Sir Stafford Sands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three things Sands pushed to build year-round tourism. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any three of: air conditioning, advertising, tax incentives, better air travel, legalised casinos (with the Development Board and Freeport).

**2.** What did bootlegging customs money pay for, and for whom? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** It dredged the harbour and brought water and electricity — for tourists, not ordinary Bahamians, as the lesson notes.
