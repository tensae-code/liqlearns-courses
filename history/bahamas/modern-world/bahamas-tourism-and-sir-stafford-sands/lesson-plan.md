# Lesson plan — Bahamas: Tourism and Sir Stafford Sands

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bahamas: Tourism and Sir Stafford Sands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Majority Rule, Independence and the Nation Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tourism grew from invalids seeking health and rich winter visitors at the Royal Victoria Hotel (1861) and Colonial Hotel (1900) into a year-round industry in the 1950s under Sir Stafford Sands, 'Father of Tourism', who headed the Development Board, developed Freeport with Wallace Groves, and pushed air conditioning, advertising, tax incentives, better air travel and legalised casinos, until research and training made tourism the number one industry by 1969–1979.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Majority Rule, Independence and the Nation Today (recommended, never forced).
