# The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who ordered the blockade of the southern ports?
a) President Abraham Lincoln  b) Woodes Rogers  c) Sir Lynden Pindling  d) Charles I
**Answer: a) President Abraham Lincoln** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the southern states need to import by sea?
a) Guns they could not make  b) Hammocks  c) Salt only  d) School books
**Answer: a) Guns they could not make** — That is what this lesson teaches.
