# Lesson plan — The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Freedom, Booms and War Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** President Abraham Lincoln blockaded the southern ports in America's Civil War (1861–1865): the plantation states had to export cotton to Europe and import guns they could not make, or lose the war against the North.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Freedom, Booms and War Work (recommended, never forced).
