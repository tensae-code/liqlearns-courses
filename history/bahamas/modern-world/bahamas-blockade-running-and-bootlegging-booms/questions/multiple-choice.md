# Bahamas: Blockade Running and Bootlegging Booms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was Nassau during the American Civil War? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The Confederacy's back door to Europe  b) The Union's capital  c) A pirate republic  d) A cotton plantation
**Answer: a) The Confederacy's back door to Europe** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did bootleggers smuggle during Prohibition? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Liquor into the United States  b) Cotton to Eleuthera  c) Books to London  d) Salt to Cuba
**Answer: a) Liquor into the United States** — That is what this lesson teaches.
