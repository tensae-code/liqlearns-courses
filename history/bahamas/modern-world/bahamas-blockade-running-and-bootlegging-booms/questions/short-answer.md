# Bahamas: Blockade Running and Bootlegging Booms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did blockade running change Nassau? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Businesses flourished and infrastructure improved on blockade money — and crime grew with the population.

**2.** Who benefited least from the bootlegging boom, and what did the money improve? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Over-the-Hill communities benefited far less; the money improved rich homes and tourist infrastructure.
