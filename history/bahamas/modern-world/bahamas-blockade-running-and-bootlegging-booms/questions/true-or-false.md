# Bahamas: Blockade Running and Bootlegging Booms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bootlegging ran from 1920 to 1933. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Over-the-Hill communities benefited most of all from bootlegging money. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False**
