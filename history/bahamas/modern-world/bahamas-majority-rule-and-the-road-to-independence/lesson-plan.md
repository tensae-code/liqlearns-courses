# Lesson plan — Bahamas: Majority Rule and the Road to Independence

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bahamas: Majority Rule and the Road to Independence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Majority Rule, Independence and the Nation Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The road ran through protest to power: the Burma Road Riot of June 1942 won a wage increase in five days; the PLP was founded in 1953, the first party of the Black majority; women won the vote in 1962; on Black Tuesday, 27 April 1965, Pindling threw the Speaker's mace out of the House window; in 1967 the PLP won and Pindling became the first Black Premier, and in 1973 independence made him Prime Minister.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Majority Rule, Independence and the Nation Today (recommended, never forced).
