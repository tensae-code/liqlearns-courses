# Bahamas: Majority Rule and the Road to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the leaders of the Women's Suffrage Movement and the first woman to register. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mary Ingraham and Georgianna Symonette led the movement to victory in 1962; Ruby Ann Cooper Darling was the first to register.

**2.** Walk the dated steps from 1942 to 1973. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Burma Road Riot 1942; PLP founded 1953; women's suffrage 1962; Black Tuesday 1965; PLP victory and Majority Rule 1967, Pindling first Black Premier; independence 1973, Pindling Prime Minister.
