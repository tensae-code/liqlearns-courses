# Lesson plan — Bahamas: Churches Founded by the Freed

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bahamas: Churches Founded by the Freed
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Freedom, Booms and War Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Prince William, a freed slave, founded the Baptist church in The Bahamas, and freed Black people broke ground for Bethel Baptist on 1 August 1790; St Matthew's (1800–1802) and St Andrew's Kirk (1798–1810) rose nearby, and centuries later Rastafarianism grew after a Bob Marley concert in the late 1970s and Islam arrived in the early 1970s.
