# Bahamas: Churches Founded by the Freed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who founded the Baptist church in The Bahamas?
a) Prince William, a freed slave  b) Woodes Rogers  c) John Wells  d) Sir Stafford Sands
**Answer: a) Prince William, a freed slave** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When did freed Black people break ground for Bethel Baptist?
a) 1 August 1790  b) 10 July 1973  c) 27 April 1965  d) 1 September 2019
**Answer: a) 1 August 1790** — That is what this lesson teaches.
