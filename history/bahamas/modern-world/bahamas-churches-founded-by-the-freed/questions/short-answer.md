# Bahamas: Churches Founded by the Freed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell how the Baptist church began in The Bahamas.
**Answer:** Prince William, a freed slave, founded it, and freed Black people broke ground for Bethel Baptist on 1 August 1790.

**2.** What two later faith arrivals does this lesson name?
**Answer:** Rastafarianism, which grew after a Bob Marley concert in the late 1970s, and Islam, which arrived in the early 1970s.
