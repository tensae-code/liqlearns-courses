# History — Bahamas course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is The Bahamas' own —
built from The Bahamas' Social Science e-books for Grades 1 and 3
and its Primary Social Studies Digital Textbooks for Grades 4, 5
and 6 (student books, English). Inside the course the organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside the
levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step.
The grade each book comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': heritage and the first peoples
first, then arrival, slavery and the pirate islands, then freedom,
the booms and war work, and last the road through Majority Rule to
independence and the nation today. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the
"start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
