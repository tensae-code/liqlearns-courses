# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Describe the three legs of the triangle in order.
**Answer:** European goods to Africa; kidnapped and purchased Africans across the Middle Passage to the Americas; then raw materials back to Europe.

**2.** Why is the Middle Passage called perilous in this lesson?
**Answer:** Because Africans were carried across the ocean as captives in a voyage of great danger and suffering — the human leg of the trade.
