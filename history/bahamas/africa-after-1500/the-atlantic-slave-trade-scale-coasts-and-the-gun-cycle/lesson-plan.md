# Lesson plan — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From the 16th to the 19th centuries, ships sailed a triangle — European goods to Africa, kidnapped and purchased Africans carried across the perilous Middle Passage, and raw materials back to Europe.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Arrival, Slavery and the Pirate Islands (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Misses the human leg of the triangle → the middle leg carried people: kidnapped and purchased Africans, carried across the Middle Passage — that is the trade's centre, not a detail.
- Go back to the one thing: From the 16th to the 19th centuries, ships sailed a triangle — European goods to Africa, kidnapped and purchased Africans carried across the perilous Middle Passage, and raw materials back to Europe. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
