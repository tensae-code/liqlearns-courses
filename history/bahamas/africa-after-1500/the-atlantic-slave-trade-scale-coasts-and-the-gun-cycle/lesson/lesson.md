# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

## Do it first

Before any explanation, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already know or can guess about this — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle. One sentence is enough. This lesson will give you the full story: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

## The story

For three centuries, from the 1500s to the 1800s, Atlantic ships sailed the same grim triangle. First leg: European goods carried to Africa. Second leg — the Middle Passage: Africans, kidnapped and purchased, carried across the ocean in peril, to be enslaved in the Americas. Third leg: raw materials from the plantations carried back to Europe, and the triangle began again. The Bahamas' own slavery story, told in the next lessons, is one shore of this vast trade.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: hold this lesson as a story with a turn — ask: who is here, where are we, and what changed?
