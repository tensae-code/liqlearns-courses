# Lesson plan — Find History in Your Own Locality

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Find History in Your Own Locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A Bahamian surname is historical evidence: names such as Rolle on Exuma record which families settled which islands, and later Greek, Chinese and Haitian arrivals each left their names — and their stories — in the islands.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and First Peoples (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a surname is just a label → trace one: Rolle is common on Exuma because a Loyalist of that name settled there with enslaved people in the late 1700s, and his descendants carry the name today.
- Go back to the one thing: A Bahamian surname is historical evidence: names such as Rolle on Exuma record which families settled which islands, and later Greek, Chinese and Haitian arrivals each left their names — and their stories — in the islands. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
