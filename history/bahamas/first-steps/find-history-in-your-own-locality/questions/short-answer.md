# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How can a surname work as historical evidence? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** It can show where a family came from and which island it settled — for example, Rolle on Exuma records the Loyalist settler of that name and his descendants.

**2.** Name two later peoples who migrated to The Bahamas and why they came. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greeks came for the sponge industry; Chinese and Haitians fled politics and sought a better life (any two, with their reasons).
