# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why is the surname Rolle common on Exuma? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A Loyalist named Rolle settled there in the late 18th century  b) It is the island's oldest Lucayan word  c) A pirate chose it  d) It comes from a hotel chain
**Answer: a) A Loyalist named Rolle settled there in the late 18th century** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why did Greeks come to The Bahamas, in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
a) For the sponge industry  b) To run the blockade  c) To build pyramids  d) For the gold mines
**Answer: a) For the sponge industry** — That is what this lesson teaches.
