# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things Bahamians hand down from generation to generation, from this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: the tales of Brer Bookie, Brer Rabbie and Anansi; ring plays; Junkanoo; bush-medicine knowledge such as cerasee and sage; sisal and straw crafts; island festivals, regattas and homecomings.

**2.** Why does this lesson say history is a main component of culture? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Because history tells a people where they have come from and where they are going — it is the memory inside the way of life.
