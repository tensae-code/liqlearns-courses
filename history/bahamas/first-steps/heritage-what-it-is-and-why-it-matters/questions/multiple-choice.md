# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is culture, as the Bahamian books teach it? (Sub lesson 1)
a) What is passed from one generation to the next  b) Only what is written in law  c) Only old buildings  d) A kind of money
**Answer: a) What is passed from one generation to the next** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When is Junkanoo celebrated? (Sub lesson 2)
a) On Boxing Day and New Year's Day  b) Only at Easter  c) On Independence Day only  d) Every Sunday
**Answer: a) On Boxing Day and New Year's Day** — That is what this lesson teaches.
