# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before any explanation, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already know or can guess about this — Dating the Past. One sentence is enough. This lesson will give you the full story: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

## The story

History is the telling of what happened in the past. The Bahamian books teach the tool for holding it: a timeline, an outline that sets dates and their stories in order. The Bahamian line begins with the Lucayans in the 1400s and reaches Columbus's landfall in 1492 at San Salvador, the island the Lucayans called Guanahani. Once events sit on a line, we can ask the historian's first questions — what came first, what followed, and what changed between them.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: hold this lesson as a story with a turn — ask: who is here, where are we, and what changed?
