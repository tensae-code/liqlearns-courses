# Lesson plan — Dating the Past

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Dating the Past
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History tells what happened in the past, and a timeline — an outline of dates and stories in order — lets us place The Bahamas' story from the Lucayans and Columbus's 1492 landfall at San Salvador (Guanahani) onward.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and First Peoples (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a timeline is just a list of dates → show that each date carries a story: the line orders the stories so we can see what came before and after.
- Go back to the one thing: History tells what happened in the past, and a timeline — an outline of dates and stories in order — lets us place The Bahamas' story from the Lucayans and Columbus's 1492 landfall at San Salvador (Guanahani) onward. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
