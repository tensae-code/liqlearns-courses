# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does a timeline help a learner of history do?
**Answer:** It sets dates and their stories in order, so we can see what came first, what followed, and how the story of a place unfolds over time.

**2.** What name did the Lucayans give the island where Columbus landed in 1492?
**Answer:** Guanahani — the island Columbus's arrival made known as San Salvador.
