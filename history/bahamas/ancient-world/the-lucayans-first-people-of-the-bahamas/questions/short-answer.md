# The Lucayans: First People of The Bahamas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Describe Lucayan life in your own words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A peaceful Arawak-speaking people from South America who settled by the sea, fished and farmed cassava, sweet potato and corn, lived in branch-and-grass huts and slept in hammocks.

**2.** Why do the Lucayans stand first on the Bahamian timeline? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Because they were the first people of the islands — every later group, from Columbus onward, arrived in a land the Lucayans already called home.
