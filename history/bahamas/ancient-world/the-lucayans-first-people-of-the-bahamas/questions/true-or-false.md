# The Lucayans: First People of The Bahamas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Lucayans farmed cassava, sweet potato and corn. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Lucayans were a warlike people who lived in stone castles. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False**
