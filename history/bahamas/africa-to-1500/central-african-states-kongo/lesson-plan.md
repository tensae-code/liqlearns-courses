# Lesson plan — Central African States: Kongo

**Course:** History — Bahamas · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Central African States: Kongo
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Today's Bahamian is said to descend from the Kongo Kingdom south of the Kongo River, and the two cultures still share one understanding — people relate to one another, children respect elders, and strangers receive unwavering hospitality.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and First Peoples (recommended, never forced).
