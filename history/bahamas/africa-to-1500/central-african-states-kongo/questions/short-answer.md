# Central African States: Kongo — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What three shared understandings link Kongo and Bahamian culture in this lesson?
**Answer:** People relate to one another, children respect elders, and strangers get unwavering hospitality.

**2.** Why does a Bahamian history course include the Kongo Kingdom?
**Answer:** Because Bahamians are taught as descending from it — it is part of where the people, and their values, come from.
