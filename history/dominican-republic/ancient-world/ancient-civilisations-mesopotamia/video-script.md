# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Between two rivers the first cities, kings and laws appear.

History's first cities rose between two rivers. In Mesopotamia — the land between the Tigris and the Euphrates — irrigation farming fed walled cities, kings ruled them, laws were carved in stone for all to see, and scribes pressed the first writing into clay tablets. The Grade 10 book opens the oriental civilisations here because so much begins here: the city, the code of law, and the written word itself.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
