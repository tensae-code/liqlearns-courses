# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Between two rivers the first cities, kings and laws appear. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

History's first cities rose between two rivers. In Mesopotamia — the land between the Tigris and the Euphrates — irrigation farming fed walled cities, kings ruled them, laws were carved in stone for all to see, and scribes pressed the first writing into clay tablets. The Grade 10 book opens the oriental civilisations here because so much begins here: the city, the code of law, and the written word itself.
