# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Between two rivers the first cities, kings and laws appear.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Between two rivers the first cities, kings and laws appear.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, mud-brick cities rise, a king proclaims laws in stone, and scribes press writing into clay.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, mud-brick cities rise, a king proclaims laws in stone, and scribes press writing into clay.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
