# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Before Writing and the First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greece's city-states gave the world democracy, theatre and philosophy — small poleis around the Aegean that invented new ways of living together.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Before Writing and the First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Greece's city-states gave the world democracy, theatre and philosophy — small poleis around the Aegean that invented new ways of living together.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: On rocky hills and islands, independent city-states argue in assemblies, stage plays and ask philosophy's first questions.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
