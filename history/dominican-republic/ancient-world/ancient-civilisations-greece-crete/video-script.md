# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Greece's city-states gave the world democracy, theatre and philosophy — small poleis around the Aegean that invented new ways of living together.

Around the Aegean Sea, on islands like Crete and the rocky Greek mainland, a new kind of community arose: the city-state. The Grade 6 book gives Greece its famous gifts — democracy, theatre and philosophy — born in small, quarrelsome, brilliant poleis that argued, voted, staged plays and asked the world's first philosophical questions in public.
The Grade 10 book tells the rising itself: Greek poleis grow around the Aegean and invent new ways of living together — citizens ruling themselves in assembly instead of kneeling to a king, colonies carrying the pattern to every shore of their sea. What rose around that sea would outlast every empire in this course.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
