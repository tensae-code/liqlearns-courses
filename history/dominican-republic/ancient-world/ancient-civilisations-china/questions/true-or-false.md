# Ancient Civilisations: China — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: China's early dynasties bound a vast land into one enduring civilisation.
