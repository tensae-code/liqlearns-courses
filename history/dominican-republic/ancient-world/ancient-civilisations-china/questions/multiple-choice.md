# Ancient Civilisations: China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) China's early dynasties bound a vast land into one enduring civilisation.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) China's early dynasties bound a vast land into one enduring civilisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Along the Yellow River, dynasty follows dynasty — writing, bronze and a single script binding a vast land into one civilisation.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Along the Yellow River, dynasty follows dynasty — writing, bronze and a single script binding a vast land into one civilisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
