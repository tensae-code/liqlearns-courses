# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: China

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
China's early dynasties bound a vast land into one enduring civilisation.

China's civilisation is the ancient world's great endurance. Early dynasties along the Yellow River bound a vast land into one civilisation — a shared script that men of different tongues could all read, bronze and silk crafts, and the ideal of a unified empire under a heaven-given mandate. Dynasties would rise and fall for two thousand years; the civilisation the Grade 10 book introduces outlasted every one of them.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
