# Lesson plan — The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After Rome fell, a rural, fragmented Europe organised itself around land and loyalty: castles, lords and peasants bound in the feudal ladder.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After Rome fell, a rural, fragmented Europe organised itself around land and loyalty: castles, lords and peasants bound in the feudal ladder.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A lord in his castle, knights who owe him service, and peasants bound to his land — each rung owing the one above.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
