# The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** After Rome fell, a rural, fragmented Europe organised itself around land and loyalty: castles, lords and peasants bound in the feudal ladder.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A lord in his castle, knights who owe him service, and peasants bound to his land — each rung owing the one above.
