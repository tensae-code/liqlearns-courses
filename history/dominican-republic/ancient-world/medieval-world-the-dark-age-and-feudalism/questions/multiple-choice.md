# The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) After Rome fell, a rural, fragmented Europe organised itself around land and loyalty: castles, lords and peasants bound in the feudal ladder.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) After Rome fell, a rural, fragmented Europe organised itself around land and loyalty: castles, lords and peasants bound in the feudal ladder.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A lord in his castle, knights who owe him service, and peasants bound to his land — each rung owing the one above.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A lord in his castle, knights who owe him service, and peasants bound to his land — each rung owing the one above.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
