## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The republic gave way to emperors, and the empire strained and divided.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: An emperor's statue towers over a forum where republican debate once rang — and far away, the over-stretched empire splits into East and West.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
