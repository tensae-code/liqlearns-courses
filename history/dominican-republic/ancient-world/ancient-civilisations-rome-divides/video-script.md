# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The republic gave way to emperors, and the empire strained and divided.

Rome's second act is strain. The republic gave way to emperors — Augustus first, then a line of rulers good and disastrous — and the empire, grown too vast to hold from one throne, strained and divided into West and East. The Grade 10 book teaches the imperial phase as the necessary sequel to conquest: what an empire does to the republic that built it, and how even Rome could be too big to govern whole.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
