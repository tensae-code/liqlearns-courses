# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The republic gave way to emperors, and the empire strained and divided.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The republic gave way to emperors, and the empire strained and divided.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) An emperor's statue towers over a forum where republican debate once rang — and far away, the over-stretched empire splits into East and West.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) An emperor's statue towers over a forum where republican debate once rang — and far away, the over-stretched empire splits into East and West.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
