# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Rome's legions and laws built an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — conquering Italy, then the Mediterranean world.

Rome began as one city among many in Italy. The Grade 6 book tells what it became: legions and laws building an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — its roads, languages, laws and cities outliving the empire itself, its name stamped on the calendar and the law courts of countries Rome never saw.
The Grade 10 book tells the conquest itself: Rome subdues Italy town by town and then, war by war — against Carthage, against the Greek kingdoms — conquers the whole Mediterranean world, until the sea at Europe's heart is a Roman lake and one city's citizenship becomes the world's.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
