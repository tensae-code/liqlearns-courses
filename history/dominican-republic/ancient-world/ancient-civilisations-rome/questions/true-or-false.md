# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Rome's legions and laws built an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — conquering Italy, then the Mediterranean world.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Rome's legions and laws built an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — conquering Italy, then the Mediterranean world.
