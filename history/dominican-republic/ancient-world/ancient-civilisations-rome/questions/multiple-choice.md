# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Rome's legions and laws built an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — conquering Italy, then the Mediterranean world.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Rome's legions and laws built an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — conquering Italy, then the Mediterranean world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A small city on the Tiber sends its legions out road by road until the whole Mediterranean is called Rome's sea.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A small city on the Tiber sends its legions out road by road until the whole Mediterranean is called Rome's sea.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
