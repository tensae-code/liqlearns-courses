# Ancient Civilisations: Rome

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Rome's legions and laws built an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — conquering Italy, then the Mediterranean world. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 3 La antigua Roma

Rome began as one city among many in Italy. The Grade 6 book tells what it became: legions and laws building an empire that shapes Europe for millennia — its roads, languages, laws and cities outliving the empire itself, its name stamped on the calendar and the law courts of countries Rome never saw.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 7 Surgimiento del Imperio romano

The Grade 10 book tells the conquest itself: Rome subdues Italy town by town and then, war by war — against Carthage, against the Greek kingdoms — conquers the whole Mediterranean world, until the sea at Europe's heart is a Roman lake and one city's citizenship becomes the world's.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example: A small city on the Tiber sends its legions out road by road until the whole Mediterranean is called Rome's sea. and name the lesson's key words: Rome, Legions, Roman law. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
