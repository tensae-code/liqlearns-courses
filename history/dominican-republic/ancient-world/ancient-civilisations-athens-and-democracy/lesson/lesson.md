# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Greek language, art and the Athenian experiment spread across the Mediterranean. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Greece did not stay in Greece. The Grade 10 book follows the expansion of Greek culture: colonies, trade and Alexander's conquests spread the Greek language and art across the Mediterranean and beyond — and with them, the Athenian experiment, citizens ruling themselves in assembly. Democracy in this telling is not a museum piece; it is an export that changed every shore it reached, and its descendants include every republic in this course, the Dominican one among them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Greek language, art and the Athenian experiment spread across the Mediterranean. Give one real example: In Athens' assembly, citizens raise hands to decide their city's fate — and Greek ships carry that language, art and experiment to every Mediterranean shore. and name the lesson's key words: Athens, Democracy, Greek expansion. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
