# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Greek language, art and the Athenian experiment spread across the Mediterranean.

Greece did not stay in Greece. The Grade 10 book follows the expansion of Greek culture: colonies, trade and Alexander's conquests spread the Greek language and art across the Mediterranean and beyond — and with them, the Athenian experiment, citizens ruling themselves in assembly. Democracy in this telling is not a museum piece; it is an export that changed every shore it reached, and its descendants include every republic in this course, the Dominican one among them.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
