## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A small band crosses a cold land bridge into a continent no human has ever lived in — and their descendants reach its far south.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
