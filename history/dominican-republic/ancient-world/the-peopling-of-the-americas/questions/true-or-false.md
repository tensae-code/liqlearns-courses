# The Peopling of the Americas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas.
