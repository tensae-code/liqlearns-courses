# The Peopling of the Americas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A small band crosses a cold land bridge into a continent no human has ever lived in — and their descendants reach its far south.
