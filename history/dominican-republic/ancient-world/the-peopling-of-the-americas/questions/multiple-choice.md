# The Peopling of the Americas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A small band crosses a cold land bridge into a continent no human has ever lived in — and their descendants reach its far south.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A small band crosses a cold land bridge into a continent no human has ever lived in — and their descendants reach its far south.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
