# The Peopling of the Americas

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

America's human story begins with a journey. Long before any ship from Europe, the first peoples crossed into a continent empty of humans and spread, generation by generation, from the far north to the far south. They filled forests, plains, mountains and coasts, and became many peoples with many languages and ways of life. Every later chapter of American history stands on this first peopling.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas. Give one real example: A small band crosses a cold land bridge into a continent no human has ever lived in — and their descendants reach its far south. and name the lesson's key words: First peoples, Migration. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
