# Video script — The Peopling of the Americas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The first peoples crossed into an empty continent and spread from north to south, peopling the Americas.

America's human story begins with a journey. Long before any ship from Europe, the first peoples crossed into a continent empty of humans and spread, generation by generation, from the far north to the far south. They filled forests, plains, mountains and coasts, and became many peoples with many languages and ways of life. Every later chapter of American history stands on this first peopling.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
