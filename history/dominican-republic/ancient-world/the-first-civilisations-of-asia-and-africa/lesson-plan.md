## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: On the great rivers of Asia and Africa the first cities, kings and scripts appear.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Beside a great river, fields, a walled city, a king's palace and a scribe writing the first script rise together.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
