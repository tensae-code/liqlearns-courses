# The First Civilisations of Asia and Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) On the great rivers of Asia and Africa the first cities, kings and scripts appear.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) On the great rivers of Asia and Africa the first cities, kings and scripts appear.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Beside a great river, fields, a walled city, a king's palace and a scribe writing the first script rise together.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Beside a great river, fields, a walled city, a king's palace and a scribe writing the first script rise together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
