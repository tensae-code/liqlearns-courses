# The First Civilisations of Asia and Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  On the great rivers of Asia and Africa the first cities, kings and scripts appear.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: On the great rivers of Asia and Africa the first cities, kings and scripts appear.
