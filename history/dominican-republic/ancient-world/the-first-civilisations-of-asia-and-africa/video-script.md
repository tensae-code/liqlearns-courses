# Video script — The First Civilisations of Asia and Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
On the great rivers of Asia and Africa the first cities, kings and scripts appear.

Civilisation begins beside great rivers. In Asia and Africa — on the Tigris and Euphrates, the Nile, the Indus and the Yellow River — farming surpluses fed the first cities, kings arose to rule them, and scripts were invented to record taxes, laws and stories. The Grade 6 book starts the world's story here: cities, kings and writing appear together, as one package, on the river plains of the East and of Africa.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
