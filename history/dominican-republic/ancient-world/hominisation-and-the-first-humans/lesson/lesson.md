# Hominisation and the First Humans

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Ape-like ancestors slowly stood, made tools and became human. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

We were not always human. The Grade 10 book teaches hominisation: ape-like ancestors, over immense spans of time, slowly stood upright, freed their hands, made tools, mastered fire and grew larger brains — each change feeding the others. Becoming human was not an event but a long process written in bones and stones, and it is the true first chapter of every history book, this one included.
