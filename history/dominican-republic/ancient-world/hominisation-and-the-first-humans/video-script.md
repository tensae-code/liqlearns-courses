# Video script — Hominisation and the First Humans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Ape-like ancestors slowly stood, made tools and became human.

We were not always human. The Grade 10 book teaches hominisation: ape-like ancestors, over immense spans of time, slowly stood upright, freed their hands, made tools, mastered fire and grew larger brains — each change feeding the others. Becoming human was not an event but a long process written in bones and stones, and it is the true first chapter of every history book, this one included.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
