## If the learner is stuck
- Imagines humans appeared suddenly -> The book teaches slowness: standing, hands, tools and brains changed together over ages — becoming human was a process, not an event.
- Go back to the one thing: Ape-like ancestors slowly stood, made tools and became human. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
