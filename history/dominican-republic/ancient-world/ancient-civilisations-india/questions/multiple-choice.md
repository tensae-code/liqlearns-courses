# Ancient Civilisations: India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) On the Indus and the Ganges, cities and faiths grew into a civilisation.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) On the Indus and the Ganges, cities and faiths grew into a civilisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Planned cities rise on the Indus while, on the Ganges, teachers found faiths that will guide Asia for three thousand years.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Planned cities rise on the Indus while, on the Ganges, teachers found faiths that will guide Asia for three thousand years.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
