# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
On the Indus and the Ganges, cities and faiths grew into a civilisation.

India's civilisation grew on two rivers. On the Indus rose some of the ancient world's most planned cities — straight streets, drains, standard bricks — and on the Ganges grew kingdoms and the teachers who founded faiths that still guide Asia. The Grade 10 book teaches ancient India as a civilisation entire in itself: cities and faiths growing together into a pattern that endured for millennia.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
