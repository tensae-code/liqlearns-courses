**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Before Writing and the First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

- **The Peopling of the Americas** (`ancient-world/the-peopling-of-the-americas/`) is recommended first — it stands just before this lesson on the path (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: For age after age, small bands hunt, gather, make fire and move with the seasons — long before any city exists.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.

- Go back to the one thing: Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
