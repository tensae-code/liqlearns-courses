# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) For age after age, small bands hunt, gather, make fire and move with the seasons — long before any city exists.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) For age after age, small bands hunt, gather, make fire and move with the seasons — long before any city exists.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
