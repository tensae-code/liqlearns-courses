# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** For age after age, small bands hunt, gather, make fire and move with the seasons — long before any city exists.
