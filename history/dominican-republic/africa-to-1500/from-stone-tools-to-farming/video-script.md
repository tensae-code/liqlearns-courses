# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born.

Before there were civilisations, there were people — for ages beyond counting. The Grade 6 book opens its world sweep with humans living by hunting and gathering: making stone tools, mastering fire, moving with the herds and the seasons. This was not a brief prelude; it was the human condition for almost the whole of our story. Only at its very end do farming, villages and then civilisations appear — which is why the book makes pupils sit with the long age first.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
