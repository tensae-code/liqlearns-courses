# From Stone Tools to Farming

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Before there were civilisations, there were people — for ages beyond counting. The Grade 6 book opens its world sweep with humans living by hunting and gathering: making stone tools, mastering fire, moving with the herds and the seasons. This was not a brief prelude; it was the human condition for almost the whole of our story. Only at its very end do farming, villages and then civilisations appear — which is why the book makes pupils sit with the long age first.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Humans lived by hunting and gathering for ages before the first civilisation was born. Give one real example: For age after age, small bands hunt, gather, make fire and move with the seasons — long before any city exists. and name the lesson's key words: Hunting and gathering, Before civilisations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
