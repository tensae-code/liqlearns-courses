# Video script — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Pharaohs raised pyramids on the Nile and ruled as gods.

Egypt is the Nile's gift organised into a kingdom. Pharaohs — kings worshipped as gods — ruled the long green valley, commanded its flood-fed farming, and raised the pyramids as tombs and as statements: this power intends to be eternal. The Grade 10 book teaches pharaonic Egypt whole — river, god-king, priests, scribes and stone — the most durable civilisation of the ancient world.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
