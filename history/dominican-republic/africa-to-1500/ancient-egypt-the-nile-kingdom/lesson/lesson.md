# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Pharaohs raised pyramids on the Nile and ruled as gods. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Egypt is the Nile's gift organised into a kingdom. Pharaohs — kings worshipped as gods — ruled the long green valley, commanded its flood-fed farming, and raised the pyramids as tombs and as statements: this power intends to be eternal. The Grade 10 book teaches pharaonic Egypt whole — river, god-king, priests, scribes and stone — the most durable civilisation of the ancient world.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Pharaohs raised pyramids on the Nile and ruled as gods. Give one real example: On the Nile's green banks, a pharaoh-god orders a mountain of stone raised over his tomb, and a kingdom organises itself around the river's flood. and name the lesson's key words: Egypt, Pharaohs, Nile. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
