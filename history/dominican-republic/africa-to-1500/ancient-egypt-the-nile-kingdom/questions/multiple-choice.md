# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Pharaohs raised pyramids on the Nile and ruled as gods.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Pharaohs raised pyramids on the Nile and ruled as gods.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) On the Nile's green banks, a pharaoh-god orders a mountain of stone raised over his tomb, and a kingdom organises itself around the river's flood.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) On the Nile's green banks, a pharaoh-god orders a mountain of stone raised over his tomb, and a kingdom organises itself around the river's flood.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
