## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Pharaohs raised pyramids on the Nile and ruled as gods.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: On the Nile's green banks, a pharaoh-god orders a mountain of stone raised over his tomb, and a kingdom organises itself around the river's flood.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
