# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Industrial empires carved up Asia and Africa between them. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The industrial age went hunting for continents. In the late nineteenth century the European empires — joined by the new industrial powers — subjected Asia and Africa: the Grade 10 book teaches imperialism as industry's foreign policy, the scramble for colonies as markets, materials and prestige. Africa was divided at conference tables within a generation. This is the road to the scramble, and it leads on to the world wars the next lessons tell.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Industrial empires carved up Asia and Africa between them. Give one real example: In a European conference room, rulers draw straight lines across a map of Africa they have never seen — a continent divided at a table. and name the lesson's key words: Imperialism, Scramble for Africa. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
