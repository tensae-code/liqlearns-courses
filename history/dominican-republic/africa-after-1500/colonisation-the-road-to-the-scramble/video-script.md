# Video script — Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Industrial empires carved up Asia and Africa between them.

The industrial age went hunting for continents. In the late nineteenth century the European empires — joined by the new industrial powers — subjected Asia and Africa: the Grade 10 book teaches imperialism as industry's foreign policy, the scramble for colonies as markets, materials and prestige. Africa was divided at conference tables within a generation. This is the road to the scramble, and it leads on to the world wars the next lessons tell.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
