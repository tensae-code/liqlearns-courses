# Video script — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The triangular trade shipped enslaved Africans to America — and the enslaved rose in rebellion.

Colonial America was built with stolen labour. The triangular trade linked three continents: manufactures sailed to Africa, enslaved Africans were shipped across the Atlantic in the middle passage, and American sugar, tobacco and cotton sailed to Europe. The Dominican book teaches the trade in its different settings — and it teaches the other half of the story too: the enslaved resisted and rose in rebellion, again and again, from the first years of the colony.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
