# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: The triangular trade shipped enslaved Africans to America — and the enslaved rose in rebellion. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Colonial America was built with stolen labour. The triangular trade linked three continents: manufactures sailed to Africa, enslaved Africans were shipped across the Atlantic in the middle passage, and American sugar, tobacco and cotton sailed to Europe. The Dominican book teaches the trade in its different settings — and it teaches the other half of the story too: the enslaved resisted and rose in rebellion, again and again, from the first years of the colony.
