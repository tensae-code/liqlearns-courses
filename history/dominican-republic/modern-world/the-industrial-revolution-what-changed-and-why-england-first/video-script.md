# Video script — The Industrial Revolution: What Changed, and Why England First

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Factories and steam remade work, cities and the balance of the world — and it began in England.

In the later eighteenth century, production itself changed nature. Factories gathered workers under one roof, steam replaced muscle and water as power, and the Grade 6 book follows the consequences: work, cities and the balance of the world are remade together. Industrialising first — England first — meant ruling the century; the book makes pupils see the revolution whole, not as a parade of inventions.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
