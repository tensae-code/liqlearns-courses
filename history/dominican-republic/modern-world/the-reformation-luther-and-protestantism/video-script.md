# Video script — The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Luther's protest split Western Christianity in two.

In 1517 a German monk protested. Martin Luther's attack on the sale of indulgences — and then on the Church's authority itself — split Western Christianity in two: Catholic and Protestant Europes, each with its churches, states and wars of religion. The Grade 10 book teaches the Reformation as a turning of the whole civilisation: print spread the protest, princes weaponised it, and the religious unity of a thousand years ended for good.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
