# Modern Empires — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Modern empires raced to own continents, seas and peoples.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Modern empires raced to own continents, seas and peoples.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) On the world map, rival European flags plant themselves across the Americas, Africa and Asia in a race no one wants to lose.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) On the world map, rival European flags plant themselves across the Americas, Africa and Asia in a race no one wants to lose.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
