# Video script — Modern Empires

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Modern empires raced to own continents, seas and peoples.

The modern age is an age of empires racing. As Europe's states grew stronger and its ships faster, they competed to own continents, seas and peoples — colonies for gold, sugar, spices, land and glory. The Grade 6 book teaches the modern empires as a race with rules of its own: plant the flag first, hold the sea lanes, keep rivals out. That race drew the map the world still lives with.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
