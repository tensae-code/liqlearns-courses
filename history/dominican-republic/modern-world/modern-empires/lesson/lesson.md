# Modern Empires

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Modern empires raced to own continents, seas and peoples. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The modern age is an age of empires racing. As Europe's states grew stronger and its ships faster, they competed to own continents, seas and peoples — colonies for gold, sugar, spices, land and glory. The Grade 6 book teaches the modern empires as a race with rules of its own: plant the flag first, hold the sea lanes, keep rivals out. That race drew the map the world still lives with.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Modern empires raced to own continents, seas and peoples. Give one real example: On the world map, rival European flags plant themselves across the Americas, Africa and Asia in a race no one wants to lose. and name the lesson's key words: Modern empires, Colonies. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
