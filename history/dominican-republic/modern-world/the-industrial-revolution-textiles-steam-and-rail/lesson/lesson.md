# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Steam, cotton mills and railways remade production and travel in the first industrial revolution, 1760–1840. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The first industrial revolution, dated by the book 1760–1840, changed how things are made and how people move. Cotton textiles mechanised first — spinning and weaving by machine in mills — steam became the universal power, and railways bound the new factory towns to ports and coalfields. The Grade 10 book teaches the three together as one system: textiles, steam and rail, remaking production and travel within a single lifetime.
