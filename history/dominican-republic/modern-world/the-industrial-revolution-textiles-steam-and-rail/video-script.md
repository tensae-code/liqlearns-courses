# Video script — The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Steam, cotton mills and railways remade production and travel in the first industrial revolution, 1760–1840.

The first industrial revolution, dated by the book 1760–1840, changed how things are made and how people move. Cotton textiles mechanised first — spinning and weaving by machine in mills — steam became the universal power, and railways bound the new factory towns to ports and coalfields. The Grade 10 book teaches the three together as one system: textiles, steam and rail, remaking production and travel within a single lifetime.
