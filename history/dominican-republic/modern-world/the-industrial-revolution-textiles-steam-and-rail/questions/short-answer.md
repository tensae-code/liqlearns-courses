# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Steam, cotton mills and railways remade production and travel in the first industrial revolution, 1760–1840.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A cotton mill's steam engine turns a hundred looms at once, and outside, the first railway carries coal and cloth faster than any horse.
