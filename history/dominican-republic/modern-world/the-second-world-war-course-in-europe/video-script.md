# Video script — The Second World War: Course in Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
A second, greater war again reshaped the hemisphere.

A generation after the first, a second and greater world war came. The Grade 6 book teaches its course in Europe — aggression, blitzkrieg, occupation, the eastern front, and the long fight back — and its American meaning: again the hemisphere is reshaped, its industry and soldiers decisive, and it emerges in 1945 inside a world order led from Washington. For the Dominican Republic, Trujillo's wartime alignment belongs to this same story.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
