# The Second World War: Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) A second, greater war again reshaped the hemisphere.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) A second, greater war again reshaped the hemisphere.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) War spreads across Europe and the oceans, and American industry, soldiers and diplomacy help decide it — and inherit its world.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) War spreads across Europe and the oceans, and American industry, soldiers and diplomacy help decide it — and inherit its world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
