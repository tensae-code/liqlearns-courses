# The Second World War: Course in Europe

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: A second, greater war again reshaped the hemisphere. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

A generation after the first, a second and greater world war came. The Grade 6 book teaches its course in Europe — aggression, blitzkrieg, occupation, the eastern front, and the long fight back — and its American meaning: again the hemisphere is reshaped, its industry and soldiers decisive, and it emerges in 1945 inside a world order led from Washington. For the Dominican Republic, Trujillo's wartime alignment belongs to this same story.
