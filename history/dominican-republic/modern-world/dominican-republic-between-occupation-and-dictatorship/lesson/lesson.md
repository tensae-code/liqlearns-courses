# The Dominican Republic Between Occupation and Dictatorship

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: The marines left, Vásquez modernised, and the republic breathed — briefly. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

When the marines left in 1924, the republic got a second chance. Horacio Vásquez, elected president, governed until 1930 in what the Grade 6 book calls a first stage of modernisation: roads, schools and public works, an elected government functioning after eight years of foreign rule. The republic breathed. But the breathing was brief — economic crisis and political manoeuvre gathered, and at the head of the army the occupation had built stood Rafael Trujillo, waiting for his moment.
