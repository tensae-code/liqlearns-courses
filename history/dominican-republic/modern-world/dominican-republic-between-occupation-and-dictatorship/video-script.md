# Video script — The Dominican Republic Between Occupation and Dictatorship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The marines left, Vásquez modernised, and the republic breathed — briefly.

When the marines left in 1924, the republic got a second chance. Horacio Vásquez, elected president, governed until 1930 in what the Grade 6 book calls a first stage of modernisation: roads, schools and public works, an elected government functioning after eight years of foreign rule. The republic breathed. But the breathing was brief — economic crisis and political manoeuvre gathered, and at the head of the army the occupation had built stood Rafael Trujillo, waiting for his moment.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
