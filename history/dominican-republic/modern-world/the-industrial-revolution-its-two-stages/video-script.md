# Video script — The Industrial Revolution: Its Two Stages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Steel, electricity and oil powered a second, bigger industrial age.

Industry had a second act, bigger than the first. From the later nineteenth century, steel replaced iron, electricity lit and powered the cities, oil fuelled new engines, and chemical industries made what nature never had. The Grade 10 book teaches the Second Industrial Revolution as its own stage — the age of giant firms, world markets and mass production that leads directly to the imperial scramble and the world wars in the lessons that follow.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
