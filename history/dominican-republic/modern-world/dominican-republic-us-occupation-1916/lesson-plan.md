# Lesson plan — The Dominican Republic: The US Occupation, 1916–1924

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Dominican Republic: The US Occupation, 1916–1924
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Dominican Republic: Annexation, Caudillos and Occupation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Dictators borrowed and defaulted until the United States stepped in — first with customs, then with marines — and occupied the country from 1916 to 1924; Dominicans resisted until the occupation ended.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 7 Dictaduras de Heureaux (Lilís) y Cáceres; hegemonía de los Estados Unidos y primera intervención** — dictators borrow and default until the United States steps in, first with customs, then with marines.
2. **Unidad 7 Historia dominicana (1916–1963): Ocupación norteamericana (1916–1924)** — US marines occupy the country for eight years and build the constabulary that will produce Trujillo.
3. **Unidad 1 Ocupación estadounidense: causas iniciales, planes y acciones, apogeo azucarero** — in 1916 the marines land, claiming to end caudillo chaos, while sugar booms under occupation.
4. **Unidad 1 Resistencia a la Ocupación y hacia la Desocupación** — Dominican patriots fight at La Barranquita and resist until the occupation winds down in 1924.
