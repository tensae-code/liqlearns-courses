# The Dominican Republic: The US Occupation, 1916–1924 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Dictators borrowed and defaulted until the United States stepped in — first with customs, then with marines — and occupied the country from 1916 to 1924; Dominicans resisted until the occupation ended.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** US marines land in 1916 claiming to end chaos — and stay eight years, while Dominican patriots fight at La Barranquita and resist to the end.
