# The Dominican Republic: The US Occupation, 1916–1924 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Dictators borrowed and defaulted until the United States stepped in — first with customs, then with marines — and occupied the country from 1916 to 1924; Dominicans resisted until the occupation ended.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Dictators borrowed and defaulted until the United States stepped in — first with customs, then with marines — and occupied the country from 1916 to 1924; Dominicans resisted until the occupation ended.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
