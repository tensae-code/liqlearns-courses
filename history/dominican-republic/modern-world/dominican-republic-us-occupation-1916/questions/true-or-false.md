# The Dominican Republic: The US Occupation, 1916–1924 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Dictators borrowed and defaulted until the United States stepped in — first with customs, then with marines — and occupied the country from 1916 to 1924; Dominicans resisted until the occupation ended.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
