# American Independence

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Thirteen colonies rebelled and founded a republic across the Atlantic. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

In 1776 thirteen British colonies in North America rebelled and founded a republic across the Atlantic — the United States. The Grade 10 book teaches the founding inside modernity and capitalism's story: a merchant, farming society that turned Enlightenment words — rights, consent, representation — into a state. For this course the founding matters doubly: the republic founded here is the same power whose expansion, occupation and interventions fill the Dominican lessons ahead.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example: Thirteen colonies along one coast declare themselves independent of the world's strongest empire — and win, founding a republic that will shadow this whole course. and name the lesson's key words: American independence, United States founded. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
