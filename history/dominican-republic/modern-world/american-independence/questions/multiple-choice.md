# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Thirteen colonies rebelled and founded a republic across the Atlantic.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Thirteen colonies rebelled and founded a republic across the Atlantic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Thirteen colonies along one coast declare themselves independent of the world's strongest empire — and win, founding a republic that will shadow this whole course.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Thirteen colonies along one coast declare themselves independent of the world's strongest empire — and win, founding a republic that will shadow this whole course.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
