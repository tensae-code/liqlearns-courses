# Video script — American Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Thirteen colonies rebelled and founded a republic across the Atlantic.

In 1776 thirteen British colonies in North America rebelled and founded a republic across the Atlantic — the United States. The Grade 10 book teaches the founding inside modernity and capitalism's story: a merchant, farming society that turned Enlightenment words — rights, consent, representation — into a state. For this course the founding matters doubly: the republic founded here is the same power whose expansion, occupation and interventions fill the Dominican lessons ahead.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
