# Video script — Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Fascism and Nazism turned crisis into dictatorship and racial war.

Between the wars, crisis bred dictatorships of a new kind. In Italy, fascism; in Germany, Nazism — movements that turned defeat, inflation and fear into one-party rule, leader cults and the persecution of enemies within. The Grade 10 book compares the two regimes as the book files them — fascist regimes together — and marks Nazism's extra horror: racism raised to a state doctrine, pointing Europe toward the second world war of the next lesson.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
