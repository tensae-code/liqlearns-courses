# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Fascism and Nazism turned crisis into dictatorship and racial war. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Between the wars, crisis bred dictatorships of a new kind. In Italy, fascism; in Germany, Nazism — movements that turned defeat, inflation and fear into one-party rule, leader cults and the persecution of enemies within. The Grade 10 book compares the two regimes as the book files them — fascist regimes together — and marks Nazism's extra horror: racism raised to a state doctrine, pointing Europe toward the second world war of the next lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example: In Rome and Berlin, uniformed movements march a continent's crisis into dictatorship — one preaching the nation, the other race. and name the lesson's key words: Fascism, Nazism. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
