# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Twice in one year Russia rose — first against the tsar, then for the soviets.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** In one year, two revolutions: crowds topple a three-hundred-year tsardom in February, and in October the soviets seize power in its place.
