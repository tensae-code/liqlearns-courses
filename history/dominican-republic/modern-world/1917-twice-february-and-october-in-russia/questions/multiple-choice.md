# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Twice in one year Russia rose — first against the tsar, then for the soviets.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Twice in one year Russia rose — first against the tsar, then for the soviets.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) In one year, two revolutions: crowds topple a three-hundred-year tsardom in February, and in October the soviets seize power in its place.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) In one year, two revolutions: crowds topple a three-hundred-year tsardom in February, and in October the soviets seize power in its place.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
