# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Twice in one year Russia rose — first against the tsar, then for the soviets.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Twice in one year Russia rose — first against the tsar, then for the soviets.
