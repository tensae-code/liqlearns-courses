# Video script — 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Twice in one year Russia rose — first against the tsar, then for the soviets.

Russia rose twice in 1917. In February, war, hunger and collapse brought crowds onto Petrograd's streets and the tsar — three hundred years of Romanov rule — fell in days. The provisional government that replaced him kept the war and lost the country; in October, the Bolsheviks seized power for the soviets, promising peace, land and bread. The Grade 10 book teaches the two revolutions separately because they are different events with different victors — the second devoured the first.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
