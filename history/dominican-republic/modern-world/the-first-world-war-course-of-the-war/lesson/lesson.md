# The First World War: Course of the War

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Europe's war reached across the ocean, and the Americas were drawn in. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The war that began in Europe in 1914 did not stay in Europe. The Grade 6 book follows its course as it reaches across the ocean: trade, loans, submarine warfare and finally soldiers draw the Americas in — the United States entering in 1917, Latin American nations breaking relations or declaring war. A hemisphere that thought itself an ocean away learned that modern war respects no ocean.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Europe's war reached across the ocean, and the Americas were drawn in. Give one real example: Trenches scar Europe while, across the Atlantic, American ships, soldiers and loans are pulled into the war's gravity. and name the lesson's key words: First World War, Americas drawn in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
