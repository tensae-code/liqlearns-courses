# Video script — The First World War: Course of the War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Europe's war reached across the ocean, and the Americas were drawn in.

The war that began in Europe in 1914 did not stay in Europe. The Grade 6 book follows its course as it reaches across the ocean: trade, loans, submarine warfare and finally soldiers draw the Americas in — the United States entering in 1917, Latin American nations breaking relations or declaring war. A hemisphere that thought itself an ocean away learned that modern war respects no ocean.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
