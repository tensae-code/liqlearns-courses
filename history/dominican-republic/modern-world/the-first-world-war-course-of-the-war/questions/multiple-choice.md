# The First World War: Course of the War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Europe's war reached across the ocean, and the Americas were drawn in.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Europe's war reached across the ocean, and the Americas were drawn in.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Trenches scar Europe while, across the Atlantic, American ships, soldiers and loans are pulled into the war's gravity.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Trenches scar Europe while, across the Atlantic, American ships, soldiers and loans are pulled into the war's gravity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
