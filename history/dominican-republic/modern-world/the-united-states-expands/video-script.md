# Video script — The United States Expands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The United States swelled westward across its continent — and its shadow fell over the Caribbean.

Through the nineteenth century the United States swelled westward — buying, settling and conquering its way from the Atlantic to the Pacific, transforming its own society as it grew. The Dominican book teaches this expansion for a Caribbean reason: the bigger and stronger the northern republic became, the longer its shadow fell over the small nations to its south — including the Dominican Republic, whose later story cannot be told without it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
