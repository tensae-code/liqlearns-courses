# The United States Expands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The United States swelled westward across its continent — and its shadow fell over the Caribbean.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** On the animated map the young United States grows and grows westward until its frontier reaches the Pacific — and its gaze turns south to the Caribbean islands.
