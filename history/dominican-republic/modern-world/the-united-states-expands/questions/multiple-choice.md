# The United States Expands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The United States swelled westward across its continent — and its shadow fell over the Caribbean.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The United States swelled westward across its continent — and its shadow fell over the Caribbean.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) On the animated map the young United States grows and grows westward until its frontier reaches the Pacific — and its gaze turns south to the Caribbean islands.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) On the animated map the young United States grows and grows westward until its frontier reaches the Pacific — and its gaze turns south to the Caribbean islands.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
