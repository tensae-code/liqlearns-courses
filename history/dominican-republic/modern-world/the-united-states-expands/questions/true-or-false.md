# The United States Expands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The United States swelled westward across its continent — and its shadow fell over the Caribbean.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The United States swelled westward across its continent — and its shadow fell over the Caribbean.
