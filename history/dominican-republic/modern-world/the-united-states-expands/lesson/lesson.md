# The United States Expands

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: The United States swelled westward across its continent — and its shadow fell over the Caribbean. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Through the nineteenth century the United States swelled westward — buying, settling and conquering its way from the Atlantic to the Pacific, transforming its own society as it grew. The Dominican book teaches this expansion for a Caribbean reason: the bigger and stronger the northern republic became, the longer its shadow fell over the small nations to its south — including the Dominican Republic, whose later story cannot be told without it.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example: On the animated map the young United States grows and grows westward until its frontier reaches the Pacific — and its gaze turns south to the Caribbean islands. and name the lesson's key words: Westward expansion, Caribbean.
