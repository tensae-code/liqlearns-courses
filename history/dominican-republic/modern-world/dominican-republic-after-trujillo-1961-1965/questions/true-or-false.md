# The Dominican Republic After Trujillo, 1961–1965 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   The tyrant fell, a council steered the country to elections, Juan Bosch won and wrote a democratic constitution — and was overthrown within months; democracy's first attempt collapsed into coups.
