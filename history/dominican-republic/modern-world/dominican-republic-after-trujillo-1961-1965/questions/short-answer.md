# The Dominican Republic After Trujillo, 1961–1965 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The tyrant fell, a council steered the country to elections, Juan Bosch won and wrote a democratic constitution — and was overthrown within months; democracy's first attempt collapsed into coups.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A council holds the ring, voters elect a writer-president who gives them a democratic constitution — and seven months later, tanks end the experiment.
