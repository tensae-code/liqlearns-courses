# Lesson plan — The Dominican Republic After Trujillo, 1961–1965

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Dominican Republic After Trujillo, 1961–1965
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Dictatorship, Revolution and the Democratic Transition (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The tyrant fell, a council steered the country to elections, Juan Bosch won and wrote a democratic constitution — and was overthrown within months; democracy's first attempt collapsed into coups.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Dictatorship, Revolution and the Democratic Transition (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 7 Transición después de la dictadura (1961–1962): Consejo de Estado** — the tyrant falls and a council tries to steer the country to elections.
2. **Unidad 7 Constitución de 1963: auge y caída de Juan Bosch** — Juan Bosch wins, writes a democratic constitution, and is overthrown within months.
3. **Unidad 3 Luchas democráticas y revolucionarias (1961–1965): transición fallida y orden golpista** — democracy's first attempt collapses into coups and a triumvirate.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The tyrant fell, a council steered the country to elections, Juan Bosch won and wrote a democratic constitution — and was overthrown within months; democracy's first attempt collapsed into coups.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
