# The English Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Parliament made war on its king, and a republic was tried in England.
