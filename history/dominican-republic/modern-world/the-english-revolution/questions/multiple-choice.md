# The English Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Parliament made war on its king, and a republic was tried in England.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Parliament made war on its king, and a republic was tried in England.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) In London, Parliament's soldiers face the king's cavaliers; the king is tried and executed, and England experiments with being a republic.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) In London, Parliament's soldiers face the king's cavaliers; the king is tried and executed, and England experiments with being a republic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
