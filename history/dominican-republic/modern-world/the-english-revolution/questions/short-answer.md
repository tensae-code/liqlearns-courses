# The English Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Parliament made war on its king, and a republic was tried in England.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** In London, Parliament's soldiers face the king's cavaliers; the king is tried and executed, and England experiments with being a republic.
