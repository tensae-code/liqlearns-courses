# The English Revolution

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Parliament made war on its king, and a republic was tried in England. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

A century before France, England tried revolution. Parliament, defending its rights against Charles I, made war on its king, defeated him, tried him and executed him — and England experimented with a republic under Cromwell. The Grade 10 book teaches the English Revolution as modernity's rehearsal: the ideas that kings rule by consent and that a people may judge its king were acted out here first, and every later revolution studied the performance.
