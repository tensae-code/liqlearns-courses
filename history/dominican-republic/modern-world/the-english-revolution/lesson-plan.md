## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Parliament made war on its king, and a republic was tried in England.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: In London, Parliament's soldiers face the king's cavaliers; the king is tried and executed, and England experiments with being a republic.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
