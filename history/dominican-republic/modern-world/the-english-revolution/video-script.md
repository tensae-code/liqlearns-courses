# Video script — The English Revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Parliament made war on its king, and a republic was tried in England.

A century before France, England tried revolution. Parliament, defending its rights against Charles I, made war on its king, defeated him, tried him and executed him — and England experimented with a republic under Cromwell. The Grade 10 book teaches the English Revolution as modernity's rehearsal: the ideas that kings rule by consent and that a people may judge its king were acted out here first, and every later revolution studied the performance.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
