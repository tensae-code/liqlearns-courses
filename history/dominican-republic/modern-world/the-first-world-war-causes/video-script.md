# Video script — The First World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Alliances, arms races and empire pulled Europe into the Great War.

The Great War had causes deeper than its spark. The Grade 10 book teaches the loaded system of 1914: rival alliances binding Europe's powers into two blocs, arms races on land and sea, and imperial competition for colonies — so that when an assassin shot an archduke in Sarajevo, the alliance ropes pulled everyone in. Wars of liberation is the book's own unit title for what followed: subject peoples, too, would try to use the war to free themselves.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
