# Video script — Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Money, markets and science combined into a new economic order — towns, trade and money loosened feudal bonds, and capitalism stirred.

Feudalism died of money. The Grade 6 book teaches capitalism's rise from the sixteenth century: money, markets and science combine into a new economic order, where land matters less than capital and profit drives production. A new world of owners and wage-earners begins to replace the feudal ladder.
The Grade 10 book shows the loosening from inside the old order: towns grow, trade revives, money circulates — and serfdom decays as peasants buy, flee or bargain their way out of bondage. Capitalism stirs first in the cracks of feudalism, long before the factory chimney: in the market square and the merchant's ledger.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
