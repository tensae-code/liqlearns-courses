# The Second World War: Causes

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: The fascist powers' aggression dragged the world into a second total war. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The second world war was caused in the 1930s, before it was fought in the 1940s. The Grade 10 book teaches the fascist powers' aggression as a sequence — each seizure unanswered, each success teaching the aggressors to take more — until the invasion of Poland in 1939 dragged the world into a second total war. Causes here are a chain, and every link was a choice by the aggressors and a hesitation by those who watched.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The fascist powers' aggression dragged the world into a second total war. Give one real example: One aggression follows another on the map — Ethiopia, Spain, Austria, Czechoslovakia, Poland — while the democracies hesitate, until the world is at war. and name the lesson's key words: Second World War causes, Aggression. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
