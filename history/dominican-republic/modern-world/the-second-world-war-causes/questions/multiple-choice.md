# The Second World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The fascist powers' aggression dragged the world into a second total war.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The fascist powers' aggression dragged the world into a second total war.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) One aggression follows another on the map — Ethiopia, Spain, Austria, Czechoslovakia, Poland — while the democracies hesitate, until the world is at war.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) One aggression follows another on the map — Ethiopia, Spain, Austria, Czechoslovakia, Poland — while the democracies hesitate, until the world is at war.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
