# Video script — The Second World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The fascist powers' aggression dragged the world into a second total war.

The second world war was caused in the 1930s, before it was fought in the 1940s. The Grade 10 book teaches the fascist powers' aggression as a sequence — each seizure unanswered, each success teaching the aggressors to take more — until the invasion of Poland in 1939 dragged the world into a second total war. Causes here are a chain, and every link was a choice by the aggressors and a hesitation by those who watched.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
