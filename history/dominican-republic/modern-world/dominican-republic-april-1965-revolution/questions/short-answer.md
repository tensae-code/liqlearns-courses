# The Dominican Republic: The April 1965 Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Constitutionalist soldiers and civilians rose to restore Bosch and the 1963 constitution — and the United States intervened, negotiating the revolution into elections.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** In April 1965, young officers and armed civilians hold the capital's streets for a constitution — and US troops land again to stop them winning.
