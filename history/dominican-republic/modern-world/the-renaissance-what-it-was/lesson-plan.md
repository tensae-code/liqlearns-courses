# Lesson plan — The Renaissance: What It Was

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Renaissance: What It Was
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Art, science and curiosity were reborn: artists and scholars rediscovered the classical world and remade Europe's mind.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Art, science and curiosity were reborn: artists and scholars rediscovered the classical world and remade Europe's mind.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: In an Italian city, an artist studies an ancient statue, a scholar opens a Greek manuscript, and a new age announces itself.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
