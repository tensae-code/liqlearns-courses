# The Renaissance: What It Was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Art, science and curiosity were reborn: artists and scholars rediscovered the classical world and remade Europe's mind.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Art, science and curiosity were reborn: artists and scholars rediscovered the classical world and remade Europe's mind.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: b) In an Italian city, an artist studies an ancient statue, a scholar opens a Greek manuscript, and a new age announces itself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
