# Video script — The Renaissance: What It Was

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Art, science and curiosity were reborn: artists and scholars rediscovered the classical world and remade Europe's mind.

After the medieval centuries, Europe looked back — and caught fire. The Grade 6 book tells the Renaissance as modernity's doorway: art, science and curiosity are reborn, and Europe steps toward the modern age. Painters, sculptors, astronomers and printers all move at once, because the movement is one: a new confidence that the world can be observed, measured and remade.
The Grade 10 book names the fuel: artists and scholars rediscover the classical world — Greek manuscripts, Roman statues, ancient philosophy — and remake Europe's mind with old materials. The Renaissance is a rebirth in the literal sense: antiquity, born again, gives birth to modernity.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
