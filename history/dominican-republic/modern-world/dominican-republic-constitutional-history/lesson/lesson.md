## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 7 Evolución constitucional: antecedentes y la Constitución de noviembre de 1844

The republic was born writing. In November 1844, months after independence, the Dominican Republic gave itself its first constitution — the founding generation setting down, before almost anything else, the rules of the state they had just created. The Grade 12 book teaches the antecedents and that first text as the start of a national habit: Dominicans settle their deepest arguments, again and again, by writing constitutions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 7 Desarrollos ulteriores: autocracia "democrática" bajo Trujillo y la Constitución de 2015

And the habit never stopped — for good and ill. Constitutions were rewritten by dictators, including a 'democratic' autocracy under Trujillo that kept the forms while emptying them, and by democrats restoring rights, down to the constitution of 2015. Read in line, the constitutions are the republic's autobiography: each text records who held power when it was written, and what they hoped — or feared — the country would be.
