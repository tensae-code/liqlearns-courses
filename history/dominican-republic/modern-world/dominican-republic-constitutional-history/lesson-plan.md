# Lesson plan — The Dominican Republic: Constitutional History

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Dominican Republic: Constitutional History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Dictatorship, Revolution and the Democratic Transition (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The republic was born writing constitutions, starting with November 1844 — and constitutions were rewritten by dictators and democrats alike, down to 2015.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Dictatorship, Revolution and the Democratic Transition (recommended, never forced).
