## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The republic was annexed to Spain in 1861 — and Dominicans fought the Restoration War to win it back.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A Dominican president hands the young republic back to Spain — and four years of guerrilla war in the hills win it back again.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
