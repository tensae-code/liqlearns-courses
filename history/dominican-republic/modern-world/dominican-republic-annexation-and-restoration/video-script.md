# Video script — The Dominican Republic: Annexation and Restoration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The republic was annexed to Spain in 1861 — and Dominicans fought the Restoration War to win it back.

The Dominican Republic's first independence did not hold unchallenged. In 1861, President Pedro Santana annexed the young republic to Spain, handing sovereignty back to the old colonial power. Dominicans answered with the Restoration War: from 1863, patriots rose — famously at the Grito de Capotillo — and fought a guerrilla war through the Cibao hills until Spain withdrew in 1865. The republic was restored: independence, lost by a signature, was re-won by arms.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
