# The Dominican Republic: Annexation and Restoration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The republic was annexed to Spain in 1861 — and Dominicans fought the Restoration War to win it back.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A Dominican president hands the young republic back to Spain — and four years of guerrilla war in the hills win it back again.
