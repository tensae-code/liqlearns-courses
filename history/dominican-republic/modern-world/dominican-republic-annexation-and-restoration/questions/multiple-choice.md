# The Dominican Republic: Annexation and Restoration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The republic was annexed to Spain in 1861 — and Dominicans fought the Restoration War to win it back.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The republic was annexed to Spain in 1861 — and Dominicans fought the Restoration War to win it back.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A Dominican president hands the young republic back to Spain — and four years of guerrilla war in the hills win it back again.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A Dominican president hands the young republic back to Spain — and four years of guerrilla war in the hills win it back again.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
