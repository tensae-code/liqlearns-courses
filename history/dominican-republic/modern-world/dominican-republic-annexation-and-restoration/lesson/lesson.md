# The Dominican Republic: Annexation and Restoration

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: The republic was annexed to Spain in 1861 — and Dominicans fought the Restoration War to win it back. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The Dominican Republic's first independence did not hold unchallenged. In 1861, President Pedro Santana annexed the young republic to Spain, handing sovereignty back to the old colonial power. Dominicans answered with the Restoration War: from 1863, patriots rose — famously at the Grito de Capotillo — and fought a guerrilla war through the Cibao hills until Spain withdrew in 1865. The republic was restored: independence, lost by a signature, was re-won by arms.
