# Latin American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Colony after colony broke from Spain and invented new republics — then spent a century fighting over what independence should mean, between liberals and conservatives.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Across Spanish America, creole leaders raise armies, proclaim republics — and then argue for a hundred years over how those republics should be run.
