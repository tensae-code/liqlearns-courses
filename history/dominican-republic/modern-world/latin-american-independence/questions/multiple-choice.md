# Latin American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Colony after colony broke from Spain and invented new republics — then spent a century fighting over what independence should mean, between liberals and conservatives.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Colony after colony broke from Spain and invented new republics — then spent a century fighting over what independence should mean, between liberals and conservatives.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Across Spanish America, creole leaders raise armies, proclaim republics — and then argue for a hundred years over how those republics should be run.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Across Spanish America, creole leaders raise armies, proclaim republics — and then argue for a hundred years over how those republics should be run.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
