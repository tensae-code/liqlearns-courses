# Latin American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Colony after colony broke from Spain and invented new republics — then spent a century fighting over what independence should mean, between liberals and conservatives.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Colony after colony broke from Spain and invented new republics — then spent a century fighting over what independence should mean, between liberals and conservatives.
