# Lesson plan — The Dominican Republic: Our History, Heroes and Symbols

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Dominican Republic: Our History, Heroes and Symbols
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage and the Historian's Craft (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A nation's past is a treasure its children inherit: its great events, its heroines and heroes, its flag, shield and anthem, and the beloved figures who carry its culture.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage and the Historian's Craft (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 5 Mi país, su identidad y cultura: Acontecimientos históricos que forman parte del patrimonio cultural** — the great events of the Dominican past are handed to children as a treasure they inherit.
2. **Unidad 5 Heroínas y héroes nacionales: legado de honor** — the nation's heroines and heroes leave an example the children are asked to honour.
3. **Unidad 5 Los símbolos patrios en la identidad** — flag, shield and anthem tell every Dominican child who they are.
4. **Unidad 5 Personajes populares: personajes destacados de mi país** — beloved popular figures carry the country's culture in music, sport and story.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: A nation's past is a treasure its children inherit: its great events, its heroines and heroes, its flag, shield and anthem, and the beloved figures who carry its culture.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A Grade 3 child can point to the flag and say: this tells every Dominican who we are.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
