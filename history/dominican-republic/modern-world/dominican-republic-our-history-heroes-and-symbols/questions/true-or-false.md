# The Dominican Republic: Our History, Heroes and Symbols — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   A nation's past is a treasure its children inherit: its great events, its heroines and heroes, its flag, shield and anthem, and the beloved figures who carry its culture.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A nation's past is a treasure its children inherit: its great events, its heroines and heroes, its flag, shield and anthem, and the beloved figures who carry its culture.
