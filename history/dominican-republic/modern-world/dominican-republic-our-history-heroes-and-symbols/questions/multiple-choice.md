# The Dominican Republic: Our History, Heroes and Symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) A nation's past is a treasure its children inherit: its great events, its heroines and heroes, its flag, shield and anthem, and the beloved figures who carry its culture.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) A nation's past is a treasure its children inherit: its great events, its heroines and heroes, its flag, shield and anthem, and the beloved figures who carry its culture.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A Grade 3 child can point to the flag and say: this tells every Dominican who we are.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A Grade 3 child can point to the flag and say: this tells every Dominican who we are.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
