# The Dominican Republic: Our History, Heroes and Symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A nation's past is a treasure its children inherit: its great events, its heroines and heroes, its flag, shield and anthem, and the beloved figures who carry its culture.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A Grade 3 child can point to the flag and say: this tells every Dominican who we are.
