## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: European ships reached America and two worlds collided, changing both forever.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Ships from Spain cross an ocean their maps left blank and land in a Caribbean world already full of peoples.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
