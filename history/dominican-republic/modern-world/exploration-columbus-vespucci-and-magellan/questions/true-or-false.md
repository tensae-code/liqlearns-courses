# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  European ships reached America and two worlds collided, changing both forever.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: European ships reached America and two worlds collided, changing both forever.
