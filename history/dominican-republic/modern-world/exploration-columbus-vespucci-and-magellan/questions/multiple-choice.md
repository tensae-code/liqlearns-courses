# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) European ships reached America and two worlds collided, changing both forever.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) European ships reached America and two worlds collided, changing both forever.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Ships from Spain cross an ocean their maps left blank and land in a Caribbean world already full of peoples.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Ships from Spain cross an ocean their maps left blank and land in a Caribbean world already full of peoples.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
