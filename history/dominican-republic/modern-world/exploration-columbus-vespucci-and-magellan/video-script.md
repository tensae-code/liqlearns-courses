# Video script — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
European ships reached America and two worlds collided, changing both forever.

At the end of the fifteenth century, European ships crossed the Atlantic and reached America — Columbus's voyages, the coasts Vespucci sailed and named, and Magellan's fleet that went on to circle the whole Earth. The Dominican book calls what followed a collision of two worlds: peoples who had never known of each other met, and neither world would ever be the same — new foods, new animals and new faiths one way; conquest, disease and a shattered world the other.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
