# Lesson plan — Liberalism and Socialism

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Liberalism and Socialism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: In a factory town, workers form a union and raise a banner: the answer to capitalism is organised, argued and marched for.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses the two answers -> The books separate them: liberalism frees the individual and the market; socialism organises the workers to remake ownership itself.
- Go back to the one thing: Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
