# Video script — Liberalism and Socialism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.

Industrial capitalism created its own critics. The Grade 6 book teaches the social thought of the age: liberalism, defending individual rights, constitutions and free markets; and socialism, arguing that the workers who make the wealth should share its ownership and power. Two answers to the same factory town — and the modern world's politics is still their argument.
The Grade 10 book follows the workers themselves: industrial labourers organise — unions, parties, strikes, internationals — and socialism becomes a world force, not just an idea in books. The movement's lesson is organisational: ideas change the world when people organise around them.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
