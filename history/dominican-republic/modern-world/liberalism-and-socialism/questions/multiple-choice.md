# Liberalism and Socialism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) In a factory town, workers form a union and raise a banner: the answer to capitalism is organised, argued and marched for.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) In a factory town, workers form a union and raise a banner: the answer to capitalism is organised, argued and marched for.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
