# Liberalism and Socialism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** In a factory town, workers form a union and raise a banner: the answer to capitalism is organised, argued and marched for.
