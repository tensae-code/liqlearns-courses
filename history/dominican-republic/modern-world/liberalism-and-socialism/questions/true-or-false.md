# Liberalism and Socialism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Workers and thinkers answered industrial capitalism with liberal and socialist ideas — and the organised workers' movement made socialism a world force.
