## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 6 La discursiva sobre la condición dominicana: dualidad conservadora-liberal y apogeo del liberalismo

Dominican history was argued with ideas as well as fought with arms. The Grade 12 book teaches the discourse on the Dominican condition: for a century, conservative and liberal visions dueled — order, church and tradition against reform, rights and education — with liberalism reaching its height as the nation's guiding argument. Every caudillo and constitution in this course had thinkers behind them; this lesson names the argument itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 6 Hostos y el positivismo

Its greatest figure was a schoolmaster. Eugenio María de Hostos — Puerto Rican-born, Dominican by adoption and grave — brought positivism, the faith that society can be studied and improved scientifically, and poured it into education: normal schools to train teachers, rational teaching, women's education. Hostos tried to remake the republic not with an army but with classrooms, and the book honours him as liberalism's most constructive Dominican apostle.
