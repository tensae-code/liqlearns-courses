# Dominican Thinkers: Liberalism and Hostos — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Dominican thinkers argued for a century over what their nation is — conservative order against liberal reform — and Eugenio María de Hostos brought positivism and a schoolmaster's faith in education to remake the republic.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** In a Santo Domingo classroom, Hostos teaches future teachers: his weapon for remaking the republic is not the sword but the school.
