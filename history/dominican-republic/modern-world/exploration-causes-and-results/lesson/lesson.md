# Exploration: Causes and Results

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Europe's states, ships and hunger for spices launched a planetary expansion. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Before the voyages, the causes. The Grade 10 book opens Europe's primacy with why expansion happened when it did: centralised states that could fund fleets, ships and navigation that could cross oceans, and a hunger for spices and gold that made the risk worth taking. Europe's planetary expansion was launched by a system, not a single sailor — and its results, conquest and colonisation of whole continents, follow from those causes through this course's next lessons.
