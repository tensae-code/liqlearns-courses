# Video script — Exploration: Causes and Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Europe's states, ships and hunger for spices launched a planetary expansion.

Before the voyages, the causes. The Grade 10 book opens Europe's primacy with why expansion happened when it did: centralised states that could fund fleets, ships and navigation that could cross oceans, and a hunger for spices and gold that made the risk worth taking. Europe's planetary expansion was launched by a system, not a single sailor — and its results, conquest and colonisation of whole continents, follow from those causes through this course's next lessons.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
