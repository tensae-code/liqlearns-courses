# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Europe's states, ships and hunger for spices launched a planetary expansion.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Europe's states, ships and hunger for spices launched a planetary expansion.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A map-maker, a spice merchant and a shipwright meet in a port city: states, ships and the hunger for spices are about to change the whole planet.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A map-maker, a spice merchant and a shipwright meet in a port city: states, ships and the hunger for spices are about to change the whole planet.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
