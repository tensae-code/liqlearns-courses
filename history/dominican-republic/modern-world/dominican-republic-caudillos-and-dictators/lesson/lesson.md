# The Dominican Republic: Caudillos and Dictators

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Red and blue caudillo parties traded power while the republic staggered. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

After the Restoration, the republic was ruled by caudillos — strongmen with personal followings — organised in two rival parties the country knew by colours: the rojos (reds) and the azules (blues). Governments rose and fell in quick succession, none more famous than the six-year government that tried to hold power longer than the pattern allowed. The book's teaching is structural: while power belonged to men and colours instead of institutions, the republic staggered from revolution to revolution.
