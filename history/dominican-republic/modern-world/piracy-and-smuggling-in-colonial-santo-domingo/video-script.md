# Video script — Piracy and Smuggling in Colonial Santo Domingo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Pirates and smugglers haunted the coasts of a colony Spain could not hold tightly.

Santo Domingo was Spain's first American colony — and one it could not hold tightly. Along its long coasts, pirates and buccaneers raided shipping and settlements, while smugglers ran a secret trade with foreign merchants that the settlers themselves depended on, because Spain's strict trade rules left the colony undersupplied. Piracy and contraband are not colourful extras in this story: they show an empire whose rules were stronger on paper than on its own shores.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
