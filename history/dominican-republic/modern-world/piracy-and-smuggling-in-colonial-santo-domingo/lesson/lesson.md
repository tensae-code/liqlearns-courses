# Piracy and Smuggling in Colonial Santo Domingo

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Pirates and smugglers haunted the coasts of a colony Spain could not hold tightly. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

Santo Domingo was Spain's first American colony — and one it could not hold tightly. Along its long coasts, pirates and buccaneers raided shipping and settlements, while smugglers ran a secret trade with foreign merchants that the settlers themselves depended on, because Spain's strict trade rules left the colony undersupplied. Piracy and contraband are not colourful extras in this story: they show an empire whose rules were stronger on paper than on its own shores.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example: On the colony's far coasts, foreign smugglers trade in secret with settlers Spain's own rules have left desperate. and name the lesson's key words: Pirates, Smuggling, Santo Domingo. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
