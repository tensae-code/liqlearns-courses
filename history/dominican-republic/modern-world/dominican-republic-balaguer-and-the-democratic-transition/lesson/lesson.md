# The Dominican Republic: Balaguer and the Democratic Transition

## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 8 El período de Joaquín Balaguer: los doce años (1966–1978)

Out of April's ashes came Joaquín Balaguer. The Grade 6 book tells his twelve years, 1966–1978: a rule between authoritarianism and development — order imposed hard, public works and growth delivered, opponents persecuted. The republic was governed, but not yet free.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 8 Alternativas democráticas 1978–1996

The Grade 12 book gives the same twelve years their filed name — the neo-authoritarian phase — and their double account: a political process of controlled elections and repression, alongside an economic boom of construction, sugar and foreign money. Growth and unfreedom, again in one regime, and the book keeps both on the page.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unidad 8 Gobierno de Leonel Fernández (1996–2000) y transformaciones económicas, educativas y tecnológicas

Then the model's own contradictions piled up: inequality, dependence on foreign favour and sugar, unrest beneath the order. The Grade 12 book teaches the structural contradictions as the twelve years' undoing — a system that could deliver growth but not legitimacy, order but not consent.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unidad 4 Fase neo-autoritaria (1966–1978): el proceso político y el auge económico

In 1978 the turn came. The Grade 6 book tells the democratic alternatives of 1978–1996: elections finally change governments — when soldiers tried to halt the count in 1978, the voters' choice prevailed — and democracy survives its tests, alternation becoming habit instead of miracle.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unidad 4 Contradicciones estructurales y políticas

The Grade 12 book walks the same transition as the change and the Ten Years: in 1978 voters choose change, and a decade of alternation — governments of different parties succeeding each other by ballot — tests and slowly roots the new democratic order.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unidad 5 Transición al actual orden democrático (1978–1996): el cambio y los Diez Años

Underneath the politics, the economy itself changed nature. Tourism and services replaced sugar as the century — and the old order — ended: the Grade 12 book teaches the transition to a service economy and gives the century's structural balance sheet, an economy no longer riding on one crop.

## Sub lesson 7 — Unidad 5 Transición a la economía de servicios y balance estructural de fin de siglo

The century closed with a promise. In 1996 Leonel Fernández became president promising modernisation — economic, educational and technological transformation — and the Grade 6 book ends its national story there: a new president, a new century coming, and a democracy that had learned, at great cost, to change its rulers without guns.
