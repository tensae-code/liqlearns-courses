# The Dominican Republic: Balaguer and the Democratic Transition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Balaguer ruled twelve hard years between authoritarianism and development; then elections finally changed governments, democracy survived its tests, the economy turned to services, and a new president promised modernisation as the century closed.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Twelve years of one man's order give way, in 1978, to voters changing their government at the ballot box — and the change, tested again and again, holds.
