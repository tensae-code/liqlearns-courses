# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Revolutions in America and France proclaimed rights the old regimes could not contain. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 5 Las grandes revoluciones del siglo XVIII

In 1789 the old order cracked. The Grade 6 book teaches the great revolutions of the eighteenth century together: America and France proclaim rights — of man, of citizens — that the old regimes cannot contain. Once rights are declared universal, every throne sits on an argument against itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 10 Revolución Francesa

The Grade 10 book gives France its own telling: the kingdom rises against its king, the Bastille falls, feudal privileges are abolished in a night, and the rights of man are proclaimed to the world. The revolution then devours and remakes France for a decade — the template, and the warning, for every revolution since.
