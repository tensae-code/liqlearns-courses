## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Revolutions in America and France proclaimed rights the old regimes could not contain.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: In Paris in 1789, the people storm a royal fortress-prison and proclaim the rights of man — and every old regime in Europe trembles.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
