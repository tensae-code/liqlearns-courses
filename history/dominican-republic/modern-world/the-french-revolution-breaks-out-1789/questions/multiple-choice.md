# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Revolutions in America and France proclaimed rights the old regimes could not contain.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Revolutions in America and France proclaimed rights the old regimes could not contain.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) In Paris in 1789, the people storm a royal fortress-prison and proclaim the rights of man — and every old regime in Europe trembles.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) In Paris in 1789, the people storm a royal fortress-prison and proclaim the rights of man — and every old regime in Europe trembles.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
