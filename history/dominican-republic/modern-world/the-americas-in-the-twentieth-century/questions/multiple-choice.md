# The Americas in the Twentieth Century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Across the hemisphere, flags multiply as colonies become nations, while the United States industrialises into a world power and Canada builds its modern state.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Across the hemisphere, flags multiply as colonies become nations, while the United States industrialises into a world power and Canada builds its modern state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
