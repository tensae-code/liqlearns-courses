# The Americas in the Twentieth Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Across the hemisphere, flags multiply as colonies become nations, while the United States industrialises into a world power and Canada builds its modern state.
