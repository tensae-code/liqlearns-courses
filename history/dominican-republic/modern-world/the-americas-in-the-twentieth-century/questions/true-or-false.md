# The Americas in the Twentieth Century — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation.
