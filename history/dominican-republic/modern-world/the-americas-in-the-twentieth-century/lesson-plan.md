# Lesson plan — The Americas in the Twentieth Century

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Americas in the Twentieth Century
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — America, Empire and the World Wars (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — America, Empire and the World Wars (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 6 El inicio del siglo XX en América y el impacto de la descolonización** — a new century and a decolonising world pull the Americas into global change.
2. **Unidad 6 Estados Unidos en el siglo XX: la gran transformación** — the United States becomes an industrial and world power within one lifetime.
3. **Unidad 6 El desarrollo canadiense: Canadá en el siglo XX** — Canada grows quietly into a modern northern nation.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Across the hemisphere, flags multiply as colonies become nations, while the United States industrialises into a world power and Canada builds its modern state.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
