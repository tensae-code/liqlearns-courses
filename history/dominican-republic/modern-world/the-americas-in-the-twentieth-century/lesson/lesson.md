# The Americas in the Twentieth Century

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: A new century and a decolonising world pulled the Americas into global change — the United States becoming a world power and Canada growing into a modern northern nation. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 6 El inicio del siglo XX en América y el impacto de la descolonización

The twentieth century remade the Americas. The Grade 6 book opens with the hemisphere in a decolonising world: old empires retreat, new nations assert themselves, and the Americas are pulled into global change as actors, not spectators. The century's American story is one of weight shifting south and north at once.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 6 Estados Unidos en el siglo XX: la gran transformación

Its northern giant transformed utterly. Within one lifetime the United States became an industrial and world power — its factories, cities, armies and dollars setting terms the whole hemisphere had to answer. The book calls it the great transformation, and teaches it as the fact every American nation, the Dominican Republic included, had to navigate.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unidad 6 El desarrollo canadiense: Canadá en el siglo XX

And the other northern nation grew quietly. Canada in the twentieth century developed into a modern northern nation — federating, industrialising, urbanising — without revolution or empire. The book includes Canada deliberately: the Americas hold more than one way of becoming modern.
