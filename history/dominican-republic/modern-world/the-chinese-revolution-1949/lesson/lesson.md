# The Chinese Revolution, 1949

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

In 1949 the world's most populous nation changed hands. Mao Zedong's communists, having won a long civil war from their rural bases against the nationalist government, entered Beijing and proclaimed the People's Republic of China. The Grade 10 book teaches the Chinese Revolution as one of the century's great turnings: a quarter of humanity under a communist state, the Cold War transformed in Asia, and a model — revolution from the countryside — that movements across the decolonising world would study.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China. Give one real example: After years of civil war, a victorious army enters Beijing and, from the gate of Tiananmen, a new China is proclaimed to a sea of people. and name the lesson's key words: Chinese Revolution 1949, Mao. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
