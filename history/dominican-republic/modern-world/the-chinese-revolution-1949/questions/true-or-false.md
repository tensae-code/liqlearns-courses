# The Chinese Revolution, 1949 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China.
