# The Chinese Revolution, 1949 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) After years of civil war, a victorious army enters Beijing and, from the gate of Tiananmen, a new China is proclaimed to a sea of people.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) After years of civil war, a victorious army enters Beijing and, from the gate of Tiananmen, a new China is proclaimed to a sea of people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
