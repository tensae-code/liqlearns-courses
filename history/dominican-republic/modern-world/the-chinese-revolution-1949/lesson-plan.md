## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: After years of civil war, a victorious army enters Beijing and, from the gate of Tiananmen, a new China is proclaimed to a sea of people.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
