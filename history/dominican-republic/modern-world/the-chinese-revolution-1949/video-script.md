# Video script — The Chinese Revolution, 1949

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Mao's communists won a civil war and proclaimed a new China.

In 1949 the world's most populous nation changed hands. Mao Zedong's communists, having won a long civil war from their rural bases against the nationalist government, entered Beijing and proclaimed the People's Republic of China. The Grade 10 book teaches the Chinese Revolution as one of the century's great turnings: a quarter of humanity under a communist state, the Cold War transformed in Asia, and a model — revolution from the countryside — that movements across the decolonising world would study.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
