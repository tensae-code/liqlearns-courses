**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) For thirty-one years one man owned the country and crushed every right — rising from the occupation's constabulary, modernising contradictorily while opponents were jailed, exiled or killed, until isolation and sanctions ended with his assassination in 1961.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
