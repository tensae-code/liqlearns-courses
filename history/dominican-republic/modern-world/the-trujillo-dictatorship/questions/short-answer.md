# The Trujillo Dictatorship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** For thirty-one years one man owned the country and crushed every right — rising from the occupation's constabulary, modernising contradictorily while opponents were jailed, exiled or killed, until isolation and sanctions ended with his assassination in 1961.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** One man's portrait hangs in every office for thirty-one years; roads and monuments rise while prisons fill — until, in 1961, shots on a night road end the regime.
