# Lesson plan — The Trujillo Dictatorship

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Trujillo Dictatorship
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Dictatorship, Revolution and the Democratic Transition (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** For thirty-one years one man owned the country and crushed every right — rising from the occupation's constabulary, modernising contradictorily while opponents were jailed, exiled or killed, until isolation and sanctions ended with his assassination in 1961.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Dictatorship, Revolution and the Democratic Transition (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 7 Dictadura de Rafael Leónidas Trujillo: vulneración de derechos humanos (1930–1961)** — for thirty-one years one man owns the country and crushes every right.
2. **Unidad 2 La dictadura de Trujillo: surgimiento y consolidación** — the occupation's constabulary chief seizes the state in 1930 and builds a personal regime.
3. **Unidad 2 Modernización contradictoria y oposición al régimen** — roads and monuments rise while opponents are jailed, exiled or killed.
4. **Unidad 2 Crisis y muerte de Trujillo** — isolated and sanctioned, the regime ends with Trujillo's assassination in 1961.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
