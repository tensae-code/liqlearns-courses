# The End of the Cold War

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: The Soviet bloc broke apart, and a new world order began. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The Cold War ended not with a war but with a collapse. Between 1989 and 1991 the Soviet bloc disintegrated: the Berlin Wall fell to its own citizens, Eastern Europe's regimes gave way, and the Soviet Union itself broke into separate republics. The Grade 10 book closes its course here — the disintegration of the Soviet bloc and the foundations of the contemporary world: one superpower left standing, old maps redrawn, and the world the Dominican Republic's own democratic transition, told in this course's last level, was happening inside of.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Soviet bloc broke apart, and a new world order began. Give one real example: A wall falls to hammers and crowds in Berlin, flags of new republics rise across Eastern Europe, and the map of forty years is redrawn in two. and name the lesson's key words: End of the Cold War, Soviet bloc disintegration. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
