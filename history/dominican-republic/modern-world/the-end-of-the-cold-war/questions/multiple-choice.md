# The End of the Cold War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The Soviet bloc broke apart, and a new world order began.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The Soviet bloc broke apart, and a new world order began.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A wall falls to hammers and crowds in Berlin, flags of new republics rise across Eastern Europe, and the map of forty years is redrawn in two.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A wall falls to hammers and crowds in Berlin, flags of new republics rise across Eastern Europe, and the map of forty years is redrawn in two.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
