# The End of the Cold War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A wall falls to hammers and crowds in Berlin, flags of new republics rise across Eastern Europe, and the map of forty years is redrawn in two.
