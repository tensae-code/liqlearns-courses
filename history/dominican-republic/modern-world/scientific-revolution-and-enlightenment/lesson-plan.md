**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Europe Transformed: Feudalism to Revolutions (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Enlightenment ideas crossed the Atlantic and began to dissolve the old order — while kings tried to modernise their empires from above.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: In a salon, thinkers argue that reason should govern society — and across the ocean, American readers take the argument seriously.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
