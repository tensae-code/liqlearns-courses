# Video script — The Scientific Revolution and the Enlightenment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Enlightenment ideas crossed the Atlantic and began to dissolve the old order — while kings tried to modernise their empires from above.

The eighteenth century thought new thoughts. The Grade 6 book teaches the Enlightenment in America: ideas of reason, rights and popular sovereignty cross the Atlantic and start to dissolve the old order in the colonies. An idea in a Paris salon becomes, an ocean away, a argument against kings — the book traces that voyage deliberately.
The old order tried to answer from above. Monarchs across Europe attempted reforms — modernising armies, schools, taxes and empires — before change could come from below. The Grade 6 book teaches these monarchical reforms as the Enlightenment's shadow: enlightened despotism, reforming everything except who holds power.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
