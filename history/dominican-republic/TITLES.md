# History — Dominican Republic course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is the
Dominican Republic's own — built from its MINERD "Libro
Abierto" Ciencias Sociales books (Spanish). Inside the course
the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below sit inside Levels 1–6, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live
only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': heritage and the
historian's craft first; humanity before writing and the
first civilisations; Europe transformed; America, empire
and the world wars; then the national story in two great
arcs — annexation to occupation, and dictatorship to the
democratic transition. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
