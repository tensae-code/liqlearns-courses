# Lesson plan — Dating the Past

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Dating the Past
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage and the Historian's Craft (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History is cut into ages and periods so it can be studied — and America and the Dominican Republic have period lines of their own.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage and the Historian's Craft (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History is cut into ages and periods so it can be studied — and America and the Dominican Republic have period lines of their own.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A timeline on the classroom wall cuts the whole past into ages, with America's and the Dominican Republic's own periods marked on it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
