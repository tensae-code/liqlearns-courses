## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 4 Edades de la historia; períodos de la historia de América y dominicana

No one can hold the whole past at once, so historians cut it into ages. The Grade 5 book teaches the great ages of history — and then does something important: it gives America, and the Dominican Republic itself, their own period lines, because a land's turning points are its own. Dating the past is not decoration; it is how the story gets chapters.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 5 Periodización de la Historia

The Grade 10 book names the craft plainly: periodisation. The past is measured and cut into periods so it can be studied — each period a stretch with its own character, each boundary a turning point worth arguing about. Learn the cuts and the whole of history becomes a map you can navigate instead of a flood you drown in.
