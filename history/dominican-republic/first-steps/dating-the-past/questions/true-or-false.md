# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   History is cut into ages and periods so it can be studied — and America and the Dominican Republic have period lines of their own.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: History is cut into ages and periods so it can be studied — and America and the Dominican Republic have period lines of their own.
