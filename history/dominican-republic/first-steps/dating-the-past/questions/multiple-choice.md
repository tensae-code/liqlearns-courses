# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) History is cut into ages and periods so it can be studied — and America and the Dominican Republic have period lines of their own.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) History is cut into ages and periods so it can be studied — and America and the Dominican Republic have period lines of their own.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A timeline on the classroom wall cuts the whole past into ages, with America's and the Dominican Republic's own periods marked on it.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A timeline on the classroom wall cuts the whole past into ages, with America's and the Dominican Republic's own periods marked on it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
