# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Before telling any of it, the course asks what history itself is.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Before telling any of it, the course asks what history itself is.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A class stops before the first story and asks: what is this thing we are about to study — and how do we know it?  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A class stops before the first story and asks: what is this thing we are about to study — and how do we know it?** — That is what this lesson teaches.
