# What History Is

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the Dominican Republic that connects to this lesson's story: Before telling any of it, the course asks what history itself is. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The Grade 10 course does not begin with a king or a battle. It begins with a question: what is history itself? Unit 5 defines history and traces its beginnings as a study — how humans first recorded and questioned their own past. The lesson's discipline is the course's foundation: a science must know what it is before it tells anything, and so must its students.
