1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Heritage places are old places that still stand where the story happened — proof in stone that the past was real.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: An old fort or colonial street in the child's own country still stands where the events of the story took place.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
