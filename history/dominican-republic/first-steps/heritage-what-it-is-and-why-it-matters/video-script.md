# Video script — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Heritage places are old places that still stand where the story happened — proof in stone that the past was real.

In the child's own country there are places the books single out: the heritage places, where the nation's history did not just pass by but happened — a landing, a founding, a battle, a first church, a first street. The buildings and stones are still standing, so the story has an address. Heritage matters because it lets a child check the story with their own eyes: the past is not only told, it can be visited.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
