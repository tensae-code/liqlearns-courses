# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Heritage places are old places that still stand where the story happened — proof in stone that the past was real.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Heritage places are old places that still stand where the story happened — proof in stone that the past was real.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) An old fort or colonial street in the child's own country still stands where the events of the story took place.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) An old fort or colonial street in the child's own country still stands where the events of the story took place.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
