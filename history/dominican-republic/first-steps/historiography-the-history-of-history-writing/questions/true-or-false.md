# Historiography: The History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  From chronicles to scientific history, the way of writing the past keeps changing.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: From chronicles to scientific history, the way of writing the past keeps changing.
