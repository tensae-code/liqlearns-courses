# Historiography: The History of History Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) From chronicles to scientific history, the way of writing the past keeps changing.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) From chronicles to scientific history, the way of writing the past keeps changing.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A medieval chronicler lists kings year by year; a modern historian questions documents in an archive — the same past, written two different ways.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A medieval chronicler lists kings year by year; a modern historian questions documents in an archive — the same past, written two different ways.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
