# Historiography: The History of History Writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** From chronicles to scientific history, the way of writing the past keeps changing.

**2.**  Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A medieval chronicler lists kings year by year; a modern historian questions documents in an archive — the same past, written two different ways.
