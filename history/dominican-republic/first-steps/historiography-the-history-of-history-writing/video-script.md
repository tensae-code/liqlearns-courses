# Video script — Historiography: The History of History Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
From chronicles to scientific history, the way of writing the past keeps changing.

History has a history. After defining the science, the Grade 10 book follows its later evolution: from chronicles that listed events year by year, through great narrative histories, to scientific history that criticises sources and argues from evidence. The way of writing the past keeps changing because each age asks the past different questions. To know historiography is to read every history book — including this course's own books — with wiser eyes.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the Dominican Republic.
