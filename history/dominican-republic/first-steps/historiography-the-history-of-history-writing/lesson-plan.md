## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: From chronicles to scientific history, the way of writing the past keeps changing.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A medieval chronicler lists kings year by year; a modern historian questions documents in an archive — the same past, written two different ways.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.
