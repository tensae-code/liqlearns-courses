# Lesson plan — Who Studies Prehistory?

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Who Studies Prehistory?
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Before Writing and the First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Before Writing and the First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: With no writings at all, a chipped stone tool still tells us someone was here, and how they lived.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in the Dominican Republic, as the books themselves work.

- Thinks no writing means nothing can be known -> Stones, bones and tools are evidence too; prehistory is read from things, not pages.
- Go back to the one thing: The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
