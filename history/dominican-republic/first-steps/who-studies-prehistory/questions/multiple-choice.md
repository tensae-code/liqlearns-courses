# Who Studies Prehistory? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) With no writings at all, a chipped stone tool still tells us someone was here, and how they lived.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) With no writings at all, a chipped stone tool still tells us someone was here, and how they lived.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
