# Who Studies Prehistory? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces.
