# Who Studies Prehistory? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The long age before writing is studied from the tools and traces people left behind, and divided into periods by those traces.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** With no writings at all, a chipped stone tool still tells us someone was here, and how they lived.
