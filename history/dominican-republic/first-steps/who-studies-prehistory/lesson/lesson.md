## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 4 Estudio del pasado: los períodos de la prehistoria

Most of the human story was lived before anyone wrote a word. The Grade 5 book teaches that this long age — prehistory — is not a blank: it is divided into periods by the tools people left behind, stone giving way to metal, each material marking a stage in what hands could do. The people who study it read things the way other historians read pages.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 6 Los prolongados inicios de la humanidad: la Prehistoria

The Grade 10 book calls prehistory the prolonged beginnings of humanity — the longest chapter of all, leaving no writing, only stones and bones. To study it is to accept a harder kind of evidence: no names, no dates written down, only traces in the ground. Yet from those traces the whole first story of humankind is rebuilt, period by period.
