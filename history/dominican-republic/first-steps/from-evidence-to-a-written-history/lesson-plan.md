# Lesson plan — From Evidence to a Written History

**Course:** History — Dominican Republic · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** From Evidence to a Written History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage and the Historian's Craft (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before the past can be told, its traces must be interrogated: sources are questioned and criticised, and helper sciences are called in, before a claim is believed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage and the Historian's Craft (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
