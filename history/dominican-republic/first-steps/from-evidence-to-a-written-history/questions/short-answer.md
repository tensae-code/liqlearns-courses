# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Before the past can be told, its traces must be interrogated: sources are questioned and criticised, and helper sciences are called in, before a claim is believed.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** A class learns to ask of an old document: who made it, when, and can it be trusted?
