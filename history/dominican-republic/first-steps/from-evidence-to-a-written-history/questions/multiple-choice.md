# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Before the past can be told, its traces must be interrogated: sources are questioned and criticised, and helper sciences are called in, before a claim is believed.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Before the past can be told, its traces must be interrogated: sources are questioned and criticised, and helper sciences are called in, before a claim is believed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) A class learns to ask of an old document: who made it, when, and can it be trusted?  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) A class learns to ask of an old document: who made it, when, and can it be trusted?** — That is what this lesson teaches.
