# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Dating the Past** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Before writing (3200 BC) come the Stone and Metal ages in a dated table; after it, ancient, medieval, modern and contemporary ages are bounded by 476, 1492 and the French Revolution. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
