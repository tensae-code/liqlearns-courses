# Dating the Past — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — before writing (3200 BC) come the Stone and Metal ages in a dated table; after it, ancient, medieval, modern and contemporary ages are bounded by 476, 1492 and the French Revolution. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
