**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 1: The concept of history and its stages”?
a) History studies man's past to understand his present and plan his future; nations keep it in chronicles, oral tales and folk stories to raise their children on it.  b) After Abu Bakr and Umar are studied, the lesson itself asks who the two caliphs after them were — the pair who held the community together and then stretched it across Persia and the Levant.  c) The third clash model is a trade in people — its causes and its results for Africa.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
