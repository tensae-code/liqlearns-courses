# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **What History Is** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 1: The concept of history and its stages

History studies man's past to understand his present and plan his future; nations keep it in chronicles, oral tales and folk stories to raise their children on it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
