# History — Mauritania course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Mauritania's own — built from Mauritania's Ministry of Education and Reform of the Education System / Institut Pédagogique National (IPN) books (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': first steps and the ancient civilisations, Arabia and the caliphates, Mauritania and the Sudan kingdoms, the emirates to independence, Europe transformed and the world wars, and the world after 1945. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
