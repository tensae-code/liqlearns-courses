# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The Renaissance's great figures and humanism”?
a) Dante (d.1321) writes in his own language; patrons shelter the revival, and humanism rescues medieval man from the belief that enjoying life is sin.  b) Lenin's October 1917 revolution overthrows the provisional government; Stalin, born 1879 in Georgia, succeeds — and the Soviet Union is proclaimed.  c) History studies man's past to understand his present and plan his future; nations keep it in chronicles, oral tales and folk stories to raise their children on it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Dante (d.1321) writes in his own language; patrons shelter the revival, and humanism rescues medieval man from the belief that enjoying life is sin.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
