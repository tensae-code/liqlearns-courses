# The Industrial Revolution: Its Two Stages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 8: The Industrial Revolution — foundations, stages, results”?
a) Political stability and accelerating invention crown the Renaissance's scientific development; the factory system replaces scattered home work.  b) The victors split into two camps and fight without fighting.  c) Farming and herding begin in the Neolithic (7000–2000 BC); as the Sahara dries, Garamantes, Bafour and Berbers migrate in — Sanhaja Berbers in the 3rd century AD settle the ponds and wadis, the Bafour hold Adrar from Azuggi behind trained dogs until the Almoravids come, Phoenician sailors trade down the Atlantic coast from 1500 BC, and Sudanic groups hold the south.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Political stability and accelerating invention crown the Renaissance's scientific development; the factory system replaces scattered home work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The Industrial Revolution”?
a) The factory age begins in Britain.  b) How Palestine became a question the whole Arab world carries.  c) The Abbasids overthrow the Umayyads, build Baghdad's caliphate and hold it, shrinking, for five centuries until the Mongol end.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The factory age begins in Britain.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
