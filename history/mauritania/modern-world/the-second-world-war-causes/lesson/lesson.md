## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 13: The Second World War 1939–1945 — causes

Factories glutted with goods and rising European unemployment push states toward war to sell their production. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
