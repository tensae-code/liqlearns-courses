# The Second World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: The Second World War 1939–1945 — causes”?
a) Factories glutted with goods and rising European unemployment push states toward war to sell their production.  b) How Palestine became a question the whole Arab world carries.  c) The Abbasids overthrow the Umayyads, build Baghdad's caliphate and hold it, shrinking, for five centuries until the Mongol end.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Factories glutted with goods and rising European unemployment push states toward war to sell their production.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 15: The Second World War 1939–1945 — causes”?
a) The dictatorships and the failed peace drive Europe back to war.  b) The course ends by characterising European civilisation as it once characterised the Islamic one.  c) Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The dictatorships and the failed peace drive Europe back to war.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
