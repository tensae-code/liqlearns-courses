# The Second World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 13: The Second World War 1939–1945 — causes”.
**Answer: False** — Factories glutted with goods and rising European unemployment push states toward war to sell their production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The dictatorships and the failed peace drive Europe back to war.
**Answer: True**
