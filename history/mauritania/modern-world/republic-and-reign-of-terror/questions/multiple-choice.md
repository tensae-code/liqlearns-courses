# Republic and Reign of Terror — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 7: The revolution's later phases”?
a) In the third phase (July 1794–November 1799) the revolutionary tide retreats, the moderate bourgeoisie rules, Robespierre's opponent is executed, and absolute monarchy gives way to a republic and a new democratic order.  b) Out of WWII's ruins the world builds one house for all nations.  c) Greece completes the lesson's trio of dead civilisations whose remains still witness their greatness, against the living Islamic, Western, Chinese, Japanese and Indian ones.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In the third phase (July 1794–November 1799) the revolutionary tide retreats, the moderate bourgeoisie rules, Robespierre's opponent is executed, and absolute monarchy gives way to a republic and a new democratic order.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4: The French Revolution — its course”?
a) Republic, terror and reaction follow the outbreak.  b) Ghana wins freedom at the ballot and leads the continent; Angola wins it by a long armed struggle.  c) A hereditary caliphate rises at Damascus, expands to its widest bounds and falls within a century.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Republic, terror and reaction follow the outbreak.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
