# Republic and Reign of Terror — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 7: The revolution's later phases”.
**Answer: False** — In the third phase (July 1794–November 1799) the revolutionary tide retreats, the moderate bourgeoisie rules, Robespierre's opponent is executed, and absolute monarchy gives way to a republic and a new democratic order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Republic, terror and reaction follow the outbreak.
**Answer: True**
