# The American Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 3: The independence of the United States of America”?
a) Britain's colonies break away and found a republic.  b) Factories glutted with goods and rising European unemployment push states toward war to sell their production.  c) Sumerians from the Caucasus, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians people the two rivers in turn; Ur, Lagash and Nippur rule, Gudea rebuilds and Ur-Nammu raises his tower.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Britain's colonies break away and found a republic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 3: The independence of the United States of America”?
a) Britain's colonies break away and found a republic.  b) India's mass movement ends the Raj and partitions the subcontinent.  c) After Abu Bakr and Umar are studied, the lesson itself asks who the two caliphs after them were — the pair who held the community together and then stretched it across Persia and the Levant.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Britain's colonies break away and found a republic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
