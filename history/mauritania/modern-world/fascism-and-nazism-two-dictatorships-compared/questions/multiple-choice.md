# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: The dictatorial regimes in Europe”?
a) Fascism grows from the 19th century's great transformations; Hitler takes over the Nazi party in 1920 and proclaims his 25-point programme.  b) Ghana wins freedom at the ballot and leads the continent; Angola wins it by a long armed struggle.  c) A hereditary caliphate rises at Damascus, expands to its widest bounds and falls within a century.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Fascism grows from the 19th century's great transformations; Hitler takes over the Nazi party in 1920 and proclaims his 25-point programme.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The crisis's political children — Mussolini's fascism and Hitler's Nazism compared.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
