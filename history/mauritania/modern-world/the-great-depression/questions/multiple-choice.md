# The Great Depression — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: The economic crisis of 1929”?
a) The crash spreads from America to the world.  b) Algeria's war becomes French Africa's turning point.  c) Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD).  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The crash spreads from America to the world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: The economic crisis of 1929”?
a) The crash spreads from America to the world.  b) Mauritania, Senegal and Mali share one river — the book ends with the organisation that dams and divides it.  c) The first model of the Islamic–Western clash — two centuries of Frankish wars in the Levant.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The crash spreads from America to the world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
