# The First World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 10: The First World War 1914–1918”?
a) Colonial rivalry over colonies and seas divides Europe into the Triple Alliance (Austria-Hungary, Germany, Italy, the Ottomans) and the Triple Entente (France, Britain, Russia).  b) Why colonised peoples everywhere rose at once after 1945.  c) The book opens with what a Muslim believes — the faith whose history the next six lessons then tell.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Colonial rivalry over colonies and seas divides Europe into the Triple Alliance (Austria-Hungary, Germany, Italy, the Ottomans) and the Triple Entente (France, Britain, Russia).** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 10: The First World War”?
a) Alliance and rivalry ignite Europe in 1914.  b) The OAU's commitments are honoured and its strategic frameworks carried into the Union that replaces it.  c) A rival caliphate is born in North Africa, takes Egypt and builds Cairo, then fades.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Alliance and rivalry ignite Europe in 1914.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
