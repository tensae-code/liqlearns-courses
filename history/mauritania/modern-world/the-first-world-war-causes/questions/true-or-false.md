# The First World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 10: The First World War 1914–1918”.
**Answer: False** — Colonial rivalry over colonies and seas divides Europe into the Triple Alliance (Austria-Hungary, Germany, Italy, the Ottomans) and the Triple Entente (France, Britain, Russia).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Alliance and rivalry ignite Europe in 1914.
**Answer: True**
