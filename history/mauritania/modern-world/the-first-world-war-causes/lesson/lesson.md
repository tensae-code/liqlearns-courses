## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 10: The First World War 1914–1918

Colonial rivalry over colonies and seas divides Europe into the Triple Alliance (Austria-Hungary, Germany, Italy, the Ottomans) and the Triple Entente (France, Britain, Russia). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns.
