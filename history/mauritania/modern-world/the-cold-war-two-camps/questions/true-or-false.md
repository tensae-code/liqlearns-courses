# The Cold War: Two Camps — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 6: The Cold War”.
**Answer: False** — The victors split into two camps and fight without fighting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The wartime allies sort themselves into an Eastern and a Western camp.
**Answer: True**
