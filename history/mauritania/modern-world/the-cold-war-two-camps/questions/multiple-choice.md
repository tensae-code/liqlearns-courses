# The Cold War: Two Camps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 6: The Cold War”?
a) The victors split into two camps and fight without fighting.  b) The Muslim world's own league takes its place beside the Arab and African ones.  c) The civilisation Islam built is measured by its science — scholars, schools and learning.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The victors split into two camps and fight without fighting.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 2: The formation of the two blocs”?
a) The wartime allies sort themselves into an Eastern and a Western camp.  b) Ibn Khaldun and Will Durant are quoted; no civilisation rises without natural, economic, social, political and cultural foundations, and writing is the cultural foundation that preserves them all.  c) Aoudaghost, the Sanhaja trading city where desert caravans meet, has its own dossier between Ghana and Takrur.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The wartime allies sort themselves into an Eastern and a Western camp.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
