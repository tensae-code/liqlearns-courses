## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 6: The Cold War** — The victors split into two camps and fight without fighting.
2. **Lesson 2: The formation of the two blocs** — The wartime allies sort themselves into an Eastern and a Western camp.
3. **Lesson 3: The Cold War** — The two camps confront each other across a divided world without a direct war.
4. **Lesson 2: The ideological patterns and the world's division into two camps** — Capitalism and communism divide the world between them.
5. **Lesson 3: The Cold War** — The camps' long confrontation — its crises and its rules.
