# The United Nations Founded

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The United Nations Founded** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 5: The United Nations organisation

Out of WWII's ruins the world builds one house for all nations. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
