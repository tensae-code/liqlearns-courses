# The United Nations Founded — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The United Nations organisation”?
a) Out of WWII's ruins the world builds one house for all nations.  b) The OAU's commitments are honoured and its strategic frameworks carried into the Union that replaces it.  c) A rival caliphate is born in North Africa, takes Egypt and builds Cairo, then fades.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Out of WWII's ruins the world builds one house for all nations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 1: The United Nations”?
a) The world organisation is the year's doorway — its founding, organs and purposes.  b) Before writing (3200 BC) come the Stone and Metal ages in a dated table; after it, ancient, medieval, modern and contemporary ages are bounded by 476, 1492 and the French Revolution.  c) Ghana, medieval black Africa's first kingdom, grows rich where the salt and gold roads cross, its king ruling through ministers, interpreters and princes.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The world organisation is the year's doorway — its founding, organs and purposes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
