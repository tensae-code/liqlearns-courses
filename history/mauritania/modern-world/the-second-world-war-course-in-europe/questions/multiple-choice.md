# The Second World War: Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: The Second World War — course and results”?
a) America enters after Pearl Harbor, France is liberated, and the map of Europe after the war closes the book.  b) One current wants a regional unity — Greater Syria or the Fertile Crescent — another a league of all Arab states; the League is what gets built.  c) One Umayyad survivor refounds his dynasty at the far end of the Islamic world, and al-Andalus rises and fragments.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) America enters after Pearl Harbor, France is liberated, and the map of Europe after the war closes the book.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 15: The Second World War — course”?
a) 1939–1945 across Europe to the Axis collapse.  b) History studies man's past to understand his present and plan his future; nations keep it in chronicles, oral tales and folk stories to raise their children on it.  c) An Islamic reform movement among the Sanhaja becomes an empire spanning the western Sahara, the Maghreb and al-Andalus, then falls as its sultans turn to pleasure, its army falters and the Almohad movement rises.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) 1939–1945 across Europe to the Axis collapse.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
