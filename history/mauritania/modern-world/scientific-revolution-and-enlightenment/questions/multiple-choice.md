# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 6: The intellectual movement in 18th-century Europe”?
a) Enlightenment philosophers, Locke "father of liberalism" (1632–1704) among them, diagnose politics, economy and religion — and prepare religious reform and revolution.  b) Fascism grows from the 19th century's great transformations; Hitler takes over the Nazi party in 1920 and proclaims his 25-point programme.  c) Ibn Khaldun and Will Durant are quoted; no civilisation rises without natural, economic, social, political and cultural foundations, and writing is the cultural foundation that preserves them all.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Enlightenment philosophers, Locke "father of liberalism" (1632–1704) among them, diagnose politics, economy and religion — and prepare religious reform and revolution.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
