## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dossier 16: The stages of independence in Mauritania** — A photograph of the National Guard's camel corps at the proclamation frames the question — what let the parties press the coloniser harder until independence came.
2. **Dossier 17: Mauritania after independence** — The new state's first chapter is itself a school competition topic — summarise Mauritania after independence.
3. **Lesson 14: The stages of Mauritania's independence** — Step by step — autonomy, referendum, proclamation — the colony becomes a state.
4. **Lesson 15: The independence of Mauritania** — 28 November 1960 — the independence the whole book has been walking toward is proclaimed.
5. **Lesson 4: The liberation movement in Mauritania 1946–1960** — Fourteen years from the first postwar parties to independence — the dated road 1946 to 1960.
6. **Lesson 8: The liberation movement in Mauritania** — Mauritania's own movement takes its place in the continental wave.
7. **Lesson 8: The liberation movements in Mauritania** — Mauritania's own liberation is studied between Algeria's and black Africa's.
