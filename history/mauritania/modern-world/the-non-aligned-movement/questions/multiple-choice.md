Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 10: The Non-Aligned Movement”?
a) States that refuse both Cold War camps build a third grouping of their own.  b) Farming and herding begin in the Neolithic (7000–2000 BC); as the Sahara dries, Garamantes, Bafour and Berbers migrate in — Sanhaja Berbers in the 3rd century AD settle the ponds and wadis, the Bafour hold Adrar from Azuggi behind trained dogs until the Almoravids come, Phoenician sailors trade down the Atlantic coast from 1500 BC, and Sudanic groups hold the south.  c) Caravans bind Bilad al-Sudan to North Africa; Taoudenni and Sebkha salt go south, the pilgrim road through Aoudaghost enriches Timbuktu, Jenne and Gao.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
