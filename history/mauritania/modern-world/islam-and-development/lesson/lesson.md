# Islam and Development

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islam and Development** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The primary course closes its Islamic half by asking what Islam says to building and developing a society today. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
