**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The geographical discoveries”?
a) Arab taxes on eastern goods push Europeans to seek a direct road to India; printing, Marco Polo's book, maps and the astrolabe arm the search.  b) A second industrial revolution bursts in late-19th-century America; textiles improve, the steam train appears, and economic life is remade.  c) Mauritania, Senegal and Mali share one river — the book ends with the organisation that dams and divides it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Arab taxes on eastern goods push Europeans to seek a direct road to India; printing, Marco Polo's book, maps and the astrolabe arm the search.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
