# The New World Order after the Cold War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4: The new world order”?
a) One camp falls, and a single-power order is proclaimed.  b) States that refuse both Cold War camps build a third grouping of their own.  c) The primary course closes its Islamic half by asking what Islam says to building and developing a society today.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) One camp falls, and a single-power order is proclaimed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4: The new world order”?
a) One camp falls, and a single-power order is proclaimed.  b) Sumerians from the Caucasus, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians people the two rivers in turn; Ur, Lagash and Nippur rule, Gudea rebuilds and Ur-Nammu raises his tower.  c) Takrur is the name of a group of Black tribes who found a kingdom in the Senegal river country south of the desert.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) One camp falls, and a single-power order is proclaimed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
