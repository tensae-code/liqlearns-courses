# The Arab League — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 10: The Arab League”?
a) One current wants a regional unity — Greater Syria or the Fertile Crescent — another a league of all Arab states; the League is what gets built.  b) Sumerians from the Caucasus, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians people the two rivers in turn; Ur, Lagash and Nippur rule, Gudea rebuilds and Ur-Nammu raises his tower.  c) Takrur is the name of a group of Black tribes who found a kingdom in the Senegal river country south of the desert.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) One current wants a regional unity — Greater Syria or the Fertile Crescent — another a league of all Arab states; the League is what gets built.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
