Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 6: The liberation movement in the Indian subcontinent”?
a) India's mass movement ends the Raj and partitions the subcontinent.  b) The course ends by characterising European civilisation as it once characterised the Islamic one.  c) Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
