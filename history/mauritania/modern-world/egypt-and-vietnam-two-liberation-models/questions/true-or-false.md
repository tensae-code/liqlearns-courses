# Egypt and Vietnam: Two Liberation Models — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 8: Liberation models — Egypt and Vietnam”.
**Answer: False** — Egypt negotiates and nationalises; Vietnam fights — its southern revolutionary front, the Viet Cong of 1960, takes the Vietnamese Liberation Army's path to unity even by force.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two models side by side — Egypt's road and Vietnam's armed road.
**Answer: True**
