# Egypt and Vietnam: Two Liberation Models — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 8: Liberation models — Egypt and Vietnam”?
a) Egypt negotiates and nationalises; Vietnam fights — its southern revolutionary front, the Viet Cong of 1960, takes the Vietnamese Liberation Army's path to unity even by force.  b) Borders, economies and armies inherited from empire trouble the new states.  c) The last great Islamic sultanate expands over three continents and declines over centuries.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Egypt negotiates and nationalises; Vietnam fights — its southern revolutionary front, the Viet Cong of 1960, takes the Vietnamese Liberation Army's path to unity even by force.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Two models side by side — Egypt's road and Vietnam's armed road.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
