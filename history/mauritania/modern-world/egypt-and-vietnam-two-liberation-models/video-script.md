# Video script — Egypt and Vietnam: Two Liberation Models

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Egypt negotiates and nationalises; Vietnam fights — its southern revolutionary front, the Viet Cong of 1960, takes the Vietnamese Liberation Army's path to unity even by force. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
