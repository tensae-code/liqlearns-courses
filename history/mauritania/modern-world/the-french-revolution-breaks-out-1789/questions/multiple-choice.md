# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 7: The French Revolution 1789–1799”?
a) Nobles and clergy hold power tax-free; Turgot and Necker fail to fix the finances; Mirabeau answers the court — "we are here by the will of the people".  b) America enters after Pearl Harbor, France is liberated, and the map of Europe after the war closes the book.  c) Egypt is Mesopotamia's pair in the lesson — the Nile valley civilisation the class must place on the map beside the two rivers.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Nobles and clergy hold power tax-free; Turgot and Necker fail to fix the finances; Mirabeau answers the court — "we are here by the will of the people".** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4: The French Revolution”?
a) The old order collapses in 1789.  b) Algeria's war becomes French Africa's turning point.  c) Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD).  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The old order collapses in 1789.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
