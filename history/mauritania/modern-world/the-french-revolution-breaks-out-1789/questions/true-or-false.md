# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 7: The French Revolution 1789–1799”.
**Answer: False** — Nobles and clergy hold power tax-free; Turgot and Necker fail to fix the finances; Mirabeau answers the court — "we are here by the will of the people".

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The old order collapses in 1789.
**Answer: True**
