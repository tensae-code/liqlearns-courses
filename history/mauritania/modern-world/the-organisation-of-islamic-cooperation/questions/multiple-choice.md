# The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation”?
a) The Muslim world's own league takes its place beside the Arab and African ones.  b) Greece completes the lesson's trio of dead civilisations whose remains still witness their greatness, against the living Islamic, Western, Chinese, Japanese and Indian ones.  c) Songhai's order is so effective a child can go to market unmolested; then on 13 May 1591 Judar Pasha enters Gao, Askia Ishaq flees, and the empire collapses.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Muslim world's own league takes its place beside the Arab and African ones.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation”?
a) The Muslim-majority states organise together.  b) A hereditary caliphate rises at Damascus, expands to its widest bounds and falls within a century.  c) The coloniser exploits the colony's resources for himself and invites foreign capital to invade its economy.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Muslim-majority states organise together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
