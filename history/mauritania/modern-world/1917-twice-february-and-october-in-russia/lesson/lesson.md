# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **1917 Twice: February and October in Russia** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 11: The Bolshevik revolution and the birth of the USSR

Lenin's October 1917 revolution overthrows the provisional government; Stalin, born 1879 in Georgia, succeeds — and the Soviet Union is proclaimed. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
