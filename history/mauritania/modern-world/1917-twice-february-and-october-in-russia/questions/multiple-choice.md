# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 11: The Bolshevik revolution and the birth of the USSR”?
a) Lenin's October 1917 revolution overthrows the provisional government; Stalin, born 1879 in Georgia, succeeds — and the Soviet Union is proclaimed.  b) India's mass movement ends the Raj and partitions the subcontinent.  c) After Abu Bakr and Umar are studied, the lesson itself asks who the two caliphs after them were — the pair who held the community together and then stretched it across Persia and the Levant.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Lenin's October 1917 revolution overthrows the provisional government; Stalin, born 1879 in Georgia, succeeds — and the Soviet Union is proclaimed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
