# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 11: The Bolshevik revolution and the birth of the USSR”.
**Answer: False** — Lenin's October 1917 revolution overthrows the provisional government; Stalin, born 1879 in Georgia, succeeds — and the Soviet Union is proclaimed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Russia's revolution remakes the war's world.
**Answer: True**
