# History — Mauritania course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Mauritania inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Mauritania's books in hand run from the Grade 5 and Grade 6 primary books (IPN, Arabic) through the preparatory years (Grades 7–10) to the secondary years (Grades 11–13, Arts stream) — ten books, nine student books and one teacher guide. The placements file's book order is the teaching order — earliest books first. Inside that order, the ladder below groups the books the way Mauritania's own story divides in them: first steps and the ancient civilisations, Arabia and the caliphates, Mauritania and the Sudan kingdoms, the emirates to independence, Europe transformed and the world wars, and the world after 1945. Each lesson sits at the level of the first book that teaches it. Levels, NOT grades: the grade numbers live only in ABOUT.md and sources.md. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Mauritania's own Ministry of Education and Reform of the Education System / Institut Pédagogique National (IPN) books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps and the Ancient Civilisations** — the historian's craft first — what history is and how the past is dated and divided — then the ancient civilisations the books set side by side (Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece) and ancient Mauritania itself: the peoples of the western Sahara, their farming, herding, rock art and chiefs — 7 lessons.
2. **Arabia, Islam Rises and the Caliphates** — Arabia before Islam, the Islamic creed the primary course opens with, the Prophet's life and the founding of the Islamic state, the Rashidun caliphs pair by pair, the Umayyads at Damascus and in al-Andalus, the Abbasids of Baghdad, the Fatimids of North Africa, Islamic science and civilisation, Islam and development, the Crusades, and the Ottoman state — 13 lessons.
3. **Mauritania and the Sudan Kingdoms: Islam, the Almoravids and the Sahara Trade** — Islam's peaceful entry into Mauritania, the Almoravid empire born on Mauritania's own soil, the Sudanese empires — Ghana, Mali and Songhai — with Aoudaghost and Takrur, the caravan trade in salt and gold that bound the desert to the Sudan, and Mauritania's historic mosques — 9 lessons.
4. **The Emirates, Colonisation, Resistance and Independence** — the Hassaniya Arabs enter Mauritania and the emirates — Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant — rule it; the Atlantic slave trade, Europe's colonial expansion and its forms of rule, resisters Omar Mukhtar and al-Hajj Umar, France's colonisation of Mauritania, the armed resistance and the political struggle, independence on 28 November 1960, and Mauritania after independence — 11 lessons.
5. **Europe Transformed and the World Wars** — the sea discoveries, Columbus, the Renaissance and its great figures, England's parliamentary system, the Enlightenment, American independence, the French Revolution and its later phases, the Industrial Revolution and its effects, then the First World War, the Russian revolution of 1917, the 1929 crisis, and fascism and Nazism — 16 lessons.
6. **The World after 1945: Liberation, the Cold War and the Organisations** — the Second World War, the United Nations, the Cold War's two camps and the new world order, why Africa and Asia rose against empire, the liberation models of Egypt and Vietnam, India's road, Algeria's war, Ghana and Angola, Palestine's question, and the bodies Mauritania belongs to — the Arab League, the African Union, the OIC, the Non-Aligned Movement and the Senegal River Organisation — closing with the problems of the new states and European civilisation characterised — 18 lessons.

**Totals: 74 lessons** (Level 1: 7 · Level 2: 13 · Level 3: 9 · Level 4: 11 · Level 5: 16 · Level 6: 18 — 154 placements stocked; all 74 lessons stand on student books, so those lessons are taught in 1 country (Mauritania); 43 lessons carry sub lessons).
