# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 9: European colonial expansion — causes, forms, results”?
a) Economic motives lead; colonialism takes forms from direct rule to the protectorate, where the colonised state keeps a nominal independence.  b) The Renaissance is defined as revival of classical civilisation; Arab Sicily is named Europe's bridge to the East, and paper and the astrolabe pass through Muslim hands.  c) India's mass movement ends the Raj and partitions the subcontinent.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Economic motives lead; colonialism takes forms from direct rule to the protectorate, where the colonised state keeps a nominal independence.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 6: European colonial expansion”?
a) Industrial Europe reaches for the world's lands.  b) Political stability and accelerating invention crown the Renaissance's scientific development; the factory system replaces scattered home work.  c) States that refuse both Cold War camps build a third grouping of their own.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Industrial Europe reaches for the world's lands.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
