# Ghana and Angola Win Freedom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 7: Liberation movements in sub-Saharan Africa — Ghana and Angola”?
a) Ghana wins freedom at the ballot and leads the continent; Angola wins it by a long armed struggle.  b) Before writing (3200 BC) come the Stone and Metal ages in a dated table; after it, ancient, medieval, modern and contemporary ages are bounded by 476, 1492 and the French Revolution.  c) Ghana, medieval black Africa's first kingdom, grows rich where the salt and gold roads cross, its king ruling through ministers, interpreters and princes.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Ghana wins freedom at the ballot and leads the continent; Angola wins it by a long armed struggle.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) South of the Sahara the empires let go, peacefully and by war.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
