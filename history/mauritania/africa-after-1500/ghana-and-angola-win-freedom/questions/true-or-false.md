# Ghana and Angola Win Freedom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 7: Liberation movements in sub-Saharan Africa — Ghana and Angola”.
**Answer: False** — Ghana wins freedom at the ballot and leads the continent; Angola wins it by a long armed struggle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** South of the Sahara the empires let go, peacefully and by war.
**Answer: True**
