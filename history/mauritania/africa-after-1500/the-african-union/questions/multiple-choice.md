# The African Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 11: The African Union”?
a) The OAU's commitments are honoured and its strategic frameworks carried into the Union that replaces it.  b) Egypt is Mesopotamia's pair in the lesson — the Nile valley civilisation the class must place on the map beside the two rivers.  c) Mali, the western Sudan's largest kingdom (7th–9th centuries AH), swells to 22 provinces until distance and bad roads defeat its administration.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The OAU's commitments are honoured and its strategic frameworks carried into the Union that replaces it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 11: The African Union (formerly the OAU)”?
a) From the OAU of the liberation years to the African Union.  b) Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD).  c) Economic motives lead; colonialism takes forms from direct rule to the protectorate, where the colonised state keeps a nominal independence.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) From the OAU of the liberation years to the African Union.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
