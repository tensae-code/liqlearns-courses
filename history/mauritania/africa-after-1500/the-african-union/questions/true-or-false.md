# The African Union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 11: The African Union”.
**Answer: False** — The OAU's commitments are honoured and its strategic frameworks carried into the Union that replaces it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** From the OAU of the liberation years to the African Union.
**Answer: True**
