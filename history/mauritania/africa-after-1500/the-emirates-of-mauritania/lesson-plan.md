# Lesson plan — The Emirates of Mauritania

**Course:** History — Mauritania · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** The Emirates of Mauritania
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Emirates, Colonisation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Emirates, Colonisation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dossier 11: The emirates in Mauritania (1)** — Contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape.
2. **Dossier 12: The emirates in Mauritania (2)** — The Arab Hassaniya tribes expand and secure their rule as the Almoravid-era order ends — Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant govern the country.
3. **Lesson 9: The emirates in Mauritania** — Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant — the emirates that ruled Mauritania when Europe arrived.
4. **Lesson 2: The Mauritanian emirates** — The emirates that grew from the Hassaniya settlement rule Mauritania for centuries before France.
5. **Lesson 8: The Mauritanian emirates** — At secondary depth, the emirates are the state-form France found in Mauritania.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
