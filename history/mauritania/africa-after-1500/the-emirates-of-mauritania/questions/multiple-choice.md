# The Emirates of Mauritania — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Dossier 11: The emirates in Mauritania (1)”?
a) Contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape.  b) Arab taxes on eastern goods push Europeans to seek a direct road to India; printing, Marco Polo's book, maps and the astrolabe arm the search.  c) Why colonised peoples everywhere rose at once after 1945.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
