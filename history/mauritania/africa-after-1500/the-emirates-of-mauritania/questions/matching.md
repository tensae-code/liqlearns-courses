# The Emirates of Mauritania — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dossier 11: The emirates in Mauritania (1) — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dossier 12: The emirates in Mauritania (2) — b)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Lesson 9: The emirates in Mauritania — c)

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Lesson 2: The Mauritanian emirates — d)

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Lesson 8: The Mauritanian emirates — e)


Meanings:
a) Contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape.
b) The Arab Hassaniya tribes expand and secure their rule as the Almoravid-era order ends — Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant govern the country.
c) Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant — the emirates that ruled Mauritania when Europe arrived.
d) The emirates that grew from the Hassaniya settlement rule Mauritania for centuries before France.
e) At secondary depth, the emirates are the state-form France found in Mauritania.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c; 4. d; 5. e
