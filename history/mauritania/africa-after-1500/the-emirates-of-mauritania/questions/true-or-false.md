# The Emirates of Mauritania — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Dossier 11: The emirates in Mauritania (1)”.
**Answer: False** — Contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Arab Hassaniya tribes expand and secure their rule as the Almoravid-era order ends — Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant govern the country.
**Answer: True**
