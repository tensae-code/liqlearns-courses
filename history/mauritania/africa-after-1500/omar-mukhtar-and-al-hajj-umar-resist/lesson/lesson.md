# Omar Mukhtar and Al-Hajj Umar Resist

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Omar Mukhtar and Al-Hajj Umar Resist** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Two resisters frame the lesson — Mukhtar holds Libya's desert against Italy for twenty years; al-Hajj Umar's Toucouleur empire fights France in the Senegal country. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
