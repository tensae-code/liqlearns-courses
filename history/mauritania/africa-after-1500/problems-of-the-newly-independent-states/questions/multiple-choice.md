# Problems of the Newly Independent States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: The problems of the states newly come to independence”?
a) Borders, economies and armies inherited from empire trouble the new states.  b) The book opens with what a Muslim believes — the faith whose history the next six lessons then tell.  c) Two maps are compared to deduce when the Arab Hassaniya tribes entered Mauritania and remade its society.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Borders, economies and armies inherited from empire trouble the new states.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: The problems of the states newly come to independence”?
a) Borders, economies and armies inherited from empire trouble the new states.  b) A rival caliphate is born in North Africa, takes Egypt and builds Cairo, then fades.  c) Why did armed resistance not stop when its greatest heroes fell, martyr or prisoner, before 1912 — and who succeeded them.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Borders, economies and armies inherited from empire trouble the new states.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
