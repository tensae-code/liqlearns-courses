# The Senegal River Organisation (OMVS)

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Senegal River Organisation (OMVS)** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 13: The Senegal River Development Organisation (OMVS)

Mauritania, Senegal and Mali share one river — the book ends with the organisation that dams and divides it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 13: The Senegal River Development Organisation
