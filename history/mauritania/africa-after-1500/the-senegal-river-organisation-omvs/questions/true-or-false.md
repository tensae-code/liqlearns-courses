# The Senegal River Organisation (OMVS) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 13: The Senegal River Development Organisation (OMVS)”.
**Answer: False** — Mauritania, Senegal and Mali share one river — the book ends with the organisation that dams and divides it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The river Mauritania shares with Senegal and Mali gets its own joint authority.
**Answer: True**
