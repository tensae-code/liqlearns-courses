# The Senegal River Organisation (OMVS) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: The Senegal River Development Organisation (OMVS)”?
a) Mauritania, Senegal and Mali share one river — the book ends with the organisation that dams and divides it.  b) The peninsula on the eve of Islam — its peoples, states and faiths — is the doorway into the next unit.  c) The dossier tours the celebrated mosques and asks which famous mosques of Islam the pupil knows — the desert cities' mosques are Mauritania's monuments.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Mauritania, Senegal and Mali share one river — the book ends with the organisation that dams and divides it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
