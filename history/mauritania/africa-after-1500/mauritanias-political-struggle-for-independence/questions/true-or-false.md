# Mauritania's Political Struggle for Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Dossier 15: The political struggle before independence”.
**Answer: False** — Imams, jurists and poets sow the seeds of political awareness before the parties take up the fight.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After the armed phase, parties and petitions take over the fight.
**Answer: True**
