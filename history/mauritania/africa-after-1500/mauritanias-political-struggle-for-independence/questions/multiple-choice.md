# Mauritania's Political Struggle for Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Dossier 15: The political struggle before independence”?
a) Imams, jurists and poets sow the seeds of political awareness before the parties take up the fight.  b) Nobles and clergy hold power tax-free; Turgot and Necker fail to fix the finances; Mirabeau answers the court — "we are here by the will of the people".  c) The OAU's commitments are honoured and its strategic frameworks carried into the Union that replaces it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Imams, jurists and poets sow the seeds of political awareness before the parties take up the fight.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) After the armed phase, parties and petitions take over the fight.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
