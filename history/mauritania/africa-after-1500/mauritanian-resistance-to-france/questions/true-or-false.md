# Mauritanian Resistance to France — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Dossier 14: The resistance in Mauritania”.
**Answer: False** — Why did armed resistance not stop when its greatest heroes fell, martyr or prisoner, before 1912 — and who succeeded them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mauritanians take up arms against the occupation rather than submit.
**Answer: True**
