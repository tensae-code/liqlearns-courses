**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Emirates, Colonisation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dossier 13: Colonialism in Mauritania** — Mauritania becomes the target of European greed from the 15th century, and the dossier follows the French advance.
2. **Lesson 10: Colonialism enters Mauritania** — The French advance from Senegal up the river and into the desert.
3. **Lesson 3: Colonialism and resistance in Mauritania — the conquest** — France occupies Mauritania piece by piece against armed refusal.
4. **Lesson 9: Colonialism and resistance in Mauritania — the occupation** — The French occupation of Mauritania, stage by stage.
