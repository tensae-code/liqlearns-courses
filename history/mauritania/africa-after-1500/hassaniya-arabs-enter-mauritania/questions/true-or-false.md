# Hassaniya Arabs Enter Mauritania — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Dossier 8: Mauritania before the occupation — the Hassaniya Arabs arrive”.
**Answer: False** — Two maps are compared to deduce when the Arab Hassaniya tribes entered Mauritania and remade its society.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On what did the Mauritanian economy rest before the French came — herding, gum, salt and the caravan trade.
**Answer: True**
