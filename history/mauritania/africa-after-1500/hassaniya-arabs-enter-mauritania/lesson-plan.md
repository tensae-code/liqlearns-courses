# Lesson plan — Hassaniya Arabs Enter Mauritania

**Course:** History — Mauritania · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Hassaniya Arabs Enter Mauritania
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Emirates, Colonisation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Emirates, Colonisation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dossier 8: Mauritania before the occupation — the Hassaniya Arabs arrive** — Two maps are compared to deduce when the Arab Hassaniya tribes entered Mauritania and remade its society.
2. **Dossier 9: Mauritania before the occupation — the economy** — On what did the Mauritanian economy rest before the French came — herding, gum, salt and the caravan trade.
3. **Lesson 8: Mauritania before the French occupation** — The country the French will invade is first shown whole — its tribes, emirates and desert trade.
4. **Lesson 1: The Hassaniya migration to Mauritania in the 13th century** — Arab Hassaniya tribes migrate into the western Sahara in the 1200s and slowly Arabise the Sanhaja country.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
