# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 2: Mesopotamia, model of a vanished civilisation”?
a) Sumerians from the Caucasus, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians people the two rivers in turn; Ur, Lagash and Nippur rule, Gudea rebuilds and Ur-Nammu raises his tower.  b) The Abbasids overthrow the Umayyads, build Baghdad's caliphate and hold it, shrinking, for five centuries until the Mongol end.  c) Two resisters frame the lesson — Mukhtar holds Libya's desert against Italy for twenty years; al-Hajj Umar's Toucouleur empire fights France in the Senegal country.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Sumerians from the Caucasus, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians people the two rivers in turn; Ur, Lagash and Nippur rule, Gudea rebuilds and Ur-Nammu raises his tower.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
