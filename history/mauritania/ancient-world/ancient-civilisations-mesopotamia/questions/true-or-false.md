# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 2: Mesopotamia, model of a vanished civilisation”.
**Answer: False** — Sumerians from the Caucasus, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians people the two rivers in turn; Ur, Lagash and Nippur rule, Gudea rebuilds and Ur-Nammu raises his tower.

**2.** Sumerians from the Caucasus, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians people the two rivers in turn; Ur, Lagash and Nippur rule, Gudea rebuilds and Ur-Nammu raises his tower.
**Answer: True**
