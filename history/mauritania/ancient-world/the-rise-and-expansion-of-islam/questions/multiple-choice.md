# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The emergence of Islam”?
a) The Prophet's birth, lineage and standing are set out in full so the pupil knows the man before the mission.  b) The last great Islamic sultanate expands over three continents and declines over centuries.  c) Arab taxes on eastern goods push Europeans to seek a direct road to India; printing, Marco Polo's book, maps and the astrolabe arm the search.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Prophet's birth, lineage and standing are set out in full so the pupil knows the man before the mission.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 6: The founding of the Islamic state — foundations and conquests”?
a) Why the Prophet emigrated, how Medina's state was founded, and how the conquests carried it outward.  b) Songhai's order is so effective a child can go to market unmolested; then on 13 May 1591 Judar Pasha enters Gao, Askia Ishaq flees, and the empire collapses.  c) Nobles and clergy hold power tax-free; Turgot and Necker fail to fix the finances; Mirabeau answers the court — "we are here by the will of the people".  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Why the Prophet emigrated, how Medina's state was founded, and how the conquests carried it outward.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
