## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 5: The emergence of Islam** — The Prophet's birth, lineage and standing are set out in full so the pupil knows the man before the mission.
2. **Lesson 6: The founding of the Islamic state — foundations and conquests** — Why the Prophet emigrated, how Medina's state was founded, and how the conquests carried it outward.
3. **Lesson 2: The Prophet's birth and upbringing** — The Prophet is born and raised in Mecca before the mission — the man before the message.
4. **Lesson 3: The prophetic mission** — The call comes, Mecca resists, and the mission that will found a state begins.
5. **Lesson 5: The spread of Islam** — Within a century the faith the pupil has just followed reaches from Arabia to the Atlantic and beyond.
6. **Model lesson plan: The emergence of Islam (Unit 2)** — The guide's worked example for the Prophet lesson — how the teacher stages the birth of Islam for first-year pupils.
7. **Lesson 2: The emergence of Islam** — The Prophet's call begins in Mecca.
8. **Lesson 3: The founding of the Islamic state** — Medina gives the call a state.
