# The Abbasid Caliphate

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Abbasid Caliphate** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 10: The Abbasid state — rise and expansion 132–656 AH

The Abbasids overthrow the Umayyads, build Baghdad's caliphate and hold it, shrinking, for five centuries until the Mongol end. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
