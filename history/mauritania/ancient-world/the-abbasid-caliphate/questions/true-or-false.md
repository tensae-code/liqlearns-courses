# The Abbasid Caliphate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 10: The Abbasid state — rise and expansion 132–656 AH”.
**Answer: False** — The Abbasids overthrow the Umayyads, build Baghdad's caliphate and hold it, shrinking, for five centuries until the Mongol end.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Baghdad's caliphate, its organisation and its long arc.
**Answer: True**
