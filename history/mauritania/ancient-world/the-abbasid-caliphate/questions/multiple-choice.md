**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 10: The Abbasid state — rise and expansion 132–656 AH”?
a) The Abbasids overthrow the Umayyads, build Baghdad's caliphate and hold it, shrinking, for five centuries until the Mongol end.  b) Aoudaghost, the Sanhaja trading city where desert caravans meet, has its own dossier between Ghana and Takrur.  c) England limits its king by parliament — the model the century's thinkers will generalise.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
