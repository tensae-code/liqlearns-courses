Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: The Ottoman state — rise, expansion, causes of weakness and fall”?
a) The last great Islamic sultanate expands over three continents and declines over centuries.  b) Two maps are compared to deduce when the Arab Hassaniya tribes entered Mauritania and remade its society.  c) A second industrial revolution bursts in late-19th-century America; textiles improve, the steam train appears, and economic life is remade.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The last great Islamic sultanate expands over three continents and declines over centuries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
