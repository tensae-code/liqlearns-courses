# Umayyad Al-Andalus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 11: The Umayyad state in al-Andalus — rise, expansion, causes of fall”?
a) One Umayyad survivor refounds his dynasty at the far end of the Islamic world, and al-Andalus rises and fragments.  b) Takrur is the name of a group of Black tribes who found a kingdom in the Senegal river country south of the desert.  c) Enlightenment philosophers, Locke "father of liberalism" (1632–1704) among them, diagnose politics, economy and religion — and prepare religious reform and revolution.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) One Umayyad survivor refounds his dynasty at the far end of the Islamic world, and al-Andalus rises and fragments.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 8: The Umayyad state in al-Andalus”?
a) The far-western Umayyad state and its civilisation.  b) The third clash model is a trade in people — its causes and its results for Africa.  c) The Paris peace conference of 1919 creates the League of Nations to keep the peace.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The far-western Umayyad state and its civilisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
