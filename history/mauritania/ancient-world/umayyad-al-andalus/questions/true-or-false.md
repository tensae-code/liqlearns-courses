# Umayyad Al-Andalus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**Answer: False** — One Umayyad survivor refounds his dynasty at the far end of the Islamic world, and al-Andalus rises and fragments.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The far-western Umayyad state and its civilisation.
