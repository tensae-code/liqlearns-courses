# The Umayyad Caliphate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 9: The Umayyad state 41–132 AH”.
**Answer: False** — A hereditary caliphate rises at Damascus, expands to its widest bounds and falls within a century.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's worked example for the Umayyad lesson, dates fixed in both calendars.
**Answer: True**
