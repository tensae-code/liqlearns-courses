# The Rashidun Caliphs: Abu Bakr and Umar — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 7: The Rashidun caliphate — Abu Bakr and Umar”.
**Answer: False** — After Abu Bakr and Umar are studied, the lesson itself asks who the two caliphs after them were — the pair who held the community together and then stretched it across Persia and the Levant.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four caliphs who ruled by consultation after the Prophet carry the young community through its first crisis.
**Answer: True**
