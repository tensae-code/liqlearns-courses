## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 8: The Rashidun caliphate — Uthman and Ali

Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
