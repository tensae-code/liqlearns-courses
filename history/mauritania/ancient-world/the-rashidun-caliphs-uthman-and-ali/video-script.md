# Video script — The Rashidun Caliphs: Uthman and Ali

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
