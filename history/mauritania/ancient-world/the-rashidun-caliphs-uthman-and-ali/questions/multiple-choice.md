# The Rashidun Caliphs: Uthman and Ali — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 8: The Rashidun caliphate — Uthman and Ali”?
a) Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD).  b) An Islamic reform movement among the Sanhaja becomes an empire spanning the western Sahara, the Maghreb and al-Andalus, then falls as its sultans turn to pleasure, its army falters and the Almohad movement rises.  c) The Renaissance is defined as revival of classical civilisation; Arab Sicily is named Europe's bridge to the East, and paper and the astrolabe pass through Muslim hands.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD).** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The Rashidun caliphate — Uthman and Ali”?
a) The second pair face the first civil strife.  b) The dossier tours the celebrated mosques and asks which famous mosques of Islam the pupil knows — the desert cities' mosques are Mauritania's monuments.  c) Political stability and accelerating invention crown the Renaissance's scientific development; the factory system replaces scattered home work.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The second pair face the first civil strife.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
