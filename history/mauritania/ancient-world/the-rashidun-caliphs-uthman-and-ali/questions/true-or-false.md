# The Rashidun Caliphs: Uthman and Ali — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 8: The Rashidun caliphate — Uthman and Ali”.
**Answer: False** — Uthman's caliphate ends in revolt; Ali restores the Islamic ranks' unity for a time and is martyred on 21 Ramadan 40 AH (661 AD).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second pair face the first civil strife.
**Answer: True**
