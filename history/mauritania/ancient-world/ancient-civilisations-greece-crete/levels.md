# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — greece completes the lesson's trio of dead civilisations whose remains still witness their greatness, against the living Islamic, Western, Chinese, Japanese and Indian ones. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
