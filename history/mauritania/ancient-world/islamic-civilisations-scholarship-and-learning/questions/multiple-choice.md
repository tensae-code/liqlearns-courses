# Islamic Civilisations: Scholarship and Learning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 6: Islamic science and civilisation”?
a) The civilisation Islam built is measured by its science — scholars, schools and learning.  b) Songhai's order is so effective a child can go to market unmolested; then on 13 May 1591 Judar Pasha enters Gao, Askia Ishaq flees, and the empire collapses.  c) Nobles and clergy hold power tax-free; Turgot and Necker fail to fix the finances; Mirabeau answers the court — "we are here by the will of the people".  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The civilisation Islam built is measured by its science — scholars, schools and learning.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 8: Al-Andalus's learning — astronomy and mathematics”?
a) The Andalusians study astronomy and mathematics; Maslama al-Majriti masters Ptolemy's books.  b) The coloniser exploits the colony's resources for himself and invites foreign capital to invade its economy.  c) The crash spreads from America to the world.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Andalusians study astronomy and mathematics; Maslama al-Majriti masters Ptolemy's books.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
