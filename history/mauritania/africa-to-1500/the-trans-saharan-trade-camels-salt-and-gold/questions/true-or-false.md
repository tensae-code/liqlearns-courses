# The Trans-Saharan Trade: Camels, Salt and Gold — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 4: Trade across the Sahara”.
**Answer: False** — Caravans bind Bilad al-Sudan to North Africa; Taoudenni and Sebkha salt go south, the pilgrim road through Aoudaghost enriches Timbuktu, Jenne and Gao.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** From the early 15th century the desert routes turn Atlantic, and Saharan trade retreats before the coastal trade.
**Answer: True**
