**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4: Trade across the Sahara”?
a) Caravans bind Bilad al-Sudan to North Africa; Taoudenni and Sebkha salt go south, the pilgrim road through Aoudaghost enriches Timbuktu, Jenne and Gao.  b) Imams, jurists and poets sow the seeds of political awareness before the parties take up the fight.  c) Out of WWII's ruins the world builds one house for all nations.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
