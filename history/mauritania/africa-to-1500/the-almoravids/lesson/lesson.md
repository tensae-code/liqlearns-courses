## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 2: The Almoravid state — rise, expansion, causes of fall

An Islamic reform movement among the Sanhaja becomes an empire spanning the western Sahara, the Maghreb and al-Andalus, then falls as its sultans turn to pleasure, its army falters and the Almohad movement rises. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns.
