# Video script — The Almoravids

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

An Islamic reform movement among the Sanhaja becomes an empire spanning the western Sahara, the Maghreb and al-Andalus, then falls as its sultans turn to pleasure, its army falters and the Almohad movement rises. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
