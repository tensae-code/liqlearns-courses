# The Almoravids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 2: The Almoravid state — rise, expansion, causes of fall”.
**Answer: False** — An Islamic reform movement among the Sanhaja becomes an empire spanning the western Sahara, the Maghreb and al-Andalus, then falls as its sultans turn to pleasure, its army falters and the Almohad movement rises.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which tribes founded the Almoravid state is the dossier's test question — the desert tribes united by a religious call become an empire.
**Answer: True**
