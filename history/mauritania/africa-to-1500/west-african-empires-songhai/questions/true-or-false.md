# West African Empires: Songhai — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 3: The Sudanese empires — Songhai”.
**Answer: False** — Songhai's order is so effective a child can go to market unmolested; then on 13 May 1591 Judar Pasha enters Gao, Askia Ishaq flees, and the empire collapses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Songhai, Ghana's successor in the lesson's pairing, rules the Niger bend.
**Answer: True**
