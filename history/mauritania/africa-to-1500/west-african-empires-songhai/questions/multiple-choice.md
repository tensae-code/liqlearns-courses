**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 3: The Sudanese empires — Songhai”?
a) Songhai's order is so effective a child can go to market unmolested; then on 13 May 1591 Judar Pasha enters Gao, Askia Ishaq flees, and the empire collapses.  b) Why did armed resistance not stop when its greatest heroes fell, martyr or prisoner, before 1912 — and who succeeded them.  c) America enters after Pearl Harbor, France is liberated, and the map of Europe after the war closes the book.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Songhai's order is so effective a child can go to market unmolested; then on 13 May 1591 Judar Pasha enters Gao, Askia Ishaq flees, and the empire collapses.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
