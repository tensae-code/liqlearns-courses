# Ancient Mauritania: Peoples of the Western Sahara — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 3: Ancient Mauritania — settlement and peoples”.
**Answer: False** — Farming and herding begin in the Neolithic (7000–2000 BC); as the Sahara dries, Garamantes, Bafour and Berbers migrate in — Sanhaja Berbers in the 3rd century AD settle the ponds and wadis, the Bafour hold Adrar from Azuggi behind trained dogs until the Almoravids come, Phoenician sailors trade down the Atlantic coast from 1500 BC, and Sudanic groups hold the south.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rock engravings at al-Ghallawiya in Adrar, engraved ostrich eggs and pottery mark the culture; the first Berbers bring the wheeled chariot, horse, metal, writing and finally the camel (1st century AD), which opens the desert to caravan trade Herodotus already called silent; with no strong states, local chiefs manage water, grazing and disputes.
**Answer: True**
