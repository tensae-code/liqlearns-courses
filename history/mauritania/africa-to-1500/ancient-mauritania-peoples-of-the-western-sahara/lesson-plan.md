# Lesson plan — Ancient Mauritania: Peoples of the Western Sahara

**Course:** History — Mauritania · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Mauritania: Peoples of the Western Sahara
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** farming and herding begin in the Neolithic (7000–2000 BC); as the Sahara dries, Garamantes, Bafour and Berbers migrate in — Sanhaja Berbers in the 3rd century AD settle the ponds and wadis, the Bafour hold Adrar from Azuggi behind trained dogs until the Almoravids come, Phoenician sailors trade down the Atlantic coast from 1500 BC, and Sudanic groups hold the south.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 3: Ancient Mauritania — settlement and peoples** — Farming and herding begin in the Neolithic (7000–2000 BC); as the Sahara dries, Garamantes, Bafour and Berbers migrate in — Sanhaja Berbers in the 3rd century AD settle the ponds and wadis, the Bafour hold Adrar from Azuggi behind trained dogs until the Almoravids come, Phoenician sailors trade down the Atlantic coast from 1500 BC, and Sudanic groups hold the south.
2. **Lesson 3: Ancient Mauritania — culture, economy and chiefs** — Rock engravings at al-Ghallawiya in Adrar, engraved ostrich eggs and pottery mark the culture; the first Berbers bring the wheeled chariot, horse, metal, writing and finally the camel (1st century AD), which opens the desert to caravan trade Herodotus already called silent; with no strong states, local chiefs manage water, grazing and disputes.
3. **Dossier 1: Mauritania before Islam** — Before Islam the Sanhaja tribes lead desert society for long ages under their own chiefs — the test asks the pupil to name the tribes and their leaders.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
