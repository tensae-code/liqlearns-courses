# West African Empires: Ghana — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 3: The Sudanese empires — Ghana”.
**Answer: False** — Ghana, medieval black Africa's first kingdom, grows rich where the salt and gold roads cross, its king ruling through ministers, interpreters and princes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghana appears in the land between the great desert and the southern forests, astride the roads the caravans must cross.
**Answer: True**
