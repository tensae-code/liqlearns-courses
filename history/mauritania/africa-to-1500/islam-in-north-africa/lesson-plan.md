## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 1: Islam enters Mauritania** — Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith.
2. **Dossier 5: Islam in Mauritania** — The pupil must date Islam's arrival and name its carriers — traders, the scholars of the mahadras and the Sufi shaykhs.
3. **Dossier 7: Mauritania's role in spreading Islam** — A map of the Islamic conquest in the Maghreb frames how Mauritania itself became a spreader of the faith southward.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
