# Islam in North Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islam in North Africa** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 1: Islam enters Mauritania

Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
