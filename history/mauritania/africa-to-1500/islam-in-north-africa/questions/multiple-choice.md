# Islam in North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 1: Islam enters Mauritania”?
a) Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith.  b) Contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape.  c) Colonial rivalry over colonies and seas divides Europe into the Triple Alliance (Austria-Hungary, Germany, Italy, the Ottomans) and the Triple Entente (France, Britain, Russia).  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The pupil must date Islam's arrival and name its carriers — traders, the scholars of the mahadras and the Sufi shaykhs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
