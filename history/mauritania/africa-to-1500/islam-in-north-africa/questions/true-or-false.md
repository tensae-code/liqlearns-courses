# Islam in North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Lesson 1: Islam enters Mauritania”.
**Answer: False** — Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The pupil must date Islam's arrival and name its carriers — traders, the scholars of the mahadras and the Sufi shaykhs.
**Answer: True**
