# Islam in North Africa — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lesson 1: Islam enters Mauritania — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dossier 5: Islam in Mauritania — b)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Dossier 7: Mauritania's role in spreading Islam — c)


Meanings:
a) Islam comes mostly peacefully, above all with Muslim traders; the preacher Abdullah ibn Yasin's followers reach a thousand men in four years, and commanding right and forbidding wrong strengthens the new faith.
b) The pupil must date Islam's arrival and name its carriers — traders, the scholars of the mahadras and the Sufi shaykhs.
c) A map of the Islamic conquest in the Maghreb frames how Mauritania itself became a spreader of the faith southward.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
