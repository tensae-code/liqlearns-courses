# History — Sri Lanka course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Sri Lanka's own — built from Sri Lanka's Educational Publications Department and National Institute of Education books (Tamil student books, with the Grade 9 teacher guide in English). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the ancient world and the island's kingdoms, foreign rule and the road to independence, the tank civilisation and Kandy with the world widening, colonial Ceylon beside the world's great turns, the ancient and medieval kingdoms as scholarship, and ancient India as archaeology. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
