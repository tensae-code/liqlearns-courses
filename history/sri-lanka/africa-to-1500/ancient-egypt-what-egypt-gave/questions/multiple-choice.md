**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1, Egypt's afterlife belief, pyramids, hieroglyphs and papyrus”?
a) A dead king's body is prepared for a second life, a pyramid rises over him, and picture-writing is scratched on stone and papyrus.  b) Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers.  c) On the yellow silt plain the Shang kings rule, ancestors are honoured in tombs, and characters are cut into tortoise shell.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A dead king's body is prepared for a second life, a pyramid rises over him, and picture-writing is scratched on stone and papyrus.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
