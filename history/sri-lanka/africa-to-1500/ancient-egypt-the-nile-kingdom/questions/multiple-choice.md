# Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1, the Nile civilisation, its pharaohs and farming”?
a) The Nile floods on time, the pharaoh is held to be a living god, and grain fills the storehouses.  b) A dead king's body is prepared for a second life, a pyramid rises over him, and picture-writing is scratched on stone and papyrus.  c) Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Nile floods on time, the pharaoh is held to be a living god, and grain fills the storehouses.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
