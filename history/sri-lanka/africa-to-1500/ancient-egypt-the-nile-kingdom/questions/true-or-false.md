# Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 1, the Nile civilisation, its pharaohs and farming”.
**Answer: False** — The Nile floods on time, the pharaoh is held to be a living god, and grain fills the storehouses.

**2.** The Nile floods on time, the pharaoh is held to be a living god, and grain fills the storehouses.
**Answer: True**
