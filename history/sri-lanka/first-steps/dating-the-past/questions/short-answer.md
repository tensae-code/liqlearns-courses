# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Historians draw the line at Rome's fall and again at the 15th century, and the middle age gets its name.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 1, an introduction to history and its sources”?
**Answer:** History is defined as the unending dialogue between past and present, and the evidence is paraded — Sigiriya's frescoes, Jetavanaramaya, a Nataraja bronze, Mirisavetiya coins — 2,500 years of written sources.
