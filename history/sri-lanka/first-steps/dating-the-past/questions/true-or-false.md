# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 5, dividing European history into ancient, medieval and modern ages”.
**Answer: False** — Historians draw the line at Rome's fall and again at the 15th century, and the middle age gets its name.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) History is defined as the unending dialogue between past and present, and the evidence is paraded — Sigiriya's frescoes, Jetavanaramaya, a Nataraja bronze, Mirisavetiya coins — 2,500 years of written sources.
**Answer: True**
