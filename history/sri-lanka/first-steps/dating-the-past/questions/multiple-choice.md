**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 5, dividing European history into ancient, medieval and modern ages”?
a) Historians draw the line at Rome's fall and again at the 15th century, and the middle age gets its name.  b) Jesus, baptised by John in Galilee, preaches a kingdom of love for three years, is crucified under Pilate, and his followers proclaim him risen.  c) Peter's successors at Rome stand over patriarchs, bishops and priests, the Church owns land and taxes all Europe, and the Pope becomes its strongest power.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Historians draw the line at Rome's fall and again at the 15th century, and the middle age gets its name.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
