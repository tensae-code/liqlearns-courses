# Comparing States How Kingdoms Hold Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1 closing comparison assignment on the four river civilisations”?
a) Four pupil groups each defend one river land as the greatest, and the class weighs the evidence side by side.  b) Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.  c) In tiny Athens every free man may speak and vote himself, and officials answer to the people who chose them.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Four pupil groups each defend one river land as the greatest, and the class weighs the evidence side by side.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
