**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, political history of the Anuradhapura period”?
a) Thirteen centuries of kings in one long narrative — dynasties rise, Tamil invasions break in, capitals hold — the political spine the younger books only sample.  b) How the ancient kingdom actually ran — king, council, provincial rulers, village headmen, taxes and justice.  c) Tank-fed rice at home, pearls and gems abroad — the economy that paid for the stupas.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
