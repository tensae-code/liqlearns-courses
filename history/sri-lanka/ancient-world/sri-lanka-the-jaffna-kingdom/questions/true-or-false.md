# Sri Lanka: The Jaffna Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 6, the Jaffna kingdom of the Arya Chakravartis”.
**Answer: False** — A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Aryachakravarti line rules the north for centuries, twelve kings keep a Tamil court with its own coinage, temples and laws while the south fragments.
**Answer: True**
