# Sri Lanka: The Jaffna Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 6, the Jaffna kingdom of the Arya Chakravartis”?
a) A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619.  b) The VOC is founded in 1602, Spilbergen lands at Batticaloa, Sebald de Weert is killed after insulting Vimaladharmasuriya I, yet Rajasinghe II signs the 1638 treaty and the Dutch take the Portuguese forts but refuse to hand them back.  c) Envoys swap gifts in 1602, 1656, 1688 and 1721, Vimaladharmasuriya II keeps the peace and wins back monks from Burma, but Kirti Sri's Kandy is invaded in 1765 and the 1766 treaty strips the kingdom of its coast.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, the Jaffna kingdom”?
a) The Aryachakravarti line rules the north for centuries, twelve kings keep a Tamil court with its own coinage, temples and laws while the south fragments.  b) Envoys swap gifts in 1602, 1656, 1688 and 1721, Vimaladharmasuriya II keeps the peace and wins back monks from Burma, but Kirti Sri's Kandy is invaded in 1765 and the 1766 treaty strips the kingdom of its coast.  c) The Land Raad gives villagers cheap justice, and clerks write every family, field and tax into thombo books — one man's 195 coconut palms owe a single rix-dollar.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Aryachakravarti line rules the north for centuries, twelve kings keep a Tamil court with its own coinage, temples and laws while the south fragments.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
