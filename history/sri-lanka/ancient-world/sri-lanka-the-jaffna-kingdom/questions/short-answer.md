# Sri Lanka: The Jaffna Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 5, the Jaffna kingdom”?
**Answer:** The Aryachakravarti line rules the north for centuries, twelve kings keep a Tamil court with its own coinage, temples and laws while the south fragments.
