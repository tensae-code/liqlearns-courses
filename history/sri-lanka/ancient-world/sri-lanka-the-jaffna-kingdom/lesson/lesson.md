# Sri Lanka: The Jaffna Kingdom

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 6, the Jaffna kingdom of the Arya Chakravartis

A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
