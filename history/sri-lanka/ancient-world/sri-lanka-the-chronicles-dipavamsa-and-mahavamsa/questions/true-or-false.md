# Sri Lanka: The Chronicles Dipavamsa and Mahavamsa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 1, the Pali chronicles”.
**Answer: False** — The Dipavamsa comes first, then the monk Mahanama shapes the Mahavamsa in the 5th–6th century — 37 chapters ending with Mahasena — and later hands continue it as the Culavamsa, while Wilhelm Geiger's edition lets the world read them critically.

**2.** The Dipavamsa comes first, then the monk Mahanama shapes the Mahavamsa in the 5th–6th century — 37 chapters ending with Mahasena — and later hands continue it as the Culavamsa, while Wilhelm Geiger's edition lets the world read them critically.
**Answer: True**
