# The Crusades — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 5, the Crusades and their results”?
a) Seljuk Turks bar pilgrims from Jerusalem, the Pope calls Europe to arms, two centuries of holy war fail to hold the city but bring Eastern goods, numbers and learning home.  b) Magha's terror scatters monks and people to the hills, and chiefs set up small strongholds at Yapahuwa, Kurunegala, Gampola and Dambadeniya.  c) Vijayabahu III founds a safer capital, hides the Tooth Relic on Kotmale hill, and his son wins it back, beats Kalinga Magha in 1255 with Pandya help, and drives off Chandrabhanu of Java.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Seljuk Turks bar pilgrims from Jerusalem, the Pope calls Europe to arms, two centuries of holy war fail to hold the city but bring Eastern goods, numbers and learning home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
