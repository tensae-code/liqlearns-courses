# India Stone Age Tools and the First Indians

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **India Stone Age Tools and the First Indians** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Across 400,000 years the first Indians knap pebbles into tools — the book draws ten Soan-valley implements one by one and maps the Stone Age across North, Central and South India. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
