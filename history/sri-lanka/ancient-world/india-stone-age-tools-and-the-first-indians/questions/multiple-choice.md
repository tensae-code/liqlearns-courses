# India Stone Age Tools and the First Indians — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Chs. 3–4, Stones and More Stones”?
a) Across 400,000 years the first Indians knap pebbles into tools — the book draws ten Soan-valley implements one by one and maps the Stone Age across North, Central and South India.  b) The centre of civilisation shifts east to the Ganges plain and south into the Deccan, told through the pots, burials and iron the plates display.  c) Stone circles and dolmens rise over the southern dead — urn burials and grave goods at places like Adichanallur open a window on the Iron Age Tamil country.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Across 400,000 years the first Indians knap pebbles into tools — the book draws ten Soan-valley implements one by one and maps the Stone Age across North, Central and South India.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
