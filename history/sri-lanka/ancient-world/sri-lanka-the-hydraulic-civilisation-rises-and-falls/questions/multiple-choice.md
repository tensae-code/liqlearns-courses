# Sri Lanka: The Hydraulic Civilisation Rises and Falls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4, rise and fall of the hydraulic civilisation”?
a) A dry land is made fertile by one linked system — dam, tank, canal, field — and when invasions and neglect break the system, the whole civilisation drains away with its water.  b) Theravada Buddhism roots itself in Burma, Thailand, Java and Cambodia — Angkor Wat rises — and island monks sail both ways, carrying higher ordination between Sri Lanka and Siam in 1753.  c) Spain and Portugal open the race, da Gama reaches India in 1498, the Dutch found the VOC in 1602 and out-trade them all, England and France follow, and Christianity sails with every flag.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A dry land is made fertile by one linked system — dam, tank, canal, field — and when invasions and neglect break the system, the whole civilisation drains away with its water.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
