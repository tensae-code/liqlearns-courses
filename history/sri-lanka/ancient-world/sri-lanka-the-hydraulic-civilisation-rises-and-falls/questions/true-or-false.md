# Sri Lanka: The Hydraulic Civilisation Rises and Falls — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 4, rise and fall of the hydraulic civilisation”.
**Answer: False** — A dry land is made fertile by one linked system — dam, tank, canal, field — and when invasions and neglect break the system, the whole civilisation drains away with its water.

**2.** A dry land is made fertile by one linked system — dam, tank, canal, field — and when invasions and neglect break the system, the whole civilisation drains away with its water.
**Answer: True**
