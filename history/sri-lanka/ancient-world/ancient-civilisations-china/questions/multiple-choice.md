# Ancient Civilisations China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1, the Hwang Ho (Yellow River) civilisation of China”?
a) On the yellow silt plain the Shang kings rule, ancestors are honoured in tombs, and characters are cut into tortoise shell.  b) Four pupil groups each defend one river land as the greatest, and the class weighs the evidence side by side.  c) Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) On the yellow silt plain the Shang kings rule, ancestors are honoured in tombs, and characters are cut into tortoise shell.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
