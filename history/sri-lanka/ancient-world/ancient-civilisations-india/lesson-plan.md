## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 1, the Indus valley civilisation** — Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers.
2. **Unit 2, ancient India: Vedic sources and the sixteen great settlements (mahajanapadas)** — Aryan tribes settle the Ganges plain and sixteen rival states, from Anga to Kamboja, contend for mastery.
3. **Unit 2, Magadha rises: Bimbisara and Ajatashatru** — Father and son build Magadha by marriage alliances, roads and new war machines until it swallows its neighbours.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
