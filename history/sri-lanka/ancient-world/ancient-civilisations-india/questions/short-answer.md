# Ancient Civilisations India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 2, ancient India: Vedic sources and the sixteen great settlements (mahajanapadas)”?
**Answer:** Aryan tribes settle the Ganges plain and sixteen rival states, from Anga to Kamboja, contend for mastery.
