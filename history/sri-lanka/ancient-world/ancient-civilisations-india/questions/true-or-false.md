# Ancient Civilisations India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 1, the Indus valley civilisation”.
**Answer: False** — Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Aryan tribes settle the Ganges plain and sixteen rival states, from Anga to Kamboja, contend for mastery.
**Answer: True**
