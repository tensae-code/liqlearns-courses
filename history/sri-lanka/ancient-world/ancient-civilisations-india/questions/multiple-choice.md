# Ancient Civilisations India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1, the Indus valley civilisation”?
a) Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers.  b) On the yellow silt plain the Shang kings rule, ancestors are honoured in tombs, and characters are cut into tortoise shell.  c) Four pupil groups each defend one river land as the greatest, and the class weighs the evidence side by side.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, ancient India: Vedic sources and the sixteen great settlements (mahajanapadas)”?
a) Aryan tribes settle the Ganges plain and sixteen rival states, from Anga to Kamboja, contend for mastery.  b) Four pupil groups each defend one river land as the greatest, and the class weighs the evidence side by side.  c) Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Aryan tribes settle the Ganges plain and sixteen rival states, from Anga to Kamboja, contend for mastery.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
