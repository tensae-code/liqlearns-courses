## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, the Indus valley civilisation

Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are laid out on a grid with drains and baths, yet no king's palace or readable writing survives to name their rulers. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
