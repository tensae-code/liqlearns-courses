# Video script — Christianity from Persecuted Sect to State Religion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Jesus, baptised by John in Galilee, preaches a kingdom of love for three years, is crucified under Pilate, and his followers proclaim him risen. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Nero blames the Christians for Rome's fire and feeds them to lions, yet Constantine's Edict of Milan (313) and Theodosius (380) make their faith the empire's own. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
