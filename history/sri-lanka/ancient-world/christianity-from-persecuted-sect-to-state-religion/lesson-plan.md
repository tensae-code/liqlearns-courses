## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 5, the origin of Christianity: Jesus of Nazareth** — Jesus, baptised by John in Galilee, preaches a kingdom of love for three years, is crucified under Pilate, and his followers proclaim him risen.
2. **Unit 5, persecution and triumph of the Church** — Nero blames the Christians for Rome's fire and feeds them to lions, yet Constantine's Edict of Milan (313) and Theodosius (380) make their faith the empire's own.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
