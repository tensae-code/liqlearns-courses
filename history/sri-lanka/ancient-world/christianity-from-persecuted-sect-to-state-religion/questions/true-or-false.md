# Christianity from Persecuted Sect to State Religion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 5, the origin of Christianity: Jesus of Nazareth”.
**Answer: False** — Jesus, baptised by John in Galilee, preaches a kingdom of love for three years, is crucified under Pilate, and his followers proclaim him risen.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Nero blames the Christians for Rome's fire and feeds them to lions, yet Constantine's Edict of Milan (313) and Theodosius (380) make their faith the empire's own.
**Answer: True**
