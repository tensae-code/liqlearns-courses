# Christianity from Persecuted Sect to State Religion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 5, the origin of Christianity: Jesus of Nazareth”?
a) Jesus, baptised by John in Galilee, preaches a kingdom of love for three years, is crucified under Pilate, and his followers proclaim him risen.  b) Peter's successors at Rome stand over patriarchs, bishops and priests, the Church owns land and taxes all Europe, and the Pope becomes its strongest power.  c) With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Jesus, baptised by John in Galilee, preaches a kingdom of love for three years, is crucified under Pilate, and his followers proclaim him risen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 5, persecution and triumph of the Church”?
a) Nero blames the Christians for Rome's fire and feeds them to lions, yet Constantine's Edict of Milan (313) and Theodosius (380) make their faith the empire's own.  b) With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour.  c) Seljuk Turks bar pilgrims from Jerusalem, the Pope calls Europe to arms, two centuries of holy war fail to hold the city but bring Eastern goods, numbers and learning home.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Nero blames the Christians for Rome's fire and feeds them to lions, yet Constantine's Edict of Milan (313) and Theodosius (380) make their faith the empire's own.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
