# Christianity from Persecuted Sect to State Religion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Jesus, baptised by John in Galilee, preaches a kingdom of love for three years, is crucified under Pilate, and his followers proclaim him risen.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 5, persecution and triumph of the Church”?
**Answer:** Nero blames the Christians for Rome's fire and feeds them to lions, yet Constantine's Edict of Milan (313) and Theodosius (380) make their faith the empire's own.
