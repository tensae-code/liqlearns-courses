**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1, how an ancient tank was built”?
a) Kings' engineers dam valleys and invent the bisokotuwa sluice that tames the rush of water, and a diagram names every part of the tank from bund to spill.  b) A table marches down the centuries — Pandukabhaya's first tanks, Vasabha, Mahasena, Dhatusena raising Kala Wewa, the Aggabodhis, and Parakramabahu I capping a thousand years of water kings.  c) Excavated gold ornaments shaped like conches, iron work, gems and foreign coins prove an island of skilled makers trading far beyond its shores.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
