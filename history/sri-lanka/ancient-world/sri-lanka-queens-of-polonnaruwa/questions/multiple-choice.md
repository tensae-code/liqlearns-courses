# Sri Lanka: Queens of Polonnaruwa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, the queens who ruled Polonnaruwa”?
a) Lilavati reigns three times amid Chola invasions, Kalyanavati rules six years, and Sugala Devi fights for her son's claim to the Tooth Relic.  b) Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt.  c) Great kings, high status for women, Shiva temples beside viharas, moonstones without the bull, and a planned royal city of palaces and lotus ponds.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Lilavati reigns three times amid Chola invasions, Kalyanavati rules six years, and Sugala Devi fights for her son's claim to the Tooth Relic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
