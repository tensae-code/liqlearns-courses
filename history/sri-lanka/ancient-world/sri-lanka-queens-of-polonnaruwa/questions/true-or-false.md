# Sri Lanka: Queens of Polonnaruwa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 4, the queens who ruled Polonnaruwa”.
**Answer: False** — Lilavati reigns three times amid Chola invasions, Kalyanavati rules six years, and Sugala Devi fights for her son's claim to the Tooth Relic.

**2.** Lilavati reigns three times amid Chola invasions, Kalyanavati rules six years, and Sugala Devi fights for her son's claim to the Tooth Relic.
**Answer: True**
