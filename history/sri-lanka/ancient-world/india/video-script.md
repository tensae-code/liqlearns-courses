# Video script — India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

After the Indus cities fall, Aryan groups cross the Khyber into Punjab around 1500 BCE and spread down the Ganges and Yamuna by the 7th century BCE. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

A lost civilisation is dug out of the Indus mounds — Mohenjo-daro's streets are mapped square by square, its undeciphered seals and planned drains arguing for a city older than the Vedas. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
