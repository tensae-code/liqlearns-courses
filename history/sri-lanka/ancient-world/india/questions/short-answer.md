# India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** After the Indus cities fall, Aryan groups cross the Khyber into Punjab around 1500 BCE and spread down the Ganges and Yamuna by the 7th century BCE.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 5, the Indus civilisation”?
**Answer:** A lost civilisation is dug out of the Indus mounds — Mohenjo-daro's streets are mapped square by square, its undeciphered seals and planned drains arguing for a city older than the Vedas.
