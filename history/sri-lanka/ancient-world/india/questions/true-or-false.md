# India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 2, the Aryan settlement of India”.
**Answer: False** — After the Indus cities fall, Aryan groups cross the Khyber into Punjab around 1500 BCE and spread down the Ganges and Yamuna by the 7th century BCE.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A lost civilisation is dug out of the Indus mounds — Mohenjo-daro's streets are mapped square by square, its undeciphered seals and planned drains arguing for a city older than the Vedas.
**Answer: True**
