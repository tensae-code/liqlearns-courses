# India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, the Aryan settlement of India”?
a) After the Indus cities fall, Aryan groups cross the Khyber into Punjab around 1500 BCE and spread down the Ganges and Yamuna by the 7th century BCE.  b) Villages join into kingdoms under a rajan, the sabha and samiti assemblies check him, priests and generals hold office, and nature gods — Agni, Varuna — rule the Vedic pantheon.  c) Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) After the Indus cities fall, Aryan groups cross the Khyber into Punjab around 1500 BCE and spread down the Ganges and Yamuna by the 7th century BCE.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, the Indus civilisation”?
a) A lost civilisation is dug out of the Indus mounds — Mohenjo-daro's streets are mapped square by square, its undeciphered seals and planned drains arguing for a city older than the Vedas.  b) Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line.  c) A dry land is made fertile by one linked system — dam, tank, canal, field — and when invasions and neglect break the system, the whole civilisation drains away with its water.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A lost civilisation is dug out of the Indus mounds — Mohenjo-daro's streets are mapped square by square, its undeciphered seals and planned drains arguing for a city older than the Vedas.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
