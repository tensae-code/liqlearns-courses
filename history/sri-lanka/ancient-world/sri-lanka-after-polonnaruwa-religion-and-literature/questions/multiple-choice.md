# Sri Lanka: After Polonnaruwa Religion and Literature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 6, religion and literature of the Dambadeniya–Kotte ages”?
a) Vijayabahu III restarts the upasampada, scholars rewrite lost books, and Sinhala and Tamil works — from the Dambadeni Kathawa to the Sarasvati Malai — flower under royal patronage.  b) A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619.  c) The VOC is founded in 1602, Spilbergen lands at Batticaloa, Sebald de Weert is killed after insulting Vimaladharmasuriya I, yet Rajasinghe II signs the 1638 treaty and the Dutch take the Portuguese forts but refuse to hand them back.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Vijayabahu III restarts the upasampada, scholars rewrite lost books, and Sinhala and Tamil works — from the Dambadeni Kathawa to the Sarasvati Malai — flower under royal patronage.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
