# Sri Lanka: Alagakkonara and Zheng He's Visits — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 6, the Alagakkonara chiefs and the Chinese admiral”.
**Answer: False** — Zheng He's fleet lands in 1405 and again in 1411, the minister Alagakkonara defies him, and the admiral carries the chief off to China before sending him home.

**2.** Zheng He's fleet lands in 1405 and again in 1411, the minister Alagakkonara defies him, and the admiral carries the chief off to China before sending him home.
**Answer: True**
