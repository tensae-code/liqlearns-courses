**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, Nissanka Malla (1187–1196)”?
a) A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.  b) Lilavati reigns three times amid Chola invasions, Kalyanavati rules six years, and Sugala Devi fights for her son's claim to the Tooth Relic.  c) Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
