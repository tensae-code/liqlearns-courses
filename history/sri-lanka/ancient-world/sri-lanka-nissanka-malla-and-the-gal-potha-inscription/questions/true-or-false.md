# Sri Lanka: Nissanka Malla and the Gal Potha Inscription — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 4, Nissanka Malla (1187–1196)”.
**Answer: False** — A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.

**2.** A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.
**Answer: True**
