# Sri Lanka: Chola Rule and Administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 3, how the Cholas governed the island”?
a) The island becomes Mumudi Chola Mandalam, ruled by a Chola general who taxes its villages and sends the revenue to Thanjavur.  b) Four Tamil powers rise and fall across the strait, and each in turn reaches for the island facing it.  c) Exiled princes like Ilanaga and Moggallana sail to India for armies, Manavamma wins his throne with Pallava help, and royal marriages bind the two shores.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The island becomes Mumudi Chola Mandalam, ruled by a Chola general who taxes its villages and sends the revenue to Thanjavur.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
