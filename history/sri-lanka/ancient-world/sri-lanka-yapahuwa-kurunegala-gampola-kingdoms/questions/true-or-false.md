# Sri Lanka: Yapahuwa Kurunegala Gampola Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 6, Yapahuwa, Kurunegala and Gampola (1272–1415)”.
**Answer: False** — Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) After Magha, capitals hop south-west — Dambadeniya's Parakramabahu II makes a golden age of literature, Yapahuwa guards the Tooth Relic, and Gampola's Bhuvanaikabahu IV rules a shrinking but cultured kingdom.
**Answer: True**
