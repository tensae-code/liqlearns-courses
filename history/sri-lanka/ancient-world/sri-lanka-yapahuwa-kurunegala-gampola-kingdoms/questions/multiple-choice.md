# Sri Lanka: Yapahuwa Kurunegala Gampola Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 6, Yapahuwa, Kurunegala and Gampola (1272–1415)”?
a) Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.  b) Zheng He's fleet lands in 1405 and again in 1411, the minister Alagakkonara defies him, and the admiral carries the chief off to China before sending him home.  c) A fortress rises in the marshes, Parakramabahu VI takes Jaffna in 1450 through his adopted son Sapumal Kumaraya, and rules a united island for the last time.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, the south-western kingdoms and their culture”?
a) After Magha, capitals hop south-west — Dambadeniya's Parakramabahu II makes a golden age of literature, Yapahuwa guards the Tooth Relic, and Gampola's Bhuvanaikabahu IV rules a shrinking but cultured kingdom.  b) A fortress rises in the marshes, Parakramabahu VI takes Jaffna in 1450 through his adopted son Sapumal Kumaraya, and rules a united island for the last time.  c) The king's three sons murder their father in 1521 and divide Kotte, Sitawaka and Raigama between them, while Jaffna and Kandy stand apart and the Portuguese are already on the coast.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) After Magha, capitals hop south-west — Dambadeniya's Parakramabahu II makes a golden age of literature, Yapahuwa guards the Tooth Relic, and Gampola's Bhuvanaikabahu IV rules a shrinking but cultured kingdom.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
