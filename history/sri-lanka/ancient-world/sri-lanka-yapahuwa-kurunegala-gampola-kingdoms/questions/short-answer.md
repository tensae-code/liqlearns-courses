# Sri Lanka: Yapahuwa Kurunegala Gampola Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 5, the south-western kingdoms and their culture”?
**Answer:** After Magha, capitals hop south-west — Dambadeniya's Parakramabahu II makes a golden age of literature, Yapahuwa guards the Tooth Relic, and Gampola's Bhuvanaikabahu IV rules a shrinking but cultured kingdom.
