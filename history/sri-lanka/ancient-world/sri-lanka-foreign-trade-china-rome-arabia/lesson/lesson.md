# Sri Lanka: Foreign Trade China Rome Arabia

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Foreign Trade China Rome Arabia** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Chinese porcelain and coins turn up in island soil, Ibn Battuta visits in 1344, Zheng He's fleet leaves a trilingual stone in 1409, and elephants, pearls and gems sail out as cloth and pottery sail in. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
