**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, what the finds and travellers tell”?
a) Chinese porcelain and coins turn up in island soil, Ibn Battuta visits in 1344, Zheng He's fleet leaves a trilingual stone in 1409, and elephants, pearls and gems sail out as cloth and pottery sail in.  b) Buddhism, Hinduism and later Islam each land with merchants and monks, Sinhala kings take Tamil titles, and three faiths root themselves in one small island.  c) The king consults his great council before deciding, the land is run in rata divisions under disavas, villages govern themselves, and temple lands answer to their own officers.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Chinese porcelain and coins turn up in island soil, Ibn Battuta visits in 1344, Zheng He's fleet leaves a trilingual stone in 1409, and elephants, pearls and gems sail out as cloth and pottery sail in.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
