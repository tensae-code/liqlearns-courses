# Sri Lanka: Medieval Foreign Policy

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Medieval Foreign Policy** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Island kings play the great game — embassies to China and Burma, wars and marriages with the Pandya country, fleets sent overseas. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
