# Sri Lanka: Medieval Foreign Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, foreign policy”?
a) Island kings play the great game — embassies to China and Burma, wars and marriages with the Pandya country, fleets sent overseas.  b) The tank economy at its height and in decline, and the society it carried — castes, villages, monks and merchants.  c) The sangha is purged and unified, the Tooth Relic becomes the kingdom's palladium, and Hindu worship stands beside the viharas.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Island kings play the great game — embassies to China and Burma, wars and marriages with the Pandya country, fleets sent overseas.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
