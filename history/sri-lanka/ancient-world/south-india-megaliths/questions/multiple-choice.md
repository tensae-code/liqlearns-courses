# South India Megaliths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 8, the great stone monuments of South India”?
a) Stone circles and dolmens rise over the southern dead — urn burials and grave goods at places like Adichanallur open a window on the Iron Age Tamil country.  b) The book's one named king earns his chapter through his own words — edicts carved on rock and pillar across an empire, read as evidence, not legend.  c) The centre of civilisation shifts east to the Ganges plain and south into the Deccan, told through the pots, burials and iron the plates display.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Stone circles and dolmens rise over the southern dead — urn burials and grave goods at places like Adichanallur open a window on the Iron Age Tamil country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
