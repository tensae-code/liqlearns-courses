# Sri Lanka: Vijayabahu the First Liberates the Island — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, Vijayabahu I's youth and war of liberation”?
a) A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura.  b) The liberator rebuilds ruined tanks like Maha Kalugalla, restores viharas, brings monks from Burma to restart ordination, and marries into Kalinga and Pandya.  c) A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, political history 1017–1270: liberation”?
a) From a Chola-ruled island, Vijayabahu I fights his way back and is crowned at Anuradhapura in 1070, then rules from Polonnaruwa.  b) A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.  c) He joins tank to tank into the Parakrama Samudra vowing no drop of rain reach the sea unused, unites the Sangha, then strikes Burma in 1164 and restores a Pandya king in 1169.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) From a Chola-ruled island, Vijayabahu I fights his way back and is crowned at Anuradhapura in 1070, then rules from Polonnaruwa.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
