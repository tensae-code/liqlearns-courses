# Sri Lanka: Vijayabahu the First Liberates the Island — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 2, political history 1017–1270: liberation”?
**Answer:** From a Chola-ruled island, Vijayabahu I fights his way back and is crowned at Anuradhapura in 1070, then rules from Polonnaruwa.
