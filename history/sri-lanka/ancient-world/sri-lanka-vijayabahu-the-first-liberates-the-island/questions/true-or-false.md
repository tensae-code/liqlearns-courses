# Sri Lanka: Vijayabahu the First Liberates the Island — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 4, Vijayabahu I's youth and war of liberation”.
**Answer: False** — A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) From a Chola-ruled island, Vijayabahu I fights his way back and is crowned at Anuradhapura in 1070, then rules from Polonnaruwa.
**Answer: True**
