# Video script — Sri Lanka: Vijayabahu the First Liberates the Island

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

From a Chola-ruled island, Vijayabahu I fights his way back and is crowned at Anuradhapura in 1070, then rules from Polonnaruwa. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
