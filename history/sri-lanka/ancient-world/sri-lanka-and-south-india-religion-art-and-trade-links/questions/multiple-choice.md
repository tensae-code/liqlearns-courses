Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 3, faith, art and goods crossing the strait”?
a) Mahayana Buddhism and Hindu worship arrive with traders, Amaravati-style statues are carved, invaders carry off 12,000 prisoners, and Karikala builds a Kaveri dam with their labour.  b) Some came to snatch a throne (Sena-Guttika, Ellalan), some to plunder (Sri Mara Sri Vallabha), and some to plant supremacy (Parantaka, before whom Udaya IV fled).  c) Rich Mahaweli soil, an old royal road, a guard-post against Ruhuna and a port near Trincomalee make the Cholas and kings after them choose it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Mahayana Buddhism and Hindu worship arrive with traders, Amaravati-style statues are carved, invaders carry off 12,000 prisoners, and Karikala builds a Kaveri dam with their labour.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
