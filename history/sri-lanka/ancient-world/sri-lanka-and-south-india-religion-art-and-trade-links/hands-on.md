# Hands-on — Sri Lanka and South India: Religion, Art and Trade Links

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Sri Lanka and South India: Religion, Art and Trade Links — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Mahayana Buddhism and Hindu worship arrive with traders, Amaravati-style statues are carved, invaders carry off 12,000 prisoners, and Karikala builds a Kaveri dam with their labour. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
