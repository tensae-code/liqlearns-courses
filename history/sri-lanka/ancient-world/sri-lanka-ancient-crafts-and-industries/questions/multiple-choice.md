# Sri Lanka: Ancient Crafts and Industries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1, crafts and industries of ancient Sri Lanka”?
a) Excavated gold ornaments shaped like conches, iron work, gems and foreign coins prove an island of skilled makers trading far beyond its shores.  b) Chinese porcelain and coins turn up in island soil, Ibn Battuta visits in 1344, Zheng He's fleet leaves a trilingual stone in 1409, and elephants, pearls and gems sail out as cloth and pottery sail in.  c) Buddhism, Hinduism and later Islam each land with merchants and monks, Sinhala kings take Tamil titles, and three faiths root themselves in one small island.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Excavated gold ornaments shaped like conches, iron work, gems and foreign coins prove an island of skilled makers trading far beyond its shores.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
