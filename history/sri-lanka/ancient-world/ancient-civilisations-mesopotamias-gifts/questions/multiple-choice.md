# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1, Mesopotamia's writing, science and medicine”?
a) Scribes press cuneiform into clay, Hammurabi's code promises an eye for an eye, and the first map and library appear.  b) The Nile floods on time, the pharaoh is held to be a living god, and grain fills the storehouses.  c) A dead king's body is prepared for a second life, a pyramid rises over him, and picture-writing is scratched on stone and papyrus.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Scribes press cuneiform into clay, Hammurabi's code promises an eye for an eye, and the first map and library appear.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
