# Video script — Ancient Civilisations Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Caesar crosses from Gaul to power and is murdered, Augustus founds the empire, Nero fiddles over a burning city, and Caracalla gives citizenship to all. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

The father rules the house, Galen writes 500 medical books, the Julian calendar counts the year, and the Colosseum rises over the city. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
