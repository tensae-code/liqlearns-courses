# Ancient Civilisations Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, Rome's founding and the Republic”?
a) Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place.  b) Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies.  c) Roman ships and coins reach the island's ports, King Bhatikabaya sends envoys to Rome, and pearls and gems sail west in return.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, Rome's emperors from Julius Caesar to Caracalla”?
a) Caesar crosses from Gaul to power and is murdered, Augustus founds the empire, Nero fiddles over a burning city, and Caracalla gives citizenship to all.  b) Roman ships and coins reach the island's ports, King Bhatikabaya sends envoys to Rome, and pearls and gems sail west in return.  c) Mahavira preaches non-violence and the Buddha, born at Lumbini, leaves his palace to find why men suffer.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Caesar crosses from Gaul to power and is murdered, Augustus founds the empire, Nero fiddles over a burning city, and Caracalla gives citizenship to all.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
