# Ancient Civilisations Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 2, Rome's emperors from Julius Caesar to Caracalla”?
**Answer:** Caesar crosses from Gaul to power and is murdered, Augustus founds the empire, Nero fiddles over a burning city, and Caracalla gives citizenship to all.
