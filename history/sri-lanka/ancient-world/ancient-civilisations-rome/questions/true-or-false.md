# Ancient Civilisations Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 2, Rome's founding and the Republic”.
**Answer: False** — Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Caesar crosses from Gaul to power and is murdered, Augustus founds the empire, Nero fiddles over a burning city, and Caracalla gives citizenship to all.
**Answer: True**
