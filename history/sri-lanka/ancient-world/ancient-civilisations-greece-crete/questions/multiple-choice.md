# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, Greece: sources (Homer's Iliad and Odyssey, Hesiod) and the city-states”?
a) Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.  b) In tiny Athens every free man may speak and vote himself, and officials answer to the people who chose them.  c) Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, Greek free thought: philosophers and scientists”?
a) Socrates questions everything and is condemned, Plato writes the Republic, Aristotle sorts the world, Hippocrates fathers medicine and Archimedes finds his principle in the bath.  b) Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place.  c) Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Socrates questions everything and is condemned, Plato writes the Republic, Aristotle sorts the world, Hippocrates fathers medicine and Archimedes finds his principle in the bath.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
