# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 2, Greece: sources (Homer's Iliad and Odyssey, Hesiod) and the city-states”.
**Answer: False** — Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Socrates questions everything and is condemned, Plato writes the Republic, Aristotle sorts the world, Hippocrates fathers medicine and Archimedes finds his principle in the bath.
**Answer: True**
