# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 2, Greek free thought: philosophers and scientists”?
**Answer:** Socrates questions everything and is condemned, Plato writes the Republic, Aristotle sorts the world, Hippocrates fathers medicine and Archimedes finds his principle in the bath.
