**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Ancient World and the Island's Kingdoms (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2, Greece: sources (Homer's Iliad and Odyssey, Hesiod) and the city-states** — Wandering Aryan tribes settle villages that grow into rival city-states, each with its own god on its own acropolis.
2. **Unit 2, Greek free thought: philosophers and scientists** — Socrates questions everything and is condemned, Plato writes the Republic, Aristotle sorts the world, Hippocrates fathers medicine and Archimedes finds his principle in the bath.
3. **Unit 2, Greek gods, temples and sculpture** — Zeus rules from Olympus, Athena gets the Parthenon, and sculptors learn to carve the human body as it truly moves.
4. **Unit 2, Greek daily life, the Olympic games, epic and drama** — Farmers eat simply, runners race at Olympia, and audiences weep and laugh at Aeschylus, Sophocles and Aristophanes.
5. **Unit 2, Alexander the Great spreads Greek civilisation** — Philip's son, king at twenty, carries Greek language and art to Egypt, Persia and the edge of India before dying young.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
