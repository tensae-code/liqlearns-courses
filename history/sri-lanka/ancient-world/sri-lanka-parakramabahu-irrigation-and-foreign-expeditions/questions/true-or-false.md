# Sri Lanka: Parakramabahu Irrigation and Foreign Expeditions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 4, Parakramabahu's tanks and wars abroad”.
**Answer: False** — He joins tank to tank into the Parakrama Samudra vowing no drop of rain reach the sea unused, unites the Sangha, then strikes Burma in 1164 and restores a Pandya king in 1169.

**2.** He joins tank to tank into the Parakrama Samudra vowing no drop of rain reach the sea unused, unites the Sangha, then strikes Burma in 1164 and restores a Pandya king in 1169.
