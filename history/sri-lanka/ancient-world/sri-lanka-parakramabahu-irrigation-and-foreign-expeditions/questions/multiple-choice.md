# Sri Lanka: Parakramabahu Irrigation and Foreign Expeditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, Parakramabahu's tanks and wars abroad”?
a) He joins tank to tank into the Parakrama Samudra vowing no drop of rain reach the sea unused, unites the Sangha, then strikes Burma in 1164 and restores a Pandya king in 1169.  b) A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.  c) Lilavati reigns three times amid Chola invasions, Kalyanavati rules six years, and Sugala Devi fights for her son's claim to the Tooth Relic.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) He joins tank to tank into the Parakrama Samudra vowing no drop of rain reach the sea unused, unites the Sangha, then strikes Burma in 1164 and restores a Pandya king in 1169.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
