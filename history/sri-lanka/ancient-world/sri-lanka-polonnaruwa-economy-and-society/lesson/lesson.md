# Sri Lanka: Polonnaruwa Economy and Society

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Polonnaruwa Economy and Society** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The tank economy at its height and in decline, and the society it carried — castes, villages, monks and merchants. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
