**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 6, economy and social conditions”?
a) The tank economy at its height and in decline, and the society it carried — castes, villages, monks and merchants.  b) The sangha is purged and unified, the Tooth Relic becomes the kingdom's palladium, and Hindu worship stands beside the viharas.  c) Sandesa poems carried by birds, chronicles and medical texts, Gal Vihara's stone Buddhas — the culture the medieval kingdoms left behind.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
