## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 5, the feudal system: how land-for-service began

With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 5, feudal society, manor life, church culture and the system's decline

The manor feeds itself behind its lord's castle while guilds appear in towns, then money, trade and strong kings slowly undo the bargain.
