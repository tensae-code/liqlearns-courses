# Medieval World the Dark Age and Feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 5, the feudal system: how land-for-service began”?
a) With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour.  b) Seljuk Turks bar pilgrims from Jerusalem, the Pope calls Europe to arms, two centuries of holy war fail to hold the city but bring Eastern goods, numbers and learning home.  c) Magha's terror scatters monks and people to the hills, and chiefs set up small strongholds at Yapahuwa, Kurunegala, Gampola and Dambadeniya.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
