# Medieval World the Dark Age and Feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 5, feudal society, manor life, church culture and the system's decline”?
**Answer:** The manor feeds itself behind its lord's castle while guilds appear in towns, then money, trade and strong kings slowly undo the bargain.
