# Medieval World the Dark Age and Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 5, the feudal system: how land-for-service began”.
**Answer: False** — With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The manor feeds itself behind its lord's castle while guilds appear in towns, then money, trade and strong kings slowly undo the bargain.
**Answer: True**
