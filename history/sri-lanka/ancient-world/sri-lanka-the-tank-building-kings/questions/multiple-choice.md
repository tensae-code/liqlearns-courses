# Sri Lanka: The Tank Building Kings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1, the chronology of tank building”?
a) A table marches down the centuries — Pandukabhaya's first tanks, Vasabha, Mahasena, Dhatusena raising Kala Wewa, the Aggabodhis, and Parakramabahu I capping a thousand years of water kings.  b) Excavated gold ornaments shaped like conches, iron work, gems and foreign coins prove an island of skilled makers trading far beyond its shores.  c) Chinese porcelain and coins turn up in island soil, Ibn Battuta visits in 1344, Zheng He's fleet leaves a trilingual stone in 1409, and elephants, pearls and gems sail out as cloth and pottery sail in.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A table marches down the centuries — Pandukabhaya's first tanks, Vasabha, Mahasena, Dhatusena raising Kala Wewa, the Aggabodhis, and Parakramabahu I capping a thousand years of water kings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
