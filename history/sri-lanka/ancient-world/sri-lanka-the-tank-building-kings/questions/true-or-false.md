# Sri Lanka: The Tank Building Kings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 1, the chronology of tank building”.
**Answer: False** — A table marches down the centuries — Pandukabhaya's first tanks, Vasabha, Mahasena, Dhatusena raising Kala Wewa, the Aggabodhis, and Parakramabahu I capping a thousand years of water kings.

**2.** A table marches down the centuries — Pandukabhaya's first tanks, Vasabha, Mahasena, Dhatusena raising Kala Wewa, the Aggabodhis, and Parakramabahu I capping a thousand years of water kings.
**Answer: True**
