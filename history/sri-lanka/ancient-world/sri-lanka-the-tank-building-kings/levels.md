# Sri Lanka: The Tank Building Kings — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A table marches down the centuries — Pandukabhaya's first tanks, Vasabha, Mahasena, Dhatusena raising Kala Wewa, the Aggabodhis, and Parakramabahu I capping a thousand years of water kings — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
