# Why South India Invaded Sri Lanka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 3, the three kinds of invasion”?
a) Some came to snatch a throne (Sena-Guttika, Ellalan), some to plunder (Sri Mara Sri Vallabha), and some to plant supremacy (Parantaka, before whom Udaya IV fled).  b) Rich Mahaweli soil, an old royal road, a guard-post against Ruhuna and a port near Trincomalee make the Cholas and kings after them choose it.  c) A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Some came to snatch a throne (Sena-Guttika, Ellalan), some to plunder (Sri Mara Sri Vallabha), and some to plant supremacy (Parantaka, before whom Udaya IV fled).** — That is what this lesson teaches.
