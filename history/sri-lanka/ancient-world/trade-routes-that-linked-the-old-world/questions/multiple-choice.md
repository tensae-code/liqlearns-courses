Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, Rome's trade with India, China and Sri Lanka”?
a) Roman ships and coins reach the island's ports, King Bhatikabaya sends envoys to Rome, and pearls and gems sail west in return.  b) Mahavira preaches non-violence and the Buddha, born at Lumbini, leaves his palace to find why men suffer.  c) Society is sorted into Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya and Shudra, merchants form guilds, and boys leave home to learn at a teacher's house.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Roman ships and coins reach the island's ports, King Bhatikabaya sends envoys to Rome, and pearls and gems sail west in return.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
