# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Roman ships and coins reach the island's ports, King Bhatikabaya sends envoys to Rome, and pearls and gems sail west in return.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 2, Sri Lanka's ancient foreign connections”?
**Answer:** The island sits at the centre of the Indian Ocean, its ports — Mantota, Gokanna, Mahatittha — linking India, China, Arabia and Rome in one trading web.
