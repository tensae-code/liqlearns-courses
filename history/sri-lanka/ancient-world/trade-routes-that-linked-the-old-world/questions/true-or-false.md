# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 2, Rome's trade with India, China and Sri Lanka”.
**Answer: False** — Roman ships and coins reach the island's ports, King Bhatikabaya sends envoys to Rome, and pearls and gems sail west in return.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The island sits at the centre of the Indian Ocean, its ports — Mantota, Gokanna, Mahatittha — linking India, China, Arabia and Rome in one trading web.
**Answer: True**
