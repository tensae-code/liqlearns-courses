# Sri Lanka: Vijayabahu the First's Services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, Vijayabahu I's political, economic and religious services”?
a) The liberator rebuilds ruined tanks like Maha Kalugalla, restores viharas, brings monks from Burma to restart ordination, and marries into Kalinga and Pandya.  b) A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.  c) He joins tank to tank into the Parakrama Samudra vowing no drop of rain reach the sea unused, unites the Sangha, then strikes Burma in 1164 and restores a Pandya king in 1169.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The liberator rebuilds ruined tanks like Maha Kalugalla, restores viharas, brings monks from Burma to restart ordination, and marries into Kalinga and Pandya.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
