# World Religions the Five Main Faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, India's new religions: Hinduism, Jainism and Buddhism”?
a) Mahavira preaches non-violence and the Buddha, born at Lumbini, leaves his palace to find why men suffer.  b) Society is sorted into Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya and Shudra, merchants form guilds, and boys leave home to learn at a teacher's house.  c) Muhammad, born at Mecca in 570, receives the revelation in the Hira cave, is driven out in the Hijra of 622, and a new era begins.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Mahavira preaches non-violence and the Buddha, born at Lumbini, leaves his palace to find why men suffer.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
