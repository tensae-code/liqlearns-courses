**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, the birth of democracy at Athens”?
a) In tiny Athens every free man may speak and vote himself, and officials answer to the people who chose them.  b) Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, the last king is expelled in 509, and consuls and the Senate rule in his place.  c) Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In tiny Athens every free man may speak and vote himself, and officials answer to the people who chose them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
