# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, Arabia and the rise of Islam”?
a) Muhammad, born at Mecca in 570, receives the revelation in the Hira cave, is driven out in the Hijra of 622, and a new era begins.  b) A thousand-year capital weakens under weak kings, Rajaraja I invades in 993, Mahinda V flees to Ruhuna, and the Cholas rename Polonnaruwa their own.  c) The island becomes Mumudi Chola Mandalam, ruled by a Chola general who taxes its villages and sends the revenue to Thanjavur.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Muhammad, born at Mecca in 570, receives the revelation in the Hira cave, is driven out in the Hijra of 622, and a new era begins.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The five duties bind the faithful, Arab scholars carry Indian numbers and Greek medicine west, build domed mosques, and trade as far as Sri Lanka and China.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
