# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Muhammad, born at Mecca in 570, receives the revelation in the Hira cave, is driven out in the Hijra of 622, and a new era begins.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 2, Islamic teaching and Arab learning, trade and building”?
**Answer:** The five duties bind the faithful, Arab scholars carry Indian numbers and Greek medicine west, build domed mosques, and trade as far as Sri Lanka and China.
