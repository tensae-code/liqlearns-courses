# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 2, Arabia and the rise of Islam”.
**Answer: False** — Muhammad, born at Mecca in 570, receives the revelation in the Hira cave, is driven out in the Hijra of 622, and a new era begins.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The five duties bind the faithful, Arab scholars carry Indian numbers and Greek medicine west, build domed mosques, and trade as far as Sri Lanka and China.
**Answer: True**
