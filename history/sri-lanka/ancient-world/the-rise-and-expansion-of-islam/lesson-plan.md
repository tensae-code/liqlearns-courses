## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2, Arabia and the rise of Islam** — Muhammad, born at Mecca in 570, receives the revelation in the Hira cave, is driven out in the Hijra of 622, and a new era begins.
2. **Unit 2, Islamic teaching and Arab learning, trade and building** — The five duties bind the faithful, Arab scholars carry Indian numbers and Greek medicine west, build domed mosques, and trade as far as Sri Lanka and China.
3. **Ch. 6, medieval Asia: Islam and India** — The caliphs carry Islam across Asia, Delhi's sultans and then Babur's Mughals rule India, Vijayanagara holds the Hindu south, and a 3000-monk massacre in Burma shows faith's darker turns.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
