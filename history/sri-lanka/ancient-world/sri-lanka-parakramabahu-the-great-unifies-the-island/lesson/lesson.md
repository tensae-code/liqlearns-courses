# Sri Lanka: Parakramabahu the Great Unifies the Island

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Parakramabahu the Great Unifies the Island** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4, Parakramabahu I's rise and unification

A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 2, Parakramabahu I and his successors

The great unifier rebuilds a war-torn kingdom, Nissanka Malla boasts in stone, and the line weakens toward invasion. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
