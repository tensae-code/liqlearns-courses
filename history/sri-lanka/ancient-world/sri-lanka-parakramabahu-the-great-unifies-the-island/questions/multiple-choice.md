# Sri Lanka: Parakramabahu the Great Unifies the Island — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, Parakramabahu I's rise and unification”?
a) A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.  b) He joins tank to tank into the Parakrama Samudra vowing no drop of rain reach the sea unused, unites the Sangha, then strikes Burma in 1164 and restores a Pandya king in 1169.  c) A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, Parakramabahu I and his successors”?
a) The great unifier rebuilds a war-torn kingdom, Nissanka Malla boasts in stone, and the line weakens toward invasion.  b) A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.  c) Lilavati reigns three times amid Chola invasions, Kalyanavati rules six years, and Sugala Devi fights for her son's claim to the Tooth Relic.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The great unifier rebuilds a war-torn kingdom, Nissanka Malla boasts in stone, and the line weakens toward invasion.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
