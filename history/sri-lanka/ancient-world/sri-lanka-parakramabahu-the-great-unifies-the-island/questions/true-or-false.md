# Sri Lanka: Parakramabahu the Great Unifies the Island — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 4, Parakramabahu I's rise and unification”.
**Answer: False** — A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The great unifier rebuilds a war-torn kingdom, Nissanka Malla boasts in stone, and the line weakens toward invasion.
**Answer: True**
