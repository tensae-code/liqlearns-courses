# Sri Lanka: Parakramabahu the Great Unifies the Island — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 2, Parakramabahu I and his successors”?
**Answer:** The great unifier rebuilds a war-torn kingdom, Nissanka Malla boasts in stone, and the line weakens toward invasion.
