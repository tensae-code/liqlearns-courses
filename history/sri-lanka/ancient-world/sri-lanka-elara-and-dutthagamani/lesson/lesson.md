# Sri Lanka: Elara and Dutthagamani

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Elara and Dutthagamani** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
