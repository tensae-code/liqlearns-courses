# Sri Lanka: Elara and Dutthagamani — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3, South Indian invasions of Anuradhapura”?
a) Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line.  b) A dry land is made fertile by one linked system — dam, tank, canal, field — and when invasions and neglect break the system, the whole civilisation drains away with its water.  c) Theravada Buddhism roots itself in Burma, Thailand, Java and Cambodia — Angkor Wat rises — and island monks sail both ways, carrying higher ordination between Sri Lanka and Siam in 1753.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
