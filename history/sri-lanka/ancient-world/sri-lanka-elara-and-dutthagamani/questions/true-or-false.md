# Sri Lanka: Elara and Dutthagamani — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 3, South Indian invasions of Anuradhapura”.
**Answer: False** — Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line.

**2.** Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line.
**Answer: True**
