# Ancient Civilisations Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1, why civilisations rose on river banks; Mesopotamia between the Euphrates and Tigris”?
a) Farmers settle the twin rivers, cities like Ur and Babylon grow, and a king's law is carved for all to see.  b) Scribes press cuneiform into clay, Hammurabi's code promises an eye for an eye, and the first map and library appear.  c) The Nile floods on time, the pharaoh is held to be a living god, and grain fills the storehouses.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Farmers settle the twin rivers, cities like Ur and Babylon grow, and a king's law is carved for all to see.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
