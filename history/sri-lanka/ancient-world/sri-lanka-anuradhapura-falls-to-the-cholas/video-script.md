# Video script — Sri Lanka: Anuradhapura Falls to the Cholas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

A thousand-year capital weakens under weak kings, Rajaraja I invades in 993, Mahinda V flees to Ruhuna, and the Cholas rename Polonnaruwa their own. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Rajaraja invades in 993, Rajendra carries King Mahinda V captive to India in 1017 and rules from Polonnaruwa, until Vijayabahu I drives the Cholas out in 1070. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
