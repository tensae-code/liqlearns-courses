# Sri Lanka: Anuradhapura Falls to the Cholas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 3, why Anuradhapura fell and the Chola conquest”?
a) A thousand-year capital weakens under weak kings, Rajaraja I invades in 993, Mahinda V flees to Ruhuna, and the Cholas rename Polonnaruwa their own.  b) The island becomes Mumudi Chola Mandalam, ruled by a Chola general who taxes its villages and sends the revenue to Thanjavur.  c) Four Tamil powers rise and fall across the strait, and each in turn reaches for the island facing it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A thousand-year capital weakens under weak kings, Rajaraja I invades in 993, Mahinda V flees to Ruhuna, and the Cholas rename Polonnaruwa their own.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3, the Chola conquest and liberation”?
a) Rajaraja invades in 993, Rajendra carries King Mahinda V captive to India in 1017 and rules from Polonnaruwa, until Vijayabahu I drives the Cholas out in 1070.  b) Four Tamil powers rise and fall across the strait, and each in turn reaches for the island facing it.  c) Exiled princes like Ilanaga and Moggallana sail to India for armies, Manavamma wins his throne with Pallava help, and royal marriages bind the two shores.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Rajaraja invades in 993, Rajendra carries King Mahinda V captive to India in 1017 and rules from Polonnaruwa, until Vijayabahu I drives the Cholas out in 1070.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
