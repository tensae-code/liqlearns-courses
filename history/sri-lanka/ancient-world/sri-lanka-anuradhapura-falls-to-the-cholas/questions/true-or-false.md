# Sri Lanka: Anuradhapura Falls to the Cholas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 3, why Anuradhapura fell and the Chola conquest”.
**Answer: False** — A thousand-year capital weakens under weak kings, Rajaraja I invades in 993, Mahinda V flees to Ruhuna, and the Cholas rename Polonnaruwa their own.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Rajaraja invades in 993, Rajendra carries King Mahinda V captive to India in 1017 and rules from Polonnaruwa, until Vijayabahu I drives the Cholas out in 1070.
**Answer: True**
