# Sri Lanka: Anuradhapura Falls to the Cholas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A thousand-year capital weakens under weak kings, Rajaraja I invades in 993, Mahinda V flees to Ruhuna, and the Cholas rename Polonnaruwa their own.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 3, the Chola conquest and liberation”?
**Answer:** Rajaraja invades in 993, Rajendra carries King Mahinda V captive to India in 1017 and rules from Polonnaruwa, until Vijayabahu I drives the Cholas out in 1070.
