# Faiths That Shaped States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 5, the Church's order and the Pope”?
a) Peter's successors at Rome stand over patriarchs, bishops and priests, the Church owns land and taxes all Europe, and the Pope becomes its strongest power.  b) With Rome fallen and Vikings raiding, weak kings trade land for soldiers, and lords trade plots to peasants for labour.  c) Seljuk Turks bar pilgrims from Jerusalem, the Pope calls Europe to arms, two centuries of holy war fail to hold the city but bring Eastern goods, numbers and learning home.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Peter's successors at Rome stand over patriarchs, bishops and priests, the Church owns land and taxes all Europe, and the Pope becomes its strongest power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
