# South Indian Kingdoms and Sri Lanka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 3, the Chera, Pallava, Pandya and Chola kingdoms”?
a) Four Tamil powers rise and fall across the strait, and each in turn reaches for the island facing it.  b) Exiled princes like Ilanaga and Moggallana sail to India for armies, Manavamma wins his throne with Pallava help, and royal marriages bind the two shores.  c) Mahayana Buddhism and Hindu worship arrive with traders, Amaravati-style statues are carved, invaders carry off 12,000 prisoners, and Karikala builds a Kaveri dam with their labour.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Four Tamil powers rise and fall across the strait, and each in turn reaches for the island facing it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
