# South Indian Kingdoms and Sri Lanka — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 3, the Chera, Pallava, Pandya and Chola kingdoms”.
**Answer: False** — Four Tamil powers rise and fall across the strait, and each in turn reaches for the island facing it.

**2.** Four Tamil powers rise and fall across the strait, and each in turn reaches for the island facing it.
**Answer: True**
