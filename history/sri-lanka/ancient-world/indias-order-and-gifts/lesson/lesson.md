# India's Order and Gifts

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **India's Order and Gifts** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Villages join into kingdoms under a rajan, the sabha and samiti assemblies check him, priests and generals hold office, and nature gods — Agni, Varuna — rule the Vedic pantheon. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
