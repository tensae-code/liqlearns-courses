# India's Order and Gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, Vedic society and government”?
a) Villages join into kingdoms under a rajan, the sabha and samiti assemblies check him, priests and generals hold office, and nature gods — Agni, Varuna — rule the Vedic pantheon.  b) Horse traders Sena and Guttika seize the throne, the just Tamil king Elara rules 44 years, Dutthagamani defeats him in 161 BCE, seven more Tamil chiefs follow, and Dhatusena at last restores the line.  c) A dry land is made fertile by one linked system — dam, tank, canal, field — and when invasions and neglect break the system, the whole civilisation drains away with its water.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Villages join into kingdoms under a rajan, the sabha and samiti assemblies check him, priests and generals hold office, and nature gods — Agni, Varuna — rule the Vedic pantheon.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
