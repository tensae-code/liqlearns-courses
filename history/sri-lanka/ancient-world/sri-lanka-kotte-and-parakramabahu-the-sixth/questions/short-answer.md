# Sri Lanka: Kotte and Parakramabahu the Sixth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A fortress rises in the marshes, Parakramabahu VI takes Jaffna in 1450 through his adopted son Sapumal Kumaraya, and rules a united island for the last time.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 3, political history 1271–1509: the later kingdoms”?
**Answer:** Yapahuwa's rock fortress, Kurunegala, Gampola, and at last Kotte, where Parakramabahu VI rules a reunified island and receives Zheng He's challenge.
