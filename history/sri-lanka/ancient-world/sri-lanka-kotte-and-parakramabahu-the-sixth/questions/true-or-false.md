# Sri Lanka: Kotte and Parakramabahu the Sixth — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 6, Kotte (1415–1521) and Parakramabahu VI”.
**Answer: False** — A fortress rises in the marshes, Parakramabahu VI takes Jaffna in 1450 through his adopted son Sapumal Kumaraya, and rules a united island for the last time.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Yapahuwa's rock fortress, Kurunegala, Gampola, and at last Kotte, where Parakramabahu VI rules a reunified island and receives Zheng He's challenge.
**Answer: True**
