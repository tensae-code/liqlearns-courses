# Sri Lanka: Kotte and Parakramabahu the Sixth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 6, Kotte (1415–1521) and Parakramabahu VI”?
a) A fortress rises in the marshes, Parakramabahu VI takes Jaffna in 1450 through his adopted son Sapumal Kumaraya, and rules a united island for the last time.  b) The king's three sons murder their father in 1521 and divide Kotte, Sitawaka and Raigama between them, while Jaffna and Kandy stand apart and the Portuguese are already on the coast.  c) Tank civilisation fades as capitals chase safety, paddy gives ground to cinnamon, pepper and arecanut for export, and caste and family life carry on in new towns.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A fortress rises in the marshes, Parakramabahu VI takes Jaffna in 1450 through his adopted son Sapumal Kumaraya, and rules a united island for the last time.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3, political history 1271–1509: the later kingdoms”?
a) Yapahuwa's rock fortress, Kurunegala, Gampola, and at last Kotte, where Parakramabahu VI rules a reunified island and receives Zheng He's challenge.  b) Tank civilisation fades as capitals chase safety, paddy gives ground to cinnamon, pepper and arecanut for export, and caste and family life carry on in new towns.  c) Vijayabahu III restarts the upasampada, scholars rewrite lost books, and Sinhala and Tamil works — from the Dambadeni Kathawa to the Sarasvati Malai — flower under royal patronage.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Yapahuwa's rock fortress, Kurunegala, Gampola, and at last Kotte, where Parakramabahu VI rules a reunified island and receives Zheng He's challenge.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
