# Sri Lanka: The First Settlements — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Vijaya legend is retold and then weighed — a prince exiled with 700 followers, a yakkha princess Kuveni who helps and is betrayed — against the archaeology of real first villages along the rivers — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
