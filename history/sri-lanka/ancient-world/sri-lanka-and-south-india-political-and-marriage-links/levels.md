# Sri Lanka and South India: Political and Marriage Links — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Exiled princes like Ilanaga and Moggallana sail to India for armies, Manavamma wins his throne with Pallava help, and royal marriages bind the two shores — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
