# Sri Lanka: Dambadeniya and Parakramabahu the Second — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 6, Dambadeniya (1232–1270) and Parakramabahu II”?
a) Vijayabahu III founds a safer capital, hides the Tooth Relic on Kotmale hill, and his son wins it back, beats Kalinga Magha in 1255 with Pandya help, and drives off Chandrabhanu of Java.  b) Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.  c) Zheng He's fleet lands in 1405 and again in 1411, the minister Alagakkonara defies him, and the admiral carries the chief off to China before sending him home.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Vijayabahu III founds a safer capital, hides the Tooth Relic on Kotmale hill, and his son wins it back, beats Kalinga Magha in 1255 with Pandya help, and drives off Chandrabhanu of Java.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
