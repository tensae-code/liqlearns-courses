# Sri Lanka: Dambadeniya and Parakramabahu the Second — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 6, Dambadeniya (1232–1270) and Parakramabahu II”.
**Answer: False** — Vijayabahu III founds a safer capital, hides the Tooth Relic on Kotmale hill, and his son wins it back, beats Kalinga Magha in 1255 with Pandya help, and drives off Chandrabhanu of Java.

**2.** Vijayabahu III founds a safer capital, hides the Tooth Relic on Kotmale hill, and his son wins it back, beats Kalinga Magha in 1255 with Pandya help, and drives off Chandrabhanu of Java.
**Answer: True**
