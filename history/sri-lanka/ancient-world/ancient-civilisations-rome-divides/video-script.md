# Video script — Ancient Civilisations Rome Divides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Weak emperors, an unpaid and corrupt army, civil wars and heavy taxes leave the west open to the Germanic tribes. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
