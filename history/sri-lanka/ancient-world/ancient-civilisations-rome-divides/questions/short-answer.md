# Ancient Civilisations Rome Divides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 2, why the Roman empire fell”?
**Answer:** Weak emperors, an unpaid and corrupt army, civil wars and heavy taxes leave the west open to the Germanic tribes.
