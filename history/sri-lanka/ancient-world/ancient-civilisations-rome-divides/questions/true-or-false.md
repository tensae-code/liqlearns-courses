# Ancient Civilisations Rome Divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 2, Constantine, Justinian and Roman law”.
**Answer: False** — Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Weak emperors, an unpaid and corrupt army, civil wars and heavy taxes leave the west open to the Germanic tribes.
**Answer: True**
