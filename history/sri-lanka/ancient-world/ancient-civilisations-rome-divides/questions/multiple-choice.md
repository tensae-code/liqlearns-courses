# Ancient Civilisations Rome Divides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, Constantine, Justinian and Roman law”?
a) Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies.  b) Roman ships and coins reach the island's ports, King Bhatikabaya sends envoys to Rome, and pearls and gems sail west in return.  c) Mahavira preaches non-violence and the Buddha, born at Lumbini, leaves his palace to find why men suffer.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Constantine moves the capital east and it outlives the west to 1453, while Justinian's code orders the laws the modern world still studies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2, why the Roman empire fell”?
a) Weak emperors, an unpaid and corrupt army, civil wars and heavy taxes leave the west open to the Germanic tribes.  b) Mahavira preaches non-violence and the Buddha, born at Lumbini, leaves his palace to find why men suffer.  c) Society is sorted into Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya and Shudra, merchants form guilds, and boys leave home to learn at a teacher's house.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Weak emperors, an unpaid and corrupt army, civil wars and heavy taxes leave the west open to the Germanic tribes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
