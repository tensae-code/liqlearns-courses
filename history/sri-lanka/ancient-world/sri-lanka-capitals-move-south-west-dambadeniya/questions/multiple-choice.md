# Sri Lanka: Capitals Move South-West — Dambadeniya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 6, after Magha: the court flees south-west”?
a) Magha's terror scatters monks and people to the hills, and chiefs set up small strongholds at Yapahuwa, Kurunegala, Gampola and Dambadeniya.  b) Vijayabahu III founds a safer capital, hides the Tooth Relic on Kotmale hill, and his son wins it back, beats Kalinga Magha in 1255 with Pandya help, and drives off Chandrabhanu of Java.  c) Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Magha's terror scatters monks and people to the hills, and chiefs set up small strongholds at Yapahuwa, Kurunegala, Gampola and Dambadeniya.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
