# Sri Lanka: The Island Splits, 1521 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The king's three sons murder their father in 1521 and divide Kotte, Sitawaka and Raigama between them, while Jaffna and Kandy stand apart and the Portuguese are already on the coast — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
