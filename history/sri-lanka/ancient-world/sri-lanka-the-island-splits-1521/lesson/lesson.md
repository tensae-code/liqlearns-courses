# Sri Lanka: The Island Splits, 1521

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: The Island Splits, 1521** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The king's three sons murder their father in 1521 and divide Kotte, Sitawaka and Raigama between them, while Jaffna and Kandy stand apart and the Portuguese are already on the coast. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
