# Sri Lanka: The Island Splits, 1521 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 6, the splitting of Kotte”.
**Answer: False** — The king's three sons murder their father in 1521 and divide Kotte, Sitawaka and Raigama between them, while Jaffna and Kandy stand apart and the Portuguese are already on the coast.
