# Sri Lanka: Magha Invasion and Polonnaruwa Falls — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 2, Magha and the move to Dambadeniya”?
**Answer:** Kalinga Magha's 1215 invasion breaks Polonnaruwa for good, and the kingdom's centre of gravity slides south-west to Dambadeniya.
