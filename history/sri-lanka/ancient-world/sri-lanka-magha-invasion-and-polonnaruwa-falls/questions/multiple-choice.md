# Sri Lanka: Magha Invasion and Polonnaruwa Falls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, Kalinga Magha's invasion and the fall of Polonnaruwa”?
a) Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt.  b) Great kings, high status for women, Shiva temples beside viharas, moonstones without the bull, and a planned royal city of palaces and lotus ponds.  c) Historians draw the line at Rome's fall and again at the 15th century, and the middle age gets its name.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Kalinga Magha's 1215 invasion breaks Polonnaruwa for good, and the kingdom's centre of gravity slides south-west to Dambadeniya.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
