# Sri Lanka: Magha Invasion and Polonnaruwa Falls — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Unit 4, Kalinga Magha's invasion and the fall of Polonnaruwa”.
**Answer: False** — Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Kalinga Magha's 1215 invasion breaks Polonnaruwa for good, and the kingdom's centre of gravity slides south-west to Dambadeniya.
**Answer: True**
