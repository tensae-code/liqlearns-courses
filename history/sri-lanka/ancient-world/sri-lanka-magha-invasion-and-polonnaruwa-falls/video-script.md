# Video script — Sri Lanka: Magha Invasion and Polonnaruwa Falls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Kalinga Magha's 1215 invasion breaks Polonnaruwa for good, and the kingdom's centre of gravity slides south-west to Dambadeniya. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
