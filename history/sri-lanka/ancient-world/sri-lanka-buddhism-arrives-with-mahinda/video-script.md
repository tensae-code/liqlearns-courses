# Video script — Sri Lanka: Buddhism Arrives With Mahinda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Ashoka sends his own son Mahinda, who meets King Devanampiyatissa hunting on Mihintale, preaches, and stays — and the king who hosted Asoka's envoys for five months at Pataliputra gives the new faith a kingdom. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
