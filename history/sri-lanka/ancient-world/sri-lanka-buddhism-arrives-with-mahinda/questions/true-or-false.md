# Sri Lanka: Buddhism Arrives With Mahinda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 4, Buddhism established in Sri Lanka”.
**Answer: False** — Ashoka sends his own son Mahinda, who meets King Devanampiyatissa hunting on Mihintale, preaches, and stays — and the king who hosted Asoka's envoys for five months at Pataliputra gives the new faith a kingdom.

**2.** Ashoka sends his own son Mahinda, who meets King Devanampiyatissa hunting on Mihintale, preaches, and stays — and the king who hosted Asoka's envoys for five months at Pataliputra gives the new faith a kingdom.
**Answer: True**
