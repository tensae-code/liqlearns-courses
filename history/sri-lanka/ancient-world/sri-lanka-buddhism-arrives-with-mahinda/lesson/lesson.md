# Sri Lanka: Buddhism Arrives With Mahinda

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Buddhism Arrives With Mahinda** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Ashoka sends his own son Mahinda, who meets King Devanampiyatissa hunting on Mihintale, preaches, and stays — and the king who hosted Asoka's envoys for five months at Pataliputra gives the new faith a kingdom. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
