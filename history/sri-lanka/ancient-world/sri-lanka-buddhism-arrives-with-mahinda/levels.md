# Sri Lanka: Buddhism Arrives With Mahinda — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Ashoka sends his own son Mahinda, who meets King Devanampiyatissa hunting on Mihintale, preaches, and stays — and the king who hosted Asoka's envoys for five months at Pataliputra gives the new faith a kingdom — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
