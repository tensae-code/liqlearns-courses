# Sri Lanka: How Faiths Came by Sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 2, religions arrive with the traders”.
**Answer: False** — Buddhism, Hinduism and later Islam each land with merchants and monks, Sinhala kings take Tamil titles, and three faiths root themselves in one small island.

**2.** Buddhism, Hinduism and later Islam each land with merchants and monks, Sinhala kings take Tamil titles, and three faiths root themselves in one small island.
**Answer: True**
