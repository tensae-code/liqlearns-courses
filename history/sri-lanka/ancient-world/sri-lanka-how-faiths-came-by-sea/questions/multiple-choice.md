# Sri Lanka: How Faiths Came by Sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, religions arrive with the traders”?
a) Buddhism, Hinduism and later Islam each land with merchants and monks, Sinhala kings take Tamil titles, and three faiths root themselves in one small island.  b) The king consults his great council before deciding, the land is run in rata divisions under disavas, villages govern themselves, and temple lands answer to their own officers.  c) A balance diagram weighs the crimes — fines and service on one side, flogging and death on the other — while Robert Knox's account shows how ordinary Kandyan homes really lived.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Buddhism, Hinduism and later Islam each land with merchants and monks, Sinhala kings take Tamil titles, and three faiths root themselves in one small island.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
