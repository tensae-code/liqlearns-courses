# Sri Lanka: Polonnaruwa Why the Capital Moved — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4, why Polonnaruwa became the capital”?
a) Rich Mahaweli soil, an old royal road, a guard-post against Ruhuna and a port near Trincomalee make the Cholas and kings after them choose it.  b) A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura.  c) The liberator rebuilds ruined tanks like Maha Kalugalla, restores viharas, brings monks from Burma to restart ordination, and marries into Kalinga and Pandya.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Rich Mahaweli soil, an old royal road, a guard-post against Ruhuna and a port near Trincomalee make the Cholas and kings after them choose it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
