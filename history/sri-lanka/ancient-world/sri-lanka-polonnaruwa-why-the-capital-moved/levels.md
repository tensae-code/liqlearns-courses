# Sri Lanka: Polonnaruwa Why the Capital Moved — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Rich Mahaweli soil, an old royal road, a guard-post against Ruhuna and a port near Trincomalee make the Cholas and kings after them choose it — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
