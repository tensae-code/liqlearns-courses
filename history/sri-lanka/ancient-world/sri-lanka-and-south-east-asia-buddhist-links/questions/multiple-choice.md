# Sri Lanka and South-East Asia: Buddhist Links — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 6, Buddhism in South-East Asia and Sri Lanka's links”?
a) Theravada Buddhism roots itself in Burma, Thailand, Java and Cambodia — Angkor Wat rises — and island monks sail both ways, carrying higher ordination between Sri Lanka and Siam in 1753.  b) Spain and Portugal open the race, da Gama reaches India in 1498, the Dutch found the VOC in 1602 and out-trade them all, England and France follow, and Christianity sails with every flag.  c) A storm blows Lourenço de Almeida into Galle in 1505, the strangers in strange dress are led to Kotte by a road made long on purpose — "like going to the Portuguese fort" becomes a proverb.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Theravada Buddhism roots itself in Burma, Thailand, Java and Cambodia — Angkor Wat rises — and island monks sail both ways, carrying higher ordination between Sri Lanka and Siam in 1753.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
