# Sri Lanka: After Donoughmore — Education and Health, 1931 to 1948 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Universal suffrage brings ministers who answer to voters: C. W. W. Kannangara makes education free in 1945, malaria is beaten back by 1946 with new sprays, death rates fall and the population surges — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
