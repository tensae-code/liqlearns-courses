# Video script — Sri Lanka: After Donoughmore — Education and Health, 1931 to 1948

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Universal suffrage brings ministers who answer to voters: C. W. W. Kannangara makes education free in 1945, malaria is beaten back by 1946 with new sprays, death rates fall and the population surges. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
