# India: Leaders of the Freedom Struggle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4, the leaders in their own life stories”?
a) Dadabhai Naoroji, the Grand Old Man, proves Britain drains India's wealth; Surendranath Banerji builds the first national movement; Tilak declares swaraj his birthright; Maulana Azad stands for unity.  b) A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.  c) 1910 brings the first elected member, the communities unite in the 1919 National Congress, and the Manning constitutions of 1920 and 1924 give elected majorities on paper while the Governor keeps the real power.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Dadabhai Naoroji, the Grand Old Man, proves Britain drains India's wealth; Surendranath Banerji builds the first national movement; Tilak declares swaraj his birthright; Maulana Azad stands for unity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
