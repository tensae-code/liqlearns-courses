# Sri Lanka: Dutch Administration and Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1, Dutch government, courts and the thombo registers”?
a) The Land Raad gives villagers cheap justice, and clerks write every family, field and tax into thombo books — one man's 195 coconut palms owe a single rix-dollar.  b) Cinnamon is a company monopoly guarded by death penalties, canals are dug, tanks restored, and pepper, coffee and tobacco planted for export.  c) The Reformed Church and its schools spread for 138 years, the first printing press arrives in 1739, and Dutch words for cupboards and lawyers, new foods and the Burgher community remain in the island's life.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Land Raad gives villagers cheap justice, and clerks write every family, field and tax into thombo books — one man's 195 coconut palms owe a single rix-dollar.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
