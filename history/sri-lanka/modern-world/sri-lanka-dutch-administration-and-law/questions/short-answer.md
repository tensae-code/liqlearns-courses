# Sri Lanka: Dutch Administration and Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Land Raad gives villagers cheap justice, and clerks write every family, field and tax into thombo books — one man's 195 coconut palms owe a single rix-dollar.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.1 second half, Dutch administration, economy and culture”?
**Answer:** The class collects what the Dutch left behind — words, clothes, foods, furniture, new port towns — and exhibits them as a wall paper.
