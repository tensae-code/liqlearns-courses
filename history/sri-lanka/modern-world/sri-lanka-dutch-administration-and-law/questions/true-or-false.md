# Sri Lanka: Dutch Administration and Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 1, Dutch government, courts and the thombo registers”.
**Answer: False** — The Land Raad gives villagers cheap justice, and clerks write every family, field and tax into thombo books — one man's 195 coconut palms owe a single rix-dollar.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The class collects what the Dutch left behind — words, clothes, foods, furniture, new port towns — and exhibits them as a wall paper.
**Answer: True**
