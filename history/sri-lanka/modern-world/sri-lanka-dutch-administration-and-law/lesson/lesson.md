# Sri Lanka: Dutch Administration and Law

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Dutch Administration and Law** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 1, Dutch government, courts and the thombo registers

The Land Raad gives villagers cheap justice, and clerks write every family, field and tax into thombo books — one man's 195 coconut palms owe a single rix-dollar. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 9.1 second half, Dutch administration, economy and culture

The class collects what the Dutch left behind — words, clothes, foods, furniture, new port towns — and exhibits them as a wall paper. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
