# The Industrial Revolution Spreads — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 10, the revolution spreads and France lags”.
**Answer: False** — The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late.

**2.** The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late.
**Answer: True**
