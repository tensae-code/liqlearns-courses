# The Industrial Revolution Spreads — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 10, the revolution spreads and France lags”?
a) The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late.  b) Louis XIV boasts "I am the state", the Estates-General sleeps from 1614 to 1789, the Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, the Bastille falls, rights are declared — and by 1793 the king and Marie Antoinette are guillotined.  c) The book pauses to define it — a people bound by land, language, race, religion and custom, claiming the right to rule themselves — the idea the revolution loosed on the world.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
