# Sri Lanka: Reforms 1910 to 1924 and the National Congress — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** 1910 brings the first elected member, the communities unite in the 1919 National Congress, and the Manning constitutions of 1920 and 1924 give elected majorities on paper while the Governor keeps the real power.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 6, the national movement organises”?
**Answer:** James Peiris's 1908 memorandum demands reform, the 1910 change lets Ramanathan become the first elected Ceylonese member, and temperance halls become the movement's recruiting grounds.
