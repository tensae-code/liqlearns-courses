# Sri Lanka: Reforms 1910 to 1924 and the National Congress — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 5, McCallum, Manning and the Ceylon National Congress”.
**Answer: False** — 1910 brings the first elected member, the communities unite in the 1919 National Congress, and the Manning constitutions of 1920 and 1924 give elected majorities on paper while the Governor keeps the real power.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) James Peiris's 1908 memorandum demands reform, the 1910 change lets Ramanathan become the first elected Ceylonese member, and temperance halls become the movement's recruiting grounds.
**Answer: True**
