# Sri Lanka: Reforms 1910 to 1924 and the National Congress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, McCallum, Manning and the Ceylon National Congress”?
a) 1910 brings the first elected member, the communities unite in the 1919 National Congress, and the Manning constitutions of 1920 and 1924 give elected majorities on paper while the Governor keeps the real power.  b) The 1931 constitution abolishes communal seats, gives every adult the vote — a first in Asia — and runs the island through a State Council of executive committees.  c) Wartime cooperation earns trust, Lord Soulbury's 1947 constitution creates a cabinet and two chambers, the Governor-General replaces the Governor, and in 1948 Ceylon is independent.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) 1910 brings the first elected member, the communities unite in the 1919 National Congress, and the Manning constitutions of 1920 and 1924 give elected majorities on paper while the Governor keeps the real power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
