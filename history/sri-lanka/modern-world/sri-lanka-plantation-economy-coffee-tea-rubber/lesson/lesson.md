## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 2, the plantation economy

Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
