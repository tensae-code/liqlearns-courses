# Sri Lanka: Plantation Economy Coffee Tea Rubber — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.2 second half, economic development under the British (competency level 2.2)”?
**Answer:** Pupils fill a two-column chart — plantation agriculture introduced, indigenous agriculture declined — and argue the gains and losses of the estate economy.
