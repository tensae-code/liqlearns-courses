# Sri Lanka: Plantation Economy Coffee Tea Rubber — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 2, the plantation economy”.
**Answer: False** — Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pupils fill a two-column chart — plantation agriculture introduced, indigenous agriculture declined — and argue the gains and losses of the estate economy.
**Answer: True**
