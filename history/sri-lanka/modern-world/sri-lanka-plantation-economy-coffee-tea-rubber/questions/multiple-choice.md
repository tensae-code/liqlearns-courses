# Sri Lanka: Plantation Economy Coffee Tea Rubber — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, the plantation economy”?
a) Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.  b) Peasant farming declines as land and labour flow to the estates, until restored tanks, an irrigation department and cooperatives try to lift it again.  c) Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
