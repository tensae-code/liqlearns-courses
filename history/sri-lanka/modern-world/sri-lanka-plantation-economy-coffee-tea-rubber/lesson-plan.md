## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 2, the plantation economy** — Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.
2. **Unit 9.2 second half, economic development under the British (competency level 2.2)** — Pupils fill a two-column chart — plantation agriculture introduced, indigenous agriculture declined — and argue the gains and losses of the estate economy.
3. **Ch. 4, changes in the economic system** — The old self-sufficient village economy is dismantled and a plantation export economy built in its place.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
