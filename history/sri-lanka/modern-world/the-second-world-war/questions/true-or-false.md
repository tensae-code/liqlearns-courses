# The Second World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 11, the road to the Second World War”.
**Answer: False** — Versailles humiliates Germany, the 1929 crash ruins millions, Mussolini takes Italy in 1922 and Hitler rides the despair — the peace built in 1919 collapses in twenty years.

**2.** Versailles humiliates Germany, the 1929 crash ruins millions, Mussolini takes Italy in 1922 and Hitler rides the despair — the peace built in 1919 collapses in twenty years.
**Answer: True**
