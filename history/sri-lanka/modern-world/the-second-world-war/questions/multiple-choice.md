# The Second World War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 11, the road to the Second World War”?
a) Versailles humiliates Germany, the 1929 crash ruins millions, Mussolini takes Italy in 1922 and Hitler rides the despair — the peace built in 1919 collapses in twenty years.  b) The Dipavamsa comes first, then the monk Mahanama shapes the Mahavamsa in the 5th–6th century — 37 chapters ending with Mahasena — and later hands continue it as the Culavamsa, while Wilhelm Geiger's edition lets the world read them critically.  c) A tear-drop island below India, a dry north and a wet south-west — the land itself writes the first draft of the island's history.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Versailles humiliates Germany, the 1929 crash ruins millions, Mussolini takes Italy in 1922 and Hitler rides the despair — the peace built in 1919 collapses in twenty years.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
