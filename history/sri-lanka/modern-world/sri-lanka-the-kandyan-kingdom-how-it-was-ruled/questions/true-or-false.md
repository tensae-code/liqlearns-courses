# Sri Lanka: The Kandyan Kingdom How it Was Ruled — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 3, government of the Kandyan kingdom”.
**Answer: False** — The king consults his great council before deciding, the land is run in rata divisions under disavas, villages govern themselves, and temple lands answer to their own officers.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) After Rajadhi Rajasinghe dies in 1798 the young Sri Vikrama takes a throne ringed by mighty chiefs, the first Adigar Pilima Talauwa pulls the strings, and a kingdom run by hereditary officials waits for its crisis.
**Answer: True**
