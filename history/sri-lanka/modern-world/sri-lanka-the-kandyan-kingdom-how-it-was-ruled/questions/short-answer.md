# Sri Lanka: The Kandyan Kingdom How it Was Ruled — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The king consults his great council before deciding, the land is run in rata divisions under disavas, villages govern themselves, and temple lands answer to their own officers.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 2, the Kandyan kingdom 1789–1815”?
**Answer:** After Rajadhi Rajasinghe dies in 1798 the young Sri Vikrama takes a throne ringed by mighty chiefs, the first Adigar Pilima Talauwa pulls the strings, and a kingdom run by hereditary officials waits for its crisis.
