# Video script — Sri Lanka: The Kandyan Kingdom How it Was Ruled

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The king consults his great council before deciding, the land is run in rata divisions under disavas, villages govern themselves, and temple lands answer to their own officers. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

After Rajadhi Rajasinghe dies in 1798 the young Sri Vikrama takes a throne ringed by mighty chiefs, the first Adigar Pilima Talauwa pulls the strings, and a kingdom run by hereditary officials waits for its crisis. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
