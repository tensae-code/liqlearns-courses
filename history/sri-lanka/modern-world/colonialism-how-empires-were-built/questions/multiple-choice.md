**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 9, colonialism (குடியேற்ற வாதம்)”?
a) The book names the system itself — how trade posts became empires and whole continents were divided among a few European powers.  b) Alliances and rivalry drag Europe into a new kind of war — aircraft, submarines and poison gas — that kills beyond all precedent.  c) President Woodrow Wilson's plan founds the League of Nations in 1919 with 42 members and three noble aims — but America stays out, no army exists, and the League fails.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The book names the system itself — how trade posts became empires and whole continents were divided among a few European powers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
