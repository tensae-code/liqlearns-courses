# India: Moderates Extremists and Swadeshi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4, moderates, extremists and the Swadeshi movement”?
a) When petitions fail, Tilak's generation demands swaraj, Bengal's partition is answered by boycotting British goods and burning foreign cloth.  b) Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it.  c) Dadabhai Naoroji, the Grand Old Man, proves Britain drains India's wealth; Surendranath Banerji builds the first national movement; Tilak declares swaraj his birthright; Maulana Azad stands for unity.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) When petitions fail, Tilak's generation demands swaraj, Bengal's partition is answered by boycotting British goods and burning foreign cloth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
