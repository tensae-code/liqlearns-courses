# The Industrial Revolution Begins in England

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Industrial Revolution Begins in England** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 10, why the Industrial Revolution began in England

Coal, colonies, capital and enclosed fields converge — small farms are fenced into great estates, the dispossessed stream to the towns, and the factory is born. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 8, the Industrial Revolution and its consequences

The machine age remakes work, towns and classes, and its effects roll outward to colonies like Ceylon. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
