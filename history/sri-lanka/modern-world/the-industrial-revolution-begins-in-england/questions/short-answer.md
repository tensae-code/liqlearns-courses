# The Industrial Revolution Begins in England — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Coal, colonies, capital and enclosed fields converge — small farms are fenced into great estates, the dispossessed stream to the towns, and the factory is born.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 8, the Industrial Revolution and its consequences”?
**Answer:** The machine age remakes work, towns and classes, and its effects roll outward to colonies like Ceylon.
