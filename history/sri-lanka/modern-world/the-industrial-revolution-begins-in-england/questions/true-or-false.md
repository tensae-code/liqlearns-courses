# The Industrial Revolution Begins in England — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 10, why the Industrial Revolution began in England”.
**Answer: False** — Coal, colonies, capital and enclosed fields converge — small farms are fenced into great estates, the dispossessed stream to the towns, and the factory is born.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The machine age remakes work, towns and classes, and its effects roll outward to colonies like Ceylon.
**Answer: True**
