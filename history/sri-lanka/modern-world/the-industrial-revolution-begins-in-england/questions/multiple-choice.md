# The Industrial Revolution Begins in England — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 10, why the Industrial Revolution began in England”?
a) Coal, colonies, capital and enclosed fields converge — small farms are fenced into great estates, the dispossessed stream to the towns, and the factory is born.  b) The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late.  c) Louis XIV boasts "I am the state", the Estates-General sleeps from 1614 to 1789, the Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, the Bastille falls, rights are declared — and by 1793 the king and Marie Antoinette are guillotined.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Coal, colonies, capital and enclosed fields converge — small farms are fenced into great estates, the dispossessed stream to the towns, and the factory is born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 8, the Industrial Revolution and its consequences”?
a) The machine age remakes work, towns and classes, and its effects roll outward to colonies like Ceylon.  b) Louis XIV boasts "I am the state", the Estates-General sleeps from 1614 to 1789, the Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, the Bastille falls, rights are declared — and by 1793 the king and Marie Antoinette are guillotined.  c) The book pauses to define it — a people bound by land, language, race, religion and custom, claiming the right to rule themselves — the idea the revolution loosed on the world.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The machine age remakes work, towns and classes, and its effects roll outward to colonies like Ceylon.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
