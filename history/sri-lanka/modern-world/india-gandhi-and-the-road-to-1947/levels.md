# India: Gandhi and the Road to 1947 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
