# India: Gandhi and the Road to 1947 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4, Gandhi, Quit India and independence”?
a) Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it.  b) Dadabhai Naoroji, the Grand Old Man, proves Britain drains India's wealth; Surendranath Banerji builds the first national movement; Tilak declares swaraj his birthright; Maulana Azad stands for unity.  c) A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
