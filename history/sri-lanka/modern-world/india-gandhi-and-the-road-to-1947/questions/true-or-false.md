# India: Gandhi and the Road to 1947 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 4, Gandhi, Quit India and independence”.
**Answer: False** — Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it.

**2.** Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it.
**Answer: True**
