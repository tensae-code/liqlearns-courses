# Sri Lanka: Kotte Splits and Dharmapala — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 9, Kotte breaks and bends to Lisbon”?
a) Mayadunne of Sitawaka wars on his brother's Kotte, the 1521 Vijayabahu killing splits the kingdom three ways, and the converted Dharmapala signs his kingdom away to Portugal.  b) Coal, colonies, capital and enclosed fields converge — small farms are fenced into great estates, the dispossessed stream to the towns, and the factory is born.  c) The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Mayadunne of Sitawaka wars on his brother's Kotte, the 1521 Vijayabahu killing splits the kingdom three ways, and the converted Dharmapala signs his kingdom away to Portugal.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
