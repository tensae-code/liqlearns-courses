# Sri Lanka: Kotte Splits and Dharmapala — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 9, Kotte breaks and bends to Lisbon”.
**Answer: False** — Mayadunne of Sitawaka wars on his brother's Kotte, the 1521 Vijayabahu killing splits the kingdom three ways, and the converted Dharmapala signs his kingdom away to Portugal.

**2.** Mayadunne of Sitawaka wars on his brother's Kotte, the 1521 Vijayabahu killing splits the kingdom three ways, and the converted Dharmapala signs his kingdom away to Portugal.
**Answer: True**
