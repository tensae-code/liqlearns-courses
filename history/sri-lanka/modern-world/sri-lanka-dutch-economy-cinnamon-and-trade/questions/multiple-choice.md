# Sri Lanka: Dutch Economy Cinnamon and Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1, Dutch economic policy”?
a) Cinnamon is a company monopoly guarded by death penalties, canals are dug, tanks restored, and pepper, coffee and tobacco planted for export.  b) The Reformed Church and its schools spread for 138 years, the first printing press arrives in 1739, and Dutch words for cupboards and lawyers, new foods and the Burgher community remain in the island's life.  c) Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Cinnamon is a company monopoly guarded by death penalties, canals are dug, tanks restored, and pepper, coffee and tobacco planted for export.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
