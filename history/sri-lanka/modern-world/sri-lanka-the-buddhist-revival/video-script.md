# Video script — Sri Lanka: The Buddhist Revival

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Pupils draw lots bearing the names Olcott, Dharmapala, Sumangala, Gunananda, Navalar and Siddhi Lebbe and must speak for three minutes as that revival leader in a musical-hat game. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
