# Sri Lanka: The Buddhist Revival — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3, the Buddhist revival”?
a) Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873.  b) The American Henry Steel Olcott and Helena Blavatsky land in 1880, take refuge as Buddhists, and drive a school-building wave — Ananda, Mahinda, Dharmaraja — that also designs the Buddhist flag.  c) Arumuga Navalar answers the missionaries with Tamil prose and Hindu schools in Jaffna, while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe, aided by the exiled Arabi Pasha, builds Muslim education.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
