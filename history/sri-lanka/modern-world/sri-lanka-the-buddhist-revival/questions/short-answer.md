# Sri Lanka: The Buddhist Revival — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.3, religious and cultural revival (competency level 3.1, 8 periods)”?
**Answer:** Pupils draw lots bearing the names Olcott, Dharmapala, Sumangala, Gunananda, Navalar and Siddhi Lebbe and must speak for three minutes as that revival leader in a musical-hat game.
