# Sri Lanka: The Buddhist Revival — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 3, the Buddhist revival”.
**Answer: False** — Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pupils draw lots bearing the names Olcott, Dharmapala, Sumangala, Gunananda, Navalar and Siddhi Lebbe and must speak for three minutes as that revival leader in a musical-hat game.
**Answer: True**
