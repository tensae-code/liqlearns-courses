## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 5, the Colebrooke–Cameron reforms** — A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.
2. **Unit 9.5, constitutional reforms and the independence movement (competency level 5.1)** — Groups complete one great table — year, reform, legislature, members, changes — marching from Colebrooke 1833 through Donoughmore to Soulbury, so parliament is seen as a gradual evolution.
3. **Ch. 3, development of the constitutional system** — From the 1833 Colebrooke–Cameron settlement the book walks each reform forward, constitution by constitution, toward 1948.
