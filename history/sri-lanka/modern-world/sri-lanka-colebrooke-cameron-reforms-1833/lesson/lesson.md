# Sri Lanka: Colebrooke–Cameron Reforms, 1833

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Colebrooke–Cameron Reforms, 1833** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 5, the Colebrooke–Cameron reforms

A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
