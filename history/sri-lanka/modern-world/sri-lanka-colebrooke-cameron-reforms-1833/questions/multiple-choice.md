# Sri Lanka: Colebrooke–Cameron Reforms, 1833 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, the Colebrooke–Cameron reforms”?
a) A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.  b) 1910 brings the first elected member, the communities unite in the 1919 National Congress, and the Manning constitutions of 1920 and 1924 give elected majorities on paper while the Governor keeps the real power.  c) The 1931 constitution abolishes communal seats, gives every adult the vote — a first in Asia — and runs the island through a State Council of executive committees.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
