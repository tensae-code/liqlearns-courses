# Sri Lanka: Colebrooke–Cameron Reforms, 1833 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 5, the Colebrooke–Cameron reforms”.
**Answer: False** — A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Groups complete one great table — year, reform, legislature, members, changes — marching from Colebrooke 1833 through Donoughmore to Soulbury, so parliament is seen as a gradual evolution.
**Answer: True**
