# Sri Lanka: Colebrooke–Cameron Reforms, 1833 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.5, constitutional reforms and the independence movement (competency level 5.1)”?
**Answer:** Groups complete one great table — year, reform, legislature, members, changes — marching from Colebrooke 1833 through Donoughmore to Soulbury, so parliament is seen as a gradual evolution.
