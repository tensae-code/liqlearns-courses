# Video script — Sri Lanka: Colebrooke–Cameron Reforms, 1833

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Groups complete one great table — year, reform, legislature, members, changes — marching from Colebrooke 1833 through Donoughmore to Soulbury, so parliament is seen as a gradual evolution. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

From the 1833 Colebrooke–Cameron settlement the book walks each reform forward, constitution by constitution, toward 1948. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
