# Sri Lanka: The 1818 Uva Rebellion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, the 1818 freedom struggle”?
a) Keppetipola leads Uva in revolt, the British burn villages to crush it, and the 1818 Kandyan proclamation tightens their grip and strips the chiefs.  b) Governor Torrington's new taxes on dogs, guns and shops spark a rising, Gongalegoda Banda is crowned at Dambulla, Puran Appu leads the attack on Matale, and martial law shoots the leaders.  c) Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Keppetipola leads Uva in revolt, the British burn villages to crush it, and the 1818 Kandyan proclamation tightens their grip and strips the chiefs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
