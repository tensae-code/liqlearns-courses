# Sri Lanka: The 1818 Uva Rebellion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 2, the 1818 freedom struggle”.
**Answer: False** — Keppetipola leads Uva in revolt, the British burn villages to crush it, and the 1818 Kandyan proclamation tightens their grip and strips the chiefs.

**2.** Keppetipola leads Uva in revolt, the British burn villages to crush it, and the 1818 Kandyan proclamation tightens their grip and strips the chiefs.
**Answer: True**
