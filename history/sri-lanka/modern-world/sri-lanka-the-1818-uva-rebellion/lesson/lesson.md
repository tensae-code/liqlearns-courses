# Sri Lanka: The 1818 Uva Rebellion

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: The 1818 Uva Rebellion** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Keppetipola leads Uva in revolt, the British burn villages to crush it, and the 1818 Kandyan proclamation tightens their grip and strips the chiefs. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
