# Sri Lanka: The Portuguese Arrive, 1505 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A storm blows Lourenço de Almeida into Galle in 1505, the strangers in strange dress are led to Kotte by a road made long on purpose — "like going to the Portuguese fort" becomes a proverb — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
