# Sri Lanka: The Portuguese Arrive, 1505 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 9, Sri Lanka and the West: the Portuguese come”?
a) A storm blows Lourenço de Almeida into Galle in 1505, the strangers in strange dress are led to Kotte by a road made long on purpose — "like going to the Portuguese fort" becomes a proverb.  b) Mayadunne of Sitawaka wars on his brother's Kotte, the 1521 Vijayabahu killing splits the kingdom three ways, and the converted Dharmapala signs his kingdom away to Portugal.  c) Coal, colonies, capital and enclosed fields converge — small farms are fenced into great estates, the dispossessed stream to the towns, and the factory is born.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A storm blows Lourenço de Almeida into Galle in 1505, the strangers in strange dress are led to Kotte by a road made long on purpose — "like going to the Portuguese fort" becomes a proverb.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
