# Video script — The United Nations Is Founded, 1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Out of a war that killed lakhs and ruined cities, Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin resolve there must never be another; fifty nations meet at San Francisco in April 1945, sign the Charter that June, and membership climbs past 185 by 1998. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Where the League failed, the nations try again in 1945 — the book closes the year with the organisation Sri Lanka would join in 1955. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
