# The United Nations Is Founded, 1945 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, the birth of the United Nations”?
a) Out of a war that killed lakhs and ruined cities, Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin resolve there must never be another; fifty nations meet at San Francisco in April 1945, sign the Charter that June, and membership climbs past 185 by 1998.  b) Chief Justice Alexander Johnston's advice wins Tamil and Sinhala officials equal standing, the 1810 Judicial Charter reforms the courts, and Governor Brownrigg (1812–1820) inherits a coast ready to swallow Kandy.  c) Universal suffrage brings ministers who answer to voters: C. W. W. Kannangara makes education free in 1945, malaria is beaten back by 1946 with new sprays, death rates fall and the population surges.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Out of a war that killed lakhs and ruined cities, Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin resolve there must never be another; fifty nations meet at San Francisco in April 1945, sign the Charter that June, and membership climbs past 185 by 1998.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
