# The United Nations Is Founded, 1945 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Out of a war that killed lakhs and ruined cities, Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin resolve there must never be another; fifty nations meet at San Francisco in April 1945, sign the Charter that June, and membership climbs past 185 by 1998.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 12, the United Nations”?
**Answer:** Where the League failed, the nations try again in 1945 — the book closes the year with the organisation Sri Lanka would join in 1955.
