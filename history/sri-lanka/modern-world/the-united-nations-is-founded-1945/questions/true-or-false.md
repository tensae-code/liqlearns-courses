# The United Nations Is Founded, 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 2, the birth of the United Nations”.
**Answer: False** — Out of a war that killed lakhs and ruined cities, Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin resolve there must never be another; fifty nations meet at San Francisco in April 1945, sign the Charter that June, and membership climbs past 185 by 1998.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where the League failed, the nations try again in 1945 — the book closes the year with the organisation Sri Lanka would join in 1955.
**Answer: True**
