# Video script — India: The National Congress, 1885

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

In December 1885 Allan Hume gathers India's movements in Bombay, W. C. Bonnerjee presides, and the Congress grows from petitions into the engine of a freedom struggle lasting to 1947. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

The guide holds India up as the mirror — Sepoy mutiny, Congress, Muslim League, Gandhi — and asks what unity across communities won there. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
