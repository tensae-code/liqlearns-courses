# India: The National Congress, 1885 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4, the Indian National Congress”?
a) In December 1885 Allan Hume gathers India's movements in Bombay, W. C. Bonnerjee presides, and the Congress grows from petitions into the engine of a freedom struggle lasting to 1947.  b) When petitions fail, Tilak's generation demands swaraj, Bengal's partition is answered by boycotting British goods and burning foreign cloth.  c) Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In December 1885 Allan Hume gathers India's movements in Bombay, W. C. Bonnerjee presides, and the Congress grows from petitions into the engine of a freedom struggle lasting to 1947.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
