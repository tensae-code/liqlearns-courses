# India: The National Congress, 1885 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** In December 1885 Allan Hume gathers India's movements in Bombay, W. C. Bonnerjee presides, and the Congress grows from petitions into the engine of a freedom struggle lasting to 1947.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.4, the Indian Nationalist Movement (competency level 4.1, 10 periods)”?
**Answer:** The guide holds India up as the mirror — Sepoy mutiny, Congress, Muslim League, Gandhi — and asks what unity across communities won there.
