# India: The National Congress, 1885 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 4, the Indian National Congress”.
**Answer: False** — In December 1885 Allan Hume gathers India's movements in Bombay, W. C. Bonnerjee presides, and the Congress grows from petitions into the engine of a freedom struggle lasting to 1947.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The guide holds India up as the mirror — Sepoy mutiny, Congress, Muslim League, Gandhi — and asks what unity across communities won there.
**Answer: True**
