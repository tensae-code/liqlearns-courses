# The First World War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 10, the First World War”?
a) Alliances and rivalry drag Europe into a new kind of war — aircraft, submarines and poison gas — that kills beyond all precedent.  b) President Woodrow Wilson's plan founds the League of Nations in 1919 with 42 members and three noble aims — but America stays out, no army exists, and the League fails.  c) Versailles humiliates Germany, the 1929 crash ruins millions, Mussolini takes Italy in 1922 and Hitler rides the despair — the peace built in 1919 collapses in twenty years.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Alliances and rivalry drag Europe into a new kind of war — aircraft, submarines and poison gas — that kills beyond all precedent.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
