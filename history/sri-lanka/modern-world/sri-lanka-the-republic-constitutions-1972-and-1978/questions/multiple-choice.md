Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 6, the republic constitutions”?
a) The 1970 coalition wins two-thirds and in 1972 Ceylon becomes the Republic of Sri Lanka under President Gopallawa, then J. R. Jayewardene's 1978 constitution installs an executive presidency and proportional representation.  b) Free education and health spread, D. S.
