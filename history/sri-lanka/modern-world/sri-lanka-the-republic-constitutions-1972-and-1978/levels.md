# Sri Lanka: The Republic Constitutions, 1972 and 1978 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The 1970 coalition wins two-thirds and in 1972 Ceylon becomes the Republic of Sri Lanka under President Gopallawa, then J. R. Jayewardene's 1978 constitution installs an executive presidency and proportional representation — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
