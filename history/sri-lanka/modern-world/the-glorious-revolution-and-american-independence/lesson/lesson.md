# The Glorious Revolution and American Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Glorious Revolution and American Independence** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Magna Carta in 1215 first binds a king, Charles I's tyranny ends in the Glorious Revolution of 1688, and in 1776 America declares that governments rule by consent. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
