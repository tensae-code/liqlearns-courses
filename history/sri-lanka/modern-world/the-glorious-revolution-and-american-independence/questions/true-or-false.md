# The Glorious Revolution and American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 7, stages of human history: England and America”.
**Answer: False** — Magna Carta in 1215 first binds a king, Charles I's tyranny ends in the Glorious Revolution of 1688, and in 1776 America declares that governments rule by consent.

**2.** Magna Carta in 1215 first binds a king, Charles I's tyranny ends in the Glorious Revolution of 1688, and in 1776 America declares that governments rule by consent.
**Answer: True**
