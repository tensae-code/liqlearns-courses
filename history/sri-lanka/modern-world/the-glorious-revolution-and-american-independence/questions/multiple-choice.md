Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 7, stages of human history: England and America”?
a) Magna Carta in 1215 first binds a king, Charles I's tyranny ends in the Glorious Revolution of 1688, and in 1776 America declares that governments rule by consent.  b) The fourth great turn in the book's list — 1917 sweeps away the Tsar and carries a new idea of the state across the world.  c) The book names the system itself — how trade posts became empires and whole continents were divided among a few European powers.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Magna Carta in 1215 first binds a king, Charles I's tyranny ends in the Glorious Revolution of 1688, and in 1776 America declares that governments rule by consent.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
