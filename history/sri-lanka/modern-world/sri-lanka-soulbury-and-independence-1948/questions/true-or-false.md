# Sri Lanka: Soulbury and Independence, 1948 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 5, the Soulbury constitution and independence”.
**Answer: False** — Wartime cooperation earns trust, Lord Soulbury's 1947 constitution creates a cabinet and two chambers, the Governor-General replaces the Governor, and in 1948 Ceylon is independent.

**2.** Wartime cooperation earns trust, Lord Soulbury's 1947 constitution creates a cabinet and two chambers, the Governor-General replaces the Governor, and in 1948 Ceylon is independent.
**Answer: True**
