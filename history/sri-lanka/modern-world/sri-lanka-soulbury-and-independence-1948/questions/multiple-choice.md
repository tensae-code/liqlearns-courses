# Sri Lanka: Soulbury and Independence, 1948 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 5, the Soulbury constitution and independence”?
a) Wartime cooperation earns trust, Lord Soulbury's 1947 constitution creates a cabinet and two chambers, the Governor-General replaces the Governor, and in 1948 Ceylon is independent.  b) D. S. Senanayake leads the UNP into independence, Dudley follows, the 1953 hartal topples him, and in 1956 Bandaranaike's MEP, carried by the five great forces, wins and makes Sinhala the official language.  c) After her husband's assassination Sirimavo becomes the world's first woman prime minister in July 1960, nationalises and founds the People's Bank, loses in 1965 to Dudley Senanayake's third premiership.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Wartime cooperation earns trust, Lord Soulbury's 1947 constitution creates a cabinet and two chambers, the Governor-General replaces the Governor, and in 1948 Ceylon is independent.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
