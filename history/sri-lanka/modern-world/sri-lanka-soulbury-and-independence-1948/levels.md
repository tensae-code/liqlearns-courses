# Sri Lanka: Soulbury and Independence, 1948 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Wartime cooperation earns trust, Lord Soulbury's 1947 constitution creates a cabinet and two chambers, the Governor-General replaces the Governor, and in 1948 Ceylon is independent — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
