# Sri Lanka: Soulbury and Independence, 1948

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Soulbury and Independence, 1948** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Wartime cooperation earns trust, Lord Soulbury's 1947 constitution creates a cabinet and two chambers, the Governor-General replaces the Governor, and in 1948 Ceylon is independent. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
