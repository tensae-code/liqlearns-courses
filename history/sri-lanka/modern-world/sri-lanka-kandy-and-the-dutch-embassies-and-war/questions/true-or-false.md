# Sri Lanka: Kandy and the Dutch Embassies and War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 1, embassies, peace and the 1766 treaty”.
**Answer: False** — Envoys swap gifts in 1602, 1656, 1688 and 1721, Vimaladharmasuriya II keeps the peace and wins back monks from Burma, but Kirti Sri's Kandy is invaded in 1765 and the 1766 treaty strips the kingdom of its coast.

**2.** Envoys swap gifts in 1602, 1656, 1688 and 1721, Vimaladharmasuriya II keeps the peace and wins back monks from Burma, but Kirti Sri's Kandy is invaded in 1765 and the 1766 treaty strips the kingdom of its coast.
**Answer: True**
