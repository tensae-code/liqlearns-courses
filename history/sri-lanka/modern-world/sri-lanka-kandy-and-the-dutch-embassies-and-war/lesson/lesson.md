# Sri Lanka: Kandy and the Dutch Embassies and War

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Kandy and the Dutch Embassies and War** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Envoys swap gifts in 1602, 1656, 1688 and 1721, Vimaladharmasuriya II keeps the peace and wins back monks from Burma, but Kirti Sri's Kandy is invaded in 1765 and the 1766 treaty strips the kingdom of its coast. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
