# Video script — Sri Lanka: Kandy and the Dutch Embassies and War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Envoys swap gifts in 1602, 1656, 1688 and 1721, Vimaladharmasuriya II keeps the peace and wins back monks from Burma, but Kirti Sri's Kandy is invaded in 1765 and the 1766 treaty strips the kingdom of its coast. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
