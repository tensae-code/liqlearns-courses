# Sri Lanka: The Fall of Kandy, 1815 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, the invasions of Kandy and the 1815 cession”?
a) The 1803 invasion starves in the hills, the interpreter John D'Oyly builds a web of Kandyan contacts, and in 1815 the chiefs themselves depose Sri Vikrama Rajasinghe and sign the kingdom away.  b) Keppetipola leads Uva in revolt, the British burn villages to crush it, and the 1818 Kandyan proclamation tightens their grip and strips the chiefs.  c) Governor Torrington's new taxes on dogs, guns and shops spark a rising, Gongalegoda Banda is crowned at Dambulla, Puran Appu leads the attack on Matale, and martial law shoots the leaders.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The 1803 invasion starves in the hills, the interpreter John D'Oyly builds a web of Kandyan contacts, and in 1815 the chiefs themselves depose Sri Vikrama Rajasinghe and sign the kingdom away.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
