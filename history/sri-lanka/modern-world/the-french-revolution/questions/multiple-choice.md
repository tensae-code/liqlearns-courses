# The French Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 11, new political thought and the French Revolution”?
a) Louis XIV boasts "I am the state", the Estates-General sleeps from 1614 to 1789, the Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, the Bastille falls, rights are declared — and by 1793 the king and Marie Antoinette are guillotined.  b) The book pauses to define it — a people bound by land, language, race, religion and custom, claiming the right to rule themselves — the idea the revolution loosed on the world.  c) Villages once made everything themselves and bartered the surplus, then the Industrial Revolution multiplies goods a hundredfold and no nation can live alone — colonies, markets and world trade are born.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Louis XIV boasts "I am the state", the Estates-General sleeps from 1614 to 1789, the Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, the Bastille falls, rights are declared — and by 1793 the king and Marie Antoinette are guillotined.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) 1789 stands in the book's short list of the turns of human history, between America's independence and Russia's revolution.  b) Villages once made everything themselves and bartered the surplus, then the Industrial Revolution multiplies goods a hundredfold and no nation can live alone — colonies, markets and world trade are born.  c) Six nations sign the European Common Market into being in 1957, UNCTAD speaks for the poor countries from 1964, and in January 1995 the WTO takes over world trade's rulebook.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) 1789 stands in the book's short list of the turns of human history, between America's independence and Russia's revolution.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
