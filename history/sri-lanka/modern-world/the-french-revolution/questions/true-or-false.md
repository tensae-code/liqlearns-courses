# The French Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 11, new political thought and the French Revolution”.
**Answer: False** — Louis XIV boasts "I am the state", the Estates-General sleeps from 1614 to 1789, the Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, the Bastille falls, rights are declared — and by 1793 the king and Marie Antoinette are guillotined.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) 1789 stands in the book's short list of the turns of human history, between America's independence and Russia's revolution.
**Answer: True**
