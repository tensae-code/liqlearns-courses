# The French Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Louis XIV boasts "I am the state", the Estates-General sleeps from 1614 to 1789, the Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, the Bastille falls, rights are declared — and by 1793 the king and Marie Antoinette are guillotined.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 7, the French Revolution as a stage of mankind”?
**Answer:** 1789 stands in the book's short list of the turns of human history, between America's independence and Russia's revolution.
