# European Expansion and the Rival Empires — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 8, the expansion of European dominance”?
a) Spain and Portugal open the race, da Gama reaches India in 1498, the Dutch found the VOC in 1602 and out-trade them all, England and France follow, and Christianity sails with every flag.  b) A storm blows Lourenço de Almeida into Galle in 1505, the strangers in strange dress are led to Kotte by a road made long on purpose — "like going to the Portuguese fort" becomes a proverb.  c) Mayadunne of Sitawaka wars on his brother's Kotte, the 1521 Vijayabahu killing splits the kingdom three ways, and the converted Dharmapala signs his kingdom away to Portugal.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Spain and Portugal open the race, da Gama reaches India in 1498, the Dutch found the VOC in 1602 and out-trade them all, England and France follow, and Christianity sails with every flag.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
