# Video script — Sri Lanka: Independence Governments, 1948 to 1956

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

D. S. Senanayake leads the UNP into independence, Dudley follows, the 1953 hartal topples him, and in 1956 Bandaranaike's MEP, carried by the five great forces, wins and makes Sinhala the official language. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Pupils chart every election since 1970 themselves, then compare the 1972 and 1978 constitutions column by column. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
