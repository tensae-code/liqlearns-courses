# Sri Lanka: Independence Governments, 1948 to 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 6, the first governments and the 1956 turning point”.
**Answer: False** — D. S. Senanayake leads the UNP into independence, Dudley follows, the 1953 hartal topples him, and in 1956 Bandaranaike's MEP, carried by the five great forces, wins and makes Sinhala the official language.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pupils chart every election since 1970 themselves, then compare the 1972 and 1978 constitutions column by column.
**Answer: True**
