# Sri Lanka: Independence Governments, 1948 to 1956 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** D. S. Senanayake leads the UNP into independence, Dudley follows, the 1953 hartal topples him, and in 1956 Bandaranaike's MEP, carried by the five great forces, wins and makes Sinhala the official language.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.6, Sri Lanka since independence (competency level 6.1, 10 periods)”?
**Answer:** Pupils chart every election since 1970 themselves, then compare the 1972 and 1978 constitutions column by column.
