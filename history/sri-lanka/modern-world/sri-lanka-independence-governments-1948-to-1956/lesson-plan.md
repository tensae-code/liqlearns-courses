# Lesson plan — Sri Lanka: Independence Governments, 1948 to 1956

**Course:** History — Sri Lanka · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Sri Lanka: Independence Governments, 1948 to 1956
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Foreign Rule, Revival and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** D. S. Senanayake leads the UNP into independence, Dudley follows, the 1953 hartal topples him, and in 1956 Bandaranaike's MEP, carried by the five great forces, wins and makes Sinhala the official language.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Foreign Rule, Revival and the Road to Independence (recommended, never forced).
