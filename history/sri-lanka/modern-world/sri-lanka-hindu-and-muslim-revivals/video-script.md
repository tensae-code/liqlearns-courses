# Video script — Sri Lanka: Hindu and Muslim Revivals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Arumuga Navalar answers the missionaries with Tamil prose and Hindu schools in Jaffna, while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe, aided by the exiled Arabi Pasha, builds Muslim education. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Arumuga Navalar (1822–1879) answers mission schools with Hindu schools — Jaffna's Hindu College rises in 1871 — while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe (1832–1898) founds Muslim journals and Colombo's Zahira College. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns.
