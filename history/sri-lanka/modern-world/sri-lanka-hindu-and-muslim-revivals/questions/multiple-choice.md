# Sri Lanka: Hindu and Muslim Revivals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3, the Hindu and Islamic revivals”?
a) Arumuga Navalar answers the missionaries with Tamil prose and Hindu schools in Jaffna, while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe, aided by the exiled Arabi Pasha, builds Muslim education.  b) Da Gama's 1498 landing opens the door, the Mughals fade, Britain rules by mid-1800s, and in 1857 the sepoys rise — the mutiny that makes India a Crown colony.  c) In December 1885 Allan Hume gathers India's movements in Bombay, W. C. Bonnerjee presides, and the Congress grows from petitions into the engine of a freedom struggle lasting to 1947.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Arumuga Navalar answers the missionaries with Tamil prose and Hindu schools in Jaffna, while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe, aided by the exiled Arabi Pasha, builds Muslim education.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
