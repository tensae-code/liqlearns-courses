# Sri Lanka: Hindu and Muslim Revivals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Arumuga Navalar answers the missionaries with Tamil prose and Hindu schools in Jaffna, while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe, aided by the exiled Arabi Pasha, builds Muslim education.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ch. 6, Navalar and Siddhi Lebbe”?
**Answer:** Arumuga Navalar (1822–1879) answers mission schools with Hindu schools — Jaffna's Hindu College rises in 1871 — while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe (1832–1898) founds Muslim journals and Colombo's Zahira College.
