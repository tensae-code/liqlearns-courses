# Sri Lanka: Hindu and Muslim Revivals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 3, the Hindu and Islamic revivals”.
**Answer: False** — Arumuga Navalar answers the missionaries with Tamil prose and Hindu schools in Jaffna, while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe, aided by the exiled Arabi Pasha, builds Muslim education.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Arumuga Navalar (1822–1879) answers mission schools with Hindu schools — Jaffna's Hindu College rises in 1871 — while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe (1832–1898) founds Muslim journals and Colombo's Zahira College.
**Answer: True**
