# Video script — Sri Lanka: British Taxes and the Pilima Talauwa Conspiracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

New taxes on everything from toddy to salt bite the coast, the Company–Crown double government fails by 1802, and chief Pilima Talauwa's plot with Governor North opens the road to Kandy. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
