# Sri Lanka: British Taxes and the Pilima Talauwa Conspiracy

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: British Taxes and the Pilima Talauwa Conspiracy** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

New taxes on everything from toddy to salt bite the coast, the Company–Crown double government fails by 1802, and chief Pilima Talauwa's plot with Governor North opens the road to Kandy. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
