# Sri Lanka: British Taxes and the Pilima Talauwa Conspiracy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — New taxes on everything from toddy to salt bite the coast, the Company–Crown double government fails by 1802, and chief Pilima Talauwa's plot with Governor North opens the road to Kandy — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
