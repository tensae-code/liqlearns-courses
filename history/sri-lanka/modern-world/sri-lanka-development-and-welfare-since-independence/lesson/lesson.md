## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Sri Lanka: Development and Welfare Since Independence** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Free education and health spread, D. S. Senanayake's Gal Oya scheme of 1949 revives the dry zone, and the Mahaweli programme with the Victoria dam carries the old tank civilisation into the age of hydro-power. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
