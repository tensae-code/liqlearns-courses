# Sri Lanka: The 1848 Matale Rebellion — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Governor Torrington's new taxes on dogs, guns and shops spark a rising, Gongalegoda Banda is crowned at Dambulla, Puran Appu leads the attack on Matale, and martial law shoots the leaders — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
