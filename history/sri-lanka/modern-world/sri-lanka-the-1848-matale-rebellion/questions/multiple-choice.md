# Sri Lanka: The 1848 Matale Rebellion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, the 1848 rebellion”?
a) Governor Torrington's new taxes on dogs, guns and shops spark a rising, Gongalegoda Banda is crowned at Dambulla, Puran Appu leads the attack on Matale, and martial law shoots the leaders.  b) Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.  c) Peasant farming declines as land and labour flow to the estates, until restored tanks, an irrigation department and cooperatives try to lift it again.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Governor Torrington's new taxes on dogs, guns and shops spark a rising, Gongalegoda Banda is crowned at Dambulla, Puran Appu leads the attack on Matale, and martial law shoots the leaders.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Governor Torrington's new taxes on dogs, guns and shops spark a rising, Gongalegoda Banda is crowned at Dambulla, Puran Appu leads the attack on Matale, and martial law shoots the leaders.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
