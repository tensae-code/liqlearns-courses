# How World Trade Grew — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 1, from the ancient self-sufficient economy to world trade”.
**Answer: False** — Villages once made everything themselves and bartered the surplus, then the Industrial Revolution multiplies goods a hundredfold and no nation can live alone — colonies, markets and world trade are born.

**2.** Villages once made everything themselves and bartered the surplus, then the Industrial Revolution multiplies goods a hundredfold and no nation can live alone — colonies, markets and world trade are born.
**Answer: True**
