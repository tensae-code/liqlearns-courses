# How World Trade Grew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1, from the ancient self-sufficient economy to world trade”?
a) Villages once made everything themselves and bartered the surplus, then the Industrial Revolution multiplies goods a hundredfold and no nation can live alone — colonies, markets and world trade are born.  b) Six nations sign the European Common Market into being in 1957, UNCTAD speaks for the poor countries from 1964, and in January 1995 the WTO takes over world trade's rulebook.  c) Out of a war that killed lakhs and ruined cities, Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin resolve there must never be another; fifty nations meet at San Francisco in April 1945, sign the Charter that June, and membership climbs past 185 by 1998.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Villages once made everything themselves and bartered the surplus, then the Industrial Revolution multiplies goods a hundredfold and no nation can live alone — colonies, markets and world trade are born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
