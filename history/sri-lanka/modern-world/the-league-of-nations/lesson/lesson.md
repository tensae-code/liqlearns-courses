# The League of Nations

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The League of Nations** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

President Woodrow Wilson's plan founds the League of Nations in 1919 with 42 members and three noble aims — but America stays out, no army exists, and the League fails. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
