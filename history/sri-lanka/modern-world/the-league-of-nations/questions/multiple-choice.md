# The League of Nations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 10, Wilson's League”?
a) President Woodrow Wilson's plan founds the League of Nations in 1919 with 42 members and three noble aims — but America stays out, no army exists, and the League fails.  b) Versailles humiliates Germany, the 1929 crash ruins millions, Mussolini takes Italy in 1922 and Hitler rides the despair — the peace built in 1919 collapses in twenty years.  c) The Dipavamsa comes first, then the monk Mahanama shapes the Mahavamsa in the 5th–6th century — 37 chapters ending with Mahasena — and later hands continue it as the Culavamsa, while Wilhelm Geiger's edition lets the world read them critically.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) President Woodrow Wilson's plan founds the League of Nations in 1919 with 42 members and three noble aims — but America stays out, no army exists, and the League fails.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
