# The League of Nations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 10, Wilson's League”.
**Answer: False** — President Woodrow Wilson's plan founds the League of Nations in 1919 with 42 members and three noble aims — but America stays out, no army exists, and the League fails.

**2.** President Woodrow Wilson's plan founds the League of Nations in 1919 with 42 members and three noble aims — but America stays out, no army exists, and the League fails.
**Answer: True**
