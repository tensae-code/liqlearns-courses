# Video script — Sri Lanka: The Dutch Take the Coast

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The VOC is founded in 1602, Spilbergen lands at Batticaloa, Sebald de Weert is killed after insulting Vimaladharmasuriya I, yet Rajasinghe II signs the 1638 treaty and the Dutch take the Portuguese forts but refuse to hand them back. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Teachers stage the story of a king who invites the Dutch in to expel one enemy and gains another, from the VOC's charter to the broken promise over the forts. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
