# Sri Lanka: The Dutch Take the Coast — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 1, the Dutch come to Asia and Kandy seeks their help”.
**Answer: False** — The VOC is founded in 1602, Spilbergen lands at Batticaloa, Sebald de Weert is killed after insulting Vimaladharmasuriya I, yet Rajasinghe II signs the 1638 treaty and the Dutch take the Portuguese forts but refuse to hand them back.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Teachers stage the story of a king who invites the Dutch in to expel one enemy and gains another, from the VOC's charter to the broken promise over the forts.
**Answer: True**
