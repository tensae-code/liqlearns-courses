# Sri Lanka: The Dutch Take the Coast — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The VOC is founded in 1602, Spilbergen lands at Batticaloa, Sebald de Weert is killed after insulting Vimaladharmasuriya I, yet Rajasinghe II signs the 1638 treaty and the Dutch take the Portuguese forts but refuse to hand them back.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.1, establishment of Dutch power and Kandyan relations (competency level 1.1, 10 periods)”?
**Answer:** Teachers stage the story of a king who invites the Dutch in to expel one enemy and gains another, from the VOC's charter to the broken promise over the forts.
