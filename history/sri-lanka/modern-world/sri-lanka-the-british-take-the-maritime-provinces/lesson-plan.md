## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 2, the British take the coast, 1796–1802** — Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North.
2. **Unit 9.2, how British authority was established (competency level 2.1)** — The guide frames 1796, the cession of Kandy in 1815 and the struggles of 1818 and 1848 as one story of a sovereignty lost in stages.
3. **Ch. 4, how the British came** — Fearing France, Britain takes the Dutch coast in 1796 on the strength of a prince's letter the islanders nickname "pittu", and by 1815 rules the whole island for 133 years.
4. **Ch. 1, British rule established: North and Maitland** — Governor North's harsh taxes provoke revolt, Thomas Maitland (1805–1812) tours every district, calms the coast, restores the cinnamon monopoly North abolished, and fences British power in law.
