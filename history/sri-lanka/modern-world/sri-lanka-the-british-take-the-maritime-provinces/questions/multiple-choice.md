# Sri Lanka: The British Take the Maritime Provinces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2, the British take the coast, 1796–1802”?
a) Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North.  b) The 1803 invasion starves in the hills, the interpreter John D'Oyly builds a web of Kandyan contacts, and in 1815 the chiefs themselves depose Sri Vikrama Rajasinghe and sign the kingdom away.  c) Keppetipola leads Uva in revolt, the British burn villages to crush it, and the 1818 Kandyan proclamation tightens their grip and strips the chiefs.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
