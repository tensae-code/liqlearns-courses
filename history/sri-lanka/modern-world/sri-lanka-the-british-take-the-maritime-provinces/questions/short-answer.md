# Sri Lanka: The British Take the Maritime Provinces — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Unit 9.2, how British authority was established (competency level 2.1)”?
**Answer:** The guide frames 1796, the cession of Kandy in 1815 and the struggles of 1818 and 1848 as one story of a sovereignty lost in stages.
