# Sri Lanka: The British Take the Maritime Provinces — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Ch. 2, the British take the coast, 1796–1802”.
**Answer: False** — Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The guide frames 1796, the cession of Kandy in 1815 and the struggles of 1818 and 1848 as one story of a sovereignty lost in stages.
**Answer: True**
