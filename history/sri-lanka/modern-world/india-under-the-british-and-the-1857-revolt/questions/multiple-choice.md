# India: Under the British and the 1857 Revolt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4, Europeans in India and the Sepoy Mutiny”?
a) Da Gama's 1498 landing opens the door, the Mughals fade, Britain rules by mid-1800s, and in 1857 the sepoys rise — the mutiny that makes India a Crown colony.  b) In December 1885 Allan Hume gathers India's movements in Bombay, W. C. Bonnerjee presides, and the Congress grows from petitions into the engine of a freedom struggle lasting to 1947.  c) When petitions fail, Tilak's generation demands swaraj, Bengal's partition is answered by boycotting British goods and burning foreign cloth.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Da Gama's 1498 landing opens the door, the Mughals fade, Britain rules by mid-1800s, and in 1857 the sepoys rise — the mutiny that makes India a Crown colony.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
