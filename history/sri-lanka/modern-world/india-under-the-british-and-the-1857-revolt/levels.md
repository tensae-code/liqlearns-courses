# India: Under the British and the 1857 Revolt — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Da Gama's 1498 landing opens the door, the Mughals fade, Britain rules by mid-1800s, and in 1857 the sepoys rise — the mutiny that makes India a Crown colony — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
