# Nationalism What it is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 11, the idea of nationalism”?
a) The book pauses to define it — a people bound by land, language, race, religion and custom, claiming the right to rule themselves — the idea the revolution loosed on the world.  b) Villages once made everything themselves and bartered the surplus, then the Industrial Revolution multiplies goods a hundredfold and no nation can live alone — colonies, markets and world trade are born.  c) Six nations sign the European Common Market into being in 1957, UNCTAD speaks for the poor countries from 1964, and in January 1995 the WTO takes over world trade's rulebook.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The book pauses to define it — a people bound by land, language, race, religion and custom, claiming the right to rule themselves — the idea the revolution loosed on the world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
