# Video script — Sri Lanka: Dutch Religion Schools and Cultural Legacy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The Reformed Church and its schools spread for 138 years, the first printing press arrives in 1739, and Dutch words for cupboards and lawyers, new foods and the Burgher community remain in the island's life. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Sri Lanka.
