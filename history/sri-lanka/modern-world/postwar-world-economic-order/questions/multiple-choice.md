# Postwar World Economic Order — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1, the institutions built after 1945”?
a) Six nations sign the European Common Market into being in 1957, UNCTAD speaks for the poor countries from 1964, and in January 1995 the WTO takes over world trade's rulebook.  b) Out of a war that killed lakhs and ruined cities, Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin resolve there must never be another; fifty nations meet at San Francisco in April 1945, sign the Charter that June, and membership climbs past 185 by 1998.  c) Chief Justice Alexander Johnston's advice wins Tamil and Sinhala officials equal standing, the 1810 Judicial Charter reforms the courts, and Governor Brownrigg (1812–1820) inherits a coast ready to swallow Kandy.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Six nations sign the European Common Market into being in 1957, UNCTAD speaks for the poor countries from 1964, and in January 1995 the WTO takes over world trade's rulebook.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
