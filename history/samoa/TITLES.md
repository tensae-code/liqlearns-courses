# History — Samoa course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Samoa's own —
built from Samoa's Social Studies books for Years 9–11 and its
History books for Years 12–13 (all student books, English).
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below sit inside the levels, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grade the
book comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': heritage and place first,
Samoa's political story and the world's rights story next,
the Mau and the civil-rights century, then the senior
six-part frame — Samoa as the home case, then the world
whole. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).
