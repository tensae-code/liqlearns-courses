# The Suez Crisis 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nasser, in power from 1952, swaps Egyptian cotton for Czechoslovak arms, is refused Aswan Dam money, and nationalises the Suez Canal; Israel, Britain and France attack on 29 October 1956 but withdraw under Soviet threats and American pressure, leaving Nasser an Arab hero and Britain and France diminished.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
