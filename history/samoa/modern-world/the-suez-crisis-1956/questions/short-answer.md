# The Suez Crisis 1956 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 4" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Nasser, in power from 1952, swaps Egyptian cotton for Czechoslovak arms, is refused Aswan Dam money, and nationalises the Suez Canal; Israel, Britain and France attack on 29 October 1956 but withdraw under Soviet threats and American pressure, leaving Nasser an Arab hero and Britain and France diminished.
