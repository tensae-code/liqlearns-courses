# The Suez Crisis 1956 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 3, Unit 4" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Nasser, in power from 1952, swaps Egyptian cotton for Czechoslovak arms, is refused Aswan Dam money, and nationalises the Suez Canal; Israel, Britain and France attack on 29 October 1956 but withdraw under Soviet threats and American pressure, leaving Nasser an Arab hero and Britain and France diminished
