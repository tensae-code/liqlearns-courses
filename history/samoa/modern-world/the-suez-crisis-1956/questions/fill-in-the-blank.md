# The Suez Crisis 1956 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Nasser, in power from 1952, swaps ____ cotton for Czechoslovak arms, is refused Aswan Dam money, and nationalises the Suez Canal; Israel, Britain and France attack on 29 October 1956 but withdraw under Soviet threats and American pressure, leaving Nasser an Arab hero and Britain and France diminished.
**Answer:** Egyptian
