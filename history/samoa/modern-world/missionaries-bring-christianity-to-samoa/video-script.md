# Video script — Missionaries Bring Christianity to Samoa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. John Williams brings the LMS in 1830 and by 1834 Sāmoan is a written language; Peter Turner returns in 1835 at the request of Sāmoans and Tonga's King George Tupou I, planting the Wesleyan Lotu Toga; French Catholic priests follow in 1845 (Lotu Pope), and shipwrecked Mormon elders land in 1899; villages still mark the landing places.
