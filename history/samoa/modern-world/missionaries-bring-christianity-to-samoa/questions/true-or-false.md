# Missionaries Bring Christianity to Samoa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** John Williams brings the LMS in 1830 and by 1834 Sāmoan is a written language; Peter Turner returns in 1835 at the request of Sāmoans and Tonga's King George Tupou I, planting the Wesleyan Lotu Toga; French Catholic priests follow in 1845 (Lotu Pope), and shipwrecked Mormon elders land in 1899; villages still mark the landing places.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 3 and Part 2, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
