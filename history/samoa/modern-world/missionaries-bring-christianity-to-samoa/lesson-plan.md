# Lesson plan — Missionaries Bring Christianity to Samoa

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Missionaries Bring Christianity to Samoa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond (CURRICULUM.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: John Williams brings the LMS in 1830 and by 1834 Sāmoan is a written language; Peter Turner returns in 1835 at the request of Sāmoans and Tonga's King George Tupou I, planting the Wesleyan Lotu Toga; French Catholic priests follow in 1845 (Lotu Pope), and shipwrecked Mormon elders land in 1899; villages still mark the landing places. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
