# The Salt March 1930 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** On 12 ____ 1930 Gandhi sets out from his Ahmedabad ashram with 78 trained followers, walks 385 kilometres to Dandi, and on 6 April bends and picks up a lump of sea salt — breaking the salt law before the world; at Dharasana salt works unarmed rows fall under police clubs and are replaced row by row, and as many as 100,000 satyagrahis fill the jails.
**Answer:** March
