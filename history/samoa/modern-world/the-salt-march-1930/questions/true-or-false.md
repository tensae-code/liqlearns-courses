# The Salt March 1930 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On 12 March 1930 Gandhi sets out from his Ahmedabad ashram with 78 trained followers, walks 385 kilometres to Dandi, and on 6 April bends and picks up a lump of sea salt — breaking the salt law before the world; at Dharasana salt works unarmed rows fall under police clubs and are replaced row by row, and as many as 100,000 satyagrahis fill the jails.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Units 3–4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
