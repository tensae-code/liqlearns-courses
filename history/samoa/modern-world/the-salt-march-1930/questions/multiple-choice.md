# The Salt March 1930 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 3, Units 3–4" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) On 12 March 1930 Gandhi sets out from his Ahmedabad ashram with 78 trained followers, walks 385 kilometres to Dandi, and on 6 April bends and picks up a lump of sea salt — breaking the salt law before the world; at Dharasana salt works unarmed rows fall under police clubs and are replaced row by row, and as many as 100,000 satyagrahis fill the jails
**Answer: d) On 12 March 1930 Gandhi sets out from his Ahmedabad ashram with 78 trained followers, walks 385 kilometres to Dandi, and on 6 April bends and picks up a lump of sea salt — breaking the salt law before the world; at Dharasana salt works unarmed rows fall under police clubs and are replaced row by row, and as many as 100,000 satyagrahis fill the jails** — That is what this lesson teaches.
