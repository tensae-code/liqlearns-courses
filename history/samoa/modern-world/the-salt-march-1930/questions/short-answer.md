# The Salt March 1930 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Units 3–4" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** On 12 March 1930 Gandhi sets out from his Ahmedabad ashram with 78 trained followers, walks 385 kilometres to Dandi, and on 6 April bends and picks up a lump of sea salt — breaking the salt law before the world; at Dharasana salt works unarmed rows fall under police clubs and are replaced row by row, and as many as 100,000 satyagrahis fill the jails.
