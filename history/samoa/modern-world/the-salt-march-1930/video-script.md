# Video script — The Salt March 1930

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. On 12 March 1930 Gandhi sets out from his Ahmedabad ashram with 78 trained followers, walks 385 kilometres to Dandi, and on 6 April bends and picks up a lump of sea salt — breaking the salt law before the world; at Dharasana salt works unarmed rows fall under police clubs and are replaced row by row, and as many as 100,000 satyagrahis fill the jails.

Now the part called "Part 3, Units 3–4". on 12 March 1930 Gandhi sets out from his Ahmedabad ashram with 78 trained followers, walks 385 kilometres to Dandi, and on 6 April bends and picks up a lump of sea salt — breaking the salt law before the world; at Dharasana salt works unarmed rows fall under police clubs and are replaced row by row, and as many as 100,000 satyagrahis fill the jails.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
