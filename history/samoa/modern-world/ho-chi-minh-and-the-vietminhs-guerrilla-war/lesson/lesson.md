# Ho Chi Minh and the Vietminh's Guerrilla War

## Do it first

Take a pencil and a page. Write the lesson's title at the top: "Ho Chi Minh and the Vietminh's Guerrilla War". Under it, leave one empty box for each part of the story, in order: Part 2, Unit 4. As you read each part, fill its box with three words: who, where, and what turned. By the end of the lesson your page is the whole story in your own hand — and this lesson is about exactly what fills those boxes.

## The story

Ho Chi Minh, schooled by travel through America, Africa, India, Russia and China, builds the Vietminh in exile, declares independence in 1945, and when France re-invades (1947) Giap abandons set battles for ambush, mines and hit-and-run — women smuggle gun parts, children carry food — until France baits a trap at Dien Bien Phu in March 1954 with 16,500 men.

Say this part back in one breath before you move on: who, where, what turned. If you can say it, you own it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: hold any story in four questions — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. A story you can question is a story you can retell.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: ho Chi Minh, schooled by travel through America, Africa, India, Russia and China, builds the Vietminh in exile, declares independence in 1945, and when France re-invades (1947) Giap abandons set battles for ambush, mines and hit-and-run — women smuggle gun parts, children carry food — until France baits a trap at Dien Bien Phu in March 1954 with 16,500 men. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: fill the boxes again from memory, then check them against the lesson.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: draw the part that struck you most, and label it with who, where and what turned.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where this story's pattern shows itself. Note it.
