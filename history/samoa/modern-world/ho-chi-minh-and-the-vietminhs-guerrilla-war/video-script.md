# Video script — Ho Chi Minh and the Vietminh's Guerrilla War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Ho Chi Minh, schooled by travel through America, Africa, India, Russia and China, builds the Vietminh in exile, declares independence in 1945, and when France re-invades (1947) Giap abandons set battles for ambush, mines and hit-and-run — women smuggle gun parts, children carry food — until France baits a trap at Dien Bien Phu in March 1954 with 16,500 men.

Now the part called "Part 2, Unit 4". Ho Chi Minh, schooled by travel through America, Africa, India, Russia and China, builds the Vietminh in exile, declares independence in 1945, and when France re-invades (1947) Giap abandons set battles for ambush, mines and hit-and-run — women smuggle gun parts, children carry food — until France baits a trap at Dien Bien Phu in March 1954 with 16,500 men.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
