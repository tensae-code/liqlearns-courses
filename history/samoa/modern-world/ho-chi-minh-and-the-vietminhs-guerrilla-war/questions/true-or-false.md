# Ho Chi Minh and the Vietminh's Guerrilla War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ho Chi Minh, schooled by travel through America, Africa, India, Russia and China, builds the Vietminh in exile, declares independence in 1945, and when France re-invades (1947) Giap abandons set battles for ambush, mines and hit-and-run — women smuggle gun parts, children carry food — until France baits a trap at Dien Bien Phu in March 1954 with 16,500 men.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
