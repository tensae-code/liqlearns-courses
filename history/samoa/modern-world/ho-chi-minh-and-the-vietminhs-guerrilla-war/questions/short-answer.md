# Ho Chi Minh and the Vietminh's Guerrilla War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 4" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Ho Chi Minh, schooled by travel through America, Africa, India, Russia and China, builds the Vietminh in exile, declares independence in 1945, and when France re-invades (1947) Giap abandons set battles for ambush, mines and hit-and-run — women smuggle gun parts, children carry food — until France baits a trap at Dien Bien Phu in March 1954 with 16,500 men.
