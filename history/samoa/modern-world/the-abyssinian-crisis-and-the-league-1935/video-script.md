# Video script — The Abyssinian Crisis and the League 1935

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Mussolini invades Abyssinia to distract Italians from the depression; Haile Selassie appeals to the League, which condemns Italy and sanctions everything except the oil that would have forced withdrawal; Britain and France secretly offer him most of the country (the leaked Hoare-Laval Pact), and Italy walks out of the League.
