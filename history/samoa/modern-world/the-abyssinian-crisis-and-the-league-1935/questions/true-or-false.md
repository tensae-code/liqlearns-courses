# The Abyssinian Crisis and the League 1935 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mussolini invades Abyssinia to distract Italians from the depression; Haile Selassie appeals to the League, which condemns Italy and sanctions everything except the oil that would have forced withdrawal; Britain and France secretly offer him most of the country (the leaked Hoare-Laval Pact), and Italy walks out of the League.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
