# France Conquers Vietnam and Builds Indochina — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 3" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Vietnam, a nation that had already endured a thousand years of Chinese rule (100 BC–AD 900), resists the French and is beaten by 1885; in 1887 France welds Vietnam, Cambodia and Laos — different languages, different cultures — into French Indochina, exports its sugar and rice cheaply, and leaves its peasants poor and shut out of government.
