# France Conquers Vietnam and Builds Indochina — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Vietnam, a nation that had already endured a thousand years of Chinese rule (100 BC–AD 900), resists the French and is beaten by 1885; in 1887 France welds Vietnam, Cambodia and Laos — different languages, different cultures — into French Indochina, exports its sugar and rice cheaply, and leaves its peasants poor and shut out of government.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
