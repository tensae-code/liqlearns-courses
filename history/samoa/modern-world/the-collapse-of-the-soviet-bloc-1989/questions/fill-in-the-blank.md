# The Collapse of the Soviet Bloc 1989 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Gorbachev's glasnost and perestroika uncork decades of resentment; in 1989 nation after nation — ____, East Germany, Czechoslovakia, Romania, Bulgaria — walks out of the COMECON bloc, East Germany itself vanishes into reunification in October 1990, and the Baltic and Central Asian republics follow the USSR into history.
**Answer:** Poland
