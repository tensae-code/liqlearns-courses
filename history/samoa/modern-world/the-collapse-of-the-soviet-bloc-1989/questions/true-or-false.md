# The Collapse of the Soviet Bloc 1989 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gorbachev's glasnost and perestroika uncork decades of resentment; in 1989 nation after nation — Poland, East Germany, Czechoslovakia, Romania, Bulgaria — walks out of the COMECON bloc, East Germany itself vanishes into reunification in October 1990, and the Baltic and Central Asian republics follow the USSR into history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
