# America the Superpower: FDR's Four Freedoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book asks why the USA is the surviving superpower — natural resources, a skilled workforce, stable capitalist democracy, military might and the "American Dream" — and prints Roosevelt's Four Freedoms address (6 January 1941) and Arsenal of Democracy argument: send the democracies "ships, planes, tanks, and guns" now, or fight the dictators later.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 5, Units 3–4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
