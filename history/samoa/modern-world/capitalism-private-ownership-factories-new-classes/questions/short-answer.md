**1.** Tell the story of "Part 5, Unit 1 "Political Philosophies That Motivate Economic Action"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Societies organise production by belief — pure market capitalism (private resources, profit, no government interference), market socialism (state ownership for equality), traditional subsistence, and the mixed systems most countries, Sāmoa included, actually run.

**2.** What came of "Part 5, Unit 1, systems compared" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The two superpower systems are set side by side — American capitalism (private ownership, personal profit, free speech and elections) against Soviet communism (state ownership of industry and agriculture, one party, controlled media, criticism of Stalin punishable by death).
