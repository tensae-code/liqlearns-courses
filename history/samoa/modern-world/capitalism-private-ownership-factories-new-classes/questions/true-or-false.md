# Capitalism Private Ownership Factories New Classes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Societies organise production by belief — pure market capitalism (private resources, profit, no government interference), market socialism (state ownership for equality), traditional subsistence, and the mixed systems most countries, Sāmoa included, actually run. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 5, Unit 1, systems compared" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
