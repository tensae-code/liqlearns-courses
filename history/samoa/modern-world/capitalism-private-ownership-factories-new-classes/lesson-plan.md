# Lesson plan — Capitalism Private Ownership Factories New Classes

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Capitalism Private Ownership Factories New Classes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Societies organise production by belief — pure market capitalism (private resources, profit, no government interference), market socialism (state ownership for equality), traditional subsistence, and the mixed systems most countries, Sāmoa included, actually run; the two superpower systems are set side by side — American capitalism (private ownership, personal profit, free speech and elections) against Soviet communism (state ownership of industry and agriculture, one party, controlled media, criticism of Stalin punishable by death).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Societies organise production by belief — pure market capitalism (private resources, profit, no government interference), market socialism (state ownership for equality), traditional subsistence, and the mixed systems most countries, Sāmoa included, actually run; the two superpower systems are set side by side — American capitalism (private ownership, personal profit, free speech and elections) against Soviet communism (state ownership of industry and agriculture, one party, controlled media, criticism of Stalin punishable by death). Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
