# The Truman Doctrine and the Marshall Plan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 5, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Yalta and Potsdam paper over a split; the atomic race and Soviet spies deepen it; Stalin builds his 1945–48 buffer of communist governments, and Truman answers not with armies but money — the Doctrine of 12 March 1947 ("misery and want" are communism's roots) and the Marshall Plan's US$13 billion to sixteen countries (1948–52), which Moscow forbids its satellites to touch.
