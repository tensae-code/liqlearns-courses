# The Truman Doctrine and the Marshall Plan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yalta and Potsdam paper over a split; the atomic race and Soviet spies deepen it; Stalin builds his 1945–48 buffer of communist governments, and Truman answers not with armies but money — the Doctrine of 12 March 1947 ("misery and want" are communism's roots) and the Marshall Plan's US$13 billion to sixteen countries (1948–52), which Moscow forbids its satellites to touch.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 5, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
