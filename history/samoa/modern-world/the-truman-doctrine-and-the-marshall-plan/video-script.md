# Video script — The Truman Doctrine and the Marshall Plan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Yalta and Potsdam paper over a split; the atomic race and Soviet spies deepen it; Stalin builds his 1945–48 buffer of communist governments, and Truman answers not with armies but money — the Doctrine of 12 March 1947 ("misery and want" are communism's roots) and the Marshall Plan's US$13 billion to sixteen countries (1948–52), which Moscow forbids its satellites to touch.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
