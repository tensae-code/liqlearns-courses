# The Mau and Black Saturday — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2, Samoa history timeline and "The Mau Movement, 1926"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Talune brings Spanish influenza on 7 November 1918 and a fifth to a quarter of Western Samoa dies; grievances over interference with the fa'a Samoa, taxes and exclusion from government form the Mau in 1926; on Black Saturday, 28 December 1929, police fire on an unarmed Apia march, killing eleven including Tupua Tamasese, whose dying words plead for peace; Samoan delegations then press the UN until independence — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2, Topic 2 "Telesia's diary" (diary story, plot summary only)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a Vaimoso-area mother watches her husband join the secret Mau meetings of 1926, agonises over the tax, registration and school boycott, sews purple uniforms for the Women's Mau led by the wives of Tamasese, Tuimaliifano, Nelson and Faumuina, marches with a thousand women — and buries her husband after police machine-gun the unarmed Beach Road march on Black Saturday, the day Tamasese is buried at Lepea — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Part 2, Unit 4, second half
