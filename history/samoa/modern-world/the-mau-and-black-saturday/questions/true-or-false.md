# The Mau and Black Saturday — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Talune brings Spanish influenza on 7 November 1918 and a fifth to a quarter of Western Samoa dies; grievances over interference with the fa'a Samoa, taxes and exclusion from government form the Mau in 1926; on Black Saturday, 28 December 1929, police fire on an unarmed Apia march, killing eleven including Tupua Tamasese, whose dying words plead for peace; Samoan delegations then press the UN until independence. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Topic 2 "Telesia's diary" (diary story, plot summary only)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
