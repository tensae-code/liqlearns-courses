# Freedom Riders and Freedom Summer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) In 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South
**Answer: d) In 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News""?
a) In 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) In 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South** — That is what this lesson teaches.
