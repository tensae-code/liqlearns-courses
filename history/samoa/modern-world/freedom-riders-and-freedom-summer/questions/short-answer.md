# Freedom Riders and Freedom Summer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**Answer:** In 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South.
