# Freedom Riders and Freedom Summer — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
