# Video script — Freedom Riders and Freedom Summer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South.

Now the part called "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"". in 1960 CORE sends black and white student "freedom riders" to test the ban on segregated interstate buses and a mob burns their bus; in 1963 "freedom summer" campaigns register black voters blocked by literacy rules in the South.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
