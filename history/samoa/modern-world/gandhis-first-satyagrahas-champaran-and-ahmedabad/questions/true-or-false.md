# Gandhi's First Satyagrahas: Champaran and Ahmedabad — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Bihar, indigo farmers must plant three-twentieths of their land for British planters and surrender the profit; Gandhi takes their case and accepts a 25 percent repayment — small money, a crushing moral defeat for the planters; at Ahmedabad he fasts until the mill owners restore the textile workers' wages.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
