# Gandhi's First Satyagrahas: Champaran and Ahmedabad — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In ____, indigo farmers must plant three-twentieths of their land for British planters and surrender the profit; Gandhi takes their case and accepts a 25 percent repayment — small money, a crushing moral defeat for the planters; at Ahmedabad he fasts until the mill owners restore the textile workers' wages.
**Answer:** Bihar
