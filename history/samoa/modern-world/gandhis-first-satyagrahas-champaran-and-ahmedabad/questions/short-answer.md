# Gandhi's First Satyagrahas: Champaran and Ahmedabad — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** In Bihar, indigo farmers must plant three-twentieths of their land for British planters and surrender the profit; Gandhi takes their case and accepts a 25 percent repayment — small money, a crushing moral defeat for the planters; at Ahmedabad he fasts until the mill owners restore the textile workers' wages.
