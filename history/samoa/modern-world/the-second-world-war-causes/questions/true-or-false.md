# The Second World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Spain's civil war rehearses the Axis and Russia; the Rome-Berlin Axis, Pact of Steel and Anti-Comintern Pact align the dictatorships; appeasement feeds Hitler Austria (Anschluss, March 1938) and the Sudetenland at Munich — the Czechs not even in the room — he takes the rest of Czechoslovakia within six months, and the Nazi-Soviet Pact of 1939 clears his road to Poland.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
