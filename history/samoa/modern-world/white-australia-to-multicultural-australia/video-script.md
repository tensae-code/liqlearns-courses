# Video script — White Australia to Multicultural Australia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Australia formed in 1901 wants only European migrants under assimilation — the "White Australia" policy; after the Second World War 5.5 million immigrants, many non-English-speaking, keep their languages, the policy ends in the 1960s for "integration", and the 1970s bring multiculturalism and open migration; prime Minister John Howard's parliament passes a bipartisan motion committing Australia to racial tolerance and cultural diversity, with statements testing whether the government is sorry that past governments allowed the Stolen Generation.
