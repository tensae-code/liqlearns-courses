# Lesson plan — White Australia to Multicultural Australia

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** White Australia to Multicultural Australia
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Samoa's Road to Independence, Rights and the Industrial World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Australia formed in 1901 wants only European migrants under assimilation — the "White Australia" policy; after the Second World War 5.5 million immigrants, many non-English-speaking, keep their languages, the policy ends in the 1960s for "integration", and the 1970s bring multiculturalism and open migration; prime Minister John Howard's parliament passes a bipartisan motion committing Australia to racial tolerance and cultural diversity, with statements testing whether the government is sorry that past governments allowed the Stolen Generation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Samoa's Road to Independence, Rights and the Industrial World — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: The Australia formed in 1901 wants only European migrants under assimilation — the "White Australia" policy; after the Second World War 5.5 million immigrants, many non-English-speaking, keep their languages, the policy ends in the 1960s for "integration", and the 1970s bring multiculturalism and open migration; prime Minister John Howard's parliament passes a bipartisan motion committing Australia to racial tolerance and cultural diversity, with statements testing whether the government is sorry that past governments allowed the Stolen Generation. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
