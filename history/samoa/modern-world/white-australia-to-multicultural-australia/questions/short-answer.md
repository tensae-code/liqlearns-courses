# White Australia to Multicultural Australia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, Topic 2 "Changes Caused By Cultural Interaction: Changes in Policy"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Australia formed in 1901 wants only European migrants under assimilation — the "White Australia" policy; after the Second World War 5.5 million immigrants, many non-English-speaking, keep their languages, the policy ends in the 1960s for "integration", and the 1970s bring multiculturalism and open migration.

**2.** What came of "Unit 2, Topic 2, the 1996 parliamentary motion" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Prime Minister John Howard's parliament passes a bipartisan motion committing Australia to racial tolerance and cultural diversity, with statements testing whether the government is sorry that past governments allowed the Stolen Generation.
