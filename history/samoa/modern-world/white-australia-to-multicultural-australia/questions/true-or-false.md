# White Australia to Multicultural Australia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Australia formed in 1901 wants only European migrants under assimilation — the "White Australia" policy; after the Second World War 5.5 million immigrants, many non-English-speaking, keep their languages, the policy ends in the 1960s for "integration", and the 1970s bring multiculturalism and open migration. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Topic 2, the 1996 parliamentary motion" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
