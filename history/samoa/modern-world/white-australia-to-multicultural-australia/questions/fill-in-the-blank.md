# White Australia to Multicultural Australia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

(Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Australia

**2.** Prime ____ John Howard's parliament passes a bipartisan motion committing Australia to racial tolerance and cultural diversity, with statements testing whether the government is sorry that past governments allowed the Stolen Generation. (Sub lesson 2)
