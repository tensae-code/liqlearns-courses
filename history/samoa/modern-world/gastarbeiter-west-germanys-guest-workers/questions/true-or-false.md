# Gastarbeiter West Germanys Guest Workers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rebuilding West Germany signs recruitment agreements (Italy and Spain 1960, Greece 1960, Turkey 1961, Portugal 1964, Yugoslavia 1968) and rotates Gastarbeiter through its factories so none settle for good; by 1973 four million foreigners work there, Turks the largest group — until the oil-crisis recession slams recruitment shut that same year.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Units 1–2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
