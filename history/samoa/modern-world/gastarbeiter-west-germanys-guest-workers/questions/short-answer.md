# Gastarbeiter West Germanys Guest Workers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Units 1–2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Rebuilding West Germany signs recruitment agreements (Italy and Spain 1960, Greece 1960, Turkey 1961, Portugal 1964, Yugoslavia 1968) and rotates Gastarbeiter through its factories so none settle for good; by 1973 four million foreigners work there, Turks the largest group — until the oil-crisis recession slams recruitment shut that same year.
