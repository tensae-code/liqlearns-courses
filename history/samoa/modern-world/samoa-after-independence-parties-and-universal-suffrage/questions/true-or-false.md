# Samoa after Independence Parties and Universal Suffrage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** For decades only matai may stand and vote in the territorial seats; the HRPP forms in 1979 as the first post-independence party, and universal suffrage finally arrives at the 1991 general elections.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Topic 2 "Casting Votes And Making Laws"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
