# Samoa after Independence Parties and Universal Suffrage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Topic 2 "Casting Votes And Making Laws"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) For decades only matai may stand and vote in the territorial seats; the HRPP forms in 1979 as the first post-independence party, and universal suffrage finally arrives at the 1991 general elections
**Answer: d) For decades only matai may stand and vote in the territorial seats; the HRPP forms in 1979 as the first post-independence party, and universal suffrage finally arrives at the 1991 general elections** — That is what this lesson teaches.
