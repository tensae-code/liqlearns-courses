# Samoan Emigration after the Second World War — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the Pacific war brings thousands of American troops and a cash boom to Sāmoa; when the military leaves, the taste for wages stays — in 1952 almost a thousand American Sāmoans apply for navy transport to Hawaii, over 6,000 reach the USA in the 1950s, and New Zealand's post-war labour hunger, its 1955 permit system and independence in 1962 open the second great flow — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
