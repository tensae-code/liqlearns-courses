# Lesson plan — Samoan Emigration after the Second World War

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Samoan Emigration after the Second World War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Pacific war brings thousands of American troops and a cash boom to Sāmoa; when the military leaves, the taste for wages stays — in 1952 almost a thousand American Sāmoans apply for navy transport to Hawaii, over 6,000 reach the USA in the 1950s, and New Zealand's post-war labour hunger, its 1955 permit system and independence in 1962 open the second great flow.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: The Pacific war brings thousands of American troops and a cash boom to Sāmoa; when the military leaves, the taste for wages stays — in 1952 almost a thousand American Sāmoans apply for navy transport to Hawaii, over 6,000 reach the USA in the 1950s, and New Zealand's post-war labour hunger, its 1955 permit system and independence in 1962 open the second great flow. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
