# Samoan Emigration after the Second World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Pacific war brings thousands of American troops and a cash boom to Sāmoa; when the military leaves, the taste for wages stays — in 1952 almost a thousand American Sāmoans apply for navy transport to Hawaii, over 6,000 reach the USA in the 1950s, and New Zealand's post-war labour hunger, its 1955 permit system and independence in 1962 open the second great flow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 5" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
