# Samoan Emigration after the Second World War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 5" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** The Pacific war brings thousands of American troops and a cash boom to Sāmoa; when the military leaves, the taste for wages stays — in 1952 almost a thousand American Sāmoans apply for navy transport to Hawaii, over 6,000 reach the USA in the 1950s, and New Zealand's post-war labour hunger, its 1955 permit system and independence in 1962 open the second great flow.
