# First European Voyagers to Samoa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Roggeveen sights the ____ islands in 1722, Bougainville follows, La Pérouse's men are attacked at Asu, Tutuila, with twelve killed, and in 1791 the Pandora's crew answer rocks and spears with Sāmoa's first taste of gunfire.
**Answer:** Manu'a
