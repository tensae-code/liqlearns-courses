# First European Voyagers to Samoa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Roggeveen sights the Manu'a islands in 1722, Bougainville follows, La Pérouse's men are attacked at Asu, Tutuila, with twelve killed, and in 1791 the Pandora's crew answer rocks and spears with Sāmoa's first taste of gunfire.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 3, explorers" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
