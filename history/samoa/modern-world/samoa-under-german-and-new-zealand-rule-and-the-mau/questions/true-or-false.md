# Samoa under German and New Zealand Rule and the Mau — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Germany rules Samoa from 1900; New Zealand seizes it in 1914 as Germany loses the First World War; resentment at New Zealand rule forms the Mau in 1926 under Tupua Tamasese at Vaimoso, resisting peacefully by refusing taxes, registrations and government schools, on the road to independence in 1962.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, "Office of the Mau, Vaimoso"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
