# Video script — Samoa under German and New Zealand Rule and the Mau

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Germany rules Samoa from 1900; New Zealand seizes it in 1914 as Germany loses the First World War; resentment at New Zealand rule forms the Mau in 1926 under Tupua Tamasese at Vaimoso, resisting peacefully by refusing taxes, registrations and government schools, on the road to independence in 1962.
