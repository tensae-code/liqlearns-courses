# Video script — Stalin the Totalitarian Dictator

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Propaganda crowns Stalin "the greatest genius in history and shining sun of the human race"; children join the Young Pioneers, art must serve socialist realism, churches are confiscated and Sunday abolished — until 1941, when he summons Russian pride for the Great Patriotic War; he dies in March 1953, and in 1956 Khrushchev's secret speech strips the cult away and his body is removed from Lenin's tomb.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
