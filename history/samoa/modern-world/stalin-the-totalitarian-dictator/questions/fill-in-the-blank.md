# Stalin the Totalitarian Dictator — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Propaganda crowns ____ "the greatest genius in history and shining sun of the human race"; children join the Young Pioneers, art must serve socialist realism, churches are confiscated and Sunday abolished — until 1941, when he summons Russian pride for the Great Patriotic War; he dies in March 1953, and in 1956 Khrushchev's secret speech strips the cult away and his body is removed from Lenin's tomb.
