# Stalin the Totalitarian Dictator — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 6, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Propaganda crowns Stalin "the greatest genius in history and shining sun of the human race"; children join the Young Pioneers, art must serve socialist realism, churches are confiscated and Sunday abolished — until 1941, when he summons Russian pride for the Great Patriotic War; he dies in March 1953, and in 1956 Khrushchev's secret speech strips the cult away and his body is removed from Lenin's tomb.
