Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 6, Unit 2" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Propaganda crowns Stalin "the greatest genius in history and shining sun of the human race"; children join the Young Pioneers, art must serve socialist realism, churches are confiscated and Sunday abolished — until 1941, when he summons Russian pride for the Great Patriotic War; he dies in March 1953, and in 1956 Khrushchev's secret speech strips the cult away and his body is removed from Lenin's tomb
