# Stalin the Totalitarian Dictator — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Propaganda crowns Stalin "the greatest genius in history and shining sun of the human race"; children join the Young Pioneers, art must serve socialist realism, churches are confiscated and Sunday abolished — until 1941, when he summons Russian pride for the Great Patriotic War; he dies in March 1953, and in 1956 Khrushchev's secret speech strips the cult away and his body is removed from Lenin's tomb.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
