# Quit India Independence and Gandhis Assassination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 6, Units 4–5" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** The 1931 London conference stalls; Gandhi campaigns for the Untouchables, demands the British quit India (1942) and is jailed until 1944; on independence day, 15 August 1947, he neither celebrates nor governs — he spins, prays and fasts amid partition's riots — and on 30 January 1948 a Hindu extremist who hated his acceptance of partition shoots him on his way to prayer; the book's judgement: satyagraha was his gift to the world, though caste and Hindu-Muslim strife outlived him.
