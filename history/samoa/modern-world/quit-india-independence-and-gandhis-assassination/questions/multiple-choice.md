# Quit India Independence and Gandhis Assassination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 6, Units 4–5" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) The 1931 London conference stalls; Gandhi campaigns for the Untouchables, demands the British quit India (1942) and is jailed until 1944; on independence day, 15 August 1947, he neither celebrates nor governs — he spins, prays and fasts amid partition's riots — and on 30 January 1948 a Hindu extremist who hated his acceptance of partition shoots him on his way to prayer; the book's judgement: satyagraha was his gift to the world, though caste and Hindu-Muslim strife outlived him
