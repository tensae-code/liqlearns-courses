# Quit India Independence and Gandhis Assassination — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1931 London conference stalls; Gandhi campaigns for the Untouchables, demands the British quit India (1942) and is jailed until 1944; on independence day, 15 August 1947, he neither celebrates nor governs — he spins, prays and fasts amid partition's riots — and on 30 January 1948 a Hindu extremist who hated his acceptance of partition shoots him on his way to prayer; the book's judgement: satyagraha was his gift to the world, though caste and Hindu-Muslim strife outlived him.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Units 4–5" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
