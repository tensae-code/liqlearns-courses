# Video script — Quit India Independence and Gandhis Assassination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The 1931 London conference stalls; Gandhi campaigns for the Untouchables, demands the British quit India (1942) and is jailed until 1944; on independence day, 15 August 1947, he neither celebrates nor governs — he spins, prays and fasts amid partition's riots — and on 30 January 1948 a Hindu extremist who hated his acceptance of partition shoots him on his way to prayer; the book's judgement: satyagraha was his gift to the world, though caste and Hindu-Muslim strife outlived him.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
