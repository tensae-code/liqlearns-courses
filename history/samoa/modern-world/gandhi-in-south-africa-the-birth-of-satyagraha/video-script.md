# Video script — Gandhi in South Africa the Birth of Satyagraha

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Thrown off a first-class train to Pretoria for being Indian, the young lawyer stays twenty years for South Africa's 90,000 Indians — founds the Natal Indian Congress (1894), fights the Franchise Bill to a London veto, builds the Phoenix Settlement, launches Indian Opinion, and leads the miners' march from Natal into the Transvaal until Smuts yields: "The saint has left our shores, I sincerely hope for ever" (1914).
