# Gandhi in South Africa the Birth of Satyagraha — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 6, Unit 3" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Thrown off a first-class train to Pretoria for being Indian, the young lawyer stays twenty years for South Africa's 90,000 Indians — founds the Natal Indian Congress (1894), fights the Franchise Bill to a London veto, builds the Phoenix Settlement, launches Indian Opinion, and leads the miners' march from Natal into the Transvaal until Smuts yields: "The saint has left our shores, I sincerely hope for ever" (1914).
