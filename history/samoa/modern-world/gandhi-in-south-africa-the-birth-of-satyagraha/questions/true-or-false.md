# Gandhi in South Africa the Birth of Satyagraha — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Thrown off a first-class train to Pretoria for being Indian, the young lawyer stays twenty years for South Africa's 90,000 Indians — founds the Natal Indian Congress (1894), fights the Franchise Bill to a London veto, builds the Phoenix Settlement, launches Indian Opinion, and leads the miners' march from Natal into the Transvaal until Smuts yields: "The saint has left our shores, I sincerely hope for ever" (1914).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
