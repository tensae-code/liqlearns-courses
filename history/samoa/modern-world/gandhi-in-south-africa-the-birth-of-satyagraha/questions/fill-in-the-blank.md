# Gandhi in South Africa the Birth of Satyagraha — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Thrown off a first-class train to ____ for being Indian, the young lawyer stays twenty years for South Africa's 90,000 Indians — founds the Natal Indian Congress (1894), fights the Franchise Bill to a London veto, builds the Phoenix Settlement, launches Indian Opinion, and leads the miners' march from Natal into the Transvaal until Smuts yields: "The saint has left our shores, I sincerely hope for ever" (1914).
