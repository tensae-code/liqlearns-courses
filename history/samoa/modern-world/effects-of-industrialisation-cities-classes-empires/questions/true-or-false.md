# Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain's population leaps from 6.2 million (1750) to 14 million (1830), workers crowd into polluted cities without clean water, a new working class grows, the 1832 law gives middle-class men the vote, and the British Empire spreads over colonised lands that feed the factories. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Topic 1 child workers' stories (plot summaries only)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
