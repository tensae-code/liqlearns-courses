# Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, Topic 1 "Changes During The Industrial Revolution"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain's population leaps from 6.2 million (1750) to 14 million (1830), workers crowd into polluted cities without clean water, a new working class grows, the 1832 law gives middle-class men the vote, and the British Empire spreads over colonised lands that feed the factories.

**2.** What came of "Unit 2, Topic 1 child workers' stories (plot summaries only)" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Seven-year-old John Saville works as a trapper in the dark of a coal mine, opening doors for wagons that run over his leg; a young girl coal-bearer hauls baskets of coal out of an 1830s mine on her back.
