Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 2, Topic 1 "Changes During The Industrial Revolution"" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Britain's population leaps from 6.2 million (1750) to 14 million (1830), workers crowd into polluted cities without clean water, a new working class grows, the 1832 law gives middle-class men the vote, and the British Empire spreads over colonised lands that feed the factories
**Answer: d) Britain's population leaps from 6.2 million (1750) to 14 million (1830), workers crowd into polluted cities without clean water, a new working class grows, the 1832 law gives middle-class men the vote, and the British Empire spreads over colonised lands that feed the factories** — That is what this lesson teaches.
