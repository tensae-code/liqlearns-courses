# Effects of Industrialisation Cities Classes Empires — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Britain's population leaps from 6.2 million (1750) to 14 million (1830), workers crowd into polluted cities without clean water, a new working class grows, the 1832 law gives middle-class men the vote, and the ____ Empire spreads over colonised lands that feed the factories. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** British

**2.** Seven-year-old ____ Saville works as a trapper in the dark of a coal mine, opening doors for wagons that run over his leg; a young girl coal-bearer hauls baskets of coal out of an 1830s mine on her back. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** John
