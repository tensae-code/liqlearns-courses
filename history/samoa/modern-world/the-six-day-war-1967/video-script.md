# Video script — The Six Day War 1967

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Fearing defeat, Israel strikes first on 5 June 1967, destroys Egypt's air force on the ground, reaches the Suez Canal in three days, takes the West Bank from Jordan and the Golan Heights from Syria before the UN ceasefire — a victory that settles nothing.

Now the part called "Part 3, Unit 4". fearing defeat, Israel strikes first on 5 June 1967, destroys Egypt's air force on the ground, reaches the Suez Canal in three days, takes the West Bank from Jordan and the Golan Heights from Syria before the UN ceasefire — a victory that settles nothing.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
