# The Six Day War 1967 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fearing defeat, Israel strikes first on 5 June 1967, destroys Egypt's air force on the ground, reaches the Suez Canal in three days, takes the West Bank from Jordan and the Golan Heights from Syria before the UN ceasefire — a victory that settles nothing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
