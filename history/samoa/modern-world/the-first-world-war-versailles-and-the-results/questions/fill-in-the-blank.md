# The First World War Versailles and the Results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Reparations fixed at £6.6 billion (1921) break ____ by 1923 — France occupies the Ruhr, workers strike, and a Berlin loaf climbs from 0.63 marks to 201 billion; Stresemann and the American Dawes Plan set the "money-go-round" turning, Locarno (1925) settles the western frontier, Germany enters the League (1926), Kellogg-Briand outlaws war (1928) and the Young Plan cuts the bill to £2 billion (1929).
