# The First World War Versailles and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 4, Unit 2, between the wars" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Reparations fixed at £6.6 billion (1921) break Germany by 1923 — France occupies the Ruhr, workers strike, and a Berlin loaf climbs from 0.63 marks to 201 billion; Stresemann and the American Dawes Plan set the "money-go-round" turning, Locarno (1925) settles the western frontier, Germany enters the League (1926), Kellogg-Briand outlaws war (1928) and the Young Plan cuts the bill to £2 billion (1929).
