# The First World War Versailles and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Reparations fixed at £6.6 billion (1921) break Germany by 1923 — France occupies the Ruhr, workers strike, and a Berlin loaf climbs from 0.63 marks to 201 billion; Stresemann and the American Dawes Plan set the "money-go-round" turning, Locarno (1925) settles the western frontier, Germany enters the League (1926), Kellogg-Briand outlaws war (1928) and the Young Plan cuts the bill to £2 billion (1929).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 2, between the wars" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
