# The Birth of Israel and the First Arab Israeli War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 3, Units 3–4" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Britain hands Palestine to the UN; partition is recommended in 1947, Israel is proclaimed in May 1948 with Ben-Gurion as first prime minister, the Arab League (formed 1945) rejects the division, and in the 1948–49 war Israel survives and takes more land than the partition gave it
