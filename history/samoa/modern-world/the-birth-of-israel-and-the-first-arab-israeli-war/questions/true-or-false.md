# The Birth of Israel and the First Arab Israeli War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain hands Palestine to the UN; partition is recommended in 1947, Israel is proclaimed in May 1948 with Ben-Gurion as first prime minister, the Arab League (formed 1945) rejects the division, and in the 1948–49 war Israel survives and takes more land than the partition gave it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Units 3–4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
