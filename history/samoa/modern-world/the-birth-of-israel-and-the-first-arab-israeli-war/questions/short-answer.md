# The Birth of Israel and the First Arab Israeli War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Units 3–4" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Britain hands Palestine to the UN; partition is recommended in 1947, Israel is proclaimed in May 1948 with Ben-Gurion as first prime minister, the Arab League (formed 1945) rejects the division, and in the 1948–49 war Israel survives and takes more land than the partition gave it.
