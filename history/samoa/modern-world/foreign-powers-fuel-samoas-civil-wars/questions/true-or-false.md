# Foreign Powers Fuel Samoas Civil Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book's verdict in table form — the three powers fuelled the wars with arms, naval backing and chiefs of their own, each putting its interests (plantations, Pago Pago harbour) before Sāmoans, and only partly eased them with treaties and the central government they wanted for their land claims.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
