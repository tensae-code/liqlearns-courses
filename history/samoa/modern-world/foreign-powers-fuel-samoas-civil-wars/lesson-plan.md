## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: The book's verdict in table form — the three powers fuelled the wars with arms, naval backing and chiefs of their own, each putting its interests (plantations, Pago Pago harbour) before Sāmoans, and only partly eased them with treaties and the central government they wanted for their land claims. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
