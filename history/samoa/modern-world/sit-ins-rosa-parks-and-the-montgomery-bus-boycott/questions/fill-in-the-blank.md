Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Black students sit peacefully all day at a ____ lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows.
