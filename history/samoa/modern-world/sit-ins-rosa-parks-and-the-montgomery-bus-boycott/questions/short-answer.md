# Sit Ins Rosa Parks and the Montgomery Bus Boycott — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows.
