# Sit Ins Rosa Parks and the Montgomery Bus Boycott — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows
**Answer: d) Black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News""?
a) Black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows** — That is what this lesson teaches.
