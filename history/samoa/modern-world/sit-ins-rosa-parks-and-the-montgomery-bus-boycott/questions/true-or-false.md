# Sit Ins Rosa Parks and the Montgomery Bus Boycott — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
