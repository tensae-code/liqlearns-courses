# Video script — Sit Ins Rosa Parks and the Montgomery Bus Boycott

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows.

Now the part called "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"". black students sit peacefully all day at a Woolworths lunch counter until the shop shuts, and keep coming until it desegregates; in Montgomery, Alabama, in December 1955 Rosa Parks stays in her seat on a segregated bus and a boycott follows.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
