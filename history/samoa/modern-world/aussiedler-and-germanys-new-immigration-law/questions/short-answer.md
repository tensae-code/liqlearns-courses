# Aussiedler and Germanys New Immigration Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Ethnic Germans (Aussiedler) return in waves — 1.4 million in 1950–87, 2.7 million more in 1988–2000 — until quotas (225,000 a year, then 103,000) slow the flow; asylum is tightened in 1993, a 2000 citizenship law gives German-born children of foreigners citizenship for the first time in some ninety years, and a green card opens the door to skilled IT workers instead.
