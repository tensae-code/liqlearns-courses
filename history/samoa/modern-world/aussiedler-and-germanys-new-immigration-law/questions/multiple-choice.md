# Aussiedler and Germanys New Immigration Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 1, Unit 2" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Ethnic Germans (Aussiedler) return in waves — 1.4 million in 1950–87, 2.7 million more in 1988–2000 — until quotas (225,000 a year, then 103,000) slow the flow; asylum is tightened in 1993, a 2000 citizenship law gives German-born children of foreigners citizenship for the first time in some ninety years, and a green card opens the door to skilled IT workers instead
**Answer: d) Ethnic Germans (Aussiedler) return in waves — 1.4 million in 1950–87, 2.7 million more in 1988–2000 — until quotas (225,000 a year, then 103,000) slow the flow; asylum is tightened in 1993, a 2000 citizenship law gives German-born children of foreigners citizenship for the first time in some ninety years, and a green card opens the door to skilled IT workers instead** — That is what this lesson teaches.
