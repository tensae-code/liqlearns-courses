# Aussiedler and Germanys New Immigration Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ethnic Germans (Aussiedler) return in waves — 1.4 million in 1950–87, 2.7 million more in 1988–2000 — until quotas (225,000 a year, then 103,000) slow the flow; asylum is tightened in 1993, a 2000 citizenship law gives German-born children of foreigners citizenship for the first time in some ninety years, and a green card opens the door to skilled IT workers instead.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
