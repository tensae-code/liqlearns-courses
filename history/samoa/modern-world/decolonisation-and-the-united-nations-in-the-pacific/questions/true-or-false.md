# Decolonisation and the United Nations in the Pacific — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the Second World War the UN trusteeship system replaces mandates; Sāmoan petitions, the Fono a Faipule and the Citizens' Committee press New Zealand from inside, and the Pacific's colonies take three roads out — pressure from within (Sāmoa, Nauru, Vanuatu), independence fostered by the colonial power (Fiji), or internal self-government under an external ruler.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 5" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
