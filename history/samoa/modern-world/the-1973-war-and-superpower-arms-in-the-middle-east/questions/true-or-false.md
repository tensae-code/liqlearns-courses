# The 1973 War and Superpower Arms in the Middle East — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sadat expels the Soviets, then attacks with Syria on 6 October 1973 to force the superpowers to act; Israel wins at high cost, and the book's arms tables show the pattern — the USSR arming the Arabs, the USA arming Israel, each fuelling the conflict it claims to calm.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Units 4–6" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
