# The Road from Trusteeship to Independence Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 4, Unit 1" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** The Sāmoa Act 1921 confirms the League mandate; Peter Fraser builds trusteeship after 1945; a Sāmoan parliament and Guy Powles's timetable follow in 1947; in the UN plebiscite of 1961, 79% vote for independence, and on 1 January 1962 the Sāmoan flag rises alone — then the Treaty of Friendship with New Zealand, UN membership in 1976, and the name "Sāmoa" confirmed in 1997.
