# The Road from Trusteeship to Independence Day — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Sāmoa Act 1921 confirms the League mandate; Peter Fraser builds trusteeship after 1945; a Sāmoan parliament and Guy Powles's timetable follow in 1947; in the UN plebiscite of 1961, 79% vote for independence, and on 1 January 1962 the Sāmoan flag rises alone — then the Treaty of Friendship with New Zealand, UN membership in 1976, and the name "Sāmoa" confirmed in 1997.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
