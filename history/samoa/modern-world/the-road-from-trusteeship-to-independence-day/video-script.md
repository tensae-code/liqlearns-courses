# Video script — The Road from Trusteeship to Independence Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Sāmoa Act 1921 confirms the League mandate; Peter Fraser builds trusteeship after 1945; a Sāmoan parliament and Guy Powles's timetable follow in 1947; in the UN plebiscite of 1961, 79% vote for independence, and on 1 January 1962 the Sāmoan flag rises alone — then the Treaty of Friendship with New Zealand, UN membership in 1976, and the name "Sāmoa" confirmed in 1997.

Now the part called "Part 4, Unit 1". the Sāmoa Act 1921 confirms the League mandate; Peter Fraser builds trusteeship after 1945; a Sāmoan parliament and Guy Powles's timetable follow in 1947; in the UN plebiscite of 1961, 79% vote for independence, and on 1 January 1962 the Sāmoan flag rises alone — then the Treaty of Friendship with New Zealand, UN membership in 1976, and the name "Sāmoa" confirmed in 1997.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
