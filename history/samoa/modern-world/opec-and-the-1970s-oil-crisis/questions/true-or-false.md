# OPEC and the 1970s Oil Crisis — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Five founder members — Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia and Venezuela — form OPEC at Baghdad on 14 September 1960 to control oil supply and price as a cartel, and the worldwide oil crisis of the early 1970s reaches even Samoa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 3 Part B, Topic 1 "OPEC: A Powerful International Organisation"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
