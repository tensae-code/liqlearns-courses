# Video script — Non Cooperation 1920 22 Khadi and Chauri Chaura

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Two British "wrongs" — Amritsar and the Khilafat — unite Hindus and Muslims behind Gandhi; he returns his medals, titles are surrendered, foreign cloth burns, the spinning wheel and khadi become the uniform of revolt (6,000 khadi-clad Congress delegates; Jinnah alone in a suit), 20,000 are jailed — and Gandhi himself halts the movement when a mob at Chauri Chaura kills twenty-two policemen.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
