# Non Cooperation 1920 22 Khadi and Chauri Chaura — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 6, Unit 4" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Two British "wrongs" — Amritsar and the Khilafat — unite Hindus and Muslims behind Gandhi; he returns his medals, titles are surrendered, foreign cloth burns, the spinning wheel and khadi become the uniform of revolt (6,000 khadi-clad Congress delegates; Jinnah alone in a suit), 20,000 are jailed — and Gandhi himself halts the movement when a mob at Chauri Chaura kills twenty-two policemen.
