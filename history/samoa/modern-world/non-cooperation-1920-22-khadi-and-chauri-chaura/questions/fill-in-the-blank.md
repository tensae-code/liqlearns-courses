# Non Cooperation 1920 22 Khadi and Chauri Chaura — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Two ____ "wrongs" — Amritsar and the Khilafat — unite Hindus and Muslims behind Gandhi; he returns his medals, titles are surrendered, foreign cloth burns, the spinning wheel and khadi become the uniform of revolt (6,000 khadi-clad Congress delegates; Jinnah alone in a suit), 20,000 are jailed — and Gandhi himself halts the movement when a mob at Chauri Chaura kills twenty-two policemen.
