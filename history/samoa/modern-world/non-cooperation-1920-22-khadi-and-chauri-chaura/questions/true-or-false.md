# Non Cooperation 1920 22 Khadi and Chauri Chaura — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two British "wrongs" — Amritsar and the Khilafat — unite Hindus and Muslims behind Gandhi; he returns his medals, titles are surrendered, foreign cloth burns, the spinning wheel and khadi become the uniform of revolt (6,000 khadi-clad Congress delegates; Jinnah alone in a suit), 20,000 are jailed — and Gandhi himself halts the movement when a mob at Chauri Chaura kills twenty-two policemen.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
