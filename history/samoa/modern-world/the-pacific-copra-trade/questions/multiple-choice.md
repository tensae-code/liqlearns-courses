# The Pacific Copra Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 3, Fact File Four "The Demand and Supply of Copra and Coconut Oil"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Northern Europe's shortage of dairy fats makes Britain and Germany hungry for copra in the 1860s, America follows in the early 1900s, and the big plantations planted in Samoa's German colonial period and under New Zealand administration age into today's low-yield trees
