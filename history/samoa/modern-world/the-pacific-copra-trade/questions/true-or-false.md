# The Pacific Copra Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Northern Europe's shortage of dairy fats makes Britain and Germany hungry for copra in the 1860s, America follows in the early 1900s, and the big plantations planted in Samoa's German colonial period and under New Zealand administration age into today's low-yield trees.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 3, Fact File Four "The Demand and Supply of Copra and Coconut Oil"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
