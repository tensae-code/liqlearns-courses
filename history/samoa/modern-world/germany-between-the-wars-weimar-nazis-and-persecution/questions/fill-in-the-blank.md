# Germany between the Wars Weimar Nazis and Persecution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Defeated, bankrupt ____ Germany cannot hold its centre; Hitler, chancellor from 1933, restores jobs and pride at the price of a totalitarian state — Jewish shops boycotted (1 April 1933), Jews barred from public jobs, the Nuremberg Laws (1935) strip their citizenship, and half of Germany's Jews, Einstein among them, flee abroad.
**Answer:** Weimar
