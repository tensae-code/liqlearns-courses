# Germany between the Wars Weimar Nazis and Persecution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Defeated, bankrupt Weimar Germany cannot hold its centre; Hitler, chancellor from 1933, restores jobs and pride at the price of a totalitarian state — Jewish shops boycotted (1 April 1933), Jews barred from public jobs, the Nuremberg Laws (1935) strip their citizenship, and half of Germany's Jews, Einstein among them, flee abroad.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
