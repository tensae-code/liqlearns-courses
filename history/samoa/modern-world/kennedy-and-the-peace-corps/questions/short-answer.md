# Kennedy and the Peace Corps — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 4, Topic 1 "Peace Corps"" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Campaigning at the University of Michigan at 2 a.m. on 14 October 1960, John F. Kennedy challenges 10,000 students to serve in the developing world; the Peace Corps is set up on 1 March 1961, reaches Samoa in 1967, and sends more than 1,700 volunteers there.
