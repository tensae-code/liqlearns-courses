# Kennedy and the Peace Corps — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Campaigning at the University of Michigan at 2 a.m. on 14 October 1960, John F. Kennedy challenges 10,000 students to serve in the developing world; the Peace Corps is set up on 1 March 1961, reaches Samoa in 1967, and sends more than 1,700 volunteers there.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 4, Topic 1 "Peace Corps"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
