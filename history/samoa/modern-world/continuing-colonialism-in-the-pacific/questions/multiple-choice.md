# Continuing Colonialism in the Pacific — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 2, Unit 6" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) The book asks whether colonialism really ended — Pacific states still depend on their former colonisers, and learners research the petition asking New Zealand to grant Sāmoans New Zealand citizenship, and why other Sāmoans objected
**Answer: d) The book asks whether colonialism really ended — Pacific states still depend on their former colonisers, and learners research the petition asking New Zealand to grant Sāmoans New Zealand citizenship, and why other Sāmoans objected** — That is what this lesson teaches.
