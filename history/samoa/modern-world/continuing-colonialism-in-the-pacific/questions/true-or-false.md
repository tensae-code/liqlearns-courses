# Continuing Colonialism in the Pacific — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book asks whether colonialism really ended — Pacific states still depend on their former colonisers, and learners research the petition asking New Zealand to grant Sāmoans New Zealand citizenship, and why other Sāmoans objected.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 6" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
