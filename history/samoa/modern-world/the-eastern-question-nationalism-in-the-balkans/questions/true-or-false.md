# The Eastern Question Nationalism in the Balkans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Balkan peoples long ruled by Turkey and Austria-Hungary demand nation states of their own; Serbia's drive to unite all South Slavs threatens to tear the Habsburg empire apart, and the alliance web — Serbia backed by Russia, Austria-Hungary by Germany — wires a local quarrel to every great power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
