# Video script — Falealupo and the Rainforest Covenant

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Falealupo village, needing a school it must fund itself, painfully votes to log its rainforest; former missionary Dr Paul Cox raises the school money overseas instead, and in 1990 the chiefs covenant to protect 50,000 acres for fifty years; matai Fuiono Senio runs six miles to stand before the bulldozers, survives two cyclones without selling, and shares the 1997 Goldman Prize with Cox before his death that year.
