# Falealupo and the Rainforest Covenant — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Falealupo village, needing a school it must fund itself, painfully votes to log its rainforest; former missionary Dr Paul Cox raises the school money overseas instead, and in 1990 the chiefs covenant to protect 50,000 acres for fifty years; matai Fuiono Senio runs six miles to stand before the bulldozers, survives two cyclones without selling, and shares the 1997 Goldman Prize with Cox before his death that year.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 3, Topic 4 "Change, Conflict And Then Consensus" case study" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
