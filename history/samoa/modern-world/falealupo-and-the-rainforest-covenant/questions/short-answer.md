# Falealupo and the Rainforest Covenant — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 3, Topic 4 "Change, Conflict And Then Consensus" case study" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Falealupo village, needing a school it must fund itself, painfully votes to log its rainforest; former missionary Dr Paul Cox raises the school money overseas instead, and in 1990 the chiefs covenant to protect 50,000 acres for fifty years; matai Fuiono Senio runs six miles to stand before the bulldozers, survives two cyclones without selling, and shares the 1997 Goldman Prize with Cox before his death that year.
