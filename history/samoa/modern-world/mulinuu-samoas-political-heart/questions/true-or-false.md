# Mulinu'u: Samoa's Political Heart — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A Faleata matai's peninsula by Apia harbour becomes Samoa's gathering ground in the mid-1800s wars, hosts the first Parliament House of the 1873 attempt at a Samoan central government, and stays the seat of parliament through the 1981 independence celebrations pictured.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Topic 4 "Mulinu'u"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
