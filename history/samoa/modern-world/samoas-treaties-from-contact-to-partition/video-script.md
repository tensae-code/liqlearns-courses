# Video script — Samoa's Treaties: From Contact to Partition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. From Bethune's 1838 harbour-dues treaty and the first consuls (Pritchard 1847, Weber 1861) through Steinberger's 1873 peace that makes Laupepa king under a European-model constitution, the Apia Municipality of 1879 and the Berlin Treaty of 1889, to the 1899 settlement that annuls it and partitions Sāmoa between Germany (west) and America (east).
