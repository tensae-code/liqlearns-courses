# Samoa's Treaties: From Contact to Partition — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: from Bethune's 1838 harbour-dues treaty and the first consuls (Pritchard 1847, Weber 1861) through Steinberger's 1873 peace that makes Laupepa king under a European-model constitution, the Apia Municipality of 1879 and the Berlin Treaty of 1889, to the 1899 settlement that annuls it and partitions Sāmoa between Germany (west) and America (east) — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
