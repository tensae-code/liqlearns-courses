# Samoa's Treaties: From Contact to Partition — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Bethune's 1838 harbour-dues treaty and the first consuls (Pritchard 1847, Weber 1861) through Steinberger's 1873 peace that makes Laupepa king under a European-model constitution, the Apia Municipality of 1879 and the Berlin Treaty of 1889, to the 1899 settlement that annuls it and partitions Sāmoa between Germany (west) and America (east).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
