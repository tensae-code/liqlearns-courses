# Samoa's Treaties: From Contact to Partition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 4, Unit 1" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** From Bethune's 1838 harbour-dues treaty and the first consuls (Pritchard 1847, Weber 1861) through Steinberger's 1873 peace that makes Laupepa king under a European-model constitution, the Apia Municipality of 1879 and the Berlin Treaty of 1889, to the 1899 settlement that annuls it and partitions Sāmoa between Germany (west) and America (east).
