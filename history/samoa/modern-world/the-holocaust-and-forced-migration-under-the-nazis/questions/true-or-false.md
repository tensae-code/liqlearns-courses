# The Holocaust and Forced Migration under the Nazis — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Priests and Confessing Church pastors who defy the broken 1933 Concordat go to labour camps; at Wannsee in January 1942 the "Final Solution" is set — six camps become extermination camps and almost six million Jews are murdered in three years, while millions of conquered peoples are dragged into Germany as slave labour for the war factories.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
