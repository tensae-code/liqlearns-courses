# The Holocaust and Forced Migration under the Nazis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 1" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Priests and Confessing Church pastors who defy the broken 1933 Concordat go to labour camps; at Wannsee in January 1942 the "Final Solution" is set — six camps become extermination camps and almost six million Jews are murdered in three years, while millions of conquered peoples are dragged into Germany as slave labour for the war factories.
