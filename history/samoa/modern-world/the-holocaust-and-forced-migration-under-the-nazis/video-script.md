# Video script — The Holocaust and Forced Migration under the Nazis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Priests and Confessing Church pastors who defy the broken 1933 Concordat go to labour camps; at Wannsee in January 1942 the "Final Solution" is set — six camps become extermination camps and almost six million Jews are murdered in three years, while millions of conquered peoples are dragged into Germany as slave labour for the war factories.

Now the part called "Part 1, Unit 1". priests and Confessing Church pastors who defy the broken 1933 Concordat go to labour camps; at Wannsee in January 1942 the "Final Solution" is set — six camps become extermination camps and almost six million Jews are murdered in three years, while millions of conquered peoples are dragged into Germany as slave labour for the war factories.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
