# Melanesian and Chinese Indentured Labour in Samoa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 3, non-European migrants" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** German plantation companies, finding Sāmoans unwilling, ship in Melanesians as young as fourteen from Bougainville, Malaita and the Bismarcks on three-year contracts, and from March 1903 the DHPG lands 289 Chinese — 2,184 by 1914; New Zealand repatriates 1,254 in 1915–19, restarts the system, then forces married men home away from their Sāmoan families, yet almost 200 settle for good.
