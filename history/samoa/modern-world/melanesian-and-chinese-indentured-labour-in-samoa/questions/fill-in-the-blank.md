# Melanesian and Chinese Indentured Labour in Samoa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** German plantation companies, finding ____ unwilling, ship in Melanesians as young as fourteen from Bougainville, Malaita and the Bismarcks on three-year contracts, and from March 1903 the DHPG lands 289 Chinese — 2,184 by 1914; New Zealand repatriates 1,254 in 1915–19, restarts the system, then forces married men home away from their Sāmoan families, yet almost 200 settle for good.
**Answer:** Sāmoans
