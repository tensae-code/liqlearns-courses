# Melanesian and Chinese Indentured Labour in Samoa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** German plantation companies, finding Sāmoans unwilling, ship in Melanesians as young as fourteen from Bougainville, Malaita and the Bismarcks on three-year contracts, and from March 1903 the DHPG lands 289 Chinese — 2,184 by 1914; New Zealand repatriates 1,254 in 1915–19, restarts the system, then forces married men home away from their Sāmoan families, yet almost 200 settle for good.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 3, non-European migrants" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
