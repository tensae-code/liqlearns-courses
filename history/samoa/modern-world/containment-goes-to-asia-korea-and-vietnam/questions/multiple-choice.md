# Containment Goes to Asia Korea and Vietnam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 5, Unit 2" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Containment leaves Europe — Korea, divided in 1945 between Soviet north and American south, goes to war in 1950 when reunification elections fail, and three years of fighting change nothing: two Koreas remain; Vietnam splits the same way, and America commits to the south
