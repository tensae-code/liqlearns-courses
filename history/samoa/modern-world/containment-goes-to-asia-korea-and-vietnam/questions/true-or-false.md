# Containment Goes to Asia Korea and Vietnam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Containment leaves Europe — Korea, divided in 1945 between Soviet north and American south, goes to war in 1950 when reunification elections fail, and three years of fighting change nothing: two Koreas remain; Vietnam splits the same way, and America commits to the south.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 5, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
