# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 6, Units 2–3" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** A 300-year autocracy (serfs until 1861, the Okhrana, a powerless Duma from 1905) bleeds in the First World War — six million casualties by 1917; hunger and strikes topple the Tsar in March, Kerensky's provisional government falters, and in October Lenin's Bolsheviks seize the capital, make peace, win the 1918–20 civil war against the White armies and their foreign backers, and proclaim the USSR by 1923.
