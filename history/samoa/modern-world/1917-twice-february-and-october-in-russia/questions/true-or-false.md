# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A 300-year autocracy (serfs until 1861, the Okhrana, a powerless Duma from 1905) bleeds in the First World War — six million casualties by 1917; hunger and strikes topple the Tsar in March, Kerensky's provisional government falters, and in October Lenin's Bolsheviks seize the capital, make peace, win the 1918–20 civil war against the White armies and their foreign backers, and proclaim the USSR by 1923.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Units 2–3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
