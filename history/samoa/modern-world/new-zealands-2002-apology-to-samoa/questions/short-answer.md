# New Zealand's 2002 Apology to Samoa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, Topic 2, Helen Clark's speech and NZ Herald articles (5 June 2002)" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** At Samoa's 40th independence celebrations, Prime Minister Helen Clark formally apologises for New Zealand's inept administration of 1914–1962 — the 1918 influenza, the 1929 shooting of unarmed Mau protesters, the banishing of matai — presenting a waka huia; at Mangere, 78-year-old Seiuli Otto Hansell, born just after the epidemic, weeps and bows to her courage, and Tupua Tamasese Tupuola Efi, whose great-uncle was shot, calls the apology deeply moving.
