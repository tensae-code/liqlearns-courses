# New Zealand's 2002 Apology to Samoa — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: at Samoa's 40th independence celebrations, Prime Minister Helen Clark formally apologises for New Zealand's inept administration of 1914–1962 — the 1918 influenza, the 1929 shooting of unarmed Mau protesters, the banishing of matai — presenting a waka huia; at Mangere, 78-year-old Seiuli Otto Hansell, born just after the epidemic, weeps and bows to her courage, and Tupua Tamasese Tupuola Efi, whose great-uncle was shot, calls the apology deeply moving — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
