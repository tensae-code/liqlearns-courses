# Video script — New Zealand's 2002 Apology to Samoa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. At Samoa's 40th independence celebrations, Prime Minister Helen Clark formally apologises for New Zealand's inept administration of 1914–1962 — the 1918 influenza, the 1929 shooting of unarmed Mau protesters, the banishing of matai — presenting a waka huia; at Mangere, 78-year-old Seiuli Otto Hansell, born just after the epidemic, weeps and bows to her courage, and Tupua Tamasese Tupuola Efi, whose great-uncle was shot, calls the apology deeply moving.
