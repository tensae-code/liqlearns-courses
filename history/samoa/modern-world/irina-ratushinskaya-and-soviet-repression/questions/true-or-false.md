# Irina Ratushinskaya and Soviet Repression — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ukrainian teacher Irina Ratushinskaya loses her job for refusing to discriminate against Jewish students, is sentenced in 1982 to seven years' hard labour for her religious poetry, and is freed and expelled in 1986 after an international outcry.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Case Study 4 "Freedom Of Religion"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
