# Video script — Irina Ratushinskaya and Soviet Repression

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Ukrainian teacher Irina Ratushinskaya loses her job for refusing to discriminate against Jewish students, is sentenced in 1982 to seven years' hard labour for her religious poetry, and is freed and expelled in 1986 after an international outcry.
