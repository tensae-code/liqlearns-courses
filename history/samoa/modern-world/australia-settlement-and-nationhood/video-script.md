# Video script — Australia Settlement and Nationhood

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Portuguese and Dutch explorers come first; Captain Cook sails the east coast in 1770 and claims it for Britain as New South Wales; convict ships follow in 1787 on Joseph Banks's suggestion, gold in 1850 pulls free migrants in crowds and pushes Aborigines off their land, and Australia becomes a nation on 1 January 1901, fighting beside Britain in the Boer War and both World Wars before post-1945 and 1980s migration waves make it multicultural.

Now the part called "Unit 2, Topic 1, Australia history box". Portuguese and Dutch explorers come first; Captain Cook sails the east coast in 1770 and claims it for Britain as New South Wales; convict ships follow in 1787 on Joseph Banks's suggestion, gold in 1850 pulls free migrants in crowds and pushes Aborigines off their land, and Australia becomes a nation on 1 January 1901, fighting beside Britain in the Boer War and both World Wars before post-1945 and 1980s migration waves make it multicultural.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
