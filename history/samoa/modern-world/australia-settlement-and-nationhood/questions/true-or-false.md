# Australia Settlement and Nationhood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Portuguese and Dutch explorers come first; Captain Cook sails the east coast in 1770 and claims it for Britain as New South Wales; convict ships follow in 1787 on Joseph Banks's suggestion, gold in 1850 pulls free migrants in crowds and pushes Aborigines off their land, and Australia becomes a nation on 1 January 1901, fighting beside Britain in the Boer War and both World Wars before post-1945 and 1980s migration waves make it multicultural.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Topic 1, Australia history box" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
