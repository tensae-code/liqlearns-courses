# Australia Settlement and Nationhood — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: portuguese and Dutch explorers come first; Captain Cook sails the east coast in 1770 and claims it for Britain as New South Wales; convict ships follow in 1787 on Joseph Banks's suggestion, gold in 1850 pulls free migrants in crowds and pushes Aborigines off their land, and Australia becomes a nation on 1 January 1901, fighting beside Britain in the Boer War and both World Wars before post-1945 and 1980s migration waves make it multicultural — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
