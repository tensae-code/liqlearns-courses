# Video script — The Berlin Wall and Flight from the East

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. 3.8 million East Germans walk west before August 1961 — on some days twenty thousand — so the Soviets seal West Berlin with the Wall; about a hundred die still trying to cross, nearly 400,000 more escape by 1988, and the West reads every arrival as a vote against communism.

Now the part called "Part 1, Unit 2 and Part 2, Unit 2". 3.8 million East Germans walk west before August 1961 — on some days twenty thousand — so the Soviets seal West Berlin with the Wall; about a hundred die still trying to cross, nearly 400,000 more escape by 1988, and the West reads every arrival as a vote against communism.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
