# The Berlin Wall and Flight from the East — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** 3.8 million East Germans walk west before August 1961 — on some days twenty thousand — so the Soviets seal West Berlin with the Wall; about a hundred die still trying to cross, nearly 400,000 more escape by 1988, and the West reads every arrival as a vote against communism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 2 and Part 2, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
