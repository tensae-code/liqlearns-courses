# The Berlin Wall and Flight from the East — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 2 and Part 2, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** 3.8 million East Germans walk west before August 1961 — on some days twenty thousand — so the Soviets seal West Berlin with the Wall; about a hundred die still trying to cross, nearly 400,000 more escape by 1988, and the West reads every arrival as a vote against communism.
