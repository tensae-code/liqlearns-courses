# The Berlin Wall and Flight from the East — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** 3.8 million ____ Germans walk west before August 1961 — on some days twenty thousand — so the Soviets seal West Berlin with the Wall; about a hundred die still trying to cross, nearly 400,000 more escape by 1988, and the West reads every arrival as a vote against communism.
**Answer:** East
