# Women Work and the Second World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the USA, Britain, New Zealand and Australia married women rarely worked for money until the Second World War emptied men's jobs; women fill them, refuse to hand them all back, and by the 1980s are training as accountants, doctors, engineers, lawyers, mechanics, police and pilots.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2 introduction, social change characteristic 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
