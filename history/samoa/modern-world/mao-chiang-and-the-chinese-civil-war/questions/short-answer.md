# Mao Chiang and the Chinese Civil War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 6, Units 4–5" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** The young Communist Party allies with Sun's Guomindang until Chiang Kai-shek's 1927 Shanghai purge drowns the alliance in blood; Mao rebuilds in the countryside, leads the 13,000 km Long March (1934–35) to Yan'an, and after 22 years of struggle the communists win in 1949 — Chiang exiled to Taiwan, Mao ruling the People's Republic until 1976.
