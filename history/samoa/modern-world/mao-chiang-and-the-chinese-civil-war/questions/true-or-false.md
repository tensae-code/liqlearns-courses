# Mao Chiang and the Chinese Civil War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The young Communist Party allies with Sun's Guomindang until Chiang Kai-shek's 1927 Shanghai purge drowns the alliance in blood; Mao rebuilds in the countryside, leads the 13,000 km Long March (1934–35) to Yan'an, and after 22 years of struggle the communists win in 1949 — Chiang exiled to Taiwan, Mao ruling the People's Republic until 1976.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Units 4–5" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
