# Mao Chiang and the Chinese Civil War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 6, Units 4–5" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) The young Communist Party allies with Sun's Guomindang until Chiang Kai-shek's 1927 Shanghai purge drowns the alliance in blood; Mao rebuilds in the countryside, leads the 13,000 km Long March (1934–35) to Yan'an, and after 22 years of struggle the communists win in 1949 — Chiang exiled to Taiwan, Mao ruling the People's Republic until 1976
**Answer: d) The young Communist Party allies with Sun's Guomindang until Chiang Kai-shek's 1927 Shanghai purge drowns the alliance in blood; Mao rebuilds in the countryside, leads the 13,000 km Long March (1934–35) to Yan'an, and after 22 years of struggle the communists win in 1949 — Chiang exiled to Taiwan, Mao ruling the People's Republic until 1976** — That is what this lesson teaches.
