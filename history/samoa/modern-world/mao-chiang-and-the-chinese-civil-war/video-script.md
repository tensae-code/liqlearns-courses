# Video script — Mao Chiang and the Chinese Civil War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The young Communist Party allies with Sun's Guomindang until Chiang Kai-shek's 1927 Shanghai purge drowns the alliance in blood; Mao rebuilds in the countryside, leads the 13,000 km Long March (1934–35) to Yan'an, and after 22 years of struggle the communists win in 1949 — Chiang exiled to Taiwan, Mao ruling the People's Republic until 1976.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
