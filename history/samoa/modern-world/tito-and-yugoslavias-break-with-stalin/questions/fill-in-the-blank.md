# Tito and Yugoslavias Break with Stalin — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Tito, the partisan hero who glued ____, Croats, Slovenes, Bosnians, Montenegrins, Albanians and Macedonians into Yugoslavia, insists in 1948 that each communist country follow its own path; Stalin expels him from the Cominform but cannot touch him — and when Tito dies in 1980 the federation's buried hatreds erupt, Croatia, Slovenia and Macedonia leaving in 1991 and Bosnia-Herzegovina in 1992.
