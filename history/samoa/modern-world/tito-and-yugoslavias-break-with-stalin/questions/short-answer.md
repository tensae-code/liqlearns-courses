# Tito and Yugoslavias Break with Stalin — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Tito, the partisan hero who glued Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Bosnians, Montenegrins, Albanians and Macedonians into Yugoslavia, insists in 1948 that each communist country follow its own path; Stalin expels him from the Cominform but cannot touch him — and when Tito dies in 1980 the federation's buried hatreds erupt, Croatia, Slovenia and Macedonia leaving in 1991 and Bosnia-Herzegovina in 1992.
