# Tito and Yugoslavias Break with Stalin — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tito, the partisan hero who glued Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Bosnians, Montenegrins, Albanians and Macedonians into Yugoslavia, insists in 1948 that each communist country follow its own path; Stalin expels him from the Cominform but cannot touch him — and when Tito dies in 1980 the federation's buried hatreds erupt, Croatia, Slovenia and Macedonia leaving in 1991 and Bosnia-Herzegovina in 1992.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
