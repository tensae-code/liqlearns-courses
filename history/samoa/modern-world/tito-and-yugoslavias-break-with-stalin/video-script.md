# Video script — Tito and Yugoslavias Break with Stalin

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Tito, the partisan hero who glued Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Bosnians, Montenegrins, Albanians and Macedonians into Yugoslavia, insists in 1948 that each communist country follow its own path; Stalin expels him from the Cominform but cannot touch him — and when Tito dies in 1980 the federation's buried hatreds erupt, Croatia, Slovenia and Macedonia leaving in 1991 and Bosnia-Herzegovina in 1992.

Now the part called "Part 2, Unit 2". Tito, the partisan hero who glued Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Bosnians, Montenegrins, Albanians and Macedonians into Yugoslavia, insists in 1948 that each communist country follow its own path; Stalin expels him from the Cominform but cannot touch him — and when Tito dies in 1980 the federation's buried hatreds erupt, Croatia, Slovenia and Macedonia leaving in 1991 and Bosnia-Herzegovina in 1992.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
