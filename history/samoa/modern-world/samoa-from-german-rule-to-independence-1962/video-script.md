# Video script — Samoa from German Rule to Independence 1962

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Britain asks New Zealand to seize German Samoa at the start of the First World War; troops land unresisted at Apia in August 1914; Samoa passes through League of Nations mandate (1919) and UN trusteeship (1945), with New Zealand charged to prepare it for self-government, until in 1962 Samoa becomes the first Pacific island country to win independence; learners return to the Year 10 Book 1 history timeline (printed pp. 46–47 of that book) and choose which events on it — takeover, influenza, Mau, independence — did the work of forming Samoa's national identity; when the First World War begins, New Zealand takes Samoa from Germany and tears German men from their Samoan wives and families, imprisoning them until the war ends.

Now the part called "Unit 1, Topic 1 "Government Development And Organisation"". Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
