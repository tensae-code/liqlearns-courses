# Samoa from German Rule to Independence 1962 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain asks New Zealand to seize German Samoa at the start of the First World War; troops land unresisted at Apia in August 1914; Samoa passes through League of Nations mandate (1919) and UN trusteeship (1945), with New Zealand charged to prepare it for self-government, until in 1962 Samoa becomes the first Pacific island country to win independence. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Activity 6 "Samoa's National Identity"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
