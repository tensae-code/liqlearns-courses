# Samoa from German Rule to Independence 1962 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Topic 1 "Government Development And Organisation"" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Britain asks New Zealand to seize German Samoa at the start of the First World War; troops land unresisted at Apia in August 1914; Samoa passes through League of Nations mandate (1919) and UN trusteeship (1945), with New Zealand charged to prepare it for self-government, until in 1962 Samoa becomes the first Pacific island country to win independence
**Answer: d) Britain asks New Zealand to seize German Samoa at the start of the First World War; troops land unresisted at Apia in August 1914; Samoa passes through League of Nations mandate (1919) and UN trusteeship (1945), with New Zealand charged to prepare it for self-government, until in 1962 Samoa becomes the first Pacific island country to win independence** — That is what this lesson teaches.
