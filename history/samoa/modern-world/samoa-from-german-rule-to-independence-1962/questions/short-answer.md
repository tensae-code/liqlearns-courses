# Samoa from German Rule to Independence 1962 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, Topic 1 "Government Development And Organisation"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain asks New Zealand to seize German Samoa at the start of the First World War; troops land unresisted at Apia in August 1914; Samoa passes through League of Nations mandate (1919) and UN trusteeship (1945), with New Zealand charged to prepare it for self-government, until in 1962 Samoa becomes the first Pacific island country to win independence.

**2.** What came of "Unit 1, Activity 6 "Samoa's National Identity"" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Learners return to the Year 10 Book 1 history timeline (printed pp. 46–47 of that book) and choose which events on it — takeover, influenza, Mau, independence — did the work of forming Samoa's national identity.
