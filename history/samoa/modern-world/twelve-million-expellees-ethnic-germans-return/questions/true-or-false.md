# Twelve Million Expellees Ethnic Germans Return — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Between 1945 and 1949 nearly twelve million German refugees and expellees stream into the shrunken Germany — families from lands handed to Poland and from German-speaking communities planted centuries before in Czechoslovakia, Hungary, Yugoslavia, Russia, Bulgaria and Romania.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 1, post-war immigration" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
