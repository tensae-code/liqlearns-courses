# Zionism and the Jewish Return to Palestine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pogroms in Russia in the early 1880s found Zionism (1882) — a Jewish nation state in the promised land; waves of Jews migrate to Palestine and build communal kibbutzim, while the Arabs who took the land in during the centuries of exile call it their homeland too.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
