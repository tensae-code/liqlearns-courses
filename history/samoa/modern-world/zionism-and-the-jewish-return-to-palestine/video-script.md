# Video script — Zionism and the Jewish Return to Palestine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. Pogroms in Russia in the early 1880s found Zionism (1882) — a Jewish nation state in the promised land; waves of Jews migrate to Palestine and build communal kibbutzim, while the Arabs who took the land in during the centuries of exile call it their homeland too.
