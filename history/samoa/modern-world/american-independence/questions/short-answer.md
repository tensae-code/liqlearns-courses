# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, Case Study 2 "The Struggle For Independence"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Thirteen British colonies, taxed without a voice in London's parliament, declare independence in 1776, win a seven-year war, and write their own constitution in 1787.

**2.** What came of "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Two "Government in the USA"" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Thirteen British colonies, heavily taxed and governing themselves in part, unite for strength, sign the Declaration of Independence in 1776, defeat Britain by 1783, and write a constitution that splits power between federal government and states.
