# American Independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Thirteen ____ colonies, taxed without a voice in London's parliament, declare independence in 1776, win a seven-year war, and write their own constitution in 1787. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** British

**2.** Thirteen ____ colonies, heavily taxed and governing themselves in part, unite for strength, sign the Declaration of Independence in 1776, defeat Britain by 1783, and write a constitution that splits power between federal government and states. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** British
