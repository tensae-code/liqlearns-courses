# American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Thirteen British colonies, taxed without a voice in London's parliament, declare independence in 1776, win a seven-year war, and write their own constitution in 1787. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Two "Government in the USA"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
