# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 2, Case Study 2 "The Struggle For Independence"" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Thirteen British colonies, taxed without a voice in London's parliament, declare independence in 1776, win a seven-year war, and write their own constitution in 1787
**Answer: d) Thirteen British colonies, taxed without a voice in London's parliament, declare independence in 1776, win a seven-year war, and write their own constitution in 1787** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Two "Government in the USA""? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Thirteen British colonies, heavily taxed and governing themselves in part, unite for strength, sign the Declaration of Independence in 1776, defeat Britain by 1783, and write a constitution that splits power between federal government and states  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Thirteen British colonies, heavily taxed and governing themselves in part, unite for strength, sign the Declaration of Independence in 1776, defeat Britain by 1783, and write a constitution that splits power between federal government and states** — That is what this lesson teaches.
