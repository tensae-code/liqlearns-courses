# Satyagraha the Rules of Nonviolent Resistance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 3" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Gandhi writes the civil resister's code — harbour no anger, suffer assault without retaliating, submit willingly to arrest, never insult the opponent, refuse the Union Jack's salute, and shield even an official from attack at the risk of your own life.
