# Satyagraha the Rules of Nonviolent Resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 3, Unit 3" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Gandhi writes the civil resister's code — harbour no anger, suffer assault without retaliating, submit willingly to arrest, never insult the opponent, refuse the Union Jack's salute, and shield even an official from attack at the risk of your own life
**Answer: d) Gandhi writes the civil resister's code — harbour no anger, suffer assault without retaliating, submit willingly to arrest, never insult the opponent, refuse the Union Jack's salute, and shield even an official from attack at the risk of your own life** — That is what this lesson teaches.
