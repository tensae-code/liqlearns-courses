# Satyagraha the Rules of Nonviolent Resistance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gandhi writes the civil resister's code — harbour no anger, suffer assault without retaliating, submit willingly to arrest, never insult the opponent, refuse the Union Jack's salute, and shield even an official from attack at the risk of your own life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
