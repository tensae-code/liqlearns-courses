# European Contact Transforms Samoa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Consuls arrive in the 1840s, settlers build their own courts and administrations, rival ____ governments fight for a kingship the foreigners feed with guns — and customary land is sold to pay for the weapons; Apia grows into a port town of hotels and shops while Godeffroy's plantations export copra to Europe.
**Answer:** Malietoa
