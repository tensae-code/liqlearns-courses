# European Contact Transforms Samoa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Consuls arrive in the 1840s, settlers build their own courts and administrations, rival Malietoa governments fight for a kingship the foreigners feed with guns — and customary land is sold to pay for the weapons; Apia grows into a port town of hotels and shops while Godeffroy's plantations export copra to Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 4, political and economic change" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
