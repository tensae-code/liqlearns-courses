# The Geneva Accords and Divided Vietnam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dien Bien Phu falls; at Geneva the great powers and both Vietnams divide the country — Ho Chi Minh's communist Democratic Republic in the north, Bao Dai's state in the south, France gone for good — while American advisers, arriving from 1950 under the "domino theory", are already choosing the south's side.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
