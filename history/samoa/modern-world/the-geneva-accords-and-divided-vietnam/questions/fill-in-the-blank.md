# The Geneva Accords and Divided Vietnam — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Dien ____ Phu falls; at Geneva the great powers and both Vietnams divide the country — Ho Chi Minh's communist Democratic Republic in the north, Bao Dai's state in the south, France gone for good — while American advisers, arriving from 1950 under the "domino theory", are already choosing the south's side.
