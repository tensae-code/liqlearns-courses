# Video script — The Geneva Accords and Divided Vietnam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Dien Bien Phu falls; at Geneva the great powers and both Vietnams divide the country — Ho Chi Minh's communist Democratic Republic in the north, Bao Dai's state in the south, France gone for good — while American advisers, arriving from 1950 under the "domino theory", are already choosing the south's side.
