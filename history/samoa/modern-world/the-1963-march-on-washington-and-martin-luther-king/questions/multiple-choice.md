# The 1963 March on Washington and Martin Luther King — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) One of the largest protest marches ever fills Washington D.C. and ends with Martin Luther King Jr.'s speech; churches plan and prepare the movement's marches
**Answer: d) One of the largest protest marches ever fills Washington D.C. and ends with Martin Luther King Jr.'s speech; churches plan and prepare the movement's marches** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News""?
a) One of the largest protest marches ever fills Washington D.C. and ends with Martin Luther King Jr.'s speech; churches plan and prepare the movement's marches  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) One of the largest protest marches ever fills Washington D.C. and ends with Martin Luther King Jr.'s speech; churches plan and prepare the movement's marches** — That is what this lesson teaches.
