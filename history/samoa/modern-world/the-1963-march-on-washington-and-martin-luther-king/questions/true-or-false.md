# The 1963 March on Washington and Martin Luther King — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** One of the largest protest marches ever fills Washington D.C. and ends with Martin Luther King Jr.'s speech; churches plan and prepare the movement's marches.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
