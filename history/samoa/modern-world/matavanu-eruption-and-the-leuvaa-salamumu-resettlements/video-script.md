# Video script — Matavanu Eruption and the Le'auva'a–Salamumu Resettlements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Matavanu erupts on Savai'i in 1905 and buries Salaeula and Lealatele; German administrators, Catholic priests and Methodist pastors move the homeless to Upolu, founding Le'auva'a (1908) and Salamumu (1909) — villages that still vote in Savai'i constituencies today.
