# Matavanu Eruption and the Le'auva'a–Salamumu Resettlements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Matavanu erupts on Savai'i in 1905 and buries Salaeula and Lealatele; German administrators, Catholic priests and Methodist pastors move the homeless to Upolu, founding Le'auva'a (1908) and Salamumu (1909) — villages that still vote in Savai'i constituencies today.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 2, natural disasters" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
