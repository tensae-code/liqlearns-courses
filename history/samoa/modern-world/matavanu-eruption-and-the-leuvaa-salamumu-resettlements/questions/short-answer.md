# Matavanu Eruption and the Le'auva'a–Salamumu Resettlements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 2, natural disasters" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Matavanu erupts on Savai'i in 1905 and buries Salaeula and Lealatele; German administrators, Catholic priests and Methodist pastors move the homeless to Upolu, founding Le'auva'a (1908) and Salamumu (1909) — villages that still vote in Savai'i constituencies today.
