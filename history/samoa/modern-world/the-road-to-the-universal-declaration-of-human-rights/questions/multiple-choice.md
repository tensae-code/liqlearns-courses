Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 2, "Important treaties and declarations"" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) A chain of documents limits power step by step — Magna Carta (1215), the Bill of Rights (1689), the American Declaration of Independence (1776) and France's Declaration of the Rights of Man and the Citizen (1789)
