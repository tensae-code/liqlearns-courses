# The Road to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A chain of documents limits power step by step — Magna Carta (1215), the Bill of Rights (1689), the American Declaration of Independence (1776) and France's Declaration of the Rights of Man and the Citizen (1789). (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, "The Universal Declaration of Human Rights"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
