# The Road to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A chain of documents limits power step by step — ____ Carta (1215), the Bill of Rights (1689), the American Declaration of Independence (1776) and France's Declaration of the Rights of Man and the Citizen (1789). (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Magna

**2.** After the ____ World War reveals the Nazi murder of six million Jews, the new United Nations adopts the Universal Declaration on 10 December 1948 — thirty articles, a common standard but not a binding law. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Second
