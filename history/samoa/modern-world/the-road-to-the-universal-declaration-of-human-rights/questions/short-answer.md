# The Road to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, "Important treaties and declarations"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A chain of documents limits power step by step — Magna Carta (1215), the Bill of Rights (1689), the American Declaration of Independence (1776) and France's Declaration of the Rights of Man and the Citizen (1789).

**2.** What came of "Unit 2, "The Universal Declaration of Human Rights"" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** After the Second World War reveals the Nazi murder of six million Jews, the new United Nations adopts the Universal Declaration on 10 December 1948 — thirty articles, a common standard but not a binding law.
