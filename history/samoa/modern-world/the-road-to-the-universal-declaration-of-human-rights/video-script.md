# Video script — The Road to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. A chain of documents limits power step by step — Magna Carta (1215), the Bill of Rights (1689), the American Declaration of Independence (1776) and France's Declaration of the Rights of Man and the Citizen (1789); after the Second World War reveals the Nazi murder of six million Jews, the new United Nations adopts the Universal Declaration on 10 December 1948 — thirty articles, a common standard but not a binding law.
