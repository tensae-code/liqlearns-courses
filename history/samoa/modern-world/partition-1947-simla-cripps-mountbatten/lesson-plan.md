**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Partition 1947 Simla Cripps Mountbatten

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Simla (1945) deadlocks on who speaks for Muslims; Cripps (1946) calls a two-piece Pakistan ungovernable; Jinnah answers with Direct Action Day, 16 August 1946 — 5,000 dead in Calcutta in four days; Attlee sends Mountbatten, the last viceroy, who warns Gandhi and Nehru to accept partition or face civil war — independence comes on 15 August 1947, and Punjab burns anyway. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
