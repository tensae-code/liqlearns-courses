# Partition 1947 Simla Cripps Mountbatten — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 4" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Simla (1945) deadlocks on who speaks for Muslims; Cripps (1946) calls a two-piece Pakistan ungovernable; Jinnah answers with Direct Action Day, 16 August 1946 — 5,000 dead in Calcutta in four days; Attlee sends Mountbatten, the last viceroy, who warns Gandhi and Nehru to accept partition or face civil war — independence comes on 15 August 1947, and Punjab burns anyway.
