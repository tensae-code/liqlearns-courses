# Partition 1947 Simla Cripps Mountbatten — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Simla (1945) deadlocks on who speaks for Muslims; Cripps (1946) calls a two-piece Pakistan ungovernable; Jinnah answers with Direct Action Day, 16 August 1946 — 5,000 dead in Calcutta in four days; Attlee sends Mountbatten, the last viceroy, who warns Gandhi and Nehru to accept partition or face civil war — independence comes on 15 August 1947, and Punjab burns anyway.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
