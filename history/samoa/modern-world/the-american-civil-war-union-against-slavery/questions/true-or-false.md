# The American Civil War Union against Slavery — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** War breaks out between the northern free states and the southern slave-owning states (also named the War of Secession, the Rebellion, the War for Southern Independence); the North wins, slavery is abolished, Lincoln is assassinated, and more Americans die than in any other war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Three "The American Civil War"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
