# The American Civil War Union against Slavery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Three "The American Civil War"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) War breaks out between the northern free states and the southern slave-owning states (also named the War of Secession, the Rebellion, the War for Southern Independence); the North wins, slavery is abolished, Lincoln is assassinated, and more Americans die than in any other war
**Answer: d) War breaks out between the northern free states and the southern slave-owning states (also named the War of Secession, the Rebellion, the War for Southern Independence); the North wins, slavery is abolished, Lincoln is assassinated, and more Americans die than in any other war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
