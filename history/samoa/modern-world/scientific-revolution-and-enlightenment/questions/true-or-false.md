# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Copernicus hides his sun-centred discovery until his deathbed; Giordano Bruno teaches it openly, refuses to recant, and is burnt at the stake in Italy in 1600.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Case Study 1 "New Thoughts And Ideas"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
