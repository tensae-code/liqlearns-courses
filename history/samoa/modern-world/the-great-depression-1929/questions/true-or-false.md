# The Great Depression 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The "era of hope" (Locarno 1925, Kellogg-Briand 1928, the Young Plan) rests on American loans and German reparations; in October 1929 Wall Street crashes, banks call in loans and collapse across Europe, and spending, production and jobs spiral down worldwide. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
