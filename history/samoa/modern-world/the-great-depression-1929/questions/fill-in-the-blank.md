# The Great Depression 1929 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The "era of hope" (____ 1925, Kellogg-Briand 1928, the Young Plan) rests on American loans and German reparations; in October 1929 Wall Street crashes, banks call in loans and collapse across Europe, and spending, production and jobs spiral down worldwide. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Locarno

**2.** The 1929 ____ crash cuts world trade by some 70 percent; banks and companies fail, unemployment soars, and in Germany and Japan desperate voters and soldiers begin listening to anyone who promises a way out. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** American
