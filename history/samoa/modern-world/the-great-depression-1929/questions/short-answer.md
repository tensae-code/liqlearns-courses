# The Great Depression 1929 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 5, Unit 5, first half" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The "era of hope" (Locarno 1925, Kellogg-Briand 1928, the Young Plan) rests on American loans and German reparations; in October 1929 Wall Street crashes, banks call in loans and collapse across Europe, and spending, production and jobs spiral down worldwide.

**2.** What came of "Part 4, Unit 2" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1929 American crash cuts world trade by some 70 percent; banks and companies fail, unemployment soars, and in Germany and Japan desperate voters and soldiers begin listening to anyone who promises a way out.
