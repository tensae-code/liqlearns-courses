# Lesson plan — The Great Depression 1929

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Great Depression 1929
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The "era of hope" (Locarno 1925, Kellogg-Briand 1928, the Young Plan) rests on American loans and German reparations; in October 1929 Wall Street crashes, banks call in loans and collapse across Europe, and spending, production and jobs spiral down worldwide; the 1929 American crash cuts world trade by some 70 percent; banks and companies fail, unemployment soars, and in Germany and Japan desperate voters and soldiers begin listening to anyone who promises a way out.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Samoa's books themselves work, telling the past as stories of people, places and turns.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: The "era of hope" (Locarno 1925, Kellogg-Briand 1928, the Young Plan) rests on American loans and German reparations; in October 1929 Wall Street crashes, banks call in loans and collapse across Europe, and spending, production and jobs spiral down worldwide; the 1929 American crash cuts world trade by some 70 percent; banks and companies fail, unemployment soars, and in Germany and Japan desperate voters and soldiers begin listening to anyone who promises a way out. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
