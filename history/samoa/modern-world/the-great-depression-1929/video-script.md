# Video script — The Great Depression 1929

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The "era of hope" (Locarno 1925, Kellogg-Briand 1928, the Young Plan) rests on American loans and German reparations; in October 1929 Wall Street crashes, banks call in loans and collapse across Europe, and spending, production and jobs spiral down worldwide; the 1929 American crash cuts world trade by some 70 percent; banks and companies fail, unemployment soars, and in Germany and Japan desperate voters and soldiers begin listening to anyone who promises a way out.

Now the part called "Part 4, Unit 2". the 1929 American crash cuts world trade by some 70 percent; banks and companies fail, unemployment soars, and in Germany and Japan desperate voters and soldiers begin listening to anyone who promises a way out.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
