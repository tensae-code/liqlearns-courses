# Stalin Seizes Power: The Testament and Trotsky — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Lenin's Testament warns that Stalin, General Secretary since 1922, holds "enormous power" he may not use with caution — Stalin buries the warning, builds a cult of Leninism with himself as heir, floods the party with 240,000 "Lenin Enrolment" recruits who owe him everything, purges the old guard, and outmanoeuvres Trotsky, the revolution's most able man.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
