# Video script — Stalin Seizes Power: The Testament and Trotsky

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Lenin's Testament warns that Stalin, General Secretary since 1922, holds "enormous power" he may not use with caution — Stalin buries the warning, builds a cult of Leninism with himself as heir, floods the party with 240,000 "Lenin Enrolment" recruits who owe him everything, purges the old guard, and outmanoeuvres Trotsky, the revolution's most able man.
