# Jim Crow Segregation in the American South — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Four "The 'Jim Crow' Laws, 1880s–1970"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Over thirty years (1890–1920) the southern states rebuild racial separation by law — railways, buses, schools, hospitals, parks all segregated under "White" and "Colored" signs, stripping back the rights black people won after the Civil War
**Answer: d) Over thirty years (1890–1920) the southern states rebuild racial separation by law — railways, buses, schools, hospitals, parks all segregated under "White" and "Colored" signs, stripping back the rights black people won after the Civil War** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Four "The 'Jim Crow' Laws, 1880s–1970""?
a) Over thirty years (1890–1920) the southern states rebuild racial separation by law — railways, buses, schools, hospitals, parks all segregated under "White" and "Colored" signs, stripping back the rights black people won after the Civil War  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Over thirty years (1890–1920) the southern states rebuild racial separation by law — railways, buses, schools, hospitals, parks all segregated under "White" and "Colored" signs, stripping back the rights black people won after the Civil War** — That is what this lesson teaches.
