# Jim Crow Segregation in the American South — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Over thirty years (1890–1920) the southern states rebuild racial separation by law — railways, buses, schools, hospitals, parks all segregated under "White" and "Colored" signs, stripping back the rights black people won after the Civil War.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Four "The 'Jim Crow' Laws, 1880s–1970"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
