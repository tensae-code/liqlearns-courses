# Jim Crow Segregation in the American South — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: over thirty years (1890–1920) the southern states rebuild racial separation by law — railways, buses, schools, hospitals, parks all segregated under "White" and "Colored" signs, stripping back the rights black people won after the Civil War — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
