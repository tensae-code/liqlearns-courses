# Video script — Jim Crow Segregation in the American South

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Over thirty years (1890–1920) the southern states rebuild racial separation by law — railways, buses, schools, hospitals, parks all segregated under "White" and "Colored" signs, stripping back the rights black people won after the Civil War.
