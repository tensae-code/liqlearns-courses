# The Industrial Revolution Textiles Steam and Rail — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Charcoal gives way to coal, an improved steam engine arrives in 1782, the steam-powered loom in 1785, and by 1835 more than 120,000 power looms are weaving.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Topic 1 inventions timeline" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
