# Australia's Stolen Generation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From 1910 to 1970 governments forcibly remove thousands of Aboriginal children to church- and state-run homes to erase their language and culture; Tjalaminu Mia, taken at ten in 1962 with her six siblings and separated from them at Sister Kate's home, calls it a terrible crime against her people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Case Study 1 "The Stolen Generation" (story told as case-study summary)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
