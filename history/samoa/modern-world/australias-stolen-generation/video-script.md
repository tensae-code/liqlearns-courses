# Video script — Australia's Stolen Generation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. From 1910 to 1970 governments forcibly remove thousands of Aboriginal children to church- and state-run homes to erase their language and culture; Tjalaminu Mia, taken at ten in 1962 with her six siblings and separated from them at Sister Kate's home, calls it a terrible crime against her people.
