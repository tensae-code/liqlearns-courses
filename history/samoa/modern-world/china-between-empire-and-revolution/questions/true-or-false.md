# China between Empire and Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Since the 1850s European powers and Japan force trade on China, run its mines, railways and tariffs, and post "Chinese and dogs forbidden" in Shanghai parks; the Opium Wars begin the humiliation Sun Yat Sen sums up — "we are the fish and the meat", the world the carving knife.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
