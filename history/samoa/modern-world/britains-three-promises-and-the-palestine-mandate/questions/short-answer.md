# Britain's Three Promises and the Palestine Mandate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 3" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Fighting the Turks, Britain promises Arab independence to Hussein of Mecca, secretly carves the Ottoman lands with France (Sykes-Picot, 1916), and promises the Jews a national home (Balfour Declaration); the mandates leave both peoples distrusting Britain, the 1930 and 1939 White Papers swing between partition and closing immigration as the Holocaust drives Jews to flee.
