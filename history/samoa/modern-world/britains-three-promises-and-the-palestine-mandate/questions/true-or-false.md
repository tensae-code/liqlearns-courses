# Britain's Three Promises and the Palestine Mandate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fighting the Turks, Britain promises Arab independence to Hussein of Mecca, secretly carves the Ottoman lands with France (Sykes-Picot, 1916), and promises the Jews a national home (Balfour Declaration); the mandates leave both peoples distrusting Britain, the 1930 and 1939 White Papers swing between partition and closing immigration as the Holocaust drives Jews to flee.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
