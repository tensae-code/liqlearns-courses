# Video script — Britain's Three Promises and the Palestine Mandate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Fighting the Turks, Britain promises Arab independence to Hussein of Mecca, secretly carves the Ottoman lands with France (Sykes-Picot, 1916), and promises the Jews a national home (Balfour Declaration); the mandates leave both peoples distrusting Britain, the 1930 and 1939 White Papers swing between partition and closing immigration as the Holocaust drives Jews to flee.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
