# The Amritsar Massacre 1919 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 1" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Protesting the Rowlatt Acts, thousands crowd into Jallianwala Bagh, a walled park with one narrow exit; General Dyer's troops fire until the official count stands at 379 dead and 1,200 injured — the Hunter Commission retires Dyer, but the massacre turns a generation of Indians, and Gandhi's movement, against the Raj for good.
