# The Amritsar Massacre 1919 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 3, Unit 1" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Protesting the Rowlatt Acts, thousands crowd into Jallianwala Bagh, a walled park with one narrow exit; General Dyer's troops fire until the official count stands at 379 dead and 1,200 injured — the Hunter Commission retires Dyer, but the massacre turns a generation of Indians, and Gandhi's movement, against the Raj for good
**Answer: d) Protesting the Rowlatt Acts, thousands crowd into Jallianwala Bagh, a walled park with one narrow exit; General Dyer's troops fire until the official count stands at 379 dead and 1,200 injured — the Hunter Commission retires Dyer, but the massacre turns a generation of Indians, and Gandhi's movement, against the Raj for good** — That is what this lesson teaches.
