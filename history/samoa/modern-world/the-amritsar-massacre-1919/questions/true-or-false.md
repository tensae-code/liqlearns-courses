# The Amritsar Massacre 1919 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Protesting the Rowlatt Acts, thousands crowd into Jallianwala Bagh, a walled park with one narrow exit; General Dyer's troops fire until the official count stands at 379 dead and 1,200 injured — the Hunter Commission retires Dyer, but the massacre turns a generation of Indians, and Gandhi's movement, against the Raj for good.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
