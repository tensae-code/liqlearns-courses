# The Sepoy Rebellion 1857 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The annexation of wealthy Oudh (1856) and Enfield cartridges rumoured greased with cow and pig fat — an offence to Hindu and Muslim alike — ignite the sepoys at Meerut on 10 May 1857; the rebels march on Delhi and hold north and central India for a year before superior British guns prevail — Indian historians name it the first war of independence.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
