# The Sepoy Rebellion 1857 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 1" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** The annexation of wealthy Oudh (1856) and Enfield cartridges rumoured greased with cow and pig fat — an offence to Hindu and Muslim alike — ignite the sepoys at Meerut on 10 May 1857; the rebels march on Delhi and hold north and central India for a year before superior British guns prevail — Indian historians name it the first war of independence.
