# Militarism in Japan the Army Above the State — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Depression guts ____ silk exports and jobs; militarists sell Manchuria as the answer — resources, land, markets; troops stage the Mukden explosion, seize Manchuria by 1932 as "Manchukuo", ignore the Lytton Commission, quit the League, and by 1937 Japan is at full war with China. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Japan's

**2.** Depression halves ____ silk prices and shuts half its factories by 1931; the army answers with empire — a staged explosion at Mukden excuses the seizure of Manchuria, the League's Lytton report names Japan the aggressor but cannot act, Japan quits the League in 1933 and invades all China by 1937. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Japan's
