# Militarism in Japan the Army Above the State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 5, Unit 5, Japan" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Depression guts Japan's silk exports and jobs; militarists sell Manchuria as the answer — resources, land, markets; troops stage the Mukden explosion, seize Manchuria by 1932 as "Manchukuo", ignore the Lytton Commission, quit the League, and by 1937 Japan is at full war with China.

**2.** What came of "Part 4, Unit 2, Japan" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Depression halves Japan's silk prices and shuts half its factories by 1931; the army answers with empire — a staged explosion at Mukden excuses the seizure of Manchuria, the League's Lytton report names Japan the aggressor but cannot act, Japan quits the League in 1933 and invades all China by 1937.
