# Militarism in Japan the Army Above the State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Depression guts Japan's silk exports and jobs; militarists sell Manchuria as the answer — resources, land, markets; troops stage the Mukden explosion, seize Manchuria by 1932 as "Manchukuo", ignore the Lytton Commission, quit the League, and by 1937 Japan is at full war with China. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 2, Japan" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
