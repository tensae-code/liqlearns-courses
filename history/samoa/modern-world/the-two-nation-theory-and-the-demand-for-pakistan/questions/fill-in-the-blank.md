# The Two Nation Theory and the Demand for Pakistan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In 1930 the ____ first demands a separate Muslim state, its name assembled from Punjab, Afghan Frontier, Kashmir and Baluchistan; at Lahore in 1940 Jinnah declares Hindus and Muslims two nations with different epics, heroes and laws — and in 1946 the League wins 425 of 496 Muslim seats on that single promise.
