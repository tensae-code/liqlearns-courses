# The Two Nation Theory and the Demand for Pakistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1930 the League first demands a separate Muslim state, its name assembled from Punjab, Afghan Frontier, Kashmir and Baluchistan; at Lahore in 1940 Jinnah declares Hindus and Muslims two nations with different epics, heroes and laws — and in 1946 the League wins 425 of 496 Muslim seats on that single promise.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
