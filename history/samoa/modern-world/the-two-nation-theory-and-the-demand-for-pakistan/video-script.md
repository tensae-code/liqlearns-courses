# Video script — The Two Nation Theory and the Demand for Pakistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In 1930 the League first demands a separate Muslim state, its name assembled from Punjab, Afghan Frontier, Kashmir and Baluchistan; at Lahore in 1940 Jinnah declares Hindus and Muslims two nations with different epics, heroes and laws — and in 1946 the League wins 425 of 496 Muslim seats on that single promise.

Now the part called "Part 3, Unit 3". in 1930 the League first demands a separate Muslim state, its name assembled from Punjab, Afghan Frontier, Kashmir and Baluchistan; at Lahore in 1940 Jinnah declares Hindus and Muslims two nations with different epics, heroes and laws — and in 1946 the League wins 425 of 496 Muslim seats on that single promise.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
