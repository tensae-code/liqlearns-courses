# Indian Indentured Labour in Fiji — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: sugar planters need hands Fijians will not sell cheap, so 60,537 Indians are recruited as indentured labourers between 1879 and 1916 — five-year contracts, 4 a.m. starts, windowless line rooms; when the system stops, many stay and build Fiji lives, until the 1987 coups push many to emigrate again — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
