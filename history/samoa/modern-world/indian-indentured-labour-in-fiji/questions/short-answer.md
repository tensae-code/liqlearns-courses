# Indian Indentured Labour in Fiji — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, "Indian Fijians — A General Settlement Description"" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Sugar planters need hands Fijians will not sell cheap, so 60,537 Indians are recruited as indentured labourers between 1879 and 1916 — five-year contracts, 4 a.m. starts, windowless line rooms; when the system stops, many stay and build Fiji lives, until the 1987 coups push many to emigrate again.
