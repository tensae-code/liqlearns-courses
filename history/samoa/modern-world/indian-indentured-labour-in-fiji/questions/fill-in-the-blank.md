# Indian Indentured Labour in Fiji — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Sugar planters need hands ____ will not sell cheap, so 60,537 Indians are recruited as indentured labourers between 1879 and 1916 — five-year contracts, 4 a.m. starts, windowless line rooms; when the system stops, many stay and build Fiji lives, until the 1987 coups push many to emigrate again.
**Answer:** Fijians
