# Indian Indentured Labour in Fiji — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sugar planters need hands Fijians will not sell cheap, so 60,537 Indians are recruited as indentured labourers between 1879 and 1916 — five-year contracts, 4 a.m. starts, windowless line rooms; when the system stops, many stay and build Fiji lives, until the 1987 coups push many to emigrate again.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, "Indian Fijians — A General Settlement Description"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
