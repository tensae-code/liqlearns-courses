# Video script — The Hungarian Uprising 1956

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Hungarians demand free elections and the right to strike, tear Stalin's star from their flag and burn his portraits; the Soviets withdraw, think again, and on November 1956 send 2,500 tanks into Budapest — the West admires and does nothing, fearing a third world war, while 200,000 Hungarians flee to Austria and at least 2,000 are executed; freedom waits until 1990.

Now the part called "Part 2, Unit 2". Hungarians demand free elections and the right to strike, tear Stalin's star from their flag and burn his portraits; the Soviets withdraw, think again, and on November 1956 send 2,500 tanks into Budapest — the West admires and does nothing, fearing a third world war, while 200,000 Hungarians flee to Austria and at least 2,000 are executed; freedom waits until 1990.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
