# The Hungarian Uprising 1956 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Hungarians demand free elections and the right to strike, tear Stalin's star from their flag and burn his portraits; the Soviets withdraw, think again, and on November 1956 send 2,500 tanks into Budapest — the West admires and does nothing, fearing a third world war, while 200,000 Hungarians flee to Austria and at least 2,000 are executed; freedom waits until 1990.
