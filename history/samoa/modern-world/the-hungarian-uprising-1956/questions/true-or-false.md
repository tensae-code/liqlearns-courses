# The Hungarian Uprising 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hungarians demand free elections and the right to strike, tear Stalin's star from their flag and burn his portraits; the Soviets withdraw, think again, and on November 1956 send 2,500 tanks into Budapest — the West admires and does nothing, fearing a third world war, while 200,000 Hungarians flee to Austria and at least 2,000 are executed; freedom waits until 1990.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
