# The Hungarian Uprising 1956 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Hungarians demand free elections and the right to strike, tear ____ star from their flag and burn his portraits; the Soviets withdraw, think again, and on November 1956 send 2,500 tanks into Budapest — the West admires and does nothing, fearing a third world war, while 200,000 Hungarians flee to Austria and at least 2,000 are executed; freedom waits until 1990.
**Answer:** Stalin's
