# Video script — New Zealand a Nation of Migrants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Maori arrive by canoe from Polynesia around 800 AD and their arrival stories pass down orally; from the 1860s some 300,000 mostly British settlers chase cheap land, followed by Scandinavians, Germans, Indians, Samoan and Tongan workers in the 1960s–70s, refugees from Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, Fiji Indians after the 1987 coups, and Asian business migrants.

Now the part called "Unit 2, Topic 4 "New Zealand: A Nation Of Migrants"". Maori arrive by canoe from Polynesia around 800 AD and their arrival stories pass down orally; from the 1860s some 300,000 mostly British settlers chase cheap land, followed by Scandinavians, Germans, Indians, Samoan and Tongan workers in the 1960s–70s, refugees from Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, Fiji Indians after the 1987 coups, and Asian business migrants.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
