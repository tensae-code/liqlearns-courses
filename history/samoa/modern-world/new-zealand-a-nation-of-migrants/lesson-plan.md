# Lesson plan — New Zealand a Nation of Migrants

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** New Zealand a Nation of Migrants
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage, Places and the Voyaging Pacific (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Maori arrive by canoe from Polynesia around 800 AD and their arrival stories pass down orally; from the 1860s some 300,000 mostly British settlers chase cheap land, followed by Scandinavians, Germans, Indians, Samoan and Tongan workers in the 1960s–70s, refugees from Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, Fiji Indians after the 1987 coups, and Asian business migrants.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage, Places and the Voyaging Pacific — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Maori arrive by canoe from Polynesia around 800 AD and their arrival stories pass down orally; from the 1860s some 300,000 mostly British settlers chase cheap land, followed by Scandinavians, Germans, Indians, Samoan and Tongan workers in the 1960s–70s, refugees from Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, Fiji Indians after the 1987 coups, and Asian business migrants. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
