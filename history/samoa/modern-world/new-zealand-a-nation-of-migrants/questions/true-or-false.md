# New Zealand a Nation of Migrants — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Maori arrive by canoe from Polynesia around 800 AD and their arrival stories pass down orally; from the 1860s some 300,000 mostly British settlers chase cheap land, followed by Scandinavians, Germans, Indians, Samoan and Tongan workers in the 1960s–70s, refugees from Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, Fiji Indians after the 1987 coups, and Asian business migrants.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Topic 4 "New Zealand: A Nation Of Migrants"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
