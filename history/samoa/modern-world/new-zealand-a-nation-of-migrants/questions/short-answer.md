# New Zealand a Nation of Migrants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, Topic 4 "New Zealand: A Nation Of Migrants"" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Maori arrive by canoe from Polynesia around 800 AD and their arrival stories pass down orally; from the 1860s some 300,000 mostly British settlers chase cheap land, followed by Scandinavians, Germans, Indians, Samoan and Tongan workers in the 1960s–70s, refugees from Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, Fiji Indians after the 1987 coups, and Asian business migrants.
