# New Zealand a Nation of Migrants — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Maori arrive by canoe from ____ around 800 AD and their arrival stories pass down orally; from the 1860s some 300,000 mostly British settlers chase cheap land, followed by Scandinavians, Germans, Indians, Samoan and Tongan workers in the 1960s–70s, refugees from Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam, Fiji Indians after the 1987 coups, and Asian business migrants.
**Answer:** Polynesia
