# The United Nations Is Founded 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Determined the new body must outmuscle the League, Roosevelt names it, Dumbarton Oaks (1944) drafts it, and 51 states sign the Charter at San Francisco in 1945; six organs follow, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948 — and the book's own balance sheet notes 30 million war dead since, and US$2.9 billion in unpaid peacekeeping bills by May 2000.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 4" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
