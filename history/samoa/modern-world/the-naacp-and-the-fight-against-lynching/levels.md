# The NAACP and the Fight Against Lynching — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: founded in 1910 and led at first by seven white leaders and W. E. B. Du Bois, whose magazine The Crisis reports race news, the NAACP fights lynching (71 lynchings in 1911, stopped by the 1950s), runs boycotts and marches, and pays lawyers to challenge unjust laws in court — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
