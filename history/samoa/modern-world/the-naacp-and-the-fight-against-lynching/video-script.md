# Video script — The NAACP and the Fight Against Lynching

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Founded in 1910 and led at first by seven white leaders and W. E. B. Du Bois, whose magazine The Crisis reports race news, the NAACP fights lynching (71 lynchings in 1911, stopped by the 1950s), runs boycotts and marches, and pays lawyers to challenge unjust laws in court.
