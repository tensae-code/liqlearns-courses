# The NAACP and the Fight Against Lynching — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Five "The NAACP"" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Founded in 1910 and led at first by seven white leaders and W. E. B. Du Bois, whose magazine The Crisis reports race news, the NAACP fights lynching (71 lynchings in 1911, stopped by the 1950s), runs boycotts and marches, and pays lawyers to challenge unjust laws in court.
