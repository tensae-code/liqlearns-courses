# The NAACP and the Fight Against Lynching — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Founded in 1910 and led at first by seven white leaders and W. E. B. Du Bois, whose magazine The Crisis reports race news, the NAACP fights lynching (71 lynchings in 1911, stopped by the 1950s), runs boycotts and marches, and pays lawyers to challenge unjust laws in court.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Fact Sheet Five "The NAACP"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
