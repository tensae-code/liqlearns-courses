# Missionaries Reshape Samoan Family Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Missionaries ban polygamy and the polula night dances, push the nuclear family against the aiga, take the priestly role from the matai (the pastor inherits the chiefly address "susuga"), and bring both new diseases and the first modern health care — until the independent nation's motto reads "Sāmoa is founded on God".
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 4 and Part 2, Unit 3, social and cultural effects" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
