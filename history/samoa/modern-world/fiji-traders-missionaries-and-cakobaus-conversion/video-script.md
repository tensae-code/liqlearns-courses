This lesson is about one thing. Abel Tasman stumbles on Fiji in 1643 and Cook sails through in 1774; shipwrecked sailors and runaway Australian convicts are the first Europeans to live there; sandalwood traders and missionaries follow by the mid-1800s, and when Ratu Seru Cakobau accepts Christianity in 1854 the country follows and tribal warfare ends.
