# Fiji Traders Missionaries and Cakobaus Conversion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Abel Tasman stumbles on Fiji in 1643 and Cook sails through in 1774; shipwrecked sailors and runaway Australian convicts are the first Europeans to live there; sandalwood traders and missionaries follow by the mid-1800s, and when Ratu Seru Cakobau accepts Christianity in 1854 the country follows and tribal warfare ends.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, "Fijians — a General Settlement History"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
