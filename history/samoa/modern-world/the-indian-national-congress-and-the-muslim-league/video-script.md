# Video script — The Indian National Congress and the Muslim League

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Congress (founded 1885) grows from polite petitions to demanding self-government, but Muslims fear a Hindu-dominated free India — Hindi replaces Urdu as official language in 1900, the Muslim League forms under Jinnah, wins separate electorates in the Lucknow Pact (1916), then rejects the Nehru Report's quarter-share as too little: a "parting of the ways".
