# The Indian National Congress and the Muslim League — short answer

**Answer:** The Congress (founded 1885) grows from polite petitions to demanding self-government, but Muslims fear a Hindu-dominated free India — Hindi replaces Urdu as official language in 1900, the Muslim League forms under Jinnah, wins separate electorates in the Lucknow Pact (1916), then rejects the Nehru Report's quarter-share as too little: a "parting of the ways".
