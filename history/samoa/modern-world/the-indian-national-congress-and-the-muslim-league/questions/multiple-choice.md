# The Indian National Congress and the Muslim League — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 3, Units 2–3" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) The Congress (founded 1885) grows from polite petitions to demanding self-government, but Muslims fear a Hindu-dominated free India — Hindi replaces Urdu as official language in 1900, the Muslim League forms under Jinnah, wins separate electorates in the Lucknow Pact (1916), then rejects the Nehru Report's quarter-share as too little: a "parting of the ways"
**Answer: d) The Congress (founded 1885) grows from polite petitions to demanding self-government, but Muslims fear a Hindu-dominated free India — Hindi replaces Urdu as official language in 1900, the Muslim League forms under Jinnah, wins separate electorates in the Lucknow Pact (1916), then rejects the Nehru Report's quarter-share as too little: a "parting of the ways"** — That is what this lesson teaches.
