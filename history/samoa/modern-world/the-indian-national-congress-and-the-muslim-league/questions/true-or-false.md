# The Indian National Congress and the Muslim League — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Congress (founded 1885) grows from polite petitions to demanding self-government, but Muslims fear a Hindu-dominated free India — Hindi replaces Urdu as official language in 1900, the Muslim League forms under Jinnah, wins separate electorates in the Lucknow Pact (1916), then rejects the Nehru Report's quarter-share as too little: a "parting of the ways".
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Units 2–3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
