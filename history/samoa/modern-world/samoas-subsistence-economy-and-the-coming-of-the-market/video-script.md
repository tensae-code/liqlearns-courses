# Video script — Samoa's Subsistence Economy and the Coming of the Market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Pre-European Sāmoa produces through aiga and village, pays specialists in fine mats, and trades ceremonial treasures with Fiji and Tonga (red feathers, 'ie toga, whale teeth); beachcombers, missionaries and traders bring sugar, flour, iron and cloth, Sāmoans grow copra for cash, and the DHPG's plantations import indentured labour and turn Apia into a commercial centre.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
