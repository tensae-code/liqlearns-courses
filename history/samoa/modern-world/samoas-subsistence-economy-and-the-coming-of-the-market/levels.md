# Samoa's Subsistence Economy and the Coming of the Market — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: pre-European Sāmoa produces through aiga and village, pays specialists in fine mats, and trades ceremonial treasures with Fiji and Tonga (red feathers, 'ie toga, whale teeth); beachcombers, missionaries and traders bring sugar, flour, iron and cloth, Sāmoans grow copra for cash, and the DHPG's plantations import indentured labour and turn Apia into a commercial centre — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
