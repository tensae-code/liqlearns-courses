# Samoa's Subsistence Economy and the Coming of the Market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pre-European Sāmoa produces through aiga and village, pays specialists in fine mats, and trades ceremonial treasures with Fiji and Tonga (red feathers, 'ie toga, whale teeth); beachcombers, missionaries and traders bring sugar, flour, iron and cloth, Sāmoans grow copra for cash, and the DHPG's plantations import indentured labour and turn Apia into a commercial centre.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 5, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
