# Lesson plan — The First World War Causes

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The First World War Causes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The World Whole: Germany, India, Vietnam, the Cold War and the Men of Power (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks; bismarck weaves the Dreikaiserbund (1873), the Dual Alliance (1879), the Triple Alliance (1882) and the Reinsurance Treaty (1887) to keep France isolated; Wilhelm II dismisses him (1890), drops Russia, and chases Weltpolitik — France and Russia ally (1894), Britain settles with France (1904) and Russia (1907), the Moroccan crises (1905, Agadir 1911) humiliate Germany, and the Dreadnought race arms both camps.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The World Whole: Germany, India, Vietnam, the Cold War and the Men of Power — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: The Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks; bismarck weaves the Dreikaiserbund (1873), the Dual Alliance (1879), the Triple Alliance (1882) and the Reinsurance Treaty (1887) to keep France isolated; Wilhelm II dismisses him (1890), drops Russia, and chases Weltpolitik — France and Russia ally (1894), Britain settles with France (1904) and Russia (1907), the Moroccan crises (1905, Agadir 1911) humiliate Germany, and the Dreadnought race arms both camps. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
