# Video script — The First World War Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks; bismarck weaves the Dreikaiserbund (1873), the Dual Alliance (1879), the Triple Alliance (1882) and the Reinsurance Treaty (1887) to keep France isolated; Wilhelm II dismisses him (1890), drops Russia, and chases Weltpolitik — France and Russia ally (1894), Britain settles with France (1904) and Russia (1907), the Moroccan crises (1905, Agadir 1911) humiliate Germany, and the Dreadnought race arms both camps.

Now the part called "Part 2, Unit 1, incidents". the Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks.

Now the part called "Part 4, Unit 1, the alliance system". Bismarck weaves the Dreikaiserbund (1873), the Dual Alliance (1879), the Triple Alliance (1882) and the Reinsurance Treaty (1887) to keep France isolated; Wilhelm II dismisses him (1890), drops Russia, and chases Weltpolitik — France and Russia ally (1894), Britain settles with France (1904) and Russia (1907), the Moroccan crises (1905, Agadir 1911) humiliate Germany, and the Dreadnought race arms both camps.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
