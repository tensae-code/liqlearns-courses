# The First World War Causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The ____ League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Balkan

**2.** Bismarck weaves the ____ (1873), the Dual Alliance (1879), the Triple Alliance (1882) and the Reinsurance Treaty (1887) to keep France isolated; Wilhelm II dismisses him (1890), drops Russia, and chases Weltpolitik — France and Russia ally (1894), Britain settles with France (1904) and Russia (1907), the Moroccan crises (1905, Agadir 1911) humiliate Germany, and the Dreadnought race arms both camps. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Dreikaiserbund
