# The First World War Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Part 2, Unit 1, incidents" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) The Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks
**Answer: d) The Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks** — That is what this lesson teaches.
