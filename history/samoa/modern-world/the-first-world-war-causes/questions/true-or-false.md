# The First World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Unit 1, the alliance system" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
