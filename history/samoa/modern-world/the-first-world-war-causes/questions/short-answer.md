# The First World War Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 1, incidents" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Balkan League beats Turkey in seven weeks (1912), then falls on itself (1913); on 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip of the Black Hand shoots Archduke Franz Ferdinand at Sarajevo; Germany's "blank cheque", Austria-Hungary's impossible ultimatum of 23 July, and the mobilisation cascade turn the murder into the First World War within five weeks.

**2.** What came of "Part 4, Unit 1, the alliance system" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bismarck weaves the Dreikaiserbund (1873), the Dual Alliance (1879), the Triple Alliance (1882) and the Reinsurance Treaty (1887) to keep France isolated; Wilhelm II dismisses him (1890), drops Russia, and chases Weltpolitik — France and Russia ally (1894), Britain settles with France (1904) and Russia (1907), the Moroccan crises (1905, Agadir 1911) humiliate Germany, and the Dreadnought race arms both camps.
