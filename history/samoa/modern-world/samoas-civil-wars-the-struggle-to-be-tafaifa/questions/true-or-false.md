# Samoa's Civil Wars: The Struggle to Be Tafa'ifā — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A tafa'ifā holds all four pāpā titles and is made by the districts, not inherited — so when foreigners push a centralised government and a "king" in 1854, Malietoa Laupepa, Malietoa Talavou, Mataafa and Tupua Tamasese all have genealogical claims, and Britain, America and Germany arm their favourites.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 1, causes" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
