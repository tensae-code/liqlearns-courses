# Samoa's Civil Wars: The Struggle to Be Tafa'ifā — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A tafa'ifā holds all four pāpā titles and is made by the districts, not inherited — so when foreigners push a centralised government and a "king" in 1854, ____ Laupepa, Malietoa Talavou, Mataafa and Tupua Tamasese all have genealogical claims, and Britain, America and Germany arm their favourites.
**Answer:** Malietoa
