# Samoa's Civil Wars: The Struggle to Be Tafa'ifā — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: a tafa'ifā holds all four pāpā titles and is made by the districts, not inherited — so when foreigners push a centralised government and a "king" in 1854, Malietoa Laupepa, Malietoa Talavou, Mataafa and Tupua Tamasese all have genealogical claims, and Britain, America and Germany arm their favourites — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
