# Stalin's Five-Year Plans and Collectivisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 6, Unit 2" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** The Five-Year Plans (1928–32, 1933–37) set steel and grain targets for a backward country, and collectivisation drives peasants — kulaks first — onto state farms; the book's verdict counts between 15 and 20 million dead from collectivisation, forced industrialisation and the purges of 1929–38, yet credits the same crash programme with the industrial strength that survived Barbarossa.
