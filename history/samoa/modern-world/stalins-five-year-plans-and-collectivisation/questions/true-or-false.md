# Stalin's Five-Year Plans and Collectivisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Five-Year Plans (1928–32, 1933–37) set steel and grain targets for a backward country, and collectivisation drives peasants — kulaks first — onto state farms; the book's verdict counts between 15 and 20 million dead from collectivisation, forced industrialisation and the purges of 1929–38, yet credits the same crash programme with the industrial strength that survived Barbarossa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Unit 2" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
