# Samoa's Civil Wars: Laupepa, Talavou and the Berlin Act — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Laupepa drives ____ from Mulinu'u in 1860, yet Talavou wins and yields him the kingship in 1873; the deportation of the American premier Steinberger splits king and Taimua-Faipule in 1877; the Lackawanna treaty (1881) makes rivals alternate as king and vice-king; German consul Weber backs Tamasese, Laupepa is exiled to the Marshalls, and the 1889 Apia hurricane wrecks the rival warships before the Berlin Act restores Laupepa under a three-power joint government.
**Answer:** Talavou
