# Samoa's Civil Wars: Laupepa, Talavou and the Berlin Act — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Laupepa drives Talavou from Mulinu'u in 1860, yet Talavou wins and yields him the kingship in 1873; the deportation of the American premier Steinberger splits king and Taimua-Faipule in 1877; the Lackawanna treaty (1881) makes rivals alternate as king and vice-king; German consul Weber backs Tamasese, Laupepa is exiled to the Marshalls, and the 1889 Apia hurricane wrecks the rival warships before the Berlin Act restores Laupepa under a three-power joint government.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 1, the wars" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
