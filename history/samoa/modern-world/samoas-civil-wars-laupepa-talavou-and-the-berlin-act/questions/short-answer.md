# Samoa's Civil Wars: Laupepa, Talavou and the Berlin Act — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 3, Unit 1, the wars" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Laupepa drives Talavou from Mulinu'u in 1860, yet Talavou wins and yields him the kingship in 1873; the deportation of the American premier Steinberger splits king and Taimua-Faipule in 1877; the Lackawanna treaty (1881) makes rivals alternate as king and vice-king; German consul Weber backs Tamasese, Laupepa is exiled to the Marshalls, and the 1889 Apia hurricane wrecks the rival warships before the Berlin Act restores Laupepa under a three-power joint government.
