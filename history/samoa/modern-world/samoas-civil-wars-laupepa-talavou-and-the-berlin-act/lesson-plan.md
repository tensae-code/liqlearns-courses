# Lesson plan — Samoa's Civil Wars: Laupepa, Talavou and the Berlin Act

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Samoa's Civil Wars: Laupepa, Talavou and the Berlin Act
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Laupepa drives Talavou from Mulinu'u in 1860, yet Talavou wins and yields him the kingship in 1873; the deportation of the American premier Steinberger splits king and Taimua-Faipule in 1877; the Lackawanna treaty (1881) makes rivals alternate as king and vice-king; German consul Weber backs Tamasese, Laupepa is exiled to the Marshalls, and the 1889 Apia hurricane wrecks the rival warships before the Berlin Act restores Laupepa under a three-power joint government.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Samoa Whole: Origins, Contact, Colony and Independence — and the World Beyond — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Laupepa drives Talavou from Mulinu'u in 1860, yet Talavou wins and yields him the kingship in 1873; the deportation of the American premier Steinberger splits king and Taimua-Faipule in 1877; the Lackawanna treaty (1881) makes rivals alternate as king and vice-king; German consul Weber backs Tamasese, Laupepa is exiled to the Marshalls, and the 1889 Apia hurricane wrecks the rival warships before the Berlin Act restores Laupepa under a three-power joint government. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
