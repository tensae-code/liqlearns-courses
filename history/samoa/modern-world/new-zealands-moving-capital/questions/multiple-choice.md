# New Zealand's Moving Capital — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Activity 15 "Moving New Zealand's capital city"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Kororareka (Russell), a missionary and whaling centre, gives way to Auckland as capital in 1840, then war in the north and the pull of a central seat move government to Wellington in 1865
**Answer: d) Kororareka (Russell), a missionary and whaling centre, gives way to Auckland as capital in 1840, then war in the north and the pull of a central seat move government to Wellington in 1865** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 1, Activity 15 "Moving New Zealand's capital city""?
a) Kororareka (Russell), a missionary and whaling centre, gives way to Auckland as capital in 1840, then war in the north and the pull of a central seat move government to Wellington in 1865  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Kororareka (Russell), a missionary and whaling centre, gives way to Auckland as capital in 1840, then war in the north and the pull of a central seat move government to Wellington in 1865** — That is what this lesson teaches.
