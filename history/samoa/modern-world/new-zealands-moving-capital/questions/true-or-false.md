# New Zealand's Moving Capital — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kororareka (Russell), a missionary and whaling centre, gives way to Auckland as capital in 1840, then war in the north and the pull of a central seat move government to Wellington in 1865.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Activity 15 "Moving New Zealand's capital city"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
