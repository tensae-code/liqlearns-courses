# Kashmir the Unfinished Business of Partition — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kashmir's Hindu maharaja delays choosing, Pakistan invades behind tribal fighters, he signs accession to India, and the first Kashmir war ends in a 1948 UN ceasefire and a plebiscite that never happens; China seizes Aksai Chin in 1962, India and Pakistan fight again in 1965 and 1971 — the year East Pakistan breaks away as Bangladesh — and the 1972 Simla Accord leaves the line of control frozen.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 3, Unit 5" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
