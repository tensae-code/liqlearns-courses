# Video script — Kashmir the Unfinished Business of Partition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Kashmir's Hindu maharaja delays choosing, Pakistan invades behind tribal fighters, he signs accession to India, and the first Kashmir war ends in a 1948 UN ceasefire and a plebiscite that never happens; China seizes Aksai Chin in 1962, India and Pakistan fight again in 1965 and 1971 — the year East Pakistan breaks away as Bangladesh — and the 1972 Simla Accord leaves the line of control frozen.
