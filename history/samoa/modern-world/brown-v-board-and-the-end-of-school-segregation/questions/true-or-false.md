# Brown v. Board and the End of School Segregation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nine-year-old Linda Brown is barred from the school near her Kansas home; her father goes to the NAACP, lawyer Thurgood Marshall takes the case to the Supreme Court, and in the book's 1955 telling the judge rules school segregation unconstitutional and orders desegregation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Topic 3 "The Black Civil Rights News"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
