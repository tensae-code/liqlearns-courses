# German Samoa Solf and the Mau a Pule — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Governor ____ (1900) abolishes the Tupu, installs Mataafa as Ali'i Sili, rewrites the fa'alupega to honour the Kaiser, and blocks the Sāmoan-owned Oloa copra company; orator Lauaki Mamoe petitions for a Sāmoan voice, and Solf exiles him and his supporters' families to Saipan — Lauaki dies at sea in 1915 on the ship finally bringing him home.
