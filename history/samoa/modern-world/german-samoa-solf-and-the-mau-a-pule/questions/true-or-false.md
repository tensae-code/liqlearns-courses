# German Samoa Solf and the Mau a Pule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Governor Solf (1900) abolishes the Tupu, installs Mataafa as Ali'i Sili, rewrites the fa'alupega to honour the Kaiser, and blocks the Sāmoan-owned Oloa copra company; orator Lauaki Mamoe petitions for a Sāmoan voice, and Solf exiles him and his supporters' families to Saipan — Lauaki dies at sea in 1915 on the ship finally bringing him home.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 4, first half" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
