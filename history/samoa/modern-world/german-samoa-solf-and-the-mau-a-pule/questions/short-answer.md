# German Samoa Solf and the Mau a Pule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 4, first half" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Governor Solf (1900) abolishes the Tupu, installs Mataafa as Ali'i Sili, rewrites the fa'alupega to honour the Kaiser, and blocks the Sāmoan-owned Oloa copra company; orator Lauaki Mamoe petitions for a Sāmoan voice, and Solf exiles him and his supporters' families to Saipan — Lauaki dies at sea in 1915 on the ship finally bringing him home.
