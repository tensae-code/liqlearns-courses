# Video script — Karl Marx and the Idea of Communism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Marx, watching industrial Britain's rich-poor gap from London exile, casts history as class warfare — capitalists against proletariat — ending in revolution, a transitional socialism, and a classless communism where the state withers away; Lenin, whose brother was executed for plotting against the Tsar, turns the theory into a disciplined Bolshevik party.
