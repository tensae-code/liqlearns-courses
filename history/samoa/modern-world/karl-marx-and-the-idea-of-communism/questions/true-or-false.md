# Karl Marx and the Idea of Communism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Marx, watching industrial Britain's rich-poor gap from London exile, casts history as class warfare — capitalists against proletariat — ending in revolution, a transitional socialism, and a classless communism where the state withers away; Lenin, whose brother was executed for plotting against the Tsar, turns the theory into a disciplined Bolshevik party.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 6, Unit 3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
