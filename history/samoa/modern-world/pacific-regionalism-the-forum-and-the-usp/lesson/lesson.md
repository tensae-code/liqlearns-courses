# Pacific Regionalism: The Forum and the USP

## Do it first

Take a pencil and a page. Write the lesson's title at the top: "Pacific Regionalism: The Forum and the USP". Under it, leave one empty box for each part of the story, in order: Unit 4, Topics 2–3, regional organisations; Part 4, Units 2–3. As you read each part, fill its box with three words: who, where, and what turned. By the end of the lesson your page is the whole story in your own hand — and this lesson is about exactly what fills those boxes.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4, Topics 2–3, regional organisations

Pacific nations build their own institutions together — the South Pacific Forum for regional politics and policy, and the University of the South Pacific, established in 1968 and jointly owned by twelve island governments with its main campus in Fiji and the School of Agriculture at Alafua in Samoa.

Hold the turn in this part: who was there, where it happened, what changed, and what came of it — that turn is what makes it history, not just a list of names.
