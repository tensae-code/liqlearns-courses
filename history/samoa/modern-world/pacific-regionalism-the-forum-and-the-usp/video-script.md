# Video script — Pacific Regionalism: The Forum and the USP

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. Pacific nations build their own institutions together — the South Pacific Forum for regional politics and policy, and the University of the South Pacific, established in 1968 and jointly owned by twelve island governments with its main campus in Fiji and the School of Agriculture at Alafua in Samoa; shut out of politics at the South Pacific Commission, the new island leaders found the South Pacific Forum at Wellington in 1971 (Sāmoa among the seven founders), build SPEC (1973), the Forum Fisheries Agency and SOPAC, win the 1989 driftnet ban, and crown regional co-operation with the 1985 Rarotonga Treaty making the South Pacific a nuclear free zone.
