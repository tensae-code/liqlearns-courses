# Pacific Regionalism: The Forum and the USP — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 4, Topics 2–3, regional organisations" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pacific nations build their own institutions together — the South Pacific Forum for regional politics and policy, and the University of the South Pacific, established in 1968 and jointly owned by twelve island governments with its main campus in Fiji and the School of Agriculture at Alafua in Samoa.

**2.** What came of "Part 4, Units 2–3" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Shut out of politics at the South Pacific Commission, the new island leaders found the South Pacific Forum at Wellington in 1971 (Sāmoa among the seven founders), build SPEC (1973), the Forum Fisheries Agency and SOPAC, win the 1989 driftnet ban, and crown regional co-operation with the 1985 Rarotonga Treaty making the South Pacific a nuclear free zone.
