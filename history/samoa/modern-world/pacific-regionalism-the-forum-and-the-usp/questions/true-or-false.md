# Pacific Regionalism: The Forum and the USP — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pacific nations build their own institutions together — the South Pacific Forum for regional politics and policy, and the University of the South Pacific, established in 1968 and jointly owned by twelve island governments with its main campus in Fiji and the School of Agriculture at Alafua in Samoa. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 4, Units 2–3" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
