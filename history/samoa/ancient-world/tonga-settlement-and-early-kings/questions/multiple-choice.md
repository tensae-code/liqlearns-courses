# Tonga Settlement and Early Kings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 4, Topic 4 "Tonga"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Ancestors sail from South East Asia across the Pacific and settle Tonga about three thousand years ago; a monarchy more than a thousand years old grows from those settlements, and Europeans such as Captain Cook later visit and name it the Friendly Islands
**Answer: d) Ancestors sail from South East Asia across the Pacific and settle Tonga about three thousand years ago; a monarchy more than a thousand years old grows from those settlements, and Europeans such as Captain Cook later visit and name it the Friendly Islands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
