# Tonga Settlement and Early Kings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ancestors sail from South East Asia across the Pacific and settle Tonga about three thousand years ago; a monarchy more than a thousand years old grows from those settlements, and Europeans such as Captain Cook later visit and name it the Friendly Islands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 4, Topic 4 "Tonga"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
