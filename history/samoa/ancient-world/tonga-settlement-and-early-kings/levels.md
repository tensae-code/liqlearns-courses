# Tonga Settlement and Early Kings — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: ancestors sail from South East Asia across the Pacific and settle Tonga about three thousand years ago; a monarchy more than a thousand years old grows from those settlements, and Europeans such as Captain Cook later visit and name it the Friendly Islands — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
