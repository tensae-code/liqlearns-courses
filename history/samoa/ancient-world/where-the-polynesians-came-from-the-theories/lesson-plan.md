# Lesson plan — Where the Polynesians Came from the Theories

**Course:** History — Samoa · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Where the Polynesians Came from the Theories
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage, Places and the Voyaging Pacific (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Three rival answers compete — ancestors sailed east from Asia through Melanesia; Thor Heyerdahl's 1947 Kon-Tiki raft backs a South American origin carried on the kumara; and Polynesian oral traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods; lapita pottery, carbon-dated at Mulifanua to about 2,990 years old, traces Austronesian ancestors from Taiwan through the Philippines, Indonesia and New Guinea to the Bismarcks by 1500 BC, Tonga by 1300 BC and Sāmoa by about 1000 BC — backed by DNA, the Austronesian language family and shared practices (siapo, tatau, ava).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Heritage, Places and the Voyaging Pacific — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Three rival answers compete — ancestors sailed east from Asia through Melanesia; Thor Heyerdahl's 1947 Kon-Tiki raft backs a South American origin carried on the kumara; and Polynesian oral traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods; lapita pottery, carbon-dated at Mulifanua to about 2,990 years old, traces Austronesian ancestors from Taiwan through the Philippines, Indonesia and New Guinea to the Bismarcks by 1500 BC, Tonga by 1300 BC and Sāmoa by about 1000 BC — backed by DNA, the Austronesian language family and shared practices (siapo, tatau, ava). Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
