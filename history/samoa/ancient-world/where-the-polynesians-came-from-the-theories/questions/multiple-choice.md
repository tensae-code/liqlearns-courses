# Where the Polynesians Came from the Theories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 2, Topic 2, origins theories" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Three rival answers compete — ancestors sailed east from Asia through Melanesia; Thor Heyerdahl's 1947 Kon-Tiki raft backs a South American origin carried on the kumara; and Polynesian oral traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods
