# Where the Polynesians Came from the Theories — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three rival answers compete — ancestors sailed east from Asia through Melanesia; Thor Heyerdahl's 1947 Kon-Tiki raft backs a South American origin carried on the kumara; and Polynesian oral traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 1 "Which Theories Explain Sāmoa's Origins?", Theory One" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
