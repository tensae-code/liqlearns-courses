# Where the Polynesians Came from the Theories — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, Topic 2, origins theories" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Three rival answers compete — ancestors sailed east from Asia through Melanesia; Thor Heyerdahl's 1947 Kon-Tiki raft backs a South American origin carried on the kumara; and Polynesian oral traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods.

**2.** What came of "Part 1, Unit 1 "Which Theories Explain Sāmoa's Origins?", Theory One" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lapita pottery, carbon-dated at Mulifanua to about 2,990 years old, traces Austronesian ancestors from Taiwan through the Philippines, Indonesia and New Guinea to the Bismarcks by 1500 BC, Tonga by 1300 BC and Sāmoa by about 1000 BC — backed by DNA, the Austronesian language family and shared practices (siapo, tatau, ava).
