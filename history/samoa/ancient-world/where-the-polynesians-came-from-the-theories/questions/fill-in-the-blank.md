# Where the Polynesians Came from the Theories — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Three rival answers compete — ancestors sailed east from ____ through Melanesia; Thor Heyerdahl's 1947 Kon-Tiki raft backs a South American origin carried on the kumara; and Polynesian oral traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Asia

**2.** Lapita pottery, carbon-dated at ____ to about 2,990 years old, traces Austronesian ancestors from Taiwan through the Philippines, Indonesia and New Guinea to the Bismarcks by 1500 BC, Tonga by 1300 BC and Sāmoa by about 1000 BC — backed by DNA, the Austronesian language family and shared practices (siapo, tatau, ava). (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mulifanua
