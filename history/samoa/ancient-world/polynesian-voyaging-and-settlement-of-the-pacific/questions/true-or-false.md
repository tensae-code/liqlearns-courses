# Polynesian Voyaging and Settlement of the Pacific — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ancestors cross the vast Pacific in canoes and settle island by island — Fiji, Tonga and Samoa among the oldest on the settlement timeline from about 1500 BC, the far corners (Hawaii, Rapanui, Aotearoa) last — peopling Micronesia, Melanesia and Polynesia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 2, deliberate or accidental voyages" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
