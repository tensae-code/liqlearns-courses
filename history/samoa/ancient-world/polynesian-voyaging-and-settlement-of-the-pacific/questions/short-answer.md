# Polynesian Voyaging and Settlement of the Pacific — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, Topic 2 "Ancient Pacific Migrations"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ancestors cross the vast Pacific in canoes and settle island by island — Fiji, Tonga and Samoa among the oldest on the settlement timeline from about 1500 BC, the far corners (Hawaii, Rapanui, Aotearoa) last — peopling Micronesia, Melanesia and Polynesia.

**2.** What came of "Part 1, Unit 2, deliberate or accidental voyages" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Hōkūle'a's 1985 Rarotonga-to-Bay of Islands crossing, timed to the pohutukawa bloom as the legends said, is used to argue the ancestors voyaged deliberately and two-way, not by one-way drift.
