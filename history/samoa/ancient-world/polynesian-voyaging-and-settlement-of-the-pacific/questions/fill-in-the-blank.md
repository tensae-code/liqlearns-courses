# Polynesian Voyaging and Settlement of the Pacific — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Ancestors cross the vast ____ in canoes and settle island by island — Fiji, Tonga and Samoa among the oldest on the settlement timeline from about 1500 BC, the far corners (Hawaii, Rapanui, Aotearoa) last — peopling Micronesia, Melanesia and Polynesia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pacific

**2.** The ____ 1985 Rarotonga-to-Bay of Islands crossing, timed to the pohutukawa bloom as the legends said, is used to argue the ancestors voyaged deliberately and two-way, not by one-way drift. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hōkūle'a's
