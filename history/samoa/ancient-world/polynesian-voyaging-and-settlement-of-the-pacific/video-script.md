# Video script — Polynesian Voyaging and Settlement of the Pacific

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Ancestors cross the vast Pacific in canoes and settle island by island — Fiji, Tonga and Samoa among the oldest on the settlement timeline from about 1500 BC, the far corners (Hawaii, Rapanui, Aotearoa) last — peopling Micronesia, Melanesia and Polynesia; the Hōkūle'a's 1985 Rarotonga-to-Bay of Islands crossing, timed to the pohutukawa bloom as the legends said, is used to argue the ancestors voyaged deliberately and two-way, not by one-way drift.
