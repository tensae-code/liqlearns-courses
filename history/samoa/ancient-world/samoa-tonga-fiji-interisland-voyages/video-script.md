# Video script — Samoa, Tonga and Fiji: Inter-Island Voyages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Tongan royalty sail to Sāmoa for highborn wives — Prince Ngata's marriage founds Tonga's Tu'i Kanokupolu line, and Vaetoifaga's daughter Salamasina grows up to hold all four pāpā titles as Sāmoa's first tafa'ifā; the Tu'i Fiti's sons come to conquer the warrior Vaea, who lifts their great boat into the trees and spares them for their sister Apaula; her son is murdered in Fiji, she returns weeping — the river Loimata o Apaula, "Apaula's tears" — and Vaea's brother Va'atausili regains his strength to avenge him.

Now the part called "Part 1, Unit 2, Tongan stories". Tongan royalty sail to Sāmoa for highborn wives — Prince Ngata's marriage founds Tonga's Tu'i Kanokupolu line, and Vaetoifaga's daughter Salamasina grows up to hold all four pāpā titles as Sāmoa's first tafa'ifā.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
