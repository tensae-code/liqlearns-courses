# Samoa, Tonga and Fiji: Inter-Island Voyages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Tongan royalty sail to ____ for highborn wives — Prince Ngata's marriage founds Tonga's Tu'i Kanokupolu line, and Vaetoifaga's daughter Salamasina grows up to hold all four pāpā titles as Sāmoa's first tafa'ifā. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sāmoa

**2.** The ____ Fiti's sons come to conquer the warrior Vaea, who lifts their great boat into the trees and spares them for their sister Apaula; her son is murdered in Fiji, she returns weeping — the river Loimata o Apaula, "Apaula's tears" — and Vaea's brother Va'atausili regains his strength to avenge him. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tu'i
