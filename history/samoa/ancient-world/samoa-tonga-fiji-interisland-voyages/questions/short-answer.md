# Samoa, Tonga and Fiji: Inter-Island Voyages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 2, Tongan stories" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tongan royalty sail to Sāmoa for highborn wives — Prince Ngata's marriage founds Tonga's Tu'i Kanokupolu line, and Vaetoifaga's daughter Salamasina grows up to hold all four pāpā titles as Sāmoa's first tafa'ifā.

**2.** What came of "Part 1, Unit 2, Fijian stories (plot summaries only)" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Tu'i Fiti's sons come to conquer the warrior Vaea, who lifts their great boat into the trees and spares them for their sister Apaula; her son is murdered in Fiji, she returns weeping — the river Loimata o Apaula, "Apaula's tears" — and Vaea's brother Va'atausili regains his strength to avenge him.
