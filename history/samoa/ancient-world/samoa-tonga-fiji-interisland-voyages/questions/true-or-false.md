# Samoa, Tonga and Fiji: Inter-Island Voyages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tongan royalty sail to Sāmoa for highborn wives — Prince Ngata's marriage founds Tonga's Tu'i Kanokupolu line, and Vaetoifaga's daughter Salamasina grows up to hold all four pāpā titles as Sāmoa's first tafa'ifā. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 2, Fijian stories (plot summaries only)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
