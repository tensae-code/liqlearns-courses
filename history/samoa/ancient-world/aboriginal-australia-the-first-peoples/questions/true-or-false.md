# Aboriginal Australia the First Peoples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aboriginal peoples settle and live in Australia for more than 50,000 years before any European sail is sighted.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Topic 1 "Types Of Cultural Interaction", Australia history box" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
