# Aboriginal Australia the First Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 2, Topic 1 "Types Of Cultural Interaction", Australia history box" tell?

b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
