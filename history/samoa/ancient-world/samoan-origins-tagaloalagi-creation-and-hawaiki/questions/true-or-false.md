# Samoan Origins: Tagaloalagi, Creation and Hawaiki — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sāmoan oral tradition answers the scientists from inside — the god Tagaloalagi created the Sāmoan people in Sāmoa itself and organised them into districts and villages, with genealogies placing him about 2600 BC; other Polynesian traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 1, Theory Three" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
