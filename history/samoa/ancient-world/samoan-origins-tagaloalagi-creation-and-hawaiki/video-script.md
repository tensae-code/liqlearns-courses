# Video script — Samoan Origins: Tagaloalagi, Creation and Hawaiki

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. Sāmoan oral tradition answers the scientists from inside — the god Tagaloalagi created the Sāmoan people in Sāmoa itself and organised them into districts and villages, with genealogies placing him about 2600 BC; other Polynesian traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods.
