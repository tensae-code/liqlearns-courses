# Ancient Civilisations Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Emperor Augustus orders a census-tax of the whole Roman Empire, so Joseph and Mary travel from Nazareth to Bethlehem to be counted — the census Luke's Gospel makes famous.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 4, Activity 3 "Census Information"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
