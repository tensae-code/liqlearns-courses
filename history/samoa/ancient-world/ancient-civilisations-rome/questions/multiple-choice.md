# Ancient Civilisations Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 4, Activity 3 "Census Information"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Emperor Augustus orders a census-tax of the whole Roman Empire, so Joseph and Mary travel from Nazareth to Bethlehem to be counted — the census Luke's Gospel makes famous
**Answer: d) Emperor Augustus orders a census-tax of the whole Roman Empire, so Joseph and Mary travel from Nazareth to Bethlehem to be counted — the census Luke's Gospel makes famous** — That is what this lesson teaches.
