# Fiji's Settlement Legend and First Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, "Fijians — a General Settlement History"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Fijian legend says the great chief Lutunasobasoba led his people across the seas to Fiji; there Melanesians and Polynesians mix into a highly developed society long before Europeans arrive
**Answer: d) Fijian legend says the great chief Lutunasobasoba led his people across the seas to Fiji; there Melanesians and Polynesians mix into a highly developed society long before Europeans arrive** — That is what this lesson teaches.
