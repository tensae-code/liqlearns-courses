# Fiji's Settlement Legend and First Peoples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fijian legend says the great chief Lutunasobasoba led his people across the seas to Fiji; there Melanesians and Polynesians mix into a highly developed society long before Europeans arrive.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, "Fijians — a General Settlement History"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
