# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Buddhism, ____, Islam and Judaism each teach how human beings must treat one another, and those teachings become one root of later rights ideas. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Christianity

**2.** Diwali, perhaps the oldest festival in human history still celebrated, begins as a ____ festival of lamps on the darkest night of Kartik and crosses religious bounds until it is a public holiday in Fiji and a national holiday in India and Trinidad. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hindu
