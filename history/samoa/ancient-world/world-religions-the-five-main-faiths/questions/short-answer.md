# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, "How ideas about human rights developed: Religions"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Buddhism, Christianity, Islam and Judaism each teach how human beings must treat one another, and those teachings become one root of later rights ideas.

**2.** What came of "Unit 1, Topic 1 resource 4A "Diwali — the Festival of Lights"" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Diwali, perhaps the oldest festival in human history still celebrated, begins as a Hindu festival of lamps on the darkest night of Kartik and crosses religious bounds until it is a public holiday in Fiji and a national holiday in India and Trinidad.
