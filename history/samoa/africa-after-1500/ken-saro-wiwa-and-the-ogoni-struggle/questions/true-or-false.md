# Ken Saro Wiwa and the Ogoni Struggle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Writer Ken Saro-Wiwa champions the Ogoni, whose oil-rich Niger Delta land is polluted while they stay poor; after Abacha's 1993 seizure of power he is jailed repeatedly and hanged on 10 November 1995 after an unfair trial.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 2, Case Study 3 "Fair And Equal Treatment For All"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
