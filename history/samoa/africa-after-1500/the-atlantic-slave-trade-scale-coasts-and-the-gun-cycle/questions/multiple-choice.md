# The Atlantic Slave Trade Scale Coasts and the Gun Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Fact Sheet One "Slaves and slavery in America"" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) From 1619 to 1803 thousands of Africans are shipped across the Atlantic and sold at markets like animals to southern plantation owners hungry for unpaid labour — between 400,000 and a million people; Virginia's law makes slave women's children slaves too, so slavery is inherited as well as captured
**Answer: d) From 1619 to 1803 thousands of Africans are shipped across the Atlantic and sold at markets like animals to southern plantation owners hungry for unpaid labour — between 400,000 and a million people; Virginia's law makes slave women's children slaves too, so slavery is inherited as well as captured** — That is what this lesson teaches.
