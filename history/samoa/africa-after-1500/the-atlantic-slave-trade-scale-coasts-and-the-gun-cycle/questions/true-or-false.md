# The Atlantic Slave Trade Scale Coasts and the Gun Cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From 1619 to 1803 thousands of Africans are shipped across the Atlantic and sold at markets like animals to southern plantation owners hungry for unpaid labour — between 400,000 and a million people; Virginia's law makes slave women's children slaves too, so slavery is inherited as well as captured.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 1, Fact Sheet One "Slaves and slavery in America"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
