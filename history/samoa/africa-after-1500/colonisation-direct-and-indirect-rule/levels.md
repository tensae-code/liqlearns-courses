# Colonisation Direct and Indirect Rule — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: rule varies by power and island — America takes Pago Pago (1878) and annexes Tutuila and Manu'a (1899) for a naval base; Germany's Solf governs Sāmoa through indentured New Guinea and Chinese labour; Britain shields Fijian chiefs and land while importing 61,000 Indians, yet strips Solomon Islanders' coastal land; France alienates some 90% of New Caledonia and crushes the 1878 and 1917 uprisings — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
