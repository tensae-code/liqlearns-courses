# Colonisation Direct and Indirect Rule — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Rule varies by power and island — ____ takes Pago Pago (1878) and annexes Tutuila and Manu'a (1899) for a naval base; Germany's Solf governs Sāmoa through indentured New Guinea and Chinese labour; Britain shields Fijian chiefs and land while importing 61,000 Indians, yet strips Solomon Islanders' coastal land; France alienates some 90% of New Caledonia and crushes the 1878 and 1917 uprisings.
**Answer:** America
