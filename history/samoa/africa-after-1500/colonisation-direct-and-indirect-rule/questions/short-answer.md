# Colonisation Direct and Indirect Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 2 "Types And Nature Of Colonial Rule"" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Rule varies by power and island — America takes Pago Pago (1878) and annexes Tutuila and Manu'a (1899) for a naval base; Germany's Solf governs Sāmoa through indentured New Guinea and Chinese labour; Britain shields Fijian chiefs and land while importing 61,000 Indians, yet strips Solomon Islanders' coastal land; France alienates some 90% of New Caledonia and crushes the 1878 and 1917 uprisings.
