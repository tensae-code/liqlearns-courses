# Colonisation Direct and Indirect Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rule varies by power and island — America takes Pago Pago (1878) and annexes Tutuila and Manu'a (1899) for a naval base; Germany's Solf governs Sāmoa through indentured New Guinea and Chinese labour; Britain shields Fijian chiefs and land while importing 61,000 Indians, yet strips Solomon Islanders' coastal land; France alienates some 90% of New Caledonia and crushes the 1878 and 1917 uprisings.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 2 "Types And Nature Of Colonial Rule"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
