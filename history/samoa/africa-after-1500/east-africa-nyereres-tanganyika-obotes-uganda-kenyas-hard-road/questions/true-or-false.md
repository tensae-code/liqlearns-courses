# East Africa: Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda and Kenya's Hard Road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tanganyika is a German colony from 1885 to 1916, passes to British control from 1919, wins independence in 1961, and in 1964 unites with Zanzibar to become Tanzania.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 4, Tanzania country note" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
