# East Africa: Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda and Kenya's Hard Road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 4, Tanzania country note" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Tanganyika is a German colony from 1885 to 1916, passes to British control from 1919, wins independence in 1961, and in 1964 unites with Zanzibar to become Tanzania
**Answer: d) Tanganyika is a German colony from 1885 to 1916, passes to British control from 1919, wins independence in 1961, and in 1964 unites with Zanzibar to become Tanzania** — That is what this lesson teaches.
