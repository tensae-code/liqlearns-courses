# Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The ____ Revolution's hunger for cheap raw materials, empire rivalry measured in colonies, the China tea trade's demand for sandalwood and bêche-de-mer, and "Christianity before the flag" carry Britain, Germany, France and America into the Pacific. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Industrial

**2.** In ____ the motives are the familiar ones — trade, routes, empire — but the push comes from men on the spot: British India annexes Burma in 1852 as a buffer against French Indochina, and Penang, Singapore and Malacca are taken in 1786–1824 to guard India and feed the China trade. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Asia
