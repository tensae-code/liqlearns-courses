# Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Industrial Revolution's hunger for cheap raw materials, empire rivalry measured in colonies, the China tea trade's demand for sandalwood and bêche-de-mer, and "Christianity before the flag" carry Britain, Germany, France and America into the Pacific. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 2, Unit 3, Asia" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
