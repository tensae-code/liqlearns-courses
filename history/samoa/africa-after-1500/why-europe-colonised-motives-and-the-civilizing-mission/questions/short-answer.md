# Why Europe Colonised Motives and the Civilizing Mission — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 2, Unit 1 "Motives Of Imperial Powers"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Industrial Revolution's hunger for cheap raw materials, empire rivalry measured in colonies, the China tea trade's demand for sandalwood and bêche-de-mer, and "Christianity before the flag" carry Britain, Germany, France and America into the Pacific.

**2.** What came of "Part 2, Unit 3, Asia" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In Asia the motives are the familiar ones — trade, routes, empire — but the push comes from men on the spot: British India annexes Burma in 1852 as a buffer against French Indochina, and Penang, Singapore and Malacca are taken in 1786–1824 to guard India and feed the China trade.
