# Video script — Historiography the History of History Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Three rival origin stories are tested against kinds of evidence — archaeology, DNA, language, oral tradition — and the eastern (Heyerdahl/Kon-Tiki, kumara, ruin shapes) theory is judged far weaker than the South East Asian one; europeans are written up at home as the "discoverers" of the Pacific, but the book sets the Sāmoan view against it — "we believe our ancestors and ancestral gods were the first discoverers of our world"; the newcomers are papalagi, "heaven-breakers".
