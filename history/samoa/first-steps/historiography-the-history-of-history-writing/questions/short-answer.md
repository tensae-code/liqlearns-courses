# Historiography the History of History Writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Part 1, Unit 1, weighing the theories" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Three rival origin stories are tested against kinds of evidence — archaeology, DNA, language, oral tradition — and the eastern (Heyerdahl/Kon-Tiki, kumara, ruin shapes) theory is judged far weaker than the South East Asian one.

**2.** What came of "Part 1, Unit 3, "discovery" contested" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Europeans are written up at home as the "discoverers" of the Pacific, but the book sets the Sāmoan view against it — "we believe our ancestors and ancestral gods were the first discoverers of our world"; the newcomers are papalagi, "heaven-breakers".
