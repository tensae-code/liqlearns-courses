# Historiography the History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three rival origin stories are tested against kinds of evidence — archaeology, DNA, language, oral tradition — and the eastern (Heyerdahl/Kon-Tiki, kumara, ruin shapes) theory is judged far weaker than the South East Asian one. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Part 1, Unit 3, "discovery" contested" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
