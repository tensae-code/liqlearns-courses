# Historiography the History of History Writing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Three rival origin stories are tested against kinds of evidence — archaeology, ____, language, oral tradition — and the eastern (Heyerdahl/Kon-Tiki, kumara, ruin shapes) theory is judged far weaker than the South East Asian one. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** DNA

**2.** Europeans are written up at home as the "discoverers" of the ____, but the book sets the Sāmoan view against it — "we believe our ancestors and ancestral gods were the first discoverers of our world"; the newcomers are papalagi, "heaven-breakers". (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pacific
