# Hands-on — Heritage What It Is and Why It Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Do the opening act of lesson/lesson.md again, as it taught — and say the lesson's one thing aloud as you finish it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw this lesson's story as a line of pictures, one for each part in order: Unit 3, Topic 2 "Distribution Of Resources"; Unit 3, Activity 13 reading "Dolphin Hunting in Lau Lagoon" (plot summary only); Unit 1 introduction and Topic 1 "Practices And Symbols"; Unit 1, Activity 8 "Understanding And Explaining Siapo"; Unit 2, Resource 1 "Sereana's Story" (plot summary only); Unit 2, Resource 2 "Museums and museum displays"; Unit 1, Topic 2 "Who Are We? National Identity"; Unit 1, Topic 3 "What Are We? Cultural Identity"; Unit 1, Topic 1 resource 5A "Fijian Firewalking" (legend, plot summary only); Unit 3, Fact File Two "The Traditional Uses of the Coconut and Coconut Tree". Under each picture write one line: who is there, where it is, and what turned. Say each line aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's pattern in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see people, places or customs carrying a story from the past, note it: what you saw, and what this lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
