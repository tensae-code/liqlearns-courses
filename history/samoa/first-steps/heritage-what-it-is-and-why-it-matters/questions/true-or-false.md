Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Barter and ceremonial exchange (the si'i presentation of 'ie toga fine mats) carry honour between families, and a traditional Samoan wedding's exchanges are set against a modern wedding's. (Sub lesson 1)
