# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Learners list what Samoan homes needed for housing, food, tools and transport fifty years ago and weigh the long-term damage that use did. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 3, Activity 11 "Interview"" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
