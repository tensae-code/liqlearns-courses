# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Activity 6 "Resources Required In The Past"" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Learners list what Samoan homes needed for housing, food, tools and transport fifty years ago and weigh the long-term damage that use did
**Answer: d) Learners list what Samoan homes needed for housing, food, tools and transport fifty years ago and weigh the long-term damage that use did** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 3, Activity 11 "Interview""? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Learners interview an elder or parent about exchanges that happened in the past, write it up as a report and draw conclusions from the class's reports  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Learners interview an elder or parent about exchanges that happened in the past, write it up as a report and draw conclusions from the class's reports** — That is what this lesson teaches.
