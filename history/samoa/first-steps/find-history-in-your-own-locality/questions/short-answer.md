# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, Activity 6 "Resources Required In The Past"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Learners list what Samoan homes needed for housing, food, tools and transport fifty years ago and weigh the long-term damage that use did.

**2.** What came of "Unit 3, Activity 11 "Interview"" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Learners interview an elder or parent about exchanges that happened in the past, write it up as a report and draw conclusions from the class's reports.
