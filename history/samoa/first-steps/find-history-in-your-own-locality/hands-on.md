# Hands-on — Find History in Your Own Locality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Do the opening act of lesson/lesson.md again, as it taught — and say the lesson's one thing aloud as you finish it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw this lesson's story as a line of pictures, one for each part in order: Unit 1, Activity 6 "Resources Required In The Past"; Unit 3, Activity 11 "Interview"; Unit 1, Activity 11 "Research On A Sacred Place"; Unit 1, Topic 2 "Treasures And Transfer", Activity 4 research; Unit 2, Activity 10 "Mix And Match"; Unit 2, Topic 3 "Telesia's History Highway" (family stories, plot summaries only); Unit 3 Part B, Topic 1 elder interview. Under each picture write one line: who is there, where it is, and what turned. Say each line aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's pattern in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see people, places or customs carrying a story from the past, note it: what you saw, and what this lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
