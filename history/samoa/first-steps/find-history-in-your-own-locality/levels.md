# Find History in Your Own Locality — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, Activity 6 "Resources Required In The Past"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners list what Samoan homes needed for housing, food, tools and transport fifty years ago and weigh the long-term damage that use did — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 3, Activity 11 "Interview"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners interview an elder or parent about exchanges that happened in the past, write it up as a report and draw conclusions from the class's reports — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 1, Activity 11 "Research On A Sacred Place"
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: each learner picks a church, asks what events in its historical past put it where it stands, and answers from elders, printed histories of Samoa's churches, the minister and observation — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 1, Topic 2 "Treasures And Transfer", Activity 4 research
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners ask five people of different ages how culture and heritage are passed on (songs, myths, stories, dances, ceremonies, school), chart the answers and present them as poster, pamphlet or report — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 2, Activity 10 "Mix And Match"
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners set Samoa in the past (weaving fine mats, walking or going by boat, asking family for help) against Samoa today (tins of coconut cream, factories) and ask what production, distribution and exchange looked like in Samoa's past — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit 2, Topic 3 "Telesia's History Highway" (family stories, plot summaries only)
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: four generations of one aiga travel to New Zealand — great-grandmother Mele, a servant girl taken by steamship in 1908 who finds Auckland cold, crowded and lonely; grandfather Ioane for medical treatment in 1958; father Peleiupu, a homesick 17-year-old scholarship boy in Wellington in 1965 who finds friends through rugby; Auntie Naomi, who nurses, marries a European in Auckland and renegotiates the fa'aSamoa; and Telesia herself, flying in hours where Mele sailed for days — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — Unit 3 Part B, Topic 1 elder interview
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners interview someone in their fifties, sixties or seventies about their memories of the early-1970s oil crisis and how it affected Samoa and its people — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
