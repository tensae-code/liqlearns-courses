# From Evidence to a Written History — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, Topic 2 "Sacred Places", source-types box
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners sort evidence into primary (someone who was there), secondary (written up by others), oral and text sources before researching — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 1, Activity 14 "Interpreting Photographs"
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners read past-and-present photographs of Mulinu'u (fono, old Falefono, village) for what people built and did, to judge how it was and remains a political place — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 2, Topic 2, "The Voyages of the Hokule'a"
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a double-hulled canoe built in Hawaii in the 1970s is sailed to Tahiti in 1976 by Satawalese navigator Mau Piailug using stars and swells, testing whether the ancestors voyaged deliberately; later voyages retrace the whole Polynesian Triangle to Rapa Nui — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 1, Topic 1 photo study
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: paired photographs dated from the 1890s to 2001 (feasts, houses, taupou, ceremonies) are sorted as practices or symbols and generalised over: most have changed a little, all were successfully passed on — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 2, Topic 3 "Technology And Change", Activity 5 photo study and interviewing cultural experts
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners re-read the past/present photo pairs (feast foods since 1890, taupou costume, house materials), then interview a grandparent-generation expert as a primary source on whether a chosen tradition (saofa'i, wedding, funeral, si'i) has changed — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit 2, Topic 2 "Telesia's Photo Album" and Activity 3
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners interrogate Tattersall's 1929–30 photographs — the Mau office at Vaimoso, prisoners carried by New Zealand marines, the thousand-women procession, the Black Saturday parade, Tamasese lying in state — with the photo-interpretation skills from Year 9 — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — Part 1, Unit 2, Dr Morgan Tuimaleali'ifano's research methods
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a USP historian rebuilds pre-contact and colonial Pacific history from documented oral traditions, cross-checking myths and legends against family genealogy books, Lands and Titles Court submissions and interviews until the sources agree — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
