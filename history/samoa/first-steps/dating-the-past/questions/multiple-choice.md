# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1, Activity 16 "Time Line"" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Learners build a chronological "history road" from the Mau's founding, the 1914 takeover, independence in 1962 and the cyclone that destroyed the Mau office, down to their own birth year
**Answer: d) Learners build a chronological "history road" from the Mau's founding, the 1914 takeover, independence in 1962 and the cyclone that destroyed the Mau office, down to their own birth year** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Unit 4, census timelines"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Learners build two timelines — censuses from Babylon in 3800 BC through China, Persia, Greece, Egypt, India and Rome, and New Zealand's census story from the 1840 Treaty of Waitangi, the 1851 first census and the Depression and war postponements to 2001  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Learners build two timelines — censuses from Babylon in 3800 BC through China, Persia, Greece, Egypt, India and Rome, and New Zealand's census story from the 1840 Treaty of Waitangi, the 1851 first census and the Depression and war postponements to 2001** — That is what this lesson teaches.
