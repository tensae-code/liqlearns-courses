# Dating the Past — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Learners build a chronological "history road" from the ____ founding, the 1914 takeover, independence in 1962 and the cyclone that destroyed the Mau office, down to their own birth year. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mau's

**2.** Learners build two timelines — censuses from ____ in 3800 BC through China, Persia, Greece, Egypt, India and Rome, and New Zealand's census story from the 1840 Treaty of Waitangi, the 1851 first census and the Depression and war postponements to 2001. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Babylon
