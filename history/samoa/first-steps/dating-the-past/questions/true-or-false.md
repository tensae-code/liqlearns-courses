# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Learners build a chronological "history road" from the Mau's founding, the 1914 takeover, independence in 1962 and the cyclone that destroyed the Mau office, down to their own birth year. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 4, census timelines" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Samoa's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
