# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, Activity 16 "Time Line"" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Learners build a chronological "history road" from the Mau's founding, the 1914 takeover, independence in 1962 and the cyclone that destroyed the Mau office, down to their own birth year.

**2.** What came of "Unit 4, census timelines" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Learners build two timelines — censuses from Babylon in 3800 BC through China, Persia, Greece, Egypt, India and Rome, and New Zealand's census story from the 1840 Treaty of Waitangi, the 1851 first census and the Depression and war postponements to 2001.
