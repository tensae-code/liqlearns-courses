# Vietnam twelve warlords and dinh bo linh — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 9, the Twelve Warlords and Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's unification", the buffalo-herding boy of Hoa Lư who drilled children with reed flags grows into the leader who, in 966–967, by turns soft and hard, defeats or wins over all twelve warlords and founds the Đinh
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam twelve warlords and dinh bo linh as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
