# Vietnam twelve warlords and dinh bo linh — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, the Twelve Warlords and Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's unification" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The buffalo-herding boy of Hoa Lư who drilled children with reed flags grows into the leader who, in 966–967, by turns soft and hard, defeats or wins over all twelve warlords and founds the Đinh.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam twelve warlords and dinh bo linh as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
