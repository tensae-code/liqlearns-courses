# Vietnam ngo quyen bach dang 938 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 8, story of Ngô Quyền's great victory over the Southern Han — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 18, Ngô Quyền and the Bạch Đằng victory of 938 — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) in 938 Ngô Quyền plants iron-tipped stakes under the Bạch Đằng river, lures the Southern Han fleet in at high tide and traps it on the falling tide; Prince Hoằng Tháo is killed and independence is secured
b) the chronicle preserves his order — great iron-tipped stakes under the tide — and the battle itself: Hoằng Tháo's fleet rides the flood in, the ebb strands it on the stakes, and half the invaders drown; the victory ends the Bắc thuộc era for good

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
