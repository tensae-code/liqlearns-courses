# Vietnam ngo quyen bach dang 938 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8, story of Ngô Quyền's great victory over the Southern Han" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 938 Ngô Quyền plants iron-tipped stakes under the Bạch Đằng river, lures the Southern Han fleet in at high tide and traps it on the falling tide; Prince Hoằng Tháo is killed and independence is secured.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 18, Ngô Quyền and the Bạch Đằng victory of 938" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The chronicle preserves his order — great iron-tipped stakes under the tide — and the battle itself: Hoằng Tháo's fleet rides the flood in, the ebb strands it on the stakes, and half the invaders drown; the victory ends the Bắc thuộc era for good.
