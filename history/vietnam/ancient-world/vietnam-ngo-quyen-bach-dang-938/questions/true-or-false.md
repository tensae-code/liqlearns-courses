# Vietnam ngo quyen bach dang 938 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 8, story of Ngô Quyền's great victory over the Southern Han", in 938 Ngô Quyền plants iron-tipped stakes under the Bạch Đằng river, lures the Southern Han fleet in at high tide and traps it on the falling tide; Prince Hoằng Tháo is killed and independence is secured (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 18, Ngô Quyền and the Bạch Đằng victory of 938", the chronicle preserves his order — great iron-tipped stakes under the tide — and the battle itself: Hoằng Tháo's fleet rides the flood in, the ebb strands it on the stakes, and half the invaders drown; the victory ends the Bắc thuộc era for good (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
