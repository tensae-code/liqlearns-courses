## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 8, story of Ngô Quyền's great victory over the Southern Han

In 938 Ngô Quyền plants iron-tipped stakes under the Bạch Đằng river, lures the Southern Han fleet in at high tide and traps it on the falling tide; Prince Hoằng Tháo is killed and independence is secured.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 18, Ngô Quyền and the Bạch Đằng victory of 938

The chronicle preserves his order — great iron-tipped stakes under the tide — and the battle itself: Hoằng Tháo's fleet rides the flood in, the ebb strands it on the stakes, and half the invaders drown; the victory ends the Bắc thuộc era for good.
