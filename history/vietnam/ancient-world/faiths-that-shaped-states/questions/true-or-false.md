# Faiths that shaped states — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 1 Confucianism's Vietnamese career: carried in under northern domination, it gets its temple-school when the Lý build the Văn Miếu in 1070, expands the Quốc Tử Giám under the Trần, reigns alone under the Lê sơ with village schools pushing it downward, receives a new Văn Miếu at the Nguyễn capital of Huế — then collapses with the examination system in the early 1900s, leaving its ethics of filial piety, loyalty and "respect above, yield below" in daily conduct", an imported doctrine rules the state for eight centuries and outlives its own schools as manners (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 1 Islam among the Chăm: entering Vietnam around the 10th century, Islam stands firm in Champa's spiritual life by the late 1400s and survives in two Chăm lines, the orthodox Chăm Islam and the indigenised Chăm Bà-ni, with their mosques and Ramadan rites", the faith of Arabia takes root in a Hindu-Buddhist kingdom and bends to its customs (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
