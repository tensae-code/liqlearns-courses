# Vietnam ba trieu uprising 248 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 16, the uprising of Bà Triệu (248)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Triệu Thị Trinh, 23, leads Thanh Hoá from her Núi Nưa base, riding an elephant into battle, vowing to ride the strong winds and cut down the whales of the Eastern Sea rather than bow as anyone's concubine; Ngô's 8,000 troops end her stand at Núi Tùng.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam ba trieu uprising 248 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
