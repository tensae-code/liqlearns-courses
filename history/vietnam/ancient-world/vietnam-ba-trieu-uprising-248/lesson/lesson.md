## Bài 16, the uprising of Bà Triệu (248)

Triệu Thị Trinh, 23, leads Thanh Hoá from her Núi Nưa base, riding an elephant into battle, vowing to ride the strong winds and cut down the whales of the Eastern Sea rather than bow as anyone's concubine; Ngô's 8,000 troops end her stand at Núi Tùng.
