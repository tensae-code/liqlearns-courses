# Video script — Vietnam the south under chenla

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam the south under chenla.

After Phù Nam falls, the South belongs on paper to Chenla — Lục Chân Lạp ruled directly, Thuỷ Chân Lạp, today's Vietnamese South, left to Phù Nam's royal descendants; climate change salts the fields, people stay sparse into the 14th century, and only centuries later do Viet settlers reclaim the land and found the first villages at Mô Xoài and Đồng Nai.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
