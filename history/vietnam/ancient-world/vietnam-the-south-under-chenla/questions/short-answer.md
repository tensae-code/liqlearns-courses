# Vietnam the south under chenla — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 18, the Nam Bộ region from the 10th to the early 16th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After Phù Nam falls, the South belongs on paper to Chenla — Lục Chân Lạp ruled directly, Thuỷ Chân Lạp, today's Vietnamese South, left to Phù Nam's royal descendants; climate change salts the fields, people stay sparse into the 14th century, and only centuries later do Viet settlers reclaim the land and found the first villages at Mô Xoài and Đồng Nai.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam the south under chenla as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
