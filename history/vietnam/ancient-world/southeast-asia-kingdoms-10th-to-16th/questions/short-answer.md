# Southeast asia kingdoms 10th to 16th — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 6, the kingdoms consolidate, late 10th–early 16th centuries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pagan unifies Myanmar, the Khmer empire flourishes, Srivijaya commands Sumatra; the Mongol invasions of the 13th century push the Thai to unite Sukhothai and Ayutthaya, the Lao to found Lan Xang, and the islands to gather under Majapahit, while Malacca is founded.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 6, Malacca, the world's market, and stronger states" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A quoted account calls 15th-century Malacca perhaps the one place on Earth where Chinese porcelain and silk, Javanese rice, West Asian woollens and Southeast Asian timber, incense and pepper all change hands; meanwhile law codes (Indonesia's Sivasasana, Đại Việt's Hình thư) tighten royal power.
