# Ancient civilisations rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 10, the Roman imperial state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From a small city-state in central Italy, Rome grows into an empire by the 1st century BC; in 27 BC Octavian is raised as Augustus, first emperor, above Senate and a now-formal People's Assembly.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 10, Greek and Roman culture" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Latin alphabet and Roman numerals we still use, Homer's Iliad and Odyssey, Sophocles, Pythagoras and Archimedes, Herodotus the historian, and the Colosseum.
