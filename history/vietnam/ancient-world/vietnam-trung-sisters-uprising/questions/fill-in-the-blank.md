# Vietnam trung sisters uprising — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8, story of Trưng Vương and the Trưng sisters" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Governor Tô Định kills Thi Sách, so his widow Trưng Trắc and her sister Trưng Nhị raise the banner "to repay the country's debt and avenge the house", sweep through Mê Linh, Cổ Loa and Luy Lâu, and Trưng Trắc reigns as queen, 40–43.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 16, the Trưng sisters' uprising in detail" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trưng Trắc swears her oath at Mê Linh ("avenge the country, restore the Hùng line"); in spring AD 40 Hát Môn rises, 65 citadels join, Luy Lâu falls and Tô Định flees; she reigns as queen until Mã Viện crushes the rising in 43.
