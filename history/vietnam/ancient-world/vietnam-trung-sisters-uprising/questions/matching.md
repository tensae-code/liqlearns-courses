# Vietnam trung sisters uprising — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 8, story of Trưng Vương and the Trưng sisters — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 16, the Trưng sisters' uprising in detail — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) governor Tô Định kills Thi Sách, so his widow Trưng Trắc and her sister Trưng Nhị raise the banner "to repay the country's debt and avenge the house", sweep through Mê Linh, Cổ Loa and Luy Lâu, and Trưng Trắc reigns as queen, 40–43
b) trưng Trắc swears her oath at Mê Linh ("avenge the country, restore the Hùng line"); in spring AD 40 Hát Môn rises, 65 citadels join, Luy Lâu falls and Tô Định flees; she reigns as queen until Mã Viện crushes the rising in 43

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
