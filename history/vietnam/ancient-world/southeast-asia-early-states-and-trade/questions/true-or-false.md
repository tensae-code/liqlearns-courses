# Southeast asia early states and trade — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 11, the early states of Southeast Asia", at the crossroads between China and India, the "cradle of wet-rice civilisation" turns bronze, iron, weaving and pottery into its first states from the last centuries BC — Văn Lang – Âu Lạc, Chăm-pa, Phù Nam and their neighbours (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 11, trade written in objects: Óc Eo and the early ports", a Roman gold coin and an Indian Sanskrit-inscribed gold ring dug up at Óc Eo, Han coins in Borneo graves and a Roman-style bronze lamp at Pong Tuk prove these ports traded across the ancient world (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
