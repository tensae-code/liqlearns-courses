# Southeast asia early states and trade — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 11, the early states of Southeast Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** At the crossroads between China and India, the "cradle of wet-rice civilisation" turns bronze, iron, weaving and pottery into its first states from the last centuries BC — Văn Lang – Âu Lạc, Chăm-pa, Phù Nam and their neighbours.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 11, trade written in objects: Óc Eo and the early ports" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A Roman gold coin and an Indian Sanskrit-inscribed gold ring dug up at Óc Eo, Han coins in Borneo graves and a Roman-style bronze lamp at Pong Tuk prove these ports traded across the ancient world.
