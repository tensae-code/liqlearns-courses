# Vietnam dai co viet dinh and tien le — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 10, the Đinh state of Đại Cổ Việt (968) — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 10, the Tiền Lê state and Đinh–Tiền Lê society and culture — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) đinh Bộ Lĩnh takes the imperial title, names the country Đại Cổ Việt and rules from mountain-walled Hoa Lư with a ten-đạo army, his own coinage, stern law and envoys sent to the Song
b) lê Hoàn's Tiền Lê keeps building — provinces reorganised by 1002, a law code issued, a two-part army of palace guards and local troops — in a society of rulers and ruled where Buddhism flourishes, monks are the learned class, and folk singing, wrestling and boat races live on

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
