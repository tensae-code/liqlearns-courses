# Vietnam dai co viet dinh and tien le — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 10, the Đinh state of Đại Cổ Việt (968)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Đinh Bộ Lĩnh takes the imperial title, names the country Đại Cổ Việt and rules from mountain-walled Hoa Lư with a ten-đạo army, his own coinage, stern law and envoys sent to the Song.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 10, the Tiền Lê state and Đinh–Tiền Lê society and culture" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lê Hoàn's Tiền Lê keeps building — provinces reorganised by 1002, a law code issued, a two-part army of palace guards and local troops — in a society of rulers and ruled where Buddhism flourishes, monks are the learned class, and folk singing, wrestling and boat races live on.
