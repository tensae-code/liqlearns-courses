# Vietnam dai co viet dinh and tien le — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 10, the Đinh state of Đại Cổ Việt (968)", đinh Bộ Lĩnh takes the imperial title, names the country Đại Cổ Việt and rules from mountain-walled Hoa Lư with a ten-đạo army, his own coinage, stern law and envoys sent to the Song (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 10, the Tiền Lê state and Đinh–Tiền Lê society and culture", lê Hoàn's Tiền Lê keeps building — provinces reorganised by 1002, a law code issued, a two-part army of palace guards and local troops — in a society of rulers and ruled where Buddhism flourishes, monks are the learned class, and folk singing, wrestling and boat races live on (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
