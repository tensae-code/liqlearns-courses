# Vietnam le so dai viet — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, the founding of the Lê sơ and its state (1428)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lê Lợi takes the throne, restores the name Đại Việt and the capital Thăng Long; the emperor holds all power down a chain of central organs, Đạo/Thừa tuyên, phủ, huyện and xã, and the Quốc triều hình luật (the Hồng Đức code, 722 articles) is praised by a later scholar as "truly a model for governing the country".

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, Lê sơ economy, territory and society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Agriculture commissioners, a ban on leaving fields waste, and the quân điền equal-field system restore the villages ("in Thái Tổ's and Thái Tông's reigns, rice filled the fields and the buffaloes were too full to eat"); export kilns at Chu Đậu and Bát Tràng fill foreign orders — some 240,000 Chu Đậu ceramics were raised from the Cù Lao Chàm shipwreck — and in 1471 the border reaches the Cù Mông pass, while Lê Thánh Tông orders that not one inch of the ancestors' land be yielded.
