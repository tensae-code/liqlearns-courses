# Vietnam bac thuoc resistance timeline — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 8, a thousand years of northern domination and its resistance timeline", after Triệu Đà takes Âu Lạc in 179 BC, rising follows rising — 40, 248, 542, 713, 766, 905 — until Ngô Quyền's Bạch Đằng victory of 938 ends domination and opens lasting independence (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 8 A thousand years of uprisings under northern rule: the Trưng sisters (40–43), Bà Triệu (248), Lý Bí founding Vạn Xuân (542–544) with Triệu Quang Phục's guerrilla victory, Phùng Hưng at Đường Lâm (late 700s), until Khúc Thừa Dụ and Ngô Quyền's 938 Bạch Đằng end the domination", empire after empire must re-conquer the same small country, until the river battle of 938 closes the account (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
