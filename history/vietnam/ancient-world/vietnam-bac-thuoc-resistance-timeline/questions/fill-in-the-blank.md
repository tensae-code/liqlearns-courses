# Vietnam bac thuoc resistance timeline — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8, a thousand years of northern domination and its resistance timeline" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Triệu Đà takes Âu Lạc in 179 BC, rising follows rising — 40, 248, 542, 713, 766, 905 — until Ngô Quyền's Bạch Đằng victory of 938 ends domination and opens lasting independence.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8 A thousand years of uprisings under northern rule: the Trưng sisters (40–43), Bà Triệu (248), Lý Bí founding Vạn Xuân (542–544) with Triệu Quang Phục's guerrilla victory, Phùng Hưng at Đường Lâm (late 700s), until Khúc Thừa Dụ and Ngô Quyền's 938 Bạch Đằng end the domination" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Empire after empire must re-conquer the same small country, until the river battle of 938 closes the account.
