# Vietnam bac thuoc resistance timeline — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 8, a thousand years of northern domination and its resistance timeline" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Triệu Đà takes Âu Lạc in 179 BC, rising follows rising — 40, 248, 542, 713, 766, 905 — until Ngô Quyền's Bạch Đằng victory of 938 ends domination and opens lasting independence.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 8 A thousand years of uprisings under northern rule: the Trưng sisters (40–43), Bà Triệu (248), Lý Bí founding Vạn Xuân (542–544) with Triệu Quang Phục's guerrilla victory, Phùng Hưng at Đường Lâm (late 700s), until Khúc Thừa Dụ and Ngô Quyền's 938 Bạch Đằng end the domination" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Empire after empire must re-conquer the same small country, until the river battle of 938 closes the account.
