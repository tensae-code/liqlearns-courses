# Vietnam later le and le thanh tong — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 12, the Later Lê dynasty and King Lê Thánh Tông", lê Lợi founds the dynasty in 1428 at Đông Kinh; under Lê Thánh Tông (1460–1497) come reforms, the Hồng Đức map, the national criminal code, regular examinations and stelae at the Văn Miếu — and a warning that not one inch of the founder's land may be given away (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 12, story of Trạng Lường Lương Thế Vinh", the maths prodigy weighs a Ming envoy's elephant by marking a boat's waterline and swapping the elephant for stones, then writes the Đại thành toán pháp, the age's great maths book (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
