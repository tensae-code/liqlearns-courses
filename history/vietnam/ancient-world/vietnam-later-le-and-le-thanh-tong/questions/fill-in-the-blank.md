# Vietnam later le and le thanh tong — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, the Later Lê dynasty and King Lê Thánh Tông" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lê Lợi founds the dynasty in 1428 at Đông Kinh; under Lê Thánh Tông (1460–1497) come reforms, the Hồng Đức map, the national criminal code, regular examinations and stelae at the Văn Miếu — and a warning that not one inch of the founder's land may be given away.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, story of Trạng Lường Lương Thế Vinh" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The maths prodigy weighs a Ming envoy's elephant by marking a boat's waterline and swapping the elephant for stones, then writes the Đại thành toán pháp, the age's great maths book.
