# Vietnam later le and le thanh tong — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 12, the Later Lê dynasty and King Lê Thánh Tông — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 12, story of Trạng Lường Lương Thế Vinh — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) lê Lợi founds the dynasty in 1428 at Đông Kinh; under Lê Thánh Tông (1460–1497) come reforms, the Hồng Đức map, the national criminal code, regular examinations and stelae at the Văn Miếu — and a warning that not one inch of the founder's land may be given away
b) the maths prodigy weighs a Ming envoy's elephant by marking a boat's waterline and swapping the elephant for stones, then writes the Đại thành toán pháp, the age's great maths book

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
