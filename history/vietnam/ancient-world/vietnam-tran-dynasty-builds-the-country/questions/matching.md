# Vietnam tran dynasty builds the country — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 10, the Trần dynasty builds the country — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 13, the founding of the Trần (1226) and its government — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) founded in 1226, the Trần keep retired emperors (Thượng hoàng) guiding young kings — Nhân Tông rides back to court, finds his son drunk and asleep, and teaches him the throne is not his to neglect; exams and schools produce the dynasty's talents
b) the fishing clan Trần gathers power behind a failing Lý throne until, in January 1226, the last Lý ruler Lý Chiêu Hoàng is made to abdicate to her husband Trần Cảnh; the dynasty rules through retired emperors (Thượng hoàng), clan endogamy, the Quốc triều hình luật, and new offices from the National History Institute to river-dyke commissioners

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
