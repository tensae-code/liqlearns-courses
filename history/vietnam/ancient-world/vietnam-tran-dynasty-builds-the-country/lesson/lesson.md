# Vietnam tran dynasty builds the country

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam tran dynasty builds the country" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 10, the Trần dynasty builds the country

Founded in 1226, the Trần keep retired emperors (Thượng hoàng) guiding young kings — Nhân Tông rides back to court, finds his son drunk and asleep, and teaches him the throne is not his to neglect; exams and schools produce the dynasty's talents.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 13, the founding of the Trần (1226) and its government

The fishing clan Trần gathers power behind a failing Lý throne until, in January 1226, the last Lý ruler Lý Chiêu Hoàng is made to abdicate to her husband Trần Cảnh; the dynasty rules through retired emperors (Thượng hoàng), clan endogamy, the Quốc triều hình luật, and new offices from the National History Institute to river-dyke commissioners.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 13, Trần economy and society

Reclamation, flood dykes and tax remissions revive farming; princes build great estates (điền trang), craft villages and guilds multiply, and Vân Đồn and Hội Thống ports crowd with foreign ships, while more peasants sink into tenancy and estate slavery.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 13, Trần culture, education and science

All three teachings are respected, but Confucian scholars rise and Trần Nhân Tông founds the Trúc Lâm Zen school; examinations run every seven years from 1232 with the Tam khôi titles; Lê Văn Hưu writes the first national history, Trần Quốc Tuấn his military treatises, and Tuệ Tĩnh his herbal medicine.
