# The maya — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2 The Maya of Central America: plaster reliefs on walls, finely crafted statues and murals that still show the community's beliefs and way of life" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A jungle civilisation writes its faith and daily life on its walls, and the walls outlive the cities.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of The maya as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
