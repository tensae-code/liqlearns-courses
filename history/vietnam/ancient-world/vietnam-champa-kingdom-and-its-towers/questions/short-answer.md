# Vietnam champa kingdom and its towers — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 7, the Chăm-pa kingdom and its temple towers" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Founded in the late 2nd century, Chăm-pa leaves brick towers down the central coast, above all the 70-plus towers of the Mỹ Sơn sanctuary in its sacred valley.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 7, stories of the Chăm towers (Pô Klong Ga-rai; Tháp Bà Pô Na-ga)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Scab-covered boy Po Ong is healed by two white dragons, becomes a king who builds irrigation works, and gets a tower in his honour; goddess Thiên Y A Na steps out of a drifting agarwood log to teach farming and weaving.
