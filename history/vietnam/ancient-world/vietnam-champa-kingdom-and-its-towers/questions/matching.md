# Vietnam champa kingdom and its towers — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 7, the Chăm-pa kingdom and its temple towers — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 7, stories of the Chăm towers (Pô Klong Ga-rai; Tháp Bà Pô Na-ga) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) founded in the late 2nd century, Chăm-pa leaves brick towers down the central coast, above all the 70-plus towers of the Mỹ Sơn sanctuary in its sacred valley
b) scab-covered boy Po Ong is healed by two white dragons, becomes a king who builds irrigation works, and gets a tower in his honour; goddess Thiên Y A Na steps out of a drifting agarwood log to teach farming and weaving

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
