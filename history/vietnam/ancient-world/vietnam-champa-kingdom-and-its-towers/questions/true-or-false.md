# Vietnam champa kingdom and its towers — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 7, the Chăm-pa kingdom and its temple towers", founded in the late 2nd century, Chăm-pa leaves brick towers down the central coast, above all the 70-plus towers of the Mỹ Sơn sanctuary in its sacred valley (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 7, stories of the Chăm towers (Pô Klong Ga-rai; Tháp Bà Pô Na-ga)", scab-covered boy Po Ong is healed by two white dragons, becomes a king who builds irrigation works, and gets a tower in his honour; goddess Thiên Y A Na steps out of a drifting agarwood log to teach farming and weaving (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
