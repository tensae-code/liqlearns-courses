# Vietnam under northern domination — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 15, how Âu Lạc changed under a thousand years of rule — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) triệu Đà annexes the country in 179 BC; châu, quận and huyện replace its order, Han officials rule down to district level after AD 43, land is seized into estates, taxes and tribute (pearls, ivory, spices) flow north, and iron and salt are monopolised
b) dykes and great rice regions spread, crafts grow (paper-making learned from China, glass, fine pottery at Luy Lâu), some Han officials are Vietnamised, local hào trưởng become an aristocracy — and the contradiction between people and regime sharpens

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
