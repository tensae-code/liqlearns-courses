# Vietnam under northern domination — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc", triệu Đà annexes the country in 179 BC; châu, quận and huyện replace its order, Han officials rule down to district level after AD 43, land is seized into estates, taxes and tribute (pearls, ivory, spices) flow north, and iron and salt are monopolised (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 15, how Âu Lạc changed under a thousand years of rule", dykes and great rice regions spread, crafts grow (paper-making learned from China, glass, fine pottery at Luy Lâu), some Han officials are Vietnamised, local hào trưởng become an aristocracy — and the contradiction between people and regime sharpens (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
