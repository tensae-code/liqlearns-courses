# Vietnam ly dynasty builds the country — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 9, the Lý dynasty builds and defends the country, with the story of Nguyên phi Ỷ Lan — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 11, the founding of the Lý and the move to Thăng Long (1010) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) over 200 years the Lý complete the state and encourage farming; a girl glimpsed leaning on an orchid bush at Dâu pagoda becomes Consort Ỷ Lan, regent who helps plan the resistance of 1076
b) the orphan Lý Công Uẩn, raised by the monk Vạn Hạnh, is enthroned in 1009; his Edict on Moving the Capital praises Đại La's dragon-coiling, tiger-crouching site, legend sees a golden dragon rise where his boat lands, and the new capital is named Thăng Long, the Rising Dragon

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
