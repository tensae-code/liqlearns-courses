# Vietnam ly dynasty builds the country — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, the Lý dynasty builds and defends the country, with the story of Nguyên phi Ỷ Lan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Over 200 years the Lý complete the state and encourage farming; a girl glimpsed leaning on an orchid bush at Dâu pagoda becomes Consort Ỷ Lan, regent who helps plan the resistance of 1076.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 11, the founding of the Lý and the move to Thăng Long (1010)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The orphan Lý Công Uẩn, raised by the monk Vạn Hạnh, is enthroned in 1009; his Edict on Moving the Capital praises Đại La's dragon-coiling, tiger-crouching site, legend sees a golden dragon rise where his boat lands, and the new capital is named Thăng Long, the Rising Dragon.
