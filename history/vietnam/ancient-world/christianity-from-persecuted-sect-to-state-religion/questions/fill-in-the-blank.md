# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 1, the birth of Christianity" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The faith of the poor and oppressed arises in Jerusalem at the start of the Common Era and by the 4th century has become the Roman Empire's state religion, the medieval Church its heir.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5.1, the birth of Christianity inside the Roman Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Christianity (Cơ Đốc giáo/Ki-tô giáo) is formed in the 1st century CE in the eastern territory of the Roman Empire, in the setting of the struggles of slaves and the poor against Roman oppression; by tradition its founder is Jesus, born at Nazareth (in today's Israel); at first the Roman ruling class seeks to destroy the new religion, but it wins more and more followers and spreads widely, and by the early 4th century the Roman authorities stop suppressing it and recognise Christianity as the official religion of the empire; the Greeks and Romans before it worship many gods, holding festivals, sacrifices and prayers to honour them, their gods depicted in human form with human characters.
