# Ancient civilisations china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 18, Qin Shi Huang's tomb and the terracotta army" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Thousands of life-size terracotta soldiers stand guard over the First Emperor's mausoleum, shown among China's signature ancient works.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 9, ancient China: rivers and dynasties" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On the Yellow River and Yangtze, the Xia (21st–16th centuries BC) yield to Shang, then Zhou; the Warring States end when Qin unifies China in 221 BC.
