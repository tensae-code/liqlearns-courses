# Vietnam van lang au lac and dong son civilisation — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 11, achievements of the Red River civilisation", about 2,700 years ago the first Vietnamese civilisation raises the states of Văn Lang and Âu Lạc, casts the Đông Sơn bronze drums and rings Cổ Loa citadel with three walls under An Dương Vương (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 11, the life of the ancient Việt read from the Ngọc Lũ drum", pictures cast on a bronze drum show stilt houses, boats, rice-pounding and feathered dancers, so children reconstruct a whole way of life from one object (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
