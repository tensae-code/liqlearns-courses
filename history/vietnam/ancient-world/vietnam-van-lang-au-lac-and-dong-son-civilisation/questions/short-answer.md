# Vietnam van lang au lac and dong son civilisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 11, achievements of the Red River civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** About 2,700 years ago the first Vietnamese civilisation raises the states of Văn Lang and Âu Lạc, casts the Đông Sơn bronze drums and rings Cổ Loa citadel with three walls under An Dương Vương.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 11, the life of the ancient Việt read from the Ngọc Lũ drum" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pictures cast on a bronze drum show stilt houses, boats, rice-pounding and feathered dancers, so children reconstruct a whole way of life from one object.
