# Vietnam van lang au lac and dong son civilisation — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 11, achievements of the Red River civilisation — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 11, the life of the ancient Việt read from the Ngọc Lũ drum — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) about 2,700 years ago the first Vietnamese civilisation raises the states of Văn Lang and Âu Lạc, casts the Đông Sơn bronze drums and rings Cổ Loa citadel with three walls under An Dương Vương
