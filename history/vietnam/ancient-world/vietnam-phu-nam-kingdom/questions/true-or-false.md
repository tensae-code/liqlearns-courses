# Vietnam phu nam kingdom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 6, the founding of the Phù Nam kingdom and the Hỗn Điền – Liễu Diệp legend", a god promises Hỗn Điền (Kaundinya) a kingdom in a dream; he sails south, his arrow pierces Queen Liễu Diệp's boat, she surrenders, they marry and rule Phù Nam in the Mekong delta (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 6, Phù Nam's archaeological remains", house-post holes at Nền Chùa, foundations at Gò Cây Thị, metal coins, clay cà ràng stoves still used today, gold earrings and the Bình Hoà wooden Buddha rebuild a rich trading kingdom from the ground (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
