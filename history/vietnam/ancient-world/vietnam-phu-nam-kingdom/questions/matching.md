# Vietnam phu nam kingdom — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 6, the founding of the Phù Nam kingdom and the Hỗn Điền – Liễu Diệp legend — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 6, Phù Nam's archaeological remains — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) a god promises Hỗn Điền (Kaundinya) a kingdom in a dream; he sails south, his arrow pierces Queen Liễu Diệp's boat, she surrenders, they marry and rule Phù Nam in the Mekong delta
b) house-post holes at Nền Chùa, foundations at Gò Cây Thị, metal coins, clay cà ràng stoves still used today, gold earrings and the Bình Hoà wooden Buddha rebuild a rich trading kingdom from the ground

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
