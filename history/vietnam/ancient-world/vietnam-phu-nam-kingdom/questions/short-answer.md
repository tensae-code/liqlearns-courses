# Vietnam phu nam kingdom — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 6, the founding of the Phù Nam kingdom and the Hỗn Điền – Liễu Diệp legend" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A god promises Hỗn Điền (Kaundinya) a kingdom in a dream; he sails south, his arrow pierces Queen Liễu Diệp's boat, she surrenders, they marry and rule Phù Nam in the Mekong delta.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 6, Phù Nam's archaeological remains" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** House-post holes at Nền Chùa, foundations at Gò Cây Thị, metal coins, clay cà ràng stoves still used today, gold earrings and the Bình Hoà wooden Buddha rebuild a rich trading kingdom from the ground.
