# Vietnam tran dynasty defeats the mongol yuan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 10, the resistance against the Mongol–Yuan invasions" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Three invasions (1258, 1285, 1287–88) are met by Trần Thủ Độ's "my head has not yet fallen — do not worry", the elders of Diên Hồng shouting "Fight!", and young Trần Quốc Toản, barred from the war council, crushing an orange in his fist and raising his own flag.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 14, the first resistance, 1258" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Mongols' three envoys are seized at Thăng Long; 30,000 men invade from Yunnan; the court empties the capital ("empty gardens, empty houses"), Trần Thủ Độ tells the anxious king "my head has not yet fallen to the ground — Your Majesty need not worry", and the counterstroke at Đông Bộ Đầu frees Thăng Long within the month.
