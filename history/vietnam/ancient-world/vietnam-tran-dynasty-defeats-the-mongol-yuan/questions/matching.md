# Vietnam tran dynasty defeats the mongol yuan — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 10, the resistance against the Mongol–Yuan invasions — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 14, the first resistance, 1258 — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) three invasions (1258, 1285, 1287–88) are met by Trần Thủ Độ's "my head has not yet fallen — do not worry", the elders of Diên Hồng shouting "Fight!", and young Trần Quốc Toản, barred from the war council, crushing an orange in his fist and raising his own flag
b) the Mongols' three envoys are seized at Thăng Long; 30,000 men invade from Yunnan; the court empties the capital ("empty gardens, empty houses"), Trần Thủ Độ tells the anxious king "my head has not yet fallen to the ground — Your Majesty need not worry", and the counterstroke at Đông Bộ Đầu frees Thăng Long within the month

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
