# Vietnam lam son uprising — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 12, the Lam Sơn uprising, 1418–1427 — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 12, story of Lê Lai giving his life for his lord — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) at the 1416 Lũng Nhai oath Lê Lợi and 18 heroes swear together; in 1418 he raises the banner as Bình Định Vương and, with Nguyễn Trãi's strategy of winning hearts, fights the Ming for ten years to complete victory
b) besieged on Chí Linh mountain in 1418, Lê Lai puts on Lê Lợi's robe, charges out and is killed in his place; the saying runs "the 21st for Lê Lai, the 22nd for Lê Lợi"

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
