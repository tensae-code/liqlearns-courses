# Vietnam lam son uprising — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, the Lam Sơn uprising, 1418–1427" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** At the 1416 Lũng Nhai oath Lê Lợi and 18 heroes swear together; in 1418 he raises the banner as Bình Định Vương and, with Nguyễn Trãi's strategy of winning hearts, fights the Ming for ten years to complete victory.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, story of Lê Lai giving his life for his lord" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Besieged on Chí Linh mountain in 1418, Lê Lai puts on Lê Lợi's robe, charges out and is killed in his place; the saying runs "the 21st for Lê Lai, the 22nd for Lê Lợi".
