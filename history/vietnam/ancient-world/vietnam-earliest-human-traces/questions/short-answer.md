# Vietnam earliest human traces — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 4, traces of early humans in Southeast Asia and Vietnam" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Java fossils two million years old, the Niah skull, fossil teeth at Thẩm Khuyên cave and stone tools at An Khê some 800,000 years old — Vietnam's oldest human trace.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam earliest human traces as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
