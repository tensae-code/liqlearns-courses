# Video script — Cities and ancient civilisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Cities and ancient civilisations.

On the Nile, Tigris–Euphrates, Indus and Ganges, farming villages of 5,000–6,000 years ago swell into cities — Uruk with 40,000–50,000 people, Babylon, Memphis, Mohenjo-daro — each civilisation's administrative, military and trading heart, rising and falling with it; Greek and Roman cities grow instead from harbours, mines and trade, Athens's Piraeus the ancient world's greatest port.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
