# Cities and ancient civilisations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Shared Theme 2 (in the Geography part), cities and the ancient civilisations" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** On the Nile, Tigris–Euphrates, Indus and Ganges, farming villages of 5,000–6,000 years ago swell into cities — Uruk with 40,000–50,000 people, Babylon, Memphis, Mohenjo-daro — each civilisation's administrative, military and trading heart, rising and falling with it; Greek and Roman cities grow instead from harbours, mines and trade, Athens's Piraeus the ancient world's greatest port.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Cities and ancient civilisations as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
