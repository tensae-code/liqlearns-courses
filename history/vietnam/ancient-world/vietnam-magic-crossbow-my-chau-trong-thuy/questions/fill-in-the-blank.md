# Vietnam magic crossbow my chau trong thuy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5, the tale of the magic crossbow (Sự tích nỏ thần)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Golden Tortoise god helps An Dương Vương build Cổ Loa and gives a claw for his crossbow trigger; enemy prince Trọng Thuỷ marries Princess Mỵ Châu, steals the trigger's secret, and Âu Lạc falls.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam magic crossbow my chau trong thuy as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
