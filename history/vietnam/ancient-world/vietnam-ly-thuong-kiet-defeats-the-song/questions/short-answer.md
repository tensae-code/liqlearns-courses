# Vietnam ly thuong kiet defeats the song — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 9, story of Lý Thường Kiệt and the war against the Song" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Refusing to "sit idle and wait", he strikes Song bases first, holds the Như Nguyệt river line in 1076, counterattacks in 1077 as the poem Nam quốc sơn hà is read out to the troops, then offers peace and the Song withdraw.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 12, the pre-emptive strike of 1075" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lý Thường Kiệt (1019–1105, born Ngô Tuấn) judges that "sitting and waiting for the enemy is not as good as striking first to blunt their strength"; in October 1075 he leads over 100,000 men by water and land into Song territory, and in 42 days takes Yongzhou, burns its stores and withdraws.
