# Vietnam ly thuong kiet defeats the song

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam ly thuong kiet defeats the song" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 9, story of Lý Thường Kiệt and the war against the Song

Refusing to "sit idle and wait", he strikes Song bases first, holds the Như Nguyệt river line in 1076, counterattacks in 1077 as the poem Nam quốc sơn hà is read out to the troops, then offers peace and the Song withdraw.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 12, the pre-emptive strike of 1075

Lý Thường Kiệt (1019–1105, born Ngô Tuấn) judges that "sitting and waiting for the enemy is not as good as striking first to blunt their strength"; in October 1075 he leads over 100,000 men by water and land into Song territory, and in 42 days takes Yongzhou, burns its stores and withdraws.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 12, the Như Nguyệt river line and the battle of 1077

An earthen wall like a city rampart, tens of kilometres long and staked with bamboo, holds the Song land army while the navy is checked in the north-east; by night the poem Nam quốc sơn hà rings from a riverside temple, and in late spring 1077 the Lý strike across the river into the Song camp.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 12, how Lý Thường Kiệt ended the war

With the invaders exhausted he offers them an honourable way out — in the historians' summary, it "does not tire our generals, costs no blood, yet preserves the ancestral temples"; Quách Quỳ accepts and hurries home.
