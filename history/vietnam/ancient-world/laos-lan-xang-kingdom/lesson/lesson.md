# Laos lan xang kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Laos lan xang kingdom" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 7, the founding of Lan Xang (1353)

Legend says Pha Ngừm is raised at the Cambodian court, married to its princess, and returns with an army to unite the Lao mường; in 1353 he founds Lan Xang, "the Million Elephants".

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 7, Lan Xang at its height and Lao culture

Seven provinces under king, vice-king and seven great officials; Viet traders swap salt, fish sauce and ironware for Lao rice, wax and buffalo (Lê Quý Đôn's account); three Burmese invasions are beaten off; the Lao make their own script, their dances, and the That Luang.
