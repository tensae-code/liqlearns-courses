# Laos lan xang kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, the founding of Lan Xang (1353)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Legend says Pha Ngừm is raised at the Cambodian court, married to its princess, and returns with an army to unite the Lao mường; in 1353 he founds Lan Xang, "the Million Elephants".

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, Lan Xang at its height and Lao culture" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Seven provinces under king, vice-king and seven great officials; Viet traders swap salt, fish sauce and ironware for Lao rice, wax and buffalo (Lê Quý Đôn's account); three Burmese invasions are beaten off; the Lao make their own script, their dances, and the That Luang.
