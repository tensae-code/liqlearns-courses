# Laos lan xang kingdom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 7, the founding of Lan Xang (1353)", legend says Pha Ngừm is raised at the Cambodian court, married to its princess, and returns with an army to unite the Lao mường; in 1353 he founds Lan Xang, "the Million Elephants" (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 7, Lan Xang at its height and Lao culture", seven provinces under king, vice-king and seven great officials; Viet traders swap salt, fish sauce and ironware for Lao rice, wax and buffalo (Lê Quý Đôn's account); three Burmese invasions are beaten off; the Lao make their own script, their dances, and the That Luang (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
