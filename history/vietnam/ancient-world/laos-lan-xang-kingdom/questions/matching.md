# Laos lan xang kingdom — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 7, the founding of Lan Xang (1353) — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 7, Lan Xang at its height and Lao culture — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) legend says Pha Ngừm is raised at the Cambodian court, married to its princess, and returns with an army to unite the Lao mường; in 1353 he founds Lan Xang, "the Million Elephants"
b) seven provinces under king, vice-king and seven great officials; Viet traders swap salt, fish sauce and ironware for Lao rice, wax and buffalo (Lê Quý Đôn's account); three Burmese invasions are beaten off; the Lao make their own script, their dances, and the That Luang

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
