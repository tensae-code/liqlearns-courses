# Vietnam khuc thua du and autonomy — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 18, the Khúc family builds autonomy and Dương Đình Nghệ defends it — a)
**2.** The one thing this lesson teaches — b)

Meanings:
a) as the Tang fade, Khúc Thừa Dụ takes Đại La in 905; his son Khúc Hạo reforms taxes, registers and corvée under the motto of a lenient, simple government; in 931 Dương Đình Nghệ drives out the Southern Han and keeps the autonomy
b) the story of Vietnam khuc thua du and autonomy as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
