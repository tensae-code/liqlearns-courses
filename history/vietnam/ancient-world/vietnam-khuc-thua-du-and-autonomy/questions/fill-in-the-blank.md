# Vietnam khuc thua du and autonomy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 18, the Khúc family builds autonomy and Dương Đình Nghệ defends it" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** As the Tang fade, Khúc Thừa Dụ takes Đại La in 905; his son Khúc Hạo reforms taxes, registers and corvée under the motto of a lenient, simple government; in 931 Dương Đình Nghệ drives out the Southern Han and keeps the autonomy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam khuc thua du and autonomy as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
