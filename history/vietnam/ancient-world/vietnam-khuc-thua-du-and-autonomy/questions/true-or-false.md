# Vietnam khuc thua du and autonomy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 18, the Khúc family builds autonomy and Dương Đình Nghệ defends it", as the Tang fade, Khúc Thừa Dụ takes Đại La in 905; his son Khúc Hạo reforms taxes, registers and corvée under the motto of a lenient, simple government; in 931 Dương Đình Nghệ drives out the Southern Han and keeps the autonomy
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam khuc thua du and autonomy as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
