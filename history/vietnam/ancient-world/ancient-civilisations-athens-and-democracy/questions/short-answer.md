# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 25, story of Athena, patron goddess of Athens" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Poseidon strikes a salt spring from the rock, but Athena's gift of the olive tree — peace and prosperity — wins her the city that takes her name.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 10, Athens and ancient democracy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The People's Assembly of all male citizens over 18 decides everything, electing the Council of 500 and the ten generals — and can vote by potsherd to exile any citizen for ten years (ostracism).
