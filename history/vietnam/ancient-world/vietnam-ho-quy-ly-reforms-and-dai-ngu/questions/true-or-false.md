# Vietnam ho quy ly reforms and dai ngu — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 15, Hồ Quý Ly's reforms and the state of Đại Ngu (1400–1407)", as the Trần rot (Chu Văn An's petition to behead seven corrupt officials is refused and he retires to teach), Hồ Quý Ly seizes power, renames the country Đại Ngu, caps land and slave holdings, issues paper money, builds the stone citadel of Tây Đô in three months, casts great cannon, and promotes chữ Nôm and examinations (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 15, the fall of the Hồ to the Ming, 1407", the Ming invade on the pretext of the usurpation; Đa Bang and Đông Đô fall in January 1407 and Tây Đô by June; Hồ Nguyên Trừng's verdict is quoted — "I do not fear fighting; I fear the people's hearts do not follow" — for the Hồ had trusted fortresses and a regular army and shut themselves into passive defence (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
