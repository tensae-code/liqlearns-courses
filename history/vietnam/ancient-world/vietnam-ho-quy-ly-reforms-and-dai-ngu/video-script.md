# Video script — Vietnam ho quy ly reforms and dai ngu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam ho quy ly reforms and dai ngu.

As the Trần rot (Chu Văn An's petition to behead seven corrupt officials is refused and he retires to teach), Hồ Quý Ly seizes power, renames the country Đại Ngu, caps land and slave holdings, issues paper money, builds the stone citadel of Tây Đô in three months, casts great cannon, and promotes chữ Nôm and examinations.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
