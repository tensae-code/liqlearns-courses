# Vietnam tran scholars nguyen hien and chu van an — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 10, stories of Nguyễn Hiền and Chu Văn An", nguyễn Hiền becomes Vietnam's youngest trạng nguyên at 13 and out-verses a Yuan envoy; teacher Chu Văn An resigns after his "memorial of seven beheadings" against corrupt officials is ignored, and is worshipped at the Văn Miếu
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam tran scholars nguyen hien and chu van an as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
