# Vietnam tran scholars nguyen hien and chu van an — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 10, stories of Nguyễn Hiền and Chu Văn An" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Nguyễn Hiền becomes Vietnam's youngest trạng nguyên at 13 and out-verses a Yuan envoy; teacher Chu Văn An resigns after his "memorial of seven beheadings" against corrupt officials is ignored, and is worshipped at the Văn Miếu.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam tran scholars nguyen hien and chu van an as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
