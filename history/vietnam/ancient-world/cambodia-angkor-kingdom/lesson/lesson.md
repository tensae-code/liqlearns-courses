## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 8, Cambodia from Chenla to the Angkor empire

In 802 Jayavarman II unifies the Khmer and renames the country Cambodia; Jayavarman VII builds roads, rest houses and hospitals across the realm, its Baray reservoirs water the fields, and in 1190 Cambodia conquers Chăm-pa itself — before faction and Thai attacks force the capital south to Phnom Penh in the 15th century.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 8, Angkor's culture

On a script created in the 7th century grows a literature of myth and verse; Angkor Wat models Mount Meru with a 65 m central tower, its sandstone blocks fitted without mortar.
