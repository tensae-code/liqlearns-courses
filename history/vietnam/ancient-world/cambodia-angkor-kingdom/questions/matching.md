# Cambodia angkor kingdom — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 8, Cambodia from Chenla to the Angkor empire — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 8, Angkor's culture — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) in 802 Jayavarman II unifies the Khmer and renames the country Cambodia; Jayavarman VII builds roads, rest houses and hospitals across the realm, its Baray reservoirs water the fields, and in 1190 Cambodia conquers Chăm-pa itself — before faction and Thai attacks force the capital south to Phnom Penh in the 15th century
b) on a script created in the 7th century grows a literature of myth and verse; Angkor Wat models Mount Meru with a 65 m central tower, its sandstone blocks fitted without mortar

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
