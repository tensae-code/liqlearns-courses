# Cambodia angkor kingdom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 8, Cambodia from Chenla to the Angkor empire", in 802 Jayavarman II unifies the Khmer and renames the country Cambodia; Jayavarman VII builds roads, rest houses and hospitals across the realm, its Baray reservoirs water the fields, and in 1190 Cambodia conquers Chăm-pa itself — before faction and Thai attacks force the capital south to Phnom Penh in the 15th century (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 8, Angkor's culture", on a script created in the 7th century grows a literature of myth and verse; Angkor Wat models Mount Meru with a 65 m central tower, its sandstone blocks fitted without mortar (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
