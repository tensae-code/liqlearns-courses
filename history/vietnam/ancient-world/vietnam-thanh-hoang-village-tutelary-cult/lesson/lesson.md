## Chuyên đề 1 The village tutelary god (Thành hoàng): born under northern domination, the cult makes every village's guardian a mirror of its memory — Lý–Trần kings enfeoff the capital's guardian as a great king, the Lê sơ court builds state altars beside the folk gods of wind, cloud, rain and thunder, and villages enthrone national heroes (the Trưng sisters, Bà Triệu), craft founders (Bát Tràng's potters' ancestor) and land-reclaimers (Thoại Ngọc Hầu in An Giang)

A village chooses its own god, and the choice is its history book.
