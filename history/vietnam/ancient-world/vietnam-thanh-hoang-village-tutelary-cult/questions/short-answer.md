# Vietnam thanh hoang village tutelary cult — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Chuyên đề 1 The village tutelary god (Thành hoàng): born under northern domination, the cult makes every village's guardian a mirror of its memory — Lý–Trần kings enfeoff the capital's guardian as a great king, the Lê sơ court builds state altars beside the folk gods of wind, cloud, rain and thunder, and villages enthrone national heroes (the Trưng sisters, Bà Triệu), craft founders (Bát Tràng's potters' ancestor) and land-reclaimers (Thoại Ngọc Hầu in An Giang)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A village chooses its own god, and the choice is its history book.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam thanh hoang village tutelary cult as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
