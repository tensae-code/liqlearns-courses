# Southeast asia feudal kingdoms 7th to 10th — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, the feudal kingdoms form, 7th–10th centuries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Đại Cổ Việt of the Viet, the Mon kingdoms Sri Ksetra and Dvaravati, Burmese Pagan, Khmer Chenla, Malay Srivijaya — the "Golden Island" of gold, silver and glass — and Indonesian Kalinga take shape.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, their economies: rice kingdoms and the spice route" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mainland kingdoms farm while Srivijaya, Kalinga and Mataram live by the sea; an Arab geographer's praise of Srivijaya's aromatics and a 10th-century price — a measure of turmeric for a horse, of ginger for a cow — show the pull of Southeast Asian spice.
