# Southeast asia feudal kingdoms 7th to 10th — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 12, the feudal kingdoms form, 7th–10th centuries", đại Cổ Việt of the Viet, the Mon kingdoms Sri Ksetra and Dvaravati, Burmese Pagan, Khmer Chenla, Malay Srivijaya — the "Golden Island" of gold, silver and glass — and Indonesian Kalinga take shape (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 12, their economies: rice kingdoms and the spice route", mainland kingdoms farm while Srivijaya, Kalinga and Mataram live by the sea; an Arab geographer's praise of Srivijaya's aromatics and a 10th-century price — a measure of turmeric for a horse, of ginger for a cow — show the pull of Southeast Asian spice (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
