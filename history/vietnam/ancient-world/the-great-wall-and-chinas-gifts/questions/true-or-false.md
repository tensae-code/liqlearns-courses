# The great wall and chinas gifts — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 18, the Great Wall of China and the story of Mạnh Khương (Meng Jiangnü)", qin Shi Huang drives over two million people to build the Wall; Mạnh Khương waits ten years for her husband, walks the Wall's length searching, and her weeping cracks the wall open over his bones (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 4, the culture of feudal China", confucianism rules thought, Buddhism prints the Diamond Sutra (868, the world's oldest printed book), Tang poetry and the Four Great Novels flourish, and the Forbidden City rises — rebuilt under the Ming by Nguyễn An, a Vietnamese architect carried off in 1407 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
