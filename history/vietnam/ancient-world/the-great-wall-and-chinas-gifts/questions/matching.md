# The great wall and chinas gifts — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 18, the Great Wall of China and the story of Mạnh Khương (Meng Jiangnü) — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 4, the culture of feudal China — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) qin Shi Huang drives over two million people to build the Wall; Mạnh Khương waits ten years for her husband, walks the Wall's length searching, and her weeping cracks the wall open over his bones
b) confucianism rules thought, Buddhism prints the Diamond Sutra (868, the world's oldest printed book), Tang poetry and the Four Great Novels flourish, and the Forbidden City rises — rebuilt under the Ming by Nguyễn An, a Vietnamese architect carried off in 1407

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
