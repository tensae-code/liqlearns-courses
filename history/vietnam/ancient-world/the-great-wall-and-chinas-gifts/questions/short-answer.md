# The great wall and chinas gifts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 18, the Great Wall of China and the story of Mạnh Khương (Meng Jiangnü)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Qin Shi Huang drives over two million people to build the Wall; Mạnh Khương waits ten years for her husband, walks the Wall's length searching, and her weeping cracks the wall open over his bones.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 4, the culture of feudal China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Confucianism rules thought, Buddhism prints the Diamond Sutra (868, the world's oldest printed book), Tang poetry and the Four Great Novels flourish, and the Forbidden City rises — rebuilt under the Ming by Nguyễn An, a Vietnamese architect carried off in 1407.
