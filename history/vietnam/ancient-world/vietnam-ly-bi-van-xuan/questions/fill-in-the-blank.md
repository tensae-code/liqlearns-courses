# Vietnam ly bi van xuan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8, story of Lý Bí and the Vạn Xuân state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Under the harsh Liang, local leader Lý Bí rises in the 6th century, the occupiers flee, and in spring 544 he takes the throne as Lý Nam Đế of a state named Vạn Xuân, "ten thousand springs".

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 16, Lý Bí's uprising and the state of Vạn Xuân" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In 542 Lý Bí throws off the Lương; in 544 he proclaims himself Lý Nam Đế of Vạn Xuân, building the Vạn Thọ palace and Khai Quốc pagoda; Triệu Quang Phục holds out in the Dạ Trạch swamp and reigns as Triệu Việt Vương, until the Sui end Vạn Xuân in 602.
