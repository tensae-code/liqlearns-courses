# Vietnam ly bi van xuan — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 8, story of Lý Bí and the Vạn Xuân state — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 16, Lý Bí's uprising and the state of Vạn Xuân — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) under the harsh Liang, local leader Lý Bí rises in the 6th century, the occupiers flee, and in spring 544 he takes the throne as Lý Nam Đế of a state named Vạn Xuân, "ten thousand springs"
b) in 542 Lý Bí throws off the Lương; in 544 he proclaims himself Lý Nam Đế of Vạn Xuân, building the Vạn Thọ palace and Khai Quốc pagoda; Triệu Quang Phục holds out in the Dạ Trạch swamp and reigns as Triệu Việt Vương, until the Sui end Vạn Xuân in 602

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
