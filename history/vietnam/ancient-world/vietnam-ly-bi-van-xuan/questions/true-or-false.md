# Vietnam ly bi van xuan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 8, story of Lý Bí and the Vạn Xuân state", under the harsh Liang, local leader Lý Bí rises in the 6th century, the occupiers flee, and in spring 544 he takes the throne as Lý Nam Đế of a state named Vạn Xuân, "ten thousand springs" (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 16, Lý Bí's uprising and the state of Vạn Xuân", in 542 Lý Bí throws off the Lương; in 544 he proclaims himself Lý Nam Đế of Vạn Xuân, building the Vạn Thọ palace and Khai Quốc pagoda; Triệu Quang Phục holds out in the Dạ Trạch swamp and reigns as Triệu Việt Vương, until the Sui end Vạn Xuân in 602 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
