# Vietnam ly bi van xuan

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam ly bi van xuan" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 8, story of Lý Bí and the Vạn Xuân state

Under the harsh Liang, local leader Lý Bí rises in the 6th century, the occupiers flee, and in spring 544 he takes the throne as Lý Nam Đế of a state named Vạn Xuân, "ten thousand springs".

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 16, Lý Bí's uprising and the state of Vạn Xuân

In 542 Lý Bí throws off the Lương; in 544 he proclaims himself Lý Nam Đế of Vạn Xuân, building the Vạn Thọ palace and Khai Quốc pagoda; Triệu Quang Phục holds out in the Dạ Trạch swamp and reigns as Triệu Việt Vương, until the Sui end Vạn Xuân in 602.
