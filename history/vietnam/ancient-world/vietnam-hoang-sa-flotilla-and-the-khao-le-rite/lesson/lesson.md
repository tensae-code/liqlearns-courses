# Vietnam hoang sa flotilla and the khao le rite

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam hoang sa flotilla and the khao le rite" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chuyên đề 3.V Lê Quý Đôn (1726–1784): the encyclopaedist who tops all three examinations, reads over 500 books including European works in Chinese translation, writes in the Vân đài loại ngữ of a round Earth with five continents, and records in the Phủ biên tạp lục how Vietnam's feudal states established and exercised sovereignty over the Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa islands

An 18th-century mandarin's notebook becomes a sovereignty archive for islands his country still defends.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 13 Vietnam, first sovereign of the two archipelagos: archaeology and the map record (Thiên Nam tứ chí lộ đồ thư 1686; Đại Nam nhất thống toàn đồ 1838) plus Lê Quý Đôn's account of the Nguyễn lords' Hoàng Sa flotilla — seventy men of An Vĩnh sailing each second lunar month for six months' provisions, returning in the eighth with wreck-salvage and sea treasures — and the Bắc Hải team; Gia Long's naval squads and Minh Mạng's annual missions, markers, rescue duty and a temple planted in 1835; the Khao lề thế lính festival still honours the flotilla's men

For two centuries village sailors on five small boats are the state's signature on two empty archipelagos.
