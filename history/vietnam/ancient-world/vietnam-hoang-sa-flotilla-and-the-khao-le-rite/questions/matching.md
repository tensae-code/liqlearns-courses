# Vietnam hoang sa flotilla and the khao le rite — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Chuyên đề 3.V Lê Quý Đôn (1726–1784): the encyclopaedist who tops all three examinations, reads over 500 books including European works in Chinese translation, writes in the Vân đài loại ngữ of a round Earth with five continents, and records in the Phủ biên tạp lục how Vietnam's feudal states established and exercised sovereignty over the Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa islands — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 13 Vietnam, first sovereign of the two archipelagos: archaeology and the map record (Thiên Nam tứ chí lộ đồ thư 1686; Đại Nam nhất thống toàn đồ 1838) plus Lê Quý Đôn's account of the Nguyễn lords' Hoàng Sa flotilla — seventy men of An Vĩnh sailing each second lunar month for six months' provisions, returning in the eighth with wreck-salvage and sea treasures — and the Bắc Hải team; Gia Long's naval squads and Minh Mạng's annual missions, markers, rescue duty and a temple planted in 1835; the Khao lề thế lính festival still honours the flotilla's men — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) an 18th-century mandarin's notebook becomes a sovereignty archive for islands his country still defends
b) for two centuries village sailors on five small boats are the state's signature on two empty archipelagos

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
