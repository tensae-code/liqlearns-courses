# Vietnam hoang sa flotilla and the khao le rite — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 3.V Lê Quý Đôn (1726–1784): the encyclopaedist who tops all three examinations, reads over 500 books including European works in Chinese translation, writes in the Vân đài loại ngữ of a round Earth with five continents, and records in the Phủ biên tạp lục how Vietnam's feudal states established and exercised sovereignty over the Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa islands" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** An 18th-century mandarin's notebook becomes a sovereignty archive for islands his country still defends.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 13 Vietnam, first sovereign of the two archipelagos: archaeology and the map record (Thiên Nam tứ chí lộ đồ thư 1686; Đại Nam nhất thống toàn đồ 1838) plus Lê Quý Đôn's account of the Nguyễn lords' Hoàng Sa flotilla — seventy men of An Vĩnh sailing each second lunar month for six months' provisions, returning in the eighth with wreck-salvage and sea treasures — and the Bắc Hải team; Gia Long's naval squads and Minh Mạng's annual missions, markers, rescue duty and a temple planted in 1835; the Khao lề thế lính festival still honours the flotilla's men" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** For two centuries village sailors on five small boats are the state's signature on two empty archipelagos.
