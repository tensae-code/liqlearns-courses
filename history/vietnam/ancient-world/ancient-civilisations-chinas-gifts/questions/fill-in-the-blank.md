# Ancient civilisations chinas gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, China's gifts: writing, thought and technique" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Oracle-bone characters grow into Han script; Confucius founds Confucianism; the Book of Songs and Sở Từ poetry, master bronze casting, the lunisolar calendar, star catalogues and pulse-diagnosis medicine.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Ancient civilisations chinas gifts as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
