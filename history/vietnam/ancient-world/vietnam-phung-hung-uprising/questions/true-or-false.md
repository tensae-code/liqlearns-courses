# Vietnam phung hung uprising — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 16, the uprising of Phùng Hưng", the Đường Lâm hào trưởng, a strongman who could wrestle buffalo and tiger, masters his region, besieges and takes Tống Bình and rules nine years; the people later honour him as Bố Cái Đại Vương
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam phung hung uprising as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
