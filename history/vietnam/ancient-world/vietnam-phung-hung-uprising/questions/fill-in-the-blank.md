# Vietnam phung hung uprising — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 16, the uprising of Phùng Hưng" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Đường Lâm hào trưởng, a strongman who could wrestle buffalo and tiger, masters his region, besieges and takes Tống Bình and rules nine years; the people later honour him as Bố Cái Đại Vương.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam phung hung uprising as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
