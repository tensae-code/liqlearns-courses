Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 16, the uprising of Mai Thúc Loan (713–722)", from Hoan Châu the revolt draws tens of thousands, wins Chăm-pa and Chenla support, takes Tống Bình and frees the country; Mai Thúc Loan proclaims himself emperor at Vạn An citadel before the Tang crush the rising in 722
