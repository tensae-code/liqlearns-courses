# Vietnam mai thuc loan uprising — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 16, the uprising of Mai Thúc Loan (713–722)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** From Hoan Châu the revolt draws tens of thousands, wins Chăm-pa and Chenla support, takes Tống Bình and frees the country; Mai Thúc Loan proclaims himself emperor at Vạn An citadel before the Tang crush the rising in 722.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam mai thuc loan uprising as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
