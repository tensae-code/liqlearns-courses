# Ancient civilisations greece crete — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 25, achievements of Greek civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The marble Parthenon on Athens's Acropolis (5th century BC), temples, and sculpture like the Discobolus that seeks the perfect human body.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 25, the history of the Olympic Games" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Heracles founds the games, first held in 776 BC; every four years the city-states stop their wars to compete, and winners receive only an olive wreath.
