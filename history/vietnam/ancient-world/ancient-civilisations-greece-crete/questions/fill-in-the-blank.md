# Ancient civilisations greece crete — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 25, achievements of Greek civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The marble Parthenon on Athens's Acropolis (5th century BC), temples, and sculpture like the Discobolus that seeks the perfect human body.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 25, the history of the Olympic Games" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Heracles founds the games, first held in 776 BC; every four years the city-states stop their wars to compete, and winners receive only an olive wreath.
