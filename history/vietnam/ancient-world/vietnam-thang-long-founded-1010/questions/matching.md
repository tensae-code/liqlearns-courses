# Vietnam thang long founded 1010 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 12, the founding and names of Thăng Long — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 9, the Lý dynasty sets its capital at Thăng Long — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) in 1010 King Lý Thái Tổ sails from Hoa Lư, sees a golden dragon rise at Đại La, and moves his capital there as Thăng Long, "rising dragon", justified in his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital
b) officials raise Lý Công Uẩn to the throne in 1009; in 1010 he moves the capital from Hoa Lư to Đại La, and children read his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital itself, quoted from the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
