# Vietnam thang long founded 1010 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 12, the founding and names of Thăng Long", in 1010 King Lý Thái Tổ sails from Hoa Lư, sees a golden dragon rise at Đại La, and moves his capital there as Thăng Long, "rising dragon", justified in his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 9, the Lý dynasty sets its capital at Thăng Long", officials raise Lý Công Uẩn to the throne in 1009; in 1010 he moves the capital from Hoa Lư to Đại La, and children read his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital itself, quoted from the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
