# Vietnam thang long founded 1010 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, the founding and names of Thăng Long" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1010 King Lý Thái Tổ sails from Hoa Lư, sees a golden dragon rise at Đại La, and moves his capital there as Thăng Long, "rising dragon", justified in his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, the Lý dynasty sets its capital at Thăng Long" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Officials raise Lý Công Uẩn to the throne in 1009; in 1010 he moves the capital from Hoa Lư to Đại La, and children read his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital itself, quoted from the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư.
