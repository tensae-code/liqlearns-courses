# Vietnam bach dang 1288 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 10, story of the Bạch Đằng river battle, 1288" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Trần Quốc Tuấn hides a stake field at the river mouth, feigns retreat at high tide, and the Yuan fleet impales itself as the tide falls; commander Ô Mã Nhi is captured alive.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam bach dang 1288 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
