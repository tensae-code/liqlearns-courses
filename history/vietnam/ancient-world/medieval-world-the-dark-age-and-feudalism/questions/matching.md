# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 1, how feudal society formed in Western Europe — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 1, the feudal manor and lord–serf relations — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) rome in crisis from the 3rd century is overrun by Germanic peoples and falls in 476; Germanic kingdoms rise, Charlemagne's Frankish empire is split into what become France, Germany and Italy, and lords and serfs crystallise as the two great classes
b) by the 9th century each lord is a petty king on his manor — own army, law, court, taxes, even coinage, immune from the king — while serfs owe rents and dues down to wedding and funeral taxes

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
