# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 1, how feudal society formed in Western Europe", rome in crisis from the 3rd century is overrun by Germanic peoples and falls in 476; Germanic kingdoms rise, Charlemagne's Frankish empire is split into what become France, Germany and Italy, and lords and serfs crystallise as the two great classes (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 1, the feudal manor and lord–serf relations", by the 9th century each lord is a petty king on his manor — own army, law, court, taxes, even coinage, immune from the king — while serfs owe rents and dues down to wedding and funeral taxes (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
