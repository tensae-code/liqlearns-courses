# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, how feudal society formed in Western Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rome in crisis from the 3rd century is overrun by Germanic peoples and falls in 476; Germanic kingdoms rise, Charlemagne's Frankish empire is split into what become France, Germany and Italy, and lords and serfs crystallise as the two great classes.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, the feudal manor and lord–serf relations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** By the 9th century each lord is a petty king on his manor — own army, law, court, taxes, even coinage, immune from the king — while serfs owe rents and dues down to wedding and funeral taxes.
