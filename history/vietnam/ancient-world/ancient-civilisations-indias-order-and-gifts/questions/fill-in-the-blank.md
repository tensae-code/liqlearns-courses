# Ancient civilisations indias order and gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8, India's gifts: script, epics, numbers and faiths" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Undeciphered Indus seals, Sanskrit, the Mahabharata and the Ramayana of Rama and Sita, the ten digits including zero carried to Europe by the Arabs, Brahmanism growing into Hinduism, and the Buddha's teaching from the 6th century BC.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Ancient civilisations indias order and gifts as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
