# Southeast asia cultural exchange india china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 13, beliefs and religions in early Southeast Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Older fertility and ancestor cults meet Hinduism and Buddhism from India; the Chăm linga becomes the God-King idea that crowns Chăm-pa's and Chenla's kings.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 13, scripts and literature: making Indian stories their own" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Khmer, Mon and Malay scripts grow from Indian ones (the Võ Cạnh stele, 3rd–4th century, is Southeast Asia's oldest Sanskrit inscription) while Vietnam takes Han characters; the Ramayana is retold as Laos's Phạ Lắc Phạ Lam, Thailand's Ramakien and Cambodia's Riêm-kê.
