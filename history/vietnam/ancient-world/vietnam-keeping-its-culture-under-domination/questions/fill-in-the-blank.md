# Vietnam keeping its culture under domination — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, the survival of Vietnam's own culture" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Through a thousand years the Vietnamese language passes mouth to ear, ancestors and nature gods are worshipped, hair is bunned, teeth blackened, betel chewed; a Yuan-era account (An Nam chí lược) still records these very customs.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, taking Chinese culture selectively" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Paper and glass techniques, Han script, New Year and Mid-Autumn festivals and Buddhism are taken in and bent to Vietnamese life — Buddhism so rooted that Viet monks lecture the Tang emperor.
