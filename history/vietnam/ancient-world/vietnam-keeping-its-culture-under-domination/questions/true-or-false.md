# Vietnam keeping its culture under domination — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 17, the survival of Vietnam's own culture", through a thousand years the Vietnamese language passes mouth to ear, ancestors and nature gods are worshipped, hair is bunned, teeth blackened, betel chewed; a Yuan-era account (An Nam chí lược) still records these very customs (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 17, taking Chinese culture selectively", paper and glass techniques, Han script, New Year and Mid-Autumn festivals and Buddhism are taken in and bent to Vietnamese life — Buddhism so rooted that Viet monks lecture the Tang emperor (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
