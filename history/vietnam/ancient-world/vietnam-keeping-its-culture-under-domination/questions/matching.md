# Vietnam keeping its culture under domination — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 17, the survival of Vietnam's own culture — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 17, taking Chinese culture selectively — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) through a thousand years the Vietnamese language passes mouth to ear, ancestors and nature gods are worshipped, hair is bunned, teeth blackened, betel chewed; a Yuan-era account (An Nam chí lược) still records these very customs
b) paper and glass techniques, Han script, New Year and Mid-Autumn festivals and Buddhism are taken in and bent to Vietnamese life — Buddhism so rooted that Viet monks lecture the Tang emperor

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
