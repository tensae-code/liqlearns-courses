# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 7, the succession of states between the two rivers" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sumerian city-states of the 4th millennium BC give way to Akkad, Assyria and Babylon in turn, until Persia conquers the land in 539 BC.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 2 Ancient Mesopotamia's art: born of Sumer–Akkad cultural interchange and sun-god worship, Babylonian relief, mural and palace art of high skill" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two peoples share one river valley and one sun god, and their walls learn to tell stories in stone.
