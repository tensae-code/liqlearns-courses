# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, the succession of states between the two rivers" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sumerian city-states of the 4th millennium BC give way to Akkad, Assyria and Babylon in turn, until Persia conquers the land in 539 BC.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2 Ancient Mesopotamia's art: born of Sumer–Akkad cultural interchange and sun-god worship, Babylonian relief, mural and palace art of high skill" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two peoples share one river valley and one sun god, and their walls learn to tell stories in stone.
