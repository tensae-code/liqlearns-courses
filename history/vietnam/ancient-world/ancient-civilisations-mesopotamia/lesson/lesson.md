## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 7, the succession of states between the two rivers

Sumerian city-states of the 4th millennium BC give way to Akkad, Assyria and Babylon in turn, until Persia conquers the land in 539 BC.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 2 Ancient Mesopotamia's art: born of Sumer–Akkad cultural interchange and sun-god worship, Babylonian relief, mural and palace art of high skill

Two peoples share one river valley and one sun god, and their walls learn to tell stories in stone.
