# Chinas dynastic cycle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4, China from Qin to Qing and the Tang golden age" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Qin Shi Huang unifies China in 221 BC; after division the Sui reunify it in 589; under Tang Taizong — "the boat is the ruler, the water the people" — examinations, equal fields and open gates make Chang'an the hub of the Silk Road.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Chinas dynastic cycle as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
