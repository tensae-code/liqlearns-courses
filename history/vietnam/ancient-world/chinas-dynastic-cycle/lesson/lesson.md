## Bài 4, China from Qin to Qing and the Tang golden age

Qin Shi Huang unifies China in 221 BC; after division the Sui reunify it in 589; under Tang Taizong — "the boat is the ruler, the water the people" — examinations, equal fields and open gates make Chang'an the hub of the Silk Road.
