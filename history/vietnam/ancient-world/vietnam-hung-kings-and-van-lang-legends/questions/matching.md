# Vietnam hung kings and van lang legends — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 7, legends of the Hùng Kings' era — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 5, the legend of Sơn Tinh and Thuỷ Tinh — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) dragon lord Lạc Long Quân and fairy Âu Cơ have 100 children; the eldest becomes Hùng Vương and founds Văn Lang at Phong Châu; later prince Lang Liêu wins the throne with humble rice cakes, bánh chưng and bánh giầy
b) the 18th Hùng King sets Mountain God and Water God a bride-price race for his daughter Mỵ Nương; the loser raises the rivers every year in revenge, explaining Vietnam's annual floods

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
