# Vietnam hung kings and van lang legends — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 7, legends of the Hùng Kings' era", dragon lord Lạc Long Quân and fairy Âu Cơ have 100 children; the eldest becomes Hùng Vương and founds Văn Lang at Phong Châu; later prince Lang Liêu wins the throne with humble rice cakes, bánh chưng and bánh giầy (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 5, the legend of Sơn Tinh and Thuỷ Tinh", the 18th Hùng King sets Mountain God and Water God a bride-price race for his daughter Mỵ Nương; the loser raises the rivers every year in revenge, explaining Vietnam's annual floods (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
