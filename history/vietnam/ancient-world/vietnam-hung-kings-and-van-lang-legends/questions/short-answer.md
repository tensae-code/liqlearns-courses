# Vietnam hung kings and van lang legends — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 7, legends of the Hùng Kings' era" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Dragon lord Lạc Long Quân and fairy Âu Cơ have 100 children; the eldest becomes Hùng Vương and founds Văn Lang at Phong Châu; later prince Lang Liêu wins the throne with humble rice cakes, bánh chưng and bánh giầy.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 5, the legend of Sơn Tinh and Thuỷ Tinh" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 18th Hùng King sets Mountain God and Water God a bride-price race for his daughter Mỵ Nương; the loser raises the rivers every year in revenge, explaining Vietnam's annual floods.
