## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 7, legends of the Hùng Kings' era** — dragon lord Lạc Long Quân and fairy Âu Cơ have 100 children; the eldest becomes Hùng Vương and founds Văn Lang at Phong Châu; later prince Lang Liêu wins the throne with humble rice cakes, bánh chưng and bánh giầy
2. **§2 Bài 5, the legend of Sơn Tinh and Thuỷ Tinh** — the 18th Hùng King sets Mountain God and Water God a bride-price race for his daughter Mỵ Nương; the loser raises the rivers every year in revenge, explaining Vietnam's annual floods
3. **§3 Chuyên đề 1 Ancestor worship scaled to a nation: the family cult of the dead widens to clan, village and country until the Hùng Kings stand as the whole people's common ancestors — a state rite already under Lê Thánh Tông, fixed to the 10th day of the third lunar month by Khải Định in 1917, given a officials' holiday by Hồ Chí Minh's decree of February 1946, and a paid public holiday from 2007; Phạm Văn Đồng in 1969 calls it the nation's unique folk belief** — one altar logic runs from a peasant family's shelf to the national festival at Đền Hùng
