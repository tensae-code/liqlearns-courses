# Vietnam ngo quyen builds independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 9, Ngô Quyền builds the independent state (939)", after Bạch Đằng he drops the Tiết độ sứ title, proclaims himself King and rules from Cổ Loa with civil and military officials and generals holding the provinces; the Trần-era historian Lê Văn Hưu judges that with him "the orthodox line of our country's kingship was restored"
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam ngo quyen builds independence as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
