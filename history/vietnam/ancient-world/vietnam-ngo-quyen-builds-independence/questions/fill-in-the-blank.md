# Vietnam ngo quyen builds independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, Ngô Quyền builds the independent state (939)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** After Bạch Đằng he drops the Tiết độ sứ title, proclaims himself King and rules from Cổ Loa with civil and military officials and generals holding the provinces; the Trần-era historian Lê Văn Hưu judges that with him "the orthodox line of our country's kingship was restored".

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam ngo quyen builds independence as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
