# Vietnam the returned sword of ho guom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, the legend of Hồ Gươm (the Lake of the Returned Sword)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** After defeating the Ming, King Lê Thái Tổ is boating on the lake when the Golden Tortoise rises and asks for the magic sword back for the Dragon King.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam the returned sword of ho guom as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
