# Vietnam the returned sword of ho guom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 12, the legend of Hồ Gươm (the Lake of the Returned Sword)", after defeating the Ming, King Lê Thái Tổ is boating on the lake when the Golden Tortoise rises and asks for the magic sword back for the Dragon King
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam the returned sword of ho guom as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
