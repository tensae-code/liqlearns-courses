# Vietnam van mieu quoc tu giam imperial academy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 13, the Temple of Literature and Imperial Academy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From the Lý dynasty the academy teaches princes and the talented, and 82 stone stelae record the names of doctors who passed the royal exams.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 11, Lý education: Văn Miếu, the first examination, Quốc Tử Giám" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Temple of Literature is built in 1070, the first examination held in 1075 (Lê Văn Thịnh takes first place and rises to Thái sư), and in 1076 the Imperial Academy opens to the princes, then to officials' sons and the talented.
