# Vietnam van mieu quoc tu giam imperial academy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 13, the Temple of Literature and Imperial Academy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From the Lý dynasty the academy teaches princes and the talented, and 82 stone stelae record the names of doctors who passed the royal exams.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 11, Lý education: Văn Miếu, the first examination, Quốc Tử Giám" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Temple of Literature is built in 1070, the first examination held in 1075 (Lê Văn Thịnh takes first place and rises to Thái sư), and in 1076 the Imperial Academy opens to the princes, then to officials' sons and the talented.
