# Vietnam van mieu quoc tu giam imperial academy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 13, the Temple of Literature and Imperial Academy", from the Lý dynasty the academy teaches princes and the talented, and 82 stone stelae record the names of doctors who passed the royal exams (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 11, Lý education: Văn Miếu, the first examination, Quốc Tử Giám", the Temple of Literature is built in 1070, the first examination held in 1075 (Lê Văn Thịnh takes first place and rises to Thái sư), and in 1076 the Imperial Academy opens to the princes, then to officials' sons and the talented (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
