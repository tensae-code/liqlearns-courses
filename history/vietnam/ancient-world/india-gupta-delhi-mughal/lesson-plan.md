# Lesson plan — India gupta delhi mughal

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** India gupta delhi mughal
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The medieval world and Đại Việt: feudal Europe, China, India and Southeast Asia beside Vietnam from Ngô Quyền to the Lê sơ, and Chăm-pa (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The medieval world and Đại Việt: feudal Europe, China, India and Southeast Asia beside Vietnam from Ngô Quyền to the Lê sơ, and Chăm-pa — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 5, India under the Gupta: a golden age** — chandragupta I unites the Ganges plain in the early 4th century; iron tools, irrigation and trade enrich the realm, and the Chinese monk Faxian's record praises its lenient laws, charity houses and hospitals — its iron pillar still does not rust
2. **§2 Bài 5, the Delhi Sultanate and Akbar's Mughal empire** — turkic Muslims found the Delhi Sultanate in 1206 and drive south; two centuries on, the Mughal Akbar (1556–1605) rebuilds India with 15 provinces, surveyed land, fair taxes, new crops and a policy of harmony among peoples and faiths
3. **§3 Bài 5, the culture of feudal India** — brahmanism ripens into Hinduism, Buddhism divides and travels abroad, Islam arrives with Delhi; Sanskrit is perfected, Kalidasa writes Shakuntala (praised by Goethe), and three faiths raise Khajuraho's temples and the 31 rock caves of Ajanta
