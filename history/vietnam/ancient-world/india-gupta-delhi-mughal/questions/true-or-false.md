# India gupta delhi mughal — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 5, India under the Gupta: a golden age", chandragupta I unites the Ganges plain in the early 4th century; iron tools, irrigation and trade enrich the realm, and the Chinese monk Faxian's record praises its lenient laws, charity houses and hospitals — its iron pillar still does not rust (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 5, the Delhi Sultanate and Akbar's Mughal empire", turkic Muslims found the Delhi Sultanate in 1206 and drive south; two centuries on, the Mughal Akbar (1556–1605) rebuilds India with 15 provinces, surveyed land, fair taxes, new crops and a policy of harmony among peoples and faiths (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
