# India gupta delhi mughal — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5, India under the Gupta: a golden age" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chandragupta I unites the Ganges plain in the early 4th century; iron tools, irrigation and trade enrich the realm, and the Chinese monk Faxian's record praises its lenient laws, charity houses and hospitals — its iron pillar still does not rust.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5, the Delhi Sultanate and Akbar's Mughal empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Turkic Muslims found the Delhi Sultanate in 1206 and drive south; two centuries on, the Mughal Akbar (1556–1605) rebuilds India with 15 provinces, surveyed land, fair taxes, new crops and a policy of harmony among peoples and faiths.
