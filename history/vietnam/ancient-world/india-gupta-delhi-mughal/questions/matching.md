# India gupta delhi mughal — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 5, India under the Gupta: a golden age — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 5, the Delhi Sultanate and Akbar's Mughal empire — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) chandragupta I unites the Ganges plain in the early 4th century; iron tools, irrigation and trade enrich the realm, and the Chinese monk Faxian's record praises its lenient laws, charity houses and hospitals — its iron pillar still does not rust
b) turkic Muslims found the Delhi Sultanate in 1206 and drive south; two centuries on, the Mughal Akbar (1556–1605) rebuilds India with 15 provinces, surveyed land, fair taxes, new crops and a policy of harmony among peoples and faiths

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
