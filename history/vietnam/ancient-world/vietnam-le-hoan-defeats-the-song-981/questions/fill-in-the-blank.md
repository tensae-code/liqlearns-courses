# Vietnam le hoan defeats the song 981 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 10, the resistance against the Song, 981" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Đinh king and his heir are assassinated, a six-year-old succeeds, and the Song strike by land and water; regent Lê Hoàn is raised to the throne, fights at Đại La, the Lục Đầu river, Bạch Đằng and Tây Kết, and the Song commander Hầu Nhân Bảo is killed.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam le hoan defeats the song 981 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
