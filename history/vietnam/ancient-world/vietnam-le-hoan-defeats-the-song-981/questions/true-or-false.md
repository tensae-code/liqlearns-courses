# Vietnam le hoan defeats the song 981 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 10, the resistance against the Song, 981", the Đinh king and his heir are assassinated, a six-year-old succeeds, and the Song strike by land and water; regent Lê Hoàn is raised to the throne, fights at Đại La, the Lục Đầu river, Bạch Đằng and Tây Kết, and the Song commander Hầu Nhân Bảo is killed
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam le hoan defeats the song 981 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
