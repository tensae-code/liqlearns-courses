# Vietnam founding of the communist party 1930

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam founding of the communist party 1930" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Bài 16 Founding the Communist Party of Vietnam: the preparation — Đường Kách mệnh's doctrine that a firm party is the helmsman without whom the boat does not run, the denunciation of colonialism in Le Paria and the Union of Colonial Peoples, and the Revolutionary Youth League (Guangzhou, June 1925) training the cadres who form three communist organisations in 1929; the crisis — three organisations competing and criticising each other; the act — Nguyễn Ái Quốc, as Comintern envoy, summons them to Kowloon, and from 6-1-1930 the conference abolishes the old conflicts, names the Party, and adopts his Brief Platform and Brief Strategy, the first Political Platform joining nation and class; the meaning — a turning point proving the proletariat mature enough to lead

The Party is born not in Vietnam but in a Hong Kong meeting room, by unification rather than proclamation.
