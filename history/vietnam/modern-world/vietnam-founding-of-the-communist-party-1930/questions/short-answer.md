# Vietnam founding of the communist party 1930 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 16 Founding the Communist Party of Vietnam: the preparation — Đường Kách mệnh's doctrine that a firm party is the helmsman without whom the boat does not run, the denunciation of colonialism in Le Paria and the Union of Colonial Peoples, and the Revolutionary Youth League (Guangzhou, June 1925) training the cadres who form three communist organisations in 1929; the crisis — three organisations competing and criticising each other; the act — Nguyễn Ái Quốc, as Comintern envoy, summons them to Kowloon, and from 6-1-1930 the conference abolishes the old conflicts, names the Party, and adopts his Brief Platform and Brief Strategy, the first Political Platform joining nation and class; the meaning — a turning point proving the proletariat mature enough to lead" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Party is born not in Vietnam but in a Hong Kong meeting room, by unification rather than proclamation.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam founding of the communist party 1930 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
