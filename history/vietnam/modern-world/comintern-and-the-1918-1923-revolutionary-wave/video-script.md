# Video script — Comintern and the 1918 1923 revolutionary wave

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Comintern and the 1918 1923 revolutionary wave.

On 9 November 1918 a Berlin general strike becomes an armed rising and the German monarchy falls; England counts 6.5 million strikers (1919–21), France a general strike of over a million (1 May 1920), Italy 1,600 strikes in 1919 and Red Guards in 1920; communist parties spring up (Hungary 1918, France and England 1920, Italy 1921) and in March 1919 the Comintern is founded in Moscow — its 1920 Theses on the national and colonial questions are the very text in which Nguyễn Ái Quốc finds his road; the Comintern is dissolved in 1943.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
