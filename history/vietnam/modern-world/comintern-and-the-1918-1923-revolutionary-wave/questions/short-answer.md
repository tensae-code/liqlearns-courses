# Comintern and the 1918 1923 revolutionary wave — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 2, the revolutionary wave and the Communist International" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** On 9 November 1918 a Berlin general strike becomes an armed rising and the German monarchy falls; England counts 6.5 million strikers (1919–21), France a general strike of over a million (1 May 1920), Italy 1,600 strikes in 1919 and Red Guards in 1920; communist parties spring up (Hungary 1918, France and England 1920, Italy 1921) and in March 1919 the Comintern is founded in Moscow — its 1920 Theses on the national and colonial questions are the very text in which Nguyễn Ái Quốc finds his road; the Comintern is dissolved in 1943.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Comintern and the 1918 1923 revolutionary wave as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
