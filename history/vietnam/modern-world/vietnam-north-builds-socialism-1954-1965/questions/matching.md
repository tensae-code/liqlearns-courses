# Vietnam north builds socialism 1954 1965 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 16, the North: land reform, recovery, first plan — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 16, the South rises: Đồng Khởi — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) five waves of land reform (late 1953–1956) give over 810,000 hectares to some 2.2 million households — 72.8% of northern rural households — making "the tiller has land" real, though the book records errors against some meritorious families, acknowledged and corrected; recovery (1955–57) passes pre-war farm output and opens factories like Cơ khí Hà Nội and Thuốc lá Thăng Long, trading with 27 countries; transformation (1958–60) gathers 40,000 agricultural cooperatives and wipes out illiteracy in the delta for under-50s; from 1959 the Trường Sơn supply route forms — Đoàn 559's traditional day is 19 May 1959 — and the first Five-Year Plan (1961–65) moves great volumes of food, weapons and medicine south, some 40,000 cadres and soldiers in 1961–63 alone, while the "Ba sẵn sàng" youth movement readies the North; Hồ Chí Minh's 1964 verdict is quoted: in ten years the North "has taken long steps never seen in the nation's history"
b) america installs the Ngô Đình Diệm government, refuses the 1956 general election and unleashes "denounce and exterminate communists" campaigns under Law 10/59's mobile guillotine; the Party's 15th Central Committee Conference (early 1959) authorises revolutionary violence with political struggle primary; scattered risings at Vĩnh Thạnh, Bác Ái and Trà Bồng (1959) become the Đồng Khởi movement at Bến Tre on 17 January 1960 — three Mỏ Cày communes rise, Bến Tre's "long-hair army" fights under Nguyễn Thị Định, landlords' land goes to poor peasants — and on 20 December 1960 the National Liberation Front is founded

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
