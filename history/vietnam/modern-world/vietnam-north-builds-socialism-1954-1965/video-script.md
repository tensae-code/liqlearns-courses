# Video script — Vietnam north builds socialism 1954 1965

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam north builds socialism 1954 1965.

Five waves of land reform (late 1953–1956) give over 810,000 hectares to some 2.2 million households — 72.8% of northern rural households — making "the tiller has land" real, though the book records errors against some meritorious families, acknowledged and corrected; recovery (1955–57) passes pre-war farm output and opens factories like Cơ khí Hà Nội and Thuốc lá Thăng Long, trading with 27 countries; transformation (1958–60) gathers 40,000 agricultural cooperatives and wipes out illiteracy in the delta for under-50s; from 1959 the Trường Sơn supply route forms — Đoàn 559's traditional day is 19 May 1959 — and the first Five-Year Plan (1961–65) moves great volumes of food, weapons and medicine south, some 40,000 cadres and soldiers in 1961–63 alone, while the "Ba sẵn sàng" youth movement readies the North; Hồ Chí Minh's 1964 verdict is quoted: in ten years the North "has taken long steps never seen in the nation's history".

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
