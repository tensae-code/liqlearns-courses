# Vietnam dang ngoai peasant uprisings — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, why Đàng Ngoài exploded: the crisis of the Lê–Trịnh regime" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The chronicles quoted here do the accusing — in 1730 tens of thousands of Hải Dương people are conscripted to dig rivers and haul bricks for Lord Trịnh Giang's pagodas; the lord's palace keeps four or five hundred arrogant eunuchs "hated and feared by the whole country"; a tax is laid on every trade, so that for the silk tax people cut down their mulberry trees and for the fish tax hide their nets; famine follows, and "a hundred đồng cannot buy one full meal".

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, the great risings of the 18th-century North" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hoàng Công Chất (1739–1769) gathers the poor, builds his base at Mường Thanh in the far north-west, protects the border and settles people's lives so the Tây Bắc peoples support him to the end, and his son fights on after him; Nguyễn Danh Phương (1740–1751) holds the Tam Đảo mountains until he is captured; Nguyễn Hữu Cầu (1741–1751), the "Quận He" who swims like the great he fish that never harms a soul at sea, strikes from Đồ Sơn, threatens Thăng Long itself and ranges into Thanh Hoá and Nghệ An — all fail, but they shake the Lê–Trịnh regime to its foundations and clear the ground for Tây Sơn.
