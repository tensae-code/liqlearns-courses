# Vietnam tay son uprising

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam tay son uprising" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 8, the raising of Tây Sơn, 1771, and the fall of the Nguyễn lords

In Đàng Trong the boy-lord Nguyễn Phúc Thuần "loves only play and singing" while the regent Trương Phúc Loan, in the quoted history's words, "sells offices and buys verdicts" for over thirty years; in 1771 the three brothers Nguyễn Nhạc, Nguyễn Huệ and Nguyễn Lữ — Nguyễn Huệ with eyes like lightning and a voice like a bell — raise the banner at Tây Sơn under the slogan "take from the rich, give to the poor"; squeezed between the Trịnh in the north and the Nguyễn in the south, Nguyễn Nhạc makes a temporary peace with the Trịnh, strikes Gia Định four times, and in 1777 captures and kills the Nguyễn lord — only Nguyễn Ánh escapes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 8 The Tây Sơn movement: against the corrupt Trương Phúc Loan regency, three brothers raise the flag in spring 1771; the table runs Quy Nhơn 1773, the Nguyễn lords fallen 1777, 50,000 Siamese smashed at Rạch Gầm–Xoài Mút 1785, Phú Xuân and the Trịnh 1786, and Quang Trung's Tết victory over 290,000 Qing troops in 1789

A highland peasant rising unifies a divided country and beats two empires in four years.
