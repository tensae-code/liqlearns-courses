# Lesson plan — Vietnam tay son uprising

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Vietnam tay son uprising
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Revolutions, industry and a divided country: the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation beside Vietnam's partition, the Tây Sơn, the Nguyễn and the first French colonial exploitation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Vietnam tay son uprising as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Revolutions, industry and a divided country: the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation beside Vietnam's partition, the Tây Sơn, the Nguyễn and the first French colonial exploitation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 8, the raising of Tây Sơn, 1771, and the fall of the Nguyễn lords** — in Đàng Trong the boy-lord Nguyễn Phúc Thuần "loves only play and singing" while the regent Trương Phúc Loan, in the quoted history's words, "sells offices and buys verdicts" for over thirty years; in 1771 the three brothers Nguyễn Nhạc, Nguyễn Huệ and Nguyễn Lữ — Nguyễn Huệ with eyes like lightning and a voice like a bell — raise the banner at Tây Sơn under the slogan "take from the rich, give to the poor"; squeezed between the Trịnh in the north and the Nguyễn in the south, Nguyễn Nhạc makes a temporary peace with the Trịnh, strikes Gia Định four times, and in 1777 captures and kills the Nguyễn lord — only Nguyễn Ánh escapes
2. **§2 Bài 8 The Tây Sơn movement: against the corrupt Trương Phúc Loan regency, three brothers raise the flag in spring 1771; the table runs Quy Nhơn 1773, the Nguyễn lords fallen 1777, 50,000 Siamese smashed at Rạch Gầm–Xoài Mút 1785, Phú Xuân and the Trịnh 1786, and Quang Trung's Tết victory over 290,000 Qing troops in 1789** — a highland peasant rising unifies a divided country and beats two empires in four years
