# Vietnam revolutionary organisations 1925 1930 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5, three organisations, three roads" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** In June 1925 Nguyễn Ái Quốc founds the Revolutionary Youth League in Canton and publishes Đường Kách mệnh (1927); its "proletarianisation" drive roots bases in all three kì by May 1929; the Tân Việt party turns toward the proletarian road, while the VNQDĐ of Nguyễn Thái Học (December 1927) answers the Bazin assassination (February 1929) with the Yên Bái uprising of 9–10 February 1930 — crushed at once, proving the bourgeois road impotent.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam revolutionary organisations 1925 1930 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
