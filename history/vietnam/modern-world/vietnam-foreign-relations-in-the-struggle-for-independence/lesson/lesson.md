## Bài 12 The Indochinese Communist Party's diplomacy, 1930–1945: as a Comintern section the Party organises support for the USSR's resistance, fundraises with French and Chinese organisations in Indochina for China's war victims, builds the Overseas Chinese movement, and contacts anti-Japanese movements from Burma to Indonesia; through Việt Minh it stands openly with the Allies — talks with the Republic of China's representatives, a seat in the Vietnam Revolutionary Alliance, and by late April 1945 a working cooperation with the American OSS, sealed in the Tân Trào photograph with the "Deer Team"

A hunted underground party ends the war photographed beside American intelligence officers.
