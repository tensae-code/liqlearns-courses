# China from civil war to reform — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 12, China: civil war, campaigns and reform" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Kuomintang loses the civil war (1946–49), flees to Taiwan, and Mao proclaims the People's Republic on 1 October 1949; land reform and recovery (1950–52) precede the first Five-Year Plan (1953–57) with Soviet help — the book pictures the 1956 "Liberation" truck — then the "Three Red Banners" of 1958, the Great Leap's steel campaign and People's Communes bring decline and famine, and the Cultural Revolution convulses 1966–76; border clashes with India (1962) and the USSR (1969) give way to détente with America, and the reform line of December 1978 sets the economy at the centre — GDP then grows over 9.1% a year (1980–91), the world's highest, with special economic zones at Shenzhen and Zhuhai.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 4 China takes the socialist road: after the national democratic revolution, the People's Republic is proclaimed in 1949, with North Vietnam following in 1954 and Laos in December 1975" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mao's proclamation in 1949 moves a quarter of humanity into the socialist camp.
