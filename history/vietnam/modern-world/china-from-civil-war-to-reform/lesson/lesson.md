# China from civil war to reform

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "China from civil war to reform" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 12, China: civil war, campaigns and reform

The Kuomintang loses the civil war (1946–49), flees to Taiwan, and Mao proclaims the People's Republic on 1 October 1949; land reform and recovery (1950–52) precede the first Five-Year Plan (1953–57) with Soviet help — the book pictures the 1956 "Liberation" truck — then the "Three Red Banners" of 1958, the Great Leap's steel campaign and People's Communes bring decline and famine, and the Cultural Revolution convulses 1966–76; border clashes with India (1962) and the USSR (1969) give way to détente with America, and the reform line of December 1978 sets the economy at the centre — GDP then grows over 9.1% a year (1980–91), the world's highest, with special economic zones at Shenzhen and Zhuhai.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 4 China takes the socialist road: after the national democratic revolution, the People's Republic is proclaimed in 1949, with North Vietnam following in 1954 and Laos in December 1975

Mao's proclamation in 1949 moves a quarter of humanity into the socialist camp.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 4 China's reform and opening: from December 1978 the socialist market economy lifts GDP from 367.9 billion yuan to over 114 trillion by 2021 — about 9.5% a year — turns a Guangdong fishing village into Shenzhen, and launches Shenzhou craft and the BeiDou satellites

In forty years a fishing village becomes a top Asian tech city, and the pupil is asked what the miracle proves.
