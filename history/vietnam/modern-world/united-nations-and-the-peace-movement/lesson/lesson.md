# United nations and the peace movement

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "United nations and the peace movement" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 27, humanity builds a peaceful world (the dove legend; Red Cross; the United Nations)

Noah's dove returns with an olive branch and becomes peace's symbol; after the Second World War, in 1945, the nations found the United Nations "to save succeeding generations from the scourge of war" — and Vietnamese soldiers now serve as its peacekeepers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 1 The United Nations founded: where the League of Nations (1920–1946) proved too weak, the Declaration by United Nations (1-1-1942, 26 countries), Tehran 1943 and Yalta's decision lead to San Francisco, where 50 countries pass the Charter and the UN is officially founded on 24-10-1945 with 51 members

The wartime alliance refuses to disband and instead writes itself a permanent house.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 1 What the UN is for: the Charter's goals (peace and security, friendly relations on self-determination, cooperation on economic, social and human-rights problems) and principles (sovereign equality, no force, no interference), then its record — no new world war, peacekeeping from El Salvador to Mozambique, the NPT 1968 and chemical-weapons ban, decolonisation to 193 members, the MDGs and the 17 SDGs, the 1948 human-rights declaration, and Vietnamese officers leaving for peacekeeping in South Sudan in 2021

The organisation Vietnam once petitioned for recognition becomes one its own soldiers serve.
