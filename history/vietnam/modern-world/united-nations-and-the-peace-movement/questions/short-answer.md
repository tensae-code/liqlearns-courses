# United nations and the peace movement — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 27, humanity builds a peaceful world (the dove legend; Red Cross; the United Nations)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Noah's dove returns with an olive branch and becomes peace's symbol; after the Second World War, in 1945, the nations found the United Nations "to save succeeding generations from the scourge of war" — and Vietnamese soldiers now serve as its peacekeepers.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 1 The United Nations founded: where the League of Nations (1920–1946) proved too weak, the Declaration by United Nations (1-1-1942, 26 countries), Tehran 1943 and Yalta's decision lead to San Francisco, where 50 countries pass the Charter and the UN is officially founded on 24-10-1945 with 51 members" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The wartime alliance refuses to disband and instead writes itself a permanent house.
