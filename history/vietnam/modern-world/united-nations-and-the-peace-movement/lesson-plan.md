# Lesson plan — United nations and the peace movement

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** United nations and the peace movement
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The whole national story in one sweep: the first states, a thousand years of northern domination, the dynasties, colonisation, 1945, 1954, 1975 and Đổi mới — with the world's first civilisations beside it (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 27, humanity builds a peaceful world (the dove legend; Red Cross; the United Nations)** — noah's dove returns with an olive branch and becomes peace's symbol; after the Second World War, in 1945, the nations found the United Nations "to save succeeding generations from the scourge of war" — and Vietnamese soldiers now serve as its peacekeepers
2. **§2 Bài 1 The United Nations founded: where the League of Nations (1920–1946) proved too weak, the Declaration by United Nations (1-1-1942, 26 countries), Tehran 1943 and Yalta's decision lead to San Francisco, where 50 countries pass the Charter and the UN is officially founded on 24-10-1945 with 51 members** — the wartime alliance refuses to disband and instead writes itself a permanent house
3. **§3 Bài 1 What the UN is for: the Charter's goals (peace and security, friendly relations on self-determination, cooperation on economic, social and human-rights problems) and principles (sovereign equality, no force, no interference), then its record — no new world war, peacekeeping from El Salvador to Mozambique, the NPT 1968 and chemical-weapons ban, decolonisation to 193 members, the MDGs and the 17 SDGs, the 1948 human-rights declaration, and Vietnamese officers leaving for peacekeeping in South Sudan in 2021** — the organisation Vietnam once petitioned for recognition becomes one its own soldiers serve
