# Capitalism becomes monopoly capitalism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 10, how imperialism formed" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After some thirty years of relative peace, competition drives production and capital to concentrate until monopoly companies — cartels and syndicates in England, France and Germany, trusts in America — dominate markets and bend politics to themselves; industrial and bank capital fuse into finance capital; and every Western power pushes out to invade and exploit colonies — the world map of empires and colonies at the 20th century's opening is the lesson's first picture.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Capitalism becomes monopoly capitalism as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
