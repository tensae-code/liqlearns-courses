# Capitalism becomes monopoly capitalism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 10, how imperialism formed", after some thirty years of relative peace, competition drives production and capital to concentrate until monopoly companies — cartels and syndicates in England, France and Germany, trusts in America — dominate markets and bend politics to themselves; industrial and bank capital fuse into finance capital; and every Western power pushes out to invade and exploit colonies — the world map of empires and colonies at the 20th century's opening is the lesson's first picture
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Capitalism becomes monopoly capitalism as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
