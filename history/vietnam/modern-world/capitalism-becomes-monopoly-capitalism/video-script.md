# Video script — Capitalism becomes monopoly capitalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Capitalism becomes monopoly capitalism.

After some thirty years of relative peace, competition drives production and capital to concentrate until monopoly companies — cartels and syndicates in England, France and Germany, trusts in America — dominate markets and bend politics to themselves; industrial and bank capital fuse into finance capital; and every Western power pushes out to invade and exploit colonies — the world map of empires and colonies at the 20th century's opening is the lesson's first picture.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
