# Vietnam resistance to france 1858 1862 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, Đà Nẵng, Chí Hoà and the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** On 1 September 1858 the Franco-Spanish fleet attacks Đà Nẵng meaning to win quickly and strike at Huế; Nguyễn Tri Phương's soldiers and the Đà Nẵng people fight so fiercely that the "quick attack, quick victory" plan collapses; the French swing south, take Gia Định (February 1859) and build their bridgehead while the court wavers and the people rise on their own; Nguyễn Tri Phương builds the great Chí Hoà entrenched camp, which falls on 24 February 1861, and the three eastern provinces of Nam Kì follow — yet Nguyễn Trung Trực's men still burn the French warship Espérance on the Nhật Tảo river that December; in 1862 the court signs the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất, recognising French rule over the three provinces and Côn Lôn island and an indemnity of some 20 million quan.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam resistance to france 1858 1862 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
