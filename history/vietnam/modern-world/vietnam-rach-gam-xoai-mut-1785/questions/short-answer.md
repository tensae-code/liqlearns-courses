# Vietnam rach gam xoai mut 1785 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 8, the destruction of the Siamese invasion fleet at Rạch Gầm – Xoài Mút" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The fugitive Nguyễn Ánh begs Siam for help, and some 50,000 Siamese troops pour into western Gia Định in 1784; Nguyễn Huệ, headquartered at Mỹ Tho, chooses the six-kilometre stretch of the Tiền river between Rạch Gầm and Xoài Mút — an islet midstream, dense banks, hidden creeks — and on 19 January 1785 feigns retreat, lures the fleet into the ambush and burns it with fire rafts; the Siamese are annihilated in a single day, and the Nguyễn court's own chronicle admits that after that defeat "though their mouths boast, in their hearts they fear Tây Sơn like a tiger".

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam rach gam xoai mut 1785 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
