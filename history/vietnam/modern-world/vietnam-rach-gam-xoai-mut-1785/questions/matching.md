# Vietnam rach gam xoai mut 1785 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 8, the destruction of the Siamese invasion fleet at Rạch Gầm – Xoài Mút — a)
**2.** The one thing this lesson teaches — b)

Meanings:
a) the fugitive Nguyễn Ánh begs Siam for help, and some 50,000 Siamese troops pour into western Gia Định in 1784; Nguyễn Huệ, headquartered at Mỹ Tho, chooses the six-kilometre stretch of the Tiền river between Rạch Gầm and Xoài Mút — an islet midstream, dense banks, hidden creeks — and on 19 January 1785 feigns retreat, lures the fleet into the ambush and burns it with fire rafts; the Siamese are annihilated in a single day, and the Nguyễn court's own chronicle admits that after that defeat "though their mouths boast, in their hearts they fear Tây Sơn like a tiger"
b) the story of Vietnam rach gam xoai mut 1785 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
