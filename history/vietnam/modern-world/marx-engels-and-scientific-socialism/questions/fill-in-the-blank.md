# Marx engels and scientific socialism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 11, Marx, Engels and the birth of scientific socialism" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The industrial revolution creates the working class — landless peasants crowding into factories and mines, demonstrating with the English Chartists by 1848; Karl Marx (1818–1883), a Trier doctor of philosophy at 23, is expelled from Germany for his revolutionary views and continues in Paris; Friedrich Engels (1820–1895), a Barmen factory-owner's son, sees his own class's greed at first hand and writes The Condition of the Working Class in England; the two meet in 1844, found the League of Communists — the international proletariat's first independent party — and in early 1848 publish the Communist Manifesto, whose appearance the book takes as the birth of scientific socialism.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Marx engels and scientific socialism as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
