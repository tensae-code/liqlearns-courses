# Marx engels and scientific socialism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 11, Marx, Engels and the birth of scientific socialism", the industrial revolution creates the working class — landless peasants crowding into factories and mines, demonstrating with the English Chartists by 1848; Karl Marx (1818–1883), a Trier doctor of philosophy at 23, is expelled from Germany for his revolutionary views and continues in Paris; Friedrich Engels (1820–1895), a Barmen factory-owner's son, sees his own class's greed at first hand and writes The Condition of the Working Class in England; the two meet in 1844, found the League of Communists — the international proletariat's first independent party — and in early 1848 publish the Communist Manifesto, whose appearance the book takes as the birth of scientific socialism
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Marx engels and scientific socialism as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
