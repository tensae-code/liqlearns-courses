# Vietnam international integration — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 3 Vietnam walks into the region: globalisation lifts Vietnam from low income to lower-middle income within 2006–2016 and literacy from 89% to 96%, even as it exposes 100 million people to climate risk; the ASEAN road runs from Phạm Văn Đồng's 1977–78 visits through Nguyễn Cơ Thạch's 1987 wish and Võ Văn Kiệt's 1991 tour to admission as seventh member at Brunei on 28-7-1995 — then Vietnam helps expand ASEAN to ten, co-founds the ARF in 1994 and pushes the ASEAN Economic Community", twenty years of knocking ends with the former outsider helping to chair the club (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 3 Vietnam at the world's tables: admitted to the United Nations on 20-9-1977 after the General Assembly recognises its government as Vietnam's only lawful one, later elected to the Human Rights Council (2014–2016 and 2023–2025) and sending peacekeepers; 21st member of APEC in 1998; and after applying in 1995 and negotiating for over a decade, a WTO member in 2007", the country once embargoed ends up reviewing others' trade policies inside the WTO (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
