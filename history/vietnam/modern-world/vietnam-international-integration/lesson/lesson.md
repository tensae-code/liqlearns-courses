# Vietnam international integration

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam international integration" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chuyên đề 3 Vietnam walks into the region: globalisation lifts Vietnam from low income to lower-middle income within 2006–2016 and literacy from 89% to 96%, even as it exposes 100 million people to climate risk; the ASEAN road runs from Phạm Văn Đồng's 1977–78 visits through Nguyễn Cơ Thạch's 1987 wish and Võ Văn Kiệt's 1991 tour to admission as seventh member at Brunei on 28-7-1995 — then Vietnam helps expand ASEAN to ten, co-founds the ARF in 1994 and pushes the ASEAN Economic Community

Twenty years of knocking ends with the former outsider helping to chair the club.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 3 Vietnam at the world's tables: admitted to the United Nations on 20-9-1977 after the General Assembly recognises its government as Vietnam's only lawful one, later elected to the Human Rights Council (2014–2016 and 2023–2025) and sending peacekeepers; 21st member of APEC in 1998; and after applying in 1995 and negotiating for over a decade, a WTO member in 2007

The country once embargoed ends up reviewing others' trade policies inside the WTO.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 11 Đổi Mới's integration harvest: from an embargoed country to diplomatic relations with 189 of 193 states, strategic and comprehensive partnerships across the board, party relations with 247 parties in 111 countries and parliamentary ties with over 140; defence relations with more than 100 countries, peacekeeping contributions, and cultural diplomacy from Quan họ performances in the Czech Republic to science-technology membership of some 100 international organisations

The once-blockaded state ends up counting its friendships in the high one-hundreds.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 14 Foreign relations in the hard decade, 1975–1985: comprehensive cooperation with the socialist world (CMEA membership 1978, the joint Soviet–Vietnamese space flight of 1980), negotiation with China over border and sea while sovereignty is held, special solidarity with Laos and Cambodia and a slow opening to ASEAN from 1980, UN development aid of some 527.9 million USD (1977–1986), and Phạm Văn Đồng addressing the Non-Aligned summit at Havana (1979) — crowned by UN membership on 20-9-1977

Even embargoed and fighting two border wars, the state keeps collecting memberships and interlocutors.

## Sub lesson 5 — Bài 14 Diplomacy of the Đổi Mới era: the 1991 Congress fixes the line — cooperation, equality and mutual benefit with all countries regardless of regime — and the Cambodia settlement (Paris, 10-1991) unlocks normalisation with China (1991) and the US (1995) and ASEAN entry (1995); relations rise to comprehensive strategic partnerships (the US President's 2023 visit the emblem), Vietnam hosts ASEAN, ASEM and APEC summits, is twice elected to the UN Security Council (2008–09, 2020–21), signs the EVFTA and the regional mega-agreements, settles land and sea borders by treaty, and answers COVID-19 and foreign disasters with medical aid

The decade's key that opens every other door is a peace agreement about a third country.
