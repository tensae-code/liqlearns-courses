# Exploration why they sailed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Shared Theme 1 (in the Geography part), why the great discoveries happened when they did" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Mid-15th-century Europe hungers for raw materials, gold and markets and imagines the East as a fairyland where "the ground everywhere is gold"; with Arabs and Turks monopolising the Mediterranean routes, the round Earth, charts, the compass, the caravel and royal sponsors make the ocean roads possible.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Exploration why they sailed as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
