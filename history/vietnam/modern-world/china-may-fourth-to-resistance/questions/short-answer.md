# China may fourth to resistance — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 3, China from May Fourth to the resistance war" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** On 4 May 1919 three thousand Beijing students march and a movement is born; the Chinese Communist Party is founded in July 1921; from 1927 to 1937 it fights a revolutionary war against the Kuomintang, then in 1937 proposes the united front against Japan.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of China may fourth to resistance as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
