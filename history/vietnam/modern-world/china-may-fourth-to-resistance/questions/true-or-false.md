# China may fourth to resistance — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 3, China from May Fourth to the resistance war", on 4 May 1919 three thousand Beijing students march and a movement is born; the Chinese Communist Party is founded in July 1921; from 1927 to 1937 it fights a revolutionary war against the Kuomintang, then in 1937 proposes the united front against Japan
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of China may fourth to resistance as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
