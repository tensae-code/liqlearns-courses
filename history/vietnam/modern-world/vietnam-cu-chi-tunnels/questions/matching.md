# Vietnam cu chi tunnels — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 26, the Củ Chi tunnels as a built underground world — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 26, digging the tunnels and the war underground — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) some 250 km of tunnels on three levels, 3–12 m deep, hide meeting rooms, stores, wells and the smokeless Hoàng Cầm kitchen invented in the earlier war against France
b) teams dig toward the tapping sounds of the team opposite, hide the soil in the river, disguise entrances as termite mounds, and hold out against US sweeps, bombs and poison — "land of steel, wall of bronze"

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
