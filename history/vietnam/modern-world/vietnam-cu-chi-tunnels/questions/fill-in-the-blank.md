# Vietnam cu chi tunnels — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 26, the Củ Chi tunnels as a built underground world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Some 250 km of tunnels on three levels, 3–12 m deep, hide meeting rooms, stores, wells and the smokeless Hoàng Cầm kitchen invented in the earlier war against France.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 26, digging the tunnels and the war underground" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Teams dig toward the tapping sounds of the team opposite, hide the soil in the river, disguise entrances as termite mounds, and hold out against US sweeps, bombs and poison — "land of steel, wall of bronze".
