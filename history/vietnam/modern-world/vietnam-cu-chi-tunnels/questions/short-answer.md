# Vietnam cu chi tunnels — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 26, the Củ Chi tunnels as a built underground world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Some 250 km of tunnels on three levels, 3–12 m deep, hide meeting rooms, stores, wells and the smokeless Hoàng Cầm kitchen invented in the earlier war against France.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 26, digging the tunnels and the war underground" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Teams dig toward the tapping sounds of the team opposite, hide the soil in the river, disguise entrances as termite mounds, and hold out against US sweeps, bombs and poison — "land of steel, wall of bronze".
