# Vietnam cu chi tunnels — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 26, the Củ Chi tunnels as a built underground world", some 250 km of tunnels on three levels, 3–12 m deep, hide meeting rooms, stores, wells and the smokeless Hoàng Cầm kitchen invented in the earlier war against France (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 26, digging the tunnels and the war underground", teams dig toward the tapping sounds of the team opposite, hide the soil in the river, disguise entrances as termite mounds, and hold out against US sweeps, bombs and poison — "land of steel, wall of bronze" (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
