# Vietnam hue 1885 counterattack — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 19, Tôn Thất Thuyết's counterattack at the Huế citadel, 1885" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The minister feigns illness to dodge a French summons, then strikes the French posts by night on 5 July 1885 and carries young King Hàm Nghi to the mountains to keep resisting.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam hue 1885 counterattack as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
