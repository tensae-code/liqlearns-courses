# The second world war causes

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 4, why the Second World War came

The Versailles settlement's quarrels over markets and colonies, deepened by the Depression, breed fascism and militarism in Germany and Japan with Italy beside them; two blocs form while the Western powers appease Hitler in hope of turning him against the USSR, against the Soviet line of an anti-fascist alliance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 2.I.2 Why the Second World War broke out: Versailles' national wounds, the League of Nations' powerlessness, the 1929–1933 crisis raising fascism in Germany, Italy and Japan, the Axis, and the Munich appeasement of 1938

The peace treaty humiliates Germany, the League watches, the crash delivers the voters, and London and Paris hand over the Sudetenland.
