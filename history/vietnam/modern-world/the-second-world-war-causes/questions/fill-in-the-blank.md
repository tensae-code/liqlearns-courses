# The second world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4, why the Second World War came" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Versailles settlement's quarrels over markets and colonies, deepened by the Depression, breed fascism and militarism in Germany and Japan with Italy beside them; two blocs form while the Western powers appease Hitler in hope of turning him against the USSR, against the Soviet line of an anti-fascist alliance.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.I.2 Why the Second World War broke out: Versailles' national wounds, the League of Nations' powerlessness, the 1929–1933 crisis raising fascism in Germany, Italy and Japan, the Axis, and the Munich appeasement of 1938" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The peace treaty humiliates Germany, the League watches, the crash delivers the voters, and London and Paris hand over the Sudetenland.
