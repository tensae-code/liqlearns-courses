# Southeast asia under western colonialism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4, how the Western powers entered Southeast Asia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The book's table runs country by country — Portugal takes Malacca in 1511 (its ship, the Flor do Mar, is now a museum there) and the eastern Indonesian islands; Spain seizes most of the Philippines by the mid-16th century and rules 350 years; the Dutch complete the conquest of Indonesia by the mid-19th century; Britain, France and the Netherlands contest Malaya, Burma and Indochina; Siam is entered by traders in the 16th century and pressed only after Britain has taken India and parts of Malaya and Burma.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4, Southeast Asia under colonial rule" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Native rulers surrender, collaborate or reign as puppets while colonial officials govern; the economy is plundered — land seized for plantations, no heavy industry allowed, railways and ports built for extraction and repression; Western culture erodes traditions and "enslavement" policies aim to assimilate; and a quoted Vietnamese history of the region shows the method — the British first merged Malaya, Singapore and Brunei into one unit, then split them into four separate regimes, so that each piece could be ruled more easily.
