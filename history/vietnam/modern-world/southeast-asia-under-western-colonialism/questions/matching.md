# Southeast asia under western colonialism — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 4, how the Western powers entered Southeast Asia — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 4, Southeast Asia under colonial rule — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the book's table runs country by country — Portugal takes Malacca in 1511 (its ship, the Flor do Mar, is now a museum there) and the eastern Indonesian islands; Spain seizes most of the Philippines by the mid-16th century and rules 350 years; the Dutch complete the conquest of Indonesia by the mid-19th century; Britain, France and the Netherlands contest Malaya, Burma and Indochina; Siam is entered by traders in the 16th century and pressed only after Britain has taken India and parts of Malaya and Burma
b) native rulers surrender, collaborate or reign as puppets while colonial officials govern; the economy is plundered — land seized for plantations, no heavy industry allowed, railways and ports built for extraction and repression; Western culture erodes traditions and "enslavement" policies aim to assimilate; and a quoted Vietnamese history of the region shows the method — the British first merged Malaya, Singapore and Brunei into one unit, then split them into four separate regimes, so that each piece could be ruled more easily

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
