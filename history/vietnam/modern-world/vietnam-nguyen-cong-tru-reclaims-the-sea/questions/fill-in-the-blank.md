# Vietnam nguyen cong tru reclaims the sea — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 13, land reclamation under the Nguyễn and the story of Nguyễn Công Trứ" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Sent as Doanh điền sứ, he gathers the landless poor to dyke the sea and founds the Tiền Hải and Kim Sơn districts; grateful settlers build his temple while he is still alive and call him "the ancestor who opened the land".

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam nguyen cong tru reclaims the sea as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
