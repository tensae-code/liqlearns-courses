# Vietnam nguyen cong tru reclaims the sea — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 13, land reclamation under the Nguyễn and the story of Nguyễn Công Trứ", sent as Doanh điền sứ, he gathers the landless poor to dyke the sea and founds the Tiền Hải and Kim Sơn districts; grateful settlers build his temple while he is still alive and call him "the ancestor who opened the land"
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam nguyen cong tru reclaims the sea as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
