# 1917 twice february and october in russia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, the Russian October Revolution, 1917" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** February overthrows the Tsar but leaves two powers side by side, the bourgeois Provisional Government and the Soviets, which cannot coexist — Lenin's quoted sentence insists power must pass "to the proletariat, the poorest strata and the peasants"; after the July repression the Bolsheviks prepare the armed rising, and on the night of 24–25 October (old style) the insurgents take Petrograd and storm the Winter Palace; Hồ Chí Minh's verdict is quoted — "Like the shining Sun, the October Revolution shines over the five continents" — and John Reed's eyewitness Ten Days That Shook the World is the lesson's set text.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of 1917 twice february and october in russia as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
