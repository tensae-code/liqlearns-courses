# Video script — 1917 twice february and october in russia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: 1917 twice february and october in russia.

February overthrows the Tsar but leaves two powers side by side, the bourgeois Provisional Government and the Soviets, which cannot coexist — Lenin's quoted sentence insists power must pass "to the proletariat, the poorest strata and the peasants"; after the July repression the Bolsheviks prepare the armed rising, and on the night of 24–25 October (old style) the insurgents take Petrograd and storm the Winter Palace; Hồ Chí Minh's verdict is quoted — "Like the shining Sun, the October Revolution shines over the five continents" — and John Reed's eyewitness Ten Days That Shook the World is the lesson's set text.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
