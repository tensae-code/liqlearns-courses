# The cold war alliances and crises — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, two camps and their crises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The book's manifestations table sets the Marshall Plan's $13 billion to 16 countries against the CMEA (1949), NATO (1949) against the Warsaw Treaty (1955), atomic bombs of 1945 and 1949 and satellites of 1957 and 1958, a Germany split into FRG (September 1949) and GDR (October 1949) — then the hot crises: Korea divided in 1948 and at war 1950–53, Cuba 1961 and the missiles of October 1962, and Vietnam, where both blocs play their parts from 1950 to 1975.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.II.1 What the Cold War was like and what it cost: no direct USA–USSR war but a fierce arms race; the Cuban missile crisis of October 1962 ends in mutual concessions and a leaders' hotline; local wars burn on — Korea costs over 3 million casualties, Vietnam's wars about 4 million dead or disabled" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The superpowers never fight each other, so the fighting is outsourced to Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan and the Middle East.
