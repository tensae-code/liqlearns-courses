# The cold war alliances and crises — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 9, two camps and their crises", the book's manifestations table sets the Marshall Plan's $13 billion to 16 countries against the CMEA (1949), NATO (1949) against the Warsaw Treaty (1955), atomic bombs of 1945 and 1949 and satellites of 1957 and 1958, a Germany split into FRG (September 1949) and GDR (October 1949) — then the hot crises: Korea divided in 1948 and at war 1950–53, Cuba 1961 and the missiles of October 1962, and Vietnam, where both blocs play their parts from 1950 to 1975 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 2.II.1 What the Cold War was like and what it cost: no direct USA–USSR war but a fierce arms race; the Cuban missile crisis of October 1962 ends in mutual concessions and a leaders' hotline; local wars burn on — Korea costs over 3 million casualties, Vietnam's wars about 4 million dead or disabled", the superpowers never fight each other, so the fighting is outsourced to Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan and the Middle East (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
