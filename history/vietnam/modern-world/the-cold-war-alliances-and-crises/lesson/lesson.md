## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 9, two camps and their crises

The book's manifestations table sets the Marshall Plan's $13 billion to 16 countries against the CMEA (1949), NATO (1949) against the Warsaw Treaty (1955), atomic bombs of 1945 and 1949 and satellites of 1957 and 1958, a Germany split into FRG (September 1949) and GDR (October 1949) — then the hot crises: Korea divided in 1948 and at war 1950–53, Cuba 1961 and the missiles of October 1962, and Vietnam, where both blocs play their parts from 1950 to 1975.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 2.II.1 What the Cold War was like and what it cost: no direct USA–USSR war but a fierce arms race; the Cuban missile crisis of October 1962 ends in mutual concessions and a leaders' hotline; local wars burn on — Korea costs over 3 million casualties, Vietnam's wars about 4 million dead or disabled

The superpowers never fight each other, so the fighting is outsourced to Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan and the Middle East.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 2 The bipolar order at war with itself, 1945 to the early 1970s: NATO (1949) faces the Warsaw Pact (1955) while the two poles fight through local wars — Korea 1950–53, France's and then America's wars in Vietnam, the Middle East from 1948 — and the order already cracks as China's 1949 victory breaks the American scheme in Asia and Western Europe and Japan outgrow American tutelage

The two camps never fight each other directly, so the fighting is outsourced to other people's countries.
