# Video script — The cold war alliances and crises

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The cold war alliances and crises.

The book's manifestations table sets the Marshall Plan's $13 billion to 16 countries against the CMEA (1949), NATO (1949) against the Warsaw Treaty (1955), atomic bombs of 1945 and 1949 and satellites of 1957 and 1958, a Germany split into FRG (September 1949) and GDR (October 1949) — then the hot crises: Korea divided in 1948 and at war 1950–53, Cuba 1961 and the missiles of October 1962, and Vietnam, where both blocs play their parts from 1950 to 1975.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
