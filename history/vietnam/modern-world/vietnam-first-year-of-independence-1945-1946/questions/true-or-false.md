# Vietnam first year of independence 1945 1946 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 13, building the new state amid two occupying armies", chiang's troops hold the North with Việt Quốc and Việt Cách saboteurs while the British open the road for France in the South; on 6 January 1946 the first general election seats over 300 deputies — Hồ Chí Minh tops the poll after his appeal of 5 January — and the March 1946 National Assembly forms a coalition resistance government, conceding 70 unelected seats to Việt Quốc and Việt Cách and food and currency circulation to Chiang's army to buy peace; a Constitution follows on 9 November 1946, People's Councils govern locally, and the Liberation Army becomes the National Guard (September 1945) then the National Army (May 1946) — Hồ Chí Minh's quoted rule for it all: every government organ "is a servant of the people" (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 13, fighting famine and illiteracy", against the famine the government launches rice jars, "share rice, share clothes" and a "golden heart day", punishes hoarders, orders "not one inch of land left fallow", gives confiscated land to poor peasants and cuts rents and taxes; the Independence Fund and Gold Week (17–24 September 1945) gather some 60 million đồng and 370 kg of gold — the book names donors such as Trịnh Văn Bô — and Vietnamese currency is issued in September 1946; against illiteracy above 90%, the decree of 8 September 1945 founds the Bình dân học vụ popular-education service, which teaches 2.5 million people in the North and Centre to read and write within a year (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
