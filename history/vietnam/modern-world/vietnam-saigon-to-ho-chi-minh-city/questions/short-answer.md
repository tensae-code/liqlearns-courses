# Vietnam saigon to ho chi minh city — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 25, the city's names across 300 years (Gia Định, Sài Gòn, Sài Gòn – Chợ Lớn, renamed Hồ Chí Minh City in 1976)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** One city carries each era in its name, from Gia Định to the 1976 renaming.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 17 "Living forever" at home: the Mausoleum and the Hồ Chí Minh Museum with fourteen branches, people's own temples to him nationwide, the statue communist prisoners smuggled out of Côn Đảo prison (returned by the prison director's son in 2019), the supply routes land and sea that bore his name, the study-and-follow campaigns of 2006 and 2016 — and in 1976 the National Assembly renames Saigon as Ho Chi Minh City, recording that its people "always have boundless reverence for President Hồ Chí Minh and ardently want the city to bear his name"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The state builds the mausoleum, but the book's evidence for immortality is what prisoners, porters and a renamed city did unasked.
