# Meiji japan and xinhai china — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2 Asia's two answers: Japan's Meiji Restoration from 1868 reforms politics, economy, army and schools, escapes colonisation — and becomes an empire itself in Northeast Asia; China's Xinhai Revolution of 1911 topples the Qing and ends two millennia of absolutist monarchy" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** One neighbour modernises from the throne down and survives; the other cuts off the throne itself.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Meiji japan and xinhai china as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
