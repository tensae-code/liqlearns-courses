# Meiji japan and xinhai china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 2 Asia's two answers: Japan's Meiji Restoration from 1868 reforms politics, economy, army and schools, escapes colonisation — and becomes an empire itself in Northeast Asia; China's Xinhai Revolution of 1911 topples the Qing and ends two millennia of absolutist monarchy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
