# Vietnam national democratic movement 1918 1930 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 5, Vietnam's national-democratic movement abroad and at home, 1918–1930 — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 5, the workers enter the struggle — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) abroad, the Tâm Tâm Xã society in Canton (1923) attempts to assassinate Governor-General Merlin at Sa Điền (1924) while Phan Châu Trinh works through the Association of Patriotic Vietnamese in France (1919); at home the Constitutional Party (1923) campaigns in Saigon, crowds boycott monopolies and demand "Vietnamese use Vietnamese goods" (1919), and mass movements demand freedom for Phan Bội Châu (1925) and Nguyễn An Ninh (1926) and fill the streets for Phan Châu Trinh's funeral (1926)
b) hải Phòng's Sác-nô sailors strike in 1919 and 600 Saigon–Chợ Lớn dyers in 1920; then, in August 1925, over a thousand Ba Son shipyard workers under Tôn Đức Thắng strike and stop a French warship carrying troops against the Chinese revolution — the milestone, quoted from historian Đinh Xuân Lâm, where Vietnamese workers enter organised struggle with a clear political purpose; from 1926 organised strikes spread to Mạo Khê, Trường Thi and Phú Riềng

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
