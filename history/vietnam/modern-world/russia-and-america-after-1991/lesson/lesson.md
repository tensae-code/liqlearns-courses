## Bài 18, Russia and America after the Cold War

Russia passes from 1990s instability over its political system to the presidential republic of the December 1993 Constitution, balances Europe and Asia in foreign policy, and climbs from crisis (1991–99: GDP growth −4.8%, inflation 22%, budget deficit) to recovery (2000–21: growth around 3.5% a year, budget surplus, a top-ten world economy with 2021 GDP near $1.5 trillion), joining the WTO in 2012 with defence, energy, mining and aerospace still its spearheads; America keeps its two-party democracy and its aim of leading the world — Clinton's 1990s "engagement and enlargement" strategy of strong military, stronger alliances and the "democracy" slogan — and remains the largest economy (over 30% of world GDP in 2000, 24% in 2021, per-capita GDP above $70,000 in 2021, leading the WTO, World Bank and IMF) despite short recessions in 1997–98, 2008–09 and 2014–15.
