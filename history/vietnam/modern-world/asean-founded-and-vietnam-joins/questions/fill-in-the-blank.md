# Asean founded and vietnam joins — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 21, the founding of ASEAN" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** On 8 August 1967 in Bangkok, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines and Singapore sign ASEAN into being to keep the region peaceful and develop together.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 21, Vietnam in ASEAN" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On 28 July 1995 Vietnam becomes ASEAN's seventh member, opening markets, investment and cultural exchange, and later chairs the association in 2010 and 2020.
