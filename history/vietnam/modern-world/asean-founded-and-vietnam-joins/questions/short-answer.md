# Asean founded and vietnam joins — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 21, the founding of ASEAN" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** On 8 August 1967 in Bangkok, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines and Singapore sign ASEAN into being to keep the region peaceful and develop together.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 21, Vietnam in ASEAN" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On 28 July 1995 Vietnam becomes ASEAN's seventh member, opening markets, investment and cultural exchange, and later chairs the association in 2010 and 2020.
