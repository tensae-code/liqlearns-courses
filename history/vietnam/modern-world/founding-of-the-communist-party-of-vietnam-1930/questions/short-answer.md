# Founding of the communist party of vietnam 1930 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 6, three parties become one" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** In 1929 three rival communist organisations appear — the Indochinese Communist Party (Hanoi, June), the Annam Communist Party (Saigon, August) and the Indochinese Communist League (September); Nguyễn Ái Quốc, as the Comintern's envoy, chairs the unification conference at Hương Cảng from 6 January 1930, which adopts the Brief Programme, Strategy and Charter, and the League joins on 24 February; the founding, the Institute of History is quoted as saying, ends the crisis over the road of national salvation.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Founding of the communist party of vietnam 1930 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
