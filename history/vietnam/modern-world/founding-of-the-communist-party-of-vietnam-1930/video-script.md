# Video script — Founding of the communist party of vietnam 1930

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Founding of the communist party of vietnam 1930.

In 1929 three rival communist organisations appear — the Indochinese Communist Party (Hanoi, June), the Annam Communist Party (Saigon, August) and the Indochinese Communist League (September); Nguyễn Ái Quốc, as the Comintern's envoy, chairs the unification conference at Hương Cảng from 6 January 1930, which adopts the Brief Programme, Strategy and Charter, and the League joins on 24 February; the founding, the Institute of History is quoted as saying, ends the crisis over the road of national salvation.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
