# The great powers 1900 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 10, the empires profiled: Britain, France, Germany, the United States" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Britain slips from first in industry to third — the quoted figures show its share of world industrial output falling from 32% in 1870 to 14% by 1914 while America's climbs to 38% — yet it still leads in capital export and colonies (33.5 million km² by 1914); France, slowed by the 1870–71 defeat, stands fourth in industry but second in capital export and colonies (10.6 million km², Indochina among them) under an unstable Third Republic; Germany, unified only in 1871, races to first in Europe and demands an arms race to redivide a world already taken; the United States doubles Britain's industrial output by 1894 behind its "oil king" Rockefeller, "steel king" Morgan and "car king" Ford, takes the Philippines and Cuba from Spain and closes Central and South America into its own sphere.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of The great powers 1900 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
