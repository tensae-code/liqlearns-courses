# Vietnam august revolution 1945 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 14, the August Revolution in Hanoi, Huế and Saigon — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 8, the road to August: famine, Việt Minh and the Japanese coup — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) japan surrenders; on 19 August Hanoi's crowds take the Residency, on 23 August Huế rises, on 25 August Saigon rises, and by month's end the general uprising has won across the country
b) france falls in June 1940 and Japan enters at Lạng Sơn that September, imposing a dual rule whose grain seizures bring the famine of late 1944–early 1945; the first risings flare at Bắc Sơn (1940), Nam Kì — where the red flag with gold star first appears (November 1940) — and Đô Lương (1941); at Pác Bó in May 1941 the Eighth Plenum under Nguyễn Ái Quốc declares "only one urgent problem: national liberation", founds the Việt Minh (19 May 1941) and, in December 1944, the Propaganda Liberation Army Team that wins its first fights at Phay Khắt and Nà Ngần; when Japan coups the French on 9 March 1945, the Party's directive of 12 March — "Japan and France shoot each other" — unleashes the anti-Japanese high tide

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
