# Vietnam august revolution 1945 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 14, the August Revolution in Hanoi, Huế and Saigon" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Japan surrenders; on 19 August Hanoi's crowds take the Residency, on 23 August Huế rises, on 25 August Saigon rises, and by month's end the general uprising has won across the country.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8, the road to August: famine, Việt Minh and the Japanese coup" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** France falls in June 1940 and Japan enters at Lạng Sơn that September, imposing a dual rule whose grain seizures bring the famine of late 1944–early 1945; the first risings flare at Bắc Sơn (1940), Nam Kì — where the red flag with gold star first appears (November 1940) — and Đô Lương (1941); at Pác Bó in May 1941 the Eighth Plenum under Nguyễn Ái Quốc declares "only one urgent problem: national liberation", founds the Việt Minh (19 May 1941) and, in December 1944, the Propaganda Liberation Army Team that wins its first fights at Phay Khắt and Nà Ngần; when Japan coups the French on 9 March 1945, the Party's directive of 12 March — "Japan and France shoot each other" — unleashes the anti-Japanese high tide.
