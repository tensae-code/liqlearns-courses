# Vietnam tet offensive and vietnamisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, Local War, Tết 1968 and Vietnamisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** America escalates to "Local War"; the victory at Vạn Tường (18 August 1965) opens the "find Americans to fight" tide and the dry-season counteroffensives of 1965–67 are defeated; then, on the night of 30–31 January 1968, the General Offensive and Uprising of Tết Mậu Thân strikes almost every city at once — the US Embassy, Independence Palace and the airport in Saigon among them — and America declares "de-Americanisation", admits the Local War strategy's failure and accepts Paris talks; under "Vietnamisation" the Provisional Revolutionary Government of the Republic of South Vietnam is founded (6 June 1969), Hồ Chí Minh dies leaving his Testament — "our people will definitely win complete victory… our Fatherland definitely will unify" — and the victories table runs on: Cambodia (April–June 1970), Lam Sơn 719 with Lao forces (February–March 1971), and the Quảng Trị strategic offensive (March–June 1972) piercing three defence lines and putting over 200,000 enemy troops out of action — Vietnamisation fails.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 8 "Local War" and the 1968 turn: after the fabricated Gulf of Tonkin incident the US lands its own army (from mid-1965) and bombs the North; Vạn Tường (18-8-1965) shows American units can be beaten, two dry-season counter-offensives are smashed, and the North shoots down 3,243 aircraft while multiplying its aid to the South tenfold; the Tet General Offensive and Uprising (early 1968), centred on the cities, breaks Washington's will — the US admits "Local War" has failed, stops bombing the North unconditionally and sits down in Paris" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The offensive that loses the ground war headlines wins the political war in American living rooms.
