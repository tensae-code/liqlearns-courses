# Vietnam nam ki resistance 1862 1874 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 17, the South refuses the treaty" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** While Huế suppresses revolts elsewhere, France seizes the three western provinces in 1867 and the southern resistance only grows; Trương Định (1820–1864), acclaimed by the people as Bình Tây Đại Nguyên soái — Grand Marshal of the Western Pacification — fights from Gò Công and Tân Phước until, wounded in a surprise attack sprung by a traitor, he draws his sword and kills himself rather than fall into enemy hands, and his son Trương Quyền fights on from Tây Ninh; Nguyễn Trung Trực (1838–1868) raids the Kiên Giang fort from across the sea and, led to execution, declares: "Until the Westerners have pulled up all the grass of the South, there will still be Southerners to fight the Westerners"; the blind poet Nguyễn Đình Chiểu and Phan Văn Trị scourge the invaders and their collaborators in verse, and Nguyễn Hữu Huân, captured twice, composes defiant poetry on his way to the scaffold.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam nam ki resistance 1862 1874 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
