# Vietnam hanoi 1882 1945 1972 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 12, three days that made modern Hanoi", governor Hoàng Diệu dies defending the citadel from the French in 1882; Hồ Chí Minh reads the Declaration of Independence at Ba Đình in 1945; Hanoi's people shoot down US bombers in the twelve days of December 1972, the "Điện Biên Phủ in the air" (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 17, the two French descents on Hanoi and the Cầu Giấy victories, 1873–1884", in November 1873 Francis Garnier storms Hanoi; Governor Nguyễn Tri Phương, wounded and captured, starves himself to death to keep his honour; on 21 December the court army and Lưu Vĩnh Phúc's Black Flags ambush the French at Cầu Giấy and kill Garnier; the Treaty of Giáp Tuất (1874) gives France all six southern provinces — and, the quoted history warns, the bases from which it will return; in April 1882 Henri Rivière takes Hanoi after Governor Hoàng Diệu's suicide in the Võ Miếu garden, spreads over the delta, and on 19 May 1883 is ambushed and killed at Cầu Giấy in his turn; France answers by storming Thuận An at Huế's sea gate and imposing the Harmand treaty (1883) and the Patenôtre treaty of 6 June 1884 — the protectorate (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
