# Vietnam can vuong and ham nghi — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4, story box: ethnic unity in the Cần Vương movement" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Nguyễn court surrenders to France, but in 1885 King Hàm Nghi issues the Cần Vương edict and the Bru–Vân Kiều, Tà Ôi and Cơ Tu peoples of Tân Sở feed, house and hide the king so well the French never find him.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 13, the Cần Vương movement against the French" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** After the 1884 treaty recognises the French protectorate, the war faction around young King Hàm Nghi and Tôn Thất Thuyết proclaims the Cần Vương edict in 1885 and risings spread nationwide.
