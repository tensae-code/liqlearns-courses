# Vietnam can vuong and ham nghi — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 4, story box: ethnic unity in the Cần Vương movement", the Nguyễn court surrenders to France, but in 1885 King Hàm Nghi issues the Cần Vương edict and the Bru–Vân Kiều, Tà Ôi and Cơ Tu peoples of Tân Sở feed, house and hide the king so well the French never find him (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 13, the Cần Vương movement against the French", after the 1884 treaty recognises the French protectorate, the war faction around young King Hàm Nghi and Tôn Thất Thuyết proclaims the Cần Vương edict in 1885 and risings spread nationwide (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
