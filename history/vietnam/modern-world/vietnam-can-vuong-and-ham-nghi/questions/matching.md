# Vietnam can vuong and ham nghi — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 4, story box: ethnic unity in the Cần Vương movement — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 13, the Cần Vương movement against the French — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the Nguyễn court surrenders to France, but in 1885 King Hàm Nghi issues the Cần Vương edict and the Bru–Vân Kiều, Tà Ôi and Cơ Tu peoples of Tân Sở feed, house and hide the king so well the French never find him
b) after the 1884 treaty recognises the French protectorate, the war faction around young King Hàm Nghi and Tôn Thất Thuyết proclaims the Cần Vương edict in 1885 and risings spread nationwide

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
