# Vietnam can vuong and ham nghi

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Vietnam can vuong and ham nghi" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 4, story box: ethnic unity in the Cần Vương movement

The Nguyễn court surrenders to France, but in 1885 King Hàm Nghi issues the Cần Vương edict and the Bru–Vân Kiều, Tà Ôi and Cơ Tu peoples of Tân Sở feed, house and hide the king so well the French never find him.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 13, the Cần Vương movement against the French

After the 1884 treaty recognises the French protectorate, the war faction around young King Hàm Nghi and Tôn Thất Thuyết proclaims the Cần Vương edict in 1885 and risings spread nationwide.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 18, the Cần Vương movement breaks out, 1885

After the Patenôtre treaty a war party still stands in the Huế court around Tôn Thất Thuyết, Minister of War, who builds forces, stockpiles food and weapons and puts the boy Hàm Nghi on the throne; when his counterattack on the French in the capital fails on 5 July 1885 he carries the king to Tân Sở in Quảng Trị and, in Hàm Nghi's name, issues the Cần Vương edict — the Appeal to Aid the King — calling literati and people across the country to rise; the movement burns to the century's end.
