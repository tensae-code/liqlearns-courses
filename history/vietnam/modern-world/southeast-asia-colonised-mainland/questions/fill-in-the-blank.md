# Southeast asia colonised mainland — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5 The mainland falls later: Britain needs three wars across sixty years (1824–1885) to take Burma; France needs nearly half a century (1858–1893) to take the three Indochina countries; Siam alone keeps formal independence as a buffer between the two empires" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The mainland kingdoms make the empires pay in decades, and one kingdom survives by standing between them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Southeast asia colonised mainland as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
