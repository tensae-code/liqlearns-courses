# Video script — Vietnam dong khoi and special war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam dong khoi and special war.

Washington's Special War strategy fields Saigon troops under US command — "use Vietnamese to fight Vietnamese" — behind strategic hamlets, helicopters and armoured vehicles, promising pacification in eighteen months; it breaks step by step: the 1962 sweeps are defeated and the hamlets smashed, at Ấp Bắc on 2 January 1963 over 2,000 Saigon troops under US advisers are beaten (and "Emulate Ấp Bắc" sweeps the South), and An Lão, Ba Gia and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) end the strategy.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
