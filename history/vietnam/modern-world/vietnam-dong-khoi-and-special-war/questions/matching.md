# Vietnam dong khoi and special war — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 16, breaking the "Special War" — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 8 A divided country, 1954–1960: the US replaces France behind Ngô Đình Diệm, aiming to partition Vietnam permanently; the North completes land reform and heals its wounds while the South demands the Geneva elections, preserves its forces, and — after the 15th Central Conference (1959) rules there is no road but revolutionary violence — rises in the Đồng khởi movement from Vĩnh Thạnh and Trà Bồng to Bến Tre (Jan 1960), whose women form the famous "long-haired army"; victory shifts the South from conserving forces to the offensive and births the National Liberation Front (20-12-1960) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) washington's Special War strategy fields Saigon troops under US command — "use Vietnamese to fight Vietnamese" — behind strategic hamlets, helicopters and armoured vehicles, promising pacification in eighteen months; it breaks step by step: the 1962 sweeps are defeated and the hamlets smashed, at Ấp Bắc on 2 January 1963 over 2,000 Saigon troops under US advisers are beaten (and "Emulate Ấp Bắc" sweeps the South), and An Lão, Ba Gia and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) end the strategy
b) village uprisings with coconut-thorn spears crack the client state open and a front is born

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
