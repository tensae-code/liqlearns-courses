# Vietnam dong khoi and special war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 16, breaking the "Special War"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Washington's Special War strategy fields Saigon troops under US command — "use Vietnamese to fight Vietnamese" — behind strategic hamlets, helicopters and armoured vehicles, promising pacification in eighteen months; it breaks step by step: the 1962 sweeps are defeated and the hamlets smashed, at Ấp Bắc on 2 January 1963 over 2,000 Saigon troops under US advisers are beaten (and "Emulate Ấp Bắc" sweeps the South), and An Lão, Ba Gia and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) end the strategy.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 8 A divided country, 1954–1960: the US replaces France behind Ngô Đình Diệm, aiming to partition Vietnam permanently; the North completes land reform and heals its wounds while the South demands the Geneva elections, preserves its forces, and — after the 15th Central Conference (1959) rules there is no road but revolutionary violence — rises in the Đồng khởi movement from Vĩnh Thạnh and Trà Bồng to Bến Tre (Jan 1960), whose women form the famous "long-haired army"; victory shifts the South from conserving forces to the offensive and births the National Liberation Front (20-12-1960)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Village uprisings with coconut-thorn spears crack the client state open and a front is born.
