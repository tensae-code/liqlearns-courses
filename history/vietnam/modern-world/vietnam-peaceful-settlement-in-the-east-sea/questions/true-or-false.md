# Vietnam peaceful settlement in the east sea — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 13 Vietnam's peaceful-settlement stance: the 1977 declaration on sea zones, white papers of 1979, 1981 and 1988, joining UNCLOS 1982 (a million km² of sea, three times the land), the 2012 Sea Law, and full implementation of the 2002 ASEAN–China Declaration on Conduct signed at Phnom Penh — disputes by peaceful means, without threat or use of force", the country that fought for the islands chooses law as its first weapon for keeping them (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 9 Defending sovereignty in the East Sea: the reunified state administers Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa; the 12-5-1977 declaration defines Vietnam's seas, Vietnam ratifies UNCLOS as its 63rd state (1994), island districts are founded (1982) and a Trường Sa town (2007), and sovereignty is exercised through exhibitions, surveys, markers, lighthouses and island communities; when Chinese forces attack Gạc Ma, Cô Lin and Len Đao (March 1988) and dozens of naval soldiers fall, Vietnam protests to the UN and presses negotiation; the 2012 Sea Law gives the defence its legal base, with the DOC pushed forward", sovereignty here is defended as much by declarations, districts and law as by the navy's sacrifice at Gạc Ma (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
