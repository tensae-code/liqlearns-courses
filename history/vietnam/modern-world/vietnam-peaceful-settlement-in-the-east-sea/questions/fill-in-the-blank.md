# Vietnam peaceful settlement in the east sea — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 13 Vietnam's peaceful-settlement stance: the 1977 declaration on sea zones, white papers of 1979, 1981 and 1988, joining UNCLOS 1982 (a million km² of sea, three times the land), the 2012 Sea Law, and full implementation of the 2002 ASEAN–China Declaration on Conduct signed at Phnom Penh — disputes by peaceful means, without threat or use of force" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The country that fought for the islands chooses law as its first weapon for keeping them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9 Defending sovereignty in the East Sea: the reunified state administers Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa; the 12-5-1977 declaration defines Vietnam's seas, Vietnam ratifies UNCLOS as its 63rd state (1994), island districts are founded (1982) and a Trường Sa town (2007), and sovereignty is exercised through exhibitions, surveys, markers, lighthouses and island communities; when Chinese forces attack Gạc Ma, Cô Lin and Len Đao (March 1988) and dozens of naval soldiers fall, Vietnam protests to the UN and presses negotiation; the 2012 Sea Law gives the defence its legal base, with the DOC pushed forward" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sovereignty here is defended as much by declarations, districts and law as by the navy's sacrifice at Gạc Ma.
