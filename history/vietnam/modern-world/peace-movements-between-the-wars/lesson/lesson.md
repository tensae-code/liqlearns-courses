## Chuyên đề 2.I.3 The struggle for peace between the wars: Lenin's Decree on Peace of 26-10-1917, the first anti-war programme in history; the League of Nations founded 10-1-1920; Locarno 1925; the 1928 Paris treaty renouncing war signed by 15 nations, later 57

Out of the trenches comes the first machinery for peace — a decree, a league, and a promise to renounce war.
