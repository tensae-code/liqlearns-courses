# Peace movements between the wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.I.3 The struggle for peace between the wars: Lenin's Decree on Peace of 26-10-1917, the first anti-war programme in history; the League of Nations founded 10-1-1920; Locarno 1925; the 1928 Paris treaty renouncing war signed by 15 nations, later 57" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Out of the trenches comes the first machinery for peace — a decree, a league, and a promise to renounce war.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Peace movements between the wars as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
