# The cold war ends 1989 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 9, how the Cold War ends", after four decades of constant tension, local wars and divided countries, the Cold War is declared ended at the informal Malta meeting of December 1989 — the book closes the lesson with the falling Berlin Wall (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 2.II.1 Why the Cold War ended: the arms race drains both sides (the USSR spending 11–13% of national income on defence against America's 7–8%); Japan and Western Europe rise; détente and SALT-1 in 1972; Gorbachev's reform mistakes; Malta, December 1989, then the USSR dissolves and settlements follow in Afghanistan, Cambodia and Namibia", the race bankrupts the runners, and at Malta the two captains shake hands over a finished war (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
