# The cold war ends 1989 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, how the Cold War ends" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After four decades of constant tension, local wars and divided countries, the Cold War is declared ended at the informal Malta meeting of December 1989 — the book closes the lesson with the falling Berlin Wall.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.II.1 Why the Cold War ended: the arms race drains both sides (the USSR spending 11–13% of national income on defence against America's 7–8%); Japan and Western Europe rise; détente and SALT-1 in 1972; Gorbachev's reform mistakes; Malta, December 1989, then the USSR dissolves and settlements follow in Afghanistan, Cambodia and Namibia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The race bankrupts the runners, and at Malta the two captains shake hands over a finished war.
