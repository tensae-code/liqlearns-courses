# The cold war ends 1989 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 9, how the Cold War ends" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After four decades of constant tension, local wars and divided countries, the Cold War is declared ended at the informal Malta meeting of December 1989 — the book closes the lesson with the falling Berlin Wall.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chuyên đề 2.II.1 Why the Cold War ended: the arms race drains both sides (the USSR spending 11–13% of national income on defence against America's 7–8%); Japan and Western Europe rise; détente and SALT-1 in 1972; Gorbachev's reform mistakes; Malta, December 1989, then the USSR dissolves and settlements follow in Afghanistan, Cambodia and Namibia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The race bankrupts the runners, and at Malta the two captains shake hands over a finished war.
