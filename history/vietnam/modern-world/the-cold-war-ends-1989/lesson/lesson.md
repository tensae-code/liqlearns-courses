# The cold war ends 1989

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The cold war ends 1989" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 9, how the Cold War ends

After four decades of constant tension, local wars and divided countries, the Cold War is declared ended at the informal Malta meeting of December 1989 — the book closes the lesson with the falling Berlin Wall.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 2.II.1 Why the Cold War ended: the arms race drains both sides (the USSR spending 11–13% of national income on defence against America's 7–8%); Japan and Western Europe rise; détente and SALT-1 in 1972; Gorbachev's reform mistakes; Malta, December 1989, then the USSR dissolves and settlements follow in Afghanistan, Cambodia and Namibia

The race bankrupts the runners, and at Malta the two captains shake hands over a finished war.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 2 The Yalta order collapses: détente and the first arms-limitation agreements (1972), the 1987 treaty abolishing intermediate missiles, the 1989 declaration that the Cold War is over, Eastern Europe's socialism falling and the USSR dissolving in December 1991 — the book counts five causes, from the ruinous cost of confrontation to Soviet crisis and reform mistakes, and notes America's brief advantage as other centres rise

The order ends not with a battle but with summits, signatures and one superpower quietly ceasing to exist.
