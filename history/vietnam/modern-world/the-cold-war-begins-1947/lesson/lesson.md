## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 9, the Cold War begins

Allies turn rivals; in March 1947 President Truman's speech to Congress proclaims the doctrine of containing communism (quoted here through historian Nguyễn Anh Thái), and Raymond Aron's opener frames the age — "peace is impossible, war cannot happen".

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 2.II.1 How the Cold War began: Truman's speech of 12-3-1947 announcing containment; the Marshall Plan's 13 billion dollars; the CMEA founded January 1949; NATO in April 1949 and the Warsaw Treaty in May 1955 locking the bipolar order

A speech, a chequebook and two alliances divide the world without a shot fired.
