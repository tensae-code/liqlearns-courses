# The cold war begins 1947 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 9, the Cold War begins", allies turn rivals; in March 1947 President Truman's speech to Congress proclaims the doctrine of containing communism (quoted here through historian Nguyễn Anh Thái), and Raymond Aron's opener frames the age — "peace is impossible, war cannot happen" (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 2.II.1 How the Cold War began: Truman's speech of 12-3-1947 announcing containment; the Marshall Plan's 13 billion dollars; the CMEA founded January 1949; NATO in April 1949 and the Warsaw Treaty in May 1955 locking the bipolar order", a speech, a chequebook and two alliances divide the world without a shot fired (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
