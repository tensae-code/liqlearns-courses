# Video script — Vietnam quang trung builds the country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam quang trung builds the country.

From Phú Xuân he issues the Edict Encouraging Agriculture to bring abandoned fields and wandering people back to production, cuts taxes, opens the passes and markets ("mở cửa ải, thông chợ búa"), proclaims the Edict Establishing Schools and makes chữ Nôm the state's official script, builds a strong army and conducts a diplomacy "both soft and resolute" — before his sudden death in 1792, mourned in Princess Ngọc Hân's verse; the book's verdict on the movement: it overthrew the Nguyễn and Trịnh regimes, ended two centuries of partition, and smashed the Siamese and Qing invasions, firmly defending the nation's independence.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
