# Vietnam quang trung builds the country — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 8, Quang Trung the builder, and the meaning of Tây Sơn — a)
**2.** The one thing this lesson teaches — b)

Meanings:
a) from Phú Xuân he issues the Edict Encouraging Agriculture to bring abandoned fields and wandering people back to production, cuts taxes, opens the passes and markets ("mở cửa ải, thông chợ búa"), proclaims the Edict Establishing Schools and makes chữ Nôm the state's official script, builds a strong army and conducts a diplomacy "both soft and resolute" — before his sudden death in 1792, mourned in Princess Ngọc Hân's verse; the book's verdict on the movement: it overthrew the Nguyễn and Trịnh regimes, ended two centuries of partition, and smashed the Siamese and Qing invasions, firmly defending the nation's independence
b) the story of Vietnam quang trung builds the country as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
