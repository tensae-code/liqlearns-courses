# Southeast asia road to independence

## Bài 6 Three stages to independence: feudal-led resistance gives way to bourgeois movements (the Philippines rising of 1896–1898); from 1920 the proletariat enters — communist parties in Indonesia (1920) and across the region in the 1930s; 1945–1975 completes it — Indonesia, Vietnam and Laos declare in 1945, the Philippines is granted 1946, Burma 1948, Brunei last in 1984

The flag changes hands from kings to parties to peoples, and in one autumn of 1945 three nations declare themselves.
