# The renaissance what changed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 3, what the Renaissance meant" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A bourgeois culture sets human worth and personal freedom against feudal ideology, voicing itself in Don Quixote's line that deeds, not bloodlines, make a person, and Machiavelli's that one's great duty is to the fatherland.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
