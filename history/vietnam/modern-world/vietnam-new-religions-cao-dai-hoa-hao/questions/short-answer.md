# Vietnam new religions cao dai hoa hao — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Chuyên đề 1 Faiths Vietnam invented: Cao Đài, founded in Tây Ninh in 1926 by officials, landlords and intellectuals fusing Buddhism, Confucianism, Daoism and southern folk belief under one Holy See — its followers count some 4,000 martyrs in the anti-American war — and Hòa Hảo Buddhism, founded in 1939 by Huỳnh Phú Sổ on lay practice and the Four Debts of Gratitude, with about 1.3 million followers by 2022" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** In one generation the colonised South answers imported religions by manufacturing its own.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam new religions cao dai hoa hao as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
