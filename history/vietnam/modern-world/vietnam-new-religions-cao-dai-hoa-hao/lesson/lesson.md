## Chuyên đề 1 Faiths Vietnam invented: Cao Đài, founded in Tây Ninh in 1926 by officials, landlords and intellectuals fusing Buddhism, Confucianism, Daoism and southern folk belief under one Holy See — its followers count some 4,000 martyrs in the anti-American war — and Hòa Hảo Buddhism, founded in 1939 by Huỳnh Phú Sổ on lay practice and the Four Debts of Gratitude, with about 1.3 million followers by 2022

In one generation the colonised South answers imported religions by manufacturing its own.
