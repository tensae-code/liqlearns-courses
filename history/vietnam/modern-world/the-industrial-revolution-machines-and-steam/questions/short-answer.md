# The industrial revolution machines and steam — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 3, the Industrial Revolution in England" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** It begins in textiles with the Jenny spinning machine (1764); Arkwright's water frame (1769) leads to the first mill in 1771; Watt's steam engine (1784) — Westminster's memorial calls him "the man who multiplied many times the strength of man" — frees factories from the riverside; Cartwright's power loom (1785), Stephenson's locomotive (1814) and the first railway (1825) follow, and by 1850 England has some 10,000 km of track and is named "the workshop of the world".

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of The industrial revolution machines and steam as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
