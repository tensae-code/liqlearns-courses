# Video script — The industrial revolution machines and steam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The industrial revolution machines and steam.

It begins in textiles with the Jenny spinning machine (1764); Arkwright's water frame (1769) leads to the first mill in 1771; Watt's steam engine (1784) — Westminster's memorial calls him "the man who multiplied many times the strength of man" — frees factories from the riverside; Cartwright's power loom (1785), Stephenson's locomotive (1814) and the first railway (1825) follow, and by 1850 England has some 10,000 km of track and is named "the workshop of the world".

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
