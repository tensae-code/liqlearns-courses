# The first world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, why the First World War came" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Uneven development reshuffles the empires' strengths until the quarrel over colonies is unbearable — the "old" empires England and France hold the most, the "young" Germany far too few; two armed blocs face off, the Triple Alliance of 1882 against the Triple Entente of 1907, Balkan tension in 1912–13 loads the gun, and the assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir by a Serb on 28 June 1914 pulls the trigger — Germany declares war on Russia on 1 August 1914.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.I.1 Why the First World War broke out: uneven capitalist development, the Triple Alliance against the Triple Entente, Germany's colonial ambition and arms race, and the Sarajevo assassination of 28-6-1914 as the drop that overflowed the glass" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two armed camps wait for an excuse, and a Serbian student in Sarajevo hands it to them.
