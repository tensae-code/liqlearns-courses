# Video script — The first world war causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The first world war causes.

Uneven development reshuffles the empires' strengths until the quarrel over colonies is unbearable — the "old" empires England and France hold the most, the "young" Germany far too few; two armed blocs face off, the Triple Alliance of 1882 against the Triple Entente of 1907, Balkan tension in 1912–13 loads the gun, and the assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir by a Serb on 28 June 1914 pulls the trigger — Germany declares war on Russia on 1 August 1914.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
