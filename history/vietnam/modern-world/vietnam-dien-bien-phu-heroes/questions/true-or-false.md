# Vietnam dien bien phu heroes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 15, stories of the heroes of Điện Biên Phủ (Bế Văn Đàn; Phan Đình Giót)", tày soldier Bế Văn Đàn kneels and takes a machine gun on his own shoulders — "Fire now!" — and dies still holding it; Phan Đình Giót, wounded again and again, throws his body across a bunker's firing slit at Him Lam
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam dien bien phu heroes as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
