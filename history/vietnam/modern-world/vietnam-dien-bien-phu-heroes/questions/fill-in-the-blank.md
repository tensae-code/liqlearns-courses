# Vietnam dien bien phu heroes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 15, stories of the heroes of Điện Biên Phủ (Bế Văn Đàn; Phan Đình Giót)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Tày soldier Bế Văn Đàn kneels and takes a machine gun on his own shoulders — "Fire now!" — and dies still holding it; Phan Đình Giót, wounded again and again, throws his body across a bunker's firing slit at Him Lam.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam dien bien phu heroes as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
