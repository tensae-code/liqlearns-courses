# Urbanisation industrial and post industrial — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Shared Theme 1, Cities: History and Present (2) — urbanisation in the industrial and post-industrial ages" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Urbanisation in the industrial age begins in the late 18th century with the first industrial revolution — machines raise productivity, production shifts from mechanised to electrified, and the world's urban share climbs from 13.6% (1900) to 36.6% (1970) in the book's UN table, with Manchester and Chicago as typical industrial cities and New York and Paris as service cities, the biggest of all Tokyo at 23.3 million (1970) — while uncontrolled growth eats farmland and breeds pollution and social evils; in the post-industrial age (from the late 20th century) the internet and knowledge economy reorganise the city: by 2024 the world counts 33 megacities, 27 of them in developing countries, city-regions merge into belts like Tokyo's and the Boston–Washington corridor, and green and smart cities become the goal; Vietnam's own urbanisation, tied to industrialisation and modernisation, reaches 38.5% in 2024 with Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City as the twin growth poles.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Urbanisation industrial and post industrial as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
