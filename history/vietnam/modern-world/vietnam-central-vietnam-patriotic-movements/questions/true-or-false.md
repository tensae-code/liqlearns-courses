# Vietnam central vietnam patriotic movements — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 18, Central Vietnam's patriotic and revolutionary tradition", from the Hương Khê rising and the Duy Tân and anti-tax movements to the Nghệ–Tĩnh Soviets of 1930, the centre keeps rising against French rule (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 19, Phan Châu Trinh and the Duy Tân movement in the Centre", phan Châu Trinh (1872–1926), a Phó bảng who resigns his court post to save the country, chooses the opposite road to Phan Bội Châu — rely on France to reform, under the slogan "open the people's intellect, raise the people's spirit, enrich the people's livelihood"; from 1906 his Duy Tân movement spreads new-style schools, lectures, industry and commerce, and mockery of corrupt officials, and calls on people to cut their hair short and wear short clothes against backward custom; it sparks the Central Vietnam anti-tax movement of 1908, which France drowns in arrests — Phan Châu Trinh among the arrested (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
