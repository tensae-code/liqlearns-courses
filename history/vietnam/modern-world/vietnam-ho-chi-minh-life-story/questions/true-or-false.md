# Vietnam ho chi minh life story — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 18, story of Nguyễn Ái Quốc – Hồ Chí Minh", a boy from Kim Liên leaves in 1911 to find a way to save the country, finds Lenin's theses in 1920, founds the Communist Party in 1930 and returns in 1941 to lead independence (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 25, Nguyễn Tất Thành leaves Bến Nhà Rồng, 1911", unlike elders who looked East, the young man sails West as kitchen hand Văn Ba on the Latouche-Tréville on 5 June 1911 to learn how France and the West work (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
