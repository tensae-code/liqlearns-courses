# Vietnam ho chi minh life story — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 18, story of Nguyễn Ái Quốc – Hồ Chí Minh" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A boy from Kim Liên leaves in 1911 to find a way to save the country, finds Lenin's theses in 1920, founds the Communist Party in 1930 and returns in 1941 to lead independence.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 25, Nguyễn Tất Thành leaves Bến Nhà Rồng, 1911" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Unlike elders who looked East, the young man sails West as kitchen hand Văn Ba on the Latouche-Tréville on 5 June 1911 to learn how France and the West work.
