**Book 5:** Title as printed: Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống) — Grade: Grade 8 (student/teacher: STUDENT) — Publisher: Nhà xuất bản Giáo dục Việt Nam; year 2023 (copyright; 3rd reprint with corrections and additions); ISBN 978-604-0-35043-5 — PDF pages: 179 (cover and title page verified against the record title; printed page = PDF page − 1) — Verdict: COMPLETE — Tally: 60 placements — 23 to existing lessons, 37 NEW LESSON NEEDED — Vietnam's own way: Grade 8 pairs the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation of Europe and America with Vietnam's own centuries of partition, peasant revolt and invasion, and closes the History part with the first French colonial exploitation — the lesson Vietnamese pupils are always brought back to when the book cites Hồ Chí Minh quoting the American and French Declarations. — Geography parts named, not placed: the whole Phần Địa lí, printed pp. 93–154 — Vietnam's territory and relief (Ch. 1, Bài 1–3), climate and waters (Ch. 2, Bài 4–8), soils and living world (Ch. 3, Bài 9–10), seas and islands (Ch. 4, Bài 11–12). Two shared themes follow it: Chủ đề chung 1, Civilisation of the Red River and Mekong Deltas (printed pp. 155–161), whose Geography sections are named here and whose History section (how people reclaimed and tamed the two deltas) is placed below; and Chủ đề chung 2, Protecting Vietnam's Sovereignty, Rights and Lawful Interests in the East Sea (printed pp. 162–171), whose law-of-the-sea sections are Civics/Geography and named here, and whose History section (the historical process of establishing sea-and-island sovereignty) is placed below. The three shared themes (printed pp. 221–235) follow the Geography part: Theme 1 Cities: History and Present (2) placed; Theme 2 Red River and Mekong Delta Civilisation (2) — culture section placed, climate-change section is Geography and not placed; Theme 3 Protecting Vietnam's sovereignty, rights and lawful interests in the East Sea (2) — historical-evidence section placed. Review lessons (Ôn tập) after chapters are not placed separately. — Vietnam's own way: the modern-history book is built as two interleaved timelines — every world chapter is answered by a Vietnam chapter (world 1918–1945, then Vietnam 1918–1945; world 1945–1991, then Vietnam 1945–1975), so pupils always read their own country's story as part of the world's, with Party documents and Hồ Chí Minh's words quoted as primary sources throughout.
