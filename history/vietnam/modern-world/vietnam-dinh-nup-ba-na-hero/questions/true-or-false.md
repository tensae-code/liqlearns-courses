# Vietnam dinh nup ba na hero — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 18, story of the hero Núp", ba Na villagers believe the French "do not bleed"; orphan Đinh Núp ambushes a French soldier, shows the blood, and the villages unite into resistance bases of spikes, stone traps and bows
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam dinh nup ba na hero as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
