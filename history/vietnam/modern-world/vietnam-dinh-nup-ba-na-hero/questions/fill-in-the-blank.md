# Vietnam dinh nup ba na hero — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 18, story of the hero Núp" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Ba Na villagers believe the French "do not bleed"; orphan Đinh Núp ambushes a French soldier, shows the blood, and the villages unite into resistance bases of spikes, stone traps and bows.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam dinh nup ba na hero as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
