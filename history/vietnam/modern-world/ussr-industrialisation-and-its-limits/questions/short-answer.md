# Ussr industrialisation and its limits — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, the USSR builds socialism, 1925–1941" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** In December 1922 four republics unite as the USSR (fifteen by 1940); from 1925 the Party drives industrialisation through Five-Year Plans — a table in the book shows steel rising from 4 to 6 to 18 million tonnes (1928/1932/1940) — while collectivisation gathers the villages, illiteracy is wiped out, and the book itself names the limits: haste, undemocratic collectivisation, neglected living standards; in June 1941 Germany's invasion suspends the third plan.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Ussr industrialisation and its limits as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
