# Ussr industrialisation and its limits — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 1, the USSR builds socialism, 1925–1941 — a)
**2.** The one thing this lesson teaches — b)

Meanings:
a) in December 1922 four republics unite as the USSR (fifteen by 1940); from 1925 the Party drives industrialisation through Five-Year Plans — a table in the book shows steel rising from 4 to 6 to 18 million tonnes (1928/1932/1940) — while collectivisation gathers the villages, illiteracy is wiped out, and the book itself names the limits: haste, undemocratic collectivisation, neglected living standards; in June 1941 Germany's invasion suspends the third plan
b) the story of Ussr industrialisation and its limits as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
