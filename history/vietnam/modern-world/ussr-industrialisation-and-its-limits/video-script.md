# Video script — Ussr industrialisation and its limits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ussr industrialisation and its limits.

In December 1922 four republics unite as the USSR (fifteen by 1940); from 1925 the Party drives industrialisation through Five-Year Plans — a table in the book shows steel rising from 4 to 6 to 18 million tonnes (1928/1932/1940) — while collectivisation gathers the villages, illiteracy is wiped out, and the book itself names the limits: haste, undemocratic collectivisation, neglected living standards; in June 1941 Germany's invasion suspends the third plan.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
