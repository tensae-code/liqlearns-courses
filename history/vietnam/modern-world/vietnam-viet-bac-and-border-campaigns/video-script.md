# Video script — Vietnam viet bac and border campaigns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam viet bac and border campaigns.

North of the 16th parallel the cities fight for three months in 1946–47 — Hanoi at the Bắc Bộ Phủ, the Bờ Hồ Post Office, Hàng Cỏ Station and Khâm Thiên street, a suicide soldier hurling a three-pronged bomb at a tank — before the main forces withdraw intact; in autumn–winter 1947 some 12,000 French troops with most of Indochina's aircraft attack Việt Bắc to destroy the resistance headquarters and seal the China border, and are ambushed on Road No. 4 and the Lô river until they flee — "quick attack, quick victory" fails completely; in September–October 1950 the Border Campaign answers the US-aided Revers Plan: Đông Khê falls first, isolating Cao Bằng, the French relief and withdrawal columns are destroyed piecemeal on Road No. 4, and France abandons Cao Bằng, Thất Khê, Na Sầm and Lạng Sơn — the army's first great offensive wins the initiative and opens the international supply route.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
