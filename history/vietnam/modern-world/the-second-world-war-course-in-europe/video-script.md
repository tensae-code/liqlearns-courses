# Video script — The second world war course in europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The second world war course in europe.

Poland is invaded on 1 September 1939 and France falls by July 1940; on 22 June 1941 Germany attacks the USSR and by October threatens Moscow; Pearl Harbor (December 1941) brings America in, the Anti-fascist Alliance forms in January 1942, and Stalingrad (November 1942–February 1943) turns the war; Normandy follows in June 1944 and the Berlin offensive of 16 April 1945 ends with Germany's surrender on 9 May 1945.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
