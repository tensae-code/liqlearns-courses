# The second world war course in europe — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 4, the war's course", poland is invaded on 1 September 1939 and France falls by July 1940; on 22 June 1941 Germany attacks the USSR and by October threatens Moscow; Pearl Harbor (December 1941) brings America in, the Anti-fascist Alliance forms in January 1942, and Stalingrad (November 1942–February 1943) turns the war; Normandy follows in June 1944 and the Berlin offensive of 16 April 1945 ends with Germany's surrender on 9 May 1945 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 2.I.3 Resistance and the Great Patriotic War: resistance movements across Europe, Asia and Africa; China's front kills over 1.5 million Japanese troops before the Pacific War; Vietnam, Laos and Indonesia win independence in 1945; the USSR's decisive war costs nearly 27 million Soviet lives", the Soviet–German front swallows the Wehrmacht's best armies, and behind every front peoples rise and take their independence (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
