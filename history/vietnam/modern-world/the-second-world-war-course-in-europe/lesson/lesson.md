## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 4, the war's course

Poland is invaded on 1 September 1939 and France falls by July 1940; on 22 June 1941 Germany attacks the USSR and by October threatens Moscow; Pearl Harbor (December 1941) brings America in, the Anti-fascist Alliance forms in January 1942, and Stalingrad (November 1942–February 1943) turns the war; Normandy follows in June 1944 and the Berlin offensive of 16 April 1945 ends with Germany's surrender on 9 May 1945.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 2.I.3 Resistance and the Great Patriotic War: resistance movements across Europe, Asia and Africa; China's front kills over 1.5 million Japanese troops before the Pacific War; Vietnam, Laos and Indonesia win independence in 1945; the USSR's decisive war costs nearly 27 million Soviet lives

The Soviet–German front swallows the Wehrmacht's best armies, and behind every front peoples rise and take their independence.
