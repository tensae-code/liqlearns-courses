# Siam reforms and survival

## Bài 5 Siam reforms and survives: Rama IV opens trade from 1851; Rama V from 1868 reforms economy, administration (1892), education (first programme 1898 after studying Europe), cuts farm taxes in 1874, tours Europe in 1897 — and buys independence by ceding claims in Laos and Cambodia to France (1907) and in Malaya to Britain (1909)

The one uncolonised kingdom pays for its flag with border provinces and a railway-age state.
