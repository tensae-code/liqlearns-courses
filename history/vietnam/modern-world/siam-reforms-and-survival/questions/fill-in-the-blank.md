# Siam reforms and survival — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5 Siam reforms and survives: Rama IV opens trade from 1851; Rama V from 1868 reforms economy, administration (1892), education (first programme 1898 after studying Europe), cuts farm taxes in 1874, tours Europe in 1897 — and buys independence by ceding claims in Laos and Cambodia to France (1907) and in Malaya to Britain (1909)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The one uncolonised kingdom pays for its flag with border provinces and a railway-age state.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Siam reforms and survival as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
