# Lesson plan — The second world war the pacific and the results

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** The second world war the pacific and the results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The modern world and Vietnam interleaved, 1918 to today: the world wars, the Cold War, the August Revolution, two resistances, unification, Đổi mới and the border wars (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The second world war the pacific and the results as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The modern world and Vietnam interleaved, 1918 to today: the world wars, the Cold War, the August Revolution, two resistances, unification, Đổi mới and the border wars — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 4, the Pacific end and the war's results** — atomic bombs fall on 6 and 9 August 1945 and Soviet forces strike the Japanese in Northeast China on 8 August; Japan surrenders on 15 August 1945; the book's results table counts 76 countries involved, 110 million under arms, 60 million dead, 90 million wounded and $4,000 billion in material damage — and stresses the USSR's decisive role
2. **§2 Chuyên đề 2.I.2 What the Second World War changed: the cost table — 76 countries, 60 million dead, 90 million disabled, 4,000 billion dollars — the USSR's victory breeds socialist states and liberation movements, the USA rises as superpower, the Yalta order forms, and the UN Charter is signed on 26-6-1945** — the fiercest war in human history ends by splitting the world between two superpowers and founding the United Nations

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
