# Video script — The second world war the pacific and the results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The second world war the pacific and the results.

Atomic bombs fall on 6 and 9 August 1945 and Soviet forces strike the Japanese in Northeast China on 8 August; Japan surrenders on 15 August 1945; the book's results table counts 76 countries involved, 110 million under arms, 60 million dead, 90 million wounded and $4,000 billion in material damage — and stresses the USSR's decisive role.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
