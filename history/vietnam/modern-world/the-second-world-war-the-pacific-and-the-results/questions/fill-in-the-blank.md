# The second world war the pacific and the results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4, the Pacific end and the war's results" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Atomic bombs fall on 6 and 9 August 1945 and Soviet forces strike the Japanese in Northeast China on 8 August; Japan surrenders on 15 August 1945; the book's results table counts 76 countries involved, 110 million under arms, 60 million dead, 90 million wounded and $4,000 billion in material damage — and stresses the USSR's decisive role.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.I.2 What the Second World War changed: the cost table — 76 countries, 60 million dead, 90 million disabled, 4,000 billion dollars — the USSR's victory breeds socialist states and liberation movements, the USA rises as superpower, the Yalta order forms, and the UN Charter is signed on 26-6-1945" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The fiercest war in human history ends by splitting the world between two superpowers and founding the United Nations.
