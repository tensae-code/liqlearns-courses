# The second world war the pacific and the results — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 4, the Pacific end and the war's results", atomic bombs fall on 6 and 9 August 1945 and Soviet forces strike the Japanese in Northeast China on 8 August; Japan surrenders on 15 August 1945; the book's results table counts 76 countries involved, 110 million under arms, 60 million dead, 90 million wounded and $4,000 billion in material damage — and stresses the USSR's decisive role (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
