# Lesson plan — Scientific revolution and enlightenment

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Scientific revolution and enlightenment
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Revolutions, industry and a divided country: the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation beside Vietnam's partition, the Tây Sơn, the Nguyễn and the first French colonial exploitation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Scientific revolution and enlightenment as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Revolutions, industry and a divided country: the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation beside Vietnam's partition, the Tây Sơn, the Nguyễn and the first French colonial exploitation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 2, the Enlightenment thinkers prepare the French Revolution** — montesquieu argues for limiting the king's power and securing citizens' freedoms, Voltaire for an enlightened monarchy, freedom of belief and the right of property, and Rousseau that monarchy must be abolished altogether for a republic — the ideas that, in the book's words, clear the road for the revolution to explode
2. **§2 Bài 13, the great scientific achievements of the 18th–19th centuries** — newton's law of universal gravitation (with the apple anecdote — watching an apple fall straight down in the family garden, he asks why it never falls sideways or upward), Lomonosov's law of the conservation and transformation of energy, Darwin's theory of evolution; beside them the social sciences advance — German materialism and dialectics in Feuerbach and Hegel, English political economy in Adam Smith and Ricardo, utopian socialism in Saint-Simon, Fourier and Owen — until Marx and Engels' scientific socialism makes, in the book's phrase, a revolution in human thought
3. **§3 Bài 21, the scientific-technical revolution's achievements** — the book's achievements table sweeps the post-1945 revolution field by field — basic science in mathematics, physics, biology and chemistry; biotechnology's genetic and cell technologies driving a "Green Revolution"; new materials from super-flexible polymers to nano; atomic, wind, solar and geothermal energy; generations of computers and the internet; supersonic aircraft and high-speed trains; satellite communications and the smartphone; and digital technology — big data, cloud computing, the internet of things and 3D printing — crowned by artificial intelligence, which not only imitates human thought but steers the rest
