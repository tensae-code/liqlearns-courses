# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 2, the Enlightenment thinkers prepare the French Revolution", montesquieu argues for limiting the king's power and securing citizens' freedoms, Voltaire for an enlightened monarchy, freedom of belief and the right of property, and Rousseau that monarchy must be abolished altogether for a republic — the ideas that, in the book's words, clear the road for the revolution to explode (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 13, the great scientific achievements of the 18th–19th centuries", newton's law of universal gravitation (with the apple anecdote — watching an apple fall straight down in the family garden, he asks why it never falls sideways or upward), Lomonosov's law of the conservation and transformation of energy, Darwin's theory of evolution; beside them the social sciences advance — German materialism and dialectics in Feuerbach and Hegel, English political economy in Adam Smith and Ricardo, utopian socialism in Saint-Simon, Fourier and Owen — until Marx and Engels' scientific socialism makes, in the book's phrase, a revolution in human thought (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
