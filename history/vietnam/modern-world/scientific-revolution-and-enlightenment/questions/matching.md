# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 2, the Enlightenment thinkers prepare the French Revolution — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 13, the great scientific achievements of the 18th–19th centuries — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) montesquieu argues for limiting the king's power and securing citizens' freedoms, Voltaire for an enlightened monarchy, freedom of belief and the right of property, and Rousseau that monarchy must be abolished altogether for a republic — the ideas that, in the book's words, clear the road for the revolution to explode
b) newton's law of universal gravitation (with the apple anecdote — watching an apple fall straight down in the family garden, he asks why it never falls sideways or upward), Lomonosov's law of the conservation and transformation of energy, Darwin's theory of evolution; beside them the social sciences advance — German materialism and dialectics in Feuerbach and Hegel, English political economy in Adam Smith and Ricardo, utopian socialism in Saint-Simon, Fourier and Owen — until Marx and Engels' scientific socialism makes, in the book's phrase, a revolution in human thought

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
