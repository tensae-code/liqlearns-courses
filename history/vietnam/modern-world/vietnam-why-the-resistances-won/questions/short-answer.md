# Vietnam why the resistances won — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 7 Why some resistances won and others failed: victories rest on a just cause, all-people unity (Bình Than conference 1282; the Diên Hồng elders shouting "Fight!" in 1285), striking first (Lý Thường Kiệt 1075) or fast (Quang Trung 1789), and gifted commanders; failures — An Dương Vương against Triệu Đà in 179 BCE, the Hồ against the Ming 1406–1407, the Nguyễn court signing away 1862–1884 — come from divided leadership and people's hearts not following, as Hồ Nguyên Trừng warned" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The same country wins when its people are one bloc and loses when its court fights alone.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 8 The lessons the uprisings leave: build forces (Lũng Nhai's gathering; Quang Trung's Nghệ An review and Thanh Hóa levy), build the great unity bloc ("use great righteousness to defeat cruelty" — a staff of landlord, scholar, noble, poor farmer and minority chief), and master a creative military art" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The textbook turns its own heroes into three transferable lessons for defending the country today.
