# Vietnam why the resistances won — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 7 Why some resistances won and others failed: victories rest on a just cause, all-people unity (Bình Than conference 1282; the Diên Hồng elders shouting "Fight!" in 1285), striking first (Lý Thường Kiệt 1075) or fast (Quang Trung 1789), and gifted commanders; failures — An Dương Vương against Triệu Đà in 179 BCE, the Hồ against the Ming 1406–1407, the Nguyễn court signing away 1862–1884 — come from divided leadership and people's hearts not following, as Hồ Nguyên Trừng warned — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 8 The lessons the uprisings leave: build forces (Lũng Nhai's gathering; Quang Trung's Nghệ An review and Thanh Hóa levy), build the great unity bloc ("use great righteousness to defeat cruelty" — a staff of landlord, scholar, noble, poor farmer and minority chief), and master a creative military art — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the same country wins when its people are one bloc and loses when its court fights alone
b) the textbook turns its own heroes into three transferable lessons for defending the country today

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
