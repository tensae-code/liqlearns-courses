# Vietnam quang trung defeats the qing 1789 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 8, the marches north: the Trịnh fall and the Lê collapses, 1786–1788 — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 8, the Tết offensive of 1789: Ngọc Hồi – Đống Đa — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) in May 1786 Nguyễn Huệ storms Phú Xuân, then rides the victory north under the banner "support the Lê, destroy the Trịnh", enters Thăng Long to popular acclaim and hands power back to the Lê king; when the north falls into chaos again he marches a second time in 1788, King Lê Chiêu Thống flees, and the Lê–Trịnh order collapses completely — whereupon the fugitive king begs the Qing for rescue
b) against Tôn Sĩ Nghị's 290,000 Qing troops Quang Trung — crowned emperor in December 1788 — first withdraws in good order to the Tam Điệp–Biện Sơn line, then strikes in the Tết holiday itself: on the night of 25 January 1789 his men cross the Gián Khẩu river and wipe out the outposts, on 28 January they surround Hà Hồi fort, and at dawn on 30 January, the fifth day of Tết, Quang Trung personally storms Ngọc Hồi while another wing takes Đống Đa — the Qing commander Sầm Nghi Đống hangs himself, Tôn Sĩ Nghị flees abandoning his seal, and by noon the victorious army enters Thăng Long; the capital is freed in five days

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
