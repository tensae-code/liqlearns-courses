# English civil war and glorious revolution — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 1, the English bourgeois revolution", england's new gentry enclose the fields for sheep and leave peasants landless; when Charles I summons Parliament in 1640 only to squeeze taxes, the two camps harden — in August 1642 the king declares war on Parliament; the king is executed on 30 January 1649 and a republic proclaimed, the monarchy is restored in 1660, and the "Glorious Revolution" of 1688–89 settles a constitutional monarchy — a revolution Marx calls the victory of the new social order, yet one that never roots out feudalism or answers the peasants' land question
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of English civil war and glorious revolution as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
