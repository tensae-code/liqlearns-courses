# English civil war and glorious revolution — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, the English bourgeois revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** England's new gentry enclose the fields for sheep and leave peasants landless; when Charles I summons Parliament in 1640 only to squeeze taxes, the two camps harden — in August 1642 the king declares war on Parliament; the king is executed on 30 January 1649 and a republic proclaimed, the monarchy is restored in 1660, and the "Glorious Revolution" of 1688–89 settles a constitutional monarchy — a revolution Marx calls the victory of the new social order, yet one that never roots out feudalism or answers the peasants' land question.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of English civil war and glorious revolution as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
