# Video script — English civil war and glorious revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: English civil war and glorious revolution.

England's new gentry enclose the fields for sheep and leave peasants landless; when Charles I summons Parliament in 1640 only to squeeze taxes, the two camps harden — in August 1642 the king declares war on Parliament; the king is executed on 30 January 1649 and a republic proclaimed, the monarchy is restored in 1660, and the "Glorious Revolution" of 1688–89 settles a constitutional monarchy — a revolution Marx calls the victory of the new social order, yet one that never roots out feudalism or answers the peasants' land question.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
