## Bài 1, the English bourgeois revolution

England's new gentry enclose the fields for sheep and leave peasants landless; when Charles I summons Parliament in 1640 only to squeeze taxes, the two camps harden — in August 1642 the king declares war on Parliament; the king is executed on 30 January 1649 and a republic proclaimed, the monarchy is restored in 1660, and the "Glorious Revolution" of 1688–89 settles a constitutional monarchy — a revolution Marx calls the victory of the new social order, yet one that never roots out feudalism or answers the peasants' land question.
