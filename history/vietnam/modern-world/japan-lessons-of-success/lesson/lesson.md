# Japan lessons of success

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Japan lessons of success" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Chuyên đề 2 Japan's success lessons, as Vietnam's textbook draws them: invest in education on Meiji foundations until illiteracy is among the world's lowest; a state that coordinates, invests and protects key industries rather than only refereeing; factory management that perfects people before products; buying the world's patents instead of reinventing them; and a national resilience forged by earthquakes and tsunamis

The examiner's last question is which of Japan's lessons Vietnam itself can use.
