# Japan lessons of success — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Chuyên đề 2 Japan's success lessons, as Vietnam's textbook draws them: invest in education on Meiji foundations until illiteracy is among the world's lowest; a state that coordinates, invests and protects key industries rather than only refereeing; factory management that perfects people before products; buying the world's patents instead of reinventing them; and a national resilience forged by earthquakes and tsunamis"?
a) the examiner's last question is which of Japan's lessons Vietnam itself can use  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) the story of Japan lessons of success as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
