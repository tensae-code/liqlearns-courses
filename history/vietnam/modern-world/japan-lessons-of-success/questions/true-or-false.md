# Japan lessons of success — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 2 Japan's success lessons, as Vietnam's textbook draws them: invest in education on Meiji foundations until illiteracy is among the world's lowest; a state that coordinates, invests and protects key industries rather than only refereeing; factory management that perfects people before products; buying the world's patents instead of reinventing them; and a national resilience forged by earthquakes and tsunamis", the examiner's last question is which of Japan's lessons Vietnam itself can use
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Japan lessons of success as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
