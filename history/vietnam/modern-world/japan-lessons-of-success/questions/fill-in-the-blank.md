# Japan lessons of success — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2 Japan's success lessons, as Vietnam's textbook draws them: invest in education on Meiji foundations until illiteracy is among the world's lowest; a state that coordinates, invests and protects key industries rather than only refereeing; factory management that perfects people before products; buying the world's patents instead of reinventing them; and a national resilience forged by earthquakes and tsunamis" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The examiner's last question is which of Japan's lessons Vietnam itself can use.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Japan lessons of success as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
