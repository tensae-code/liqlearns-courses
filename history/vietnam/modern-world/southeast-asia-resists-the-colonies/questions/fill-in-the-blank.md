# Southeast asia resists the colonies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 6 The region fights back: Prince Diponegoro's Java war (1825–1830) bleeds the Dutch; Dagohoy's Bohol uprising lasts 85 years (1744–1829), the longest in the Philippines; Burma's guerrillas fight on ten years after annexation; Vietnam ruins France's "quick attack, quick victory" from 1858 and costs it 26 years; Cambodia's Pucombo insurgents ally with Trương Định's son across the border" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Annexation proclamations end wars on paper only — the jungles keep fighting for generations.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Southeast asia resists the colonies as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
