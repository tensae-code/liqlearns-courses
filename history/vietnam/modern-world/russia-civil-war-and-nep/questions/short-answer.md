# Russia civil war and nep — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, Russia after October: civil war and the New Economic Policy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Entente powers and White generals plunge Soviet Russia into civil war; War Communism (1919) requisitions the peasants' grain while the Red Army wins by 1920 — then Lenin turns: the March 1921 NEP replaces requisitioning with a grain tax and lets trade breathe, and the shattered economy climbs back.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Russia civil war and nep as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
