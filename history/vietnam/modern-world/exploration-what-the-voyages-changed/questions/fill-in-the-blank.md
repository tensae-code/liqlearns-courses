# Exploration what the voyages changed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2, consequences of the discoveries and the birth of capitalism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gold and silver flood in, the slave trade and colonial plunder begin, and at home England's enclosures drive peasants off the land — "sheep eat men", in Thomas More's phrase — into the workshops of a new bourgeoisie facing a new proletariat.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Shared Theme 1, the effects of the great discoveries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** World trade widens and Western merchants and workshop owners grow rich; peoples learn of new routes and lands and cultures meet — but labourers sink into poverty, and the discoveries open the slave trade and the plunder of colonies.
