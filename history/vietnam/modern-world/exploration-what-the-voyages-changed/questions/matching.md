Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 2, consequences of the discoveries and the birth of capitalism — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Shared Theme 1, the effects of the great discoveries — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) gold and silver flood in, the slave trade and colonial plunder begin, and at home England's enclosures drive peasants off the land — "sheep eat men", in Thomas More's phrase — into the workshops of a new bourgeoisie facing a new proletariat
