## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 2, consequences of the discoveries and the birth of capitalism

Gold and silver flood in, the slave trade and colonial plunder begin, and at home England's enclosures drive peasants off the land — "sheep eat men", in Thomas More's phrase — into the workshops of a new bourgeoisie facing a new proletariat.

## Sub lesson 2 — Shared Theme 1, the effects of the great discoveries

World trade widens and Western merchants and workshop owners grow rich; peoples learn of new routes and lands and cultures meet — but labourers sink into poverty, and the discoveries open the slave trade and the plunder of colonies.
