# Vietnam economy and towns 16th to 18th — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 9, agriculture and crafts in the two regions — a)
**2.** The one thing this lesson teaches — b)

Meanings:
a) war ruins the North's farming — communal fields pass into private hands, peasants become tenants owing rent, tax and service, and disasters drive them from their villages — while in the South reclamation builds a rich agriculture, above all in the Mekong delta; in both regions state workshops cast weapons and coin, and the folk crafts flourish in famous villages: Thổ Hà and Bát Tràng pottery, La Khê silk, the iron villages of Nho Lâm, Hiền Lương and Phú Bài, Châu Khê paper — the folk verse quoted at the lesson's head dreams of Bát Tràng bricks to build with
b) the story of Vietnam economy and towns 16th to 18th as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
