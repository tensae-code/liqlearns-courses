# Vietnam economy and towns 16th to 18th — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 9, agriculture and crafts in the two regions", war ruins the North's farming — communal fields pass into private hands, peasants become tenants owing rent, tax and service, and disasters drive them from their villages — while in the South reclamation builds a rich agriculture, above all in the Mekong delta; in both regions state workshops cast weapons and coin, and the folk crafts flourish in famous villages: Thổ Hà and Bát Tràng pottery, La Khê silk, the iron villages of Nho Lâm, Hiền Lương and Phú Bài, Châu Khê paper — the folk verse quoted at the lesson's head dreams of Bát Tràng bricks to build with
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam economy and towns 16th to 18th as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
