# Vietnam economy and towns 16th to 18th — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 9, agriculture and crafts in the two regions" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** War ruins the North's farming — communal fields pass into private hands, peasants become tenants owing rent, tax and service, and disasters drive them from their villages — while in the South reclamation builds a rich agriculture, above all in the Mekong delta; in both regions state workshops cast weapons and coin, and the folk crafts flourish in famous villages: Thổ Hà and Bát Tràng pottery, La Khê silk, the iron villages of Nho Lâm, Hiền Lương and Phú Bài, Châu Khê paper — the folk verse quoted at the lesson's head dreams of Bát Tràng bricks to build with.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam economy and towns 16th to 18th as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
