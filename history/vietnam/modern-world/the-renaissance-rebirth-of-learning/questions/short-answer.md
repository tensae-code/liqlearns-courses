# The renaissance rebirth of learning — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 3, the achievements of the Renaissance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Dante opens the movement; Cervantes' Don Quixote and Shakespeare's plays, Leonardo's Last Supper and Mona Lisa, Michelangelo painting the Sistine ceiling lying on his back for over four years, and Copernicus, Bruno and Galileo — "and yet it moves" — defying the Church.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of The renaissance rebirth of learning as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
