# Video script — Effects of industrialisation winners and losers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Effects of industrialisation winners and losers.

France industrialises from 1830, Germany from the 1840s on heavy industry, the United States early with the cotton gin (1793); the revolution lifts production, builds industrial cities and creates the bourgeoisie and the proletariat — but a quoted German account of a paper mill shows the other face: air thick with white dust, and small children set to splice broken threads beneath the machines so the looms never stop.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
