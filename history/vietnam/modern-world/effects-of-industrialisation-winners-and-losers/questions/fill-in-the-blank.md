# Effects of industrialisation winners and losers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 3, the spread and the two faces of industrialisation" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** France industrialises from 1830, Germany from the 1840s on heavy industry, the United States early with the cotton gin (1793); the revolution lifts production, builds industrial cities and creates the bourgeoisie and the proletariat — but a quoted German account of a paper mill shows the other face: air thick with white dust, and small children set to splice broken threads beneath the machines so the looms never stop.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Effects of industrialisation winners and losers as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
