## Bài 9, culture, religions and the birth of chữ Quốc ngữ

Confucianism still rules study and office, Buddhism and Daoism revive, and from 1533 Catholicism spreads until by the 18th century it reaches the whole country — bringing with it the Latin-based chữ Quốc ngữ, devised by missionaries learning Vietnamese to preach and later prized for printing and science; Han literature still leads, but Nôm literature flowers — the verse chronicle Thiên Nam ngữ lục, Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm (the folk's Trạng Trình), the Trạng Quỳnh tales — beside communal-house carving of daily life, the thousand-armed Quan Âm of Bút Tháp pagoda, chèo and tuồng.
