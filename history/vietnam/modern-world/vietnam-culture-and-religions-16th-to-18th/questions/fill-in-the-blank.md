# Vietnam culture and religions 16th to 18th — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9, culture, religions and the birth of chữ Quốc ngữ" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Confucianism still rules study and office, Buddhism and Daoism revive, and from 1533 Catholicism spreads until by the 18th century it reaches the whole country — bringing with it the Latin-based chữ Quốc ngữ, devised by missionaries learning Vietnamese to preach and later prized for printing and science; Han literature still leads, but Nôm literature flowers — the verse chronicle Thiên Nam ngữ lục, Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm (the folk's Trạng Trình), the Trạng Quỳnh tales — beside communal-house carving of daily life, the thousand-armed Quan Âm of Bút Tháp pagoda, chèo and tuồng.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam culture and religions 16th to 18th as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
