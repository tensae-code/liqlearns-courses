# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 2, France before the revolution and the storming of the Bastille" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A backward, famine-haunted agriculture, a bankrupt crown raising taxes, and a society of three estates where the Third Estate — over 90% of the people — carries every burden; on 14 July 1789 the crowds storm the Bastille fortress-prison, the day France still keeps as its National Day, under the tricolour first raised there.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 1 What the bourgeois revolutions achieved: England's revolution founds constitutional monarchy; America's war frees the colonies and births the first bourgeois state outside Europe; France's is the "great revolution" — Lenin's verdict that the whole nineteenth century passed under its sign" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Three revolutions, three templates — a king tamed, a nation born, a world re-imagined.
