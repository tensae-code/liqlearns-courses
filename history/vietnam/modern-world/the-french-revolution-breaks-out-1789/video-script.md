# Video script — The french revolution breaks out 1789

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The french revolution breaks out 1789.

A backward, famine-haunted agriculture, a bankrupt crown raising taxes, and a society of three estates where the Third Estate — over 90% of the people — carries every burden; on 14 July 1789 the crowds storm the Bastille fortress-prison, the day France still keeps as its National Day, under the tricolour first raised there.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
