# Vietnam founding of the vietnam liberation army 1944 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 14, story of the founding of the Vietnam Propaganda Liberation Army" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** On 22 December 1944, in a Cao Bằng forest, Võ Nguyên Giáp proclaims a unit of 34 soldiers under the rule "politics is weightier than military matters" — the seed of the People's Army.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam founding of the vietnam liberation army 1944 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
