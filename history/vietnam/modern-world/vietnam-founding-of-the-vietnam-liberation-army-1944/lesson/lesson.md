## Bài 14, story of the founding of the Vietnam Propaganda Liberation Army

On 22 December 1944, in a Cao Bằng forest, Võ Nguyên Giáp proclaims a unit of 34 soldiers under the rule "politics is weightier than military matters" — the seed of the People's Army.
