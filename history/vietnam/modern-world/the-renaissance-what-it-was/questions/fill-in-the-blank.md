# The renaissance what it was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5.2, the Renaissance civilisation (14th–17th centuries)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Renaissance takes its name from the Cultural Renaissance movement — a Western European current founded on reviving the achievements and values of ancient Greek–Roman civilisation; its science and technique prepare the ground for new thought, above all materialist philosophy with scholars like Francis Bacon of England and Descartes of France; Renaissance thinkers fiercely denounce the Christian Church's monopoly, aim their struggle at the decaying feudal regime of the time, and exalt human value and the right to individual freedom; these shifts of thought greatly affect the political and social situation, prepare the ground for Europe's social revolutions, and lay the foundation for the great advances of thought and philosophy in the ages that follow; the book's judgement is that Renaissance culture is the first open struggle of the bourgeoisie against the outdated feudal regime in the field of culture and thought, opening the way for Western European civilisation to develop through the following centuries — and the practice section sets Engels' sentence before the class: without the foundation of Greek civilisation and the Roman Empire, there would be no modern Europe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of The renaissance what it was as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
