# Japan postwar recovery and miracle — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 12, Japan's recovery and miracle" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Under American occupation (1945–52) Japan signs the US–Japan Security Treaty (8 September 1951), accepts the nuclear "umbrella" and US bases, and adopts a constitution making the Diet supreme, the Emperor symbolic and keeping no army (quoted through Phan Ngọc Liên); the LDP rules from 1955 to 1991; reforms of 1945–52 give way to the 1960s "miracle" — GNP growing 9.4% a year (1953–73), fed partly by America's war in Vietnam — until Japan passes Western Europe, stands second only to the USA, and by 1988 counts GDP per head of $25,000, above America's; the first shinkansen runs in 1964.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chuyên đề 2 Occupied Japan remade (1945–1952): surrender signed 2-9-1945 leaves some 3 million dead and 13 million jobless; SCAP dissolves the armed forces, tries war criminals, breaks the zaibatsu, carries out land reform, and the 1946 constitution moves sovereignty from Emperor to Diet and renounces force as a means of settling disputes; the 1947 education laws decree equality in schooling; the 1951 peace and security treaties make Japan America's Asian ally" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The victor occupies the islands for the first time in their history and rewrites their state in six years.
