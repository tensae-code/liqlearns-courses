# Japan postwar recovery and miracle

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 12, Japan's recovery and miracle

Under American occupation (1945–52) Japan signs the US–Japan Security Treaty (8 September 1951), accepts the nuclear "umbrella" and US bases, and adopts a constitution making the Diet supreme, the Emperor symbolic and keeping no army (quoted through Phan Ngọc Liên); the LDP rules from 1955 to 1991; reforms of 1945–52 give way to the 1960s "miracle" — GNP growing 9.4% a year (1953–73), fed partly by America's war in Vietnam — until Japan passes Western Europe, stands second only to the USA, and by 1988 counts GDP per head of $25,000, above America's; the first shinkansen runs in 1964.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 2 Occupied Japan remade (1945–1952): surrender signed 2-9-1945 leaves some 3 million dead and 13 million jobless; SCAP dissolves the armed forces, tries war criminals, breaks the zaibatsu, carries out land reform, and the 1946 constitution moves sovereignty from Emperor to Diet and renounces force as a means of settling disputes; the 1947 education laws decree equality in schooling; the 1951 peace and security treaties make Japan America's Asian ally

The victor occupies the islands for the first time in their history and rewrites their state in six years.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chuyên đề 2 The economic miracle (1952–1973): growth averaging about 10% a year lifts GDP from 44.3 billion USD in 1960 (one-sixteenth of America's) to 212.6 billion in 1970 (one-fifth), exports from 8.5 to 38.3 billion, with the 1964 Tokyo Olympics and first bullet train as shop windows; Ikeda's income-doubling plan steers it, and American war orders from Korea and Vietnam blow as a "divine wind"

The defeated country becomes the workshop the victor's wars keep ordering from.
