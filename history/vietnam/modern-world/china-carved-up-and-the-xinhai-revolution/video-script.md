# Video script — China carved up and the xinhai revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: China carved up and the xinhai revolution.

In 1840 Britain makes war on the pretext that the Qing have seized and burned British merchants' opium; the Treaty of Nanjing (1842) opens five ports, exacts an indemnity and takes Hong Kong; then the spheres are drawn — Germany in Shandong, Britain in the Yangtze basin, Russia and Japan in the Northeast, France in Yunnan, Guangxi and Guangdong — until the Boxer Protocol of 1901 leaves China, in the book's formula, a feudal semi-colony.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
