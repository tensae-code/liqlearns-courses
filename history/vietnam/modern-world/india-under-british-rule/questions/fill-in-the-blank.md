# India under british rule — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 15, India under the British, later 19th century" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** On 1 January 1877 Queen Victoria is proclaimed Empress of India at the great durbar pictured here, sealing a conquest administered by a Viceroy and a five-man council ruling like a government; the British conciliate a slice of the feudal upper class into henchmen and deliberately deepen ethnic and religious divisions; plantations eat the peasants' land, mines and processing works and new ports grow for export, and an official "keep them ignorant" policy fosters backward customs — against it stand the Sepoy rising (1857–1859) and the Congress-led movement of 1905–1908.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of India under british rule as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
