# India under british rule — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 15, India under the British, later 19th century", on 1 January 1877 Queen Victoria is proclaimed Empress of India at the great durbar pictured here, sealing a conquest administered by a Viceroy and a five-man council ruling like a government; the British conciliate a slice of the feudal upper class into henchmen and deliberately deepen ethnic and religious divisions; plantations eat the peasants' land, mines and processing works and new ports grow for export, and an official "keep them ignorant" policy fosters backward customs — against it stand the Sepoy rising (1857–1859) and the Congress-led movement of 1905–1908
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of India under british rule as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
