# The reformation luther — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 3, Martin Luther and the outbreak of the Reformation" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Wittenberg professor who hates the sale of indulgences nails his theses to the church door in 1517 and is excommunicated for refusing to recant.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of The reformation luther as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
