# Video script — Vietnam nguyen economy society and uprisings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam nguyen economy society and uprisings.

The court organises reclamation — Nguyễn Công Trứ founds the new districts of Tiền Hải and Kim Sơn in the lower Red River delta — and mining and trade grow, but landlords and strongmen seize the fields, northern floods and droughts recur, and the closed-door policy starves famous towns like Thăng Long, Phố Hiến and Hội An; the people's answer is counted by the quoted history of Phan Huy Lê: from 1802 to 1862 some 405 popular uprisings against the court — Phan Bá Vành's (1821–1827), Lê Duy Lương's (1833–1838), Nông Văn Vân's (1833–1835), Cao Bá Quát's (1854–1856) among them.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
