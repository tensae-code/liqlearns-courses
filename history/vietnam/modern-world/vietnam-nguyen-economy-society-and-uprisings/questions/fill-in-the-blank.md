# Vietnam nguyen economy society and uprisings — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 16, the Nguyễn economy and a society in revolt" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The court organises reclamation — Nguyễn Công Trứ founds the new districts of Tiền Hải and Kim Sơn in the lower Red River delta — and mining and trade grow, but landlords and strongmen seize the fields, northern floods and droughts recur, and the closed-door policy starves famous towns like Thăng Long, Phố Hiến and Hội An; the people's answer is counted by the quoted history of Phan Huy Lê: from 1802 to 1862 some 405 popular uprisings against the court — Phan Bá Vành's (1821–1827), Lê Duy Lương's (1833–1838), Nông Văn Vân's (1833–1835), Cao Bá Quát's (1854–1856) among them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam nguyen economy society and uprisings as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
