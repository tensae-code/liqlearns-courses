# Southeast asia after independence asean — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 6 After independence: the colonial legacy (dependent economies, divide-and-rule ethnic wounds, Lee Kuan Yew's "we cannot live on charity") and the climb out — import substitution, then export-led industrialisation, Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia turning to markets in the late 1980s; ASEAN, founded at Bangkok on 8-8-1967, grows to eleven members and the world's fifth economy", the granary that starved learns to export factories, and eleven former colonies build one common home (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 4 From ASEAN 5 to ASEAN 10: Brunei joins in 1984, Vietnam in 1995, Laos and Myanmar in 1997, Cambodia in 1999; the stages run from trust-building (ZOPFAN 1971), through the 1976 Bali Concord and Treaty of Amity and the 1992 free-trade agreement and 1994 Regional Forum, to organisational completion with the 2007 Charter", it takes thirty-two years for the club to hold every Southeast Asian nation, and Vietnam's 1995 entry is the hinge (Sub lesson 2)
