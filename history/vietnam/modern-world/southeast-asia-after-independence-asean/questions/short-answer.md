# Southeast asia after independence asean — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 6 After independence: the colonial legacy (dependent economies, divide-and-rule ethnic wounds, Lee Kuan Yew's "we cannot live on charity") and the climb out — import substitution, then export-led industrialisation, Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia turning to markets in the late 1980s; ASEAN, founded at Bangkok on 8-8-1967, grows to eleven members and the world's fifth economy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The granary that starved learns to export factories, and eleven former colonies build one common home.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 4 From ASEAN 5 to ASEAN 10: Brunei joins in 1984, Vietnam in 1995, Laos and Myanmar in 1997, Cambodia in 1999; the stages run from trust-building (ZOPFAN 1971), through the 1976 Bali Concord and Treaty of Amity and the 1992 free-trade agreement and 1994 Regional Forum, to organisational completion with the 2007 Charter" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** It takes thirty-two years for the club to hold every Southeast Asian nation, and Vietnam's 1995 entry is the hinge.
