# Southeast asia after independence asean — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 6 After independence: the colonial legacy (dependent economies, divide-and-rule ethnic wounds, Lee Kuan Yew's "we cannot live on charity") and the climb out — import substitution, then export-led industrialisation, Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia turning to markets in the late 1980s; ASEAN, founded at Bangkok on 8-8-1967, grows to eleven members and the world's fifth economy — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 4 From ASEAN 5 to ASEAN 10: Brunei joins in 1984, Vietnam in 1995, Laos and Myanmar in 1997, Cambodia in 1999; the stages run from trust-building (ZOPFAN 1971), through the 1976 Bali Concord and Treaty of Amity and the 1992 free-trade agreement and 1994 Regional Forum, to organisational completion with the 2007 Charter — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the granary that starved learns to export factories, and eleven former colonies build one common home
b) it takes thirty-two years for the club to hold every Southeast Asian nation, and Vietnam's 1995 entry is the hinge

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
