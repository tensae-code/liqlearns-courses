# Roosevelt and the new deal

## Bài 2, America from boom to New Deal

The 1920s are America's "golden age" — industrial output up 69% (1923–29) and 48% of world output by 1929 — then the crash; Roosevelt's New Deal relieves unemployment, reorganises the banks and creates jobs, while abroad Washington recognises the USSR (1933) and preaches the Good Neighbour policy.
