# Roosevelt and the new deal — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 2, America from boom to New Deal" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The 1920s are America's "golden age" — industrial output up 69% (1923–29) and 48% of world output by 1929 — then the crash; Roosevelt's New Deal relieves unemployment, reorganises the banks and creates jobs, while abroad Washington recognises the USSR (1933) and preaches the Good Neighbour policy.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Roosevelt and the new deal as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
