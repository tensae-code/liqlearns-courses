# World peace movement in the cold war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.IV.1 The people's peace movement in the Cold War: the World Peace Congress at Paris on 26-4-1949 elects the World Peace Council under Joliot-Curie and takes Picasso's dove as its symbol; the Stockholm Appeal against atomic weapons gathers hundreds of millions of signatures" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A painted dove and half a billion signatures become the other superpower of the Cold War.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of World peace movement in the cold war as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
