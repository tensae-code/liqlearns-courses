# Japan meiji restoration — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 14, the Meiji Restoration, 1868" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The boy emperor Mutsuhito, enthroned at 15 and taking the era name Meiji, resolves to take back power and reform; the book's diagram sets out the four fronts — politics (a new government, separatism abolished, the 1889 Constitution reserving supreme power to the Emperor, bourgeois-ised aristocrats and big bourgeois brought into rule), economy (one currency and market, land freely bought and sold, roads and bridges), the military (a Western-style conscript army, shipbuilding and arms, foreign experts hired), and education (compulsory schooling weighted to science and technology, the best students sent to the West) — a reform the book calls the equal of a bourgeois revolution.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Japan meiji restoration as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
