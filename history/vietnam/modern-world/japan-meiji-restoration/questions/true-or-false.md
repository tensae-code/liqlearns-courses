# Japan meiji restoration — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 14, the Meiji Restoration, 1868", the boy emperor Mutsuhito, enthroned at 15 and taking the era name Meiji, resolves to take back power and reform; the book's diagram sets out the four fronts — politics (a new government, separatism abolished, the 1889 Constitution reserving supreme power to the Emperor, bourgeois-ised aristocrats and big bourgeois brought into rule), economy (one currency and market, land freely bought and sold, roads and bridges), the military (a Western-style conscript army, shipbuilding and arms, foreign experts hired), and education (compulsory schooling weighted to science and technology, the best students sent to the West) — a reform the book calls the equal of a bourgeois revolution
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Japan meiji restoration as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
