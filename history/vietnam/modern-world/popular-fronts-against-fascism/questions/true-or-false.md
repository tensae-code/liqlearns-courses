# Popular fronts against fascism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 2.I.3 The Popular Fronts: communist, socialist and patriotic forces unite against fascism; France's Popular Front reforms and holds the republic; Spain's republic is defended by International Brigades from 53 countries; Indochina's Democratic Front gathers forces in 1936–1939", when fascism knocks, enemies on the left lock arms — and volunteers cross oceans to hold Madrid
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Popular fronts against fascism as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
