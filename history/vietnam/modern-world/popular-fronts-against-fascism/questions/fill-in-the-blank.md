# Popular fronts against fascism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.I.3 The Popular Fronts: communist, socialist and patriotic forces unite against fascism; France's Popular Front reforms and holds the republic; Spain's republic is defended by International Brigades from 53 countries; Indochina's Democratic Front gathers forces in 1936–1939" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** When fascism knocks, enemies on the left lock arms — and volunteers cross oceans to hold Madrid.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Popular fronts against fascism as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
