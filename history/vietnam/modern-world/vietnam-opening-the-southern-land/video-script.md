# Video script — Vietnam opening the southern land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam opening the southern land.

Nguyễn Hoàng takes up the government of Thuận Hoá in 1558 — the Nguyễn chronicle praises a lord "lenient with his soldiers, gathering heroes, loved and obeyed by the people", from whom "the imperial enterprise was built"; by the late 16th century Thuận–Quảng counts some 1,226 villages, and the milestones run on: Phú Yên prefecture in 1611, Thái Khang in 1653, Thuận Thành in 1693, Gia Định prefecture in 1698 — when Nguyễn Hữu Cảnh formally organises the far south — and by 1757 the Nguyễn administration covers a Nam Bộ roughly as it is today.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
