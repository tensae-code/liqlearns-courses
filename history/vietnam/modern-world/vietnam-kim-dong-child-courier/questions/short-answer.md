# Vietnam kim dong child courier — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 14, story of the boy courier Kim Đồng" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Nùng boy Nông Văn Dền, first captain of the Children Save-the-Nation team, spots an ambush on cadres meeting in a cave, draws the enemy's fire onto himself and is killed at 14.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam kim dong child courier as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
