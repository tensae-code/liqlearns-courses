# Post cold war world order — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 18, a multipolar order forms — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Chuyên đề 2.III.1 Wars that did not stop: Bosnia 1992–1995 kills about 100,000 over ethnic and religious divides; the Israel–Palestine conflict grinds on; Libya, Somalia and Sudan slide into civil war, hunger and epidemic — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the USSR's collapse in 1991 ends the Yalta two-pole order (1945–1991) — the book's box defines it: two superpowers, America's capitalist system against the USSR's socialist system; confrontation gives way to dialogue and détente, peace becomes the main trend though local civil wars and ethnic and religious conflicts persist, and a multipolar order forms around the US, EU, Japan, China and the Russian Federation; America aspires to a US-dominated unipolar order but cannot achieve it — the EU deepens its integration, Japan seeks a matching political position, Russia recovers, and China, the world's second economy since 2010, is America's fiercest superpower rival (the lesson closes with a US–China trade-war cartoon)
b) the Cold War ends but the shooting does not — the Balkans, Gaza and Africa keep the century's habit

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
