# Post cold war world order — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 18, a multipolar order forms" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The USSR's collapse in 1991 ends the Yalta two-pole order (1945–1991) — the book's box defines it: two superpowers, America's capitalist system against the USSR's socialist system; confrontation gives way to dialogue and détente, peace becomes the main trend though local civil wars and ethnic and religious conflicts persist, and a multipolar order forms around the US, EU, Japan, China and the Russian Federation; America aspires to a US-dominated unipolar order but cannot achieve it — the EU deepens its integration, Japan seeks a matching political position, Russia recovers, and China, the world's second economy since 2010, is America's fiercest superpower rival (the lesson closes with a US–China trade-war cartoon).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.III.1 Wars that did not stop: Bosnia 1992–1995 kills about 100,000 over ethnic and religious divides; the Israel–Palestine conflict grinds on; Libya, Somalia and Sudan slide into civil war, hunger and epidemic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Cold War ends but the shooting does not — the Balkans, Gaza and Africa keep the century's habit.
