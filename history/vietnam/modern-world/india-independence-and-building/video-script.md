# Video script — India independence and building

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: India independence and building.

Congress-led struggle forces the Mountbatten Plan of 1947 and partition into Hindu India and Muslim Pakistan; the struggle continues to full independence and the Republic is proclaimed on 26 January 1950; non-aligned abroad, India builds heavy industry including nuclear power at home, wins food self-sufficiency through the Green Revolution, and by the 1980s stands among the world's top ten industrial producers, with an atomic bomb in 1974 and a satellite in 1975.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
