# India independence and building — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 12, India: partition to republic", congress-led struggle forces the Mountbatten Plan of 1947 and partition into Hindu India and Muslim Pakistan; the struggle continues to full independence and the Republic is proclaimed on 26 January 1950; non-aligned abroad, India builds heavy industry including nuclear power at home, wins food self-sufficiency through the Green Revolution, and by the 1980s stands among the world's top ten industrial producers, with an atomic bomb in 1974 and a satellite in 1975
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of India independence and building as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
