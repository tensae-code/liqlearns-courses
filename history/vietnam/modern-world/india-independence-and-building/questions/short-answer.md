# India independence and building — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 12, India: partition to republic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Congress-led struggle forces the Mountbatten Plan of 1947 and partition into Hindu India and Muslim Pakistan; the struggle continues to full independence and the Republic is proclaimed on 26 January 1950; non-aligned abroad, India builds heavy industry including nuclear power at home, wins food self-sufficiency through the Green Revolution, and by the 1980s stands among the world's top ten industrial producers, with an atomic bomb in 1974 and a satellite in 1975.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of India independence and building as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
