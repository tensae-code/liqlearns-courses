# Video script — Republic and reign of terror

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Republic and reign of terror.

The timeline runs from the constitutional monarchy of 1789 and the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (26 August 1789 — a line of it, too, is quoted by Hồ Chí Minh in 1945) through the first republic of 1792 to the Jacobin revolutionary-democratic dictatorship of 1793, the revolution's peak, before the counter-revolutionary coup of 27 July 1794; the book calls it the "giant broom" that swept away the rubbish of feudal Europe.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
