# Republic and reign of terror — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 2, the stages of the French Revolution and its meaning", the timeline runs from the constitutional monarchy of 1789 and the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (26 August 1789 — a line of it, too, is quoted by Hồ Chí Minh in 1945) through the first republic of 1792 to the Jacobin revolutionary-democratic dictatorship of 1793, the revolution's peak, before the counter-revolutionary coup of 27 July 1794; the book calls it the "giant broom" that swept away the rubbish of feudal Europe
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Republic and reign of terror as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
