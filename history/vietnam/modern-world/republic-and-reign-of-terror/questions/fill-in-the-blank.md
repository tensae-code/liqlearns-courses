# Republic and reign of terror — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2, the stages of the French Revolution and its meaning" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The timeline runs from the constitutional monarchy of 1789 and the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (26 August 1789 — a line of it, too, is quoted by Hồ Chí Minh in 1945) through the first republic of 1792 to the Jacobin revolutionary-democratic dictatorship of 1793, the revolution's peak, before the counter-revolutionary coup of 27 July 1794; the book calls it the "giant broom" that swept away the rubbish of feudal Europe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Republic and reign of terror as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
