# Vietnam hoang sa truong sa 1884 to 1975 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 13 Sovereignty under protectorate and republic: France, speaking for the Nguyễn court, protests China's 1909 surveys, answers a British insurer that the Qing cannot pay for the wrecked Bellona because the islands are not China's, mounts the first scientific expeditions (1925, 1927), builds the 1937 lighthouse and 1938 sovereignty stele; at San Francisco in 1951 no state objects to Vietnam's claim; after Geneva 1954 the southern governments garrison and administer both archipelagos until the Liberation Army takes over Trường Sa in April 1975", a protectorate's lawyers and lighthouse-keepers keep a claim alive that armies later inherit
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam hoang sa truong sa 1884 to 1975 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
