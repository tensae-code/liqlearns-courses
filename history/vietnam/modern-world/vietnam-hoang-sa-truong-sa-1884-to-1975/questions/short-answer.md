# Vietnam hoang sa truong sa 1884 to 1975 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 13 Sovereignty under protectorate and republic: France, speaking for the Nguyễn court, protests China's 1909 surveys, answers a British insurer that the Qing cannot pay for the wrecked Bellona because the islands are not China's, mounts the first scientific expeditions (1925, 1927), builds the 1937 lighthouse and 1938 sovereignty stele; at San Francisco in 1951 no state objects to Vietnam's claim; after Geneva 1954 the southern governments garrison and administer both archipelagos until the Liberation Army takes over Trường Sa in April 1975" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A protectorate's lawyers and lighthouse-keepers keep a claim alive that armies later inherit.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam hoang sa truong sa 1884 to 1975 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
