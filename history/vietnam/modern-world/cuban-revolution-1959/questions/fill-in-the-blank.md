# Cuban revolution 1959 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 12, Cuba: the Moncada to Havana" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Latin America is the United States' "backyard" of pro-American dictatorships, yet national-democratic revolutions stir from Bolivia to Nicaragua (the book's box follows the Sandino Front to its 1990 defeat by US intervention); in Cuba, Batista's US-backed dictatorship (March 1952) is attacked at the Moncada barracks by Fidel Castro and 135 youths (July 1953); exiled in Mexico, Castro founds the July 26 Movement, lands from the Granma with 81 fighters (November 1956), and on 1 January 1959 enters Havana — the Republic of Cuba then builds socialism from 1961, turning a sugar monoculture toward machine-building and energy, with the region's best education and life expectancy up from 64 (1960) to 74 (1991).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4 Socialism reaches Asia and the Caribbean: Cuba's 1959 revolution chooses socialism from 1961 under American embargo and rebuilds a one-crop economy into industry, schools and hospitals" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An island ninety miles from Florida defies the embargo and keeps its free clinics.
