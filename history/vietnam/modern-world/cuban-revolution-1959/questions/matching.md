# Cuban revolution 1959 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 12, Cuba: the Moncada to Havana — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 4 Socialism reaches Asia and the Caribbean: Cuba's 1959 revolution chooses socialism from 1961 under American embargo and rebuilds a one-crop economy into industry, schools and hospitals — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) latin America is the United States' "backyard" of pro-American dictatorships, yet national-democratic revolutions stir from Bolivia to Nicaragua (the book's box follows the Sandino Front to its 1990 defeat by US intervention); in Cuba, Batista's US-backed dictatorship (March 1952) is attacked at the Moncada barracks by Fidel Castro and 135 youths (July 1953); exiled in Mexico, Castro founds the July 26 Movement, lands from the Granma with 81 fighters (November 1956), and on 1 January 1959 enters Havana — the Republic of Cuba then builds socialism from 1961, turning a sugar monoculture toward machine-building and energy, with the region's best education and life expectancy up from 64 (1960) to 74 (1991)
b) an island ninety miles from Florida defies the embargo and keeps its free clinics

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
