# Vietnam hoang sa flotilla and the khao le rite — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 17, the Khao lề thế lính Hoàng Sa rite of Lý Sơn island", the Nguyễn lords sent a flotilla of local sailors to the Hoàng Sa islands in crude boats; the islanders still hold a farewell rite for soldiers who might never return (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 3, the Hoàng Sa flotilla story", lord Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên raises a 70-man flotilla from Lý Sơn island; each year they sail three days and nights to the Hoàng Sa islands for six months in tiny boats, and many never come home, so the island holds the Khao lề farewell rite for them (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
