# Vietnam hoang sa flotilla and the khao le rite — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 17, the Khao lề thế lính Hoàng Sa rite of Lý Sơn island" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Nguyễn lords sent a flotilla of local sailors to the Hoàng Sa islands in crude boats; the islanders still hold a farewell rite for soldiers who might never return.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 3, the Hoàng Sa flotilla story" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lord Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên raises a 70-man flotilla from Lý Sơn island; each year they sail three days and nights to the Hoàng Sa islands for six months in tiny boats, and many never come home, so the island holds the Khao lề farewell rite for them.
