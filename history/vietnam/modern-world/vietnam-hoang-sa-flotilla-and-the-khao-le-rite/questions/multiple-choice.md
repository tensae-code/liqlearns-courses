# Vietnam hoang sa flotilla and the khao le rite — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Bài 17, the Khao lề thế lính Hoàng Sa rite of Lý Sơn island"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the Nguyễn lords sent a flotilla of local sailors to the Hoàng Sa islands in crude boats; the islanders still hold a farewell rite for soldiers who might never return  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Bài 3, the Hoàng Sa flotilla story"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) lord Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên raises a 70-man flotilla from Lý Sơn island; each year they sail three days and nights to the Hoàng Sa islands for six months in tiny boats, and many never come home, so the island holds the Khao lề farewell rite for them  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
