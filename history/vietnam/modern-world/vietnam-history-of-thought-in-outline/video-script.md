# Video script — Vietnam history of thought in outline

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam history of thought in outline.

In the founding age, ideas of diligent labour, harmony, solidarity and brave struggle against nature and invaders are carried in legend and myth; from the early centuries CE Vietnam selectively absorbs Hinduism and Buddhism from India and Confucianism, Taoism and Legalist thought from China, then creates schools of its own — Trúc Lâm Buddhism under the Trần (13th–14th centuries, its founder-king Trần Nhân Tông's statue at Yên Tử is pictured) and Bửu Sơn Kỳ Hương in the mid-19th century; the modern age brings the French Revolution's "liberty, equality, fraternity" (1789) and Sun Yat-sen's Three Principles into the patriotic movement, and from 1920, when Nguyễn Ái Quốc becomes Vietnam's first communist (pictured at the Tours Congress), Marxism-Leninism spreads — after the Party's founding in 1930 — and is creatively applied as Hồ Chí Minh Thought, defined in the Ninth Congress documents quoted here.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
