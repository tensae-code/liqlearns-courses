# Vietnam hue 1945 bao dai abdication — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 19, the August Revolution in Huế, 1945" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** On 30 August 1945, before the Ngọ Môn gate, Emperor Bảo Đại reads his abdication edict and hands the royal seal and sword to the revolutionary government.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam hue 1945 bao dai abdication as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
