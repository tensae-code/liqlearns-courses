## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 5.2, the great figures of Renaissance literature, art and science** — renaissance literature reaches its peak across poetry, the novel and drama — Dante Alighieri with the Divine Comedy and the Vita Nuova, Petrarch with his lyric poems, Giovanni Boccaccio with the Decameron (Mười ngày), François Rabelais of France with Gargantua and Pantagruel, Miguel de Cervantes of Spain with Don Quixote, and above all William Shakespeare of England with Hamlet, Othello and Romeo and Juliet; art begins in Italy and spreads across Europe, peaking in the 15th–16th centuries with Leonardo da Vinci (the Last Supper, the Mona Lisa), Michelangelo (the David, the Pietà) and Raphael (the Sistine Madonna, the School of Athens — pictured with the Greek scholars gathered in it); architecture stresses geometry, symmetry and proportion, its typical work the dome of St Peter's Basilica in Italy (the Vatican); and the age's outstanding science is astronomy — Nicolaus Copernicus of Poland with the heliocentric theory, Giordano Bruno of Italy proving the Sun is only the centre of the Solar System, and Galileo Galilei of Italy, who makes the telescope to observe the sky — while technique advances in weaving, mining, metallurgy, shipbuilding and weapons-making, machines are built, and water power is used in many branches of production
2. **§2 Bài 2 The Renaissance stage: the movement to revive Greek and Roman art, human values, perspective space and oil paint — Botticelli's Birth of Venus, 1486** — venus rises from the sea again in Florence, and art turns back to the human being
