# Vietnam democratic front 1936 1939 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, the Democratic Front years" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** After the Comintern's Seventh Congress (July 1935) and France's Popular Front victory, the Indochinese Communist Party founds the Indochinese Democratic Front (July 1936); the Indochina Congress movement petitions openly, crowds greet the Goda mission and Governor-General Brévié (1937), railwaymen strike at Trường Thi, 25,000 rally in Hanoi on 1 May 1938, and a legal press flourishes — a rehearsal, the book judges, for the August Revolution.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam democratic front 1936 1939 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
