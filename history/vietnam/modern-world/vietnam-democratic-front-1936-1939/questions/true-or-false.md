# Vietnam democratic front 1936 1939 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 7, the Democratic Front years", after the Comintern's Seventh Congress (July 1935) and France's Popular Front victory, the Indochinese Communist Party founds the Indochinese Democratic Front (July 1936); the Indochina Congress movement petitions openly, crowds greet the Goda mission and Governor-General Brévié (1937), railwaymen strike at Trường Thi, 25,000 rally in Hanoi on 1 May 1938, and a legal press flourishes — a rehearsal, the book judges, for the August Revolution
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Vietnam democratic front 1936 1939 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
