# Video script — Vietnam democratic front 1936 1939

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam democratic front 1936 1939.

After the Comintern's Seventh Congress (July 1935) and France's Popular Front victory, the Indochinese Communist Party founds the Indochinese Democratic Front (July 1936); the Indochina Congress movement petitions openly, crowds greet the Goda mission and Governor-General Brévié (1937), railwaymen strike at Trường Thi, 25,000 rally in Hanoi on 1 May 1938, and a legal press flourishes — a rehearsal, the book judges, for the August Revolution.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
