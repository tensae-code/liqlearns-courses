# Ussr and eastern europe 1945 1991 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 10, the USSR: recovery, zenith, dissolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The USSR rebuilds by 1950 — industry up 73% in the 1946–50 plan, over 6,000 factories restored or built — and by the 1960s is the second industrial power with some 20% of world industrial output; it launches the first satellite (1957) and Gagarin (1961) and gives free education to a population three-quarters secondary- or university-educated (1971); stagnation from the mid-1970s brings Gorbachev (March 1985) and perestroika with glasnost and multi-party politics; after the failed coup of 19 August 1991 the republics secede and on 25 December 1991 Gorbachev resigns and the USSR dissolves.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 10, Eastern Europe's people's democracies" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** With the Red Army's help, people's democracies arise in 1944–46 (the GDR in 1949) and build through land reform, nationalisation and CMEA cooperation — Albania fully electrified by 1970, the GDR's national income quadrupled (1949–70), Bulgarian industry multiplied 55-fold (1939–75); decline follows from the mid-1970s — income growth falls from 3.3% (1981–85) to 0.5% (1989) under $128.6 billion of foreign debt (1987) — and the book photographs Poland's Solidarity strike of August 1980 before the 1989 elections bring anti-socialist forces to power and the CMEA dissolves in 1991.
