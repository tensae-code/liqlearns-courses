Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 18, story of the patriot Huỳnh Thúc Kháng", he tops the royal exams in 1904 but refuses office to lead the Duy Tân movement, spends 13 years exiled on Côn Đảo, and in 1946 serves as acting head of state
