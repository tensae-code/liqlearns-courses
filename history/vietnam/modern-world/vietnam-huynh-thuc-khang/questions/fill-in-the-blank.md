# Vietnam huynh thuc khang — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 18, story of the patriot Huỳnh Thúc Kháng" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** He tops the royal exams in 1904 but refuses office to lead the Duy Tân movement, spends 13 years exiled on Côn Đảo, and in 1946 serves as acting head of state.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam huynh thuc khang as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
