# Video script — Vietnam constitutions 1946 to 2025

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam constitutions 1946 to 2025.

Since 1946 Vietnam has issued five constitutions — 1946, 1959, 1980, 1992, 2013 — each born of its moment (independence won; the Geneva division; unification; Đổi mới under way; Đổi mới deepened and integration widening), and in 2025 National Assembly term XV passes Resolution 203/2025/QH15 amending the 2013 text; a constitution settles the country's most basic questions — political regime, socio-economic policy, human and citizen rights and duties, the state apparatus — and stands as the root law of highest legal force; the 1946 Constitution, passed on 9 November 1946 with a preamble, 7 chapters and 70 articles, is Vietnam's first and, the book notes, a democratic and progressive constitution for Southeast Asia at the time: its Articles 6, 7 and 10 (quoted) give all citizens equality before the law "in every respect — political, economic, cultural" and the freedoms of speech, press, organisation, assembly, belief, residence and movement; the Đổi mới constitutions follow — 1992 (passed 15 April 1992; its Article 15 writes the multi-component commodity economy under a market mechanism with state management into law, and its foreign policy is peace and friendship without distinction of regime) and 2013 (clearer division of powers among Assembly, President, Government and judiciary; the principle of "power control" written in for the first time in Vietnam's constitutional history; a human-rights chapter of 36 articles — equality before the law, protection of citizens abroad, the right to life, inviolability of the body and of private life and correspondence); the 2025 amendment affirms the Fatherland Front's central role and rebuilds local government in two tiers — province and grassroots — ending the district level from 1 July 2025.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
