# Colonial rule in southeast asia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5 How the colonies were ruled: local feudal elites kept as tools, all real power in colonial hands, divide-and-rule, native troops, taxation and plantations draining rubber, coffee, tea and rice — and deliberate ignorance: 8.5% literacy in Malaya in 1931, over 90% illiterate in Vietnam, opium and alcohol as policy; Nguyễn Ái Quốc's Colonial Regime on Trial quoted against France" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The empire's cheapest weapon is an unschooled, divided, drugged colony.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Colonial rule in southeast asia as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
