# Lesson plan — Vietnam subsidy period and doi moi

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Vietnam subsidy period and doi moi
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The whole national story in one sweep: the first states, a thousand years of northern domination, the dynasties, colonisation, 1945, 1954, 1975 and Đổi mới — with the world's first civilisations beside it (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Vietnam subsidy period and doi moi as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The whole national story in one sweep: the first states, a thousand years of northern domination, the dynasties, colonisation, 1945, 1954, 1975 and Đổi mới — with the world's first civilisations beside it — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 17, life in the subsidy (bao cấp) period, 1976–1986** — goods come by ration stamp and the precious rice book, farmers work for cooperative points, and in 1978–79 a whole neighbourhood crowds into the one house with a black-and-white television while the owner charges the battery nightly
2. **§2 Bài 17, the country in the Đổi mới period** — from 1986 Vietnam moves from food shortage to one of the world's top rice exporters, with factories, cars and computers of its own making
3. **§3 Bài 20, unification, the subsidy years and Đổi mới** — after spring 1975 two state forms persist until the Political Consultative Conference (Saigon, 15–21 November 1975), the common general election of 25 April 1976 and the National Assembly's first session (24 June–3 July 1976) decide the country's name — the Socialist Republic of Vietnam — its emblem, flag, anthem, capital Hanoi, and Saigon–Gia Định renamed Ho Chi Minh City, and the first Thống Nhất trains leave both capitals at the end of 1976; the 1976–85 years run two five-year plans, Party Congresses IV (1976) and V (1982), the 1980 Constitution and UN membership (1977), building works like the Hoà Bình hydroelectric dam (begun 1979) — yet production falls short, food especially, and inflation runs high; crisis, embargo and the world's changes (China's reform of 1978, the USSR's perestroika of 1985) bring the Sixth Congress (December 1986) and the Đổi mới line: abolish the bureaucratic subsidy model, build a multi-component commodity economy under a market mechanism with state management, industrialise and modernise, open to foreign investment and integrate internationally — and the achievements follow: food enough to eat, with reserves and exports, from 1989 (the first rice and crude oil exported that year; by 2005 Vietnam leads the world in pepper and stands second in rice, coffee and cashew), GDP rising from 76.7 thousand billion đồng (1991) to 10,310 (2023), WTO membership in 2007, relations normalised with America and ASEAN joined in 1995, new Constitutions in 1992 and 2013, illiteracy eradicated nationwide by 2000 — though the book closes by naming the remaining limits: an economy not yet sustainable or competitive, a degrading environment and widening regional income gaps
4. **§4 Chuyên đề 3, II.2, the Socialist Republic's state in Đổi mới and integration** — after the Great Victory of Spring 1975 two state forms still exist side by side, so the second nationwide general election (April 1976) elects National Assembly term VI, whose first session renames the country the Socialist Republic of Vietnam — completing unification in state terms and preparing the ground for building socialism, defending the Fatherland and integrating internationally; from 1986 this state carries out Đổi mới: in agriculture, land contracting solves the food problem and makes Vietnam a leading rice exporter; in industry and services, the Foreign Investment Law (1987) and the Company and Private Enterprise Laws (1990) open the way to enterprises of every scale, including transnational groups; abroad it pursues an open, multilateral foreign policy — the achievements chart runs ASEAN membership and normalised relations with the United States (1995), APEC (1998), WTO membership as the organisation's 150th member (2006, the documents handover is photographed) and the free-trade agreement signed with the European Union (2019)
5. **§5 Bài 4 Socialism renews itself after 1991: Vietnam's Đổi Mới from 1986 lifts the country out of crisis to middle income, Laos renews from 1986, and Cuba reforms step by step under embargo** — the surviving socialist states save the system by changing how it feeds people
6. **§6 Bài 10 Đổi Mới begins, 1986–1995: the Sixth Congress (12-1986), "a very basic turning point", rules that renewal is not changing the socialist goal but achieving it by correct conceptions and suitable steps; the centre is economic — abolish the centralised bureaucratic subsidy mechanism for a state-managed market, run the three great programmes (food, consumer goods, exports), beat inflation — while political, cultural and social renewal follows in step and foreign policy multilateralises: "Vietnam wants to be a friend of all countries"** — a ruling party votes to dismantle the economic machine it built, and calls the dismantling fidelity to its goal
7. **§7 Bài 10 Đổi Mới deepens, 1996 to today: the Eighth Congress (1996) sets industrialisation and modernisation as the period's task — big infrastructure, an open integrating economy, education and science as top national policy, culture as both goal and engine; from 2006 the Tenth Congress pushes comprehensive renewal out of underdevelopment — perfecting the socialist-oriented market economy, industrialisation tied to the knowledge economy, and the shift from "international economic integration" to proactive, comprehensive integration across every field** — stage two builds the hardware, stage three rewires the country's whole relationship with the world
8. **§8 Bài 11 What Đổi Mới achieved in the economy: out of crisis into growth above 6% a year (positive even through COVID-19), GDP reaching 409 billion USD in 2022; the structure transforms — agriculture falls from 38.06% of GDP (1986) to 11.88% (2022) as industry and services climb; the ration-stamp regime is abolished (1-4-1989), the Hanoi–Hải Phòng expressway opens to international standard, and the 1,487 km 500 kV North–South line (1992–93) gives the country its first power backbone** — the subsidy era's defining object, the ration stamp, is retired within three years of the decision that doomed it
9. **§9 Bài 11 Lessons of Đổi Mới: hold fast to national independence joined with socialism on the foundation of Marxism-Leninism and Hồ Chí Minh thought; renew comprehensively and in step, starting from practice and solving what practice raises; renew for the people's benefit under the maxim "the people are the root" — people know, discuss, do, inspect, supervise and enjoy; and combine inner with outer strength, national strength with the age's** — the reform era grades itself, and its fourth lesson is the same combination-of-strengths formula the war lessons taught
