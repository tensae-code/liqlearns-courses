# Vietnam subsidy period and doi moi — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, life in the subsidy (bao cấp) period, 1976–1986" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Goods come by ration stamp and the precious rice book, farmers work for cooperative points, and in 1978–79 a whole neighbourhood crowds into the one house with a black-and-white television while the owner charges the battery nightly.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 17, the country in the Đổi mới period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From 1986 Vietnam moves from food shortage to one of the world's top rice exporters, with factories, cars and computers of its own making.
