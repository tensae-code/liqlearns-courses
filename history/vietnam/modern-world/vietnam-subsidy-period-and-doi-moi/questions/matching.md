# Vietnam subsidy period and doi moi — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 17, life in the subsidy (bao cấp) period, 1976–1986 — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bài 17, the country in the Đổi mới period — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) goods come by ration stamp and the precious rice book, farmers work for cooperative points, and in 1978–79 a whole neighbourhood crowds into the one house with a black-and-white television while the owner charges the battery nightly
b) from 1986 Vietnam moves from food shortage to one of the world's top rice exporters, with factories, cars and computers of its own making

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
