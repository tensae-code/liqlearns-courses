# Vietnam subsidy period and doi moi — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 17, life in the subsidy (bao cấp) period, 1976–1986", goods come by ration stamp and the precious rice book, farmers work for cooperative points, and in 1978–79 a whole neighbourhood crowds into the one house with a black-and-white television while the owner charges the battery nightly (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 17, the country in the Đổi mới period", from 1986 Vietnam moves from food shortage to one of the world's top rice exporters, with factories, cars and computers of its own making (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
