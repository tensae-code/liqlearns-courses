# Video script — The first world war versailles and the results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The first world war versailles and the results.

An imperialist, unjust war on both sides ends in the Entente's victory and Germany's surrender in November 1918, at a cost the book tabulates: 38 countries, 10 million dead, 20 million wounded, 85 billion dollars of economic loss; Europe's states all become debtors of an America enriched by the arms trade, whose national income doubles and foreign investment quadruples, while Japan seizes German islands and rises in East Asia.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
