# The first world war versailles and the results — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 12, the war's course and its cost", an imperialist, unjust war on both sides ends in the Entente's victory and Germany's surrender in November 1918, at a cost the book tabulates: 38 countries, 10 million dead, 20 million wounded, 85 billion dollars of economic loss; Europe's states all become debtors of an America enriched by the arms trade, whose national income doubles and foreign investment quadruples, while Japan seizes German islands and rises in East Asia (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 2.I.1 What the First World War left: the cost table — 38 countries, 74 million mobilised, 10 million dead, 20 million disabled, 85 billion dollars — four empires collapse, the October Revolution wins, and the Versailles–Washington order is drawn", europe counts its dead in millions and redraws its map, but the new order plants the next war (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
