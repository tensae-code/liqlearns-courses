# The first world war versailles and the results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 12, the war's course and its cost" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** An imperialist, unjust war on both sides ends in the Entente's victory and Germany's surrender in November 1918, at a cost the book tabulates: 38 countries, 10 million dead, 20 million wounded, 85 billion dollars of economic loss; Europe's states all become debtors of an America enriched by the arms trade, whose national income doubles and foreign investment quadruples, while Japan seizes German islands and rises in East Asia.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chuyên đề 2.I.1 What the First World War left: the cost table — 38 countries, 74 million mobilised, 10 million dead, 20 million disabled, 85 billion dollars — four empires collapse, the October Revolution wins, and the Versailles–Washington order is drawn" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Europe counts its dead in millions and redraws its map, but the new order plants the next war.
