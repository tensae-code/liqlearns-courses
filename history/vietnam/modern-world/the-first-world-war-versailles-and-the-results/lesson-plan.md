# Lesson plan — The first world war versailles and the results

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** The first world war versailles and the results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Revolutions, industry and a divided country: the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation beside Vietnam's partition, the Tây Sơn, the Nguyễn and the first French colonial exploitation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The first world war versailles and the results as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Revolutions, industry and a divided country: the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation beside Vietnam's partition, the Tây Sơn, the Nguyễn and the first French colonial exploitation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 12, the war's course and its cost** — an imperialist, unjust war on both sides ends in the Entente's victory and Germany's surrender in November 1918, at a cost the book tabulates: 38 countries, 10 million dead, 20 million wounded, 85 billion dollars of economic loss; Europe's states all become debtors of an America enriched by the arms trade, whose national income doubles and foreign investment quadruples, while Japan seizes German islands and rises in East Asia
2. **§2 Chuyên đề 2.I.1 What the First World War left: the cost table — 38 countries, 74 million mobilised, 10 million dead, 20 million disabled, 85 billion dollars — four empires collapse, the October Revolution wins, and the Versailles–Washington order is drawn** — europe counts its dead in millions and redraws its map, but the new order plants the next war
