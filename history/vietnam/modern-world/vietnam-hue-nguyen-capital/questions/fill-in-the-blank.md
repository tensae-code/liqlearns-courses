# Vietnam hue nguyen capital — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 19, the old capital of Huế" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Nguyễn dynasty builds a three-ringed citadel on the Perfume River, with Thiên Mụ pagoda and the royal tombs — Vietnam's first UNESCO World Heritage site.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam hue nguyen capital as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
