# Lesson plan — Vietnam mac dynasty and north south war

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Vietnam mac dynasty and north south war
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Revolutions, industry and a divided country: the bourgeois revolutions and industrialisation beside Vietnam's partition, the Tây Sơn, the Nguyễn and the first French colonial exploitation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Vietnam mac dynasty and north south war as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 5, the founding of the Mạc dynasty (1527)** — as the Lê sơ rots in factional strife and peasant risings, the strongman Mạc Đăng Dung — a seventh-generation descendant of the laureate Mạc Đĩnh Chi, risen through the palace guard — destroys his rivals one by one; the chronicles quoted here concede that "Đăng Dung's prestige grows day by day, the court all submits" and "the people's hearts all turn to him"; in 1527 he forces the Lê king to abdicate and founds the Mạc
2. **§2 Bài 5, the North–South dynasties war, 1533–1592** — the Lê loyalist Nguyễn Kim sets up a rival "Southern dynasty" in Thanh Hoá in 1533 under the banner "support the Lê, destroy the Mạc"; for decades Thanh–Nghệ and the northern delta are the battlefield, until the Southern army takes Thăng Long in the early 1590s and the Mạc flee to Cao Bằng; the country is left divided, its villages ruined and its families scattered — Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm's poem Thương loạn, quoted here, grieves over fields where nothing is planted and war without end
