# Vietnam mac dynasty and north south war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 5, the founding of the Mạc dynasty (1527)", as the Lê sơ rots in factional strife and peasant risings, the strongman Mạc Đăng Dung — a seventh-generation descendant of the laureate Mạc Đĩnh Chi, risen through the palace guard — destroys his rivals one by one; the chronicles quoted here concede that "Đăng Dung's prestige grows day by day, the court all submits" and "the people's hearts all turn to him"; in 1527 he forces the Lê king to abdicate and founds the Mạc (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 5, the North–South dynasties war, 1533–1592", the Lê loyalist Nguyễn Kim sets up a rival "Southern dynasty" in Thanh Hoá in 1533 under the banner "support the Lê, destroy the Mạc"; for decades Thanh–Nghệ and the northern delta are the battlefield, until the Southern army takes Thăng Long in the early 1590s and the Mạc flee to Cao Bằng; the country is left divided, its villages ruined and its families scattered — Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm's poem Thương loạn, quoted here, grieves over fields where nothing is planted and war without end (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
