# Video script — Vietnam mac dynasty and north south war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam mac dynasty and north south war.

As the Lê sơ rots in factional strife and peasant risings, the strongman Mạc Đăng Dung — a seventh-generation descendant of the laureate Mạc Đĩnh Chi, risen through the palace guard — destroys his rivals one by one; the chronicles quoted here concede that "Đăng Dung's prestige grows day by day, the court all submits" and "the people's hearts all turn to him"; in 1527 he forces the Lê king to abdicate and founds the Mạc.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
