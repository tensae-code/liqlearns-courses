# Vietnam fatherland protection wars overview — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7 Vietnam's wars of defence in one view: geography makes the country a bridge everyone must cross, so its history is a chain of defensive wars; the table of victories runs Bạch Đằng 938, 981 and 1288, Như Nguyệt 1075–1077, Rạch Gầm–Xoài Mút 1785, Ngọc Hồi–Đống Đa 1789 — and in 1637 envoy Giang Văn Minh answers the Ming king's mocking couplet with "the Bạch Đằng's blood is still red"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** One river wins three wars across 350 years, and a diplomat wins a fourth with a sentence.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 9 Lessons of the Fatherland-protection wars, 1945 to today: keep patriotism alight in every generation (Hồ Chí Minh's image of patriotism as a treasure sometimes displayed, sometimes hidden in a chest, which leaders must bring out into action); consolidate the great national unity bloc; combine national strength with the strength of the age while fusing the two strategic tasks, building and defending; and keep developing people's-war art into an all-people national defence that protects the country "early, from afar"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Eighty years of wars are distilled into four transferable lessons for a peacetime generation.
