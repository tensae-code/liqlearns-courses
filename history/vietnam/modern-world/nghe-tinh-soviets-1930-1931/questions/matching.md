# Nghe tinh soviets 1930 1931 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 7, the Nghệ-Tĩnh soviets — a)
**2.** The one thing this lesson teaches — b)

Meanings:
a) the Depression bites the colony and strikes flare at Phú Riềng, Nam Định and Bến Thuỷ; on 1 May 1930 workers demonstrate for the first time and a red flag flies on Bài Thơ mountain; in September–October 1930 the peasants of Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh raise soviets that divide the public land, abolish taxes and run literacy classes — until French aircraft bomb an 8,000-strong peasant demonstration at Hưng Nguyên (12 September 1930) and the movement is crushed by early 1931, leaving the worker–peasant alliance as its legacy
b) the story of Nghe tinh soviets 1930 1931 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
