# Nghe tinh soviets 1930 1931 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 7, the Nghệ-Tĩnh soviets" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Depression bites the colony and strikes flare at Phú Riềng, Nam Định and Bến Thuỷ; on 1 May 1930 workers demonstrate for the first time and a red flag flies on Bài Thơ mountain; in September–October 1930 the peasants of Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh raise soviets that divide the public land, abolish taxes and run literacy classes — until French aircraft bomb an 8,000-strong peasant demonstration at Hưng Nguyên (12 September 1930) and the movement is crushed by early 1931, leaving the worker–peasant alliance as its legacy.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Nghe tinh soviets 1930 1931 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
