# Video script — Nghe tinh soviets 1930 1931

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Nghe tinh soviets 1930 1931.

The Depression bites the colony and strikes flare at Phú Riềng, Nam Định and Bến Thuỷ; on 1 May 1930 workers demonstrate for the first time and a red flag flies on Bài Thơ mountain; in September–October 1930 the peasants of Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh raise soviets that divide the public land, abolish taxes and run literacy classes — until French aircraft bomb an 8,000-strong peasant demonstration at Hưng Nguyên (12 September 1930) and the movement is crushed by early 1931, leaving the worker–peasant alliance as its legacy.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
