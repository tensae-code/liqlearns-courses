# Video script — Militarism in japan the army above the state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Militarism in japan the army above the state.

The indemnity from the Sino-Japanese War (1894–95) feeds an industry whose spearheads are shipbuilding, weapons and military equipment, steel and electricity; the great monopoly houses — Mitsui, Mitsubishi, Sumitomo — come to dominate the country's economic and political life and export capital into China and Korea; victory over Russia (1904–1905) brings the Liaodong peninsula, southern Sakhalin, Taiwan, Lüshun and Shandong into the Japanese empire mapped here.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
