## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 14, Japan turns imperialist** — the indemnity from the Sino-Japanese War (1894–95) feeds an industry whose spearheads are shipbuilding, weapons and military equipment, steel and electricity; the great monopoly houses — Mitsui, Mitsubishi, Sumitomo — come to dominate the country's economic and political life and export capital into China and Korea; victory over Russia (1904–1905) brings the Liaodong peninsula, southern Sakhalin, Taiwan, Lüshun and Shandong into the Japanese empire mapped here
2. **§2 Bài 3, Japan between the wars** — japan's post-war boom lasts eighteen months before the 1920–21 slump; the Communist Party is founded in July 1922, Tokyo's banks crash in 1927, and the Depression cuts industrial output by 32.5% and trade by 80% with some 3 million unemployed; the 1927 Tanaka memorial points the army outward — Manchuria in September 1931, Indochina in September 1940, Pearl Harbor in December 1941, surrender on 15 August 1945
