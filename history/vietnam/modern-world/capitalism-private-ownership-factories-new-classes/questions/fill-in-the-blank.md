# Capitalism private ownership factories new classes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2 Capitalism takes the map: established in the Netherlands and England in the later 1600s, in France and North America by the late 1700s, in Italy and Germany by the later 1800s — the German emperor proclaimed at Versailles in January 1871" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In two centuries the counting-house system conquers two continents capital by capital.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2 From free competition to monopoly: three decades of new energy and science bankrupt the small firms; cartels and syndicates in Germany and France, trusts in America — by the early 1900s about 1% of firms hold over three-quarters of the world's steam and electric power" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Competition eats itself — the cartoonists draw the trust as an octopus strangling the oil barrel.
