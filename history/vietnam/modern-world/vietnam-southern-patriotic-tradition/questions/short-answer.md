# Vietnam southern patriotic tradition — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 24, the South's patriotic tradition and Trương Định" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** When the French seize Gia Định, commander Trương Định refuses a court posting in 1862, stays to fight, is proclaimed "Marshal who Pacifies the West" by the people, and kills himself rather than be captured; Nguyễn Trung Trực burns the French ship Espérance.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 24, Nguyễn Thị Định and the Đồng Khởi movement" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** She launches and leads the Đồng Khởi uprising in Bến Tre and becomes deputy commander of the southern liberation armed forces against the US.
