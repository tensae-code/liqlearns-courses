# Vietnam southern patriotic tradition — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 24, the South's patriotic tradition and Trương Định", when the French seize Gia Định, commander Trương Định refuses a court posting in 1862, stays to fight, is proclaimed "Marshal who Pacifies the West" by the people, and kills himself rather than be captured; Nguyễn Trung Trực burns the French ship Espérance (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 24, Nguyễn Thị Định and the Đồng Khởi movement", she launches and leads the Đồng Khởi uprising in Bến Tre and becomes deputy commander of the southern liberation armed forces against the US (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
