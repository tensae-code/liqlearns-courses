# September 11 and the war on terror — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 2.III.2 11 September 2001 and America's global war on terror: al-Qaeda hijackers strike with civilian aircraft, killing about 3,000; President Bush launches pre-emptive wars in Afghanistan (2001) and Iraq (2003), and a new anti-terror strategy follows in 2018" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Four hijacked airliners turn terrorism into America's top enemy and pull the world into its war machine.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of September 11 and the war on terror as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
