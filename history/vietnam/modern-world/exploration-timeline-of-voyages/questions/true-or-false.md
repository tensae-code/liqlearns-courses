# Exploration timeline of voyages — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 2, the great voyages", dias rounds Africa's cape in 1487 — a storm blows him past it, so it is first named Cape of Storms, then Good Hope; Columbus crosses the Atlantic in 1492; da Gama reaches Calicut in 1498; Magellan's fleet circles the Earth, 1519–1522 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Shared Theme 1, the voyages of Columbus and Magellan", columbus (1451–1506) wins Spain's backing for a westward plan, sails with three ships in August 1492, and after two months reaches the Caribbean still believing it the East Indies — he returns in 1493, 1498 and 1502; Magellan (1480–1521) finds the strait at America's tip, names the calm Pacific, and is killed in the Philippines, but Elcano brings the fleet home in September 1522, proving the Earth round (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
