## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 15, the home fronts, 1951–1954** — the Party's Second Congress meets at Vĩnh Quang, Tuyên Quang in February 1951 — the "Resistance Congress" — and the Liên Việt Front is founded that March; the 1952 Congress of Emulation Soldiers honours heroes Cù Chính Lan, La Văn Cầu, Nguyễn Thị Chiên and Trần Đại Nghĩa; production emulation and thrift feed the front, rent reduction and land reform begin in early 1953, the defence industry supplies thousands of tons of weapons and equipment, aid arrives from China and the USSR, and education is reformed under the motto "serve the resistance, serve the people, serve production"
2. **§2 Bài 7 The resistance matures, 1951–1953: the Second Party Congress (Feb 1951) brings the Party into the open as the Vietnam Workers' Party and restates the revolution's twin tasks; the Liên Việt Front unites the home front (3-3-1951); production-and-thrift campaigns, the first national emulation congress (1952) and the December 1953 Land Reform Law feed and legitimise the war, while the Trần Hưng Đạo, Hoàng Hoa Thám, Quang Trung, Hòa Bình, Tây Bắc and Thượng Lào campaigns keep the battlefield initiative** — by 1953 the resistance is no longer surviving — it is administering, taxing, reforming land and choosing where to attack
