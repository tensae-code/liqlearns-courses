# Video script — Vietnam resistance 1951 1954 home fronts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vietnam resistance 1951 1954 home fronts.

The Party's Second Congress meets at Vĩnh Quang, Tuyên Quang in February 1951 — the "Resistance Congress" — and the Liên Việt Front is founded that March; the 1952 Congress of Emulation Soldiers honours heroes Cù Chính Lan, La Văn Cầu, Nguyễn Thị Chiên and Trần Đại Nghĩa; production emulation and thrift feed the front, rent reduction and land reform begin in early 1953, the defence industry supplies thousands of tons of weapons and equipment, aid arrives from China and the USSR, and education is reformed under the motto "serve the resistance, serve the people, serve production".

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
