# Vietnam dien bien phu campaign 1954 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 15, the course of the Điện Biên Phủ campaign" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From 13 March to 7 May 1954, in three waves, the army takes the strongpoint groups one by one — after hauling its artillery in by hand, and then, on new orders, hauling it all out and in again by another road.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 15, story of capturing General De Castries alive" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On the afternoon of 7 May 1954 Tạ Quốc Luật's five-man assault team storms the bunker shouting "Hands up!"; 23 French officers file out, De Castries surrenders, and the "Determined to fight, determined to win" flag flies at 17:30.
