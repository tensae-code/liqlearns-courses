# Vietnam dien bien phu campaign 1954 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 15, the course of the Điện Biên Phủ campaign", from 13 March to 7 May 1954, in three waves, the army takes the strongpoint groups one by one — after hauling its artillery in by hand, and then, on new orders, hauling it all out and in again by another road (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 15, story of capturing General De Castries alive", on the afternoon of 7 May 1954 Tạ Quốc Luật's five-man assault team storms the bunker shouting "Hands up!"; 23 French officers file out, De Castries surrenders, and the "Determined to fight, determined to win" flag flies at 17:30 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
