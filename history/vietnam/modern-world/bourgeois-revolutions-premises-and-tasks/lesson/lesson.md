# Bourgeois revolutions premises and tasks

## Bài 1 What a bourgeois revolution needs and does: capitalism growing inside feudalism (England's coal at four-fifths of Europe's output before 1640; America's cotton and tobacco chained to England — Hồ Chí Minh's "why did America make revolution?"), absolutist kings, a rich bourgeoisie without power, and Enlightenment ideas from Montesquieu, Voltaire and Rousseau; the twin tasks are national unity and democracy

Before a single barricade rises, four premises must ripen — money, anger, numbers and ideas.
