# Bourgeois revolutions premises and tasks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 1 What a bourgeois revolution needs and does: capitalism growing inside feudalism (England's coal at four-fifths of Europe's output before 1640; America's cotton and tobacco chained to England — Hồ Chí Minh's "why did America make revolution?"), absolutist kings, a rich bourgeoisie without power, and Enlightenment ideas from Montesquieu, Voltaire and Rousseau; the twin tasks are national unity and democracy" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Before a single barricade rises, four premises must ripen — money, anger, numbers and ideas.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Bourgeois revolutions premises and tasks as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
