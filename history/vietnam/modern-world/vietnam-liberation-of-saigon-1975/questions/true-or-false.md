# Vietnam liberation of saigon 1975 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 25, tanks enter the Independence Palace, 30 April 1975", tank 390 smashes the palace gate while Bùi Quang Thận of tank 843 runs the revolutionary flag up at 11:30, ending the war (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 16, the course of the Hồ Chí Minh Campaign", the campaign opens at 17:00 on 26 April 1975; five wings close on Saigon, and at 10:45 on 30 April liberation troops enter the Independence Palace, the flag rising at 11:30 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
