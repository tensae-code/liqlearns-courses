# Vietnam liberation of saigon 1975 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 25, tanks enter the Independence Palace, 30 April 1975" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tank 390 smashes the palace gate while Bùi Quang Thận of tank 843 runs the revolutionary flag up at 11:30, ending the war.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 16, the course of the Hồ Chí Minh Campaign" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The campaign opens at 17:00 on 26 April 1975; five wings close on Saigon, and at 10:45 on 30 April liberation troops enter the Independence Palace, the flag rising at 11:30.
