## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 25, tanks enter the Independence Palace, 30 April 1975** — tank 390 smashes the palace gate while Bùi Quang Thận of tank 843 runs the revolutionary flag up at 11:30, ending the war
2. **§2 Bài 16, the course of the Hồ Chí Minh Campaign** — the campaign opens at 17:00 on 26 April 1975; five wings close on Saigon, and at 10:45 on 30 April liberation troops enter the Independence Palace, the flag rising at 11:30
3. **§3 Bài 16, story of the Quyết thắng squadron at Tân Sơn Nhất** — six pilots, among them Nguyễn Thành Trung, master captured A-37 jets in three days and on 28 April bomb Tân Sơn Nhất airfield with the enemy's own planes, paralysing it
4. **§4 Bài 16, story of the Saigon government's last moments** — as the flag rises over the palace, President Dương Văn Minh walks to Saigon radio and broadcasts unconditional surrender, dissolving his government (told from the national archive record)
5. **§5 Bài 17, Spring 1975: the general offensive** — the Paris Agreement (27 January 1973) commits America and the other signatories to respect Vietnam's independence, sovereignty, unity and territorial integrity; American troops leave by late March 1973 but advisers and aid remain and Saigon launches "land-grabbing" operations; after Phước Long falls (January 1975) the Politburo resolves to liberate the South in 1975 if the chance comes early or late; three campaigns follow — Tây Nguyên (4–24 March, opened at Buôn Ma Thuột on 10 March), Huế–Đà Nẵng (21–29 March) and the Hồ Chí Minh Campaign (26–30 April) under the motto "lightning speed, boldness, surprise, certain victory": at 10:45 on 30 April Liberation Army tanks enter the Independence Palace, President Dương Văn Minh declares unconditional surrender, and at 11:30 the liberation flag flies over the palace roof; Châu Đốc, the last southern province, is freed on 2 May — ending 21 years of war against America and 30 years of liberation war, and unifying the country
6. **§6 Bài 8 The end, 1973–1975: with American troops gone but 20,000 "advisers" and aid remaining, the South fights on three fronts — military, political, diplomatic; Phước Long (late 1974–early 1975) proves the army's strength and America's reluctance; the Spring 1975 General Offensive runs through Tây Nguyên (4–24-3), Huế–Đà Nẵng (26–29-3) and the Hồ Chí Minh Campaign, Võ Nguyên Giáp's 7-4 order — "faster, still faster; bolder, still bolder" — driving five wings into Saigon until the flag rises over Independence Palace at 11:30 on 30-4-1975** — a two-year plan collapses into fifty-five days as each campaign hands the next its momentum
