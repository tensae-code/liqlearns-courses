# The internationals and may day — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 11, the Internationals and the workers' parties", the First International (1864–1876), founded by Marx and Engels, carries scientific socialism into the movement and wins resolutions for strikes, unions and the eight-hour day; on 1 May 1886 some 400,000 Chicago workers strike for that eight-hour day and, though repressed, over 50,000 win it — from 1889 the day is International Labour Day; workers' parties follow (German Social Democrats 1875, French Workers' Party 1879, Russia's Emancipation of Labour group 1883), the Second International is founded in Paris on 14 July 1889, and after Engels' death, as revisionism creeps in, Lenin takes up Marx and Engels' cause
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of The internationals and may day as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
