# Vietnam yen the uprising — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 18, the Yên Thế peasant uprising, 1884–1913" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** In the hills of Yên Thế the peasants rise under Đề Nắm and then Đề Thám (Hoàng Hoa Thám, 1858–1913) not for a king but, in the book's words, to "keep the land, keep the village, protect a free life"; they beat off French sweeps at Cao Thượng (November 1890) and three times at Hố Chuối (December 1890) and rule the whole region; twice France buys truces (1894–95 and 1897–1908), then from 1909 besieges the base in earnest until, in February 1913, Đề Thám is murdered by French agents — the largest peasant uprising against invasion in Vietnam's modern history.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Vietnam yen the uprising as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
