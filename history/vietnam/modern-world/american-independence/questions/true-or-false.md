# American independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 1, the war of independence of the 13 British colonies", boston's people dump the taxed tea into the harbour in December 1773; the king vows to punish the "rebellion" and war breaks out in April 1775; the Declaration of July 1776 proclaims that all men are created equal, with rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness — the very line Hồ Chí Minh will quote in Vietnam's own Declaration of 1945, as the lesson's exercise points out; Saratoga (1777) turns the war, Yorktown (1781) ends it, and the Treaty of Paris (1783) recognises the United States
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of American independence as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
