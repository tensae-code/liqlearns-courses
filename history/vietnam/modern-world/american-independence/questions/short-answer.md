# American independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, the war of independence of the 13 British colonies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Boston's people dump the taxed tea into the harbour in December 1773; the king vows to punish the "rebellion" and war breaks out in April 1775; the Declaration of July 1776 proclaims that all men are created equal, with rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness — the very line Hồ Chí Minh will quote in Vietnam's own Declaration of 1945, as the lesson's exercise points out; Saratoga (1777) turns the war, Yorktown (1781) ends it, and the Treaty of Paris (1783) recognises the United States.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of American independence as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
