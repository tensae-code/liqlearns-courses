# American independence — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bài 1, the war of independence of the 13 British colonies — a)
**2.** The one thing this lesson teaches — b)

Meanings:
a) boston's people dump the taxed tea into the harbour in December 1773; the king vows to punish the "rebellion" and war breaks out in April 1775; the Declaration of July 1776 proclaims that all men are created equal, with rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness — the very line Hồ Chí Minh will quote in Vietnam's own Declaration of 1945, as the lesson's exercise points out; Saratoga (1777) turns the war, Yorktown (1781) ends it, and the Treaty of Paris (1783) recognises the United States
b) the story of American independence as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
