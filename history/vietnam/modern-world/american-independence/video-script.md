# Video script — American independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: American independence.

Boston's people dump the taxed tea into the harbour in December 1773; the king vows to punish the "rebellion" and war breaks out in April 1775; the Declaration of July 1776 proclaims that all men are created equal, with rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness — the very line Hồ Chí Minh will quote in Vietnam's own Declaration of 1945, as the lesson's exercise points out; Saratoga (1777) turns the war, Yorktown (1781) ends it, and the Treaty of Paris (1783) recognises the United States.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
