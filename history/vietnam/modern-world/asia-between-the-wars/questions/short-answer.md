# Asia between the wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 3, Asia's two roads, 1918–1945" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Across Asia two trends contend — bourgeois-democratic and proletarian; in India Gandhi's Congress leads the boycott of British goods; Mongolia becomes a People's Republic (1921–24); Turkey is reborn as a republic out of its 1919–22 liberation war.

**2.** State the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words.
**Answer:** The story of Asia between the wars as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
