# Asia between the wars — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 3, Asia's two roads, 1918–1945", across Asia two trends contend — bourgeois-democratic and proletarian; in India Gandhi's Congress leads the boycott of British goods; Mongolia becomes a People's Republic (1921–24); Turkey is reborn as a republic out of its 1919–22 liberation war

**2.** True or false: The one thing this lesson teaches is the story of Asia between the wars as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
