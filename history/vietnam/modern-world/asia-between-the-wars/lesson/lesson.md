## Bài 3, Asia's two roads, 1918–1945

Across Asia two trends contend — bourgeois-democratic and proletarian; in India Gandhi's Congress leads the boycott of British goods; Mongolia becomes a People's Republic (1921–24); Turkey is reborn as a republic out of its 1919–22 liberation war.
