# Lesson plan — Vietnam phan dinh phung huong khe uprising

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Vietnam phan dinh phung huong khe uprising
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The whole national story in one sweep: the first states, a thousand years of northern domination, the dynasties, colonisation, 1945, 1954, 1975 and Đổi mới — with the world's first civilisations beside it (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Vietnam phan dinh phung huong khe uprising as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The whole national story in one sweep: the first states, a thousand years of northern domination, the dynasties, colonisation, 1945, 1954, 1975 and Đổi mới — with the world's first civilisations beside it — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 13, story of Phan Đình Phùng and the Hương Khê uprising** — the Hà Tĩnh scholar-official builds his base in the Hương Khê forests, draws heroes from four provinces, and holds out for over ten years until he falls in battle at the end of 1895 — the Cần Vương movement's finest rising
2. **§2 Bài 18, the great Cần Vương risings: Hương Khê, Bãi Sậy, Ba Đình** — at Hương Khê in the Hà Tĩnh mountains Phan Đình Phùng (1847–1895) and his general Cao Thắng build the movement's model army — organised in ranks, and casting rifles copied from the French pattern — fighting in two phases (building to 1888, then unified fierce resistance) until the French mass to encircle Ngàn Trươi and Phùng dies in December 1895; at Bãi Sậy Nguyễn Thiện Thuật turns Hưng Yên's reed swamps into a guerrilla fortress (1883–1892); at Ba Đình Phạm Bành and Đinh Công Tráng raise Kinh, Mường and Thái together behind village ramparts until the great French assault of January 1887 forces a bloody breakout
