# Vietnam phan dinh phung huong khe uprising — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 13, story of Phan Đình Phùng and the Hương Khê uprising", the Hà Tĩnh scholar-official builds his base in the Hương Khê forests, draws heroes from four provinces, and holds out for over ten years until he falls in battle at the end of 1895 — the Cần Vương movement's finest rising (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 18, the great Cần Vương risings: Hương Khê, Bãi Sậy, Ba Đình", at Hương Khê in the Hà Tĩnh mountains Phan Đình Phùng (1847–1895) and his general Cao Thắng build the movement's model army — organised in ranks, and casting rifles copied from the French pattern — fighting in two phases (building to 1888, then unified fierce resistance) until the French mass to encircle Ngàn Trươi and Phùng dies in December 1895; at Bãi Sậy Nguyễn Thiện Thuật turns Hưng Yên's reed swamps into a guerrilla fortress (1883–1892); at Ba Đình Phạm Bành and Đinh Công Tráng raise Kinh, Mường and Thái together behind village ramparts until the great French assault of January 1887 forces a bloody breakout (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
