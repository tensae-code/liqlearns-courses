# Video script — America and western europe 1945 1991

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: America and western europe 1945 1991.

A two-party democracy pursues a global strategy against socialism — NATO founded at Washington in 1949, SEATO in 1954, friendly governments propped up abroad and progressive movements suppressed at home; when Nixon escalates in 1969, some 4 million Americans demonstrate in May 1970 alone and hundreds of schools close; détente and Nixon's China visit follow, then the 1989 joint declaration ending the Cold War — while America's economic share slides from 56% of world industrial output (1948) to 39.8% (1973) and its GDP share from 27.2% (1950) to 21.1% (1991).

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
