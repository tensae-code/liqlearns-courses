# America and western europe 1945 1991 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 11, the United States after 1945" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A two-party democracy pursues a global strategy against socialism — NATO founded at Washington in 1949, SEATO in 1954, friendly governments propped up abroad and progressive movements suppressed at home; when Nixon escalates in 1969, some 4 million Americans demonstrate in May 1970 alone and hundreds of schools close; détente and Nixon's China visit follow, then the 1989 joint declaration ending the Cold War — while America's economic share slides from 56% of world industrial output (1948) to 39.8% (1973) and its GDP share from 27.2% (1950) to 21.1% (1991).

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 11, Western Europe: recovery and integration" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The book's period table runs from Marshall aid of some $13 billion and recovery to the pre-war level by 1950, through the high-growth decades of the ECSC (1951) and EEC (1957) — with France, the book's typical case, seeking less dependence on America — to the low-growth years after 1973 that still end with the 1991 agreement on the European Union and a single currency, the euro.
