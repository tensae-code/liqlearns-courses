# The great depression 1929 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2, the Great Depression" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The October 1929 crash in the United States spreads worldwide and bottoms out in 1932; the book's source box counts the cost — 50 million unemployed and 17 million strikers between 1928 and 1933.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of The great depression 1929 as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
