**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 1, I.1, how history was traditionally presented", before print, the past is kept on bamboo slips, bark, paper, clay, stone, animal skin, wood and metal — the book shows Egyptian hieroglyphs on papyrus and Sumerian writing on clay; the classical forms are annals (events in time order), veritable records (the king's deeds and words), topical outlines — the Nguyễn court's Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục is pictured — and story-biographies of persons and events; beyond books, history is also carried by storytelling, festivals, song and dance, images, film and theatre (a documentary frame from "Hanoi in the 12 days and nights" and a chèo play are shown), and in the modern age by scientific works built from collected evidence (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
