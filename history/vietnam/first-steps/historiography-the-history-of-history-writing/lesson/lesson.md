# Historiography the history of history writing

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 2, primary and secondary sources, and why accounts differ

The same past yields different histories because each historian lights only the part their sources and viewpoint reach.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 1, I.1, how history was traditionally presented

Before print, the past is kept on bamboo slips, bark, paper, clay, stone, animal skin, wood and metal — the book shows Egyptian hieroglyphs on papyrus and Sumerian writing on clay; the classical forms are annals (events in time order), veritable records (the king's deeds and words), topical outlines — the Nguyễn court's Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục is pictured — and story-biographies of persons and events; beyond books, history is also carried by storytelling, festivals, song and dance, images, film and theatre (a documentary frame from "Hanoi in the 12 days and nights" and a chèo play are shown), and in the modern age by scientific works built from collected evidence.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chuyên đề 1, I.2, general history (thông sử) and Vietnam's own

A general history presents all fields of a place's, nation's or the world's past from origins to today, weighting the most influential persons, events and processes; Vietnam's first national general history is the Đại Việt sử ký, compiled under the Trần by Lê Văn Hưu and finished in 1272, and Ngô Sĩ Liên's Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư is basically complete by late 1479 and printed in 1697; the Nguyễn court adds the Đại Nam thực lục and the topical-outline history; foreign scholars take the form abroad — Lê Thành Khôi's Le Vietnam: histoire et civilisation (France, 1955), Joseph Buttinger's Vietnam: A Dragon Embattled (USA, 1967), Stanley Karnow's Vietnam: A History (USA, 1983) — and modern Vietnam answers with multi-volume general histories of its own.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 1 What art history is: a social science studying art events in time order, and Vietnam's own discipline milestones (Institute of Fine Arts and Crafts founded 1962 under painter Nguyễn Đỗ Cung; the Theory and History of Fine Arts department founded 1978)

A young science gives itself a birthday twice, in 1962 and 1978, and names its founders.

## Sub lesson 5 — Chuyên đề 3.I What a "danh nhân" is and why history keeps them: outstanding people whose contributions the community honours with worship, festivals and statues; UNESCO's "great personalities" lists since 1954; the 1484 doctoral stele's line that worthy talent is the nation's vital energy

A stone carved in 1484 argues that a country's strength is the sum of its remembered great people.
