# Fact or opinion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, application: meeting unverified information online" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The modern-revolutions lesson ends by turning its own medium into the exercise: in today's digital era much information is shared on the internet without being verified — if you are one of the people who receives such information, how should you handle it properly?; pupils must also collect materials from books, newspapers and the internet on one achievement of the modern industrial revolutions and draw a mind map (in their own design) to present it — the lesson's closing discipline is that a claim is not knowledge until its source has been checked.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Fact or opinion as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
