# Video script — Fact or opinion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Fact or opinion.

The modern-revolutions lesson ends by turning its own medium into the exercise: in today's digital era much information is shared on the internet without being verified — if you are one of the people who receives such information, how should you handle it properly?; pupils must also collect materials from books, newspapers and the internet on one achievement of the modern industrial revolutions and draw a mind map (in their own design) to present it — the lesson's closing discipline is that a claim is not knowledge until its source has been checked.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
