# What history is — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, History and life: what history is and why study it" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From ENIAC (1946) to the laptop, everything changes with time; Hồ Chí Minh's couplet "Our people must know our history" and his 1954 words at Đền Hùng open the subject.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chuyên đề 1, I.3–4, history by field; national history and world history" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The same past can be studied field by field — political, military, economic, cultural, social and intellectual history, and also the histories of science and technology, education, religion and belief, literature and diplomacy — each field giving a deep, concrete knowledge that helps explain the whole; a nation's history is the shared history of all communities on its territory from the first humans to today, while world history is not the sum of national histories but the interaction of many peoples, trends and forces across them.
