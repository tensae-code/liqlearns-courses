# What history is — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 1, History and life: what history is and why study it", from ENIAC (1946) to the laptop, everything changes with time; Hồ Chí Minh's couplet "Our people must know our history" and his 1954 words at Đền Hùng open the subject (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 1, I.3–4, history by field; national history and world history", the same past can be studied field by field — political, military, economic, cultural, social and intellectual history, and also the histories of science and technology, education, religion and belief, literature and diplomacy — each field giving a deep, concrete knowledge that helps explain the whole; a nation's history is the shared history of all communities on its territory from the first humans to today, while world history is not the sum of national histories but the interaction of many peoples, trends and forces across them (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
