# Heritage what it is and why it matters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 3, the traditional culture of your own locality (festivals, dishes, customs)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tết bánh chưng wrapping opens a lesson where children catalogue their own province's festivals, foods and customs as things handed down.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 6, festivals and folk arts of the Northern Midlands and Mountains (Gầu Tào, Lồng Tồng, Then singing, Xoè Thái dance, highland markets)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Highland peoples pray for a good year through festival, song and dance that UNESCO listed in 2019 and 2021.
