# From evidence to a written history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 1, artefacts and pictures as learning tools" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Cổ Loa bronze arrowheads are held up as surviving things from the past that let us describe people who left no writing.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 2, the kinds of source (material, written, oral)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Historians work like detectives: a Ngọc Lũ drum's patterns, Thăng Long citadel finds, the Văn Miếu stelae and Hồ Chí Minh's 1969 Testament are each interrogated for what they can tell.
