# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 1, artefacts and pictures as learning tools" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Cổ Loa bronze arrowheads are held up as surviving things from the past that let us describe people who left no writing.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 2, the kinds of source (material, written, oral)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Historians work like detectives: a Ngọc Lũ drum's patterns, Thăng Long citadel finds, the Văn Miếu stelae and Hồ Chí Minh's 1969 Testament are each interrogated for what they can tell.
