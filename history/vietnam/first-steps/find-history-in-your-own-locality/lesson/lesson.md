# Find history in your own locality

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Find history in your own locality" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 3, telling the story of a famous person of your own locality and planning a visit to a local historic site

Each child must find a local hero, retell their story, and plan a real visit to a local relic.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chuyên đề 1, practice: research the history of your own school

The topic's application exercise hands the method to the pupils: collect evidence and present one problem of Vietnamese history through a single field; then, as a class, investigate the formation and development of the very school they study in — choosing the form (general history or one field), fixing the object and scope, outlining the content, and reporting the group's findings to the class.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 2.2–3, connecting history with life, and collecting and processing sources

History is not learned only in lessons — museums, historical-cultural relics and artworks teach it too, and everything around us is a witness of history: every house, street, village, relic (communal house, temple, pagoda, monument), every school has its own story waiting to be asked — when was this relic built, who built it, what does it mean; when was your school founded, why does it bear its name, what traditions does it hold; to explore history scientifically one works from sources (sử liệu — every form of material carrying information about the human past, like the dragon-patterned roof tile from the Thăng Long Imperial Citadel, a 10,000-đồng banknote, and the Nhân Dân newspaper front page of 1–2 January 1968 pictured here): first collect them — list what is needed, search and gather, the more diverse and complete the better — then process them: classify, evaluate, authenticate and compare the sources to fix the accuracy, reliability and value of the information each carries; the applied task sends pupils to collect materials on the past of their own family, school or home locality and present what those materials reveal.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Find history in your own locality as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
