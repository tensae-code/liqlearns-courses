# Find history in your own locality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 3, telling the story of a famous person of your own locality and planning a visit to a local historic site" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Each child must find a local hero, retell their story, and plan a real visit to a local relic.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chuyên đề 1, practice: research the history of your own school" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The topic's application exercise hands the method to the pupils: collect evidence and present one problem of Vietnamese history through a single field; then, as a class, investigate the formation and development of the very school they study in — choosing the form (general history or one field), fixing the object and scope, outlining the content, and reporting the group's findings to the class.
