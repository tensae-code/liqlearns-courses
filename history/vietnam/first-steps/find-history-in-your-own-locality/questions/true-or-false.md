# Find history in your own locality — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 3, telling the story of a famous person of your own locality and planning a visit to a local historic site", each child must find a local hero, retell their story, and plan a real visit to a local relic (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Chuyên đề 1, practice: research the history of your own school", the topic's application exercise hands the method to the pupils: collect evidence and present one problem of Vietnamese history through a single field; then, as a class, investigate the formation and development of the very school they study in — choosing the form (general history or one field), fixing the object and scope, outlining the content, and reporting the group's findings to the class (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
