# Find history in your own locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Bài 3, telling the story of a famous person of your own locality and planning a visit to a local historic site"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) each child must find a local hero, retell their story, and plan a real visit to a local relic  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Chuyên đề 1, practice: research the history of your own school"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the topic's application exercise hands the method to the pupils: collect evidence and present one problem of Vietnamese history through a single field; then, as a class, investigate the formation and development of the very school they study in — choosing the form (general history or one field), fixing the object and scope, outlining the content, and reporting the group's findings to the class  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
