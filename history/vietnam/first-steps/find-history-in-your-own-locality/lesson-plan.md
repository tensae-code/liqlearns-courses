## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 3, telling the story of a famous person of your own locality and planning a visit to a local historic site** — each child must find a local hero, retell their story, and plan a real visit to a local relic
2. **§2 Chuyên đề 1, practice: research the history of your own school** — the topic's application exercise hands the method to the pupils: collect evidence and present one problem of Vietnamese history through a single field; then, as a class, investigate the formation and development of the very school they study in — choosing the form (general history or one field), fixing the object and scope, outlining the content, and reporting the group's findings to the class
3. **§3 Bài 2.2–3, connecting history with life, and collecting and processing sources** — history is not learned only in lessons — museums, historical-cultural relics and artworks teach it too, and everything around us is a witness of history: every house, street, village, relic (communal house, temple, pagoda, monument), every school has its own story waiting to be asked — when was this relic built, who built it, what does it mean; when was your school founded, why does it bear its name, what traditions does it hold; to explore history scientifically one works from sources (sử liệu — every form of material carrying information about the human past, like the dragon-patterned roof tile from the Thăng Long Imperial Citadel, a 10,000-đồng banknote, and the Nhân Dân newspaper front page of 1–2 January 1968 pictured here): first collect them — list what is needed, search and gather, the more diverse and complete the better — then process them: classify, evaluate, authenticate and compare the sources to fix the accuracy, reliability and value of the information each carries; the applied task sends pupils to collect materials on the past of their own family, school or home locality and present what those materials reveal

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
