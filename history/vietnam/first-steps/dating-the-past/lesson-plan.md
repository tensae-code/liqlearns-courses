# Lesson plan — Dating the past

**Course:** History — Vietnam · **Title:** First steps · **Lesson:** Dating the past
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First stories at home — Vietnam region by region: your own locality and its heroes, the Hùng Kings and Văn Lang, Thăng Long founded in 1010, Huế, Hội An and the South's first heroes (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Dating the past as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First stories at home — Vietnam region by region: your own locality and its heroes, the Hùng Kings and Văn Lang, Thăng Long founded in 1010, Huế, Hội An and the South's first heroes — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 1, maps, data tables and the timeline (trục thời gian)** — children learn to read a timeline by placing 1945 (August Revolution), 1954 (Điện Biên Phủ) and 1975 (Hồ Chí Minh Campaign) in order
2. **§2 Bài 3, time in history** — hourglass, water clock and sundial give way to lunar and solar calendars; BC/AD, decades, centuries and millennia let children place Âu Lạc's founding, c. 208 BC

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Nothing before this in the course — this is the first lesson of the ladder (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: the story of Dating the past as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
2. Retell the story of Dating the past — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own place and people.

## If the learner is stuck
- Retells names but not the turn → ask: "What changed in this story, and what came of it?" The turn is the proof.
- Go back to the one thing: the story of Dating the past as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
