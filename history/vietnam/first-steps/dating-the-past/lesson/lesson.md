# Dating the past

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Dating the past" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 1, maps, data tables and the timeline (trục thời gian)

Children learn to read a timeline by placing 1945 (August Revolution), 1954 (Điện Biên Phủ) and 1975 (Hồ Chí Minh Campaign) in order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 3, time in history

Hourglass, water clock and sundial give way to lunar and solar calendars; BC/AD, decades, centuries and millennia let children place Âu Lạc's founding, c. 208 BC.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Dating the past as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's story in your own words.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the story happens in real life. Note it.
