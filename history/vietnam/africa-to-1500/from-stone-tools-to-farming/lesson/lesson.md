# From stone tools to farming

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "From stone tools to farming" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 5, primitive society: from horde to clan commune

Cave-dwelling bands chip stone, master fire by friction, invent the bow and paint hunts on cave walls; with modern humans come clans that farm, make pottery, weave and bury their dead.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 5, primitive life in Vietnam (Hoà Bình and Bắc Sơn cultures)

Ground stone axes, Quỳnh Văn pottery and the faces carved on Đồng Nội cave walls show Vietnam's own Stone Age turning to farming.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 6, the discovery of metal and the breaking-up of primitive society

Copper is found in campfire ashes in the 4th millennium BC, bronze follows about 2000 BC and iron last; surplus becomes property, property becomes classes.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 6, Vietnam's bronze-age cultures (Phùng Nguyên, Đồng Đậu, Gò Mun, tiền Sa Huỳnh, Đồng Nai)

From slag at Phùng Nguyên (c. 2000 BC) to Gò Mun, where over half the finds are bronze, metal tools carry settlement down to the great river plains where the first states will stand.
