# From stone tools to farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5, primitive society: from horde to clan commune" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cave-dwelling bands chip stone, master fire by friction, invent the bow and paint hunts on cave walls; with modern humans come clans that farm, make pottery, weave and bury their dead.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 5, primitive life in Vietnam (Hoà Bình and Bắc Sơn cultures)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ground stone axes, Quỳnh Văn pottery and the faces carved on Đồng Nội cave walls show Vietnam's own Stone Age turning to farming.
