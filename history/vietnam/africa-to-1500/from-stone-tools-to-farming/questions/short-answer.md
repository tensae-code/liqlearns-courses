# From stone tools to farming — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 5, primitive society: from horde to clan commune" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cave-dwelling bands chip stone, master fire by friction, invent the bow and paint hunts on cave walls; with modern humans come clans that farm, make pottery, weave and bury their dead.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 5, primitive life in Vietnam (Hoà Bình and Bắc Sơn cultures)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ground stone axes, Quỳnh Văn pottery and the faces carved on Đồng Nội cave walls show Vietnam's own Stone Age turning to farming.
