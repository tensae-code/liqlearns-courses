# From stone tools to farming — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 5, primitive society: from horde to clan commune", cave-dwelling bands chip stone, master fire by friction, invent the bow and paint hunts on cave walls; with modern humans come clans that farm, make pottery, weave and bury their dead (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 5, primitive life in Vietnam (Hoà Bình and Bắc Sơn cultures)", ground stone axes, Quỳnh Văn pottery and the faces carved on Đồng Nội cave walls show Vietnam's own Stone Age turning to farming (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
