# Africa cradle of humankind — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 4, the origin of humankind" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Over millions of years ape-men become early humans (about 4 million years ago) and then modern humans, with East Africa named among the cradles.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The one thing this lesson teaches is __________.
**Answer:** The story of Africa cradle of humankind as Vietnam's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
