# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 24, Egypt and the gift of the Nile" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Herodotus's line "Egypt is the gift of the Nile" opens a civilisation of pyramids, sundials and papyrus on a 6,700 km river.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 24, story of Queen Cleopatra" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Queen at 17, speaker of many languages, builder of an army and fleet to face the Roman Empire, and remembered by Egyptians almost as a goddess.
