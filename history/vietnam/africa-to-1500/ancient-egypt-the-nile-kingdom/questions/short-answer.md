# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 24, Egypt and the gift of the Nile" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Herodotus's line "Egypt is the gift of the Nile" opens a civilisation of pyramids, sundials and papyrus on a 6,700 km river.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 24, story of Queen Cleopatra" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Queen at 17, speaker of many languages, builder of an army and fleet to face the Roman Empire, and remembered by Egyptians almost as a goddess.
