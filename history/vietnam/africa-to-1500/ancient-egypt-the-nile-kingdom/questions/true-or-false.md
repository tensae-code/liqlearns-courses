# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 24, Egypt and the gift of the Nile", herodotus's line "Egypt is the gift of the Nile" opens a civilisation of pyramids, sundials and papyrus on a 6,700 km river (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 24, story of Queen Cleopatra", queen at 17, speaker of many languages, builder of an army and fleet to face the Roman Empire, and remembered by Egyptians almost as a goddess (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
