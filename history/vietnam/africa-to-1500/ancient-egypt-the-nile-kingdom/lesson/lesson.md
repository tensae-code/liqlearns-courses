## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 24, Egypt and the gift of the Nile

Herodotus's line "Egypt is the gift of the Nile" opens a civilisation of pyramids, sundials and papyrus on a 6,700 km river.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 24, story of Queen Cleopatra

Queen at 17, speaker of many languages, builder of an army and fleet to face the Roman Empire, and remembered by Egyptians almost as a goddess.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 7, the gift of the rivers: Egypt and Mesopotamia form

Herodotus calls Egypt "the gift of the Nile"; farmers of 6,000 years ago invent the plough and irrigation, and about 3200 BC Menes unites Egypt's communes into one kingdom.
