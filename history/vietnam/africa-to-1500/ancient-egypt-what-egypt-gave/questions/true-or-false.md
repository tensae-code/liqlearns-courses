# Ancient egypt what egypt gave — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bài 24, story of building Khufu's pyramid", hundreds of thousands of people labour some 20 years to stack 2.3 million blocks, each 2.5 to 4 tons, into a 147-metre tomb fitted without mortar (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bài 7, Egypt's cultural achievements", picture-writing, papyrus (the root of the word "paper"), decimal counting, a 12-month calendar, embalming, and the pyramids and Sphinx (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
