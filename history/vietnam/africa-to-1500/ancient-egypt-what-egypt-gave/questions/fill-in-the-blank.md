# Ancient egypt what egypt gave — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 24, story of building Khufu's pyramid" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Hundreds of thousands of people labour some 20 years to stack 2.3 million blocks, each 2.5 to 4 tons, into a 147-metre tomb fitted without mortar.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bài 7, Egypt's cultural achievements" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Picture-writing, papyrus (the root of the word "paper"), decimal counting, a 12-month calendar, embalming, and the pyramids and Sphinx.
