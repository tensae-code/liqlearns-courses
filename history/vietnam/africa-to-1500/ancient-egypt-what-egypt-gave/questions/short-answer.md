# Ancient egypt what egypt gave — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bài 24, story of building Khufu's pyramid" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Hundreds of thousands of people labour some 20 years to stack 2.3 million blocks, each 2.5 to 4 tons, into a 147-metre tomb fitted without mortar.

**2.** Tell the story of "Bài 7, Egypt's cultural achievements" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Picture-writing, papyrus (the root of the word "paper"), decimal counting, a 12-month calendar, embalming, and the pyramids and Sphinx.
