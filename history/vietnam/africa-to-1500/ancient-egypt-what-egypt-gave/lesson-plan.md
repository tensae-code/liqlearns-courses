## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Bài 24, story of building Khufu's pyramid** — hundreds of thousands of people labour some 20 years to stack 2.3 million blocks, each 2.5 to 4 tons, into a 147-metre tomb fitted without mortar
2. **§2 Bài 7, Egypt's cultural achievements** — picture-writing, papyrus (the root of the word "paper"), decimal counting, a 12-month calendar, embalming, and the pyramids and Sphinx
3. **§3 Bài 4.2a, the achievements of ancient Egyptian civilisation** — the ancient Egyptians create one of the world's oldest writing systems, their pictorial hieroglyphs, more than 3,000 years BCE, and on that basis the Phoenicians build the Phoenician alphabet, held to be the origin of today's alphabets; thanks to writing the Egyptians leave precious records in history, literature, astronomy and mathematics — for centuries scholars could not read the script, until in 1822 the French linguist Jean-François Champollion (1790–1832) finds the way to read it by comparing the inscriptions on the Rosetta Stone (dated 196 BCE, found at Memphis, now in the British Museum); in science the Egyptians use the decimal system and addition and subtraction, compute the area of a triangle and the circumference of a circle with π taken as 3.16, observe the heavens and make a calendar on the Sun's cycle, and understand the body's organs relatively exactly, using anatomical knowledge in surgery and in the embalming art; their signature architecture is palaces, temples and pyramids — 138 pyramids have been found in Egypt, the most famous the Giza complex with the Sphinx, and their sculpture gives the bust of Nefertiti and the golden coffin lid of Pharaoh Tutankhamun
4. **§4 Bài 2 Egypt of the Nile: a polytheist farming people who believed the soul immortal and made burial rites complex, so their art aims at eternity and permanence** — because death was not the end, Egyptian art was built to last forever
