# Eritrea: Ottoman Coast and the Naibs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Turks held the coast for some 300 years. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Turks mastered the highlands as well as the coast. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the highlands stayed out of their grip; they never mastered the highlands
