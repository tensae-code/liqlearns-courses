# Eritrea: Ottoman Coast and the Naibs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From what year did the Turks hold the coast? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 1557  b) 1517  c) 1840  d) 1890
**Answer: a) 1557** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who judged disputes and settled grazing rights for the Turks? (Sub lesson 3)
a) the Naib of the Belau family  b) the Bahri Negassi alone  c) a Turkish governor  d) the Council of Elders
**Answer: a) the Naib of the Belau family** — That is what this lesson teaches.
