# Video script — Eritrea: Armed Struggle — the ELF

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Denied self-determination, Eritreans moved from the ELM's secret organising from 1958 and workers' strikes to the ELF, founded in Cairo in 1960 — and Hamid Idris Awate fired the first shot at Mount Adal on 1 September 1961; but a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands bred the defections of 1967.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The road to arms is walked in stages. The ELM organises in secret from 1958; workers strike; peaceful protest meets bans and arrests. In 1960 the ELF is founded in Cairo. The political struggle of 1958–1961 is the road from protest to arms — each peaceful door closed, until only the armed door remains.

The Grade 8 book tells the founding whole. Denied self-determination, Eritreans move from the ELM's coup hopes to the ELF in 1960 — and on 1 September 1961 Hamid Idris Awate fires the first shot at Mount Adal. But the front carries a flaw from birth: a remote Supreme Council giving orders from abroad and ethnic zonal commands divide the fighters, and by 1967 the defections begin.

Remember it this way: Denied self-determination, Eritreans moved from the ELM's secret organising from 1958 and workers' strikes to the ELF, founded in Cairo in 1960 — and Hamid Idris Awate fired the first shot at Mount Adal on 1 September 1961; but a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands bred the defections of 1967.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
