# Eritrea: Armed Struggle — the ELF — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The ELM organised in secret from 1958, before the ELF was founded. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The ELF's zonal commands and remote Supreme Council strengthened the front's unity. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands bred the defections of 1967
