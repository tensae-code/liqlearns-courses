# Eritrea: Armed Struggle — the ELF — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** On what date was the first shot of the armed struggle fired? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1 September 1961.

**2.** What peaceful steps came before the armed struggle? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The ELM's secret organising from 1958 and workers' strikes — the political struggle of 1958–1961.
