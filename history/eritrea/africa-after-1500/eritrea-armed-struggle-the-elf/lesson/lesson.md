# Eritrea: Armed Struggle — the ELF

## Do it first

Think of a story about the past that you already know — from home, from school, from your street. Say out loud who was in it, where it happened, and what changed by its end. Keep that shape in mind: this lesson's story is told the same way — people, place, the turn, and what came of it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 10.4, the political struggle 1958–1961

The road to arms is walked in stages. The ELM organises in secret from 1958; workers strike; peaceful protest meets bans and arrests. In 1960 the ELF is founded in Cairo. The political struggle of 1958–1961 is the road from protest to arms — each peaceful door closed, until only the armed door remains.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: 1 September 1961 is the date Eritrea counts from: one man, one shot at Mount Adal, and a thirty-year war begins.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 7.1, The Eritrean Liberation Front

The Grade 8 book tells the founding whole. Denied self-determination, Eritreans move from the ELM's coup hopes to the ELF in 1960 — and on 1 September 1961 Hamid Idris Awate fires the first shot at Mount Adal. But the front carries a flaw from birth: a remote Supreme Council giving orders from abroad and ethnic zonal commands divide the fighters, and by 1967 the defections begin.
