# Video script — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Italy ruled the highlands through salaried, closely controlled appointed chiefs (mislene) and the lowlands through tribal leaders kept in place but stripped of power — direct control wearing a traditional face.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Italy's rule wears two faces. In the highlands it rules through appointed chiefs — mislene — salaried and closely controlled, direct rule in local dress. In the lowlands it keeps tribal leaders in place but strips them of power. Either way the decisions are Italy's: direct control wearing a traditional face, and a lesson in how to read any colonial government — follow the salary and the power of dismissal.

Remember it this way: Italy ruled the highlands through salaried, closely controlled appointed chiefs (mislene) and the lowlands through tribal leaders kept in place but stripped of power — direct control wearing a traditional face.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
