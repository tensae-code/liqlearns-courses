# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What were Italy's appointed highland chiefs called?
a) mislene  b) nabtab  c) Naibs  d) Diglal
**Answer: a) mislene** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did Italy rule the lowlands?
a) through tribal leaders kept in place but stripped of power  b) through elected councils  c) through Italian settlers only  d) it did not rule the lowlands
**Answer: a) through tribal leaders kept in place but stripped of power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
