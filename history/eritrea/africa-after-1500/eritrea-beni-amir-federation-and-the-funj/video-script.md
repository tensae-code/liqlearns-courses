# Video script — Eritrea: Beni Amir Federation and the Funj

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Funj agent Ali Abu Gassim married into the Belaw royal line and was killed by them; his posthumous son returned with an army, defeated the Belaw and united some 21 tribes under the Diglal, in a society of nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves in three castes.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

A marriage makes a federation, and a murder unmakes a family. The Funj agent Ali Abu Gassim marries into the Belaw royal line — and is killed by them. His son, born after his death, returns with an army, defeats the Belaw, and unites some 21 tribes under the Diglal. The federation he builds is ordered in three castes: nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves — unity and hierarchy founded in the same act.

Remember it this way: The Funj agent Ali Abu Gassim married into the Belaw royal line and was killed by them; his posthumous son returned with an army, defeated the Belaw and united some 21 tribes under the Diglal, in a society of nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves in three castes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
