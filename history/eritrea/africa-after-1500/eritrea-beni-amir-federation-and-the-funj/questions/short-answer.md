# Eritrea: Beni Amir Federation and the Funj — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How was the federation founded, in one telling?
**Answer:** Ali Abu Gassim married into the Belaw line and was killed by them; his posthumous son returned with an army, defeated the Belaw and united some 21 tribes under the Diglal.

**2.** Name the three castes of the federation.
**Answer:** Nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves.
