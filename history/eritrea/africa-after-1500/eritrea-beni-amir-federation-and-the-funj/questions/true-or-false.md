# Eritrea: Beni Amir Federation and the Funj — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ali Abu Gassim was killed by the Belaw family he married into.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Beni Amir federation had no classes or castes.
**Answer: False** — it was ordered in three castes: nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves
