# Eritrea: Beni Amir Federation and the Funj — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many tribes did the Diglal unite?
a) some 21 tribes  b) five tribes  c) nine tribes  d) three tribes
**Answer: a) some 21 tribes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who was the Funj agent who married into the Belaw royal line?
a) Ali Abu Gassim  b) the Naib  c) Yeshaq  d) Tedla Bairu
**Answer: a) Ali Abu Gassim** — That is what this lesson teaches.
