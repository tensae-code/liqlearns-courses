# Eritrea: Political Parties and the UN Question — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name Eritrea's three first party leaders. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Woldeab Woldemariam, Ibrahim Sultan and Ras Tesemma Asberom.

**2.** Which parties demanded independence? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Muslim League and the Liberal Progressive Party.
