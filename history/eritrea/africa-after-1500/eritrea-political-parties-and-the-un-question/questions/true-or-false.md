# Eritrea: Political Parties and the UN Question — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Four Powers agreed quickly on Eritrea's future. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the Four Powers were deadlocked and handed Eritrea's future to the United Nations

**2.** Ethiopia built the Unionist Party to argue for union with Ethiopia. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True**
