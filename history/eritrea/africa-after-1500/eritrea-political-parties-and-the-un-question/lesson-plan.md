# Lesson plan — Eritrea: Political Parties and the UN Question

**Course:** History — Eritrea · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea: Political Parties and the UN Question
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Colony, Federation and Liberation, 1890–1993 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After 1941 Eritrea's first political leaders — Woldeab Woldemariam, Ibrahim Sultan and Ras Tesemma Asberom — organised parties demanding the country's own future: Mahber Fiqri Hager seeded them, the Muslim League and Liberal Progressive Party demanded independence, Ethiopia built the Unionist Party, and the deadlocked Four Powers handed Eritrea's future to the United Nations.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Colony, Federation and Liberation, 1890–1993 (recommended, never forced).
