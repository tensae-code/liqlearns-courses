# Video script — Eritrea: Turkish Expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ozdemir Pasha took Massawa in 1557 and pushed for the highlands; Bahri Negassi Yeshaq first barred him, then allied with the Turks against Abyssinia's King Minas, and was killed fighting King Sertse-Dengel in 1578 — after which Turkish rule shrank back to the coast and the Naibs.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Ozdemir Pasha takes Massawa in 1557 and pushes for the highlands — and meets the Bahri Negassi Yeshaq. Yeshaq first bars the Turkish advance; then, calculating that Abyssinia is the greater threat, he allies with the Turks against Abyssinia's King Minas. In 1578 he is killed fighting Minas's successor, King Sertse-Dengel. After his death, Turkish rule shrinks back to the coast and the Naibs: the highlands were defended, at the price of their defender.

Remember it this way: Ozdemir Pasha took Massawa in 1557 and pushed for the highlands; Bahri Negassi Yeshaq first barred him, then allied with the Turks against Abyssinia's King Minas, and was killed fighting King Sertse-Dengel in 1578 — after which Turkish rule shrank back to the coast and the Naibs.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
