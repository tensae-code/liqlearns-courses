# Eritrea: Turkish Expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yeshaq first barred the Turkish advance, then allied with the Turks against Abyssinia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** After Yeshaq's death the Turks conquered the highlands.
**Answer: False** — Turkish rule shrank back to the coast and the Naibs
