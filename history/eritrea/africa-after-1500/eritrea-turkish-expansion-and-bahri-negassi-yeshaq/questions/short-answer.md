# Eritrea: Turkish Expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Against which Abyssinian king did Yeshaq ally with the Turks?
**Answer:** King Minas.

**2.** Who killed Yeshaq, in the fighting of 1578?
**Answer:** He was killed fighting King Sertse-Dengel.
