# Lesson plan — Eritrea: Italian Colonial Administration

**Course:** History — Eritrea · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea: Italian Colonial Administration
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Colony, Federation and Liberation, 1890–1993 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy ran Eritrea for the coloniser: separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — and, from 1937, Racial Laws barring Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Colony, Federation and Liberation, 1890–1993 (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 10.3, life under Italy** — Life under Italy is a system, not a series of accidents: separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters on the walls, forced labour on the roads and on the great Massawa–Asmara cableway.
2. **Unit 6.1, Italian colonial administration and the Racial Laws** — The administration's own paperwork shows the design.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
