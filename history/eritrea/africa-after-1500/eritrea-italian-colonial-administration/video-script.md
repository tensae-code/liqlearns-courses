# Video script — Eritrea: Italian Colonial Administration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Italy ran Eritrea for the coloniser: separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — and, from 1937, Racial Laws barring Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Life under Italy is a system, not a series of accidents: separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters on the walls, forced labour on the roads and on the great Massawa–Asmara cableway. Every part of the colony is arranged so that wealth and power flow to the coloniser.

The administration's own paperwork shows the design. Commissariati and municipalities run a colony whose aims shift — settler farms, then markets, then a springboard for the invasion of Ethiopia. And in 1937 the Racial Laws make the design explicit: Eritreans are barred from Italian streets and bars, and from schooling beyond grade 4.

Remember it this way: Italy ran Eritrea for the coloniser: separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — and, from 1937, Racial Laws barring Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
