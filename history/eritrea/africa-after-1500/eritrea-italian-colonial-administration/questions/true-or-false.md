# Eritrea: Italian Colonial Administration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Eritrean and Italian children attended the same schools under Italy. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — there were separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children

**2.** The Racial Laws barred Eritreans from Italian streets and bars. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
