# Eritrea: Italian Colonial Administration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two ways the colony was run for the coloniser. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children; Fascist propaganda posters; forced labour on roads and the Massawa–Asmara cableway.

**2.** How did the colony's aims shift, as the administration tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From settler farms, to markets, to a springboard against Ethiopia.
