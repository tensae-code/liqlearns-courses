# Eritrea: Portuguese-Turkish Rivalry and the Gragn Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What were the Portuguese and Turks fighting over?

**Answer:** They tore through the highlands and weakened the order the Bahri Negestat held together.
