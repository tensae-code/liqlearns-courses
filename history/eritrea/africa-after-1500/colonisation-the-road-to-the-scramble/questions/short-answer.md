# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the Suez Canal change?
**Answer:** It made the Red Sea the short road between Europe and Asia, so every empire wanted a shore on it.

**2.** Which two powers were already on the Red Sea when Italy came?
**Answer:** Britain (Aden and Egypt) and France (Djibouti).
