# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France took Djibouti and Britain sat in Aden and Egypt.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Italy chose Eritrea entirely on its own, with no other power's encouragement.
**Answer: False** — Britain encouraged Italy forward to keep France out
