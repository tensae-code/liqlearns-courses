# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What opened in 1869 to make the Red Sea a prize?
a) the Suez Canal  b) the Massawa–Asmara cableway  c) the port of Assab  d) the railway to Asmara
**Answer: a) the Suez Canal** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why did Britain encourage Italy forward?
a) to keep France out  b) to help Eritrea  c) to fight Egypt  d) to open trade schools
**Answer: a) to keep France out** — That is what this lesson teaches.
