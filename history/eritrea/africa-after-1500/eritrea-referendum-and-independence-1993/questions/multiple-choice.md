# Eritrea: Referendum and Independence 1993 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What share of voters chose independence in April 1993? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 99.8 percent  b) 50.1 percent  c) 75 percent  d) 90 percent
**Answer: a) 99.8 percent** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which bodies observed the referendum? (Sub lesson 3)
a) the UN, the OAU and the Arab League  b) only Ethiopia  c) the Four Powers  d) Italy and Britain
**Answer: a) the UN, the OAU and the Arab League** — That is what this lesson teaches.
