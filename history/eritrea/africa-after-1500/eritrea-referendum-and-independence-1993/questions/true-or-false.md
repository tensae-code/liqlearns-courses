# Eritrea: Referendum and Independence 1993 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The referendum was held under international observation. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Independence was declared on the same day as liberation, 24 May 1991. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — liberation came on 24 May 1991; independence was formally declared on 24 May 1993, after the referendum
