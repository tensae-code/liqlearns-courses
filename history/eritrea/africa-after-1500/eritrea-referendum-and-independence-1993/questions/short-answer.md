# Eritrea: Referendum and Independence 1993 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** On which days was the referendum held? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** 23–25 April 1993.

**2.** Why does independence day fall on 24 May? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Independence was declared on 24 May 1993, exactly two years after liberation day, 24 May 1991.
