# Video script — Eritrea: British Military Administration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Britain defeated Italy in 1941 — at Keren in March — and ruled Eritrea as an occupied territory for eleven years: politics banned, Italian property protected, factories stripped, and plans made to join Eritrea's west to Sudan.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

In 1941 Britain defeats Italy, and Eritrea changes masters without gaining freedom. For eleven years Britain rules it as an occupied territory, while Eritreans — no longer under Italian law — begin to organise for their own future. The party years grow in the space between one empire's fall and the world's decision.

The Grade 8 book is blunter about the occupation. Britain wins at Keren in March 1941 and rules through a Military Administration that bans politics, protects Italian property, and strips factories out of the country — working, the book says, to keep Eritrea weak and divided, so that its west can be joined to Sudan. Eritrea's future is being bargained away while Eritreans are forbidden to bargain.

Remember it this way: Britain defeated Italy in 1941 — at Keren in March — and ruled Eritrea as an occupied territory for eleven years: politics banned, Italian property protected, factories stripped, and plans made to join Eritrea's west to Sudan.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
