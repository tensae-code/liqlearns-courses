# Lesson plan — Eritrea: British Military Administration

**Course:** History — Eritrea · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea: British Military Administration
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Colony, Federation and Liberation, 1890–1993 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain defeated Italy in 1941 — at Keren in March — and ruled Eritrea as an occupied territory for eleven years: politics banned, Italian property protected, factories stripped, and plans made to join Eritrea's west to Sudan.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Colony, Federation and Liberation, 1890–1993 (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the story in the names and dates → go back to the one turn: who wanted what, what changed, and what came of it. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: Britain defeated Italy in 1941 — at Keren in March — and ruled Eritrea as an occupied territory for eleven years: politics banned, Italian property protected, factories stripped, and plans made to join Eritrea's west to Sudan. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
