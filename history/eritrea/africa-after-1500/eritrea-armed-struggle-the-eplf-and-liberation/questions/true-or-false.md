# Eritrea: Armed Struggle — the EPLF and Liberation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The armed struggle lasted thirty years, from 1961 to liberation in 1991. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The strategic retreat to Sahil was the EPLF's final defeat. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the front endured the strategic retreat and fought through to liberation
