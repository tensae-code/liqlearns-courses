# Eritrea: Egyptian Expansion and Resistance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Egypt took Kassala in 1840 and Massawa in 1846. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Egypt's highland invasion succeeded in 1875–76. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the highland drive was broken at Gundet and Gura in 1875–76
