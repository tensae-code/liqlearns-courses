# Video script — Eritrea: Egyptian Expansion and Resistance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Egypt pushed into Eritrea — Kassala 1840, Massawa 1846, Keren 1874 — until its highland invasion was broken at Gundet and Gura in 1875–76, where Woldemichael Solomon followed the retreat and captured 700 rifles.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Egypt pushes south and east from the Sudan: Kassala in 1840, Massawa in 1846, Keren in 1874 — the Egyptian frontier climbing toward the highlands town by town. Until, in 1875–76, its highland invasion breaks. The advance that took thirty years to build is stopped in two campaigning seasons.

The Grade 8 book tells the breaking in full. Muhammad Ali's Egypt swallows the Sudan, Kassala, the Barka and Keren under Munzinger — until Khedive Ismail's highland drive is broken at Gundet and Gura in 1875–76. And Woldemichael Solomon follows the retreating Egyptians and captures 700 rifles: the invasion leaves Eritrea armed with its enemy's own weapons.

Remember it this way: Egypt pushed into Eritrea — Kassala 1840, Massawa 1846, Keren 1874 — until its highland invasion was broken at Gundet and Gura in 1875–76, where Woldemichael Solomon followed the retreat and captured 700 rifles.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
