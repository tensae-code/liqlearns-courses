# Eritrea: Abyssinian Expansion and Highland Resistance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Saho beat Ras Alula's men at Rub-Rubia. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Abyssinian rulers only ever raided once and never taxed the highlands. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — they raided and taxed the highlands through the 19th century, and the exactions are remembered with the famine years of 1888–1892
