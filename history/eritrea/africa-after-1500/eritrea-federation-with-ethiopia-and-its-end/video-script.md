# Video script — Eritrea: Federation with Ethiopia and Its End

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950 federated Eritrea with Ethiopia in 1952, with Eritrea's own government under Chief Executive Tedla Bairu and its own taxes — but Ethiopia dismantled the federation piece by piece and annexed Eritrea outright on 14 November 1962.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

In 1952 Eritrea is federated with Ethiopia: one empire, two governments, with Eritrea keeping its own government, its own flag and its own assembly. Then Ethiopia dismantles the federation piece by piece — the flag, the languages, the assembly — until nothing is left of the bargain but the union itself.

The Grade 5 book gives the legal spine. UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950 federates Eritrea with Ethiopia; Chief Executive Tedla Bairu heads Eritrea's own government. But Ethiopia strips the federation away step by step and annexes Eritrea outright on 14 November 1962. A country voted into a federation is absorbed by decree — and the armed struggle is already a year old.

The Grade 8 book stresses what the federation genuinely gave: Eritrea's own government and its own taxes under the UN resolution. That is what makes the dismantling a theft and not a tidying-up — Ethiopia weakens Eritrea's economy and strips the federation away piece by piece, and Eritreans watch a written promise unwritten.

Remember it this way: UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950 federated Eritrea with Ethiopia in 1952, with Eritrea's own government under Chief Executive Tedla Bairu and its own taxes — but Ethiopia dismantled the federation piece by piece and annexed Eritrea outright on 14 November 1962.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
