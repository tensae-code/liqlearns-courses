# Eritrea: Federation with Ethiopia and Its End — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which UN resolution federated Eritrea with Ethiopia? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950  b) Resolution 390A(V) of 1962  c) the Treaty of Wuchale  d) the Suez agreement
**Answer: a) Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In which year did the federation begin? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 1952  b) 1950  c) 1962  d) 1941
**Answer: a) 1952** — That is what this lesson teaches.
