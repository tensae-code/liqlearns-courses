# Eritrea: Federation with Ethiopia and Its End — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Chief Executive Tedla Bairu headed Eritrea's own government under the federation. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ethiopia strengthened the federation year by year. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — Ethiopia weakened Eritrea's economy and stripped the federation away piece by piece
