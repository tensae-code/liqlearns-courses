# Eritrea: Federation with Ethiopia and Its End — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** On what date did Ethiopia annex Eritrea outright? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 14 November 1962.

**2.** How did the federation end? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ethiopia dismantled it piece by piece — flag, languages, assembly — until nothing was left, then annexed Eritrea outright.
