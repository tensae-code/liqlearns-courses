# Video script — Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Italian rule built roads, railways and factories for military and settler needs, while education was deliberately capped to produce obedient, low-skilled subjects — industrialisation that served the coloniser, not the colony.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

What did Italian rule leave behind? Roads, railways and factories — real things, built for military and settler needs. Industrialisation serves the coloniser. And on the other side of the ledger, education is deliberately capped, designed to produce obedient, low-skilled subjects. The colony is developed and held down by the same hands, on purpose.

Remember it this way: Italian rule built roads, railways and factories for military and settler needs, while education was deliberately capped to produce obedient, low-skilled subjects — industrialisation that served the coloniser, not the colony.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
