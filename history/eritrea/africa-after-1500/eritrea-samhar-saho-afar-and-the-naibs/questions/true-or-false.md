# Eritrea: Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Saho and Afar had no master-serf relations.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Samhar was always ruled by the Naibs.
**Answer: False** — the Saho and Afar governed themselves through elected councils of elders until Samhar passed under the Naib family
