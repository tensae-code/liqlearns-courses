# Eritrea: Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What made the eastern lowlands' government different?
**Answer:** There were no master-serf relations; the tribes governed themselves through elected councils of elders.

**2.** Which two peoples does this lesson cover?
**Answer:** The Saho and the Afar (of Samhar and Dankalia).
