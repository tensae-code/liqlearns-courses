# Eritrea: Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did the Saho and Afar govern themselves?
a) through elected councils of elders  b) through hereditary kings  c) through the Naibs from the start  d) through slave-owners
**Answer: a) through elected councils of elders** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which family of Belau origin later took Samhar under its rule?
a) the Naib family  b) the Diglal's family  c) the Bahri Negassi's family  d) the Funj
**Answer: a) the Naib family** — That is what this lesson teaches.
