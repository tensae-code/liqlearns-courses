# Video script — Eritrea: Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Saho and Afar of the eastern lowlands, with no master-serf relations, governed themselves through elected councils of elders — until Samhar passed under the Naib family of Belau origin.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The eastern lowlands govern themselves differently. The Saho and Afar tribes of Samhar and Dankalia have no master-serf relations: they rule themselves through elected councils of elders, chosen, not born. Only later does Samhar pass under the Naib family of Belau origin — the coast's tax-men reaching into a land that had governed itself by election.

Remember it this way: The Saho and Afar of the eastern lowlands, with no master-serf relations, governed themselves through elected councils of elders — until Samhar passed under the Naib family of Belau origin.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
