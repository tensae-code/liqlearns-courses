# Video script — How Slave Society Worked

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When farming produced a surplus and private property appeared, people split into classes for the first time: debtors and prisoners of war became slaves with no rights, owned by slave-owners beside free farmers and craftsmen.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The ladder of societies climbs to its hardest step. Once farming yields a surplus and private property appears, people split into classes for the first time. Debtors who cannot pay and prisoners taken in war become slaves — people with no rights, owned outright by slave-owners — while free farmers and craftsmen work beside them. Inequality is not natural in this telling; it is made, by surplus and by force.

Remember it this way: When farming produced a surplus and private property appeared, people split into classes for the first time: debtors and prisoners of war became slaves with no rights, owned by slave-owners beside free farmers and craftsmen.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
