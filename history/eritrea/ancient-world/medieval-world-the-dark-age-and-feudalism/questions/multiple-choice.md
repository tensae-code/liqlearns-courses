# Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does a serf owe the lord?
a) labour and taxes  b) rent in gold  c) military service only  d) nothing
**Answer: a) labour and taxes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** A serf is sold with what?
a) the land itself  b) his tools  c) his house only  d) nothing — serfs are free
**Answer: a) the land itself** — That is what this lesson teaches.
