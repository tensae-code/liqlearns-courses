# Video script — Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Feudalism appears when unproductive slaves are given plots and become serfs — bound to a lord's estate, owing labour and taxes, and sold with the land itself.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Feudalism grows out of slavery's failure. Slaves with no stake in their work are unproductive, so they are given plots of land — and become serfs: bound to a lord's estate, owing labour and taxes, and sold with the land itself when the estate changes hands. It is one road by which feudalism appears: not freedom, but a new kind of bondage, tied to soil instead of a master.

Remember it this way: Feudalism appears when unproductive slaves are given plots and become serfs — bound to a lord's estate, owing labour and taxes, and sold with the land itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
