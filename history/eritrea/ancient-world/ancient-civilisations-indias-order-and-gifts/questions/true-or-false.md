# Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the caste order, your level is inherited — fixed by birth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Merchants and farmers stand at the top of the caste order.
**Answer: False** — priests stand first, then warriors, then merchants and farmers, then servants and workers
