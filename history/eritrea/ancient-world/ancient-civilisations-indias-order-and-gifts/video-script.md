# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In ancient India society hardened into inherited levels — priests, warriors, merchants and farmers, servants and workers — the caste order, and Sparta's slave society was built the same inherited way.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Ancient India shows what happens when classes harden into birth. Society sets into inherited levels — priests, warriors, merchants and farmers, servants and workers — the caste order, where the place you are born in is the place you die in. And Sparta's slave society, the book adds, is built the same inherited way: your station assigned before you can speak.

Remember it this way: In ancient India society hardened into inherited levels — priests, warriors, merchants and farmers, servants and workers — the caste order, and Sparta's slave society was built the same inherited way.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
