# Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts

## Do it first

Think of a story about the past that you already know — from home, from school, from your street. Say out loud who was in it, where it happened, and what changed by its end. Keep that shape in mind: this lesson's story is told the same way — people, place, the turn, and what came of it.

Ancient India shows what happens when classes harden into birth. Society sets into inherited levels — priests, warriors, merchants and farmers, servants and workers — the caste order, where the place you are born in is the place you die in. And Sparta's slave society, the book adds, is built the same inherited way: your station assigned before you can speak.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: An inherited order means your grandfather's place becomes your place: ask who that helps, and who it traps.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In ancient India society hardened into inherited levels — priests, warriors, merchants and farmers, servants and workers — the caste order, and Sparta's slave society was built the same inherited way. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
