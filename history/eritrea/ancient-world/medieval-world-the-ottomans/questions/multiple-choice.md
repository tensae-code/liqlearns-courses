# Medieval World: The Ottomans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In which year did the Ottomans land on the Eritrean coast?
a) 1517  b) 1557  c) 1869  d) 615
**Answer: a) 1517** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two ports did the Ottomans hold by 1557?
a) Massawa and Hirghigo  b) Assab and Djibouti  c) Adulis and Kohaito  d) Keren and Debarwa
**Answer: a) Massawa and Hirghigo** — That is what this lesson teaches.
