# Video script — Medieval World: The Ottomans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Ottoman Empire, having taken Egypt, Sudan and Arabia, landed on the Eritrean coast in 1517 and by 1557 held Massawa and Hirghigo, taxing the port trade.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The empire arrives from the north. The Ottoman Empire, having taken Egypt, Sudan and Arabia, lands on the Eritrean coast in 1517 — and by 1557 holds Massawa and Hirghigo, the ports where the highlands' goods meet the sea. The Turks tax the port trade and hold the shore; the mountains behind it are another matter.

Remember it this way: The Ottoman Empire, having taken Egypt, Sudan and Arabia, landed on the Eritrean coast in 1517 and by 1557 held Massawa and Hirghigo, taxing the port trade.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
