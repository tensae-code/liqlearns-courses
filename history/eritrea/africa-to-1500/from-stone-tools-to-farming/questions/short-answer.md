# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did the first Eritreans live before settled peoples arrived? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** They shaped stone tools, painted animals on cave walls, and lived by hunting and gathering.

**2.** Name the steps of the transformation in order. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Fire, the bow and arrow, metal tools, domesticated animals — then farming and surplus.
