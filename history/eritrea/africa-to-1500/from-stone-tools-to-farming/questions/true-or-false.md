# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The earliest Eritreans lived by hunting and gathering. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Farming came before stone tools in this story. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — stone tools come first; farming and surplus come at the end of the ladder, after fire, the bow and arrow, metal tools and domesticated animals
