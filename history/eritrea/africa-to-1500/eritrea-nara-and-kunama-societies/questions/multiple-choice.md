# Eritrea: Nara and Kunama Societies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Through which line do the Kunama know a person?
a) the mother line  b) the father line  c) the chief's line  d) the priest's line
**Answer: a) the mother line** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In which lowlands do the Nara and Kunama live?
a) the Gash-Setit lowlands  b) the Samhar coast  c) Dankalia  d) the Barka Valley
**Answer: a) the Gash-Setit lowlands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
