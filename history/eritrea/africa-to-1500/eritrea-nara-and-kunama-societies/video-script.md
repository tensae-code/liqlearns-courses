# Video script — Eritrea: Nara and Kunama Societies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Nara and the Kunama of the Gash-Setit lowlands ordered life by kinship and ancestor worship — the Kunama know a person through the mother line and believe in the creator Anna.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

In the Gash-Setit lowlands of the south-west, the Nara and the Kunama order life without kings. Kinship is the constitution and ancestor worship the faith: the Kunama know a person through the mother line — your mother's people are your people — and believe in the creator Anna. A society can be held together by family and reverence alone.

Remember it this way: The Nara and the Kunama of the Gash-Setit lowlands ordered life by kinship and ancestor worship — the Kunama know a person through the mother line and believe in the creator Anna.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
