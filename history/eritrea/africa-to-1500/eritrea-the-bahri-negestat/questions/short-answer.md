# Eritrea: The Bahri Negestat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does 'Bahri Negassi' mean, and what land did he rule? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 'Lord of the Sea'; he ruled Medri Bahri, the highlands, from Debarwa.

**2.** Why did the Bahri Negestat decline? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Districts grew out of groups of villages and fighting among the local chiefs hollowed out the Bahri Negassi's authority from inside.
