# Eritrea: The Bahri Negestat — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Bahri Negassi ruled with a Council of Elders' approval. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** A foreign army destroyed the Bahri Negestat in battle. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — fighting among the local chiefs hollowed out the Bahri Negassi's authority from inside
