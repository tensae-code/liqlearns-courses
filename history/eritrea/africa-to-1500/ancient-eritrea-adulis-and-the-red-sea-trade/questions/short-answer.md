# Ancient Eritrea: Adulis and the Red Sea Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What do the ruins of Adulis, Kohaito and Metera prove? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** That a trading civilisation stood on the Red Sea coast in ancient times — stelae, temples and dams show building and trade.

**2.** Name two goods shipped out of Eritrea's ports and two brought in. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Out: any two of leopard skins, ivory, rhino horn, cowries, tortoise shell. In: goods from Arabia and India (cloth, glass and wine in the Periplus account).
