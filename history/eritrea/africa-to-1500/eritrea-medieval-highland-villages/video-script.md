# Video script — Eritrea: Medieval Highland Villages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Medieval highland villages around Debarwa fed themselves — wheat, barley, teff and sorghum — while churches and monasteries such as Debre Bizen held land worked by tenants.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The medieval highland village fed itself and asked little of the world. Around Debarwa, self-sufficient villages grow wheat, barley, teff and sorghum, while churches and monasteries such as Debre Bizen hold land worked by tenants. Grain, faith and tenancy: that triangle organised highland life for centuries.

Remember it this way: Medieval highland villages around Debarwa fed themselves — wheat, barley, teff and sorghum — while churches and monasteries such as Debre Bizen held land worked by tenants.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
