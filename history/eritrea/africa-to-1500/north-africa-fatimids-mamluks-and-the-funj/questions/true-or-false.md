# North Africa: Fatimids, Mamluks and the Funj — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Funj Kingdom expanded in the 16th century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Funj Kingdom ruled Eritrea's highlands.
**Answer: False** — it pulled the Beja tribes of the western lowlands, between the Barka and the Sahil, under its control
