# North Africa: Fatimids, Mamluks and the Funj — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From which city did the Funj Kingdom expand?
a) Sennar  b) Debarwa  c) Massawa  d) Khartoum
**Answer: a) Sennar** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which tribes did the Funj pull under its control?
a) the Beja tribes between the Barka and the Sahil  b) the Kunama  c) the Saho  d) the Afar
**Answer: a) the Beja tribes between the Barka and the Sahil** — That is what this lesson teaches.
