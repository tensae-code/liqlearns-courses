# North Africa: Fatimids, Mamluks and the Funj — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which kingdom reached into Eritrea's western lowlands in the 16th century?
**Answer:** The Funj Kingdom of Sennar.

**2.** Between which two places did the Beja tribes live whom the Funj controlled?
**Answer:** Between the Barka and the Sahil.
