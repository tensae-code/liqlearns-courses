# Video script — North Africa: Fatimids, Mamluks and the Funj

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the 16th century the Funj Kingdom of Sennar expanded and pulled the Beja tribes between the Barka and the Sahil under its control.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

From Sennar, far up the Nile, the Funj Kingdom reaches east in the 16th century and pulls the Beja tribes between the Barka and the Sahil under its control. Eritrea's western lowlands are drawn into a Sudanese kingdom's orbit — proof that Eritrea's story was never only about the Red Sea; the Nile side pulled too.

Remember it this way: In the 16th century the Funj Kingdom of Sennar expanded and pulled the Beja tribes between the Barka and the Sahil under its control.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
