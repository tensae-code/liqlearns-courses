# Lesson plan — Ancient Eritrea: Christianity and Geez Writing

**Course:** History — Eritrea · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Eritrea: Christianity and Geez Writing
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Eritrea and the Peoples to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the mid-4th century the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius brought Christianity to the Aksumite world, the Sabean alphabet yielded to Geez, Aksumite coins changed their symbols, and by the 5th century the Bible was translated into Geez.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Eritrea and the Peoples to 1500 (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

2. **Unit 8.1, religion, language and writing** — The older book tells how the faith landed: South Arabian and Greek gods give way in the mid-4th century when the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius bring Christianity.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In the mid-4th century the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius brought Christianity to the Aksumite world, the Sabean alphabet yielded to Geez, Aksumite coins changed their symbols, and by the 5th century the Bible was translated into Geez.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Eritrea's books themselves work, telling the past as stories to be asked about, placed and retold.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the story in the names and dates → go back to the one turn: who wanted what, what changed, and what came of it. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: In the mid-4th century the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius brought Christianity to the Aksumite world, the Sabean alphabet yielded to Geez, Aksumite coins changed their symbols, and by the 5th century the Bible was translated into Geez. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
