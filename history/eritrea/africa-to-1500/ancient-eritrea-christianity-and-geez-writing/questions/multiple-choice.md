Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Through whom did Christianity come to the Aksumite world in the 4th century? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Frumentius  b) Gragn  c) Ozdemir Pasha  d) Ali Abu Gassim

**2.** Which alphabet yielded to Geez? (Sub lesson 2)
