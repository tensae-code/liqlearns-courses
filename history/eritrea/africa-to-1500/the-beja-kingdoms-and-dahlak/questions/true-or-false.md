# The Beja Kingdoms and Dahlak — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Christianity reached Eritrea across the Red Sea about 350 CE. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Dahlak kingdom ruled the highlands from Debarwa. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the Dahlak kingdom rules the islands; the Bahri Negassi rules the highlands from Debarwa
