# Video script — The Beja Kingdoms and Dahlak

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From the sea the faiths arrived — Christianity about 350 CE and Islam, whose first believers found refuge in Dahlak in 615 CE — and the land ordered itself into the Dahlak kingdom on the islands and five Beja kingdoms in the western lowlands.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The faiths arrive by sea. Christianity reaches Eritrea across the Red Sea about 350 CE; and in 615 CE the first Muslims, fleeing persecution, find refuge in Dahlak — planting Islam on the islands and the coast. Eritrea's shore was a refuge before it was a frontier.

By the medieval age the pattern has hardened into states: the Dahlak kingdom rules the islands, the Beja hold the western lowlands, and mosques and inscriptions mark a coast turned toward the Islamic world. Islands, lowlands and highlands now face different seas of faith.

The Grade 6 book names the Beja order precisely: the Zenafij Beja push through the Barka Valley at the end of the 7th century and raise five kingdoms — Naqis, Baqlin, Bazin, Jarin and Qata — while Arab Muslims found the state of Dahlak and Islam spreads from the coast inland. Five small kingdoms, one island state: the lowlands governed themselves in their own way.

Remember it this way: From the sea the faiths arrived — Christianity about 350 CE and Islam, whose first believers found refuge in Dahlak in 615 CE — and the land ordered itself into the Dahlak kingdom on the islands and five Beja kingdoms in the western lowlands.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
