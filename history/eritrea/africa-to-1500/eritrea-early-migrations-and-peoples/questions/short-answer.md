# Eritrea: Early Migrations and Peoples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the waves of peoples in the order the older book gives them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pygmoid first inhabitants, then Nilotes, then Kushite herders, then Semites from Arabia, and later Beja, Arab Muslims in Dahlak and Afar groups.

**2.** What did the Semites from Arabia bring with them? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Camels, horses and new crops.
