# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is history, as this lesson teaches?
a) the record of the past built only from facts  b) a collection of old stories  c) the news of today  d) a list of kings and dates
**Answer: a) the record of the past built only from facts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What must stand behind a fact about the past?
a) a source  b) a guess  c) a fine story  d) a rumour
**Answer: a) a source** — That is what this lesson teaches.
