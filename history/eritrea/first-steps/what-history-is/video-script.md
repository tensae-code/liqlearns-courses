# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is the record of the past built only from facts, and facts come from sources that tell us exactly what happened.

Look at the map. We are in Eritrea — its highlands, its Red Sea coast and its lowlands, and, where the story widens, in the lands around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The book opens with a definition a learner can test: history is the record of the past, and it is built only from facts. A fact is not a guess or a fine story — it is something a source can stand behind. So the historian's first habit is simple: for everything claimed about the past, ask what source tells us exactly what happened.

Remember it this way: History is the record of the past built only from facts, and facts come from sources that tell us exactly what happened.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
