# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Oral tradition is checked against other sources, not trusted alone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Francisco Alvarez's account of the locust invasion is a secondary source because he wrote long after it. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: False** — Alvarez was an eyewitness who wrote what he saw in 1520–27, so his account is a primary source
