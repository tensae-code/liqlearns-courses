# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two kinds of source the past is recovered from. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: oral tradition told by elders; written books/documents; dug-up tools and weapons (archaeological remains); family ancestry lines; picture sources.

**2.** What is the difference between a primary and a secondary source? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A primary source was made at the time (an eyewitness, a diary, a letter); a secondary source is the story retold later, down the generations.
