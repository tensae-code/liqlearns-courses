# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a primary source? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a diary written at the time  b) a story retold down the generations  c) a modern school book  d) a legend told by elders
**Answer: a) a diary written at the time** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the Periplus of the Erythraean Sea describe leaving Adulis? (Sub lesson 3)
a) ivory, tortoise shell and rhino horn  b) silk and spices  c) gold and silver coins  d) horses and camels
**Answer: a) ivory, tortoise shell and rhino horn** — That is what this lesson teaches.
