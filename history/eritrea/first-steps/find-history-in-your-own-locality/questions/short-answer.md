# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How can an old building be a history source? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** It is evidence of who lived in the place and what happened there — who built it, when, and how the town grew.

**2.** What should a learner do with the ancient sites of their own region? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Visit them and record them — ruins, inscriptions and old buildings are sources to be read, not just read about.
