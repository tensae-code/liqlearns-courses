# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An old church or mosque in your town can be read as evidence of who lived there. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** History can only be found in faraway books, not in your own region. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — every zone of Eritrea holds its own ancient sites, and the learner's own region is a history source to be visited and recorded
