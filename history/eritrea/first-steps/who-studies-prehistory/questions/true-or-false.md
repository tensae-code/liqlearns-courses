# Who Studies Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The National Museum keeps the country's archaeological finds.
**Answer: True**

**2.** An ecofact is a tool made by human hands.
**Answer: False** — an ecofact is a natural remain — the chapter's glossary sets objects, features, relics and ecofacts apart as the archaeologist's toolkit
