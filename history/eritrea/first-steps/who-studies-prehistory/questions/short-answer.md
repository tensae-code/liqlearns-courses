# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does the National Museum of Eritrea keep?
**Answer:** The country's finds — the objects dug from Eritrea's ground.

**2.** Name two words from the archaeologist's toolkit as the glossary defines them.
**Answer:** Any two of: archaeological objects, features, relics, ecofacts.
