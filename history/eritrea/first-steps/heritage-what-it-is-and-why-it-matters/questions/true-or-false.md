# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Heritage sites are named site by site across all six zones of Eritrea. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Heritage means only natural things like land and rivers. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — heritage is what is passed from the past to the present — cultural heritage in weddings, dress and custom, and natural heritage beside it
