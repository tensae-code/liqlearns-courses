# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is heritage? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Heritage is what is passed from the past to the present.

**2.** Name two kinds of heritage the older book sets side by side. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cultural heritage (weddings, dress and custom) and natural heritage.
