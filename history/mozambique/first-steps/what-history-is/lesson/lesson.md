# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 1: A História como ciência: definição e importância; Lição 1: O conceito de História. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 1: A História como ciência: definição e importância

A learner asks what history is for; history is defined as the science of human life in society over time, and studying it explains the present.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 1: O conceito de História

A new cycle begins by asking what history itself is — its object, its subject and why this social science matters.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
