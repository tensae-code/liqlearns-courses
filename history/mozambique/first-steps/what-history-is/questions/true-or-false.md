# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A learner asks what history is for; history is defined as the science of human life in society over time, and studying it explains the present. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 1: O conceito de História” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A new cycle begins by asking what history itself is — its object, its subject and why this social science matters.
