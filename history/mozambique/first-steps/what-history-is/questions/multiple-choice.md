# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 1: A História como ciência: definição e importância”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A learner asks what history is for; history is defined as the science of human life in society over time, and studying it explains the present.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: O conceito de História” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) A new cycle begins by asking what history itself is — its object, its subject and why this social science matters.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
