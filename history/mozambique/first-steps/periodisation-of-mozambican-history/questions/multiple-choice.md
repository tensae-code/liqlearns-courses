# Periodisation of Mozambican History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 1: Periodização da História de Moçambique”?
a) Before telling Mozambique's story, the module cuts it into periods, so the learner holds the whole sequence in view from the start.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: Periodização da História de Moçambique” is right?
a) Before telling Mozambique's story, the module cuts it into periods, so the learner holds the whole sequence in view from the start.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
