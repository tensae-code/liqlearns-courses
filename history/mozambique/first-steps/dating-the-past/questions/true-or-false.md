# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Before stories can be ordered, time itself must be counted — calendars, centuries and the before/after-Christ divide turn events into a sequence. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 13: O facto e tempo histórico” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The historical fact is the key element of reconstruction, and time is what gives facts their historicity — two concepts every history stands on.
