# History and the Other Sciences — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History cannot work alone; geography, archaeology, anthropology and sociology each lend it tools to place people in time and space.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 4: A relação entre a História e outras ciências” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: History cannot work alone; geography, archaeology, anthropology and sociology each lend it tools to place people in time and space.
