# History and the Other Sciences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 4: A relação entre a História e outras ciências”?
a) History cannot work alone; geography, archaeology, anthropology and sociology each lend it tools to place people in time and space.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 4: A relação entre a História e outras ciências” is right?
a) History cannot work alone; geography, archaeology, anthropology and sociology each lend it tools to place people in time and space.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
