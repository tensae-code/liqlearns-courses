# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every community keeps places where its story happened; learners identify Mozambique's historic sites, down to their own locality, and learn why they must be preserved.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 3: Os locais de interesse histórico. Every community keeps places where its story happened; learners identify Mozambique's historic sites, down to their own locality, and learn why they must be preserved.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
