# Find History in Your Own Locality

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 3: Os locais de interesse histórico. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 3: Os locais de interesse histórico

Every community keeps places where its story happened; learners identify Mozambique's historic sites, down to their own locality, and learn why they must be preserved.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: every community keeps places where its story happened; learners identify Mozambique's historic sites, down to their own locality, and learn why they must be preserved. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Find History in Your Own Locality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
