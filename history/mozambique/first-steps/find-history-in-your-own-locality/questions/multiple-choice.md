# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 3: Os locais de interesse histórico”?
a) Every community keeps places where its story happened; learners identify Mozambique's historic sites, down to their own locality, and learn why they must be preserved.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 3: Os locais de interesse histórico” is right?
a) Every community keeps places where its story happened; learners identify Mozambique's historic sites, down to their own locality, and learn why they must be preserved.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
