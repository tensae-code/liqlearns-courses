# Problems, Tasks and Methods of African Historiography — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 11: Problemas, tarefas e métodos da historiografia africana”?
a) Scarce sources, weak chronologies and myth make African history hard to rebuild — but it must be written, and the lesson sets out how.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 11: Problemas, tarefas e métodos da historiografia africana” is right?
a) Scarce sources, weak chronologies and myth make African history hard to rebuild — but it must be written, and the lesson sets out how.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
