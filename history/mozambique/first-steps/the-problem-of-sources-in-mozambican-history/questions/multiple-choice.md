# The Problem of Sources in Mozambican History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 2: A problemática das fontes da História de Moçambique”?
a) The historian cannot visit the past, only its traces; Mozambique's traces are thin and tricky, and the lesson faces that problem head-on.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 2: A problemática das fontes da História de Moçambique” is right?
a) The historian cannot visit the past, only its traces; Mozambique's traces are thin and tricky, and the lesson faces that problem head-on.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
