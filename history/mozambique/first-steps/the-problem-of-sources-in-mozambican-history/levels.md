# The Problem of Sources in Mozambican History — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the historian cannot visit the past, only its traces; Mozambique's traces are thin and tricky, and the lesson faces that problem head-on — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
