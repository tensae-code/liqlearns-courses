# Periodisation in History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 6: A periodização em História”?
a) Historians cut the long past into periods to make it graspable; the lesson compares the periodisations of world, African and Mozambican history.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: A periodização em História” is right?
a) Historians cut the long past into periods to make it graspable; the lesson compares the periodisations of world, African and Mozambican history.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
