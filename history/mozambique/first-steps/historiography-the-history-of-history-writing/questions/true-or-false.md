# Historiography: The History of History-Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Greece is named the birthplace of history because the Greeks first treated the past scientifically, unlike the older literary traditions. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 3: A historiografia cristã antiga” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A birth in Bethlehem becomes the hinge of the calendar; early Christian writing reframes all history around Christ.
