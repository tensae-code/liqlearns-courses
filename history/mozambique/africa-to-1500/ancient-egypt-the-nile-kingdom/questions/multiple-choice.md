# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 14: Estrutura sociopolítica e económica do Estado egípcio”?
a) The pharaoh's Egypt is unpacked layer by layer — king, officials, peasants — and the farming and craft economy that fed it.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
