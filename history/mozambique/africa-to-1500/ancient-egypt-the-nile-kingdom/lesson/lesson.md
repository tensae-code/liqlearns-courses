# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 14: Estrutura sociopolítica e económica do Estado egípcio. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 14: Estrutura sociopolítica e económica do Estado egípcio

The pharaoh's Egypt is unpacked layer by layer — king, officials, peasants — and the farming and craft economy that fed it.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
