# West African Empires: Songhai — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Songhai succeeds Mali on the Niger, an empire run on rice, cattle and slave labour, trading across Sahara routes without unified weights and measures.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 24 (Songhai part): Impérios do Ghana, Mali e Songhai” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Songhai succeeds Mali on the Niger, an empire run on rice, cattle and slave labour, trading across Sahara routes without unified weights and measures.
