# West African Empires: Songhai

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 24 (Songhai part): Impérios do Ghana, Mali e Songhai. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 24 (Songhai part): Impérios do Ghana, Mali e Songhai

Songhai succeeds Mali on the Niger, an empire run on rice, cattle and slave labour, trading across Sahara routes without unified weights and measures.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
