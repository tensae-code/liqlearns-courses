# Bantu Expansion and Settlement in Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 21: Os povos de língua bantu: organização económica, social e ideológica; Lição 4: As sociedades moçambicanas após a expansão bantu. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 21: Os povos de língua bantu: organização económica, social e ideológica

Bantu farming peoples migrate from the Great Lakes region in the 2nd–3rd centuries and settle Mozambique, bringing crops, iron and new social organisation.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 4: As sociedades moçambicanas após a expansão bantu

Farmers and herders arrive with the Bantu expansion and plant settled agricultural societies across Mozambique and southern Africa.
