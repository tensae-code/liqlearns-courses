# Bantu Expansion and Settlement in Mozambique — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bantu farming peoples migrate from the Great Lakes region in the 2nd–3rd centuries and settle Mozambique, bringing crops, iron and new social organisation. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 4: As sociedades moçambicanas após a expansão bantu” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Farmers and herders arrive with the Bantu expansion and plant settled agricultural societies across Mozambique and southern Africa.
