# Lesson plan — Bantu Expansion and Settlement in Mozambique

**Course:** History — Mozambique · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Bantu Expansion and Settlement in Mozambique
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Origins and the First States (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** bantu farming peoples migrate from the Great Lakes region in the 2nd–3rd centuries and settle Mozambique, bringing crops, iron and new social organisation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Origins and the First States — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
