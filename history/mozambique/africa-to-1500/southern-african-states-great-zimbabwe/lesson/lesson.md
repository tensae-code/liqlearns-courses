# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 22: O Reino de Zimbabwe; Lição 1: O Estado do Zimbabwe. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 22: O Reino de Zimbabwe

Shona-Karanga builders raise Great Zimbabwe (c.1250–1450), a stone-capital kingdom grown rich on gold and cattle, then decline sets in.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 1: O Estado do Zimbabwe

The oldest of Mozambique's first political organisations is centred on Great Zimbabwe and reaches into Mozambique through its regional centre at Manyikeni.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
