# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 22: O Reino de Zimbabwe”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Shona-Karanga builders raise Great Zimbabwe (c.1250–1450), a stone-capital kingdom grown rich on gold and cattle, then decline sets in.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: O Estado do Zimbabwe” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The oldest of Mozambique's first political organisations is centred on Great Zimbabwe and reaches into Mozambique through its regional centre at Manyikeni.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
