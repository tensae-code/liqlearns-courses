# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shona-Karanga builders raise Great Zimbabwe (c.1250–1450), a stone-capital kingdom grown rich on gold and cattle, then decline sets in. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 1: O Estado do Zimbabwe” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The oldest of Mozambique's first political organisations is centred on Great Zimbabwe and reaches into Mozambique through its regional centre at Manyikeni.
