# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 8: África — berço da humanidade”?
a) The oldest hominid bones keep turning up in African soil, so the human story starts in Africa and spreads out from there.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 8: África — berço da humanidade” is right?
a) The oldest hominid bones keep turning up in African soil, so the human story starts in Africa and spreads out from there.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
