# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The oldest hominid bones keep turning up in African soil, so the human story starts in Africa and spreads out from there.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 8: África — berço da humanidade” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The oldest hominid bones keep turning up in African soil, so the human story starts in Africa and spreads out from there.
