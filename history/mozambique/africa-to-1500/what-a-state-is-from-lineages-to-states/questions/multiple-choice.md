# What a State Is: From Lineages to States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 5: Algumas noções sobre Estado”?
a) From Khoisan bands without stable organisation, Bantu settlement brings lineages; as economy and population grow, lineages strain toward something new — the state.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 5: Algumas noções sobre Estado” is right?
a) From Khoisan bands without stable organisation, Bantu settlement brings lineages; as economy and population grow, lineages strain toward something new — the state.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
