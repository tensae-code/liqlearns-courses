# Mozambique: The Khoisan First Communities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Before the Bantu arrived, Khoisan hunter-gatherer communities lived across Mozambique, organised around kin, nature and spirit beliefs. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 3: As primeiras comunidades em Moçambique” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Mozambique's first people live by hunting and gathering, in small mobile communities long before farming or states.
