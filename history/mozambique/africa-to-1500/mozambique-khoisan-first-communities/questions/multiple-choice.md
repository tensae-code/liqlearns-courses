# Mozambique: The Khoisan First Communities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 20: Moçambique: da comunidade primitiva à formação dos primeiros estados”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Before the Bantu arrived, Khoisan hunter-gatherer communities lived across Mozambique, organised around kin, nature and spirit beliefs.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own

a) Mozambique's first people live by hunting and gathering, in small mobile communities long before farming or states.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
