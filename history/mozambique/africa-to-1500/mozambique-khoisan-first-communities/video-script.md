# Video script — Mozambique: The Khoisan First Communities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Before the Bantu arrived, Khoisan hunter-gatherer communities lived across Mozambique, organised around kin, nature and spirit beliefs.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 20: Moçambique: da comunidade primitiva à formação dos primeiros estados. Before the Bantu arrived, Khoisan hunter-gatherer communities lived across Mozambique, organised around kin, nature and spirit beliefs.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 3: As primeiras comunidades em Moçambique. Mozambique's first people live by hunting and gathering, in small mobile communities long before farming or states.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
