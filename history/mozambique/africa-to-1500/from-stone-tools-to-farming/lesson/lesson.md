# From Stone Tools to Farming

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 11: A evolução da vida económica, social e espiritual dos homens em África; Lição 12: O surgimento da agricultura e a domesticação dos animais. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 11: A evolução da vida económica, social e espiritual dos homens em África

From gathering what nature gives to making tools, organising society and creating art and beliefs — early Africans build an economic and spiritual life.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 12: O surgimento da agricultura e a domesticação dos animais

People stop wandering, plant crops and tame animals, settle in one place and eat better — the farming turn that ends nomad life.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
