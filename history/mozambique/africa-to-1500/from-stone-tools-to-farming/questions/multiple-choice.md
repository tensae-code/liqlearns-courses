# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 11: A evolução da vida económica, social e espiritual dos homens em África”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) From gathering what nature gives to making tools, organising society and creating art and beliefs — early Africans build an economic and spiritual life.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 12: O surgimento da agricultura e a domesticação dos animais” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) People stop wandering, plant crops and tame animals, settle in one place and eat better — the farming turn that ends nomad life.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
