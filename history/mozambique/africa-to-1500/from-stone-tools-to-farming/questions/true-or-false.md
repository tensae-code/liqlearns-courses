# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From gathering what nature gives to making tools, organising society and creating art and beliefs — early Africans build an economic and spiritual life. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 12: O surgimento da agricultura e a domesticação dos animais” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: People stop wandering, plant crops and tame animals, settle in one place and eat better — the farming turn that ends nomad life.
