# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From gathering what nature gives to making tools, organising society and creating art and beliefs — early Africans build an economic and spiritual life.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 11: A evolução da vida económica, social e espiritual dos homens em África. From gathering what nature gives to making tools, organising society and creating art and beliefs — early Africans build an economic and spiritual life.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 12: O surgimento da agricultura e a domesticação dos animais. People stop wandering, plant crops and tame animals, settle in one place and eat better — the farming turn that ends nomad life.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
