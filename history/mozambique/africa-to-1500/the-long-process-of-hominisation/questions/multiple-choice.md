# The Long Process of Hominisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 9: O lento processo de hominização”?
a) Over six million years primate ancestors slowly stand upright, free their hands and grow brains — the long becoming-human called hominisation.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 9: O lento processo de hominização” is right?
a) Over six million years primate ancestors slowly stand upright, free their hands and grow brains — the long becoming-human called hominisation.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
