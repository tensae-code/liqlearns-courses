# The Conquest of Fire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Early humans tame fire, the first energy put to work — warmth, cooked food, light and safety transform daily life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 10: A conquista do fogo” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Early humans tame fire, the first energy put to work — warmth, cooked food, light and safety transform daily life.
