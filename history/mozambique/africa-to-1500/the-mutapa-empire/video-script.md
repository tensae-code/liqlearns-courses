# Video script — The Mutapa Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
As Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 23: O Império Mutapa. As Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 2: O Estado dos Mwenemutapa. As Zimbabwe declines, the Mwenemutapa state rises north of its ruins in the mid-15th century, stretching from the Kalahari to the Indian Ocean on the Shona-Karanga gold trade.
