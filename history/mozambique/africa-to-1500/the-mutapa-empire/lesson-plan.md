# Lesson plan — The Mutapa Empire

**Course:** History — Mozambique · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** The Mutapa Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Origins and the First States (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** as Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Human Origins and the First States — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

2. **Lição 2: O Estado dos Mwenemutapa** — As Zimbabwe declines, the Mwenemutapa state rises north of its ruins in the mid-15th century, stretching from the Kalahari to the Indian Ocean on the Shona-Karanga gold trade.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: as Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines.
2. Give one real example of it from the story as taught — a person, a place and the turn in their fate.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Mozambique, Africa and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
