# The Mutapa Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** As Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 2: O Estado dos Mwenemutapa” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: As Zimbabwe declines, the Mwenemutapa state rises north of its ruins in the mid-15th century, stretching from the Kalahari to the Indian Ocean on the Shona-Karanga gold trade.
