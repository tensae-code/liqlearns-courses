# The Mutapa Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 23: O Império Mutapa”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) As Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 2: O Estado dos Mwenemutapa” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) As Zimbabwe declines, the Mwenemutapa state rises north of its ruins in the mid-15th century, stretching from the Kalahari to the Indian Ocean on the Shona-Karanga gold trade.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
