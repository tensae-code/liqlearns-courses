# The Mutapa Empire

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 23: O Império Mutapa; Lição 2: O Estado dos Mwenemutapa. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 23: O Império Mutapa

As Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 2: O Estado dos Mwenemutapa

As Zimbabwe declines, the Mwenemutapa state rises north of its ruins in the mid-15th century, stretching from the Kalahari to the Indian Ocean on the Shona-Karanga gold trade.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Mutapa Empire. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
