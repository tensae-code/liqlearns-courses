# Foreign Merchants in Mozambique from the 9th Century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 4: Os estados em Moçambique e a penetração mercantil estrangeira”?
a) From the 9th century, for almost ten centuries, Asian then European merchants penetrate Mozambique — a long, strictly commercial link to the world, with no political domination intended.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 4: Os estados em Moçambique e a penetração mercantil estrangeira” is right?
a) From the 9th century, for almost ten centuries, Asian then European merchants penetrate Mozambique — a long, strictly commercial link to the world, with no political domination intended.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
