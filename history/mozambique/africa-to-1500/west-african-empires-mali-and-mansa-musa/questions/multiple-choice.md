# West African Empires: Mali and Mansa Musa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 24 (Mali part): Impérios do Ghana, Mali e Songhai”?
a) Sundiata Keita founds Mali, and under its mansas — above all Mansa Musa — it becomes the great gold empire of the Niger bend.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 24 (Mali part): Impérios do Ghana, Mali e Songhai” is right?
a) Sundiata Keita founds Mali, and under its mansas — above all Mansa Musa — it becomes the great gold empire of the Niger bend.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
