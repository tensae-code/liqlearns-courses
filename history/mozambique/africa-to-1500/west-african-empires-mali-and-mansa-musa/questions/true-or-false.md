# West African Empires: Mali and Mansa Musa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sundiata Keita founds Mali, and under its mansas — above all Mansa Musa — it becomes the great gold empire of the Niger bend.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 24 (Mali part): Impérios do Ghana, Mali e Songhai” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Sundiata Keita founds Mali, and under its mansas — above all Mansa Musa — it becomes the great gold empire of the Niger bend.
