# Video script — Origins of Humankind: Evolution and Creation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Two rival answers to where humans came from — creation and Darwinian evolution — are set side by side for the learner to weigh.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 7: A origem e evolução do Homem: teorias da evolução e teoria da criação. Two rival answers to where humans came from — creation and Darwinian evolution — are set side by side for the learner to weigh.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
