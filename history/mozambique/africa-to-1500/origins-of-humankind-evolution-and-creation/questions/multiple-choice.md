# Origins of Humankind: Evolution and Creation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 7: A origem e evolução do Homem: teorias da evolução e teoria da criação”?
a) Two rival answers to where humans came from — creation and Darwinian evolution — are set side by side for the learner to weigh.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 7: A origem e evolução do Homem: teorias da evolução e teoria da criação” is right?
a) Two rival answers to where humans came from — creation and Darwinian evolution — are set side by side for the learner to weigh.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
