# Origins of Humankind: Evolution and Creation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two rival answers to where humans came from — creation and Darwinian evolution — are set side by side for the learner to weigh.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 7: A origem e evolução do Homem: teorias da evolução e teoria da criação” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Two rival answers to where humans came from — creation and Darwinian evolution — are set side by side for the learner to weigh.
