# Fascist Policy in Portugal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 6: A política fascista em Portugal”?
a) Fascist ideas travel; Portugal's version takes hold and reaches across the sea into Africa and Mozambique.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: A política fascista em Portugal” is right?
a) Fascist ideas travel; Portugal's version takes hold and reaches across the sea into Africa and Mozambique.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
