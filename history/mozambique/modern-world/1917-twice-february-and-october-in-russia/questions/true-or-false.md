# 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Russia's workers and soldiers rise in October 1917; the revolution's causes and the birth of socialism change the ideologies of the whole world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 1: A Revolução Socialista de Outubro de 1917” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Russia's workers and soldiers rise in October 1917; the revolution's causes and the birth of socialism change the ideologies of the whole world.
