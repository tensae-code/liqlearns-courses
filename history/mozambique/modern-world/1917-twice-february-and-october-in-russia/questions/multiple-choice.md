# 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 1: A Revolução Socialista de Outubro de 1917”?
a) Russia's workers and soldiers rise in October 1917; the revolution's causes and the birth of socialism change the ideologies of the whole world.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: A Revolução Socialista de Outubro de 1917” is right?
a) Russia's workers and soldiers rise in October 1917; the revolution's causes and the birth of socialism change the ideologies of the whole world.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
