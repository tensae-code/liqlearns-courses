# 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 1: A Revolução Socialista de Outubro de 1917. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 1: A Revolução Socialista de Outubro de 1917

Russia's workers and soldiers rise in October 1917; the revolution's causes and the birth of socialism change the ideologies of the whole world.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: russia's workers and soldiers rise in October 1917; the revolution's causes and the birth of socialism change the ideologies of the whole world. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
