# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 5: Fascismo na Itália e o nazismo na Alemanha. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 5: Fascismo na Itália e o nazismo na Alemanha

Mussolini builds fascist Italy after WWI; Hitler, inspired by it, installs Nazism in a defeated Germany in 1933 — two dictatorships compared.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
