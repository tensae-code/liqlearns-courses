# From Free Competition to Monopoly Capitalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 25: Do capitalismo de livre concorrência ao capitalismo monopolista (imperialismo)”?
a) Industry concentrates into monopolies; the concentrated capital creates imperialist powers that dispute and dominate the world.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 25: Do capitalismo de livre concorrência ao capitalismo monopolista (imperialismo)” is right?
a) Industry concentrates into monopolies; the concentrated capital creates imperialist powers that dispute and dominate the world.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
