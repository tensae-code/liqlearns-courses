# From Free Competition to Monopoly Capitalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Industry concentrates into monopolies; the concentrated capital creates imperialist powers that dispute and dominate the world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 25: Do capitalismo de livre concorrência ao capitalismo monopolista (imperialismo)” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Industry concentrates into monopolies; the concentrated capital creates imperialist powers that dispute and dominate the world.
