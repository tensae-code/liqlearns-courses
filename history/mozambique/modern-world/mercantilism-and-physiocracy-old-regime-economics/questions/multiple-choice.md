# Mercantilism and Physiocracy: Old Regime Economics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 8: As teorias económicas do Antigo Regime”?
a) Two rival recipes for national wealth face off — mercantilism hoards bullion through trade, physiocracy bets on the land.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 8: As teorias económicas do Antigo Regime” is right?
a) Two rival recipes for national wealth face off — mercantilism hoards bullion through trade, physiocracy bets on the land.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
