# Post Wwi Economic and Political Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 2: O desenvolvimento económico e sócio-político de alguns países depois da I Guerra Mundial”?
a) The USA enters the war Europe's debtor and leaves its creditor; Germany and Italy struggle with the post-war order.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 2: O desenvolvimento económico e sócio-político de alguns países depois da I Guerra Mundial” is right?
a) The USA enters the war Europe's debtor and leaves its creditor; Germany and Italy struggle with the post-war order.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
