# Republic and Reign of Terror — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 15: Etapas da Revolução Francesa”?
a) The revolution marches through its stages — Constituent Assembly, Convention, Directory, Consulate — each turn more radical than the last.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 15: Etapas da Revolução Francesa” is right?
a) The revolution marches through its stages — Constituent Assembly, Convention, Directory, Consulate — each turn more radical than the last.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
