# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 9: As consequências da II Guerra Mundial”?
a) A war fought with tanks, submarines, aircraft and poison gas leaves nations ruined — the lesson counts what such conflict costs development.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 9: As consequências da II Guerra Mundial” is right?
a) A war fought with tanks, submarines, aircraft and poison gas leaves nations ruined — the lesson counts what such conflict costs development.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
