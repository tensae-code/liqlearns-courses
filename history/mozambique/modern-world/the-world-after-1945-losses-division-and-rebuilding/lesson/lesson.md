# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 9: As consequências da II Guerra Mundial. ## Lição 9: As consequências da II Guerra Mundial

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
