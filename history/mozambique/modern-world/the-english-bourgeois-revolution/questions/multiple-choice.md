# The English Bourgeois Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 13: A Revolução burguesa na Inglaterra e o seu significado”?
a) England's bourgeoisie breaks absolutist power in revolution, opening a new phase of economic, political and social transformation for Europe and the world.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 13: A Revolução burguesa na Inglaterra e o seu significado” is right?
a) England's bourgeoisie breaks absolutist power in revolution, opening a new phase of economic, political and social transformation for Europe and the world.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
