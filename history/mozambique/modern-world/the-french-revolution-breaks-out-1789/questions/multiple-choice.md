# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 14: As causas e arranque da Revolução Francesa”?
a) Absolutism and feudal privilege drive France to revolt; the manifestations against the old regime ignite a revolution the whole world will feel.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 14: As causas e arranque da Revolução Francesa” is right?
a) Absolutism and feudal privilege drive France to revolt; the manifestations against the old regime ignite a revolution the whole world will feel.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
