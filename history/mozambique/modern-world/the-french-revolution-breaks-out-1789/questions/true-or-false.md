# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Absolutism and feudal privilege drive France to revolt; the manifestations against the old regime ignite a revolution the whole world will feel.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 14: As causas e arranque da Revolução Francesa” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
