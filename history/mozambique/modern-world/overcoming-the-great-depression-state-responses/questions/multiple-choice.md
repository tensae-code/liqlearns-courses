# Overcoming the Great Depression: State Responses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 4: As tentativas da superação da crise”?
a) State after state intervenes to escape the Depression, learning that producing without buyers ends in ruin.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 4: As tentativas da superação da crise” is right?
a) State after state intervenes to escape the Depression, learning that producing without buyers ends in ruin.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
