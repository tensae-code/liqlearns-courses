# The First World War Versailles and the Results

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 34: O fim da I Guerra Mundial e a criação da Sociedade das Nações. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 34: O fim da I Guerra Mundial e a criação da Sociedade das Nações

The victors meet to engineer a lasting peace and create the League of Nations to guard it.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the victors meet to engineer a lasting peace and create the League of Nations to guard it. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The First World War Versailles and the Results. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
