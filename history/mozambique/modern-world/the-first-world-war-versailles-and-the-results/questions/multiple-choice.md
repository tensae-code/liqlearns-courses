# The First World War Versailles and the Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 34: O fim da I Guerra Mundial e a criação da Sociedade das Nações”?
a) The victors meet to engineer a lasting peace and create the League of Nations to guard it.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 34: O fim da I Guerra Mundial e a criação da Sociedade das Nações” is right?
a) The victors meet to engineer a lasting peace and create the League of Nations to guard it.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
