# The Renaissance What It Was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 9: O Renascimento e o Humanismo”?
a) Between the 14th and 16th centuries Europe tries to recover a culture it believes the Middle Ages lost — Renaissance art and Humanist thought put man at the centre.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 9: O Renascimento e o Humanismo” is right?
a) Between the 14th and 16th centuries Europe tries to recover a culture it believes the Middle Ages lost — Renaissance art and Humanist thought put man at the centre.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
