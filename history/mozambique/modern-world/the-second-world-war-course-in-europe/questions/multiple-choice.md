# The Second World War Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 8: As fases da II Guerra Mundial”?
a) The war widens from Europe to the world, then turns — Allied counter-offensives roll back the Axis to victory.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 8: As fases da II Guerra Mundial” is right?
a) The war widens from Europe to the world, then turns — Allied counter-offensives roll back the Axis to victory.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
