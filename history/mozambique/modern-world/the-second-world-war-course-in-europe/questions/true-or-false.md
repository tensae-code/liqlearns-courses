# The Second World War Course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war widens from Europe to the world, then turns — Allied counter-offensives roll back the Axis to victory.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 8: As fases da II Guerra Mundial” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The war widens from Europe to the world, then turns — Allied counter-offensives roll back the Axis to victory.
