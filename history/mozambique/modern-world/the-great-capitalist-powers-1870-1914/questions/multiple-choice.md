# The Great Capitalist Powers 1870–1914 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 29: O desenvolvimento sócio-económico e político dos principais países capitalistas (finais séc. XIX–princípios XX)”?
a) The great capitalist powers race ahead unevenly at the turn of the century, and their contradictions sharpen toward confrontation.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 29: O desenvolvimento sócio-económico e político dos principais países capitalistas (finais séc. XIX–princípios XX)” is right?
a) The great capitalist powers race ahead unevenly at the turn of the century, and their contradictions sharpen toward confrontation.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
