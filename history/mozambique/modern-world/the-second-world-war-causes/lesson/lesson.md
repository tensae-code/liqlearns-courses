# The Second World War Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 7: As causas da II Guerra Mundial. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 7: As causas da II Guerra Mundial

Humiliated at Versailles, the defeated powers tear up the treaty, expand and arm until tension erupts into the Second World War.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: humiliated at Versailles, the defeated powers tear up the treaty, expand and arm until tension erupts into the Second World War. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Second World War Causes. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
