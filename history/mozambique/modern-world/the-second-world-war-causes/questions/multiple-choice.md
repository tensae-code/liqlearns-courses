# The Second World War Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 7: As causas da II Guerra Mundial”?
a) Humiliated at Versailles, the defeated powers tear up the treaty, expand and arm until tension erupts into the Second World War.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 7: As causas da II Guerra Mundial” is right?
a) Humiliated at Versailles, the defeated powers tear up the treaty, expand and arm until tension erupts into the Second World War.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
