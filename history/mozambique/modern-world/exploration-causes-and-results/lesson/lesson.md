# Exploration Causes and Results

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 3: Expansão europeia e o comércio colonial; Lição 7: As consequências da 1ª expansão europeia. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 3: Expansão europeia e o comércio colonial

Short of goods and hungry for markets, raw materials and converts, Portugal and Spain lead Europe onto the oceans and colonial trade follows the ships.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 7: As consequências da 1ª expansão europeia

The first expansion's bill arrives — economic, social, political, religious and scientific consequences for every region it touched.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
