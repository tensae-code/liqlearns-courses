# Exploration Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 3: Expansão europeia e o comércio colonial”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Short of goods and hungry for markets, raw materials and converts, Portugal and Spain lead Europe onto the oceans and colonial trade follows the ships.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 7: As consequências da 1ª expansão europeia” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The first expansion's bill arrives — economic, social, political, religious and scientific consequences for every region it touched.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
