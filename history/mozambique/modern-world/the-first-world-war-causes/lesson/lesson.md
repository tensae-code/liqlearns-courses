# The First World War Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 30: Formação das alianças e blocos militares e os primeiros conflitos entre as potências imperialistas; Lição 31: As causas da 1ª Guerra Mundial. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.
