# The First World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rival powers lock themselves into alliances and military blocs, fight the first imperialist clashes, and slide toward world war. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 31: As causas da 1ª Guerra Mundial” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Imperialist economic competition hardens into rivalries until the powers choose war — the causes of the First World War laid out.
