# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 17: A Guerra Fria”?
a) The USA and USSR, preaching rival ideologies, split the world into two zones of influence without fighting each other directly — the Cold War.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
