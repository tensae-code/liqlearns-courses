# Europe and the World at the Start of the 15th Century

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 1: A Europa e o mundo no início do séc. XV. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 1: A Europa e o mundo no início do séc. XV

Feudal Europe begins to crack; new economic and social forces push the continent from the feudal regime toward a capitalist world system.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: feudal Europe begins to crack; new economic and social forces push the continent from the feudal regime toward a capitalist world system. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Europe and the World at the Start of the 15th Century. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
