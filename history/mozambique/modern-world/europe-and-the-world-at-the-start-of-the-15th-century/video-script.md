# Video script — Europe and the World at the Start of the 15th Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Feudal Europe begins to crack; new economic and social forces push the continent from the feudal regime toward a capitalist world system.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 1: A Europa e o mundo no início do séc. XV. Feudal Europe begins to crack; new economic and social forces push the continent from the feudal regime toward a capitalist world system.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
