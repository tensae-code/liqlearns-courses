# Europe and the World at the Start of the 15th Century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 1: A Europa e o mundo no início do séc. XV”?
a) Feudal Europe begins to crack; new economic and social forces push the continent from the feudal regime toward a capitalist world system.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: A Europa e o mundo no início do séc. XV” is right?
a) Feudal Europe begins to crack; new economic and social forces push the continent from the feudal regime toward a capitalist world system.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
