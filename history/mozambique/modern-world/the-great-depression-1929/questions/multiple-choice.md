# The Great Depression 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 3: A crise económica mundial de 1929 a 1933”?
a) Overproduction in the USA bursts into the 1929 crash; bankruptcies spread until the crisis grips the whole world, Mozambique included.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 3: A crise económica mundial de 1929 a 1933” is right?
a) Overproduction in the USA bursts into the 1929 crash; bankruptcies spread until the crisis grips the whole world, Mozambique included.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
