# The Great Depression 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Overproduction in the USA bursts into the 1929 crash; bankruptcies spread until the crisis grips the whole world, Mozambique included.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 3: A crise económica mundial de 1929 a 1933” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Overproduction in the USA bursts into the 1929 crash; bankruptcies spread until the crisis grips the whole world, Mozambique included.
