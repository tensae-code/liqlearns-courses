# The Third World and the Non-Aligned Movement

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 18: Os países do Terceiro Mundo e o Movimento dos Países Não Alinhados. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 18: Os países do Terceiro Mundo e o Movimento dos Países Não Alinhados

Poor countries refuse to line up behind either superpower bloc and organise as the Non-Aligned Movement — a third position in a two-bloc world.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: poor countries refuse to line up behind either superpower bloc and organise as the Non-Aligned Movement — a third position in a two-bloc world. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Third World and the Non-Aligned Movement. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
