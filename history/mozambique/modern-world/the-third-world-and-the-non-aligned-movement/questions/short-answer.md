# The Third World and the Non-Aligned Movement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Lição 18: Os países do Terceiro Mundo e o Movimento dos Países Não Alinhados”?
**Answer:** Poor countries refuse to line up behind either superpower bloc and organise as the Non-Aligned Movement — a third position in a two-bloc world.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Poor countries refuse to line up behind either superpower bloc and organise as the Non-Aligned Movement — a third position in a two-bloc world.
