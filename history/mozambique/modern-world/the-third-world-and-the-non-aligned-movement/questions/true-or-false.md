# The Third World and the Non-Aligned Movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Poor countries refuse to line up behind either superpower bloc and organise as the Non-Aligned Movement — a third position in a two-bloc world.
**Answer: True**
