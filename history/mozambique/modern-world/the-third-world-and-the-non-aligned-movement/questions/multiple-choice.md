# The Third World and the Non-Aligned Movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 18: Os países do Terceiro Mundo e o Movimento dos Países Não Alinhados”?
a) Poor countries refuse to line up behind either superpower bloc and organise as the Non-Aligned Movement — a third position in a two-bloc world.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 18: Os países do Terceiro Mundo e o Movimento dos Países Não Alinhados” is right?
a) Poor countries refuse to line up behind either superpower bloc and organise as the Non-Aligned Movement — a third position in a two-bloc world.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
