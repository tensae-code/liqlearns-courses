# The First World War Course of the War

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 32: As fases da 1ª Guerra Mundial. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 32: As fases da 1ª Guerra Mundial

From 1914 to 1918 Europe's war moves through its phases — opening offensives, deadlock and final collapse.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: from 1914 to 1918 Europe's war moves through its phases — opening offensives, deadlock and final collapse. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The First World War Course of the War. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
