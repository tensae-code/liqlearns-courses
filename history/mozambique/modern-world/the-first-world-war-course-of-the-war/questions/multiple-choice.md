# The First World War Course of the War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 32: As fases da 1ª Guerra Mundial”?
a) From 1914 to 1918 Europe's war moves through its phases — opening offensives, deadlock and final collapse.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 32: As fases da 1ª Guerra Mundial” is right?
a) From 1914 to 1918 Europe's war moves through its phases — opening offensives, deadlock and final collapse.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
