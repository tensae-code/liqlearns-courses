# Imperialism and Imperialist Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 24: O imperialismo e a política imperialista”?
a) Around the mid-19th century free-competition capitalism mutates into monopoly capitalism — imperialism — and a new policy of domination follows.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 24: O imperialismo e a política imperialista” is right?
a) Around the mid-19th century free-competition capitalism mutates into monopoly capitalism — imperialism — and a new policy of domination follows.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
