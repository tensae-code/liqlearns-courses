# Absolutism in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 12: O Absolutismo”?
a) As feudalism decays, kings gather all power into their own hands; absolutist regimes rule most of Europe from the 15th to the early 19th century.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 12: O Absolutismo” is right?
a) As feudalism decays, kings gather all power into their own hands; absolutist regimes rule most of Europe from the 15th to the early 19th century.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
