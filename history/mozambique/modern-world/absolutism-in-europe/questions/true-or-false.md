# Absolutism in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** As feudalism decays, kings gather all power into their own hands; absolutist regimes rule most of Europe from the 15th to the early 19th century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 12: O Absolutismo” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
