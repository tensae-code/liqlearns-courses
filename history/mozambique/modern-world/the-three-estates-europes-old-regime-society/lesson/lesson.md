# The Three Estates: Europe's Old Regime Society

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 2: A população europeia no período de transição. ## Lição 2: A população europeia no período de transição

European society stands in three ranked orders — clergy, nobility and the burdened Third Estate — a structure straining under transition.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
