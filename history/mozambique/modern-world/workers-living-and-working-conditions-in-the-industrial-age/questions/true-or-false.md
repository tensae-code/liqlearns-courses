# Workers' Living and Working Conditions in the Industrial Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Factory owners grow rich while workers live and labour in misery; the inequality breeds new ways of thinking and acting.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 19: As condições de vida e de trabalho dos operários” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Factory owners grow rich while workers live and labour in misery; the inequality breeds new ways of thinking and acting.
