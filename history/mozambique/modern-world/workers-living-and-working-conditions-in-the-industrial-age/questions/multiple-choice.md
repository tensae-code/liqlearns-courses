# Workers' Living and Working Conditions in the Industrial Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 19: As condições de vida e de trabalho dos operários”?
a) Factory owners grow rich while workers live and labour in misery; the inequality breeds new ways of thinking and acting.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 19: As condições de vida e de trabalho dos operários” is right?
a) Factory owners grow rich while workers live and labour in misery; the inequality breeds new ways of thinking and acting.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
