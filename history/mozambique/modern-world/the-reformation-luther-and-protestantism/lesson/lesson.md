# The Reformation Luther and Protestantism

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 10: A crise religiosa do século XVI; Lição 11 (Reformation part): A Reforma religiosa e a resposta da Igreja Católica. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 10: A crise religiosa do século XVI

Protest against Catholic leadership swells into a religious crisis that breaks the Church's centuries-old hegemony and births new churches.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 11 (Reformation part): A Reforma religiosa e a resposta da Igreja Católica

Luther revolts against indulgences and wins followers; Lutheranism, then Calvinism in Switzerland and Anglicanism in England, spread the break.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
