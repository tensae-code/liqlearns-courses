# The Reformation Luther and Protestantism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Protest against Catholic leadership swells into a religious crisis that breaks the Church's centuries-old hegemony and births new churches. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 11 (Reformation part): A Reforma religiosa e a resposta da Igreja Católica” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Luther revolts against indulgences and wins followers; Lutheranism, then Calvinism in Switzerland and Anglicanism in England, spread the break.
