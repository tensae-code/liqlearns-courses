# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 16: Localização geográfica da Mesopotâmia e o Código de Hamurabi. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 16: Localização geográfica da Mesopotâmia e o Código de Hamurabi

Between the Tigris and Euphrates, King Hammurabi carves his laws in stone so every subject can see what justice demands.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: between the Tigris and Euphrates, King Hammurabi carves his laws in stone so every subject can see what justice demands. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
