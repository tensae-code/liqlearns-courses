# Social Differentiation and the First Class Societies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Surpluses split once-equal communities into classes; Egypt is used as the case where rulers and ruled first appear. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 6: O surgimento da diferenciação social” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Growing production, population, taxation and defence split society into layers and set them struggling — differentiation, the precondition of the state.
