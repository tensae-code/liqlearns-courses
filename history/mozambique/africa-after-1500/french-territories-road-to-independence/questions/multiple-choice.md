# French Territories Road to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 7: O caminho para a independência nos territórios franceses”?
a) France's 1946 Fourth Republic constitution gives colonials citizenship on paper but not their interests; the French territories push on toward independence.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 7: O caminho para a independência nos territórios franceses” is right?
a) France's 1946 Fourth Republic constitution gives colonials citizenship on paper but not their interests; the French territories push on toward independence.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
