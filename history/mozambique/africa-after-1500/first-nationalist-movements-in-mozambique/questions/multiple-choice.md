# First Nationalist Movements in Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 13: As primeiras manifestações nacionalistas em Moçambique”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Africans resist colonisation in defence of sovereignty; cultural associations become the seedbed of Mozambique's first nationalist manifestations.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 17: As primeiras formulações nacionalistas” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Out of the colonial order's own contradictions, the first nationalist formulations appear in Mozambique — the idea of the nation takes shape.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
