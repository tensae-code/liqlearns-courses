# First Nationalist Movements in Mozambique — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Africans resist colonisation in defence of sovereignty; cultural associations become the seedbed of Mozambique's first nationalist manifestations. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 17: As primeiras formulações nacionalistas” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Out of the colonial order's own contradictions, the first nationalist formulations appear in Mozambique — the idea of the nation takes shape.
