# Decolonisation Ghana Kenya and the South — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 5: O percurso para a independência nas colónias inglesas”?
a) Empires proclaimed permanent last only three generations; the British colonies travel their own road to independence.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 5: O percurso para a independência nas colónias inglesas” is right?
a) Empires proclaimed permanent last only three generations; the British colonies travel their own road to independence.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
