# Independence in British Central Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nyasaland and the two Rhodesias travel Britain's road to independence, each with local particularities that bend the route. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 12: Independência na África Central britânica (Rodésia do Sul)” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Southern Rhodesia's settlers refuse London's neocolonial proposal and entrench their own regime against the nationalist tide.
