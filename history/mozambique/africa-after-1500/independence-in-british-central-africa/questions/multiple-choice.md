# Independence in British Central Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 11: A independência na África Central britânica”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Nyasaland and the two Rhodesias travel Britain's road to independence, each with local particularities that bend the route.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 12: Independência na África Central britânica (Rodésia do Sul)” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Southern Rhodesia's settlers refuse London's neocolonial proposal and entrench their own regime against the nationalist tide.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
