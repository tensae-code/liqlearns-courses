# Maji Maji and Resistance in the South and North

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 14: A resistência na África Central e Austral. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.
