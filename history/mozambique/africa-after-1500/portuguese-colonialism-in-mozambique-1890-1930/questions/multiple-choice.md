# Portuguese Colonialism in Mozambique 1890–1930 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 2: O colonialismo português em Moçambique (1890–1930)”?
a) In the great colonising movement of the turn of the century, Portugal occupies Mozambique among its other territories.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 2: O colonialismo português em Moçambique (1890–1930)” is right?
a) In the great colonising movement of the turn of the century, Portugal occupies Mozambique among its other territories.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
