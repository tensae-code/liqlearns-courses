# The Gaza State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 10: O Estado de Gaza”?
a) The south had only chiefdoms and small kingdoms until the Mfecane's shockwave from South Africa culminates in the Gaza state, created from 1821.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 10: O Estado de Gaza” is right?
a) The south had only chiefdoms and small kingdoms until the Mfecane's shockwave from South Africa culminates in the Gaza state, created from 1821.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
