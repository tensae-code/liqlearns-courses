# The Maravi States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** North of the Zambezi, the Maravi states take their place among Mozambique's first states, with their own time, territory and organisation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 3: Os Estados Marave” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: North of the Zambezi, the Maravi states take their place among Mozambique's first states, with their own time, territory and organisation.
