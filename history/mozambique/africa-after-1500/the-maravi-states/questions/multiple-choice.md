# The Maravi States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 3: Os Estados Marave”?
a) North of the Zambezi, the Maravi states take their place among Mozambique's first states, with their own time, territory and organisation.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 3: Os Estados Marave” is right?
a) North of the Zambezi, the Maravi states take their place among Mozambique's first states, with their own time, territory and organisation.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
