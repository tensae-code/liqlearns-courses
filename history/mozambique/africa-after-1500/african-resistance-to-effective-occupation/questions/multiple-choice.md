# African Resistance to Effective Occupation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 28: A luta dos povos africanos contra a ocupação efectiva em África”?
a) Africans do not submit quietly — peoples resist imperialist occupation across the continent, with the struggles in South Africa and Namibia told in detail.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 28: A luta dos povos africanos contra a ocupação efectiva em África” is right?
a) Africans do not submit quietly — peoples resist imperialist occupation across the continent, with the struggles in South Africa and Namibia told in detail.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
