# Company Rule, Direct Rule and Assimilation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 2: As estruturas coloniais”?
a) Colonial states are built to extract — political structures, courts, forced labour and head taxes named as the machinery of domination.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 2: As estruturas coloniais” is right?
a) Colonial states are built to extract — political structures, courts, forced labour and head taxes named as the machinery of domination.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
