# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 1: África diante do desafio colonial”?
a) After studying how history-writing evolved, the focus turns to Africa itself facing the colonial challenge and the domination to come.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: África diante do desafio colonial” is right?
