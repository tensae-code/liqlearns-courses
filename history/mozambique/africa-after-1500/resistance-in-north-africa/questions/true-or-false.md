# Resistance in North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Morocco and Tunisia resist the European takeover of the Maghreb, where Ottoman ties and local rulers shape the fight. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 12: A resistência na Líbia” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Libya, bound by tradition to the Ottoman world like its Maghreb neighbours, resists Italian occupation in its own long struggle.
