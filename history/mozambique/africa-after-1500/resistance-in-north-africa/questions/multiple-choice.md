# Resistance in North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 11: A resistência na África do Norte (Marrocos e Tunísia)”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Morocco and Tunisia resist the European takeover of the Maghreb, where Ottoman ties and local rulers shape the fight.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 12: A resistência na Líbia” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Libya, bound by tradition to the Ottoman world like its Maghreb neighbours, resists Italian occupation in its own long struggle.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
