# Video script — Resistance in North Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Morocco and Tunisia resist the European takeover of the Maghreb, where Ottoman ties and local rulers shape the fight.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 12: A resistência na Líbia. Libya, bound by tradition to the Ottoman world like its Maghreb neighbours, resists Italian occupation in its own long struggle.
