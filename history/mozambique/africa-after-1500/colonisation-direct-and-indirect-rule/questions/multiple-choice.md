# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 36: Estabelecimento do sistema colonial em África”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) After Berlin each power installs its own administration — by diplomacy and by military action — extinguishing the sovereignty of African states.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: Os territórios britânicos” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Britain, with France the greatest colonial power, holds west, north-east, east, central and southern Africa — and rules them its own way.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
