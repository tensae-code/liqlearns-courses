# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Berlin each power installs its own administration — by diplomacy and by military action — extinguishing the sovereignty of African states. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 6: Os territórios britânicos” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Britain, with France the greatest colonial power, holds west, north-east, east, central and southern Africa — and rules them its own way.
