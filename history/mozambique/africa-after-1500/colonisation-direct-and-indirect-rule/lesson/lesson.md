# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 36: Estabelecimento do sistema colonial em África; Lição 6: Os territórios britânicos. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 36: Estabelecimento do sistema colonial em África

After Berlin each power installs its own administration — by diplomacy and by military action — extinguishing the sovereignty of African states.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 6: Os territórios britânicos

Britain, with France the greatest colonial power, holds west, north-east, east, central and southern Africa — and rules them its own way.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
