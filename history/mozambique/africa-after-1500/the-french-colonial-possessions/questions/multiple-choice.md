# The French Colonial Possessions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 4: As possessões francesas”?
a) France launches its own colonial project to exploit Africa's natural and human resources, with methods distinct from its rivals.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 4: As possessões francesas” is right?
a) France launches its own colonial project to exploit Africa's natural and human resources, with methods distinct from its rivals.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
