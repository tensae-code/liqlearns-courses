# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 5: A partilha e conquista de África. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 5: A partilha e conquista de África

Berlin's rule of effective occupation lands hardest — a flag on the coast no longer counts; claimants must seize and hold the interior.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
