# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 5: A partilha e conquista de África”?
a) Berlin's rule of effective occupation lands hardest — a flag on the coast no longer counts; claimants must seize and hold the interior.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 5: A partilha e conquista de África” is right?
a) Berlin's rule of effective occupation lands hardest — a flag on the coast no longer counts; claimants must seize and hold the interior.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
