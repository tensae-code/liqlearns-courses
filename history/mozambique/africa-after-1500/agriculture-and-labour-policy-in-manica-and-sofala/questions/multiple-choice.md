# Agriculture and Labour Policy in Manica and Sofala — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 12: A agricultura e a política laboral em Manica e Sofala”?
a) Under the Company, great plantations grow raw materials for export, and a labour policy is built to feed them with African workers.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 12: A agricultura e a política laboral em Manica e Sofala” is right?
a) Under the Company, great plantations grow raw materials for export, and a labour policy is built to feed them with African workers.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
