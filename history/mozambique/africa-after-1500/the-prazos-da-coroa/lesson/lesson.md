# The Prazos Da Coroa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 6: Os prazos da coroa. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 6: Os prazos da coroa

Portuguese penetration of the Mwenemutapa state births a new institution — crown land grants, the prazos — with their own economic, political and social order.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: portuguese penetration of the Mwenemutapa state births a new institution — crown land grants, the prazos — with their own economic, political and social order. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Prazos Da Coroa. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
