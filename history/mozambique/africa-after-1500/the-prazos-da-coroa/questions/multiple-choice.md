# The Prazos Da Coroa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 6: Os prazos da coroa”?
a) Portuguese penetration of the Mwenemutapa state births a new institution — crown land grants, the prazos — with their own economic, political and social order.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: Os prazos da coroa” is right?
a) Portuguese penetration of the Mwenemutapa state births a new institution — crown land grants, the prazos — with their own economic, political and social order.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
