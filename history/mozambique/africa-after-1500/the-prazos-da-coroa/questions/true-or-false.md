# The Prazos Da Coroa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Portuguese penetration of the Mwenemutapa state births a new institution — crown land grants, the prazos — with their own economic, political and social order.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 6: Os prazos da coroa” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Portuguese penetration of the Mwenemutapa state births a new institution — crown land grants, the prazos — with their own economic, political and social order.
