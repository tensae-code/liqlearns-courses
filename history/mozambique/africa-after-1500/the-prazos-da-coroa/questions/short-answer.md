# The Prazos Da Coroa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Lição 6: Os prazos da coroa”?
**Answer:** Portuguese penetration of the Mwenemutapa state births a new institution — crown land grants, the prazos — with their own economic, political and social order.
