# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Imperialist ambitions collide over Africa until the powers sit down in Berlin to settle the partition of a continent that is not theirs. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 3: A corrida imperialista e a partilha de África” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: European presence is still thin by the 1870s, then the imperialist race breaks out and the powers rush to partition a continent.
