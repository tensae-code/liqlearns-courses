# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 27: Os impérios coloniais em África”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Imperialist ambitions collide over Africa until the powers sit down in Berlin to settle the partition of a continent that is not theirs.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 3: A corrida imperialista e a partilha de África” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) European presence is still thin by the 1870s, then the imperialist race breaks out and the powers rush to partition a continent.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
