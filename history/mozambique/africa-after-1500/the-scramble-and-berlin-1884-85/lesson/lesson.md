# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 27: Os impérios coloniais em África; Lição 3: A corrida imperialista e a partilha de África. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 27: Os impérios coloniais em África

Imperialist ambitions collide over Africa until the powers sit down in Berlin to settle the partition of a continent that is not theirs.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 3: A corrida imperialista e a partilha de África

European presence is still thin by the 1870s, then the imperialist race breaks out and the powers rush to partition a continent.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
