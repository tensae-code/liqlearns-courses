# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 5: A penetração mercantil europeia. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 5: A penetração mercantil europeia

After five centuries of Arab presence, Portuguese merchants trade gold with the Mwenemutapa Shona, ivory with the Maravi and slaves with Zambezi-valley and coastal Afro-Islamic polities for nearly four centuries.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
