# The Ideology of African Resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 10: A ideologia da resistência”?
a) Was resistance scattered reaction or organised defiance? The lesson debates how resistance should be read and where it ranks in Africa's story.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 10: A ideologia da resistência” is right?
a) Was resistance scattered reaction or organised defiance? The lesson debates how resistance should be read and where it ranks in Africa's story.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
