# Africas Political Map After the Conquest — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — between 1890 and 1914 Africa's political map is redrawn at lightning speed — the lesson measures the violence inside that transformation — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
