# European Presence in Mozambique

Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 23: A presença europeia em Moçambique. ## Lição 23: A presença europeia em Moçambique

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
