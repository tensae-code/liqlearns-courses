# European Presence in Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 23: A presença europeia em Moçambique”?
a) Vasco da Gama's landfall opens the European presence in Mozambique; rivalry follows as colonists move from the coast to effective penetration.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 23: A presença europeia em Moçambique” is right?
a) Vasco da Gama's landfall opens the European presence in Mozambique; rivalry follows as colonists move from the coast to effective penetration.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
