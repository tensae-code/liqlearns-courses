# The Mozambique Company — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A company with sovereign rights runs Manica and Sofala for Portugal and international capital from 1892 to 1942 — the clearest face of the alliance.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 11: A Companhia de Moçambique” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A company with sovereign rights runs Manica and Sofala for Portugal and international capital from 1892 to 1942 — the clearest face of the alliance.
