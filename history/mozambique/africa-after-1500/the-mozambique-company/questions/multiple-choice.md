# The Mozambique Company — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 11: A Companhia de Moçambique”?
a) A company with sovereign rights runs Manica and Sofala for Portugal and international capital from 1892 to 1942 — the clearest face of the alliance.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 11: A Companhia de Moçambique” is right?
a) A company with sovereign rights runs Manica and Sofala for Portugal and international capital from 1892 to 1942 — the clearest face of the alliance.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
