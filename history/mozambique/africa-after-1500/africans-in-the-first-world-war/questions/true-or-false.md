# Africans in the First World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 16: A I Guerra Mundial e a crise económica e social da década de 1920” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The First World War worsens living conditions in Mozambique, and the economic and social crisis of the 1920s follows the war home.
