# Africans in the First World War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 33: O envolvimento dos africanos na I Guerra Mundial”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 16: A I Guerra Mundial e a crise económica e social da década de 1920” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The First World War worsens living conditions in Mozambique, and the economic and social crisis of the 1920s follows the war home.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
