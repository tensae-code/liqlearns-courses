# Africans in the First World War

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 33: O envolvimento dos africanos na I Guerra Mundial; Lição 16: A I Guerra Mundial e a crise económica e social da década de 1920. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 33: O envolvimento dos africanos na I Guerra Mundial

A European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 16: A I Guerra Mundial e a crise económica e social da década de 1920

The First World War worsens living conditions in Mozambique, and the economic and social crisis of the 1920s follows the war home.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
