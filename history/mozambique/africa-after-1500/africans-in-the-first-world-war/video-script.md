# Video script — Africans in the First World War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 33: O envolvimento dos africanos na I Guerra Mundial. A European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 16: A I Guerra Mundial e a crise económica e social da década de 1920. The First World War worsens living conditions in Mozambique, and the economic and social crisis of the 1920s follows the war home.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
