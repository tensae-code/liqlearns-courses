# Theories of the Partition of Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 8: Teorias sobre a partilha de África”?
a) Why did Europe occupy Africa, and why only at the end of the 19th century? Rival theories of the partition are put to the learner.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 8: Teorias sobre a partilha de África” is right?
a) Why did Europe occupy Africa, and why only at the end of the 19th century? Rival theories of the partition are put to the learner.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
