# Colonial Plunder and Unequal Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From the 16th century Europe drains African wealth — goods seized, people trafficked, trades rigged unequal — and the plunder builds European capitalism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 6: A pilhagem colonial e as trocas desiguais” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: From the 16th century Europe drains African wealth — goods seized, people trafficked, trades rigged unequal — and the plunder builds European capitalism.
