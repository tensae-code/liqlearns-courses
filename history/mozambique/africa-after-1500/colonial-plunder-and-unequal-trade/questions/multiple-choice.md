# Colonial Plunder and Unequal Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 6: A pilhagem colonial e as trocas desiguais”?
a) From the 16th century Europe drains African wealth — goods seized, people trafficked, trades rigged unequal — and the plunder builds European capitalism.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: A pilhagem colonial e as trocas desiguais” is right?
a) From the 16th century Europe drains African wealth — goods seized, people trafficked, trades rigged unequal — and the plunder builds European capitalism.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
