# The Making of Mozambique's Borders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 4: O estabelecimento das fronteiras de Moçambique”?
a) Mozambique's borders are drawn through the sponsorship of international capital and Portugal's tactical concessions and alliances with rival colonial powers.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 4: O estabelecimento das fronteiras de Moçambique” is right?
a) Mozambique's borders are drawn through the sponsorship of international capital and Portugal's tactical concessions and alliances with rival colonial powers.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
