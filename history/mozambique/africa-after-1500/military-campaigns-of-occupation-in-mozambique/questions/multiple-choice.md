# Military Campaigns of Occupation in Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 5: As campanhas militares de ocupação de Moçambique”?
a) Lines on a map do not conquer a country; African communities resist the threat to their sovereignty, and Portugal answers with military campaigns.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 5: As campanhas militares de ocupação de Moçambique” is right?
a) Lines on a map do not conquer a country; African communities resist the threat to their sovereignty, and Portugal answers with military campaigns.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
