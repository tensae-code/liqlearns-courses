# Driving Forces of African Nationalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 2: Os grupos motores do nacionalismo africano”?
a) Colonial oppression falls on all Africans, but some groups understand the situation first and turn grievance into ideas and action.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 2: Os grupos motores do nacionalismo africano” is right?
a) Colonial oppression falls on all Africans, but some groups understand the situation first and turn grievance into ideas and action.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
