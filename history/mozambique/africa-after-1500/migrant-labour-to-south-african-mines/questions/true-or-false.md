# Migrant Labour to South African Mines — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the south it is South African mining capital that rules; southern Mozambique's economy is bound to the mines, and its men migrate to work them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 3: A continuação da exportação de mão-de-obra e da dependência em relação ao capital estrangeiro” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Salazar preaches ending dependence on foreign capital, yet the Estado Novo keeps exporting Mozambican labour — the contradiction at the regime's heart.
