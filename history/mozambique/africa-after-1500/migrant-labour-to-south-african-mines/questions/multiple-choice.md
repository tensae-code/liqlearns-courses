# Migrant Labour to South African Mines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 14: O Sul e o trabalho migratório”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) In the south it is South African mining capital that rules; southern Mozambique's economy is bound to the mines, and its men migrate to work them.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 3: A continuação da exportação de mão-de-obra e da dependência em relação ao capital estrangeiro” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Salazar preaches ending dependence on foreign capital, yet the Estado Novo keeps exporting Mozambican labour — the contradiction at the regime's heart.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
