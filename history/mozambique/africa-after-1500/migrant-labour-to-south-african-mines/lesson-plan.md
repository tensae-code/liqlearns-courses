# Lesson plan — Migrant Labour to South African Mines

**Course:** History — Mozambique · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Migrant Labour to South African Mines
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 8 — Portuguese Colonialism in Mozambique and the Road to Liberation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in the south it is South African mining capital that rules; southern Mozambique's economy is bound to the mines, and its men migrate to work them.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 8 — Portuguese Colonialism in Mozambique and the Road to Liberation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Mozambique, Africa and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
