# Migrant Labour to South African Mines

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 14: O Sul e o trabalho migratório; Lição 3: A continuação da exportação de mão-de-obra e da dependência em relação ao capital estrangeiro. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 14: O Sul e o trabalho migratório

In the south it is South African mining capital that rules; southern Mozambique's economy is bound to the mines, and its men migrate to work them.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 3: A continuação da exportação de mão-de-obra e da dependência em relação ao capital estrangeiro

Salazar preaches ending dependence on foreign capital, yet the Estado Novo keeps exporting Mozambican labour — the contradiction at the regime's heart.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
