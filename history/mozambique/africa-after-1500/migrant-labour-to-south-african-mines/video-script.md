# Video script — Migrant Labour to South African Mines

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the south it is South African mining capital that rules; southern Mozambique's economy is bound to the mines, and its men migrate to work them.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 14: O Sul e o trabalho migratório. In the south it is South African mining capital that rules; southern Mozambique's economy is bound to the mines, and its men migrate to work them.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 3: A continuação da exportação de mão-de-obra e da dependência em relação ao capital estrangeiro. Salazar preaches ending dependence on foreign capital, yet the Estado Novo keeps exporting Mozambican labour — the contradiction at the regime's heart.
