# Independent Africa and Its Challenges — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 13: África independente”?
a) 1960 is the crucial year of the decolonisation wave; independence opens a new phase of enormous challenges still unmet today.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 13: África independente” is right?
a) 1960 is the crucial year of the decolonisation wave; independence opens a new phase of enormous challenges still unmet today.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
