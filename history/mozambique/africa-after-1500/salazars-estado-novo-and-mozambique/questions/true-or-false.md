# Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 1: A conjuntura política e económica e os marcos de viragem” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: In the first phase (1890–1930) international companies and South African mining capital, not Portugal, ran Mozambique's economy; 1930–1937 lays the bases of Salazar's economic nationalism.
