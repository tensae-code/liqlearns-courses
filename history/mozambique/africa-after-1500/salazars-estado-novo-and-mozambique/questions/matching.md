# Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lição 12: O Estado Novo de Salazar e a situação nas colónias de Moçambique — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Lição 1: A conjuntura política e económica e os marcos de viragem — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it.
b) In the first phase (1890–1930) international companies and South African mining capital, not Portugal, ran Mozambique's economy; 1930–1937 lays the bases of Salazar's economic nationalism.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
