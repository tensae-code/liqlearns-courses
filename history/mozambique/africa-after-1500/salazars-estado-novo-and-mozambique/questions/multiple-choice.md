# Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 12: O Estado Novo de Salazar e a situação nas colónias de Moçambique”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: A conjuntura política e económica e os marcos de viragem” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) In the first phase (1890–1930) international companies and South African mining capital, not Portugal, ran Mozambique's economy; 1930–1937 lays the bases of Salazar's economic nationalism.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
