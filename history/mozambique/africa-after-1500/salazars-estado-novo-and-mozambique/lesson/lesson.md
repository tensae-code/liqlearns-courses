# Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 12: O Estado Novo de Salazar e a situação nas colónias de Moçambique; Lição 1: A conjuntura política e económica e os marcos de viragem. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 12: O Estado Novo de Salazar e a situação nas colónias de Moçambique

A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 1: A conjuntura política e económica e os marcos de viragem

In the first phase (1890–1930) international companies and South African mining capital, not Portugal, ran Mozambique's economy; 1930–1937 lays the bases of Salazar's economic nationalism.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
