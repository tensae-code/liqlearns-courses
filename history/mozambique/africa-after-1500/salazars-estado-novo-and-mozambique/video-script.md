# Video script — Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 12: O Estado Novo de Salazar e a situação nas colónias de Moçambique. A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 1: A conjuntura política e económica e os marcos de viragem. In the first phase (1890–1930) international companies and South African mining capital, not Portugal, ran Mozambique's economy; 1930–1937 lays the bases of Salazar's economic nationalism.
