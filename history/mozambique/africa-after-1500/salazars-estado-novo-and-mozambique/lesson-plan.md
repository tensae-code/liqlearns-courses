# Lesson plan — Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique

**Course:** History — Mozambique · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Twentieth Century and Mozambique's Road to Freedom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Twentieth Century and Mozambique's Road to Freedom — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Mozambique, Africa and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
