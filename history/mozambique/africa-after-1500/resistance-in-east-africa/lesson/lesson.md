# Resistance in East Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 13: A resistência na África Oriental. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 13: A resistência na África Oriental

Kenya, Tanganyika and Uganda each produce their own moments of resistance as foreign occupation closes in.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
