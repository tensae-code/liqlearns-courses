# Resistance in East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 13: A resistência na África Oriental”?
a) Kenya, Tanganyika and Uganda each produce their own moments of resistance as foreign occupation closes in.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 13: A resistência na África Oriental” is right?
a) Kenya, Tanganyika and Uganda each produce their own moments of resistance as foreign occupation closes in.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
