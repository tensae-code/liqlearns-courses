# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 4: A Conferência de Berlim. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 4: A Conferência de Berlim

Rivalries over Africa threaten open war in the early 1880s; the powers sit down in Berlin to settle the dispute on paper.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Colonisation: The Berlin Conference. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
