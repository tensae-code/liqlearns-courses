**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 4: A Conferência de Berlim”?
a) Rivalries over Africa threaten open war in the early 1880s; the powers sit down in Berlin to settle the dispute on paper.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
