# Africa in the Second World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ruled by the belligerents, African states are made to join the war; thousands of African men are recruited to the fronts for Europe's quarrel.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 10: A participação de África na Segunda Guerra Mundial” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Ruled by the belligerents, African states are made to join the war; thousands of African men are recruited to the fronts for Europe's quarrel.
