# Africa's Socio-Economic Structure in the 18th–19th Centuries — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — before colonial domination Africa has its own socio-economic structure and world relations; the late 18th and early 19th centuries begin to bend it — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
