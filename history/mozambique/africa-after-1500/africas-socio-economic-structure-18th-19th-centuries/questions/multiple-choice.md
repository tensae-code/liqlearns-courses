# Africa's Socio-Economic Structure in the 18th–19th Centuries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 22: A estrutura sócio-económica da África nos séculos XVIII/XIX”?
a) Before colonial domination Africa has its own socio-economic structure and world relations; the late 18th and early 19th centuries begin to bend it.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 22: A estrutura sócio-económica da África nos séculos XVIII/XIX” is right?
a) Before colonial domination Africa has its own socio-economic structure and world relations; the late 18th and early 19th centuries begin to bend it.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
