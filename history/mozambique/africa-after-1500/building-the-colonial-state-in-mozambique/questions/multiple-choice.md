# Building the Colonial State in Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 9: A montagem do Estado colonial”?
a) Alongside conquest, the colonial administration is installed; unable to exploit the colony itself, the colonial state exists to serve international capital.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 9: A montagem do Estado colonial” is right?
a) Alongside conquest, the colonial administration is installed; unable to exploit the colony itself, the colonial state exists to serve international capital.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
