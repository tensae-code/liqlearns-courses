# Building the Colonial State in Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 9: A montagem do Estado colonial. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 9: A montagem do Estado colonial

Alongside conquest, the colonial administration is installed; unable to exploit the colony itself, the colonial state exists to serve international capital.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
