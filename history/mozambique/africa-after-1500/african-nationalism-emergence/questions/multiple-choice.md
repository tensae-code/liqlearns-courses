# African Nationalism Emergence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 1: O nacionalismo africano”?
a) Opposition is born with occupation itself in the late 19th century; nationalism ebbs in the early 20th century but never dies, and returns stronger.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 1: O nacionalismo africano” is right?
a) Opposition is born with occupation itself in the late 19th century; nationalism ebbs in the early 20th century but never dies, and returns stronger.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
