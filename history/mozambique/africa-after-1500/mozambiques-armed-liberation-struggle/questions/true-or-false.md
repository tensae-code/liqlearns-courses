# Mozambique's Armed Liberation Struggle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 9 (armed struggle part): A criação da FRELIMO e o desencadeamento da luta armada” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Faced with Lisbon's refusal to negotiate, the united front launches the armed liberation struggle.
