# Mozambique's Armed Liberation Struggle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 15: A luta armada de libertação nacional”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 9 (armed struggle part): A criação da FRELIMO e o desencadeamento da luta armada” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Faced with Lisbon's refusal to negotiate, the united front launches the armed liberation struggle.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
