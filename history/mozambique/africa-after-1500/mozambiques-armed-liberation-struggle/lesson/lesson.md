# Mozambique's Armed Liberation Struggle

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 15: A luta armada de libertação nacional; Lição 9 (armed struggle part): A criação da FRELIMO e o desencadeamento da luta armada. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 15: A luta armada de libertação nacional

Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 9 (armed struggle part): A criação da FRELIMO e o desencadeamento da luta armada

Faced with Lisbon's refusal to negotiate, the united front launches the armed liberation struggle.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
