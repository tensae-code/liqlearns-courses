# Video script — Mozambique's Armed Liberation Struggle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 15: A luta armada de libertação nacional. Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 9 (armed struggle part): A criação da FRELIMO e o desencadeamento da luta armada. Faced with Lisbon's refusal to negotiate, the united front launches the armed liberation struggle.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
