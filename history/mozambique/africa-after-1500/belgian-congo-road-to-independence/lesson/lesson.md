# Belgian Congo Road to Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 9: A independência do Congo (dominação belga); Lição 10: A independência do Congo (Lumumba). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 9: A independência do Congo (dominação belga)

Belgium stays inflexible before the nationalist movement, making armed struggle the price of Congolese independence; Belgian domination is dissected.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 10: A independência do Congo (Lumumba)

Patrice Lumumba's action carries the Congo to independence in the 1950s surge, even as conflicts split the nationalist movement itself.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
