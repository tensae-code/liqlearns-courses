# Belgian Congo Road to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Belgium stays inflexible before the nationalist movement, making armed struggle the price of Congolese independence; Belgian domination is dissected. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 10: A independência do Congo (Lumumba)” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Patrice Lumumba's action carries the Congo to independence in the 1950s surge, even as conflicts split the nationalist movement itself.
