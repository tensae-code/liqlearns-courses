# Conquest of Central Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 7: A conquista do centro”?
a) In the politically patchwork centre, the conquest is carried out not by the state but by the chartered Mozambique Company.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 7: A conquista do centro” is right?
a) In the politically patchwork centre, the conquest is carried out not by the state but by the chartered Mozambique Company.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
