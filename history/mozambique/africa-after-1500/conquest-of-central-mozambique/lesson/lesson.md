# Conquest of Central Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 7: A conquista do centro. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 7: A conquista do centro

In the politically patchwork centre, the conquest is carried out not by the state but by the chartered Mozambique Company.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in the politically patchwork centre, the conquest is carried out not by the state but by the chartered Mozambique Company. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Conquest of Central Mozambique. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
