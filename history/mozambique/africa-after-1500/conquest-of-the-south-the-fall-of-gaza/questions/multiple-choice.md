# Conquest of the South: The Fall of Gaza — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 6: A conquista militar no sul de Moçambique”?
a) The conquest begins in the south, where the Gaza state stands as the obstacle to colonial rule in the region and the whole country.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: A conquista militar no sul de Moçambique” is right?
a) The conquest begins in the south, where the Gaza state stands as the obstacle to colonial rule in the region and the whole country.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
