# Conquest of the South: The Fall of Gaza

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 6: A conquista militar no sul de Moçambique. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 6: A conquista militar no sul de Moçambique

The conquest begins in the south, where the Gaza state stands as the obstacle to colonial rule in the region and the whole country.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Conquest of the South: The Fall of Gaza.
