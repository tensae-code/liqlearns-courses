# Colonial Social Policy and Racial Discrimination in Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 15: A política social. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 15: A política social

Colonisation remakes Mozambican society — new classes form with the colonial economy, and racial discrimination and wage injustice become everyday facts.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
