# Conquest and Resistance in Northern Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 8: A conquista e resistência no norte”?
a) The north's diversity resists as diversely — Islamised coastal formations, Makua confederations in Nampula, Yao states in Niassa and lineage societies inland.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 8: A conquista e resistência no norte” is right?
a) The north's diversity resists as diversely — Islamised coastal formations, Makua confederations in Nampula, Yao states in Niassa and lineage societies inland.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
