# Conquest and Resistance in Northern Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 8: A conquista e resistência no norte. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 8: A conquista e resistência no norte

The north's diversity resists as diversely — Islamised coastal formations, Makua confederations in Nampula, Yao states in Niassa and lineage societies inland.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
