# Video script — From the Oau to the African Union

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 14: A passagem da OUA para União Africana. The OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union.
