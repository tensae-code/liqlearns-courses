# From the Oau to the African Union

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 14: A passagem da OUA para União Africana. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 14: A passagem da OUA para União Africana

The OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: From the Oau to the African Union. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
