# From the Oau to the African Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 14: A passagem da OUA para União Africana”?
a) The OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 14: A passagem da OUA para União Africana” is right?
a) The OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
