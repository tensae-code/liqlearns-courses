# From the Oau to the African Union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 14: A passagem da OUA para União Africana” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The OAU, born for decolonisation, cannot keep up with Africa's multiplying problems, and the continent refounds its unity as the African Union.
