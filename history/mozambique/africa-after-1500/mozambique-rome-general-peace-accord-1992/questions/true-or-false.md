# Mozambique: The Rome General Peace Accord 1992 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After 16 years of war between the Government and RENAMO, negotiators in Rome finally silence the guns with the General Peace Accord. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 22: A importância da assinatura do Acordo Geral de Paz de Roma” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Peace after 16 years of war — the lesson weighs what the Rome Accord made possible for Mozambique's recovery.
