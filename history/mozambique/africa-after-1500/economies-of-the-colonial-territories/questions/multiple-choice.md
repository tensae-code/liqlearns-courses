# Economies of the Colonial Territories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 7: A economia dos diferentes territórios”?
a) Every colonial administration asks the same question — how to make the colony pay the metropole the highest possible return.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 7: A economia dos diferentes territórios” is right?
a) Every colonial administration asks the same question — how to make the colony pay the metropole the highest possible return.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
