# Video script — Mozambique Independence 1975

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The armed struggle forces the Lusaka Accords and a transition government; on 25 June 1975 independence is proclaimed and the People's Republic is born.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 16: A proclamação da independência nacional e a constituição da República Popular de Moçambique. The armed struggle forces the Lusaka Accords and a transition government; on 25 June 1975 independence is proclaimed and the People's Republic is born.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
