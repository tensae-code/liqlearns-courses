# Mozambique Independence 1975 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The armed struggle forces the Lusaka Accords and a transition government; on 25 June 1975 independence is proclaimed and the People's Republic is born.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 16: A proclamação da independência nacional e a constituição da República Popular de Moçambique” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The armed struggle forces the Lusaka Accords and a transition government; on 25 June 1975 independence is proclaimed and the People's Republic is born.
