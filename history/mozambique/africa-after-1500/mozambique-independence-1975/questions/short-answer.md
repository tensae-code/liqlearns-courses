# Mozambique Independence 1975 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Lição 16: A proclamação da independência nacional e a constituição da República Popular de Moçambique”?
**Answer:** The armed struggle forces the Lusaka Accords and a transition government; on 25 June 1975 independence is proclaimed and the People's Republic is born.
