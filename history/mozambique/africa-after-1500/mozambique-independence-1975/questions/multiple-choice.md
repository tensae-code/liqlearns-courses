# Mozambique Independence 1975 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 16: A proclamação da independência nacional e a constituição da República Popular de Moçambique”?
a) The armed struggle forces the Lusaka Accords and a transition government; on 25 June 1975 independence is proclaimed and the People's Republic is born.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 16: A proclamação da independência nacional e a constituição da República Popular de Moçambique” is right?
a) The armed struggle forces the Lusaka Accords and a transition government; on 25 June 1975 independence is proclaimed and the People's Republic is born.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
