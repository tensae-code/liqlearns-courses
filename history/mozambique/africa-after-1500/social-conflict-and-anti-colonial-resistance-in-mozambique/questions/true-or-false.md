# Social Conflict and Anti-Colonial Resistance in Mozambique — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Oppression breeds contestation — strikes, revolts and other struggles by students, peasants and workers; after WWII nationalism speaks through associations, press and poetry. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 7: Os conflitos sociais e a resistência anti-colonial (organizações)” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: After the war, nationalist organisations multiply — the MJDM youth movement and the NESAM secondary students' nucleus among them.
