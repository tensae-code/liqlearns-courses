# Video script — The Founding of Frelimo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Three exile movements merge into one front — FRELIMO — so a united Mozambican people can fight Portuguese colonialism together.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 14: A fundação da FRELIMO. Three exile movements merge into one front — FRELIMO — so a united Mozambican people can fight Portuguese colonialism together.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 8: A luta anti-colonial. Beyond the PIDE's reach, Mozambican workers in neighbouring countries build the first exile movements — UDENAMO (1960), UNAMI (1961) and MANU (1961) — whose seed becomes FRELIMO.

On the map, follow this part: Lição 9 (founding part): A criação da Frente de Libertação de Moçambique (FRELIMO) e o desencadeamento da luta armada. The nationalist peak is unification — the diverse forces of the early 1960s merge into a single movement, FRELIMO.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
