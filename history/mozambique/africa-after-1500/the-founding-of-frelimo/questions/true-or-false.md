# The Founding of Frelimo — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three exile movements merge into one front — FRELIMO — so a united Mozambican people can fight Portuguese colonialism together. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 8: A luta anti-colonial” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Beyond the PIDE's reach, Mozambican workers in neighbouring countries build the first exile movements — UDENAMO (1960), UNAMI (1961) and MANU (1961) — whose seed becomes FRELIMO.
