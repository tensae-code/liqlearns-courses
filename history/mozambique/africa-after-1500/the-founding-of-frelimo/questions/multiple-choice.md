# The Founding of Frelimo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 14: A fundação da FRELIMO”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Three exile movements merge into one front — FRELIMO — so a united Mozambican people can fight Portuguese colonialism together.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 8: A luta anti-colonial” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Beyond the PIDE's reach, Mozambican workers in neighbouring countries build the first exile movements — UDENAMO (1960), UNAMI (1961) and MANU (1961) — whose seed becomes FRELIMO.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
