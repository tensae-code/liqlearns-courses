# The Founding of Frelimo

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 14: A fundação da FRELIMO; Lição 8: A luta anti-colonial; Lição 9 (founding part): A criação da Frente de Libertação de Moçambique (FRELIMO) e o desencadeamento da luta armada. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 14: A fundação da FRELIMO

Three exile movements merge into one front — FRELIMO — so a united Mozambican people can fight Portuguese colonialism together.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 8: A luta anti-colonial

Beyond the PIDE's reach, Mozambican workers in neighbouring countries build the first exile movements — UDENAMO (1960), UNAMI (1961) and MANU (1961) — whose seed becomes FRELIMO.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lição 9 (founding part): A criação da Frente de Libertação de Moçambique (FRELIMO) e o desencadeamento da luta armada

The nationalist peak is unification — the diverse forces of the early 1960s merge into a single movement, FRELIMO.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
