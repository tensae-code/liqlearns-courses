# Mozambique's Colonial Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 10: A economia colonial: características gerais”?
a) Two-thirds of Mozambique — centre and north — are handed to international capital through the company system; the colonial economy's general features follow.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 10: A economia colonial: características gerais” is right?
a) Two-thirds of Mozambique — centre and north — are handed to international capital through the company system; the colonial economy's general features follow.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
