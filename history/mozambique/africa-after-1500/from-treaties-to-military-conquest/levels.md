# From Treaties to Military Conquest — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — african chiefs sign what they think are trade treaties, then watch European troops move in — the paper road from treaty to military conquest — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
