# From Treaties to Military Conquest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 6: Dos tratados à conquista militar”?
a) African chiefs sign what they think are trade treaties, then watch European troops move in — the paper road from treaty to military conquest.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: Dos tratados à conquista militar” is right?
a) African chiefs sign what they think are trade treaties, then watch European troops move in — the paper road from treaty to military conquest.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
