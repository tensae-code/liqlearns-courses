# From Treaties to Military Conquest — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Lição 6: Dos tratados à conquista militar”?
**Answer:** African chiefs sign what they think are trade treaties, then watch European troops move in — the paper road from treaty to military conquest.
