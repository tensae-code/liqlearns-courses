# From Treaties to Military Conquest — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** African chiefs sign what they think are trade treaties, then watch European troops move in — the paper road from treaty to military conquest.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 6: Dos tratados à conquista militar” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: African chiefs sign what they think are trade treaties, then watch European troops move in — the paper road from treaty to military conquest.
