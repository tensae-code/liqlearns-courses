# European Powers Face African Nationalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 4: As potências europeias diante do movimento nacionalista”?
a) By mid-century nationalism is irreversible; the colonial powers must decide how to face a movement they can no longer stop.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 4: As potências europeias diante do movimento nacionalista” is right?
