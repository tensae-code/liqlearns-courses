# West Africa Independence by Organisation and Negotiation

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 6: O Gana independente. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 6: O Gana independente

In 1957 Ghana becomes the first sub-Saharan country independent; a new government must manage victory, defeated rivals and relations with the old power.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in 1957 Ghana becomes the first sub-Saharan country independent; a new government must manage victory, defeated rivals and relations with the old power. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: West Africa Independence by Organisation and Negotiation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
