# West Africa Independence by Organisation and Negotiation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 6: O Gana independente”?
a) In 1957 Ghana becomes the first sub-Saharan country independent; a new government must manage victory, defeated rivals and relations with the old power.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 6: O Gana independente” is right?
a) In 1957 Ghana becomes the first sub-Saharan country independent; a new government must manage victory, defeated rivals and relations with the old power.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
