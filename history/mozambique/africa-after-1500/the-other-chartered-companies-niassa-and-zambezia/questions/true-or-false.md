# The Other Chartered Companies: Niassa and Zambezia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** North of Manica and Sofala, the Zambezi Valley, Niassa and Cabo Delgado are likewise ceded to chartered companies, Niassa's with sovereign rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Lição 13: As outras companhias” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: North of Manica and Sofala, the Zambezi Valley, Niassa and Cabo Delgado are likewise ceded to chartered companies, Niassa's with sovereign rights.
