# The Legitimate Trade in Palm Oil and Produce — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 7: O comércio do marfim”?
a) Changamire Dombo's 1693 revolt drives the Portuguese to Tete, Sena and Quelimane and pushes the prazos north; gold fades and ivory becomes the most wanted commodity.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 7: O comércio do marfim” is right?
a) Changamire Dombo's 1693 revolt drives the Portuguese to Tete, Sena and Quelimane and pushes the prazos north; gold fades and ivory becomes the most wanted commodity.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
