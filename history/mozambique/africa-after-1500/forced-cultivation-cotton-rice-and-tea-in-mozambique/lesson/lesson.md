# Forced Cultivation: Cotton, Rice and Tea in Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 2: O capital comercial no quadro da agricultura forçada: o caso do algodão, arroz e chá. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 2: O capital comercial no quadro da agricultura forçada: o caso do algodão, arroz e chá

Portugal's textile industry starves for raw cotton; the answer is forced cultivation — Mozambican peasants made to grow cotton, rice and tea for the metropole.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
