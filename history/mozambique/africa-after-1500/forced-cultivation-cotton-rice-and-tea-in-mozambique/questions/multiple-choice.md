# Forced Cultivation: Cotton, Rice and Tea in Mozambique — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 2: O capital comercial no quadro da agricultura forçada: o caso do algodão, arroz e chá”?
a) Portugal's textile industry starves for raw cotton; the answer is forced cultivation — Mozambican peasants made to grow cotton, rice and tea for the metropole.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 2: O capital comercial no quadro da agricultura forçada: o caso do algodão, arroz e chá” is right?
a) Portugal's textile industry starves for raw cotton; the answer is forced cultivation — Mozambican peasants made to grow cotton, rice and tea for the metropole.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
