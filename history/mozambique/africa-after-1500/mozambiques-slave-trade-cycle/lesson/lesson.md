# Mozambique's Slave Trade Cycle

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 8: O ciclo dos escravos (1750/60–séc. XX). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 8: O ciclo dos escravos (1750/60–séc. XX)

After three centuries of trading Mozambique's products, from the mid-18th century the merchants traffic the producer himself — Mozambicans captured, sold and stripped of all rights.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
