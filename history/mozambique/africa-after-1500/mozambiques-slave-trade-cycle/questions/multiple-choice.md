# Mozambique's Slave Trade Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 8: O ciclo dos escravos (1750/60–séc. XX)”?
a) After three centuries of trading Mozambique's products, from the mid-18th century the merchants traffic the producer himself — Mozambicans captured, sold and stripped of all rights.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lição 8: O ciclo dos escravos (1750/60–séc. XX)” is right?
a) After three centuries of trading Mozambique's products, from the mid-18th century the merchants traffic the producer himself — Mozambicans captured, sold and stripped of all rights.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Mozambique's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
