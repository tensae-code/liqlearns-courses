# Mozambique's Slave Trade Cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After three centuries of trading Mozambique's products, from the mid-18th century the merchants traffic the producer himself — Mozambicans captured, sold and stripped of all rights.
