# The First World War: Consequences for Africa and Europe

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 35: As consequências da 1ª Guerra Mundial para África e Europa. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Lição 35: As consequências da 1ª Guerra Mundial para África e Europa

The war leaves indelible marks — development set back and defenceless populations victimised — in Europe and, in its own ways, in Africa.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it — that turn is the lesson.
