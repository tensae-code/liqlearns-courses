# What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lição 8: Impacto e significado do colonialismo em África a nível político”?
a) Fifty years of permanent contact with Europe reshape Africa politically — the lesson weighs colonialism's political impact and meaning.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Mozambican way of its own
