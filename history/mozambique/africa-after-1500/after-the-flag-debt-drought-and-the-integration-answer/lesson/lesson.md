# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer

Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Lição 15: Integração económica de África (SADCC); Lição 16: Integração económica de África (SADC). ## Sub lesson 1 — Lição 15: Integração económica de África (SADCC)

Common strategies are proposed for Africa's economic problems; in Mozambique's own southern region the SADCC is created to coordinate them.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lição 16: Integração económica de África (SADC)

Namibia's 1990 independence and South Africa's transformation outdate the SADCC; it is refounded as SADC to meet the new reality.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
