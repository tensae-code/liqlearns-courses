# Caribbean First Peoples: Fishers and Farmers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the Antilles, sea-going fisher-gatherers and pottery-making farming villagers meet — their ceramics carry the evidence of two worlds touching.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: in the Antilles, sea-going fisher-gatherers and pottery-making farming villagers meet — their ceramics carry the evidence of two worlds touching.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
