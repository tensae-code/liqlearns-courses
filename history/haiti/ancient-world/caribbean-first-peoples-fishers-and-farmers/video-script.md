# Video script — Caribbean First Peoples: Fishers and Farmers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
In the Antilles, sea-going fisher-gatherers and pottery-making farming villagers meet — their ceramics carry the evidence of two worlds touching.

The world story lands in the Antilles. The islands' first peoples were sea-going fisher-gatherers, living from the reef and the shore, moving by canoe. Then farming villagers arrived — agriculteurs-éleveurs with fields, settled villages, and pottery. Two worlds touched on the same shores. How do we know? The ceramics carry the evidence: fired clay survives where wood and fibre rot, and in the shards — their clay, shapes and decorations mixing and changing — archaeologists read the meeting of the fisher-gatherers and the farmers, the first chapter of the Caribbean's own past.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
