# The Taïno of Quisqueya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 2, Un territoire et une société « archipéliques »"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) An island people spread across an archipelago — villages, ball courts and sea paths link Quisqueya's communities long before any European sail appears  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The Taïno spread across an archipelago — villages, ball courts and sea paths linking Quisqueya's communities — ruled by chiefs called caciques, though pupils must test whether the 'caciquat' in the books is Taïno reality or a shape imposed by Spanish writers  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
