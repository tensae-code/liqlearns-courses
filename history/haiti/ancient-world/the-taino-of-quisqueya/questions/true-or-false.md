# The Taïno of Quisqueya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An island people spread across an archipelago — villages, ball courts and sea paths link Quisqueya's communities long before any European sail appears. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: the Taïno spread across an archipelago — villages, ball courts and sea paths linking Quisqueya's communities — ruled by chiefs called caciques, though pupils must test whether the 'caciquat' in the books is Taïno reality or a shape imposed by Spanish writers. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
