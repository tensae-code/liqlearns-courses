# The Taïno of Quisqueya — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: a Taïno chief ruling a village and its people. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** cacique

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: the Taïno name carried for the island the course's story begins on. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Quisqueya
