# Lesson plan — Prehistoric Art: Cave Paintings and Sculpture

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Prehistoric Art: Cave Paintings and Sculpture
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first images humans made of their world — painted cave walls and carved figures — appear among the hunter peoples, and when settled villagers come, they carve sculpture and raise megaliths: settled life invents new images of the unseen world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
