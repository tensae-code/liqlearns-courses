# Prehistoric Art: Cave Paintings and Sculpture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Painted cave walls and carved figures appear — the first images humans made of their world, and a fight to preserve them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: the first images humans made of their world — painted cave walls and carved figures — appear among the hunter peoples, and when settled villagers come, they carve sculpture and raise megaliths: settled life invents new images of the unseen world. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
