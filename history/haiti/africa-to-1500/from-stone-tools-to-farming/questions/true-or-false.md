# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways rather than one simply replacing the other.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways — the farming world grows beside the hunting world, not simply over its grave.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
