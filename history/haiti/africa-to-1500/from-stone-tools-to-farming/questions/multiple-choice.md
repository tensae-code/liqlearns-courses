# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 5, Nomades et sédentaires, cohabitation et hybridation"?
a) Hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways rather than one simply replacing the other  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways — the farming world grows beside the hunting world, not simply over its grave  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
