# Hands-on — From Stone Tools to Farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List everything you ate yesterday. Which parts could a hunter-gatherer find wild, and which only exist because someone farms? This lesson tells how the two ways of life met.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways — the farming world grows beside the hunting world, not simply over its grave. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
