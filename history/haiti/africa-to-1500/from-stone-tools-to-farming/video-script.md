# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways — the farming world grows beside the hunting world, not simply over its grave.

The first farmers did not erase the hunters. For long ages the two worlds lived side by side in the same lands: hunter-gatherers who moved with the herds and the seasons, and sedentary farmer-herders settled by their fields. They met, and meeting changed both — they traded meat for grain, married across the line, borrowed tools, words and ways. The book's word is hybridation: mixing. The road from stone tools to farming is not a replacement but a long neighbourhood, and much of humanity's story since is the two ways still learning from each other.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
