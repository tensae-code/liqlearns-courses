# Lesson plan — From Stone Tools to Farming

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** From Stone Tools to Farming
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways — the farming world grows beside the hunting world, not simply over its grave.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks farming simply replaced hunting everywhere at once → the book teaches cohabitation and mixing: the two worlds lived side by side for ages, trading and borrowing, before farming spread.
- Go back to the one thing: hunter-gatherers and farmer-herders live side by side, trading, mixing and borrowing each other's ways — the farming world grows beside the hunting world, not simply over its grave. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
