# Hands-on — Africa, Cradle of Humankind

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Put one finger on Africa on a map or globe. Now trace a road out of it — across to Asia, up to Europe, far to the Americas. This lesson walks that road with the first modern humans.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Homo sapiens walks out of Africa and across every continent, meeting older human kinds on the way — Africa is where the human story begins. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
