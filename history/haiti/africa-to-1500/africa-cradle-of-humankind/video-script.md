# Video script — Africa, Cradle of Humankind

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Homo sapiens walks out of Africa and across every continent, meeting older human kinds on the way — Africa is where the human story begins.

The human story begins in Africa. There Homo sapiens — our own kind — first walked, and from Africa the road led outward: across into Asia, up into Europe, island by island toward the far Pacific, and at last into the Americas. No continent was empty of wonder on the way: in the new lands, sapiens met older human kinds who had left Africa long before, and the kinds lived side by side for ages before the older ones vanished. One species, one birthplace, every continent: that is the walk this lesson takes.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
