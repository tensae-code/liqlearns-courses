# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 2, Sapiens à la conquête du monde"?
a) Homo sapiens walks out of Africa and across every continent, meeting older human kinds on the way  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Homo sapiens walks out of Africa and across every continent, meeting older human kinds on the way — Africa is where the human story begins  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
