# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Homo sapiens walks out of Africa and across every continent, meeting older human kinds on the way.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Homo sapiens walks out of Africa and across every continent, meeting older human kinds on the way — Africa is where the human story begins.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
