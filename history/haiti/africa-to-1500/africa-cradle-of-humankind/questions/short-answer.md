# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Séance 2, Sapiens à la conquête du monde": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The human story begins in Africa. There Homo sapiens — our own kind — first walked, and from Africa the road led outward: across into Asia, up into Europe, island by island toward the far Pacific, and at last into the Americas. No continent was empty of wonder on the way: in the new lands, sapiens met older human kinds who had left Africa long before, and the kinds lived side by side for ages before the older ones vanished. One species, one birthplace, every continent: that is the walk this lesson takes.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Homo sapiens walks out of Africa and across every continent, meeting older human kinds on the way — Africa is where the human story begins.
