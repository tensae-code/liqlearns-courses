# The French Revolution Breaks Out (1789) — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: the proclaimed rights — men born free and equal — whose words every group in the colony reached for.
**Answer:** rights of man

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: the colony's ranked order: great planters, small whites, free people of colour, and the enslaved.
**Answer:** Saint-Domingue society
