# The French Revolution Breaks Out (1789) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rich, racially ranked Saint-Domingue hears that Paris has proclaimed the rights of man — every group in the colony reaches for the words for itself.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: rich, racially ranked Saint-Domingue hears that Paris has proclaimed the rights of man — and every group in the colony reaches for the words for itself.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
