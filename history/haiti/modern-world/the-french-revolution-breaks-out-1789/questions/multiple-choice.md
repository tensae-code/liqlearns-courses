# The French Revolution Breaks Out (1789) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 1, La société coloniale et la Révolution française"?
a) Rich, racially ranked Saint-Domingue hears that Paris has proclaimed the rights of man — every group in the colony reaches for the words for itself  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Rich, racially ranked Saint-Domingue hears that Paris has proclaimed the rights of man — and every group in the colony reaches for the words for itself  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
