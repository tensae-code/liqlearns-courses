# The US Occupation of Haiti (1915–1934) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Presidents fall one after another, debts press in, and President Vilbrun Guillaume Sam is assassinated on 28 July 1915 — US Marines land the same day. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: nineteen years of occupation judged from Haitian newspapers, memoirs and resistance writing: the Marines land the day President Sam is assassinated in 1915, an imposed convention takes customs, finances and a US-officered gendarmerie, roads are built with forced corvée labour, the Cacos resist under Charlemagne Péralte, collaboration shifts with the years — and the pupils weigh the bilan in 1934. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
