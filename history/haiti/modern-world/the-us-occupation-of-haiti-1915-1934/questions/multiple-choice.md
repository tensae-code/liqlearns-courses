# The US Occupation of Haiti (1915–1934) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 1, Le contexte externe et interne ou les préludes à l'occupation"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Presidents fall one after another, debts press in, and President Vilbrun Guillaume Sam is assassinated on 28 July 1915 — US Marines land the same day  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nineteen years of occupation judged from Haitian newspapers, memoirs and resistance writing: the Marines land the day President Sam is assassinated in 1915, an imposed convention takes customs, finances and a US-officered gendarmerie, roads are built with forced corvée labour, the Cacos resist under Charlemagne Péralte, collaboration shifts with the years — and the pupils weigh the bilan in 1934  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
