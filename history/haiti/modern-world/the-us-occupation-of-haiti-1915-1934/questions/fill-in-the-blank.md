# The US Occupation of Haiti (1915–1934) — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: foreign military rule of a country — here the United States in Haiti, 1915–1934. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** occupation

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: forced unpaid labour — the road gangs of the occupation. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** corvée
