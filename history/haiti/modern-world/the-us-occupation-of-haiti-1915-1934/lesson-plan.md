# Lesson plan — The US Occupation of Haiti (1915–1934)

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The US Occupation of Haiti (1915–1934)
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Building the State, the Debt and the Occupation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** nineteen years of occupation judged from Haitian newspapers, memoirs and resistance writing: the Marines land the day President Sam is assassinated in 1915, an imposed convention takes customs, finances and a US-officered gendarmerie, roads are built with forced corvée labour, the Cacos resist under Charlemagne Péralte, collaboration shifts with the years — and the pupils weigh the bilan in 1934.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Building the State, the Debt and the Occupation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Séance 1, Le contexte externe et interne ou les préludes à l'occupation** — Presidents fall one after another, debts press in, and President Vilbrun Guillaume Sam is assassinated on 28 July 1915 — US Marines land the same day.
2. **Séance 2, La mise sous tutelle de l'État haïtien ?** — A imposed convention hands Washington Haiti's customs, finances and a US-officered gendarmerie — sovereignty survives on paper only.
3. **Séance 3, L'occupation et le processus d'institutionnalisation** — The occupation builds roads with forced corvée labour and a centralised armed force — the machinery pupils are asked to link to Haiti's later authoritarian model.
4. **Séance 4, Les résistances à l'occupation** — Caco guerrillas under leaders like Charlemagne Péralte fight from the hills while writers and nationalists resist in print.
5. **Séance 5, La collaboration avec l'occupant, des relations complexes et changeantes** — Through the era's newspapers, pupils watch elites who served, hedged or turned against the occupier as the years changed the bargain.
6. **Séance 6, Vers un bilan de l'occupation américaine ?** — The Marines leave in 1934; pupils weigh roads and sanitation against humiliation, deaths and a militarised state, and deliver a verdict.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
