# Hands-on — Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take a flag you know and look at its colours one by one. Who chose them, and what story do they tell? This lesson tells how Haiti's flag was made — by tearing one flag apart and sewing a new one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: at Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tore the white from the French flag and sewed a new one; the date, 18 May, became Flag Day, retold every year. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
