# Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: the town where the independence leaders met in May 1803 and made the new flag.
**Answer:** Arcahaie

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: 18 May, the yearly commemoration of the flag's making in 1803.
**Answer:** Flag Day
