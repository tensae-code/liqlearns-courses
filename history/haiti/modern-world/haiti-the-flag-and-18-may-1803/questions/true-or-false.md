# Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tear the white from the French flag and sew a new one; the date becomes Flag Day, retold every year.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: at Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tore the white from the French flag and sewed a new one; the date, 18 May, became Flag Day, retold every year.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
