# Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Study object: the date 18 May": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** In May 1803, while the independence war still raged, the leaders of the coming Haiti met at Arcahaie. There, the story says, they took the French flag — blue, white and red — and tore the white band out of it. The blue and the red that remained were sewn together: the colours of the Black and mixed-race fighters, joined in one flag for one new people. The date of that making, 18 May, never left the calendar: it became Flag Day, and the story of the torn flag is retold every year — a commemoration date that is itself a piece of history, studied in the very first book.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** At Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tore the white from the French flag and sewed a new one; the date, 18 May, became Flag Day, retold every year.
