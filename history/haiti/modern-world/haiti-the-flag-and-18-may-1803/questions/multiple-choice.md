# Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Study object: the date 18 May"?
a) At Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tear the white from the French flag and sew a new one; the date becomes Flag Day, retold every year  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) At Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tore the white from the French flag and sewed a new one; the date, 18 May, became Flag Day, retold every year  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
