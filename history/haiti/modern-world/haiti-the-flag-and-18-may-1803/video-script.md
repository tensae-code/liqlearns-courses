# Video script — Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
At Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tore the white from the French flag and sewed a new one; the date, 18 May, became Flag Day, retold every year.

In May 1803, while the independence war still raged, the leaders of the coming Haiti met at Arcahaie. There, the story says, they took the French flag — blue, white and red — and tore the white band out of it. The blue and the red that remained were sewn together: the colours of the Black and mixed-race fighters, joined in one flag for one new people. The date of that making, 18 May, never left the calendar: it became Flag Day, and the story of the torn flag is retold every year — a commemoration date that is itself a piece of history, studied in the very first book.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
