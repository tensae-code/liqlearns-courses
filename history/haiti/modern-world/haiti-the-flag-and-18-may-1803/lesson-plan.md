# Lesson plan — Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Haiti: The Flag and 18 May 1803
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).

- Thinks the flag was designed quietly long after independence → the book's study object dates it exactly: Arcahaie, May 1803, in the middle of the independence war — the white torn out, the new flag sewn on the spot.
- Go back to the one thing: at Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tore the white from the French flag and sewed a new one; the date, 18 May, became Flag Day, retold every year. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
