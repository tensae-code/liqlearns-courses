# The Haitian Revolution (1791–1804) — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: the Vodou ceremony of August 1791 where, in the founding story, the great rising was sealed by an oath. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bois Caïman

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: the battle of 1803 where Dessalines' army broke the French and opened the road to independence. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Vertières
