# The Haitian Revolution (1791–1804) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 2, Les premières insurrections"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Free people of colour demand their rights and the enslaved begin to rise — the colony cracks before the great August night  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The only slave revolution that founded a state: from the first insurrections and Bois Caïman in August 1791, through Sonthonax's abolition of 1793 and Paris's of 1794, Louverture's rise and his 1801 constitution, to Dessalines' victory at Vertières and independence on 1 January 1804 — told in the book's four acts, with the founding texts mined for who counted as a citizen  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
