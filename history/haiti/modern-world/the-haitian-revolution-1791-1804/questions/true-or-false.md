# The Haitian Revolution (1791–1804) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Free people of colour demand their rights and the enslaved begin to rise — the colony cracks before the great August night. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: the only slave revolution that founded a state: from the first insurrections and Bois Caïman in August 1791, through Sonthonax's abolition of 1793 and Paris's of 1794, Louverture's rise and his 1801 constitution, to Dessalines' victory at Vertières and independence on 1 January 1804 — told in the book's four acts, with the founding texts mined for who counted as a citizen. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
