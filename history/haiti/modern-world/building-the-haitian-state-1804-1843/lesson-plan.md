# Lesson plan — Building the Haitian State (1804–1843)

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Building the Haitian State (1804–1843)
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Building the State, the Debt and the Occupation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** state-building read constitution by constitution: Dessalines' empire and assassination, the 1806 split into Christophe's northern kingdom and Pétion's southern republic, Boyer's reunification of 1820 and the whole island in 1822, and the Rural Code of 1826 hardening the state into authoritarian rule.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Building the State, the Debt and the Occupation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Séance 1, L'État, la société et la déclaration d'indépendance (1er janvier 1804)** — On the ruins of Saint-Domingue, Black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be.
2. **Séance 2, Un État autoritaire, l'empire dessalinien et la crise d'orientation** — Dessalines crowns himself emperor and rules by the sword; his assassination in 1806 leaves the young state without an agreed direction.
3. **Séance 3, Rupture dans la stratégie unitaire de construction de l'État** — The 1806 constitution fight breaks the founding unity — two rival models of organising the state and the community face off.
4. **Séance 4, Un territoire et deux États (1): l'État d'Haïti et le royaume de Christophe** — In the north Henri Christophe builds a kingdom — palace, code and court — betting that discipline and grandeur will earn respect.
5. **Séance 5, Un territoire et deux États (2): l'État de l'ouest et du sud, la République d'Haïti sous Pétion** — In the west and south Pétion's republic takes the opposite bet — land to soldiers, a senate, and help for Bolívar's independence fight.
6. **Séance 6, La réunification de la République d'Haïti** — After Christophe's fall in 1820, Boyer reunites north and republic — one Haiti again, soon stretched over the whole island (1822).
7. **Séance 8, Le code rural de 1826 et la consolidation de l'État autoritaire** — Boyer's Rural Code tries to chain peasants back to plantation work — the state wins on paper, the countryside resists in fact.
