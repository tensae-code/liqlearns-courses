# Building the Haitian State (1804–1843) — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Séance 1, L'État, la société et la déclaration d'indépendance (1er janvier 1804)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: On the ruins of Saint-Domingue, Black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Séance 2, Un État autoritaire, l'empire dessalinien et la crise d'orientation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Dessalines crowns himself emperor and rules by the sword; his assassination in 1806 leaves the young state without an agreed direction — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Séance 3, Rupture dans la stratégie unitaire de construction de l'État
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The 1806 constitution fight breaks the founding unity — two rival models of organising the state and the community face off — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Séance 4, Un territoire et deux États (1): l'État d'Haïti et le royaume de Christophe
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In the north Henri Christophe builds a kingdom — palace, code and court — betting that discipline and grandeur will earn respect — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Séance 5, Un territoire et deux États (2): l'État de l'ouest et du sud, la République d'Haïti sous Pétion
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In the west and south Pétion's republic takes the opposite bet — land to soldiers, a senate, and help for Bolívar's independence fight — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Séance 6, La réunification de la République d'Haïti
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: After Christophe's fall in 1820, Boyer reunites north and republic — one Haiti again, soon stretched over the whole island (1822) — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — Séance 8, Le code rural de 1826 et la consolidation de l'État autoritaire
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Boyer's Rural Code tries to chain peasants back to plantation work — the state wins on paper, the countryside resists in fact — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
