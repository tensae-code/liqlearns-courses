# Building the Haitian State (1804–1843) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On the ruins of Saint-Domingue, Black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: state-building read constitution by constitution: Dessalines' empire and assassination, the 1806 split into Christophe's northern kingdom and Pétion's southern republic, Boyer's reunification of 1820 and the whole island in 1822, and the Rural Code of 1826 hardening the state into authoritarian rule. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
