# Building the Haitian State (1804–1843) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 1, L'État, la société et la déclaration d'indépendance (1er janvier 1804)"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) On the ruins of Saint-Domingue, Black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) State-building read constitution by constitution: Dessalines' empire and assassination, the 1806 split into Christophe's northern kingdom and Pétion's southern republic, Boyer's reunification of 1820 and the whole island in 1822, and the Rural Code of 1826 hardening the state into authoritarian rule  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
