# Haiti's Forts Built to Defend Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A free Black state, fearing the slave powers would return, rings itself with forts — Fort Jacques, the Citadelle, Fort Décidé — whose faces still stare out from Haiti's banknotes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: a free Black state, fearing the slave powers would return, ringed itself with forts — Fort Jacques, the Citadelle, Fort Décidé — whose faces still stare out from Haiti's banknotes.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
