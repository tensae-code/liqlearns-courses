# Hands-on — Haiti's Forts Built to Defend Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your town has just won its freedom and the old masters might come back by sea. Where would you build, and what? This lesson looks at the forts a free Haiti built to guard its independence.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a free Black state, fearing the slave powers would return, ringed itself with forts — Fort Jacques, the Citadelle, Fort Décidé — whose faces still stare out from Haiti's banknotes. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
