# Lesson plan — Haiti's Forts Built to Defend Independence

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Haiti's Forts Built to Defend Independence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a free Black state, fearing the slave powers would return, ringed itself with forts — Fort Jacques, the Citadelle, Fort Décidé — whose faces still stare out from Haiti's banknotes.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the forts were built by the colonisers → the book's study object is the opposite: the forts were raised by free Haiti itself, after independence, against the feared return of the slave powers.
- Go back to the one thing: a free Black state, fearing the slave powers would return, ringed itself with forts — Fort Jacques, the Citadelle, Fort Décidé — whose faces still stare out from Haiti's banknotes. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
