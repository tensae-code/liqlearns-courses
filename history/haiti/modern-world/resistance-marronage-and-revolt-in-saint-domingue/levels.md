# Resistance, Marronage and Revolt in Saint-Domingue — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Séance 1, Résister et marronner
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Starting from Albert Mangonès' statue of the Unknown Maroon, pupils trace everyday resistance and flight — the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Séance 2, De la vision des maîtres à l'analyse des historiens
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The same revolt is read twice — in the masters' frightened gazettes and in historians' reconstructions — and the two stories do not match — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Séance 3, Révoltes et sociétés marronnes
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Named risings — Quartier-Morin 1697, Maribaroux 1720, Makandal's poison network in the 1750s — grow into maroon communities that negotiate, raid and survive — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Séance 4, Le vaudou et le syncrétisme, moyens et formes de la résistance
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Banned again and again by law from the Code Noir onward, Vodou gatherings persist — the repeated bans themselves prove the faith was a meeting-place of resistance — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
