# Resistance, Marronage and Revolt in Saint-Domingue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 1, Résister et marronner"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Starting from Albert Mangonès' statue of the Unknown Maroon, pupils trace everyday resistance and flight — the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Resistance never stopped: everyday defiance and flight built maroon counter-societies, named risings — Padrejean (1679), Quartier-Morin 1697, Maribaroux 1720, Makandal's poison network — shook the colony, and banned Vodou gatherings persisted as a meeting-place of resistance, recovered name by name because the enslaved left almost no writings  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
