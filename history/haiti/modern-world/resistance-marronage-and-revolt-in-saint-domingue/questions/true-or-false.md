# Resistance, Marronage and Revolt in Saint-Domingue — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Starting from Albert Mangonès' statue of the Unknown Maroon, pupils trace everyday resistance and flight — the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: resistance never stopped: everyday defiance and flight built maroon counter-societies, named risings — Padrejean (1679), Quartier-Morin 1697, Maribaroux 1720, Makandal's poison network — shook the colony, and banned Vodou gatherings persisted as a meeting-place of resistance, recovered name by name because the enslaved left almost no writings. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
