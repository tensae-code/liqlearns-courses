# Lesson plan — Resistance, Marronage and Revolt in Saint-Domingue

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Resistance, Marronage and Revolt in Saint-Domingue
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Plantation, Resistance and Revolution (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** resistance never stopped: everyday defiance and flight built maroon counter-societies, named risings — Padrejean (1679), Quartier-Morin 1697, Maribaroux 1720, Makandal's poison network — shook the colony, and banned Vodou gatherings persisted as a meeting-place of resistance, recovered name by name because the enslaved left almost no writings.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Plantation, Resistance and Revolution — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Séance 1, Résister et marronner** — Starting from Albert Mangonès' statue of the Unknown Maroon, pupils trace everyday resistance and flight — the maroon who runs becomes a whole hidden counter-society.
2. **Séance 2, De la vision des maîtres à l'analyse des historiens** — The same revolt is read twice — in the masters' frightened gazettes and in historians' reconstructions — and the two stories do not match.
3. **Séance 3, Révoltes et sociétés marronnes** — Named risings — Quartier-Morin 1697, Maribaroux 1720, Makandal's poison network in the 1750s — grow into maroon communities that negotiate, raid and survive.
4. **Séance 4, Le vaudou et le syncrétisme, moyens et formes de la résistance** — Banned again and again by law from the Code Noir onward, Vodou gatherings persist — the repeated bans themselves prove the faith was a meeting-place of resistance.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
