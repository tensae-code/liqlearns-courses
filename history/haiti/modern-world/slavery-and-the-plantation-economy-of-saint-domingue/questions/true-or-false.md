# Slavery and the Plantation Economy of Saint-Domingue — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europeans seize the island, clear it and rebuild it as sugar and coffee plantations worked by Africans — a chronology told through local remains. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Europeans seized the island and rebuilt it as sugar and coffee plantations worked by enslaved Africans — a system read from the ground up: its archaeology, the enslaved day rebuilt from engravings and texts, the enslaved people's own voice hunted behind masters' distortions, and its story retold by literature, with the book itself warning that 'plantation' may really mean a slave-labour camp. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
