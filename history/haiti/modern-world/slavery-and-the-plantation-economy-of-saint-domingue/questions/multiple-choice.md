# Slavery and the Plantation Economy of Saint-Domingue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 1, De la conquête et de la colonisation européennes à l'économie de plantation"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Europeans seize the island, clear it and rebuild it as sugar and coffee plantations worked by Africans — a chronology told through local remains  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Europeans seized the island and rebuilt it as sugar and coffee plantations worked by enslaved Africans — a system read from the ground up: its archaeology, the enslaved day rebuilt from engravings and texts, the enslaved people's own voice hunted behind masters' distortions, and its story retold by literature, with the book itself warning that 'plantation' may really mean a slave-labour camp  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
