# Haiti's Independence Debt: Neocolonialism by Debt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France offers recognition at a price — the 1825 ordinance turns independence itself into a bill Haiti must pay. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: France sold recognition at a price: the 1825 ordinance turned independence itself into a bill — an indemnity to former slave-owners that became a 'double debt' as Haiti borrowed from French banks to pay it, draining the treasury for decades; in 2003 President Aristide demanded the money back, and the argument is alive today. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
