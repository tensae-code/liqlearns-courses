# Haiti's Independence Debt: Neocolonialism by Debt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: the sum France demanded in 1825 as the price of recognising Haiti's independence, paid to former slave-owners. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** indemnity

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: owing the indemnity and, at the same time, the bank loans taken to pay it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** double debt
