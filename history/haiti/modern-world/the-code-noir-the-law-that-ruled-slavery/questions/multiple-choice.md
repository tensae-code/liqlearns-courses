# The Code Noir: The Law That Ruled Slavery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 4, Travail servile et conditions de vie des esclaves dans les plantations, le Code noir (2)"?
a) The Code Noir's articles are read line by line — the law that called people property, and the gap between its paper protections and plantation reality  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) The Code Noir's articles, read line by line, made the law that called people property — and the gap between its paper protections and plantation reality is the lesson  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
