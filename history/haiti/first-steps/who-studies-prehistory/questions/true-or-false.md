# Who Studies Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bones and stone tools found together force a question — which hominids made them, and how can a dig's layers tell?.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: prehistory is studied from what the ground keeps: bones and stone tools found together force the question of which hominids made them, and a dig's layers — stratigraphy — tell the order of the past.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
