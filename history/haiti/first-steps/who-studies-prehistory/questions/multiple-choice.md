# Who Studies Prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 1, Des fossiles, des femmes, des hommes et des outils"?
a) Bones and stone tools found together force a question — which hominids made them, and how can a dig's layers tell?  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Prehistory is studied from what the ground keeps: bones and stone tools found together force the question of which hominids made them, and a dig's layers — stratigraphy — tell the order of the past  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
