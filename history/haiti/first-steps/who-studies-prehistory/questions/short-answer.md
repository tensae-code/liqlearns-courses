# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Séance 1, Des fossiles, des femmes, des hommes et des outils": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The prehistory book opens inside a dig. In the earth lie fossil bones — women, men, early human kinds — and beside them, stone tools. Found together, they force a sharp question: which hominids made these tools? The answer comes from the ground itself: a dig is dug in layers, and stratigraphy reads them — the deeper the layer, the older what lies in it. Bones and tools in the same layer belong to the same time. This is who studies prehistory: scientists who make the earth give evidence in order, layer by layer, until the time before writing can be told.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Prehistory is studied from what the ground keeps: bones and stone tools found together force the question of which hominids made them, and a dig's layers — stratigraphy — tell the order of the past.
