# Video script — Who Studies Prehistory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Prehistory is studied from what the ground keeps: bones and stone tools found together force the question of which hominids made them, and a dig's layers — stratigraphy — tell the order of the past.

The prehistory book opens inside a dig. In the earth lie fossil bones — women, men, early human kinds — and beside them, stone tools. Found together, they force a sharp question: which hominids made these tools? The answer comes from the ground itself: a dig is dug in layers, and stratigraphy reads them — the deeper the layer, the older what lies in it. Bones and tools in the same layer belong to the same time. This is who studies prehistory: scientists who make the earth give evidence in order, layer by layer, until the time before writing can be told.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
