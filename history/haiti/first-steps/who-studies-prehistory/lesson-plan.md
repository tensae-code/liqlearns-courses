# Lesson plan — Who Studies Prehistory

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Who Studies Prehistory
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** prehistory is studied from what the ground keeps: bones and stone tools found together force the question of which hominids made them, and a dig's layers — stratigraphy — tell the order of the past.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
