# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils trace how their object is used today — in exhibitions, commemorations, money and images — and build a public presentation of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: history lives where you live: an everyday object like a coffee mill can be read as a witness of past work and tastes, and the past is still at work today in exhibitions, commemorations, money and images. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
