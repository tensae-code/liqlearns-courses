# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 4, Les usages de l'histoire"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Pupils trace how their object is used today — in exhibitions, commemorations, money and images — and build a public presentation of it  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) History lives where you live: an everyday object like a coffee mill can be read as a witness of past work and tastes, and the past is still at work today in exhibitions, commemorations, money and images  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
