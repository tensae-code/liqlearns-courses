# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils hunt, gather and sort documents until the pile of traces starts answering their question. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: a written history is built, not found: documents are hunted, gathered and sorted into a corpus, then re-read and argued over until they can carry an interpretation the historian presents in public. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
