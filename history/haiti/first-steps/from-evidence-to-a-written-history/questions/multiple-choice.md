# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Faire de l'histoire au quotidien (2): research, collection and use of a document corpus"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Pupils hunt, gather and sort documents until the pile of traces starts answering their question  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) A written history is built, not found: documents are hunted, gathered and sorted into a corpus, then re-read and argued over until they can carry an interpretation the historian presents in public  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
