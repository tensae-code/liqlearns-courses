# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Queen Anacaona and the Taïno past are fought over in novels, comics and museums today — whose heritage is it, and who gets to show it?. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: heritage is the past a society chooses to keep and argue over — Queen Anacaona and the Taïno past fought over in novels, comics and museums, and slavery remembered through statues, commemorations and laws: whose heritage it is, and who gets to show it, is itself a historical question. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
