# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 4, Un patrimoine et des images en débat"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Queen Anacaona and the Taïno past are fought over in novels, comics and museums today — whose heritage is it, and who gets to show it?  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Heritage is the past a society chooses to keep and argue over — Queen Anacaona and the Taïno past fought over in novels, comics and museums, and slavery remembered through statues, commemorations and laws: whose heritage it is, and who gets to show it, is itself a historical question  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
