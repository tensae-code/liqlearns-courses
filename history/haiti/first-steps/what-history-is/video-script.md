# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
History in Haiti's first book does not begin with dates: it begins near home, with one building, one everyday object and one commemoration date, and turns the curiosity they wake into a real historical question.

Haiti's first history book does not open with kings or dates. It opens near home: one building the pupils pass every day, one everyday object, one commemoration date on the calendar. The class looks at the three and lets curiosity work — who made this, when, why is this day remembered? Then comes the historian's move: the loose curiosity is sharpened into a problématique, one clear question that research can actually answer. A question that can be answered is the door into history; everything else in the book walks through it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
