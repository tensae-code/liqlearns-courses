# Lesson plan — What History Is

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What History Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** history in Haiti's first book does not begin with dates: it begins near home, with one building, one everyday object and one commemoration date, and turns the curiosity they wake into a real historical question.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: history in Haiti's first book does not begin with dates: it begins near home, with one building, one everyday object and one commemoration date, and turns the curiosity they wake into a real historical question.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the Haiti they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Haiti and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
