# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Faire de l'histoire au quotidien (1): posing the question and building a problématique": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Haiti's first history book does not open with kings or dates. It opens near home: one building the pupils pass every day, one everyday object, one commemoration date on the calendar. The class looks at the three and lets curiosity work — who made this, when, why is this day remembered? Then comes the historian's move: the loose curiosity is sharpened into a problématique, one clear question that research can actually answer. A question that can be answered is the door into history; everything else in the book walks through it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** History in Haiti's first book does not begin with dates: it begins near home, with one building, one everyday object and one commemoration date, and turns the curiosity they wake into a real historical question.
