# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils face a local building, object and date and turn curiosity into a real historical question.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: history in Haiti's first book does not begin with dates: it begins near home, with one building, one everyday object and one commemoration date, and turns the curiosity they wake into a real historical question.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
