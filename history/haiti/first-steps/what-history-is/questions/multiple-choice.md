# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Faire de l'histoire au quotidien (1): posing the question and building a problématique"?
a) Pupils face a local building, object and date and turn curiosity into a real historical question  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) History in Haiti's first book does not begin with dates: it begins near home, with one building, one everyday object and one commemoration date, and turns the curiosity they wake into a real historical question  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
