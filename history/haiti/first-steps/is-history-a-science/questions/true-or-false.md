# Is History a Science? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils take apart a docu-fiction film and sort what science knows from what the film invents. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: history is tested like a science: pupils take apart docu-fiction films and sort what research knows from what the film invents — and judge again, at the sequence's end, how research becomes a story told to the public. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
