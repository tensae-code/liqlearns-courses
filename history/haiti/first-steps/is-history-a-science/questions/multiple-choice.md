# Is History a Science? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Séance 4, De la recherche scientifique à la vulgarisation (1)"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Pupils take apart a docu-fiction film and sort what science knows from what the film invents  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) History is tested like a science: pupils take apart docu-fiction films and sort what research knows from what the film invents — and judge again, at the sequence's end, how research becomes a story told to the public  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Haiti's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
