# Is History a Science? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Séance 4, De la recherche scientifique à la vulgarisation (1)": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The prehistory sequence turns its own method on the screen. The class watches a docu-fiction — a film that dresses research in invented scenes — and takes it apart. This fire, these clothes, these words spoken around the hearth: which of them does science actually know from bones, tools and layers, and which did the filmmakers invent to make a story? Sorting the two is the historian's habit carried into the cinema: never let a good story smuggle in what the evidence never said.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** History is tested like a science: pupils take apart docu-fiction films and sort what research knows from what the film invents — and judge again, at the sequence's end, how research becomes a story told to the public.
