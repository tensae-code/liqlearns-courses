# Lesson plan — Is History a Science?

**Course:** History — Haiti · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Is History a Science?
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** history is tested like a science: pupils take apart docu-fiction films and sort what research knows from what the film invents — and judge again, at the sequence's end, how research becomes a story told to the public.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Historian's Craft and the First Peoples — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

- Takes a well-made film as the past itself → the book dissects docu-fiction: research gives knowledge, film adds invention — the pupil's job is to sort the two and say which is which.
- Go back to the one thing: history is tested like a science: pupils take apart docu-fiction films and sort what research knows from what the film invents — and judge again, at the sequence's end, how research becomes a story told to the public.
