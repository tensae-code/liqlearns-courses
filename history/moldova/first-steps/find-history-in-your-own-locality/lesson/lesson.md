# Find history in your own locality

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Find history in your own locality" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 6: Localitatea mea

Ionel traces his own locality's name and past; Chișinău, first mentioned in 1436 and named for the Chișla Nouă spring, is the worked example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tema 26: Activități și produse de consolidare a competențelor

Pupils write a page of their own locality's history, collect its photographs and read its monuments as storytellers.

## Sub lesson 3 — Activități de învățare pe bază de proiect: Vizită tematică la Muzeul Național de Istorie a Moldovei

The class project sends pupils into the National History Museum to gather, keep, research and exhibit objects — the museum as the locality's memory house.

## Sub lesson 4 — Activități de învățare pe bază de proiect

Three pupil projects — "History is beside you, know it!", a study of the native locality, and an expedition "In the footsteps of our ancestors"

## Sub lesson 5 — Unitatea 5: Activități de învățare în bază de proiect — vestigiile patrimoniului local

Pupils study "the past beside us" — observe a local vestige, analyse a monument of the modern age and walk an excursion to the locality's places of worship.

## Sub lesson 6 — Activități de învățare în bază de proiect

Two local projects — the role of women in the locality's public life, debated for gender equality, and a poster or school-newspaper article on a local personality.

## Sub lesson 7 — Activităţi de învăţare în bază de proiect

Four local projects — the role of women in contemporary history, testimonies of the locality's own 1992 war veterans, the diaspora and its role, and the Soviet-Afghan war's mark on Moldavian veterans.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Find history in your own locality as Moldova's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's story in your own words.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the story happens in real life. Note it.
