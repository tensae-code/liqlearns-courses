# Find history in your own locality — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 6: Localitatea mea" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ionel traces his own locality's name and past; Chișinău, first mentioned in 1436 and named for the Chișla Nouă spring, is the worked example.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 26: Activități și produse de consolidare a competențelor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils write a page of their own locality's history, collect its photographs and read its monuments as storytellers.
