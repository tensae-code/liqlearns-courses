# Find history in your own locality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 6: Localitatea mea" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ionel traces his own locality's name and past; Chișinău, first mentioned in 1436 and named for the Chișla Nouă spring, is the worked example.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 26: Activități și produse de consolidare a competențelor" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils write a page of their own locality's history, collect its photographs and read its monuments as storytellers.
