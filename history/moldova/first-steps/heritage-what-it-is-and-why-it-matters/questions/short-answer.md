# Heritage what it is and why it matters — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 21: Biserici și mănăstiri – patrimoniu cultural" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Churches and monasteries dot the land as kept heritage; master Manole's legend says the builder of Curtea de Argeș could not come down from his finished monastery.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 23: Obiceiuri și tradiții strămoșești" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Customs, tales, music and poetry are the people's archives; hand-woven carpets carry centuries of patterns into today's homes.
