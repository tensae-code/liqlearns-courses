# Heritage what it is and why it matters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 21: Biserici și mănăstiri – patrimoniu cultural" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Churches and monasteries dot the land as kept heritage; master Manole's legend says the builder of Curtea de Argeș could not come down from his finished monastery.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 23: Obiceiuri și tradiții strămoșești" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Customs, tales, music and poetry are the people's archives; hand-woven carpets carry centuries of patterns into today's homes.
