Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 21: Biserici și mănăstiri – patrimoniu cultural", churches and monasteries dot the land as kept heritage; master Manole's legend says the builder of Curtea de Argeș could not come down from his finished monastery (Sub lesson 1)
