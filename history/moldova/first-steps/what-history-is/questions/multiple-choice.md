# What history is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Tema 1: Istoria – învățătoarea vieții"?
a) Pupil Dragoș asks Clio what history is; she answers it is the true story of humankind's road, and Herodotus is named its father.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
