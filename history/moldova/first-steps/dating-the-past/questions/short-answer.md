# Dating the past — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 3: Timpul și spațiul în istorie" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Marin's grandfather's old coins open the lesson on chronology — years, decades, centuries, millennia, the timeline and the historical map.

**2.** Tell the story of "Introducere: cronologia și spațiul istoric" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils learn to count years BC and AD and to read law codes — Hammurabi, Manu, the Twelve Tables, Dacia's Belagines — as dated evidence in a place.
