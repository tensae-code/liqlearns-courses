# Video script — Dating the past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Dating the past.

Marin's grandfather's old coins open the lesson on chronology — years, decades, centuries, millennia, the timeline and the historical map.

Pupils learn to count years BC and AD and to read law codes — Hammurabi, Manu, the Twelve Tables, Dacia's Belagines — as dated evidence in a place.

The calendar itself has a history — its name from the Latin calendae — as peoples learn to cut time into years, months and days by the sky.

Medieval people order prayer, markets and war by bells, calendars and feast days — measuring time is itself a historical skill.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
