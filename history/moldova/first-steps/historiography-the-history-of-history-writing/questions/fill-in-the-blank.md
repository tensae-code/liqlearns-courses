# Historiography the history of history writing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Extindere II: Marii cronicari ai Țării Moldovei" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Grigore Ureche first writes the chronicle in the ancestors' own language — "from Rome we descend" — and Miron Costin carries the story on.
