# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 2: Izvoarele istorice" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Doina learns the past is rebuilt from written and unwritten sources — tools, coins, chronicles — kept in museums, libraries and archives.

**2.** Tell the story of "Introducere: izvoare scrise și nescrise" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** History begins where writing begins (~3000 BC); before that, unwritten traces and auxiliary sciences rebuild the primitive community's image.
