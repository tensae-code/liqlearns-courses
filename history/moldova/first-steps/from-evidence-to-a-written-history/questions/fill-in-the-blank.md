# From evidence to a written history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 2: Izvoarele istorice" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Doina learns the past is rebuilt from written and unwritten sources — tools, coins, chronicles — kept in museums, libraries and archives.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Introducere: izvoare scrise și nescrise" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** History begins where writing begins (~3000 BC); before that, unwritten traces and auxiliary sciences rebuild the primitive community's image.
