# From evidence to a written history

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "From evidence to a written history" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 2: Izvoarele istorice

Doina learns the past is rebuilt from written and unwritten sources — tools, coins, chronicles — kept in museums, libraries and archives.

## Sub lesson 2 — Introducere: izvoare scrise și nescrise

History begins where writing begins (~3000 BC); before that, unwritten traces and auxiliary sciences rebuild the primitive community's image.
