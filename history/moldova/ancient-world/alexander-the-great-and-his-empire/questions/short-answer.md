# Alexander the great and his empire — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 5: Afirmarea Macedoniei. Imperiul lui Alexandru cel Mare" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Peloponnesian War exhausts the poleis, Macedon rises, and Alexander crosses to Asia in 334 BC, dying at Babylon in 323 with plans for Arabia unfulfilled.
