# Alexander the great and his empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 5: Afirmarea Macedoniei. Imperiul lui Alexandru cel Mare" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Peloponnesian War exhausts the poleis, Macedon rises, and Alexander crosses to Asia in 334 BC, dying at Babylon in 323 with plans for Arabia unfulfilled.
