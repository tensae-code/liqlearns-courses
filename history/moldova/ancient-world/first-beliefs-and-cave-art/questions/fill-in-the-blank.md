# First beliefs and cave art — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 4: Apariția credințelor și artei" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** People who cannot explain nature pray, sacrifice and paint — the Mother Goddess cult, and 15,000 cave images at Lascaux and Altamira turn walls into prayers for the hunt.
