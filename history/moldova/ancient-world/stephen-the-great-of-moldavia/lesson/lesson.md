# Stephen the great of moldavia

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Stephen the great of moldavia" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare

Stephen the Great rules 47 years, wins battle after battle against the Ottomans and raises a church after each victory.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare

Caught between Hungary, Poland and the Ottomans, Stephen wins at Vaslui in 1475, calls Moldavia "the gate of Christendom" and begs Europe's monarchs for help that barely comes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Stephen the great of moldavia as Moldova's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
