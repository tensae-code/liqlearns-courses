# Stephen the great of moldavia — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Stephen the Great rules 47 years, wins battle after battle against the Ottomans and raises a church after each victory.
b) Caught between Hungary, Poland and the Ottomans, Stephen wins at Vaslui in 1475, calls Moldavia "the gate of Christendom" and begs Europe's monarchs for help that barely comes.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
