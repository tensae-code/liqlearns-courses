# Stephen the great of moldavia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare", Stephen the Great rules 47 years, wins battle after battle against the Ottomans and raises a church after each victory (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare", caught between Hungary, Poland and the Ottomans, Stephen wins at Vaslui in 1475, calls Moldavia "the gate of Christendom" and begs Europe's monarchs for help that barely comes (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
