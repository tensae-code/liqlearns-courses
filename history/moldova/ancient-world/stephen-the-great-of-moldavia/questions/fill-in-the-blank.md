# Stephen the great of moldavia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Stephen the Great rules 47 years, wins battle after battle against the Ottomans and raises a church after each victory.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Caught between Hungary, Poland and the Ottomans, Stephen wins at Vaslui in 1475, calls Moldavia "the gate of Christendom" and begs Europe's monarchs for help that barely comes.
