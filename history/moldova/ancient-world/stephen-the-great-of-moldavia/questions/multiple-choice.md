# Stephen the great of moldavia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Stephen the Great rules 47 years, wins battle after battle against the Ottomans and raises a church after each victory.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Caught between Hungary, Poland and the Ottomans, Stephen wins at Vaslui in 1475, calls Moldavia "the gate of Christendom" and begs Europe's monarchs for help that barely comes.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
