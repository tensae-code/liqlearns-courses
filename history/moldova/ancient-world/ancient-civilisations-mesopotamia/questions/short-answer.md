# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 3: Orașe-state, regate și imperii (Mesopotamia, Babilonul, Egiptul)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Sargon's Akkad becomes the first empire and falls to revolt; Babylon flowers again under Nebuchadnezzar II, while Akhenaten tries to make Egypt worship one god.
