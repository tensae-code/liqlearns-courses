# Carolingian renaissance and byzantine culture — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 8: Cultura în Europa Occidentală și Bizanț" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Monastery schools teach writing on wax tablets; Alcuin teaches Charlemagne's children the seven liberal arts, and Justinian's Latin law code becomes Europe's legal model.
