# Barbarian peoples and the fall of rome — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea VII, Lecția 2: Popoarele barbare și căderea lumii antice" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Attila, "the scourge of God", terrifies two empires; in the West nine puppet emperors pass in twenty years until 476 ends the ancient world.
