# Ancient civilisations rome — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea V, Lecția 2: Întemeierea Romei – istorie și legendă. Roma regală" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Romulus and the she-wolf found Rome in legend; seven kings rule 753–509 BC before the city expels kingship — a copy of the Capitoline Wolf stands in Chișinău.
