# Burebista unifier of the geto dacians — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Burebista gathers the scattered Geto-Dacian tribes into one kingdom, the first unification in the ancestors' story.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 4: Dacia pe timpul lui Burebista" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Burebista unifies the quarrelling tribes by war and diplomacy, treats with Pompey against Caesar, and escapes punishment only by Caesar's assassination.
