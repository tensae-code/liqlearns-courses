# Burebista unifier of the geto dacians — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Burebista gathers the scattered Geto-Dacian tribes into one kingdom, the first unification in the ancestors' story.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 4: Dacia pe timpul lui Burebista" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Burebista unifies the quarrelling tribes by war and diplomacy, treats with Pompey against Caesar, and escapes punishment only by Caesar's assassination.
