# Burebista unifier of the geto dacians — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor", Burebista gathers the scattered Geto-Dacian tribes into one kingdom, the first unification in the ancestors' story (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unitatea IV, Lecția 4: Dacia pe timpul lui Burebista", Burebista unifies the quarrelling tribes by war and diplomacy, treats with Pompey against Caesar, and escapes punishment only by Caesar's assassination (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
