# Burebista unifier of the geto dacians — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unitatea IV, Lecția 4: Dacia pe timpul lui Burebista — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Burebista gathers the scattered Geto-Dacian tribes into one kingdom, the first unification in the ancestors' story.
b) Burebista unifies the quarrelling tribes by war and diplomacy, treats with Pompey against Caesar, and escapes punishment only by Caesar's assassination.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
