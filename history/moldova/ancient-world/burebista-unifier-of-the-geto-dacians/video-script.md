# Video script — Burebista unifier of the geto dacians

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Burebista unifier of the geto dacians.

Burebista gathers the scattered Geto-Dacian tribes into one kingdom, the first unification in the ancestors' story.

Burebista unifies the quarrelling tribes by war and diplomacy, treats with Pompey against Caesar, and escapes punishment only by Caesar's assassination.

Burebista's unified state shatters at his death into small kingdoms, and a century of fragmentation follows until Decebal gathers Dacia again.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
