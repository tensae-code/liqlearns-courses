# Consolidating the romanian lands — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 11: Consolidarea țărilor române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alexandru cel Bun and his fellow rulers strengthen Moldavia and Wallachia with laws, trade, churches and embroidery workshops at Bistrița monastery.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 8: Viața politică a Țării Moldovei. Alexandru cel Bun" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alexander the Good defends Moldavia's independence, guards its trade roads, founds bishoprics and brings St John the New's relics to Suceava, while Poland and Hungary sign the 1412 Treaty of Lubla over his land.
