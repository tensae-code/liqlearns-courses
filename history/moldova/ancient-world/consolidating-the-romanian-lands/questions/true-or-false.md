# Consolidating the romanian lands — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 11: Consolidarea țărilor române", Alexandru cel Bun and his fellow rulers strengthen Moldavia and Wallachia with laws, trade, churches and embroidery workshops at Bistrița monastery (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unitatea II, Lecția 8: Viața politică a Țării Moldovei. Alexandru cel Bun", Alexander the Good defends Moldavia's independence, guards its trade roads, founds bishoprics and brings St John the New's relics to Suceava, while Poland and Hungary sign the 1412 Treaty of Lubla over his land (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
