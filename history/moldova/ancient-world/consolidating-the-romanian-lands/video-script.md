# Video script — Consolidating the romanian lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Consolidating the romanian lands.

Alexandru cel Bun and his fellow rulers strengthen Moldavia and Wallachia with laws, trade, churches and embroidery workshops at Bistrița monastery.

Alexander the Good defends Moldavia's independence, guards its trade roads, founds bishoprics and brings St John the New's relics to Suceava, while Poland and Hungary sign the 1412 Treaty of Lubla over his land.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
