# Consolidating the romanian lands

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Consolidating the romanian lands" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 11: Consolidarea țărilor române

Alexandru cel Bun and his fellow rulers strengthen Moldavia and Wallachia with laws, trade, churches and embroidery workshops at Bistrița monastery.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unitatea II, Lecția 8: Viața politică a Țării Moldovei. Alexandru cel Bun

Alexander the Good defends Moldavia's independence, guards its trade roads, founds bishoprics and brings St John the New's relics to Suceava, while Poland and Hungary sign the 1412 Treaty of Lubla over his land.
