# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea V, Lecția 6: Credințele și apariția creștinismului", from household Penates and the Roman pantheon a new faith rises — Jesus, condemned by the Roman governor of Judaea, is crucified and, his followers proclaim, rises on the third day
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
