# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea V, Lecția 6: Credințele și apariția creștinismului" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** From household Penates and the Roman pantheon a new faith rises — Jesus, condemned by the Roman governor of Judaea, is crucified and, his followers proclaim, rises on the third day.
