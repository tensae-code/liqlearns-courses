# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea V, Lecția 6: Credințele și apariția creștinismului" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** From household Penates and the Roman pantheon a new faith rises — Jesus, condemned by the Roman governor of Judaea, is crucified and, his followers proclaim, rises on the third day.
