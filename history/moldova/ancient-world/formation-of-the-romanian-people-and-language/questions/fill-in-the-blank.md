# Formation of the romanian people and language — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 3: Formarea poporului român și a limbii române" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Geto-Dacian locals absorb Rome's civilisation and popular Latin; Constantine's 313 turn spreads Christianity, and one people with one language — in dialects north and south of the Danube — emerges between Nistru, Tisa and the sea.
