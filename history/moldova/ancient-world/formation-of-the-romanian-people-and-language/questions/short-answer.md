# Formation of the romanian people and language — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 3: Formarea poporului român și a limbii române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Geto-Dacian locals absorb Rome's civilisation and popular Latin; Constantine's 313 turn spreads Christianity, and one people with one language — in dialects north and south of the Danube — emerges between Nistru, Tisa and the sea.
