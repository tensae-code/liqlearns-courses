# The roman empire augustus to trajan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea V, Lecția 4: Imperiul Roman" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Augustus, remembering Caesar's fate, gathers all power quietly and rules 41 years; under Trajan the empire turns outward again to its greatest extent.
