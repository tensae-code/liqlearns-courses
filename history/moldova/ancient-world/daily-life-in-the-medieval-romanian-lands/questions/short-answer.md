# Daily life in the medieval romanian lands — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 7: Viața cotidiană în Evul Mediu" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Bread and millet mămăliga for all, meat mostly at nobles' tables and great feasts; a bride's dowry follows her rank — and in Moldavia even sons could receive one.
