# Daily life in the medieval romanian lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 7: Viața cotidiană în Evul Mediu" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Bread and millet mămăliga for all, meat mostly at nobles' tables and great feasts; a bride's dowry follows her rank — and in Moldavia even sons could receive one.
