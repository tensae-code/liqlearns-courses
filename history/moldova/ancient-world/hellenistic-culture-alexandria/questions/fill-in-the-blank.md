# Hellenistic culture alexandria — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 7: Cultura elenistică" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Alexander's cities fuse Greek and conquered cultures; Alexandria's Pharos lighthouse, Museum and Library crown the blend, and Archimedes' screws and levers move the world.
