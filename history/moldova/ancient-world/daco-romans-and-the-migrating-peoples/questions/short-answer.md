# Daco romans and the migrating peoples — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 5: Daco-romanii și migratorii" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After Aurelian withdraws, wave after wave — Sarmatians, then others — crosses the Daco-Romans' land, and the more numerous, more Romanised locals absorb the newcomers.
