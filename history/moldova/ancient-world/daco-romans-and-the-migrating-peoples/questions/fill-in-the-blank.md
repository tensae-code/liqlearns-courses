# Daco romans and the migrating peoples — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 5: Daco-romanii și migratorii" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** After Aurelian withdraws, wave after wave — Sarmatians, then others — crosses the Daco-Romans' land, and the more numerous, more Romanised locals absorb the newcomers.
