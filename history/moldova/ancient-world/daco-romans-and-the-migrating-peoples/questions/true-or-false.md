# Daco romans and the migrating peoples — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea VI, Lecția 5: Daco-romanii și migratorii", after Aurelian withdraws, wave after wave — Sarmatians, then others — crosses the Daco-Romans' land, and the more numerous, more Romanised locals absorb the newcomers
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
