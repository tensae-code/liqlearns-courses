# Medieval world byzantium and justinian — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 6: Imperiul Roman de Răsărit (Bizanțul) în secolele V–XI" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The East Roman empire of 395 keeps the imperial claim, grows rich on trade and minerals, defends its borders with soldier-farmers, then loses the West after Justinian to Persians, Avars and Slavs.
