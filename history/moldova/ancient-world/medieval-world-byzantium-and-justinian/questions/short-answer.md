# Medieval world byzantium and justinian — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 6: Imperiul Roman de Răsărit (Bizanțul) în secolele V–XI" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The East Roman empire of 395 keeps the imperial claim, grows rich on trade and minerals, defends its borders with soldier-farmers, then loses the West after Justinian to Persians, Avars and Slavs.
