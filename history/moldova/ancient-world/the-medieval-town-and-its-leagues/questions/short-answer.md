# The medieval town and its leagues — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 11: Economie şi societate (sec. XI–XV). Oraşul medieval" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Villagers flee to the burg and become burghers; trading towns band into the Lombard, Rhenish and Hanseatic leagues, and crafts flourish in Moldavia, Wallachia and Transylvania.
