# The medieval town and its leagues — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 11: Economie şi societate (sec. XI–XV). Oraşul medieval" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Villagers flee to the burg and become burghers; trading towns band into the Lombard, Rhenish and Hanseatic leagues, and crafts flourish in Moldavia, Wallachia and Transylvania.
