# The medieval town and its leagues — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea II, Lecția 11: Economie şi societate (sec. XI–XV). Oraşul medieval", villagers flee to the burg and become burghers; trading towns band into the Lombard, Rhenish and Hanseatic leagues, and crafts flourish in Moldavia, Wallachia and Transylvania
