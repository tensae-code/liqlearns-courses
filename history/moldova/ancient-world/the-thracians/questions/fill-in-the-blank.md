# The thracians — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 1: Neamul tracilor" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Indo-European herdsmen mingle with the locals and the Thracians spread from the Balkans to the Dniester — Odrysians, Moesians, Bessi and dozens of tribes.
