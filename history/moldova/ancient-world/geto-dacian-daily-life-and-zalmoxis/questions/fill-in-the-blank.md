# Geto dacian daily life and zalmoxis — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 5: Modul de viață și religia geto-dacilor" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Herders and farmers who trade by barter and early coin, whose god Zalmoxis teaches them they do not die — every fifth year a messenger is sent to him.
