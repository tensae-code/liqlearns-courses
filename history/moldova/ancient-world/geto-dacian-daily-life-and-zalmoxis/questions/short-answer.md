# Geto dacian daily life and zalmoxis — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 5: Modul de viață și religia geto-dacilor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Herders and farmers who trade by barter and early coin, whose god Zalmoxis teaches them they do not die — every fifth year a messenger is sent to him.
