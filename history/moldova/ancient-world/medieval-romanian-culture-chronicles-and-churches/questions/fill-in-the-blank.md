# Medieval romanian culture chronicles and churches — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 6: Cultura românească în Evul Mediu" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Slavonic writing centres glow at Putna, Neamț and Tismana; the Tales of Vlad Țepeș travel in Slavonic and German, and Gothic stone rises at Bran, Hunedoara and Brașov's Black Church.
