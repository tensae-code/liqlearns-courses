Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea VI, Lecția 3: Dacia sub stăpânirea Romei", conquered Dacia is cut into provinces — Ulpia Traiana Sarmizegetusa, Superior, Porolissensis — ringed by the limes of forts and earthworks
