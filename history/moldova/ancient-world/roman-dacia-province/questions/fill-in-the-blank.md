# Roman dacia province — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 3: Dacia sub stăpânirea Romei" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Conquered Dacia is cut into provinces — Ulpia Traiana Sarmizegetusa, Superior, Porolissensis — ringed by the limes of forts and earthworks.
