# Greek culture education science and art — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 6: Cultura greacă. Educația și știința. Artele" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Herodotus and Thucydides invent history-writing from wars they lived; vase painters and sculptors make myth and sport into art.
