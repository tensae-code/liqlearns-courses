# Roman culture law literature and art — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea V, Lecția 5: Cultura Romei Antice" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Conquered Greece conquers its conqueror, says Horace; Virgil sings Aeneas, Ovid is exiled by Augustus, and Roman law and building outlive the legions.
