# How the romanian lands were governed — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 5: Organizarea internă a Țărilor Române. Biserica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The prince is supreme landowner, army chief and treaty-maker; Transylvania is run through counties and Saxon and Szekler seats, and Moldavia's fortresses — Suceava, Neamț, Soroca, Hotin, Cetatea Albă — form its shield.
