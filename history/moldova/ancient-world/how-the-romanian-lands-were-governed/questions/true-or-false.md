# How the romanian lands were governed — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea II, Lecția 5: Organizarea internă a Țărilor Române. Biserica", the prince is supreme landowner, army chief and treaty-maker; Transylvania is run through counties and Saxon and Szekler seats, and Moldavia's fortresses — Suceava, Neamț, Soroca, Hotin, Cetatea Albă — form its shield
