# Crisis of the roman empire constantine — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea VII, Lecția 1: Criza Imperiului Roman" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** 235–284, soldiers proclaim their own emperors and the empire nearly dies; Constantine ends the persecutions with the Edict of Milan in 313 and Christianity becomes official.
