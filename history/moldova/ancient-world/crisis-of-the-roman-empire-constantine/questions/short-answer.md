# Crisis of the roman empire constantine — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea VII, Lecția 1: Criza Imperiului Roman" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** 235–284, soldiers proclaim their own emperors and the empire nearly dies; Constantine ends the persecutions with the Edict of Milan in 313 and Christianity becomes official.
