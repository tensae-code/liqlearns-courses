# Cucuteni trypillia civilisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 5: Urme ale omului preistoric în spațiul românesc. Civilizația Cucuteni-Tripolie" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** On the ancestors' own land, the Cucuteni-Tripolie farmers raise one of Europe's earliest civilisations, with painted pottery and goddess figurines from the Carpathians to the Dnieper.
