# The geto dacians and sarmizegetusa — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 2: Geto-dacii. Sarmizegetusa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The northern Thracians become the Geto-Dacians — Greeks and Romans' name for one people — with tribes from the Carpathians to the Black Sea and their holy capital in the mountains.
