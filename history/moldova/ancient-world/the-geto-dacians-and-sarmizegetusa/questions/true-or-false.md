# The geto dacians and sarmizegetusa — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea IV, Lecția 2: Geto-dacii. Sarmizegetusa", the northern Thracians become the Geto-Dacians — Greeks and Romans' name for one people — with tribes from the Carpathians to the Black Sea and their holy capital in the mountains
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
