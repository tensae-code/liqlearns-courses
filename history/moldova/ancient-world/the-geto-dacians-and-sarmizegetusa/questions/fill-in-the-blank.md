# The geto dacians and sarmizegetusa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 2: Geto-dacii. Sarmizegetusa" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The northern Thracians become the Geto-Dacians — Greeks and Romans' name for one people — with tribes from the Carpathians to the Black Sea and their holy capital in the mountains.
