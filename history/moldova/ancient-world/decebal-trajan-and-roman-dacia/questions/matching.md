# Decebal trajan and roman dacia — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 9: De la romani la români — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unitatea VI, Lecția 2: Dacia pe timpul lui Decebal. Războaiele daco-romane — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Decebal hides Dacia's treasure by diverting a river, Trajan conquers Dacia anyway, and from Romans and Dacians together the Romanian people and language are born.
b) Decebal reunifies and reforms Dacia, fights Trajan in 101–102, breaks the peace in Rome's telling, and falls in 105–106 when Rome comes for him and his capital.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
