# Decebal trajan and roman dacia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 9: De la romani la români" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Decebal hides Dacia's treasure by diverting a river, Trajan conquers Dacia anyway, and from Romans and Dacians together the Romanian people and language are born.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 2: Dacia pe timpul lui Decebal. Războaiele daco-romane" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Decebal reunifies and reforms Dacia, fights Trajan in 101–102, breaks the peace in Rome's telling, and falls in 105–106 when Rome comes for him and his capital.
