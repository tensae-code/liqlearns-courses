# Decebal trajan and roman dacia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 9: De la romani la români", Decebal hides Dacia's treasure by diverting a river, Trajan conquers Dacia anyway, and from Romans and Dacians together the Romanian people and language are born (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unitatea VI, Lecția 2: Dacia pe timpul lui Decebal. Războaiele daco-romane", Decebal reunifies and reforms Dacia, fights Trajan in 101–102, breaks the peace in Rome's telling, and falls in 105–106 when Rome comes for him and his capital (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
