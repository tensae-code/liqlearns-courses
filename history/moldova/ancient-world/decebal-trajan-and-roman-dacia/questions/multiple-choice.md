# Decebal trajan and roman dacia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Tema 9: De la romani la români"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Decebal hides Dacia's treasure by diverting a river, Trajan conquers Dacia anyway, and from Romans and Dacians together the Romanian people and language are born.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
