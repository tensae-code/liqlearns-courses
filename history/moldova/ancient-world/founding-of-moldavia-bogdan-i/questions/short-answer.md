# Founding of moldavia bogdan i — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Dragoș's first voivodate, Bogdan I crosses from Maramureș in 1359 and founds an independent Moldavia, with Baia as its first capital.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bogdan rebels against Maramureș's lost autonomy, crosses the Carpathians in 1359, defeats Balc and becomes voivode of an independent Moldavia.
