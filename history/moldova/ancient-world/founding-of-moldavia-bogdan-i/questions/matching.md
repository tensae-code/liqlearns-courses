# Founding of moldavia bogdan i — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) After Dragoș's first voivodate, Bogdan I crosses from Maramureș in 1359 and founds an independent Moldavia, with Baia as its first capital.
b) Bogdan rebels against Maramureș's lost autonomy, crosses the Carpathians in 1359, defeats Balc and becomes voivode of an independent Moldavia.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
