# Founding of moldavia bogdan i — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I", after Dragoș's first voivodate, Bogdan I crosses from Maramureș in 1359 and founds an independent Moldavia, with Baia as its first capital (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei", Bogdan rebels against Maramureș's lost autonomy, crosses the Carpathians in 1359, defeats Balc and becomes voivode of an independent Moldavia (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
