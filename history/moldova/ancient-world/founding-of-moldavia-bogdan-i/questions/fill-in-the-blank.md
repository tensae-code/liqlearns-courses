# Founding of moldavia bogdan i — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Dragoș's first voivodate, Bogdan I crosses from Maramureș in 1359 and founds an independent Moldavia, with Baia as its first capital.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bogdan rebels against Maramureș's lost autonomy, crosses the Carpathians in 1359, defeats Balc and becomes voivode of an independent Moldavia.
