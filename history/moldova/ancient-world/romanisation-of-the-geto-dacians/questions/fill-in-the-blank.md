# Romanisation of the geto dacians — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 4: Romanizarea geto-dacilor" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Colonists, towns of villa and vicus, and above all the legions and their settled veterans carry Latin into Dacia until the Dacians speak and live Roman.
