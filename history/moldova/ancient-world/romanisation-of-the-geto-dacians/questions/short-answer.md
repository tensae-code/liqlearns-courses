# Romanisation of the geto dacians — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 4: Romanizarea geto-dacilor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Colonists, towns of villa and vicus, and above all the legions and their settled veterans carry Latin into Dacia until the Dacians speak and live Roman.
