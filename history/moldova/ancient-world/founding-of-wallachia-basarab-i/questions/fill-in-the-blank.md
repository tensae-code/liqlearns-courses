# Founding of wallachia basarab i — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Românească" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The voivodeships of Glad, Gelu and Menumorut give way to Basarab I, who unifies the local autonomies and founds Wallachia, named as its ruler in a 1324 document.
