# Founding of wallachia basarab i — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Românească", the voivodeships of Glad, Gelu and Menumorut give way to Basarab I, who unifies the local autonomies and founds Wallachia, named as its ruler in a 1324 document
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
