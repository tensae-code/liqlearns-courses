# Founding of wallachia basarab i — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Românească" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The voivodeships of Glad, Gelu and Menumorut give way to Basarab I, who unifies the local autonomies and founds Wallachia, named as its ruler in a 1324 document.
