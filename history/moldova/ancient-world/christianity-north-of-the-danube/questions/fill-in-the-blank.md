# Christianity north of the danube — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 7: Creștinismul în spațiul nord-dunărean" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** North of the Danube, Christianity becomes a badge of Roman identity — Romanian keeps the Latin basilica for "church", a word almost no other Romance language kept.
