# Christianity north of the danube — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea VI, Lecția 7: Creștinismul în spațiul nord-dunărean" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** North of the Danube, Christianity becomes a badge of Roman identity — Romanian keeps the Latin basilica for "church", a word almost no other Romance language kept.
