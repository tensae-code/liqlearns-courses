# Ancient civilisations india — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Orașe-state, regate și imperii (India)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Aśoka binds India by its roads, reorganises Buddhism and leaves his inscriptions — India's first written documents — and the Laws of Manu.
