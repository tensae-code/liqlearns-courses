# Mircea the elder of wallachia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea II, Lecția 7: Țara Românească în timpul domniei lui Mircea cel Bătrân (1386–1418)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Mircea strengthens Wallachia and joins the western crusade, which ends in disaster at Nicopolis on 25 September 1396.
