# The franks and charlemagnes empire — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea I, Lecția 5: Regatul Franc și Imperiul lui Carol cel Mare", Clovis's Franks build a kingdom; Charlemagne conquers the Spanish March, divides his empire into 200 counties under counts and margraves, and is watched by royal inspectors
