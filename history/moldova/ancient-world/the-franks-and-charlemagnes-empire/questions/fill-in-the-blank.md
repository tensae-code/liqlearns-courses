# The franks and charlemagnes empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 5: Regatul Franc și Imperiul lui Carol cel Mare" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Clovis's Franks build a kingdom; Charlemagne conquers the Spanish March, divides his empire into 200 counties under counts and margraves, and is watched by royal inspectors.
