# Peoples of ancient italy and the etruscans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea V, Lecția 1: Omul și mediul în Peninsula Italică" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Aeneas flees burning Troy for Italy in legend; in fact Latins, Samnites and Sabines share the peninsula while the religious Etruscans read the gods' will in signs.
