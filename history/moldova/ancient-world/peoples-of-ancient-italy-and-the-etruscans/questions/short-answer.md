# Peoples of ancient italy and the etruscans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea V, Lecția 1: Omul și mediul în Peninsula Italică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Aeneas flees burning Troy for Italy in legend; in fact Latins, Samnites and Sabines share the peninsula while the religious Etruscans read the gods' will in signs.
