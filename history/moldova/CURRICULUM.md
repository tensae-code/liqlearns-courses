# History — Moldova course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Moldova inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade the book comes from appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Moldova's own History books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

**How the ladder is derived:** Moldova's books teach one continuous story — *Istoria românilor și universală*, the history of the Romanians and universal history together — in the placements file's own teaching order, earliest books first. The youngest book tells the whole national story once, as dialogues with Clio, from the Geto-Dacians to independence; the next books then walk the ages in order — the ancient world whole, the medieval age and the birth of the modern world, the modern age's first period ending with Bessarabia taken by Russia in 1812, and the contemporary age whole with Bessarabia and the Moldavian SSR at its centre; the two lycée books then re-run the modern and contemporary ages at full depth. The ladder follows that progression: each level is one book's turn of the story, and a lesson stands at the level of the earliest book that teaches it; its later, deeper tellings are stocked into the same lesson. One filed book (Grade 8) is UNREAD and carries no placements, so it makes no level — it is named in ABOUT.md as not yet in the course. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate. History is taught as stories: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.
