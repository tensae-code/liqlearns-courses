# Romania between the wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 12: România în perioada interbelică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greater Romania unifies its currency in 1920, electrifies (electric industry up 429%), peaks economically in 1938 — and lives the drama of Carol II's return in June 1930 and Iuliu Maniu's resignation.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 17: România de la democrație la autoritarism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1923 Constitution takes the 1866 text and separates the powers; in January 1926 the Crown Council accepts Prince Carol's renunciation and proclaims the child Mihai heir — the road to authoritarianism opens.
