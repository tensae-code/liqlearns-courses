# Romania between the wars — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 12: România în perioada interbelică", Greater Romania unifies its currency in 1920, electrifies (electric industry up 429%), peaks economically in 1938 — and lives the drama of Carol II's return in June 1930 and Iuliu Maniu's resignation (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 17: România de la democrație la autoritarism", the 1923 Constitution takes the 1866 text and separates the powers; in January 1926 the Crown Council accepts Prince Carol's renunciation and proclaims the child Mihai heir — the road to authoritarianism opens (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
