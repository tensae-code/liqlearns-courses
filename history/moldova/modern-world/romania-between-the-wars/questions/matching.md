# Romania between the wars — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 12: România în perioada interbelică — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 17: România de la democrație la autoritarism — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Greater Romania unifies its currency in 1920, electrifies (electric industry up 429%), peaks economically in 1938 — and lives the drama of Carol II's return in June 1930 and Iuliu Maniu's resignation.
b) The 1923 Constitution takes the 1866 text and separates the powers; in January 1926 the Crown Council accepts Prince Carol's renunciation and proclaims the child Mihai heir — the road to authoritarianism opens.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
