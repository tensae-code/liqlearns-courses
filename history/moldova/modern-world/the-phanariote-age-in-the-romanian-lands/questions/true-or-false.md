# The phanariote age in the romanian lands — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 14: Epoca fanariotă în Țările Române", the Porte appoints Greek Phanariote princes for three-year terms, confirmed yearly and deposed at whim — a political fix that becomes an age of burden (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§15: Organizarea politică a Principatelor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII", the Mavrocordats, a Greek family from Chios, supply princes; the case study follows the grand vizier's messenger the moment a Moldavian prince dies and a Phanariote is named (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
