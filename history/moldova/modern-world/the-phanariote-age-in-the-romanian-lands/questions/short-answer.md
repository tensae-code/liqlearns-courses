# The phanariote age in the romanian lands — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 14: Epoca fanariotă în Țările Române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Porte appoints Greek Phanariote princes for three-year terms, confirmed yearly and deposed at whim — a political fix that becomes an age of burden.

**2.** Tell the story of "§15: Organizarea politică a Principatelor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Mavrocordats, a Greek family from Chios, supply princes; the case study follows the grand vizier's messenger the moment a Moldavian prince dies and a Phanariote is named.
