# The phanariote age in the romanian lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 14: Epoca fanariotă în Țările Române" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Porte appoints Greek Phanariote princes for three-year terms, confirmed yearly and deposed at whim — a political fix that becomes an age of burden.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15: Organizarea politică a Principatelor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Mavrocordats, a Greek family from Chios, supply princes; the case study follows the grand vizier's messenger the moment a Moldavian prince dies and a Phanariote is named.
