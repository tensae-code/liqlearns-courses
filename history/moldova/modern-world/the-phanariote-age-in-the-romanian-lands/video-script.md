# Video script — The phanariote age in the romanian lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The phanariote age in the romanian lands.

The Porte appoints Greek Phanariote princes for three-year terms, confirmed yearly and deposed at whim — a political fix that becomes an age of burden.

The Mavrocordats, a Greek family from Chios, supply princes; the case study follows the grand vizier's messenger the moment a Moldavian prince dies and a Phanariote is named.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
