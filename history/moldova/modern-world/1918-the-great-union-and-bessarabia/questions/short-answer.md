# 1918 the great union and bessarabia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 15: Anul 1918 în istorie" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1918 the Sfatul Țării under Ion Inculeț votes Bessarabia's union with Romania, completing the Great Union of all Romanians.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 4: Formarea Statului Național Unitar Român. Recunoașterea Marii Uniri" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1918, Romania's astral hour — Bessarabia, Bukovina under Iancu Flondor's Cernăuți assembly, and Transylvania with its national councils and guards unite with the Old Kingdom as two empires vanish.
