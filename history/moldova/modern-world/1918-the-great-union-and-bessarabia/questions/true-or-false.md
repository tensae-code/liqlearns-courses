# 1918 the great union and bessarabia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 15: Anul 1918 în istorie", in 1918 the Sfatul Țării under Ion Inculeț votes Bessarabia's union with Romania, completing the Great Union of all Romanians (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 4: Formarea Statului Național Unitar Român. Recunoașterea Marii Uniri", 1918, Romania's astral hour — Bessarabia, Bukovina under Iancu Flondor's Cernăuți assembly, and Transylvania with its national councils and guards unite with the Old Kingdom as two empires vanish (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
