# Video script — 1918 the great union and bessarabia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: 1918 the great union and bessarabia.

In 1918 the Sfatul Țării under Ion Inculeț votes Bessarabia's union with Romania, completing the Great Union of all Romanians.

1918, Romania's astral hour — Bessarabia, Bukovina under Iancu Flondor's Cernăuți assembly, and Transylvania with its national councils and guards unite with the Old Kingdom as two empires vanish.

On 4 October 1918 deputy Isopescu-Grecul demands wide autonomy for Austria-Hungary's 4 million Romanians; within weeks the unitary national state is formed.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
