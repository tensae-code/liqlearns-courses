# Bessarabias national movement 1917 1918 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 3: Mișcarea națională a românilor din Basarabia și din teritoriile din stânga Nistrului (1917–1918)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Moldavian National Party of Stroescu, Gore, Halippa and Pelivan leads the movement; Ukraine's Rada claims Bessarabia, and Romanians east of the Nistru send their first deputies to the Sfatul Țării.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 7: Basarabia în anii 1917–1918" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** National consciousness wakes in a land "torn away abusively in 1812"; Bessarabia's return to the Romanian state proceeds in stages through the Sfatul Țării.
