# Bessarabias national movement 1917 1918 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 3: Mișcarea națională a românilor din Basarabia și din teritoriile din stânga Nistrului (1917–1918)", the Moldavian National Party of Stroescu, Gore, Halippa and Pelivan leads the movement; Ukraine's Rada claims Bessarabia, and Romanians east of the Nistru send their first deputies to the Sfatul Țării (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 7: Basarabia în anii 1917–1918", national consciousness wakes in a land "torn away abusively in 1812"; Bessarabia's return to the Romanian state proceeds in stages through the Sfatul Țării (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
