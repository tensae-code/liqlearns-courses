# Video script — Bessarabias national movement 1917 1918

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Bessarabias national movement 1917 1918.

The Moldavian National Party of Stroescu, Gore, Halippa and Pelivan leads the movement; Ukraine's Rada claims Bessarabia, and Romanians east of the Nistru send their first deputies to the Sfatul Țării.

National consciousness wakes in a land "torn away abusively in 1812"; Bessarabia's return to the Romanian state proceeds in stages through the Sfatul Țării.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
