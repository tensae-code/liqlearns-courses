# The european union nations together — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 17: Europa, uniune a națiunilor libere" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Free European nations pool their future in the EU — parliament at Strasbourg, motto "Unity in diversity" proposed by Europe's young people in 2002.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 40: Integrarea Europeană. Uniunea Europeană la începutul mileniului III" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Integration begins in 1951 as the answer to war's crisis; Nice (2001) brings majority voting, and on 1 June 2023 Europe's leaders summit at Bulboaca in Moldova itself.
