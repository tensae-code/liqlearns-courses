# The european union nations together — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 17: Europa, uniune a națiunilor libere" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Free European nations pool their future in the EU — parliament at Strasbourg, motto "Unity in diversity" proposed by Europe's young people in 2002.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 40: Integrarea Europeană. Uniunea Europeană la începutul mileniului III" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Integration begins in 1951 as the answer to war's crisis; Nice (2001) brings majority voting, and on 1 June 2023 Europe's leaders summit at Bulboaca in Moldova itself.
