# Video script — The european union nations together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The european union nations together.

Free European nations pool their future in the EU — parliament at Strasbourg, motto "Unity in diversity" proposed by Europe's young people in 2002.

Integration begins in 1951 as the answer to war's crisis; Nice (2001) brings majority voting, and on 1 June 2023 Europe's leaders summit at Bulboaca in Moldova itself.

Schuman's 9 May 1950 declaration makes war between France and Germany "materially impossible" through shared coal and steel; the 1985 Single European Act promises a space without internal frontiers.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
