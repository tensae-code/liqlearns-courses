# Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§36: Unificarea Germaniei" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Three wars — Denmark, Austria, France — unify the Germans; Bismarck tells the budget commission in 1862 that the great questions are settled by iron, and the Second French Empire's fall clears the last road.
