# Meiji japan modernisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§39: Epoca Meiji în Japonia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After long isolation Japan opens its doors; the fifteen-year-old emperor Mutsuhito takes power in 1867, the 1889 constitution is promulgated, and state-built industry modernises the country.
