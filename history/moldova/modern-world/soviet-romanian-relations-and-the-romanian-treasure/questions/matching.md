# Soviet romanian relations and the romanian treasure — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 18: Relațiile sovieto-române între 1918 și 1940 — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 14: Relațiile româno-sovietice în perioada interbelică — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Lenin renounces Bessarabia he cannot hold; the 1924 Vienna conference fails, and the Romanian Treasure, evacuated to allied Russia in 1916, becomes the hostage of the whole relationship.
b) Soviet press campaigns discredit Romania; the Tatarbunary rising of September 1924 is the local explosion, and in August 1939 Moscow negotiates with Berlin while the western military delegations are still in town.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
