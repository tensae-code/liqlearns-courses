# Soviet romanian relations and the romanian treasure — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 18: Relațiile sovieto-române între 1918 și 1940" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lenin renounces Bessarabia he cannot hold; the 1924 Vienna conference fails, and the Romanian Treasure, evacuated to allied Russia in 1916, becomes the hostage of the whole relationship.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 14: Relațiile româno-sovietice în perioada interbelică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Soviet press campaigns discredit Romania; the Tatarbunary rising of September 1924 is the local explosion, and in August 1939 Moscow negotiates with Berlin while the western military delegations are still in town.
