# Soviet romanian relations and the romanian treasure — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 18: Relațiile sovieto-române între 1918 și 1940", Lenin renounces Bessarabia he cannot hold; the 1924 Vienna conference fails, and the Romanian Treasure, evacuated to allied Russia in 1916, becomes the hostage of the whole relationship (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 14: Relațiile româno-sovietice în perioada interbelică", Soviet press campaigns discredit Romania; the Tatarbunary rising of September 1924 is the local explosion, and in August 1939 Moscow negotiates with Berlin while the western military delegations are still in town (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
