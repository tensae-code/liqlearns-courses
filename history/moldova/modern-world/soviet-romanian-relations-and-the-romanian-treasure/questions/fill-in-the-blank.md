# Soviet romanian relations and the romanian treasure — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 18: Relațiile sovieto-române între 1918 și 1940" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lenin renounces Bessarabia he cannot hold; the 1924 Vienna conference fails, and the Romanian Treasure, evacuated to allied Russia in 1916, becomes the hostage of the whole relationship.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 14: Relațiile româno-sovietice în perioada interbelică" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Soviet press campaigns discredit Romania; the Tatarbunary rising of September 1924 is the local explosion, and in August 1939 Moscow negotiates with Berlin while the western military delegations are still in town.
