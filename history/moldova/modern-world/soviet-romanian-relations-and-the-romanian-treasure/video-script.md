# Video script — Soviet romanian relations and the romanian treasure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Soviet romanian relations and the romanian treasure.

Lenin renounces Bessarabia he cannot hold; the 1924 Vienna conference fails, and the Romanian Treasure, evacuated to allied Russia in 1916, becomes the hostage of the whole relationship.

Soviet press campaigns discredit Romania; the Tatarbunary rising of September 1924 is the local explosion, and in August 1939 Moscow negotiates with Berlin while the western military delegations are still in town.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
