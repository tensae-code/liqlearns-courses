# Bessarabia 1812 the treaty of bucharest — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 20: Principatele Române în contextul politicii Marilor Puteri. Pacea de la București" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Russia and the Porte fight over the Danubian Principalities; Kutuzov first plans to annex both, and the 1812 Peace of Bucharest takes Bessarabia instead.

**2.** Tell the story of "§24: Anul 1812 în istoria românilor. Anexarea Basarabiei" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Napoleon and Alexander agree at Tilsit; Russia offers to cede only Hotin, talks fail, and the Bucharest treaty's Article 4 makes the Prut the border — the Bessarabian question is born.
