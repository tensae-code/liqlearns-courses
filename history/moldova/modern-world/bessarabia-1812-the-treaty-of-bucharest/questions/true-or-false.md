# Bessarabia 1812 the treaty of bucharest — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 20: Principatele Române în contextul politicii Marilor Puteri. Pacea de la București", Russia and the Porte fight over the Danubian Principalities; Kutuzov first plans to annex both, and the 1812 Peace of Bucharest takes Bessarabia instead (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§24: Anul 1812 în istoria românilor. Anexarea Basarabiei", Napoleon and Alexander agree at Tilsit; Russia offers to cede only Hotin, talks fail, and the Bucharest treaty's Article 4 makes the Prut the border — the Bessarabian question is born (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
