# Bessarabia 1812 the treaty of bucharest — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 20: Principatele Române în contextul politicii Marilor Puteri. Pacea de la București" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Russia and the Porte fight over the Danubian Principalities; Kutuzov first plans to annex both, and the 1812 Peace of Bucharest takes Bessarabia instead.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24: Anul 1812 în istoria românilor. Anexarea Basarabiei" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Napoleon and Alexander agree at Tilsit; Russia offers to cede only Hotin, talks fail, and the Bucharest treaty's Article 4 makes the Prut the border — the Bessarabian question is born.
