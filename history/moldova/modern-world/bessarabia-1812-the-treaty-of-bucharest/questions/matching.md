# Bessarabia 1812 the treaty of bucharest — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 20: Principatele Române în contextul politicii Marilor Puteri. Pacea de la București — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §24: Anul 1812 în istoria românilor. Anexarea Basarabiei — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Russia and the Porte fight over the Danubian Principalities; Kutuzov first plans to annex both, and the 1812 Peace of Bucharest takes Bessarabia instead.
b) Napoleon and Alexander agree at Tilsit; Russia offers to cede only Hotin, talks fail, and the Bucharest treaty's Article 4 makes the Prut the border — the Bessarabian question is born.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
