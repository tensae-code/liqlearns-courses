# Video script — Bessarabia 1812 the treaty of bucharest

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Bessarabia 1812 the treaty of bucharest.

Russia and the Porte fight over the Danubian Principalities; Kutuzov first plans to annex both, and the 1812 Peace of Bucharest takes Bessarabia instead.

Napoleon and Alexander agree at Tilsit; Russia offers to cede only Hotin, talks fail, and the Bucharest treaty's Article 4 makes the Prut the border — the Bessarabian question is born.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
