# Contemporary world culture and science — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 41: Cultura și știința universală în Epoca Contemporană" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Dolly the sheep is cloned, COVID-19 transforms life in 2019–22, Mies van der Rohe raises the Seagram Building and Niemeyer builds Brazil's parliament.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 53: Realizările tehnice şi ştiinţifice din Epoca Contemporană" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sputnik opens the space age on 4 October 1957 and humanity flies off its own planet; endocrinology, cytology and the microbes-and-viruses revolution remake medicine.
