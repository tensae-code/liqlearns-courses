# Contemporary world culture and science — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 41: Cultura și știința universală în Epoca Contemporană" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Dolly the sheep is cloned, COVID-19 transforms life in 2019–22, Mies van der Rohe raises the Seagram Building and Niemeyer builds Brazil's parliament.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 53: Realizările tehnice şi ştiinţifice din Epoca Contemporană" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sputnik opens the space age on 4 October 1957 and humanity flies off its own planet; endocrinology, cytology and the microbes-and-viruses revolution remake medicine.
