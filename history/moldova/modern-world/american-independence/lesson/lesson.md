# American independence

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "American independence" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lecția 5: Războiul de Independenţă a coloniilor din America de Nord. Constituirea SUA

The metropolis treats the colonies as cheap raw material and a captive market; on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration proclaims life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, and planter Washington leads the new republic.

## Sub lesson 2 — §11: Războiul de Independenţă al coloniilor engleze. Constituirea SUA

The thirteen colonies win at Yorktown in 1781, Britain recognises independence at Versailles in 1783, and the 1791 Bill of Rights opens with freedom of religion and speech.
