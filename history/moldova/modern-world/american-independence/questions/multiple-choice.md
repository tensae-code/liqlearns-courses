# American independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lecția 5: Războiul de Independenţă a coloniilor din America de Nord. Constituirea SUA"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The metropolis treats the colonies as cheap raw material and a captive market; on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration proclaims life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, and planter Washington leads the new republic.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
