# American independence — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 5: Războiul de Independenţă a coloniilor din America de Nord. Constituirea SUA — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §11: Războiul de Independenţă al coloniilor engleze. Constituirea SUA — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The metropolis treats the colonies as cheap raw material and a captive market; on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration proclaims life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, and planter Washington leads the new republic.
b) The thirteen colonies win at Yorktown in 1781, Britain recognises independence at Versailles in 1783, and the 1791 Bill of Rights opens with freedom of religion and speech.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
