# American independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 5: Războiul de Independenţă a coloniilor din America de Nord. Constituirea SUA", the metropolis treats the colonies as cheap raw material and a captive market; on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration proclaims life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, and planter Washington leads the new republic (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§11: Războiul de Independenţă al coloniilor engleze. Constituirea SUA", the thirteen colonies win at Yorktown in 1781, Britain recognises independence at Versailles in 1783, and the 1791 Bill of Rights opens with freedom of religion and speech (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
