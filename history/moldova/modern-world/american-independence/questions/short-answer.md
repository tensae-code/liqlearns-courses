# American independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 5: Războiul de Independenţă a coloniilor din America de Nord. Constituirea SUA" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The metropolis treats the colonies as cheap raw material and a captive market; on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration proclaims life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, and planter Washington leads the new republic.

**2.** Tell the story of "§11: Războiul de Independenţă al coloniilor engleze. Constituirea SUA" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The thirteen colonies win at Yorktown in 1781, Britain recognises independence at Versailles in 1783, and the 1791 Bill of Rights opens with freedom of religion and speech.
