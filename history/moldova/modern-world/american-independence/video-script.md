# Video script — American independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: American independence.

The metropolis treats the colonies as cheap raw material and a captive market; on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration proclaims life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, and planter Washington leads the new republic.

The thirteen colonies win at Yorktown in 1781, Britain recognises independence at Versailles in 1783, and the 1791 Bill of Rights opens with freedom of religion and speech.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
