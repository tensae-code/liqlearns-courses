# Video script — The making of the moldavian ssr 1940

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The making of the moldavian ssr 1940.

After the 28 June–3 July 1940 operation the Moldavian SSR is proclaimed; kolkhozes multiply from 23 to 120 in a year, and on 14 June 1941 the NKVD reports 19,575 people "lifted" in the republic.

From November 1940 the central press and Russian-speaking cadres from outside the republic staff the new order in the Moldavian SSR.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
