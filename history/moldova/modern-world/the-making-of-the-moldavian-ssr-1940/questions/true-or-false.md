# The making of the moldavian ssr 1940 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 21: Formarea RSSM și instaurarea regimului comunist", after the 28 June–3 July 1940 operation the Moldavian SSR is proclaimed; kolkhozes multiply from 23 to 120 in a year, and on 14 June 1941 the NKVD reports 19,575 people "lifted" in the republic (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 24: Formarea RSSM și instaurarea regimului comunist", from November 1940 the central press and Russian-speaking cadres from outside the republic staff the new order in the Moldavian SSR (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
