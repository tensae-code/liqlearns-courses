# The making of the moldavian ssr 1940 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 21: Formarea RSSM și instaurarea regimului comunist" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After the 28 June–3 July 1940 operation the Moldavian SSR is proclaimed; kolkhozes multiply from 23 to 120 in a year, and on 14 June 1941 the NKVD reports 19,575 people "lifted" in the republic.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 24: Formarea RSSM și instaurarea regimului comunist" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From November 1940 the central press and Russian-speaking cadres from outside the republic staff the new order in the Moldavian SSR.
