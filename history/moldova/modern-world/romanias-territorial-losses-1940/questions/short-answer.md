# Romanias territorial losses 1940 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 20: Pierderile teritoriale ale României în anul 1940" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Molotov's ultimatum demands Bessarabia back "at any price"; the Crown Council, meeting at 3 a.m. under threat of invasion, accepts the Vienna "arbitration" — a dictat — to save the state.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 24: Anul 1940 în istoria românilor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Six Crown Council members — Ciobanu, Dragomir, Iamandi, Iorga, Pop, Urdăreanu — vote to accept the Soviet conditions and save the state; Carol II, his authority broken, hands power to General Antonescu.
