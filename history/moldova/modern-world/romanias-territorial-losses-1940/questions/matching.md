# Romanias territorial losses 1940 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 20: Pierderile teritoriale ale României în anul 1940 — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 24: Anul 1940 în istoria românilor — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Molotov's ultimatum demands Bessarabia back "at any price"; the Crown Council, meeting at 3 a.m. under threat of invasion, accepts the Vienna "arbitration" — a dictat — to save the state.
b) Six Crown Council members — Ciobanu, Dragomir, Iamandi, Iorga, Pop, Urdăreanu — vote to accept the Soviet conditions and save the state; Carol II, his authority broken, hands power to General Antonescu.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
