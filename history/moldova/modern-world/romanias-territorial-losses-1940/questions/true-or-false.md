# Romanias territorial losses 1940 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 20: Pierderile teritoriale ale României în anul 1940", Molotov's ultimatum demands Bessarabia back "at any price"; the Crown Council, meeting at 3 a.m. under threat of invasion, accepts the Vienna "arbitration" — a dictat — to save the state (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 24: Anul 1940 în istoria românilor", six Crown Council members — Ciobanu, Dragomir, Iamandi, Iorga, Pop, Urdăreanu — vote to accept the Soviet conditions and save the state; Carol II, his authority broken, hands power to General Antonescu (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
