# Video script — Romanias territorial losses 1940

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Romanias territorial losses 1940.

Molotov's ultimatum demands Bessarabia back "at any price"; the Crown Council, meeting at 3 a.m. under threat of invasion, accepts the Vienna "arbitration" — a dictat — to save the state.

Six Crown Council members — Ciobanu, Dragomir, Iamandi, Iorga, Pop, Urdăreanu — vote to accept the Soviet conditions and save the state; Carol II, his authority broken, hands power to General Antonescu.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
