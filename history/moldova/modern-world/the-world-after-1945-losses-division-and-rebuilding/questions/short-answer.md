# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 25: Consecințele celui de-al Doilea Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe ends in ruins — 23.6 million homes destroyed; Romania's own incomplete count runs to 92,620 dead and 367,976 missing, prisoners in Soviet camps excluded.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 29: Consecințele celui de-Al Doilea Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Over 60 million dead — some 25 million soldiers and 35 million civilians; the USSR's territorial acquisitions of 1939–41 are tabled beside the human bill.
