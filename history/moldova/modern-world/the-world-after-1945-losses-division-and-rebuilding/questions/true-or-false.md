# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 25: Consecințele celui de-al Doilea Război Mondial", Europe ends in ruins — 23.6 million homes destroyed; Romania's own incomplete count runs to 92,620 dead and 367,976 missing, prisoners in Soviet camps excluded (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 29: Consecințele celui de-Al Doilea Război Mondial", over 60 million dead — some 25 million soldiers and 35 million civilians; the USSR's territorial acquisitions of 1939–41 are tabled beside the human bill (Sub lesson 2)
