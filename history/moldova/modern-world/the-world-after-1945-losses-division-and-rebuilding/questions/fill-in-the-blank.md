# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 25: Consecințele celui de-al Doilea Război Mondial" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe ends in ruins — 23.6 million homes destroyed; Romania's own incomplete count runs to 92,620 dead and 367,976 missing, prisoners in Soviet camps excluded.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 29: Consecințele celui de-Al Doilea Război Mondial" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Over 60 million dead — some 25 million soldiers and 35 million civilians; the USSR's territorial acquisitions of 1939–41 are tabled beside the human bill.
