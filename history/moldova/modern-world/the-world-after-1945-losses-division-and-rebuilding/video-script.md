# Video script — The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding.

Europe ends in ruins — 23.6 million homes destroyed; Romania's own incomplete count runs to 92,620 dead and 367,976 missing, prisoners in Soviet camps excluded.

Over 60 million dead — some 25 million soldiers and 35 million civilians; the USSR's territorial acquisitions of 1939–41 are tabled beside the human bill.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
