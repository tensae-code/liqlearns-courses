# Video script — Communist states of eastern europe and asia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Communist states of eastern europe and asia.

The percentages agreement divides the spoils; Soviet-model plans and forced industrialisation follow, Dubček is arrested in August 1968, Poland protests in 1956, 1970 and 1980, and Deng Xiaoping's line wins China in 1978.

Groza's government is imposed over Rădescu's Romania; Tito's partisans make their own Yugoslavia, Rákosi opens Budapest's 1948 fair as Hungary's communists take over, and the GDR is proclaimed on Soviet-zone territory.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
