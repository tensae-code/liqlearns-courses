# Communist states of eastern europe and asia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 33: Statele comuniste din Europa de Est și Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The percentages agreement divides the spoils; Soviet-model plans and forced industrialisation follow, Dubček is arrested in August 1968, Poland protests in 1956, 1970 and 1980, and Deng Xiaoping's line wins China in 1978.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 34: Instaurarea regimului comunist în țările Europei Centrale și de Est" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Groza's government is imposed over Rădescu's Romania; Tito's partisans make their own Yugoslavia, Rákosi opens Budapest's 1948 fair as Hungary's communists take over, and the GDR is proclaimed on Soviet-zone territory.
