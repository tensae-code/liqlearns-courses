# Communist states of eastern europe and asia — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 33: Statele comuniste din Europa de Est și Asia — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 34: Instaurarea regimului comunist în țările Europei Centrale și de Est — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The percentages agreement divides the spoils; Soviet-model plans and forced industrialisation follow, Dubček is arrested in August 1968, Poland protests in 1956, 1970 and 1980, and Deng Xiaoping's line wins China in 1978.
b) Groza's government is imposed over Rădescu's Romania; Tito's partisans make their own Yugoslavia, Rákosi opens Budapest's 1948 fair as Hungary's communists take over, and the GDR is proclaimed on Soviet-zone territory.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
