# Communist states of eastern europe and asia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 33: Statele comuniste din Europa de Est și Asia", the percentages agreement divides the spoils; Soviet-model plans and forced industrialisation follow, Dubček is arrested in August 1968, Poland protests in 1956, 1970 and 1980, and Deng Xiaoping's line wins China in 1978 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 34: Instaurarea regimului comunist în țările Europei Centrale și de Est", Groza's government is imposed over Rădescu's Romania; Tito's partisans make their own Yugoslavia, Rákosi opens Budapest's 1948 fair as Hungary's communists take over, and the GDR is proclaimed on Soviet-zone territory (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
