# The great depression 1929 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 20: Marea Depresiune. „New Deal"" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** US industrial production falls 46% by 1932; the New Deal answers with work-week, price and union rules — "no discrimination between union members" — as a model for escaping crisis.
