# Vasile lupu and matei basarab law and printing — depth

This lesson has 2 sub lessons, in order: 1. Extindere I: Epoca lui Vasile Lupu și Matei Basarab; 2. Unitatea III, Lecția 5: Țările Române în prima jumătate a sec. al XVII-lea. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the story of Vasile lupu and matei basarab law and printing as Moldova's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it — in their own words, retell the story (who, where, what turned, what came of it), and answer the lesson's questions. Each sub lesson opens by proving the one before it: the prove-it check is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the sub lesson's whole question set (the gate pool — no hardness ranking is written in questions/). Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain story as told in the lesson, then tellings from the later, deeper books stocked in this lesson, then the story applied in a new place — the learner's own locality and people.
