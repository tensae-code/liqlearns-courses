# Video script — Vasile lupu and matei basarab law and printing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Vasile lupu and matei basarab law and printing.

Two rival princes outdo each other in good works — Vasile Lupu's Pravila of 1646 is Moldavia's first printed law code, moving the land from custom to written law.

Matei Basarab allies with Transylvania's Rákóczi but clashes with Moldavia's Vasile Lupu; in Transylvania Gabriel Bethlen's harsh rule provokes general resistance.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
