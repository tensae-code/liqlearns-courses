# Vasile lupu and matei basarab law and printing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Extindere I: Epoca lui Vasile Lupu și Matei Basarab" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two rival princes outdo each other in good works — Vasile Lupu's Pravila of 1646 is Moldavia's first printed law code, moving the land from custom to written law.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 5: Țările Române în prima jumătate a sec. al XVII-lea" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Matei Basarab allies with Transylvania's Rákóczi but clashes with Moldavia's Vasile Lupu; in Transylvania Gabriel Bethlen's harsh rule provokes general resistance.
