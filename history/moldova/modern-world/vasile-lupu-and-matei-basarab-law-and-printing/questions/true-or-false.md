# Vasile lupu and matei basarab law and printing — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Extindere I: Epoca lui Vasile Lupu și Matei Basarab", two rival princes outdo each other in good works — Vasile Lupu's Pravila of 1646 is Moldavia's first printed law code, moving the land from custom to written law (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unitatea III, Lecția 5: Țările Române în prima jumătate a sec. al XVII-lea", Matei Basarab allies with Transylvania's Rákóczi but clashes with Moldavia's Vasile Lupu; in Transylvania Gabriel Bethlen's harsh rule provokes general resistance (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
