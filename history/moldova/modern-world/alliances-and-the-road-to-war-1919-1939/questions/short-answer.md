# Alliances and the road to war 1919 1939 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 17: Alianțe și tratate politico-militare în perioada interbelică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Little Entente treaties of 1920–21 face the Anti-Comintern Pact; the Rome–Berlin–Tokyo Axis forms in 1937 as Japan opens its decisive offensive in China.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 12: Sistemul de tratate în perioada interbelică. Politica conciliatoristă" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Berlin's refusal to pay brings France into the Ruhr on 11 January 1923; the two revisionist states, Germany and Italy, form the Rome–Berlin Axis on 25 October 1936 and Japan adheres.
