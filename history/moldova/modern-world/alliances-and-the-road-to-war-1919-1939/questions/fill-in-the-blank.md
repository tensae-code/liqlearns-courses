# Alliances and the road to war 1919 1939 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 17: Alianțe și tratate politico-militare în perioada interbelică" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Little Entente treaties of 1920–21 face the Anti-Comintern Pact; the Rome–Berlin–Tokyo Axis forms in 1937 as Japan opens its decisive offensive in China.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 12: Sistemul de tratate în perioada interbelică. Politica conciliatoristă" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Berlin's refusal to pay brings France into the Ruhr on 11 January 1923; the two revisionist states, Germany and Italy, form the Rome–Berlin Axis on 25 October 1936 and Japan adheres.
