# Video script — Alliances and the road to war 1919 1939

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Alliances and the road to war 1919 1939.

The Little Entente treaties of 1920–21 face the Anti-Comintern Pact; the Rome–Berlin–Tokyo Axis forms in 1937 as Japan opens its decisive offensive in China.

Berlin's refusal to pay brings France into the Ruhr on 11 January 1923; the two revisionist states, Germany and Italy, form the Rome–Berlin Axis on 25 October 1936 and Japan adheres.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
