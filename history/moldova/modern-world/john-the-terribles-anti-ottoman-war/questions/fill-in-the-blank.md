# John the terribles anti ottoman war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 3: Evoluția politică a Țării Moldovei. Războiul antiotoman sub conducerea lui Ioan Vodă" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** John the Terrible revolts against the rising Ottoman burden; betrayed at Lake Cahul, his army breaks and retreats to Roșcani, where the prince is killed.
