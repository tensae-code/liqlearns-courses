# Video script — Germany italy and japan after 1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Germany italy and japan after 1945.

Kohl unifies Germany — economic union on 1 July 1990, proclamation on 3 October; Christian-Democrat Italy industrialises unevenly north and south, and Japan bets on robotics, electronics and research.

West Germany's "social market economy" refuses direct regulation; Japan's ruined cities rise on kaisha foundations until the Nissan Sunny becomes the 1960s middle-class symbol.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
