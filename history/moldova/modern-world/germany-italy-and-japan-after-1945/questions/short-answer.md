# Germany italy and japan after 1945 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 28: RFG, Italia, Japonia în perioada postbelică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kohl unifies Germany — economic union on 1 July 1990, proclamation on 3 October; Christian-Democrat Italy industrialises unevenly north and south, and Japan bets on robotics, electronics and research.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 31: Fenomenul miracolului economic: Germania şi Japonia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** West Germany's "social market economy" refuses direct regulation; Japan's ruined cities rise on kaisha foundations until the Nissan Sunny becomes the 1960s middle-class symbol.
