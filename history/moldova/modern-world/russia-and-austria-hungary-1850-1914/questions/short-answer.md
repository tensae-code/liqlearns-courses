# Russia and austria hungary 1850 1914 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§37: Imperiul Rus şi Imperiul Austro-Ungar" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Beaten by Prussia in 1866, Austria saves itself by the dualist compromise with Hungary; Franz Joseph reigns 68 years, Alexander III's Russia reforms and represses, and the 1877–78 war exposes both empires' Balkan rivalry.
