# The romanian principalities economy and society 1650 1800 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 10: Principatele Române: economie și societate" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Under Ottoman suzerainty the Principalities keep medieval relations while market seeds sprout; boyar tables take oriental spices and olives while peasants rarely taste meat.

**2.** Tell the story of "§4: Dezvoltarea economică a Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** After long war and calamity, harvests grow and towns fill with locksmiths, armourers and masons, but the Porte's excessive demands brake the Principalities' integration into the market.
