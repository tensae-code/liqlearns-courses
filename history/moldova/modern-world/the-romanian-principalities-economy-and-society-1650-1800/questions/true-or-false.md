# The romanian principalities economy and society 1650 1800 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 10: Principatele Române: economie și societate", under Ottoman suzerainty the Principalities keep medieval relations while market seeds sprout; boyar tables take oriental spices and olives while peasants rarely taste meat (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§4: Dezvoltarea economică a Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII", after long war and calamity, harvests grow and towns fill with locksmiths, armourers and masons, but the Porte's excessive demands brake the Principalities' integration into the market (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
