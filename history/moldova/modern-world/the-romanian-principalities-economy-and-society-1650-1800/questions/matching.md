# The romanian principalities economy and society 1650 1800 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 10: Principatele Române: economie și societate — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §4: Dezvoltarea economică a Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Under Ottoman suzerainty the Principalities keep medieval relations while market seeds sprout; boyar tables take oriental spices and olives while peasants rarely taste meat.
b) After long war and calamity, harvests grow and towns fill with locksmiths, armourers and masons, but the Porte's excessive demands brake the Principalities' integration into the market.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
