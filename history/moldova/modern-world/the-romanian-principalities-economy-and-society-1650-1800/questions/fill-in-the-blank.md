# The romanian principalities economy and society 1650 1800 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 10: Principatele Române: economie și societate" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Under Ottoman suzerainty the Principalities keep medieval relations while market seeds sprout; boyar tables take oriental spices and olives while peasants rarely taste meat.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4: Dezvoltarea economică a Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** After long war and calamity, harvests grow and towns fill with locksmiths, armourers and masons, but the Porte's excessive demands brake the Principalities' integration into the market.
