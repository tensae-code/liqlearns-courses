# Video script — The romanian principalities economy and society 1650 1800

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The romanian principalities economy and society 1650 1800.

Under Ottoman suzerainty the Principalities keep medieval relations while market seeds sprout; boyar tables take oriental spices and olives while peasants rarely taste meat.

After long war and calamity, harvests grow and towns fill with locksmiths, armourers and masons, but the Porte's excessive demands brake the Principalities' integration into the market.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
