# Romanias foreign policy 1878 1914 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§45: Politica externă a României (1878–1914)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Independent Romania becomes an international actor; the road to Berlin runs through Vienna into the Triple Alliance, and the Balkan Wars and the 1913 Bucharest Peace are the alliance's severest test.
