# The moldavian ssr economy and society 1944 1985 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 30: RSSM. Economie și societate (1944–1985)", the party controls every institution; peasants who miss obligatory deliveries are jailed and stripped, Bodiul's "Moldavian experiment" builds agro-industrial giants, and by 1985 the republic counts 565 enterprises whose goods people still cannot afford (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 36: Politica „Centrului" în RSSM: economie, societate şi mod de viaţă", between 1944 and 1952 the state collects the village's produce through famine and deportation; grain combines rise from 3,400 to 4,500 by 1985, wages climb from 81 roubles (1970) to 171 (1988), and Moscow's cadres hold 70% of leading posts by 1984 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
