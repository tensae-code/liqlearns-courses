# Video script — The moldavian ssr economy and society 1944 1985

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The moldavian ssr economy and society 1944 1985.

The party controls every institution; peasants who miss obligatory deliveries are jailed and stripped, Bodiul's "Moldavian experiment" builds agro-industrial giants, and by 1985 the republic counts 565 enterprises whose goods people still cannot afford.

Between 1944 and 1952 the state collects the village's produce through famine and deportation; grain combines rise from 3,400 to 4,500 by 1985, wages climb from 81 roubles (1970) to 171 (1988), and Moscow's cadres hold 70% of leading posts by 1984.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
