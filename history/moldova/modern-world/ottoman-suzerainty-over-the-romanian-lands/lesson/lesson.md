## Unitatea III, Lecția 2: Suzeranitatea otomană în Țările Române

After 1538 the Porte rules indirectly — tribute (haraciu) and gifts rise year by year, the prince must be "friend of the friends and enemy of the enemies" of the empire, and peasant households are ruined.
