# Ottoman suzerainty over the romanian lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 2: Suzeranitatea otomană în Țările Române" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** After 1538 the Porte rules indirectly — tribute (haraciu) and gifts rise year by year, the prince must be "friend of the friends and enemy of the enemies" of the empire, and peasant households are ruined.
