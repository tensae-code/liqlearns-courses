# Ottoman suzerainty over the romanian lands — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 2: Suzeranitatea otomană în Țările Române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After 1538 the Porte rules indirectly — tribute (haraciu) and gifts rise year by year, the prince must be "friend of the friends and enemy of the enemies" of the empire, and peasant households are ruined.
