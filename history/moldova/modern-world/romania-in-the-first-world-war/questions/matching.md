# Romania in the first world war — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 2: România în Primul Război Mondial — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 5: România în Primul Război Mondial — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Bound by a secret 1883 alliance to the Central Powers, Romania waits neutral until 1916, then is crushed by a stronger enemy, retreats, and endures hunger, epidemics and industrial collapse.
b) Romania argues its 1883 treaty was defensive only, hesitates, enters, is encircled, and signs the separate peace of 7 May 1918.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
