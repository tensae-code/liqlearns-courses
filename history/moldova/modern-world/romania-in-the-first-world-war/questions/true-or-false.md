# Romania in the first world war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 2: România în Primul Război Mondial", bound by a secret 1883 alliance to the Central Powers, Romania waits neutral until 1916, then is crushed by a stronger enemy, retreats, and endures hunger, epidemics and industrial collapse (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 5: România în Primul Război Mondial", Romania argues its 1883 treaty was defensive only, hesitates, enters, is encircled, and signs the separate peace of 7 May 1918 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
