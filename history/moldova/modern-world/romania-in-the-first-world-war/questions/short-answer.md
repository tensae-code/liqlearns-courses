# Romania in the first world war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 2: România în Primul Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bound by a secret 1883 alliance to the Central Powers, Romania waits neutral until 1916, then is crushed by a stronger enemy, retreats, and endures hunger, epidemics and industrial collapse.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 5: România în Primul Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Romania argues its 1883 treaty was defensive only, hesitates, enters, is encircled, and signs the separate peace of 7 May 1918.
