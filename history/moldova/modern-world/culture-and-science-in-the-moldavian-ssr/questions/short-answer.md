# Culture and science in the moldavian ssr — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 42: Cultura și știința în RSSM (1944–1991)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Education becomes an instrument of ideology under censorship, yet the Ablov chemistry school wins world fame and the "Joc" dance ensemble, founded in 1945, carries Moldavian dance abroad; Emil Loteanu films the national values.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 37: Politica conducerii sovietice din RSSM în domeniul culturii şi ştiinţei" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The doctrine of a separate Moldavian people and language returns; Russian becomes the lingua franca, Romanian-speaking teachers are lacking so courses run in Russian, and of 14 exhibitions in 1952–53 only five show authentic local artists.
