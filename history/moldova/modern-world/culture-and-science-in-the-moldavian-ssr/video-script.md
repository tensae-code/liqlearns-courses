# Video script — Culture and science in the moldavian ssr

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Culture and science in the moldavian ssr.

Education becomes an instrument of ideology under censorship, yet the Ablov chemistry school wins world fame and the "Joc" dance ensemble, founded in 1945, carries Moldavian dance abroad; Emil Loteanu films the national values.

The doctrine of a separate Moldavian people and language returns; Russian becomes the lingua franca, Romanian-speaking teachers are lacking so courses run in Russian, and of 14 exhibitions in 1952–53 only five show authentic local artists.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
