# The paris peace conference and the league of nations — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 5: Conferința de Pace de la Paris și noua ordine internațională — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 9: Sistemul Versailles–Washington și noua ordine internaţională — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The Big Four and 28 states remake the world in 1919–20 without Soviet Russia; Romania signs Versailles on 28 June 1919 then walks out, and every treaty carries the League of Nations in its preamble.
b) Separate treaties are signed with Germany's allies too; Austria-Hungary is dismantled and Austria, Hungary, Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia are constituted.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
