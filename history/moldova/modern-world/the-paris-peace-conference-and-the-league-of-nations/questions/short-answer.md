# The paris peace conference and the league of nations — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 5: Conferința de Pace de la Paris și noua ordine internațională" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Big Four and 28 states remake the world in 1919–20 without Soviet Russia; Romania signs Versailles on 28 June 1919 then walks out, and every treaty carries the League of Nations in its preamble.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 9: Sistemul Versailles–Washington și noua ordine internaţională" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Separate treaties are signed with Germany's allies too; Austria-Hungary is dismantled and Austria, Hungary, Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia are constituted.
