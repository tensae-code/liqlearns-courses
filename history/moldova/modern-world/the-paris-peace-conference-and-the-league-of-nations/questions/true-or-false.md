# The paris peace conference and the league of nations — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 5: Conferința de Pace de la Paris și noua ordine internațională", the Big Four and 28 states remake the world in 1919–20 without Soviet Russia; Romania signs Versailles on 28 June 1919 then walks out, and every treaty carries the League of Nations in its preamble (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 9: Sistemul Versailles–Washington și noua ordine internaţională", separate treaties are signed with Germany's allies too; Austria-Hungary is dismantled and Austria, Hungary, Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia are constituted (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
