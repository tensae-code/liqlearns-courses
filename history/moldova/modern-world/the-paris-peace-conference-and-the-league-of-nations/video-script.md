# Video script — The paris peace conference and the league of nations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The paris peace conference and the league of nations.

The Big Four and 28 states remake the world in 1919–20 without Soviet Russia; Romania signs Versailles on 28 June 1919 then walks out, and every treaty carries the League of Nations in its preamble.

Separate treaties are signed with Germany's allies too; Austria-Hungary is dismantled and Austria, Hungary, Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia are constituted.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
