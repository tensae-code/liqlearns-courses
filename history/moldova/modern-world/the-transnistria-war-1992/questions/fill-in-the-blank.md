# The transnistria war 1992 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 37: Războiul de pe Nistru" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Separatists backed by the 14th Army and Cossacks block the Nistru bridges; fighting flares at Tighina, Cocieri and Coșnița, and the 1999 Istanbul summit obliges Russia to withdraw its troops.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 49: Războiul pentru Apărarea Independenței și Integrității Teritoriale ale Republicii Moldova" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Balta–Tiraspol autonomous entity of 1924 becomes the separatist base; Chișinău fights with almost no international support, and Transnistrian separatism cuts deep into the national economy.
