# The transnistria war 1992 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 37: Războiul de pe Nistru", separatists backed by the 14th Army and Cossacks block the Nistru bridges; fighting flares at Tighina, Cocieri and Coșnița, and the 1999 Istanbul summit obliges Russia to withdraw its troops (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 49: Războiul pentru Apărarea Independenței și Integrității Teritoriale ale Republicii Moldova", the Balta–Tiraspol autonomous entity of 1924 becomes the separatist base; Chișinău fights with almost no international support, and Transnistrian separatism cuts deep into the national economy (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
