# Video script — The transnistria war 1992

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The transnistria war 1992.

Separatists backed by the 14th Army and Cossacks block the Nistru bridges; fighting flares at Tighina, Cocieri and Coșnița, and the 1999 Istanbul summit obliges Russia to withdraw its troops.

The Balta–Tiraspol autonomous entity of 1924 becomes the separatist base; Chișinău fights with almost no international support, and Transnistrian separatism cuts deep into the national economy.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
