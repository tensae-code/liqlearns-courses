# 1848 the spring of peoples — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§19: „Primăvara popoarelor." Anul revoluţionar 1848 în Europa", Lamartine rejects the red flag for the tricolour before Paris's city hall on 25 February 1848; the year's revolutions strike the old regimes decisively and open the road to democratic reform
