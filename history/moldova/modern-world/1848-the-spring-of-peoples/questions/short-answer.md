# 1848 the spring of peoples — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§19: „Primăvara popoarelor." Anul revoluţionar 1848 în Europa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Lamartine rejects the red flag for the tricolour before Paris's city hall on 25 February 1848; the year's revolutions strike the old regimes decisively and open the road to democratic reform.
