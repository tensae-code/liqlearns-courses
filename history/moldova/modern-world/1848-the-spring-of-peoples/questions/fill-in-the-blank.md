# 1848 the spring of peoples — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§19: „Primăvara popoarelor." Anul revoluţionar 1848 în Europa" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Lamartine rejects the red flag for the tricolour before Paris's city hall on 25 February 1848; the year's revolutions strike the old regimes decisively and open the road to democratic reform.
