# The organic regulations and native princes — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 24: Domniile pământene și Regulamentele Organice în Țările Române — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §17: Principatele Române (1822–1848). Suzeranitatea otomană şi protectoratul rusesc — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The Phanariotes fall, native princes return, and the Organic Regulations of 1831–32 — confirmed by Russia only in 1833 — open the road to the modern Romanian state after Adrianople (1829) restores the Danube fortresses.
b) Between suzerainty and protectorate, General Kiseleff presides over the divans and backs the Organic Regulations to win the boyars and strengthen Russia's hold.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
