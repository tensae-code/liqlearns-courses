# The organic regulations and native princes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 24: Domniile pământene și Regulamentele Organice în Țările Române", the Phanariotes fall, native princes return, and the Organic Regulations of 1831–32 — confirmed by Russia only in 1833 — open the road to the modern Romanian state after Adrianople (1829) restores the Danube fortresses (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§17: Principatele Române (1822–1848). Suzeranitatea otomană şi protectoratul rusesc", between suzerainty and protectorate, General Kiseleff presides over the divans and backs the Organic Regulations to win the boyars and strengthen Russia's hold (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
