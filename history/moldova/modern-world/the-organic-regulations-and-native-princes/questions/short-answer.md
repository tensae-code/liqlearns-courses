# The organic regulations and native princes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 24: Domniile pământene și Regulamentele Organice în Țările Române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Phanariotes fall, native princes return, and the Organic Regulations of 1831–32 — confirmed by Russia only in 1833 — open the road to the modern Romanian state after Adrianople (1829) restores the Danube fortresses.

**2.** Tell the story of "§17: Principatele Române (1822–1848). Suzeranitatea otomană şi protectoratul rusesc" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Between suzerainty and protectorate, General Kiseleff presides over the divans and backs the Organic Regulations to win the boyars and strengthen Russia's hold.
