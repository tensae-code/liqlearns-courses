# Video script — The organic regulations and native princes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The organic regulations and native princes.

The Phanariotes fall, native princes return, and the Organic Regulations of 1831–32 — confirmed by Russia only in 1833 — open the road to the modern Romanian state after Adrianople (1829) restores the Danube fortresses.

Between suzerainty and protectorate, General Kiseleff presides over the divans and backs the Organic Regulations to win the boyars and strengthen Russia's hold.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
