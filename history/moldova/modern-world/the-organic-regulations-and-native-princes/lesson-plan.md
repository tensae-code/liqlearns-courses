# Lesson plan — The organic regulations and native princes

**Course:** History — Moldova · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The organic regulations and native princes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The modern age, first period: revolutions and enlightenment in the West, the Principalities between three empires, and Bessarabia taken by Russia in 1812 (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The modern age, first period: revolutions and enlightenment in the West, the Principalities between three empires, and Bessarabia taken by Russia in 1812 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
