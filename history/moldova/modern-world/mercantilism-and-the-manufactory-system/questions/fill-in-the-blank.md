# Mercantilism and the manufactory system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1: Economia occidentală în epoca preindustrială. Mercantilismul" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A strong state is one that hoards precious metals at its rivals' expense; Colbert runs France's economy on that rule, and production gathers into centralised, dispersed and mixed manufactories.
