# Decolonisation of asia and africa and its problems — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 44: Problemele ţărilor decolonizate" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The new states inherit bloated states, armies in politics and authoritarian habits; most choose republics, a few — the Emirates, Kuwait — keep monarchic or theocratic rule.
