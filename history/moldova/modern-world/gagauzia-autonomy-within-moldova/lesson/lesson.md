## Tema 51: Unitatea Teritorială Autonomă cu statut special Găgăuzia (Gagauz-Yeri)

The law makes Gagauzia "a form of self-determination of the Gagauz, part of the Republic of Moldova", with its own authorities and the Valkaneș free economic zone.
