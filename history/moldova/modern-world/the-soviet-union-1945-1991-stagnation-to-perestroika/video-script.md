# Video script — The soviet union 1945 1991 stagnation to perestroika

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The soviet union 1945 1991 stagnation to perestroika.

Stalin's last years bring collectivisation, famine and terror; growth slides from 5.2% to 2%, Chernobyl explodes on 26 April 1986 and glasnost wakes the national movements.

In the MSSR alone, 1946–47 terror plans "educate" the population while forced requisitions feed exports; Khrushchev condemns Stalin yet drowns Hungary's 1956 revolution in blood, Prague 1968 restores the authoritarian grip, and Gorbachev's last troops leave Afghanistan on 15 February 1989.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
