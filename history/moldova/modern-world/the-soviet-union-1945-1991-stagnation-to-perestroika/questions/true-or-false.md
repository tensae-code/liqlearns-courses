# The soviet union 1945 1991 stagnation to perestroika — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 29: Uniunea Sovietică în perioada postbelică", Stalin's last years bring collectivisation, famine and terror; growth slides from 5.2% to 2%, Chernobyl explodes on 26 April 1986 and glasnost wakes the national movements (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 33: URSS în perioada postbelică", in the MSSR alone, 1946–47 terror plans "educate" the population while forced requisitions feed exports; Khrushchev condemns Stalin yet drowns Hungary's 1956 revolution in blood, Prague 1968 restores the authoritarian grip, and Gorbachev's last troops leave Afghanistan on 15 February 1989 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
