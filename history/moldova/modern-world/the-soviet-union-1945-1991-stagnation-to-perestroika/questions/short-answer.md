# The soviet union 1945 1991 stagnation to perestroika — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 29: Uniunea Sovietică în perioada postbelică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Stalin's last years bring collectivisation, famine and terror; growth slides from 5.2% to 2%, Chernobyl explodes on 26 April 1986 and glasnost wakes the national movements.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 33: URSS în perioada postbelică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In the MSSR alone, 1946–47 terror plans "educate" the population while forced requisitions feed exports; Khrushchev condemns Stalin yet drowns Hungary's 1956 revolution in blood, Prague 1968 restores the authoritarian grip, and Gorbachev's last troops leave Afghanistan on 15 February 1989.
