# Culture in bessarabia under russian rule — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 28: Cultura în Basarabia în prima jumătate a secolului al XIX-lea" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Annexed Bessarabia gets a new cultural ambience — imperial schools under university districts, enlightenment authors in public office, and Chișinău rebuilt after 1812 in its upper town.

**2.** Tell the story of "§52: Cultura în Basarabia sub dominaţia ţaristă (1812–1918)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Annexed Bessarabia keeps its own culture on a secularising path; religious books and folklore lead, Bănulescu-Bodoni builds its church life, and Bessarabians feed the empire's science.
