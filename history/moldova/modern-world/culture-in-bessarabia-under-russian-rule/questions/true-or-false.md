# Culture in bessarabia under russian rule — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 28: Cultura în Basarabia în prima jumătate a secolului al XIX-lea", annexed Bessarabia gets a new cultural ambience — imperial schools under university districts, enlightenment authors in public office, and Chișinău rebuilt after 1812 in its upper town (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§52: Cultura în Basarabia sub dominaţia ţaristă (1812–1918)", annexed Bessarabia keeps its own culture on a secularising path; religious books and folklore lead, Bănulescu-Bodoni builds its church life, and Bessarabians feed the empire's science (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
