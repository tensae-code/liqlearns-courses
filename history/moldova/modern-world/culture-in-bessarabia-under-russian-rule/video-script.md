# Video script — Culture in bessarabia under russian rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Culture in bessarabia under russian rule.

Annexed Bessarabia gets a new cultural ambience — imperial schools under university districts, enlightenment authors in public office, and Chișinău rebuilt after 1812 in its upper town.

Annexed Bessarabia keeps its own culture on a secularising path; religious books and folklore lead, Bănulescu-Bodoni builds its church life, and Bessarabians feed the empire's science.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
