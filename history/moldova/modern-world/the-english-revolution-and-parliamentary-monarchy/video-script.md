# Video script — The english revolution and parliamentary monarchy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The english revolution and parliamentary monarchy.

Charles I leaves London in 1642 and civil war follows; Cromwell's Protectorate restores order and modernises, and in 1688 the army deserts James II — parliament wins.

In 1628 king and Parliament collide over the Petition of Right; James I calls debating the king's power sedition, and Cromwell ends as Lord Protector holding all power.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
