# The english revolution and parliamentary monarchy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 3: Revoluția engleză. Anglia – de la absolutism la monarhia parlamentară" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Charles I leaves London in 1642 and civil war follows; Cromwell's Protectorate restores order and modernises, and in 1688 the army deserts James II — parliament wins.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9: Anglia, de la monarhia absolutistă la cea parlamentară" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In 1628 king and Parliament collide over the Petition of Right; James I calls debating the king's power sedition, and Cromwell ends as Lord Protector holding all power.
