# The english revolution and parliamentary monarchy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 3: Revoluția engleză. Anglia – de la absolutism la monarhia parlamentară", Charles I leaves London in 1642 and civil war follows; Cromwell's Protectorate restores order and modernises, and in 1688 the army deserts James II — parliament wins (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§9: Anglia, de la monarhia absolutistă la cea parlamentară", in 1628 king and Parliament collide over the Petition of Right; James I calls debating the king's power sedition, and Cromwell ends as Lord Protector holding all power (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
