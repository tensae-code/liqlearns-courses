# The english revolution and parliamentary monarchy — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 3: Revoluția engleză. Anglia – de la absolutism la monarhia parlamentară — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §9: Anglia, de la monarhia absolutistă la cea parlamentară — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Charles I leaves London in 1642 and civil war follows; Cromwell's Protectorate restores order and modernises, and in 1688 the army deserts James II — parliament wins.
b) In 1628 king and Parliament collide over the Petition of Right; James I calls debating the king's power sedition, and Cromwell ends as Lord Protector holding all power.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
