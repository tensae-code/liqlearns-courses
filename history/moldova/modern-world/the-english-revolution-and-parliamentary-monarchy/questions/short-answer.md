# The english revolution and parliamentary monarchy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 3: Revoluția engleză. Anglia – de la absolutism la monarhia parlamentară" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Charles I leaves London in 1642 and civil war follows; Cromwell's Protectorate restores order and modernises, and in 1688 the army deserts James II — parliament wins.

**2.** Tell the story of "§9: Anglia, de la monarhia absolutistă la cea parlamentară" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In 1628 king and Parliament collide over the Petition of Right; James I calls debating the king's power sedition, and Cromwell ends as Lord Protector holding all power.
