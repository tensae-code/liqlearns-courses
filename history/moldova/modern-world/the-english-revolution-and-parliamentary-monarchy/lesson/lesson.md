## Sub lesson 1 — Lecția 3: Revoluția engleză. Anglia – de la absolutism la monarhia parlamentară

Charles I leaves London in 1642 and civil war follows; Cromwell's Protectorate restores order and modernises, and in 1688 the army deserts James II — parliament wins.

## Sub lesson 2 — §9: Anglia, de la monarhia absolutistă la cea parlamentară

In 1628 king and Parliament collide over the Petition of Right; James I calls debating the king's power sedition, and Cromwell ends as Lord Protector holding all power.
