# The romanian lands in the 1500s neagoe and petru rares — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea III, Lecția 1: Situația politică a Țărilor Române în prima jumătate a secolului al XVI-lea. Campania din 1538" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Neagoe Basarab backs the merchants and writes his Teachings; Petru Rareș fights Poland over Pocuția and joins the Holy League before Suleiman's 1538 campaign changes everything.
