## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 1: Lumea la cumpăna sec. XIX–XX. Primul Război Mondial

The great powers' race for world supremacy, fed by the second industrial revolution, locks Europe into two alliance blocs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tema 4: Primul Război Mondial – conflict pentru reîmpărțirea lumii

The Sarajevo assassination of Franz Ferdinand on 28 June 1914 is the pretext; the alliance mechanism does the rest, and the east front collapses into the Brest-Litovsk peace of 3 March 1918.
