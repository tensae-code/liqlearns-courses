# The first world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 1: Lumea la cumpăna sec. XIX–XX. Primul Război Mondial" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The great powers' race for world supremacy, fed by the second industrial revolution, locks Europe into two alliance blocs.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 4: Primul Război Mondial – conflict pentru reîmpărțirea lumii" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Sarajevo assassination of Franz Ferdinand on 28 June 1914 is the pretext; the alliance mechanism does the rest, and the east front collapses into the Brest-Litovsk peace of 3 March 1918.
