# The first world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 1: Lumea la cumpăna sec. XIX–XX. Primul Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The great powers' race for world supremacy, fed by the second industrial revolution, locks Europe into two alliance blocs.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 4: Primul Război Mondial – conflict pentru reîmpărțirea lumii" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Sarajevo assassination of Franz Ferdinand on 28 June 1914 is the pretext; the alliance mechanism does the rest, and the east front collapses into the Brest-Litovsk peace of 3 March 1918.
