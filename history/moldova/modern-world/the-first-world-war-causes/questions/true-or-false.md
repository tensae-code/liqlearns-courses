# The first world war causes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 1: Lumea la cumpăna sec. XIX–XX. Primul Război Mondial", the great powers' race for world supremacy, fed by the second industrial revolution, locks Europe into two alliance blocs (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 4: Primul Război Mondial – conflict pentru reîmpărțirea lumii", the Sarajevo assassination of Franz Ferdinand on 28 June 1914 is the pretext; the alliance mechanism does the rest, and the east front collapses into the Brest-Litovsk peace of 3 March 1918 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
