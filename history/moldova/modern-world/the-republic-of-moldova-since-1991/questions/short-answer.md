# The republic of moldova since 1991 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 38: Republica Moldova la etapa actuală" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Presidents come and go, the "theft of the billion" (13.3 billion lei) scars the economy, direct presidential election returns in 2016, and after Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine Moldova moves toward the EU.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 47: Democratizarea vieții politice. Constituția Republicii Moldova" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Within months some 20 parties and organisations form; the name "Romanian language" survives in 1995, communist-era governments subordinate the press, and the 5 April 2009 elections open a new crisis.
