# The contemporary age totalitarianism and its seeds — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Introducere: Epoca Contemporană" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The contemporary age opens with totalitarian regimes rising in Europe and the Versailles treaty system sowing the germs of the next extremisms.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 1: Introducere în studierea istoriei contemporane" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** "a people that does not know its history" is the journal's opening warning; integration begins with the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the age turns on 1989–91.
