# The contemporary age totalitarianism and its seeds — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Introducere: Epoca Contemporană" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The contemporary age opens with totalitarian regimes rising in Europe and the Versailles treaty system sowing the germs of the next extremisms.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 1: Introducere în studierea istoriei contemporane" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** "a people that does not know its history" is the journal's opening warning; integration begins with the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the age turns on 1989–91.
