# Video script — The contemporary age totalitarianism and its seeds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The contemporary age totalitarianism and its seeds.

The contemporary age opens with totalitarian regimes rising in Europe and the Versailles treaty system sowing the germs of the next extremisms.

"a people that does not know its history" is the journal's opening warning; integration begins with the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the age turns on 1989–91.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
