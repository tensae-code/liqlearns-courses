# Video script — The west after 1945 usa france britain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The west after 1945 usa france britain.

America fights in Korea and Vietnam, signs peace in 1973 and is shaken by Watergate; Mitterrand nationalises and shortens the week, Thatcher breaks the miners and accepts mass unemployment.

Truman inherits Roosevelt's course and launches containment; de Gaulle returns on the Algerian war and proposes the 1958 constitution; women win legal ground against job discrimination yet rarely reach the top — Japan has never had a woman prime minister.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
