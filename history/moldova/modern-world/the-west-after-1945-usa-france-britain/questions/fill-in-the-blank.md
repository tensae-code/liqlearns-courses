# The west after 1945 usa france britain — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 27: Statele Unite, Franța, Anglia în perioada postbelică" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** America fights in Korea and Vietnam, signs peace in 1973 and is shaken by Watergate; Mitterrand nationalises and shortens the week, Thatcher breaks the miners and accepts mass unemployment.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 30: State democratice în perioada postbelică" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Truman inherits Roosevelt's course and launches containment; de Gaulle returns on the Algerian war and proposes the 1958 constitution; women win legal ground against job discrimination yet rarely reach the top — Japan has never had a woman prime minister.
