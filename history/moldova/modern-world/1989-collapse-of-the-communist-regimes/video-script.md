# Video script — 1989 collapse of the communist regimes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: 1989 collapse of the communist regimes.

Wałęsa's Solidarity leads the workers against the workers' state; Charter 77's Czechoslovakia splits peacefully on 1 January 1993, and the "velvet and un-velvet" revolutions of 1989 sweep the region.

Poland votes freely in June 1989; the Ceaușescus are captured at Târgoviște, Czechoslovakia's "velvet divorce" makes Havel and Kováč presidents in 1993, and even Albania accepts multiparty politics.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
