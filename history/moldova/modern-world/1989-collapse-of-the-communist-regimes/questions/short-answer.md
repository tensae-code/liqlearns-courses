# 1989 collapse of the communist regimes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 35: Criza sistemului totalitar și colapsul regimurilor comuniste în Europa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Wałęsa's Solidarity leads the workers against the workers' state; Charter 77's Czechoslovakia splits peacefully on 1 January 1993, and the "velvet and un-velvet" revolutions of 1989 sweep the region.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 45: Căderea regimurilor comuniste în Europa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Poland votes freely in June 1989; the Ceaușescus are captured at Târgoviște, Czechoslovakia's "velvet divorce" makes Havel and Kováč presidents in 1993, and even Albania accepts multiparty politics.
