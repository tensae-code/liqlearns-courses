# 1989 collapse of the communist regimes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 35: Criza sistemului totalitar și colapsul regimurilor comuniste în Europa", Wałęsa's Solidarity leads the workers against the workers' state; Charter 77's Czechoslovakia splits peacefully on 1 January 1993, and the "velvet and un-velvet" revolutions of 1989 sweep the region (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 45: Căderea regimurilor comuniste în Europa", Poland votes freely in June 1989; the Ceaușescus are captured at Târgoviște, Czechoslovakia's "velvet divorce" makes Havel and Kováč presidents in 1993, and even Albania accepts multiparty politics (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
