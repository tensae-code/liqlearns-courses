# 1989 collapse of the communist regimes — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 35: Criza sistemului totalitar și colapsul regimurilor comuniste în Europa — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 45: Căderea regimurilor comuniste în Europa — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Wałęsa's Solidarity leads the workers against the workers' state; Charter 77's Czechoslovakia splits peacefully on 1 January 1993, and the "velvet and un-velvet" revolutions of 1989 sweep the region.
b) Poland votes freely in June 1989; the Ceaușescus are captured at Târgoviște, Czechoslovakia's "velvet divorce" makes Havel and Kováč presidents in 1993, and even Albania accepts multiparty politics.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
