# Political regimes of modern europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§31: Regimuri politice în Europa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Most states stay monarchies until 1914, but the liberal society of money and schooling is carried by the middle class, and after 1848 liberalism imposes itself across most of Europe.
