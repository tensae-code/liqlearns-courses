# Italy unifies cavour and the patriots — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§35: Unificarea Italiei" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After 1848 Italy is "only a geographical notion"; Cavour's great wave rises, Garibaldi's Thousand take Sicily and Naples, and the Kingdom of Italy is proclaimed at Turin under Victor Emmanuel II.
