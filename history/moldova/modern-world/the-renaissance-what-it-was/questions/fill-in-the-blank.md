# The renaissance what it was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 3: Cultura Europei: Renașterea și Umanismul" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A towns-born culture turns from God to man and nature; art goes secular and Renaissance building fuses antique, Romanesque and Gothic.
