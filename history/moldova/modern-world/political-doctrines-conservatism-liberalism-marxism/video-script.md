This lesson tells one story: Political doctrines conservatism liberalism marxism.

Frightened elites seek a theory for the old order; conservatives would burn Diderot's Encyclopaedia, liberals preach spontaneous harmony, and socialism, Marxism and anarchism answer from below.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.
