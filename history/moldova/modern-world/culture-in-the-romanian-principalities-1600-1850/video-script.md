# Video script — Culture in the romanian principalities 1600 1850

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Culture in the romanian principalities 1600 1850.

Ureche, Costin, Cantemir and Cantacuzino found Romanian historiography; primers are printed, and in 1835 Iași inaugurates its Academy.

Over 300 printed titles appear in 18th-century Moldavia and Wallachia; boyars hire foreign tutors for Greek, French and German, and Cantemir's learning wins European renown.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
