# Culture in the romanian principalities 1600 1850 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 27: Cultura în Principatele Române", Ureche, Costin, Cantemir and Cantacuzino found Romanian historiography; primers are printed, and in 1835 Iași inaugurates its Academy (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§50: Evoluţia culturii Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII", over 300 printed titles appear in 18th-century Moldavia and Wallachia; boyars hire foreign tutors for Greek, French and German, and Cantemir's learning wins European renown (Sub lesson 2)
