# Culture in the romanian principalities 1600 1850 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 27: Cultura în Principatele Române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ureche, Costin, Cantemir and Cantacuzino found Romanian historiography; primers are printed, and in 1835 Iași inaugurates its Academy.

**2.** Tell the story of "§50: Evoluţia culturii Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Over 300 printed titles appear in 18th-century Moldavia and Wallachia; boyars hire foreign tutors for Greek, French and German, and Cantemir's learning wins European renown.
