# Culture in the romanian principalities 1600 1850 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 27: Cultura în Principatele Române — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §50: Evoluţia culturii Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Ureche, Costin, Cantemir and Cantacuzino found Romanian historiography; primers are printed, and in 1835 Iași inaugurates its Academy.
