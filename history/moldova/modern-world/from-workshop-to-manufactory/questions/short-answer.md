# From workshop to manufactory — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 1: De la atelierul meșteșugăresc la manufactură" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** China gives paper, gunpowder and compass, India the finest cloth; guild masters resist the new looms while merchants seize the whole road from making to selling.
