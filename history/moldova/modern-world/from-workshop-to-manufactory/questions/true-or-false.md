# From workshop to manufactory — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unitatea IV, Lecția 1: De la atelierul meșteșugăresc la manufactură", China gives paper, gunpowder and compass, India the finest cloth; guild masters resist the new looms while merchants seize the whole road from making to selling
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
