# From workshop to manufactory — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 1: De la atelierul meșteșugăresc la manufactură" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** China gives paper, gunpowder and compass, India the finest cloth; guild masters resist the new looms while merchants seize the whole road from making to selling.
