# Video script — Moldova independence 1991

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Moldova independence 1991.

750,000 people at the 1989 Great National Assembly demand Romanian as state language in Latin script, and in 1991 the Republic of Moldova declares independence.

Gorbachev's perestroika frees speech; a Transnistrian republic is proclaimed against Chișinău, the Communist Party is suspended, and on 27 August 1991 over 600,000 people at the Great National Assembly demand independence.

In April 1988 the journal Nistru first demands the Latin script back; the Gagauz proclaim their own ASSR in November 1989; the tricolour is adopted on 27 April 1990, the coat of arms on 3 November 1990, and independence follows on 27 August 1991.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
