# Moldova independence 1991 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 16: Republica Moldova, stat independent și democratic", 750,000 people at the 1989 Great National Assembly demand Romanian as state language in Latin script, and in 1991 the Republic of Moldova declares independence (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 36: RSSM între 1985 și 1991. Proclamarea independenței Republicii Moldova", Gorbachev's perestroika frees speech; a Transnistrian republic is proclaimed against Chișinău, the Communist Party is suspended, and on 27 August 1991 over 600,000 people at the Great National Assembly demand independence (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
