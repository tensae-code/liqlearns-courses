# Moldova independence 1991 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 16: Republica Moldova, stat independent și democratic — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Tema 36: RSSM între 1985 și 1991. Proclamarea independenței Republicii Moldova — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) 750,000 people at the 1989 Great National Assembly demand Romanian as state language in Latin script, and in 1991 the Republic of Moldova declares independence.
b) Gorbachev's perestroika frees speech; a Transnistrian republic is proclaimed against Chișinău, the Communist Party is suspended, and on 27 August 1991 over 600,000 people at the Great National Assembly demand independence.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
