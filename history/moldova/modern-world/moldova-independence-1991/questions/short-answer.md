# Moldova independence 1991 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 16: Republica Moldova, stat independent și democratic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 750,000 people at the 1989 Great National Assembly demand Romanian as state language in Latin script, and in 1991 the Republic of Moldova declares independence.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 36: RSSM între 1985 și 1991. Proclamarea independenței Republicii Moldova" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gorbachev's perestroika frees speech; a Transnistrian republic is proclaimed against Chișinău, the Communist Party is suspended, and on 27 August 1991 over 600,000 people at the Great National Assembly demand independence.
