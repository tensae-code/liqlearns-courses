# Video script — The second world war course in europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The second world war course in europe.

Paulus capitulates at Stalingrad with 90,000 men and 24 generals and Germany loses the land initiative; Bulgaria's government falls on 9 September and joins the anti-Hitler coalition.

Germany demands Danzig after the pact of 23 August 1939; pupils must explain the western blitzkrieg, Athens falls on 30 April 1941 with swastikas over the Acropolis, and in April 1945 the Ruhr pocket closes.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
