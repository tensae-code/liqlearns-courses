# The second world war course in europe — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 22: Desfășurarea războiului", Paulus capitulates at Stalingrad with 90,000 men and 24 generals and Germany loses the land initiative; Bulgaria's government falls on 9 September and joins the anti-Hitler coalition (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 23: Al Doilea Război Mondial. Strategii și acțiuni militare (1939–1945)", Germany demands Danzig after the pact of 23 August 1939; pupils must explain the western blitzkrieg, Athens falls on 30 April 1941 with swastikas over the Acropolis, and in April 1945 the Ruhr pocket closes (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
