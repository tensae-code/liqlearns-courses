# The second world war course in europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 22: Desfășurarea războiului" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Paulus capitulates at Stalingrad with 90,000 men and 24 generals and Germany loses the land initiative; Bulgaria's government falls on 9 September and joins the anti-Hitler coalition.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 23: Al Doilea Război Mondial. Strategii și acțiuni militare (1939–1945)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Germany demands Danzig after the pact of 23 August 1939; pupils must explain the western blitzkrieg, Athens falls on 30 April 1941 with swastikas over the Acropolis, and in April 1945 the Ruhr pocket closes.
