# 1917 twice february and october in russia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 10: Rusia/Uniunea Sovietică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lenin's Bolsheviks take power from Kerenski's failing Provisional Government, civil war follows, NEP's "Soviet bourgeoisie" appears, then Stalin from 1928 forces industrialisation and collectivisation — by 1939 over 25 million have been repressed.

**2.** Tell the story of "Extensie curriculară: Revoluţia din februarie 1917. Lovitura de stat bolşevică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** February's revolution and the Bolshevik coup are studied as theory and practice of Bolshevism.
