# 1917 twice february and october in russia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 10: Rusia/Uniunea Sovietică" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lenin's Bolsheviks take power from Kerenski's failing Provisional Government, civil war follows, NEP's "Soviet bourgeoisie" appears, then Stalin from 1928 forces industrialisation and collectivisation — by 1939 over 25 million have been repressed.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Extensie curriculară: Revoluţia din februarie 1917. Lovitura de stat bolşevică" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** February's revolution and the Bolshevik coup are studied as theory and practice of Bolshevism.
