# Video script — 1917 twice february and october in russia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: 1917 twice february and october in russia.

Lenin's Bolsheviks take power from Kerenski's failing Provisional Government, civil war follows, NEP's "Soviet bourgeoisie" appears, then Stalin from 1928 forces industrialisation and collectivisation — by 1939 over 25 million have been repressed.

February's revolution and the Bolshevik coup are studied as theory and practice of Bolshevism.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
