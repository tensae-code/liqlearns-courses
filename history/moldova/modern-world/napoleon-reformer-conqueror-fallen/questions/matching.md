# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 8: Consulatul. Primul Imperiu Francez — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §13: Epoca napoleoniană şi impactul ei asupra societăţii europene — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Napoleon's armies carry the revolution across Europe and his Civil Code still governs France; master of the continent except England, he invades Russia in 1812 and meets half a million soldiers at Leipzig in 1813.
b) Bonaparte's dictatorship defends the bourgeoisie, amnesties the émigré nobles, blockades Britain — and of the Grande Armée only 20–30,000 men come out of Russia before the 1813 coalition forms.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
