# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 8: Consulatul. Primul Imperiu Francez" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Napoleon's armies carry the revolution across Europe and his Civil Code still governs France; master of the continent except England, he invades Russia in 1812 and meets half a million soldiers at Leipzig in 1813.

**2.** Tell the story of "§13: Epoca napoleoniană şi impactul ei asupra societăţii europene" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bonaparte's dictatorship defends the bourgeoisie, amnesties the émigré nobles, blockades Britain — and of the Grande Armée only 20–30,000 men come out of Russia before the 1813 coalition forms.
