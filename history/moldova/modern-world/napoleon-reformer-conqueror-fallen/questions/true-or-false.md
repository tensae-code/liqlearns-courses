# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 8: Consulatul. Primul Imperiu Francez", Napoleon's armies carry the revolution across Europe and his Civil Code still governs France; master of the continent except England, he invades Russia in 1812 and meets half a million soldiers at Leipzig in 1813 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§13: Epoca napoleoniană şi impactul ei asupra societăţii europene", Bonaparte's dictatorship defends the bourgeoisie, amnesties the émigré nobles, blockades Britain — and of the Grande Armée only 20–30,000 men come out of Russia before the 1813 coalition forms (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
