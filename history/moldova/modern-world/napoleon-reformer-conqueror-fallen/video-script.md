# Video script — Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen.

Napoleon's armies carry the revolution across Europe and his Civil Code still governs France; master of the continent except England, he invades Russia in 1812 and meets half a million soldiers at Leipzig in 1813.

Bonaparte's dictatorship defends the bourgeoisie, amnesties the émigré nobles, blockades Britain — and of the Grande Armée only 20–30,000 men come out of Russia before the 1813 coalition forms.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
