# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 2: Marea călătorie în jurul lumii" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Columbus and Vasco da Gama open the ocean roads, and in 1519 Magellan's Spanish ships circle South America and cross the Pacific on a three-year voyage around the world.
