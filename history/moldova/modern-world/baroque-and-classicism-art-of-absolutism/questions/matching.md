# Baroque and classicism art of absolutism — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 26: Iluminismul în cultură: baroc și clasicism — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §49: Stilurile artistice: baroc şi clasicism — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Classicism disciplines imagination on antique models in Louis XIV's France; Rembrandt's expressive painting crowns Dutch art, and Versailles becomes royal architecture itself.
b) Baroque is born as the Counter-Reformation's art in Catholic lands — Borromini and Maderno raise Rome — while Boileau's classicism binds theatre to the rule of the three unities and David founds French neoclassicism.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
