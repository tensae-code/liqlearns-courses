# Baroque and classicism art of absolutism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 26: Iluminismul în cultură: baroc și clasicism", classicism disciplines imagination on antique models in Louis XIV's France; Rembrandt's expressive painting crowns Dutch art, and Versailles becomes royal architecture itself (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§49: Stilurile artistice: baroc şi clasicism", baroque is born as the Counter-Reformation's art in Catholic lands — Borromini and Maderno raise Rome — while Boileau's classicism binds theatre to the rule of the three unities and David founds French neoclassicism (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
