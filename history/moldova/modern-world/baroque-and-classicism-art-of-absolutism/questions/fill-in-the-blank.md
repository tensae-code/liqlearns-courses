# Baroque and classicism art of absolutism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 26: Iluminismul în cultură: baroc și clasicism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Classicism disciplines imagination on antique models in Louis XIV's France; Rembrandt's expressive painting crowns Dutch art, and Versailles becomes royal architecture itself.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§49: Stilurile artistice: baroc şi clasicism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Baroque is born as the Counter-Reformation's art in Catholic lands — Borromini and Maderno raise Rome — while Boileau's classicism binds theatre to the rule of the three unities and David founds French neoclassicism.
