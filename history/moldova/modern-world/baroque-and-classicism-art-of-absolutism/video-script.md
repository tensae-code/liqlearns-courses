# Video script — Baroque and classicism art of absolutism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Baroque and classicism art of absolutism.

Classicism disciplines imagination on antique models in Louis XIV's France; Rembrandt's expressive painting crowns Dutch art, and Versailles becomes royal architecture itself.

Baroque is born as the Counter-Reformation's art in Catholic lands — Borromini and Maderno raise Rome — while Boileau's classicism binds theatre to the rule of the three unities and David founds French neoclassicism.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
