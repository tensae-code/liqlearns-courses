# Anti communist movements in postwar europe 1956 1968 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 42: Mișcări anticomuniste în Europa postbelică" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** De-Stalinisation blames Stalin alone; Hungary rises and Operation Whirlwind answers with artillery and some 6,000 Soviet tanks in Budapest, while Prague's reformers secretly beg Moscow to intervene.
