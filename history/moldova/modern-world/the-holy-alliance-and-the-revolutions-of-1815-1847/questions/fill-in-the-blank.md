# The holy alliance and the revolutions of 1815 1847 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§18: Europa „Sfintei Alianţe". Mişcări revoluţionare (1815–1847)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The French Revolution teaches peoples that sovereignty resides in the nation; Greece declares independence, the allied fleets destroy the Ottoman fleet at Navarino in 1827, but insurgents without the masses win only temporary successes.
