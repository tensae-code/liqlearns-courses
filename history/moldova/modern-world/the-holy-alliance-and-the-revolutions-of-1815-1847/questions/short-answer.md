# The holy alliance and the revolutions of 1815 1847 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§18: Europa „Sfintei Alianţe". Mişcări revoluţionare (1815–1847)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The French Revolution teaches peoples that sovereignty resides in the nation; Greece declares independence, the allied fleets destroy the Ottoman fleet at Navarino in 1827, but insurgents without the masses win only temporary successes.
