# The thirty years war and westphalia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 6: Europa în timpul Războiului de 30 de Ani" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Habsburg power alarms Europe into thirty years of war; France's victory at Rocroi in 1643 breaks the myth of Spanish invincibility, and the Peace of Westphalia (24 October 1648) redraws the map.
