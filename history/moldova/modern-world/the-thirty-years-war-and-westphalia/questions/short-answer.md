# The thirty years war and westphalia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 6: Europa în timpul Războiului de 30 de Ani" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Habsburg power alarms Europe into thirty years of war; France's victory at Rocroi in 1643 breaks the myth of Spanish invincibility, and the Peace of Westphalia (24 October 1648) redraws the map.
