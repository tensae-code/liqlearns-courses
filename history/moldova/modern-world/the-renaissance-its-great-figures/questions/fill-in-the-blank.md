# The renaissance its great figures — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea IV, Lecția 3: Marii reprezentanți ai Renașterii" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Humanist writers write in the people's own language and awaken national self-consciousness; Michelangelo's young David, sling in hand, stands for the citizen defending his city.
