# The moldavian assr 1924 1940 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 14: RASSM (1924–1940) și politica expansionistă a URSS" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Moscow builds a Moldavian ASSR on the Nistru's left bank as a claim on Bessarabia; collectivisation reaches 96.6% by 1937, the "kulaks" are destroyed, and the Latin script is banned as a Romanianising danger.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 18: Constituirea RASSM: politici și impact" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The memorandum of 4 February 1924 sets up the Moldavian ASSR; agriculture expands, then the liquidation of the "chiaburi" catalogues over 3,200 peasant households for destruction.
