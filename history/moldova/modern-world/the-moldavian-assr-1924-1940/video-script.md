# Video script — The moldavian assr 1924 1940

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The moldavian assr 1924 1940.

Moscow builds a Moldavian ASSR on the Nistru's left bank as a claim on Bessarabia; collectivisation reaches 96.6% by 1937, the "kulaks" are destroyed, and the Latin script is banned as a Romanianising danger.

The memorandum of 4 February 1924 sets up the Moldavian ASSR; agriculture expands, then the liquidation of the "chiaburi" catalogues over 3,200 peasant households for destruction.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
