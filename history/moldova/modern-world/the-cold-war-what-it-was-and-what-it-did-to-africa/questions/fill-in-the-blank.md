# The cold war what it was and what it did to africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 26: Relațiile internaționale în perioada 1945–1991. ONU" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two superpower blocs face off; the 1946 Paris conference settles the satellites' treaties, Germany splits into two states in 1949, and the UN is built with its Assembly, Security Council and Court.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 38: Formarea lumii bipolare. Războiul Rece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Churchill's Iron Curtain speech of 5 March 1946 opens open confrontation; the Warsaw Treaty Organisation stations Soviet troops to keep its regimes, the Berlin Wall embodies the split, and by the mid-1980s the world again nears nuclear war.
