# Video script — The cold war what it was and what it did to africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The cold war what it was and what it did to africa.

Two superpower blocs face off; the 1946 Paris conference settles the satellites' treaties, Germany splits into two states in 1949, and the UN is built with its Assembly, Security Council and Court.

Churchill's Iron Curtain speech of 5 March 1946 opens open confrontation; the Warsaw Treaty Organisation stations Soviet troops to keep its regimes, the Berlin Wall embodies the split, and by the mid-1980s the world again nears nuclear war.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
