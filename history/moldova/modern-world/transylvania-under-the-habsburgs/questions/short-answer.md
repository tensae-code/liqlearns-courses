# Transylvania under the habsburgs — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 13: Transilvania sub dominația habsburgică" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Vienna makes Transylvania its south-eastern military bastion, annexes Oltenia toward the Lower Danube, and reforms every corner of life — Bishop Inochentie Micu pleads the Romanians' cause.
