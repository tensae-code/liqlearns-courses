# Transylvania under the habsburgs — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 13: Transilvania sub dominația habsburgică", Vienna makes Transylvania its south-eastern military bastion, annexes Oltenia toward the Lower Danube, and reforms every corner of life — Bishop Inochentie Micu pleads the Romanians' cause
