# Transylvania under the habsburgs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 13: Transilvania sub dominația habsburgică" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Vienna makes Transylvania its south-eastern military bastion, annexes Oltenia toward the Lower Danube, and reforms every corner of life — Bishop Inochentie Micu pleads the Romanians' cause.
