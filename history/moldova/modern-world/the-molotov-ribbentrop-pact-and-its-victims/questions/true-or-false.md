Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 19: Pactul Ribbentrop-Molotov și consecințele lui", the secret additional protocol is the pact's core — Stalin and Hitler redraw Eastern Europe between them; Finland loses 23,000 soldiers and cedes Hanko for thirty years (Sub lesson 1)
