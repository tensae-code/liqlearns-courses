# The molotov ribbentrop pact and its victims — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 19: Pactul Ribbentrop-Molotov și consecințele lui" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The secret additional protocol is the pact's core — Stalin and Hitler redraw Eastern Europe between them; Finland loses 23,000 soldiers and cedes Hanko for thirty years.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 15: Pactul Ribbentrop–Molotov și impactul lui asupra destinului popoarelor din Europa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The secret protocol divides Europe; Finland cedes Hangö for thirty years, and within days of the Winter War's end Bessarabia, northern Bukovina and Herța are occupied.
