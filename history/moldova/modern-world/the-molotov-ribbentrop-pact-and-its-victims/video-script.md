# Video script — The molotov ribbentrop pact and its victims

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The molotov ribbentrop pact and its victims.

The secret additional protocol is the pact's core — Stalin and Hitler redraw Eastern Europe between them; Finland loses 23,000 soldiers and cedes Hanko for thirty years.

The secret protocol divides Europe; Finland cedes Hangö for thirty years, and within days of the Winter War's end Bessarabia, northern Bukovina and Herța are occupied.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
