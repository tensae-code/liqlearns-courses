# Wartime diplomacy and the founding of the un — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 27: Diplomaţie și coaliţii în perioada celui de-Al Doilea Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Tehran fixes the May 1944 landing in northern France with a supporting Soviet offensive; Potsdam's Truman, Churchill then Attlee agree a permanent UN with a Security Council.
