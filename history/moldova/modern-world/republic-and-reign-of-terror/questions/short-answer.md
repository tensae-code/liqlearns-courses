# Republic and reign of terror — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 7: Faza radicală și finală a Revoluției Franceze" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The republic hardens into Jacobin dictatorship; the Law of Suspects can make any Frenchman a suspect, terror devours its makers, and Napoleon exploits the exhaustion — Egypt's campaign even births Egyptology.

**2.** Tell the story of "§12: Prima Republică și dictatura iacobină" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Allied armies advance in summer 1792, the Jacobins seize power outside the law, and within a day of the Convention's turn, Robespierre and 22 leaders of the Terror are arrested.
