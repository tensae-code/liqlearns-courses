# Republic and reign of terror — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 7: Faza radicală și finală a Revoluției Franceze", the republic hardens into Jacobin dictatorship; the Law of Suspects can make any Frenchman a suspect, terror devours its makers, and Napoleon exploits the exhaustion — Egypt's campaign even births Egyptology (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§12: Prima Republică și dictatura iacobină", allied armies advance in summer 1792, the Jacobins seize power outside the law, and within a day of the Convention's turn, Robespierre and 22 leaders of the Terror are arrested (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
