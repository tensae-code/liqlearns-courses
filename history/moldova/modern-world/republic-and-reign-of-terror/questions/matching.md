# Republic and reign of terror — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 7: Faza radicală și finală a Revoluției Franceze — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §12: Prima Republică și dictatura iacobină — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The republic hardens into Jacobin dictatorship; the Law of Suspects can make any Frenchman a suspect, terror devours its makers, and Napoleon exploits the exhaustion — Egypt's campaign even births Egyptology.
b) Allied armies advance in summer 1792, the Jacobins seize power outside the law, and within a day of the Convention's turn, Robespierre and 22 leaders of the Terror are arrested.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
