# Video script — Republic and reign of terror

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Republic and reign of terror.

The republic hardens into Jacobin dictatorship; the Law of Suspects can make any Frenchman a suspect, terror devours its makers, and Napoleon exploits the exhaustion — Egypt's campaign even births Egyptology.

Allied armies advance in summer 1792, the Jacobins seize power outside the law, and within a day of the Convention's turn, Robespierre and 22 leaders of the Terror are arrested.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
