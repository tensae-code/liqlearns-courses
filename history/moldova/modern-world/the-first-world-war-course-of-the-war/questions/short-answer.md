# The first world war course of the war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 1: Desfășurarea războiului" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A local war turns world war; 6 million Entente and 3.5 million Alliance soldiers are mobilised in August 1914, Jutland is the greatest sea battle, and Brusilov's Russians break through in 1916.
