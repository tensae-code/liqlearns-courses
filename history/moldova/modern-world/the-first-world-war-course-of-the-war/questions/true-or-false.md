# The first world war course of the war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 1: Desfășurarea războiului", a local war turns world war; 6 million Entente and 3.5 million Alliance soldiers are mobilised in August 1914, Jutland is the greatest sea battle, and Brusilov's Russians break through in 1916
