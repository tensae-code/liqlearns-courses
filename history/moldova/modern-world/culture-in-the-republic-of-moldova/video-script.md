# Video script — Culture in the republic of moldova

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Culture in the republic of moldova.

Independence reopens the theatres — Guguță puppet theatre founded in 1992 — research passes to the ministries in 2018, and monuments to Blaga, Iorga and Eliade rise through the 1990s.

Science is proclaimed a national strategic priority; historic churches at Orhei are rebuilt, universities multiply, and O-Zone and Zdob și Zdub carry Moldovan pop and rock to the world.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
