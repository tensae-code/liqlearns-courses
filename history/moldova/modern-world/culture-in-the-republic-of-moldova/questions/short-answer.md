# Culture in the republic of moldova — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 43: Evoluția culturii în Republica Moldova" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Independence reopens the theatres — Guguță puppet theatre founded in 1992 — research passes to the ministries in 2018, and monuments to Blaga, Iorga and Eliade rise through the 1990s.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 55: Cultura şi ştiinţa în Republica Moldova" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Science is proclaimed a national strategic priority; historic churches at Orhei are rebuilt, universities multiply, and O-Zone and Zdob și Zdub carry Moldovan pop and rock to the world.
