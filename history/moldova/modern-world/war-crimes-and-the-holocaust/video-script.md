# Video script — War crimes and the holocaust

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: War crimes and the holocaust.

After Kristallnacht the persecution spreads; some 135,000 Romanian Jews perish in northern Transylvania, Roma nomads are deported in summer 1942, and Polish officers are shot in the back of the head and buried in common graves.

The Gulag deports whole peoples — 1,209,000 Soviet Germans, 479,000 Chechens and Ingush, 191,014 Crimean Tatars; the Holocaust's victim table is estimated, and Tokyo's tribunal judges Japan's war criminals from May 1946.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
