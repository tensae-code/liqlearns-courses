# War crimes and the holocaust — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 24: Crime de război. Holocaust" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Kristallnacht the persecution spreads; some 135,000 Romanian Jews perish in northern Transylvania, Roma nomads are deported in summer 1942, and Polish officers are shot in the back of the head and buried in common graves.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 28: Crime împotriva umanităţii: Gulag, Holocaust, Katyn" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Gulag deports whole peoples — 1,209,000 Soviet Germans, 479,000 Chechens and Ingush, 191,014 Crimean Tatars; the Holocaust's victim table is estimated, and Tokyo's tribunal judges Japan's war criminals from May 1946.
