# War crimes and the holocaust — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 24: Crime de război. Holocaust", after Kristallnacht the persecution spreads; some 135,000 Romanian Jews perish in northern Transylvania, Roma nomads are deported in summer 1942, and Polish officers are shot in the back of the head and buried in common graves (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 28: Crime împotriva umanităţii: Gulag, Holocaust, Katyn", the Gulag deports whole peoples — 1,209,000 Soviet Germans, 479,000 Chechens and Ingush, 191,014 Crimean Tatars; the Holocaust's victim table is estimated, and Tokyo's tribunal judges Japan's war criminals from May 1946 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
