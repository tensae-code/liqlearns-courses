# Tudor vladimirescus revolution 1821 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821", Tudor, a small boyar from Oltenia, raises the pandurs and addresses memoranda to sultan and emperors to block foreign intervention; in spring 1821 Chișinău fills with refugees from the Eterist rising (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu", Tudor expects Russia to back the Eteria and the Greeks to help the Romanians; Ypsilanti instead means to use the Principalities, Tudor reaches Colentina outside Bucharest, and the rising is crushed (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
