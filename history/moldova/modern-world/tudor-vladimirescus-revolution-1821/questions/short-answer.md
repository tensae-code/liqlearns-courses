# Tudor vladimirescus revolution 1821 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tudor, a small boyar from Oltenia, raises the pandurs and addresses memoranda to sultan and emperors to block foreign intervention; in spring 1821 Chișinău fills with refugees from the Eterist rising.

**2.** Tell the story of "§16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tudor expects Russia to back the Eteria and the Greeks to help the Romanians; Ypsilanti instead means to use the Principalities, Tudor reaches Colentina outside Bucharest, and the rising is crushed.
