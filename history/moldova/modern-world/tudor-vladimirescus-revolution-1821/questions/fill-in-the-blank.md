# Tudor vladimirescus revolution 1821 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tudor, a small boyar from Oltenia, raises the pandurs and addresses memoranda to sultan and emperors to block foreign intervention; in spring 1821 Chișinău fills with refugees from the Eterist rising.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tudor expects Russia to back the Eteria and the Greeks to help the Romanians; Ypsilanti instead means to use the Principalities, Tudor reaches Colentina outside Bucharest, and the rising is crushed.
