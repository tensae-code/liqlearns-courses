# Tudor vladimirescus revolution 1821 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 23: Revoluția de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu din 1821 — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §16: Răscoala de la 1821 de sub conducerea lui Tudor Vladimirescu — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Tudor, a small boyar from Oltenia, raises the pandurs and addresses memoranda to sultan and emperors to block foreign intervention; in spring 1821 Chișinău fills with refugees from the Eterist rising.
b) Tudor expects Russia to back the Eteria and the Greeks to help the Romanians; Ypsilanti instead means to use the Principalities, Tudor reaches Colentina outside Bucharest, and the rising is crushed.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
