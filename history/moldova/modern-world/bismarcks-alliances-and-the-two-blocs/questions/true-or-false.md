# Bismarcks alliances and the two blocs — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§43: Europa între afirmarea naţiunilor şi rivalitatea marilor puteri", Bismarck the puppeteer works the three emperors; the Triple Alliance of 1882 faces the Franco-Russian treaty of 1893, and the Hague conventions try to cage the new weapons (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 3: Rivalitatea Marilor Puteri și amenințarea păcii", the Berlin Congress of 1878 opens the rivalry; the Entente Cordiale of 1904 settles Egypt and Morocco between France and Britain and the blocs harden (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
