# Bismarcks alliances and the two blocs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§43: Europa între afirmarea naţiunilor şi rivalitatea marilor puteri" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bismarck the puppeteer works the three emperors; the Triple Alliance of 1882 faces the Franco-Russian treaty of 1893, and the Hague conventions try to cage the new weapons.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 3: Rivalitatea Marilor Puteri și amenințarea păcii" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Berlin Congress of 1878 opens the rivalry; the Entente Cordiale of 1904 settles Egypt and Morocco between France and Britain and the blocs harden.
