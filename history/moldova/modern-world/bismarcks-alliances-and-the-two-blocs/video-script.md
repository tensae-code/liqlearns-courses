# Video script — Bismarcks alliances and the two blocs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Bismarcks alliances and the two blocs.

Bismarck the puppeteer works the three emperors; the Triple Alliance of 1882 faces the Franco-Russian treaty of 1893, and the Hague conventions try to cage the new weapons.

The Berlin Congress of 1878 opens the rivalry; the Entente Cordiale of 1904 settles Egypt and Morocco between France and Britain and the blocs harden.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
