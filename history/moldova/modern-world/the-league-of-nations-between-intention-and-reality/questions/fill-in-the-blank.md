# The league of nations between intention and reality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 10: Liga Naţiunilor: între intenţie şi realitate" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The League's covenant is adopted on 28 April 1919; its real achievements include the Nansen passport for refugees (1922–38) and the fight against the opium trade.
