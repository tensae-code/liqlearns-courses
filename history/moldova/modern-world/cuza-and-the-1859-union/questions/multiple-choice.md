# Cuza and the 1859 union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Cuza is elected in both Moldavia and Wallachia in 1859 and unites them into one state — but Bessarabia, taken by Russia in 1812, is left outside.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
