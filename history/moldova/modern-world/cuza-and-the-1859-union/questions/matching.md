# Cuza and the 1859 union — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §32: Unirea Principatelor Române — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Cuza is elected in both Moldavia and Wallachia in 1859 and unites them into one state — but Bessarabia, taken by Russia in 1812, is left outside.
b) The ad-hoc assemblies show Europe the Romanians' will to unite; the sultan dissolves them by firman, the double election defeats the firman, and even Bessarabia's nobles draft an address on the union.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
