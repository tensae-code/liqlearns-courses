# Cuza and the 1859 union — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cuza is elected in both Moldavia and Wallachia in 1859 and unites them into one state — but Bessarabia, taken by Russia in 1812, is left outside.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§32: Unirea Principatelor Române" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The ad-hoc assemblies show Europe the Romanians' will to unite; the sultan dissolves them by firman, the double election defeats the firman, and even Bessarabia's nobles draft an address on the union.
