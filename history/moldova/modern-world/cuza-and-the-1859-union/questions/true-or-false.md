# Cuza and the 1859 union — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii", Cuza is elected in both Moldavia and Wallachia in 1859 and unites them into one state — but Bessarabia, taken by Russia in 1812, is left outside (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§32: Unirea Principatelor Române", the ad-hoc assemblies show Europe the Romanians' will to unite; the sultan dissolves them by firman, the double election defeats the firman, and even Bessarabia's nobles draft an address on the union (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
