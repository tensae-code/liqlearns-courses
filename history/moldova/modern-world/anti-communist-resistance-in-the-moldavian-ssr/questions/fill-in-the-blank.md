# Anti communist resistance in the moldavian ssr — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 32: Mișcarea anticomunistă în RSSM" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Starving villagers raid the grain stores; a leaflet "Truth for the people" earns its author seven years in Mordovia in 1964, and Mihai Moroșanu fights to save Stephen the Great's monument.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 43: Rezistența anticomunistă în RSSM" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 110 people are arrested for anti-Soviet resistance organisations; in October 1955 the Academy's own scientific conference recognises the "Romanian-Moldavian" linguistic identity the regime denied.
