# Video script — Anti communist resistance in the moldavian ssr

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Anti communist resistance in the moldavian ssr.

Starving villagers raid the grain stores; a leaflet "Truth for the people" earns its author seven years in Mordovia in 1964, and Mihai Moroșanu fights to save Stephen the Great's monument.

110 people are arrested for anti-Soviet resistance organisations; in October 1955 the Academy's own scientific conference recognises the "Romanian-Moldavian" linguistic identity the regime denied.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
