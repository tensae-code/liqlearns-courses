# Romanias 1866 constitution and constitutional monarchy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§34: Instituirea monarhiei constituţionale. Constituţia de la 1866" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A foreign prince of Hohenzollern-Sigmaringen swears to rule constitutionally, and on 29 June 1866 the Elective Assembly unanimously approves Romania's first constitution.
