# Romanias 1866 constitution and constitutional monarchy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§34: Instituirea monarhiei constituţionale. Constituţia de la 1866" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A foreign prince of Hohenzollern-Sigmaringen swears to rule constitutionally, and on 29 June 1866 the Elective Assembly unanimously approves Romania's first constitution.
