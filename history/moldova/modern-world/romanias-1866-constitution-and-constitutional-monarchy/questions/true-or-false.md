# Romanias 1866 constitution and constitutional monarchy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§34: Instituirea monarhiei constituţionale. Constituţia de la 1866", a foreign prince of Hohenzollern-Sigmaringen swears to rule constitutionally, and on 29 June 1866 the Elective Assembly unanimously approves Romania's first constitution
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
