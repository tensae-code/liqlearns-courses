# Video script — Famine repression and deportations in the moldavian ssr

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Famine repression and deportations in the moldavian ssr.

Drought-year 1945 deliveries are raised to 272,000 tonnes of grain; the 1949 deportation order confiscates vines, orchards, cattle and houses, and in 1951 Jehovah's Witnesses are deported to Tomsk and Irkutsk.

"divide et impera" sows discord between Romanians and Ukrainians; the party letter of 17 March 1949 opens the final assault on the village, and Operation "South" of 6 July 1949 becomes the peak of mass terror between Prut and Nistru.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
