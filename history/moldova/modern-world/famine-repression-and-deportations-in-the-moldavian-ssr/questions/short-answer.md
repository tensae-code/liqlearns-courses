# Famine repression and deportations in the moldavian ssr — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 31: Foametea, represiunile și deportările staliniste din RSSM" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Drought-year 1945 deliveries are raised to 272,000 tonnes of grain; the 1949 deportation order confiscates vines, orchards, cattle and houses, and in 1951 Jehovah's Witnesses are deported to Tomsk and Irkutsk.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 35: Reinstaurarea regimului comunist în RSSM. Colectivizarea forțată, foametea și deportările" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** "divide et impera" sows discord between Romanians and Ukrainians; the party letter of 17 March 1949 opens the final assault on the village, and Operation "South" of 6 July 1949 becomes the peak of mass terror between Prut and Nistru.
