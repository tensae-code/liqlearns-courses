# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 6: Revoluţia Franceză. Faza ascendentă – de la supus la cetăţean — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §12: Revoluţia Franceză de la sfârşitul secolului al XVIII-lea — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Louis XVI recognises the Constituent Assembly, the night of 4 August 1789 abolishes privilege, and Marat's "Friend of the People" turns the press into a revolutionary weapon.
b) The Old Regime's crisis hides behind an appearance of supremacy; Sieyès asks "What is the Third Estate?", and the Champ de Mars demonstration for abolishing royal power is put down by arms.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
