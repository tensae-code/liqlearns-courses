# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 6: Revoluţia Franceză. Faza ascendentă – de la supus la cetăţean" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Louis XVI recognises the Constituent Assembly, the night of 4 August 1789 abolishes privilege, and Marat's "Friend of the People" turns the press into a revolutionary weapon.

**2.** Tell the story of "§12: Revoluţia Franceză de la sfârşitul secolului al XVIII-lea" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Old Regime's crisis hides behind an appearance of supremacy; Sieyès asks "What is the Third Estate?", and the Champ de Mars demonstration for abolishing royal power is put down by arms.
