# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 6: Revoluţia Franceză. Faza ascendentă – de la supus la cetăţean", Louis XVI recognises the Constituent Assembly, the night of 4 August 1789 abolishes privilege, and Marat's "Friend of the People" turns the press into a revolutionary weapon (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§12: Revoluţia Franceză de la sfârşitul secolului al XVIII-lea", the Old Regime's crisis hides behind an appearance of supremacy; Sieyès asks "What is the Third Estate?", and the Champ de Mars demonstration for abolishing royal power is put down by arms (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
