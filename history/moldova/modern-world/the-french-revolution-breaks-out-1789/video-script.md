# Video script — The french revolution breaks out 1789

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The french revolution breaks out 1789.

Louis XVI recognises the Constituent Assembly, the night of 4 August 1789 abolishes privilege, and Marat's "Friend of the People" turns the press into a revolutionary weapon.

The Old Regime's crisis hides behind an appearance of supremacy; Sieyès asks "What is the Third Estate?", and the Champ de Mars demonstration for abolishing royal power is put down by arms.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
