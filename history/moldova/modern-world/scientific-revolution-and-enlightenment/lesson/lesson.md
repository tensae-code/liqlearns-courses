# Scientific revolution and enlightenment

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Lecția 4: Iluminismul – raţiune şi viziuni modernizatoare

Telescopes and microscopes remake knowledge; Galileo is condemned for putting the Sun at the centre, while Locke grounds the state in a social contract defending the property a man's labour creates.

## Sub lesson 2 — §10: Iluminismul – revoluţia ideilor

Locke's natural freedom, Montesquieu's twenty years on The Spirit of Laws and three kinds of government, and Kant's Germany carry the revolution of ideas.
