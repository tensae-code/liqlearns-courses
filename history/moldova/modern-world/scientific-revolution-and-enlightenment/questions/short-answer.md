# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 4: Iluminismul – raţiune şi viziuni modernizatoare" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Telescopes and microscopes remake knowledge; Galileo is condemned for putting the Sun at the centre, while Locke grounds the state in a social contract defending the property a man's labour creates.

**2.** Tell the story of "§10: Iluminismul – revoluţia ideilor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Locke's natural freedom, Montesquieu's twenty years on The Spirit of Laws and three kinds of government, and Kant's Germany carry the revolution of ideas.
