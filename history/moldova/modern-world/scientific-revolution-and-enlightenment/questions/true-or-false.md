# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 4: Iluminismul – raţiune şi viziuni modernizatoare", telescopes and microscopes remake knowledge; Galileo is condemned for putting the Sun at the centre, while Locke grounds the state in a social contract defending the property a man's labour creates (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§10: Iluminismul – revoluţia ideilor", Locke's natural freedom, Montesquieu's twenty years on The Spirit of Laws and three kinds of government, and Kant's Germany carry the revolution of ideas (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
