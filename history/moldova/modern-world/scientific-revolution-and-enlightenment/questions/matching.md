# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 4: Iluminismul – raţiune şi viziuni modernizatoare — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §10: Iluminismul – revoluţia ideilor — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Telescopes and microscopes remake knowledge; Galileo is condemned for putting the Sun at the centre, while Locke grounds the state in a social contract defending the property a man's labour creates.
b) Locke's natural freedom, Montesquieu's twenty years on The Spirit of Laws and three kinds of government, and Kant's Germany carry the revolution of ideas.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
