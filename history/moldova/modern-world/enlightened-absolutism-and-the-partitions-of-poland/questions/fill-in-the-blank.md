# Enlightened absolutism and the partitions of poland — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 12: Absolutismul luminat. Imperiul Rus. Imperiul Habsburgic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Five powers now rule Europe's balance; Joseph II's tolerance and serfdom patents are rolled back by Leopold II, Peter I and Catherine II build Russia's might, and Poland-Lithuania is carved up in three partitions.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14: Absolutismul luminat în Europa în secolul al XVIII-lea" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Joseph II issues the Patent abolishing serfdom, Catherine II preaches citizens' equality under the same laws, and Peter I's reforms are the case study of modernisation from the throne.
