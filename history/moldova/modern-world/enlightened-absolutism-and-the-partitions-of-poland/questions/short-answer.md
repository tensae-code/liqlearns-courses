# Enlightened absolutism and the partitions of poland — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 12: Absolutismul luminat. Imperiul Rus. Imperiul Habsburgic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Five powers now rule Europe's balance; Joseph II's tolerance and serfdom patents are rolled back by Leopold II, Peter I and Catherine II build Russia's might, and Poland-Lithuania is carved up in three partitions.

**2.** Tell the story of "§14: Absolutismul luminat în Europa în secolul al XVIII-lea" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Joseph II issues the Patent abolishing serfdom, Catherine II preaches citizens' equality under the same laws, and Peter I's reforms are the case study of modernisation from the throne.
