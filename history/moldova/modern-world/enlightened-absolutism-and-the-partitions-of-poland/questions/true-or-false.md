# Enlightened absolutism and the partitions of poland — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 12: Absolutismul luminat. Imperiul Rus. Imperiul Habsburgic", five powers now rule Europe's balance; Joseph II's tolerance and serfdom patents are rolled back by Leopold II, Peter I and Catherine II build Russia's might, and Poland-Lithuania is carved up in three partitions (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§14: Absolutismul luminat în Europa în secolul al XVIII-lea", Joseph II issues the Patent abolishing serfdom, Catherine II preaches citizens' equality under the same laws, and Peter I's reforms are the case study of modernisation from the throne (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
