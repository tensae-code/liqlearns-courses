# Video script — Enlightened absolutism and the partitions of poland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Enlightened absolutism and the partitions of poland.

Five powers now rule Europe's balance; Joseph II's tolerance and serfdom patents are rolled back by Leopold II, Peter I and Catherine II build Russia's might, and Poland-Lithuania is carved up in three partitions.

Joseph II issues the Patent abolishing serfdom, Catherine II preaches citizens' equality under the same laws, and Peter I's reforms are the case study of modernisation from the throne.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
