# Romanias war of independence 1877 1878 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§44: Obţinerea independenţei României" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Captain Mărgineanu's and Major Șonțu's battalions raise the tricolour over the redoubt and fall; Russia threatens to disarm the Romanian army that saved its front, and southern Bessarabia is re-annexed in the peace.
