# Bessarabia under russian rule administration — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 21: Administrarea Basarabiei și a teritoriilor din stânga Nistrului" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tsarism rushes to integrate the annexed province for its Balkan strategy; Bessarabia is folded into Novorossiya and in 1873 made a plain gubernia with no self-rule.

**2.** Tell the story of "§25: Lichidarea autonomiei Basarabiei" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Governor Vorontsov presses integration from 1823; colonists found 24 new localities by the early 1840s, Capodistria and Scarlat Sturdza frame the province's first order, and the native peasants' lot is the hardest.
