# Bessarabia under russian rule administration — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 21: Administrarea Basarabiei și a teritoriilor din stânga Nistrului — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §25: Lichidarea autonomiei Basarabiei — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Tsarism rushes to integrate the annexed province for its Balkan strategy; Bessarabia is folded into Novorossiya and in 1873 made a plain gubernia with no self-rule.
b) Governor Vorontsov presses integration from 1823; colonists found 24 new localities by the early 1840s, Capodistria and Scarlat Sturdza frame the province's first order, and the native peasants' lot is the hardest.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
