# Video script — Bessarabia under russian rule administration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Bessarabia under russian rule administration.

Tsarism rushes to integrate the annexed province for its Balkan strategy; Bessarabia is folded into Novorossiya and in 1873 made a plain gubernia with no self-rule.

Governor Vorontsov presses integration from 1823; colonists found 24 new localities by the early 1840s, Capodistria and Scarlat Sturdza frame the province's first order, and the native peasants' lot is the hardest.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
