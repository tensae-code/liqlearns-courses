# Video script — Bessarabia economy society and peoples 1812 1850

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Bessarabia economy society and peoples 1812 1850.

Cut off from the Romanian market, Bessarabia exports livestock, wine and tobacco through Noua Suliță; Russian colonisation doubles its population by 1850 and remakes its ethnic map.

An agrarian province of vines, orchards and cattle; the state leases out the Danube mouths and southern lakes, and the Heers' Sărata farm-machinery factory shows industry's first sparks.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
