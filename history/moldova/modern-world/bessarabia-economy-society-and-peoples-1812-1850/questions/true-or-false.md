# Bessarabia economy society and peoples 1812 1850 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 22: Viața economică și socială a Basarabiei în prima jumătate a secolului al XIX-lea", cut off from the Romanian market, Bessarabia exports livestock, wine and tobacco through Noua Suliță; Russian colonisation doubles its population by 1850 and remakes its ethnic map (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§26: Economia Basarabiei (1812–1914)", an agrarian province of vines, orchards and cattle; the state leases out the Danube mouths and southern lakes, and the Heers' Sărata farm-machinery factory shows industry's first sparks (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
