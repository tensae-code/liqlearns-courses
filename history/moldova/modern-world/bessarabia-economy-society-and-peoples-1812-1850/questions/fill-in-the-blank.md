# Bessarabia economy society and peoples 1812 1850 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 22: Viața economică și socială a Basarabiei în prima jumătate a secolului al XIX-lea" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cut off from the Romanian market, Bessarabia exports livestock, wine and tobacco through Noua Suliță; Russian colonisation doubles its population by 1850 and remakes its ethnic map.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26: Economia Basarabiei (1812–1914)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An agrarian province of vines, orchards and cattle; the state leases out the Danube mouths and southern lakes, and the Heers' Sărata farm-machinery factory shows industry's first sparks.
