# Bessarabia economy society and peoples 1812 1850 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 22: Viața economică și socială a Basarabiei în prima jumătate a secolului al XIX-lea — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §26: Economia Basarabiei (1812–1914) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Cut off from the Romanian market, Bessarabia exports livestock, wine and tobacco through Noua Suliță; Russian colonisation doubles its population by 1850 and remakes its ethnic map.
b) An agrarian province of vines, orchards and cattle; the state leases out the Danube mouths and southern lakes, and the Heers' Sărata farm-machinery factory shows industry's first sparks.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
