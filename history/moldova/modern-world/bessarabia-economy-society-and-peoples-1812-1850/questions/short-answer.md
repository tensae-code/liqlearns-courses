# Bessarabia economy society and peoples 1812 1850 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 22: Viața economică și socială a Basarabiei în prima jumătate a secolului al XIX-lea" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cut off from the Romanian market, Bessarabia exports livestock, wine and tobacco through Noua Suliță; Russian colonisation doubles its population by 1850 and remakes its ethnic map.

**2.** Tell the story of "§26: Economia Basarabiei (1812–1914)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An agrarian province of vines, orchards and cattle; the state leases out the Danube mouths and southern lakes, and the Heers' Sărata farm-machinery factory shows industry's first sparks.
