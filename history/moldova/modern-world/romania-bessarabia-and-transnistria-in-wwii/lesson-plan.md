# Lesson plan — Romania bessarabia and transnistria in wwii

**Course:** History — Moldova · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Romania bessarabia and transnistria in wwii
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The contemporary age whole: the world wars, Bessarabia's union and losses, the Moldavian SSR, famine and deportations, independence and the Transnistria war (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Romania bessarabia and transnistria in wwii as Moldova's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The contemporary age whole: the world wars, Bessarabia's union and losses, the Moldavian SSR, famine and deportations, independence and the Transnistria war — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tema 23: România, Basarabia și Transnistria în anii celui de-al Doilea Război Mondial** — Carol II falls, the National-Legionary state is proclaimed in September 1940, Romania enters the war in 1941, and the Iași–Chișinău offensive of 20 August 1944 joins two Soviet fronts at Leușeni.
2. **Tema 25: Participarea României în cel de-Al Doilea Război Mondial** — Democratic Romania of 1918–38 is drawn into war; American B-24s raid Ploiești on 1 August 1943, Soviet troops enter Bucharest on 31 August 1944, and Romania joins the United Nations coalition.
3. **Tema 26: Basarabia și Transnistria în perioada celui de-Al Doilea Război Mondial** — The Romanian administration organises the Transnistria Governorate between the rivers while whole urban quarters of Bessarabia's towns are destroyed.
