# Video script — Romania bessarabia and transnistria in wwii

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Romania bessarabia and transnistria in wwii.

Carol II falls, the National-Legionary state is proclaimed in September 1940, Romania enters the war in 1941, and the Iași–Chișinău offensive of 20 August 1944 joins two Soviet fronts at Leușeni.

Democratic Romania of 1918–38 is drawn into war; American B-24s raid Ploiești on 1 August 1943, Soviet troops enter Bucharest on 31 August 1944, and Romania joins the United Nations coalition.

The Romanian administration organises the Transnistria Governorate between the rivers while whole urban quarters of Bessarabia's towns are destroyed.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
