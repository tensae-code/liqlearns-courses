# Romania bessarabia and transnistria in wwii — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 23: România, Basarabia și Transnistria în anii celui de-al Doilea Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Carol II falls, the National-Legionary state is proclaimed in September 1940, Romania enters the war in 1941, and the Iași–Chișinău offensive of 20 August 1944 joins two Soviet fronts at Leușeni.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 25: Participarea României în cel de-Al Doilea Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Democratic Romania of 1918–38 is drawn into war; American B-24s raid Ploiești on 1 August 1943, Soviet troops enter Bucharest on 31 August 1944, and Romania joins the United Nations coalition.
