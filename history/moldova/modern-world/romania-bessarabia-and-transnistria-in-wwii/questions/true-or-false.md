# Romania bessarabia and transnistria in wwii — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 23: România, Basarabia și Transnistria în anii celui de-al Doilea Război Mondial", Carol II falls, the National-Legionary state is proclaimed in September 1940, Romania enters the war in 1941, and the Iași–Chișinău offensive of 20 August 1944 joins two Soviet fronts at Leușeni (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Tema 25: Participarea României în cel de-Al Doilea Război Mondial", democratic Romania of 1918–38 is drawn into war; American B-24s raid Ploiești on 1 August 1943, Soviet troops enter Bucharest on 31 August 1944, and Romania joins the United Nations coalition (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
