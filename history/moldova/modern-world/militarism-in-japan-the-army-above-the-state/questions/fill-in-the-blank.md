# Militarism in japan the army above the state — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 11: Extremul Orient" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Japan democratises in the 1920s — universal suffrage in 1925, 14 million voters — then turns militarist; in China the communists, beaten at Shanghai and Canton in 1927, take to guerrilla war in Hunan.
