# The 1848 revolution in the romanian lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§20: Revoluţia din 1848–1849 în Ţările Române" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Rosetti, Brătianu and Heliade Rădulescu prepare a politico-military plan; the Moldavian petition-proclamation and Transylvania's Blaj Pronouncement demand national and democratic rights.
