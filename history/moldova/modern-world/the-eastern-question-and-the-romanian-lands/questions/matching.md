# The eastern question and the romanian lands — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lecția 16: Războaiele ruso-austro-otomane și consecințele lor pentru Țările Române — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §23: Principatele Române în contextul Chestiunii Orientale — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Vienna's relief in 1683 tips the balance; war after war crosses the Principalities, the Eastern Question sharpens as Ottoman power fades, and colonisation of the steppe follows the armies.
b) Ottoman expansion halts and decline begins; Cantemir signs the Lutsk treaty with Peter the Great on 13 April 1711 against Poland's designs, Brâncoveanu balances between Turks and Russians, and Rumyantsev wins at the Larga lakes in 1770.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
