# The eastern question and the romanian lands — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lecția 16: Războaiele ruso-austro-otomane și consecințele lor pentru Țările Române", Vienna's relief in 1683 tips the balance; war after war crosses the Principalities, the Eastern Question sharpens as Ottoman power fades, and colonisation of the steppe follows the armies (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§23: Principatele Române în contextul Chestiunii Orientale", Ottoman expansion halts and decline begins; Cantemir signs the Lutsk treaty with Peter the Great on 13 April 1711 against Poland's designs, Brâncoveanu balances between Turks and Russians, and Rumyantsev wins at the Larga lakes in 1770 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
