# The eastern question and the romanian lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lecția 16: Războaiele ruso-austro-otomane și consecințele lor pentru Țările Române" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Vienna's relief in 1683 tips the balance; war after war crosses the Principalities, the Eastern Question sharpens as Ottoman power fades, and colonisation of the steppe follows the armies.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23: Principatele Române în contextul Chestiunii Orientale" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ottoman expansion halts and decline begins; Cantemir signs the Lutsk treaty with Peter the Great on 13 April 1711 against Poland's designs, Brâncoveanu balances between Turks and Russians, and Rumyantsev wins at the Larga lakes in 1770.
