# Video script — The eastern question and the romanian lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The eastern question and the romanian lands.

Vienna's relief in 1683 tips the balance; war after war crosses the Principalities, the Eastern Question sharpens as Ottoman power fades, and colonisation of the steppe follows the armies.

Ottoman expansion halts and decline begins; Cantemir signs the Lutsk treaty with Peter the Great on 13 April 1711 against Poland's designs, Brâncoveanu balances between Turks and Russians, and Rumyantsev wins at the Larga lakes in 1770.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
