# Video script — Daily life in modern europe and the romanian lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Daily life in modern europe and the romanian lands.

World trade spreads new goods and tastes; in the Principalities oriental furniture gives way to European style in towns while village boyar houses stay rustic.

European expansion spreads new goods and a new public sphere — coffee houses and gathering places where people read, argue and form opinion.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
