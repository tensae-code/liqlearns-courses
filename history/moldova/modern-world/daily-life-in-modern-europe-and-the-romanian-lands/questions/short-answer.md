# Daily life in modern europe and the romanian lands — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lecția 19: Viața cotidiană în Europa Occidentală și în Țările Române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** World trade spreads new goods and tastes; in the Principalities oriental furniture gives way to European style in towns while village boyar houses stay rustic.

**2.** Tell the story of "§8: Mod de viaţă şi de gândire în Europa Occidentală şi în Ţările Române" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** European expansion spreads new goods and a new public sphere — coffee houses and gathering places where people read, argue and form opinion.
