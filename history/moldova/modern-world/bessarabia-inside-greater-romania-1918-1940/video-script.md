# Video script — Bessarabia inside greater romania 1918 1940

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Bessarabia inside greater romania 1918 1940.

Land reform expropriates the estates peasants had already seized in 1917; 600 km of national roads and over 4,000 new bridges tie Bessarabia to the rest of the country by 1940.

The Sfatul Țării deputies who on 27 November 1918 vote both the agrarian reform and Bessarabia's unconditional union; parties fuse with the Old Kingdom's and maize and vines lead the province's farming.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
