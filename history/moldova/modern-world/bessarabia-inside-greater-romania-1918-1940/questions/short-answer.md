# Bessarabia inside greater romania 1918 1940 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 13: Basarabia în cadrul României Mari (1918–1940)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Land reform expropriates the estates peasants had already seized in 1917; 600 km of national roads and over 4,000 new bridges tie Bessarabia to the rest of the country by 1940.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 22: Basarabia în componența României Mari" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Sfatul Țării deputies who on 27 November 1918 vote both the agrarian reform and Bessarabia's unconditional union; parties fuse with the Old Kingdom's and maize and vines lead the province's farming.
