# The first world war versailles and the results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Tema 1: Urmările războiului" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Four empires — Russian, German, Austro-Hungarian, Ottoman — disintegrate; Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia and a reborn Poland appear on the map.

**2.** Tell the story of "Tema 6: Consecințele Primului Război Mondial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Industry falls 20%, disease spreads, and the Romanian army's intervention removes Béla Kun's Hungarian Soviet Republic — the war's bill is counted province by province.
