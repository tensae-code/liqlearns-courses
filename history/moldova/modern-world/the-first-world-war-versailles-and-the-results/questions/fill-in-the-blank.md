# The first world war versailles and the results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 1: Urmările războiului" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Four empires — Russian, German, Austro-Hungarian, Ottoman — disintegrate; Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia and a reborn Poland appear on the map.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 6: Consecințele Primului Război Mondial" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Industry falls 20%, disease spreads, and the Romanian army's intervention removes Béla Kun's Hungarian Soviet Republic — the war's bill is counted province by province.
