# The first world war versailles and the results

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The first world war versailles and the results" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 1: Urmările războiului

Four empires — Russian, German, Austro-Hungarian, Ottoman — disintegrate; Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia and a reborn Poland appear on the map.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tema 6: Consecințele Primului Război Mondial

Industry falls 20%, disease spreads, and the Romanian army's intervention removes Béla Kun's Hungarian Soviet Republic — the war's bill is counted province by province.
