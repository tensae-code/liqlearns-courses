# Video script — Michael the brave first union 1600

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Michael the brave first union 1600.

Michael the Brave brings Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule in 1600; his seal joins the eagle, the aurochs head and the Transylvanian lions.

Orphan boy Patrașcu marches with Michael's army over the Carpathians and rests in a village on the Oituz, seeing the union as a soldier's road.

Michael buys his throne from the Turks, turns on them, and enters Alba Iulia on 1 November 1599 to hold Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
