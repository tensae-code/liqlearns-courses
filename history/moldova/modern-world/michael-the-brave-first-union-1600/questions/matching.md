# Michael the brave first union 1600 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Tema 13: Întâiul unificator de țară: Mihai Viteazul — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Bucuria lecturii: Oastea Unirii — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Michael the Brave brings Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule in 1600; his seal joins the eagle, the aurochs head and the Transylvanian lions.
b) Orphan boy Patrașcu marches with Michael's army over the Carpathians and rests in a village on the Oituz, seeing the union as a soldier's road.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
