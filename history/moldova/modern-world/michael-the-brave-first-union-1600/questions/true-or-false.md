# Michael the brave first union 1600 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tema 13: Întâiul unificator de țară: Mihai Viteazul", Michael the Brave brings Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule in 1600; his seal joins the eagle, the aurochs head and the Transylvanian lions (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Bucuria lecturii: Oastea Unirii", orphan boy Patrașcu marches with Michael's army over the Carpathians and rests in a village on the Oituz, seeing the union as a soldier's road (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
