# Michael the brave first union 1600 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 13: Întâiul unificator de țară: Mihai Viteazul" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Michael the Brave brings Wallachia, Transylvania and Moldavia under one rule in 1600; his seal joins the eagle, the aurochs head and the Transylvanian lions.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bucuria lecturii: Oastea Unirii" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Orphan boy Patrașcu marches with Michael's army over the Carpathians and rests in a village on the Oituz, seeing the union as a soldier's road.
