# Romania and the versailles system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tema 11: România și Sistemul de la Versailles" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The great powers inform the small allies of the Austrian treaty's terms rather than negotiate them; Article 59 disposes of Bukovina, and the Bessarabian question poisons Romanian-Soviet relations until 28 June 1940.
