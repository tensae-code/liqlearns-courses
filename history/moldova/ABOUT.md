## The way of the books

Moldova's own way, as filed book by book:

- **Book 1 (Moldova Grade 4 moldova-g04-history):** a child's first history book taught as dialogues with Clio, muse of history — every theme opens with a schoolchild asking Clio questions, and the national story (Geto-Dacians → Moldavia → unions → independence) is interleaved with family, village and heritage work.
- **Book 2 (Moldova Grade 5 moldova-g05-history):** a full ancient-history course (Prehistory → Ancient Orient → Greece → Thraco-Geto-Dacians → Rome → Dacians and Romans → Decline) where every universal topic is paired with the ancestors' own space — Cucuteni-Tripolie, Sarmizegetusa, Roman Dacia — and every lesson ends in a "Laborator istoric" of source analysis.
- **Book 3 (Moldova Grade 6 moldova-g06-history):** the medieval year is taught in three acts (early / middle / late Middle Ages) plus the birth of the modern world, with the Romanian lands woven through every act — feudalism, Byzantium, the Crusades and the Ottomans are all read from Moldavia's and Wallachia's side of the story.
- **Book 4 (Moldova Grade 7 moldova-g07-history):** the first modern period (mid-1600s–mid-1800s) is taught as a double story — Western revolutions and enlightenment on one track, the Romanian Principalities squeezed between Ottoman, Habsburg and Russian empires on the other — ending with a whole unit on Bessarabia under Russian rule after 1812.
- **Book 6 (Moldova Grade 9 moldova-g09-history):** the whole contemporary age (1914–today) is told with Bessarabia/Moldova at its centre — the 1918 union, the Moldavian ASSR, the 1940 annexation, famine and deportations, anti-communist resistance, independence and the Transnistria war each get their own theme beside the world story.
- **Book 7 (Moldova Grade 11 moldova-g11-history):** the lycée modern-age course (§1–§53, each with Curs / Dosar source files / Studiu de caz) runs the world story and the Romanian story in parallel chapters — and gives Bessarabia under tsarism a whole chapter of its own, "from autonomy to gubernia".
- **Book 8 (Moldova Grade 12 moldova-g12-history):** the final lycée year re-runs the whole contemporary age at examination depth — every theme built as Jurnal istoric / Timp istoric / Dicționar istoric with primary sources — and closes not with wars but with global problems, migration, values and four local-history projects.
- **Book 5 (Moldova Grade 8 moldova-g08-history):** UNREAD — no way of the book can be filed; the file was never held (see Honesty notes).
