# Colonisation its effects on africa — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§46: Evoluţia sistemului colonial (sec. XIX – începutul sec. XX)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Colonisation wars shatter populations across Africa, Asia and Latin America; in 1768 Amerindian chiefs petition the English, and the book prints an appeal "to the nations of the world" against the colonial partition — Sudan, Uganda, Niger, Orange and Transvaal named among the conquered.
