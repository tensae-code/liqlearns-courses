# From stone tools to farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unitatea I, Lecția 2: Ocupațiile omului în Preistorie" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Ice-age hunters sew clothes from skins and master fire, then the turn comes — people stop only taking from nature and begin producing, farming and herding.
