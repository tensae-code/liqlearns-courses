# Find History in Your Own Locality — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where do you come from?
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Every child traces back to a common family or clan ancestor, reads the clan's timeline, and finds their own place in the line — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Clans in the local community / Tribes in the community
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Families sharing one ancestor form clans, clans sharing one culture form tribes — children name and map the original groups of their own place — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Significant events in the history of the district
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The district's turning-point events are chosen, dated and shown on one timeline — a first local history written by the class itself — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Province's history
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The same turning-point method comes home — each province's significant political, social, spiritual and disaster events are listed and drawn on its own timeline — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Important person – province
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: One named provincial figure, one contribution — children describe what their own province's leader did and why the province remembers them — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
