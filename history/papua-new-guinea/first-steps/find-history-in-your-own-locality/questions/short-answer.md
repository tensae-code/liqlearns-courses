# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** History lives in your own place — your family's ancestor, your clan and tribe, your district's turning points and its remembered leaders — and you can trace, date and write it yourself.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Clans in the local community / Tribes in the community”?
**Answer:** Families sharing one ancestor form clans, clans sharing one culture form tribes — children name and map the original groups of their own place.
