# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Where do you come from?”.
**Answer: False** — History lives in your own place — your family's ancestor, your clan and tribe, your district's turning points and its remembered leaders — and you can trace, date and write it yourself.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Families sharing one ancestor form clans, clans sharing one culture form tribes — children name and map the original groups of their own place.
**Answer: True**
