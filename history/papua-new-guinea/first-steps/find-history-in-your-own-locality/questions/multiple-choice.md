# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Where do you come from?”?
a) Every child traces back to a common family or clan ancestor, reads the clan's timeline, and finds their own place in the line.  b) The nation's turning points — discovery, the coming of missionaries, Independence and international events, with natural disasters and economic shocks beside them — are listed, explained and drawn on one national timeline.  c) Messages once travelled by garamut slit-gong, conch shell and kundu drum, and work by dugout canoe, salt-making and stone tools; modern communication and technology arrive and the class weighs what was gained and what was lost.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Every child traces back to a common family or clan ancestor, reads the clan's timeline, and finds their own place in the line.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
