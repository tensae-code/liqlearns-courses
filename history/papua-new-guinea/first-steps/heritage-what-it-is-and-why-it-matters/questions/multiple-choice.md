# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Traditional Warriors / Time of magicians and sorcerers / Worship of spirits / Comparing myths and legends”?
a) Each clan remembers its great warriors, magicians and spirits in legends; children tell one, then compare the legends side by side.  b) The record itself changes — film, internet, email and storage devices now carry pictures and sound to the whole world at once, where oral and written records once travelled slowly.  c) Explorers bring canned beef and rice; native sugarcane, banana and pandanus give ground — for taste, for saved garden time, for prestige, and because town life leaves no land to grow — and shelter and clothing (the meri blouse beside bark cloth) change the same way.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Each clan remembers its great warriors, magicians and spirits in legends; children tell one, then compare the legends side by side.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
