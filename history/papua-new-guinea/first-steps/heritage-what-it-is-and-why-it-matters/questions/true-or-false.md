# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Traditional Warriors / Time of magicians and sorcerers / Worship of spirits / Comparing myths and legends”.
**Answer: False** — Heritage is what a people hands down — its legends, festivals, treasured things and ways of passing culture on — and keeping it alive is how a people's past stays part of its present.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A museum is where a people's important things — artefacts of artistic, historical and scientific value — are kept, studied and shown; children name PNG's museums and build a classroom museum of their own.
**Answer: True**
