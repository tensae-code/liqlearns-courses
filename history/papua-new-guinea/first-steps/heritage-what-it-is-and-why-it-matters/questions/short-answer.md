# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Heritage is what a people hands down — its legends, festivals, treasured things and ways of passing culture on — and keeping it alive is how a people's past stays part of its present.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Museums”?
**Answer:** A museum is where a people's important things — artefacts of artistic, historical and scientific value — are kept, studied and shown; children name PNG's museums and build a classroom museum of their own.
