# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional Warriors / Time of magicians and sorcerers / Worship of spirits / Comparing myths and legends
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Each clan remembers its great warriors, magicians and spirits in legends; children tell one, then compare the legends side by side — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 4 — Culture transmission
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 6 — Transmission of culture in the Pacific Region
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Across the Pacific, culture survives the same way PNG's does — language, marriage practice, ritual and belief taught by parents and close kin, generation to generation — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
