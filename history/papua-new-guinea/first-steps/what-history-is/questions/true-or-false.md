# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “What is history?”.
**Answer: False** — History is the study of past events — what already happened, learned from the pictures, stories and traces the past leaves behind.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The past is not gone — knowledge, skills, stories and legends handed down generation to generation still shape how the class lives today.
**Answer: True**
