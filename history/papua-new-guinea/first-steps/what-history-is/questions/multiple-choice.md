# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “What is history?”?
a) Children look at pictures of Independence and life before it, name what already happened, and land on history as the study of past events.  b) Parents, brothers and sisters go onto a drawn family tree and family events onto a simple timeline — the child's first act of dating the past.  c) Grand Chief Michael Somare, Sir Maori Kiki, Vincent Eri and Sir John Guise — what each man did in the events of self-government and Independence, and what it built for the nation.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Children look at pictures of Independence and life before it, name what already happened, and land on history as the study of past events.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Why do we learn about the past?”?
a) The past is not gone — knowledge, skills, stories and legends handed down generation to generation still shape how the class lives today.  b) Four kinds of outsiders reach the island of New Guinea one after another — navigators charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners and traders after wealth — children name them, date them and trace their routes on the map.  c) Parents, brothers and sisters go onto a drawn family tree and family events onto a simple timeline — the child's first act of dating the past.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The past is not gone — knowledge, skills, stories and legends handed down generation to generation still shape how the class lives today.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
