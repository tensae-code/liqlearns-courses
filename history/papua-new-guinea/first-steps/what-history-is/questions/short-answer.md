# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** History is the study of past events — what already happened, learned from the pictures, stories and traces the past leaves behind.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Why do we learn about the past?”?
**Answer:** The past is not gone — knowledge, skills, stories and legends handed down generation to generation still shape how the class lives today.
