# Dating the Past — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — People in my family / My family tree
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Parents, brothers and sisters go onto a drawn family tree and family events onto a simple timeline — the child's first act of dating the past — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — What was life like in the past? / Let's use a timeline
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Past life is rebuilt event by event, then the district's significant dates are plotted in order so the class can read its own age — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Island of New Guinea
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: All land once joined, then split; the Australian landmass drifts north and crushes up New Guinea's mountains — shellfish fossils on Mt Wilhelm prove the peaks rose from the sea — and children draw… — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Constructing Timelines
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Timelines record the past as a chain — title, scale, events, arrows into past and future; children construct one step by step (example: PNG's Independence) and learn to read decades and centuries off… — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
