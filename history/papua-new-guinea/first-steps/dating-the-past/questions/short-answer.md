# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The past is put in order with timelines — family trees, dated events and scaled lines that let us read decades, centuries and even the 200-million-year story of the land itself.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “What was life like in the past? / Let's use a timeline”?
**Answer:** Past life is rebuilt event by event, then the district's significant dates are plotted in order so the class can read its own age.
