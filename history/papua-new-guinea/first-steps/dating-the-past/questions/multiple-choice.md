# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “People in my family / My family tree”?
a) Parents, brothers and sisters go onto a drawn family tree and family events onto a simple timeline — the child's first act of dating the past.  b) Grand Chief Michael Somare, Sir Maori Kiki, Vincent Eri and Sir John Guise — what each man did in the events of self-government and Independence, and what it built for the nation.  c) Four kinds of outsiders reach the island of New Guinea one after another — navigators charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners and traders after wealth — children name them, date them and trace their routes on the map.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Parents, brothers and sisters go onto a drawn family tree and family events onto a simple timeline — the child's first act of dating the past.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
