# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “People in my family / My family tree”.
**Answer: False** — The past is put in order with timelines — family trees, dated events and scaled lines that let us read decades, centuries and even the 200-million-year story of the land itself.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Past life is rebuilt event by event, then the district's significant dates are plotted in order so the class can read its own age.
**Answer: True**
