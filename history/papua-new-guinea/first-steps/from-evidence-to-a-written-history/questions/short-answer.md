# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** We know the past from what survives it — stories spoken person to person, and written records, objects and living witnesses — and every piece of evidence must be tested for whether it can be trusted.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Traditional stories”?
**Answer:** Elders' stories, chants, paintings and tattoos carry a person or a place's history before any book does, and the class learns and retells one.
