# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “How do we know what happened in the past?”.
**Answer: False** — We know the past from what survives it — stories spoken person to person, and written records, objects and living witnesses — and every piece of evidence must be tested for whether it can be trusted.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Elders' stories, chants, paintings and tattoos carry a person or a place's history before any book does, and the class learns and retells one.
**Answer: True**
