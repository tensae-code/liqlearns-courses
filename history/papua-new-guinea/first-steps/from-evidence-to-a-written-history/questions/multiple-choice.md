# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “How do we know what happened in the past?”?
a) The past survives two ways — spoken from person to person, and written in books, stones, accounts and objects — and children learn to tell the two apart.  b) Who lived in this place first, how they lived, and how the community came to be — the class collects the settlement story from past records and stories.  c) Where did the first Papua New Guineans come from? Children trace and name their origins on a map and a timeline, using basic research skills.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The past survives two ways — spoken from person to person, and written in books, stones, accounts and objects — and children learn to tell the two apart.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
