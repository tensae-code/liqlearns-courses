# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The past nobody wrote down is recovered by named trades — history, oral history, palaeontology, archaeology, anthropology and linguistics — each working from its own kind of evidence.
