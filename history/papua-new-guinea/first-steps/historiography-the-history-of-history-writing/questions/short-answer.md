# Historiography: The History of History Writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** How history is recorded changes with its tools — from firelight stories, tattoos and itang specialists to film, computers and the internet — and each age keeps, stores and spreads the past in its own way.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Construction, storage, management and dissemination of Pacific History”?
**Answer:** Pacific history is kept in bodies and buildings — tattooing at puberty, Chuuk's itang specialists trained in history, oratory and war strategy, East Sepik artefacts stored in the Haus Tambaran, and storytelling, dance and initiation passing it parent to child.
