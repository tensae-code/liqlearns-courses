# Historiography: The History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “How do we record history for the future?”.
**Answer: False** — How history is recorded changes with its tools — from firelight stories, tattoos and itang specialists to film, computers and the internet — and each age keeps, stores and spreads the past in its own way.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pacific history is kept in bodies and buildings — tattooing at puberty, Chuuk's itang specialists trained in history, oratory and war strategy, East Sepik artefacts stored in the Haus Tambaran, and storytelling, dance and initiation passing it parent to child.
**Answer: True**
