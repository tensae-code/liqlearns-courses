# Historiography: The History of History Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “How do we record history for the future?”?
a) The record itself changes — film, internet, email and storage devices now carry pictures and sound to the whole world at once, where oral and written records once travelled slowly.  b) Explorers bring canned beef and rice; native sugarcane, banana and pandanus give ground — for taste, for saved garden time, for prestige, and because town life leaves no land to grow — and shelter and clothing (the meri blouse beside bark cloth) change the same way.  c) A Scottish stonemason's son, converted in a revival, sails to Rarotonga then New Guinea as "Tamate", builds the southern-coast mission chain, interprets at the 1884 Protectorate ceremony, and is killed with Oliver Tompkins on Goaribari Island in 1901 — the last punitive expedition and a judge's suicide follow.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The record itself changes — film, internet, email and storage devices now carry pictures and sound to the whole world at once, where oral and written records once travelled slowly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
