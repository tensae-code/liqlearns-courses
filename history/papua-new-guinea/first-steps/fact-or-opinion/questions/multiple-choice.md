# Fact or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Validity and reliability of historical events in PNG”?
a) "a photograph cannot lie" — or can it? Every account is seen through a biased lens; students test one contested massacre story for validity and reliability before accepting or rejecting it.  b) 1945–1960 the empires let go, each colony its own way — peaceful and orderly in some, revolution in Indonesia and India, long civil war in French Indo-China — while the new UN and the Cold War pull the newborn states in opposite directions.  c) Japan takes itself apart as the example — kimono statues in parks, the language taught in every school, self-discipline, family bonds and group duty preserved deliberately as national culture.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) "a photograph cannot lie" — or can it? Every account is seen through a biased lens; students test one contested massacre story for validity and reliability before accepting or rejecting it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
