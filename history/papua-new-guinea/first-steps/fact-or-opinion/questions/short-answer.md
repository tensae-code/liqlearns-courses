# Fact or Opinion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A historical account must be tested, not just believed — every account is seen through someone's eyes, so validity, reliability and hidden bias decide what can be accepted as true.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Effects of critical literacy”?
**Answer:** Critical literacy takes texts apart to find the discrimination and prejudiced power relations authors embed unnoticed — reading beyond the words to free the oppressed reader.
