# Fact or Opinion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Validity and reliability of historical events in PNG”.
**Answer: False** — A historical account must be tested, not just believed — every account is seen through someone's eyes, so validity, reliability and hidden bias decide what can be accepted as true.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Critical literacy takes texts apart to find the discrimination and prejudiced power relations authors embed unnoticed — reading beyond the words to free the oppressed reader.
**Answer: True**
