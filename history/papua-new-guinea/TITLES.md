# History — Papua New Guinea course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Papua New Guinea's own — built from Papua New Guinea's Department of Education teacher guides (English). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': first steps at home and in the local community, the nation and deep time, arrivals, colonisation and the road to Independence, the national history in full, the Pacific Region and Asia, and the world wars era read critically. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
