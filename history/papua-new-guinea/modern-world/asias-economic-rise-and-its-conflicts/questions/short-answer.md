# Asia's Economic Rise and Its Conflicts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Since the 1960s Asia has grown richer faster than any region — East Asian extreme poverty fell from 56% to 12%, the four Asian Tigers industrialised, China and India rose — yet subnational conflicts multiplied against the theory that prosperity brings peace.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Asia's development: past and present / Regional and global development influences”?
**Answer:** The four Asian Tigers industrialise between the 1960s and 1990s; China and India's rise shifts world power from West to East and raises the fear of a clash with US dominance.
