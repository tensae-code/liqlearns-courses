# Asia's Economic Rise and Its Conflicts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Economic systems in Asia / Impact of economic decisions on the people and environment / Economic influence on movement of people / Economic influence on conflicts in Asia”.
**Answer: False** — Since the 1960s Asia has grown richer faster than any region — East Asian extreme poverty fell from 56% to 12%, the four Asian Tigers industrialised, China and India rose — yet subnational conflicts multiplied against the theory that prosperity brings peace.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The four Asian Tigers industrialise between the 1960s and 1990s; China and India's rise shifts world power from West to East and raises the fear of a clash with US dominance.
**Answer: True**
