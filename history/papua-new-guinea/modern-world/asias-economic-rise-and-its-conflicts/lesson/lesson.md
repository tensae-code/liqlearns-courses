## Sub lesson 1 — Economic systems in Asia / Impact of economic decisions on the people and environment / Economic influence on movement of people / Economic influence on conflicts in Asia

Since the 1960s Asia grows richer faster than any region — East Asian extreme poverty falls from 56% to 12% — yet subnational conflicts multiply against the theory that prosperity brings peace; China's turn from export industrialisation remakes its economy and its environment, and millions migrate for work. ## Sub lesson 2 — Asia's development: past and present / Regional and global development influences

The four Asian Tigers industrialise between the 1960s and 1990s; China and India's rise shifts world power from West to East and raises the fear of a clash with US dominance. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. ## Sub lesson 3 — Governments and change in Asia and the world / Impact of different governments' actions / Government reactions and contributions to change / Sustainability or change policies and plans of Asian governments (incl. investigating China's Belt and Road)

Asian governments bet on western capitalism and win — agricultural exporters become industrial powers in twenty years; Malaysia and Singapore steer with industrial policy, Deng Xiaoping blends Marx with markets as "capitalism with Chinese characteristics", and a 56%-middle-class Asia plans its next stage.
