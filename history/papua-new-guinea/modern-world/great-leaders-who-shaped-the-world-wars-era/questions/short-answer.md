# Great Leaders Who Shaped the World Wars Era — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Single people bend history — Hitler hunting lebensraum, Stalin, Mussolini, Churchill, Wilson, Roosevelt, Truman, and leaders judged by Weber's charisma and by legacy, down to Gaddafi — each studied in depth for how one person reshapes politics, society and belief.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Folio”?
**Answer:** Ten politically significant lives from WWII to the present, at least one Papua New Guinean, profiled and judged — early life, beliefs, rise, achievements good and bad, and the legacy each left.
