# Great Leaders Who Shaped the World Wars Era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Influence of great leaders (just before WWI – WWII and aftermath)”.
**Answer: False** — Single people bend history — Hitler hunting lebensraum, Stalin, Mussolini, Churchill, Wilson, Roosevelt, Truman, and leaders judged by Weber's charisma and by legacy, down to Gaddafi — each studied in depth for how one person reshapes politics, society and belief.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Ten politically significant lives from WWII to the present, at least one Papua New Guinean, profiled and judged — early life, beliefs, rise, achievements good and bad, and the legacy each left.
**Answer: True**
