# Papua New Guinea: United Nations Trusteeship and the Road to Independence — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After the war the UN trusteeship replaced the mandate — Australia's 'New Deal' abolished indenture and compensated war damage, the 1962 Foot mission pushed native advancement against planter resistance, and Somare turned the development plans into the Eight Point Plan — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
