# Video script — Papua New Guinea: United Nations Trusteeship and the Road to Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The UN trusteeship replaces the mandate; Australia's "New Deal" abolishes indenture and compensates war damage; the 1962 Foot mission pushes native advancement against planter resistance, and Somare turns the development plans into the Eight Point Plan. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
