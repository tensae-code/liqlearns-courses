# Video script — Papua New Guinea: Foreign Governments and Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Colonial rulers set out to transform peoples they called backward — western schooling erodes traditional knowledge even as laws like corpse-burial protect health; English becomes the language of government, and today's governments still react to change with laws such as the 2016 Cybercrime Code. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
