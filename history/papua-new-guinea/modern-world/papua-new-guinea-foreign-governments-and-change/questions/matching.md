# Papua New Guinea: Foreign Governments and Change — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Governments and change in PNG / Impact of foreign government actions in PNG / Governments' reactions and contributions to change and sustainability — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Plans and policies of change and sustainability in PNG / Foreign government intervention and change in PNG — b)

Meanings:
a) Colonial rulers set out to transform peoples they called backward — western schooling erodes traditional knowledge even as laws like corpse-burial protect health; English becomes the language of government, and today's governments still react to change with laws such as the 2016 Cybercrime Code.
b) Intervention never ended — it changed costume: Australian police partnerships, the Manus asylum programme, WHO immunisation and aid from Australia, New Zealand, the EU and UN agencies, judged by historical example.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
