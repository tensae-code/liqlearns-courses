# Papua New Guinea: Foreign Governments and Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Foreign governments and organisations never stopped shaping Papua New Guinea and the Pacific — from colonial transformation projects to police partnerships, the Manus programme and today's aid — and their actions are judged by historical example.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Plans and policies of change and sustainability in PNG / Foreign government intervention and change in PNG”?
**Answer:** Intervention never ended — it changed costume: Australian police partnerships, the Manus asylum programme, WHO immunisation and aid from Australia, New Zealand, the EU and UN agencies, judged by historical example.
