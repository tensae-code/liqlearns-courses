# Papua New Guinea: Foreign Governments and Change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Governments and change in PNG / Impact of foreign government actions in PNG / Governments' reactions and contributions to change and sustainability”.
**Answer: False** — Foreign governments and organisations never stopped shaping Papua New Guinea and the Pacific — from colonial transformation projects to police partnerships, the Manus programme and today's aid — and their actions are judged by historical example.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Intervention never ended — it changed costume: Australian police partnerships, the Manus asylum programme, WHO immunisation and aid from Australia, New Zealand, the EU and UN agencies, judged by historical example.
**Answer: True**
