# Monocultural and Multicultural Societies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Monocultural and multicultural societies”.
**Answer: False** — One culture or many — monoculturalism, with its ethnocentrism and assimilation or a nation sealing itself like Japan and the Koreas, weighed against multicultural society, advantage against disadvantage.

**2.** One culture or many — monoculturalism, with its ethnocentrism and assimilation or a nation sealing itself like Japan and the Koreas, weighed against multicultural society, advantage against disadvantage.
**Answer: True**
