# Video script — Asia's Transitions to Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The closing decades of the 20th century flip Asia's regimes — crowds topple Marcos in 1986, Korea and Taiwan negotiate out of military rule in 1987, the UN intervenes in Cambodia in 1993, Suharto falls in 1998, East Timor is rehabilitated in 2001. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
