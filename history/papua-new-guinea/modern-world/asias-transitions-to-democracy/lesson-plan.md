# Lesson plan — Asia's Transitions to Democracy

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Asia's Transitions to Democracy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The World Wars Era and Critical History (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The closing decades of the 20th century flipped Asia's regimes — crowds toppled Marcos in 1986, Korea and Taiwan negotiated out of military rule in 1987, the UN intervened in Cambodia in 1993, Suharto fell in 1998 and East Timor was rehabilitated in 2001.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The World Wars Era and Critical History (recommended, never forced).
