# Asia's Transitions to Democracy

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Asia's Transitions to Democracy** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The closing decades of the 20th century flip Asia's regimes — crowds topple Marcos in 1986, Korea and Taiwan negotiate out of military rule in 1987, the UN intervenes in Cambodia in 1993, Suharto falls in 1998, East Timor is rehabilitated in 2001. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
