# Lesson plan — The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The World Wars Era and Critical History (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Out of the Second World War came a new order — 51 states founded the United Nations on 24 October 1945 to prevent another world war, and a trading order built at Bretton Woods ran from GATT in 1947 to the 164-member WTO in 1995.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The World Wars Era and Critical History (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **World Trade Organizations (WTO)** — Out of Bretton Woods comes the post-war trading order — GATT in 1947, sibling to the IMF and World Bank, replaced by the WTO in 1995 as the 164-member referee of world trade.
2. **United Nations** — 24 October 1945 — 51 states found the UN to replace the League and prevent another world war; 193 members and six organs later, its goals are peace, cooperation and better lives.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
