# Video script — The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Out of Bretton Woods comes the post-war trading order — GATT in 1947, sibling to the IMF and World Bank, replaced by the WTO in 1995 as the 164-member referee of world trade. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

24 October 1945 — 51 states found the UN to replace the League and prevent another world war; 193 members and six organs later, its goals are peace, cooperation and better lives. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
