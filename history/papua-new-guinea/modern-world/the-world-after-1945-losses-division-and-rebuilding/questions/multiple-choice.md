# The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “World Trade Organizations (WTO)”?
a) Out of Bretton Woods comes the post-war trading order — GATT in 1947, sibling to the IMF and World Bank, replaced by the WTO in 1995 as the 164-member referee of world trade.  b) Three European explosions — reborn learning, ocean discovery, factory industry — arm Europe to go out and take the world; groups map and timeline each one.  c) WWII, the Cold War, Sputnik and the internet named as the century's hinges; students investigate a global event properly — questions and hypotheses, sources located and interrogated, even visiting the ground where it happened.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Out of Bretton Woods comes the post-war trading order — GATT in 1947, sibling to the IMF and World Bank, replaced by the WTO in 1995 as the 164-member referee of world trade.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
