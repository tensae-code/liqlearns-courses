# The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “World Trade Organizations (WTO)”.
**Answer: False** — Out of the Second World War came a new order — 51 states founded the United Nations on 24 October 1945 to prevent another world war, and a trading order built at Bretton Woods ran from GATT in 1947 to the 164-member WTO in 1995.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) 24 October 1945 — 51 states found the UN to replace the League and prevent another world war; 193 members and six organs later, its goals are peace, cooperation and better lives.
**Answer: True**
