# The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Out of the Second World War came a new order — 51 states founded the United Nations on 24 October 1945 to prevent another world war, and a trading order built at Bretton Woods ran from GATT in 1947 to the 164-member WTO in 1995.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “United Nations”?
**Answer:** 24 October 1945 — 51 states found the UN to replace the League and prevent another world war; 193 members and six organs later, its goals are peace, cooperation and better lives.
