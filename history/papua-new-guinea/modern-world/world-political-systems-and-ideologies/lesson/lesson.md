## Sub lesson 1 — World political systems and ideologies

Power, authority and legitimacy defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units and every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ideology and hegemony

Doctrine against domination — political and epistemological ideologies sorted, and hegemony named as one group's authority over a society or one nation's over others, and how it works on the less powerful. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
