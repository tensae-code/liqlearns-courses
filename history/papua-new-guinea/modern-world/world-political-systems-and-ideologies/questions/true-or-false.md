# World Political Systems and Ideologies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “World political systems and ideologies”.
**Answer: False** — Power, authority and legitimacy can be defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units, ideologies and hegemony — every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Doctrine against domination — political and epistemological ideologies sorted, and hegemony named as one group's authority over a society or one nation's over others, and how it works on the less powerful.
**Answer: True**
