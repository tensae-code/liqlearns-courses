# World Political Systems and Ideologies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Power, authority and legitimacy can be defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units, ideologies and hegemony — every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Ideology and hegemony”?
**Answer:** Doctrine against domination — political and epistemological ideologies sorted, and hegemony named as one group's authority over a society or one nation's over others, and how it works on the less powerful.
