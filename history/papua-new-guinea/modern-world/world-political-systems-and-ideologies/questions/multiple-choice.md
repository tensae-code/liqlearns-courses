# World Political Systems and Ideologies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “World political systems and ideologies”?
a) Power, authority and legitimacy defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units and every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced.  b) Six decades of peacekeeping and observer missions, and since the 1990s wars ended by UN mediation — Sierra Leone, Liberia, Burundi, Sudan's north-south conflict and Nepal named as the proof.  c) Work that steals childhood, potential and dignity — from debt bondage and serfdom to the worst forms named and outlawed; students examine the laws against it and how economic needs drive it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Power, authority and legitimacy defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units and every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
