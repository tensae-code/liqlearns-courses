# World Political Systems and Ideologies — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) World political systems and ideologies — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Ideology and hegemony — b)

Meanings:
a) Power, authority and legitimacy defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units and every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced.
b) Doctrine against domination — political and epistemological ideologies sorted, and hegemony named as one group's authority over a society or one nation's over others, and how it works on the less powerful.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
