# Lesson plan — International Economic Relationships and Instrumental Rationality

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** International Economic Relationships and Instrumental Rationality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The World Wars Era and Critical History (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** National economies fuse into one world economy — Australia–PNG and Japan the case links — and the philosophy that greases it is instrumental rationality: 'the end justifies the means', doing whatever it takes, right or wrong, to reach the goal.
