# International Economic Relationships and Instrumental Rationality — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — National economies fuse into one world economy — Australia–PNG and Japan the case links — and the philosophy that greases it is instrumental rationality: 'the end justifies the means', doing whatever it takes, right or wrong, to reach the goal — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
