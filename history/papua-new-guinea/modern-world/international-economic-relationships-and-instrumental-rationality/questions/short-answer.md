# International Economic Relationships and Instrumental Rationality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** National economies fuse into one world economy — Australia–PNG and Japan the case links — and the philosophy that greases it is instrumental rationality: 'the end justifies the means', doing whatever it takes, right or wrong, to reach the goal.

**2.** What happens in “International economic relationships (incl. Australia–PNG and Japan case links) / Instrumental rationality”?
