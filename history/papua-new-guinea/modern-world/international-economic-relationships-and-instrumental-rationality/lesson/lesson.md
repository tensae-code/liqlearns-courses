# International Economic Relationships and Instrumental Rationality

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **International Economic Relationships and Instrumental Rationality** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
