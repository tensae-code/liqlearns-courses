# Papua New Guinea: Self-Government, Independence and Bougainville Autonomy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's road runs in one dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — while Bougainville took its own separate road to autonomous government.

**Answer:** One dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — each event's causes and effects traced forward.
