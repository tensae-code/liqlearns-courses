# Papua New Guinea: Self-Government, Independence and Bougainville Autonomy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Self-Government in Papua New Guinea / Independence in Papua New Guinea / Birth of Autonomous Government in Bougainville”.
**Answer: False** — Papua New Guinea's road runs in one dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — while Bougainville took its own separate road to autonomous government.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) One dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — each event's causes and effects traced forward.
**Answer: True**
