# Papua New Guinea: Self-Government, Independence and Bougainville Autonomy — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Self-Government in Papua New Guinea / Independence in Papua New Guinea / Birth of Autonomous Government in Bougainville — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Causes and effects of historical events in PNG — b)

Meanings:
a) Three different endings to colonial rule are weighed side by side — PNG governs itself (Somare, Tei Abal, Julius Chan, Peter Lus, Josephine Abaijah in the pre-Independence government), then full Independence, and Bougainville's separate road to autonomous government.
b) One dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — each event's causes and effects traced forward.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
