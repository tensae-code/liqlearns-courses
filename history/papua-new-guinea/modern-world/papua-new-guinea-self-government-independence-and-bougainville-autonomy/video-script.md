# Video script — Papua New Guinea: Self-Government, Independence and Bougainville Autonomy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Three different endings to colonial rule are weighed side by side — PNG governs itself (Somare, Tei Abal, Julius Chan, Peter Lus, Josephine Abaijah in the pre-Independence government), then full Independence, and Bougainville's separate road to autonomous government. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

One dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — each event's causes and effects traced forward. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
