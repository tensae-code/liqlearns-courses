# Lesson plan — Papua New Guinea: Self-Government, Independence and Bougainville Autonomy

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Papua New Guinea: Self-Government, Independence and Bougainville Autonomy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Arrivals, Colonisation and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Papua New Guinea's road runs in one dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — while Bougainville took its own separate road to autonomous government.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Arrivals, Colonisation and the Road to Independence (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Self-Government in Papua New Guinea / Independence in Papua New Guinea / Birth of Autonomous Government in Bougainville** — Three different endings to colonial rule are weighed side by side — PNG governs itself (Somare, Tei Abal, Julius Chan, Peter Lus, Josephine Abaijah in the pre-Independence government), then full Independence, and Bougainville's separate road to autonomous government.
2. **Causes and effects of historical events in PNG** — One dated chain — protectorate and annexation 1884, mandate 1921, joint administration 1949, elections 1961, House of Assembly 1964, self-government 1973, the kina and full Independence in 1975 — each event's causes and effects traced forward.
