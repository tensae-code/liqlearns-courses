# Video script — French Nuclear Testing in the Pacific

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

2 July 1966, Mururoa atoll — France, having lost Algeria, moves its bomb tests to its remote Pacific territory; the lagoon boils, coconut palms bend, and a radioactive cloud rises over Fangataufa and Mururoa. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
