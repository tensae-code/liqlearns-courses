# French Nuclear Testing in the Pacific — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “A past event of the Pacific”?
a) 2 July 1966, Mururoa atoll — France, having lost Algeria, moves its bomb tests to its remote Pacific territory; the lagoon boils, coconut palms bend, and a radioactive cloud rises over Fangataufa and Mururoa.  b) You lose your job on Black Thursday and keep a journal; the Depression spreads worldwide, and from the wreckage the dictators rise — their ideas profiled and their propaganda in cartoons and posters dissected.  c) Power, authority and legitimacy defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units and every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) 2 July 1966, Mururoa atoll — France, having lost Algeria, moves its bomb tests to its remote Pacific territory; the lagoon boils, coconut palms bend, and a radioactive cloud rises over Fangataufa and Mururoa.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
