# Papua New Guinea: Early Explorers, Missionaries, Miners and Traders Arrive — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Early Explorers / Missionaries / Miners and Traders”.
**Answer: False** — Outsiders reached New Guinea in waves — explorers charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners, traders, settlers and administrators — and each wave built a piece of colonial power.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Five European groups build colonial power — administrators (the Company, then Governor Hahl), gold miners who open Sudest, Misima and Wau-Bulolo, settlers like Queen Emma of Rabaul (welcomed by some, resisted by the Binandere), and mission churches whose local catechists and pastors carry schools and health centres.
**Answer: True**
