# Papua New Guinea: Early Explorers, Missionaries, Miners and Traders Arrive — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Outsiders reached New Guinea in waves — explorers charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners, traders, settlers and administrators — and each wave built a piece of colonial power.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Roles of Europeans in Papua and New Guinea”?
**Answer:** Five European groups build colonial power — administrators (the Company, then Governor Hahl), gold miners who open Sudest, Misima and Wau-Bulolo, settlers like Queen Emma of Rabaul (welcomed by some, resisted by the Binandere), and mission churches whose local catechists and pastors carry schools and health centres.
