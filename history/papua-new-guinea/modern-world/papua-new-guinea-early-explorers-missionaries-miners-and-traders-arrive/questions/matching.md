# Papua New Guinea: Early Explorers, Missionaries, Miners and Traders Arrive — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Early Explorers / Missionaries / Miners and Traders — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Roles of Europeans in Papua and New Guinea — b)

Meanings:
a) Four kinds of outsiders reach the island of New Guinea one after another — navigators charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners and traders after wealth — children name them, date them and trace their routes on the map.
b) Five European groups build colonial power — administrators (the Company, then Governor Hahl), gold miners who open Sudest, Misima and Wau-Bulolo, settlers like Queen Emma of Rabaul (welcomed by some, resisted by the Binandere), and mission churches whose local catechists and pastors carry schools and health centres.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
