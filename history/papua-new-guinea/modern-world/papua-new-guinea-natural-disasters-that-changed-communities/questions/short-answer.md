# Papua New Guinea: Natural Disasters That Changed Communities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The same sea that carried the first migrants can erase a coastline in minutes — tsunamis, earthquakes, eruptions like Rabaul in 1994, cyclones and El Niño have remade Papua New Guinean communities again and again.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “El Niño in the Pacific”?
**Answer:** Every few years the warm pool slides east — drought where rain belongs in Indonesia, Australia and PNG, floods on South America's dry coast, a weakened Indian monsoon — a climate event with a human history.
