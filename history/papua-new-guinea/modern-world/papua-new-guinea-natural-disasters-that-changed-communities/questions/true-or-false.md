# Papua New Guinea: Natural Disasters That Changed Communities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Tidal waves and tsunamis”.
**Answer: False** — The same sea that carried the first migrants can erase a coastline in minutes — tsunamis, earthquakes, eruptions like Rabaul in 1994, cyclones and El Niño have remade Papua New Guinean communities again and again.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Every few years the warm pool slides east — drought where rain belongs in Indonesia, Australia and PNG, floods on South America's dry coast, a weakened Indian monsoon — a climate event with a human history.
**Answer: True**
