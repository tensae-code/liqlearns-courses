# Papua New Guinea in the Two World Wars — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) World War I / World War II / Impacts of World War II — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Impact of World War II — b)

Meanings:
a) Two world wars started far away arrive in PNG — the colonisers fight each other in WWI, and in WWII whole provinces feel the fighting; children research and document the impacts on communities, individuals and groups.
b) Japan takes Rabaul in 1942 and makes it its South-West Pacific headquarters; ANGAU runs the unoccupied territory with PNG carriers, stretcher-bearers and interpreters; American abundance — and black soldiers with white soldiers' privileges — astonishes villagers, while bombs destroy gardens, homes and lives.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
