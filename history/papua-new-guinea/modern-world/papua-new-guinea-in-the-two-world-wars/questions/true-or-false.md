# Papua New Guinea in the Two World Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “World War I / World War II / Impacts of World War II”.
**Answer: False** — Two world wars started far away arrived in Papua New Guinea — Rabaul fell in 1942 and became a headquarters, ANGAU ran the unoccupied territory with PNG carriers and stretcher-bearers, and the Kokoda, Black Cat and Bulldog tracks became lifelines.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Japan takes Rabaul in 1942 and makes it its South-West Pacific headquarters; ANGAU runs the unoccupied territory with PNG carriers, stretcher-bearers and interpreters; American abundance — and black soldiers with white soldiers' privileges — astonishes villagers, while bombs destroy gardens, homes and lives.
**Answer: True**
