# Papua New Guinea in the Two World Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “World War I / World War II / Impacts of World War II”?
a) Two world wars started far away arrive in PNG — the colonisers fight each other in WWI, and in WWII whole provinces feel the fighting; children research and document the impacts on communities, individuals and groups.  b) Three systems compared — the German Company state that learns locals hold real power and faces Tolai resentment over lost land and sacred places, Britain's law-and-patrol administration, and Australia's single-territory rule.  c) Colonial rulers set out to transform peoples they called backward — western schooling erodes traditional knowledge even as laws like corpse-burial protect health; English becomes the language of government, and today's governments still react to change with laws such as the 2016 Cybercrime Code.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Two world wars started far away arrive in PNG — the colonisers fight each other in WWI, and in WWII whole provinces feel the fighting; children research and document the impacts on communities, individuals and groups.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Japan takes Rabaul in 1942 and makes it its South-West Pacific headquarters; ANGAU runs the unoccupied territory with PNG carriers, stretcher-bearers and interpreters; American abundance — and black soldiers with white soldiers' privileges — astonishes villagers, while bombs destroy gardens, homes and lives.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
