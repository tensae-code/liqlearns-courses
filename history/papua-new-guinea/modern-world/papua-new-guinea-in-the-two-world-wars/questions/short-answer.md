# Papua New Guinea in the Two World Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Two world wars started far away arrived in Papua New Guinea — Rabaul fell in 1942 and became a headquarters, ANGAU ran the unoccupied territory with PNG carriers and stretcher-bearers, and the Kokoda, Black Cat and Bulldog tracks became lifelines.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Impact of World War II”?
**Answer:** Japan takes Rabaul in 1942 and makes it its South-West Pacific headquarters; ANGAU runs the unoccupied territory with PNG carriers, stretcher-bearers and interpreters; American abundance — and black soldiers with white soldiers' privileges — astonishes villagers, while bombs destroy gardens, homes and lives.
