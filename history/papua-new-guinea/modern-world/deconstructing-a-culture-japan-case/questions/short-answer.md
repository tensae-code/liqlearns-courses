# Deconstructing a Culture: The Japan Case — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A culture can be deconstructed to see how it holds together — Japan preserves its language, its kimono, self-discipline, family bonds and group duty deliberately as national culture.

**2.** What happens in “Deconstructing cultures”?
**Answer:** Japan takes itself apart as the example — kimono statues in parks, the language taught in every school, self-discipline, family bonds and group duty preserved deliberately as national culture.
