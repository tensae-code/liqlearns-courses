# Deconstructing a Culture: The Japan Case — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Deconstructing cultures”.
**Answer: False** — A culture can be deconstructed to see how it holds together — Japan preserves its language, its kimono, self-discipline, family bonds and group duty deliberately as national culture.

**2.** A culture can be deconstructed to see how it holds together — Japan preserves its language, its kimono, self-discipline, family bonds and group duty deliberately as national culture.
**Answer: True**
