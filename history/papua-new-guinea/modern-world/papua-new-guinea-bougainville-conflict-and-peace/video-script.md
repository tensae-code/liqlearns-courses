# Video script — Papua New Guinea: The Bougainville Conflict and Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Oceania's biggest conflict — ten years of civil war between the PNG Government and the Bougainville Revolutionary Army, an estimated 15,000–20,000 dead by 1999, and a slow reconciliation still being woven. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

1988–1998, government against the BRA; 15,000–20,000 Bougainvillean lives; the 1998 Peace Agreement trades the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government — students analyse causes, effects and the conflict's mark on community and nation. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

A report on the Crisis's significant events and what they did to every stakeholder and to the national economy — the nation's deepest wound studied as current history. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
