# Papua New Guinea: The Bougainville Conflict and Peace — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Military force and change in PNG — Case Study: The Bougainville Conflict”.
**Answer: False** — The Bougainville conflict — Oceania's biggest conflict, a decade of civil war between the PNG Government and the Bougainville Revolutionary Army with an estimated 15,000–20,000 dead — ended in a peace agreement that traded the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government, and its causes, effects and mark on the nation are studied as history.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) 1988–1998, government against the BRA; 15,000–20,000 Bougainvillean lives; the 1998 Peace Agreement trades the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government — students analyse causes, effects and the conflict's mark on community and nation.
**Answer: True**
