# Papua New Guinea: The Bougainville Conflict and Peace — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Bougainville conflict — Oceania's biggest conflict, a decade of civil war between the PNG Government and the Bougainville Revolutionary Army with an estimated 15,000–20,000 dead — ended in a peace agreement that traded the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government, and its causes, effects and mark on the nation are studied as history.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Impact of historical events on societies in PNG”?
**Answer:** 1988–1998, government against the BRA; 15,000–20,000 Bougainvillean lives; the 1998 Peace Agreement trades the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government — students analyse causes, effects and the conflict's mark on community and nation.
