# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea: The Bougainville Conflict and Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Papua New Guinea: The Bougainville Conflict and Peace — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Bougainville conflict — Oceania's biggest conflict, a decade of civil war between the PNG Government and the Bougainville Revolutionary Army with an estimated 15,000–20,000 dead — ended in a peace agreement that traded the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government, and its causes, effects and mark on the nation are studied as history. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
