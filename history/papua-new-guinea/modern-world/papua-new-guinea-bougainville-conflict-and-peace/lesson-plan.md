# Lesson plan — Papua New Guinea: The Bougainville Conflict and Peace

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Papua New Guinea: The Bougainville Conflict and Peace
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The National History in Full: Colonisation, War, Trusteeship and Change (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Bougainville conflict — Oceania's biggest conflict, a decade of civil war between the PNG Government and the Bougainville Revolutionary Army with an estimated 15,000–20,000 dead — ended in a peace agreement that traded the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government, and its causes, effects and mark on the nation are studied as history.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Military force and change in PNG — Case Study: The Bougainville Conflict** — Oceania's biggest conflict — ten years of civil war between the PNG Government and the Bougainville Revolutionary Army, an estimated 15,000–20,000 dead by 1999, and a slow reconciliation still being woven.
2. **Impact of historical events on societies in PNG** — 1988–1998, government against the BRA; 15,000–20,000 Bougainvillean lives; the 1998 Peace Agreement trades the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government — students analyse causes, effects and the conflict's mark on community and nation.
3. **Papua New Guinea as a Nation** — A report on the Crisis's significant events and what they did to every stakeholder and to the national economy — the nation's deepest wound studied as current history.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
