# Papua New Guinea: Impacts of Early Outsiders on Local People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The early outsiders put Papua New Guinea on the world record but changed local lives for good and ill — and each group's impact must be appraised group by group.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Cultural influence”?
**Answer:** Cultures change by contact and diffusion; western schooling, phones and Christianity spread through PNG until people rarely call on ancestors for help — old skills are lost as new ones are learnt, faster in some places than others.
