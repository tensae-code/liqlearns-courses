# Papua New Guinea: Impacts of Early Outsiders on Local People — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Impacts of Missionaries on Local People / Impacts of Miners and Traders — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Cultural influence — b)
