# Papua New Guinea: Impacts of Early Outsiders on Local People — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Impacts of Missionaries on Local People / Impacts of Miners and Traders”.
**Answer: False** — The early outsiders put Papua New Guinea on the world record but changed local lives for good and ill — and each group's impact must be appraised group by group.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Cultures change by contact and diffusion; western schooling, phones and Christianity spread through PNG until people rarely call on ancestors for help — old skills are lost as new ones are learnt, faster in some places than others.
**Answer: True**
