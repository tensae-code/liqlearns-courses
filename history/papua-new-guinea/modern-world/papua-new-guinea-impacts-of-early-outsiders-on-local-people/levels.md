# Papua New Guinea: Impacts of Early Outsiders on Local People — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Impacts of Missionaries on Local People / Impacts of Miners and Traders
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The arrivals put PNG on the world record but changed local lives for good and ill — the class appraises what explorers, missionaries, miners and traders each did to the people already there — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cultural influence
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Cultures change by contact and diffusion; western schooling, phones and Christianity spread through PNG until people rarely call on ancestors for help — old skills are lost as new ones are learnt,… — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Responses to imperialism
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: First contact is role-played, then judged — how colonial powers imposed and held control, what imperialism did to indigenous societies, and how reactions differed from Africa to the Asia-Pacific — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
