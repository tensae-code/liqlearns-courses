# Lesson plan — Papua New Guinea: Impacts of Early Outsiders on Local People

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Papua New Guinea: Impacts of Early Outsiders on Local People
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Arrivals, Colonisation and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The early outsiders put Papua New Guinea on the world record but changed local lives for good and ill — and each group's impact must be appraised group by group.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Impacts of Missionaries on Local People / Impacts of Miners and Traders** — The arrivals put PNG on the world record but changed local lives for good and ill — the class appraises what explorers, missionaries, miners and traders each did to the people already there.
2. **Cultural influence** — Cultures change by contact and diffusion; western schooling, phones and Christianity spread through PNG until people rarely call on ancestors for help — old skills are lost as new ones are learnt, faster in some places than others.
3. **Responses to imperialism** — First contact is role-played, then judged — how colonial powers imposed and held control, what imperialism did to indigenous societies, and how reactions differed from Africa to the Asia-Pacific.
