# Video script — Decolonisation of Asia and the Commonwealth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

1945–1960 the empires let go, each colony its own way — peaceful and orderly in some, revolution in Indonesia and India, long civil war in French Indo-China — while the new UN and the Cold War pull the newborn states in opposite directions. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

WWII breaks the empires; students map Africa, Asia and the Pacific with independence dates, interrogate USA and UN involvement, and build five case studies — colonisation, the strategy that won freedom, the key people, and progress since. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Why the empires fell after 1945 — the Depression starved the colonies, Japan conquered Europe's Asia and broke its prestige, the exhausted Europeans could no longer afford empire, and nationalism rose like a tide. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
