# Decolonisation of Asia and the Commonwealth — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Foreign influence on governance in Asia and Commonwealth countries”.
**Answer: False** — After 1945 the empires let go — the Depression starved the colonies, Japan conquered Europe's Asia and broke its prestige, the exhausted Europeans could no longer afford empire, and nationalism rose like a tide — each colony winning freedom its own way, peaceful in some, revolution or long civil war in others.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) WWII breaks the empires; students map Africa, Asia and the Pacific with independence dates, interrogate USA and UN involvement, and build five case studies — colonisation, the strategy that won freedom, the key people, and progress since.
**Answer: True**
