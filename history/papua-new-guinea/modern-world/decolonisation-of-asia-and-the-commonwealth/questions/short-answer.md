# Decolonisation of Asia and the Commonwealth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** After 1945 the empires let go — the Depression starved the colonies, Japan conquered Europe's Asia and broke its prestige, the exhausted Europeans could no longer afford empire, and nationalism rose like a tide — each colony winning freedom its own way, peaceful in some, revolution or long civil war in others.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Decolonisation and Independence”?
**Answer:** WWII breaks the empires; students map Africa, Asia and the Pacific with independence dates, interrogate USA and UN involvement, and build five case studies — colonisation, the strategy that won freedom, the key people, and progress since.
