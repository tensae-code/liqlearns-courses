# Pacific Colonisation: Reasons and Impacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Colonisation spread across the Pacific from Britain's settlement of Australia in 1788 — whalers, then competition for spice, sandalwood, pearl shell and bêche-de-mer — and the colonisers brought a whole way of life whose social, economic, political and cultural impacts are still counted.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Colonizers' cultures in the Pacific”?
**Answer:** Colonisers bring a whole way of life — administration and mission stations, schools, new clothes for "naked" natives, ancestral worship branded pagan, and a new language, Tok Pisin, born from the contact itself.
