# Pacific Colonisation: Reasons and Impacts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Colonisation in the Pacific”.
**Answer: False** — Colonisation spread across the Pacific from Britain's settlement of Australia in 1788 — whalers, then competition for spice, sandalwood, pearl shell and bêche-de-mer — and the colonisers brought a whole way of life whose social, economic, political and cultural impacts are still counted.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Colonisers bring a whole way of life — administration and mission stations, schools, new clothes for "naked" natives, ancestral worship branded pagan, and a new language, Tok Pisin, born from the contact itself.
**Answer: True**
