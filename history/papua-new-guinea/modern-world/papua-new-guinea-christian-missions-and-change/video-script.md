# Video script — Papua New Guinea: Christian Missions and Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Missions arriving from the mid-1800s settle region by region until whole provinces belong to the first church that came; commentators say the missions changed marriage, dress, schooling and belief more deeply than the government ever did. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Region-wide, the missions' handwriting is everywhere — a church in every settlement, church schools and health services, and Christian practice woven into marriage, childrearing, dress, art, government and farming. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
