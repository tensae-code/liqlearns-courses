# Papua New Guinea: Christian Missions and Change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Religions and change in PNG”.
**Answer: False** — Missions arriving from the mid-1800s settled region by region until whole provinces belonged to the first church that came — and commentators say the missions changed marriage, dress, schooling and belief more deeply than the government ever did.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Region-wide, the missions' handwriting is everywhere — a church in every settlement, church schools and health services, and Christian practice woven into marriage, childrearing, dress, art, government and farming.
**Answer: True**
