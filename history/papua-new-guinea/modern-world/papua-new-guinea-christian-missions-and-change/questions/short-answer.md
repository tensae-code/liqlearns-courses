# Papua New Guinea: Christian Missions and Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Missions arriving from the mid-1800s settled region by region until whole provinces belonged to the first church that came — and commentators say the missions changed marriage, dress, schooling and belief more deeply than the government ever did.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Religion and change in the Pacific”?
**Answer:** Region-wide, the missions' handwriting is everywhere — a church in every settlement, church schools and health services, and Christian practice woven into marriage, childrearing, dress, art, government and farming.
