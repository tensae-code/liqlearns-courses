# Papua New Guinea: Colonial Governance to Self-Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Colonial Governance / Leaders During Colonial Governance / Impacts of Colonial Governance”.
**Answer: False** — Colonial government actually ran Papua New Guinea through administrators, patrols and local leaders — Germany, Britain and then Australia each in turn — until the long transition from colonial rule toward self-government began.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Billy Hughes claims New Guinea at Paris in 1919 as Australia's fortress wall; the mandate years bring the 1929 Rabaul strike, Edie Creek and Bulolo gold, the Leahy patrols into the Highlands, then war, the 1949 union of the two territories, self-government in 1972 and Independence on 16 September 1975.
**Answer: True**
