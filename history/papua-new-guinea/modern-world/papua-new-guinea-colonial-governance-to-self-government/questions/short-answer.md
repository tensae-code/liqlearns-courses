# Papua New Guinea: Colonial Governance to Self-Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Colonial government actually ran Papua New Guinea through administrators, patrols and local leaders — Germany, Britain and then Australia each in turn — until the long transition from colonial rule toward self-government began.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Australia's administration in Papua and New Guinea”?
**Answer:** Billy Hughes claims New Guinea at Paris in 1919 as Australia's fortress wall; the mandate years bring the 1929 Rabaul strike, Edie Creek and Bulolo gold, the Leahy patrols into the Highlands, then war, the 1949 union of the two territories, self-government in 1972 and Independence on 16 September 1975.
