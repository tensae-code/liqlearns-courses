# Asian Leaders Who Changed Their Nations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Influences of individuals and groups on governance in Asia and the Commonwealth”.
**Answer: False** — Asia was changed by named individuals — Gandhi, the political giant who was really a social activist, breaking an empire with nonviolent disobedience; Aung San Suu Kyi holding her convictions through years of house arrest — beside Mao Zedong, Hirohito, Chiang Kai-shek, Indira Gandhi, the Khans, Emperor Meiji and the parties that remade China.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The long line of Asia's changemakers — Genghis Khan and Kublai Khan, Emperor Meiji, the Tokugawa Shogunate, the Qin, the Kuomintang, and the Chinese Communist Party ruling since Mao and remaking China into the world's second economy.
**Answer: True**
