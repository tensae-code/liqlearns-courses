# Asian Leaders Who Changed Their Nations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Asia was changed by named individuals — Gandhi, the political giant who was really a social activist, breaking an empire with nonviolent disobedience; Aung San Suu Kyi holding her convictions through years of house arrest — beside Mao Zedong, Hirohito, Chiang Kai-shek, Indira Gandhi, the Khans, Emperor Meiji and the parties that remade China.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Individuals and groups as agents of change”?
**Answer:** The long line of Asia's changemakers — Genghis Khan and Kublai Khan, Emperor Meiji, the Tokugawa Shogunate, the Qin, the Kuomintang, and the Chinese Communist Party ruling since Mao and remaking China into the world's second economy.
