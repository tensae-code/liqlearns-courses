## Sub lesson 1 — Influences of individuals and groups on governance in Asia and the Commonwealth

Aung San Suu Kyi holds her convictions through years of house arrest; Gandhi, the "political giant" who was really a social activist, breaks an empire with nonviolent disobedience — beside Mao Zedong, Hirohito, Chiang Kai-shek and Indira Gandhi. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Individuals and groups as agents of change

The long line of Asia's changemakers — Genghis Khan and Kublai Khan, Emperor Meiji, the Tokugawa Shogunate, the Qin, the Kuomintang, and the Chinese Communist Party ruling since Mao and remaking China into the world's second economy. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
