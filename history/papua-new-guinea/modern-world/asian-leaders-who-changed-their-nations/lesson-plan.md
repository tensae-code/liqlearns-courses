# Lesson plan — Asian Leaders Who Changed Their Nations

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Asian Leaders Who Changed Their Nations
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The World Wars Era and Critical History (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Asia was changed by named individuals — Gandhi, the political giant who was really a social activist, breaking an empire with nonviolent disobedience; Aung San Suu Kyi holding her convictions through years of house arrest — beside Mao Zedong, Hirohito, Chiang Kai-shek, Indira Gandhi, the Khans, Emperor Meiji and the parties that remade China.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The World Wars Era and Critical History (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Influences of individuals and groups on governance in Asia and the Commonwealth** — Aung San Suu Kyi holds her convictions through years of house arrest; Gandhi, the "political giant" who was really a social activist, breaks an empire with nonviolent disobedience — beside Mao Zedong, Hirohito, Chiang Kai-shek and Indira Gandhi.
2. **Individuals and groups as agents of change** — The long line of Asia's changemakers — Genghis Khan and Kublai Khan, Emperor Meiji, the Tokugawa Shogunate, the Qin, the Kuomintang, and the Chinese Communist Party ruling since Mao and remaking China into the world's second economy.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
