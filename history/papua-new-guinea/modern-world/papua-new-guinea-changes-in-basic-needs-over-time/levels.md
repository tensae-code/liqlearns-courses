# Papua New Guinea: Changes in Basic Needs over Time — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Basic needs change over time — explorers' canned beef and rice pushed aside garden foods, and shelter and clothing changed the same way, for taste, for saved garden time, for prestige and because town life leaves no land to grow — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
