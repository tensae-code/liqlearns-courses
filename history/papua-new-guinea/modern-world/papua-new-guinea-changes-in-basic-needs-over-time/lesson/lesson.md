# Papua New Guinea: Changes in Basic Needs over Time

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Papua New Guinea: Changes in Basic Needs over Time** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Explorers bring canned beef and rice; native sugarcane, banana and pandanus give ground — for taste, for saved garden time, for prestige, and because town life leaves no land to grow — and shelter and clothing (the meri blouse beside bark cloth) change the same way. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
