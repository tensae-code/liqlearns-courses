# Papua New Guinea: Changes in Basic Needs over Time — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Basic needs change over time — explorers' canned beef and rice pushed aside garden foods, and shelter and clothing changed the same way, for taste, for saved garden time, for prestige and because town life leaves no land to grow.

**2.** What happens in “Changes in Food / Changes in Shelter / Changes in Clothing”?
**Answer:** Explorers bring canned beef and rice; native sugarcane, banana and pandanus give ground — for taste, for saved garden time, for prestige, and because town life leaves no land to grow — and shelter and clothing (the meri blouse beside bark cloth) change the same way.
