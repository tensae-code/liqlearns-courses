# Pacific Leaders and Groups Who Shaped Governments — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Power in the Pacific also sits outside parliaments — Tonga's monarchy and chiefs steering a modern state, Bainimarama returning Fiji to the Pacific Islands Forum, Queen Emma's plantations and Rabuka's coups.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Promoters and sustainers of change in the Pacific Region”?
**Answer:** Queen Emma Forsyth's copra plantations reshape the New Guinea Islands under German rule and the plantation economy outlives her by a century; in Fiji, Sitiveni Rabuka becomes the Pacific's first coup leader.
