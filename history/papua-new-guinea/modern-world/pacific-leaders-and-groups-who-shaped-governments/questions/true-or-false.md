# Pacific Leaders and Groups Who Shaped Governments — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Influence of individuals and groups in the Pacific Region”.
**Answer: False** — Power in the Pacific also sits outside parliaments — Tonga's monarchy and chiefs steering a modern state, Bainimarama returning Fiji to the Pacific Islands Forum, Queen Emma's plantations and Rabuka's coups.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Queen Emma Forsyth's copra plantations reshape the New Guinea Islands under German rule and the plantation economy outlives her by a century; in Fiji, Sitiveni Rabuka becomes the Pacific's first coup leader.
**Answer: True**
