Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Influence of individuals and groups in the Pacific Region”?
a) Power also sits outside parliaments — Bainimarama returning Fiji to the Pacific Islands Forum after ten years, and Tonga's monarchy, the Pacific's last, with chiefs still steering national development inside modern states.  b) Since the 1960s Asia grows richer faster than any region — East Asian extreme poverty falls from 56% to 12% — yet subnational conflicts multiply against the theory that prosperity brings peace; China's turn from export industrialisation remakes its economy and its environment, and millions migrate for work.  c) The European empires — Britain, France, Germany, Russia, Italy, Turkey, Austria-Hungary — dominate the age of imperialism; small island Britain rules the seas on coal, steel and the world's largest colonial empire, told through narratives from the French Revolution to the Moroccan Crisis and the arms race.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
