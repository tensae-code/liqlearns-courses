# Video script — Pacific Leaders and Groups Who Shaped Governments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

This lesson tells one story.

Power also sits outside parliaments — Bainimarama returning Fiji to the Pacific Islands Forum after ten years, and Tonga's monarchy, the Pacific's last, with chiefs still steering national development inside modern states. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Queen Emma Forsyth's copra plantations reshape the New Guinea Islands under German rule and the plantation economy outlives her by a century; in Fiji, Sitiveni Rabuka becomes the Pacific's first coup leader. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns.
