## Sub lesson 1 — Influence of individuals and groups in the Pacific Region

Power also sits outside parliaments — Bainimarama returning Fiji to the Pacific Islands Forum after ten years, and Tonga's monarchy, the Pacific's last, with chiefs still steering national development inside modern states. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Promoters and sustainers of change in the Pacific Region

Queen Emma Forsyth's copra plantations reshape the New Guinea Islands under German rule and the plantation economy outlives her by a century; in Fiji, Sitiveni Rabuka becomes the Pacific's first coup leader. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
