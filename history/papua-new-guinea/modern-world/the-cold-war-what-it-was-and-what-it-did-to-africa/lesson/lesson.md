## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Cold War told from PNG — origins and causes written as a history-journal article, conflict timelines, the summits that led to détente, UN peacekeeping judged, and Taliban, Al Qaeda and Tamil Tigers compared belief by belief. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
