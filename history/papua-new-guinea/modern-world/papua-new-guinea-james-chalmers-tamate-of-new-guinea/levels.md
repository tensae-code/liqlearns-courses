# Papua New Guinea: James Chalmers, "Tamate" of New Guinea — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — James Chalmers — 'Tamate' — built the southern-coast mission chain, interpreted at the 1884 Protectorate ceremony, and was killed with Oliver Tompkins on Goaribari Island in 1901; one missionary's life shows how missions and empire intertwined — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
