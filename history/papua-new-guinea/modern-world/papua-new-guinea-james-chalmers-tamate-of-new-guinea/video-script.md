# Video script — Papua New Guinea: James Chalmers, "Tamate" of New Guinea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

A Scottish stonemason's son, converted in a revival, sails to Rarotonga then New Guinea as "Tamate", builds the southern-coast mission chain, interprets at the 1884 Protectorate ceremony, and is killed with Oliver Tompkins on Goaribari Island in 1901 — the last punitive expedition and a judge's suicide follow. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
