# Papua New Guinea: James Chalmers, "Tamate" of New Guinea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** James Chalmers — 'Tamate' — built the southern-coast mission chain, interpreted at the 1884 Protectorate ceremony, and was killed with Oliver Tompkins on Goaribari Island in 1901; one missionary's life shows how missions and empire intertwined.

**2.** What happens in “Case study – James Chalmers”?
**Answer:** A Scottish stonemason's son, converted in a revival, sails to Rarotonga then New Guinea as "Tamate", builds the southern-coast mission chain, interprets at the 1884 Protectorate ceremony, and is killed with Oliver Tompkins on Goaribari Island in 1901 — the last punitive expedition and a judge's suicide follow.
