Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “PNG's National Goals and Directive Principles”?
a) PNG's founding fathers write their development model into the Constitution — growth refused if it only profits foreigners; a nation built on justice and fairness, with the five National Goals as the yardstick every policy is measured against.  b) Media as modern culture's most significant force — phones, radio, television and social feeds shaping opinions and thoughts about events before historians ever reach them.  c) Every child traces back to a common family or clan ancestor, reads the clan's timeline, and finds their own place in the line.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) PNG's founding fathers write their development model into the Constitution — growth refused if it only profits foreigners; a nation built on justice and fairness, with the five National Goals as the yardstick every policy is measured against.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
