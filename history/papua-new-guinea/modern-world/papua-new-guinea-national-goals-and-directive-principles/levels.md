# Papua New Guinea: National Goals and Directive Principles — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Papua New Guinea's founding fathers wrote their development model into the Constitution — growth refused if it only profits foreigners, a nation built on justice and fairness, with the five National Goals as the yardstick every policy is measured against — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
