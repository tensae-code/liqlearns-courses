# Papua New Guinea: National Goals and Directive Principles

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Papua New Guinea: National Goals and Directive Principles** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

PNG's founding fathers write their development model into the Constitution — growth refused if it only profits foreigners; a nation built on justice and fairness, with the five National Goals as the yardstick every policy is measured against. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
