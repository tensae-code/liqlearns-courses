Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Colonial administration in Papua and New Guinea”?
a) Three systems compared — the German Company state that learns locals hold real power and faces Tolai resentment over lost land and sacred places, Britain's law-and-patrol administration, and Australia's single-territory rule.  b) Colonial rulers set out to transform peoples they called backward — western schooling erodes traditional knowledge even as laws like corpse-burial protect health; English becomes the language of government, and today's governments still react to change with laws such as the 2016 Cybercrime Code.  c) Sea, mountains, rivers, swamps and rain fight the explorers; MacGregor pushes up nearly every navigable river, crosses the island and climbs Mount Victoria to plant colonial influence in the interior.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Three systems compared — the German Company state that learns locals hold real power and faces Tolai resentment over lost land and sacred places, Britain's law-and-patrol administration, and Australia's single-territory rule.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
