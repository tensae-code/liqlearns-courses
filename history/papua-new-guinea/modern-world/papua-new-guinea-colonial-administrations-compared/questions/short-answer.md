# Papua New Guinea: Colonial Administrations Compared — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Three colonial systems can be compared side by side — the German Company state that learned locals hold real power, Britain's law-and-patrol administration, and Australia's single-territory rule.
