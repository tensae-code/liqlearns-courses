# Papua New Guinea: Australia's Colonisation of Papua — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Australia took Papua in 1906, fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour; Hubert Murray then ruled three decades through 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system and hospitals for TB and leprosy — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
