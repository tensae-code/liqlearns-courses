# Papua New Guinea: Australia's Colonisation of Papua — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Australia took Papua in 1906, fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour; Hubert Murray then ruled three decades through 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system and hospitals for TB and leprosy.

**2.** What happens in “Australia's colonisation of Papua (1906–1914)”?
**Answer:** Fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour, Australia takes Papua under the Papuan Act; Hubert Murray rules three decades — 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system, hospitals for TB and leprosy.
