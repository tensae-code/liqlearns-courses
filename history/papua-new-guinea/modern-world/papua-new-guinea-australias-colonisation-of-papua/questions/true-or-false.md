# Papua New Guinea: Australia's Colonisation of Papua — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Australia's colonisation of Papua (1906–1914)”.
**Answer: False** — Australia took Papua in 1906, fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour; Hubert Murray then ruled three decades through 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system and hospitals for TB and leprosy.

**2.** Australia took Papua in 1906, fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour; Hubert Murray then ruled three decades through 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system and hospitals for TB and leprosy.
**Answer: True**
