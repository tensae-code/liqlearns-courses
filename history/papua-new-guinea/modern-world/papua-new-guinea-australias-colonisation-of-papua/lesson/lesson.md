# Papua New Guinea: Australia's Colonisation of Papua

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Papua New Guinea: Australia's Colonisation of Papua** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour, Australia takes Papua under the Papuan Act; Hubert Murray rules three decades — 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system, hospitals for TB and leprosy. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
