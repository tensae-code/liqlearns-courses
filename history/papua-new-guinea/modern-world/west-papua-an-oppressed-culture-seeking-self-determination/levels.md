# West Papua: An Oppressed Culture Seeking Self-Determination — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — One island, two fates — West Papuans, Melanesians with the same ancestors and cultures as Papua New Guineans, have been part of Indonesia since Dutch colonisation and still struggle for the independence their eastern half won — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
