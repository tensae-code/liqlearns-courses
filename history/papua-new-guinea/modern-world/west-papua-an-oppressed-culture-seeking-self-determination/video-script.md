# Video script — West Papua: An Oppressed Culture Seeking Self-Determination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

One island, two fates — West Papuans, Melanesians with the same ancestors and cultures as PNG, have been part of Indonesia since Dutch colonisation and still struggle for the independence their eastern half won. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
