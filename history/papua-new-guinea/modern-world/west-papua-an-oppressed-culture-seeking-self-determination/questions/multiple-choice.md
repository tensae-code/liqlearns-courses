**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Oppressive and non-oppressive cultures – Asia”?
a) One island, two fates — West Papuans, Melanesians with the same ancestors and cultures as PNG, have been part of Indonesia since Dutch colonisation and still struggle for the independence their eastern half won.  b) Wealth gaps light wars — from colonial powers seizing assets and trade routes to today's Afghanistan, Syria, the Kurds, the US–China trade war and seven nations disputing the South China Sea.  c) Arrows on a map show goods flowing between peoples long before Europeans — students name the trade items and explain what the trade meant to everyone it touched.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
