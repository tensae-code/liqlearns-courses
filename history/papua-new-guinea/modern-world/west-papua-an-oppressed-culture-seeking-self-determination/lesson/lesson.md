# West Papua: An Oppressed Culture Seeking Self-Determination

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **West Papua: An Oppressed Culture Seeking Self-Determination** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

One island, two fates — West Papuans, Melanesians with the same ancestors and cultures as PNG, have been part of Indonesia since Dutch colonisation and still struggle for the independence their eastern half won. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
