# Pacific Governments Change over Time — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Change of governments over time in the Pacific Region / Election processes in the Pacific Region”.
**Answer: False** — Pacific governments mutate over time — traditional systems, colonial rule, then reorganised participation and independence — and elections every five years now make and unmake Pacific governments, with coups in Fiji and crises in Vanuatu and Solomon Islands as the exceptions that test the rule.

**2.** Pacific governments mutate over time — traditional systems, colonial rule, then reorganised participation and independence — and elections every five years now make and unmake Pacific governments, with coups in Fiji and crises in Vanuatu and Solomon Islands as the exceptions that test the rule.
