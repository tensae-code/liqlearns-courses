# Pacific Governments Change over Time — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Pacific governments mutate over time — traditional systems, colonial rule, then reorganised participation and independence — and elections every five years now make and unmake Pacific governments, with coups in Fiji and crises in Vanuatu and Solomon Islands as the exceptions that test the rule — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
