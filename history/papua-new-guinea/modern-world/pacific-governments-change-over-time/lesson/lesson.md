# Pacific Governments Change over Time

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Pacific Governments Change over Time** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Governments mutate — traditional systems, colonial rule, then reorganised participation: Samoa's Legislative Council majority in 1947, French territories' autonomy in 1956, Western Samoa first to independence in 1962; elections every five years now make and unmake Pacific governments, with coups in Fiji and crises in Vanuatu and Solomon Islands as the exceptions that test the rule. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
