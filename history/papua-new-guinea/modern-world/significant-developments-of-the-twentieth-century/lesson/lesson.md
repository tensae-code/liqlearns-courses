# Significant Developments of the Twentieth Century

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Significant Developments of the Twentieth Century** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

WWII, the Cold War, Sputnik and the internet named as the century's hinges; students investigate a global event properly — questions and hypotheses, sources located and interrogated, even visiting the ground where it happened. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
