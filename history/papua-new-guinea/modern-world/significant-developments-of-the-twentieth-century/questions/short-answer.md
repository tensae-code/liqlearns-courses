# Significant Developments of the Twentieth Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The twentieth century turns on named hinges — the Second World War, the Cold War, Sputnik and the internet — and a global event is investigated properly: questions and hypotheses, sources located and interrogated, even visiting the ground where it happened.

**2.** What happens in “Significant developments in history”?
**Answer:** WWII, the Cold War, Sputnik and the internet named as the century's hinges; students investigate a global event properly — questions and hypotheses, sources located and interrogated, even visiting the ground where it happened.
