# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The First World War crumbled the European empires and both democracies and dictatorships climbed from the ruins — Hitler, Austrian-born, took Germany in 1933, ruled until his 1945 suicide, and triggered the second war — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
