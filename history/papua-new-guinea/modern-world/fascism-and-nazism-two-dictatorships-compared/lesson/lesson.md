# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

WWI crumbles the European empires and both democracies and dictatorships climb from the ruins — Hitler, Austrian-born, takes Germany in 1933 and rules until his 1945 suicide, triggering the second war. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
