# Papua New Guinea: Nation Builders — Independence Leaders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Important persons or people – nation”?
a) Grand Chief Michael Somare, Sir Maori Kiki, Vincent Eri and Sir John Guise — what each man did in the events of self-government and Independence, and what it built for the nation.  b) Four kinds of outsiders reach the island of New Guinea one after another — navigators charting coasts, missionaries carrying the word of God, then miners and traders after wealth — children name them, date them and trace their routes on the map.  c) Outside powers take the island and draw governing boundaries between them; children name and map the colonial powers, plot colonisation's events on a timeline, and judge what colonisation did to PNG and its people.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Grand Chief Michael Somare, Sir Maori Kiki, Vincent Eri and Sir John Guise — what each man did in the events of self-government and Independence, and what it built for the nation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
