# Papua New Guinea: Nation Builders — Independence Leaders — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Important persons or people – nation”.
**Answer: False** — Nations are built by named people — Somare, Maori Kiki, Vincent Eri, John Guise, Tei Abal, Josephine Abaijah, Susan Karike and more — whose deeds carried Papua New Guinea to self-government and Independence.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sir Michael Somare, Dame Josephine Abaijah, and Sir Tei Abal — the Enga medical assistant with no formal schooling who reached Parliament in 1964, led the Opposition at Independence in 1975, and said moka talks were "much the same as meetings of Parliament" — each leader's contribution to independence is profiled and their home province mapped.
**Answer: True**
