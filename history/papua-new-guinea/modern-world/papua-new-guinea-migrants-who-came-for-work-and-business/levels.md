# Papua New Guinea: Migrants Who Came for Work and Business — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Migrants and firms came to Papua New Guinea chasing work and profit — Chinese traders, Filipino workers, Brian Bell's business empire, logging multinationals — adding skills and workforce while draining brains and pressuring services at home and in PNG — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
