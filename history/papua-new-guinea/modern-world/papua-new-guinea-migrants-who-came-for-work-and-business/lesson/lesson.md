# Papua New Guinea: Migrants Who Came for Work and Business

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Papua New Guinea: Migrants Who Came for Work and Business** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Migrants and firms come chasing work and profit — Chinese traders, Filipino workers, Brian Bell's business empire, logging multinationals — adding skills and workforce while draining brains and pressuring services at home and in PNG. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
