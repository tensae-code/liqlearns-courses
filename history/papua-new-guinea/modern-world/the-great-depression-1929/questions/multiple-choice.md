# The Great Depression, 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Depression and the dictators”?
a) You lose your job on Black Thursday and keep a journal; the Depression spreads worldwide, and from the wreckage the dictators rise — their ideas profiled and their propaganda in cartoons and posters dissected.  b) Power, authority and legitimacy defined and sorted — monarchs, feudal units and every system by which a society's collective decisions get made, enforced and reproduced.  c) Six decades of peacekeeping and observer missions, and since the 1990s wars ended by UN mediation — Sierra Leone, Liberia, Burundi, Sudan's north-south conflict and Nepal named as the proof.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) You lose your job on Black Thursday and keep a journal; the Depression spreads worldwide, and from the wreckage the dictators rise — their ideas profiled and their propaganda in cartoons and posters dissected.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
