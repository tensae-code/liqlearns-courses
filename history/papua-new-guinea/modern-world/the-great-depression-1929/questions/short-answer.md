# The Great Depression, 1929 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** You lose your job on Black Thursday and keep a journal — the Depression spread worldwide, and from the wreckage the dictators rose, their ideas profiled and their propaganda dissected.

**2.** What happens in “Depression and the dictators”?
**Answer:** You lose your job on Black Thursday and keep a journal; the Depression spreads worldwide, and from the wreckage the dictators rise — their ideas profiled and their propaganda in cartoons and posters dissected.
