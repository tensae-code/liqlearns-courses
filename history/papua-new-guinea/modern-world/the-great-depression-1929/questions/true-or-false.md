# The Great Depression, 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Depression and the dictators”.
**Answer: False** — You lose your job on Black Thursday and keep a journal — the Depression spread worldwide, and from the wreckage the dictators rose, their ideas profiled and their propaganda dissected.

**2.** You lose your job on Black Thursday and keep a journal — the Depression spread worldwide, and from the wreckage the dictators rose, their ideas profiled and their propaganda dissected.
**Answer: True**
