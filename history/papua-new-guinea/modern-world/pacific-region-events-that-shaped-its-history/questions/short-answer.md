# Pacific Region: Events That Shaped Its History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Pacific's history is shaped by named events — King George Tupou I's Tonga, Fiji's coups, the Bougainville Crisis, independence arriving unevenly across the region — sorted and interpreted as political, economic, social, religious and cultural history.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Events that occurred in the Pacific Region”?
**Answer:** Independence comes unevenly — different colonial masters, most of Melanesia free in the 1970s under indigenous elites, then the crises: Bougainville's resource rebellion, Fiji's denial of Indo-Fijian rights, and island economies dependent on foreign companies extracting timber, minerals, oil and fish.
