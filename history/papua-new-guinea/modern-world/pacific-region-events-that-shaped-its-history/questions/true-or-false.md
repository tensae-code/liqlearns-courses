# Pacific Region: Events That Shaped Its History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Major events that have shaped the history of the Pacific Region”.
**Answer: False** — The Pacific's history is shaped by named events — King George Tupou I's Tonga, Fiji's coups, the Bougainville Crisis, independence arriving unevenly across the region — sorted and interpreted as political, economic, social, religious and cultural history.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Independence comes unevenly — different colonial masters, most of Melanesia free in the 1970s under indigenous elites, then the crises: Bougainville's resource rebellion, Fiji's denial of Indo-Fijian rights, and island economies dependent on foreign companies extracting timber, minerals, oil and fish.
**Answer: True**
