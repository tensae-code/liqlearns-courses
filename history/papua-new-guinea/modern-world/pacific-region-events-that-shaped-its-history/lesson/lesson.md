## Sub lesson 1 — Major events that have shaped the history of the Pacific Region

King George Tupou I's Tonga expansion wars and his stand as "Maker of Modern Tonga", Rabuka's two Fiji coups, the Bougainville Crisis draining PNG's budget, and Port Moresby's APEC summit — political, economic, social, religious and cultural events sorted and interpreted. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Events that occurred in the Pacific Region

Independence comes unevenly — different colonial masters, most of Melanesia free in the 1970s under indigenous elites, then the crises: Bougainville's resource rebellion, Fiji's denial of Indo-Fijian rights, and island economies dependent on foreign companies extracting timber, minerals, oil and fish. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
