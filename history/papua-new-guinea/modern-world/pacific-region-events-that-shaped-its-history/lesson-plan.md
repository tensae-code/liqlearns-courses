# Lesson plan — Pacific Region: Events That Shaped Its History

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Pacific Region: Events That Shaped Its History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Pacific Region and Asia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Pacific's history is shaped by named events — King George Tupou I's Tonga, Fiji's coups, the Bougainville Crisis, independence arriving unevenly across the region — sorted and interpreted as political, economic, social, religious and cultural history.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Pacific Region and Asia (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Major events that have shaped the history of the Pacific Region** — King George Tupou I's Tonga expansion wars and his stand as "Maker of Modern Tonga", Rabuka's two Fiji coups, the Bougainville Crisis draining PNG's budget, and Port Moresby's APEC summit — political, economic, social, religious and cultural events sorted and interpreted.
2. **Events that occurred in the Pacific Region** — Independence comes unevenly — different colonial masters, most of Melanesia free in the 1970s under indigenous elites, then the crises: Bougainville's resource rebellion, Fiji's denial of Indo-Fijian rights, and island economies dependent on foreign companies extracting timber, minerals, oil and fish.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
