# Global Alliances: Commonwealth and Treaty Groups — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — States bind themselves in alliances — the Commonwealth, the British Commonwealth become a free association of sovereign states keeping ties of friendship and cooperation under a symbolic monarch, is the worked example — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
