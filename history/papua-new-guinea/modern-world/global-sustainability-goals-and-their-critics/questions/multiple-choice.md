Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Global sustainable goals”?
a) 2015 — the UN General Assembly sets 17 goals for 2030, from no poverty to clean water; Grade 12 students critique whether the intended outcomes can actually happen.  b) National economies fuse into one world economy — and the philosophy that greases it: "the end justifies the means", doing whatever it takes, right or wrong, to reach the goal.  c) Each clan remembers its great warriors, magicians and spirits in legends; children tell one, then compare the legends side by side.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
