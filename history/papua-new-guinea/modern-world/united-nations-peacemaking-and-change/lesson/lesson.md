# United Nations Peacemaking and Change

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **United Nations Peacemaking and Change** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Six decades of peacekeeping and observer missions, and since the 1990s wars ended by UN mediation — Sierra Leone, Liberia, Burundi, Sudan's north-south conflict and Nepal named as the proof. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
