# United Nations Peacemaking and Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The United Nations keeps peace as well as sets goals — six decades of peacekeeping and observer missions, and since the 1990s wars ended by UN mediation, with Sierra Leone, Liberia, Burundi, Sudan and Nepal as the proof.

**2.** What happens in “Promoting change and maintaining sustainability”?
**Answer:** Six decades of peacekeeping and observer missions, and since the 1990s wars ended by UN mediation — Sierra Leone, Liberia, Burundi, Sudan's north-south conflict and Nepal named as the proof.
