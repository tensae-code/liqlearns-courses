# Papua New Guinea: The Sandline Affair and Policy Change — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In the Sandline affair of the late 1990s, Prime Minister Sir Julius Chan hired a British mercenary company to end the Bougainville conflict — the decision backfired and Chan was forced to step down — a case of how government policy choices remake governments — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
