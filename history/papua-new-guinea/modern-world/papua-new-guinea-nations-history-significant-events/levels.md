# Papua New Guinea: The Nation's History — Significant Events — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A nation's history can be told as its turning points — discovery, the coming of missionaries, Independence and great shocks beside them — listed, explained and drawn on one national timeline — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
