# Papua New Guinea: The Nation's History — Significant Events — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A nation's history can be told as its turning points — discovery, the coming of missionaries, Independence and great shocks beside them — listed, explained and drawn on one national timeline.

**2.** What happens in “Nation's history”?
**Answer:** The nation's turning points — discovery, the coming of missionaries, Independence and international events, with natural disasters and economic shocks beside them — are listed, explained and drawn on one national timeline.
