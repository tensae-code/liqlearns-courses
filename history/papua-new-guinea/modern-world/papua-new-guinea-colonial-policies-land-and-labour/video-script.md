# Video script — Papua New Guinea: Colonial Policies — Land and Labour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Germany rules through luluais and head tax — every unmarried man owes plantation years; MacGregor answers differently, blocking cheap land sales and Queensland-style labour abuse; Australia keeps the 99-year lease and the indenture contract. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
