# Papua New Guinea: Colonial Policies — Land and Labour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Colonial rule bit hardest through land and labour — Germany ruling through luluais and a head tax that owed plantation years, MacGregor blocking cheap land sales and Queensland-style labour abuse, and Australia keeping the 99-year lease and the indenture contract.

**2.** What happens in “Policies of colonial governments in Papua and New Guinea”?
**Answer:** Germany rules through luluais and head tax — every unmarried man owes plantation years; MacGregor answers differently, blocking cheap land sales and Queensland-style labour abuse; Australia keeps the 99-year lease and the indenture contract.
