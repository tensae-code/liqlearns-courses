# Papua New Guinea: Colonial Policies — Land and Labour — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Colonial rule bit hardest through land and labour — Germany ruling through luluais and a head tax that owed plantation years, MacGregor blocking cheap land sales and Queensland-style labour abuse, and Australia keeping the 99-year lease and the indenture contract — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
