# Global Migration and Labour Mobility

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Global Migration and Labour Mobility** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Economic influence on global migration / Labour mobility

Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Intercultural migration

Migrants remake the developed world — east-to-west flows inside Europe, Middle East conflict and African poverty pushing outward, the US, Argentina and Brazil built on immigrants, and Germany asking how long before a newcomer belongs. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
