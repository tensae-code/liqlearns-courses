# Global Migration and Labour Mobility — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Economic influence on global migration / Labour mobility”.
**Answer: False** — Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries are both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Migrants remake the developed world — east-to-west flows inside Europe, Middle East conflict and African poverty pushing outward, the US, Argentina and Brazil built on immigrants, and Germany asking how long before a newcomer belongs.
**Answer: True**
