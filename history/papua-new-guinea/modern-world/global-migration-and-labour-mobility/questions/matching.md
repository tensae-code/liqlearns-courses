# Global Migration and Labour Mobility — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Economic influence on global migration / Labour mobility — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Intercultural migration — b)

Meanings:
a) Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit.
b) Migrants remake the developed world — east-to-west flows inside Europe, Middle East conflict and African poverty pushing outward, the US, Argentina and Brazil built on immigrants, and Germany asking how long before a newcomer belongs.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
