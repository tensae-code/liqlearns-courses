# Global Migration and Labour Mobility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries are both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Intercultural migration”?
**Answer:** Migrants remake the developed world — east-to-west flows inside Europe, Middle East conflict and African poverty pushing outward, the US, Argentina and Brazil built on immigrants, and Germany asking how long before a newcomer belongs.
