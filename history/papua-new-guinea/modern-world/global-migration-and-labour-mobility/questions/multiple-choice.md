# Global Migration and Labour Mobility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Economic influence on global migration / Labour mobility”?
a) Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit.  b) Before the story, the tools — history, oral history, primary and secondary source, palaeontology, archaeology, anthropology and linguistics defined as the trades that recover a past nobody wrote down.  c) Japan reaches for an empire of its own in Asia — students investigate the reasons and build the wanted-poster case for and against the expansion.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
