# Video script — Papua New Guinea: Problem Solving in the Past — Moka and Compensation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

In Mt Hagen, wrongs and status were settled by Moka — reciprocal gifts of pigs, always bigger than what was received, building a Big Man's prestige; children compare that system with other traditional compensation across PNG and judge what it did well and where it fell short. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
