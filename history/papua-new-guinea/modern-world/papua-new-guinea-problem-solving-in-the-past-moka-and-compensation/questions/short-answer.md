# Papua New Guinea: Problem Solving in the Past — Moka and Compensation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** In the Highlands, wrongs and status were settled by Moka — reciprocal gifts of pigs, always bigger than what was received, building a Big Man's prestige — and by traditional compensation, systems judged here for what they did well and where they fell short.

**2.** What happens in “Case study – Moka (Western Highlands)”?
**Answer:** In Mt Hagen, wrongs and status were settled by Moka — reciprocal gifts of pigs, always bigger than what was received, building a Big Man's prestige; children compare that system with other traditional compensation across PNG and judge what it did well and where it fell short.
