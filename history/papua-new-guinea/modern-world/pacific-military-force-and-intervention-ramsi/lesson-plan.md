# Lesson plan — Pacific Military Force and Intervention: RAMSI

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Pacific Military Force and Intervention: RAMSI
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Pacific Region and Asia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** When Solomon Islands collapsed into ethnic conflict, the Pacific answered together — the Townsville peace after the 2000 seizure of the Prime Minister, then RAMSI landing in 2003 with 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Pacific Region and Asia (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Military force and change in the Pacific Region / Intervention of external forces in the Pacific Region** — Guadalcanal and Malaita tensions explode in 1998; the Malaita Eagle Force seizes the Prime Minister in 2000, parliament elects a new one aboard an Australian warship, the Townsville peace follows — and in 2003 RAMSI lands 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias.
2. **Foreign intervention** — Intervention against isolationism — sanctions, embargoes, blockades and armies; the Iraq wars' cost to the US is the worked example of what intervening does to the intervener.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
