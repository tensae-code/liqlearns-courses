# Pacific Military Force and Intervention: RAMSI — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Military force and change in the Pacific Region / Intervention of external forces in the Pacific Region”.
**Answer: False** — When Solomon Islands collapsed into ethnic conflict, the Pacific answered together — the Townsville peace after the 2000 seizure of the Prime Minister, then RAMSI landing in 2003 with 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Intervention against isolationism — sanctions, embargoes, blockades and armies; the Iraq wars' cost to the US is the worked example of what intervening does to the intervener.
**Answer: True**
