# Pacific Military Force and Intervention: RAMSI — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** When Solomon Islands collapsed into ethnic conflict, the Pacific answered together — the Townsville peace after the 2000 seizure of the Prime Minister, then RAMSI landing in 2003 with 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Foreign intervention”?
**Answer:** Intervention against isolationism — sanctions, embargoes, blockades and armies; the Iraq wars' cost to the US is the worked example of what intervening does to the intervener.
