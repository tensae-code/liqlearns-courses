# Pacific Military Force and Intervention: RAMSI — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Military force and change in the Pacific Region / Intervention of external forces in the Pacific Region — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Foreign intervention — b)

Meanings:
a) Guadalcanal and Malaita tensions explode in 1998; the Malaita Eagle Force seizes the Prime Minister in 2000, parliament elects a new one aboard an Australian warship, the Townsville peace follows — and in 2003 RAMSI lands 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias.
b) Intervention against isolationism — sanctions, embargoes, blockades and armies; the Iraq wars' cost to the US is the worked example of what intervening does to the intervener.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
