# Video script — Pacific Military Force and Intervention: RAMSI

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Guadalcanal and Malaita tensions explode in 1998; the Malaita Eagle Force seizes the Prime Minister in 2000, parliament elects a new one aboard an Australian warship, the Townsville peace follows — and in 2003 RAMSI lands 2,200 soldiers and police from eight Pacific nations to disarm the militias. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Intervention against isolationism — sanctions, embargoes, blockades and armies; the Iraq wars' cost to the US is the worked example of what intervening does to the intervener. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Papua New Guinea.
