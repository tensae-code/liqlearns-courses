# The First World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Powerful nations' influence on world politics (before WWI)”.
**Answer: False** — Before the First World War the European empires — Britain, France, Germany, Russia, Italy, Turkey, Austria-Hungary — dominated the age of imperialism, and rivalry, the arms race and crises like Morocco built the pressure that broke into war.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The student becomes an American journalist in Europe in 1914, explaining to readers back home why the war broke out and who was responsible — arguing with the historians, not just repeating them.
**Answer: True**
