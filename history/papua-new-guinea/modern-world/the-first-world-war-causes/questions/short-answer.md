# The First World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Before the First World War the European empires — Britain, France, Germany, Russia, Italy, Turkey, Austria-Hungary — dominated the age of imperialism, and rivalry, the arms race and crises like Morocco built the pressure that broke into war.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “World War I and its Aftermath”?
**Answer:** The student becomes an American journalist in Europe in 1914, explaining to readers back home why the war broke out and who was responsible — arguing with the historians, not just repeating them.
