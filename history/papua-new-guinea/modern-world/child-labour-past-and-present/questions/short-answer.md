# Child Labour: Past and Present — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Child labour is work that steals childhood, potential and dignity — from debt bondage and serfdom to the worst forms named and outlawed — and economic needs are what drive it.

**2.** What happens in “Child labour”?
**Answer:** Work that steals childhood, potential and dignity — from debt bondage and serfdom to the worst forms named and outlawed; students examine the laws against it and how economic needs drive it.
