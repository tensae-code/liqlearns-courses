# Child Labour: Past and Present — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Child labour”?
a) Work that steals childhood, potential and dignity — from debt bondage and serfdom to the worst forms named and outlawed; students examine the laws against it and how economic needs drive it.  b) Children look at pictures of Independence and life before it, name what already happened, and land on history as the study of past events.  c) Parents, brothers and sisters go onto a drawn family tree and family events onto a simple timeline — the child's first act of dating the past.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Work that steals childhood, potential and dignity — from debt bondage and serfdom to the worst forms named and outlawed; students examine the laws against it and how economic needs drive it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
