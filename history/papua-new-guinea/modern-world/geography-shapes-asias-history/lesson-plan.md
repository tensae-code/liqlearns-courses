# Lesson plan — Geography Shapes Asia's History

**Course:** History — Papua New Guinea · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Geography Shapes Asia's History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Pacific Region and Asia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Asia's Himalaya, plateaus, steppes and seas set the stage of its history — mountains raise cultures in heavy clothing at altitude while river valleys fill with people, and geography's influence on development and relationships is tested, not assumed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Pacific Region and Asia (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Geographical systems of Asia and Pacific / Influence of geographical systems on development and relationships in Asia** — Himalaya, plateaus, steppes and seas set the stage — geographic determinism, first argued from the Roman Empire's fate, is tested against Asia's civilisations and China's case.
2. **Geographic systems of Asia and the Pacific / Role of geographical systems on development and relationships in Asia / Impact of industries in Asia** — Mountains raise cultures in heavy clothing at altitude while river valleys fill with people; Southeast Asia's volcanic soils feed millions as logging and colonisation projects clear the forests and turn shifting agriculture into farms.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
