# Geography Shapes Asia's History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Geographical systems of Asia and Pacific / Influence of geographical systems on development and relationships in Asia”.
**Answer: False** — Asia's Himalaya, plateaus, steppes and seas set the stage of its history — mountains raise cultures in heavy clothing at altitude while river valleys fill with people, and geography's influence on development and relationships is tested, not assumed.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Mountains raise cultures in heavy clothing at altitude while river valleys fill with people; Southeast Asia's volcanic soils feed millions as logging and colonisation projects clear the forests and turn shifting agriculture into farms.
**Answer: True**
