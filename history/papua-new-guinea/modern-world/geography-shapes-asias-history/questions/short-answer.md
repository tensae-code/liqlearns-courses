# Geography Shapes Asia's History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Asia's Himalaya, plateaus, steppes and seas set the stage of its history — mountains raise cultures in heavy clothing at altitude while river valleys fill with people, and geography's influence on development and relationships is tested, not assumed.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Geographic systems of Asia and the Pacific / Role of geographical systems on development and relationships in Asia / Impact of industries in Asia”?
**Answer:** Mountains raise cultures in heavy clothing at altitude while river valleys fill with people; Southeast Asia's volcanic soils feed millions as logging and colonisation projects clear the forests and turn shifting agriculture into farms.
