# Papua New Guinea: Colonisation — Powers and Impacts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “What is Colonization? / Impacts of Colonization / Timeline of Colonization”?
a) Outside powers take the island and draw governing boundaries between them; children name and map the colonial powers, plot colonisation's events on a timeline, and judge what colonisation did to PNG and its people.  b) Fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour, Australia takes Papua under the Papuan Act; Hubert Murray rules three decades — 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system, hospitals for TB and leprosy.  c) Before any state, power sits with Big Men in patrilineal societies and chiefs in the matrilineal island societies where women hold wealth — and colonisation's "each for himself" teaching corrodes the old duty of care toward minorities.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Outside powers take the island and draw governing boundaries between them; children name and map the colonial powers, plot colonisation's events on a timeline, and judge what colonisation did to PNG and its people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
