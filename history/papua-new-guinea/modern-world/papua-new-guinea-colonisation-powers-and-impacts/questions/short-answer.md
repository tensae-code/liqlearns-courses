# Papua New Guinea: Colonisation — Powers and Impacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Outside powers took the island of New Guinea and drew governing boundaries between themselves — Germany seizing the north-east and Britain proclaiming its protectorate over the south in 1884 — driven by economic, political and strategic motives, with deep impacts on Papua New Guinea and its people.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “German and British colonisation of Papua and New Guinea”?
**Answer:** Portuguese de Menezes names Papua ("frizzy hair") in 1526 and de Retes names New Guinea in 1545, but the real taking comes in 1884 — Germany seizes the north-east as Kaiser-Wilhelmsland through the German New Guinea Company while Britain proclaims its protectorate over the south, MacGregor governing "by patrol".
