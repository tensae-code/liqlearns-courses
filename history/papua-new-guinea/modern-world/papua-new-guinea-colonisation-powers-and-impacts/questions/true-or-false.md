# Papua New Guinea: Colonisation — Powers and Impacts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “What is Colonization? / Impacts of Colonization / Timeline of Colonization”.
**Answer: False** — Outside powers took the island of New Guinea and drew governing boundaries between themselves — Germany seizing the north-east and Britain proclaiming its protectorate over the south in 1884 — driven by economic, political and strategic motives, with deep impacts on Papua New Guinea and its people.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Portuguese de Menezes names Papua ("frizzy hair") in 1526 and de Retes names New Guinea in 1545, but the real taking comes in 1884 — Germany seizes the north-east as Kaiser-Wilhelmsland through the German New Guinea Company while Britain proclaims its protectorate over the south, MacGregor governing "by patrol".
**Answer: True**
