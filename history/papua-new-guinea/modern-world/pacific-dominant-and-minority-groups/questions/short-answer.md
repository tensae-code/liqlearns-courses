# Pacific Dominant and Minority Groups — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands, and US bases stalled Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority.

**2.** What happens in “Dominant and minority groups in the Pacific”?
**Answer:** In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands; US bases in the Marshalls, Guam and Hawaii stall Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority.
