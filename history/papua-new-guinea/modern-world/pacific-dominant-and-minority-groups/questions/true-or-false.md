# Pacific Dominant and Minority Groups — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Dominant and minority groups in the Pacific”.
**Answer: False** — In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands, and US bases stalled Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority.

**2.** In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands, and US bases stalled Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority.
**Answer: True**
