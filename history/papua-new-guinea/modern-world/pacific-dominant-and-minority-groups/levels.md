# Pacific Dominant and Minority Groups — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands, and US bases stalled Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
