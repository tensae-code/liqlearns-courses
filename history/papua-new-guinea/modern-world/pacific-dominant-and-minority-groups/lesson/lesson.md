# Pacific Dominant and Minority Groups

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Pacific Dominant and Minority Groups** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands; US bases in the Marshalls, Guam and Hawaii stall Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
