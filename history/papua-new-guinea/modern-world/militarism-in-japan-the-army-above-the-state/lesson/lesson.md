# Militarism in Japan: The Army above the State

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Militarism in Japan: The Army above the State** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Japan reaches for an empire of its own in Asia — students investigate the reasons and build the wanted-poster case for and against the expansion. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
