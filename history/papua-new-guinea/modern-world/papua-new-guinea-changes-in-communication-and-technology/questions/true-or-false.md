# Papua New Guinea: Changes in Communication and Technology — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Traditional Communication / Modern Communication / Effects of Modern Communication / Traditional Technology / Modern Technology / Impacts of Modern Technology”.
**Answer: False** — Messages once travelled by garamut slit-gong, conch shell and kundu drum, and work was done with dugout canoes, salt-making and stone tools — modern communication and technology changed both, with gains and losses to weigh.

**2.** Messages once travelled by garamut slit-gong, conch shell and kundu drum, and work was done with dugout canoes, salt-making and stone tools — modern communication and technology changed both, with gains and losses to weigh.
