# Papua New Guinea: Changes in Communication and Technology — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Messages once travelled by garamut slit-gong, conch shell and kundu drum, and work was done with dugout canoes, salt-making and stone tools — modern communication and technology changed both, with gains and losses to weigh.

**2.** What happens in “Traditional Communication / Modern Communication / Effects of Modern Communication / Traditional Technology / Modern Technology / Impacts of Modern Technology”?
**Answer:** Messages once travelled by garamut slit-gong, conch shell and kundu drum, and work by dugout canoe, salt-making and stone tools; modern communication and technology arrive and the class weighs what was gained and what was lost.
