# Papua New Guinea: Changes in Communication and Technology

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Papua New Guinea: Changes in Communication and Technology** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Messages once travelled by garamut slit-gong, conch shell and kundu drum, and work by dugout canoe, salt-making and stone tools; modern communication and technology arrive and the class weighs what was gained and what was lost. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
