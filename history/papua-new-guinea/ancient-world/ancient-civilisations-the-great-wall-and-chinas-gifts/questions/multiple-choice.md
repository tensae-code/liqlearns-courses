# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Dominant cultures and groups in Asia”?
a) China the rising dominant culture — among the earliest civilisations, a co-inventor of writing beside Egypt and Greece, its Great Wall maintained as a reminder, now a one-party superpower of over a billion people.  b) Migration touches every state in a globalising world — people cross borders for work and safety, origin and destination countries both transformed, and working overseas becomes a career strategy weighed cost against benefit.  c) Before the story, the tools — history, oral history, primary and secondary source, palaeontology, archaeology, anthropology and linguistics defined as the trades that recover a past nobody wrote down.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) China the rising dominant culture — among the earliest civilisations, a co-inventor of writing beside Egypt and Greece, its Great Wall maintained as a reminder, now a one-party superpower of over a billion people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
