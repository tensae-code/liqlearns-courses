# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Dominant cultures and groups in Asia”.
**Answer: False** — China is among the earliest civilisations — a co-inventor of writing beside Egypt and Greece, keeper of the Great Wall — and now stands as the rising dominant culture of Asia.

**2.** China is among the earliest civilisations — a co-inventor of writing beside Egypt and Greece, keeper of the Great Wall — and now stands as the rising dominant culture of Asia.
**Answer: True**
