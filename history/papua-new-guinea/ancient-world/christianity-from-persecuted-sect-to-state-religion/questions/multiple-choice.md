Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Impact of Christianity on local cultures”?
a) The Church outlives Rome — preserving literacy after the Western Empire falls, replacing the Empire as Europe's unifying force, raising the cathedrals, fixing Easter, Christmas and the Gregorian calendar by which the world still counts from Jesus's birth.  b) 2015 — the UN General Assembly sets 17 goals for 2030, from no poverty to clean water; Grade 12 students critique whether the intended outcomes can actually happen.  c) National economies fuse into one world economy — and the philosophy that greases it: "the end justifies the means", doing whatever it takes, right or wrong, to reach the goal.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Church outlives Rome — preserving literacy after the Western Empire falls, replacing the Empire as Europe's unifying force, raising the cathedrals, fixing Easter, Christmas and the Gregorian calendar by which the world still counts from Jesus's birth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
