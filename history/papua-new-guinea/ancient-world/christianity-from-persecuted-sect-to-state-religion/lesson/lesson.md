# Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Church outlives Rome — preserving literacy after the Western Empire falls, replacing the Empire as Europe's unifying force, raising the cathedrals, fixing Easter, Christmas and the Gregorian calendar by which the world still counts from Jesus's birth. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
