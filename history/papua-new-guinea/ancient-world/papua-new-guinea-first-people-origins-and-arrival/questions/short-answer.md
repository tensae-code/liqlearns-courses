# Papua New Guinea: The First People's Origins and Arrival — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's first people came from across the sea in deep prehistory, and tracing where they originated, how they crossed and why they settled decides identity and customary land rights to this day.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Prehistoric migration”?
**Answer:** Where did our ancestors originate, how did they cross the sea, what did the sea throw at them, and why did they settle here — in PNG the answers decide identity and customary land rights today.
