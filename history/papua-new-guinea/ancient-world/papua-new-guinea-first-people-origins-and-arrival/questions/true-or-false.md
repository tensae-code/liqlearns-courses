# Papua New Guinea: The First People's Origins and Arrival — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Origins of the First People of Papua New Guinea”.
**Answer: False** — Papua New Guinea's first people came from across the sea in deep prehistory, and tracing where they originated, how they crossed and why they settled decides identity and customary land rights to this day.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where did our ancestors originate, how did they cross the sea, what did the sea throw at them, and why did they settle here — in PNG the answers decide identity and customary land rights today.
**Answer: True**
