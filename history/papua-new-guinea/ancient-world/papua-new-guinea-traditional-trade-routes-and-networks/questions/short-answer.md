# Papua New Guinea: Traditional Trade Routes and Networks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Long before Europeans, goods flowed between Papua New Guinea's peoples along traditional trade routes — and the trade bound together everyone it touched.

**2.** What happens in “Traditional trade routes activity”?
**Answer:** Arrows on a map show goods flowing between peoples long before Europeans — students name the trade items and explain what the trade meant to everyone it touched.
