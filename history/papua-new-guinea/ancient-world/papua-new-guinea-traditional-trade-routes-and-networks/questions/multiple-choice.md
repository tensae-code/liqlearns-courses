Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Traditional trade routes activity”?
a) Arrows on a map show goods flowing between peoples long before Europeans — students name the trade items and explain what the trade meant to everyone it touched.  b) The Cold War told from PNG — origins and causes written as a history-journal article, conflict timelines, the summits that led to détente, UN peacekeeping judged, and Taliban, Al Qaeda and Tamil Tigers compared belief by belief.  c) The Church outlives Rome — preserving literacy after the Western Empire falls, replacing the Empire as Europe's unifying force, raising the cathedrals, fixing Easter, Christmas and the Gregorian calendar by which the world still counts from Jesus's birth.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
