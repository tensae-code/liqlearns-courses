# Papua New Guinea: Early Settlers of the Local Community — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Early settlers
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Who lived in this place first, how they lived, and how the community came to be — the class collects the settlement story from past records and stories — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Life of early settlers in PNG / Why was life like that?
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Houses, dress, transport, work and leisure of PNG's early settlers are rebuilt from photographs and books, then explained — why clothes, shelters and work took the shape they did — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lifestyle of the First People / Food / Shelter / Clothing
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The first people survive by what they grow, build and wear, each fitted to its environment — houses against sickness, enemies and wild animals; children draw the foods, shelters and clothes and… — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
