# Papua New Guinea: Early Settlers of the Local Community

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Papua New Guinea: Early Settlers of the Local Community** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Early settlers

Who lived in this place first, how they lived, and how the community came to be — the class collects the settlement story from past records and stories. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Life of early settlers in PNG / Why was life like that?

Houses, dress, transport, work and leisure of PNG's early settlers are rebuilt from photographs and books, then explained — why clothes, shelters and work took the shape they did. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lifestyle of the First People / Food / Shelter / Clothing

The first people survive by what they grow, build and wear, each fitted to its environment — houses against sickness, enemies and wild animals; children draw the foods, shelters and clothes and compare them with today's. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The first people of each place lived fitted to their environment — their houses, food, dress and work took the shape the land required — and their settlement story is collected from records and from stories. Give one real example: who lived in this place first, how they lived, and how the community came to be — the class collects the settlement story from past records and stories. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
