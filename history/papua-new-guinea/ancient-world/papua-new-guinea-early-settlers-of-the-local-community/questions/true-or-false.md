# Papua New Guinea: Early Settlers of the Local Community — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Early settlers”.
**Answer: False** — The first people of each place lived fitted to their environment — their houses, food, dress and work took the shape the land required — and their settlement story is collected from records and from stories.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Houses, dress, transport, work and leisure of PNG's early settlers are rebuilt from photographs and books, then explained — why clothes, shelters and work took the shape they did.
**Answer: True**
