# Papua New Guinea: Early Settlers of the Local Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The first people of each place lived fitted to their environment — their houses, food, dress and work took the shape the land required — and their settlement story is collected from records and from stories.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Life of early settlers in PNG / Why was life like that?”?
**Answer:** Houses, dress, transport, work and leisure of PNG's early settlers are rebuilt from photographs and books, then explained — why clothes, shelters and work took the shape they did.
