# Papua New Guinea: Big Man and Chieftaincy Systems — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Before any state, power in Papua New Guinea sat with Big Men in patrilineal societies and with chiefs in the matrilineal island societies where women hold wealth — and colonisation's 'each for himself' teaching corroded the old duty of care toward minorities — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
