# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

## Sub lesson 1 — Religion as agent of change

Asia is the birthplace of the world's faiths — Buddhism, Christianity, Confucianism, Hinduism, Islam, Jainism, Judaism, Shinto, Sikhism, Taoism and Zoroastrianism — with Islam the largest at some 1.1 billion followers, and Singapore and Vietnam among the most religiously diverse countries on earth. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Spread of world religions

Local gods give way to universal faiths that can be practised anywhere — Hindu caste ordering India, Confucian ethics ordering China, Muslim traders doing business with fellow Muslims in distant lands, and believer-communities grown larger than any village. Stay with the people in it and the place they stand in, and watch for the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn shapes everyone who comes after — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Impact of religions on people

Each faith carries a whole culture — Buddhism's Buddha, who never claimed to be a god, pointing at enlightenment and escape from rebirth's wheel, set beside the other great traditions and their consequences for the peoples they shaped. Follow who is in it, where they stand, and the moment the story turns. After that turn, life is different for the people who follow — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
