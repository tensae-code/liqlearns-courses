## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Religion as agent of change** — Asia is the birthplace of the world's faiths — Buddhism, Christianity, Confucianism, Hinduism, Islam, Jainism, Judaism, Shinto, Sikhism, Taoism and Zoroastrianism — with Islam the largest at some 1.1 billion followers, and Singapore and Vietnam among the most religiously diverse countries on earth.
2. **Spread of world religions** — Local gods give way to universal faiths that can be practised anywhere — Hindu caste ordering India, Confucian ethics ordering China, Muslim traders doing business with fellow Muslims in distant lands, and believer-communities grown larger than any village.
3. **Impact of religions on people** — Each faith carries a whole culture — Buddhism's Buddha, who never claimed to be a god, pointing at enlightenment and escape from rebirth's wheel, set beside the other great traditions and their consequences for the peoples they shaped.
