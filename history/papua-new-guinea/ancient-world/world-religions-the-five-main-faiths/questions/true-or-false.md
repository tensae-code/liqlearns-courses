# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Religion as agent of change”.
**Answer: False** — Asia is the birthplace of the world's faiths, and each faith carries a whole culture that shapes the peoples who live by it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Local gods give way to universal faiths that can be practised anywhere — Hindu caste ordering India, Confucian ethics ordering China, Muslim traders doing business with fellow Muslims in distant lands, and believer-communities grown larger than any village.
**Answer: True**
