# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Asia is the birthplace of the world's faiths, and each faith carries a whole culture that shapes the peoples who live by it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Spread of world religions”?
**Answer:** Local gods give way to universal faiths that can be practised anywhere — Hindu caste ordering India, Confucian ethics ordering China, Muslim traders doing business with fellow Muslims in distant lands, and believer-communities grown larger than any village.
