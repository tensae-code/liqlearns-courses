**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 6: ancient landmarks — the pyramids of Egypt”?
a) Pupils match the world's old civilisations to their surviving landmarks, and Egypt's pyramids stand for the Nile kingdom's state power.  b) Sulayman, grandfather of Osman, migrates from Turkmenistan to Anatolia; his son Ertugrul wins Seljuk land by fighting the Romans, and Osman inherits and founds the state on Rome's frontier.  c) Resistance is as old as the occupation itself but surges after the Second World War on self-determination principles, new media and the example of Syria, Lebanon and Jordan winning independence; the UN General Assembly's Resolution 1514 declares colonialism a crime — with Palestine named the exception still under occupation.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
