# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pupils match the world's old civilisations to their surviving landmarks, and Egypt's pyramids stand for the Nile kingdom's state power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 5 Lesson 3: the invention of writing — Egyptian picture writing” is mainly about this: The Zionist movement plants its first settlement, Montefiore, south-west of Jerusalem's wall in 1856, and the settlements double as training camps and arms stores; after the 1948 Nakba the occupation holds over 78% of Palestine.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Writing begins as pictures in Pharaonic Egypt, one of humanity's oldest ways of sending thought across distance.
