# Mamluk Egypt — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mamluks defend the Islamic state — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Battle of Ain Jalut — b)


Meanings:
a) Hulagu's Mongols take Damascus, Aleppo and Hama and threaten Egypt; Sultan Qutuz, with Baybars scouting ahead, meets them at Ain Jalut north-west of Beisan in Palestine and breaks the Mongol advance.
b) Slave-soldiers bought young by the Ayyubid sultans rise to rule Egypt and al-Sham, restore the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo after Baghdad's fall, and Sultan al-Ashraf Khalil ibn Qalawun expels the last Franks from Acre in 690 AH / 1291 CE, freeing Palestine for good.

**Answer key:** 1. b; 2. a
