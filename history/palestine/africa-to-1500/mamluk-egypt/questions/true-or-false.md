# Mamluk Egypt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Slave-soldiers bought young by the Ayyubid sultans rise to rule Egypt and al-Sham, restore the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo after Baghdad's fall, and Sultan al-Ashraf Khalil ibn Qalawun expels the last Franks from Acre in 690 AH / 1291 CE, freeing Palestine for good.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Battle of Ain Jalut” is mainly about this: Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Hulagu's Mongols take Damascus, Aleppo and Hama and threaten Egypt; Sultan Qutuz, with Baybars scouting ahead, meets them at Ain Jalut north-west of Beisan in Palestine and breaks the Mongol advance.
