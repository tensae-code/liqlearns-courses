# Mamluk Egypt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mamluks defend the Islamic state”?
**Answer:** Slave-soldiers bought young by the Ayyubid sultans rise to rule Egypt and al-Sham, restore the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo after Baghdad's fall, and Sultan al-Ashraf Khalil ibn Qalawun expels the last Franks from Acre in 690 AH / 1291 CE, freeing Palestine for good. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Battle of Ain Jalut” end?
**Answer:** Hulagu's Mongols take Damascus, Aleppo and Hama and threaten Egypt; Sultan Qutuz, with Baybars scouting ahead, meets them at Ain Jalut north-west of Beisan in Palestine and breaks the Mongol advance. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
