# Lesson plan — Mamluk Egypt

**Course:** History — Palestine · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Mamluk Egypt
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Storms and Empires: Decline to the Ottoman Centuries (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Slave-soldiers bought young by the Ayyubid sultans rise to rule Egypt and al-Sham, restore the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo after Baghdad's fall, and Sultan al-Ashraf Khalil ibn Qalawun expels the last Franks from Acre in 690 AH / 1291 CE, freeing Palestine for good.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Storms and Empires: Decline to the Ottoman Centuries (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mamluks defend the Islamic state** — Slave-soldiers bought young by the Ayyubid sultans rise to rule Egypt and al-Sham, restore the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo after Baghdad's fall, and Sultan al-Ashraf Khalil ibn Qalawun expels the last Franks from Acre in 690 AH / 1291 CE, freeing Palestine for good.
2. **Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Battle of Ain Jalut** — Hulagu's Mongols take Damascus, Aleppo and Hama and threaten Egypt; Sultan Qutuz, with Baybars scouting ahead, meets them at Ain Jalut north-west of Beisan in Palestine and breaks the Mongol advance.
3. **Unit 2 Lesson 1: the powers ruling the Arab homeland before the Ottomans** — Three powers divide the map — Mamluks in Egypt, al-Sham and the Hijaz, Safavids in Iraq, Portuguese seizing Muscat, Bahrain and Hormuz — with local families in North Africa, and the lesson retells who the Mamluks were through the Ain Jalut campaign map.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
