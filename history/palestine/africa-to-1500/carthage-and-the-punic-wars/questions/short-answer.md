# Carthage and the Punic Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 5: Carthage”?
**Answer:** Phoenicians found Carthage in Tunis and it rules the coasts of Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and southern Spain, until Rome besieges it four years, burns it for seventeen days and takes its people captive in the second century BCE (told through a quoted passage from Mustafa Murad al-Dabbagh's ancient history of the Near East). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 5: Carthage” end?
**Answer:** Phoenicians found Carthage in Tunis and it rules the coasts of Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and southern Spain, until Rome besieges it four years, burns it for seventeen days and takes its people captive in the second century BCE (told through a quoted passage from Mustafa Murad al-Dabbagh's ancient history of the Near East). Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
