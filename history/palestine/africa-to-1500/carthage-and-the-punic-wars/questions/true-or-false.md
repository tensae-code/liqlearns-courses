# Carthage and the Punic Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Phoenicians found Carthage in Tunis and it rules the coasts of Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and southern Spain, until Rome besieges it four years, burns it for seventeen days and takes its people captive in the second century BCE (told through a quoted passage from Mustafa Murad al-Dabbagh's ancient history of the Near East).
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 2 Lesson 5: Carthage” is mainly about this: The first humans wander unsettled, hunting and gathering fruit, then farming settles them, barter gives way to coined money, and the Canaanites are already using coinage in Palestine five thousand years ago.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Phoenicians found Carthage in Tunis and it rules the coasts of Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and southern Spain, until Rome besieges it four years, burns it for seventeen days and takes its people captive in the second century BCE (told through a quoted passage from Mustafa Murad al-Dabbagh's ancient history of the Near East).
