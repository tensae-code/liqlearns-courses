# Carthage and the Punic Wars

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Carthage and the Punic Wars** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Phoenicians found Carthage in Tunis and it rules the coasts of Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and southern Spain, until Rome besieges it four years, burns it for seventeen days and takes its people captive in the second century BCE (told through a quoted passage from Mustafa Murad al-Dabbagh's ancient history of the Near East). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
