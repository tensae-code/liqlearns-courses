# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first humans wander unsettled, hunting and gathering fruit, then farming settles them, barter gives way to coined money, and the Canaanites are already using coinage in Palestine five thousand years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 2 Lesson 2: the tools ancient humans used” is mainly about this: Resistance is as old as the occupation itself but surges after the Second World War on self-determination principles, new media and the example of Syria, Lebanon and Jordan winning independence; the UN General Assembly's Resolution 1514 declares colonialism a crime — with Palestine named the exception still under occupation.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Need mothers invention as flint is knapped sharp for spears, axes, sickles and grain-pounders, so the age is named the Stone Age.
