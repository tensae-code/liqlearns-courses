# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 1: aspects of ancient human life”?
a) The first humans wander unsettled, hunting and gathering fruit, then farming settles them, barter gives way to coined money, and the Canaanites are already using coinage in Palestine five thousand years ago.  b) Genghis Khan unites the nomad tribes of the Mongolian plateau into an empire reaching the Danube, and the invasions come for expansion and plunder of the Islamic lands.  c) A divine-right monarchy under Louis XVI, privileged clergy and nobles, a salt tax on the common people, and a financial crisis deepened by funding the American revolution bring France to 1789.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The first humans wander unsettled, hunting and gathering fruit, then farming settles them, barter gives way to coined money, and the Canaanites are already using coinage in Palestine five thousand years ago.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
