# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 1: aspects of ancient human life”?
**Answer:** The first humans wander unsettled, hunting and gathering fruit, then farming settles them, barter gives way to coined money, and the Canaanites are already using coinage in Palestine five thousand years ago. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 2: the tools ancient humans used” end?
**Answer:** Need mothers invention as flint is knapped sharp for spears, axes, sickles and grain-pounders, so the age is named the Stone Age. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
