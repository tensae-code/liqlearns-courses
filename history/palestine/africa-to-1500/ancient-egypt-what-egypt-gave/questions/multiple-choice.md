Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 5: Egypt's gifts — coinage, sciences and mummification”?
a) Egyptians invent coinage that spreads worldwide, make paper from papyrus, and embalm their dead because they believe in life after death.  b) After the Seven Years' War Britain taxes the colonies that had fought beside it; revolt breaks out in 1775, George Washington leads the volunteers with French help, and independence is declared at Philadelphia in 1776.  c) After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Egyptians invent coinage that spreads worldwide, make paper from papyrus, and embalm their dead because they believe in life after death.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
