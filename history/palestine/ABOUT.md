# About the History — Palestine course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Palestine's own Ministry of Education books. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Palestine inside the History folder (one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **six levels**, following the story as Palestine's own books present it (see CURRICULUM.md for the derivation): Level 1 — first steps and the first humans (5 lessons); Level 2 — the ancient world around Palestine (11 lessons); Level 3 — Islam rises, expands — and Palestine opens to it (4 lessons); Level 4 — storms and empires: decline to the Ottoman centuries (6 lessons); Level 5 — the modern world arrives: Renaissance to colonialism (11 lessons); Level 6 — Palestine's own modern story: villages, Nakba and the Authority (6 lessons).

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *The arc whole:* "First, what history is and how we know it — then the first humans themselves: fire, stone tools, farming, villages and cities, with Palestine's own Natufian caves and Jericho at the centre. Then the ancient world around this land — Mesopotamia, Egypt, the Canaanites who built its cities and invented the alphabet, Yemen, Rome and Carthage. Then Islam: the call, the state, the conquests — and Jerusalem opening to Umar. Then the storms: decline, Franks and Mongols, Mamluks and Ottomans, judged from Palestine itself. Then the modern world arriving — Renaissance, discoveries, factories, revolutions — and colonialism closing in. And last, Palestine's own story whole: the villages and 1948, the Mandate and the revolts, the PLO, the wars, the intifadas, Oslo and the Authority."
