# Yemen's Kingdoms: Saba, Himyar and the Marib Dam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ma'in (1200–650 BCE, capital Qarnaw) and Saba (950–115 BCE) rise in Yemen on rain, the Zamzam-like wells and incense trade; Saba's farmers dam the floods at Marib, terrace the mountains and leave Bilqis's throne as their emblem.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 2 Lesson 1: the kingdoms of South Arabia” is mainly about this: After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Ma'in (1200–650 BCE, capital Qarnaw) and Saba (950–115 BCE) rise in Yemen on rain, the Zamzam-like wells and incense trade; Saba's farmers dam the floods at Marib, terrace the mountains and leave Bilqis's throne as their emblem.
