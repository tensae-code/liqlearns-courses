# Yemen's Kingdoms: Saba, Himyar and the Marib Dam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 1: the kingdoms of South Arabia”?
**Answer:** Ma'in (1200–650 BCE, capital Qarnaw) and Saba (950–115 BCE) rise in Yemen on rain, the Zamzam-like wells and incense trade; Saba's farmers dam the floods at Marib, terrace the mountains and leave Bilqis's throne as their emblem. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
