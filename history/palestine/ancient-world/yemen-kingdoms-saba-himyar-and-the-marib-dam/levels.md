# Yemen's Kingdoms: Saba, Himyar and the Marib Dam — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Ma'in (1200–650 BCE, capital Qarnaw) and Saba (950–115 BCE) rise in Yemen on rain, the Zamzam-like wells and incense trade; Saba's farmers dam the floods at Marib, terrace the mountains and leave Bilqis's throne as their emblem. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
