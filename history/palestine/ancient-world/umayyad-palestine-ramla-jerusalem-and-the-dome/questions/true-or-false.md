# Umayyad Palestine: Ramla, Jerusalem and the Dome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Muawiya restores Jerusalem's walls, the Umayyads rebuild Ascalon, expand Caesarea and Acre, and raise the Dome of the Rock; Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik founds Ramla, the only city Muslims built from nothing in Palestine, and Jerusalem's wall gets its last rebuilding under the Ottoman sultan.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 2 Lesson 3: architectural achievements in Palestine” is mainly about this: The Great Wall is named among the ancient world's civilisation landmarks pupils must know, beside Petra and the Roman theatre at Sebastia in Palestine.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Muawiya restores Jerusalem's walls, the Umayyads rebuild Ascalon, expand Caesarea and Acre, and raise the Dome of the Rock; Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik founds Ramla, the only city Muslims built from nothing in Palestine, and Jerusalem's wall gets its last rebuilding under the Ottoman sultan.
