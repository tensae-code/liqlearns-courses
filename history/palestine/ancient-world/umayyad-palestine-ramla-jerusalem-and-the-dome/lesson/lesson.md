# Umayyad Palestine: Ramla, Jerusalem and the Dome

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Umayyad Palestine: Ramla, Jerusalem and the Dome** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Muawiya restores Jerusalem's walls, the Umayyads rebuild Ascalon, expand Caesarea and Acre, and raise the Dome of the Rock; Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik founds Ramla, the only city Muslims built from nothing in Palestine, and Jerusalem's wall gets its last rebuilding under the Ottoman sultan. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
