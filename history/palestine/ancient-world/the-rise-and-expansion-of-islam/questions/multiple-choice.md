**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 5 Lesson 1: Islam appears in Mecca”?
a) Muhammad receives the revelation in Cave Hira at forty; the secret call gathers Khadijah, Ali, Abu Bakr, Zayd and Bilal, the public call brings Quraysh's torture — Sumayya becomes Islam's first martyr — and the believers flee to the just Negus in Abyssinia.  b) The homeland's straits, trade routes, resources and markets draw the powers from Portugal's 1500s Indian Ocean thrust onward, Ottoman weakness opening the way; the outcome is partition into small states behind artificial borders and a Zionist entity planted in Palestine to split Arab Asia from Arab Africa.  c) The past is rebuilt from written sources — scripture, histories like Ibn al-Athir's al-Kamil, official documents, land deeds (kawashin), travel books and personal memoirs.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
