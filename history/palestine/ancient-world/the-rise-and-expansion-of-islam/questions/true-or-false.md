# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Muhammad receives the revelation in Cave Hira at forty; the secret call gathers Khadijah, Ali, Abu Bakr, Zayd and Bilal, the public call brings Quraysh's torture — Sumayya becomes Islam's first martyr — and the believers flee to the just Negus in Abyssinia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 5 Lesson 1: boycott, Taif, Isra and Mi'raj, and the Hijra” is mainly about this: Early humans signal with grunts, gestures, drawings and fire-smoke, and writing arrives with the Sumerian cuneiform the lesson names, before pigeons, telegraph and telephone carry messages further.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Quraysh besiege Banu Hashim in Shi'b Abi Talib for three years, Taif stones the Prophet, the night journey carries him to al-Aqsa in Jerusalem, the Aqaba pledges open Yathrib, and the Hijra succeeds by planning — Ali in the bed, Abu Bakr beside him, Cave Thawr, a twisting road.
