# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 5 Lesson 1: Islam appears in Mecca”?
**Answer:** Muhammad receives the revelation in Cave Hira at forty; the secret call gathers Khadijah, Ali, Abu Bakr, Zayd and Bilal, the public call brings Quraysh's torture — Sumayya becomes Islam's first martyr — and the believers flee to the just Negus in Abyssinia. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 5 Lesson 1: boycott, Taif, Isra and Mi'raj, and the Hijra” end?
**Answer:** Quraysh besiege Banu Hashim in Shi'b Abi Talib for three years, Taif stones the Prophet, the night journey carries him to al-Aqsa in Jerusalem, the Aqaba pledges open Yathrib, and the Hijra succeeds by planning — Ali in the bed, Abu Bakr beside him, Cave Thawr, a twisting road. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
