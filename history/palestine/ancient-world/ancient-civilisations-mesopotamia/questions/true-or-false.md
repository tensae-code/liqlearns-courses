# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Early humans signal with grunts, gestures, drawings and fire-smoke, and writing arrives with the Sumerian cuneiform the lesson names, before pigeons, telegraph and telephone carry messages further.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 6: rise of states — Babylonian and Assyrian civilisations” is mainly about this: The village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The lesson's own test asks where the Babylonian and Assyrian civilisations rose — in Iraq, the Mesopotamian heartland of early states.
