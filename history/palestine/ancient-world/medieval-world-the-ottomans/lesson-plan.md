## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottoman state arises** — Sulayman, grandfather of Osman, migrates from Turkmenistan to Anatolia; his son Ertugrul wins Seljuk land by fighting the Romans, and Osman inherits and founds the state on Rome's frontier.
2. **Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottomans take the Arab homeland** — Chaldiran (1514) breaks the Safavids, Marj Dabiq (1516) takes al-Sham from the Mamluks, Ridaniya (1517) makes Egypt an Ottoman province, and the Sharif of Mecca brings the Hijaz in the same year.
3. **Unit 2 Lesson 3: the Arab homeland under Ottoman rule** — The system is sultan, grand vizier, sipahi and janissary army, defterdar over finance and Shaykh al-Islam over religion; in the late era feudal iltizam, heavy taxes, fleeing peasants and unsafe roads sink the Arab economy.
4. **Unit 5 Lesson 4: the end of Ottoman rule** — Weakness piles up — European colonial penetration, the Committee of Union and Progress's Turkification from 1908, and foreign capitulations; Germany becomes the ally (Berlin–Baghdad and Hijaz railways, with Daraa–Haifa and Jerusalem–Jaffa lines in Palestine), the empire fights the First World War beside the Central Powers, and defeat brings Sèvres, the Paris settlement backing the mandate and Zionism, and the fall of the last caliphate.
