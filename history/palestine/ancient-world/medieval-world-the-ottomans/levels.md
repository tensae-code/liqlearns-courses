# The Medieval World: The Ottomans — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottoman state arises
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sulayman, grandfather of Osman, migrates from Turkmenistan to Anatolia; his son Ertugrul wins Seljuk land by fighting the Romans, and Osman inherits and founds the state on Rome's frontier. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottomans take the Arab homeland
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Chaldiran (1514) breaks the Safavids, Marj Dabiq (1516) takes al-Sham from the Mamluks, Ridaniya (1517) makes Egypt an Ottoman province, and the Sharif of Mecca brings the Hijaz in the same year. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 2 Lesson 3: the Arab homeland under Ottoman rule
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The system is sultan, grand vizier, sipahi and janissary army, defterdar over finance and Shaykh al-Islam over religion; in the late era feudal iltizam, heavy taxes, fleeing peasants and unsafe roads sink the Arab economy. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 5 Lesson 4: the end of Ottoman rule
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Weakness piles up — European colonial penetration, the Committee of Union and Progress's Turkification from 1908, and foreign capitulations; Germany becomes the ally (Berlin–Baghdad and Hijaz railways, with Daraa–Haifa and Jerusalem–Jaffa lines in Palestine), the empire fights the First World War beside the Central Powers, and defeat brings Sèvres, the Paris settlement backing the mandate and Zionism, and the fall of the last caliphate. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
