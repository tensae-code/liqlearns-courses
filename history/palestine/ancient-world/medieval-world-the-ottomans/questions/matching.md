# The Medieval World: The Ottomans — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottoman state arises — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottomans take the Arab homeland — b)


Meanings:
a) Chaldiran (1514) breaks the Safavids, Marj Dabiq (1516) takes al-Sham from the Mamluks, Ridaniya (1517) makes Egypt an Ottoman province, and the Sharif of Mecca brings the Hijaz in the same year.
b) Sulayman, grandfather of Osman, migrates from Turkmenistan to Anatolia; his son Ertugrul wins Seljuk land by fighting the Romans, and Osman inherits and founds the state on Rome's frontier.

**Answer key:** 1. b; 2. a
