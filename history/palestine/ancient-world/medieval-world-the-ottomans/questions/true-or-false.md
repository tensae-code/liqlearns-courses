# The Medieval World: The Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sulayman, grandfather of Osman, migrates from Turkmenistan to Anatolia; his son Ertugrul wins Seljuk land by fighting the Romans, and Osman inherits and founds the state on Rome's frontier.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottomans take the Arab homeland” is mainly about this: Egyptians invent coinage that spreads worldwide, make paper from papyrus, and embalm their dead because they believe in life after death.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Chaldiran (1514) breaks the Safavids, Marj Dabiq (1516) takes al-Sham from the Mamluks, Ridaniya (1517) makes Egypt an Ottoman province, and the Sharif of Mecca brings the Hijaz in the same year.
