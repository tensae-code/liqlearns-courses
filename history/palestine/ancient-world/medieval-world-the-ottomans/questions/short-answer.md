# The Medieval World: The Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottoman state arises”?
**Answer:** Sulayman, grandfather of Osman, migrates from Turkmenistan to Anatolia; his son Ertugrul wins Seljuk land by fighting the Romans, and Osman inherits and founds the state on Rome's frontier. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 2: the Ottomans take the Arab homeland” end?
**Answer:** Chaldiran (1514) breaks the Safavids, Marj Dabiq (1516) takes al-Sham from the Mamluks, Ridaniya (1517) makes Egypt an Ottoman province, and the Sharif of Mecca brings the Hijaz in the same year. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
