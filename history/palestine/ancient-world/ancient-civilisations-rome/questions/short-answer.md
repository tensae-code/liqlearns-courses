# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 2: Palmyra (Tadmor) and Queen Zenobia”?
**Answer:** Desert Palmyra grows rich as a caravan crossroads, Queen Zenobia extends its power over parts of Egypt and Asia Minor, and Rome crushes and occupies it in 273 CE, ending the state. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 2: Palmyra (Tadmor) and Queen Zenobia” end?
**Answer:** Desert Palmyra grows rich as a caravan crossroads, Queen Zenobia extends its power over parts of Egypt and Asia Minor, and Rome crushes and occupies it in 273 CE, ending the state. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
