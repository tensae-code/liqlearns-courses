**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 2: civilisations of Bilad al-Sham — the Canaanites”?
a) Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade.  b) From the later 1700s Britain replaces hand work with water- and steam-driven machines, helped by post-1688 political stability, capital, coal, cheap labour and empire; the revolution spreads to France, Germany, Italy and North America.  c) After the "water war" over the Jordan diversion (1964–65), a surprise air strike on 5 June 1967 destroys the Egyptian, Jordanian and Syrian air forces on the ground; in six days the Golan, the West Bank, Gaza and Sinai are occupied, the Security Council answers with Resolution 242, and the occupation's first orders (the military governor's proclamation of 7 June 1967) subordinate the economy and demolish the Latrun villages Imwas, Yalu and Beit Nuba — Canada Park rises on Imwas's land.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
