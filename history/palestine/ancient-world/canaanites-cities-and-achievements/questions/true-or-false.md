# The Canaanites: Cities and Achievements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 2 Lesson 3: Palestine (Land of Canaan)” is mainly about this: History is defined first as time itself, then as the study of the past that explains the present, connects pupils to their ancestors and teaches nations to guard their heritage.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Canaanites build cities from Ras Shamra (Ugarit) to Gaza and Rafah — Urusalim (Jerusalem, "god of peace"), Shechem (Nablus), Jaffa, Hebron, Megiddo — mint shekel coinage, trade with Babylon, Egypt and Yemen, and endure successive Pharaonic, Assyrian, Chaldean and Persian occupations because their land commands the routes.
