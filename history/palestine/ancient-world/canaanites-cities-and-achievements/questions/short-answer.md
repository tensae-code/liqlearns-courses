# The Canaanites: Cities and Achievements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 2: civilisations of Bilad al-Sham — the Canaanites”?
**Answer:** Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 3: Palestine (Land of Canaan)” end?
**Answer:** The Canaanites build cities from Ras Shamra (Ugarit) to Gaza and Rafah — Urusalim (Jerusalem, "god of peace"), Shechem (Nablus), Jaffa, Hebron, Megiddo — mint shekel coinage, trade with Babylon, Egypt and Yemen, and endure successive Pharaonic, Assyrian, Chaldean and Persian occupations because their land commands the routes. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
