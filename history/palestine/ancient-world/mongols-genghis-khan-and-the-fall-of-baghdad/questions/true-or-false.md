# The Mongols: Genghis Khan and the Fall of Baghdad — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Genghis Khan unites the nomad tribes of the Mongolian plateau into an empire reaching the Danube, and the invasions come for expansion and plunder of the Islamic lands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mongols turn Muslim” is mainly about this: Ma'in (1200–650 BCE, capital Qarnaw) and Saba (950–115 BCE) rise in Yemen on rain, the Zamzam-like wells and incense trade; Saba's farmers dam the floods at Marib, terrace the mountains and leave Bilqis's throne as their emblem.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Golden Horde is the first Mongol people to embrace Islam, others follow, and the converts become defenders and builders of Islamic civilisation, raising monuments such as the Taj Mahal in India.
