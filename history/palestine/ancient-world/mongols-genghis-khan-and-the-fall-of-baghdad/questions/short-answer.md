# The Mongols: Genghis Khan and the Fall of Baghdad — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mongols invade the Islamic East”?
**Answer:** Genghis Khan unites the nomad tribes of the Mongolian plateau into an empire reaching the Danube, and the invasions come for expansion and plunder of the Islamic lands. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mongols turn Muslim” end?
**Answer:** The Golden Horde is the first Mongol people to embrace Islam, others follow, and the converts become defenders and builders of Islamic civilisation, raising monuments such as the Taj Mahal in India. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
