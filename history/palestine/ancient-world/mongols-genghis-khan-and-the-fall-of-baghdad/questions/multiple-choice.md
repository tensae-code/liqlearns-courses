# The Mongols: Genghis Khan and the Fall of Baghdad — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mongols invade the Islamic East”?
a) Genghis Khan unites the nomad tribes of the Mongolian plateau into an empire reaching the Danube, and the invasions come for expansion and plunder of the Islamic lands.  b) In 1948 Palestinians proclaim the All-Palestine Government in Gaza under Haj Amin al-Husseini — with its own flag and passports — refusing a mere civil administration, until international pressure aborts it.  c) The Great Wall is named among the ancient world's civilisation landmarks pupils must know, beside Petra and the Roman theatre at Sebastia in Palestine.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Genghis Khan unites the nomad tribes of the Mongolian plateau into an empire reaching the Danube, and the invasions come for expansion and plunder of the Islamic lands.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the Mongols turn Muslim”?

**Answer: a) The Golden Horde is the first Mongol people to embrace Islam, others follow, and the converts become defenders and builders of Islamic civilisation, raising monuments such as the Taj Mahal in India.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
