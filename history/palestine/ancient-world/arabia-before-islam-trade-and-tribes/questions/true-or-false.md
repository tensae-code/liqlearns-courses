# Arabia before Islam: Trade and Tribes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Jahiliyya Arabs worship stones, trees, sun and moon beside the Kaaba's idols; the family and then the tribe rule life, women range from Queen Zenobia to daughters buried alive (wa'd), and Bedouin tent-dwellers live hard against settled townsfolk of Yemen, al-Sham, Iraq and the Hijaz.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 2: political life before Islam — kingdoms and empires” is mainly about this: Every community's traditional dress carries its identity, and Palestine's embroidered thobe for women and hatta, iqal and qumbaz for men are presented as living heritage to protect.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Hereditary kingdoms (Ma'in, Saba and Himyar in Yemen, the Munadhira in Iraq, Nabataeans, Ghassanids and Palmyra in al-Sham) stand beside tribal sheikhdoms, while Persia and Rome occupy most Arab lands and the Hijaz cities — Mecca under Qusay's Quraysh, feuding Yathrib, Taif — keep their independence.
