# Al-Andalus: Taifa Kingdoms and the Fall of Granada — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After eight centuries, luxury and power-struggles split al-Andalus into 22 taifa statelets that fall one by one; Spain then breaks the Granada surrender treaty — expelling hundreds of thousands, closing mosques, burning Arabic books and setting up the Inquisition.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 4 Lesson 4: the Islamic state collapses in al-Andalus” is mainly about this: Desert Palmyra grows rich as a caravan crossroads, Queen Zenobia extends its power over parts of Egypt and Asia Minor, and Rome crushes and occupies it in 273 CE, ending the state.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After eight centuries, luxury and power-struggles split al-Andalus into 22 taifa statelets that fall one by one; Spain then breaks the Granada surrender treaty — expelling hundreds of thousands, closing mosques, burning Arabic books and setting up the Inquisition.
