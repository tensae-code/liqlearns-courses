# Lesson plan — Al-Andalus: Taifa Kingdoms and the Fall of Granada

**Course:** History — Palestine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Al-Andalus: Taifa Kingdoms and the Fall of Granada
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Storms and Empires: Decline to the Ottoman Centuries (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After eight centuries, luxury and power-struggles split al-Andalus into 22 taifa statelets that fall one by one; Spain then breaks the Granada surrender treaty — expelling hundreds of thousands, closing mosques, burning Arabic books and setting up the Inquisition.
