# Abbasid Decline and the Breakaway States

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Abbasid Decline and the Breakaway States** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

An empire stretched from India's edge to the Atlantic outgrows its caliphs' grip; palace luxury, army interference, sectarian revolts and separatist governors hollow it out from the second Abbasid era, provinces slip into nominal loyalty, and al-Andalus is the first region to break away. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
