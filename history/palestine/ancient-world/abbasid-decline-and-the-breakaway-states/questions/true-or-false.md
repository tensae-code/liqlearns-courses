# Abbasid Decline and the Breakaway States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An empire stretched from India's edge to the Atlantic outgrows its caliphs' grip; palace luxury, army interference, sectarian revolts and separatist governors hollow it out from the second Abbasid era, provinces slip into nominal loyalty, and al-Andalus is the first region to break away.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 4 Lesson 1: internal factors weakening the Islamic state in the Abbasid period” is mainly about this: About 4,000 years ago the Canaanites of Palestine invent alphabetic writing, borrowing signs from their own world — the ox-head for the first letter — and the alphabet travels from them to the whole world.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: An empire stretched from India's edge to the Atlantic outgrows its caliphs' grip; palace luxury, army interference, sectarian revolts and separatist governors hollow it out from the second Abbasid era, provinces slip into nominal loyalty, and al-Andalus is the first region to break away.
