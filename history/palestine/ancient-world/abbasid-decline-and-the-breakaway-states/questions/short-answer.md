# Abbasid Decline and the Breakaway States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 4 Lesson 1: internal factors weakening the Islamic state in the Abbasid period”?
**Answer:** An empire stretched from India's edge to the Atlantic outgrows its caliphs' grip; palace luxury, army interference, sectarian revolts and separatist governors hollow it out from the second Abbasid era, provinces slip into nominal loyalty, and al-Andalus is the first region to break away. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
