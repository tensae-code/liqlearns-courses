# The Canaanites and the First Alphabet — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 5 Lesson 3: the Canaanites invent the alphabetic script”?
**Answer:** About 4,000 years ago the Canaanites of Palestine invent alphabetic writing, borrowing signs from their own world — the ox-head for the first letter — and the alphabet travels from them to the whole world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 5 Lesson 3: the Canaanites invent the alphabetic script” end?
**Answer:** About 4,000 years ago the Canaanites of Palestine invent alphabetic writing, borrowing signs from their own world — the ox-head for the first letter — and the alphabet travels from them to the whole world. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
