# The Canaanites and the First Alphabet — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** About 4,000 years ago the Canaanites of Palestine invent alphabetic writing, borrowing signs from their own world — the ox-head for the first letter — and the alphabet travels from them to the whole world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 5 Lesson 3: the Canaanites invent the alphabetic script” is mainly about this: The burning of the al-Qibli mosque in al-Aqsa on 23 August 1969 triggers a general strike across the Arab and Islamic lands and the call for an Islamic summit, out of which the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded with Jerusalem's liberation written into its charter.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: About 4,000 years ago the Canaanites of Palestine invent alphabetic writing, borrowing signs from their own world — the ox-head for the first letter — and the alphabet travels from them to the whole world.
