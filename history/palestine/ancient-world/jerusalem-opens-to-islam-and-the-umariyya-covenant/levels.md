# Jerusalem Opens to Islam and the Umariyya Covenant — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — At Ajnadayn (13 AH / 634 CE) near Ajjur — a village the occupation would destroy in 1948 — Amr ibn al-As opens Palestine; Jerusalem surrenders to Caliph Umar himself, whose Umariyya Covenant, read in full, guarantees its people safety for lives, property, churches and crosses in return for jizya. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
