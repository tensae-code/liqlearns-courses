# Jerusalem Opens to Islam and the Umariyya Covenant — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Ajnadayn (13 AH / 634 CE) near Ajjur — a village the occupation would destroy in 1948 — Amr ibn al-As opens Palestine; Jerusalem surrenders to Caliph Umar himself, whose Umariyya Covenant, read in full, guarantees its people safety for lives, property, churches and crosses in return for jizya.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 5 Lesson 7: Palestine under the Rashidun Caliphate” is mainly about this: Pupils match the world's old civilisations to their surviving landmarks, and Egypt's pyramids stand for the Nile kingdom's state power.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: At Ajnadayn (13 AH / 634 CE) near Ajjur — a village the occupation would destroy in 1948 — Amr ibn al-As opens Palestine; Jerusalem surrenders to Caliph Umar himself, whose Umariyya Covenant, read in full, guarantees its people safety for lives, property, churches and crosses in return for jizya.
