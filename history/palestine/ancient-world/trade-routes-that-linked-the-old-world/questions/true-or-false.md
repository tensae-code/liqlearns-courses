# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Quraysh caravans journey to Yemen in winter and to al-Sham in summer, India trades spices with its neighbours, and Arabs and China trade silk across the old world's routes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 3: economic life — Arab trade routes” is mainly about this: After the "water war" over the Jordan diversion (1964–65), a surprise air strike on 5 June 1967 destroys the Egyptian, Jordanian and Syrian air forces on the ground; in six days the Golan, the West Bank, Gaza and Sinai are occupied, the Security Council answers with Resolution 242, and the occupation's first orders (the military governor's proclamation of 7 June 1967) subordinate the economy and demolish the Latrun villages Imwas, Yalu and Beit Nuba — Canada Park rises on Imwas's land.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Arabs herd, farm oases and make swords and pottery, and their caravans link Persia, Rome, Abyssinia and India by the eastern and western land roads and the sea road to East Africa, Quraysh running the winter journey to Yemen and the summer journey to al-Sham.
