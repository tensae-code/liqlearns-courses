Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 2: sources of historical knowledge — what history means”?
a) History is defined first as time itself, then as the study of the past that explains the present, connects pupils to their ancestors and teaches nations to guard their heritage.  b) Muawiya restores Jerusalem's walls, the Umayyads rebuild Ascalon, expand Caesarea and Acre, and raise the Dome of the Rock; Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik founds Ramla, the only city Muslims built from nothing in Palestine, and Jerusalem's wall gets its last rebuilding under the Ottoman sultan.  c) From the later 1700s Britain replaces hand work with water- and steam-driven machines, helped by post-1688 political stability, capital, coal, cheap labour and empire; the revolution spreads to France, Germany, Italy and North America.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
