# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 1 Lesson 2: sources of historical knowledge — what history means”?
**Answer:** History is defined first as time itself, then as the study of the past that explains the present, connects pupils to their ancestors and teaches nations to guard their heritage. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 2: sources of historical knowledge — what history means” end?
**Answer:** History is defined first as time itself, then as the study of the past that explains the present, connects pupils to their ancestors and teaches nations to guard their heritage. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
