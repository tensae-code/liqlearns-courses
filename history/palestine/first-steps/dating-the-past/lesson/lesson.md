# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Dating the Past** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Pupils learn to read ق.م (before Christ), م (CE) and the Hijri era Muslims took from the Prophet's migration, the three clocks every later lesson runs on. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
