# From Evidence to a Written History

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **From Evidence to a Written History** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The past is rebuilt from written sources — scripture, histories like Ibn al-Athir's al-Kamil, official documents, land deeds (kawashin), travel books and personal memoirs. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
