# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 1 Lesson 2: written historical sources”?
**Answer:** The past is rebuilt from written sources — scripture, histories like Ibn al-Athir's al-Kamil, official documents, land deeds (kawashin), travel books and personal memoirs. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 2: written historical sources” end?
**Answer:** The past is rebuilt from written sources — scripture, histories like Ibn al-Athir's al-Kamil, official documents, land deeds (kawashin), travel books and personal memoirs. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
