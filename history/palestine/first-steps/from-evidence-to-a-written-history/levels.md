# From Evidence to a Written History — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The past is rebuilt from written sources — scripture, histories like Ibn al-Athir's al-Kamil, official documents, land deeds (kawashin), travel books and personal memoirs. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
