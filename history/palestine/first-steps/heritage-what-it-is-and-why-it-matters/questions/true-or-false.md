# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every community's traditional dress carries its identity, and Palestine's embroidered thobe for women and hatta, iqal and qumbaz for men are presented as living heritage to protect.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 1 Lesson 2: threats to Palestine's antiquities and protecting them” is mainly about this: On 29 November 1947 the UN recommends dividing Palestine into an Arab state (Upper Galilee, the central belt from Jenin to Beersheba, the southern coast with Gaza, parts of the Negev) and a Jewish state (eastern Galilee, Marj Ibn Amir, the central coastal plain), and the lesson weighs the Palestinian, Arab, Zionist and international positions on it.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Palestine's antiquities are looted and its history forged to erase Palestinian presence, so pupils must name historic sites near home and propose ways to protect them.
