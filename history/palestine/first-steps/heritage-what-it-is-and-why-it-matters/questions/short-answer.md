# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 3: heritage dress of peoples”?
**Answer:** Every community's traditional dress carries its identity, and Palestine's embroidered thobe for women and hatta, iqal and qumbaz for men are presented as living heritage to protect. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 2: threats to Palestine's antiquities and protecting them” end?
**Answer:** Palestine's antiquities are looted and its history forged to erase Palestinian presence, so pupils must name historic sites near home and propose ways to protect them. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
