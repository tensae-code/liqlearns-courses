## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2 Lesson 3: heritage dress of peoples** — Every community's traditional dress carries its identity, and Palestine's embroidered thobe for women and hatta, iqal and qumbaz for men are presented as living heritage to protect.
2. **Unit 1 Lesson 2: threats to Palestine's antiquities and protecting them** — Palestine's antiquities are looted and its history forged to erase Palestinian presence, so pupils must name historic sites near home and propose ways to protect them.
3. **Unit 2 Lesson 3: dangers facing Islamic antiquities in Jerusalem** — Since the gangs seized most of Palestine in 1948 and Jerusalem in 1967, a policy of erasing Arab-Islamic landmarks runs against Security Council Resolution 252 (1968), and even the al-Buraq Wall is renamed — heritage defence as a present duty.
4. **Unit 5 Lesson 4: protecting the human cultural heritage** — Heritage faces natural dangers (climate cracking and eroding old buildings) and human ones — war, the excavations around al-Aqsa and the closure of Jerusalem's Orient House; the 1954 Hague Convention (Article 4) bans stealing or destroying cultural property, and after Palestine's full UNESCO membership on 13 October 2011 its sites, Jerusalem's Old Town first, enter the World Heritage list.
