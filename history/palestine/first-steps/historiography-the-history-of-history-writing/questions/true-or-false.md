# Historiography: The History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Muslims begin by writing the Prophet's sira and campaigns, then produce al-Tabari's History of Nations and Kings, al-Ya'qubi's history, Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan and al-Idrisi's world map — and Abd al-Hamid al-Katib's letter is read as a founding text of Arabic prose.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 2 Lesson 4: Muslims write history and geography” is mainly about this: Quraysh caravans journey to Yemen in winter and to al-Sham in summer, India trades spices with its neighbours, and Arabs and China trade silk across the old world's routes.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Muslims begin by writing the Prophet's sira and campaigns, then produce al-Tabari's History of Nations and Kings, al-Ya'qubi's history, Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan and al-Idrisi's world map — and Abd al-Hamid al-Katib's letter is read as a founding text of Arabic prose.
