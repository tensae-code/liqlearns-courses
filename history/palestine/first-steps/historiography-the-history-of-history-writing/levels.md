# Historiography: The History of History Writing — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Muslims begin by writing the Prophet's sira and campaigns, then produce al-Tabari's History of Nations and Kings, al-Ya'qubi's history, Yaqut's Mu'jam al-Buldan and al-Idrisi's world map — and Abd al-Hamid al-Katib's letter is read as a founding text of Arabic prose. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
