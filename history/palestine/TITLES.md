# History — Palestine course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Palestine's own — built from Palestine's Ministry of Education *Social Studies* books for Grades 5–9 and *Geography of Palestine and its Modern and Contemporary History* books for Grade 10 (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': first steps and the first humans, the ancient world around Palestine, Islam rising and Palestine opening to it, decline and the Ottoman centuries, the modern world arriving as colonialism, and Palestine's own modern story — villages, Nakba and the Authority. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
