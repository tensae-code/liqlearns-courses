# Hands-on — The OIC Founded after the 1969 Al-Aqsa Arson

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — The OIC Founded after the 1969 Al-Aqsa Arson — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The burning of the al-Qibli mosque in al-Aqsa on 23 August 1969 triggers a general strike across the Arab and Islamic lands and the call for an Islamic summit, out of which the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded with Jerusalem's liberation written into its charter. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
