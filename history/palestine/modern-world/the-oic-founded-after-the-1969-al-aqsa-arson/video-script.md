# Video script — The OIC Founded after the 1969 Al-Aqsa Arson

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The burning of the al-Qibli mosque in al-Aqsa on 23 August 1969 triggers a general strike across the Arab and Islamic lands and the call for an Islamic summit, out of which the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded with Jerusalem's liberation written into its charter. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Palestine.
