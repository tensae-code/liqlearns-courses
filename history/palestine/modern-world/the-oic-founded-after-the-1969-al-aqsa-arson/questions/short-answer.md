# The OIC Founded after the 1969 Al-Aqsa Arson — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 5 Lesson 3: Palestine and the Islamic world — the OIC is born”?
**Answer:** The burning of the al-Qibli mosque in al-Aqsa on 23 August 1969 triggers a general strike across the Arab and Islamic lands and the call for an Islamic summit, out of which the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded with Jerusalem's liberation written into its charter. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 5 Lesson 3: Palestine and the Islamic world — the OIC is born” end?
**Answer:** The burning of the al-Qibli mosque in al-Aqsa on 23 August 1969 triggers a general strike across the Arab and Islamic lands and the call for an Islamic summit, out of which the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded with Jerusalem's liberation written into its charter. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
