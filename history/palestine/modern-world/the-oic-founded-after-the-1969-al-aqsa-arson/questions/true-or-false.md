# The OIC Founded after the 1969 Al-Aqsa Arson — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The burning of the al-Qibli mosque in al-Aqsa on 23 August 1969 triggers a general strike across the Arab and Islamic lands and the call for an Islamic summit, out of which the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded with Jerusalem's liberation written into its charter.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 5 Lesson 3: Palestine and the Islamic world — the OIC is born” is mainly about this: North America's native peoples (called "Red Indians") hold that the Great Spirit gave the land to all and it cannot be sold; Europeans then migrate fleeing religious persecution, tyranny and poverty, settling the thirteen colonies on the east coast.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The burning of the al-Qibli mosque in al-Aqsa on 23 August 1969 triggers a general strike across the Arab and Islamic lands and the call for an Islamic summit, out of which the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded with Jerusalem's liberation written into its charter.
