# The Six-Day War and Its Consequences — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the "water war" over the Jordan diversion (1964–65), a surprise air strike on 5 June 1967 destroys the Egyptian, Jordanian and Syrian air forces on the ground; in six days the Golan, the West Bank, Gaza and Sinai are occupied, the Security Council answers with Resolution 242, and the occupation's first orders (the military governor's proclamation of 7 June 1967) subordinate the economy and demolish the Latrun villages Imwas, Yalu and Beit Nuba — Canada Park rises on Imwas's land.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 5 Lesson 1: the June 1967 War (the Naksa)” is mainly about this: From the later 1700s Britain replaces hand work with water- and steam-driven machines, helped by post-1688 political stability, capital, coal, cheap labour and empire; the revolution spreads to France, Germany, Italy and North America.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After the "water war" over the Jordan diversion (1964–65), a surprise air strike on 5 June 1967 destroys the Egyptian, Jordanian and Syrian air forces on the ground; in six days the Golan, the West Bank, Gaza and Sinai are occupied, the Security Council answers with Resolution 242, and the occupation's first orders (the military governor's proclamation of 7 June 1967) subordinate the economy and demolish the Latrun villages Imwas, Yalu and Beit Nuba — Canada Park rises on Imwas's land.
