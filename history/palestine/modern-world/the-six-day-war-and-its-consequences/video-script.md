# Video script — The Six-Day War and Its Consequences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

After the "water war" over the Jordan diversion (1964–65), a surprise air strike on 5 June 1967 destroys the Egyptian, Jordanian and Syrian air forces on the ground; in six days the Golan, the West Bank, Gaza and Sinai are occupied, the Security Council answers with Resolution 242, and the occupation's first orders (the military governor's proclamation of 7 June 1967) subordinate the economy and demolish the Latrun villages Imwas, Yalu and Beit Nuba — Canada Park rises on Imwas's land. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Palestine.
