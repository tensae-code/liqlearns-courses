# Exploration and the First Global Age

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Exploration and the First Global Age** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4 Lesson 2: the geographical discoveries

Portugal sails east — Diaz rounds the Cape of Good Hope and da Gama reaches India guided by the Arab pilot Ahmad ibn Majid — while Spain sails west with Columbus to San Salvador and Cuba, and Magellan's voyage circles the world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 4 Lesson 2: results of the discoveries

World trade's centre shifts from Mediterranean ports to Atlantic ones; the Arab ports of Mamluk Egypt and al-Sham, and Venice with them, decline as the Cape route takes the India trade. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
