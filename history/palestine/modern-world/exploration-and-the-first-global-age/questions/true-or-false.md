# Exploration and the First Global Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Portugal sails east — Diaz rounds the Cape of Good Hope and da Gama reaches India guided by the Arab pilot Ahmad ibn Majid — while Spain sails west with Columbus to San Salvador and Cuba, and Magellan's voyage circles the world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 2: results of the discoveries” is mainly about this: At Ajnadayn (13 AH / 634 CE) near Ajjur — a village the occupation would destroy in 1948 — Amr ibn al-As opens Palestine; Jerusalem surrenders to Caliph Umar himself, whose Umariyya Covenant, read in full, guarantees its people safety for lives, property, churches and crosses in return for jizya.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: World trade's centre shifts from Mediterranean ports to Atlantic ones; the Arab ports of Mamluk Egypt and al-Sham, and Venice with them, decline as the Cape route takes the India trade.
