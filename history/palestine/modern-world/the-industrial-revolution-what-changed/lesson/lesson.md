# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Industrial Revolution: What Changed** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

From the later 1700s Britain replaces hand work with water- and steam-driven machines, helped by post-1688 political stability, capital, coal, cheap labour and empire; the revolution spreads to France, Germany, Italy and North America. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
