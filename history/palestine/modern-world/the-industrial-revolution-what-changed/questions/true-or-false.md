# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From the later 1700s Britain replaces hand work with water- and steam-driven machines, helped by post-1688 political stability, capital, coal, cheap labour and empire; the revolution spreads to France, Germany, Italy and North America.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Industrial Revolution” is mainly about this: Muawiya restores Jerusalem's walls, the Umayyads rebuild Ascalon, expand Caesarea and Acre, and raise the Dome of the Rock; Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik founds Ramla, the only city Muslims built from nothing in Palestine, and Jerusalem's wall gets its last rebuilding under the Ottoman sultan.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From the later 1700s Britain replaces hand work with water- and steam-driven machines, helped by post-1688 political stability, capital, coal, cheap labour and empire; the revolution spreads to France, Germany, Italy and North America.
