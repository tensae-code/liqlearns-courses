# Palestine under Ottoman Rule: Zahir al-Umar and al-Jazzar — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Palestine is governed as sanjaks (Jerusalem's first among them), but local power keeps breaking away — Fakhr al-Din al-Ma'ni ruling Lebanon and northern Palestine from 1603, Zahir al-Umar al-Zaydani (1689–1775) building his Galilee state, and Ahmad Pasha al-Jazzar installed at Acre after crushing Zahir in 1775.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 2 Lesson 4: Ottoman building works in Palestine” is mainly about this: Phoenicians found Carthage in Tunis and it rules the coasts of Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and southern Spain, until Rome besieges it four years, burns it for seventeen days and takes its people captive in the second century BCE (told through a quoted passage from Mustafa Murad al-Dabbagh's ancient history of the Near East).
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Suleiman the Magnificent rebuilds Jerusalem's wall, restores the al-Qibli mosque and its sanctuary gates and the Church of the Holy Sepulchre and monasteries, and the era leaves mosques and clock towers across Palestine.
