# Palestine under Ottoman Rule: Zahir al-Umar and al-Jazzar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 4: Palestine in the Ottoman era — its rulers”?
**Answer:** Palestine is governed as sanjaks (Jerusalem's first among them), but local power keeps breaking away — Fakhr al-Din al-Ma'ni ruling Lebanon and northern Palestine from 1603, Zahir al-Umar al-Zaydani (1689–1775) building his Galilee state, and Ahmad Pasha al-Jazzar installed at Acre after crushing Zahir in 1775. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 4: Ottoman building works in Palestine” end?
**Answer:** Suleiman the Magnificent rebuilds Jerusalem's wall, restores the al-Qibli mosque and its sanctuary gates and the Church of the Holy Sepulchre and monasteries, and the era leaves mosques and clock towers across Palestine. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
