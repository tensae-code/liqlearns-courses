# Palestine: Villages Destroyed in 1948 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 4: the Palestinian village and its destruction in 1948”?
a) The village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees.  b) Desert Palmyra grows rich as a caravan crossroads, Queen Zenobia extends its power over parts of Egypt and Asia Minor, and Rome crushes and occupies it in 273 CE, ending the state.  c) After eight centuries, luxury and power-struggles split al-Andalus into 22 taifa statelets that fall one by one; Spain then breaks the Granada surrender treaty — expelling hundreds of thousands, closing mosques, burning Arabic books and setting up the Inquisition.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
