# Palestine: Villages Destroyed in 1948 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 2 Lesson 1: migration and the displacement of the Palestinians” is mainly about this: After Europe's Industrial Revolution its factory goods flood in and undercut the Arab lands' traditional industries, one strand of the late-Ottoman decline.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After defining migration, the lesson turns to the Nakba: the 1948 war drives about a million Palestinians from their homes into camps in Jordan, Syria and Lebanon, and pupils are asked for a just solution to the refugee question.
