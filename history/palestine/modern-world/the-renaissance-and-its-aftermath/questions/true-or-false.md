# The Renaissance and Its Aftermath — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Against a church-bound Middle Ages of superstition, free thought, humanism and art flower (Florence, da Vinci's Mona Lisa); among its causes the book counts the Crusades' contact with Islamic civilisation, Constantinople's fall in 1453 sending scholars west, and printing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 1: Arab-Islamic civilisation feeds the Renaissance” is mainly about this: Muhammad receives the revelation in Cave Hira at forty; the secret call gathers Khadijah, Ali, Abu Bakr, Zayd and Bilal, the public call brings Quraysh's torture — Sumayya becomes Islam's first martyr — and the believers flee to the just Negus in Abyssinia.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From al-Andalus, European students carry Muslim learning into France and, through Sicily, into southern Italy; Crusades and trade carry more from Egypt, al-Sham and Iraq — al-Farabi's philosophy, Ibn Rushd, and Arabic medical books among the cargo.
