# Video script — The United Nations and the Postwar Order

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

On 29 November 1947 the UN recommends dividing Palestine into an Arab state (Upper Galilee, the central belt from Jenin to Beersheba, the southern coast with Gaza, parts of the Negev) and a Jewish state (eastern Galilee, Marj Ibn Amir, the central coastal plain), and the lesson weighs the Palestinian, Arab, Zionist and international positions on it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Palestine.
