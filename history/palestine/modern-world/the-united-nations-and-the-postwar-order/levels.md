# The United Nations and the Postwar Order — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — On 29 November 1947 the UN recommends dividing Palestine into an Arab state (Upper Galilee, the central belt from Jenin to Beersheba, the southern coast with Gaza, parts of the Negev) and a Jewish state (eastern Galilee, Marj Ibn Amir, the central coastal plain), and the lesson weighs the Palestinian, Arab, Zionist and international positions on it. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
