# The United Nations and the Postwar Order — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 3 Lesson 5: Partition Resolution 181 (1947)”?
**Answer:** On 29 November 1947 the UN recommends dividing Palestine into an Arab state (Upper Galilee, the central belt from Jenin to Beersheba, the southern coast with Gaza, parts of the Negev) and a Jewish state (eastern Galilee, Marj Ibn Amir, the central coastal plain), and the lesson weighs the Palestinian, Arab, Zionist and international positions on it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
