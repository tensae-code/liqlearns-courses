# The United Nations and the Postwar Order — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On 29 November 1947 the UN recommends dividing Palestine into an Arab state (Upper Galilee, the central belt from Jenin to Beersheba, the southern coast with Gaza, parts of the Negev) and a Jewish state (eastern Galilee, Marj Ibn Amir, the central coastal plain), and the lesson weighs the Palestinian, Arab, Zionist and international positions on it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 3 Lesson 5: Partition Resolution 181 (1947)” is mainly about this: Palestine is governed as sanjaks (Jerusalem's first among them), but local power keeps breaking away — Fakhr al-Din al-Ma'ni ruling Lebanon and northern Palestine from 1603, Zahir al-Umar al-Zaydani (1689–1775) building his Galilee state, and Ahmad Pasha al-Jazzar installed at Acre after crushing Zahir in 1775.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: On 29 November 1947 the UN recommends dividing Palestine into an Arab state (Upper Galilee, the central belt from Jenin to Beersheba, the southern coast with Gaza, parts of the Negev) and a Jewish state (eastern Galilee, Marj Ibn Amir, the central coastal plain), and the lesson weighs the Palestinian, Arab, Zionist and international positions on it.
