# Video script — The Yom Kippur War and the Oil Shock

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Palestine.
