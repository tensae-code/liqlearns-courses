# The Yom Kippur War and the Oil Shock — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 5 Lesson 4: the October 1973 War and Palestine”?
**Answer:** After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 5 Lesson 4: the October 1973 War and Palestine” end?
**Answer:** After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
