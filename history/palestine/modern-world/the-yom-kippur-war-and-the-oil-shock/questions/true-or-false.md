# The Yom Kippur War and the Oil Shock — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Unit 5 Lesson 4: the October 1973 War and Palestine” is mainly about this: After the Seven Years' War Britain taxes the colonies that had fought beside it; revolt breaks out in 1775, George Washington leads the volunteers with French help, and independence is declared at Philadelphia in 1776.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows.
