# The Yom Kippur War and the Oil Shock — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After the War of Attrition and the rejected Rogers Plan (1970), Egypt crosses the Suez Canal and storms the Bar Lev line while Syria advances into the Golan toward Mount Hermon and the Hula; Arab states cut oil production and embargo the United States, the Palestine Liberation Army fights on the Egyptian front, and Resolution 338 follows. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
