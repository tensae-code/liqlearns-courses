# The French Revolution and Napoleon — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A divine-right monarchy under Louis XVI, privileged clergy and nobles, a salt tax on the common people, and a financial crisis deepened by funding the American revolution bring France to 1789.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 5: course and results of the French Revolution” is mainly about this: Genghis Khan unites the nomad tribes of the Mongolian plateau into an empire reaching the Danube, and the invasions come for expansion and plunder of the Islamic lands.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, Paris storms the Bastille on 14 July 1789, and the revolution yields a republic, a constitution with separated powers, equality and free expression — principles that spread across Europe.
