# The French Revolution and Napoleon — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 4 Lesson 5: causes of the French Revolution”?
**Answer:** A divine-right monarchy under Louis XVI, privileged clergy and nobles, a salt tax on the common people, and a financial crisis deepened by funding the American revolution bring France to 1789. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 5: course and results of the French Revolution” end?
**Answer:** The Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, Paris storms the Bastille on 14 July 1789, and the revolution yields a republic, a constitution with separated powers, equality and free expression — principles that spread across Europe. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
