# The French Revolution and Napoleon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 5: causes of the French Revolution”?
a) A divine-right monarchy under Louis XVI, privileged clergy and nobles, a salt tax on the common people, and a financial crisis deepened by funding the American revolution bring France to 1789.  b) The Great Wall is named among the ancient world's civilisation landmarks pupils must know, beside Petra and the Roman theatre at Sebastia in Palestine.  c) Muawiya restores Jerusalem's walls, the Umayyads rebuild Ascalon, expand Caesarea and Acre, and raise the Dome of the Rock; Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik founds Ramla, the only city Muslims built from nothing in Palestine, and Jerusalem's wall gets its last rebuilding under the Ottoman sultan.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A divine-right monarchy under Louis XVI, privileged clergy and nobles, a salt tax on the common people, and a financial crisis deepened by funding the American revolution bring France to 1789.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
