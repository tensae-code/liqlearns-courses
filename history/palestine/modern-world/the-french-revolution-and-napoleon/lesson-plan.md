## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 4 Lesson 5: causes of the French Revolution** — A divine-right monarchy under Louis XVI, privileged clergy and nobles, a salt tax on the common people, and a financial crisis deepened by funding the American revolution bring France to 1789.
2. **Unit 4 Lesson 5: course and results of the French Revolution** — The Third Estate swears the Tennis Court Oath, Paris storms the Bastille on 14 July 1789, and the revolution yields a republic, a constitution with separated powers, equality and free expression — principles that spread across Europe.
3. **Unit 5 Lesson 3: the French campaign on Egypt and al-Sham** — Napoleon seeks to cut Britain's road to India and seize Egypt's wealth; Cairo revolts twice (1798, 1800) and is shelled and burned; after Britain destroys his fleet at Abu Qir (1798) he marches on Palestine (1799) — Acre holds out over two months behind al-Jazzar with Palestinian help, Nablus fighters ambush his columns in the hills ("Mountain of Fire"), and the Jaffa massacre stains the campaign.
