# Wars of Decolonisation: Algeria, Vietnam and Others — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Resistance is as old as the occupation itself but surges after the Second World War on self-determination principles, new media and the example of Syria, Lebanon and Jordan winning independence; the UN General Assembly's Resolution 1514 declares colonialism a crime — with Palestine named the exception still under occupation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 2 Lesson 2: forms of resistance” is mainly about this: Sulayman, grandfather of Osman, migrates from Turkmenistan to Anatolia; his son Ertugrul wins Seljuk land by fighting the Romans, and Osman inherits and founds the state on Rome's frontier.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Resistance runs armed and peaceful side by side — the PLO adopts armed struggle from 1965 and the intifada renews it, while civil resistance boycotts goods, exposes the occupier's crimes in world forums and courts, and cuts economic dependence; everywhere it fights short of men, arms, unity and supply lines.
