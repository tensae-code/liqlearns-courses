## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2 Lesson 2: forms of resistance
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Resistance runs armed and peaceful side by side — the PLO adopts armed struggle from 1965 and the intifada renews it, while civil resistance boycotts goods, exposes the occupier's crimes in world forums and courts, and cuts economic dependence; everywhere it fights short of men, arms, unity and supply lines. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 2 Lesson 3: models of Arab liberation — Libya, Syria, Iraq
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Libya passes from Italy to Britain and France to a UN trusteeship and independence in 1951; Syria's Yusuf al-Azma falls with some 800 men at Maysalun (1920) against France, which splits Syria into Alawite, Druze and Aleppo statelets and meets revolts up to the Great Syrian Revolt; Iraq rises against Britain in 1920 after the San Remo mandate. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
