# The American Revolution and Its Ideas — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After the Seven Years' War Britain taxes the colonies that had fought beside it; revolt breaks out in 1775, George Washington leads the volunteers with French help, and independence is declared at Philadelphia in 1776. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
