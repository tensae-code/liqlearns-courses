# The American Revolution and Its Ideas

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The American Revolution and Its Ideas** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

After the Seven Years' War Britain taxes the colonies that had fought beside it; revolt breaks out in 1775, George Washington leads the volunteers with French help, and independence is declared at Philadelphia in 1776. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
