**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 4: the American colonies' revolution and independence”?
a) After the Seven Years' War Britain taxes the colonies that had fought beside it; revolt breaks out in 1775, George Washington leads the volunteers with French help, and independence is declared at Philadelphia in 1776.  b) Pupils match the world's old civilisations to their surviving landmarks, and Egypt's pyramids stand for the Nile kingdom's state power.  c) At Ajnadayn (13 AH / 634 CE) near Ajjur — a village the occupation would destroy in 1948 — Amr ibn al-As opens Palestine; Jerusalem surrenders to Caliph Umar himself, whose Umariyya Covenant, read in full, guarantees its people safety for lives, property, churches and crosses in return for jizya.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
