# Video script — Empires and the Race for Colonies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The homeland's straits, trade routes, resources and markets draw the powers from Portugal's 1500s Indian Ocean thrust onward, Ottoman weakness opening the way; the outcome is partition into small states behind artificial borders and a Zionist entity planted in Palestine to split Arab Asia from Arab Africa. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
The colonial powers slice the homeland into small states behind artificial borders to serve their interests and block Arab unity; Arabs answer with unity projects — the Arab League (charter 1945), the United Arab Republic, the Gulf Cooperation Council and the Maghreb Union. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Palestine.
