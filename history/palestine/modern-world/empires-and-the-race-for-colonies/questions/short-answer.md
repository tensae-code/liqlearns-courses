# Empires and the Race for Colonies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 5 Lesson 2: the colonial rivalry over the Arab homeland”?
**Answer:** The homeland's straits, trade routes, resources and markets draw the powers from Portugal's 1500s Indian Ocean thrust onward, Ottoman weakness opening the way; the outcome is partition into small states behind artificial borders and a Zionist entity planted in Palestine to split Arab Asia from Arab Africa. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 1: political partition of the Arab homeland” end?
**Answer:** The colonial powers slice the homeland into small states behind artificial borders to serve their interests and block Arab unity; Arabs answer with unity projects — the Arab League (charter 1945), the United Arab Republic, the Gulf Cooperation Council and the Maghreb Union. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
