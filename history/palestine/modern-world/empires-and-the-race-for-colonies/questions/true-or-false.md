# Empires and the Race for Colonies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The homeland's straits, trade routes, resources and markets draw the powers from Portugal's 1500s Indian Ocean thrust onward, Ottoman weakness opening the way; the outcome is partition into small states behind artificial borders and a Zionist entity planted in Palestine to split Arab Asia from Arab Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 1 Lesson 1: political partition of the Arab homeland” is mainly about this: After eight centuries, luxury and power-struggles split al-Andalus into 22 taifa statelets that fall one by one; Spain then breaks the Granada surrender treaty — expelling hundreds of thousands, closing mosques, burning Arabic books and setting up the Inquisition.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The colonial powers slice the homeland into small states behind artificial borders to serve their interests and block Arab unity; Arabs answer with unity projects — the Arab League (charter 1945), the United Arab Republic, the Gulf Cooperation Council and the Maghreb Union.
