## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 5 Lesson 2: the colonial rivalry over the Arab homeland** — The homeland's straits, trade routes, resources and markets draw the powers from Portugal's 1500s Indian Ocean thrust onward, Ottoman weakness opening the way; the outcome is partition into small states behind artificial borders and a Zionist entity planted in Palestine to split Arab Asia from Arab Africa.
2. **Unit 1 Lesson 1: political partition of the Arab homeland** — The colonial powers slice the homeland into small states behind artificial borders to serve their interests and block Arab unity; Arabs answer with unity projects — the Arab League (charter 1945), the United Arab Republic, the Gulf Cooperation Council and the Maghreb Union.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
