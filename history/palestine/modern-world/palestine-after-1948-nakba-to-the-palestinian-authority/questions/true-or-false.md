# Palestine after 1948: Nakba to the Palestinian Authority — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In 1948 Palestinians proclaim the All-Palestine Government in Gaza under Haj Amin al-Husseini — with its own flag and passports — refusing a mere civil administration, until international pressure aborts it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 3: Palestine divided after 1948” is mainly about this: Portugal sails east — Diaz rounds the Cape of Good Hope and da Gama reaches India guided by the Arab pilot Ahmad ibn Majid — while Spain sails west with Columbus to San Salvador and Cuba, and Magellan's voyage circles the world.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Inside the new state, laws do the demographic work — army land confiscation (1949), the Absentees' Property Law and the Law of Return (both 1950); the Jericho Conference (1 December 1948) resolves to join the West Bank to Jordan, and Gaza (365 km²) passes under Egyptian administration.
