# Palestine after 1948: Nakba to the Palestinian Authority — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the All-Palestine Government”?
**Answer:** In 1948 Palestinians proclaim the All-Palestine Government in Gaza under Haj Amin al-Husseini — with its own flag and passports — refusing a mere civil administration, until international pressure aborts it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 3: Palestine divided after 1948” end?
**Answer:** Inside the new state, laws do the demographic work — army land confiscation (1949), the Absentees' Property Law and the Law of Return (both 1950); the Jericho Conference (1 December 1948) resolves to join the West Bank to Jordan, and Gaza (365 km²) passes under Egyptian administration. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
