Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 2: the All-Palestine Government”?
a) In 1948 Palestinians proclaim the All-Palestine Government in Gaza under Haj Amin al-Husseini — with its own flag and passports — refusing a mere civil administration, until international pressure aborts it.  b) Phoenicians found Carthage in Tunis and it rules the coasts of Africa, Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and southern Spain, until Rome besieges it four years, burns it for seventeen days and takes its people captive in the second century BCE (told through a quoted passage from Mustafa Murad al-Dabbagh's ancient history of the Near East).  c) Palestine is governed as sanjaks (Jerusalem's first among them), but local power keeps breaking away — Fakhr al-Din al-Ma'ni ruling Lebanon and northern Palestine from 1603, Zahir al-Umar al-Zaydani (1689–1775) building his Galilee state, and Ahmad Pasha al-Jazzar installed at Acre after crushing Zahir in 1775.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
