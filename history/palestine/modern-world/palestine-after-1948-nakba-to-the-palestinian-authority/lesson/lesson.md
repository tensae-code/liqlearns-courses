## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 4 Lesson 5: the PLO and the contemporary revolution

The first Palestinian National Council meets in Jerusalem on 28 May 1964 with King Hussein present and founds the PLO under Ahmad Shuqairi; Fatah launches on 1 January 1965 with the Eilabun operation, Yahya Hammuda holds the chair in between, and Yasser Arafat takes the leadership in 1969. ## Sub lesson 10 — Unit 6 Lesson 4: the 1987 Intifada
