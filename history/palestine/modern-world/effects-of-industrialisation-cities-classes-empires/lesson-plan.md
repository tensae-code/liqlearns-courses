## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2 Lesson 3: European industry outcompetes Arab crafts** — After Europe's Industrial Revolution its factory goods flood in and undercut the Arab lands' traditional industries, one strand of the late-Ottoman decline.
2. **Unit 4 Lesson 3: effects of the Industrial Revolution** — Society splits into a factory-owning bourgeoisie and a proletariat in cramped housing on long hours and low pay, with child and women labour spreading; industrial cities grow, production and trade expand — and factory pollution begins.
