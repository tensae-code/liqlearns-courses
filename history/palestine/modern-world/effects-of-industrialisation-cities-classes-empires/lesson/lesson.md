# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2 Lesson 3: European industry outcompetes Arab crafts

After Europe's Industrial Revolution its factory goods flood in and undercut the Arab lands' traditional industries, one strand of the late-Ottoman decline. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 4 Lesson 3: effects of the Industrial Revolution

Society splits into a factory-owning bourgeoisie and a proletariat in cramped housing on long hours and low pay, with child and women labour spreading; industrial cities grow, production and trade expand — and factory pollution begins. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
