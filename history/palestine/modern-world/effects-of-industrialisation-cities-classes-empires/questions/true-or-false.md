# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) After Europe's Industrial Revolution its factory goods flood in and undercut the Arab lands' traditional industries, one strand of the late-Ottoman decline.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 4 Lesson 3: effects of the Industrial Revolution” is mainly about this: In the Jahiliyya Arabs worship stones, trees, sun and moon beside the Kaaba's idols; the family and then the tribe rule life, women range from Queen Zenobia to daughters buried alive (wa'd), and Bedouin tent-dwellers live hard against settled townsfolk of Yemen, al-Sham, Iraq and the Hijaz.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Society splits into a factory-owning bourgeoisie and a proletariat in cramped housing on long hours and low pay, with child and women labour spreading; industrial cities grow, production and trade expand — and factory pollution begins.
