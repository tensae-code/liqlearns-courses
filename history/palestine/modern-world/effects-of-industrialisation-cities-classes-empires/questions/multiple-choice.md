# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Unit 2 Lesson 3: European industry outcompetes Arab crafts”?
a) After Europe's Industrial Revolution its factory goods flood in and undercut the Arab lands' traditional industries, one strand of the late-Ottoman decline.  b) Pupils learn to read ق.م (before Christ), م (CE) and the Hijri era Muslims took from the Prophet's migration, the three clocks every later lesson runs on.  c) Desert Palmyra grows rich as a caravan crossroads, Queen Zenobia extends its power over parts of Egypt and Asia Minor, and Rome crushes and occupies it in 273 CE, ending the state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) After Europe's Industrial Revolution its factory goods flood in and undercut the Arab lands' traditional industries, one strand of the late-Ottoman decline.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
