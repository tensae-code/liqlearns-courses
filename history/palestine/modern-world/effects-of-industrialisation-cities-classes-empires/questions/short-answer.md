# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 2 Lesson 3: European industry outcompetes Arab crafts”?
**Answer:** After Europe's Industrial Revolution its factory goods flood in and undercut the Arab lands' traditional industries, one strand of the late-Ottoman decline. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 3: effects of the Industrial Revolution” end?
**Answer:** Society splits into a factory-owning bourgeoisie and a proletariat in cramped housing on long hours and low pay, with child and women labour spreading; industrial cities grow, production and trade expand — and factory pollution begins. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
