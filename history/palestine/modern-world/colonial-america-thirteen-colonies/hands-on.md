# Hands-on — Colonial America: The Thirteen Colonies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Colonial America: The Thirteen Colonies — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: North America's native peoples (called "Red Indians") hold that the Great Spirit gave the land to all and it cannot be sold; Europeans then migrate fleeing religious persecution, tyranny and poverty, settling the thirteen colonies on the east coast. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
