# Colonial America: The Thirteen Colonies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unit 4 Lesson 4: the first peoples and the settlers”?
**Answer:** North America's native peoples (called "Red Indians") hold that the Great Spirit gave the land to all and it cannot be sold; Europeans then migrate fleeing religious persecution, tyranny and poverty, settling the thirteen colonies on the east coast. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Unit 4 Lesson 4: the first peoples and the settlers” end?
**Answer:** North America's native peoples (called "Red Indians") hold that the Great Spirit gave the land to all and it cannot be sold; Europeans then migrate fleeing religious persecution, tyranny and poverty, settling the thirteen colonies on the east coast. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
