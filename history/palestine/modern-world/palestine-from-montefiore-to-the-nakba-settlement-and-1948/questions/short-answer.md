# Palestine from Montefiore to the Nakba: Settlement and 1948 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Unit 1 Lesson 1: partition inside Palestine”?
**Answer:** The Zionist movement plants its first settlement, Montefiore, south-west of Jerusalem's wall in 1856, and the settlements double as training camps and arms stores; after the 1948 Nakba the occupation holds over 78% of Palestine. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unit 1 Lesson 4: the laws that keep refugees out” end?
**Answer:** The Absentees' Property Law hands the state the lands of Palestinians driven out in 1948, and the "present absentee" rule bars villagers like those of Ein Hawd — who hid in the Carmel forest from the Haganah and still live nearby — from ever returning to their own village. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
