# Palestine from Montefiore to the Nakba: Settlement and 1948 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Zionist movement plants its first settlement, Montefiore, south-west of Jerusalem's wall in 1856, and the settlements double as training camps and arms stores; after the 1948 Nakba the occupation holds over 78% of Palestine.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 1 Lesson 4: the laws that keep refugees out” is mainly about this: Against a church-bound Middle Ages of superstition, free thought, humanism and art flower (Florence, da Vinci's Mona Lisa); among its causes the book counts the Crusades' contact with Islamic civilisation, Constantinople's fall in 1453 sending scholars west, and printing.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Absentees' Property Law hands the state the lands of Palestinians driven out in 1948, and the "present absentee" rule bars villagers like those of Ein Hawd — who hid in the Carmel forest from the Haganah and still live nearby — from ever returning to their own village.
