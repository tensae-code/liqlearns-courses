# Colonialism and Its End — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Colonialism is defined as seizing land by force, plundering its wealth and enslaving its people; its shared motives are markets and raw materials (Britain forcing Egypt to grow cotton) and strategic positions, and its forms run from military occupation and settlement to protectorate (France in Tunisia, 1883), mandate after the First World War, economic control (the Suez Canal) and cultural invasion.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unit 1 Lesson 2: the colonial economic policy” is mainly about this: Slave-soldiers bought young by the Ayyubid sultans rise to rule Egypt and al-Sham, restore the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo after Baghdad's fall, and Sultan al-Ashraf Khalil ibn Qalawun expels the last Franks from Acre in 690 AH / 1291 CE, freeing Palestine for good.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Arab economy is made a dependent appendage — foreign goods favoured, local industry stunted, loans used as leverage; Arab answers include nationalisation and boycotts, while in Palestine the post-1967 occupation seizes land and turns the West Bank and Gaza into dumping grounds.
