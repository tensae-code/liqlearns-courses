# History — Guyana course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Guyana's own —
built from Guyana's Social Studies book for Grade 1 (student
book, English). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside Level 1,
in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The
grade the book comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: the nation's story enters
at the very start of schooling as dates a young child can
hold — independence day, republic day — wrapped in the flag,
anthem, pledge and national festivals of Guyana's many
peoples. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — our nation** (Social Studies For Our Children,
Book 1, student book):

- `modern-world/` — Level 1, the course's one title. Our
  Nation: Independence and Symbols (Guyana became independent
  on 26 May 1966 and is led by a President; the flag, coat of
  arms, national bird, the National Anthem and the National
  Pledge give the new nation a face its children can draw and
  sing; and the nation keeps its days together — Remembrance
  Day poppies for those who went to war, and Mashramani for
  the Co-operative Republic of 23 February 1970).
