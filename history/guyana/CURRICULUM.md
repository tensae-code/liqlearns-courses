# History — Guyana course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Guyana inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school
grade the book comes from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Guyana's book in hand is one book —
the first Social Studies book, where the nation's story enters
at the very start of schooling. Its own progression is the
ladder: first the nation itself (Guyana became independent,
and is led by a President), then the face the new nation shows
its children (flag, coat of arms, national bird, anthem,
pledge), then the days the nation keeps together (Remembrance
Day, and Mashramani for the Co-operative Republic). One book,
one level — the ladder stops where the book in hand stops,
not because Guyana teaches nothing more.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Social Studies book — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a
box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours;
the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book
list, the combined-book contents map, and the acceptance
reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Modern World → Our Nation: Independence and Symbols** —
   Guyana's nation story in one lesson with three parts that
   build in order: Guyana is an independent state (independent
   on 26 May 1966, led by a President); the national symbols,
   the National Anthem and the National Pledge; and the
   celebrations the nation keeps — Remembrance Day poppies,
   and Mashramani for the day Guyana became a Co-operative
   Republic, 23 February 1970. 1 lesson, in one title:
   `modern-world/` (1 lesson).

**Totals: 1 lesson** (Level 1: 1 — all from one student book).
