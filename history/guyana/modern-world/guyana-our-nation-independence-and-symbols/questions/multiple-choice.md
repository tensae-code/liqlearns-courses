# Our Nation: Independence and Symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On what day did Guyana become independent? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 26 May 1966  b) 23 February 1970  c) Remembrance Day  d) the first day of school
**Answer: a) 26 May 1966** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is one of Guyana's national symbols named in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the flag  b) a school bell  c) a market stall  d) a rain cloud
**Answer: a) the flag** — That is what this lesson teaches.
