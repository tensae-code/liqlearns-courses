# Our Nation: Independence and Symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How do the flag, coat of arms, national bird and anthem help a new nation? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** They give the new nation a face its children can know — the flag and coat of arms to see and draw, the national bird standing for the country, and the anthem to sing together.

**2.** Why do people wear red poppies on Remembrance Day? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The poppy is a remembering flower: wearing it honours those who went to war and did not all come back, and says they are not forgotten.
