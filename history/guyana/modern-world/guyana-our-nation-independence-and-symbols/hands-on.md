# Hands-on — Our Nation: Independence and Symbols

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the two dates out loud: 26 May 1966 and 23 February 1970. Now say what each one did: the first made Guyana independent; the second made it a Co-operative Republic. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw Guyana's flag, and under it draw a poppy on one side and a Mashramani costume on the other. Say each part aloud as you place it: the flag for the nation, the poppy for Remembrance Day, the costume for Mashramani.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see the flag flying, hear the anthem, or hear a celebration day named, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
