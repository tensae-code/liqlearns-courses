# History — Kuwait course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Kuwait's own —
built from Kuwait's Social Studies books for Grades 6–9, its
Grade 10 Kuwait History book, its Grade 11 Islamic History
book and its Grade 12 modern-world History books (Arabic).
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grade
each book comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': start from Kuwait, widen
to the Gulf, the Islamic world and the world — method first,
then era by era. That ladder IS the path order (recommended,
never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know the Past, and the First Humans**:

- `first-steps/` — What History Is; From Evidence to a Written History; Fact or Opinion.
- `africa-to-1500/` — From Stone Tools to Farming.

**Level 2 — The Ancient Gulf and the Birth of Islam**:

- `ancient-world/` — Gulf Civilisations: Dilmun, Magan and Failaka; Kuwait: Ubaid and Hellenistic Failaka; Kuwait's Islamic Heritage Sites; Trade Routes That Linked the Old World; Arabia Before Islam; The Rise and Expansion of Islam; The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda; The Rashidun Caliphate: Umar's Conquests; The Rashidun Caliphate: Uthman and Ali.

**Level 3 — Islamic Civilisation: How It Worked, and What Europe Learned**:

- `ancient-world/` — Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features; Islamic Civilisation: Institutions and City Life; Islamic Civilisation and Europe's Awakening.

**Level 4 — Kuwait Is Born: Peoples, the Old Economy and the Sea**:

- `modern-world/` — Gulf Population: Origins and Migrations; Kuwait's Founding: The Utub and Al Sabah; Founding of the Other Gulf States; Kuwait's Old Economy: Herding, Crafts and Trade; Kuwait: Pearl Diving.

**Level 5 — Kuwait and the Gulf in the Modern World**:

- `modern-world/` — Kuwait: History and National Identity; Kuwait's Rulers: Challenges and Achievements; Kuwait: From Emirate to Modern State; European Colonial Rivalry in the Gulf; Kuwait: The 1899 Protection Agreement with Britain; Kuwait and Gulf Independence; Kuwait: Constitution and Political Life; The Gulf Cooperation Council: Founding; The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation: Founding; The United Nations: Founding, and Kuwait Joins; OPEC: Founding and Kuwait.

**Level 6 — The Modern World: Europe Outward, from Renaissance to World Wars**:

- `modern-world/` — The Renaissance: What It Was; The Renaissance: Its Great Figures; Exploration: Causes and Results; Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes; Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan; English and French Voyages of Discovery; European Colonialism: Concept, Factors and Forms; Colonial Rivalry Among European Powers; Effects of European Colonialism; The Industrial Revolution: What Changed, and Why England First; The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail; Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires; The English Revolution; American Independence; The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789; The First World War: Causes; The World Between the Wars, 1919–1939; The Second World War: Course in Europe; The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding.
