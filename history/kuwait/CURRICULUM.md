# History — Kuwait course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Kuwait inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school
grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Kuwait's books in hand run in the
country's own teaching order — Social Studies that starts
from Kuwait and widens term by term (Kuwait and the Gulf,
then the Arab homeland, the Islamic world, and the world),
then secondary History in three arcs (Kuwait itself, Islamic
History, and the modern and contemporary world). The ladder
follows that order and the eras as the books present them:
method first, then the ancient Gulf and the birth of Islam,
Islamic civilisation whole, Kuwait's founding and old
economy, Kuwait and the Gulf in the modern world, and last
the Grade 12 world arc from the Renaissance to the world
wars. Six levels, in the books' own order. The ladder is
a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — Kuwait's own
Ministry of Education History and Social Studies books —
except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are
the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the
acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — First Steps: How We Know the Past, and the First Humans** — the historian's craft first — what history and civilisation are, how a fact travels from a dusty source into written history, and how truth is told from forgery — with humanity's own first steps, from cave bands to farming, as the first story the craft is practised on. 4 lessons, 6 placements: What History Is; From Stone Tools to Farming; From Evidence to a Written History; Fact or Opinion.

2. **Level 2 — The Ancient Gulf and the Birth of Islam** — the Gulf's deep past — Dilmun, Magan and Failaka on the old sea roads, Ubaid potters and Greek settlers on Kuwait's own ground, the trade routes that linked the old world — then Arabia before the Message, the Prophet's life, the Hijra, and the Rashidun caliphs who carried the new state outward. 9 lessons, 14 placements: Gulf Civilisations: Dilmun, Magan and Failaka; Kuwait: Ubaid and Hellenistic Failaka; Kuwait's Islamic Heritage Sites; Trade Routes That Linked the Old World; Arabia Before Islam; The Rise and Expansion of Islam; The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda; The Rashidun Caliphate: Umar's Conquests; The Rashidun Caliphate: Uthman and Ali.

3. **Level 3 — Islamic Civilisation: How It Worked, and What Europe Learned** — the Islamic civilisation studied whole — what it is and the bases it rose on, its state, markets and cities at work, and how Europe's translators carried its learning home into a renaissance. 3 lessons, 7 placements: Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features; Islamic Civilisation: Institutions and City Life; Islamic Civilisation and Europe's Awakening.

4. **Level 4 — Kuwait Is Born: Peoples, the Old Economy and the Sea** — the Gulf's peoples arrive and settle — the migrations that filled the coast, the Utub's journey and the choosing of Al Sabah, the other Gulf states born beside Kuwait — and the old working world that fed them: herding, crafts, ships and the pearl banks. 5 lessons, 10 placements: Gulf Population: Origins and Migrations; Kuwait's Founding: The Utub and Al Sabah; Founding of the Other Gulf States; Kuwait's Old Economy: Herding, Crafts and Trade; Kuwait: Pearl Diving.

5. **Level 5 — Kuwait and the Gulf in the Modern World** — Kuwait's own modern road — its history kept as the guard of its identity, its rulers' march from emirate to modern state, foreign rivalry and the 1899 protection paper, independence in 1961 and the 1962 constitution — and the councils Kuwait helped build: the GCC, the OIC, the United Nations and OPEC. 11 lessons, 14 placements: Kuwait: History and National Identity; Kuwait's Rulers: Challenges and Achievements; Kuwait: From Emirate to Modern State; European Colonial Rivalry in the Gulf; Kuwait: The 1899 Protection Agreement with Britain; Kuwait and Gulf Independence; Kuwait: Constitution and Political Life; The Gulf Cooperation Council: Founding; The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation: Founding; The United Nations: Founding, and Kuwait Joins; OPEC: Founding and Kuwait.

6. **Level 6 — The Modern World: Europe Outward, from Renaissance to World Wars** — the world history of Kuwait's oldest secondary book — Europe's rebirth, the discovery voyages and the colonial empires they hardened into, the factory revolution, the three great revolutions, and the two world wars that remade the world Kuwait lives in. 19 lessons, 21 placements: The Renaissance: What It Was; The Renaissance: Its Great Figures; Exploration: Causes and Results; Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes; Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan; English and French Voyages of Discovery; European Colonialism: Concept, Factors and Forms; Colonial Rivalry Among European Powers; Effects of European Colonialism; The Industrial Revolution: What Changed, and Why England First; The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail; Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires; The English Revolution; American Independence; The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789; The First World War: Causes; The World Between the Wars, 1919–1939; The Second World War: Course in Europe; The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding.

**Totals: 51 lessons, 72 placements stocked** (Level 1: 4 lessons, Level 2: 9, Level 3: 3, Level 4: 5, Level 5: 11, Level 6: 19).
