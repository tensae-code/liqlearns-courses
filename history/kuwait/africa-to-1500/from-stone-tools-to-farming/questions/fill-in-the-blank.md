# From Stone Tools to Farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** First humans hunt and gather in small cave-dwelling bands, then learn farming, settle by water, tame animals and build mud-and-stone houses, inventing the wooden plough in Mesopotamia around 5000 BCE, before industry and the information revolution turn the relationship into a burden on the earth — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** hunting and gathering

**2.** Human beings moved step by step from hunting and gathering in small cave-dwelling bands to farming, settled villages and tamed animals — and each step changed what people did to the earth around them — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** farming
