# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** First humans hunt and gather in small cave-dwelling bands, then learn farming, settle by water, tame animals and build mud-and-stone houses, inventing the wooden plough in Mesopotamia around 5000 BCE, before industry and the information revolution turn the relationship into a burden on the earth.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Human beings moved step by step from hunting and gathering in small cave-dwelling bands to farming, settled villages and tamed animals — and each step changed what people did to the earth around them.
