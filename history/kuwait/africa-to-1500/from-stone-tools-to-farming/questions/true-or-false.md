# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** First humans hunt and gather in small cave-dwelling bands, then learn farming, settle by water, tame animals and build mud-and-stone houses, inventing the wooden plough in Mesopotamia around 5000 BCE, before industry and the information revolution turn the relationship into a burden on the earth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
