# From Stone Tools to Farming — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: first humans hunt and gather in small cave-dwelling bands, then learn farming, settle by water, tame animals and build mud-and-stone houses, inventing the wooden plough in Mesopotamia around 5000 BCE, before industry and the information revolution turn the relationship into a burden on the earth — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
