# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human beings moved step by step from hunting and gathering in small cave-dwelling bands to farming, settled villages and tamed animals — and each step changed what people did to the earth around them.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

First humans hunt and gather in small cave-dwelling bands, then learn farming, settle by water, tame animals and build mud-and-stone houses, inventing the wooden plough in Mesopotamia around 5000 BCE, before industry and the information revolution turn the relationship into a burden on the earth.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
