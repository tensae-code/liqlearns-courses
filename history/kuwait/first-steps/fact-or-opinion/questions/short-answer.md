# Fact or Opinion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** Forged pasts are set beside true ones so learners see how a lie about yesterday bends a whole society's awareness today.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A past can be forged as well as told truly, and telling the difference matters: a lie about yesterday bends a whole society's awareness today.
