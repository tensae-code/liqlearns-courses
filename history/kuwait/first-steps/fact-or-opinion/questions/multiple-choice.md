# Fact or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) forged pasts are set beside true ones so learners see how a lie about yesterday bends a whole society's awareness today  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) forged pasts are set beside true ones so learners see how a lie about yesterday bends a whole society's awareness today** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) a past can be forged as well as told truly, and telling the difference matters: a lie about yesterday bends a whole society's awareness today  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) a past can be forged as well as told truly, and telling the difference matters: a lie about yesterday bends a whole society's awareness today** — That is what this lesson teaches.
