# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) learners ask what makes a civilisation, then see Gulf geography, water and settlement turn scattered villages into ordered towns  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) learners ask what makes a civilisation, then see Gulf geography, water and settlement turn scattered villages into ordered towns** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) learners pin down what history is — not a pile of dates but a science with pillars — and why a nation keeps it  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) learners pin down what history is — not a pile of dates but a science with pillars — and why a nation keeps it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
