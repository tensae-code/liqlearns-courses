# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Learners ask what makes a civilisation, then see Gulf geography, water and settlement turn scattered villages into ordered towns.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Learners pin down what history is — not a pile of dates but a science with pillars — and why a nation keeps it.
