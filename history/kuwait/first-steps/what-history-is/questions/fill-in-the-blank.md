# What History Is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Learners ask what makes a civilisation, then see Gulf geography, water and settlement turn scattered villages into ordered towns — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** history

**2.** Learners pin down what history is — not a pile of dates but a science with pillars — and why a nation keeps it — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** civilisation
