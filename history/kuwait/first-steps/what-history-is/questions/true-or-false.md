# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Learners ask what makes a civilisation, then see Gulf geography, water and settlement turn scattered villages into ordered towns. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
