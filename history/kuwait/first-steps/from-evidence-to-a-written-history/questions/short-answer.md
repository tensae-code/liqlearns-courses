# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The class follows a fact from a dusty source through the research steps until it stands as written history.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The researcher cross-examines an event the way a judge hears witnesses, testing each account before judging what happened.
