# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the class follows a fact from a dusty source through the research steps until it stands as written history  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the class follows a fact from a dusty source through the research steps until it stands as written history** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) the researcher cross-examines an event the way a judge hears witnesses, testing each account before judging what happened  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the researcher cross-examines an event the way a judge hears witnesses, testing each account before judging what happened** — That is what this lesson teaches.
