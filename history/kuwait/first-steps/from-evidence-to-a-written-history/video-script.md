# Video script — From Evidence to a Written History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A written history is built, not found: the researcher gathers sources, follows set research steps, and cross-examines each account of an event the way a judge hears witnesses before judging what happened.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

The class follows a fact from a dusty source through the research steps until it stands as written history.
The researcher cross-examines an event the way a judge hears witnesses, testing each account before judging what happened.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
