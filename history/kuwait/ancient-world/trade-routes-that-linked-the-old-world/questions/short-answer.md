# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** Caravans link the Gulf to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen while Gulf ships carry goods to India, Yemen and East Africa.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The old Gulf traded in both directions at once: caravans linked it overland to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen, while its ships carried goods by sea to India, Yemen and East Africa.
