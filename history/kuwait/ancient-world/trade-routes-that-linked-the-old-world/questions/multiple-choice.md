# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) caravans link the Gulf to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen while Gulf ships carry goods to India, Yemen and East Africa  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) caravans link the Gulf to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen while Gulf ships carry goods to India, Yemen and East Africa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) the old Gulf traded in both directions at once: caravans linked it overland to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen, while its ships carried goods by sea to India, Yemen and East Africa  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the old Gulf traded in both directions at once: caravans linked it overland to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen, while its ships carried goods by sea to India, Yemen and East Africa** — That is what this lesson teaches.
