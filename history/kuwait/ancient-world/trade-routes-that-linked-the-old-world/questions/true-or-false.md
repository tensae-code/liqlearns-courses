# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Caravans link the Gulf to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen while Gulf ships carry goods to India, Yemen and East Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
