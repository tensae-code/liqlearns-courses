# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Caravans link the Gulf to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen while Gulf ships carry goods to India, Yemen and East Africa — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** caravan routes

**2.** The old Gulf traded in both directions at once: caravans linked it overland to Iraq, Egypt, Syria and Yemen, while its ships carried goods by sea to India, Yemen and East Africa — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** sea trade
