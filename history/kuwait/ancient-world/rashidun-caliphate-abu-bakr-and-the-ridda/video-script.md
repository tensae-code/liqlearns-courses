# Video script — The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When the Prophet died, the community nearly split at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; when tribes then apostatised and withheld zakat in the Ridda, Abu Bakr's wars pulled Arabia back into one state that began to expand.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

The Prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the Ridda, and Abu Bakr's wars pull Arabia back into one state that begins to expand.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
