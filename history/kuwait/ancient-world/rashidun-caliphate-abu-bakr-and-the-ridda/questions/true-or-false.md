# The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the Ridda, and Abu Bakr's wars pull Arabia back into one state that begins to expand.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
