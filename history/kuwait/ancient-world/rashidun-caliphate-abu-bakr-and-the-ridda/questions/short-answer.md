# The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** The Prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the Ridda, and Abu Bakr's wars pull Arabia back into one state that begins to expand.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** When the Prophet died, the community nearly split at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; when tribes then apostatised and withheld zakat in the Ridda, Abu Bakr's wars pulled Arabia back into one state that began to expand.
