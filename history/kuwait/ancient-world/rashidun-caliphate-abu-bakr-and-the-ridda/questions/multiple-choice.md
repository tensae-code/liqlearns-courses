# The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the Prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the Ridda, and Abu Bakr's wars pull Arabia back into one state that begins to expand  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the Prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the Ridda, and Abu Bakr's wars pull Arabia back into one state that begins to expand** — That is what this lesson teaches.
