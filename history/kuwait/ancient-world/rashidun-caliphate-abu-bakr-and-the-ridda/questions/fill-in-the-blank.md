# The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The Prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the Ridda, and Abu Bakr's wars pull Arabia back into one state that begins to expand — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Saqifa

**2.** When the Prophet died, the community nearly split at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; when tribes then apostatised and withheld zakat in the Ridda, Abu Bakr's wars pulled Arabia back into one state that began to expand — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Ridda
