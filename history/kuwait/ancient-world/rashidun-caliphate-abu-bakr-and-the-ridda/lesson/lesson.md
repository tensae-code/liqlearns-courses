# The Rashidun Caliphate: Abu Bakr and the Ridda

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the rashidun caliphate: abu bakr and the ridda. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: when the Prophet died, the community nearly split at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; when tribes then apostatised and withheld zakat in the Ridda, Abu Bakr's wars pulled Arabia back into one state that began to expand.

The Prophet's death is the young state's first crisis. At the Saqifa, the community debates its future and nearly splits — then pledges Abu Bakr as caliph. The crisis deepens: across Arabia, tribes apostatise and withhold zakat, the Ridda. Abu Bakr refuses to let the state dissolve. His wars pull Arabia back into one state — and the reunited state, its energies gathered, begins to expand beyond the Peninsula.
