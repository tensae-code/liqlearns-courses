# Gulf Civilisations: Dilmun, Magan and Failaka — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the Gulf sits between the old world's continents, so Dilmun in Bahrain ships copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farms palms, Hili buries its dead in round stone tombs and Magan smelts copper into swords — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
