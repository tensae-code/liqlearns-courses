# Video script — Gulf Civilisations: Dilmun, Magan and Failaka

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Gulf's position between the old world's lands made it a working sea from the earliest times: Dilmun shipped copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farmed palms, Hili raised round stone tombs, and Magan smelted copper into swords.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

The Gulf sits between the old world's continents, so Dilmun in Bahrain ships copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farms palms, Hili buries its dead in round stone tombs and Magan smelts copper into swords.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
