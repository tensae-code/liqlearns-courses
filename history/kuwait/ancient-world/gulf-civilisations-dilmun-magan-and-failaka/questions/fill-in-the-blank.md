# Gulf Civilisations: Dilmun, Magan and Failaka — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The Gulf sits between the old world's continents, so Dilmun in Bahrain ships copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farms palms, Hili buries its dead in round stone tombs and Magan smelts copper into swords — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Dilmun

**2.** The Gulf's position between the old world's lands made it a working sea from the earliest times: Dilmun shipped copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farmed palms, Hili raised round stone tombs, and Magan smelted copper into swords — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Magan
