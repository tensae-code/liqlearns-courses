# Gulf Civilisations: Dilmun, Magan and Failaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** The Gulf sits between the old world's continents, so Dilmun in Bahrain ships copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farms palms, Hili buries its dead in round stone tombs and Magan smelts copper into swords.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Gulf's position between the old world's lands made it a working sea from the earliest times: Dilmun shipped copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farmed palms, Hili raised round stone tombs, and Magan smelted copper into swords.
