# Gulf Civilisations: Dilmun, Magan and Failaka — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Gulf sits between the old world's continents, so Dilmun in Bahrain ships copper, wood, dates and pearls, Tarut farms palms, Hili buries its dead in round stone tombs and Magan smelts copper into swords.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
