# Kuwait: Ubaid and Hellenistic Failaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Potters and grain-grinders settle at Subiya in Ubaid times (5500–4500 BCE), then Greeks settle well-watered Failaka, call it Ikaros, and leave coins and Dilmun seals behind.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Before any state, Kuwait's land gathers civilisations on its coast and islands, the deep roots the modern country claims as its own.
