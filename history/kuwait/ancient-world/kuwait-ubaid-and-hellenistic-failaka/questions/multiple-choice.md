# Kuwait: Ubaid and Hellenistic Failaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) potters and grain-grinders settle at Subiya in Ubaid times (5500–4500 BCE), then Greeks settle well-watered Failaka, call it Ikaros, and leave coins and Dilmun seals behind  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
