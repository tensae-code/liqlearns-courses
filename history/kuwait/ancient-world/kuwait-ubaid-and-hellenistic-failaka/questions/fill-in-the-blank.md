# Kuwait: Ubaid and Hellenistic Failaka — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Potters and grain-grinders settle at Subiya in Ubaid times (5500–4500 BCE), then Greeks settle well-watered Failaka, call it Ikaros, and leave coins and Dilmun seals behind — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ubaid

**2.** Before any state, Kuwait's land gathers civilisations on its coast and islands, the deep roots the modern country claims as its own — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Failaka
