# Kuwait: Ubaid and Hellenistic Failaka — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Potters and grain-grinders settle at Subiya in Ubaid times (5500–4500 BCE), then Greeks settle well-watered Failaka, call it Ikaros, and leave coins and Dilmun seals behind. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
