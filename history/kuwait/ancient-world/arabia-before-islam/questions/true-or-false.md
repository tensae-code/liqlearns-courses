# Arabia Before Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Peninsula is mapped first, then its peoples are taken apart piece by piece — scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world the Message is about to enter.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
