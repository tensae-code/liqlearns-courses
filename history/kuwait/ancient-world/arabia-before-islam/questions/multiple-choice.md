# Arabia Before Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the Peninsula is mapped first, then its peoples are taken apart piece by piece — scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world the Message is about to enter  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the Peninsula is mapped first, then its peoples are taken apart piece by piece — scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world the Message is about to enter** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) before the Message, Arabia was a land of scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, its own customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world Islam was about to enter  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) before the Message, Arabia was a land of scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, its own customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world Islam was about to enter** — That is what this lesson teaches.
