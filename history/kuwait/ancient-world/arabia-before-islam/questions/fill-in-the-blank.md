# Arabia Before Islam — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The Peninsula is mapped first, then its peoples are taken apart piece by piece — scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world the Message is about to enter — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Peninsula

**2.** Before the Message, Arabia was a land of scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, its own customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world Islam was about to enter — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** tribal life
