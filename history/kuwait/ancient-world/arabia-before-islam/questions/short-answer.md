# Arabia Before Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** The Peninsula is mapped first, then its peoples are taken apart piece by piece — scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world the Message is about to enter.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Before the Message, Arabia was a land of scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, its own customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world Islam was about to enter.
