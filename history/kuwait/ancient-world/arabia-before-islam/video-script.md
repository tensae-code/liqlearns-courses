# Video script — Arabia Before Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Before the Message, Arabia was a land of scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, its own customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world Islam was about to enter.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

The Peninsula is mapped first, then its peoples are taken apart piece by piece — scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world the Message is about to enter.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
