# Arabia Before Islam

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — arabia before islam. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: before the Message, Arabia was a land of scattered political systems, trade and herding economies, tribal social life, its own customs, many religions and a thin intellectual life — the world Islam was about to enter.

Islamic History begins by mapping the stage. First the Peninsula itself — its lands and coasts. Then its peoples, taken apart piece by piece: political systems scattered and divided; economies of trade and herding; a social life organised by tribe; customs of the age; religions many and varied; and an intellectual life the book calls thin. This is the world as it stood on the eve of the Message — the Arabia that Islam was about to enter and remake.
