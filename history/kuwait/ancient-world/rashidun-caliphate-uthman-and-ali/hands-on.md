# Hands-on — The Rashidun Caliphate: Uthman and Ali

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud, in your own words: expansion continued under Uthman, the third caliph, and the age closes with Ali, the fourth caliph — the end of the Rashidun age. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson (a picture of the line of the four Rashidun caliphs drawn as four lamps in a row, the last one Ali's), and say each part aloud as you draw it: who is there, where it happens, and what turned.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
