# The Rashidun Caliphate: Uthman and Ali

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the rashidun caliphate: uthman and ali. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: expansion continued under Uthman, the third caliph, and the age closes with Ali, the fourth caliph — the end of the Rashidun age.

The expansion does not stop with Umar. Under Uthman ibn Affan, the third caliph, it continues — the state still growing on the momentum of the conquests. Then the book closes its Rashidun account with Ali ibn Abi Talib, the fourth caliph. Four caliphs, one age: the Rashidun age ends here, and the Islamic story passes to the dynasties that follow.
