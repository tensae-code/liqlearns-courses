# The Rashidun Caliphate: Uthman and Ali — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) expansion continues under Uthman, and the term closes with Ali, the fourth caliph, as the Rashidun age ends  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) expansion continues under Uthman, and the term closes with Ali, the fourth caliph, as the Rashidun age ends** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) expansion continued under Uthman, the third caliph, and the age closes with Ali, the fourth caliph — the end of the Rashidun age  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) expansion continued under Uthman, the third caliph, and the age closes with Ali, the fourth caliph — the end of the Rashidun age** — That is what this lesson teaches.
