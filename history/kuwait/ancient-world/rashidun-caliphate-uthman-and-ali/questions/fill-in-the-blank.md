# The Rashidun Caliphate: Uthman and Ali — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Expansion continues under Uthman, and the term closes with Ali, the fourth caliph, as the Rashidun age ends — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Uthman ibn Affan

**2.** Expansion continued under Uthman, the third caliph, and the age closes with Ali, the fourth caliph — the end of the Rashidun age — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Ali ibn Abi Talib
