# Video script — Islamic Civilisation: Institutions and City Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Islamic civilisation worked as a living order: a state of caliphate, ministry, diwans, provinces and police; an economy of markets, crafts, farming and trade; and cities rising in planned stone around their mosques and streets.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

Caliphate, ministry, diwans, provinces and police take shape as a working state, with Ali ibn Abi Talib's police chief, the sahib al-shurta, keeping the streets.
Markets, crafts, farming and trade fill the Islamic world's purse, the wealth that pays for its cities and schools.
The money story runs on into streets, mosques and houses as Islamic cities rise in planned stone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
