# Islamic Civilisation: Institutions and City Life — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Caliphate, ministry, diwans, provinces and police take shape as a working state, with Ali ibn Abi Talib's police chief, the sahib al-shurta, keeping the streets — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** diwans

**2.** Markets, crafts, farming and trade fill the Islamic world's purse, the wealth that pays for its cities and schools — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
