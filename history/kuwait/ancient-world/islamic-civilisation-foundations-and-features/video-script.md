# Video script — Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Islamic civilisation joins faith, knowledge and building into one whole: it is defined by its values, ethics, inventions and works, with a material side and a spiritual side, and its marks can be spotted in any city, book or building it left behind.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

Learners weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue why the Islamic one, joining faith, knowledge and building, stands first for them.
Learners rebuild the definition from its pieces — values, ethics, inventions and works — and sort its material and spiritual sides.
The civilisation's marks are drawn out one by one so learners can spot them in any city, book or building it left behind.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
