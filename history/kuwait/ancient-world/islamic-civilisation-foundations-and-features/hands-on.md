# Hands-on — Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud, in your own words: Islamic civilisation joins faith, knowledge and building into one whole: it is defined by its values, ethics, inventions and works, with a material side and a spiritual side, and its marks can be spotted in any city, book or building it left behind. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson (a picture of an Islamic city in one view — mosque, library, market and carved doorway together), and say each part aloud as you draw it: who is there, where it happens, and what turned.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
