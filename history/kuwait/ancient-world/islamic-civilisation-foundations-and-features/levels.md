# Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1 Lesson 1: Islamic civilisation as the most important civilisation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue why the Islamic one, joining faith, knowledge and building, stands first for them — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: learners rebuild the definition from its pieces — values, ethics, inventions and works — and sort its material and spiritual sides — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 1 Lesson 4: Characteristics of Islamic civilisation
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the civilisation's marks are drawn out one by one so learners can spot them in any city, book or building it left behind — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
