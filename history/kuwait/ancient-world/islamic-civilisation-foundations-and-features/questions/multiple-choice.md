# Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) learners weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue why the Islamic one, joining faith, knowledge and building, stands first for them  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) learners weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue why the Islamic one, joining faith, knowledge and building, stands first for them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) learners rebuild the definition from its pieces — values, ethics, inventions and works — and sort its material and spiritual sides  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) learners rebuild the definition from its pieces — values, ethics, inventions and works — and sort its material and spiritual sides** — That is what this lesson teaches.
