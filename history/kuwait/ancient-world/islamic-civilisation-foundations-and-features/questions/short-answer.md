# Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Learners weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue why the Islamic one, joining faith, knowledge and building, stands first for them.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Learners rebuild the definition from its pieces — values, ethics, inventions and works — and sort its material and spiritual sides.
