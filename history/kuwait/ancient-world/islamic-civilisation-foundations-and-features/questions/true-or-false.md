# Islamic Civilisation: Foundations and Features — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Learners weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue why the Islamic one, joining faith, knowledge and building, stands first for them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
