# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Islam rose in Arabia in one lifetime — the call in Makkah, the Hijra to Madinah where a state was founded, the campaigns that defended it and united Arabia — and within the first Islamic century it had reached the Gulf and far beyond, carried on by the caliphates.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

Islam reaches the Gulf in the first Islamic century and leaves mosques and sites from Bahrain's Khamis Mosque to Suhar in Oman that learners sort by country.
The call begins in Makkah with Muhammad, spreads across Arabia by the Farewell Sermon in year 10 of the Hijra, reaches the Sasanian and Byzantine frontiers, and the caliphates carry it on from Madinah and Kufa to Damascus, Baghdad and the Ottoman capital.
Muhammad is born into Quraysh, travels to Syria with his uncle Abu Talib at about twelve, joins the Fijar war at fifteen, marries Khadija at twenty-five in 595, settles the Black Stone quarrel when the Kaaba is rebuilt in 605, receives the revelation, and calls people in secret, then among his kin, then openly while Quraysh turns against him.
The Prophet migrates to Yathrib, builds the mosque, reconciles Aws and Khazraj, binds Muhajirun and Ansar as brothers and sets down the Sahifa charter — a state exists.
Nakhla, Badr in year 2, Uhud in year 3, the Trench in year 5 and Hudaybiyya's truce in year 6 defend the young state, then Makkah is opened in year 8 and the Farewell Pilgrimage in year 10 seals a united Arabia.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
