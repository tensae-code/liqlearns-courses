# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islam reaches the Gulf in the first Islamic century and leaves mosques and sites from Bahrain's Khamis Mosque to Suhar in Oman that learners sort by country. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
