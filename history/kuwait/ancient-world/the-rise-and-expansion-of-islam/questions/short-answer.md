# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Islam reaches the Gulf in the first Islamic century and leaves mosques and sites from Bahrain's Khamis Mosque to Suhar in Oman that learners sort by country.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The call begins in Makkah with Muhammad, spreads across Arabia by the Farewell Sermon in year 10 of the Hijra, reaches the Sasanian and Byzantine frontiers, and the caliphates carry it on from Madinah and Kufa to Damascus, Baghdad and the Ottoman capital.
