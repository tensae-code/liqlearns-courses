# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Islam reaches the Gulf in the first Islamic century and leaves mosques and sites from Bahrain's Khamis Mosque to Suhar in Oman that learners sort by country — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Hijra

**2.** The call begins in Makkah with Muhammad, spreads across Arabia by the Farewell Sermon in year 10 of the Hijra, reaches the Sasanian and Byzantine frontiers, and the caliphates carry it on from Madinah and Kufa to Damascus, Baghdad and the Ottoman capital — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the caliphates
