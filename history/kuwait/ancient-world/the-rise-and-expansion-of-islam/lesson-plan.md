# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Kuwait · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient Gulf and the Birth of Islam (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Islam rose in Arabia in one lifetime — the call in Makkah, the Hijra to Madinah where a state was founded, the campaigns that defended it and united Arabia — and within the first Islamic century it had reached the Gulf and far beyond, carried on by the caliphates.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient Gulf and the Birth of Islam (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2 Lesson 4: Islamic civilisation in the Gulf states region** — Islam reaches the Gulf in the first Islamic century and leaves mosques and sites from Bahrain's Khamis Mosque to Suhar in Oman that learners sort by country.
2. **Unit 1 Lesson 2: A sketch of the birth of the Islamic state and its civilisation** — The call begins in Makkah with Muhammad, spreads across Arabia by the Farewell Sermon in year 10 of the Hijra, reaches the Sasanian and Byzantine frontiers, and the caliphates carry it on from Madinah and Kufa to Damascus, Baghdad and the Ottoman capital.
3. **Chapter 1: The Peninsula, cradle of the Message — the Prophet's birth, life and call** — Muhammad is born into Quraysh, travels to Syria with his uncle Abu Talib at about twelve, joins the Fijar war at fifteen, marries Khadija at twenty-five in 595, settles the Black Stone quarrel when the Kaaba is rebuilt in 605, receives the revelation, and calls people in secret, then among his kin, then openly while Quraysh turns against him.
4. **Chapter 1: The Hijra and founding the Islamic state in Madinah** — The Prophet migrates to Yathrib, builds the mosque, reconciles Aws and Khazraj, binds Muhajirun and Ansar as brothers and sets down the Sahifa charter — a state exists.
5. **Chapter 1: The campaigns and the unification of Arabia** — Nakhla, Badr in year 2, Uhud in year 3, the Trench in year 5 and Hudaybiyya's truce in year 6 defend the young state, then Makkah is opened in year 8 and the Farewell Pilgrimage in year 10 seals a united Arabia.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. 2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — or, for this course's lessons, from the Kuwait story itself as the books tell it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Kuwait's books themselves work, starting from Kuwait and widening to the Gulf and the world.
