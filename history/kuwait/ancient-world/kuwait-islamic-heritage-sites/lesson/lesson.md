# Kuwait's Islamic Heritage Sites

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — kuwait's islamic heritage sites. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait itself, and the state guards them: the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah was founded to collect and protect Islamic heritage.

The Islamic age is not only in other countries' soil — it survives inside Kuwait itself, in Islamic-era sites across the land. And Kuwait answers its own past with care: the state founded the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah, the House of Islamic Antiquities, to collect and guard that heritage — objects gathered, kept and shown, so that what the centuries left behind is not lost. A civilisation is known by what it keeps.
