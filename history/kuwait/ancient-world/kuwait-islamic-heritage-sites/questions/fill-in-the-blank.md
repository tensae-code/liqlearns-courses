# Kuwait's Islamic Heritage Sites — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait, and the state answers by founding the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah to collect and guard Islamic heritage — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Islamic heritage sites

**2.** Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait itself, and the state guards them: the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah was founded to collect and protect Islamic heritage — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah
