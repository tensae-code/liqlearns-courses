# Kuwait's Islamic Heritage Sites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait, and the state answers by founding the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah to collect and guard Islamic heritage  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait, and the state answers by founding the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah to collect and guard Islamic heritage** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait itself, and the state guards them: the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah was founded to collect and protect Islamic heritage  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait itself, and the state guards them: the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah was founded to collect and protect Islamic heritage** — That is what this lesson teaches.
