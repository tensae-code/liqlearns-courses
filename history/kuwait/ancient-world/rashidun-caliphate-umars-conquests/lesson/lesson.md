# The Rashidun Caliphate: Umar's Conquests

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the rashidun caliphate: umar's conquests. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: under Umar ibn al-Khattab the Islamic state burst outward: the eastern conquests rolled over Persia's world, and Syria was taken from Byzantium.

Under the second caliph, Umar ibn al-Khattab, the state bursts outward. To the east, the conquests roll over the Sasanian Persian world, an empire of centuries falling within years. To the north, Syria is taken from Byzantium. The book keeps this chapter short and fast — the point is the scale: in one caliphate, the Islamic state goes from Arabia to a world power.
