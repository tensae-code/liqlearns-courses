# Islamic Civilisation and Europe's Awakening

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — islamic civilisation and europe's awakening. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: Europe woke by borrowing: its translators travelled to the Islamic world's shining centres, carried home books such as al-Idrisi's geography with its round-earth world map, and Europe's renaissance began from what it learned.

The lesson ends with a debt. While Islamic cities shone as centres of learning, Europe sent its translators to them. The translators carried home the books — among them al-Idrisi's geography, with its map of a round earth — and translated them for Europe's own scholars. What Europe learned helped wake it into its renaissance. The book teaches the flow honestly: learning travelled, and one civilisation's shining lit another's morning.
