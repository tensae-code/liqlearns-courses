# Islamic Civilisation and Europe's Awakening — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europe sends translators to the Islamic world's shining centres, carries home books like al-Idrisi's geography with its round-earth world map, and wakes into its renaissance.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
