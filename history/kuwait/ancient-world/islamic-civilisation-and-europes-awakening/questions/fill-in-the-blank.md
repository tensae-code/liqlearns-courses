# Islamic Civilisation and Europe's Awakening — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Europe sends translators to the Islamic world's shining centres, carries home books like al-Idrisi's geography with its round-earth world map, and wakes into its renaissance — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** al-Idrisi

**2.** Europe woke by borrowing: its translators travelled to the Islamic world's shining centres, carried home books such as al-Idrisi's geography with its round-earth world map, and Europe's renaissance began from what it learned — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** translators
