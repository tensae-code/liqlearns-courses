# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Britain's American colonies rebel against taxes and rule from across the ocean and fight their way into a new republic  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) Britain's American colonies rebel against taxes and rule from across the ocean and fight their way into a new republic** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) Britain's American colonies rebelled against taxes and rule from across the ocean, and fought their way into a new republic  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Britain's American colonies rebelled against taxes and rule from across the ocean, and fought their way into a new republic** — That is what this lesson teaches.
