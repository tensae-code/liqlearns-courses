# American Independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Britain's American colonies rebel against taxes and rule from across the ocean and fight their way into a new republic — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the American Revolution

**2.** Britain's American colonies rebelled against taxes and rule from across the ocean, and fought their way into a new republic — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the new republic
