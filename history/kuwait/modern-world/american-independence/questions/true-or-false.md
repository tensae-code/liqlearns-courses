# American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain's American colonies rebel against taxes and rule from across the ocean and fight their way into a new republic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
