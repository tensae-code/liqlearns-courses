# American Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — american independence. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: Britain's American colonies rebelled against taxes and rule from across the ocean, and fought their way into a new republic.

Across the ocean, Britain's own colonies took up the question. Taxed from London and ruled from across the Atlantic, the American colonies rebelled — and fought their way, war and all, into independence. What they built was new: a republic, ruled without a king. The American Revolution carries England's question to its boldest answer yet.
