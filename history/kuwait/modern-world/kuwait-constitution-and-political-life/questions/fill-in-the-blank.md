# Kuwait: Constitution and Political Life — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The Father of the Constitution, Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem, proclaims the 1962 constitution, and learners meet sovereignty, democracy, human rights and Kuwaiti women's political rights — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the 1962 constitution

**2.** The Father of the Constitution, Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem, proclaimed the 1962 constitution, and with it learners meet sovereignty, democracy, human rights and Kuwaiti women's political rights — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** sovereignty
