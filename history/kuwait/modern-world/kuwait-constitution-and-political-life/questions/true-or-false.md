# Kuwait: Constitution and Political Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Father of the Constitution, Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem, proclaims the 1962 constitution, and learners meet sovereignty, democracy, human rights and Kuwaiti women's political rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
