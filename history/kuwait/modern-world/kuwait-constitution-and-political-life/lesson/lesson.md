# Kuwait: Constitution and Political Life

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — kuwait: constitution and political life. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the Father of the Constitution, Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem, proclaimed the 1962 constitution, and with it learners meet sovereignty, democracy, human rights and Kuwaiti women's political rights.

Independence gave Kuwait its freedom; the next year gave it its rules. In 1962 Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — now remembered also as the Father of the Constitution — proclaimed Kuwait's constitution. The book walks learners through the ideas inside it: sovereignty, the people's own right to rule themselves; democracy; human rights; and Kuwaiti women's political rights. A constitution is a country's promise to itself, written down — 1962 is when Kuwait wrote its promise.
