# The United Nations: Founding, and Kuwait Joins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Second World War ends in 1945, the world judges the League of Nations (1920) a failure, and builds the UN instead; Kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raises Kuwait's flag at the organisation. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
