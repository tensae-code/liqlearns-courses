# The United Nations: Founding, and Kuwait Joins — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The Second World War ends in 1945, the world judges the League of Nations (1920) a failure, and builds the UN instead; Kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raises Kuwait's flag at the organisation — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the United Nations

**2.** Out of the second war's ruin the victors build the United Nations to keep the peace their League had failed to keep — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the League of Nations
