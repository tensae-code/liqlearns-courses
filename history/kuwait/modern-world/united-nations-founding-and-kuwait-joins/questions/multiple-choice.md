# The United Nations: Founding, and Kuwait Joins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the Second World War ends in 1945, the world judges the League of Nations (1920) a failure, and builds the UN instead; Kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raises Kuwait's flag at the organisation  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the Second World War ends in 1945, the world judges the League of Nations (1920) a failure, and builds the UN instead; Kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raises Kuwait's flag at the organisation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) out of the second war's ruin the victors build the United Nations to keep the peace their League had failed to keep  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) out of the second war's ruin the victors build the United Nations to keep the peace their League had failed to keep** — That is what this lesson teaches.
