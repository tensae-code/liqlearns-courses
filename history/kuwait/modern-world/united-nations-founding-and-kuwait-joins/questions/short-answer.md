# The United Nations: Founding, and Kuwait Joins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Second World War ends in 1945, the world judges the League of Nations (1920) a failure, and builds the UN instead; Kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raises Kuwait's flag at the organisation.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Out of the second war's ruin the victors build the United Nations to keep the peace their League had failed to keep.
