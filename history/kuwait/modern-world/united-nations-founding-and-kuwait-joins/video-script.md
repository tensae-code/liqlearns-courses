# Video script — The United Nations: Founding, and Kuwait Joins

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Out of the Second World War's ruin, the world judged the League of Nations (1920) a failure and built the United Nations in 1945 instead; Kuwait joined in 1963, and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raised Kuwait's flag at the organisation.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

The Second World War ends in 1945, the world judges the League of Nations (1920) a failure, and builds the UN instead; Kuwait joins in 1963 and its foreign minister Sheikh Sabah Al Ahmad Al Jaber Al Sabah raises Kuwait's flag at the organisation.
Out of the second war's ruin the victors build the United Nations to keep the peace their League had failed to keep.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
