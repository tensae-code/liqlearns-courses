# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The post-1945 world takes its shape — two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order — the world the learners themselves live in.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
