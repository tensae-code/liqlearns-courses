# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The post-1945 world takes its shape — two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order — the world the learners themselves live in — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** two camps

**2.** The post-1945 world took the shape learners still live in: two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the rebuilt order
