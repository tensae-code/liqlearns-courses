# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the post-1945 world takes its shape — two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order — the world the learners themselves live in  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the post-1945 world takes its shape — two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order — the world the learners themselves live in** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) the post-1945 world took the shape learners still live in: two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the post-1945 world took the shape learners still live in: two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order** — That is what this lesson teaches.
