# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the world after 1945: losses, division and rebuilding. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the post-1945 world took the shape learners still live in: two camps, new organisations and a rebuilt order.

The course's final chapter is the present. After 1945 the world took the shape the learners themselves live in: two camps facing each other, new organisations built to keep the peace, and a rebuilt order rising from the ruins. The book calls them the features of our contemporary world — a history book ending, honestly, at the reader's own doorstep.
