# Kuwait's Rulers: Challenges and Achievements — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** From Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah the rulers hold the country's independence against pressure, and Mubarak Al Sabah opens the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Mubarak Al Sabah

**2.** Ruler by ruler, Kuwait's leaders held the country's independence against pressure — from Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah — until Mubarak Al Sabah opened the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Ahmad Al Jaber
