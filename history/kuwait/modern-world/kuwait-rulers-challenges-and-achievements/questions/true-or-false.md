# Kuwait's Rulers: Challenges and Achievements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah the rulers hold the country's independence against pressure, and Mubarak Al Sabah opens the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
