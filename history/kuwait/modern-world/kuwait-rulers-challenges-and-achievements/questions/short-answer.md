# Kuwait's Rulers: Challenges and Achievements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** From Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah the rulers hold the country's independence against pressure, and Mubarak Al Sabah opens the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Ruler by ruler, Kuwait's leaders held the country's independence against pressure — from Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah — until Mubarak Al Sabah opened the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler.
