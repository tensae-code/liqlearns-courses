# Kuwait's Rulers: Challenges and Achievements

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — kuwait's rulers: challenges and achievements. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: ruler by ruler, Kuwait's leaders held the country's independence against pressure — from Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah — until Mubarak Al Sabah opened the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler.

The book marches Kuwait's rulers past one by one, as a march of challenges met and achievements made. From Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah, the rulers' first task was holding the country's independence against the pressures around it. Then Mubarak Al Sabah opened a new work: the building of the modern state. The march runs down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler — each reign a verse in one long argument that Kuwait endured because its rulers held it.
