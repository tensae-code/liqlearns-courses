# Video script — Kuwait's Rulers: Challenges and Achievements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ruler by ruler, Kuwait's leaders held the country's independence against pressure — from Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah — until Mubarak Al Sabah opened the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

From Abdullah II through Muhammad bin Sabah the rulers hold the country's independence against pressure, and Mubarak Al Sabah opens the building of the modern state, down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
