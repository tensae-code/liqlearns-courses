# English and French Voyages of Discovery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) England and France follow late into the race, planting flags and claims across North America and the seas Portugal and Spain opened  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) England and France follow late into the race, planting flags and claims across North America and the seas Portugal and Spain opened** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) the race for new lands ends at the earth's frozen ends, the last blank spaces on the map  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the race for new lands ends at the earth's frozen ends, the last blank spaces on the map** — That is what this lesson teaches.
