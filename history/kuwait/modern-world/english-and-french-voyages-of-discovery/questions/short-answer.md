# English and French Voyages of Discovery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** England and France follow late into the race, planting flags and claims across North America and the seas Portugal and Spain opened.

**Answer:** The race for new lands ends at the earth's frozen ends, the last blank spaces on the map.
