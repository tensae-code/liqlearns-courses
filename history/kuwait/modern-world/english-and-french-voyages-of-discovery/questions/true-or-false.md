# English and French Voyages of Discovery — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** England and France follow late into the race, planting flags and claims across North America and the seas Portugal and Spain opened. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
