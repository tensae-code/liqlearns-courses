# English and French Voyages of Discovery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** England and France follow late into the race, planting flags and claims across North America and the seas Portugal and Spain opened — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the latecomers

**2.** The race for new lands ends at the earth's frozen ends, the last blank spaces on the map — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Poles
