# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed, and Why England First

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the industrial revolution: what changed, and why england first. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: making things passed from cottages to factories — and it happened in England first because coal, capital, colonies and hands gathered there before anywhere else.

Why England, and why then? The book lists the gathered factors: coal under its hills for power; capital in its banks for machines; colonies for materials and markets; and hands — workers for the mills. Where the four gathered, the change came: making things passed out of cottages and into factories, and work itself was reorganised around the machine. The Industrial Revolution begins as a definition, and lands as a list of causes a learner can test.
