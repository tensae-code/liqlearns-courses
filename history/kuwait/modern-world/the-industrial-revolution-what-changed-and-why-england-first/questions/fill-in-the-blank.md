# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed, and Why England First — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Coal, capital, colonies and hands gather in England first, and making things passes from cottages to factories — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Industrial Revolution

**2.** Making things passed from cottages to factories — and it happened in England first because coal, capital, colonies and hands gathered there before anywhere else — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** why England first
