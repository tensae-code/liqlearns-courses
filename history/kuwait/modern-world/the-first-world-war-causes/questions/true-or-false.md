# The First World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Alliances, arms races and colonial rivalry load the gun, an assassination fires it, and Europe's empires bleed for four years.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
