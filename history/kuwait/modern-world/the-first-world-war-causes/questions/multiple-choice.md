# The First World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) alliances, arms races and colonial rivalry load the gun, an assassination fires it, and Europe's empires bleed for four years  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) alliances, arms races and colonial rivalry load the gun, an assassination fires it, and Europe's empires bleed for four years** — That is what this lesson teaches.
