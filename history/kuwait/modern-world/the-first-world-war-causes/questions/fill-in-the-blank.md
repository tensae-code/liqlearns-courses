# The First World War: Causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Alliances, arms races and colonial rivalry load the gun, an assassination fires it, and Europe's empires bleed for four years — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** alliances

**2.** Alliances, arms races and colonial rivalry loaded the gun of 1914; an assassination fired it, and Europe's empires bled for four years — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the assassination
