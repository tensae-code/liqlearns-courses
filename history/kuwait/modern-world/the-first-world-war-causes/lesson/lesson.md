# The First World War: Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the first world war: causes. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: alliances, arms races and colonial rivalry loaded the gun of 1914; an assassination fired it, and Europe's empires bled for four years.

The war chapters begin with the loading of a gun. Alliances divided Europe into armed camps; arms races stocked them; colonial rivalry — the book's own earlier chapter — embittered them. Then an assassination fired the loaded gun. The First World War, 1914–1918, bled Europe's empires for four years. Causes first, the book insists: a war this size is never an accident.
