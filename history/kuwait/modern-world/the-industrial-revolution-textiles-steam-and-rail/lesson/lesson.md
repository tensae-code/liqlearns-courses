# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the industrial revolution: textiles, steam and rail. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: spinning machines, steam power and railways remade work, towns and travel within a single generation.

The revolution's face is three inventions deep. Spinning machines multiplied the thread one worker could make. Steam power drove the machines — and then escaped the factory onto the railway, where locomotives remade travel itself. Within a single generation, work, towns and journeys all looked different. Manifestations, the book calls them: the revolution made visible, in thread, steam and rail.
