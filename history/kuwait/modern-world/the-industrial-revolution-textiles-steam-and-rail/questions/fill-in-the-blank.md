# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Spinning machines, steam power and railways remake work, towns and travel within a generation — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** steam power

**2.** Spinning machines, steam power and railways remade work, towns and travel within a single generation — the key name for this is ____.
