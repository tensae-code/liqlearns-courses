# The Second World War: Course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The unfinished business of 1918 explodes into a wider, deadlier war that redraws the whole world by 1945.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
