# The Second World War: Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the unfinished business of 1918 explodes into a wider, deadlier war that redraws the whole world by 1945  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the unfinished business of 1918 explodes into a wider, deadlier war that redraws the whole world by 1945** — That is what this lesson teaches.
