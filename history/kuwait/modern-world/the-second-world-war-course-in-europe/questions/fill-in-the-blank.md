# The Second World War: Course in Europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The unfinished business of 1918 explodes into a wider, deadlier war that redraws the whole world by 1945 — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the unfinished business of 1918

**2.** The unfinished business of 1918 exploded in 1939 into a wider, deadlier war that redrew the whole world by 1945 — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** 1939–1945
