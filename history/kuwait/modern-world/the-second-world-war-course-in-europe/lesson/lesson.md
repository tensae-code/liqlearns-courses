# The Second World War: Course in Europe

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the second world war: course in europe. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the unfinished business of 1918 exploded in 1939 into a wider, deadlier war that redrew the whole world by 1945.

In 1939 the unfinished business of 1918 exploded. The Second World War was wider and deadlier than the first — fought across Europe and far beyond it. By its end in 1945, the whole world's map and order stood redrawn. The book teaches its course in Europe; the results belong to everyone, Kuwait's learners included — the world of 1945 is the world they were born into.
