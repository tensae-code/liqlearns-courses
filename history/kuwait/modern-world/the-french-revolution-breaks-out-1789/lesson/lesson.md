# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the french revolution breaks out, 1789. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: a bankrupt king summoned the Estates, the Third Estate rose, and France overturned its old order in 1789.

France's old order fell in a single year. The king, bankrupt, summoned the Estates to raise money — and opened a door he could not close. The Third Estate, the common people of France, rose and claimed to speak for the nation. In 1789 France overturned its old order entirely. Of the three revolutions in this chapter, the French is the earthquake: the one that shook every throne in Europe.
