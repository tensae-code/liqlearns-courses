# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A bankrupt king summons the Estates, the Third Estate rises, and France overturns its old order in 1789 — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Third Estate

**2.** A bankrupt king summoned the Estates, the Third Estate rose, and France overturned its old order in 1789 — the key name for this is ____.
