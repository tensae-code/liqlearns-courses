# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a bankrupt king summons the Estates, the Third Estate rises, and France overturns its old order in 1789  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) a bankrupt king summons the Estates, the Third Estate rises, and France overturns its old order in 1789** — That is what this lesson teaches.
