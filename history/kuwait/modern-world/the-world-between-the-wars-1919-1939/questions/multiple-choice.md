# The World Between the Wars, 1919–1939 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a shaky peace, new states and rising dictators fill the twenty years that lead straight back to war  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) a shaky peace, new states and rising dictators fill the twenty years that lead straight back to war** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) the twenty years between the wars were a shaky peace: new states and rising dictators filled them, and they led straight back to war  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the twenty years between the wars were a shaky peace: new states and rising dictators filled them, and they led straight back to war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
