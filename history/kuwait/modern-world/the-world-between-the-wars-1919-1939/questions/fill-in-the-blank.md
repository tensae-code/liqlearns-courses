# The World Between the Wars, 1919–1939 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A shaky peace, new states and rising dictators fill the twenty years that lead straight back to war — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the shaky peace

**2.** The twenty years between the wars were a shaky peace: new states and rising dictators filled them, and they led straight back to war — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** rising dictators
