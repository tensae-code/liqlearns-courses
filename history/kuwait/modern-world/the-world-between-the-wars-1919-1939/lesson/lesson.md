# The World Between the Wars, 1919–1939

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the world between the wars, 1919–1939. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the twenty years between the wars were a shaky peace: new states and rising dictators filled them, and they led straight back to war.

Peace, when it came, did not hold. The twenty years between the wars — 1919 to 1939 — filled with new states drawn on redrawn maps, economies in trouble, and dictators rising on the discontent. The book teaches the period as a road: a shaky peace, walked badly, leads straight back to war. The between-wars years are the second war's first cause.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: the shaky peace, rising dictators. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
