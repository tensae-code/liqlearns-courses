# Effects of European Colonialism

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — effects of european colonialism. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the colonised lands paid the bill of colonialism — in wealth, in borders and in lives — while the colonisers grew rich.

Every system sends its bill somewhere. Colonialism's bill was paid by the colonised: wealth drained out of their lands, borders drawn through their peoples, lives spent under foreign rule — while the colonisers grew rich on the other side of the ledger. The book lays the bill out item by item. Effects are the historian's final question for any system, and for colonialism the answer is an account of loss.
