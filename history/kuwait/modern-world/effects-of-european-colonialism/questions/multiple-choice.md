# Effects of European Colonialism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the colonised lands pay in wealth, borders and lives while the colonisers grow rich — the bill the chapter lays out item by item  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the colonised lands pay in wealth, borders and lives while the colonisers grow rich — the bill the chapter lays out item by item** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) the colonised lands paid the bill of colonialism — in wealth, in borders and in lives — while the colonisers grew rich  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) the colonised lands paid the bill of colonialism — in wealth, in borders and in lives — while the colonisers grew rich** — That is what this lesson teaches.
