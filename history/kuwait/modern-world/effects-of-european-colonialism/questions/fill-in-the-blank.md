# Effects of European Colonialism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The colonised lands pay in wealth, borders and lives while the colonisers grow rich — the bill the chapter lays out item by item — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the bill

**2.** The colonised lands paid the bill of colonialism — in wealth, in borders and in lives — while the colonisers grew rich — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the colonisers' gain
