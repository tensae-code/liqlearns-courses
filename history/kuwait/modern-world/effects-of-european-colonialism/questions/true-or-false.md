# Effects of European Colonialism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The colonised lands pay in wealth, borders and lives while the colonisers grow rich — the bill the chapter lays out item by item.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
