# The Gulf Cooperation Council: Founding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The six Gulf states join in one council to act together, the founding act that the unit's cooperation lessons then unpack.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
