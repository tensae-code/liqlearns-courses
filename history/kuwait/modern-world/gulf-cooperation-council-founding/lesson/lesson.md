# The Gulf Cooperation Council: Founding

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the gulf cooperation council: founding. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the six Gulf states joined in one council to act together — the founding act that the unit's lessons on joint security, economic, cultural and educational cooperation then unpack.

The Gulf states had been born separately; in the modern age they chose to act together. The six Gulf states joined in one body — the Gulf Cooperation Council — to cooperate in security, economy, culture and education. This lesson is the founding act itself: six states, one council. The lessons that follow it in the book unpack what the council does together; the founding is the turn — neighbours deciding that their shared coast is also a shared future.
