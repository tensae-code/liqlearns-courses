# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — effects of industrialisation: cities, classes, empires. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the factory system spread its results around the world: crowded cities, new rich and poor classes, and a hungry search for colonies.

Results follow the factories round the world. Cities crowd as workers gather at the mills. Society itself re-sorts into new classes — a new rich that owns, a new poor that labours. And the factory system's hunger — for materials, for markets — drives a fresh search for colonies, tying this chapter to the colonial story before it. The Industrial Revolution's results are the modern world's foundations, for good and ill.
