# Kuwait: Pearl Diving — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Crews sail for the season, life aboard binds men in solidarity, women and children wait to welcome the fleet home, and the trade later fades as men turn away from its hardships — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** pearl diving

**2.** Pearl diving was the old Gulf's hardest riches: crews sailed out for the season, bound together in solidarity aboard, while women and children waited to welcome the fleet home — a trade that later faded as men turned away from its hardships — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the fleet's return
