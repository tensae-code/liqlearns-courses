# Kuwait: Pearl Diving — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Crews sail for the season, life aboard binds men in solidarity, women and children wait to welcome the fleet home, and the trade later fades as men turn away from its hardships.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
