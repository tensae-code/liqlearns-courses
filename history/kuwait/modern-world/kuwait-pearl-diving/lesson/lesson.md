# Kuwait: Pearl Diving

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — kuwait: pearl diving. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: pearl diving was the old Gulf's hardest riches: crews sailed out for the season, bound together in solidarity aboard, while women and children waited to welcome the fleet home — a trade that later faded as men turned away from its hardships.

The richest of the sea's gifts was also its hardest work. Pearl-diving crews sailed out for the whole season, living crowded aboard, bound to each other in solidarity — a man's life depended on the crew around him. Ashore, women and children waited out the season, then gathered to welcome the fleet home. Pearls made merchants rich and divers poor and broken. In time, men turned away from the hardships, and the great trade faded — but the waiting, the sailing and the homecoming remain the old Kuwait's deepest memory.
