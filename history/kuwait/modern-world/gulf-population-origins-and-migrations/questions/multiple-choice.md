# Gulf Population: Origins and Migrations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) learners untangle stock, migration, internal and external migration, then trace Arab tribes moving into the Gulf until the coast fills with new communities  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) learners untangle stock, migration, internal and external migration, then trace Arab tribes moving into the Gulf until the coast fills with new communities** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)

**Answer: b) drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade push tribe after tribe toward Arabia's east coast, where the moves knit into new political bodies** — That is what this lesson teaches.
