# Gulf Population: Origins and Migrations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Learners untangle stock, migration, internal and external migration, then trace Arab tribes moving into the Gulf until the coast fills with new communities.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Drought, thin rain, quarrels and hard trade push tribe after tribe toward Arabia's east coast, where the moves knit into new political bodies.
