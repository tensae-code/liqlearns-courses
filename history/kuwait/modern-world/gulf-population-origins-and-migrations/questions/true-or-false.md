# Gulf Population: Origins and Migrations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Learners untangle stock, migration, internal and external migration, then trace Arab tribes moving into the Gulf until the coast fills with new communities. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
