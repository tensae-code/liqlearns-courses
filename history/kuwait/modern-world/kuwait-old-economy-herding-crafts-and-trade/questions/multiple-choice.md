# Kuwait's Old Economy: Herding, Crafts and Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) herders water the flocks while women milk them and churn the milk in the leather samil, and palm and field farming feed the settlements  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) herders water the flocks while women milk them and churn the milk in the leather samil, and palm and field farming feed the settlements** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)

**Answer: b) wool is clipped, women gather to work it, and the old crafts turn local wool, clay and fibre into the goods every household needs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
