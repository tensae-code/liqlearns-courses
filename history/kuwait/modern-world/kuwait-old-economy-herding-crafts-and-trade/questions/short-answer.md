# Kuwait's Old Economy: Herding, Crafts and Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Herders water the flocks while women milk them and churn the milk in the leather samil, and palm and field farming feed the settlements.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Wool is clipped, women gather to work it, and the old crafts turn local wool, clay and fibre into the goods every household needs.
