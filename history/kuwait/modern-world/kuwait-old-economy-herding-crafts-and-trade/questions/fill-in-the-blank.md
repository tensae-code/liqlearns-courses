# Kuwait's Old Economy: Herding, Crafts and Trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Herders water the flocks while women milk them and churn the milk in the leather samil, and palm and field farming feed the settlements — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** samil

**2.** Wool is clipped, women gather to work it, and the old crafts turn local wool, clay and fibre into the goods every household needs — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** handicrafts
