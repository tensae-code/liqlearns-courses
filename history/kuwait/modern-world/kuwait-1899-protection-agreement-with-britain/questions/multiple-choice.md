# Kuwait: The 1899 Protection Agreement with Britain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Kuwait stands uneasily beside the Ottoman state, and Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signs the 1899 agreement so Britain shields Kuwait from outside claims  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) Kuwait stands uneasily beside the Ottoman state, and Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signs the 1899 agreement so Britain shields Kuwait from outside claims** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) standing uneasily beside the Ottoman state, Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signed the 1899 agreement so that Britain would shield Kuwait from outside claims  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) standing uneasily beside the Ottoman state, Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signed the 1899 agreement so that Britain would shield Kuwait from outside claims** — That is what this lesson teaches.
