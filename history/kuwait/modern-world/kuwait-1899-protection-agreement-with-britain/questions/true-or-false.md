# Kuwait: The 1899 Protection Agreement with Britain — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kuwait stands uneasily beside the Ottoman state, and Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signs the 1899 agreement so Britain shields Kuwait from outside claims.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
