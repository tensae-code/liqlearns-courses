# Kuwait: The 1899 Protection Agreement with Britain — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Kuwait stands uneasily beside the Ottoman state, and Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signs the 1899 agreement so Britain shields Kuwait from outside claims — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the 1899 agreement

**2.** Standing uneasily beside the Ottoman state, Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signed the 1899 agreement so that Britain would shield Kuwait from outside claims — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Mubarak Al Sabah
