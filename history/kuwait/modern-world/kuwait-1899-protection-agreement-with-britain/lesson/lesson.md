# Kuwait: The 1899 Protection Agreement with Britain

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — kuwait: the 1899 protection agreement with britain. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: standing uneasily beside the Ottoman state, Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signed the 1899 agreement so that Britain would shield Kuwait from outside claims.

By 1899 Kuwait stood in a tight place, uneasily beside the great Ottoman state, with outside claims pressing on its independence. Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah made his move on paper: he signed a protection agreement with Britain. Kuwait would handle its foreign affairs with Britain, and Britain would shield it from outside claims. A signature, not a battle, kept Kuwait standing — the 1899 agreement is the paper that guarded the country's survival.
