# Video script — Kuwait: From Emirate to Modern State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Kuwait grew from emirate to modern state under its later rulers — Jaber Al Ahmad, the Amir of Hearts, who proposed the Gulf Council, opened Kuwait Towers in 1979 and won women their political rights, then Saad Al Abdullah, the Hero of Liberation — and today's Kuwait under Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad practises balanced diplomacy and mediation, answering aggression with sovereignty held firm.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

Ruler by ruler — Jaber Al Ahmad, 'Amir of Hearts' (1977–2006), who proposed the Gulf Council, opened Kuwait Towers in 1979 and won women their political rights, then Saad Al Abdullah, 'Hero of Liberation' — the emirate becomes a modern state.
Today's Kuwait under Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad practises balanced diplomacy and mediation, gathers world honours, and answers aggression — the book names Iran's 2026 missile and drone attacks — with sovereignty held firm.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
