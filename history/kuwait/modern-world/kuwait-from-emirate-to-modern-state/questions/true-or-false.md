# Kuwait: From Emirate to Modern State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ruler by ruler — Jaber Al Ahmad, 'Amir of Hearts' (1977–2006), who proposed the Gulf Council, opened Kuwait Towers in 1979 and won women their political rights, then Saad Al Abdullah, 'Hero of Liberation' — the emirate becomes a modern state. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
