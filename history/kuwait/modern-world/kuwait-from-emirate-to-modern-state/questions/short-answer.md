# Kuwait: From Emirate to Modern State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ruler by ruler — Jaber Al Ahmad, 'Amir of Hearts' (1977–2006), who proposed the Gulf Council, opened Kuwait Towers in 1979 and won women their political rights, then Saad Al Abdullah, 'Hero of Liberation' — the emirate becomes a modern state.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Today's Kuwait under Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad practises balanced diplomacy and mediation, gathers world honours, and answers aggression — the book names Iran's 2026 missile and drone attacks — with sovereignty held firm.
