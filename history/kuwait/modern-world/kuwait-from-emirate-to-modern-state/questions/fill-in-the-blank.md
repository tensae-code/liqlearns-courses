# Kuwait: From Emirate to Modern State — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Ruler by ruler — Jaber Al Ahmad, 'Amir of Hearts' (1977–2006), who proposed the Gulf Council, opened Kuwait Towers in 1979 and won women their political rights, then Saad Al Abdullah, 'Hero of Liberation' — the emirate becomes a modern state — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Jaber Al Ahmad

**2.** Today's Kuwait under Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad practises balanced diplomacy and mediation, gathers world honours, and answers aggression — the book names Iran's 2026 missile and drone attacks — with sovereignty held firm — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Saad Al Abdullah
