# Kuwait and Gulf Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — kuwait and gulf independence. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: as Britain withdrew, the Gulf peoples pressed for freedom, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — the Father of Independence — led Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following.

Empires do not last for ever, and after the world wars Britain began to withdraw from the Gulf. The Gulf peoples pressed for their freedom — and Kuwait moved first among its neighbours. Under Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem, remembered as the Father of Independence, Kuwait became independent on 19 June 1961. The protection age ended; Kuwait stood on its own. The other Gulf states followed their own roads to the same shore of freedom.
