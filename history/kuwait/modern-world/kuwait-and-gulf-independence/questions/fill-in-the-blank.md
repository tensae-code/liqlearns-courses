# Kuwait and Gulf Independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Gulf peoples press for freedom as Britain withdraws, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — Father of Independence — leads Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Abdullah Al Salem

**2.** As Britain withdrew, the Gulf peoples pressed for freedom, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — the Father of Independence — led Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** independence
