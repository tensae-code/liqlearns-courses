# Kuwait and Gulf Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Gulf peoples press for freedom as Britain withdraws, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — Father of Independence — leads Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) Gulf peoples press for freedom as Britain withdraws, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — Father of Independence — leads Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) as Britain withdrew, the Gulf peoples pressed for freedom, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — the Father of Independence — led Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) as Britain withdrew, the Gulf peoples pressed for freedom, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — the Father of Independence — led Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following** — That is what this lesson teaches.
