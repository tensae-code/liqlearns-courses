# Kuwait and Gulf Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gulf peoples press for freedom as Britain withdraws, and Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — Father of Independence — leads Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, with the other Gulf states following.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
