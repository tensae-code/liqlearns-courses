# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Spain sails west instead of east and stumbles on a whole new world, changing both shores forever — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Spanish voyages

**2.** Spain sailed west instead of east and stumbled on a whole new world, changing both shores forever — the key name for this is ____.
