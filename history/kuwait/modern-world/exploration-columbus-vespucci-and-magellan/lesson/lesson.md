# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — exploration: columbus, vespucci and magellan. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: Spain sailed west instead of east and stumbled on a whole new world, changing both shores forever.

Spain chose the other direction. Instead of creeping around Africa, Spanish ships sailed west across the Atlantic, expecting Asia — and stumbled on a whole new world in between. The book's word is the honest one: stumbled. Neither shore was ready for the meeting, and both were changed for ever by it. The Spanish voyages turned a search for a shortcut into the largest turning point of the age.
