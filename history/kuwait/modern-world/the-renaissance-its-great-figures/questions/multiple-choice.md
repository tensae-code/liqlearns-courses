# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the rebirth shows itself in art, letters, science and building, the works by which the age is still recognised  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) the rebirth shows itself in art, letters, science and building, the works by which the age is still recognised** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
