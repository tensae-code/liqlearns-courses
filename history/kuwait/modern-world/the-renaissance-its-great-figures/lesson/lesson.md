# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the renaissance: its great figures. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the rebirth shows itself in works: art, letters, science and building — the manifestations by which the age is still recognised.

How do you know a rebirth when you see one? By its works. The Renaissance shows itself in art and letters, in science and in building — paintings, books, discoveries and domes. These manifestations are not decoration on the age; they are the age, the evidence by which it is still recognised five centuries later. The book's method is the historian's: judge an age by what it made.
