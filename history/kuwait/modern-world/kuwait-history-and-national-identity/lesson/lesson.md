# Kuwait: History and National Identity

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — kuwait: history and national identity. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: a people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity: Kuwait's past is kept and taught as the guard of Kuwait's character.

Why keep history at all? This lesson gives the national answer. A people's memory of itself becomes a wall around its identity — the shared story that says who we are and keeps that character from being washed away. Kuwait's past, in the book's telling, is exactly that guard: the founding, the rulers, the survivals, kept and taught so that Kuwait's character stands protected. History here is not decoration; it is defence.
