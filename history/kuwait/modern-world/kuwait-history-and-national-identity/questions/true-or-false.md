# Kuwait: History and National Identity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity, and Kuwait's past is presented as the guard of Kuwait's character.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
