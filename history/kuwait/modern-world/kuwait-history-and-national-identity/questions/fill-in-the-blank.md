# Kuwait: History and National Identity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity, and Kuwait's past is presented as the guard of Kuwait's character — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** national identity

**2.** A people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity: Kuwait's past is kept and taught as the guard of Kuwait's character — the key name for this is ____.
