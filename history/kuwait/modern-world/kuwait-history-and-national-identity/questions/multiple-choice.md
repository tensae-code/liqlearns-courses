# Kuwait: History and National Identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity, and Kuwait's past is presented as the guard of Kuwait's character  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) a people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity, and Kuwait's past is presented as the guard of Kuwait's character** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) a people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity: Kuwait's past is kept and taught as the guard of Kuwait's character  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) a people's memory of itself becomes the wall around its identity: Kuwait's past is kept and taught as the guard of Kuwait's character** — That is what this lesson teaches.
