# Colonial Rivalry Among European Powers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The empires measure their shares of Africa and Asia against each other until the rivalry itself becomes a road to war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
