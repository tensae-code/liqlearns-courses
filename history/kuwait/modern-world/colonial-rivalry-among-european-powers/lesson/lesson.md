# Colonial Rivalry Among European Powers

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — colonial rivalry among european powers. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: the empires measured their shares of Africa and Asia against each other until the rivalry itself became a road to war.

Empires do not share politely. Each European state measured its share of Africa and Asia against its rivals' — a port here envied, a valley there demanded. The measuring never stopped, because no empire would accept being second. The book's verdict is in its shape: the colonial rivalry itself became a road to war, one of the loads on the gun of 1914.
