# Founding of the Other Gulf States

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — founding of the other gulf states. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: Kuwait was not born alone: Arab migrations settled the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raised the political bodies that became today's Gulf states.

Kuwait's birth was one of several. The same age of Arab migration settled the whole Gulf coast, and in each land a founding leader gathered the settlers into a political body — a ruled community with a name and a future. Side by side along the shore, those bodies grew into the Gulf states of today. The book widens the lens on purpose: Kuwait's story is its own, and it is also the coast's shared story.
