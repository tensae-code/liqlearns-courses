# Founding of the Other Gulf States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Arab migrations settle the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raise the political bodies that become today's Gulf states  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) Arab migrations settle the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raise the political bodies that become today's Gulf states** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) Kuwait was not born alone: Arab migrations settled the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raised the political bodies that became today's Gulf states  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Kuwait was not born alone: Arab migrations settled the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raised the political bodies that became today's Gulf states** — That is what this lesson teaches.
