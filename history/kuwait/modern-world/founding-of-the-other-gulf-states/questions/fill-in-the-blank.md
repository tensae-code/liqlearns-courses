# Founding of the Other Gulf States — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Arab migrations settle the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raise the political bodies that become today's Gulf states — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** founding leaders

**2.** Kuwait was not born alone: Arab migrations settled the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raised the political bodies that became today's Gulf states — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Gulf states
