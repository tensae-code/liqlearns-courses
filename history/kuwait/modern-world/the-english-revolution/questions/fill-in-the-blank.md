# The English Revolution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** King and parliament collide over who rules England until revolution settles the question and limits the crown — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** parliament

**2.** King and parliament collided over who rules England, until revolution settled the question and limited the crown — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the limited crown
