# The English Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) king and parliament collide over who rules England until revolution settles the question and limits the crown  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) king and parliament collide over who rules England until revolution settles the question and limits the crown** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) king and parliament collided over who rules England, until revolution settled the question and limited the crown  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) king and parliament collided over who rules England, until revolution settled the question and limited the crown** — That is what this lesson teaches.
