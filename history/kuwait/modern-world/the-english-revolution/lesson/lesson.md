# The English Revolution

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the english revolution. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: king and parliament collided over who rules England, until revolution settled the question and limited the crown.

The revolution chapters open in England, with a single question: who rules? King and parliament each claimed the answer, and the collision could not be talked away. Revolution settled it — the crown survived, but limited, bounded by the settlement the revolution imposed. England's answer would echo: every revolution after it argues with this one.
