# Kuwait's Founding: The Utub and Al Sabah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mid-1600s drought in Najd drives the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families — the Utub — from al-Hadar to Zubara, then on to al-Qurain in search of a lasting home.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Al-Qurain, named for its hill seen from the sea, becomes Kuwait, the little fort; in 1716 the three families ally, split rule, trade and sea work, and in 1718 the people pledge Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber as first ruler.
