# Kuwait's Founding: The Utub and Al Sabah — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Mid-1600s drought in Najd drives the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families — the Utub — from al-Hadar to Zubara, then on to al-Qurain in search of a lasting home — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Utub

**2.** Al-Qurain, named for its hill seen from the sea, becomes Kuwait, the little fort; in 1716 the three families ally, split rule, trade and sea work, and in 1718 the people pledge Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber as first ruler — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sabah bin Jaber
