# Kuwait's Founding: The Utub and Al Sabah — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mid-1600s drought in Najd drives the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families — the Utub — from al-Hadar to Zubara, then on to al-Qurain in search of a lasting home. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
