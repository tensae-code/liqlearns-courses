# Video script — Kuwait's Founding: The Utub and Al Sabah

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Kuwait was born from a migration: drought in Najd drove the Utub families — Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma — from al-Hadar to Zubara and on to al-Qurain, which became Kuwait, the little fort; in 1716 the families allied and shared the work of rule, trade and sea, and in 1718 the people pledged Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber as first ruler, founding a way of ruling by chosen leadership and shared counsel.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

Mid-1600s drought in Najd drives the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families — the Utub — from al-Hadar to Zubara, then on to al-Qurain in search of a lasting home.
Al-Qurain, named for its hill seen from the sea, becomes Kuwait, the little fort; in 1716 the three families ally, split rule, trade and sea work, and in 1718 the people pledge Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber as first ruler.
The Utub arrive and a way of ruling is born with them — chosen leadership and shared counsel — the seed of Kuwait's whole political order.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
