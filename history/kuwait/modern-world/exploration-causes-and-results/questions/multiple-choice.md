# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Europe wants the spices and gold of the East without the middlemen, gains ships and compasses to try, and pushes out onto unknown seas  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) Europe wants the spices and gold of the East without the middlemen, gains ships and compasses to try, and pushes out onto unknown seas** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) trade routes shift from the Mediterranean to the oceans, prices and peoples are overturned, and the old world's map — and power — never look the same  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) trade routes shift from the Mediterranean to the oceans, prices and peoples are overturned, and the old world's map — and power — never look the same** — That is what this lesson teaches.
