# Exploration: Causes and Results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Europe wants the spices and gold of the East without the middlemen, gains ships and compasses to try, and pushes out onto unknown seas — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the discovery movement

**2.** Trade routes shift from the Mediterranean to the oceans, prices and peoples are overturned, and the old world's map — and power — never look the same — the key name for this is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the results
