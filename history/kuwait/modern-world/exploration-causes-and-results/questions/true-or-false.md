# Exploration: Causes and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europe wants the spices and gold of the East without the middlemen, gains ships and compasses to try, and pushes out onto unknown seas. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
