# Exploration: Causes and Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe wants the spices and gold of the East without the middlemen, gains ships and compasses to try, and pushes out onto unknown seas.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trade routes shift from the Mediterranean to the oceans, prices and peoples are overturned, and the old world's map — and power — never look the same.
