# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — exploration: portugal and the sea routes. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: little Portugal led the race, sailing down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour.

The race was led by the smallest runner. Little Portugal, on Europe's Atlantic edge, took the lead — sailing down Africa's coast step by step, harbour by harbour, feeling for the way around the continent toward India. Each anchorage was a lesson and a claim. The Portuguese voyages are the discovery story's patient chapter: no single leap, just decades of coast, until the sea road to India lay open.
