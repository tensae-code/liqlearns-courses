# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) little Portugal leads the race down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) little Portugal leads the race down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) little Portugal led the race, sailing down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) little Portugal led the race, sailing down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour** — That is what this lesson teaches.
