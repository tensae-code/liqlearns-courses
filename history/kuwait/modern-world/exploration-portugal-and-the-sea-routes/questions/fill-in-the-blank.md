# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Little Portugal leads the race down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** Portugal

**2.** Little Portugal led the race, sailing down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the sea route to India
