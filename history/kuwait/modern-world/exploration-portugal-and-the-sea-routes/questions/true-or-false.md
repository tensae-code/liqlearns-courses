# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Little Portugal leads the race down Africa's coast toward India, harbour by harbour.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
