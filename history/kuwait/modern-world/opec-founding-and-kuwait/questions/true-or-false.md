# OPEC: Founding and Kuwait — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Oil runs modern transport and industry, so the exporting states found OPEC to set production quotas and guard producers' and consumers' balance, Kuwait among its members, and in 2016 widen the pact into OPEC+.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
