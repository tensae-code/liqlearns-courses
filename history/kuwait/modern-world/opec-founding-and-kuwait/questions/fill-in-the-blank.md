# OPEC: Founding and Kuwait — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Oil runs modern transport and industry, so the exporting states found OPEC to set production quotas and guard producers' and consumers' balance, Kuwait among its members, and in 2016 widen the pact into OPEC+ — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** OPEC

**2.** Because oil runs modern transport and industry, the exporting states founded OPEC to set production quotas and guard the balance of producers and consumers — Kuwait among its members — and in 2016 widened the pact into OPEC+ — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** production quotas
