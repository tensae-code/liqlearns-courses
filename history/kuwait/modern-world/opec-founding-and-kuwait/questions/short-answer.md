# OPEC: Founding and Kuwait — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story.
**Answer:** Oil runs modern transport and industry, so the exporting states found OPEC to set production quotas and guard producers' and consumers' balance, Kuwait among its members, and in 2016 widen the pact into OPEC+.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Because oil runs modern transport and industry, the exporting states founded OPEC to set production quotas and guard the balance of producers and consumers — Kuwait among its members — and in 2016 widened the pact into OPEC+.
