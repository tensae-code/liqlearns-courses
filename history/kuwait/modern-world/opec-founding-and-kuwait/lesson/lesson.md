# OPEC: Founding and Kuwait

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — opec: founding and kuwait. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: because oil runs modern transport and industry, the exporting states founded OPEC to set production quotas and guard the balance of producers and consumers — Kuwait among its members — and in 2016 widened the pact into OPEC+.

Not every world organisation sits inside the UN. Oil runs modern transport and industry, so the states that export it needed a body of their own: OPEC, founded by the exporting states to set production quotas and guard the balance between producers and consumers. Kuwait stands among its members. And the pact kept growing — in 2016 it widened into OPEC+, drawing more producers into the same balancing act. The book holds OPEC up as the model of an organisation independent of the UN.
