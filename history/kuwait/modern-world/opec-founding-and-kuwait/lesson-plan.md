# Lesson plan — OPEC: Founding and Kuwait

**Course:** History — Kuwait · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** OPEC: Founding and Kuwait
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Kuwait and the Gulf in the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** because oil runs modern transport and industry, the exporting states founded OPEC to set production quotas and guard the balance of producers and consumers — Kuwait among its members — and in 2016 widened the pact into OPEC+.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Kuwait and the Gulf in the Modern World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: because oil runs modern transport and industry, the exporting states founded OPEC to set production quotas and guard the balance of producers and consumers — Kuwait among its members — and in 2016 widened the pact into OPEC+.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — or, for this course's lessons, from the Kuwait story itself as the books tell it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Kuwait's books themselves work, starting from Kuwait and widening to the Gulf and the world.
