# Hands-on — OPEC: Founding and Kuwait

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud, in your own words: because oil runs modern transport and industry, the exporting states founded OPEC to set production quotas and guard the balance of producers and consumers — Kuwait among its members — and in 2016 widened the pact into OPEC+. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson (a picture of oil derricks and a tanker at a Gulf port, with the member states' flags along the quay), and say each part aloud as you draw it: who is there, where it happens, and what turned.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
