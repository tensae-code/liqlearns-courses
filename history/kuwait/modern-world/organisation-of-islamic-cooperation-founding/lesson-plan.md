# Lesson plan — The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation: Founding

**Course:** History — Kuwait · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation: Founding
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Kuwait and the Gulf in the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the OIC was born from a wound: Britain's 1917 Balfour promise, the Nakba's expulsions and the 1967 loss of Jerusalem built the pressure; King Faisal called in 1965 for an Islamic gathering, and after the 1969 al-Aqsa fire the Islamic Conference — later the OIC — was born as the Muslim states' one voice, its summits running from Morocco in 1969, through the fifth that Kuwait's ruler chaired, to Istanbul's thirteenth in 2016 and the fourteenth after it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Kuwait and the Gulf in the Modern World (recommended, never forced).
