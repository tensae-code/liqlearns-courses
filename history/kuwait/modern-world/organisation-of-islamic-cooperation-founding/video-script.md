# Video script — The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation: Founding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The OIC was born from a wound: Britain's 1917 Balfour promise, the Nakba's expulsions and the 1967 loss of Jerusalem built the pressure; King Faisal called in 1965 for an Islamic gathering, and after the 1969 al-Aqsa fire the Islamic Conference — later the OIC — was born as the Muslim states' one voice, its summits running from Morocco in 1969, through the fifth that Kuwait's ruler chaired, to Istanbul's thirteenth in 2016 and the fourteenth after it.

Look at the map. Here is Kuwait, at the head of the Gulf, where its own story meets the Gulf's and the world's.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
