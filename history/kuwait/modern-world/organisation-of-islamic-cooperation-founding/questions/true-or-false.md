# The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation: Founding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain's 1917 Balfour promise, the Nakba's expulsions and the 1967 loss of Jerusalem build the pressure; King Faisal calls in 1965 for an Islamic gathering, and after the 1969 al-Aqsa fire the Islamic Conference — later the OIC — is born as the Muslim states' one voice. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in this story ever changed and nothing came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's whole work is the turn: what changed, and what came of it.
