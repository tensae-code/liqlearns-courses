# The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation: Founding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's main teaching about its story. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain's 1917 Balfour promise, the Nakba's expulsions and the 1967 loss of Jerusalem build the pressure; King Faisal calls in 1965 for an Islamic gathering, and after the 1969 al-Aqsa fire the Islamic Conference — later the OIC — is born as the Muslim states' one voice.

**2.** What is the second thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The first summit meets in Morocco in 1969 over the al-Aqsa fire, Kuwait's ruler chairs the fifth, and the line runs on to Istanbul's thirteenth in 2016 and the fourteenth after it.
