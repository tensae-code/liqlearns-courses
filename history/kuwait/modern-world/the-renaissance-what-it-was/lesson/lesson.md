# The Renaissance: What It Was

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — the renaissance: what it was. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: Europe stirred from its medieval sleep in a rebirth: cities, wealth, old manuscripts and new patrons gave scholars and artists the means to be reborn.

Grade 12's world history opens with a waking. Medieval Europe stirs from its long sleep — and the book names the means of the rebirth: cities growing rich, wealth gathering, old manuscripts resurfacing, and patrons willing to pay for learning and art. Given means, scholars and artists did the rest. The Renaissance — the rebirth — is Europe deciding that the ancient world was not finished with it yet.
