# The Renaissance: What It Was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Europe stirs from its medieval sleep as cities, wealth, old manuscripts and new patrons give scholars and artists the means to be reborn — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** the Renaissance

**2.** Europe stirred from its medieval sleep in a rebirth: cities, wealth, old manuscripts and new patrons gave scholars and artists the means to be reborn — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** patrons
