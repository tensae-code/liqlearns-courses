# The Renaissance: What It Was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Europe stirs from its medieval sleep as cities, wealth, old manuscripts and new patrons give scholars and artists the means to be reborn  b) the past can be known without any evidence at all  c) nothing in this story ever changed  d) only rulers have a past
**Answer: a) Europe stirs from its medieval sleep as cities, wealth, old manuscripts and new patrons give scholars and artists the means to be reborn** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) stories need never be checked against what happened  b) Europe stirred from its medieval sleep in a rebirth: cities, wealth, old manuscripts and new patrons gave scholars and artists the means to be reborn  c) Kuwait's story is only a footnote to other countries  d) every age lived in exactly the same way
**Answer: b) Europe stirred from its medieval sleep in a rebirth: cities, wealth, old manuscripts and new patrons gave scholars and artists the means to be reborn** — That is what this lesson teaches.
