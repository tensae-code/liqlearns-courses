# European Colonial Rivalry in the Gulf

## Do it first

Before you read, do this: say out loud, in your own words, what you already think this story is about — european colonial rivalry in the gulf. Keep your guess in mind. This lesson tells the story properly: European powers raced for the Gulf's sea lanes and ports until Britain's grip closed over the coast the Gulf peoples call home.

The sea roads that made the Gulf rich also drew the world's empires. European powers raced each other for the Gulf's lanes and ports — each wanting the route to India guarded by its own flag. One by one the rivals fell away, until Britain's grip closed over the coast. The Gulf peoples still called it home; but the sea outside their harbours now answered to a foreign power.
