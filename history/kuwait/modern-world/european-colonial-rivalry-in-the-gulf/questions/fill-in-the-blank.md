# European Colonial Rivalry in the Gulf — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** European powers race for the Gulf's sea lanes and ports until Britain's grip closes over the coast the Gulf peoples call home — the key name for this is ____.
**Answer:** colonial rivalry

**2.** European powers raced for the Gulf's sea lanes and ports until Britain's grip closed over the coast the Gulf peoples call home — the key name for this is ____.
