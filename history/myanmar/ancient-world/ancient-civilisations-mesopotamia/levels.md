# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — between two rivers, Mesopotamia runs through four ages in turn — Sumerian, Babylonian, Assyrian and Chaldean — city priests, law-giving kings, iron armies and star-watchers succeeding one another on the same river plain — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
