# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name Mesopotamia's four ages in order.
**Answer:** Sumerian, Babylonian, Assyrian and Chaldean.

**2.** What kind of power marks the Chaldean age?
**Answer:** The star-watchers — priests who read the heavens and mapped the skies.
