# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many ages does the book walk through in Mesopotamia?
a) four — Sumerian, Babylonian, Assyrian and Chaldean  b) two  c) seven  d) one unchanging age
**Answer: a) four — Sumerian, Babylonian, Assyrian and Chaldean** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which age is remembered for its iron armies?
a) the Assyrian age  b) the Sumerian age  c) the Chaldean age  d) the Babylonian age
**Answer: a) the Assyrian age** — That is what this lesson teaches.
