# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mesopotamia's four ages succeeded one another on the same land between two rivers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Babylonian age is the age of the star-watchers.
**Answer: False** — The star-watchers belong to the Chaldean age; Babylon's age is the law-giving kings.
