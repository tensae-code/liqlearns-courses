# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: China

**Course:** History — Myanmar · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: China
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Great Ancient Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chinese culture was born on the Yellow River and grew from that river region outward — a civilisation that would renew itself, age after age, for four thousand years.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Great Ancient Civilisations — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Chinese culture was born on the Yellow River and grew from that river region outward — a civilisation that would renew itself, age after age, for four thousand years.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the Myanmar they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Myanmar and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.

- Thinks of China's civilisation as one short kingdom among others → the book's mark for China is its long, self-renewing life — four thousand years from its Yellow River birthplace.
- Go back to the one thing: Chinese culture was born on the Yellow River and grew from that river region outward — a civilisation that would renew itself, age after age, for four thousand years. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
