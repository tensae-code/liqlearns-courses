# Ancient Civilisations: China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where was Chinese culture born?
a) on the Yellow River  b) on the Nile  c) between two rivers in Mesopotamia  d) on the Irrawaddy plain
**Answer: a) on the Yellow River** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** For how long does the lesson say China's civilisation renewed itself?
a) four thousand years  b) four hundred years  c) one century  d) it never renewed itself
**Answer: a) four thousand years** — That is what this lesson teaches.
