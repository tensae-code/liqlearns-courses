# Ancient Civilisations: China — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two things does the book teach about Chinese culture?
**Answer:** The region where it was born — the Yellow River region — and how it developed from there.

**2.** What makes China's story stand out among this course's civilisations?
**Answer:** Its length and self-renewal: it remained recognisably the same civilisation across four thousand years.
