# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: China

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a river and a small settlement at its bank. Draw the settlement again, bigger, beside the first drawing; then again, bigger still, in the same place. Ask: what makes a civilisation able to grow old without disappearing? China's story starts at that riverbank.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Chinese culture was born on the Yellow River and grew from that river region outward — a civilisation that would renew itself, age after age, for four thousand years. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
