# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: China

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Chinese culture was born on the Yellow River and grew from that river region outward — a civilisation that would renew itself, age after age, for four thousand years.

The book begins Chinese culture where the book says it was born: on the Yellow River, in the region where the river's water and soil first fed farming villages. From that river region, the civilisation grew — villages into towns, towns into kingdoms, customs and crafts and writing taking shape along the river that had mothered them.

What marks China's story among the civilisations of this course is its length and its self-renewal. Empires in this course rise and give way to the next age; China's civilisation, born on the Yellow River, developed and renewed itself for four thousand years — changing rulers, changing ages, and yet remaining recognisably the same civilisation, carrying its culture forward through every change.

So the lesson's shape is a birthplace and a long life: born on the Yellow River, developed outward from that region, and renewed again and again across four thousand years.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
