# The Pyu City-States of Ancient Myanmar — depth

This lesson has 2 sub lessons, each opening when the one before it is proved; the gate pool for each is that sub lesson's whole question set (no hardness ranking is written in questions/). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Myanmar's first cities were the Pyu city-states: farming skill, craft knowledge and growing capability gathered people behind walls, and a chain of walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities — Beikthano, Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi and Waitharli — flourished for centuries before any Myanmar empire existed — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
