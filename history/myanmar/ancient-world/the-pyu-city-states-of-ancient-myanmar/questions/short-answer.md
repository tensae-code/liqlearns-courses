# The Pyu City-States of Ancient Myanmar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what turns a settlement into a city-state? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** When knowledge, capability and technology grow — farmers growing more than their family eats, craftspeople making what others need, builders raising walls and guiding water — people gather behind walls and the city rules itself and the fields around it.

**2.** Name three of the Pyu city-states. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any three of: Beikthano (the Vishnu city), Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi and Waitharli.
