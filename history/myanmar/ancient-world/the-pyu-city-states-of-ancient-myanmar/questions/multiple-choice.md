# The Pyu City-States of Ancient Myanmar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What made Myanmar's first cities emerge? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the growth of knowledge, capability and technology — farming skill and craft knowledge gathering people behind walls  b) a king's order given in one day  c) the arrival of an empire  d) a single great battle
**Answer: a) the growth of knowledge, capability and technology — farming skill and craft knowledge gathering people behind walls** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is one of the Pyu city-states named in the lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Sri Ksetra  b) Bagan  c) Angkor  d) Rome
**Answer: a) Sri Ksetra** — That is what this lesson teaches.
