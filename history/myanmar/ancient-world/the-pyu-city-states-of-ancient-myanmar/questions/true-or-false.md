# The Pyu City-States of Ancient Myanmar — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Pyu city-states flourished for centuries before any Myanmar empire existed. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Only one faith was practised inside the Pyu cities. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Two great faiths lived side by side in them — Buddhism and Hinduism.
