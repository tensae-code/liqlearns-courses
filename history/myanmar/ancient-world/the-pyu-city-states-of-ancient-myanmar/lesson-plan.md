# Lesson plan — The Pyu City-States of Ancient Myanmar

**Course:** History — Myanmar · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Pyu City-States of Ancient Myanmar
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myanmar's Own Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Myanmar's first cities were the Pyu city-states: farming skill, craft knowledge and growing capability gathered people behind walls, and a chain of walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities — Beikthano, Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi and Waitharli — flourished for centuries before any Myanmar empire existed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Myanmar's Own Past — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **How cities emerge** — The development of knowledge, capability and technology that created the ancient city-states.
2. **The Pyu city-states one by one** — Beikthano (the Vishnu city), Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi and Waitharli — a chain of walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
