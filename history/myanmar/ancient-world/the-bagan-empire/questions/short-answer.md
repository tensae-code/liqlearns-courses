# The Bagan Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Bagan emerge? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A new power rose from the Pyu world and gathered the Irrawaddy plain's peoples and cities under one rule.

**2.** What three things made Bagan rich, literate and stone-built? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Taxes paid in rice, the sangha of monks who kept learning and literature, and the building of thousands of pagodas in stone.
