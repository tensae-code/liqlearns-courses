# The Bagan Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Bagan Empire gather under one rule? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the Irrawaddy plain, out of the older Pyu world  b) the Greek city-states  c) the Nile valley  d) the cities of China
**Answer: a) the Irrawaddy plain, out of the older Pyu world** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How were Bagan's taxes paid? (Sub lesson 2)
a) in rice from the plain's fields  b) in gold coins only  c) in bronze tools  d) they were never paid
**Answer: a) in rice from the plain's fields** — That is what this lesson teaches.
