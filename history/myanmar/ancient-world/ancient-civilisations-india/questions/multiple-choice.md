# Ancient Civilisations: India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did India's culture travel to other lands?
a) with its traders, across the seas  b) only by armies and conquest  c) it never travelled  d) by rivers flowing out of India
**Answer: a) with its traders, across the seas** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which city does the book name as Indianised?
a) Angkor, in Cambodia  b) Bagan, in Myanmar  c) Rome, in Italy  d) Hanlin, in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Angkor, in Cambodia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
