# Ancient Civilisations: India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things that travelled with India's traders.
**Answer:** Any two of: the worship of Shiva (India's gods), Buddhism, and India's scripts.

**2.** How does the book measure a civilisation's reach in this lesson?
**Answer:** Not by its armies, but by what other peoples chose to keep — its gods, faith and scripts, as at Angkor.
