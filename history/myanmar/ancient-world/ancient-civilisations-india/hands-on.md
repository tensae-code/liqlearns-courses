# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pack an imaginary trader's bag on the table: cloth, spice, a small carved god, a book of letters. Walk it across the room to a 'far shore'. Ask of each thing in the bag: what happens to the place where this is unpacked? India's reach is that unpacking, across whole seas.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: India's ancient civilisation reached far beyond India: its gods and its scripts travelled with its traders — Shiva worship, and Buddhism arriving and spreading — until Indian culture was building temples in Cambodia, in the Indianised city of Angkor. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
