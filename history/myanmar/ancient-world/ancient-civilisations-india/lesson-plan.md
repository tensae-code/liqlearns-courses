# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: India

**Course:** History — Myanmar · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: India
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Great Ancient Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** India's ancient civilisation reached far beyond India: its gods and its scripts travelled with its traders — Shiva worship, and Buddhism arriving and spreading — until Indian culture was building temples in Cambodia, in the Indianised city of Angkor.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Great Ancient Civilisations — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Measures India's reach by conquest and armies → the book measures it by travel and adoption — gods, faith and scripts carried by traders and kept by other peoples, as at Angkor.
- Go back to the one thing: India's ancient civilisation reached far beyond India: its gods and its scripts travelled with its traders — Shiva worship, and Buddhism arriving and spreading — until Indian culture was building temples in Cambodia, in the Indianised city of Angkor. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
