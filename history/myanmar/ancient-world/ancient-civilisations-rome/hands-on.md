# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take a drawing a friend made and copy it — then change one thing to make it your own and useful in a new place. Ask: is the copy still your friend's, or has it become yours too? Rome did this with the culture of a whole civilisation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Rome took Greek culture and carried it further than Greece ever reached: what Rome learned from the Greeks it joined to what Rome itself built — roads, law and an empire's reach. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
