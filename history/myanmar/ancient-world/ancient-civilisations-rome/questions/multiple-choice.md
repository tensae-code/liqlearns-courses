# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From which culture did Rome learn much of what it became?
a) Greek culture  b) Egyptian culture  c) Chinese culture  d) Pyu culture
**Answer: a) Greek culture** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What three things does the lesson name as what Rome built and gave?
a) roads, law and an empire's reach  b) pagodas, rice taxes and monks  c) a calendar, a granary and a kingdom  d) walls, irrigation and markets
**Answer: a) roads, law and an empire's reach** — That is what this lesson teaches.
