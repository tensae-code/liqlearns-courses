# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the double turn of Rome's story?
**Answer:** Greece gave Rome a culture; Rome gave that culture roads, law and an empire's reach, and carried it further.

**2.** What did Rome's roads make possible?
**Answer:** Armies, traders and laws could travel and bind city to city across a vast land.
