# The Origin of Human Society: Hunters and First Farmers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things fire gave the hunters. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: warmth, light, protection against wild animals, cooked food.

**2.** Where does the book place the beginnings of agriculture as part of civilisation? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In the Mesopotamia region and in Egypt.
