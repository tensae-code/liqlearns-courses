# The Origin of Human Society: Hunters and First Farmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Rock Age hunters develop, besides weapons and tools? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the mastery of fire  b) writing  c) iron ploughs  d) stone temples
**Answer: a) the mastery of fire** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What replaced camps when farming began? (Sub lesson 2)
a) villages — people settled in one place  b) larger hunting bands  c) walled empires at once  d) nothing changed
**Answer: a) villages — people settled in one place** — That is what this lesson teaches.
