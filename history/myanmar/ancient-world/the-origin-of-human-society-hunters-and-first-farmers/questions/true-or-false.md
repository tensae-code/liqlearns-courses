# The Origin of Human Society: Hunters and First Farmers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The hunters' story is told in three stages: early, middle and late Rock Age. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Agriculture arrived in a single day and changed everything at once. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The book's word is gradually — farming became part of human civilisation step by step.
