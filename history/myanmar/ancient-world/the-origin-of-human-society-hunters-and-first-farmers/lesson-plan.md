# Lesson plan — The Origin of Human Society: Hunters and First Farmers

**Course:** History — Myanmar · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Origin of Human Society: Hunters and First Farmers
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First People of the World: Hunters, Farmers and the River Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human society begins with the hunters — bands of the early, middle and late Rock Age who developed weapons and tools and mastered fire — and turns when farming begins: planting replaces chasing, villages replace camps, and agriculture gradually becomes part of human civilisation, in the Mesopotamia region and Egypt.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First People of the World: Hunters, Farmers and the River Kingdoms — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Hunters: weapons, tools and fire** — Early, middle and late Rock Age hunters develop weapons and tools and master fire — the first technologies of the human story.
2. **Agriculture and country life** — Agriculture gradually becomes part of human civilisation, in the Mesopotamia region and Egypt — planting replaces chasing, villages replace camps.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
