# Myanmar Prehistory: Rock, Bronze and Metal Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three ages of Myanmar's prehistory, in order.
**Answer:** The Rock Age, the Bronze Age and the Metal Age.

**2.** How did Myanmar's first people live with their environment through these ages?
**Answer:** In harmony with it — the rivers, forests and seasons gave what they needed, and their growing power grew inside that world.
