# Myanmar Prehistory: Rock, Bronze and Metal Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In Myanmar's prehistory, what did people of the Rock Age make their tools from?
a) stone, shaped by knapping  b) cast bronze  c) worked iron  d) steel
**Answer: a) stone, shaped by knapping** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What new power did the Bronze Age bring?
a) metal could be melted and cast into moulds, keeping a sharper edge than stone  b) people stopped using tools  c) iron ploughs appeared at once  d) cities were built immediately
**Answer: a) metal could be melted and cast into moulds, keeping a sharper edge than stone** — That is what this lesson teaches.
