# Myanmar Prehistory: Rock, Bronze and Metal Ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The three ages of Myanmar's prehistory are named for the best material people could work.
**Answer: True**

**2.** People of the Metal Age were weaker over nature than people of the Rock Age.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: each new material bought people new power over nature.
