# Myanmar Prehistory: Rock, Bronze and Metal Ages — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — in Myanmar's prehistory, people's technology climbed step by step — from the Rock Age, when they knapped stone, through the Bronze Age, when they cast bronze, to the Metal Age, when they worked iron — and each new material bought them new power over nature, while they lived in harmony with their environment — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
