# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

**Course:** History — Myanmar · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Great Ancient Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ancient Greece was not one kingdom but many small, proud city-states, whose economy, religion and social structures — their trading, their worship and their arguing — wove them into one shared Greek civilisation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Great Ancient Civilisations — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## If the learner is stuck
- Looks for the one king who ruled all Greece → there was none — Greece was many self-ruling city-states, made one civilisation by shared trade, worship and argument.
- Go back to the one thing: ancient Greece was not one kingdom but many small, proud city-states, whose economy, religion and social structures — their trading, their worship and their arguing — wove them into one shared Greek civilisation. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
