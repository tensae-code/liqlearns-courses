# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What three things did the Greek city-states do that wove them into a shared culture?
**Answer:** They traded, worshipped and argued their way into a shared culture.

**2.** Why could a Greek travelling to another city feel on familiar ground?
**Answer:** Because the same gods were worshipped and the same festivals kept across the city-states.
