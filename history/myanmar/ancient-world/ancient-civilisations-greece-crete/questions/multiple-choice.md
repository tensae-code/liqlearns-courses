# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was ancient Greece made of?
a) many small, proud city-states, each ruling itself  b) one single kingdom  c) four river empires  d) a chain of farming villages only
**Answer: a) many small, proud city-states, each ruling itself** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Through which three windows does the book study Greek civilisation?
a) its economy, its religion and its social structures  b) its kings, queens and crowns  c) its rivers, deserts and mountains  d) its armies only
**Answer: a) its economy, its religion and its social structures** — That is what this lesson teaches.
