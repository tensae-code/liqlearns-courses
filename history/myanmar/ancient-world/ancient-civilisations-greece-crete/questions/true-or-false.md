# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Greek city-states were tied together by trade across the sea.
**Answer: True**

**2.** One king ruled all of ancient Greece and made it one civilisation.
**Answer: False** — No single throne made Greece one — the sharing of trade, worship and argument did.
