# History — Myanmar course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Myanmar's
own — built from Myanmar's Grade 6 *Teacher Guide —
History*, in English with Myanmar glossary terms. Inside
the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**:
the titles below sit inside Levels 1–3, in path order —
what the learner proves, step by step. The grade the book
comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: the year is split in
two arcs — Myanmar's own past first (prehistory, the Pyu
city-states, Bagan), then world history (hunters, first
farmers, Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece, Rome, China, India).
That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced —
a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — Myanmar's own past** (Teacher Guide — History —
Grade 6, Chapters 1–3):

- `ancient-world/` — Levels 1–3, Myanmar's ancient past and
  the ancient world in one title folder, in path order.
  Myanmar Prehistory: Rock, Bronze and Metal Ages (the slow
  climb of technology, and life in harmony with the
  environment) · The Pyu City-States of Ancient Myanmar
  (how cities emerge, then the cities one by one —
  Beikthano, Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi,
  Waitharli) · The Bagan Empire (how it emerged and became
  powerful; its economy, society, literature and
  architecture, judged critically).

**Level 2 — the first people of the world: hunters, farmers
and the river kingdoms** (Chapters 4.1–4.2.2), in
`ancient-world/` and `africa-to-1500/`, in path order:

- The Origin of Human Society: Hunters and First Farmers
  (the Rock Age hunters and their mastery of fire; then
  agriculture and country life in the Mesopotamia region
  and Egypt) · Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia (four
  ages between two rivers — Sumerian, Babylonian, Assyrian,
  Chaldean) · Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom (a river's
  flood become a calendar, a granary and a kingdom), in
  `africa-to-1500/`.

**Level 3 — the great ancient civilisations** (Chapters
4.3.1–4.3.4), in `ancient-world/`, in path order:

- Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete (the economy,
  religion and social structures of the Greek city-states)
  · Ancient Civilisations: Rome (what Rome built, and what
  it learned from Greek culture) · Ancient Civilisations:
  China (born on the Yellow River, renewing itself for four
  thousand years) · Ancient Civilisations: India (its gods
  and scripts travelling with its traders as far as Angkor).
