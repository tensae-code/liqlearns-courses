# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Nile's flood become for Egypt?
a) a calendar, a granary and a kingdom  b) a desert and a wall  c) a sea road to Greece  d) nothing — it was only a danger
**Answer: a) a calendar, a granary and a kingdom** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What kind of agriculture does the book teach for Egypt?
a) plough agriculture on the Nile  b) hunting on the plains  c) farming without water  d) sea fishing only
**Answer: a) plough agriculture on the Nile** — That is what this lesson teaches.
