# Lesson plan — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

**Course:** History — Myanmar · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The First People of the World: Hunters, Farmers and the River Kingdoms (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in Egypt, the Nile's flood became a calendar, a granary and a kingdom: plough agriculture on the river's gift of water and soil carried a whole civilisation — its culture and its technology — on one river.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The First People of the World: Hunters, Farmers and the River Kingdoms — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. - Thinks Egypt's kingdom was built despite the river → the kingdom was built on the river: the Nile's flood became its calendar, its granary and its kingdom.
- Go back to the one thing: in Egypt, the Nile's flood became a calendar, a granary and a kingdom: plough agriculture on the river's gift of water and soil carried a whole civilisation — its culture and its technology — on one river. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
