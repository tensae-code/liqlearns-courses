# Hands-on — Egyptian Civilisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One river, one kingdom, three thousand years. Picture a green ribbon of fields with desert on both sides, great stone pyramids on the skyline, and a king who is also a god. Say out loud: "The Nile made Egypt."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Beside Mesopotamia stand the other two basins of the First Age in Turkey's book: pharaonic Egypt on the Nile, a kingdom held together by its river and its god-king, and the Hittite world of Anatolia, an empire on the plateau the students themselves live on. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
