# Lesson plan — Egyptian Civilisation

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Egyptian Civilisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Beside Mesopotamia stand the other two basins of the First Age in Turkey's book: pharaonic Egypt on the Nile, a kingdom held together by its river and its god-king, and the Hittite world of Anatolia, an empire on the plateau the students themselves live on.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (recommended, never forced).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Egypt and Mesopotamia are the same place → neighbours, not twins: Mesopotamia sits between two rivers (Tigris and Euphrates); Egypt hangs on one river, the Nile — and Anatolia's Hittites ruled a plateau, not a river valley.
- Go back to the one thing: Beside Mesopotamia stand the other two basins of the First Age in Turkey's book: pharaonic Egypt on the Nile, a kingdom held together by its river and its god-king, and the Hittite world of Anatolia, an empire on the plateau the students themselves live on. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
