# Video script — Egyptian Civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The first age had more basins than one: Egypt on the Nile, and the Hittites on Anatolia's plateau.
Fly west from the two rivers. One river now — the Nile — a green ribbon through desert. On its banks: a kingdom ruled by a pharaoh, a king who is also a god. Pyramids on the skyline, built to outlast ages. The flood comes every year, the mud feeds the fields, the kingdom endures for thousands of years.
Now fly north, up onto a high plateau: Anatolia. Fortified cities, chariot armies — the Hittite empire, strong enough to face Egypt as an equal and end a war with a treaty.
Three first worlds side by side: two rivers, one river, one plateau. The map of the first age is complete.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
