# Egyptian Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What river is Egypt's civilisation strung along?
a) The Tigris  b) The Euphrates  c) The Nile  d) Two rivers
**Answer: c) The Nile** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** The Hittite world stood where?
a) On the Nile delta  b) On the high plateau of Anatolia  c) In the south of Mesopotamia  d) Across the sea
**Answer: b) On the high plateau of Anatolia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
