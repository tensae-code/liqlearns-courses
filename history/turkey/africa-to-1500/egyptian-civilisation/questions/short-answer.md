# Egyptian Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the Nile's yearly flood give Egypt?
**Answer:** Rich mud laid on the fields — the desert blooming in a green ribbon of farms that could feed a kingdom.

**2.** How did the Hittites show they were Egypt's equals?
**Answer:** They met Egypt as an equal power — fighting it and ending the war with a famous negotiated peace treaty.
