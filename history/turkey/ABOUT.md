# About the History — Turkey course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Ministry of National Education (MEB) books. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us,
the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are
the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Turkey inside the History folder (one
course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what
they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a
recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school
grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`, as provenance. History is taught as
stories: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

The course as built has **six levels**, derived from the
books' own progression (see CURRICULUM.md for the derivation):

1. **Level 1 — First Steps: The Republic Remembered in
   Poems.** National memory as special-days poems for
   seven-year-olds (Grade 2 book).
2. **Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep
   Past.** The Grade 9 book's craft and first civilisations.
3. **Level 3 — The Medieval World and the Turks of the
   Steppe.** Medieval states, trade, and the Turks' entrance.
4. **Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam.**
   Islam's rise, the caliphates, the golden age, the Turks'
   acceptance of Islam, the Great Seljuks.
5. **Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of
   Independence.** The Grade 12 book's first movement:
   founder, measured empire, Great War, National Struggle.
6. **Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the
   World.** The reforms, the interwar diplomacy, the Second
   World War, the Western anchor, the Cold War decades, and
   the post-Cold War threshold.

The course's 40 lessons all stand on **student books** and
count in the acceptance reckoning below.

## The way of the books (their own shape, preserved)

**Hayat Bilgisi, Grade 2** — before any history course
exists, the state teaches national memory to seven-year-olds
as poems for the year's special days — Republic Day, Atatürk
Week, July 15 — plus the daily Student Oath (Andımız)
printed at the front of the book. *(Student book.)*

**Tarih, Grade 9** — world history is told with the Turks
walked through it: prehistory and the first civilisations,
then the medieval world, then a whole unit on the Turks of
the first and middle ages (steppe, kut, the Orhun stones),
before Islam's birth and the Turks' own entry into Islam
close the book with the Seljuks. *(Student book.)*

**İnkılap Tarihi, Grade 12** — the final-year course is the
republic's own biography, told in eight units from the dying
Ottoman world to the 21st century's threshold: one man's
life opens it (Mustafa Kemal's schooling and soldiering),
and nearly every unit is measured against his principles.
*(Student book.)*
