# History — Turkey course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Turkey inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school
grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Turkey's books in hand are three,
and their own order is the ladder. The earliest book teaches
national memory before it teaches history at all — the Grade 2
life-studies book's special-days poems (Level 1). The Grade 9
*Tarih* then runs the long survey the state gives its
fourteen-year-olds: the historian's craft and the deep past
(Level 2), the medieval world with the Turks' steppe entrance
(Level 3), and Islam's rise with the Turks' entry into it,
closing on the Seljuks (Level 4). The Grade 12 *İnkılap
Tarihi* is the Republic's own biography in two movements: the
empire's end and the War of Independence (Level 5), and the
Republic remade and set in the world, from the six principles
to the post-Cold War threshold (Level 6). The ladder is a
recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Ministry of National Education books — except the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the combined-book contents
map, and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps → First Steps: The Republic Remembered in
   Poems** — before any history course exists, the state
   teaches national memory to seven-year-olds as poems:
   Republic Day (29 October), Atatürk Week and the 09:05
   moment, and 15 July, Democracy and National Unity Day.
   1 lesson, in one title: `first-steps/` (1 lesson).

2. **First Steps / Ancient World / Africa to 1500 → First
   Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past** — what history
   is, dating the past, and who studies prehistory; then the
   invention of writing, Mesopotamia between the rivers,
   Egypt on the Nile with Anatolia's Hittites, early
   migrations, the first states and the first law codes.
   9 lessons, in three titles: `first-steps/` (3 lessons),
   `ancient-world/` (5 lessons), `africa-to-1500/`
   (1 lesson).

3. **Ancient World → The Medieval World and the Turks of the
   Steppe** — the medieval world's states and empires and
   their armies, its trade from farm to caravan; then the
   first Turks of the steppe, the Göktürk and Uyghur
   khaganates and the ruler's kut, and the Migration of
   Peoples that helped end Rome. 6 lessons, in one title:
   `ancient-world/` (6 lessons).

4. **Ancient World → Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam**
   — Arabia on Islam's eve; the rise and expansion of Islam
   (Badr, Uhud, the Trench, Hudaybiyya, the Four Caliphs);
   the Umayyad dynasty and the Abbasids who first hired the
   Turks; the golden age of science; the Turks' acceptance of
   Islam from Talas to the Karakhanids; and the Great Seljuk
   Empire and its machine. 7 lessons, in one title:
   `ancient-world/` (7 lessons).

5. **Modern World / Ancient World → The Empire's End and the
   War of Independence** — Mustafa Kemal's schooling and
   service; the Ottoman state measured and found wanting; the
   Great War gamble and the fronts, Gallipoli among them;
   then the National Struggle prepared (Mondros, the
   congresses, the Assembly, Sèvres refused) and fought on
   three fronts to 30 August 1922, and the peace kept at
   Lausanne. 7 lessons, in two titles: `modern-world/`
   (6 lessons), `ancient-world/` (1 lesson).

6. **Modern World → The Republic Remade and Turkey in the
   World** — the six principles and the reforms (the state,
   the alphabet, the clock, the surname, women's rights, the
   economy and health); 'peace at home, peace in the world'
   between the wars, to Montreux and Atatürk's death; the
   Second World War stayed out of, and its results; the
   ballot box of 1950 and the Western anchor (Council of
   Europe, Korea, NATO); the Cold War's front line; the
   Middle East wars; the coups-and-Cyprus decades; and the
   post-Cold War threshold. 10 lessons, in one title:
   `modern-world/` (10 lessons).

**Totals: 40 lessons** (Level 1: 1 · Level 2: 9 · Level 3: 6 ·
Level 4: 7 · Level 5: 7 · Level 6: 10 — 45 placements stocked,
every lesson on a student book).
