# History — Turkey course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Turkey's own — built from Turkey's Grade 2 *Hayat Bilgisi* (Book 1), Grade 9 *Tarih* and Grade 12 *İnkılap Tarihi ve Atatürkçülük* student books (all Turkish, Ministry of National Education). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grade each book comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': memory first, as poems for the youngest; then the Grade 9 survey from what history is to the Seljuks; then the Grade 12 biography of the Republic, from the founder's schooling to the twenty-first century's threshold. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
