# The Invention of Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Writing grew step by step, out of the need to make counts and words stay.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Hieroglyphs were the first alphabet.
**Answer: False** — hieroglyphs are Egypt's picture-signs; the alphabet — signs for single sounds — came as the later, simpler step.
