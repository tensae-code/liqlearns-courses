# The Invention of Writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the steps writing grew through, in order.
**Answer:** Tokens and marks for counting; picture-signs standing for things and words; cuneiform in Mesopotamia and hieroglyphs in Egypt; and at last the alphabet, signs for single sounds.

**2.** Why does history proper begin with writing?
**Answer:** Because from then on the past leaves documents — its own words — for historians to read, instead of only objects to dig up.
