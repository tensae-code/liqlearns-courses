# The Invention of Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Sumerians press into wet clay to write?
a) Alphabet letters  b) Wedge-shaped cuneiform signs  c) Painted pictures only  d) Coins
**Answer: b) Wedge-shaped cuneiform signs** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What makes the alphabet such a great step?
a) It uses pictures of animals  b) It needs hundreds of signs  c) A small set of signs, one per sound, can write any word  d) It is carved only in stone
**Answer: c) A small set of signs, one per sound, can write any word** — That is what this lesson teaches.
