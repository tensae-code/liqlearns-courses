# Video script — The Invention of Writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Writing grew in steps — tokens, pictures, cuneiform, hieroglyphs, and at last the alphabet — and with it, history proper begins.
Watch a hand count sheep with little clay tokens, one token for one sheep. The count must not be forgotten — that need is writing's mother.
Now the tokens become pictures, and the pictures become signs. In Mesopotamia, a reed presses wedges into clay: cuneiform. In Egypt, painted signs cover a temple wall: hieroglyphs.
Hundreds of signs — hard to learn. Then the breakthrough: a small set of signs, one for each sound. The alphabet. Any word, writable. Anyone, able to learn it.
From that day, the past speaks for itself. Documents begin — and so does history.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
