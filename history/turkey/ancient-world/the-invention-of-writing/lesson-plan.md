# Lesson plan — The Invention of Writing

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Invention of Writing
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History proper begins when writing is invented: from tokens and picture-signs to Sumerian cuneiform pressed in clay, Egyptian hieroglyphs, and at last the alphabet — signs for sounds that anyone can learn.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- **Who Studies Prehistory** (`first-steps/who-studies-prehistory/`) is recommended first — it ends where this lesson begins: at the first written sign (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History proper begins when writing is invented: from tokens and picture-signs to Sumerian cuneiform pressed in clay, Egyptian hieroglyphs, and at last the alphabet — signs for sounds that anyone can learn.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the marks you make yourself — a shopping list, a name scratched on your book — small signs doing the same job: making words stay.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson told properly (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks writing appeared all at once → it grew: tokens and marks, then picture-signs, then cuneiform and hieroglyphs, then the alphabet — each step answering a need of the step before.
- Go back to the one thing: History proper begins when writing is invented: from tokens and picture-signs to Sumerian cuneiform pressed in clay, Egyptian hieroglyphs, and at last the alphabet — signs for sounds that anyone can learn. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
