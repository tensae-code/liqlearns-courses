# Early Migrations of Peoples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three pushes this lesson gives for migration.
**Answer:** Climate turning (failed rains, drying pastures), hunger, and pressure from stronger neighbours.

**2.** Why does the book call migration an engine of the ancient world?
**Answer:** Because walking peoples carried civilisation's pieces — tools, seeds, skills and languages — across the world, mixing peoples and redrawing the map before empires and trade routes did.
