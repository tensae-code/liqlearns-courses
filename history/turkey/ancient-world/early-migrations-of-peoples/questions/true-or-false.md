# Early Migrations of Peoples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** One people's migration could push the next people onward, in a chain.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Migration only ever weakened the lands people moved into.
**Answer: False** — it delivered whole ways of life — tools, seeds, farming and languages arrived with the newcomers.
