# Early Migrations of Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is one of the pushes that set early peoples moving?
a) Boredom  b) Climate turning — rains failing and pastures drying  c) Orders from historians  d) Love of maps
**Answer: b) Climate turning — rains failing and pastures drying** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What travels with a migrating people?
a) Nothing — they leave everything  b) Only their weapons  c) Tools, seeds, animals, crafts and their languages  d) Written books
**Answer: c) Tools, seeds, animals, crafts and their languages** — That is what this lesson teaches.
