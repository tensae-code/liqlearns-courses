# Video script — Early Migrations of Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Migration is the engine of the ancient world — peoples move, and tools, seeds and languages move with them.
Look at the map and ask why anyone would leave home. Watch the rains fail over a pasture. Watch a stronger people press in from the border. Hunger and pressure: the pushes that set peoples walking.
Now walk with one people. On their backs and in their carts: tools, seed grain, animals, crafts — and in their mouths, their language. Where they stop, all of it is planted in new soil.
Trace the arrows across the map: skills spreading, peoples mixing, borders redrawn — long before any empire. The engine of the ancient world was feet.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
