# The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's story beat, in your own words?
**Answer:** The empire that would later convert the Turks first hired them: Abbasid Baghdad leaned on Turkish soldiers, and Turks rose inside the empire to its very centre.

**2.** What problem grew from the caliphs' reliance on Turkish guards?
**Answer:** The sword-arm gained a will of its own — Turkish commanders became powers the caliphs themselves found hard to command.
