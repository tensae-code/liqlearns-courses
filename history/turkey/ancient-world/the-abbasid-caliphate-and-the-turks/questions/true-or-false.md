# The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Turkish officers rose to commands, governorships and court power inside the Abbasid state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Turks first met Islam as a conquered people.
**Answer: False** — they met it first as hired soldiers of the caliphate — service came before conversion.
