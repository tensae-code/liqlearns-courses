# Video script — The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Baghdad's caliphate leaned on Turkish soldiers — and the Turks rose inside the empire.
Watch the capital move east: Damascus fades, Baghdad rises — round city, great court, an empire facing Persia and the steppe.
Now watch the recruiting: riders coming in from the steppe frontier, hired as guards and regiments. The caliph wants soldiers outside every faction — and the Turk is the finest horseman of the age.
Follow those riders' grandsons: commands, governorships, court power. The hired are rising; the sword-arm has a will of its own.
The empire that will convert the Turks first hires them. The meeting begins with a soldier's contract.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
