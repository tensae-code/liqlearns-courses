# The Ottoman State at the Start of the Twentieth Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give one finding from each of the lesson's three lights.
**Answer:** Political: national movements and great-power pressure strain the state, reforms stall. Social: a burdened mosaic of peoples, heavy taxes, old ways arguing with new ideas. Economic: foreign debt, capitulations and foreign capital binding its markets, industry lagging behind Europe.

**2.** Why does the book place this measure before the war story?
**Answer:** Because the war's outcome grows out of it: the empire that gambles in 1914 is already strained, burdened and dependent — the report explains the fall before the fall is told.
