# The Ottoman State at the Start of the Twentieth Century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In how many lights does the book measure the late Ottoman state?
a) One — military  b) Two — land and sea  c) Three — political, social and economic  d) Four — by continent
**Answer: c) Three — political, social and economic** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What bound the empire's own markets, in the economic measure?
a) Guild rules  b) Foreign capital and capitulations — privileges for foreign merchants  c) The iqta system  d) Seljuk law
**Answer: b) Foreign capital and capitulations — privileges for foreign merchants** — That is what this lesson teaches.
