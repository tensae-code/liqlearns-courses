# Hands-on — The Ottoman State at the Start of the Twentieth Century

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Six hundred years old. Ruler of three continents within living memory. And yet — open its ledgers, walk its provinces, count its debts. Say out loud: "A great state, found wanting."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Ottoman state that entered the twentieth century — six hundred years old — is measured on every page and found wanting: politically strained, socially burdened and economically dependent, an empire in its last decades. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
