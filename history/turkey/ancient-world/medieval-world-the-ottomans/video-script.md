# Video script — The Ottoman State at the Start of the Twentieth Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Ottoman state entered the twentieth century measured — and found wanting on every page.
Hold up the empire's portrait: six hundred years old, three continents within its memory. Now inspect it in three lights.
Political light: national movements pulling peoples away, great powers pressing in, reforms stalling. Social light: a mosaic of peoples, taxed peasants, towns arguing between old ways and new ideas. Economic light: foreign debts, foreign privileges in its own markets, industry lagging behind Europe.
That is the report. Everything after it in this book — the war, the fronts, the end — grows out of these five pages.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
