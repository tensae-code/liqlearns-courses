# The Migration of Peoples: The Huns and the Fall of Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What set the Migration of Peoples in motion?
a) A Roman law  b) The Huns' westward push out of the steppe  c) A famine in Egypt  d) The building of Constantinople
**Answer: b) The Huns' westward push out of the steppe** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Germanic peoples do under the Huns' pressure?
a) Stayed still  b) Built a wall  c) Moved across Rome's frontier into the empire's lands  d) Sailed away
**Answer: c) Moved across Rome's frontier into the empire's lands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
