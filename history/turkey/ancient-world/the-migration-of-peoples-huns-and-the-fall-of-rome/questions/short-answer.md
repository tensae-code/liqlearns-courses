# The Migration of Peoples: The Huns and the Fall of Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did a steppe migration help end the Roman Empire in the west?
**Answer:** The Huns' push drove the Germanic peoples across Rome's frontiers; a generation of peoples moving into its lands strained and broke the already divided empire's defences and authority, province by province.

**2.** What is the Turkish name the book gives this movement, and what does the movement close?
**Answer:** Kavimler Göçü — the Migration of Peoples; it closes the ancient world in Europe.
