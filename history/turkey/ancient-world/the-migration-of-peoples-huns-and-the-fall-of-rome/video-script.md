# Video script — The Migration of Peoples: The Huns and the Fall of Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Huns pushed west, the Germanic peoples moved, and Rome's western empire fell — the Migration of Peoples.
Trace the arrows on the map. First arrow, from deep in Asia: the Huns riding west. Second arrows: the Germanic peoples along Rome's frontier, pressed, moving — across the frontier, into the empire. Third: more peoples behind them, a chain of movement across a continent.
Now watch Rome strain: frontiers breached not once but for a generation; provinces slipping; at last the western emperor gone — 476, the date the books give the fall.
One push on the steppe, two continents moved, an age closed. Kavimler Göçü — the Migration of Peoples.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
