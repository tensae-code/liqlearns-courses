# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture a dry land — and through it, two great rivers. Where the rivers flood, the earth turns green and grain grows thick. Say out loud: "Between the rivers, the first cities rose."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, where two rivers flood, the first cities, kings and law codes grew — Sumer, Akkad, Babylon and Assyria in turn ruling the land between the rivers. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
