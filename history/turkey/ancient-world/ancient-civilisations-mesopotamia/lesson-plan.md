# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, where two rivers flood, the first cities, kings and law codes grew — Sumer, Akkad, Babylon and Assyria in turn ruling the land between the rivers.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, where two rivers flood, the first cities, kings and law codes grew — Sumer, Akkad, Babylon and Assyria in turn ruling the land between the rivers.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a garden that only grows where someone carries water to it — the rivers made the land rich, and organising the water made the cities.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up the four powers' order → the river tells it: Sumer in the south first, then Akkad uniting, Babylon the famous capital, Assyria the northern empire last.
- Go back to the one thing: Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, where two rivers flood, the first cities, kings and law codes grew — Sumer, Akkad, Babylon and Assyria in turn ruling the land between the rivers. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
