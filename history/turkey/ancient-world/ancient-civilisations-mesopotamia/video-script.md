# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, the first cities, kings and codes grew.
Look at the map: dry land, and two rivers cutting through it. Where the rivers flood, the fields turn green. Grain gathers people; people gather into villages; villages grow into the first cities on earth.
First, in the south: Sumer — city-states, temples, kings, scribes pressing cuneiform. Then Akkad draws the cities under one rule. Then Babylon, the great capital of a famous law-giving king. Then Assyria in the north, an empire of soldiers.
Four powers, one river land. The city, the code and the written word all begin here — in the basin between the rivers.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
