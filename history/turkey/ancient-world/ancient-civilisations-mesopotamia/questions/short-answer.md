# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four powers of Mesopotamia in the order the lesson gives.
**Answer:** Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, Assyria.

**2.** Why is Mesopotamia called a civilisation basin?
**Answer:** Because civilisation pooled and deepened there — cities, kingship, writing and law grew on its flood-fed fields and spread outward from it.
