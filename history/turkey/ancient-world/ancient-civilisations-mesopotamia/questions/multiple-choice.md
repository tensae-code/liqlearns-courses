# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does 'Mesopotamia' mean?
a) Land of the pyramids  b) The land between the rivers  c) The northern empire  d) City of scribes
**Answer: b) The land between the rivers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which people built the first cities in the south of the basin?
a) The Assyrians  b) The Babylonians  c) The Sumerians  d) The Akkadians
**Answer: c) The Sumerians** — That is what this lesson teaches.
