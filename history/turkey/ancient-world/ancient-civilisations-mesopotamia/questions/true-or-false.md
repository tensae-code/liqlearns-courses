# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Tigris and Euphrates' floods made farming able to feed many people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Assyria was the first power of Mesopotamia.
**Answer: False** — Sumer came first; Assyria, the northern military empire, came last of the four.
