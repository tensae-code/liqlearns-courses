# Hands-on — Trade in the Medieval World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A camel caravan is crossing a desert with silk and spices. At the same moment, a ship is loading grain in a harbour, and a weaver is joining his guild in a walled town. Say out loud: "Trade stitched the medieval world together."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Medieval farming fed growing towns, towns bred craftsmen organised in guilds, and caravans and ships on the great trade routes stitched the whole medieval world together — richer than its wars divided it. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
