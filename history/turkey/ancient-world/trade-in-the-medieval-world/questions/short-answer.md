# Trade in the Medieval World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the lesson's three steps in order.
**Answer:** Agriculture produces a surplus; freed hands gather in towns, where crafts organise in guilds; the great trade routes — caravans and ships — carry goods between all the states.

**2.** What travelled the trade routes besides goods?
**Answer:** Coins, news and ideas travelled the same roads.
