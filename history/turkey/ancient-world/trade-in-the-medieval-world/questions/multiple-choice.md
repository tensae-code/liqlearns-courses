# Trade in the Medieval World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the seed from which towns and trade grow, in this lesson?
a) War booty  b) The farm surplus — food beyond what the countryside eats  c) Kings' treasure  d) Guild rules
**Answer: b) The farm surplus — food beyond what the countryside eats** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is a guild?
a) A kind of ship  b) A tax  c) An organisation of craftsmen of one trade in a town  d) A caravan leader
**Answer: c) An organisation of craftsmen of one trade in a town** — That is what this lesson teaches.
