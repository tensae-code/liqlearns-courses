# Roman Law, Roads and Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What exactly did later ages inherit from Roman law?
**Answer:** The habit of system — law as an ordered whole with definitions, distinctions and reasons — and sharpened concepts such as ownership, contract, inheritance and legal personhood.

**2.** Name two carriers that kept Roman law alive in the medieval world.
**Answer:** Any two of: Byzantium's codification; church law; local court practice and custom; the medieval jurists who studied the Roman texts again.
