# Roman Law, Roads and Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Medieval jurists studied Roman law as a model of what law could be.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Roman law vanished completely the moment the western empire fell.
**Answer: False** — it survived in Byzantium's codification, in church law and court practice, and was later taken up again by jurists.
