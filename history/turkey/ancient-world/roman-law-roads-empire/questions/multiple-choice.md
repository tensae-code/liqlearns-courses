# Roman Law, Roads and Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What of Rome's kept governing after the western empire fell?
a) Its army  b) Its emperors  c) Its law  d) Its roads alone
**Answer: c) Its law** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where was Roman law kept and codified in the east?
a) In Persia  b) In Byzantium  c) On the steppe  d) In the guilds
**Answer: b) In Byzantium** — That is what this lesson teaches.
