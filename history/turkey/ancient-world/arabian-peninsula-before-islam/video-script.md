# Video script — The Arabian Peninsula Before Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. On Islam's eve: two tired empires, and a trading peninsula of tribes between them.
Look at the map. North-west: Byzantium. East: Sassanid Persia. Watch their long wars flicker along the border — decades of them — until both giants are worn thin.
Now drop south, into Arabia: desert, oases, caravan tracks stitching the peninsula together. Tribes on the move with their herds; trading towns loading camels; and Mecca, where pilgrims and merchants gather from every direction.
No empire rules here. But everyone passes through, and news travels with the caravans. The stage is set — two tired empires, a waiting peninsula.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
