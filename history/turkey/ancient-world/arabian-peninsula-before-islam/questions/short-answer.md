# The Arabian Peninsula Before Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are the three parts of the stage this lesson builds?
**Answer:** Two tired empires — Byzantium and Sassanid Persia; a trading peninsula — Arabia; and its tribal peoples with their caravan towns, Mecca among them.

**2.** Why does the lesson say the peninsula was 'rich in movement, poetry and news'?
**Answer:** Because caravans and pilgrims constantly crossed it, carrying goods and stories between the empires and the sea — ideas could travel fast there.
