# The Arabian Peninsula Before Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How does the book describe the two great empires on Islam's eve?
a) Young and rising  b) Tired — drained by long wars against each other  c) United as allies  d) Ruled from Mecca
**Answer: b) Tired — drained by long wars against each other** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How was Arabia itself organised?
a) As one empire  b) As a feudal kingdom  c) In tribes, with caravan trading towns  d) As Roman provinces
**Answer: c) In tribes, with caravan trading towns** — That is what this lesson teaches.
