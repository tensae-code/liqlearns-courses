# Hands-on — The Arabian Peninsula Before Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two great empires, worn out from fighting each other. Between them, a desert peninsula of tribes, markets and caravans — and a city where traders gather from every direction. Say out loud: "The stage is set."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On the eve of Islam, Arabia was a trading peninsula of tribes and caravan cities, squeezed between two tired empires — Byzantium and Sassanid Persia — and the world was waiting for a prophet. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
