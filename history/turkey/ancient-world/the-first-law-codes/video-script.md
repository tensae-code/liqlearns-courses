# Video script — The First Law Codes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Law was written down — from Ur-Namma to Hammurabi's stone — and justice stopped being the strong man's mood.
Picture a judgement: a ruler deciding a quarrel from his chair, inventing the rule as he speaks. Tomorrow, the opposite rule. Nobody can argue — the law is his mood.
Now walk into a market square in Babylon. A tall stone stands there, covered in carved laws, the king at its top receiving them from the god of justice. Hammurabi's code. Before it, Ur-Namma's in Sumer — among the first ever written.
Anyone can have the stone read aloud. The rules are fixed, public, the same for judge and judged.
Justice became a stone everyone could read. The world never went back.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
