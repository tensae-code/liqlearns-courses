# The First Law Codes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does it matter that Hammurabi's laws were carved on a stone in public?
**Answer:** Because the rules became fixed and visible to all: anyone could have them read, and justice stopped depending on the ruler's mood of the day.

**2.** What stood at the top of Hammurabi's stone, in the carving?
**Answer:** The king shown receiving the law from the god of justice.
