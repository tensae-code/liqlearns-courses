# The First Law Codes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Whose code is carved on the famous tall stone set up in public?
a) Ur-Namma's  b) Hammurabi's  c) The pharaoh's  d) A Greek king's
**Answer: b) Hammurabi's** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the great change this lesson teaches?
a) Kings became kinder  b) Armies grew  c) Law became written and public — a stone everyone could read  d) Writing was invented
**Answer: c) Law became written and public — a stone everyone could read** — That is what this lesson teaches.
