# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In twenty years, a persecuted handful became a state; in thirty more, an empire.
Start in Mecca: a small band of believers, persecuted by the city's rulers. Watch them leave — the road to Medina, where the Prophet is invited to rule. A handful becomes a state.
Now the dated turns. 624, Badr: victory against the odds. 625, Uhud: the hard lesson. 627, the Trench: a ditch, a failed siege. 628, Hudaybiyya: a treaty — and the road opens. Mecca is won; Arabia unites.
Then the Four Caliphs ride out: Persia falls; Syria and Egypt leave Byzantium. Fifty years from a persecuted handful — an empire. Watch the map fill.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
