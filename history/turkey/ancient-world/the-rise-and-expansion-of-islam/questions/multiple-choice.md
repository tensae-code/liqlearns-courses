# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** At which battle did the small Muslim force win its famous victory against the odds?
a) Uhud  b) Badr (624)  c) The Trench  d) Hudaybiyya
**Answer: b) Badr (624)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was Hudaybiyya (628)?
a) A siege  b) A sea battle  c) A treaty — a truce that opened the road ahead  d) A city
**Answer: c) A treaty — a truce that opened the road ahead** — That is what this lesson teaches.
