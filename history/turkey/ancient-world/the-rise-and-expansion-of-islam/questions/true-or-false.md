# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Prophet's community became a state at Medina.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Persia survived the Four Caliphs' conquests intact.
**Answer: False** — Sassanid Persia fell entirely; Byzantium lost Syria, Egypt and more.
