# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the four dated turns in order, with what each was.
**Answer:** Badr (624), victory against the odds; Uhud (625), a hard lesson when the battle turned; the Trench (627), Medina's ditch defeating a siege; Hudaybiyya (628), a treaty that opened the road to Mecca's winning and Arabia's unity.

**2.** Who carried the expansion after the Prophet's death, and how far did it reach?
**Answer:** The Four Caliphs, his successors: in thirty years Persia fell and Byzantium lost Syria and Egypt — the Medina state became an empire from Persia to the Mediterranean.
