# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In twenty years a persecuted handful in Mecca became a state at Medina — through the battles and treaties of Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627) and Hudaybiyya (628) — and in thirty more, under the Four Caliphs, that state became an empire.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In twenty years a persecuted handful in Mecca became a state at Medina — through the battles and treaties of Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627) and Hudaybiyya (628) — and in thirty more, under the Four Caliphs, that state became an empire.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a small team that everyone expects to fold, surviving its hardest seasons, signing the right truce at the right moment — and a generation later, running the league.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up the four dated turns → the rhyme of the dates: Badr victory (624), Uhud lesson (625), Trench defence (627), Hudaybiyya treaty (628) — fight, lesson, ditch, pen.
- Thinks Hudaybiyya was a battle → it was a treaty, a truce — and its lesson is that the pen gained what the sword had not.
- Go back to the one thing: In twenty years a persecuted handful in Mecca became a state at Medina — through the battles and treaties of Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627) and Hudaybiyya (628) — and in thirty more, under the Four Caliphs, that state became an empire. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
