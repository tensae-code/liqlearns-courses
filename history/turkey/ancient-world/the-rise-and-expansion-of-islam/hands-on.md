# Hands-on — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A small group, driven out of its own city. Twenty years later it rules Arabia. Fifty years later, an empire. Say out loud: "A handful became a state; a state became an empire."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In twenty years a persecuted handful in Mecca became a state at Medina — through the battles and treaties of Badr (624), Uhud (625), the Trench (627) and Hudaybiyya (628) — and in thirty more, under the Four Caliphs, that state became an empire. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
