# Lesson plan — The Turks Accept Islam

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Turks Accept Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From the Talas encounter of 751 to the Karakhanids — the first Turkish Muslim state — the Turks accepted Islam over generations; and the faith changed the khagan's titles, the law and the city, while the steppe kept many of its own customs too.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Turks' acceptance of Islam** — The meeting begins, in the book's telling, beside a river: **Talas, in 751**, where Turkish peoples encountered the Arab armies of the expanding caliphate in Central Asia.
2. **Islam's effect on Turkish state and society** — What did the new faith change? This part walks the change through the state's own house.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Turks converted in a single day at Talas → Talas (751) is the encounter that opens the door; the acceptance itself is generations of soldiers, traders and tribes, crowned by states like the Karakhanids.
- Thinks conversion erased the old Turkish ways → the book's own balance: titles, law and the city changed — and the steppe kept many customs too. A meeting, not an erasure.
- Go back to the one thing: From the Talas encounter of 751 to the Karakhanids — the first Turkish Muslim state — the Turks accepted Islam over generations; and the faith changed the khagan's titles, the law and the city, while the steppe kept many of its own customs too. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
