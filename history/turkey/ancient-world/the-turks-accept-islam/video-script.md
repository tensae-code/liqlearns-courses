# Video script — The Turks Accept Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. From Talas in 751 to the Karakhanids, the Turks accepted Islam — and the faith and the steppe changed each other.
Start at a river in Central Asia, 751: Turkish peoples and Arab armies meet. Not a conversion — an encounter. A door opens.
Walk through it slowly, across generations: Turkish soldiers in Baghdad's service, traders on the routes, tribes professing the faith — until a whole state stands in the doorway: the Karakhanids, the first Turkish Muslim state.
Now look inside the changed house: new titles for the ruler, Islamic law in the courts, mosque and madrasa at the town's centre. And look again: the old customs still at the table — council, warfare, family ways.
A meeting, not an erasure. Steppe and Islam, joined for a thousand years.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
