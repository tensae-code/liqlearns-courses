# Hands-on — The Turks Accept Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two worlds meet beside a river in 751. One carries the steppe in its blood; the other carries a new faith and great cities. Over the next two hundred years, they become one story. Say out loud: "A thousand-year meeting begins at Talas."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From the Talas encounter of 751 to the Karakhanids — the first Turkish Muslim state — the Turks accepted Islam over generations; and the faith changed the khagan's titles, the law and the city, while the steppe kept many of its own customs too. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
