## Sub lesson 1 — The Turks' acceptance of Islam
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The meeting begins, in the book's telling, beside a river: **Talas, in 751**, where Turkish peoples encountered the Arab armies of the expanding caliphate in Central Asia. Not a conversion in a day — an **encounter**: the door opening between the steppe world and the Islamic world.

## Sub lesson 2 — Islam's effect on Turkish state and society
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: What did the new faith change? This part walks the change through the state's own house. The **khagan's titles** change: the ruler now styles himself with Muslim titles alongside the old, and his rule is preached as God's trust, not Heaven's alone. **Law** changes: Islamic law takes its place beside — and over — steppe custom in the cities and courts. And the **city** changes: mosque, madrasa and market square become the Turkish town's new centre, with Arabic and Persian words flowing into Turkish speech and writing.
