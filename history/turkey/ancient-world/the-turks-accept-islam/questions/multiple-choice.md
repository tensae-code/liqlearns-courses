# The Turks Accept Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where and when does the book's meeting of Turks and Islam begin?
a) At Baghdad, in 900  b) Beside the Talas river, in 751  c) At Damascus, in 661  d) In Anatolia, in 1071
**Answer: b) Beside the Talas river, in 751** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What did the steppe keep, even as Islam changed the state?
a) Nothing — all customs vanished  b) Its old titles only  c) Many of its own customs — in family life, warfare and the ruler's council  d) Its old religion entire
**Answer: c) Many of its own customs — in family life, warfare and the ruler's council** — That is what this lesson teaches.
