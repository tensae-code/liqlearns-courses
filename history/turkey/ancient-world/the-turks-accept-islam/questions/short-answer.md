# The Turks Accept Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How did the Turks come to accept Islam — in what ways, over how long?
**Answer:** Over generations: through the Talas encounter of 751, through service as the caliphate's soldiers, through trade and neighbourhood with Muslim states — tribe by tribe and state by state, crowned by the Karakhanids.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two things Islam changed in the Turkish state and society, and one thing it did not erase.
**Answer:** Changed: the ruler's titles (Muslim titles, rule as God's trust) and the law (Islamic law in cities and courts) — also the city itself, centred on mosque, madrasa and market. Not erased: steppe customs in family life, warfare and council.
