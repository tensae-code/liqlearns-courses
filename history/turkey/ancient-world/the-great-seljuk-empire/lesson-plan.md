# Lesson plan — The Great Seljuk Empire

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Great Seljuk Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Great Seljuks — a clan of Oghuz Turks who defeated the Ghaznavids at Dandanakan (1040) — built an empire from Central Asia to the Mediterranean and took up the caliphate's protection; and Nizam al-Mulk, its great vizier, built the machine that ran it — sultan, vizier, iqta — and the Nizamiyya madrasas that taught its servants.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the caliph still ruled the empire → the Seljuk bargain divides it: the caliph keeps spiritual authority; the sultan holds the power and protects the caliphate.
- Thinks iqta is a tax farmers pay → it is the other side of the coin: grants of land revenue given to soldiers and officials in exchange for service — how the empire pays its servants.
- Go back to the one thing: The Great Seljuks — a clan of Oghuz Turks who defeated the Ghaznavids at Dandanakan (1040) — built an empire from Central Asia to the Mediterranean and took up the caliphate's protection; and Nizam al-Mulk, its great vizier, built the machine that ran it — sultan, vizier, iqta — and the Nizamiyya madrasas that taught its servants. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
