# The Great Seljuk Empire — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Great Seljuk State, 1040–1157
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The **Seljuks** begin as one clan of the **Oghuz** Turks, pressing south from the steppe into the Islamic world's eastern lands. Their founding victory has a name and a date: **Dandanakan, 1040**, where the clan's horsemen defeated the **Ghaznavids** — the established Turkish-ruled empire of the region — and took its place in Khorasan.

## Sub lesson 2 — Government and society in the Great Seljuk State
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: An empire that size cannot be run from a saddle. This part opens the machine. At the top, the **sultan**; beneath him, the **vizier** — the chief minister who runs the administration day by day. The greatest of them gives the part its human face: **Nizam al-Mulk**, vizier for decades, organiser of the empire and author of its governing wisdom.
