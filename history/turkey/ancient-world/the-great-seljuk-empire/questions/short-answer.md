# The Great Seljuk Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How far did the Great Seljuk Empire reach, and between which dates does the book frame it?
**Answer:** From Central Asia to the Mediterranean — Persia, Iraq, Syria and Anatolia's door — framed 1040–1157.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who was Nizam al-Mulk, and what two things did he build?
**Answer:** The Seljuks' great vizier — chief minister for decades. He built the empire's administrative machine (sultan, vizier, iqta) and its schools, the Nizamiyya madrasas.
