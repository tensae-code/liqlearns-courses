# The Great Seljuk Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Seljuk sultan became the caliphate's protector, while the caliph kept spiritual authority.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Nizamiyya madrasas were military barracks.
**Answer: False** — they were great schools — a network training the judges, officials and scholars the empire ran on.
