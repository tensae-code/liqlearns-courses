# The Great Seljuk Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At which battle, in 1040, did the Seljuks defeat the Ghaznavids?
a) Talas  b) Dandanakan  c) Manzikert  d) Badr
**Answer: b) Dandanakan** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is an iqta?
a) A kind of madrasa  b) A grant of land's revenue given in exchange for service  c) The sultan's title  d) A siege engine
**Answer: b) A grant of land's revenue given in exchange for service** — That is what this lesson teaches.
