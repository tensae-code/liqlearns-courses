# Video script — The Great Seljuk Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Seljuks conquered an empire — and Nizam al-Mulk built the machine that ran it.
Start at Dandanakan, 1040: Oghuz horsemen, an empire's army broken, a clan standing in Khorasan's doorway. Follow them to Baghdad: the caliph receives their leader — spiritual authority on one side, the sultan's sword on the other. The caliphate has a protector.
Pull back to the full map: Central Asia to the Mediterranean, one Turkish dynasty over Islam's heartlands — the Great Seljuk Empire.
Now open the machine: sultan, then vizier — Nizam al-Mulk, the great organiser. Iqta grants paying soldiers from the land. And rising over Baghdad, the Nizamiyya madrasa, training the men who run it all.
Conquest made them great. Administration made them an empire.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
