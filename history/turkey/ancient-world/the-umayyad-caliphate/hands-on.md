# Hands-on — The Umayyad Caliphate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The first caliphs were chosen. Then a father handed the rule to his son — and nobody chose again for ninety years. Say out loud: "Election gave way to inheritance."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Under the Umayyads the caliphate became a dynasty: rule by election among the Prophet's companions gave way to rule by inheritance at Damascus, and the empire reached its greatest extent as a family monarchy. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
