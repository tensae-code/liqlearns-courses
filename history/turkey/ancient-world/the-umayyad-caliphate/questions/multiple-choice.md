# The Umayyad Caliphate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From which city did the Umayyad caliphs rule?
a) Medina  b) Baghdad  c) Damascus  d) Cordoba
**Answer: c) Damascus** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the great change of the Umayyad age?
a) The empire shrank  b) Rule by election gave way to rule by inheritance  c) The capital moved to Egypt  d) Trade ended
**Answer: b) Rule by election gave way to rule by inheritance** — That is what this lesson teaches.
