# The Umayyad Caliphate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did a man become caliph under the Four Caliphs, and how under the Umayyads?
**Answer:** The first caliphs were chosen by the community from among the Prophet's companions; under the Umayyads the office passed by inheritance, father to son, inside one family at Damascus.

**2.** How far did the empire reach at its Umayyad greatest extent?
**Answer:** From the borders of Central Asia and India in the east to Spain in the far west.
