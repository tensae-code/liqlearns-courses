# The Umayyad Caliphate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under the Umayyads the empire reached its greatest extent.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Everyone accepted that the caliphate should be one family's property.
**Answer: False** — many never accepted it, and that argument helped bring the dynasty down within a century.
