# Video script — The Umayyad Caliphate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. At Damascus, the caliphate became a dynasty — election gave way to inheritance.
Remember how the first caliphs were raised: chosen, from among the Prophet's companions. Now watch the change: one family, the Umayyads, takes the office — and a father hands it to his son.
The capital moves to Damascus. The empire stretches to its widest: from Central Asia's edge to Spain. Governors, Arabic administration, imperial coinage — a monarchy in all but name.
But hear the argument growing underneath: should the Prophet's succession be a family's property? That argument will bring this dynasty down within a hundred years.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
