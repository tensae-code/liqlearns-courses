# Lesson plan — The Umayyad Caliphate

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Umayyad Caliphate
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Under the Umayyads the caliphate became a dynasty: rule by election among the Prophet's companions gave way to rule by inheritance at Damascus, and the empire reached its greatest extent as a family monarchy.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Islam Rises and the Turks Enter Islam (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Under the Umayyads the caliphate became a dynasty: rule by election among the Prophet's companions gave way to rule by inheritance at Damascus, and the empire reached its greatest extent as a family monarchy.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a club whose president was always chosen by the members, becoming a family firm passed from father to son — the work goes on, but the rule of choosing has changed.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Umayyads were elected like the first caliphs → the dynasty's defining change is the opposite: inheritance replaced election, and that change is the lesson.
- Go back to the one thing: Under the Umayyads the caliphate became a dynasty: rule by election among the Prophet's companions gave way to rule by inheritance at Damascus, and the empire reached its greatest extent as a family monarchy. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
