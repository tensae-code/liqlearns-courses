# Medieval World: States and Empires — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which empire is Rome's eastern heir, surviving in Constantinople?
a) The Sassanid empire  b) The Byzantine empire  c) A feudal kingdom  d) A steppe khanate
**Answer: b) The Byzantine empire** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does feudal Europe raise its army?
a) By paying professionals all year  b) By obligation — land held in exchange for service  c) By hiring steppe peoples only  d) It has no army
**Answer: b) By obligation — land held in exchange for service** — That is what this lesson teaches.
