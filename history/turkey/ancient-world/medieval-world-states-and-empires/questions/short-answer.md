# Medieval World: States and Empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name three kinds of rule found in the medieval world in this lesson.
**Answer:** Any three of: the Byzantine emperor in Constantinople; the Sassanid King of Kings in Persia; feudal lords holding land for service in Europe; steppe khans ruling confederations of horse peoples.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book read the period's politics through its soldiers?
**Answer:** Because a state's power follows its army: who can raise soldiers, of what kind, and for how long decides who rules and how long they rule.
