# Medieval World: States and Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The medieval world had one single system of rule everywhere.
**Answer: False** — it held a dozen kinds of rule at once — emperors, kings of kings, feudal lords and steppe khans side by side.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) On the steppe, the whole people could serve as the army, every herdsman a mounted archer.
**Answer: True**
