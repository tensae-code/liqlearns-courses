# Video script — From Tribe to State: The First Kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. A chief's word became a king's law — and the state was born.
Start small: a clan around a fire, an elder speaking, everyone listening because he is theirs. His word is enough — the group is one family.
Now grow the picture. Fields fill, a town rises, thousands of strangers crowd its streets. The elder's voice cannot reach them. Who judges? Who defends? Who stores the grain?
Watch the new shape form: a king whose word is law for kin and stranger alike; officials, courts, an army, taxes. Not blood holding people together now — rule over a land.
Tribe to state. Every empire in this course stands on the far side of that crossing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
