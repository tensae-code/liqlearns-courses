# From Tribe to State: The First Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A state rules over a land and its people, not only over relatives.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The first states appeared while people still lived only by hunting.
**Answer: False** — it was farming lands filling up — villages becoming towns of strangers — that outgrew the chief's word.
