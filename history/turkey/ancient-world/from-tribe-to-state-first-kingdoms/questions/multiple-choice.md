# From Tribe to State: The First Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What held the earliest kin groups together?
a) Written law  b) Blood — family and clan ties, under a chief  c) Taxes  d) Standing armies
**Answer: b) Blood — family and clan ties, under a chief** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is a king's word, that a chief's word is not?
a) Advice to relatives  b) A story  c) Law — the same for kin and stranger  d) A poem
**Answer: c) Law — the same for kin and stranger** — That is what this lesson teaches.
