# From Tribe to State: The First Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why could a chief's word no longer run a growing town?
**Answer:** A town holds thousands of strangers, not relatives; it needs rules that stand when the ruler is absent, plus officials, courts, soldiers and taxes — more than one man's voice and memory.

**2.** Name the machinery that appears around the king in a state.
**Answer:** Officials, courts, an army, records — and taxes and stores to feed them.
