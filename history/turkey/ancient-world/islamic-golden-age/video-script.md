# Video script — The Islamic Golden Age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. For four centuries, the Islamic world was the world's classroom — translation, algebra, medicine, observatories.
Begin with the translators: Greek, Persian and Indian books flowing into Baghdad, turned into Arabic — one language holding the ancient world's library.
Now build on it. An algebra page filling with symbols. A hospital ward with physicians on their rounds. An observatory dome open to the night, astronomers correcting the star maps.
Pull back to two lights on the map: Baghdad in the east, Cordoba in the west — libraries and scholars outshining every rival.
The Civilisation of Science. Empires rose and fell around it; the knowledge it kept and grew outlasted them all.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
