# The Islamic Golden Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three fields in which this age advanced, from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any three of: algebra, medicine, astronomy (observatories), optics, geography, chemistry — all built on the great translation movement.

**2.** How long did the flowering last, and why does a history course give it a whole topic?
**Answer:** From the eighth to the twelfth centuries; because the flowering is part of the civilisation's story itself — it preserved the ancient world's knowledge, advanced it, and handed it on.
