# The Islamic Golden Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the book call this age?
a) The Age of Conquest  b) The Civilisation of Science (Bilim Medeniyeti)  c) The Age of Damascus  d) The Steppe Age
**Answer: b) The Civilisation of Science (Bilim Medeniyeti)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two cities stand for the age as the world's classrooms?
a) Mecca and Medina  b) Damascus and Cairo  c) Baghdad and Cordoba  d) Kabul and Samarkand
**Answer: c) Baghdad and Cordoba** — That is what this lesson teaches.
