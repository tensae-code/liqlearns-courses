# Video script — The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The clans became khaganates — and the kağan's kut, granted by Heaven, held them together.
Watch the steppe fill with one flag: clan after clan drawn under the Göktürk kağan, an empire of grass from Manchuria toward the Black Sea. After it, the Uyghurs hold the same world.
What binds them? A word carved into the age: kut — power granted by Gök Tanrı, Heaven itself. Held on condition: misrule and defeat can take it away.
Now stand before tall stones on the empty steppe: the Orhun inscriptions. Bilge Kağan speaks from them to his own people, in the Turks' own alphabet. And hear Mete Han's letter to China's emperor — a steppe ruler dictating terms.
The empire that writes in stone remembers itself. That is the Göktürk gift.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
