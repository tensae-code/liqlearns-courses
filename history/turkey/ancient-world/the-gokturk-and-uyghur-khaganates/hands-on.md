# Hands-on — The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A clan follows its chief because he is theirs. But how do a hundred clans follow one ruler? The steppe's answer is a word: kut. Say out loud: "Heaven grants the power."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The steppe clans became states: the Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates, ruled by a kağan whose power — his kut — was granted by Gök Tanrı, Heaven; and in the Orhun inscriptions the empire wrote its own story in stone, in its own alphabet, as Bilge Kağan's words and Mete Han's letter declare. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
