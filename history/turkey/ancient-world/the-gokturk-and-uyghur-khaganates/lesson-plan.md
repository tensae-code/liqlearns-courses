# Lesson plan — The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval World and the Turks of the Steppe (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The steppe clans became states: the Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates, ruled by a kağan whose power — his kut — was granted by Gök Tanrı, Heaven; and in the Orhun inscriptions the empire wrote its own story in stone, in its own alphabet, as Bilge Kağan's words and Mete Han's letter declare.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval World and the Turks of the Steppe (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks kut is just 'luck' → it is the doctrine of rule itself: power granted by Heaven (Gök Tanrı), held on condition — the steppe's answer to 'why obey this man?', and it can be lost.
- Thinks the Orhun stones are decoration → they are the empire's own history of itself, in its own alphabet — the first great Turkish writing the course meets.
- Go back to the one thing: The steppe clans became states: the Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates, ruled by a kağan whose power — his kut — was granted by Gök Tanrı, Heaven; and in the Orhun inscriptions the empire wrote its own story in stone, in its own alphabet, as Bilge Kağan's words and Mete Han's letter declare. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
