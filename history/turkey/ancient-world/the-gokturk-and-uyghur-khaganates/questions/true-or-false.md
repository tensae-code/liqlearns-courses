# The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Uyghur khaganate held the steppe world after the Göktürks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Kut, once granted, could never be lost.
**Answer: False** — kut is held on condition — defeat, famine and misrule can take Heaven's favour, and the right to rule, away.
