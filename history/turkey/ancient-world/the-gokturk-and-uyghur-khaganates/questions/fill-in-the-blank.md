# The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The Göktürk and Uyghur empires are called ____, after their ruler, the kağan.
**Answer:** khaganates

**2.** ____ ____, Heaven, is the god who grants the kağan his kut.
**Answer:** Gök Tanrı
