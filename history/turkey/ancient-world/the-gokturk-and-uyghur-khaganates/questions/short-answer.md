# The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Whose words does the book quote, and what does each declare?
**Answer:** Mete Han's letter to the Chinese emperor declares the steppe ruler's pride and terms to the greatest settled power; Bilge Kağan's Orhun words warn his people how empires are won and lost.

**2.** Why do the Orhun stones matter so much to this course?
**Answer:** They are the Turks remembering themselves — the empire's own story in its own alphabet — instead of being known only through neighbours' chronicles.
