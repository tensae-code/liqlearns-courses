# The Göktürk and Uyghur Khaganates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is kut?
a) A kind of felt tent  b) The ruler's power and fortune, granted by Gök Tanrı (Heaven)  c) A tax on herds  d) The kağan's army
**Answer: b) The ruler's power and fortune, granted by Gök Tanrı (Heaven)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What are the Orhun inscriptions?
a) Chinese chronicles about the Turks  b) Temple paintings  c) The empire's own story carved in stone, in its own alphabet  d) Trade records
**Answer: c) The empire's own story carved in stone, in its own alphabet** — That is what this lesson teaches.
