# Lesson plan — The First Turks of the Steppe

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The First Turks of the Steppe
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval World and the Turks of the Steppe (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The first Turks appear in Eurasia's steppe — the Huns and the peoples around them — and the grassland itself writes their whole way of life: the horse, the felt tent and the herds are answers to a land with no walls and no plough.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval World and the Turks of the Steppe (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Before this lesson
- **Roman Law, Roads and Empire** (`ancient-world/roman-law-roads-empire/`) is recommended first — from the settled empires, the course now rides out onto the steppe (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The first Turks appear in Eurasia's steppe — the Huns and the peoples around them — and the grassland itself writes their whole way of life: the horse, the felt tent and the herds are answers to a land with no walls and no plough.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a sailor's whole kit shaped by the sea — boat, sail, rope, weather-eye. The steppe Turk's kit — horse, tent, herd — is shaped the same way, by his 'sea' of grass.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the steppe peoples lived 'without' civilisation → they lived differently, not emptily: horse, tent and herds are precise answers to the grassland's conditions — geography shaped the life, as the book's own title says.
- Go back to the one thing: The first Turks appear in Eurasia's steppe — the Huns and the peoples around them — and the grassland itself writes their whole way of life: the horse, the felt tent and the herds are answers to a land with no walls and no plough. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
