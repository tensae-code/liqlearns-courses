# The First Turks of the Steppe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What land shapes the first Turks' way of life?
a) A river valley  b) The Eurasian steppe — the wide grassland  c) A desert oasis  d) A sea coast
**Answer: b) The Eurasian steppe — the wide grassland** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why must a steppe herdsman's house be a felt tent?
a) Felt is cheap  b) It is beautiful  c) It packs up and moves when the herds move to new grass  d) Kings ordered it
**Answer: c) It packs up and moves when the herds move to new grass** — That is what this lesson teaches.
