# The First Turks of the Steppe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The horse gave the steppe peoples transport, war, food and wealth in one animal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The steppe offered plenty of wood and stone for building cities.
**Answer: False** — the steppe denies wood, stone and shelter — which is exactly why the tent, not the stone house, is its dwelling.
