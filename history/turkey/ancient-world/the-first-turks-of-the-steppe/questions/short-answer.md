# The First Turks of the Steppe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three great answers the steppe Turks gave to their land.
**Answer:** The horse, the felt tent, and the herds.

**2.** What does the book mean by 'a way of life shaped by geography'?
**Answer:** That the land's conditions — grass and space in plenty, wood and rain denied — dictated how the people lived: mobile, mounted and herd-rich rather than settled and city-building.
