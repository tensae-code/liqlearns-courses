# Video script — The First Turks of the Steppe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The steppe wrote the first Turks' whole way of life — horse, felt tent and herds.
Open the map on grass: horizon to horizon, no walls, no cities, wind moving through it like water. This is the Eurasian steppe, and here the first Turkish traces appear — the Huns and the peoples around them.
Now read the land's bargain: grass and space in plenty; wood, stone and rain denied. Watch the answers form. The horse — food, wealth and war in one animal. The felt tent — a house that packs up in an hour. The herds — turning grass into milk, meat and wool.
A settled farmer reads this land as empty. The Turks read it as home — and everything they became starts here.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
