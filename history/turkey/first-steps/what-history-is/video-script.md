# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. History is the memory of humankind — and a people without memory cannot know itself.
Picture a person who wakes with no memory. They do not know their own name, their own family, their own story. Now picture a whole people like that. That is a nation that does not learn its history.
But memory must be kept honestly. Watch the historian work: a question is asked, evidence is hunted — a document, a coin, a buried wall — and the evidence is weighed before the story is told.
And the historian has helpers: archaeology digs, geography maps the stage, chronology lays out the years in order. Each science hands over a witness.
History: humankind's memory, kept by method. That is the subject this whole course serves.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
