# Lesson plan — What History Is

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What History Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History is the memory of humankind: it studies the human past with a method of its own, helped by sister sciences — and a people without memory cannot know itself, which is why nations learn it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — First Steps: What History Is and the Deep Past (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History is the memory of humankind: it studies the human past with a method of its own, helped by sister sciences — and a people without memory cannot know itself, which is why nations learn it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — your own family's memory — the stories your grandparents tell that let you know who your family is.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks history is just a list of kings and dates → go back to the memory picture: dates are the memory's order, but the subject is the whole human past, studied with evidence.
- Thinks any old story counts as history → a story becomes history when evidence stands behind it; the method — question, evidence, weighing — is what makes it history.
- Go back to the one thing: History is the memory of humankind: it studies the human past with a method of its own, helped by sister sciences — and a people without memory cannot know itself, which is why nations learn it. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
