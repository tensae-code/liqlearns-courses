# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The book introduces history as what?
a) A list of kings  b) The memory of humankind  c) A book of maps  d) A collection of poems
**Answer: b) The memory of humankind** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What makes a story count as history, in this lesson?
a) It is very old  b) It is exciting  c) Evidence stands behind it, weighed by the historian's method  d) A king ordered it written
**Answer: c) Evidence stands behind it, weighed by the historian's method** — That is what this lesson teaches.
