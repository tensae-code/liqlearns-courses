# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why do nations learn history, according to this lesson?
**Answer:** Because history is humankind's memory: a people without memory cannot know itself — who it is, where it came from, and what its past teaches.

**2.** Name two helper sciences of history and what each gives.
**Answer:** Any two of: archaeology (what hands made and buried), geography (the stage), chronology (the order of time); coins, seals and languages are read by their own sciences too.
