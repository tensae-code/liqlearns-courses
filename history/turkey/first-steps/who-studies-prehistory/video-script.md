# Video script — Who Studies Prehistory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Before writing, the past is silent — and archaeology reads it from bones, stones and cave walls.
Picture darkness: no books, no letters, nothing written anywhere on earth. The first humans are living — hunting, making fire, painting — and leaving no words behind.
Now dig. A shaped stone comes up — a tool, made by a hand. Bones come up. Ash from a hearth. And deep in a cave, a painted animal waits on the wall.
These are the witnesses of the Stone Age, and archaeology is the science that questions them, fragment by fragment.
The human story starts long before writing. This is how we know.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
