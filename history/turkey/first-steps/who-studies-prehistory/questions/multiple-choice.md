# Who Studies Prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why does prehistory have no documents?
a) People hid them  b) Writing did not exist yet  c) Kings banned writing  d) Paper rotted
**Answer: b) Writing did not exist yet** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which science reads the prehistoric past?
a) Chronology  b) Geography  c) Archaeology  d) Astronomy
**Answer: c) Archaeology** — That is what this lesson teaches.
