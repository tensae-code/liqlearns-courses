# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three kinds of witnesses this lesson gives for the first humans.
**Answer:** Bones, stones (shaped into tools), and cave walls (with their paintings).

**2.** How is knowledge of prehistory different from knowledge of later history?
**Answer:** Later history is read from documents people wrote; prehistory is recovered from things — every claim is argued from fragments dug up and examined.
