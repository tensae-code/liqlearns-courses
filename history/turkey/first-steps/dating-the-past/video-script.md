# Video script — Dating the Past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Before the story, cut the time: ages, centuries, calendars — that is chronology.
Picture one long road stretching back further than the eye can see. Now watch the road being cut: first the great cuts — the ages, the long eras of the human story.
Cut smaller: centuries, one hundred years at a time, counted in order. Smaller still: single years, counted by calendars — and different peoples start their counts at different places, so the same year can wear different numbers.
Now lay the cuts in a line. A timeline. Your eye can travel it: before, after, at the same time.
Every story in this course will be hung on that frame. First the frame — then the stories.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
