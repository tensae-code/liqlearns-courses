# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why must time be divided before the past can be studied?
**Answer:** The past is too vast to tell at once; cutting it into ages, centuries and years — and laying it on a timeline — lets every event take its place: before, after, or at the same time as the others.

**2.** What does a timeline let the eye do?
**Answer:** Travel through time in order — seeing what came before, what came after, and what happened at the same time.
