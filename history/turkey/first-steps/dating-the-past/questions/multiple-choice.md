# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the biggest cuts into which the past is divided?
a) Days  b) Ages — the long eras of the human story  c) Reigns only  d) Seasons
**Answer: b) Ages — the long eras of the human story** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is chronology?
a) A kind of calendar  b) A list of kings  c) The ordering of events in time  d) A helper that digs
**Answer: c) The ordering of events in time** — That is what this lesson teaches.
