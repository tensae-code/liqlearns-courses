# Turkey's National Days: Republic Day and Remembering Atatürk — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Before Turkey's children learn history as a school subject, they learn their country's memory as poems: 29 October, Republic Day, when the homeland's form of government became a republic; Atatürk Week in November, remembering the founder at the 09:05 moment of his death; and 15 July, Democracy and National Unity Day, the night the nation stood up for democracy.
