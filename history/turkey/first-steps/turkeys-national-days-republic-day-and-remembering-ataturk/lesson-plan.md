# Lesson plan — Turkey's National Days: Republic Day and Remembering Atatürk

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Turkey's National Days: Republic Day and Remembering Atatürk
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: The Republic Remembered in Poems (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before Turkey's children learn history as a school subject, they learn their country's memory as poems: 29 October, Republic Day, when the homeland's form of government became a republic; Atatürk Week in November, remembering the founder at the 09:05 moment of his death; and 15 July, Democracy and National Unity Day, the night the nation stood up for democracy.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: The Republic Remembered in Poems (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up the three days → line them up by season: autumn's end, 29 October, the Republic is born; a week into November, the founder is remembered at 09:05; high summer, 15 July, democracy defended in one night.
- Go back to the one thing: Before Turkey's children learn history as a school subject, they learn their country's memory as poems: 29 October, Republic Day, when the homeland's form of government became a republic; Atatürk Week in November, remembering the founder at the 09:05 moment of his death; and 15 July, Democracy and National Unity Day, the night the nation stood up for democracy. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
