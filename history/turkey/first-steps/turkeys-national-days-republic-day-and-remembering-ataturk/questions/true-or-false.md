# Turkey's National Days: Republic Day and Remembering Atatürk — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On Republic Day, the nation decks itself in flags.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The 15 July poem tells of a night when the nation gave up democracy.
**Answer: False** — the poem retells the night the nation-army fought for democracy and won.
