# Turkey's National Days: Republic Day and Remembering Atatürk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened on 29 October, the day the poem celebrates?
a) The founder died  b) The homeland's form of government became a republic  c) The army fought for democracy in one night  d) School began
**Answer: b) The homeland's form of government became a republic** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What time of day does the Atatürk Week poem remember with grief?
a) Midnight  b) Noon  c) 09:05 — five past nine in the morning  d) Sunset
**Answer: c) 09:05 — five past nine in the morning** — That is what this lesson teaches.
