# Turkey's National Days: Republic Day and Remembering Atatürk — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Where do the crowds run to in the Atatürk Week poem, and who is remembered there?
**Answer:** They run to Anıtkabir, the mausoleum in Ankara, remembering Atatürk, the founder of the Republic — patriotic, honest, determined and hardworking.

**2.** How does a seven-year-old in Turkey first learn these three days?
**Answer:** As poems — the special-days poem spreads in the first school book, read aloud and learned by heart.
