# Hands-on — Turkey's National Struggle Begins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The war is lost. Foreign soldiers are landing. The capital's government signs what it is told to sign. And in the Anatolian towns, officers and ordinary people begin to organise — a second state, in secret and then in the open. Say out loud: "Anatolia refuses."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Out of defeat and occupation, the National Struggle is prepared: the Mondros armistice, the occupations, the Kuvâ-yi Milliye resistance, the Amasya Circular, the Erzurum and Sivas congresses, the opening of the Grand National Assembly — and the Treaty of Sèvres, the death warrant the capital signed and Anatolia refused. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
