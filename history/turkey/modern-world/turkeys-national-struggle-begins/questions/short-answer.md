# Turkey's National Struggle Begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the building steps of the second state, in order.
**Answer:** The Amasya Circular (the principle and the call); the Erzurum Congress; the Sivas Congress (one indivisible homeland, mandates rejected); and the opening of the Grand National Assembly in Ankara, April 1920.

**2.** What did the Mondros armistice set in motion?
**Answer:** The end of the war on Allied terms: demobilisation of the army and Allied occupation of strategic points — the occupation the National Struggle rose against.
