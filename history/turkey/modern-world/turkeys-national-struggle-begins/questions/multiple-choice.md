# Turkey's National Struggle Begins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the Kuvâ-yi Milliye?
a) The capital's police  b) Local, irregular National Forces resisting occupation in their own districts  c) A foreign army  d) A political party in Istanbul
**Answer: b) Local, irregular National Forces resisting occupation in their own districts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Treaty of Sèvres do?
a) Freed Anatolia  b) Partitioned the homeland among the victors — the death warrant Anatolia refused  c) Opened the Assembly  d) Ended the occupation
**Answer: b) Partitioned the homeland among the victors — the death warrant Anatolia refused** — That is what this lesson teaches.
