# Turkey's National Struggle Begins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Grand National Assembly opened in Ankara in April 1920.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Amasya Circular asked a foreign power to protect Turkey as a mandate.
**Answer: False** — it declared the opposite: the nation's independence would be saved by the nation's own determination, and the congresses rejected mandates.
