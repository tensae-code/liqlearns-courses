# Video script — Turkey's National Struggle Begins

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Occupied and disarmed, Anatolia organised a second state — while the capital signed its death warrant.
Begin with the armistice: Mondros. The army demobilises; Allied troops take the capital and ports; Greek forces land at İzmir. The house is occupied.
Watch the first answer flare locally: the Kuvâ-yi Milliye, townsmen and veterans resisting district by district. Then the organiser lands at Samsun — Mustafa Kemal, with papers that say inspector and a purpose far larger.
Follow the paper trail of a new state: the Amasya Circular — independence saved by the nation's own determination. Erzurum. Sivas. And in Ankara, April 1920, the Grand National Assembly opens its doors.
Cut to Istanbul, signing Sèvres — the partition, the death warrant. Two capitals now. The next lesson is the war between their fates.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
