# Lesson plan — Turkey's National Struggle Begins

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey's National Struggle Begins
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Out of defeat and occupation, the National Struggle is prepared: the Mondros armistice, the occupations, the Kuvâ-yi Milliye resistance, the Amasya Circular, the Erzurum and Sivas congresses, the opening of the Grand National Assembly — and the Treaty of Sèvres, the death warrant the capital signed and Anatolia refused.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the resistance began as a regular army → it begins as the Kuvâ-yi Milliye, local and irregular, while the congresses and the Assembly build the state that will raise the regular army of the next lesson.
- Thinks Sèvres was accepted by the nation → Sèvres is the capital's signature; Anatolia's whole movement exists to refuse it — it is never applied.
- Go back to the one thing: Out of defeat and occupation, the National Struggle is prepared: the Mondros armistice, the occupations, the Kuvâ-yi Milliye resistance, the Amasya Circular, the Erzurum and Sivas congresses, the opening of the Grand National Assembly — and the Treaty of Sèvres, the death warrant the capital signed and Anatolia refused. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
