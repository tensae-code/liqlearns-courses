# The Middle East Wars, 1967–1988 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened in the Six-Day War of 1967?
a) Iraq invaded Iran  b) Israel struck first and took Sinai, the West Bank, Gaza and the Golan in under a week  c) Egypt took Tel Aviv  d) The war lasted eight years
**Answer: b) Israel struck first and took Sinai, the West Bank, Gaza and the Golan in under a week** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How long did the Iran-Iraq War last?
a) Six days  b) One year  c) Eight years, 1980–1988  d) Forty years
**Answer: c) Eight years, 1980–1988** — That is what this lesson teaches.
