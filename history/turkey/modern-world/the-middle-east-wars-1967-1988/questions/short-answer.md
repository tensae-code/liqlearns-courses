# The Middle East Wars, 1967–1988 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the two fires of this lesson and their dates.
**Answer:** The Arab-Israeli wars — 1967 (Six-Day War) and 1973, with Lebanon's civil war drawing armies in; and the Iran-Iraq War, 1980–1988.

**2.** What lever redrew the region's weight in the world, and how?
**Answer:** Oil: its price and supply, wielded from 1973 onward, made the producing region a power in every capital's calculations, Ankara's included.
