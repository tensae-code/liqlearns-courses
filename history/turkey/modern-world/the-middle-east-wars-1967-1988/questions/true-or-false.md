# The Middle East Wars, 1967–1988 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1973 war brought the oil weapon into the world's economy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Turkey was a belligerent in the Arab-Israeli wars.
**Answer: False** — Turkey watched its own borders; the wars were its southern neighbours', fought on its doorstep.
