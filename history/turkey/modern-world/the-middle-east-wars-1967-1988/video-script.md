# Video script — The Middle East Wars, 1967–1988

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. On Turkey's southern doorstep: three generations of wars, and oil redrawing the region's weight.
1967: six days. Israel strikes, and the map is redrawn at a stroke — Sinai, West Bank, Gaza, Golan. 1973: Egypt and Syria strike back; the superpowers edge toward the brink; and the oil weapon enters the world's economy.
Then the long fire: 1980 to 1988, Iran and Iraq in the trenches — eight years, the century's longest conventional war, ending where it began.
Through every frame, one lever moves the world: oil — its price, its supply, the region's new weight.
And at the top of the map, Turkey borders three of the fighters. Watching its own borders is its part in these wars.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
