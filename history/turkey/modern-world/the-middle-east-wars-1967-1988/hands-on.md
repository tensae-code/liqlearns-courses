# Hands-on — The Middle East Wars, 1967–1988

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
South of Turkey, the map drawn in 1948 catches fire again and again: 1967, 1973, and then an eight-year war between two neighbours, Iran and Iraq. Say out loud: "Three generations of wars on the southern doorstep."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the age the book calls the age of social revolution, Turkey's southern neighbours fought three generations of wars — the Arab-Israeli wars and the long Iran-Iraq War — while oil redrew the region's weight in the world and Turkey watched its own borders. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
