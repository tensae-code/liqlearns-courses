# Lesson plan — The Middle East Wars, 1967–1988

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Middle East Wars, 1967–1988
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the age the book calls the age of social revolution, Turkey's southern neighbours fought three generations of wars — the Arab-Israeli wars and the long Iran-Iraq War — while oil redrew the region's weight in the world and Turkey watched its own borders.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In the age the book calls the age of social revolution, Turkey's southern neighbours fought three generations of wars — the Arab-Israeli wars and the long Iran-Iraq War — while oil redrew the region's weight in the world and Turkey watched its own borders.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — living on a street where the houses at the far end keep catching fire, decade after decade — you do not join the fires, but you keep your own hoses ready and your doors watched.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Arab-Israeli wars and the Iran-Iraq War are one conflict → two fires: Israel against its Arab neighbours (1967, 1973 and Lebanon's war), and Iran against Iraq (1980–1988) — different enemies, one burning neighbourhood.
- Go back to the one thing: In the age the book calls the age of social revolution, Turkey's southern neighbours fought three generations of wars — the Arab-Israeli wars and the long Iran-Iraq War — while oil redrew the region's weight in the world and Turkey watched its own borders. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
