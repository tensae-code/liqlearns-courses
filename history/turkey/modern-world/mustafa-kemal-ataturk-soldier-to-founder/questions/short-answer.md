# Mustafa Kemal Atatürk: Soldier to Founder — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the ladder of Mustafa Kemal's schools in order.
**Answer:** Şemsi Efendi's school (Salonica); Salonica military middle school; Monastir military high school; the War College and the War Academy in Istanbul.

**2.** What double lesson does the book draw from his postings?
**Answer:** The empire is failing — and a generation of officers, formed in its schools and far-flung posts, is learning exactly why, and what a new state would need.
