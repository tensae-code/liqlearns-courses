# Mustafa Kemal Atatürk: Soldier to Founder — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mustafa Kemal graduated from the War Academy as a staff officer.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Mustafa Kemal's first posting was to Sofia as military attaché.
**Answer: False** — his first station was Damascus; Sofia, as military attaché, came later, on the eve of the Great War.
