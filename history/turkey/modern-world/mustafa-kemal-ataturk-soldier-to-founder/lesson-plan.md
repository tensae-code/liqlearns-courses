# Lesson plan — Mustafa Kemal Atatürk: Soldier to Founder

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Mustafa Kemal Atatürk: Soldier to Founder
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Republic's founder is introduced the Turkish way — as a schoolboy formed by teachers before he is a general: Şemsi Efendi's school, the military schools of Salonica and Monastir, the War College and Academy, and postings from Damascus to Sofia that made the soldier who would found a state.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (recommended, never forced).
