# Hands-on — Mustafa Kemal Atatürk: Soldier to Founder

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before the general, there is a schoolboy. Before the founder of a republic, there are teachers whose names his country still knows. Say out loud: "First the schoolboy, then the soldier, then the founder."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Republic's founder is introduced the Turkish way — as a schoolboy formed by teachers before he is a general: Şemsi Efendi's school, the military schools of Salonica and Monastir, the War College and Academy, and postings from Damascus to Sofia that made the soldier who would found a state. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
