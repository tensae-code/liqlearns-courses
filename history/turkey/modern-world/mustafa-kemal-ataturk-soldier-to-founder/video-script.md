# Video script — Mustafa Kemal Atatürk: Soldier to Founder

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Before the founder, the schoolboy: Mustafa Kemal's teachers and schools made the soldier.
Start in Salonica, at Şemsi Efendi's school — the modern school a father chose for his son. Climb the ladder: the military school where a teacher adds 'Kemal' to his name; Monastir, where history fires the cadet; Istanbul's War College and Academy, where he graduates a staff officer.
Now follow the postings across a dying empire's map: Damascus, first station; the Balkans; Sofia, the attaché watching Europe arm.
At every stop, the same lesson: the empire is failing, and this officer is learning why. The founder is formed before the founding begins.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
