# The Second World War: The Course of the War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was Turkey courted by both camps?
**Answer:** Because of where it sits: the Straits and the road between Europe and the Middle East — each camp wanted its entry, or at least its continued quiet, and bid for it.

**2.** What motive does the book give for Turkey's refusal to fight?
**Answer:** Memory: the ruling generation had itself fought 1914–1922 and lost an empire in the last war; it would not spend this war the same way.
