# The Second World War: The Course of the War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three words describe Turkey's wartime policy in the book?
a) Armed, neutral, courted  b) Passive, poor, forgotten  c) Allied, beaten, occupied  d) Secret, small, late
**Answer: a) Armed, neutral, courted** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When did Turkey declare war, and how?
a) 1939, fully  b) 1941, after invasion  c) February 1945 — a symbolic declaration at the war's end  d) Never at all
**Answer: c) February 1945 — a symbolic declaration at the war's end** — That is what this lesson teaches.
