# The Second World War: The Course of the War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Turkey kept a great army mobilised through the war years, at heavy cost.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Turkey joined the Axis in 1941.
**Answer: False** — it joined neither camp's war; its only declaration came symbolically in February 1945, when the war was decided.
