# Video script — The Second World War: The Course of the War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Through the whole Second World War, Turkey stayed out — armed, neutral, courted by both camps.
Watch the war spread across the map from 1939: Europe, then the world. Now find Turkey on it — uncoloured, on the Straits, between the camps.
Three faces of its policy. Armed: a million men under arms, year after year, a poor country paying to be believed. Neutral: trade and treaties with both sides, war with neither. Courted: envoys and prime ministers flying in — Churchill at Adana — bidding for the country that holds the Straits.
The motive is memory: this ruling generation lost an empire in the last war. They will not spend this one the same way.
February 1945: a symbolic declaration, no battles — and a seat earned at the table of the world being rebuilt. The results come next.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
