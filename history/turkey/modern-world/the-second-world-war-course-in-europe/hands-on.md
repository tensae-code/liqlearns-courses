# Hands-on — The Second World War: The Course of the War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The whole world is fighting — and one country, sitting on the straits both sides need, refuses to fight at all. Both camps court it; both camps threaten it; it arms, waits, and balances. Say out loud: "Armed, neutral, and courted by both sides."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Through the Second World War's course, Turkey — a country that had lost an empire in the last war — stayed out: armed, neutral and courted by both camps, balancing each against the other until a symbolic declaration of war in February 1945, at the very end. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
