# Atatürk's Reforms Remake Turkey — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The six principles and the new state
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The revolution's own name for its doctrine is **Atatürkism**, and it explains itself in **six principles** — the six arrows: **republicanism** (the nation's sovereignty, ruled as a republic), **nationalism** (the nation's unity and pride), **populism** (rule for the whole people, no privileged class), **statism** (the state steering the economy where private hands are too weak), **secularism** (religion and state affairs separated), and **revolutionism** (the reforms themselves, kept moving).

## Sub lesson 2 — Education, culture and social life
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Now the revolution walks out of the palace and into the street, the school and the home. Education is unified: the **unification of education** (1924) brings every school under one national system — one ladder for every child, religious and state schools no longer running rival nations.

## Sub lesson 3 — Economy and health
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The revolution's third front is bread and health. Its doctrine is spoken first and early: the **İzmir Economic Congress (1923)** — held before the Republic is even proclaimed — declares that **political independence must be paid for with economic independence**: a nation that does not produce will not stay free.
