# Video script — Atatürk's Reforms Remake Turkey

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Six principles — and a whole country rebuilt to match them: state, school, alphabet, clock, name, economy, health.
Hear the six words first: republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism, secularism, revolutionism. Then watch the strokes land. 1 November 1922: the sultanate abolished. 29 October 1923: the Republic proclaimed. 1924: the caliphate gone, education unified under one system.
Now walk into daily life: a blackboard in a village square, a nation relearning its letters in the new alphabet. Clocks and calendars changed to the world's. A law of 21 June 1934 handing every citizen a surname — the founder's own, Atatürk, among them. And women stepping to the ballot box in 1934.
Last, the bread and the health: İzmir's congress declaring economic independence the price of the political kind; fields, factories, ships — and clinics rising with them.
Six words. Everything rebuilt. That is the Turkish Revolution as its own book tells it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
