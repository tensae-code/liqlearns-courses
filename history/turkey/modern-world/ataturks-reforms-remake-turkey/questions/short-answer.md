# Atatürk's Reforms Remake Turkey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the six principles of Atatürkism.
**Answer:** Republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism, secularism, revolutionism.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three daily-life reforms from this part of the lesson.
**Answer:** Any three of: the Latin-based alphabet (1928) and the language reform; the calendar, clock and measures changed to international standards; the Surname Law (21 June 1934); women's rights up to full national suffrage in 1934; the unification of education (1924).
