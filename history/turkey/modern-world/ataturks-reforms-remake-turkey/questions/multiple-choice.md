# Atatürk's Reforms Remake Turkey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When was the sultanate abolished?
a) 29 October 1923  b) 1 November 1922  c) 21 June 1934  d) 24 July 1923
**Answer: b) 1 November 1922** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What did the İzmir Economic Congress of 1923 declare?
a) That Turkey should import everything  b) That political independence must be paid for with economic independence  c) That the caliphate should return  d) That agriculture should be abandoned
**Answer: b) That political independence must be paid for with economic independence** — That is what this lesson teaches.
