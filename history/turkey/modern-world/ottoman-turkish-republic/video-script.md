# Video script — Ottoman to Turkish Republic: Mudanya and Lausanne

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Mudanya silenced the guns; Lausanne won the peace — and buried Sèvres.
First document, October 1922: Mudanya. A small town, a short armistice, the guns silent, Eastern Thrace handed over without a battle.
Second document, the hard one: Lausanne, Switzerland. Watch the two sides face each other — the victors expecting Sèvres softened; Ankara's delegation holding the National Pact and a won war. Months of refusal, clause by clause.
24 July 1923: the signatures. Borders recognised, sovereignty full, capitulations abolished — by the very powers that imposed Sèvres.
What the sword won at Sakarya, the pen keeps at Lausanne. The Republic can now be proclaimed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
