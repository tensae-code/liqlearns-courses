# Ottoman to Turkish Republic: Mudanya and Lausanne — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Mudanya Armistice of 11 October 1922 do?
a) Proclaimed the Republic  b) Silenced the guns and handed over Eastern Thrace  c) Abolished the caliphate  d) Founded the Assembly
**Answer: b) Silenced the guns and handed over Eastern Thrace** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Lausanne Treaty abolish?
a) The Assembly  b) The National Pact  c) The capitulations — the old foreign privileges  d) The army
**Answer: c) The capitulations — the old foreign privileges** — That is what this lesson teaches.
