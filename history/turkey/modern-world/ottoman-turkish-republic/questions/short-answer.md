# Ottoman to Turkish Republic: Mudanya and Lausanne — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the book say 'what the sword won at Sakarya, the pen keeps at Lausanne'?
**Answer:** Because the battlefield victory only became a recognised country when diplomacy converted it into a treaty: Lausanne won the world's recognition of the new state's borders and sovereignty.

**2.** Name two things Lausanne settled for the new Turkey.
**Answer:** Any two of: recognition of its borders; full sovereignty; abolition of the capitulations; the burial of the Sèvres partition.
