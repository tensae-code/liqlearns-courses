# Ottoman to Turkish Republic: Mudanya and Lausanne — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Lausanne was signed on 24 July 1923.
**Answer: True**

**2.** At Lausanne the victors succeeded in keeping Sèvres with small changes.
**Answer: False** — Ankara refused the partition's terms; Lausanne recognised full sovereignty and buried Sèvres.
