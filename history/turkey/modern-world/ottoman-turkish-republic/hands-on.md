# Hands-on — Ottoman to Turkish Republic: Mudanya and Lausanne

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The fighting is over — but a victory is not a country until the world signs its name to it. Two documents remain: one to stop the guns, one to win the peace. Say out loud: "What the sword won, the pen keeps."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: What the sword won, the pen kept: the Mudanya Armistice (11 October 1922) silenced the guns, and the Lausanne Peace Treaty (24 July 1923) won the peace — the world's recognition of the new Turkey and the burial of Sèvres. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
