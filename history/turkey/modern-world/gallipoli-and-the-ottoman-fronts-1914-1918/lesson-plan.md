# Lesson plan — Gallipoli and the Ottoman Fronts, 1914–1918

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Gallipoli and the Ottoman Fronts, 1914–1918
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The empire fought on more fronts than any ally — offensive fronts (Caucasus, the Canal), defensive fronts (Çanakkale/Gallipoli, Iraq, Syria-Palestine, Hijaz-Yemen) and aid fronts (Galicia, Romania, Macedonia) — and its one immortal victory, at Çanakkale, was won under the colonel who would found the Republic.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up the three kinds of fronts → sort by direction: offensive = we attack (Caucasus, Canal); defensive = we hold our ground (Çanakkale, Iraq, Syria-Palestine, Hijaz-Yemen); aid = we fight for allies abroad (Galicia, Romania, Macedonia).
- Go back to the one thing: The empire fought on more fronts than any ally — offensive fronts (Caucasus, the Canal), defensive fronts (Çanakkale/Gallipoli, Iraq, Syria-Palestine, Hijaz-Yemen) and aid fronts (Galicia, Romania, Macedonia) — and its one immortal victory, at Çanakkale, was won under the colonel who would found the Republic. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
