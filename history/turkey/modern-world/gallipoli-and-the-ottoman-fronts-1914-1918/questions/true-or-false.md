# Gallipoli and the Ottoman Fronts, 1914–1918 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The empire fought on more fronts than any of its allies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Caucasus and Canal offensives succeeded at small cost.
**Answer: False** — both offensive gambles failed at great cost; the Caucasus winter became a byword for disaster.
