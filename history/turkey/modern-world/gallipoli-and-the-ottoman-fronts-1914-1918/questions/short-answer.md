# Gallipoli and the Ottoman Fronts, 1914–1918 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three kinds of fronts and give one example of each.
**Answer:** Offensive: Caucasus (or the Canal). Defensive: Çanakkale/Gallipoli (or Iraq, Syria-Palestine, Hijaz-Yemen). Aid: Galicia (or Romania, Macedonia).

**2.** Why does the book bind Çanakkale to Mustafa Kemal?
**Answer:** Because the defence of the straits was bound up with the divisional commander Mustafa Kemal — the empire's one immortal victory was won by the colonel who would found the republic.
