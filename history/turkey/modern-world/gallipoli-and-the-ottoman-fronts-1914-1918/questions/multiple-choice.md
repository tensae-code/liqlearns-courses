# Gallipoli and the Ottoman Fronts, 1914–1918 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which front was the empire's immortal victory?
a) The Caucasus  b) The Canal  c) Çanakkale (Gallipoli)  d) Galicia
**Answer: c) Çanakkale (Gallipoli)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Galicia, Romania and Macedonia were what kind of fronts?
a) Offensive fronts  b) Defensive fronts  c) Aid fronts — Ottoman soldiers sent to fight for allies abroad  d) Naval fronts
**Answer: c) Aid fronts — Ottoman soldiers sent to fight for allies abroad** — That is what this lesson teaches.
