# Video script — Gallipoli and the Ottoman Fronts, 1914–1918

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. More fronts than any ally — and one immortal victory at Çanakkale.
Spread the map and sort the fronts. Offensive: the Caucasus snows, the Suez Canal strike — both failing dearly. Defensive: Iraq, Syria-Palestine, Hijaz-Yemen — the south slipping away province by province.
Stop at the straits. Çanakkale. Allied fleets and armies come to force the passage to Istanbul — and are thrown back. Find the divisional commander in the defence: Mustafa Kemal. The empire's one immortal victory belongs to the colonel who will found the republic.
Aid fronts last: Ottoman regiments in Galicia, Romania, Macedonia — fighting other people's wars on other continents.
Count the cost of the width. That exhaustion walks into the next lesson.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
