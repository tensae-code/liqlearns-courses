# Turkey Joins the West: Cold War and NATO — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the Western steps in order, with dates.
**Answer:** Council of Europe (1949); the Turkish brigade in the Korean War (from 1950); NATO membership (1952).

**2.** Why does the book bind the 1950 election and the Western anchor together?
**Answer:** Because they are one turn in two halves: democracy practised at home and alliance with the democratic West abroad — each presented as the other's guarantee.
