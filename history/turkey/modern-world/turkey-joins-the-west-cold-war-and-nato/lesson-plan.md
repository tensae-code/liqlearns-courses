# Lesson plan — Turkey Joins the West: Cold War and NATO

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey Joins the West: Cold War and NATO
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In five years after 1945 the Republic changed camps and constitutions at once: the passage to multi-party life (1945–1950) ended with the Democrat Party's 1950 victory — the republic voting its founders' party out — and Turkey anchored itself to the West: the Council of Europe (1949), a brigade in the Korean War, and NATO membership, remaking the Turkey of the 1950s.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the 1950 change of power was a coup → it was an election: the Democrat Party won at the ballot box and the founders' party handed over power — the book presents it as democracy's proof.
- Thinks NATO membership was simply granted → in the book's telling it was earned: the Korean brigade's fighting under the UN flag is the price and the proof.
- Go back to the one thing: In five years after 1945 the Republic changed camps and constitutions at once: the passage to multi-party life (1945–1950) ended with the Democrat Party's 1950 victory — the republic voting its founders' party out — and Turkey anchored itself to the West: the Council of Europe (1949), a brigade in the Korean War, and NATO membership, remaking the Turkey of the 1950s. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
