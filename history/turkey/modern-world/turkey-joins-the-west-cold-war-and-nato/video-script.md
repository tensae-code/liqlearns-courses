# Video script — Turkey Joins the West: Cold War and NATO

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In five years, the republic votes its founders' party out — and anchors itself to the West.
Home first: 1945 to 1950. An opposition organises — the Democrat Party. Campaigns, grievances, hopes. Then the count: 1950, the founders' party loses, and power changes hands by ballot. Democracy practised, not just promised.
Now the outward turn. Soviet demands press on the Straits. Turkey chooses: the Council of Europe, 1949. A brigade under the UN flag in Korea, fighting hard and far. And the earned seat: NATO, 1952.
See the 1950s that follow: tractors in the villages, roads, aid, a freer market — a second founding.
Ballot box at home, Western anchor abroad. That is the turn of 1945–1950.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
