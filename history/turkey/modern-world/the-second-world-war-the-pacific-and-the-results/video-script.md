# Video script — The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The war's results: two superpowers inherit the world — and the Middle East next door is redrawn.
Survey the morning after. Across the map, stand the two powers left at full strength: the United States and the Soviet Union, facing each other over a ruined Europe. The old empires wane. A new organisation rises — the United Nations, built to have the force the League lacked.
Count the social and economic column: tens of millions dead, cities rubble, economies rebuilt on new plans — welfare states west, command economies east, and the peace already hardening into two camps.
Now zoom to Turkey's doorstep: mandates ending, new states appearing — Israel in 1948 — and a new conflict born with them.
That is the world the next lessons live in: two superpowers above, a redrawn Middle East beside.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
