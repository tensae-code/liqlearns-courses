# Hands-on — The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The guns stop. Now count what the war changed: who is strong, who is finished, what new lines are drawn on the map — including on Turkey's own doorstep. Say out loud: "Two superpowers inherit the world."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Second World War's results redrew the world: two superpowers — the United States and the Soviet Union — inherited it, the old empires waned, a new world organisation rose, and next door to Turkey the Middle East was redrawn, with new states and new conflicts. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
