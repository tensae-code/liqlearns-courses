# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three columns in which the results are counted.
**Answer:** Political, social and economic.

**2.** Why does the book end the results in the Middle East?
**Answer:** Because that is where Turkey lives: the redrawn region — new states and the Arab-Israeli conflict — becomes the setting of Turkey's own later foreign policy, told in this book's last units.
