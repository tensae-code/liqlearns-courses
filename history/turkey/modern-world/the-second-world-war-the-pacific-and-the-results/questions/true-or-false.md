# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war left tens of millions dead and cities in rubble across Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Middle East was left unchanged by the war's results.
**Answer: False** — next door to Turkey the region was redrawn — mandates ended, new states appeared, among them Israel in 1948, and the Arab-Israeli conflict began.
