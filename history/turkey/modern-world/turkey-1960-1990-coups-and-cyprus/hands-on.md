# Hands-on — Turkey 1960–1990: Coups and Cyprus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Three times in thirty years, the army steps in. One island, just off the southern coast, pulls the country to the brink again and again — until 1974. And underneath the politics, a whole people moves from village to city. Say out loud: "Interventions, an island, and a country remade."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Turkey's 1960–1990 decades are a double story: at home, three military interventions punctuate a country remade by migration and markets; abroad, foreign policy is dominated by the Cyprus problem — the island crisis that ends in 1974 — by relations with Greece, and by the Armenian question. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
