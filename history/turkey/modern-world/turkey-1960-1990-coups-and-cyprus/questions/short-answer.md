# Turkey 1960–1990: Coups and Cyprus — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the foreign-policy files of these decades, from the lesson.
**Answer:** The Cyprus problem above all; relations with Greece; and the Armenian question, with its claims and the attacks on Turkish diplomats.

**2.** What remade Turkey beneath the politics, in the book's beat?
**Answer:** Migration and markets: millions moving from villages to cities and to work in Europe, with industry and exports growing — a different society by 1990.
