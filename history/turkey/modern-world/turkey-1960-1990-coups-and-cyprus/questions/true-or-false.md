# Turkey 1960–1990: Coups and Cyprus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Turkey was one of Cyprus's guarantor powers from its independence in 1960.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Turkey's society in 1990 was much the same peasant republic as in 1960.
**Answer: False** — migration and markets remade it — urban, commercial, transformed by movement to the cities and work abroad.
