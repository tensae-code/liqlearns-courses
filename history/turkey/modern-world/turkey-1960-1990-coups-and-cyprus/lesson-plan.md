# Lesson plan — Turkey 1960–1990: Coups and Cyprus

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey 1960–1990: Coups and Cyprus
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Turkey's 1960–1990 decades are a double story: at home, three military interventions punctuate a country remade by migration and markets; abroad, foreign policy is dominated by the Cyprus problem — the island crisis that ends in 1974 — by relations with Greece, and by the Armenian question.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks 1974 was the start of the Cyprus problem → the problem runs from independence in 1960 through years of crisis; 1974 — junta coup, then Turkish intervention — is its ending in division, not its beginning.
- Thinks the decades are only coups → the book's braid is double: three interventions above, and below them migration and markets remaking the whole society.
- Go back to the one thing: Turkey's 1960–1990 decades are a double story: at home, three military interventions punctuate a country remade by migration and markets; abroad, foreign policy is dominated by the Cyprus problem — the island crisis that ends in 1974 — by relations with Greece, and by the Armenian question. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
