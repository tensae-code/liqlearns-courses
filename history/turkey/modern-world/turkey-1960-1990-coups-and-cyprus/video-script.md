# Video script — Turkey 1960–1990: Coups and Cyprus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Turkey 1960–1990: three interventions, one island crisis ending in 1974 — and a country remade by migration and markets.
Abroad first: Cyprus. An island shared by two communities, guaranteed by Turkey, sliding into violence — Athens and Ankara, allies, again and again at the brink. 1974: a junta coup on the island; Turkey intervenes; the island divides, and stays divided.
At home, the punctuation marks: 1960, 1971, 1980 — three times the military steps in and resets the order.
And underneath everything, the quiet transformation: village squares emptying, city suburbs rising, workers leaving for Europe, factories and exports growing. The Turkey of 1990 is not the Turkey of 1960.
Coups and Cyprus above; migration and markets below. That braid is the lesson.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
