# Video script — Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Three fronts — and a regular army built from nothing that won them all.
East first: Kars. The border settled, the front closed, soldiers freed for the west. South: city by city — Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana — streets defended by their own people; Antep walks away with a new name: Gazi, the Veteran.
Now the main war, the Western Front. İnönü: the advance checked. Kütahya-Eskişehir: the hard retreat. Sakarya: the river line held — while at home the Tekâlif-i Milliye empties the nation's cupboards into the army's carts: socks, grain, oxen, guns.
Then August 1922. The Great Offensive breaks the line; 30 August, the Commander-in-Chief's battle ends it. A hundred miles in a fortnight — İzmir, 9 September. The war is won.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
