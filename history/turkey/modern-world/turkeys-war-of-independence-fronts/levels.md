# Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The War of Independence was fought on three fronts — the Eastern Front settled at Kars; the Southern Front's cities (Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana) defending themselves street by street; and the Western Front, where a regular army built from nothing won İnönü, survived Kütahya-Eskişehir, held at Sakarya on the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies, and ended the war with the Great Offensive and the Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August 1922.
