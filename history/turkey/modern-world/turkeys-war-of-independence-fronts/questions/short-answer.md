# Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three fronts and how each was settled.
**Answer:** Eastern: settled around Kars, securing the east. Southern: the cities — Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana — freed themselves street by street. Western: the regular army's campaign — İnönü, Kütahya-Eskişehir, Sakarya, then the Great Offensive and 30 August 1922.

**2.** What happened on 30 August 1922, and what followed within days?
**Answer:** The Commander-in-Chief's battle broke the Greek army at the climax of the Great Offensive; the hundred-mile advance reached İzmir on 9 September, ending the war.
