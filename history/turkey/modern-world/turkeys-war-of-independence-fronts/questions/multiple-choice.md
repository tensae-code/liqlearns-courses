# Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which city's resistance earned it the title 'Gazi' — the Veteran?
a) Maraş  b) Urfa  c) Antep  d) Adana
**Answer: c) Antep** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were the Tekâlif-i Milliye?
a) A Greek army corps  b) The National Obligations — the whole people levied for the army's needs  c) A peace treaty  d) A tax on foreigners
**Answer: b) The National Obligations — the whole people levied for the army's needs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
