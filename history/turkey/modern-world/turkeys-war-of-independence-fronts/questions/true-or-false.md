# Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The regular army of the Western Front was built from nothing, out of the resistance and the Assembly's levies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The battle of Sakarya ended the war in 1922.
**Answer: False** — Sakarya held the line in 1921; the war ended with the Great Offensive and the Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August 1922.
