# Hands-on — Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Three fronts at once — east, south and west — and an army that, two years earlier, did not exist. Its people give it their socks, their grain, their ox-carts. Say out loud: "An army built from nothing, fed by a nation."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The War of Independence was fought on three fronts — the Eastern Front settled at Kars; the Southern Front's cities (Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana) defending themselves street by street; and the Western Front, where a regular army built from nothing won İnönü, survived Kütahya-Eskişehir, held at Sakarya on the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies, and ended the war with the Great Offensive and the Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August 1922. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
