# Lesson plan — Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The War of Independence was fought on three fronts — the Eastern Front settled at Kars; the Southern Front's cities (Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana) defending themselves street by street; and the Western Front, where a regular army built from nothing won İnönü, survived Kütahya-Eskişehir, held at Sakarya on the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies, and ended the war with the Great Offensive and the Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August 1922.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the fronts' settlements → east is settled by treaty-ground around Kars; the south frees its own cities (Gazi Antep); the west is decided by the regular army's battles, İnönü to Sakarya to 30 August.
- Thinks Sakarya was the final victory → Sakarya holds the line (1921); the war is ended a year later by the Great Offensive and the 30 August 1922 battle.
- Go back to the one thing: The War of Independence was fought on three fronts — the Eastern Front settled at Kars; the Southern Front's cities (Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana) defending themselves street by street; and the Western Front, where a regular army built from nothing won İnönü, survived Kütahya-Eskişehir, held at Sakarya on the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies, and ended the war with the Great Offensive and the Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August 1922. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
