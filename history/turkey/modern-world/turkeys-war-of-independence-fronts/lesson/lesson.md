# Turkey's War of Independence: The Fronts

## Do it first

Three fronts at once — east, south and west — and an army that, two years earlier, did not exist. Its people give it their socks, their grain, their ox-carts. Say out loud: "An army built from nothing, fed by a nation." This lesson fights the War of Independence, front by front.

## The story

The second state of the last lesson had a claim; this lesson is the claim defended in arms, on **three fronts** at once.

The **Eastern Front** is settled first: the Assembly's army advances against Armenia and the border is fixed by the settlement around **Kars** — the east secured, freeing every soldier for the greater danger in the west.

The **Southern Front** is a people's war of cities against French occupation: **Maraş, Urfa, Antep and Adana** defend themselves street by street — Antep's resistance earns it the title **Gazi**, 'the Veteran', worn in its name to this day, Gaziantep. The south frees itself largely by its own hand.

The **Western Front** is the main war, against the Greek army advancing from İzmir — and it is fought by a **regular army built from nothing** out of the Kuvâ-yi Milliye and the Assembly's levies. Its stations are the book's litany: the **İnönü battles**, first checks to the advance; **Kütahya-Eskişehir**, a hard retreat that trades ground for time; then **Sakarya** — the river-line battle where Mustafa Kemal commands in person ('there is no line of defence, there is a surface of defence, and that surface is the whole homeland'), fed by the **Tekâlif-i Milliye** — the National Obligations, the whole people levied for socks, grain, carts and guns.

And the ending comes at a run: the **Great Offensive** of August 1922, and the **Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August** — the Greek army broken in a fortnight's hundred-mile offensive, İzmir reached on 9 September. The book's beat: **three fronts, one regular army built from nothing, and a hundred-mile offensive that ends the war in a fortnight**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The War of Independence was fought on three fronts — the Eastern Front settled at Kars; the Southern Front's cities (Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana) defending themselves street by street; and the Western Front, where a regular army built from nothing won İnönü, survived Kütahya-Eskişehir, held at Sakarya on the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies, and ended the war with the Great Offensive and the Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August 1922. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
