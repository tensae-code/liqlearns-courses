# The Second World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three paper walls the world built against war.
**Answer:** The League of Nations; the Locarno treaties; the Kellogg-Briand Pact.

**2.** How did the 1929 crisis help cause the war?
**Answer:** Mass unemployment and despair discredited democracies and lifted regimes that promised bread, work and pride by any means — the dictatorships the paper walls could not stop.
