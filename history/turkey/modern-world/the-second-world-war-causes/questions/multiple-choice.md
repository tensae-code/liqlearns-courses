# The Second World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the Kellogg-Briand Pact?
a) A trade deal  b) The pact in which nations renounced war itself  c) A military alliance  d) A peace treaty with Turkey
**Answer: b) The pact in which nations renounced war itself** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What 'blew down' the paper walls, in the book's telling?
a) A single battle  b) The 1929 world economic crisis  c) The Montreux Convention  d) A revolution in France
**Answer: b) The 1929 world economic crisis** — That is what this lesson teaches.
