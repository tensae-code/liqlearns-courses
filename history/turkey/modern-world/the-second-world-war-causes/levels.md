# The Second World War: Causes — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The road to the Second World War runs through failed peace-keeping — the League of Nations, Locarno and Kellogg-Briand, paper walls against war — and through the 1929 world economic crisis, which blew those walls down and lifted the regimes that marched the world back to war.
