# Video script — The Second World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Paper walls against war — and the 1929 crash blew them down.
See the walls go up after 1919: the League of Nations, nations promising to talk, not fight. Locarno: borders guaranteed by signature. Kellogg-Briand: war itself renounced, on paper, by the world.
Now test the walls: no army behind the League, promises binding only while wished. Aggressors push — the paper tears, and nothing happens to them.
Then the wind: 1929. Watch the crash roll from America across the world — factories dark, queues of unemployed, democracies blamed. Desperate peoples reach for dictators who promise bread and pride.
The walls were paper. The crash was the storm. The next lesson is the war.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
