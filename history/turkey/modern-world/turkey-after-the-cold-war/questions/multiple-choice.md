# Turkey After the Cold War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened to the Soviet Union in 1991?
a) It joined NATO  b) It dissolved — broke up into new states  c) It invaded Turkey  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) It dissolved — broke up into new states** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What war came to Turkey's southern border in 1991?
a) The Iran-Iraq War  b) The Gulf War, after Iraq's invasion of Kuwait  c) The Korean War  d) The Six-Day War
**Answer: b) The Gulf War, after Iraq's invasion of Kuwait** — That is what this lesson teaches.
