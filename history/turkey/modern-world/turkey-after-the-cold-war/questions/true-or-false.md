# Turkey After the Cold War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Several of the new states east of Turkey after 1991 were Turkic republics.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The book closes Turkey's post-Cold War story with a final settlement of its place in the world.
**Answer: False** — it closes deliberately open — a renegotiation between Europe and its region, on the twenty-first century's threshold.
