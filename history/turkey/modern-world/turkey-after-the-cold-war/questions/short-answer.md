# Turkey After the Cold War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two great changes open this lesson's period?
**Answer:** The Berlin Wall falls (1989) and the Eastern bloc and then the Soviet Union dissolve (1991) — ending the Cold War order; and war comes to the neighbourhood, above all the Gulf War of 1991.

**2.** Between what two poles does the republic renegotiate its place?
**Answer:** Between Europe — its European vocation, customs union and candidacy steps — and its own region, where it builds weight as a bridge and a power: Turkic east, Balkans and Middle East.
