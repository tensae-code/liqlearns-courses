# Lesson plan — Turkey After the Cold War

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey After the Cold War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After 1990 the Cold War's world falls away: the wall falls, the neighbourhood catches fire — the Gulf crisis and wars on Turkey's borders — and the Republic renegotiates its place between Europe and its region on the threshold of the twenty-first century.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After 1990 the Cold War's world falls away: the wall falls, the neighbourhood catches fire — the Gulf crisis and wars on Turkey's borders — and the Republic renegotiates its place between Europe and its region on the threshold of the twenty-first century.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a bridge-city whose two great neighbouring empires suddenly vanish or change: every road through it must be renegotiated, and every neighbour's quarrel arrives at its gates.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Cold War's end brought Turkey a quiet neighbourhood → the book's beat is the opposite: the wall falls and the neighbourhood catches fire — Gulf War, Balkans, Caucasus — making Turkey a frontier state in every direction.
- Go back to the one thing: After 1990 the Cold War's world falls away: the wall falls, the neighbourhood catches fire — the Gulf crisis and wars on Turkey's borders — and the Republic renegotiates its place between Europe and its region on the threshold of the twenty-first century. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
