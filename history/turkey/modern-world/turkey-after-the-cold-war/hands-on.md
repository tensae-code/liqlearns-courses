# Hands-on — Turkey After the Cold War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The wall falls. The superpower that faced Turkey across the Black Sea dissolves. And in the same years, war comes to the neighbours — Iraq invades Kuwait, and the Gulf burns on Turkey's own border. Say out loud: "A new world, and the republic renegotiates its place." This final lesson brings Turkey to the twenty-first century's threshold.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After 1990 the Cold War's world falls away: the wall falls, the neighbourhood catches fire — the Gulf crisis and wars on Turkey's borders — and the Republic renegotiates its place between Europe and its region on the threshold of the twenty-first century. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
