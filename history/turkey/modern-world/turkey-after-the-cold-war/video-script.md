# Video script — Turkey After the Cold War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. After 1990: the wall falls, the neighbourhood catches fire, and Turkey renegotiates its place between Europe and its region.
1989: the Berlin Wall comes down. 1991: the Soviet Union itself dissolves. Watch Turkey's Cold War map lose its meaning — the Black Sea ringed now by new states, several of them Turkic republics, a new east opening.
But peace does not follow. 1991: the Gulf War on the southern border — sanctions, refugees, a remade northern Iraq. Fires in the Balkans and the Caucasus on the other edges.
So the republic renegotiates: toward Europe — customs union, candidacy steps — and toward its region, building weight as bridge and power alike.
The book ends open, on a threshold. So does this course — exactly where Turkey's books in hand end.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
