# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why is the Cold War called 'cold'?
a) It was fought in winter  b) The superpowers never fought each other directly  c) It involved no weapons  d) It lasted only a year
**Answer: b) The superpowers never fought each other directly** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What put Turkey on the Cold War's front line?
a) Its deserts  b) Its straits and its borders facing the Soviet Union  c) Its colonies  d) Its oil
**Answer: b) Its straits and its borders facing the Soviet Union** — That is what this lesson teaches.
