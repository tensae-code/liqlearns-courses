# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** NATO and the Warsaw Pact were the two blocs' alliances.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Cold War had no real wars anywhere.
**Answer: False** — the centre stayed frozen, but hot wars were fought at the edges through allies — Korea first among them.
