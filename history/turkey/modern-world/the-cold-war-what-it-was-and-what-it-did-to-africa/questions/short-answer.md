# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the two blocs and their leaders.
**Answer:** The Eastern bloc, led by the Soviet Union; the Western bloc, led by the United States.

**2.** By what means was the Cold War fought, if not by direct battle?
**Answer:** Alliances, the arms race, rival ideologies, espionage and propaganda — and proxy wars at the edges fought through allies and clients.
