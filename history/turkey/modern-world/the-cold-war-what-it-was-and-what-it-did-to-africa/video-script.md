# Video script — The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Cold War: two armed camps, a frozen centre, hot edges — and Turkey on the front line.
Draw the line down the world: east, the Soviet-led bloc; west, the American-led. Alliances harden along it — NATO here, the Warsaw Pact there. Arsenals grow; ideologies preach across it.
But at the centre, no direct battle: the giants, nuclear-armed, dare not swing. So the war leaks to the edges — Korea, and proxy struggles after it.
Now find Turkey on the map: the Straits, the Black Sea shore, the Caucasus border — the Western camp's south-eastern bastion, on the front line of a war that never declares itself.
That frozen geography is the air the rest of this book breathes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
