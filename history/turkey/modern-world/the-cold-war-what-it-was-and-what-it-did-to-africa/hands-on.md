# Hands-on — The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A war with no declaration, no final battle, and two armed camps facing each other for forty years. Its front line runs through Turkey's own straits. Say out loud: "Two armed camps, one frozen war."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Cold War split the world into two armed camps — the Eastern and Western blocs — a war of alliances, arms and ideology fought without direct battle between the superpowers; and Turkey's Straits put it on the front line of that frozen conflict. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
