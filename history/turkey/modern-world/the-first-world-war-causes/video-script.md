# Video script — The First World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The empire gambled its existence on one alliance — and the Great War became its last war.
Open on Europe, 1914: alliance systems like loaded springs, great powers bound to their partners, a local shot pulling all of them into general war.
Now Istanbul. The government of a strained empire weighs its fears — Russia above all — and its hopes of recovery. A signature: alliance with Germany and the Central Powers. Ottoman guns open the empire's war.
Name the bet plainly: not a province, not a border — the state's existence, staked on partners' victory.
Two pages, one signature. The fronts come next.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
