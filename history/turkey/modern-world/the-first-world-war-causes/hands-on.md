# Hands-on — The First World War: Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A war starts in Europe between other people's empires. The Ottoman state does not have to enter it — and then it does, by one signature, betting everything. Say out loud: "The empire gambled its existence on one alliance."

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The First World War drew the Ottoman state in through alliance: the empire gambled its very existence on one alliance — joining the Central Powers — and so a European war became the empire's last war. Draw it or lay it out with objects, part by part, and say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
