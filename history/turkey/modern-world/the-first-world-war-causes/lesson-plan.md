# Lesson plan — The First World War: Causes

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The First World War: Causes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The First World War drew the Ottoman state in through alliance: the empire gambled its very existence on one alliance — joining the Central Powers — and so a European war became the empire's last war.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Empire's End and the War of Independence (recommended, never forced).

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The First World War drew the Ottoman state in through alliance: the empire gambled its very existence on one alliance — joining the Central Powers — and so a European war became the empire's last war.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Ottomans were attacked into the war with no choice → the book's framing is a gamble chosen: alliance with the Central Powers, signed by the empire's own government, staking the state's existence.
- Go back to the one thing: The First World War drew the Ottoman state in through alliance: the empire gambled its very existence on one alliance — joining the Central Powers — and so a European war became the empire's last war. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
