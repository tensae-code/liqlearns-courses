# The First World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did the Ottoman state enter the First World War?
a) By invasion only  b) By alliance with Germany and the Central Powers  c) By declaring neutrality  d) By a Seljuk treaty
**Answer: b) By alliance with Germany and the Central Powers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the empire stake on the alliance, in the book's words?
a) One province  b) Its existence — the state itself  c) Its navy only  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Its existence — the state itself** — That is what this lesson teaches.
