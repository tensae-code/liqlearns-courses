# The First World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the Ottoman government seek the alliance?
**Answer:** Fearing Russia above all, and hoping to recover lost ground, the strained empire sought the protection and promise of Germany and the Central Powers.

**2.** Why does the book call the entry a gamble?
**Answer:** Because the stake was total: win with its partners and the empire might recover; lose, and there might be no empire left at all.
