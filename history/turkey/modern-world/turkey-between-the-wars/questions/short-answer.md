# Turkey Between the Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four joinings and settlements of the 1930s in date order.
**Answer:** League of Nations (1932); Balkan Entente (1934); Montreux Convention (1936); Sadabad Pact (1937) — with Hatay joining the homeland at the period's end.

**2.** What happened at home in 1926, and what happened on 10 November 1938?
**Answer:** In 1926 an assassination attempt on Atatürk was uncovered and punished; on 10 November 1938 Atatürk died — at 09:05, the moment the nation's poems remember.
