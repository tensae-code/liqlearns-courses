# Turkey Between the Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Montreux Convention of 1936 restore to Turkey?
a) Mosul  b) Full sovereignty over the Straits, with the right to fortify them  c) Hatay  d) The caliphate
**Answer: b) Full sovereignty over the Straits, with the right to fortify them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was Atatürk's foreign-policy maxim?
a) Expansion in all directions  b) Peace at home, peace in the world  c) Neutrality forever  d) Alliance with one camp only
**Answer: b) Peace at home, peace in the world** — That is what this lesson teaches.
