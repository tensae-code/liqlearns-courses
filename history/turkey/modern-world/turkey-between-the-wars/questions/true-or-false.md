# Turkey Between the Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Turkey joined the League of Nations in 1932.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The early multi-party experiments of the 1920s succeeded and continued.
**Answer: False** — the early parties and multi-party tries failed — opposition parties closed or were closed; full multi-party life came after the Second World War.
