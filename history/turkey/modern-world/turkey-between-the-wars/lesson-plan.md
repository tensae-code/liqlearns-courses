# Lesson plan — Turkey Between the Wars

**Course:** History — Turkey · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey Between the Wars
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Between the wars, Turkey practised Atatürk's maxim — 'peace at home, peace in the world' — border by border: settling with Greece, France, Britain (over Mosul) and the Soviets, joining the League of Nations (1932), building the Balkan Entente (1934), regaining the Straits at Montreux (1936) and the Sadabad Pact (1937), and bringing Hatay home — while at home the early multi-party tries failed, an assassination attempt was made (1926), and the founder died on 10 November 1938.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The Republic Remade and Turkey in the World (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread among the pacts → hang each on its direction: Balkan Entente = west (1934), Sadabad = east (1937), Montreux = the Straits at the centre (1936), League = the world (1932).
- Thinks Mosul was a Turkish victory → the book's honesty: the file was settled with Britain by negotiation and went Britain's way (1926) — peace was chosen over the claim.
- Go back to the one thing: Between the wars, Turkey practised Atatürk's maxim — 'peace at home, peace in the world' — border by border: settling with Greece, France, Britain (over Mosul) and the Soviets, joining the League of Nations (1932), building the Balkan Entente (1934), regaining the Straits at Montreux (1936) and the Sadabad Pact (1937), and bringing Hatay home — while at home the early multi-party tries failed, an assassination attempt was made (1926), and the founder died on 10 November 1938. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
