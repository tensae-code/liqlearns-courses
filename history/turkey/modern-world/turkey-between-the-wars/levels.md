# Turkey Between the Wars — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Between the wars, Turkey practised Atatürk's maxim — 'peace at home, peace in the world' — border by border: settling with Greece, France, Britain (over Mosul) and the Soviets, joining the League of Nations (1932), building the Balkan Entente (1934), regaining the Straits at Montreux (1936) and the Sadabad Pact (1937), and bringing Hatay home — while at home the early multi-party tries failed, an assassination attempt was made (1926), and the founder died on 10 November 1938.
