# Video script — Turkey Between the Wars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. 'Peace at home, peace in the world' — practised border by border, while the founder's chair empties.
At home: new parties tried and failing; 1926, an assassination attempt uncovered. The revolution guards itself as it builds.
Now walk the borders with the maxim in hand. Greece: enemies to partners. Britain: Mosul settled by negotiation. France: normalised. The Soviets: friendship kept. Then the joinings: the League of Nations, 1932. The Balkan Entente, 1934. Montreux, 1936 — the Straits fully Turkey's again, won by pens. Sadabad, 1937. And Hatay, coming home by diplomacy.
Last frame: a clock at 09:05, 10 November 1938. The founder is gone. The policy he named remains the republic's compass.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
