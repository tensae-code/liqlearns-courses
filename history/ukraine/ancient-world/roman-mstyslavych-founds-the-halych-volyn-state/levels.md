# Roman Mstyslavych Founds the Halych-Volyn State — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Roman Mstyslavych, prince of Volyn, took Halych in 1199 and united the two western lands into the strongest state in Rus — then died in Poland in 1205, leaving infant sons and circling boyars and kings — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
