# Roman Mstyslavych Founds the Halych-Volyn State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Volyn-Halych state was the strongest state in Rus when Roman made it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Roman's sons were grown men when he died.
**Answer: False** — Danylo and Vasylko were small children — which is why boyars and foreign kings circled at once.
