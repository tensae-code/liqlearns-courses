Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did Roman unite Volyn and Halych?
a) 1199  b) 1097  c) 1240  d) 1362
**Answer: a) 1199** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened to Roman's state after his death in 1205?
a) boyars rose and Poland and Hungary reached for the inheritance of his infant sons  b) it passed peacefully to his adult son  c) it was annexed by Kyiv  d) nothing changed
**Answer: a) boyars rose and Poland and Hungary reached for the inheritance of his infant sons** — That is what this lesson teaches.
