# Roman Mstyslavych Founds the Halych-Volyn State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was the united western state so strong?
**Answer:** It joined Halych's salt and trade wealth to Volyn under one ruler, respected abroad and feared by the steppe.

**2.** Who were Roman's sons?
**Answer:** Danylo and Vasylko.
