# Lesson plan — Roman Mstyslavych Founds the Halych-Volyn State

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Roman Mstyslavych Founds the Halych-Volyn State
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Halych-Volyn and the Mongol Storm (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Roman Mstyslavych, prince of Volyn, took Halych in 1199 and united the two western lands into the strongest state in Rus — then died in Poland in 1205, leaving infant sons and circling boyars and kings.
