# Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Yaroslav end the Pecheneg threat to Kyiv?
**Answer:** He defeated them in battle, ending their danger to the capital for good.

**2.** Why is 'the Wise' a fitting name for Yaroslav, from this lesson?
**Answer:** He built (St Sophia), bound Rus to Europe by marriages, and above all gave it a written law code.
