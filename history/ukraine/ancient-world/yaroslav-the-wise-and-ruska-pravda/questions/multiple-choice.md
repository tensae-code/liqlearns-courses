# Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the Ruska Pravda?
a) Rus's first written law code  b) a chronicle of Yaroslav's wars  c) a church service book  d) a list of tribute dues
**Answer: a) Rus's first written law code** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Ruska Pravda put in place of blood vengeance?
a) fines fixed by law  b) exile for every wrong  c) trial by combat  d) nothing — vengeance stayed
**Answer: a) fines fixed by law** — That is what this lesson teaches.
