# Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Yaroslav beat his brothers and the Pechenegs, raised St Sophia in Kyiv, married his children into Europe's royal houses — and gave Rus its first written law code, the Ruska Pravda, replacing blood vengeance with fines — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
