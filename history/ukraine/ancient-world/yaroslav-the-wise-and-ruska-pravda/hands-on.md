# Hands-on — Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A feud is settled two ways: with a sword, or with a payment fixed by law. Before reading, decide which way builds a safer country — then meet the prince who chose the second and wrote it down.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Yaroslav beat his brothers and the Pechenegs, raised St Sophia in Kyiv, married his children into Europe's royal houses — and gave Rus its first written law code, the Ruska Pravda, replacing blood vengeance with fines. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
