# Lesson plan — Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Golden Age: Volodymyr and Yaroslav (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Yaroslav beat his brothers and the Pechenegs, raised St Sophia in Kyiv, married his children into Europe's royal houses — and gave Rus its first written law code, the Ruska Pravda, replacing blood vengeance with fines.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Golden Age: Volodymyr and Yaroslav — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
