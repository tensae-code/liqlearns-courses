# Video script — Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Yaroslav beat his brothers and the pechenegs, raised st sophia in kyiv, married his children into europe's royal houses — and gave rus its first written law code, the ruska pravda, replacing blood vengeance with fines.

Yaroslav, called the Wise, fought his way to sole rule through the brothers' wars that followed Volodymyr's death, and finished what his father began against the steppe: his victory over the Pechenegs ended their threat to Kyiv for good.

In peace he built. St Sophia's cathedral rose in Kyiv — stone, mosaics and frescoes declaring that Rus stood level with the great Christian capitals. His children married into Europe's royal houses, from France to Norway to Hungary, until Yaroslav was called the father-in-law of Europe.

His deepest gift was a book of law: the Ruska Pravda, Rus's first written law code. Where custom had allowed blood vengeance — a killing answered by a killing — the code set fines: wrongs were paid for in money, at rates fixed by law, the same for all. A state that began with tribute rounds now judged by a written rule.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
