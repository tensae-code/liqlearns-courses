# The Halych-Volyn State after Danylo — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yurii I took the royal title of king of Rus again.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Halych-Volyn state ended in a great Mongol sack of Lviv.
**Answer: False** — It ended by succession — the line died out in 1340 and Poland and Lithuania divided the inheritance.
