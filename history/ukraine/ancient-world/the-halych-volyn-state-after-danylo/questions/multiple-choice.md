# The Halych-Volyn State after Danylo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** To which city did Lev move the capital?
a) Lviv  b) Kholm  c) Kyiv  d) Halych
**Answer: a) Lviv** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened in 1340?
