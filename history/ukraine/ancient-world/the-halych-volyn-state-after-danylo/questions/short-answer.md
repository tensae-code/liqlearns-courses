# The Halych-Volyn State after Danylo — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who took Halych after the line ended, and who took Volyn?
**Answer:** Poland's king Casimir III took Halych; Lithuania gathered Volyn.

**2.** Why is a state carried by one family fragile, from this lesson?
**Answer:** When the family's heirs thin out and end, there is no succession — neighbours divide what the line can no longer hold.
