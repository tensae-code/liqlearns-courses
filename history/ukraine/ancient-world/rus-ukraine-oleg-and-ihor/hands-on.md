# Hands-on — Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two rulers, two ends. One dies with treaties in his hand; the other is killed by the very people who pay him. Before reading, guess: what makes tribute fair, and what makes it deadly? Keep your answer for the end of the lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Oleh seized Kyiv in 882, subdued the neighbouring tribes and won trade treaties from Byzantium; Ihor kept the tribute rounds going until the Derevlians, squeezed once too often, killed him in 945. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
