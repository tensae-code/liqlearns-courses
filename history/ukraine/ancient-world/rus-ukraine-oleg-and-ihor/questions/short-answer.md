# Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the trade treaties with Byzantium give Rus?
**Answer:** The right for Rus merchants to come to Constantinople, trade and be protected there.

**2.** Why did the Derevlians kill Ihor?
**Answer:** He demanded their tribute a second time in the same round — squeezed once too often, they rose and killed him.
