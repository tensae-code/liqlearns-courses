# Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Byzantium signed trade treaties with Rus after Oleh's campaigns.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ihor died on campaign against Constantinople.
**Answer: False** — He was killed by the Derevlians, his own tributaries, in 945.
