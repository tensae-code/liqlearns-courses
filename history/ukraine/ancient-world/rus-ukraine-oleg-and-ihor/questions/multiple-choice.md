# Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did Oleh seize Kyiv?
a) 882  b) 988  c) 945  d) 1240
**Answer: a) 882** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who killed Prince Ihor in 945?
a) the Derevlians, after he demanded tribute a second time  b) the Byzantines  c) the Pechenegs  d) his own brother
**Answer: a) the Derevlians, after he demanded tribute a second time** — That is what this lesson teaches.
