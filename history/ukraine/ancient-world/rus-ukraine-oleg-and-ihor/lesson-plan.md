# Lesson plan — Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Origins: Slavs, Kyiv and the Birth of Rus-Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Oleh seized Kyiv in 882, subdued the neighbouring tribes and won trade treaties from Byzantium; Ihor kept the tribute rounds going until the Derevlians, squeezed once too often, killed him in 945.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Origins: Slavs, Kyiv and the Birth of Rus-Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
