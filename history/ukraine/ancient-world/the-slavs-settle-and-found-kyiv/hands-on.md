# Hands-on — The Slavs Settle and Kyiv Is Founded

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Tell the founding story as the chronicle tells it: three brothers and a sister on green hills above a great river. Then ask: why would people choose those hills? List three reasons a river hill is a good place for a city.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Slavic tribes spread across Eastern Europe, farmed, traded and joined into tribal unions — and on the Dnipro hills Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid founded Kyiv, the city everything after this grows from. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
