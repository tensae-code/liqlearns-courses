# The Slavs Settle and Kyiv Is Founded — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Slavic tribes spread across Eastern Europe, farmed, traded and joined into tribal unions — and on the Dnipro hills Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid founded Kyiv, the city everything after this grows from — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
