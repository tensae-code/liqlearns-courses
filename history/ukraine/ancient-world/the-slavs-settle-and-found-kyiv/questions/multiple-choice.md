# The Slavs Settle and Kyiv Is Founded — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** According to the chronicle's founding story, who founded Kyiv?
a) Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid  b) Oleh alone  c) three Greek merchants  d) the Scythian kings
**Answer: a) Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How were the Slavs organised as they spread across Eastern Europe?
a) in tribes joined into tribal unions  b) in one empire  c) in city republics  d) as lone families with no ties
**Answer: a) in tribes joined into tribal unions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
