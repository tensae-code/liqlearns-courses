# The Slavs Settle and Kyiv Is Founded — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kyiv stands on hills above the Dnipro river.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Slavs lived only by hunting and never farmed.
**Answer: False** — They farmed, raised cattle, worked crafts and traded along the rivers.
