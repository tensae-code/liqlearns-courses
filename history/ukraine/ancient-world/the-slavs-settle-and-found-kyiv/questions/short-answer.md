# The Slavs Settle and Kyiv Is Founded — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was the hill site above the Dnipro a strong place for a city?
**Answer:** The hills overlooked and controlled the river crossing on the road between the forest and the steppe, and were easy to defend.

**2.** From which brother does the name Kyiv come, in the founding story?
**Answer:** Kyi, the eldest of the three brothers.
