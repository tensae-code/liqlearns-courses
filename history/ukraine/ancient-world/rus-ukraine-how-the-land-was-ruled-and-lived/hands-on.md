# Hands-on — How Rus-Ukraine Was Ruled and Lived

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Build the ladder of this society on paper: draw five steps and, as you read, put each group on its step — prince, boyars, druzhyna, smerds, zakupy, kholopy — and write beside each what they did all day.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the golden-age state day to day: prince, boyars, druzhyna and viche at the top; free smerds, dependent zakupy and kholopy below; towns of craftsmen and merchants and villages of farmers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
