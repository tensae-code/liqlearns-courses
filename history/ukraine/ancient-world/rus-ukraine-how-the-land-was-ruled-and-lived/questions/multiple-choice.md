# How Rus-Ukraine Was Ruled and Lived — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who were the smerds?
a) free peasants working their own land  b) the prince's armed retinue  c) town merchants  d) wholly unfree slaves
**Answer: a) free peasants working their own land** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was the viche?
a) the assembly of free townsmen  b) the prince's law book  c) a tribute collection point  d) a frontier fortress
**Answer: a) the assembly of free townsmen** — That is what this lesson teaches.
