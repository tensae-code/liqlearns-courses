# How Rus-Ukraine Was Ruled and Lived — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zakupy were men working off a loan on the lender's land.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Kholopy were free farmers who owned their land.
**Answer: False** — Kholopy stood at the bottom — wholly unfree, owned like property.
