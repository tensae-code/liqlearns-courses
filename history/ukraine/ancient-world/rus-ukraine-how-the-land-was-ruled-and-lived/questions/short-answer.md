# How Rus-Ukraine Was Ruled and Lived — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three groups at the top of the state beside the prince.
**Answer:** The boyars (great landowners and advisers), the druzhyna (armed retinue), and in the towns the viche assembly.

**2.** What did the towns add to the life of Rus?
**Answer:** Crafts — smiths, potters, jewellers, armourers — and trade, with merchants carrying goods to Constantinople and Europe.
