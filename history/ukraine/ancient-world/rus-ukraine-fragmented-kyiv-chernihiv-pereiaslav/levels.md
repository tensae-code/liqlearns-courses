# Fragmented Rus: Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — after Mstyslav the unity was gone for good — Kyiv was fought over and even sacked by a Rus prince in 1169, while Chernihiv and Pereiaslav went their own ways on the steppe frontier — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
