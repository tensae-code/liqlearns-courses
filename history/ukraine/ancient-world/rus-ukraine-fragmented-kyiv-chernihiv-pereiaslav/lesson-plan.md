# Lesson plan — Fragmented Rus: Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Fragmented Rus: Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Fragmentation: Many Princes, One Land (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after Mstyslav the unity was gone for good — Kyiv was fought over and even sacked by a Rus prince in 1169, while Chernihiv and Pereiaslav went their own ways on the steppe frontier.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Fragmentation: Many Princes, One Land — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
