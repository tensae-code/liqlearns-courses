# Fragmented Rus: Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After 1132 the unity of Rus was gone for good.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Andrei Bogoliubsky stayed in Kyiv and ruled it after 1169.
**Answer: False** — He sacked the city and did not stay to rule it — he ruled from his own north-eastern land.
