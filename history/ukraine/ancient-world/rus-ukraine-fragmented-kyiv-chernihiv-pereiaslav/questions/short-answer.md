# Fragmented Rus: Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the sack of 1169 matter so much in this story?
**Answer:** A Rus prince plundered the mother city of Rus itself — proof that the old centre no longer held anyone's loyalty.

**2.** Name the three great southern principalities of the fragmented age.
**Answer:** Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav.
