# Fragmented Rus: Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who sacked Kyiv in 1169?
a) Andrei Bogoliubsky, a Rus prince of the north-east  b) Batu the Mongol  c) Oleh of Chernihiv  d) a Polovtsian khan
**Answer: a) Andrei Bogoliubsky, a Rus prince of the north-east** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was Pereiaslav's special role among the southern principalities?
a) the frontier shield on the steppe edge, first to meet the raids  b) the main sea port  c) the church capital  d) the salt-trading centre
**Answer: a) the frontier shield on the steppe edge, first to meet the raids** — That is what this lesson teaches.
