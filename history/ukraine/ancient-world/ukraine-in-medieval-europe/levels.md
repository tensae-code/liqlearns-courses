# Ukraine in Medieval Europe: The Course Closes — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the course closes by testing Serhii Plokhy's image of Ukraine as 'the gates of Europe' against everything the year has covered — a journey through medieval Ukraine from Kyiv's founding to the end of the 15th century — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
