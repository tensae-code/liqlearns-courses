# Hands-on — Ukraine in Medieval Europe: The Course Closes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One image for a whole course: gates. Before reading, walk the course backwards — founding, baptism, law, division, Mongols, kings, Lithuania, towns — and decide at each stop: is this a gate opening, or a gate being shut?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the course closes by testing Serhii Plokhy's image of Ukraine as 'the gates of Europe' against everything the year has covered — a journey through medieval Ukraine from Kyiv's founding to the end of the 15th century. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
