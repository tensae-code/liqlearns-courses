# Lesson plan — Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Origins: Slavs, Kyiv and the Birth of Rus-Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Princess Olha avenged Ihor, then reformed tribute itself — fixed dues and collection points — and was baptised in Constantinople; her son Sviatoslav the Brave smashed Khazaria and died in a Pecheneg ambush in 972.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Origins: Slavs, Kyiv and the Birth of Rus-Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
