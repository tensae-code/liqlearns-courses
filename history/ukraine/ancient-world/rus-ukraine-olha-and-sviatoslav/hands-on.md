# Hands-on — Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A widow takes power. First she punishes the men who killed her husband; then she changes the rule that caused the killing. As you read, keep two lists: what Olha did in anger, and what she did in wisdom. Which list is longer?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Princess Olha avenged Ihor, then reformed tribute itself — fixed dues and collection points — and was baptised in Constantinople; her son Sviatoslav the Brave smashed Khazaria and died in a Pecheneg ambush in 972. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
