# Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did Olha's reform matter for ordinary people?
**Answer:** Tribute became fixed and predictable — set dues at set places — instead of a prince taking whatever he could seize.

**2.** What two worlds did Sviatoslav's wars touch?
**Answer:** The steppe world (Khazaria) and the Balkan-Byzantine world to the south.
