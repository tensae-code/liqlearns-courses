# Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Olha's tribute reform fix?
a) set dues (uroky) and fixed collection points (pohosty)  b) the price of grain in Kyiv  c) the borders with Byzantium  d) the order of succession
**Answer: a) set dues (uroky) and fixed collection points (pohosty)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did Sviatoslav die in 972?
a) in a Pecheneg ambush at the Dnipro rapids  b) in bed in Kyiv  c) fighting in Constantinople  d) in a duel with his brother
**Answer: a) in a Pecheneg ambush at the Dnipro rapids** — That is what this lesson teaches.
