# Volodymyr the Great Baptises Rus-Ukraine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year was Rus baptised?
a) 988  b) 882  c) 945  d) 1253
**Answer: a) 988** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Volodymyr build along the steppe frontier?
a) a ring of fortress towns  b) a fleet of sea ships  c) a wall of earth across the whole steppe  d) monasteries only
**Answer: a) a ring of fortress towns** — That is what this lesson teaches.
