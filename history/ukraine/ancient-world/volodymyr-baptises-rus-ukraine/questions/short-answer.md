# Volodymyr the Great Baptises Rus-Ukraine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things that followed the baptism inside the country.
**Answer:** Churches were built and schools opened (the tithe also sustained the church).

**2.** Why did Volodymyr ring the frontier with fortress towns?
**Answer:** So Pecheneg raids from the steppe would break on defended walls instead of falling on open villages.
