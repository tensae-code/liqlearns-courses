# Volodymyr the Great Baptises Rus-Ukraine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A tithe — a tenth of the prince's income — was set aside for the church after the baptism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The baptism cut Rus off from the rest of Europe.
**Answer: False** — It did the opposite: baptised Rus entered Christian Europe and its family of states.
