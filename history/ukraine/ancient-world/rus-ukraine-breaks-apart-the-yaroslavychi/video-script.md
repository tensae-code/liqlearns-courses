# Video script — Rus-Ukraine Breaks Apart: The Yaroslavychi

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Yaroslav's testament divided rus among his sons, and within years the brothers were at war with each other while the polovtsians raided a land whose princes no longer stood together.

Yaroslav, dying, divided Rus among his sons by testament: Iziaslav, the eldest, received Kyiv and seniority; Sviatoslav received Chernihiv; Vsevolod received Pereiaslav; the younger sons their own towns. The idea was a family ladder — the brothers ruling together, the eldest first.

The ladder broke within years. The Yaroslavychi — Yaroslav's sons — quarrelled over towns, deposed each other from Kyiv, and made war brother on brother. Each prince now thought first of his own share, his own druzhyna, his own quarrels.

And through the cracks came the steppe. The Polovtsians, the new nomad power of the south, raided a land whose princes no longer stood together — defeating divided Rus armies and carrying off plunder and captives. Political fragmentation — one state becoming many rival principalities — is the name of the age that now opens.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
