# Rus-Ukraine Breaks Apart: The Yaroslavychi — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Yaroslav's testament divided Rus among his sons, and within years the brothers were at war with each other while the Polovtsians raided a land whose princes no longer stood together — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
