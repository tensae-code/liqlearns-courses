# Hands-on — Rus-Ukraine Breaks Apart: The Yaroslavychi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One father, five sons, one state. On paper, draw the state as a pie and cut it five ways. Before reading, answer: what holds the slices together once the father is gone? Watch what the brothers do instead.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Yaroslav's testament divided Rus among his sons, and within years the brothers were at war with each other while the Polovtsians raided a land whose princes no longer stood together. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
