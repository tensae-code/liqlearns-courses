# Rus-Ukraine Breaks Apart: The Yaroslavychi — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is 'political fragmentation' as this lesson uses it?
**Answer:** One state breaking into many rival principalities whose princes put their own shares first.

**2.** Which lands did Sviatoslav and Vsevolod receive?
**Answer:** Sviatoslav received Chernihiv; Vsevolod received Pereiaslav.
