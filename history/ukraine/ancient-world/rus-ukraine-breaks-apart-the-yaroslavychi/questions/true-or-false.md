# Rus-Ukraine Breaks Apart: The Yaroslavychi — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yaroslav divided Rus among his sons by testament.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Yaroslavychi ruled together in peace for a generation.
**Answer: False** — Within years they quarrelled, deposed each other and made war brother on brother.
