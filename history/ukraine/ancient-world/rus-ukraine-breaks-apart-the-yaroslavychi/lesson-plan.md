# Lesson plan — Rus-Ukraine Breaks Apart: The Yaroslavychi

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Rus-Ukraine Breaks Apart: The Yaroslavychi

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Rus was conquered by an enemy at this point → no foreign conqueror did this: the princes themselves divided and fought over the inheritance, and the raiders only exploited the cracks.
- Go back to the one thing: Yaroslav's testament divided Rus among his sons, and within years the brothers were at war with each other while the Polovtsians raided a land whose princes no longer stood together. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
