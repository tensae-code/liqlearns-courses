# The Mongols Invade Rus-Ukraine

## Do it first

A warning comes in 1223 — and the land goes back to quarrelling. Seventeen years later the storm returns. Before reading, answer: what should the princes have done with those seventeen years?

In 1223 a Mongol army appeared from the eastern steppe. The Rus princes and their old enemies the Polovtsians joined forces to meet it — and fell together at the Kalka river. The Mongols then vanished back into the steppe, and divided Rus went back to its quarrels, the warning unlearned.

In 1237 they returned under Batu, grandson of Chinggis Khan, with the full strength of an empire. The north-eastern lands fell first; then the storm turned south. In December 1240 Kyiv — the mother city, shrunk by fragmentation but still the symbol of Rus — was stormed, burned and left in ruins, its people killed or scattered.

The Ukrainian lands now lay inside a new order: the Ulus of Jochi, the Golden Horde, ruling the steppe from the Volga. The surviving princes kept their thrones only as the khan's tributaries, travelling to his court for permission to rule. A state tradition that began in the ninth century now had to survive under a foreign yoke — and in the west, it would.
