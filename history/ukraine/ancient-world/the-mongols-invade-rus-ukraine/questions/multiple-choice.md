# The Mongols Invade Rus-Ukraine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did the Rus princes and Polovtsians fall together in 1223?
a) at the Kalka river  b) at Kyiv's walls  c) on the Dnister  d) at Liubech
**Answer: a) at the Kalka river** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When was Kyiv stormed and burned by Batu's army?
a) December 1240  b) spring 1223  c) 1199  d) 1362
**Answer: a) December 1240** — That is what this lesson teaches.
