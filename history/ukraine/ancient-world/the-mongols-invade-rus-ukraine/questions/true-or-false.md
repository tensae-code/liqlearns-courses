# The Mongols Invade Rus-Ukraine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Golden Horde ruled the steppe and took tribute from the Rus princes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The princes used the years after 1223 to unite against the Mongols.
**Answer: False** — They went back to their quarrels — the warning of the Kalka went unlearned.
