# The Mongols Invade Rus-Ukraine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who led the great invasion of 1237–1241?
**Answer:** Batu, grandson of Chinggis Khan.

**2.** What did surviving princes have to do under the Golden Horde?
**Answer:** Rule as the khan's tributaries, travelling to his court for permission — the yarliq — to hold their thrones.
