# The Culture of the Halych-Volyn Land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the Halych-Volyn Chronicle?
a) the western land's own chronicle continuing the Kyiv tradition  b) a Mongol tax register  c) a Polish royal decree  d) a book of church services
**Answer: a) the western land's own chronicle continuing the Kyiv tradition** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Halych-Volyn land preserve through the Mongol centuries?
a) a Ukrainian state tradition — chronicle, royal memory, church and towns  b) only its salt trade  c) the Pecheneg language  d) nothing — its culture vanished
**Answer: a) a Ukrainian state tradition — chronicle, royal memory, church and towns** — That is what this lesson teaches.
