# The Culture of the Halych-Volyn Land — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things besides the chronicle that kept the culture alive.
**Answer:** Church schools and writing; stone fortresses; painted icons (any two).

**2.** Why does this lesson call the west the 'keeper of the inheritance'?
**Answer:** With no Kyiv state standing, the western land preserved Rus's chronicle, royal memory, church and town life for later ages.
