# The Culture of the Halych-Volyn Land — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Stone fortresses rose at places like Kholm, Lviv and Lutsk.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Chronicle writing stopped everywhere in Rus after 1240.
**Answer: False** — It continued in the west — the Halych-Volyn Chronicle carried the Kyiv tradition on.
