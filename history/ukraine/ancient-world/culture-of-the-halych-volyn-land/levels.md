# The Culture of the Halych-Volyn Land — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — under Mongol shadow the west kept the Rus inheritance alive — the Halych-Volyn Chronicle, church schools and writing, stone fortresses and painted icons in the land that preserved a Ukrainian state tradition — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
