# Hands-on — The Culture of the Halych-Volyn Land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
When a state lives under a shadow, what does it save first? Before reading, choose: gold, walls, or words. Then see what the Halych-Volyn land actually saved.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: under Mongol shadow the west kept the Rus inheritance alive — the Halych-Volyn Chronicle, church schools and writing, stone fortresses and painted icons in the land that preserved a Ukrainian state tradition. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
