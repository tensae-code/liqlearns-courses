# Ukrainian Lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the borderlands went other ways — Transcarpathia under Hungary since the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians and Prince Laborets' doomed defence of Ungvar, Northern Bukovina inside Moldavia, and the north-eastern borderlands drawn toward Moscow — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
