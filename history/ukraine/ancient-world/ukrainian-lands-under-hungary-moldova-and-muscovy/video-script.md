# Video script — Ukrainian Lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The borderlands went other ways — transcarpathia under hungary since the magyar crossing of the carpathians and prince laborets' doomed defence of ungvar, northern bukovina inside moldavia, and the north-eastern borderlands drawn toward moscow.

The borderlands went other ways. In the far west, Transcarpathia had belonged to Hungary's world since the Magyars crossed the Carpathians at the end of the ninth century. Local memory keeps Prince Laborets, who held the castle of Ungvar against the newcomers and died in its doomed defence — the land's own founding sorrow, told as the chronicles and legends give it. Under the Hungarian crown the land kept its Rus population, its faith and its mountain villages for centuries.

To the south, Northern Bukovina lay inside Moldavia — the principality the book names Russo-Wallachia — where Rus and Romanian worlds met in its towns and monasteries.

And the north-eastern borderlands, the old Chernihiv-Severian marches, were drawn step by step toward Moscow, the rising power of the forest north-east. One people, three borderland roads: Hungarian, Moldavian, Muscovite. The map of Ukraine was now a patchwork sewn by other states' hands.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
