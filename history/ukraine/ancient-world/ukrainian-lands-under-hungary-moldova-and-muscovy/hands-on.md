# Hands-on — Ukrainian Lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Not every land followed Kyiv's road. Three borderlands, three different masters. Before reading, guess which borderland's story starts with a prince defending a castle on a mountain pass road.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the borderlands went other ways — Transcarpathia under Hungary since the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians and Prince Laborets' doomed defence of Ungvar, Northern Bukovina inside Moldavia, and the north-eastern borderlands drawn toward Moscow. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
