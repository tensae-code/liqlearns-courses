# Ukrainian Lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Transcarpathia came under Hungary long before 1340, from the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians.
**Answer: True**

**2.** All Ukrainian lands joined Lithuania after 1340.
**Answer: False** — The borderlands went other ways — Hungary, Moldavia and Muscovy each drew their share.
