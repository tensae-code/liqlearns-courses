# Ukrainian Lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which prince is remembered for the doomed defence of Ungvar?
a) Prince Laborets  b) Algirdas  c) Roman Mstyslavych  d) Yurii I
**Answer: a) Prince Laborets** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Northern Bukovina lay inside which principality?
a) Moldavia (Russo-Wallachia)  b) Lithuania  c) the Golden Horde  d) Poland
**Answer: a) Moldavia (Russo-Wallachia)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
