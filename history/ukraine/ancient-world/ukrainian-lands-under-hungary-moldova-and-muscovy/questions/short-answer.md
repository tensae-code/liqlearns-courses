# Ukrainian Lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three powers that held the three borderlands of this lesson.
**Answer:** Hungary (Transcarpathia), Moldavia (Northern Bukovina) and Muscovy (the north-eastern borderlands).

**2.** How is Laborets' story told in this lesson?
**Answer:** As local memory and legend give it — the prince who held Ungvar castle against the Magyars and died in its defence.
