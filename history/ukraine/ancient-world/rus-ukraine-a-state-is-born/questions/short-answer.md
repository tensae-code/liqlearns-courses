# Rus-Ukraine: A State Is Born — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What question about the word 'Rus' does the book weigh?
**Answer:** What the word 'Rus' first meant — a people, a prince's war-band, or a land.

**2.** Name two things that became single when the state formed.
**Answer:** One prince ruling from Kyiv and tribute flowing to one centre (also: one defence of the whole land).
