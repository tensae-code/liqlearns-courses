# Rus-Ukraine: A State Is Born — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In which century does the chronicle date the rise of Rus?
a) the ninth century  b) the fifth century  c) the thirteenth century  d) the fifteenth century
**Answer: a) the ninth century** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What changed when the tribes were united under one prince at Kyiv?
a) a loose tribal world became one state  b) the tribes stopped farming  c) Kyiv was abandoned  d) the rivers dried up
**Answer: a) a loose tribal world became one state** — That is what this lesson teaches.
