# Hands-on — Rus-Ukraine: A State Is Born

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take nine counters or stones and scatter them: each is a tribe with its own chief. Now sweep them into one circle with one stone in the middle. This lesson is about that sweep — how scattered tribes became one state.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: in the ninth century the East Slavic tribes were united under one prince at Kyiv, and a loose tribal world became a state — Rus-Ukraine. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
