# Video script — Rus-Ukraine: A State Is Born

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
In the ninth century the east slavic tribes were united under one prince at kyiv, and a loose tribal world became a state — rus-ukraine.

The chronicle dates Rus to the ninth century. But what did the word 'Rus' mean at first — a people, a prince's war-band, a land? The book weighs the question the way historians do, because the answer decides whose story this is.

What is clear is the change itself. The East Slavic tribes, each ruling itself, were drawn together under one prince seated at Kyiv. Tribute now flowed to one centre; one war-band defended the whole; the river roads were guarded as one system.

That is the birth this lesson marks: not a birthday party but a joining. A loose tribal world becomes a state — the state this book calls Rus-Ukraine, Ukraine's own first state, with Kyiv at its head. Every reign that follows stands on this joining.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
