# Ukraine Enters the Middle Ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The steppe south of Ukraine was home to Cimmerians, Scythians and Sarmatians in turn.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Middle Ages on Ukrainian lands began in empty land where no one had lived before.
**Answer: False** — The lands were already old and busy — steppe riders and Greek coastal cities came first.
