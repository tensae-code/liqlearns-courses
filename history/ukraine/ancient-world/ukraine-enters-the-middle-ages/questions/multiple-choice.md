# Ukraine Enters the Middle Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What stood on the Black Sea coast in the ancient world before the Slavs' story?
a) Greek poleis like Olbia and Chersonesus  b) Mongol camps  c) Polish castles  d) Roman legions' forts
**Answer: a) Greek poleis like Olbia and Chersonesus** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** From whose communities does the story of this course continue?
a) the Slavs' farming communities  b) the Greek merchants  c) the Scythian kings  d) the Cimmerian riders
**Answer: a) the Slavs' farming communities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
