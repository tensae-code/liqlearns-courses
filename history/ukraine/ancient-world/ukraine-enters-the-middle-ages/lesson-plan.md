# Lesson plan — Ukraine Enters the Middle Ages

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ukraine Enters the Middle Ages
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Origins: Slavs, Kyiv and the Birth of Rus-Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Middle Ages on Ukrainian lands begin from an older world — steppe horsemen, Greek cities on the coast — and the Slavs, from whose farming communities the whole story continues.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Origins: Slavs, Kyiv and the Birth of Rus-Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
