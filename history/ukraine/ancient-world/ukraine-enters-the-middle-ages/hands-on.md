# Hands-on — Ukraine Enters the Middle Ages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page and mark it 'the Middle Ages'. Before the line, write three names you meet in this lesson: Cimmerians, Scythians, Greeks. After the line, write one word: Slavs. This lesson is about what stands on each side of that line.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the Middle Ages on Ukrainian lands begin from an older world — steppe horsemen, Greek cities on the coast — and the Slavs, from whose farming communities the whole story continues. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
