# Video script — Danylo, King of Rus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Roman's son danylo fought boyars, poles, hungarians and mongols to rebuild his father's state, founded kholm and lviv, bowed to batu at sarai to buy time — and in 1253 accepted a royal crown from the pope's legate, becoming king of rus.

Danylo Romanovych spent his youth fighting for his father's inheritance — against the boyars who would rule through child princes, against Poland and Hungary who would take the land outright. Step by step he won it back, and by the eve of the Mongol storm the Halych-Volyn state stood rebuilt.

The storm still had to be survived. Danylo founded and fortified new cities — Kholm, and Lviv, named for his son Lev — and when Batu demanded submission he travelled to the khan's capital at Sarai and bowed, buying his land time and his throne survival. It was humiliation used as a tool: the state lived.

Then the west answered his diplomacy. In 1253, at Dorohochyn, a legate of the Pope crowned Danylo with a royal crown sent from Rome. The prince of Halych-Volyn became king of Rus — the only crowned king in this whole story — and his state stood recognised in both worlds, Horde-facing and Europe-facing at once.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
