# Lesson plan — Danylo, King of Rus

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Danylo, King of Rus
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Halych-Volyn and the Mongol Storm (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Roman's son Danylo fought boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols to rebuild his father's state, founded Kholm and Lviv, bowed to Batu at Sarai to buy time — and in 1253 accepted a royal crown from the Pope's legate, becoming king of Rus.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Halych-Volyn and the Mongol Storm — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Danylo's crown was a gift that fell into his lap → it was the end of decades of fighting — boyars, two neighbouring kingdoms and the Mongols all had to be outlasted or outplayed first.
- Go back to the one thing: Roman's son Danylo fought boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols to rebuild his father's state, founded Kholm and Lviv, bowed to Batu at Sarai to buy time — and in 1253 accepted a royal crown from the Pope's legate, becoming king of Rus. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
