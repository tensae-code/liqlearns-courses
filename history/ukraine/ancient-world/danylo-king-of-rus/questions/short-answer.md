# Danylo, King of Rus — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Against whom did Danylo have to fight to rebuild his father's state?
**Answer:** The boyars at home, and Poland and Hungary abroad — and then the Mongols had to be survived.

**2.** Why did Danylo bow to Batu at Sarai?
**Answer:** To buy time and survival for his land and throne under the Horde's power — submission used as a tool, not an end.
