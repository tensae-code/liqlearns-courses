# Danylo, King of Rus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Danylo travelled to Batu's capital at Sarai and submitted to buy his land time.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Danylo's crown came from the Byzantine emperor.
**Answer: False** — It was sent from Rome and placed by the Pope's legate in 1253.
