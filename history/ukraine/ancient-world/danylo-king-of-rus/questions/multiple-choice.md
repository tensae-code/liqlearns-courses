# Danylo, King of Rus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year was Danylo crowned king?
a) 1253  b) 1199  c) 1240  d) 1340
**Answer: a) 1253** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which city did Danylo found and name for his son Lev?
a) Lviv  b) Kyiv  c) Kholm  d) Halych
