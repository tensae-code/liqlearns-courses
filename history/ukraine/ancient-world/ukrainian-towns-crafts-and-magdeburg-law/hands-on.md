# Hands-on — Ukrainian Towns, Crafts and Magdeburg Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A town that governs itself: elects its council, holds its court, sets its market rules. Before reading, list what a town gains by that charter — and what it must be able to do to deserve it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: three-field farming spread, guild crafts grew — Lviv's armourers were famous by 1394 — trade roads linked the lands to Europe and the Black Sea, and town after town won Magdeburg self-government. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
