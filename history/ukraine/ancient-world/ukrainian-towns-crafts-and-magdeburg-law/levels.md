# Ukrainian Towns, Crafts and Magdeburg Law — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — three-field farming spread, guild crafts grew — Lviv's armourers were famous by 1394 — trade roads linked the lands to Europe and the Black Sea, and town after town won Magdeburg self-government — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
