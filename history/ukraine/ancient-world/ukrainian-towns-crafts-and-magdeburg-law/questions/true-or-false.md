# Ukrainian Towns, Crafts and Magdeburg Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three-field farming let the same soil feed more people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Guilds had no rules — anyone could sell any work anywhere.
**Answer: False** — Guilds bound masters, journeymen and apprentices under strict rules of training and quality.
