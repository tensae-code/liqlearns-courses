# Ukrainian Towns, Crafts and Magdeburg Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is Magdeburg law?
a) a charter giving a town self-government — its own council and court  b) a tax on markets  c) a guild's rule book  d) a farming method
**Answer: a) a charter giving a town self-government — its own council and court** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which town's armourers were famous by 1394?
