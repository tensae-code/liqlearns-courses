# Ukrainian Towns, Crafts and Magdeburg Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did the three-field system work?
**Answer:** The fields rotated: a winter crop, a spring crop, and one field resting fallow each year, so the soil recovered.

**2.** Name two things Ukrainian trade roads carried west and south.
**Answer:** Grain, wax, honey, furs and salt (any two).
