# Hands-on — Ukrainian Culture in the 14th and 15th Centuries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A people with no state sends a son to run a famous university abroad. Before reading, answer: what carries a culture when there is no state to carry it? Collect the carriers as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: with no state of their own, the culture held — church and monastery schools, students like Yurii Drohobych reaching Bologna and Kraków and becoming rector at Bologna, fortress and church building from Lutsk to Kamianets. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
