# Ukrainian Culture in the 14th and 15th Centuries — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — with no state of their own, the culture held — church and monastery schools, students like Yurii Drohobych reaching Bologna and Kraków and becoming rector at Bologna, fortress and church building from Lutsk to Kamianets — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
