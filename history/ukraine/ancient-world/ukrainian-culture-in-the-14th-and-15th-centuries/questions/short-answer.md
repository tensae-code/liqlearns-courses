# Ukrainian Culture in the 14th and 15th Centuries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does Drohobych matter to this lesson?
**Answer:** A son of a land with no state rose to rector of Bologna, one of Europe's oldest universities — proof the culture lived and even led abroad.

**2.** Name two places named for fortress and church building in this age.
**Answer:** Lutsk and Kamianets.
