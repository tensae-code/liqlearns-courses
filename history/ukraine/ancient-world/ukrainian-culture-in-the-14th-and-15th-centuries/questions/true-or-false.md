# Ukrainian Culture in the 14th and 15th Centuries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fortresses and churches rose from Lutsk to Kamianets in this period.
**Answer: True**

**2.** No Ukrainian studied abroad in the 14th–15th centuries.
**Answer: False** — Drohobych reached Bologna and Kraków and became rector at Bologna; others followed to Prague and beyond.
