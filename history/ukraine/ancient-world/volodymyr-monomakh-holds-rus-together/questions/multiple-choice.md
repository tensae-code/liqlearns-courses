# Volodymyr Monomakh Holds Rus Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Liubech congress of 1097 try to do?
a) end the princes' feuds and unite them against the Polovtsians  b) divide Rus into five kingdoms  c) baptise the Polovtsians  d) build St Sophia
**Answer: a) end the princes' feuds and unite them against the Polovtsians** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is Monomakh's Instruction?
a) his written advice to his children — memoir and rule of life  b) a tax register  c) a treaty with Byzantium  d) a list of fortresses
**Answer: a) his written advice to his children — memoir and rule of life** — That is what this lesson teaches.
