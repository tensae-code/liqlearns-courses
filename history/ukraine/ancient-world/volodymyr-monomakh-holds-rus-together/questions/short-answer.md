# Volodymyr Monomakh Holds Rus Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Until what year did Mstyslav the Great hold the reunited land together?
**Answer:** Until 1132, the year of his death.

**2.** Name two kinds of work Monomakh did for Rus.
**Answer:** War — united campaigns that broke Polovtsian pressure; and words — the Instruction, his advice to his children (also: ruling Kyiv as reuniter).
