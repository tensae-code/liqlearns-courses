# Volodymyr Monomakh Holds Rus Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Monomakh reunited most of Rus under Kyiv once more.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Liubech congress ended the princely feuds for good.
**Answer: False** — It failed — the feuds continued, and Monomakh's campaigns and rule did the reuniting instead.
