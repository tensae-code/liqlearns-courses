# Crimea and the Northern Black Sea in the Late Middle Ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Theodoro was a Greek principality in Crimea's mountains.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Crimean Khanate disappeared within a generation.
**Answer: False** — It faced Ukraine — raiding, trading, allying and fighting — for three centuries.
