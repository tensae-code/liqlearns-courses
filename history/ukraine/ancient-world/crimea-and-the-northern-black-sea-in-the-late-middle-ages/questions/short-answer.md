# Crimea and the Northern Black Sea in the Late Middle Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three worlds that met in late-medieval Crimea.
**Answer:** The Genoese trading cities, the Greek principality of Theodoro, and the Crimean Khanate of the Girays.

**2.** What did the Genoese ports trade in?
**Answer:** The goods of steppe and sea — grain, furs, wax, and captives.
