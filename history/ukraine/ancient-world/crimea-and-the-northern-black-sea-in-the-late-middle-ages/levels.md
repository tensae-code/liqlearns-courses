# Crimea and the Northern Black Sea in the Late Middle Ages — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Genoese trading cities on the coast, the Greek principality of Theodoro in the mountains, and from the 1440s the Crimean Khanate of the Girays — a new steppe power that would face Ukraine for three centuries — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
