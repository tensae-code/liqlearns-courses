# Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — after the Romanovych line ended in 1340, Lithuania gathered most Ukrainian lands — Algirdas took Kyiv after Blue Waters in 1362 — and for a century the lands kept their laws, faith and Rus language inside a state whose princes ruled 'without changing the old ways' — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
