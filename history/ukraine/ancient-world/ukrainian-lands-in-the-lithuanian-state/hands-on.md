# Hands-on — Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A conqueror arrives — and changes nothing. Laws stay, faith stays, language stays. Before reading, decide: is that weakness, or the smartest way to rule a land bigger than your own?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: after the Romanovych line ended in 1340, Lithuania gathered most Ukrainian lands — Algirdas took Kyiv after Blue Waters in 1362 — and for a century the lands kept their laws, faith and Rus language inside a state whose princes ruled 'without changing the old ways'. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
