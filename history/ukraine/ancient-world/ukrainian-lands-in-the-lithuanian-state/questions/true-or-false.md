# Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Grand Duchy's chancery wrote its records in the Rus language.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Lithuania took Kyiv in 1240.
**Answer: False** — Kyiv fell to the Mongols in 1240; Algirdas took it after Blue Waters in 1362.
