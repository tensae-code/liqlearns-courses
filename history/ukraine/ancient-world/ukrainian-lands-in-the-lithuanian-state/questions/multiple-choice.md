# Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who took Kyiv after the battle of Blue Waters in 1362?
a) Grand Duke Algirdas of Lithuania  b) Casimir III of Poland  c) Batu the Mongol  d) Danylo of Halych
**Answer: a) Grand Duke Algirdas of Lithuania** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Lithuania's rule 'without changing the old ways' mean?
a) the lands kept their laws, faith and Rus language  b) everything was replaced by Lithuanian custom  c) the towns were emptied  d) the church was closed
**Answer: a) the lands kept their laws, faith and Rus language** — That is what this lesson teaches.
