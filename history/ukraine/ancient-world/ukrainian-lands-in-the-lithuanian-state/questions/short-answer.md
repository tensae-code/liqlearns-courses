# Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the Ukrainian lands accept Lithuanian rule for a century?
**Answer:** It protected them from the Horde while leaving their laws, faith, princes and language in place.

**2.** What battle opened the way to Kyiv for Algirdas?
**Answer:** The battle of Blue Waters (1362), where he broke the Horde's hold.
