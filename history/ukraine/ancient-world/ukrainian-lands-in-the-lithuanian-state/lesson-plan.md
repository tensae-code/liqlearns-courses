# Lesson plan — Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Late Middle Ages: Ukrainian Lands in New States (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after the Romanovych line ended in 1340, Lithuania gathered most Ukrainian lands — Algirdas took Kyiv after Blue Waters in 1362 — and for a century the lands kept their laws, faith and Rus language inside a state whose princes ruled 'without changing the old ways'.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Late Middle Ages: Ukrainian Lands in New States — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
