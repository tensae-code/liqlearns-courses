# Video script — Ukrainian Lands in the Lithuanian State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
After the romanovych line ended in 1340, lithuania gathered most ukrainian lands — algirdas took kyiv after blue waters in 1362 — and for a century the lands kept their laws, faith and rus language inside a state whose princes ruled 'without changing the old ways'.

With the Romanovych line ended, a new power gathered the Ukrainian lands: the Grand Duchy of Lithuania, pushing south from the Baltic forests. Its princes took land after land, and in 1362 Grand Duke Algirdas broke the Horde's hold at the battle of Blue Waters and took Kyiv itself.

What followed surprises: Lithuania ruled, in its own formula, 'without changing the old ways'. The Rus lands kept their laws — the Ruska Pravda tradition lived on — their Orthodox faith, their princes in many places, and their language: the Grand Duchy's own chancery wrote its records in the Rus tongue, and its law codes would be written in it too.

For a century, then, most Ukrainian lands lived inside a large, tolerant state that needed them as much as they needed its protection from the Horde and the Teutonic Knights. It was not independence — but it was continuity: law, faith and word survived under a Lithuanian crown.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
