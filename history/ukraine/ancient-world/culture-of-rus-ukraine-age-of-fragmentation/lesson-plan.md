# Lesson plan — The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Age of Fragmentation

**Course:** History — Ukraine · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Age of Fragmentation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Fragmentation: Many Princes, One Land (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** even as the state splintered, its culture flowered — Nestor's chronicle, the Tale of Ihor's Campaign, graffiti scratched by ordinary Kyivans on St Sophia's walls, schools, medicine and white-stone churches in every principality.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Fragmentation: Many Princes, One Land — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
