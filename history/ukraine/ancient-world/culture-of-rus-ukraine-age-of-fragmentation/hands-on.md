# Hands-on — The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Age of Fragmentation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A state breaks; its songs get better. Before reading, guess why hard times might make a people write more, not less. Then find the Kyivans who wrote on a church wall — what did ordinary people want to say?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: even as the state splintered, its culture flowered — Nestor's chronicle, the Tale of Ihor's Campaign, graffiti scratched by ordinary Kyivans on St Sophia's walls, schools, medicine and white-stone churches in every principality. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
