# The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Age of Fragmentation — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — even as the state splintered, its culture flowered — Nestor's chronicle, the Tale of Ihor's Campaign, graffiti scratched by ordinary Kyivans on St Sophia's walls, schools, medicine and white-stone churches in every principality — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
