# The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Age of Fragmentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who shaped the great chronicle, the Tale of Bygone Years?
a) the monk Nestor  b) Prince Ihor  c) Yaroslav Osmomysl  d) a Byzantine scribe
**Answer: a) the monk Nestor** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the Tale of Ihor's Campaign turn its hero's defeat into?
a) a plea for the princes to unite  b) a call for revenge on Kyiv  c) a tribute list  d) a saint's life
**Answer: a) a plea for the princes to unite** — That is what this lesson teaches.
