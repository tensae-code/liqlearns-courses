# The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Age of Fragmentation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ordinary Kyivans scratched graffiti on St Sophia's walls.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Culture stopped growing once the state fragmented.
**Answer: False** — It flowered — chronicle, Tale, schools, medicine and church building all spread in the age of fragmentation.
