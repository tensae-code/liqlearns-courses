# The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Age of Fragmentation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens to Prince Ihor in the Tale of Ihor's Campaign?
**Answer:** He rides against the Polovtsians in 1185, is defeated and captured — and escapes to tell it.

**2.** What do the St Sophia graffiti prove about writing in this age?
**Answer:** Writing had spread beyond monks and princes — ordinary townspeople wrote prayers, names and notes in their own hands.
