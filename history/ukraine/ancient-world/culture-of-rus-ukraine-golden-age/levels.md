# The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Golden Age — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the golden age in culture: byliny epics sung about Volodymyr's heroes, the first schools and libraries, chronicle writing beginning, and St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church making Kyiv a city of stone and books — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
