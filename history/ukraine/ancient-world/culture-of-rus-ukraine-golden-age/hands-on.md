# Hands-on — The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Golden Age

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A city can be rich in gold or rich in stories. Walk through golden-age Kyiv in your mind and count its treasures: a song, a school, a library, a mosaic. Which of them can still be seen or read today?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the golden age in culture: byliny epics sung about Volodymyr's heroes, the first schools and libraries, chronicle writing beginning, and St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church making Kyiv a city of stone and books. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
