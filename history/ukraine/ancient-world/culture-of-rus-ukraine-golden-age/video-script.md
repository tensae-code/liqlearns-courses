# Video script — The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Golden Age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The golden age in culture: byliny epics sung about volodymyr's heroes, the first schools and libraries, chronicle writing beginning, and st sophia's mosaics and the desiatynna church making kyiv a city of stone and books.

The golden age sang. Byliny — epic songs — were performed about Volodymyr and his heroes, the bogatyrs who defended the frontier, and the singing carried the state's memory from court to village long before most people could read.

But reading was coming. With the church came schools, and with schools, books: Yaroslav gathered a library at St Sophia and set translators to work, and in the monasteries the first chronicle writing began — the year-by-year record from which this whole course's story is drawn.

And Kyiv itself changed material: from wood to stone. The Desiatynna — the Tithe church — rose first, then St Sophia with its mosaics and frescoes, golden work by masters of the Byzantine world. A city of stone churches, sung heroes, schoolboys and scribes: that is the golden age as its culture shows it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
