# The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Golden Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yaroslav gathered a library at St Sophia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Golden-age Kyiv was built entirely of wood, with no stone churches.
**Answer: False** — The Desiatynna church and St Sophia made Kyiv a city of stone as well as books.
