# The Culture of Rus-Ukraine: The Golden Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two kinds of writing or learning that began in the golden age.
**Answer:** Chronicle writing and book work — schools, Yaroslav's library at St Sophia, translators at work (any two).

**2.** Why is the Desiatynna church's name a clue to its funding?
**Answer:** It was sustained by the tithe — the tenth of income set aside for the church.
