# Hands-on — Halych and Volyn Rise

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Salt is worth money, and a river is a road. On a map in your mind, mark the salt country and the Dnister river in the west. This lesson is about the two western lands growing rich while the steppe stays dangerous.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: in the west Yaroslav Osmomysl made Halych rich on salt and the Dnister trade while Volyn grew beside it — and all the while Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians rode the steppe and Crimea changed hands beyond the mountains. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
