# Video script — Halych and Volyn Rise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
In the west yaroslav osmomysl made halych rich on salt and the dnister trade while volyn grew beside it — and all the while pechenegs, torks and polovtsians rode the steppe and crimea changed hands beyond the mountains.

While the Dnipro lands quarrelled, the west rose. Halych, on the Dnister river under the Carpathians, grew rich on two gifts: salt, dug and boiled in its lands and sold far and wide, and the Dnister trade road linking the Baltic world to the Black Sea. Under Yaroslav Osmomysl — 'the Eight-Minded', famed for wisdom — Halych became a power whose friendship kings sought. Beside it, Volyn, with its own towns and princes, grew steadily in the forests and fields to the north.

South and east lay the steppe, and the steppe kept its own succession: Pechenegs first, then Torks, then Polovtsians — nomad peoples whose camps, herds and raids set the rhythm of the frontier for three centuries.

Beyond the mountains, Crimea was a world of its own — Greek and steppe towns, trading ports, changing hands between empires and khans. The west rising, the steppe riding, Crimea trading: that is the board on which the next century will be played.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Ukraine.
