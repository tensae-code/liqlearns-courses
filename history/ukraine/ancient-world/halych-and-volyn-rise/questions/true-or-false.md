# Halych and Volyn Rise — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Volyn grew beside Halych in the west.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The steppe peoples stood still — one people held the steppe for the whole period.
**Answer: False** — They succeeded each other: Pechenegs, then Torks, then Polovtsians.
