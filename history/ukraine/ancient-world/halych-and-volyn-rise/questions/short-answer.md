# Halych and Volyn Rise — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the succession of nomad peoples on the steppe in this period.
**Answer:** Pechenegs, then Torks, then Polovtsians.

**2.** What two gifts made Halych rich?
**Answer:** Salt from its own lands, and the Dnister trade road between the Baltic world and the Black Sea.
