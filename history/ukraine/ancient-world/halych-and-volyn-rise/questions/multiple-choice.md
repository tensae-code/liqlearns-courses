# Halych and Volyn Rise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What made Halych rich?
a) salt and the Dnister trade  b) gold mines  c) the fur trade only  d) sea fishing
**Answer: a) salt and the Dnister trade** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who was Yaroslav Osmomysl?
a) the famed wise prince who made Halych a power  b) a Polovtsian khan  c) the founder of Kyiv  d) a Byzantine emperor
**Answer: a) the famed wise prince who made Halych a power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
