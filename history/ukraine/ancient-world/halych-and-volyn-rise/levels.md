# Halych and Volyn Rise — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — in the west Yaroslav Osmomysl made Halych rich on salt and the Dnister trade while Volyn grew beside it — and all the while Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians rode the steppe and Crimea changed hands beyond the mountains — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
