# Cambodia: The Chenla Era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom (550–802 CE)” is mainly about this: The great powers arm and ally — Germany, Austria-Hungary and Italy against the rest — colonial rivalry sharpens, the Ottoman Empire decays, and the Balkans become the powder keg.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Funan's inland successor splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla; Bhavavarman I (550–600) heads the king list of a state the Chinese call Zhenla.
