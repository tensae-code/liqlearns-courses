# Cambodia: The Chenla Era — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The Chenla era”?
a) The kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.  b) Thirteen English colonies of Arawak, Sioux and Apache lands (photo: a Native American woman) declare independence and invent a republic across the Atlantic.  c) The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
