# Cambodia: The Chenla Era — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The Chenla era”?
**Answer:** The kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom (550–802 CE)” end?
**Answer:** Funan's inland successor splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla; Bhavavarman I (550–600) heads the king list of a state the Chinese call Zhenla. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
