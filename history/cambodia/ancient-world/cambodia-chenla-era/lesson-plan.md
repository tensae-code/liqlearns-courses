## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The Chenla era** — The kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.
2. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom (550–802 CE)** — Funan's inland successor splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla; Bhavavarman I (550–600) heads the king list of a state the Chinese call Zhenla.
3. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom** — Land Chenla of the upper Mekong (in today's Laos) and Water Chenla facing the sea divide Funan's inheritance between them (map, p. 142).
