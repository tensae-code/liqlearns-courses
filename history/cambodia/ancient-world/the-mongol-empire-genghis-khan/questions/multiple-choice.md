# The Mongol Empire: Genghis Khan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 5, The Mongol Empire”?
a) In 1206 the steppe clans unite under Genghis Khan (illustration, p. 191); within a generation his horsemen hold the largest land empire ever — the storm that also broke over Champa and Dai Viet.  b) The British East India Company takes Bengal and Calcutta, Burma falls in stages (Rangoon 1850, annexation January 1886), and France assembles Indochina — the machine that also took Cambodia.  c) Ten million dead and twenty million wounded; the Russian, Austro-Hungarian and German empires fall, new states are drawn on the map, and America and Japan rise.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In 1206 the steppe clans unite under Genghis Khan (illustration, p. 191); within a generation his horsemen hold the largest land empire ever — the storm that also broke over Champa and Dai Viet.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
