# The Mongol Empire: Genghis Khan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1206 the steppe clans unite under Genghis Khan (illustration, p. 191); within a generation his horsemen hold the largest land empire ever — the storm that also broke over Champa and Dai Viet.
**Answer: True**
