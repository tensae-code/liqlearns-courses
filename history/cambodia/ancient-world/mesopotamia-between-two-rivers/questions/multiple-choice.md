Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The states of Mesopotamia”?
a) Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, falling from the Armenian mountains, Sumerian cities invent writing, law and the wheel (map, p. 86).  b) Fa Ngum, a Lao prince raised at the Angkor court and married to a Khmer princess, carries Buddhism home and unites the Lao valleys as Lan Xang, the kingdom of a million elephants (1353).  c) Thirteen English colonies of Arawak, Sioux and Apache lands (photo: a Native American woman) declare independence and invent a republic across the Atlantic.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, falling from the Armenian mountains, Sumerian cities invent writing, law and the wheel (map, p. 86).** — That is what this lesson teaches.
