**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 1, The feudal regime in the medieval era”?
a) After Rome falls to the "barbarians" (les barbares), Europe spends a thousand years as a ladder of lords and vassals — land for loyalty, peasants bound below.  b) The empire is proclaimed at Versailles on 18 January 1871 — 25 states under one Kaiser, Bundesrat and Reichstag — and by 1914 Germany stands as Europe's second industrial power.  c) The Munich photograph of 29 September 1938 — Chamberlain, Daladier, Hitler, Mussolini — opens a lesson that runs from the mandate system's grievances and Wilson's self-determination promise to total war.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
