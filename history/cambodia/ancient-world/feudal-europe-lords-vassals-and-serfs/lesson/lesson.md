# Feudal Europe: Lords, Vassals and Serfs

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Feudal Europe: Lords, Vassals and Serfs** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

After Rome falls to the "barbarians" (les barbares), Europe spends a thousand years as a ladder of lords and vassals — land for loyalty, peasants bound below. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
