# The Hebrews: One God and a Covenant — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A herding people from Mesopotamia settles Palestine between the Jordan river and the Dead Sea and keeps one God by covenant.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The early Israelites” is mainly about this: Locke and Montesquieu (portrait, p. 114) arm liberalism; conservatism answers; socialism and Proudhon's anarchism (portrait, p. 119) speak for the workers the factories made.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A herding people from Mesopotamia settles Palestine between the Jordan river and the Dead Sea and keeps one God by covenant.
