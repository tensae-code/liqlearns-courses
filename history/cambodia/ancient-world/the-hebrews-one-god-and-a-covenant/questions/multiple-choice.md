# The Hebrews: One God and a Covenant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The early Israelites”?
a) A herding people from Mesopotamia settles Palestine between the Jordan river and the Dead Sea and keeps one God by covenant.  b) After Rome falls to the "barbarians" (les barbares), Europe spends a thousand years as a ladder of lords and vassals — land for loyalty, peasants bound below.  c) Arab and Indian traders bring Islam to the Malay ports; Malacca's sultanate spreads the faith and the Malay tongue across the peninsula and islands Cambodia trades with.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A herding people from Mesopotamia settles Palestine between the Jordan river and the Dead Sea and keeps one God by covenant.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
