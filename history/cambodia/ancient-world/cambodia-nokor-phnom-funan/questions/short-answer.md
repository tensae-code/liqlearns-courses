# Cambodia: Nokor Phnom (Funan) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §2, The Nokor Phnom (Funan) era”?
**Answer:** The Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Funan kingdom (50–550 CE)” end?
**Answer:** Chinese envoys' reports (read by Paul Pelliot) describe a delta kingdom 3,000 li across (map, p. 120) whose ports grow rich on the India–China sea road. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
