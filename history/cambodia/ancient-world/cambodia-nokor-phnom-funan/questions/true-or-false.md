# Cambodia: Nokor Phnom (Funan) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Funan kingdom (50–550 CE)” is mainly about this: Peter the Great and Catherine II built a many-peopled empire — Russians, Ukrainians, Lithuanians, Baltic and Finnic peoples; Alexander II reforms too late, and the structure cracks toward October 1917.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Chinese envoys' reports (read by Paul Pelliot) describe a delta kingdom 3,000 li across (map, p. 120) whose ports grow rich on the India–China sea road.
