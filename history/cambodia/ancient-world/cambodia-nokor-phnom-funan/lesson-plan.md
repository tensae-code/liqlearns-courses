## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §2, The Nokor Phnom (Funan) era** — The Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls.
2. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Funan kingdom (50–550 CE)** — Chinese envoys' reports (read by Paul Pelliot) describe a delta kingdom 3,000 li across (map, p. 120) whose ports grow rich on the India–China sea road.
3. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The ancient Funan kingdom** — Chinese records measure Funan at 3,000 li (1 li = 576 m); its civilisation glitters at Oc Eo — gold jewellery, Vishnu and Buddha images (photos, p. 136).
