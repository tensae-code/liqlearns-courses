a) Italy's city on seven hills conquers Sicily, then the whole Mediterranean world (map, p. 104), passing from republic to empire.  b) Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.  c) The British East India Company takes Bengal and Calcutta, Burma falls in stages (Rangoon 1850, annexation January 1886), and France assembles Indochina — the machine that also took Cambodia.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
