# Korea: Three Kingdoms and Joseon — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 5, Korea (contents page mislabels it Burma)”?
**Answer:** From the Joseon foundation legend (2333 BCE) through Goguryeo, Baekje and Silla's unification (676), Balhae and Goryeo, Mongol invasions, to Lee Seonggye's Joseon dynasty (1392) that lasts until 1910. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 5, Korea (contents page mislabels it Burma)” end?
**Answer:** From the Joseon foundation legend (2333 BCE) through Goguryeo, Baekje and Silla's unification (676), Balhae and Goryeo, Mongol invasions, to Lee Seonggye's Joseon dynasty (1392) that lasts until 1910. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
