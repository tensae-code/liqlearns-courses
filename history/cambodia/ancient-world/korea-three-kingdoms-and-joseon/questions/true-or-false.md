# Korea: Three Kingdoms and Joseon — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From the Joseon foundation legend (2333 BCE) through Goguryeo, Baekje and Silla's unification (676), Balhae and Goryeo, Mongol invasions, to Lee Seonggye's Joseon dynasty (1392) that lasts until 1910.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 3 Lesson 5, Korea (contents page mislabels it Burma)” is mainly about this: Masaryk's republic is born from the First World War (1918), governed with Beneš, dismembered at Munich in 1938, and remade after 1945 as a people's democracy of 15 million.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From the Joseon foundation legend (2333 BCE) through Goguryeo, Baekje and Silla's unification (676), Balhae and Goryeo, Mongol invasions, to Lee Seonggye's Joseon dynasty (1392) that lasts until 1910.
