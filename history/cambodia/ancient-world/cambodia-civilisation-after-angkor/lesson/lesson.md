# Cambodia: Civilisation after Angkor

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Cambodia: Civilisation after Angkor** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

After 1431 the court still governs by appointed officials, laws are written as verse codes (kram and chbab) to be memorised, and Theravada Buddhism reshapes literature and daily life. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
