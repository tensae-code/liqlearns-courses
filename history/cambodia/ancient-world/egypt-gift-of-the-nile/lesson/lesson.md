# Egypt: Gift of the Nile

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Egypt: Gift of the Nile** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

An oasis civilisation strung along the Nile (map, p. 80) — delta and valley united under pharaohs who command the flood's calendar. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
