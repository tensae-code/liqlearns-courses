# Egypt: Gift of the Nile — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 2, Ancient Egypt”?
a) An oasis civilisation strung along the Nile (map, p. 80) — delta and valley united under pharaohs who command the flood's calendar.  b) Ramathibodi founds Ayutthaya on the Chao Phraya in 1351; the lesson traces Siam from the "Syam" named in Angkor's 12th-century inscriptions to the power that will sack Angkor.  c) Louis XVI summons the Estates-General to Versailles (5 May 1789); the Third Estate declares itself the National Assembly (17 June) and the old order begins to fall.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) An oasis civilisation strung along the Nile (map, p. 80) — delta and valley united under pharaohs who command the flood's calendar.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
