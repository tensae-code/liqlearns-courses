# The Phoenicians: Sailors, Traders and the Alphabet — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tyre, Byblos and Sidon (map, p. 94) grow rich on purple dye and cedar, and their traders carry a simple alphabet to every shore.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 1 Lesson 5, The Phoenician state” is mainly about this: The British East India Company takes Bengal and Calcutta, Burma falls in stages (Rangoon 1850, annexation January 1886), and France assembles Indochina — the machine that also took Cambodia.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Tyre, Byblos and Sidon (map, p. 94) grow rich on purple dye and cedar, and their traders carry a simple alphabet to every shore.
