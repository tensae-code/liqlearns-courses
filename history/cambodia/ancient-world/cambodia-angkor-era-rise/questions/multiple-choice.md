# Cambodia: The Angkor Era Rises — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 5, The Angkor era”?
a) Jayavarman II (802–850), back from captivity in Java, is proclaimed universal monarch on Phnom Kulen in 802 and unites the two Chenlas; Yasovarman I founds the city of Angkor in the 9th century, and temple-building kings raise Angkor Wat over a land watered by great reservoirs.  b) Arab and Indian traders bring Islam to the Malay ports; Malacca's sultanate spreads the faith and the Malay tongue across the peninsula and islands Cambodia trades with.  c) Peter the Great and Catherine II built a many-peopled empire — Russians, Ukrainians, Lithuanians, Baltic and Finnic peoples; Alexander II reforms too late, and the structure cracks toward October 1917.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Jayavarman II (802–850), back from captivity in Java, is proclaimed universal monarch on Phnom Kulen in 802 and unites the two Chenlas; Yasovarman I founds the city of Angkor in the 9th century, and temple-building kings raise Angkor Wat over a land watered by great reservoirs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
