# Cambodia: The Angkor Era Rises — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Jayavarman II (802–850), back from captivity in Java, is proclaimed universal monarch on Phnom Kulen in 802 and unites the two Chenlas; Yasovarman I founds the city of Angkor in the 9th century, and temple-building kings raise Angkor Wat over a land watered by great reservoirs.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Angkor Empire” is mainly about this: The small wars of 1898, 1899–1902 and 1904–05 rehearse the great one; four years of trenches end with a new League meant to keep the peace.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The empire runs 802–1432 through six centuries of temple kings: Jayavarman II (802–850), Indravarman I (877–889; Bakong and a 3,000 × 8,000 m reservoir), Yasovarman I (889–900; Bakheng and the great eastern baray).
