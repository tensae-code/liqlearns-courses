# Cambodia: The Angkor Era Rises — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 4 Lesson 5, The Angkor era”?
**Answer:** Jayavarman II (802–850), back from captivity in Java, is proclaimed universal monarch on Phnom Kulen in 802 and unites the two Chenlas; Yasovarman I founds the city of Angkor in the 9th century, and temple-building kings raise Angkor Wat over a land watered by great reservoirs. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Angkor Empire” end?
**Answer:** The empire runs 802–1432 through six centuries of temple kings: Jayavarman II (802–850), Indravarman I (877–889; Bakong and a 3,000 × 8,000 m reservoir), Yasovarman I (889–900; Bakheng and the great eastern baray). Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
