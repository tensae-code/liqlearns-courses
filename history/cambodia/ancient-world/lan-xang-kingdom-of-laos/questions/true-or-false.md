# Lan Xang: The Kingdom of Laos — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fa Ngum, a Lao prince raised at the Angkor court and married to a Khmer princess, carries Buddhism home and unites the Lao valleys as Lan Xang, the kingdom of a million elephants (1353).
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Lan Xang state” is mainly about this: Roosevelt names the "United Nations" in the declaration of 1 January 1942; on 24 October 1945 the organisation is born with 51 founding members.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Fa Ngum, a Lao prince raised at the Angkor court and married to a Khmer princess, carries Buddhism home and unites the Lao valleys as Lan Xang, the kingdom of a million elephants (1353).
