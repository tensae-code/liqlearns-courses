# Lan Xang: The Kingdom of Laos — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Lan Xang state”?
a) Fa Ngum, a Lao prince raised at the Angkor court and married to a Khmer princess, carries Buddhism home and unites the Lao valleys as Lan Xang, the kingdom of a million elephants (1353).  b) Victoria's Britain (1837–1901) rules the world's greatest colonial empire with India as its jewel, Whigs and Tories alternating at home while it rivals Russia in Asia and Germany at sea.  c) Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Fa Ngum, a Lao prince raised at the Angkor court and married to a Khmer princess, carries Buddhism home and unites the Lao valleys as Lan Xang, the kingdom of a million elephants (1353).** — That is what this lesson teaches.
