# China: The Yellow River and the First Dynasties

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **China: The Yellow River and the First Dynasties** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

At Zhoukoudian in 1924 diggers find "Peking Man" (Sinanthropus), 500,000–200,000 years old; civilisation follows on the Yellow River (map, p. 134). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
