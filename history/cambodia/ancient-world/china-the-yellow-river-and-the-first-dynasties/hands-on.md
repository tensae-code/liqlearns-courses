# Hands-on — China: The Yellow River and the First Dynasties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — China: The Yellow River and the First Dynasties — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: At Zhoukoudian in 1924 diggers find "Peking Man" (Sinanthropus), 500,000–200,000 years old; civilisation follows on the Yellow River (map, p. 134). Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
