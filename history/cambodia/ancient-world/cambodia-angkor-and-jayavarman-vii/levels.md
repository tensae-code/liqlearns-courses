# Angkor and Jayavarman VII — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Exam questions reach back to Angkor Wat's builder and to Jayavarman VII, the king who raised hospitals, roads and the Bayon — the medieval empire the modern state claims as its root. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
