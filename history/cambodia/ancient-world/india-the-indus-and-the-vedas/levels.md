# India: The Indus and the Vedas — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Under the 2,000 km Himalaya wall (Everest 8,848 m) the Indus and Ganges plains grow some of the world's oldest cities (map, p. 138). — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
