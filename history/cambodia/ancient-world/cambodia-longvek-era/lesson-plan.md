## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Longvek era (16th century)** — Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.
2. **Ch. 4 Lesson 3, The Longvek era (16th–17th centuries)** — Ang Chan walls a new capital at Longvek (its moats and ramparts still traceable, photos p. 110); Siam presses from the west, and after Longvek's fall the court wanders again.
3. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Longvek era in the 16th century** — Longvek is a fortress-city of moats, five gates and a royal palace with pagodas, open to Portuguese and Japanese traders — built strong after Ang Chan's death (1526) against the Siamese threat.
