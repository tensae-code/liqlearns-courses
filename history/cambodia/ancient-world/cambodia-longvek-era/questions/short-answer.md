# Cambodia: The Longvek Era — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Longvek era (16th century)”?
**Answer:** Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 3, The Longvek era (16th–17th centuries)” end?
**Answer:** Ang Chan walls a new capital at Longvek (its moats and ramparts still traceable, photos p. 110); Siam presses from the west, and after Longvek's fall the court wanders again. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
