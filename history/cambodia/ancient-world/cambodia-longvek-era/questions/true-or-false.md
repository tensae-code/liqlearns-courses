# Cambodia: The Longvek Era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 4 Lesson 3, The Longvek era (16th–17th centuries)” is mainly about this: Founded on 7 October 1949 on the Soviet model — 108,333 km², 17 million people (1988) — East Germany lives and dies as the other Germany, its Berlin avenue (photo) built for parades.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Ang Chan walls a new capital at Longvek (its moats and ramparts still traceable, photos p. 110); Siam presses from the west, and after Longvek's fall the court wanders again.
