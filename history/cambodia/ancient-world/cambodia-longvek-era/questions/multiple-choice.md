# Cambodia: The Longvek Era — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Longvek era (16th century)”?
a) Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.  b) The great powers arm and ally — Germany, Austria-Hungary and Italy against the rest — colonial rivalry sharpens, the Ottoman Empire decays, and the Balkans become the powder keg.  c) France emerges from Vichy and de Gaulle's 18 June 1940 appeal, then fights to hold Indochina and Algeria while Germany is divided and the Cold War begins (Barbarossa, 22 June 1941; Pearl Harbor, 7 December 1941).  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
