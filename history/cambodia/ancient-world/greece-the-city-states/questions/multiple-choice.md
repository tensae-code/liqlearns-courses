# Greece: The City-States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 6, Ancient Greece”?
a) A rocky Aegean land of islands and colonies from Ionia to Asia Minor (map, p. 98) breaks into rival city-states that invent citizens.  b) Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.  c) Locke and Montesquieu (portrait, p. 114) arm liberalism; conservatism answers; socialism and Proudhon's anarchism (portrait, p. 119) speak for the workers the factories made.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A rocky Aegean land of islands and colonies from Ionia to Asia Minor (map, p. 98) breaks into rival city-states that invent citizens.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
