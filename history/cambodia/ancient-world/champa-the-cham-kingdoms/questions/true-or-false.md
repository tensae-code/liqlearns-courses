# Champa: The Cham Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Cham raise a chain of coastal kingdoms — Simhapura, Indrapura, Vijaya, Kauthara, Panduranga — from 192 CE, sending embassies to China (336, 420 CE) and fighting Angkor and Dai Viet for a thousand years.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 1, The Champa state (2nd–18th centuries)” is mainly about this: France clings to its recovered provinces and reparations while the franc slides (1923–1926 crisis); Britain's world lead quietly drains away.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Cham raise a chain of coastal kingdoms — Simhapura, Indrapura, Vijaya, Kauthara, Panduranga — from 192 CE, sending embassies to China (336, 420 CE) and fighting Angkor and Dai Viet for a thousand years.
