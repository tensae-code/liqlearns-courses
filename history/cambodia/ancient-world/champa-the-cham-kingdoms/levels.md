# Champa: The Cham Kingdoms — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Cham raise a chain of coastal kingdoms — Simhapura, Indrapura, Vijaya, Kauthara, Panduranga — from 192 CE, sending embassies to China (336, 420 CE) and fighting Angkor and Dai Viet for a thousand years. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
