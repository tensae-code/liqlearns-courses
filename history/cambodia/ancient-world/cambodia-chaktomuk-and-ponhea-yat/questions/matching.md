# Cambodia: Chaktomuk and Ponhea Yat — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Chaktomuk era (15th century) — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Ch. 4 Lesson 2, The Chaktomuk era (15th–16th centuries) — b)


Meanings:
a) Ponhea Yat (1434–1463) plants the capital where the four rivers meet after Angkor's fall; his successors Norayreachea, Srei Reachea and Srei Sokonthor rule a kingdom rebuilding between Siam and Vietnam.
b) Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.

**Answer key:** 1. b; 2. a
