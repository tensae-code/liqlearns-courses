# Cambodia: Chaktomuk and Ponhea Yat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Chaktomuk era (15th century)”?
a) Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.  b) Peter the Great and Catherine II built a many-peopled empire — Russians, Ukrainians, Lithuanians, Baltic and Finnic peoples; Alexander II reforms too late, and the structure cracks toward October 1917.  c) The empire that was the world's largest before 1914 shrinks after 1945 into a 224,046 km² island state of 58 million (2006), its politics rebuilt around parties, welfare and Europe.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
