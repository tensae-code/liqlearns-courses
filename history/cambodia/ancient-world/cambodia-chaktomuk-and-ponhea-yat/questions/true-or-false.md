# Cambodia: Chaktomuk and Ponhea Yat — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 4 Lesson 2, The Chaktomuk era (15th–16th centuries)” is mainly about this: A defeated Germany is split at Potsdam, blockaded at Berlin, and governed as two states for forty years until the Wall falls and the two become one again in 1990–91.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Ponhea Yat (1434–1463) plants the capital where the four rivers meet after Angkor's fall; his successors Norayreachea, Srei Reachea and Srei Sokonthor rule a kingdom rebuilding between Siam and Vietnam.
