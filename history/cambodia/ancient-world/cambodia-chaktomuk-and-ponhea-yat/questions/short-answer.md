# Cambodia: Chaktomuk and Ponhea Yat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Chaktomuk era (15th century)”?
**Answer:** Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 2, The Chaktomuk era (15th–16th centuries)” end?
**Answer:** Ponhea Yat (1434–1463) plants the capital where the four rivers meet after Angkor's fall; his successors Norayreachea, Srei Reachea and Srei Sokonthor rule a kingdom rebuilding between Siam and Vietnam. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
