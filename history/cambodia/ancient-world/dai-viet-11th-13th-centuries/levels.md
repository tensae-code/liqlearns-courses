# Dai Viet, 11th–13th Centuries — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Lac Viet of the Van Lang legend throw off a thousand years of Chinese rule in 938 and build Dai Viet along the Red River — a state 1,650 km long that Angkor must reckon with. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
