# Dai Viet, 11th–13th Centuries

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Dai Viet, 11th–13th Centuries** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Lac Viet of the Van Lang legend throw off a thousand years of Chinese rule in 938 and build Dai Viet along the Red River — a state 1,650 km long that Angkor must reckon with. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
