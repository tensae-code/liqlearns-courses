# Dai Viet, 11th–13th Centuries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Lac Viet of the Van Lang legend throw off a thousand years of Chinese rule in 938 and build Dai Viet along the Red River — a state 1,650 km long that Angkor must reckon with.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 2, The Dai Viet state (11th–13th centuries)” is mainly about this: Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Lac Viet of the Van Lang legend throw off a thousand years of Chinese rule in 938 and build Dai Viet along the Red River — a state 1,650 km long that Angkor must reckon with.
