# Angkor: Decline and Abandonment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 2 Lesson 6, The weakening of the Angkor empire”?
a) Jayavarman VIII's long reign strains the empire; Cham wars (1262) and Mongol-Yuan pressure (embassies of the 1280s) close in — the causes of decline are weighed one by one.  b) The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia.  c) Roosevelt names the "United Nations" in the declaration of 1 January 1942; on 24 October 1945 the organisation is born with 51 founding members.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Jayavarman VIII's long reign strains the empire; Cham wars (1262) and Mongol-Yuan pressure (embassies of the 1280s) close in — the causes of decline are weighed one by one.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Jayavarman VIII (1243–1296), Indravarman III (1295–1307, Zhou Daguan's host), the last inscription kings to Jayavarman IX (1327–1336) — then the court's slow road away from Angkor.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
