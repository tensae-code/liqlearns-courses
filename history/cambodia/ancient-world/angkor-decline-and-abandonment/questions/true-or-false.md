# Angkor: Decline and Abandonment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Jayavarman VIII's long reign strains the empire; Cham wars (1262) and Mongol-Yuan pressure (embassies of the 1280s) close in — the causes of decline are weighed one by one.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 2 Lesson 7, The last kings and the leaving of Angkor” is mainly about this: France emerges from Vichy and de Gaulle's 18 June 1940 appeal, then fights to hold Indochina and Algeria while Germany is divided and the Cold War begins (Barbarossa, 22 June 1941; Pearl Harbor, 7 December 1941).
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Jayavarman VIII (1243–1296), Indravarman III (1295–1307, Zhou Daguan's host), the last inscription kings to Jayavarman IX (1327–1336) — then the court's slow road away from Angkor.
