# Angkor: Decline and Abandonment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “History Ch. 2 Lesson 6, The weakening of the Angkor empire”?
**Answer:** Jayavarman VIII's long reign strains the empire; Cham wars (1262) and Mongol-Yuan pressure (embassies of the 1280s) close in — the causes of decline are weighed one by one. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 2 Lesson 7, The last kings and the leaving of Angkor” end?
**Answer:** Jayavarman VIII (1243–1296), Indravarman III (1295–1307, Zhou Daguan's host), the last inscription kings to Jayavarman IX (1327–1336) — then the court's slow road away from Angkor. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
