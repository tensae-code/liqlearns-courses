# The Mon States: Dvaravati and Thaton — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Mon state”?
**Answer:** The Mon (Talaing) build Dvaravati and Thaton between the Khmer and the Burmans (map, p. 146); their language and Buddhism shape all their neighbours, then fade — by the 20th century the Mon are a small people keeping an old tongue. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Mon state” end?
**Answer:** The Mon (Talaing) build Dvaravati and Thaton between the Khmer and the Burmans (map, p. 146); their language and Buddhism shape all their neighbours, then fade — by the 20th century the Mon are a small people keeping an old tongue. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
