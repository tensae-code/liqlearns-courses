# The Mon States: Dvaravati and Thaton — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Mon (Talaing) build Dvaravati and Thaton between the Khmer and the Burmans (map, p. 146); their language and Buddhism shape all their neighbours, then fade — by the 20th century the Mon are a small people keeping an old tongue.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Mon state” is mainly about this: The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Mon (Talaing) build Dvaravati and Thaton between the Khmer and the Burmans (map, p. 146); their language and Buddhism shape all their neighbours, then fade — by the 20th century the Mon are a small people keeping an old tongue.
