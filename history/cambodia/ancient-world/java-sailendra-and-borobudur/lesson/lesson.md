# Java: Sailendra and Borobudur

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Java: Sailendra and Borobudur** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

On the island south of Borneo (map, p. 142) a Hindu-Buddhist kingdom of Java builds Borobudur and trades across the archipelago — the southern mirror of Angkor. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
