# Java: Sailendra and Borobudur — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 3, The Java state”?
**Answer:** On the island south of Borneo (map, p. 142) a Hindu-Buddhist kingdom of Java builds Borobudur and trades across the archipelago — the southern mirror of Angkor. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 3, The Java state” end?
**Answer:** On the island south of Borneo (map, p. 142) a Hindu-Buddhist kingdom of Java builds Borobudur and trades across the archipelago — the southern mirror of Angkor. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
