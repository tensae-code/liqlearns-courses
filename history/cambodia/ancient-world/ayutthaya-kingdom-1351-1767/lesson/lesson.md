# The Ayutthaya Kingdom, 1351–1767

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Ayutthaya Kingdom, 1351–1767** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Ramathibodi founds Ayutthaya on the Chao Phraya in 1351; the lesson traces Siam from the "Syam" named in Angkor's 12th-century inscriptions to the power that will sack Angkor. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
