# The Ayutthaya Kingdom, 1351–1767 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ramathibodi founds Ayutthaya on the Chao Phraya in 1351; the lesson traces Siam from the "Syam" named in Angkor's 12th-century inscriptions to the power that will sack Angkor.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 3, The Ayutthaya state (1351–1767)” is mainly about this: The Munich photograph of 29 September 1938 — Chamberlain, Daladier, Hitler, Mussolini — opens a lesson that runs from the mandate system's grievances and Wilson's self-determination promise to total war.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Ramathibodi founds Ayutthaya on the Chao Phraya in 1351; the lesson traces Siam from the "Syam" named in Angkor's 12th-century inscriptions to the power that will sack Angkor.
