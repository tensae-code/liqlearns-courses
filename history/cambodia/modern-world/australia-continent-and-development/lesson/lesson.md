# Australia: Continent and Development

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Australia: Continent and Development** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

A continent-country of 7,686,850 square kilometres in the south-west Pacific, 15,000 kilometres from Britain, mapped state by state as the closing survey of the region. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
