# Cambodia under the French Protectorate, 1863–1953 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pressed by Siam and Vietnam, Cambodia accepts French protection in 1863; France shields the territory and rebuilds the state, yet rules it — until Sihanouk's crusade wins full independence in 1953.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 2 Lesson 1, Cambodia under the French protectorate (1863–1953)” is mainly about this: Ramathibodi founds Ayutthaya on the Chao Phraya in 1351; the lesson traces Siam from the "Syam" named in Angkor's 12th-century inscriptions to the power that will sack Angkor.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: King Ang Duong (1841–1860), squeezed between Siam and Vietnam, looks for a protector; his successors sign with France in 1863, and ninety years of protection end with Sihanouk's independence crusade.
