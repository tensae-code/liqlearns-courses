# Cambodia under the French Protectorate, 1863–1953 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 1, Cambodia under the French Protectorate (1863–1953)”?
**Answer:** Pressed by Siam and Vietnam, Cambodia accepts French protection in 1863; France shields the territory and rebuilds the state, yet rules it — until Sihanouk's crusade wins full independence in 1953. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Ch. 2 Lesson 1, Cambodia under the French protectorate (1863–1953)” end?
**Answer:** King Ang Duong (1841–1860), squeezed between Siam and Vietnam, looks for a protector; his successors sign with France in 1863, and ninety years of protection end with Sihanouk's independence crusade. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
