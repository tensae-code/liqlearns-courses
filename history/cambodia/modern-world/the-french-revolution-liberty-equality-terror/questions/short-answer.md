# The French Revolution: Liberty, Equality, Terror — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The French Revolution”?
**Answer:** Louis XVI summons the Estates-General to Versailles (5 May 1789); the Third Estate declares itself the National Assembly (17 June) and the old order begins to fall. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The French Revolution” end?
**Answer:** Louis XVI summons the Estates-General to Versailles (5 May 1789); the Third Estate declares itself the National Assembly (17 June) and the old order begins to fall. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
