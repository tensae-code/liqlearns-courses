# The French Revolution: Liberty, Equality, Terror — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Louis XVI summons the Estates-General to Versailles (5 May 1789); the Third Estate declares itself the National Assembly (17 June) and the old order begins to fall. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
