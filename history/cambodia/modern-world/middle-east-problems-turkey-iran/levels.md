# Middle East Problems: Turkey and Iran — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Ottoman collapse leaves a Turkish republic (from 1919) that seeks Europe's door for seventy years; Iran's oil and revolution make it the region's other pivot. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
