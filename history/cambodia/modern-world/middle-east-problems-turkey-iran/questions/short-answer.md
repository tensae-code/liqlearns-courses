# Middle East Problems: Turkey and Iran — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 4, Problems of the countries of East Asia (Turkey and Iran)”?
**Answer:** The Ottoman collapse leaves a Turkish republic (from 1919) that seeks Europe's door for seventy years; Iran's oil and revolution make it the region's other pivot. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 4, Problems of the countries of East Asia (Turkey and Iran)” end?
**Answer:** The Ottoman collapse leaves a Turkish republic (from 1919) that seeks Europe's door for seventy years; Iran's oil and revolution make it the region's other pivot. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
