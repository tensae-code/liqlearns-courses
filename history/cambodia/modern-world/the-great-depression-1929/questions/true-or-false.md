# The Great Depression, 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Prosperity shadowed by the Red Scare — Palmer's raids, Sacco and Vanzetti, the Scopes trial — then the 1929 crash turns the richest country into the Depression's epicentre.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 1, The United States (1860–1990)” is mainly about this: The 1970 coup removes Sihanouk, the republic is proclaimed, and five years of civil war tear the country apart.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From independence (4 July 1776) and the 1787 constitution through continental growth to the 1929 crisis and its worldwide consequences — the long arc of the republic the lesson keeps returning to.
