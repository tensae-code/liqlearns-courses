# The Great Depression, 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 11, The United States (1919–1939)”?
a) Prosperity shadowed by the Red Scare — Palmer's raids, Sacco and Vanzetti, the Scopes trial — then the 1929 crash turns the richest country into the Depression's epicentre.  b) Malays (53%), Chinese (36%) and Indians (11%) share one peninsula; the federation of Malaya, Sabah, Sarawak and Singapore is proclaimed on 31 August 1963, and Singapore departs in 1965.  c) King Norodom Sihanouk leads the Royal Crusade and Cambodia wins full independence from French rule on 9 November 1953; the Victory Monument ceremony closes the festival.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Prosperity shadowed by the Red Scare — Palmer's raids, Sacco and Vanzetti, the Scopes trial — then the 1929 crash turns the richest country into the Depression's epicentre.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
