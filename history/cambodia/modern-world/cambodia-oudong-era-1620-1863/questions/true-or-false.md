# Cambodia: The Oudong Era, 1620–1863 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Srei Sauriyopear moves the court to Oudong (1604); Chey Chettha II marries a Vietnamese princess and opens Prey Nokor to Vietnamese settlement (1620) — two centuries of shrinking land and civil war end at the French protectorate.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The capital at Oudong and the internal conflicts” is mainly about this: Unification in 1861 under Victor Emmanuel II leads through Mussolini's fascist state and defeat to the republic of 1946 — Rome, Milan, Turin and Naples as the stages of one national story.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Oudong, 45 km from today's capital, is built as the royal city in 1625 (photo: Phnom Oudong); kings divided by rival queens' factions invite Siam and Vietnam in, and the kingdom shrinks for two centuries.
