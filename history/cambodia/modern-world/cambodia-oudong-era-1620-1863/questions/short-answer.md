# Cambodia: The Oudong Era, 1620–1863 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Oudong era (1620–1863)”?
**Answer:** Srei Sauriyopear moves the court to Oudong (1604); Chey Chettha II marries a Vietnamese princess and opens Prey Nokor to Vietnamese settlement (1620) — two centuries of shrinking land and civil war end at the French protectorate. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The capital at Oudong and the internal conflicts” end?
**Answer:** Oudong, 45 km from today's capital, is built as the royal city in 1625 (photo: Phnom Oudong); kings divided by rival queens' factions invite Siam and Vietnam in, and the kingdom shrinks for two centuries. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
