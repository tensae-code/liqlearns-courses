## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Oudong era (1620–1863)** — Srei Sauriyopear moves the court to Oudong (1604); Chey Chettha II marries a Vietnamese princess and opens Prey Nokor to Vietnamese settlement (1620) — two centuries of shrinking land and civil war end at the French protectorate.
2. **Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The capital at Oudong and the internal conflicts** — Oudong, 45 km from today's capital, is built as the royal city in 1625 (photo: Phnom Oudong); kings divided by rival queens' factions invite Siam and Vietnam in, and the kingdom shrinks for two centuries.
3. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 4, The Oudong era (1620–1863)** — The usurper Sdach Korn is overthrown with Siamese help (1613); Chey Chettha II (1618–1628) moves the capital to Oudong in 1620 — and the long era of rival kings and foreign patrons begins.
