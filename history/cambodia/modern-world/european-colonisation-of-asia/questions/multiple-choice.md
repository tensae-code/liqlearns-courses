# The European Colonisation of Asia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 2, The European colonisation of Asia”?
a) The British East India Company takes Bengal and Calcutta, Burma falls in stages (Rangoon 1850, annexation January 1886), and France assembles Indochina — the machine that also took Cambodia.  b) France emerges from Vichy and de Gaulle's 18 June 1940 appeal, then fights to hold Indochina and Algeria while Germany is divided and the Cold War begins (Barbarossa, 22 June 1941; Pearl Harbor, 7 December 1941).  c) Three centuries from VOC traders to the Dutch East Indies (1799) and the Cultivation System; the 1908 awakening, the 1926–27 revolts and the 1928 Youth Pledge forge a nation before Sukarno proclaims it.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The British East India Company takes Bengal and Calcutta, Burma falls in stages (Rangoon 1850, annexation January 1886), and France assembles Indochina — the machine that also took Cambodia.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
