# The European Colonisation of Asia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The British East India Company takes Bengal and Calcutta, Burma falls in stages (Rangoon 1850, annexation January 1886), and France assembles Indochina — the machine that also took Cambodia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 2, The European colonisation of Asia” is mainly about this: 647 square kilometres and nearly five million people (2008) — Lee Kuan Yew's generation turns a colonial port into a city-state where Chinese, Malays and Indians trade with the world.
