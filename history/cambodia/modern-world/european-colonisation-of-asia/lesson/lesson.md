# The European Colonisation of Asia

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The European Colonisation of Asia** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The British East India Company takes Bengal and Calcutta, Burma falls in stages (Rangoon 1850, annexation January 1886), and France assembles Indochina — the machine that also took Cambodia. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
