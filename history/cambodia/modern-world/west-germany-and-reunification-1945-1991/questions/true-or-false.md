# West Germany and Reunification, 1945–1991 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A defeated Germany is split at Potsdam, blockaded at Berlin, and governed as two states for forty years until the Wall falls and the two become one again in 1990–91.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The Federal Republic of Germany (1945–1991)” is mainly about this: A grandfather tells Nari and Bonna how the boat-race festival began, so children inherit the story behind the races, the moon salutation and the flattened-rice offering.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A defeated Germany is split at Potsdam, blockaded at Berlin, and governed as two states for forty years until the Wall falls and the two become one again in 1990–91.
