# The First World War: Versailles and the Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 9, Ordering the world after the First World War”?
a) Ten million dead and twenty million wounded; the Russian, Austro-Hungarian and German empires fall, new states are drawn on the map, and America and Japan rise.  b) Three centuries from VOC traders to the Dutch East Indies (1799) and the Cultivation System; the 1908 awakening, the 1926–27 revolts and the 1928 Youth Pledge forge a nation before Sukarno proclaims it.  c) Pressed by Siam and Vietnam, Cambodia accepts French protection in 1863; France shields the territory and rebuilds the state, yet rules it — until Sihanouk's crusade wins full independence in 1953.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Ten million dead and twenty million wounded; the Russian, Austro-Hungarian and German empires fall, new states are drawn on the map, and America and Japan rise.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Some nine million dead, empires fallen, America turned world creditor; the victors build a League of Nations to keep a peace their own treaty undermines.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
