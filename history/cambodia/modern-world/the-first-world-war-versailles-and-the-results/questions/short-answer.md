# The First World War: Versailles and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 9, Ordering the world after the First World War”?
**Answer:** Ten million dead and twenty million wounded; the Russian, Austro-Hungarian and German empires fall, new states are drawn on the map, and America and Japan rise. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 2, Peace and the League of Nations” end?
**Answer:** Some nine million dead, empires fallen, America turned world creditor; the victors build a League of Nations to keep a peace their own treaty undermines. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
