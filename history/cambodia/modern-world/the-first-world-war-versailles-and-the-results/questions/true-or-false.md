# The First World War: Versailles and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ten million dead and twenty million wounded; the Russian, Austro-Hungarian and German empires fall, new states are drawn on the map, and America and Japan rise.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 1 Lesson 2, Peace and the League of Nations” is mainly about this: Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Some nine million dead, empires fallen, America turned world creditor; the victors build a League of Nations to keep a peace their own treaty undermines.
