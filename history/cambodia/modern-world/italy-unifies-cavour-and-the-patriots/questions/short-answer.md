# Italy: Unification to Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 5, Italy”?
**Answer:** Unification in 1861 under Victor Emmanuel II leads through Mussolini's fascist state and defeat to the republic of 1946 — Rome, Milan, Turin and Naples as the stages of one national story. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 5, Italy” end?
**Answer:** Unification in 1861 under Victor Emmanuel II leads through Mussolini's fascist state and defeat to the republic of 1946 — Rome, Milan, Turin and Naples as the stages of one national story. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
