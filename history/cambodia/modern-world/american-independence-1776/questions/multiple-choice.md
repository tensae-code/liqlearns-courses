**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 5, The founding of the United States of America”?
a) Thirteen English colonies of Arawak, Sioux and Apache lands (photo: a Native American woman) declare independence and invent a republic across the Atlantic.  b) Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression.  c) Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Macedonians, Montenegrins and Bosnians (peoples map, p. 74) are joined in one kingdom on 1 December 1918, shattered by invasion in 1941, and held together after the war as a federation.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
