# Vietnam: French Rule to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 2, Vietnam”?
a) French missionaries and gunboats arrive from the 17th century; Gia Long (1802–1820) unifies Vietnam with French help, yet by 1885 France rules it all — and works its rubber plantations with hundreds of thousands of labourers.  b) An oasis civilisation strung along the Nile (map, p. 80) — delta and valley united under pharaohs who command the flood's calendar.  c) The Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) French missionaries and gunboats arrive from the 17th century; Gia Long (1802–1820) unifies Vietnam with French help, yet by 1885 France rules it all — and works its rubber plantations with hundreds of thousands of labourers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
