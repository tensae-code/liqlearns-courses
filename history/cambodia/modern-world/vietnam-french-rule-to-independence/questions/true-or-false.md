# Vietnam: French Rule to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) French missionaries and gunboats arrive from the 17th century; Gia Long (1802–1820) unifies Vietnam with French help, yet by 1885 France rules it all — and works its rubber plantations with hundreds of thousands of labourers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 5, Vietnam” is mainly about this: Jayavarman II (802–850), back from captivity in Java, is proclaimed universal monarch on Phnom Kulen in 802 and unites the two Chenlas; Yasovarman I founds the city of Angkor in the 9th century, and temple-building kings raise Angkor Wat over a land watered by great reservoirs.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The lesson is Ho Chi Minh's life (photos, p. 156): born 19 May 1890, sailor and exile, founder of the Indochinese Communist Party (3 February 1930), proclaimer of the republic (1945), victor over France (1954), dead in Hanoi on 3 September 1969.
