# Vietnam: French Rule to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 2, Vietnam”?
**Answer:** French missionaries and gunboats arrive from the 17th century; Gia Long (1802–1820) unifies Vietnam with French help, yet by 1885 France rules it all — and works its rubber plantations with hundreds of thousands of labourers. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 5, Vietnam” end?
**Answer:** The lesson is Ho Chi Minh's life (photos, p. 156): born 19 May 1890, sailor and exile, founder of the Indochinese Communist Party (3 February 1930), proclaimer of the republic (1945), victor over France (1954), dead in Hanoi on 3 September 1969. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
