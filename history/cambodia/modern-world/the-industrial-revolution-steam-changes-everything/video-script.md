# Video script — The Industrial Revolution: Steam Changes Everything

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

England's steam factories (chapter frontispiece) spread to France and Germany (steel by 1870), remake farming (threshing, rye and barley rotations), swell and shift populations (French regional table, 1851–1999), and put women and children into the mills. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Cambodia.
