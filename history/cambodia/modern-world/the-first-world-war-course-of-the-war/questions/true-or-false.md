Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The small wars of 1898, 1899–1902 and 1904–05 rehearse the great one; four years of trenches end with a new League meant to keep the peace.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 8, The First World War (1914–1918) and the founding of the League of Nations” is mainly about this: King Norodom Sihanouk leads the Royal Crusade and Cambodia wins full independence from French rule on 9 November 1953; the Victory Monument ceremony closes the festival.
