# The United States, 1860–1914: Railways, Land and Growing Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 1, The United States (1860–1914)”?
a) A continent stitched together by railways (first transcontinental line, 1869) and by buying and taking land — Louisiana, Florida, Texas, Alaska for $7.2 million — while immigration swells the cities from 31 to 50 million people.  b) Unification in 1861 under Victor Emmanuel II leads through Mussolini's fascist state and defeat to the republic of 1946 — Rome, Milan, Turin and Naples as the stages of one national story.  c) Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A continent stitched together by railways (first transcontinental line, 1869) and by buying and taking land — Louisiana, Florida, Texas, Alaska for $7.2 million — while immigration swells the cities from 31 to 50 million people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
