**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 10, Yugoslavia”?
a) Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Macedonians, Montenegrins and Bosnians (peoples map, p. 74) are joined in one kingdom on 1 December 1918, shattered by invasion in 1941, and held together after the war as a federation.  b) Survivors rebuild from zero; the Paris Agreements of 23 October 1991 and UNTAC bring elections, and Angkor joins the World Heritage List in 1992.  c) Tyre, Byblos and Sidon (map, p. 94) grow rich on purple dye and cedar, and their traders carry a simple alphabet to every shore.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Macedonians, Montenegrins and Bosnians (peoples map, p. 74) are joined in one kingdom on 1 December 1918, shattered by invasion in 1941, and held together after the war as a federation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
