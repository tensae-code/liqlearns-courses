# Yugoslavia: Peoples Joined in One State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Macedonians, Montenegrins and Bosnians (peoples map, p. 74) are joined in one kingdom on 1 December 1918, shattered by invasion in 1941, and held together after the war as a federation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 10, Yugoslavia” is mainly about this: Italy's city on seven hills conquers Sicily, then the whole Mediterranean world (map, p. 104), passing from republic to empire.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Macedonians, Montenegrins and Bosnians (peoples map, p. 74) are joined in one kingdom on 1 December 1918, shattered by invasion in 1941, and held together after the war as a federation.
