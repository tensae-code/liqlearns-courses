# Yugoslavia: Peoples Joined in One State

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Yugoslavia: Peoples Joined in One State** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Macedonians, Montenegrins and Bosnians (peoples map, p. 74) are joined in one kingdom on 1 December 1918, shattered by invasion in 1941, and held together after the war as a federation. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
