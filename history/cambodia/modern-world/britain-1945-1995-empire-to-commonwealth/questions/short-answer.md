# Britain, 1945–1995: Empire to Commonwealth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 2, The British Empire”?
**Answer:** The empire that was the world's largest before 1914 shrinks after 1945 into a 224,046 km² island state of 58 million (2006), its politics rebuilt around parties, welfare and Europe. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 2, The British Empire” end?
**Answer:** The empire that was the world's largest before 1914 shrinks after 1945 into a 224,046 km² island state of 58 million (2006), its politics rebuilt around parties, welfare and Europe. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
