**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The French Republic”?
a) France emerges from Vichy and de Gaulle's 18 June 1940 appeal, then fights to hold Indochina and Algeria while Germany is divided and the Cold War begins (Barbarossa, 22 June 1941; Pearl Harbor, 7 December 1941).  b) A continent-country of 7,686,850 square kilometres in the south-west Pacific, 15,000 kilometres from Britain, mapped state by state as the closing survey of the region.  c) Before any writing, people left stone axes, pottery and bronze at Samrong Sen, Mlu Prei, Anlong Phdao and the Kampot caves — the dug-up things are the only witnesses.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
