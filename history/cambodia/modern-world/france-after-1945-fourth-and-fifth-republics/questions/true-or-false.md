# France after 1945: Fourth and Fifth Republics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France emerges from Vichy and de Gaulle's 18 June 1940 appeal, then fights to hold Indochina and Algeria while Germany is divided and the Cold War begins (Barbarossa, 22 June 1941; Pearl Harbor, 7 December 1941).
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The French Republic” is mainly about this: Cave-dwellers hunt, gather and fish with stone tools; then they settle, farm, keep animals and honour the neak ta spirits — the turn from wandering to village life.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: France emerges from Vichy and de Gaulle's 18 June 1940 appeal, then fights to hold Indochina and Algeria while Germany is divided and the Cold War begins (Barbarossa, 22 June 1941; Pearl Harbor, 7 December 1941).
