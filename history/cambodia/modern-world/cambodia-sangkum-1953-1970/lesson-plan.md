## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 2, the Sangkum Reastr Niyum regime (1953–1970)** — Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case.
2. **Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum regime (1955–1970)** — Geneva (1954) confirms independence; Sihanouk abdicates on 2 March 1955, founds the Sangkum on 22 March, and sweeps the September 1955 elections — the prince rules as head of a national movement.
3. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum era (1955–1970)** — Sihanouk (born 31 October 1922; photo, p. 112) wins independence on 9 November 1953, abdicates in March 1955, founds the Sangkum, and takes 99.80% in the September 1955 elections.
