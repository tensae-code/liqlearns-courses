# Cambodia: The Sangkum, 1953–1970 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 2, the Sangkum Reastr Niyum regime (1953–1970)”?
a) Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case.  b) On the island south of Borneo (map, p. 142) a Hindu-Buddhist kingdom of Java builds Borobudur and trades across the archipelago — the southern mirror of Angkor.  c) Ramathibodi founds Ayutthaya on the Chao Phraya in 1351; the lesson traces Siam from the "Syam" named in Angkor's 12th-century inscriptions to the power that will sack Angkor.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
