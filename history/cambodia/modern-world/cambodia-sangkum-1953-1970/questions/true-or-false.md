# Cambodia: The Sangkum, 1953–1970 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum regime (1955–1970)” is mainly about this: After Rome falls to the "barbarians" (les barbares), Europe spends a thousand years as a ladder of lords and vassals — land for loyalty, peasants bound below.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Geneva (1954) confirms independence; Sihanouk abdicates on 2 March 1955, founds the Sangkum on 22 March, and sweeps the September 1955 elections — the prince rules as head of a national movement.
