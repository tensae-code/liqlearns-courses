# The Second World War: Course in Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 16, The Second World War (1939–1945)”?
**Answer:** The Munich photograph of 29 September 1938 — Chamberlain, Daladier, Hitler, Mussolini — opens a lesson that runs from the mandate system's grievances and Wilson's self-determination promise to total war. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 16, The Second World War (1939–1945)” end?
**Answer:** The Munich photograph of 29 September 1938 — Chamberlain, Daladier, Hitler, Mussolini — opens a lesson that runs from the mandate system's grievances and Wilson's self-determination promise to total war. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
