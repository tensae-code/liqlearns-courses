# Cambodia: Independence, 1953 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Appendix text 15, National Independence Day ceremony”?
**Answer:** King Norodom Sihanouk leads the Royal Crusade and Cambodia wins full independence from French rule on 9 November 1953; the Victory Monument ceremony closes the festival. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Appendix song 16, "The Flag of Cambodia"” end?
**Answer:** Children sing the national flag as the sign of the independent country won in 1953. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
