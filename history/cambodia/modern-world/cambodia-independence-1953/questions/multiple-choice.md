# Cambodia: Independence, 1953 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Appendix text 15, National Independence Day ceremony”?
a) King Norodom Sihanouk leads the Royal Crusade and Cambodia wins full independence from French rule on 9 November 1953; the Victory Monument ceremony closes the festival.  b) Under the 2,000 km Himalaya wall (Everest 8,848 m) the Indus and Ganges plains grow some of the world's oldest cities (map, p. 138).  c) The Lac Viet of the Van Lang legend throw off a thousand years of Chinese rule in 938 and build Dai Viet along the Red River — a state 1,650 km long that Angkor must reckon with.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) King Norodom Sihanouk leads the Royal Crusade and Cambodia wins full independence from French rule on 9 November 1953; the Victory Monument ceremony closes the festival.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Children sing the national flag as the sign of the independent country won in 1953.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
