# Cambodia: Independence, 1953 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) King Norodom Sihanouk leads the Royal Crusade and Cambodia wins full independence from French rule on 9 November 1953; the Victory Monument ceremony closes the festival.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Appendix song 16, "The Flag of Cambodia"” is mainly about this: Fa Ngum, a Lao prince raised at the Angkor court and married to a Khmer princess, carries Buddhism home and unites the Lao valleys as Lan Xang, the kingdom of a million elephants (1353).
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Children sing the national flag as the sign of the independent country won in 1953.
