# Thailand: The Kingdom That Was Never Colonised — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 3, Thailand”?
a) Siam survives the 19th century uncolonised, a buffer between British Burma and French Indochina, by reforming its state under the Chakri kings and trading land for sovereignty.  b) Between the Tigris and the Euphrates, falling from the Armenian mountains, Sumerian cities invent writing, law and the wheel (map, p. 86).  c) The kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Siam survives the 19th century uncolonised, a buffer between British Burma and French Indochina, by reforming its state under the Chakri kings and trading land for sovereignty.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
