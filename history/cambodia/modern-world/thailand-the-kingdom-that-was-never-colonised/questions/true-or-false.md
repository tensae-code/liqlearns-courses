# Thailand: The Kingdom That Was Never Colonised — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Siam survives the 19th century uncolonised, a buffer between British Burma and French Indochina, by reforming its state under the Chakri kings and trading land for sovereignty.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 4, Thailand” is mainly about this: Exam questions reach back to Angkor Wat's builder and to Jayavarman VII, the king who raised hospitals, roads and the Bayon — the medieval empire the modern state claims as its root.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Sukhothai (1238–1350), Ayutthaya (1350–1767), Thonburi (1767–1800), Bangkok (1800– ) — and on 24 June 1932 seven centuries of absolute monarchy end in a single morning's revolution.
