# Thailand: The Kingdom That Was Never Colonised — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 3, Thailand”?
**Answer:** Siam survives the 19th century uncolonised, a buffer between British Burma and French Indochina, by reforming its state under the Chakri kings and trading land for sovereignty. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 4, Thailand” end?
**Answer:** Sukhothai (1238–1350), Ayutthaya (1350–1767), Thonburi (1767–1800), Bangkok (1800– ) — and on 24 June 1932 seven centuries of absolute monarchy end in a single morning's revolution. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
