# Indonesia: Dutch Rule to Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Indonesia: Dutch Rule to Independence** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 3 Lesson 1, Indonesia

Three centuries from VOC traders to the Dutch East Indies (1799) and the Cultivation System; the 1908 awakening, the 1926–27 revolts and the 1928 Youth Pledge forge a nation before Sukarno proclaims it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — History Ch. 3 Lesson 1, Indonesia

17,508 islands and 300 peoples (185 million in 1990) were ruled as one colony from the VOC's 17th-century factories to the Dutch East Indies — the largest country of the region made by empire. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
