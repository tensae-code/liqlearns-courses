**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 1, Indonesia”?
a) Three centuries from VOC traders to the Dutch East Indies (1799) and the Cultivation System; the 1908 awakening, the 1926–27 revolts and the 1928 Youth Pledge forge a nation before Sukarno proclaims it.  b) Before any writing, people left stone axes, pottery and bronze at Samrong Sen, Mlu Prei, Anlong Phdao and the Kampot caves — the dug-up things are the only witnesses.  c) At Zhoukoudian in 1924 diggers find "Peking Man" (Sinanthropus), 500,000–200,000 years old; civilisation follows on the Yellow River (map, p. 134).  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
