# Indonesia: Dutch Rule to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 1, Indonesia”?
**Answer:** Three centuries from VOC traders to the Dutch East Indies (1799) and the Cultivation System; the 1908 awakening, the 1926–27 revolts and the 1928 Youth Pledge forge a nation before Sukarno proclaims it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 1, Indonesia” end?
**Answer:** 17,508 islands and 300 peoples (185 million in 1990) were ruled as one colony from the VOC's 17th-century factories to the Dutch East Indies — the largest country of the region made by empire. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
