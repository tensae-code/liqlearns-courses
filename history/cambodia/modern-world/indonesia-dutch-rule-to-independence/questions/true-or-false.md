# Indonesia: Dutch Rule to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Three centuries from VOC traders to the Dutch East Indies (1799) and the Cultivation System; the 1908 awakening, the 1926–27 revolts and the 1928 Youth Pledge forge a nation before Sukarno proclaims it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 1, Indonesia” is mainly about this: On the island south of Borneo (map, p. 142) a Hindu-Buddhist kingdom of Java builds Borobudur and trades across the archipelago — the southern mirror of Angkor.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: 17,508 islands and 300 peoples (185 million in 1990) were ruled as one colony from the VOC's 17th-century factories to the Dutch East Indies — the largest country of the region made by empire.
