# The World after 1945: The United Nations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 17, The United Nations”?
**Answer:** Roosevelt names the "United Nations" in the declaration of 1 January 1942; on 24 October 1945 the organisation is born with 51 founding members. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The United Nations” end?
**Answer:** Founded on 24 October 1945 with 51 member states, the UN writes 111 charter articles on sovereign equality and peaceful settlement. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
