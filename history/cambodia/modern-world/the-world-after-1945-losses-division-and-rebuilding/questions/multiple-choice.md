# The World after 1945: The United Nations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 17, The United Nations”?
a) Roosevelt names the "United Nations" in the declaration of 1 January 1942; on 24 October 1945 the organisation is born with 51 founding members.  b) In 1947 Britain hands Palestine to the UN; the land is partitioned, Israel is proclaimed, and the Arab-Israeli wars begin — a small state of eight million at the centre of a long conflict.  c) Five full practice papers rehearse the regime-by-regime story under exam conditions.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Roosevelt names the "United Nations" in the declaration of 1 January 1942; on 24 October 1945 the organisation is born with 51 founding members.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The United Nations”?
a) Founded on 24 October 1945 with 51 member states, the UN writes 111 charter articles on sovereign equality and peaceful settlement.  b) Locke and Montesquieu (portrait, p. 114) arm liberalism; conservatism answers; socialism and Proudhon's anarchism (portrait, p. 119) speak for the workers the factories made.  c) The small wars of 1898, 1899–1902 and 1904–05 rehearse the great one; four years of trenches end with a new League meant to keep the peace.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Founded on 24 October 1945 with 51 member states, the UN writes 111 charter articles on sovereign equality and peaceful settlement.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
