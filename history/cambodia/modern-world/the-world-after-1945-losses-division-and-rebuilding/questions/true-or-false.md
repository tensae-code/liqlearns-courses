# The World after 1945: The United Nations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Roosevelt names the "United Nations" in the declaration of 1 January 1942; on 24 October 1945 the organisation is born with 51 founding members.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The United Nations” is mainly about this: Before any writing, people left stone axes, pottery and bronze at Samrong Sen, Mlu Prei, Anlong Phdao and the Kampot caves — the dug-up things are the only witnesses.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Founded on 24 October 1945 with 51 member states, the UN writes 111 charter articles on sovereign equality and peaceful settlement.
