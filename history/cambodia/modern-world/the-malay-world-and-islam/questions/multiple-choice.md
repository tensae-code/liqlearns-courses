**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 5, The Malay world”?
a) Arab and Indian traders bring Islam to the Malay ports; Malacca's sultanate spreads the faith and the Malay tongue across the peninsula and islands Cambodia trades with.  b) The Munich photograph of 29 September 1938 — Chamberlain, Daladier, Hitler, Mussolini — opens a lesson that runs from the mandate system's grievances and Wilson's self-determination promise to total war.  c) The century closes with shared dangers no state can solve alone — pollution and climate damage, population growth, foreign debt and international terrorism.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
