## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)** — The Khmer Rouge take Phnom Penh, empty the cities and impose a regime whose genocide devastates Cambodia before it falls in January 1979.
2. **Ch. 2 Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)** — On 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge enter Phnom Penh and empty it within days; their programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money, markets and religion — turns the country into a prison camp.
3. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 4, The Democratic Kampuchea regime (1975–1979)** — On 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge empty Phnom Penh in three days; Angkar's programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money and markets, defrock the monks, execute the old leaders — is listed point by point.
