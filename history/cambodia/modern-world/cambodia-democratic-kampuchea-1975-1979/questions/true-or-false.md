# Cambodia: Democratic Kampuchea, 1975–1979 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Khmer Rouge take Phnom Penh, empty the cities and impose a regime whose genocide devastates Cambodia before it falls in January 1979.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 2 Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)” is mainly about this: Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: On 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge enter Phnom Penh and empty it within days; their programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money, markets and religion — turns the country into a prison camp.
