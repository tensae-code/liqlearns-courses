# Cambodia: Democratic Kampuchea, 1975–1979 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)”?
a) The Khmer Rouge take Phnom Penh, empty the cities and impose a regime whose genocide devastates Cambodia before it falls in January 1979.  b) The Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls.  c) After Rome falls to the "barbarians" (les barbares), Europe spends a thousand years as a ladder of lords and vassals — land for loyalty, peasants bound below.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Khmer Rouge take Phnom Penh, empty the cities and impose a regime whose genocide devastates Cambodia before it falls in January 1979.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) On 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge enter Phnom Penh and empty it within days; their programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money, markets and religion — turns the country into a prison camp.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
