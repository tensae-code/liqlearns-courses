# The British Empire, 1865–1914 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The British Empire (1865–1914)”?
a) Victoria's Britain (1837–1901) rules the world's greatest colonial empire with India as its jewel, Whigs and Tories alternating at home while it rivals Russia in Asia and Germany at sea.  b) Masaryk's republic is born from the First World War (1918), governed with Beneš, dismembered at Munich in 1938, and remade after 1945 as a people's democracy of 15 million.  c) Siam survives the 19th century uncolonised, a buffer between British Burma and French Indochina, by reforming its state under the Chakri kings and trading land for sovereignty.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Victoria's Britain (1837–1901) rules the world's greatest colonial empire with India as its jewel, Whigs and Tories alternating at home while it rivals Russia in Asia and Germany at sea.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
