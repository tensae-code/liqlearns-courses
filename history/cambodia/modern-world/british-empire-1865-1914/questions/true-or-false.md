# The British Empire, 1865–1914 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Victoria's Britain (1837–1901) rules the world's greatest colonial empire with India as its jewel, Whigs and Tories alternating at home while it rivals Russia in Asia and Germany at sea.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The British Empire (1865–1914)” is mainly about this: In 1947 Britain hands Palestine to the UN; the land is partitioned, Israel is proclaimed, and the Arab-Israeli wars begin — a small state of eight million at the centre of a long conflict.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Victoria's Britain (1837–1901) rules the world's greatest colonial empire with India as its jewel, Whigs and Tories alternating at home while it rivals Russia in Asia and Germany at sea.
