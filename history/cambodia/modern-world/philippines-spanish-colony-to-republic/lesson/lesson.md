# The Philippines: Spanish Colony to Republic

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Philippines: Spanish Colony to Republic** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
