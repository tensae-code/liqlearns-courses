# The Philippines: Spanish Colony to Republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Philippines” is mainly about this: The kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin.
