# The Philippines: Spanish Colony to Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Philippines”?
**Answer:** Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Philippines” end?
**Answer:** Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
