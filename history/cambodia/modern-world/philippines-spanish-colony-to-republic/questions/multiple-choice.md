Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 4, The Philippines”?
a) Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin.  b) A grandfather tells Nari and Bonna how the boat-race festival began, so children inherit the story behind the races, the moon salutation and the flattened-rice offering.  c) The Mon (Talaing) build Dvaravati and Thaton between the Khmer and the Burmans (map, p. 146); their language and Buddhism shape all their neighbours, then fade — by the 20th century the Mon are a small people keeping an old tongue.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Magellan is killed by Lapulapu at Mactan in 1521, yet Spain returns, names the 7,000 islands for Philip II and founds Manila (1571) — three centuries of Spanish rule begin.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
