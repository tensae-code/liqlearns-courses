# The Second World War: Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Second World War: Causes** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 1 Lesson 15, The great problems before the Second World War

Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — History Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The Second World War (1939–1945)

The causes are sorted into three baskets — the economic crash, the political rise of Nazism, and the failure of the peace system. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
