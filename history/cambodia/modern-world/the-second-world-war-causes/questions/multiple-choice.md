# The Second World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 15, The great problems before the Second World War”?
a) Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression.  b) Siam survives the 19th century uncolonised, a buffer between British Burma and French Indochina, by reforming its state under the Chakri kings and trading land for sovereignty.  c) Survivors rebuild from zero; the Paris Agreements of 23 October 1991 and UNTAC bring elections, and Angkor joins the World Heritage List in 1992.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The causes are sorted into three baskets — the economic crash, the political rise of Nazism, and the failure of the peace system.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
