# The Second World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 15, The great problems before the Second World War”?
**Answer:** Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The Second World War (1939–1945)” end?
**Answer:** The causes are sorted into three baskets — the economic crash, the political rise of Nazism, and the failure of the peace system. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
