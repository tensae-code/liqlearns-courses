# The Second World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Germany's 138 billion gold marks of reparations are cut by the Dawes and Young plans into a chain of American loans — a settlement built on credit that collapses with the Depression.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 1 Lesson 3, The Second World War (1939–1945)” is mainly about this: Five full practice papers rehearse the regime-by-regime story under exam conditions.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The causes are sorted into three baskets — the economic crash, the political rise of Nazism, and the failure of the peace system.
