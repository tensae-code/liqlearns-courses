# France and Britain between the Wars

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **France and Britain between the Wars** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

France clings to its recovered provinces and reparations while the franc slides (1923–1926 crisis); Britain's world lead quietly drains away. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
