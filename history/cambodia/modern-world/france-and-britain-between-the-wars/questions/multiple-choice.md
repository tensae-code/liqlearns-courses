Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 14, France and Britain (1919–1939)”?
a) France clings to its recovered provinces and reparations while the franc slides (1923–1926 crisis); Britain's world lead quietly drains away.  b) French missionaries and gunboats arrive from the 17th century; Gia Long (1802–1820) unifies Vietnam with French help, yet by 1885 France rules it all — and works its rubber plantations with hundreds of thousands of labourers.  c) The Khmer Rouge take Phnom Penh, empty the cities and impose a regime whose genocide devastates Cambodia before it falls in January 1979.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) France clings to its recovered provinces and reparations while the franc slides (1923–1926 crisis); Britain's world lead quietly drains away.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
