# Video script — Singapore: From Port to City-State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

647 square kilometres and nearly five million people (2008) — Lee Kuan Yew's generation turns a colonial port into a city-state where Chinese, Malays and Indians trade with the world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
Expelled from Malaysia in September 1965, a 646 km² island of 3 million (1998) — Chinese 76.6%, Malays 14% — is driven by Lee Kuan Yew's government to first-world wealth in one generation. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Cambodia.
