# Singapore: From Port to City-State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) 647 square kilometres and nearly five million people (2008) — Lee Kuan Yew's generation turns a colonial port into a city-state where Chinese, Malays and Indians trade with the world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 3, Singapore” is mainly about this: The Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Expelled from Malaysia in September 1965, a 646 km² island of 3 million (1998) — Chinese 76.6%, Malays 14% — is driven by Lee Kuan Yew's government to first-world wealth in one generation.
