# Malaysia: Federation, 1963 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 2, Malaysia”?
a) Malays (53%), Chinese (36%) and Indians (11%) share one peninsula; the federation of Malaya, Sabah, Sarawak and Singapore is proclaimed on 31 August 1963, and Singapore departs in 1965.  b) Children lay the kings end to end — Hun Tien in the 1st century, the Hun Pan line (225–243), the Srei line (430–484), Kaundinya Jayavarman (484–514) who raised Hinduism high — six centuries on one arrow.  c) Under the 2,000 km Himalaya wall (Everest 8,848 m) the Indus and Ganges plains grow some of the world's oldest cities (map, p. 138).  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Malays (53%), Chinese (36%) and Indians (11%) share one peninsula; the federation of Malaya, Sabah, Sarawak and Singapore is proclaimed on 31 August 1963, and Singapore departs in 1965.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
