# Malaysia: Federation, 1963 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Malays (53%), Chinese (36%) and Indians (11%) share one peninsula; the federation of Malaya, Sabah, Sarawak and Singapore is proclaimed on 31 August 1963, and Singapore departs in 1965.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 3 Lesson 2, Malaysia” is mainly about this: The Mon (Talaing) build Dvaravati and Thaton between the Khmer and the Burmans (map, p. 146); their language and Buddhism shape all their neighbours, then fade — by the 20th century the Mon are a small people keeping an old tongue.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Malacca falls to Portugal (1511), the Dutch (1641), then Britain (1815); Japan storms in on 8 December 1941 and Singapore falls on 15 February 1942 — the road to a federation of Malays (55%), Chinese (32%) and Indians (8%).
