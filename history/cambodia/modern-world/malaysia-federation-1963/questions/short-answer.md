# Malaysia: Federation, 1963 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 2, Malaysia”?
**Answer:** Malays (53%), Chinese (36%) and Indians (11%) share one peninsula; the federation of Malaya, Sabah, Sarawak and Singapore is proclaimed on 31 August 1963, and Singapore departs in 1965. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 3 Lesson 2, Malaysia” end?
**Answer:** Malacca falls to Portugal (1511), the Dutch (1641), then Britain (1815); Japan storms in on 8 December 1941 and Singapore falls on 15 February 1942 — the road to a federation of Malays (55%), Chinese (32%) and Indians (8%). Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
