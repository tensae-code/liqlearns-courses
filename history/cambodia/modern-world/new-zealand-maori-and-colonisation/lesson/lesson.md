# New Zealand: Māori and Colonisation

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **New Zealand: Māori and Colonisation** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Māori voyagers settle the islands (by c. 1300), Abel Tasman sights them in 1642 and names them for Zeeland; Wellington, Auckland and Christchurch grow into a Pacific state. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
