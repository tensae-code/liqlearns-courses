# New Zealand: Māori and Colonisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 3 Lesson 6, New Zealand (contents page mislabels it Vietnam)”?
**Answer:** Māori voyagers settle the islands (by c. 1300), Abel Tasman sights them in 1642 and names them for Zeeland; Wellington, Auckland and Christchurch grow into a Pacific state. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 6, New Zealand (contents page mislabels it Vietnam)” end?
