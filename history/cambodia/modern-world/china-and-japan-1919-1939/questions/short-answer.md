# China and Japan, 1919–1939 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 12, China and Japan (1919–1939)”?
**Answer:** Sun Yat-sen's reorganised Kuomintang and the Communists (party founded 1921) move from alliance to civil war, while militarist Japan presses on the mainland. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 12, China and Japan (1919–1939)” end?
**Answer:** Sun Yat-sen's reorganised Kuomintang and the Communists (party founded 1921) move from alliance to civil war, while militarist Japan presses on the mainland. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
