# France: The Third Republic, 1871–1914

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **France: The Third Republic, 1871–1914** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
