# France: The Third Republic, 1871–1914 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 5, The French Republic (1871–1914)”?
**Answer:** The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 5, The French Republic (1871–1914)” end?
**Answer:** The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
