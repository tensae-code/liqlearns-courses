# France: The Third Republic, 1871–1914 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 5, The French Republic (1871–1914)” is mainly about this: A continent-country of 7,686,850 square kilometres in the south-west Pacific, 15,000 kilometres from Britain, mapped state by state as the closing survey of the region.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Third Republic survives its shaky birth and builds the second colonial empire — into Indochina by the Tientsin treaty of 1885 and the union of 1887, the empire that also held Cambodia.
