# Cambodia: Rebuilding and Peace, 1979–1993 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Survivors rebuild from zero; the Paris Agreements of 23 October 1991 and UNTAC bring elections, and Angkor joins the World Heritage List in 1992.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 2 Lesson 5, The People's Republic of Kampuchea and the State of Cambodia (1979–1993)” is mainly about this: Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Cadres who fled the purges (Heng Samrin, photo) found a salvation front on 2 December 1978; victory on 7 January 1979 starts the rebuilding that ends with the Paris Agreements and UNTAC.
