# Cambodia: Rebuilding and Peace, 1979–1993 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 5, People's Republic of Kampuchea and State of Cambodia (1979–1993)”?
**Answer:** Survivors rebuild from zero; the Paris Agreements of 23 October 1991 and UNTAC bring elections, and Angkor joins the World Heritage List in 1992. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Ch. 2 Lesson 5, The People's Republic of Kampuchea and the State of Cambodia (1979–1993)” end?
**Answer:** Cadres who fled the purges (Heng Samrin, photo) found a salvation front on 2 December 1978; victory on 7 January 1979 starts the rebuilding that ends with the Paris Agreements and UNTAC. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
