# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 6, The Russian Empire (1855–1917)”?
**Answer:** Peter the Great and Catherine II built a many-peopled empire — Russians, Ukrainians, Lithuanians, Baltic and Finnic peoples; Alexander II reforms too late, and the structure cracks toward October 1917. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 10, The Soviet Union (1919–1939)” end?
**Answer:** February 1917 topples the Tsar, October brings the Bolsheviks; Lenin's party builds the USSR out of the revolution while the West shuns it. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
