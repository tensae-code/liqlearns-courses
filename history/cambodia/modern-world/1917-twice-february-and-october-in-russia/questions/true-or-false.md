# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Peter the Great and Catherine II built a many-peopled empire — Russians, Ukrainians, Lithuanians, Baltic and Finnic peoples; Alexander II reforms too late, and the structure cracks toward October 1917.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 10, The Soviet Union (1919–1939)” is mainly about this: Cambodia sits inside ASEAN's ten nations and a globalising world of food insecurity, migration and terrorism; the EU is held up as the model of regional union.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: February 1917 topples the Tsar, October brings the Bolsheviks; Lenin's party builds the USSR out of the revolution while the West shuns it.
