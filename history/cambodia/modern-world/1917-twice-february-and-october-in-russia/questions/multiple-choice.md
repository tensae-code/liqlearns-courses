# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 6, The Russian Empire (1855–1917)”?
a) Peter the Great and Catherine II built a many-peopled empire — Russians, Ukrainians, Lithuanians, Baltic and Finnic peoples; Alexander II reforms too late, and the structure cracks toward October 1917.  b) The century closes with shared dangers no state can solve alone — pollution and climate damage, population growth, foreign debt and international terrorism.  c) Māori voyagers settle the islands (by c. 1300), Abel Tasman sights them in 1642 and names them for Zeeland; Wellington, Auckland and Christchurch grow into a Pacific state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Peter the Great and Catherine II built a many-peopled empire — Russians, Ukrainians, Lithuanians, Baltic and Finnic peoples; Alexander II reforms too late, and the structure cracks toward October 1917.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
