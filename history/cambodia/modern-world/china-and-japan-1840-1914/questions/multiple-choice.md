# China and Japan, 1840–1914 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 2, China and Japan (1840–1914)”?
a) Qing China (1642–1912), rich and self-satisfied, is forced open by foreign powers, while Japan studies the same West and modernises instead of submitting.  b) The Soviet and American blocs race in arms, ideology and technology while the UN is blocked and new nations are split by the rivalry.  c) French missionaries and gunboats arrive from the 17th century; Gia Long (1802–1820) unifies Vietnam with French help, yet by 1885 France rules it all — and works its rubber plantations with hundreds of thousands of labourers.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Qing China (1642–1912), rich and self-satisfied, is forced open by foreign powers, while Japan studies the same West and modernises instead of submitting.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
