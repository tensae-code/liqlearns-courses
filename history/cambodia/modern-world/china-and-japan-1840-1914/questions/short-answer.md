# China and Japan, 1840–1914 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 2, China and Japan (1840–1914)”?
**Answer:** Qing China (1642–1912), rich and self-satisfied, is forced open by foreign powers, while Japan studies the same West and modernises instead of submitting. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 2, China and Japan (1840–1914)” end?
**Answer:** Qing China (1642–1912), rich and self-satisfied, is forced open by foreign powers, while Japan studies the same West and modernises instead of submitting. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
