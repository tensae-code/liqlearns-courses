# China and Japan, 1840–1914 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Qing China (1642–1912), rich and self-satisfied, is forced open by foreign powers, while Japan studies the same West and modernises instead of submitting.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 2, China and Japan (1840–1914)” is mainly about this: The Ottoman collapse leaves a Turkish republic (from 1919) that seeks Europe's door for seventy years; Iran's oil and revolution make it the region's other pivot.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Qing China (1642–1912), rich and self-satisfied, is forced open by foreign powers, while Japan studies the same West and modernises instead of submitting.
