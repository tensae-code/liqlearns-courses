# China and Japan, 1840–1914

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **China and Japan, 1840–1914** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Qing China (1642–1912), rich and self-satisfied, is forced open by foreign powers, while Japan studies the same West and modernises instead of submitting. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
