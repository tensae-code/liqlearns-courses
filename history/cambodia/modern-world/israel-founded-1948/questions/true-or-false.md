# Israel: Founded, 1948 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1947 Britain hands Palestine to the UN; the land is partitioned, Israel is proclaimed, and the Arab-Israeli wars begin — a small state of eight million at the centre of a long conflict.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 3 Lesson 5, Israel” is mainly about this: In 1206 the steppe clans unite under Genghis Khan (illustration, p. 191); within a generation his horsemen hold the largest land empire ever — the storm that also broke over Champa and Dai Viet.
