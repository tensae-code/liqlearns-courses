**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 3 Lesson 5, Israel”?
a) In 1947 Britain hands Palestine to the UN; the land is partitioned, Israel is proclaimed, and the Arab-Israeli wars begin — a small state of eight million at the centre of a long conflict.  b) Tyre, Byblos and Sidon (map, p. 94) grow rich on purple dye and cedar, and their traders carry a simple alphabet to every shore.  c) Exam questions reach back to Angkor Wat's builder and to Jayavarman VII, the king who raised hospitals, roads and the Bayon — the medieval empire the modern state claims as its root.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
