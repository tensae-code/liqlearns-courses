# Israel: Founded, 1948 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In 1947 Britain hands Palestine to the UN; the land is partitioned, Israel is proclaimed, and the Arab-Israeli wars begin — a small state of eight million at the centre of a long conflict. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
