# Israel: Founded, 1948

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Israel: Founded, 1948** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

In 1947 Britain hands Palestine to the UN; the land is partitioned, Israel is proclaimed, and the Arab-Israeli wars begin — a small state of eight million at the centre of a long conflict. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
