# The First World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The great powers arm and ally — Germany, Austria-Hungary and Italy against the rest — colonial rivalry sharpens, the Ottoman Empire decays, and the Balkans become the powder keg.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 1 Lesson 1, The First World War (1914–1918)” is mainly about this: Pressed by Siam and Vietnam, Cambodia accepts French protection in 1863; France shields the territory and rebuilds the state, yet rules it — until Sihanouk's crusade wins full independence in 1953.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Twenty great powers armed with colonial empires race for colonies and weapons until the alliance blocs drag the world into war.
