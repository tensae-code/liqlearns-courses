# The First World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 7, Europe before the First World War”?
**Answer:** The great powers arm and ally — Germany, Austria-Hungary and Italy against the rest — colonial rivalry sharpens, the Ottoman Empire decays, and the Balkans become the powder keg. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 1, The First World War (1914–1918)” end?
**Answer:** Twenty great powers armed with colonial empires race for colonies and weapons until the alliance blocs drag the world into war. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
