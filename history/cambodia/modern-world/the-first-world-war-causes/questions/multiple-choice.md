# The First World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 7, Europe before the First World War”?
a) The great powers arm and ally — Germany, Austria-Hungary and Italy against the rest — colonial rivalry sharpens, the Ottoman Empire decays, and the Balkans become the powder keg.  b) A word born in the 1960s takes off in the 1980s as transport and communication costs fall, finance and technology circle the planet, and bodies like the WTO bind national economies together.  c) A continent-country of 7,686,850 square kilometres in the south-west Pacific, 15,000 kilometres from Britain, mapped state by state as the closing survey of the region.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The great powers arm and ally — Germany, Austria-Hungary and Italy against the rest — colonial rivalry sharpens, the Ottoman Empire decays, and the Balkans become the powder keg.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 1, The First World War (1914–1918)”?
a) Twenty great powers armed with colonial empires race for colonies and weapons until the alliance blocs drag the world into war.  b) Srei Sauriyopear moves the court to Oudong (1604); Chey Chettha II marries a Vietnamese princess and opens Prey Nokor to Vietnamese settlement (1620) — two centuries of shrinking land and civil war end at the French protectorate.  c) Baghdad's land between two seas; nine states and a population growing from 66.2 million (1937) to 75.5 million (1960) — the region Cambodia's final history chapter maps before its empires.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Twenty great powers armed with colonial empires race for colonies and weapons until the alliance blocs drag the world into war.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
