# Globalisation: How It Grew — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 1 Lesson 12, The evolution of globalisation”?
**Answer:** A word born in the 1960s takes off in the 1980s as transport and communication costs fall, finance and technology circle the planet, and bodies like the WTO bind national economies together. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 1 Lesson 12, The evolution of globalisation” end?
**Answer:** A word born in the 1960s takes off in the 1980s as transport and communication costs fall, finance and technology circle the planet, and bodies like the WTO bind national economies together. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
