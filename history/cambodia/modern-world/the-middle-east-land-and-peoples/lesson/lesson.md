# The Middle East: Land and Peoples

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Middle East: Land and Peoples** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Baghdad's land between two seas; nine states and a population growing from 66.2 million (1937) to 75.5 million (1960) — the region Cambodia's final history chapter maps before its empires. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
