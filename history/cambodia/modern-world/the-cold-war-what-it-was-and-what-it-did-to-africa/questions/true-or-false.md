# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Soviet and American blocs race in arms, ideology and technology while the UN is blocked and new nations are split by the rivalry.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 1 Lesson 6, The Soviet Union (1945–1991)” is mainly about this: A herding people from Mesopotamia settles Palestine between the Jordan river and the Dead Sea and keeps one God by covenant.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The USSR, which lost some 25 million people, answers Truman's containment doctrine (12 March 1947) and the Marshall Plan by building its own bloc — until the bloc and the union dissolve in 1991.
