# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Section, Regional and World History, Cold War question

The Soviet and American blocs race in arms, ideology and technology while the UN is blocked and new nations are split by the rivalry. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 1 Lesson 6, The Soviet Union (1945–1991)

The USSR, which lost some 25 million people, answers Truman's containment doctrine (12 March 1947) and the Marshall Plan by building its own bloc — until the bloc and the union dissolve in 1991. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
