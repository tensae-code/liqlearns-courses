# Cambodia: The Second Kingdom, 1993–Today — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Lesson 6, the Second Kingdom of Cambodia (1993–today)”?
**Answer:** The 1993 elections restore the monarchy; Hun Sen's win-win policy ends the war, and Preah Vihear's World Heritage listing becomes a national triumph. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Lesson 6, the Second Kingdom of Cambodia (1993–today)” end?
**Answer:** The 1993 elections restore the monarchy; Hun Sen's win-win policy ends the war, and Preah Vihear's World Heritage listing becomes a national triumph. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
