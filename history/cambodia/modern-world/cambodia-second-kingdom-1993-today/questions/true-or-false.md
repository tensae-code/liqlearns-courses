# Cambodia: The Second Kingdom, 1993–Today — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1993 elections restore the monarchy; Hun Sen's win-win policy ends the war, and Preah Vihear's World Heritage listing becomes a national triumph.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Lesson 6, the Second Kingdom of Cambodia (1993–today)” is mainly about this: Srei Sauriyopear moves the court to Oudong (1604); Chey Chettha II marries a Vietnamese princess and opens Prey Nokor to Vietnamese settlement (1620) — two centuries of shrinking land and civil war end at the French protectorate.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The 1993 elections restore the monarchy; Hun Sen's win-win policy ends the war, and Preah Vihear's World Heritage listing becomes a national triumph.
