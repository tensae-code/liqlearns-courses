# Nineteenth-Century Ideologies: Liberalism and Socialism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 2, The ideologies of the 19th century”?
**Answer:** Locke and Montesquieu (portrait, p. 114) arm liberalism; conservatism answers; socialism and Proudhon's anarchism (portrait, p. 119) speak for the workers the factories made. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 2, The ideologies of the 19th century” end?
**Answer:** Locke and Montesquieu (portrait, p. 114) arm liberalism; conservatism answers; socialism and Proudhon's anarchism (portrait, p. 119) speak for the workers the factories made.
