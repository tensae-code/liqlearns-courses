# Video script — Nineteenth-Century Ideologies: Liberalism and Socialism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Locke and Montesquieu (portrait, p. 114) arm liberalism; conservatism answers; socialism and Proudhon's anarchism (portrait, p. 119) speak for the workers the factories made. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Cambodia.
