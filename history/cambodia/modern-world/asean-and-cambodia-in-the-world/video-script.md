# Video script — ASEAN and Cambodia in the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Cambodia sits inside ASEAN's ten nations and a globalising world of food insecurity, migration and terrorism; the EU is held up as the model of regional union. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
Five nations found ASEAN at Bangkok on 8 August 1967; Brunei (1984), Vietnam (1995), Laos and Myanmar (1997) follow, and Cambodia completes the ten on 30 April 1999. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Cambodia.
