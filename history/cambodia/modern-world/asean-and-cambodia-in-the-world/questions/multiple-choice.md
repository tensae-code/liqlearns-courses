# ASEAN and Cambodia in the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Section, Regional and World History”?
a) Cambodia sits inside ASEAN's ten nations and a globalising world of food insecurity, migration and terrorism; the EU is held up as the model of regional union.  b) Far across the ocean the Maya raise calendar-cities in the forest (map, p. 110), flourishing from the 3rd to the 9th century CE on maize farming.  c) How Angkorian society was named, ranked and fed; its inscriptions (Sdok Kok Thom, photo p. 160) and the Khmer script's growth from Pallava letters — the civilisation read through its own stones.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Cambodia sits inside ASEAN's ten nations and a globalising world of food insecurity, migration and terrorism; the EU is held up as the model of regional union.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
