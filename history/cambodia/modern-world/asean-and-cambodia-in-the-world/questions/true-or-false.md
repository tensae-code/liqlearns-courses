# ASEAN and Cambodia in the World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cambodia sits inside ASEAN's ten nations and a globalising world of food insecurity, migration and terrorism; the EU is held up as the model of regional union.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 1 Lesson 5, ASEAN” is mainly about this: The Lac Viet of the Van Lang legend throw off a thousand years of Chinese rule in 938 and build Dai Viet along the Red River — a state 1,650 km long that Angkor must reckon with.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Five nations found ASEAN at Bangkok on 8 August 1967; Brunei (1984), Vietnam (1995), Laos and Myanmar (1997) follow, and Cambodia completes the ten on 30 April 1999.
