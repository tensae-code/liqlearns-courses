# ASEAN and Cambodia in the World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Section, Regional and World History”?
**Answer:** Cambodia sits inside ASEAN's ten nations and a globalising world of food insecurity, migration and terrorism; the EU is held up as the model of regional union. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 5, ASEAN” end?
**Answer:** Five nations found ASEAN at Bangkok on 8 August 1967; Brunei (1984), Vietnam (1995), Laos and Myanmar (1997) follow, and Cambodia completes the ten on 30 April 1999. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
