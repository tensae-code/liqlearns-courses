# Czechoslovakia, 1918–1993 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Masaryk's republic is born from the First World War (1918), governed with Beneš, dismembered at Munich in 1938, and remade after 1945 as a people's democracy of 15 million. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
