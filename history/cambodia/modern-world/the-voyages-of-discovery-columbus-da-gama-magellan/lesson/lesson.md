# The Voyages of Discovery: Columbus, Da Gama, Magellan

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Voyages of Discovery: Columbus, Da Gama, Magellan** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Henry the Navigator's school sends Dias around the Cape (1488) and Vasco da Gama (portrait, p. 80) to Calicut in 1498 — the sea road to India changes the world's trade. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
