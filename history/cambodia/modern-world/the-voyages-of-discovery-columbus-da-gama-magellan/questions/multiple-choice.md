# The Voyages of Discovery: Columbus, Da Gama, Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 2, The explorations of the Europeans”?
a) Henry the Navigator's school sends Dias around the Cape (1488) and Vasco da Gama (portrait, p. 80) to Calicut in 1498 — the sea road to India changes the world's trade.  b) Sun Yat-sen's reorganised Kuomintang and the Communists (party founded 1921) move from alliance to civil war, while militarist Japan presses on the mainland.  c) The Soviet and American blocs race in arms, ideology and technology while the UN is blocked and new nations are split by the rivalry.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Henry the Navigator's school sends Dias around the Cape (1488) and Vasco da Gama (portrait, p. 80) to Calicut in 1498 — the sea road to India changes the world's trade.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
