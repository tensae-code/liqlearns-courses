# Cambodia: The Khmer Republic and Civil War, 1970–1975 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The 1970 coup removes Sihanouk, the republic is proclaimed, and five years of civil war tear the country apart.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Khmer Republic (1970–1975)” is mainly about this: Jayavarman VIII's long reign strains the empire; Cham wars (1262) and Mongol-Yuan pressure (embassies of the 1280s) close in — the causes of decline are weighed one by one.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The March 1970 coup removes Sihanouk; the republic fights a losing civil war — the lesson opens on the funeral of its soldiers (photo, 23 March 1973).
