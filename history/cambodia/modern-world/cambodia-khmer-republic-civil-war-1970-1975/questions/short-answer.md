# Cambodia: The Khmer Republic and Civil War, 1970–1975 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 3, the Khmer Republic (1970–1975)”?
**Answer:** The 1970 coup removes Sihanouk, the republic is proclaimed, and five years of civil war tear the country apart. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Khmer Republic (1970–1975)” end?
**Answer:** The March 1970 coup removes Sihanouk; the republic fights a losing civil war — the lesson opens on the funeral of its soldiers (photo, 23 March 1973). Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
