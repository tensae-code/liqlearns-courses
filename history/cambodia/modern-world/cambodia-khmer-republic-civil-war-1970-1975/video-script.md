# Video script — Cambodia: The Khmer Republic and Civil War, 1970–1975

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The 1970 coup removes Sihanouk, the republic is proclaimed, and five years of civil war tear the country apart. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
The March 1970 coup removes Sihanouk; the republic fights a losing civil war — the lesson opens on the funeral of its soldiers (photo, 23 March 1973). Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
Crisis builds through Samlaut (1967) and a 1,500-million-riel deficit (1969); Lon Nol's "national salvation government" (August 1969) precedes the 18 March 1970 vote that removes Sihanouk while he is in Moscow and Beijing. Hold the place in your mind as the story moves. The turn in the middle is the heart of it: before the turn things stand one way, after it they stand another, and the people in the story must live in the new way.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Cambodia.
