# About the History — Cambodia course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Cambodia's own Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS) books. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Cambodia inside the History folder (one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **six levels**, following the story as Cambodia's own books present it (see CURRICULUM.md for the derivation): Level 1 — first steps: heritage, evidence and prehistory (5 lessons); Level 2 — the ancient world and Cambodia's first kingdoms (17 lessons); Level 3 — Angkor's civilisation, its neighbours and the capitals after Angkor (12 lessons); Level 4 — the wider world opens: voyages, revolutions and empires to 1914 (16 lessons); Level 5 — the world wars and the world between them (10 lessons); Level 6 — the world after 1945 and Cambodia's own modern story (31 lessons).

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *The arc whole:* "First, what history is and who digs it up — and heritage itself, in the festival stories, old buildings and instruments a child learns to keep. Then the ancient world whole, and at home Nokor Phnom and Chenla rising toward Angkor, the temple empire whose kings, reservoirs and inscriptions the books read stone by stone. Then the neighbours — Champa, Dai Viet, Ayutthaya, Lan Xang — and the capitals after Angkor: Chaktomuk, Longvek, Oudong. Then the wider world: the sea voyages, the revolutions, steam, and the empires that also held Cambodia. Then the two world wars. And last the world after 1945, and Cambodia's own story told regime by regime — protectorate, independence in 1953, the Sangkum, the republic and civil war, Democratic Kampuchea, rebuilding and peace, and the Second Kingdom."
