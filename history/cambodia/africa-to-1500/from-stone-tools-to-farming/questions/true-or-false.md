# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cave-dwellers hunt, gather and fish with stone tools; then they settle, farm, keep animals and honour the neak ta spirits — the turn from wandering to village life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §1, How prehistoric people lived” is mainly about this: Louis XVI summons the Estates-General to Versailles (5 May 1789); the Third Estate declares itself the National Assembly (17 June) and the old order begins to fall.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Cave-dwellers hunt, gather and fish with stone tools; then they settle, farm, keep animals and honour the neak ta spirits — the turn from wandering to village life.
