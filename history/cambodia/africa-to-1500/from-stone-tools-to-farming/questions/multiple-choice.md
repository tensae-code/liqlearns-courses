**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §1, How prehistoric people lived”?
a) Cave-dwellers hunt, gather and fish with stone tools; then they settle, farm, keep animals and honour the neak ta spirits — the turn from wandering to village life.  b) The Cham raise a chain of coastal kingdoms — Simhapura, Indrapura, Vijaya, Kauthara, Panduranga — from 192 CE, sending embassies to China (336, 420 CE) and fighting Angkor and Dai Viet for a thousand years.  c) Henry the Navigator's school sends Dias around the Cape (1488) and Vasco da Gama (portrait, p. 80) to Calicut in 1498 — the sea road to India changes the world's trade.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
