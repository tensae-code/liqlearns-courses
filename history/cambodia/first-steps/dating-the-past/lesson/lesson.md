# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Dating the Past** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Children lay the kings end to end — Hun Tien in the 1st century, the Hun Pan line (225–243), the Srei line (430–484), Kaundinya Jayavarman (484–514) who raised Hinduism high — six centuries on one arrow. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
