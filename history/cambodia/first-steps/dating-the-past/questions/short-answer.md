# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §3, Timeline of Nokor Phnom's kings”?
**Answer:** Children lay the kings end to end — Hun Tien in the 1st century, the Hun Pan line (225–243), the Srei line (430–484), Kaundinya Jayavarman (484–514) who raised Hinduism high — six centuries on one arrow. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §3, Timeline of Nokor Phnom's kings” end?
**Answer:** Children lay the kings end to end — Hun Tien in the 1st century, the Hun Pan line (225–243), the Srei line (430–484), Kaundinya Jayavarman (484–514) who raised Hinduism high — six centuries on one arrow. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
