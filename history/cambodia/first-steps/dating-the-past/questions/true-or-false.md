# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Children lay the kings end to end — Hun Tien in the 1st century, the Hun Pan line (225–243), the Srei line (430–484), Kaundinya Jayavarman (484–514) who raised Hinduism high — six centuries on one arrow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §3, Timeline of Nokor Phnom's kings” is mainly about this: England (map, p. 86) cuts off its king's head, tries Cromwell's republic (to 1658), and learns to bind its monarchs by law.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Children lay the kings end to end — Hun Tien in the 1st century, the Hun Pan line (225–243), the Srei line (430–484), Kaundinya Jayavarman (484–514) who raised Hinduism high — six centuries on one arrow.
