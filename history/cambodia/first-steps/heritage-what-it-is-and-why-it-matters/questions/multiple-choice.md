# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Appendix story 2, History of the Om Touk festival”?
a) A grandfather tells Nari and Bonna how the boat-race festival began, so children inherit the story behind the races, the moon salutation and the flattened-rice offering.  b) The Lac Viet of the Van Lang legend throw off a thousand years of Chinese rule in 938 and build Dai Viet along the Red River — a state 1,650 km long that Angkor must reckon with.  c) England (map, p. 86) cuts off its king's head, tries Cromwell's republic (to 1658), and learns to bind its monarchs by law.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A grandfather tells Nari and Bonna how the boat-race festival began, so children inherit the story behind the races, the moon salutation and the flattened-rice offering.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
