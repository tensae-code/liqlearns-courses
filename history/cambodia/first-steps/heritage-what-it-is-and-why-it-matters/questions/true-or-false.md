# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A grandfather tells Nari and Bonna how the boat-race festival began, so children inherit the story behind the races, the moon salutation and the flattened-rice offering.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Ch. 4 Lesson 1, Old buildings” is mainly about this: Thirteen English colonies of Arawak, Sioux and Apache lands (photo: a Native American woman) declare independence and invent a republic across the Atlantic.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The old National Assembly hall (opened 1920), the National Museum (built 1917) and high wooden stilt houses survive as evidence; children learn to read a building's age and to draw the stilt house their ancestors built.
