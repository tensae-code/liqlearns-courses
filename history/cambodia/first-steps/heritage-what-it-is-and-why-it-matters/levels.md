# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Appendix story 2, History of the Om Touk festival
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A grandfather tells Nari and Bonna how the boat-race festival began, so children inherit the story behind the races, the moon salutation and the flattened-rice offering. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 4 Lesson 1, Old buildings
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The old National Assembly hall (opened 1920), the National Museum (built 1917) and high wooden stilt houses survive as evidence; children learn to read a building's age and to draw the stilt house their ancestors built. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch. 4 Lesson 1, Models of ancient Khmer ornament
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Three mother-patterns — bodhi leaf, swan's tail, snail spiral — grow by rule into the carved borders (kbach) on temples, pagodas and silk; children learn the geometry step by step and draw it themselves. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Ch. 4 Lesson 2, Preserving traditional musical instruments
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The mohori and pinpeat orchestras — roneat xylophones, tro fiddles, khim — survive only if each generation keeps, repairs and plays them. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Ch. 4 Lesson 3, Old buildings
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Phnom Penh's Royal Palace, first built by Ponhea Yat in 1432 and rebuilt by Norodom in 1866 — eleven buildings inside walls 465 by 402 metres — is a national treasure children must know and protect. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Ch. 4 Lesson 1, Traditional musical instruments
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: From temple-wall carvings to today's yike theatre (photo: drums, tro fiddle, sralai oboe), Khmer instruments — skor drums, roneat, tro, khim — carry the sound of the ancestors and must be learned to be kept. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
