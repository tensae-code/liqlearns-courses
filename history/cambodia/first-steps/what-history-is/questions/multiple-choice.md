# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “History Ch. 1 Lesson 1, Foundations of history”?
a) History begins with evidence — broken pots, inscriptions (photo: a Khmer inscription stone), things handed down by word of mouth — and divides into prehistory and history.  b) The First Emperor walls the north (215–214 BCE, photo p. 183); the Han dynasty that follows gives China its lasting name, script and bronze art.  c) Srei Sauriyopear moves the court to Oudong (1604); Chey Chettha II marries a Vietnamese princess and opens Prey Nokor to Vietnamese settlement (1620) — two centuries of shrinking land and civil war end at the French protectorate.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) History begins with evidence — broken pots, inscriptions (photo: a Khmer inscription stone), things handed down by word of mouth — and divides into prehistory and history.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
