# Who Studies Prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §1, Cambodia's prehistory and its sites”?
a) Before any writing, people left stone axes, pottery and bronze at Samrong Sen, Mlu Prei, Anlong Phdao and the Kampot caves — the dug-up things are the only witnesses.  b) In 1206 the steppe clans unite under Genghis Khan (illustration, p. 191); within a generation his horsemen hold the largest land empire ever — the storm that also broke over Champa and Dai Viet.  c) After 1431 the court still governs by appointed officials, laws are written as verse codes (kram and chbab) to be memorised, and Theravada Buddhism reshapes literature and daily life.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Before any writing, people left stone axes, pottery and bronze at Samrong Sen, Mlu Prei, Anlong Phdao and the Kampot caves — the dug-up things are the only witnesses.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
