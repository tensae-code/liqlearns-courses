# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §1, Cambodia's prehistory and its sites”?
**Answer:** Before any writing, people left stone axes, pottery and bronze at Samrong Sen, Mlu Prei, Anlong Phdao and the Kampot caves — the dug-up things are the only witnesses. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “History Ch. 2 Lesson 1, Prehistoric Cambodia” end?
**Answer:** People have lived on this land since deep prehistory; Khmer origins are traced through Southeast Asia's caves and early villages toward the first kingdoms. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
