# Who Studies Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Before any writing, people left stone axes, pottery and bronze at Samrong Sen, Mlu Prei, Anlong Phdao and the Kampot caves — the dug-up things are the only witnesses.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “History Ch. 2 Lesson 1, Prehistoric Cambodia” is mainly about this: Henry the Navigator's school sends Dias around the Cape (1488) and Vasco da Gama (portrait, p. 80) to Calicut in 1498 — the sea road to India changes the world's trade.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: People have lived on this land since deep prehistory; Khmer origins are traced through Southeast Asia's caves and early villages toward the first kingdoms.
