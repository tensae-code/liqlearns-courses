# History — Cambodia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Cambodia's own — built from Cambodia's MoEYS Social Studies and History books (Khmer). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': first steps and the deep past, the ancient world and Cambodia's first kingdoms, Angkor and the capitals after it, the wider world to 1914, the world wars, and the world after 1945 closing with Cambodia's own regimes one by one. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
