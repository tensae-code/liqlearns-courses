# History — Micronesia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is
Micronesia's own — built from the Federated States of
Micronesia's own books: *Over Seas and Time* (Grade 9),
which tells one national story island by island in strict
time order; *Micronesian Government* (Grade 11), which
retells the same centuries as a story of leadership; and
the three Pohnpei Studies history readers —
Kapingamarangi, Mwoakilloa and Nukuoro — each atoll
telling its own share in its own language. Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below sit inside the levels, in path order — what
the learner proves, step by step. The grade each book
comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': evidence and the old
order first, then the voyagers, then contact, missions
and trade, then the four foreign rulers in turn — Spain,
Germany, Japan, America — and the road out of foreign
rule to the independent FSM. That ladder IS the path
order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).
