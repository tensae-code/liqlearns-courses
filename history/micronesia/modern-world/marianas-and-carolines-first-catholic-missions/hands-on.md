# Hands-on — The Marianas and Carolines: First Catholic Missions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A guest arrives in your village bringing a new teaching. At first there is welcome. Then the guest's guards begin giving orders. Trace, before you read, the exact point where welcome could turn. Then read how the first Catholic missions in the Marianas walked that line.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the lesson's two scenes side by side: the welcome — priest and islanders meeting at the shore — and the turn — soldiers standing behind the mission. Under each, write one line on what changed. Then walk to a church or mission site near you, note its founding date from any plaque or elder, and add it as a third scene in your drawing's timeline.
