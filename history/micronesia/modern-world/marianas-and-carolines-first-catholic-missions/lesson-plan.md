# Lesson plan — The Marianas and Carolines: First Catholic Missions

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Marianas and Carolines: First Catholic Missions
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The first Catholic missions planted the new faith in Spain's Pacific: Fr. Sanvitores founded the mission on Guam, the Ladrones were renamed the Marianas, Spanish soldiers turned welcome into conflict — and a generation later Fr. Cantova carried word of the faith onward into the Carolines.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
