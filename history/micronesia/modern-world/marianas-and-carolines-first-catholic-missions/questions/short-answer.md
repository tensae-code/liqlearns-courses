# The Marianas and Carolines: First Catholic Missions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did welcome turn to conflict on Guam?
**Answer:** Spanish soldiers stood behind the mission; islanders who had received the priests resisted the power that arrived with them.

**2.** Where did the mission story travel after the Marianas?
**Answer:** South into the Carolines, carried by Fr. Cantova toward Micronesia's own islands.
