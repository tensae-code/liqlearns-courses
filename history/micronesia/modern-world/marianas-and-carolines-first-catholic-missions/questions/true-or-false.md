# The Marianas and Carolines: First Catholic Missions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Spanish soldiers stood behind the Guam mission, and welcome turned into conflict.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Fr. Cantova carried word of the faith into the Carolines before Sanvitores reached Guam.
**Answer: False — Sanvitores's Guam mission came first; Cantova carried the faith into the Carolines a generation later.**
