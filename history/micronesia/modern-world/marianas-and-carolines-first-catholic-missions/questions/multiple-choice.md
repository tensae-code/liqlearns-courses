# The Marianas and Carolines: First Catholic Missions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who planted the first Catholic mission on Guam?
a) Fr. Cantova  b) Fr. Sanvitores  c) Dioga da Rocha  d) Reverend Doane
**Answer: b) Fr. Sanvitores** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were the Marianas called before the renaming?
a) the Carolines  b) the Ladrones  c) the Marshalls  d) the Mortlocks
**Answer: b) the Ladrones** — That is what this lesson teaches.
