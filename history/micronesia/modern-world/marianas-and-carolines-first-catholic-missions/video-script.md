# Video script — The Marianas and Carolines: First Catholic Missions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The first Catholic mission in Spain's Pacific begins with welcome — and the soldiers behind it turn welcome into conflict.

Fr. Sanvitores plants the mission on Guam. The islands called the Ladrones take a new name: the Marianas. A faith has arrived, and it means to stay.

But look behind the priests. Spanish soldiers stand there. The islanders who welcomed the teaching resist the power that comes with it, and Guam's mission years become struggle years.

A generation later, Fr. Cantova carries word of the faith south, into the Carolines — the mission story moving toward Micronesia's own islands. Remember it this way: first the mission, then the renaming, then the conflict — and the faith travelling on. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
