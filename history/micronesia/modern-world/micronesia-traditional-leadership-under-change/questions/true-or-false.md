# Micronesia's Traditional Leadership Under Change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Traditional leadership stayed exactly the same through the foreign centuries.
**Answer: False — warfare's end, population collapse, churches and land reforms moved its grounds.**

**2.** Nanpei's case shows a chiefly line continuing on new grounds of authority.
**Answer: True**
