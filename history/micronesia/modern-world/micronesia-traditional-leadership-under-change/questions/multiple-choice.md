# Micronesia's Traditional Leadership Under Change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From what, besides his Nahnken father, did Henry Nanpei's power come?
a) a Spanish title  b) church office and reform-granted land  c) a German salary alone  d) the kogakko schools
**Answer: b) church office and reform-granted land** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which foreign change redrew who held the land?
a) the China trade  b) German land reforms  c) the galleon road  d) the copra stores
