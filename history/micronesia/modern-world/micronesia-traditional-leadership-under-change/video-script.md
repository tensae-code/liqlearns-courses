# Video script — Micronesia's Traditional Leadership Under Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Tradition is not a statue. Under the foreign centuries, even the chiefs' ground moved.

Warfare ends. Populations collapse. Churches rise. German land reforms redraw the land itself. Every footing of the old authority shifts at once.

So meet Henry Nanpei: a Nahnken's son, yes — but his power stands also on church office and on land the reforms granted him. Descent, church, deed: the house rebuilt while the family lives in it.

Remember it this way: the line continued — on new ground. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
