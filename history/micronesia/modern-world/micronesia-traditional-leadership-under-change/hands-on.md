# Hands-on — Micronesia's Traditional Leadership Under Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A chief's son becomes a leader — but not only because of his father. His power also comes from a church office and from land a foreign reform handed him. Before you read, think: when the grounds of authority change, is the tradition still itself? Then read how Micronesia's government book traces leadership under change.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw one leader's authority as a house on footings: label each footing with its ground — descent, election, church, land, office, knowledge. Do it twice: for a chief in the old way, and for a leader in your community today. Then walk to an elder and check your second drawing against what they remember of the first.
