# Lesson plan — Micronesia's Traditional Leadership Under Change

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia's Traditional Leadership Under Change
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Foreign centuries changed what chiefly authority rested on: warfare ended, populations collapsed, churches and German land reforms moved power — so that a leader like Henry Nanpei held authority from church office and reform-granted land, not only from his Nahnken father: tradition itself was rebuilt on new ground.
