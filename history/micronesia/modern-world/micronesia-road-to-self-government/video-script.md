# Video script — Micronesia's Road to Self-Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Independence was not handed over. The Congress of Micronesia won it — patiently, institutionally, and in island words first.

1965, the meetinghouse: Andon Amaraich tells the octopus. Killed, its arms scattered — but its blood waited in the rafters, to come back together. Scattered Micronesia, hidden survival, returning unity: the whole argument in one story.

Then the years of pressing: the Congress negotiates with the United States, and when America stalls, appeals to the United Nations. The Marshalls and Palau choose their own ways. Four districts approve a constitution — the Federated States of Micronesia.

And the book closes with lives: the founding fathers, named and told, the way the atolls tell their chiefs. Remember it this way: the blood came back together. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
