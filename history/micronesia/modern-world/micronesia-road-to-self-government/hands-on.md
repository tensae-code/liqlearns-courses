# Hands-on — Micronesia's Road to Self-Government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
An octopus is killed and its arms scattered — but its blood, hidden in the meetinghouse rafters, waits to bring it back together. Before you read, say what such a story could teach a people about independence lost and regained. Then read the road to self-government, as the leaders who walked it told it.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and retell the octopus: draw the meetinghouse, the scattered arms, the rafters — and under the drawing, write the story's meaning in two sentences of your own. Then walk the road in person: visit your local government office or congress building if you can, and note one thing it decides that the Trust Territory once decided from far away.
