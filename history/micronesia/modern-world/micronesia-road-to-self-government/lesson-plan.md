# Lesson plan — Micronesia's Road to Self-Government

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia's Road to Self-Government
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Modern World III: The American Time and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Congress of Micronesia led the road out of the Trust Territory: Andon Amaraich's octopus told in the meetinghouse why scattered independence would come back together, the Congress pressed the United States for years and appealed to the United Nations when America stalled, the Marshalls and Palau chose their own ways — and four districts approved a constitution creating the Federated States of Micronesia, carried by the leaders the nation names its founding fathers.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 15, The Road to Self-Government** — The Congress of Micronesia leads status negotiations with the United States, the Marshalls and Palau choose to go their own way, and four districts approve a constitution creating a federation — the Federated States of Micronesia.
2. **Introduction, The Octopus in the Meetinghouse** — In 1965 Congress of Micronesia member Andon Amaraich tells the octopus story — independence was killed and scattered like the octopus's arms, but its blood waited in the rafters to come back together — to explain self-rule in island words.
3. **Chapter 10, The Congress of Micronesia and the Fight for Independence** — The young leaders of the Congress of Micronesia spend years pressing the United States — and appealing to the United Nations when America stalls — until the Trust Territory wins independence.
4. **Founding Fathers of the FSM** — Short lives of the leaders who carried the independence fight, the men the new nation names its founding fathers.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
