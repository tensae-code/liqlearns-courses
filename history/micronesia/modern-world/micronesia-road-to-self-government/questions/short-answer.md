# Micronesia's Road to Self-Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does the octopus's blood in the rafters mean in Amaraich's telling? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** That Micronesia's unity, killed and scattered by the foreign centuries, survived hidden in the people's own house — ready to come back together as self-rule.

**2.** How does the government book close the independence story? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** With short lives of the leaders who carried the fight — the men the nation names its founding fathers.
