# Micronesia's Road to Self-Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who tells the octopus story in the meetinghouse, in 1965? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Andon Amaraich, a Congress of Micronesia member  b) Fr. Sanvitores  c) the Nahnken of Kitti  d) a German district officer
**Answer: a) Andon Amaraich, a Congress of Micronesia member** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two peoples chose to go their own way instead of joining the federation? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Kosrae and Yap  b) the Marshalls and Palau  c) Chuuk and Pohnpei  d) Kapingamarangi and Nukuoro
**Answer: b) the Marshalls and Palau** — That is what this lesson teaches.
