# Micronesia's Road to Self-Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** When America stalled, the Congress of Micronesia appealed to the United Nations. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True**

**2.** All the Trust Territory's districts joined the Federated States of Micronesia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False — the Marshalls and Palau went their own way; four districts approved the constitution and formed the FSM.**
