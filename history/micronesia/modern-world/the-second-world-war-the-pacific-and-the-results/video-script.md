# Video script — The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The war made Micronesia a road, then a battlefield, then a relief line — and islanders lived all three.

First, the lifeline: Japan's empire runs on the South Seas, and islanders labour for the Japanese Navy. Then, the battlefield: American bombing, and people hiding from a war they did not choose.

At the end, American forces land — handing out food and medicine. Peace arrives as relief, with a new power on the beach.

Remember it this way: lifeline, battlefield, relief — the mandate ends, the next era begins. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
