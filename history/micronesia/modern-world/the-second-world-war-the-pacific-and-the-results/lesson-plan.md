# Lesson plan — The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the Second World War the South Seas became Japan's lifeline and then its battlefield: islanders laboured for the Japanese Navy, hid from American bombing, and at the war's end met American forces handing out food and medicine — the mandate world ending in war, and a new foreign power arriving with relief.
