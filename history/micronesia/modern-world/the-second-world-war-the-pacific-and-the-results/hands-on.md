# Hands-on — The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your islands sit on the supply road of an empire at war. First the road is busy and demanding; then the bombs come; then strangers land with food. Before you read, think what each of those three moments asks of the people who simply live there. Then read the Pacific war as Micronesia's book tells it.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the war's three moments as three panels — the labour gang, the hiding place, the relief line — with one line under each on what islanders did. Then walk to any war trace in your place — a ruin, a memorial, a story site — and add its panel from what an elder or a plaque tells you.
