# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The South Seas became Japan's lifeline before becoming a battlefield.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Islanders were untouched by the fighting because the war stayed at sea.
**Answer: False — American bombing reached the islands and islanders hid from it.**
