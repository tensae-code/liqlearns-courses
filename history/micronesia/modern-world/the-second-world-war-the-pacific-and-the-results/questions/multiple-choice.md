# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did islanders do for the Japanese war effort, as the book teaches?
a) sailed the galleons  b) laboured for the Japanese Navy  c) sat in the yes-yes councils  d) built the kogakko schools
**Answer: b) laboured for the Japanese Navy** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the war end for the islands in this telling?
a) with a treaty in Europe  b) with American forces handing out food and medicine  c) with Spain's return  d) with a typhoon
**Answer: b) with American forces handing out food and medicine** — That is what this lesson teaches.
