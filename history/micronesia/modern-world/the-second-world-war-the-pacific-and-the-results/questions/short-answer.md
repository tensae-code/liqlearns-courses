# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two roles did the South Seas play in order?
**Answer:** First Japan's lifeline — supplies, labour and position — then its battlefield.

**2.** What arrived with the American forces at the war's end?
**Answer:** Food and medicine — relief, and with it the next foreign power on the beach.
