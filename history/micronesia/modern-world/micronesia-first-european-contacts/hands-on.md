# Hands-on — Micronesia's First European Contacts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a sail on your lagoon's horizon — the first your people have ever seen. What do you do: paddle out, wait, hide, prepare food, prepare weapons? List two welcomes and two fears. Then read what the first meetings in Micronesia were really like, on the high islands and on two small atolls that kept their own registers of arrivals.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and keep a register: rule a page into lines and enter this lesson's arrivals in order — Rocha's Portuguese, Tongohiti, the Wareai traders, the 1870 Marshalls canoes at Nukuoro, the Moduroo people — with a one-line note on each. Then walk your own shore or street and ask: what is the first arrival your place remembers? Enter it as the next line, in the same form.
