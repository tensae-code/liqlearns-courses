# Micronesia's First European Contacts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Under whom did the storm-blown Portuguese reach Ulithi? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Dioga da Rocha  b) Columbus  c) Sanvitores  d) Lütke
**Answer: a) Dioga da Rocha** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which arrivals does Nukuoro's register date to 1870? (Sub lesson 3)
a) Spanish galleons  b) money and canoes from the Marshalls  c) the first missionaries  d) German officials
**Answer: b) money and canoes from the Marshalls** — That is what this lesson teaches.
