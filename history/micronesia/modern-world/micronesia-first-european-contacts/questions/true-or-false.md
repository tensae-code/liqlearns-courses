# Micronesia's First European Contacts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** First contact in Micronesia happened on one day, everywhere at once. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False — it came island by island over centuries, each meeting decided on its own beach.**

**2.** Kapingamarangi's reader remembers its first outsiders one by one, by name. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
