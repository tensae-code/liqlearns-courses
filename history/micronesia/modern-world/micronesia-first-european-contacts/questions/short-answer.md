# Micronesia's First European Contacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did some welcomes turn hostile? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ships demanded provisions — in Chuuk Lagoon and at Pulap the meetings turned hostile as crews demanded food and supplies.

**2.** What does Nukuoro's register show about contact? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** That contact was older and wider than Europe: most logged arrivals came by canoe from other islands, and the European sightings of 1806 and 1840 are two entries among many.
