# Video script — World War Two on Micronesia's Islands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. On the islands, the war never fully left — it stayed in stories and in iron.

Ask the elders: they carry the labour, the bombing, the hiding, the relief, in told memory. Walk the points and mountains: rusted cannons, bunkers, the empire's iron litter, still in the ground.

And the book's last war assignment is yours: research how your own island remembers the war's end. Gather it before the gatherers are gone.

Remember it this way: the memory is the monument. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
