# World War Two on Micronesia's Islands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why are elders' stories evidence in this lesson?
**Answer:** They carry the war as the island lived it — labour, bombing, hiding, relief — and survive only while someone gathers them, like the settlement legends.

**2.** What war traces does the lesson name in the ground?
**Answer:** Rusted cannons and mountain bunkers — the iron litter of the empire's last stand.
