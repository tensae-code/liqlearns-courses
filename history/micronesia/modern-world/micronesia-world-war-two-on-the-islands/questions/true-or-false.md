# World War Two on Micronesia's Islands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war on the islands is kept alive only in written documents.
**Answer: False — it lives in elders' told stories and in the rusted cannons and bunkers on the ground.**

**2.** Learners are asked to research their own island's memory of the war.
**Answer: True**
