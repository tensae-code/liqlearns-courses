# World War Two on Micronesia's Islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what two forms does the war stay alive on the islands?
a) school exams and flags  b) elders' stories, and rusted cannons and mountain bunkers  c) songs and copra prices  d) treaties and maps
**Answer: b) elders' stories, and rusted cannons and mountain bunkers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the book send learners to research?
a) how ships are built  b) how their own island remembers the war's end  c) the price of phosphate  d) star paths
**Answer: b) how their own island remembers the war's end** — That is what this lesson teaches.
