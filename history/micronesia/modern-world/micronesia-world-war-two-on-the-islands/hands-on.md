# Hands-on — World War Two on Micronesia's Islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk past an old ruin in your place — a gun, a bunker, a scarred wall. Someone near you can tell its story. Before you read, think: what is the difference between the war in a history book and the war in an elder's memory of the same beach? Then read how Micronesia's book teaches the war that stayed behind.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Do the book's own fieldwork: take paper and a pencil, walk to one war trace in your place — a bunker, a gun, a memorial, a scarred building — and sketch it with its measurements paced out. Then interview one elder or keeper of the site, write down their account in their words, and file the two together: the iron and the story, one evidence page.
