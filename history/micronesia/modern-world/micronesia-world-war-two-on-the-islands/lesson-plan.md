# Lesson plan — World War Two on Micronesia's Islands

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** World War Two on Micronesia's Islands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The war stays on the islands after it ends: in elders' stories, in rusted cannons and mountain bunkers, the war in island memory remains alive — and the book sends learners out to research how their own island remembers its end.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
