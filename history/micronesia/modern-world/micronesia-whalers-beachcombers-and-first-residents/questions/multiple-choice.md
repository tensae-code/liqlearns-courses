# Micronesia's Whalers, Beachcombers and First Residents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What were deserting sailors who stayed ashore called?
a) beachcombers  b) nahnmwarki  c) beachcombers' chiefs  d) galleon men
**Answer: a) beachcombers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What quiet cost cut island populations in the whaling age?
a) drought  b) disease — the pox  c) earthquakes  d) copra prices
**Answer: b) disease — the pox** — That is what this lesson teaches.
