# Micronesia's Whalers, Beachcombers and First Residents — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two trades crowded the eastern Carolines?
**Answer:** Whaling, and the beche-de-mer trade — gathering the sea slug for the China market.

**2.** What happened to the crews of the Waverly and the Harriet?
**Answer:** They were massacred — meetings of worlds ending in killing, among the age's costs to both sides.
