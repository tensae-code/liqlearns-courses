# Micronesia's Whalers, Beachcombers and First Residents — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Beachcombers lived under the chiefs, inside island society.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The whaling age brought trade and no losses to the islands.
**Answer: False — the Waverly and Harriet crews were massacred, and disease — the pox — cut island populations.**
