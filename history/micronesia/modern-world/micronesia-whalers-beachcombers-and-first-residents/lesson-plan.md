# Lesson plan — Micronesia's Whalers, Beachcombers and First Residents

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia's Whalers, Beachcombers and First Residents
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the whaling age the eastern Carolines filled with foreign crews: whalers and beche-de-mer traders crowded the anchorages, deserting sailors became beachcombers living under chiefs — and the age's costs landed on the islands, in the massacres of the Waverly and Harriet crews and in disease, the pox, cutting island populations.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
