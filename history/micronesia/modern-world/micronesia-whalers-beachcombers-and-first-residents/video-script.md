# Video script — Micronesia's Whalers, Beachcombers and First Residents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The whaling age crowded Micronesia's anchorages — and the islands paid for the crowd.

Whalers come for water and food. Beche-de-mer traders come for the sea slug and the China market. And some sailors never leave: deserters become beachcombers, living ashore under the chiefs, on island terms.

But hold the whole picture. The crews of the Waverly and the Harriet are massacred. And the ships bring the pox — disease that cuts island populations, emptying villages the ships only visited.

Remember it this way: the first residents stayed — and so did the losses. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
