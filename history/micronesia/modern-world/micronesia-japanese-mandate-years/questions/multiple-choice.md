# Micronesia in the Japanese Mandate Years — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Under what legal dress did Japan rule the islands after 1914? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a League of Nations Mandate  b) the Compact of Free Association  c) the Treaty of Tordesillas  d) a German lease
**Answer: a) a League of Nations Mandate** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were the councils where chiefs only agreed called? (Sub lesson 3)
a) district congresses  b) yes-yes councils  c) nets  d) municipalities
**Answer: b) yes-yes councils** — That is what this lesson teaches.
