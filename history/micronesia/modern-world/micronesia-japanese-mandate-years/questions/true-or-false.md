# Micronesia in the Japanese Mandate Years — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under the mandate, islanders became a minority in their own land. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Kogakko schools aimed island children at jobs outside the Japanese system. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — they aimed children toward civil-service and trade jobs inside the imperial system.**
