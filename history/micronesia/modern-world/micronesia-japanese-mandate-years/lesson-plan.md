# Lesson plan — Micronesia in the Japanese Mandate Years

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia in the Japanese Mandate Years
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Japan seized Germany's islands in 1914 and ruled them as a League of Nations Mandate, transforming them — towns, industries, kogakko schools and waves of Okinawan and Japanese immigrants made islanders a minority in their own land, exports tied the islands to the Empire, and chiefs, given no real place, sat in 'yes-yes councils' that only agreed — until the mandate years ran into the Pacific war in 1945.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 11, The Japanese Period of Transformation** — Japan seizes Germany's islands in 1914 and rules them as a League of Nations Mandate, building towns, industries and a cash economy that makes islanders a minority in their own land.
2. **Chapter 11, Japanese schools, immigrants and island work** — Okinawan and Japanese immigrants pour in, island children pass through kogakko schools toward civil-service and trade jobs, and exports such as katsuobushi and phosphate tie the islands to the Japanese Empire.
3. **Chapter 8, Micronesia's Japanese Occupation** — Japanese rule excites islanders with trips and new chances for young leaders, but chiefs find no real place in the system and sit in 'yes-yes councils' that only agree.
4. **Chapter 9.5, The Time of the Japanese, 1914–1945** — Japan takes over in 1914 and the mandate years run until the Pacific war reaches the islands in 1945.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
