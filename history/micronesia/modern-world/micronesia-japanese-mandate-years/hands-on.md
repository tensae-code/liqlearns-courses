# Hands-on — Micronesia in the Japanese Mandate Years

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A new ruler arrives with building plans, schools and shiploads of its own people. Within a generation, your people are a minority at home. Before you read, list what that transformation gives and what it takes. Then read the Japanese mandate years — the deepest foreign reshaping Micronesia had known.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the mandate's balance sheet: one column of buildings — town, school, industry, exports — one column of standings — who is the majority, who decides, who only agrees. Then walk your own town or school and ask the same three standing questions of it today, writing the answers beside the mandate's.
