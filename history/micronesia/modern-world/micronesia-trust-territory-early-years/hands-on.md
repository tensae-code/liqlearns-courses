# Hands-on — Micronesia's Trust Territory Early Years

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The war ends. The villages are broken, the people scattered, a new power is in charge — and this time the ruled begin building governments of their own inside the ruler's frame. Before you read, think: what is the difference between being governed and beginning to govern? Then read the Trust Territory's early years.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the ladder of the early years — municipality, magistrate's court, district congress — with one line on what each rung could decide. Then walk to your own local council or municipal office, note what it decides today, and write today's body as the ladder's next rung, with its date.
