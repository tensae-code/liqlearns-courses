# Lesson plan — Micronesia's Trust Territory Early Years

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia's Trust Territory Early Years
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Modern World III: The American Time and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the war the US Navy rebuilt the shattered islands, repatriation reshaped the villages, and in 1947 the UN Trust Territory began thirty more years of foreign rule — but a rule with a difference: islanders built municipalities, magistrates and district congresses of their own, and on Kapingamarangi the American time opens with municipalities and elected councils.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Modern World III: The American Time and Independence — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 13, Early Years of American Occupation** — The US Navy rebuilds the war-torn islands, repatriation reshapes villages, and in 1947 the UN Trust Territory begins thirty more years of foreign rule, now with municipalities, magistrates and district congresses.
2. **Chapter 9, From Municipalities to the Congress of Micronesia** — In the 1940s–60s islanders build municipalities and district congresses that must respect traditional leadership and satisfy American demands for democracy at the same time.
3. **Chapter 9.6, The Beginning of the American Time** — After Japan's defeat the Americans (Baalangi) take charge, and the atoll enters the Trust Territory world of municipalities and elected councils.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, teaching Micronesia's past as its own islands' story.
