# Video script — Micronesia's Trust Territory Early Years

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Trust Territory was still foreign rule — but islanders began governing themselves inside it.

The US Navy rebuilds the war-torn islands. Repatriation reshapes the villages. In 1947 the frame gets its name: the United Nations Trust Territory — thirty more years, American now.

And inside the frame, a ladder: municipalities, magistrates, district congresses — each body standing on two grounds, traditional leadership respected, American democracy satisfied. Kapingamarangi calls the new rulers the Baalangi and dates the Beginning of the American Time by its municipalities and elected councils.

Remember it this way: governed from outside, governing from inside — both at once. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
