# Micronesia's Trust Territory Early Years — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Trust Territory ended foreign rule in Micronesia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False — it began thirty more years of foreign rule — American rule — with island self-government growing inside it.**

**2.** Kapingamarangi's reader calls the Americans the Baalangi. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True**
