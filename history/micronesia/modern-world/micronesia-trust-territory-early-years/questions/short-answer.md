# Micronesia's Trust Territory Early Years — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two reshapings open the American occupation? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The US Navy's rebuilding of the war-torn islands, and repatriation reshaping the villages as scattered people came home.

**2.** What small machinery of self-government reaches even Kapingamarangi? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Municipalities and elected councils — the Trust Territory's forms arriving on the far atoll.
