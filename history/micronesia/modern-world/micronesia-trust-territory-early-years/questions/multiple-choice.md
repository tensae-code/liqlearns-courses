# Micronesia's Trust Territory Early Years — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did the UN Trust Territory begin? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 1914  b) 1947  c) 1965  d) 1979
**Answer: b) 1947** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What must the islanders' new municipalities and district congresses do at the same time? (Sub lesson 2)
a) copy Spain and Germany  b) respect traditional leadership and satisfy American demands for democracy  c) abolish the chiefs  d) refuse all elections
**Answer: b) respect traditional leadership and satisfy American demands for democracy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
