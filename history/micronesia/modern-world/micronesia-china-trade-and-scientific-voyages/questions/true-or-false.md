# Micronesia, the China Trade and the Scientific Voyages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** About 150 quiet years passed between the first contacts and the Europeans' return.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The scientific voyages left the islands unnamed and uncharted.
**Answer: False — they named and charted the islands, so the outside world could find them again.**
