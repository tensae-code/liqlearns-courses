# Hands-on — Micronesia, the China Trade and the Scientific Voyages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your island sits beside a busy sea road, but for a long time almost no ships stop. Then trade shifts, the road fills, and strangers begin landing — some to buy, some to measure and map. Before you read, list what changes for an island when the world starts stopping again. Then read Micronesia's return to the crossroads.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and chart your own place the way a voyage would: draw your island or street from above, name its landmarks, and mark the road a stranger would take through it. Then walk the road you drew, checking each name and turning against the real ground, and correct your chart where the ground disagrees — the chart-maker's own discipline.
