# Video script — Micronesia, the China Trade and the Scientific Voyages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Micronesia's quiet centuries end when trade moves — and the measurers follow the traders.

For about a hundred and fifty years, few ships stop. Then the China trade fills the Pacific road, and the islands become a crossroads again.

And with the traders come the scientific voyages — Russian and European expeditions, Lütke's among them — making first contacts in Kosrae and the Mortlocks, naming the islands, charting the reefs.

Remember it this way: a charted island can be found again. The quiet ends with trade routes and maps. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
