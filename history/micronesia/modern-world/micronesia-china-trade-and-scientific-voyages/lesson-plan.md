# Lesson plan — Micronesia, the China Trade and the Scientific Voyages

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia, the China Trade and the Scientific Voyages
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After some 150 quiet years, the China trade made Micronesia a crossroads again, and Russian and European scientific voyages — Lütke's among them — made first contacts in Kosrae and the Mortlocks, naming and charting the islands for the outside world's maps.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
