# Micronesia, the China Trade and the Scientific Voyages

## Do it first

Your island sits beside a busy sea road, but for a long time almost no ships stop. Then trade shifts, the road fills, and strangers begin landing — some to buy, some to measure and map. Before you read, list what changes for an island when the world starts stopping again. Then read Micronesia's return to the crossroads.

For about 150 years after the first contacts, Micronesia lay quiet — off the world's busy roads. Then the China trade changed the map of shipping: vessels crossing the Pacific for China made the islands a crossroads again, and sails returned to lagoons that had seen few for generations.

With the traders come the measurers. Russian and European scientific voyages — Lütke's expedition among them — make first contacts in Kosrae and the Mortlocks, and do the work empires value: naming the islands and charting them. A charted island is an island the outside world can find again — the quiet centuries end not with a battle but with trade routes and maps.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: after 150 quiet years the China trade made Micronesia a crossroads again, and scientific voyages such as Lütke's made first contacts in Kosrae and the Mortlocks, naming and charting the islands. Give one real example of a road or route changing a quiet place, and name the lesson's key words: China trade, crossroads, scientific voyage, charting. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: list three changes for an island when the world's ships start stopping again, and match each to this lesson.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: draw the China-trade road across the Pacific with Micronesia at its crossroads and Kosrae and the Mortlocks marked.
3. Teach this lesson to someone at home, and this week find one island or reef name near you that a foreign voyage put on a chart. Note whose name it is.
