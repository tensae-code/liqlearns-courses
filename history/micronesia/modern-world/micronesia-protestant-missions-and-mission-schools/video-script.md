# Video script — Micronesia's Protestant Missions and Mission Schools

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Protestant faith in Micronesia landed with Americans — and spread with islanders.

The 1850s: American missionaries land in Kosrae and Pohnpei. Chiefs weigh the new teaching — the Nahnken of Kitti among them — and where chiefs judge, the faith takes root.

Then the carriers change. Islander missionaries take the faith to the Marshalls, the Mortlocks, Chuuk. Mission schools train islander teachers and pastors. English church-and-school words settle into the island languages. Literacy makes native Christians too strong to be pushed back.

And Kosrae tells the deepest version: a people down ninety percent in fifty years, their old religion seeming to have failed — and the church rebuilding leadership and faith together, in the same generation.

Remember it this way: landed by foreigners, carried by islanders, rebuilt into the islands' own order. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
