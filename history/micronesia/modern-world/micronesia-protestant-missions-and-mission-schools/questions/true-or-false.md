# Micronesia's Protestant Missions and Mission Schools — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islander missionaries carried the faith onward to the Marshalls, the Mortlocks and Chuuk. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Mission schools kept teaching in foreign hands so islanders stayed pupils forever. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the schools trained islander teachers and pastors, and literacy made native Christians strong.**
