# Micronesia's Protestant Missions and Mission Schools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did mission schools do besides teach reading? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** They trained islander teachers and pastors, brought English church-and-school words into the island languages, and made native Christians too strong for the last stands against them.

**2.** What double work did the church do on Kosrae? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** It rebuilt Kosraean leadership and faith together — new belief and new leaders rising in the same generation after the population collapse.
