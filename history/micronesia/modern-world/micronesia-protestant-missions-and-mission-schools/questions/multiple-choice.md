# Micronesia's Protestant Missions and Mission Schools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did American Protestant missionaries first land in this lesson, in the 1850s? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Yap and Saipan only  b) Kosrae and Pohnpei  c) Guam and Palau only  d) the Marshalls only
**Answer: b) Kosrae and Pohnpei** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** By how much had Kosrae's population fallen in fifty years when the missionaries reached it? (Sub lesson 3)
a) ten percent  b) half  c) ninety percent  d) not at all
**Answer: c) ninety percent** — That is what this lesson teaches.
