# Hands-on — Micronesia's Protestant Missions and Mission Schools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A new teaching arrives in a community that has lost almost everyone it had. Before you read, think: what does a community in that condition need from a new faith — comfort, order, leaders, schools? Then read how the Protestant missions actually grew in Micronesia: carried, in the end, by islanders.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the carrying map: Kosrae and Pohnpei first, then the outward legs to the Marshalls, the Mortlocks and Chuuk, marking on each leg who carried the faith. Then walk to the nearest church or school in your place, ask when it began and who its first local teacher or pastor was, and add that name to your map's margin — the carrying continues.
