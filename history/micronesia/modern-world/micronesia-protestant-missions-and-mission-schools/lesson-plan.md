# Lesson plan — Micronesia's Protestant Missions and Mission Schools

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia's Protestant Missions and Mission Schools
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** American Protestant missionaries landed in Kosrae and Pohnpei in the 1850s and the faith they planted was carried onward by islanders themselves: chiefs weighed the new teaching, islander missionaries took it to the Marshalls, the Mortlocks and Chuuk, and mission schools trained islander teachers and pastors — on a Kosrae whose population had fallen ninety percent, the church rebuilt leadership and faith together.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 7, The Foundations of Christianity** — American Protestant missionaries land in Kosrae and Pohnpei in the 1850s, chiefs such as the Nahnken of Kitti weigh the new faith, and islander missionaries carry it on to the Marshalls, the Mortlocks and Chuuk.
2. **Chapter 7, mission schools and the language of school** — Mission schools train islander teachers and pastors, English church-and-school words enter the island languages, and literacy makes native Christians too strong for the last stands against them.
3. **Chapter 6, American Missionaries in Micronesia** — American missionaries reach a Kosrae whose population has fallen ninety percent in fifty years and whose old religion seems to have failed, and the church rebuilds Kosraean leadership and faith together.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
