# Lesson plan — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Europe's race for the Indies set the Pacific's foreign story in motion: Columbus sailed west and found America instead, the Treaty of Tordesillas split the world between Spain and Portugal, and Spanish sailors struggled for decades to find a return route across the Pacific until the Manila Galleon road of 1565 made the crossing a road.
