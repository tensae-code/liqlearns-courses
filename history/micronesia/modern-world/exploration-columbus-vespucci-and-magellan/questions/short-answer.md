# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did Europe race for the Indies?
**Answer:** For the spice-rich east — the wealth of the Indies was the prize that sent the ships out and split the world by treaty.

**2.** Why does the galleon road matter for Micronesia?
**Answer:** Micronesia lay across the road between Asia and America, so the regular crossing ended the islands' quiet centuries and brought the outside world in.
