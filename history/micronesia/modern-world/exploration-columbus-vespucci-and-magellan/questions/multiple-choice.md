# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Columbus find when he sailed west for the Indies?
a) the Indies themselves  b) America instead  c) an empty ocean  d) the Pacific return route
**Answer: b) America instead** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In what year did the Manila Galleon road make the Pacific crossing a regular road?
a) 1492  b) 1565  c) 1899  d) 1947
**Answer: b) 1565** — That is what this lesson teaches.
