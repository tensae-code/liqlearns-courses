# Video script — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Micronesia's foreign story begins with a race it never entered — Europe's race for the Indies.

Columbus sails west, promising Asia. He finds America instead. Two kingdoms, Spain and Portugal, are racing — so they settle the quarrel with a line: the Treaty of Tordesillas splits the whole world between them, on paper, in a room in Europe.

Then the Pacific tests them. Ships can cross it — but how do they get back? For decades Spanish sailors struggle to find the return route, the winds that carry a ship home. In 1565 the answer becomes a road: the Manila Galleon road, Asia to America and back, year after year.

And Micronesia lies across that road. Remember it this way: first the race, then the line, then the road — and the islands in the road's path. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
