# Hands-on — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page and divide everything you own with a friend who is not in the room — half each, by the line. That is what two European kingdoms did to the whole world. Before you read how, say what could go wrong with such a division. Then read the race that caused it.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the story as a route map: Europe, the Atlantic, America, the Pacific, the Indies — with Columbus's outward arrow, the Tordesillas line, and the galleon's return arrow of 1565. Then walk the problem: cross a yard one way easily, then try to return by a different, longer way round, and say aloud why a one-way road is only half a road.
