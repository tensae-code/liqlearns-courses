# Video script — Micronesia's Copra Trade and Foreign Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. First the coconut. Then the company. Then the warship. Law follows trade — in that order.

Whale oil fades. Beche-de-mer fades. Copra takes over — dried coconut for the world's factories. Big trading companies move in. Resident traders open stores. Island life rearranges around the coconut and the counter.

And then the quiet second half: foreign warships in the lagoons, and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870, bringing foreign law in behind the trade.

Remember it this way: the order is the lesson — trade, stores, then other people's rules. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
