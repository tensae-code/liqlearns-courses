# Hands-on — Micronesia's Copra Trade and Foreign Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your island grows one thing the whole world suddenly wants. Traders arrive, stores open, money appears — and then, quietly, other people's rules arrive too. Before you read, list in order what you think comes first, second and third: trade, stores, foreign law. Then read the order the book teaches.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the arrival chain in four boxes — coconut, company store, warship, treaty — with an arrow between each and one line on what each box changed. Then walk to a store in your place that sells an imported or exported good, note where the good comes from or goes to, and add a fifth box: what rules travel with that trade today.
