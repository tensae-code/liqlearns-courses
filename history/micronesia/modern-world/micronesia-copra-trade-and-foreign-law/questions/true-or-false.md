# Micronesia's Copra Trade and Foreign Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Foreign law arrived in Micronesia before any foreign trade did.
**Answer: False — the trade came first — copra, companies and stores — and law followed in behind warships and the 1870 treaty.**

**2.** Resident traders settled in the islands and opened stores during the copra age.
**Answer: True**
