# Micronesia's Copra Trade and Foreign Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What took over as the islands' great export when whale oil and beche-de-mer faded?
a) copra — dried coconut  b) pearl shell  c) rice  d) timber
**Answer: a) copra — dried coconut** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What brought foreign law in behind the trade?
a) mission schools  b) foreign warships and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870  c) the star songs  d) the Congress of Micronesia
**Answer: b) foreign warships and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870** — That is what this lesson teaches.
