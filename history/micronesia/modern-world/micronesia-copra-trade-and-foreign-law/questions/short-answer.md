# Micronesia's Copra Trade and Foreign Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is copra?
**Answer:** The dried meat of the coconut, the export the world's factories wanted — the crop that took over the islands' trade.

**2.** In what order does the book teach the change?
**Answer:** Trade first — copra, big companies, resident traders and stores — then foreign law, brought in behind warships and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870.
