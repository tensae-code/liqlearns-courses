# Lesson plan — Micronesia's Copra Trade and Foreign Law

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia's Copra Trade and Foreign Law
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** When whale oil and beche-de-mer faded, the coconut took over: copra made Micronesia valuable in a new way, big trading companies and resident traders opened stores — and foreign law followed the trade in, behind warships and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Modern World I: Contact, Missions and Trade — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
