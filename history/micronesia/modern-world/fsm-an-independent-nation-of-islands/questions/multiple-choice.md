# The FSM: An Independent Nation of Islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Whose 1979 inauguration ends nearly a century of foreign rule?
a) President Nakayama's  b) Andon Amaraich's  c) Henry Nanpei's  d) Fr. Cantova's
**Answer: a) President Nakayama's** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What ties the FSM to America after independence?
a) the Treaty of Tordesillas  b) the Compact of Free Association  c) the League Mandate  d) the Pohnpei treaty of 1870
**Answer: b) the Compact of Free Association** — That is what this lesson teaches.
