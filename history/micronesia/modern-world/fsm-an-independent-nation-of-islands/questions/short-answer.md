# The FSM: An Independent Nation of Islands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two marks of nationhood does the chapter name besides the inauguration?
**Answer:** United Nations membership, and November 3 Independence Day — with the Compact of Free Association as the chosen American partnership.

**2.** How do emigration and remittances shape the new nation?
**Answer:** Emigration spreads Micronesians abroad, and the money they send home — remittances — binds the diaspora to the islands.
