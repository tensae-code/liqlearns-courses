# Video script — The FSM: An Independent Nation of Islands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In 1979, the foreign century ends — and a nation of islands begins.

President Nakayama is inaugurated. Behind him, the folded flags: Spain, Germany, Japan, America — nearly a hundred years of other people's rule, closed.

The new nation arranges its world: the Compact of Free Association, a chosen partnership with America. A seat in the United Nations. November 3, Independence Day, on the calendar for good. And its people spreading abroad, sending remittances home — the nation wider than its lagoons.

Remember it this way: self-governed, freely associated, seated among nations, and carried in its people's hearts wherever they live. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
