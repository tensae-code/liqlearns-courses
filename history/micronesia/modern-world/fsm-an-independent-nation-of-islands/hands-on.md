# Hands-on — The FSM: An Independent Nation of Islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A president is inaugurated, and for the first time in nearly a hundred years no foreign flag governs your islands. Before you read, think: what does a new nation still have to arrange — friends, money, a seat among nations, its people abroad? Then read the FSM's founding chapter.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and make the nation's founding page: the date 1979, the president's name, the four folded flags in order, the Compact, the UN seat, November 3. Then walk to a flagpole, government office or celebration ground in your place and note what nationhood displays there today — and one thing it does not display that this lesson would add.
