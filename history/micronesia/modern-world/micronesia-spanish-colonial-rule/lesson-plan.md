# Lesson plan — Micronesia Under Spanish Colonial Rule

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia Under Spanish Colonial Rule
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Spain, the first European explorer of the Carolines, became their first coloniser — ruling from administrations on Yap and Pohnpei, fighting Pohnpeians who attacked within three months, quarrelling with Protestant missionaries such as Reverend Doane — while far-off Kapingamarangi lived the same Spanish era at the empire's edge, until Spain sold the islands in 1899.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 9, Initial Rule by the Spain** — Spain, first explorer of the Carolines, becomes first coloniser with administrations on Yap and Pohnpei, fights Pohnpeians who attack only three months in, and quarrels with Protestant missionaries such as Reverend Doane.
2. **Chapter 9.3, The Time of the Spanish, to 1899** — Kapingamarangi lives its Spanish era at the edge of an empire that rules the Carolines from far away, until Spain sells the islands in 1899.
