# Hands-on — Micronesia Under Spanish Colonial Rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The country that first drew your islands on its maps now returns to rule them. Before you read, think about what changes when a visitor becomes a government: taxes, officials, soldiers, rules about worship. Then read Spain's rule in the Carolines, from Pohnpei's resistance to a small atoll at the empire's edge.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw Spain's rule as two distances: near — Pohnpei, officials and the attack at three months; far — Kapingamarangi, a flag and no officials in sight. Label what rule felt like at each distance. Then walk your own place and list which of its rules are made near and which far away, and by whom — the Spanish lesson in present-day form.
