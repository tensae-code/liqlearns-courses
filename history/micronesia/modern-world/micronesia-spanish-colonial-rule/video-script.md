# Video script — Micronesia Under Spanish Colonial Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Spain explored the Carolines first — and then came back to rule them.

Administrations rise on Yap and on Pohnpei: officials, soldiers, orders. The first coloniser has arrived. Pohnpei answers within three months — attack. And a second quarrel runs on: Spain against the Protestant missionaries, Reverend Doane among them, two worlds on one shore.

Far south, Kapingamarangi lives the same era at a distance — the Time of the Spanish, the atoll's reader calls it — ruled from far away, until 1899, when Spain sells the islands and the era ends on paper in Europe.

Remember it this way: first explorer, first coloniser — resisted at the centre, distant at the edge, ended by a sale. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
