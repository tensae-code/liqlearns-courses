# Micronesia Under Spanish Colonial Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On which two islands did Spain set up its administrations? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Kosrae and Chuuk  b) Yap and Pohnpei  c) Guam and Saipan only  d) the Marshalls and Mortlocks
**Answer: b) Yap and Pohnpei** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the Spanish era end for the Carolines, as Kapingamarangi's reader dates it? (Sub lesson 2)
a) a local revolution  b) Spain sold the islands in 1899  c) the missionaries left  d) a typhoon
**Answer: b) Spain sold the islands in 1899** — That is what this lesson teaches.
