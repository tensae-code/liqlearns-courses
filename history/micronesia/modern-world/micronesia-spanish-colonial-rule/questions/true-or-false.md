# Micronesia Under Spanish Colonial Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pohnpeians attacked only three months into Spanish rule. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Spain ruled Kapingamarangi with a large administration based on the atoll itself. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the atoll lived the era at the empire's edge, ruled from far away.**
