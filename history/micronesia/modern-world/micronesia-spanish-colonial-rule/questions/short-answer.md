# Micronesia Under Spanish Colonial Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** With whom did Spain quarrel besides the resisting Pohnpeians? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Protestant missionaries already in the islands, such as Reverend Doane — Spain's Catholic empire against their American Protestant world.

**2.** How does Kapingamarangi's reader name and bound the Spanish era? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** As 'The Time of the Spanish', a dated section running to 1899, when Spain sold the islands.
