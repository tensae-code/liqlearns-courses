# Lesson plan — Micronesia's Leap into the Modern World

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia's Leap into the Modern World
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Modern World III: The American Time and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the 1960s–70s the Trust Territory transformed at speed: money, radios, new schools, hospitals, roads and airfields remade island life, Peace Corps volunteers and Civic Action Teams arrived — and Micronesianization put islanders in charge of more and more of their own government.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Modern World III: The American Time and Independence — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
