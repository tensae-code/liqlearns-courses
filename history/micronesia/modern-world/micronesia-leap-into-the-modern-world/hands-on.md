# Hands-on — Micronesia's Leap into the Modern World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Within one childhood, your island gains money in daily use, a radio's voice, a hospital, a road, an airfield. Before you read, list what such a leap changes first — and what it cannot change. Then read Micronesia's leap into the modern world.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and interview the leap: ask one elder to name the first radio, road, school building or airfield they remember in your place, and when it came. Draw your place twice — before the leap and after — from their account, and label each difference with its decade.
