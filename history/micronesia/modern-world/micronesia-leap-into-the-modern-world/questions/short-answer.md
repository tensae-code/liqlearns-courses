# Micronesia's Leap into the Modern World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What transformed the islands' material life in the 1960s–70s?
**Answer:** Money, radios, new schools, hospitals, roads and airfields — a single generation's world remade.

**2.** Why is Micronesianization the deepest part of the leap?
**Answer:** Because it changed who governed: islanders ran more and more of the Trust Territory government itself, before independence.
