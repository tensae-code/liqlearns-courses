# Micronesia's Leap into the Modern World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is Micronesianization?
a) a radio programme  b) islanders taking charge of more of the Trust Territory government  c) a copra company  d) a school subject
**Answer: b) islanders taking charge of more of the Trust Territory government** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two kinds of American teams and volunteers arrived in the leap years?
a) galleon crews and district officers  b) Peace Corps volunteers and Civic Action Teams  c) yes-yes councils and magistrates  d) traders and beachcombers
**Answer: b) Peace Corps volunteers and Civic Action Teams** — That is what this lesson teaches.
