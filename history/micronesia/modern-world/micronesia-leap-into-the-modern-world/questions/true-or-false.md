# Micronesia's Leap into the Modern World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The leap of the 1960s–70s happened after independence was complete.
**Answer: False — it happened inside the Trust Territory, a generation before the FSM — preparation, not arrival.**

**2.** Radios, new schools, hospitals, roads and airfields all belong to the leap.
**Answer: True**
