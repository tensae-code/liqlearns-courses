# Video script — Micronesia's Leap into the Modern World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In twenty years, Micronesia leapt — in things, and in who held the offices.

Money spreads. Radios speak in island homes. Schools, hospitals, roads, airfields — a generation's material world remade. Peace Corps volunteers arrive in the villages; Civic Action Teams arrive with their tools.

And in the government offices, the quiet revolution: Micronesianization. Islanders in charge of more and more of their own administration — the daily machine in local hands before independence has a date.

Remember it this way: the leap was concrete — and it was personnel. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
