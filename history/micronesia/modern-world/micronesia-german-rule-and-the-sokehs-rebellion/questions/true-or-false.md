# Micronesia Under German Rule and the Sokehs Rebellion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** German district offices stood on Saipan, Yap and Pohnpei. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** On Yap, Germany refused all dealing with chiefs and built its roads against them. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False — on Yap roads were built with the chiefs — German rule worked through traditional leaders there, and against them on Pohnpei.**
