# Micronesia Under German Rule and the Sokehs Rebellion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did Germany get the Carolines in 1899? (Sub lesson 1)
a) conquered them from the islanders  b) bought them from Spain for just over four million dollars  c) won them from Japan  d) inherited them from Portugal
**Answer: b) bought them from Spain for just over four million dollars** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened in the Sokehs Rebellion of 1910? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Sokehs welcomed German rule  b) the people of Sokehs rebelled against the strict reforms and killed the German district officer  c) a typhoon destroyed the district office  d) the chiefs sold Sokehs
**Answer: b) the people of Sokehs rebelled against the strict reforms and killed the German district officer** — That is what this lesson teaches.
