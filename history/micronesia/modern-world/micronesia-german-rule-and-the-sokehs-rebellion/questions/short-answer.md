# Micronesia Under German Rule and the Sokehs Rebellion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What reforms did German rule push? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Roads, cash crops, and land and labour reforms, run from the district offices — development at a pace the islands did not choose.

**2.** How does Kapingamarangi's reader bound the German era, and how did it end? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** As 'The Time of the Germans, 1899–1914' — ended by the First World War in 1914.
