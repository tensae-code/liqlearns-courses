# Lesson plan — Micronesia Under German Rule and the Sokehs Rebellion

**Course:** History — Micronesia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia Under German Rule and the Sokehs Rebellion
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Germany bought the Carolines from Spain in 1899 for just over four million dollars and ruled through district offices, pushing roads, cash crops and land and labour reforms — working through chiefs where it could, and meeting armed answer in the Sokehs Rebellion of 1910, when Sokehs killed the German district officer and Germany sent soldiers against the rebels in 1911.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern World II: Four Foreign Rulers and the War — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 10, German Attempts at Development** — Germany buys the Carolines from Spain in 1899 for just over four million dollars and pushes roads, cash crops and land and labour reforms from district offices on Saipan, Yap and Pohnpei.
2. **Chapter 10, the Sokehs Rebellion and stormy weather** — In 1910 the people of Sokehs rebel against the strict new reforms and kill the German district officer, while typhoons drive outer islanders onto the high islands and the Catholic Church expands into Chuuk.
3. **Chapter 7, Micronesia's German Occupation** — German rule works through and against traditional leaders — roads built with Yapese chiefs on one island, soldiers sent against the Sokehs rebels on Pohnpei in 1911 on another.
4. **Chapter 9.4, The Time of the Germans, 1899–1914** — German administration follows the purchase of 1899, bringing new officials, new rules and new trade to the atoll until the First World War ends it.
