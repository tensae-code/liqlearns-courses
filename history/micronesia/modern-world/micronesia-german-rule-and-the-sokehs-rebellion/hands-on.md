# Hands-on — Micronesia Under German Rule and the Sokehs Rebellion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A new flag goes up — bought, not conquered. The new rulers are energetic: roads, crops, new rules for land and labour, all at speed. Before you read, think: when reform comes fast and from outside, what do people weigh — the road, or the hand that orders it built? Then read Germany's Micronesia, and Sokehs's answer.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and draw the German years as a ledger: left page, what Germany built and changed (roads, crops, reforms, offices); right page, what it cost and who answered (Sokehs, 1910; the soldiers, 1911). Rule a line under both and write the balance in one sentence. Then walk one road near you and note one thing a road changes besides travel — land, work, who passes — as the German roads changed Pohnpei.
