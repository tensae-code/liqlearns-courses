# Video script — Micronesia Under German Rule and the Sokehs Rebellion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Germany bought Micronesia — and Sokehs answered the receipt.

1899: just over four million dollars, Spain to Germany, an empire sold on paper. Then the businesslike rule: district offices on Saipan, Yap and Pohnpei; roads, cash crops, land and labour reforms, pushed at speed.

On Yap the roads are built with the chiefs. On Pohnpei the pressure breaks: 1910, Sokehs rebels against the strict reforms and kills the district officer; 1911, Germany sends soldiers. Typhoons scatter outer islanders onto the high islands, and the Catholic Church quietly expands into Chuuk.

Kapingamarangi's reader dates it coldly: the Time of the Germans, 1899 to 1914 — ended by a world war decided in Europe. Remember it this way: bought in 1899, answered in 1910, ended in 1914. Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
