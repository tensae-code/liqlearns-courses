# Hands-on — Micronesia's First Settlement: The Austronesian Voyagers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand on a beach and look at the horizon. Every island in Micronesia was reached across that water, by people in canoes, carrying everything a new home would need. Before this lesson names anyone, list what you would pack for a voyage to an island you have never seen, with no way back for supplies. Then read how the real voyagers did it.

## The lesson in your hands and on your feet
Take paper and a pencil and build the voyage on paper: draw your outrigger, its sail and its cargo, and write the star song's job beside the sky. Then walk it: pace out a zigzag tack across a yard or beach — five steps one way, five the other, never straight into the wind — and at each turn name one thing the real voyagers carried and why. Finish by listing what your own island's first settlers must have brought, from what grows and lives there now.
