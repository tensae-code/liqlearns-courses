# Micronesia's First Settlement: The Austronesian Voyagers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From where did the Austronesian migration into Micronesia begin, and about when? (Sub lesson 1)
a) southeast China, reaching Taiwan about 5,000 BC  b) Japan, about 500 BC  c) America, about AD 1000  d) Australia, about AD 500
**Answer: a) southeast China, reaching Taiwan about 5,000 BC** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which three kinds of evidence does Kapingamarangi's reader use to trace the two waves? (Sub lesson 3)
a) coins, maps and flags  b) archaeology, linguistics and DNA  c) songs alone  d) ships' logs
**Answer: b) archaeology, linguistics and DNA** — That is what this lesson teaches.
