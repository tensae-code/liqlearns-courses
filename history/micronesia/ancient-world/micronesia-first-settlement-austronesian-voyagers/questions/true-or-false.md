# Micronesia's First Settlement: The Austronesian Voyagers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Austronesian voyagers were the first people ever to move into the Pacific region. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False — older peoples had already crossed the Sunda and Sahul land bridges; the Austronesians were the second great wave.**

**2.** Navigators carried the star paths in written charts. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the star paths were sung — star songs learned by heart, the sky itself the map.**
