# Micronesia's First Settlement: The Austronesian Voyagers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did a settlement canoe carry, and why? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Adzes, coir rope, pigs, chickens and seed crops — everything needed to build a subsistence life on each new island.

**2.** In which languages do the three atoll readers retell the two-wave story? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** In Kapingamarangi, Mwoakillese and Nukuoroan — each atoll tells the shared beginning in its own language for its own children.
