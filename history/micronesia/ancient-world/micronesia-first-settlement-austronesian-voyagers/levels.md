# Micronesia's First Settlement: The Austronesian Voyagers — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 2, Centuries of Migration and the two paths of settlement
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: crowded villages push Austronesian families out of southeast China to Taiwan about 5,000 BC, then island by island through the Philippines and along two paths into Micronesia, while older peoples had already crossed the Sunda and Sahul land bridges — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 2, Traveling Beyond the Horizon: the outrigger, star songs and the tool kit
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: navigators sing star paths instead of writing them, tack against the wind in outrigger canoes, and land with adzes, coir rope, pigs, chickens and seed crops to build a subsistence life on each new island — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 1, The Origin of the People of Kapingamarangi
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: archaeology, linguistics and DNA together trace two waves into the Pacific — the first across the Sunda and Sahul land bridges some 50,000 years ago, the second the Austronesian Lapita voyagers from Taiwan who reach the Polynesian outliers, Kapingamarangi among them, from Tuvalu — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
