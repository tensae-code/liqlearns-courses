# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In Pohnpei's model, who stands at the top of each of the five chiefdoms? (Sub lesson 3)
a) one king alone  b) two paramount chiefs — the Nahnmwarki and the Nahnken  c) a council of foreigners  d) the clan chiefs of Chuuk
**Answer: b) two paramount chiefs — the Nahnmwarki and the Nahnken** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How many Nahmwarki does Mwoakilloa's reader chronicle in order? (Sub lesson 7)
a) three  b) eighteen — from Lakaidak to Sahn Johnson  c) fifty  d) none are named
**Answer: b) eighteen — from Lakaidak to Sahn Johnson** — That is what this lesson teaches.
