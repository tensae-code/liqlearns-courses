# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What one principle holds underneath all three models? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Power is balanced between leaders and people — shared through meetings and consensus, with the people's voice checking the chief.

**2.** To what is a Kapingamarangi ariki's authority bound? (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** To the land-holding people — the people of the land; the chief's place draws its meaning from them.
