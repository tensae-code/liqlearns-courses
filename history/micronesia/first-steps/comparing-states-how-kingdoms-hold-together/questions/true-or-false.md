# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Chuuk's model, one chief holds power over all the other chiefs. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — Chuuk's clan and lineage chiefs hold little power over their own people and none over other chiefs.**

**2.** In Yap, the councils of the ranked village nets make nearly every important decision. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True**
