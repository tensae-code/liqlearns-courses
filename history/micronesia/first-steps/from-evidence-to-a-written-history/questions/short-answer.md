# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Against what three kinds of evidence are Pohnpei's and Chuuk's settlement legends checked? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Language families, and shared tools and shared customs — the similarities trace who came from where.

**2.** How far back does Mwoakilloa's told tradition reach, in the community's own reckoning? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Some 1,700 years — the reader sets that oral record down as the island's first history.
