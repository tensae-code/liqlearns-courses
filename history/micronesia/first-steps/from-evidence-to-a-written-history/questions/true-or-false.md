# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A written history is found whole and simply copied out. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False — it is made — the historian selects the surviving pieces and pieces them together, like a people's family album.**

**2.** Nukuoro's traditions preserve the coming of Vave, his deeds and his death, in sequence. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer: True**
