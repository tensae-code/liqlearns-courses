# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the past survive as, before a historian works on it? (Sub lesson 1)
a) whole finished books  b) pieces — letters, journals, reports, songs and told traditions  c) only government files  d) nothing at all
**Answer: b) pieces — letters, journals, reports, songs and told traditions** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who is Utamatua in Kapingamarangi's tradition? (Sub lesson 3)
a) a Spanish governor  b) the son of a spared castaway, taught navigation by his father, the ancestor the atoll traces itself from  c) a whaling captain  d) the first schoolteacher
**Answer: b) the son of a spared castaway, taught navigation by his father, the ancestor the atoll traces itself from** — That is what this lesson teaches.
