# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble

## Do it first

Think of a story about the past that you already know — from home, from school, from your street. Say out loud who was in it, where it happened, and what changed by its end. Keep that shape in mind: this lesson's story is told the same way — people, place, the turn, and what came of it.

Rwanda was a kingdom, ruled by its own powerful kings — the Abami. Then came the age when European powers seized Africa. Rwanda lost its independence: first it fell under Germany, and after Germany's defeat in war it was handed to Belgium. The kings still sat on the throne, but the country's freedom was gone. And Rwanda's fate was Africa's fate: across the whole continent, only two countries escaped colonisation — Ethiopia and Liberia.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Hold the order tight: first Germany, then Belgium. Two rulers from outside, one after the other — that order unlocks the rest of Rwanda's colonial story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Rwanda, ruled by its own powerful Abami kings, lost its independence to foreign rule — first Germany's, then Belgium's — in the age when Europe divided Africa; of all Africa, only Ethiopia and Liberia escaped colonisation. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
