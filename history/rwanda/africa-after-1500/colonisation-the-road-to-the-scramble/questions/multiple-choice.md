# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who ruled Rwanda before colonisation?
a) its own powerful Abami kings  b) the Belgians from the start  c) a republic  d) no one
**Answer: a) its own powerful Abami kings** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two countries escaped colonisation?
a) Ethiopia and Liberia  b) Rwanda and Burundi  c) Kenya and Uganda  d) Ghana and Mali
**Answer: a) Ethiopia and Liberia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
