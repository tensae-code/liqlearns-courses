# Video script — Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rwanda, ruled by its own powerful Abami kings, lost its independence to foreign rule — first Germany's, then Belgium's — in the age when Europe divided Africa; of all Africa, only Ethiopia and Liberia escaped colonisation.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Rwanda was a kingdom, ruled by its own powerful kings — the Abami. Then came the age when European powers seized Africa. Rwanda lost its independence: first it fell under Germany, and after Germany's defeat in war it was handed to Belgium. The kings still sat on the throne, but the country's freedom was gone. And Rwanda's fate was Africa's fate: across the whole continent, only two countries escaped colonisation — Ethiopia and Liberia.

Remember it this way: Rwanda, ruled by its own powerful Abami kings, lost its independence to foreign rule.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
