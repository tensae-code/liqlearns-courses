# Rwanda: National Heroes and Heroes Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three things does the book say Rwanda's heroes did? (Sub lesson 1)
a) ended the genocide, freed the country from bad rule, promoted unity  b) built the railways, founded Save, drew the maps  c) won trophies, wrote books, built schools only  d) ruled Germany, Belgium and Rwanda
**Answer: a) ended the genocide, freed the country from bad rule, promoted unity** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Sister Félicité Niyitegeka refuse to do? (Sub lesson 1)
a) abandon the Tutsi families sheltering with her  b) leave her school  c) stop teaching  d) split her students by group
**Answer: a) abandon the Tutsi families sheltering with her** — That is what this lesson teaches.
