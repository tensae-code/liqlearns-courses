# Video script — Rwanda: Second Republic 1973–1994

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Habyarimana's Second Republic suspended the constitution, banned parties and ruled through the MRND one-party state — until pressure forced parties open in 1991, and three transitional governments under Nsanzimana, Nsengiyaremye and Uwilingiyimana tried to carry the country through.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Habyarimana's republic began by closing the country's politics: the constitution was suspended, parties were banned, and the MRND ruled as a one-party state. For years that was the whole system. Then pressure — inside and outside — forced the door: in 1991 parties were allowed again. Three transitional governments followed, led in turn by Nsanzimana, Nsengiyaremye and Uwilingiyimana, each trying to carry a divided country toward a settlement it never safely reached.

Remember it this way: Habyarimana's Second Republic suspended the constitution, banned parties and ruled through the MRND one-party state.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
