# Rwanda: Second Republic 1973–1994 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three leaders of the transitional governments, in order.
**Answer:** Nsanzimana, Nsengiyaremye and Uwilingiyimana.

**2.** Through what party did Habyarimana rule?
**Answer:** The MRND, as a one-party state.
