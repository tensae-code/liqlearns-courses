# Rwanda: Second Republic 1973–1994 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Habyarimana do at the start of the Second Republic?
a) suspended the constitution and banned parties  b) restored the monarchy  c) held multi-party elections at once  d) resigned
**Answer: a) suspended the constitution and banned parties** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In what year were parties allowed again?
a) 1991  b) 1973  c) 1962  d) 2000
**Answer: a) 1991** — That is what this lesson teaches.
