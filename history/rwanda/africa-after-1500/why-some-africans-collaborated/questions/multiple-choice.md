# Why Some Africans Collaborated — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which peoples does the book name as weakened before they collaborated?
a) the Maasai and the Banyankole  b) the Abami and the Azande  c) the Swahili and the Arabs  d) the Tutsi and the Hutu only
**Answer: a) the Maasai and the Banyankole** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What killed the herders' cattle in this lesson?
a) rinderpest  b) a drought only  c) the slave trade  d) a flood
