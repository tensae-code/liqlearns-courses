# Why Some Africans Collaborated — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Some Africans collaborated hoping for goods, protection or missionary favour.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Maasai and Banyankole collaborated because they were at their strongest.
**Answer: False** — they had been weakened by disease, rinderpest and conflict
