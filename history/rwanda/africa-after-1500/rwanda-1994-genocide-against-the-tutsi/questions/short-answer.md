# Rwanda: 1994 Genocide Against the Tutsi — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three things that built the hatred before April 1994. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any three of: divisionism, poor leadership, impunity, the Akazu, Hutu Power, Kangura, RTLM.

**2.** What does prevention mean in this lesson, and who are the Abunzi? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Prevention means unity, healing and reconciliation; the Abunzi are the mediators through whom reconciliation is done.
