# Rwanda: 1994 Genocide Against the Tutsi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did colonial rule do to Rwanda's social classes, as the book teaches it? (Sub lesson 1)
a) turned them into fixed ethnic groups with identity cards  b) abolished them  c) made them richer  d) moved them to Uganda
**Answer: a) turned them into fixed ethnic groups with identity cards** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who formed a government on 19 July 1994, after the RPF defeated the perpetrators? (Sub lesson 2)
a) a Government of National Unity  b) the Akazu  c) the MRND alone  d) the colonial administration
**Answer: a) a Government of National Unity** — That is what this lesson teaches.
