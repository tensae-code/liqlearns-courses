# Lesson plan — Rwanda: 1994 Genocide Against the Tutsi

**Course:** History — Rwanda · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Rwanda: 1994 Genocide Against the Tutsi
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Independent Rwanda: Heroes, Republics, Liberation and Genocide (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi was built before it exploded: colonial rule hardened social classes into ethnic groups with identity cards, and divisionism, poor leadership, impunity, the Akazu, Hutu Power, Kangura and RTLM built the hatred — then came the planned killing, lists and militia, the murder of Prime Minister Uwilingiyimana, and the RPF's victory and Government of National Unity on 19 July 1994; what remains is to refuse the ideology and denial, and to heal through unity and the Abunzi.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Independent Rwanda: Heroes, Republics, Liberation and Genocide (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 12.1–12.2: Meaning and causes of the Genocide** — The book begins with causes, because the genocide was not a sudden madness.
2. **Units 12.3–12.4: Planning, execution and how it was stopped** — The killing was planned: the Akazu prepared a 'final solution' — lists of 'traitors' to be killed, machetes imported in bulk, and the Interahamwe and Impuzamugambi militia trained to do it.
3. **Units 12.6–12.8: Ideology, denial, prevention and consequences** — The lesson ends facing forward.
4. **Unit 12 (guide): Teaching the Genocide unit** — The teacher's guide sequences the whole unit as evidence work: meaning, causes, planning, execution, the Holocaust comparison, ideology and denial, prevention and consequences — with memorial-site field visits as core evidence work, where learners stand where it happened and read the proof themselves..

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
