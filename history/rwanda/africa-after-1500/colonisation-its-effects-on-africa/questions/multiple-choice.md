# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who are the two colonialists the book names?
a) Carl Peters for Germany and Lugard for Britain  b) Speke and Stanley  c) Krapf and Mackay  d) Peters for Britain and Lugard for Germany
**Answer: a) Carl Peters for Germany and Lugard for Britain** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is named as something colonial rule brought?
a) roads, western schooling and medicine  b) the abolition of all taxes  c) the end of all disease  d) independence in 1885
**Answer: a) roads, western schooling and medicine** — That is what this lesson teaches.
