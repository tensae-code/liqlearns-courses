# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three reasons Europeans colonised Rwanda.
**Answer:** Any three of: wolfram, gold and coltan; fertile soil; markets for their goods; to spread Christianity.

**2.** Why did Europe's factories need colonies?
**Answer:** They needed raw materials to feed them and markets to sell what they made.
