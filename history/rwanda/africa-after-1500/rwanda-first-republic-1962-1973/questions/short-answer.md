# Rwanda: First Republic 1962–1973 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What quota did the First Republic impose, and on whom?
**Answer:** A 9% quota on Tutsi.

**2.** What two events stand at the First Republic's beginning?
**Answer:** The violence of 1959 and the 1961 referendum abolishing the monarchy.
