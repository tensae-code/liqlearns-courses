# Rwanda: First Republic 1962–1973 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the 1961 referendum do?
a) abolished the monarchy  b) restored King Musinga  c) joined Rwanda to Belgium  d) ended the quota
**Answer: a) abolished the monarchy** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who overthrew Kayibanda, and when?
a) Habyarimana, on 5 July 1973  b) Kagame, in 1990  c) Rudahigwa, in 1959  d) the Belgians, in 1962
**Answer: a) Habyarimana, on 5 July 1973** — That is what this lesson teaches.
