# Video script — Rwanda: First Republic 1962–1973

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The First Republic was born from the 1959 violence and the 1961 referendum that abolished the monarchy: Kayibanda's PARMEHUTU republic ruled as one party, imposed a 9% Tutsi quota and drove Tutsi from schools — until Habyarimana overthrew Kayibanda on 5 July 1973.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Independence did not arrive quietly. After the violence of 1959, a referendum in 1961 abolished the monarchy itself, and the republic that followed was Kayibanda's: his PARMEHUTU party ruled alone, one party over the whole country. The republic imposed a 9% quota on Tutsi and drove Tutsi out of schools. A republic that began by ending kings ended by closing doors — and on 5 July 1973 the army's Habyarimana overthrew Kayibanda, and the First Republic fell.

Remember it this way: The First Republic was born from the 1959 violence and the 1961 referendum that abolished the monarchy: Kayibanda's PARMEHUTU republic ruled as one party, imposed a 9% Tutsi quota and drove Tutsi from schools.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
