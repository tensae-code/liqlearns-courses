# Rwanda: Liberation War 1990–1994 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When did the Liberation War begin, and from where? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 1 October 1990, from Uganda  b) 1 July 1962, from Kigali  c) 4 July 1994, from Arusha  d) 5 July 1973, from Burundi
**Answer: a) 1 October 1990, from Uganda** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who took command of the RPF after Rwigema fell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Kagame  b) Kayibanda  c) Habyarimana  d) Nsengiyaremye
**Answer: a) Kagame** — That is what this lesson teaches.
