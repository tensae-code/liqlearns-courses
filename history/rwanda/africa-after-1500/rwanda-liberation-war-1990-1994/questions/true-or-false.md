# Rwanda: Liberation War 1990–1994 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Arusha Accords were signed in 1993. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The RPF's aim had nothing to do with exile or discrimination. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — the RPF fought to end exile and discrimination and come home
