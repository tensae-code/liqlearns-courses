# Rwanda: Liberation War 1990–1994 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happened on 4 July 1994? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The RPF stopped the genocide and liberated Kigali.

**2.** How does the teacher's guide teach the Liberation War? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** By narration and evidence: the teacher tells a short story, shows photographs and a film clip, then learners explain how the two Republics' injustices, divisionism and corruption led exiles to fight their way home.
