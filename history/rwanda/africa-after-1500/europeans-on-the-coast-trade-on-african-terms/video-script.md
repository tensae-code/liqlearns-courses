# Video script — Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In Rwanda the traders came before the rulers: Carl Peters founded the German East Africa Company in 1897, trading minerals and skins — and the administrators followed the traders in.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Colonial rule in Rwanda did not begin with an army. It began with a company. In 1897 Carl Peters founded the German East Africa Company in Rwanda, and its men traded — minerals and skins bought and sold. Trade looked peaceful, but it planted a foreign power inside the country: after the traders came the administrators, and the company town became the colony's door.

Remember it this way: In Rwanda the traders came before the rulers: Carl Peters founded the German East Africa Company in 1897, trading minerals and skins.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
