# Rwanda: Road to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rudahigwa abolished Ubuhake and Uburetwa and demanded independence in 1954. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Rudahigwa supported the tribal identity cards. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — he opposed the tribal identity cards
