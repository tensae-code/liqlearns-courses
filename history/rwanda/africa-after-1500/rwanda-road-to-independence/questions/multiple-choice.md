# Rwanda: Road to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Berlin conference of 1884–85 do to Rwanda? (Sub lesson 1)
a) handed it to Germany  b) made it independent  c) gave it to Belgium first  d) divided it into five parties
**Answer: a) handed it to Germany** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When did Rwanda become independent, and who was its first President? (Sub lesson 2)
a) 1 July 1962, Grégoire Kayibanda  b) 1 July 1959, Rudahigwa  c) 4 July 1994, Kayibanda  d) 1885, Musinga
**Answer: a) 1 July 1962, Grégoire Kayibanda** — That is what this lesson teaches.
