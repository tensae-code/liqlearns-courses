# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Missions brought schools and hospitals, and also undermined local culture. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Missionaries played no part in ending the slave trade. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — missions helped abolish the slave trade, and Livingstone travelled fighting it
