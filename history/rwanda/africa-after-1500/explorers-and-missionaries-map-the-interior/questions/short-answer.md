# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the two explorers the book names in Rwanda. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Baumann and von Götzen.

**2.** Name two good things the missions brought to East Africa. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Any two of: literacy, hospitals, churches, new crops — and help in abolishing the slave trade.
