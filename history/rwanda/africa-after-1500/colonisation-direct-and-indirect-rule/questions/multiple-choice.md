# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did Germany rule Rwanda?
a) indirectly, through the king  b) directly, with its own officials everywhere  c) it never ruled Rwanda  d) through the missions only
**Answer: a) indirectly, through the king** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Belgium's direct rule do to the king?
a) it stripped the king's power  b) it doubled his power  c) it made him emperor  d) nothing changed
**Answer: a) it stripped the king's power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
