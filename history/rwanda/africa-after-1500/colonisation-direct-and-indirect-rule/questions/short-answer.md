# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the difference between a colony and a protectorate, as the guide teaches it?
**Answer:** A colony is ruled outright by the foreign power; a protectorate keeps a local ruler under foreign control.

**2.** Which power ruled Rwanda indirectly, and which directly?
**Answer:** Germany ruled indirectly, through the king; Belgium ruled directly and stripped the king's power.
