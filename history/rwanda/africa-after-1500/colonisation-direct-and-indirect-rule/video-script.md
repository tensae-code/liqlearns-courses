# Video script — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Colonial powers ruled in two forms — the colony and the protectorate — and Rwanda felt both hands: Germany ruled indirectly, through the king, while Belgium ruled directly and stripped the king's power.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The teacher's guide sets out the forms plainly. A colony is ruled outright by the foreign power; a protectorate keeps a local ruler under foreign control. Rwanda knew both hands. Germany ruled indirectly — through the king, leaving the throne standing while real power leaned on it. Belgium ruled directly: its officials governed themselves, and the king's power was stripped away. Same country, two systems — and the difference decided how Rwandans experienced colonial rule.

Remember it this way: Colonial powers ruled in two forms.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
