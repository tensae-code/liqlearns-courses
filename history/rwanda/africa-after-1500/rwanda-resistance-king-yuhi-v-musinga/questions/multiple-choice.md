# Rwanda Resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why was King Musinga exiled? (Sub lesson 1)
a) he refused Belgian direct rule and the loss of his power  b) he asked to leave Rwanda  c) he lost a war against Germany  d) he broke a trade agreement
**Answer: a) he refused Belgian direct rule and the loss of his power** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where was Musinga exiled to, in order? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Kamembe, then Moba  b) Moba, then Kamembe  c) Bujumbura only  d) Save mission
**Answer: a) Kamembe, then Moba** — That is what this lesson teaches.
