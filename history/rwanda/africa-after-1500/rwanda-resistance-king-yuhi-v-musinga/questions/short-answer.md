# Rwanda Resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In what year was Musinga exiled, and who exiled him? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1931, by the Belgians backed by the Catholic Church.

**2.** What two reasons for resistance does the teacher's guide name? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The king's power was lowered, and Rwandans were divided against each other by the Belgians.
