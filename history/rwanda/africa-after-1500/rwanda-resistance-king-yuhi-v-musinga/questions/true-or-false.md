# Rwanda Resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Musinga died in exile in 1944. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Belgians acted alone, with no church support, in exiling Musinga. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — the Belgians and the Catholic Church exiled him
