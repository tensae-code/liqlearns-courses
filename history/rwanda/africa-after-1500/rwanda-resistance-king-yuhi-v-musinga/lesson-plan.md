# Lesson plan — Rwanda Resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga

**Course:** History — Rwanda · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Rwanda Resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Colonisation, Resistance and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** King Yuhi V Musinga refused Belgium's direct rule and the loss of his power — so the Belgians and the Catholic Church exiled him, to Kamembe and then Moba in 1931, and he died in exile in 1944; his fall taught Rwandans what resistance cost, and how the Belgians divided Rwandans to rule them.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Colonisation, Resistance and the Road to Independence (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 10.3: Reaction of Rwandans to colonial rule** — When Belgium took direct rule, King Yuhi V Musinga refused.
2. **Unit 10 Lessons 3–4: Resistance and its impact (guide)** — The teacher's guide walks pupils through the resistance lesson by lesson: why Rwandans resisted — the king's power lowered, and Rwandans divided against each other by the Belgians — and what Musinga's exile cost the country.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
