# Rwanda: Key Dates — Independence, Genocide and Liberation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three dates this lesson places on Rwanda's timeline, and what each one is.
**Answer:** 1962 — independence; 1994 — the Genocide against the Tutsi and Liberation on 4 July; 2016 — Rwanda hosts the African Union summit.

**2.** Why are these dates taught on the timeline to young pupils?
**Answer:** So the country's biggest events land in their right time and order — freedom, catastrophe and rescue, and Rwanda hosting Africa.
