# Rwanda: Key Dates — Independence, Genocide and Liberation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened in Rwanda in 1962?
a) the country gained independence  b) the Genocide against the Tutsi  c) the African Union summit  d) Berlin handed Rwanda to Germany
**Answer: a) the country gained independence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** On what day in 1994 did Liberation come?
a) 4 July  b) 1 July  c) 1 February  d) 28 November
**Answer: a) 4 July** — That is what this lesson teaches.
