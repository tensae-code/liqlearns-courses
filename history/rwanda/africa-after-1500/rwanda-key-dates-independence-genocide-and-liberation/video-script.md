# Video script — Rwanda: Key Dates — Independence, Genocide and Liberation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Three dates carry modern Rwanda on the timeline: 1962, independence; 1994, the Genocide against the Tutsi and then Liberation on 4 July; and 2016, when Rwanda hosted the African Union summit.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The youngest historians in Rwanda learn their country's story as three dates on the timeline line. First, 1962: the country gains independence. Then 1994, the heaviest year: the Genocide against the Tutsi — and then, on 4 July of that same year, Liberation. And 2016: Rwanda hosts the African Union summit, the continent meeting on Rwandan soil. Past, marked in years a child can place: freedom, catastrophe and rescue, and a country hosting Africa.

Remember it this way: Three dates carry modern Rwanda on the timeline: 1962, independence; 1994, the Genocide against the Tutsi and then Liberation on 4 July; and 2016, when Rwanda hosted the African Union summit..

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
