# Peoples of East Africa: Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite Migrations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did the Nilotes reach East Africa, as the book tells it? (Sub lesson 1)
a) they came down the Nile  b) they sailed from Asia  c) they crossed the Sahara on camels  d) they never moved
**Answer: a) they came down the Nile** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** A person who leaves a country to live in another is called what in the place they arrive? (Sub lesson 2)
a) an immigrant  b) a tourist  c) a hero  d) an elder
**Answer: a) an immigrant** — That is what this lesson teaches.
