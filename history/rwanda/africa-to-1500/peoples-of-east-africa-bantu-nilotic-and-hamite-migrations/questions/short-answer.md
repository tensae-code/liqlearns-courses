# Peoples of East Africa: Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite Migrations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the peoples who settled East Africa in this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bantu farmers, Nilotes from the Nile, and Hamite and Nilo-Hamite pastoralists from Ethiopia's direction.

**2.** What does the teacher's guide send learners to do about the peoples of East Africa? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Research other Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite groups themselves, in library and internet sources.
