# Peoples of East Africa: Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite Migrations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Bantu settled in East Africa as farmers. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** People move only for war; trade and work never move anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — people move for trade, work and safety
