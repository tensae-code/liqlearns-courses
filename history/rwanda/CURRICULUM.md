# History — Rwanda course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Rwanda inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school
grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Rwanda's books in hand climb in the
country's own teaching order, and the ladder follows it. The
Primary 2 and 3 books (Kinyarwanda) begin where the child
stands — history is what happened in time past, found by
asking parents, the head teacher, elders and the Sector, and
set in order on the timeline (umurongo ndangagihe) with
Rwanda's own dates. The Primary 6 book then reaches back to
East Africa's peopling and its trading coast. The Primary 5
book tells colonisation through Rwanda's own kings — Musinga
exiled for refusing, Rudahigwa reforming and demanding
independence — to 1 July 1962. And the Primary 6 book closes
with independent Rwanda whole: its heroes, its two Republics,
the Liberation War, and the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi
compared with the world's other genocides. Four levels, in
the books' own order: finding history, the peoples before
colonial rule, colonisation and the road out, and independent
Rwanda. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced
gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — Rwanda's own
Rwanda Education Board (REB) Social Studies books — except
the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.
See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance
reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — First Steps: Finding History Where You Live** — 3 lessons: What History Is; Find History in Your Own Locality; Dating the Past.

2. **Level 2 — Peoples and the Coast: East Africa Before the Europeans Ruled** — 2 lessons: Peoples of East Africa: Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite Migrations; East African Coast: The Swahili Cities.

3. **Level 3 — Colonisation, Resistance and the Road to Independence** — 9 lessons: Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble; Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission; Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior; Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms; Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule; Rwanda Resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga; Why Some Africans Collaborated; Rwanda: Road to Independence; Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa.

4. **Level 4 — Independent Rwanda: Heroes, Republics, Liberation and Genocide** — 8 lessons: Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters; Rwanda: National Heroes and Heroes Day; Rwanda: Key Dates — Independence, Genocide and Liberation; Rwanda: First Republic 1962–1973; Rwanda: Second Republic 1973–1994; Rwanda: Liberation War 1990–1994; Rwanda: 1994 Genocide Against the Tutsi; The Holocaust and Other Genocides.

**Totals: 22 lessons, 39 placements stocked** (Levels: Level 1: 3, Level 2: 2, Level 3: 9, Level 4: 8).
