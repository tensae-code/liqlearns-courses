# History — Rwanda course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Rwanda's own —
built from the Rwanda Education Board's Social Studies books
for Primary 2, 3, 5 and 6 (Kinyarwanda, then English). Inside
the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what
the learner proves, step by step. The grade each book comes
from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': find history where you
live first, then East Africa's peoples and coast, then
colonisation through Rwanda's own kings to independence,
then independent Rwanda — heroes, republics, liberation and
genocide. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — First Steps: Finding History Where You Live**:

- `first-steps/` — What History Is; Find History in Your Own Locality; Dating the Past.

**Level 2 — Peoples and the Coast: East Africa Before the Europeans Ruled**:

- `africa-to-1500/` — Peoples of East Africa: Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite Migrations; East African Coast: The Swahili Cities.

**Level 3 — Colonisation, Resistance and the Road to Independence**:

- `africa-after-1500/` — Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble; Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission; Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior; Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms; Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule; Rwanda Resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga; Why Some Africans Collaborated; Rwanda: Road to Independence; Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa.

**Level 4 — Independent Rwanda: Heroes, Republics, Liberation and Genocide**:

- `first-steps/` — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters.
- `africa-after-1500/` — Rwanda: National Heroes and Heroes Day; Rwanda: Key Dates — Independence, Genocide and Liberation; Rwanda: First Republic 1962–1973; Rwanda: Second Republic 1973–1994; Rwanda: Liberation War 1990–1994; Rwanda: 1994 Genocide Against the Tutsi.
- `modern-world/` — The Holocaust and Other Genocides.
