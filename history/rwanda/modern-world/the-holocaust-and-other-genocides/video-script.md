# Video script — The Holocaust and Other Genocides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rwanda's pupils compare genocides to understand their own: Rwanda's 100 days, killing by machete, stopped by Rwandans themselves — set beside Hitler's killing of over 6 million Jews, Pol Pot's Cambodia, and Bosnia.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The Rwandan classroom looks outward, to measure its own catastrophe against the world's. Rwanda's genocide lasted about 100 days, killed with machetes at close range, and was stopped by Rwandans themselves — the RPF. Beside it stands the Holocaust: Hitler's organised killing of over 6 million Jews. Beside that, Pol Pot's Cambodia, where a government turned on its own people, and Bosnia, where massacre returned to Europe. Different countries, different weapons — the same crime, and the same question: how does a people let it happen, and how is it stopped?

Remember it this way: Rwanda's pupils compare genocides to understand their own: Rwanda's 100 days, killing by machete, stopped by Rwandans themselves.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
