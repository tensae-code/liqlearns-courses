# The Holocaust and Other Genocides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three other genocides Rwanda's pupils compare theirs with.
**Answer:** The Holocaust, Pol Pot's Cambodia, and Bosnia.

**2.** How was Rwanda's killing done, and how does that differ from the Holocaust as compared here?
**Answer:** In Rwanda the killing was done with machetes at close range over about 100 days; the Holocaust was Hitler's organised killing of over 6 million Jews.
