# The Holocaust and Other Genocides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How long did Rwanda's genocide last, as the comparison teaches it?
a) about 100 days  b) ten years  c) one week  d) thirty years
**Answer: a) about 100 days** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How many Jews were killed in the Holocaust, as the book states it?
a) over 6 million  b) about 100,000  c) one million exactly  d) the book gives no number
**Answer: a) over 6 million** — That is what this lesson teaches.
