# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The timeline in Kinyarwanda is called umurongo ndangagihe. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** On the timeline, the time to come stands behind us. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — the time to come stands ahead; the past is behind us and today is in the middle
