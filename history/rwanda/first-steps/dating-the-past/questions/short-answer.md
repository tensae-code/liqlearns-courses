# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was the boy in the story stuck before he learned the timeline? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** He could not order events — he did not know which came first and which came after.

**2.** What can you read off once birth years are placed on the line? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Who came first, how long ago things happened, and what each marked year means.
