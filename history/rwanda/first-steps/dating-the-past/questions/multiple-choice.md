# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the three parts of the timeline? (Sub lesson 1)
a) past, today, and time to come  b) kings, wars and heroes  c) home, school and Sector  d) morning, noon and night
**Answer: a) past, today, and time to come** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What do pupils place on the timeline when they use it? (Sub lesson 2)
a) their own and their parents' birth years  b) only kings' names  c) the weather  d) nothing — they only look
**Answer: a) their own and their parents' birth years** — That is what this lesson teaches.
