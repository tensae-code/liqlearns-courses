# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is what happened in time past — and knowing it shows where you come from and where you are going; the past holds both good things to copy, like heroes and festivals, and bad things to avoid, like wars and famine.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

A class sits down to its first history lesson and learns the word itself: history is what happened in time past — the things people did before today. Then the class practises the historian's first job, sorting. Past events are held up one by one and judged: heroes and festivals go on the good side; wars and famine go on the bad side. History, the children learn, is not just old stories — it is the past, sorted so we can learn from it.

The teacher's guidance deepens the same lesson: pupils are led to say in their own words that history is what happened in past time, and then to say why it matters. Knowing the past shows you where you come from — and where you are going. The guide asks the teacher to know Rwanda's history well, especially the history of the school's own locality, so the children's first history is true history, close to home.

Remember it this way: History is what happened in time past.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
