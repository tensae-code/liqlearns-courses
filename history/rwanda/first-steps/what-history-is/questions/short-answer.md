# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is history? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** History is what happened in time past — the things people did before today.

**2.** Name one good thing and one bad thing from the past, as the class sorted them. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Good: heroes (or festivals). Bad: wars (or famine).
