# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Heroes and festivals are sorted as good things in the past. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** History is only about bad things like wars and famine. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the class sorts the past into good and bad — heroes and festivals stand on the good side
