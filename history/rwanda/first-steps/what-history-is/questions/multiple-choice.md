# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is history? (Sub lesson 1)
a) what happened in time past  b) only stories about kings  c) the news of today  d) a list of dates to memorise
**Answer: a) what happened in time past** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does knowing history matter, as the teacher's guidance teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) it shows where you come from and where you are going  b) it makes tests easier only  c) it is only for old people  d) it replaces all other subjects
**Answer: a) it shows where you come from and where you are going** — That is what this lesson teaches.
