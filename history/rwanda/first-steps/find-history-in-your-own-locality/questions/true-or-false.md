# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils visit the head teacher to ask when the school was built and who built it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** After interviewing leaders, pupils keep what they heard to themselves. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — the guidance has pupils explain what they heard back to the whole class
