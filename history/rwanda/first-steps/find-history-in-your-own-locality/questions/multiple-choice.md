# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where do the children first look for history? (Sub lesson 1)
a) at home, asking their parents  b) in a faraway country  c) only in old books  d) on television
**Answer: a) at home, asking their parents** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who do pupils question about the Sector's history? (Sub lesson 4)
a) elders who lived there long and the Sector office  b) only other children  c) travellers passing through  d) no one — they guess
**Answer: a) elders who lived there long and the Sector office** — That is what this lesson teaches.
