# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things parents said made their place known. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: rich harvests, honest heroes, dancers, people who work together.

**2.** What four questions do pupils ask the head teacher about the school? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** When was it built? Who built it? Who led it first? What trophies has it won?
