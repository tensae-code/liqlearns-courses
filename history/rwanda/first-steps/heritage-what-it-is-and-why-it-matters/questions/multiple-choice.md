# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the three categories of Rwandan heroes?
a) Imanzi, Imena and Ingenzi  b) Abami, Akazu and Inyenzi  c) MSM, UNAR and RADER  d) Imana, Imena and Ingabo
**Answer: a) Imanzi, Imena and Ingenzi** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When does Rwanda remember its heroes?
a) every 1 February  b) every 1 July  c) every 4 July  d) only in wartime
**Answer: a) every 1 February** — That is what this lesson teaches.
