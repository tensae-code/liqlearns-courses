# Video script — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A country's heritage of courage has names and ranks: Rwanda sorts its heroes into Imanzi, Imena and Ingenzi, remembers them every 1 February, and counts among them Rwigema, the Unknown Soldier, and the Nyange school students who in 1997 refused to split by ethnic group and were killed.

Look at the map. We are in Rwanda — and, where the story widens, in East Africa around it. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Rwanda keeps its heroes the way other countries keep treasure — sorted, named and honoured on a fixed day. The heroes stand in three companies: Imanzi, Imena and Ingenzi. Every 1 February the country remembers them together. In their ranks stand Rwigema, who led the liberation, and the Unknown Soldier, who stands for every fighter whose name was lost. And there are schoolchildren among them: the Nyange school students, who in 1997 were ordered to split themselves by ethnic group, refused — and were killed for refusing. Heritage, this lesson teaches, is courage remembered on purpose.

Remember it this way: A country's heritage of courage has names and ranks: Rwanda sorts its heroes into Imanzi, Imena and Ingenzi, remembers them every 1 February, and counts among them Rwigema, the Unknown Soldier, and the Nyange school students who in 1997 refused to split by ethnic group and were killed..

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
