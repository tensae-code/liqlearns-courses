# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Houses, dress, food and festivals on four continents show how ordinary people lived through the centuries of discovery and empire. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Factory towns, railways, schools and newspapers change how Western families live, dress, read and celebrate.
