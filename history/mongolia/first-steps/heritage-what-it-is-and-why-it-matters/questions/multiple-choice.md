# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. II.8: Life and culture of the world's peoples in the 15th–18th c.? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Houses, dress, food and festivals on four continents show how ordinary people lived through the centuries of discovery and empire  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Houses, dress, food and festivals on four continents show how ordinary people lived through the centuries of discovery and empire** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. II.7: Life and culture of Western countries in the 18th–early 20th c.? (Sub lesson 2)

**Answer: a) Factory towns, railways, schools and newspapers change how Western families live, dress, read and celebrate** — That is what this lesson teaches.
