# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** One timeline runs from five khans in twelve years (1388–1400) through Dayan Khan's enthronement, Ligdan Khan's fall, the Dolon Nor submission of 1691 and the Nerchinsk and Kyakhta treaties to the first Russian consulate and telegraph. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: The Middle Ages run from Rome's fall in 476 to the 15th c.; pupils convert years to centuries and meet the eras peoples dated by — creation (5508 BC), the Hijra (622), the Mongolian lunar count (from 1027) and Chinggis's birth (1162).
