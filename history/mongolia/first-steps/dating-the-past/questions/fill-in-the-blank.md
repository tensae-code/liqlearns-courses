# Dating the Past — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** One timeline runs from five khans in twelve years (____–1400) through Dayan Khan's enthronement, Ligdan Khan's fall, the Dolon Nor submission of 1691 and the Nerchinsk and Kyakhta treaties to the first Russian consulate and telegraph. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1388

**2.** The ____ Ages run from Rome's fall in 476 to the 15th c.; pupils convert years to centuries and meet the eras peoples dated by — creation (5508 BC), the Hijra (622), the Mongolian lunar count (from 1027) and Chinggis's birth (1162). (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Middle
