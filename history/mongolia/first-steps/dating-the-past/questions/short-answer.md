**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Overview timeline spread: Mongolia and the world, 15th–early 20th c.. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** One timeline runs from five khans in twelve years (1388–1400) through Dayan Khan's enthronement, Ligdan Khan's fall, the Dolon Nor submission of 1691 and the Nerchinsk and Kyakhta treaties to the first Russian consulate and telegraph.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 1.1 What will we study in medieval history?. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Middle Ages run from Rome's fall in 476 to the 15th c.; pupils convert years to centuries and meet the eras peoples dated by — creation (5508 BC), the Hijra (622), the Mongolian lunar count (from 1027) and Chinggis's birth (1162).
