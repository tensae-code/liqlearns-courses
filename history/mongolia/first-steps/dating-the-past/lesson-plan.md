## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Overview timeline spread: Mongolia and the world, 15th–early 20th c.** — One timeline runs from five khans in twelve years (1388–1400) through Dayan Khan's enthronement, Ligdan Khan's fall, the Dolon Nor submission of 1691 and the Nerchinsk and Kyakhta treaties to the first Russian consulate and telegraph.
2. **Ch. 1.1 What will we study in medieval history?** — The Middle Ages run from Rome's fall in 476 to the 15th c.; pupils convert years to centuries and meet the eras peoples dated by — creation (5508 BC), the Hijra (622), the Mongolian lunar count (from 1027) and Chinggis's birth (1162).
3. **Ch. 1.1 How to study modern Mongolian history** — Pupils build the period timeline themselves — modern Mongolian history begins in 1911 and falls into three periods (1911–1924, 1924–1990, 1990–today) whose boundaries they must justify.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
