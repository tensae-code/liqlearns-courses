# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Opening section: how to study Mongolia and the world in the new era (15th–early 20th c.), with statesman A. Amar's call to know the national history. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** History has no body without its sources; pupils learn to rebuild the past from written records and to draw lessons from it for today's problems.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 1.1 "When, where, what?", opening story "The secret under the ice". (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hikers find Ötzi in the Alps in 1991; scientists, working like detectives on his body, clothes and the arrowhead in his shoulder, rebuild a 5,200-year-old herdsman's last day — and the pupil learns to ask when, where and what happened.
