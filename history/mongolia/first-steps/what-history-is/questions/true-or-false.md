# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History has no body without its sources; pupils learn to rebuild the past from written records and to draw lessons from it for today's problems. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Hikers find Ötzi in the Alps in 1991; scientists, working like detectives on his body, clothes and the arrowhead in his shoulder, rebuild a 5,200-year-old herdsman's last day — and the pupil learns to ask when, where and what happened.
