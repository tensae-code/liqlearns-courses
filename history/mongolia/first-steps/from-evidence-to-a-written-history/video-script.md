# Video script — From Evidence to a Written History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The past speaks only through what it leaves behind — tools, bones, ruins, writing — and the historian's craft is to question each trace before trusting it; Castles, coins, contracts and chronicles are the medieval world's leavings; a worked example reads the Secret History's verse and Guillaume de Rubruck's silver-tree fountain at Karakorum as evidence.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: The past speaks only through what it leaves behind — tools, bones, ruins, writing — and the historian's craft is to question each trace before trusting it.

Then: Castles, coins, contracts and chronicles are the medieval world's leavings; a worked example reads the Secret History's verse and Guillaume de Rubruck's silver-tree fountain at Karakorum as evidence.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
