# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The past speaks only through what it leaves behind — tools, bones, ruins, writing — and the historian's craft is to question each trace before trusting it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Castles, coins, contracts and chronicles are the medieval world's leavings; a worked example reads the Secret History's verse and Guillaume de Rubruck's silver-tree fountain at Karakorum as evidence.
