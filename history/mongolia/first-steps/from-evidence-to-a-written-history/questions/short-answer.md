# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 1.2 "How do we know history?". (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The past speaks only through what it leaves behind — tools, bones, ruins, writing — and the historian's craft is to question each trace before trusting it.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 1.2 How do we study medieval history?. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Castles, coins, contracts and chronicles are the medieval world's leavings; a worked example reads the Secret History's verse and Guillaume de Rubruck's silver-tree fountain at Karakorum as evidence.
