# History — Mongolia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Mongolia's own — built from Mongolia's History and Social Studies student books (Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Sports, Mongolian). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grade each book comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the historian's craft first, then the ancient world bending back to the steppe, the medieval world before the Chinggis hinge, Chinggis and the empire, Mongolia and the world interleaved from the 15th century, and last the modern arc — restored, socialist, democratic. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
