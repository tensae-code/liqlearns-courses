# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** For almost three million years people live by hunting and gathering, chipping stone tools and taming fire, spreading slowly across the earth. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: People learn to herd animals and sow grain; producers replace foragers, villages grow, and life can be stored against the winter.
