# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.1 Early humans: the foraging age. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** For almost three million years people live by hunting and gathering, chipping stone tools and taming fire, spreading slowly across the earth.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.2 How did early humans' economy change?. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** People learn to herd animals and sow grain; producers replace foragers, villages grow, and life can be stored against the winter.
