# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 2.1 Early humans: the foraging age? (Sub lesson 1)
a) For almost three million years people live by hunting and gathering, chipping stone tools and taming fire, spreading slowly across the earth  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) For almost three million years people live by hunting and gathering, chipping stone tools and taming fire, spreading slowly across the earth** — That is what this lesson teaches.
