# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Nile's flood makes one kingdom under the pharaohs; pyramids, the sphinx and mummies carry Egypt's story down five thousand years.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: The Nile's flood makes one kingdom under the pharaohs; pyramids, the sphinx and mummies carry Egypt's story down five thousand years.
