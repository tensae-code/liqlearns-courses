# Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.6 The Byzantine Empire. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Constantinople keeps Rome's eastern half alive for a thousand years — emperors, Orthodoxy, and trade between three continents.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 5.6 The fall of the Byzantine Empire. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In 1453 Mehmed II's cannon break Constantinople's walls; the thousand-year empire ends and its scholars flee west.
