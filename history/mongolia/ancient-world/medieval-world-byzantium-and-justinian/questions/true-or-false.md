# Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Constantinople keeps Rome's eastern half alive for a thousand years — emperors, Orthodoxy, and trade between three continents. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: In 1453 Mehmed II's cannon break Constantinople's walls; the thousand-year empire ends and its scholars flee west.
