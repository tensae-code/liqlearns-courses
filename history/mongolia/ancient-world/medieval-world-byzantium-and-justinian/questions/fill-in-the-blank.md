# Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ keeps Rome's eastern half alive for a thousand years — emperors, Orthodoxy, and trade between three continents. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Constantinople

**2.** In ____ Mehmed II's cannon break Constantinople's walls; the thousand-year empire ends and its scholars flee west. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1453
