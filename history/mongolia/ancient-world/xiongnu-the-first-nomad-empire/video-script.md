# Video script — Xiongnu: The First Nomad Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In 209 BC Modu unites the steppe tribes into the Xiongnu empire, the first great nomad state, which treats and wars with Han China as an equal; Modu's Xiongnu (209 BC) organises the steppe into a decimal military state, builds towns and crafts, and faces Han China as an equal empire.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: In 209 BC Modu unites the steppe tribes into the Xiongnu empire, the first great nomad state, which treats and wars with Han China as an equal.

Then: Modu's Xiongnu (209 BC) organises the steppe into a decimal military state, builds towns and crafts, and faces Han China as an equal empire.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
