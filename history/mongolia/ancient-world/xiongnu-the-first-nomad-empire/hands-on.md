# Hands-on — Xiongnu: The First Nomad Empire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: In 209 BC Modu unites the steppe tribes into the Xiongnu empire, the first great nomad state, which treats and wars with Han China as an equal; Modu's Xiongnu (209 BC) organises the steppe into a decimal military state, builds towns and crafts, and faces Han China as an equal empire. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw the story as a timeline or a simple map: mark where it happens, who is there, the turn, and what came of it. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's pattern in real life — at home, at school, in the street: a place, a group or a rule changing the way this story's turn changed things. Note what happened, who was there, and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
