# Xiongnu: The First Nomad Empire

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Xiongnu: The First Nomad Empire. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 5.1 The Xiongnu empire, the nomads' first state

In 209 BC Modu unites the steppe tribes into the Xiongnu empire, the first great nomad state, which treats and wars with Han China as an equal. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: when a story has many parts, give each part one short name of your own and say the names in order — the whole story will stay in your head.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 2.1 The Xiongnu empire

Modu's Xiongnu (209 BC) organises the steppe into a decimal military state, builds towns and crafts, and faces Han China as an equal empire. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.
