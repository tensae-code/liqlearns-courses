# Xiongnu: The First Nomad Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 5.1 The Xiongnu empire, the nomads' first state. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 209 BC Modu unites the steppe tribes into the Xiongnu empire, the first great nomad state, which treats and wars with Han China as an equal.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.1 The Xiongnu empire. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Modu's Xiongnu (209 BC) organises the steppe into a decimal military state, builds towns and crafts, and faces Han China as an equal empire.
