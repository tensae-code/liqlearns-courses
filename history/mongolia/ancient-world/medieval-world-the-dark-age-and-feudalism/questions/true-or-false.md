# Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kings grant land to lords, lords to knights, peasants work it — the feudal ladder orders everyone's life and obligations. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Castle, manor and village — how the feudal order fed, taxed and judged the peasants who were most of medieval Europe.
