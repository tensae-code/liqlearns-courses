# Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 5.2 The Xianbei and Rouran states

After the Xiongnu fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: when a story has many parts, give each part one short name of your own and say the names in order — the whole story will stay in your head.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 2.2 The states after the Xiongnu

Xianbei, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.
