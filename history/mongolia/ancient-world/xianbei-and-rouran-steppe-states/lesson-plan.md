# Lesson plan — Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States

**Course:** History — Mongolia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World and the First Steppe States (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the Xiongnu fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive; Xianbei, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World and the First Steppe States — it stands on the levels before it in this course as a recommended path (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
