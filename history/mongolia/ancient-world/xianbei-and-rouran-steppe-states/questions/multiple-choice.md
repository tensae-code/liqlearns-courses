# Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 5.2 The Xianbei and Rouran states? (Sub lesson 1)
a) After the Xiongnu fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) After the Xiongnu fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 2.2 The states after the Xiongnu? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Xianbei, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Xianbei, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition** — That is what this lesson teaches.
