# Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the Xiongnu fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Xianbei, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition.
