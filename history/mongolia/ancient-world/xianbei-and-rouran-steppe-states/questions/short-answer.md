# Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 5.2 The Xianbei and Rouran states. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After the Xiongnu fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.2 The states after the Xiongnu. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Xianbei, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition.
