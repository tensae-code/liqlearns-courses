# Xianbei and Rouran: Steppe States — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** After the ____ fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Xiongnu

**2.** ____, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Xianbei
