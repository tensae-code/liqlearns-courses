# Mongolia Before Chinggis: Peoples and States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mongol-speaking tribes drift west and settle the Onon, Kherlen and Tuul river valleys, the homeland from which a nation will be made. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: The Khamag Mongol confederation and its khans hold the eastern steppe, fighting and trading with the Khitan Liao and Jin empires.
