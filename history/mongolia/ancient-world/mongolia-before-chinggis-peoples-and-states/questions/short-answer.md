# Mongolia Before Chinggis: Peoples and States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.1 The Mongols' migration and settlement. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mongol-speaking tribes drift west and settle the Onon, Kherlen and Tuul river valleys, the homeland from which a nation will be made.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.2 Mongol aimags and states, 6th–12th c.. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Khamag Mongol confederation and its khans hold the eastern steppe, fighting and trading with the Khitan Liao and Jin empires.
