# Video script — Mongolia Before Chinggis: Peoples and States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mongol-speaking tribes drift west and settle the Onon, Kherlen and Tuul river valleys, the homeland from which a nation will be made; The Khamag Mongol confederation and its khans hold the eastern steppe, fighting and trading with the Khitan Liao and Jin empires.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Mongol-speaking tribes drift west and settle the Onon, Kherlen and Tuul river valleys, the homeland from which a nation will be made.

Then: The Khamag Mongol confederation and its khans hold the eastern steppe, fighting and trading with the Khitan Liao and Jin empires.

Then: Chinggis's own Borjigin line descends through the Darligin and Nirugun clans; their feuds and marriages set the board his unification will overturn.

Then: Khamag Mongol khans, Tatar, Kereit, Naiman and Merkit rivals — a crowded steppe of feuding aimags awaits a unifier.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
