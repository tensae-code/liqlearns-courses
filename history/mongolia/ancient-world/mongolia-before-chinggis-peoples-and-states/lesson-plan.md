## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 2.1 The Mongols' migration and settlement** — Mongol-speaking tribes drift west and settle the Onon, Kherlen and Tuul river valleys, the homeland from which a nation will be made.
2. **Ch. 2.2 Mongol aimags and states, 6th–12th c.** — The Khamag Mongol confederation and its khans hold the eastern steppe, fighting and trading with the Khitan Liao and Jin empires.
3. **Ch. 2.3 The Darligin and Nirugun aimags** — Chinggis's own Borjigin line descends through the Darligin and Nirugun clans; their feuds and marriages set the board his unification will overturn.
4. **Ch. 3.1 The Mongols before Chinggis Khan** — Khamag Mongol khans, Tatar, Kereit, Naiman and Merkit rivals — a crowded steppe of feuding aimags awaits a unifier.
