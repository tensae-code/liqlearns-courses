# First Settlements and the Birth of Civilisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Beside the great rivers people settle for good, raise the first towns, and divide work between farmer, herder, craftworker and ruler. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Writing, the wheel, irrigation and laws appear; a settled people's inventions become the pattern every later civilisation inherits.
