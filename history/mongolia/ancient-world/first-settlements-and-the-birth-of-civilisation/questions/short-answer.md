# First Settlements and the Birth of Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.1 Where and how did people first settle?. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Beside the great rivers people settle for good, raise the first towns, and divide work between farmer, herder, craftworker and ruler.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.2 How did civilisation develop?. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Writing, the wheel, irrigation and laws appear; a settled people's inventions become the pattern every later civilisation inherits.
