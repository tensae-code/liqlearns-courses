# Video script — First Settlements and the Birth of Civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Beside the great rivers people settle for good, raise the first towns, and divide work between farmer, herder, craftworker and ruler; Writing, the wheel, irrigation and laws appear; a settled people's inventions become the pattern every later civilisation inherits.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Beside the great rivers people settle for good, raise the first towns, and divide work between farmer, herder, craftworker and ruler.

Then: Writing, the wheel, irrigation and laws appear; a settled people's inventions become the pattern every later civilisation inherits.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
