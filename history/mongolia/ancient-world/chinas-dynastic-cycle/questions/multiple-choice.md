# China's Dynastic Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 3.10 The Tang and Song states?
a) Tang reunites China in splendour, Song follows with printing, paper money and gunpowder — the rich neighbour the Mongols will conquer  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Tang reunites China in splendour, Song follows with printing, paper money and gunpowder — the rich neighbour the Mongols will conquer** — That is what this lesson teaches.
