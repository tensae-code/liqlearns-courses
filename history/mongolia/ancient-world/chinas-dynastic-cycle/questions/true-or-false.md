# China's Dynastic Cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tang reunites China in splendour, Song follows with printing, paper money and gunpowder — the rich neighbour the Mongols will conquer.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Tang reunites China in splendour, Song follows with printing, paper money and gunpowder — the rich neighbour the Mongols will conquer.
