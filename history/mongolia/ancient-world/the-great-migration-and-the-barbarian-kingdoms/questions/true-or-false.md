# The Great Migration and the Barbarian Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Huns, Goths, Vandals and Franks pour into the Roman world; the West breaks into barbarian kingdoms on Rome's ruins.
