**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 3.7 The Russians found their state?
a) Slavs, Varangians and Byzantine faith combine in Kievan Rus', the first East Slav state, baptised in 988  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Slavs, Varangians and Byzantine faith combine in Kievan Rus', the first East Slav state, baptised in 988** — That is what this lesson teaches.
