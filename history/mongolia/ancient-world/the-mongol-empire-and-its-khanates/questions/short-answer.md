# The Mongol Empire and Its Khanates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.2.1 Ögödei Khan consolidates the Great Mongol Empire. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ögödei builds Karakorum's walls, extends the yam and the law, and sends the armies that take Rus' and Hungary.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.2.2 The empire under Güyük and Möngke Khans. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Regencies and succession struggles end with Möngke, whose census, religious tolerance and world-embracing court make the empire a single system.
