# The Mongol Empire and Its Khanates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ögödei builds Karakorum's walls, extends the yam and the law, and sends the armies that take Rus' and Hungary. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Regencies and succession struggles end with Möngke, whose census, religious tolerance and world-embracing court make the empire a single system.
