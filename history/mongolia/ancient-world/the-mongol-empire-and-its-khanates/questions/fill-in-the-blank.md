# The Mongol Empire and Its Khanates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Ögödei builds ____ walls, extends the yam and the law, and sends the armies that take Rus' and Hungary. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Karakorum's

**2.** Regencies and succession struggles end with ____, whose census, religious tolerance and world-embracing court make the empire a single system. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Möngke
