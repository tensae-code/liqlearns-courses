## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Mongolian Life and Culture. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.
