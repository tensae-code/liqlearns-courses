# Mongolian Life and Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.4 The Mongols' life and culture (6th–12th c.). (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Felt gers, herds, shamanist rites and the first Mongol scripts — the daily world the Secret History will later describe.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.1.4 The Mongols' life and culture (13th c.). (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herding, hunting, felt and milk foods, Tengri and shaman rites — the ordinary world inside the empire's camps and at Karakorum.
