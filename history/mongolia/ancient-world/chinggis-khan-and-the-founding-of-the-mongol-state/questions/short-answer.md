# Chinggis Khan and the Founding of the Mongol State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.1.1 The Chinggis Khan we know. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The orphaned Temüjin of the Secret History — his family captured, his wife abducted and rescued — becomes the man who will unite the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.1.2 Chinggis Khan founds the Great Mongol State. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In 1206 the Onon kurultai proclaims Chinggis Khan over all the felt-walled tribes; the Great Mongol State (Yeke Mongγol Ulus) is born.
