# Chinggis Khan and the Founding of the Mongol State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The orphaned Temüjin of the Secret History — his family captured, his wife abducted and rescued — becomes the man who will unite the steppe. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: In 1206 the Onon kurultai proclaims Chinggis Khan over all the felt-walled tribes; the Great Mongol State (Yeke Mongγol Ulus) is born.
