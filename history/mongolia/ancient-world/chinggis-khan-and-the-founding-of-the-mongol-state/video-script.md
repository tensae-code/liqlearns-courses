# Video script — Chinggis Khan and the Founding of the Mongol State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The orphaned Temüjin of the Secret History — his family captured, his wife abducted and rescued — becomes the man who will unite the steppe; In 1206 the Onon kurultai proclaims Chinggis Khan over all the felt-walled tribes; the Great Mongol State (Yeke Mongγol Ulus) is born.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: The orphaned Temüjin of the Secret History — his family captured, his wife abducted and rescued — becomes the man who will unite the steppe.

Then: In 1206 the Onon kurultai proclaims Chinggis Khan over all the felt-walled tribes; the Great Mongol State (Yeke Mongγol Ulus) is born.

Then: The Yassa law code, the decimal army, the Uyghur-derived script and the yam relay bind the new state together before the conquests begin.

Then: The book weighs his legacy — one people, one script, one law and a state tradition every later Mongol age claims descent from.

Then: 1206's kurultai, the conquests of Jin, Khwarezm and Rus', the Yassa, the script and the yam — the state Chinggis built, studied through its sources.

Then: Conqueror or state-founder? The chapter weighs his destruction against the Pax Mongolica, the relay roads and the single Eurasian world he opened.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
