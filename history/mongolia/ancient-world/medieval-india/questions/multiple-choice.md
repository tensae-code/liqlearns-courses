Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 3.11 Medieval India?
a) Gupta splendour gives way to regional kingdoms and then the Delhi Sultanate, while Hindu and Muslim cultures meet on the subcontinent  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Gupta splendour gives way to regional kingdoms and then the Delhi Sultanate, while Hindu and Muslim cultures meet on the subcontinent** — That is what this lesson teaches.
