# Mongolia and the Turkic Khaganates, 6th–10th Centuries

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Mongolia and the Turkic Khaganates, 6th–10th Centuries. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

Turkic and Uyghur khaganates rule the steppe and raise cities like Khar Balgas while the Mongol tribes gather strength in the east. That is the whole story of this lesson: who is there, where it happens, what turns, and what comes of it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: tell the story back to yourself once, in your own words, before you answer any question — if you can tell it, you know it.
