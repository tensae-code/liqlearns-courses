# Mongolia: MPR Education, Culture and Welfare — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From temple schools to universal literacy — schools in every sum, Cyrillic script, and a university in 1942. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Theatres, film, publishing and a new national art are built — under the party's watchful eye.
