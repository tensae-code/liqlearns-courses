# Video script — Mongolia: MPR Education, Culture and Welfare

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From temple schools to universal literacy — schools in every sum, Cyrillic script, and a university in 1942; Theatres, film, publishing and a new national art are built — under the party's watchful eye.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: From temple schools to universal literacy — schools in every sum, Cyrillic script, and a university in 1942.

Then: Theatres, film, publishing and a new national art are built — under the party's watchful eye.

Then: Hospitals, vaccinations and pensions reach the steppe for the first time; population and life expectancy climb.

Then: Universal schooling (89.3% literacy by 1970, a UNESCO prize), universities, film, theatre and Olympic medals from 1964 — the socialist decades' real cultural harvest.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
