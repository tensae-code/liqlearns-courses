# Mongolia: Building Socialism, Plans and Negdels — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. III.7 The attempt to build socialism in the MPR. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1940 constitution, five-year plans from 1948, the railway, Darkhan and Erdenet, negdel collectivisation completed by 1959 — and the stagnation of the 1980s.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. III.8 Changes in the MPR's society and economy. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1956's reckoning with the personality cult, the Atar virgin-lands campaign, the Academy of Sciences (1961) and a cosmonaut in 1981 — a modern society inside an authoritarian frame.
