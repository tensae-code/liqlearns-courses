# Mongolia: Building Socialism, Plans and Negdels — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1940 constitution, five-year plans from 1948, the railway, Darkhan and Erdenet, negdel collectivisation completed by 1959 — and the stagnation of the 1980s. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: 1956's reckoning with the personality cult, the Atar virgin-lands campaign, the Academy of Sciences (1961) and a cosmonaut in 1981 — a modern society inside an authoritarian frame.
