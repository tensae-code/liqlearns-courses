# Video script — Mongolia: Building Socialism, Plans and Negdels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The 1940 constitution, five-year plans from 1948, the railway, Darkhan and Erdenet, negdel collectivisation completed by 1959 — and the stagnation of the 1980s; 1956's reckoning with the personality cult, the Atar virgin-lands campaign, the Academy of Sciences (1961) and a cosmonaut in 1981 — a modern society inside an authoritarian frame.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: The 1940 constitution, five-year plans from 1948, the railway, Darkhan and Erdenet, negdel collectivisation completed by 1959 — and the stagnation of the 1980s.

Then: 1956's reckoning with the personality cult, the Atar virgin-lands campaign, the Academy of Sciences (1961) and a cosmonaut in 1981 — a modern society inside an authoritarian frame.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
