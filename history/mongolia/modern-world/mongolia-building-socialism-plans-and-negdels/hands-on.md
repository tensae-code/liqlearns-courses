# Hands-on — Mongolia: Building Socialism, Plans and Negdels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: The 1940 constitution, five-year plans from 1948, the railway, Darkhan and Erdenet, negdel collectivisation completed by 1959 — and the stagnation of the 1980s; 1956's reckoning with the personality cult, the Atar virgin-lands campaign, the Academy of Sciences (1961) and a cosmonaut in 1981 — a modern society inside an authoritarian frame. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw the story as a timeline or a simple map: mark where it happens, who is there, the turn, and what came of it. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's pattern in real life — at home, at school, in the street: a place, a group or a rule changing the way this story's turn changed things. Note what happened, who was there, and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
