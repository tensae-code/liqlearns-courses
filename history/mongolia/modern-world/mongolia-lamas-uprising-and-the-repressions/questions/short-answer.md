# Mongolia: Lamas' Uprising and the Repressions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.9 The lamas' "counter-revolutionary uprising". (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1932 monks and herders rise against collectivisation and monastery closures across the western aimags; the rising is crushed with Soviet help.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.10 The dark spot in Mongolian history. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1937–39 terror executes tens of thousands — lamas, nobles, Buryats, intellectuals — a wound the book names plainly as the black page of the national story.
