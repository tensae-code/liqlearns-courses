# Mongolia: Lamas' Uprising and the Repressions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In ____ monks and herders rise against collectivisation and monastery closures across the western aimags; the rising is crushed with Soviet help. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1932

**2.** The ____–39 terror executes tens of thousands — lamas, nobles, Buryats, intellectuals — a wound the book names plainly as the black page of the national story. (Sub lesson 2)
