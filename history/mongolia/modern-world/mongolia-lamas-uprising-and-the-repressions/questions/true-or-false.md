# Mongolia: Lamas' Uprising and the Repressions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1932 monks and herders rise against collectivisation and monastery closures across the western aimags; the rising is crushed with Soviet help. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: The 1937–39 terror executes tens of thousands — lamas, nobles, Buryats, intellectuals — a wound the book names plainly as the black page of the national story.
