# Mongolia After the Empire: Fragmentation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the empire breaks, khans rise and fall in quick succession and the aimags pull apart while Ming China presses from the south. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: The seven-year-old Dayan Khan is raised on Chinggis's throne before the Eight White Gers and reunites the Mongols; a century later Altan Khan founds Khökh Khot and in 1577 receives the title Dalai Lama from Sonam Gyatso.
