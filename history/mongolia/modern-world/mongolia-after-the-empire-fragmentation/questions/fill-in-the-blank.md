# Mongolia After the Empire: Fragmentation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** After the empire breaks, khans rise and fall in quick succession and the aimags pull apart while ____ China presses from the south. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ming

**2.** The seven-year-old ____ Khan is raised on Chinggis's throne before the Eight White Gers and reunites the Mongols; a century later Altan Khan founds Khökh Khot and in 1577 receives the title Dalai Lama from Sonam Gyatso. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Dayan
