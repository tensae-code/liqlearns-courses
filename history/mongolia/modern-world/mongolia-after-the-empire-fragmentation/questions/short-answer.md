# Mongolia After the Empire: Fragmentation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Part II, Ch. I.1: Mongolia in the 15th–18th centuries. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After the empire breaks, khans rise and fall in quick succession and the aimags pull apart while Ming China presses from the south.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. I.2: The struggle to restore united power. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The seven-year-old Dayan Khan is raised on Chinggis's throne before the Eight White Gers and reunites the Mongols; a century later Altan Khan founds Khökh Khot and in 1577 receives the title Dalai Lama from Sonam Gyatso.
