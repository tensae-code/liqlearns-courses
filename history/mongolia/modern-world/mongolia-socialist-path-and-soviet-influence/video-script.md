# Video script — Mongolia: Socialist Path and Soviet Influence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Comintern steers Mongolia onto the "non-capitalist road"; Soviet advisers, trade and example shape every choice of the young republic; On the Bogd's death in 1924 the First Great Khural proclaims the Mongolian People's Republic and adopts its first constitution.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: The Comintern steers Mongolia onto the "non-capitalist road"; Soviet advisers, trade and example shape every choice of the young republic.

Then: On the Bogd's death in 1924 the First Great Khural proclaims the Mongolian People's Republic and adopts its first constitution.

Then: The 1924 First Great Khural proclaims the People's Republic; the constitution promises equal citizens and abolishes noble and church privilege.

Then: National democrats (Tserendorj, Dambadorj, Amar) argue for a Mongolian road while the Comintern pushes Bolshevisation; the "left deviation" of 1929-32 — confiscations and forced communes — is the conflict's climax.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
