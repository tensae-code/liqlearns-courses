## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 3.1 Mongolia's development orientation and the USSR's influence** — The Comintern steers Mongolia onto the "non-capitalist road"; Soviet advisers, trade and example shape every choice of the young republic.
2. **Ch. 3.2 The transition to a republic** — On the Bogd's death in 1924 the First Great Khural proclaims the Mongolian People's Republic and adopts its first constitution.
3. **Ch. III.1 Mongolia's first Constitution** — The 1924 First Great Khural proclaims the People's Republic; the constitution promises equal citizens and abolishes noble and church privilege.
4. **Ch. III.2 Development orientations and conflicts** — National democrats (Tserendorj, Dambadorj, Amar) argue for a Mongolian road while the Comintern pushes Bolshevisation; the "left deviation" of 1929-32 — confiscations and forced communes — is the conflict's climax.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
