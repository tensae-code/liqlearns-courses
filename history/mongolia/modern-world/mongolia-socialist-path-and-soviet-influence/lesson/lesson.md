## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 3.1 Mongolia's development orientation and the USSR's influence

The Comintern steers Mongolia onto the "non-capitalist road"; Soviet advisers, trade and example shape every choice of the young republic. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

The 1924 First Great Khural proclaims the People's Republic; the constitution promises equal citizens and abolishes noble and church privilege. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Ch. III.2 Development orientations and conflicts

National democrats (Tserendorj, Dambadorj, Amar) argue for a Mongolian road while the Comintern pushes Bolshevisation; the "left deviation" of 1929-32 — confiscations and forced communes — is the conflict's climax.
