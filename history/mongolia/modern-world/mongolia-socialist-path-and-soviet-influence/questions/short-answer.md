# Mongolia: Socialist Path and Soviet Influence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.1 Mongolia's development orientation and the USSR's influence. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Comintern steers Mongolia onto the "non-capitalist road"; Soviet advisers, trade and example shape every choice of the young republic.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.2 The transition to a republic. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On the Bogd's death in 1924 the First Great Khural proclaims the Mongolian People's Republic and adopts its first constitution.
