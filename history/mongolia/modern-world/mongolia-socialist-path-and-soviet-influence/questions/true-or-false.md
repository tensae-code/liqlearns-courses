# Mongolia: Socialist Path and Soviet Influence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Comintern steers Mongolia onto the "non-capitalist road"; Soviet advisers, trade and example shape every choice of the young republic. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: On the Bogd's death in 1924 the First Great Khural proclaims the Mongolian People's Republic and adopts its first constitution.
