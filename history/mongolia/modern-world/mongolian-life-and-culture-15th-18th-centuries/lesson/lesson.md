## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. I.6: Life and culture of the Mongols in the 15th–18th c.

Herding life goes on under the khans; Erdene Zuu rises on Karakorum's ruins, Zanabazar casts his Buddhas, and in 1662 Sagan Setsen writes the Erdeniin Tovch chronicle. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.
