# Mongolian Life and Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. I.6: Life and culture of the Mongols in the 15th–18th c.. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Herding life goes on under the khans; Erdene Zuu rises on Karakorum's ruins, Zanabazar casts his Buddhas, and in 1662 Sagan Setsen writes the Erdeniin Tovch chronicle.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. I.4: Life of the Mongols in the 18th–early 20th c.. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Taxes, corvée and debt to Chinese firms grind the herders while the banner princes and monasteries hold the land and herds.
