# Mongolian Life and Culture — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Herding life goes on under the khans; ____ Zuu rises on Karakorum's ruins, Zanabazar casts his Buddhas, and in 1662 Sagan Setsen writes the Erdeniin Tovch chronicle. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Erdene

**2.** Taxes, corvée and debt to ____ firms grind the herders while the banner princes and monasteries hold the land and herds. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chinese
