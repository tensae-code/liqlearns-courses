# Mongolian Life and Culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Herding life goes on under the khans; Erdene Zuu rises on Karakorum's ruins, Zanabazar casts his Buddhas, and in 1662 Sagan Setsen writes the Erdeniin Tovch chronicle. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Taxes, corvée and debt to Chinese firms grind the herders while the banner princes and monasteries hold the land and herds.
