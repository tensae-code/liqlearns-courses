# Video script — Mongolia Between Revolutions, 1919–1921

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Chinese general Xu Shuzheng abolishes Mongolia's autonomy in 1919; then Baron Ungern's White troops take Urga in 1921 — occupation answers occupation; Chen Yi's 64 demands and Xu Shuzheng's troops abolish autonomy in 1919; Mongolia's leaders split over submission while the secret circles form.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Chinese general Xu Shuzheng abolishes Mongolia's autonomy in 1919; then Baron Ungern's White troops take Urga in 1921 — occupation answers occupation.

Then: Chen Yi's 64 demands and Xu Shuzheng's troops abolish autonomy in 1919; Mongolia's leaders split over submission while the secret circles form.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
