# Lesson plan — Mongolia: Market Transition

**Course:** History — Mongolia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Mongolia: Market Transition
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Restored, Socialist and Democratic Mongolia, 1911–Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Price liberalisation, privatisation vouchers and the shock of the 1990s — herds are privatised, factories stall, and a market economy is born in hardship; Price liberalisation (Jan 1991), voucher privatisation (the pink and blue coupons), herds and flats into private hands, land law — and the pain: inflation, unemployment, poverty.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Restored, Socialist and Democratic Mongolia, 1911–Today — it stands on the levels before it in this course as a recommended path (recommended, never forced).
