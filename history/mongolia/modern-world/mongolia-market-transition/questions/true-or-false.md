# Mongolia: Market Transition — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Price liberalisation, privatisation vouchers and the shock of the 1990s — herds are privatised, factories stall, and a market economy is born in hardship. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Price liberalisation (Jan 1991), voucher privatisation (the pink and blue coupons), herds and flats into private hands, land law — and the pain: inflation, unemployment, poverty.
