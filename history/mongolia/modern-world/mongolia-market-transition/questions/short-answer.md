# Mongolia: Market Transition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.5 Mongolia's economic change. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Price liberalisation, privatisation vouchers and the shock of the 1990s — herds are privatised, factories stall, and a market economy is born in hardship.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. IV.3 The reform of the socio-economic system. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Price liberalisation (Jan 1991), voucher privatisation (the pink and blue coupons), herds and flats into private hands, land law — and the pain: inflation, unemployment, poverty.
