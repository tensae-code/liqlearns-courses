# Mongolia: Market Transition

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Mongolia: Market Transition. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 4.5 Mongolia's economic change

Price liberalisation, privatisation vouchers and the shock of the 1990s — herds are privatised, factories stall, and a market economy is born in hardship. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: when a story has many parts, give each part one short name of your own and say the names in order — the whole story will stay in your head.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. IV.3 The reform of the socio-economic system

Price liberalisation (Jan 1991), voucher privatisation (the pink and blue coupons), herds and flats into private hands, land law — and the pain: inflation, unemployment, poverty. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.
