# Video script — Mongolia: Market Transition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Price liberalisation, privatisation vouchers and the shock of the 1990s — herds are privatised, factories stall, and a market economy is born in hardship; Price liberalisation (Jan 1991), voucher privatisation (the pink and blue coupons), herds and flats into private hands, land law — and the pain: inflation, unemployment, poverty.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Price liberalisation, privatisation vouchers and the shock of the 1990s — herds are privatised, factories stall, and a market economy is born in hardship.

Then: Price liberalisation (Jan 1991), voucher privatisation (the pink and blue coupons), herds and flats into private hands, land law — and the pain: inflation, unemployment, poverty.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
