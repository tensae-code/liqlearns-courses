# Video script — Mongolia in World War II

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mongolia gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia; Khalkhin Gol 1939, the total war economy, the "Revolutionary Mongolia" tank column, and the August 1945 campaign against Japan.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Mongolia gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia.

Then: Khalkhin Gol 1939, the total war economy, the "Revolutionary Mongolia" tank column, and the August 1945 campaign against Japan.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
