# Mongolia in World War II — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mongolia gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Khalkhin Gol 1939, the total war economy, the "Revolutionary Mongolia" tank column, and the August 1945 campaign against Japan.
