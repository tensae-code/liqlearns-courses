**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 3.12 The MPR's participation in the Second World War? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Mongolia gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
