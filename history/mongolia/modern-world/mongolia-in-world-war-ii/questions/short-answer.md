# Mongolia in World War II — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.12 The MPR's participation in the Second World War. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mongolia gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. III.6 The MPR in the Second World War; the consolidation of independence. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Khalkhin Gol 1939, the total war economy, the "Revolutionary Mongolia" tank column, and the August 1945 campaign against Japan.
