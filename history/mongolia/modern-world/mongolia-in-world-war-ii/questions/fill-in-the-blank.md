# Mongolia in World War II — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mongolia

**2.** ____ Gol 1939, the total war economy, the "Revolutionary Mongolia" tank column, and the August 1945 campaign against Japan. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Khalkhin
