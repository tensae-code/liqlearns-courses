# Mongolia 1911: Independence Restored — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.1 The Mongolian national revolution of 1911. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** As Qing collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.2 Independent Mongolia. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Bogd Khanate builds ministries, an army and a parliament of two chambers, and wins the 1915 Kyakhta treaty — autonomy under Chinese suzerainty, less than the full independence proclaimed.
