# Mongolia 1911: Independence Restored — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** As ____ collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Qing

**2.** The ____ Khanate builds ministries, an army and a parliament of two chambers, and wins the 1915 Kyakhta treaty — autonomy under Chinese suzerainty, less than the full independence proclaimed. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bogd
