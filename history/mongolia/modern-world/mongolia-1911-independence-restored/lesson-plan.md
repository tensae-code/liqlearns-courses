## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 2.1 The Mongolian national revolution of 1911** — As Qing collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia.
2. **Ch. 2.2 Independent Mongolia** — The Bogd Khanate builds ministries, an army and a parliament of two chambers, and wins the 1915 Kyakhta treaty — autonomy under Chinese suzerainty, less than the full independence proclaimed.
3. **Ch. II.1 The victory and significance of the 1911 revolution** — On 29 December 1911 the Bogd is enthroned and independence declared; the book weighs 1911 as the national revolution that restarted Mongolian statehood.
4. **Ch. II.2 The beginning of Mongolia's restored rise, 1911–1920** — A two-chamber khural, an army, schools, a bank and 370 enterprises — the Bogd Khanate builds a modern state inside the limits Kyakhta allowed.
