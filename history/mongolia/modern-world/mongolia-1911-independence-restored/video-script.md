# Video script — Mongolia 1911: Independence Restored

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
As Qing collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia; The Bogd Khanate builds ministries, an army and a parliament of two chambers, and wins the 1915 Kyakhta treaty — autonomy under Chinese suzerainty, less than the full independence proclaimed.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: As Qing collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia.

Then: The Bogd Khanate builds ministries, an army and a parliament of two chambers, and wins the 1915 Kyakhta treaty — autonomy under Chinese suzerainty, less than the full independence proclaimed.

Then: On 29 December 1911 the Bogd is enthroned and independence declared; the book weighs 1911 as the national revolution that restarted Mongolian statehood.

Then: A two-chamber khural, an army, schools, a bank and 370 enterprises — the Bogd Khanate builds a modern state inside the limits Kyakhta allowed.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
