# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The revolution's general crowns himself emperor, rewrites France's laws, conquers most of Europe — and falls.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it.
