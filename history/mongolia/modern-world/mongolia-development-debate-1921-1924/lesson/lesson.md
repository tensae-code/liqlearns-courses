# Mongolia: Development Debate, 1921–1924

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Mongolia: Development Debate, 1921–1924. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

Bodoo's, Danzan's and Sükhbaatar's rival roads — national-democratic renewal, trade-led growth, or the Comintern's socialist line; the argument decides Mongolia's century. That is the whole story of this lesson: who is there, where it happens, what turns, and what comes of it.
