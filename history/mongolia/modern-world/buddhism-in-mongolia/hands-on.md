# Hands-on — Buddhism in Mongolia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: Monasteries multiply across Khalkha, the Bogd Jebtsundamba's Ikh Khüree becomes a city of temples, and a large share of men live as lamas; The socialist state first taxes and restricts the monasteries, then moves to destroy them; a living church of hundreds of temples is reduced to almost none. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw the story as a timeline or a simple map: mark where it happens, who is there, the turn, and what came of it. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's pattern in real life — at home, at school, in the street: a place, a group or a rule changing the way this story's turn changed things. Note what happened, who was there, and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
