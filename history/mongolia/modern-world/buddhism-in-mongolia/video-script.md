# Video script — Buddhism in Mongolia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Monasteries multiply across Khalkha, the Bogd Jebtsundamba's Ikh Khüree becomes a city of temples, and a large share of men live as lamas; The socialist state first taxes and restricts the monasteries, then moves to destroy them; a living church of hundreds of temples is reduced to almost none.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Monasteries multiply across Khalkha, the Bogd Jebtsundamba's Ikh Khüree becomes a city of temples, and a large share of men live as lamas.

Then: The socialist state first taxes and restricts the monasteries, then moves to destroy them; a living church of hundreds of temples is reduced to almost none.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
