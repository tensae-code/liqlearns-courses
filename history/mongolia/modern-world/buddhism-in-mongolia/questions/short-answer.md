# Buddhism in Mongolia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. I.3: Buddhism spreads widely in Mongolia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Monasteries multiply across Khalkha, the Bogd Jebtsundamba's Ikh Khüree becomes a city of temples, and a large share of men live as lamas.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 3.8 Buddhism and socialism. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The socialist state first taxes and restricts the monasteries, then moves to destroy them; a living church of hundreds of temples is reduced to almost none.
