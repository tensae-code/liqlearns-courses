# Buddhism in Mongolia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. I.3: Buddhism spreads widely in Mongolia? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Monasteries multiply across Khalkha, the Bogd Jebtsundamba's Ikh Khüree becomes a city of temples, and a large share of men live as lamas  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Monasteries multiply across Khalkha, the Bogd Jebtsundamba's Ikh Khüree becomes a city of temples, and a large share of men live as lamas** — That is what this lesson teaches.
