# Mongolia: Democratic Revolution, 1990 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In ____ 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** December

**2.** Parties multiply, the first free elections are held in ____ 1990, and a grand coalition begins the transition. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** July
