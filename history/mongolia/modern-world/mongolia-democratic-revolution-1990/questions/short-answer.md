# Mongolia: Democratic Revolution, 1990 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.2 The democratic revolution begins in Mongolia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 4.3 The spread of the democratic revolution. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Parties multiply, the first free elections are held in July 1990, and a grand coalition begins the transition.
