# Mongolia: Democratic Revolution, 1990 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Parties multiply, the first free elections are held in July 1990, and a grand coalition begins the transition.
