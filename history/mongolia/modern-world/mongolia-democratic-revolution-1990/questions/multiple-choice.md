# Mongolia: Democratic Revolution, 1990 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. 4.2 The democratic revolution begins in Mongolia? (Sub lesson 1)
a) In December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
