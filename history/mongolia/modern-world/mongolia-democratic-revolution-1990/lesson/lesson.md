# Mongolia: Democratic Revolution, 1990

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 4.2 The democratic revolution begins in Mongolia

In December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.
