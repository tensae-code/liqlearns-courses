# Video script — Mongolia: Democratic Revolution, 1990

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990; Parties multiply, the first free elections are held in July 1990, and a grand coalition begins the transition.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: In December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990.

Then: Parties multiply, the first free elections are held in July 1990, and a grand coalition begins the transition.

Then: In January 1992 Mongolia adopts a democratic constitution — human rights, multiparty rule and private property replace the people's republic.

Then: The Democratic Union's first rally (10 Dec 1989), the March 1990 hunger strike, the Politburo's resignation, and the first free elections that July — a revolution without a shot.

Then: Multiparty registration, the Little Khural, and the 1992 Constitution — parliament, president and constitutional court replace the people's republic.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
