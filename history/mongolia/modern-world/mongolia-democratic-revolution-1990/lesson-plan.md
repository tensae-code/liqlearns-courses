## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 4.2 The democratic revolution begins in Mongolia** — In December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990.
2. **Ch. 4.3 The spread of the democratic revolution** — Parties multiply, the first free elections are held in July 1990, and a grand coalition begins the transition.
3. **Ch. 4.4 Democratic elections and the new Constitution** — In January 1992 Mongolia adopts a democratic constitution — human rights, multiparty rule and private property replace the people's republic.
4. **Ch. IV.1 The 1990 democratic revolution: course, victory and significance** — The Democratic Union's first rally (10 Dec 1989), the March 1990 hunger strike, the Politburo's resignation, and the first free elections that July — a revolution without a shot.
5. **Ch. IV.2 Changes in the political system** — Multiparty registration, the Little Khural, and the 1992 Constitution — parliament, president and constitutional court replace the people's republic.
