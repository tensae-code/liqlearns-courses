# Mongolia Under the Manchu Qing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Ch. I.3: Mongolia (mid-17th–18th c.)? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Khalkha splits into Tüsheet, Sechen and Jasagtu khanates, the Oirats rise in the west under Galdan, and Mongol-on-Mongol war opens the door to the Manchu  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Khalkha splits into Tüsheet, Sechen and Jasagtu khanates, the Oirats rise in the west under Galdan, and Mongol-on-Mongol war opens the door to the Manchu** — That is what this lesson teaches.
