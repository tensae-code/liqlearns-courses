# Mongolia Under the Manchu Qing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. I.3: Mongolia (mid-17th–18th c.). (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Khalkha splits into Tüsheet, Sechen and Jasagtu khanates, the Oirats rise in the west under Galdan, and Mongol-on-Mongol war opens the door to the Manchu.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. I.5: Mongolia loses its independence. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ligdan, the last great khan, is defeated by the Manchu and dies in flight in 1634; Inner Mongolia submits in 1636, and in 1691 the Khalkha nobles at Dolon Nor accept Qing rule.
