# Mongolia Under the Manchu Qing — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Ch. I.3: Mongolia (mid-17th–18th c.) — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Ch. I.5: Mongolia loses its independence — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Khalkha splits into Tüsheet, Sechen and Jasagtu khanates, the Oirats rise in the west under Galdan, and Mongol-on-Mongol war opens the door to the Manchu
b) Ligdan, the last great khan, is defeated by the Manchu and dies in flight in 1634; Inner Mongolia submits in 1636, and in 1691 the Khalkha nobles at Dolon Nor accept Qing rule

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
