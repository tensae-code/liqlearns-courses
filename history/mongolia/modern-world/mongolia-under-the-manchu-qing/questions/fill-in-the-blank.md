# Mongolia Under the Manchu Qing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ splits into Tüsheet, Sechen and Jasagtu khanates, the Oirats rise in the west under Galdan, and Mongol-on-Mongol war opens the door to the Manchu. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Khalkha

**2.** ____, the last great khan, is defeated by the Manchu and dies in flight in 1634; Inner Mongolia submits in 1636, and in 1691 the Khalkha nobles at Dolon Nor accept Qing rule. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ligdan
