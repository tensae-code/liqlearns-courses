# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Reason is turned on throne and altar alike; the enlighteners argue that knowledge, not birth, should order society. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Telegraph, steamship, electricity and medicine — the inventions of the 18th–19th c. shrink the world the Mongols live in too.
