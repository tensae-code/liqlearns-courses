Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. II.4: The Enlightenment movement. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Reason is turned on throne and altar alike; the enlighteners argue that knowledge, not birth, should order society.
