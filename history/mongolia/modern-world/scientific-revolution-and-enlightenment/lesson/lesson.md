# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. II.4: The Enlightenment movement

Reason is turned on throne and altar alike; the enlighteners argue that knowledge, not birth, should order society. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: when a story has many parts, give each part one short name of your own and say the names in order — the whole story will stay in your head.
