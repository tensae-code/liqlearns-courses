# Mongolia 1921: People's Revolution

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Mongolia 1921: People's Revolution. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 2.8 The Mongolian revolution of 1921

Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 2.9 The first measures of the people's government

Serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch. II.4 The victory and significance of the 1921 revolution

The MPP's founding, Kyakhta's fall, Ungern's capture of Urga, and the July 1921 liberation with Red Army help — independence regained, at a price. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Ch. II.5 Mongolia, a limited constitutional monarchy, 1921–1924

The Tangaragiin treaty bounds the Bogd's power; serfdom is abolished (1922), local rule democratised, and the first constitution drafted — a monarchy dissolving into a republic. That is the heart of this part of the story: hold on to who is there, where it happens, and what it leads to, because the next part builds on it.
