# Video script — Mongolia 1921: People's Revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government; Serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government.

Then: Serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society.

Then: The MPP's founding, Kyakhta's fall, Ungern's capture of Urga, and the July 1921 liberation with Red Army help — independence regained, at a price.

Then: The Tangaragiin treaty bounds the Bogd's power; serfdom is abolished (1922), local rule democratised, and the first constitution drafted — a monarchy dissolving into a republic.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
