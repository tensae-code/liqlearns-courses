# Mongolia 1921: People's Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society.
