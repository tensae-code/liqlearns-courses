# Mongolia 1921: People's Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.8 The Mongolian revolution of 1921. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government.

**2.** Tell the story of this part of the lesson: Ch. 2.9 The first measures of the people's government. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society.
