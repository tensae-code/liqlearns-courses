# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Tsar falls, the Bolsheviks take power, and revolution becomes exportable — Mongolia's northern neighbour will never be the same.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: The Tsar falls, the Bolsheviks take power, and revolution becomes exportable — Mongolia's northern neighbour will never be the same.
