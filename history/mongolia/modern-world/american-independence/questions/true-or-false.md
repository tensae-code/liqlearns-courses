# American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain's American colonies declare independence and found a republic built on the rights of man.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Britain's American colonies declare independence and found a republic built on the rights of man.
