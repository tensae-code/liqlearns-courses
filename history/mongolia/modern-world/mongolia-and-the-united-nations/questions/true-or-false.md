# Mongolia and the United Nations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the 1945 referendum confirms independence and China recognises it, Mongolia's long-blocked UN application finally succeeds in 1961. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson's story turns on what its parts tell: Yalta's status-quo promise, the 20 October 1945 referendum (98.4% for independence), and China's recognition in 1946 make Mongolia's sovereignty de jure at last.
