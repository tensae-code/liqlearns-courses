# Video script — Mongolia and the United Nations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After the 1945 referendum confirms independence and China recognises it, Mongolia's long-blocked UN application finally succeeds in 1961; Yalta's status-quo promise, the 20 October 1945 referendum (98.4% for independence), and China's recognition in 1946 make Mongolia's sovereignty de jure at last.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: After the 1945 referendum confirms independence and China recognises it, Mongolia's long-blocked UN application finally succeeds in 1961.

Then: Yalta's status-quo promise, the 20 October 1945 referendum (98.4% for independence), and China's recognition in 1946 make Mongolia's sovereignty de jure at last.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
