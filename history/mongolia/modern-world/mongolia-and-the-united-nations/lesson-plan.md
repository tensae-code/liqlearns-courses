# Lesson plan — Mongolia and the United Nations

**Course:** History — Mongolia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Mongolia and the United Nations
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Restored, Socialist and Democratic Mongolia, 1911–Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the 1945 referendum confirms independence and China recognises it, Mongolia's long-blocked UN application finally succeeds in 1961; Yalta's status-quo promise, the 20 October 1945 referendum (98.4% for independence), and China's recognition in 1946 make Mongolia's sovereignty de jure at last.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Restored, Socialist and Democratic Mongolia, 1911–Today — it stands on the levels before it in this course as a recommended path (recommended, never forced).
