# Video script — Mongolia: MPR Economy, Collectivisation and Plans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cooperatives, the first collectivisation drive and its collapse, then state farms, negdels and five-year plans remake herding Mongolia; War economy, post-war rebuilding and industrialisation — Ulaanbaatar's factories, Darkhan and Erdenet rise on Soviet-backed plans.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Cooperatives, the first collectivisation drive and its collapse, then state farms, negdels and five-year plans remake herding Mongolia.

Then: War economy, post-war rebuilding and industrialisation — Ulaanbaatar's factories, Darkhan and Erdenet rise on Soviet-backed plans.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
