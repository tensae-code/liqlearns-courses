# The Industrial Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Topic 1.2 Social change: industrial society?
a) Britain's industrialisation in the late 18th century births a new kind of society — machines replace muscle, cities swell, and Tönnies's Gemeinschaft gives way to Gesellschaft  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Britain's industrialisation in the late 18th century births a new kind of society — machines replace muscle, cities swell, and Tönnies's Gemeinschaft gives way to Gesellschaft** — That is what this lesson teaches.
