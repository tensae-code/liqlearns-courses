# Video script — Mongolia: Democratic Society and Foreign Policy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Free speech, free religion and free travel return; monasteries reopen and a new, plural society argues with itself; The "third neighbour" policy reaches beyond Russia and China to the US, Japan, Korea and the EU.

Look at the map. Find Mongolia — and the wider world its books set beside it, because Mongolia's books always tell the two stories together.

First: Free speech, free religion and free travel return; monasteries reopen and a new, plural society argues with itself.

Then: The "third neighbour" policy reaches beyond Russia and China to the US, Japan, Korea and the EU.

Then: Free press and free faith return; Chinggis is honoured again, the Secret History's 750th anniversary fills Naadam 1990, the repressed are rehabilitated and 10 September becomes their memorial day.

Then: The third-neighbour policy, nuclear-weapon-free status, peacekeepers abroad, the 2004 Russian debt settlement, and ties with 180 states.

Remember it this way: who was there, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
