# Mongolia: New Turn Policy, 1932–1940

## Do it first

Before you read anything, try this: say out loud what you think this story is about — Mongolia: New Turn Policy, 1932–1940. Who do you think is in it? Where does it happen? What do you think turns — what changes? Keep your guesses. This lesson tells the story properly, and at the end you will check your guesses against it.

Moscow admits the "leftist" error; communes are dissolved, private herding restored, industry founded (the Industrial Combine, 1934), literacy climbs and Mongolian culture breathes again. That is the whole story of this lesson: who is there, where it happens, what turns, and what comes of it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: tell the story back to yourself once, in your own words, before you answer any question — if you can tell it, you know it.
