# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Columbus reaches America in 1492, Vasco da Gama rounds Africa to India in 1497–98, Magellan's ships circle the globe in 1519–22 — and the new sea roads become the first colonial empires.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's story had no turn in it — nothing changed for anyone in it.
