# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of this part of the lesson — Part II, Ch. II.1: The great geographical discoveries and the beginning of colonialism?
a) Columbus reaches America in 1492, Vasco da Gama rounds Africa to India in 1497–98, Magellan's ships circle the globe in 1519–22 — and the new sea roads become the first colonial empires  b) nothing changed and no one acted  c) the story happened somewhere else, to other people, for no reason  d) the past left no trace of it at all
**Answer: a) Columbus reaches America in 1492, Vasco da Gama rounds Africa to India in 1497–98, Magellan's ships circle the globe in 1519–22 — and the new sea roads become the first colonial empires** — That is what this lesson teaches.
