# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where was Tenochtitlan built?
a) On a mountain in the Andes  b) On an island in Lake Texcoco  c) On the coast of Brazil  d) In the desert of northern Mexico
**Answer: b) On an island in Lake Texcoco** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does the book study an Aztec drawing?
a) It is the oldest map of Europe  b) It is the empire's own testimony — how Aztec artists saw their order, ruler and gods  c) It shows the route of Magellan  d) It was drawn by Spanish conquerors
**Answer: b) It is the empire's own testimony — how Aztec artists saw their order, ruler and gods** — That is what this lesson teaches.
