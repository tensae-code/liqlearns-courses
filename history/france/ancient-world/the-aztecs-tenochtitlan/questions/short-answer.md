# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How was the Aztec empire held together?
**Answer:** By war, tribute and fear — conquered peoples paid tribute in gold, feathers and food to the emperors in Tenochtitlan.

**2.** What makes the Aztec drawing different from the conquerors' accounts?
**Answer:** It shows the empire through its own people's eyes — their ruler, gods and order — instead of through the eyes of the men who destroyed it.
