# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Around which sea did the Greek cities spread?
a) The Baltic Sea  b) The Mediterranean Sea  c) The Red Sea only  d) The Atlantic Ocean
**Answer: b) The Mediterranean Sea** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What three things does this lesson name as the Greek footprint?
a) Castles, cannons and printing presses  b) Language, art and the citizen-run city (the polis)  c) Roads, aqueducts and Roman law  d) Monasteries, universities and cathedrals
**Answer: b) Language, art and the citizen-run city (the polis)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
