# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Greeks were one single country ruled by one king.
**Answer: False** — They were many separate city-states, rivals as often as partners, sharing language, gods, art and the city way of life.

**2.** Greek cities were founded on shores as far apart as Italy, Sicily, France and Asia Minor.
**Answer: True**
