# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did Greek cities send out ships to found new cities?
**Answer:** When a city grew crowded or wanted trade, it sent colonists to found a new city on a distant shore.

**2.** What is the polis, as this lesson teaches it?
**Answer:** A city run by its citizens — the Greek idea of the city that spread with their language and art.
