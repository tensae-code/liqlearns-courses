# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Sail out from Greece in an old wooden ship and keep the coast in sight. At every good harbour — in Italy, in Sicily, on the shores of France and Spain and across to Asia — you find the same thing: a Greek city, speaking Greek, building Greek temples. This lesson follows that footprint.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Greek cities spread all around the Mediterranean Sea and leave their language, their art and their way of running a city as a footprint on every shore they touch. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
