# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

**Course:** History — France · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Mediterranean in Antiquity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greek cities spread all around the Mediterranean Sea and leave their language, their art and their way of running a city as a footprint on every shore they touch.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Mediterranean in Antiquity — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Greek cities spread all around the Mediterranean Sea and leave their language, their art and their way of running a city as a footprint on every shore they touch.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a word from the learner's own language that travelled from somewhere else — like Greek words for theatre, school and democracy that still travel the world today.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Greeks were one single country → they were many separate city-states, rivals as often as partners; what they shared was language, gods, art and the city way of life.
- Go back to the one thing: Greek cities spread all around the Mediterranean Sea and leave their language, their art and their way of running a city as a footprint on every shore they touch. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
