# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What ran across the Mediterranean between the three worlds?
a) Nothing — the shores never met  b) Routes of exchange carrying goods, techniques and learning  c) Only pilgrims, never merchants  d) Roman roads on the seabed
**Answer: b) Routes of exchange carrying goods, techniques and learning** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does this lesson describe exchange and conflict?
a) As twins — the same sea carried spice ships and war fleets  b) Exchange ended all conflict  c) Conflict made exchange impossible  d) They happened in different centuries only
**Answer: a) As twins — the same sea carried spice ships and war fleets** — That is what this lesson teaches.
