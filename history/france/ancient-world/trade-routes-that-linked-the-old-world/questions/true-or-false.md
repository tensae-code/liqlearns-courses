# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Merchants of different faiths dealt with each other in shared ports.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Only goods crossed the Mediterranean; ideas and learning stayed home.
**Answer: False** — Techniques, crops, mathematics, medicine, stories and words crossed with the cargoes.
