# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four kinds of contact between the three worlds, as this lesson lists them.
**Answer:** Any four of: trade, diplomacy, pilgrimage, ransoms and war.

**2.** What did the Christian West borrow from the Muslim world across these routes?
**Answer:** Learning the Muslim world had kept and grown — including mathematics and medicine — along with techniques and crops.
