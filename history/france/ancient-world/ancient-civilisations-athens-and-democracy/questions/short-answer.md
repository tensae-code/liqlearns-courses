# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Pericles help even poor citizens take part in the city's justice?
**Answer:** He paid citizens for jury service, so the poor could afford to serve.

**2.** Why does this lesson say Athens has a 'double face'?
**Answer:** Because it gave its own citizens a real share in power while ruling its allied cities as an empire without asking them.
