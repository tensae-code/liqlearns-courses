# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Athens, the citizens themselves met in assembly and voted on the city's business.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Women, enslaved people and foreigners could all vote in Athens' democracy.
**Answer: False** — Only free Athenian men were citizens who voted; women, enslaved people and foreigners were shut out.
