# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a city where ordinary men — farmers, rowers, craftsmen — gather on a hill and vote on war and peace with their own hands. No king tells them. That city is Athens, and this lesson meets the man who led it at its greatest moment: Pericles.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Pericles leads Athens at the height of its democracy, and the city that rules itself also becomes the head of an empire over other Greek cities. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
