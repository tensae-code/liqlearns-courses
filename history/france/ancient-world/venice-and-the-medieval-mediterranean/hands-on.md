# Hands-on — Venice and the Medieval Mediterranean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Build a city where there is no land — on mud and wooden piles in a lagoon — and it should sink. Instead it becomes the richest city of the sea. This lesson asks how Venice did it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Venice, a city built on water in a lagoon, grows rich and powerful by commanding the sea's trade routes — its ships, its merchants and its diplomacy make it the Mediterranean's great middleman. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
