# Venice and the Medieval Mediterranean — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Venice's lagoon position kept it safe from armies while its ships traded.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Venetian merchants avoided the Byzantine East and traded only with France.
**Answer: False** — Venice traded above all with the East — winning privileges in Constantinople and the Levant ports.
