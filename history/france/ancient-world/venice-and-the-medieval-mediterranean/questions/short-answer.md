# Venice and the Medieval Mediterranean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did Venice have to live by the sea?
**Answer:** Built on lagoon islands, it had no fields and no roads — the sea was its only way to live, so it became a city of sailors, shipbuilders and merchants.

**2.** What passed through Venetian warehouses toward Europe?
**Answer:** Eastern luxuries such as spices and silk, carried from Constantinople and the Levant.
