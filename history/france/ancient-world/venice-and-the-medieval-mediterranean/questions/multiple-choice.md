# Venice and the Medieval Mediterranean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On what was Venice built?
a) A high mountain plateau  b) Wooden piles in a lagoon  c) A Roman road junction  d) A desert oasis
**Answer: b) Wooden piles in a lagoon** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did Venice's wealth come from?
a) Its vast farmland  b) Commanding the sea's trade routes as middleman between East and West  c) Gold mines in its hills  d) Taxing pilgrims only
**Answer: b) Commanding the sea's trade routes as middleman between East and West** — That is what this lesson teaches.
