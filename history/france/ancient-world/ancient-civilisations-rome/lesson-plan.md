## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Augustus called himself emperor or king → he carefully avoided those hated words; he ruled as princeps, 'first citizen', keeping the Republic's shapes while holding its power.
- Go back to the one thing: Augustus ends Rome's civil wars and founds the principate — one man ruling as 'first citizen' — and Rome stamps its order on the whole Mediterranean world. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
