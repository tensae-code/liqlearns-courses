# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Augustus hold power while claiming to restore the Republic?
**Answer:** He kept the Senate and the old offices but held the real power himself — over the armies and the provinces — as princeps, first citizen.

**2.** What troubles did the principate bring to an end?
**Answer:** The civil wars between rival generals that had torn the late Republic apart for a century.
