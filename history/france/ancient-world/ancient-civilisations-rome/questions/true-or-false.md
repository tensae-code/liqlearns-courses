# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Augustus openly crowned himself king of Rome.
**Answer: False** — He avoided the words king and emperor; he ruled as princeps, first citizen, keeping the Republic's shapes while holding its power.

**2.** Under Augustus, Rome's order covered the whole Mediterranean world.
**Answer: True**
