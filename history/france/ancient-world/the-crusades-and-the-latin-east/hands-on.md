# Hands-on — The Crusades and the Latin East

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A monk with a voice like thunder rides through France preaching: take the cross, march east, free the Holy Land. Thousands answer. This lesson follows Bernard's crusade — and the fragile states the crusaders built at the end of their journey.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Bernard of Clairvaux preaches a crusade, and knights sail east to carve out Latin states in the Levant — Christian kingdoms planted on a foreign shore, always fragile, always needing the sea. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
