# The Crusades and the Latin East — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The First Crusade took Jerusalem in 1099.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Latin states could supply themselves entirely from their own land and needed nothing from Europe.
**Answer: False** — Men, horses, money and food had to keep arriving by sea from Europe — that lifeline was their survival.
