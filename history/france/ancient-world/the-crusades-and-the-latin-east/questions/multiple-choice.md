# The Crusades and the Latin East — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who preached the Second Crusade at Vézelay?
a) Pericles  b) Bernard of Clairvaux  c) Colbert  d) Voltaire
**Answer: b) Bernard of Clairvaux** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why were the Latin states fragile?
a) They had no castles  b) They were a thin coastal strip, always short of men, supplied only by sea  c) They refused all trade  d) They were built inland in the desert
**Answer: b) They were a thin coastal strip, always short of men, supplied only by sea** — That is what this lesson teaches.
