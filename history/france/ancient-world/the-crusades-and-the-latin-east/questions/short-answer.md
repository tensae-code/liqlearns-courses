# The Crusades and the Latin East — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does 'taking the cross' mean in this lesson?
**Answer:** Sewing a cross on your clothing as the sign that you had vowed to go on crusade to the Holy Land.

**2.** Who garrisoned the frontiers of the Latin East?
**Answer:** Military orders of warrior-monks, alongside the knights, with castles guarding the roads.
