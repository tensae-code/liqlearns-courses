# Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three powers of the medieval Mediterranean and the shore each holds.
**Answer:** The Byzantine Empire in the east, the Muslim world on the southern and eastern shores, and the Christian West in the west — all facing each other across one sea.

**2.** What two works of Justinian does this lesson name?
**Answer:** He gathered Roman law into one code, and he raised the great church of Hagia Sophia in Constantinople.
