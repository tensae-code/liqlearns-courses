# Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which three powers face each other around the medieval Mediterranean in this lesson?
a) Athens, Sparta and Persia  b) The Byzantine Empire, the Muslim world and the Christian West  c) Venice, Genoa and Pisa  d) France, England and Spain only
**Answer: b) The Byzantine Empire, the Muslim world and the Christian West** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who was Justinian?
a) A Venetian merchant prince  b) A great Byzantine emperor who codified Roman law and raised Hagia Sophia  c) The preacher of the Second Crusade  d) The founder of the Muslim caliphate
**Answer: b) A great Byzantine emperor who codified Roman law and raised Hagia Sophia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
