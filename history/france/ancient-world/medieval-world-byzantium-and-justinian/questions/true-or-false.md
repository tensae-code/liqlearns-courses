# Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Byzantine emperor ruled from Constantinople and was Greek-speaking and Christian.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Muslim world stayed small, confined to Arabia alone.
**Answer: False** — It grew with astonishing speed into caliphates stretching from Spain to Persia.
