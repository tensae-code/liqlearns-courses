# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Constantine favoured Christianity, which earlier emperors had persecuted.
**Answer: True**

**2.** After Constantine, the empire's East and West grew closer together in language and wealth.
**Answer: False** — They drifted apart — Latin West and Greek East — and the West eventually fell while the East lived on as the Byzantine world.
