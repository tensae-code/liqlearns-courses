# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does where a capital sits matter so much?
**Answer:** Because power, money and law gather where the emperor sits — moving the capital east pulled the empire's centre of gravity east with it.

**2.** What grew out of Constantine's eastern turn over the following generations?
**Answer:** The lasting division of the Roman world into a Latin West and a Greek East.
