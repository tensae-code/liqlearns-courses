# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What two great turns did Constantine make?
a) He rebuilt Athens and restored its democracy  b) He favoured Christianity and built a new eastern capital, Constantinople  c) He divided the tribute among the allies and freed the cities  d) He moved the capital to Venice and taxed the sea trade
**Answer: b) He favoured Christianity and built a new eastern capital, Constantinople** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did Constantine build his new capital?
a) On the Tiber in Italy  b) On the straits between Europe and Asia, in the east  c) On the Nile in Egypt  d) On an island in the Atlantic
**Answer: b) On the straits between Europe and Asia, in the east** — That is what this lesson teaches.
