# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the scientific method as this lesson teaches it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Look, measure, calculate — and let the evidence argue: knowledge tested by observation rather than received from authority.

**2.** Name two scientific works by du Châtelet besides the Newton translation. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Her physics textbook, the Institutions de physique, and her experimental work on fire and the force of moving bodies.
