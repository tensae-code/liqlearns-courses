# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book's word for the Enlightenment's spread of science through society is 'diffusion'. (Sub lesson 1) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Women of du Châtelet's time could enter the academies of science on equal terms. (Sub lesson 2) (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Women were shut out of the academies; du Châtelet built her laboratory and study at home, at the château of Cirey.
