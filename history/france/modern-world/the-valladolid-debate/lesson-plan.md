1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In 1550, Spain stops to debate whether the peoples of America have souls and rights: Las Casas defends them as fully human, Sepúlveda argues they are natural slaves — and the conscience of an empire is put on trial.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the debate ended the conquest or freed the peoples of America → it did not; colonisation continued. Its weight is different: an empire argued in public about the humanity of those it ruled, and Las Casas's defence stayed on record to accuse the conquest forever after.
- Go back to the one thing: In 1550, Spain stops to debate whether the peoples of America have souls and rights: Las Casas defends them as fully human, Sepúlveda argues they are natural slaves — and the conscience of an empire is put on trial. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
