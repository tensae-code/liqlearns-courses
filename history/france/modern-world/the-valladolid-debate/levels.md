# The Valladolid Debate — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In 1550, Spain stops to debate whether the peoples of America have souls and rights: Las Casas defends them as fully human, Sepúlveda argues they are natural slaves — and the conscience of an empire is put on trial. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
