# The Valladolid Debate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sepúlveda argued that the peoples of America were natural slaves who needed Spanish masters.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Valladolid debate immediately ended the conquest of America.
**Answer: False** — The judges gave no clean verdict and colonisation continued; the debate's weight is that the empire's conscience was argued on record.
