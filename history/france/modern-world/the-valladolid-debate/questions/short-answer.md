# The Valladolid Debate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did Las Casas argue about the lands of America?
**Answer:** That they belong to their own peoples — fully human, rational and free — and that faith can be preached but never imposed by the sword.

**2.** Who ordered the debate, pausing the conquests while it sat?
**Answer:** The emperor Charles V of Spain.
