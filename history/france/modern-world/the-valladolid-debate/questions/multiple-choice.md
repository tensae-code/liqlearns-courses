# The Valladolid Debate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was debated at Valladolid in 1550?
a) Whether silver should be taxed  b) Whether the peoples of America have souls and rights  c) Whether France should build Versailles  d) Whether the Earth moves around the Sun
**Answer: b) Whether the peoples of America have souls and rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who defended the peoples of America as fully human?
a) Sepúlveda  b) Las Casas  c) Colbert  d) Bernard of Clairvaux
**Answer: b) Las Casas** — That is what this lesson teaches.
