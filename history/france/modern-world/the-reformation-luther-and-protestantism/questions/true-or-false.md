# The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Luther translated the Bible into German so ordinary people could read it. (Sub lesson 1) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Erasmus supported Luther's break with Rome to the end. (Sub lesson 2) (Sub lesson 2)
