# The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did Luther's protest spread so fast and so far? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Printing spread the theses, princes protected Luther, and his refusal to recant — Scripture and conscience above popes and councils — turned a protest into new churches.

**2.** Name two causes that prepared the ground for the reformations. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any two of: humanist criticism of corruption (Erasmus editing the New Testament and mocking abuses), the sale of indulgences, Rome's wealth and worldliness, and princes glad to defy pope and emperor.
