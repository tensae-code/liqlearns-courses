# Hands-on — The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A German monk nails a list of arguments to a church door — so the famous story runs — and within a generation, half of Europe has left the Church of Rome. This lesson follows the crack as it spreads, and meets the two men whose friendship broke on the way.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Luther's theses crack the Western Church open and start a century of reformations — and the ground was prepared by humanist criticism, until Erasmus and Luther themselves broke apart over free will. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
