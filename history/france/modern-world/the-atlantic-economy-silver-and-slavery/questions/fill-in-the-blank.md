# The Atlantic Economy: Silver and Slavery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The great silver flood came from mines like ____ in America. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Potosí

**2.** Bordeaux grew rich on goods from plantations, above all from ____ in the Caribbean. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Saint-Domingue
