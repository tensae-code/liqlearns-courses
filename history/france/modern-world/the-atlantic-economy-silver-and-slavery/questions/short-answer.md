# The Atlantic Economy: Silver and Slavery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the économie-monde this lesson describes? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A world-economy centred on Europe: Europe organising, America supplying raw wealth like silver, and Africa supplying enslaved people.

**2.** What paid for the shipowners' fine houses on the quays of Nantes and Bordeaux? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The profits of Atlantic trade built on enslaved labour — slaving expeditions from Nantes and colonial goods, above all from Saint-Domingue, through Bordeaux.
