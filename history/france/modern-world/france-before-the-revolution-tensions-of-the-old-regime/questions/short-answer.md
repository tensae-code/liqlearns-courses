# France Before the Revolution: Tensions of the Old Regime — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the book call rich and poor in Paris 'one tension' rather than two facts? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Because extreme wealth and desperate poverty stood visible in the same streets, daily — and the contrast itself became harder and harder to justify.

**2.** How did the salons help prepare the Revolution without firing a shot? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** They formed public opinion: ideas, books and reputations circulated there, and the regime was judged and found wanting by the very classes whose support it needed.
