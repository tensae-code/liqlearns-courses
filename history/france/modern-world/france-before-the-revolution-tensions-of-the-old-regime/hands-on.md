# Hands-on — France Before the Revolution: Tensions of the Old Regime

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A kingdom glittering at Versailles — and barefoot rebels in Normandy rising against a salt tax; splendid mansions in Paris — and families in one room, hungry in winter. In the salons, meanwhile, polite voices discuss whether the whole order makes sense. This lesson walks through a regime that is cracking before it falls.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Tax revolts like the Va-nu-pieds, the rich and poor of Paris side by side, and salons where a new public argues about everything — the Old Regime in the decades before 1789 is cracking under its own weight. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
