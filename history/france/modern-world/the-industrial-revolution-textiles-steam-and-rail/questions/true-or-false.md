# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France's book treats the steam engine as applied science — the study of heat, pressure and vacuum turned into a machine.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Steam power left work unchanged, since mills still depended only on muscle and rivers.
**Answer: False** — Steam freed production from muscle, water and wind; factories gathered workers into towns and production multiplied.
