# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the book file the steam engine in the sciences chapter rather than a chapter on inventions alone?
**Answer:** Because the engine is the scientific spirit applied: knowledge of pressure, vacuum and heat, built into a machine and improved model by model.

**2.** Name three places steam power spread to from the mines.
**Answer:** Textile mills (looms and spinning machines), boats, and the railways with the locomotive.
