# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did the first steam engines of this lesson work?
a) In royal palaces  b) Pumping water out of coal mines  c) In Venetian ships  d) In printing shops
**Answer: b) Pumping water out of coal mines** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Watt's improved engine of the 1770s add?
a) It ran on wind  b) It used far less coal and could turn wheels, driving machinery  c) It translated Newton  d) It printed books
**Answer: b) It used far less coal and could turn wheels, driving machinery** — That is what this lesson teaches.
