1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The steam engine turns Enlightenment science into industrial power: heat and pressure, studied by the new science, become the machine that will remake work, towns and travel.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a bicycle pump growing warm in your hands as you work it — heat from pressure and motion, the same family of facts the steam engine puts to work.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
