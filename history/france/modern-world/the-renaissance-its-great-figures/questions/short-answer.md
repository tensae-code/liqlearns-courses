# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What tools did the humanists use, as this lesson names them?
**Answer:** Languages, criticism and laughter — editing ancient texts and mocking the folly and corruption of their age.

**2.** How did artists' status change in the Renaissance?
**Answer:** From unnamed workshop craftsmen to named masters — commissioned by popes, negotiating as equals, and signed into history, like Michelangelo.
