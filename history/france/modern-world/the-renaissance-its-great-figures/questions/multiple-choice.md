# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who is named 'prince of the humanists'?
a) Luther  b) Erasmus  c) Sepúlveda  d) Columbus
**Answer: b) Erasmus** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is 'the birth of the artist' in this lesson?
a) The first painting ever made  b) Artists stepping out of workshop anonymity to be named, signed and celebrated as creators  c) The founding of the first museum  d) The invention of oil paint
**Answer: b) Artists stepping out of workshop anonymity to be named, signed and celebrated as creators** — That is what this lesson teaches.
