# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the colonists' cry against London's taxes?
a) One king, one faith  b) No taxation without representation  c) Down with all laws  d) Trade with France only
**Answer: b) No taxation without representation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the United States found instead of a limited monarchy?
a) An empire under Washington  b) A republic — power from the people, a president instead of a king  c) A colony of France  d) A military dictatorship
**Answer: b) A republic — power from the people, a president instead of a king** — That is what this lesson teaches.
