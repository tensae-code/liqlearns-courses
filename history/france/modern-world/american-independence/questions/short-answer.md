# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Where did the colonists' ideas of rights and consent come from, as this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** From Britain itself — English law and the English model of liberty, which the colonists turned against the crown that taught them.

**2.** In what year did Britain recognise the United States, and in what year was its constitution made?
**Answer:** Britain recognised the United States in 1783; the constitution followed in 1787.
