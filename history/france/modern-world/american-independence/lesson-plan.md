**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Making of the State: France and the British World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain's American colonists take Britain's own ideas of liberty, rebel against its crown, and under Washington found a republic — the English model crossing the Atlantic and coming back transformed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Making of the State: France and the British World — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Britain's American colonists take Britain's own ideas of liberty, rebel against its crown, and under Washington found a republic — the English model crossing the Atlantic and coming back transformed.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a pupil who learns the school's rulebook better than the teachers, quotes it in a dispute, and ends by founding a new club with the rules written fair from the start.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
