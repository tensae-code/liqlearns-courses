# Versailles and Absolute Monarchy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Louis XIV moves the court to Versailles and governs by himself — the palace is not decoration but an instrument: the great nobles live where the king can watch them, and all power radiates from one man. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
