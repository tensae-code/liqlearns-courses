# Versailles and Absolute Monarchy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The court moved to Versailles for good in 1682.
**Answer: True**

**2.** At Versailles the nobles gained independence from the king and ruled their provinces more freely.
**Answer: False** — The opposite: court life consumed them in ceremony and competition for the king's favour, under his eye.
