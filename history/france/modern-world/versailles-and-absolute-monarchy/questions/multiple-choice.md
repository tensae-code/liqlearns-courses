# Versailles and Absolute Monarchy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Louis XIV announce when Mazarin died in 1661?
a) That he would share power with the nobles  b) That he would govern by himself — be his own first minister  c) That he would restore the Edict of Nantes  d) That he would move the capital to Nantes
**Answer: b) That he would govern by himself — be his own first minister** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How was Versailles an instrument of power?
a) It was a fortress against foreign armies  b) It kept the great nobles at court under the king's eye, competing for favour instead of plotting in the provinces  c) It housed the national assembly  d) It stored the kingdom's silver
**Answer: b) It kept the great nobles at court under the king's eye, competing for favour instead of plotting in the provinces** — That is what this lesson teaches.
