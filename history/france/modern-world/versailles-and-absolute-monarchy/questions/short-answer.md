# Versailles and Absolute Monarchy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What was the Fronde, and why did it shape Louis XIV?
**Answer:** A civil war of great nobles against the crown during Louis's childhood — he never forgot it, and built a system where nobles could not do it again.

**2.** Who carried the king's orders into the provinces?
**Answer:** The intendants — royal officials who acted over the heads of the old provincial governors.
