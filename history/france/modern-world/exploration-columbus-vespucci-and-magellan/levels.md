# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Europeans 'discover' lands that other peoples already call home — and France's book puts the very word 'discovery' on trial, asking what it hides and whom it erases. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
