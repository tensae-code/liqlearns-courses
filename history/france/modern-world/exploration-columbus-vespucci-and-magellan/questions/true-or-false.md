# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Columbus reached the Caribbean in 1492, seeking a westward road to Asia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The book teaches that 'discovery' is a perfectly innocent word that harms no one.
**Answer: False** — The book shows the word erases the peoples already living there and turns an encounter — soon a conquest — into an innocent finding.
