# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Whose name was given to the whole continent?
**Answer:** Vespucci's — America is named after Amerigo Vespucci.

**2.** Say the event of 1492 in one sentence from the shore's point of view.
**Answer:** Strangers arrived by sea at a land that was already home — an encounter, and soon an invasion, not the discovery of an empty world.
