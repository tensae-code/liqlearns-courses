# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does France's book put on trial in this lesson?
a) The seaworthiness of Columbus's ships  b) The very word 'discovery'  c) The shape of the strait Magellan found  d) The price of spices in Lisbon
**Answer: b) The very word 'discovery'** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is 'discovery' a misleading word for 1492?
a) Columbus never left Europe  b) The Americas were already home to millions of people, with cities and empires  c) The lands were drawn on maps centuries earlier by Europeans  d) Nobody sailed anywhere in 1492
**Answer: b) The Americas were already home to millions of people, with cities and empires** — That is what this lesson teaches.
