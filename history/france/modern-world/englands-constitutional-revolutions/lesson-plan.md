## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: England tries its king, executes him, and ends by binding the crown itself with habeas corpus and a Bill of Rights — and Voltaire holds English liberty up to shame France, while the book asks how free England really was.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a club that removes its president for breaking the rules, then writes the rules down so no president can break them again — the writing-down is the revolution that lasts.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
