# England's Constitutional Revolutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does habeas corpus protect? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The subject against arbitrary arrest: the king's officers must bring a prisoner before a judge rather than hold him at their pleasure.

**2.** Name two limits of English liberty that the book asks pupils to notice. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Any two of: Parliament represented landowners, most men and all women could not vote, and the same kingdom ran colonies and a slave trade.
