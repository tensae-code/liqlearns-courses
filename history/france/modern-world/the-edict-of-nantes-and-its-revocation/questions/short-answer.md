# The Edict of Nantes and Its Revocation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does this lesson call the edict a peace treaty inside one kingdom rather than modern equality?
**Answer:** Because the kingdom stayed officially Catholic, and Protestant worship was protected only in specified places with limited rights — a settlement to end civil war, not equal citizenship.

**2.** What did Louis XIV want in place of toleration?
**Answer:** One king, one law, one faith — religious uniformity under the crown.
