# The Edict of Nantes and Its Revocation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Edict of Nantes grant France's Protestants in 1598?
a) Their own separate kingdom  b) Peace: freedom of conscience, worship in set places, civil rights and towns of safety  c) The throne of France  d) Exemption from all taxes forever
**Answer: b) Peace: freedom of conscience, worship in set places, civil rights and towns of safety** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Louis XIV do in 1685?
a) He signed the Edict of Nantes  b) He revoked the Edict of Nantes, banning Protestant worship  c) He converted to Protestantism  d) He gave Protestants more towns
**Answer: b) He revoked the Edict of Nantes, banning Protestant worship** — That is what this lesson teaches.
