# The Edict of Nantes and Its Revocation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Henri IV had himself been a Protestant before taking the throne as a Catholic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** After the revocation, no Protestants left France.
**Answer: False** — Some two hundred thousand fled to England, Holland, Prussia and beyond, though the law forbade them to leave.
