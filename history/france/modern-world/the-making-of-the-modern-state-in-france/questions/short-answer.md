# The Making of the Modern State in France — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which king signed the Ordinance of Villers-Cotterêts, and in what year? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** François Ier, in 1539.

**2.** What is Colbertism, in one sentence? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The state directing the economy as one design — ordering finances, founding and protecting factories, and building fleets — to make national wealth serve the king's power.
