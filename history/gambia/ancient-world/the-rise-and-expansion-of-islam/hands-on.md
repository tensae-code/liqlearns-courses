# Hands-on — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of how a song or a saying travels: person to person, town to town, until people far away are singing it. A faith can travel the same way — carried not by armies but by people on the move. Who moves most between towns? Hold that answer. This lesson follows a faith along the trade roads.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Islam crossed the Sahara with traders and scholars and took root in West African courts and towns — the faith travelled the same caravan roads as salt and gold. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
