# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did Islam mainly reach West Africa?
a) by sea, in warships  b) across the Sahara with traders and scholars  c) it began in West Africa  d) by European missionaries
**Answer: b) across the Sahara with traders and scholars** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did Islam take root in West Africa?
a) only in desert camps  b) in courts and towns along the trade routes  c) nowhere — it was rejected everywhere  d) only on the coast
**Answer: b) in courts and towns along the trade routes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
