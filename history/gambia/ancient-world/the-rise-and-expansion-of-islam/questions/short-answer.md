# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why were traders and scholars effective carriers of a faith?
**Answer:** Their work moved them from town to town, their learning impressed courts, and their faith travelled with their goods and example.

**2.** Why did rulers welcome Muslim scholars at court?
**Answer:** Scholars were useful counsellors and writers, and linked the court to the wider world of trade and learning.
