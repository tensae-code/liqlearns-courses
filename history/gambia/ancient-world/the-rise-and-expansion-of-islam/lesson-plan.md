# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Gambia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Trade and Faith: West Africa to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Islam crossed the Sahara with traders and scholars and took root in West African courts and towns — the faith travelled the same caravan roads as salt and gold.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Trade and Faith: West Africa to 1500 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Islam crossed the Sahara with traders and scholars and took root in West African courts and towns — the faith travelled the same caravan roads as salt and gold.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a trader or traveller the learner knows who carried a custom, a skill or a belief from one town to another simply by moving and living among people.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
