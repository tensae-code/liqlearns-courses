# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After 1945, West African states took seats in the United Nations.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The OAU still exists unchanged under its original name.
**Answer: False** — It was succeeded and reshaped as the AU — the African Union.
