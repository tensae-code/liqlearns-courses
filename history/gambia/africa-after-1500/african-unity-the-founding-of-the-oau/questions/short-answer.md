# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did West African states build their own bodies as well as joining the UN?
**Answer:** The UN gave them the world's stage; their own bodies — OAU/AU and ECOWAS — let the continent and the region defend their interests and act together, down to keeping peace at home.

**2.** What does the lesson mean by 'unity is machinery'?
**Answer:** Unity was built as working organisations with members, purposes and forces — not left as a slogan.
