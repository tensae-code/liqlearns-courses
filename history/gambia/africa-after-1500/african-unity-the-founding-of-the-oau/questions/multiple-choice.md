# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which bodies does this lesson name, from the world down to the region?
a) NATO, EU and AU  b) the United Nations, the OAU (later the AU), and ECOWAS  c) the League of Nations only  d) no bodies — unity stayed a slogan
**Answer: b) the United Nations, the OAU (later the AU), and ECOWAS** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did ECOWAS add beyond meetings and treaties?
a) a common currency in 1960  b) peacekeepers — West Africans keeping peace among West Africans in the region's wars  c) a single president for the region  d) nothing
**Answer: b) peacekeepers — West Africans keeping peace among West Africans in the region's wars** — That is what this lesson teaches.
