# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After 1945 West Africa joined the world's bodies and built its own — the United Nations, then the OAU, later the AU, and ECOWAS with its peacekeepers: unity organised, at home and in the world. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
