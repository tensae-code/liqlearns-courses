# Lesson plan — African Unity: The Founding of the OAU

**Course:** History — Gambia · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** African Unity: The Founding of the OAU
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Independence and the Wider Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After 1945 West Africa joined the world's bodies and built its own — the United Nations, then the OAU, later the AU, and ECOWAS with its peacekeepers: unity organised, at home and in the world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Independence and the Wider Story — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
