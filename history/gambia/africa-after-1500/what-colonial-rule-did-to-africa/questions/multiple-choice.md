# What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What were colonial rule's three great levers in this lesson?
a) schools, hospitals and railways alone  b) taxes, forced labour and export economies  c) missions, markets and marriages  d) flags, anthems and parades
**Answer: b) taxes, forced labour and export economies** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why did a money tax reorganise village life?
a) it did not — taxes changed nothing  b) families that had fed themselves now had to earn cash, by selling labour or growing crops for sale  c) money taxes were paid in yams  d) villages enjoyed paying them
**Answer: b) families that had fed themselves now had to earn cash, by selling labour or growing crops for sale** — That is what this lesson teaches.
