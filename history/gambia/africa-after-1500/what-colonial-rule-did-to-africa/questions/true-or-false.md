# What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Forced labour was work commanded by the administration, not freely hired.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Colonial rule left what people grew and how they worked essentially unchanged.
**Answer: False** — Taxes, forced labour and export economies remade West African life — crops, work and the purpose of land all changed.
