# What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is an export economy, as the lesson uses it?
**Answer:** Land and labour steered toward crops and minerals for shipment out — groundnuts, cocoa, palm produce, gold — while food for home took what was left.

**2.** Name two lasting shapes the remaking left behind.
**Answer:** Any two of: regions valued only for exports, men absent on labour or migration, chiefs turned into tax collectors, new crops and new hungers.
