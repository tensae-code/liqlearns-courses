# Lesson plan — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

**Course:** History — Gambia · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Contact, Slavery and Colonial Rule (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Christian missions came to West Africa with the anti-slavery conscience, preaching the 'three Cs' — commerce, Christianity and civilisation — as the answer to the slave trade.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Contact, Slavery and Colonial Rule — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Christian missions came to West Africa with the anti-slavery conscience, preaching the 'three Cs' — commerce, Christianity and civilisation — as the answer to the slave trade.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a school or clinic in the learner's area that began as a mission foundation — or any institution whose founders said they came to replace something harmful with something better.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in West Africa, as the book itself works.

- Go back to the one thing: Christian missions came to West Africa with the anti-slavery conscience, preaching the 'three Cs' — commerce, Christianity and civilisation — as the answer to the slave trade. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
