# Hands-on — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
When a great wrong ends, people ask: what should stand in its place? After centuries of the slave trade, some Europeans answered with a programme of three words, all starting with the same letter. Guess what three things they might have proposed before you read. Hold your guesses. This lesson gives the book's answer.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Christian missions came to West Africa with the anti-slavery conscience, preaching the 'three Cs' — commerce, Christianity and civilisation — as the answer to the slave trade. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
