# Video script — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Christian missions came to West Africa with the anti-slavery conscience, preaching the 'three Cs' — commerce, Christianity and civilisation — as the answer to the slave trade.

The slave trade's long shadow produced an answering movement. As the trade was attacked and abolished in the Atlantic world, **Christian missionaries** came to West Africa carrying what the book calls the **anti-slavery conscience** — the conviction that the traffic in people must be replaced, not merely stopped.

Their programme was summed up in the **'three Cs'**: **commerce** — honest trade in goods instead of trade in people; **Christianity** — the faith the missionaries preached and taught; and **civilisation** — the schools, medicine and ways of life the missions believed would transform the region. The three were preached as one package: legitimate commerce would kill the slave trade's market, and the faith and its schools would rebuild society.

Missionaries thus became more than preachers. They opened schools, learned and wrote down languages, treated the sick, and travelled into the interior — mapping, in knowledge and in presence, lands the coastal trade had never reached. Whatever judgment later lessons bring, the missions' arrival changed what Europeans knew of West Africa, and what many West Africans learned in mission schools.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in West Africa.
