# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why were the three Cs preached as one package?
**Answer:** Legitimate commerce would kill the slave trade's market, while the faith and its schools would rebuild society.

**2.** Name two things missionaries did beyond preaching.
**Answer:** Any two of: opened schools, learned and wrote down languages, treated the sick, travelled into the interior.
