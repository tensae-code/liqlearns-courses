# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What were the missionaries' 'three Cs'?
a) cotton, cocoa and coffee  b) commerce, Christianity and civilisation  c) coast, colony and crown  d) churches, castles and cannons
**Answer: b) commerce, Christianity and civilisation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What conscience did the missions bring with them?
a) the anti-slavery conscience — the conviction that the traffic in people must be replaced, not merely stopped  b) indifference to the slave trade  c) a wish to restart the trade  d) no conscience at all
**Answer: a) the anti-slavery conscience — the conviction that the traffic in people must be replaced, not merely stopped** — That is what this lesson teaches.
