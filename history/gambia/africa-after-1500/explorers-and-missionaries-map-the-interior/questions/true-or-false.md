# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The missions proposed honest commerce in goods as a replacement for trade in people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Missionaries only preached; they opened no schools and treated no sick.
**Answer: False** — They opened schools, wrote down languages, treated the sick and travelled into the interior.
