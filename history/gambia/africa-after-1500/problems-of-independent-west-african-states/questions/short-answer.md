# Problems of Independent West African States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did debt become a shadow on independence?
**Answer:** Borrowing for development became a burden that ate the new states' budgets.

**2.** What hard problem stood behind the coups and one-party rule?
**Answer:** How to govern states whose borders, economies and institutions had been made by the colonial rule they had escaped.
