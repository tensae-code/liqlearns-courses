# Problems of Independent West African States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did independence open with, in the book's telling?
a) coups and debt at once  b) schools, roads and clinics — the new states building fast  c) a return to colonial rule  d) nothing changed
**Answer: b) schools, roads and clinics — the new states building fast** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which three shadows fell across the promise?
a) drought, flood and locusts only  b) coups, debt and one-party rule  c) missions, markets and mines  d) rival kings, priests and traders
**Answer: b) coups, debt and one-party rule** — That is what this lesson teaches.
