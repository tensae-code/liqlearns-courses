# Problems of Independent West African States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The new states inherited economies shaped for export and borders drawn by the scramble.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The lesson teaches that independence simply failed.
**Answer: False** — It teaches that independence began a harder problem — governing states whose shapes and institutions were made by the rule they had escaped.
