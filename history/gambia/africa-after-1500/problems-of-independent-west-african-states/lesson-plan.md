**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Independence and the Wider Story — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Independence opened with schools, roads and clinics — the new West African states building fast — and then coups, debt and one-party rule shadowed the promise.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a new school, club or family venture the learner knows that began with energy and building, then met leadership quarrels or money owed — and what decided whether it survived.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in West Africa, as the book itself works.
