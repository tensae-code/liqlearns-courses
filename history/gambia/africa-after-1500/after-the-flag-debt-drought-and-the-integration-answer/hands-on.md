# Hands-on — After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One trader alone cannot fill a ship or bargain with a giant buyer. Ten traders together can. Think of any problem that is simply too big for one household but manageable for a street, a village, a union. Hold that arithmetic. This lesson applies it to whole countries.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Small, indebted states looked to each other for the scale none had alone — regional integration was West Africa's answer to economic problems no single small state could solve. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
