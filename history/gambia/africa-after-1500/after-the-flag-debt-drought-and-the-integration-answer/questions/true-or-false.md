# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Together, the region had a scale none of its small states had alone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Integration instantly erased debt and drought.
**Answer: False** — Debt and drought did not read treaties; integration changed the question to what the region could do together.
