# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why were the independent states' economic problems structural?
a) their leaders were lazy  b) the states were mostly small and indebted, with export earnings swinging on prices they did not control  c) they had no resources at all  d) they refused all trade
**Answer: b) the states were mostly small and indebted, with export earnings swinging on prices they did not control** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was the regional-integration answer?
a) each state borrowing more alone  b) states pooling scale — shared markets, bargaining power and projects — that none had alone  c) returning to colonial rule  d) closing all borders
**Answer: b) states pooling scale — shared markets, bargaining power and projects — that none had alone** — That is what this lesson teaches.
