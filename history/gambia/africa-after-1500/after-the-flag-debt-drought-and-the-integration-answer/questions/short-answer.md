# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What could a region do that a single small state could not?
**Answer:** Bargain, build and borrow on stronger terms — bigger shared markets, shared bargaining power, and projects too large for one budget.

**2.** How did integration change the question facing the states?
**Answer:** From what one small state could do alone, to what West Africa could do as a region.
