# Lesson plan — The Environment in West African History

**Course:** History — Gambia · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The Environment in West African History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Independence and the Wider Story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Climate, disease and landscape shaped West African states and trade as surely as kings did — the environment is not the stage of the region's history but one of its actors.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Independence and the Wider Story — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Climate, disease and landscape shaped West African states and trade as surely as kings did — the environment is not the stage of the region's history but one of its actors.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a plan in the learner's own life changed by rain, heat, sickness or the land itself — and the learner saying what the 'actor' in that small story was.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
