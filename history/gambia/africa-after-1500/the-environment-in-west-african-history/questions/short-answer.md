# The Environment in West African History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three environmental actors this lesson follows.
**Answer:** Climate, disease and landscape.

**2.** Why do the authors place this chapter at the end of the book?
**Answer:** Because it re-reads every earlier chapter: states, trade and wars were all shaped by the environment as well as by people — half the causes are missing without it.
