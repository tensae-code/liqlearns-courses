# The Environment in West African History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is this chapter's claim about the environment?
a) it is mere scenery behind human events  b) it is an actor in history — climate, disease and landscape shaped states and trade as surely as kings did  c) it stopped mattering after 1500  d) it matters only to farmers
**Answer: b) it is an actor in history — climate, disease and landscape shaped states and trade as surely as kings did** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did disease act in West African history?
a) it never affected events  b) fevers guarded the coast against outsiders for centuries, and sickness could empty regions or break armies  c) it only affected Europeans  d) it was cured by the missions at once
**Answer: b) fevers guarded the coast against outsiders for centuries, and sickness could empty regions or break armies** — That is what this lesson teaches.
