# The Environment in West African History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Caravan routes and seasons were set by climate as much as by merchants' wishes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bringing the environment into history removes people from the story.
**Answer: False** — People still chose and decided — but inside a world of rain, fever and land that rewarded some choices and punished others.
