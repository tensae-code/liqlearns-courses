# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under the Berlin Act, a power had to govern what it claimed, not merely draw it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A claim on a map was already a working colony, with no occupation needed.
**Answer: False** — The Berlin Act counted a claim only if effectively occupied — officials and administration on the ground.
