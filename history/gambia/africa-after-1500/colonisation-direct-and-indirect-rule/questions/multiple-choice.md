# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Berlin Act require for a claim to count?
a) a bigger flag  b) effective occupation — real administration on the ground  c) a royal signature alone  d) nothing at all
**Answer: b) effective occupation — real administration on the ground** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the effective-occupation rule turn paper claims into?
a) treaties  b) administrations — real systems of colonial government  c) trading posts only  d) mission stations
**Answer: b) administrations — real systems of colonial government** — That is what this lesson teaches.
