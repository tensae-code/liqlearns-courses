# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the Berlin rule change colonisation from a map exercise into government?
**Answer:** Because holding territory now required officials, posts and administration in every claimed district — revenue, order and machinery of rule.

**2.** What question does this lesson hand to the next one?
**Answer:** How exactly to govern — the empires answered differently, ruling through chiefs or directly through their own officials.
