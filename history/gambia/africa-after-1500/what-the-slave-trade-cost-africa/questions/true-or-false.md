# What the Slave Trade Cost Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The trade's damage was done only to the people shipped across the Atlantic.
**Answer: False** — Its deepest damage was in West Africa itself — kingdoms, farms and families bent around the demand for captives.

**2.** Wars fought to take captives warped West African politics.
**Answer: True**
