# What the Slave Trade Cost Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In which three fields does this lesson count the trade's cost at home?
a) music, dress and language  b) politics, farming and daily life  c) ports, ships and sails  d) coinage, roads and bridges
**Answer: b) politics, farming and daily life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the trade bend farming?
a) it introduced new crops everywhere  b) people were seized in planting and harvest seasons, villages abandoned, labour drained in its strongest years  c) it made farming unnecessary  d) it doubled every harvest
**Answer: b) people were seized in planting and harvest seasons, villages abandoned, labour drained in its strongest years** — That is what this lesson teaches.
