# What the Slave Trade Cost Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did power change in kingdoms caught in the trade?
**Answer:** Rulers grew rich and armed by selling people, wars were fought to take captives, and power came to be measured in guns and captives.

**2.** What was the cost in daily life?
**Answer:** Fear woven into the ordinary: journeys made in company, settlements moved to safer ground, families broken by raids and never rejoined.
