# Lesson plan — What the Slave Trade Cost Africa

**Course:** History — Gambia · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** What the Slave Trade Cost Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Contact, Slavery and Colonial Rule (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The slave trade bent West African kingdoms, farms and families around the demand for captives — its cost was paid in politics, in farming and in daily life.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Contact, Slavery and Colonial Rule — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The slave trade bent West African kingdoms, farms and families around the demand for captives — its cost was paid in politics, in farming and in daily life.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a family or village the learner knows of that was reshaped by people being taken away — by war, by migration for work — and what changed for those who stayed.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in West Africa, as the book itself works.
