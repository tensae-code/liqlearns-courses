# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When did Portuguese ships reach the Senegal river, opening this story?
a) 1342  b) 1442  c) 1542  d) 1884
**Answer: b) 1442** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** On whose terms did the first coastal trade run?
a) on European terms, set by ship captains  b) on African terms — rulers decided where Europeans could trade and what they must pay  c) on no terms at all  d) on terms set in Lisbon for both sides
**Answer: b) on African terms — rulers decided where Europeans could trade and what they must pay** — That is what this lesson teaches.
