# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did African rulers control in the first coastal trade?
**Answer:** Where Europeans could land and trade, what goods were wanted, and what the strangers paid for the privilege.

**2.** How did the new European traders arrive, differently from the old ones?
**Answer:** By sea, in ships — where the older trade had come across the desert by camel caravan.
