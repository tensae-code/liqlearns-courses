# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What were European powers racing to do in the scramble?
**Answer:** To claim West African territory before each other — partitioning the region into colonies.

**2.** What does 'the map flipped' mean here?
**Answer:** In about forty years the region changed from African states and coastal trading posts into colonies claimed by European empires.
