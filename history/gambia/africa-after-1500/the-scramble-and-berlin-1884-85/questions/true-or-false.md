# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The scramble's straight borders are still visible on today's map of West Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** By 1910, most of West Africa still stood outside European rule.
**Answer: False** — By 1910 only Liberia and Ethiopia escaped European rule.
