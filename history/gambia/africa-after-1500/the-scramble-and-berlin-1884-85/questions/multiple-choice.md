# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In about how long did the map of West Africa flip in the scramble?
a) four hundred years  b) forty years  c) four years  d) it never flipped
**Answer: b) forty years** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two lands escaped European rule by 1910?
a) Ghana and Nigeria  b) Liberia and Ethiopia  c) Senegal and Mali  d) Sierra Leone and Gambia
**Answer: b) Liberia and Ethiopia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
