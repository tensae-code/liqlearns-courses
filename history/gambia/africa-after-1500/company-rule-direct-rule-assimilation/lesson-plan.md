# Lesson plan — Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation

**Course:** History — Gambia · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Contact, Slavery and Colonial Rule (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain's indirect rule and France's direct rule and assimilation were opposite recipes for ruling the same region — ruling through chiefs on one side, ruling through French officials to make Frenchmen on the other — and West Africans lived the difference.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Contact, Slavery and Colonial Rule — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Britain's indirect rule and France's direct rule and assimilation were opposite recipes for ruling the same region — ruling through chiefs on one side, ruling through French officials to make Frenchmen on the other — and West Africans lived the difference.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: two neighbouring schools, offices or local governments the learner knows that run the same kind of place by opposite recipes — through local leaders, or directly from the centre.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
