# Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was Britain's recipe of rule in this comparison?
a) assimilation  b) indirect rule — governing through existing chiefs who answered to British officials  c) no rule at all  d) rule by missionaries
**Answer: b) indirect rule — governing through existing chiefs who answered to British officials** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What stood behind France's direct rule?
a) indirect rule  b) assimilation — the idea that colonised people could become French in language, law and ways  c) a plan to leave quickly  d) rule through emirs
**Answer: b) assimilation — the idea that colonised people could become French in language, law and ways** — That is what this lesson teaches.
