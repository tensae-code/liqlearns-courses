# Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under indirect rule, chiefs kept their thrones but answered to foreign officials above them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The British and French systems were identical in practice.
**Answer: False** — They were opposite recipes — indirect rule through chiefs against direct rule and assimilation — and West Africans lived the difference.
