# Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was indirect rule attractive to Britain?
**Answer:** It was cheap and used the authority people already knew — governing through chiefs rather than paying for officials everywhere.

**2.** Who lived the difference between the two systems, and in what?
**Answer:** West Africans — in taxes, schooling, a chief's power and a family's future, depending on which empire's border enclosed them.
