# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the empires' advancing lines create danger between the empires themselves?
**Answer:** Because rival powers were pushing toward each other across the same continent; where their columns met, as at Fashoda, partition nearly became war between them.

**2.** How was the Fashoda collision avoided, and who was left out?
**Answer:** By diplomacy between the empires; the peoples inside the lines were left out of both the drawing and the settling.
