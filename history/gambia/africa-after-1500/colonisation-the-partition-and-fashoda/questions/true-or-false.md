# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Boundaries drawn in the partition cut through peoples and grazing lands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The peoples inside the new boundaries were consulted in the drawing and settling of the lines.
**Answer: False** — They were not consulted in either the drawing or the settling.
