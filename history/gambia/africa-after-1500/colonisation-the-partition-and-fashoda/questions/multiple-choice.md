# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How was the partition carried out after the claims were declared?
a) it enforced itself  b) on the ground — expeditions, treaties, planted flags, boundaries drawn and enforced  c) by African parliaments  d) it was never carried out
**Answer: b) on the ground — expeditions, treaties, planted flags, boundaries drawn and enforced** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What nearly happened at Fashoda?
a) a great battle between African states  b) rival European empires nearly collided — the partition nearly turned into war between the partitioners  c) a volcanic eruption  d) the discovery of gold ended the scramble
**Answer: b) rival European empires nearly collided — the partition nearly turned into war between the partitioners** — That is what this lesson teaches.
