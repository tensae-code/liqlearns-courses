# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first captives of the trade were taken at the Senegal river in 1442.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The trans-Atlantic slave trade lasted only a few decades.
**Answer: False** — It ran from 1442 to 1866 — more than four centuries.
