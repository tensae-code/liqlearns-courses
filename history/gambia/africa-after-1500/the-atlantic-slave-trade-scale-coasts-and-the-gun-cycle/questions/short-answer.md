# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was it dangerous for a state to refuse the trade while its neighbours armed?
**Answer:** Because the gun cycle armed trading states; an unarmed neighbour stood exposed to raids by gun-holding rivals.

**2.** What turned human beings into the coast's most sought-after export?
**Answer:** European demand for labour in the Americas.
