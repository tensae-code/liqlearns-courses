# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the book's dating, when does the trans-Atlantic slave trade run?
a) 1442–1866  b) 1500–1600 only  c) 1807–1900  d) 1884–1914
**Answer: a) 1442–1866** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the gun cycle?
a) guns repaired in a circle of workshops  b) captives exchanged for guns; guns making wars and raids that produced more captives; more captives buying more guns  c) a yearly gun festival  d) guns traded only for gold
**Answer: b) captives exchanged for guns; guns making wars and raids that produced more captives; more captives buying more guns** — That is what this lesson teaches.
