# Women and Authority in West African History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two forms of authority women held in the precolonial world of this chapter. (Sub lesson 1) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spiritual authority — as priestesses and custodians of shrines — and political authority in councils, markets and royal courts.

**2.** Through what system did Ahebi Ugbabe rise? (Sub lesson 2) (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Through British indirect rule itself — she became a warrant chief under it, and then king.
