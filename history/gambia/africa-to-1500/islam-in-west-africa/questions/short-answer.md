# Islam in West Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did kingship change under Islam's influence?
**Answer:** Rulers gained new titles and ceremonies and a link to a world-wide community of believers.

**2.** What does it mean that the two changed each other?
**Answer:** Islam reshaped West African law, learning and kingship, while West African life gave the faith its own local colours and forms.
