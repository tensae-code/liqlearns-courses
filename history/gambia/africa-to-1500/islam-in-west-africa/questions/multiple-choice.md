# Islam in West Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which three things did Islam reshape in West Africa?
a) farming, fishing and weaving  b) law, learning and kingship  c) roads, ports and coinage  d) music, dress and food only
**Answer: b) law, learning and kingship** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened to the older ways when Islam took root?
a) they vanished at once  b) they lived side by side with the new faith, and the two changed each other  c) they were outlawed in every town  d) they drove Islam back across the desert
**Answer: b) they lived side by side with the new faith, and the two changed each other** — That is what this lesson teaches.
