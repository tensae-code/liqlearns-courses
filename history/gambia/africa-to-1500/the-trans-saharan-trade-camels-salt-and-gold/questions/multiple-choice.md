# The Trans-Saharan Trade: Camels, Salt and Gold — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What organised the trans-Saharan traffic, as this lesson teaches?
a) ships and sails  b) camels, salt and gold  c) railways and steam  d) elephants and ivory alone
**Answer: b) camels, salt and gold** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When did the routes linking West Africa to the Mediterranean begin?
a) only after Islam arrived  b) long before Islam  c) with the Portuguese ships  d) in the colonial period
**Answer: b) long before Islam** — That is what this lesson teaches.
