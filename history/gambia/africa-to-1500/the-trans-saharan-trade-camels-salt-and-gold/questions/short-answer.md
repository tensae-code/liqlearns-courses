# The Trans-Saharan Trade: Camels, Salt and Gold — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was salt worth carrying across a desert?
**Answer:** The south needed it to live and to preserve food, and salt was scarce there — while the desert north had salt mines.

**2.** Why call the camel the ship of this sea?
**Answer:** Because it crossed the desert as a ship crosses water — carrying heavy loads for days without water, where no cart could go.
