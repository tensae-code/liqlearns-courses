# The Trans-Saharan Trade: Camels, Salt and Gold — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Salt travelled south and gold travelled north in the trans-Saharan trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Sahara could not be crossed before modern transport.
**Answer: False** — Camel caravans crossed it like a sea, in an organised system of routes, wells and staging towns.
