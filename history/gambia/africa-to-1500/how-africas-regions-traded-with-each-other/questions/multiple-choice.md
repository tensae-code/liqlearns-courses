# How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three things did the trans-Saharan trade grow across the savanna?
a) ports, railways and factories  b) towns, states and scholarship  c) plantations, mines and barracks  d) missions, forts and prisons
**Answer: b) towns, states and scholarship** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did rulers and trade help each other?
a) rulers banned trade to stay pure  b) rulers taxed caravans and markets, and in return protected the roads  c) traders ruled the states directly  d) states and trade never met
**Answer: b) rulers taxed caravans and markets, and in return protected the roads** — That is what this lesson teaches.
