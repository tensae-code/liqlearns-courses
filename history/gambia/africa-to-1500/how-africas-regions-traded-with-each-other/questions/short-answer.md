# How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does a caravan staging point become a town?
**Answer:** Caravans need rest, water, reloading and exchange; markets form at those points, and markets draw farmers, craftsmen, guards and scholars until a town stands there.

**2.** Why did states have a reason to keep peace along the trade routes?
**Answer:** Because they taxed the trade — peace on the roads kept the caravans, and the revenue, coming.
