# Lesson plan — How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other

**Course:** History — Gambia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Trade and Faith: West Africa to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The trans-Saharan trade grew towns, states and scholarship across the savanna — where caravans stopped, markets rose; where markets rose, rulers taxed and protected them, and learning travelled the same roads as goods.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The trans-Saharan trade grew towns, states and scholarship across the savanna — where caravans stopped, markets rose; where markets rose, rulers taxed and protected them, and learning travelled the same roads as goods.
