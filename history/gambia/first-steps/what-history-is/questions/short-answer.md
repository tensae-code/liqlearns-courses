# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What question does the historian ask that a fact-collector does not?
**Answer:** What life was like for the people of the past, and why they acted as they did.

**2.** Name the craft this chapter teaches, in the book's own words.
**Answer:** Historiography and historical skills — the craft of writing history.
