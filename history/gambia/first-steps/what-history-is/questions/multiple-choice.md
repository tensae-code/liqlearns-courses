# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the historian's real goal, as this lesson teaches?
a) collecting as many facts as possible  b) understanding the people who lived the past  c) memorising every date in order  d) copying old books word for word
**Answer: b) understanding the people who lived the past** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is a bare list of dates and names not yet history?
a) it is too short  b) it says nothing about what life was like for the people or why they acted  c) lists are forbidden in history books  d) dates cannot be written down
**Answer: b) it says nothing about what life was like for the people or why they acted** — That is what this lesson teaches.
