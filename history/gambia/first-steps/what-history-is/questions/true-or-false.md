# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The historian's goal is collecting facts, the way a basket collects fruit.
**Answer: False** — The lesson is plain: the goal is understanding the people who lived the past; facts are only the road to it.

**2.** A written history succeeds when the people of the past become understandable.
**Answer: True**
