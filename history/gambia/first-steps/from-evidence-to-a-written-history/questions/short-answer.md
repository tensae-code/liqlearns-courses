# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does it mean to cross-examine sources?
**Answer:** To check each source against the others — where they agree the ground is firm; where they disagree, the disagreement itself is a clue.

**2.** Give one strength and one weakness of written records.
**Answer:** Strength: writing fixes words in place. Weakness: every writer had a purpose, a side and a blind spot.
