# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the historian's three kinds of sources in this lesson?
a) kings, priests and soldiers  b) oral tradition, written records and material evidence  c) books, films and newspapers  d) maps, coins and flags
**Answer: b) oral tradition, written records and material evidence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is no single source believed alone?
a) sources are always false  b) each kind can err or mislead in its own way, so they are cross-examined against each other  c) historians are paid by the source  d) one source is too heavy to carry
**Answer: b) each kind can err or mislead in its own way, so they are cross-examined against each other** — That is what this lesson teaches.
