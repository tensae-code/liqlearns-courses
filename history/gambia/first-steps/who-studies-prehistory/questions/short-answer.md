# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why would the past be almost all darkness if only written sources counted?
**Answer:** Because writing is a late invention — the long ages before it left no documents, only things.

**2.** Name three kinds of remains an archaeologist reads.
**Answer:** Any three of: shaped stone tools, bones, ash from hearths, broken pots, floors of vanished houses.
