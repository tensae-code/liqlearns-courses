# Who Studies Prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who studies prehistory, and from what?
a) historians, from letters  b) archaeologists, from stones, bones and buried remains  c) griots, from genealogies  d) priests, from sacred books
**Answer: b) archaeologists, from stones, bones and buried remains** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is the position of a buried find part of the evidence?
a) it makes the find prettier  b) where a thing lay, and what lay beside it, helps read what it meant and when  c) deep objects are worth more money  d) position does not matter at all
**Answer: b) where a thing lay, and what lay beside it, helps read what it meant and when** — That is what this lesson teaches.
