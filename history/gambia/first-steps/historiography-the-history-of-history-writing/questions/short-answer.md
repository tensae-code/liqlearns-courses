# Historiography: The History of History-Writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does knowing who wrote a history, and when, add to reading it?
**Answer:** It shows the questions and blind spots the writer brought — part of judging the history itself.

**2.** Whose voices helped prove African history could be studied rigorously?
**Answer:** African historians, and the oral traditions carried for centuries by griots.
