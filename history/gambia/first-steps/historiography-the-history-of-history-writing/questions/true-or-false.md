# Historiography: The History of History-Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** European writers once claimed Africa had no history worth the name.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The past itself changes every time a new history is written.
**Answer: False** — The past does not change; its writing does — each age rewrites it with its own questions.
