# Historiography: The History of History-Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why does each age rewrite the past?
a) because the past keeps changing  b) because each age asks the past its own questions  c) because old books wear out  d) because historians dislike old work
**Answer: b) because each age asks the past its own questions** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did African history have to do before scholars took it seriously?
a) wait for European permission to exist  b) fight its way into the academy, proving itself from Africa's own sources  c) abandon oral tradition entirely  d) be written only in Latin
**Answer: b) fight its way into the academy, proving itself from Africa's own sources** — That is what this lesson teaches.
