# History — Bulgaria course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Bulgaria's own — built from Bulgaria's Рива (Riva) *История и цивилизации* (History and Civilizations) student books (Bulgarian). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': one unbroken national story — first steps and the ancient Balkans, the First Bulgarian Empire inside medieval Europe, the Second Empire and the Ottoman centuries, the Revival and Liberation, the free state and the wars, and communism, 1989 and the Euro-Atlantic road. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
