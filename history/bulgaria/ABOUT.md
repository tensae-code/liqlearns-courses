# About the History — Bulgaria course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Bulgaria's own Рива (Riva) *История и цивилизации* (History and Civilizations) student books. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Bulgaria inside the History folder (one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **six levels**, following the national story as Bulgaria's own books present it (see CURRICULUM.md for the derivation): Level 1 — first steps and the ancient Balkans (10 lessons); Level 2 — medieval Europe and the First Bulgarian Empire (18 lessons); Level 3 — the Second Empire and the Ottoman centuries (16 lessons); Level 4 — Revival, Uprising and Liberation (11 lessons); Level 5 — the free state, the wars and the interwar years (12 lessons); Level 6 — communism, 1989 and the Euro-Atlantic road (9 lessons).

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *The arc whole:* "First, how history is written — from evidence, from numbers, from the legends elders still tell — and the deep past of these lands: the first fires, the Karanovo mound, the Thracians' gold. Then an empire is founded on the Danube in 681, baptised under Boris, and raised to a Golden Age under Simeon, inside a medieval Europe of Byzantium, Charlemagne, knights and cathedrals. Then the Second Empire of the Asenevtsi, and the long Ottoman centuries, when the Church and the monasteries keep a people alive without a state. Then the Revival: Paisii's handwritten history wakes a nation, schools and a church of its own are won, the April Uprising bleeds, and Liberation comes in 1878. Then the free state — unification, independence, two Balkan wars and two world wars, and the Jews of Bulgaria saved. And last the communist decades, the fall of 10 November 1989, and the road to NATO and the European Union."

## The way of the books

Bulgaria's own way, as filed: Grade 7 is one unbroken national story from the 18th century to EU membership — Ottoman rule and the Revival awakeners, the April Uprising and Liberation, Tarnovo's constitution-makers, the saved Bulgarian Jews of the Second World War, communist takeover and totalitarianism, and the 1989 transition — told through "Historical Laboratory" document work and self-control checks. Grade 6 runs medieval Europe and medieval Bulgaria in parallel columns — Byzantium beside the First Bulgarian Empire, Cyril and Methodius beside Charlemagne — and closes with the Bulgarian lands inside the Ottoman state, so the national story is never separated from the continent's. Grade 10 is the whole national epic in one volume — Thracians and the Karanovo mound, Old Great Bulgaria, both medieval empires, five Ottoman centuries, the Revival, the wars, Zhivkov and the transition — the same story the Grade 6 and 7 books split in two, now told end to end with "Historical Laboratory" document work at every age. The same Bulgarian lessons are therefore stocked two or three times over — once from each book that teaches them — and each book's pass stands as its own sub lesson (or part) inside the one Bulgaria lesson, in file order.
