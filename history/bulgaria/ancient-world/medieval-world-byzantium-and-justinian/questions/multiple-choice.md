# Byzantium and Justinian — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 3: Byzantium — the unbreakability of the empire”?
a) The eastern empire survives a thousand years where the west fell.  b) Krum's laws and victories, Omurtag's building — pagan Bulgaria at its strongest.  c) Guilds, markets and village communes organise life under the timar system.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The eastern empire survives a thousand years where the west fell.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 9: The changes in Byzantium and Western Europe in the 9th century”?
a) Icons, emperors and new kingdoms reshape both halves of the Christian world.  b) Homes, food, dress and feast days in medieval Bulgaria.  c) The state tradition is restored after 1878 — prince, assembly and a nation to rebuild.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Icons, emperors and new kingdoms reshape both halves of the Christian world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
