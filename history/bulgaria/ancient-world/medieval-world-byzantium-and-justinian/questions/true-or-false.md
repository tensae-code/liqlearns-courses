# Byzantium and Justinian — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The eastern empire survives a thousand years where the west fell.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 9: The changes in Byzantium and Western Europe in the 9th century” is mainly about this: Behind the Iron Curtain, Bulgaria is the Warsaw Pact's most faithful member.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Icons, emperors and new kingdoms reshape both halves of the Christian world.
