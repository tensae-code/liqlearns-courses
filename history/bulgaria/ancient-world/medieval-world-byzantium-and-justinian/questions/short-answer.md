# Byzantium and Justinian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 3: Byzantium — the unbreakability of the empire”?
**Answer:** The eastern empire survives a thousand years where the west fell. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 9: The changes in Byzantium and Western Europe in the 9th century” end?
**Answer:** Icons, emperors and new kingdoms reshape both halves of the Christian world. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
