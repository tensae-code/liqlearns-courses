# The Ottoman Empire: Rise and Fall — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 37: The conquest of Southeastern Europe by the Ottoman Turks”?
**Answer:** The Ottomans cross into Europe and conquer the Balkans state by state. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 20: The Ottoman conquest and the end of the medieval Bulgarian state” end?
**Answer:** Tarnovo falls (1393) and Vidin follows — the medieval Bulgarian state ends. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
