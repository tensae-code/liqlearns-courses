# The Ottoman Empire: Rise and Fall — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Ottomans cross into Europe and conquer the Balkans state by state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 20: The Ottoman conquest and the end of the medieval Bulgarian state” is mainly about this: Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Tarnovo falls (1393) and Vidin follows — the medieval Bulgarian state ends.
