# Medieval Bulgarian Society and Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 19: Society and everyday life of the Bulgarians to the 10th–11th centuries”?
**Answer:** Boyars and peasants, villages and fortresses — how medieval Bulgarians lived. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 19: The everyday life of the Bulgarians in the Middle Ages” end?
**Answer:** Homes, food, dress and feast days in medieval Bulgaria. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
