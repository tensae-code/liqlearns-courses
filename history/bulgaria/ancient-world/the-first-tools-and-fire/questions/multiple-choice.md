# The First Tools and Fire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 1: The appearance of humans on the Balkans in the prehistoric era”?
a) The first humans reach the Balkans and master tools and fire in Europe's oldest inhabited corner.  b) Bulgaria joins the Central Powers to regain Macedonia and loses a second national catastrophe.  c) Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The first humans reach the Balkans and master tools and fire in Europe's oldest inhabited corner.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The first humans reach the Balkans and master tools and fire in Europe's oldest inhabited corner.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
