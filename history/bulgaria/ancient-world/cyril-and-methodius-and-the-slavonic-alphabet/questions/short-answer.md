# Cyril and Methodius and the Slavonic Alphabet — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 22: The work of SS Cyril and Methodius and their disciples”?
**Answer:** Two brothers invent an alphabet for the Slavs; their disciples carry it to Bulgaria. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 9: The holy brothers Cyril and Methodius — apostles and enlighteners of the Slavic world” end?
**Answer:** The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
