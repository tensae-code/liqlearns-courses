# Cyril and Methodius and the Slavonic Alphabet — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two brothers invent an alphabet for the Slavs; their disciples carry it to Bulgaria.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 9: The holy brothers Cyril and Methodius — apostles and enlighteners of the Slavic world” is mainly about this: Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples.
