# Cyril and Methodius and the Slavonic Alphabet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 22: The work of SS Cyril and Methodius and their disciples”?
a) Two brothers invent an alphabet for the Slavs; their disciples carry it to Bulgaria.  b) Hemmed in by Neuilly, Bulgaria seeks peaceful revision of the treaty map.  c) Crafts, trade and a rising Bulgarian economy grow inside the Ottoman world.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Two brothers invent an alphabet for the Slavs; their disciples carry it to Bulgaria.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 9: The holy brothers Cyril and Methodius — apostles and enlighteners of the Slavic world”?
a) The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples.  b) The year's story opens by recalling medieval Europe's heritage and Bulgaria's place in it, before the modern national story begins.  c) In 1908 Bulgaria declares full independence and Ferdinand takes the title of Tsar.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
