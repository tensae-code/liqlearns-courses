# Cities, Guilds and Fairs Revive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 30–31: Society in Western Europe; the cities of Western Europe”?
a) Cities revive with guilds, fairs and burghers who owe no lord.  b) Newspapers, theatres and painting modernise the Revival's spirit.  c) Show trials, camps and propaganda build the totalitarian state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Cities revive with guilds, fairs and burghers who owe no lord.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
