# Bulgarian Society, 12th–14th Centuries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 32: Societies in Byzantium and Bulgaria in the 12th–14th centuries”?
**Answer:** Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 18: The economic life of the Bulgarians” end?
**Answer:** Fields, crafts and trade sustain the Second Empire's towns and villages. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
