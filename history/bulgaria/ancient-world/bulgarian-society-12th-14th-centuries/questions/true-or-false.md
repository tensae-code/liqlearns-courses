# Bulgarian Society, 12th–14th Centuries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 18: The economic life of the Bulgarians” is mainly about this: Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Fields, crafts and trade sustain the Second Empire's towns and villages.
