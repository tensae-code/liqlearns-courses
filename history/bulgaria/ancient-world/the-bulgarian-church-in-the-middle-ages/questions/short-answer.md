# The Bulgarian Church in the Middle Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Lesson 17: The Bulgarian Orthodox Church in the 10th–14th centuries”?
**Answer:** Patriarchate, exarchate and hesychasm — the church carries the nation through empire and occupation. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** How does the story in “Lesson 17: The Bulgarian Orthodox Church in the 10th–14th centuries” end?
**Answer:** Patriarchate, exarchate and hesychasm — the church carries the nation through empire and occupation. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
