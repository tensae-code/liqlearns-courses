# The Crusades: Why Europeans Went — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Crusading becomes a way of life for two centuries of knights, pilgrims and merchants.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 14: Kaloyan and the Crusaders” is mainly about this: Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Fourth Crusade's Latin Empire meets its match in Bulgaria's tsar.
