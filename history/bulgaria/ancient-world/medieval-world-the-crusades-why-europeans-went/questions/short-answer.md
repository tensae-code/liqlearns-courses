# The Crusades: Why Europeans Went — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 26: The Crusades — a way of life”?
**Answer:** Crusading becomes a way of life for two centuries of knights, pilgrims and merchants. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 14: Kaloyan and the Crusaders” end?
**Answer:** The Fourth Crusade's Latin Empire meets its match in Bulgaria's tsar. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
