# Bulgaria: The Last Century of the Second Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 29: The last century of the medieval Bulgarian state”?
**Answer:** Ivan Alexander's divided realm weakens as the Ottomans cross into Europe. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 16: Bulgaria in the second half of the 13th century — remains of past greatness” end?
**Answer:** Tatar raids, peasant tsars and shrinking borders — the empire lives on its past. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
