# Bulgaria: The Last Century of the Second Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ivan Alexander's divided realm weakens as the Ottomans cross into Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 16: Bulgaria in the second half of the 13th century — remains of past greatness” is mainly about this: Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Tatar raids, peasant tsars and shrinking borders — the empire lives on its past.
