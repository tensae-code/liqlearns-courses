# The Ottoman State and Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lessons 39–40: The structure of the Ottoman state; economic and social relations in the Ottoman Empire”?
**Answer:** Sultan, timars and millets — how the Ottoman state organised the lands it ruled. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lessons 23–24: Economic and social life; Bulgarian society in the 15th–17th centuries” end?
**Answer:** Guilds, markets and village communes organise life under the timar system. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
