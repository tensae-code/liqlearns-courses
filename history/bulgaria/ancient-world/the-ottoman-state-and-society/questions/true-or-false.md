# The Ottoman State and Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sultan, timars and millets — how the Ottoman state organised the lands it ruled.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lessons 23–24: Economic and social life; Bulgarian society in the 15th–17th centuries” is mainly about this: Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Guilds, markets and village communes organise life under the timar system.
