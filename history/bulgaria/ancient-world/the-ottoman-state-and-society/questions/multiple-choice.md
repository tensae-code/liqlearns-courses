# The Ottoman State and Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 39–40: The structure of the Ottoman state; economic and social relations in the Ottoman Empire”?
a) Sultan, timars and millets — how the Ottoman state organised the lands it ruled.  b) Committees and revolutionaries — the "apostles of Bulgarian freedom" — organise the rising against Ottoman rule.  c) Opposition is crushed, the constitution replaced, and a totalitarian regime is imposed.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Sultan, timars and millets — how the Ottoman state organised the lands it ruled.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
