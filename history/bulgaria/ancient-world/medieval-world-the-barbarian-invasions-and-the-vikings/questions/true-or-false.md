# The Barbarian Invasions and the Vikings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) New peoples pour into the Roman world and redraw the map of Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 4: The Great Migration of the peoples” is mainly about this: Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Peoples on the move shatter the old Roman order across Europe.
