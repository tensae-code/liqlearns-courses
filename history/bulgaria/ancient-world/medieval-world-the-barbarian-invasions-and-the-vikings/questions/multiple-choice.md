# The Barbarian Invasions and the Vikings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 2: The new peoples”?
a) New peoples pour into the Roman world and redraw the map of Europe.  b) Boyars and peasants, villages and fortresses — how medieval Bulgarians lived.  c) Taxes, faith and village life — how Bulgarians lived inside the empire for centuries.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) New peoples pour into the Roman world and redraw the map of Europe.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4: The Great Migration of the peoples”?
a) Peoples on the move shatter the old Roman order across Europe.  b) Homes, clothes, tools, games and feasts — the material world medieval people touched.  c) The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Peoples on the move shatter the old Roman order across Europe.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
