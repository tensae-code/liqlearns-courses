# Bulgaria: Founding of the First Empire, 681 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 6: The creation and strengthening of the Bulgarian state on the Balkan Peninsula”?
a) Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and found a state (681) that Byzantium must recognise.  b) Revival Plovdiv — "the heart of Rumelia" — trades its way into the modern world.  c) The 1908 declaration makes Bulgaria a fully independent kingdom.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
