# Bulgaria: Founding of the First Empire, 681 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 6: The creation and strengthening of the Bulgarian state on the Balkan Peninsula”?
**Answer:** Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and found a state (681) that Byzantium must recognise. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 6: The birth of Danubian Bulgaria” end?
**Answer:** Kubrat's son Asparuh founds Danubian Bulgaria south of the river (681). Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
