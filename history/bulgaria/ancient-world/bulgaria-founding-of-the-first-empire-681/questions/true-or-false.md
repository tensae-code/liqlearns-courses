# Bulgaria: Founding of the First Empire, 681 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and found a state (681) that Byzantium must recognise.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 6: The birth of Danubian Bulgaria” is mainly about this: Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Kubrat's son Asparuh founds Danubian Bulgaria south of the river (681).
