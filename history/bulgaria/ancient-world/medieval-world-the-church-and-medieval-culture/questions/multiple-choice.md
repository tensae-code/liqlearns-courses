# The Church and Medieval Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 21: Culture and cultural heritage of Christian Europe”?
a) The church carries learning and art through the medieval centuries.  b) The Asen brothers raise the Second Bulgarian Empire (1185) and Kaloyan and Ivan Asen II make it great.  c) Peoples on the move shatter the old Roman order across Europe.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The church carries learning and art through the medieval centuries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 21: Culture and cultural heritage of Christian Europe”?
a) The church carries learning and art through the medieval centuries.  b) Layer on layer at Karanovo, farming villages rise in the Bulgarian lands across thousands of years.  c) Tatar raids, peasant tsars and shrinking borders — the empire lives on its past.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The church carries learning and art through the medieval centuries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
