# Christianity and the Other Religions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 15: Christianity and the other religions”?
a) Christians, Jews and Muslims live side by side — and collide — in the medieval world.  b) Agrarian rule, coups and royal authoritarianism swing Bulgaria between dictatorship and democracy between the wars.  c) The Slavs settle eastern Europe and raise their first states.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Christians, Jews and Muslims live side by side — and collide — in the medieval world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
