# Bulgaria: Christianisation under Boris I — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 10: The Christianisation of the Bulgarian state”?
**Answer:** Boris I baptises Bulgaria (864/865) and bargains between Rome and Constantinople for his church. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 8: The Christianisation of the Bulgarians and the struggle for an independent Bulgarian church” end?
**Answer:** Boris I accepts baptism and fights Rome and Constantinople for a church of Bulgaria's own. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
