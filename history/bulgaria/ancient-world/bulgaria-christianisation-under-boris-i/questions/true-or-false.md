# Bulgaria: Christianisation under Boris I — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Boris I baptises Bulgaria (864/865) and bargains between Rome and Constantinople for his church.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 8: The Christianisation of the Bulgarians and the struggle for an independent Bulgarian church” is mainly about this: Boyars and peasants, villages and fortresses — how medieval Bulgarians lived.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Boris I accepts baptism and fights Rome and Constantinople for a church of Bulgaria's own.
