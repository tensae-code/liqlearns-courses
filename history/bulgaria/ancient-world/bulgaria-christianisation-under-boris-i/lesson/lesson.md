# Bulgaria: Christianisation under Boris I

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bulgaria: Christianisation under Boris I** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 10: The Christianisation of the Bulgarian state

Boris I baptises Bulgaria (864/865) and bargains between Rome and Constantinople for his church. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 8: The Christianisation of the Bulgarians and the struggle for an independent Bulgarian church

Boris I accepts baptism and fights Rome and Constantinople for a church of Bulgaria's own. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
