# Bulgaria: Simeon the Great — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 11: The rule of Tsar Simeon the Great”?
a) Simeon makes Bulgaria an empire in arms and letters, knocking at Constantinople's gates.  b) Behind the Iron Curtain, Bulgaria is the Warsaw Pact's most faithful member.  c) Russia's war with the Ottomans ends in Bulgaria's liberation and a new state on the map.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Simeon makes Bulgaria an empire in arms and letters, knocking at Constantinople's gates.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 10: Bulgaria under Tsar Simeon the Great (893–927)”?
a) Simeon's wars and court make Bulgaria the Balkans' greatest power.  b) Secular schools multiply and a Bulgarian culture of books, art and learning is built.  c) Villages and towns change between the wars while Bulgarian culture reaches toward Europe.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Simeon's wars and court make Bulgaria the Balkans' greatest power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
