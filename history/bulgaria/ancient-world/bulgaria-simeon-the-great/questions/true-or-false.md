# Bulgaria: Simeon the Great — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Simeon makes Bulgaria an empire in arms and letters, knocking at Constantinople's gates.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 10: Bulgaria under Tsar Simeon the Great (893–927)” is mainly about this: Thracian tribes and kingdoms — Sitalk and the Odrysians — rule the Balkans before Rome.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Simeon's wars and court make Bulgaria the Balkans' greatest power.
