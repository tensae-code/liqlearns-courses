# Bulgaria: Golden Age of Culture

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bulgaria: Golden Age of Culture** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 23: The Golden Age of Bulgarian culture

Preslav and Ohrid make Bulgaria the literary heart of the Slavic world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 11: The "Golden Age" of Bulgarian culture

Pliska and Veliki Preslav flower in the Golden Age of Slavonic letters. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
