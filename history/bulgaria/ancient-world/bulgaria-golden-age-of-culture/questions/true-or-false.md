# Bulgaria: Golden Age of Culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Preslav and Ohrid make Bulgaria the literary heart of the Slavic world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 11: The "Golden Age" of Bulgarian culture” is mainly about this: Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Pliska and Veliki Preslav flower in the Golden Age of Slavonic letters.
