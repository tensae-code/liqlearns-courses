# Bulgaria: Golden Age of Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 23: The Golden Age of Bulgarian culture”?
a) Preslav and Ohrid make Bulgaria the literary heart of the Slavic world.  b) Learners practise turning evidence into a written history of their own.  c) Railways, factories and new towns change how Bulgarians work and live.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Preslav and Ohrid make Bulgaria the literary heart of the Slavic world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 11: The "Golden Age" of Bulgarian culture”?
a) Pliska and Veliki Preslav flower in the Golden Age of Slavonic letters.  b) In 1885 Bulgarians unite the two halves Berlin had split — and defend the union in war.  c) NATO (2004) and EU (2007) membership anchor Bulgaria in the Euro-Atlantic family.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Pliska and Veliki Preslav flower in the Golden Age of Slavonic letters.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
