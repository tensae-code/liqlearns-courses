# Bulgaria: Golden Age of Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 23: The Golden Age of Bulgarian culture”?
**Answer:** Preslav and Ohrid make Bulgaria the literary heart of the Slavic world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 11: The "Golden Age" of Bulgarian culture” end?
**Answer:** Pliska and Veliki Preslav flower in the Golden Age of Slavonic letters. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
