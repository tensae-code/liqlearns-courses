# Byzantine Society and Daily Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 18: Society in Byzantium and its everyday life”?
a) Constantinople's court, markets and households — daily life in the thousand-year empire.  b) Peter's long peace, then Samuil's desperate wars end with Basil II and the fall of the First Empire (1018).  c) Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Constantinople's court, markets and households — daily life in the thousand-year empire.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Constantinople's court, markets and households — daily life in the thousand-year empire.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
