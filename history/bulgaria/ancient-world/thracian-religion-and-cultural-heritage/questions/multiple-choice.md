Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 3: Religious beliefs and cultural heritage of the ancient Thracians”?
a) Orpheus, rock sanctuaries, golden treasures and royal tombs — the Thracian inheritance.  b) Simeon makes Bulgaria an empire in arms and letters, knocking at Constantinople's gates.  c) Cities revive with guilds, fairs and burghers who owe no lord.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
