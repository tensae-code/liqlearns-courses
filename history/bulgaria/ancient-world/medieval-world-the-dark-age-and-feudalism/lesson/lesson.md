## Sub lesson 1 — Review: The heritage of medieval Europe — Bulgaria and the Bulgarians

The year's story opens by recalling medieval Europe's heritage and Bulgaria's place in it, before the modern national story begins. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 1: Introduction to the European Middle Ages

The fall of Rome opens the Middle Ages — the age of feudalism Europe is about to build. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
