# The Slavs and Their First States

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Slavs and Their First States** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 8: The Slavs and the emergence of their states

The Slavs settle eastern Europe and raise their first states. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 4: The Slavs and the Great Migration of the peoples

The Slavs pour south in the Great Migration and settle the Balkans. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

## Say it to your companion
