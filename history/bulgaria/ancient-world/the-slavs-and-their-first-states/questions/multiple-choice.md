# The Slavs and Their First States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 8: The Slavs and the emergence of their states”?
a) The Slavs settle eastern Europe and raise their first states.  b) Bulgaria lives under Byzantine rule while revolts gather toward liberation.  c) Homes, food, dress and feast days in medieval Bulgaria.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Slavs settle eastern Europe and raise their first states.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 4: The Slavs and the Great Migration of the peoples”?
a) The Slavs pour south in the Great Migration and settle the Balkans.  b) The first humans reach the Balkans and master tools and fire in Europe's oldest inhabited corner.  c) Klokotnitsa (1230) makes Ivan Asen II master of Southeastern Europe.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Slavs pour south in the Great Migration and settle the Balkans.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
