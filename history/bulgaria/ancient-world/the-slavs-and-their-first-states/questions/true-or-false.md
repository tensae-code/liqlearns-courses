# The Slavs and Their First States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Slavs settle eastern Europe and raise their first states.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 4: The Slavs and the Great Migration of the peoples” is mainly about this: Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Slavs pour south in the Great Migration and settle the Balkans.
