# Bulgaria: The Second Empire — the Asenevtsi — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Asen brothers raise the Second Bulgarian Empire (1185) and Kaloyan and Ivan Asen II make it great.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 13: Peter and Asen — rebels and tsars; the resurrection of Bulgaria (1186–1196)” is mainly about this: Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Two brothers raise a tax revolt at Tarnovo and resurrect the Bulgarian state.
