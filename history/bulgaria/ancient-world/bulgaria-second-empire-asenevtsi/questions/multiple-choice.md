# Bulgaria: The Second Empire — the Asenevtsi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 28: The rule of the Asenevtsi”?
a) The Asen brothers raise the Second Bulgarian Empire (1185) and Kaloyan and Ivan Asen II make it great.  b) Thracian tribes and kingdoms — Sitalk and the Odrysians — rule the Balkans before Rome.  c) Patriarchate, exarchate and hesychasm — the church carries the nation through empire and occupation.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Asen brothers raise the Second Bulgarian Empire (1185) and Kaloyan and Ivan Asen II make it great.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: Peter and Asen — rebels and tsars; the resurrection of Bulgaria (1186–1196)”?
a) Two brothers raise a tax revolt at Tarnovo and resurrect the Bulgarian state.  b) Donations build schools across the lands — enlightenment as civic duty.  c) Stamboliyski, the 1923 and 1934 coups, and the Tsar's authoritarian turn.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Two brothers raise a tax revolt at Tarnovo and resurrect the Bulgarian state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
