# Bulgaria: The Second Empire — the Asenevtsi — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 28: The rule of the Asenevtsi”?
**Answer:** The Asen brothers raise the Second Bulgarian Empire (1185) and Kaloyan and Ivan Asen II make it great. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 13: Peter and Asen — rebels and tsars; the resurrection of Bulgaria (1186–1196)” end?
**Answer:** Two brothers raise a tax revolt at Tarnovo and resurrect the Bulgarian state. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
