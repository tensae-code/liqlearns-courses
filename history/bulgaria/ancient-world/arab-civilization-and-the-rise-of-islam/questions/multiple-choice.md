# Arab Civilization and the Rise of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The Arabs and the beginning of their civilization”?
a) The Arabs unite under Islam and build a civilization from Arabia to Spain.  b) Tarnovo falls (1393) and Vidin follows — the medieval Bulgarian state ends.  c) The Tarnovo Constitution and its builders lay the modern state's foundations.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Arabs unite under Islam and build a civilization from Arabia to Spain.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
