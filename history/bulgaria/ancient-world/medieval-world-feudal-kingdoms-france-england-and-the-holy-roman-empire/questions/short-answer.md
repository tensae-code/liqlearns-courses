# Feudal Kingdoms: France, England and the Holy Roman Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 24: The restoration of the empire in Western Europe”?
**Answer:** The Ottonians restore an empire in the west — the Holy Roman Empire is born. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 25: Centralisation of state power in France and England” end?
**Answer:** French and English kings gather power from feudal lords into centralised kingdoms. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
