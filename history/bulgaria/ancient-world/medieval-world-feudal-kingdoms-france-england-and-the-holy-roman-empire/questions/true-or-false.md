# Feudal Kingdoms: France, England and the Holy Roman Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Ottonians restore an empire in the west — the Holy Roman Empire is born.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 25: Centralisation of state power in France and England” is mainly about this: Thracian tribes and kingdoms — Sitalk and the Odrysians — rule the Balkans before Rome.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: French and English kings gather power from feudal lords into centralised kingdoms.
