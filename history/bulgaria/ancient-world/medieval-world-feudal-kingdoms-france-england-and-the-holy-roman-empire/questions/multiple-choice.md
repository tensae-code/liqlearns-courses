# Feudal Kingdoms: France, England and the Holy Roman Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 24: The restoration of the empire in Western Europe”?
a) The Ottonians restore an empire in the west — the Holy Roman Empire is born.  b) Boris I accepts baptism and fights Rome and Constantinople for a church of Bulgaria's own.  c) Church, mosques and synagogues share the Bulgarian lands under the millet order.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Ottonians restore an empire in the west — the Holy Roman Empire is born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 25: Centralisation of state power in France and England”?
a) French and English kings gather power from feudal lords into centralised kingdoms.  b) Tarnovo falls (1393) and Vidin follows — the medieval Bulgarian state ends.  c) The Tarnovo Constitution and its builders lay the modern state's foundations.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) French and English kings gather power from feudal lords into centralised kingdoms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
