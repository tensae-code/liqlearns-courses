# Bulgaria: Peter, Samuil and the Fall of the First Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Peter's long peace, then Samuil's desperate wars end with Basil II and the fall of the First Empire (1018).
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 12: The sunset of the First Bulgarian Kingdom (976–1018)” is mainly about this: Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.
