# Bulgaria: Peter, Samuil and the Fall of the First Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: Tsar Peter and his heirs; the wars of Samuil”?
a) Peter's long peace, then Samuil's desperate wars end with Basil II and the fall of the First Empire (1018).  b) Bulgarians fight the Greek Patriarchate for their own church — the first national cause won.  c) The 9 September 1944 coup and the Fatherland Front impose communist rule under Soviet shadow.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Peter's long peace, then Samuil's desperate wars end with Basil II and the fall of the First Empire (1018).** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: The sunset of the First Bulgarian Kingdom (976–1018)”?
a) Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.  b) Bulgaria joins the Central Powers to regain Macedonia and loses a second national catastrophe.  c) Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
