# Bulgaria: Peter, Samuil and the Fall of the First Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 12: Tsar Peter and his heirs; the wars of Samuil”?
**Answer:** Peter's long peace, then Samuil's desperate wars end with Basil II and the fall of the First Empire (1018). Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 12: The sunset of the First Bulgarian Kingdom (976–1018)” end?
**Answer:** Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
