# Bulgaria: Krum and Omurtag — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 7: Bulgaria in the first half of the 9th century”?
**Answer:** Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 7: Wars, laws and reforms in the first half of the 9th century” end?
**Answer:** Krum's laws and victories, Omurtag's building — pagan Bulgaria at its strongest. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
