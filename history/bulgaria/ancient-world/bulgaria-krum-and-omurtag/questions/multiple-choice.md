# Bulgaria: Krum and Omurtag — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 7: Bulgaria in the first half of the 9th century”?
a) Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.  b) Shipka and Pleven decide the war; San Stefano and Berlin draw the new Bulgaria.  c) The Fatherland Front's "people's democracy" shows its illusions as the communists take hold.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
