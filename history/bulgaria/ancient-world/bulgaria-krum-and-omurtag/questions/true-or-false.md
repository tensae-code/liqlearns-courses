# Bulgaria: Krum and Omurtag — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 7: Wars, laws and reforms in the first half of the 9th century” is mainly about this: Boris I baptises Bulgaria (864/865) and bargains between Rome and Constantinople for his church.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Krum's laws and victories, Omurtag's building — pagan Bulgaria at its strongest.
