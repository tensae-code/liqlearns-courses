# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Techniques of History: Numbers in history”?
a) Numbers, quantities and chronology are handled as historical evidence in their own right.  b) Secular schools multiply and a Bulgarian culture of books, art and learning is built.  c) Villages and towns change between the wars while Bulgarian culture reaches toward Europe.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Numbers, quantities and chronology are handled as historical evidence in their own right.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the heart of the story in “Techniques of History: Numbers in history”?
a) Numbers, quantities and chronology are handled as historical evidence in their own right.  b) The Balkan Wars — victory over the Ottomans, then catastrophe against former allies.  c) Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and found a state (681) that Byzantium must recognise.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Numbers, quantities and chronology are handled as historical evidence in their own right.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
