# Bulgaria: Interwar Society and Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lessons 27–29: Socio-economic changes between the wars; the cultural horizons of the Bulgarians”?
**Answer:** Villages and towns change between the wars while Bulgarian culture reaches toward Europe. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lessons 55–57: Socio-economic changes between the wars; the wide horizon of education; Bulgarian culture and modern Europe” end?
**Answer:** Cooperatives, schools for all, and a culture reaching into modern Europe. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
