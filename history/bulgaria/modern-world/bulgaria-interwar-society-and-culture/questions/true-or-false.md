# Bulgaria: Interwar Society and Culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Villages and towns change between the wars while Bulgarian culture reaches toward Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lessons 55–57: Socio-economic changes between the wars; the wide horizon of education; Bulgarian culture and modern Europe” is mainly about this: Thracian tribes and kingdoms — Sitalk and the Odrysians — rule the Balkans before Rome.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Cooperatives, schools for all, and a culture reaching into modern Europe.
