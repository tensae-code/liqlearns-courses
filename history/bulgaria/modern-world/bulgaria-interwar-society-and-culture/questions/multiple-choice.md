# Bulgaria: Interwar Society and Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 27–29: Socio-economic changes between the wars; the cultural horizons of the Bulgarians”?
a) Villages and towns change between the wars while Bulgarian culture reaches toward Europe.  b) Taxes, faith and village life — how Bulgarians lived inside the empire for centuries.  c) Two brothers raise a tax revolt at Tarnovo and resurrect the Bulgarian state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Villages and towns change between the wars while Bulgarian culture reaches toward Europe.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 55–57: Socio-economic changes between the wars; the wide horizon of education; Bulgarian culture and modern Europe”?
a) Cooperatives, schools for all, and a culture reaching into modern Europe.  b) The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples.  c) Damaskins, frescoes and monastery schools keep Bulgarian letters alive without a state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Cooperatives, schools for all, and a culture reaching into modern Europe.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
