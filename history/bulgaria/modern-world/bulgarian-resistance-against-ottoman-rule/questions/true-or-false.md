# Bulgarian Resistance against Ottoman Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Haiduti in the mountains and rising after rising — resistance never fully sleeps.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 22: The anti-Ottoman resistance of the Bulgarians” is mainly about this: Thracian tribes and kingdoms — Sitalk and the Odrysians — rule the Balkans before Rome.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.
