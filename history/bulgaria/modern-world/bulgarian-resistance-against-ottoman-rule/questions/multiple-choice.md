# Bulgarian Resistance against Ottoman Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 43: The resistance of the Bulgarians against Ottoman power”?
a) Haiduti in the mountains and rising after rising — resistance never fully sleeps.  b) 10 November 1989 ends the regime, and a hard transition to democracy begins.  c) Village and town life — work, food, housing and faith through the year.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Haiduti in the mountains and rising after rising — resistance never fully sleeps.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 22: The anti-Ottoman resistance of the Bulgarians”?
a) Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.  b) Peter's long peace, then Samuil's desperate wars end with Basil II and the fall of the First Empire (1018).  c) Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
