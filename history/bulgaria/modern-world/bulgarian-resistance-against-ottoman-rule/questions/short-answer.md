# Bulgarian Resistance against Ottoman Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 43: The resistance of the Bulgarians against Ottoman power”?
**Answer:** Haiduti in the mountains and rising after rising — resistance never fully sleeps. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 22: The anti-Ottoman resistance of the Bulgarians” end?
**Answer:** Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
