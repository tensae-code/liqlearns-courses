# Bulgarians in the Ottoman Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 41: The Bulgarians in the Ottoman Empire”?
**Answer:** Taxes, faith and village life — how Bulgarians lived inside the empire for centuries. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 21: The Bulgarians in the Ottoman state in the 15th–17th centuries” end?
**Answer:** Rayah status, taxes and the devshirme — Bulgarian life inside the empire's order. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
