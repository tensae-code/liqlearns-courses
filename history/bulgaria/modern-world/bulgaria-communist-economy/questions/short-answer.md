# Bulgaria: The Communist Economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 32: Economic changes in village and town”?
**Answer:** Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 61 + Historical Laboratory: Economic and social changes; the woman in socialist society” end?
**Answer:** Planned industry and the socialist woman — "tears in the eyes, stars in the hair". Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
