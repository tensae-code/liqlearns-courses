# Bulgaria: The Communist Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 32: Economic changes in village and town”?
a) Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town.  b) Guilds, markets and village communes organise life under the timar system.  c) Ferdinand's contested throne and Stambolov's regency steer through crisis.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
