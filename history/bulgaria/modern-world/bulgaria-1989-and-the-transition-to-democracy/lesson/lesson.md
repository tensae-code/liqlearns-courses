## Sub lesson 1 — Lessons 36–37: The end of the communist regime; the Bulgarian transition to democracy

10 November 1989 ends the regime, and a hard transition to democracy begins. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
