# Bulgaria: 1989 and the Transition to Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 36–37: The end of the communist regime; the Bulgarian transition to democracy”?
a) 10 November 1989 ends the regime, and a hard transition to democracy begins.  b) The 1908 declaration makes Bulgaria a fully independent kingdom.  c) The year's story opens by recalling medieval Europe's heritage and Bulgaria's place in it, before the modern national story begins.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) 10 November 1989 ends the regime, and a hard transition to democracy begins.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 64: The end of the communist regime”?
a) Zhivkov falls on 10 November 1989 and the regime ends.  b) The Tarnovo legislators write a constitution and build the institutions of a modern state.  c) Village and town life — work, food, housing and faith through the year.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Zhivkov falls on 10 November 1989 and the regime ends.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
