# Bulgaria: 1989 and the Transition to Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lessons 36–37: The end of the communist regime; the Bulgarian transition to democracy”?
**Answer:** 10 November 1989 ends the regime, and a hard transition to democracy begins. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 64: The end of the communist regime” end?
**Answer:** Zhivkov falls on 10 November 1989 and the regime ends. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
