# Bulgaria: 1989 and the Transition to Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) 10 November 1989 ends the regime, and a hard transition to democracy begins.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 64: The end of the communist regime” is mainly about this: Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Zhivkov falls on 10 November 1989 and the regime ends.
