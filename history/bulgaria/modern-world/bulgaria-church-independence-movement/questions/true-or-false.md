# Bulgaria: The Church Independence Movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bulgarians fight the Greek Patriarchate for their own church — the first national cause won.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 33: The movement for an independent Bulgarian church” is mainly about this: Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The struggle for the Exarchate (1870) wins the first national institution.
