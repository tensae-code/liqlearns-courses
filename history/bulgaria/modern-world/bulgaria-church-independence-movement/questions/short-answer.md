# Bulgaria: The Church Independence Movement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 8: The movement for an independent Bulgarian church”?
**Answer:** Bulgarians fight the Greek Patriarchate for their own church — the first national cause won. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 33: The movement for an independent Bulgarian church” end?
**Answer:** The struggle for the Exarchate (1870) wins the first national institution. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
