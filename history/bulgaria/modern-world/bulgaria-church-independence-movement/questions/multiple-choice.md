# Bulgaria: The Church Independence Movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 8: The movement for an independent Bulgarian church”?
a) Bulgarians fight the Greek Patriarchate for their own church — the first national cause won.  b) The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples.  c) Damaskins, frescoes and monastery schools keep Bulgarian letters alive without a state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Bulgarians fight the Greek Patriarchate for their own church — the first national cause won.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
