# Bulgaria: The Totalitarian Regime

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bulgaria: The Totalitarian Regime** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 31: The imposition of the totalitarian regime

Opposition is crushed, the constitution replaced, and a totalitarian regime is imposed. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 59 + Historical Laboratory: Building the totalitarian state; between propaganda and political repression

Show trials, camps and propaganda build the totalitarian state. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
