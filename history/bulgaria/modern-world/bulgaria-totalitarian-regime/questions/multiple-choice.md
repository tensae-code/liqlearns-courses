# Bulgaria: The Totalitarian Regime — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 31: The imposition of the totalitarian regime”?
a) Opposition is crushed, the constitution replaced, and a totalitarian regime is imposed.  b) Simeon's wars and court make Bulgaria the Balkans' greatest power.  c) Europe modernises while the Bulgarians enter their Revival inside a weakening empire.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Opposition is crushed, the constitution replaced, and a totalitarian regime is imposed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 59 + Historical Laboratory: Building the totalitarian state; between propaganda and political repression”?
a) Show trials, camps and propaganda build the totalitarian state.  b) Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.  c) Unification proclaimed at Plovdiv and defended against Serbia at Slivnitsa.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Show trials, camps and propaganda build the totalitarian state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
