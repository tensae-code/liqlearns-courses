# Bulgaria: The Totalitarian Regime — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Opposition is crushed, the constitution replaced, and a totalitarian regime is imposed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 59 + Historical Laboratory: Building the totalitarian state; between propaganda and political repression” is mainly about this: Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Show trials, camps and propaganda build the totalitarian state.
