## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 13: The restoration of Bulgarian statehood

After five centuries, Bulgarian statehood is restored — a principality under Ottoman suzerainty and Eastern Rumelia apart. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 39: The restoration of the Bulgarian state tradition

The state tradition is restored after 1878 — prince, assembly and a nation to rebuild. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
