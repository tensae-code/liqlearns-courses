# Bulgaria: Restoration of Statehood, 1878 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 13: The restoration of Bulgarian statehood”?
a) After five centuries, Bulgarian statehood is restored — a principality under Ottoman suzerainty and Eastern Rumelia apart.  b) Three decades of Zhivkov — stability, stagnation and total loyalty to Moscow.  c) Committees and revolutionaries — the "apostles of Bulgarian freedom" — organise the rising against Ottoman rule.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
