# Bulgaria: Restoration of Statehood, 1878 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) After five centuries, Bulgarian statehood is restored — a principality under Ottoman suzerainty and Eastern Rumelia apart.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 39: The restoration of the Bulgarian state tradition” is mainly about this: Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The state tradition is restored after 1878 — prince, assembly and a nation to rebuild.
