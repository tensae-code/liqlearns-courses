# Bulgaria: Society under Communism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lessons 34–35: Bulgarian society (1944–1989) and culture in the second half of the 20th century”?
**Answer:** Everyday life, work and culture under the regime — control, shortage and quiet adaptation. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 63: Spiritual life during the totalitarian regime” end?
**Answer:** Writers and believers navigate censorship and control of the spirit. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
