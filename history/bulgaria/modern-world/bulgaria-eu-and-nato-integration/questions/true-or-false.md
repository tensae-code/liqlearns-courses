# Bulgaria: EU and NATO Integration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) NATO (2004) and EU (2007) membership anchor Bulgaria in the Euro-Atlantic family.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lessons 67–68: Bulgaria — part of the Euro-Atlantic community; Bulgarian society and European values” is mainly about this: Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: NATO and EU accession close the transition; Bulgarian society measures itself against European values.
