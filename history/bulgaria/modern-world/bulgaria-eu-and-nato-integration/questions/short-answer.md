# Bulgaria: EU and NATO Integration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lessons 38–39: Bulgaria — part of the Euro-Atlantic family; the Bulgarian citizen in the modern world”?
**Answer:** NATO (2004) and EU (2007) membership anchor Bulgaria in the Euro-Atlantic family. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lessons 67–68: Bulgaria — part of the Euro-Atlantic community; Bulgarian society and European values” end?
**Answer:** NATO and EU accession close the transition; Bulgarian society measures itself against European values. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
