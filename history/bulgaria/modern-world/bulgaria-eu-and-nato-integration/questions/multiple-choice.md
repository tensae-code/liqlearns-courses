Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 38–39: Bulgaria — part of the Euro-Atlantic family; the Bulgarian citizen in the modern world”?
a) NATO (2004) and EU (2007) membership anchor Bulgaria in the Euro-Atlantic family.  b) Round tables, first free elections and the 1991 Constitution — democracy's foundation and pillar.  c) In 1885 Bulgarians unite the two halves Berlin had split — and defend the union in war.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
