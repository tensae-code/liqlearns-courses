# Bulgaria in the Second World War and the Saved Jews — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 26 + Historical Laboratory: The Kingdom of Bulgaria in the Second World War; The Saved”?
**Answer:** Bulgaria joins the Axis yet its Jews are saved from Hitler's grasp — the laboratory lesson tells how. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 54 + Historical Laboratory: The Kingdom of Bulgaria in the years of the Second World War; the fate of the Bulgarian Jews” end?
**Answer:** Alliance with Germany, regained Southern Dobruja — and the rescue that saved Bulgaria's Jews. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
