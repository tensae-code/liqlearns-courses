# Bulgaria in the Second World War and the Saved Jews — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bulgaria joins the Axis yet its Jews are saved from Hitler's grasp — the laboratory lesson tells how.
**Answer: True**
