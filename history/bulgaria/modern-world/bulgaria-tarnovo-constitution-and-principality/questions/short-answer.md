# Bulgaria: The Tarnovo Constitution and the Principality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 14 + Historical Laboratory: Building the Principality of Bulgaria”?
**Answer:** The Tarnovo legislators write a constitution and build the institutions of a modern state. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 40 + Historical Laboratory: Building the Principality of Bulgaria — the builders of modern Bulgaria” end?
**Answer:** The Tarnovo Constitution and its builders lay the modern state's foundations. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
