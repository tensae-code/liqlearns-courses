# Bulgaria: Revival School and Culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Secular schools multiply and a Bulgarian culture of books, art and learning is built.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 32: Education — the spiritual space of the Bulgarians” is mainly about this: Towns, families, dress and daily life change as a new Bulgarian society takes shape.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Donations build schools across the lands — enlightenment as civic duty.
