# Bulgaria: Revival School and Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lessons 6–7: The new Bulgarian school and Revival culture”?
**Answer:** Secular schools multiply and a Bulgarian culture of books, art and learning is built. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 32: Education — the spiritual space of the Bulgarians” end?
**Answer:** Donations build schools across the lands — enlightenment as civic duty. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
