# Bulgaria: Revival School and Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 6–7: The new Bulgarian school and Revival culture”?
a) Secular schools multiply and a Bulgarian culture of books, art and learning is built.  b) Orpheus, rock sanctuaries, golden treasures and royal tombs — the Thracian inheritance.  c) Fields, crafts and trade sustain the Second Empire's towns and villages.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Secular schools multiply and a Bulgarian culture of books, art and learning is built.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 32: Education — the spiritual space of the Bulgarians”?
a) Donations build schools across the lands — enlightenment as civic duty.  b) The Fourth Crusade's Latin Empire meets its match in Bulgaria's tsar.  c) The struggle for the Exarchate (1870) wins the first national institution.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Donations build schools across the lands — enlightenment as civic duty.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
