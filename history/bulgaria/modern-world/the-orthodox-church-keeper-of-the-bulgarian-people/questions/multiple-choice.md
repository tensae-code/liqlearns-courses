# The Orthodox Church: Keeper of the Bulgarian People — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 42: The Orthodox Church — unifier of the Bulgarian people”?
a) Monasteries and priests keep language and identity alive when the state is gone.  b) Bulgaria joins the Axis yet its Jews are saved from Hitler's grasp — the laboratory lesson tells how.  c) Icons, emperors and new kingdoms reshape both halves of the Christian world.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Monasteries and priests keep language and identity alive when the state is gone.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 25: The Orthodox Church and the other religious communities”?
a) Church, mosques and synagogues share the Bulgarian lands under the millet order.  b) The Arabs unite under Islam and build a civilization from Arabia to Spain.  c) The Ottonians restore an empire in the west — the Holy Roman Empire is born.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Church, mosques and synagogues share the Bulgarian lands under the millet order.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
