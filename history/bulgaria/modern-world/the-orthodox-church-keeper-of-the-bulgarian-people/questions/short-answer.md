# The Orthodox Church: Keeper of the Bulgarian People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 42: The Orthodox Church — unifier of the Bulgarian people”?
**Answer:** Monasteries and priests keep language and identity alive when the state is gone. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 25: The Orthodox Church and the other religious communities” end?
**Answer:** Church, mosques and synagogues share the Bulgarian lands under the millet order. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
