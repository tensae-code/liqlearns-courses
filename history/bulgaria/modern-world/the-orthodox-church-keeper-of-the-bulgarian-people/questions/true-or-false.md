# The Orthodox Church: Keeper of the Bulgarian People — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Monasteries and priests keep language and identity alive when the state is gone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 25: The Orthodox Church and the other religious communities” is mainly about this: Two neighbouring societies — imperial and Bulgarian — in the empire's last centuries.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Church, mosques and synagogues share the Bulgarian lands under the millet order.
