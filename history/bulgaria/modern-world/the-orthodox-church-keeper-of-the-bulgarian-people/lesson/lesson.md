# The Orthodox Church: Keeper of the Bulgarian People

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Orthodox Church: Keeper of the Bulgarian People** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 42: The Orthodox Church — unifier of the Bulgarian people

Monasteries and priests keep language and identity alive when the state is gone. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 25: The Orthodox Church and the other religious communities

Church, mosques and synagogues share the Bulgarian lands under the millet order. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
