# Bulgaria: Liberation, 1878 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Russia's war with the Ottomans ends in Bulgaria's liberation and a new state on the map.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 37: The Russo-Turkish War (1877–1878) and the Liberation of Bulgaria” is mainly about this: Boyars and peasants, villages and fortresses — how medieval Bulgarians lived.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Shipka and Pleven decide the war; San Stefano and Berlin draw the new Bulgaria.
