# Bulgaria: Liberation, 1878 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 12: The Russo-Turkish War (1877–1878) and the Liberation of Bulgaria”?
a) Russia's war with the Ottomans ends in Bulgaria's liberation and a new state on the map.  b) Paisii's history (1762) tells Bulgarians to know their own past and be proud.  c) Bulgaria's Great War ends in the 1918 crash — Salonica front, collapse and Neuilly.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Russia's war with the Ottomans ends in Bulgaria's liberation and a new state on the map.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 37: The Russo-Turkish War (1877–1878) and the Liberation of Bulgaria”?
a) Shipka and Pleven decide the war; San Stefano and Berlin draw the new Bulgaria.  b) Macedonia's Bulgarians organise schools, churches and finally arms.  c) Privatisation, crisis and reform — the painful road to a market economy.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Shipka and Pleven decide the war; San Stefano and Berlin draw the new Bulgaria.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
