# Bulgaria: Bulgarians outside the Free State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bulgarians left in Macedonia and Thrace under Ottoman rule keep the national cause alive.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 45: The Bulgarians outside the borders of the free state” is mainly about this: The 1908 declaration makes Bulgaria a fully independent kingdom.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Macedonia's Bulgarians organise schools, churches and finally arms.
