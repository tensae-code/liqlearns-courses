# Bulgaria: Bulgarians outside the Free State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 21: The Bulgarians outside the borders of the free state”?
a) Bulgarians left in Macedonia and Thrace under Ottoman rule keep the national cause alive.  b) The April Uprising rises and is drowned in blood — and Europe finally hears of Bulgaria.  c) Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Bulgarians left in Macedonia and Thrace under Ottoman rule keep the national cause alive.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 45: The Bulgarians outside the borders of the free state”?
a) Macedonia's Bulgarians organise schools, churches and finally arms.  b) Bulgaria joins the Axis yet its Jews are saved from Hitler's grasp — the laboratory lesson tells how.  c) Icons, emperors and new kingdoms reshape both halves of the Christian world.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Macedonia's Bulgarians organise schools, churches and finally arms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
