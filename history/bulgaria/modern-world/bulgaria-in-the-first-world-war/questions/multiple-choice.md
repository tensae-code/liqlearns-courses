# Bulgaria in the First World War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 23: Bulgaria in the Great War (1915–1919)”?
a) Bulgaria joins the Central Powers to regain Macedonia and loses a second national catastrophe.  b) Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.  c) Crusading becomes a way of life for two centuries of knights, pilgrims and merchants.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Bulgaria joins the Central Powers to regain Macedonia and loses a second national catastrophe.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 50: The Crash (1915–1918)”?
a) Bulgaria's Great War ends in the 1918 crash — Salonica front, collapse and Neuilly.  b) Two brothers invent an alphabet for the Slavs; their disciples carry it to Bulgaria.  c) Haiduti in the mountains and rising after rising — resistance never fully sleeps.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Bulgaria's Great War ends in the 1918 crash — Salonica front, collapse and Neuilly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
