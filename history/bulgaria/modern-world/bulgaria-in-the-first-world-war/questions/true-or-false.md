# Bulgaria in the First World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bulgaria joins the Central Powers to regain Macedonia and loses a second national catastrophe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 50: The Crash (1915–1918)” is mainly about this: Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Bulgaria's Great War ends in the 1918 crash — Salonica front, collapse and Neuilly.
