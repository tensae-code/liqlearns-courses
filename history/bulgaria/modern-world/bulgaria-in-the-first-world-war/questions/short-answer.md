# Bulgaria in the First World War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 23: Bulgaria in the Great War (1915–1919)”?
**Answer:** Bulgaria joins the Central Powers to regain Macedonia and loses a second national catastrophe. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 50: The Crash (1915–1918)” end?
**Answer:** Bulgaria's Great War ends in the 1918 crash — Salonica front, collapse and Neuilly. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
