# Bulgaria: Independence, 1908 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 18: Strength makes independence”?
**Answer:** In 1908 Bulgaria declares full independence and Ferdinand takes the title of Tsar. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 48: Strength makes independence” end?
**Answer:** The 1908 declaration makes Bulgaria a fully independent kingdom. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
