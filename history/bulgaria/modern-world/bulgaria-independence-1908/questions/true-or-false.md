# Bulgaria: Independence, 1908 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In 1908 Bulgaria declares full independence and Ferdinand takes the title of Tsar.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 48: Strength makes independence” is mainly about this: Towns, families, dress and daily life change as a new Bulgarian society takes shape.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The 1908 declaration makes Bulgaria a fully independent kingdom.
