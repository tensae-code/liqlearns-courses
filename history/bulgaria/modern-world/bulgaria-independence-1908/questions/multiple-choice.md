# Bulgaria: Independence, 1908 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 18: Strength makes independence”?
a) In 1908 Bulgaria declares full independence and Ferdinand takes the title of Tsar.  b) Villages and towns change between the wars while Bulgarian culture reaches toward Europe.  c) Boris I baptises Bulgaria (864/865) and bargains between Rome and Constantinople for his church.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In 1908 Bulgaria declares full independence and Ferdinand takes the title of Tsar.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 48: Strength makes independence”?
a) The 1908 declaration makes Bulgaria a fully independent kingdom.  b) Asparuh's Bulgars cross the Danube and found a state (681) that Byzantium must recognise.  c) French and English kings gather power from feudal lords into centralised kingdoms.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The 1908 declaration makes Bulgaria a fully independent kingdom.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
