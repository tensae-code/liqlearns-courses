## Sub lesson 1 — Lessons 9–10 + Historical Laboratory: The organised liberation movement and the Bulgarian liberation revolution (1869–1875)

Committees and revolutionaries — the "apostles of Bulgarian freedom" — organise the rising against Ottoman rule. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lessons 34–35: The paths of the Bulgarian revolution; the Bulgarian liberation revolution (1869–1875)

Rakovski, Levski and Botev — ideas and political projects for liberation. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
