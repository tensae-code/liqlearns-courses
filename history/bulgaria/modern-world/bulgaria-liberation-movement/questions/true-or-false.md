# Bulgaria: The Liberation Movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Committees and revolutionaries — the "apostles of Bulgarian freedom" — organise the rising against Ottoman rule.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lessons 34–35: The paths of the Bulgarian revolution; the Bulgarian liberation revolution (1869–1875)” is mainly about this: Collectivisation takes the land and forced industrialisation remakes village and town.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Rakovski, Levski and Botev — ideas and political projects for liberation.
