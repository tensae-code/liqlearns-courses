# Bulgaria: The Revival Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 2: The Bulgarian economy in the 18th–19th centuries”?
a) Crafts, trade and a rising Bulgarian economy grow inside the Ottoman world.  b) Two brothers invent an alphabet for the Slavs; their disciples carry it to Bulgaria.  c) Haiduti in the mountains and rising after rising — resistance never fully sleeps.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Crafts, trade and a rising Bulgarian economy grow inside the Ottoman world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 28: The Bulgarian economy in the 18th–19th centuries”?
a) Revival Plovdiv — "the heart of Rumelia" — trades its way into the modern world.  b) The Ottomans cross into Europe and conquer the Balkans state by state.  c) Pliska and Veliki Preslav flower in the Golden Age of Slavonic letters.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Revival Plovdiv — "the heart of Rumelia" — trades its way into the modern world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
