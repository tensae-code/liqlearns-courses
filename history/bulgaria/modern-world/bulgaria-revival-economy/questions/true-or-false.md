# Bulgaria: The Revival Economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Crafts, trade and a rising Bulgarian economy grow inside the Ottoman world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 28: The Bulgarian economy in the 18th–19th centuries” is mainly about this: Koprivshtitsa rises; Batak's massacre turns Europe's conscience.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Revival Plovdiv — "the heart of Rumelia" — trades its way into the modern world.
