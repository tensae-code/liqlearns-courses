## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 2: The Bulgarian economy in the 18th–19th centuries

Crafts, trade and a rising Bulgarian economy grow inside the Ottoman world. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 28: The Bulgarian economy in the 18th–19th centuries

Revival Plovdiv — "the heart of Rumelia" — trades its way into the modern world. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
