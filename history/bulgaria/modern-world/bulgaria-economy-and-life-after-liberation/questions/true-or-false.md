# Bulgaria: Economy and Life after Liberation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Railways, factories and new towns change how Bulgarians work and live.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lessons 46–47: The Bulgarian economy at the end of the 19th and beginning of the 20th century; the changing Bulgarian society” is mainly about this: Behind the Iron Curtain, Bulgaria is the Warsaw Pact's most faithful member.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Industry, banks and a changing society carry Bulgaria into the new century.
