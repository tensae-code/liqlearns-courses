# Bulgaria: Economy and Life after Liberation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lessons 19–20: The Bulgarian economy after the Liberation and the changing world of the Bulgarians”?
**Answer:** Railways, factories and new towns change how Bulgarians work and live. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lessons 46–47: The Bulgarian economy at the end of the 19th and beginning of the 20th century; the changing Bulgarian society” end?
**Answer:** Industry, banks and a changing society carry Bulgaria into the new century. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
