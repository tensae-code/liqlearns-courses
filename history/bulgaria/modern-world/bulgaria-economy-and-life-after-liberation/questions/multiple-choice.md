# Bulgaria: Economy and Life after Liberation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 19–20: The Bulgarian economy after the Liberation and the changing world of the Bulgarians”?
a) Railways, factories and new towns change how Bulgarians work and live.  b) In 1908 Bulgaria declares full independence and Ferdinand takes the title of Tsar.  c) The eastern empire survives a thousand years where the west fell.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Railways, factories and new towns change how Bulgarians work and live.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lessons 46–47: The Bulgarian economy at the end of the 19th and beginning of the 20th century; the changing Bulgarian society”?
a) Industry, banks and a changing society carry Bulgaria into the new century.  b) 10 November 1989 ends the regime, and a hard transition to democracy begins.  c) Village and town life — work, food, housing and faith through the year.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Industry, banks and a changing society carry Bulgaria into the new century.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
