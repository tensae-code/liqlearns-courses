# Bulgaria: Unification, 1885

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bulgaria: Unification, 1885** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 15: The Unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia

In 1885 Bulgarians unite the two halves Berlin had split — and defend the union in war. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 42: The Unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia and its defence (1885–1886)

Unification proclaimed at Plovdiv and defended against Serbia at Slivnitsa. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
