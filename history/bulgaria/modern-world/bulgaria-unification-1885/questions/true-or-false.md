# Bulgaria: Unification, 1885 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In 1885 Bulgarians unite the two halves Berlin had split — and defend the union in war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 42: The Unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia and its defence (1885–1886)” is mainly about this: Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Unification proclaimed at Plovdiv and defended against Serbia at Slivnitsa.
