# Bulgaria: Unification, 1885 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 15: The Unification of the Principality of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia”?
a) In 1885 Bulgarians unite the two halves Berlin had split — and defend the union in war.  b) Cooperatives, schools for all, and a culture reaching into modern Europe.  c) A monk's handwritten history and a bishop's books wake the Bulgarians to who they are.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In 1885 Bulgarians unite the two halves Berlin had split — and defend the union in war.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
