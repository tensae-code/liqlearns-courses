# Bulgaria: The April Uprising, 1876 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 11: The April Uprising of 1876”?
**Answer:** The April Uprising rises and is drowned in blood — and Europe finally hears of Bulgaria. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 36: The April Uprising of 1876” end?
**Answer:** Koprivshtitsa rises; Batak's massacre turns Europe's conscience. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
