# Bulgaria: The April Uprising, 1876 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 11: The April Uprising of 1876”?
a) The April Uprising rises and is drowned in blood — and Europe finally hears of Bulgaria.  b) Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.  c) Unification proclaimed at Plovdiv and defended against Serbia at Slivnitsa.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The April Uprising rises and is drowned in blood — and Europe finally hears of Bulgaria.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Koprivshtitsa rises; Batak's massacre turns Europe's conscience.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
