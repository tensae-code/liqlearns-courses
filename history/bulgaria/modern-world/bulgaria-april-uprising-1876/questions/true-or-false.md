# Bulgaria: The April Uprising, 1876 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The April Uprising rises and is drowned in blood — and Europe finally hears of Bulgaria.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 36: The April Uprising of 1876” is mainly about this: Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Koprivshtitsa rises; Batak's massacre turns Europe's conscience.
