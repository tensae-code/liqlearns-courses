# Bulgaria: The Balkan Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Balkan Wars — victory over the Ottomans, then catastrophe against former allies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 49: Triumph and pogrom — the Balkan Wars” is mainly about this: Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Triumph over the Ottomans, then the 1913 pogrom between allies.
