# Bulgaria: The Balkan Wars

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bulgaria: The Balkan Wars** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 22: The fire of the Balkans

The Balkan Wars — victory over the Ottomans, then catastrophe against former allies. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 49: Triumph and pogrom — the Balkan Wars

Triumph over the Ottomans, then the 1913 pogrom between allies. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
