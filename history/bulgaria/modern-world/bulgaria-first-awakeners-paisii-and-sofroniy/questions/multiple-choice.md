# Bulgaria: The First Awakeners — Paisii and Sofroniy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 5: The first awakeners — Paisii Hilendarski and Sofroniy Vrachanski”?
a) A monk's handwritten history and a bishop's books wake the Bulgarians to who they are.  b) Sultan, timars and millets — how the Ottoman state organised the lands it ruled.  c) Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A monk's handwritten history and a bishop's books wake the Bulgarians to who they are.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
