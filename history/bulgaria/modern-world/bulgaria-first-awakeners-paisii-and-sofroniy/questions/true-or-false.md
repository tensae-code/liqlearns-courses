# Bulgaria: The First Awakeners — Paisii and Sofroniy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A monk's handwritten history and a bishop's books wake the Bulgarians to who they are.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 31: The birth of national self-awareness — Paisii Hilendarski and Sofroniy Vrachanski” is mainly about this: Behind the Iron Curtain, Bulgaria is the Warsaw Pact's most faithful member.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Paisii's history (1762) tells Bulgarians to know their own past and be proud.
