## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 5: The first awakeners — Paisii Hilendarski and Sofroniy Vrachanski

A monk's handwritten history and a bishop's books wake the Bulgarians to who they are. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 31: The birth of national self-awareness — Paisii Hilendarski and Sofroniy Vrachanski
