# Bulgaria: The Communist Takeover, 1944 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The 9 September 1944 coup and the Fatherland Front impose communist rule under Soviet shadow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 58: The illusions of "people's democracy"” is mainly about this: Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Fatherland Front's "people's democracy" shows its illusions as the communists take hold.
