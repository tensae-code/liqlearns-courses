# Bulgaria: Cold War Foreign Policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bulgaria sits as the Soviet Union's most loyal satellite through the Cold War.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 62: Bulgaria behind the "Iron Curtain"” is mainly about this: Round tables, first free elections and the 1991 Constitution — democracy's foundation and pillar.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Behind the Iron Curtain, Bulgaria is the Warsaw Pact's most faithful member.
