# Bulgaria: Cold War Foreign Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 33: Bulgaria's foreign policy”?
a) Bulgaria sits as the Soviet Union's most loyal satellite through the Cold War.  b) Donations build schools across the lands — enlightenment as civic duty.  c) Stamboliyski, the 1923 and 1934 coups, and the Tsar's authoritarian turn.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Bulgaria sits as the Soviet Union's most loyal satellite through the Cold War.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 62: Bulgaria behind the "Iron Curtain"”?
a) Behind the Iron Curtain, Bulgaria is the Warsaw Pact's most faithful member.  b) Industry, banks and a changing society carry Bulgaria into the new century.  c) NATO and EU accession close the transition; Bulgarian society measures itself against European values.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Behind the Iron Curtain, Bulgaria is the Warsaw Pact's most faithful member.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
