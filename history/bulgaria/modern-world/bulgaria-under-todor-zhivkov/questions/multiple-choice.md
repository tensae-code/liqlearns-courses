Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 60: Bulgaria under the rule of Todor Zhivkov”?
a) Three decades of Zhivkov — stability, stagnation and total loyalty to Moscow.  b) Tatar raids, peasant tsars and shrinking borders — the empire lives on its past.  c) Koprivshtitsa rises; Batak's massacre turns Europe's conscience.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Three decades of Zhivkov — stability, stagnation and total loyalty to Moscow.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
