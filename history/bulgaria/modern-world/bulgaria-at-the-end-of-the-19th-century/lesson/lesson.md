# Bulgaria at the End of the 19th Century

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bulgaria at the End of the 19th Century** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 17: Bulgaria at the end of the 19th century — Konstantin Stoilov

Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 44: Bulgaria at the end of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th century

A generation after liberation, Bulgaria stands as a modernising Balkan kingdom. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
