# Bulgaria at the End of the 19th Century — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 44: Bulgaria at the end of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th century” is mainly about this: Koprivshtitsa rises; Batak's massacre turns Europe's conscience.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A generation after liberation, Bulgaria stands as a modernising Balkan kingdom.
