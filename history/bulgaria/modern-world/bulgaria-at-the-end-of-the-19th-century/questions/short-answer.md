# Bulgaria at the End of the 19th Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 17: Bulgaria at the end of the 19th century — Konstantin Stoilov”?
**Answer:** Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 44: Bulgaria at the end of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th century” end?
**Answer:** A generation after liberation, Bulgaria stands as a modernising Balkan kingdom. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
