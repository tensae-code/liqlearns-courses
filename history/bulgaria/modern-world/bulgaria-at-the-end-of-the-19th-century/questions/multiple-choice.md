# Bulgaria at the End of the 19th Century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 17: Bulgaria at the end of the 19th century — Konstantin Stoilov”?
a) Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov.  b) Europe enters the New Time while the Bulgarians live under Ottoman rule — the starting point of the Revival.  c) The Balkan Wars — victory over the Ottomans, then catastrophe against former allies.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Parties, courts and a maturing parliamentary life close the century under Stoilov.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 44: Bulgaria at the end of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th century”?
a) A generation after liberation, Bulgaria stands as a modernising Balkan kingdom.  b) New peoples pour into the Roman world and redraw the map of Europe.  c) The church carries learning and art through the medieval centuries.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) A generation after liberation, Bulgaria stands as a modernising Balkan kingdom.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
