# Bulgaria: The Stambolov Era — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 16: Crisis and modernisation — Stefan Stambolov”?
a) Stambolov's strong hand drags Bulgaria into modernisation through crisis and conflict with Russia.  b) Zhivkov falls on 10 November 1989 and the regime ends.  c) The Tarnovo legislators write a constitution and build the institutions of a modern state.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Stambolov's strong hand drags Bulgaria into modernisation through crisis and conflict with Russia.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 43: The Principality of Bulgaria at a crossroads (1886–1894)”?
a) Ferdinand's contested throne and Stambolov's regency steer through crisis.  b) Committees and revolutionaries — the "apostles of Bulgarian freedom" — organise the rising against Ottoman rule.  c) Opposition is crushed, the constitution replaced, and a totalitarian regime is imposed.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Ferdinand's contested throne and Stambolov's regency steer through crisis.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
