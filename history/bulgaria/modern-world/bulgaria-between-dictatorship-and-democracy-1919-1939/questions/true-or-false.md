# Bulgaria between Dictatorship and Democracy, 1919–1939 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Agrarian rule, coups and royal authoritarianism swing Bulgaria between dictatorship and democracy between the wars.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lessons 51–52: Between dictatorship and democracy (1919–1931); from constitutional to authoritarian regime (1931–1939)” is mainly about this: Krum crushes an emperor at Pliska and Omurtag rebuilds — Bulgaria grows into a Balkan power.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Stamboliyski, the 1923 and 1934 coups, and the Tsar's authoritarian turn.
