# Bulgaria between Dictatorship and Democracy, 1919–1939

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Bulgaria between Dictatorship and Democracy, 1919–1939** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.


## Sub lesson 1 — Lessons 24–25: Between dictatorship and democracy (1919–1939); Bulgaria in the uneasy European peace

Agrarian rule, coups and royal authoritarianism swing Bulgaria between dictatorship and democracy between the wars. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lessons 51–52: Between dictatorship and democracy (1919–1931); from constitutional to authoritarian regime (1931–1939)

Stamboliyski, the 1923 and 1934 coups, and the Tsar's authoritarian turn. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
