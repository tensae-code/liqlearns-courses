# Bulgaria under Ottoman Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Lesson 1: The New Time, the Ottoman Empire and the Bulgarians”?
**Answer:** Europe enters the New Time while the Bulgarians live under Ottoman rule — the starting point of the Revival. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Lesson 27: The New Time, the Ottoman Empire and the Bulgarians” end?
**Answer:** Europe modernises while the Bulgarians enter their Revival inside a weakening empire. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
