# Bulgaria under Ottoman Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Lesson 1: The New Time, the Ottoman Empire and the Bulgarians”?
a) Europe enters the New Time while the Bulgarians live under Ottoman rule — the starting point of the Revival.  b) The church organises medieval life — parishes, monasteries, and the split of East and West.  c) Gothic cathedrals rise and universities gather scholars — culture flowers across Europe.  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
