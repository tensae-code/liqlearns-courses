# Bulgaria under Ottoman Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Europe enters the New Time while the Bulgarians live under Ottoman rule — the starting point of the Revival.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Lesson 27: The New Time, the Ottoman Empire and the Bulgarians” is mainly about this: Early risings at Tarnovo and Chiprovtsi answer Ottoman rule with arms.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Europe modernises while the Bulgarians enter their Revival inside a weakening empire.
