# Hands-on — The Vikings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a longship: a long narrow boat with a curled prow. Write under it two lists — what such a ship could carry to a raid, and what it could carry to a new home. The Vikings did both: keep both lists in mind.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Vikings are a whole world in themselves — their land, their raids, their voyages, their everyday life and their legacy reach from Scandinavia to America. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
