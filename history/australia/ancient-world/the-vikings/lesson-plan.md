# Lesson plan — The Vikings

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Vikings
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Vikings are a whole world in themselves — their land, their raids, their voyages, their everyday life and their legacy reach from Scandinavia to America.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- **The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism** (`ancient-world/medieval-world-the-dark-age-and-feudalism/`) is recommended first — it stands just before this lesson on the path (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Vikings are a whole world in themselves — their land, their raids, their voyages, their everyday life and their legacy reach from Scandinavia to America.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any seafaring people you know of who both raided and settled — the Vikings' double story is a sailor's story everywhere.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Vikings were only raiders → the book studies them whole: land, raids, voyages, everyday life and legacy — farmers and settlers as much as warriors.
- Go back to the one thing: The Vikings are a whole world in themselves — their land, their raids, their voyages, their everyday life and their legacy reach from Scandinavia to America. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
