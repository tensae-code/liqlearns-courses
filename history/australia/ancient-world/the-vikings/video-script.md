# Video script — The Vikings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Vikings are a whole world in themselves — their land, their raids, their voyages, their everyday life and their legacy reach from Scandinavia to America.

Out of Scandinavia's fjords and farms came the Vikings, and the book studies them as a whole depth study, not a cartoon. Their land first: poor soil and long winters pushing a seafaring people outward. Their raids: the sudden longship attacks that terrified Europe's coasts and rivers. Their voyages: further than the raids — to Iceland, Greenland, and the edge of America itself. And beneath the fame, their everyday life — farms, families, law assemblies, gods and crafts — and their legacy: the towns they founded, the lands they settled, the words and genes they left across the north. Raider and farmer in one people: the whole study is needed to hold both truths.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
