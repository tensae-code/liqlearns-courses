# The Vikings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Vikings.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Vikings are a whole world in themselves — their land, their raids, their voyages, their everyday life and their legacy reach from Scandinavia to America. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
