# The Vikings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Vikings are studied as a whole depth study — their land, raids, voyages, everyday life and legacy (contents, printed p. 214).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Vikings are a whole world in themselves — their land, their raids, their voyages, their everyday life and their legacy reach from Scandinavia to America.
