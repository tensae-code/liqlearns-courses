# Lesson plan — Japan Under the Shoguns

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Japan Under the Shoguns
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Japan passes from early medieval times to the Tokugawa shogunate — Shinto and Buddhism side by side, castles and forests, isolation under threat, and then modernisation: a country that chose its own road into the modern world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Japan passes from early medieval times to the Tokugawa shogunate — Shinto and Buddhism side by side, castles and forests, isolation under threat, and then modernisation: a country that chose its own road into the modern world.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any rule in your school that keeps outsiders out to keep things safe — the shoguns ran that rule for a whole country.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
