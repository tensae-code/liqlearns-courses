# Japan Under the Shoguns — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From early medieval Japan to the Tokugawa shogunate — Shinto and Buddhism, castles and forests, isolation under threat, then modernisation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Japan passes from early medieval times to the Tokugawa shogunate — Shinto and Buddhism side by side, castles and forests, isolation under threat, and then modernisation: a country that chose its own road into the modern world.
