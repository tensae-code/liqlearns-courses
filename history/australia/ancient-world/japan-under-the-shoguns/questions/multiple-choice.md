# Japan Under the Shoguns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Japan under the Shoguns” is right?
a) From early medieval Japan to the Tokugawa shogunate — Shinto and Buddhism, castles and forests, isolation under threat, then modernisation.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Japan passes from early medieval times to the Tokugawa shogunate — Shinto and Buddhism side by side, castles and forests, isolation under threat, and then modernisation: a country that chose its own road into the modern world.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
