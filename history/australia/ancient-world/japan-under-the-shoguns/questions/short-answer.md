# Japan Under the Shoguns — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Japan under the Shoguns.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because japan passes from early medieval times to the Tokugawa shogunate — Shinto and Buddhism side by side, castles and forests, isolation under threat, and then modernisation: a country that chose its own road into the modern world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
