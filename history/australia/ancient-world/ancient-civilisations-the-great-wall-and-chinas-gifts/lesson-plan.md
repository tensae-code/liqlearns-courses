# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On China's frontiers, contact, trade and warfare run side by side — and early China's legacy, its gifts to the world, outlasts every wall built to guard it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: On China's frontiers, contact, trade and warfare run side by side — and early China's legacy, its gifts to the world, outlasts every wall built to guard it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any border town you know — walls and gates, soldiers and markets, all in one place, as on China's frontier.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
