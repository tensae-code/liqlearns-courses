# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Early China's legacy.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because on China's frontiers, contact, trade and warfare run side by side — and early China's legacy, its gifts to the world, outlasts every wall built to guard it. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
