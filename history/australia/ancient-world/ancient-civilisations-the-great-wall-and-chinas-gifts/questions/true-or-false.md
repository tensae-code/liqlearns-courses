# Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Contact, trade and warfare on China's frontiers, and the legacy early China leaves.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — On China's frontiers, contact, trade and warfare run side by side — and early China's legacy, its gifts to the world, outlasts every wall built to guard it.
