# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: The Great Wall and China's Gifts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a wall across the top of a page and a road through a gate in it. Write one thing the wall keeps out and two things that travel the road anyway. That is China's frontier in one drawing.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On China's frontiers, contact, trade and warfare run side by side — and early China's legacy, its gifts to the world, outlasts every wall built to guard it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
