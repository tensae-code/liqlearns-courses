# China's Dynastic Cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Middle Kingdom under the Dragon Throne — traditional society, culture and beliefs — meets contact, conflict and change under the Empress Dowager Cixi, and enters the modern world as the dynasty falls.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Under the Dragon Throne, China's Middle Kingdom turns on its dynastic cycle — traditional society, culture and beliefs holding for millennia — until contact, conflict and change under the Empress Dowager Cixi bring the dynasty down into the modern world.
