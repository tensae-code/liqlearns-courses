# China's Dynastic Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** China and the world.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because under the Dragon Throne, China's Middle Kingdom turns on its dynastic cycle — traditional society, culture and beliefs holding for millennia — until contact, conflict and change under the Empress Dowager Cixi bring the dynasty down into the modern world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
