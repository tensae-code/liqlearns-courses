# Video script — China's Dynastic Cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Under the Dragon Throne, China's Middle Kingdom turns on its dynastic cycle — traditional society, culture and beliefs holding for millennia — until contact, conflict and change under the Empress Dowager Cixi bring the dynasty down into the modern world.

China enters the Stage 5 book as the Middle Kingdom: an empire that saw itself as the centre of the world, its traditional society, culture and beliefs ordered under the Dragon Throne and renewed, dynasty after dynasty, on the great cycle of rise, decay and replacement. The study follows the cycle's last turn. Under the Empress Dowager Cixi, the old order meets what it cannot absorb: contact with industrial empires, conflict it keeps losing, and change pressing from inside and out at once. The dynasty falls, and China is hurled into the modern world — the wheel that turned for two thousand years breaking, in one lifetime, against the age this course is about.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
