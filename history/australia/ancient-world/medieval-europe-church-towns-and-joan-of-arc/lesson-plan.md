## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Medieval Europe is built by the Catholic Church's cathedrals, shaken by Islam's meeting with the West, and changed by growing towns and commerce — and its research project writes a letter from Joan of Arc.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a great church or mosque in your own town, still standing at its centre — the medieval Church's building habit, alive today.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
