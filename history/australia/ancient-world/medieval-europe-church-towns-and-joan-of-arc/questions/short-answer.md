# Medieval Europe: Church, Towns and Joan of Arc — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Medieval Europe — Church, towns and significant individuals.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because medieval Europe is built by the Catholic Church's cathedrals, shaken by Islam's meeting with the West, and changed by growing towns and commerce — and its research project writes a letter from Joan of Arc. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
