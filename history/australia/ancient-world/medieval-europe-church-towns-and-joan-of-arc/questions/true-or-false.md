# Medieval Europe: Church, Towns and Joan of Arc — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Catholic Church builds cathedrals, Islam meets the West, towns and commerce grow, and the research project writes a letter from Joan of Arc.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Medieval Europe is built by the Catholic Church's cathedrals, shaken by Islam's meeting with the West, and changed by growing towns and commerce — and its research project writes a letter from Joan of Arc.
