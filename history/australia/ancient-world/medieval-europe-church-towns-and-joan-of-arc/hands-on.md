# Hands-on — Medieval Europe: Church, Towns and Joan of Arc

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the first two lines of a letter from a young person who believes she has been given a mission too big for her. Sign it 'Joan'. This lesson is about the world she wrote from — churches, towns and faith at war.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Medieval Europe is built by the Catholic Church's cathedrals, shaken by Islam's meeting with the West, and changed by growing towns and commerce — and its research project writes a letter from Joan of Arc. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
