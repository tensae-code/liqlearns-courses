# The Black Death in Asia, Europe and Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Before the plague: medieval medicine; then the Black Death spreads through Asia, Europe and Africa — impact, responses and effects.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Black Death spreads through Asia, Europe and Africa — and the book begins before it, with medieval medicine, then follows the plague's impact, the responses it provoked, and the world it changed.
