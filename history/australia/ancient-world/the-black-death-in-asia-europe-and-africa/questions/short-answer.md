# The Black Death in Asia, Europe and Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Black Death.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Black Death spreads through Asia, Europe and Africa — and the book begins before it, with medieval medicine, then follows the plague's impact, the responses it provoked, and the world it changed. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
