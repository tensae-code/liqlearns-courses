**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Black Death in Asia, Europe and Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Black Death spreads through Asia, Europe and Africa — and the book begins before it, with medieval medicine, then follows the plague's impact, the responses it provoked, and the world it changed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Black Death spreads through Asia, Europe and Africa — and the book begins before it, with medieval medicine, then follows the plague's impact, the responses it provoked, and the world it changed.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the sickness rules your own school lived by in recent years — closures, distance, fear — the Black Death's responses in modern dress.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
