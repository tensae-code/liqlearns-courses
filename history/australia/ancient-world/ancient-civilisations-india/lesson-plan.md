# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: India

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: India
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ancient India rises in the Indus Valley, is reshaped by Aryan arrivals, deepened by its beliefs and philosophies, ruled by Ashoka and the Guptas — a civilisation carried by its ideas as much as its kings.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Ancient India rises in the Indus Valley, is reshaped by Aryan arrivals, deepened by its beliefs and philosophies, ruled by Ashoka and the Guptas — a civilisation carried by its ideas as much as its kings.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a belief or practice from India alive in your own town today — yoga, a festival, a word — India's ideas still travelling.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
