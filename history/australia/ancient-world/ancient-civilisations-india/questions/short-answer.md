# Ancient Civilisations: India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient India.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because ancient India rises in the Indus Valley, is reshaped by Aryan arrivals, deepened by its beliefs and philosophies, ruled by Ashoka and the Guptas — a civilisation carried by its ideas as much as its kings. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
