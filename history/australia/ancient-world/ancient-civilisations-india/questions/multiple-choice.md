# Ancient Civilisations: India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Ancient India” is right?
a) The Indus Valley civilisation, Aryan invasions, beliefs and philosophies, Ashoka, the Gupta Empire and daily life.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Ancient India rises in the Indus Valley, is reshaped by Aryan arrivals, deepened by its beliefs and philosophies, ruled by Ashoka and the Guptas — a civilisation carried by its ideas as much as its kings.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
