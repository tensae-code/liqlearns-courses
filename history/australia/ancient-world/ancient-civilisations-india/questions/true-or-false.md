# Ancient Civilisations: India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Indus Valley civilisation, Aryan invasions, beliefs and philosophies, Ashoka, the Gupta Empire and daily life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Ancient India rises in the Indus Valley, is reshaped by Aryan arrivals, deepened by its beliefs and philosophies, ruled by Ashoka and the Guptas — a civilisation carried by its ideas as much as its kings.
