# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find a river on a map of India — the Indus or the Ganges. Write one line on what a river valley gives a first civilisation, remembering Egypt. Then write one idea — about life, duty or the soul — that you know came out of India.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Ancient India rises in the Indus Valley, is reshaped by Aryan arrivals, deepened by its beliefs and philosophies, ruled by Ashoka and the Guptas — a civilisation carried by its ideas as much as its kings. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
