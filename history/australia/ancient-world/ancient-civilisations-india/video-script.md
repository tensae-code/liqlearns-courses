# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ancient India rises in the Indus Valley, is reshaped by Aryan arrivals, deepened by its beliefs and philosophies, ruled by Ashoka and the Guptas — a civilisation carried by its ideas as much as its kings.

India's ancient story begins in a river valley, like Egypt's — but the Indus Valley cities astonish in their own way: planned streets, drains, great baths, a civilisation of order whose writing we still cannot read. Over that world came Aryan arrivals, bringing new languages, gods and social orders, and out of the meeting grew the beliefs and philosophies that mark India ever after — deep teachings about duty, the soul and release from suffering. The book follows the story through its great rulers: Ashoka, the conqueror who turned from war to preach peace and carved his edicts on pillars across his empire, and the Gupta Empire, a golden age of art and learning — with daily life, as always in this book, carrying the story at ground level.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
