**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Before the Spanish arrive, the pre-Columbian world stands on its own: the Aztecs build Tenochtitlan on a lake and raise a whole belief around it — a civilisation complete before contact.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any city you know built on difficult ground — a swamp, a desert, a hill — Tenochtitlan is that story at imperial scale.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
