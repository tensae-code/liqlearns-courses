# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The pre-Columbian world and the building of Aztec belief in Tenochtitlán precede the Spanish arrival.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Before the Spanish arrive, the pre-Columbian world stands on its own: the Aztecs build Tenochtitlan on a lake and raise a whole belief around it — a civilisation complete before contact.
