# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient civilisations of the Americas and Aztec belief.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because before the Spanish arrive, the pre-Columbian world stands on its own: the Aztecs build Tenochtitlan on a lake and raise a whole belief around it — a civilisation complete before contact. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
