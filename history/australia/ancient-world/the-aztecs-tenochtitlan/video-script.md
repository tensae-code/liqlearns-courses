# Video script — The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Before the Spanish arrive, the pre-Columbian world stands on its own: the Aztecs build Tenochtitlan on a lake and raise a whole belief around it — a civilisation complete before contact.

The Americas' story in this book does not begin with European ships. It begins with the pre-Columbian world — peoples and civilisations entire, who had never heard of Europe. At its centre stands Tenochtitlan, the Aztec capital built on a lake: causeways and canals, markets feeding a huge city, chinampa gardens floating on the shallows. Around the city the book builds Aztec belief — the gods, the calendar, the great temple where the world's survival was ritually renewed. This lesson deliberately stops before the Spanish arrival: the Aztec world must first be understood as complete in itself, or the conquest that follows cannot be honestly weighed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
