**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: China

**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Qin Shi Huangdi unites China and rules it as First Emperor — government, society and beliefs under one hand, farming and everyday life beneath it, and a mausoleum that still guards his ambition.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the single money, single measures or single school rules of your own country — China's First Emperor invented that kind of oneness.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
