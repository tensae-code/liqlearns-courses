# Ancient Civilisations: China — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Early Imperial China.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because qin Shi Huangdi unites China and rules it as First Emperor — government, society and beliefs under one hand, farming and everyday life beneath it, and a mausoleum that still guards his ambition. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
