# Ancient Civilisations: China — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Qin Shi Huangdi's government, society and beliefs, farming and everyday life, and the First Emperor's mausoleum as site study.

**Answer: False** — Qin Shi Huangdi unites China and rules it as First Emperor — government, society and beliefs under one hand, farming and everyday life beneath it, and a mausoleum that still guards his ambition.
