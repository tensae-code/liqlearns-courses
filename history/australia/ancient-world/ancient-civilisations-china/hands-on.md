# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: China

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down one rule that holds your whole school or household together. Then write what it would take for one ruler to hold a whole country of millions together the same way. That gap is Qin Shi Huangdi's problem — and his answer.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Qin Shi Huangdi unites China and rules it as First Emperor — government, society and beliefs under one hand, farming and everyday life beneath it, and a mausoleum that still guards his ambition. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
