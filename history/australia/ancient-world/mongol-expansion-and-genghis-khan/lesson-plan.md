# Lesson plan — Mongol Expansion and Genghis Khan

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Mongol Expansion and Genghis Khan
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Temujin rises as Genghis Khan and builds the largest land empire the world had seen — conquest, the law of the Mongol lands, Yuan China, Marco Polo at the khan's court, defeat and legacy in one arc.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Temujin rises as Genghis Khan and builds the largest land empire the world had seen — conquest, the law of the Mongol lands, Yuan China, Marco Polo at the khan's court, defeat and legacy in one arc.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any leader you know who united quarrelling groups into one force — Temujin did it for the whole steppe.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
