# Mongol Expansion and Genghis Khan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Mongol Expansion.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because temujin rises as Genghis Khan and builds the largest land empire the world had seen — conquest, the law of the Mongol lands, Yuan China, Marco Polo at the khan's court, defeat and legacy in one arc. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
