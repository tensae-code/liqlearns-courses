# Mongol Expansion and Genghis Khan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Temujin rises as Genghis Khan; the Mongol army of conquest, the law of the Mongol lands, Yuan China, Marco Polo at the khan's court, and the empire's defeat and legacy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Temujin rises as Genghis Khan and builds the largest land empire the world had seen — conquest, the law of the Mongol lands, Yuan China, Marco Polo at the khan's court, defeat and legacy in one arc.
