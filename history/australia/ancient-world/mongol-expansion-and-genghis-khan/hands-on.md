# Hands-on — Mongol Expansion and Genghis Khan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the name 'Temujin' and under it three things an orphan boy on the steppe would need to become ruler of half the world. Keep the list: this lesson tests it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Temujin rises as Genghis Khan and builds the largest land empire the world had seen — conquest, the law of the Mongol lands, Yuan China, Marco Polo at the khan's court, defeat and legacy in one arc. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
