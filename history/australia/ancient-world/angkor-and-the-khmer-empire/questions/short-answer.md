# Angkor and the Khmer Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Angkor/Khmer Empire.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Khmer Empire rises in Southeast Asia, builds Angkor's temples, orders its people's roles and everyday life — and then declines, leaving its stones to tell the story. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
