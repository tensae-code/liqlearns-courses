# Angkor and the Khmer Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Khmer empire rises, builds Angkor's temples, orders roles and everyday life, then declines — blogged by students in the research project.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Khmer Empire rises in Southeast Asia, builds Angkor's temples, orders its people's roles and everyday life — and then declines, leaving its stones to tell the story.
