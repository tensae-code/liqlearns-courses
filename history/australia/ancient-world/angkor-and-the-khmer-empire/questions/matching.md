# Angkor and the Khmer Empire — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Angkor and the Khmer Empire — a)

**2.** The Angkor/Khmer Empire — b)


Meanings:
a) The Khmer Empire rises in Southeast Asia, builds Angkor's temples, orders its people's roles and everyday life — and then declines, leaving its stones to tell the story.
b) The topic of this lesson, taught in Australia's own book: Angkor and the Khmer Empire.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
