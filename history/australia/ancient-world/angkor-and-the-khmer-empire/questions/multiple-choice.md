# Angkor and the Khmer Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The Angkor/Khmer Empire” is right?
a) The Khmer empire rises, builds Angkor's temples, orders roles and everyday life, then declines — blogged by students in the research project.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Khmer Empire rises in Southeast Asia, builds Angkor's temples, orders its people's roles and everyday life — and then declines, leaving its stones to tell the story.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
