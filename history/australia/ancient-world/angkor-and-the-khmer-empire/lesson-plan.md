# Lesson plan — Angkor and the Khmer Empire

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Angkor and the Khmer Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Khmer Empire rises in Southeast Asia, builds Angkor's temples, orders its people's roles and everyday life — and then declines, leaving its stones to tell the story.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Khmer Empire rises in Southeast Asia, builds Angkor's temples, orders its people's roles and everyday life — and then declines, leaving its stones to tell the story.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any great building in your country raised by many hands for one ruler's glory — Angkor is that story at its largest.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
