# Video script — Angkor and the Khmer Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Khmer Empire rises in Southeast Asia, builds Angkor's temples, orders its people's roles and everyday life — and then declines, leaving its stones to tell the story.

In the forests of Southeast Asia the Khmer Empire raised the largest religious building on earth. The book follows its whole arc: the rise of Khmer kings ruling from Angkor, a city of temples and reservoirs; the building — Angkor's stone mountains raised as heavens on earth for god-kings; the order of roles and everyday life beneath them, farmers feeding the court from engineered waters; and then the decline, as the empire faded and the forest took the stones back. The research project blogs the empire — learners posting Angkor's story as if reporting it — rise, glory, order and fall in one people's telling.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
