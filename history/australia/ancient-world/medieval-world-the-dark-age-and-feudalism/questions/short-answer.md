# The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The medieval world; Medieval Europe — feudal society.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because after Rome, Europe orders itself by feudal bonds — lords, knights and peasants bound in a ladder of land and loyalty — and the survey first, then the depth study, shows that order whole: everyday life, crime and punishment, and towns. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
