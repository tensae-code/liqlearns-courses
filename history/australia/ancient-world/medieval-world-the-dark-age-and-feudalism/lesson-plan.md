# Lesson plan — The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After Rome, Europe orders itself by feudal bonds — lords, knights and peasants bound in a ladder of land and loyalty — and the survey first, then the depth study, shows that order whole: everyday life, crime and punishment, and towns.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After Rome, Europe orders itself by feudal bonds — lords, knights and peasants bound in a ladder of land and loyalty — and the survey first, then the depth study, shows that order whole: everyday life, crime and punishment, and towns.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any team or school where each level owes duties up and protection down — the feudal ladder's small modern echo.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
