## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Ancient Greece is studied whole — its setting among sea and islands, its politics and society, its everyday life, its wars and its religion form one Greek world.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the sea trade your own town lives by, if it has a port — Greece's island-and-harbour world is the ancient form of it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
