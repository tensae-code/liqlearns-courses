# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ancient Greece is studied whole — its setting among sea and islands, its politics and society, its everyday life, its wars and its religion form one Greek world.

Greece begins with its setting: a rocky land cut into valleys, opening onto a sea scattered with islands. No single king could easily hold such a land together, so Greece grew as many small city-states, each jealous of its freedom, all sharing one language, one set of gods and one sea. The book studies that world whole — its politics and society, the everyday life of its farms and workshops and harbours, its wars between cities and against empires, and its religion, the stories and festivals that made the separate cities feel like one people. Crete stands at the study's edge as the older island world Greece inherited from. Greece whole, not one famous city: that is the study's shape.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
