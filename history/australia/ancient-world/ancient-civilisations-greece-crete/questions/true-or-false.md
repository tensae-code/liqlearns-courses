# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Greece is studied whole — setting, politics and society, everyday life, war and religion.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Ancient Greece is studied whole — its setting among sea and islands, its politics and society, its everyday life, its wars and its religion form one Greek world.
