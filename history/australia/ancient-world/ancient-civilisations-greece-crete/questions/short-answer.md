# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient Greece.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because ancient Greece is studied whole — its setting among sea and islands, its politics and society, its everyday life, its wars and its religion form one Greek world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
