# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Politics, the Olympic Games and two famous Greeks.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because in Athens the citizens themselves ruled: the ecclesia debated and decided — and the book makes learners re-enact that debate, with the Olympic Games and two famous Greeks as the human faces of the Greek world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
