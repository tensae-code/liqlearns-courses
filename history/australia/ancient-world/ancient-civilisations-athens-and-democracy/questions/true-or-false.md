# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Athenian democracy is re-enacted in a research-project debate in the ecclesia, with the Olympic Games and two famous Greeks as the human faces.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — In Athens the citizens themselves ruled: the ecclesia debated and decided — and the book makes learners re-enact that debate, with the Olympic Games and two famous Greeks as the human faces of the Greek world.
