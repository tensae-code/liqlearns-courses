# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw one big circle labelled 'one empire'. Now draw lines splitting it into pieces, and label each piece with what it keeps — language, laws, faith, armies. Write one line: what survives when an empire divides?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Roman world transforms and divides as antiquity turns into the medieval world — one empire becomes the seed of several worlds. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
