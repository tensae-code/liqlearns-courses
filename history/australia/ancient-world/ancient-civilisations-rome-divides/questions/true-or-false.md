# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Roman world transforms and divides as antiquity turns into the medieval world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Roman world transforms and divides as antiquity turns into the medieval world — one empire becomes the seed of several worlds.
