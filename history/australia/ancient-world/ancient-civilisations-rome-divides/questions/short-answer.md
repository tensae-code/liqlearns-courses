# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Transformation of the Roman world.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Roman world transforms and divides as antiquity turns into the medieval world — one empire becomes the seed of several worlds. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
