# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Roman world transforms and divides as antiquity turns into the medieval world — one empire becomes the seed of several worlds.

Empires do not only fall; they transform. The overview follows the Roman world as it changes and divides — an empire too large for one ruler, split between east and west; the west breaking into kingdoms, the east holding on in Greek-speaking strength. Roads, laws, cities and faith all pass through the division and come out changed on the other side. This is the hinge of the whole Stage 4 course: antiquity turning into the medieval world, and the learner is asked to watch the turn itself, not just the worlds before and after it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
