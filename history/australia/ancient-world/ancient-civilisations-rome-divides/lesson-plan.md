## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Roman world transforms and divides as antiquity turns into the medieval world — one empire becomes the seed of several worlds.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a big organisation you know that split into smaller ones, each keeping part of the old ways — Rome's division at small scale.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

- Go back to the one thing: The Roman world transforms and divides as antiquity turns into the medieval world — one empire becomes the seed of several worlds. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
