# Hands-on — Polynesian Expansion Across the Pacific

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a triangle on a map of the Pacific with corners at Hawaii, Easter Island and New Zealand. Inside it write: 'settled by canoe'. Write one line on what those sailors must have known about stars, winds and birds.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Polynesian voyagers settle the whole Pacific — the greatest sea journey in the ancient world — and the study follows life and death on Easter Island, the land of the long white cloud, Maoritanga and Polynesian power. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
