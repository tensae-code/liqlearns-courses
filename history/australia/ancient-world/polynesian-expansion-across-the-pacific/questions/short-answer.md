# Polynesian Expansion Across the Pacific — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Polynesian expansion across the Pacific.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because polynesian voyagers settle the whole Pacific — the greatest sea journey in the ancient world — and the study follows life and death on Easter Island, the land of the long white cloud, Maoritanga and Polynesian power. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
