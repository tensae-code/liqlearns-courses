# Polynesian Expansion Across the Pacific — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Polynesian voyagers settle the Pacific — life and death on Easter Island, the land of the long white cloud, Maoritanga and Polynesian power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Polynesian voyagers settle the whole Pacific — the greatest sea journey in the ancient world — and the study follows life and death on Easter Island, the land of the long white cloud, Maoritanga and Polynesian power.
