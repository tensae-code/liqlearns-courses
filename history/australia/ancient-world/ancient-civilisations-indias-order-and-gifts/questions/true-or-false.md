# Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Indian influence spreads across Asia; its art, science and technology are weighed as gifts to the world in the Indus Valley research project.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — India's influence spreads across Asia, and its art, science and technology are weighed as gifts to the world — India's order travels as far as its armies never went.
