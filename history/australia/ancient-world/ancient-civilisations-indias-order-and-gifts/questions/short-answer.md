# Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** India's spread, impact and gifts.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because india's influence spreads across Asia, and its art, science and technology are weighed as gifts to the world — India's order travels as far as its armies never went. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
