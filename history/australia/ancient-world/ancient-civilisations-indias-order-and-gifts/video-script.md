# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
India's influence spreads across Asia, and its art, science and technology are weighed as gifts to the world — India's order travels as far as its armies never went.

The India study closes by looking outward. Indian influence spread across Asia — along trade roads and sea routes, with merchants, monks and storytellers — until Indian scripts, stories, gods and ways of building stood from Central Asia to the islands of the south-east. The book asks learners to weigh that spread in the Indus Valley research project: India's art, its science and its technology assessed as gifts to the world. The weighing matters. Empires spread by taking; India also spread by giving — and the study leaves learners to judge which kind of spread lasts longer.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
