# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** India's influence spreads across Asia, and its art, science and technology are weighed as gifts to the world — India's order travels as far as its armies never went.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: India's influence spreads across Asia, and its art, science and technology are weighed as gifts to the world — India's order travels as far as its armies never went.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the number system you used in maths today — India's gifts reached your exercise book without any army.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
