# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: India's Order and Gifts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one number, one medicine idea, or one story that reached your country from far away. Write one line: how do gifts travel — by armies, by traders, or by teachers?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: India's influence spreads across Asia, and its art, science and technology are weighed as gifts to the world — India's order travels as far as its armies never went. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
