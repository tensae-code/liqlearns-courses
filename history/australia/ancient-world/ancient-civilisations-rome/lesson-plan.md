# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Rome
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Rome grows from Republic to Empire: its everyday life, its contacts and conflicts, its religion and the figure of Julius Caesar carry the story of the ancient world's greatest power.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the main road out of your own town — Rome's roads did to Italy what yours does to your district, only further.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
