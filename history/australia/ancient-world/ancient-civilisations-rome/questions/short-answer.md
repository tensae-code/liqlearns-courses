# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient Rome.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because rome grows from Republic to Empire: its everyday life, its contacts and conflicts, its religion and the figure of Julius Caesar carry the story of the ancient world's greatest power. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
