# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rome from Republic to Empire — everyday life, contacts and conflicts, religion, Julius Caesar, and a time-travel research project.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Rome grows from Republic to Empire: its everyday life, its contacts and conflicts, its religion and the figure of Julius Caesar carry the story of the ancient world's greatest power.
