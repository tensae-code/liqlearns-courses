# Lesson plan — Who Studies Prehistory

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Who Studies Prehistory
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: The Historian's Craft (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Historians are not alone with the deep past: anthropologists and archaeologists read human remains and old things, and dating methods like dendrochronology and radiocarbon tell how old they are.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: The Historian's Craft (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Historians are not alone with the deep past: anthropologists and archaeologists read human remains and old things, and dating methods like dendrochronology and radiocarbon tell how old they are.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — an old tool or pot in a museum near you — an archaeologist's find, dated and read like a document.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
