# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Other experts, methods and human remains.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because historians are not alone with the deep past: anthropologists and archaeologists read human remains and old things, and dating methods like dendrochronology and radiocarbon tell how old they are. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
