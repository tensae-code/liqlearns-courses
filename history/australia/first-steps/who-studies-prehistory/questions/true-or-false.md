# Who Studies Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Anthropologists, archaeologists and dating methods (dendrochronology, radiocarbon) read human remains — Tutankhamun's injuries and Ötzi the Iceman, found in the Ötztal Alps in 1991, are the case studies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Historians are not alone with the deep past: anthropologists and archaeologists read human remains and old things, and dating methods like dendrochronology and radiocarbon tell how old they are.
