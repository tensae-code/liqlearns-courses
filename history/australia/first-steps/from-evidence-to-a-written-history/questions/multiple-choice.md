# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Sources — the historian's clues” is right?
a) Sources are analysed and used, with a special section on sources for ancient Australia, before any history can be written.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) No history can be written until sources are analysed and used — and for ancient Australia, the sources have their own special section and their own special care.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
