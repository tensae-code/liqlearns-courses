# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sources are analysed and used, with a special section on sources for ancient Australia, before any history can be written.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — No history can be written until sources are analysed and used — and for ancient Australia, the sources have their own special section and their own special care.
