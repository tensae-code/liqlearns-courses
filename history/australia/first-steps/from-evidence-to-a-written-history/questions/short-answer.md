# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Sources — the historian's clues.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because no history can be written until sources are analysed and used — and for ancient Australia, the sources have their own special section and their own special care. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
