# Hands-on — From Evidence to a Written History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find two things at home that tell about the same past event — a photo and a story someone tells, or two messages about one day. Write what each one shows, and what only the two together can prove.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: No history can be written until sources are analysed and used — and for ancient Australia, the sources have their own special section and their own special care. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
