# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is detective work on the past: historians investigate significant questions from clues, forming hypotheses and testing them against evidence.

Every history in this course begins the way this book begins: with a detective. A historian does not start with answers. A historian starts with a significant question — a question that matters — and hunts for clues. Each clue is weighed: where did it come from, can it be trusted, what does it really show? From the clues the historian forms a hypothesis, a best-guess answer, and then tests that guess against more evidence, keeping it only if the evidence holds it up. That is the craft underneath every story in this course: question, clues, hypothesis, test.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
