# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Historians are detectives of the past: they investigate significant questions from clues, forming hypotheses and testing them against evidence.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — History is detective work on the past: historians investigate significant questions from clues, forming hypotheses and testing them against evidence.
