# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Investigating the Ancient Past.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because history is detective work on the past: historians investigate significant questions from clues, forming hypotheses and testing them against evidence. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
