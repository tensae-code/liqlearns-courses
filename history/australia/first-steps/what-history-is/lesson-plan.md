# Lesson plan — What History Is

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What History Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: The Historian's Craft (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History is detective work on the past: historians investigate significant questions from clues, forming hypotheses and testing them against evidence.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: The Historian's Craft (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- This is the first lesson on the path — nothing is recommended before it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History is detective work on the past: historians investigate significant questions from clues, forming hypotheses and testing them against evidence.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a question about your own street or town — its name, its oldest building — investigated from clues, exactly as this lesson teaches.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation — the lesson told properly (lesson/lesson.md).**

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks history is a list of dates to memorise → the book opens by making the historian a detective: a question first, then clues, then a tested answer.
- Go back to the one thing: History is detective work on the past: historians investigate significant questions from clues, forming hypotheses and testing them against evidence. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
