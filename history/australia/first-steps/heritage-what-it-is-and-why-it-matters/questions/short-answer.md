# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Conserving the past.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the past is worth conserving: when rising water threatened Ramesses II's temples at Abu Simbel, the world cut them apart and moved them to safety — heritage is the past we choose to keep. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
