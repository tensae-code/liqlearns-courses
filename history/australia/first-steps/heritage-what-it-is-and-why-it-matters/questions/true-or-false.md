# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ramesses II's Abu Simbel temples are cut and moved (1964–68) to save them from Lake Nasser; a virtual site study of Stonehenge teaches why the past is conserved.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The past is worth conserving: when rising water threatened Ramesses II's temples at Abu Simbel, the world cut them apart and moved them to safety — heritage is the past we choose to keep.
