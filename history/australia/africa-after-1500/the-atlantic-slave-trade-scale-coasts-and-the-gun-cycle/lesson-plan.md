## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Industrial Revolution moves peoples by force: enslaved Africans' experiences and slavery's impacts are studied in their own right — the human cargo at the foundation of the Atlantic world.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the freedoms you used this morning without thinking — movement, work, family — are exactly what this trade stole, by the million.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
