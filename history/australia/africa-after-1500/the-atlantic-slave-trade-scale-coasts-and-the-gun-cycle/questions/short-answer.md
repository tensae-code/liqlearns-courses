# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Movement of peoples — experiences and impacts of slavery.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Industrial Revolution moves peoples by force: enslaved Africans' experiences and slavery's impacts are studied in their own right — the human cargo at the foundation of the Atlantic world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
