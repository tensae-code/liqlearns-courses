# Video script — Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the 19th century Europe's empires expand across the globe, carrying the modern world by force — imperial expansion is the Industrial Revolution's shadow, reaching every shore including Australia's.

The modern world did not spread by invitation. In the 19th century Europe's empires expand across the globe — Africa partitioned, Asia pried open, the Pacific claimed island by island — carrying the industrial world by force: its goods and guns, its flags and its faith, its claim to be civilizing the peoples it ruled. Australia itself sits inside this expansion, a colony of the empire that led it. The overview's cold lesson is that the modern world's arrival and its violence are the same event seen from different shores, and the course will keep landing world processes on Australian soil exactly as this overview lands empire on the world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
