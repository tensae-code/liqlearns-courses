# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** European imperial expansion.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because in the 19th century Europe's empires expand across the globe, carrying the modern world by force — imperial expansion is the Industrial Revolution's shadow, reaching every shore including Australia's. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
