# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europe's empires expand across the globe in the 19th century, carrying the modern world by force.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — In the 19th century Europe's empires expand across the globe, carrying the modern world by force — imperial expansion is the Industrial Revolution's shadow, reaching every shore including Australia's.
