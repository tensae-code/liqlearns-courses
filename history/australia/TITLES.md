# History — Australia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Australia's
own — built from Australia's *Retroactive 1* and *Retroactive
2*, the NSW Australian Curriculum History books for Stage 4
and Stage 5 (Jacaranda, English). Stage 4 runs one long arc
from the historian's craft through the ancient and medieval
worlds to colonisation and contact on Australia's own shores;
Stage 5 turns the same depth-study method onto the modern
world, with Australia as the protagonist. Inside the course
the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below sit inside the levels, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grade each book comes from lives
only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': method before story
(detectives, sources, heritage), the ancient world whole, the
medieval and expanding world whole, contact judged from both
shores — and then the modern world landed, again and again,
on Australian soil: convicts, nation-making, Gallipoli,
Darwin, the Freedom Ride, Mabo, Maralinga. That ladder IS the
path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).
