# Australia in the First World War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Australia in World War I.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because australia's First World War runs from Gallipoli and the Western Front to the home front — prisoners of war, the conscription referendums that divide the nation, commemoration, and the making of the Anzac legend. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
