# Australia in the First World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gallipoli and the Western Front, prisoners of war, the home front, the conscription referendums that divide the nation, commemoration and the making of the Anzac legend.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Australia's First World War runs from Gallipoli and the Western Front to the home front — prisoners of war, the conscription referendums that divide the nation, commemoration, and the making of the Anzac legend.
