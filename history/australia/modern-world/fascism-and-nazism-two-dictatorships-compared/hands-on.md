# Hands-on — Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two columns: 'rights taken by law' and 'rights taken by violence'. Nazism used both, in that order. Keep the columns: this lesson fills them year by year.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Nazism rules Germany first by law and then by pogrom — the legal destruction of rights from 1933 to 1938, and Kristallnacht on 9–10 November 1938 — on the road to genocide. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
