1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Nazism rules Germany first by law and then by pogrom — the legal destruction of rights from 1933 to 1938, and Kristallnacht on 9–10 November 1938 — on the road to genocide.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
