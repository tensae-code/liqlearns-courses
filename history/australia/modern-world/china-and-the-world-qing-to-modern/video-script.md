# Video script — China and the World: Qing to Modern

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the whole dynastic story ends in, and the beginning of China's modern place in the world.

The China study closes on its turn. Out of the fallen Qing order, China emerges into the modern world — not as the Middle Kingdom receiving tribute, but as one nation among nations, forced to learn the modern world's ways while defending its own civilisation's core. The book gives this emergence its own short lesson because it is the hinge of the region Australia lives in: every later story in this course — the Pacific war, the Cold War, Vietnam, migration — has modern China standing in it. The dynastic wheel has stopped; a new story, still running, has begun.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
