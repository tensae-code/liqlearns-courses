# Hands-on — China and the World: Qing to Modern

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one line on what a country keeps when its old order falls, and one line on what it must invent. China's modern story is the working-out of those two lines.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the whole dynastic story ends in, and the beginning of China's modern place in the world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
