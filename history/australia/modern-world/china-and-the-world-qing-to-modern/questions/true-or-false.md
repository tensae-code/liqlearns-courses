# China and the World: Qing to Modern — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the dynastic story ends in.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the whole dynastic story ends in, and the beginning of China's modern place in the world.
