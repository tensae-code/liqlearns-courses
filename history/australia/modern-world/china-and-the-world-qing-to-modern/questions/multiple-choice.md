# China and the World: Qing to Modern — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “China in the modern world” is right?
a) China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the dynastic story ends in.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the whole dynastic story ends in, and the beginning of China's modern place in the world.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
