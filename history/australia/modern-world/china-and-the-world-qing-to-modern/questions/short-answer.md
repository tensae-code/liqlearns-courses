# China and the World: Qing to Modern — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** China in the modern world.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because china emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the whole dynastic story ends in, and the beginning of China's modern place in the world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
