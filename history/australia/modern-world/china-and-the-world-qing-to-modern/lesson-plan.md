# Lesson plan — China and the World: Qing to Modern

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** China and the World: Qing to Modern
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — China's Turn and the World Wars (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the whole dynastic story ends in, and the beginning of China's modern place in the world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — China's Turn and the World Wars (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: China emerges from empire into the modern world — the turn the whole dynastic story ends in, and the beginning of China's modern place in the world.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — goods and students and news moving between your country and China today — the modern relationship this lesson's turn began.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
