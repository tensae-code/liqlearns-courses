# Australia: Popular Culture After 1945 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Popular culture.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because post-war popular culture — American and British influence, television's arrival, sport, music and film — changes what Australians believe and value: a nation's culture remade in its lounge rooms. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
