# Australia: Popular Culture After 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Post-war popular culture — US and British influence, television's arrival, sport, music and film — changes Australian beliefs and values.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Post-war popular culture — American and British influence, television's arrival, sport, music and film — changes what Australians believe and value: a nation's culture remade in its lounge rooms.
