# Lesson plan — Australia: Popular Culture After 1945

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Australia: Popular Culture After 1945
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a Changing Nation (CURRICULUM.md).
