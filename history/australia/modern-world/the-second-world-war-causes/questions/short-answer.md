# The Second World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** World War II — the origins.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the origins of the Second World War are traced in the Europe of the dictators — the peace that failed, the Depression's desperation, and the dictators who turned both into a road to war. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
