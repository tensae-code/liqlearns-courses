# The Second World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The origins of the Second World War are traced in the Europe of the dictators.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The origins of the Second World War are traced in the Europe of the dictators — the peace that failed, the Depression's desperation, and the dictators who turned both into a road to war.
