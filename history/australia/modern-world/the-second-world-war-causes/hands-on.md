# Hands-on — The Second World War: Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write three boxes: 'a peace that humiliates' → 'an economy that collapses' → 'a dictator who promises revenge'. Europe between the wars filled every box. This lesson checks each one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The origins of the Second World War are traced in the Europe of the dictators — the peace that failed, the Depression's desperation, and the dictators who turned both into a road to war. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
