# Aboriginal Australia: Colonisation, Contact and Dispossession — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Aboriginal and Indigenous Peoples, Colonisation and Contact (Australia parts)” is right?
a) Life before the Europeans, first contact, dispute and dispossession; Aboriginal land, Dreaming and law; battling to survive under "protection" — the colonial legacy.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Aboriginal Australia — its land, Dreaming and law — meets European colonisation: first contact, dispute and dispossession, and the long battle to survive under 'protection'; this is Australia's own contact story, told from its own book.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
