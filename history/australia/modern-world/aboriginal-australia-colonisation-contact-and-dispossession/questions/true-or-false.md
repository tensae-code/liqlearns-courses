# Aboriginal Australia: Colonisation, Contact and Dispossession — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Life before the Europeans, first contact, dispute and dispossession; Aboriginal land, Dreaming and law; battling to survive under "protection" — the colonial legacy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Aboriginal Australia — its land, Dreaming and law — meets European colonisation: first contact, dispute and dispossession, and the long battle to survive under 'protection'; this is Australia's own contact story, told from its own book.
