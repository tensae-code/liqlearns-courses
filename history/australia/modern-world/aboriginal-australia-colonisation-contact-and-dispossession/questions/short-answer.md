# Aboriginal Australia: Colonisation, Contact and Dispossession — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Aboriginal and Indigenous Peoples, Colonisation and Contact (Australia parts).

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because aboriginal Australia — its land, Dreaming and law — meets European colonisation: first contact, dispute and dispossession, and the long battle to survive under 'protection'; this is Australia's own contact story, told from its own book. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
