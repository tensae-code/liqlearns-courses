# Lesson plan — Aboriginal Australia: Colonisation, Contact and Dispossession

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Aboriginal Australia: Colonisation, Contact and Dispossession
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Contact, Conquest and Colonisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Aboriginal Australia — its land, Dreaming and law — meets European colonisation: first contact, dispute and dispossession, and the long battle to survive under 'protection'; this is Australia's own contact story, told from its own book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Contact, Conquest and Colonisation (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Aboriginal Australia — its land, Dreaming and law — meets European colonisation: first contact, dispute and dispossession, and the long battle to survive under 'protection'; this is Australia's own contact story, told from its own book.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the oldest continuous living culture on earth is this lesson's subject — and it is the country this course belongs to: Australia itself.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
