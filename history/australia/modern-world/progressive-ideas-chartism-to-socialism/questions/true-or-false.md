# Progressive Ideas: Chartism to Socialism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Chartists petition (1836–1860), Darwin's ideas shake belief, egalitarians and socialists answer capitalism and imperialism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The century answers capitalism with ideas: Chartists petition for the vote, Darwin's ideas shake belief, and egalitarians and socialists build the case against capitalism and imperialism.
