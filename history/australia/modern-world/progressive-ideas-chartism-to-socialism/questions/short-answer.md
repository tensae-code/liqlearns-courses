# Progressive Ideas: Chartism to Socialism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Capitalism, Chartism, Darwinism, egalitarianism, socialism.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the century answers capitalism with ideas: Chartists petition for the vote, Darwin's ideas shake belief, and egalitarians and socialists build the case against capitalism and imperialism. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
