# Hands-on — Progressive Ideas: Chartism to Socialism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a three-line petition: one change you want, one reason, one signature line. The Chartists gathered millions of signatures on petitions like yours — this lesson follows what such paper can and cannot do.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The century answers capitalism with ideas: Chartists petition for the vote, Darwin's ideas shake belief, and egalitarians and socialists build the case against capitalism and imperialism. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
