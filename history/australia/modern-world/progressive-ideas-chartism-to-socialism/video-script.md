# Video script — Progressive Ideas: Chartism to Socialism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The century answers capitalism with ideas: Chartists petition for the vote, Darwin's ideas shake belief, and egalitarians and socialists build the case against capitalism and imperialism.

The progressive-ideas study widens into the century's whole argument. The Chartists petition — between 1836 and 1860 millions sign for the people's charter: votes for working men, fair parliaments — and are refused, and refuse to disappear. Darwin's ideas shake belief itself, recasting humanity's place in nature and arming every later argument about progress. And against capitalism and imperialism rise the systematic answers: egalitarianism, demanding real equality, and socialism, arguing the factories and land should serve all, not owners alone. The book teaches these as one shelf of weapons: every movement in the Australian lessons ahead — unions, suffrage, labour parties — reaches for something forged here.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
