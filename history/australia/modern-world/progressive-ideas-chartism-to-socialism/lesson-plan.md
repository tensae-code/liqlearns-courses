## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The century answers capitalism with ideas: Chartists petition for the vote, Darwin's ideas shake belief, and egalitarians and socialists build the case against capitalism and imperialism.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the vote every adult in your country holds — the Chartists petitioned for exactly that, and were refused for years.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
