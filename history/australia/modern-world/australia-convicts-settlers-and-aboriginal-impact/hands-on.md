# Hands-on — Australia: Convicts, Settlers and the Aboriginal Impact

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two sentences: one beginning 'The convicts gained…' and one beginning 'The Aboriginal people lost…'. The whole tragedy and making of colonial Australia stands between those sentences.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Convicts and free settlers build the Australian colonies — the research project follows the female convicts — while European settlement devastates the Aboriginal people who were already there. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
