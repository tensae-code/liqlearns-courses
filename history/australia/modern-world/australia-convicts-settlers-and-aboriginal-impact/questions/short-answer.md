# Australia: Convicts, Settlers and the Aboriginal Impact — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Convicts, settlers and Aboriginal people.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because convicts and free settlers build the Australian colonies — the research project follows the female convicts — while European settlement devastates the Aboriginal people who were already there. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
