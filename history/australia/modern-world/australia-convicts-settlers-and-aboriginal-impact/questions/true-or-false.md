# Australia: Convicts, Settlers and the Aboriginal Impact — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Convicts (the research project follows female convicts) and free settlers build Australia while European settlement devastates Aboriginal people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Convicts and free settlers build the Australian colonies — the research project follows the female convicts — while European settlement devastates the Aboriginal people who were already there.
