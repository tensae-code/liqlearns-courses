# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Changing ways of life and global impact.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because industrialisation changes ways of life everywhere it touches — new cities, new classes, a global reach — and the book follows its impact all the way to Australia and across the globe. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
