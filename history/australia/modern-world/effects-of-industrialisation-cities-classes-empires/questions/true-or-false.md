# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes, Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Industrialisation changes ways of life, reaches Australia, and spreads its impact across the globe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Industrialisation changes ways of life everywhere it touches — new cities, new classes, a global reach — and the book follows its impact all the way to Australia and across the globe.
