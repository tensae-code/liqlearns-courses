## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Industrialisation changes ways of life everywhere it touches — new cities, new classes, a global reach — and the book follows its impact all the way to Australia and across the globe.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — your own suburb or town, planned around work and transport — industrialisation's city-shape is the one you live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
