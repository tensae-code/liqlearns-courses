# The First World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The origins of the First World War are traced before Australia answers the call.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The origins of the First World War are traced before Australia answers the call — alliances, empires, arms races and one assassination lighting a Europe wired to explode.
