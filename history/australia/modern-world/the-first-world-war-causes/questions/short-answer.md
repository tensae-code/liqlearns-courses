# The First World War: Causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** World War I — the origins.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the origins of the First World War are traced before Australia answers the call — alliances, empires, arms races and one assassination lighting a Europe wired to explode. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
