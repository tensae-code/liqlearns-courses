## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The origins of the First World War are traced before Australia answers the call — alliances, empires, arms races and one assassination lighting a Europe wired to explode.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any quarrel you have seen where friends of each side got dragged in — alliance logic at playground scale is the war's logic at world scale.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
