# Hands-on — The Spanish Conquest of the Americas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two columns: 'What the Spanish wanted' and 'What the Aztecs had'. Fill two lines each. The conquest happens where the columns collide — keep them for the lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Spanish journey across the Atlantic ends in the conquest of the Aztec empire — gold and glory the lure, La Malinche at the story's centre, and colonial control the lasting result. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
