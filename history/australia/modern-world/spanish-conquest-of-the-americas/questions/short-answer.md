# The Spanish Conquest of the Americas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Spanish Conquest of the Americas.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Spanish journey across the Atlantic ends in the conquest of the Aztec empire — gold and glory the lure, La Malinche at the story's centre, and colonial control the lasting result. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
