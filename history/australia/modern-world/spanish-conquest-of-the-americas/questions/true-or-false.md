# The Spanish Conquest of the Americas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Spanish journey, the quest for gold and glory, the conquest of the Aztec empire, La Malinche — "mother of Mexico?" — and colonial control.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Spanish journey across the Atlantic ends in the conquest of the Aztec empire — gold and glory the lure, La Malinche at the story's centre, and colonial control the lasting result.
