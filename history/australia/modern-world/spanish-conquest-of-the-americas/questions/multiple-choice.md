# The Spanish Conquest of the Americas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The Spanish Conquest of the Americas” is right?
a) The Spanish journey, the quest for gold and glory, the conquest of the Aztec empire, La Malinche — "mother of Mexico?" — and colonial control.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Spanish journey across the Atlantic ends in the conquest of the Aztec empire — gold and glory the lure, La Malinche at the story's centre, and colonial control the lasting result.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
