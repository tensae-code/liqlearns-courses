# Lesson plan — The US Civil Rights Movement

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The US Civil Rights Movement
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a Changing Nation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles — a movement watched on Australian televisions and answered on Australian roads.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a Changing Nation (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles — a movement watched on Australian televisions and answered on Australian roads.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the protest footage in today's newsfeeds, watched worldwide within hours — the Freedom Ride generation had the same idea with television and newspapers.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
