# The US Civil Rights Movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The Civil Rights Movement” is right?
a) America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles — a movement watched on Australian televisions and answered on Australian roads.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
