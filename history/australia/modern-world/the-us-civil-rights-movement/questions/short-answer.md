# The US Civil Rights Movement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Civil Rights Movement.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because america's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles — a movement watched on Australian televisions and answered on Australian roads. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
