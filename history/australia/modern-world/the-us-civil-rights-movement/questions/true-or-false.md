# The US Civil Rights Movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles — a movement watched on Australian televisions and answered on Australian roads.
