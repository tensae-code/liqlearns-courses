# Hands-on — The US Civil Rights Movement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one line on what a protest on the other side of the world can give a protest at home. Australia's freedom riders answered that question with a bus: this lesson shows where they got it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles — a movement watched on Australian televisions and answered on Australian roads. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
