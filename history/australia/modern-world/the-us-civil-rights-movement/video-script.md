# Video script — The US Civil Rights Movement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America's civil rights movement is studied as the mirror and spark for Australia's own freedom struggles — a movement watched on Australian televisions and answered on Australian roads.

Inside the rights-and-freedoms study, the book turns to America. The US civil rights movement — the long fight against segregation, from buses and lunch counters to the great marches and the laws they forced — is studied in its own right, and then in its Australian reflection. Australia's activists watched it: on the new television, in the papers, in the shared language of freedom rides and sit-ins deliberately borrowed. Charles Perkins said openly that the American movement taught the Freedom Ride its tactics and its name. Mirror and spark, the book calls it: a mirror in which Australia saw its own discrimination plainly, and a spark that helped light its fire.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
