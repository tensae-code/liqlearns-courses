# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Pacific and the ending of the war.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Pacific war comes to Australia's doorstep — and its prisoners of war and its ending reshape the whole region Australia lives in. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
