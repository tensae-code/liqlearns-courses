# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Pacific war comes to Australia's doorstep; prisoners of war and the war's ending reshape the region.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Pacific war comes to Australia's doorstep — and its prisoners of war and its ending reshape the whole region Australia lives in.
