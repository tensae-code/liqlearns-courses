# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Industrial Revolution.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Industrial Revolution runs on a chain: agriculture improves, cotton leads, and coal, iron and steam transport power the factory age. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
