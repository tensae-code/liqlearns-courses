# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Agriculture improves, cotton leads, coal and iron and steam transport power the factory age.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Industrial Revolution runs on a chain: agriculture improves, cotton leads, and coal, iron and steam transport power the factory age.
