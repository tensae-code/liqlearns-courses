# Lesson plan — Australia After 1945: War, Peace and Rights

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Australia After 1945: War, Peace and Rights
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a Changing Nation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Australia after 1945 is framed in one overview: war and peace abroad, the fight for rights and freedoms at home, and a developing world reshaping the nation's region and its people.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a Changing Nation (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Australia after 1945 is framed in one overview: war and peace abroad, the fight for rights and freedoms at home, and a developing world reshaping the nation's region and its people.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the Australia you live in — multicultural, rights-arguing, Asia-facing — was built inside this overview's frame.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
