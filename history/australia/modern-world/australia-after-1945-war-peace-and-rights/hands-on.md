# Hands-on — Australia After 1945: War, Peace and Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write three headings — 'War and peace', 'Rights and freedoms', 'A changing world' — and under each, one thing you already know Australia did after 1945. This overview organises the rest of the course's last level around your three headings.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Australia after 1945 is framed in one overview: war and peace abroad, the fight for rights and freedoms at home, and a developing world reshaping the nation's region and its people. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
