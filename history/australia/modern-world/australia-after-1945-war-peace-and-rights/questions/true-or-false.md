# Australia After 1945: War, Peace and Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** War and peace, the fight for rights and freedoms, and a developing world — Australia's post-war frame in one overview.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Australia after 1945 is framed in one overview: war and peace abroad, the fight for rights and freedoms at home, and a developing world reshaping the nation's region and its people.
