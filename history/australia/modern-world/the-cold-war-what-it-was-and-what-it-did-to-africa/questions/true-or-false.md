# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Australia lives the Cold War and what followed it — the bipolar world from an Australian seat.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Australia lives the Cold War and what followed it — the bipolar world of two superpowers, seen from an Australian seat: alliances, fears and wars at the edges of a frozen centre.
