# Hands-on — The Holocaust: Ghettos and Final Solution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one line on why evidence matters after a crime this size — names, photographs, documents, testimony. The last part of this lesson is about the fight to keep that evidence believed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Holocaust proceeds through the ghettos of 1939–45 to the 'Final Solution' — and the book ends with the aftermath, including the hard fact that the Holocaust itself is contested by deniers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
