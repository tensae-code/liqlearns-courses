# The Holocaust: Ghettos and Final Solution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Holocaust.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Holocaust proceeds through the ghettos of 1939–45 to the 'Final Solution' — and the book ends with the aftermath, including the hard fact that the Holocaust itself is contested by deniers. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
