# Video script — The Holocaust: Ghettos and Final Solution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Holocaust proceeds through the ghettos of 1939–45 to the 'Final Solution' — and the book ends with the aftermath, including the hard fact that the Holocaust itself is contested by deniers.

The study follows the murder to its machinery. From 1939, Europe's Jews are herded into ghettos — walled starvation districts in the conquered cities, where disease and hunger kill while the regime decides. Then the decision: the 'Final Solution', the bureaucratised extermination of a whole people in death camps, carried out with timetables, transport lists and industrial method. And the book does not stop at liberation: it teaches the aftermath — the trials, the survivors' testimony, the evidence assembled — including the fact that the Holocaust itself is contested by deniers, and that the record exists precisely so their contest fails. To study the Holocaust whole is to accept a duty: remember with evidence.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
