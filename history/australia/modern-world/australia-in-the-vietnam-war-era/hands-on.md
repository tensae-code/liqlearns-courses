# Hands-on — Australia in the Vietnam War Era

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one line on what a nation owes the soldiers it sends to a war it later doubts. Vietnam asked Australia that question in public, in the streets. Keep the line for the lesson's end.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Australia in the Vietnam era fights communism at home and in Vietnam, divides over the war, and lives with its lasting impact — with the research project turning to the nuclear tests at Maralinga. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
