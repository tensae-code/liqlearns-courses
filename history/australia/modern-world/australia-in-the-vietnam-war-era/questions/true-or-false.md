# Australia in the Vietnam War Era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Crushing communists at home, fighting in Vietnam, a nation divided over the war, its lasting impact — and the research project on nuclear testing at Maralinga.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Australia in the Vietnam era fights communism at home and in Vietnam, divides over the war, and lives with its lasting impact — with the research project turning to the nuclear tests at Maralinga.
