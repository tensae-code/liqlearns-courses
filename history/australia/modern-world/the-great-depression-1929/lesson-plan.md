## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Between the world wars the Great Depression bites — work vanishes, savings die, governments flounder — and the book's research project asks what life was like inside it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the budget cuts your own family would make in a hard month — the Depression made whole nations live that list.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
