# The Great Depression, 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Between the wars the Great Depression bites; the research project asks what life was like inside it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Between the world wars the Great Depression bites — work vanishes, savings die, governments flounder — and the book's research project asks what life was like inside it.
