# The Great Depression, 1929 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Between the World Wars.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because between the world wars the Great Depression bites — work vanishes, savings die, governments flounder — and the book's research project asks what life was like inside it. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
