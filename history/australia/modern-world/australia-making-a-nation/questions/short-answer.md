# Australia: Making a Nation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Making a nation; Indigenous contact and non-European experiences.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because australia makes itself a nation: settlement expands, living and working conditions are fought over, self-government and democracy are won — and Indigenous and non-European experiences, closing with Indigenous and women's rights, stand inside the making, not outside it. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
