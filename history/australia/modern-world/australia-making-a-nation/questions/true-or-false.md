# Australia: Making a Nation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Settlement expands, living and working conditions are fought over, self-government and democracy are won, and social legislation builds the new Australian nation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Indigenous contact experiences and non-European migrants' experiences stand inside nation-making, closing with Indigenous and women's rights.
**Answer: True**
