# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Making of the Modern World.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the Industrial Revolution is the making of the modern world: production, work, cities and power all change — and the book opens by asking what changed, and why it happened in England first. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
