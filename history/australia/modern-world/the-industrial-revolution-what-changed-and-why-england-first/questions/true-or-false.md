# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Industrial Revolution's nature and significance open the modern world — what changed and why England first.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The Industrial Revolution is the making of the modern world: production, work, cities and power all change — and the book opens by asking what changed, and why it happened in England first.
