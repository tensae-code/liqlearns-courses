# Video script — The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Industrial Revolution is the making of the modern world: production, work, cities and power all change — and the book opens by asking what changed, and why it happened in England first.

Stage 5 opens with the event that made the modern world: the Industrial Revolution. The overview asks its two governing questions. What changed? Not one machine but everything — how goods are made, where people live, how time is ruled, who holds power, how fast the world moves. And why England first? The book sets learners hunting the combination — coal, capital, colonies, labour, invention — that lit the fire in one country before all others. Every Stage 5 story that follows, from Australia's convicts to the world wars, happens inside the world this revolution made, so the course begins by naming it whole.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
