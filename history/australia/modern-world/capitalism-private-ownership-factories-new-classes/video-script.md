# Video script — Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Capitalism remakes the modern world — private ownership, factories and new classes — and rival ideas rise to answer it: the economic, social and political ideas of the age are one story.

Ideas move the modern world as surely as machines. The overview sets out the age's great economic idea — capitalism: property privately owned, production organised in factories, society re-sorted into new classes of owners and wage-workers — and, in the same breath, the rival ideas it provoked. Every grievance the factory system created called an answer into being, and the answers — reform, union, socialism, revolution — fight through the rest of the Stage 5 book, in Australia as much as in Europe. The learner is being given the course's toolkit: name the system, and you can name its critics.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
