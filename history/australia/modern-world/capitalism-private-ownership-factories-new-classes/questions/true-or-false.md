# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Capitalism and its rival ideas remake ownership, factories and classes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Capitalism remakes the modern world — private ownership, factories and new classes — and rival ideas rise to answer it: the economic, social and political ideas of the age are one story.
