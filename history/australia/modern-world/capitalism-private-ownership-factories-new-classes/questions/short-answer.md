# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Economic, social and political ideas of the modern world.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because capitalism remakes the modern world — private ownership, factories and new classes — and rival ideas rise to answer it: the economic, social and political ideas of the age are one story. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
