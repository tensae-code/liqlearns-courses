# Hands-on — Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down who owns the place where someone in your family works, who works there, and who decides. Three different answers — that triangle is capitalism's shape. This lesson names it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Capitalism remakes the modern world — private ownership, factories and new classes — and rival ideas rise to answer it: the economic, social and political ideas of the age are one story. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
