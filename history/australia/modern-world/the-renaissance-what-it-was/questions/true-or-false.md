# The Renaissance: What It Was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** New ideas about the world emerge at the close of the medieval age, pointing toward the Renaissance.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — At the close of the medieval age, new ideas about the world emerge — the turn toward the Renaissance: old learning reborn, human beings and their world looked at with fresh eyes.
