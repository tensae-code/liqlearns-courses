# The Renaissance: What It Was — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The emergence of new ideas about the world.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because at the close of the medieval age, new ideas about the world emerge — the turn toward the Renaissance: old learning reborn, human beings and their world looked at with fresh eyes. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
