# Lesson plan — The Renaissance: What It Was

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Renaissance: What It Was
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** At the close of the medieval age, new ideas about the world emerge — the turn toward the Renaissance: old learning reborn, human beings and their world looked at with fresh eyes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: At the close of the medieval age, new ideas about the world emerge — the turn toward the Renaissance: old learning reborn, human beings and their world looked at with fresh eyes.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a skill or idea that came back into fashion in your lifetime after being forgotten — the Renaissance is that, for a whole civilisation.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
