# Australia: Rights and Freedoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Rights and freedoms” is right?
a) From the UN and the UDHR (with Doc Evatt) through early Aboriginal activism and the Stolen Generations, Charles Perkins and the 1965 Freedom Ride, land rights and Mabo, Bringing Them Home and the Apology.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) From the UN and the Universal Declaration — with Australia's Doc Evatt at the table — through early Aboriginal activism and the Stolen Generations, the 1965 Freedom Ride, land rights and Mabo, to Bringing Them Home and the Apology: Australia's rights story told whole.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
