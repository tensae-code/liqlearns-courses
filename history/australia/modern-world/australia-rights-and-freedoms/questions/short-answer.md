# Australia: Rights and Freedoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Rights and freedoms.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because from the UN and the Universal Declaration — with Australia's Doc Evatt at the table — through early Aboriginal activism and the Stolen Generations, the 1965 Freedom Ride, land rights and Mabo, to Bringing Them Home and the Apology: Australia's rights story told whole. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
