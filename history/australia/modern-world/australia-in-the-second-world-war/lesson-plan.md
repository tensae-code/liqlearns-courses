# Lesson plan — Australia in the Second World War

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Australia in the Second World War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — China's Turn and the World Wars (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Australia's home front carries the Second World War — women's roles change under the pressure of total war — and the research project investigates why the government withheld details about the bombing of Darwin.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — China's Turn and the World Wars (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Australia's home front carries the Second World War — women's roles change under the pressure of total war — and the research project investigates why the government withheld details about the bombing of Darwin.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the women in your own family's wartime photographs, in uniforms and overalls their mothers never wore — this lesson's home front is theirs.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
