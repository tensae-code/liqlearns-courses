# Australia in the Second World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The home front, women's changing roles, and the research project: why did the government withhold details about the bombing of Darwin?
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Australia's home front carries the Second World War — women's roles change under the pressure of total war — and the research project investigates why the government withheld details about the bombing of Darwin.
