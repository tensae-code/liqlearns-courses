# Australia: Postwar Migration Experiences — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Migration experiences.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because post-war migration remakes Australia: government policies and patterns, hardship and hostels, world events driving the arrivals — and the migrant contribution that changes the nation itself. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
