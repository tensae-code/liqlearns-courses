# Australia: Postwar Migration Experiences — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Post-war migration — government policies and patterns, hardship and hostels, world events driving arrivals, and the migrant contribution to Australia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Post-war migration remakes Australia: government policies and patterns, hardship and hostels, world events driving the arrivals — and the migrant contribution that changes the nation itself.
