# The Second World War: Course in Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The European war.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the war in Europe runs through North Africa and the Mediterranean to the Eastern Front — with the Holocaust at the centre of the European war, not at its edge. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
