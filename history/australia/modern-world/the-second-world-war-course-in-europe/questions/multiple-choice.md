# The Second World War: Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The European war” is right?
a) The war in Europe, North Africa and the Mediterranean, the Eastern Front — and the Holocaust at the centre of the European war.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The war in Europe runs through North Africa and the Mediterranean to the Eastern Front — with the Holocaust at the centre of the European war, not at its edge.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
