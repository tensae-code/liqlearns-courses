# The Second World War: Course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war in Europe, North Africa and the Mediterranean, the Eastern Front — and the Holocaust at the centre of the European war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The war in Europe runs through North Africa and the Mediterranean to the Eastern Front — with the Holocaust at the centre of the European war, not at its edge.
