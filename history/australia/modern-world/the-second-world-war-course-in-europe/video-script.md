# Video script — The Second World War: Course in Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The war in Europe runs through North Africa and the Mediterranean to the Eastern Front — with the Holocaust at the centre of the European war, not at its edge.

The European war is the largest land war in history. The book runs its course: Germany's early conquests; the war spilling into North Africa and the Mediterranean, where Australian troops held Tobruk and fought at El Alamein; and the Eastern Front, where the German invasion of the Soviet Union opened a war of annihilation that consumed millions of soldiers and civilians. And the book is explicit about the centre: the Holocaust — the Nazi murder of Europe's Jews and other targeted peoples — is not an appendix to this war but its core crime, the purpose toward which the Nazi war machine, in the end, bent its conquests. The theatres explain how the war moved; the centre explains what it was.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
