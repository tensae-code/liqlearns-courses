# Hands-on — Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down one song, flag or story that makes people in your country feel 'we are one people'. That feeling has a name and a history — this lesson gives both.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Nationalism — the feeling that a people is a nation and deserves its own state — is one of the century's progressive ideas, and it remade the map of the world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
