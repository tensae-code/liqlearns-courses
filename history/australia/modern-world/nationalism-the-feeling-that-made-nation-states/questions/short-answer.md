# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Progressive ideas and movements — nationalism.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because nationalism — the feeling that a people is a nation and deserves its own state — is one of the century's progressive ideas, and it remade the map of the world. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
