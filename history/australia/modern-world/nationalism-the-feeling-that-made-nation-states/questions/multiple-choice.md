# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Progressive ideas and movements — nationalism” is right?
a) Nationalism, the feeling that made nation-states, is one of the century's progressive ideas.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Nationalism — the feeling that a people is a nation and deserves its own state — is one of the century's progressive ideas, and it remade the map of the world.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
