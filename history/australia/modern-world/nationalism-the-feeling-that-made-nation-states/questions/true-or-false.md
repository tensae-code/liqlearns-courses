# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nationalism, the feeling that made nation-states, is one of the century's progressive ideas.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Nationalism — the feeling that a people is a nation and deserves its own state — is one of the century's progressive ideas, and it remade the map of the world.
