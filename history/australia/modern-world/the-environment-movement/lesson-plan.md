# Lesson plan — The Environment Movement

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Environment Movement
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a Changing Nation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The environment movement grows from two stories of sustainability into national and international action — environmental awareness becoming campaigns, and campaigns becoming law.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a Changing Nation (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The environment movement grows from two stories of sustainability into national and international action — environmental awareness becoming campaigns, and campaigns becoming law.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the protected park or beach you visit — somebody's campaign in this movement's history put the fence and the sign there.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
