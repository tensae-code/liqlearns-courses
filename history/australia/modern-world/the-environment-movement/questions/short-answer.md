# The Environment Movement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The environment movement.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the environment movement grows from two stories of sustainability into national and international action — environmental awareness becoming campaigns, and campaigns becoming law. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
