# The Environment Movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two stories of sustainability open a movement: environmental awareness grows, campaigns win national and international action.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The environment movement grows from two stories of sustainability into national and international action — environmental awareness becoming campaigns, and campaigns becoming law.
