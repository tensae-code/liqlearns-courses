# Video script — The Environment Movement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The environment movement grows from two stories of sustainability into national and international action — environmental awareness becoming campaigns, and campaigns becoming law.

The environment movement study opens with two stories of sustainability — places and peoples who learned, or never forgot, how to live within a land's limits. From those stories the movement grows: environmental awareness spreading through a prosperous, urban Australia that begins to value wilderness for itself; campaigns — blockades, petitions, court cases — that turn awareness into conflict; and victories written into national and international action, parks declared and protections legislated. The book places the movement among the great post-war changes for a reason: it changed what a nation thinks land is for — from resource alone to inheritance — and it is still arguing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
