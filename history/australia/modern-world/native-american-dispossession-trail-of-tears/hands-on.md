# Hands-on — Native American Dispossession: The Trail of Tears

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one line on what a government gains by moving a whole people off their land, and one line on what that people loses that can never be repaid. Hold both lines against this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: America's Indigenous peoples are dispossessed along the Trail of Tears — the comparison Australia's book sets beside its own contact history, with the Lewis and Clark expedition blogged as the research project. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
