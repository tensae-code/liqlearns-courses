# Native American Dispossession: The Trail of Tears — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** America's Trail of Tears.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because america's Indigenous peoples are dispossessed along the Trail of Tears — the comparison Australia's book sets beside its own contact history, with the Lewis and Clark expedition blogged as the research project. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
