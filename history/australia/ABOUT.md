# About the History — Australia course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government-curriculum textbooks — this
country's own *Retroactive 1* and *Retroactive 2*, the NSW
Australian Curriculum History books for Stages 4 and 5
(Jacaranda). The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in
a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours;
the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Australia inside the History folder
(one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what
they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a
recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school
grade each book comes from appears only in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **seven levels**, derived from the
books' own order (the Stage 4 book first, the Stage 5 book
second) and the eras as the books present them:

1. **Level 1 — First Steps: The Historian's Craft.** History
   as detective work, the other experts and dating methods,
   sources, and heritage conserved (Stage 4, Depth Study 1).
2. **Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First
   Civilisations.** Africa the cradle, the farming turn,
   Egypt, Greece, Rome, India, China (Stage 4 overviews and
   Depth Studies 2–3).
3. **Level 3 — The Medieval and Expanding World.** Rome
   dividing, feudalism, the Vikings, Church and towns, the
   Ottomans, Angkor, the shoguns, the Polynesian voyagers,
   the Mongols, the Black Death, the Aztecs, the Renaissance
   turn (Stage 4 overviews and Depth Studies 4–6).
4. **Level 4 — Contact, Conquest and Colonisation.** The
   Spanish conquest, Aboriginal Australia under colonisation
   and dispossession, and America's Trail of Tears as the
   book's own comparison (Stage 4, Depth Study 6d).
5. **Level 5 — The Making of the Modern World.** Industry,
   empire, capitalism and its rival ideas, the movements of
   peoples (enslaved Africans; convicts and settlers), and
   Australia making a nation (Stage 5, Overview 1 and Depth
   Studies 1–2a).
6. **Level 6 — China's Turn and the World Wars.** The
   dynastic cycle breaking, the two world wars with Australia
   in both, the Depression between them, and the Holocaust
   (Stage 5, Depth Studies 2b, 3 and 6a).
7. **Level 7 — Australia After 1945: Rights, Culture and a
   Changing Nation.** The Cold War from an Australian seat,
   the rights-and-freedoms road to the Apology, popular
   culture, the environment movement, post-war migration, and
   the Vietnam era with Maralinga (Stage 5, Overview 2 and
   Depth Studies 4–6b).

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the
books' own shape, walked in order):

- *Detectives first, then the whole world, landed at home:*
  "Learn the detective's craft first — questions, clues,
  sources, and the past we choose to save. Then walk the
  ancient world from Africa's first people to the First
  Emperor's tomb, cross the medieval world from feudal
  manors to Angkor and the Mongol steppe, and stand on both
  shores of contact — Tenochtitlan falling, Aboriginal
  Australia dispossessed, the Trail of Tears. Then the
  modern world arrives, and it keeps landing in Australia:
  the factories and the convict ships, the nation made and
  fought over, Gallipoli and the Somme, Darwin bombed and
  the news held back, a bus called the Freedom Ride, a court
  case called Mabo, an apology in parliament, and a mushroom
  cloud at Maralinga. This is world history the Australian
  way — every big story brought home."

## The way of the books (their own shape, preserved)

**Retroactive 1 (Stage 4)** — the NSW Stage 4 course is built
as depth studies around source work: every topic opens with
an overview, works through sources and a research project (a
virtual site study, a debate in the ecclesia, an interview
with Genghis Khan), and Australia's own past enters through
ancient-Australia sources and the closing
colonisation-and-contact study, where Aboriginal dispossession
is taught beside America's Trail of Tears. *(Student book.)*

**Retroactive 2 (Stage 5)** — Stage 5 turns the same
depth-study method onto the modern world, but Australia is
now the protagonist: convicts and settlers, nation-making,
Gallipoli and the Anzac legend, conscription's division, the
Darwin bombing the government hushed up, the Freedom Ride,
Mabo, the Apology, Maralinga — world events are always landed
on Australian soil. *(Student book.)*
