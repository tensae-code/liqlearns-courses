# Lesson plan — Africa: Cradle of Humankind

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Africa: Cradle of Humankind
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world is an African chapter, and every people's story starts there.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world is an African chapter, and every people's story starts there.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — your own family's deepest roots — this lesson says every family's road, followed far enough back, starts in Africa.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
