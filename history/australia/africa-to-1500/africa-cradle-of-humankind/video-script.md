# Video script — Africa: Cradle of Humankind

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world is an African chapter, and every people's story starts there.

The ancient world does not begin with kings or cities. It begins in Africa, where humanity itself begins. From that African cradle, generation by generation, people spread — walking into new valleys, new coasts, new continents — until the whole world held human life. The book places this spread first, as the opening chapter of everything that follows: before Egypt, before Greece, before any empire, there is this one great journey out of Africa. Every civilisation studied later in the course is a descendant of that journey.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
