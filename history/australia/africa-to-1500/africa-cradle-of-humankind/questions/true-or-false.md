# Africa: Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world is an African chapter, and every people's story starts there.
