# Africa: Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** From Africa to the world.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world is an African chapter, and every people's story starts there. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
