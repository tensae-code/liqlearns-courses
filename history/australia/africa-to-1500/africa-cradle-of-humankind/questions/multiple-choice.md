# Africa: Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “From Africa to the world” is right?
a) Humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Humanity begins in Africa and spreads across the world — the first chapter of the ancient world is an African chapter, and every people's story starts there.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
