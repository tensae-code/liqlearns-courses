# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a river. On its banks, draw what a farming people would need the river to do for them each year, and who would have to organise it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Pharaonic Egypt is a whole world built on the Nile: its chronology, its river setting, its society, its everyday life and its religion stand together as one civilisation. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
