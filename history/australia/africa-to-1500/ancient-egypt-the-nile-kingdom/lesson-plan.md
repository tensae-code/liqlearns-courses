# Lesson plan — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pharaonic Egypt is a whole world built on the Nile: its chronology, its river setting, its society, its everyday life and its religion stand together as one civilisation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Pharaonic Egypt is a whole world built on the Nile: its chronology, its river setting, its society, its everyday life and its religion stand together as one civilisation.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — any river in your own country — its floods and dry seasons still order the farming year around it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
