# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Ancient Egypt.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because pharaonic Egypt is a whole world built on the Nile: its chronology, its river setting, its society, its everyday life and its religion stand together as one civilisation. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
