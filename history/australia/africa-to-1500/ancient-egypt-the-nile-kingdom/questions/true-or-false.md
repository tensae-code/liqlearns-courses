# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pharaonic Egypt is studied whole — chronology, the Nile setting, society, everyday life and religion.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Pharaonic Egypt is a whole world built on the Nile: its chronology, its river setting, its society, its everyday life and its religion stand together as one civilisation.
