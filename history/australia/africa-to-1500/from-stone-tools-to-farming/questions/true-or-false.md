# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Evidence shows societies emerging — tools, farming, then whole civilisations.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — The evidence shows societies emerging step by step — tools first, then farming, then whole civilisations: farming is the turn that makes settled, growing societies possible.
