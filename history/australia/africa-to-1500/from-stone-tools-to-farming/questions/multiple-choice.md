# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Evidence of emerging societies” is right?
a) Evidence shows societies emerging — tools, farming, then whole civilisations.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The evidence shows societies emerging step by step — tools first, then farming, then whole civilisations: farming is the turn that makes settled, growing societies possible.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
