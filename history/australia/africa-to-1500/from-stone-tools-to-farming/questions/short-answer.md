# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Evidence of emerging societies.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Australia?
**Answer:** Because the evidence shows societies emerging step by step — tools first, then farming, then whole civilisations: farming is the turn that makes settled, growing societies possible. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
