# Lesson plan — From Stone Tools to Farming

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** From Stone Tools to Farming
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The evidence shows societies emerging step by step — tools first, then farming, then whole civilisations: farming is the turn that makes settled, growing societies possible.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The evidence shows societies emerging step by step — tools first, then farming, then whole civilisations: farming is the turn that makes settled, growing societies possible.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the bread on your own table — grown, not gathered: the farming turn feeding you today.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.

- Go back to the one thing: The evidence shows societies emerging step by step — tools first, then farming, then whole civilisations: farming is the turn that makes settled, growing societies possible. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
