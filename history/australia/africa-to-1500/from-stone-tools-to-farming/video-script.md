# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The evidence shows societies emerging step by step — tools first, then farming, then whole civilisations: farming is the turn that makes settled, growing societies possible.

How do we know societies emerged? The book answers: from the evidence, in order. First the tools — shaped stone, worked by hands that planned. Then the turn that changed everything: farming. People who plant and harvest can stay in one place, store food, feed more children, and free some hands for new work — pots, walls, weaving, worship. Settlements grow into villages, villages into towns, and at the end of the evidence trail stand whole civilisations. The overview teaches the learner to read that trail like the historians of the first level: tools, farming, civilisations — each stage proved by what was left behind.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
