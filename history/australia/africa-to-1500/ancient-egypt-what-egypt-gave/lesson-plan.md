# Lesson plan — Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

**Course:** History — Australia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Egypt's legacy is what it gave the world — and the book makes learners curate that legacy themselves, assembling an exhibit of Egypt's gifts.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Egypt's legacy is what it gave the world — and the book makes learners curate that legacy themselves, assembling an exhibit of Egypt's gifts.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the calendar on your wall — one of the gifts this lesson's exhibit would claim for Egypt.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the books themselves work, telling the past as a story.
