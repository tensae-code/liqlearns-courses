# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Egypt's legacy is curated in a research-project exhibit — what Egypt gave the world, assembled by the students.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Australia.
**Answer: False** — Egypt's legacy is what it gave the world — and the book makes learners curate that legacy themselves, assembling an exhibit of Egypt's gifts.
