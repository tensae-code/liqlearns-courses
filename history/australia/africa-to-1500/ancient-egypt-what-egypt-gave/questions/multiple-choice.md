# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Ancient Egypt's legacy” is right?
a) Egypt's legacy is curated in a research-project exhibit — what Egypt gave the world, assembled by the students.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Australia's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Egypt's legacy is what it gave the world — and the book makes learners curate that legacy themselves, assembling an exhibit of Egypt's gifts.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Australia has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
