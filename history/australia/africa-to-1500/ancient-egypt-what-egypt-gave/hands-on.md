# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose three things in your home whose roots you could trace to an old civilisation — writing, a calendar, a tool. Write one line for each, as if it were a label in a museum exhibit.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Egypt's legacy is what it gave the world — and the book makes learners curate that legacy themselves, assembling an exhibit of Egypt's gifts. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
