# Video script — Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Egypt's legacy is what it gave the world — and the book makes learners curate that legacy themselves, assembling an exhibit of Egypt's gifts.

The Egypt study does not end at the fall of the pharaohs. It ends with a question: what did Egypt give the world? The book hands that question to the learners as a research project — an exhibit, curated by the students themselves. To fill it they must weigh Egypt's gifts: its writing, its calendar, its building, its ideas about the soul and the ordered state, and decide what truly travelled from the Nile into the wider world. Curating is the historian's craft from Level 1 turned outward — choosing the evidence, writing the labels, and defending the claim that this civilisation still gives to people who never saw the Nile.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
