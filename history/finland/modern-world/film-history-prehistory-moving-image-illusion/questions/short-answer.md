# Film History: Prehistory — The Moving-Image Illusion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  Name two of the spinning illusion devices.
**Answer:** Thaumatropes, phenakistoscopes and zoetropes (any two)

**2.**  What does the eye see when still pictures are shown fast in order?
**Answer:** Movement that is not really there — the moving-image illusion
