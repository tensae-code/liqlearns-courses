# Film History: Prehistory — The Moving-Image Illusion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  A thaumatrope joins two pictures into one when it spins.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The prehistory inventors already used photographs in their devices.
**Answer: False** — Their pictures were drawn; the illusion of movement was discovered before photography joined it.
