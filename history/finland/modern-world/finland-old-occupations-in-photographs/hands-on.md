# Hands-on — Finland's Old Occupations in Photographs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask someone at home about a job in your family that no longer exists, or has wholly changed. Write the job's name and the one tool that tells its story.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Finland's work has changed beyond recognition: museum photographs from the 1910s to 1977 show lift girls, lamp cleaners, shoeblack boys and log-floaters in trades that have vanished or transformed. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
