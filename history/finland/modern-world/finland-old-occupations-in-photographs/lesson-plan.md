# Lesson plan — Finland's Old Occupations in Photographs

**Course:** History — Finland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Finland's Old Occupations in Photographs

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Finland's work has changed beyond recognition: museum photographs from the 1910s to 1977 show lift girls, lamp cleaners, shoeblack boys and log-floaters in trades that have vanished or transformed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Finland's work has changed beyond recognition: museum photographs from the 1910s to 1977 show lift girls, lamp cleaners, shoeblack boys and log-floaters in trades that have vanished or transformed.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Log-floaters ride the Vantaa river's timber in one photograph, while a shoeblack boy works Helsinki station in Olympic year 1952 in another — two vanished worlds in one set.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
