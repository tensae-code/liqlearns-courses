# Finland's Old Occupations in Photographs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The gas-lamp cleaner of 1912 works in a trade about to vanish.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The photographs in the set were taken last year for the book.
**Answer: False** — They are real museum photographs from the 1910s to 1977, held in the national photo archive.
