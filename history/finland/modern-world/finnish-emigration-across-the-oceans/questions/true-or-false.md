# Finnish Emigration Across the Oceans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3)  The empathy exercises forbid merely summarising the emigration.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  Sointula and Colonia Finlandesa are settlements in Finland.
**Answer: False** — Sointula is the Finnish settlement in Canada; Colonia Finlandesa is in Argentina — the leaving reached the whole world.
