# Lesson plan — Finnish Emigration Across the Oceans

**Course:** History — Finland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Finnish Emigration Across the Oceans
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Work and Leaving: Photographs of a Changing Finland (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Around 1900 Finns cross the Atlantic in crowds chasing the American dream — and the story is rebuilt from single photographs: a studio portrait, work gangs, a mine crew, a house rising in Sointula.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Work and Leaving: Photographs of a Changing Finland (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **introduction: Finns chase the American dream** — Around 1900 the pull begins.
2. **Task 1, Mimmi Törnroos's studio portrait** — The first photograph is a portrait.
3. **Task 2, emigrant workers in photographs** — The next photographs show the landing's other side: the jobs Finns actually got.
4. **Task 3, Finnish miners at Red Lodge** — Underground, a Finnish mine crew at Red Lodge poses in its work clothes for the camera.
5. **introduction and task choice** — The empathy book offers the same crossing twice.
6. **individual task: write from one photograph** — The individual task spreads the whole geography of leaving on the table.
7. **pair/group task: a dialogue performed to the class** — The pair task puts two voices inside one photograph and makes them argue the leaving out loud: the hopes, the fears, the farewells, performed before the class.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Around 1900 Finns cross the Atlantic in crowds chasing the American dream — and the story is rebuilt from single photographs: a studio portrait, work gangs, a mine crew, a house rising in Sointula.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Finland, as the books themselves work.
