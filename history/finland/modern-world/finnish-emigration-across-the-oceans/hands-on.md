# Hands-on — Finnish Emigration Across the Oceans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand as if for a formal portrait before a long journey. Have someone note what your pose says about your hopes — then write the diary entry that voice would write.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Around 1900 Finns cross the Atlantic in crowds chasing the American dream — and the story is rebuilt from single photographs: a studio portrait, work gangs, a mine crew, a house rising in Sointula. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
