# Finland's Estate Society in the 1800s — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Which group stands at the very bottom of the estate ladder?
a) The landless lodgers (loiset)  b) The burghers  c) The crofters  d) The clergy
**Answer: a) The landless lodgers (loiset)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3)  What is the model letter's heading?
a) Helsinki, 1 Jan 1800  b) Säkylä, 26 Feb 1941  c) Turku, 5 Oct 1889  d) Mäntyharju, 5 Oct 1889
**Answer: d) Mäntyharju, 5 Oct 1889** — That is what this lesson teaches.
