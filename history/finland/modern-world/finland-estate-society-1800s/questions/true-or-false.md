# Finland's Estate Society in the 1800s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3)  In the estate society, your birth decided your work, dress and prospects.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  Every Finn in the 1800s had a surname.
**Answer: False** — Name research shows surnames were not universal; estate decided whether a family had one at all.
