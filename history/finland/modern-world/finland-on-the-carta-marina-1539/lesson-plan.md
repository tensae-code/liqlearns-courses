## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks an old map is a photograph of the land → Olaus worked in Venice from travellers' reports; the map is an argument about the North, drawn to persuade — its wrong shapes are part of its evidence.
- Go back to the one thing: Finland first steps onto Europe's maps as Finlandia: Olaus Magnus spends twelve years drawing the North, and his Carta Marina of 1539 stays in use for a hundred years. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
