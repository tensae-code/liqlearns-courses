# Finland on the Carta Marina, 1539 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  How long did Olaus Magnus work on the Carta Marina?
a) Twelve years  b) One winter  c) Forty years  d) Three months
**Answer: a) Twelve years** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  Which edition of the map does the class hunt on?
a) The 1539 woodcut only  b) Anders Bure's map  c) A modern atlas  d) The 1572 edition
**Answer: d) The 1572 edition** — That is what this lesson teaches.
