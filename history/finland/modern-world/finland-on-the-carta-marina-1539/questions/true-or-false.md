# Finland on the Carta Marina, 1539 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2)  The Carta Marina stayed in use for about a hundred years.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  Olaus Magnus drew the map while travelling in Finland.
**Answer: False** — He worked in Venice, from travellers' reports, before engravers cut the copper plates.
