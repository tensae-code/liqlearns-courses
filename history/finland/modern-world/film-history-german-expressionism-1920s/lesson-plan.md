## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Expressionist film paints a character's inner fear onto the picture itself: distorted sets and hard shadow carry Caligari, Nosferatu and Metropolis.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: In Caligari the streets lean and the shadows are painted on — the world is drawn the way a frightened mind feels it.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
