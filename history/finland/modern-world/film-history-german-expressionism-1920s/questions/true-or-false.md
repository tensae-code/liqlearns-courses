# Film History: German Expressionism, 1920s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  In expressionist film, sets and shadow show a character's inner state.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The crooked sets of Caligari are building mistakes.
**Answer: False** — They are deliberate: the world is drawn as a frightened mind feels it.
