# Film History: German Expressionism, 1920s — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Which film unfolds in leaning streets with painted shadows?
a) Caligari (The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari)  b) Nosferatu only  c) Metropolis only  d) Battleship Potemkin
**Answer: a) Caligari (The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What is the book's word for everything placed inside the picture?
a) Montage  b) Framing  c) Attraction  d) Mise-en-scène
**Answer: d) Mise-en-scène** — That is what this lesson teaches.
