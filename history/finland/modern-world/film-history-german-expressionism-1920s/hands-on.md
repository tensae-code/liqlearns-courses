# Hands-on — Film History: German Expressionism, 1920s

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw your room twice: once as it is, once as it would look if the drawing had to show fear. Write which lines you bent, and why.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Expressionist film paints a character's inner fear onto the picture itself: distorted sets and hard shadow carry Caligari, Nosferatu and Metropolis. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
