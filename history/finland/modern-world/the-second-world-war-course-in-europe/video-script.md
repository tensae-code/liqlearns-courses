# Video script — The Second World War Course in Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Finland long called its Continuation War a separate war apart from Germany's — and its own schoolbook sets the birthday-visit photographs against that claim, and the claim cracks.

Finland has a comforting sentence about its war: it was a separate war, fought beside Germany but not with her. This lesson's book does something brave with that sentence — it sets the birthday photographs against it. Hitler received as an honoured guest at the Marshal's birthday, the courtesies, the closeness: read as evidence, the photographs press hard on the claim, and the claim cracks. The lesson belongs to Finland's wider Second World War course in Europe because it teaches the national story the way historians must: a country's own favourite sentence, tested against its own archive.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Finland.
