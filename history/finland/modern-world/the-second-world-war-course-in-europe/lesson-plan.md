## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Finland long called its Continuation War a separate war apart from Germany's — and its own schoolbook sets the birthday-visit photographs against that claim, and the claim cracks.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: The courtesies of Hitler's 1942 visit to Mannerheim's birthday are read as evidence: a 'separate war' does not receive such guests in such a way.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
