# The Second World War Course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The lesson uses Finland's own schoolbook to question Finland's own favourite sentence.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The birthday visit shows Finland keeping a cold distance from Germany.
**Answer: False** — The courtesies of Hitler's honoured visit show closeness — that is why the photographs press the 'separate war' claim so hard.
