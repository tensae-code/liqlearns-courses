# The Second World War Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What claim do the birthday photographs test?
a) That Finland's war was a separate war from Germany's  b) That the war was short  c) That Finland fought alone against Britain  d) That Mannerheim disliked guests
**Answer: a) That Finland's war was a separate war from Germany's** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What happens to the claim under the photographs?
a) It is proven  b) It is forgotten  c) It is printed  d) It cracks
**Answer: d) It cracks** — That is what this lesson teaches.
