# Hands-on — The Second World War Course in Europe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the 'separate war' claim in one sentence, then the strongest single piece of photographic evidence against it from this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Finland long called its Continuation War a separate war apart from Germany's — and its own schoolbook sets the birthday-visit photographs against that claim, and the claim cracks. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
