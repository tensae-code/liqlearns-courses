# Finland's Wartime Rationing and Shortage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3)  Ration cards were classed by the heaviness of a person's work.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  Sugar and coffee were the last things rationed.
**Answer: False** — They were rationed first; then nearly all food and clothes followed.
