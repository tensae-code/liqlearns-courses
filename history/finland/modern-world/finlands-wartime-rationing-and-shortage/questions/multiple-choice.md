# Finland's Wartime Rationing and Shortage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Which ministry ran the rationing?
a) The Ministry of Public Provision  b) The State Information Office  c) The War Museum  d) The National Archives
**Answer: a) The Ministry of Public Provision** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3)  What do eight people in Tohmajärvi share?
a) One ration card  b) One cow  c) One radio  d) One pair of new shoes
**Answer: d) One pair of new shoes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
