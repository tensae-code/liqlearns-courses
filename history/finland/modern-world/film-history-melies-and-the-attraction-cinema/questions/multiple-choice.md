# Film History: Méliès and the Attraction Cinema — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  What accident taught Méliès the stop trick?
a) A jammed film that made a bus become a hearse  b) A broken projector lamp  c) A lost reel  d) A fire in the studio
**Answer: a) A jammed film that made a bus become a hearse** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  In which year did Méliès sail his travellers to the Moon?
a) 1895  b) 1925  c) 1915  d) 1902
**Answer: d) 1902** — That is what this lesson teaches.
