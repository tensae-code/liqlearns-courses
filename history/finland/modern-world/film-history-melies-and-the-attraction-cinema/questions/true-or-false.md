# Film History: Méliès and the Attraction Cinema — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Méliès was a stage magician before he was a filmmaker.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  Attraction cinema pretends to be unedited real life.
**Answer: False** — It performs wonders openly — transformations, appearances and flights — like a magician's act.
