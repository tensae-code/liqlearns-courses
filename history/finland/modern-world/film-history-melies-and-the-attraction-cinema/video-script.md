# Video script — Film History: Méliès and the Attraction Cinema

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Stage magician Georges Méliès turns the camera into a magic box: a jammed film teaches him the stop trick, and in 1902 he sails his travellers to the Moon.

Georges Méliès is a stage magician who buys a camera — and then the camera jams. When the film runs again, the street has jumped: a bus has become a hearse, people have vanished and appeared. Another man would curse the machine; Méliès builds an art on the accident. His studio becomes a magic box where the stop trick makes anything transform, appear or fly — and in 1902 his travellers are shot to the Moon in a rocket, landing in its eye. This is attraction cinema: the film does not pretend to be life; it performs wonders and dares you not to stare.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Finland.
