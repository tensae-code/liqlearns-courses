# Hands-on — Film History: Méliès and the Attraction Cinema

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Film or act a tiny disappearance: stop, move one object while nothing records, start again. Write what the stop trick did to the scene.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Stage magician Georges Méliès turns the camera into a magic box: a jammed film teaches him the stop trick, and in 1902 he sails his travellers to the Moon. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
