# Finland's Continuation War, 1941–44 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3)  Some of the army's 'combat' photographs were staged.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The SA-kuva captions were written neutrally by later historians.
**Answer: False** — Captions were written by the photographers themselves, in the period's coloured language — hence criticism before belief.
