# Lesson plan — Finland's Continuation War, 1941–44

**Course:** History — Finland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Finland's Continuation War, 1941–44
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Finland at War: Letters, Rationing and the Home Front (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Continuation War is taught through the army's own photographs: information companies shoot some 160,000 pictures to shape mood — of the front, its leaders, its leisure and its bombed home front — and pupils interrogate every one.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Finland at War: Letters, Rationing and the Home Front (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **the SA-kuva archive and the TK companies** — The photographs come first, and so does their warning.
2. **Task 1, analysing one war-front photograph** — At the war front, the first task sets pupils on one photograph at a time.
3. **Task 1, Hitler's visit to Mannerheim's 75th birthday** — The leadership set turns on one ceremonial visit.
4. **Task 1, the front-life photo set** — Front life, in the army's pictures, is surprisingly cheerful.
5. **Task 2, the Lotta canteen visit** — The second front-life task crosses the war's gender divide on paper.
6. **Task 1, bomb damage in Kallio, Helsinki, 9 July 1941** — The home front set opens with a sound.
7. **Task 2, overview of the Helsinki bombing, 9 July 1941** — The second task stays on the same day's bombing and goes to the map.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Continuation War is taught through the army's own photographs: information companies shoot some 160,000 pictures to shape mood — of the front, its leaders, its leisure and its bombed home front — and pupils interrogate every one.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Hitler walks in to Mannerheim's 75th birthday in 1942 and sits through the Marshal's speech; pupils read Finland's closeness to Germany out of the courtesies in the photographs.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Finland, as the books themselves work.
