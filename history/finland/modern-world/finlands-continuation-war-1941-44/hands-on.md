# Hands-on — Finland's Continuation War, 1941–44

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose one war photograph from this lesson in your mind and write its interrogation: who took it, for whom, what is outside its frame?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Continuation War is taught through the army's own photographs: information companies shoot some 160,000 pictures to shape mood — of the front, its leaders, its leisure and its bombed home front — and pupils interrogate every one. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
