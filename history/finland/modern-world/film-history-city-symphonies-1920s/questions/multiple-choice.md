# Film History: City Symphonies, 1920s — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Who is the hero of a city symphony?
a) The city itself  b) One named detective  c) The cameraman's family  d) A train driver
**Answer: a) The city itself** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Which filmmaker follows his own cameraman through the city?
a) Ruttmann  b) Strand  c) Eisenstein  d) Vertov
**Answer: d) Vertov** — That is what this lesson teaches.
