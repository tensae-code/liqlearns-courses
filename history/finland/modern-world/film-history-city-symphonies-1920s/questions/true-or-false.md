# Film History: City Symphonies, 1920s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Ruttmann's symphony films Berlin's day.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  City symphonies need a single main character with a name.
**Answer: False** — Crowds, trams, machines and streets share the hero's part — the city performs its own music.
