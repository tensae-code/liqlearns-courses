# Finland's Karelian Evacuees: 420,000 New Homes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 7)  The quick-settlement law of 1940 shared out land for the evacuees.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The evacuees were welcomed everywhere without friction.
**Answer: False** — Friction flared over dialect, customs and Orthodox faith — mockery and raised rents stand in the letters beside warm welcomes.
