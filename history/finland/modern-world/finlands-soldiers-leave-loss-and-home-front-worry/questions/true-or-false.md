# Finland's Soldiers: Leave, Loss and Home-Front Worry — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 4)  Most of the Jahvetti letter-writers are wives and mothers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The Oulu mother's plea for her third son's leave is granted.
**Answer: False** — Leave is refused; the office's answer holds the army's arithmetic against the family's grief.
