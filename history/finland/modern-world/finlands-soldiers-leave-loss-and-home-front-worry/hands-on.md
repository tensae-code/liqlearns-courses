# Hands-on — Finland's Soldiers: Leave, Loss and Home-Front Worry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the field-post letter a soldier sends home in one page: what he may say, what he cannot, and the one sentence that carries the most.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Hundreds of thousands serve but only a third fight at the front; age and trade decide a man's post, letters are the only link home — and about 95,000 Finns die, while families argue leave, loss and fairness by letter. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
