# Lesson plan — Finland's Soldiers: Leave, Loss and Home-Front Worry

**Course:** History — Finland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Finland's Soldiers: Leave, Loss and Home-Front Worry

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **reading the letters through women's experience** — The teacher sheet for the whole Jahvetti set begins by naming who actually writes the letters: most of them are wives and mothers.
2. **introduction: who served, where, and the field post** — The introduction sets the scale.
3. **Task 1, Oulu letter 1944** — From Oulu in 1944, a mother writes who has already buried two sons.
4. **Task 2, Hämeenlinna letter Dec 1944** — From Hämeenlinna in December 1944, parents protest a stranger turn: the war with the Soviet Union is over by armistice, yet the young men born in 1925 still fight in the north — now against the Germans, in the Lapland War — while their letters and parcels vanish on the way.
5. **Task 3, Kotala letter 1942** — From Kotala in 1942, the argument is about usefulness.
6. **Task 4, Perniö letter Oct 1944** — From Perniö in October 1944, a wife charges unfairness in the going home itself.
7. **Task 5, Inkeroinen letter 1944 with Jahvetti's reply printed** — From Inkeroinen in 1944, a soldier's granted funeral leave is cancelled — and this time Jahvetti's reply is printed with the letter.
8. **Task 6, Lappeenranta letter c.1941** — The last letter, from Lappeenranta around 1941, is a veteran's protest.
