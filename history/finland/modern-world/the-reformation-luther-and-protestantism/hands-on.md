# Hands-on — The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one sentence for each side: why a Lutheran king might banish a Catholic archbishop, and why that archbishop might answer with a map.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Reformation splits the North's church: Olaus Magnus draws his richly pictured map in exile in Italy hoping the Pope will win the newly Lutheran Nordic kingdoms back. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
