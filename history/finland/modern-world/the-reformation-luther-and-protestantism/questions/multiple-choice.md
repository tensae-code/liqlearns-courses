# The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  Why did Olaus Magnus fill his map with pictures of northern life?
a) As an advertisement to win the Pope to restoring the Catholic North  b) To sell maps to sailors  c) To teach children animals  d) To decorate a church
**Answer: a) As an advertisement to win the Pope to restoring the Catholic North** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Who was Sweden's last Catholic archbishop?
a) Olaus himself  b) Anders Bure  c) Yrjö Kilpeläinen  d) Johannes, Olaus's brother
**Answer: d) Johannes, Olaus's brother** — That is what this lesson teaches.
