# The Reformation: Luther and Protestantism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The Nordic kingdoms had newly become Lutheran when Olaus drew his map.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  Olaus drew the map in Sweden as its archbishop.
**Answer: False** — He drew it in exile in Italy; his brother Johannes had been Sweden's last Catholic archbishop.
