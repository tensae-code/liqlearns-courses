# Film History: Soviet Montage, 1920s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  In Kuleshov's experiment the actor's face never changes.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  Audiences in the experiment praised three different actors.
**Answer: False** — They praised one actor's one expression, read as hunger, grief or desire beside different shots.
