# Film History: The Birth of Cinema — Lumière, 1895 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  On what date did the Lumière brothers first light their cinematograph in public?
a) 28 December 1895  b) 28 December 1905  c) 1 January 1900  d) 4 July 1944
**Answer: a) 28 December 1895** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What is born together with cinema in this chapter?
a) Montage  b) Sound  c) Colour  d) Framing — the choice of what the picture holds
**Answer: d) Framing — the choice of what the picture holds** — That is what this lesson teaches.
