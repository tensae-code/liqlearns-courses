# Film History: The Birth of Cinema — Lumière, 1895 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  The first audience flinched at a train that seemed to rush at them.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The Lumière show happened in a palace theatre.
**Answer: False** — It happened in a Paris café, with the cinematograph projecting workers leaving the factory and the arriving train.
