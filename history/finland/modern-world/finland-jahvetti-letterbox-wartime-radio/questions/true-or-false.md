# Finland's Jahvetti Letterbox: Wartime Radio — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2)  The Jahvetti office was part of the State Information Office.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  About 1,000 letters arrived over the whole war.
**Answer: False** — Some 100,000 letters arrived; over 70,000 survive on eighteen shelf-metres.
