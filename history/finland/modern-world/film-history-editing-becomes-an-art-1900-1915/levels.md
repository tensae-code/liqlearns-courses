# Film History: Editing Becomes an Art, 1900–1915 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Between 1900 and 1915 cutting joins the pieces into film's own grammar: Smith cuts a kiss in a tunnel to a passing train, Porter stages the Great Train Robbery, and Griffith builds the language. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
