# Film History: Editing Becomes an Art, 1900–1915 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Griffith joins shots into film's own grammar.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  Editing means filming everything in one unbroken take.
**Answer: False** — Editing is the join: separate shots cut together make meanings neither holds alone.
