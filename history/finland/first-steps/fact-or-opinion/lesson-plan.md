## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In wartime, a rumour is not a fact: Jahvetti's speech teaches the nation to name rumour-mongers' tales for what they are and to check before repeating.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: In the 4 July 1944 speech, tales gathered from listeners' own letters are read aloud and labelled rumours — the audience learns to starve a rumour instead of passing it on.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
