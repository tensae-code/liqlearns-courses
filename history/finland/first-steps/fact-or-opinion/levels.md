# Fact or Opinion — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In wartime, a rumour is not a fact: Jahvetti's speech teaches the nation to name rumour-mongers' tales for what they are and to check before repeating. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
