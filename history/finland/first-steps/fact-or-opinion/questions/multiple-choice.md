# Fact or Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  In which speech does Jahvetti take up the rumours?
a) The radio speech of 4 July 1944  b) The speech of 7 March 1944  c) A dugout letter of 1942  d) The 1942 Helsinki letter
**Answer: a) The radio speech of 4 July 1944** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  Where did the rumour tales in the speech come from?
a) Enemy broadcasts only  b) Newspaper editorials  c) The front's orders  d) Listeners' own letters
**Answer: d) Listeners' own letters** — That is what this lesson teaches.
