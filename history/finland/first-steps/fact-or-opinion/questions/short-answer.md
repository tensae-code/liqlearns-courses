# Fact or Opinion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**  What is the difference between a fact and a rumour, as the speech teaches it?
**Answer:** A fact can be checked and carried; a rumour is unchecked hearsay fed by fear and repetition

**2.**  Whose tales does Jahvetti name in the passage?
**Answer:** The rumour-mongers' tales
