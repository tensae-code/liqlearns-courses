# Fact or Opinion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  A rumour is hearsay that must be checked before it can be treated as fact.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  Jahvetti tells the nation to pass striking tales on quickly.
**Answer: False** — He names the rumour-mongers' tales and tells the nation what to do with a rumour — starve it, not spread it.
