# Lesson plan — Find History in Your Own Locality

**Course:** History — Finland · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Find History in Your Own Locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Finding History Where You Live (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History is not only far away: a finished period of the past can be found again in your own neighbourhood — in its streets, workplaces and museums — and you can step inside it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Finding History Where You Live (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Löytöretket: historian opiskelua koulun lähiympäristössä (Seppo game + green screen discovery day)** — A class has finished studying a period of history, and now it must find that same past again — outside the classroom, in its own neighbourhood.
2. **Part 3: workplace visit or museum visit with interview questions** — The hunt for history can also start from a job.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- This is the first lesson on the path — nothing is recommended before it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History is not only far away: a finished period of the past can be found again in your own neighbourhood — in its streets, workplaces and museums — and you can step inside it.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: A class that has studied explorers goes out, phone clues in hand, and photographs itself by green screen aboard Columbus's Santa Maria — the past found on its own streets.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Finland, as the books themselves work.

- Go back to the one thing: History is not only far away: a finished period of the past can be found again in your own neighbourhood — in its streets, workplaces and museums — and you can step inside it. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
