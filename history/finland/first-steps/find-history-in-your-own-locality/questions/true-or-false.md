# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2)  The discovery expedition happens in the school's own surroundings.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  In the workplace-visit part, pupils must never ask questions.
**Answer: False** — Pupils arrive with interview questions ready; questioning the worker or reading the museum's traces is the work.
