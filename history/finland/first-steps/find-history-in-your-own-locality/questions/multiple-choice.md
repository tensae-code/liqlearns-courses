# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What do pupils use to play the discovery game in their own neighbourhood?
a) Phones with location clues  b) Printed textbooks only  c) A bus tour  d) Radio sets
**Answer: a) Phones with location clues** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  What does the green screen let pupils do?
a) Print the expedition diary  b) Hide the neighbourhood  c) Grade the expedition  d) Photograph themselves inside the period studied
**Answer: d) Photograph themselves inside the period studied** — That is what this lesson teaches.
