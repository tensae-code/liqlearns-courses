# Who Studies Prehistory? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**  Prehistory is the time before writing, so its objects must be read as evidence.
**Answer: True**

**2.**  The mystery objects come with full written labels from their own time.
**Answer: False** — Prehistory left no writings; the objects are silent and their uses must be argued from the objects themselves.
