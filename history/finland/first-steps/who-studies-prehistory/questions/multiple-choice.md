# Who Studies Prehistory? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**  How many mystery objects does the class work on?
a) Nine  b) Three  c) Twenty  d) One
**Answer: a) Nine** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**  What do pupils do before the museum's answers are revealed?
a) Read the answers first  b) Draw the museum  c) Memorise the dates  d) Guess each object's use in pairs and defend the guess
**Answer: d) Guess each object's use in pairs and defend the guess** — That is what this lesson teaches.
