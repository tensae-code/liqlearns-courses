# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  What must pupils do first with an old work photograph?
a) Note what puzzles and surprises them  b) Frame it at once  c) Copy its caption  d) Colour it in
**Answer: a) Note what puzzles and surprises them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 6)  Why can a complaint-letter archive distort a picture of the past?
a) The letters are all forged  b) Letters cannot be read  c) Archives burn them  d) People write when something is wrong; the contented stay silent
**Answer: d) People write when something is wrong; the contented stay silent** — That is what this lesson teaches.
