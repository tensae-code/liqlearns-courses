# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 6)  The Carta Marina's Latin was the shared tongue of Europe's scholars.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  A staged photograph can tell us nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — It cannot prove the scene happened so, but it proves what its makers wanted viewers to imagine.
