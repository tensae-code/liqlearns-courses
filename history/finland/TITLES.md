# History — Finland course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Finland's
own — built from Finland's open learning materials (Finna
Luokkahuone / Finna.fi, the National Archives, AOE.fi, the
National Museum and the Valve Film School, in Finnish and
Swedish). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels,
not grades**: the titles below sit inside Levels 1–6, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the
books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': learn the historian's
craft on photographs, maps and letters first; read Finland
before writing from its museum objects; watch Finland enter
Europe's maps and live its estate order from inside; follow
its work and its great leaving through the photo archive;
take the film school's time journey through the camera's
century; and end with Finland at war, in the home front's own
letters and the army's own photographs. That ladder IS the
path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump
in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).
