# Finland's Viking Age: Locks, Keys and Daily Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  Padlocks with loops for chains guarded Viking Age stores.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The ear spoon shows the Viking Age had no daily hygiene.
**Answer: False** — The 59 mm ear spoon is itself an instrument of daily hygiene, and its wax was later prized in folk medicine.
