# Finland's Prehistory: Bronze and Iron Age Objects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  How wide can the bronze spectacle brooches span?
a) 20 centimetres  b) 2 centimetres  c) 2 metres  d) Half a metre
**Answer: a) 20 centimetres** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1)  Where was the Roman-made bronze cauldron found?
a) In Rome  b) In a Kiuruvesi rapids  c) At Hämeenlinna  d) In the Levänluhta water burial pond in Ostrobothnia
**Answer: d) In the Levänluhta water burial pond in Ostrobothnia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
