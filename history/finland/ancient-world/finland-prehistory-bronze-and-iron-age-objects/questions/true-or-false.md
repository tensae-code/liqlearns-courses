# Finland's Prehistory: Bronze and Iron Age Objects — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  The oval strike-a-light was believed to guard harvest and fertility in ritual hands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The spectacle brooches were plain, purely practical fasteners.
**Answer: False** — At up to 20 cm across in luxury bronze, they were display — wealth worn on the cloak.
