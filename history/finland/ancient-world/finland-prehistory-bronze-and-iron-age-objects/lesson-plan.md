# Lesson plan — Finland's Prehistory: Bronze and Iron Age Objects

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Esine 9: Pronssiset silmälasisoljet (bronze spectacle brooches, c. 1800–500 BCE)** — The bronze spectacle brooches open the metal ages.
2. **Esine 2: Soikea tuluskivi (oval strike-a-light, older Iron Age, found at Lapua and Kangasala)** — The oval strike-a-light belongs to the older Iron Age, found at Lapua and Kangasala.
3. **Esine 3: Pronssikattila (bronze cauldron from the Levänluhta water burial, made in the Roman world, Merovingian c. 550–800)** — The bronze cauldron travelled farthest of all.
