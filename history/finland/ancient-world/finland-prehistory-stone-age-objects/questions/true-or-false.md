# Finland's Prehistory: Stone Age Objects — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)  The chewed birch pitch still carries its chewer's tooth marks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)  The giant gouge was worn out by long use.
**Answer: False** — It was apparently never used; it was sunk in rapids, most likely as an offering.
