# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the elder asked about?
**Answer:** What they know of sacred and traditional practices — and, for the class list, the traditional fishing practices and the fish that were caught.

**2.** Why must the class write the elder's remembering down promptly?
**Answer:** Because it lives only in the elder's memory — unwritten, it is lost when its holder is gone; written down, it can be handed on.
