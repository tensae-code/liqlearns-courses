# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Niue, each district owned its coast and shared it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The islands' traditional food crops were all brought in recently as imports, so the gardens hold nothing from the ancestors. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: False — the gardens grow the traditional crops ancestors learned to grow on coral soil; each garden is a living archive of that handed-down knowledge.**
