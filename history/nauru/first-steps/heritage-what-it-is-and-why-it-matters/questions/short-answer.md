# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How was the Pacific people's understanding of the environment carried through time? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** It was passed down through the generations — an inheritance carried in memory from grandparents to children, including knowing when to plant, fish and spare the land.

**2.** In the teacher guide's notes for Book 3, what does a lesson about the coconut palm become? (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** A lesson about inheritance — teachers discuss with the class how knowledge is passed down through generations, using the coconut palm's place in Pacific life: what a people chooses to hand on.
