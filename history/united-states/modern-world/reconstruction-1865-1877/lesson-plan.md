# Lesson plan — Reconstruction, 1865–1877

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Reconstruction, 1865–1877
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Reconstruction tried to remake the South: Lincoln's lenient restoration gave way to Congress's Radical Reconstruction — amendments, Black officeholders, a new South begun — then violence, exhaustion and the compromise of 1877 collapsed it, leaving the freedpeople to Jim Crow.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
