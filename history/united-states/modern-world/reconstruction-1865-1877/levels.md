# Reconstruction, 1865–1877 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Reconstruction tried to remake the South: Lincoln's lenient restoration gave way to Congress's Radical Reconstruction — amendments, Black officeholders, a new South begun — then violence, exhaustion and the compromise of 1877 collapsed it, leaving the freedpeople to Jim Crow. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
