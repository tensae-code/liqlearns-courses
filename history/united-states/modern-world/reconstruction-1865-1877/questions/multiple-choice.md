# Reconstruction, 1865–1877 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What ended Reconstruction in 1877?
a) a new war  b) the compromise of 1877  c) the cotton gin  d) the Seneca Falls convention
**Answer: b) the compromise of 1877** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Radical Reconstruction's amendments do?
a) restored slavery  b) abolished slavery and made the freedpeople citizens, with Black men voting  c) ended the vote  d) left the South unchanged
**Answer: b) abolished slavery and made the freedpeople citizens, with Black men voting** — That is what this lesson teaches.
