# America in the 1960s: Civil Rights and Great Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two actions of the civil rights movement.
**Answer:** Any two of: sit-ins, freedom rides, Birmingham, and the March on Washington.

**2.** What did the wider generation do in the 1960s?
**Answer:** It challenged the status quo on every front.
