# America in the 1960s: Civil Rights and Great Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was Johnson's great domestic programme called?
a) the New Deal  b) the Great Society  c) the Open Door  d) the Fair Deal of 1890
**Answer: b) the Great Society** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these was a civil rights action of the 1960s?
a) the Boston Tea Party  b) the freedom rides  c) the Trail of Tears  d) the Scopes trial
**Answer: b) the freedom rides** — That is what this lesson teaches.
