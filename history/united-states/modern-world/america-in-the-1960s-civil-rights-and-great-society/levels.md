# America in the 1960s: Civil Rights and Great Society — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In the 1960s, Kennedy's promise and Johnson's Great Society met the civil rights movement marching on — sit-ins, freedom rides, Birmingham, the March on Washington — while a wider generation challenged the status quo on every front. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
