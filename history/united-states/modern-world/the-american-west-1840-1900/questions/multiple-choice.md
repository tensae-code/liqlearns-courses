# The American West, 1840–1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did homesteaders chase in the West?
a) free land  b) factory jobs  c) sea trade  d) university places
**Answer: a) free land** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did the wars of the West end in 1890?
a) Sand Creek  b) Wounded Knee  c) Gettysburg  d) Vicksburg
**Answer: b) Wounded Knee** — That is what this lesson teaches.
