# The American West, 1840–1900 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In the West of 1840–1900, homesteaders chased free land and gold and cattle built boom economies — while Chinese immigrants and Hispanic citizens were pushed to the margins, and the United States waged a generation-long assault on American Indian life and culture, from Sand Creek to Wounded Knee. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
