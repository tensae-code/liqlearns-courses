# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two groups besides soldiers who fought their own revolution inside the war.
**Answer:** Any two of: women, loyalists, and the enslaved.

**2.** Why did Britain's opening strategy fail?
**Answer:** It treated a people's rebellion as a disturbance to be policed — a law-and-order problem — and that misfired.
