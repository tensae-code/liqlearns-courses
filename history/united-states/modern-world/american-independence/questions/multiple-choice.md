# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where was the war decided?
a) Boston  b) Yorktown  c) Jamestown  d) Philadelphia's harbour
**Answer: b) Yorktown** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was Washington's army's first great achievement?
a) capturing London  b) surviving the early years  c) building a navy  d) ending slavery
**Answer: b) surviving the early years** — That is what this lesson teaches.
