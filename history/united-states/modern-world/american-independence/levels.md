# American Independence — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In the war of 1775–1783, Britain's law-and-order strategy misfired, Washington's army survived the early years against the odds, and the war moved south to its decision at Yorktown — while soldiers, women, loyalists and the enslaved each fought their own revolution inside the war. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
