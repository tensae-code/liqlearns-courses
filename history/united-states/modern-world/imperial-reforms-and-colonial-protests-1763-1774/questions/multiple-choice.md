# Imperial Reforms and Colonial Protests, 1763–1774 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did Britain impose new taxes after 1763?
a) to pay its war debt  b) to build schools in America  c) to fund the colonies' armies  d) to lower prices
**Answer: a) to pay its war debt** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the First Continental Congress discover?
a) a route to India  b) an American identity  c) a new tax  d) a new king
**Answer: b) an American identity** — That is what this lesson teaches.
