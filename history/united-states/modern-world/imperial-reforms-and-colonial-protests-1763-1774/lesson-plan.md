# Lesson plan — Imperial Reforms and Colonial Protests, 1763–1774

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Imperial Reforms and Colonial Protests, 1763–1774
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Revolution and the Early Republic (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain's war debt brought new taxes — the Stamp Act first — and the colonies answered as one: the Sons and Daughters of Liberty boycotted, the Townshend Acts and the Boston Tea Party followed, the Coercive Acts punished Massachusetts, and the First Continental Congress discovered an American identity.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Tea Party was about the price of tea → it was protest against taxation imposed from London; the tea was the symbol, the tax was the quarrel.
- Go back to the one thing: Britain's war debt brought new taxes — the Stamp Act first — and the colonies answered as one: the Sons and Daughters of Liberty boycotted, the Townshend Acts and the Boston Tea Party followed, the Coercive Acts punished Massachusetts, and the First Continental Congress discovered an American identity. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
