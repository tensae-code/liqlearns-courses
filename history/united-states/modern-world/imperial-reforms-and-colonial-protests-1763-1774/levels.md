# Imperial Reforms and Colonial Protests, 1763–1774 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Britain's war debt brought new taxes — the Stamp Act first — and the colonies answered as one: the Sons and Daughters of Liberty boycotted, the Townshend Acts and the Boston Tea Party followed, the Coercive Acts punished Massachusetts, and the First Continental Congress discovered an American identity. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
