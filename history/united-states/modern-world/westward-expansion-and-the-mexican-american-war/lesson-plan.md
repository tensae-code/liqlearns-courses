# Lesson plan — Westward Expansion and the Mexican-American War

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Westward Expansion and the Mexican-American War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** As the nation moved west — Lewis and Clark crossing the continent, Missouri's admission cracking the Union's balance, Texas breaking from Mexico — the Mexican-American War of 1846–1848 took half of Mexico, leaving the unanswerable question over every new acre: free soil or slave soil?
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
