# Westward Expansion and the Mexican-American War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who crossed the continent on the famous expedition?
a) Lewis and Clark  b) Jackson and Paine  c) Grant and Lee  d) no one — it was uncrossed
**Answer: a) Lewis and Clark** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How much of Mexico did the United States take in the war of 1846–1848?
a) a small border strip  b) half of Mexico  c) none  d) all of Mexico
**Answer: b) half of Mexico** — That is what this lesson teaches.
