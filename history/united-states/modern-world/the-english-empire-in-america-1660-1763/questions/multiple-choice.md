# The English Empire in America, 1660–1763 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What reached America from England's Glorious Revolution?
a) nothing at all  b) its effects crossed the ocean to the colonies  c) only new coins  d) a new language
**Answer: b) its effects crossed the ocean to the colonies** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two movements taught colonists to think across colony lines?
a) the Great Awakening and the Enlightenment  b) the Reformation and the Crusades  c) Populism and Progressivism  d) the Jazz Age and the New Deal
**Answer: a) the Great Awakening and the Enlightenment** — That is what this lesson teaches.
