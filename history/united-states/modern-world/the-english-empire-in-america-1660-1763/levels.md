# The English Empire in America, 1660–1763 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — From 1660 to 1763 the English empire in America multiplied and tightened: the Restoration colonies grew, the Glorious Revolution reached across the ocean, slavery and consumer goods bound the colonies ever tighter to Britain — and the Great Awakening, the Enlightenment and the wars for empire taught colonists to think of themselves as one people. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
