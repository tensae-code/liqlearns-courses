# Lesson plan — The English Empire in America, 1660–1763

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The English Empire in America, 1660–1763
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 1660 to 1763 the English empire in America multiplied and tightened: the Restoration colonies grew, the Glorious Revolution reached across the ocean, slavery and consumer goods bound the colonies ever tighter to Britain — and the Great Awakening, the Enlightenment and the wars for empire taught colonists to think of themselves as one people.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
