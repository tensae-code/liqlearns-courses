# The New Deal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the First New Deal stop?
a) the boom  b) the fall — banks, jobs and farms steadied  c) the war  d) immigration
**Answer: b) the fall — banks, jobs and farms steadied** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Second New Deal create for old age?
a) the League of Nations  b) Social Security  c) Prohibition  d) the cotton gin
**Answer: b) Social Security** — That is what this lesson teaches.
