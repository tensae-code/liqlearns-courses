# Lesson plan — The New Deal

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The New Deal
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Franklin Roosevelt's New Deal stopped the fall and then went further: the First New Deal's banks, jobs and farm programmes, then the Second New Deal — Social Security, labour rights, works programmes — remaking the federal government's place in American life for good.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
