# Industrial Transformation in the North — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did the mills of the industrial transformation rise?
a) the Northeast  b) the deep South  c) the far West  d) the Caribbean
**Answer: a) the Northeast** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What stitched the new economy together?
a) canals and railroads  b) stagecoaches only  c) rivers alone, unchanged  d) sailing ships only
**Answer: a) canals and railroads** — That is what this lesson teaches.
