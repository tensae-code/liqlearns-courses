# Lesson plan — Industrial Transformation in the North

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Industrial Transformation in the North
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Between 1800 and 1850 the North transformed: mills rose in the Northeast, a vibrant capitalist republic of banks and markets followed, canals and railroads stitched it together in a transportation revolution — and wage labour sorted the North into new classes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works, telling America's past as one national survey.
