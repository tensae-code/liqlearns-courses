# Industrial Transformation in the North — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Between 1800 and 1850 the North transformed: mills rose in the Northeast, a vibrant capitalist republic of banks and markets followed, canals and railroads stitched it together in a transportation revolution — and wage labour sorted the North into new classes. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
