# Lesson plan — The Early American Republic, 1790–1820

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Early American Republic, 1790–1820
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Revolution and the Early Republic (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The young republic fought over its own soul: Federalists and Democratic-Republicans clashed over what the nation should be, political parties were born in the fighting — and in 1812 the nation went back to war with Britain and survived it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Revolution and the Early Republic — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
