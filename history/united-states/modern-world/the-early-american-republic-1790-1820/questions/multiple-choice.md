# The Early American Republic, 1790–1820 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which two camps fought over the young republic's soul?
a) Federalists and Democratic-Republicans  b) North and South  c) Army and Navy  d) Cities and Ports
**Answer: a) Federalists and Democratic-Republicans** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In what year did the nation go back to war with Britain?
a) 1787  b) 1812  c) 1776  d) 1861
**Answer: b) 1812** — That is what this lesson teaches.
