# The Early American Republic, 1790–1820 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The young republic fought over its own soul: Federalists and Democratic-Republicans clashed over what the nation should be, political parties were born in the fighting — and in 1812 the nation went back to war with Britain and survived it. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
