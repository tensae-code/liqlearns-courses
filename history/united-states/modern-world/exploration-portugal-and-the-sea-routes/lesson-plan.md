# Lesson plan — Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Portugal crept down Africa's coast and rounded it to India, Columbus sailed for Spain instead and reached the Americas, and the Spanish conquistadors then toppled the Aztec and Inca empires with steel, horses, disease and Native allies.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Portugal crept down Africa's coast and rounded it to India, Columbus sailed for Spain instead and reached the Americas, and the Spanish conquistadors then toppled the Aztec and Inca empires with steel, horses, disease and Native allies.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a place, a day or a thing in their own life that this story explains.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works, telling America's past as one national survey.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Columbus sailed for Portugal → he sailed for Spain; Portugal's own road went around Africa to India.
- Go back to the one thing: Portugal crept down Africa's coast and rounded it to India, Columbus sailed for Spain instead and reached the Americas, and the Spanish conquistadors then toppled the Aztec and Inca empires with steel, horses, disease and Native allies. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
