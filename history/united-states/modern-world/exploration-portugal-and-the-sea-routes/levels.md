# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Portugal crept down Africa's coast and rounded it to India, Columbus sailed for Spain instead and reached the Americas, and the Spanish conquistadors then toppled the Aztec and Inca empires with steel, horses, disease and Native allies. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
