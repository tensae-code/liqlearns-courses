# Video script — Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Portugal crept down Africa's coast and rounded it to India, Columbus sailed for Spain instead and reached the Americas, and the Spanish conquistadors then toppled the Aztec and Inca empires with steel, horses, disease and Native allies.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

Portugal moved first, step by step: its sailors crept down the coast of Africa, harbour by harbour, until they rounded the continent and reached India by sea.

Columbus took a different road. Sailing for Spain, he crossed the Atlantic and reached the Americas — a world Spain had not known it was looking for.

Then came the conquest. Small bands of Spanish conquistadors toppled two great empires, the Aztec and the Inca. They did not do it by numbers: they had steel weapons, horses the Americas had never seen, diseases that emptied whole towns, and Native allies who had their own reasons to fight the empires that ruled them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
