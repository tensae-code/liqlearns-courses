# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which country crept down Africa's coast and rounded it to India?
a) Spain  b) Portugal  c) France  d) England
**Answer: b) Portugal** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** For which country did Columbus sail?
a) Portugal  b) England  c) Spain  d) the Netherlands
**Answer: c) Spain** — That is what this lesson teaches.
