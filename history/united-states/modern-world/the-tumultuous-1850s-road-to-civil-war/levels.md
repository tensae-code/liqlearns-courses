# The Tumultuous 1850s: Road to Civil War — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The 1850s broke the Union's compromises one by one: the Compromise of 1850 only paused the quarrel, the Kansas-Nebraska Act bled Kansas and birthed the Republican Party, Dred Scott told Black Americans they had no rights a court must respect, John Brown raided Harpers Ferry — and Lincoln's election in 1860 split the Union. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
