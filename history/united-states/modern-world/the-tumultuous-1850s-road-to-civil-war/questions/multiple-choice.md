# The Tumultuous 1850s: Road to Civil War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which act bled Kansas and birthed the Republican Party?
a) the Stamp Act  b) the Kansas-Nebraska Act  c) the Coercive Acts  d) the Townshend Acts
**Answer: b) the Kansas-Nebraska Act** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Dred Scott decision say?
a) slavery was abolished  b) Black Americans had no rights a court must respect  c) Kansas was free  d) the Union was dissolved
**Answer: b) Black Americans had no rights a court must respect** — That is what this lesson teaches.
