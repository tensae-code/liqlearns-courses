# Colonial Societies in the Americas — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Between 1500 and 1700 the newcomers built ranked colonial societies in the Americas: Spain ordered its empire by rank, the Dutch and French chased furs and empire, and England's settlers — Jamestown's tobacco planters and Massachusetts' Puritans — remade Native lands into colonies that transformed everyone they touched. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
