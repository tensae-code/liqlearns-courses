# Video script — From Cold War to Culture Wars, 1980–2000

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Reagan's revolution cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union; the Cold War then ended and a new world order was proclaimed — while at home, Clinton's new economy presided over a nation now fighting its culture wars.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

Ronald Reagan opened the period with a revolution of his own: taxes cut, government shrunk in promise, and the Soviet Union confronted head-on as an evil empire to be outlasted, not accommodated.

Then, astonishingly, the long freeze broke: the Cold War ended, the Soviet Union dissolved, and a new world order was proclaimed — America the sole superpower.

But peace abroad did not mean peace at home. Under Clinton, a booming new economy of computers and global trade enriched the nation — while Americans fought the culture wars: over values, identity, faith and the country's story itself. The enemy was no longer abroad; the argument was within.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
