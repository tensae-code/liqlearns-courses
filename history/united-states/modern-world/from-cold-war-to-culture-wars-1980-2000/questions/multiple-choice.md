# From Cold War to Culture Wars, 1980–2000 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Reagan's revolution do?
a) raised taxes and expanded government  b) cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union  c) restored Prohibition  d) ended the New Deal's Social Security
**Answer: b) cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What wars did Americans fight at home in the 1990s?
a) the culture wars  b) the civil war again  c) the frontier wars  d) the sea wars
**Answer: a) the culture wars** — That is what this lesson teaches.
