# Lesson plan — From Cold War to Culture Wars, 1980–2000

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** From Cold War to Culture Wars, 1980–2000
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Postwar America to the Twenty-First Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Reagan's revolution cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union; the Cold War then ended and a new world order was proclaimed — while at home, Clinton's new economy presided over a nation now fighting its culture wars.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Postwar America to the Twenty-First Century — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Reagan's revolution cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union; the Cold War then ended and a new world order was proclaimed — while at home, Clinton's new economy presided over a nation now fighting its culture wars.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a place, a day or a thing in their own life that this story explains.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works, telling America's past as one national survey.
