# Lesson plan — The First World War: Course of the War

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The First World War: Course of the War
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** America clung to isolation while Europe burned, until U-boats and the Zimmermann telegram pulled it in during 1917; a new kind of home front mobilised the whole nation — and Wilson went to Paris to make the peace, then lost the fight for the League of Nations at home.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
