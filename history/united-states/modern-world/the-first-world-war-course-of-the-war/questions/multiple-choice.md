# The First World War: Course of the War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did the United States enter the Great War?
a) 1914  b) 1917  c) 1919  d) 1898
**Answer: b) 1917** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What secret German offer to Mexico helped pull America in?
a) the Open Door note  b) the Zimmermann telegram  c) the Big Stick  d) the Emancipation Proclamation
**Answer: b) the Zimmermann telegram** — That is what this lesson teaches.
