# The First World War: Course of the War — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — America clung to isolation while Europe burned, until U-boats and the Zimmermann telegram pulled it in during 1917; a new kind of home front mobilised the whole nation — and Wilson went to Paris to make the peace, then lost the fight for the League of Nations at home. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
