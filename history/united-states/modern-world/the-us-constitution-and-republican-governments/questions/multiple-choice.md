# The US Constitution and Republican Governments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Paine's Common Sense attack?
a) taxes only  b) monarchy itself, in plain print  c) the French  d) the state governments
**Answer: b) monarchy itself, in plain print** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In what year did the Constitutional Convention build the federal republic?
a) 1776  b) 1787  c) 1800  d) 1812
**Answer: b) 1787** — That is what this lesson teaches.
