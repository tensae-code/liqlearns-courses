# The US Constitution and Republican Governments — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Between 1776 and 1790 the new states learned to govern themselves: Paine's Common Sense killed the king in print, the states experimented with democracy and argued over how much change the Revolution really made — and the Constitutional Convention of 1787 built a federal republic strong enough to replace the failing Articles of Confederation. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
