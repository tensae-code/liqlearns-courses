# Lesson plan — The US Constitution and Republican Governments

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The US Constitution and Republican Governments
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Revolution and the Early Republic (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Between 1776 and 1790 the new states learned to govern themselves: Paine's Common Sense killed the king in print, the states experimented with democracy and argued over how much change the Revolution really made — and the Constitutional Convention of 1787 built a federal republic strong enough to replace the failing Articles of Confederation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Revolution and the Early Republic — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Constitution came first → the Articles of Confederation came first and failed; the Convention of 1787 replaced them.
- Go back to the one thing: Between 1776 and 1790 the new states learned to govern themselves: Paine's Common Sense killed the king in print, the states experimented with democracy and argued over how much change the Revolution really made — and the Constitutional Convention of 1787 built a federal republic strong enough to replace the failing Articles of Confederation.
