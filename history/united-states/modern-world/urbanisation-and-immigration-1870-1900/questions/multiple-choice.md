# Urbanisation and Immigration, 1870–1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the African American movement to the cities called?
a) the Trail of Tears  b) the Great Migration  c) the Great Awakening  d) the Open Door
**Answer: b) the Great Migration** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who fought the chaos of the swollen cities?
a) reformers  b) filibusters  c) conquistadors  d) no one
**Answer: a) reformers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
