# Lesson plan — Urbanisation and Immigration, 1870–1900

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Urbanisation and Immigration, 1870–1900
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Industrial America and Empire (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Between 1870 and 1900 America's cities swelled faster than they could govern themselves: the African American Great Migration and new European immigration remade them, reformers fought the chaos, and a new generation of writers and thinkers reflected a changed America.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Industrial America and Empire — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
