# Lesson plan — Gilded Age Politics and Populism

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Gilded Age Politics and Populism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Industrial America and Empire (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Gilded Age politics ran on corruption and patronage, the tariff and gold questions divided the parties — and indebted farmers revolted in the Populist movement, as the 1890s closed in strikes and social unrest.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Industrial America and Empire — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
