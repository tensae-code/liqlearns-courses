# Antebellum Reform and Abolition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What fired the generation of reformers?
a) the Second Great Awakening  b) the cotton gin  c) the War of 1812  d) the national bank
**Answer: a) the Second Great Awakening** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened at Seneca Falls in 1848?
a) a battle  b) the first organised demand for women's rights  c) a cotton sale  d) a bank charter
**Answer: b) the first organised demand for women's rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.
