# Antebellum Reform and Abolition — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Second Great Awakening fired a generation of reformers: utopian communes, temperance and health reform, the abolitionist assault on slavery — and at Seneca Falls in 1848, the first organised demand for women's rights. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
