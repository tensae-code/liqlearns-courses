# Video script — Atlantic World: Rival Empires and Faiths

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Reformation split Europe's churches, and the split crossed the ocean: Dutch, French and English challengers raided, traded and planted colonies in the Atlantic world Spain had claimed alone.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

Europe's churches split. The Reformation broke the old unity of western Christianity, and rival faiths now stood behind rival kingdoms.

The quarrel did not stay in Europe. It crossed the Atlantic with the ships: where Spain had claimed the new world alone, challengers now appeared.

The Dutch, the French and the English raided Spanish treasure, traded where Spain said no trade was allowed, and planted colonies of their own. The Atlantic world became a contest — of empires, and of faiths.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
