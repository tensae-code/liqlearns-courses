# Atlantic World: Rival Empires and Faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What split Europe's churches before the quarrel crossed the ocean?
a) the Reformation  b) the printing of maps  c) a drought  d) a single battle
**Answer: a) the Reformation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which peoples challenged Spain's supremacy in the Atlantic?
a) the Dutch, French and English  b) only the Portuguese  c) no one  d) the Aztec and Inca
**Answer: a) the Dutch, French and English** — That is what this lesson teaches.
