# Video script — America in the Twenty-First Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The survey closes in the new century: 9/11 launched the war on terror and wars in Afghanistan and Iraq; at home the financial crisis struck, old disputes over race and rights continued — and the hope-and-change election of Barack Obama closed the book's story.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

The century turned, and on 11 September 2001 it broke open. The 9/11 attacks killed thousands in a morning and launched the war on terror — and with it, long wars in Afghanistan and Iraq that shaped a generation.

At home the ground shook differently: the financial crisis of 2008 broke banks, jobs and houses, and the old American disputes — over race, over rights, over who belongs — continued into the new century unresolved.

The survey closes on a door opening: the election of Barack Obama, run on hope and change — the first African American president, in a nation whose whole story, as this book tells it, is the widening and contesting of freedom. The story does not end; the book simply hands it to the reader.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
