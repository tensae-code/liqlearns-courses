# America in the Twenty-First Century — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The survey closes in the new century: 9/11 launched the war on terror and wars in Afghanistan and Iraq; at home the financial crisis struck, old disputes over race and rights continued — and the hope-and-change election of Barack Obama closed the book's story. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
