# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was America's Pacific strategy called?
a) the Big Stick  b) island-hopping  c) nullification  d) the Open Door
**Answer: b) island-hopping** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** On which two cities did the atomic bombs fall?
a) Tokyo and Osaka  b) Hiroshima and Nagasaki  c) Berlin and Rome  d) Paris and London
**Answer: b) Hiroshima and Nagasaki** — That is what this lesson teaches.
