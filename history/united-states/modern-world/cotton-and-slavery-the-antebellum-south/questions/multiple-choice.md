# Cotton and Slavery: The Antebellum South — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What machine made cotton — and slavery — more profitable?
a) the steam engine  b) the cotton gin  c) the railroad  d) the telegraph
**Answer: b) the cotton gin** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who hunted new slave states in the Caribbean?
a) abolitionists  b) filibusters  c) Puritans  d) conquistadors
**Answer: b) filibusters** — That is what this lesson teaches.
