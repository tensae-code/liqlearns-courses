# Lesson plan — Cotton and Slavery: The Antebellum South

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Cotton and Slavery: The Antebellum South
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The cotton gin made slavery more profitable than ever, and cotton built a whole world: its economics, the lives of enslaved and free African Americans under it, the planter culture it raised — and the filibusters who hunted new slave states in the Caribbean.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Expansion, Slavery and Civil War — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the cotton gin reduced the need for enslaved labour → it did the opposite: cotton became so profitable that slavery grew stronger.
- Go back to the one thing: The cotton gin made slavery more profitable than ever, and cotton built a whole world: its economics, the lives of enslaved and free African Americans under it, the planter culture it raised — and the filibusters who hunted new slave states in the Caribbean. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
