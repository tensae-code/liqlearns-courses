# Cotton and Slavery: The Antebellum South — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The cotton gin made slavery more profitable than ever, and cotton built a whole world: its economics, the lives of enslaved and free African Americans under it, the planter culture it raised — and the filibusters who hunted new slave states in the Caribbean. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
