# Video script — The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Secession became war, and 1863 turned it: Emancipation made freedom the Union's weapon, Gettysburg and Vicksburg broke the Confederacy's hopes — and Grant's grinding campaigns ended with the Union triumphant and slavery destroyed.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

States seceded, and secession became war — brother against brother, the bloodiest war in American history. Both sides mobilised whole societies: armies, factories, farms and families.

The turn came in 1863. Lincoln's Emancipation made freedom itself a Union weapon, striking at the labour the Confederacy stood on. At Gettysburg the southern invasion of the North was thrown back; at Vicksburg the Union cut the Confederacy in two along the Mississippi.

After 1863 came the grind: Grant's campaigns pressed without pause until the Confederacy collapsed. The Union was triumphant — and slavery, the war's cause, was destroyed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
