# The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which year turned the Civil War?
a) 1861  b) 1863  c) 1865  d) 1850
**Answer: b) 1863** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two victories marked the turn?
a) Yorktown and Boston  b) Gettysburg and Vicksburg  c) Harpers Ferry and Seneca Falls  d) Normandy and Berlin
**Answer: b) Gettysburg and Vicksburg** — That is what this lesson teaches.
