# Jacksonian Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** For whom did Jacksonian democracy widen the vote?
a) everyone equally  b) white men  c) only bankers  d) only soldiers
**Answer: b) white men** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was the forced westward drive of the eastern Native nations called?
a) the Great Awakening  b) the Trail of Tears  c) the transportation revolution  d) nullification
**Answer: b) the Trail of Tears** — That is what this lesson teaches.
