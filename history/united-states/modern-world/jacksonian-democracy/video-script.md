# Video script — Jacksonian Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Andrew Jackson's rough new political style widened democracy for white men, crushed the nullification crisis and the national bank — and drove the eastern Native nations west on the Trail of Tears in the Indian Removal.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

Andrew Jackson arrived like a storm: a rough, fighting style of politics that spoke for the common white man against the polished elites. Under Jacksonian democracy, the vote widened — for white men.

Jackson crushed two challenges. When South Carolina claimed the right of nullification — to void federal law — he faced it down. When the national bank stood as the symbol of moneyed power, he destroyed it.

But the same presidency drove a catastrophe. In the Indian Removal, the eastern Native nations were forced from their homelands and driven west — a forced march remembered by the Cherokee name for it: the Trail of Tears, where thousands died. Democracy widened and cruelty widened with it; the book holds both.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
