# Political Storms, 1968–1980: Vietnam and Watergate — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — From 1968 to 1980 the storms broke: identity politics fractured the old coalitions, Vietnam spiralled down and divided the nation, and Watergate brought down a president — the seventies ended with trust in government in ruins. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
