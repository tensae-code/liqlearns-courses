# Lesson plan — Political Storms, 1968–1980: Vietnam and Watergate

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Political Storms, 1968–1980: Vietnam and Watergate
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Postwar America to the Twenty-First Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 1968 to 1980 the storms broke: identity politics fractured the old coalitions, Vietnam spiralled down and divided the nation, and Watergate brought down a president — the seventies ended with trust in government in ruins.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Postwar America to the Twenty-First Century — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
