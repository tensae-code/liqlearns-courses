# Political Storms, 1968–1980: Vietnam and Watergate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three storms of the period.
**Answer:** Identity politics fracturing the coalitions; Vietnam dividing the nation; Watergate bringing down a president.

**2.** What was Watergate, in one line?
**Answer:** A break-in and cover-up that climbed until a president resigned.
