# Video script — Postwar America and Cold War Fears

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After 1945, veterans came home to the American Dream of suburbs and televisions while the Cold War froze foreign policy and McCarthyism hunted enemies at home — and underneath the prosperity, the African American struggle for civil rights gathered force.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

The veterans came home to a boom. Jobs, houses and babies multiplied: suburbs spread around the cities, televisions entered the living room, and the American Dream — a house, a car, a rising life — seemed open to millions.

But fear lived inside the prosperity. Abroad, the Cold War froze the world into two armed camps. At home, McCarthyism hunted supposed enemies within — hearings, blacklists, ruined careers on accusation.

And underneath it all, a third story gathered force: African Americans, who had served and worked through the war, pressed their struggle for civil rights with new strength. The next decade would bring it into the open.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
