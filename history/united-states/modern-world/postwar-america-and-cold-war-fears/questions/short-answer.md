# Postwar America and Cold War Fears — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two marks of the postwar prosperity.
**Answer:** Any two of: suburbs spreading, televisions in the living room, jobs and houses multiplying — the American Dream.

**2.** What was McCarthyism?
**Answer:** The hunt for supposed enemies at home — hearings and blacklists on accusation.
