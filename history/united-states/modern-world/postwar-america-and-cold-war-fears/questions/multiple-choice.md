# Postwar America and Cold War Fears — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What hunted supposed enemies at home in the Cold War years?
a) Populism  b) McCarthyism  c) the Great Awakening  d) patronage
**Answer: b) McCarthyism** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did veterans come home to?
a) the Depression  b) the American Dream of suburbs and televisions  c) Prohibition  d) the frontier
**Answer: b) the American Dream of suburbs and televisions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
