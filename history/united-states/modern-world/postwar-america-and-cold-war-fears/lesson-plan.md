# Lesson plan — Postwar America and Cold War Fears

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Postwar America and Cold War Fears
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Postwar America to the Twenty-First Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After 1945, veterans came home to the American Dream of suburbs and televisions while the Cold War froze foreign policy and McCarthyism hunted enemies at home — and underneath the prosperity, the African American struggle for civil rights gathered force.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Postwar America to the Twenty-First Century — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works, telling America's past as one national survey.
