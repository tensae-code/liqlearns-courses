# The Great Depression, 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened in 1929?
a) a great boom began  b) the crash that opened the Great Depression  c) the New Deal  d) the League fight
**Answer: b) the crash that opened the Great Depression** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were the shantytowns of the homeless called?
a) Hoovervilles  b) boom towns  c) speakeasies  d) suburbs
**Answer: a) Hoovervilles** — That is what this lesson teaches.
