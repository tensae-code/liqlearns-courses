# Lesson plan — The Great Depression, 1929

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Great Depression, 1929
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The crash of 1929 wiped out savings and jobs across the nation; Hoover answered with voluntarism and limited action while breadlines and Hoovervilles spread — and the book judges his years on the eve of the New Deal.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works, telling America's past as one national survey.
