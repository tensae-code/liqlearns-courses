# Lesson plan — The Second World War: Course in Europe

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Second World War: Course in Europe
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Fascism and Japanese expansion drew a still-isolationist America toward a war it did not seek; Pearl Harbor ended the argument, and American power landed in North Africa, Italy and Normandy on the road to Germany's defeat.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
