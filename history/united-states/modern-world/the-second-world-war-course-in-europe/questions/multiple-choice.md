# The Second World War: Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What attack brought America into the Second World War?
a) the Zimmermann telegram  b) Pearl Harbor  c) the Boston Tea Party  d) the Maine explosion
**Answer: b) Pearl Harbor** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did the great invasion open the road through France?
a) Italy  b) Normandy  c) North Africa  d) Berlin
**Answer: b) Normandy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
