# Video script — Big Business and Industrial America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Between 1870 and 1900, inventors like Edison lit a new age: inventions became industries and corporations grew to giant size — built on the backs of labour, whose strikes and unions answered back — while a new consumer culture sold the products to everyone.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

The age lit up — literally. Inventors like Thomas Edison turned electricity into light and a hundred devices into businesses. Inventions did not stay in workshops: they became industries, and industries became corporations of a size the world had not seen.

The giants were built on labour: millions working long hours in mines, mills and railroads. Labour answered back — organising unions and striking, sometimes at the cost of blood, for shorter hours and a living wage.

And a new consumer culture completed the circle: the corporations' products — sewn, canned, lit and ready-made — were sold to everyone, with advertising teaching the nation to want them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
