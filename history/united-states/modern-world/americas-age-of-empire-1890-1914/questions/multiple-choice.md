# America's Age of Empire, 1890–1914 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which war won the United States an overseas empire?
a) the Civil War  b) the Spanish-American War of 1898  c) the War of 1812  d) the Mexican-American War
**Answer: b) the Spanish-American War of 1898** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Mahan argue makes nations great?
a) sea power  b) isolation  c) farming alone  d) gold
**Answer: a) sea power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
