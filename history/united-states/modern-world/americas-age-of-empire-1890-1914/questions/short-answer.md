# America's Age of Empire, 1890–1914 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did Turner's frontier thesis say?
**Answer:** That the frontier had made America and was now closed — its energy must find new frontiers.

**2.** What prize did the war of 1898 win?
**Answer:** An overseas empire — Spain's islands, from the Caribbean to the Philippines.
