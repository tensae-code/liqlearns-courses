# Lesson plan — America's Age of Empire, 1890–1914

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** America's Age of Empire, 1890–1914
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Industrial America and Empire (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Turner's frontier thesis and Mahan's sea-power argument pushed America outward: the Spanish-American War of 1898 won an overseas empire, the Open Door reached into China, and Roosevelt's Big Stick made the Caribbean an American lake.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Industrial America and Empire — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
