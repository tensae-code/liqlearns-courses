# America's Age of Empire, 1890–1914 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Turner's frontier thesis and Mahan's sea-power argument pushed America outward: the Spanish-American War of 1898 won an overseas empire, the Open Door reached into China, and Roosevelt's Big Stick made the Caribbean an American lake. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
