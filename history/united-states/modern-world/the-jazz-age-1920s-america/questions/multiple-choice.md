# The Jazz Age: 1920s America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three products did prosperity mass-produce in this lesson?
a) cars, radio and movies  b) cotton, tobacco and tea  c) ships, guns and gold  d) books, ploughs and candles
**Answer: a) cars, radio and movies** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Prohibition ban?
a) cars  b) drink (alcohol)  c) radio  d) movies
**Answer: b) drink (alcohol)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
