# Lesson plan — The Jazz Age: 1920s America

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Jazz Age: 1920s America
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The 1920s boomed: prosperity mass-produced cars, radio and movies, and a new generation flouted the old rules — while Prohibition, the Ku Klux Klan's revival and the Scopes trial answered with backlash, under a Republican ascendancy governing the boom.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — World Wars, Depression and the New Deal — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
