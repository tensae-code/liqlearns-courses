# Lesson plan — Exploration: Causes and Results

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Causes and Results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Plants, animals, germs and people poured both ways across the Atlantic in the Columbian Exchange: potatoes and maize fed the Old World, smallpox emptied the New, and the hunger for labour built the Atlantic slave system.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies — it stands on the levels and lessons before it in this course (recommended, never forced).
