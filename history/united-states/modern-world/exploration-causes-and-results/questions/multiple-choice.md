# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the two-way movement of plants, animals, germs and people across the Atlantic called?
a) the Columbian Exchange  b) the Reformation  c) the Glorious Revolution  d) the Great Awakening
**Answer: a) the Columbian Exchange** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which foods from the Americas fed the Old World?
a) rice and tea  b) potatoes and maize  c) wheat and grapes  d) coffee and sugar only
**Answer: b) potatoes and maize** — That is what this lesson teaches.
