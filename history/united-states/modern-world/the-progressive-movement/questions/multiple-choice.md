# The Progressive Movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who were the muckrakers?
a) party bosses  b) journalists who exposed corruption and squalor  c) railroad owners  d) gold miners
**Answer: b) journalists who exposed corruption and squalor** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two presidents carried Progressivism into the White House?
a) Lincoln and Grant  b) Theodore Roosevelt and Woodrow Wilson  c) Hoover and Coolidge  d) Kennedy and Johnson
**Answer: b) Theodore Roosevelt and Woodrow Wilson** — That is what this lesson teaches.
