# Video script — The Progressive Movement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Progressivism answered the Gilded Age: muckrakers and grassroots reformers attacked corruption, monopoly and city squalor, new voices rose for women and African Americans — and the movement reached the White House with Theodore Roosevelt and Woodrow Wilson.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

Against the gilded rot, a reform movement gathered: Progressivism. Its scouts were the muckrakers — journalists who raked up the muck: rotten meat, bought legislatures, monopoly's grip — and printed it for everyone to read.

Grassroots reformers attacked on every front: corruption in city hall, monopoly in industry, squalor in the tenements. New voices rose inside the movement — for women's vote and full rights, and for African Americans against Jim Crow.

Then reform reached the top. With Theodore Roosevelt, and after him Woodrow Wilson, Progressivism entered the White House: trust-busting, regulation, and the federal government turned — for a time — into the people's counterweight to big business.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
