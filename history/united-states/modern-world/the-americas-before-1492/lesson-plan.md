# Lesson plan — The Americas Before 1492

**Course:** History — United States · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Americas Before 1492
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before any European ship arrived, three worlds stood ready to meet: the Americas held many different Native societies, Europe was on the brink of change, and West Africa already knew slavery in forms of its own — forms the Atlantic trade would soon transform beyond recognition.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Contact and Colonies — it stands at the foot of the United States' ladder, on nothing before it in this course (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. ## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works, telling America's past as one national survey.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Americas were empty before Europeans came → they were full of peoples and societies; the lesson begins with them, on purpose.
- Go back to the one thing: Before any European ship arrived, three worlds stood ready to meet: the Americas held many different Native societies, Europe was on the brink of change, and West Africa already knew slavery in forms of its own — forms the Atlantic trade would soon transform beyond recognition. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
