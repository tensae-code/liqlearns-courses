# The Americas Before 1492 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which three worlds does this lesson set side by side before 1492?
a) the Americas, Europe and West Africa  b) Asia, Australia and the Pacific  c) the North Pole, the South Pole and the equator  d) Rome, Greece and Egypt
**Answer: a) the Americas, Europe and West Africa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the lesson say about slavery in West Africa before the Atlantic trade?
a) it did not exist anywhere  b) it already existed in local forms  c) it was invented in America  d) it was only in Europe
**Answer: b) it already existed in local forms** — That is what this lesson teaches.
