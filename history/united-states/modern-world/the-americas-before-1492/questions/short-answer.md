# The Americas Before 1492 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name one thing Native societies in the Americas did before 1492.
**Answer:** They farmed, built towns and cities, traded over long distances and governed themselves — any one of these, in the learner's own words.

**2.** What would the Atlantic trade do to slavery?
**Answer:** It would transform it beyond recognition — into something far larger than the local forms that existed before.
