# Video script — The Americas Before 1492

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Before any European ship arrived, three worlds stood ready to meet: the Americas held many different Native societies, Europe was on the brink of change, and West Africa already knew slavery in forms of its own — forms the Atlantic trade would soon transform beyond recognition.

Look at the map. Here is the land this story walks.

Long before 1492, the Americas were not empty and not one people. Native societies across two continents farmed, built towns and cities, traded over long distances and governed themselves in many different ways.

Across the ocean, Europe stood on the brink of change — its kingdoms growing, its sailors and merchants reaching for new routes and new wealth. And in West Africa, strong societies traded and farmed, and slavery already existed there in local forms.

The turn this lesson holds is a warning and a promise: when these three worlds met, everything changed for all of them — and the slavery the Atlantic trade built would be something far larger and crueller than the forms that came before it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
