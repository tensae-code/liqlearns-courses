# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 8 Lesson 3: Other early civilisations — Mesopotamia"?
**Answer:** A 1949 expedition finds Uruk on its mound in Iraq; Sumerian farmers' surplus raises craftsmen, merchants, priests and scribes, and picture writing presses into cuneiform

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 1: Civilisation arises — Mesopotamia"?
**Answer:** Farming and herding turn gatherers into producers on the two rivers, and the first cities, states and cuneiform writing appear
