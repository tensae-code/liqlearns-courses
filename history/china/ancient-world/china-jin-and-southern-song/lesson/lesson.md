# China jin and southern song

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "China jin and southern song" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Lesson 8: Jin confronts Southern Song

Aguda's Jurchen destroy Liao in 1125, then turn on their Song allies; two Song emperors are carried off, Kaifeng falls in 1127, and a Jin general marvels that a few thousand men on the Yellow River bank could have stopped him

## Lesson 10: Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (2)

Jin destroys Liao and Northern Song, then the Mongols destroy Jin, Xia and Southern Song in turn

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of China jin and southern song as China's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
