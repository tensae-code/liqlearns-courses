# China jin and southern song — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aguda's Jurchen destroy Liao in 1125, then turn on their Song allies; two Song emperors are carried off, Kaifeng falls in 1127, and a Jin general marvels that a few thousand men on the Yellow River bank could have stopped him
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China jin and southern song" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
