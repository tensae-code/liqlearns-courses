Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**2.** In "Unit 8 Lesson 1: The homeland of our primitive ancestors", the story turns on: ____.
**Answer:** At Zhoukoudian, Peking Man chips stone tools, hunts and gathers in groups and masters fire in a cave of ash and burnt bone, while Upper Cave Man, 30,000 years later, learns to make fire himself
