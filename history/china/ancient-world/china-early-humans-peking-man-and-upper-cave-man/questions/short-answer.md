# China early humans peking man and upper cave man — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 8 Lesson 1: The homeland of our primitive ancestors"?
**Answer:** At Zhoukoudian, Peking Man chips stone tools, hunts and gathers in groups and masters fire in a cave of ash and burnt bone, while Upper Cave Man, 30,000 years later, learns to make fire himself

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 1: Origins of Chinese civilisation"?
**Answer:** In May 1965 geologists dig two shovel-shaped incisor fossils from a Yunnan hillside — Yuanmou Man, 1.7 million years old — opening a map of hundreds of sites from Peking Man to Upper Cave Man
