# China early humans peking man and upper cave man — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Zhoukoudian, Peking Man chips stone tools, hunts and gathers in groups and masters fire in a cave of ash and burnt bone, while Upper Cave Man, 30,000 years later, learns to make fire himself
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China early humans peking man and upper cave man" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
