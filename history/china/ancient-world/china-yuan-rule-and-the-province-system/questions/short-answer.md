# China yuan rule and the province system — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 11: Yuan rule"?
**Answer:** Kublai builds the largest territory in Chinese history; peoples move and mix until Persian and Arab settlers merge into the new Hui people, and the temporary 'branch secretariats' harden into the province system China still uses

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 10: Yuan unifies"?
**Answer:** In 1279 the Mongols complete the greatest unification since Han and Tang and govern it through the new provinces
