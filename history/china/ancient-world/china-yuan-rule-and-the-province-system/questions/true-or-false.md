# China yuan rule and the province system — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kublai builds the largest territory in Chinese history; peoples move and mix until Persian and Arab settlers merge into the new Hui people, and the temporary 'branch secretariats' harden into the province system China still uses
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China yuan rule and the province system" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
