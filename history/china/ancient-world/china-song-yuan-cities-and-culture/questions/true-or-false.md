# China song yuan cities and culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kaifeng's night markets never close, Daxiangguo Temple opens five times a month as a fair for birds, books and antiques, and Lin'an's wazi theatres fill with acrobats, storytellers and zaju plays
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China song yuan cities and culture" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
